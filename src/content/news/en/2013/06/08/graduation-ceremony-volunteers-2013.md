It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 8, 2013, to recruit 55 volunteers for the 2013 Commencement.

## Recruitment

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 55 |
| Eligibility | All students |
| Requirements | Strong communication, organization and teamwork, politeness and good presentation of STU students |
| Service period | Afternoon and evening of June 27 and morning of June 28, 2013 (details to be announced) |
| Deadline | 12:00, June 13, 2013 |
| How to apply | Download and complete the application form, rename as “Grade + Major + Name” and send to the designated email address |

The form and email are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy. Selection will be based on applications and event needs, with service hours recorded per volunteer regulations.

> ⚠️ The application form and contacts are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
