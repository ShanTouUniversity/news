It is reported that the Shantou University Resources Management Office has published **May 2013 utility charges for faculty and staff**. The current meter readings were taken on **June 1 and 2**, following the previous reading on **May 1**. Relevant faculty and staff may log in to the STU utility-management system at `http://power.stu.edu.cn` to check their individual charges.

Questions or objections may be verified at the Water and Electricity Center from **June 8 through 14**. The center can be reached at `2489`. After the verification period, charges without objections will be collected under the university's financial arrangements; the specific payment method follows internal financial procedures.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | May 2013 |
| Current meter readings | June 1 and 2, 2013 |
| Previous meter reading | May 1, 2013 |
| Query method | Log in to the STU utility-management system |
| Verification period | June 8-14, 2013 |
| Verification location | Water and Electricity Center |
| Telephone | `2489` |
| Payment | Charges are collected under the university's financial arrangements; the specific method follows internal procedures |

This article does not reproduce individual charge amounts, residence information or other personal financial records.
