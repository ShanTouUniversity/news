It is reported that Shantou University's School of Science will organize a Marine Ecology field practicum on Nan'ao Island from **June 10 to 11, 2013**, for students from across the university who are enrolled in the course. Sun Zewei of the Marine Biology Research Institute will lead the practicum, with a student coordination group assisting with organization.

## Meeting and Accommodation

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting time | 12:30 p.m. on June 10 |
| Meeting point | The STU motto stele |
| Transportation | Chartered bus to Nan'ao Island |
| Accommodation | Outdoor camping |
| Camping site | The beach at the STU Nan'ao Coastal Experimental Station |
| Return | Return to STU at 5:00 p.m. on June 11 |

## Practicum Schedule

| Time | Activities |
| --- | --- |
| Afternoon, June 10 | Survey the main species, vertical distribution and ecological succession of marine organisms on the rocky intertidal shore; examine major geological types and the state of ecological protection in the intertidal zone; and study how gravel-beach conditions affect marine-organism distribution |
| 7:30 p.m., June 10 | Observe the nocturnal activity patterns of intertidal marine organisms at the STU Nan'ao Station |
| Morning, June 11 | Study nearshore shallow-water aquaculture in Nan'ao County and its effects on shallow-sea ecosystems; visit the Marine Biology Research Institute to learn about graduate thesis experiment design; and conduct surveys of the ecological environment of offshore fishing villages and aquaculture areas, together with interviews with fishers |
| Afternoon, June 11 | Visit Nan'ao County Haiwang Marine Biology Aquaculture Base Co., Ltd. and survey the Zhuxiwan beach ecosystem before returning to STU |

## Safety and Preparation

Participants must stay with the group and follow the accompanying instructor's directions. They may not leave the group without permission or swim in the sea. The notice advises participants to prepare rain and sun protection according to the weather and to wear slip-resistant shoes. Personal supplies include a pen, notebook, flashlight or headlamp, spare clothing, beach sandals or slippers, drinking water, dry food, mosquito repellent and small change. Participants may also bring a sheet or warm clothing for camping.

Participants should protect the environment and avoid littering. Interviews with fishers should be conducted courteously and respectfully. The names and internal short numbers of student coordinators in the original notice are omitted here.
