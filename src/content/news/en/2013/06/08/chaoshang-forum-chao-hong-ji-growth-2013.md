The 6th Chaoshan Business Forum at Shantou University's Business School will be held from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on June 15, 2013**, in the Library Lecture Hall. The topic is **"Chao Hong Ji's Growth and Future"**, and Liao Chuangbin, director and president of Guangdong Chao Hong Ji Industrial Co., Ltd., will be the guest speaker.

## Guest Profile

Liao Chuangbin holds a Master of Business Administration from Lingnan College of Sun Yat-sen University. He is director and president of Guangdong Chao Hong Ji Industrial Co., Ltd. and a visiting professor at Shantou University. He also serves as a member of the National Technical Committee on Jewelry and Jade Standardization and vice president of the China Gems and Jewelry Association. He is a representative to the 12th Guangdong Provincial People's Congress and the 13th Shantou Municipal People's Congress.

According to the public profile in the notice, Liao was appointed by the former Ministry of Labor and Social Security in **2008** to serve as a judge for national vocational skills competitions. In **2009**, he was appointed by the National Gemstone Testing Center as a special expert invited to revise the national standard **"Diamond Grading."**
