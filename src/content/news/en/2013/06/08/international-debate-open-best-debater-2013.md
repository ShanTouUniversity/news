The fourth China Open Debate Tournament, or China Open 2013 Debate Tournament, was held at Xi'an International Studies University from **May 31 to June 1**, according to an STU notice. Organized by an international debate education organization, the tournament aimed to promote university debate in China and encourage exchange among debaters from different institutions and regions.

The event brought together **452 debaters**, **141 judges** and participants from **98 universities** worldwide. Participating institutions included Beijing Foreign Studies University, Fudan University, China Agricultural University, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, Shantou University, and universities from countries and regions including the United States and South Korea.

Shantou University sent **eight debaters** to the tournament. Myra Ingmanson of the English Language Centre served as team leader and accompanying judge. After two days of competition, He Y, a 2010 public administration student, ranked 10th among all 452 debaters and was named **Best Debater**. The other STU participants also gained experience in debate techniques through exchanges with competitors from China and overseas.
