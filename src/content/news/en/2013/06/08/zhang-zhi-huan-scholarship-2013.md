It is reported that the Shantou University Student Affairs Office announced the launch of the **2013 Zhang Zhihuan Scholarship** selection on **June 8**. The scholarship was established by Professor Zhang Jianping of the U.S. National Science Foundation in memory of his father, Zhang Zhihuan. It aims to encourage outstanding students from Fengshun, Guangdong, to apply to STU and to support diligent and ambitious science and engineering students.

The selection is open to undergraduates in the School of Science and the School of Engineering who are from Fengshun, Guangdong. Applicants must have good conduct, comply with university rules, demonstrate strong academic performance and diligence, and show some ability in scientific research and innovation. Each recipient will receive **RMB 2,000**.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Undergraduates from Fengshun, Guangdong, in the School of Science or School of Engineering |
| Selection criteria | Good conduct and compliance with rules; diligent study and strong academic performance; research and innovation ability |
| Award | RMB 2,000 per recipient |
| Student application deadline | Before June 14 (Friday) |
| Application channel | Submit an application to the Youth League committee of the applicant's school |
| School recommendation deadline | Before June 18 |
| Receiving office | Student Affairs Office, Education Management Section |

Eligible students must submit applications to the Youth League committee of their school **by June 14**. After preliminary review, each school must submit its recommended list and supporting materials to the Education Management Section of the Student Affairs Office **by June 18**.
