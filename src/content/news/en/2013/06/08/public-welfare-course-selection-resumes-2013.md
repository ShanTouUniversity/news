Shantou University's credit-based academic system has completed emergency repairs, restoring registration for public welfare courses, according to a notice issued by the Academic Affairs Office on June 8. Undergraduate students were asked to complete registration during the corresponding periods.

The initial registration period runs from 12:00 p.m. on June 8 to 5:00 p.m. on June 9. During this period, only students who have not previously taken a public welfare course may make their initial selections. The add/drop period runs from 9:00 a.m. on June 13 to 5:00 p.m. on June 14 and is open to all undergraduates, who may add or drop courses during that period.

The Academic Affairs Office reminded students to note the registration schedule and share the information with their classmates.
