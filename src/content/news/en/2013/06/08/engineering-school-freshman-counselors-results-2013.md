The Youth League Committee of Shantou University's Engineering School announced the interview results for 2013 freshman extracurricular counselors on **June 8, 2013**. The interviews were held on **June 5**. After considering applicants' work experience and abilities, interview performance, academic results and the overall needs of the counselor team, the school selected **23 students** to serve as extracurricular counselors for the incoming freshman cohort.

## Totals by Major

| Major | Selected students |
| --- | ---: |
| Electronics | 4 |
| Communications | 3 |
| Computer Science | 4 |
| Mechanical and Electrical Engineering | 6 |
| Civil Engineering | 6 |
| **Total** | **23** |

The extracurricular counselors will participate in services for the 2013 freshman cohort at the Engineering School. The original notice included the names of the selected students; those names are not reproduced here to protect students' personal information.
