It is reported that the Shantou University Law School Party general branch issued a commendation decision on June 8, 2013. Six individuals were awarded the title of Outstanding Party Affairs Worker of the Law School, while 22 were awarded the title of Outstanding Communist Party Member.

The commendation followed democratic review and recommendations by the Law School's Party branches. It recognized exemplary work during the previous year in developing learning-oriented and service-oriented Party organizations, and was intended to encourage Party members to serve as positive role models. The Party general branch called on all Party members to learn from the recipients and continue contributing to the Law School and its student services.

| Commendation category | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Party Affairs Workers | 6 |
| Outstanding Communist Party Members | 22 |

Personal names and other identifying details of student Party members are not reproduced in this article.
