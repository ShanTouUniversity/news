The Shantou University Office for the Development of the Three Campus Standards has reported the results of classroom conduct inspections conducted during **Week 15 of the spring semester of the 2012-2013 academic year**. The inspections took place at **8:00 a.m. on June 4** and **4:00 p.m. on June 6** in **Teaching Building E**.

The June 4 inspection recorded **one** student arriving no more than five minutes late, **six** arriving more than five minutes late, and **one** bringing breakfast into a classroom. The June 6 inspection recorded **one** student arriving no more than five minutes late and **one** arriving more than five minutes late.

| Inspection date | Time | Location | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late | Breakfast brought into classroom |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| June 4, 2013 | 8:00 a.m. | Teaching Building E | 1 | 6 | 1 |
| June 6, 2013 | 4:00 p.m. | Teaching Building E | 1 | 1 | 0 |

The university asked schools, residential colleges and relevant units to provide education to the students concerned, reinforce compliance with university rules, and continue work to improve learning conduct. This article does not reproduce students' names or student numbers. Detailed inspection records and the related attachment are available in the original notice on the STU OA system.
