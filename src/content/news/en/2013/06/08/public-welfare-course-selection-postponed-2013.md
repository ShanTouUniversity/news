It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on June 8, 2013, that the public welfare course selection originally scheduled for 9:00 that morning has been postponed.

The postponement was caused by a technical problem with the credit system. The Network Center is working urgently to fix the issue, and the specific new selection time will be announced separately. The office asked students to inform each other and apologized for the inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
