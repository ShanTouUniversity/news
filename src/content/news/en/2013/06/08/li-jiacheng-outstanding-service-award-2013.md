Shantou University recently announced the recipients of the 2012–2013 Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Service Award. Following nominations from university units and a review by the Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Service Award Selection Committee, Li Wanli, Lin Lushui and Zheng Shaoyan were selected for the award.

The award recognizes faculty and staff members who have performed exceptionally in their work at the university. Shantou University published the recipient list on June 8, 2013.
