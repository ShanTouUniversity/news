With support from the Network Center, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office put its online outdoor development equipment management system into trial operation on **June 8, 2013**. The system is intended to standardize the borrowing, return, cleaning and storage of outdoor equipment. It is available to university units and formally registered student organizations or clubs; individual applications are not accepted for the time being.

## System Functions and Application Process

Users can log in to the Digital Campus platform at <http://m.stu.edu.cn/> and open **Outdoor Development Equipment Management > Browse and Apply for Equipment**. The system provides the following functions:

- Browse stocked equipment and rental rates by category;
- Complete the **Application Form for Borrowing Outdoor Development Equipment** and read the application instructions;
- Apply for a package of basic camping equipment, including tents, trekking poles, headlamps, cooking equipment, tarps and sleeping pads;
- Have the accompanying instructor conduct a preliminary review through **Application Preliminary Review and Approval > Preliminary Review of Equipment Applications** before formal submission;
- Check the application status through **Equipment Application > Application Status Query**.

After approval, the applying unit must visit the Student Affairs Office Outdoor Development Office within **two days** to complete the required procedures. Equipment reservations should be submitted at least **five working days** in advance.

## Fees and Loan Periods

The system uses different rental methods depending on whether an item requires paid cleaning:

| Equipment type | Fee method |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment requiring paid cleaning, such as clothing, sleeping bags and backpacks | Rental fee equals the cleaning fee plus a wear-and-tear charge calculated at **1%** of the item's purchase price |
| Basic camping equipment without paid cleaning, such as tents, trekking poles, headlamps, cook sets and stove burners | Package rental of **RMB 30 per set**; each set can serve **five people** |

Equipment is rented by the occasion. Except for long-distance, intermediate- or advanced-level outdoor development activities during the summer or winter vacation, the normal maximum loan period is **five days**, including the time required to complete check-out and check-in procedures. Overdue equipment incurs a late fee calculated as follows:

> Late fee = (total equipment rental fee ÷ number of days requested) × number of overdue days

Questions and suggestions may be sent to the Student Affairs Office Outdoor Development Office at `expedition@stu.edu.cn`.
