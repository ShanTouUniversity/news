It is reported that the Shantou University Student Affairs Office announced the results of the **2012–2013 Zhang Zhihuan Scholarship** selection on **June 19**. The scholarship was established by Professor Zhang Jianping of the U.S. National Science Foundation in memory of his father, Zhang Zhihuan. It aims to encourage outstanding students from Fengshun, Guangdong, to apply to STU and to recognize diligent and ambitious students in science and engineering.

The selection was conducted through student applications and school recommendations. Two students received the scholarship, one from the School of Engineering and one from the School of Science.
