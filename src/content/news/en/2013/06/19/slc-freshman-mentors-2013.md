It is reported that Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) plans to offer university adaptation classes when the 2013 freshmen begin the fall semester. The center will recruit 28 student mentors from the main campus, generally one from each major.

## Mentor Responsibilities

Mentors will help freshmen understand their majors, plan their university coursework, and learn the procedures and rules for course selection. They will also share effective study methods, introduce campus learning activities, and assist freshmen with issues related to university study and daily life. The work will take place during the 2013 summer and fall semesters, with compensation provided according to the university's work-study standard for SLC student mentors.

## Eligibility and Application

Applicants must be current undergraduate students with strong academic performance, generally ranking among the top 20 percent of their class by GPA. They should have a solid foundation in their major, effective study methods, strong communication skills, patience, and a sense of responsibility and willingness to help fellow students improve.

Applicants should submit the application form through the Student Work-Study Application System (http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/) by 5:00 PM on June 23. If applications exceed the available positions, SLC will select candidates for interviews based on the stated requirements. The interview list is expected to be announced on June 24, and applicants not selected for interviews will not be contacted separately.
