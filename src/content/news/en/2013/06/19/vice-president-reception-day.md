It is reported that Shantou University's weekly leadership reception day is scheduled for Thursday, June 20, 2013, at 3:00 p.m., with Vice President Lin Danming on duty to meet visiting faculty and students.

According to the Party and Administration Office, the arrangements for this reception day are as follows:

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Leader on Duty | Vice President Lin Danming |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., Thursday, June 20, 2013 |
| Appointment Phone | 8650 2306 |

The Party and Administration Office reminded faculty and students who wish to visit to make an appointment by phone in advance.
