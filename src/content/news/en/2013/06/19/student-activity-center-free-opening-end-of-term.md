It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs announced on June 19 that the Student Activity Center will open free of charge to all faculty and students at the end of the term to help students balance study and relaxation during final exam preparation.

## Opening Hours

| Period | Hours |
|--------|-------|
| Monday to Friday | 15:00–22:00 |
| Saturday and Sunday | 10:00–22:00 |

The free opening runs from June 20 to June 30. Group bookings are not available to individuals; details are subject to on-site notices or staff guidance.

## Free Activities

All facilities are free of charge, including video games, board games, billiards, fitness, and climbing. Entry requires registration with a campus card, with no time limit per person.
