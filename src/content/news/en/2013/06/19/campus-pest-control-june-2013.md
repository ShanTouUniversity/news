It is reported that, to improve campus sanitation and strengthen plant pest control, the Shantou University Resource Management Office will carry out spraying operations from June 21 to 27 in green spaces, plants, fruit trees and drainage areas across the campus and the School of Continuing Education. The work will also include control of the “Four Pests”. Faculty, staff and students are advised to take care when passing through the affected areas.

## Area schedule

The notice lists the following daily work areas:

| Date | Work areas |
| --- | --- |
| June 21 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, P Building, student-residence areas, reservoir dam, Building 789 and A–B faculty and staff residences |
| June 22 | From the affiliated middle and primary schools to the Mental Health Center |
| June 23 | Around the dining hall, Administration Building and Administrative Center |
| June 24 | Around the Campus Security Team office, campus entrance, campus roads and outside the New Library |
| June 25 | Medical Building, New Teaching Building, and G, H, I and J student residences |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
