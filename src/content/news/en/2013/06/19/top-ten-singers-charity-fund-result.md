It is reported that Shantou University's Campus Top Ten Singers 20th-anniversary concert was held at the Great Hall on May 11, 2013. Admission was arranged through charitable donations, which raised a total of **RMB 5,711.00**.

## Fundraising Result

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Shantou University Campus Top Ten Singers 20th-anniversary concert |
| Date | May 11, 2013 |
| Fundraising method | Charitable admission tickets |
| Amount raised | RMB 5,711.00 |
| Destination | Fully transferred to the STU Firefly Charity Foundation |

The STU Communist Youth League Committee said that the full amount raised through the event had been transferred to the STU Firefly Charity Foundation. Questions about the fundraising activity or the amount may be directed to the STU Youth League Committee.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and STU Student Union)
