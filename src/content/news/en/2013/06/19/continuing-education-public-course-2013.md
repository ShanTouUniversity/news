Shantou University's Continuing Education Center announced on June 19 the arrangements for its 2013 continuing education public course training. The course, intended for relevant personnel from units on the main campus, is titled “Ecological Civilization Development in Guangdong in the New Era.” Detailed teaching times, locations, and formats have been published through the university's office information system and training website.

## Training and Assessment

The course carries 20 training hours, including a special lecture, exercises and question-and-answer sessions, and an open-book examination. During the training, attendance will be recorded each morning through an identity-card recognition system. Participants must bring their second-generation identity cards to check in.

Each unit will arrange the examination according to its circumstances, and the examination must be completed before the end of the short semester. Participants applying for professional-title evaluation in 2013 who pass the examination are expected to receive certificates for the individual course in mid-to-late August. Other participants are expected to receive certificates for completed courses in early December.

## Registration and Fees

Those who have not yet registered can sign up through the STU training system and download study materials from its “Download Area.” After the number of registrants is confirmed, book vouchers will be distributed to the relevant units. Participants can use the vouchers to collect textbooks at the training site.

The fees consist of 60 yuan in tuition and 28 yuan for textbooks, totaling 88 yuan. Based on registration records in the training system, the university will authorize the Finance Office to deduct the fees from the relevant participants' salaries.
