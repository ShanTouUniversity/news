Shantou University's Research Office announced on June 19 that the university had opened applications for the 2013 National Education Science Planning Projects. The application program covers key, general and young investigator projects under the National Social Science Fund for Education, as well as key and young investigator projects under the Ministry of Education. The university may recommend up to five projects.

## Categories and Requirements

The key bidding projects are key education projects under the National Social Science Fund, with reference funding of 200,000 to 250,000 yuan. Other categories include general and young investigator projects under the National Social Science Fund, and key and young investigator projects under the Ministry of Education, with reference funding of 120,000 to 150,000 yuan, 100,000 to 120,000 yuan, 30,000 yuan and 20,000 yuan respectively. Applicants must prepare budgets based on the actual needs of their research.

Applicants are generally required to hold an associate senior professional title or an equivalent qualification. Applicants and participating members of young investigator projects must be no older than 35, meaning they must have been born after June 4, 1978. Applicants without an associate senior title must be recommended by two peers holding senior professional titles. Each project leader may apply for only one project among the five categories in the same year. Additional restrictions apply to leaders of ongoing projects, recipients of relevant government research funding and researchers who do not meet the limits specified in the notice.

The 2013 program provides a project guide only for key bidding projects; applicants for other categories may propose their own project titles. Projects should generally be completed within one to three years and no later than five years. Applications will undergo peer preliminary review and centralized review by expert panels, and the university will recommend projects on a merit-based basis within its quota.

## Submission Schedule

Applicants must download and complete the appropriate application and review form for their project category, then send an electronic copy to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn for formal review by July 3. Materials that pass the review must be submitted to the Research Office by July 10. Applicants for key bidding projects must submit seven printed copies; applicants for other categories must submit four printed copies. Seven copies of the anonymous project design and argumentation document are also required, along with its electronic version.
