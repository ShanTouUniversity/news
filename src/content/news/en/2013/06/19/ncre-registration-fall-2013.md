STU Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on June 19 opening registration for the second-half 2013 National Computer Rank Examination. Examinations will be conducted in batches from September 21, with testing scheduled between 9:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. each day. The examination will adopt the 2013 syllabus and a new certificate system; all levels and subjects will use paperless testing except for Level 3 retake candidates.

## Registration

Registration will run from June 24 through 28 at the Student Affairs Window of the Academic Affairs Office on the third floor of the New Administration Center. Candidates must bring the original of their identity card, complete electronic photography and information collection, and sign the printed registration form after verifying the information. The registration fee is RMB 137 for each of Levels 1 through 4.

## Subjects under the 2013 syllabus

The new-syllabus subjects for Levels 1 through 4 are listed below. All use paperless testing:

| Level | Subjects and codes | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Computer Fundamentals and WPS Office Applications (14); Computer Fundamentals and MS Office Applications (15); Computer Fundamentals and Photoshop Applications (16) | 90 minutes |
| Level 2 | C Language Programming (24); VB Language Programming (26); VFP Database Programming (27); Java Language Programming (28); Access Database Programming (29); C++ Language Programming (61); MySQL Database Programming (63); Web Programming (64); Advanced MS Office Applications (65) | 120 minutes |
| Level 3 | Network Technology (35); Database Technology (36); Software Testing Technology (37); Information Security Technology (38); Embedded Systems Development Technology (39) | 120 minutes |
| Level 4 | Network Engineer (41); Database Engineer (42); Software Test Engineer (43); Information Security Engineer (44); Embedded Systems Development Engineer (45) | 90 minutes |

## Retake subjects under the 2012 syllabus

Retakes for Level 3 PC Technology, Information Management Technology, Network Technology, and Database Technology will continue under the 2012 syllabus. Their subject codes are 33, 34, 35, and 36 respectively. The written test lasts 120 minutes and the computer-based test lasts 60 minutes; the format may be written or computer-based according to the assigned arrangement. The notice states that retake candidates are excluded from the full paperless-testing arrangement introduced for this session.

Candidates can consult the OA notice attachments for detailed information on the textbooks and syllabus under the new system.
