Shantou University's Network and Information Center and Resources Management Office announced on June 19 that the university had begun verifying office telephone bills for May 2013. The bills cover fixed-line services provided by China Telecom Shantou and China Mobile Shantou, and the relevant data has been imported into the university's telephone management system.

## Verification and Feedback

Authorized staff from each university unit can access the telephone management system at http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn and review the bills under the “China Telecom Fixed Lines” and “China Mobile Fixed Lines” sections. After confirming that a bill is accurate, staff should submit the verification through the system. Any discrepancy between the bill details and actual telephone use must be reported by June 25.

Questions about China Telecom fixed-line bills can be sent to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn, while questions about China Mobile fixed-line bills can be sent to stunic@stu.edu.cn. Bills for which no objection is raised or no confirmation is submitted by the end of the verification period will be treated as confirmed by the system.

The university plans to have the Finance Office deduct the relevant charges on June 26 using the funding account numbers provided by each unit.
