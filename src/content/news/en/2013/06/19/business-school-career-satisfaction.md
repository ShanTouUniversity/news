Shantou University's Business School will host its 11th academic lecture of 2013 on June 21. Dr. Fan Guanhua of Nankai University will discuss career satisfaction and its influencing factors among recent university graduates entering knowledge-intensive firms. The lecture is open to all faculty members and students.

## Lecture Details

- Topic: Research on Career Satisfaction among Newly Employed University Graduates and Related Influencing Factors: Evidence from New Employees in Knowledge-Intensive Firms
- Time: 10:00-11:30 a.m., Friday, June 21
- Venue: Business School Academic Seminar Room, second floor of Wenxi Building
- Speaker: Dr. Fan Guanhua, Nankai University
- Moderator: Professor Ouyang Feng

The lecture will focus on the transition from student to worker and from school to employment among university graduates entering their first jobs, as well as the mechanisms shaping career satisfaction during this early stage. Drawing on the theory of planned behavior, sensemaking theory, and social network theory, Dr. Fan will examine how individual factors, organizational institutional environments, informal social relationships, and interpersonal behavior jointly influence career satisfaction among recent graduates working in firms.

Dr. Fan is a native of Shantou, Guangdong, and holds a Ph.D. from the Department of Human Resource Management at Nankai University's Business School. His research interests include career management and development, new employee studies, employee behavior in technology firms, and knowledge management. During his doctoral studies, he participated in a number of national, government, and enterprise research projects and published related work in leading Chinese academic journals.
