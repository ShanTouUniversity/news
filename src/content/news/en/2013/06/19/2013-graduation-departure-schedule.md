It is reported that Shantou University's Party and Government Office, Student Affairs Office and Graduate School published the graduation departure schedule for the Class of 2013 on June 19, 2013. The schedule provides guidance for graduating master's and doctoral students as well as undergraduates preparing to complete departure procedures.

The notice states that graduates should complete the relevant procedures according to the departure schedule. Graduate and undergraduate students may print the departure form themselves or collect it from the Student Affairs Office's Graduate Employment Guidance Center in Room 106 of the New Administration Center.

The arrangement covers procedures during the graduates' departure period. The specific dates, steps and responsibilities of each unit are subject to the attached departure schedule issued by the university.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office, Student Affairs Office and Graduate School)
> ⚠️ The graduation departure schedule and related departure-form attachments are available on the OA system.
