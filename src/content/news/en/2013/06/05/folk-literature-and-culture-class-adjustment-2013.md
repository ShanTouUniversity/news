Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office issued a class-adjustment notice on **June 5**. Because the instructor from the Chinese Department is attending a graduate thesis defense, the Week 15 Friday session of *Folk Literature and Folk Culture*, originally scheduled in **Room D207 on June 7**, will be suspended. The make-up class will take place during the **AB periods on June 7** in the same room.

Students enrolled in the course should inform one another and attend the make-up class at the adjusted time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
