The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University is inviting faculty members, students and photography enthusiasts from inside and outside the university to submit photographs documenting the campus scenes and activities of the 2013 Open Day.

## Submission Requirements

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible participants | Shantou University faculty and students, middle-school teachers and students, and photography enthusiasts from outside the university |
| Submission limit | No more than five photographs per participant |
| File format | JPG |
| Image size | At least 1024 × 768 pixels |
| Deadline | June 16, 2013 |

Each submission must include the work's title, the author's name, affiliated organization and contact information. Participants should compress their files and email them to the address specified in the notice, using the required subject line and archive name; the submission address should be confirmed in the original OA notice. Questions about the submission process may be directed to Teacher Lan at 86502244.

## Prizes and Usage

The competition offers one first-place prize of RMB 300, two second-place prizes of RMB 200 each and three third-place prizes of RMB 150 each. Winners will be selected according to the quality of the works, and an award may be left vacant.

Participants must agree that the organizer may use submitted works for publication and promotion, including in publications such as the *Shantou University Undergraduate Admissions Supplement*, posters and admissions websites.

The notice describes the event being documented as the **fifth Open Day**, while other 2013 STU Open Day notices refer to the corresponding event as the **sixth Open Day**. This article preserves the discrepancy in the source records.
