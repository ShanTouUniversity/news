It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University issued a notice on June 5, 2013, to hold the first Alumni Scholarship Award Ceremony and the 17th Alumni Lecture. All students of the Business School are welcome to attend. Details on time and venue are shown in the image attached to the original OA notice.

## Alumni Scholarship

To encourage students to study diligently and help those with financial difficulties, alumni Guo Shijie, Lin Chang, Lin Zelian and others initiated the Shantou University Business School Alumni Scholarship and jointly donated 200,000 yuan as the initial fund. The scholarship is designated for full-time undergraduates of the Business School who demonstrate both academic excellence and financial need.

## Alumni Lecture Speaker

The lecture will be delivered by alumnus Guo Shijie, who graduated from the Business School in 1997 with a major in Business Administration. He has long worked in stock, finance and real estate investment and currently serves as an investment advisor at Shantou Hezhong Heyue Trading Co., Ltd. and Shenzhen Xingfu Investment Consulting Co., Ltd.
