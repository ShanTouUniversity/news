The STU Degree Office and Graduate School issued a meeting notice on **June 5**. The second meeting of Shantou University's eighth Academic Degree Evaluation Committee is scheduled for **2:30 p.m. on June 19** in **Room 201 of the New Administration Center**.

Each unit should notify its Academic Degree Evaluation Committee members to attend on time. To ensure a quorum, members should avoid scheduling business travel during the meeting. Those who are genuinely unable to attend for special reasons must notify the Degree Office by **June 17**.

The Degree Office can be reached at `xwb@stu.edu.cn` or campus extension `4745`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Degree Office and Graduate School)
