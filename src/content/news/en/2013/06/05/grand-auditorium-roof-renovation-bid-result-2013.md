Shantou University's Office of Resource Management issued a public notice on **June 5**. Following the recommendation of the university's tendering and bidding leadership group, **Guangdong Runmin Construction and Installation Engineering Co., Ltd.** was named the winning contractor for the *Grand Auditorium Roof Panel Replacement and Repair Project*, with a bid amount of **RMB 426,276.50**.

## Bid Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Grand Auditorium Roof Panel Replacement and Repair Project at Shantou University |
| Winning contractor | Guangdong Runmin Construction and Installation Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Bid amount | RMB 426,276.50 |
| Public-notice period | June 5–7, 2013 |

## Inquiries and Complaints

The tenderer is the STU Infrastructure Office. Project inquiries may be made by telephone at `0754-86502490` or by fax at `0754-86502314`. Objections during the public-notice period may be submitted to the STU Supervision and Audit Office at `0754-86502346` by telephone or `0754-86503663` by fax.
