Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office issued a **June 5** notice outlining student internship arrangements for the 2013–2014 summer and fall terms and asking colleges and departments to organize related materials from the 2012–2013 spring term. The arrangements follow the *STU Undergraduate Student Internship Management Regulations* (STU Academic Affairs Document No. 6, 2010).

## Preparation and Organization

Before an internship begins, each college or department should arrange or review the host unit, assign supervisors, plan the budget, prepare or revise the course syllabus and develop an internship plan. It should also hold a mobilization meeting covering safety, discipline and professional conduct. For students from the 2009 cohort onward, the internship record must be included among the criteria used to assess the internship course.

Host units are generally to be contacted and arranged by the college or department. A student who wishes to find a host unit independently must obtain approval and report the unit's name, address and contact details before the internship; the college or department remains responsible for unified management and assessment. For centrally arranged placements, the academic administrator creates and publishes internship topics for students to select. Students who arrange their own placements may create and submit their own topics.

Supervisors are responsible for communication with host units, student guidance, education and management, and for reminding students to keep internship records and complete reports. They should monitor independently arranged placements, retrieve internship evaluations and attendance records at the end of centrally arranged placements, and assess the internship results.

## Student and Safety Requirements

Students must accept management and assessment by the university and host unit, follow workplace rules, labor discipline and confidentiality requirements, attend on time and follow the required leave procedure. They must keep internship records and write an internship report, submitting them to the college or department supervisor within **10 working days** after the internship ends. Any accidental injury or infringement of rights during the internship should be reported promptly to the supervisor.

The accompanying safety and discipline guide calls on students to follow traffic rules and host-unit safety procedures, protect themselves, cooperate with one another, maintain appropriate dress and conduct, and respect host-unit managers and supervisors. Confidential information belonging to the host unit must not be disclosed.

## Assessment

Internship results are assessed on a five-level scale: **excellent, good, satisfactory, pass and fail**. The assessment considers the host unit's evaluation, work attitude, organizational discipline, attendance, work ability, internship records and the quality of the internship report.

A failing result applies when attendance is below 70% of the full internship period, unexcused absence reaches or exceeds 15%, misconduct is not corrected after education or is serious, the report is not completed on time, the report is plagiarized, the host unit or internship facts are falsified, or another condition warrants a failing result under the applicable rules.

## Reporting and Archiving

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting deadline | By June 20, 2013 |
| Reporting body | Each college or department submits materials to the Academic Affairs Office |
| 2012–2013 spring-term material | Internship Summary Form for each internship course |
| 2013–2014 summer and fall-term materials | Internship Teaching Plan Form and Internship Mobilization, Safety and Discipline Meeting Record for each internship course |
| College or department archive | Course syllabus, teaching plan, summary form, student internship reports, internship evaluations and attendance records |

The notice provides templates for internship course syllabi, teaching plans, summary reports, mobilization-meeting records, introduction letters, evaluation forms, attendance records and independently arranged placement information. These templates are intended for use by teaching units under the applicable procedures.
