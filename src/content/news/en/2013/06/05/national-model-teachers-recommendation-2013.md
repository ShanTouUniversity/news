The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education has opened nominations for the 2013 National Model Teachers in Teaching and Educating selection. People's Daily, Guangming Daily and China Central Television, together with the Ministry of Education, plan to organize the selection before the 29th Teachers' Day. Shantou University's Human Resources Office forwarded the notice to university units on **June 5**.

## Eligibility and Criteria

The nomination is open to teachers at schools of all levels and types who have made outstanding contributions to teaching and educating and have received provincial- or ministerial-level honors or above. Candidates should demonstrate commitment to education, high professional ethics, strong expertise, notable teaching and mentoring achievements, outstanding contributions, compelling stories and broad public recognition.

The notice says that professional ethics, moving stories, work performance and the scale of contributions should guide the assessment. Preference should be given to outstanding teachers working on the front lines in rural, remote and ethnic-minority areas.

## Quotas and Selection Process

Guangdong has been allocated **two** places in the national model-teacher selection, and each prefecture-level city and relevant university may recommend **one person**. Units are to make recommendations from the bottom up through a transparent, fair and impartial process. Proposed candidates must be publicized within their region and institution for **five days**.

The Guangdong education authority will review eligibility and recommend two candidates to the Ministry of Education. Central and education media will then publicize the candidates' achievements, while members of the public may vote through newspapers, online media and other channels. A selection committee composed of central media representatives, experts, scholars and representatives of previous national model teachers will select 10 National Model Teachers based on the voting results and other circumstances.
