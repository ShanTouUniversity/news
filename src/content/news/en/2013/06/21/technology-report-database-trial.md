Shantou University Library has opened trial access to a technology reports database, providing students and faculty with research materials from government agencies, research institutions, universities and social organizations.

The platform includes DOE reports produced by research institutions affiliated with the U.S. Department of Energy and their contractors, DTIC reports collected and published by the Defense Technical Information Center, NASA reports published by the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, and PB reports published by the U.S. Office of Publication Board. It also includes technical and economic research reports from institutions such as HP Labs, Stanford University's computer science program, IBM, the World Bank and the University of Washington's economics department.

During the trial period, readers can access the platform remotely via the internet and request full-text documents by email. Requested documents are delivered as clear, complete PDF files and can also be converted into Word documents.
