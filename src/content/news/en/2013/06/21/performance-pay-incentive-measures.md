It is reported that the "Shantou University Measures for Distributing Incentive Performance Pay to Faculty and Staff" were discussed and approved at the university's 7th leadership meeting on May 28, 2013, and formally issued by the university on June 21 for implementation by its units.

The measures apply to Shantou University faculty and staff. Specific payment arrangements and implementation details are set out in the text of the measures issued by the university.
