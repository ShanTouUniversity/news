It is reported that Shantou University's Office of Academic Affairs issued the Rules for the Implementation of Student Academic Supervision at Shantou University on June 21, 2013. The document, numbered STU Academic Affairs Document [2013] No. 07, was formulated based on the Shantou University Regulations on Undergraduate Student Status under the Credit System (STU Issue [2013] No. 67) to provide an institutional basis for the university's student academic supervision work.

The rules take effect on September 15, 2013. Relevant academic supervision work across the university will be carried out under the new rules.
