With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, the Shantou University union will host a birthday gathering for single faculty and staff members born in **May, June, July or August**. The event will be held at **Blue Shore Music Restaurant** on **July 2, 2013**, with a Western set meal.

## Event Details

| Item | Time or information |
| --- | --- |
| Event date | July 2, 2013 |
| Venue | Blue Shore Music Restaurant |
| Eligible participants | Single faculty and staff members born from May through August; voluntary registration |
| Discounted price for birthday celebrants | RMB 50 per person |
| Other relatives, friends or colleagues | RMB 105 per person at the group-discount rate |

Faculty and staff members celebrating a birthday that month, as well as those who did not attend the May–August birthday dinner at the Junhua Hotel, may attend at the discounted price of RMB 50 per person. Relatives, friends and colleagues may purchase tickets at RMB 105 per person. If registrations exceed the restaurant's capacity, the union will give priority to single faculty and staff members celebrating a birthday that month.

## Reservations and Tickets

| Item | Time or information |
| --- | --- |
| Online reservations | Noon on June 21 to noon on June 27 |
| Deduction or payment | June 28 |
| Ticket collection | 9:30 a.m.–noon on July 1 |
| Reservation system | <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/> |

Registration is valid only through the online reservation system. Faculty and staff members may pay in cash or use campus-card deduction; the union's event plan governs the detailed payment and ticket-collection procedures. After the reservation system closes, reservations and cancellations will no longer be accepted. Tickets cannot be refunded after registration and payment. Failure to pay on time, or a failed campus-card deduction caused by insufficient balance, will be treated as withdrawal from the event. The university will not arrange transportation, so attendees must travel independently and observe traffic safety.
