It is reported that Shantou University issued a notice on June 21, 2013, to launch the selection and development of university-level “2011 Collaborative Innovation Centers and Collaborative Innovation Development Centers (Collaborative Education Platforms).”

To implement the *Opinions of the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Finance on Implementing the Program for Enhancing Innovation Capacity of Higher Education Institutions* (Jiao Ji [2012] No. 6) and accelerate the 2011 Plan, the university will carry out cultivation for the establishment of national and Guangdong provincial collaborative innovation centers.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Initiative | Selection and development of university-level 2011 Collaborative Innovation Centers and Collaborative Innovation Development Centers (Collaborative Education Platforms) |
| Basis | Jiao Ji [2012] No. 6, Program for Enhancing Innovation Capacity of Higher Education Institutions |
| Approved at | 8th university leadership meeting, June 18, 2013 |
| Issued by | Research Office |

Units are asked to attach great importance to the work and actively organize applications. The full call and attachments are available in the OA system.
