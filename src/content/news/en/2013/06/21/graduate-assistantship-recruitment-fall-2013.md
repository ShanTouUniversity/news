It is reported that Shantou University Graduate School opened recruitment on June 21 for graduate assistantship positions for the fall 2013 semester. The recruitment is open to all graduate students at the main campus, while the position settings and detailed vacancies are provided in the notice attachments.

## Application Arrangements

Graduate students interested in applying must submit an application form to the responsible staff member of the relevant host unit **by 12:00 on June 28**. Host units must consolidate the applications and submit the list to the Graduate School **by 17:00 on June 28**, along with the electronic form as required.

Applicants may contact the relevant host unit for position and application details. Questions may be directed to the Graduate School.

The position table, application form and consolidated list contain personal or position-level details and are not reproduced here. Specific vacancies and form requirements are subject to the attachments to the Graduate School OA notice.
