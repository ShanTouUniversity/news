Shantou University's 2013 commencement ceremony will be held at the university gymnasium on June 28. The Office of Party and Government Affairs has invited 100 faculty and staff representatives from across the university to attend the ceremony and witness the graduating students' milestone.

Faculty and staff members who wish to attend may collect tickets from their school or college office. Faculty and staff from other teaching and teaching-support units should collect tickets from the Academic Affairs Office, while administrative and logistics staff should collect them from the labor union. The notice asks each unit to send a representative to collect the tickets from the confidential affairs office in Room 237 of the New Administration Building before the end of the workday on June 26.
