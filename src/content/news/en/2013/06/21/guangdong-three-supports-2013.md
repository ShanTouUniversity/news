Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office recently forwarded the implementation plan for Guangdong’s 2013 Three Supports and One Assistance program to the university’s schools and residential colleges, launching on-campus promotion and outreach for the program.

The plan was jointly issued by the Organization Department of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and seven other government departments. It sets out the province’s arrangements for the program for university graduates. The Student Affairs Office asked each school and residential college to promote the policy in accordance with the plan and help eligible graduates learn about the grassroots employment program and related arrangements.

The detailed implementation provisions, eligibility requirements and application procedures are subject to the original attachment.
