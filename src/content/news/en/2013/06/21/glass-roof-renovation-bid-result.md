It is reported that, based on the review and recommendation of the Shantou University bidding leadership group, the university announced on June 21 the bidding results for the construction of its public restroom glass roof renovation project. Shantou Longhua Construction General Company emerged as the winning bidder with a contract amount of RMB 273,836.54.

## Award Details

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Project | STU Public Restroom Glass Roof Renovation Project |
| Winning Bidder | Shantou Longhua Construction General Company |
| Award Amount | RMB 273,836.54 |
| Public Notice Period | June 21 to June 23, 2013 |

## Objections and Complaints

During the notice period, objections to the bidding results may be raised with the tenderer, the STU Infrastructure Office (contacts: Yao Yijia and Lin Yuqi; tel: 0754-86502490; fax: 0754-86502314).

Complaints are handled by the university's Supervision and Audit Office (tel: 0754-86502346; fax: 0754-86503663).
