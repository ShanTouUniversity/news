It is reported that Shantou University's tendering and bidding leading group completed its review and recommendation of a contractor for the teaching-area public restroom renovation project. The university announced the result on June 21, 2013.

Shantou Chaoyang First Construction General Co., Ltd. won the bid with a contract amount of RMB 288,666. The result was open for public comment from June 21 to June 23.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Teaching-area public restroom renovation |
| Winning contractor | Shantou Chaoyang First Construction General Co., Ltd. |
| Contract amount | RMB 288,666 |
| Public notice period | June 21-23, 2013 |

During the public notice period, relevant complaints were handled by Shantou University's Office of Supervision and Audit.
