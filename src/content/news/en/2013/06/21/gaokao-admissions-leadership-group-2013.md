It is reported that Shantou University established a leading group for its 2013 admissions work for the national college entrance examination on June 21, 2013. The decision was issued as STU Issue [2013] No. 83 and is intended to coordinate the university's undergraduate admissions work for the year.

Gu Peihua was appointed group leader, with Wen Xiaobo and Xu Zongling as deputy group leaders. The members are Sun Fenqiang, Lu Xiaohua, Yu Hong, and Zeng Rui. The group will organize and coordinate the university's 2013 admissions work in accordance with relevant instructions from higher-level authorities.
