It is reported that, in accordance with the regulations on annual-salary faculty position appointments, and based on teacher applications and recommendations from various units, the Faculty Appointment Committee of Shantou University completed its evaluation on June 18, 2013. A total of 33 faculty members are proposed for appointment to 2012 annual-salary senior positions, with the list published for public comment starting June 21.

## Proposed Appointees

### Professors (12)

Li Yuanyou, Lin Furong, Wu Ping, Wang Jiangyong, Zhang Xin, Bao Nengsheng, Cai Weihong, Sun Haojun, Yan Jingwen, Zhao Xiaohua, Zhuang Zhemin, Ma Fengqi

### Associate Professors (21)

Cai Zemin, Chen Qinwu, Jiang Dazhi, Li Xutao, Tang Yajuan, Wang Qinhua, Wei Chuliang, Wu Fupei, Yuan Ye, An Ning, Luo Qianjun, Yang Weigang, Chen Ge, Hu Shaodong, Lin Jiali, Yan Bo, Tiemuer Gaolitao, Yan Shenghua, Liao Wenlan, Yu Duanrong, Bai Wenjun

## Public Comment Period

According to the appointment procedures for annual-salary faculty positions, the public comment period runs from June 21 to June 25, 2013. Anyone with objections may submit written feedback to the university before June 25; submissions from units must bear an official seal, while those from individuals must carry the submitter's real name.
