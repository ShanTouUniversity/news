According to the Office of Student Affairs at Shantou University, the Student Learning Center (SLC) has completed its selection of duty staff for the 2013–2014 academic year. Based on the position requirements and applicants' interview performance, the center selected five students to serve as duty staff during the new academic year.

The selection results have been made available to the relevant students. The selected-student list and related materials are subject to the original notice published on Shantou University's OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Student Affairs)
>
> ⚠️ The selected-student list and other materials containing students' personal information are available on the OA system.
