It is reported that STU's Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 21 publishing the successful parent ticket reservations for the 2013 Commencement. The ceremony is scheduled to take place in the university gymnasium on June 28, while the reservation results and related roster are available in the OA attachment.

## Notice Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Notice | Successful parent ticket reservations for the 2013 Commencement |
| Notice date | June 21, 2013 |
| Comment deadline | 5 p.m. on June 24, 2013 |
| Receiving office | Graduate Employment Guidance Center, Student Affairs Office |
| Telephone | 0754-86502252 |

During the notice period, units or individuals with comments may contact the receiving office in writing, in person, or by telephone. Submissions from units must bear the official seal, while individuals must provide their real names.

Student names and reservation rosters are not reproduced here to protect personal information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
