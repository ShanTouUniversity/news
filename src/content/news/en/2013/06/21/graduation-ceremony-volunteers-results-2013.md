It is reported that Shantou University Student Affairs Office announced the results of its 2013 graduation-ceremony volunteer recruitment on June 21. The recruitment opened to students across the university on June 8 and closed on June 13, receiving 469 applications. A total of 55 volunteers and eight alternates were selected.

Volunteers must participate throughout the graduation rehearsal beginning on the afternoon of June 27 and the graduation ceremony on the morning of June 28. Volunteer training will be held at 20:30 on June 24 in Room D301.

Student names, rosters, and personal contact information are not reproduced here. The selection results and detailed duties are subject to the Student Affairs Office notice.
