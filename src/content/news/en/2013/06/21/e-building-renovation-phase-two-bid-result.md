It is reported that STU's Resource Management Office announced the bidding results for the construction contractor of Phase II of the E-Building renovation project on June 21, 2013. Following the review and recommendation of the STU bidding leadership group, Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. was named the winning bidder at RMB 3,287,467.45.

## Award Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | STU E-Building Renovation Project, Phase II |
| Winning bidder | Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Bid amount | RMB 3,287,467.45 |
| Public notice period | June 21 to June 23, 2013 |
| Tenderer | STU Infrastructure Office |

## Objections and Complaints

Objections to the bidding results during the public notice period may be raised with the STU Infrastructure Office. The contacts are Yao Yijia and Lin Yuqi; the telephone number is 0754-86502490 and the fax number is 0754-86502314.

Complaints are handled by the STU Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346, with fax service available at 0754-86503663.
