It is reported that Shantou University Law School announced the results of its recruitment of volunteer advisors for the 2013 incoming class on June 21. Following preliminary screening, interviews, and other stages, the school selected 40 students to serve as freshman advisors.

The volunteer advisors will support orientation and related services for the 2013 incoming class, helping new students become familiar with the university's academic and campus environment. The final roster and subsequent arrangements are subject to the Law School notice.

Student names and the roster are not reproduced here. Please consult the original notice in the OA system for the complete selection results and related attachments.
