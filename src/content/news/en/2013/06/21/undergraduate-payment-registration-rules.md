It is reported that Shantou University issued the "Shantou University Undergraduate Payment and Registration Management Measures" on June 21, 2013. The document, numbered STU Issue [2013] No. 82, was approved at the university's 8th leadership meeting of 2013 on June 18 and provides an institutional basis for undergraduate payment and registration procedures.

The measures take effect on September 15, 2013. The "Shantou University Student Payment and Registration Management Measures (Trial)" (STU Issue [2009] No. 54) is repealed at the same time.
