It is reported that Shantou University Engineering School shared internship recruitment information on June 21 for Yiminute Technology Co., Ltd. in Shantou. The company was seeking summer interns in areas including mobile app development, project management and UI/UX design, with the internships intended to give students practical project experience and help strengthen their professional skills and overall capabilities.

## Recruitment Information

The opportunities were open to Shantou University students. Specific positions, requirements, internship dates, locations and application procedures are subject to the attachments to the Engineering School's OA notice. Students should consult the attachments for the position details and application requirements.

The position and application details in the original attachments are not reproduced here.
