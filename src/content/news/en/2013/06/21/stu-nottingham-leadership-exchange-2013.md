It is reported that Shantou University will continue its cooperation with the University of Nottingham Ningbo China on the third student leadership exchange program. The program is scheduled for late August to mid-September 2013. Each university will send 10 student representatives to the other campus for an exchange of about three weeks, where they will help prepare and conduct new-student welcome activities and learn about student life and work at the partner university.

## Program Arrangements

The exchange is expected to last three weeks. Each university will cover transportation for its own students traveling to the partner campus and provide visiting students with free dormitory accommodation. Students will be responsible for their own living expenses during the exchange.

The selection is open to students who have worked or previously worked in a student organization for at least one year, maintain good academic performance, and have strong English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. Candidates will go through applications, unit recommendations, preliminary material review, exchange-project design, and interviews. Places will be allocated among university-level student organizations, relevant student societies, schools and residential colleges, and students who have completed the FLY Training Program's "Spread Your Wings" plan. Recommending units should not exceed their allocated quotas, and 10 representatives will be selected in total.

## Selection Schedule

Applicants must submit their materials to the Youth League Committee of their school or residential college, or to the head of their university-level student organization, by 10:00 a.m. on June 26, 2013. Recommending units must submit their recommended lists and application forms to the University Youth League Committee Office by 4:00 p.m. that day. Electronic documents should be sent to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`.

Two rounds of interviews are tentatively scheduled for the morning of June 29 and the afternoon of June 30. The sessions will include personal presentations, exchange-plan presentations, an English test, and panel questions. The review panel will make the final selection of 10 student representatives.
