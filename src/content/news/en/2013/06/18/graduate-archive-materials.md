Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has arranged the collection of archive materials for the 2013 graduating class. As graduate archives are due to be dispatched, relevant university units must organize and submit materials that need to be included in the archives.

The materials must be delivered by **June 28** to Room 106 of the New Administration Center, where the Graduate Employment Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office is located, for centralized filing.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
