Shantou University's Department of Social Sciences has announced arrangements for a 2013 summer social survey. The activity is intended for undergraduate students from the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts who will take Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics in the fall 2013 semester.

With “My Hometown, My Home” as its theme, the survey allows students to choose their own topics based on their local experiences and produce a report of approximately 3,000 Chinese characters. The assignment forms part of the course's social-practice component, and the report will count toward students' overall course grades as regular assessment.

Students must submit their reports to their course instructors by the end of October 2013. The report format should follow the template titled “2013 Summer Social Survey Report of Shantou University's Department of Social Sciences.” The department said the activity is designed to encourage students to connect theory with practice, understand society and complete the practical requirements of the course.
