It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University issued a public notice on June 18 proposing to recognize nine outstanding graduates from the Class of 2013. The proposed list was prepared under the school's criteria for outstanding graduates after applications from graduating students, review by the school Youth League Committee, and deliberation by the school.

The assessment criteria covered the following areas:

- Well-rounded development in moral character, academic performance, and physical education, with no repeated courses;
- Receipt of an Outstanding Student Scholarship at or above the third-prize level in previous years, including either at least one first prize or at least two second prizes;
- An excellent grade for the graduation thesis;
- Service as a student leader at the school level or above, or participation in volunteer and public-interest activities.

The public notice will remain open until June 21. Comments may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Business School Party General Branch or Youth League Committee.
