It is reported that the bid result for the renovation of selected Type A and Type B staff dormitories at Shantou University was announced on June 18. Following the recommendation of the university's Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Co., Ltd. was selected as the contractor, with a contract value of RMB 296,511.

The result will be publicized from June 18 to June 20. The project procuring entity is the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University. Inquiries may be directed to Yao Yijia or Lin Yuqi at (0754) 86502490. Complaints may be submitted to the university's Supervision, Audit and Inspection Office at (0754) 86502346.
