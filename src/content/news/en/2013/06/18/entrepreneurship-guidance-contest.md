Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office and Student Affairs Office recently forwarded the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education's notice on the First Guangdong Collegiate Contest on Entrepreneurship Guidance Course Teaching and Student Entrepreneurship Training. The university is coordinating registration for teachers who are interested in participating.

The contest focuses on the teaching of entrepreneurship guidance courses and practical training for student entrepreneurship. Relevant university offices have asked academic units to circulate the information and encourage interested teachers to complete registration as required.

Interested teachers must register by June 30, 2013, through the competition homepage on the College Graduate Employment Online platform, entering the National College Student Entrepreneurship Service Network for unified registration. After registering, participants should contact the Student Entrepreneurship Center.

For registration inquiries, contact Chen Zepeng at 86502256 or zpchen@stu.edu.cn.
