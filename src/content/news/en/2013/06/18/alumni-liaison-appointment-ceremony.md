Shantou University will hold an appointment ceremony for class alumni liaisons from the 2013 graduating cohort on June 25, marking the formal confirmation stage of the appointment process. The liaisons will help the university maintain contact with alumni from their graduating classes.

The ceremony is scheduled for 10 to 11:30 a.m. in Conference Room 330 on the third floor of the New Administrative Center. Alumni-affairs officials responsible for the relevant schools, staff from their alumni offices and the 2013 class alumni liaisons will attend.

The university previously launched the appointment process for class alumni liaisons from the 2013 graduating cohort to strengthen long-term communication among graduates, the university and their class alumni.
