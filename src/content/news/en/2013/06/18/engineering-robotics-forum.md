Shantou University's School of Engineering will host its 159th Engineering Research Forum at 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, June 19. The forum will focus on “Trends in Robotics.”

Dr. Jiang Qimi of the Canadian company MTI will deliver the presentation. The event will take place in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room. It is primarily intended for graduate students in the Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering and relevant faculty members, while other interested faculty and students are also welcome to attend.
