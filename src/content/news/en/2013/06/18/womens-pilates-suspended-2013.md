It is reported that the Trade Union issued a notice on June 18, 2013, that due to the need to set up the venue for the commencement ceremony, the gymnastics room of the Gymnasium will be temporarily closed, and the women’s faculty Pilates training class for this semester has been suspended from June 12.

Participants are asked to inform each other.
