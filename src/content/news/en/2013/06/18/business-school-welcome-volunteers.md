It is reported that the STU Business School began recruiting student volunteers on June 18 to support registration for the 2013 freshman class. The school plans to select 50 volunteers.

The volunteers will assist with collecting files, checking information, entering student records and guiding students on the day of freshman registration. The recruitment is open to all Business School students. Applicants should be careful and responsible, follow work arrangements, and may not be extracurricular counselors for the 2013 freshman class. They must also be able to return to campus by August 30, 2013. Applicants with previous experience in welcome activities will receive priority.

Applications close at 8:00 p.m. on June 24. The school will make selections based on the number of applications and operational needs. The application form and detailed submission instructions are available in the original attachment on the OA system.
