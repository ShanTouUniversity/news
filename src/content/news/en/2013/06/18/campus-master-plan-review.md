Shantou University will hold a briefing and consultation meeting on the preliminary revision plan for its campus master plan on the afternoon of June 20. University leaders, representatives of university units, and the head of the Project Coordination Office have been invited to attend and offer comments and suggestions on the preliminary plan.

According to the notice, the university launched the revision of its campus master plan in line with relevant guidance from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on strengthening capital construction management at provincial universities and in response to the university's development needs. Following a tender, the project was assigned to the Shantou Urban Planning and Design Institute. Since late February 2013, the institute's specialists have conducted on-site surveys and research at the university, while holding several discussions with relevant university departments, the Project Coordination Office, and the Office of Resource Management. The institute has now completed and submitted the preliminary revision plan.

The meeting will begin at 2:30 p.m. on Thursday, June 20, in Conference Room 330 of the Administration Center. The Shantou Urban Planning and Design Institute will introduce the planning background, followed by a presentation of the preliminary revision plan for Shantou University's master plan. The agenda will then include discussion and comments from university units, remarks from consultants and experts, and a concluding statement from university leaders.
