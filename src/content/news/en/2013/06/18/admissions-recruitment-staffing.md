Shantou University's Admissions Office has begun arranging recommendations for staff for the 2013 national college entrance examination admissions process. The admissions work is expected to begin in early July and conclude in late July, with staff selected from relevant university units.

The recommendation quotas are assigned by unit. The Discipline Inspection and Audit Office, the College of Liberal Arts, College of Science, College of Engineering, College of Law, Business School, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Medical College should each recommend one person. The Academic Affairs Office and Network and Information Center should each recommend two people. The Resource Management Office (Security Office) should assign one leader to oversee security, utilities, and other logistical support during the admissions period.

Recommended staff should act impartially, work conscientiously, maintain an upright conduct, and observe relevant discipline. They must be familiar with computer operations, have no immediate family member taking the national college entrance examination that year, and be able to participate throughout the period of new-student admissions. Relevant units should complete the recommendation list and submit it to the university Admissions Office by **June 21**.
