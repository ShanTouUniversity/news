The Joint Christian Studies Center of Shantou University and The Chinese University of Hong Kong, together with the Christian Studies Center of STU's School of Liberal Arts, will host the 2013 International Academic Roundtable on “Religious Charity in Chaoshan” from **June 20 to 21**. The event will take place in Meeting Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC) at Shantou University and is open to faculty members and students.

### Focus on religious charity traditions

The roundtable will focus on the history and current development of charitable work associated with Buddhism, Taoism, Catholicism and Christianity in Chaoshan. Participants will also discuss the interaction between religious charity and Chaoshan society and culture. Research on religious charity in other regions will be included for comparison and to bring a broader perspective to the field.
