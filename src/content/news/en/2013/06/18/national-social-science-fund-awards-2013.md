It is reported that the results of the 2013 National Social Science Fund projects, excluding separately listed programs such as arts, were recently announced. Two general projects from Shantou University were approved, and both project leaders are from the Department of Social Sciences.

Shantou University submitted 27 applications for this category of projects this year, including 18 general-project applications and nine youth-project applications. The approved projects are as follows:

| Project | Project Leader | Category | Unit | Approval Number |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| A Theory of Practice and Time-Space | Yang Mu | General Project | Department of Social Sciences | 13BZX007 |
| Pathways to Enhancing Local Government Credibility amid New Urbanization | Shen Haijun | General Project | Department of Social Sciences | 13BZZ042 |

The university's Research Office called on the project leaders to organize and carry out their research carefully and ensure high-quality completion. It also encouraged faculty members across the university to continue applying for research programs, strengthen the originality and argumentation of their proposals, and produce more high-level research results.
