Shantou University Human Resources published an obituary notice on **June 11, 2013**, together with arrangements for flower wreaths and transportation. The notice did not provide specific information about the deceased's identity.

## Flower-Wreath Arrangements

Units and individuals wishing to send a flower wreath were asked to contact the Supervision and Audit Office by 4:00 p.m. on June 12 at **86502346**. The notice also included contact information for the deceased's relatives; that personal contact information is omitted here for privacy.

## Transportation Schedule

Transportation was scheduled for June 13 from the following locations:

| Boarding location | Time |
| --- | --- |
| In front of the reception room on campus | 8:00 a.m. |
| Anju entrance | 8:10 a.m. |
| Fourth Hospital in Dajiao | 8:20 a.m. |
| Baiheyuan campus bus stop | 8:30 a.m. |

The arrangements were issued jointly by the Organization and United Front Work Department, Human Resources, the University Labor Union and the Supervision and Audit Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
