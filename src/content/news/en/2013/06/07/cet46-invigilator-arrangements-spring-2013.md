The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on invigilation arrangements for the **first-half 2013 National College English Tests (CET-4 and CET-6)**. The exams would be held on **Saturday, June 15**: CET-4 would run from **9:00 to 11:20**, and CET-6 from **15:00 to 17:20**.

## Invigilation Arrangements

Before June 15, invigilators must familiarize themselves with the invigilator rules, candidate rules, the CET-4 and CET-6 invigilation procedures and examination-room record forms, the procedures for handling unexpected incidents, and the classification of candidate violations and cheating. Training would be conducted before the exams on the basis of these materials.

| Exam | Assembly for training and paper collection | Post-exam paper handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CET-4 | 7:45 on June 15 at E Staircase Room 101 | Return the papers to E Staircase Room 101 for counting |
| CET-6 | 13:45 on June 15 at E Staircase Room 101 | Return the papers to E Staircase Room 101 for counting |

As required by the Guangdong Provincial Education Examination Authority, invigilation sessions and specific examination-room assignments for the unified exams would be announced on the day of the examination. Invigilators must undergo security screening before entering the examination rooms and must not improperly carry or use communication, camera, or other electronic equipment at the examination site. The examination office would be located in **E Staircase Room 101**. Examination contacts are **2402** and **2623**.

The notice also included an invigilator list as an attachment. Because it contains personal names, the list and personal information in examination-work forms are not reproduced here.
