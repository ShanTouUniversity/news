On **June 7**, the Shantou University Discipline Inspection Office forwarded the Guangdong Education Discipline Inspection Working Committee&apos;s call for **micro-drama, micro-animation, micro-fiction and reportage** (Yue Jiao Ji Gong [2013] No. 37) and invited faculty, staff and students to take part in the integrity-themed submission. The activity is organized by the Publicity and Education Office of the Guangdong Commission for Discipline Inspection and the *Guangdong Party Style* magazine to promote integrity culture and a clean social environment.

## Schedule

| Stage | Date or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Provincial submission period | May 1 to July 31, 2013 |
| STU internal submission | **By July 13, 2013**; the university will submit works collectively |
| Provincial collection deadline | University discipline-inspection offices are asked to submit collected works to the provincial education discipline-inspection office by **July 15** |
| Preliminary review | August 1–15, 2013 |
| Final review | August 15–30, 2013 |
| Publication of results | *Guangdong Party Style* and the Guangdong Discipline Inspection and Supervision Network, also known as Nanyue Qingfeng |

The original notice says the activity is generally conducted within Guangdong and primarily targets Party members and cadres and public servants, while also welcoming members of the public. Units, organizations and enterprises may recommend works, but the same work must not be submitted more than once.

## Categories and Requirements

The call covers five categories: micro-drama scripts, micro-drama video works, micro-animation, micro-fiction and reportage. Entrants may choose their own titles. Works should focus on anti-corruption and integrity-building, with a clear perspective and strong appeal. The notice sets out these requirements:

- Works must be original and independently created by the entrant. Participants must observe academic ethics and standards and must not plagiarize. Content must be healthy and must not violate Chinese laws and regulations.
- Micro-drama scripts, micro-fiction and reportage must be submitted as A4 Word documents or printed copies. The original format requirements specify SimSun, small-four font, single line spacing, 31.7 mm left and right margins, and 25.4 mm top and bottom margins. Entrants were asked to add their name, contact details and correspondence address at the end; those personal fields are not reproduced here.
- Micro-drama video works and micro-animation must be submitted on a CD. Files should be named “author name + work title,” use uncompressed AVI format, and run for 15, 30 or 60 seconds. The first and last five seconds should contain a black screen or color bars, and the resolution must be at least 720 × 576 pixels.
- Micro-fiction is limited to 800 Chinese characters, and reportage to 2,500 Chinese characters. Works will not be returned, so contributors should retain their own backups.
- Copyright remains with the author. The organizers may display, exhibit, compile or publish winning works, or use them in arts-education-related activities; the original notice governs the detailed rights arrangement.

## Awards

Micro-drama, micro-animation, micro-fiction and reportage will each have their own awards, with micro-drama divided into separate script and video awards. Each category offers three first prizes, five second prizes and ten third prizes, plus several excellence awards. Winners will receive certificates, and selected works may be recommended or published through relevant media.

STU submissions should reach Room 422 of the New Administration Center **by July 13**. The university&apos;s official submission email is <o_jj@stu.edu.cn>, and the telephone number is `86502346`.
