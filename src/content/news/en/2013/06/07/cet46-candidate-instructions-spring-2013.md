The first-half 2013 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 examinations will be held on **Saturday, June 15**. The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office reminds candidates to study the Guangdong CET examination rules, candidate requirements and classification of violations, and to prepare the required documents, headphones and stationery.

## Examination Times and Office

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Band 4 examination | June 15, 9:00–11:20 a.m. |
| Band 6 examination | June 15, 3:00–5:20 p.m. |
| Band 4 entry | Entry at 8:45 a.m.; no admission after 9:00 a.m. |
| Band 6 entry | Entry at 2:45 p.m.; no admission after 3:00 p.m. |
| Examination office | E Staircase Lecture Room 101, teaching building E |
| Examination contacts | 2402 and 2623 |

After entering the examination room, candidates should promptly test their headphones and confirm reception of the university's listening signal at **FM73.2**. Candidates arriving after the examination begins may not enter, and candidates may not answer early, leave early or submit their papers early.

Before the examination formally begins, candidates must complete their admission-permit number, name and other required information on Answer Sheets 1 and 2, and correctly write their admission-permit number and name on the back of the test booklet. The barcode strip on the back of the test booklet must be removed and attached to the barcode box in the upper-left corner of Answer Sheet 1. Incorrect personal information, or a wrongly placed or missing barcode, will be handled as an examination violation.

The examination continued to use the "multiple test papers" format: different versions were used in the same examination room, with differences in difficulty balanced through score equating. A candidate's reported score would therefore not be affected by the version taken. Candidates could begin answering only after the formal start of the examination. The writing prompt for the first section was printed on the back of the test booklet and was to be answered in the designated area of Answer Sheet 1. Questions for the other sections were inside the booklet, which candidates had to open at the invigilator's instruction and at the designated time; otherwise, the conduct would be handled as a violation.

The archived version dates the notice under the National College English Test Committee Office to **June 3, 2016**, although Shantou University published it on **June 8, 2013**, and its text concerns the June 2013 examinations. This date discrepancy is retained here as it appears in the source.

## Documents, Supplies and Security Checks

Candidates must bring their **admission permit, student card and valid identity document**. The documents must be placed in the upper-left corner of the desk for inspection. Under the requirements of the Guangdong examination authority, a campus one-card cannot replace a student card. Candidates who have lost a required document must follow the notice's procedure and may sit the examination with proof signed by an academic administrator and stamped by their school or department.

All candidates must undergo security checks before entering. Candidates should prepare headphones, batteries, a 2B pencil, a black ink pen and an eraser. Required information on the answer sheets and test booklet must be completed as instructed, and the barcode on the back of the test booklet must be removed and attached to the designated area on Answer Sheet 1.

Books, paper, bags, recording equipment, electronic organizers and communication devices may not be brought into the examination room. Communication devices include mobile phones, MP3 players and walkie-talkies. Items already brought in must be handed to invigilators for safekeeping; failure to follow this requirement will be handled as an examination violation or cheating. Candidates may not borrow stationery from one another in the examination room.

## Examination Rules

The Guangdong CET candidate rules require candidates to sit in their assigned seats, begin answering only after the start signal, use a black ink pen and 2B pencil as required, and write or mark answers only in the designated areas. Missing, incorrect or illegible information on an answer sheet may make the sheet invalid. Test booklets, answer sheets and scratch paper may not be taken out of the examination room.

Candidates must remain quiet, may not smoke, make noise or remain outside the examination room, and must follow the instructions of examination staff. The violation classification also covers answering at the wrong time, continuing to wear headphones after the listening section, looking at another candidate's work or exchanging signals, exchanging items or test papers, using communication or other electronic devices, carrying materials related to the examination, copying, impersonation, forged documents and disrupting examination operations. Such conduct will be handled under Guangdong CET rules and relevant university regulations.
