According to a Dragon Boat Festival service notice issued by the Shantou University Network and Information Center on **June 7, 2013**, the campus-card service windows at the **Expert Apartments** and **beneath the No. 2 Dining Hall** would close from **June 10 to 12** and resume service on **June 13**.

During the closure, faculty members and students may use the integrated deposit-transfer and inquiry machines for self-service top-ups. Card-loss reports and balance inquiries are available through the voice self-service line at **82903414**, or at campus extension **3414**.

For other campus-card service questions during the holiday, users may call the service hotline **86503420** or email [`support@stu.edu.cn`](mailto:support@stu.edu.cn).

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
