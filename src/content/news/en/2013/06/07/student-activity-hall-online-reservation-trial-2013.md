To improve management and services at the New Library Student Activity Hall, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office, with support from the Network Center, began trialing an online reservation system for relevant units and student organizations.

## Reservation Process

Applicants can log in to **MySTU > STU Reservation Center (`booking.stu.edu.cn`) > Campus Venue Reservations > My Transactions > Reserve a Venue** and submit the required information. Once a reservation is approved, it will appear under “My Venue Reservations.” The applicant must print the *Library Student Activity Hall Reservation Form*, obtain the signature and official seal of the responsible person of the organizing unit, and submit it to the Student Activity Hall within **two days** after approval.

Reservations must be made at least **five working days** in advance.

## Fees and Opening Hours

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Private booking | RMB 150 per hour; RMB 100 per hour for time beyond the reservation |
| Monday–Friday | 3:00–10:00 p.m. |
| Saturday–Sunday | 10:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m. |

Questions and suggestions may be sent to the Student Activity Hall's official email address, <StudentLounge@stu.edu.cn>. The original notice also listed personal contact details for a student management-team leader, which are withheld here for privacy.
