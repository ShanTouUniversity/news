According to a notice issued by the Shantou University Sports Department on **June 7, 2013**, the university's sports facilities would close during the Dragon Boat Festival holiday because of the holiday schedule and needs related to Open Day activities.

The closure will run from **June 10 to 12** and will cover **indoor sports facilities, the tennis courts, and the gym**. Faculty and students should adjust their exercise plans accordingly; the reopening time was to be announced by the Sports Department separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
