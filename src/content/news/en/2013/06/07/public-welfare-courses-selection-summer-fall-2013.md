The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has released course-selection arrangements for public-welfare courses in the summer and fall semesters of the 2013–2014 academic year. Beginning with the 2012 undergraduate cohort, public-welfare courses became required general-education courses. The two semesters will offer **19 courses** and **758 places**, with most courses scheduled in July or on weekends during the fall semester.

## Selection Schedule and Rules

| Stage | Time | Participants or notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial selection | **9:00 a.m. June 8 to 5:00 p.m. June 9** | Students who have not previously taken a public-welfare course |
| Add-drop selection | **9:00 a.m. June 13 to 5:00 p.m. June 14** | All undergraduates; students may add or drop a course |

Selection follows a first-come, first-served principle until places are filled. Each student may select one course. Students already admitted through a course interview should not take part in this selection round. No selection or selection-change requests will be accepted after the add-drop period. Students should confirm that they can attend on schedule before selecting a course; under the notice&apos;s unified assessment rules, a student who selects a course but does not participate normally will receive a score of zero.

The courses combine service learning, training, public-welfare practice, reflection sharing and assessment, and are taught by university instructors who have completed the relevant training. Specific student requirements, implementation dates and assessment methods vary by course and follow each course syllabus.

## How to Select

Students should log in to the credit-based system and open **Guided Courses > Select a Topic**. They should search for the **2013–2014 summer and fall semesters**, then choose an available topic under “All Teaching Units” and “Public-Welfare Course.” Public-welfare course codes begin with `SER`; students must choose from the topics listed in the system and may not create their own topics.

Course information is also available through the Public-Welfare Course website at <http://gykc.stu.edu.cn>. For questions about selection, students may contact the Academic Affairs Office at `86502416`.
