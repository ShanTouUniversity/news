It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs announced on June 7 the list of graduates of the Class of 2013 approved for deferred employment.

The notice is addressed to all graduates, with the detailed list provided in the attachment. Graduates whose applications for deferred employment have been approved should watch for follow-up notices on the OA system regarding signing the deferral agreement and visit the Career Guidance Center of the Office of Student Affairs (Room 106, Xinzheng Building) to complete the relevant formalities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Student Affairs)
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
