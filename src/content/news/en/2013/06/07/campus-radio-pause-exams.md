It is reported that with the final review and examination period underway, the campus radio station of Shantou University has adjusted its daily schedule: from June 8, noon and afternoon programs will be suspended until the new semester begins.

The suspension was announced by the university's News Center on June 7. Units with special broadcasting needs may contact the station (contact: Ms./Mr. Liu, extension 2203, o_gbds@stu.edu.cn).

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Scope | Noon and afternoon programs |
| Start of suspension | June 8 |
| Resumption | After the new semester opens |
| Announcing department | News Center |

Suspending some radio programs during the exam season is a common practice among universities, aimed at creating a quiet environment for students preparing for their examinations.

> Source: STU OA Notice (News Center)
