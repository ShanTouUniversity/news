According to a document issued by the Shantou University Degree Evaluation Committee, the second meeting of the eighth committee approved the Supplementary Provisions for the Rules on Bachelor Degree Conferral in Adult Higher Education on June 19, 2013. The committee issued the document on June 25 to inform university units of the provisions and requested that they be implemented accordingly.

The document is numbered STU Degree Evaluation Committee Document No. 5 (2013) and concerns institutional arrangements for bachelor degree work in adult higher education. The full supplementary provisions and implementation details are available in the original attachment on the Shantou University OA system.
