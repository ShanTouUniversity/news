It is reported that STU's 2013 summer vacation will run from June 30 to August 17. The Student Affairs Office, Graduate School, Veritas College, and Resources Management Office have jointly released arrangements for students who need to stay on campus during the break, with online applications required within the designated period.

## Stay Application

Students who need to remain on campus for valid reasons such as military training, Medical College classes, academic research, public service activities, or exam preparation should log in to the Student Dormitory Management System (http://dorm.stu.edu.cn/) with their campus network account between June 25 and 15:00 on July 2, 2013, and fill in the stay period, contact number, reason, and remarks. All students who intend to stay during the vacation must apply within this window.

## Utilities

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Billing | Utilities during the vacation are borne by approved stay applicants; no subsidy applies |
| Billing periods | June 30–July 16 and July 17–August 15; staying within one period is charged for that period, spanning both periods is charged for the entire vacation |
| Cost sharing | Roommates with differing usage should negotiate sharing and payment among themselves |
| Power cut | Dormitories without approved applications will have power cut on July 2, with supply restored on August 16 for the new semester |
| Early departure | Students who leave early after approval and leave the room empty should register for power cut at the Student Community Management Service Center (opposite the entrance of Building C) |

## Safety and Discipline

Students staying on campus should remain vigilant about personal and property safety, ensuring doors, windows, water, and power are secured when leaving. Any anomalies or emergencies should be reported promptly to the building duty staff or campus security. All stay students must strictly observe the STU Student Dormitory Regulations and cooperate with dormitory administrators. During the vacation, all dormitory areas enforce 24-hour access control: students without approved stay will have access authorization revoked, while approved students must carry their campus card and swipe for entry and exit. Hosting outside visitors overnight is prohibited and will be dealt with seriously upon discovery.
