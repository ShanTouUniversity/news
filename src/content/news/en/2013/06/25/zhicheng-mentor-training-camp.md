It is reported that Zhicheng Residential College of Shantou University will hold its sixth mentor quality-development training camp over five days, from July 1 to 5, 2013. The camp supports the university's goals of cultivating internationally minded, well-rounded graduates and strengthening mentors' overall competence.

## Camp Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Sixth Mentor Quality-Development Training Camp |
| Dates | July 1–5, 2013 (five days) |
| Participants | Incoming mentors for 2013–2014 (alternates included) |
| Assembly point | Lecture room J-354 |

According to the college, the camp focuses on developing participants' personal, communication, teamwork and leadership abilities so they can carry out their mentoring duties effectively.

All incoming mentors and alternates are asked to prepare accordingly and gather at lecture room J-354 by 7:45 a.m. on July 1 for the opening ceremony and subsequent course sessions.
