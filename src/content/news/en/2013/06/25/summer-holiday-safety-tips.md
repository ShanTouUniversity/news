The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued safety tips for the 2013 summer holiday, reminding students to take appropriate precautions according to their plans to remain on campus or leave for home.

## Staying on or Leaving Campus

Students staying on campus should follow residence hall and campus safety rules. They should not accommodate people from outside the university without authorization, install unauthorized wiring or use high-power electrical appliances in residence halls, and should keep cash, identification documents and other valuables secure. Before leaving a room, students should lock the doors and windows and switch off all indoor power. Students staying on campus must not take open flames into the hills or barbecue, and all campus recreational activities should be conducted with safety as the priority. Any unusual situation should be reported to the university promptly.

When traveling home, students are advised to travel with companions where possible and stay in contact with family members or friends. They should choose transportation with proper operating qualifications and safety coverage, and avoid unlicensed vehicles. Before traveling, students should check the weather at their destination, choose safe routes and activity areas, and avoid remote areas or locations officially designated as dangerous. They should not readily disclose their identity, address, telephone number, itinerary or property details to strangers, or accept food and drinks from them.

## Outdoor and Traffic Safety

During thunderstorms, students should promptly move to places equipped with lightning protection and avoid sheltering under isolated utility poles, eaves, large trees or chimneys. They should also stay away from metal objects. Before swimming, students should warm up and prepare necessary safety equipment, and should not swim or fish in reservoirs, lakes, beaches or other waters without adequate safety protection. The Student Affairs Office also reminds students to guard against theft, robbery and fraud in public places, follow traffic regulations, and avoid drunk driving, fatigued driving, unlicensed driving and speeding.

## Part-time Work and Emergency Contacts

Students seeking part-time work during the summer should verify recruitment information and be alert to offers that require training fees or deposits. They should guard against pyramid schemes and fraud conducted under the guise of recruitment.

Students staying on campus who encounter an emergency may contact the Student Community Management and Service Center at 86502661 or the campus police and emergency assistance line at 82904110.
