It is reported that Shantou University will implement temporary traffic restrictions in parts of campus during the Board of Trustees meeting, following arrangements by the municipal public security authorities.

## Restricted Period and Area

The restrictions will run from midnight on June 27, 2013, through 6:00 p.m. on June 28. The restricted area covers the vicinity of the Changjiang College of Arts and Design and the Academic Exchange Center. The Security Office will set up a checkpoint beside Building P.

## Vehicle Access and Parking

During the restricted period, vehicles other than specially authorized vehicles will not be permitted to enter the area. Affected vehicles may park in front of Building P. Motor vehicles will also be prohibited from parking on the pedestrian street and at both of its entrances.

Members of the campus community are advised to follow on-site instructions and plan their routes and parking arrangements in advance.
