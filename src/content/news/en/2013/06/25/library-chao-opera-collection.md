Shantou University Library has presented a feature on Chao opera, introducing the traditional local genre's history, artistic characteristics, and cultural significance. Also known as Chaozhou opera or Chaozhou dialect opera, Chao opera is performed in the Chaozhou dialect and was included in the first batch of China's national intangible cultural heritage list in 2006.

Chao opera developed from Nanxi opera of the Song and Yuan periods and has a history spanning several centuries. It is mainly performed and circulated in eastern Guangdong, southern Fujian, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Southeast Asia. Its vocal style, performance techniques, and musical forms reflect distinctive regional characteristics. Through the movement of Chaozhou communities, the genre has also reached regions around the world and become an important representative of Chaoshan culture.
