It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University has announced the first-class arrangements for each group of the Xiangyue Lantian Creative Research and Practice Service public-service course in the 2013–2014 summer term.

## Course Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course code | SER0410 |
| Course | Xiangyue Lantian Creative Research and Practice Service |
| Term | 2013–2014 summer term |
| Issuing unit | Shantou University Law School |
| Arrangement | First class time and location for each group |

Students enrolled in the course should attend the first session according to their group and the published arrangements. The specific time and location for each group are subject to the details in the Law School's OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School)
