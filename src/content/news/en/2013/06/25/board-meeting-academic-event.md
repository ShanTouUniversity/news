Shantou University is scheduled to hold an open academic event during the first meeting of its ninth Board of Trustees. The event will be open to faculty members and students, providing an opportunity for academic exchange on campus. The detailed agenda and arrangements will be subject to information released by the organizer.

## Event Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Academic event during the first meeting of STU's ninth Board of Trustees |
| Participants | Faculty members and students of STU |
| Format | Open academic event |
| Detailed arrangements | Subject to the OA attachment |

The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation issued the notice. Please refer to the attachment in the OA system for the event time, venue, agenda, and other details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
>
> ⚠️ The detailed arrangements and related attachment are available on the OA system.
