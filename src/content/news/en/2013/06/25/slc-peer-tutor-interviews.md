The Student Learning Center (SLC) under Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has completed its review of applications for peer tutor positions in the university adaptation tutoring class. According to the center, a total of 31 applicants were shortlisted based on registrations received through June 24 and the center's actual staffing needs.

Interviews are being held from June 25 to 28 (Tuesday through Friday) at the SLC, divided into several sessions:

| Day | Time | Candidates |
|-----|------|------------|
| Tuesday | 3:00–4:00 PM | 3 |
| Tuesday | 4:00–5:30 PM | 6 |
| Tuesday | 7:30–8:30 PM | 3 |
| Wednesday | 3:00–4:30 PM | 7 |
| Wednesday | 4:30–5:30 PM | 4 |
| Wednesday | 7:30–8:30 PM | 3 |
| Wednesday | 8:30–9:30 PM | 5 |

Each shortlisted student has been notified individually of their assigned session. Applicants are reminded to arrive on time; questions may be directed to the SLC (tel: 86502250, email: slc@stu.edu.cn).
