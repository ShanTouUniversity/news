Shantou University Law School has announced a student exchange opportunity with the School of Law at Queen's University Belfast (QUB). The programme will send two students to QUB for one semester during the 2013-2014 academic year. Students from the Law and Public Administration departments are eligible to apply.

## Programme Details

Selected students may study at QUB during either the first or second semester of the 2013-2014 academic year. The teaching and assessment period for the first semester is 30 September 2013 to 28 January 2014, while the second-semester period runs from 3 February to 7 June 2014. QUB's School of Law will assist in arranging a suitable programme of study according to each student's circumstances.

QUB is located in Northern Ireland. Its School of Law was ranked eighth in the United Kingdom in the Guardian University Guide published on 4 June 2013.

## Eligibility and Application

Applicants are preferably expected to have a GPA of 3.5 or above and meet one of the stated English-language benchmarks: a CET-6 score above 500, an IELTS score of 6.5, or a TOEFL score of 90. The Law School will assess application materials and interview performance. Recommended candidates must then obtain QUB's approval for one-semester study.

Electronic applications were due by 5 p.m. on 26 June, and shortlisted students were scheduled to attend interviews on the morning of 27 June. Nominated students would subsequently submit QUB application materials, a formal reference and an STU transcript, and complete UK visa and other required procedures after admission.

## Costs and Accommodation

Students would continue to pay their STU tuition and cover travel, accommodation and living expenses, but would not pay tuition fees for their studies at QUB. The information provided by the university estimated personal living expenses at approximately £3,750 per semester in Northern Ireland, excluding travel, although actual costs would vary. Students were responsible for arranging accommodation and could also consult QUB's accommodation information.

Interested students could contact Ms Yao Jinjin at the Law School for further details. The application form and programme attachments are available in the original notice on the STU OA system.
