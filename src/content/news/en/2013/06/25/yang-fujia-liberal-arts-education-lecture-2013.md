It is reported that Prof. Yang Fujia, Trustee of Shantou University, will visit the university on the evening of June 27 to deliver a lecture titled "Liberal Arts Education: Significance and Challenges", discussing the value of liberal arts education and the difficulties in putting it into practice.

The lecture is jointly organized by the Institute of Higher Education and the Academic Affairs Office, and is open to all students and faculty members.

## About the Speaker

Yang Fujia is a renowned nuclear physicist and educator. He is Professor of Physics at Fudan University and was elected Academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences in 1991. Born in Shanghai in 1936, he graduated from the Department of Physics at Fudan University in 1958 and later conducted research at the Niels Bohr Institute in Copenhagen, Denmark. He became Professor of Physics at Fudan in 1980 and served as Director of the Shanghai Institute of Nuclear Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences, from 1987 to 2001.

In higher education administration, Yang served as President of Fudan University from 1993 to 1999 and was the founding president of the Association of University Presidents of China. He served as Chancellor of the University of Nottingham in the UK from 2000 to 2012, and has been President of the University of Nottingham Ningbo China since 2004. He also serves as a member of the National Education Advisory Committee and the National Education Examination Steering Committee.

As a scholar, Yang has authored textbooks including *Nuclear Physics* and *Atomic Physics*, which have received national teaching achievement awards and Ministry of Education textbook awards. He has been awarded honorary doctorates by universities in China and abroad.
