It is reported that, in line with the university's work schedule for the Class of 2013, Shantou University's Student Community Management Center has issued guidelines for graduation dormitory checkout. Centralized processing runs from June 28 to July 1, 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. daily, and all graduates must complete checkout and leave campus by 5:30 p.m. on July 1.

## Checkout Process at a Glance

| Step | Details |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Pack personal items in advance; take recyclable used goods to designated collection points and dispose of other waste in sorting bins |
| 2 | Call the floor property manager one hour before checkout for inspection of furniture and cleanliness; receive an inspection voucher |
| 3 | Process checkout at the building's desk with the departure form and inspection voucher; return keys and obtain the checkout slip |
| 4 | Stamp completion at the Student Community Management Center (1F, Building C) with the checkout slip and departure form |

In response to the call to build a resource-conserving society and promote a green campus, graduates are asked to sort and pack still-usable items such as bedding, clothing, books and appliances and move them to designated second-hand collection points on each floor before checkout. Beds, wardrobes, drawers and mobile cabinets must be cleared of all belongings before procedures can begin.

Graduates should note that keys must be returned on the spot at checkout; anyone who has lost a key should have it replaced at least one day in advance to avoid delays. After checkout, students may no longer stay in dormitories, borrow keys or store personal belongings — so those not leaving campus the same day are advised not to check out early.

Students unable to check out on time due to extended study periods or similar reasons must submit an extension request at the counselor's office (B106) and may remain housed only after approval. Because of the high volume of students, the university recommends checking out individually rather than in groups. Where non-graduating roommates are away, departing students should lock doors and windows and shut off water and electricity before leaving.
