It is reported that STU's Resources Management Office released the summer vacation hot water schedule for student dormitories on June 25, 2013. To support the study and daily life of students remaining on campus for military training, the university will extend hot water service through July 15, subject to available resources.

## Supply Schedule

| Dates | Arrangement |
|-------|-------------|
| June 29–July 15 | Hot water supplied daily from 18:30 to 19:30 |
| July 16–August 15 | Hot water supply suspended |
| From August 16 | Hot water supply restored |

The schedule applies to hot water service in student dormitories. Implementation is subject to arrangements made by the relevant university offices.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
