It is reported that, in preparation for the arrival of the Class of 2013, Shantou University's Student Community Management and Services Center will organize the cleaning and repair of vacant beds in student dormitories from July 1 to August 15. The work will include beds vacated by graduating students.

## Cleaning and Repair Arrangements

Non-university-provided items on vacant beds or within the area projected by those beds will be removed. Students are asked to place personal belongings, chairs and activity cabinets beneath their assigned beds so that they are not mistakenly cleared from vacant-bed areas.

The relevant vacant beds have been marked and registered. Beds may not be changed without approval. Students who find an incorrect bed marking may call 86502661 to contact the management staff for correction.
