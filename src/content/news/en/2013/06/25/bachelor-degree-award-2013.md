It is reported that the Academic Degree Evaluation Committee of Shantou University issued Document No. 04 [2013] on June 25, 2013, following deliberation at its second meeting of the eighth session on June 19, to confer bachelor’s degrees on qualified undergraduates of the Class of 2013.

A total of 1,538 graduates who met requirements for ethics and physical fitness, completed their programs and earned the required credits were conferred degrees, distributed as follows:

| Category | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Bachelor of Arts (regular) | 374 |
| Bachelor of Laws (regular) | 99 |
| Bachelor of Management (regular) | 183 |
| Bachelor of Science (regular) | 153 |
| Bachelor of Engineering (regular) | 258 |
| Bachelor of Economics (regular) | 84 |
| Bachelor of Medicine (regular) | 256 |
| Bachelor of Arts (adult) | 1 |
| Bachelor of Laws (adult) | 3 |
| Bachelor of Management (adult) | 33 |
| Bachelor of Engineering (adult) | 7 |
| Bachelor of Medicine (adult) | 47 |
| Bachelor of Science (adult) | 40 |
| Double bachelor’s degrees | 2 |

The full list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here for privacy.
