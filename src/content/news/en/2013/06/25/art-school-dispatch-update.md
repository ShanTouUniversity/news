It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs issued an adjustment on June 25 concerning procedures for undergraduate graduates of the Art School to collect dispatch certificates or sign deferred-employment agreements.

The procedures, originally scheduled for June 27 from 14:30 to 17:00, will instead take place that day from 09:00 to 11:30. The location and other requirements remain unchanged, and graduates should complete the procedures during the revised time slot.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Student Affairs)
