It is reported that Shantou University’s Network and Information Center recently announced that the campus cards of 2013 undergraduate and graduate students will be settled and closed on July 4. After settlement, the remaining balances will be refunded to the graduates’ personal bank accounts opened at the Bank of China Shantou University Branch, with refunds expected to be completed by August 23.

## Account Information Confirmation

Graduates whose previously registered Bank of China Shantou University Branch accounts have been closed may log in to the [campus-card closure and refund website](http://smartcardcancel.stu.edu.cn) from June 26 to confirm and provide valid personal bank-account information. The notice requires the account to belong to the graduate and remain valid during the refund period leading up to August 23.

The notice also states that student accounts do not need to be closed before graduation. They will be closed automatically two years after the balance reaches zero. Passbook deposits at the Bank of China Shantou University Branch can be accessed across Guangdong, while bank cards can be used nationwide.

| Matter | Time or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Campus-card settlement and closure | July 4, 2013 |
| Confirmation of alternative bank-account information | From June 26, 2013, through the designated website |
| Balance refunds | Expected by August 23, 2013 |
| Account requirement | Provide a personal bank account and keep it valid until the refund |

Graduates should verify their account information promptly and share the arrangement with classmates. Personal bank-account information is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
