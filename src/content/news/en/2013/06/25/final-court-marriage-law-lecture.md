Shantou University Law School will host a public lecture on the implications of a recent Hong Kong Court of Final Appeal marriage ruling on June 28. Lo Chi-lik, a partner at Woo Kwan Lee & Lo and former president of the Law Society of Hong Kong, will deliver the lecture.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The impact of a recent Hong Kong Court of Final Appeal ruling on the basic principles of Hong Kong marriage law |
| Time | 10:00–11:30 a.m., Friday, June 28, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, Law School |
| Speaker | Lo Chi-lik |
| Audience | All faculty members and students |

The lecture will use W v Registrar of Marriages as its central case. The case concerned whether a person who had undergone gender reassignment surgery could marry legally as a woman. The ruling prompted broad public discussion. Lo Chi-lik will introduce the background of the case and examine its possible implications for the basic principles of Hong Kong marriage law.

Lo Chi-lik is a partner at Woo Kwan Lee & Lo and has served as a council member and president of the Law Society of Hong Kong. He has also held roles with organisations including the Hospital Authority of Hong Kong and the Hong Kong Open University Council. He is qualified to practise law in Hong Kong and several other common-law jurisdictions.
