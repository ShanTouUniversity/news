It is reported that Shantou University has made arrangements for the orientation of its 2013 incoming class. Freshmen will register on **September 1, 2013**, and the university's orientation work will run from September 1 to September 15. During this period, the university will organize enrollment education, campus services and adaptation activities to help new students complete preparations for university life.

## Registration and Reception

The university will establish reception points on September 1 at the exit of Shantou Railway Station, including the coach terminal, the bus stop at the university gate, and Shantou Bus Terminal. Each school will also set up a reception point on the ground floor of the Administration Center to process registration procedures. A green channel and a consultation point for national student loans will be available on site.

Student residences are scheduled to accept new residents from August 31, with the Student Community Management Service Center arranging duty coverage during the orientation period. Freshmen must bring their admission letters, identification documents, archives and household-registration transfer documents as required, and complete accommodation procedures after registration.

## Enrollment Education and Services

The orientation program will include an opening ceremony, an introduction to the university, education on university rules and regulations, credit-system briefings and system-operation guidance, library orientation, physical examinations and guidance on physical exercise. The English placement examination is scheduled for September 3 and 4, with the detailed schedule to be announced separately. Freshmen will also attend briefings on the English Enhancement Program and integrated-thinking training.

The Admissions Office, Academic Affairs Office, student affairs units, Security Office, Resource Management Office, Network and Information Center, Library, University Hospital, schools and other relevant units will handle admissions review, registration and course selection, accommodation, campus safety, network support, medical services and on-site guidance. The university requires units to keep orientation displays simple, regulate campus publicity and unauthorized commercial activity, and carefully verify freshmen's enrollment materials so that registration proceeds in an orderly manner.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

The university also requires orientation staff to wear work badges and provide services according to their assignments. The detailed orientation schedule and related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
