It is reported that the second meeting of the eighth Shantou University Degree Evaluation Committee was held on June 19, 2013, to review doctoral degree applications from graduating graduate students. In accordance with the national degree regulations, STU's degree regulations, and relevant doctoral degree provisions, the committee approved the conferment of doctoral degrees.

## Conferral Summary

| Degree | Recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| Doctor of Science | 8 |
| Doctor of Medicine | 11 |
| Total | 19 |

The decision was issued as STU Degree Evaluation Committee Document No. 2 (2013), dated June 25, 2013. Because the recipients are students, their individual names are not reproduced here. The complete list and original notice are available through the Shantou University OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Degree Evaluation Committee)
