The Resources Management Office of Shantou University announced the student-dormitory utility-bill deduction arrangements on June 25. The Water and Electricity Center completed the relevant dormitory meter readings on June 23, covering the period from June 1 through June 23. Utility charges incurred after June 24 will be collected separately.

The Network and Information Center planned to deduct the charges from campus cards on June 26. Students can log in to the [Shantou University Utility Management System](http://power.stu.edu.cn/) to check their bills using their campus-network accounts. The university reminded students with insufficient campus-card balances to top up promptly. Members of the 2013 graduating cohort were especially advised to make the necessary preparations in advance so that the arrangements would not affect their departure procedures on June 27 and 28.

Students who identify discrepancies in their bills may complete and sign the Utility-Bill Verification Application Form and request a review at the Water and Electricity Center on June 25. Cases in which dormitory occupancy information does not match the student records should be reported to the relevant counselor's office. The application form and other attachments are available in the original OA notice.

| Matter | Time or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Meter reading and billing period | June 1–23, 2013 |
| Utility-bill deduction | June 26, 2013, from campus cards |
| Bill inquiries | Log in to the Shantou University Utility Management System |
| Subsequent charges | Utility use after June 24 will be collected separately |
