According to a document issued by Shantou University's Academic Degrees Committee, the second meeting of the committee's eighth term was held on June 19, 2013, to review relevant master's degree applications. In accordance with China's academic degree regulations, the University's academic degree regulations, and related implementation rules, the committee approved the conferral of master's degrees.

The decision covers 698 recipients: 442 graduating research postgraduate students, 239 professional-degree postgraduate students, 13 applicants with equivalent educational qualifications, and four in-service master's students who are university teachers. By degree category, the recipients include 48 Master of Arts, 17 Master of Education, five Master of Laws, 47 Master of Management, five Master of Economics, 107 Master of Science, 94 Master of Engineering, and 136 Master of Medicine. The decision also covers six Master of Public Administration professional degrees, 11 Master of Business Administration professional degrees, five professional master's degrees in engineering, 12 master's degrees in engineering, 191 professional master's degrees in clinical medicine, and 14 professional master's degrees in public health.

The committee stated that the conferral decision was based on its review of the applications and the University's applicable regulations. The decision spans academic and professional degrees across multiple fields, reflecting the breadth of the University's postgraduate education.
