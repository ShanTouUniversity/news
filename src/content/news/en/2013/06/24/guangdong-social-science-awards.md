The results of the 2010-2011 Guangdong Outstanding Achievements in Philosophy and Social Sciences Awards were recently announced, with three Shantou University research achievements receiving awards: one first prize and two third prizes. The competition covered achievements published between January 1, 2010 and December 31, 2011. Across Guangdong, 39 first prizes, 73 second prizes and 116 third prizes were awarded.

The first-prize achievement from STU was the School of Liberal Arts paper “Willingness to Communicate in English: A Model in the Chinese EFL Classroom Context,” co-authored by Associate Professor Peng Jian'e and Lindy Woodrow. The third-prize achievements included the School of Journalism's book “Saving Newspapers” and Zheng Songhui's School of Medicine paper “A Preliminary Study of Copyright Protection in Library Oral History Work.”

Seventeen STU achievements were entered in this round, covering papers, books and research reports. In the previous round, Professor Wang Weilian's book “An Introduction to Curriculum Studies in Higher Education” won a first prize. STU has therefore received a first prize in two consecutive editions of the provincial awards.
