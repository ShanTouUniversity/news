On June 24, Shantou University\'s Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department circulated the Guangdong Provincial Education Work Committee\'s Guideline on Further Improving the Quality of Student Party Member Development to all general Party branches and directly affiliated Party branches. The circulation calls on the University\'s primary Party organizations to implement the guidance in light of their circumstances.

Issued by the Guangdong Provincial Education Work Committee under document No. Yue Jiaogongwei Zuhan [2013] No. 25, the guideline is to be studied and implemented by the relevant Party organizations. They are also asked to report on implementation to the University\'s Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department in a timely manner.

Relevant units may submit implementation updates through the department\'s designated channel at o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. The complete guideline and circulated document are available in the attachment on the OA system.
