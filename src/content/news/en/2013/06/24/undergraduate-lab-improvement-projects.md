Shantou University recently announced the approval results for the 2013 Special Initiative for Supporting and Improving Undergraduate Laboratory Teaching. Established in 2013, the initiative is intended to improve undergraduate laboratory teaching conditions, strengthen teaching laboratory development, and safeguard and enhance the quality of laboratory instruction.

According to the announcement, project applications were submitted by university units, reviewed by the University Laboratory Affairs Committee, and approved after deliberation by university leadership. The projects receiving support mainly fall into two categories: those focused on laboratory teaching quality assurance and improvement, including the purchase of instruments and equipment; and those involving laboratory environment development, renovation, and facility provision. The specific projects and funding allocations for each unit are listed in the announcement attachment.

The university asked the relevant units to advance project development according to schedule and ensure the quality of implementation.
