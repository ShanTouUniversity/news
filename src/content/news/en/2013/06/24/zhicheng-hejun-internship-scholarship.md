It is reported that Shantou University’s Zhicheng Academy has announced nine candidates for the 2013 Hejun Business Internship Scholarship. The scholarship review committee conducted its assessment on June 20 in accordance with the relevant scholarship assessment rules, with the candidates ranked by the number of strokes in their surnames.

The selected students will take part in business internships and submit internship summary materials in mid-September according to the assessment schedule. The committee will determine the final scholarship recipients based on the candidates’ summary materials and public defenses.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Scholarship | 2013 STU Zhicheng Academy Hejun Business Internship Scholarship |
| Candidates | 9 students |
| Review date | June 20, 2013 |
| Follow-up | Participate in the internship and submit summary materials in mid-September |
| Final assessment basis | Internship summary materials and public defense |
| Reviewing body | Zhicheng Academy scholarship review committee |
