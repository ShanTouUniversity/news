It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University issued a public notice on June 24, stating that four recruited faculty members at the university are proposed to receive funding under the Guangdong Higher Education Talent Recruitment Special Fund following the university's application and an evaluation by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education.

The public notice was issued in accordance with the 2007 interim measures jointly issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Provincial Department of Personnel and the Provincial Department of Finance. The proposal is open for public comment from June 24 to 28, 2013. The detailed basic information of the proposed recipients is provided in the OA attachment.

## Public Notice Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed recipients | Four recruited faculty members at Shantou University |
| Funding program | Guangdong Higher Education Talent Recruitment Special Fund |
| Public comment period | June 24-28, 2013 |
| Provincial office | Faculty Management Division, Guangdong Provincial Department of Education |
| University contact | Xia Lin Wu, Human Resources Office |
