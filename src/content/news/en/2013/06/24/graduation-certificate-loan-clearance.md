It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs at Shantou University has announced arrangements for 2013 graduates with national student loans or outstanding fees to collect original or photocopied graduation and degree certificates. The relevant graduates must complete the procedures in the lobby on the first floor of the Administrative Center from June 28 to 29.

Graduates who have signed a repayment agreement with Bank of China and completed the required departure procedures may present their departure clearance form and original identity card to collect photocopies of both certificates, the relevant proof, and the repayment agreement. Graduates who have not yet signed a repayment agreement must first contact the bank to complete repayment confirmation. Graduates with outstanding fees, including graduates with national student loans who have not paid their tuition in full, must obtain the university's approval for deferred payment and sign a repayment agreement before collecting the relevant materials.

The collection of original certificates depends on when the loan or outstanding fees were settled. Graduates who submitted a loan settlement notice to the Student Financial Aid Management Center on or before June 20 may collect the originals from their colleges. Those who settled their loans or fees after June 20 may present their departure clearance form, original identity card, and loan settlement notice or relevant receipt to the Student Financial Aid Management Center to collect the originals.

Graduates continuing to domestic postgraduate study or a second degree who intend to apply for an extension of their loan term must bring their admission notice, identity card, loan contract, repayment agreement, and other required materials to the Shantou Branch of Bank of China by June 30 to sign a supplementary agreement for the extension. Otherwise, repayment will begin according to the original contract. Anyone authorizing another person to collect the certificates must also provide a letter of authorization and photocopies of both parties' identity cards.

The Student Financial Aid Management Center can be reached at `0754-86502249`. The Personal Finance Department of the Shantou Branch of Bank of China can be reached at `0754-88266321` or `0754-88949407`; its address is No. 98 Jinsha Road, Shantou, Guangdong.
