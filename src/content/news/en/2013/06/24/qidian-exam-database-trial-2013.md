It is reported that the Library issued a notice on June 24, 2013, to open trial access to the Qidian Exam Database.

The database, contributed by nearly a hundred experts from prominent universities, covers English, computer skills, civil service, judicial, accounting, postgraduate entrance, engineering, qualification and medical examinations, with more than 150 categories, 600 subjects and nearly 40,000 authentic and simulated papers. The system offers multi-format simulations to help students familiarize themselves with exam patterns and reinforce knowledge.
