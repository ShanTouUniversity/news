The Zhicheng College Xintongda Scholarship review committee conducted its 2013 review on **June 20** and recently announced the results. A total of **four college residents** were selected as scholarship recipients.

### Review process

The review was conducted under the *STU Zhicheng College Xintongda Scholarship Review Measures*, with Zhicheng College organizing the review committee. The selection was carried out according to the principles of fairness, impartiality and openness, and the recipients were listed in order of the number of strokes in their surnames in the notice.

The scholarship is available to Zhicheng College residents who meet the requirements of the review measures and recognizes students who demonstrate outstanding commitment to the college's motto, “Sincerity, Respect, Humility and Harmony.” Zhicheng College had previously published information on the scholarship's establishment, eligibility and review rules.

The notice attachment contains the individual recipient list. Because the attachment includes student personal information, this article does not reproduce students' names, majors or other personal details.
