Shantou University has completed its 2012-2013 Good News Awards, according to the News Center. The annual competition received 73 news entries and 38 photo series from units across the University.

The entries were reviewed anonymously in two stages by experts from within and outside the University. The judging awarded three first prizes, five second prizes and ten third prizes in the news category, as well as two first prizes, three second prizes and five third prizes in the photo category. The detailed list of winning entries and authors is provided in the official results attachment.

Correspondents representing the winning units should collect the prize money from the News Center office by July 2. For inquiries, contact the News Center at 86502368.
