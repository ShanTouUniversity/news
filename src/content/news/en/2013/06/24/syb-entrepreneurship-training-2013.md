Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office and Student Affairs Office announced on June 24 that registration had opened for the university’s first entrepreneurship training course under the SYB program. The course is open to students interested in the training.

Registration is handled through an online booking system. Applicants should visit the [Shantou University registration system](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/) to submit a booking. Places will be allocated in booking order, and registration will close once the quota is reached. The university plans to publish the registration results on its OA system by July 2.

| Registration item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Training | First SYB entrepreneurship training course |
| Registration method | Online booking through the Shantou University registration system |
| Closing condition | Registration closes when the quota is full |
| Results | Posted on the OA system by July 2 |

The course schedule and registration requirements contained in the attachments are not reproduced here. Readers should consult the original notice and attachments in the Shantou University OA system.
