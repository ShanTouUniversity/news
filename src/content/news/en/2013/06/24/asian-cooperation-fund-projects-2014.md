Shantou University’s Research Office announced on June 24 that it had launched the call for projects supported by the 2014 Asian Regional Cooperation Special Fund. Applications should address the needs of national development and Asian regional cooperation, and materials should be prepared in accordance with the application guidelines.

Applicants must provide a special-fund use application, a detailed project expenditure budget, a feasibility study report, and proof of matching funds. Electronic documents must be sent to the Research Office by July 1, while three paper copies must also be submitted to the office.

| Application item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Fund | 2014 Asian Regional Cooperation Special Fund |
| Requirements | Address national development and Asian regional cooperation needs and follow the application guidelines |
| Required materials | Fund-use application, detailed expenditure budget, feasibility study report, and proof of matching funds |
| Deadline | By July 1 |
| Submission | Send electronic documents through the Research Office's designated channel and submit three paper copies to the office |
| Contacts | Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng; 86502813 |

The application guidelines and related project materials in the attachments are not reproduced here. Applicants should consult the original notice and attachments in the Shantou University OA system.
