It is reported that the President’s Office and the Student Affairs Office jointly issued a notice on June 24, 2013, that the rehearsal for the 2013 Commencement and awards ceremony will be held on June 27 in the Gymnasium.

## Rehearsal Schedule

| Session | Time | Participants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | 17:00, June 27, 2013 | Members and advisors of the wind band, choir and etiquette team, and selected members of the Antarctic scientific expedition program |
| Second | 19:30, June 27, 2013 | Recipients of the Teaching Excellence Award, Outstanding Service Award, STU Medal and Sportsmanship Medal, doctoral graduates (in doctoral gowns) and deans of all colleges |

Relevant staff from the President’s Office, Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, News Center, Network and Information Center, Sports Department and Resource Management Office, as well as counselors in charge of commencement matters in colleges and residential colleges, are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (President’s Office, Student Affairs Office)
