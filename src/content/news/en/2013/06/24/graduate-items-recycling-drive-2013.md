It is reported that the Green Ribbon Service-Learning Environmental Volunteer Team of Shantou University’s Young Volunteers Association will organize a graduate-item recycling drive from June 26 to 30, 2013, promoting resource reuse and an orderly departure for graduating students. The event is organized by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the STU Young Volunteers Association, and related volunteer-service teams.

## Collection Arrangements

Volunteers will collect items from the AB, CD, EF, G, HI, L, Y, B13, and B14 dormitory buildings. Door-to-door collection will be available from June 26 to 28 each day during 09:30–12:30, 15:30–18:00, and 20:30–22:30. From June 29 to 30, collection times will be arranged according to graduates’ needs.

The collection includes used books, magazines, appliances, and other items that graduating students no longer need. The organizers plan to hold a charity sale at the beginning of the fall semester. The collected goods and proceeds will support the Firefly Charity Action and students facing financial hardship.

## Volunteer Recruitment

The event is also recruiting volunteers from across the University. Applications close on June 25, and recruitment will follow a first-come, first-served order. Volunteer service hours will be recorded according to actual participation.
