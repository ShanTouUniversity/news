It is reported that the Li Ka Shing Foundation has launched a book-donation initiative for the Shantou University graduating class of 2013, providing one book to each graduate. The book was written by Peng Pangtu; its title cannot be fully identified because of encoding corruption in the source record.

The Student Affairs Office asked each college and residential college to send a representative to collect the books from the Graduate Employment Guidance Center in Room 106 of the New Administration Center by 5 p.m. on June 26, 2013. The units will then arrange distribution to graduates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
