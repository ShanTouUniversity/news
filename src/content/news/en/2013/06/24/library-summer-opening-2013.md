Shantou University Library recently announced its opening arrangements for the 2013 summer vacation, providing information about library services available to faculty, staff and students during the break.

## Opening Schedule

During the vacation period for administrative and support units, from July 3 to August 14, 2013, the library will provide services on July 4, 6, 9, 11, 13, 16, 18, 20, 23, 25, 27 and 30, and August 1, 3, 6, 8, 10 and 13. Opening hours will be 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The Special Collections Area, Reading Hall and Seminar Rooms will be temporarily closed to students on campus during the student summer vacation, from June 30 to August 17, 2013. Other library services will resume their regular opening schedule on August 15.
