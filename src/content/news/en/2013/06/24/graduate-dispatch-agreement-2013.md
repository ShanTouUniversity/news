It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs announced arrangements for employment procedures for main-campus graduates of the Class of 2013. From June 25 to 27, graduates who applied for deferred employment must sign deferred employment agreements at the Student Employment Guidance Center; those who did not apply for deferred employment and are not continuing their studies should collect their dispatch certificates; and graduates continuing their studies, including overseas study, should submit a file transfer letter or admission notice as soon as possible and sign the Application Form for Graduates of Guangdong Higher Education Institutions Not Participating in Employment Due to Further Studies.

The deferred-employment agreement and dispatch-certificate procedures will be handled at Reception Room 105 in the New Administrative Center. Further-study procedures will be handled in Room 106. The schedule by college is as follows:

| Date | Time | Colleges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 25 | 09:00–11:30 | College of Liberal Arts; Law School |
| June 25 | 14:30–17:00 | Business School |
| June 26 | 09:00–11:30 | College of Science; School of Journalism |
| June 26 | 14:30–17:00 | College of Engineering |
| June 27 | 09:00–11:30 | Graduate School |
| June 27 | 14:30–17:00 | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

The Office of Student Affairs reminded graduates to check the details of their dispatch certificates and contact the Student Employment Guidance Center if any issue arises. Certificates may be collected by another person, but must be forwarded to the graduate promptly. Graduates must report to the designated address within the specified period. Students who have not graduated normally, are still taking courses, or have not yet confirmed whether they will graduate should not collect a dispatch certificate; if one has already been collected, it must be returned to the Student Employment Guidance Center, otherwise another certificate may not be issued upon normal graduation. These dispatch procedures affect the destination of graduates' files and should be handled carefully.

The Student Employment Guidance Center can be reached at `0754-86502252`.
