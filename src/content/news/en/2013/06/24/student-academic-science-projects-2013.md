Shantou University has launched the approval process for its 2013 undergraduate extracurricular academic and science projects, according to the University Student Extracurricular Academic and Science Activities Leading Group. The program is being conducted under the university's regulations on the application and management of student extracurricular academic and science projects, with the aim of organizing and supporting students' participation in extracurricular research practice.

The approval process will run from June 24, 2013, to March 1, 2014. Each college will set its own schedule based on local circumstances and organize outreach, project applications, and review by college-level expert groups. Within one week after completing its review, each college must submit summary materials for proposed projects, application forms, expected outcomes and benefits analyses, and work schedules to the Office of the University Youth League Committee. The University Student Extracurricular Academic and Science Activities Leading Group will then review and approve the projects under the relevant regulations and allocate the corresponding project funds.

After projects are completed, the relevant units must organize expert reviews and submit completion reports, summaries of fund usage, and performance evaluation reports. The university asked all relevant units to provide the necessary organization, services, and support so that approved projects can be implemented as planned.
