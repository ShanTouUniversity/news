Shantou University announced its Interim Policy on Encouraging Long-Term Service by International Faculty on June 24, 2013. The policy applies to all units of the university.

According to the notice, the policy was discussed and approved at the eighth university leadership meeting of 2013, held on June 18. The university asked all units to carry out relevant work in accordance with the published policy to support the long-term service of international faculty.

The detailed provisions and implementation requirements are included in the notice attachment.
