The Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication recently released a notice announcing the class schedule for the public photography course (course code SER2410), offered during the 2013–2014 summer term.

The course is available to relevant students, with the exact class times and locations included in the schedule attachment issued with the notice. Students who need the complete arrangement should refer to the attachment in the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication)
>
> ⚠️ The exact class times, locations, and schedule are available in the original attachment on the OA system.
