According to the Party General Branch of Shantou University’s Graduate School, the branch issued its commendation decision on June 26, 2013, following democratic evaluation and recommendations by student Party branches, as well as review and public notice by the Graduate School Party General Branch.

The commendation recognizes seven individuals as Outstanding Party Affairs Workers of the Graduate School and 27 individuals as Outstanding CPC Members of the Graduate School. The awards acknowledge the work carried out by Party branches and members during the 2012–2013 academic year in support of building service-oriented and learning-oriented Party organizations, while highlighting their exemplary role.

The Graduate School Party General Branch encouraged the honorees to maintain the advanced qualities expected of CPC members, continue serving as role models, and make further contributions in their studies, work and Party activities.

To protect students’ personal information, this report does not reproduce the names of student honorees. The complete list should be consulted in the original notice on the Shantou University OA system.
