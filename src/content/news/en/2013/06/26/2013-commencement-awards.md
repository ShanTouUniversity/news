It is reported that Shantou University will hold the 2013 commencement and awards ceremony at the University Gymnasium at 7:50 a.m. on Friday, June 28, 2013. The Party and Government Office and the Student Affairs Office issued the arrangements on June 26, asking graduates, parents and event staff to enter on time and maintain order at the venue.

## Entry and Viewing Arrangements

| Group | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Graduates | Schools must organize their graduate groups by 6:30 a.m.; counselors will lead them into the venue from 6:35 a.m. |
| Invited parents | Enter at 6:40 a.m. with tickets and sit in the assigned seats; tickets for different sections correspond to different Gymnasium entrances |
| School leaders | Report to the Gymnasium fitness room in academic regalia by 7:00 a.m. |
| Parents without reserved seats | Watch the live broadcast in the Science and Technology Report Hall from 7:30 to 9:30 a.m. |

The notice asks everyone entering the venue to dress appropriately and follow staff instructions. Photography should be taken from one's seat, mobile phones should be set to vibrate, and children under 12 will not be admitted.

Specific seating, ticketing and related attachment information is subject to the original notice on the university OA system. Attachments containing personal information are not reproduced here; relevant participants should consult the original notice through the OA system.
