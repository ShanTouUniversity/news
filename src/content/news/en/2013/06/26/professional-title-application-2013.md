It is reported that Shantou University has issued a notice (STUFa [2013] No. 89) on the application and review of professional and technical qualifications for 2013, in line with documents from the provincial education department and human resources department. Several new measures take effect this year.

## New Measures This Year

**Online review of representative papers**: For the first time, applicants for senior professional titles will have their representative papers submitted and evaluated online. Applicants enter paper details in the online title application system; indexed papers are uploaded via link with downloaded copies, while non-indexed ones are uploaded as scans. Monographs, artworks and other items unsuitable for scanning may be submitted as originals or photocopies directly to the university. The school will verify that submissions match originals, and papers submitted improperly will be treated as failing review.

**Continuing education requirement**: As required by provincial authorities, applicants must provide proof of completing continuing education courses from 2010 to 2012 (current-year courses are not required).

**Green channel for high-level returnees**: Overseas high-level scholars returning to China may apply through this channel, provided their overseas achievements are certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate or appraised by at least three domestic peer experts. Foreign language, computer skills and continuing education requirements are waived, but overseas degrees must first be authenticated.

**One-vote veto on ethics**: Teacher conduct remains a key element of the review, with strict enforcement of a one-vote veto rule for ethics violations. Fabrication or academic misconduct will be dealt with severely. Serious teaching accidents in the 2012–2013 academic year also trigger a one-vote veto.

## Materials and Other Requirements

All papers, research projects, patents and awards submitted must list the applicant's workplace as the first affiliation, and materials remain valid only until August 31 this year. Applications are made online via the Guangdong provincial teacher title system.

Under the review procedure, grassroots units carefully examine materials and sign off before the HR Office displays them publicly for seven working days, with the Discipline Inspection and Audit Office receiving faculty comments and petitions.

Fees follow provincial standards: RMB 780 per senior title applicant (including RMB 200 for paper appraisal), RMB 140 defense fee where applicable, RMB 450 for intermediate titles and RMB 280 for junior titles. Fees may be deducted from salary accounts or paid in person at the Finance Office.
