Shantou University's Infrastructure Office under the Resource Management Office announced on June 26 that it is recruiting one engineering secretary from the public to support the university's construction work.

## Position

The position requires a college diploma or higher, with preference given to applicants in civil engineering, building construction or related fields. Applicants with engineering secretarial or project management experience, a professional technical qualification in construction engineering, or a relevant professional registration certificate will receive priority. The role also requires familiarity with CAD and common office software, as well as organizational, coordination, writing and teamwork skills. Relevant professional work experience is preferred.

The engineering secretary will assist project supervisors with organizing project documents and drawings, manage the receipt, registration and filing of project correspondence, support tendering work, help organize engineering meetings and prepare minutes, and assist with drafting project plans and summaries.

## Appointment and Application

The successful applicant will be hired as an auxiliary contract worker. The appointment does not transfer the applicant's personnel relationship to Shantou University, and compensation will follow relevant university regulations. Applicants must submit a resume and copies of relevant education and qualification documents by sealed letter or email no later than July 7, 2013. Candidates who pass the initial screening will be contacted for an interview.
