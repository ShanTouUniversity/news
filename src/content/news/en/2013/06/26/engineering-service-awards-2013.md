It is reported that Shantou University College of Engineering has publicized nominees for its 2012–2013 teaching and service awards. The selection followed the college's existing award measures, with recommendations from departments, centers, and the college administration, followed by consideration by the college affairs committee and assessment by the college academic committee.

## Three Awards Under Public Review

| Award | Proposed recipient and unit |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Teaching Award | Professor Cui Yan, Department of Electronics |
| Best Teaching Service Award | Mr. Chen Zhanglin, Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering |
| Best Administrative Service Award | Mr. Yao Yi, College Youth League Committee |

The college is now seeking public comments on the proposed recipients. Comments may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the College of Engineering Office by the end of the workday on June 27, 2013. Submissions must include the commenter’s real name.
