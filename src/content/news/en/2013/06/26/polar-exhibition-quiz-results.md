Shantou University held an online prize quiz during the Antarctic Project polar exhibition, covering topics including the polar regions, the ocean and global warming. The activity aimed to deepen students' understanding of public environmental issues.

The quiz ended on June 25, with 233 students taking part. Ten students answered all 20 questions about the polar regions correctly. Under the activity rules, each of them is eligible to receive a commemorative quick-dry T-shirt from the Antarctic exploration project.

Prize recipients should collect their gifts from the Student Affairs Office's Outdoor Development Office, Room 106 of the Administration Center, during working hours by 5 p.m. on July 2. The answer key has been posted in the “Latest News” section of the Antarctic Project website.
