Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has announced preparations for the 2013 housing subsidy applications. Formal applications are scheduled to begin in September 2013, and the preparatory work is intended for newly transferred, formally appointed faculty and staff members.

## Eligibility

Applicants must hold formal appointments at units directly administered by Guangdong Province and have their personnel relationship registered with Shantou University. Applicants and their spouses must also meet all of the following conditions:

- They have not purchased a public-sector housing-reform residence, a hardship-relief residence, or participated in employer-funded collective housing construction.
- They have not received a government-recognized housing subsidy.
- They have stopped receiving housing benefits under the current housing-reform or low-rent system and are paying rent at the cost-based or commercial rate.

## Preparing Documents

Eligible faculty and staff may prepare the relevant supporting documents in advance. The application is expected to require a housing subsidy review form, together with original documents applicable to the applicant’s circumstances, including a dispatch certificate, letters of introduction, certificates confirming that no qualifying housing has been purchased, identification documents for both spouses, and a divorce agreement or court judgment where applicable. All applicants must also present their employment contract with Shantou University. The form is expected to be made available for download in September, with the formal completion schedule to be announced separately by the University Property Office.

For transferred employees, certificates from former employers and the housing-reform office in the place of the original household registration confirming that no qualifying housing was purchased may be obtained during the summer. Applicants may also contact the University Property Office, which can issue an inquiry letter where appropriate.

For inquiries, contact the University Property Office.
