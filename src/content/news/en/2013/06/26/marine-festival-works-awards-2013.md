It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University announced on June 26, 2013, the winners of the first “Youthful Vitality, Vibrant Ocean” Marine Festival works competition on maritime territory awareness and marine ecological protection for the 2012–2013 academic year.

After more than a month of submissions, evaluation by judges and voting by visitors on the university’s open day, 13 works were selected. Winners are asked to collect their awards at the Youth League Committee of the School of Science before June 28. The winning works will be exhibited in the old administration building until the end of the semester.
