It is reported that Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication established its Outstanding Graduate award in 2013 to encourage well-rounded student development and foster a positive academic and college environment. The selection considers conduct, participation in collective activities, overall qualities, graduation thesis performance, social practice, and scholarship history.

## Five Graduates Proposed for Selection

The selection received applications from 10 students. After review by the college affairs committee, the school proposed five members of the 2013 graduating class for the Outstanding Graduate award. The proposed results are being publicized from June 26 to 28.

Comments on the proposed results may be submitted under the commenter’s real name to the Youth League Committee Office of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication. The contact numbers are 82903651 and 86503651, and the email address is xinwenxytw@stu.edu.cn.
