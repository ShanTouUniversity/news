It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on June 26, 2013, that parent tickets for the 2013 commencement ceremony had been distributed to the university's schools. Graduates who received tickets may collect them through the Youth League committee of their school.

## Collection Arrangements

| Group | Collection location |
| --- | --- |
| General undergraduate graduates | The Youth League committee of their school |
| Graduate students | The Graduate School |
| Medical School graduates | The Medical School Career Guidance Center |

The notice states that the specific recipient list and ticket quantities are provided in the OA attachment. The attachment containing student information is not reproduced here. Relevant graduates should access the original notice through the university OA system and follow the stated procedures.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The attachment with student names and ticket quantities is available through the original notice in the OA system.
