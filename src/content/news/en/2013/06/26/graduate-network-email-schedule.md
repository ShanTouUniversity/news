It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University has announced a unified adjustment of campus network account privileges and STU email accounts for 2013 undergraduate and graduate graduates.

## Key Dates

| Item | Date |
| ---- | ---- |
| Campus internet access disabled | July 1 |
| STU email accounts closed | August 20 |
| Alumni email accounts activated | August 20 |

## Key Points

- **Internet access**: Graduates' campus internet access will be disabled on July 1;
- **Authentication privileges**: Access to various application systems (the credit-based system, utilities system, Mystu, etc.) will be adjusted by their respective administrative units;
- **Email data**: After the STU email accounts are closed, personal email data will no longer be kept on the server, so graduates should transfer their messages in advance;
- **Alumni email**: Alumni email accounts for the Class of 2013 will be activated on August 20, with details available on the alumni website xyh.stu.edu.cn;
- **Staying on campus**: Graduates who remain to work at the university may re-register their campus network account and STU email at the network center after completing HR onboarding procedures.

For questions, call 86503400-2 or email support@stu.edu.cn.
