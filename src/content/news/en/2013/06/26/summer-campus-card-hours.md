It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University issued the summer vacation campus card service schedule on June 26, 2013, providing guidance for faculty and students who need card-related services during the break.

## Service Window Schedule

| Service location | Summer vacation arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Expert Apartments campus card service window | Remains open during regular working hours and handles all campus card services |
| Campus card recharge window beneath the Second Canteen | Closed from July 3 to August 18 and reopens on August 19 |

While the recharge window is closed, faculty and students may use the card-loading, transfer and balance-query self-service machine to add funds. Lost-card reporting and balance queries are available through the voice self-service number 82903414, or extension 3414 when calling from campus.

For questions about campus card services during the summer vacation, contact the center at 86503420 or support@stu.edu.cn.
