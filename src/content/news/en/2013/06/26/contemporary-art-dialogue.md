Yangtze College of Arts and Design will host the 17th Kan Kan Culture event at 10:30 a.m. on Friday, June 28, 2013. Titled “About Contemporary Art,” the event will feature a dialogue between Wang Shouzhi and Xu Bing.

The event is intended to give faculty members and students an opportunity to learn about contemporary art practice and related ideas through an exchange with the two guests. Details of the event and the attachment are subject to the original notice published in the university OA system.
