An exhibition featuring the Li Ka Shing Foundation's major public welfare projects in China is being held on the first floor of Shantou University's New Administration Center from June 26 to July 5. It presents the foundation's work in education, healthcare, culture and public welfare to the university community.

According to the organizers, the exhibition uses project materials and stories of beneficiaries to show how the initiatives have developed and how public welfare efforts have affected individuals and society. Project staff will be available on site from 9:00 a.m. to noon and from 2:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. each day to explain the projects. Commemorative items will also be available to visitors.

Online Information

The Li Ka Shing Foundation has also provided an online exhibition e-card page: [http://cws.stu.edu.cn/e-card/](http://cws.stu.edu.cn/e-card/).
