It is reported that Shantou University Engineering School has opened campus registration and preparations for the 2013 National Undergraduate Electronic Design Contest. Centered on electronic circuit application design, the contest covers areas including analog and digital circuits, microcontrollers, programmable devices and EDA tools. Its theoretical design, hands-on production and debugging components are intended to assess participants' ability to apply knowledge, innovate and conduct experiments.

Students will register in teams of three, and each student may join only one team. The school recommends that teams combine complementary strengths in hardware circuit design, software programming and written communication. Campus training will begin on July 7 and continue through September. Through the training and selection process, the school plans to choose 10 outstanding teams to represent Shantou University at the 2013 national contest. The national contest is generally held in September of odd-numbered years and lasts four days; the exact dates will be announced separately.

Students interested in participating must submit their registration information as required by June 24. Further details are available on the official website of the National Undergraduate Electronic Design Contest or from the relevant team in the Engineering School's Department of Electronic Engineering.
