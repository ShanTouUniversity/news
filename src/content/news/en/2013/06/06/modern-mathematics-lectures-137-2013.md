The School of Science at Shantou University will host the **137th** Toward Modern Mathematics academic lecture from **7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on Thursday, June 6** in **Classroom E406**. The session will feature two presentations and is open to interested faculty members and students.

## Presentations

Professor He Wei of Nanjing Normal University's School of Mathematical Sciences will present “Mathematics: The Science of Structure.” From the perspective of the Bourbaki school, the talk will introduce three categories of mathematical structures: order, algebraic and topological structures. Examples will be used to discuss the connections among them.

Professor Chen Shuili of Jimei University's School of Science will present “Fuzzy Information Technology and Its Applications in Unmanned Low-Altitude Remote Sensing.” The talk will cover UAV autopilots, image-information extraction and optimization based on fuzzy control, practical applications in UAV image processing, and the independently developed “Luhang” series of UAVs.
