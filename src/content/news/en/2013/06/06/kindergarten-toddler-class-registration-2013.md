The kindergarten under Shantou University's Resource Management Office planned to enroll new toddler-class children for 2013. The program was open to children of main-campus faculty and staff who were **over two and under three years old**.

Eligible children had to have been born on or before **August 31, 2011**, and after **August 31, 2010**. Parents were asked to prepare original documents and photocopies verifying staff affiliation, family relationship and the child's date of birth as required by the notice. Personal document details are not reproduced here.

## Registration Arrangements

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Registration date | June 14, 2013 |
| Morning session | 9:00–11:00 a.m. |
| Afternoon session | 3:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Second floor of the STU kindergarten |
| Eligibility | Children of main-campus faculty and staff, over two and under three years old |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
