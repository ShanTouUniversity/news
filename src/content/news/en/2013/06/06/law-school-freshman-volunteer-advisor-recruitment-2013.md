The Law School's Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union at Shantou University opened recruitment on **June 6** for volunteer advisors supporting the 2013 freshmen. The role is intended to help new students become familiar with the campus, adjust to university life and make the transition into their new environment. Applicants are drawn from the Law School's **2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts**.

## Service Duties

Volunteer advisors will support reception, material distribution, directions and question answering during the freshmen's **registration and orientation education on September 2**. After the freshmen enroll, advisors will also proactively contact them and provide reasonable assistance with their academic and daily lives.

## Applications and Interviews

Applications are open from **June 6 through June 10**. Applicants must complete an electronic application form, print **six copies** themselves and submit them at the interview. Interviews will be conducted in the order in which forms are submitted. The interview is tentatively scheduled for **June 11**, with the specific time and venue to be announced separately.

The 2013 freshmen were expected to enroll in early September, with orientation training and related work scheduled for **mid-to-late August**. Selected volunteer advisors must return to campus and participate in the orientation activities as required.

Application forms and submission instructions should be taken from the original STU OA notice and attachments. The application email is a personal contact and is omitted here.
