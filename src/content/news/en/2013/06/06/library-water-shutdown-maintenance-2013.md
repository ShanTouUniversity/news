Following a notice from the university's Water and Electricity Center, the library will service a water connection point from **9:00 p.m. on Thursday, June 6, to 9:00 a.m. on Friday, June 7**. Water service in the library will be suspended during this period.

The disruption will affect the library's air conditioning, drinking-water dispensers and restroom sinks. Services will resume after the maintenance is completed.
