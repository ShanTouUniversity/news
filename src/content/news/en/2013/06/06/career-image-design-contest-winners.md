It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University held the final of the Career Image Design Contest on May 31. After keen competition, awards were decided for champion, runner-up, second runner-up and the most popular team.

## Winners

| Award | Team |
| --- | --- |
| Champion | Keep Shinning |
| Runner-up | Yazhuang |
| Second Runner-up | Qiupai Shangde Mantou |
| Most Popular | PC |

The Student Affairs Office congratulated the winning students and thanked teachers and students across the university for their support of the contest.
