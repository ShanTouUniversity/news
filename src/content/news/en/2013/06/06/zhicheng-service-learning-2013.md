It is reported that applications for Zhicheng Residential College's summer rural service-learning public welfare course recently closed, drawing an enthusiastic response from 104 students. After an initial screening of application materials and interviews held on June 5, the college's program team confirmed 40 students as regular participants and 2 as alternates.

All admitted students are required to log in to the credit-based course system and record their course selections before 10:00 p.m. on June 6. Detailed instructions have been sent to each participant's email.
