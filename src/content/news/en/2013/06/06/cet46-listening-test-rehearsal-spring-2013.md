To help candidates for the spring 2013 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 examinations confirm that they can receive the listening signal, Shantou University will arrange a broadcast test from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Friday, June 7** in **Buildings E, G and D**.

Candidates must bring their own headphones and go to their assigned examination location during this period for testing. The listening signal will be transmitted at **FM 73.2**.

On June 8, the Academic Affairs Office issued a further notice stating that the listening signal in Buildings D and G had been restored. A new test was scheduled for **4:00 to 5:00 p.m. that day**. Candidates taking the examination in Buildings D and G were asked to bring their headphones, adjust them and test the signal at their examination locations, and inform one another of the arrangement.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office, June 6, 7 and 8, 2013)
