The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold the 16th Kan Kan Culture dialogue at **7:30 p.m. on Friday, June 7** in the **STU Science Lecture Hall**. The theme is “I love good things from other countries. Am I not patriotic?”

The dialogue will feature Wang Shouzhi, dean and professor of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Li Yongquan, also known as Tommy Li, a Hong Kong brand consultant and designer. Wang has worked extensively in design history and theory and design education. Li works primarily in brand consulting and design and has been invited to join the Alliance Graphique Internationale (AGI).
