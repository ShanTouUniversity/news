The STU Student Affairs Office announced on **June 6** that the university would begin holding regular leadership-student dinners in the spring 2013 semester. The series is intended to encourage students to offer ideas and participate in university development while creating a channel for direct dialogue with university leaders. The first dinner is scheduled for **6:00 p.m. on June 18** in **Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant**.

## Event Arrangements

A university leader will host the dinner. Leaders from relevant administrative offices, four to five faculty representatives and students will take part in an open discussion on university development and issues of interest to students. The first session is open to full-time STU students, including exchange students, and plans to invite **40 students**.

Interested students must complete the registration materials through the [reservation system](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm) by **June 12**. They may also submit questions collected from fellow students or topics of personal interest. The Student Affairs Office will select student representatives in reservation order and post the list on the university's internal office-information network. The system will close automatically once the quota is reached.

## Catering and Feedback

The university commissioned Hongtai Restaurant to prepare one standard meal for each participant, with the cost paid centrally by the university. Participants were encouraged to respect one another, stay engaged with university development and student growth, and objectively convey issues of concern among their peers.

Comments and suggestions about the dinner may be sent to the Student Affairs Office at `lslin@stu.edu.cn`.
