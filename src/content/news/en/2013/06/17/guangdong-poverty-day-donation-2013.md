It is reported that the President’s Office and the Trade Union issued a notice on June 17, 2013, to organize the 4th Guangdong Poverty Alleviation Day donation drive among all faculty and staff, in line with provincial and municipal arrangements to promote the spirit of helping the poor and contributing with gratitude and solidarity.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Poverty alleviation, contributing with love |
| Participants | All faculty and staff |
| Organization | Donations collected and registered by department unions |
| Deadline | By June 21, 2013, to be consolidated at the university Trade Union |
| Follow-up | The Trade Union will transfer the funds to designated accounts as required; the donor list will be published on the campus office information network |
| Issued by | President’s Office, Trade Union |

Units are asked to mobilize faculty and staff to participate through various channels.

> Source: STU OA Notice (President’s Office, Trade Union)
