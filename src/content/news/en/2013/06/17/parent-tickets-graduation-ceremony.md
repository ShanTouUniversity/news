It is reported that Shantou University will hold its 2013 graduation ceremony in the university Gymnasium on June 28. The university will reserve seats for parents invited by graduating students.

Graduates wishing to invite parents may register through the [STU Booking Center](http://activity.stu.edu.cn) from 9:00 a.m. on June 19. The system will close at 4:00 p.m. on June 20. Each graduate may in principle reserve tickets for no more than two parents. The actual number of tickets available to each graduate will depend on overall demand, and non-graduating accounts may not register.

Approximately 450 parent tickets are expected to be available. After registration closes, the university will review the applications and use a random draw to determine the graduates receiving tickets and the number of tickets allocated to them. The results will be announced through the office-automation system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
