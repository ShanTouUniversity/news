It is reported that the annual review, commendation and election meeting of the Shantou University Science Association was held on the evening of June 7 in Classroom E-204. The meeting approved the annual work report delivered by the president of the twelfth session, commended a group of outstanding officers and members, and produced the executive committee for the association's thirteenth session through a competitive election.

The new nine-member committee is as follows:

| Name | Year & Major | Position |
| ---- | ------------ | -------- |
| Zhong YH | Class of 2011, Mechatronics | President |
| Xu ZM | Class of 2011, Computer Science | Vice President |
| Cai J | Class of 2011, Economics | Vice President |
| Hu JQ | Class of 2012, Biology | Committee Member |
| Huang SQ | Class of 2012, Civil Engineering | Committee Member |
| Chen DC | Class of 2012, Mechatronics | Committee Member |
| Zhang K | Class of 2012, Civil Engineering | Committee Member |
| Deng WJ | Class of 2012, Electronics | Committee Member |
| Chen ZH | Class of 2012, Mechatronics | Committee Member |

Zhong YH was elected president of the thirteenth session, with Xu ZM and Cai J serving as vice presidents.

The election results were jointly announced by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the university's Science Association, marking the completion of this leadership transition. The new committee will carry on the association's science popularization activities and other work.
