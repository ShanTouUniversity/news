It is reported that the Chaoshan-Jiedong Theater of the Guangdong Public Security Firefighting Corps will conduct a cross-regional rescue drill at Shantou University's Riyuekeng Reservoir on the morning of June 19. Students and faculty members may attend the event as observers.

### Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Drill | Cross-Regional Rescue Drill by the Chaoshan-Jiedong Theater of the Guangdong Public Security Firefighting Corps |
| Time | Morning of June 19, 2013 |
| Location | Riyuekeng Reservoir, Shantou University |
| Attendance | Students and faculty members may observe the drill |

The university reminded observers to pay attention to safety, follow the directions of staff members, and keep away from the water.
