It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on June 17, 2013, that students who have not yet selected a public-service course may register for remaining seats in courses offered during the summer and fall semesters. Registration is handled on a first-come, first-served basis, and each student may select only one course.

## Summer Semester Courses

| Offering unit | Course code | Course | Schedule | Seats remaining | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Department of Public Administration | SER0410 | “Meeting the Blue Sky” Creative Research and Service | July 16–19 | 1 | — |
| School of Journalism | SER2410 | Public-Service Photography | July 1–8 | 6 | Students with a single-lens reflex camera are preferred; students from the 2012 cohort are excluded due to military training |
| Zhicai College | SER3112 | “Entering University, Setting Sail for the Future” Team Summer Camp for Secondary School Students | July 8–14 | 8 | Students from the 2012 cohort are excluded due to military training |
| University Youth League Committee | SER3012 | Dawn Assistance Service | June 29–30: theory and development training; July 17–21: public-service practice; July 22: summary and sharing | 3 | — |

Students interested in registering should send their registration information to `gykc@stu.edu.cn` by 5 p.m. on June 19. The Academic Affairs Office stated that registration will close when all seats are filled. Questions may be directed to Ms. Liu at `86502416` or extension `882416`. This article does not reproduce students' names, student numbers or personal contact details used for registration.
