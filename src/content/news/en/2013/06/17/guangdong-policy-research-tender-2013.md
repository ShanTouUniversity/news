The Guangdong Provincial Government Development Research Center has opened a public tender for the 2013 major decision-consulting research projects of Guangdong Province. It is reported that five projects are available, each with funding of 150,000 yuan. Shantou University's Research Affairs Office has also issued arrangements for on-campus bids.

The projects are:

| No. | Project | Funding |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 1 | Research on Reforming Guangdong's Income Distribution System | 150,000 yuan |
| 2 | Paths and Countermeasures for Improving Guangdong's Social Security System | 150,000 yuan |
| 3 | Advancing Financial Reform and Innovation in the Pearl River Delta Through Guangdong-Hong Kong Financial Cooperation | 150,000 yuan |
| 4 | A Comprehensive Study of the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge's Impact on Development in Areas Along Both Sides of the Pearl River | 150,000 yuan |
| 5 | Urbanization of Guangdong's Rural Migrant Population and Innovation in the Rural Land System | 150,000 yuan |

Units and researchers with the relevant research capability and conditions may bid. On-campus applicants must complete the Application Form for the Social Tender of Guangdong Major Decision-Consulting Research Projects and the Research Outline, then send electronic copies to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn for a form review by July 10, 2013. After the review, applicants must submit 11 printed copies of the final application form and 19 printed copies of the research outline to the Research Affairs Office by July 15, while also sending the electronic materials.

The campus contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun, reachable at 86502563.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Affairs Office)
