Shantou University's Resource Management Office forwarded a notice on Shantou's air-raid siren test on June 17, 2013, reminding university units to review the relevant arrangements.

According to the forwarded notice, the siren test will last 15 minutes. The specific timing and other details are provided in the accompanying attachment, which university units may consult as needed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
> ⚠️ The air-raid siren test notice and other attachments are available on the OA system.
