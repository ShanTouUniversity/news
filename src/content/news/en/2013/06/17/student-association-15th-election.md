It is reported that the 15th Council Congress of Shantou University Student Association Federation was held on June 5 in Room E-201. The congress reviewed the work report of the 14th Standing Committee and elected the 15th Standing Committee and presidium through an uncontested election.

The election results are as follows:

| Position | Elected representatives |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Zhang HR |
| Vice chairs | Li QY and Liang SP |
| Standing committee members | Zhang HR, Li QY, Liang SP, Chen BE, Kong CX, Liu Y, Sun JW, Yuan MM, and Zhang Q |

The students' names are abbreviated under the privacy rules. Their year and major information from the original notice is not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Association Federation)
