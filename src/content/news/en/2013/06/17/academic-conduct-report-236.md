It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued Academic Conduct Inspection Report No. 236 on June 17, 2013, summarizing classroom inspection results from Week 16 of the spring semester of the 2012-2013 academic year. The inspection team conducted two checks in Teaching Building E, focusing on students arriving late for class.

## Inspection Results

| Inspection date | Time | Location | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| June 9, 2013 | 2:00 p.m. | Teaching Building E | 4 | 1 |
| June 13, 2013 | 2:00 p.m. | Teaching Building E | 4 | 4 |

The two inspections recorded eight arrivals no more than five minutes late and five arrivals more than five minutes late. The Student Affairs Office asked schools, residential colleges and relevant units to provide education to the students concerned, reinforce compliance with university rules and continue efforts to improve academic conduct.

This article does not reproduce students' names, student numbers or other personal information. The detailed list and related inspection attachment are available in the original notice on the STU OA system.
