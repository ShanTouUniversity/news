Shantou University Law School has announced the preliminary results of the VIS International Commercial Arbitration Moot. Following a combined assessment of preliminary interviews and paper scores, 19 students advanced to the next round of selection.

The next round is scheduled to take place at the beginning of the next semester. The selected students are expected to prepare relevant knowledge of the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) and international commercial arbitration law.
