The Shantou University Student Association Office suspended its duty services from June 17, 2013, the beginning of the seventeenth week, as the semester approached its end and most activities organized by student groups and associations had concluded.

The adjustment covers both regular office duty and approvals for material applications. Student organizations with urgent material requests may consult the Student Association or the Communist Youth League Committee through the relevant university channels. Specific contact details should be checked in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
