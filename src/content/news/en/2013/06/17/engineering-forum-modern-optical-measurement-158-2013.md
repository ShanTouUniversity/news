The 158th Engineering Research Forum of Shantou University's School of Engineering will be held at **3 p.m. on Monday, June 17** in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room. Professor Kang Xin, a doctoral supervisor at the School of Science of Nanjing University of Science and Technology, will speak on **modern optical measurement and mechanics**.

The forum is intended for graduate students and relevant faculty members in the Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering. Other interested students and faculty members are also welcome.
