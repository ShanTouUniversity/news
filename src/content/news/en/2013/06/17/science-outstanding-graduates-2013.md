It is reported that Shantou University School of Science released the proposed list of outstanding graduates from the Class of 2013 on June 17, 2013. The selection was conducted under the school's criteria for outstanding graduates. Following applications from graduating students, review by the School Youth League Committee and discussion by the school leadership, 17 graduates are proposed to be recognized as the School of Science's outstanding graduates from the Class of 2013.

| --- | --- |
