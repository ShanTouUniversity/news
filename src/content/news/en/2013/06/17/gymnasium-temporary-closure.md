The Shantou University Physical Education Department announced on June 17, 2013, that the university gymnasium would be temporarily closed. The public body of the notice did not specify the closure period or reason. Faculty, staff, and students should consult the original notice in the university OA system for detailed arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
