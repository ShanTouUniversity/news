It is reported that the annual review of the 12th council and the 13th council election of Shantou University's Chuang Path to Success Network were held on the evening of June 2, 2013, in Room E207. The meeting reviewed the 12th council's work report and elected the 13th council through an uncontested election.

## Members of the 13th Council

| Position | Elected representative |
| --- | --- |
| Chief Executive Officer (CEO) | Luo LF |
| Chief Operating Officer (COO) | Chen SL |
| Chief Technology Officer (CTO) | Chen CY |
| Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) | Jiang WL |

The students' names are abbreviated under the privacy rules; their full names, year levels and majors from the original notice are not published here. The original notice and related roster information are available through the Shantou University OA system.
