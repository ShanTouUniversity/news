Shantou University's Alumni Affairs Office and the Alumni Office of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold an alumni career-planning forum from **4 to 6 p.m. on Saturday, June 22** in ACC Meeting Room 5. The event is open to faculty members and students across the university.

Leslie Tsang Raymond, Asia-Pacific human-resources director at Bacardi-Martini Asia Pacific, will open the event with a speech in English. The 13 alumni who took part in a musical in 2005 will introduce their respective fields of work and share reflections on career planning. The program will also include an exchange between faculty members, students and the guests.
