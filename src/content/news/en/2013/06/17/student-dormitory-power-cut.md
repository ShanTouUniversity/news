It is reported that the Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University issued a notice on June 17, 2013, reminding student dormitories with unpaid utility bills from May 2013 or earlier to complete payment. The university will suspend power supply to the affected dormitories from June 18 and restore it after all outstanding charges have been paid.

## Deduction and Restoration Schedule

From June 18 to 20, the university will process two automatic deductions through the campus card system each day. The first deduction will take place at 10:30 a.m., with power expected to be restored between 2:30 and 4:30 p.m. for dormitories that complete payment successfully. The second deduction will take place at 4:30 p.m., with restoration expected between 5:30 and 8:00 p.m. Dormitories that pay after 4:30 p.m. will be scheduled for restoration between 2:30 and 4:30 p.m. the following day.

After June 20, one deduction will be processed at 4:30 p.m. each day. Students concerned should ensure that their campus cards and linked bank accounts have sufficient balances and complete the required account top-up process. Students who have lost their campus cards may set up delegated payment through the utility management system or use the payment channel designated by the university. The Finance Office does not accept cash payments directly.

For specific payment instructions, restoration arrangements and other requirements, please refer to the original notice on the STU OA system.
