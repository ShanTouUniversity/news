Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office will organize a student evaluation of public welfare courses offered in the spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. The evaluation is intended to assess course teaching and support improvements in teaching quality, and covers public welfare courses offered by the relevant teaching units on the main campus.

## Evaluation Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Courses | Public welfare courses offered in the spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year |
| Evaluation period | June 14–28, 2013 |
| Access | The “Course Evaluation” section of the credit system |

Students should log in to the credit system, open “Course Evaluation,” view the public welfare courses awaiting evaluation, and complete the relevant evaluations. The Academic Affairs Office reminds students to finish the process within the specified period. Those who fail to do so must submit a written explanation; otherwise, they will be unable to view their public welfare course grades.
