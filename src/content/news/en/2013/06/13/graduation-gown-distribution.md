It is reported that Shantou University's Resource Management Office has arranged the distribution and collection of academic gowns for the 2013 graduation ceremony. The conferral ceremony will be held on June 28. Each college will handle the collection as a unit before distributing the gowns to its departments and programs.

## Collection Schedule

Gowns will be available from 2:30 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. on June 21 at the auditorium foyer. The collection periods are as follows:

| Time | Colleges |
| --- | --- |
| 2:30—3:30 p.m. | School of Journalism, School of Arts, Graduate School |
| 3:30—4:30 p.m. | School of Liberal Arts, Business School, School of Law |
| 4:30—5:30 p.m. | School of Engineering, School of Science, School of Continuing Education |

Each college should designate a counselor to complete the collection using the college checklist and help maintain order at the venue. Gown sizes will be allocated proportionally according to the available statistics, and the number of gowns assigned to each college will follow the distribution table.

## Return and Attire

Gowns must be returned to the auditorium foyer from 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. on June 28. Colleges are responsible for checking all sizes and returning the gowns through their designated counselors within the stated period. Damaged gowns will incur compensation at the original price. Gowns not returned by 11:00 a.m. on July 2 will be treated as lost and charged at the original price.

For the conferral ceremony, no additional clothing should be worn over the academic gown. The hood should lie flat over the shoulders and be fastened to the second button of the gown. Graduates are advised to wear a white or light-colored shirt underneath; men should wear ties and women may wear bow ties, with dark trousers or dark, plain skirts and dark leather shoes. Before the degree is conferred, the tassel should hang at the middle of the right front side of the academic cap. The Resource Management Office also asked graduates to keep their gowns, hoods and caps clean and undamaged.

Any updates will be released through the university's office automation system. The gown collection checklist, distribution table and attire reference photographs are available in the original OA notice.
