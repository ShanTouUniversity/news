Shantou University will hold a coordination meeting on the departure of the Class of 2013 and new student orientation at **9:00 a.m. on June 17, 2013 (Monday)** in Room 330 of the Administration Center. The meeting will coordinate work related to graduating students' departure and the arrival of new students, and heads of the relevant units are expected to attend on time.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | Coordination meeting on the departure of the Class of 2013 and new student orientation |
| Time | 9:00 a.m., June 17, 2013 (Monday) |
| Venue | Room 330, Administration Center |
| Organizing offices | Party and Government Office; Student Affairs Office |

## Participating Units

Participants will include the Party general branches of all schools, Zhicheng College, and the Party and Government Office, Admissions Office, Student Affairs Office (including the Career Guidance Center and the Residential Student Advisors' Office), Youth League Committee, Organization and United Front Work Office, Publicity Office, Academic Affairs Office, Finance Office, Resource Management Office and its related service units, Security Office, Library, Network and Information Center, English Language Center, Arts Education Center, International Studies Center of the School of Liberal Arts, Computer Teaching Center, Physical Education Department, Alumni Office and Student Affairs Office of the Medical College.

The meeting is jointly organized by the Party and Government Office and the Student Affairs Office. Relevant heads will coordinate the connection and arrangements for graduate departure and new student orientation.
