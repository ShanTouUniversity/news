It is reported that the Arts Education Center of Shantou University has completed interviews for the *Hulusi Performance A* course in the fall semester of the 2013-2014 academic year. The course is scheduled for periods 6–7 on Tuesdays, and the interview results have now been officially released.

According to the announcement from the Cheung Kong Art and Design College and the Arts Education Center, the list of students who passed the interview has been published as an attachment, which applicants can check to see whether they have been admitted.

Selected students are reminded to contact their class representative, Cen WM, promptly after seeing the notice to complete follow-up confirmation and ensure a smooth course registration.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Arts Education Center)
