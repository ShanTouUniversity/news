The 6th Chaoshan Business Forum at Shantou University's Business School, originally scheduled for **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on June 15, 2013**, has been canceled. The Business School issued the notice on June 13, without specifying the reason, and apologized for any inconvenience caused by the cancellation.
