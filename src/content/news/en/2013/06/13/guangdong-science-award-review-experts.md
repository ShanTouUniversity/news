The 2013 review for the Guangdong Science and Technology Awards is scheduled to begin in late June. Shantou University's Research Affairs Office has circulated a notice inviting eligible faculty members to be recommended as review experts.

## Eligibility

Candidates should have strong professional ethics, a rigorous and impartial working style, and an interest in participating in science and technology award reviews. They should also demonstrate academic expertise, policy knowledge, practical experience and sound judgment, as well as familiarity with scientific and technological developments in their fields in China and abroad.

Candidates must hold a senior professional title, be under 65 years old, be in good health, remain active at the forefront of contemporary science and technology, and be engaged in scientific and technological innovation. Priority will be given to recipients of national science and technology awards and principal contributors to projects that have received Guangdong Science and Technology Awards.

## Application Arrangements

Interested faculty members should log in to the Guangdong Science and Technology Affairs Integrated Management System by June 17 to add or update their expert profiles. The required information includes personal details, intellectual property, awards and publications, together with supporting documents. After updating their profiles, applicants should complete the Summary Form for Recommended Review Experts for the Guangdong Science and Technology Awards and email it to ygluo@stu.edu.cn for submission by the Research Affairs Office.

For inquiries, contact Luo Yingguang at the Research Affairs Office at 86503271.
