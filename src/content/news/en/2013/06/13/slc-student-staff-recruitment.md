Shantou University's Learning Support Center (SLC) is recruiting three undergraduate student staff members to support its daily operations during the 2013–2014 academic year. According to the notice circulated by the Student Affairs Office, eligible students may apply through the university's work-study application system by 10:00 p.m. on June 19.

## Position Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Positions | 3 |
| Eligible applicants | Economically disadvantaged undergraduates at STU's main campus |
| Service period | 2013–2014 academic year; specific shifts to be discussed |
| Compensation | Paid according to the university's work-study position standards |
| Application deadline | Wednesday, June 19, 2013, at 10:00 p.m. |

Applicants should have a strong service orientation, a sense of responsibility, a conscientious and friendly attitude, and basic planning and organizational skills. The student staff members will handle front-desk shifts and reception, assist with SLC administration and service-quality supervision, help promote SLC services, and support temporary assignments.

If the number of applications is high, SLC will select candidates for interviews based on the stated requirements. The interview list is expected to be released on June 20. Applicants who are not selected for an interview will not be notified separately.

## Application and Contact

Interested students should log in to the Student Work-Study Application System at http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn, submit the application form, and complete all required information. SLC can be reached at 86502250, slc@stu.edu.cn, or http://slc.stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
