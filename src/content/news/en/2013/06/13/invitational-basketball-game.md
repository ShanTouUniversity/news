The Jimei University basketball team will visit Shantou University on June 15 for an invitational game against STU's Wanbian King Kong team, according to information released by the university. The game will be held at the STU Gymnasium and will be free to attend. Faculty and students are welcome to watch, subject to the gymnasium's management rules and orderly entry arrangements.

## Game Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Match | Jimei University basketball team vs. STU Wanbian King Kong team |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., Saturday, June 15, 2013 |
| Venue | STU Gymnasium |
| Admission | Free |
| Organizing department | Sports Department |

The Sports Department is also recruiting 10 students to work at the game, assisting with venue management and maintaining order. Applicants should complete the form in the OA attachment and email it to the Sports Department at o_tyb@stu.edu.cn by the end of the workday on June 14. Students who take part in the volunteer work will receive a Wanbian King Kong team T-shirt; arrangements are subject to the department's notice.

For inquiries, contact 86503727 or the campus short number 883727. The registration form and event attachment are available on the OA system.
