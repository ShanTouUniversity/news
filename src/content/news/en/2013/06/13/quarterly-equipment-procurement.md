Shantou University has opened submissions for its third-quarter 2013 equipment procurement plans. According to the university's interim measures for government procurement, all units must prepare their plans and submit both hard-copy and electronic materials to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by June 28. Late submissions will be deferred to the fourth quarter.

## Submission Requirements by Value

For plans totaling no more than RMB 20,000, the relevant unit should place the order through the university equipment bidding website. Lenovo, HP, Dell and Apple computers will continue to be purchased through an agreement-based supply arrangement; these requests must include the plan form and the Computer Purchase Application Registration Form.

Plans totaling more than RMB 20,000 must include the plan form and plan document, and must complete the required approvals under the university's rules on financial approval authority before submission. Equipment costing more than RMB 100,000 per unit also requires a feasibility report for the purchase of large-scale equipment.

Requests involving computers must include the Computer Purchase Application Registration Form. Requests involving air conditioners must state the installation location and the condition of the existing air conditioner in the remarks section of the plan form. Units must complete all fields, including the equipment code, name, model and specifications, technical parameters and budget. Budgets should include taxes, transportation, warranty and after-sales service costs, and brands should generally not be specified.

## Submission Information

Materials should be submitted to the Equipment and Materials Management Office, formerly located in the reception room. The office can be reached at 86502558, through Ms. Huang, or by email at zxhuang@stu.edu.cn.
