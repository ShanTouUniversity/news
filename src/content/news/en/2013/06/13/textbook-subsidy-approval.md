Shantou University Academic Affairs has announced its first-half 2013 textbook development and publication subsidy project. Following an application by the textbook author or chief editor, a college recommendation and an expert review organized by Academic Affairs, the textbook *Chaoshan Merchant Culture and Modern Enterprise Management* was approved for inclusion in the project.

The review was conducted under the university's Measures for Textbook Development and Publication Subsidies to support textbook development and publication at the university. Complete project information is provided in the original Academic Affairs OA attachment.
