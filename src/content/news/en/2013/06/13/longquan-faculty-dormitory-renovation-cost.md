Shantou University's Resource Management Office has published a preliminary estimate for additional work related to the renovation of five Type C faculty dormitory buildings at Longquan Garden. The project began on October 29, 2012, and the additional items arose from site conditions and design adjustments identified during construction.

The additional work mainly covers wall and floor repairs, indoor and outdoor waterproofing and flooring, kitchen and bathroom plumbing adjustments, power and low-voltage room facilities, doors and windows, railings and other minor repairs. Demolition, removal and rebuilding associated with room layout changes and loosened walls caused by some residents' secondary renovations are also included.

| Cost range | The additional cost is below the 10% limit set by the university |

| Offices receiving feedback | Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office |

Written objections or other comments on the preliminary additional-cost estimate may be submitted to the Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office by the deadline.
