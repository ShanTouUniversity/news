According to a notice circulated by Shantou University's Business School, Bank of China Shantou Branch is recruiting 10 talent-reserve interns from full-time STU undergraduate and graduate students. The internship will run from July 1 to August 31, 2013, and interns are generally expected to complete the full placement period.

## Positions and Schedule

Interns will assist with organizing, entering, and maintaining non-confidential records at the Bank of China Shantou Branch headquarters and its affiliated offices. They must comply with the bank's information confidentiality requirements. Working hours are 8:30 a.m. to noon and 2:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday through Friday. Selected interns will sign an internship agreement with the branch, complete pre-placement and on-the-job training, and take part in training, routine, and end-of-placement assessments.

The bank will assign interns to specific offices according to their qualifications and staffing needs, and may adjust placements as work requirements change. The internship may be terminated if an intern is found unsuitable for the position or violates the bank's management rules.

## Support and Prospects

Interns will receive a daily allowance of RMB 40, with individual income tax withheld by Bank of China Shantou Branch as required. The host office will provide lunch, and the branch will purchase group personal accident insurance for the interns. Those who perform well in the assessments may enter the branch's reserve talent pool for campus recruitment and may receive priority consideration under equal conditions after meeting the requirements of Bank of China's campus recruitment process and completing the relevant procedures.

## Application Information

Applicants must submit the required materials through the Business School by 5:00 p.m. on June 21, 2013. Pre-placement training is scheduled for 3:00 p.m. on June 24 in the Business School seminar room on the second floor of the Wenxi Building. Bank of China Shantou Branch and the Business School will separately notify selected applicants of the detailed training and placement arrangements.
