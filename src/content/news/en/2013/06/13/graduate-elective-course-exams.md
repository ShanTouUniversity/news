Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has announced the final examination arrangements for graduate students taking Education and Psychology as electives. Both examinations will be closed-book, and students enrolled in the courses must attend at the designated time and venue.

## Examination Arrangements

| Course | Time | Venue | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Education | 4:00–6:00 p.m., June 24, 2013 (Monday) | Room E303 | Closed-book |
| Psychology | 4:00–6:00 p.m., June 19, 2013 (Wednesday) | Room 503, D Building | Closed-book |

The Academic Affairs Office reminds candidates to bring their student ID cards. Invigilators will arrange seating according to the examination requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
