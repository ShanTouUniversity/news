Shantou University's Law School will host the public lecture **“Smiling Taiwan: Three Things You May Not Know” at 7:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 15, 2013**, in Room 213 of the Law School. The event is open to students and faculty across the university.

## Lecture Information

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Smiling Taiwan: Three Things You May Not Know |
| Speaker | Lin Shixun, Executive Director of the Sino-Europe International Education Group |
| Language | Mandarin Chinese |
| Time | 7:00 p.m., June 15, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, Law School |
| Organizer | Shantou University Law School |

The lecture will discuss Taiwan's society and culture, cross-Strait higher-education exchange, Taiwan's policy of opening employment to mainland professionals, and travel and food culture in Taiwan. Lin Shixun has worked for more than two decades in cross-Strait higher-education development and exchange, and has served as a guest professor at National Cheng Kung University's Cultural and Creative Center, general manager of the Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan International Study Center, executive director of the Sino-Europe International Education Center, and publisher and executive director of Taiwan's *CHINA GO* magazine.
