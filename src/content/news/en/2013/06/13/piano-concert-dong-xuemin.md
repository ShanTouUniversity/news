The Arts Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design will host a piano concert on June 14 to showcase the results of its piano teaching and enrich campus cultural life, according to information released by the university. Dr. Dong Xuemin and five students will perform for the university community.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., Friday, June 14, 2013 |
| Venue | Music Rehearsal Hall, first floor, Arts Education Center |
| Performers | Dr. Dong Xuemin and five students |
| Audience | University faculty and students |

The event information was released by the Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design and the Arts Education Center. The repertoire and biographies of the student performers are available in the OA attachments.
