It is reported that Shantou University's School of Science will host the academic lecture **“The Beauty of Groups and Symmetry: Starting with Wallpaper Patterns”** from 7:00 to 8:00 p.m. on Monday, June 17, 2013, in Room D201. The speaker will be Professor Peter J. Webb of the Department of Mathematics at the University of Minnesota.

## Lecture Information

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The Beauty of Groups and Symmetry: Starting with Wallpaper Patterns |
| Speaker | Professor Peter J. Webb, University of Minnesota |
| Time | 7:00–8:00 p.m., June 17, 2013 |
| Venue | Room D201 |
| Organizer | Shantou University School of Science |

Beginning with the artwork of Dutch artist M. C. Escher, the lecture will examine how different forms of symmetry can combine to create intricate patterns. Professor Webb will introduce groups as an abstract mathematical structure for studying symmetry, and explain how group theory can be used to understand and classify wallpaper patterns.

Professor Webb received his PhD from the University of London in 1979, worked in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Manchester from 1980 to 1987, and became a professor in the University of Minnesota's Department of Mathematics in 1988. His research focuses on representations and cohomology of finite groups, and he is a recipient of the London Mathematical Society's Junior Whitehead Prize. The University of Minnesota's mathematics department has more than 70 full-time faculty members, and the National Science Foundation's Institute for Mathematics and its Applications (IMA) is based there.
