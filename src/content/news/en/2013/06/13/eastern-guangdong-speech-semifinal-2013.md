It is reported that the eighth-group semifinal of the Guangdong university-student speech competition themed **“Study and Implement the Spirit of the 18th National Congress and Let Youth Shine Brilliantly”** will be held at **7:20 p.m. on June 14, 2013**, in the Science and Technology Lecture Hall of Shantou University. Entrusted by the relevant higher-level organizations, STU will host the event, where student contestants from universities in eastern Guangdong will deliver speeches on the theme.

The competition is organized by the Organization Department of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China, the Education Working Committee of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China and the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League. The Organization and United Front Work Department of the STU Party Committee, the Office of Student Affairs and the STU Committee of the Communist Youth League will undertake the event, with the STU Student Societies Federation as co-organizer. The participating institutions are Shantou University, Hanshan Normal University, Jiaying University, Huizhou University, Heyuan Polytechnic, Shanwei Polytechnic, Jieyang Polytechnic, Chaoshan Vocational and Technical College and Huizhou Economics and Polytechnic College.

The event supports the “two studies” campaign, which calls on university students to study the report of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China and the Party Constitution, while showcasing the vitality of young people in Guangdong. **Two contestants** will be selected to advance to the Guangdong provincial semifinal. The remaining contestants will receive certificates of excellence from the organizers. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
