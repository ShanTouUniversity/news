Shantou University's Yangtze College of Journalism and Communication will host the media salon **“I Will Never Watch Reality Shows Again” at 7:30 p.m. on Friday, June 14, 2013**, in the Library Lecture Hall. The speaker will be Xia Jing, director of the program department of the foreign-language channel at Shanghai Oriental Media Group.

## Salon Information

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | I Will Never Watch Reality Shows Again |
| Speaker | Xia Jing, director of the program department of Shanghai Oriental Media Group's foreign-language channel |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., June 14, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Shantou University Yangtze College of Journalism and Communication |

Shanghai Oriental Media Group Co., Ltd. was formerly known as Shanghai Media Group, established in August 2001 through the integration of Shanghai Television, Shanghai Oriental Television, Shanghai Cable Television, Shanghai People's Radio and Shanghai Oriental Radio. The salon will use reality television as its subject and provide an opportunity for media-related discussion on campus.
