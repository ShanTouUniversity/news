It is reported that Shantou University will test the access control system at the New Administration Center on June 16. The test is scheduled for Sunday noon, and power and network services inside the center will be temporarily interrupted. Relevant personnel are advised to plan their work in advance.

## Test Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Test time | June 16, 2013 (Sunday), 12:30-13:00 |
| Test subject | Access control system at the New Administration Center |
| Impact | Temporary interruption of power and network services inside the center |

The university advises relevant personnel to adjust their work arrangements around the test period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office)
