Shantou University Law School recently completed its selection of a team for the Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition and announced the results on June 14.

The selection was conducted through interviews and evaluation by a Law School selection panel. Eight students were selected as members of the competition team, while four additional students will join the Jessup team as research assistants. In accordance with privacy requirements, the students' individual names are not published here.

The Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition is an international law advocacy competition for students. Teams generally prepare by researching legal issues, developing legal arguments and practicing oral pleadings. The formation of this team will support related practical legal education and the development of students' legal skills at the Law School.
