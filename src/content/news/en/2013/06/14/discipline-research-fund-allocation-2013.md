It is reported that Shantou University announced the allocation plan for its 2013 Discipline Development and Research Enhancement Special Funds on June 14, 2013. The plan was formed after applications from individual disciplines and review by the Research Committee. It covers research platform operations, discipline development and research enhancement, and reserved research enhancement funds.

## Research Platform Operations

The university allocated funds to research platforms specifically to establish open funds at provincial-level research platforms:

| No. | Platform | Amount (10,000 yuan) |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Guangdong Key Laboratory of Marine Biotechnology | 10 |
| 2 | Guangdong Key Laboratory of Digital Signal and Image Processing Technology | 10 |
| 3 | Guangdong Key Laboratory of Structure and Wind Tunnel for Universities | 6 |
| 4 | Shantou University Institute of Local Government Development | 5 |
| 5 | Shantou University Institute for Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation | 5 |

## Discipline Development and Research Enhancement

These funds were allocated to specific disciplines and are subject to approval by the discipline heads. They are mainly intended for visits and lectures by distinguished external experts, as well as preliminary costs for recruiting academic talent. Two-thirds of the total allocation will be disbursed initially.

## Reserved Research Enhancement Funds

The reserved funds are primarily intended for humanities and social sciences disciplines. They support monograph publication, academic conferences or forums, and subsidies for participation in academic conferences. The Research Office will coordinate the funds and approve their use under the Interim Measures for the Reserved Funds of Shantou University's Discipline Development and Research Enhancement Special Funds.
