It is reported that Shantou University's College of Cheung Kong Art and Design, together with the Student Affairs Office, has launched a global warming public-service poster competition. The competition invites faculty and students to use original visual works to learn about Antarctica and marine ecosystems and to engage with public issues including global warming and environmental pollution.

## Background

Shantou University launched an Antarctic expedition project in December 2012 and has since organized educational activities centered on polar exploration, including a polar expedition proposal competition, a general education course, lecture series and exhibitions. As part of these campus cultural activities, the College of Cheung Kong Art and Design also planned exhibitions at the library, Administrative Center and Art Museum featuring information on the polar regions, oceans, exploration and culture, an exhibition of marine life and exploration equipment, and the poster works from this competition.

The competition aims to provide students with a platform to showcase original design and promote original campus culture. The accompanying exhibition will also encourage the campus community to protect the environment and deepen its understanding of Antarctica and the marine environment.

## Competition Information

Details are available on the official competition website (http://www.ckad.stu.edu.cn/qqbn2013). The official Sina Weibo account is “@2013全球变暖海报设计比赛”. Inquiries may be sent to o_bm@stu.edu.cn.

The competition is organized by Shantou University's College of Cheung Kong Art and Design and Student Affairs Office, co-organized by Hong Kong Wildside Limited, and supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation.
