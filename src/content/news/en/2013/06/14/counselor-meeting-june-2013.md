According to a notice issued by the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University, the university will hold its third counselor meeting of 2013 at 3:00 p.m. on Monday, June 17, in Room 330 of the Administration Center.

The meeting is intended for all counselors and relevant personnel in the student affairs system. Attendees are asked to arrange their work in advance and arrive on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
