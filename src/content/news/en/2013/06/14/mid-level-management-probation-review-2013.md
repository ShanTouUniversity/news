According to the Organization and United Front Work Department of Shantou University, the university will conduct probation-completion reviews for mid-level management personnel appointed since November 2012 whose probationary periods have ended. The reviews will follow the *Measures for Probation-Completion Assessment and Promotion Appointments of Management Personnel* (STU Party Committee Document No. 7, 2011).

Democratic assessments will be conducted during the review period. The relevant personnel's duty reports will also be displayed on the university's internal office-information network, allowing university units and faculty and students to learn about the review process. Units or individuals with comments may submit written feedback to the Organization and United Front Work Department by **June 20, 2013**. Submissions from units must bear an official seal, while individual submissions must include the sender's real name.
