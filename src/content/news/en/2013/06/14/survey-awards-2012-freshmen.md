Shantou University's Admissions Office has announced the appreciation and prize arrangements for participants in the survey of the 2012 freshman cohort. The survey received valid responses from 1,050 students.

As a token of appreciation, the Admissions Office will distribute one STU scenic postcard to each participant through the Youth League committees of the relevant schools. In addition, 180 students were selected at random to receive an STU scenic card-style USB drive, an “Because of You” T-shirt, or an “Because of You” polo shirt.

Prize winners may collect their prizes from Room 107 on the first floor of the New Administration Center between 9:00 a.m. and 12:00 noon from June 17 to 20. They should bring their own campus cards. Clothing sizes are limited and will be selected on a first-come, first-served basis.

The winner and participant lists are included in the notice attachment. Student personal information is not reproduced in this article; the attachment is available through the original notice on the STU OA system.
