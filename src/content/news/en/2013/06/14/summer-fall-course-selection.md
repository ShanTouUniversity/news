Shantou University's Office of Academic Affairs announced on June 14 that registration had opened for guided courses in the 2013-2014 summer and fall semesters. Public-interest courses are excluded from this arrangement, and relevant students are required to register through the credit-based academic system.

Registration will close on June 28. After that date, the status of guided-course sections in the credit-based system will be changed to closed, and registration will generally no longer be accepted. Courses for which an offering unit has issued a separate arrangement will follow that arrangement.

The notice attachment lists the course titles, eligible majors and year groups, and registration method for each guided course. Depending on course requirements, students may select a topic from options provided by their school or department, create and submit a topic themselves, or have an academic affairs officer at their school or department enter the topic and student information.

For courses that allow students to create and submit their own topics, students must accurately enter the topic title or internship organization and other relevant information. If the specific title is not yet available, they may temporarily enter “To be determined,” but the information must be updated before the instructor assesses the student's grade.

The course list and detailed registration requirements are provided in the attachment to the OA notice. The attachment is available through the original notice on the STU OA system.
