It is reported that the Publicity Department of the Party Committee issued a notice on June 14, 2013, to hold a Party Central Group study session on June 18.

## Session Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00, June 18, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administration Center |
| Topic 1 | Study guidance on the spirit of the 21st National Party Building Conference — Speaker: Zeng Dabin, Standing Committee Member of the University Party Committee and Head of the Organization and United Front Work Department |
| Topic 2 | Guidance on the Eight Regulations — Speaker: Sun Fenqiang, Deputy Secretary of the Discipline Inspection Commission |
| Topic 3 | Guidance on “Opinions on Strengthening and Improving Ideological and Political Work for Young Teachers in Universities” — Speaker: Bai Youjin, Head of the Publicity Department |
| Participants | Members of the University Party Committee, secretaries of general Party branches and directly affiliated branches, and heads of the Discipline Inspection Commission, President’s Office, Organization and United Front Work Department, Publicity Department, News Center and Student Affairs Office, as well as the Director of the Department of Social Sciences, Vice Chair of the University Trade Union and Secretary of the Youth League Committee |
| Issued by | Publicity Department of the Party Committee |

Relevant personnel are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department of the Party Committee)
