It is reported that the Arts Education Center of Shantou University completed interviews for the piano course in the fall semester of the 2013-2014 academic year and released the results on June 14.

The Cheung Kong Art and Design College and the Arts Education Center stated that the list of students who passed the interview was issued as an attachment to the notice. To protect student privacy, individual names are not published here. Relevant students may consult the attachment on the OA system to confirm their results.

Students who passed the interview are required to gather at the Music Rehearsal Hall on the first floor of the Arts Education Center at 19:00 on June 14 to discuss follow-up course matters.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong Art and Design College and Arts Education Center)
