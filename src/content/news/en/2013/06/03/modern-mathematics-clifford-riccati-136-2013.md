The Science School of Shantou University will hold the 136th lecture in its Modern Mathematics series on **June 5, 2013**. Professor Ren Guangbin of the University of Science and Technology of China and Professor Chen Zongxuan of South China Normal University will give consecutive presentations in the Mathematics Department Conference Room on the fourth floor of Gongbei Building.

## Schedule

| Time | Speaker and institution | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:30 a.m. | Professor Ren Guangbin, University of Science and Technology of China | *Several Clifford analysis* |
| 11:20 a.m. | Professor Chen Zongxuan, South China Normal University | Properties of Solutions to Complex-Domain Difference Riccati Equations |

Ren Guangbin's presentation will introduce several Clifford analysis. The abstract describes it as an extension of complex analysis and quaternionic analysis. It states that the presentation establishes a several-Clifford version of the Bochner-Martinelli formula, unifying the Cauchy integral formula for one complex variable with corresponding formulas for several complex variables and several quaternionic variables. It will also discuss Hartogs phenomena in several Clifford analysis, under which compact singularities can be removed as in several complex variables.

Chen Zongxuan's presentation will examine solutions to complex-domain difference Riccati equations, including the growth of meromorphic solutions, properties of differences of meromorphic solutions, and families of solutions with parameters.

## Speaker Profiles

Ren Guangbin is a professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Science and Technology of China. The notice states that he was selected for the Ministry of Education's New Century Excellent Talents Support Program in 2005, received the Baosteel Excellent Teacher Award in 2012, and has led four National Natural Science Foundation of China projects. It also reports four years of visiting-scholar, postdoctoral and research-fellow work in Europe and more than 50 papers in journals including *J. Reine Angew. Math.* and *J. Approximation Theory*. His listed research areas are Clifford analysis, function spaces and operator theory, and several complex variables.

Chen Zongxuan is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Mathematical Sciences of South China Normal University. The notice states that he received a State Council government special allowance in 1997 and was named a National Model Teacher in 2001. His listed research areas are complex analysis, complex-domain difference equations and complex-domain differential equations.
