The Organization and United Front Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee issued arrangements for the **July 1** commemorative activities on **June 3, 2013**. The university will not hold a university-wide event during this year's July 1 period. Instead, each unit will organize theme-day activities in connection with study of the spirit of the 18th CPC National Congress and the “Mass Line: Everyone Discusses” campaign, and will arrange oath ceremonies for new Party members.

The arrangements apply to the CPC Party Committee of the Medical College, the Party general branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches. Units should inform the Organization and United Front Department of their activity times in advance. The department will provide a Party flag and the oath text and arrange for department leaders to attend the activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee)
