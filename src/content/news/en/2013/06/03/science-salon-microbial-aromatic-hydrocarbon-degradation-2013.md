The College of Science at Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture at **10:00 a.m. on Wednesday, June 5, 2013**, in Science and Technology Center Room 205. Zhou Ningyi, a researcher at the Wuhan Institute of Virology of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, will present “Microbial Degradation and Metabolism of Aromatic Hydrocarbons.” Faculty members and students interested in the topic are welcome to attend.

## About the Speaker

Zhou earned a bachelor's degree in microbiology from Wuhan University and a doctorate in microbiology from Imperial College London. He conducted postdoctoral research at Imperial College London and Bangor University in Wales. According to the original notice, he was selected for the Chinese Academy of Sciences Hundred Talents Program and served as director of the CAS Key Laboratory of Agricultural and Environmental Microbiology, chair of the Wuhan Institute of Virology Degree Evaluation Committee, director of its Applied and Environmental Microbiology Research Center, leader of its environmental microbiology research group and a member of the institute's Academic Committee.

He also served as deputy chair of the Environmental Microbiology Committee of the Chinese Society for Microbiology and the Industrial and Environmental Biotechnology Committee of the Chinese Society of Bioengineering. His editorial roles included deputy editor-in-chief of *Microbiology China*, an editor of *Acta Microbiologica Sinica* and board memberships for several international microbiology journals. His research has focused on the microbial degradation and metabolism of aromatic-hydrocarbon pollutants and the bioremediation of contaminated soil. The notice reports more than 50 international journal papers, six granted invention patents and honors including a State Council special government allowance, Wuhan Outstanding Scientific and Technological Worker, the Chinese Academy of Sciences Zhu Li Yuehua Outstanding Teacher Award and a second-prize Hubei Natural Science Award.
