The Business School of Shantou University will hold a resume-writing lecture from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on June 5, 2013**, in E Classroom 101. Guest speaker Su Junzhi will discuss techniques and methods for preparing resumes.

The lecture is primarily intended for first-, second- and third-year Business School students. Students from other schools are also welcome.
