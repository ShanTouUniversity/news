The CPC General Branch Committee of Shantou University Law School published a public notice on **June 3, 2013**, concerning proposed awardees for Outstanding Party-Affairs Workers and Outstanding Communist Party Members for the 2012–2013 academic year. The notice said that the school's Party branches and members had worked toward building service-oriented and learning-oriented Party organizations and had produced examples of outstanding service through the university Party Committee's “Four Top-Ten” initiative.

Following democratic evaluations by the Party branches, the Law School CPC General Branch Committee proposed the following recognitions:

| Proposed recognition | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Party-Affairs Workers | 6 |
| Outstanding Communist Party Members | 22 students |

Comments may be submitted in writing to the Law School CPC General Branch Committee by **June 7, 2013**. Feedback must be signed with the commenter's real name. The telephone number is `86502215`. The proposed list includes student Party members' names and major or class information, which this article does not reproduce.
