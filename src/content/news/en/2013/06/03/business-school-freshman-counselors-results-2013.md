The Youth League Committee of Shantou University's Business School announced the selection results for extracurricular counselors for the 2013 freshman cohort on **June 3, 2013**. Nearly 200 students applied. The Business School's five major student organizations first conducted internal assessments and recommendations at a rate of **12 percent of each organization's membership**, followed by interviews. A total of 60 students were selected as extracurricular counselors for the incoming freshman cohort.

The selected group consisted of four students from the 2011 cohort and 56 from the 2012 cohort. To protect students' personal information, the names of the selected students are not reproduced here.

## Training and Orientation Preparation

The selected students were asked to return to campus by **August 18**. The school planned to conduct training and preparation activities from **August 19** until the 2013 freshman cohort arrived, supporting the new students as they became familiar with the Business School and adjusted to university life. Students unable to return early because of unexpected commitments were asked to submit a withdrawal request to a Youth League teacher in advance.
