Changjiang College of Arts and Design will hold the “Printmaking and Teaching Works Exhibition by Chen Qisheng and Lin Dexiong” at its Art Museum from **June 4 to 14, 2013**. The opening ceremony is scheduled for **10:00 a.m. on June 4**. Organized by Changjiang College of Arts and Design, the exhibition is open to faculty members and students across the university.

## Featured Faculty Artists

**Chen Qisheng**, born in Raoping, Guangdong, in 1966, graduated from Shantou University's Art and Design Department in 1988 and stayed at the university. He later completed graduate-level color studies at the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts. Chen is a National Second-Class Artist and a member of the China Artists Association. He serves as deputy director of the Experimental Teaching Demonstration Center and teaches printmaking at Changjiang College of Arts and Design; he is also deputy chair of the Printmaking Art Committee of the Shantou Artists Association. His prints have appeared in national printmaking exhibitions and contemporary academic printmaking exhibitions, and related works have been collected by institutions including the China Sports Museum. His works and papers have also been published in professional art journals.

**Lin Dexiong** is a lecturer at Changjiang College of Arts and Design, a member of the China Artists Association, a member of the Printmaking Art Committee of the Guangdong Artists Association and a contracted artist of the Guangdong Painting Academy. He served as a judge for the fourth through sixth Guangdong Printmaking Exhibitions. Lin has participated in national printmaking and fine-arts exhibitions, the Guanlan International Print Biennial and other exhibitions. His awards include gold, silver and other prizes at Guangdong and national printmaking exhibitions; his works are held by institutions including the National Art Museum of China and Zhejiang Art Museum.

The exhibition will present printmaking creations and teaching works by the two faculty artists. Faculty members and students are welcome to visit.
