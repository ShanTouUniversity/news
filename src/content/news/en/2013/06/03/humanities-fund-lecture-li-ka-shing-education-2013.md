The Research Office and Social Sciences Department will host a University Humanities Research Fund academic lecture at **3:00 p.m. on June 5, 2013**, in the Social Sciences Department conference room at Shantou University. Bai Youjin will discuss “Mr. Li Ka-shing's Educational Thought and Shantou University's Distinctive Development.” Faculty members and students across the university are welcome to attend.
