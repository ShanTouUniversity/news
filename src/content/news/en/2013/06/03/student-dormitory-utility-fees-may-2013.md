The Property and Resources Management Office of Shantou University published the review and payment arrangements for **May 2013 student-dormitory utility bills** on **June 3, 2013**. The latest meter reading was taken on **May 31**, following the previous reading on **April 30**. Students could check their individual bills through the Shantou University utility-fee management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/> using their campus-network accounts.

## Review and Payment Schedule

| Stage | Time or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Bill review and objection requests | Working hours, June 3-7 |
| Campus-card deductions begin | June 8, after the objection period if no objections are raised |
| Regular payment windows | June 8-9 and June 13-14 |
| Overdue handling | From June 17 under university regulations |

Students who found errors or had questions could complete and sign a Utility-Fee Review Application Form and submit it to the Utility Center during the review period. The center is beside the Science and Technology Building and can be reached at `86502489`. Questions about a mismatch between accommodation information and student records could be directed to the Counselor Office at `86503355`, located in Building B, Room 106.

The Network Center would collect the fees through the campus card; its telephone number was `86503545`. Students whose campus-card balances were insufficient were advised to top up promptly. Objections raised after the review period would require **three to five working days** to process if verification showed that a correction was necessary.

The XLS attachment distributed with the notice contains utility-bill details and links between individual bills, accommodation information and accounts. Specific amounts, rooms and student records are not reproduced here. The complete details and review form are available in the original OA attachment.
