The Art Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design announced the audition arrangements for its piano courses in the fall semester of the 2013-2014 academic year on **June 3, 2013**. The three courses are open to students across the university, run from Weeks 1 to 16, are taught by Dong Xuemin and meet in Music Classroom (2), STU202.

## Course Schedule

| Class number | Course | Credits | Class time |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 62480 | [AED0004] Piano Art | 1.0 | Monday, Periods 3-4 |
| 62482 | [AED0004] Piano Art | 1.0 | Tuesday, Periods 3-4 |
| 62483 | [AED0004] Piano Art (Advanced Class) | 2.0 | Monday, Periods A-B |

Students interested in taking a course may attend the audition. The notice states that applicants may participate regardless of whether they have prior experience. The audition will include staff notation and rhythm tests.

## Audition Arrangements

- **Time:** 8:00 p.m. on Sunday, June 9, 2013
- **Venue:** Music Rehearsal Hall 101, Art Education Center Building

The class numbers, instructor and classroom information come from the course attachment distributed with the OA notice. Specific enrollment and audition matters are subject to the original notice from the Art Education Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center)
