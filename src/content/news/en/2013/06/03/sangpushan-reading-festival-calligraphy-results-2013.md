The “Ancient Ink, Fragrant Books” soft-brush calligraphy competition of the 9th Shantou University Sangpushan Reading Festival has concluded. The organizers announced the results on **June 3, 2013**.

The competition recognized 10 entries in total, distributed as follows:

| Award | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 1 |
| Second prize | 2 |
| Third prize | 3 |
| Merit award | 4 |

The names, majors and academic years of the recognized students are not reproduced here. The notice was jointly issued by the Shantou University Youth League Committee, Shantou University Library and Shantou University Student Societies Federation.
