Shantou University's Business School opened its selection of Outstanding Graduates of the Class of 2013 on **June 3, 2013**, establishing an Outstanding Graduate Scholarship to recognize graduates with strong academic and overall performance and encourage students to strive for excellence. Eligible graduates had to submit their applications by **June 10**; late applications would be treated as forfeiture.

## Selection Criteria

| Category | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Overall performance | All-round development in moral, academic and physical terms, with excellent grades |
| Scholarship record | Third-class scholarship or better for three consecutive years, including one first-class year or two second-class years |
| Graduation thesis | Graded as excellent |
| Campus involvement | Served as a student cadre at college level or above, including a class cadre, and participated in volunteer or public-welfare activities |

Applicants were required to satisfy the selection conditions listed in the notice. Eligible Class-of-2013 graduates were asked to complete the application form and send it to the Business School Youth League Committee's official email address, `o_stw@stu.edu.cn`, by the deadline; failure to apply on time would be treated as withdrawal.

The application form distributed with the notice requests personal information including names, student numbers, majors, GPA, repeat-course status, scholarship records, thesis grades, leadership experience and volunteer-service history. The form and individual application materials are not reproduced here.
