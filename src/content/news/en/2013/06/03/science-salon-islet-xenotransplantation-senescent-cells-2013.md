The College of Science at Shantou University will hold two Science Salon talks from **9:00 to 10:00 a.m. on Friday, June 7, 2013**, in the auditorium of the Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research. The program will cover “Porcine Islet Xenotransplantation” and “Transfer of bio-macromolecules and organelles rejuvenates senescent cells.”

## Lecture Information

| Talk | Speaker information |
| --- | --- |

| Time | 9:00-10:00 a.m. on Friday, June 7, 2013 |
| Venue | Auditorium, Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research |

## About the Speaker

The original notice identifies the first speaker as “Dr. Gina Raya,” while the attached curriculum vitae gives the name as “Gina R. Rayat.” The CV identifies her as an associate professor in the Department of Surgery at the Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry, University of Alberta. At the time, she also served as director of the university's Surgical-Medical Research Institute, director of the Pancreatic Islet Isolation and Transplantation Core Laboratory at the Alberta Diabetes Institute and associate director of graduate education in the Department of Surgery.

Gina R. Rayat earned a bachelor's degree in molecular biology from the University of Winnipeg, a master's degree in transplantation immunology from the University of Manitoba and a doctorate in diabetes from the University of Alberta. She conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Alberta and the University of Colorado Health Sciences Center. Her research interests include transplantation immunology, autoimmunity, cellular immunology and islet xenotransplantation, with current work covering type 1 diabetes, islet isolation and transplantation and transplant tolerance. The attached CV reports 40 journal publications and reviewing or editorial work for several professional journals.

The second talk will examine the transfer of biomacromolecules and organelles as a research approach to rejuvenating senescent cells. Faculty members and students interested in the talks are welcome to attend.
