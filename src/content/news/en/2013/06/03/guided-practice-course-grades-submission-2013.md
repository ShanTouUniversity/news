The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on **June 3, 2013**, requiring teaching units to complete grade entry and submission for guided-practice courses in accordance with the *Shantou University Course Assessment Management Measures* (Document No. 汕大发〔2007〕78号).

The notice covers guided-practice courses offered in **Spring 2013 and earlier semesters** for which grades have not yet been submitted. Relevant instructors must enter and submit the grades for the student projects they supervise by **Friday of Week 18** of the semester.

When a student project has multiple instructors, the instructors should consult one another and designate one instructor to handle grade entry and submission. If a course spans semesters and cannot yet have its grades entered and submitted during the current semester, the offering unit must provide the Academic Affairs Office with a written explanation using the attached template.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The cross-semester-course explanation template contains course and student academic-information fields. Please consult the original OA system for the attachment; it is not reproduced here.
