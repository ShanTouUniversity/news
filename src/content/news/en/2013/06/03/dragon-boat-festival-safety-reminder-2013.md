The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a Dragon Boat Festival holiday safety reminder on **June 3, 2013**. Under the arrangements from the Party and Administration Office, the university holiday period would run from **June 10 to 12**. As the period fell during the flood season, the office reminded students to watch for heavy rain, severe convective weather, hail, lightning, flooding and landslides.

## Transport and Travel

During lightning, strong winds or hail, students should move into a safe building, stay away from trees, billboards and temporary structures, avoid umbrellas with metal shafts and keep away from overhead metal objects. The notice also advised students traveling during the crowded and busy holiday period to buy tickets at legitimate passenger stations, follow traffic regulations, and refuse unlicensed, unregistered or overloaded vehicles. Driving after drinking was prohibited.

Students traveling outside the campus should follow safety guidance from their destinations, choose regulated travel companies and eat at establishments with reliable food-hygiene conditions. Those taking part in hiking, rock climbing or outdoor expeditions should follow local safety instructions and stay out of prohibited areas. Students should return to campus on schedule, secure cash and valuables before leaving, tell family members, friends or teachers about their plans, and travel with companions when possible.

## Campus and Health Safety

Students remaining on campus should follow university schedules and safety-management rules. They should not accommodate off-campus visitors or people of the opposite sex in dormitories, or allow strangers to enter. Open flames and high-power appliances such as rapid water heaters were prohibited in student dormitories. Students should switch off appliances and power before leaving and should not block fire-evacuation routes.

The notice advised students not to fish or swim in the university reservoir, lakes, beaches or other dangerous waters. On-campus recreational activities should be conducted only under safe conditions. Students should not burn candles, set off fireworks, use fire in no-fire zones, carry open flames into the hills or barbecue. The office also encouraged moderate exercise, regular handwashing and avoiding close contact with poultry during the holiday.

Abnormal situations should be reported promptly. The Student Community Management Service Center can be reached at **86502661**; the campus emergency and police assistance number is **8290 4110**.
