Zhicheng Residential College of Shantou University released Issue 3 of 2013, or Issue No. 40 overall, of its newsletter *Zhicheng Life* on **June 3, 2013**. The issue reviews activities from April and May involving career development, cultural exchange, student-organization work, service learning and external engagement. The complete issue was distributed as a PDF attachment with the OA notice.

## Career Development and Public Speaking

On April 29 and 30, Zhicheng Residential College held the sixth Career Development Intensive Camp, attended by **25 participants** from Zhicheng and other residential communities. Through practical exercises, the camp focused on self-awareness, interview and job-search experience, communication and teamwork. A feedback survey found that **83 percent of participants** were fairly satisfied or very satisfied, while **100 percent** said the camp met or exceeded their expectations.

On May 18, the college held a public-speaking training camp. Wu Hao, general manager of the Shantou branch of Jucheng Group, provided training and on-stage guidance to **58 participants** on speech preparation, communication techniques and overcoming stage anxiety.

## Salons and College Activities

On May 8, Wu Jiasheng, a former news spokesperson for the Hong Kong Police Force, joined the fifth Zhicheng Salon. He discussed public service and his 35-year career in the Hong Kong police force, reflecting on transparency, integrity, passion and service. On May 11, Professor Lin Daqing, dean of the School of Public Health at the University of Hong Kong, joined the sixth Zhicheng Salon and shared thoughts on university-life planning and the development of personal interests through drama.

On May 22, the college held the 2012-2013 summary meeting for its Residential Activities Funding Program. The review committee conducted final inspections of completed projects and selected **five outstanding projects**.

On May 25, the college held a Mentor Face-to-Face activity titled "Getting Close to Dolphins," with college mentor Liu Wenhua and graduate students taking part. A group of **26 people** experienced the marine environment from a fishing boat. On May 26, the mentor committee and Student Union jointly held the "A Patch of Grass" music exchange, combining band performances, instrumental performances and interviews to encourage interaction among residents.

## Student Organizations and Service Learning

Zhicheng completed the re-election of its Party general branch on May 24 and held the first meeting of the new general-branch committee. The newsletter also reviewed the May 22 handover event of the Deyi Qin Society, which included a guqin-class showcase.

The election of the sixth Zhicheng Student Union president lasted nearly one month and included campaign publicity, meetings with residents, ability presentations, a courtyard speech and voting. The newsletter records **453 votes** and an **81 percent turnout**; student candidate names are not reproduced here.

On May 25, the fifth mentor committee held "A Meeting with Graduates," inviting **seven graduating students** to share experiences in overseas study, domestic postgraduate study and employment. On May 18, the Evergreen Service-Learning Association of Zhicheng took part in a youth-volunteer community service event and organized a charity sale near the entrance to Huaqiao New Village on Jinxin Road in Shantou.

From May 28 to June 3, the college accepted registrations for the 2013 summer rural service-learning public-welfare course, which was planned for mid-July. From May 28 to June 4, the public-welfare course "Entering University, Setting Sail in Life: Team Development Camp for Secondary-School Students" opened for registration, with current university students expected to organize team-development activities for secondary-school students.

## College Exchanges

On May 10, a **12-member** group of hospice-service workers from Hong Kong and the mainland visited Zhicheng to learn about its layout, facilities, student self-governance and residential activities. On May 23, a **six-member** delegation from Zhangjiakou University in Hebei and a **seven-member** delegation from Guangdong Innovative Technical College visited Zhicheng in separate exchanges on residential-college ideas and mechanisms for student self-education, management and service.
