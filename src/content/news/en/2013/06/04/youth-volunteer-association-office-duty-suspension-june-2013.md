As the final examination period approached and most student-organization activities had ended for the semester, the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association office would suspend duty service from **Thursday, June 5, 2013, the 15th week of the semester**. The suspension also covered approval of office and material requests.

Student organizations with urgent material needs were asked to contact the responsible person named in the notice. The original notice provides a student's personal mobile and campus short number; those personal contact details are not reproduced here. Organizations may consult the original OA notice or use the association's public channels for assistance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Communist Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association)
>
> ⚠️ The personal emergency contact information in the original notice is not reproduced here; consult the original OA notice for the complete details.
