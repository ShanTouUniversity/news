The Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association held its 19th Board election on the evening of **May 31, 2013**, in the Science Lecture Hall. The assembly approved the work report of the 18th Board and elected the 19th Board by vote.

## Election Result

The 19th Board has **five members**: one president and four vice presidents. Because all elected members are students, their names, year groups and majors are not published here.

The election result was jointly announced by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association.
