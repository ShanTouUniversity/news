New Grade Seven students who plan to attend the STU Affiliated Middle School in **fall 2013** and are children of Shantou University employees must complete registration at the school's first-floor Academic Affairs Office from **9:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Tuesday, June 25**.

Applicants should bring the household register and copies containing only the head-of-household and student sections, together with the primary-school evaluation booklet. Children of School of Medicine employees, excluding employees of the affiliated hospital, must also provide a work certificate for their parents issued by the School of Medicine office.

Families unable to register on time for special reasons should contact the school’s Academic Affairs Office in advance. Failure to register by the deadline will be treated as voluntarily giving up the place.

For inquiries, call `86502051` or contact Director Huang.
