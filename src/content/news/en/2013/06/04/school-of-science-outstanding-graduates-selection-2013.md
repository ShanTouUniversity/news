Shantou University's School of Science launched an outstanding-graduate selection for the **2013 undergraduate cohort**. The selection recognizes students with strong academic and personal records who have participated in collective and public-service activities and supported the university's graduation and employment work, with the stated aim of promoting a positive academic and campus culture.

## Selection Criteria

The notice says applicants should comply with laws and university rules, respect the university and teachers, cooperate with classmates, demonstrate overall development and good academic performance and have no course retakes. They should also have taken an active part in collective activities and have experience in volunteer or public-service work.

Academic and graduation requirements include receiving an academic excellence scholarship at second prize or above in at least two years, or receiving a university special scholarship at least once; earning a good result for the graduation thesis; and actively cooperating with the university's graduation and employment work while leaving campus in an orderly manner. These are the published criteria; no student's individual grades or award history is included here.

## Procedure and Award

Eligible graduates must apply personally and complete the *STU School of Science Outstanding Graduate Application Form*. A school selection committee will review the applications and determine the recipients. The School of Science will confer the title of “Outstanding Graduate” and issue certificates and awards. The award for an outstanding undergraduate graduate is **800 yuan**.

The notice asks applicants to submit materials to the School of Science Youth League Committee by **noon on June 7, 2012 (Friday)**. This date conflicts with the 2013 cohort named in the notice and with the notice's 2013 publication date. The conflict is retained as reported rather than corrected speculatively.

The attached form requests personal information including name, student number, major, birth date, sex, ethnicity, political status, telephone number, award records, yearly grades and rankings, graduation-thesis information and public-service experience. The form and personal records are not reproduced here.
