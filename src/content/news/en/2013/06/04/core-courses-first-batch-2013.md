Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has announced the first batch of **26 core courses**. More than 70 courses were submitted by faculty across the university. The Core Curriculum Expert Group selected the first batch after two reviews and several rounds of consultation and revision with the submitting instructors. The Academic Affairs Office will provide funding for the selected courses and encourages the instructors to offer them and continue improving teaching quality.

## Continued Development

The Core Curriculum Expert Group will continue consulting with instructors whose course proposals are being revised. Additional courses will be announced after approval. The Academic Affairs Office and the expert group also plan to solicit new core courses each semester as part of the continuing development of the program and improvement of teaching quality.
