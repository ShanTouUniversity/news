The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold the 15th Kan Kan Culture event at **3 p.m. on Wednesday, June 5, 2013**, in the STU Grand Auditorium. Titled “A Design Journey (2),” the event will feature a conversation between Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Kan Tai-keung, an internationally renowned graphic designer, chair professor and former dean of the school.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Kan Kan Culture 15 |
| Theme | A Design Journey (2) |
| Time | 3 p.m., Wednesday, June 5, 2013 |
| Venue | STU Grand Auditorium |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Conversation | Wang Shouzhi and Kan Tai-keung |

Wang Shouzhi is dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. Kan Tai-keung is a chair professor and former dean of the school and a member of the Alliance Graphique Internationale (A.G.I.).

The event is open to faculty members, students and members of the public interested in design culture. Further arrangements should follow information released by the organizer.
