The 157th Engineering Research Forum of Shantou University's School of Engineering will be held at **10 a.m. on Wednesday, June 5** in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room. Dr. Cai Xinye of the College of Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics will speak on **“Research on the Search-Based Large-Scale Software Release Planning Problem.”**

Graduate students and relevant faculty members in the Department of Electronic Engineering are expected to attend. Other interested students and faculty members are also welcome.

## Lecture Focus

During software development and upgrades, companies must decide which features to implement or add in the next release while balancing measures such as customer benefit and development cost. The lecture treats this as the software-requirements-engineering “release planning problem,” presenting a multiobjective model based on dependencies among requirements. It also covers extracting large-scale real-world requirements data by mining open-source bug databases, followed by optimization-algorithm design, solution methods and comparisons among different algorithms.

## Speaker

Cai Xinye graduated from the Department of Electronic and Information Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2004, received a research master's degree in electronic engineering from the University of York in 2006, and earned a doctorate in electrical and computer engineering from Kansas State University in 2009. Cai subsequently joined the College of Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. Research interests include evolutionary computation, optimization design, search-based software engineering and computational biology, with a focus on intelligent optimization and its applications under large-scale, multiobjective and uncertain conditions.
