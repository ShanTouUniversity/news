ZhiCheng College of Shantou University issued **Document No. 2 of 2013** to recognize students who performed strongly in peer-mentor work in the college's residential communities during the 2012–2013 academic year. The college decided to name **seven students** Outstanding Peer Mentors.

The selection considered peer mentors' work reports, defenses, self-assessments and evaluations by residential students. ZhiCheng College encouraged the recognized students to value the honor, remain modest, continue their efforts and contribute to the development of the college.
