Zhichi Interactive Technology will work with Shantou University's School of Engineering to offer summer internships. The recruitment is open to **students in the 2010 and 2011 cohorts and high-performing students in the 2012 cohort** across the university. Positions cover software and web development, UI and interaction design and graphic design. The internships will be based at STU's 789 Student Entrepreneurship Park during the summer.

## Recruitment Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizations | Zhichi Interactive Technology and STU School of Engineering |
| Positions | Software and web development; UI and interaction design; graphic design |
| Location | STU 789 Student Entrepreneurship Park; the attachment specifies Building 7 |
| Period | Summer; beginning after final examinations, for one to two months |
| Applicants | Students in the 2010 and 2011 cohorts and high-performing students in the 2012 cohort across STU |
| Application deadline | June 28, 2013 |
| Application email | `zctech@foxmail.com` |
| Interviews | To be arranged according to the number of applications |

## Positions and Eligibility

Members of the MT Enterprise Innovation Club, students with strong project performance and applicants with project experience will receive priority consideration. Applicants should complete the form for their preferred position. The form requests personal information including name, gender, student number, department, year, major, email, telephone number, self-introduction and skills; those fields and application materials are not reproduced here.

The internship departments are Development and Design. The attached profile lists meal subsidies, office snacks, regular dinners and late-night meals among the benefits, and says an internship certificate may be provided according to the internship arrangement. Final terms are subject to the recruiting organization.

## Company Profile

The attached material describes the company as a creative technology enterprise established in May 2013, bringing together mobile-network product development, interactive electronics design, somatosensory-interaction equipment design and sales and Internet-of-Things technology research and development. Its listed representative products include the MAIA projection interactive system, MAIA-I advertising interactive system, MAIA-II conference interactive system and WeChat Tong. The profile says these products had been promoted in Hong Kong, Shantou, Guangzhou and Shenzhen and used to provide human-computer interaction solutions for indoor events and conferences.

The notice names the company “Shantou Zhichi Interactive Network Technology Co., Ltd.,” while the attached company profile uses “Zhichi Interactive Network Technology (Shantou) Co., Ltd.” This article retains the difference in the source naming.
