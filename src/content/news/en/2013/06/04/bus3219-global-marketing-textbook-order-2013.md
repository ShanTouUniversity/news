The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office announced that the textbook for the **[BUS3219] Global Marketing** course offered in fall 2013 has changed to *Global Marketing* by **Warren J. Keegan and Mark C. Green**. The book is published by Renmin University of China Press and is priced at **49 yuan**.

Students who need to order the new textbook may log in to the credit-system platform and place an order directly on the textbook-ordering page. The reordering window runs from **noon on June 4 through 5 p.m. on June 5**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
