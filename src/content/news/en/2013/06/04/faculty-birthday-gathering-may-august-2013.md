With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, the Shantou University union will host a birthday gathering for faculty and staff members born in **May, June, July or August**. The event will take place at **6:30 p.m. on Saturday, June 15, 2013**, in the western restaurant of the Junhua Hotel. Faculty, staff and family members may attend with tickets.

## Reservation Schedule

| Item | Time or information |
| --- | --- |
| Online reservations | Noon on June 4 to noon on June 10 |
| Payment deduction or payment | June 10–12 |
| Ticket collection | 9:30 a.m.–noon on June 14 |
| Reservation system | <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/> |
| System support | Network Center: 3548 ext. 8842; 3551 ext. 8842 |

Participants must enter the reservation system under their birth month. Tickets cannot be refunded after registration and payment. The university will not arrange transportation, so attendees should travel independently and observe traffic safety. Children must be accompanied by at least one parent, and parents are responsible for children's safety and personal belongings during the event.

Faculty and staff members choosing cash payment should follow the payment and ticket-collection procedures in the notice and event plan. Detailed arrangements and payment rules are subject to the materials issued by the union.
