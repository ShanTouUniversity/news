It is reported that the School of Engineering announced on June 4, 2013, the initial review results for its 2013 student extracurricular science and technology innovation projects.

Of 35 applications, 32 projects including “STU Campus Goods Exchange (Yi Shou Wang)” passed the initial defense and were approved. Project teams are expected to carry out research and development under faculty supervision. In addition, the project “Vehicle-Mounted Traffic Light Recognition Device Based on Image Processing,” which had applied for deferred completion in 2012, passed its final defense.

The review notice issued on May 28 specified that reviews would be held within the students' departments rather than through a school-wide defense. The arrangements were:

| Department | Time | Venue | Reviewers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electromechanical Engineering | May 28, 10 a.m. | Department meeting room | Chen Shaoke and Zheng Liming |
| Computer Science | May 29, 3 p.m. | E203 classroom | Xiong Zhi and Chen Yindong |
| Civil Engineering | May 30, 3 p.m. | Department meeting room | Li Gengying and Zhou Qi |
| Electronics | June 1, 3 p.m. | Department meeting room | Wang Jun and Lin Yiwen |

Each project leader had to prepare a **five-minute PPT** introduction. A project not represented at the review as required would be treated as withdrawn. The May 28 review notice says that **32 projects** had applied, while the June 4 results notice says there were **35 applications**, of which 32 passed the initial review. This source-level difference in the application count is retained.
