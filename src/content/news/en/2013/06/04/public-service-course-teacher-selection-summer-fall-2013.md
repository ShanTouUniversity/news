Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office opened **faculty selection for public-service courses in the summer and fall terms of the 2013–2014 academic year**. The university will offer **19 public-service courses** across the two terms, and this notice seeks instructors for selected courses according to their staffing needs.

Faculty members who passed the first through fourth public-service course teacher-training sessions may select courses according to the published needs table and complete the public-service course faculty selection information form. The notice lists the following selected course needs:

## Summer-Term Course Needs

| Course | Coordinator | Instructors needed | Course and service schedule | Service location |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Xinhuo Xiangchuan Heart Action | Yao Qin | 4 | July 17–21 | Longxi Primary School in Xilu Town, Chaoyang District, Shantou, and Xiazuo Primary School in Leiling Town, Chaonan District, Shantou |
| STU Student Culture, Science and Health “Three Rural Areas” Service-Learning Course | Yao Qin | 2 | July 13–14: lectures and personal-development training; July 16–22: public-service practice; July 23: review and sharing | Dongshan County, Zhangzhou, Fujian |
| Shuguang Student-Aid Service | Yao Qin | 2 | June 29–30: lectures and personal-development training; July 17–21: public-service practice; July 22: review and sharing | Fengtang Town, Chao'an County, Chaozhou |

## Fall-Term Course Needs

| Course | Coordinator | Instructors needed | Course period | Service location |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Shuguang Volunteer-Teaching Service-Learning Course | Yao Qin | 4 | September–December | Primary schools or communities in urban Shantou |

The listed courses include classroom instruction, personal-development training, community service and review and sharing. Detailed arrangements are subject to the course needs table and subsequent notices.

## Selection Arrangements

Faculty members who wish to undertake course duties must complete the public-service course faculty selection information form, name the file “Public-Service Course Faculty Selection Information-Name” as required, and send it as an email attachment to Liu Xiangling's work mailbox at the Academic Affairs Office, `gykc@stu.edu.cn`, by **noon on June 6, 2013**. The notice also attaches qualified-personnel lists for the first through fourth training sessions and the faculty selection form. These materials contain personal information and are not reproduced here.
