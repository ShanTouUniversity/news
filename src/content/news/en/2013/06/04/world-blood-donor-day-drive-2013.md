Ahead of **World Blood Donor Day on June 14**, the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association will hold a “June 14 World Blood Donor Day” blood drive from **9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Friday, June 7**. The drive is open to faculty members and students across the university, with stations in the open area in front of the shopping area below Dining Hall 2 and below Dining Hall 4.

## Event Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | “June 14 World Blood Donor Day” blood drive |
| Time | 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. on Friday, June 7 |
| Locations | Open area in front of the shopping area below Dining Hall 2; below Dining Hall 4 |
| Organizers | STU Youth Volunteers Association and STU Communist Youth League Committee |

## Participation Guidance

The notice states that donors should be 18 to 55 years old, weigh at least 50 kilograms for men or 45 kilograms for women, and be in good health without infectious disease. Donors should not have had a cold or fever or taken medication during the preceding week. They should rest adequately the day before, eat normally on the day of donation, avoid donating on an empty stomach and donate no more than once every six months.

Donors should bring an identity card or blood-donation certificate for identity verification and to protect donor rights. Final eligibility and on-site arrangements are subject to staff guidance and medical screening.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Communist Youth League Committee)
