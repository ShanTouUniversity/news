In accordance with Shantou University's 2013 holiday and class-adjustment schedule, the Student Learning Center (SLC) will close to the public on **Wednesday, June 12**, for the Dragon Boat Festival.

Its academic-consultation courses will be adjusted as follows:

| Original date | Adjusted arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Saturday, June 8 | Exchanged with the course scheduled for Monday, June 10 |
| Sunday, June 9 | Exchanged with the course scheduled for Tuesday, June 11 |

Faculty and students who need academic consultations should plan around the adjusted dates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
