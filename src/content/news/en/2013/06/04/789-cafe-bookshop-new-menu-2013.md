The Cafe Bookshop in Building 789 at Shantou University, a student-entrepreneurship practice project, has introduced new meal and afternoon-tea sets for the university community. The shop also offers air conditioning throughout the day, private bookings and invoices, and remains open during the summer.

## Menu Information

### Meals

| Type | Item | Price and details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese-style meal | Chicken-rice and chicken-wing-rice sets, with curry, black-pepper or cola flavors | 14, 13 or 12 yuan; add 2 yuan for a drink |
| Chinese-style meal | Beijing-style shredded pork with rice | 11 yuan; add 2 yuan for a drink |
| Chinese-style meal | Udon noodles | 10 yuan; add 2 yuan for a drink |
| Western-style meal | Cheese-baked rice | 14 yuan, offered at noon on Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays |
| Western-style meal | Pasta, with black-pepper, meat-sauce or octopus options | 14 yuan; add 2 yuan for a drink |
| Western-style meal | Udon noodles | 10 yuan; add 2 yuan for a drink |
| Western-style meal | Sandwich | 13 yuan; add 2 yuan for a drink |
| Western-style meal | Pizza | The notice said it would be introduced the following week; no price was listed |

### Afternoon Tea

Afternoon tea is available from **1 to 6 p.m.** A serving of the shop's specialty cookie is included with kung fu tea or flower tea. After **9 p.m.**, milk tea and fruit tea are available at 60% of the regular price.

The Cafe Bookshop's ordering hotline is **13750405075 (campus short number 665075)**.
