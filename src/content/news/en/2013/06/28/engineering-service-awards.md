Shantou University Engineering College has announced the recipients of its 2012-2013 Best Teaching Service Award and Best Administrative Service Award. Chen Zhanglin of the Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering received the Best Teaching Service Award, while Yao Yi of the College Youth League Office received the Best Administrative Service Award.

The awards recognize teaching support and administrative staff who contributed to the College's teaching, research, and administrative work. The recipients were selected through department nominations, recommendation by the College Affairs Committee, and evaluation by the College Academic Committee.
