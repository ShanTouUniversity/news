It is reported that Shantou University will hold the 2013 student military training mobilization meeting at 8:30 a.m. on July 1 in the Grand Auditorium. The colleges will arrange for students to arrive and take their designated seats by 8:10 a.m., while secretaries of the colleges and residential colleges will sit on the rostrum. These arrangements support the organization of the military training mobilization work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Department and People's Armed Forces Department)
> ⚠️ Please consult the original attachment in the OA system for the seating plan.
