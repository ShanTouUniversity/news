Shantou University has launched its 2013 Student Entrepreneurship Practice Program and is accepting applications for the first batch of internship positions from all students, including graduate students. Organized by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and other units, the program is intended to help students gain practical experience in entrepreneurship and employment while developing innovation and career skills.

Shantou University has operated the Student Entrepreneurship Practice Program since 2003. This year's program is being carried out in connection with the Guangdong student internship service initiative known as the "Zhanchi Plan." Participants may receive an internship certificate or assessment certificate issued by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League. Students with outstanding performance may also be recommended by the university for selection as outstanding individuals in Guangdong's internship service initiative. Internships are generally expected to last from two weeks to two months, with position details and specific requirements provided in the relevant attachments.

Interested students must submit the required internship application materials and a personal résumé by 24:00 on July 1, 2013. Position information, application forms, and other program attachments are available in the original notice on the Shantou University OA system.
