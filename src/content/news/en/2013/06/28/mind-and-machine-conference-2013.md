The 10th Mind and Machine Conference will be held at Shantou University from September 20 to 22, 2013. The conference will be jointly hosted by the Mind and Machine Seminar, the Christian Studies Centre of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University, and the Department of Computer Science and Technology of the Shantou University College of Engineering.

Founded in 2004, the Mind and Machine conference series has been held annually. This year's conference is themed “Mind and Machine: A Ten-Year Review and Outlook.” It will bring together research achievements from the past decade of the series and publish a conference proceedings volume.

### Conference Topics

Discussions will focus on six areas:

1. Mind, psychology, and the physical world
2. Mind, computation, and 4ECS
3. Logic, language, computation, and cognition
4. Emotion, consciousness, and brain neuroscience
5. Mind, machine, and human intelligence
6. Life, cognition, and ethics

The Mind and Machine Seminar was initiated by scholars from Peking University, Renmin University of China, Fudan University, Zhejiang University, Beijing Normal University, Sun Yat-sen University, East China Normal University, and Shantou University. It has long focused on frontier issues in artificial intelligence and the intersections between artificial intelligence and philosophy.

### Call for Papers

Teachers at Shantou University interested in related fields may submit papers for consideration. Submissions should include the conference response form, a paper title, and an abstract of approximately 800 Chinese characters. The submission deadline is August 10, 2013. For submissions and conference inquiries, please follow the original OA notice to contact the organizing committee.
