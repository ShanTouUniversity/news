It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University has announced the class schedules for three 2013 summer public welfare courses: the Sanxiaxiang Service Learning course, the Shuguang Tutoring Service, and the Xinhuo Xiangchuan Love Action. The detailed session times and venues have been published in the attachments, and enrolled students are expected to prepare in advance and attend on time.

## Overview of Courses and Service Sites

| Course | Service Site |
| --- | --- |
| Sanxiaxiang Service Learning Course | Baicheng Village, Dongshan County, Zhangzhou City, Fujian Province |
| Shuguang Tutoring Service | Yingfeng Primary School, Chaozhou |
| Xinhuo Xiangchuan Love Action | Xiachu Primary School |
| Xinhuo Xiangchuan Love Action | Longxi Primary School |
