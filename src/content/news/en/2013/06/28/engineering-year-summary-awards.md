Shantou University Engineering College will hold its 2012-2013 annual review and awards ceremony on July 1 to review the College's work during the academic year and recognize relevant faculty members and students.

The event is scheduled for 3:00 p.m. at the Library Lecture Hall. All Engineering College faculty members and award-winning students are expected to attend.
