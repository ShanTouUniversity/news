According to the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the Shantou University Young Volunteers Firefly Foundation completed interviews for student representative trustees on the evening of May 12, 2013. After considering the applicants' individual circumstances and interview performance, the foundation proposed three students for appointment as new student representative trustees on its board.

Five students applied and took part in the interviews. The proposed appointees are Li QM, a 2012 clinical medicine student; Lin RJ, a 2012 economics student; and Li XY, a 2011 biology student.

The public comment period will close at 5:00 p.m. on July 3, 2013. Organizations or individuals with objections may submit written comments or contact the University Youth League Committee by the deadline. Organizations must affix their official seal, while individuals must provide their real names. The committee can be reached at 82902302 or o_tw@stu.edu.cn.
