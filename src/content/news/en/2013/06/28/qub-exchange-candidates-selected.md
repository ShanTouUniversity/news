Shantou University Law School has completed interviews for its overseas exchange programme with Queen's University Belfast (QUB) and announced its proposed candidates on June 28.

On the morning of June 27, the Law School interviewed four students who had passed the preliminary selection at Conference Room 213. The panel was chaired by Michael Palmer, Dean of the Law School, and included Terry Bodenhorn, Dean of the College of Liberal Arts; Chen Xueyi, a director at the Law School; and Chen Yanying, an assistant at the Law School Career Development Centre. The assessment covered English communication, analytical and adaptive abilities, independent living overseas, teamwork, motivation for exchange study, and individual strengths. Applicants were evaluated on their overall GPA, English proficiency, and interview performance.

Following the comprehensive assessment, the Law School proposed Wu SS, a 2011 law student, and Luo HT, a 2010 public administration student, as the programme's formal shortlisted candidates. After the public notice period, the School will submit their application materials to QUB, which will make the final admission decision.

The public notice period runs from June 28 to July 2. Written objections may be submitted to the Law School before the deadline.
