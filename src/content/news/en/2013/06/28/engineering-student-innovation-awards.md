Shantou University Engineering College has announced its 2012-2013 recognition decision for student extracurricular academic and technological innovation. Over the past year, students and faculty members from the College participated in events including the Third National Undergraduate Engineering Training and Comprehensive Ability Competition and the 11th Guangdong Undergraduate Programming Competition (GPCDC'2013), achieving outstanding results.

The College decided to award Innovation Medals to students who performed outstandingly in these activities and to confer the title of Outstanding Instructor on 13 faculty members in recognition of their guidance and support for student academic and technological innovation. The decision was made under the College's regulations on awards for student extracurricular academic and technological innovation activities, following review by the leadership group for student academic and technological works and deliberation by the College Affairs Committee.
