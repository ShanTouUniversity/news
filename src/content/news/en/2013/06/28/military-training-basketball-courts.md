It is reported that Shantou University's 2013 student military training will run from July 1 to July 14. According to a notice from the Student Affairs Office, military skills training will take place from 7:30 to 10:30 a.m. and from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. each day during the training period.

As the basketball courts will be used as military training grounds, students are asked to arrange other physical exercise activities during these periods and avoid courts occupied by training, so as not to disrupt the military training of the 2012 cohort.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
