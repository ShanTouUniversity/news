Shantou University’s Office of Academic Affairs will conduct a midterm review during the summer semester of student innovation experiment projects approved in 2012. The review is intended to assess project progress and interim results.

Project leaders are required to submit a midterm review form and a project progress log. The log should record experiments and project-team meeting minutes according to the actual progress of each project. Projects that have produced interim results, such as papers or developed physical products, may also provide the relevant written and visual materials as required.

Under the notice, electronic materials must be submitted through the designated channel by September 1, 2013, and paper materials must be delivered to the Office of Academic Affairs service desk by September 18. The midterm review will serve as an important basis for the final project evaluation. Projects that do not pass the review must complete corrective work within the required period. Their corrective plans must pass a follow-up review before the projects can continue and use the second installment of project funds; projects that still fail to meet the requirements will be terminated.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Academic Affairs)

> ⚠️ The midterm review form, project progress log template, and requirements for submitting interim results are available in the original notice on the OA system.
