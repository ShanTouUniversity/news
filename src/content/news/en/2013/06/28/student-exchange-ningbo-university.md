It is reported that 20 Shantou University students were confirmed for exchange study at Ningbo University in the fall semester of the 2013-2014 academic year after college-level recommendations and a review under the relevant exchange agreement.

The Academic Affairs Office scheduled the students to collect their Ningbo University admission letters and sign commitment letters at 4:30 p.m. on June 28, 2013, in the Academic Affairs Office Conference Room (Room 312, Administration Centre).

### Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Host university | Ningbo University |

| Number of students | 20 |

| Location | Academic Affairs Office Conference Room (Room 312, Administration Centre) |
| Required actions | Collect admission letters and sign commitment letters |
