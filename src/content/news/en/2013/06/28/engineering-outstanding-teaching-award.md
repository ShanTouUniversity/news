Shantou University Engineering College has announced the recipient of its 2012-2013 Outstanding Teaching Award. Professor Cui Yan of the Department of Electronic Engineering received the honor.

The award recognizes faculty members who made contributions to teaching, the College's EIP-CDIO engineering education reform, and teaching research. The recipient was selected through department nominations, recommendation by the College Affairs Committee, and evaluation by the College Academic Committee.
