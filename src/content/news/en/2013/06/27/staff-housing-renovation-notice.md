According to a construction advisory issued by the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University's Resource Management Office, the university will renovate five vacant staff residences. Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. will carry out the work. Construction is scheduled to begin on July 1, 2013, and be completed by August 19, with an awarded contract value of RMB 296,500.

## Project Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contractor | Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Scope | Five vacant staff residences |
| Main work | Replacing wooden doors, aluminum-alloy doors and windows, and stainless-steel insect screens; removing and re-finishing ceiling and wall plaster; laying polished floor tiles; repairing and renewing terrazzo flooring; and installing parts of the electrical, low-voltage and water-supply systems |
| Contract value | RMB 296,500 |
| Schedule | July 1 to August 19, 2013 |

The work may generate noise, vibration and dust. The university asks faculty, staff, students and other pedestrians to take care near the construction areas. Entry to the construction site is prohibited without permission from the relevant university offices and the contractor, and people should avoid staying near the work areas whenever possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
