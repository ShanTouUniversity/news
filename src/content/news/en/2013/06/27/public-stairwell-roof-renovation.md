According to a construction advisory issued by the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University's Resource Management Office, the university will renovate the glass roofs of public stairwells at Buildings 9 and 10, the Second Dining Hall and Building D. Work is scheduled to begin on July 1, 2013, and be completed by August 29.

## Project Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contractor | Shantou Longhua Construction General Corporation |
| Sites | Public stairwell glass roofs at Buildings 9 and 10, the Second Dining Hall and Building D |
| Main work | Complete roof removal and reconstruction, along with repairs to steel windows, wall paint, roof insulation tiles and exterior glass mosaic tiles |
| Contract value | RMB 273,800 |
| Schedule | July 1 to August 29, 2013 |

The work may generate noise, vibration and dust. The university asks pedestrians to take care near the construction areas. Faculty, staff and students should not enter the sites without permission from the relevant university offices and the contractor, and should avoid staying near the work areas whenever possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
