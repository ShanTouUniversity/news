It is reported that Shantou University issued the "Notice on the Appointment of Guan Chaoran and Others" (Document No. Shan Da Fa [2013] No. 90) on June 27, announcing two personnel decisions.

The appointments were decided at the 8th University Leadership Meeting on June 18, 2013, as follows:

| Person | Position | Note |
|--------|----------|------|
| Guan Chaoran | Director, Center for Teacher Development and Educational Assessment | Six-month probation |
| Ma Fengqi | Executive Director, Center for Teacher Development and Educational Assessment | Reassigned |

The appointments take effect from the date of issuance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
