According to a notice issued by the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University's Resource Management Office, the university will begin the second phase of renovations at Building E. The project will be managed by the university's Infrastructure Office and carried out by Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. Work is scheduled to start on July 1, 2013, and is expected to be completed by September 13.

The project will mainly cover the fourth floor of Building E and the complete interior demolition and reconstruction of public restrooms on floors 1 through 5. The awarded contract value is 3.2875 million yuan.

The university noted that the construction may cause noise, vibration and dust. Faculty, students and other pedestrians should take care when passing the area. Entry to the construction site is prohibited without permission from the university's relevant offices and the contractor, and people should avoid staying near the site whenever possible.
