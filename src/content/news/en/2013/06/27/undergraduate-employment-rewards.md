It is reported that Shantou University has completed the distribution of **employment rewards for its 2013 main-campus undergraduate graduates**. The relevant funds have been deposited into the Bank of China accounts of eligible recipients.

Eligible graduates are advised to check their accounts for the incoming funds. The notice was issued by the Student Affairs Office, while the specific list of recipients is available in the original campus notice attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The student list and attachment contain personal information. Please access the original notice through the OA system.
