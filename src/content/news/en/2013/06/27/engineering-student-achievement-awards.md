Shantou University's College of Engineering has announced the proposed awards for students and faculty who achieved outstanding results in extracurricular academic and science innovation activities during the 2012-2013 academic year.

During the academic year, faculty members and students from the college took part in events including the Third National Undergraduate Engineering Training Comprehensive Ability Competition and the 11th Guangdong Undergraduate Programming Contest (GPCDC'2013). Following a review by the college's leadership group for student extracurricular academic and science projects, and approval by the college's administrative committee, the college proposed awarding eligible students the title of “Innovation Medal” and naming 13 faculty members “Outstanding Instructors.”

Members of the community who had objections to the proposed awards could submit a written response to the College of Engineering Youth League Committee, or raise the matter in person or by telephone, before the end of working hours on June 27, 2013. Responses were required to include the complainant's real name.

The lists of student award recipients and outstanding instructors are available in the OA attachments. Because the attachments contain personal-list information, readers should consult the original notice in Shantou University's OA system for those details.
