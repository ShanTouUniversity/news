It is reported that Shantou University's Publicity Department will distribute *Those Years, When the Golden Poinciana Blossomed: Stories Told by STU People* to main-campus units from June 27 to 28, 2013.

Representatives of the units may collect the book from Room 315 of the New Administration Center. Each unit may receive one copy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department)
