It is reported that Shantou University's Student Affairs Department and People's Armed Forces Department released the arrangements for the year's student military training on June 27, 2013. The training will take place on campus from **July 1 to 14**, with specific training grounds and classrooms assigned according to the university's schedule.

## Training and Classes

Military-skills training will be held each morning from **7:30 to 10:30** and each afternoon from **4:00 to 6:00** throughout the training period. Military theory classes will be held in campus classrooms at the times listed in the training schedule, and military lectures will also be arranged during the period.

| Item | Time | Venue or details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Military training mobilization meeting | 8:30 a.m., July 1 | Grand Auditorium |
| Military-skills training | July 1–14, daily from 7:30–10:30 a.m. and 4:00–6:00 p.m. | On campus; specific grounds assigned in the schedule |
| Military theory classes and lectures | During the training period, according to the schedule | Classrooms and Grand Auditorium |
| Military training summary meeting | Afternoon of July 14 | Track and field ground |

## Training Supplies

Training supplies will be collected by each school or college on a unified basis. The Student Affairs Office arranged distribution and sorting at **Room 105 of the Administration Center from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. on June 30**, with each school or college organizing collection according to the notice. Participating students must also bring their own drinking water.

The detailed training schedule, grounds, seating plan, and arrangements for supplies and activities are provided in the original notice attachments.
