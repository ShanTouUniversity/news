It is reported that the national bar examination training organized by Shantou University Law School will resume on **July 1, 2013**. The summer program will run on a full-time basis until the examination and will complete the remaining components of the current training program.

## Courses and Schedule

The remaining program includes the unfinished part of the systematic intensive course, a statute review course, a mock-examination intensive course, and a final pre-examination course. The daily schedule is as follows:

| Period | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Morning | 8:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. |
| Afternoon | 2:30–6:00 p.m. |
| Evening | 7:00–10:00 p.m., centralized review |

Because of construction in the E Building during the summer, the training will initially take place in **Lecture Hall 2**. The venue will return to the moot court from **August 18**. Participants are required to bring their admission cards and attend on time.
