Shantou University is scheduled to hold a public academic lecture during the first meeting of its ninth University Council. The event will be open to faculty members and students across the university.

The information was issued by the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation. Faculty members and students are encouraged to attend. Further details, including the time, venue and other arrangements, will be subject to subsequent university announcements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
