It is reported that the air-dome basketball training hall project, one of Shantou University's key construction projects, is scheduled to pour concrete for its roof panel on June 21, with the work expected to last one day. On that day, concrete pump trucks and transport vehicles will travel along the route of the campus gate → the old administrative building → the engineering building → the structural and wind tunnel laboratory (medical teaching building), and will park on the road northwest of Student Dormitory G to carry out the pouring work.

As a result, the road on the northwest side of Dormitory G will be closed for one day. The university has reminded vehicles and pedestrians who normally use this route to take alternative paths.
