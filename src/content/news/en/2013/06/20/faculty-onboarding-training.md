Shantou University will organize its 2013 faculty induction training during the summer, according to the university. The program is intended for teachers, educational staff and other relevant personnel newly recruited by higher education institutions in Guangdong this year, as well as teachers who have not yet obtained a faculty induction training certificate.

The program will be delivered in an intensive, full-time format from July 17 to 30, for a total of 100 class hours. It will include special lectures, teaching observations and other activities. The detailed schedule will follow the 2013 course timetable issued by the Faculty Induction Training Center of Hanshan Normal University.

Each university unit must collect the required materials and submit them to Room 315 of the New Administration Center by June 25. Participants must register through the STU Training website at training.stu.edu.cn; registration authorizes the Finance Department to deduct the RMB 300 training fee from their salaries. After completing the training and receiving a completion certificate, participants will be reimbursed for the training fee in accordance with university regulations. Other expenses during the training period will be borne by the participants.

Applicants seeking course exemptions must also submit a copy of their graduation transcript. Participants are additionally required to provide a classroom teaching practice evaluation form and recent one-inch identification photos as requested. Questions about registration and the training program can be directed to 86502340.
