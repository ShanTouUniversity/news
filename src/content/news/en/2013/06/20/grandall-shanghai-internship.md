Grandall Law Firm's Shanghai office plans to recruit two law students through Shantou University's School of Law for internships. The internships are expected to begin in early July and last two months, and are open to students in the 2010 or 2011 cohort.

## Internship Details

- **Host organisation**: Grandall Law Firm, Shanghai office
- **Number of positions**: 2
- **Eligible applicants**: Law students in the 2010 or 2011 cohort at Shantou University's School of Law
- **Internship period**: Starting in early July 2013, for two months
- **Support provided**: The firm will provide accommodation, with interns responsible for a small portion of the cost, and a monthly transportation subsidy of RMB 300

According to the notice, applicants should demonstrate responsibility, attention to detail and a full commitment to the internship. The School of Law will select applicants based on factors including their cumulative GPA across semesters since enrolment; cumulative GPA accounts for 50% of the selection criteria listed in the notice. The Career Development Centre will arrange interviews, with the time and location to be announced separately.

Interested students must submit the required application materials by June 23.
