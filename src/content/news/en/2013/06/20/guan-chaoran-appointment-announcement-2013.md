It is reported that Shantou University issued a pre-appointment announcement on June 20, 2013, proposing to appoint Guan Chaoran as Director of the Center for Teacher Development and Educational Assessment following the 8th University Leadership Meeting on June 18.

## Announcement Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed position | Director, Center for Teacher Development and Educational Assessment, Shantou University |
| Candidate | Guan Chaoran |
| Decision meeting | 8th University Leadership Meeting, June 18, 2013 |
| Basis | Interim Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres of Shantou University (STU Party Document [2009] No. 47) |
| Public comment period | Until June 26, 2013 |
| Responsible office | Personnel Office |

Units or individuals with differing opinions are asked to submit written comments to the Personnel Office by June 26. Submissions from units should bear an official seal, and those from individuals should include the real name.
