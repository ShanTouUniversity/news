According to a notice from the Sports Department of Shantou University, the university swimming pool opened to faculty and students on June 20. It is open daily from 5:00 to 6:30 p.m. Entry is managed through the campus card system, with a fee of RMB 5 per visit.

The Sports Department reminded swimmers to pay attention to safety and follow the pool regulations. Entering the pool area outside opening hours is prohibited.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
