It is reported that, to recognize exemplary individuals and set role models, the Party General Branch of Shantou University's Graduate School has publicized its proposed commendation list for the 2012–2013 academic year after evaluation by student party branches and review by the General Branch. The list covers 34 student party members in total.

According to the announcement, Ge ZJ and six others are recommended for the title of "Outstanding Party Worker of the Graduate School," while Li PP and 26 others are recommended for the title of "Outstanding Communist Party Member of the Graduate School."
