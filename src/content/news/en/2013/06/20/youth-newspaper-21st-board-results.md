It is reported that the election meeting for the 21st editorial board of *Shantou Youth Newspaper* was held on the afternoon of May 25, 2013. The meeting reviewed the work report of the 20th editorial board and elected the new board by vote.

The announced structure contains 10 position assignments: one editor-in-chief, two deputy editors-in-chief and heads of seven departments, covering editorial work, reporting, photography, new media, visual design, public relations and planning, and administrative affairs. The new editorial board will be responsible for campus newspaper production, photography, new-media operations, visual design and organizational coordination.

The STU Youth League Committee and *Shantou Youth Newspaper* released the election results. Details of individual appointments are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
