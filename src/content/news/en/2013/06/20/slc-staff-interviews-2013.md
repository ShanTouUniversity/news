It is reported that Shantou University's Learning Support Center (SLC) has selected 16 candidates for interviews based on applications received by June 19 and the requirements for its student staff recruitment. The interviews will be held at the Learning Support Center on June 21, and candidates are expected to arrive on time according to the schedule.

## Interview Schedule

| Time | Location | Candidates |
|------|----------|------------|
| 9:30–10:30 a.m. | Learning Support Center (SLC) | 8 |
| 10:30–11:30 a.m. | Learning Support Center (SLC) | 8 |

Questions about the interviews may be directed to SLC at 86502250 or slc@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
