The STU School of Science will organize graduation group photographs for its 2013 graduating class from 09:15 to 10:00 on June 24 in front of the Administration Building. If it rains, a different location will be announced.

Each graduating class should assemble 20 minutes early, wear academic regalia, and arrange its photo formation in advance. The Biology, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry classes are scheduled as follows:

| Class | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2009 Biology | 09:15–09:25 |
| 2009 Mathematics | 09:25–09:35 |
| 2009 Physics | 09:35–09:45 |
| 2009 Chemistry | 09:45–09:55 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science)
