It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University will recruit 180 student volunteers from across the university to support orientation for the 2013 incoming class and help new students settle into campus life.

The volunteers will assist with new student reception, related logistical services and registration procedures. Applicants are expected to have good communication and teamwork skills, take their responsibilities seriously, follow work arrangements and ensure that they have no conflicting classes or other activities during the orientation period. Selected volunteers must return to campus by August 25, 2013, to attend work arrangements and training.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Eligible applicants | Students from across the university |
| Positions | 180 volunteers |
| Application deadline | 24:00 on June 30, 2013 |
| Service period | The orientation period beginning September 2, 2013 |
| Application method | The application form and submission details are specified in the attachments to the original OA notice. |
| Selection | Applicants will be selected based on applications; earlier applications receive priority in cases of equal suitability |

The selected volunteer list will be announced through the office automation system and the BBS volunteer forum. The application form and related event attachments are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ The application form and event attachments are available on the OA system.
