It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University announced on June 20 its commendation decision for outstanding student cadres in the 2012–2013 academic year, with a total of 27 graduate students awarded the title of "Outstanding Student Cadre of the Graduate School."

According to the school, over the past year, thanks to the support of university leaders and relevant departments as well as the joint efforts of all supervisors and students, graduate student affairs were carried out smoothly and achieved encouraging results. To recognize exemplary individuals and encourage student cadres to strive for excellence, candidates went through personal application, recommendation by the supervising unit, and review by the Graduate School before the final list was confirmed.

The Graduate School expressed the hope that the recognized students and all graduate students will take this opportunity to keep forging ahead and studying diligently, contributing their own strength to the development of the university.
