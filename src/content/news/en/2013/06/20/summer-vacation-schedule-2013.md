The Office of Party and Administration Affairs at Shantou University issued a notice on June 20 outlining arrangements for the 2013 summer vacation, summer term and fall term. The schedule covers the student vacation period, registration and class dates, freshman reporting, and summer work arrangements for faculty and administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff.

## Student Vacation and Semester Dates

The summer vacation for enrolled students will run from June 30 to August 17. Registration for the summer term will take place on August 18, with classes beginning on August 19. New students will report on September 1. Students will register for the fall term on September 15, and classes will begin on September 16. Military training for the 2012 undergraduate cohort will be arranged separately by the Student Affairs Office.

| Item | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Student summer vacation | June 30 to August 17 |
| Summer-term registration | August 18 |
| Summer-term classes begin | August 19 |
| Freshman reporting | September 1 |
| Fall-term registration | September 15 |
| Fall-term classes begin | September 16 |

Students who remain on campus during the vacation and need accommodation must apply to the counselor's office in the student residence area. Arrangements will be coordinated by the relevant offices. The university also asked schools and relevant departments to strengthen safety education for students and faculty during the vacation. Graduate training units and supervisors will arrange teaching, research and practical activities according to training plans and actual circumstances.

## Faculty and Staff Arrangements

Faculty members will begin their summer vacation on July 3 and return to campus on August 16 to prepare for the new semester. They will also guide freshmen in course selection according to arrangements made by their departments and participate in summer-term teaching from August 19. Teaching units were encouraged to use the summer term for teaching and research activities and to arrange suitable social-practice opportunities for students who are not taking courses.

Administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff will continue to follow the university's winter and summer work system. Administrative and service-support units must maintain normal administrative services during the vacation, while units may arrange staff rotation according to operational needs. From July 3 to August 14, the adjusted working hours will be 8:30-11:30 a.m. and 3:00-5:00 p.m. Staff on rotation must remain reachable and report for duty when needed. Normal working hours will resume for all administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff on August 15.

All units were also required to submit their vacation staffing arrangements to the Office of Party and Administration Affairs by the end of the working day on June 25.
