Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has announced the second batch of core courses. The batch includes 16 courses covering the core curriculum areas of culture and values, citizenship and society, and science and scientific methods. The university will provide funding for the selected courses.

The university had previously invited teaching units and faculty members to propose core courses and received more than 70 submissions. After two rounds of review by the core-curriculum expert group, as well as multiple rounds of communication and revisions with course proposers, the university finalized this batch. The Academic Affairs Office and the expert group will continue to solicit new core-course proposals each semester.
