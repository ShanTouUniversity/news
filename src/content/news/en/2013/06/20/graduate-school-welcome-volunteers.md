The Graduate School recently announced the recruitment of 50 student volunteers from across the school to support its 2013 orientation activities for new graduate students.

The volunteers will assist with collecting materials, checking information, entering data and guiding students on the registration day for the 2013 cohort. They will also support related activities during the new-student orientation period. Applicants are expected to be careful, courteous, responsible and willing to follow work arrangements. They must be able to return to campus by August 30, 2013.

Applications close at 8 p.m. on June 30, 2013. The Graduate School will select volunteers according to the number of applications and the needs of the work.

Graduate students interested in applying should complete the application form and submit it through the method specified in the original OA notice. The detailed application requirements and selection arrangements are subject to the Graduate School notice.
