The Alumni Affairs Office of Shantou University Business School has launched a graduation-message campaign titled “Graduation, I Want to Tell You...” for the Business School's Class of 2013. The campaign invites graduating students to record memories and messages from their university years.

The campaign is open to the Business School's 2013 undergraduate and graduate students, as well as MBA and course-program participants. It runs from June 20 to 28. Participants can search for and join the Sina Weibo topic “Graduation, I Want to Tell You...” to share their messages, then send their name and contact details by private message. Submissions must comply with Sina Weibo's posting-length requirements, and selected entries will later be published on the Business School alumni website.

The organizers said that five lucky alumni will be selected at random after the campaign, with commemorative gifts presented to them. The campaign is intended to give graduates an opportunity to look back on their shared campus experience and preserve those memories in writing.
