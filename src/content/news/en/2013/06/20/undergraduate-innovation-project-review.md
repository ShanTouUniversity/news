Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office recently completed the final evaluation of selected undergraduate innovation experiment projects and undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects, and released the results on June 20, 2013.

The evaluation covered 83 projects from seven schools. The projects included 45 undergraduate innovation experiment projects and 17 undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects; some other projects applied for an extension or were terminated. After reviewing the final reports, hearing project defenses, and assessing project objectives, innovation, outputs, and use of funds, the expert panel determined that 62 projects passed the final evaluation, including five projects rated excellent.

According to the Academic Affairs Office, the projects produced papers, research reports, software, physical designs, websites, publications, and exhibitions. A total of 25 projects produced 31 papers, while 14 projects completed 17 research reports. Some entrepreneurship practice projects also advanced company formation, brand design, product development, sales, and promotion. One project won an award in the “Challenge Cup” competition, and another established a company and carried out product development, sales, and promotion.

The office said that the projects receiving stronger evaluations generally placed emphasis on research-process management, followed their plans, and organized their outputs in a timely manner. Projects granted extensions are required to continue their research according to follow-up plans, seek guidance when problems arise, and complete the remaining evaluation requirements.

Detailed project evaluation results and lists are available in the original attachment on Shantou University's OA system. As the attachment contains project-level details and other materials, its contents are not reproduced here.
