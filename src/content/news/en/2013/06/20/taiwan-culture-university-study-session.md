Shantou University will hold a briefing on June 22 about the self-funded study programme at Chinese Culture University in Taiwan scheduled for September 2013. Two staff members from Chinese Culture University's Cross-Strait Affairs Group will visit the university to introduce the programme. Students planning to study in Taiwan and other interested members of the university community are welcome to attend.

## Briefing Details

- **Time**: 4:00-6:00 p.m. on June 22, 2013
- **Venue**: ACC Room 3
- **Programme period**: September 2013
- **Programme type**: Self-funded study at Chinese Culture University in Taiwan
- **Speakers**: Liu Junliang, editor, and Diao Shengfeng, project manager, from Chinese Culture University's Cross-Strait Affairs Group

The briefing will provide information about the study programme. Students interested in joining the September 2013 programme can attend to learn more about the arrangements.
