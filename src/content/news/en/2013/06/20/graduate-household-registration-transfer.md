Shantou University has announced arrangements for 2013 graduates whose household registration was transferred to the university when they enrolled. Applications will be handled from June 21 through July 3, with service available on Saturdays. Graduates may complete the procedure using their graduation certificate number, which is listed in the notice attachments.

## Procedures

Graduates who need to transfer their household registration should collect their household registration card from the Security Office on the second floor of the campus post office and bring their identity card. Those transferring their registration to urban areas of Shantou, including Chenghai, Chaoyang, Chaonan and Nan'ao, may take the card directly to the police station at their destination to complete local registration. They do not need to first visit Tuopu Police Station to process the transfer.

Graduates returning their registration to their place of origin must write the full destination address on the back of the household registration card. Those transferring it to an employer must also provide a registration certificate or an acceptance letter from the employer's human resources department.

After receiving their graduation certificate, graduates who need to authorize another person to handle the procedure must prepare an authorization letter, a photocopy of the graduation certificate stamped by their school, and photocopies of both parties' identity cards. Authorization is not permitted before the graduation certificate has been issued.

The university reminded graduates to complete local registration within the one-month validity period stated on the Household Registration Transfer Certificate after the transfer. Once this period expires, the certificate cannot be returned for reprocessing. The household registration office of Tuopu Police Station can be reached at 82756003.
