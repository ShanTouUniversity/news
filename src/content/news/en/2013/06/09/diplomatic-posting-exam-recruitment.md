The Ministry of Foreign Affairs is scheduled to hold its ninth examination for cadre secondment in Beijing in August 2013. Candidates who pass the examination will be seconded by the ministry to work at overseas diplomatic and consular missions in stages and will receive diplomatic personnel benefits during their secondment. Following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Foreign Affairs Office, Shantou University's Office of International Exchange and Cooperation has begun the internal recommendation process.

Interested personnel should prepare their application materials as required and submit them to the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation by the end of the workday on June 13, 2013. After review and approval by the university, STU will consolidate the information and submit it to the Guangdong Provincial Foreign Affairs Office by June 15.
