Three works associated with Shantou University received Excellence Awards in the 2012 Shantou Literary and Art Awards, according to an award announcement issued by the university's Publicity Department on June 9, 2013. The awards were organized and assessed by the Publicity Department of the Shantou Municipal Committee of the Communist Party of China.

The recognized works are the commentary collection *Feminist Studies* by Zhuang Y, the print *I Am Pregnant* by Lin Dexiong, and the paper *An Analysis of the Authenticity of Documentary Photography from the Perspective of Semiotic Theory* by Xie Lin.

According to the relevant decision, the 2012 Shantou Literary and Art Awards included one Special Award, two Achievement Awards for individuals, three Creative Awards and 30 Excellence Awards. The university's Publicity Department congratulated the award recipients.
