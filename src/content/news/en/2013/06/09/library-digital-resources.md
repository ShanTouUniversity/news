It is reported that Shantou University Library issued the Digital Resources Update (Issue 6 of 2013, No. 12 overall) on June 9, introducing digital resources and online study services available to faculty and students. The update covers journal retrieval, standards and patent searches, judicial examination preparation, professional examination practice and book citation analysis.

The resources highlighted in the update include the VIP Journal integrated service platform for Chinese scientific and technical journal searches, citation analysis and scientific indicator queries; the CNKI technical reference resource experience service for browsing and downloading national standards, industry standards, patents and scientific achievements; Peking University Legal Information's online judicial examination service, which includes mock examinations, examination outlines, essential regulations and legal documents; the Yinfu B12 examination question bank, which covers examinations such as the College English Test and computer proficiency examinations; and the 2012 book citation report in the Duxiu Knowledge Base, which helps users examine citation patterns for books and authors.

The resources can be accessed through the Library's database page or the relevant platform websites. The VIP Journal platform is available at http://lib.cqvip.com, the CNKI technical reference resource service at http://q.help.cnki.net/JSCKServiceActive.aspx, and the Yinfu examination question bank at http://www.yfzxmn.cn. The access pages for Peking University Legal Information and the Duxiu Knowledge Base can be found through the Library's database page.
