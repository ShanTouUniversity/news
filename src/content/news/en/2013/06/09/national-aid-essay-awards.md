The Department of Education of Guangdong Province held the “National Student Aid, Help Me Grow” essay competition in July 2012. The competition aimed to promote national and provincial student-aid policies, highlight the achievements of financial support for students from economically disadvantaged families, and encourage students to develop a sense of aspiration and gratitude.

Two essays submitted by Shantou University students won third prize in the university category. They were *Walking on the Sunny Road*, written by Lai ZJ, a 2011-level Applied Physics student in the School of Science, and *National Student Aid: The “Visible Hand” in Higher Education*, written by Chen GX, a 2011-level student in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation in the School of Engineering. The university congratulated the award-winning students.
