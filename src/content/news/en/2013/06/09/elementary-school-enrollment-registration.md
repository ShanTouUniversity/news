The STU Affiliated Primary School recently announced arrangements for registering first-grade students for its 2013 autumn intake. The registration is for eligible children of staff members based at the main campus.

According to the notice, children of main-campus staff who will be six years old that year, meaning those born on or before August 31, 2007, may register as required. Parents were asked to visit the principal's office of the affiliated primary school from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on June 13 to complete registration so that textbooks could be ordered in time. They were required to bring the child's birth certificate and household registration booklet, as well as the parent's staff ID.

The notice is dated June 9, 2012, and its body also lists June 13, 2012, as the registration date. These dates differ from the 2013 autumn intake named in the title and the OA publication date of June 9, 2013; all details above have been retained from the original notice record.
