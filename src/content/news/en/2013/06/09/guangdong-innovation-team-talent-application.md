The Guangdong Provincial Leading Group Office for Talent Affairs has launched applications for its fourth batch of innovation research teams and leading talents. Shantou University's Office of Human Resources has circulated the application announcement to the university's schools and begun coordinating internal applications.

The university encourages each school to identify and recruit high-level talent in accordance with the eligibility requirements in the announcement, and to organize qualified research teams and individuals to prepare applications. Application materials must be submitted to the Office of Human Resources by June 26, with an electronic copy sent to `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn`.

Questions during the application process may be directed to Xu Yan at the Office of Human Resources at 86502351 or `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn`.
