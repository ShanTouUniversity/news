It is reported that Shantou University announced its 2012 commendations for student extracurricular academic and sci-tech activities on June 9, 2013. The university will recognize individuals and groups with outstanding achievements under the Regulations of Shantou University for Evaluating Student Performance in Sci-Tech Competitions.

## Commendation Results

| Award | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Supervisor | Six teachers received the title |
| Student Research Innovation Award | Researchers of 12 student research projects received the award |
| Outstanding Organization Award | Two units, including the College of Science, received the award |

The university encouraged faculty and students to learn from the recognized individuals and groups, continue advancing student academic and sci-tech activities, and contribute to the cultivation of high-caliber talent and the university's development.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
