It is reported that Shantou University's College of Science will hold a sharing session for its spring 2013 "Toward the Ocean" public service course on Monday, June 10, from 9 to 11:30 a.m. in Room 303 of Building E. The session is open to all students and staff.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Spring 2013 "Toward the Ocean" Public Service Course Sharing Session |
| Time | Monday, June 10, 2013, 9:00–11:30 a.m. |
| Venue | Room 303, Building E |
| Organizer | Shantou University College of Science |
| Participants | All students and staff |

"Toward the Ocean" is a public service course offered by the College of Science at Shantou University. The course group will use the session to share the course's learning and practical activities, and welcomes students and staff across the university to attend.
