Shantou University has announced the results of the 2012-2013 Li Ka Shing Foundation Awards for Outstanding Teaching. Following recommendations from the schools and a review by the selection committee, Associate Professor Yu Jin of the School of Engineering and Professor Liu Daojun of the Medical College were selected for the award.

The award recognizes faculty members who have demonstrated outstanding performance in teaching. The university has formally released the results of this year's selection.

## Award Recipients

| Academic Year | Award | Recipient | Unit and Title |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2012-2013 | Li Ka Shing Foundation Award for Outstanding Teaching | Yu Jin | Associate Professor, School of Engineering |
| 2012-2013 | Li Ka Shing Foundation Award for Outstanding Teaching | Liu Daojun | Professor, Medical College |
