It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 9, 2013, regarding exam week services at the Mutual Learning Center (SLC).

Fixed academic consultation services for this semester will end on Saturday of week 16. From week 17 to Thursday of week 18 (June 16–27), all discussion rooms will remain open, providing academic consultation, group discussion and self-study services.

Details of the fixed consultation courses are available in the OA attachment. Students are welcome to use the SLC services.
