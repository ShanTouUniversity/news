Shantou University will hold a review and awards ceremony for its 2012 extracurricular academic and scientific activities at 7:20 p.m. on June 13, 2013, in Conference Room ACC3. The event will review the activities and recognize individuals and groups that achieved outstanding results during the 2012 activities.

The ceremony will also present awards to students who received honors in the ninth Shantou University Challenge Cup Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Competition. Members of the university's student extracurricular academic and scientific activities leading group, relevant officers and Communist Youth League officials from each school, representatives of award-winning groups, individuals and projects, and student representatives will attend.
