It is reported that the interview results for the "Huanyu Ambassador" recruitment for the 2013 "I Love Lighting · Chaoren Cup" International Sailing Regatta have been announced, with ten students including Zhou Jiahui successfully selected.

## Regatta Background

The 2013 "I Love Lighting · Chaoren Cup" International Sailing Regatta will be held in Shantou Inner Bay from December 21 to 23. The event has been successfully held for two consecutive years since 2011, and the Shantou-based vessel "Chaoren Jia Yi" once represented the Chinese national sailing team at the 25th King's Cup International Sailing Regatta with excellent results. This year, the addition of "International" to the event name reflects the State Administration of Sport's emphasis on and support for sailing in Shantou.

## Huanyu Ambassadors

Aligning with the Business School's educational philosophy of "excellence in business, reaching across the globe," the organizing committee recruited 10 "Huanyu Ambassadors" through the school to foster cultural exchange between the international sailing event and university life. After interviews, Zhou Jiahui and nine other students were selected as ambassadors for this year's regatta.
