It is reported that the Shantou University Volunteers Association will hold a 20th anniversary charity exhibition on December 13, comprehensively presenting the organization's development journey and public welfare achievements over the past twenty years since its founding in 1994.

Founded in 1994, the Shantou University Volunteers Association has seen its volunteers steadfastly committed to service without interruption. Over twenty years, the association has gradually explored an innovative path transitioning from traditional volunteer service to a "service-learning" model, extending from social practice to public welfare courses, and evolving from providing simple services to engaging in more in-depth public welfare activities.

The exhibition showcases through visual materials and activity photographs the development trajectory of the association over twenty years, highlighting the evolution from single events to diversified public welfare programs, as well as the growth stories of outstanding volunteers who have dedicated themselves to serving others and contributing to society.
