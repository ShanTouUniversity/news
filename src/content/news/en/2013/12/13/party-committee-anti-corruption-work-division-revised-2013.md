Shantou University’s Party Committee issued a revised document assigning work on building Party integrity and combating corruption on December 13, 2013. The revision was discussed and approved at the 15th meeting of the university Party Committee Standing Committee in 2013. Numbered No. 38 [2013] of the STU Party Committee, it applied to units on the main campus.

The revision updated an earlier work-assignment document issued in 2005. Specific responsibilities are set out in the OA attachment; this report does not add details that were not included in the notice body.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Party Committee)
