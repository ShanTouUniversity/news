Tian Changen, a professor at Guangzhou University’s School of Life Sciences, was scheduled to give a research talk at Shantou University from 10 a.m. to noon on Sunday, December 15, 2013. The lecture, part of the College of Science’s Science Salon series, was to take place in meeting room ACC #3. Its title was “The Calmodulin-Binding Protein AtIQM1 Participates in Stomatal Movement and Defense Regulation.”

Tian’s research focuses on the functions and applications of genes including the plant IQM family and fungal laccases. Faculty and students interested in the subject were invited to attend.
