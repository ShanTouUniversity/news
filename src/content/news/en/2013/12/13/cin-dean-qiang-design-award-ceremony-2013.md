It is reported that the Chin Shu-King Design Award 2013 ceremony will be held at the Art Museum of the College of Arts and Design, Shantou University, on December 15. Experts, scholars, and past award winners from across the country will gather at STU to celebrate this annual design industry event.

The award ceremony and the opening of the exhibition of winning works will take place at 4:00 PM at the College of Arts and Design Art Museum. The exhibition will showcase outstanding design works from universities nationwide, covering graphic design, environmental design, product design, and other fields.

In addition, renowned designer Chin Shu-King will deliver an academic lecture at the university, sharing his design philosophy and creative insights with faculty and students, further enriching the campus academic atmosphere.
