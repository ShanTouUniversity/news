It is reported that the Law School at Shantou University will invite Wang Jie, director of the Hearing and Speech Rehabilitation Department at Shantou Disability Rehabilitation Center and president of the Blue Sky Volunteer Association, to deliver an academic lecture titled "Organization and Management of the Blue Sky Volunteer Association" on December 15.

Since founding the volunteer team through the internet in January 2002, Wang Jie and her Blue Sky volunteers have long provided voluntary services to over 1,000 disadvantaged families. Under her leadership, the Blue Sky Volunteer Association has organized and planned diverse public welfare activities, including book delivery to rural areas, post-disaster cleanup, charity nutrition meals, and the Chongyang Banquet for 100 Elders, making a significant impact in the local public welfare sector.

The lecture will share insights on the organizational structure, management models, and operational experience of the Blue Sky Volunteer Association, providing valuable reference for students interested in nonprofit management.
