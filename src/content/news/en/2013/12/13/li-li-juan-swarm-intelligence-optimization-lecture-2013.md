It is reported that the School of Engineering at Shantou University will invite Professor Li Lijuan, a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Civil and Transportation Engineering, Guangdong University of Technology, to deliver an academic lecture on December 20.

The lecture, titled "Swarm Intelligence Optimization Algorithms and Their Applications in Structural Optimization Design," focuses on the theoretical framework of swarm intelligence optimization algorithms and their practical applications in engineering structural optimization design. Professor Li's report covers cutting-edge methods such as ant colony optimization and particle swarm optimization, offering valuable insights for intelligent development in structural optimization design.

A question-and-answer session will follow the report. Faculty, graduate students, undergraduate students, and all interested individuals are welcome to attend and engage in discussion.
