Shantou University’s intermediate Qimuzhang outdoor expedition advanced to a second selection round. The Student Affairs Office announced on December 13, 2013, that 40 of the 79 faculty and students who took the first fitness test had passed. The second-round “Sangpu Mountain Shuzhai Cave Wilderness Skills Workshop” was scheduled for Sunday, December 15, from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.; participants were to meet at the university motto monument at 7:57 a.m.

For the workshop, 36 students would form six teams and take part in wilderness survival challenges, with coaches scoring their performance. The 12 members of the top two teams, six outstanding students from other teams and four faculty members—22 people in total—were to advance to the Qimuzhang expedition. The workshop was to take place on campus and around Shuzhai Cave in Sangpu Mountain, with a RMB 5 lunch food fee per participant. Student names and personal contact details are not reproduced.
