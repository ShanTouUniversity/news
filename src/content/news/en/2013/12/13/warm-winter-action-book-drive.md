It is reported that the Youth Volunteer Association of Shantou University's College of Arts and Design has launched a "Warm Winter Action" book and stationery drive to collect learning supplies for children at Diqing No. 2 Primary School in Yunnan Province.

During the event, association volunteers handcrafted various DIY souvenirs for sale, with all proceeds dedicated to purchasing learning materials for the target school. The association is also calling on the university community to donate healthy books, notebooks, pens, rulers, erasers, and other study supplies.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Drive Period | December 15–25, 2013 |
| Location | Jin San Jiao (Golden Triangle) |
| Items Needed | Books, notebooks, pens, rulers, erasers, and other study supplies |

The drive has received a positive response from faculty and students across campus, showcasing the spirit of public service and generosity among STU students.
