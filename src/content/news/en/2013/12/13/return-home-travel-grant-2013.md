It is reported that the "Love伴归程" winter break travel grant program, hosted by the Firefly Charity Foundation of the Shantou University Volunteers Association, has officially opened for applications. The program aims to alleviate the financial burden of travel costs for economically disadvantaged students returning home for the winter break, ensuring that every student can safely make the journey home.

The program is open to all undergraduate and graduate students. Eligible applicants must meet the following criteria: confirmed plans to return home during the winter break; economically disadvantaged with a frugal lifestyle; planning to travel by means other than air and able to provide relevant travel documentation.

The Firefly Charity Foundation, a public welfare organization under the STU Volunteers Association, has long been committed to supporting economically disadvantaged students through travel grants, holiday assistance, and other forms of practical help.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Item | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Target | All undergraduate and graduate students |
| Basic Requirement | Confirmed plans to return home during winter break |
| Financial Requirement | Economically disadvantaged, frugal lifestyle |
| Travel Requirement | Non-air travel, with supporting documentation |
