Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design scheduled an employment meeting for its 2014 graduating class for 3 p.m. on December 25, 2013, in the lecture hall on the school’s first floor. The school said the meeting was intended to help graduates understand employment conditions, develop informed career choices and prepare to enter the workforce.

Graduating students were asked to attend on time.
