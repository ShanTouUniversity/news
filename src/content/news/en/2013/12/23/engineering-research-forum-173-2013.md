| Speaker | Dr. Wu Chuanhai, Deputy Chief Engineer, Professor and Senior Engineer, Guangdong Hualu Transportation Technology Co., Ltd. |
| Time | 10:00, December 26, 2013 |
| Venue | Room D503, Academic Building |
| Participants | Undergraduates, postgraduates and doctoral students of the Department of Civil Engineering and related faculty, with other interested teachers and students welcome |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |
