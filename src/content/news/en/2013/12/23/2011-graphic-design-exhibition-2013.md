It is reported that the annual exhibition of student works from the 2011-level Graphic Design program at the School of Arts and Design, Shantou University, will be held from December 24 to 26 in the first-floor exhibition hall. The exhibition will showcase the academic achievements and creative practices of the students in graphic design, covering visual communication, brand design, layout design and other directions.

## Exhibition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exhibition | 2011 Graphic Design Student Exhibition |
| Dates | December 24–26, 2013 |
| Opening Ceremony | 3:00 PM, December 24 |
| Venue | First-floor Exhibition Hall, School of Arts and Design |
| Organizer | School of Arts and Design |

Faculty and students are welcome to visit and exchange ideas on design.
