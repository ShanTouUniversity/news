It is reported that the International Exchange and Cooperation Office and the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University have jointly launched the selection process for the "STU-HKUST Global China Studies 3+1 Bachelor/Master Joint Training Program." The project agreement has been formally signed, aiming to enhance academic exchange between the two universities and promote friendly cooperation.

## Program Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program Name | STU-HKUST "Global China Studies" 3+1 Joint Program |
| Eligible Candidates | Outstanding third-year undergraduate students at the main campus |
| English Requirement | IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100 / CSE 600 |
| Training Model | 3 years at STU + 1 year at HKUST |
| Degree | Combined bachelor's/master's program |
| Organizers | International Exchange and Cooperation Office, Academic Affairs Office |

Applicants must demonstrate strong English proficiency. Specific selection criteria and procedures are detailed in the OA notice attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (International Exchange and Cooperation Office, Academic Affairs Office)
