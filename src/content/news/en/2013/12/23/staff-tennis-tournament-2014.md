It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University will host a staff tennis tournament in early January 2014 at the STU tennis courts. All faculty and staff who enjoy playing tennis are welcome to sign up.

The competition includes men's singles, women's singles, and doubles (mixed-gender teams allowed). The tournament will adopt a round-robin format after random draw grouping. Specific match times will be announced based on the number of registrations.

## Tournament Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Early January 2014 (specific time to be announced) |
| Venue | STU Tennis Courts |
| Events | Men's Singles, Women's Singles, Doubles (mixed-gender allowed) |
| Format | Round-robin after random draw grouping |
| Organizer | University Labor Union |

Interested participants are encouraged to register with the Labor Union promptly.
