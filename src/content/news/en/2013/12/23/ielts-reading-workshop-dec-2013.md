It is reported that the English Language Institute of Shantou University will host an IELTS reading skills workshop on December 25 from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM at CILL (Center for International Language Learning).

This workshop is part of the IELTS preparation series, designed to help test-takers master problem-solving techniques and answering strategies for the reading section, thereby improving their scores.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | IELTS Reading Skills Workshop |
| Time | Thursday, December 25, 2013, 16:00–18:00 |
| Venue | CILL |
| Organizer | English Language Institute |

Students preparing for the IELTS are welcome to attend.
