It is reported that Shantou University's 2014 New Year Concert will be jointly organized by the College of Art & Design and the School of Arts Education, celebrating the arrival of the new year with elegant music.

The concert has invited the South China University of Technology Youth Symphony Orchestra to perform, conducted by renowned conductor Mr. He Ping. The STU Chorale will also participate in the performance. Dean Wang Shouzhi of the College of Art & Design will serve as the host.

## Program Highlights (Partial)

| Piece | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| *Symphony No. 5* ("Fate") | Beethoven classic |
| *The Red Detachment of Women* | Excerpts from classic Chinese ballet |
| *The Land of Hope and Glory* | British military march |

A highlight of this year's concert is the seamless fusion of Chinese folk music with Western classical traditions, offering audiences a refreshing auditory experience.

Specific date and venue information will be announced shortly.
