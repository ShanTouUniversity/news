It is reported that 15 public courses will be offered university-wide during the Spring 2014 semester (2013-2014 academic year). The courses are open to faculty who have completed the required training and wish to teach them. Faculty who have passed the training should submit their course selection forms by 5:00 PM on December 25, 2013.

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Semester | Spring 2014 (2013-2014 academic year) |
| Number of Public Courses | 15 |
| Eligible Candidates | Faculty who have completed training |
| Deadline | 5:00 PM, December 25, 2013 |
| Submission | Course selection information form |

Trained faculty members are urged to submit their selections promptly; late submissions will not be accepted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
