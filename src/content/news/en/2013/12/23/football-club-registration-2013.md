It is reported that the Shantou University Sports Department Football Club has opened registration for the Spring 2014 semester. The football club is one of the pilot projects for advanced undergraduate teaching reform, aiming to promote advanced football culture and carry out various forms of football training and exchange activities.

## Registration Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project Type | Advanced undergraduate teaching reform pilot |
| Goal | Promote football culture through training and exchange |
| Participation | Earn PE course credits + experience training, matches, refereeing, and event organization |
| Spots Available | Approximately 30 (first come, first served) |
| Registration | Through the Sports Department Football Club |

Students who join the club can complete their PE course credits while immersing themselves in the joys of football training, competition, refereeing, and event organization. All students are encouraged to sign up.
