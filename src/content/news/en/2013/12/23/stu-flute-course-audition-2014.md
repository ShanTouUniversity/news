The Arts Education Center of Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design announced on December 23, 2013, that the Flute Performance course taught by Lin Weiliang for the spring 2014 semester was scheduled to hold an audition the following evening. The course section number is 67046.

The audition is scheduled from 9 to 10 p.m. in rehearsal room STU101 at the Arts Education Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Arts Education Center)
