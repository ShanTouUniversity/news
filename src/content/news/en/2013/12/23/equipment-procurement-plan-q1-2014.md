Shantou University’s Office of Asset Management opened submissions for its first-quarter 2014 equipment procurement plan on December 23, 2013. Units were asked to submit both paper and electronic plans to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by January 15, 2014. Late submissions would be deferred to the second quarter.

Under the notice, purchases budgeted at no more than RMB 20,000 were to be made through the university equipment bidding website. Lenovo, HP, Dell and Apple computers remained subject to the university’s framework-supply arrangement. Plans above RMB 20,000 required a plan form and proposal and approval under the university’s spending-authority rules; equipment priced above RMB 100,000 also required a feasibility report. Computer purchases required a separate registration form, while air-conditioner requests had to state the installation location and condition of existing equipment.

Plans were to identify equipment and account for taxes, shipping and after-sales service. Questions could be directed to Ms. Huang at the Equipment and Materials Management Office (86502558; `zxhuang@stu.edu.cn`).
