Shantou University’s “Three Styles” Development Office reported results from two class-attendance checks during the 14th week of the fall 2013 semester. The checks took place at E Building at 8 a.m. on December 17 and 2 p.m. on December 19.

The December 17 check recorded one student arriving up to five minutes late and seven arriving more than five minutes late. The December 19 check recorded eight and five students in those respective categories. The original notice referred to an attached list; names and other identifying details are omitted here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (“Three Styles” Development Office)
