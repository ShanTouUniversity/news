It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will host an academic lecture on December 26 from 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM in the Business School Academic Seminar Room (2nd Floor, Wenxi Building). Associate Professor Zong Jichuan from Dongbei University of Finance and Economics will deliver the talk, with Professor Lyu Yuan presiding.

The lecture, titled "Experimental Economics: Real Economy in the Lab," will explore the essence of experimental methods and how behavioral decision-making under controlled conditions can reveal real economic behaviors through laboratory settings.
