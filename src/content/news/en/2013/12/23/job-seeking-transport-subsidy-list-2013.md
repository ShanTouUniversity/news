It is reported that based on submissions from various colleges, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University plans to provide transportation subsidies to selected graduating students for attending job fairs, civil service examinations and other job-seeking activities. The proposed list is now open for public review.

## Notice Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Content | Proposed list of graduates eligible for job-seeking transportation subsidies |
| Comment Deadline | December 27, 2013, 12:00 PM |
| Feedback Channel | Written or email to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center |
| Subsidy Purpose | Transportation costs for job fairs, civil service exams, etc. |

Anyone with objections may submit them in writing or by email to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center before the deadline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
