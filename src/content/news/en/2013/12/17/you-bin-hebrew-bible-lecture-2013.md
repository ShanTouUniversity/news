The Center for Christian Studies at the College of Liberal Arts, Shantou University, will host a lecture by Professor You Bin on December 24 (Tuesday) at 7:00 PM in the Library Auditorium. The lecture, titled "The Core Values of the Hebrew Bible and Its Significance and Implication for the Chinese People," will explore the foundational values of the Hebrew Bible and their cultural relevance to contemporary Chinese society.

The lecture is organized by the Center for Christian Studies and open to all faculty, staff, and students.
