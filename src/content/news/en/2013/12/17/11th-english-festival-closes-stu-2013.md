The 11th English Festival at Shantou University was held from December 13 to 15 at the Grand Auditorium, themed "My Life in Our World." The event aimed to enhance students' comprehensive English proficiency and broaden their international perspectives.

Contestants from Sun Yat-sen University, Seoul National University (South Korea), Hanshan Normal University, and the University of Macau joined STU students in a series of compelling competitions. STU participants performed outstandingly, showcasing the linguistic competence and spirited demeanor of the university's student body.
