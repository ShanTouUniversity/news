It is reported that the Shantou University Academic Affairs Office recently announced the recognition results for the third batch of core courses. Out of 22 course proposals submitted by faculty members, 11 core courses have been approved for funding after thorough review by the core course expert committee across two rounds of evaluation and multiple rounds of communication and revision.

The Academic Affairs Office stated that it will continue to solicit new core course proposals each semester to promote ongoing curriculum development and teaching reform.
