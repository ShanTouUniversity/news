Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts announced information about “The Long Night of Long Poems—2013 Shantou University Poetry Gathering” on December 17, 2013. The accompanying biographies introduced poets Ouyang Jianghe, Wang Jiaxin and Xiao Xiao, as well as Sinologist Gu Bin.

The available notice body did not specify the event’s date or venue, so no further schedule is stated here. Readers should refer to the original STU OA notice for the arrangements.
