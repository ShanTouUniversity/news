The Business School at Shantou University has invited Dr. Honglei Li, Senior Lecturer at the Department of Mathematics and Information Sciences, to deliver an academic lecture titled "The Play of IS Socialization—Dancing into the Future Digital World."

Dr. Li's presentation will focus on the frontier topic of information system socialization, exploring development trends and practical pathways for IS in the context of digital transformation, offering deep insights into the future digital world for faculty and students alike.

Faculty, staff, and students from the Business School and all interested parties are welcome to attend.
