Shantou University’s College of Engineering planned to hold its “Future Promise” themed Youth League activity on Saturday, December 21, 2013, for first- to third-year students. The activity focused on study habits and was scheduled in department-specific sessions: Mechanical and Electrical Engineering from 2 to 2:40 p.m.; Electronic Engineering from 2:40 to 3:20 p.m.; Civil Engineering from 3:20 to 4 p.m.; and Computer Science from 4 to 4:40 p.m.

Sessions for Mechanical and Electrical Engineering and Civil Engineering were assigned to E Lecture Room 101; those for Electronic Engineering and Computer Science were assigned to E Lecture Room 102. The activity aimed to encourage positive study attitudes, classroom participation and exam conduct.
