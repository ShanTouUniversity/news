The Graduate School of Shantou University has announced that, following on-site presentations and secret ballot voting by the 8th Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee, three disciplines—Law, Nursing, and Social Work—have been recommended to apply for new master's professional degree programs in 2013.

The public review period runs from December 17 to 23. Any objections regarding the proposed programs may be submitted to the relevant university departments in accordance with the notice requirements.

The addition of these three master's professional degree programs will further expand STU's graduate education discipline layout and enhance the university's capacity in applied talent cultivation.
