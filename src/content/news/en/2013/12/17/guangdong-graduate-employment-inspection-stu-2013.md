A Guangdong Education Department inspection team was scheduled to visit Shantou University on December 18, 2013, to review the university’s graduate employment work. The Student Affairs Office announced the meeting arrangements on December 17. Both sessions were set for Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center.

| Meeting | Scheduled time at the time | Participants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-assessment report briefing | 8:30 a.m., December 18 | Party-branch secretaries or deputy secretaries of colleges and residential colleges, employment counselors, relevant Student Affairs Office leaders and Career Guidance Center staff |
| Feedback meeting | 4 p.m., December 18 | The same group |

Attendees were asked to arrive 10 minutes early.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
