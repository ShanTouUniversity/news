Luo Nianchao, Shantou University’s Deputy Party Secretary, was scheduled to hold a university leadership reception at 3 p.m. on Thursday, December 19, 2013. The Party and Administration Office announced the arrangement on December 17. Visitors were asked to make an appointment in advance by calling 86502306.
