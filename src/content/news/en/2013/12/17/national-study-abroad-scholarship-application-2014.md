Shantou University’s Human Resources Office circulated a Guangdong Education Department notice on applications for 2014 funding for overseas study on December 17, 2013, asking relevant units to inform eligible faculty and staff.

Applications for the Senior Research Scholar and Visiting Scholar programs, including postdoctoral researchers, were due to the Human Resources Office by January 9, 2014. Materials for the Special Program for Arts Talent Development and the Short-Term Overseas Exchange Program for Doctoral Supervisors were due by March 24, 2014; late submissions would not be accepted. The contact was Chen Ying, at 86502340 or wing@stu.edu.cn.
