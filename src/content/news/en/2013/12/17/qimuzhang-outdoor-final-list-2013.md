It is reported that the selection process for the Qimuzhang mid-level outdoor expansion activity at Shantou University has concluded as of December 15. After comprehensive evaluation through two rounds — a physical fitness test and a wilderness survival workshop at Shanpu Mountain — a final list of 22 participants, including four faculty members, has been determined for the activity scheduled from December 20 to 22.

## Selection Process

The activity received a strong response from the campus community, with 79 faculty and students participating in the first-round physical fitness test. Those who passed advanced to the second round — a wilderness survival workshop at Shanpu Mountain's Shuzhai Cave. Coaches from the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association evaluated participants based on their performance in both rounds, ensuring a fair and transparent selection process.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Activity Dates | Afternoon of December 20 to December 22, 2013 |
| Location | Qimuzhang Nature Reserve, Heyuan |

Selected participants are required to attend the briefing meeting to learn about the activity format, team assignments, routes, safety precautions, and camping equipment distribution.
