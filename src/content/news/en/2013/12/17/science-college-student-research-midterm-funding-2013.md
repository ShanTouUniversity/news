Shantou University’s College of Science completed its 2013 midterm review of student extracurricular research projects and approved continued funding for 15 projects, totaling RMB 44,000. The college announced the results on December 17, 2013. An expert panel reviewed 16 previously approved projects through presentations and discussion, considering progress, project difficulty and funding needs before the college approved the additional support.

The college encouraged teams to continue their work and said strong results could receive further support and become candidates for preparation toward the national “Challenge Cup” competition. Individual project names and allocations were not itemized in the notice body.
