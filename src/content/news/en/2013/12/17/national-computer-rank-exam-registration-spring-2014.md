The spring 2014 National Computer Rank Examination was scheduled to begin in sessions at 9 a.m. on March 29, 2014. Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced campus registration arrangements on December 17, 2013: registration ran from December 18 to 25, excluding weekends, at the Student Affairs Service Window on the third floor of the New Administration Center. Candidates had to bring their original ID for an electronic photograph, check their registration details and sign to confirm them. The fee was RMB 137 for each of the four levels.

The examination adopted the 2013 syllabus and a revised certificate system; all tests were computer-based. Subjects and durations were as follows:

| Level | Subjects | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Computer Fundamentals and WPS Office, MS Office, or Photoshop applications | 90 minutes |
| 2 | C, VB, VFP, Java, Access, C++, MySQL, Web Programming, or Advanced MS Office | 120 minutes |
| 3 | Network Technology, Database Technology, Software Testing, Information Security, or Embedded Systems Development | 120 minutes |
| 4 | Network Engineer, Database Engineer, Software Testing Engineer, Information Security Engineer, or Embedded Systems Development Engineer | 90 minutes |

Inquiries could be directed to 86502402 or 86502411. Further information about textbooks and the syllabus was in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
