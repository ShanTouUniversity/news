Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department issued an assessment notice on December 17, 2013. The university planned to review mid- and senior-level management staff whose appointment probation periods had ended since January 2013, including a democratic evaluation process.

The staff members’ work reports were made available on the university’s internal office network at the time. Units or individuals wishing to comment could submit written materials to the Party Committee’s Organization and United Front Work Department by December 23. Unit submissions required an official seal, while individuals were required to sign their real names. Inquiries could be directed to 86502345 or o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. The reports and personal materials are not reproduced.
