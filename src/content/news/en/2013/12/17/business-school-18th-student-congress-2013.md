Shantou University’s Business School planned to hold its 18th student congress from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, December 18, 2013, in the Science Lecture Hall. The meeting was to review the work of the 17th Student Union and elect the chair of the 18th.

The congress was to include 216 formal delegates and 56 observers. Formal delegates were elected democratically at class meetings. Candidates and delegates were asked to check in by 2:15 p.m.
