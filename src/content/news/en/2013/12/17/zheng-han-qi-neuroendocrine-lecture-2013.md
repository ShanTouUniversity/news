The College of Science at Shantou University has invited Professor Zheng Han Qi, Dean of the Centre for Cancer Research at the Hong Kong Applied Science and Technology Research Institute and Vice Dean of the School of Biomedical Sciences at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, to deliver a lecture titled "The neuroendocrine control of the reproductive axis in teleost: insights from targeted gene knockout studies."

Professor Zheng's lecture will focus on the neuroendocrine regulatory mechanisms of the reproductive axis in teleost fish, drawing on the latest advances in targeted gene knockout research to explore core scientific questions and research outcomes in the field. The report holds significant reference value for faculty and students engaged in fish biology, neuroendocrinology, and reproductive biology research.

Faculty, staff, and students from the College of Science and all interested parties are welcome to attend.
