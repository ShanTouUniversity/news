Expert review comments for 2013 Guangdong Natural Science Fund proposals that were not selected for funding were available from December 17, 2013, through January 15, 2014. The university Research Office notified applicants on December 17 that they could sign in to the provincial fund management system using their application accounts and select “Feedback on Expert Opinions for Unfunded Projects” under “Application Management.”
