At the 11th FLTRP Cup National English Speaking Competition held in Beijing this December, Jin Xiaoyi, a 2010 English major at Shantou University, stood out among 94 contestants from 29 provinces, municipalities, autonomous regions, and the Macao and Taiwan regions of China, claiming the national championship. This marks the first time an STU contestant has won the top prize in this competition, achieving a historic breakthrough.

It is reported that Jin had already won the Guangdong provincial championship in November, representing both Guangdong Province and Shantou University at the national finals. In the competition, participants went through four intense rounds before Jin was crowned champion.

The FLTRP Cup National English Speaking Competition, organized by Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press, is one of the most influential English oral communication contests in China, attracting outstanding students from universities across the country each year.
