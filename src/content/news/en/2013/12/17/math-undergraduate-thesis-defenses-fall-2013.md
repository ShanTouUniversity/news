Shantou University’s College of Science planned fall thesis defenses for a portion of its 2014 mathematics graduates at 3 p.m. on Wednesday, December 25, 2013, in the Mathematics Department laboratory in Engineering North Room 412. Ye Ruisong was named committee chair, with Wei Caimin and Lin Xiaoping as members. The notice listed three student candidates; their names, student numbers and thesis titles are not reproduced to protect student privacy.
