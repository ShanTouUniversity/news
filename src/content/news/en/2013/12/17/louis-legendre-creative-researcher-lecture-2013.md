The College of Science at Shantou University has invited Professor Louis Legendre, a renowned expert from the Villefranche Oceanographic Station in France, to deliver a lecture titled "Becoming and remaining creative, successful researchers" on December 19 at 7:30 PM in ACC #3.

Professor Legendre is a world-renowned expert in marine science with extensive research experience at the Villefranche Oceanographic Station. His lecture will share profound insights and valuable experiences on how to become and sustain creativity and success in scientific research, offering significant inspiration for young scholars and graduate students.

Faculty, staff, and students from the College of Science and all interested parties are welcome to attend.
