Shantou University’s General Office issued a work contact directory for university leaders and heads of its main-campus units on December 30, 2013. The directory was intended to support work-related communication among faculty and staff; names and contact details were provided in an OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
> ⚠️ The directory of unit leaders and their contact details is available in the OA attachment.
