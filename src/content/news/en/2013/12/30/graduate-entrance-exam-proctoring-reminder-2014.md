Shantou University’s Graduate School issued an examination-administration reminder on December 30, 2013, for the 2014 master’s entrance examinations, scheduled for January 4–5, 2014. The examinations were set for 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 2:00–5:00 p.m. on both days.

Faculty responsible for subjects with papers set by the university were asked to remain reachable by phone during the examinations so they could respond to examination-related questions. Any change to their contact numbers was to be reported to the Graduate School. The notice listed the Graduate School’s office number as 86502424; detailed exam times for individual subjects were provided separately in an attachment.
