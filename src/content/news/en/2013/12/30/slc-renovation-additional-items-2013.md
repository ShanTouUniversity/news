On December 30, 2013, Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office disclosed estimated additional work for the new site of the Student Learning Center (SLC). The renovation began on December 25; floor outlets, standard outlets, and associated wiring were added to meet the user unit’s furniture layout.

## Additional Work Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Estimated additional cost | About RMB 3,000 |
| Added work | Floor and standard outlets and associated electrical wiring |
| Disclosure date | December 30, 2013 |
| Comment deadline | 5:30 p.m., January 2, 2014 |
| Receiving offices | Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office |

The Infrastructure Office said the estimated additional cost was below the university’s 10-percent limit.
