On December 30, 2013, Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office issued a second disclosure about additional work on the Longquan Garden Phase II staff-dormitory renovation. The project began on September 18. During construction, the original air-conditioning outlet locations were found not to meet safety requirements, while additional bathroom outlets, a spare tap, and kitchen drainage fixtures were added for practical use.

## Additional Work

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Estimated additional cost | About RMB 10,000 |
| Work | Outlet changes; added bathroom outlets, spare tap, drainpipe, and floor drain |
| Disclosure date | December 30, 2013 |
| Comment deadline | 5:30 p.m., January 2, 2014 |
| Receiving offices | Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office |

The Infrastructure Office said the cumulative additions remained below the university’s 10-percent limit. The notice followed an earlier disclosure concerning the same renovation project.
