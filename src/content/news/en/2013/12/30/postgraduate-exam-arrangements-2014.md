Shantou University’s Graduate School announced arrangements for the Shantou test site of the 2014 postgraduate entrance examination on December 30, 2013. The examination was scheduled for January 4–5, 2014, at the old campus of Shantou No. 1 Middle School. An earlier notice had arranged buses for registered STU candidates.

## Examination and Transport Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exam dates | January 4–5, 2014 |
| Daily sessions | 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 2–5 p.m. |
| Test site | Old campus of Shantou No. 1 Middle School, 64 Zhongshan Road, Shantou |
| Site check | Candidates were asked to visit by 5 p.m. on January 3 |
| Bus service | For STU candidates who had registered for transport |
| Departure | 7:15 a.m. on both exam days, from the STU motto monument |
| Bus registration deadline | 5:30 p.m., December 26, 2013 |

The bus information came from an earlier Graduate School notice. Registered passengers who needed to cancel could contact the Graduate School as directed in that notice. Detailed subject schedules and seat assignments are not reproduced.
