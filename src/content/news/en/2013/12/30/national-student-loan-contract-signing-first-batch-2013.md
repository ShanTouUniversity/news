Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced the contract-signing arrangements for the first round of 2013–2014 national student loans on December 30, 2013. Following a notice from the Shantou branch of the Bank of China, the signing session was scheduled for 2:30 p.m. on January 3, 2014, in E-101.

## Signing Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Applicants in the first student-loan round |
| Time | 2:30 p.m., January 3, 2014 |
| Venue | E-101 lecture room |
| Documents | ID card, student ID, Bank of China debit card, and a black pen |

Witnessing staff from each school or residential college were also scheduled to attend. Student names and personal loan records from the attachment are not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The applicant list and personal loan documents are available on the OA system.
