On December 30, 2013, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced plans for the fifth leadership–student dinner. The event was scheduled for 6 p.m. on January 9, 2014, in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant, bringing university leaders, department officials, faculty representatives, and students together for discussion.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled time | 6 p.m., January 9, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Student places | 32 |
| Registration deadline | 10 p.m., January 1, 2014 |
| Format | Discussion of campus development and issues of interest to students |

Full-time students could apply through the reservation system, which was to close when the places were filled. The university was to cover the meal costs, and participants were encouraged to bring forward concerns raised by fellow students. This report records the announced plan and does not confirm that the event took place.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
