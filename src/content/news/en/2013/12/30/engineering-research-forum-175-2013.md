Shantou University’s College of Engineering announced the program for its 175th Engineering Research Forum on December 30, 2013. Dr. Chen Zhenyuan of National Sun Yat-sen University in Taiwan was scheduled to speak the next morning in the College’s Academic Exchange Room. His topic examined how applied information-engineering methods could support academic development, knowledge management, and industry service.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Dr. Chen Zhenyuan, National Sun Yat-sen University, Taiwan |
| Topic | Applying information-engineering methods to academic development, knowledge management, and industry service |
| Time | 10 a.m.–noon, December 31, 2013 |
| Venue | College of Engineering Academic Exchange Room |
| Audience | Engineering faculty and students, and other interested members of the university |

The notice provided the planned program but no post-event account.
