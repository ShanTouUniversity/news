Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has announced the schedule for relocation households to sign resettlement-housing agreements and collect keys. Following the completion of public housing selection for households relocated from the west campus zone, the procedures will be handled in batches from December 31 through January 3.

## Schedule

| Date and time | Area or buildings |
| --- | --- |
| Morning, December 31 | Longquanyuan C5 and C6 |
| Afternoon, December 31 | Longquanyuan C1, C2, C3 and A |
| Morning, January 2 | West Quadrangle — Yulan Park |
| Afternoon, January 2 | West Quadrangle — Palm Park |
| Morning, January 3 | West Quadrangle — Fuxiang Park |
| Afternoon, January 3 | West Quadrangle — Huichun Park |

Agreement signing and key collection will take place at the Property Management Office, Room 313 on the west side of the former Administrative Building. Households should complete the procedures during their assigned time slot.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
