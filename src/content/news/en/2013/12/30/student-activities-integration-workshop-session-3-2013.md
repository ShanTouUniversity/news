On December 30, 2013, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced the schedule for the third workshop on systems thinking in student activities. The meeting was planned for the morning of December 31 in Room 330 of the Administrative Center, with case sharing and discussion about applying systems thinking to student activities.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | Third workshop on systems thinking in student activities |
| Time | 10:30–11:30 a.m., December 31, 2013 |
| Venue | Administrative Center, Room 330 |
| Focus | Case studies and discussion of systems thinking in student activities |
| Participants | Teachers responsible for related projects |

The notice asked project leads to arrange their work and attend on time. This report records the announced schedule and does not confirm that the meeting took place.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
