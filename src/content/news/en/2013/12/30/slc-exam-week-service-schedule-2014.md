Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) announced its New Year and exam-week service plan on December 30, 2013. Fixed academic consultations were suspended on January 1, 2014, while appointments remained available. Academic consultations and tutoring for the semester were scheduled to continue through January 4.

## Service Schedule

| Date | Service |
| --- | --- |
| January 1, 2014 | Fixed consultations paused; appointment service remained available |
| Through January 4, 2014 | Academic consultations and tutoring |
| January 5–16, 2014 | Seminar rooms, appointment-based consultations, room reservations, and self-study access |
