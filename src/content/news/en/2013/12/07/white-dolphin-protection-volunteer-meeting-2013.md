It is reported that the volunteer meeting for the Chinese White Dolphins Conservation Action was recently held at Shantou University. The event, hosted by the Guangdong Provincial Ocean and Fisheries Bureau and organized by the Institute of Marine Biology at Shantou University, attracted over 140 student applicants, with 75 ultimately selected to participate in the conservation effort.

Selected volunteers are required to attend the semester's volunteer meeting on Sunday, December 8, at 10:00 AM in Building D, Room 503, and bring the questionnaire distributed during the earlier registration process.

The Chinese white dolphin, a nationally Class I protected animal in China, is an important and rare species in the Pearl River Estuary. This conservation action aims to raise awareness of white dolphin and habitat protection through volunteer participation, promoting ecological civilization across campus and beyond.
