Shantou University’s Resource Management Office scheduled pesticide spraying from December 7 to 13, 2013, to address plant pests and improve campus grounds. Work covered green spaces, trees, fruit trees and drains on campus and at the Continuing Education College. Faculty, staff and students were advised to take care.

| Date | Areas |
| --- | --- |
| Dec. 7 | Medical Science Building, New Teaching Building and G, H, I and J student residences |
| Dec. 8 | Affiliated Middle and Primary Schools through the Mental Health Center |
| Dec. 9 | Areas around campus security offices, gates and roads, and the New Library exterior |
| Dec. 10 | Canteens, Administration Building and Administration Center |
| Dec. 11 | Academic Exchange Center, P Building, student residences, reservoir embankment, Building 789 and staff residences A–B |
| Dec. 12 | Artificial lake and athletics field |
| Dec. 13 | Continuing Education College |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
