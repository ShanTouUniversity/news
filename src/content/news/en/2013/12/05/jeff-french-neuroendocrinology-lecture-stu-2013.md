It is reported that Distinguished Professor Jeff Allen French from the University of Nebraska at Omaha will visit the School of Science at Shantou University to deliver an academic lecture on December 8.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Integrative Neuroendocrinology and Social Behavior in Nonhuman Primates |
| Speaker | Prof. Jeff Allen French (University of Nebraska at Omaha) |
| Time | Sunday, December 8, 2013, 20:00 |
| Venue | ACC3# Conference Room |

Professor French is the founder and director of the Marmoset Research Center and department chair of neuroscience at the University of Nebraska at Omaha. He has long conducted research on animal behavior and currently holds the title of Distinguished Professor in both biology and psychology at the university.

It is also learned that Professor French will hold discussions with the School of Science regarding potential cooperative programs between the two institutions.
