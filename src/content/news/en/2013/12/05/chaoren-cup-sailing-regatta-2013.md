It is reported that the 2013 "I Love Lighting · Chaoren Cup" International Sailing Regatta will be held in Shantou Inner Bay from December 21 to 23. Building on the success of previous editions, this year's event has been upgraded to an international regatta, further showcasing Shantou's image as a leading city in eastern Guangdong.

## Background

Shantou successfully hosted the "Chaoren Cup" sailing regatta in 2011 and 2012. The Shantou-based vessel "Chaoren Jia Yi" once represented the Chinese national sailing team at the 25th King's Cup International Sailing Regatta with excellent results. Approved by the Water Sports Management Center of the General Administration of Sport of China, the 2013 event adds "International" to its name, reflecting the central government's emphasis on and support for sailing in Shantou.

## Huanyu Ambassador Recruitment

Aligning with the Business School's educational philosophy of "excellence in business, reaching across the globe," the organizing committee is recruiting 10 "Huanyu Ambassadors" campus-wide through the Business School to promote cultural exchange between the international regatta and university life.
