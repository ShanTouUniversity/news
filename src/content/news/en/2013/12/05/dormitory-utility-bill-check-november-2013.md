Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced the review and payment schedule for November 2013 student dormitory utility bills on December 5. Meter readings for the billing period were taken on November 29–30. Students could use their campus network accounts to view individual bills in the university’s utility management system.

Students who found an error could complete and sign a review form and submit it to the Water and Electricity Center beside the Science and Technology Building during working hours from December 6–12. The center’s telephone number was `86502489`. Bills without objections were to be collected through campus cards starting December 13; the regular payment period ran from December 13–18, with overdue bills subject to university rules beginning December 19. Confirmed corrections requested after the review period could take three to five working days.

This report does not reproduce individual bills or dormitory information from the attachment.
