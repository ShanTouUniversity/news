It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University announced on December 5 the quarterfinal matchups for the staff badminton team competition. The matches have been rescheduled to start at 17:00 on December 6 at the gymnasium badminton courts.

## Quarterfinal Bracket

| Match | Court | Teams |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match 1 | Court 2 | Administration Office vs. School of Science |
| Match 2 | Court 3 | Law & Business United vs. Arts United |
| Match 3 | Court 5 | Library & Research United vs. Physical Education Dept. |
| Match 4 | Court 6 | Liberal Arts & News United vs. School of Engineering Team 2 |

## Match Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Round | Second stage — Quarterfinals |
| Time | Saturday, December 6, 2013, from 17:00 |
| Venue | Gymnasium Badminton Courts |
| Organizer | University Labor Union |

All competing teams are asked to arrive on time.
