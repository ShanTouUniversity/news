The first 2013 round of faculty professional-title reviews at Shantou University was completed by the first Guangdong higher-education senior teacher-title review committee and the relevant intermediate-title committees. STU’s Human Resources Office issued a public notice on December 5 saying that the people listed in the attachment had passed and been awarded the corresponding qualifications. The notice period ran through December 13.

Objections could be submitted to the Teacher Affairs Division of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, STU’s Human Resources Office or its Supervision and Audit Office. The STU contacts listed were Chen Ying at the Human Resources Office (`0754-86502340`) and Lin Ling at the Supervision and Audit Office (`0754-86502346`). The original attachment is the authoritative source for the names of those reviewed; this report does not reproduce the list.
