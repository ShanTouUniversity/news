Shantou University’s College of Journalism and Communication scheduled a media salon for December 6, 2013, on how advertising catchphrases reflect changes in Taiwan society. Wang Caiyun, president of Brain Magazine and general manager of Brain Communications, was invited to speak.

Wang had worked as an advertising copywriting lecturer and served as a judge for radio and television advertising awards and advertising-slogan competitions. The event was set for 7:30–9 p.m. in the New Library Lecture Hall and was open to the university community.
