Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office issued a reminder on December 26, 2013, concerning final examinations for the fall semester. Course schedules for the university’s main campus had been posted on the credit-system portal, where faculty and students were asked to check their relevant sessions.

Students were required to arrive at least 10 minutes before an exam and bring their student ID. Proctors were to verify candidates’ identities and seat assignments. The university’s existing proctoring rules and examination-room code of conduct governed exam discipline, including the handling of late arrivals and misconduct; proctors were also required to document incidents.

For exam administration, instructors were expected to prepare A and B versions with broadly comparable content and difficulty. Grading and score submission were generally due within three days after an exam, or within one week for courses with larger enrollments. Printing requests, exam papers and candidate lists were to be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office’s printing center one week in advance. Instructors responsible for setting exams were required to deliver papers for printing in person, and course units were to retain exam materials and inspection records.
