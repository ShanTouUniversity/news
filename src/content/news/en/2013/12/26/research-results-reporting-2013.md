Shantou University’s Research Office opened its annual science and social-science reporting process on December 26, 2013, in line with an annual-reporting request from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. The reporting period covered outputs and academic exchanges from January 1 through December 31, 2013.

## Reporting Deadlines

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting period | January 1–December 31, 2013 |
| Platform | STU research management system |
| Faculty data deadline | January 8, 2014 |
| Unit review deadline | January 15, 2014 |
| Contact | Chen Jinshan |
| Details | 86503271; chenjs@stu.edu.cn |

Faculty were to enter their outputs and upload electronic materials, or submit hard copies to their units for review. The university said future research-related reviews for annual evaluations, salary arrangements, and professional-title assessments would rely on the system’s data.
