It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University issued a notice on December 26 regarding the renewal of urban-rural basic medical insurance for students currently enrolled in the Shantou insurance program. The current coverage period will expire at the end of December 2013.

Per the Jinhua Social Security Branch of Shantou, enrolled students must pay the premium for the next insurance year by December 31, 2013. The new coverage period runs from January 1 to December 31, 2014, at a rate of RMB 30 per student per year under the Shantou urban-rural basic medical insurance Tier 1.

To facilitate the process, the Finance Office will automatically deduct the premium from each student's Bank of China account. Students are reminded to ensure sufficient funds are deposited in their accounts before the end of 2013.
