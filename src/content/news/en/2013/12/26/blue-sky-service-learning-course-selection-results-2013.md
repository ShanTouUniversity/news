It is reported that the Law School at Shantou University announced the interview results for the "Blue Sky" Creative Service-Learning Public Course for the Spring 2014 semester on December 26. Following nearly five hours of intensive interviews involving multiple group discussion sessions, 80 students were ultimately selected to enroll in the course.

Selected students will be automatically enrolled into the credit system by the course management team and do not need to register for the course themselves. Students with questions may email 1515610750@qq.com before January 2.
