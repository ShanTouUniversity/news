It is reported that the Law School at Shantou University announced the interview results for the "Blue Sky" Creative Service-Learning Public Course for the Spring 2014 semester on December 26. An interview notice issued on December 23 said that more than 170 students had reserved places. The course team subsequently scheduled interviews from 1 to 6 p.m. on December 25, with candidates waiting in E203 and sessions held in E202-1 and E202-2. Each group discussion involved six students and lasted about 20 minutes. After nearly five hours of interviews, 80 students were selected.

Selected students would be entered into the credit system by the course management team and did not need to register themselves. Questions could be directed to the team by January 2; a student’s personal email address is not reproduced.
