On December 26, 2013, Shantou University’s Sports Department announced a temporary classroom change for Class 63812 of *Fitness Theory and Jogging Development Plan*, taught by Qu Xiaofeng. The afternoon class, scheduled for periods 8–9, was moved to Room 407 in Building E.
