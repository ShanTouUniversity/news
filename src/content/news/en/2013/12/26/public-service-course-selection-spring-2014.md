It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on December 26, 2013, announcing course registration for 15 public service courses in the spring semester of 2014.

## Overview

Public service courses have been compulsory general education courses since the 2012 cohort, carrying one credit as part of the common core. Each course comprises 48 hours, including service learning, training, public service practice, sharing and evaluation, taught by trained teachers. Details are available at http://gykc.stu.edu.cn. Courses are mainly held on Wednesday afternoons or weekends.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Initial selection | 9:00 Dec 27 – 17:00 Dec 29, open only to students who have not yet taken a public service course |
| Add/drop | 9:00 Jan 2 – 17:00 Jan 3, 2014, open to all undergraduates |
| How to register | Log in to the credit system — Guided Courses — Select Topic, choose spring 2013–2014 and select a topic from courses coded SER (do not create topics) |
| Principle | First-come, first-served until full; one course per student |
| Courses | 15 courses with capacities from 0 to 60; some have been selected separately |

Examples include “Guardianship — Caring for Autistic Children” (Business School), “Public Service Photography” (Journalism School) and “Volunteer Teaching Support” (School of Engineering). Students who fail to attend after registration will receive zero. For questions, contact Ms./Mr. Liu at 86502416 (882416).
