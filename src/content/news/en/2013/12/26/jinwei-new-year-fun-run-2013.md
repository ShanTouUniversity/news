Shantou University’s Sports Department reported on December 26, 2013, that 260 students had registered for Shantou’s “Jinwei” New Year fun run. The event was scheduled for December 28, with participants due to gather at the university’s motto monument before traveling to the start.

## Event Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registrants | 260 students |
| Gathering time | 6 a.m., December 28, 2013 |
| Meeting point | STU motto monument |
| Start | Shantou People’s Square |
| Finish | Overseas Chinese Park |
| Return | Participants were scheduled to return by their original buses and collect souvenirs at the gymnasium |

The notice arranged transport on Route 39 buses, with schools and residential colleges assigned to different vehicles. The participant list and personal contact details are not reproduced.
