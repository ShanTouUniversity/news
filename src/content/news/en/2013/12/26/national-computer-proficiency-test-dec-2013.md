It is reported that, per the provincial examination management center, the 2nd National College Computer Proficiency Test of 2013 will take place on December 28 at the E101 computer lab. The exam will be administered entirely via computer, with questions delivered online.

Two sessions are scheduled:

| Session | Start Time | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session 001 | December 28, 8:00 AM | 105 minutes |
| Session 002 | December 28, 10:15 AM | 105 minutes |

The Academic Affairs Office reminds candidates that students arriving more than 15 minutes after the start time will not be permitted to enter. Submit the exam approximately 2 minutes before time expires; after completing operation tasks, save files and close or exit the relevant software. Candidates must bring their admission ticket, ID card, and student ID; no other materials or items, including draft paper, are allowed in the exam room.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
