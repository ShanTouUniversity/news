It is reported that the Admissions Office will hold the second training session for the "Drink Water, Remember Source, Pass on Dreams" recruitment campaign on the morning of December 28, aimed at ensuring consistent and accurate promotional messaging and sharing experiences from previous years' outstanding volunteer recruiters.

Since the first training session, 621 students from various colleges and residential colleges have registered to participate. The details of this session are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date & Time | December 28, 2013, 10:00-11:30 AM |
| Venue | E Building Lecture Hall 101 |
| Key Content | Recruitment campaign arrangements and guidelines; Q&A session |

Students who did not attend the first training session are encouraged to attend this session on time.
