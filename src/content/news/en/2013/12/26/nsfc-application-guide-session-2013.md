It is reported that the Research Office will hold a guidance session for the 2014 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) project applications on December 30 at 10:00 AM in Conference Room ACC3.

**Agenda**

- External NSFC peer-review experts will share experiences on application preparation and evaluation
- Directors from relevant divisions of the NSFC will hold interactive discussions with attendees

**Target Audience**

All faculty members planning to apply for NSFC projects in 2014.

Faculty members interested in applying are encouraged to attend and prepare accordingly.
