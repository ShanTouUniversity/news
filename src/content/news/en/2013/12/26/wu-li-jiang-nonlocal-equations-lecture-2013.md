**Time**: December 27 (Friday), 10:30 AM

**Venue**: Mathematics Laboratory

**About the Speaker**: Dr. Wu Lijiang is a scholar at Carnegie Mellon University, with research focus on nonlocal interaction equations and related mathematical fields.
