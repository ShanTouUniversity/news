On December 26, 2013, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office released the review information for the first round of 2013–2014 national student-loan applications. The Student Aid Center reviewed 40 applications; applications from students admitted in 2013 were to be handled in a separate second round.

## Applications and Follow-up

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Academic year | 2013–2014 |
| Round | First |
| Number of applicants | 40 |
| Next step | Loan contracts were to be signed if no objections were raised during publicity |

The notice scheduled contract signing for January 3, 2014, for applications without objections. Student names and personal financial-aid details in the attachment are not reproduced. The source contains an inconsistent year for the publicity deadline, so that date is omitted here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The applicant list and loan documents are available on the OA system.
