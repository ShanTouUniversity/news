Shantou University’s Arts Education Center announced the results of auditions for the Piano Art and advanced Piano Art electives for the spring semester of the 2013–2014 academic year. A total of 73 students passed. The original notice listed the successful applicants; their names and student IDs are omitted here to protect their privacy.

The successful applicants were assigned to four class sections with 17, 21, 17 and 18 students, respectively. The courses are taught by Dong Xuemin.
