It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office released the final exam schedule for graduate students enrolled in Education and Psychology courses on December 26, urging candidates to prepare accordingly.

Both exams will be conducted in closed-book format. The schedule is as follows:

| Course | Exam Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education | January 14, 2014 (Week 18, Tuesday) 13:30-15:30 | E301 |
| Psychology | January 7, 2014 (Week 17, Tuesday) 19:00-21:00 | E305 |

The office reminds students to follow the seating arrangements assigned by invigilators and to bring their student ID cards; those without valid identification will not be admitted to the examination hall.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
