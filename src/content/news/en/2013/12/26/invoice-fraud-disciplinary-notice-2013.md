It is reported that the Office of Discipline Inspection and Audit at Shantou University issued a disciplinary notice on December 26 regarding an invoice fraud case. During a routine audit of reimbursement documents, the Finance Office discovered abnormally large photocopying and printing invoices submitted by a faculty member of the Law School. Joint verification by the Finance Office and the Office of Discipline Inspection and Audit confirmed that the reimbursed invoice amount exceeded the actual cost of the printing services, constituting a violation of the PRC Invoice Management Measures.

Given that the faculty member voluntarily acknowledged the wrongdoing and the inflated invoices had not yet been fully processed for reimbursement, the university decided to issue a formal reprimand.

The two offices jointly urge all faculty and staff to take this case as a warning, strictly observe financial regulations, and avoid similar violations.
