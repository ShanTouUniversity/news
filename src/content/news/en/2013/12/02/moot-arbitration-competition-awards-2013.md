It is reported that the 11th "CIETAC Cup" International Commercial Arbitration Moot was held in Beijing from November 18 to 23, with the Shantou University Law School team achieving outstanding results. Zhang Jiemin from the 2011 law cohort was awarded "Best Orator," while Han Zhi from the same cohort received the "Outstanding Team Contribution" award.

This year's competition featured the largest scale in the tournament's history, with teams from law schools of 29 renowned universities across China. The STU team, composed primarily of second- and third-year students, was one of the youngest delegations among all participants. Through multiple rounds of intense debate, STU competitors distinguished themselves with solid theoretical knowledge of arbitration and impressive courtroom performance.
