Assoc. Prof. Li Wanli, Deputy Director of the Academic Affairs Office and a supervisor for master's students at Shantou University, will deliver a lecture titled "Civil Law in Daily Life" on Wednesday, December 4, from 4:25 PM to 5:25 PM in Lecture Hall E-203.

The lecture is open to faculty, students, and parents, aiming to introduce foundational concepts of civil law and help attendees understand the close connection between civil law and everyday life, thereby enhancing legal awareness.

All are welcome to attend.
