It is reported that Professor Zhang Fan from the National School of Development at Peking University will deliver a lecture titled "The Economics Behind the News" on December 4 from 19:00 to 21:00 in the Library Lecture Hall.

Professor Zhang holds a Ph.D. in economics from Wayne State University, Michigan, and currently serves as a professor at Peking University's National School of Development. He previously worked as a senior economist for the City of New York. The lecture is hosted by the College of Communication and Journalism and is open to all faculty and students interested in the economics of news.
