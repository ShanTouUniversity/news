Following an expert review organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, seven Shantou University faculty members—Liu Yang, Zhang Yanyan, Chen Yindong, Yu Chengjie, Zheng Muqiang, Yu Ying and Fan Lisheng—were proposed as 2013 participants in the Guangdong Higher Education Outstanding Young Teachers Development Program. STU’s Human Resources Office issued the public notice on December 2, 2013, with a notice period through December 8.

Objections could be submitted during the notice period to the provincial department’s Teacher Affairs Division or STU’s Human Resources Office. The STU contact was Chen Ying, telephone `0754-86502340`, email `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`. Submissions were required to include specific facts and the reporter’s real name.

This notice concerned proposed program participants following expert review and was distinct from the university’s earlier notice of applicants.
