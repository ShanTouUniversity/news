It is reported that the IBCC Case Analysis Club of the STU Business School will hold an E-Commerce Case Competition from December 11 to 28, open to all students on campus.

The competition aims to enrich campus academic life, strengthen students' case analysis skills, and deepen their understanding of business models. Participants are encouraged to explore how traditional industries in Shantou and Guangdong can leverage e-commerce or internet marketing to develop brand transformation and upgrading strategies for both products and enterprises.
