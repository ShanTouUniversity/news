The Center for English Language Learning (CILL) at Shantou University will host a series of IELTS preparation workshops, with the inaugural session focused on listening skills. The workshop is scheduled for Wednesday, December 4, from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM at CILL.

The workshop series aims to help students preparing for the IELTS exam systematically improve their language abilities. The first session on listening will cover question types and problem-solving strategies specific to the IELTS listening test.

Students with interest are welcome to attend.
