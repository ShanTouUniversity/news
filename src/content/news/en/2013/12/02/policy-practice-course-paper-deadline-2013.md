Shantou University’s Department of Social Sciences reminded students in the 2014 undergraduate graduating class who took the “Situation and Policy Education” practice course in fall 2013 to complete a paper on their assigned topic. Papers were due by 5 p.m. on January 6, 2014.

Papers were to be 3,000–5,000 Chinese characters, handwritten or printed on A4 paper using the standard template. Students were asked to submit a hard copy to their assigned instructor’s mailbox at room 204-1, second floor of the west wing of the G Teaching Building. Topics and instructors could be checked in the credit-system portal under “Guided Courses.” Early graduates were also required to submit.

> ⚠️ The paper template and assigned topics are available through the OA and credit-system portals.
