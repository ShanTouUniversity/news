It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has released the shortlist for the 2013-2014 donor-funded (enterprise and individual) scholarships. A total of 178 students have advanced to the next round of review: 171 for the Outstanding Senior Student Award Program and 7 for the Hakka Student Award Program.

Applications opened on November 21, when senior students were invited to apply online for the two programs. By the deadline of 5:00 pm on November 28, 551 students had submitted applications — 512 for the Outstanding Senior Student Award Program (113 awards available) and 39 for the Hakka Student Award Program (2 awards available).

## Selection Criteria

Under the Interim Measures for the Administration of Donor-Funded Scholarships, both programs rank candidates by their cumulative GPA percentage:

| Program | Applicants | Total Awards | Shortlisted | Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outstanding Senior Student Award | 512 | 113 | 171 | Ranked by GPA percentage within each college's quota at a 1:1.5 ratio |
| Hakka Student Award | 39 | 2 | 7 | Top GPA percentage candidate from each college |

## Required Documents

Shortlisted students have been asked to submit four paper documents to the Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center) by 5:00 pm on Monday, December 9: the scholarship application form (printed from the Student Affairs System), the shortlist recommendation form, an essay of up to 1,200 words written by the student on their learning experiences and how these shaped their goals and aspirations, and an official transcript stamped by their college.

The university also clarified that national scholarships, national encouragement scholarships, university merit scholarships and enterprise scholarships cannot be held concurrently; students receiving the national grant who also win a donor-funded scholarship may only take the monetary award of whichever is higher.
