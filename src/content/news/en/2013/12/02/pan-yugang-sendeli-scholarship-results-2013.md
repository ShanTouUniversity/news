The College of Science at Shantou University has announced the results of the 2013 Pan Yugang Scholarship and Sendeli Chemical Scholarship selections. This year, a total of 90 applications were received — 51 for the Pan Yugang Scholarship and 39 for the Sendeli Chemical Scholarship.

Following preliminary review and final defense presentations by the selection committee, eight candidates were selected as awardees: Chen Shumin, Lin Bing, Yang Yan, and Lu Zhuomin for the Pan Yugang Scholarship; and Chen Yi, Cai Zhiping, Li Jiong, and Chen Jin for the Sendeli Chemical Scholarship.

Both scholarships are funded by corporate and individual donors, aiming to recognize students who excel academically and demonstrate strong comprehensive abilities, and to inspire the broader student body to strive for excellence.
