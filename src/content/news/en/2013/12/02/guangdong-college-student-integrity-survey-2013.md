In 2013, the Guangdong Education Department commissioned Guangdong Shangxia Wuqian Nian Credit Technology Co. to conduct a special survey on college students’ integrity across the province. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office circulated the arrangement on December 2, asking colleges and residential colleges to organize students to complete an online questionnaire on the company’s website by December 15. The university’s sample was expected to cover more than 30% of its student population.

The broader survey also included in-person questionnaires, case follow-up and student representative discussions. The notice body did not provide detailed online instructions; those were included in the OA attachment.
