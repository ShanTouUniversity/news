It is reported that Researcher Ren Liyong from the Xi'an Institute of Optics and Precision Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, will deliver academic lectures on December 5 at 16:10 in the College of Science Lecture Hall (Room Li-352). The two lecture topics are "Light Slowing (Slow Light) Technology and Applications" and "Polarization Optical Imaging Technology and Applications."

Researcher Ren has been engaged in optical research for many years. The lectures will cover the latest developments in slow-light technology and its practical applications, as well as polarization optical imaging technology. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
