It is reported that the College of Art and Design and the Art Education Center at STU will co-host the closing piano recital of the 2nd Performing Arts Rising Stars series. Young pianist Zhen Xiyuan will perform classical works by Mozart, Liszt, Berg, and Chopin, and share personal insights from his musical journey.

Born in Shantou, Guangdong in 1993, Zhen began piano at age 8. His accomplishments include winning the gold medal in the South China region of the 4th Steinway Youth Piano Competition in 2009, placing fourth in the professional youth category at the 2011 Zhujiang-Kaiserman National Piano Competition, and entering the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts on a full scholarship in 2012. Specific date and venue details will be announced separately.
