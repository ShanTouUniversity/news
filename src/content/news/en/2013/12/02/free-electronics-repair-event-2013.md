It is reported that the STU Tech Innovation Club will hold a free electronics repair event on December 7 (Saturday) from 9:30 to 17:00 at the Golden Triangle plaza, offering complimentary repair services for small household appliances to faculty, staff, and students.

The event covers a wide range of items including desk lamps, fans, speakers, power strips, electric kettles, rice cookers, hair dryers, radios, and induction cookers. Organized by club volunteers, the initiative aims to apply professional skills to serve the campus community and foster a culture of mutual assistance at STU.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, University Youth League)
