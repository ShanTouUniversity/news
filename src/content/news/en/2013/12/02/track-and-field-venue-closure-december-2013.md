Shantou University’s athletics and football fields were scheduled to close from 3 to 6 p.m. on December 4, 2013, for an athletics-meet rehearsal, and all day from December 6 to 8 for the competition.

The notice was jointly issued by the Physical Education Department and Resource Management Office to alert the campus community to the venue schedule.
