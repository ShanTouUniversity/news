Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office issued Bulletin No. 247 on December 2, 2013, summarizing checks at the E Teaching Building during Week 11 of the fall semester. The bulletin recorded 15 instances of students arriving late; the student names in its attachment are not reproduced.

| Check date and time | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
|------|------:|------:|
| November 27 (Wednesday), 8 a.m. | 2 instances | 4 instances |
| November 28 (Thursday), 8 a.m. | 9 instances | Not specified |

The findings were subsequently shared with the university’s schools and residential colleges for study-style education. This report presents aggregate figures only and does not publish individual names.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Three-Style Development Office)
