Shantou University’s Finance Office announced its year-end settlement schedule in December 2013. The Accounting Section suspended public reimbursement services from December 30 through January 10, 2014. Units and staff were asked to complete accounting matters according to the payment category.

| Item | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Settle receipts and invoices; return unused receipts | By Dec. 27 |
| Repay advances and complete staff reimbursements | By Dec. 27 |
| Authorized treasury payments | File by Dec. 20 and withdraw funds from the bank by Dec. 30 |
| Direct treasury payments and remittances | File by Dec. 24 |

Treasury-payment processing was to pause from December 24 until the provincial treasury approved the 2014 budget. The Finance Office listed 86502446, 86502100 and 86502467 for urgent inquiries.
