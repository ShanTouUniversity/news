The Guangdong arts-sector senior professional-title review committee approved Liu Guanghui of Shantou University’s Arts Education Center for promotion to first-class performer. STU’s Human Resources Office issued the public notice on December 9, 2013, with a notice period through December 17.

Objections could be submitted to the personnel office of the Guangdong Department of Culture, the professional personnel division of the Guangdong Department of Human Resources and Social Security, or STU’s Human Resources Office. The STU office telephone number was `0754-86502340`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
