It is reported that the December 2013 National College English Test (CET) will be held at Shantou University on Saturday, December 14. The CET-4 will take place from 9:00 AM to 11:25 AM, and the CET-6 from 3:00 PM to 5:25 PM.

The Academic Affairs Office reminds all students to familiarize themselves with the exam procedures and requirements in advance and to arrive on time for the test.

## Exam Schedule

| Exam | Time |
| --- | --- |
| CET-4 | Saturday, December 14, 9:00–11:25 AM |
| CET-6 | Saturday, December 14, 3:00–5:25 PM |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
