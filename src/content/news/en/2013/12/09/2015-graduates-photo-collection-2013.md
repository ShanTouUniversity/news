It is reported that the Graduate School at Shantou University has announced that image collection for the 2015 graduating graduate students' degree certificates will take place on Monday, December 16, 2013, from 3:30 PM to 5:30 PM at the Moot Court in Teaching Building E.

The collection applies to all graduate students (master's and doctoral) at the main campus who are expected to graduate in 2015. The collected images serve as a critical basis for producing degree certificates, and students are urged to attend on time to avoid any delays in certificate issuance.

| --- | --- |
| Time | Monday, December 16, 2013, 15:30–17:30 |
