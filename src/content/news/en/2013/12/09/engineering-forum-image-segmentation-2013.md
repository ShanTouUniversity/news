The 170th Shantou University Engineering Research Forum was scheduled for December 10, 2013, on “Saliency Detection and Image Segmentation.” Dr. Zou Wenbin was the speaker.

The forum was set for 10 a.m. in the College of Engineering’s Academic Exchange Room, room 314 of the south Engineering Building. Faculty members and students from the Computer Science Department, as well as other interested attendees, were welcome.
