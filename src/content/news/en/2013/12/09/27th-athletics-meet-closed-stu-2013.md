It is reported that the 27th Shantou University Annual Track and Field Meet concluded successfully on December 8 at the university sports ground. The multi-day competition attracted athletes from all schools and residential colleges across campus.

## Competition Highlights

The 27th annual track and field meet featured a range of traditional events including sprints, distance runs, long jump, high jump, shot put, and relay races. Student-athletes from various colleges and residential colleges showcased outstanding competitive spirit and teamwork, delivering an exciting sports spectacle for the university community.

## Results Publication

Following the conclusion of the meet, the organizing committee has compiled the official results booklet, which is available for reference by team leaders, coaches, and athletes from all participating units.
