It is reported that the semifinals and additional matches of the Shantou University faculty and staff badminton team competition will take place at 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday, December 10, at the gymnasium badminton courts.

## Match Schedule

| Round | Matchup | Court |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Semifinal | Administration Team vs. Arts, Social Sciences, Continuing Education & Affiliated Units Joint Team | Court 5 |
| Semifinal | Science Library & Research Joint Team vs. College of Engineering Team 2 | Court 6 |
| Additional Match | College of Science vs. Law & Business Joint Team | Court 2 |
| Additional Match | Department of Physical Education vs. Liberal Arts & Journalism Joint Team | Court 3 |

The competition is organized by the University Union. All faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend.
