It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University has officially announced the launch of the 2013 Golden Phoenix Award selection. The award aims to recognize faculty and staff members who have made outstanding and sustained contributions to student work.

## Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the following requirements:
- Have been continuously working in the student work system for at least 3 years
- Demonstrated outstanding performance in their duties
- Have not previously received the Golden Phoenix Award or an equivalent honor

The selection covers staff from the Student Affairs Office (including the University Youth League Committee and the People's Armed Forces Department), as well as student workers from all colleges and residential colleges.

Named after the Golden Phoenix flower—the floral emblem of Shantou University—the award symbolizes the dedication and brilliance of those who work diligently in student affairs, serving as an important recognition of their contributions to the university community.
