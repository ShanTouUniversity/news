Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced invigilation arrangements for the fall 2013 national College English Test on December 9. The CET-4 and CET-6 exams were scheduled for Saturday, December 14: CET-4 from 9 to 11:25 a.m. and CET-6 from 3 to 5:25 p.m.

Invigilators were asked to study provincial exam rules, candidate and invigilator guidelines, operating procedures and incident-response instructions in advance. CET-4 invigilators were to report to Lecture Hall E101 at 7:45 a.m. for briefing and exam-paper collection; CET-6 invigilators were to report to the same location at 1:45 p.m. Individual assignments and rooms were to be announced on exam day, and invigilators would undergo security screening before entering exam rooms.
