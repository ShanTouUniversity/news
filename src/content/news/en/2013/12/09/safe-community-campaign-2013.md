It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee will hold the Safe Community safety publicity series to enhance safety awareness and protect lives and property.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Prevent Theft and Fraud for a Happier Community |
| Participants | All students on campus |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |
| Co-organizer | Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee |
| Program | Safety publicity: December 12, 11:30–12:30, with bookmark handouts and Q&A to share anti-theft and anti-fraud tips; Video screening: December 14, 19:30–22:00, screening short videos on common theft and fraud tactics |
| Venue | Jin San Jiao and Activity Room on the first floor of Building A |
| Inquiries | Sina Weibo @STU Dormitory Self-Management Committee |

All students are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
