It is reported that the results of the 2013 National Graduate Mathematical Modeling Contest have been announced, with twelve graduate student teams from Shantou University achieving their best-ever results — three national second prizes, one third prize, and eight participation awards.

## Competition Overview

Hosted by the Development Center for Graduate Education of the Ministry of Education and undertaken by Central South University, the 2013 contest attracted 3,884 teams from 332 universities across 30 provinces, municipalities, and regions, as well as research institutes nationwide, including 573 doctoral students. STU sent 36 graduate students representing 12 teams.

## Historic Breakthrough

Since 2009, STU has been participating in the National Graduate Mathematical Modeling Contest, consistently achieving excellent results in recent years. The winning students expressed their intention to continue working hard and encouraging more junior graduate students to participate in academic competitions, aiming to achieve even better results next year and bring more honors to the university.
