It is reported that Professor Yin Cunyi of the School of Public Policy and Management at Tsinghua University and Deputy Director of the Taiwan Studies Institute will deliver an academic lecture entitled "Central-Local Relations and Regional Development" on Monday, December 16, 2013, from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM at the Library Lecture Hall, Shantou University.

Professor Yin currently serves as a professor at Tsinghua University's School of Public Policy and Management and Deputy Director of its Taiwan Studies Institute. He also holds council positions at the Chinese Regional Studies Association, the China Council for the Promotion of National Unity, and the 7th China Peaceful Reunification Promotion Committee. He received his B.A. from Wuhan University, his M.A. from the Central University for Nationalities, and his Ph.D. from Nankai University. In 1998 he was a visiting scholar at the University of Toronto, and in 2008 at Leiden University in the Netherlands.

## Speaker Profile

Professor Yin has long focused his research on Chinese regional development and cross-strait relations. He has conducted visiting research at prominent institutions including the University of Toronto and Leiden University, and holds council positions in several academic and policy organizations related to regional studies and national reunification.
