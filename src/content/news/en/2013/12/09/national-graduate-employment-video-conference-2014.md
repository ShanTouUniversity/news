Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office arranged a viewing of the national 2014 college graduate employment video conference on December 9, 2013. The meeting was scheduled for 3 p.m. the following day, December 10, in Room 330 of the New Administration Building.

Attendees were to include Party general-branch secretaries and deputy secretaries from the university’s schools and residential colleges, employment counselors, and staff from the Student Affairs Office’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center.
