Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced an initial schedule for viewing the national 2014 college graduate employment video conference on December 9, 2013, then issued a revised arrangement the following day. The updated session was set for 10 a.m. on December 11 in Room 213 of the New Administration Building; it had originally been scheduled for 3 p.m. on December 10 in Room 330.

Attendees were to include Party general-branch secretaries and deputy secretaries from the university’s schools and residential colleges, employment counselors, and staff from the Student Affairs Office’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center.
