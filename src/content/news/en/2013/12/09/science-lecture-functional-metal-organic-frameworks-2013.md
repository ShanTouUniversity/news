Shantou University’s College of Science invited Northeast Normal University Professor Su Zhongmin to speak on December 9, 2013, as part of its Science Forum series. His topic was the synthesis and applications of functional metal-organic framework materials.

The lecture was scheduled for 3:30 p.m. in the auditorium of the Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center and was open to the university community.
