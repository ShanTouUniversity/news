Shantou University’s Law School Career Development Center opened registration in December 2013 for its 2014 National Judicial Examination preparation program. The six stages covered past-paper analysis, introductory civil and criminal law, systematic review, score improvement, full-length mock exams and final preparation. Training was expected to continue until before the examination in September 2014; class times were to be announced separately.

The program was open to Law School undergraduates in the Class of 2011 and graduate students. Applicants were asked to register with their class monitor by December 16 and submit a one-inch photo for a training-pass card.
