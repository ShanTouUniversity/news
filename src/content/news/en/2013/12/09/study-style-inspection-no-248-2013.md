Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office issued Bulletin No. 248 on December 9, 2013, summarizing classroom checks during Week 12 of the fall semester. Checks on December 2 and 4 recorded 14 instances of students arriving late; names in the original attachment are not reproduced.

| Check time and location | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
|------|------:|------:|
| December 2 (Monday), 7:10 p.m., E204 | 2 instances | Not specified |
| December 4 (Wednesday), 8 a.m., E Teaching Building | 8 instances | 2 instances |
| December 4 (Wednesday), 10:10 a.m., E Teaching Building | Not specified | 2 instances |

The office said the findings had been shared with the university’s schools and residential colleges for study-style education. This report presents aggregate figures only and does not publish individual names.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Three-Style Development Office)
