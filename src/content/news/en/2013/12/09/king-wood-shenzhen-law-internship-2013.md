King & Wood’s Shenzhen office opened internship applications to Shantou University Law School in December 2013, planning to take several law students from the Class of 2010. Applicants were asked to complete an application form and email it to the Law School Career Development Center at `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` by December 15.

The center said it would arrange interviews depending on the number of applications; the time and location had not yet been set. The firm’s website was listed as <http://www.kingandwood.com/>.

On December 18, the Law School announced that eight students had advanced through the preliminary review. Interviews were scheduled for 10:30 a.m. on December 23 in Room 206 of the Law School. The students’ names are not published to protect their privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School)
> ⚠️ The internship application form is available as an attachment in the original OA notice.
