Shantou University’s College of Science scheduled an alumni gathering for December 15, 2013, bringing graduates from across the country back to campus for an awards ceremony and交流. The event aimed to strengthen ties between alumni, the college and one another.

Alumni-established Physics and Chemistry funds, along with other scholarships and grants, support full-time undergraduates with strong academic records and financial need. The gathering was set for 9:15–10:30 a.m. in the New Library Lecture Hall and was open to faculty members and students.
