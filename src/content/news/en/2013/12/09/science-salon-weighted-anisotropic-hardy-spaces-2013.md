Shantou University’s College of Science scheduled a seminar with Professor Zhao Kai of Qingdao University’s School of Mathematical Sciences for December 17, 2013. Titled “The Weighted Anisotropic Hardy Spaces,” the talk covered anisotropic Hardy spaces, weighted anisotropic Hardy spaces and Calderón–Zygmund operators on those spaces.

The seminar was set for 4–5 p.m. in the Mathematics Laboratory, room 412 of the north Engineering Building. Zhao’s research focuses on harmonic analysis, and he had published more than 50 related papers.
