Shantou University announced its on-campus pre-registration arrangements for winter-break train tickets on December 9, 2013. Undergraduates and graduate students, including those at the Medical College, could log in with their campus network accounts and enter return-route details through the university booking center from 8 a.m. on December 10 through 5:30 p.m. on December 17. Entries were to be randomized to set the queue order for students unable to book online; results were expected on the internal university information network.

After coordinating with Shantou Railway Station, the university planned temporary ticket-sales and free ticket-collection windows for December 22–23, with the exact time and location to be announced separately. Online booking from December 20 was expected to cover student return tickets through February 24, 2014. Registrants were asked to enter their route as instructed and prepare a valid student ID, national ID and student discount card; travel dates were generally to follow the university winter-break schedule.

Questions could be directed to the counselor office in Dormitory D, Room 103, or to `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn`. No individual student booking information is included.
