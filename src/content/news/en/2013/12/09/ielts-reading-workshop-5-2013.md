Shantou University’s Center for International Language Learning (CILL) announced the fifth workshop in its IELTS preparation series on December 9, 2013. The session was scheduled for Wednesday, December 11, from 4 to 6 p.m. at CILL and focused on IELTS reading skills.

Seats were limited. Students could reserve a place through the Eventbrite page supplied by the organizers at the time: <http://www.eventbrite.com/e/ielts-workshop-51-reading-skills-tickets-9193729693>. The notice asked registrants to attend if they booked a place.
