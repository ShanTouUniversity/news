It is reported that the seventh lecture in Shantou University’s General Education Forum series will be held on December 10, 2013. Professor Yang Xusheng will deliver a lecture on “World Ethics and Its Chinese Dimension.” The event is jointly organized by the Institute of Higher Education and the Christian Studies Center of the School of Liberal Arts.

## The World Ethics Theme

According to the lecture brief, the concept of “world ethics” (Weltethos / Global Ethic) originated with German thinker Hans Küng’s 1990 book *A Global Ethic*. The Parliament of the World’s Religions adopted the *Declaration toward a Global Ethic* in 1993, which became a foundational statement for the global ethics movement. The lecture will use this intellectual background to discuss the relationship between world ethics and its Chinese dimension.

## Academic Background of the Speaker

The notice describes Professor Yang Xusheng as having served as the Chinese coordinator of the religious group of the China-Europa-Forum and as a project director at the Weltethos Foundation (Stiftung Weltethos). It also states that he has served as a council member of the European-Chinese Association, the Chinese Religious Studies Association and the Chinese Association for the History of Sino-Foreign Relations. His previous teaching or research affiliations included the Institute of Philosophy at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, the Department of Sinology at the East Asian Institute of the University of Tübingen, an intercultural religion and history of religions research project at the University of Bonn, the Department of Sinology at the Institute of Non-European Cultures at the University of Erlangen, and Renmin University of China.

His listed books and translations include *Immanente Transzendenz: Eine Untersuchung der Transzendenzerfahrung in der antiken chinesischen Religiosität* (*Immanent Transcendence: An Inquiry into Experiences of Transcendence in Ancient Chinese Religiosity*), *Escape to the World: A Spiritual History of Daoist and Chan Thought*, *Tracing the World’s Religions* and *Handbook of World Ethics*.
