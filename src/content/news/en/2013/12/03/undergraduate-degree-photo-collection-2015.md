Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced on December 3, 2013, that image collection for degree certificates for campus-based undergraduates graduating in 2015 was scheduled for December 16 at the Moot Court in Teaching Building E. The collection covered students in the Class of 2011 and students from the Classes of 2008–2010 who had not previously completed the process. The notice’s introduction and college-by-college schedule both point to December 16, 2013, although one line in the body lists 2012; this report follows the consistent date and schedule.

The schedule covered seven colleges:

| College | Expected students | Time |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| College of Liberal Arts | 140 | 08:15–08:35 |
| College of Arts and Design | 167 | 08:35–08:55 |
| Business School | 269 | 08:55–09:30 |
| College of Science | 192 | 09:30–10:00 |
| School of Journalism and Communication | 185 | 10:00–10:30 |
| Law School | 179 | 10:30–11:00 |
| College of Engineering | 376 | 14:00–15:30 |

The notice said students in the Class of 2011 were required to be photographed on campus. Students from the Classes of 2008–2010 who could not return because of internships or other special circumstances could contact the Guangdong Image Collection Center to make separate arrangements and submit the required photo materials to the Academic Affairs Office. Students taking photos on campus were to pay through their program’s academic staff and receive a barcode; the fee was RMB 15 per person. College academic staff were asked to submit fees, participant counts and unused barcodes by December 11. Students who had already completed image collection in a previous year were advised not to repeat it.
