The Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University has announced the schedule for the 2014 Test for English Majors-Band 8 (TEM-8) speaking test. The exam will be held on Saturday, December 7, at 8:30 AM in the E-Block Language Lab. Candidates are required to take the test in the seats assigned during the mock session.

Students must bring their admission ticket, ID card, and student ID to the exam venue — all three documents are mandatory. Candidates are advised to prepare in advance and arrive on time.
