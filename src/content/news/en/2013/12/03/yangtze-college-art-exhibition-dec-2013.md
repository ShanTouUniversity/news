The art gallery at the College of Art and Design, Shantou University, has opened its annual exhibition, inviting faculty, staff, and students to attend and engage with contemporary artistic works.

The opening ceremony was held on December 3 at 11:00 AM, and the exhibition will remain open through December 10 at the Yangtze College of Art and Design Gallery on campus. The showcase features works by multiple faculty and students, spanning painting, sculpture, installation art, and other media, offering a comprehensive look at the college's recent teaching and creative achievements.

Organizers stated that the gallery serves as a key platform for campus cultural and artistic exchange, and will continue to welcome the STU community throughout its operating hours, enriching campus cultural life and fostering a vibrant artistic atmosphere.
