The 13th EIP-CDIO Quality Enhancement Training Camp at Shantou University's College of Engineering has opened enrollment, targeting freshmen of the 2013 cohort with a quota of 30 students.

EIP-CDIO is the core philosophy behind STU's engineering education reform, emphasizing an approach that covers the full lifecycle of "Conceive, Design, Implement, Operate." The training camp aims to help new students gain a deeper understanding of the EIP-CDIO concept, improve their hands-on skills and innovative thinking, and strengthen their comprehensive qualities.

Students interested in participating should submit their registration forms before the deadline.
