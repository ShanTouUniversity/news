The first installment of the "E-Learning for the 21st Century: Teaching Design and Practice" workshop series was held on December 3 in Room E409, organized by the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University. The session focused on MOOCs (Massive Open Online Courses), drawing participation from faculty and students across multiple departments.

MOOCs have emerged as a significant innovation in higher education worldwide. The workshop explored three core questions: What are MOOCs? Why have they emerged? And what value do they hold for students, professors, and universities? Drawing on real-world examples from institutions around the globe, the presenter examined both the challenges and opportunities that MOOCs present to traditional higher education, as well as ways in which STU faculty can engage with and leverage this new paradigm.

The workshop is part of STU's ongoing effort to strengthen digital teaching capacity on campus and support faculty in adopting emerging educational technologies.
