Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office opened nominations for the candidate-university list under its 2014 overseas study funding plan on December 3, 2013. Supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the plan at that time accepted undergraduate applications for overseas master’s study. Applicants could select up to three universities from a list set by the university.

The office used the 2013 global university rankings to compile a list of the top 200 institutions and asked each school, including the Medical College, to recommend additional universities with strong academic standing in specific disciplines but outside that ranking group. Schools were asked to email suggestions to `xyli@stu.edu.cn` by December 16. The foundation would use their input to finalize the candidate list for 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The ranking and candidate-university lists are available as attachments in the original OA notice.
