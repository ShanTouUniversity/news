It is reported that the STU Admissions Office will launch the "Drink Water, Remember the Source; Pass on Dreams" winter recruitment outreach program during the 2014 winter break, recruiting undergraduate and graduate students to return to their alma mater high schools to promote the university.

The program includes introducing STU's distinctive educational features and achievements to high school administrators, teachers, and students, as well as conducting surveys on high school students' enrollment intentions to gather firsthand information for the university's admissions work. Students with enthusiasm are encouraged to sign up.
