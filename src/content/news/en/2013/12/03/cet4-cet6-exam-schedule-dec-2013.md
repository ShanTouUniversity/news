The Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University has announced the schedule for the first-half 2014 National College English Test (CET). The CET-4 will be held on Saturday, December 7, from 9:00 AM to 11:25 AM, while the CET-6 follows on the same afternoon from 3:00 PM to 5:25 PM.

The exam format has been adjusted this session, and candidates are advised to familiarize themselves with the new question types and test structure in their preparation.

Students are encouraged to manage their review time effectively and arrive on time for the examination.
