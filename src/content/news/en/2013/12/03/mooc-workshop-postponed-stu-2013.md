The Shantou University 21st Century E-Learning Teaching Design and Practice MOOC Workshop has been rescheduled from December 6 to December 13, 2013, due to a change in the keynote speaker's availability.

The workshop, organized by the College of Liberal Arts, will take place on Friday, December 13, from 14:30 to 16:00 in Room E409. It will address key questions including "What are MOOCs?" and "What's in it for me?" — exploring the value of massive open online courses for both educators and students.

The event is part of STU's ongoing initiative to promote teaching innovation through digital learning platforms. Attendees will learn about the latest developments in MOOC design and how to leverage these tools in their own teaching practice.
