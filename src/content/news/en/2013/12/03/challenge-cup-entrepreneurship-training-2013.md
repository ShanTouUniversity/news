The STU Youth League Committee has officially launched a series of training sessions for the campus selection of the 9th "Challenge Cup" Guangdong College Student Entrepreneurship Plan Competition. Themed "Creativity, Entrepreneurship, Creating the Future," the program aims to enroll 30 participants, with each team allowed to send two to three representatives.

The first session will be held on Saturday, December 14, from 9:00 AM to 11:30 AM, with the theme "Dreams Take Off." Subsequent sessions will cover topics such as business plan writing and pitch techniques, helping competitors improve the quality of their entrepreneurship plans and competition readiness.
