Shantou University’s Zhicheng College published Issue 44 of *Zhicheng Life* on December 3, 2013, reviewing college activities and student achievements from November. One feature covered an outdoor component of the college’s Team Theory and Leadership Practice course. From November 9–10, 25 students traveled to Sangpu Mountain in Jieyang with instructors Chen Wenbin, Liu Minquan and Jiang Dongping for hiking and team-based leadership exercises.

On November 24, the college and Dongxia Middle School held a student-leadership camp that provided 48 middle-school students with team-building, communication and leadership activities. The issue also reported that five Zhicheng students received Hejun commercial internship scholarships and that college students won awards in the National Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling, a national English debate competition and local speech contests. Other coverage included college arts activities, preparation for the university sports meet and inter-school exchanges.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce student names, psychological-test content or personal testimonials; it summarizes the college’s activities and group achievements.
