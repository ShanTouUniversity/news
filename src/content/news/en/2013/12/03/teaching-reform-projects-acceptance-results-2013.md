Shantou University completed the review of university-level teaching reform projects launched in 2009 and 2011 and released the results on December 3, 2013. A total of 43 projects entered the review, 11 of which applied for an extension. Among the projects rated in this round, five were deemed excellent, 17 good and 10 satisfactory.

The university said the results would inform future project approvals and applications for teaching achievement awards. The 11 projects granted extensions received one additional year and were required to complete review by October 2014; their results would be released with the next review group. Projects that passed were expected to continue developing and applying their work.
