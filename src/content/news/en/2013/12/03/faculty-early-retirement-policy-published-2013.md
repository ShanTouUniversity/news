Shantou University formally published its *Regulations on Faculty Leaving Posts for Early Retirement* on December 3, 2013. The university document said the regulations had been discussed and approved at the 15th university leadership meeting on November 26 and instructed university units to follow them.

The regulations and accompanying forms were released with the notice for university units and staff to consult. This report covers the policy’s publication and approval only and does not infer its eligibility or benefit terms.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
> ⚠️ The regulations and accompanying forms are available as attachments in the original OA notice.
