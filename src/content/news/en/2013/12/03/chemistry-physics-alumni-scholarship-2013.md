The selection committee of the College of Science at Shantou University has announced the results of the 2013 Alumni Scholarships for the Chemistry and Physics departments, with 40 students honored across both programs.

The selection process followed principles of fairness, transparency, and rigor, with the committee evaluating applicants based on academic performance and overall student development. Li Huina and 19 other students were awarded the Chemistry Department Alumni Scholarship, while He Guotang and 19 other students received the Physics Department Alumni Scholarship. Each recipient is awarded 2,500 RMB.

Funded by overseas alumni donations, the alumni scholarships aim to encourage students to pursue academic excellence and personal growth, while strengthening the bond between alumni and their alma mater.
