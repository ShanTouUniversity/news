Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office posted a notice on December 3, 2013, concerning proposed additional work for the second-phase renovation of staff dormitories at Longquanyuan. The project began on September 18. During construction, a misalignment between an original kitchen partition wall and the reinforced-concrete frame beam meant wall tiles could not be installed at a uniform height, prompting proposed work that included adding plastic kitchen ceilings.

The office initially estimated the additional cost at about RMB 20,000 and said it was below the university’s 10-percent threshold for project additions. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on December 5.
