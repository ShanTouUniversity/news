The first STU Guinness Record Competition was launched as one of the activities for the Student Clubs Culture Month, inviting students across campus to participate in a series of fun challenges.

The competition features multiple events spread over several days. On December 4, participants can try their hand at pi memorization and Rubik's Cube solving. December 7 brings a series of dexterity contests including typing showcase, threading a needle, slow cycling, and one-legged rope jumping. The final day, December 8, rounds out the event with a men's three-point shooting contest, a women's basketball shooting contest, a standing reach test, and finger-spinning a basketball.

All STU students are welcome to register and join the challenges.
