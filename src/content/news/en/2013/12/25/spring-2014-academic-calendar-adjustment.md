Shantou University announced an adjustment to the 2013–14 spring academic calendar on December 25, 2013. A university leadership meeting the previous day had moved the semester forward by one week, setting February 23, 2014, as the start date and June 28 as the end date.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
