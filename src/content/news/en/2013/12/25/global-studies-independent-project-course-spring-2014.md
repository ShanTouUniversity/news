Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts Center for Global Studies, formerly the Center for International Studies, outlined its CIS6900A Independent Study Project course for the spring 2014 semester on December 25, 2013. Open to undergraduates across the university, the course covered history, philosophy, political science, anthropology, film studies, American studies, cultural studies and psychology. Instruction could be in Chinese or English, depending on the supervisor and project.

Students were asked at the time to contact a supervisor in their chosen field and develop a project proposal by January 10, 2014. Supervisors would confirm students through an interview or meeting and register approved projects in the credit system. Projects generally lasted 10–12 weeks, or about 40–50 hours. Students earned one credit after submitting a report or paper and passing the assessment. The process covered supervisor contact and project planning, confirmation of students and plans, guided research, and a final report or paper.
