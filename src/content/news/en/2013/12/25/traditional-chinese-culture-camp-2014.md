It is reported that the eighth session of Zhicheng College's "Traditional Chinese Culture Camp" has opened for registration. The camp will be held from February 17 to 22, 2014 (the week before the spring semester begins).

**Program Content**

The camp provides a comprehensive overview of traditional Chinese culture, with study sessions covering Confucian classics such as Di Zi Gui (Standards for Students) and Zhou Yi (Book of Changes).

**Learning Format**

Participants will engage in classic recitation and small-group seminars to deepen their understanding of traditional cultural essence.

**Purpose**

The camp aims to inherit and promote traditional Chinese culture, fostering the integration of humanistic literacy and scientific spirit.

Interested students are encouraged to sign up.
