The results of the 2013 Guangdong College Students' "Langge Cup" English Interpretation Competition were recently announced. The competition, hosted by the Guangdong Foreign Language Association and organized by the School of Foreign Languages at Guangzhou University, is a provincial-level English interpreting contest for university students.

Three second-year students from the Department of Foreign Languages — Shen Zhuoxin, Peng Qiuhui, and Peng Junwen — along with their instructor Chen Jie, participated in the competition and were awarded third prize. Teacher Chen Jie also received the "Outstanding Instructor" award for her exemplary guidance.

The result highlights the solid training in foreign language education at STU and the collaborative efforts of teachers and students in developing practical interpreting skills.
