It is reported that the School of Liberal Arts will invite Karsten Krueger to deliver an academic lecture in the near future.

**Lecture Title**: A Short History of the Science Fiction Film in 90 Minutes / 时光穿梭，太空探索——90分钟的科幻电影简史

The lecture will explore core themes in science fiction cinema, including magic, time travel, and space exploration, guiding the audience through the development of sci-fi film history.

Specific time and venue details will be announced separately.
