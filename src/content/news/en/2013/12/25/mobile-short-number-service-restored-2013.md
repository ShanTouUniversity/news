Shantou University’s Network and Information Center updated the status of campus short-number services on December 25, 2013. After the mobile carrier resolved the fault, calls between campus mobile and landline short numbers resumed.

## Service Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Restored service | Calls between mobile and landline short numbers |
| Notice date | December 25, 2013 |
| Issuing unit | Network and Information Center |
| Service email | stunic@stu.edu.cn |

The update followed the Center’s fault notice issued the previous day.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
