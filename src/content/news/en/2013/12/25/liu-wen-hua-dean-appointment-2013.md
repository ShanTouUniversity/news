It is reported that, following the 16th STU Leadership Meeting held on December 10, 2013, the following personnel decisions were made:

- **Liu Wenhua** has been appointed Dean of the School of Science, with a six-month probation period
- **Lou Zengjian** will no longer serve as Dean of the School of Science

> Source: STU OA Notice (President's Office)
