It is reported that the School of Science has invited Professor Chen Jing from the Institute of Applied Physics and Computational Mathematics (IMPCC) to deliver an academic lecture on December 27.

**Topic**: Photoionization of Matter — From the Photoelectric Effect to Attosecond Physics

**Time**: December 27 (Friday), 16:10

**Venue**: School of Science Lecture Hall (Building Li-352)

**About the Speaker**: Chen Jing is a researcher at IMPCC, with long-standing research focused on laser physics and light-matter interaction.

The report will discuss how the invention and development of laser technology has expanded and deepened research on light-matter interactions, and trace the evolution of understanding the photoionization process of matter from weak-field to strong-field regimes.
