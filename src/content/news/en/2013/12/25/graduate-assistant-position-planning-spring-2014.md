Shantou University’s Graduate School announced the planning process for spring 2014 postgraduate assistantships on December 25, 2013. After reviewing requests from university units, the school determined quotas and asked units to create teaching and administrative assistant positions in line with those allocations and their needs.

At the time, units were to submit a *Graduate Teaching/Administrative Assistant Position Plan* to the Graduate School by January 6, 2014, and email a digital copy to fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn. The notice listed Wang Fengqi as the contact and 86504000 as the phone number. Unit-specific quotas were in the original notice attachments.
