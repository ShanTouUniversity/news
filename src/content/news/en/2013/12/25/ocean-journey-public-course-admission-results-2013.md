It is reported that the Science College at Shantou University announced the admission results for the "Ocean Journey" public course for the Spring 2014 semester on December 25. The course registration notice was published on December 18, and by noon on December 24, over 100 applications had been received. After screening the application resumes, 80 students were ultimately selected to take the course.

Additionally, the course team recruited 2 course assistants: Zhang K (Civil Engineering, Class of 2012) and Zhang JX (Journalism, Class of 2012).

Admitted students do not need to register for the course; the course team will input their information directly into the credit system. Students with questions about class placement should contact junyingwang@stu.edu.cn promptly for adjustments.
