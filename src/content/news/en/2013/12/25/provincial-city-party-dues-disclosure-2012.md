Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department circulated two notices on December 25, 2013, concerning disclosure of 2012 Party dues income and expenditure at the provincial and municipal levels. Issued by the Shantou Municipal Party Committee Organization Department, the notices were sent to the university’s campus-level Party committees and directly affiliated branches for communication to members and feedback collection.

The campus organizations were asked to send members’ comments to the university department by the end of the morning on December 27, 2013. The financial disclosure details were provided in the original notice attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ The provincial- and municipal-level Party dues disclosure materials are available in the original OA attachments.
