Shantou University issued a personnel decision on December 25, 2013, appointing Xiao Liangen to an intermediate management post. The appointment was considered at the 16th university leadership meeting on December 10 and carried a six-month probationary period.

## Appointment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Appointee | Xiao Liangen |
| Position | Intermediate management post |
| Meeting date | December 10, 2013 |
| Probation | Six months |

The decision finalized the appointment following an earlier public notice of the proposed appointment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
