The Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee approved a revised implementation framework for the university’s responsibility system on Party conduct, integrity building and anti-corruption work at its 16th meeting of 2013. The university issued the document on December 25, 2013, and called on its units to implement it. The revision updates rules first issued by the university in 2001.

The revised rules assign the university’s Party and administrative leadership collective responsibility for integrity-building work within their remit. The university Party secretary, president and heads of subordinate units serve as the corresponding first responsible persons, while other leadership members take direct responsibility according to their portfolios. The discipline inspection and supervision-audit offices coordinate oversight and help monitor implementation.

The framework calls for integrity work to be incorporated into institutional plans and annual priorities, alongside education on Party discipline and regulations, stronger systems and checks on the exercise of authority. It also sets expectations for personnel appointments, major decisions, projects and significant expenditures. Annual inspections and assessments will review implementation; leaders may be held accountable when inadequate performance causes serious consequences.
