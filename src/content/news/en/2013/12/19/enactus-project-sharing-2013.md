It is reported that the Shantou University Enactus team will hold its 2013 Project Sharing Session on December 22.

The session is scheduled for 15:00 on Sunday, December 22, at the Library Lecture Hall. Team members will present four ongoing projects: the Fenghuang Mountain Tea Promotion Project, the Leili-Fengxing Project, the Porcelain Renewal Project, and the Chaozhou Embroidery Revival Project. Representatives of the Li Ka Shing Foundation, leaders of the Business School, faculty advisors, academic advisors, and business advisors are expected to attend.

Founded on March 14, 2008, as SIFE (Students in Free Enterprise), the STU Enactus team now comprises 60 students from seven colleges. Enactus — a blend of "Entrepreneurial," "Action," and "Us" — is a global nonprofit headquartered in the United States, with independently operated student teams at 2,000 universities across 45 countries. Guided by a commitment to serving communities and supporting disadvantaged groups, the STU team has developed a series of social entrepreneurship projects aimed at empowering beneficiaries.
