It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office has launched the 2013 university-level Teaching Achievement Award selection to recognize individuals and groups that have made notable contributions to teaching and teaching administration, and to prepare candidates for provincial and national awards.

## Evaluation Focus

| Category | Focus |
|----------|-------|
| Teaching reform | Achievements in discipline and program restructuring, talent cultivation model reform, curriculum development, content and methodology reform, and practical teaching reform with demonstrated results |
| Teaching administration | Achievements in organizing teaching, advancing reform in teaching and administration, strengthening basic teaching infrastructure, and ensuring teaching quality |

## Eligibility

- Active faculty, teaching support staff, and teaching administrators (including contract-based personnel) are eligible; retired personnel may apply only for work completed while in service;
- Submissions may be by individuals or teams, with no more than five principal contributors for team projects; for inter-university collaborations, only achievements led by STU personnel are accepted;
- Entries must not have previously been submitted for the university-level award; previously awarded achievements with substantive new breakthroughs may be resubmitted based on the new developments;
- Supporting materials should include papers published in journals approved by provincial-level or higher press authorities plus reports on promotion and application, or for physical achievements, a description, test reports and appraisal certificates.

## Application and Evaluation Process

1. **Departmental review and recommendation**: Applicants complete the *STU Teaching Achievement Award Recommendation Form* (Attachment 1) and submit it to their academic unit or administrative department. Each unit forms an expert panel of no fewer than five members to conduct a preliminary review and recommend first-, second-, and third-prize candidates. By January 10, 2014, units should deliver five copies each of the recommendation form and attachments (summary report, supporting materials) plus one copy of the summary sheet (Attachment 2, signed by panel members) to the Teaching Reform and Evaluation Section, Academic Affairs Office (3rd Floor, New Administration Center), and email an electronic copy to liz@stu.edu.cn. Contact: Li Zhe, Tel: 86502736;
2. **University review and public notice**: The Academic Affairs Office organizes a review committee to determine prize levels and publishes the results for university-wide comment;
3. **Appeals and approval**: During the notice period, any individual or unit may file a written appeal; after the period ends, results are submitted to the supervising vice president for approval, then formally announced and rewarded.
