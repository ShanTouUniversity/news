It is reported that the Shantou University Admissions Office will hold a training session for the "Drink Water, Remember Source; Pass on Dreams" admissions outreach program at 10 am on Dec. 21 (Saturday) in Room 101, Lecture Hall E.

As of the morning of Dec. 19, 577 students across all colleges had signed up to participate in the winter admissions outreach. The training will cover event arrangements and key注意事项, and feature experience sharing from outstanding volunteers from previous years.
