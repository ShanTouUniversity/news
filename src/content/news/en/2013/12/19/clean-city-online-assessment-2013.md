On December 20, 2013, Shantou University’s Office of Discipline Inspection forwarded notices from the Shantou discipline inspection authority and Guangdong’s office for building a clean government about an online assessment of clean-city initiatives. Faculty, staff and students were invited to provide an objective assessment of Shantou’s integrity and anti-corruption work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Discipline Inspection)
