The School of Engineering at Shantou University will host a lecture by Dr. Zhang Kaixu on December 25 at 10:00 AM in the Academic Exchange Room (Engineering South 312). The lecture, titled "Subword Feature Research in Chinese Natural Language Processing," will explore the application and prospects of subword features in Chinese information processing.

Subword features represent an important research direction in natural language processing, playing a significant role in improving the accuracy and efficiency of Chinese text understanding. The lecture will discuss recent advances and their implications for Chinese NLP systems.

Faculty and students from the Department of Computer Science and all interested parties are welcome to attend.
