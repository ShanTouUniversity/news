The College of Science at Shantou University has invited Professor Tang Yuan Yan of the University of Macau to deliver an academic lecture titled "Mathematics from an Engineering Perspective" on December 20 at 4:00 PM in the Mathematics Department Laboratory.

Using Hilbert spaces from functional analysis as a prime example, the lecture will explore how the highly abstract and generalized nature of mathematics offers profound insights for faculty and students outside the mathematical disciplines. Prof. Tang's talk aims to help STEM students and researchers better appreciate the intrinsic connections between mathematical theory and engineering practice.

Faculty, staff, and students from the College of Science and other interested parties are welcome to attend.
