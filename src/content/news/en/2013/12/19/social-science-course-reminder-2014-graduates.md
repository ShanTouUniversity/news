Shantou University’s Social Sciences Department reminded the Class of 2014 on December 19, 2013, to compare their completed ideological and political theory courses with the requirements of their respective schools and take any outstanding compulsory courses that semester. The department said missing requirements discovered during graduation review could affect graduation.

The notice also reiterated that all students were required to take Basic Principles of Marxism (three credits) and Outline of Modern Chinese History (two credits). Students were asked to check the applicable course codes and credit requirements for other courses, including Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law and Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics, according to their cohort and curriculum plan.
