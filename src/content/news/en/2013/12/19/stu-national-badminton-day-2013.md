Shantou University’s Sports Department announced plans for a “National Badminton Day” event on December 19, 2013. The activity was scheduled for 3 p.m. on Saturday, December 21, at the campus gym. The Sports Department was to host it, with the Shantou University Badminton Club organizing it. Current undergraduates and graduate students were eligible to attend.

The program featured four badminton games. Players could earn a ticket by landing at least three of five net shots in the target area, landing at least two of five high clears in a target area near the baseline, or catching at least two shuttlecocks in a tube as staff fed them across the court. In “Hundred Steps to Hit the Target,” each participant had five attempts and could earn a ticket by putting at least three shots through the larger opening or two through the smaller one. Prizes were tiered according to the number of tickets earned.
