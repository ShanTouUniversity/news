It is reported that the "动感地带" 21st Shantou University Top Ten Singers Competition Grand Final will be held at the STU Auditorium on the evening of December 21 (Saturday), organized by the University Youth League Committee and the Student Union.

## Final Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date & Time | December 21, 2013 (Saturday), 19:30 |
| Venue | STU Auditorium |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Student Union |

Admission to the grand final is free, with early-entry and VIP wristbands available. Two entry channels will be open — wristband holders may enter from 19:00, while other guests enter from 19:10. Audience members will receive a program booklet; tearing off the stub allows voting for favorite singers, with the highest vote-getter winning the "Most Popular Singer Award," presented live at the event.

## Pre-Event Activity

On December 20 (Friday), 11:00–13:00, the organizing committee will hold a "Heart · Voice" singing event at the Golden Triangle, where participants can win early-entry or VIP wristbands. For more details, follow the Student Union Weibo (@汕头大学学生会) or visit the official website.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
