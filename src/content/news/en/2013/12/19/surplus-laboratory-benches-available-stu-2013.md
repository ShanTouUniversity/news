Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced surplus laboratory equipment for campus reassignment on December 19, 2013. Following a laboratory renovation, seven benches in Engineering Building Room 402 were available for units to claim through the Equipment and Materials Management Office.

| Dimensions | Quantity | Features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,400 × 650 × 850 mm | 1 | Gray, three doors, three drawers and an electrical outlet |
| 1,200 × 750 × 850 mm | 6 | Gray, two doors, two drawers and an electrical outlet |

The office could be reached at 86502442 to arrange reassignment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
