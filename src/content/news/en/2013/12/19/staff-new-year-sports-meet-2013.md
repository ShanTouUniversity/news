It is reported that the Shantou University Labor Union will host a New Year fun sports meet for faculty and staff at 2:45 pm on Dec. 31 at the skating rink, celebrating the coming of the new year and enriching extracurricular life.

Teams will be formed by departmental unions, with ten teams registered: the Liberal Arts and News Joint Team, College of Sciences, College of Engineering, Law/Business Joint Team, Art/Physical Education Joint Team, Research/Graduate School Joint Team, Social Sciences/Continuing Education/Primary School Joint Team, Library, Administrative Staff Team, and Resources Management. Teams with a larger number of participants may form up to two squads for the events.
