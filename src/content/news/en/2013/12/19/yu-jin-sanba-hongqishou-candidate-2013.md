It is reported that the women's committee of the Shantou University Labor Union has proposed Prof. Yu Jin from the College of Engineering as the school's candidate for the Shantou "March 8 Red Banner Holder" award. The public notice period runs until Dec. 22, 2013.

Any objections may be submitted in writing or by phone to the university labor union at 86502983, or via email at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn.
