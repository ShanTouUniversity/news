Shantou University’s Research Office announced the 2013 internal research-funding results on December 19, 2013. A total of 22 projects received support: 10 National Fund cultivation projects, three Youth Research Fund projects and nine humanities research projects. Proposals underwent external review and a Research Committee vote before approval by the University Academic Committee.

Funded topics included “Catalytic and Photochemical Reaction Mechanisms of OH Radicals with Some VOCs” and “Supporting Rural Women’s Participation in Village-Level Governance through a ‘Project Advancement’ Model.” The programs were established to support academic development, strengthen faculty research and prepare applications for higher-level funding. The full award list was attached to the OA notice.
