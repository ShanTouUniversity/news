Shantou University’s Center for Global Studies in the College of Liberal Arts planned a special discussion titled “Globalization: More Than a Concept” from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on Friday, December 20, 2013, in Room E409. The event, to be conducted in Chinese, was organized by the center, formerly known as the Center for International Studies.

Speaker Wang CF was then a third-year doctoral student in global studies at Japan’s Sophia University. Her research interests included sociology of knowledge and taste, urban cultures in China and Japan, and interactions between globalization and localization. She had studied in Japan and worked in Tokyo and Shanghai.
