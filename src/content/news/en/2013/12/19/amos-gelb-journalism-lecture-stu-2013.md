Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication announced on December 19, 2013, that television producer Amos Gelb was scheduled to speak at 7 p.m. that evening in the Library Lecture Hall. The notice said Gelb had worked for CNN, ABC, FOX, National Geographic and Discovery, had more than 20 years of media experience, and had received Emmy, cable television and duPont-Columbia awards.

The lecture description also noted that Gelb had worked at Xinhua News Agency during the early period of China’s reform and opening-up.
