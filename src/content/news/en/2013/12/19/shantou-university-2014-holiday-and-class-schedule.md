Shantou University’s Party and Administration Office announced the university’s 2014 holiday and class schedule on December 19, 2013, following the State Council General Office’s holiday calendar for that year.

| Holiday | Schedule announced at the time |
| --- | --- |
| New Year’s Day | January 1, one day off |
| Spring Festival | January 31–February 6, seven days off |
| Qingming Festival | April 5 off, with April 7 as a compensatory day off |
| Labor Day | May 1–3 off; May 4 a working day, with May 2 classes swapped with the May 4 schedule |
| Dragon Boat Festival | June 2 off, adjoining the weekend |
| Mid-Autumn Festival | September 8 off, adjoining the weekend |
| National Day | October 1–7 off; September 28 and October 11 were working days. October 6 classes were swapped with September 28, and October 7 classes with October 11. |

The notice also asked units to conduct safety and energy-saving checks before the breaks and arrange duty coverage. The university said the holiday and class arrangements could be revised if required by higher authorities or changing circumstances.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administration Office)
