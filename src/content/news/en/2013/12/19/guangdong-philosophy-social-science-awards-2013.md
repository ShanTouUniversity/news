The results of the 2013 Guangdong Philosophy and Social Sciences "12th Five-Year Plan" project approvals were recently announced, with three Shantou University projects awarded funding. Among them, one is a general project and two are youth projects, with a total grant of 90,000 RMB.

This year, the university submitted 24 proposals, spanning seven units including the College of Art and Design, Law School, Higher Education Research Institute, Business School, Department of Social Sciences, College of Liberal Arts, and Changjiang College of Journalism and Communication. The successful applications demonstrate the broad research strength and sustained innovation capacity of STU in the field of philosophy and social sciences.
