It is reported that Prof. Li Zhongqing from the Department of History at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology will deliver an academic lecture titled "Wealth Distribution in Late Imperial and Contemporary China" on the evening of December 12, from 7:00 to 9:00 PM, at the Library Lecture Hall, Shantou University.

Prof. Li is a distinguished historian with significant contributions to the fields of demographic history and social history. The lecture will explore patterns of wealth distribution in China from the late Qing dynasty to the present day, drawing on historical data and case studies to analyze the dynamics of economic and social transformation over several centuries.

All faculty, students, and staff are welcome to attend.
