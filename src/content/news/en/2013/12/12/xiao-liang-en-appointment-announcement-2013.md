It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University announced on December 12 that, following the 16th university leadership meeting held on December 10, Xiao Liang-en has been proposed for appointment to a mid-level management position.

According to the "Shantou University Trial Measures for Management Cadre Appointment" (汕大党字〔2009〕47号), the public notice details are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed Position | Mid-level Management |
| Proposed Appointee | Xiao Liang-en |
| Deciding Body | 16th University Leadership Meeting, December 10, 2013 |
| Notice Deadline | Before December 18, 2013 |
| Submission Method | Written materials or calls to the Human Resources Office; institutional documents must be sealed with official stamp, personal submissions must bear real name |
| Phone | 86503390 |
| Email | rscgs@stu.edu.cn |
| Issuing Office | Human Resources Office |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
