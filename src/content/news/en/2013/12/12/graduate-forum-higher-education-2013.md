The higher-education session of Shantou University’s ninth graduate academic forum was scheduled for December 15, 2013, under the theme “Education Around Us.” Organized by the Graduate School and hosted by the Institute of Higher Education, it was set for 7–10 p.m. in room E203.

Guests Kang Quanli, Hu Wenlong and Lin Lushui were listed, alongside five student presentations on residential colleges and university belonging, the purpose of graduate education, student study habits, centralization and university autonomy in modern France, and a comparison of MIT-CDIO and STU’s OBE outcomes-based model. Student presenters are identified using surname-and-initials privacy formatting: Chen JM, Mao JD, Sun DL, Yan MH and Zhang Y.
