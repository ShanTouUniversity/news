Shantou University’s College of Science scheduled a seminar on December 16, 2013, with Zhao Ruhan, associate professor in the Department of Mathematics at the State University of New York at Brockport. His talk, “Topological Structure of the Spaces of Composition Operators on Hilbert Spaces of Dirichlet Series,” examined topological properties of spaces of bounded composition operators and included joint research with Bingyang Hu and Le Hai Khoi.

The seminar was set for 4:30 p.m. in the Mathematics Laboratory. Zhao’s research focuses on function spaces and operator theory; he had published more than 50 mathematics papers.
