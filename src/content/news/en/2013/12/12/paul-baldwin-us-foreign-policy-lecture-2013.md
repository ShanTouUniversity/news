It is reported that Paul R. Baldwin, an expert on U.S. foreign policy, will deliver an academic lecture at Shantou University in December, providing an in-depth analysis of American diplomatic strategy.

The lecture will cover the various factors that influence the formulation of U.S. foreign policy, including domestic politics, the international landscape, interest groups, and public opinion, while systematically explaining the mechanisms and decision-making processes behind American diplomatic policy.

All faculty, students, and staff are welcome to attend.
