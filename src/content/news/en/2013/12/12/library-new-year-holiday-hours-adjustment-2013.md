It is reported that the Library of Shantou University announced on December 12 that, starting from the 2014 New Year's Day, the Library will maintain regular opening hours during national holidays that fall within the academic semester.

This adjustment means that during the semester, the Library will no longer close on national holidays such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Qingming Festival, Labour Day, Dragon Boat Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, and National Day. The Library will continue to provide book lending and study space services to faculty and students throughout these holidays.
