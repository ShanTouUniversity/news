It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University announced on December 12 the finals bracket for the staff badminton team competition. Four championship matches will be held consecutively starting at 17:00 on December 13 at the gymnasium badminton courts.

## Finals Bracket

| Match | Court | Teams |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Champion vs. Runner-up | Court 6 | Administration Office vs. Library & Research United |
| 3rd vs. 4th place | Court 5 | Arts & Society United vs. School of Engineering Team 2 |
| 5th vs. 6th place | Court 2 | Physical Education Dept. vs. Law & Business United |
| 7th vs. 8th place | Court 3 | School of Science vs. Liberal Arts & News United |

## Match Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Round | Finals |
| Time | Friday, December 13, 2013, from 17:00 |
| Venue | Gymnasium Badminton Courts |
| Organizer | University Labor Union |

All faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend and cheer.
