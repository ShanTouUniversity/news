The sixth Shantou New Year “China Life Cup” table tennis tournament for leadership officials was scheduled for January 3–5, 2014, at the Shantou Table Tennis School. The Shantou Municipal Sports Bureau was the organizer; the Shantou Table Tennis School and Association were co-organizers, with China Life’s Shantou branch and Xintianhua Advertising as supporting organizations.

Shantou University’s labor union circulated the event information on December 12, 2013. University employees interested in participating were asked to send a registration form to o_xgh@stu.edu.cn by December 18. Inquiries could be directed to Ms. Ye at 86503197. Eligibility and competition rules were set out in the organizers’ regulations.
