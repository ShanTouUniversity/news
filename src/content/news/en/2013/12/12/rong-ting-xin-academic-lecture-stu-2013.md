It is reported that the School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will invite Rong Tingxin, chief editor of Taiwan'"s Wang Bao and China Times, to deliver an academic lecture on December 13.

The lecture, titled "My News Marathon," will explore Rong Tingxin's experiences navigating press censorship in Taiwan and his commitment to journalistic ethics amidst Taiwan's evolving press freedom. The event is open to all faculty and students.
