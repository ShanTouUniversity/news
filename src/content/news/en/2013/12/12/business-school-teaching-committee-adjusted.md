It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University has recently issued a document adjusting the membership of its Teaching Committee, citing institutional personnel changes and work needs, and following the relevant regulations and procedures set out in the school's Working Regulations for the Teaching Committee.

Under the new composition, Huang Yan serves as chair of the committee and Xu Xiaojing as secretary. The members, listed in order of surname stroke count, include Wang X (one character of this name could not be fully confirmed due to a display issue in the original document), Long Yue'e, Ji Yun, Lü Yuan, Li Yue, Song Xiaobao, Lin Quan, Ouyang Feng, Huang Wangming, Qin Zhong, and Zeng Qiang.

The current committee was established in June 2012 and serves a three-year term.

| Term | From June 2012, three years |
