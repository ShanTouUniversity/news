Shantou University’s Business School issued a document on December 12, 2013, revising the membership of its degree evaluation subcommittee. Lü Yuan was named chair and Ouyang Feng deputy chair. Members were Wang Yi, Wang Jianhua, Ji Yun, Song Xiaobao, Chen Yinglin, Huang Yan, Huang Wangming, Qin Zhong and Zeng Qiang, listed by the stroke count of their surnames.

The adjustment was made in response to organizational and personnel changes and operational needs. The document, No. 16 [2013] of the Business School, was sent to the Shantou University Degree Evaluation Committee.
