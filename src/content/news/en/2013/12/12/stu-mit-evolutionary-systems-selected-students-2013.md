It is reported that the School of Engineering at Shantou University announced on December 12 that, after a selection process, 50 students have been successfully selected for the 2014 STU-MIT exchange course "Evolutionary Processes and Systems."

Selected students will undertake the course from March 10 to May 16, 2014, using a blended learning model combining on-campus instruction with remote learning. MIT professors will teach on campus for two weeks, while the intervening period will be conducted via the edX platform.
