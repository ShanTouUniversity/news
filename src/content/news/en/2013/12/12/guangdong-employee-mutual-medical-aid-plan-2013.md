After consultations involving the university labor union, finance office and human resources office, Shantou University planned to include eligible employees in the 2013 Class A Guangdong Employee Mutual Medical Aid Program. The labor union announced the arrangement on December 12, 2013. Eligibility covered employees who had joined Shantou social insurance before November 2013 and were under 55 if female or under 60 if male.

Each unit of coverage cost RMB 80 and provided RMB 10,000 in benefits for a three-year term, from December 10, 2013, to December 9, 2016. First-time participants were subject to a 180-day waiting period. The notice listed six major conditions: primary cancer, chronic renal failure (uremia), surgery for a primary intracranial tumor, coronary artery bypass surgery, heart valve replacement surgery and atypical pneumonia. The existing special-disease insurance for women was incorporated into this program and would not be renewed separately.
