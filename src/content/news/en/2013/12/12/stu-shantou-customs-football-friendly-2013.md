The Shantou University staff football team and the Shantou Customs football team planned to play a friendly match from 3 to 5 p.m. on Saturday, December 14, 2013, at Baijia Peninsula Football Field. The university labor union issued the arrangements on December 12.

Participating staff could travel to the venue by bus, departing from the labor union office at 1:50 p.m. and Anju Project at 2 p.m. The event contact was Luo Yingguang, reachable at 86503271.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
