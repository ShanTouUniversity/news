It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on December 12 the launch of registration for the second phase of the Peer Growth "Study Friends Program." This phase's theme is "Growing Through Public Welfare," aimed at sharing experiences from campus and community public welfare activities.

This phase's senior student speakers include Huang Yilin (News, class of 2011) and Zheng Yingyi (Clinical Medicine, class of 2012), who will share their personal growth journeys through public welfare engagement.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Peer Growth "Study Friends Program" Phase 2 |
| Theme | Growing Through Public Welfare |
| Time | Sunday, December 15, 2013, 19:30–21:30 |
| Target Audience | Lower-year students, those passionate about public welfare |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

Students interested in participating are encouraged to register promptly.
