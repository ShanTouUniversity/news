It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on December 12, 2013, that the teaching quality assessment for the fall 2013 semester will begin shortly.

## Preparations

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Timetable check | Academic officers of each teaching unit should log in to the credit system at http://credit.stu.edu.cn to verify course information (course names, instructors, student lists and numbers); report any changes to the Teaching Section |
| Revise indicators | Revise assessment indicators for course groups based on unit curricula |
| System submission | Submit assessment indicators and course groupings in the credit system |
| Submission for approval | By December 19, print the reviewed indicators and groupings, have them signed by the unit head and submit to the Teaching Reform and Assessment Section |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
