It is reported that the Personnel Office issued a notice on December 12, 2013, that following deliberation at the 16th university leadership meeting on December 10, 2013, Liu Wenhua is proposed to be appointed as Dean of the College of Science, now publicized for comment.

In accordance with the *Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres of Shantou University* (Party Document [2009] No. 47), arrangements are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed post | Dean, College of Science |
| Candidate | Liu Wenhua |
| Decision | 16th leadership meeting, December 10, 2013 |
| Public comment deadline | December 18, 2013 |
| How to comment | Submit in writing to the Personnel Office; organizations should affix an official seal, individuals should sign with real name |
| Phone | 86503390 |
| Email | rscgs@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Personnel Office |
