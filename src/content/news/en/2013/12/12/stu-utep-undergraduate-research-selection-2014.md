Shantou University’s College of Science and the University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP) planned to continue their summer undergraduate research program in 2014. The college opened an internal selection process on December 12, 2013, seeking about three full-time chemistry undergraduates from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts. The program lasted 10 weeks. Applicants were expected to have taken part in an undergraduate research or extracurricular science project and, in principle, to have passed CET-6; applicants with a strong CET-4 result instead needed a recommendation from an English teacher.

Applications required a form, proof of GPA and class ranking, a copy of English-proficiency results, and an English essay of at least 500 words on the applicant’s academic goals and how the exchange could help achieve them. Materials were due at the Chemistry Department office by 11 a.m. on December 23, 2013; electronic copies could be sent to o_lxyhxx@stu.edu.cn. Selection included an application review, a laboratory assessment and an English interview. About three students and one or two alternates were to be selected; assessment and interview dates would be announced later. The program covered participants’ round-trip travel, accommodation, medical insurance and basic living expenses.
