Shantou University’s Zhicheng College opened registration in December 2013 for its spring 2014 general-education course “University Experience and Career Development.” The course aimed to help students plan their academic and professional paths, understand employer needs and strengthen workplace skills.

It was open to voluntary applicants from Zhicheng College and selected student residences, with 50 places. Students who completed 24 hours plus the required practical work and assessment could earn one credit. Bai Xu, a college mentor and corporate human-resources executive, was the instructor. Classes were scheduled every other Friday evening in room J-354 at Zhicheng College.

Registration closed at 5 p.m. on December 18. Applications were to be emailed to ATP@stu.edu.cn. The notice also specified a filename format; full instructions were in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The application form and detailed instructions are available on the OA system.
