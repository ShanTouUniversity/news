On December 27, 2013, the Shantou University Student Union office announced service changes for the end of term and New Year period. From January 1, 2014, it suspended office duty and stopped processing requests for audio and other supplies and poster boards. The notice did not give a reopening date.

## Affected Services

| Service | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Office duty | Suspended from January 1, 2014 |
| Supply requests | Approval suspended |
| Poster-board requests | Approval suspended; urgent cases could contact the duty staff listed in the notice |
| Issuing units | STU Youth League Committee and Student Union |

Student contact names and personal phone numbers in the notice are not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee)
