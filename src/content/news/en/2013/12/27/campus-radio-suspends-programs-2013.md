It is reported that, with the final review and examination period approaching, the STU Campus Radio Station will suspend its noon and afternoon programs starting December 30, 2013, with normal broadcasting to resume after the new semester begins.

Departments with special broadcasting needs may contact the station. Contact: Ms./Mr. Liu, Tel: 86502203, Email: o_gbds@stu.edu.cn.

|------|---------|
