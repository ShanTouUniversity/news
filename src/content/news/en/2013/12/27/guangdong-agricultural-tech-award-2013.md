It is reported that, pursuant to a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Agricultural Technology Promotion Award Review Committee, the 2013 application window is now open.

**Eligibility Requirements**

Recommended projects must comply with the conditions stipulated in the *Guangdong Provincial Agricultural Technology Promotion Reward Interim Measures* and its implementation rules. Detailed evaluation criteria and application procedures are available in the official application guidelines.

Faculty and researchers interested in applying are encouraged to monitor further notices and prepare their application materials in accordance with the requirements.
