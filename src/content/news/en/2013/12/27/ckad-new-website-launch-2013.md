It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design issued a notice on December 27, 2013, that its new website has been officially launched.

The new website was designed and compiled by Yang Hua of the Interface and Interaction Design program, together with laboratory administrator Chen Jianfeng and a student team of the program. The URL remains www.ckad.stu.edu.cn, and the school will keep the site updated with the latest information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
