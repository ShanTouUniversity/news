Shantou University’s Women’s Studies Center in the College of Liberal Arts introduced three women’s studies-related general education courses for the spring 2014 semester on December 27, 2013: “Women, International Philanthropy and Culture,” “Introduction to Social Work and Women’s Issues,” and “Fieldwork and Women’s Oral Histories.”

“Women, International Philanthropy and Culture,” selected for the university’s third group of core courses, was to be taught in both English and Chinese. It would examine the development of women’s philanthropy from global and local perspectives and use case studies to introduce the work of related projects and organizations. The notice also provided the center’s website for further course information.
