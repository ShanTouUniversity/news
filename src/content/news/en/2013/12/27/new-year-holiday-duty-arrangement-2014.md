Shantou University’s General Office released duty arrangements for the 2014 New Year holiday on December 27, 2013. The university compiled duty information for its units to facilitate internal coordination during the break; the detailed roster was provided as an OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
> ⚠️ The names and contact details in the unit-by-unit duty roster are available on the OA system.
