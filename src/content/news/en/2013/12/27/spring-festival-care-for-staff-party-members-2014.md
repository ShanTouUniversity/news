Shantou University planned to provide assistance before the 2014 Spring Festival to staff Party members experiencing financial or other hardship. On December 27, 2013, the university Party Committee organized an initial review under the university’s interim measures for its Party-member care and mutual-assistance fund.

Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches were asked to identify relevant cases and submit names and basic information to the Organization and United Front Work Department by January 3, 2014. This report does not reproduce names or personal details concerning individuals’ hardship.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
