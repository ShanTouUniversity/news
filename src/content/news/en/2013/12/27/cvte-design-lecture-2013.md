Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design announced a design lecture on December 27, 2013. CVTE design director Zhong Jinhui was scheduled to discuss everyday aesthetics and design at the School’s art museum on the evening of December 30. The planned topics included noticing beauty in daily life, the reach of design, and creating conditions that support design.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Zhong Jinhui, design director at CVTE |
| Time | 7:30–9 p.m., December 30, 2013 |
| Venue | STU School of Art and Design Art Museum |
| Theme | Everyday aesthetics and design |
| Experience | Former design director at Creative Technology; recipient of six Red Dot awards and three CES awards |

The notice also introduced CVTE and its campus recruitment channels. This report records the announced program and does not independently confirm that the event took place.
