It is reported that the Business School plans to select enrolled students to participate in the international case competition scheduled for the second half of 2014. Student selection and training will take place in January 2014.

**Eligibility Requirements**

- Undergraduate students from the 2011 or 2012 cohorts
- Completion of economics and business management courses
- ELC course average score of no less than 80

**Registration Deadline**: December 30, 2013

Students interested in participating are encouraged to register promptly to qualify for subsequent training and selection.
