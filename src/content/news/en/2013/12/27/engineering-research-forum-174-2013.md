On December 27, 2013, Shantou University’s College of Engineering announced the program for its 174th Engineering Research Forum. The forum was scheduled for the morning of January 2, 2014, in the College’s Academic Exchange Room, on “Mechanics of Functionally Graded Materials and Structures.”

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Zhong Zheng, then professor and doctoral supervisor at Tongji University’s School of Aerospace Engineering and Applied Mechanics |
| Topic | Mechanics of functionally graded materials and structures |
| Time | 10 a.m.–noon, January 2, 2014 |
| Venue | College of Engineering Academic Exchange Room |
| Audience | Engineering faculty and students, and other interested members of the university |

The notice identified Zhong as a recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars. This report records the announced schedule and does not independently confirm that the event took place.
