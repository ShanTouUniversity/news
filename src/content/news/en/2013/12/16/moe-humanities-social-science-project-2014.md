It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University announced on December 16 the launch of the 2014 Ministry of Education Humanities and Social Sciences Research General Program, open to all faculty members.

The program is divided into three categories: Planned Research Projects (funding up to 100,000 RMB), Youth Research Projects (funding up to 80,000 RMB), and Self-funded Projects (self-funding not less than 80,000 RMB). The research period is typically three years. Faculty interested in applying should contact the Research Office for detailed guidelines and materials.

| Category | Funding |
| --- | --- |
| Planned Research Project | Up to 100,000 RMB |
| Youth Research Project | Up to 80,000 RMB |
| Self-funded Project | Minimum 80,000 RMB self-funding |
| Research Duration | Typically 3 years |
