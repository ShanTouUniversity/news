It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice to all schools on December 16, announcing that two classes of the Education course offered by the Higher Education Research Institute would be suspended. The arrangements are as follows:

| Class Number | Suspension Time |
| --- | --- |
| 63009 | Tuesday of week 14 (December 17), periods 1–2 |
| 63010 | Tuesday of week 14 (December 17), periods 3–4 |

Students in the relevant classes may adjust their study plans for the day accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
