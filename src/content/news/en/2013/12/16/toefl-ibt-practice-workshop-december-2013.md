Shantou University’s English Language Center planned a TOEFL iBT practice session with Gena Rhoades and Kristine Adams from 3:30 to 5 p.m. on Friday, December 20, 2013, at CILL. Seats were limited, and the original notice provided an Eventbrite page for reservations.

The center advised students not to register unless they were sure they could attend and asked registrants to be present.
