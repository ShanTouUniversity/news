It is reported that the Graduate School at Shantou University will host the civil engineering session of the 9th Graduate Academic Forum on the morning of December 18, themed "Innovative Research in Civil Engineering."

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | 9th Graduate Academic Forum — Civil Engineering Session |
| Theme | Innovative Research in Civil Engineering |
| Date & Time | Wednesday, December 18, 2013, 8:00 AM–12:00 PM |
| Venue | Room 307, Building G |
| Organizer | Graduate School |

The forum will feature presentations by graduate students on cutting-edge topics including polyvinyl alcohol-modified EPS insulation mortar ratio research, nonlinear finite element analysis of ultra-long large-diameter pile bearing capacity, compressive behavior of concrete columns reinforced with steel fiber and carbon fiber cloth, self-centering node performance based on ABAQUS, and key technologies for seamless bridges. Guest judges include Professors Zhuang Lihong, Liu Jilu, Yu Ying, Hong Chengyu, and Jiang Guoyi.
