Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office planned to hold a briefing for the 2013 undergraduate cohort from 3 to 5 p.m. on Wednesday, December 18, 2013, in the Auditorium. The session would explain the university’s free English or Business Administration double-degree and minor options, established following a university leadership meeting decision.

Topics were to include the purpose of the program, its implementation rules, and the study requirements for the English and Business Administration programs, followed by a question-and-answer session. Interested students in the 2013 cohort were invited to attend.
