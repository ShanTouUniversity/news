It is reported that the Business School at Shantou University will hold an information session for Spring 2014 elective courses on December 18 from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM in Lecture Hall 1. Course instructors will be present to introduce course content, teaching arrangements, and assessment methods, and will answer student questions on-site.

The event is open to all students across the university. It is especially recommended that students attend to plan their course selections in advance. Please note that for non-Business School students enrolled before 2013 who take these courses, credits will be handled in accordance with regulations set by the Academic Affairs Office.
