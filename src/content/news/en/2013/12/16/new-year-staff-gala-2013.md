It is reported that the Shantou University Labor Union will host a New Year's staff gala on the afternoon of December 29, supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation. All main campus faculty and staff are invited to attend, and they may bring their family members and children.

The event features a varied program including special performances, afternoon tea tasting, and a lucky draw, aiming to enrich staff life outside work and foster a warm and harmonious campus atmosphere.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | December 29, 2013, 15:30–18:00 |
| Venue | 君华大酒店 2nd Floor Ballroom |
| Attendees | Main campus faculty and staff (family members welcome) |
| Activities | Special performances, afternoon tea, lucky draw |
