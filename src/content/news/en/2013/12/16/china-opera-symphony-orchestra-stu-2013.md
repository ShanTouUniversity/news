It is reported that the symphony orchestra of the China National Opera & Dance Drama Corporation will perform at the Lin Baixin Convention Center on the evening of December 30. The STU Labor Union is organizing this event, offering STU faculty and students a 15% discount on tickets with their campus ID.

## Performance Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Performer | Symphony Orchestra, China National Opera & Dance Drama Corporation |
| Chief Conductor | Liu Fengde |
| Soloists | Wawa, Tongyao, Geng Liping, Xu Bin |
| Date & Time | Monday, December 30, 2013, 20:00 |
| Venue | Lin Baixin Convention Center |
| Ticket Prices | 80, 180, 280, 380, 480, 680 RMB (limited 880 RMB) |
| Discount | 15% off for STU faculty and students with campus ID |

## Ticket Purchase

On-campus ticket sales: December 20, 10:00–17:30, first floor of the Labor Union. Phone reservations: 0754-88630100, 13790800862 (Lin Tingmei). Event website: http://dlj.st001.com/.
