It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced on December 16 the launch of the "Heart & Voice" singing auditions for the 21st Campus Top Ten Singers Competition, open to all students.

The audition period runs from December 16 to 20. Students may participate in two ways: by sharing the official Weibo post and leaving their name, phone number, favorite song, and desired performer; or by visiting the Golden Triangle event site to fill out a registration card in person.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Period | December 16–20, 2013 |
| Method 1 | Share on Weibo with name, phone, favorite song, and desired singer |
| Method 2 | Fill out a card at the Golden Triangle event site (Dec 16–20) |

The Campus Top Ten Singers Competition is one of STU's most popular annual cultural events, attracting music-loving students from across campus.
