It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Tourism Bureau Industry Post Training Steering Committee will hold the first Guangdong Hotel Vocational English Speech Competition from December 2013 to March 2014. Shantou University may recommend up to two students to compete, and the campus selection process has begun.

Students interested in participating must submit their registration materials before 11:00 AM on January 13, 2014. The competition aims to improve vocational English speaking skills among university students and identify talented individuals for Guangdong's hotel industry.
