It is reported that Associate Professor Zhao Ruhhan from the Department of Mathematics at the State University of New York at Brockport will deliver an academic lecture titled "Mathematics and Science Fiction" at Shantou University on the evening of December 18.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Mathematics and Science Fiction |
| Speaker | Dr. Zhao Ruhhan (Associate Professor, Dept. of Mathematics, SUNY Brockport) |
| Time | Wednesday, December 18, 2013, 19:00 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall 202, Building E |

Dr. Zhao received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the University of Joensuu, Finland, in 1996. From 1998 to 1999, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Tokyo supported by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science, and has served as a visiting scholar at multiple U.S. universities since 1999. His research focuses on function spaces and operator theory, and he has published over 50 papers in journals such as *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society*.

## Abstract

While mathematics and science fiction may seem unrelated, many Western mathematicians have authored science fiction works, and numerous novels incorporate mathematical themes. The lecture will be divided into two parts: the first introduces mathematicians who write science fiction, and the second explores science fiction novels centered on mathematical concepts.
