It is reported that the 1st Lingnan Cup "My Chinese Dream" English Writing Competition was held on December 7 at Guangzhou Maritime University. The competition, jointly organized by the Guangzhou Foreign Language Association and Guangzhou Daily's "Guangzhou English Morning Post," attracted students from multiple universities across Guangdong Province.

Led by teacher Peng Jian'e, two STU students stood out among numerous contestants, winning third prize in their respective categories.

Tang Xiaoting, a 2012-level student in the Foreign Languages Department of the School of Liberal Arts, won third prize in the Professional English Undergraduate Group. Ren Heyin from the College of Journalism and Communication won third prize in the Public English Undergraduate Group. The results demonstrate STU students' excellent English writing proficiency and reflect the positive outcomes of English teaching reform at the university.
