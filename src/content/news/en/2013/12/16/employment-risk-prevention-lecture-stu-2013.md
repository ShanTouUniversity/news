It is reported that the School of Engineering at Shantou University will host a lecture on "Employment Risk Prevention for College Students" on December 18, delivered by Associate Professor Shen Yi Yong, targeting all graduating students of the school.

As the employment landscape for college graduates becomes increasingly complex, various risks in the job search process have become more prominent. This lecture aims to help graduating students correctly understand potential risks they may encounter, enhance their self-protection awareness and coping abilities, and guide them toward rational and safe employment.

The lecture is expected to cover practical topics including recruitment fraud identification, key points in signing labor contracts, and protection of employment rights, providing important guidance for graduating students participating in campus recruitment and internships.
