It is reported that the Tielin Charity Foundation of Shantou City has established the "Ziqiang Scholarship" at Shantou University to support Anhui-born undergraduate students from economically disadvantaged families. After student applications, foundation review, and public notification by the Student Affairs Office, 20 students including Zhang Qin have been selected to receive 2,500 yuan each.

## Scholarship Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Sponsor | Tielin Charity Foundation, Shantou City |
| Eligibility | Anhui-born students from economically disadvantaged families at STU |
| Award Amount | 2,500 yuan per student |
| Number of Recipients | 20 students |

The Ziqiang Scholarship aims to promote the virtue of giving back to society and helping those in need. Recipients are encouraged to continue working diligently and strive to become talented individuals with ambition, knowledge, perseverance, and vigor, repaying the support of alumni and the broader community with outstanding achievements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
