It is reported that the Administration Sub-forum of the 9th Graduate Academic Forum at Shantou University will be held on December 18 in Room 213 of the Wennei Building, with the theme "Separation of Powers and Chinese Politics."

Hosted by the Graduate School, this academic forum aims to promote intellectual freedom, create an open and harmonious learning environment with a strong academic atmosphere, guide graduate students to actively engage in scientific research, stimulate inquiry-based learning awareness, cultivate innovative spirit and expressive skills, and provide a platform for graduate students to showcase their research achievements.

The Administration Sub-forum will explore the theme of "Separation of Powers and Chinese Politics," encouraging academic dialogue to deepen graduate students'" understanding of public administration theory and Chinese political practice.
