It is reported that the 2013 Shantou University Staff Badminton Team Competition has concluded. In the championship match, the Ketu Research Team defeated the Administrative Office Team 3:2 to claim the title.

| Rank | Team |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | Ketu Research Team |
| 2nd | Administrative Office Team |
| 3rd | Engineering School Team 2 |
| 4th | Arts & Social Sciences / Adult Education / Affiliated Primary School Joint Team |
| 5th | Physical Education Department |
| 6th | Law & Business Joint Team |
| 7th | Science School |
| 8th | Liberal arts & Journalism Joint Team |

The competition, organized by the Labor Union, brought together multiple departments and showcased the athletic spirit and competitive level of university staff.
