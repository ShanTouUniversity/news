It is reported that the School of Science at Shantou University will invite Professor Liu Wenhua from the Marine Biology Research Institute at STU to deliver an academic lecture titled "Basics of Whales and Dolphins" on December 21.

Professor Liu Wenhua, born in 1970, received his B.Sc. in Virology from Wuhan University in 1992 and his Ph.D. in Biology and Chemistry from City University of Hong Kong in 2005. He formerly served as an associate researcher at the Third Institute of Oceanography, State Oceanic Administration, in Xiamen. His main research interests include marine mammal conservation biology and ecological toxicology, with extensive field research experience in whale and dolphin conservation and marine environmental assessment.

The lecture will introduce basic biological knowledge of whales and dolphins, present the latest developments in global cetacean conservation research, and inspire students'" interest in marine mammal protection.
