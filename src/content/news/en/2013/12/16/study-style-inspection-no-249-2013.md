Shantou University’s “Three Styles” Development Office released bulletin No. 249 on December 16, 2013, summarizing two classroom inspections during week 13 of the fall semester. Checks took place at 8 a.m. on December 11 in the E teaching building and at 8 a.m. on December 12 in the DG and E teaching buildings.

The bulletin recorded five students arriving no more than five minutes late on December 11. On December 12, it recorded 10 students arriving no more than five minutes late and one arriving more than five minutes late. The students’ names are not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University “Three Styles” Development Office)
