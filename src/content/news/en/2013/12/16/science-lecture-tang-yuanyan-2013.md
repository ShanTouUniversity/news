It is reported that the School of Science will hold a Science Lecture Hall academic lecture on the evening of December 19, 2013, featuring Prof. Tang Yuanyan of the University of Macau.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The 21st Century, Computers, and Pattern Recognition |
| Speaker | Prof. Tang Yuanyan, University of Macau |
| Time | 19:00, December 19, 2013 |
| Venue | Room E202 |
| Organizer | School of Science |

The lecture will review the history of computers, showcasing typical models from various stages with many precious photos, including the world’s earliest computer, computers from the 1950s and 1960s, and the first personal computer, taken at the British Museum in London. It will outline future trends in computer science and technology, focusing on pattern recognition—an important branch of artificial intelligence—its basic concepts, methods, international developments and research frontiers, with application examples in medical and military fields. All faculty and students are welcome to attend. A speaker profile is available in the OA attachment.
