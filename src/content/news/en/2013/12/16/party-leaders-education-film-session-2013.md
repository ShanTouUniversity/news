Shantou University’s Publicity Department announced on December 16, 2013, that a collective educational film session was planned for 4 p.m. on Thursday, December 19, in the Science Lecture Hall. The session was intended for Party officials at deputy division level and above. Units were also asked to check the email notice for further information.
