It is reported that the Department of Social Sciences at Shantou University will host an academic lecture on Saturday, Dec. 21, from 9 am to 11 am in Building E lecture hall. Dr. Cai Cai'ang, standing deputy director of the Shantou Municipal Social Work Committee, will deliver the keynote address.

The lecture topic is "Social Construction and Social Work," and the session is open to students enrolled in the course "Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics" this semester.
