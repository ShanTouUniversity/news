Shantou University Library announced on December 18, 2013, a two-stage plan to remove long-unused belongings occupying shared spaces.

The first stage was scheduled for December 25, when cups left in pantry areas on each floor would be collected and held in Seminar Room 116 on the first floor. Readers could claim them by January 19, 2014; items left after that date would be treated as unclaimed property. The second stage was scheduled for January 20, 2014, covering items left in reading halls, book-area lockers and on shelves or desks in floor printing rooms. Items would also be held in Seminar Room 116, except cups, which would be placed on pantry shelves on the same floor. The claim deadline for the second-stage items was March 23, 2014, after which they would be disposed of. Readers were asked to remove personal belongings in advance.
