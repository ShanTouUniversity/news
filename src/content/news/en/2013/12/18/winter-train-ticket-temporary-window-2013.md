It is reported that in coordination with Shantou Railway Station, the STU Student Affairs Office will operate a temporary train ticket sales and free pickup window on Dec. 22 and 23 for students returning home for the winter break.

The window will be open on Dec. 22 from 9:30 am to 12:30 pm and 1:30 pm to 4:30 pm, and on Dec. 23 from 9:30 am to 12:30 pm. Students who pre-registered on the university website should bring their ID cards and student IDs to queue for tickets in purchase order. Students who have already purchased tickets on the 12306 website may hand in their relevant documents at the window for pickup.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
