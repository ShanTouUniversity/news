Shantou University’s labor union planned to convene its Women’s Work Committee, department-level women’s committee members and women chairs at 10:30 a.m. on Thursday, December 19, 2013, in the meeting room on the second floor of the university labor union. The union announced the arrangement on December 18 and asked participants to attend on time.
