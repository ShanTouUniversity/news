Shantou University’s Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office announced arrangements on December 18, 2013, for dormitories with outstanding utility bills. At the time, the university planned to suspend electricity beginning December 19 in dormitories that had not paid bills for November 2013 or earlier, restoring service after payment. No individual dormitory or student is identified in this report.

| Payment debit schedule | Restoration window after successful payment |
| --- | --- |
| First debit at 10:30, December 19–20 | 2:30–4:30 p.m. the same day |
| Second debit at 4:30 p.m., December 19–20 | 5:30–8 p.m. the same day |
| Payment after 4:30 p.m. | 2:30–4:30 p.m. the following day |
| From December 23 onward | One debit daily at 4:30 p.m. |

The notice also advised residents with insufficient balances to top up and described proxy payment through the campus utility management system. The detailed procedure was set out in the university’s notice at the time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office)
