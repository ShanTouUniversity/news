It is reported that the STU Guitar Enthusiasts Association will host the "Under the Tree" Christmas Unplugged Concert at 7 pm on Sunday, Dec. 22, at the stairway between the gym and the track field (next to the gym's glass wall).

The event features acoustic performances without electrical amplification, aiming to convey holiday blessings and create a warm, romantic Christmas atmosphere. All faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
