It is reported that the STU Student Affairs Office announced the 2014 winter break train ticket purchase ranking results on the afternoon of Dec. 18. Since the pre-registration notice was issued on Dec. 9, a total of 298 students registered, with 274 successfully completing the process.

Under the witness of the Student Union Rights Protection Department, the Student Self-Management Committee, and student representatives, the purchase ranking was determined through a random sorting procedure. Students listed in the attachment are requested to purchase tickets on Dec. 19 during the designated time slots (8:30-11:30 am and 2:30-5:30 pm).
