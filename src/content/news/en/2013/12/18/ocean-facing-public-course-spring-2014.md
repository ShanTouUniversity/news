It is reported that the College of Sciences at Shantou University will offer the public course "Toward the Ocean" in the spring semester, enrolling 80 undergraduate students from across campus divided into two sections.

Lectures and discussions will be held on Friday afternoons, periods 6-7 or 8-9, during weeks 5 to 14 of the spring semester. Practical sessions will be scheduled on weekends, with specific times and venues to be announced. Service locations include Shantou urban area and surrounding regions.

Students interested in enrolling should submit a registration form for pre-enrollment. The final admitted list will be published on the campus information office website.
