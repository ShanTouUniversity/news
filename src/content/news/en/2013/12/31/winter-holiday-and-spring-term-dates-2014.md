Shantou University’s General Office announced the 2014 winter break and spring-semester schedule on December 31, 2013. Students were scheduled to be on break from January 19 through February 22, register on February 23 and begin classes on February 24. Faculty were to begin their break on January 22 and return on February 20 to prepare for the semester.

Students wishing to remain in campus housing during the break were required to apply in advance through their residential college counselor’s office for coordinated arrangements. Administrative, academic-support and logistics units were to continue providing necessary services, with staff rotations permitted according to operational needs from January 22 to February 19. Adjusted working hours during that period were 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 3:00–5:00 p.m.; regular hours resumed on February 20. Relevant departments and selected teaching buildings were to maintain duty coverage during the Spring Festival holiday, January 31–February 6.

| Group | Winter break and return schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Students | Break: January 19–February 22; registration: February 23; classes: February 24 |
| Faculty | Break begins January 22; return February 20 for preparation; classes begin February 24 |
| Administrative, academic-support and logistics staff | Rotations permitted January 22–February 19; regular work resumes February 20 |
