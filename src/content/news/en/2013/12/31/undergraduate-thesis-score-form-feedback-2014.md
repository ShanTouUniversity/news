On December 31, 2013, Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office opened a consultation on revisions to the 2014 undergraduate thesis and design assessment form. The form had been used since 2013, and the Office planned to adopt an improved version for the new year, inviting units and faculty to comment on their experience with the existing form.

## Consultation Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | Revisions to the 2014 undergraduate thesis and design assessment form |
| Participants | Schools, departments, and faculty |
| Deadline | January 15, 2014 |
| Contact | Chen Yangzhou |
| Details | 86503202; yzchen@stu.edu.cn |

Comments were to be submitted by email. The 2013 assessment-form attachment is not reproduced.
