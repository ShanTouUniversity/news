The eighth 2013 issue of Zhicheng Life (No. 45 overall), published by Shantou University’s Zhicheng College, reviewed the college’s sports, talks, community programs and student-organization activities in December.

Zhicheng athletes placed fourth overall at Shantou University’s 27th track and field meet and finished second in the women’s 4×100-meter and 4×400-meter relays. The college also held a career-planning talk, a dean’s discussion and luncheon, a TEDx Shantou University event and a Christmas gathering, while continuing group discussions with first-year residents.

The college’s 2013–2014 residential student activity fund selected 15 projects: one fully funded project, 12 partially funded projects and two projects funded independently. The issue also covered the service-learning association’s midyear review, the student union’s semester summary and visiting delegations. Student names and individual mental-health cases mentioned in the source publication are not reproduced.
