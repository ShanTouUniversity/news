It is reported that the Academic Degree Evaluation Committee of Shantou University issued Document No. Shan University Degree [2013] 08 on December 31, 2013. The 8th Committee reviewed the master's degree applications of 4 academic-degree postgraduates including Xie XH and 32 professional-degree postgraduates including Lin C via correspondence review, and decided to confer master's degrees on 36 students in accordance with the *Regulations on Academic Degrees of the People's Republic of China*, the *Regulations on Degree Work of Shantou University* and relevant detailed rules for master's degrees.

## Conferment Details

| Degree Category | Number | Recipients (anonymized) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Master of Education | 1 | Xie XH |
| Master of Management | 2 | Ran Y, Li LP |
| Master of Medicine | 1 | Ji CN |
| Master of Public Administration (Professional) | 12 | Lin C, Wang CY, Zeng RL, Lin X, Zhang M, Tang BJ, Li ZB, Bao M*, Yang L, Wu WH, Chen J, Yao SY |
| Master of Business Administration (Professional) | 18 | Chen J, Zhang JP, She SL, Lu JL, Liu DC, Wu QS, Chen WL, Cai XH, Luo JL, Jiang WJ, Chen WH, Cai QZ, Yao Y, Li SZ, Li J, Zhang K, Gao T, Liu SW |
| Master of Clinical Medicine (Professional) | 1 | Zou YH |
| Master of Public Health (Professional) | 1 | Ma WX |

*Note: The given name of one MPA recipient surnamed Bao is garbled in the original document and is rendered as Bao M.

The conferment covers 36 graduates across academic and professional degrees, with copies sent to relevant units.
