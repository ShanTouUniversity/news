Shantou University’s Network and Information Center announced its campus card service schedule for the New Year holiday on December 31, 2013. The service windows at the Expert Apartments and beneath Dining Hall No. 2 were scheduled to close on January 1, 2014, and resume service on January 2.

During the closure, students and staff could use self-service machines to add funds, check balances or report lost cards. The center listed 82903414 (campus extension 3414) for automated balance and lost-card services, 86503420 as its service hotline and support@stu.edu.cn as its service email. A personal mobile number included in the original notice is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
