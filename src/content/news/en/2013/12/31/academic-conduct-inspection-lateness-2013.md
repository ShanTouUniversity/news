Shantou University’s Office for the Three Campus Initiatives reported the results of classroom inspections in Teaching Building E during Week 15 of the fall 2013–2014 semester. Inspection teams visited on the morning of December 26 and the afternoon of December 27, recording six students who arrived more than five minutes late: five during the first inspection and one during the second.

The notice asked the relevant colleges and residential college to provide education on classroom conduct. The original attachment contains a list of students; this report gives only the aggregate count and does not identify individuals.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office for the Three Campus Initiatives)
> ⚠️ The student-identifying inspection list is not reproduced; the original attachment is available on the OA system.
