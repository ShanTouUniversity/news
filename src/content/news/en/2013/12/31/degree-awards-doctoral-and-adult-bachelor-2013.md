Shantou University’s Eighth Academic Degrees Committee announced two degree-awarding decisions on December 31, 2013. Following a review by correspondence, the committee approved doctoral degrees for five graduating graduate students—four in science and one in medicine—and bachelor’s degrees in management for 18 adult-education students from the fall 2013 semester.

The adult-education applicants had completed their program requirements, earned the required credits and met the university’s degree criteria. The students’ names in the original decisions are not reproduced to protect their privacy.
