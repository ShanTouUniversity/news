Shantou University’s College of Engineering announced the program for its 176th Engineering Research Forum on December 31, 2013. Professor Pung of the National University of Singapore was scheduled to speak on the afternoon of January 3, 2014, in Room 312 of the College’s South Engineering Building. The talk concerned a physical and software infrastructure for supporting context awareness.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Professor Pung, National University of Singapore (identified as Professor Feng in the Chinese notice) |
| Topic | A physical and software infrastructure for supporting context awareness |
| Time | 2:30 p.m., January 3, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 312, South Engineering Building |
| Audience | Engineering faculty and students, and other interested members of the university |

The notice provided the planned program but no post-event account.
