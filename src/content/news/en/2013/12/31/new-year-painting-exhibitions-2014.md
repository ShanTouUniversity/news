The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University scheduled two painting exhibitions to open simultaneously at 10 a.m. on January 1, 2014. Qiu Jinshi’s oil-painting exhibition “Impressions of Memory” was to be held at the school’s art gallery, while Ren Kaining’s solo exhibition “空⊙明” was scheduled for the first-floor exhibition hall. The school organized both exhibitions and invited the university community to attend.

| Exhibition | Venue | Opening |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qiu Jinshi’s oil-painting exhibition “Impressions of Memory” | School art gallery | 10 a.m., January 1, 2014 |
| Ren Kaining’s solo exhibition “空⊙明” | First-floor exhibition hall | 10 a.m., January 1, 2014 |
