Shantou University’s Sports Department announced its New Year opening schedule on December 31, 2013. In line with the university holiday, the gym, indoor badminton courts, indoor table-tennis courts and tennis courts were scheduled to close on January 1, 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
