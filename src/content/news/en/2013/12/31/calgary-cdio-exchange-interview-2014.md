Shantou University’s College of Engineering announced interview arrangements on December 31, 2013, for the 2014 CDIO exchange program with the University of Calgary in Canada. The university had opened campus selection on December 24; by December 27, it had received 60 applications, of which 57 applicants qualified for interviews.

Interviews were scheduled for 7 p.m. on January 2, 2014, in Room 306 of the College of Engineering’s South Building. Candidates were asked to check in at the academic exchange room, South 312, by 6:40 p.m. The interviews would use an unstructured group-discussion format, with topics drawn on site. Each candidate’s self-introduction was limited to one minute and group discussions to 10 minutes, followed by questions from the panel as needed.
