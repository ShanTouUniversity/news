It is reported that Shantou University's weekly leadership reception day is scheduled for Thursday, January 2, 2014, at 3:00 p.m., with President Xu Xiaohu on duty to meet visiting faculty and students.

According to the Party and Administration Office, the arrangements for this reception day are as follows:

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Leader on Duty | President Xu Xiaohu |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., Thursday, January 2, 2014 |
| Appointment Phone | 8650 2306 |

The Party and Administration Office reminded that faculty and students who wish to visit should make an appointment by phone in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administration Office)
