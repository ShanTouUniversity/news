Shantou University’s Human Resources Office notified relevant main-campus units on December 31, 2013, to collect certificates for the 2013 pre-service training program and teacher qualifications. Units were asked to send representatives to Room 315 of the Administrative Center by January 3, 2014.

The office would handle reimbursement of training fees for employees who passed the pre-service training. Reimbursements were expected to be deposited into recipients’ Bank of China accounts in mid-to-late January 2014. The OA attachment includes a list of individuals collecting certificates; this report does not reproduce personal identity information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
