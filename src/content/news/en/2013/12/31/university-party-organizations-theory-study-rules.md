The Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee approved rules governing theoretical study by the university’s central study group and grassroots Party organizations at its 16th meeting of 2013. The university issued the two documents on December 31, 2013, setting requirements for organization, planning and oversight at both levels.

The university-level central group, led by the Party Committee, was to meet for collective study in principle once a month and at least six times a year. Grassroots Party organizations could set their own schedules based on local needs but were also required to hold at least six focused study sessions annually. Both levels were to prepare annual plans, connect study with the university’s and their units’ work, and maintain attendance, study and discussion records and annual summaries.

Study formats could include individual reading, presentations, key-point speeches, group discussions, reading exchanges and site visits. Annual plans and summaries were to be filed with the Party Committee’s Publicity Department or the relevant higher-level authority as required.
