The Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association planned to suspend office duty and the approval of materials requests from January 1, 2014. The university Youth League Committee issued the arrangement on December 31, 2013, as the semester was ending and student organizations were winding down most activities.

The notice said urgent materials requests could still be directed to a designated contact. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the individual’s name or phone number; organizations can consult the original OA notice for that information.

The Student Societies Federation office also suspended duty and materials-request approvals from December 31. Both arrangements were made as the semester ended and student organizations wound down their activities. The source notices list student contacts for urgent requests; their personal details are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ Contact details for urgent materials requests are available in the original OA notice.
