At its 16th meeting of 2013, the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee considered the proposed appointment of Zhang Guojun as director of the university’s Medical College Affiliated Tumor Hospital. The university’s Organization and United Front Work Department issued a pre-appointment notice on December 24 and invited comments from campus units and individuals.

Comments were due by December 30, 2013, and could be submitted in writing, in person or by phone. The department listed 86502345 and o_zzb@stu.edu.cn as contact details. The candidate’s basic profile was provided in the original notice attachment.
