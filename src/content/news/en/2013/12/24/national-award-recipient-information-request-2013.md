Shantou University’s Party and University Administration Office asked university units on December 24, 2013, to report individuals and organizations that received national-level honors during the year. Submissions were to include the award title and awarding body and be sent to the office by email by December 27, 2013.

The information was requested ahead of a Shantou New Year gathering for representatives from different sectors, tentatively scheduled for mid-to-late January 2014, at which representatives of the honorees would be invited. The office listed jyk@stu.edu.cn and 86503186 for contact.
