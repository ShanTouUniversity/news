Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department asked Party committees and directly affiliated branches on December 24, 2013, to submit summaries of the Party’s mass line education and practice campaign. As the university entered the rectification and institution-building phase, units were asked to review the work carried out, lessons learned and results achieved, while identifying shortcomings.

The deadline was the morning of December 25, 2013. Hard copies were to be delivered to Room 315 of the Administration Center, with electronic copies sent to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. The notice listed Jirongxin as the contact and 86502345 as the phone number.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
