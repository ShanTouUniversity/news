Shantou University issued its *Measures for Faculty Recruitment* on December 24, 2013. The policy had been discussed at the university’s 16th leadership meeting on December 10 and took effect upon publication.

The new measures replaced the talent-recruitment provisions in the university’s 1997 interim staff-allocation rules and repealed seven earlier documents:

| Earlier document | Reference number |
| --- | --- |
| Notice amending provisions of the interim staff-allocation rules | 汕大发〔1999〕170号 |
| Notice requiring English tests for transferred teaching staff | 汕大发〔2001〕168号 |
| Notice establishing the talent recruitment contribution award | 汕大发〔2003〕75号 |
| Interim rules for recruiting outstanding overseas PhD graduates | 汕大发〔2004〕26号 |
| Notice on standardizing recruitment procedures | 汕大发〔2005〕111号 |
| Decision on accountability for talent recruitment and appointments | 汕大发〔2006〕03号 |
| Interim rules for recruiting teaching and research staff | 汕大发〔2009〕96号 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
> ⚠️ The full faculty recruitment measures are available in the original OA notice attachments.
