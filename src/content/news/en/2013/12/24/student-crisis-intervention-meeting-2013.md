An arrangement issued on December 24, 2013, by the office of Shantou University’s Student Crisis Intervention Working Group scheduled its annual working meeting for 3 p.m. on December 27 in Room 330 of the New Administration Center. Planned attendees included heads of relevant units, such as Student Affairs, the Youth League Committee, the Party and University Administration Office, the Publicity Department, the Resource Management Office, colleges and residential colleges, the student-residence counselor office, the Center for Mental Health Education and Counseling, campus security and the university hospital.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
