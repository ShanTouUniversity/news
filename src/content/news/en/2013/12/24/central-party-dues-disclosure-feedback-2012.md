Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department circulated a notice on December 24, 2013, concerning the disclosure of income and expenditure for centrally managed Chinese Communist Party membership dues in 2012. The notice was sent to the university’s campus-level Party committees and directly affiliated Party branches, which were asked to inform members and collect their comments.

The organizations were asked to submit members’ feedback to the department by December 26, 2013. Details of the disclosure are in the original notice materials.
