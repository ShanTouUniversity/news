Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has announced arrangements for public housing selection for households relocated from the west campus zone. The selection will begin at 3 p.m. on December 28 in Room 101, Building E, and will be conducted in two rounds.

## Selection schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date and time | December 28, starting at 3 p.m. |
| First round | 3–4:30 p.m. |
| Second round | 4:30–6 p.m. |
| Venue | Room 101, Building E |

If the first round runs over time, the start of the second round will be delayed accordingly. Relocation households unable to attend because of travel or other reasons may authorize another person in writing to complete the selection.

Households that have not submitted an application for resettlement housing but require housing should submit a supplementary application by December 26. Applications received later will be handled based on the housing remaining after the public selection. Applicants who submitted an application but are not listed in the selection order should also raise the issue and provide supplementary information by December 26. Selection will be conducted by household; where two employed members of one household submitted separate applications, the member with the higher professional or administrative rank will participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
> ⚠️ Housing numbers, areas, rent standards and the selection order are available in the attachments on the OA system.
