The Shantou University Committee of the Communist Party of China issued revised *Rules on Deliberation and Decision-Making by the University Party Committee Standing Committee* on December 24, 2013. The revision was discussed at the committee’s 15th meeting of the year; the previous rules were issued in 2006 as document 汕大党字〔2006〕第47号.

University units were asked to organize study of the revised rules. Details of the changes were provided in the original notice attachment.
