It is reported that the Network and Information Center issued a notice on December 24, 2013, regarding a fault affecting calls between mobile short numbers and fixed-line short numbers.

Due to a connection fault between the carrier’s fixed-line network and the mobile short-number network, calls from mobile to fixed-line short numbers prompt an empty-number tone, while fixed-line to mobile short numbers indicate the group network service is not enabled. Forwarding fixed-line calls to mobile short numbers is also affected and should be cancelled temporarily. The carrier is urgently investigating, with restoration time pending.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Symptom | Mobile to fixed-line short number shows empty number; fixed-line to mobile short number shows service not enabled |
| Scope | Interconnection between mobile and fixed-line short numbers and forwarding to mobile short numbers |
