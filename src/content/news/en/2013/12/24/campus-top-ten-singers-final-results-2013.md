The final of Shantou University’s 21st “M-Zone” Campus Top Ten Singers competition took place on December 21, 2013. The university Youth League Committee announced the results on December 24, with Zeng W, Xu L and Cai SZ placing first, second and third, respectively.

The competition also presented awards for best costume, best stage presence and popularity, and named 10 performers to the “2013 Campus Top Ten Singers.” Student names are abbreviated to protect their privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee)
