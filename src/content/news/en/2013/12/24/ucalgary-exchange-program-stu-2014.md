It is reported that the exchange program between the School of Engineering at Shantou University and the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Calgary will officially launch in May 2014.

Under the agreement, the University of Calgary Faculty of Engineering will send 20 students to Shantou University for a one-month course of study, while STU will select an equal number of students to attend classes alongside them at the University of Calgary. The courses will be taught by professors appointed by the University of Calgary Faculty of Engineering, with English as the language of instruction. Students who complete the program will receive 4 credits.

## Program Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Launch Date | May 2014 |
| Duration | One month |
| Participants | 20 students from each institution |
| Instruction Language | English |
| Credits | 4 credits |
| Hosts | STU School of Engineering, University of Calgary Faculty of Engineering |

This program offers valuable international exchange opportunities for students of both universities. Interested students are encouraged to watch for further registration announcements.
