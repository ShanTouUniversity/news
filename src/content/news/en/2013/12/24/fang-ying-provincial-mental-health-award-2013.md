It is reported that in the 2012-2013 Outstanding Individual in Guangdong University Mental Health Education and Counseling评选, Fang Ying from the Mental Health Counseling Center of the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University was awarded the title of "Outstanding Individual in Guangdong University Mental Health Education and Counseling" after review by the Guangdong Higher Education Mental Health Education and Counseling Professional Committee.

The award, selected by the Guangdong Higher Education Mental Health Education and Counseling Professional Committee, aims to recognize individuals who have made outstanding contributions to university mental health education and counseling work.

Students are encouraged to visit the Mental Health Counseling Center for support and assistance.
