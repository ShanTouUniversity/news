In a notice issued on December 24, 2013, Shantou University’s Arts Education Center invited students across the university to audition for three instrumental electives in the spring 2014 semester: Trumpet Performance, French Horn Performance and Saxophone Performance.

Audition times, locations and requirements were provided in the original notice attachments. Interested students were directed to consult the Arts Education Center for details.
