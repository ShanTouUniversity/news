It is reported that the "Moyun Shuxin" (Ink Rhythm, Heartfelt Calligraphy) Calligraphy Joint Exhibition will be held from December 26 to 31 at the Art Museum of the School of Arts and Design, Shantou University. The exhibition is jointly organized by the School of Arts and Design of STU, Shantou Calligraphers Association, Dongguan Calligraphers Association, Nancheng District Federation of Literary and Art Circles of Dongguan, and Dongguan Yizhan Painting and Calligraphy Academy, bringing together outstanding works from calligraphy enthusiasts across the region.

## Exhibition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exhibition | "Moyun Shuxin" Calligraphy Joint Exhibition |
| Opening Ceremony | 4:00 PM, December 26 |
| Exhibition Period | December 26–31, 2013 |
| Venue | Art Museum, School of Arts and Design |
| Organizers | School of Arts and Design (STU), Shantou Calligraphers Association, Dongguan Calligraphers Association, etc. |

The exhibition will feature works from multiple calligraphers. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
