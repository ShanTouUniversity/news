Shantou University’s Resource Management Office rescheduled a water shutdown for pipe repairs from December 24 to December 26, 2013, from 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. The change was made to avoid interrupting service on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day and followed suggestions from foreign faculty members.

The affected area included the A and B staff-housing blocks, the kindergarten, Building 789, campus hospital, Arts Education Center, Foundation office, construction building, D-shaped building, affiliated secondary and primary schools, and Network Center. The Chinese notice listed A1-A14, while its English version listed A1-A12. Residents and units in the affected area were asked to plan for the revised schedule.
