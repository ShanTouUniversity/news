Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced its second-half 2013 textbook development and publication subsidy projects on December 20. Following an application by the textbook’s editor, a college recommendation and expert review, *Major Theorists of Modern and Contemporary Western Culture* (《现当代西方文化名家》) was approved for support.

The project details identify Peking University Press as the publisher and list RMB 20,000 in combined textbook-development and publication subsidies.
