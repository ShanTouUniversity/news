Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office opened the fall 2013 classroom teaching evaluation on December 20, 2013. Students were asked to use the university’s credit-system portal to evaluate their courses and instructors by January 3, 2014.

The office called for careful, objective and fair evaluations. Students who had not submitted an evaluation by the deadline were required to provide a written explanation; otherwise, they would be unable to view their course grades for the semester. Evaluations for public-service courses were to be announced separately.
