It is reported that the "Basics of Cetaceans" lecture, originally scheduled for December 21 from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM in E-Lecture Hall 101, hosted by the School of Science, has been rescheduled due to a change in arrangements. The lecture will now take place on December 21 from 3:30 PM to 5:30 PM in Room E204.

## Rescheduled Details

| Item | Original | Updated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Date | December 21 (Saturday) | December 21 (Saturday) |
| Time | 19:00–21:00 | 15:30–17:30 |
| Venue | E-Lecture Hall 101 | Room E204 |

## Speaker Profile

Professor Liu Wenhua is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the Marine Biology Institute, Shantou University. His main research areas include marine mammal conservation biology and ecotoxicology.

All faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend.
