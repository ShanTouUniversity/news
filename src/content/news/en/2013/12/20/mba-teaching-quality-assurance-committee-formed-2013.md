Shantou University’s Business School established an MBA Teaching Quality Assurance Committee to support the MBA program and safeguard its training quality. The school issued the document on December 20, 2013. Xu Erming was named adviser and Huang Yan chair; Liu Wei, Li Jinming, Qiu Guanyao and Xie Shunlong were members, with Lin Huaye as secretary.

The committee was formed under university rules and in response to the needs of the Business School and its MBA teaching program. Document No. 19 [2013] of the Business School was sent to the Shantou University MBA Education Committee.
