It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on December 20, 2013, announcing the recipients of the 2012-2013 National Scholarship, National Inspirational Scholarship, and the 2013 National Student Grant.

According to the Ministry of Education's announcement on 2012-2013 National Scholarship recipients (Jiao Cai [2013] No. 8) and following evaluation by the Ministry's National Scholarship Review Committee, 19 STU students were awarded the 2012-2013 National Scholarship. Separately, 291 students received the 2012-2013 National Inspirational Scholarship after expert review organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. In addition, 1,300 students were selected for the 2013 National Student Grant following nominations by colleges and residential colleges, university review and public display, and approval by the provincial education department.

## Awards at a Glance

| Program | Recipients | Amount | Notes |
|---------|-----------|--------|-------|
| National Scholarship | 19 | 8,000 yuan each | Certificate of honor awarded |
| National Inspirational Scholarship | 291 | 5,000 yuan each | — |
| National Student Grant | 1,300 | 3,000 yuan each | Disbursed over two semesters |

The selection covered all colleges and residential colleges, with a combined total of 1,610 awards and grants. The detailed lists were released as attachments to the original notice.
