Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced on December 20, 2013, that six sections of the courses “Career Guidance” and “Career Counseling and Training” were scheduled to visit Guangdong Chao Hong Ji Industrial Co., Ltd. on December 26. The visit was to introduce the company’s culture and include a tour of its museum.

The participating sections were 63539, 63537 and 63536 of “Career Guidance,” and 63541, 63545 and 63540 of “Career Counseling and Training.” Students were instructed to gather at the university motto monument at 2:25 p.m. before traveling to the company.
