It is reported that the Student Entrepreneurship Center of Shantou University will host an entrepreneurship experience sharing session themed "Discussing Self-Employment, Promoting Mutual Development" on December 27 from 15:00 to 17:00 in Teaching Building Room E203. The event aims to create a favorable entrepreneurial atmosphere on campus and enhance students' entrepreneurial capabilities.

Three experienced entrepreneurs have been invited to share their insights.

## Guest Speakers

| Name | Profile |
| --- | --- |
| Wei Guangchao | Head, Shantou Chaoyun Network Technology Co., Ltd. |
| Kong Deshan | Head, Shantou Toutda Youth Craft & Gift Co., Ltd. |
| Huang Haiyang | Head, Shantou Baizhuo Culture Communication Co., Ltd. |

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

Students interested in entrepreneurship are welcome to attend.
