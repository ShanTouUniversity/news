Shantou University’s Network and Information Center and Office of Asset Management asked units on December 20, 2013, to verify electronic bills for November office landline service from China Telecom and China Mobile. The bills had been entered into the university’s telephone management system.

Reviewers were asked to check the bills in the system and report discrepancies by December 25. Issues concerning China Telecom lines could be sent to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn`, while those concerning China Mobile lines could be sent to `stunic@stu.edu.cn`. Bills neither disputed nor confirmed by the deadline would be treated as confirmed by the system. The Finance Office planned to deduct the charges from the units’ designated funding accounts on December 27.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center; Office of Asset Management)
