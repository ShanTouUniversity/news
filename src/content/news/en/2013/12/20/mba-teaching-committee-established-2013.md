It is reported that the Business School issued Document No. 18 [2013] on December 20, 2013, establishing an MBA Teaching Committee to advance MBA program development.

## Committee Members

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Lyu Yuan |
| Members (by stroke order) | A faculty member surnamed Wang (name garbled in the original), Ji Yun, Liu Dawei, Lin Jiali, Ouyang Feng, Yao Ming’an, Huang Wangming, Qin Zhong, Zeng Qiang |
| Secretaries | Yin Xiaoqing, Zhang Xiaoxiao |

The committee was established following deliberation by the college leadership.
