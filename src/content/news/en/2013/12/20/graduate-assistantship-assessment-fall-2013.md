Shantou University’s Graduate School announced the fall 2013 assessment schedule for graduate “three-assistant” posts on December 20. The university had established 142 posts that semester: 89 teaching-assistant posts and 53 administrative-assistant posts.

Instructors with teaching assistants were responsible for evaluating them. Units with administrative assistants were to assess the graduate students in those posts and, where required, provide proof that the stipends had been paid. Consolidated materials were due to the Graduate School in Room 401 of the Administration Center by the end of the workday on December 27. Units that did not complete assessments, or could not document stipend payments for administrative assistants, would not have their applications for spring 2014 posts accepted.
