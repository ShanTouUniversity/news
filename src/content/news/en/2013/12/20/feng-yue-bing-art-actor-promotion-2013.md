It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University issued a public notice on December 20, announcing that following review by the Shantou Art Professional Qualifications Committee, Feng Yuebing from the School of Arts Education has been approved for promotion to Level III Art Actor (艺术专业三级演员).

## Notice Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate | Feng Yuebing (School of Arts Education) |
| Proposed Title | Level III Art Actor |
| Reviewing Body | Shantou Art Professional Qualifications Committee |
| Notice Start Date | December 20, 2013 |
| Notice End Date | December 26, 2013 |

Any concerns regarding the above notice may be submitted to the University Human Resources Office during the notice period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
