It is reported that Jiangmen Daily, the leading municipal party newspaper in Guangdong province and located in Jiangmen—the "Number One Hometown of Chinese Overseas," is recruiting interns from Shantou University to strengthen its talent pipeline. Jiangmen Daily is known for its strong reform awareness and progressive editorial philosophy.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Jiangmen Daily Society |
| Target Candidates | Graduate students and undergraduate graduating seniors |
| Preferred Majors | Journalism, Public Communication, Creative Media Design, Chinese |
| Other Eligible Candidates | Students from Law, Management, Economics, Computer Science with strong writing skills and interest in news/media |
| Internship Support | Guidance provided; accommodation and living conditions arranged |

The society has committed to providing comprehensive internship guidance and adequate living arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Journalism and Communication)
