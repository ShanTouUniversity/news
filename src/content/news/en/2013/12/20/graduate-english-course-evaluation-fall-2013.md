Shantou University’s Graduate School announced on December 20, 2013, that the course evaluation system for Graduate English in the first semester of academic year 2013–2014 was open. Graduate students enrolled in the course were asked at the time to submit fair and considered evaluations of their instructors by December 27.

Students with questions could contact the graduate academic affairs coordinator in their college or the Graduate School.
