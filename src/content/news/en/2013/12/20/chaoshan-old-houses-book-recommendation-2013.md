Shantou University Library’s third installment of its “New Books on Chaoshan Literature” series, published on December 20, 2013, featured *Old Houses of Chaoshan* by Lin Kailong. Using text and photographs, the book documents traditional buildings in the region’s villages and older urban districts. It also examines the origins and evolution of Chaoshan architecture, the layout of historic residences, decorative techniques, and related history and folk culture.

Lin is an associate professor and graduate adviser at Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. The book was jointly planned by the university’s Center for the Study of Christianity and Sanlian Bookstore as part of the “Chaoshan Society and Religion Studies” series.
