It is reported that the Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteers Association of the Business School issued a notice on December 4, 2013, to hold the “Orange Apple Peace Night” gift exchange.

Christmas Eve offers an opportunity to convey greetings and warmth. Continuing a fine tradition, the association will provide a platform for students and teachers to exchange gifts and express blessings, with all proceeds donated to impoverished students in Wuqiao to showcase university students’ dedication.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Slogan | Small gift, great warmth. You and I meet on Peace Night |
| Orders and booth | December 5–7 at Jin San Jiao, the second canteen and the fourth canteen |
| Gift delivery | December 23–24 by association volunteers to designated recipients |
| How to participate | Choose gifts for teachers or friends and fill in recipient information |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteers Association, Business School |

The event looks forward to active participation.
