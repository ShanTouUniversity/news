It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced on December 4 the semi-final results of the 21st Campus Top Ten Singers Competition. The semi-final was held on the evening of November 30 at the university auditorium, where ten singers advanced to the final round after intense competition.

An additional "Best Medley Collaboration Award" was presented to Group 5, comprising Chen Jie, Huang Zhipeng, Chunzi, Lai Ruiqi, and Meng Liang. Details of the final will be announced separately.
