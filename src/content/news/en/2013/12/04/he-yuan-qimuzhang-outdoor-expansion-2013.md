It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University will hold a mid-level outdoor expansion activity at Qimuzhang Nature Reserve in Heyuan from the afternoon of December 20 to December 22, open to all faculty and students. The activity is undertaken by the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association.

## Background

Qimuzhang Mountain Range spans Wuhua County in Meizhou City and Zijin County and Longchuan County in Heyuan City. Its main peak reaches an altitude of 1,318.6 meters and offers views of seven surrounding counties, hence the name "Qimuzhang" (Seven Peaks). The Qimuzhang Nature Reserve, known as a "treasure house of species," is the largest nature reserve in eastern Guangdong, featuring dramatic rock formations, winding valleys, and numerous streams that provide ideal habitats for diverse flora and fauna.

## Activity Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | December 20-22, 2013 |
| Location | Qimuzhang Nature Reserve, Heyuan |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |
| Co-organizer | Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |
| Participants | All faculty and students |

The activity includes trekking, wild camping, team-building exercises, and survival skills training, aiming to cultivate interest in outdoor sports, teach survival skills, enhance safety awareness, teamwork ability, and foster a positive and courageous attitude toward life. A physical fitness screening will be conducted first to ensure participant safety.
