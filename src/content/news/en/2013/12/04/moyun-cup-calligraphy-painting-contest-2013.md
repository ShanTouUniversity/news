It is reported that the Shantou University Youth Communist League Committee has launched the "Moyun Cup" Calligraphy and Painting Contest, themed "Ink Expresses Emotions, Harmonious Campus." The contest aims to inherit and promote excellent traditional Chinese culture while balancing academic rigor with accessibility and innovation.

The competition is divided into two categories: calligraphy and painting. The calligraphy category includes both brush and hard pen (pencil/pen) submissions, while the painting category encompasses Chinese painting, oil painting, pastel painting, printmaking, sketch, and speed drawing. For the preliminary round, participants are required to create works based on content from Chinese classics or quotations from renowned figures at home and abroad.

Winning entries, specially invited works, and pieces collected from universities across the ChaoShan region will be exhibited together following the contest, offering the campus community a showcase of traditional artistic excellence.
