Shantou University issued Document No. 142 (2013) on December 4, 2013, recognizing outstanding undergraduates for the 2012–2013 academic year. Awards were determined under the university’s undergraduate scholarship rules. To protect student privacy, recipient names are not reproduced.

| Award | Number of recipients |
|------|------:|
| Outstanding Student Scholarship | 20 |
| First-, second- and third-class Academic Excellence Scholarships | 169, 430 and 616 |
| Awards for outstanding research and technology, leadership, social service, and arts and sports contributions | 10 each |
| Active Participation in Academic and Technology Activities Award | 45 |
| Active Participation in Campus Culture Award | 147 |
| Active Participation in Social Service Award | 131 |
| Active Participation in Campus Service Award | 136 |

Under the university’s rules, recipients of the National Scholarship or National Encouragement Scholarship were not also eligible for the Outstanding Student Scholarship. The university encouraged award recipients to continue participating in research, community service and campus cultural activities.
