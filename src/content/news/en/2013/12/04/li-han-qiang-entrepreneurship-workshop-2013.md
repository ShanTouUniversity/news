It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on December 4 that Dr. Lee Han-Keung of the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology will visit campus on December 6 to host an entrepreneurship workshop themed "Starting a High-Tech Company," open to all students.

The workshop aims to help students interested in entrepreneurship understand the key steps and practical paths of launching a high-tech startup, covering the critical process from idea to commercial implementation. Due to limited capacity, interested students must register by sending an email to zpchen@stu.edu.cn by 17:00 on December 5, including their name, major, year of study, and contact information.
