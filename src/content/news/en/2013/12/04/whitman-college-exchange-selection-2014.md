It is reported that the Office of International Affairs of Shantou University announced on December 4, 2013, the selection results for the 2014 Whitman College (US) exchange program.

A review panel composed of representatives from the English Language Center, the Academic Affairs Office, the Student Affairs Office, Whitman College and the Office of International Affairs conducted an initial review of application materials, shortlisted 8 of the 15 applicants for interviews, and carried out comprehensive evaluations.

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | 2014 Whitman College Exchange (fall 2014, half-year in the US) |
| Applicants | 15 |
| Interviewed | 8 |
| Evaluation criteria | English proficiency, analytical skills, leadership, personality and specialties |
| Review time | Afternoon of December 4, 2013 |
| Recommended candidate | Zhang YD, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication (anonymized) |

Following the interviews and evaluations, the panel recommended the above student as the exchange participant for fall 2014.
