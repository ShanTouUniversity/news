It is reported that the Publicity Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee announced on December 4 the launch of the "Build the Chinese Dream" online learning and exchange campaign, calling on all party general branches and directly affiliated branches to widely promote participation as part of the party's mass-line education practice activity.

The campaign, organized by the Organization Department of the CPC Central Committee, features a dedicated webpage on the Communist Party Member Network (http://zgm.12371.cn/special/), providing an online platform for study and interactive exchange aimed at helping faculty and students deepen their understanding of the Chinese Dream concept and practice.
