In commemoration of International Volunteer Day on December 5, the University Youth League Committee at Shantou University will host a "Campus Service Street" event on December 7, mobilizing volunteer associations from various colleges to offer diverse free services to faculty, staff, and students.

The event, coordinated by the University Youth Volunteer Work Department, features nine affiliated youth associations each showcasing their unique skills:

| Association | Service Offered |
| --- | --- |
| Literature Youth Association | Warm wishes and greetings |
| Medical Youth Association | CPR and first aid demonstrations |
| Journalism Youth Association | DSLR photography hands-on workshop |
| Business Youth Association | Silk scarf and tie styling tutorials |
| Science Youth Association | Limescale removal tips |
| Law Youth Association | Glasses maintenance and legal advice |
| Art Youth Association | DIY handcrafts |
| Engineering Youth Association | Free small appliance repair |
| Zhicheng Ivy League Volunteer Group | Charity wall and mutual aid activities |

The organizers hope that such practical, student-oriented service events will promote the volunteer spirit of "dedication, friendship, mutual aid, and progress," and showcase the responsibility and commitment of STU's youth.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee)
