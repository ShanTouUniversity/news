It is reported that the STU AIESEC team issued a notice on December 4, recruiting fall overseas volunteers for Asia-Africa projects campus-wide, with applications closing at 18:00 on December 7.

## About AIESEC

AIESEC is a global, non-political, independent and non-profit organization run by university students and recent graduates, with more than 80,000 members across over 2,400 universities in 124 countries and regions, headquartered in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Its Overseas Volunteer Program focuses on topics including cultural exchange, HIV prevention, community and rural development, environmental protection, language education, microfinance and women's rights, offering participants immersive local life and cultural experience alongside local AIESEC members.
