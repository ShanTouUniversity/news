It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on December 4 that Cheng Hongbo, Senior Human Resources Manager for Asia-Pacific at Xilinx Inc., will deliver a career planning lecture at the university on December 6, helping students clarify their career goals and enhance their employability.

Cheng earned his undergraduate degree from Nankai University and later obtained an MBA from Nanjing University. He is a Six Sigma advocate and Green Belt certified instructor, a professional career coach, and an MBTI certified facilitator. Prior to joining Xilinx, he served as Senior HR Manager for Asia-Pacific at Philips Professional Lighting. He has delivered career talks at numerous prestigious universities and brings rich experience in career development and talent cultivation.
