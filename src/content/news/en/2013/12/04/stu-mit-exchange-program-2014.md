It is reported that the School of Engineering at Shantou University will launch an exchange and cooperation program with the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) starting in March 2014, giving students a chance to experience teaching from one of the world's leading universities.

The program runs from March 10 to May 16, 2014, combining on-campus instruction with online learning. MIT professors will visit STU for two intensive weeks—March 10–14 and May 12–16—while the intervening period will be conducted remotely through the edX platform. Students who complete the course will earn 2 credits, receive a certificate of completion, and gain hands-on experience applying the knowledge in practical projects.
