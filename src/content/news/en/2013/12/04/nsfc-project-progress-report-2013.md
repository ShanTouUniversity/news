It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University announced on December 4 the launch of the 2013 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) project progress and completion report submission.

Two types of reports are required: project leaders of ongoing NSFC projects must prepare a Progress Report, while those whose projects are due for completion at the end of 2013 must prepare a Completion Report. All reports must be submitted online through the ISIS (Information System for Management of NSFC Projects) web platform.

## Report Categories

| Report Type | Applicable To |
| --- | --- |
| Progress Report | Project leaders of ongoing 2013 NSFC projects |
| Completion Report | Project leaders whose 2013 NSFC projects are due for completion |

## Submission Method

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| System | ISIS (Information System for Management of NSFC Projects) |
| How to submit | Log in to the ISIS system and complete the online form |

Project leaders are reminded to check their status and submit reports promptly.
