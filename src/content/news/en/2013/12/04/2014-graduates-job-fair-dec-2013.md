The STU 2014 Graduates Supply-Demand Job Fair will be held on December 7 at 9:00 AM, located at the south, north, and east sides of the Admin Building as well as the ground-floor arcade beneath the Business School building.

To date, 62 companies have confirmed their participation, including Country Garden, Shenzhen South China City Group, Shantou Super Sonic Display Co., Ltd., Wanxiang Jincheng Apparel, and Xinda Semiconductor. The event offers diverse employment opportunities for graduating students. All 2014 graduates are encouraged to attend and seize these career prospects.

To ensure smooth operations and campus traffic safety, the administration has arranged for temporary suspension of motorcycle, bicycle, and e-bike parking at the Admin Building arcade and beneath the Business School building on December 6–7.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office)
