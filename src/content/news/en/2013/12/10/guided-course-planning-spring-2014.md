Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office began arranging guided courses for the spring 2014 semester on December 10, 2013. Teaching units were asked to submit course information, create guided classes and publish topics in stages. Graduation theses and public-interest courses were covered by separate arrangements.

| Task | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Submit proposed course names and related information | December 16, 2013 (Monday of Week 14) |
| Create and publish guided classes in the credit-system platform | December 18, 2013 (Wednesday of Week 14) |
| Create and publish topics for each class | December 27, 2013 (Friday of Week 15) |
| Issue the guided-course registration notice | Week 16 |
| Students complete course selection | Before Week 18; the system would then close |

Units could enter provisional topic names when final titles were not yet available, but were expected to revise them before grades were entered. Course information was to be emailed to the Academic Affairs Office at `yzchen@stu.edu.cn`. The course-planning template was attached to the original notice.
