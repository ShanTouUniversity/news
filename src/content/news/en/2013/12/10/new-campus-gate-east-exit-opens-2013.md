It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University announced on December 10 that the east motor vehicle entrance at the new campus gate will officially open on December 13 (Friday).

Previously, the west entrance and main roadway had begun trial operations on November 11. After over a month of adjustment and improvement, supporting facilities at the east entrance—including road markings, speed bumps, and traffic signs—have been substantially completed, making it ready for official opening.

## Traffic Rules

| Entrance | Allowed Traffic |
| --- | --- |
| East motor vehicle lane | Motor vehicles only |
| West main road | Pedestrians and bicycles only; all motor vehicles prohibited |

Please note that the vehicle barrier gate at the east entrance is not yet in operation due to ongoing equipment调试. Faculty and students are reminded to pay attention to traffic safety and follow on-site management instructions.
