It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on December 10, 2013, to publicize the proposed recipients for the Shantou Tielin Charity Association’s Self-Strengthening Grant.

Established at Shantou University to support students from Anhui with financial difficulties, the grant embodies the spirit of benefiting from and giving back to society. Following student applications and evaluation by the charity association, 20 students are proposed to receive 2,500 yuan each. Names are anonymized to protect privacy.
