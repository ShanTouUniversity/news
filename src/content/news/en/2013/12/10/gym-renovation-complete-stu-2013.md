It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University announced on December 10 that the gymnasium fitness center renovation has been fully completed, with trial operation beginning immediately.

All fitness equipment has been replaced with brand-new machines. Access remains managed via campus card swiping. The trial operation schedule is as follows:

| Day | Opening Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Tuesday, Wednesday | 16:00–21:00 |
| Thursday, Friday | 18:00–21:00 |
| Saturday, Sunday | 15:00–18:00 |
| Monday | Closed for cleaning and maintenance |

Faculty and students are encouraged to make the most of the renovated facility and exercise safely.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
