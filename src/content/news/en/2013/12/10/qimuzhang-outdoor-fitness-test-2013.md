It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association issued a notice on December 10, 2013, to hold the fitness test for the Heyuan Qimuzhang intermediate outdoor training on the afternoon of December 11.

## Test Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Afternoon of December 11, 2013 (no make-up tests, on-site registration accepted) |
| Content | Team and individual events carry equal weight; total scores are ranked, with the top 40 advancing to the wilderness survival workshop selection on December 15 |
| Organizer | Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |
| Notes | Those with heart disease, asthma, hypertension, anemia or other conditions unsuitable for strenuous exercise must not participate; follow university regulations and volunteer guidance; pay attention to safety; wear appropriate sportswear; test proceeds regardless of weather |

Drinking water and glucose water will be provided. The association organizes the selection and reserves the right of interpretation. Registration details and contacts are in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy. Updates are posted on Weibo @Snow Leopard Outdoor Association.

> ⚠️ Test grouping and contacts are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Snow Leopard Outdoor Association)
