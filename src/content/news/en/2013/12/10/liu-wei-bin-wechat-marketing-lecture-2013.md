It is reported that Liu Weibin, a distinguished alumnus of the Business School at Shantou University and current CEO of Shenzhen Yongtu Times Network Technology Co., Ltd. and Vice President of the Shenzhen Internet Technology Application Association, will deliver a lecture titled "WeChat Marketing Leads the New Era of Marketing" on Thursday, December 12, at 7:00 PM in Lecture Hall E-203.

Mr. Liu authored the book "The Way of Network Marketing — How Enterprises Expand Markets Through the Internet" in 2006 and has deep expertise in online marketing. His lecture will explore how WeChat, as an emerging social platform, is transforming corporate marketing models and creating new opportunities, sharing practical insights on marketing innovation in the mobile internet era.

All faculty, students, and staff interested in network marketing and social media operations are welcome to attend.
