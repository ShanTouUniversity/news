Shantou University’s Resources Management Office announced on December 10, 2013, that the university planned to hold a fourth consultation with households affected by the West-Campus relocation the following day. University leaders and department representatives were expected to attend.

| --- | --- |
| Time | 4:00–5:30 p.m., December 11, 2013 |
| Venue | Third floor, University Union |
| Topic | Consultation with West-Campus relocation households |
