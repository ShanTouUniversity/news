Shantou University’s General Administration Office set out the university’s annual reporting process on December 10, 2013, asking all units, campus Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to review their 2013 work and prepare plans for 2014.

The reviews were to cover key activities and results, lessons learned, problems and possible improvements. The plans were to reflect each unit’s development goals and the university’s comprehensive reform agenda. Submissions were due to the Secretariat Section of the General Administration Office by close of business on December 27.
