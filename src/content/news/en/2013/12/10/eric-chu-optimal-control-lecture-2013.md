It is reported that Dr. Eric King Wah Chu from Monash University, Australia, will deliver an academic report titled "Optimal control, via Riccati equations, by doubling, Newton's and Smith methods" on the afternoon of December 12, from 3:00 to 4:00 PM, in Room D-207, Shantou University.

The report will focus on optimal control theory, introducing three numerical solution methods based on Riccati equations: the doubling method, Newton's method, and Smith's method, exploring the mathematical principles and computational characteristics of each approach.

Faculty and students in mathematics, control theory, and related fields are welcome to attend.
