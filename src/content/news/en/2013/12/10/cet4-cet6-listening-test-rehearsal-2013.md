To help candidates check reception before the College English Test, Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office scheduled a listening-signal test for the afternoon of December 11, 2013. Broadcasts were to be available in the E, G and D teaching buildings, and candidates could bring their own earphones to test them at their examination locations. The receiving frequency was FM 73.2.

The test was scheduled from 3 to 5 p.m., ahead of the 2013 second-half CET-4 and CET-6 examinations.
