It is reported that the Library of Shantou University announced on December 10 an introduction to the Passport database, including its usage and resource features.

Passport is the flagship product independently researched by Euromonitor International, offering global market intelligence solutions covering over 200 countries. The database content is organized across three levels—from macro-level country and regional information, to meso-level industry and sector data, down to micro-level corporate information—providing comprehensive and detailed data.

The database helps readers gain a thorough understanding of commercial environments and trends across different countries, making it suitable for market research, business analysis, academic research, and other purposes.

## Database Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Database Name | Passport |
| Publisher | Euromonitor International |
| Coverage | Over 200 countries |
| Content Levels | Macro (countries/regions) → Meso (industries/sectors) → Micro (enterprises) |
| Applications | Market research, business analysis, academic research |
| Issuing Office | Library |

Faculty and students with interest are encouraged to visit the Library for more details on access and usage.
