It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Zhicheng College and the Ivy Service-Learning Association will host TEDx ShantouUniversity on December 14, 2013.

TEDx ShantouUniversity is an officially licensed TED event that brings together speakers from diverse fields to share experiences and ideas.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Bang |
| Time | 19:30, December 14, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee of Zhicheng College, Ivy Service-Learning Association |

## Speakers

| Speaker | Profile |
| --- | --- |
| Cheng Chen | Maker evangelist, initiator of the C2D2 maker-themed sharing sessions at Beijing Makerspace, now at DFROBOT |
| Zhang Jie | MD, psychiatrist, long engaged in clinical and research work in psychiatry; former researcher in developmental neurobiology of child psychology at Yale University |
| Yang N | Senior-three student at Danyang Middle School, founder of the first gender equality group in Chaoshan |
| Zheng Muqiang | Associate Professor, Business School, Shantou University, seventh cohort of the Guangdong “Thousand-Hundred-Ten” Project; research on enterprise clusters and international trade |
| Hao Nan | Associate Professor, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design; MFA in Visual Arts |
| Zhang Fan | Principal of Beijing Midi School of Music and founder of the Midi Music Festival |

TED (Technology, Entertainment, Design) is a U.S. nonprofit known for its conferences; TEDx denotes independently organized local TED-like events.
