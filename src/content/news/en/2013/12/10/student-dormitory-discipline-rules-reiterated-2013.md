Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office reiterated dormitory rules on December 10, 2013, reminding students to observe fire-safety, electrical-use and quiet-hour requirements. Barbecuing in residential areas and posting advertisements that disturb others were prohibited.

The notice cited Article 14 of the university’s interim student disciplinary regulations. Repeated disruption of order in dormitories, classrooms or public areas, or noise and entertainment during quiet hours after prior instruction, could result in a warning or more serious penalty, depending on the circumstances. Keeping pets after corrective instruction could also lead to a warning or higher penalty. Hosting a person of the opposite sex overnight could result in a demerit or higher penalty. Repeated fire- or electricity-safety violations could result in a warning or higher penalty, with more serious consequences for conduct causing serious harm. Unauthorized commercial stalls or repeated commercial postings could also result in at least a warning.

The office additionally urged students to take extra care with fire and electrical safety during the dry winter season.
