It is reported that the 11th STU English Festival will be held at Shantou University from December 13 to 15, under the theme "My Life in Our World."

Hosted by the English Language Centre, the festival aims to enhance students' English communication skills and broaden their international perspective. Representatives from Seoul National University (South Korea), Sun Yat-sen University, the University of Macau, and other domestic and international institutions will participate in exchange activities, showcasing cross-linguistic and cross-cultural youth spirit through speeches, debates, and cultural interactions.

All faculty, students, and staff are encouraged to join the events.
