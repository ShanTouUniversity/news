It is reported that on December 11, the CPC Committee of Shantou University issued a document (STU Dang Zi [2013] No. 37) formally releasing the Implementation and Supervision Measures of Shantou University for Carrying Out the Central Eight-Point Regulation. The measures had been examined and approved at the 15th meeting of the university Party Committee's standing committee in 2013.

The measures were distributed to all units at the university's main campus, aiming to translate the requirements of the central Eight-Point Regulation into the university's daily operations and to supervise their implementation. In the document, the university Party Committee required all units to carry out the measures conscientiously.

The full text of the measures was published as an attachment to the document and can be accessed through the OA system.
