It is reported that to better serve the health needs of faculty and staff, Shantou University First Affiliated Hospital will send an expert team to campus to provide complimentary health consultations.

Addressing various health issues identified in recent employee health check-ups, the hospital has organized a team of fifteen department directors spanning respiratory medicine, cardiovascular medicine, nephrology, neurology, gastroenterology, endocrinology, dermatology, rehabilitation medicine, general surgery, urology, orthopedics, otolaryngology, ophthalmology, dentistry, and gynecology. The team will offer one-on-one health consultations and medical guidance to employees.

Faculty and staff with health concerns are encouraged to participate. Specific dates and venues will be announced separately.
