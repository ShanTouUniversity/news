Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office announced a drainage-channel crossing project on December 11, 2013. The site was north of the E Building teaching complex parking lot, where Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Co., Ltd. was to build a pedestrian bridge over the channel.

The project included an 11-meter-span, 10-meter-long reinforced-concrete culvert, sloped concrete protection, steps and landscaped buffer strips on both sides. The contract price was RMB 313,600. Work was scheduled to start on December 11, 2013, and finish by February 23, 2014.

Construction could cause noise, vibration and dust. The university urged pedestrians to take care and not enter or linger near the worksite without authorization.
