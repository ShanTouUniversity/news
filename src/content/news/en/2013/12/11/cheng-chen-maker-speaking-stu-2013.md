It is reported that Cheng Chen, initiator of the maker-themed sharing events at Beijing maker space "C2D2" and a活跃的 maker evangelist, will deliver a lecture titled "A Different Kind of University" on Friday, December 13, from 7:30 to 9:00 PM in Lecture Room J-354, Zhicheng College, Shantou University.

Cheng Chen is passionate about promoting innovation and entrepreneurship culture on campus. The lecture is open to all students of Zhicheng College and aims to inspire interest in maker culture and explore new possibilities in university learning.

Students interested in attending are requested to register by sending an email to o_zctw@stu.edu.cn before December 13. All are welcome to attend.
