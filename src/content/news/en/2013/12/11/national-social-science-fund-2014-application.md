It is reported that applications for the 2014 National Social Science Fund projects have opened, and the Research Office of Shantou University has recently arranged the university's submission process. The university applies no campus-level cap on the number of applications this year, but late submissions will not be accepted.

## Project Categories and Funding

The 2014 fund covers three categories: key projects, general projects and youth projects. Key projects receive 400,000 yuan in funding, while general and youth projects each receive 200,000 yuan. Applicants may request an appropriate amount based on their research needs and prepare a reasonable budget.

## Eligibility

| Category | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Key / General projects | Applicants must hold a professional title of associate senior level or above (or equivalent), or hold a doctoral degree |
| Youth projects | Applicants and team members must be no older than 35 (born after March 1, 1979); those without the above qualifications need recommendations from two experts holding full senior titles |

In addition, a project leader may apply for only one project per year and may not participate in other applications as a member; a team member may join at most two applications; full-time graduate students are not eligible to apply; leaders of ongoing national-level projects may not apply, and applicants may not simultaneously submit to the National Natural Science Foundation or other national science and technology programs in the same year.

## Timeline

| Milestone | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Before January 20, 2014 | Submit application materials for formal review |
| Before February 19 | Send qualified electronic versions of the Application Form and Topic Argumentation loose-leaf |
| Before February 21 | Deliver seven copies of the double-sided A3-printed, center-stitched Application Form and Topic Argumentation loose-leaf |

## Other Highlights

According to the notice, topics in the project guide fall into two types — scope-based entries and specific titles — and applicants in all 23 disciplines that meet the guiding principles are encouraged to propose self-selected topics, which are treated the same as guide topics in evaluation procedures, standards and funding intensity. Basic theoretical research generally runs 3–5 years, while applied policy research runs 2–3 years. Final results undergo anonymous correspondence appraisal, and must be appraised before publication except under special circumstances.

The Research Office also cautions that anyone found guilty of fraud or plagiarism will be barred from applying for three years, and any awarded project will be terminated with a public criticism.
