Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office announced a renovation of public restrooms at Building 10 and the east stairwell of the Second Dining Hall on December 11, 2013. Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Co., Ltd. was contracted for the work.

The interior was to be rebuilt, including a new aluminum ceiling, tiled walls and floors, partitions, washbasins, sinks, a steel entrance door and electrical and plumbing lines. The contract price was RMB 378,700, and the project was scheduled for completion by February 23, 2014.

The work could cause noise, vibration and dust. The university advised people to keep safe and stay out of the worksite unless authorized.
