It is reported that Prof. Huang Zhi Chao from the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology at the Chinese University of Hong Kong will deliver an academic report titled "Identification of active components for the reproductive toxicity of Chinese herbal medicines for pregnancy" on the morning of December 13, from 10:00 to 11:00 AM, in Room 504, Science and Technology Building, Shantou University.

Prof. Huang has long been engaged in research on the safety of traditional Chinese medicines, with particular influence in the field of medication safety during pregnancy. The report will introduce scientific methods for identifying active components in herbal medicines that may cause reproductive toxicity, providing a theoretical basis for the safe clinical use of traditional Chinese medicines.

All faculty, students, and staff are welcome to attend.
