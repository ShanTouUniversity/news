Shantou University’s College of Journalism and Communication scheduled a briefing on its Japan exchange program for December 15, 2013. Hirai Nobutaka, representing Tokyo Film School, Tokyo Design Technology Center and an animation school, was to introduce the institutions’ teaching approaches.

The briefing was set for 11 a.m. in the New Library Lecture Hall and was aimed at students interested in the Japan exchange program.
