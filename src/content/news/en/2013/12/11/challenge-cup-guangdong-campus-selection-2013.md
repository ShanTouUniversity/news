Shantou University’s Youth League Committee opened its campus selection for the 9th Guangdong College Students’ Entrepreneurship Plan Competition, known as the Challenge Cup, on December 11, 2013. Full-time undergraduates and master’s and doctoral students were eligible; in-service postgraduate students were excluded. Cross-school and cross-discipline teams were encouraged.

Individuals or teams could submit one entry. Each proposal was expected to develop a market-oriented product or service into a business plan covering the opportunity, target customers, competition, business model, earning potential, management team and action plan. Plans were generally expected to be 20–40 pages, including appendices.

| Stage | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Campus submission deadline | 6 p.m., January 17, 2014 |
| Paper submissions | January 6–17, 4:30–5:30 p.m. daily, Science and Technology Association Office, Room 732, Building 789 |
| Campus review and presentations | Planned for mid-March 2014 |
| Provincial competition submission | Planned before May 2014 |

Both printed and electronic materials were required, including an application form and business plan. The original notice listed student email contacts for electronic submissions; their names and personal email addresses are not reproduced here. Application forms and detailed submission instructions remain available in the OA attachments.
