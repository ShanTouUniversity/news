Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office announced on December 11, 2013, plans to build a substation for high- and low-voltage equipment in the West-Campus area. Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Co., Ltd. was contracted to construct a single-story frame building with a floor area of 67 square meters.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contract price | RMB 150,300 |
| Planned schedule | December 11, 2013, to no later than February 23, 2014 |
| Possible impact | Noise, vibration and dust |

The university advised passersby to take care near the worksite, avoid entering without authorization and keep clear of the immediate construction area where possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office)
