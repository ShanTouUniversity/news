Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office shared information on online mock exams on December 11, 2013, inviting students to take practice tests for the College English Test and the national graduate entrance examination. The notice linked to separate NetEase Education portals and asked participants to add their university details so their participation could count toward STU’s total and related university rewards.

The notice listed December 13 at midnight as the participation-tracking deadline. The original links were:

- [CET mock-test session](http://study.163.com/activities/university.htm?id=15459)
- [Graduate entrance exam mock-test session](http://study.163.com/activities/university.htm?examType=kaoyan&id=15459)

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
