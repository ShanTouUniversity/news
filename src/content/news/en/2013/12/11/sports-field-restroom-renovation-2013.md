Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office announced on December 11, 2013, a renovation of the public restrooms beneath the sports-field stands. Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation was awarded the work.

The project involved rebuilding the interior, repainting the ceiling, tiling the walls and floors, and installing partitions, washbasins, sinks, aluminum windows and electrical and plumbing lines. The contract price was RMB 87,300, and completion was planned by January 19, 2014.

Construction could cause noise, vibration and dust. The university reminded campus users to stay safe and avoid entering the site or lingering nearby without authorization.
