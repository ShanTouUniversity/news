Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office announced on December 11, 2013, plans to replace outdoor water-supply branches and valves serving staff-residence buildings A1–A14 and B1–B16 on the main campus. Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation was contracted for the project.

The contract price was RMB 293,500. Work was scheduled to begin on December 11, 2013, and finish by January 24, 2014. Construction could cause noise, vibration and dust; the university advised nearby residents and passersby to take care and avoid unauthorized access to the work area.
