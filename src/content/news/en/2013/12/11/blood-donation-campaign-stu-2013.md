It is reported that the 2013 "Warm Blood" voluntary blood donation campaign, jointly organized by the University Youth League of Shantou University and Shantou Central Blood Station, will be held on December 13 and 15.

The event runs from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM each day at two locations: the area below the Second Cafeteria (opposite the supermarket) on December 13 and 15, and in front of the Fourth Cafeteria on December 13. The campaign aims to promote the spirit of selfless dedication and help others, raise awareness of voluntary blood donation, and expand the blood supply for Shantou's clinical needs.

All faculty, students, and staff are encouraged to participate and share the gift of life.
