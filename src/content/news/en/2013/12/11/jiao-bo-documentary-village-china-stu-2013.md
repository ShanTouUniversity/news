It is reported that renowned documentary filmmaker Jiao Bo will screen his new film "Village in China" and share insights on his creative process on the evening of December 12, from 7:00 to 10:00 PM, at the Science Lecture Hall, hosted by the College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University.

Jiao Bo is a prominent figure in Chinese documentary filmmaking, best known for his acclaimed work "My Father and My Mother." His latest film, "Village in China," follows the daily lives of several residents and families in Shaoyu Village, located in the Yimeng Mountains of Shandong Province, exploring the collision and integration of traditional customs and modern civilization on this land.

During the event, Jiao Bo will share the creative journey and behind-the-scenes stories of the documentary, discussing how the medium can authentically capture China's social transformation through real footage. All faculty, students, and staff are welcome to attend.
