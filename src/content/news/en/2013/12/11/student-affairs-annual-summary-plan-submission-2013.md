Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office began collecting annual student-affairs materials from the university’s schools and residential colleges on December 11, 2013. Units were asked to summarize their 2013 work, including Youth League activities, and prepare 2014 plans aligned with their own and the university’s development goals.

The review covered areas such as scholarships and aid, student development, peer learning, residential-college support, employment and entrepreneurship, enrichment programs, outdoor activities and campus culture. Submissions were due at Room 106 of the Student Affairs Office by December 23. Printed copies required the seal of the relevant Party general branch, and electronic files were to be sent to `o_xsc@stu.edu.cn`.
