It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on December 6 the final exam schedule for the fall 2013 semester. All academic units are asked to notify their faculty and students to log in to the credit system (http://credit.stu.edu.cn/) to view their specific exam schedules and make the necessary preparations.

## Exam Schedule Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exam type | Final examinations, fall 2013 semester |
| How to check | Log in to the credit system at http://credit.stu.edu.cn/ |
| Notification responsibility | Academic administrators at each unit |
| Issuing office | Academic Affairs Office |

All teaching units are reminded to ensure that every relevant faculty member and student receives the exam information in a timely manner.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
