It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University announced on December 4 that Ms. Lin Yiling, Editor-in-Chief of Taiwan Xingbao, will visit campus on December 6 to deliver an academic lecture, sharing her extensive experience in journalism and communication with faculty and students.

Lin holds a master's degree in journalism from the University of Oklahoma and an EMBA from the Business School of National Chengchi University. She currently serves as Editor-in-Chief of Taiwan Xingbao and as an adjunct lecturer at both National Taiwan Normal University and Culture University. Her media career includes roles as a reporter and coordinator at United Daily News, deputy director of the Central Daily News interview department, editor-in-chief of Fortune Magazine (Taiwan), and editor-in-chief of Christian Forum News. She has also hosted radio and television programs, including "News Topics" on CBRN News Network and "2100 Xingbao Live" on Taiwan Arts Television.
