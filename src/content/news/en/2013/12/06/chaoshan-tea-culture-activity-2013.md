It is reported that the Xingchao Society, under the Youth League Committee of Shantou University, will launch the "观茶入微" (Observing Tea with Care) Chaoshan Tea Culture series of events starting December 7, aimed at promoting traditional Chaoshan tea culture and deepening faculty and students' understanding of local heritage. The events are open to all.

The series includes a Chaoshan tea culture lecture and a tea-mountaining excursion to Fenghuang Mountain. The lecture features distinguished guest Zheng Huifeng, director of the Shantou Chaoshan Gongfu Tea Research Institute, inheritor of intangible cultural heritage, senior tea assessor, and visiting professor at Hanshan Normal University, who will share insights into the art and culture of Chaoshan Gongfu tea preparation.
