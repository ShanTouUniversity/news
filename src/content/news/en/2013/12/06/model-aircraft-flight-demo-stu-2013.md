It is reported that the University Youth League Committee and the Science and Technology Association at Shantou University will jointly host the "In the Clouds" model aircraft flight performance on Saturday, December 14, from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM on the campus track field.

The event will feature a remote-controlled aircraft stunt performance by members of the Shantou Aeromodelling Association, showcasing the skills and excitement of aeromodelling flight. The event also announced the establishment of a university aeromodelling interest group, aimed at bringing together students interested in the hobby and preparing for the school\'s aeromodelling competition scheduled for next semester.
