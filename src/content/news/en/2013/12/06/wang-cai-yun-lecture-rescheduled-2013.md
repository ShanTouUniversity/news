It is reported that the lecture by Wang Caisyun, originally scheduled for December 6 from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM at the New Library Lecture Hall, has been rescheduled due to a change in the speaker's itinerary. The lecture will now take place on December 7 (Saturday) from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM in ACC Room 3, hosted by the College of Communication and Journalism.

Wang Caisyun holds an EMBA from National Chengchi University and currently serves as Publisher of Brain Magazine and General Manager of Brain Communication Co., Ltd. He also serves as the Taiwan representative for the Clio International Advertising Awards and the London International Advertising Awards. His creative works have received multiple prestigious honors, including Gold Bell Awards for radio and television advertising, as well as the Gold Recording Award for television advertising.

## Speaker Profile

Wang Caisyun graduated from the EMBA program at National Chengchi University. He currently serves as Publisher of Brain Magazine and General Manager of Brain Communication Co., Ltd. With extensive experience in advertising and communications, he has served as Taiwan representative for the Clio International Advertising Awards and the London International Advertising Awards, and his creative works have won multiple honors including Gold Bell Awards for radio and TV advertising and the Gold Recording Award for TV advertising.
