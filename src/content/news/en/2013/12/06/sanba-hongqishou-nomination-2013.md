It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University forwarded a notice from the Shantou Women’s Federation on December 6, 2013, to recommend candidates for the Shantou March 8th Red-Banner Pacesetter and Red-Banner Collective awards.

## Nomination Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizer | Shantou Women’s Federation |
| Commendation | During the March 8th International Women’s Day period in 2014 |
| University quota | 1 Red-Banner Pacesetter |
| Deadline | December 16, 2013, to the University Labor Union |
| Contact | Email o_xgh@stu.edu.cn, Phone 86502983 |

## Eligibility

Candidates should love the Party and the country, implement the Scientific Outlook on Development, uphold Party policies and observe laws; contribute actively to building a prosperous and happy Shantou through pioneering and hard work; practice socialist values with high moral standards and outstanding professional achievements; demonstrate solidarity, integrity and exemplary conduct with high prestige among colleagues; and have received district-level or higher commendations.

Eligible female staff may apply by completing the *Shantou March 8th Red-Banner Pacesetter Recommendation Form*.

> ⚠️ The recommendation form is available on the OA system.
>
