It is reported that the Research Office at Shantou University has announced the opening of applications for the 2014 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) and Hong Kong Research Grant Council (RGC) Joint Research Collaboration Fund.

The program aims to foster academic cooperation and scientific exchange between mainland China and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Funding is available in six disciplines: information science, life sciences, new materials science, marine and environmental sciences, medical sciences, and management sciences. Each project may be funded for up to four years, with a maximum grant of 900,000 RMB per project.

Faculty members interested in applying are encouraged to prepare their proposals promptly and submit them before the application deadline.
