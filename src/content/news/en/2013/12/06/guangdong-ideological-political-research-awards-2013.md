It is reported that the Research Office at Shantou University has announced the results of the 2013 Guangdong Provincial Universities Ideological and Political Research Project applications. Out of eleven proposals submitted by the university, three were approved for funding, yielding an approval rate of approximately 27%.

## Approved Projects

| Funding Type | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Key-funded Project | 1 |
| General-funded Project | 1 |
| Self-funded General Project | 1 |
| Total | 3 |

The Research Office has urged all project leaders to take their responsibilities seriously and conduct research in accordance with the project requirements and timelines, ensuring the successful completion of their funded projects.
