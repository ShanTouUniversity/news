Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office reported the first-batch disbursement results for national student loans for the 2013–2014 academic year on December 6, 2013. Following bank approval, 439 students received the first-batch loans. The notice said the loans were used to offset tuition and accommodation fees.

Recipients could check their own fee records through the university’s student affairs system. Questions could be directed to the Student Aid Management Center at `86502249`. This report gives only the aggregate number and does not publish student names, individual loan amounts, bank-account information or fee arrears.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Disbursement details and personal fee records are available through the university system; individual financial information is not reproduced here.
