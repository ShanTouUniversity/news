As winter temperatures fell and air-conditioner use declined, Shantou University’s Student Community Management and Services Center asked students who had registered to receive air-conditioner remotes to return them to their residence-building duty rooms by December 10, 2013. The return was intended to support maintenance and reduce the risk of lost remotes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Community Management and Services Center)
