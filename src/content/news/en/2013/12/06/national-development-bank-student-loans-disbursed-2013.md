Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced on December 6, 2013, that China Development Bank had completed disbursement of student-origin credit loans for the 2013–2014 academic year. A total of 127 STU students received disbursements.

Recipients could check their individual fee information through the university’s student-charge query system. To protect students’ financial privacy, this report gives only the aggregate count and does not reproduce borrower names, account details, individual balances or arrears.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Borrower lists and personal payment information are available only to students through the university system and are not reproduced here.
