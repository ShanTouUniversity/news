It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University has launched the application process for the 2014 Guangdong Provincial "Model Posts for Women" (Jin Guo Wen Ming Gang).

The initiative is organized in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Administrative Measures for Model Posts for Women (Yue Fu Zi [2003] No. 10) and aims to implement the "Women's Meritorious Service" campaign proposed at the 11th National Women's Congress, mobilizing women to contribute to Guangdong's goal of taking the lead in basically realizing socialist modernization. According to a notice from the Shantou Women's Federation, STU has been allocated one nomination for the provincial honor in 2014. Eligible units may apply in line with the criteria set out in the administrative measures.

## Application Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Quota | 1 nomination |
| Basis | Administrative Measures for Guangdong Model Posts for Women (Yue Fu Zi [2003] No. 10) |
| Deadline | December 16, 2013 |
| Submission to | University Labor Union |
| Email | o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |
| Tel | 86502983 |

Applicants are required to complete the Model Post for Women Application Form and submit it to the Labor Union before the deadline. The administrative measures and application form are available as attachments to the OA notice.
