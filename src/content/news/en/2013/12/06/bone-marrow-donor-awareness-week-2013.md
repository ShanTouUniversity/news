It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Volunteers Association of the Business School at Shantou University will jointly hold the "Love for Marrow" (爱相"髓") awareness week from December 11 to 15, 2013. The initiative aims to raise awareness about leukemia and other blood diseases, promote the Chinese Bone Marrow Donor Registry, and encourage more faculty and students to volunteer as hematopoietic stem cell donors.

During the event, organizers will set up an information booth on campus to introduce the blood sample collection process for the Chinese Bone Marrow Donor Registry and answer questions about donation. A prize quiz will also be held to increase engagement. Additionally, a lecture on bone marrow donation will be organized to explain the principles, significance, and safety aspects of stem cell donation in depth. A signature petition supporting bone marrow donation will be available on site for students and faculty to sign.
