It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Societies Federation will hold the 2013 freshman orienteering event from September 9 to 12, running daily from 2:00 to 4:30 p.m. The event is open to freshmen across the university, while students of the Medical College and postgraduate students are not included.

According to the organizers, all undergraduate freshmen except those of the Medical College are required to take part, with group assignments detailed in the attachment to the notice. Participants must attach sleeve stickers and gather at the Gymnasium at 1:10 p.m. on the day of the event. Students are also advised to wear sportswear and sports shoes, bring their own water bottles, and keep an eye on the weather.

At noon on September 4, the organizers will also hold a prize quiz at the Golden Triangle, and students are welcome to join in.
