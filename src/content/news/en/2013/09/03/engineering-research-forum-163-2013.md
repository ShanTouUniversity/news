It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold the 163rd Engineering Research Academic Forum on September 5, 2013, inviting Dr. Li Songsheng, a software and wireless communication engineer at China Telecom Shenzhen Branch, to give a talk on positioning in wireless network environments.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Positioning in Wireless Network Environments |
| Speaker | Dr. Li Songsheng, software and wireless communication engineer, China Telecom Shenzhen Branch |
| Time | 11:00 a.m., Thursday, September 5, 2013 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering |
| Participants | Postgraduates, doctoral students and related faculty of the College of Engineering; interested teachers and students are welcome |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |
