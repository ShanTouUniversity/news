It is reported that, in accordance with the deployment of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee and the university's overall arrangements, and to further advance the Party's mass-line education campaign, Shantou University issued a notice on September 3 on soliciting opinions for mid-level cadres, requiring them to gather a broad range of views and identify prominent problems under the "four malfeasances" — formalism, bureaucratism, hedonism and extravagance — while correcting problems as they continue their studies.

The notice states that mid-level cadres should take the initiative to seek opinions from colleagues in their own units as well as from the people they serve, so as to understand their genuine views and suggestions. Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches are responsible for organizing and implementing opinion collection and rectification within their units, and must report the consolidated opinions to the office of the university's education campaign leading group by September 10. For related matters, contact o_zzb@stu.edu.cn or telephone 86502345.
