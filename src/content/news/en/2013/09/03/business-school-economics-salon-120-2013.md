It is reported that the 120th session of the Economics Salon at Shantou University's Business School will be held from 19:45 to 21:30 on Saturday, September 7, 2013, at the Science Report Hall. Pu Yonghao, Chief Investment Officer for Asia Pacific at the UBS Wealth Management Investment Office, has been invited as the speaker, and his topic is "Challenges and Opportunities for Emerging Markets in the Global Economic Transformation." Undergraduates and graduate students of the Business School are expected to attend, and all faculty and students of the university are welcome.

### About the speaker

Pu Yonghao earned a bachelor's degree in accounting from Xiamen University in 1982 and a master's degree in economics from Xiamen University in 1984, followed by a master's degree in statistics from the London School of Economics.

He joined UBS in 2004 as regional head of the wealth management research department and chief regional economist. He became Chief Investment Strategist for Asia Pacific in 2009 and was appointed Chief Investment Officer for Asia Pacific in 2012, leading investment strategy research across the region.

Before joining UBS, Pu served as head of the Asian economics department at Cazenove (Asia), a senior advisor at the Asian Development Bank, and a senior economist at Nomura International (Hong Kong) and BOCI (UK). He frequently appears on CNBC Asia, Bloomberg TV and Reuters, and regularly publishes bylined articles in the Hong Kong Economic Times and Entrepreneur.
