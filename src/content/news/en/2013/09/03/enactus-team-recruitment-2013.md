As the new academic year begins, the Shantou University Enactus team (formerly SIFE, Students in Free Enterprise) has launched a series of recruitment activities, inviting students from across the university to join regardless of major or year of study.

Enactus — a blend of "Entrepreneurial," "Action" and "Us" — is a global nonprofit organization headquartered in the United States, with student teams at 2,000 universities in 45 countries around the world. The STU team focuses on community livelihoods and service, and runs a number of social entrepreneurship projects that help disadvantaged groups improve their lives.

### Recruitment schedule

| Activity | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruitment roadshow | September 3–5, 10:30 a.m.–7:00 p.m. | Golden Triangle |
| Themed information session, "Achieve Business Excellence, Create Social Value" | September 8, 7:00–9:00 p.m. | Science Report Hall |
| Recruitment interviews | September 13–14, 5:00–11:00 p.m. | Academic Seminar Room, 2nd floor, Business School |

### How to apply

Students interested in joining can download the application form (see attachment) and submit it as instructed in the application guide. The deadline for registration is midnight on Wednesday, September 11.
