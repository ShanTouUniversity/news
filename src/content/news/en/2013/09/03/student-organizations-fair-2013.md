It is reported that to help freshmen quickly adapt to and integrate into university life and learn about student organizations and campus culture, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University will hold the 2013 campus student organizations fair from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on September 3 (Tuesday) in the Library Lecture Hall.

The fair is open to all freshmen, who are welcome to attend and learn about the university-level student organizations.
