It is reported that, in connection with the ongoing education and practice campaign on the Party's mass line, the Society for Party Building and Ideological Work of Shantou University decided at a council meeting to hold a themed paper solicitation during its 2013 annual conference. A notice was issued on September 3, inviting faculty and students across the university to take an active part.

## Theme

The call centers on "the Party's mass line and the study of mechanisms for Party building and ideological work in universities," focusing on how the campaign relates to Party building and ideological work in higher education. A list of suggested topics is attached to the notice for reference, and authors may also propose their own titles.

## Submission Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | The Party's mass line and mechanisms of Party building and ideological work in universities |
| Topics | Authors may follow the attached topic list or choose their own titles |
| Format | Papers must follow the document format attached to the notice |
| Submission | Send WORD files to o_xcb@stu.edu.cn |
| Deadline | October 15, 2013 |

## Review and Publication

According to the notice, the society will organize experts to review the submissions and select outstanding papers. Papers that meet the requirements will be collected and published by Shantou University Press.

> ⚠️ The suggested topic list and document format are available as attachments on the OA system.
>
