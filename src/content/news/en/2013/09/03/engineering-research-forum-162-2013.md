It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold the 162nd Engineering Research Academic Forum on September 6, 2013, inviting Professor Hiroshi Yamaguchi of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Doshisha University, Japan, to give a talk on solar thermal power generation and ultra-low-temperature refrigeration. Professor Yamaguchi is a Fellow of the Engineering Academy of Japan and a Fellow of the Royal Society in the United Kingdom.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Solar Thermal Power Generation and Ultra-low-temperature Refrigeration |
| Speaker | Professor Hiroshi Yamaguchi, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Doshisha University, Japan; Fellow of the Engineering Academy of Japan and Fellow of the Royal Society (UK) |
| Time | 10:00 a.m., Friday, September 6, 2013 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering |
| Participants | Postgraduates, doctoral students and related faculty of the College of Engineering; interested teachers and students are welcome |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |
