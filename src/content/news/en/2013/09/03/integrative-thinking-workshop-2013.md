It is reported that, to advance reform of integrative thinking teaching methods and to share ways of embedding integrative thinking into courses, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold two integrative thinking workshops on September 11. A notice has been issued inviting teachers to sign up.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Introduction to STU's integrative thinking course and ways to embed integrative thinking in courses |
| Organizer | Professor Guo Weiwen |
| First session | 10:00–12:00, September 11 |
| Second session | 15:00–17:00, September 11 |
| Venue | Room 201, New Administration Center |
| How to attend | The two sessions are identical; teachers may choose either one |

## Participants

According to the notice, leaders of integrative thinking teaching reform projects are required to attend, and other interested teachers are welcome.

## Preparation

Participants may download relevant materials from the Academic Affairs Office website in advance (http://jwc.stu.edu.cn/download/zhsw.zip) and share questions and insights at the workshops.
