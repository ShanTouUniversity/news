It is reported that to cooperate with the special audit and investigation on research fund management of provincial universities organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Leading Group for the Special Audit Self-Inspection of Research Fund Management (Supervision and Audit Office) of Shantou University issued a notice on September 3 to deploy a self-inspection of research projects ongoing in 2011 and 2012.

## Background

The self-inspection is conducted in accordance with the *Notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on the Special Audit and Investigation of Research Fund Management of Provincial Universities* (Yue Jiao Shen Han [2013] No. 15). The provincial department will conduct sampling inspections on the basis of self-inspections by each university regarding the management of research funds in 2011 and 2012.

## Scope

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Projects covered | Ongoing research projects in 2011 and 2012 (see Attachment 3 for the list) |
| Content | Management of project funds, to be reported item by item in the *STU Self-Inspection Form for Research Project Fund Management*, one form per project |

## Requirements and Deadlines

| No. | Requirement | Deadline | Submission method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Each unit designates a responsible person and reports the name and contact number | Before 9:00 on September 5 | Send to the Supervision and Audit Office mailbox o_jj@stu.edu.cn |
| 2 | Project leaders carefully fill in the self-inspection form and take responsibility for its contents | — | One form per project |
| 3 | Units collect and submit all self-inspection forms of the unit | Before September 13, 2013 | Submit to the Supervision and Audit Office (Room 422, New Administration Center) |
