It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 3, 2013 that the Second Distinguished Lecture, originally scheduled for 19:30 on September 5 at the Grand Hall, has been postponed because of a change in the speaker’s itinerary.

## Lecture Adjustment

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Original Time | 19:30, September 5, 2013 |
| Original Venue | Grand Hall |
| Reason for Postponement | Change in the speaker’s itinerary |
| Follow-up Arrangement | The new time and venue will be announced separately |

Faculty and students interested in attending are advised to watch for further notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
