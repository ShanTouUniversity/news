It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has finalised the list of students shortlisted for interview for duty staff positions at the Student Learning Center (SLC), based on applications received as of September 21 and on the centre's recruitment needs.

Shortlisted students are asked to attend the interview on time at the SLC. The interviews will be held on Tuesday, September 24, 2013 and will be conducted in time slots, with the earliest starting at 9:30 am. The venue is the Student Learning Center (SLC). Students with questions should contact the SLC first. Telephone: 86502250; email: slc@stu.edu.cn.
