It is reported that the general education course Travel Photography Creation and Research (Class No. 63287) at Shantou University was originally scheduled to be taught by instructor Xie Lin during periods 6 and 7 on the afternoon of September 22, but the session was called off because of a typhoon. To keep the course on schedule, the Academic Affairs Office and the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication have arranged a make-up class for the course.

## Make-up Class Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Travel Photography Creation and Research (general education course) |
| Class No. | 63287 |
| Original class time | Periods 6 and 7 on the afternoon of September 22 |
| Reason for cancellation | Typhoon |
| Make-up class time | Periods 8 and 9 on Tuesday, September 24 |
| Make-up class venue | E307 |

Students taking the course are reminded to pass the word to one another and attend at the adjusted time and place.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office; Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
