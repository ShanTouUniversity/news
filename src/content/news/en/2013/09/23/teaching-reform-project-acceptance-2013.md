It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office will conduct completion reviews of university-level teaching reform research projects approved in 2009 and 2011. In accordance with the STU Administrative Measures for Undergraduate Teaching Reform Research Projects and the research and practice schedules of the projects, the review covers projects approved in the two years that have not yet been completed.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | STU teaching reform research projects approved in 2009 and 2011 that have not yet been completed (see Attachment 1 for the list) |
| Materials | Three copies of the completion review form; three copies of the originals or photocopies of the research outputs; electronic versions identical to the paper materials |
| Deadline | By October 23, 2013 (Wednesday) |
| Submission | Academic Affairs Office service window, 3rd Floor, New Administration Center |
| Contact | Zhou Mengyi, Tel: 86502736, Email: myzhou@stu.edu.cn |

## Material Requirements

Project leaders must complete the STU Teaching Reform Research Project Completion Review Form (three copies; see Attachment 2) and submit the originals or photocopies of the research outputs listed in the form (three copies each). Electronic materials must match the content of the paper versions: for outputs in book form, scans of the cover and copyright page are sufficient, and published papers may be downloaded from journal websites.

## Other Circumstances

Project leaders unable to complete their projects on schedule should submit a written application; where a project leader has left the university, other members of the project team may organize participation in the completion review.

> ⚠️ The project list and the completion review form are available in the original notice on the OA system.
