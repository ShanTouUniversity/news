It is reported that after a round of intense auditions, the results of the University Choir's fall 2013 recruitment at Shantou University have been announced: 19 students passed the auditions and became new choir members. The list was jointly released by the Cheung Kong School of Art & Design and the Arts Education Center.

## Assembly for Selected Students

Selected students are required to gather at the choir rehearsal hall on the first floor of the Arts Education Center (the original audition venue) at 13:50 on Wednesday, September 25.

## Notes on Selection

According to the organizers, the selection took into account the requirements of choral performance formats, the needs of the choir, and each candidate's vocal timbre. Some unsuccessful candidates sang well individually but were not selected because their distinctive timbre was harder to blend into a chorus. The organizers thanked everyone who attended and encouraged all students to keep their passion for music alive.

## Supplementary Audition

Since many interested students were unable to attend the first round for various reasons, the choir will hold an additional audition from 1:00 pm to 2:00 pm on Wednesday, September 25, in the music rehearsal hall on the first floor of the Arts Education Center. Interested students are welcome to participate and are encouraged to spread the word.
