It is reported that to help freshmen understand the characteristics of study at university and develop study methods suited to it, the Law School of Shantou University will hold a special lecture on the afternoon of September 25, 2013, with Professor Li Guanghui as the speaker.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Characteristics and Methods of Study at University |
| Speaker | Professor Li Guanghui |
| Time | 2:40 pm – 4:00 pm, September 25, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 203, E Lecture Hall, Academic Building |

Students are welcome to attend.
