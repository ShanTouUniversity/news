It is reported that, in order to implement the Excellent Engineer Training Programme and in line with the College of Engineering's EIP-CDIO engineering education reform, the College of Engineering will hold an outdoor quality development training camp for its 2013 freshmen at Lianhua Mountain in Chenghai on September 28, 2013. All 2013 freshmen of the college and all academic mentors for freshmen will take part.

According to the organisers, the camp aims to enhance communication among students and between students and their academic mentors, build harmonious relationships, strengthen a sense of pride in the college, and train the freshmen's willpower as well as their teamwork, leadership and organisational skills.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | September 28, 2013 |
| Venue | Lianhua Mountain, Chenghai |
| Participants | All 2013 freshmen of the College of Engineering and all academic mentors for freshmen |
| Assembly | 7:30 am gather by major at the University Motto Monument; 7:50 am assembly completed and departure by bus; 5:00 pm gather at the same place for the return trip |
| Programme | 9:30 am – 5:00 pm: outdoor training, followed by awards and a group photo |
| Leave application | Freshmen who cannot attend for physical or other special reasons must submit a written leave request to the Youth League Committee Office of the College of Engineering (Gongnan 210) before 5:30 pm on September 25; telephone 0754-86502986 |

Participants are also reminded to note the following:

- Do not leave the training without permission once it has begun; those with genuine urgent matters must inform the team coach in advance;
- Do not climb the camp's facilities without authorisation, and strictly follow the coach's instructions;
- Notify the team coach immediately if feeling unwell;
- Take care with fire and electrical safety, and do not smoke or discard cigarette butts in non-smoking areas;
- Wear sportswear and sports shoes; do not wear skirts or high heels; try not to bring valuables in case they are lost or damaged.
