It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University and the STU GMC Organizing Committee have opened registration for the 2013 Global Management Challenge (GMC), inviting students across the university to take part.

The GMC is the world's largest business-management simulation competition, officially accredited by the European Foundation for Management Development (EFMD) and spanning five continents. Originating in Europe more than three decades ago, it is an annual international event that aims to improve participants' business management knowledge and skills worldwide through modern simulation-based training. Further details are available at www.gmc-china.net.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| About the competition | A business-management simulation contest officially accredited by the European Foundation for Management Development (EFMD); it originated in Europe, has a history of more than 30 years, spans five continents and is held annually |
| Eligibility | STU undergraduates and graduate students who have completed at least two economics or management courses and are proficient in Excel |
| Entry format | Individual entry, with the GMC organizing committee helping to form teams, or team entry with three to five members per team |
| Deadline | Midnight, October 12, 2013 |
| How to register | Send the registration form to the STU GMC Organizing Committee email address |
| Management staff recruitment | The committee is recruiting several management staff members to help organize the competition and outreach activities; interested applicants should write “management staff application” in the remarks section when submitting their form |

Registration forms must be sent to the STU GMC Organizing Committee email address by midnight on October 12. The contact person and contact details are provided in the original OA notice.
