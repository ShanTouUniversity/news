It is reported that the Publicity Department of the Party Committee issued a notice on September 23, 2013, announcing that the university will hold a Party Committee central group study session on September 25 to study the spirit of Xi Jinping's important speech at the National Publicity and Ideological Work Conference.

## Session Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00, Wednesday, September 25, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administration Center |
| Theme | Studying the spirit of Xi Jinping's important speech at the National Publicity and Ideological Work Conference |
| Speakers sharing study reflections | Bai Youjin, Yu Shanyan |
| Attendees | Members of the University Party Committee; secretaries of general Party branches and directly affiliated branches; heads of the Discipline Inspection Commission, the Party and Government Office, the Organization and United Front Work Department, the Publicity Department, the News Center and the Student Affairs Office; the Director of the Department of Social Sciences, the Vice Chair of the University Trade Union and the Secretary of the Youth League Committee |
| Issued by | Publicity Department of the Party Committee |

Those listed in the notice are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department of the Party Committee)
