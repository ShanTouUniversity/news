It is reported that the Student Affairs Office has issued Bulletin No.237 on study style inspections for week 1 of the fall semester of the 2013-2014 academic year. Members of the inspection team checked teaching buildings during the week: at 8:00 a.m. on September 16 (Monday), three students were late by less than 5 minutes and one student by more than 5 minutes at Teaching Building E; at 8:00 a.m. on September 17 (Tuesday), five students were late by less than 5 minutes at Teaching Building E.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Inspection time | 8:00 a.m., September 16 (Monday) and September 17 (Tuesday), 2013 |
| Location | Teaching Building E |
| Late arrivals | September 16: 3 students late by less than 5 minutes and 1 by more than 5 minutes; September 17: 5 students late by less than 5 minutes |
| Follow-up | The list was sent to all schools (residential colleges), which are asked to criticize and educate the students concerned and to keep working on study style |
| Issuing office | Student Affairs Office |

The bulletin also noted that the list of students who failed to observe university rules was forwarded to each school (residential college) as an attachment, asking them to criticize and educate the students concerned and to keep consistently improving the study style.

> ⚠️ The list of late-arriving students is available in the OA notice attachment and is not republished on this news page.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
