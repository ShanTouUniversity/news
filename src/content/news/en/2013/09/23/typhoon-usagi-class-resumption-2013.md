It is reported that on September 23 the Shantou Meteorological Observatory lifted the weather warnings it had issued earlier: the yellow rainstorm warning was lifted at 6:43 a.m. and the blue typhoon warning at 6:44 a.m. Shantou University then announced that classes and work would resume as normal from that afternoon.

According to the "Three Defences" Command of Shantou University, the university decided to end the class suspension and restore normal teaching and work arrangements as Typhoon Usagi weakened and the warnings were lifted. Earlier, because waterlogging and fallen trees had blocked roads across the city, the university had decided to keep classes suspended on the morning of September 23, as previously reported by STU News.

The university reminded faculty and students to keep an eye on changing weather conditions and to travel safely. The notice was issued by the "Three Defences" Command of Shantou University on September 23, 2013.
