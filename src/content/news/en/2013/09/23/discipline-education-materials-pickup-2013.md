It is reported that, to support this year's Discipline Education Month, the Discipline Inspection and Supervision Working Committee of the Provincial Education Department and the Publicity and Education Office of the Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection jointly compiled the 2013 edition of the Anti-Corruption Education Reader for the Guangdong Education System. The Discipline Inspection Commission Office of Shantou University has issued a notice to distribute the reader to all Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches, to cadres above the division level, and to section-level cadres working in personnel, finance, logistics and related fields.

The notice asks each Party general branch and directly affiliated Party branch to send a staff member to collect the reader at the university's Discipline Inspection Commission Office (Room 422, New Administration Center) before 5:00 pm on Wednesday, September 25.

In addition, the office keeps special anti-corruption education videos, including features on positive role models and analyses of negative cases, which units may borrow and organize viewing sessions for study. The attachment issued with the notice lists the main study materials for this year's Discipline Education Month.
