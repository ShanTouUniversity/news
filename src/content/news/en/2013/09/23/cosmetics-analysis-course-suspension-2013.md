It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on September 23, 2013, stating that one session of the course Cosmetics and Analysis will be cancelled because its instructor, Fang Yiwen of the Chemistry Department, is away on graduate admissions publicity. Students taking the course have been asked to inform one another.

## Cancellation Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Cosmetics and Analysis |
| Class number | 63749 |
| Cancelled session | Periods 1 and 2, Thursday of week 2 (September 26) |
| Venue | E207 |
| Reason | Instructor away on graduate admissions publicity |
| Make-up class | Time to be determined and announced separately |

Students enrolled in the course are advised to inform one another and watch for the follow-up notice on the make-up session.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
