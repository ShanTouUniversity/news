It is reported that the Audit Office, the Finance Office and the Logistics Management Office of Shantou University have jointly issued a notice to all university units and staff, reiterating the requirement to follow procurement rules. The notice noted that as government procurement regulations have been implemented, the university's own procurement system has been steadily improved. Centralized purchasing is currently carried out through a quarterly procurement plan, while urgent needs are met through channels such as the Guangdong provincial government framework supply agreement and the national university online bidding system, with the aim of raising procurement efficiency and cutting costs.

According to the notice, some students and faculty recently purchased equipment directly without going through price inquiries at the equipment management department because they were unfamiliar with the university's procurement rules. The equipment they bought cost about 10 percent more than the government framework supply price, which made reimbursement difficult. The notice also reminded readers that departments such as the provincial discipline inspection and audit authorities, the Finance Department and the Education Department inspect and audit procurement work at universities every year; if an institution is found to have failed to follow government procurement rules, it will suffer considerable negative consequences.

The issuing offices therefore reiterated that when purchasing equipment and materials, all departments, faculty members and students must strictly follow the rules of the relevant government departments and university documents, and report purchases to the equipment and materials procurement management department according to the procurement procedures.
