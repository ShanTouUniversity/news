It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University has issued a notice: the campus info session of the Guangdong Youth E-commerce Startup Competition (Season 1) will be held from 15:00 to 17:00 on September 25, 2013, at Classroom E-Jie 201. Guest speaker Hu Ying will present the spirit of the competition and explain its rules, while the organizing committee will also hold an open exchange with students and accept on-site registration. Faculty and students across the university are welcome to attend.

## Competition at a Glance

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Info session time | 15:00–17:00, September 25, 2013 |
| Info session venue | Classroom E-Jie 201 |
| Guest speaker | Hu Ying |
| Competition theme | Entrepreneurship Navigation, Yubo Soaring |
| Competition period | September 2013 – January 2014 |
| Competition venues | Shantou and Guangzhou |
| Competition format | Submission of an e-commerce marketing plan plus hands-on practice at a training base |
| Eligibility | Guangdong-registered college students and young adults from Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Shantou |
| Team requirement | Teams of 2–5 members, each choosing its own team name |
| Recruitment | Open recruitment through online announcements, media coverage and on-campus recruitment; applicants upload team members' resumes, role assignments and other materials through the official website as instructed |
| Prizes | One champion, one runner-up and one third-place team, each receiving a cash award and a startup fund |

## Prize Structure

| Prize | Cash award | Startup fund |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Champion (1 team) | RMB 30,000 | RMB 1.2 million |
| Runner-up (1 team) | RMB 20,000 | RMB 800,000 |
| Third place (1 team) | RMB 10,000 | RMB 500,000 |
