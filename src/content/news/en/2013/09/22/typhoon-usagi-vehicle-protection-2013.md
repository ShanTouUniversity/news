It is reported that, to prevent vehicles from being crushed by broken branches and steel canopies, the "Three Defences" Command of Shantou University issued an urgent reminder on September 22, asking faculty and students to move their vehicles to open areas as soon as possible.

The university noted that during Typhoon Usagi, tree branches on and around campus can easily snap and temporary steel canopies may also be damaged, putting vehicles parked under trees or near such structures at risk. Open sections such as the new road east of the new library are suggested as temporary parking spots.
