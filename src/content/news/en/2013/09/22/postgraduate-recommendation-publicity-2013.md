It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a public notice on September 22 proposing to formally recommend 39 outstanding 2014 graduates for exemption from the postgraduate entrance examination, including 24 candidates for academic degree programs and 15 for professional degree programs. The list remains open for comment during the publicity period.

Under the university's Measures for the Management of Recommending Outstanding Undergraduates for Exemption from the Postgraduate Entrance Examination (Shanda Fa [2009] No. 119), candidates were first recommended by their colleges and then reviewed by the university's working group for postgraduate recommendation before the list was finalised.

During the publicity period, anyone with objections to the proposed candidates may write to the Academic Affairs Office by email before September 25, 2013. The university stressed that feedback must be signed with the sender's real name so that it can be verified and the sender held accountable. Email: o_jwc@stu.edu.cn.
