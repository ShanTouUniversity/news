It is reported that, as of **September 22, 2013**, some student dormitories at Shantou University had still not paid their water and electricity bills for August 2013 and earlier. Under university regulations, power will be cut to these dormitories from **September 23** until all arrears are paid in full.

To make payment easier, the Network Centre will deduct payments twice a day through the campus card from **September 23 to 25**. The first deduction is at **10:30 a.m.**, and dormitories whose payments go through will have power restored between **2:30 p.m. and 4:30 p.m.** the same day. The second deduction is at **4:30 p.m.**, and dormitories whose payments go through will have power restored between **5:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m.** the same day. Dormitories that complete payment after 4:30 p.m. will have power restored between **2:30 p.m. and 4:30 p.m.** the following day.

**After September 25**, only one deduction will be made each day, at **4:30 p.m.**

The university also reminded students to watch their account balances. Those whose campus card balance is insufficient should top up at a recharge machine in time, while those whose bank account balance is insufficient should deposit enough money during banking hours and then transfer it to their campus card. Students who have lost their campus card can complete payment by setting up a proxy payment in the Utilities Management System, or pay cash into Shantou University's account (No. 628857760147) at the Bank of China STU branch and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for recording. The Finance Office does not accept cash in any form.
