It is reported that Shantou University has launched centralized management of the private passports held by serving leading cadres at the deputy-division level or above. In line with regulations from higher authorities, these cadres must hand their personal passports to the university's Organization and United Front Work Department for unified safekeeping, in a move aimed at strengthening day-to-day supervision of leading cadres and standardizing the management of documents for private travel abroad.

## Clearance and Reporting

Under the national registration-and-filing system for public employees, each unit must carry out a full review of private passports held by its cadres at deputy-division level or above and submit a stamped written report to the department **by September 27**, along with an electronic copy.

Specific requirements include:

- Cadres who do not hold a private passport should state so in the filing form;
- Those who already hold passports but have not yet surrendered them should do so within the same deadline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ Filing forms and other attachments are available on the OA system.
