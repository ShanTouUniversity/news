It is reported that after the university's "three-prevention" (typhoon, flood and drought) command office ordered classes suspended, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced that the course adjustment period, originally set to close at 5:00 p.m. on September 22, would be extended by one day, pending the resumption of classes.

All afternoon classes were canceled that day. The office has reminded students who have not yet finalized their course selections to watch for the resumption schedule and use the extra time to complete their adjustments in the system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
