It is reported that the Flood Control, Drought Relief and Wind Protection Headquarters of Shantou University issued an urgent notice on September 22, 2013, to suspend classes university-wide for the afternoon of September 22 due to Super Typhoon Usagi.

According to the meteorological forecast, the center of Usagi was moving toward Shantou. All units were asked to follow the Shantou University Typhoon Prevention Plan, take precautions against wind and heavy rain, halt outdoor activities and prevent falling objects from injuring pedestrians. If the typhoon warning is not lifted by the next morning, classes will continue to be suspended.

## Duty and Inspection Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Decision maker | University Flood Control Headquarters |
| Suspension | Afternoon of September 22, 2013, all classes suspended |
| Duty requirement | Headquarters, reservoir, power supply, campus security and hospital staff remain on 24-hour duty; emergency teams and duty personnel of all units stay at their posts |
| Communication | Use walkie-talkies, mobile phones and other devices to respond to emergencies at any time |
| Key inspections | Reservoirs, dams and flood drainage channels, among other key sites |
| Follow-up | Flood-control emergency plan to be activated if necessary |
| Issued by | Flood Control Headquarters, Resource Management Office |

Units are asked to notify faculty and students to take precautions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office, Flood Control Headquarters)
