It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Young Volunteers Association released on September 22 the service-hour records of orientation volunteers recruited directly by the committee for 2013, asking them to check the figures carefully and to report any errors before 10:00 am on September 25.

During the 2013 orientation, volunteers recruited directly by the Youth League Committee took part in various welcome services, and their service hours have now been tallied and published along with the notice (see attachment). Volunteers should check their own records item by item. Anyone who finds an error should report it to the Volunteer Service Hour Management Group of the STU Young Volunteers Association before the deadline. Staff will verify the feedback and correct any genuine mistakes without delay.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
