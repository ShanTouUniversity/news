It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued the Decision on Commending Outstanding Groups and Individuals in the 2013 Student Military Training (Student Affairs Office Document No. 7 [2013]). The university's military training leading group decided to commend the groups and individuals that achieved outstanding results in the various military training assessments.

Following a rigorous selection by the **2013 STU Student Military Training Regiment**, a total of **nine outstanding classes**, **16 outstanding instructors**, **59 outstanding individuals** and **14 sharpshooters** were named.
