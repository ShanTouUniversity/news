It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has started the certification of financially disadvantaged undergraduates for the 2013-14 academic year. The work is carried out under the Ministry of Education and Ministry of Finance guidance on certifying financially disadvantaged students at higher education institutions (Jiao Cai [2007] No. 8) and the university's own trial measures for such certification (Shan Da Fa [2010] No. 70).

Under the arrangements, each college (residential college) will set up a certification working group and guide each class (year group, Youth League branch) in forming a review panel; panel members are to be registered in the student affairs system. Students who wish to apply must submit their applications through the student affairs system (https://sao.stu.edu.cn) by October 9 and hand in the STU Student Family Financial Situation Questionnaire and other supporting documents to their review panel. Those already certified whose family circumstances have not changed significantly need only submit the online application this time.

From October 10 to 18, class-level review panels will examine the applications, decide the list of certified students and their grades, enter the results into the system, and submit a review summary report to their college. From October 19 to 28, college certification working groups will verify the results, publicize them among students, confirm them in the system, and send a summary report and a report on the public notice results to the Financial Aid Management Center.

The office said students with questions can email zjliu@stu.edu.cn or call the Financial Aid Management Center at 86504749 (contact: Mr. Liu).
