It is reported that, following the suspension of all classes on the afternoon of September 22, the "Three Defences" Command of Shantou University issued another notice the same day, deciding to keep classes suspended on the morning of September 23 because waterlogging and fallen trees have blocked roads across the city. All units were also asked to arrange duty for faculty and staff who live on campus.

Shantou University had suspended all classes from the afternoon of September 22 as Typhoon Usagi approached, as previously reported by STU News. With flooding and toppled trees hindering traffic on many city roads after the typhoon, the university decided to extend the suspension and reminded faculty and students to travel safely and to stay away from trees, billboards and temporary structures.
