It is reported that the 268th lecture in Shantou University Law School's lecture series will be held from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on September 26** in the lecture hall of the STU Library. Professor Xu Songlin, executive dean of the School of Law at South China University of Technology, has been invited to deliver the talk, titled "Issues Concerning the Crime of Theft — with a Discussion of the Boundaries Between Several Common Crimes."

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Issues Concerning the Crime of Theft — with a Discussion of the Boundaries Between Several Common Crimes |
| Speaker | Professor Xu Songlin (Executive Dean, School of Law, South China University of Technology) |
| Time | 3:00–5:00 p.m., Thursday, September 26, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, STU Library |

According to the organizers, Xu Songlin is the executive dean of the School of Law at South China University of Technology and a Grade-III professor. A well-known scholar of criminal law, he serves as vice president of the Guangdong Criminal Law Society, the Guangdong Procuratorial Society and the Guangdong Juvenile Delinquency Research Society, is an adjunct professor at HKU SPACE, and heads the national-level excellent course "Criminal Law."

The lecture will focus on the identification and application of the crime of theft, and will also discuss the boundaries between several common crimes, helping faculty and students clarify how related charges are distinguished. The lecture is open to all faculty and students.
