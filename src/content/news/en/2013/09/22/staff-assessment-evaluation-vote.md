It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University recently announced that, in line with the university's performance assessment arrangements for faculty and staff, the assessment for the 2012-2013 academic year now includes a new stage — a democratic evaluation of personnel assessed directly by university leaders.

## How and When to Participate

The evaluation will be conducted through an online voting system, which is open to all on-campus faculty and staff members. Participants are asked to log in to the designated voting system, cast their votes, and provide written evaluation comments.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluation targets | Personnel assessed by university leaders |
| Eligible participants | On-campus faculty and staff |
| Method | Online voting with written comments via the voting system |
| Deadline | 5:30 PM, September 26 |

According to the Human Resources Office, this democratic evaluation forms part of this year's performance assessment process, and staff members are encouraged to complete their votes and comments before the deadline.
