It is reported that Chen Weizhou, a teacher at the Institute of Marine Biology, was on a business trip to Nan'ao and was unable to return to the university because of a typhoon, so his course, Introduction to Seaweed Resources (class number 62789), will be suspended.

The suspension falls on Monday of week 2, which is September 23, 2013, and covers periods 3 and 4 of the course, normally held in Room E306. The Academic Affairs Office said the make-up session has yet to be scheduled and asked students taking the course to inform one another and watch for further arrangements.
