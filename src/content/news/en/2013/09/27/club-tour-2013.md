It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Societies Federation issued a notice on September 27, 2013, to hold the “2013 Club Tour” on the evening of September 28.

To enrich extracurricular life, promote campus culture and showcase student societies, the event will be held from 19:30 to 22:00 on September 28, 2013 (Saturday) at the Pedestrian Street and Basketball Courts, where societies will conduct recruitment. All faculty and students are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Student Societies Federation)
