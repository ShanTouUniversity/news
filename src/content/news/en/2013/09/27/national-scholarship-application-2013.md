It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 27, 2013, setting out arrangements for the assessment of the 2013 National Scholarship, National Inspirational Scholarship and National Grant, specifying the eligible recipients, award amounts, application procedures and review process for the three programmes.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| National Scholarship | 8,000 yuan per student; for full-time undergraduate students in or above the second year who excel in both character and academic performance |
| National Inspirational Scholarship | 5,000 yuan per student; for full-time undergraduate students in or above the second year who are from financially disadvantaged families and excel in both character and academic performance |
| National Grant | 3,000 yuan per student; for full-time undergraduate students with financial difficulties |
| Online application deadline | Before 5:00 pm on Thursday, October 10, 2013 |
| Submission of materials | Hand in to the counselor of your college (residential college) before the end of the workday on October 10; medical college students submit to the Medical College Student Affairs Department |
| College submissions | National Scholarship materials to the Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center) by the end of the workday on October 18; National Inspirational Scholarship and National Grant materials by October 31 |
| Enquiry emails | xyli@stu.edu.cn (online application issues); zjliu@stu.edu.cn (electronic documents from colleges) |

## Programme Highlights

**National Scholarship**

- For full-time undergraduate students in or above the second year who excel in both character and academic performance; students in special programmes such as combined bachelor's–master's or master's–doctoral study are, in principle, no longer eligible from the sixth year of enrolment.
- The award is 8,000 yuan per student.
- Applicants should, in principle, rank in the top 10% academically and must have no failed courses.
- Applicants must write a personal record: written in the third person, titled “\*\*\*\* Personal Record”, with the title in Song typeface, size 3, bold, and the body in FangSong typeface, size 4, single-spaced, about 1,500 characters, printed double-sided on A4 paper.

**National Inspirational Scholarship**

- For full-time undergraduate students in or above the second year who are from financially disadvantaged families and excel in both character and academic performance.
- The award is 5,000 yuan per student.

**National Grant**

- For full-time undergraduate students with financial difficulties.
- The grant is 3,000 yuan per student.

The National Scholarship, National Inspirational Scholarship and National Grant may not be held concurrently with the Outstanding Student Scholarship or enterprise scholarships. Students who encounter problems with the online application may email xyli@stu.edu.cn for advice.

## Application and Assessment Procedure

Applicants should log in to the STU scholarship website (http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn/chinese/applyIntro2.aspx) to apply online before 5:00 pm on Thursday, October 10, and print double-sided the National Scholarship application and approval form and the National Inspirational Scholarship application and approval form, and print the National Grant application form online; signatures must be made with a black pen and no alterations are allowed. These materials should be handed to the counselor of the applicant's college (residential college) before the end of the workday on October 10; medical college students submit them to the Medical College Student Affairs Department.

## College Review Arrangements

Following the *Guide to National Scholarship Work in Regular Higher Education Institutions* and the *Shantou University Guidelines for National Scholarship Selection*, each college (residential college) sets up a selection committee composed of college leaders, faculty representatives, counselors and student representatives, strictly applies the review standards and standardizes the selection process, and determines National Scholarship recipients through interviews. The interview results are confirmed by the signatures of the selection committee and publicized within the college for 5 working days. Before the end of the workday on October 18, the college submits paper materials to the Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center), namely the recommended candidates and preliminary list for the National Scholarship, the National Scholarship application and approval forms, and the National Scholarship selection report, with electronic documents sent to zjliu@stu.edu.cn; after university review, the results are reported to the provincial Department of Education.

Recommended candidates for the National Inspirational Scholarship and the National Grant should, in principle, have passed the financial hardship certification; where candidates applied for the certification but were not approved because of quota limits or other reasons, each college (residential college) must submit a unified report explaining the circumstances. Each college (residential college) formulates detailed selection rules under the *Interim Measures for the Management of the National Inspirational Scholarship* and the *Interim Measures for the Management of the National Grant*, and submits the preliminary lists for the National Inspirational Scholarship and the National Grant, the public notice result reports and other paper materials to the Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center) by October 31, with electronic materials sent to zjliu@stu.edu.cn.
