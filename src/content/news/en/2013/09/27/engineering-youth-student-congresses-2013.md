It is reported that, with approval from the university Youth League committee and the college Party general branch, the College of Engineering of Shantou University plans to hold its 11th Youth League Congress and 20th Student Congress at **7:30 p.m. on Sunday, Sept 29, 2013**, in **tiered lecture hall E101**. A total of 108 student delegates will attend, calculated at 10 percent of the college's undergraduate population.

### Congress information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Congress name | 11th Youth League Congress and 20th Student Congress, College of Engineering |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., Sunday, Sept 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Tiered lecture hall E101 |
| Main agenda | Review the work of the 10th Youth League committee and the 19th Student Union since their establishment; elect the 11th Youth League committee members and the 20th Student Union executive committee members |
| Delegates | 108 students, 10 percent of the college's undergraduate population |

## Tasks of the congresses

The congresses will review and summarize the work of the college's 10th Youth League committee and 19th Student Union executive committee since their establishment, elect the 11th committee members of the Communist Youth League of China at Shantou University's College of Engineering and the executive committee members of the college's 20th Student Union, and set out future work tasks and approaches. The aim is for the Youth League committee and the Student Union to better serve as a bridge between students and the college and to fulfill their functions of self-education, self-management and self-service.

## Delegates and selection

Under the arrangements, the elected delegates will serve both as delegates to the Youth League Congress and as delegates to the Student Congress. Delegates must be members of the Communist Youth League of China, probationary members or full members of the Communist Party of China in the College of Engineering. They must uphold the Four Cardinal Principles, hold firm political positions, perform well in both academic study and personal conduct, maintain close ties with fellow students, play an exemplary role in their own duties, and possess a certain degree of representativeness and the ability to deliberate. Classes will elect delegates through class-wide student meetings under the principle of democratic centralism.

The congresses are being organized by the preparatory group for the 11th Youth League Congress and 20th Student Congress of the College of Engineering; the notice is dated Sept 27, 2013.
