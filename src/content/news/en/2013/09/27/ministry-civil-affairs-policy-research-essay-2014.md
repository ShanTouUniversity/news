It is reported that the Ministry of Civil Affairs has launched a call for 2014 civil affairs policy research essays, inviting policy research papers from civil affairs departments nationwide and from all sectors of society, and has released a list of general essay topics. The call aims to strengthen theoretical research on civil affairs policy, study major theoretical and practical issues in depth, and provide new ideas, measures and insights for the sound development of civil affairs. The STU Research Office has forwarded the relevant notice.

## General Essay Topics

The Ministry of Civil Affairs released 26 general essay topics:

1. Research on the historical evolution and development trends of civil affairs
2. Research on improving policies to support and guide social organizations
3. Research on mechanisms for ensuring job placement for demobilized soldiers assigned work by the government
4. Research on the reform and adjustment of preferential treatment and support policies
5. Research on the management system and mechanisms for resettling retired military cadres
6. Research on disaster reduction and relief coordination mechanisms
7. Research on the rural housing insurance system
8. Research on the standardized management of social assistance
9. Research on the interface between social assistance and other social security and charity efforts
10. Research on improving grassroots social governance mechanisms
11. Research on developing urban and rural community service systems
12. Research on optimizing the establishment of urban-type administrative divisions in the process of new urbanization
13. Research on improving the public service system for place names
14. Research on administrative boundary lines and the management and use of resources in border areas
15. Research on developing a moderately universal social welfare system
16. Research on the development system, policies and environment for charity
17. Research on the social protection system for minors
18. Research on improving the child adoption system
19. Research on the implementation of preferential and supportive policies for privately run elderly care institutions
20. Research on the reform and improvement of the marriage registration system
21. Research on policy coordination and service systems for cross-strait marriage and family
22. Research on building a funeral and interment management and service system compatible with ecological civilization
23. Research on the development, setup and incentive policies of professional social work positions
24. Research on capacity-building standards for professional social work talent
25. Research on the professionalization and career development of the civil affairs workforce
26. Research on the construction of civil affairs public service facilities

## Research Requirements

Applicants may choose a research direction on their own, or define a specific topic within the directions above, and conduct their research independently. Papers should present clear viewpoints and substantial content in a rigorous, standardized style, and must be no fewer than 5,000 Chinese characters. The format is as follows: the title in SimSun 16 pt, centered; section headings and body text in SimSun 14 pt with a fixed line spacing of 25 pt; footnotes at the bottom of pages; and references in SimSun 10.5 pt listed after the main text.

Papers must be unpublished, and should be original, pioneering and practical; papers that have already won awards are not eligible. Manuscripts must state the author's name, work unit, mailing address, telephone number and email address.

## Submission and Review

General papers may be submitted directly to the Ministry of Civil Affairs at mzb58123794@sina.com (electronic text only) by May 31, 2014, with "2014 Civil Affairs Theoretical Research Essay" marked in the email subject line. Alternatively, papers may be sent to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn by April 28, 2014, for the university to collect and forward to the ministry.

General papers will be reviewed anonymously by experts organized by the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Ten first prizes, 30 second prizes and 40 third prizes will be awarded, with certificates issued to the winners. The contacts for the call are Liu Lijuan and Yao Chuanming, telephone 010-58123794 and 010-58123793.
