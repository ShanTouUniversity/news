It is reported that the Network and Information Center issued a notice on September 27, 2013, announcing that the campus card service windows will be closed during the National Day holiday.

According to the notice, the campus card windows at the Expert Apartments and on the ground floor of the Second Canteen will be closed from October 1 to October 7, 2013, and will resume service on October 8. During the holiday, faculty and students can top up their cards using the self-service transfer and balance inquiry machines, while card loss reporting and balance inquiries are available through the automated voice inquiry hotline at 82903414.

For any other inquiries during the holiday, the Network and Information Center can be reached via the hotline at 86503420 or by email at support@stu.edu.cn.
