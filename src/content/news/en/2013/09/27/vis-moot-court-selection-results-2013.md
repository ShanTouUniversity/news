It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University released on September 27 the list of students selected for the Willem C. Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot. Following interviews and assessment by the school's selection panel, 14 students were chosen to form the team for the competition.

The Willem C. Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot is a widely influential moot arbitration event in the field of international commercial arbitration. Set against the rules and practice of international commercial arbitration, it tests participants' legal analysis, written advocacy and oral pleading skills. The selection was organized by the Law School and assessed applicants through interviews.
