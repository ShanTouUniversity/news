It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on September 27, 2013, announcing that, in accordance with the *Shantou University Measures for the Selection of Outstanding Graduate Students*, the university is currently carrying out the selection of outstanding graduate students and asked all graduate training units to do a good job in the review and recommendation work.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | Full-time master's and doctoral students enrolled at STU with registered student status |
| Awards | First Prize and Second Prize for Outstanding Graduate Students |
| Recommendation quota | Each college recommends no more than 5% of its candidates |
| Submission deadlines | Applicants submit materials to their college by October 8; colleges complete review and recommendation by October 13; colleges submit materials to the Graduate School by October 18 |
| Public notice and commendation | The Graduate School reviews materials from October 21 to 25; the candidate list is publicized and commendations issued from October 28 to 31 |

## Scope and Eligibility

All full-time master's and doctoral students enrolled at STU with registered student status may take part; a student may participate only once during master's studies and once during doctoral studies respectively. Students who received a disciplinary sanction for violating university rules in the academic year, or who failed a course in the academic year (a pass for degree courses is 70 or above), are not eligible for that year's selection.

**First Prize for Outstanding Graduate Students**

- Love the motherland, observe laws and regulations, respect teachers, unite with fellow students, care about the collective and be ready to help others;
- Study hard with excellent results, with an average score of no less than 85 and no individual course score below 80;
- For non-arts-and-sports graduate students applying for the First Prize, doctoral students must have published at least 3 papers in core journals of their field during doctoral studies (excluding the period of master's studies), and master's students at least 1 paper in a core journal of their field during master's studies; the papers must be first-authored, or, if the applicant is the second author, the first author must be their supervisor;
- For arts-and-sports master's students applying for the First Prize who do not meet the required number of publications, provincial-level or higher awards may serve as a substitute, that is, at least 1 provincial-level or higher award for creation, performance, competition or similar during master's studies.

**Second Prize for Outstanding Graduate Students**

- An average score of no less than 80 and no individual course score below 75 (may be relaxed appropriately for those with particularly outstanding research results);
- Publication of at least 1 research paper in an important journal of the field (as first author, or with the supervisor as first author and the graduate student as second author), or, though not yet published, the paper's academic conclusions or experimental results have been appraised or adopted by the relevant authorities and have relatively high academic value and good social or economic benefits, or the applicant has won a provincial-level or higher award;
- Students who have served as student cadres in the Youth League Committee of the Graduate School, the Graduate Student Union, their class or other university-level student organizations (for one year or one term or more) with fairly outstanding achievements are given priority under equal conditions.

## Selection Schedule and Materials

- **By October 8**: Applicants submit to their college (graduate training unit) one *Shantou University Outstanding Graduate Student Review Form* (with an electronic version), one set of copies of research results (copies of research projects undertaken, appraisal certificates and award certificates, and the cover, table of contents and full text of published papers), and one transcript of the recommended candidate; applications that do not meet the requirements or are submitted late will not be accepted.
- **By October 13**: Each college completes its review and recommendation, with a review panel organized by the unit recommending candidates within a quota of no more than 5% of the number of participants.
- **By October 18**: After publicizing the review results for 3 working days, each college submits to the Graduate School one review form, one set of copies of research results and one transcript, together with one *Shantou University Outstanding Graduate Student Recommendation Summary Form* (with the college's official seal, an electronic version, and the recommended names ranked in order of priority).
- **October 21 to 25**: The Graduate School reviews the materials of the candidates recommended by each college and, as needed, engages experts to form a review panel for assessment, finally determining this year's outstanding graduate students and their awards.
- **October 28 to 31**: The Graduate School publicizes the candidate list and issues commendations.

> ⚠️ Attachments such as the review form and the recommendation summary form are available on the OA system.
