It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 27, announcing that the university will hold the first workshop on integrated thinking training for student activities on the morning of September 29 (Sunday).

## Workshop Details

The workshop will run from 9:00 to 11:30 on September 29 in Meeting Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Service Center. Against the backdrop of STU's efforts to advance integrated thinking education, the session will offer discussions and training on how integrated thinking can be applied to student activities.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Workshop on Integrated Thinking Training for Student Activities (Session 1) |
| Time | 9:00–11:30, Sunday, September 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 5, Academic Exchange Service Center |
| Content | Discussions and training on applying integrated thinking to student activities in the context of STU's integrated thinking initiative |
| Participants | Faculty members in charge of the relevant projects |

## Attendance Arrangements

According to the notice, faculty members in charge of the relevant projects are required to arrange their work in advance and attend the meeting on time.
