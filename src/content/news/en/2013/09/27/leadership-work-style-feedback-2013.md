It is reported that, as part of the university's education practice activity, the Education Practice Activity Leading Group Office issued a notice on September 27, seeking further comments and suggestions from all university units and members of the university community on the Ledger of Feedback Summary and Rectification on Work Style Issues of the STU Leadership Team.

## Public Consultation Arrangements

According to the notice, comments and suggestions from units and individuals should be submitted to the secretariat of the Education Practice Activity Leading Group Office by October 11, with o_zzb@stu.edu.cn as the designated email address.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Document for consultation | Ledger of Feedback Summary and Rectification on Work Style Issues of the STU Leadership Team |
| Consultation target | All university units, faculty, staff and students |
| Feedback deadline | Before October 11, 2013 |
| Feedback email | o_zzb@stu.edu.cn |
| Issuing body | Education Practice Activity Leading Group Office |

> ⚠️ The Ledger of Feedback Summary and Rectification on Work Style Issues of the STU Leadership Team is attached to the OA system notice and is not reproduced on this news page.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee)
