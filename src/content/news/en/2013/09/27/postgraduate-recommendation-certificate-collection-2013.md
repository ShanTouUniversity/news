It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on September 27, 2013, stating that students who have obtained the qualification for recommendation-based admission, without sitting the entrance examination, to master’s degree programmes as outstanding undergraduate graduates of the Class of 2014 may collect their qualification certificates at the Academic Affairs Office service window.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | Students who have obtained the qualification for recommendation-based admission to master’s degree programmes as outstanding undergraduate graduates of the Class of 2014 |
| Document to collect | Qualification certificate |
| Collection location | Academic Affairs Office service window |
| Issuing office | Academic Affairs Office |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
