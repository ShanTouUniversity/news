It is reported that, following review and confirmation by the Teacher Continuing Education Guidance Center of the Guangdong Provincial Education Department, the certificates for the **Education** and **Psychology** course training for teacher certification taken by non-teacher-education students in the **spring 2013 semester** have been delivered to Shantou University. The Academic Affairs Office has asked students who passed the training to collect their certificates at the designated service window.

### Collection details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate | Certificate for the Education and Psychology course training for teacher certification |
| Eligible recipients | Non-teacher-education students who took the two-subject training in the spring 2013 semester and passed |
| Collection location | Academic Affairs service window, third floor, New Administration Center |
| Required documents | Personal collection: the recipient's valid identity document; collection by proxy: valid identity documents for both the proxy and the certificate holder |
| Issuing office | Academic Affairs Office, Shantou University |

The Academic Affairs Office reminded recipients to present their own valid identity document when collecting a certificate, and to present valid identity documents for both the proxy and the certificate holder when collecting on someone else's behalf. The office also asked students who took the training and passed to share the news with one another.

> ⚠️ The list of students who passed is available in the OA notice attachment and is not reproduced on this news page.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
