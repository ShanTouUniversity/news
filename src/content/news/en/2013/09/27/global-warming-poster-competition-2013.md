It is reported that the Global Warming-themed public welfare poster design competition hosted by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will close for submissions on October 10. The organizing team has already received entries from students and has invited several outstanding off-campus designers to contribute exhibition works.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | Global Warming public welfare poster design |
| Submission deadline | October 10, 2013 |
| Official website | http://www.ckad.stu.edu.cn/qqbn2013 |
| Official Weibo | @2013全球变暖海报设计比赛 |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

The submission channel remains open, and students interested in participating are encouraged to submit promptly. Detailed requirements and submission guidelines are available on the official competition website.
