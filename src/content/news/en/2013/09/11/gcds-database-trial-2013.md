It is reported that the Shantou University Library has opened trial access to the Global Cases Discovery System (GCDS), which will remain available until December 10, 2013. Faculty and students can reach the system through the library's database details page.

GCDS is the first specialized discovery system in the field of case studies. It serves users engaged in case development and case teaching by offering one-stop retrieval and delivery of case literature. The system comprises three databases — the Tsinghua SEM Teaching Case Full-text Database, the Chinese Case Materials Database and Global Cases Online — which provide full texts of cases, case materials and case indexes respectively.

## Trial Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Database | Global Cases Discovery System (GCDS) |
| Component databases | Tsinghua SEM Teaching Case Full-text Database; Chinese Case Materials Database; Global Cases Online |
| Access link | http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=377 |
| Trial deadline | December 10, 2013 |
| Contact | Library Subject Services Division (Tel: 86503247; Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn) |

The Library noted that during the trial period users can browse but not download documents, while downloading is available to full subscribers. Teaching notes in the Tsinghua SEM Teaching Case Full-text Database are open only to faculty members, who need to register and pass a review; use of the favorites function likewise requires registration and approval.

Users with comments or suggestions are invited to contact the Library Subject Services Division.
