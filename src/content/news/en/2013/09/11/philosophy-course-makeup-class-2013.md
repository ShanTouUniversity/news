It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 11, 2013, announcing the make-up arrangement for the *Philosophy of Life* course. One session had been suspended earlier because instructor Ma Fengqi of the Institute of Higher Education was away attending a conference, and the time and venue for the make-up class have now been set.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Philosophy of Life* (class number 63667) |
| Instructor | Ma Fengqi (Institute of Higher Education) |
| Suspended session | Periods ABC, Tuesday of week 1 (August 20) of the summer session |
| Make-up session | Periods 6-8, Saturday of week 4 (September 14) of the summer session |
| Venue | Room E-303 |

Students taking the course are asked to inform one another and to attend the class as rescheduled.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
