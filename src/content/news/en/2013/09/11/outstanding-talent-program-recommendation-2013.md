It is reported that Shantou University is organizing nominations for the third batch of candidates under the "Hundred Eminent Talents of Guangdong" development program. Electronic copies of application materials should be sent to the Human Resources Office at `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn`, while the eligibility criteria, application forms and specific procedures are detailed in the four attachments to the notice (not reproduced here).
