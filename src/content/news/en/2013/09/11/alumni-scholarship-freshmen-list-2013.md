It is reported that, to carry forward the alumni spirit advocated by Mr. Li Ka-shing — "striving for self-improvement, working together in solidarity, giving back to the alma mater, and serving the nation" — caring alumni donated funds to establish the 2013 Alumni Scholarship, which supports Shantou University students in financial need, including 15 freshmen.

Following review and recommendation by the university's colleges (residential colleges) and verification by the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Affairs Office, Shantou University has decided to grant the scholarship to 15 freshmen, with each recipient receiving RMB 2,500.

## Award Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | 2013 Alumni Scholarship (freshmen) |
| Recipients | 15 |
| Amount | RMB 2,500 per person |
| Funding source | Donations from caring alumni |
| Selection process | Review and recommendation by colleges (residential colleges) → verification by the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Affairs Office → decision by the university |

The university said that it hopes the recipients will study diligently and strive to become talented people who are ambitious, knowledgeable, persistent, and capable of action.

The list of recipients is provided in the attachment to the OA notice.
