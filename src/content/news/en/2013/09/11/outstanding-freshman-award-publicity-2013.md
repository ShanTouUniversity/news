It is reported that Shantou University has completed the 2013 selection under its Outstanding Freshman Award Program in accordance with the Rules of the Outstanding Freshman Award Program. Following applications submitted by the students themselves and interviews conducted by the selection committee, the university proposes to award first-class scholarships to five students and second-class scholarships to 25 students.

In addition, in line with the regulations of the Outstanding Female Freshman from Other Provinces Award Program and this year's admissions situation, the university proposes to grant scholarships donated by enterprises and individuals to 25 students.

## Proposed Awards

| Award | Proposed recipients |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Freshman First-Class Scholarship | 5 |
| Outstanding Freshman Second-Class Scholarship | 25 |
| Enterprise- and individual-donated scholarships | 25 |

## Publicity and Objections

The proposed list is open to public scrutiny. Any unit or individual with objections may submit written materials to the Student Financial Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office (Office 106, Administration Center) before 5:00 p.m. on September 11 (Wednesday).

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Receiving office | Student Financial Aid Management Center, Student Affairs Office (Office 106, Administration Center) |
| Deadline | 5:00 p.m., September 11, 2013 (Wednesday) |
| Form of submission | Written materials; units must affix an official seal, and individuals must sign their real names |

The full proposed list is provided in the attachment to the OA notice.
