It is reported that the English-major oral test office of the National Foreign Language Teaching Advisory Committee for Higher Education Institutions has issued arrangements for the 2014 TEM-8 Oral and Interpreting Test, and the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has notified relevant units on campus about the registration details. The test is scheduled for Saturday, December 7, 2013, starting at 8:30 a.m.

According to the arrangements, registration is open to English majors enrolled in 2010, and the fee is 90 yuan per person. Candidates must complete their registration online through the university's credit system portal within the specified period, which runs from September 12 to 21, 2013.

Payment is handled by the Finance Office, which has entrusted the Network Center to make a single deduction from applicants' campus card accounts on September 22. Candidates must make sure their card balance is sufficient beforehand. The university noted that a registration will be considered unsuccessful if the deduction fails.
