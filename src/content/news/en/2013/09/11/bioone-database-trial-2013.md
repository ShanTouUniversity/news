It is reported that the Shantou University Library has opened trial access to the BioOne bioscience journal database, which will remain available until December 8, 2013.

Founded in 1999, BioOne is a non-profit organization sponsored by a number of renowned universities and jointly formed by learned societies and university presses. It is an alliance that integrates high-impact journals in the biosciences, with subject coverage spanning agriculture, biology, biochemistry, ecology, environmental science, entomology, evolutionary biology, genetics, marine and freshwater biology, microbiology, botany, veterinary science and zoology.

The database contains 176 high-quality bioscience journals from 132 societies and non-profit publishers. Of these, 72% are listed in the ISI-JCR reports and more than 90% are peer-reviewed journals, with only 0.2% overlap with ASP. It includes more than 100,000 articles spanning over 890,000 pages, with content dating back to 1995; current issues are updated daily, and 100% of the content is provided in full text.
