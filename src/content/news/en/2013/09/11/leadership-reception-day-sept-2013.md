It is reported that Shantou University's leadership reception day this week is scheduled for 3:00 p.m. on Thursday, September 12, 2013, when Vice President Wulan Hasi will be on duty to meet visiting faculty and students.

The arrangements released by the General Administration Office are as follows:

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Leader on Duty | Vice President Wulan Hasi |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., Thursday, September 12, 2013 |
| Appointment Phone | 86502306 |
| Note | Please make an appointment in advance |

The General Administration Office reminded faculty and students who wish to visit to make an appointment by phone in advance.
