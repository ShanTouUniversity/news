It is reported that the Line Dance Club at Shantou University is recruiting members from among the 2013 freshmen for the autumn semester of the 2013—2014 academic year, with six places reserved. Places are allocated in order of registration on a first-come, first-served basis and will be closed once filled.

According to the club's arrangements, registration is only considered successful after the applicant receives a confirmation text message in reply; if no confirmation message is received, registration has already closed. The registration deadline is 17:00 on September 15. After registration closes, the club will release further notices through its "Shantou University Line Dance Club" Weibo account, where applicants can check their results.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target | 2013 freshmen |
| Reserved places | 6 |
| Selection | First come, first served |
| Registration deadline | 17:00, September 15 |
| Class time | Periods 8 and 9 every Tuesday |

The club notes that because classes are held during these periods, participants cannot enrol in other courses for periods 8 and 9 on Tuesdays. Follow-up notices will be published on the "Shantou University Line Dance Club" Weibo account.
