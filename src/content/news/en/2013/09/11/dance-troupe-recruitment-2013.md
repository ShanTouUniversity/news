Shantou University's Dance Troupe has officially opened recruitment to students from across the university. According to the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center, there are no restrictions on gender, year of study or major, and students with no dance background are also welcome to apply.

## Recruitment Arrangements

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-up and consultation session | Noon, Sept 12 (Thursday), Golden Triangle |
| Audition | 9:00 a.m., Sept 14 (Saturday), Dance Rehearsal Hall, Arts Education Center |
| Who can apply | All STU students, male and female, any year or major; no dance experience required |

Students interested in joining may visit the Golden Triangle at noon on Sept 12 to learn about the troupe and ask questions on site, and those who sign up are required to attend the audition at the Dance Rehearsal Hall of the Arts Education Center at 9:00 a.m. on Sept 14.

The troupe says students of all years and majors are welcome to apply, with further details set out in the attachment.
