It is reported that Shantou University's Law School will arrange internships at courts in Dongguan and Huizhou for students of its 2010 law cohort in two groups, departing on 23 September and 8 October respectively. The host courts will provide accommodation for the participating students.

## Group Arrangements

The internships are divided into two groups, covering four courts across Dongguan and Huizhou.

| Group | Host courts | Period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | Dongguan Intermediate People's Court; Dongguan No. 1 People's Court | 23 September to 17 November |
| Group 2 | Dongguan No. 2 People's Court; Huizhou Intermediate People's Court | 8 October to 1 December |

## How to Apply

Interested students must complete the application form and email it to the Law School at `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn`, using the subject line "10法学+姓名+（地区）实习" (Class of 2010 Law + Name + (Region) Internship), with applicants filling in their own name in place of "Name". Applications close on 16 September 2013.

Students who have already selected courses for the current semester cannot take part in the internships. Those who wish to join must first move the relevant courses to the following semester.

> ⚠️ The application form for the law teaching internship is available in the original attachment on the OA system.
