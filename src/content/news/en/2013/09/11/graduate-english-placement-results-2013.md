It is reported that the Graduate School and the English Language Center of Shantou University have released the results of the English placement test for 2013 doctoral and master's students at the main campus. Students may purchase textbooks according to their assigned level. As the list contains personal student information, this article does not reproduce it.

## Textbook Reference

| Level | Textbook | Publisher |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graduate Level 1 | Global English Course 3 (Student's Book) | Shanghai Foreign Language Education Press |
| Graduate Level 2 | Global English Course 4 (Student's Book) | Shanghai Foreign Language Education Press |

Students are reminded to purchase the textbooks for their assigned level on their own.

> ⚠️ The full placement list is available in the original attachment on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School, English Language Center)
