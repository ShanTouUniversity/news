It is reported that, responding to requests from Pilates enthusiasts on campus, the staff Pilates class at Shantou University will continue this semester. Classes are held every Monday, Wednesday and Friday, with different time slots for each group: the Monday and Wednesday groups meet from 18:00 to 19:00, while the Friday group meets from 12:40 to 13:40.

Because the class times for some groups have changed this semester, returning members were asked to confirm by September 13 whether they would continue and which session they would attend. Those who did not confirm before 17:30 on September 13 are regarded as not participating, and the places thus freed will be offered to new members. The organizers noted that places are limited and will be filled on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Class Schedule

| Group | Class Time | First Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday group | Every Monday, 18:00—19:00 | September 16 |
| Wednesday group | Every Wednesday, 18:00—19:00 | September 18 |
| Friday group | Every Friday, 12:40—13:40 | September 22 |

Participants are required to bring their own yoga mats and wear loose-fitting clothing. Classes are suspended automatically on public holidays, with no separate notice issued.
