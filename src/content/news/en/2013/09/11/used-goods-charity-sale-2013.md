It is reported that Shantou University will hold a used-goods charity sale under the theme "Love Continues Through Used Goods" in mid-September 2013, aiming to improve resource utilization, promote the reuse of useful items, and strengthen students' environmental awareness. The event is jointly initiated by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the STU Young Volunteers Association, and the "Green Ribbon" service-learning environmental volunteer team.

## Event Details

The sale booths will be set up at the Golden Triangle on campus, selling items collected during the used-goods recycling campaign, including old books and used electrical appliances. All proceeds will be donated to the Firefly Charity Foundation.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Love Continues Through Used Goods |
| Dates | September 14–15, 2013 |
| Venue | Golden Triangle |
| Items for Sale | Recycled old books, used appliances, etc. |
| Proceeds | Donated in full to the Firefly Charity Foundation |

Each day's sale is divided into several time slots (8:00–11:30, 11:30–14:30, 14:30–17:30, 17:30–19:30), making it convenient for staff and students to shop at different times.

## Volunteer Recruitment

The event also recruits 25 volunteers on a first-come, first-served basis. Service hours will be recorded according to actual participation after the event. Interested students should download the registration form, complete it, and send it to the designated email before 12:00 on September 13, or sign up by text message; the specific registration email and phone number are available in the attachments of the original OA notice.

The organizers warmly invite all faculty and students to take part in this event that combines environmental protection with public welfare.
