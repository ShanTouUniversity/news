It is reported that the 14th academic lecture of the Shantou University Business School in 2013 will be held from 16:00 to 17:30 on Monday, September 16, 2013, in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of the Wenxi Building. Dr. Zhu Jianqi of National Sun Yat-sen University in Taiwan has been invited as the speaker. His topic is "A Study of Comparative Institutional Economics on Shareholder Rights Protection under Globalization," and Associate Professor Ji Yun will chair the lecture.

### Lecture Overview

According to the organizers, the lecture will explore how the shareholder-protection function of corporate law affects the market for corporate control. The study takes as its comparative empirical basis the "Anti-Director Rights Index (ADRI)" proposed by La Porta, Lopez-de-Silanes, Shleifer and Vishny (LLSV), and the "Shareholder Protection Index (SPI)" developed by John Armour and colleagues in the United Kingdom. It reviews the features and limitations of LLSV's empirical evaluation of corporate law across legal families and tests the findings with data from the stock market and the mergers and acquisitions market. Drawing on revision suggestions by Harvard scholar Spamann and the SPI coding methodology, the research also codes the company laws of mainland China and Taiwan to address the shortage of samples in international datasets.

### About the Speaker

Dr. Zhu Jianqi is from Taiwan. He holds a PhD in business administration with a focus on finance and law from National Sun Yat-sen University in Taiwan, and currently serves as an adjunct assistant professor at Tunghai University in Taiwan and as a consultant to law firms and consulting companies. He previously worked as a senior management specialist at a solar energy company and has undertaken short-term research and exchanges at the University of Washington in the United States and the University of Victoria in Canada. He is now collaborating with a corporate governance team at the University of Cambridge and has published several papers.
