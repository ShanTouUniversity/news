It is reported that, according to a notice from the infrastructure department, Shantou University will upgrade the timer-control system for the power supply in student dormitories. Student dormitory buildings A to F will have a power outage during the construction work. The Student Community Management and Service Center issued a reminder about the arrangements on Sept 11.

## Outage Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Affected area | Student dormitory buildings A to F |
| Time | 9:00 a.m. – 12:00 noon, Sept 12, 2013 |
| Reason | Construction to upgrade the timer-control system for dormitory power supply |

The Student Community Management and Service Center advises students living in the affected buildings to switch off electrical appliances ahead of the outage so as to avoid damage to equipment or other accidents when power is cut off or restored.
