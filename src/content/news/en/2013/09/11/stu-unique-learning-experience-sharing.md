It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold the "A Different Learning Experience" sharing session from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on Thursday, September 12, 2013, at the Library Lecture Hall. Two recipients of the 2012 Semester at Sea scholarship — Jin XY and Lin JQ — were invited to share their university learning experiences with freshmen of the Class of 2013.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | "A Different Learning Experience" sharing session |
| Time | September 12, 2013 (Thursday), 19:30-21:00 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |
| Speakers | Jin XY and Lin JQ, 2012 Semester at Sea scholarship recipients |

The Semester at Sea program is a distinctive STU initiative in which students spend a semester studying aboard a ship overseas. At the session, the two scholarship recipients will recount their application journey and life aboard, and interact with freshmen on topics such as study methods and time management.

The university welcomed active participation from the Class of 2013, hoping that the seniors' first-hand stories would help new students adapt quickly to campus life and plan their academic paths.
