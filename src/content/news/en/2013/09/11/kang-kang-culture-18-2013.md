It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold the 18th session of the "Culture Dialogue" series at **7:30 p.m. on Wednesday, September 11, 2013**, in the first-floor lecture hall of the school, under the theme "Landscape Design and Career Development."

The session will feature a conversation between Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Yaki Miodovinik, president of the US landscape design firm Andropogon. They will exchange views on the principles of landscape design, the application of water in landscape design, and the career development of landscape architects. Wang Shouzhi will also serve as the English interpreter for the event.
