It is reported that the Department of Foreign Languages under the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University will hold a series of lectures and workshops on **September 13, 2013**, to further strengthen its academic research capacity and faculty development. Professors from Peking University, Guangdong University of Foreign Studies and Tianjin Foreign Studies University have been invited to present their research interests and share their teaching experience.

The sessions will run in the morning from 9:00 to 11:00 and in the afternoon from 2:00 to 6:00 at ACC 3# (Meeting Room 3, Academic Exchange Center). Faculty members of the department and other interested staff are welcome to attend.
