It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold the 164th Engineering Research Academic Forum at 10:00 a.m. on Friday, September 13, in the Academic Exchange Room of the College of Engineering. The forum will focus on "Research on Key Physical Layer Technologies for WBAN-Based Wireless Medical Image Transmission," with Dr. Fang Yi from Xiamen University as the speaker.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Research on Key Physical Layer Technologies for WBAN-Based Wireless Medical Image Transmission |
| Speaker | Dr. Fang Yi, Xiamen University |
| Time | 10:00 a.m., Friday, September 13, 2013 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering |
| Participants | Postgraduates, doctoral students and related faculty of the College of Engineering; interested teachers and students are welcome |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |
