It is reported that STU's Human Resources Office will hold a meeting on the 2012–2013 academic year staff performance review in Room 330 of the New Administration Center in the coming days. The exact time is given in the original OA notice, which requires the heads of all units to attend on time.

The university launched this academic year's staff performance review on August 29.
