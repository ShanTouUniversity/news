It is reported that ahead of the 29th Teachers' Day, Shantou University is organizing a "Gratitude to Teachers" campaign for all 2013 undergraduate freshmen, encouraging them to express thanks to their high school teachers through hand-painted postcards.

Coordinated by the Student Affairs Office with support from colleges and residential colleges, the university will distribute two specially designed hand-painted postcards and envelopes to each freshman. Students can write messages of gratitude and best wishes to their former teachers, fill in the recipient, address and postal code, and drop the postcards into the collection box in the lobby on the first floor of the New Administration Center by September 5. The Education Management Section will collect and sort the cards and mail them before Teachers' Day, with postage covered by the university.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | "Gratitude to Teachers" Teachers' Day Campaign |
| Participants | All 2013 undergraduate freshmen |
| Distribution | September 2–3, two postcards and envelopes per student via colleges/residential colleges |
| Submission Deadline | By September 5, to the collection box in the New Administration Center lobby (1F) |
| Mailing | Education Management Section to mail uniformly before Teachers' Day; postage covered by the university |

The initiative aims to encourage freshmen to honor their teachers and carry forward the tradition of respecting educators.
