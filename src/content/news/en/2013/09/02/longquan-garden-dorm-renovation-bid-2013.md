It is reported that, on the recommendation of the STU Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, the Resources Management Office has announced the result of the tender for the contractor of the Phase II renovation of staff housing at Longquan Garden. Guangdong Aifulan Construction Co., Ltd. won the bid with a bid amount of RMB 3,053,521.44.

The result is publicized from September 2 to 4, 2013. Organizations or individuals with objections may contact the project owner, the STU Infrastructure Office, or the complaint-handling office, the STU Supervision and Audit Office, during the publicity period.
