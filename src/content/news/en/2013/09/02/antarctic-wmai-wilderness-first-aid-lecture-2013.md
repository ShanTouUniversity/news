It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 2, 2013 that a lecture in the Antarctic Program series, themed WMAI Wilderness First Aid, will be held on the evening of September 5.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | WMAI Wilderness First Aid |
| Speakers | David Johnson (Dr. DJ, known as the "father of wilderness medicine") and Sun Lingye |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, Thursday, September 5, 2013 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 3, Academic Exchange Center |

## Main Topics

- The importance of outdoor safety assessment
- Sharing on medical risks in Antarctic expeditions

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
