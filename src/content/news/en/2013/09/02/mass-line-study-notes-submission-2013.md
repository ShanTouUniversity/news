It is reported that, in accordance with the overall arrangements of the Party's mass-line education campaign and in order to assess the results of the intensive study held earlier, the office of Shantou University's leading group for the education campaign issued a notice on September 2 requiring participants in the university's intensive study sessions to submit their study notes and reflections by Friday, September 6.

According to the notice, participants are required to write up their personal reflections based on the content of the intensive study and submit them to the office of the university's education campaign leading group. The office has released the submission channel and contact details; materials may be sent by email or questions raised by phone.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Who submits | Participants in the university's intensive study sessions |
| What to submit | Study notes and reflections |
| Deadline | By Friday, September 6 |
| Submitted to | Office of the University's Education Campaign Leading Group |
| Email | o_zzb@stu.edu.cn |
| Telephone | 86502345 |
