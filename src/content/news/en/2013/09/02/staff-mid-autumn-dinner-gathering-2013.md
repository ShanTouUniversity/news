It is reported that to celebrate the 2013 Mid-Autumn Festival and bring a sense of home to faculty and staff away from their families, the University Union will host a dinner gathering on September 19 (Mid-Autumn Day) at Lan Zhi An Music Restaurant. The event is open to all faculty and staff on the main campus.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Date | September 19, 2013 (Mid-Autumn Festival) |
| Venue | Lan Zhi An Music Restaurant |
| Format | Western-style dinner |
| Capacity | 15–40 participants (event will be cancelled if fewer than 15 register) |
| Eligibility | Main-campus faculty and staff |

## Fees

- Faculty/staff registration: 30 yuan per person;
- Family members and friends may join at the group discount price of 105 yuan per person. If registrations exceed capacity, single faculty and staff will be given priority.

## Registration and Payment

| Step | Details |
|------|---------|
| How to register | Email o_xgh@stu.edu.cn; inquiries: 86502983 |
| Registration deadline | September 9 |
| Payment | September 11 at the University Union office |

## Notes

Tickets are non-refundable after payment; entry is by ticket for both faculty/staff and accompanying guests. No shuttle service will be provided; participants should arrange their own transportation and travel safely.
