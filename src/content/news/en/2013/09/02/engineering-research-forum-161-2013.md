It is reported that the 161st Engineering Research Academic Forum of the College of Engineering will be held at 10:00 a.m. on Monday, September 2, 2013, in the Academic Exchange Room of the College of Engineering. Dr. Qu Xiaobo, Assistant Professor at the Department of Electronic Science, Xiamen University, will give an academic talk.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Application of Adaptive Sparse Transforms in MRI Image Reconstruction |
| Speaker | Dr. Qu Xiaobo, Assistant Professor, Department of Electronic Science, Xiamen University |
| Time | 10:00 a.m., Monday, September 2, 2013 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering |
| Participants | Postgraduate students and relevant faculty members of the College of Engineering; interested teachers and students are also welcome |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |
