It is reported that, in line with arrangements by the Overseas Examinations Center of the Ministry of Education, registration for the second half of 2013 for the BEC Business English exams runs from September 2 to September 17.

There are no restrictions on age, gender, occupation, region or educational background, and candidates may register for BEC Preliminary, Vantage or Higher. Applicants must bring their ID card, a one-inch recent photograph and a cash payment slip. Payment is made with a cash payment slip at the Bank of China Shantou University branch; the account details and the contact information of the staff member in charge are available in the original OA notice.

Registration is open daily from 8:30 to 11:30 and from 14:30 to 17:00, with an additional weekend registration day on Saturday, September 14, from 8:30 to 17:00. Admission tickets are collected with an ID card within the two weeks before the exam, and contact details for registration inquiries are available in the original OA notice.

The exam schedule is as follows:

| Level | Exam date | Schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BEC Preliminary | November 23 | Written test in the morning, speaking test in the afternoon |
| BEC Vantage | November 30 | Written test in the morning, speaking test in the afternoon and the following day |
| BEC Higher | November 16 | Written test in the morning, speaking test in the afternoon and the following day |

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Liberal Arts)
