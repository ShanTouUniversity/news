It is reported that EBSCO has opened trial access for Shantou University to two databases, the MLA International Bibliography and the Literary Reference Center. The trial is available to all students and faculty until October 31, 2013.

Both databases can be searched simultaneously with the university's existing EBSCO full-text databases ASP, BSP and CMMC. Readers can enter through the library's database details page at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=151>, or connect to each database separately.

## About the Databases

The **MLA International Bibliography**, produced by the Modern Language Association of America, indexes publications in language, literature, linguistics and folklore since 1926. It covers more than 4,400 journals, books from more than 1,000 publishers and conference proceedings, with more than 2.2 million records and about 65,000 new records added each year. It covers languages including English, French, Spanish, German and Italian.

The **Literary Reference Center** provides more than 35,000 plot summaries, nearly 100,000 essays of literary criticism, more than 252,000 author biographies, 450 full-text literary journals, more than 700,000 book reviews, more than 78,400 poems, more than 19,700 short stories, the full text of more than 8,200 classic novels and more than 6,600 author interviews.
