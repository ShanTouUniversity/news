It is reported that the renovation of Teaching Building E at Shantou University (Phase II) began on July 1, 2013. Owing to practical issues arising during construction, the university's Capital Construction Office has proposed additional items for the project, estimated to cost about RMB 80,000. The cumulative added cost remains within the 10% limit set by the university.

## Reasons for the Additions

According to the Capital Construction Office, the proposed additions mainly cover the following:

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Lighting modification | Public restroom ceiling lights were poorly placed and will be replaced with light panels and downlights |
| Stoppage compensation | Compensation for 8-day stoppages per session to accommodate TOEFL exams at the Overseas Examination Center |
| Stoppage compensation | Compensation for 2-day stoppages per session for meetings of the Chaoshan senior Party and government management training program |
| Others | Cleaning and sanitation costs |

## Public Comment

In accordance with Shantou University's Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering Management of Capital Construction Projects, the additional project costs are now published for public comment. Objections may be submitted in writing to the Office of Supervision and Audit or the Capital Construction Office before 5:30 p.m. on September 4.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Capital Construction Office)
