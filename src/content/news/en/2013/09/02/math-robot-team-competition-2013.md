It is reported that the 14th "Future Partner Cup" China Intelligent Robot Competition and the 2013 WER World Educational Robotics International Invitational were held from August 17 to 18 at Luoyang Normal University. A four-member robot team from Shantou University's Department of Mathematics, led by teacher Gu Minqiang, entered the humanoid robot all-around and the robot firefighting events, winning first prize in the humanoid robot all-around and second prize in robot firefighting.

The events were co-hosted by the World Educational Robotics Association, the Intelligent Robot Professional Committee of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence, and the Robot Competition Working Committee of the Chinese Association of Automation. The four STU team members were Zhang JX, Huang YD, Ma JM and Chen RJ. Two of them won first prize in the humanoid robot all-around, while the other two took second prize in the robot firefighting contest.

| Event | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Humanoid Robot All-Around | First Prize (two team members) |
| Robot Firefighting | Second Prize (two team members) |

The competition is described as China's oldest and largest comprehensive robotics event, held for 14 consecutive editions and drawing more than 100 universities each year, including Tsinghua University, Harbin Institute of Technology and Northwestern Polytechnical University.

The STU Math Department robot team was founded in 2012 and was the only team from a mathematics department at this year's competition. The result follows the department's first prize in the same event last year.
