It is reported that to welcome the 2013 freshmen, the Physical Education Department of Shantou University has arranged orientation fitness guidance sessions, to be held from September 5 to 8 between 17:00 and 18:00 each day. Due to venue constraints, badminton sessions will take place only on September 5 and 8.

The sessions cover badminton, tennis, swimming, football and basketball, held respectively at the gymnasium (badminton), the tennis courts, the swimming pool, the football field and the outdoor basketball courts. Consultation desks will be set up at each venue, where PE teachers and members of sports clubs and associations will offer technical advice to freshmen.

Freshmen may book in advance by phone or email with the contact person for each sport; contact details are provided in the notice attachment. Participants must present the "New Journey" experience voucher for admission.

| Sport | Venue | Time |
|-------|-------|------|
| Badminton | Gymnasium | September 5 and 8, 17:00-18:00 (two days only due to venue constraints) |
| Tennis | Tennis Courts | September 5 to 8, 17:00-18:00 |
| Swimming | Swimming Pool | September 5 to 8, 17:00-18:00 |
| Football | Football Field | September 5 to 8, 17:00-18:00 |
| Basketball | Outdoor Basketball Courts | September 5 to 8, 17:00-18:00 |

The department also reminds freshmen to take good care of sports equipment, wear sportswear and sports shoes, bring their own rackets for badminton and tennis, wear a swimsuit for swimming (non-swimmers are not allowed in the deep pool), and follow venue rules while paying attention to safety.
