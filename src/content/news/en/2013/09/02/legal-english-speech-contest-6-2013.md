It is reported that Shantou University Law School has opened registration for the sixth Legal English Speech Contest. The contest is open to all undergraduates and graduate students of the Law School and is intended to improve students' spoken English and foster a strong environment for English learning.

The contest is conducted as an interview-based selection, with the top ten speakers from the preliminary round advancing to the final.

| Round | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary | 3:00–5:30 p.m., Wednesday, September 25 | Room 206, Law School (Wenbei) |
| Final | 3:00–5:00 p.m., Wednesday, October 16 | To be announced |

The preliminary speech is themed "Expressing Your Legal Views in English." Contestants may choose their own topics based on focal, hot or difficult issues and may read or recite their speeches on the day of the contest. Judges will score them on pronunciation, expression and language organization, and speeches must be 200 to 500 words long.

When submitting their entries, contestants must include registration information such as name, college, year and major, using the form attached to the OA notice.

The registration form must be sent to the Law School at cdclaw@stu.edu.cn by 12:00 noon on September 23. Requirements for the final will be announced separately.

> ⚠️ The registration form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
