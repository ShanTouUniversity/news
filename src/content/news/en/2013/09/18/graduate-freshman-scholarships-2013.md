It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University issued Document No. 03 [2013] on September 18, announcing the results of the 2013 Outstanding Freshman Scholarship selection for master's students.

In accordance with the Measures of Shantou University for the Assessment of the Outstanding Freshman Scholarship for Master's Students, the selection proceeded through applications by the students themselves, recommendations from the schools that train them, and review and assessment by the Graduate School. A total of 51 master's students received awards, with 6 winning first prizes and 45 winning second prizes.

## Key Facts

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Basis of assessment | Measures of Shantou University for the Assessment of the Outstanding Freshman Scholarship for Master's Students |
| Assessment procedure | Application by the student → recommendation by the training school → review and assessment by the Graduate School |
| First-prize scholarships | 6 |
| Second-prize scholarships | 45 |
| Total | 51 |
| Document number | Document No. 03 [2013] |

The list of recipients was released as an attachment to the notice.
