It is reported that the English Language Center (ELC) of Shantou University will hold an IELTS preparation workshop from 16:00 to 17:00 on September 25 (Wednesday) at CILL.

Aimed at students who plan to take the IELTS exam, the session will preview the content of a series of IELTS preparation workshops that will be launched soon. Seats are limited, and those who wish to attend need to register in advance.
