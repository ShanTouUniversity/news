It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office issued a construction notice on September 18, 2013, regarding Phase II renovation of Longquanyuan faculty housing.

## Project Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Overall renovation of 1 building of Type A and 2 buildings of Type C (C3, C7) |
| Contractor | Guangdong Aifulan Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Winning bid | 3.0535 million yuan |
| Schedule | September 18, 2013 – February 9, 2014 |
| Impact | Noise, vibration and dust are expected during construction |
| Issued by | Infrastructure Office, Resource Management Office |

For safety, pedestrians are advised to take care, and teachers are asked not to enter the construction site without permission from university functional departments and the contractor, and to avoid lingering nearby.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
