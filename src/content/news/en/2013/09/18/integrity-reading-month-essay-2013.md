It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection and the Provincial Department of Culture have issued a notice on organizing the third Provincial Integrity Reading Month (粤纪发〔2013〕12号). The Discipline Inspection Commission Office of Shantou University has forwarded the notice and asked all units on campus to organize faculty, staff and students to take part in an essay activity on reflections from integrity-themed reading.

Entries should be written around the theme "Read Books on Integrity, Promote a Clean and Upright Spirit", with titles chosen by the authors themselves. All submissions must be original, and the organizers will use dedicated equipment to check for plagiarism. Essays must not have been published in newspapers, online media or other outlets, and may not be submitted to more than one outlet at the same time. There are no restrictions on genre, and each entry should be no longer than 3,000 characters.

Electronic versions of entries should be sent to the office's email address, o_jj@stu.edu.cn, with "廉洁图书读后感征文" (essay on reading integrity-related books) noted in the subject line. A printed A4 copy should also be delivered to the Discipline Inspection Commission Office at Room 422 of the New Administration Center. The submission deadline is October 14, 2013, and the contact number is 86502346.
