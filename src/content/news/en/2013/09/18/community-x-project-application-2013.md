It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 18, 2013, to launch the call for proposals for the 2013–2014 Residential Community X-Project, aimed at fostering a humanistic, innovative, vibrant and harmonious residential community.

Launched in November 2010 and having completed two cycles, the program now invites proposals for activities to be held from October 2013 to June 2014 for undergraduate students in all residential areas.

## Project Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target participants | Undergraduate students in all residential areas |
| Period | October 2013 – June 2014 |
| Project types | (1) Innovative projects: self-designed activities around health, planning, study, time management, interpersonal relations and emotions, with novel and feasible formats; (2) Undertaking existing projects: improve upon activities with titles set by the organizer without changing the titles, as listed in the attachment |
| Follow-up | Submit a press release and photos within one week after the activity and a memo, e-album or DV within two weeks to Room G238, counselor office, for archiving |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

## Application

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | September 30, 17:00 |
| Materials | *Application Form for 2013–2014 Residential Community X-Project* plus supplementary materials, with a designated contact person |
| Submission | Counselor office, Room G238, residential area, and electronic version to ymfan@stu.edu.cn |
| Phone | 86503118 |
