It is reported that the 2013 TCC Program for incoming graduate students of Shantou University kicked off on 9 September. The event drew active participation from all freshmen and has now concluded successfully, with the results announced.

## Event Information

| Item | Time / Venue |
| --- | --- |
| Start date | 9 September 2013 |
| Conclusion | Concluded successfully; results announced on 18 September |
| Prize collection time | 26 September 2013, 14:30–17:30 |
| Prize collection venue | Central hall of the Graduate Students' Union (2F, Building Y) |

## Prize Collection

Winning teams must collect their prizes between 14:30 and 17:30 on 26 September at the central hall of the Graduate Students' Union (2F, Building Y). Prizes will not be kept after the deadline.

The Graduate School congratulates the winning students and thanks all freshmen across the University for their active participation. The list of winners is provided as an attachment on the OA system.

> ⚠️ The list of winners is available as an attachment in the original notice on the OA system.
