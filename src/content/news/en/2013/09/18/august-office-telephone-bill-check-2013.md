It is reported that the electronic office telephone bills of Shantou University units for August 2013, covering both China Telecom fixed lines and China Mobile fixed lines, have been sent to the university by China Telecom Shantou Branch and China Mobile Shantou Branch respectively, and the relevant data have been loaded into the Telephone Management System. Each unit is asked to have its designated verifier check its own bills.

According to the arrangement, verifiers should log in to the Telephone Management System at http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn and click on "China Telecom Fixed Lines" and "China Mobile Fixed Lines" respectively to carry out the verification. If there is no objection to a bill, they should click "confirm the selected bill" to complete the confirmation. If the bill details do not match the actual use of the telephones, they should report this by email before September 25, sending China Telecom fixed-line bills to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn and China Mobile fixed-line bills to stunic@stu.edu.cn.

The notice makes clear that if no objection is raised or no confirmation is made before the verification deadline, the system will treat the charges as confirmed by default. Once the bills are verified, the Finance Office will deduct payment on September 29 according to the funding account numbers provided by each unit.
