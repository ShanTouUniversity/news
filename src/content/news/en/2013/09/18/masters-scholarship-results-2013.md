It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University issued Document No. 04 [2013] on September 18, announcing the results of the 2013-14 scholarship assessment for 2013-entry master's students.

In accordance with the Measures of Shantou University for the Assessment of Graduate Scholarships, the training schools and the Graduate School assessed the scholarships of 2013-entry master's students for the 2013-14 academic year. A total of 312 master's students received awards, with 100 winning first prizes, 147 winning second prizes and 65 winning third prizes.
