It is reported that the Green Ribbon service-learning environmental volunteer team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association held the Energy Saving Star competition from April to June 2013 under the theme “Advocating Thrift and Building a Harmonious Campus,” and the results have now been announced. A total of 153 dormitories registered for the activity, and 10 dormitories won awards.

According to the organizers, electricity use in student dormitories fell markedly during the activity. In April, dormitories saved 1,486 kWh compared with March. Although student dormitories switched to an all-day summer power supply from April 18, electricity consumption in May this year still dropped substantially compared with May last year.

## Key Facts

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Activity period | April to June 2013 |
| Dormitories registered | 153 |
| Electricity saved | 1,486 kWh in April compared with March; May consumption also fell sharply year on year |
| Awards | 1 Gold, 3 Silver and 6 Bronze — 10 dormitories in total |
| Data source | Statistics from the Water and Electricity Center |
| Organizer | Green Ribbon Service-Learning Environmental Volunteer Team, Youth Volunteers Association |

The organizers said the prizes and cash awards will be delivered to the winning dormitories.
