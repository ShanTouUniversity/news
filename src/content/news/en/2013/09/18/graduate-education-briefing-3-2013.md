It is reported that the Graduate School released the third issue of the 2013 Shantou University briefing on degree and graduate education on 18 September 2013.

This third issue of 2013, issued by the Graduate School, covers matters related to degree and graduate education. The text of the briefing was not published on the notice page; it is provided as an attachment on the OA system, where the original notice can be consulted.

> ⚠️ The full briefing is available as an attachment in the original notice on the OA system.
