It is reported that, to support the University Party Committee in carrying out its Discipline Education and Study Month and to further strengthen the effect of the study campaign, the Discipline Inspection Commission Office of Shantou University, together with the Publicity Department and the Party and Administrative Office, has jointly organized special exhibitions on the "Party's Mass Line Education and Practice Campaign" and on "Secrecy Law Publicity and Education". The exhibition opened on 18 September 2013 at the union bulletin board beside the post office.

## Exhibition Content

Centred on the theme of this year's provincial Discipline Education and Study Month, the exhibition is divided into two sections: one themed "Serving the People, Being Pragmatic and Being Honest", and the other themed "Illustrated Secrecy Law".
