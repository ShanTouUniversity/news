It is reported that, with the 2013 Mid-Autumn Festival holiday approaching, the Party and Administrative Office has consolidated the holiday duty arrangements of the university's departments into a single roster and made it available to all departments for review, so that they can reach one another during the holiday.

The full roster is contained in the attachment on the OA system. Departments with questions about the duty arrangements can email o_jdk@stu.edu.cn.
