It is reported that the Graduate School has announced class adjustments for postgraduates on the main campus during the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holidays, in accordance with the university notice on 2013 holiday arrangements (STU Party and Administration Office [2013] No. 3).

## Rescheduling at a Glance

| Holiday | Classes Suspended | Swap Arrangement | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|-------|
| Mid-Autumn Festival | Sept. 19 | Classes on Sept. 20 (Fri) swapped with Sept. 22 (Sun) | — |
| National Day | Oct. 1–3 | Classes on Sept. 29 (Sun) swapped with Oct. 4 (Fri); classes on Oct. 7 (Mon) swapped with Oct. 12 (Sat) | Classes on Oct. 5–6 (Sat–Sun) continue as normal |

Postgraduate units and students are advised to take note of the changes and plan their studies accordingly to avoid missing any sessions.
