It is reported that, because of the Mid-Autumn Festival holiday, the campus card service windows at the Expert Apartment and the Second Canteen of Shantou University will be closed from September 19 to September 21 and will resume normal service on September 22.

During the holiday, faculty and students can still top up their campus cards at the self-service transfer, deposit and inquiry machines. Those who need to report a lost card or check their card balance may call the automated voice inquiry service at 82903414. For other questions, they may call the service hotline at 86503420 or send an email to support@stu.edu.cn.

The Network and Information Center advises faculty and students to arrange their card services, such as top-ups, in advance so that normal use is not affected during the holiday.
