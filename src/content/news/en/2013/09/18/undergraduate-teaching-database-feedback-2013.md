It is reported that, in order to implement the spirit of the Ministry of Education's Opinions on Undergraduate Teaching Evaluation in Regular Higher Education Institutions (Jiao Gao Han [2011] No. 9) and to build a sound national undergraduate teaching database for higher education institutions, the Higher Education Evaluation Center of the Ministry of Education has revised the undergraduate teaching database tables and their explanatory notes. The revision drew on data collection experience from newly established undergraduate institutions and pilot institutions for audit evaluation, and resulted in the notice Seeking Comments on the National Undergraduate Teaching Database (Jiao Gao Ping Zhong Xin Han [2013] No. 41), which asks colleges and universities for their opinions. The database will be an important part of the second round of undergraduate teaching evaluation.

The university recently forwarded the notice and asked teaching units, departments and offices, the Youth League Committee, the Labour Union, the Graduate School, the Library, and the Network and Information Center, among others, to carefully read the attached materials. They are asked to examine the rationality and applicability of the data item design, the accuracy and completeness of the explanatory notes, and the feasibility of completing the forms, and to put forward proposed revisions.

According to the notice, the units concerned should send their feedback in electronic form to the Academic Affairs Office before the end of the morning session on September 22, 2013 (Sunday). Contact: Shen Shaolong; telephone: 86502406; email: o_jwc@stu.edu.cn.
