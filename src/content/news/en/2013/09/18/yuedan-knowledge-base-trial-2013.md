It is reported that Shantou University Library has opened trial access to the Yue Dan Knowledge Base, which will remain available to faculty and students through December 31, 2013 via the library's database page.

The knowledge base is published and distributed by Yuanzhao Publishing (元照出版公司). Its collections focus on works by leading authors in five disciplines — law, education, economics, public administration, and medicine and health — published in journals, books, teaching resources, question banks and lectures, and master's and doctoral dissertations. To serve research, teaching, learning and professional practice, it also gathers resources such as question banks and lectures, dictionaries and reference works, commonly used regulations across the Taiwan Strait, and selected judicial decisions, supplemented by full-journal and full-book collections of key titles.

The library said faculty and students are welcome to use the database, and that trial evaluations and comments may be sent to the Library's Subject Services Division.
