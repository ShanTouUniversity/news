It is reported that the Shantou University gymnasium has temporarily suspended reservations because some equipment is being dismantled and moved to the air-supported basketball training hall and the gymnasium court layout is being reorganized.

## Adjustment Scope

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Facilities affected | Gymnasium, indoor badminton court and indoor basketball court |
| Adjustments | Moving some equipment to the air-supported basketball training hall and reorganizing the gymnasium court layout |
| Reopening | The facilities will reopen after the equipment and court adjustments are completed; the specific date will be announced separately by the Department of Physical Education |
| Issuing unit | Department of Physical Education |
| Issue date | September 18, 2013 |

The Department of Physical Education advises faculty and students to take note of the arrangement and not to seek reservations at the gymnasium while reservations are suspended. The university apologizes for the inconvenience and thanks faculty and students for their understanding.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Department of Physical Education)
