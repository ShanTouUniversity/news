It is reported that the Firefly Love Action Team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association issued a notice on September 18, 2013, to recruit volunteers for its Sunshine Community Service project university-wide.

Founded on May 28, 2007, with the motto “Wherever fireflies fly, there is light and love,” the team has carried out volunteer services for disadvantaged groups, including home visits to elderly living alone, single-parent families and others in need, offering housekeeping, counseling, free repairs and clinics, academic guidance and outreach.

## Recruitment

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Sunshine Community Service |
| Deadline | September 23, 2013 |
| On-site registration | 11:30–13:00 and 17:00–19:00, September 22–23, at consultation points downstairs of Canteens 2 and 4 |
| Paper form | Download and submit the form to designated drop-off points |
| Email | Download and send the completed form to designated email addresses (electronic submission encouraged for environmental reasons) |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Youth Volunteers Association Firefly Team |
