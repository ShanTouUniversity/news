It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University released the final list and court assignments for its internships at courts in Dongguan and Huizhou on 18 September, with 43 students selected.

After the Law School issued its notice on the internships, students applied in large numbers. Based on the students' stated preferences and the number of internship places available at each court, and through coordination by the school's Career Development Centre, the final list and the allocation of students among the host courts were determined.

## Students Selected by Court

| Host court | Students selected |
| --- | --- |
| Dongguan Intermediate People's Court | 5 |
| Dongguan No. 1 People's Court | 16 |
| Dongguan No. 2 People's Court | 12 |
| Huizhou Intermediate People's Court | 10 |
| **Total** | **43** |

As previously reported by STU News, the internships will depart in two groups, on 23 September and 8 October respectively, with accommodation provided by the host courts.
