It is reported that the Go tournament of the 1st Shantou Municipal Government Games was held on September 14 and 15. Shantou University sent a Go team made up of three teachers: Zhu Wei'an, Ji Jie, and Xu Chuang.

Facing a strong field, the STU team produced good results over seven tense rounds: Zhu Wei'an finished third, Ji Jie fourth, and Xu Chuang eighth.
