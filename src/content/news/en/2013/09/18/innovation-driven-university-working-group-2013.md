It is reported that, in line with documents from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance, Shantou University has decided to set up a working group for its Innovation-Driven University Project in order to organize and advance the implementation of the university's work under the initiative. The decision was made at the 11th university leadership meeting held on September 17, 2013, and was announced in an official document (STU Fa [2013] No. 110) issued on September 18.

According to the document, the working group is headed by Gu Peihua, with Wen Xiaobo, Lin Danming and Wulanhasi serving as deputy heads. Lin Feng serves as secretary.

## Working Group Composition

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Head | Gu Peihua |
| Deputy heads | Wen Xiaobo, Lin Danming, Wulanhasi |
| Members (ordered by stroke count of surname) | Li Shengping, Lu Xiaohua, Wu Ping, Zou Zhibo, Lin Feng, Hu Zhong, Huang Dongyang, Liao Xuequan |
| Secretary | Lin Feng |

The document lists the members in order of the number of strokes in their surnames. The creation of the working group is intended to strengthen coordination of the university's Innovation-Driven University Project and to move its construction tasks forward in an orderly way.
