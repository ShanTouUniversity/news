It is reported that, to accelerate preparations for collaboration between Shantou University and the Technion-Israel Institute of Technology, Shantou University has decided to set up a working group on the university's side. The decision was made at the 11th university leadership meeting held on September 17, 2013, and was announced in an official document (STU Fa [2013] No. 111) issued on September 18.

According to the document, the working group is headed by Gu Peihua, with Lin Danming serving as deputy head.

## Working Group Composition

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Head | Gu Peihua |
| Deputy head | Lin Danming |
| Members (ordered by stroke count of surname) | Li Shengping, Bian Junhui, Chen Shaogang, Wu Ping, Zou Zhibo, Lu Xiaohua, Lin Feng, Zeng Rui, Liao Xuequan |
| Secretary | Lin Feng |

The document lists the members in order of the number of strokes in their surnames. The working group is intended to coordinate and advance the various preparation tasks for the collaboration between Shantou University and the Technion.
