It is reported that an evening cleaning period from 19:00 to 21:00 has been added for the A-F student dormitories starting September 13, 2013.

The arrangement was issued by the Logistics Management Office and the Student Community Management Service Center to further improve cleanliness in the A-F student dormitories and reduce the accumulation of waste overnight.

The notice has been published on the university's OA system.

## Key Facts

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Effective date | September 13, 2013 |
| Cleaning period | 19:00–21:00 |
| Scope | A-F student dormitories |
| Issuing units | Logistics Management Office; Student Community Management Service Center |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Logistics Management Office)
