It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 7, 2013, that the university adaptation tutoring program offered by the Mutual Learning Center (SLC) for new students will be available to all undergraduates from Sunday, September 8 (week 4, summer semester) to Monday, September 30 (week 3, fall semester).

Students can discuss questions with peer tutors from different majors. The center is open daily from 9:00 to 12:00 and 15:00 to 22:00, and students are welcome to use its services. Details of the tutoring schedule are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The tutoring schedule and other attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
