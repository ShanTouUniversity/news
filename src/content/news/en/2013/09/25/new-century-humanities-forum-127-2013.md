It is reported that the 127th New Century Humanities Forum of Shantou University will be held on the evening of September 27. Professor Wang Xiansheng of the School of Foreign Languages at Zhengzhou University has been invited to speak on “The Differences Between Chinese and Western Cultures.” The forum is organized by the College of Liberal Arts, and faculty and students across the university are welcome to attend.

## Forum Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | New Century Humanities Forum No. 127 |
| Topic | The Differences Between Chinese and Western Cultures |
| Speaker | Wang Xiansheng, professor at the School of Foreign Languages, Zhengzhou University |
| Time | 7:00–9:00 p.m., September 27, 2013 |
| Venue | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Organizer | College of Liberal Arts, Shantou University |

## Lecture Outline

According to the organizer, the lecture will examine Chinese-Western cultural comparison along the following lines:

- Introduction: the two upsurges in Chinese-Western cultural comparison
- Religious sentiment and secular sentiment
- The pursuit of truth and the pursuit of goodness
- Seeking differences and seeking common ground
- The sublime and the beautiful

## About the Speaker

Wang Xiansheng is a professor at the School of Foreign Languages of Zhengzhou University, academic leader of the university’s master’s program in English Language and Literature and of its Master of Translation and Interpreting program, and vice president of the Henan Translators Association. He previously served as a member of the English Sub-Committee of the Ministry of Education’s Steering Committee for Foreign Language Teaching. He has taught English majors for more than 30 years, offering more than ten courses in culture and translation at undergraduate and postgraduate levels, and has published more than ten translated works and textbooks.
