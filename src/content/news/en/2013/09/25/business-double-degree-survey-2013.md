It is reported that on September 25, 2013, the Business School of Shantou University launched a survey of the 2013 cohort's interest in pursuing a double degree in Business Administration to support subsequent teaching arrangements and enrollment planning.

## Application Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Students in the 2013 cohort interested in pursuing a Business Administration double degree |
| Application material | Business Administration double-degree application form |
| Submission email | o_gjsw@stu.edu.cn |
| Deadline | 17:00 on October 5, 2013 |
| Curriculum reference | Page 98 of the 2013 *Academic Guide* |

The Business School will compile the application forms, develop a related plan and submit it to the relevant university authorities before arranging teaching for the Business Administration double-degree and minor programs. Students who submit the application form and meet the basic eligibility requirements will receive priority consideration for admission.

> ⚠️ The Business Administration double-degree application form and curriculum information are available in the original notice on the OA system.
>
