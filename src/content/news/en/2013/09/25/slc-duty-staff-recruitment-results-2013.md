It is reported that the Student Learning Center (SLC) under the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has completed the recruitment of duty staff for the 2013-14 academic year and released the results. Based on the requirements of the duty staff posts and the candidates' performance at interview, the centre finally selected six students.

The interviews for this recruitment round were held in time slots at the Student Learning Center on Tuesday, September 24. Shortlisted students had been informed earlier to attend on time.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number appointed | Six students |
| Academic year | 2013-14 |
| Basis of selection | Requirements of the duty staff posts and candidates' interview performance |
