It is reported that Shantou University's leadership reception day this week is scheduled for 3 p.m. on Thursday, September 26, 2013, when Vice President Wen Xiaobo will be on duty to meet visiting faculty and students.

The Administrative Office reminded faculty and students who wish to visit to make an appointment by phone in advance on 86502306.
