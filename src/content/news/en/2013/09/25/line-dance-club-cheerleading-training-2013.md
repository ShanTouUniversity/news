It is reported that the Line Dance Club, one of the pilot projects of the Physical Education Department's advanced undergraduate teaching reform at Shantou University, plans to open a cheerleading training class in the new semester and is recruiting a group of new members from across the university.

The club aims to promote and spread fitness line dancing — an emerging international fitness activity — and to hold line dance training and exchange activities in a variety of forms. Cheerleading, the club explains, is a form of organized dance performed to cheer on athletes in competition, and it is also a sport full of energy, passion and confidence.

## Key details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizer | Line Dance Club, Physical Education Department, Shantou University |
| Activity | Recruitment of new members for the cheerleading training class |
| Registration time | 2:00–4:00 p.m., Wednesday, September 25, 2013 |
| Registration venue | Gymnastics room, University Gymnasium |
| Interview | Learn and perform a short cheerleading routine on site; applicants may also bring their own dance piece |

## How to register

Students interested in joining may register on site at the gymnastics room of the University Gymnasium between 2:00 and 4:00 p.m. on Wednesday, September 25, and take the interview there.
