It is reported that the office of the Shantou University Student Union officially resumed duty on Wednesday, September 25, 2013. After resuming operations, it is open at fixed hours from Monday to Friday to serve students across the university.

## Duty Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Resumed on | September 25, 2013 (Wednesday) |
| Duty hours | 17:00–18:00, Monday to Friday |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Union |

The notice was jointly issued by the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Student Union. After resuming duty, the Student Union office will handle students' matters during the hours above.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, STU Student Union)
