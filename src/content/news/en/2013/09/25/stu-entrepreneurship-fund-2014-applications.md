It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced the launch of applications for the 2014 STU Entrepreneurship Fund. Students interested in starting their own businesses may submit application materials to the Student Entrepreneurship Center as required.

## Wide Range of Eligible Applicants

According to the notice, applicants for the fund generally fall into the following categories:

| Applicant Category | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Current undergraduates | Full-time, third year or above |
| Recent graduates | Full-time undergraduates within six months of graduation |
| Graduate students | Full-time senior graduate students currently enrolled |

This means that senior undergraduates still on campus, recent graduates who left school only months ago, and senior graduate students are all eligible to apply, provided they meet the requirements.

## Submitting Applications

The notice also outlines the intended uses of the fund and the submission requirements. Detailed rules can be found in the attachment documents forwarded by the Student Affairs Office, and applicants are expected to prepare and submit materials in line with the requirements of the Student Entrepreneurship Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Attachments detailing the fund's intended uses and submission requirements are available on the OA system.
