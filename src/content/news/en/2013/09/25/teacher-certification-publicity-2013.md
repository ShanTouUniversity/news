It is reported that, following review by the Expert Review Committee for Higher Education Teaching Certification in Guangdong Province, the university proposes to certify a number of teachers for higher education teaching and has released the names for public comment.

The publicity period runs from September 25 to October 8, 2013. During this period, anyone with objections to the proposed certification may raise them in writing with the Teaching Certification Office of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education or with the university's human resources department. The authorities stressed that any objection must include specific facts and be signed with the sender's real name; objections that provide no supporting facts or carry no signature will not be accepted.

The designated channels are as follows. Within the university, the receiving department is the Human Resources Office of Shantou University; the contact is Chen Ying, telephone (0754) 86502340. At the provincial level, the receiving department is the Guangdong Provincial Teaching Certification Office; the contact is Chen Lixia, fax (020) 37627657; the address is 723 Dongfeng East Road, Guangzhou, postal code 510080.
