It is reported that, in line with the notice issued by the university's Party and Administrative Office on holiday and class-adjustment arrangements for 2013, the Student Learning Center (SLC) will be closed during the National Day holiday.

Under the arrangement, the SLC will not be open to users from October 1 to October 7, 2013, and will resume normal opening on October 8. The university reminds students and staff to note the change in opening hours and to plan their study and activities in advance.
