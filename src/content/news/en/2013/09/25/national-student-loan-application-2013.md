It is reported that, to help students from financially disadvantaged families complete their studies, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has made arrangements for handling national student loans for the 2013 undergraduate cohort. Applications, the review and submission of documents, and the signing of contracts will proceed in stages on a fixed timetable.

## Loan information session on September 29

The office will hold an information session on national student loans for the 2013 cohort at 19:30 on September 29 in the Moot Court classroom. Students preparing to apply are required to attend on time with their campus cards.

## Online applications due by October 9

From September 25 to October 9, applicants should log on to the Student Affairs System (http://sao.stu.edu.cn), submit their applications under "My Applications - Loan Application", print the National Student Loan Application Form without signing it for the time being, and hand the form in together with the other documents to the counselor of their school (residential college).

The documents required are:

- the original STU Student Family Financial Situation Survey;
- photocopies of the second-generation identity cards of the applicant and both parents, with the front and back sides copied onto one sheet of A4 paper;
- photocopies of pages 2 and 3 of the student card, the pages carrying the photograph and the major information;
- a photocopy of the Bank of China debit card, signed on the back, and the receipt for the enquiry into the account linked to the card number.

## Schools and colleges to complete review by October 25

By October 25, counselors of each school (residential college) will verify the applications in the Student Affairs System and examine the supporting documents, print the STU Family Financial Situation Certification Application Form (double-sided on A4 paper), sign and stamp the photocopies, attach a photocopy of the identity card of the witnessing teacher, and submit the compiled materials in batches to the Financial Aid Management Center (Administrative Center 106).

| Submission dates | Units |
| --- | --- |
| October 28-29 | Law School, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Zhicheng College |
| October 30 - November 1 | School of Liberal Arts, School of Science, School of Engineering, Business School |

## Supplementary documents accepted until March 2014

Students who cannot get all their documents ready before November 4 may first submit their applications in the system, but should deliver the documents to the Financial Aid Management Center by March 10, 2014.

## Notes and contact details

The office reminds students who have already obtained a student loan from their place of origin that they may not apply for a national student loan. Applicants must make sure their applications have been submitted through the Student Affairs System, as those not submitted through the system will not be accepted. Documents that pass the preliminary review will be kept as attachments to the loan contract and will not be returned, so students should photocopy the family financial situation survey for their own records.

According to the office, the Financial Aid Management Center will submit the applications to the Shantou Branch of the Bank of China in batches by academic year according to the term of the loans and will organize students to sign loan contracts. Students with questions can email zjliu@stu.edu.cn or call 82904749 (contact: Mr. Liu).
