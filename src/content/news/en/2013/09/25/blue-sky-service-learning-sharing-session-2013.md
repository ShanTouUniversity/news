It is reported that the public-welfare course "Blue Sky" Creative Workshop at Shantou University will hold a wrap-up sharing session at 19:30 on September 27, 2013 in Lecture Room 201 of Building E. The session will review the service and study work carried out during this summer's course, and faculty members and students are welcome to attend and share their views.
