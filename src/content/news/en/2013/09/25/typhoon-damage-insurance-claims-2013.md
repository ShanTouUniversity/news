It is reported that Shantou University has taken out comprehensive property insurance. To help units and individuals that suffered property losses in the typhoon file insurance claims in a timely manner, the university’s Logistics Management Office and Finance Office have jointly issued a notice to begin collecting claim documents.

Under the notice, units and individuals that meet the claim conditions should provide photographs and other supporting materials as soon as possible so that the losses can be reported to the insurer promptly. The university reminds those concerned to prepare the materials as required so that their claims are not delayed.
