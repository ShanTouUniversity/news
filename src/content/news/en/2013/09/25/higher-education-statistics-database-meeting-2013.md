It is reported that Shantou University will hold a statistics work meeting at 11 a.m. on Thursday, September 26, 2013, in Meeting Room 201 of the New Administration Building. The meeting will set out arrangements for the university's grassroots higher education statistics and basic database development for the 2013/2014 academic year and will provide training on the relevant statistical software.

The Administrative Office has asked departments and units including the Medical College, Graduate School, Academic Affairs Office, School of Continuing Education, Human Resources Office, Research Office, Office of International Cooperation and Exchange, Organization and United Front Work Department, Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office, Library, Network and Information Center, University Assets Office and Equipment Section to arrange one staff member responsible for statistics work to attend on time.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

| Attendance | Departments and units concerned must arrange one staff member responsible for statistics work to attend on time |

## Main Statistical Categories

The statistics work covers the following information:

- Enrollment, including doctoral and master's students, regular undergraduate and junior-college students, and adult undergraduate and junior-college students;
- Political affiliation of enrolled students;
- International students;
- Faculty and staff;
- Political affiliation of faculty and staff;
- Campus land area;
- Library holdings;
- Digital resources;
- Information technology development;
- Fixed assets.
