It is reported that the service-learning course "Blue Sky" Creative Workshop, a public-welfare course at Shantou University, has rescheduled its teaching and cultural-sports support activities at Dachang Primary School in Jinping District because the class time clashed with the Mid-Autumn Festival holiday.

The new class time will be announced in a follow-up notice, and students taking part in the course are asked to watch for updates.

Students are also reminded to pass the word to one another.
