It is reported that the Student Community Service Center of Shantou University has issued a notice stating that some fans were removed from the CD dormitories after air conditioners were installed, and that, because of limited space in the rooms, the removed fans can no longer be reinstalled or used. The center will therefore arrange for staff to visit the rooms and collect the fans from September 27, and has asked students in the CD dormitories to cooperate.
