It is reported that the College of Science of Shantou University will hold a lecture in its "Science, Society and Life" series at 15:00 on September 30 at Tiered Classroom E201, featuring Prof. Xu Yuesheng, a chair professor at the School of Mathematics of Sun Yat-sen University, who will speak on "My University Dream, My Path of Study".

Prof. Xu is among the first batch of national distinguished experts under the "Thousand Talents Plan" of the Central Organization Department. According to the organizers, he will draw on his experience as a former educated youth in the countryside, a university student, a student in the United States and a newly returned scholar to share his university dream and his path of study.

The organizers said the talk will discuss how dreams light the way in life, and will encourage young people not to copy the paths taken by others but to listen to their inner voice and take a distinctive path of their own. All faculty and students are welcome.

## Key points of the talk

- The speaker will share his university dream and path of study as a former educated youth, a university student, a student in the United States and a newly returned scholar.
- Dreams light the way and guide personal growth and choices.
- Rather than copying the paths of others, listeners are encouraged to heed their inner voice and take a distinctive path of their own.
