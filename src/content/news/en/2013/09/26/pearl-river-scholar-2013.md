It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University has recently released a notice announcing the designated disciplines and selected candidates for the 2013 session of Guangdong Province's "Pearl River Scholar Program."

The Pearl River Scholar Program is a key initiative of Guangdong Province to strengthen the development of high-level talent in universities. By establishing posts for distinguished professors and lecture professors, it aims to attract and cultivate academic leaders and promote the growth of key disciplines.
