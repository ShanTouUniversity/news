It is reported that, under the Notice on the Revision of the Undergraduate Curriculum Plan for the 2013 Cohort, Shantou University is changing the way faculty profiles are published starting with the 2013 cohort: the profiles will be entered directly into the credit system and will no longer be printed in the *Study Guide*.

The Academic Affairs Office said it has already entered into the credit system the faculty profile information submitted by teaching units when they developed the curriculum plans for the 2013 cohort, and asked all teachers to log in to the credit system to check and verify their own profiles. If any information is found to be missing or incorrect, teachers may send their latest profile to the designated email address for the office to update, or they may maintain and edit their own profiles in the credit system.
