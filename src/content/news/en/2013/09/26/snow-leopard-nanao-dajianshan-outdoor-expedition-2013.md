It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a notice announcing that the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association of Shantou University will hold a beginner outdoor expedition at Dajianshan on Nanao Island from October 2 to 3, 2013, and is recruiting participants from among all faculty members and students.

According to the notice, the event aims to cultivate an interest in outdoor sports and let participants feel the positive energy of nature; to help them build sound interpersonal relationships through outdoor team activities and develop an optimistic, strong and courageous attitude toward life; and to help them master basic wilderness survival skills and improve their ability to cope with environmental changes. The event also seeks to enrich extracurricular activities for faculty and students, offer outdoor enjoyment, raise environmental awareness, and foster teamwork and communication skills.

## Event at a Glance

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | October 2-3, 2013 |
| Venue | Dajianshan, Nanao Island |
| Who may apply | All faculty members and students of the university |
| Quota | 20 places planned; if applications exceed the quota, places will be decided by lot |
| Registration deadline | 20:00, September 29, 2013 (Sunday) |
| Cost | 30 yuan per person for transportation, plus the cost of outdoor meals for two days and one night (about 20 yuan), to be borne by participants |
| Registration email | snowleopardstu@163.com |

## Program Content

The program includes hiking and trekking, wild camping, team-building activities and wilderness survival training.

## Requirements for Applicants

- Be reasonably confident in one's physical fitness, keen on outdoor activities, modest and willing to learn, and ready to endure hardship;
- Be good at cooperation and willing to help others;
- Follow instructions in all actions and treat everyone with a smile;
- Have no other commitments during the event (the whole of October 2 and 3);
- Follow the coaches' instructions during the event, keep strictly to schedule, and never leave the team without permission.

## How to Apply

Those interested may download and fill in the attached application form, "Shantou University Snow Leopard Outdoor Association Nanao Island Outdoor Expedition Application Form", prefix the file name with "year of study + major + name", and email the electronic form to snowleopardstu@163.com. Applications that do not follow the required format will not be accepted. Questions may be directed to the same email address.

The organizers will purchase personal accident insurance for participants and arrange the necessary camping equipment.
