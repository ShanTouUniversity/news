It is reported that Shantou University will administer the National Student Physical Health Standard test to students of the 2011, 2012 and 2013 cohorts in October, with the test held on four rest days. To support on-site organization and the sorting of test data, the Physical Education Department plans to recruit 12 student staff from undergraduates at the main campus, with pay provided in line with the university's work-study standards.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Test dates | Oct 13 (Sun), 19 (Sat), 20 (Sun) and 26 (Sat) |
| Number of recruits | 12 (no more than half may be female) |
| Pay | In line with the university's work-study standards |
| Application deadline | Before the end of the afternoon working period on Sept 27, 2013 |
| Application email | o_tyb@stu.edu.cn |
| Next steps | The department will notify applicants about interviews and training after collecting applications |

## Job Duties

The department says the student staff will mainly assist teachers on duty with carrying equipment, giving starting signals, timing, counting and recording; supervise test data, keep order at test venues and promptly correct improper testing behavior; and help teachers sort and enter data.

## Requirements

Applicants are expected to be in good health, able to work outdoors and have strong organizational and management skills. They must also make sure they have no other work or study commitments on the test dates, and have sufficient spare time outside class to help teachers sort and enter data.

## How to Apply

Prospective applicants should email their student ID number, name, year and major, and mobile phone number to the department at o_tyb@stu.edu.cn before the end of the afternoon working period on Sept 27, using "体质测试工作人员" (Fitness Test Staff) in the subject line. The Physical Education Department says interviews and training will be arranged and announced after it collects the applications.
