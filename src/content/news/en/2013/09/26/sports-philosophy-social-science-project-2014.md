It is reported that applications have opened for the 2014 Sports Philosophy and Social Science Research Projects of the General Administration of Sport of China (known as administration-managed projects). The Research Office of Shantou University has issued a notice organizing applications from faculty members and explaining the eligibility rules, timeline and material requirements.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project categories | Key projects, general projects and youth projects |
| Research period | Key projects generally 1-2 years; general and youth projects generally 1 year |
| Eligibility | Applicants for key and general projects must hold a senior professional and technical title (associate professor or above) or an administrative post at or above the division level; youth project applicants must already hold a master's degree or above, hold no senior professional title, and be under 39 years of age (born after Oct. 10, 1974) |
| University quota | Up to 3 projects |
| Deadlines | Materials for formal review by Sept. 30, 2013; paper materials by Oct. 8, 2013 |
| Contact | Huang Yumei, Zhuo Yanchun; Tel: 86502563; Email: yczhuo@stu.edu.cn |

## Eligibility and Notes

- Leaders of ongoing administration-managed sports social science projects or National Social Science Fund projects may not apply.
- Each project leader may apply for only 1 project and may not join another project as a team member.
- Team members may not take part in two or more project applications at the same time.
- Those applying for translation and compilation projects must resolve copyright issues themselves.
- Any applicant found to have falsified information will, once verified, be barred from applying for three years.
- Shantou University may submit up to 3 projects.

## Application Procedure

1. Applicants should truthfully complete the application materials and send them to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn by Sept. 30, 2013 for formal review.
2. By Oct. 8, 2013, submit to the Research Office two copies of the application form, which must have passed the formal review and be computer-typed and double-sided printed on A4 paper, together with six copies of the separate "loose-leaf" document, whose content must match the "Project Design and Argumentation" section of the application form.
3. While submitting the paper materials, send electronic versions to the same email address.

Contacts: Huang Yumei, Zhuo Yanchun; Tel: 86502563.
