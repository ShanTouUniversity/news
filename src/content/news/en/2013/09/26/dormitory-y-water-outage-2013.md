It is reported that, because the Water and Electricity Center is upgrading the water pipes of LY Dormitory, the water supply pipeline of Y Dormitory will be connected on September 27, and Y Dormitory will be without water during the construction. In a notice issued on September 26, the Student Community Service Center asked students living in Y Dormitory to store water in advance.

## Outage Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Reason | The Water and Electricity Center is upgrading the water pipes of LY Dormitory and will connect the water supply pipeline of Y Dormitory |
| Date | September 27, 2013 |
| Time | 8:30–15:30 |
| Reminder | Students in Y Dormitory should store water in advance |
| Issuing unit | Student Community Service Center (September 26, 2013) |

The Student Community Service Center apologized for the inconvenience the outage may cause.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Logistics Management Office)
