It is reported that, in line with a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University is required to report resource information on its 2014 graduates, including main-campus undergraduate graduates and all graduate students, to support the preparation of the 2014 employment plan and a series of subsequent recommendation and dispatch procedures.

The Student Affairs Office recently asked all colleges (residential colleges) to remind graduates who have not yet filled in their basic information to log in to http://gdc.stu.edu.cn/ and complete their personal information by October 10, 2013. The office noted that graduates' basic information will be used in subsequent work such as graduate dispatch, and asked all graduates to take the submission seriously. Detailed operating instructions are available in the attachment to the notice.
