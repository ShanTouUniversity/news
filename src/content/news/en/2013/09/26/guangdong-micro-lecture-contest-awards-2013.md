It is reported that the first Guangdong Micro-Lecture Contest was held from April to August 2013. Shantou University organized faculty applications and recommended four entries in total, three of which won awards.

Wu Ming's *Bridge Design Procedures* won a first prize in the undergraduate science and engineering category and will represent Guangdong in the final of the National University Micro-Lecture Teaching Competition. Zeng Lijuan's *Experiential Teaching Practice That Brings the Classroom to Life: On the Calculation of Fund Equivalent Value and Its Practical Application* and Wang Yingzi's *The Beginning of Design: Research Work* won second prizes in the same category. With the active participation of relevant units and strong support from the Center for Modern Educational Technology, Shantou University received the contest's Outstanding Organization Award.

On September 18, the organizers held the awards ceremony and the Guangdong University Faculty Teaching Skills Showcase and Exchange Conference at the International Conference Hall of South China Normal University. Du Hong, Associate Director of the STU Office of Academic Affairs, attended together with teachers Wu Ming and Zeng Lijuan.

The contest was hosted by the National Teacher Education Network Training Center of the Ministry of Education and organized by the Guangdong Higher Education Educational Technology Teaching Guidance Committee and South China Normal University. A total of 288 entries from 51 universities took part, and the contest finally awarded one grand prize, 15 first prizes (five for undergraduate humanities and social sciences, five for undergraduate science and engineering, and five for higher vocational and professional colleges), 29 second prizes and 56 third prizes. The contest aims to implement the spirit of the State Council's Proposal on Strengthening the Building of the Teaching Workforce and the Ministry of Education's Several Opinions on Comprehensively Improving the Quality of Higher Education, to promote the professional development and teaching ability of university faculty, to advance the integration of information technology with disciplinary teaching, and to provide a platform for faculty to exchange teaching experience and showcase their teaching.

The results were released by the Office of Academic Affairs and the Center for Modern Educational Technology.
