It is reported that a student entrepreneurship team from Shantou University won the Excellence Award at the second "Help You Start a Business, Win in Guangzhou" university student startup competition with its project, the "Multifunctional Smart Control Set-top Box."

The team consists of Zeng XJ (Class of 2010, Electronic Engineering), Huang CL and Liu MJ (Class of 2010, Finance), and Wu YT (Class of 2010, Communication Engineering). The project was completed under the guidance of teacher Yang Ge. The competition ran from April to September this year, with the final rounds held at Guangdong University of Technology.

According to reports, the competition was jointly organized by the Guangdong Provincial Employment Guidance Center for College Graduates and the Guangzhou Human Resources and Social Security Bureau. It attracted more than 1,300 registered projects, of which 455 advanced to the semifinals and 30 reached the grand final.
