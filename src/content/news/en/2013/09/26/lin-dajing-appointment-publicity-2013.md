It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Communist Party of China Shantou University Committee has recently issued a document (Shanda Zuzi [2013] No. 10), a publicity notice regarding the proposed appointment of Comrade Lin Dajing as Deputy Secretary of the Discipline Inspection Commission of Shantou University Medical College.

According to the notice, following deliberation at the 11th meeting of the Standing Committee of the STU Party Committee in 2013, Comrade Lin Dajing is proposed to be appointed Deputy Secretary of the Discipline Inspection Commission of Shantou University Medical College. In accordance with the requirements of the "Trial Measures for Pre-appointment Publicity of Cadres at Shantou University," Comrade Lin Dajing's basic information is hereby released for the scrutiny of faculty, students and staff.

## Publicity Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Document number | Shanda Zuzi [2013] No. 10 |
| Proposed position | Deputy Secretary of the Discipline Inspection Commission, Shantou University Medical College |
| Deliberative meeting | 11th meeting of the Standing Committee of the STU Party Committee in 2013 |
| Publicity period | Until October 9, 2013 |
| How to respond | Submit opinions in writing, in person or by phone to the Organization and United Front Work Department of the University Party Committee |
| Telephone | 86502345 |
| Email | o_zzb@stu.edu.cn |

## Requirements for Submitting Opinions

The notice states that units or individuals with differing opinions may report them before October 9, 2013. Opinions submitted on behalf of a unit must bear the official seal, while those submitted by individuals must be signed with a real name for accountability.
