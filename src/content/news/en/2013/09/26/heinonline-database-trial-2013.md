It is reported that the Library of Shantou University has recently opened trial access to the HeinOnline law journal database. The trial runs until October 31, 2013, during which students and faculty members may search and read the full texts free of charge.

HeinOnline is a well-known American full-text journal database centered on law and also covering politics, international relations, diplomacy and other fields. It is the world's largest and most comprehensive full-text legal literature database based on PDF browsing, covering more than 2,100 of the most authoritative law journals worldwide. It also includes 2,876 volumes of authoritative works on international law, more than 100,000 cases, over 4,000 classic legal academic monographs and the full text of U.S. federal government reports, giving it considerable academic value. The database has received a number of awards from the International Association of Law Libraries (IALL), the American Association of Law Libraries (AALL) and other organizations.

## How to Use

1. On the database homepage, click "Log into HeinOnline" on the right side of the page;
2. Enter the trial account provided by the library (username: shantou; password: ry7a3b2);
3. Click the Law Journal Library to enter the law journal search interface, or click the name of any other collection as needed.

The library welcomes active use of the database by students and faculty and asks users to send their evaluations and suggestions to the Subject Services Division of the Library (Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn).
