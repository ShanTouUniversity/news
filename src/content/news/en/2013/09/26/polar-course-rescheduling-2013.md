It is reported that, to accommodate guest expert lectures invited by the Institute of Marine Biology, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has announced rescheduling for two sessions of the *Polar Course*.

## Rescheduling Details

| Class No. | Original Session (Canceled) | Original Venue | Make-up Session | Make-up Venue |
|-----------|-----------------------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| 64323 | Week 3, Saturday (Oct. 5, 2013), Periods 3–4 | Building D, Room 503 | Week 4, Wednesday (Oct. 9, 2013), Periods 3–4 | Building E, Room 308 |
| 64323 | Week 4, Saturday (Oct. 12, 2013), Periods 3–4 | Building D, Room 503 | Week 5, Saturday (Oct. 19, 2013), Periods 1–2 | Building D, Room 503 |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in the course to share the updated schedule with one another and attend classes at the rescheduled times and venues.
