It is reported that the campus card precharge for 2013 undergraduate and postgraduate freshmen is 100 yuan, and the university carried out a second bank deduction on Sept 23. The Network and Information Center says a small number of students' payments still failed after that deduction, and their names are listed in the attachment to the OA notice.

## Cards to Be Frozen from Oct 23

The campus cards of these students will be frozen starting Oct 23. Once frozen, the cards cannot be used for purchases at canteens and shops, and can no longer receive various deductions and subsidies.

## Cash Payment Required by Oct 30

The Network and Information Center reminds these students to pay the precharge in cash at the campus card window. The deadline is Oct 30, 2013. Those who have still not paid successfully by then will have their cards reported to the university for account cancellation.

Earlier, on Sept 16, the university released the arrangements for the second deduction; this notice follows up on the results of that deduction. The center also asks students to pass the message on to one another.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Precharge amount | 100 yuan (2013 undergraduate and postgraduate freshmen) |
| Second deduction | Sept 23, 2013 |
| Card freeze | From Oct 23, 2013 |
| Payment method | Cash payment at the campus card window |
| Payment deadline | Oct 30, 2013 |
| Location | Campus card window, first floor of the Expert Apartment |
| Office hours | Mon–Thu 8:30–12:00 and 14:30–16:15; Fri 8:30–12:00 (the Friday afternoon is reserved for settlement, with no window service) |
