It is reported that *Night Singing Brother*, a stage production by Hong Kong professional a cappella theatre group Yat Po Singers and the opening show of Colorful Arts Season 2013 Autumn, drew an enthusiastic response after it was announced, and all tickets for the reserved performance were quickly distributed. To accommodate faculty and students who could not collect tickets but still wish to attend, the organizers have made special arrangements for standby seats left empty at show time.

## Standby Seating Arrangements

1. Faculty and students without tickets may queue in the designated area outside the main entrance of the Auditorium from 19:00 on September 27.
2. If, at 19:30 before the performance begins, seats for which tickets have been issued are still empty, the organizers will regard the ticket holders as having given up their rights, and staff will then lead the students queuing on site to those seats.
3. Seating is limited, and admission will stop once the venue is full.

Because the number of standby seats left empty cannot be predicted, those queuing may not be able to get in. The organizers also reminded audiences holding tickets to take their seats 10 minutes before the performance in accordance with the ticket terms, so that seats do not go unused.
