It is reported that the Russian National Ballet will perform the classic ballet *Swan Lake* at the Lin Bai Xin Convention and Exhibition Center in Shantou on the evening of October 7, 2013. The Labor Union of Shantou University has announced discounted ticket arrangements, under which STU faculty and students can receive a 15% discount with a Yitong Card.

## Performance Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Production | *Swan Lake* |
| Performing company | Russian National Ballet |
| Artistic director | Vyacheslav Gordeyev (Russia) |
| Date and time | October 7, 2013, 20:00 |
| Venue | Lin Bai Xin Convention and Exhibition Center Grand Hall |

## Ticket Prices and Sales

Ticket prices are set at RMB 180, 280, 380, 480, 680 and VIP 880. STU faculty and students can receive a 15% discount when purchasing tickets with a Yitong Card.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| On-campus sales | September 30, 2013, 09:30–17:30 |
| Sales location | Under the STU Labor Union building |
| Official website | http://teh.st001.com |
| Official contact number | 0754-88630100 |

The Labor Union advises those interested in purchasing tickets to visit the sales location during the designated period. Final ticketing details are subject to the organizer's arrangements.
