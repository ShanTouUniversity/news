It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold the fourth counselor regular meeting of 2013 at 3:00 p.m. on Monday, September 9, in Room 205 of the Science Building.

All counselors and relevant personnel in the student affairs system have been asked to arrange their schedules accordingly and attend on time. Such regular meetings serve as an important mechanism for coordination and communication within the university's student affairs system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
