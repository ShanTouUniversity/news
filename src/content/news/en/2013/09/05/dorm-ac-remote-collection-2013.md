It is reported that the air-conditioning units and the supporting power distribution lines in Shantou University student dormitories have been fully installed and are now in service. In a notice issued on September 5, the Student Affairs Office and the Resources Management Office asked each dormitory to send one resident to the duty room of its building to collect an air-conditioner remote control.

## Collection and custody

Students are required to sign an "Undertaking for the Collection and Custody of the Air-Conditioner Remote Control" when they collect the remote control, pledging to use the air conditioner reasonably and to keep the device in good care. The remote control must be returned in advance when a student leaves the university or checks out of the dormitory. If it is damaged through human error or lost, compensation will be charged at the set price.

## Reporting faults

If the air conditioner breaks down during use, residents can call the 24-hour service hotline 400-8365-365, which is also printed on the indoor unit. When reporting a fault, students should give the exact dormitory building and room number so that repairs can be arranged promptly.
