It is reported that the second half of the 2013 National Computer Rank Examination (NCRE) will be held in batches on September 21. To test the examination software and system environment, the Academic Affairs Office has scheduled a mock test for the afternoon of September 9 in Room E102.

Unlike previous sessions, all levels and language subjects will be examined in a paperless format starting with this exam. The mock test will be arranged on a first-come, first-served basis, and candidates may attend according to the following schedule.

## Mock Test Schedule

| Session | Time | Participants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First session | 15:00—16:00 | Candidates registered for Levels 3 and 4 |
| Second session | 16:00—17:00 | Candidates registered for Levels 1 and 2 |

Venue: Room E102

The Academic Affairs Office asks candidates to inform one another and to attend the session matching their registered level at Room E102, so that they can familiarize themselves with the examination software and system environment in advance.
