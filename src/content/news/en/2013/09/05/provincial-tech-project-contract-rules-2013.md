It is reported that, in order to further strengthen the management of provincial science and technology programs, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology has recently issued two documents: the Implementation Rules of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology on Contract Management of Provincial Science and Technology Programs (Trial) and the Implementation Rules of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology on Completion Management of Provincial Science and Technology Programs (Trial).

The Research Office of Shantou University has forwarded the two documents to relevant units on campus and asked them to comply with the rules.

The full texts of both sets of rules are contained in the attachments to the notice.
