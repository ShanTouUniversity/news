It is reported that, to give faculty and students, especially the 2013 freshmen, a better understanding of outdoor activities, the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association of Shantou University will hold an outdoor development activities showcase and exchange on the lawn in front of the Grand Hall from 4:30 p.m. on September 7 to 7:00 a.m. on September 8. The event is organized by the Student Affairs Office and the association.

## Activities

The program includes a display and exchange of wilderness survival skills, sharing of outdoor experiences, outdoor film appreciation, and an on-campus camping experience.

## Camping registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 20 |
| How to apply | Complete the application form and send it to snowleopardstu@163.com |
| Selection | Places will be drawn at random if more than 20 people apply |
| Deadline | Midnight on September 6 |

## Notes

- Please protect the lawn and the surrounding environment;
- Personal rubbish must be disposed of properly; bringing your own rubbish bags is recommended;
- Please follow the arrangements of the event staff;
- Do not bring valuables that are not needed for the event.

Further details are available on the association's Sina Weibo account, @汕头大学雪豹户外协会 (Shantou University Snow Leopard Outdoor Association).

> ⚠️ The application form and other event attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
