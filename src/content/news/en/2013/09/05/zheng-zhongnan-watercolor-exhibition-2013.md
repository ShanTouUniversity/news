It is reported that the exhibition "Beginnings: Zheng Zhongnan Watercolor Children Portraits" will be held from September 13 to 20 at the Gallery of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Shantou University.

The exhibition focuses on children as its subject and presents a body of figure paintings that Zheng Zhongnan created in watercolor. Running for one week, it is open to faculty, students and art lovers.

Further details about the exhibition can be found in the attachment issued with the notice.
