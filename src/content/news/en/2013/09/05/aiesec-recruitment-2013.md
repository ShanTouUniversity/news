It is reported that the AIESEC team of Shantou University issued a recruitment notice on September 5, 2013, to launch a series of recruitment activities open to all students.

AIESEC is a global, non-political, independent and not-for-profit organization run by students and recent graduates, with more than 80,000 members across over 2,400 universities in 124 countries and regions on five continents. Headquartered in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, it operates regional headquarters worldwide. The STU chapter was founded on June 18, 2012, with strong support from the Business School and is a key focus of AIESEC Mainland China.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | All students, any major or year |
| Information session | 20:00–22:00, September 8, Student Activity Hall (1st floor, Library), featuring team introduction, experience camp and Human Library |
| On-campus booth | September 5 at Jin San Jiao, accepting on-site applications |
| Interviews | Resume screening, group interview and individual interview; time and venue notified by SMS after screening |
| Deadline | 23:30, September 11, 2013 |
| How to apply | Drop the application form in envelopes at dorms A331, D240, E264, G322 or J316; collect a form at Jin San Jiao or Canteens 2 and 4 on September 5–6; or collect a form at the September 8 information session |
| Organizer | STU AIESEC Team, Business School |

Further details are available via Weibo @AIESECShantouUniversity and the WeChat public account. Students interested in volunteering abroad, internships overseas and building a global network are welcome to apply.
