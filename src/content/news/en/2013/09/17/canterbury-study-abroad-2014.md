It is reported that, under the student exchange agreement between Shantou University and the University of Canterbury in New Zealand, STU has been able to recommend outstanding undergraduates for a study abroad programme (a non-degree programme) at the University of Canterbury in Christchurch since 2013. The International Office has issued a notice opening applications for the 2014 programme.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Programme | University of Canterbury Study Abroad Programme (non-degree) |
| Academic year | Semester 1: February – June 2014; Semester 2: July – November 2014 |
| Application deadlines | Semester 1: November 1, 2013; Semester 2: May 1, 2014 |
| Eligibility | Enrolled undergraduates in their second year or above, ranked in the top 30% of their year in their school by GPA |
| English requirements | IELTS overall 6.0 with no band below 6.0; TOEFL iBT 80 overall with listening, reading and writing at 19 or above; TOEFL PBT 500 overall with a Test of Written English (TWE) score of 4.5 or above |
| Submission | Paper materials to be submitted to the International Office (Room 240, New Administrative Centre) by October 25 |
| Costs | Tuition, international travel, living expenses abroad, visa application fees and other costs are borne by the students |

## Eligibility

- Enrolled undergraduates in their second year or above;
- GPA in the top 30% of their year in their school;
- Mature and self-disciplined, with a strong academic motivation;
- IELTS overall 6.0 with no band below 6.0; TOEFL iBT 80 overall with listening, reading and writing at 19 or above; TOEFL PBT 500 overall with TWE 4.5 or above;
- Other conditions are set out in the programme handbook.

## Application Materials

| Material | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Application form | To be completed as required |
| Copy of passport | To be provided as required |
| Original transcript of completed courses | Stamped with the official seal of the Academic Affairs Office |
| Proof of English proficiency | IELTS, TOEFL or equivalent results |

## Costs

Students bear the costs themselves, including tuition, international travel, living expenses abroad and visa application fees.

## Application Procedure

Interested students should submit their paper materials to the International Office (Room 240, New Administrative Centre) by October 25. The International Office will issue a single list of recommended candidates to the University of Canterbury and send the application materials, in accordance with the agreement between the two universities. The University of Canterbury will decide the admission list on the basis of the application materials and send invoices and offer letters directly to students. After students complete the overseas academic year and return to STU, the Academic Affairs Office will assess and recognise their results according to the courses taken and the content taught. Enquiries can be sent by email to rghuang@stu.edu.cn.

## About the University of Canterbury

The University of Canterbury was founded in 1873 and is a public research university. It has five colleges — Business and Law, Arts, Education, Engineering, and Science — with more than 30 departments and over 100 programmes. It ranked 238th in the QS World University Rankings 2013–2014, with civil and structural engineering placed 21st, while education, psychology, law, geography, history, philosophy, linguistics, and accounting and finance ranked in the top 100. The university has about 850 teaching staff and researchers and more than 16,000 students, including over 1,200 international students from 80 countries and regions. It is located in the northwest of Christchurch, the largest city on New Zealand's South Island; Christchurch, the country's second-largest city, is known for its pleasant environment and is called the "Garden City".
