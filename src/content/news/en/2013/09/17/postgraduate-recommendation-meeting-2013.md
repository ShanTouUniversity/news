It is reported that Shantou University's postgraduate recommendation working group will hold a meeting at 15:00 on September 18 (Wednesday) to determine the university's proposed formal list of recommended candidates for 2014, under the program that allows outstanding final-year undergraduates to be exempted from the entrance examination for master's degree studies.

According to a notice issued by the Academic Affairs Office, the meeting will review nominations submitted by the university's colleges together with the 2014 recommendation quota allocated to Shantou University by the Teaching Department of the Ministry of Education, and will discuss and confirm the proposed candidates.

The university has asked the Business School, the College of Liberal Arts, the Law School, the College of Science, the College of Engineering, the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, the Medical School and the Graduate School to each send one representative to attend on time.
