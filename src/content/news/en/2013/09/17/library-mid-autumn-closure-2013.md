According to the library, in line with the notice on the university's 2013 holiday and class-adjustment arrangements issued by the university's Party and Administrative Office, the library will be closed for one day on the Mid-Autumn Festival in 2013.

The closure falls on September 19 (Thursday), the day of the Mid-Autumn Festival. Normal opening hours will resume afterwards.
