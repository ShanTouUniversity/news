The Student Learning Center (SLC) will be closed to users during the 2013 Mid-Autumn Festival holiday, the STU Student Affairs Office said, in line with the notice on the university's 2013 holiday and class-adjustment arrangements issued by the university's Party and Administrative Office.

Under the arrangement, the SLC will not be open from September 19 to 21. Normal opening will resume on September 22, when the discussion rooms and related services return to their regular schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
