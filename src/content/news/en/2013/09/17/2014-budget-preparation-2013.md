It is reported that Shantou University has issued the Notice on Preparing the 2014 Budget (Shanda Fa〔2013〕No. 109), formally launching preparation of the 2014 annual budget. Units have from September 17 to October 16 to prepare their proposals, which must first be submitted to their supervising university leader for review and emailed to the Finance Office by October 16.

| --- | --- |
| Preparation period | September 17 to October 16, 2013 |
| Submission | Email the proposal to o_cwc@stu.edu.cn by October 16, after review by the supervising university leader |

## Funding Categories Open to Application

Units may apply for three categories of special funding: teaching-reform projects, employment projects and self-proposed projects. Admissions-related funding is submitted centrally by the Admissions Office. Routine operating funds will be calculated by the Finance Office using fixed data such as employee numbers, student numbers and teaching hours, so units do not need to apply for them. Application arrangements for teaching-reform projects will be announced by the Academic Affairs Office. Employment special funds should be applied for around key performance indicators jointly set by the university and each unit, using Form 6 in Attachment 1. The university has also set up a "Discipline Development and Research Enhancement" special fund, with application arrangements to be announced by the Research Office. Self-proposed projects, corresponding to Form 7 in Attachment 1, are for work proposed by units to advance the university's development.

In addition, salary and benefit budgets for all university-employed staff are submitted centrally by the Human Resources Office and do not need to be included in unit proposals.

## Preparation Requirements

Units must complete the 2014 budget application forms provided in Attachment 1 as required. Form 1, the basic-data form for budget units, should be filled in according to authorized staffing and actual headcount in September. Form 2 covers target completion in 2012 and should record each unit's original targets and actual completion, together with a comparative explanation. Once completed, proposals must be sent to the unit's supervising university leader for review. The Medical College prepares its budget using its previous method or by referring to the main campus model.

## Points to Note

Units should pay close attention to the common problems identified during the review of the 2013 budget (see Attachment 2). When completing Forms 6 and 7, the amount applied for must be broken down and entered strictly according to the provided expense details; units may not create their own detail subjects. The accounting content of each expense detail subject is set out in Attachment 3. Units with questions during preparation may contact staff members surnamed Zhao (86502468) or Su (86503358).
