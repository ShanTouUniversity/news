The Student Reporters Team of the STU News Center has launched a new round of recruitment, inviting undergraduates of all majors to apply for positions as writing reporters, photography reporters and new media reporters. Interviews are scheduled for September 21.

## About the team

The Student Reporters Team is a student media organization supervised by the STU News Center. In the year since its establishment, the team has built working relationships with campus media and with student media at a number of peer universities, and has been invited to join the China University Media Union. Work produced by its members has been recommended for publication in off-campus newspapers such as *China Education Daily* and has won a number of national and provincial awards for outstanding journalism.

## Positions and requirements

Three categories of positions are open this round.

| Position | Main requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Writing reporter | Basic writing and critical-thinking skills, enthusiasm for reporting, and a proactive, careful and responsible approach to work |
| Photography reporter | A foundation in photography or an interest in the field, with a distinctive eye for images; writing ability is an advantage, and owning a camera is preferred |
| New media reporter | News sensitivity and graphic editing skills, a knack for spotting interesting stories, and enthusiasm for campus activities and social media |

## How to apply

Applicants should complete the application form and submit it to the team's designated collection point by September 20; the specific location is given in the original OA notice. Alternatively, they may download the electronic form and send it together with their work to `o_xb@stu.edu.cn`. Writing reporters must submit at least two literary works in any form, while photography reporters must submit at least three photographs on any subject. On-site registration will also be accepted at the interviews.
