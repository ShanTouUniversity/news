It is reported that, with the approval of the university, the Student Entrepreneurship Center of Shantou University is openly inviting student startup teams from across the university to run the second phase of the student entrepreneurship training site, currently known as the Coffee House. The recruitment is intended both to develop students' entrepreneurial spirit and ability and to meet the consumption needs of faculty and staff and enrich campus life.

## Operating objectives

According to the center, the site should offer consumers including Chinese and international faculty and staff a comfortable and convenient environment; food of good quality and taste at reasonable prices, with attentive service; and services that are gradually expanded in response to consumer demand.

## Basic operating requirements

The site covers a total area of 183 square metres. Because of these space constraints, operators may only heat finished products or handle semi-finished products on site; rough processing such as deep-frying, stir-frying and open-flame grilling is not allowed.

Operators must also strictly comply with the Food Safety Law, the Measures for the Hygienic Administration of School Canteens and Group Dining for Students in Guangdong Province and the Hygiene Requirements for Catering, and accept supervision by the university's canteen hygiene center. Water and electricity charges are borne by the contractor, who must pay site rent as agreed in the contract and follow the Guidelines for the Operation and Management of Student Entrepreneurship Training Sites.

## Competition and selection

Competing teams must submit a detailed management plan covering their operating model and management responsibilities, the layout of the site and equipment, processing flows, diagrams of hygiene facilities, food safety rules and regulations, health examination certificates for those taking part in the entrepreneurial practice, and training certificates for food safety managers.

Where a plan is practical and complies with the rules, the center will invite experts, staff from relevant departments and consumer representatives to form a review panel. The panel will make a comprehensive assessment based on team strength, previous entrepreneurial experience, asset investment and service commitments, and may ask competitors to defend their proposals in person.

The winning team must apply to the municipal Food and Drug Administration for a catering license and, once the license is granted, sign the Student Entrepreneurship Training Site Use Contract with the university.

## How to submit

Interested teams should seal their plan and submit five copies to the Student Entrepreneurship Center (Room 106, New Administration Building) by the end of the working day on September 30.
