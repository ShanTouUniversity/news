It is reported that the Sports Department of Shantou University has issued a notice in recent days on adjustments to the gym's equipment: part of the existing fitness equipment will be relocated to the air-supported basketball training hall, while a batch of new equipment will be brought into the gym.

Because of the replacement work, the gym is temporarily closed during the changeover and will reopen once the equipment has been fully replaced.

The department asked students and staff to bear with the inconvenience.
