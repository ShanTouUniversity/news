It is reported that the "China Education Television Shantou University Reporter Station", approved by China Education Television (CETV), has been formally established and has begun operations. The Shantou University Campus TV Station has meanwhile launched a new round of recruitment, inviting students from across the university to apply for positions as radio and television presenters, as well as writing reporters, camera reporters and designers. Applications close on September 23.

## Purpose of the recruitment

The Campus TV Station said the recruitment aims to better communicate university information, showcase Shantou University and enliven campus cultural life, while building an internship and practical training platform for students in related fields. The recruitment is open to students from the whole university, and those selected will take part in producing and broadcasting the station's programmes.

## Positions and requirements

| Position | Eligible applicants | Main requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presenter | Students enrolled in 2012 and 2013 | Enthusiasm for radio and television work, good on-screen presence; standard Mandarin and good communication skills; open to both men and women |
| Programme editor and planner | Students enrolled in 2012 and 2013 | Enthusiastic, careful and responsible, with ideas and a capacity for innovation; a good team player |
| Camera reporter | Students enrolled in 2012 and 2013 | Enthusiastic and careful, and above all responsible; an interest in camera work and a love of shooting; experience preferred |
| Designer | Students enrolled in 2011, 2012 and 2013 | Able to set up programme venues according to television production requirements; some ability to work in a team |

Holders of these positions may take on duties across more than one role.

## Appointment and rewards

Successful applicants will receive an appointment letter from the University Party Committee Propaganda Department (News Center), and those whose performance is assessed as outstanding will receive an annual award.

## Cooperation with China Education Television

Thanks to the cooperation with China Education Television (CETV), footage shot by students who join the station through this recruitment will be broadcast on China Education Television under their own names. The university said these videos can also serve as portfolio pieces when students apply for internships or job interviews.

## How to apply and interview arrangements

The application deadline is September 23, and on-site registration is accepted. Applicants may download the attachment "Shantou University Campus TV Station Recruitment Application Form", fill in the required information and send it to `o_gbds@stu.edu.cn`. The time and venue of interviews will be notified by text message or email after the application form is received; those who register on site may follow "@汕头大学广播电台" for the relevant arrangements.
