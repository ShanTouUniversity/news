The Human Resources Office of Shantou University has issued a pre-appointment publicity notice, inviting comments on a proposed mid-level management appointment. Following deliberation at the 11th University Leadership Meeting on September 17, 2013, the university proposes to appoint Deng Yong to a mid-level management post.

The publicity is conducted under the *Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres of Shantou University* (STU Party Committee Document No. 47 of 2009).

During the publicity period, units and individuals with differing views on the proposed appointment may submit written comments to the Human Resources Office by September 23, 2013. Statements submitted on behalf of a unit must bear an official seal, while those submitted by individuals must be signed with the person's real name.
