It is reported that since Shantou University launched its 2013 student extracurricular academic and technological research projects, the units concerned have carried out their work in accordance with the university's relevant regulations and requirements. To advance the later stages of this year's projects and assess current progress, the university has now begun the midterm review, asking the units and individuals concerned to complete the review and submit their materials on time.

## Submission requirements

The units and individuals concerned must complete the midterm review of the 2013 student extracurricular academic and technological research projects as required and submit a midterm summary. The electronic versions of the midterm summary and of the project list following the midterm review must be submitted to the Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` by 5 p.m. on September 29. Hard copies, stamped with the college seal, should be delivered to the Youth League Committee for archiving.

## Key information

| Item | Deadline | How to submit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midterm review and midterm summary | By 5 p.m., September 29, 2013 | Submit the electronic version to the Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` |
| Project list following the midterm review | By 5 p.m., September 29, 2013 | Submit the electronic version to the same email address |
| Hard-copy materials for archiving | Together with the electronic version as required | Deliver to the Youth League Committee with the college seal |

The notice was issued on September 17, 2013 by the Leading Group for Student Extracurricular Academic and Technological Activities of Shantou University.
