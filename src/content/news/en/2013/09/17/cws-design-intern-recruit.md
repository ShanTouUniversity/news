It is reported that the Women's Studies Center of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University has recently opened recruitment for one design assistant intern, with applications due by 5:00 p.m. on September 26.

## Position and Requirements

The position, "Intern – Design Assistant," is open to undergraduates and postgraduates of the 2011 and 2012 cohorts, with preference given to graphic design majors. The center is looking for candidates who are responsible, proactive, able to work independently, and skilled in poster and promotional material design, video production, and PowerPoint design.

Once selected, the intern will design lecture posters and other promotional materials, create e-cards and PowerPoint presentations, and take on other tasks assigned by the center.

## How to Apply

Applicants should complete the center's intern application form, name the file "Name + Application Form", and send it to the center's office email with the subject line indicating application for the internship. Candidates who pass the initial screening will be invited to an interview, where they should bring original or electronic copies of their work; final selections will be announced by phone.
