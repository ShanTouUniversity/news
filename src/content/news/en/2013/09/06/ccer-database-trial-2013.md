It is reported that the STU Library opened trial access to the CCER China Economic and Financial Database on September 6, and the trial will run until November 30, 2013.

The CCER database is provided by Sinofin Information Technology Co., Ltd. together with the National School of Development at Peking University. Its main databases cover the stock market, publicly offered funds, the bond market, the money market, corporate finance, industry statistics, national annual macroeconomic data, the special database, high-frequency data, fund related-party transactions and qualified foreign institutional investors (QFII).
