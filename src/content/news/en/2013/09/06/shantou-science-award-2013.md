It is reported that the Shantou Science and Technology Bureau has issued a call for nominations for the 2013 Shantou Science and Technology Award. STU's Research Office subsequently forwarded the notice to relevant campus units and is organizing applications. Faculty and project teams intending to apply should contact the Research Office; materials, once reviewed by the applicant's unit, must be submitted in full to the office by September 18.

## Application at a Glance

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Governing rules | Shantou Science and Technology Award Measures and its implementation rules (trial) |
| Eligibility | Projects evaluated and registered as scientific achievements; developed or applied within Shantou, or jointly developed with Shantou as first completing unit |
| Campus deadline | September 18, 2013 |
| Contact | Research Office |

## Eligibility Requirements

According to the notice, nominated projects must have completed achievement evaluation and official registration. They must also have been researched, developed or applied within Shantou, or developed through cooperation with institutions at home or abroad with Shantou as the first completing unit. Nominating units must obtain consent from the project's completing organizations and individuals before nomination, and any project with unresolved disputes over intellectual property or attribution may not enter review until the dispute is settled.

## Required Materials

Applicants must prepare the award recommendation dossier, including a recommendation form and attachments, in three copies printed double-sided on A4 paper and bound in order. For industrial and agricultural projects, attachments include the achievement appraisal certificate, research report, technical and economic benefit analysis, testing report, novelty search report, user feedback and intellectual property documents; medical projects additionally require the correspondence appraisal form and published papers.

Projects not yet registered as achievements must complete registration via the National Scientific Achievement Registration System V7.0. In addition, applicants must submit 26 copies of the project summary form and two copies of the application status form. Electronic copies are to be sent to the Research Office's designated email address.
