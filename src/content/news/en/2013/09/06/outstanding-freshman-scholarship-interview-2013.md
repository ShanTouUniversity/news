It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 6, 2013, announcing interview arrangements for the 2013 Outstanding Freshman Scholarship.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 08:30, September 9, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 118, Administration Center |
| Format | Leaderless group discussion |
| Participants | Shortlisted candidates for the Outstanding Freshman Scholarship |

## Procedure

1. Self-introduction by group members (no more than 1 minute per person);
2. Draw lots to determine the discussion topic (with 1 minute preparation time);
3. Leaderless group discussion (10 minutes);
4. Panel questioning (8 minutes).

Candidates are asked to prepare a table card with their name and attend on time according to the schedule in the attachment. The interview list and schedule are available in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The interview list and schedule are available on the OA system.
