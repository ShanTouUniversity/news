It is reported that STU Law School will hold Session 267 of its lecture series on the morning of September 7. Professor Ma Jingren, head of the Department of Public Administration at the College of Management, Shenzhen University, has been invited to speak on "Path Analysis of China's Political and Administrative Reform after the 18th CPC National Congress."

The lecture will run from 9:30 to 11 a.m. in Room 213 of the Law School and is open to faculty members and students across the university.

## About the Speaker

According to the speaker profile, Professor Ma Jingren is head of the Department of Public Administration and coordinator of the master's program at Shenzhen University's College of Management. He also serves as a member of the Ministry of Education's Public Administration Discipline Teaching Advisory Committee and of the Public Administration Specialized Committee of the National Examination Committee.
