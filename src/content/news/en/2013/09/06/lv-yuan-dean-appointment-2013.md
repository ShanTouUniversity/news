It is reported that Shantou University has issued Document No. 99 [2013], formally announcing the appointment of a new dean of the Business School: **Lyu Yuan** has been appointed dean of the Business School for a **six-month probationary period**, while **Xu Zongling** no longer serves as dean. The document was issued on September 6, 2013.

The appointment was decided at the **4th university leadership meeting of 2013**, held on **April 9**. Before the formal appointment, the university issued a pre-appointment public notice on **August 30** concerning Lyu Yuan's proposed appointment as Business School dean to seek feedback. The new document confirms the appointment after that publicity process.
