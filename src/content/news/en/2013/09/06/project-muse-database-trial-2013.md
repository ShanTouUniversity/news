It is reported that the Library issued a notice on September 6, 2013, to open trial access to the Project MUSE database.

Launched in 1995 by Johns Hopkins University Press and the Milton S. Eisenhower Library, Project MUSE aggregates over 540 journals from 180 publishers, with more than 250,000 articles dating back to 1993. Covering humanities, arts and social sciences, some titles link directly to JSTOR for earlier full texts.
