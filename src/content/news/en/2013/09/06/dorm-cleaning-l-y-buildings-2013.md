It is reported that to further improve the living environment, the Student Community Management Service Center of Shantou University will carry out a thorough cleaning of the interior floors and bathrooms of the dormitory buildings L and Y on September 7 and 8.

During the cleaning, each dormitory room is asked to arrange for at least one resident to stay in the room to assist the cleaning staff.

According to the arrangement, Building Y will be cleaned on September 7 and Building L on September 8, while Buildings B13 and B14 will be scheduled separately depending on the progress made in Buildings L and Y. As Building Y has a relatively large number of dormitory rooms, cleaning for some rooms may be postponed to September 8, and residents are asked to take note.

| Building | Cleaning Date |
| --- | --- |
| Building Y | September 7 |
| Building L | September 8 |
| Buildings B13 and B14 | To be arranged later based on progress in Buildings L and Y |
