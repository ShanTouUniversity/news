It is reported that the Finance Office of Shantou University has published an education fee disclosure notice, presenting the university's education fee items and standards in tabular form. The disclosure is intended to improve the transparency of fee collection and to help faculty members and students learn about the relevant fees.

Specific fee items and standards are available in the attachment on the OA system. Faculty members and students who wish to learn about the fee standards may consult the disclosure attachment or contact the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office)
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
