It is reported that to improve campus sanitation and strengthen plant pest and disease control, the Resources Management Office of Shantou University will spray green areas, flowers and trees, fruit trees and ditches on the campus and at the Adult Education College from September 9 to 15, along with efforts to eliminate the "four pests."

The Resources Management Office reminds faculty, staff and students to take care during the spraying period and avoid lingering in sprayed areas or touching plants and trees.

| Date | Spraying Area |
| --- | --- |
| September 9 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, the student dormitory area, the reservoir dam, Buildings 789, and faculty dormitories A-B |
| September 10 | Canteens, around the Administration Building, and the Administration Center |
| September 11 | Around the campus guard office, the campus gates, campus roads, and the perimeter of the new library |
| September 12 | The Medical Building, the new teaching building, and student dormitories G/H/I/J |
| September 13 | The artificial lake area and the sports field |
| September 14 | The Adult Education College |
| September 15 | From the Affiliated Middle School and Affiliated Primary School to the Mental Health Center |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
