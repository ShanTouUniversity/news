It is reported that, in accordance with the requirements of the Central Committee and the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee for the Party's mass line education and practice campaign, and after listening extensively to comments and suggestions from faculty members and students, the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee has made ten public commitments to the whole university on improving its work style, centering on serving the people, being pragmatic and honest, and opposing the "four forms of decadence." The commitments are dated August 30, 2013.

## The Ten Commitments

| Area | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Political discipline | Firm up ideals and convictions, implement the Party's education policy, and carry out the decisions and arrangements of the Central and provincial committees |
| The mass line | Conduct in-depth research at the grassroots level, maintain the system for university leaders to keep in contact with frontline units, and work to resolve pressing concerns raised by faculty and students |
| Study, writing and meeting styles | Cut down on meetings and paperwork and standardize assessment, inspection and commendation activities |
| Democratic centralism | Promote intra-Party democracy, follow the Standing Committee's rules of procedure for deliberation and decision-making, and seek a wide range of views on major decisions |
| Reform and innovation | Advance the university's internationalization and deepen its comprehensive reform |
| Solid, practical work | Set no targets divorced from reality and issue no decisions that have not been studied and assessed |
| Frugality | Manage and use university funds well, strictly control "three public" spending on official receptions, official vehicles and overseas trips, and regulate foreign affairs and reception work |
| Strict university governance | Govern the university in accordance with the law, explore a modern university system, and foster a sound campus ethos and academic atmosphere |
| Primary-level Party organizations | University leaders take part in branch organizational activities as ordinary Party members and keep in contact with, and provide guidance for, primary-level Party building |
| Clean conduct and self-discipline | Comply with the rules for clean governance, report personal matters as required, and accept no cash gifts, securities or the like |

The commitments have been made public across the university, and the Party Standing Committee has invited all faculty members and students to oversee their implementation.
