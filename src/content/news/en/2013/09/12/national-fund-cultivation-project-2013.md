It is reported that Shantou University's Research Office has begun accepting applications for the 2013 National Fund Cultivation Projects. Eligible serving faculty at the university's main campus may apply through their home units. Specific eligibility requirements follow the Measures for the Management of STU Academic Innovation Teams and National Fund Cultivation Projects, and applicants must complete the STU National Fund Cultivation Project Application Form.

### Priority funding areas

To encourage the development of research platforms in the humanities and social sciences, the university has again designated priority funding areas this year: local government development studies, Guangdong-Taiwan business cooperation studies, and Chaoshan history and culture studies. Applicants may select their own topics or develop and justify proposals within these areas. Projects in the priority areas must clearly identify their area in the application form; applications without a clear research object or problem focus will not be accepted. The university will give priority to qualifying proposals in approval and funding.

### How to apply and deadline

Applicants must submit one A4 copy of the application form plus an electronic version to their home unit. After the unit reviews the application, provides its opinion and affixes its official seal, it must deliver the Application Form and the Application List to the Research Office and send the electronic documents in a consolidated batch by October 10, 2013. Electronic documents for natural-science projects should be sent to yxhu@stu.edu.cn, and those for humanities and social science projects to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn. Late submissions will not be accepted.
