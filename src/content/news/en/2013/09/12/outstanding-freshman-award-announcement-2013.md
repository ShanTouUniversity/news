It is reported that Shantou University announced on September 12 the winners of its 2013 Outstanding Freshman Award Program. The proposed list had been open for public comment on September 11.

To encourage students who excel in moral, intellectual and physical development to apply to STU and to motivate them to keep studying hard after enrollment, the university established the Outstanding Freshman Award Program. This year's selection, based on students' own applications and interviews by the selection committee, resulted in 5 first-prize scholarships and 25 second-prize scholarships. In addition, in line with the regulations of the Outstanding Out-of-Province Female Student Award Program and this year's enrollment situation, 25 students received scholarships funded by corporate and individual donations.

## Awards at a Glance

| Award | Recipients |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Freshman First-Prize Scholarship | 5 |
| Outstanding Freshman Second-Prize Scholarship | 25 |
| Outstanding Out-of-Province Female Student Donor Scholarship | 25 |

The university hopes that the commended students will build on their achievements and strive for greater success, and that all freshmen will actively aim for excellence.

The full list of winners is available in the attachment to the original notice; student names are not reproduced here.
