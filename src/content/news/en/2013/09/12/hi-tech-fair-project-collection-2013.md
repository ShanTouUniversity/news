It is reported that the Research Office issued a notice on September 12, 2013, calling for participating projects from across the university for the 15th China Hi-Tech Fair (CHTF).

## Fair Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 15th China Hi-Tech Fair |
| Theme | Persisting in innovation-driven development and improving the quality of economic growth |
| Dates | November 16–21, 2013 |
| Venue | Shenzhen Convention and Exhibition Center |

## Focus Areas

The call covers fields including electronic information, new energy, energy conservation and environmental protection, advanced manufacturing, new materials and biomedicine, with emphasis on project results that feature independent innovation and hold independent intellectual property rights.

## Materials and Requirements

Interested project teams or faculty members must fill in the Hi-Tech Fair Project Registration Form and provide three to five high-resolution images of the project results. If physical exhibits or models are to be displayed, the required on-site floor space and exhibition requirements such as water and electricity must be specified. The above materials should be submitted to the Research Office by **September 19**.
