It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office has issued a notice on a teaching incident in which a final examination paper carried printed standard answers.

According to the notice, during the final examination of the course **Intermediate Financial Accounting (BUS2010)**, held on the morning of June 25, 2013, in E Staircase Room 101, the paper contained a serious error: standard answers were printed on 20 single-choice questions (30 points in total) and 10 true-or-false questions (15 points in total).

## Finding

In accordance with the relevant provisions of the university's Measures for the Identification of Undergraduate Teaching Incidents (Trial), the matter constitutes an ordinary teaching incident. The instructor of the course was identified as the person responsible and received a public reprimand.

## Reflection and Corrective Measures

The Academic Affairs Office said the flawed paper had passed through layers of review and signatures, exposing gaps in teaching management and a lack of conscientiousness among those involved, and that the office itself also bears responsibility. It will take measures to strengthen teaching quality assurance.

The office also reminded all academic units and teachers to strictly follow teaching management rules and regulations and to organize examinations conscientiously so as to avoid teaching incidents.
