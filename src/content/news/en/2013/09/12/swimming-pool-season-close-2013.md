It is reported that, as the weather gradually turns cooler, the Shantou University swimming pool will stop opening to the public from September 16 and will be used only for swimming class instruction.

Those who need information about the swimming class schedule may consult the Physical Education Department.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
