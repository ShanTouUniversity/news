It is reported that studying and implementing the spirit of a series of key speeches by Xi Jinping is an important part of the Party's ongoing mass line education and practice campaign. In accordance with the arrangements of the provincial Party committee and the university's campaign plan, the book Studying Xi Jinping's Key Speeches has been designated as key reference material for the campaign and is being distributed by the Organization and United Front Work Department of the STU Party Committee.

## Distribution Arrangements

Units are asked to collect the materials from the department before September 18 and to organize and mobilize Party members among faculty, students and staff to study them earnestly and gain a deep understanding.

The materials are issued to members of the university leadership team, cadres at the mid level and above, and secretaries of Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches, as well as grassroots Party branches, with one copy for each grassroots Party branch.

Study progress should be reported to the office of the university's campaign leading group in a timely manner.
