It is reported that the Student Learning Center (SLC) of the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 12, 2013, recruiting six duty staff from undergraduates across the university to meet its operational needs. Applications close at 10:00 pm on Saturday, September 21.

## Requirements

- A 2013 undergraduate freshman at STU's main campus
- A strong spirit of service
- A strong sense of responsibility, diligence and a friendly attitude
- Some planning and organizing ability

## Duties and Pay

Duty staff work at the SLC front desk and receive visitors, assist with the center's administrative affairs management and service-quality supervision, help promote SLC services, and take on temporary assignments. The work period covers the 2013–2014 academic year, with specific arrangements to be discussed in person; pay follows the university's work-study rates.

## Application and Interviews

Applicants should log in to the Student Work-Study Application System (http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn), fill in all information carefully, and submit the application form before 10:00 pm on Saturday, September 21. The SLC says that if there are too many applicants, a number of them will be shortlisted for interviews; the interview list will be published on Monday, September 23, and those not shortlisted will not be notified separately.
