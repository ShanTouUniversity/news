It is reported that the Discipline Inspection Commission of Shantou University forwarded a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection on September 12, 2013, on taking forceful measures to persistently correct the Four Winds (Document Yue Ji Fa [2013] No. 19), asking all units to implement it conscientiously.

## Key Requirements

Building on strict implementation of existing rules, the notice reiterates the “Five Absolute Prohibitions”:

| No. | Prohibition |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | No banquets between superiors and subordinates or between units in the name of festivals |
| 2 | No participation in galas, banquets or lotteries funded by enterprises |
| 3 | No commendation activities that distribute bonuses or goods |
| 4 | No giving or receiving gifts, cash gifts or securities paid with public funds |
| 5 | No performances or galas funded by public finance |

Units should publicize the spirit of the document to every faculty and staff member and address prominent problems related to the Four Winds.
