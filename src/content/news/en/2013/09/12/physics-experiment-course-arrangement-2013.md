It is reported that the Department of Physics of the College of Science has announced the arrangements for this semester's General Physics Experiment course. The first week will be devoted to a theory class covering error theory, and students will move into the laboratory for experiments from the second week.

The error theory class will be held separately for each course section. Students enrolled in the Monday section (class number 63289) will attend from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Monday, September 16, while those enrolled in the Tuesday section (class number 63290) will attend at the same hours on Tuesday, September 17. Both sessions will take place in Lecture Hall E203.

Students taking the course are asked to inform one another and attend on time.
