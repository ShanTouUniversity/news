It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on September 12, 2013, announcing that the fall 2013 adjustment course selection period has been changed in line with the university's Mid-Autumn Festival holiday arrangements. The adjusted window runs from 9:00 on September 16 to 17:00 on September 22.

According to the notice, students who need to submit paper course selection applications should hand them in during office hours within that period at the Academic Affairs Office service window on the third floor of the Administration Center. After the adjustment period ends, the Academic Affairs Office will officially release the fall semester course opening plan, and no requests for course selection changes will be accepted after 17:00 on September 22.

Course selection for this year's freshmen is tentatively scheduled to begin on September 12, as previously announced to new students.
