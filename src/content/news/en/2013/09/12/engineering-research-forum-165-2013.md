It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold the 165th Engineering Research Academic Forum on September 17, 2013, under the theme "Railway Reform and Research Opportunities." The speaker will be Professor Tianjian He of the University of Wollongong, Australia.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Railway Reform and Research Opportunities |
| Speaker | Professor Tianjian He, University of Wollongong, Australia |
| Time | 10:00 a.m., Tuesday, September 17, 2013 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 3, Academic Exchange Center (ACC Hall 3) |
| Participants | Postgraduate and doctoral students and related faculty of the College of Engineering; interested faculty and students are welcome |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |

The organizer welcomes faculty and students interested in the field to attend.
