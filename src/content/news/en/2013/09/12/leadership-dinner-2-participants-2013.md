It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 12 announcing that reservations for the second leadership-student dinner, a session dedicated to freshmen, have closed. Thirty-two students of the 2013 cohort reserved places successfully, and the list was released as an attachment to the notice. This article does not reproduce student names.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2nd Leadership-Student Dinner — Freshmen Session |
| Time | 6:00 p.m., Saturday, September 14, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Participants | 32 students of the 2013 cohort who reserved successfully |
| Arrival requirements | Bring your campus card and arrive 45 minutes early |
| List | Available in the attachment to the Student Affairs Office notice |

The Student Affairs Office asks students who reserved successfully to bring their campus card and arrive 45 minutes early, by 5:15 p.m., at Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant.

The dinner is dedicated to freshmen. The Student Affairs Office issued a reservation notice on September 10 inviting 32 students of the 2013 cohort, with sign-up closing on September 12. A university leader will host the event, where university leaders, heads of relevant administrative offices and faculty representatives will exchange views with participating students on university development and issues of interest to students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The list of the 32 students is available in the attachment to the OA notice; this article does not reproduce student names.
