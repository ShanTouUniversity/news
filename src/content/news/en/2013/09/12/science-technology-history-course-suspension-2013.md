It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 12, stating that seven sessions of the *History of Science* course across three classes will be suspended because instructor Chen Hongxi of the Institute of Higher Education is on leave. Make-up dates for all affected sessions have yet to be determined.

## Suspension Schedule

| Class | Day | Suspended date | Periods | Original venue | Make-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 64488 | Monday | Week 1, September 16 | Periods A-B | E Building Tiered Classroom 203 | TBD |
| 64488 | Monday | Week 2, September 23 | Periods A-B | E Building Tiered Classroom 203 | TBD |
| 64488 | Monday | Week 3, September 30 | Periods A-B | E Building Tiered Classroom 203 | TBD |
| 64489 | Tuesday | Week 1, September 17 | Periods 1-2 | E Building Tiered Classroom 201 | TBD |
| 64489 | Tuesday | Week 2, September 24 | Periods 1-2 | E Building Tiered Classroom 201 | TBD |
| 64490 | Wednesday | Week 1, September 18 | Periods 1-2 | E Building Tiered Classroom 201 | TBD |
| 64490 | Wednesday | Week 2, September 25 | Periods 1-2 | E Building Tiered Classroom 201 | TBD |

Class 64488 loses three consecutive weekly sessions, while classes 64489 and 64490 each lose two. No make-up dates have been set for the three classes. The Academic Affairs Office asks students taking these courses to inform one another and to watch for further notice.
