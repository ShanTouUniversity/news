It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication has decided, upon deliberation by its school affairs committee, to add two faculty members, Xu Chengqun and Xu Hui, to the college's Teaching Committee, in order to strengthen teaching management and advance disciplinary development and teaching reform.

## Teaching Committee Composition After the Adjustment

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Fan Dongsheng |
| Convener | Bai Jing |
| Members | Song Jihong, Wang Zong'an, Cheng Jinfu, Xie Lin, Hu Lufeng, Fan Linjun, Xu Chengqun, Xu Hui |
| Secretary | Lin Yan |
