It is reported that STU Library issued a notice on September 12, 2013, opening trial access to Oxford Scholarship Online (OSO) for faculty and students.

The library says OSO is a digital library developed by Oxford University Press that specializes in the publisher's finest scholarly monographs, whose authors include Nobel laureates and other world-class scholars. Titles admitted to the collection must be reviewed and approved by 12 members of Oxford University's academic committee. The database currently holds more than 8,000 scholarly monographs spanning the social sciences, the humanities and the natural sciences.

During the trial, faculty and students can reach the database through the library's database details page (http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=378). The library welcomes use of the resource and asks users to send trial evaluations and comments to the Subject Services Department (phone 86503247, email lqxie@stu.edu.cn).
