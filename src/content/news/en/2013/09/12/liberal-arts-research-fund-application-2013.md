It is reported that the Research Office issued a notice on September 12, 2013, launching applications for the 2013 Shantou University Liberal Arts Research Fund.

## Eligibility

According to the notice, applicants must be in-service faculty members in the humanities and social sciences appointed by the university's main campus, and must meet the following conditions:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project record | Must not have led a research project at or above the provincial or ministerial level |
| Research groundwork | Must have preliminary research related to the proposed topic |
| Review record | Applicants who have taken part in the National Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning Project application in the past five years and passed the correspondence review stage two or more times will automatically receive funding |

## Funding Categories

The fund is divided into two categories this year. Category A covers priority funding fields. Applicants may design their own topics, but must clearly indicate the field in the application; applications without a clear research object or question will not be accepted. The university will give priority to funding Category A projects. Category B covers self-selected topics.

| Category | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Category A (priority fields) | Local government development studies, Guangdong-Taiwan enterprise cooperation studies, Chaoshan history and culture studies |
| Category B | Self-selected topics |

## How to Apply

Applications are generally organized and submitted by each unit; individual applications will not be accepted. Applicants must fill in the STU Liberal Arts Research Fund Project Application Form and the Argumentation Sheet, and submit to their unit two copies of the application form printed double-sided on A4 paper, four copies of the argumentation sheet, and electronic versions of both.

After reviewing and signing the applications, each unit must deliver the paper application forms, argumentation sheets and the Application Summary Table to the Research Office by **October 10, 2013**, and send electronic versions to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn. Late submissions will not be accepted.
