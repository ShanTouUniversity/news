It is reported that the Network and Information Center issued a notice on September 12, 2013, relaying an arrangement by China Unicom Shantou Branch concerning the cutover of a fiber-optic line. Because of the cutover work, the line will be interrupted during the construction period, affecting access to Unicom websites for all campus users.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Reason | Unicom fiber-optic line cutover |
| Time | 00:00–05:00, September 13, 2013 |
| Scope | Access to Unicom websites for all campus users |
| Recovery | No further notice will be issued |
| Issued by | Network and Information Center |
