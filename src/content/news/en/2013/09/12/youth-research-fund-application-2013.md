It is reported that Shantou University's Research Office has opened applications for the 2013 Youth Research Fund to eligible young faculty at the university's main campus. The fund is positioned as a guiding fund that supports young researchers in carrying out research independently. In principle it does not provide repeat funding, and each faculty member may receive the university's Youth Research Fund only once.

### Eligibility

Applicants must be serving faculty members in natural-science disciplines appointed at STU's main campus, and must meet all of the following requirements: holding an intermediate or higher professional and technical title, or a master's degree or above; being aged 40 or under (including 40); having never led a research project at the provincial or ministerial level or above; and being dedicated to their work and capable of conducting research independently.

### How to apply and deadline

Applications must be organized and submitted by each home unit; individual applications will not be accepted. Applicants must complete the STU Youth Research Fund Project Application Form and submit one A4 double-sided printed copy together with an electronic version to their home unit. After the unit reviews and endorses the application, it must deliver the paper materials to the Research Office and send the electronic version to cszhu@stu.edu.cn by October 10, 2013. Late submissions will not be accepted.
