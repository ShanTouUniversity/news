It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University announced on September 12 the appointment list for graduate teaching and administrative assistant positions for the fall 2013 semester. The list was finalized after submissions from the university's host units for "three assistants" positions and review by the Graduate School.

According to the notice, each host unit is required to handle the appointment, management and assessment of the graduate students taking up the positions in accordance with university regulations. The notice also attached an assessment form for teaching assistants and one for administrative assistants to support the assessment work.
