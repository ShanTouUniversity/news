It is reported that the Chaozhou Embroidery Revival Project of Shantou University Enactus and the Shantou Intangible Cultural Heritage Center for Nail-Gold Embroidery will jointly hold the third Chaozhou Embroidery Cultural Camp for students from across the university.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | “Traditional Crafts on Campus” Third Chaozhou Embroidery Cultural Camp |
| Dates | October 4–7, October 13, October 19 and October 20 |
| Venue | Shantou Intangible Cultural Heritage Center for Nail-Gold Embroidery |
| Participants | All STU students |
| Organizers | STU Enactus Chaozhou Embroidery Revival Project and the Shantou Intangible Cultural Heritage Center for Nail-Gold Embroidery |

## Background

Chaozhou embroidery is a traditional handicraft with a long history in the Chaozhou region. The organizers said the camp aims to introduce more students to Chaozhou embroidery, provide opportunities to learn the craft and support the preservation and development of traditional embroidery.

## Registration

Interested students should complete the registration form and submit the required materials by 24:00 on October 2. On-site registration will also be available at Jin San Jiao on September 27. Further information is available through the official project account, “@STU Enactus Chaozhou Embroidery Revival Project.”
