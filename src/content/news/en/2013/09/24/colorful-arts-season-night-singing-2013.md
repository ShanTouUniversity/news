It is reported that the opening performance of Colorful Arts Season 2013 Autumn, run by the Art Education Centre of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, will begin at **7:30 p.m. on September 27** in the Auditorium, where the professional a cappella theatre group Yat Po Singers will present its new stage production, *Night Singing Brother*. The show will also raise the curtain on the seventh season of the Colorful Arts Season. Audience members are asked to take their seats 10 minutes before the performance begins.

Launched by the Art Education Centre in the fall semester of 2010, the Colorful Arts Season invites well-known artists and arts groups from home and abroad to give high-level performances on campus every semester, and it has now reached its seventh season. Yat Po Singers, invited back to STU this time, performed to a packed Auditorium and an enthusiastic response in 2012. Besides its new production, the group will be joined on stage by artists from Hong Kong and Taiwan.

## Ticket Collection

Tickets are available on September 26 from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon and from 2:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. at Room 309 of the Art Education Centre. Faculty and students should register with their personal campus card (one-card) to collect tickets, which are free on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last. Only audience members aged 12 or above are admitted.
