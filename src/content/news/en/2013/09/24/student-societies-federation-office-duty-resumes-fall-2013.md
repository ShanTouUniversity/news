It is reported that the office of the Shantou University Student Societies Federation officially resumed duty on September 24 (Tuesday), serving student societies at fixed hours from Monday to Friday.

## Duty Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Resumed on | September 24, 2013 (Tuesday) |
| Location | Room 731, Building 7 |
| Duty hours | 16:15–17:30, Monday to Friday |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Societies Federation |

After resuming duty, the Student Societies Federation office will handle matters related to student societies during the hours above.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, STU Student Societies Federation)
