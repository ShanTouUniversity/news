Shantou University College of Engineering has announced the class schedule for its fall 2013 service-learning course **Love in Education** (course code `SER1711`). The course covers three stages — training, field practice and a closing session — and runs from late September to late December.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Course code | SER1711 |
| Course | Love in Education |
| Training | September 25 and October 9, 2013 |
| Field practice | October 2 – December 14, 2013 |
| Closing session | December 21, 2013 |
| Details | Class times, venues and enrolled-student information are in the OA notice attachment |

Under the arrangement, training takes place twice, in late September and early October; the field practice stage runs from early October to mid-December; and a closing session is held before the course ends. The school reminds students enrolled in the course to prepare in advance and attend each stage on time.

> ⚠️ Detailed class times, venues and the list of enrolled students are available in the attachment to the OA notice and are not reproduced on this page.
