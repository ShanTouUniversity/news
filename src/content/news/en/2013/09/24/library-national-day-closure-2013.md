It is reported that, in line with the "Notice on the University's 2013 Partial Holiday and Class-Adjustment Arrangements" issued by the university's Party and Administrative Office, the Shantou University Library will be closed during the 2013 National Day holiday, suspending service from **October 1 to 3**, a total of three days.

All library services will be suspended during the closure, and readers may plan their borrowing and study schedules accordingly. Normal opening hours are due to resume on October 4; any further adjustments will be subject to the library's notices.
