It is reported that the China Mobile line (500 Mbps) serving the campus network's outbound traffic was disrupted on **the evening of September 23**. After contacting China Mobile Communications Shantou Branch, the Network and Information Center confirmed that the cause was a power failure at the company's Tuopu machine room.

The campus network has four outbound lines, run by China Telecom, China Unicom, CERNET and China Mobile respectively. Following the outage, the Network and Information Center immediately took emergency measures to safeguard normal network access for faculty and students across the university. Because total outbound bandwidth has fallen by nearly half, users' connection speeds have dropped somewhat, but the network is generally stable.

The Network and Information Center said that no separate notice will be issued on when the line is restored. Anyone with questions may call `86503400` or send an email to `stunic@stu.edu.cn`.
