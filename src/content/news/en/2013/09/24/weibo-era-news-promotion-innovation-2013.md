It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication issued a notice on September 24, 2013, that its continuing education course *Innovation in University News and Publicity Work in the Weibo Era* will soon open, and enrolled students are asked to attend on time.

## Course Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | *Innovation in University News and Publicity Work in the Weibo Era* |
| Instructor | Fan Dongsheng |
| Time | 10:00–12:00, September 27, 2013 |
| Venue | Room D304, Teaching Building |
| Registration | Log in at http://training.stu.edu.cn with campus network account and password |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |
