It is reported that a lecture titled “A Journey of Antarctic Exploration” will be held on the evening of September 27 (Friday), as one of the lectures in Shantou University’s Antarctic Program series. The speaker will be Cao Jun, vice president of the Shenzhen Mountaineering and Outdoor Sports Association, who will introduce Antarctic exploration and share his experiences with faculty and students.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | A Journey of Antarctic Exploration |
| Speaker | Cao Jun, Vice President of the Shenzhen Mountaineering and Outdoor Sports Association |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, September 27, 2013 (Friday) |
| Venue | Meeting Room 5, Academic Exchange Center |
| Main Content | Background on Antarctica, adventure travel routes to Antarctica, base camp facilities, emperor penguin expeditions, the Vinson Massif climb, hiking one degree of latitude, camp life and Antarctic scenery |
| Participants | All faculty and students |

## Lecture Content

According to the organizers, the lecture will cover background knowledge about Antarctica, adventure travel routes to the continent and the facilities at Antarctic exploration base camps. The speaker will also share experiences including emperor penguin expeditions, the Vinson Massif climb and hiking one degree of latitude, as well as camp life and the scenery of Antarctica.

The lecture is open to all faculty and students, and those interested are welcome to attend.
