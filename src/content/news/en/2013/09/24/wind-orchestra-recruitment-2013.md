It is reported that the Shantou University Wind Orchestra is recruiting new members from across the university, with auditions scheduled for the afternoon of Wednesday, September 25. Students interested in joining may register on site.

## Audition Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Audition time | 2:00 pm–6:00 pm, Wednesday, September 25 |
| Venue | Second floor of the Art Education Center (next to Building 789) |
| Registration | On-site registration |
| Instruments sought | Flute, piccolo, clarinet, oboe, bassoon, saxophone, French horn, trumpet, trombone, euphonium, tuba and percussion |
| Audition content | A piece of the candidate's own choice |
| Instruments | Provided by the Art Education Center; candidates may also bring their own |

According to the notice, students who do not play any of the instruments listed above but have a foundation in other instruments are also welcome to audition. More information is available on the orchestra's official Weibo account, @汕头大学交响管乐团 (Shantou University Wind Orchestra).

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center)
