It is reported that a poster design exhibition jointly presented by four classes of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design opened on September 24 and runs through October 7 at the college's art museum and first-floor hall.

The exhibition brings together poster works by students of 2010 Graphic Design Class 1 and Class 2 and 2011 Graphic Design Class 1 and Class 2, with Han Ran and Cai Qizhen serving as advisors. Organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, the exhibition is open to faculty members and students, who are welcome to visit.
