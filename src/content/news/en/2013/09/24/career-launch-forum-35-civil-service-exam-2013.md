It is reported that the 35th session of Shantou University's Career Launch Forum will be held on the evening of September 26, offering free guidance on preparing for the 2014 National Civil Service Exam, including an analysis of the exam and revision advice for students who plan to take it.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 19:00–21:00, September 26, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall III |
| Topic | Free guidance on preparing for the 2014 National Civil Service Exam |
| Speaker | Hu Weiling |
| Highlights | Career planning and the importance of a first job; analysis of past exams and the latest trends for the 2014 exam; high-score preparation guidance and revision advice |

## Lecture Content

According to the organizers, the lecture will cover three areas: career planning for university students and the importance of their first job; an analysis of past National Civil Service Exams and an interpretation of the latest trends for the 2014 exam; and guidance on high-score preparation and revision.

## About the Speaker

Hu Weiling is a veteran researcher and trainer in civil service exam preparation with distinctive insights into the quantitative relations section. She took part in the review and revision of the 2012 Guangdong Administrative Aptitude Test Textbook. Her teaching is rigorous and focused on clarifying problem-solving approaches and summarizing techniques, and she has delivered lecture tours at a number of well-known universities in China.
