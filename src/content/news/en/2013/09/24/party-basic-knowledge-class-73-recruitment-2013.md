It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee has issued a notice stating that the 73rd Party basic-knowledge study class is scheduled to begin in **mid-October**, and has asked Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to nominate candidates, carry out preliminary reviews and submit the required materials. Quota allocations and the application form are provided in the OA attachments.

According to the notice, every nominee must have submitted a Party membership application at least one year earlier and have been designated as a key development candidate. After the application form is signed and stamped by the nominee's Party general branch or directly affiliated Party branch, it must be submitted together to the Party School Office. At the same time, the electronic version of the consolidated list of nominees should be sent to the designated email address. The Party School Office will finalize the list of participants after a further review.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Class start | Mid-October 2013 |
| Nomination requirements | Applicants who submitted a Party membership application at least one year earlier and have been designated as key development candidates |
| Submission deadline | By September 30, 2013 |
| Submission location | Party School Office (Room 315, New Administrative Center) |
| Email | `o_dxb@stu.edu.cn` |

> ⚠️ The quota allocation table, application form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee)
