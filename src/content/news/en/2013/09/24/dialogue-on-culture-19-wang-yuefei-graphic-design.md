It is reported that the 19th session of the "Dialogue on Culture" series organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art & Design (CKAD) will be held at 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, September 24, at the first-floor lecture hall of the school.

Themed "Graphic Designer and Military Enthusiast," the session will feature a dialogue between Wang Shouzhi, Dean of CKAD, and Wang Yuefei, a renowned graphic designer, exploring graphic design practice alongside personal interests.
