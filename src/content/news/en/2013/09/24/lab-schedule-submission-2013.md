The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has assigned teaching units the task of compiling laboratory course schedules for the fall semester of the 2013/2014 academic year, asking them to organize their undergraduate teaching laboratories to complete and submit the forms in time.

## Submission Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Who must report | Teaching units, which are to organize their undergraduate teaching laboratories |
| Deadline | Forms to be completed and submitted by September 30, 2013 |
| How to submit | Electronic documents first, followed by printouts; electronic files should be sent to yzchen@stu.edu.cn |
| Retention requirement | Printouts are to be made in duplicate and collected by each department's laboratory director: one copy goes to the Academic Affairs Office, the other is kept by the department's academic affairs officer |

According to the notice, compiling laboratory schedules is a routine part of teaching preparation each semester, and teaching units are expected to work through the items one by one so that the schedule information is accurate and complete and reaches the office on time.

> ⚠️ The laboratory course schedule form is an attachment to the OA notice and is not reproduced on this news page.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
