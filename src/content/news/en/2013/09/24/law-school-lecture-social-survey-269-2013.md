It is reported that the 269th lecture in Shantou University Law School's lecture series (No. 269) will be held from **4:00 to 5:30 p.m. on September 27, 2013** in Room 213 of the Law School. Li Wei, director of the Social Development Research Office at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and an associate researcher, has been invited to speak on "Social Surveys: The Chinese Practice." The lecture is open to all faculty and students.

## About the speaker

According to the organizers, Li Wei received his doctoral degree from the Graduate School of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences in 1999. His research focuses on the sociology of development and economic sociology. He is currently director and associate researcher of the Social Development Research Office at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, and serves as the chief executive of the influential Chinese Social Survey (CSS) project.

## About the lecture

The lecture will discuss how to select suitable social survey data for academic research. It will introduce the features and influence of China's major micro-level social survey datasets, including CHIPS, CGSS, CFPS, CHFS, CLDS and CSS, explain how a rigorous, internationally recognized Chinese social survey dataset is produced, and share the experience, results, practices and frontier issues from more than 20 years of social surveys in China.
