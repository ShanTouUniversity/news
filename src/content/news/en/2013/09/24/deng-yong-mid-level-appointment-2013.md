It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University issued the "Notice on the Appointment of Deng Yong to a Mid-Level Post" (Shan Da Ren [2013] No. 2). Following deliberation at the 11th university leadership meeting on September 17, 2013, the university decided to appoint Deng Yong to a mid-level management post, with a six-month probation period. The proposed appointment had previously been publicized on September 17 in accordance with the relevant regulations on the appointment of management cadres.

The notice was issued by the Human Resources Office on September 24, 2013.
