It is reported that, in accordance with the notice of the Guangdong Provincial Education Examinations Authority on the 2013 second-half national College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 (Yue Kao Yuan [2013] No. 279), registration for the exams has begun. This round of exams will be held on December 14, with CET-4 running from 9:00 to 11:20 and CET-6 from 15:00 to 17:20.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exam date | December 14, 2013 |
| CET-4 time | 9:00–11:20 |
| CET-6 time | 15:00–17:20 |
| Registration method | Log in to the credit-based course selection website and complete the steps "I Want to Take Courses" → "Qualification Exam Registration" |
| Online registration | September 4–21, 2013 |
| Fee | 38 yuan |
| Fee deduction | Deducted from campus card accounts by the Network Center on September 22; if unsuccessful, deducted from tuition accounts by Bank of China on the morning of September 23 |
| Required documents | ID card, student ID and admission ticket — all three are required |

## Eligibility

| Level | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| CET-4 | Undergraduates other than first-year students whose English placement level is Level 2 or above this semester; art undergraduates must have completed ELC EA3 or passed the Practical English Test; English majors enrolled in 2012 |
| CET-6 | A CET-4 certificate or a CET-4 score of 425 or above; a TEM-4 (English majors) certificate |

Students who already hold a CET-6 certificate or scored 425 or above may register again, provided that undergraduates have completed ELC4 and do not retake the exam more than twice.

## Registration and Payment

Registration is conducted entirely online. Students must log in to the credit-based course selection website and complete the process by clicking "I Want to Take Courses" → "Qualification Exam Registration." The registration period runs from September 4 to 21.

The registration fee is 38 yuan. For both graduate and undergraduate students, the fee will be deducted from campus card accounts by the Network Center on behalf of the Finance Office on September 22; if the deduction fails, Bank of China will deduct the fee from tuition accounts on the morning of September 23 on behalf of the Finance Office. Students are reminded to keep sufficient balance during this period — a failed deduction means a failed registration.

## Notes

- Non-enrolled candidates may not register for this round of the exams.
- Candidates must bring their ID card, student ID and admission ticket to the exam; all three are required, with none to be missing.
