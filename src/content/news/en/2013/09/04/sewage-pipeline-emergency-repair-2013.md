It is reported that, with the university's approval, the Capital Construction Section of Shantou University's Resources Management Office has launched an emergency repair project on the campus sewage pipeline, commissioning Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. to carry out the work.

The repairs cover part of the sewage pipeline on the side of the pedestrian street facing the sports field. Work began on September 4 and is scheduled for completion before September 15.

## Project Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contractor | Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Scope | Part of the sewage pipeline along the pedestrian street by the sports field |
| Start date | September 4, 2013 |
| Expected completion | Before September 15, 2013 |

The university said that the work may cause noise and dust in the surrounding area and asked for the understanding of staff and students. Pedestrians and vehicles passing the construction zone were also reminded to stay alert and mind their safety.
