It is reported that the campus radio station of Shantou University has launched a new round of recruitment, open to students across the university. Applications close on September 7, and on-site registration will be accepted that day; interviews are also scheduled for September 7.

## Departments and Requirements

| Department | Main requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Broadcasting Department | Enthusiastic and sincere, with good expression and communication skills |
| Reporter Department | Passion for reporting and solid writing skills; English reporters need good English writing ability |
| Publicity Department | Artistic or drawing skills, or familiarity with software such as Ps and Ai; familiarity with platforms such as Weibo and WeChat |
| Secretary Department | Responsible and conscientious, familiar with office software, with planning skills |

## Application and Interview Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Who can apply | All STU students, regardless of year or major |
| Application deadline | September 7, 2013 (on-site registration accepted) |
| How to apply | Download the application form (attachment), fill it in and send it to the designated email address; the address is available in the original OA notice |
| Interview time | September 7, 2013, 8:00–12:00 and 13:00–18:00 |
| Interview venue | Announced on the station's Sina Weibo account, @汕头大学广播电台 |

The radio station welcomes students interested in campus broadcasting and news work to apply.

> Source: STU OA Notice (News Center)
> ⚠️ The application form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
