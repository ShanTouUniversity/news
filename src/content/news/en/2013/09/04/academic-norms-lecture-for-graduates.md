It is reported that on the afternoon of September 5, 2013, the Graduate School of Shantou University will hold a lecture on academic norms at the Science Lecture Hall, aimed at strengthening scientific ethics and research integrity among graduate students.

The lecture, themed "Scientific Ethics and Academic Norms," will be delivered by Professor Wulanhasi, Vice President of Shantou University, running from 3:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. All graduate students of the class of 2013 were required to attend, and faculty members across campus were also welcome.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Scientific Ethics and Academic Norms |
| Speaker | Professor Wulanhasi, Vice President of STU |
| Time | September 5, 2013, 15:00–16:30 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Audience | Class of 2013 graduate students (faculty welcome) |

According to the Graduate School, such lectures are intended to uphold academic integrity and dignity, further raise the university's standards of scientific conduct and academic culture, and improve the quality of talent cultivation.
