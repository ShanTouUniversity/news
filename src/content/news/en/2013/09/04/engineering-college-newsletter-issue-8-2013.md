It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University released Issue No. 1 of 2013 (No. 8 overall) of the *Shantou University College of Engineering Newsletter* on September 4. With sections such as "College Highlights" and "Work Updates", the issue reviews the college's recent progress in teaching reform, research and international exchange.

## College Highlights

- The college took part in the 2013 CDIO Asian Regional Meeting;
- The STU–University of Calgary CDIO exchange program was completed successfully;
- The college co-hosted the 2012 China CDIO Engineering Education Annual Conference with Guangzhou University;
- An STU team won second prize in the CDIO Academy student project competition at the 9th International CDIO Conference;
- The college held its work summary and commendation meeting for the 2012–2013 academic year;
- Associate Professor Yu Jin received the 2012–2013 Li Ka Shing Foundation Award for Outstanding Teaching.

## Work Updates

**Undergraduate teaching.** The college held a meeting on program development and the curriculum system, and received delegations from five universities, including the Beijing University of Technology, that came to study its engineering education reform. It sent 10 students to the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology for summer study, and 3 students qualified for exchange study at Ningbo University. One national-level off-campus practice education base for university students was approved, and 12 projects were approved under the 2013 university student innovation and entrepreneurship training program.

**Research.** Nine faculty members of the college received 2013 grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China. From January to August, 30 new research projects of various types were added, with total funding of RMB 10.2682 million. The issue also reports on enrollment for the 2013 Master of Engineering program.

**Academic exchange.** Professor Hiroshi Yamaguchi, a member of the Engineering Academy of Japan, visited the college, as did a number of other scholars from China and abroad.

**Student affairs.** College of Engineering students won awards at the second Guangdong University Students' Comprehensive Engineering Training Ability Competition and achieved good results in their first participation in the Guangdong Java Programmer Competition.
