It is reported that, to implement the notice issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on a special audit survey of research funding management at provincial universities and to carry out the self-inspection of research project funding management, Shantou University's leading group for the research funding management self-inspection will hold a working meeting at 10:00 a.m. on September 5 at the Science Lecture Hall to lay out the self-inspection work.

According to the arrangements, attendees include the leaders in charge of research work at each unit, the staff designated by each unit to take charge of this self-inspection, and the principal investigators of research projects that were underway in 2011 and 2012.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 10:00 a.m., September 5, 2013 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Main Agenda | Laying out the self-inspection of research project funding management |
| Attendees | Leaders in charge of research work at each unit; staff designated by each unit for this self-inspection; principal investigators of research projects underway in 2011 and 2012 |

The meeting notice was issued by the leading group for the research funding management self-inspection on September 4, 2013.
