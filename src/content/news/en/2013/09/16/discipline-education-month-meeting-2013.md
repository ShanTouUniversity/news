It is reported that Shantou University will hold a kick-off meeting for the 2013 Discipline Education Month at 15:00 on September 18 in Room 213 of the New Administrative Centre to set out arrangements for this year's programme. The meeting is part of the university's effort to implement the spirit of the Notice on Launching the 2013 Discipline Education Month at Shantou University.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00, September 18, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administrative Centre |
| Agenda | Arrangements for the university's Discipline Education Month activities |
| Attendees | Principal leaders of the Medical College Party Committee and of each Party general branch and directly affiliated Party branch at the main campus |
| Contact | 86502346 |

Principal leaders of the Medical College Party Committee and of Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches at the main campus are asked to attend on time; matters related to the meeting can be raised with the Discipline Inspection Commission Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Commission Office)
