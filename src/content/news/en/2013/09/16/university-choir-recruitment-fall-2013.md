It is reported that the Shantou University Choir is now recruiting new members from all students on campus, with no special music background or instrument skills required — anyone interested in singing may sign up. The recruitment is jointly organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Art Education Center.

## Audition Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Who May Apply | All students (no special music background or instrument skills required; anyone interested in singing is welcome) |
| Audition Times | 14:00–16:30, Wednesday, September 18, 2013; 9:00–12:00 and 14:00–16:30, Saturday, September 21, 2013 |
| Venue | Music Rehearsal Hall, 1st Floor, STU Building, Art Education Center |
| Registration | On-site registration at the audition |

## Audition Content

According to the organizers, the audition consists of two parts: pitch-matching and the performance of a self-selected song excerpt. There are no restrictions on song genre; students with instrumental skills may bring their instruments for a live demonstration, and self-provided accompaniment is allowed.

Students who would like to learn more can follow the choir’s official Weibo account (weibo.com/stuchorus).

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design / Art Education Center)
