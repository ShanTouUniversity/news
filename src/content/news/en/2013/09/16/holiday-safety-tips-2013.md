With the Mid-Autumn and National Day holidays approaching, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued holiday safety tips, reminding all students to take precautions during travel, when leaving or staying on campus, and during outdoor activities so that they can enjoy a safe holiday.

## Travel and Property Safety

The office advises students who plan to travel to pay attention to safety advisories for their destinations, to choose properly licensed travel agencies, and to consider buying travel insurance. During the trip, cash and valuables should be stored and kept safely, doors and windows should be closed and locked when leaving accommodation, and students should inform relatives, family members or teachers of where they are going.

## Transport and Personal Safety

When leaving campus, students should buy tickets and board buses at lawful, licensed passenger stations, obey traffic regulations, refuse to ride in unlicensed or overloaded vehicles, and never drive without a licence. When going into the countryside or visiting relatives and friends, they should travel in groups as far as possible, take care of their personal safety and property, guard against being cheated, robbed or stolen from, and avoid displaying valuables in public places.

## Fraud Prevention and Emergency Response

The office also reminds students to raise their awareness of self-protection and to guard against fraud through false information, phone calls and online shopping. In the event of an emergency, they should stay calm and, when necessary, discuss how to handle the situation with classmates or teachers.

## Staying on Campus and Fire Safety

Students staying on campus during the holiday must observe the university's daily schedule and safety management rules, must not let outsiders stay overnight, and must not allow strangers to enter their dormitories. As autumn and winter bring dry, windy weather, fire safety deserves particular attention: open flames and high-power appliances such as immersion heaters ("red-hot" water heaters) are strictly prohibited in dormitories; fishing and swimming are not allowed at reservoirs or other dangerous waters; candles, fireworks and sky lanterns must not be lit during celebrations; fires must not be started in no-fire zones; and carrying open flames up hills or having barbecues there is strictly forbidden.

Students are also asked not to climb over walls, railings or fruit trees, and in particular not to step on the sunshade panels on the second floor of the student dormitory area. Any abnormal situation should be reported to the university promptly.
