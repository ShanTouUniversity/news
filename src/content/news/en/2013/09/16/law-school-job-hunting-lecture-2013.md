It is reported that, to help students master job-hunting methods and interview skills and land a satisfying job, the Law School of Shantou University will hold a talk titled “Job-Hunting Experience”, part of its career guidance lecture series, featuring STU alumnus Su Xiangwu, deputy section chief of the Shantou Municipal Government Office, who will share his own job-hunting experience with students.

According to the organizers, the talk will cover job-hunting preparation and mindset adjustment, career choices, channels for finding a job, and strategies for the civil service examination — issues of wide concern among students. Interested students are welcome to attend.
