It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will take all of its 2013 freshmen to Lianhua Mountain in Chenghai for a two-day outdoor training camp on September 20–21. The school will hold a general mobilization meeting at 15:30 on September 18 in Room E101, and all 2013 students and 2012 team leaders are required to attend on time.

The school said that, guided by Mr. Li Ka-shing's educational philosophy of "Aspiration, Knowledge, Perseverance and Achievement," the Business School runs extracurricular activities oriented toward developing the professional competence and professional qualities of a new generation of business administrators. The Freshman Outdoor Training Camp is the school's most important orientation activity, designed to foster self-reliance, perseverance, a willingness to take on challenges, a spirit of continuous striving and an enterprising attitude, while also developing student leaders' leadership and organizational skills.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Freshman Outdoor Training Camp |
| Date | September 20–21, 2013 |
| Venue | Lianhua Mountain, Chenghai |
| Participants | All 2013 freshmen of the Business School |
| Activities | Team building, hiking and peak climbing, wilderness survival, social activities, development training |

## Notes for Participants

- Students unable to take part for health or other special reasons must submit a written leave request to the Business School Youth League office by 17:00 on September 18;
- Participants must strictly observe the daily schedule and activity rules and follow instructions during the event;
- Participants should bring their own sportswear and sports shoes.

## Pre-departure Meeting

The school will hold a general mobilization meeting at 15:30 on September 18 in Room E101. All 2013 students and 2012 team leaders are required to attend on time, and each team and group should complete team building and prepare supplies before departure.
