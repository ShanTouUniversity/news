It is reported that the Student Learning Center (SLC) of the Student Affairs Office is recruiting tutors for the fall 2013 semester to meet needs related to academic inquiries and course offerings. The recruitment is led by SLC student liaisons in each school.

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Undergraduate students of the Shantou University main campus (second year and above) |
| Criteria | Strong sense of responsibility, conscientious work, a friendly attitude and good communication skills; excellent academic performance, with grades in the course to be tutored ranking within the top 20 percent of that course’s assessment (in special cases, certification from the course instructor or another teacher familiar with the situation is required) |

## Responsibilities

Tutors are expected to foster a culture of self-directed learning, share course study methods, experience and insights, patiently answer students’ questions, and organize discussions in their subject areas so that students develop the habit of independent learning and exploration. They also need to understand the course progress and needs of the students they serve and proactively provide suitable services. The regular working period is Weeks 3 to 16 of each semester, and pay follows the university’s work-study tutor standard.

## How to Apply

Prospective applicants may consult the SLC student liaison of their school about the specific recruitment requirements for the course they wish to tutor, and must submit an application form through the Student Work-Study Application System (http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/) before 22:00 on Friday, September 20.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | 22:00, Friday, September 20, 2013 |
| How to apply | Submit an application form through the Student Work-Study Application System (http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/) |
| Inquiries | Contact the SLC student liaison of your school about the requirements for the target course |
| SLC contacts | Phone 86502250, email slc@stu.edu.cn, website http://slc.stu.edu.cn |
