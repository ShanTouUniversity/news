It is reported that Shantou University's Network and Information Center announced on September 16 that, so that 2013 freshmen (including graduate freshmen) could use their campus cards immediately after collecting them on arrival day, the university had prepaid RMB 100 into the freshmen's campus card accounts. On September 10, the Finance Office commissioned Bank of China to carry out the first batch deduction from the freshmen's accounts, but the deduction failed for 98 students.

## Second deduction arrangement

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Affected students | 98 freshmen (undergraduate and graduate) whose first deduction failed |
| Prepaid amount | RMB 100 per student |
| First deduction | September 10, conducted in batches by Bank of China on behalf of the Finance Office |
| Second deduction | September 23, to be commissioned by the Finance Office |

The Network and Information Center reminds these students to deposit RMB 100 into their Bank of China accounts from now on so that the second deduction can be completed successfully. The list of students whose deductions failed is provided in the OA attachment.
