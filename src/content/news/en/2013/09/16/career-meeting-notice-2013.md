It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a notice announcing that a graduate employment work meeting will be held at 16:30 on Tuesday, September 17, 2013 in Meeting Room 213 of the New Administration Building.

According to the notice, Party general branch secretaries (and deputy secretaries) of all colleges (residential colleges), counselors responsible for employment work, and staff of the Graduate Employment Guidance Centre of the Student Affairs Office are asked to attend the meeting on time.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:30, Tuesday, September 17, 2013 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 213, New Administration Building |
| Attendees | Party general branch secretaries (and deputy secretaries) of all colleges (residential colleges), counselors responsible for employment work, and staff of the Graduate Employment Guidance Centre of the Student Affairs Office |
| Requirement | Relevant staff are asked to attend on time |

It is also learned that the meeting was later rescheduled because of work arrangements, with the specific time and venue to be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
