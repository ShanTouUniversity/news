It is reported that, in accordance with a notice issued by the Guangdong Higher Education Teaching Examination Management Center on registration for the second English proficiency examination of 2013, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has launched registration for the exam. The examination will take the form of a computer-based online test and is scheduled for December 15, 2013.

## Key details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Computer-based online examination |
| Examination date | December 15, 2013 |
| Eligibility | Sports students and students of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Online registration | September 17 – October 7, 2013 |
| Fee | 16 yuan per person |

Eligible students should log in to the credit-based system website and complete their online registration via “My Course Selection → Qualification Examination Registration”. The Finance Office will authorize the Network Center to deduct the fee from registrants' campus card accounts in a single collection. Registrants must keep sufficient balance in advance, as an unsuccessful deduction will result in a failed registration.
