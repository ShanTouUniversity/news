It is reported that the International Office has issued a notice announcing that applications are now open for the 2014 Winter Program at Victoria University of Wellington, New Zealand. The program will be open to STU students from 2014.

According to the organizers, the program, titled the Intercultural Leadership Summer Course, is a short-term overseas winter study program organized by Victoria University of Wellington for undergraduates from its partner universities in China. Through lectures, seminars and social exchange activities, it aims to help students develop a global perspective and leadership skills, deepen their understanding of international and regional issues, strengthen cross-cultural communication skills, and improve their English in an all-English environment while learning about cultural exchanges between China and New Zealand. Students will receive a program certificate upon completion.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | Intercultural Leadership Summer Course |
| Dates | January 14 – February 8, 2014 |
| Places | 30 in total (including places reserved for Peking University), allocated on a first-come, first-served basis |
| Fee | NZ$3,600 |
| Eligibility | Enrolled second- and third-year undergraduate students |
| Applications | Open now; earlier applications are more likely to succeed under the first-come, first-served rule |

## Eligibility

- Enrolled second- or third-year undergraduate students;
- Strong academic performance;
- Good cross-cultural communication skills, the ability to live independently and adaptability;
- Excellent English listening, speaking, reading and writing, equivalent to IELTS 6 / TOEFL 550;
- A sound family financial situation.

## Application Materials

| Material | Details |
| --- | --- |
| STU International Exchange Program Application Form | To be completed as required |
| Chinese transcript | Stamped by the Academic Affairs Office |
| Proof of English proficiency | Equivalent to IELTS 6 / TOEFL 550 |
| English essay | Topic: "Why would you like to participate in the programme and what do you hope to gain from this experience?" |

## Fees

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Accommodation and meals | NZ$1,050 |
| Field trips and visits | NZ$700 |
| Program fee (including local medical care and insurance, airport–campus transfers, teaching materials, activities and more) | NZ$1,850 |
| Total | NZ$3,600 |

The program fee does not cover international travel, public transport in Wellington (about NZ$25 per week), entertainment (about NZ$40 per week), or personal snacks, phone bills and similar expenses (about NZ$30 per week).

## Applications and Enquiries

Victoria University of Wellington applies a first-come, first-served rule, so earlier applicants have a better chance of success. Students who withdraw before the program starts after being admitted and paying the fee will have part of the fee deducted. Details are subject to the attachments; enquiries can be sent by email to rghuang@stu.edu.cn.

## About Victoria University of Wellington

Founded in 1899, Victoria University of Wellington is one of New Zealand's oldest universities. Located in Wellington, the capital of New Zealand, it comprises six faculties — Architecture, Education, Humanities and Social Sciences, Commerce and Administration, Law, and Science — across four campuses. Its Law Faculty enjoys a strong reputation in the Asia-Pacific region, and its research ranks first among New Zealand universities, placing 265th in the QS World University Rankings 2013–2014. The university has 20,380 students, including 3,400 international or exchange students from more than 100 countries.
