It is reported that Public Welfare Courses have been a compulsory general education course for undergraduates admitted from 2012 onwards, forming part of the common core curriculum, and students who pass the assessment earn one credit. In the fall semester of the 2013–2014 academic year, nine such courses are being offered across the university. After the first round of selection in June this year, places remain in some courses, and selection is now open to all undergraduates.

Each course runs for 48 credit hours and consists of five parts: service learning, training, public welfare practice, sharing of reflections and assessment. Teaching is mainly delivered by trained university teachers, and classes are mostly scheduled on Wednesday afternoons or at weekends.

## Selection Schedule

| Stage | Time | Eligibility and details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary selection | 09:00 September 17 – 17:00 September 18 | Open only to students who have not yet taken a Public Welfare Course, for preliminary topic selection |
| Supplementary selection and withdrawal | 09:00 September 22 – 17:00 September 23 | Open to all undergraduates; students may withdraw from or add courses |
| After supplementary selection | — | No further course selection or course changes will be accepted |

To select a course, students log in to the credit-based system, go to “Guided Courses” and then “Select a Topic”, search the fall 2013–2014 semester, and click the available topics whose course codes begin with SER offered by the teaching units. The university reminds students to choose from the listed topics only and not to create their own, as self-created topics will be regarded as invalid.

Topics are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis until places are filled. Each student may select only one course, and students already enrolled in a fall Public Welfare Course should not take part in this selection. Students who fail to attend a course after selecting it will be assessed under the university's unified grading rules, and the course result will be recorded as zero.
