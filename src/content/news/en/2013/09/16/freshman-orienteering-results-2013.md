It is reported that the 2013 freshman orienteering event, organized by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Societies Federation, kicked off on September 9. The event drew active participation from freshmen across the university and has now concluded successfully, with the list of winners officially released.

According to the organizers, the list of winners is available in the attachment to the notice on the OA system and is not reproduced here. Winning teams are asked to collect their prizes from Room 731, Building 7, between 2:30 and 5:30 p.m. on September 18.

## Prize Collection

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 2:30–5:30 p.m., September 18, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 731, Building 7 |
| Who | Winning teams |
| Note | Prizes will not be kept after the deadline |

The organizers congratulated the winning students and thanked all freshmen across the university for their active participation.

> ⚠️ The list of winners is available in the original attachment on the OA system.
>
