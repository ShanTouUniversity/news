It is reported that the International Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 16, 2013, announcing that applications are now open for the 2014 study abroad program at Victoria University of Wellington, New Zealand.

Under a student exchange agreement between the two universities, STU has been able to recommend outstanding undergraduates to Victoria University of Wellington since 2013. The study abroad program is a non-degree program.

According to the notice, the academic year at Victoria University of Wellington is divided into two semesters: the first runs from February to June, and the second from July to November. The deadline for first-semester study applications is December 1, 2013, and for second-semester study applications May 1, 2014.

## Eligibility

- Enrolled second- and third-year undergraduates;
- GPA ranking in the top 30 percent of the applicant's year group in their school;
- Mature, self-disciplined and academically motivated;
- IELTS overall score of at least 6.0 with no band below 5.5, or TOEFL iBT of at least 80 or CBT of at least 213;
- Other requirements are set out in the program handbook.

## Costs

Students bear all costs themselves, including tuition, international travel, living expenses abroad and visa application fees. Under the agreement between the two universities, Victoria University of Wellington will reduce tuition by 10 percent for each STU applicant.

## About Victoria University of Wellington

Founded in 1899, Victoria University of Wellington is one of New Zealand's oldest universities. Located in Wellington, the capital of New Zealand, it comprises six faculties — Architecture, Education, Humanities and Social Sciences, Commerce and Administration, Law, and Science — across four campuses. Its research ranks first among New Zealand universities (2013 Performance-Based Research Fund), and it placed 265th in the QS World University Rankings 2013–2014. The university has 20,380 students, including 3,400 international or exchange students from more than 100 countries.
