It is reported that the Discipline Inspection Committee of the CPC Shantou University Committee has forwarded to all university units a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection on earnestly implementing the spirit of the Central Eight-Point Regulation and firmly curbing misconduct such as the use of public funds for gifts during the Mid-Autumn and National Day period (Document Yue Ji Fa [2013] No. 22). Units are required to implement the notice conscientiously and to firmly stop the use of public funds for holiday gifts, dining, travel and extravagance, so as to spend a clean and upright Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day.
