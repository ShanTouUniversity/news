It is reported that the “Science Salon” academic lecture series of the College of Science at Shantou University will be held at 16:10 on Tuesday, September 17, in the College of Science meeting room (Li-349). Researcher Zhang Zaoli of the Erich Schmid Institute of Materials Science, Austrian Academy of Sciences, will give a talk titled “Advanced electron microscopy for materials science.” Teachers and students are welcome to attend.

## Lecture Overview

The talk will introduce the latest advances in electron microscopy, including spherical and chromatic aberration correction techniques and how they promote materials research. Drawing on interface studies of metal–ceramic interfaces and transition metal nitride films, the speaker will present the following work:

- Quantitative measurement of atomic column positions that produced the first observation of Friedel oscillations in the interplanar spacing at a metal–ceramic (Cu-MgO) interface, as well as an analysis of periodic fluctuations in Cu atomic positions induced by dislocation cores;
- Direct measurement of Cu-O bond lengths in the coherent region of the interface, which was found to agree closely with theoretical calculations;
- Imaging of misfit dislocations along different crystallographic orientations, quantitatively showing the strain field around dislocation cores from a near-three-dimensional perspective;
- Visualization of nitrogen vacancies at the Cr/CrN interface through simultaneous imaging and spectroscopy, characterizing their atomic and electronic structure and establishing the relationship between nitrogen vacancy concentration, lattice constants and the ionicity of CrN.
