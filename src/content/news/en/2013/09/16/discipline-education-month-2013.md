It is reported that the CPC Shantou University Committee has issued the Notice on Launching the 2013 Discipline Education Month at Shantou University (STU Party Document No. 26 of 2013), setting out arrangements for the programme across the university. The activity will run from September 18 to October 18 under the theme "Strict Discipline, Sound Conduct, Clean Governance".

The arrangements are based on the Opinions on Launching the 2013 Discipline Education Month in Guangdong's Education System (Guangdong Education Working Committee Office Document No. 18 of 2013), the Notice on Conducting the Discipline Education Month in Guangdong's Universities in Conjunction with the Party's Mass Line Education and Practice Campaign (Guangdong Education Telegraph No. 7 of 2013), and related requirements of the Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection and the Provincial Secrecy Committee, adapted to the university's own circumstances.

Under the arrangements, the programme implements the general requirements of the Party's mass line education and practice campaign — "look in the mirror, straighten the collar, take a bath and treat the illness" — focuses on the "four forms of decadence", and provides Party members and officials with education on ideals and convictions, work style and discipline, political integrity and moral conduct, and self-discipline and integrity.

## Study Content

The study content includes important speeches by central, provincial and municipal leaders; the Eight-Point Regulation of the Political Bureau of the 18th CPC Central Committee on improving work style and maintaining close ties with the people, together with Guangdong's implementing measures; Party regulations such as the responsibility system for improving Party conduct and clean government and the code of conduct on clean governance for Party member leading officials; and rules on combating corruption and promoting integrity in the province's education system, along with positive and negative examples.

## Education Priorities

The programme focuses on education in ideals and convictions, Party discipline education with political discipline as the priority, and work-style education centred on tackling mediocrity, laziness, laxity and extravagance.

## Organisation

A steering group for the Discipline Education Month has been established, headed by Song Yaozhen, Secretary of the University Party Committee, with Xu Xiaohu, Deputy Secretary of the Party Committee and President, and Luo Nianchao, Deputy Secretary of the Party Committee and Secretary of the Discipline Inspection Commission, as deputy heads. Its members are the heads of the Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches at the main campus, and of the Discipline Inspection Commission Office, the Party and Administrative Office, the Organisation and United Front Work Department, the Publicity Department, the Student Affairs Department, the Graduate School and the Labour Union. The steering group office is located in the Discipline Inspection Commission Office; its telephone number is 86502346 and its email address is o_jj@stu.edu.cn.

## Requirements

Units are asked to strengthen organisation and leadership, enrich the formats and channels of the activities, and focus on their effectiveness, closely combining the programme with the Party's mass line education and practice campaign. Leading officials at section level and above are required to submit their study reflections to the Discipline Inspection Commission Office by October 23, and Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches are required to submit written summaries together with the 2013 Discipline Education Month Statistics Form by October 28.
