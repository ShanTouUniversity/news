It is reported that three staff members from the main campus of Shantou University have been honored under the Decision on Commending Outstanding Principals, Outstanding Teachers and Outstanding Education Workers in Shantou (Document Shan Shi Fa [2013] No. 11), issued by the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee and the Shantou Municipal People's Government. The Human Resources Office released the news on September 13.

Chen Zhimin and Yu Jin received the title of Shantou Outstanding Teacher, while Huang Yumei received the title of Shantou Outstanding Education Worker.

## Honored Staff

| Name | Honor |
| --- | --- |
| Chen Zhimin | Shantou Outstanding Teacher |
| Yu Jin | Shantou Outstanding Teacher |
| Huang Yumei | Shantou Outstanding Education Worker |
