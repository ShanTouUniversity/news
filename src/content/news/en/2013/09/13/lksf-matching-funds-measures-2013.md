It is reported that Shantou University published the *Management Measures for Provincial Matching Funds for Li Ka Shing Foundation Donations to Shantou University* on September 13. The measures were discussed and approved at the university's 10th leadership meeting on September 3, and all units of the university are required to comply with them.

The document was copied to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. The full text is available in the original attachment on the OA system.
