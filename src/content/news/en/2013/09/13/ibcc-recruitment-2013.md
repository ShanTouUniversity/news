It is reported that the International Business Case Club (IBCC) of Shantou University will hold recruitment interviews from 19:00 to 22:30 on September 15 in the seminar room on the fourth floor of the Business School (Wenxi Building, fourth floor).

## Interview information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 15, 19:00–22:30 |
| Venue | Seminar room, fourth floor of the Business School (Wenxi Building, fourth floor) |
| Format | One-minute English self-introduction, leaderless group case discussion, questions from interviewers |
| Registration | Send the completed application form to the club registration mailbox (see the original OA notice) before 22:00 on September 13; on-site registration is also accepted |

## About the club and its training programme

IBCC plans and hosts large domestic and international case analysis competitions, supported by case analysis training. In less than a year since its founding, the club has successfully hosted the South China division of the first National Case Elite Competition.

This year the club has introduced a mentor system and a points system for member training. Members form teams by department, prepare case analyses within two weeks under the guidance of assigned mentors, and take part in an in-house simulated case competition held once a month. The two teams with the highest total points will receive places in the major case competitions hosted by the club.
