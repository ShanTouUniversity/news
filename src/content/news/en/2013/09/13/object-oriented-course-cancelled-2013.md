It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on September 13, 2013, that the course Object-Oriented and Visual Programming, offered in the fall 2013 semester, has been cancelled because the instructor is hospitalized with an illness and can no longer take on the teaching duties. Students who have enrolled in the course are asked to inform one another.
