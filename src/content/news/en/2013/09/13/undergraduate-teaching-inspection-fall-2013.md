It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on September 13, 2013, to inspect undergraduate teaching for the fall 2013 semester.

September 16 is the first day of classes. The office asked academic units to notify faculty and students of timetables in advance, remind teachers to arrive on time, and check attendance and teaching order for the first week. Units should promptly contact departmental academic officers or the Academic Section (86502402) if abnormalities occur.

The Academic Affairs Office will also conduct unscheduled inspections starting on the first day of classes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
