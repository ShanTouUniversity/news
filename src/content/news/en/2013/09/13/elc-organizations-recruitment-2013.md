It is reported that five English activity organizations under the English Language Center (ELC) of Shantou University will hold recruitment interviews from the evening of September 13 to September 15, 2013. The interviews will take place at the English Activity Center (EAC) on the second floor of Building 9, and all five organizations accept on-site registration.

## Interview schedule

| Organization | Interview time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English Lounge (EL) | September 14, 09:30–11:30 and 14:30–17:00 | EAC (second floor, Building 9) |
| English Debating Society (EDS) | September 14, 09:00–12:00 and 14:30–17:30 | EAC |
| Creative Expression Club (CEC) | First round on September 14 and second round on September 15, with specific times to be announced by text message | EAC |
| ShantouBeat | September 13, 20:00–21:30; September 14, 09:00–12:00 and 14:30–17:30 | EAC |
| English Corner (EC) | First round on September 14, 08:00–22:00; second round on September 15, 08:00–22:00 | First round: Room 731, Building 7 (above the Coffee Book House); second round: EAC |

## Recruitment details

**English Lounge (EL)** is marking its tenth anniversary this year. It holds English activities on different themes from Monday to Thursday each week, and organizes the English Festival speech contest as well as themed parties such as Halloween. Its interviews are scheduled for 09:30–11:30 and 14:30–17:00 on September 14.

**English Debating Society (EDS)** is an academic English learning organization that focuses on spoken English, public speaking and critical thinking. Its interviews will run from 09:00 to 12:00 and from 14:30 to 17:30 on September 14.

**Creative Expression Club (CEC)** is a student-centered English organization where students learn English through singing, dancing, drama performances and speeches. Its recruitment is open to all 2013 freshmen regardless of major. Interviews will be held in two rounds, the first on September 14 and the second on September 15, with the specific times to be announced by text message.

**ShantouBeat** focuses mainly on writing in English and has added a new media department this year, producing English videos. Its interviews are scheduled for 20:00–21:30 on September 13 and for 09:00–12:00 and 14:30–17:30 on September 14.

**English Corner (EC)** has seven departments: Ice Break, Mr. Question, I Discover, EC Channel, Chit Chat Column, Special and Marketing. Each department plans to recruit five to six members, and the organization is open to students across the university regardless of major or year. The first round of interviews will take place from 08:00 to 22:00 on September 14 in Room 731, Building 7 (above the Coffee Book House), and the second round from 08:00 to 22:00 on September 15 at the EAC.
