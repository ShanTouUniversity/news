It is reported that, under the university's interim measures for government procurement, Shantou University has opened submissions for its fourth-quarter 2013 equipment procurement plans. Units must submit both hard-copy and electronic materials to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by **October 15, 2013**. Late submissions will be deferred to the first quarter of 2014.

## Filing requirements

For plans totaling RMB 20,000 or less, departments should place orders directly through the University Equipment Bidding Website (http://bid.stu.edu.cn). Lenovo, HP, Dell and Apple computers will continue to be supplied under the university's agreement-based arrangement.

For plans totaling more than RMB 20,000, units must complete the Plan Form and the Plan Document and, in line with the university's rules on financial approval authority, obtain approval from the responsible university leader, the President or the university leadership meeting, as applicable, before submission.

Plans that include equipment priced at more than RMB 100,000 per unit also require a Feasibility Report for the Purchase of Large-Scale Equipment. Computer purchases require a Computer Purchase Application Registration Form, while air-conditioner purchases must state the installation location and the condition of the existing air conditioner there in the "Remarks" column of the plan form.

All fields of the plan form must be completed. Equipment codes should follow the Guangdong Province 2013 Government Centralized Procurement Catalogue and Threshold Standards, and only the equipment name should be entered as the item name, with brand and model information placed under "Remarks". Budgets must take into account taxes, transportation, warranty and after-sales service costs.
