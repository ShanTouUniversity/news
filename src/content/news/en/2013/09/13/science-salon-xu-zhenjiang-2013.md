It is reported that the Science Salon lecture series of the College of Science at Shantou University will host a special talk on organic chemistry. The talk will be held from 10:15 to 11:30 a.m. on Tuesday, September 17, in Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC). Xu Zhenjiang, associate researcher at the Shanghai-Hong Kong Joint Laboratory of Chemical Synthesis, Shanghai Institute of Organic Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences, will speak on "Atom-Group Transfer Reactions Catalyzed by Transition-Metal Complexes." Faculty and students are welcome to attend.

### Abstract

The talk will review progress in the synthesis of transition-metal complexes based on ruthenium, iron and other metals and in the atom-group transfer reactions they catalyze, focusing on the relationship between the structure of metal complexes and their catalytic activity and reaction selectivity. The research has produced a series of new transition-metal complexes applied to carbene transfer reactions, selective olefin oxidation, asymmetric dipolar cycloaddition and asymmetric fluorination. The talk will note that these efficient and highly selective green catalytic synthetic methods hold important prospects for the synthesis of fine chemicals and pharmaceutical molecules.
