It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University will organize a team-building experience program for all student leaders of the Graduate Student Union and the Master's and Doctoral Volunteer Team on September 14 and 15. The activity aims to improve teamwork in the two organizations, cultivate team awareness and coordination skills, and further strengthen their service functions.

The Graduate School asks all members to be well prepared and to arrive at Room E207 for the training at the appointed time. A detailed schedule is available in the attachment to the OA notice.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 14, 9:00–17:00; September 15, 8:30–17:00 |
| Venue | Room E207 |
| Participants | All student leaders of the Graduate Student Union and the Master's and Doctoral Volunteer Team |
| Reporting requirement | Members should be prepared and arrive at Room E207 by 8:45 a.m. on September 14 for the training |
| Organizer | Graduate School, Shantou University |
