It is reported that the Mind and Machine conference series is marking its 10th anniversary this year. The conference will be held on September 21-22 at Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC) at Shantou University, with discussions in morning and afternoon sessions on both days: 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 noon and 2:00 to 6:00 p.m.

The conference is jointly hosted by the Mind and Machine Seminar, the Christian Studies Centre of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University, and the Department of Computer Science and Technology of the College of Engineering at Shantou University.

Founded in 2004, the conference series was initiated by scholars from Peking University, Renmin University of China, Fudan University, Zhejiang University, Beijing Normal University, Sun Yat-sen University, East China Normal University and Shantou University. It aims to explore frontier issues in artificial intelligence and the interplay between artificial intelligence and philosophy, offering scholars in artificial intelligence, philosophy of science and technology, and psychology a platform for free exploration, equal dialogue and the exchange of ideas.

This year's conference will feature frontier discussions on intersecting themes such as mind, machine, life, logic and language. The university had earlier issued a call for papers for the conference.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
