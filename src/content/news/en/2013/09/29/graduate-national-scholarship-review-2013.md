It is reported that Shantou University has started its 2013 review of the Graduate National Scholarship in line with the Notice on Printing and Distributing the Interim Measures for the Administration of the Graduate National Scholarship in Regular Higher Education Institutions of Guangdong Province (Yue Cai Jiao [2013] No. 17).

Funded by the central government, the scholarship is open to non-employed full-time master and doctoral students who hold STU student status, are in their second year of study or above and remain within the standard program duration. Doctoral students receive 30,000 yuan a year and master students 20,000 yuan a year. Applicants are expected to love the socialist motherland and support the leadership of the Communist Party of China, abide by the Constitution, laws and university rules, demonstrate honesty and sound moral character, and show outstanding academic performance, notable research ability and strong development potential.

## Places Allocated

For 2013, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and the Department of Finance allocated STU a total of 56 places. Four are for doctoral students, two each for the main campus and the Medical College, while 52 are for master students: 28 on the main campus and 24 in the Medical College's three-year and seven-year programs.

## Review Structure

Selection follows a three-step process: individual application by students, preliminary review and recommendation by the review committee of each postgraduate training unit, and approval by the university's leading group for the Graduate National Scholarship review. The university-level group, composed of the university leader in charge of postgraduate work, heads of the Graduate School and representatives of postgraduate supervisors, is responsible for leading, coordinating and supervising the review. Each college (department or institute) has set up its own review committee, chaired by the dean (director) and including the leader in charge of postgraduate work, the postgraduate secretary, supervisor representatives and student representatives; it draws up the unit's implementing rules and organizes applications and the preliminary review. Units with fewer than one recommended place are reviewed together with other units and must submit the materials of one recommended candidate to the Graduate School by October 8 for a joint review meeting convened by the Graduate School.

## How to Apply

Students intending to apply must complete their online application at STU's Student Affairs System (https://sao.stu.edu.cn/default.aspx) before 5:00 pm on October 8 (Thursday). After opening the website, they should click "My Applications", then "Graduate National Scholarship Application", fill in all the information, change the current status at the lower left corner of the form to "Student Application" and click "Confirm".

Applicants must also print the Graduate National Scholarship Application and Approval Form on both sides, sign it in black ink without any alteration, and submit it, together with certificates of awards, transcripts, published papers, patent certificates and other supporting materials that demonstrate their abilities and qualities (originals and copies), signed by their supervisor (recommender), to the review committee of their college by October 8, 2013. The university noted that all applications must be completed through the system; those who fail to apply online within the deadline will not be accepted by their college, and the system will close at 5:00 pm on October 8.

From October 9 to 17, the review committees of the colleges will conduct preliminary reviews of the application materials and publicize the results, giving full weight to the recommendations of academic teams and postgraduate supervisors and, where necessary, arranging on-site defenses by applicants. After deciding on the recipients, each college must publicize its list within the unit for at least five working days. Students with objections should appeal to their college review committee during the publicity period and will receive a timely reply. Colleges must report their review results before October 18; after the publicity period closes without objections, they should compile the summary forms for doctoral and master recipients and for recipients' research work and awards, and submit them, together with the application and approval forms and their own review report (including the list of committee members, the implementing rules and review details), in paper and electronic form, to Huang Meiping at the Student Affairs Office of the Graduate School, email mphuang@stu.edu.cn.

From October 21 to 29, the Graduate School will submit the lists recommended by the colleges to the university's leading group for approval, and the results will be publicized across the university for at least five working days. Students who still disagree with their college review committee's reply may ask the university leading group to rule during the university-wide publicity period. After the review is completed, the Graduate School will report the work and the results to the higher-level competent authority before October 30.
