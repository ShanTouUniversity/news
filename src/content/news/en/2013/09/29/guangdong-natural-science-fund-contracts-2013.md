It is reported that the list of projects funded by the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation in 2013 has been announced, and STU's Research Office has issued arrangements for funded project leaders (see Attachment 1 of the OA notice) to sign project contracts with the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Management Committee.

Before signing, project leaders should carefully read the contract terms as well as the Measures for the Administration of Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Projects and the Measures for the Administration of Funding for Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Projects, both of which are available on the foundation website (http://gdsf.gdstc.gov.cn/). The main research content, objectives and key researchers set out in the contract must be consistent with the application, and the contract start date is uniformly set as October 1, 2013.

On the schedule, project leaders must fill in and submit their contracts on the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation website between September 29 and October 16 for review by the Research Office, while the foundation's office will review the contracts online from October 21 to 27. Once a contract shows the status "accepted by the foundation committee", the project leader should download it, print three copies double-sided on A4 paper and deliver them to the Research Office before October 29.

The Research Office also noted that projects involving partner institutions and the allocation of funds must include a written cooperation agreement in seven copies (see Attachment 2 of the OA notice), under which STU's share of the government funding must be no less than 60 percent. Expert review comments on projects that were not funded will be fed back to applicants online in December.

For further information, project leaders may contact Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at the Research Office, telephone 86502813.
