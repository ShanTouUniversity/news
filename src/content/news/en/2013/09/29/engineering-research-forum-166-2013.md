It is reported that the 166th Engineering Research Forum of Shantou University's College of Engineering will be held at **9:00 a.m. on Monday, September 30** in the Academic Exchange Room of the College of Engineering. The topic is "Computational Perceptual Control of Socially Interactive Robots Based on PCT," and Dr. Zhang Jiaming will be the speaker.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | The 166th session |
| Topic | Computational Perceptual Control of Socially Interactive Robots Based on PCT |
| Speaker | Dr. Zhang Jiaming |
| Time | 9:00 a.m., Monday, September 30, 2013 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering (Gongnan 314) |
| Participants | Postgraduates, doctoral students and related faculty of the College of Engineering; interested teachers and students are welcome |

Postgraduates, doctoral students and related faculty of the College of Engineering are invited to attend, and other interested teachers and students are also welcome.
