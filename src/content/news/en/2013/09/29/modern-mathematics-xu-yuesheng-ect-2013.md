It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Modern Mathematics lecture on September 30, 2013, featuring Prof. Xu Yuesheng of Sun Yat-sen University.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Prof. Xu Yuesheng, Sun Yat-sen University |
| Title | Fixed-Point Algorithms for Emission Computed Tomography Reconstruction |
| Time | 17:00, September 30, 2013 |
| Venue | Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412) |
| Organizer | School of Science |

The talk will present a preconditioned alternating projection algorithm (PAPA) for the maximum a posteriori ECT reconstruction problem, its formulation as a constrained convex optimization with total variation regularization, fixed-point characterization and numerical comparisons with EM-TV methods. All faculty and students are welcome.
