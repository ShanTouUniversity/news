Following bank approval, **102 students** at Shantou University were included in the third batch of national student loans for the 2012-2013 academic year. The loans will offset tuition and accommodation fees, and any remaining balance will be paid into the recipient's personal Bank of China account.

## Disbursement and Procedures

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Batch | Third batch of 2012-2013 national student loans |
| Recipients | 102 students |
| Use of funds | Offset tuition and accommodation fees; any balance paid into the recipient's personal Bank of China account |
| Contract collection deadline | Before October 11, 2013 |
| Collection location | Student Financial Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center) |
| Required document | Campus card |
| Inquiry channel | "My Information - My Personal Information - Tuition and Miscellaneous Fee Payment" in the Student Affairs System |
| Enquiry telephone | 86504749 |

Students who have received loans may log in to the Student Affairs System and check their individual payment status under "My Information - My Personal Information - Tuition and Miscellaneous Fee Payment," and should pay any outstanding balance at the Finance Office in good time. Students listed in the attachment must collect their national student loan contracts from the Student Financial Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center) with their campus cards before October 11. For further questions, please call the Student Financial Aid Management Center at `86504749`.

> ⚠️ The disbursement details for loan recipients are available in the attachment to the OA notice and are not reproduced here.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
