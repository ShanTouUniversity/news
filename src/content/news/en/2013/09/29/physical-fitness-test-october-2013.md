It is reported that, in line with documents from the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University must complete this semester's student physical fitness testing within October. The Physical Education Department has scheduled the tests for on-campus undergraduates on October 13, 19, 20 and 26 at the track and field ground.

## Test Items Increased to Nine

The Ministry of Education has made considerable adjustments to this year's physical fitness testing. The national unified test list for university students has grown from six items to nine (11 sub-items in total): the grip strength test has been removed, and eyesight, the 50-metre sprint, the sit-and-reach, and pull-ups (men) / one-minute sit-ups (women) have been added.

## Who Takes the Test

The tests cover all on-campus undergraduates enrolled in 2011, 2012 and 2013. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan students, international students, students with incomplete ethnicity information and students with incomplete ID numbers will not take part for the time being, as their data cannot be reported. Those students can check the college lists in the *Standards* test schedule; if their names do not appear there, they do not need to be tested.

## Test Items and Order

1. Sit-and-reach;
2. 50-metre sprint;
3. Standing long jump;
4. Pull-ups (men) / one-minute sit-ups (women);
5. 1,000-metre run (men) / 800-metre run (women).

Data for the other items will be obtained by the Physical Education Department from the university hospital.

Each class's specific test time is set out in the attached *Standards* test schedule. The venue is the track and field ground.

## Test Organization

Each college is responsible for organizing its own students. Classes must arrive at the test venue strictly according to the schedule, neither early nor late. Classes are advised to proceed in student ID order, which makes testing more efficient. After finishing one item, each student should move on to the next and work through the items in order.

## Key Reminders

- All five scores are required. Missing any one item invalidates all the others and counts as an incomplete test. Students should make sure their results are recorded in the teacher's register.
- Attend strictly at the assigned time; test times may not be changed. Those who fail to attend without good reason will face further action by the university.
- Wear sportswear and sports shoes, and warm up beforehand to avoid injury.
- Students with physical defects, chronic illnesses, heart conditions, congenital developmental disorders or other conditions unsuitable for the required tests should fill in the *Application for Exemption from the National Student Physical Health Standard* form, attach supporting medical documentation, bind the pages together and place them in the exemption application drop box at the gymnasium office before October 20.

## Data Reporting Brought Forward

This year's testing timetable is tight: the data reporting deadline has been moved forward from the end of December in previous years to before October 31. At the same time, the Ministry of Education has stepped up data verification and will conduct spot checks on the data reported by universities nationwide in November.
