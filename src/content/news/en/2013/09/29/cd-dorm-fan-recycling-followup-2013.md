It is reported that the Student Community Service Center of Shantou University (STU) had previously carried out door-to-door collection of dismantled fans in the CD dormitories. On September 27, the center arranged for staff to visit the dormitories, but some fans could not be collected because no one was present in certain rooms.

## Second Visit on the Afternoon of September 29

To complete the collection of the remaining fans, the Student Community Service Center will send staff to the dormitories again at 3 p.m. on September 29.

The center reminds students to place their dismantled fans outside their dormitory doors in advance so that staff can collect them smoothly. For rooms where no one is present, staff will lead moving workers into the dormitories to collect the fans, and students are asked to cooperate.
