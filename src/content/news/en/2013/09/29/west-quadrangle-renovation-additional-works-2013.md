It is reported that the West Campus Quadrangle renovation project of Shantou University (STU) has been under construction since July 29, 2013, and several circumstances requiring additional investment emerged during the works. The Infrastructure Office of the Logistics Management Office completed a preliminary estimate of the expected additional items and publicized the cost of the additional works on September 29.

## Main Reasons for the Additional Works

According to the Infrastructure Office, the estimated extra cost stems mainly from two aspects. First, the original ceiling materials were found to be rotting during construction and needed to be replaced to meet safety requirements. Second, Typhoon Usagi caused damage to the site, and the related repair costs must also be included.

## Estimated Amount and Compliance

The Infrastructure Office's preliminary estimate puts the additional items at about RMB 200,000. The cost of the additional works accounts for less than 10 percent of the contract amount, which is within the limit set by the university.

It is understood that the publicity was carried out in accordance with STU's Measures for the Implementation of Bidding and Tendering Management for Capital Construction Projects.

During the publicity period, anyone with objections to the cost of the additional works may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 5:30 p.m. on October 8.
