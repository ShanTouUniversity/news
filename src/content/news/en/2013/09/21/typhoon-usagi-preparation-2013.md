It is reported that, according to the meteorological authorities, Super Typhoon Usagi, the 19th typhoon of 2013, is carrying winds of force 16 at its centre and is expected to make landfall on the coast of central-eastern Guangdong on the night of September 21, severely affecting Shantou from that night onward.

The "Three Defences" Command of Shantou University has therefore issued a notice asking faculty, staff and students to inform one another and to make preparations against the typhoon and heavy rain in advance.

## Precautionary Requirements

- Close and reinforce the doors and windows of offices, laboratories, classrooms, dormitories, corridors and nearby public venues as soon as possible, and switch off the power.
- Faculty, staff and students should inform one another and jointly prepare against the typhoon and rainstorms.
- In the event of rainstorms, flash floods or landslides, priority must be given to ensuring the personal safety of faculty, staff and students.
- Specific precautionary measures follow the STU Typhoon Response Plan.

> ⚠️ The attachment of the STU Typhoon Response Plan is available on the OA system.
