It is reported that the second half of the 2013 National Computer Rank Examination (NCRE) will take place at Shantou University on Sept 21 (Saturday). The exam will be held in batches at the E102 computer lab starting at 9:00, and candidates should arrive at the time printed on their admission ticket. Seat numbers will be assigned by drawing lots at random before the exam.

Different from previous years, all levels of the National Computer Rank Examination will be fully computer-based starting with this session.

## Levels and Exam Duration

| Level | Subjects | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Computer Basics and WPS Office Applications, MS Office Applications, Photoshop Applications, among others | 90 minutes |
| Level 2 | C, VB, VFP, Java, Access, C++, MySQL, Web Programming, Advanced MS Office Applications, among others | 120 minutes |
| Level 3 | Network Technology, Database Technology, Software Testing Technology, Information Security Technology, Embedded System Development Technology | 120 minutes |
| Level 4 | Network Engineer, Database Engineer, Software Test Engineer, Information Security Engineer, Embedded System Development Engineer | 90 minutes |

## Exam Reminders

The university reminds candidates of the following:

- Candidates must bring their ID card and admission ticket and place them at the upper-left corner of their seat for inspection; those without complete documents will not be admitted;
- Candidates who arrive late will not be allowed to enter the exam room;
- Candidates taking the computer-based test must gather at Room E204 30 minutes before the exam to draw their seat numbers;
- Candidates should bring their own 2B pencils, erasers and blue-black pens; books, communication devices and similar items must not be brought into the exam room.

## Collecting Admission Tickets

Undergraduate students of the university can collect their admission tickets from the academic affairs officer of their department, while other candidates should bring their ID card to the Student Services counter of the Academic Affairs Office. For inquiries, please call the Academic Affairs Office at 86502411.
