It is reported that the Office of the Leading Group for the Mass Line Education and Practice Campaign issued a notice on September 10, 2013, asking all general Party branches and directly affiliated branches to summarize the first phase.

According to the superior’s deployment and the university’s campaign arrangements, the “study, education and opinion soliciting” phase is concluding. To advance simultaneous learning, inspection and reform, units are asked to conscientiously summarize the phase and submit the report to the Office of the Leading Group by September 12, 2013.
