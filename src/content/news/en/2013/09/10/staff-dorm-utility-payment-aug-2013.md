It is reported that the Resources Management Office of Shantou University has published the utility bills for faculty and staff housing for August 2013, and staff can now log in to the utility management system to check them. Anyone with questions or objections may visit the Water and Electricity Center from September 10 to 13; charges that remain undisputed at the end of that period will be deducted from September salaries by the Finance Office.

## Billing and meter-reading details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | August 2013 |
| Current meter readings | September 1 and 2, 2013 |
| Previous meter reading | August 1, 2013 |

## How to check and verify charges

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| System | Utility management system |
| Web address | http://power.stu.edu.cn |
| Login account | Campus network account |
| Verification period | September 10 to 13 |
| Verification location | Water and Electricity Center |
| Telephone | 2489 |

After logging in, staff can view the August utility charges for their own housing. Those who have questions about the charges or find that the figures do not match actual use may visit the Water and Electricity Center during the verification period to check the records.

## Payment arrangements

| Case | How it is handled |
| --- | --- |
| No objection by the end of the period | Deducted from September salaries by the Finance Office |

The Resources Management Office has reminded affected staff to check their charges promptly so as not to miss the verification period.
