It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold the flag-raising ceremony for the 2013 freshman class at 6:30 am on Monday, Sept 16, at the flag platform in front of the university motto monument. All 2013 freshmen, including graduate students, are to attend, and each unit will organize students to arrive at 6:10 am to line up.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 6:30 am, Monday, Sept 16 |
| Venue | Flag platform in front of the motto monument |
| Participants | All 2013 freshmen (including graduate students) |
| Assembly | Units organize students to arrive at 6:10 am to line up |

## Requirements

- Students should dress neatly and gather at the designated place on time;
- Each school (residential college) should bring its own school flag;
- Freshmen should bring their signed oath sheets and hand them in at the on-site collection point in an orderly manner after the ceremony;
- Each unit should count attendance and report it to the university Youth League Committee.

## Ceremony Programme

The ceremony will begin with the host announcing it open, followed by the playing of the national anthem and the raising of the national flag, an oath led by the oath leader and a speech by university leadership, after which the ceremony will conclude.

> ⚠️ The oath sheet and related attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office)
