It is reported that Shantou University will hold the second leadership-student dinner in September to encourage students to share ideas and take part in university development, and to build a channel of communication between university leaders and students. The second session is dedicated to freshmen and is scheduled for **6:00 p.m. on September 14** in **Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant**, with **32 students** from the 2013 cohort to be invited.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2nd Leadership-Student Dinner |
| Time | 6:00 p.m., September 14, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Invited guests | University leaders, leaders of relevant administrative offices, and four to five faculty representatives |
| Student participants | 32 freshmen of the 2013 cohort |
| Registration deadline | September 12, 2013 |
| How to register | Sign up through the university reservation system |
| List of invitees | Posted on the university's internal office-information network |
| Meal cost | Paid centrally by the university |

## Format

A university leader will host the dinner. The invited guests and students will exchange views freely on university development and issues of interest to students. The Student Affairs Office hopes that participating students will act as a bridge, objectively gathering issues of concern among their peers and bringing them to the discussion.

## Registration

Interested freshmen may sign up through the [reservation system](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm) by **September 12** and may also submit questions collected in advance. The system will close automatically once the quota is full, and the list of invited students will be posted on the university's internal office-information network.

Comments and suggestions about the event may be sent to the Student Affairs Office at `lslin@stu.edu.cn`.
