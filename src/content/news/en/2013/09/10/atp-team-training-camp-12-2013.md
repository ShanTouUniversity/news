It is reported that Zhicheng College issued a notice on September 10, 2013, to hold the 12th ATP (Action-Teamwork-Passion) Team Training Camp from the evening of September 13 to September 15.

The camp is designed for all 2013 freshmen of Zhicheng College as a compulsory residential college course. It aims to help freshmen integrate into university life, plan their studies, and improve overall competencies including responsibility, confidence, independence, communication, collaboration and leadership. The program is included in the university’s general education curriculum; students who complete the required sessions and pass the assessment will receive 1 credit.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Evening of September 13 – September 15, 2013 |
| Participants | All 2013 freshmen of Zhicheng College and related volunteers |
| Credit | 1 credit upon completion and passing assessment |
| Issued by | Zhicheng College |

All 2013 freshmen are asked to attend the opening ceremony and related sessions on time, and volunteers are asked to prepare accordingly.
