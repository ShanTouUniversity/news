It is reported that, in line with the university's common core course reform, the Social Sciences Department of Shantou University has adjusted selected ideological-political theory courses. The reforms took effect from the **fall 2013 semester** and concern course selection for the 2013 freshmen. The department reminded freshmen to check course codes and credit values carefully when registering.

## Course Comparison

| Course | Course Code | Credits | Applies To |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law (morality part) | SOC6130B | 2 | Compulsory for all 2013 freshmen |
| Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law (law part) | SOC6131B | 2 | Exempted for law majors; compulsory for freshmen of other majors |
| Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law (old scheme) | SOC6120A | 4 | Continuing students follow the old scheme |
| Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics | SOC6150B | 4 | Compulsory for 2013 freshmen |
| Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics (old scheme) | SOC6150A | 6 | Continuing students follow the old scheme |

## Details of the Changes

*Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law* is split into two courses. The morality part carries the code **SOC6130B** and 2 credits, and is compulsory for all 2013 freshmen. The law part carries the code **SOC6131B** and 2 credits; it is exempted for law majors and compulsory for freshmen in other majors. Continuing students remain on the old scheme and take **SOC6120A**, worth 4 credits.

For *Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics*, 2013 freshmen must take the 4-credit course **SOC6150B**, while continuing students follow the old scheme and take the 6-credit course **SOC6150A**.

## Policy for Overseas Students

The existing policy continues to apply to ideological-political theory courses for overseas students, who may be exempted from five courses offered by the Social Sciences Department: Basic Principles of Marxism; Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law; Outline of Modern Chinese History; Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics; and Current Affairs and Policy Education.
