It is reported that the Shantou University Football Club has opened recruitment for the fall semester of the 2013–2014 academic year, reserving 10 places for the 2013 intake of freshmen. The club is one of the Physical Education Department's pilot projects for advanced undergraduate teaching reform, aiming to promote football culture on campus and to run football training and exchange activities.

According to the Physical Education Department, students who join the club can meet their physical education course credit requirements while taking part in training and matches, serving as referees in competitions and helping organize events, gaining football skills and related experience through practice.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target | 2013 freshmen |
| Places | 10 |
| Class time | Thursday, periods 8 and 9 |
| Registration deadline | 5:00 pm, Sept 15 |
| How to apply | Complete the application form and email it to xwliu@stu.edu.cn with “足球俱乐部报名” (Football Club registration) as the subject |
| Selection | A football skills test will be held to select candidates if applications exceed the quota |

The notice notes that club sessions are scheduled for periods 8 and 9 on Thursdays, a time slot in which no other courses can be taken, so the arrangement does not conflict with the course selection system. Students interested in applying should complete the application form and send it to the designated email address before the deadline.

> ⚠️ The application form attachment is available in the original notice on the OA system.
