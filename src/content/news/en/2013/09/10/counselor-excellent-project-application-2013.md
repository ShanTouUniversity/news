It is reported that in line with the requirements of the Ministry of Education's Department of Ideological and Political Work (Document No. 58 of 2013), Shantou University has launched the application process for university counselor excellence projects. The program focuses on cultivating and funding a number of education and teaching projects by counselors, promoting standardized, high-quality and scientific counselor work, and advancing the professionalization of the counselor team.

## Application Categories

Applications fall into two categories:

| Category | Main Content |
| --- | --- |
| Counselor courses | Party and Youth League classes, as well as required or elective courses such as Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics, current affairs and policy education, mental health education, and employment and entrepreneurship guidance |
| Daily ideological and political education | Party and Youth League organization and class building, themed education, campus culture development, practice-based education, online ideological and political education, mental health education, employment and entrepreneurship education, academic guidance and career planning, emergency response, financial aid education, themed class meetings, and more |

Projects must center on the fundamental task of fostering virtue through education, and must have been carried out in practice with initial results achieved.

## Eligibility and Application Materials

Applicants include full-time counselors, student affairs leaders of colleges and departments, Youth League branch secretaries and deputy Party branch secretaries. Required materials include: an application form (honors listed must be at or above the university level); a written project description of up to 3,000 words covering the theme and approach, implementation methods and process, main results and experience, and improvement plans; and supporting materials such as videos and PowerPoint presentations burned onto a CD.

Electronic versions should be sent to lslin@stu.edu.cn by October 11, and printed copies submitted to the Education Management Section, Room 106, Administration Center.

The university will conduct a preliminary review, select one project and submit it to the provincial education department for further selection. The Ministry of Education's Department of Ideological and Political Work will provide funding for the selected projects.
