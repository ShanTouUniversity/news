It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University has completed the English placement assessment for the 2013 undergraduate cohort. The Academic Affairs Office and the English Language Center have released the results, which will guide students in selecting English courses and purchasing the corresponding textbooks.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment group | 2013 undergraduate cohort |
| Organizing unit | English Language Center (ELC) |
| Use of results | Students should select courses and purchase textbooks according to their placement results |
| Results | The full list and placement results are provided in the OA attachment |

Students are reminded to complete course selection and textbook preparation according to their individual placement results. As the attachment contains personal student information, this article does not reproduce student names, student numbers or the detailed placement list.

> ⚠️ The full list and English placement results are available in the original attachment on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office, English Language Center)
