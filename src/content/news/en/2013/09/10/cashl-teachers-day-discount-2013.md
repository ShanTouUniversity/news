It is reported that the Library issued a notice on September 10, 2013, to launch the CASHL (China Academic Social Sciences and Humanities Library) Teachers’ Day national discount service month.

The campaign runs from September 10 to October 10, 2013, and is open to faculty and all students. Registered CASHL users can enjoy discounted document delivery during the period.

## Discounts

| Service | Subsidy |
| --- | --- |
| CASHL journal articles | 100% |
| CASHL special collections | 50% |
| CASHL book chapters (partial) | 50% |

Interlibrary loan on behalf of users and book borrowing are not covered.

## How to Access

**New users**: Register on the CASHL website (http://www.cashl.edu.cn) under “Personalized Services,” then send registration username, name, school, grade and campus card number to yxu@stu.edu.cn for account confirmation. Log in with the confirmed account to search and request documents.

**Existing users**: Log in with the original username and password; if the account is expired or credentials are forgotten, email the contact to reactivate.

**Public trial account**: A free shared account `stu_free` / password `123456` is provided until October 10, 2013 (a valid personal email must be entered on the request page to receive documents).

Database links and guides are available in the original OA notice. For details, contact the Library.
