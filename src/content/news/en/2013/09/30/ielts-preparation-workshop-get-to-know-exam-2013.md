It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University will hold an IELTS preparation workshop on Wednesday, October 9, from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m. at the Centre for Independent Language Learning (CILL), under the theme "Getting to know the exam."

The session covers three areas: the format of the IELTS test; a detailed breakdown of the four skills — listening, reading, writing and speaking; and the importance of following instructions. Seats are limited, and those interested must register at https://ieltswsseries1.eventbrite.com. The organizer asked students who are unsure whether they can attend not to sign up, and said those who register must attend.
