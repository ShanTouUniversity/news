It is reported that, in accordance with a notice from the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Management Committee and the fund's project management rules, the Research Office of Shantou University launched the annual execution review of the university's 2013 Guangdong Natural Science Fund projects on September 30. The review covers three categories, and a total of 49 projects must submit annual execution reports.

## Projects Covered

Annual execution reports are required from three groups of projects: 13 ongoing projects funded this year; 21 projects to be completed this year; and 15 follow-up projects, namely projects completed in 2011 and 2012, with doctoral startup projects excluded from the follow-up group.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Projects covered | 13 ongoing projects, 21 projects to be completed this year, and 15 follow-up projects (projects completed in 2011 and 2012, excluding doctoral startup projects) |
| Online submission period | October 10 to November 15, 2013 |
| Paper submission deadline | One copy to the Research Office before November 18, 2013 |
| Contacts and telephone | Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng, 86502813 |
