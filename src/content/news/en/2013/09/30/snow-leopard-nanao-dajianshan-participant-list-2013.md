It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University and the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association of Shantou University issued a notice on September 30 announcing the participant list for the beginner outdoor expedition at Dajianshan on Nanao Island. According to the notice, the event has received an enthusiastic response from faculty members and students since it was announced, with 275 application forms received in total.

Following the principles of random selection and fairness, the organizers confirmed 27 faculty members and students as participants. After taking various factors into account and on the premise of ensuring safety, the number of places was increased from the originally planned 20 to 27. According to the earlier event notice, the expedition is scheduled for October 2 and 3 at Dajianshan on Nanao Island.

## Briefing Session

The briefing session for the Nanao expedition will be held from 9:00 to 10:00 on October 1 in Room 354 of the Zhicheng Building. Participants are asked to attend on time.

## What Comes Next

For faculty members and students who were not selected, the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association said it will seek to hold more outdoor activities so that more members of the university community can benefit, and invited everyone to stay tuned.
