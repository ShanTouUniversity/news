It is reported that, with approval from the relevant authorities, the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University will carry out repair work on the steel roof sheets of the Grand Hall and has commissioned Guangdong Runmin Construction and Installation Engineering Co., Ltd. to undertake the construction. The project is scheduled to begin on October 8, 2013 and to be completed by December 16, 2013.

## Key Project Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Grand Hall steel roof sheet replacement and repair |
| Scope of work | Complete replacement of the Grand Hall's steel roof sheets |
| Contractor | Guangdong Runmin Construction and Installation Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Winning bid price | RMB 426,300 |
| Schedule | Begins October 8, 2013; to be completed by December 16, 2013 |
| Safety reminder | Pedestrians should take care; do not enter the work site without permission from the university's functional departments and the contractor, and avoid lingering near the site |

## Construction Impacts and Safety Reminder

The university said that the work will produce some noise, vibration and dust, and asked for understanding for the inconvenience caused. For safety, faculty members and passers-by are reminded to take care, not to enter the construction site without permission from the university's functional departments and the contractor, and to avoid staying near the site.

The construction notice was issued by the Infrastructure Office on September 30, 2013.
