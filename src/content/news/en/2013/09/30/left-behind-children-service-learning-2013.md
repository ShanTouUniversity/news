It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University has released the class schedule for its fall 2013 service-learning course **Caring for Left-Behind Children**. Taught by Zeng Fanqi, the course will meet on Wednesdays in the 6th, 7th and 8th periods at Room E208.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Caring for Left-Behind Children (class taught by Zeng Fanqi) |
| Instructor | Zeng Fanqi |
| Class dates | October 9, 16, 23 and 30, 2013 (all Wednesdays) |
| Periods | 6th, 7th and 8th periods |
| Venue | E208 |
| Issued by | Business School Academic Affairs Office |

Under the arrangement, the course opens on Wednesday of the fourth teaching week (October 9), continues in the same time slot every Wednesday, and ends on Wednesday of the seventh week (October 30), with all four sessions held at E208. The Business School Academic Affairs Office asks students enrolled in the course to pass the message on and attend on time.
