It is reported that, following the review and recommendation of the Shantou University Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, the university publicized the bidding results for the construction contractors of four minor infrastructure repair projects on September 30. The publicity period runs from September 30 to October 8, 2013.

## Bid Results

| No. | Project | Winning contractor | Bid amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Halal snack room project | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. | ￥79,918.97 |
| 2 | Staff dormitory stairwell repair works | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. | ￥13,935.08 |
| 3 | Roof insulation brick repair of the U-shaped classrooms in Building E | Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. | ￥69,216.00 |
| 4 | Prefabricated reinforced-concrete tables and chairs in campus gardens | Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. | ￥12,600.00 |
