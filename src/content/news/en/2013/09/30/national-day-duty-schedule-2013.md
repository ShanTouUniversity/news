It is reported that the Party and Government Office issued a notice on September 30, 2013, summarizing duty arrangements for the National Day holiday.

To facilitate inter-departmental contact during the holiday, the consolidated duty roster is now available for departments to check. Details are in the OA attachment. Questions may be sent to the Party and Government Office at o_jdk@stu.edu.cn.

> ⚠️ The duty roster is available on the OA system.
>
