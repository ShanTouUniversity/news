It is reported that Zhicheng College of Shantou University has published Issue 5 (No. 42 in total) of its journal *Zhicheng Life* for 2013. The issue reviews the college's activities around September, covering team-building training, summer service learning, mentors taking up their posts, Party study sessions, orientation, preparations for the fifth-anniversary College Day, a dean's sharing session, Mid-Autumn Festival events, training camps for new members of student organizations, and external exchanges.

## Team building: 12th "ATP" camp serves 257 new students

From the evening of September 13 to September 15, the college held the 12th "ATP (Action-Teamwork-Passion)" team-building training camp, a two-day, two-night intensive program for all 257 freshmen of the Class of 2013. It focused on developing a strong sense of social responsibility, self-confidence, independence, teamwork and leadership. Dean Li Dan gave a keynote speech based on his own rich experience, guiding participants to understand themselves and develop their potential, and moved them through education on gratitude. In the team-building sessions, each squad rehearsed and performed a program themed "Responsibility · Dedication · Being an STUer" or "New Life at STU", giving full expression to their expectations and creativity. A survey showed that more than 88 percent of participants felt the camp met or exceeded their expectations. One participant said, "In every game, the teamwork, communication and cooperation made me feel what team spirit really is," adding that "ATP" — the source of life energy — brings a constant stream of inspiration to freshmen.

## Summer service learning: a public-welfare course reaches a mountain village in Heyuan

From July 17 to 21, 45 teachers and students of the college's summer public-welfare course traveled to Jiantou Town Central Primary School and Xintian Village in Dongyuan County, Heyuan City, where they held a quality-development summer camp for local primary and secondary school students under the theme "Cultivating Virtue and Self-Cultivation · Gratitude and Dedication". They offered art courses including dance, paper folding, music and paper cutting, taught the students how to conduct themselves, inspired them to dream of going to university, and staged a gala evening together with local villagers.

## Mentors: the sixth cohort completes a second round of training and formally takes up posts

On August 31, the college held the second round of training for the sixth cohort of mentors, covering topics such as guiding students from the outset, case analysis of typical work situations, and peer psychological support. After the training, all 35 mentors of the sixth mentor committee received their letters of appointment from Vice Dean Chen Wenbin and formally took up their posts.

## Party study: intensive sessions on the spirit of the Party's mass-line education campaign

From August 27 to 29, members of the college's Party general branch held intensive study sessions on the spirit of "deepening the educational campaign to practice the Party's mass line", organized by Chen Wenbin, secretary of the general branch. On August 30, Li Dan, vice president of the university and a member of the university Party committee's standing committee, and Bai Youjin, director of the publicity department, visited the college to attend a special democratic life meeting and to guide the college's educational campaign.

## Orientation: the Zhicheng family welcomes 257 freshmen of the Class of 2013

From August 30 to September 1, orientation activities organized and planned by the Zhicheng student union were held, with Li Dan, vice president of Shantou University and dean of Zhicheng College, and Yu Hong, director of the Student Affairs Office, on hand to offer guidance. This year the college received 257 new students who moved into the residence halls. On September 1, Vice Dean Chen Wenbin and all teachers of the college affairs office visited the new students; on September 3, the college held a general meeting for all 2013 freshmen in the Science Lecture Hall, where Dean Li Dan delivered a speech titled "From Good to Great" and student organizations of the college and the university student union gave presentations.

## College Day: preparations advance for the fifth-anniversary gala

To prepare for the fifth-anniversary "College Day" gala of Zhicheng College, the organizing committee held a host-selection contest on the "Yifang" lawn on the evening of September 15, selecting outstanding hosts for the "In the Prime of Youth" gala and giving residents a stage on which to present and improve themselves. On September 27, the preparatory committee conducted a first review of the performances in Room D503 of the teaching building, where the judges offered constructive suggestions on each act.

## Dean's sharing session: "Don't Wait Until After Graduation"

On the evening of September 26, Dean Li Dan attended the first dean's sharing session of the semester, themed "Don't Wait Until After Graduation". He noted that students should meet a constantly changing society with "today's unchanging effort". He said that university is the stage of life when mistakes cost adults the least, so students should be bold in trying things, not fear mistakes, and learn from them. Life is a marathon, he said, and one should not place too much weight on temporary success or failure: "Life is a rising exponential curve; as long as you keep accumulating, one day it will take off." He also reminded students not to simply divide knowledge into the useful and the useless.

## Mid-Autumn Festival: a garden party where "My Mid-Autumn Festival, My Way"

On the evening of September 18, the life services department of the college's student union held a garden party titled "My Mid-Autumn Festival, My Way" on the "Yifang" lawn. Through four segments — a lantern riddle garden tour for residents, a tangyuan-making contest, displays by residential zones, and a final lantern riddle showdown between zones — the event created a warm and lively Mid-Autumn atmosphere and let residents staying on campus feel the warmth of the college family.

## New-member training camps: two student organizations hold their own programs

On September 20 and 21, the Zhicheng student union and the Ivy Service-Learning Association held their own training camps for new members. The camps taught work skills, helped new members become familiar with work procedures and improve efficiency, strengthened unity and overall cooperation within the organizations, and gave new members a sense of belonging.

## Environmental venture contest: Amway Green Dream Factory roadshow held at STU

On September 25, the eastern Guangdong roadshow of the "Amway Green Dream Factory" venture contest was held at Shantou University. The contest is hosted by the Youth League Committee of Zhicheng College and co-organized by the Ivy Service-Learning Association and the "Green Ribbon" Service-Learning Environmental Volunteer Team of the STU Youth Volunteer Association. It aims to inspire environmental creativity among college students and environmental advocates and to attract more students to join environmental efforts. The roadshow drew keen attention from STU students.

## External exchanges: two groups of guests visit the college

On August 21, 60 primary and secondary school principals from Chaozhou City visited the college while taking part in Shantou University's "Ten Hundred Thousand Talent Training Project" for outstanding principals. Vice Dean Chen Wenbin and all teachers of the college affairs office received them and introduced the college's founding philosophy and development. On September 16, a four-member delegation led by Feng Chongzhang, vice president of the Henan Provincial Association for the Promotion of Filial Piety Culture, visited the college; Vice Dean Chen Wenbin and teachers Zhang Yan and Jiang Dongping received them, introduced the college's educational philosophy and practice, and held in-depth exchanges on carrying forward fine traditional Chinese culture.
