It is reported that Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) will continue to provide academic consultation services for all undergraduates this semester, with the service starting on the Tuesday of the fourth week of the semester (October 8). Students are welcome to use the services at SLC.

## Key information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service start | October 8, 2013 (the Tuesday of the fourth week of the semester) |
| Who can use it | All undergraduates of the university |
| Opening hours | 3:00–10:00 p.m. daily |
| Attachments | Three attachments in total, including the academic consultation schedule and the user guide; see the original OA notice |

SLC is open daily from 15:00 to 22:00. The academic consultation schedule and the user guide for this semester are provided as attachments to the notice, three in total, so that students can learn the times of each service and how to use it.

> ⚠️ The service schedule and user guide are available in the OA notice attachments.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
