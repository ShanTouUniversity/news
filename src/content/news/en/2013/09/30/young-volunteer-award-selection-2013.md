It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued on September 30 the *Notice on the Selection of Shantou University’s 2012–2013 “Young Volunteer Pacesetters” and “Outstanding Young Volunteers”* (Shantou University Youth League Joint Document No. 7 of 2013). The Youth League Committee and the STU Young Volunteers Association will commend a number of “Young Volunteer Pacesetters” and “Outstanding Young Volunteers” who worked actively and achieved outstanding results in 2012–2013. Recommendations will be made by the Youth League committees and volunteer organizations of the colleges.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | Shantou University’s 2012–2013 “Young Volunteer Pacesetters” and “Outstanding Young Volunteers” |
| Document No. | Shantou University Youth League Joint Document No. 7 of 2013 |
| Eligible Candidates | Young volunteers who worked actively with outstanding results in 2012–2013 |
| Recommendation Deadline | 6:00 p.m., Monday, October 21, 2013 |
| Recommended Quotas | Main-campus colleges: 1–2 pacesetters and 2–4 outstanding volunteers each; Medical College (including affiliated hospitals): 4 pacesetters and 8 outstanding volunteers; directly affiliated units: 4 pacesetters and 10 outstanding volunteers |
| Commendation Occasion | The first plenary meeting of the STU Young Volunteers Association in 2013–2014 |

## Selection Criteria

The notice sets out four criteria:

1. Progressive in thinking, positive and aspiring, and law-abiding;
2. Taking part in volunteer activities for the right purpose, not calculating personal gains and losses, and being willing to contribute;
3. Regularly taking part in volunteer service on and off campus, performing actively and being recognized by the organization;
4. Making contributions and achieving outstanding results in volunteer service activities.

## Recommendation Process and Required Materials

Candidates are recommended by the Youth League committees and volunteer organizations of the colleges. The electronic version of the detailed materials must be sent to the mailbox of the STU Young Volunteers Association office (contact details are available in the OA notice) **before 6:00 p.m. on Monday, October 21, 2013**, and printed materials must be submitted at the same time. The Youth League Committee and the Association will then review the materials and make the final selection.

The materials to be submitted include:

1. One electronic copy and one printed copy of the *Recommendation and Application Form for Individual Volunteer Service Awards of the Shantou University Young Volunteers Association*, with the electronic file named in the format *Shantou University Young Volunteers Association Volunteer Service “Young Volunteer Pacesetter” Recommendation Form–College (Residential College)–Name*, or *…“Outstanding Young Volunteer” Recommendation Form–College (Residential College)–Name*;
2. A 1,000–2,000 character account of the candidate’s volunteer service, one electronic copy and one printed copy, named in the same format;
3. One electronic personal life photo, named in the same format;
4. One electronic copy and one printed copy of the *Shantou University Volunteer Service Hours Certificate*, named in the same format. The certificate serves as one of the reference materials for the selection and is based on the hours recorded in the Shantou University Young Volunteer Registration and Management System.

## Recommended Quotas

| Recommending Unit | “Young Volunteer Pacesetters” | “Outstanding Young Volunteers” |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main-campus colleges | 1–2 | 2–4 |
| Medical College (including affiliated hospitals) | 4 | 8 |
| Directly affiliated units | 4 | 10 |

The notice also notes that the “Outstanding Volunteer” and “Young Volunteer Pacesetter” awards cannot be applied for at the same time. The selected “Young Volunteer Pacesetters” and “Outstanding Young Volunteers” will be commended together at the first plenary meeting of the Shantou University Young Volunteers Association in 2013–2014, so as to encourage the advanced and set an example.
