It is reported that, in accordance with the relevant notice of the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Management Committee and the fund's project management measures, the Research Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 30 to launch the university's 2013 completion process for Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects. A total of 21 projects are to be completed this year, covering fields including engineering, science and management.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Projects to be completed | 21 |
| Acceptance format | Panel review for key projects; review by correspondence for general projects (free application and doctoral startup) |
| Expert requirements | No more than one acceptance expert from STU; experts must come from different institutions (secondary units count as the same institution); experts must be peers with an associate professorship or above; three experts for general projects and five for key projects |
| Online completion period | October 9 to November 12, 2013 |
| Submission of materials | November 24 to 30, 2013, with two paper copies submitted to the Research Office |
| Deadline for extension requests | Contact the Research Office before October 15, 2013 |
| Contact telephone | 86502813 |

## Acceptance Format and Expert Requirements

Acceptance is organized separately according to project category. Key projects use panel review: project leaders must report the acceptance time and the preliminary list of experts to the Research Office before October 15, and the provincial fund office will conduct spot checks. General projects (free application and doctoral startup) use review by correspondence. The detailed operating steps for project completion are set out in the *Online Project Completion Procedure for Provincial Fund Projects* (Attachment 1).

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Acceptance format for key projects | Panel review; acceptance time and preliminary expert list reported to the Research Office before October 15 for spot checks by the provincial fund office |
| Acceptance format for general projects | Review by correspondence |
| Number of experts from STU | No more than one |
| Expert affiliations | Experts must come from different institutions, with secondary units counting as the same institution |
| Expert qualifications | Peer experts with an associate professorship or above |
| Number of experts | Three for general projects, five for key projects |

## Schedule

| Time | Work arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| October 9 to November 12 | Project leaders enter and submit the provincial fund project completion report online |
| November 12 to 18 | Formal review by the Research Office |
| November 19 to 23 | Review by the provincial fund committee |
| November 24 to 30 | Project leaders submit two paper copies approved by the fund committee to the Research Office |

The paper materials submitted include the project completion report (with the original expert group comments), a copy of the project contract, published papers and monographs (papers must carry an acknowledgement of provincial fund support), patent certificates and other supporting materials for project completion.

## Extension Requests and Contact

Project leaders who need to extend their project completion must contact the Research Office before October 15. Contacts: Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng; telephone: 86502813.
