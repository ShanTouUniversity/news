It is reported that severe typhoons Usagi and Wutip have successively caused considerable impact in Guangdong, with waterlogging, fallen trees and casualties reported in some areas. As the 2013 National Day holiday approaches, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University reiterated its holiday safety tips on September 30, asking students to keep their own safety in mind while travelling and going out so that they can fully enjoy a safe and happy holiday.

## Travel and Property Safety

The office advises students to pay attention to safety advisories issued by the cities they visit, choose properly licensed travel agencies and consider buying travel insurance. Before setting out, they should store and keep cash and valuables properly and close doors and windows, and are advised to tell relatives, family members or teachers where they are going and leave a contact telephone number.

## Transport Safety

During public holidays there are many travellers and heavy pressure on transport. Students leaving campus should buy tickets and board at lawful, licensed passenger stations; when going out they must strictly observe traffic regulations, take care when riding, refuse unlicensed vehicles and overloaded vehicles, never drive without a licence, and guard against traffic accidents.

## Outdoor Activities and Fraud Prevention

When going into the countryside or visiting relatives and friends, students should travel in groups as far as possible, take care of their personal safety and property, guard against being cheated, robbed or stolen from, and avoid displaying valuables in public. They should also raise their awareness of self-protection, improve their ability to tell true from false, guard against fraud through false information, telephone calls and online shopping, and not take chances hoping for gains obtained improperly. In an emergency they should stay calm and handle the situation steadily, consulting classmates or teachers when necessary.

## Staying on Campus and Fire Safety

Students staying on campus must observe the university's daily schedule and safety management rules, must not let outsiders stay overnight, should not casually let strangers into their dormitories, and should keep away from broken branches and steel canopies. Autumn and winter are dry and windy and a period when fires occur frequently, so fire safety calls for special attention: open flames and high-power appliances such as immersion heaters are strictly prohibited in student dormitories; fishing and swimming at reservoirs or other dangerous waters are not allowed; entertainment activities held on campus must take place only under safe conditions and must not affect or hinder other students; candles, fireworks, sky lanterns and the like must not be lit during celebrations; no fire may be started in no-fire zones; and carrying open flames up hills or having barbecues there is strictly forbidden.

## Avoiding Falls and Reporting Problems

Students are also reminded not to climb over walls, railings or fruit trees, so as to avoid dangers such as falls, and in particular not to step on the sunshade panels on the second floor of the student dormitory area. Any abnormal situation found during the holiday should be reported promptly: the Student Community Management Service Centre can be reached at 86502661 and the Campus Security Section at 82904110.
