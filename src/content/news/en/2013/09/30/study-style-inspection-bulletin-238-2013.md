It is reported that the "Three Styles" Development Office of Shantou University, which works on teaching style, study style and school spirit, has issued Bulletin No. 238 on study style inspections for week 2 of the fall semester of the 2013-2014 academic year. Members of the inspection team checked the teaching buildings that week. At 8:00 a.m. on September 25 (Wednesday), 12 students were found late for class by less than 5 minutes at Teaching Building E; at 8:00 a.m. on September 26 (Thursday), 15 students were found late by less than 5 minutes and three by more than 5 minutes in the same teaching building.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bulletin number | No. 238 |
| Week | Week 2 of the fall semester, 2013-2014 academic year |
| Time and place | 8:00 a.m., September 25 (Wednesday) and September 26 (Thursday), both at Teaching Building E |
| Late arrivals | September 25: 12 students late by less than 5 minutes; September 26: 15 late by less than 5 minutes and 3 by more than 5 minutes |
| Follow-up | The list was sent to all schools (residential colleges), which are asked to criticize and educate the students concerned and to keep working on the study style |
| Issuing office | STU "Three Styles" Development Office |

The bulletin forwarded the list of students who failed to observe university rules and regulations to each school (residential college), asking them to criticize and educate the students concerned and to keep consistently improving the study style.
