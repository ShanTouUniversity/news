It is reported that, with strong support from faculty members, the Shantou University 2013 teaching methods essay and selection activity organized by the Academic Affairs Office has concluded successfully. The office arranged for experts from within the university to review the submitted papers, and a total of two first prizes, three second prizes and six third prizes were awarded.

## Award List (in no particular order)

**First Prize (2 papers)**

1. Reform and Practice of Teaching Methods for the *Road Engineering* Course Based on Engineering Competence Development (Wu Ming and Lin Peng, Department of Civil Engineering, School of Engineering)
2. *Alternatives to teacher feedback for improving college students’ writing in China* (Zhang Li, English Language Center)

**Second Prize (3 papers)**

1. Educational Opportunities in Music Teaching at Regular Higher Education Institutions (Lu Xiaoling, Art Education Center, School of Arts)
2. Teacher Quality, Educational Philosophy and Teaching Methods (Zhu Wang, Department of Foreign Languages, School of Liberal Arts)
3. A Preliminary Study of Case-Based Teaching for the Course *Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Legal Basis* (Shen Yiyong, Social Sciences Department)

**Third Prize (6 papers)**

1. Cultivating Accounting Students' Competence to Match Market Demand: The Perspective of Embedding “Integrated Thinking” (Long Yue'e and Xu Zongling, Business School)
2. Teaching Reform of *Graph Theory* Aimed at Improving Teaching Quality and Cultivating Innovation Ability (Lin Xiaoping, Department of Mathematics, School of Science)
3. From the University Classroom to Regional Humanistic Care: A Preliminary Study of the “4C” Educational Philosophy and Photography Teaching Models in Higher Education (Xie Lin, Journalism School)
4. English Teaching and Education (Zhang Li, English Language Center)
5. Design and Implementation of a Life Education Course Based on the “Service Learning” Model: The Case of Shantou University's Life Education Course (Zhang Xin, Student Affairs Office)
6. A Preliminary Study of Teaching Methods for the Issue of Justifiable Defense (Shen Yiyong, Social Sciences Department)

## Selection and Recognition

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizer | Academic Affairs Office |
| Review method | Experts from within the university reviewed the submitted papers |
| Awards | 2 first prizes, 3 second prizes and 6 third prizes, 11 papers in total |
| Recognition | Honorary certificates; winning papers compiled into a volume and sent to each teaching unit, with the electronic version posted on the Academic Affairs Office homepage |

The Academic Affairs Office will present honorary certificates to the winners and compile the winning papers into a volume to be sent to each teaching unit, with the electronic version posted on the office's homepage for faculty and students to study and exchange.
