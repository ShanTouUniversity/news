It is reported that, to build a better outdoor atmosphere on campus and respond to the enthusiasm of STU faculty and students for outdoor activities, the Student Affairs Office and the Shantou University Snow Leopard Outdoor Association will hold the second Campus Camping and Outdoor Film Exchange from 4:00 p.m. on October 5 to 7:00 a.m. on October 6 on the lawn in front of the Grand Hall. The programme includes a display and exchange of wilderness survival skills, sharing of outdoor experiences, outdoor film appreciation and an on-campus camping experience, and all faculty and students are welcome to join.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Second Campus Camping and Outdoor Film Exchange |
| Time | 4:00 p.m. October 5 to 7:00 a.m. October 6 |
| Venue | Lawn in front of the Grand Hall |
| Programme | Wilderness survival skills display and exchange, outdoor experience sharing, outdoor film appreciation, on-campus camping experience |
| Camping places | 20 |
| Registration email | snowleopardstu@163.com |
| Deadline | Midnight on October 2 (24:00) |

## Registration

There is no limit on the number of places for the earlier exchange segments of the event, so outdoor enthusiasts may simply take part. Only 20 places are offered for the on-campus camping experience; those interested should complete the application form and send it to snowleopardstu@163.com. If more than 20 people apply, participants will be selected at random. Registration closes at midnight on October 2.

## Notes

- Please take good care of the lawn and the surrounding environment during the event, dispose of personal rubbish properly, and bring your own rubbish bags if needed;
- Please follow the arrangements of the event staff and help maintain order on site;
- Please do not bring valuables that are not related to the event.

Further details are available on the association's Sina Weibo account, @汕头大学雪豹户外协会 (Shantou University Snow Leopard Outdoor Association).
