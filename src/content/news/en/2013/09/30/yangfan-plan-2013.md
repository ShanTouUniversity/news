It is reported that, in order to implement the strategic arrangements of the Provincial Party Committee and the Provincial Government for further accelerating the revitalization and development of eastern, western and northern Guangdong, and to vigorously carry out the "Talent Development Support Plan for Eastern, Western and Northern Guangdong" (the "Yangfan Plan"), and in accordance with the Notice on the 2013 Yangfan Plan Application Work (Yue Ren Cai Ban [2013] No. 2), the Human Resources Office and the Research Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 30 to organize applications for three Yangfan Plan programs relevant to the university: innovation and entrepreneurship teams, urgently needed top talent, and high-level talent cultivation. On-campus application materials must be submitted to the Human Resources Office by October 9.

## Program 1: Introduction of Innovation and Entrepreneurship Teams

This program is open to teams from overseas, from outside Guangdong province and from the Pearl River Delta region. Following the principle of the "three emphases and two alignments" — emphasizing team strength, technological achievements and industrialization prospects, and aligning with the needs of accelerated development and of optimizing the economic structure — ten teams will be introduced across three tiers, with provincial funding of RMB 8 million, RMB 5 million and RMB 3 million respectively.

An applicant team must be closely linked to the key industrial fields for development in eastern, western and northern Guangdong, with technology at an advanced level within its industry, mastery of key core technologies and independent intellectual property rights, and good prospects for industrialization, so that it can lead local industrial development and generate significant economic and social benefits. A team consists of one leader and no fewer than three core members, who must have sound moral character and professional integrity, abide by the law and be in good health. After being introduced, the team must work in the region for more than five years, with at least one member working full time in the region, while other members spend no less than three months a year working there.

Science and technology innovation teams must also meet the following requirements:

- Team members have had stable working relationships for more than two years at a university, enterprise, research institution or on a research project;
- Team members are under 60 years of age (born after December 31, 1952), while top-tier talent such as academicians of the Chinese Academy of Sciences or the Chinese Academy of Engineering and academicians of developed countries are under 65 (born after December 31, 1947);
- The team leader holds a doctoral degree or a professional title of associate senior level or above, has more than three years of research experience and outstanding achievements, and the remaining members hold a master's degree or above, or an intermediate professional title or above;
- Team members sign a letter of intent with the employer, undertaking to sign a work contract and take up the post within three months of selection; if a formal contract has already been signed, it must have been first signed after January 1, 2011.

Applicant teams must apply to the municipal Science and Technology Bureau where they are located by October 10. The municipal Science and Technology Bureau reviews the authenticity of the application materials and completes a summary table of recommendations; after examination by the Municipal Party Committee Organization Department, the materials are submitted to the Special Office for the Introduction of Innovation and Entrepreneurship Teams of the Provincial Department of Science and Technology by October 20. Teams must complete the 2013 Yangfan Plan Innovation and Entrepreneurship Team Application Form and submit ten printed copies of the application form, two copies of the attachments, and two CDs containing the electronic versions of the form and attachments.

Other provisions: the talent-introduction scope is determined by the region of the talent's full-time employer before coming to work in the region; talent mobility within the same prefecture-level city and between different branches of the same enterprise or institution is not covered by this program; "full-time work in eastern, western and northern Guangdong" requires at least nine months of work per year, and domestically introduced talent must transfer their personnel file or social insurance relationship to the receiving place; innovation and entrepreneurship teams in the region that are selected under the "Pearl River Talent Plan" are exempt from review, automatically selected and entitled to this program's funding.

## Program 2: Introduction of Urgently Needed Top Talent

This program introduces 20 top talents urgently needed in the region, from outside Guangdong province and from overseas, for leading, distinctive and advantageous industries and major projects in eastern, western and northern Guangdong, as well as top talent urgently needed for discipline development and the commercialization of research results in universities and research institutes. The provincial government provides RMB 1 million per person.

Applicants must abide by the law, be morally upright, in good health and highly committed; be under 55 years of age (born after December 31, 1957); hold a doctoral degree or a professional title of associate senior level or above; work full time in eastern, western and northern Guangdong for more than three years after being introduced; and sign a letter of intent with the employer undertaking to sign a work contract and take up the post within three months of selection, with any formal contract already signed having been first signed after January 1, 2011. Applicants must also meet one of the following conditions:

- Technology at an advanced level domestically, within the province or within the industry, with independent intellectual property rights, contributing to enterprise innovation, product upgrading and industrial development (priority is given to those whose results, technology or patents have already been commercialized or industrialized);
- Key technical talent that helps advance key projects and major works (priority is given to those with experience in national major projects or key works);
- Top talent that helps develop disciplines in universities and research institutions;
- Other high-level talent urgently needed for local economic and social development.

Applicants must apply to the municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau where they are located by October 10. The bureau conducts a compliance review of the application materials and selects recommended candidates; after examination by the Municipal Party Committee Organization Department, the materials are submitted to the Professional and Technical Personnel Management Division of the Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security by October 20. Applicants must complete the 2013 Yangfan Plan Urgently Needed Top Talent Application Form and submit 15 copies together with an electronic version; the municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau must also submit written materials on the selection and recommendations as well as a recommendation summary table.

Other provisions: the talent-introduction scope is determined by the region of the talent's full-time employer before coming to work in the region; talent introductions from within the province and mobility between different branches of the same enterprise or institution are not covered by this program; "full-time work in eastern, western and northern Guangdong" requires at least nine months of work per year, and domestically introduced talent must transfer their personnel file or social insurance relationship to the receiving place; leading talent in the region that is selected under the "Pearl River Talent Plan" is exempt from review, automatically selected and entitled to this program's funding.

## Program 3: Cultivation of High-Level Talent

This program selects 30 outstanding young and middle-aged talents with a solid foundation, high quality and strong potential from eastern, western and northern Guangdong as key cultivation targets, with provincial funding of RMB 200,000 for each.

Applicants must hold Chinese nationality, abide by the law, be morally upright and in good health, and have a relatively high level of research and development or technical expertise; hold a doctoral degree or a professional title of associate senior level or above; be under 50 years of age (born after December 31, 1962); have worked full time continuously in the region from September 17, 2010 to the present; and work full time in the region for more than three years after selection. Applicants must also meet one of the following conditions:

- Being one of the top two principal contributors to a provincial (ministerial) Science and Technology Award of second prize or above, or the first contributor to a municipal first prize in science and technology;
- Being a recipient of the National Natural Science Foundation;
- Being the head of a provincial-level laboratory or a national engineering (technology) research center;
- Holding more than one invention patent (ranked first);
- Having served as a principal technical person in charge of a provincial-level or higher science and technology project or a major technological transformation project with outstanding contributions.

Applicants must apply to the municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau where they are located by October 10. The bureau conducts a compliance review of the application materials and selects recommended candidates; after examination by the Municipal Party Committee Organization Department, the materials are submitted to the Professional and Technical Personnel Management Division of the Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security by October 20. Applicants must complete the 2013 Yangfan Plan High-Level Talent Cultivation Application Form and submit 15 copies together with an electronic version; the municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau must also submit written materials on the selection and recommendations as well as a recommendation summary table.

Other provisions: "full-time work in eastern, western and northern Guangdong" requires personnel file relations to be in the region and at least nine months of work per year; high-level talent in the region that is selected under the "Guangdong Special Support Plan" is exempt from review, automatically selected and entitled to this program's funding; experts in the region who already receive the State Council special allowance or who are national candidates of the New Century Hundred-Thousand-Ten Thousand Project are not eligible as cultivation targets for this program.

## Scope and Application Requirements

The Yangfan Plan mainly covers eastern, western and northern Guangdong, including prefecture-level cities and their counties (cities and districts) outside the Pearl River Delta region — namely Shantou, Shaoguan, Heyuan, Meizhou, Shanwei, Yangjiang, Zhanjiang, Maoming, Qingyuan, Chaozhou, Jieyang and Yunfu — as well as Longmen County of Huizhou and the counties of Guangning, Deqing, Fengkai and Huaiji in Zhaoqing.

The implementation measures, application forms, application tables and summary tables for the programs can be downloaded from the Guangdong Organization Work website (http://www.gdzz.cn). Applicants may not apply for two or more programs at the same time.

## On-Campus Submission

Application materials and electronic versions must be submitted to the Human Resources Office by October 9, 2013. Printed materials should be delivered to the Administrative Center, Room 315, electronic versions sent to wing@stu.edu.cn, and inquiries made by telephone at 86502349. The notice was issued jointly by the Human Resources Office and the Research Office.
