It is reported that, citing institutional personnel changes, the Business School of Shantou University has adjusted the members of its school council in accordance with the provisions and procedures of the Working Regulations of the Business School Council.

Under the adjustment, Lü Yuan, dean of the school, serves as director of the council. The members include Wang Yi, Ji Yun, Song Xiaobao, Chen Yinglin, Ouyang Feng, Zhou Xiao'e, Huang Yan, Huang Wangming, Qin Zhong and Zeng Qiang (listed in surname stroke order), with Huang Xianglong serving as secretary.

The decision was issued as Document Shan Da Shang Fa [2013] No. 12 and dated September 9. The school said the complete list after the adjustment is subject to the official university document.
