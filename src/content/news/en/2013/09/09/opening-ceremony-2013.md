It is reported that the Party and Government Office and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold the 2013 opening ceremony at 9:00 a.m. on Friday, September 13, in the university auditorium. Each school will organize its 2013 freshmen to line up and enter the venue in advance.

## Ceremony Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date and time | 9:00 a.m., Friday, September 13, 2013 |
| Venue | University auditorium |
| Freshman entry | Entry begins at 8:25 a.m. and must be completed by 8:50 a.m. |
| Rostrum seating | Heads of schools and administrative departments gather at the South VIP Lounge by 8:50 a.m. before entering |

## Entry Arrangements

Each school is responsible for organizing its 2013 freshmen to line up and enter the auditorium in advance. The auditorium opens for entry at 8:25 a.m., and all freshmen must be seated by 8:50 a.m. Heads of schools and administrative departments who are scheduled to sit on the rostrum must gather at the South VIP Lounge of the auditorium by 8:50 a.m. and then enter together.

## On-site Requirements

All attendees must dress neatly and may not enter in slippers, undershirts or shorts. They must follow the directions of on-site staff to keep the atmosphere lively and orderly.

Students carrying cameras may take photographs from their seats but may not move around the venue at will. Students seated in the first row of the balcony should not place water cups or other items on the guardrail. During the ceremony, all attendees must set their mobile phones to vibrate mode.
