It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Union are staging the university's "Nine-College" Star Debate Competition, an event aimed at carrying on STU's debating tradition and sharpening students' critical thinking. The contest is scheduled for 7:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. on September 9 in the Science Lecture Hall.

## Event Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 9, 7:30 p.m.–9:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Teams | Representatives of the debate teams of nine colleges (residential colleges) |
| Topic | College students in a relationship need / do not need to take marriage as the goal |
| Format | 44 minutes in total, four debaters per side, with rounds including opening statements, cross-examination, cross-examination summary, free debate, closing statements and a super one-on-one debate |

## Format and Judging

According to the organizers, each side fields four debaters and the contest runs 44 minutes in total. The rounds proceed in sequence: opening statements, cross-examination, cross-examination summary, free debate, closing statements and a super debate round, with the free-debate round giving each side five minutes of speaking time. After the rounds, judges score both sides to decide the winning team.
