It is reported that Shantou University has announced its class suspension and adjustment arrangements for the 2013 Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holidays. Classes will be suspended on the day of the Mid-Autumn Festival and on the first three days of the National Day holiday, while some weekdays before and after the holidays will run as teaching days, with the relevant classes swapped with weekend classes. Classes originally scheduled for October 5 and 6 (Saturday and Sunday) will be held as usual.

## Class Suspension Dates

- **Mid-Autumn Festival**: classes suspended on September 19 (Thursday)
- **National Day**: classes suspended from October 1 to 3 (Tuesday to Thursday)

## Class Swap Table

| Classes swapped | New teaching date |
| --- | --- |
| September 20 (Friday) classes | September 22 (Sunday) |
| October 4 (Friday) classes | September 29 (Sunday) |
| October 7 (Monday) classes | October 12 (Saturday) |

## Classes Held as Scheduled

Under the swap arrangement, September 22 (Sunday) will follow the September 20 (Friday) timetable, September 29 (Sunday) will follow the October 4 (Friday) timetable, and October 12 (Saturday) will follow the October 7 (Monday) timetable. Classes originally scheduled for October 5 and 6 (Saturday and Sunday) will be held as usual.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
