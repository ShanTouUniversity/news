It is reported that, as part of the education and practice campaign on the mass line, Shantou University will screen an integrity education film at 3:00 p.m. on September 11 (Wednesday) in the Grand Auditorium. Faculty and student Party members across the university will attend the screening.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., September 11 (Wednesday) |
| Venue | Grand Auditorium |
| Participants | Faculty and student Party members across the university |
| Organizers | Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches |

The notice requests all Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to organize their faculty and student Party members to attend. Participants should enter the venue in advance and take their seats in the assigned areas; specific seating arrangements are set out in the attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Commission Office)

> ⚠️ The event attachment with the seating arrangements is available on the OA system.
