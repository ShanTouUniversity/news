It is reported that, to prepare for the MBA teaching qualification evaluation, the Business School of Shantou University decided to adjust the members of its MBA evaluation preparatory group following a meeting on MBA work held on September 5, 2013.

Under the adjustment, Lü Yuan, the school's newly appointed dean, serves as head of the group; Xu Zongling and Xu Erming serve as advisers; Ouyang Feng, Wang Yi and Chen Yinglin serve as deputy heads. The members include Xie Shunlong, Lin Jiali, Zeng Qiang, Huang Wangming, Song Xiaobao, Qin Zhong, Ji Yun and Huang Xianglong, while Yin Xiaoqing and Deng Lizhen serve as secretaries.

The decision was issued as Document Shan Da Shang Fa [2013] No. 11, copied to the Graduate School and dated September 9. The school said the complete list after the adjustment is subject to the official university document.
