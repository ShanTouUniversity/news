It is reported that to further improve the living environment, the Student Community Management Service Center of Shantou University will carry out a thorough cleaning of the interior floors and bathrooms of Buildings B13 and B14 starting at **12:30 p.m. on September 10**.

During the cleaning, each dormitory room is asked to arrange for at least one resident to stay in the room to assist the cleaning staff.

The notice was issued by the Student Community Management Service Center on September 9, 2013.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
