It is reported that, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Education Examinations Authority's notice on registration for the 2013 second-half national College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 (Yue Kao Yuan [2013] No. 279), the Graduate School of Shantou University issued a notice on September 9 setting out the arrangements for graduate students. The exams will be held on December 14, with CET-4 running from 9:00 to 11:20 and CET-6 from 15:00 to 17:20.

## CET-6 Eligibility

- Enrolled graduate students who hold a CET-4 certificate, or scored 425 or above in CET-4, may register for CET-6; CET-4 certificates are reviewed by the graduate secretary of each school.
- Enrolled graduate students who hold a TEM-4 certificate may register for CET-6; the certificate is likewise reviewed by the graduate secretary of each school.
- Enrolled graduate students who hold a CET-6 certificate, or scored 425 or above in CET-6, may register again, but the number of re-registrations after reaching 425 may not exceed two.

## Registration and Payment

Main-campus graduate students must log in to the registration system with their campus network account, follow the registration procedure, select the exam level (CET-4 or CET-6) and submit. After registering successfully, they must confirm with the graduate secretary of their school (department, institute) before the registration deadline; those who fail to do so will bear the consequences. Online registration runs from September 9 to 21.

The fee is 38 yuan. For both graduate and undergraduate students, the fee will be deducted from registrants' campus card accounts by the Network Center on behalf of the Finance Office on September 22; if the deduction fails, Bank of China will deduct it from tuition accounts on the morning of September 23 on behalf of the Finance Office. Students should keep sufficient balance in their accounts during this period; a failed deduction means a failed registration.

## Notes

- Under the relevant regulations, non-enrolled graduate students may not register for this round of the CET-4/6 exams.
- Candidates must bring their ID card, student ID and admission ticket to the exam; anyone missing any of the three will not be admitted, so please prepare these documents in advance.
- For technical problems during registration, see the original OA notice for the technical support contact details.
