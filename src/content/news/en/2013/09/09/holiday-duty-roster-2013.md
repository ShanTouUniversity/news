It is reported that the Party and Government Office of Shantou University issued a notice on September 9, asking relevant units to submit their duty rosters for the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holidays by September 16 so that the arrangements can be compiled and released.

## Submission Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| What to submit | Duty roster for the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holidays |
| Duty dates | Mid-Autumn Festival: September 19; National Day: October 1–3 |
| Deadline | By September 16, 2013 |
| Submit to | Party and Government Office |
| Contact | o_jdk@stu.edu.cn, telephone 86502305 |

## Units Covered

The submission follows the requirements of Document No. 3〔2013〕 of the Party and Government Office and covers the Party and Government Office, the Resources Management Office, the Student Affairs Office, schools and colleges, the News Center, the Network and Information Center, the Science and Technology Center, and the D, E and G teaching buildings.

The Party and Government Office said the rosters are being collected so that duty arrangements can be published during the holidays and departments can reach one another easily. Once all units have reported, the office will compile and release the consolidated rosters.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
>
> ⚠️ The duty roster attachment is available on the OA system.
