It is reported that, citing institutional personnel changes, the Business School of Shantou University has decided to adjust the members of its faculty appointment group. The relevant document was addressed to the STU Faculty Appointment Committee.

Under the adjustment, Lü Yuan, dean of the school, serves as head of the group. The members include faculty members such as Ji Yun, Song Xiaobao, Chen Yinglin and Ouyang Feng (listed in surname stroke order), with Huang Xianglong serving as secretary.

In April this year the school formed a new faculty appointment group responsible for appointments to annual-salary positions, then headed by Xu Zongling; the latest adjustment follows the change of dean. The decision was issued as Document Shan Da Shang Fa [2013] No. 13 and dated September 9. The school said the complete list after the adjustment is subject to the official university document.
