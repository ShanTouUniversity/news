It is reported that, to enrich students' extracurricular life, enliven campus culture and showcase the university's student clubs, the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Students' Associations Union will hold the 17th Club Night at 8:00 p.m. on Tuesday, September 10, on the open ground in front of the Grand Hall.

## Event Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 10 (Tuesday), 8:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Open ground in front of the Grand Hall |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee of Shantou University; Shantou University Students' Associations Union |

The organizers said the 17th Club Night is open to all students and faculty, and everyone is welcome to attend and experience the spirit of STU's student clubs.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
