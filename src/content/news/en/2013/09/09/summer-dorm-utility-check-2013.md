It is reported that the Resources Management Office of Shantou University has released the utility charges for student dormitories for the 2013 summer vacation. Students may log in to check their records now, with the verification period running from September 9 to 13. Charges that are not disputed during that period will be deducted through the All-in-One Card from September 16.

Under the university's summer arrangements (STU Party and Administration Office Document No. 4 of 2013), the summer vacation for students on campus ran from June 30 to August 17, 2013. Because the military training of the 2012 cohort and the relocation of some medical students changed dormitory occupancy, the Water and Electricity Center took four sets of meter readings during the summer so that water and electricity use could be calculated for each stage.

## Summer meter-reading schedule

| Reading | Date | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | July 2 | After graduating students left campus |
| Second | July 16 | After the military training of the 2012 cohort ended |
| Third | August 16 | Before the end of the summer vacation |
| Fourth | August 31 | Before new students registered |

## Checking and verifying charges

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| How to check | Log in to the utility management system at http://power.stu.edu.cn/ |
| Login account | Campus network account |
| Verification period | Office hours, September 9 to 13 |
| Where to submit objections | Water and Electricity Center (beside the Science and Technology Building), tel. 86502489 |
| Accommodation list discrepancies | Counselor's Office (Room 106, Building B), tel. 86503355 |

Students who find errors or omissions in their charges may complete and sign the Utility Fee Review Application Form and submit it to the Water and Electricity Center during office hours from September 9 to 13. Those whose dormitory occupancy does not match the student list may contact the Counselor's Office.

## Payment arrangements

| Item | Time or details |
| --- | --- |
| All-in-One Card deduction begins | From September 16, by the Network Center (tel. 86503545) |
| Regular payment period | September 16 to 18 |
| Overdue payment | Handled under university regulations on September 23 |
| Corrections requested after the verification period | Three to five working days |

The Resources Management Office noted that charges are deemed accepted if no objection is raised by the end of the verification period. Because corrections require coordination among several departments, objections raised after that period are expected to take three to five working days to process. Students are also reminded to top up their All-in-One Cards in time so that an insufficient balance does not affect the deduction.
