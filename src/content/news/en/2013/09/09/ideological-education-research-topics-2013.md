It is reported that applications for Guangdong Province's 2013 college ideological education research topics are now open. The STU Research Office issued a notice on September 9 explaining the topic categories, eligibility requirements and application procedures. Applicants must be full-time staff currently working in ideological and political education at the university, and STU is limited to 12 applications.

## Categories and Topic Selection

The topics fall into four categories: key funded projects, key self-funded projects, general funded projects and general self-funded projects. Topics must be selected from the 2013 Guangdong Provincial Higher Education Institutions Ideological and Political Education Topic Guide (Attachment 1); self-devised titles will not be accepted. Applicants may add a subtitle under the main topic title if a research direction needs to be refined.

## Number of Projects and Research Period

According to the notice, 145 projects are planned, including 57 key projects and 88 general projects. Key projects normally have a two-year research period, while general projects normally have a one-year period.

## Eligibility

- Applicants must be full-time staff currently engaged in ideological and political education at the university.
- The "University Counselor Team Building" topic is open only to frontline full-time counselors; approved projects will be assigned a dedicated mentor to guide the research. Counselors are also encouraged to apply for topics related to their practical work.
- Each project leader may apply for only one topic, and may take part in no more than two applications as a non-leader.
- Those with unfinished Guangdong provincial higher education ideological and political education projects may not apply; those with a record of poor academic integrity in previous ideological and political education research may not apply within three years.
- STU is limited to 12 applications.
