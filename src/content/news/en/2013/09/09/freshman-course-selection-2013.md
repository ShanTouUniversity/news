It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a notice on course selection for the 2013 freshman cohort. Selection starts on September 12 and runs through three stages in sequence — first selection, second selection and course adjustment — ending at 5:00 p.m. on September 20.

## Course-Selection Schedule

| Stage | Time | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First selection | September 12, 2:00 p.m.–September 13, 4:30 p.m. | Students may select beyond a class's seat quota; after the stage closes, excess selections in over-subscribed classes will be deleted at random, and this random deletion applies to the 2013 freshman cohort only |
| Second selection | September 14, 9:00 a.m.–September 15, 5:00 p.m. | Selections are handled on a first-come, first-served basis |
| Course adjustment | September 16, 9:00 a.m.–September 20, 5:00 p.m. | Students may adjust their own selections; those unable to do so may submit a course-change application. After the stage closes, the Academic Affairs Office will officially release the course-opening plan, and selection records will no longer be changed |

## Textbook Ordering

The notice states that the university has ordered first-semester textbooks for the 2013 freshmen in advance. The books cover public compulsory courses such as Public English, Public Mathematics, Introduction to Mobile Programming, Integrative Thinking and ideological and political courses, as well as most major-specific compulsory courses. Once a freshman selects a course, the corresponding textbook will be ordered by default.

The textbook-order list will be based on the course-selection list as of **5:00 p.m. on September 15**. Course adjustments made after that time will not affect textbook orders.

The Academic Affairs Office said freshmen with questions about course selection may contact the academic administrator of their major.
