It is reported that the Dawn Education Project of the STU Youth Volunteers Association has launched the third phase of Qishu Action, themed "Let Smiles Remain in the Sunshine," and is recruiting **16 volunteers** from across the university. The program will run on **Wednesday afternoons from late October to late December** at the Shantou Disabled Persons' Activity Center.

## Background

In September 2011, the Shantou Disabled Persons' Federation opened Minghui Basic Education Class, the city's first basic-education class for blind children, which follows a primary school curriculum with subjects including Chinese, mathematics, English and handicrafts. The Dawn Education Project has partnered with the class to run Qishu Action, completing two phases over the past year to provide sustained educational support for children with visual impairments.

## Service Content

Volunteers will offer "second classroom" activities such as storytelling and performance, music, handicrafts, science activities and physical exercise, delivered through group teaching or one-to-one tutoring. The program aims to accompany children with visual impairments as they learn and understand the social environment.

## Recruitment and Registration

Applicants should be committed to public service, responsible and patient, and have good communication and teamwork skills. Volunteers will be selected through interviews to be held on **September 14–15 and 21–22**, with specific times to be announced separately.

Students interested in applying may download the electronic application form and email it to `qishuxingdong@163.com`, with the file name prefixed by their grade, major and name. Information and on-site registration desks will operate at the Golden Triangle from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. and 5:00 to 7:00 p.m. on September 9, 10 and 16. Registration closes at **6:00 p.m. on September 16**.
