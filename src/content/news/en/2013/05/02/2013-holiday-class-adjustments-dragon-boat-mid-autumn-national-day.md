STU's Party and Government Office, Academic Affairs Office and Graduate School have issued Document No. 3〔2013〕 of the Party and Government Office, publishing the 2013 holiday, workday and class-adjustment schedules for the Dragon Boat, Mid-Autumn and National Day holidays. The university said that any changes required by higher authorities or special university circumstances would be announced separately.

### Holiday and class schedules

| Holiday | Holiday and makeup workdays | Classes suspended | Class swaps and regular classes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dragon Boat Festival | June 10–12, three days; workdays on June 8 (Saturday) and June 9 (Sunday) | No classes on June 12 | June 8 classes swap with June 10 (Monday); June 9 classes swap with June 11 (Tuesday) |
| Mid-Autumn Festival | September 19–21, three days; September 22 (Sunday) is a workday | No classes on September 19 | September 20 (Friday) classes swap with September 22 classes |
| National Day | October 1–7, seven days; workdays on September 29 (Sunday) and October 12 (Saturday) | No classes on October 1–3 | September 29 classes swap with October 4 (Friday); October 7 (Monday) classes swap with October 12; October 5 and 6 classes run as usual |

### Holiday operations

All units must inspect for safety hazards before each holiday and implement safety and energy-conservation checks. The Security Office and Resources Management Office will handle campus security and logistical support, while the Network and Information Center will maintain and manage the campus network backbone. Schools and colleges must also manage faculty and student activities during the holidays and keep track of their scale, content, format and participant numbers to ensure safety.

The Party and Government Office, Resources Management Office, Student Affairs Office, schools and colleges, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and the D, E and G teaching buildings must arrange holiday duty. Duty information must be submitted to the Party and Government Office one week before each holiday. The public institutional submission channel is `o_jdk@stu.edu.cn`, and the office telephone is `86502305`. The duty-roster attachment contains internal staff names, duty rooms and telephone numbers, which are not reproduced here.
