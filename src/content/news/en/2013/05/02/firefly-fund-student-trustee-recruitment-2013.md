The STU Youth Volunteer Firefly Love Foundation is recruiting **three student representative trustees** to strengthen the foundation's management. Formally established on **April 25, 2008**, the foundation operates under the guidance of the STU Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association. It conducts campus-wide charitable fundraising and supports students and designated groups through aid grants, a general assistance fund and special funds.

### Eligibility

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cohort | Students from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts, including graduate students |
| Youth Volunteer Association status | Must not be a Youth Volunteer Association member in the next academic year; ordinary volunteers are exempt |
| Public-service commitment | Enthusiasm for the foundation's charitable work |
| Experience and responsibility | Relevant experience and the ability to perform trustee duties responsibly |
| Oversight and integrity | Ability to supervise the use of donations for the foundation's charitable purposes; clean conduct and impartiality |

Student representative trustees may participate in the foundation's leadership, vote on major decisions and offer operational recommendations. They must also fulfill the responsibilities and obligations set out in the foundation's management regulations.

### Board responsibilities

The foundation council reviews the management regulations and funding plans, supervises daily operations and the use of funds, decides whom the foundation assists, reviews annual financial and work reports, and handles other major matters. The management regulations attached to the notice state that the council generally consists of seven trustees and that student representative trustees serve one-year terms.

### Application arrangements

Applications are accepted from **May 2 through 11:30 p.m. on May 10**. Paper forms may be placed in the designated envelopes below posters near the Golden Triangle and the entrance to the Fourth Dining Hall, or submitted to staff at the publicity booths at those two locations on **May 6**. Interview time and location will be announced separately.

The email link in the application form points to the foundation's publicly used address, `fireflyfundofstu@163.com`, while the notice body lists different email information. Applicants should confirm the final submission address through official channels of the Youth League Committee, Student Affairs or the foundation.

The application form requests students' names, sex, date of birth, school or department, year, contact details, student number, political status, personal profile and relevant work experience. Its contents are not reproduced here.
