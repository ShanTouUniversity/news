The STU Youth League Committee has issued Youth League Document [2013] No. 10, formally recognizing outstanding Youth League organizations and individuals from 2012. The commendations cover **27 Outstanding Youth League Branches (General Branches)**, **46 Outstanding Youth League Cadres** and **244 Outstanding Youth League Members**.

### Commendation categories

| Category | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Youth League Branches (General Branches) | 27 |
| Outstanding Youth League Cadres | 46 |
| Outstanding Youth League Members | 244 |

The Youth League Committee said that, over the past year, Youth League organizations carried out work in ideological education, organizational development, social practice, scientific and technological innovation, entrepreneurship and campus culture, producing a number of outstanding groups and individuals. It encouraged the honorees to build on the recognition and called on Youth League organizations and members across STU to learn from them and contribute to the university's development and Youth League work.

The list of honorees is included in the OA attachment. Because it contains student names and affiliated units, this article reports only the categories and totals and does not reproduce the individual list.
