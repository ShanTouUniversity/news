It is reported that the fitness test selection for Shantou University's Antarctic Scientific Expedition project was successfully completed on April 29–30. The Student Affairs Office recently announced the results: among the 124 entrants in the fitness test, Cai ZR and 35 other students, along with Zeng Qiang and 19 other teachers, have advanced to the Nan'ao training camp selection stage.

## A Look Back at the Two-Day Tests

The fitness tests spanned two days, each with a distinct focus:

| Date | Test Items | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April 29 | Swimming, treading water, sit-ups, push-ups, pull-ups, weighted running, etc. | Fitness, swimming ability, and willpower |
| April 30 | Ten individual games including paper airplane, penguin census, password to Antarctica, Antarctic natives, etc. | Basic problem-solving, coordination, and general knowledge |

## Makeup Water Tests for Some Entrants

Six other entrants (three students and three teachers) requested deferrals of the water-based test items due to health reasons, having completed all other items. Under the arrangements, these six must take a makeup water test on May 4. In the makeup session, entrants must complete at least 50 meters of the 100-meter swim to advance; failure to do so counts as elimination.

## Upcoming Selection Stages

The Nan'ao training camp selection is scheduled for May 11–12, during which each entrant's performance throughout the process will be assessed. Entrants who cannot attend will be considered withdrawn. After the camp selection comes the final interview, which will ultimately select 16 students and 4 teachers to carry out the Antarctic project. The training camp results will count for 30% of the final evaluation.

In addition, the scientific research plan is part of the final interview assessment. Entrants must resubmit an improved plan through the system by May 15 — the theme must remain unchanged, and the entire plan may not exceed three single-sided A4 pages. The exact reopening time of the system will be announced by email.
