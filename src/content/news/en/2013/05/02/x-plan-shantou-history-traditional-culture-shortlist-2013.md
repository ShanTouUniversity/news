STU Student Affairs has announced the shortlist for the Student Community X Plan project on Shantou history and traditional culture. The notice says that **26 teams** had registered by **April 27**, and that judges selected **13 teams** for the next stage based on their overall performance.

### Research themes of the shortlisted teams

The attachment also lists the shortlisted teams' research themes. To protect student privacy, team names that may contain personal names, team leaders' names, year and major information and other personal details are not reproduced. The research themes are retained below:

| No. | Research theme |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Historical sites and local customs in the old urban district |
| 2 | Shantou inlaid-porcelain culture |
| 3 | Buddhist culture in Shantou |
| 4 | Chaoshan worship and traditional festival culture |
| 5 | Chen Cihong's former residence |
| 6 | “The Most Shantou” |
| 7 | Traces of the old district |
| 8 | Exploring Shantou's charitable culture |
| 9 | Red junks and Yingge dance culture |
| 10 | Following the traces of the Teochew city |
| 11 | Food and architecture near Xiaogongyuan Park |
| 12 | Exploring Mazu culture |
| 13 | Documentary study of Chaozhou |

Student Affairs asks the shortlisted teams to prepare for the next stage according to the project arrangements. This notice does not restate subsequent dates, funding or award arrangements; details should follow later notices from the organizers.
