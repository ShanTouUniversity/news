STU Academic Affairs Office has published a make-up class arrangement. The class taught by Bai Wenjun of the Social Sciences Department, **Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law**, section **58949**, was canceled in Week 8 on Tuesday (**April 16**).

The make-up class is scheduled as follows:

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Make-up time | Week 11 Sunday, May 5, during the A-B-C periods |
| Location | Room 203, E Teaching Building |
| Course | Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law, section 58949 |

Academic Affairs asks students enrolled in the course to inform one another.
