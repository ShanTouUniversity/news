STU has announced the results of Guangdong's 2012 selections for Outstanding Youth League Members, Outstanding Youth League Cadres and May Fourth Red-Flag Youth League Organizations. **Li P**, a student at STU Medical School, was named an Outstanding Youth League Member of Guangdong Province.

The STU Youth League Committee congratulated the honoree and called on Youth League organizations, cadres and members to learn from outstanding individuals and contribute actively to the university's development and Youth League work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
