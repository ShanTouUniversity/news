STU Resource Management Office has published the medical-examination schedule for 2011-cohort undergraduates on the main campus. Examinations begin on the **afternoon of May 6, 2013**. Each major has two scheduled options, allowing students to choose a time around their classes. The notice says that the examination for this cohort consists only of chest X-rays.

### Chest-X-ray schedule

The examination period is **2:30–4:50 p.m.** on every listed date.

| Date | Majors |
| --- | --- |
| May 6 (Monday) | 2011 English, Physics, Mathematics and Electronics |
| May 7 | 2011 Law, Civil Engineering and Public Administration |
| May 8 (Wednesday) | 2011 Chinese Language and Literature, Chemistry, Chinese as a Foreign Language, Biology, and Mechanical and Electrical Engineering |
| May 9 (Thursday) | 2011 Physics, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Law, Biology, Electronics, Mathematics, Journalism, and International Trade |
| May 10 (Friday) | 2011 Arts, Journalism and Communication, Public Administration, Computer Science, and English |
| May 13 (Monday) | 2011 Chinese Language and Literature, Business Administration, Communications, International Trade, Civil Engineering, and Chemistry |
| May 14 (Tuesday) | 2011 Arts, Business Administration, Computer Science, and Communications |

The original attachment labels May 7 with the anomalous weekday text “星期四二.” Because that weekday entry contains a character or formatting error, this article retains the confirmed date without attempting to correct it speculatively.

The public information covers only the examination type and the schedule by major. No student list, identifying information, health information or examination results is reproduced. Detailed check-in requirements should follow notices from the relevant schools and Zhicheng College.
