The STU Youth Volunteer Firefly Love Foundation has opened applications for its 2013 Xue Hai Ying Guang student aid grant. Established in 2009, the program provides assistance to students in need. This year's application round was launched with the approval of the foundation council.

### Grant amount and eligibility

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Grant amount | RMB 500 per recipient |
| Available places | 20 |
| Eligible applicants | Students enrolled at STU |
| Application period | From the notice date through midnight on May 11 |

Applicants must meet the following conditions:

- Have received no other form of student aid or scholarship during the 2012–2013 academic year;
- Come from a financially disadvantaged family and maintain a simple lifestyle;
- Demonstrate good academic performance and conduct;
- Care about university life and actively participate in volunteer activities organized by the university.

### Review and materials

Applicants must submit both electronic and paper materials. The published requirements include an application form, academic transcript, materials reviewed and stamped by the relevant school or college Youth League organization, a guarantor-opinion form, a student-card copy and a campus-card spending record from the previous month. The foundation secretary-general will lead the review, and seven council members will vote on the applications. Personal financial circumstances, family hardship and lifestyle are the main considerations; part-time income and participation in campus activities are reference factors only.

The application and guarantor forms contain personal information about students' family finances, spending, academic records and identity, so their contents are not reproduced. The email address, paper-submission locations and telephone number in the original notice belong to individual contacts and are omitted here. Applicants should confirm submission channels and locations through official channels of the Youth League Committee, Student Affairs or the foundation.
