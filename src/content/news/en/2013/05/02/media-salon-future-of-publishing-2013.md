STU's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a media salon from **7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on May 3** in Room 307, E Teaching Building. Zhao Dongxiao, managing director and general manager of Hong Kong Chung Hwa Book Company, will speak on “The Future of Publishing.”

### Lecture details

| Time | 7:00–9:00 p.m., May 3 (Friday) |
| Venue | Room 307, E Teaching Building |

### Discussion topics

The salon will cover three parts:

1. **The state of the publishing industry**: traditional publishing represented by print publications, the rapid development and intense competition of online publishing, and mergers and acquisitions in the publishing industry.
2. **What is publishing?** The lecture will examine publishing, traditional publishing, electronic publishing and online publishing, discuss the relationship between the publishing and cultural industries, and ask whether publishing in the future will still be publishing.
3. **What determines the future of publishing?** The notice repeats the three subtopics from Part 2 under this heading and provides no further distinction. This article retains that source detail.

### Speaker

Zhao Dongxiao graduated from Renmin University of China with a doctorate in literature. He previously served as deputy president of Renmin University Press and as an expert member of the National Committee on Distribution Standards. He is now managing director and general manager of Hong Kong Chung Hwa Book Company, a director of Singapore Chung Hwa Book Company and an adjunct master's supervisor at the School of Journalism of Renmin University of China. His research interests include cultural communication, publishing operations and management. His books include *Online Publishing and Its Impact* and *Publishing Marketing*.

Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
