The finals of STU's 17th University Debate Competition will take place **May 4–5**. Organized by the Communist Youth League Committee and the Student Union, this year's competition carries the theme **“Walk, and Debate”** (且行，且思辩). After a month of preliminary contests, the Journalism and Business schools advanced to the championship match, while the Medical and Science schools advanced to the third-place match.

### Final-round schedule

| Match | Teams | Debate motion | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Third-place match | Medical School (affirmative) vs. Science School (negative) | Should communicators or supervisors bear primary responsibility for the spread of online rumors? | 7:30 p.m., May 4 (Saturday) | Lecture Classroom 101, E Teaching Building |
| Championship match | Business School (affirmative) vs. Journalism School (negative) | Which matters more: “who you walk with” or “where you are going”? | 7:30 p.m., May 5 (Sunday) | Science Report Hall |

The Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union invite faculty, staff and students to attend the matches.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
