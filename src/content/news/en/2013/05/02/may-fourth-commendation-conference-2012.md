STU will hold its 2012 May Fourth commendation conference and a discussion marking the 94th anniversary of the May Fourth Movement at **3:00 p.m. on May 3** in **Conference Room 205, Science and Technology Building**. The university Youth League Committee has asked each school Youth League committee and directly affiliated branch to organize attendance.

The event will combine the university's 2012 May Fourth commendations with a discussion commemorating the 94th anniversary of the May Fourth Movement. The notice does not publish the honorees or the discussion's speaking arrangements; any further information will depend on subsequent announcements from the organizers.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
