Shantou University Library has opened trials of **Essential Science Indicators (ESI)** and **InCites** from **May 2 through May 31, 2013**. Faculty, staff and students are invited to use the resources and provide feedback to the Library.

### ESI: an indicator database for international research impact

ESI is an analytical database developed by Thomson Reuters based on academic literature indexed in the ISI Web of Science SCIE and SSCI databases and the references cited in that literature. The notice says that researchers can use ESI to analyze international scientific literature systematically and selectively, examining the development and influence of scientists, research institutions or universities, countries or regions and academic journals in particular fields. Research administrators can also use its data for decision analysis.

ESI access page: [lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=367](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=367)

### InCites: a research-evaluation and benchmarking tool

InCites is based on citation data from Web of Science SCIE, SSCI and AHCI and combines multiple metrics with international benchmarks by discipline and year covering 30 years. According to the notice, users can use InCites to track institutional research output and influence, compare institutional performance with other institutions and with global and field averages, identify researchers with academic influence and development potential, and monitor research collaborations in order to seek potential partners.

InCites access page: [lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=368](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=368)
