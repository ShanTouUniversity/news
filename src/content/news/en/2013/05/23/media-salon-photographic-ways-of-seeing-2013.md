STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a media salon on **“Ways of Seeing Photography” from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on May 27** in the **STU Library Lecture Hall**. Su Zhigang, a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Photography of Beijing Film Academy, will speak. Faculty members and students across the university were welcome to attend.

## Speaker

Su Zhigang was director of the Training Center of Beijing Film Academy and had previously served as dean of its School of Photography. He was also vice chair of the Photography Committee of the Chinese Higher Education Society and a visiting researcher in Asian photography studies at the Korea Institute of Photography History.

His honors included:

- the fifth China Photography Education Golden Statue Award from the China Federation of Literary and Art Circles;
- the eighth China Photography Theory Golden Statue Award from the China Federation of Literary and Art Circles;
- the Chinese Photographers Association award for virtue and artistic excellence;
- the fifth Golden Candle Award from the China Higher Photography Education Award Fund under the former State Education Commission; and
- the sixth Outstanding Contribution Award for China Higher Photography Education from the same fund.
