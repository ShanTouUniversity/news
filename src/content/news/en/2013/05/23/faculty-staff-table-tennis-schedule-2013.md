The Shantou University Labor Union released the schedule for the 2013 faculty and staff table tennis team tournament. The tournament would run from **Monday, May 27 through Friday, May 31**, with eight teams competing in group matches, semifinals and finals. Matches would begin at **4:30 p.m.** each day.

## Participating Teams

The schedule listed the following teams: Government and Administrative Team, College of Engineering, College of Liberal Arts, Library, Journalism and Research Union Team, Resource Management Team, College of Science, Law and Business Union Team, and Arts Union Team. The regulations used different names for two combined teams: the schedule’s Library, Journalism and Research Union Team appeared as the “Research/Journalism/Library Union Team,” while its Arts Union Team appeared as the “Arts/Social Sciences, Continuing Education and Affiliated Schools Union Team.” Both source wordings are retained here.

## Rules

Players had to be current STU faculty or staff members. Each match used a **best-of-three, 11-point format** and consisted, in order, of men’s singles, women’s singles, men’s doubles, mixed doubles and women’s doubles. All five matches had to be played. Male players could not enter more than one event; female players could enter one additional event, and at least three women had to take the court. Teams from units with 80 or fewer employees could register up to 12 players, while larger units could register up to 15.

The group stage used a single round-robin format: a win earned two points, a loss one point and a forfeit zero. Ties were resolved in sequence by head-to-head results, match wins and losses, games won and lost, and points scored and conceded.

The top four teams would receive awards, while the other teams would receive participation awards. Every player would receive a commemorative gift. Teams were asked to arrive on time and follow the referee’s decisions.
