Following an arrangement issued jointly by the Shantou Municipal Bureau of Culture, Radio, Television, Film and Press and other organizations, STU’s Publicity Department would organize faculty members, staff and students from the main campus to see the inspirational educational film *Goalball Champion*. The film was jointly produced by the China Disabled Persons’ Federation and Beijing Dahe Dongliu Culture Communication Co., Ltd., a partner of China Central Television. It tells the story of blind athletes overcoming difficulties, training hard and competing for national honor, while portraying the development of goalball for blind athletes in China.

## Registration

Attendance was voluntary. Participants had to complete the registration form and email it to the Publicity Department at `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn` by **Sunday, May 26** so that tickets could be purchased centrally. Registrants were expected to attend; attendance in blocks of adjacent seats might be checked, and ticket costs could be recovered from people who registered but failed to attend, to prevent waste. Attendees were responsible for their own transportation.
