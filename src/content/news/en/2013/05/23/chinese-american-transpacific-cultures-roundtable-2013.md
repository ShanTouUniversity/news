STU’s College of Liberal Arts would hold an alumni-faculty-student roundtable titled **“Cultures of the Chinese/American Transpacific” from 4 to 6 p.m. on Friday, May 24**, in **E202-1**. The forum would examine transpacific culture, history and literature and was open to the university community.

## Topics

1. **“Medicine and Poison: Travel and Translation of Opium in Late Qing Dynasty”**
   - Speaker: visiting alumnus Wang Yu (Rain), who held a PhD in history from Toronto University.
2. **“Dreaming of Home: Gothic Tales and Uncanny Towers of Kaiping Overseas Chinese”**
   - Speakers: James McDougall, associate professor of American studies, and a senior student from the College of Liberal Arts.
3. **“The Writer as Migrant: on Ha Jin’s Chinese and Immigrant Writing”**
   - Speaker: a student in an international-culture-related program who used the pen name Roman Shelley.

The names of the student speakers are not published here.
