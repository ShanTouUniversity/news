Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts planned to hold its **21st Student Congress at 2:30 p.m. on Wednesday, May 29**, in **E204 stepped lecture room**. The congress would review the work of the 20th Student Union Executive Committee, elect the 21st executive committee and set its priorities for the next term.

## Representative Selection

The congress was expected to have **about 80 representatives**, representing approximately 15% of the College of Liberal Arts student population. Representatives had to be current undergraduate students in the college with strong academic and personal records, a representative role and the ability to deliberate. Each class would elect its representatives through a meeting of all students under the principle of democratic centralism.

Student representatives and candidates were asked to sign in and take their seats at **2:15 p.m. on May 29**.
