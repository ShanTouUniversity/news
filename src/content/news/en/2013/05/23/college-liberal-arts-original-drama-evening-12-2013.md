Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts would hold its **12th Original Drama Evening at 7:30 p.m. on Saturday, May 25**, in the **Grand Auditorium**.

The notice indicated seating in the **middle seats of rows 6, 7 and 8**. It did not provide a program or ticketing details.
