It is reported that the Business School announced on May 23, 2013 the electives open to all students for the fall 2013 semester. The detailed course list is available in the OA attachment.

According to the Academic Affairs Office, credits earned by non-Business School students for these courses will count as social sciences general education credits, while credits for Business School students will count as Business School elective credits.

For inquiries, contact the Business School Academic Affairs Office at 86502277.

> ⚠️ The course schedule is available on the OA system.
