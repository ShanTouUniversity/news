STU’s Department of Social Sciences would offer **Situation and Policy Education (Theory)** in the **summer 2013 semester** to main-campus undergraduate students in the 2012 cohort. The university-wide required course carries **one credit** and was offered specifically to this cohort through **21 classes**. The notice warned that failing to take the course in this offering could affect graduation.

## Course Structure

Situation and Policy Education consists of theory and practical components worth **two credits in total**. The summer-semester offering covers the one-credit theory component. The practical component follows this timeline:

- Students study the Ministry of Education’s higher-education Situation and Policy teaching points and research and interpret related current affairs during their sophomore, junior and senior years.
- The course-selection notice for the practical component is issued in the sixth semester.
- Students complete the practical component in the seventh semester by writing a current-affairs report and commentary based on the assigned topic, their three years of study and their observations of major domestic and international events.

The original notice did not provide specific class times, venues or course-selection procedures. Students should follow the academic system and subsequent notices for those details.
