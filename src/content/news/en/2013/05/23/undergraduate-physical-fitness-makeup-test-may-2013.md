STU’s Physical Education Department announced arrangements for a makeup physical-fitness test for main-campus undergraduates. The unified test for the 2010 and 2011 cohorts was completed on **May 4 and 5, 2013**. Students who had not completed the test or otherwise qualified for a makeup test could attend on **May 26**.

Students taking the makeup test were asked to arrive according to the attached schedule and confirm that all six test scores had been entered on the instructor’s record. All six scores were required. The attachment contained individual arrival arrangements, so this article does not reproduce student names, arrival times or exemption-application information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
