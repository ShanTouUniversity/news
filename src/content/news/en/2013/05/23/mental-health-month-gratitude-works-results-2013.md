Shantou University’s eighth Mental Health Publicity Month, organized by the Student Affairs Office’s Mental Health Education and Counseling Center and hosted by the Psychological Growth Association, had concluded. The “Say It Out Loud: Gratitude” works competition awarded **four prizes**:

| Award | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 1 |
| Second prize | 1 |
| Third prize | 2 |

One student host was assigned to each of the opening and closing ceremonies. The names, years and colleges of the student awardees and hosts are not published here.
