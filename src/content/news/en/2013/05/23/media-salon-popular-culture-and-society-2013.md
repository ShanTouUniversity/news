STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication would hold a media salon titled **“Popular Culture and Society”** at **7:30–9:30 p.m. on May 25** in **ACC Conference Hall 3**. The guest speaker would be Feng Yingqian, dean of the School of Journalism and Communication at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. Faculty members and students across the university were welcome to attend.

The original notice rendered the start time as “19：:30-21:30,” which contains a formatting anomaly. This article records the readable time as 7:30–9:30 p.m.
