The term of the 20th editorial board of the *STU Youth* newspaper will end in late May. At the request of the 20th board, the STU Communist Youth League Committee will hold the newspaper's **20th annual meeting and election of its 21st editorial board at 2:30 p.m. on Saturday, May 25, 2013**, in **Room 207, Building E**.

## Meeting Agenda

The meeting will hear and review the 20th editorial board's annual work report, recognize outstanding *STU Youth* contributors and departments for 2012, and elect the 21st editorial board.

The 21st editorial board is planned to have **10 members**. Students may volunteer to run, after which preliminary candidates will be identified under the *STU Youth* newspaper charter. Following assessment interviews by the 20th editorial board and approval by the university Youth League Committee, **15 candidates** will participate in the election. Candidates will give speeches at the annual meeting and present their plans. All members of the 20th board will vote, and the 10 members will be selected according to the combined ranking of votes and interview results.

After the annual meeting, the 21st editorial board will hold its first meeting to elect an editor-in-chief and deputy editor-in-chief. Candidate names and personal application materials are not reproduced here.
