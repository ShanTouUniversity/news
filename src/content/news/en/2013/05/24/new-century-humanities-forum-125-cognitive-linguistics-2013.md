The 125th New Century Humanities Forum of Shantou University's School of Liberal Arts will be held from **7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on May 28, 2013**, in **ACC Meeting Room 3**. Professor Wang Yin of Sichuan International Studies University will deliver a talk on “Cognitive Linguistics and Chinese–English Contrastive Studies.”

## Forum Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Cognitive Linguistics and Chinese–English Contrastive Studies |
| Speaker | Professor Wang Yin, professor at the Foreign Languages and Literature Research Center and director of the Institute of Cognitive Science, Sichuan International Studies University |
| Time | 7:00–9:00 p.m., May 28, 2013 |
| Venue | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Organizer | Shantou University School of Liberal Arts |

The talk will introduce cognitive linguistics and humanism through the development of linguistics and philosophy. It will also discuss experiential philosophy, SOS and an experiential humanistic perspective, using comparisons of English translations of Chinese poetry and case studies to examine English–Chinese verb-noun and gerund constructions as well as simile idioms.

Professor Wang's research focuses on cognitive linguistics, semantics and Chinese–English contrastive studies. He has held research and teaching positions at several universities, led a National Social Science Fund project, published more than 20 monographs and textbooks, and produced more than 130 papers and other articles. Several of his research achievements have received awards.
