The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has launched the 2013 featured Youth League Day program, “Dialogue with Faith, Building Dreams.” From **May to September 2013**, Youth League committees at the schools and directly affiliated branches will organize themed activities adapted to their academic and professional contexts. The program is intended to encourage theoretical study, strengthen ideals and convictions, reflect on the value of life and turn reflection on faith and dreams into action.

## Activity Requirements

Youth League organizations may develop varied formats around the theme and organize activities with substantial content, broadening participation and impact. The university Youth League Committee encourages schools to publicize their activities through Weibo, QQ, Youth League websites, *STU Youth* newspaper and mobile newspaper, and other affiliated media platforms. Each school-level committee should conduct its own evaluation, recommend outstanding projects for the university-level review and summarize its experience to support longer-term work.

## Submission and Evaluation

By **September 17**, each school-level Youth League committee must submit paper copies of its activity plan, summary report and related materials to the university Youth League Committee Office, and send electronic copies to the official committee email, `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. Projects recommended for the university-level Outstanding Featured Youth League Day Award must also submit two copies of the recommendation form and detailed materials of approximately **1,500 Chinese characters**.

The program will offer **two Outstanding Organization Awards** and **six Outstanding Featured Youth League Day Awards**. University-level evaluation is planned to take place through a results presentation and sharing session provisionally scheduled for late September. Project leaders may use PowerPoint or another format to present results and share experience, after which an evaluation panel organized by the university Youth League Committee will select the awardees.

The attached recommendation form asks for basic activity information, an approximately **300-character** account of the activity's main achievements, and supporting activity plans, summaries, photographs or videos. Because the notice body and attachment specify different lengths, approximately 1,500 Chinese characters in the notice and approximately 300 characters in the form, applicants should follow the latest requirements from the university Youth League Committee and the OA attachment. Personal information such as project-leader names, telephone numbers and email addresses from the recommendation form is not reproduced here.
