The STU Youth Film Festival, organized by the Student Affairs Office, the STU Youth League Committee, STU Youth and the STU Student Activity Hall, will run from **May 22 to 31, 2013**, at the **Library Student Activity Hall**. The festival aims to help students and faculty explore Chinese-language films and their cultural meanings while enriching campus life.

## Screening Schedule

All screenings are scheduled for **7:00–9:00 p.m.**

| Date | Day | Film |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 22 | Wednesday | *万箭穿心* |
| May 23 | Thursday | *白鹿原* |
| May 24 | Friday | *有人赞美聪慧有人则不* |
| May 25 | Saturday | *天津闲人* |
| May 26 | Sunday | *爱情银行* |
| May 27 | Monday | *告诉他们，我乘鹤去了* |
| May 28 | Tuesday | *神探亨特张* |
| May 29 | Wednesday | *边境风云* |
| May 30 | Thursday | *飞越老人院* |
| May 31 | Friday | STU Original Microfilm Showcase |

## Student Activity Hall Offers

During the festival, the Student Activity Hall will offer one free activity according to the day of the week: fitness on Monday; climbing on Tuesday; X-Box on Wednesday; board games on Thursday; fitness on Friday; board games on Saturday; and climbing on Sunday. Availability is subject to the hall's on-site arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, STU Youth League Committee, STU Youth and STU Student Activity Hall)
