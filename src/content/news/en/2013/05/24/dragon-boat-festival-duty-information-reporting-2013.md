Following a relevant university policy document, STU’s General Office asked relevant units to submit the names and telephone numbers of personnel on duty on **June 12, 2013**, the Dragon Boat Festival holiday, by **June 5**. The information would be compiled into a university duty schedule for publication.

Reports could be sent to `o_jdk@stu.edu.cn`; the Party and Administration Office listed **8650 2305** as its telephone number. The attached holiday duty-schedule form also included fields for the duty room and notes and requested unit leaders' time away from campus, contact addresses and telephone numbers. Those personal duty and travel-contact details are internal reporting information and are not reproduced here.
