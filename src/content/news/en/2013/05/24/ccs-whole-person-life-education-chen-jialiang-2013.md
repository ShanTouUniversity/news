The College of Liberal Arts CCS Whole-Person Life Education public lecture series at Shantou University will host a lecture at **7:30 p.m. on Friday, May 31, 2013**, in the **Library Lecture Hall**. Professor Chen Jialiang, dean of the Faculty of Medicine and Choh-Ming Li Professor of Medicine and Therapeutics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, will present **“My Life in Medical Education.”** He will share reflections from his work in medicine, teaching and scholarship.

Professor Chen graduated from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 1988. He has served as head of the Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology, head of the Institute of Digestive Disease, associate dean of the Faculty of Medicine and director of the Division of Internal Medicine in the Graduate School. He received a Best Teacher Award for five consecutive years and the President’s Exemplary Teacher Award in 2000 and 2007.

Professor Chen’s research focuses mainly on gastroenterology and the treatment of gastrointestinal bleeding. He had published more than 300 research papers in leading international journals, including eight first-author articles in the *New England Journal of Medicine* and *The Lancet*. He has chaired the Hong Kong Society of Gastroenterology, served as deputy editor of the *American Journal of Gastroenterology*, and served on the editorial boards of international journals and research-funding review panels. He received a National Science and Technology Progress Award in 2007 for his contributions to the treatment of gastrointestinal bleeding. He also received the David Y. Graham Lectureship, becoming the first scholar based outside the United States to receive the honor.

In public service, Professor Chen established an intestinal screening program for residents of the eastern New Territories in 1999. During the 2003 SARS outbreak, he conducted screening for covert SARS infection among more than 12,000 Hong Kong residents. In recognition of his long-term contributions to Hong Kong society, he was appointed a Justice of the Peace by the Hong Kong SAR Government in 2010.
