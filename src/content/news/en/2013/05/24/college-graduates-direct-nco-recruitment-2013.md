It is reported that STU’s Student Affairs Office released information on the 2013 direct recruitment of non-commissioned officers from regular college graduates, following arrangements from the General Staff Department and the Shantou People’s Government Conscription Office. Eligible male college graduates could register by **June 10, 2013**.

## Eligibility and Majors

Applicants had to be male graduates of regular colleges, unmarried, no older than 24, and trained in a major needed by the armed forces. Political and physical requirements followed the applicable rules for conscripting enlisted personnel. Where the graduate’s institution offered vocational-skills certification in the relevant major, applicants also had to hold a nationally issued intermediate-level or higher vocational qualification certificate.

Shantou was focusing on graduates from the following four fields:

| Vocational field | Corresponding undergraduate major | Code and note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechatronics technology | Electrical Engineering and Automation | 80601; naval position |
| Computer network technology | Network Engineering | 080613 |
| Computer multimedia technology | Computer Science and Technology | 080605 |
| Applied electronic technology | Electronic Information Engineering | 080603 |

Graduates from other majors could also apply if they met the province-wide recruitment requirements.

## Registration

The process consisted of registration, a physical examination, political review, professional assessment, approval for enlistment, agreement signing, and handover and transportation. Online registration was being piloted in 2013. Graduates could visit the national college-student pre-enlistment platform to check the eligible majors and submit an application. The notice printed the addresses as `http//zbbm.chsi.com.cn` and `http//zbbm.chsi.cn`, both missing the colon after the protocol marker. In standard URL form they read `http://zbbm.chsi.com.cn` and `http://zbbm.chsi.cn`; applicants should verify the platform with the relevant conscription authority.

Applicants could also register by June 10 at the county or district conscription office where their household registration was held, or at the Shantou Conscription Office. Current graduates could register at the county-level conscription office where their graduating institution was located. Personal identity, education and other application materials listed in the notice are not reproduced here.

## Appointment and Benefits

Regular college graduates accepted through the program would be appointed that year to a corresponding non-commissioned-officer grade. Between approval for active service and the first conferral of rank, they would receive allowances at the rate for newly enlisted personnel. From the month in which the NCO order was issued, they would receive the corresponding NCO salary, insurance, housing, medical and subsidy benefits, with annual adjustments under the applicable rules. Years spent studying after high school within the nationally prescribed program length would count as active-service time.

NCOs who met the conditions for retirement, transfer to civilian work or demobilization would be handled under national regulations. Transfer and demobilization payments would be issued as one-time subsidies under the relevant rules. The STU People’s Armed Forces Department provided **86503299** for inquiries.
