Shantou University's student selection for the Antarctic expedition project has concluded. The judges conducted a comprehensive assessment of **38 candidates who reached the interview stage**, considering their performance at the Nan'ao training camp, their expedition proposals and their interviews, before determining the successful and alternate candidates.

## Selection and Eligibility Requirements

The results considered performance at the Nan'ao training camp, the Antarctic expedition proposal and the interview. The university also stated that a selected student will lose eligibility if he or she has any failing course grade in either the **fall or spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year**. If a successful candidate cannot participate because of academic performance or other personal reasons, eligible alternates will be called up in order.

The Student Affairs Office asked the students concerned to follow subsequent notices and cooperate with the next stages of the project. Because the list contains student names and other personal information, this article does not reproduce the successful or alternate lists; the original OA notice governs the detailed result.
