Shantou University will conduct interviews for faculty candidates for its Antarctic expedition project on **Wednesday, May 29, 2013**, in **Administrative Center Meeting Room 221**. The notice schedules **21 faculty candidates** for interviews: 12 from 9:00 a.m. to noon and nine from 2:30 to 5:00 p.m.

## Interview Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | May 29: 9:00 a.m.–noon and 2:30–5:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Administrative Center Meeting Room 221 |
| Candidates | 12 in the morning and 9 in the afternoon, 21 in total |
| Format | Individual interviews of 10–15 minutes each |
| Arrival | Candidates should wait outside the room 10 minutes before their scheduled time |

## Selection Criteria

The selection will assess three components: performance at the Nan'ao training camp, 30%; the Antarctic expedition proposal, 40%; and the comprehensive interview assessment, 30%. Proposal assessment will focus on feasibility, academic value and significance, methodological soundness and potential impact on STU faculty and students. The interview assessment will consider academic and research background, teaching or student-supervision experience, relevant overseas-exchange experience, interview performance and English proficiency.

The interview schedule attachment contains personal information about the candidates. This article does not reproduce the names or individual schedule.

## Selection Results

A follow-up notice issued by the Student Affairs Office on **May 31, 2013** states that the panel had completed a comprehensive assessment of the 21 faculty candidates who reached the interview stage. The assessment covered performance at the Nan'ao training camp, the Antarctic expedition proposals and the interview performance, after which selected and alternate lists were determined. The notice publishes the lists as an image; the faculty names are not reproduced here.

Under the project rules, selected faculty members must obtain approval from their employing units before participating. If a selected member cannot participate because approval is not granted or for another personal reason, eligible alternates will be admitted in order. The faculty concerned were asked to monitor subsequent notices and cooperate with the next stage of work.
