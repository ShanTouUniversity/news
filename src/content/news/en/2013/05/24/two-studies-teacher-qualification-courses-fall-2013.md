Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office will offer training courses in **Psychology** and **Education** during the **fall 2013 semester**. The courses are intended for **2014 and 2015 graduates**, including undergraduate and graduate students. Undergraduate enrollment information will be entered into the credit-based academic system and counted as general-education coursework, with priority given to 2014 graduates this semester.

## Registration and Course Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration period | 9:00 a.m. May 24 to 5:00 p.m. June 14, 2013, excluding weekends and public holidays |
| Registration venue | Academic Affairs Office service window, third floor, New Administration Center |
| Eligible students | 2014 and 2015 undergraduate and graduate students |
| Credits and hours | 2 credits and 36 class hours per course |
| Education textbook | A November 2009 Peking University Press edition by Yu Wensen and Wang [name not reproduced] |
| Psychology textbook | Instructor-prepared lecture notes |

Graduate students and students in the Medical College were asked to confirm their class selection when registering. Students who pass the examinations may later apply for the Certificate of Completion for Education and Psychology Training for Guangdong Teacher Qualification Recognition, issued by the Guangdong Teacher Qualification Recognition Office. The certificate is a required document for applying for teacher qualification and is valid within Guangdong Province.
