STU Youth held the “Rhythm and Motion on Campus” photography contest across the university from April to May 2013. The contest received **39 entries**, and the organizers announced the results on May 24 after the judging process was completed.

## Awards

| Award | Number of recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 1 |
| Second prizes | 2 |
| Third prizes | 3 |

The contest encouraged students to record musical and sporting scenes on campus while improving their photography skills and taking part in sports and music-related activities. To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce the winners' names, years or majors; the original OA notice governs the detailed list.
