The Shantou University campus selection for a Guangdong student speech competition on studying and implementing the spirit of the **18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China** was held on **May 17, 2013**, in Conference Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building. Twenty students registered after the call for applications was issued. The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the campus results.

## Awards

| Award | Number of recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 1 |
| Second prize | 2 |
| Third prize | 3 |

The first-prize recipient would represent Shantou University in the next round for eastern Guangdong. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish the recipients’ names, years or majors. One third-prize name was garbled in the source and has not been reconstructed.
