STU’s Academic Affairs Office announced the results of its selection for the 2013 national-level Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program. Following applications, internal selection and a public notice conducted under a Guangdong Provincial Department of Education program notice, the university selected **25 projects** for the 2013 program on the main campus.

## Project List

### Innovation Training

1. Exploring Public Relations in Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises in Shantou
2. A Chronicle of the Cultural Revolution in Chenghai, Shantou: Interviews with People Who Experienced the Cultural Revolution
3. Survey of the Current State of Dialect Broadcasting in the Chaoshan Region, Including Chaozhou, Shantou and Jieyang
4. Innovative Research on Building an Information-Communication Platform for Modern University Students
5. Development and Promotion of “A Taste of Shantou” Postcards
6. Analysis of College Students’ Entrepreneurship Conditions and Expectations: A Case Study of Shantou University
7. Building a Shantou University Business School Employment-Information Exchange Platform for Employer Presentations, Job Fairs and On-Campus Information Exchange
8. Analyzing the Feasibility of Delaying the Retirement Age from a Labor-Structure Perspective: A Case Study of the Chaoshan Region
9. Survey of College Students’ Participation in Gender Issues from a Feminist Perspective: A Case Study of Three Universities in the Chaoshan Region
10. Changes in and Adaptation of Folk Religious Activities in Traditional Communities during Urbanization: A Case Study of Yanzao Village, Chenghai District, Shantou
11. Physical-Model Study of Reinforcing Soil with New GFRP Soil Nails
12. Improving the Fire Performance of a Calcium-Silicate/Polyurethane Composite Thermal-Insulation Wall System
13. Automatic Counting and Packaging Machine for Disposable Cups
14. Water-Surface Waste-Cleaning Device Based on Jet-Flow Drive and Image-Recognition Technology
15. “Wind Fire Wheel”
16. Editing an Engaging Book about Mathematical Mysteries
17. Bioactive Surface Modification of Glass Slides
18. Research on Copper-Induced Crystallization of Amorphous Silicon

### Entrepreneurial Practice

19. “Shan Zhi Yun”
20. Shantou University Campus DIY Creative Home
21. Physical Store
22. Online Mall

### Entrepreneurial Training

23. Market Planning and Feasibility Study for a New Environmentally Friendly Integrated Thermal-Insulation Wall System
24. Interactive Catering-Advertising System
25. “Haibin Zoulu”: Design and Marketing of Shantou-Themed Postcards

The Academic Affairs Office asked each project team to carry out its innovation or entrepreneurship training according to its submitted plan and to strengthen project management so that the projects could be implemented successfully. The attached list also contained project numbers, student leaders, student numbers, other members and supervisors. This article publishes project titles only and does not disclose student identities or student numbers.
