The Shantou University Guitar Association will host the “Five Western Birds” theme music festival, with professional musicians, bands from inside and outside the university, campus music enthusiasts and alumni bands invited to perform. The event is organized by the STU Youth League Committee, the Student Societies Federation and the Guitar Association.

## Schedule

| Activity | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concert preview | 2:00 p.m., May 22, 2013 | Jinsanjiao |
| Theme music concert | 5:30 p.m., May 25, 2013 | Lawn to the left of the old Administration Building |

The event will distribute postcards and stickers and offer a stamp-collecting activity on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last. Sponsors will also provide ukuleles and teddy-bear toys as gifts.

Organizers reminded participants to observe site order and safety requirements and cooperate with event staff. If heavy rain causes the event to be postponed or relocated, follow-up information will be released through official channels.
