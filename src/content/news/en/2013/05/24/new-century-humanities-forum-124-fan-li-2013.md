The 124th New Century Humanities Forum of Shantou University's School of Liberal Arts will be held from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on May 27, 2013**, in **ACC Meeting Room 3**. Zhang Wenjiang, a researcher at the Literature Institute of the Shanghai Academy of Social Sciences, will speak on “Fan Li in the Yue House of the *Records of the Grand Historian*.”

## Lecture Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Fan Li in the Yue House of the *Records of the Grand Historian* |
| Speaker | Zhang Wenjiang, researcher at the Literature Institute of the Shanghai Academy of Social Sciences |
| Time | 3:00–5:00 p.m., May 27, 2013 |
| Venue | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Organizer | Shantou University School of Liberal Arts |

The lecture will analyze Fan Li's life choices and values in the Yue House of the *Records of the Grand Historian*, and discuss the political, economic and cultural ideas contained in the account as a way of reflecting on society and the self. Zhang's research focuses on classical Chinese studies, pre-Qin culture and literature. He studied Chinese at East China Normal University from 1978 to 1985 and has worked at the Literature Institute of the Shanghai Academy of Social Sciences since 1985.
