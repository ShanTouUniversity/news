Shantou University has decided to establish the **Changjiang Industrial Design Center** under the Changjiang School of Art and Design. The decision was reviewed and approved at the sixth university leadership meeting of 2013 on May 14 and announced in a university document dated **May 24, 2013**.

The university also made the following appointments:

| Position | Appointee |
| --- | --- |
| Center director | Wang Shouzhi |
| Executive director | Li Haoyu |

The Changjiang Industrial Design Center will carry out related work under the Changjiang School of Art and Design. The notice does not provide further details about the center's specific responsibilities or scope of operations.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University, Document No. 汕大发〔2013〕64号)
