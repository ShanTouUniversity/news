The fourth issue of Shantou University Library's special-collections feature, the second issue of 2013, focuses on Rao Zongyi. It introduces the Chaoshan-born scholar's work in Dunhuang studies, oracle-bone studies, paleography, early Chinese history, Chaoshan studies and art, and recommends nine representative books held by the library.

## Scholarship and Art

Rao Zongyi was born in Chao'an, Guangdong, in 1917. He taught at institutions including the University of Hong Kong and The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and served as a visiting or honorary professor at several universities. His work covers classical Chinese studies, Dunhuang studies, oracle-bone studies, Qin-slip studies, the history of China-Western and Chinese-foreign exchanges, ci poetry, religious studies and calligraphy and painting. The feature states that he had published more than 100 works, including over 60 monographs, more than 1,000 papers, over 10 collections of poetry and prose, and 45 collections of calligraphy and painting.

The feature also notes that Rao received honors including the Julien Prize, the Hong Kong Arts Development Council Award for Lifetime Achievement and the World Chinese Studies Contribution Award. In 2011, the International Astronomical Union named asteroid 10017 “Rao Zongyi.”
