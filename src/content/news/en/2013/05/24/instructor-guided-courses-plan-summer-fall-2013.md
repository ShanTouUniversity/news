STU’s Academic Affairs Office set out requirements for preparing instructor-guided course plans for the summer and fall 2013 semesters. Public-welfare courses would be handled separately. Academic administrators at each school or department were asked to complete course-information reporting, guided-class creation and publication, and topic setup in stages to standardize the operation and selection of instructor-guided courses in the credit-based system.

## Deadlines

| Task | Deadline | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submit course information | By May 29 | Complete the required template with course names and other information and email it to `yzchen@stu.edu.cn` |
| Create and publish guided classes | By May 30 | Create and publish each guided class in the credit-based system |
| Create and publish topics | By June 6 | Create and publish the topics set by each school or department for every class |
| Student selection | By the 17th week | The Academic Affairs Office would issue a selection notice in the 15th week; students had to complete selection by the 17th week, after which the system would close |

The notice labeled May 29 as “Tuesday of the 14th week” and June 6 as “Friday of the 15th week.” The weekday labels may not align with the calendar dates or may reflect a formatting error; this article retains the dates and weekday wording given in the notice rather than speculating about a correction.

If a topic’s exact title could not yet be determined, a school or department could enter a provisional title followed by “provisional, pending revision.” The title had to be updated as soon as the exact wording was known and no later than the entry of grades. The attached instructor-guided course plan template included the semester, course code and name, eligible student majors and year levels, and notes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
