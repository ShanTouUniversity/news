The ninth 2013 academic lecture of Shantou University's Business School will be held from **10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Tuesday, May 28, 2013**, in the **Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of the West Building**. Professor Liu Dawei of East China Normal University will speak on “Research on Human-Resource Integration in Corporate Mergers and Restructuring.” Professor Zeng Qiang will chair the lecture, which is open to the university community.

## Lecture Focus

The talk will examine human-resource integration in corporate mergers and acquisitions, including its legal, technical and corporate-culture dimensions, as well as risks and crises that may arise when integration fails. It will consider how human-resource integration can affect the success of a merger and discuss the issue from a business-management perspective.

## Speaker Profile

Liu Dawei teaches at East China Normal University and serves as deputy director of the Human-Resource Professional Committee of the Shanghai Society of Human Resources and Social Security, a council member of the Compensation Committee of the China Labour Society and vice president of the Shanghai Talent Service Industry Association. He previously taught at Liaoning University of Science and Technology and served as a professor at institutions including Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. He has also served as chief human-resources consultant to more than 80 multinational companies.

According to the notice, Liu has published more than 20 books and more than four million Chinese characters of articles, including over 20 papers in core journals. The profile also lists honors including “China's Famous Management Consultant,” “One of Shanghai's Top Ten Management Consultants,” “China's Most Influential Human-Resources Figure” and the American Human Resources Institute (ACI) “Elite Professor Award.” In 2008, he was invited by the U.S. Department of State to study international labor issues in the United States and deliver a special lecture.
