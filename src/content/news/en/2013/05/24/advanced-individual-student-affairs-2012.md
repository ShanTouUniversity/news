Following a review organized by the Guangdong Association for University Student Affairs, STU teacher **Zhuo Yanchun** was named a **2012 Advanced Individual in University Student Affairs in Guangdong**.

The provincial selection recognized university student-affairs workers who served as role models, remained committed to their work and contributed selflessly. It was intended to strengthen the university student-affairs workforce and encourage its initiative and creativity.
