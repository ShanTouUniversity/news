The term of the 14th Standing Committee of the Shantou University Student Societies Federation was nearing its end. The Federation planned to hold its **15th Representatives’ Assembly on June 5, 2013**, where it would elect the 15th Standing Committee. It invited students from across the university to apply as candidates.

## Positions and Eligibility

The 15th Standing Committee would have **nine members**: one chair, two vice chairs, five coordinators for the five major categories of societies, and one head of the Secretariat. Candidates were expected to have strong academic and personal records, be committed to student societies and campus-culture development, represent and safeguard societies’ interests, and help coordinate relations among the university, societies, other societies and the broader community. They also needed to understand the Federation’s development, have experience in student work, deliberation and social activities, work conscientiously, and be able to serve continuously for one academic year.

## Application and Election

Candidates had to complete the preliminary candidate-registration form for the 15th Standing Committee and submit a work plan of at least 300 Chinese characters for the position they sought. All materials had to be submitted through the method specified in the original notice by **11 p.m. on May 30**. The form requested names, student numbers, mobile numbers, email addresses, year and major, and personal experience, so the submission address, student contact number and personal application materials are not reproduced here.

The chair and vice-chair group would be elected as a three-person team, with at least one member required to have served on the 14th Student Societies Federation. Other Standing Committee positions would be contested individually. Candidates would give public speeches at the 15th Representatives’ Assembly, and the new Standing Committee would be elected through a representative vote by the assembly.
