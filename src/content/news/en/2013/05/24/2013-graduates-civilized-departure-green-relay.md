Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has written to the Class of 2013, calling on graduating students to support the campus community before departure, continue the university's tradition of responsible departure and help maintain a quiet study environment for students preparing for examinations.

## Departure Initiatives

The office encouraged graduates to take part in the “Green Relay · Public Bicycles” initiative by donating bicycles that remain usable. Students may also sort and donate used study supplies so that they can continue to serve students on campus. Graduates needing assistance may contact the Student Affairs Office residential-community counselor office in **Room B106 of the student residence area**, or email `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn`.

While packing and completing departure procedures, graduates were also asked to observe the residential-community schedule and avoid making unnecessary noise around students preparing for examinations. The initiative seeks to help graduates complete the final stage of university life in an orderly and considerate manner.
