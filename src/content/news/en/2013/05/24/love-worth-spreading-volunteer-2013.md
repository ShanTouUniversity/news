It is reported that the Graduate Volunteer Group of the Graduate School will hold the “Love Worth Spreading” Children’s Day teaching support activities on May 30–31, 2013.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Love Worth Spreading |
| Activity 1 | May 30, 12:30–18:30, Zhiying Middle School, Chaonan District, Shantou; 12 volunteers |
| Activity 2 | May 30 (full day) and May 31 morning, Zoutang Primary School, Fengmei Village, Jieyang (teaching on May 30, Children’s Day celebration on May 31 morning); about 10 volunteers for each session on May 30, no limit on May 31 |
| Registration | Send email with name, major, activity and contact details to designated addresses |
| Deadline | 24:00, May 28, 2013; first-come, first-served if oversubscribed |
| Organizer | Graduate School Graduate Volunteer Group |

Participants may bring small gifts. Email addresses are in the OA notice and are withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ Registration emails are available on the OA system.
>
