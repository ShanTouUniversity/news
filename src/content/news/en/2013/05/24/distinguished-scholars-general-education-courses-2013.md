As course selection for the summer and fall 2013 semesters approached, Shantou University's Institute of Higher Education and Academic Affairs Office invited three scholars to teach general-education courses for undergraduates across the university. The courses are scheduled for Weeks 1–3 of the summer semester and Weeks 13–15 of the fall semester.

## Course Schedule

| Course code | Course | Instructor | Semester and weeks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HED3003 | Philosophical Foundations of Scientific Methods | Xie Xialing, professor and doctoral supervisor at Fudan University | Summer semester, Weeks 1–3 |
| HED3002 | Topics in the Humanities | You Xilin, professor and doctoral supervisor at Shaanxi Normal University | Summer semester, Weeks 1–3 |
| HED3004 | History of Western Philosophy | Zhao Lin, professor and doctoral supervisor at Wuhan University | Fall semester, Weeks 13–15 |

The Academic Affairs Office reminded undergraduates to select courses through the credit-based academic system in accordance with their programs and study plans. Specific selection dates and processing rules are governed by the office's course-selection notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Institute of Higher Education and Academic Affairs Office)
