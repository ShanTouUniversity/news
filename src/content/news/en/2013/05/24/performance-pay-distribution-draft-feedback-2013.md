Shantou University circulated the *Draft Rules on the Distribution of Performance-Based Incentive Pay for Faculty and Staff*, dated May 24, 2013, for feedback from main-campus faculty and staff. Comments had to be submitted to the Labor Union’s second-floor office by **5 p.m. on May 27, 2013**, or emailed to `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.

## Distribution Framework

The draft was based on the Guangdong measures for performance pay at provincial public institutions and STU’s provisional faculty-and-staff performance-assessment measures. It sought to establish a more scientific, fair and motivating internal distribution mechanism. Annual performance-assessment results would be the primary basis, following the principle of rewarding stronger performance and distributing pay according to contribution.

The proposed recipients were all main-campus faculty and staff who took part in the annual performance assessment, excluding people whose contracts provided for a separate assessment bonus and people who left the university before the performance-based incentive pay was distributed.

The university would calculate a total incentive coefficient for each assessment unit according to assessment results and allocate it to that unit. Under the draft, each assessed employee counted as 1.00, each person rated “very outstanding” added 0.50, and each person rated “exceeding expectations” added 0.20. University leaders subject to a separate assessment and people who left before distribution were excluded from the unit calculation.

Unit heads would determine individual incentive coefficients, rounded to two decimal places, based on annual performance grades and relative performance among employees in the same grade:

| Performance grade | Individual coefficient |
| --- | --- |
| Unsatisfactory | A=0 |
| Needs improvement | 0≤A≤0.30 |
| Meets expectations | 0.50≤A≤1.10 |
| Exceeds expectations | 1.10＜A≤1.40 |
| Very outstanding | 1.40≤A≤1.60 |

Individual performance-based incentive pay would be calculated using the employee’s post allowance, individual incentive coefficient, the university-wide total incentive-pay pool and corresponding data for all participating employees. Coefficients for university leaders subject to assessment would be set by university leaders within the range for the relevant grade, while leaders’ incentive pay would follow the provincial performance-pay measures.

## Payment and Other Provisions

Incentive pay would be distributed once a year after the university completed its annual performance assessment. Payments would be based on the actual number of months an assessed employee was actively employed during the assessment period. Employees who retired during the assessment period would also be paid according to their actual months of service, with an individual coefficient of 1.10.

For teaching staff without the national salary structure, the draft provided for reference to provincially standardized post-allowance levels for corresponding professional and technical ranks. Personnel employed through agency arrangements would be handled according to the post-allowance standards in the relevant employment and compensation measures. Employees whose contracts provided for an annual or completion bonus, and employees of units operating under contracting arrangements, would receive the relevant bonus or incentive pay according to their contracts and annual assessment results.

The draft would take effect upon publication and would be interpreted by the office of the University Performance-Assessment Leading Group. This article summarizes the policy framework and coefficient ranges without disclosing any individual employee’s salary, assessment result or income record.
