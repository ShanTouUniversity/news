Shantou University's Engineering School has announced the track allocations for the 2011 cohort of its Civil Engineering Department. After the department's teaching committee approved the allocation plan, students submitted their preferences, and the department completed the assignment according to the allocation principles and track-selection criteria. The results were publicized in the classes without objections.

## Allocation Results

The published results list three named tracks, with **78 students** allocated in total:

| Track | Students allocated |
| --- | ---: |
| Building Engineering | 30 |
| Road and Bridge Engineering | 25 |
| Engineering Management | 23 |
| Total | 78 |

## Principles and Procedure

Beginning with the 2011 cohort, the Civil Engineering Department broadened its professional training tracks. Students had to rank all three tracks. Each track was to prioritize students who listed it as their first choice. If applications exceeded a track's preset limit, the plan provided for adjustment according to GPA values on transcripts from the first three semesters; students who were not placed in their first-choice track would be considered for their subsequent choices. Under the plan, students who submitted their forms late, left preferences incomplete or were not admitted to any listed choice were treated as accepting departmental adjustment.

The plan set a target of **26 students** and a maximum of **30 students** for each track, with stages for an orientation meeting, adviser consultations, preference submission, allocation and results publicity. The final results were publicized in the classes before this announcement.

One passage in the original plan says that students would be divided into “four professional tracks,” while the attachment lists three options and the published results cover the same three: Building Engineering, Road and Bridge Engineering and Engineering Management. This article follows the three tracks actually listed in the attachment and results and does not speculate about a missing fourth track.

The results attachment and preference form contain student names, student numbers, email addresses, telephone numbers, course grades, credits and GPA data. Student rosters and individual assignments are not reproduced here; readers should consult the original OA attachments for the complete list.
