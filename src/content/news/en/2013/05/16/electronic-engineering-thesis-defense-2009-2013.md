It is reported that the Department of Electronic Engineering of the College of Engineering issued a notice on May 16, 2013, regarding the graduation thesis defense for the 2009 cohort majoring in Electronic Information Engineering and Communication Engineering.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Group 2 | Jiang Yongquan | Lin Yiwen, Zhang Shaoling, Zhou Wen, Fan Lisheng |
| Group 3 | Yan Jingwen | Wang Jun, Sun Lisha, Li Xutao, Tang Yajuan |
| Group 4 | Cui Yan | Chen Li, Wei Chuliang, Cai Zemin |

## Procedures

The defense consists of two stages: a pre-defense review of the thesis content by the group, counted toward the score, and the oral defense where students present their main points, objectives and structure (5–10 minutes), answer faculty questions (5–10 minutes) and have their responses recorded. Students should prepare PowerPoint slides.

## Evaluation

Scores are given on a five-level scale based on the review, defense performance, supervisor recommendation and peer review. Outstanding theses are recommended by each group and finally determined by an outstanding thesis evaluation panel. Grading criteria are available in the OA attachment.

## Schedule

| Time | Venues |
| --- | --- |
| Morning of May 23, periods 3–4 | D406, D201, G401, G307 |
| Afternoon of May 23, periods 6–9 | E201, E202, E203, E204 |

After the defense, students should revise their theses as advised and submit the final bound copies to the department by May 28. Students may leave the campus only after final grades are confirmed.
