The College of Science at Shantou University will hold a science salon from **10 to 11:30 a.m. on Monday, May 20, 2013**, in Science and Technology Center Room 205. Professor Yan Xinghong, a doctoral supervisor at Shanghai Ocean University, will present “Research on the Genetics and Breeding of Porphyra haitanensis.” Faculty members and students interested in the topic were welcome to attend.

Porphyra haitanensis is a seaweed species native to China and widely cultivated along the coasts of Fujian, Zhejiang and Guangdong. It accounts for more than 75 percent of China’s total nori production. The presentation will introduce its life cycle, sex and sex-determination mechanisms, apomixis, meiosis and differentiation of thallus cells, as well as related genetic and breeding research.

Building on fundamental genetic studies, Professor Yan’s research group established breeding techniques for improved Porphyra haitanensis varieties and developed China’s first new Porphyra haitanensis variety, “Shenfu No. 1,” which has been promoted in production and contributed to higher yields and income. Yan serves on the National Aquatic Original and Improved Varieties Examination and Approval Committee and has received recognition including Shanghai Leading Talent and Shanghai Outstanding Academic Leader. He also receives a special government allowance from the State Council. His work has included major projects under the National High Technology Research and Development Program, National Natural Science Foundation projects and more than 20 provincial and ministerial projects. His awards include a second-prize National Science and Technology Progress Award and a first-prize Shanghai Science and Technology Progress Award.

Yan earned a doctorate in aquatic biology from Tokyo University of Fisheries, Japan, and studies seaweed genetics, breeding and biotechnology.
