To help faculty and students follow campus culture activities and plan their extracurricular time, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office opened the Campus Culture Activity Calendar. The calendar presents basic campus culture activities by semester and month, together with information about organizing units, activity formats, target groups and participation numbers.

The calendar is available at `http://sdc.stu.edu.cn/`. It can also be reached through the “Campus Culture Activity Calendar” link under “Friendly Links” on the Student Affairs Office homepage. Comments and suggestions can be sent to `o_sztz@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
