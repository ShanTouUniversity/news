The Biology Department of Shantou University’s College of Science announced two-round defense arrangements for its 2013 undergraduate thesis cohort. First-round defenses were scheduled in four groups on **May 24, 2013**, followed by a second round for candidates for outstanding theses. Student assignments and thesis titles were provided in the attachments and are not reproduced here.

Mei Zhiping chaired the defense committee. Its members included Chen Meizhen, Ding Lanping, Wei Chiju and Liu Yang, with Yang Mulan as secretary.

## Second Round and Assessment

Each first-round group would make a preliminary assessment and recommend two candidates for outstanding-thesis second-round defenses. The second-round panel would consist of defense committee members and thesis supervisors, using the same procedure as the first round. Each student would spend approximately 10 minutes presenting the thesis’s purpose, materials and methods, results and significance, followed by approximately five minutes of questions. A recorder would document the discussion. After both rounds, the department thesis defense committee would determine final results through deliberation, considering the supervisor’s and reviewer’s recommended grades.

The notice records the outstanding-thesis second-round defense as **May 28, 2012, at 2:30 p.m.**, identified as Tuesday of Week 14. This date conflicts with the 2013 graduating cohort and should be verified against a later College of Science notice or the original attachment. The venue given in the notice was the College of Science Lecture Hall (Science G352).

Theses were graded on a five-level scale with comments: excellent, good, average, pass and fail. Assessment considered completion of the assignment, completeness and depth of the work, scientific quality and originality, theoretical and practical value, independent views, the ability to analyze and solve practical problems, and clarity and accuracy in answering questions.
