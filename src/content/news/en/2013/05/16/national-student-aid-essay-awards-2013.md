The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education announced the results of the first national and Guangdong “Student Aid Helps Me Grow” themed essay competition. Shantou University received two recognitions: an essay titled “The Best Future” by School of Medicine student Liu XY won a third prize in the national university category, and STU received the Guangdong university award for outstanding organization.

The essay competition focused on student-aid policies and the outcomes of aid-based education. It was intended to publicize national and Guangdong student-aid policies, present how students from financially disadvantaged families pursued their development with aid, and support education on aspiration and gratitude. The winning works would be compiled by the organizers, with selected essays recommended for publication in education journals and promotion through news media.
