Following work arrangements issued by the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office will organize completion reviews for selected undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects approved in 2012 with a one-year implementation period. Completion will consist of written materials and a public defense. The specific projects were listed in Attachment 3.

## Materials

Project groups were to submit completion materials to the service desk of the Academic Affairs Office on the third floor of the New Administration Center by **June 3, 2013**. The package included the Guangdong Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Project Completion Acceptance Form, with the supervisor’s comments, and a work summary from the project group covering successful and unsuccessful experiences, lessons learned and reflections. Groups were also to provide supporting evidence such as survey reports, research reports, business plans, research logs, experiment records, patent applications, software, inventions, published papers and copies of publications.

Three paper copies were required, along with an identical electronic version sent to `yjguo@stu.edu.cn`. Electronic files were to follow the Academic Affairs Office’s filing convention based on the department, project number and project leader; the exact format was provided in the original attachment.

## Public Defense

Public defenses were tentatively scheduled for **June 9, 2013**. The specific time, venue and group assignments would be announced separately. An expert panel organized by the Academic Affairs Office would review the materials, hear a PowerPoint presentation from each group and ask questions. Each project was allocated 10–12 minutes, including approximately seven minutes for the presentation and five minutes for questions. All group members were required to arrive 30 minutes early. Groups were to bring supporting results such as physical models or software; photographs or video could be used when an item could not be brought to the room. Groups requiring special equipment or a particular venue were asked to contact the Academic Affairs Office in advance.

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Completion results | Excellent, good, pass or fail |
| Research-innovation credits | Projects rated excellent, good or pass would receive two credits, with results entered in the credit-based system |
| Extensions | A written request signed by the supervisor was required |
| Funding management | Projects poorly implemented, unjustifiably delayed without specific improvement measures or involving improper fund use could lose further funding and have part or all of their support recovered |
