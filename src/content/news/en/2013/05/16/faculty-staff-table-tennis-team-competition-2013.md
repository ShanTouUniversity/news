Because of weather conditions, Shantou University postponed its faculty and staff basketball competition originally planned for May until the next semester. The University Union will instead hold a faculty and staff table tennis team competition on **Monday, May 27, 2013**. Eight teams were registered: the College of Science, College of Liberal Arts, College of Engineering, Law and Business joint team, Arts/Social Sciences Continuing Education/Affiliated Middle School joint team, Administrative Offices, Research/Journalism/Library joint team and Resource Management.

Teams were to send their registration forms to the University Union at `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn` by **Wednesday, May 22**. Competitors had to be current STU faculty or staff members.

## Competition Rules

Matches would use a best-of-five format with 11 points per game, although all five events had to be played in every match. The order was men’s singles, women’s singles, men’s doubles, mixed doubles and an open singles match. Players could not enter more than one event. Teams from units with no more than 80 faculty or staff members could register up to 12 players; teams from larger units could register up to 15.

The competition would use a single round-robin format. A win was worth two points, a loss one point and a forfeit zero points. Rankings would be determined by points; ties would be resolved successively by head-to-head results, the number of match wins in a three-team round-robin tie, match differential, game differential and total point differential.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Competition date | May 27, games starting at 4:30 p.m. |
| Group-stage venues | University Union first-floor table tennis room and the affiliated primary school’s sixth-floor multifunctional hall |
| Semifinals and final | University Union first-floor table tennis room |
| Awards | Teams ranked first through fourth would receive awards; other teams would receive participation awards, and all competitors would receive souvenirs |

Competitors were asked to arrive early for warm-up, follow the officials’ decisions, wear sports clothing and shoes, and keep the venues clean. A detailed schedule would be issued separately.
