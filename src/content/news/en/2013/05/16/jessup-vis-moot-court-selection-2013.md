After a strong 2012–2013 season, Shantou University’s School of Law opened selection for its 2013–2014 Jessup International Law Moot Court and Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot teams. The selection was open to all undergraduate and graduate students in the School of Law.

## Team Size and Eligibility

The Jessup team would include five to eight competitors, including five official team members, plus four research assistants, primarily targeting first-year students. The Vis team would include 12 competitors. Applicants were expected to have a solid foundation in law, a strong interest in the competitions and the willingness to devote substantial extracurricular time. English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, legal reasoning and the ability to research English-language materials were also required.

## Selection Process

Written and oral components each accounted for 50 percent of the total score. For the written component, applicants were to write an English essay of approximately 400 words based on the relevant competition topic. Work had to be original and sources had to be cited. The oral component was an English interview assessing speaking ability, willingness to compete and basic legal knowledge. Interviews were tentatively planned for June, with the exact time to be announced separately.
