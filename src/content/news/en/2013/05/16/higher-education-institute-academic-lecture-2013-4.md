Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education will hold an academic lecture from **9 a.m. to noon on May 20** in its conference room, G201. The lecture was open to faculty members and students across the university and covered two studies on undergraduate talent development and the history of university educational ideas in China.

| Topic | Research project | Speaker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research on the ideas, practice and effects of integrated curricular and extracurricular talent development | “Research on Pathways for Developing Applied Undergraduates through Integrated Curricular and Extracurricular Learning” (Cai Yinghui Research Start-up Fund) | Researcher Cai Yinghui |
| Changes in and reflections on Chinese university educational ideas over a century | “Change, Inheritance and Integration: A Study of Chinese University Educational Ideas over a Century” (Kang Quanli National Fund Development Project) | Associate Researcher Kang Quanli |
