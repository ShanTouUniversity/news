Southern Weekend’s television project is recruiting interns through Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication. Since its launch, the project has produced documentaries including *Nobel Archives*, *Riding the Train with Lung Ying-tai* and *Seeing Japan through Japan*, and has completed program plans for *Say It Briefly* and *Shuzhai*. The programs were scheduled for gradual release on television stations and video websites.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Areas | Television editing, camera work and production |
| Applicants | Students who were not graduating in 2013, preferably third-year undergraduates or first-year graduate students |
| Major | Open to all majors |
| Location | Guangzhou |
| Duration | Preferably at least three months |
| Deadline | 10 p.m. on Friday, May 17, 2013 |
| Application email | `classicmdtsx@gmail.com` |

The project sought applicants who were proactive, dependable, hardworking, broad-minded, creative and quick to learn. Experience in professional camera work or proficiency with Final Cut Pro, Premiere or After Effects was preferred. English ability was also an advantage. Interns would receive a modest allowance, and outstanding interns could have an opportunity to join the project as employees.

Applicants were asked to send a résumé as required. Résumés and other personal application materials are not reproduced here.
