Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC), under the Student Affairs Office, is recruiting student liaisons for the 2013–2014 academic year. Twelve positions were available, with one in each discipline or college category, covering Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Computer Science and Civil Engineering in the College of Engineering; Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology and Physics in the College of Science; and the School of Journalism, School of Arts, the College of Liberal Arts’ language and public courses, and the College of Liberal Arts.

## Eligibility and Duties

Applicants were expected to be responsible, conscientious and capable of communicating and coordinating effectively, with established connections to faculty and students across their department or college. Liaisons would plan academic consultation services and activities at SLC for their area, recruit and assist with student tutors, and promote SLC services within their college. The position covered the 2013–2014 academic year and was paid under the university’s work-study arrangements.

## Application

Applicants were to log in to the Student Work-Study Application System at `http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/` and submit an application by **10 p.m. on Wednesday, May 22**. If more than five people applied for a category, five applicants would be shortlisted for interviews based on their applications. The interview list was expected to be announced on May 23.

The application form requested personal contact details and reasons for applying; those details are not reproduced here. SLC could be reached at `0754-86502250` (campus extension `882250`) or `slc@stu.edu.cn`.
