The School of Science at Shantou University will hold a science-salon lecture at **11:15 on Wednesday, June 5, 2013**, in the **School of Science Lecture Hall (Science-352)**. Associate Professor Xie Weiguang of the Department of Physics at Jinan University will speak on “Graphene Preparation and the Development of Field-Effect Devices.”

The talk will introduce mechanical-exfoliation methods for preparing graphene field-effect devices and discuss their interfacial electrical properties and the effect of interface doping on device performance. According to the notice, the reported research achieved graphene field-effect devices with mobility as high as **47,000 cm2/Vs** through interface control; it also states that high-mobility devices have potential applications in flexible displays.

## Speaker Profile

Dr. Xie Weiguang completed his doctoral studies in condensed-matter physics at Sun Yat-sen University in December 2007. From March 2008 to December 2010, he conducted postdoctoral research in the Solid-State Physics Laboratory of the Department of Electronic Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. He joined the Department of Physics at Jinan University in January 2011. The notice states that he currently leads or participates in one National Natural Science Foundation of China project in each capacity, has published 25 SCI papers, including nine as first or corresponding author in journals such as *Advanced Materials*, *Applied Physics Letter* and *Journal of Physical Chemistry C*, and has contributed to an international monograph on nanomaterials.
