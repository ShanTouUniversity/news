The Graduate School of Shantou University has opened the Graduate English course evaluation system for the second semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. Master's students enrolled in Graduate English must use their campus accounts to evaluate the courses taught by their instructors carefully and fairly.

The evaluation period is **June 3–7, 2013**. The evaluation system is available at <http://demo.stu.edu.cn/postsurvey/>.

Students with questions during the process may contact the graduate academic affairs coordinator in their school or college or the Graduate School.
