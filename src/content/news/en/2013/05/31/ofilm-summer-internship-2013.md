It is reported that the College of Engineering issued a notice on May 31, 2013, to recruit summer interns in cooperation with Shenzhen O-Film Technology Co., Ltd., to strengthen exchange between the college’s O-Film Innovation Club and the company.

The internship will take place during the summer vacation for one to two weeks at O-Film’s Nanchang branch. Students will participate in product innovation processes including project management, product design, user needs and experience, technology development, operation and services, to enhance professional and comprehensive capabilities.

## Positions and Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Positions | MCU programming (2–3), PCB layout and connection (1–2), coordination (1) |
| Eligibility | Students of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Classes of 2010 and 2011; members of the O-Film Innovation Club and those with outstanding project performance are preferred; no failed courses in 2012–2013 |
| Location | O-Film Technology Co., Ltd., Nanchang Branch |
| Deadline | Submit resume by June 7, 2013 |
| How to apply | Send resume with subject “Grade + Major + Name + Summer Internship” to the designated email address |
| Issued by | College of Engineering |

The designated email and contact details are available in the OA notice and are withheld here for privacy.
