The Student Community X Plan project team has completed the microfilm “Parallel Space at STU” after more than a year of preparation and filming. The project aims to portray the lives and spirit of STU students while showcasing student capabilities in directing, acting, photography and multimedia production.

The film follows students from different academic years and with different personalities. Through stories presented as real-life experiences, it depicts the transition from first-year uncertainty and second-year growth to third-year maturity and fourth-year development, recording everyday life at STU.

The film was released on the following platforms:

- STU Feiyang BT: <http://bt.789go.cn/details.php?id=89170&hit=1>
- Tudou: <http://www.tudou.com/programs/view/eRUjNq-DO0Y/>
- Tudou: <http://www.tudou.com/programs/view/NEgShgSTrJc/>

The notice directs readers to the Sina Weibo account “@叱咤社” for more information.
