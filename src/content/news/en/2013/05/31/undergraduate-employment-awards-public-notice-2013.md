It is reported that Shantou University's Student Affairs Office plans to provide employment awards to 2013 main-campus undergraduate graduates who took proactive steps toward employment, in accordance with STU Document No. 54 (2013). The related matter is now open for public comment. The workbook attached to the notice contains 319 student records, with personal fields including names, student numbers, majors, educational level and employment batch; the list and individual fields are not reproduced here. The award standard and payment details should be confirmed in the relevant document and the original OA notice.

Units or individuals with comments may submit them **by 17:00 on June 5, 2013**, in writing or in person, or by telephone to the Student Affairs Office Graduate Employment Guidance Center. Unit submissions must bear an official seal, while individual submissions must include the commenter's real name.
