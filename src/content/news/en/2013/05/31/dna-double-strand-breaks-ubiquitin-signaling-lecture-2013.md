The School of Science at Shantou University will hold a science-salon lecture at **10:00 a.m. on Monday, June 3, 2013**, in **Science and Technology Center 205**. Wu Jiaxue, a researcher and doctoral supervisor at the School of Life Sciences, Fudan University, will speak on “Regulators of Ubiquitin Signaling at DNA Double-Strand Breaks.” Faculty and students interested in the topic are welcome to attend.

## Speaker Profile

Wu Jiaxue pursued doctoral studies at Fudan University from September 2003 to July 2006, conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Michigan from February 2007 to December 2011, and has been a researcher at Fudan University's School of Life Sciences since January 2012.

The notice lists the following representative publications from the past five years:

1. Liu C, Wu J, Paudyal SC, You Z and Yu X (2013), *CHFR is important for the first wave of ubiquitination at DNA damage sites*, *Nucleic Acids Res.* 41(3):1698–1710.
2. Wu J, Liu C, Chen J and Yu X (2012), *RAP80 protein is important for genomic stability and is required for stabilizing BRCA1-A complex at DNA damage sites in vivo*, *J Biol Chem.* 287(27):22919–22926.
3. Wu J, Chen Y, Lu LY, Wu Y, Paulsen MT, Ljungman M, Ferguson DO and Yu X (2011), *Chfr and RNF8 synergistically regulate ATM activation*, *Nat Struct Mol Biol.* 18(7):761–768.
4. Wu J, Huen MS, Lu LY, Ye L, Dou Y, Ljungman M, Chen J and Yu X (2009), *Histone ubiquitination associates with BRCA1-dependent DNA damage response*, *Mol Cell Biol.* 29(3):849–860.
5. Zhang F, Ma J, Wu J, Ye L, Cai H, Xia B and Yu X (2009), *PALB2 links BRCA1 and BRCA2 in the DNA-damage response*, *Curr Biol.* 19(6):524–529.
6. Wu J, Prindle MJ, Dressler GR and Yu X (2009), *PTIP regulates 53BP1 and SMC1 at the DNA damage sites*, *J Biol Chem.* 284(27):18078–18084.
7. Zhang F, Wu J and Yu X (2009), *Integrator3, a partner of single-stranded DNA-binding protein 1, participates in the DNA damage response*, *J Biol Chem.* 284(44):30408–30415.
