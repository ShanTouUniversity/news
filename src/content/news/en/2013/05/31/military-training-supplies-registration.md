It is reported that, according to the university's work arrangement, Shantou University's 2013 student military training will be held within two weeks after the end of the spring semester, with specific dates and a detailed plan to be announced later. To prepare for the training, the People's Armed Forces Department has made unified arrangements for students to purchase training supplies.

All students participating in the 2013 training are required to refer to the size chart for training uniforms and shoes and complete online registration at http://gdc1.stu.edu.cn/junxun **by 6:00 p.m. on June 7**. To ensure smooth collection, students must accurately indicate their college during registration — students from residential colleges should select Zhi Cheng College, while transfer students should select the college they are transferring into.

Regarding costs, each set of training supplies — including a cap, camouflage uniform, belt, and shoes — is priced at 49 yuan. To ease students' financial burden, the university subsidizes half of the cost, leaving each student to pay only 24.5 yuan. Students purchasing the supplies must deposit sufficient funds into their campus card accounts **by June 10** so the fee can be collected in bulk.
