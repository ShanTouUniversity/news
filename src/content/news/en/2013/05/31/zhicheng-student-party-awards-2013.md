The CPC General Branch Committee of ZhiCheng College issued **Document No. 1 of 2013** on **May 31, 2013**, recognizing student Party members and Party-affairs workers who made outstanding contributions to college development and the “strive for excellence” initiative during the 2012–2013 academic year.

The notice said that the college's Party members had fulfilled their duties, completed tasks assigned by Party organizations and played an active role in study, work, daily life and Party or non-Party activities. Following recommendations from the student Party branches and deliberation by the college CPC General Branch Committee, the following honors were awarded:

| Honor | Number recognized |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Student Party Members | 14 |
| Outstanding Party-Affairs Workers | 2 |

The college CPC General Branch Committee called on all student Party members to learn from the recognized individuals, play an exemplary role, study diligently and contribute to the development of the college. The original notice lists students' names and major or class information; this article does not reproduce the individual list.
