Shantou University's Law Department will continue offering **law double-degree and minor programs** to undergraduates in the **fall semester of the 2013–2014 academic year**. The programs are intended to provide systematic cross-disciplinary legal study and broaden students' academic and career options. Students who complete the relevant requirements and pass the Academic Affairs Office's review may receive an STU minor-program certificate or double-degree certificate.

## Eligibility and Application

The program is open to students from STU's colleges and schools, except students in the Law Department itself. Applicants must meet all of the following conditions:

- a solid English foundation and completion of, or current enrollment in, ELC2;
- a GPA of at least 3.0 for completed courses;
- a ranking within the top 30% of the student's year; and
- no repeated courses.

Applicants must complete the **STU Application Form for Students Pursuing a Double Degree or Minor** and submit it to the Law School Academic Affairs Office in **Wenbei Room 202** by **June 10**. After approval, students must complete the course-registration form and register for courses.

## Program Requirements

| Program | Credit and completion requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Minor | At least 31 credits in required professional courses; no thesis required |
| Double degree | At least 47 credits, including 31 credits of required courses, 10 credits of professional electives and a six-credit thesis |
| Maximum completion period | The main program and the minor or double-degree program must be completed within six years in total |

Under STU's implementation rules, a double-degree program should generally belong to a different academic discipline category from the student's major. Students must give priority to their major studies. A minor or double-degree registration automatically becomes invalid if a student's course load is restricted because of academic performance or the student receives a probation warning.

### Professional Electives: Select 10 Credits

Students select a total of 10 credits from the following courses: Marriage, Family and Succession Law (LAW2105, 2 credits); Law of Obligations (LAW2106, 2); Property Law (LAW2207, 2); Private International Law (LAW2202, 3); Labor and Social Security Law (LAW2220, 3); Contract Law (LAW2206, 2); Legal Writing (LAW2213, 2); Evidence Law (LAW2214, 2); Intellectual Property Law (LAW3101, 3); International Economic Law (LAW3102, 3); Environmental Protection Law (LAW3103, 2); Company Law (LAW3108, 2); Tax Law (LAW3109, 2); Tort Law (LAW3111, 2); International Trade Law (LAW3114, 2); Securities Law (LAW3207, 2); Competition Law (LAW3208, 2); and Real Estate Law (LAW3302, 2).

Double-degree students must also complete **LAW4040 Thesis (6 credits)**. Under the implementation rules, a course already completed for the major may replace the same course in the minor or double-degree curriculum. With approval from the major program, credits earned in the minor or double-degree program may also count toward the major's public or cross-disciplinary electives. Registered students select courses according to the program schedule and may study with major students or in a separately organized class. A separate class will not be opened when fewer than 15 students enroll.

## Fees and Certificates

Minor and double-degree study is fee-based. Course fees are calculated as **course credits × RMB 100**; students pay the university's Finance Office after registering for the program and completing course selection each semester. The thesis is charged at the same rate. After completing the program and passing review, students may apply to the Academic Affairs Office for the relevant certificate.

The application form, registration form, transcripts and approval records contain personal identity and academic information and are not reproduced here. Consult the original OA attachments for the detailed submission requirements.
