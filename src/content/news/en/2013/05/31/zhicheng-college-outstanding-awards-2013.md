ZhiCheng College issued **Document No. 1 of 2013** on **May 31, 2013**, recognizing students who made outstanding contributions to student work and volunteer service during the 2012–2013 academic year.

The college said that its work had progressed over the previous year, with student organizations making contributions to team building and student self-management. The Student Union, Youth League general branches, Evergreen Service-Learning Association, *ZhiCheng* editorial board and grade societies conducted the selection, which was submitted to the college Youth League Committee for review before the college made its decision.

| Honor | Number recognized |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Student Cadres | 28 residents |
| Active Contributors to Student Work | 45 residents |
| Outstanding Volunteers | 6 students |

The notice encouraged the recognized students to value the honor, remain modest, continue their efforts and contribute to the development of the college. The original notice lists student names and major or class information; this article does not reproduce the individual list.
