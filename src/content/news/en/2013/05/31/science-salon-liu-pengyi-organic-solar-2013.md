It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on June 5, 2013, featuring Prof. Liu Pengyi of the Department of Physics, Jinan University.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Improvement of rubrene/C70 Organic Solar Cells via Electrode Modification |
| Speaker | Prof. Liu Pengyi, Jinan University |
| Time | 10:15, June 5, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |

The talk will cover the working principles and progress of organic solar cells and focus on how electrode modification improves rubrene/C70 device performance and degradation mechanisms. All interested faculty and students are welcome.
