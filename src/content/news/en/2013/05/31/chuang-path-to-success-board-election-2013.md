It is reported that, with approval from the Shantou University Youth League Committee, STU's **Chuàng Path to Success Network** will hold its **13th board election** from **19:00 to 21:00 on June 2, 2013**, in **Classroom E207 of the Teaching Building**. The meeting will review the 12th board's annual work and elect members of the next board.

## Positions and Election Process

The 13th board is planned to include one person in each of the following four positions:

- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- Chief Operating Officer (COO)
- Chief Administrative Officer (CAO)

Candidates are openly recruited from students across the university through voluntary application. The network will identify preliminary candidates under its relevant rules, conduct assessment and interviews through the 12th board, and submit the candidates to the Youth League Committee for review. Candidates will deliver public speeches, present their work plans and answer questions at the election. All members of the 12th board will vote, and the candidate receiving the most votes for each position will be elected.

## Application Arrangements

Applications close at **22:00 on Saturday, June 1, 2013**. Applicants must complete an application form and attach a personal work plan; the specific submission channel is provided in the original OA notice.

The original OA attachment contains personal application fields such as name, gender, birth date, political status, contact details, college and major, work history, interests and honors. The form and students' personal information are not reproduced here.
