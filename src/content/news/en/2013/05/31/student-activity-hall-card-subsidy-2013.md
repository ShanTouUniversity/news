After upgrading its environment and facilities, the Student Activity Hall at STU's Library will trial a **June 2013 campus-card spending subsidy** for all faculty members and students. The subsidy is calculated as a percentage of eligible spending and is subject to daily and monthly limits.

## Subsidy Rules

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible users | All faculty members and students' June campus-card spending at the Student Activity Hall |
| Subsidy rate | 50% of actual spending |
| Daily quota | The first 60 consumers each day |
| Daily per-person limit | RMB 5 |
| Monthly per-person limit | RMB 50 |
| Spending conditions | Register entry with a campus card and pay through the dedicated POS terminal; no time restriction |

The subsidy applies only to recreational activities at the Student Activity Hall. Private bookings, drinks and stationery are excluded. Any violation will result in cancellation of subsidy eligibility.

## Settlement and Hours

At the beginning of the fall semester, the Network Center will compile each person's Student Activity Hall spending, determine the subsidy amount and, after confirmation by the relevant offices, credit the subsidy to the corresponding campus-card account.

The Student Activity Hall's opening hours are:

- Monday to Friday: 3:00–10:00 p.m.
- Saturday and Sunday: 10:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m.
