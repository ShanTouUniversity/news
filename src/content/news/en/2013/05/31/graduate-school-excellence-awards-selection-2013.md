It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University has launched its 2012–2013 selection and recognition of outstanding graduate student leaders, outstanding Party affairs workers and outstanding student Party members. The selection is open to graduate students on the main campus. It aims to commend role models, encourage graduate student leaders, Party affairs workers and student Party members to serve actively and contribute to their communities, and strengthen the exemplary role of recognized students.

## Eligibility and Criteria

Candidates for outstanding graduate student leader and outstanding Party affairs worker include cadres of the university- and school-level graduate student unions, Graduate Youth League Committee members, class monitors, Youth League branch secretaries and committee members in training units, and graduate student Party branch secretaries and committee members. For class and Youth League branch cadres, recommendations are to be made at a ratio of 15 percent of the relevant cadre population. Candidates must have served as graduate student cadres for at least one academic year, comply with university rules, demonstrate sound academic and research performance, have no make-up or repeated courses during the assessment year, and not be under disciplinary sanction.

The outstanding student Party member selection is open to all enrolled graduate student Party members. Candidates must comply with university and Party rules, maintain a serious and successful approach to study, participate in academic, scientific, cultural, social-practice and volunteer activities, attend organizational activities, pay Party dues on time, and have no disciplinary record. In principle, each student Party branch may recommend no more than 6 percent of its Party members.

## Process and Honors

Graduate organizations, professional classes and Party and Youth League branches are to conduct democratic review and nomination. Candidates for outstanding graduate student leader and outstanding Party affairs worker complete the relevant application forms for review by their training unit or the Graduate School Party General Branch. Candidates for outstanding student Party member apply individually; their Party branch committee then conducts a review and public notice before submitting the recommendation to the Graduate School Party General Branch.

The outstanding graduate student leader award has first-, second- and third-prize levels, assessed mainly on service recipients, service scope and work performance. Recipients recognized as outstanding Party affairs workers or outstanding student Party members receive the corresponding title and a certificate.
