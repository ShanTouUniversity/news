It is reported that the Legal Aid Research Center of the Law School issued a notice on May 31, 2013, to recruit new members for juvenile background investigation and legal aid work.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct background investigations for minors as entrusted by the Longhu District People’s Court and present reports in court;
- Participate in daily duty, legal consultations and public welfare legal aid activities;
- Participate in legal aid work for employees organized by the Shantou Federation of Trade Unions.

## Application

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | June 6, 2013 |
| Eligibility | Law students of the classes of 2011 and 2012 |
| How to apply | Download and complete the application form, and send it to laclaw@stu.edu.cn with subject “Grade + Name + Investigator Application” |
| Inquiries | See OA attachment, withheld here for privacy |
| Issued by | Law School |
