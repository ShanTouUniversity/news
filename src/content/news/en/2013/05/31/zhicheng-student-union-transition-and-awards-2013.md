ZhiCheng College will hold its 2013 student union transition and outstanding student cadre awards meeting at **7:30 p.m. on Sunday, June 2, 2013**, in **Lecture Classroom E204**.

The meeting will include a work report by the fifth Student Union, an inaugural speech by the sixth Student Union president, and the introduction of the new Student Union leadership team. It will also recognize outstanding student cadres of ZhiCheng College for the 2012–2013 academic year.

Recognized student cadres and all Student Union cadres are required to attend. Student Union members and other residents of the college are welcome.

The notice was jointly issued by the ZhiCheng College Student Union, ZhiCheng College Youth League Committee and ZhiCheng College Office.
