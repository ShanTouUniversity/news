It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University issued a notice on May 9, 2013, to publicize the applicants for 2013 university teacher qualification recognition submitted for review, in accordance with the Guangdong Department of Education notice on teacher qualification recognition.

The publicity period runs from May 9 to May 16, 2013. The list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here to protect privacy.

Those with differing opinions are asked to submit written or email comments to the Personnel Office during the period, with real name and supporting facts and materials.
