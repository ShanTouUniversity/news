It is reported that the Center for International Studies (CIS) of the College of Liberal Arts will hold an Open House on Friday, May 10, from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. at Room E-204.

CIS faculty will present courses for the fall semester of 2013 and discuss them in detail with attendees. The Center will also introduce its Concentration in Cross-Cultural Studies for undergraduate students in the Chinese and English programs of the College of Liberal Arts, covering elective requirements and registration for the coming semester.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Event | CIS Open House |
| Date & Time | May 10, 2013, 7:00–9:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Room E-204 |
| Highlights | Fall 2013 course previews, faculty introductions, Cross-Cultural Studies concentration briefing |

Attendees are encouraged to share what kinds of general education courses they would like to see offered and to discuss their academic interests with faculty. Chocolate will be served, and all faculty and students are welcome.
