The STU Student Affairs Office has announced that, because of teaching arrangements, the classroom session for section 59589 of *Career Counseling and Training* scheduled for **periods 3 and 4 on Thursday of Week 11, May 9, 2013**, has been suspended and will be replaced by a company visit. The specific time of the visit will be announced separately.

Students enrolled in the section should inform one another and follow subsequent announcements from the Student Affairs Office and the course organizers.
