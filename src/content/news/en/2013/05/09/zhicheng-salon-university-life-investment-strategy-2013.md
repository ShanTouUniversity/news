STU Zhicheng College will hold **Zhicheng Salon 6** from **8:30 to 10 p.m. on Saturday, May 11, 2013**, in **Lecture Room J-354**. ### Speaker profile

Lin Daqing is a public-health scholar and tobacco-control expert who has been appointed as a consultant and expert by the World Health Organization on multiple occasions. He has published approximately 550 papers in international journals and has received recognition from the World Health Organization, the Chinese Association on Tobacco Control and Guangzhou for contributions to tobacco control in Hong Kong and China. His public roles have included vice chair of the Hong Kong Council on Smoking and Health, a member and honorary adviser of the Family Planning Association, and a member of the Hong Kong Medical Association. He has received the Bronze Bauhinia Star of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the title of honorary citizen of Guangzhou, among other honors.

Alongside his public-health work, Lin has been active in amateur theater since secondary school, taking part in writing, directing and performing numerous productions. His scripts have received the Youth Literature Award on multiple occasions. He founded the Lixing Drama Society in 1977 and is its artistic director. He has also chaired the Drama and Xiqu Committee of the Hong Kong Arts Development Council and published several original plays.
