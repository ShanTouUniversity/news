The **Shunza and Mom Silk Trio Concert** will be held at **7:30 p.m. on May 14, 2013**, in the **STU Grand Auditorium**. Pop singer Shunza will perform with pianist Eileen Huang and the Italian Silk Trio in a program combining popular, classical and crossover music. Admission is limited to ticket holders aged **12 and above**.

### Tickets and performance

Tickets will be distributed from **12:30 to 1:30 p.m. on Friday, May 10**, in **Room 309 of the Arts Education Center**. Distribution is first come, first served, while supplies last. Audience members should arrive 10 minutes before the performance; latecomers may be refused entry.

The Silk Trio consists of Italian violinist Francesco Solombrino, Italian cellist Pierluigi Marotta and pianist Eileen Huang. Shunza will provide vocals. The program includes performances by Shunza, Huang, the trio and various combinations of the performers, with arrangements for some works by Luigi Mogrovejo.

### Program

The attached program lists the following works:

- Jin Ping, *Three Chinese Folk Songs* (2001): *Little Creek*, *Guessing Riddles* and *Lullaby*;
- Huang Anlun, *Tajik Dance*;
- Smetana, *Piano Trio*, Op. 15, including the Moderato, Allegro ma non agitato and Presto movements;
- Shunza's original songs *Writing a Song*, *I Am Not a Star* and *Come Home*, performed with the trio;
- *Someone Who Gets Me*, performed by Shunza;
- *Send in the Clowns*, performed by Shunza and Eileen Huang;
- the second movement of Beethoven's *Pathétique Sonata*, performed by Shunza and Eileen Huang;
- *Tarantella*, in a Rossini–Liszt–Luigi Mogrovejo arrangement, performed by Eileen Huang and the trio;
- *Do You Know Where You Are Going To*;
- *Ave Maria* by S. Bach and C. Gounod; and
- other crossover works listed in the program, including the Chinese title “知音” for *Someone Who Gets Me*.

### Audience etiquette

The organizers ask audience members to dress neatly, with formal attire preferred. Food, smoking and objects likely to make noise are prohibited in the auditorium; mobile phones and other sound-emitting devices should be turned off after entry. Once the performance begins, audience members should not walk around or make unnecessary noise and should remain in their seats unless necessary. Anyone who leaves during the performance may not re-enter. Photography, video recording and audio recording are prohibited unless approved in advance by the organizers; violators may be asked to leave. All audience members must also observe STU rules for public venues.
