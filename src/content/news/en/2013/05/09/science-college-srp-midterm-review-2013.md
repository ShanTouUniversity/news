It is reported that the College of Science of Shantou University has completed the midterm review of its 2012 Student Research Program (SRP), with results recently announced.

## Review Results

Review panels from each department evaluated the 42 previously approved projects through presentations and discussions, determining additional funding based on progress, difficulty and financial needs. Following deliberation at the college level, 39 projects were granted continued additional funding totaling RMB 120,500. Detailed results are available in the attachment.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Total projects | 42 |
| Projects receiving additional funding | 39 |
| Total additional funding | RMB 120,500 |
| Review method | Defense and discussion |
| Review bodies | Departmental panels and college deliberation |

## Next Steps

The Leading Group for Extracurricular Academic Activities of the College of Science stated that departments will track project progress and a final defense is planned for late September 2013. The college urged project members to redouble their efforts and advisors to strengthen supervision to achieve stronger results in the later phase.
