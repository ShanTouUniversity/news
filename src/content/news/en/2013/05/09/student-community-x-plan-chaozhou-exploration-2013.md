The STU Student Community X Plan activity “Exploring: What Lies Behind the City” will organize student teams to visit Chaozhou and explore its cultural symbols and the stories behind them through field observation, interviews and documentation. Open to students across the university, the activity will accept team registrations from **May 8 to 12**. Selected teams will receive training, conduct fieldwork in mid-May and present and exhibit their results later in the month.

### Schedule

| Date | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| May 8–12 | Team registration, task selection and submission of action plans |
| May 14 | Selected teams announced; staff will provide further notice |
| May 15 | Training in safety and social research |
| May 18 or 19 | Teams travel to Chaozhou to complete fieldwork tasks |
| May 25 | Results presentation and selection of first, second and third prizes |
| May 26–31 | Campus exhibition of entries |

Each team will have **3–5 members**. After evaluating each team's task choices and route plan, the organizers will select **6–8 teams** for activity-funding support. Participating teams must attend the results presentation, submit results in the form of a PowerPoint presentation, video, electronic photo album, Word document or another format, and take part in an on-site presentation and defense. Teams may also select outstanding work for a campus photo exhibition at the Golden Triangle and poster boards below the student communities.

### Exploration tasks

The attached task sheet provides the following fixed tasks. Teams may choose among them and may design up to three additional tasks that fit the theme and do not duplicate the fixed tasks:

| No. | Task | Main requirement | Base points |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | A Glimpse of the Streetscape | Photograph a Chaozhou landscape with distinctive humanistic features | 10 |
| 2 | Chaozhou People in Chaozhou | Photograph a scene that best reflects characteristics of Chaozhou people | 10 |
| 3 | Distinctive Chaozhou Residences | Explore the architectural features and current condition of Chaoshan residences | 20 |
| 4 | “Chao”zhou | Explore how traditional and modern culture meet in Chaozhou, such as in new forms of Chao opera and popular songs in the Chaozhou dialect | 20 |
| 5 | Pausing in the Ancient City | Select a site such as Paifang Street, Guangji Bridge, the ancient city wall or the Han Yu Memorial Hall and examine its current condition and conservation | 30 |
| 6 | A Journey through Books | Compare Chaozhou libraries by collection size, collection types, design, services and problems, and propose improvements | 40 |
| 7 | “Fun” Is Not Perfect | Investigate the development of Chaozhou tourism, analyze its strengths and shortcomings, and propose practical solutions | 50 |
| 8 | The Rickshaw Industry | Investigate Chaozhou's rickshaw service industry and analyze its current condition and future direction | 50 |
| 9 | Traditional Colors and Commercial Appeal | Investigate the current and future development of Chaozhou's traditional products, including Chaozhou embroidery, wood carving, ceramics and opera costumes | 50 |
| 10–12 | Self-designed task | Each team may design one to three tasks that fit the theme and do not overlap with the fixed tasks | 20 each |

### Registration and evaluation

Teams must prepare an action plan for their selected tasks, describing the task choices, methods, expected results and a route plan linking the schedule, locations and tasks. The activity score will be calculated as follows:

> Total score = 30% × total points for completed tasks + 70% × judges' score for the activity results

The activity will award one first prize with a **500-yuan** prize, one second prize with a **300-yuan** prize and one third prize with a **200-yuan** prize. Teams must make safety arrangements for the field visit, attend the organizers' safety training and protect the safety of all members during the activity.

The registration form must be submitted through the channel specified in the original notice **by 11:59 p.m. on May 12**. It requests student names, majors and years, dormitories, campus email addresses, telephone numbers, training availability and action-plan details. These personal and planning fields are not reproduced here, and student registration email addresses and consultation telephone numbers are not published on this news page. Applicants should consult the original OA notice and attachments through the OA system.
