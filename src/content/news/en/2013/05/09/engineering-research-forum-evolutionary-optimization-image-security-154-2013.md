The 154th STU Engineering Research Forum will be held from **10 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. on May 16, 2013**, in **ACC Meeting Room 3**. Professors Yaochu Jin and Anthony T.S. Ho of the University of Surrey will present talks on evolutionary optimization, image steganography, watermarking and digital forensics.

### Talks

The first talk, **“A Systems Approach to Evolutionary Optimization of Complex Engineering Problems,”** will be presented by Yaochu Jin, Professor of Computational Intelligence at the University of Surrey. It will address challenges in complex engineering optimization, including representation, time-consuming multidisciplinary quality evaluation, changing environments, vague evaluation criteria and interactions among multiple subsystems. Jin will propose a systems approach to evolutionary design optimization across four levels: system properties, time, space and process. The talk will also outline future research toward life-like design based on principles found in biological systems, including self-organization, self-repair and scalability.

The second talk, **“Image Steganography, Watermarking and Forensic Techniques for secure communication and protection of image content,”** will be jointly presented by Jin and Anthony T.S. Ho. It will introduce data-hiding techniques for digital images, including image steganography for sending secret messages through an open channel, digital watermarking for content protection and integrity authentication, and image-forensic methods for identifying cameras from captured images and detecting image forgery.

### Speaker profiles

Yaochu Jin received bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees in automatic control from Zhejiang University in 1988, 1991 and 1996, respectively, and a Dr.-Ing. degree in computer science from Ruhr University Bochum, Germany, in 2001. He is Professor of Computational Intelligence in the Department of Computing at the University of Surrey and heads its Nature Inspired Computing and Engineering group. Before joining Surrey, he was a principal scientist and group leader at the Honda Research Institute Europe.

Jin's research covers computational approaches to understanding evolution, learning and development in biology, as well as bio-inspired approaches to complex-systems design. He has authored or edited books and conference proceedings and has published more than 150 peer-reviewed journal and conference papers. The attached profile also lists his roles with the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society, *BioSystems*, several IEEE journals and *Soft Computing*.

Anthony T.S. Ho is Professor of Multimedia Security and Head of the Department of Computing at the University of Surrey, where he leads the Multimedia Security and Forensics research group. He received the Institution of Engineering and Technology's 2006 Innovation in Engineering Award in the Security category for work on digital watermarking and its commercialization.

Ho received a BSc (Hons) in Physical Electronics from Northumbria University in 1979, an MSc in Applied Optics from Imperial College London in 1980 and a PhD in Digital Image Processing from King's College London in 1983. He then worked in technical management in the United Kingdom and Canada for 11 years and taught at Nanyang Technological University from 1994 to 2005. In 1998, he co-founded DataMark Technologies at NTU and became its chief executive; the company specialized in digital watermarking and steganography and later became a subsidiary of Singapore Technologies Engineering.

Ho has published more than 130 international journal and conference papers and holds eight international patents related to watermarking and steganography. He is editor-in-chief of Elsevier's *Information Security Technical Report* and is a fellow of the Institution of Engineering and Technology, the Institute of Physics and the British Computer Society, as well as a senior member of IEEE.
