STU's Snow Leopard Outdoor Association will hold a climbing-exchange activity in the Student Activity Hall during **weeks 12–15** of the semester and is recruiting participants from across the university. The program includes introductory climbing theory and indoor practice. It plans to admit **15 participants**; if applications exceed the quota, places will be determined by lottery.

Sessions will run from **5 to 6 p.m. every Wednesday and Saturday** during the activity period in the **Climbing Room, Student Activity Hall, first floor of the Library**. The participation fee is **10 yuan per person**.

### Activity content

The theory sessions will cover the development and classification of climbing, basic indoor-wall knowledge, warm-up exercises, climbing-hold categories and corresponding grips, and basic techniques for artificial-wall climbing. Indoor climbing practice will follow the theory exchange.

Participants should be interested in outdoor activities, prepared to work hard, follow instructions and observe the training schedule and venue rules. After joining the training, participants are expected to honor the opportunity and not withdraw without a valid reason. They must follow the coach's instructions and may not leave the training venue without authorization.
