The 153rd STU Engineering Research Forum will be held from **7:30 to 9 p.m. on May 9, 2013**, in **Science and Technology Building Meeting Room 413**. The topic is “EEG/ERP Analysis Techniques and Their Applications.” He Shaodong, a manager at Shenzhen Hanxiang Biomedical Electronics Co., Ltd., will give the presentation.

### Presentation focus

The talk will introduce the principles, technical characteristics and data-analysis methods of electroencephalography (EEG) and event-related potentials (ERP), as well as recent applications of EEG/ERP event-related-potential instruments.

Against the background of cognitive-neuroscience research, the presentation will discuss the non-invasive nature and high temporal resolution of event-related potentials and their combination with traditional behavioral measures to study cognitive processing in the brain. Related techniques have been applied in psychology, cognitive brain science, neuromedicine, artificial intelligence and ergonomics.
