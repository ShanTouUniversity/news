It is reported that the Resource Management Office and the Student Affairs Office issued a joint proposal on May 9, 2013, calling on students to participate in waste sorting in dormitories.

To meet ISO 14000 requirements and improve reduction, recycling and harmless treatment of domestic waste, the Logistics Office has equipped all student dormitories with sorted collection bins, and Huanrong Cleaning Company has assigned staff for sorted collection.

Promotional and educational activities on waste sorting will be organized in the dormitory area, and students are encouraged to take part.
