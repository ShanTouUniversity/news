STU's Physical Education Department is recruiting undergraduates from the main campus for its Football Club. The club is one of the department's undergraduate teaching-reform pilot projects, promoting football culture through training and exchange activities. Participation can count toward physical-education credits and allows students to take part in training, matches, refereeing and event organization.

The club plans to recruit approximately **30 members** on a first-come basis. If applications exceed the quota, applicants will take a football skills assessment and be selected on merit. Sessions are scheduled for **Thursday periods 8 and 9**.

### Registration information

The notice record is dated **May 9, 2013**, but its registration period in the body is still marked as 2012. The source does not explain the discrepancy, so this article preserves the dates rather than inferring or changing them to 2013.

The club provides a learning and exchange platform for football enthusiasts and uses sports training and teamwork to support physical and psychological development, cooperation and a striving spirit.
