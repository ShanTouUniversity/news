Shantou University has issued CPC Shantou University Committee Document No. 17 [2013], appointing **Wang Changzhi** as president of the **Second Affiliated Hospital of the STU Medical College** for a **six-month probationary period**.

The appointment was decided at the fifth 2013 meeting of the STU Party Committee Standing Committee. The document was issued on **May 9, 2013**, sent to the Education Working Committee of the CPC Guangdong Provincial Committee and copied to the Organization Department of the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee, the Shantou Municipal Human Resources Bureau and the Shantou Municipal Health Bureau.
