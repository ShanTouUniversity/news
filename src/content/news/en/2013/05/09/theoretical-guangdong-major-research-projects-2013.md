To build the “Theoretical Guangdong” initiative and advance philosophy and social-science research in Guangdong, the Publicity Department of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee has opened a 2013 call for major real-world research projects. STU's Office of Research has circulated the call to relevant university units and is collecting applications from eligible research groups.

### Funding and principal-investigator requirements

Each major project receives **500,000 yuan** in research funding, while each youth project receives **250,000 yuan**.

A principal investigator must be a Chinese national with an employment relationship or household registration in Guangdong, have strong political qualifications, academic expertise and research experience, and take actual responsibility for organizing and guiding the project. A major-project principal investigator must hold a full senior or higher professional title and have previously served as the first principal investigator of a provincial- or ministerial-level social-science fund project. A youth-project principal investigator must hold an associate-senior or higher professional title and be no older than 35 on the bid deadline.

Individual applications are not accepted. Applications must be submitted by a research group with approval from the principal investigator's unit. Each principal investigator may apply for only one project and may not join another bid as a group member. A subproject leader may participate in only one bid, while an ordinary group member may participate in no more than two. Principal investigators and members must also have relevant prior research results.

### Research requirements

Proposed titles should show a clear problem orientation and a limited research objective, avoid overly broad designs and emphasize practical application and theoretical guidance. Both major and youth projects should include several subprojects focused on key issues; each project should generally have no more than five subprojects.

Research should include field investigation, strategic thinking and forward-looking analysis. Expected results, especially conclusions and policy recommendations, should provide a scientific basis for important decisions by the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee and government. Major projects must finish their final results within two years and youth projects within one year. During the research period, both categories must submit a progress report and interim results at least once every six months.
