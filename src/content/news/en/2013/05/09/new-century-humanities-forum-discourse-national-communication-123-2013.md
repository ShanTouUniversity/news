The 123rd New Century Humanities Forum of the STU School of Liberal Arts will be held from **7 to 9 p.m. on Sunday, May 12, 2013**, in **ACC Meeting Room 3**. Chen Rudong, professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Communication at Peking University's School of Journalism and Communication, will speak on “Trends in Discourse Studies and the Transformation of National Communication Models.”

### Outline

The talk will address Chinese language, interdisciplinary interpretations of language, national communication models and cultural outreach through six topics:

1. The beauty of Chinese: my spiritual home;
2. Order and civilization: interpreting language from an interdisciplinary perspective;
3. National officialese: national communication models in the planned-economy era;
4. Transforming communication models: national choices in a global new-media environment;
5. Trends in discourse studies: constructing national rhetoric and national discourse capacity;
6. Cultural outreach: a national strategy for Chinese-language education.

Chen Rudong is a professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Communication at Peking University's School of Journalism and Communication. His public roles include president of the Global Rhetoric Association, president of the Global Alliance for Media Ethics and Law, honorary president of the World Chinese Rhetoric Association, council member of the International Society for the History of Rhetoric and a member of the Global Ethics Coalition of the Carnegie Council for Ethics in International Affairs.
