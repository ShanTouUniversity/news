| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Collection group | Designated employees listed in the notice |
| Collection time | Working hours |
| Collection location | Employee Medical Insurance Section, second floor of the University Hospital |
| Materials | Employee medical-insurance handbook and medical-insurance IC card |

The original notice names the individual recipients. Those names are not reproduced because they are linked to personal medical-insurance information.
