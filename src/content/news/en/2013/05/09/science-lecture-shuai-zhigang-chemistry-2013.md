It is reported that the School of Science will hold a Science Lecture on May 13, inviting Distinguished Professor Shuai Zhigang from Tsinghua University to deliver a keynote speech.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Prof. Shuai Zhigang (Distinguished Professor, Changjiang Scholars Program, Tsinghua University) |
| Title | Chemistry Driving the Progress of Civilization — Report at the Launch of the UN International Year of Chemistry |
| Time | May 13, 2013 (Monday) 19:30–21:00 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall, Science Building |
| Organizer | School of Science |
| Release date | May 9, 2013 |

## About the Lecture

The report was originally delivered by Prof. Shuai at the launch of the International Year of Chemistry held at UNESCO headquarters in Paris in January 2011. Taking China's long civilization as the main thread, it illustrates the important role of chemistry in China's 6,000-year development through advances in bronze smelting, ironmaking, alchemy, traditional Chinese medicine, ceramics, pigments and brewing.

The lecture also reviews the development of modern chemistry in the West, emphasizing that chemistry plays an irreplaceable role and bears great responsibility in addressing global challenges of the 21st century such as food, energy and the environment, as well as in sustainable development.
