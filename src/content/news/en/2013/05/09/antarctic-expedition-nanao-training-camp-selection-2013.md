Shantou University's Antarctic Expedition Project will hold its Nan'ao training-camp selection on **May 11–12, 2013**. The Student Affairs Office has instructed shortlisted students and teachers to assemble at the campus motto stele at **7:20 a.m. on May 11** before departure. The group is expected to return from Nan'ao at noon on May 12.

A pre-departure briefing is scheduled for **4:10 p.m. on May 9** in E Building Lecture Room 202. Questions may be sent to the project staff at `expedition@stu.edu.cn`.

### Equipment and preparation

Participants should bring a windproof and waterproof jacket, warm clothing, a headlamp or flashlight, one change of clothes, personal medication, sun protection, a watch, a ballpoint pen, a water bottle, sunglasses and personal items such as a toothbrush, towel and sandals. They should also prepare a simple breakfast and lunch for May 11. The organizers will provide tents, sleeping mats, dinner on May 11 and breakfast on May 12.

Participants must follow the instructions of coaches and staff and observe Leave No Trace principles, including reducing disposable items where possible. The campsite is beside a ring road, so participants must watch for traffic. A life jacket is required before entering the water. Staff will check luggage at the start; prohibited items such as lighters will be held temporarily. Phones and other communication devices must be handed to staff for safekeeping before the training begins.

Participants should bring waterproof protection if needed. Self-provided food may be used only for breakfast and lunch on May 11. Cash may be brought but cannot be used during the activity. Participants should also pay attention to rain, food hygiene, heat and the care of personal belongings.

The activity forms the Nan'ao training-camp stage of the Antarctic Expedition Project selection process. Shortlisted students and teachers should attend on time, follow the arrangements and avoid acting independently during the camp.
