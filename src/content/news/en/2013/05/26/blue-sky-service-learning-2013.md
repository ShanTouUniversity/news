It is reported that the Department of Public Administration, Law School, has announced arrangements for the service-learning course *Meet the Blue Sky* for the summer and fall semesters of 2013–2014, now open for reservations.

The course is open to Shantou University undergraduates, including those from the Medical College, who have not yet taken a service-learning course. Interested students may send their preferred topic, name, major and relevant skills to the course email address to reserve a place. Student IDs and personal contacts are withheld here for privacy and should be submitted via official channels.

Details are available in the OA notice.
