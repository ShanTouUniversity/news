Following requirements from the Shantou Municipal Committee Organization Department, Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Department asked Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to mobilize faculty and student Party members for the “Southern Guangdong Pioneers, Chinese Dream” essay call. The event was jointly organized by the Organization Department of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China and Nanfang Daily to encourage reflection on the Chinese Dream and Guangdong’s “three strategic positions and two leading achievements.”

## Topics and Requirements

Participants were encouraged to draw on their personal growth, work experience and reflections to discuss the meaning, value and significance of the Chinese Dream and the “three strategic positions and two leading achievements,” examine their dialectical relationship, and present Party members’ hopes, dreams, efforts and aspirations. Essays could combine narrative and discussion and were expected to be approximately 1,000 Chinese characters.

Selected essays would be published in Nanfang Daily and Guangdong Organization Work Newsletter. The organizers would also conduct an evaluation and issue certificates of honor to authors of outstanding submissions.
