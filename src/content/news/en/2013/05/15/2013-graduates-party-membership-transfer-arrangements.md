As the 2013 graduating class prepared to leave campus, Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Department issued arrangements for transferring Party membership, completing registration and handing over related records. Under the principle of territorial Party administration, graduating student Party members were expected to transfer their membership to the Party organization at their workplace or in their place of residence.

## Destination Registration

Graduates whose employers had established Party organizations were to transfer their membership to those organizations. Graduates without confirmed employment, including those with deferred employment, or whose employers had no Party organization could contact a Party organization at a talent exchange center holding their personnel records, the subdistrict Party working committee where they worked, the subdistrict or township Party committee where they or their parents lived, or their parents’ employer. Graduates were to confirm acceptance with the destination organization in advance.

Each college was to compile destination information, verify the receiving organization and the addressee of the introduction letter with each graduating Party member, and send the electronic materials to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` by **June 13**. One paper copy was also required for further review. Graduates were responsible for confirming the full name of the receiving grassroots Party organization and the correct addressee.

## Introduction Letters and Registration

Based on the destination register, designated college officials were to visit the Organization and United Front Department on **June 20 and 21** to process the Party Membership Introduction Letters according to the attached schedule. After paying Party dues through June 2013, graduates were to collect their letters in person from their college Party general branch. Colleges were required to keep signed collection records, and letters could not be collected by another person.

Party members were to report to the destination Party committee within the prescribed period. Those transferring outside Guangdong had to take the university’s letter to the Organization Department of the Shantou Municipal Committee for processing, at Building 8 inside the Municipal Committee compound, 8 Haibin Road, Shantou; telephone `0754-88276114`. Introduction letters were to be kept securely. Individuals were responsible for consequences arising from failing to report or holding a letter for an extended period without completing the transfer. If the receiving organization changed within three months after reporting, the individual could return to STU with the original letter and identification document to process the change.

## Record Materials

Materials for full Party members were to be placed directly in the student’s personal academic record by the college Party general branch. Materials for probationary Party members were to be organized and sealed by the college, signed for by the graduate and carried to the receiving organization. Materials for applicants who had not been accepted as probationary Party members were likewise to be organized and sealed for transfer to the student’s next organization. Colleges were required to maintain receipt records and provide training on membership transfers and Party-member responsibilities.
