It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, together with the Shantou University Student Club Federation, issued a document on May 15 to roll out a university-wide update of student club records. The move aims to strengthen management, improve services and promote the standardization and institutionalization of student clubs at the university, in accordance with the Regulations on the Administration of Student Clubs of Shantou University.

The initiative covers all college league committees and student clubs across the university and involves four main areas:

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Adviser records | Each club must submit the Personal Resume of Student Club Adviser form, with a signed paper version; clubs whose adviser position is vacant due to job transfers must re-appoint a university teacher as adviser within the month |
| Publication registration | Clubs that have founded publications must fill in the Club Publication Registration Form (electronic version) |
| Seal collection | Clubs that have carved a seal must submit an A4 sheet bearing the seal impression and the signature of the club president |
| College-level club filing | Colleges with college-level clubs must file the approval documents with the university league committee |

Electronic materials should be sent to the designated mailbox of the Student Club Federation (see the OA system for the exact address), while paper documents should be submitted to the Student Club Federation at Room 731, Building 789, from May 15 to May 31, 16:30–17:30 daily except Saturdays and Sundays.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
