The results of the 2013 National Junior Secondary Students’ Chemistry Literacy and Experimental Skills Competition, the 23rd Tianyuan Cup competition organized by the Chinese Society of Education, were announced in May 2013. Students from Shantou University Affiliated School received multiple honors.

Chen Z, Wang J and Xu Q won national first prizes, while Weng Y received a third prize in Shantou. Their teacher, **Huang Xiaoyan**, received the national Gardener Award from the Chinese Society of Education. Student names are abbreviated to protect their privacy.
