The Chinese Department of STU's College of Liberal Arts has announced the undergraduate thesis-defense arrangements for the 2009 cohort. The defenses will take place in groups from **2:30 to 6 p.m. on May 22, 2013**, covering modern and contemporary literature, classical literature, literary aesthetics and criticism, linguistics, foreign literature and comparative literature.

The surviving notice text does not provide complete names for the defense committee or defense groups, and the student roster is not reproduced. Missing committee information is not reconstructed. The database record dates the notice to **May 14, 2013**, while the notice is signed **May 15, 2013**; this article uses the signed date as its publication date.

### Defense procedure

Each respondent will begin with a self-introduction of about five minutes covering the basis for the topic, main arguments, problem awareness and logical structure of the thesis. Defense teachers will then ask questions in turn. Respondents will record the questions and leave the room to prepare; after the questioning stage, they will return in the order of their self-introductions to answer.

Each group will assess results based on the defense and recommend outstanding theses. The defense committee will make the final selection by considering thesis quality and defense performance. Recorders must accurately document the full process, including teachers' questions and respondents' answers.
