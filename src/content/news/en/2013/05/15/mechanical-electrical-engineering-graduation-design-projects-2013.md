Shantou University’s Engineering College Mechanical-Electrical Department issued a notice on May 15, 2013, concerning graduation design defenses for the 2009 cohort. The readable archived text lists **72 graduation design projects** by student-number entries and assigns supervisors. The projects cover mechanical design, automatic control, imaging, wind power, fluid heat transfer, micro-aircraft and mechatronic systems.

## Project Areas

The listed projects include food-packaging and cup-making equipment, servo motion and control systems, microcontroller and temperature-monitoring devices, imaging platforms, automated manipulators, wind turbines and grid-connected control, magnetofluid heat transfer, flapping-wing micro-aircraft and mechatronic systems. The work includes mechanical structures and equipment, control programs, simulation studies and experimental platforms.

| Overview | Details |

| Supervision | The notice assigns supervisors to individual projects, with some names affected by character corruption in the archive |

Although the notice title concerns graduation design defenses, the readable main text does not state a defense date, time, venue or procedure. Those details cannot be inferred from the available material. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce student names, student numbers or the mapping between individuals and projects, and it does not attempt to restore damaged names.
