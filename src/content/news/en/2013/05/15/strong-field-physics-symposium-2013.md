Shantou University’s Physics Department will host a Strong-Field Physics Symposium from **May 16 to 19, 2013**, in Conference Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center. Strong-field physics is a highly active area of contemporary physics. Initiated by Professor Chen Changjin, the symposium will bring 11 invited scholars from universities and research institutions in China, the United States and Japan to STU for academic presentations.

Professor Chen Changjin of STU will chair the opening, and Professor Li Shaohui, chair of the Physics Department, will deliver opening remarks. Presentations will address Fano resonances in strong laser fields, electron correlation in helium double ionization, molecular dissociation, Coulomb focusing, strong-field ionization, high-order harmonic generation and tunneling ionization.

The attached program is organized into Sessions 1–4 and includes chairs, talks, a group photograph, coffee breaks and lunch. The archived program lists two Session 3 presentations at the same time, 2:35–3:10 p.m.; readers should confirm the exact timing at the symposium or against the original attachment.

Faculty members and students were welcome to attend.
