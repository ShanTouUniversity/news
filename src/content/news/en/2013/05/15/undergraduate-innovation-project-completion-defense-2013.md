Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office will organize completion reviews for selected undergraduate innovation experiment projects approved in 2010 and 2011, following Guangdong’s project requirements and the university’s implementation measures. Completion will consist of written materials and a public defense.

## Eligible Projects and Materials

The review covered all projects approved in 2010 that had not yet been completed, as well as 2011 projects with an implementation period of one and a half years. The specific projects were listed in the attachments. Project groups were to submit their materials to the service desk of the Academic Affairs Office on the third floor of the New Administration Center by **June 3, 2013**.

The written package included the STU Undergraduate Innovation Experiment Project Completion Acceptance Form, the STU Undergraduate Innovation Experiment Project Supervisor Work Manual and a research summary from the project group. Groups were also to provide evidence of relevant results, such as survey reports, research reports, patent applications, software, inventions, published papers and copies of publications. Three paper copies were required, along with an identical electronic version sent to `yjguo@stu.edu.cn`.

## Public Defense

The public defenses were tentatively scheduled for **June 9, 2013**. The specific time, venue and group assignments would be announced separately. A university expert panel would review completion materials, hear a PowerPoint presentation from each group and ask questions. Each project was allocated 10–12 minutes, including approximately seven minutes for the presentation and five minutes for questions. All group members were required to arrive 30 minutes early. Project leaders were to prepare a summary and supporting materials; photographs or video could be used when physical items or software could not be brought to the room. Groups with special equipment or venue requirements were asked to contact the Academic Affairs Office in advance.

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Completion results | Excellent, good, pass or fail |
| Credits | Projects rated excellent, good or pass would receive two research-innovation credits, with results entered in the credit-based system |
| Extensions | A written request signed by the supervisor was required; projects already extended could not apply again |
| Project management | Projects poorly implemented, unjustifiably delayed without improvement measures or involving improper fund use could lose further funding and have part or all of their support recovered |
