It is reported that the Art Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, as part of the Colorful Arts Season Spring 2013, has invited Taiwan’s Shakespeare’s Sisters troupe to perform *Ai Ai* and to hold a theatre creativity workshop.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | *Flip the Table and Chairs: Let’s Love Together* theatre workshop |
| Guest | Shakespeare’s Sisters troupe, Taiwan |
| Time | 15:00–17:00, May 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Dance Rehearsal Hall, 1st floor, Art Education Center |
| Eligibility | All students, any major |
| Deadline | 09:00, May 22, 2013 |
| How to apply | Send name, gender, college, grade and mobile number to o_ysjyzx@stu.edu.cn; places are limited and allocated first-come, first-served, with interviews if needed |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center |

Details of the workshop are available in the OA attachments and are withheld here for privacy.
