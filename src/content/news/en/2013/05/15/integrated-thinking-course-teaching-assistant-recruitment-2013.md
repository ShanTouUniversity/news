Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts Integrated Thinking Project invited students to apply for several teaching-assistant positions in small-group tutorials. Introduced to all first-year undergraduates in fall 2011, the course combines one weekly large-group lecture with two weekly small-group tutorial hours. The format is designed to develop students’ use of conceptual tools, discussion skills and practical thinking habits.

## Eligibility

Applicants should have a strong interest in learning and teaching Integrated Thinking and should normally be at fourth-year level or above beginning in the fall 2013 semester. Particularly outstanding third-year students could also be considered. Applicants were expected to work well in teams and demonstrate communication, expression and reasoning skills. Preference would be given to students with strong academic performance, prior completion of the STU Integrated Thinking course or experience leading small-group seminars.

Selected assistants would be required to attend an Integrated Thinking training program, expected to take place in the first week of September 2013. Applicants passing the initial review would be invited to an interview separately.
