The Higher Education Institute at Shantou University will hold its third academic salon of 2013 at 7:30 p.m. on May 16 in Room G201. The event is open to all STU faculty members and students. Professor Ma Fengqi will provide commentary on two presentations.

| Topic | Speaker |
| --- | --- |
| “Exploring the Field of Study: The ‘Detached Tribe’ — Engagement and Coping Strategies among Humanities Graduate Students at Non-Research Universities” | Mao Jinde |
| “Education Lost — Beginning with the Fudan Graduate Student Poisoning Case” | Chen Bingyun |

The salon will examine humanities graduate students’ study and research engagement at non-research universities and broader questions about education.
