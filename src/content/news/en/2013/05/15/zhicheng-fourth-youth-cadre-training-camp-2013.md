To strengthen Youth League cadres’ overall capabilities, service awareness and innovation, Zhicheng College will hold its fourth Youth League Cadre Training Camp from **May 18 to 19, 2013**. The program is also intended to support the development of medium-sized teams within the residential college.

Newly elected secretaries and deputy secretaries of the sixth college Youth League general branches and Youth League branch secretaries were required to arrive at J-354 of Zhicheng College at 2:20 p.m. on Saturday, May 18, for the camp’s opening ceremony and classes. During the camp, male participants were required to wear formal attire, while female participants were asked to dress in a dignified and understated style.
