Shantou University’s Engineering College Civil Engineering Department announced the undergraduate thesis and design defense arrangements for the 2009 cohort. The archived notice lists **54 projects** for regular defenses and also establishes an outstanding group, whose participants were to be nominated by students and recommended by supervisors. Both sets of defenses were scheduled for May 24, 2013.

## Defense Times and Venues

| Type | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular Group 1 | May 24, 8:30 a.m.–noon | D301 |
| Regular Group 2 | May 24, 8:30 a.m.–noon | G302 |
| Regular Group 3 | May 24, 8:30 a.m.–noon | G401 |
| Regular Group 4 | May 24, 8:30 a.m.–noon | E309 |
| Outstanding group | May 24, 2:30–6 p.m. | E306 |

Each defense was allocated 15 minutes, including the transition between students. Students were to present their design work within eight minutes, followed by approximately seven minutes of questions from the committee. Before each defense, students had to complete and print the STU Undergraduate Thesis (Design) Defense Record and bring it to the venue. The committee would also assess students’ command of relevant basic theory, knowledge and skills, as well as their ability to analyze and solve problems.

Lin Peng chaired the defense committee. Its members were Liu Zhe, Hong Chengyu, Huang Diyu, Yang Yunping, Gu Jiuren, Xie Xinlu, Xiong Guangjing, Zhao Xiaohua, Li Gengying, Chen Liqiang, Yang Yanchun, Liu Jinwei, Yu Ying, Lu Xiaohua, Xu Pingsheng, Zeng Lijuan, Wang Qinhua, Wu Ming, Liu Jilu, Wang Yingzi, Zhuang Lihong and Zhou Qi. All committee members were to attend the outstanding-group defense, with Huang Diyu serving as secretary.

The regular groups covered road and bridge design, residential architecture and structural design, steel structures, transport facilities, geotechnical and structural engineering, and campus and public-building planning. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce student names, student numbers or the mapping between individuals and projects.
