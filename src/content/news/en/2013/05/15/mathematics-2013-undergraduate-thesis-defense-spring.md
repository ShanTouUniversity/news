Shantou University’s Mathematics Department in the College of Science announced spring defense arrangements for the 2013 undergraduate thesis and design cohort. The defenses were scheduled for **May 24, 2013, from 2:30 to 6 p.m.**, in four groups. Student assignments and thesis titles were provided in the attachments.

| Group 2 | G403 | Ye Ruisong (chair), Li Pengtao and Tan Chaoqiang |
| Group 3 | G404 | Lin Xiaoping (chair), Wei Caimin, Yang Zhongqiang and Li Junde |

Ye Ruisong chaired the defense committee. Its members were Xu Fei, Yu Chengjie, Luo Jun, Li Pengtao, Tan Chaoqiang, Lin Xiaoping, Wei Caimin, Yang Zhongqiang, Li Junde, Lin Furong, Li Jian, Gu Minqiang and Christian Rau.

## Defense and Assessment

Group chairs would announce the order and procedures. Students were to prepare a PowerPoint presentation and spend approximately five minutes introducing the thesis’s main arguments, purpose, structure and features, followed by questions from the faculty. A recorder would document the questions and answers. After the defenses, each group would make a preliminary assessment under the thesis-design scoring standards. The department thesis defense committee and participating faculty would then determine final results based on the supervisor’s and reviewer’s recommended grades and the group’s preliminary assessment.

Assessment considered completion of the assignment, the completeness and depth of the work, its scientific quality and originality, theoretical and practical value, independent views, ability to apply knowledge to solve practical problems, and clarity and accuracy in answering questions. Theses were graded on a five-level scale with comments: excellent required strong completion, independent work and some original insight; good required broad completion and generally correct work; average required basic completion without fundamental errors; pass indicated marginal completion with weak independent ability; and fail covered incomplete work, numerous fundamental errors or inability to answer major questions.
