Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office launched a Student Community X Plan activity exploring the history behind Chaoshan kungfu tea. Under the theme “Tea’s Charm,” the event invited students from across the university to use interviews, documentary research and photography to examine the origins and development of the tea tradition and its influence on life in Chaoshan.

## Schedule

| Date | Stage | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 15–18 | Team registration | Form teams of three to four students and submit team information |
| May 19 | Briefing | Introduce the activity content and tasks |
| May 20–25 | Activity period | Gather materials and prepare a presentation |
| May 26 | Judging and showcase | Evaluate entries and present the results |

One first prize, one second prize and one third prize would be awarded, with prizes of RMB 400, RMB 200 and RMB 100 respectively. Other participating teams would receive souvenirs. Presentations could use PowerPoint, electronic albums, photographs, Word documents or a combination of formats.

Students interested in participating were to register at `12lzhan@stu.edu.cn` by **11:59 p.m. on May 18** as required by the activity. Team-member names and campus contact numbers in the registration materials are personal information and are not reproduced here.
