Shantou University began revising undergraduate curriculum plans in 2013 to align programs with their updated educational objectives and standards. The university planned to compile the plans and course descriptions into an Academic Guide for the 2013 cohort as part of its advanced undergraduate education initiative.

## Revision Priorities

Beginning with the 2013 cohort, every enrolling program was required to provide curriculum plans for double-degree and minor programs. To standardize course coding, the university would recode existing courses. Program curriculum plans and course descriptions therefore had to list course information under the new codes and include old-to-new course-code mappings.

Each program was to submit one copy each of its 2013 curriculum plan, double-degree and minor curriculum plans, course descriptions, old-to-new course-code mapping and faculty profiles. Paper copies required signatures from the department, center or institute director and the chair of the college teaching committee. Departments, centers and institutes only needed to submit course descriptions, code mappings and faculty profiles. From 2013 onward, faculty profiles would be entered directly into the credit-based academic system rather than printed in the Academic Guide.
