It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on May 15, 2013, regarding the distribution of the 2012 Postgraduate National Scholarship.

The award certificates have been collected from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. Graduate training units are asked to dispatch representatives to collect them from the Graduate School. The list of recipients has been submitted to the Finance Office, and grants will soon be transferred to the winners’ campus Bank of China accounts for collection.

For inquiries, contact Ms. Huang Meiping of the Graduate School (mphuang@stu.edu.cn) or Mr. Zhang Shaowei of the Finance Office (swzhang@stu.edu.cn).
