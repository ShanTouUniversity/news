To mark its tenth anniversary, Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host a lecture series on May 20 and 21, 2013, in the New Library Lecture Hall. The school invited Wang Xiangjiang and Li Hao, reporters from Qinghai’s Xihai Metropolis Daily, to discuss the “Qinghai Journey: Retracing China’s Northwest” project and its significance.

| Date and time | Speaker | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 20, 7:30–9:30 p.m. | Wang Xiangjiang | “In That Distant Place: The Last Pure Land of Humanity” |
| May 21, 7:30–9 p.m. | Li Hao | “The Significance of Carrying Forward the Spirit of Fan Changjiang in the Internet Age” |

The “Qinghai Journey: Retracing China’s Northwest” has been an ongoing project of the school for several years. The anniversary program was intended to revisit the project’s decade-long path and further explore its contemporary significance.
