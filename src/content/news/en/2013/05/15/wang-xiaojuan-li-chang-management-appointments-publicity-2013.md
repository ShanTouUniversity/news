Shantou University’s Human Resources Office issued a pre-appointment notice on May 15, 2013. Following discussion at the sixth university leadership meeting on May 14, the university proposed appointing **Wang Xiaojuan** and **Li Chang** to middle-level management posts. The notice cited the university’s Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres (STU Party document No. 47, 2009).

Organizations and individuals with comments on the proposed appointments could submit written feedback to the Human Resources Office by **May 22**. Organizational submissions had to carry an official seal, while individuals were required to sign their real names.
