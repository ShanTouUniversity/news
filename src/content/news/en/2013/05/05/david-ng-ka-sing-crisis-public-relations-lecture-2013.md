At the invitation of STU, Dr. David Ng Ka-sing, former head of the Hong Kong Police Public Relations Branch and a police force spokesperson, will give a public lecture titled **“Crisis and PR Management in Government”** from **4:00 to 5:30 p.m. on May 7** in the Library Lecture Hall. Professor Michael Palmer will moderate the session, which is open to the university community.

### Speaker background

Dr. Ng joined the Hong Kong Police in 1977. He later served as head of the Hong Kong Police Public Relations Branch and as a police force spokesperson responsible for crisis management. He also served as head of Interpol Hong Kong, working on international cooperation and liaison concerning cross-border crime. According to the English CV issued with the lecture notice, he worked in the Hong Kong Police Force until October 2011, leaving as Assistant Commissioner (Crime), after commanding or specializing in areas including organized crime and triad, commercial crime, narcotics and criminal intelligence.

He received the Police Meritorious Service Medal in 2005 and the Police Distinguished Service Medal in 2011. He has also taught or served as a guest lecturer at institutions including The Chinese University of Hong Kong and Hong Kong Open University. His listed academic background includes a sociology bachelor's degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, a master's degree in criminology from the University of Hong Kong and a Ph.D. in sociology from The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

### Lecture focus

Drawing on practical cases, Dr. Ng will discuss crisis-management strategies, communication methods, operating mechanisms and key response principles. The lecture will also address how organizations can improve media-response management and strengthen crisis prediction, prevention and recovery.
