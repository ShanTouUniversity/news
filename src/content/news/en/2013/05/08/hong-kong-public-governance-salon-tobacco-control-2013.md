The fourth session of the Law School's **Hong Kong Public Governance Experience Salon** will be held from **4:00 to 6:00 p.m. on Sunday, May 12** in the Science Report Hall. Titled **“Does This Also Need to Be Governed? A Tobacco-Control Story,”** the salon will feature Professor Lam Tai-hing, dean of the School of Public Health at the University of Hong Kong. Chan Wong Sui, an honorary fellow of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, will join the discussion, moderated by Associate Professor Cai Zhengzhong of STU Law School.

### Discussion themes

Using Hong Kong's tobacco-control experience since 1982 as a case study, the salon will examine the relationship among individual freedom, government regulation, commercial interests, public health and international factors. The discussion will address:

- What governments should regulate and when legislation is needed;
- How to balance individual freedom and public intervention;
- How to advocate for and influence government policy;
- How to evaluate different views, interests and social forces;
- The division of labor, cooperation and checks and balances between government and civil society;
- Strategies for building media and public support;
- How legislation is advanced and its effectiveness evaluated;
- How policy costs and benefits are calculated;
- Responses to powerful commercial resources and lobbying;
- Choosing effective methods of public education, publicity and warnings.

### Guest profiles

Lam Tai-hing is dean of the School of Public Health at the University of Hong Kong and the 罗旭龢 Foundation Professor in Public Health. He has worked in social medicine and tobacco-control research, served repeatedly as a consultant and expert for the World Health Organization, and published approximately 550 papers in international journals. He has also served as vice chairman of Hong Kong's Council on Smoking and Health and received recognition from the World Health Organization, the Chinese Association on Tobacco Control and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, among others.

Chan Wong Sui is an honorary fellow of The Chinese University of Hong Kong. She previously served as director-general of the Consumer Council of Hong Kong and president of Consumers International and held positions with bodies including the University Grants Committee, Law Reform Commission, Hospital Authority and Housing Authority. At the time of the notice, she was chair of the Hong Kong Deposit Protection Board, deputy chair of the council of Hong Kong Baptist University and honorary vice president of the Community Chest.
