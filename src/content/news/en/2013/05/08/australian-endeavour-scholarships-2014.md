The Australian Embassy in Beijing has circulated information on the 2014 **Australian Government Endeavour Scholarships and Fellowships**. The program is part of the Australia Awards and supports people from the Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Europe and the Americas to study, conduct research and pursue professional development in Australia. It also supports Australians undertaking similar activities overseas.

Applications for the 2014 awards opened on **April 6, 2013** and close at **11:59 a.m. on June 30, 2013, Australian local time**. The awards are administered by the Australian Department of Industry, Innovation, Climate Change, Science, Research and Tertiary Education (DIICCSRTE).

### Awards relevant to Chinese applicants

| Award | Maximum value | Maximum duration | Main purpose or study level |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Endeavour Postgraduate Scholarship (incoming only) | Up to A$228,500 for PhD students; A$118,500 for master's students | Up to 2 years for a master's degree; 4 years for a doctorate | Study or research toward an Australian master's or doctoral degree |
| Endeavour Research Fellowship, including the Endeavour Australia Cheung Kong Research Fellowship | A$23,500 | 4–6 months | Research toward a master's or doctoral degree in the applicant's home country, or postdoctoral research |
| Endeavour Vocational Education and Training (VET) Scholarship (incoming only) | A$119,500 | 1–2.5 years | Diploma, advanced diploma or associate-degree study |
| Endeavour Executive Fellowship | A$18,500 | 1–4 months | Professional development training |
| Prime Minister's Australia Asia Incoming Postgraduate Scholarship (incoming only) | A$263,500 | Up to 4 years, plus an optional internship of no more than 1 year | Research-based doctoral study |

The 20 strongest doctoral applicants for the incoming Endeavour Postgraduate Scholarship from participating Asian countries and areas, including China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, will receive the Prime Minister's Australia Asia scholarship. No separate application is required for that award.

### Basic conditions for Chinese applicants

The overview lists these primary conditions: applicants must be at least 18 years old, be Chinese citizens or permanent residents currently living in China, not hold an Australian student visa, and not have received an Australian Endeavour or AusAID Scholarship during the two years before the 2014 award. Awardees must travel to Australia within the required period in 2014. Each award also has additional specific requirements.

Interested applicants should visit the [official Endeavour Scholarships website](http://www.innovation.gov.au/internationaleducation/endeavour/Pages/Endeavour.aspx), download the application guide and apply online. STU's Office of International Exchange and Cooperation circulated the information for faculty and student reference.
