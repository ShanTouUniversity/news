STU Business School has issued Document Shanda Business [2013] No. 09, announcing the concurrent personnel assignments and division of responsibilities for its Alumni Office. The arrangement is intended to strengthen the school's alumni work and expand its areas of service.

The document appoints Cui Weiwei as concurrent director of the Business School Alumni Office and assigns her responsibility for undergraduate alumni affairs. Another concurrent staff member is responsible for graduate alumni affairs, while Zhang Xiaoxiao is responsible for MBA and course-cohort alumni affairs.

| Area | Person or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Alumni Office director and undergraduate alumni affairs | Cui Weiwei |
| Graduate alumni affairs | Assigned to a concurrent staff member; the name is damaged in the OA text and cannot be reliably identified |
| MBA and course-cohort alumni affairs | Zhang Xiaoxiao |

The document is dated **May 8, 2013**, and was sent to the STU Alumni Affairs Office.
