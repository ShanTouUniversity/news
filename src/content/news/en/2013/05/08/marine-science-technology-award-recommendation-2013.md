STU's Office of Research has circulated the call for recommendations for the 2013 **Marine Science and Technology Award**, inviting relevant university units and faculty to consider submitting outstanding marine science and technology achievements. The award was established jointly by the China Society of Oceanography, China Pacific Society and China Society of Oceanology and Limnology after the former State Oceanic Administration Marine Innovation Achievement Award was converted into the new award.

### Award scope

The award recognizes major achievements and technology-transfer results in:

- Marine environmental protection;
- Integrated marine management;
- Marine public-interest services;
- Marine security, safety and rights protection;
- Marine resource development and use;
- Sustainable development of the marine economy and society.

### Deadlines and contact

The Guangdong Provincial Ocean and Fisheries Bureau will accept recommendation materials until **May 20**. STU faculty interested in applying should contact the Office of Research by **May 10** so that the university can begin the recommendation process.
