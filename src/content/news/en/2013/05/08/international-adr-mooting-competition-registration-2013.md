The Career Development Center of STU Law School has opened registration to select outstanding students for the **2013 International ADR Mooting Competition**, scheduled for **July 28 to August 3** at City University of Hong Kong. Applications are due by **May 14**, and the interview time and venue will be announced separately.

### Selection criteria

Because the competition requires knowledge of alternative dispute resolution and is conducted in English, selection will focus on:

- A sound academic foundation in alternative dispute resolution;
- Strong written and spoken English.

### Application

Interested students must complete the application form in the OA attachment and email it to `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` by May 14. The notice asks applicants to use the format “year + name + ADR competition” for the email subject and attachment filename. The Career Development Center will arrange interviews based on the number of applications.

| --- | --- |
| Competition | 2013 International ADR Mooting Competition |

| Reference | [City University of Hong Kong ADR Moot](http://www.cityu.edu.hk/slw/ADR_Moot/) |

The application form contains fields for names, contact details, grades and personal experience. This article does not reproduce students' personal information or application materials.
