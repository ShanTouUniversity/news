STU's Network and Information Center has notified university units that each unit will be responsible for mobile fixed-line telephone charges incurred from April 2013 onward. The related electronic bills will be imported into the Telephone Management System each month for designated unit reviewers to inspect.

The university introduced a new telephone system in October 2012 and leased trunk lines from China Mobile's Shantou branch. China Mobile Shantou sends the electronic bills to the Network and Information Center, which imports the relevant data into the system monthly.

### Verification process

Unit reviewers should log in to the [Telephone Management System](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn), select “移动固话” (mobile fixed lines) and review the itemized bills. The procedure is the same as the earlier verification process for China Telecom fixed-line bills. The specific verification period will be announced through the office automation system.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Charges from | April 2013 onward |
| Telephone type | Mobile fixed lines |
| Bill provider | China Mobile Shantou branch |
| Verification system | [Telephone Management System](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) |
| System section | “移动固话” (mobile fixed lines) |
| Reviewers | Designated reviewers in each unit |
| Specific timing | As announced through the office automation system |

This article describes the unit-level verification process only and does not reproduce telephone numbers, call records or itemized charges from the bills.
