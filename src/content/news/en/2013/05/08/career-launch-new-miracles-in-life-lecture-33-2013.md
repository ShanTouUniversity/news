STU's Student Affairs Office will hold Career Launch Forum No. 33 from **7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on May 11, 2013**, under the theme “Toward a New Miracle in Life.” The event will take place in **E Lecture Room 202** and is open to all faculty and students.

The session will focus on preparing job résumés, online applications and self-expression, offering practical guidance on preparing for job searches. The speaker will be Zheng Yunfei.

### About the speaker

Zheng Yunfei is a senior corporate trainer, senior financial planner, senior professional manager and AACTP international certified training manager. He currently serves as a specially appointed learning consultant for Yuexiu Group and chief training instructor for Guangzhou Futures.

His previous positions include department manager at the Wuhan office of Industrial Securities, Hubei regional manager for Wyeth China, director of the Hunan Professional Manager and Financial Planner Training Center, and general manager and investment director of Shenzhen Mingrui Investment Consulting.

He has provided training for organizations including Pacific Life China, China Construction Bank, China Merchants Bank, Industrial Securities, Fortune Securities, Meier Ya Futures, Ruida Futures, ICBC Hunan Finance College, Wuhan Iron and Steel Group, Hunan Shenxiang Group, Guangdong Mobile and Guangzhou Telecom.
