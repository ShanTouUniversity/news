STU's School of Science has opened applications for its 2013 extracurricular academic and science-and-technology research projects, with applications open to all enrolled undergraduates in the school. Projects must be completed by students during extracurricular time and must not be part of the teaching plan. Coursework, graduation theses, graduation projects and annual papers are not eligible.

### Project categories

Projects generally fall into five categories:

- Social-science survey reports and academic papers;
- Academic papers in science, engineering, agriculture and medicine;
- Inventions and technical creations;
- Literary and artistic works;
- Entrepreneurship plans.

The School of Science has also provided a list of suggested research topics. Students may develop their own topic and find a supervisor or select a topic from the list. The suggested topics cover areas including physics, chemistry and biology; consult the OA attachment for titles and requirements.

### Rules and schedule

Each project must have a clear division of responsibilities. A student may apply for or join only one project, and cross-project applications are not allowed. To maintain supervision quality, each supervisor should generally oversee no more than two projects. The school will arrange a separate review by a project evaluation panel.
