The STU Changjiang College of Arts and Design will host a design dialogue for **Kan Kan Culture 14** from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, May 10, 2013**, in the **STU Science Lecture Hall**. The event is titled **“Where Is Inspiration?”**, with the subtitle *How I Play with Design*. Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Changjiang College of Arts and Design, will speak with Chinese graphic designer Chen Shaohua. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

### Participants

Wang Shouzhi is dean and professor of the STU Changjiang College of Arts and Design. His research covers the history and theory of architecture, industrial products, graphic design, fashion, automobiles, urban planning, illustration, modern and contemporary art, and entertainment. He graduated from Wuhan University's graduate school and served as deputy head of the Department of Design at Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts in the 1980s. In 1987, he conducted research and teaching in the United States as a Fulbright scholar. From 1988, he taught design theory at ArtCenter College of Design in Pasadena, becoming a tenured full professor in 1993 and taking responsibility for modern design theory and modern design history. He also taught design history at the California Institute of the Arts, Otis College of Art and Design, the Southern California Institute of Architecture and the University of Southern California. He has served on Tsinghua University's Academic Development Committee and as a visiting professor at leading art and design institutions. His publications include the Wang Shouzhi Design History and Theory series, *A History of Modern Architecture in the World* and the Wang Shouzhi Talks series.

Chen Shaohua is a Chinese contemporary graphic designer, an executive council member of the Shenzhen Graphic Design Association, creative director of Chen Shaohua Design Co., Ltd. and a senior adviser to the Shenzhen Interior Designers Association. He served as art director for the 1988 feature film *The King of the Children* and received the Eighth Golden Rooster Award for Chinese Film Art for that work. His graphic-design work has also received awards at the Graphic Design in China exhibitions, the Brno International Biennial of Graphic Design and national art exhibitions. He designed commemorative stamps for the Fourth World Conference on Women, participated in the selected design for the Beijing 2008 Olympic bid logo, and designed the emblems of the Central Academy of Fine Arts and the Asian Development Bank's Shanghai annual meeting, as well as World AIDS Day and zodiac stamps. He joined the Alliance Graphique Internationale (AGI) in 1999 and designed the Year of the Dragon stamp issued in 2012.
