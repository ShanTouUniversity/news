With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the Shantou University Trade Union will hold the **“Happy Family Parent-Child Garden Event” from 11:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. on Saturday, June 1, 2013**. The event is for staff at the main campus, who may bring family members and children.

The program includes refreshments at Junhua Hotel, a high-definition 4D film at Zhongshan Park and free time in the park. Each film screening will last about 30 minutes, with two to three screenings planned.

### Reservations and payment

| Item | Time or standard |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 11:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m., June 1 |
| Reservations | May 13–19 |
| Payment or deduction | May 20–22 |
| Ticket collection | 9:00 a.m.–noon, May 24 |
| Fee | 55 yuan per person by campus-card deduction; 65 yuan per person in cash. Adults and children are charged at the same rate. |
| Capacity rule | Each staff member may reserve up to four places; the event will be canceled if fewer than 100 people attend. |

Staff must register through the [online reservation system](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/); a reservation is valid only after registration in the system. Participants may choose automatic deduction from a campus card during the specified period or cash payment. Those paying in cash must follow the Union's bank-payment process and exchange the receipt for admission tickets. The university bank-account details are not reproduced in this article.

A reservation may be canceled in the system during the reservation period without charge. Once the system closes, no new reservations or cancellations will be accepted and resulting fees will not be refunded. Failure to complete a cash payment on time, or a failed campus-card deduction caused by insufficient balance, will be treated as withdrawal.

### Itinerary and safety

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 11:00 a.m. | Depart from the STU motto stele |
| 11:10 a.m. | Pick up passengers at Anju Shantou Garden |
| 11:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m. | Refreshments at Junhua Hotel |
| 1:10 p.m. | Gather at the entrance of Junhua Hotel |
| 1:30–3:00 p.m. | Watch high-definition 4D films at Zhongshan Park, with two to three screenings planned |
| 3:30 p.m. | Return to STU from Zhongshan Park |

Each child must be accompanied by at least one parent. Staff members are asked to supervise children, keep them away from the park's artificial lake, wear flat athletic shoes and comfortable clothing, protect the park environment and avoid wasting refreshments.
