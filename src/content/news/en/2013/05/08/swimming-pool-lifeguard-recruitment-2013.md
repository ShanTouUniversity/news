STU's Human Resources Office and Department of Physical Education are recruiting **12 swimming-pool lifeguards**. Applications are due by **May 15, 2013**.

### Requirements

- Good conduct, a strong sense of responsibility and professional ethics;
- A responsible, collaborative and service-oriented approach to work;
- A national occupational qualification as a swimming lifeguard.

### Duties

Lifeguards will prepare the pool before opening by arriving on time, wearing lifeguard uniforms, preparing rescue equipment, and clearing debris from the deck and floating objects from the water. During opening hours, they will observe swimmers, prevent safety incidents, discourage rule violations and respond to temporary safety incidents. After closing, they will clear the pool, inspect it for possible hazards, store rescue equipment, and assist with replacing and washing filter bags and removing debris. Other duties assigned by the supervising teacher may also be required.

### Application

Applicants may email their materials to `o_tyb@stu.edu.cn` by May 15 or send a letter to the STU Department of Physical Education with “Lifeguard Application” written on it. The notice requests a résumé and copies of identity, education and technical-qualification documents; this article does not reproduce applicants' personal records or credential details.
