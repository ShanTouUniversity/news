STU will hold the first preparatory meeting for the **2013 graduation ceremony and awards presentation** at **3:30 p.m. on Thursday, May 9, 2013**, in Room 201 of the New Administrative Center. The meeting will address organizational preparations for the ceremony.

The notice asks the relevant units to assign responsible staff to attend. They include the General Office of Party and Government Affairs, Student Affairs Office, University Youth League Committee, Office of Academic Affairs, Graduate School, Publicity Department and News Center, Resource Management Office, Security Office, Network and Information Center, Department of Physical Education, Center for Arts Education and the Student Affairs Office of the Medical College.
