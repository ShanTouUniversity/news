STU's School of Engineering has publicized the selection results for its summer program with the **Technion – Israel Institute of Technology**. Following English-language eligibility screening, an English interview and a comprehensive assessment, the review panel selected **seven undergraduates and three graduate students**.

### Selection and scoring

In the first round, undergraduates were required to have passed CET-6 and achieved a GPA of at least 3.0. Graduate applicants were required to have passed CET-6 and achieved an average of at least 80 in completed courses. The second round was an English interview. In the comprehensive assessment, undergraduate scores were calculated as interview 40 percent, GPA 30 percent and written application 30 percent; graduate scores were interview 40 percent, completed-course average 30 percent and written application 30 percent.
