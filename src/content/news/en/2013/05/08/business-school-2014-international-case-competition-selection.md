STU Business School has opened selection for students to take part in its 2014 international case-competition program, with participant training and selection planned for late May 2013. Registration runs from **May 8 to 15**. Shortlisted candidates will receive an English case by May 20 and take part in an **all-English interview on the afternoon of May 22**.

### Registration and selection

The selection is open to enrolled undergraduates in the Business School from the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts. Applicants should have completed economics, business-management or related courses and demonstrate strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills. Their average grade in ELC Levels 1–5 must be at least 80.

Interested students may use the “Case Competitions Registration” quick link on the Business School website or visit the [case-competition registration system](http://kingwu.stu.edu.cn/CaseCompetitions/Login.aspx) directly. Shortlisted candidates will analyze an English case, prepare an English PowerPoint presentation and attend the all-English interview. The final participant list will be posted on the website.
