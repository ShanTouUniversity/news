STU's credit system has added an **In-Semester Teaching Feedback** feature, available from **May 8** through Week 14 of the current semester. The feature gives students an anonymous channel for course suggestions and feedback and allows teachers to review and respond to classroom comments.

### How to use it

After signing in to the credit system, students should go to **Course Evaluation → In-Semester Teaching Feedback**, select the relevant course, enter a comment and submit it. Feedback is submitted anonymously.

Teachers can use the same feature to view feedback from students in their classes and click individual messages to reply. Students can then view the teacher's response.

| User | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Students | Select a course and submit an anonymous comment |
| Teachers | Review class feedback and reply to messages |
| Availability | From May 8, 2013 through Week 14 of the semester |
| Issuing offices | Office of Academic Affairs and Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment |

The university described teaching as an interactive process between teachers and students and said that effective communication can help improve teaching. The live credit-system interface governs the detailed operation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Academic Affairs and Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment)
