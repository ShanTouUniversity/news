The STU Laboratory Work Committee will meet at **3 p.m. on Tuesday, May 14, 2013**, in **Conference Room 330 of the New Administrative Center**. The meeting will discuss the allocation of funding for the 2013 **Undergraduate Experimental Teaching Support and Enhancement** special projects.

The members are listed in pinyin order by surname. Meeting materials will be sent to committee members electronically in advance. Members should review the materials and copy them to their laptops before the meeting. No paper materials will be distributed; attendees should bring a laptop that has been charged in advance.
