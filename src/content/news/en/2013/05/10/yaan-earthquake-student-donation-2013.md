Following the earthquake in Ya'an, Sichuan, STU faculty members and students raised relief funds through collection points on campus and an on-site donation drive held during a prayer ceremony. By the morning of **April 27, 2013**, the campaign had raised **RMB 6,466**.

The money was donated on **April 27** in the name of Shantou University to the China Youth Development Foundation's special **Ya'an Youth Disaster Relief Fund** for post-earthquake relief. The result was announced on May 10 by the STU Committee of the Communist Youth League and the STU Youth Volunteers Association.
