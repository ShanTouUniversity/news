STU will hold a **basic lifesaving workshop from 1:45 to 5 p.m. on May 15, 2013**, with places for **20 participants**. If applications exceed the available places, a draw will determine the final participants. Registration closes at **midnight on May 12**.

The registration materials are provided through a form associated with the STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association. Applicants should follow the original notice and form when submitting materials; the designated channel and required file format should be confirmed through the OA system. The form requests personal fields including names, gender, student or teacher status, major and year or affiliated unit, telephone numbers, QQ numbers, email addresses, first-aid training experience and suggestions. These details are not reproduced here.

### Rescue team and trainer

The attached profile introduces the Shantou Mosang Zilu Water Rescue Team and Zheng An. The team is described as a private nonprofit organization legally registered with the Shantou Civil Affairs Bureau and, at the time of the notice, the only professional civilian rescue team in Shantou. The profile explains “Mosang Zilu” as a group of strangers who have experienced life's vicissitudes and gathered around a shared belief and pursuit, guided by the principles of “universal love and humanitarianism.”

Zheng An is one of 46 coaches of the Guangdong Red Cross Water Lifesaving Team and has specialized swimming and lifesaving skills. According to the attachment, he completed professional moving-water rescue and diving search-and-rescue training in Taiwan after being sent there by the Guangdong Red Cross in May 2011. In August of the same year, he was appointed a water-sports supervisor in Shenzhen for the 26th Summer Universiade. He also has experience in education, educational psychology and water-based instruction.
