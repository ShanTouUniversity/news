STU has announced the results of the **2013 Shantou University Medal** selection. Established by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the award recognizes outstanding graduating students, with the selection conducted by the Shantou University Medal Selection Committee.

The selection covered **20 graduating-student candidates** nominated by the schools and colleges. It resulted in **one Shantou University Medal recipient** and **six nomination-award recipients**. The medal recipient also receives **RMB 10,000** from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, while each nomination-award recipient receives **RMB 2,000**.

### Results

Student names are abbreviated as family names plus initials of given names for privacy. The given-name portions of two student names are corrupted in the source, and no speculative reconstruction is attempted.

The committee evaluated candidates comprehensively under the university's medal-selection principles, considering moral character, academic and research performance, extracurricular activities, participation in public affairs, judgment, talent and creativity shown in interviews, and the views of the relevant school or college dean.

### Selection committee

Vice President Li Dan chaired the committee. Its members included Professor Huang Yan of the Business School, Professor Bao Nengsheng of the School of Engineering, Associate Professor Li Haoyu of the Changjiang College of Arts and Design, and Student Affairs Office Director Yu Hong.

### Source discrepancy

The results notice attachment says that **12 candidates** were selected from the 20 candidates for the second-round interviews. An STU Student Affairs Office interview-arrangement notice issued on **May 6** says that **13 applicants** were selected from the same group of 20. Neither source explains the difference, so both figures are recorded according to their original wording.
