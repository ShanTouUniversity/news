It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the Business School of Shantou University will hold the third Life Education experiential activity on May 15 in the Student Activity Hall.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Experiential life education series and case sharing on life education |
| Purpose | To help students understand the meaning of life, cherish their own lives, respect others’ lives and reflect on their value and purpose |
| Participants | All 2012 undergraduates of the Business School |
| Places | 50 |
| Time | May 15, 2013 |
| Venue | Student Activity Hall |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Business School |

The program includes experiential activities and case sharing and exchange, requiring participants to strictly observe discipline, possess good physical fitness, wear sportswear and sports shoes, fully participate in moderate-intensity training, and submit a written reflection afterwards.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Allocation | 25 places randomly drawn first, remaining places by voluntary application with lottery if oversubscribed |
| Deadline | May 13, 2013 |
| How to apply | Send registration details to the designated email address |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee, Business School |

The registration email is available on the OA system.
