The seventh 2013 STU Business School academic lecture will be held from **9 a.m. to noon on May 15, 2013**, in the **Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of the Business School**. The program consists of three talks on the localization of economic anthropology in China, the driving forces behind state-led economic development, and foreign direct investment (FDI) spillovers and innovation performance among small and medium-sized enterprises through collaborative innovation networks.

### Program

| Speaker | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| Tian Guang | The Localization Path and Theoretical Contributions of Economic Anthropology in China |
| Ji Yun | State-led Economy: Factor-driven or Innovation-driven? |
| Zheng Muqiang | FDI Spillovers and Innovation Performance of Small and Medium-sized Enterprises Based on Collaborative Innovation Networks |

The lecture is open to faculty members and students across the university. The notice does not provide separate time slots for the three talks; attendees may come during the scheduled program to hear the presentations.
