It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 10, 2013, asking all graduates of the Class of 2013 to report their employment destinations.

Per the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, graduates must submit information by May 29 via http://gdc.stu.edu.cn (see OA attachment for instructions) to allow compilation of the employment plan and handling of reporting certificates and archives transfer. Separate attachments explain applications for deferred employment, further study and overseas study.

The employment plan and dispatch procedures directly affect archives, household registration and other matters, so graduates should submit carefully. For questions, contact the Graduate Employment Guidance Center: job@stu.edu.cn, 0754-86502252.

> ⚠️ Instructions and attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
