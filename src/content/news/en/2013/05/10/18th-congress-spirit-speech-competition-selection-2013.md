STU will hold a campus selection speech competition on the theme **“Study and Implement the Spirit of the 18th National Congress and Let Youth Shine Brilliantly.”** The competition is open to all STU students and will take place from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on May 17, 2013**, in **Conference Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building**.

The selection is intended to support the “two studies” campaign, which calls for studying the report of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China and the Party Constitution, and to identify a representative for the eastern Guangdong division of the Guangdong university-student speech competition on the same theme. The campus winner will represent STU in the next stage.

### Competition requirements

Each participant must prepare a speech of approximately **five minutes**. The speech should closely address the theme, be positive, grounded in reality and compelling; the specific title and content are up to the participant. Speeches must be delivered from memory. Participants should dress appropriately and prepare any background music or PowerPoint presentation themselves. Music files must be in MP3 format.

### Awards

The competition will present one first prize, two second prizes and three third prizes, for **six awardees** in total. Winners will receive certificates and corresponding rewards.

The registration form and speech manuscript must be submitted according to the original notice by **May 14**. The attached form requests fields including name, gender, year and major, dormitory address, telephone number, email address, speech title, personal profile and award history. These personal details are not reproduced here; the submission channel should be confirmed through the OA system.
