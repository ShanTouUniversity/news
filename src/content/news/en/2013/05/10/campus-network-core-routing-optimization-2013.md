The STU Information and Network Center will optimize the campus network core routing as part of the campus network upgrade plan. During the maintenance windows, internet access for all campus network users and external services provided by internal campus systems will experience brief interruptions.

### Maintenance windows

| Time | Affected services |
| --- | --- |
| 12 a.m.–6 a.m., May 11, 2013 | Campus network access and external services of internal campus systems |
| 12 a.m.–6 a.m., May 12, 2013 | Campus network access and external services of internal campus systems |

The recovery time will not be announced separately. The Information and Network Center asks users to inform one another and apologizes for the inconvenience.

For network-service inquiries, contact the Information and Network Center at **0754-86503400-1** or `stunic@stu.edu.cn`.
