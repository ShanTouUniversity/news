The sixth 2013 STU Business School academic lecture will be held from **2:30 to 5 p.m. on Wednesday, May 15, 2013**, in **ACC Meeting Room 5**. Titled **“New Trends in Academic Publishing: Suggestions to the Chinese Scholars for Publishing Academic Work in the US,”** the lecture will be delivered by Dr. David Smith and chaired by Professor Tian Guang. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome to attend.

### Topics

The presentation will discuss publishing research in U.S. academic business journals and will cover:

1. An overview of publishing in American academic business journals;
2. The academic business-journal environment;
3. Journal selection and the issue of rankings;
4. The scientific method;
5. General selection criteria;
6. Writing skills.

### Speaker profile

The notice describes Dr. David Smith as an editor-in-chief in the North American business-publishing field, while another part of the notice calls him editor-in-chief of “North American Business News.” The profile also says that he was a full-time professor before beginning a seven-year editorial career, and that his research record includes more than 50 peer-reviewed publications, numerous litigation and international conference reports, and five business-related books.

Smith's research areas include strategic management, international management and strategic marketing. He began his career as a management consultant and has worked with the U.S. Department of Commerce, the Small Business Administration, the organization responsible for agricultural exports, the Export Development Corporation and numerous economic and trade development groups on projects intended to guide international marketing development in the United States and Canada. He has also advised several Fortune 500 companies on international market opportunities, export and capital-acquisition issues, and strategic planning.
