It is reported that the Japan Reporting Team of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a sharing session on Saturday, May 11, at 19:30 in the Library Lecture Hall.

The team visited Tokyo, Sendai, Fukushima, and Ishinomaki to gain a first-hand understanding of post-earthquake Japan. At the session, students will screen documentaries and multimedia works they filmed and produced themselves, sharing how local residents have responded to the disaster as well as the team's own reflections from the journey.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Event | STU Japan Reporting Team Sharing Session |
| Time | May 11 (Saturday), 19:30 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |
| Program | Documentary and multimedia screenings, perspectives on post-disaster resilience, team reflections |
| Reporting Locations | Tokyo, Sendai, Fukushima, Ishinomaki |

All interested faculty and students are welcome to attend.
