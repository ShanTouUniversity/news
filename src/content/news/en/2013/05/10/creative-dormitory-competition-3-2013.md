STU will hold its third creative dormitory competition under the theme **“Endless Paper Creativity, Paper Makes You Happy.”** The event will take place on **May 18, 2013**, at the **Golden Triangle and student residential areas**. It is open to all STU students and aims to enrich residential life, develop creative thinking and resource-saving awareness, and provide a platform for dormitory teams to present their creativity.

### Teams and works

Participants must form teams representing one dormitory or a group of dormitories, with **three to six members** per team. Every member must take part in creating the work. Teams must make their entries on site. The two categories are:

- **Three-dimensional paper crafts**, such as origami or three-dimensional models;
- **Flat paper crafts**, such as paper-cutting or collages.

The organizers will provide scissors, glue, double-sided tape, craft knives, pens, used posters, newspapers and magazines. Teams may bring additional materials if needed. After the competition, each team must attach a written explanation to its work and submit it to the implementing organizers.

### Competition and awards

The competition consists of a quiz, games and an artwork-creation round, accounting for **10%, 20% and 70%** of the total score respectively.

| Award | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Craftsmanship Award | RMB 300 and a gift |
| Special Thanks to You Award | RMB 200 and a gift |
| Race Against Time Award | RMB 100 and a gift |
| Participation Award | A small gift for each participant |

### Registration

Students may collect a paper form from a publicity point in their residential area and place it in the collection box at the entrance to the area, or download and submit the attached form. The notice body gives a registration deadline of **noon on May 17**, while the attached form gives a deadline of **midnight on May 17**. Because the source does not explain the difference, both times are preserved here; participants should follow the latest arrangement from the implementing organizers.

The form asks for a team name and size, as well as members' names, year and major, dormitory, campus email address and telephone number. Individual registration fields are not reproduced here. Activity consultation contacts are also omitted.
