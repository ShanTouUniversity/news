It is reported that the Business School will hold Economics Salon Vol.118 on May 16, featuring Dr. David Smith, Editor-in-Chief of North American Business Press, to deliver a themed talk.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Dr. David Smith, Editor-in-Chief, North American Business Press, formerly Full Professor |
| Topic | From Business School Dean to CEO of a Publishing Company |
| Date | May 16, 2013 (Thursday) |
| Time | 14:30–17:00 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall |
| Language | English |
| Organizer | Business School |
| Release date | May 10, 2013 |

The lecture is open to all faculty and students and interested participants are welcome.

## About the Speaker

According to the notice, Dr. Smith has served as Editor-in-Chief at North American Business Press for seven years, previously as a Full Professor. As an active researcher, he has authored over 50 peer-reviewed publications, proceedings and international conference presentations, and five business-related books, with research interests in strategic management, international management and strategic marketing.

Dr. Smith began his career as a management consultant and has worked with the U.S. Department of Commerce, Small Business Administration, Foreign Agricultural Service, Export Development Corporation and various economic, trade and development groups on projects promoting international marketing expansion for the United States and Canada. His consulting experience also includes advising numerous Fortune 500 firms on international market expansion, export capital acquisition and strategic planning.
