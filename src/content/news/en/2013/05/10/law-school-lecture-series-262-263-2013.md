The STU Law School will host sessions **262 and 263** of its law lecture series, featuring lawyer Huang Jinshan. Both lectures will be held in **Room 213 of the Law School** and are open to faculty members and students across the university.

### Two lectures

| Session | Topic | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 262 | Cybercrime: Recent Developments and Legal Responses | 4–5:30 p.m., Tuesday, May 14, 2013 |
| 263 | Intellectual-Property Legal Work and Practice: The Hong Kong Situation | 3–4:30 p.m., Wednesday, May 15, 2013 |

### Speaker

Huang Jinshan is a senior consultant at a Hong Kong law firm and has worked in the legal profession for more than 20 years. According to the speaker profile, his work covers intellectual-property law in Hong Kong, mainland China and overseas jurisdictions, as well as related international developments. His recent publication is **_Intellectual Property Law and Practice in Hong Kong (2012)_**. During the semester, he is also teaching a master's-level cybercrime course at the University of Hong Kong Faculty of Law.
