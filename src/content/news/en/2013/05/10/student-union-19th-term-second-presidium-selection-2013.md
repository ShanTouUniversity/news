As the first term of the 19th STU Student Union approaches its end, the university will hold a mid-term election for the second term on **May 26, 2013**. Student teams from across the university are invited to stand for the Student Union presidium, which consists of **one chair and three vice-chairs**.

### Teams and qualifications

Enrolled students who meet the requirements for Student Union committee candidates and the relevant STU regulations on student cadres may form teams regardless of year or major. Each campaign team must have **four members**. Candidates should demonstrate an innovative and enterprising spirit, enthusiasm for Student Union work, diligence, a practical working style, student-work experience and strong social-activity skills. They must also be able to serve continuously for at least one year.

### Selection process

Candidate teams must submit the Candidate Preliminary Nominee Registration Form, a team work proposal and transcripts for each completed academic year verified by the relevant school or college. The registration forms, transcripts, transcript verification and work proposal for each team must be bound together and delivered to the Mid-term Election Secretariat in **Room 721, Building 789** by **5:15 p.m. on May 12**. Electronic materials should be submitted according to the original notice.

The Mid-term Election Secretariat will organize an assessment group to review the preliminary teams and identify qualified candidate teams. The candidate teams will then deliver campaign speeches at the election meeting, where all Student Union members will vote to elect the new-term presidium.
