The STU Youth Volunteers Association and youth volunteer organizations from the university's schools will hold the **“Pass on Positive Youth Energy and Build the Chinese Dream Together” Community Service Street** from **9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. on Saturday, May 11, 2013**. The attached activity plan identifies the venue as the **entrance to the pedestrian street of Huaqiao Xincun on Jinxin Road** in Shipaotai Subdistrict, Jinping District, Shantou, with local residents as the service population.

The activity is organized by the Jinping District Civilization Office, the Shantou Working Committee of the Communist Youth League, the Party Committee and office of Shipaotai Subdistrict, the STU Committee of the Communist Youth League and the STU Youth Volunteers Association, among others. It aims to bring volunteer services into the community, provide practical assistance and promote the volunteer spirit of “dedication, friendship, mutual assistance and progress.”

### Service stations

| Volunteer organization | Services |
| --- | --- |
| Law School Youth Volunteers Association | Prize-based legal-knowledge quiz and consultation on legal questions |
| Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication Youth Volunteers Association | Free postcards and stamps for residents to send messages to family and friends; free photography with same-day photo processing and delivery |
| Cheung Kong College of Arts and Design Youth Volunteers Association | Yarn-doll making and instruction, portrait sketching and basic drawing guidance, Chinese-knot instruction and a charity postcard sale |
| Medical College Youth Volunteers Association | Free blood-pressure, blood-glucose, height and weight measurements, with related health-information guidance |
| Engineering School Youth Volunteers Association | Computer-knowledge promotion, free computer cleaning for residents and interactive computer-knowledge games |
| Zhicheng College “Evergreen” Service-Learning Association | Charity sale of STU-made products, donated goods and other public-welfare items, with proceeds placed in the Evergreen Love Fund |
| College of Liberal Arts Youth Volunteers Association | Information sessions and materials on senior health, eye care and healthy family life |

Proceeds from the Arts Association's postcard sale will be donated to the Firefly Love Fund. The Business School and School of Science youth volunteer associations will not participate in this event because of scheduling conflicts with other activities of their schools, according to the activity plan.
