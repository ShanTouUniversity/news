In preparation for the 2013 Shantou Dragon Boat Race, a dragon-boat team associated with the Jinping District representative team will conduct pre-competition training at **Riyuekeng Reservoir west of the STU ACC** from **May 11 through 31, 2013**. Training will take place daily from **4:30 to 6 p.m.**

Water-based training will be conducted around the reservoir during this period. The university asks faculty members and students not to gather at the reservoir to watch, in order to protect public safety.
