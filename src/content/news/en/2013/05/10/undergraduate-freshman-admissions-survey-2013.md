The STU Admissions Office has launched a survey for the 2012 undergraduate cohort to collect opinions and suggestions about the university's admissions work, improve related services and support the quality of future undergraduate recruitment.

All 2012 undergraduates should visit the admissions survey page at [http://zs.stu.edu.cn/vote/Default.aspx](http://zs.stu.edu.cn/vote/Default.aspx) and complete and submit the questionnaire **by Sunday, May 19, 2013**.

### Survey incentives

Students who submit a valid questionnaire on time will receive one STU campus-scenery postcard and enter a prize draw. The prizes include:

| Prize | Quantity |
| --- | ---: |
| STU campus-scenery card-style USB drive | 50 |
| “Because of You” T-shirts | 70 |
| “Because of You” polo shirts | 60 |

The draw results and collection arrangements will be announced separately through the university office-information network. The questionnaire content is not reproduced here.

| --- | --- |

| Purpose | Collect opinions and suggestions about university admissions work |

| Deadline | By May 19, 2013 |
| Survey page | [zs.stu.edu.cn/vote/Default.aspx](http://zs.stu.edu.cn/vote/Default.aspx) |
| Organizing office | Admissions Office |
