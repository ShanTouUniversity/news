STU has published **April 2013 utility charges for faculty and staff**. Meters for this billing cycle were read on **May 1 and 2**, following the previous reading on **April 1**. Faculty and staff may log in to the STU utility-management system at `http://power.stu.edu.cn` to check their charges.

Anyone with a question or objection may visit the Water and Electricity Center for verification from **May 10 through 15**. The center can be reached at `2489`. After the review period, charges without objections will be deducted by the Finance Office from the employee's **May 2013 salary account**. Employees without a salary account must pay the Finance Office directly.
