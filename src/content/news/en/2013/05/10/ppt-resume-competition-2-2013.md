To help students learn PPT electronic-resume skills, understand the basic content of a résumé and improve their future employability, the STU Student Affairs Office will hold its second PPT electronic-resume competition in **May 2013**. The competition is open to all enrolled students, who enter individually.

### Competition schedule

| Stage | Time or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | By noon on May 15 |
| Training and guidance | May 15, 3–5 p.m., E Lecture Room 202; Su Junzhi and Wu Chao will cover résumé content and PPT techniques |
| Preliminary entry submission | By midnight on May 20 |
| Final | May 31; detailed arrangements to be announced |

The final will include work presentations, participant speeches and judges' questions. Each entry must be created independently by its participant and contain the basic content of a résumé in an innovative format that shows the participant's individuality as an STU student. Entries must be submitted in PPT format, with a playback time of no more than **three minutes** and a file size of no more than **10 MB**.

### Awards and opportunity

| Award | Quota and reward |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | One awardee; certificate and RMB 300 |
| Second prize | Two awardees; certificate and RMB 200 each |
| Third prize | Three awardees; certificate and RMB 100 each |
| Excellence award | Four awardees; certificate and a gift |
| Best Popularity award | One awardee; certificate and a gift |

Finalists will have an opportunity to receive internship positions offered by China United Network Communications Group Co., Ltd. Shantou Branch, subject to subsequent arrangements from the relevant organization.

### Registration

Participants must download the attached registration form. Electronic forms should be submitted according to the original notice, and printed forms may be delivered to B304, C238, F116, G435 or J311. The notice and form require participants to submit personal information and contact details. The form includes fields for name, school and major, student number, telephone number, email address and dormitory; no participant's personal information is reproduced here. Confirm the submission channel through the original notice in the OA system.
