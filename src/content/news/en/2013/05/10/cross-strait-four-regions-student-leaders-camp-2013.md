The 2013 **“Youth, Innovation and Development: Dreams Guide Growth” Student Leaders Study Camp for the Mainland, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan** will be held from **July 21 through 29**. Organized by the Overseas Chinese Affairs Office of the State Council and the China Overseas Exchange Association and hosted by Jinan University, the camp has invited STU to nominate one outstanding student leader. Eligible candidates are **undergraduate and graduate students from the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts**.

The nine-day camp is organized around four modules: leadership development; personal development and team building; social practice and cultural study; and exchange, cooperation and development. The organizers will cover accommodation, meals, local transportation, study activities, visits and insurance during the camp. Participants must cover round-trip transportation between their home university and Jinan University.

### Camp schedule

| Date | Main arrangements |
| --- | --- |
| July 21, Sunday | Registration and check-in throughout the day |
| July 22, Monday | Opening ceremony; lecture on leadership culture and leadership skills; personal-development activities; Pearl River night cruise; group study, sharing and discussion |
| July 23, Tuesday | Lectures on Confucian culture and Confucian management thought and on the psychological qualities required of student leaders; Chinese martial-arts study; “Youth Dreams” sharing session |
| July 24, Wednesday | Visit to Vatti Gas Appliance Co., Ltd. in Zhongshan; high-speed rail to Changsha, followed by coach travel to Fenghuang |
| July 25, Thursday | Visit to Fenghuang Ancient Town; travel to Zhangjiajie; group study, sharing and discussion |
| July 26, Friday | Visit to Zhangjiajie; coach travel to Changsha in the evening |
| July 27, Saturday | Exchange with Hunan University and visit to Yuelu Academy; visit to the Changsha Bamboo Slips Museum; high-speed rail back to Guangzhou in the evening |
| July 28, Sunday | Group thematic presentations; sharing session and closing ceremony; gala evening |
| July 29, Monday | Departure assistance and group dispersal throughout the day |

The camp schedule also calls for a **“Imagine Your Youth Dreams”** themed essay to be submitted in **mid-June**. Participants should follow subsequent instructions from the organizers for related requirements.

### Nomination and submission

Each school, residential college or relevant unit may nominate one student leader candidate. Candidates should demonstrate an active and positive character, team spirit, leadership experience or achievements, participation in campus cultural activities, organizational and management skills, and the ability to introduce STU and its educational characteristics. The Student Affairs Office will organize a review and submit one university nominee to Jinan University.

Candidates must complete the Outstanding Student Leader Personal Recommendation Form and provide copies of relevant award certificates. Electronic and printed materials must be submitted by **4 p.m. on May 17, 2013** to the Student Quality Development Center in Room 106 of the Administrative Center. Electronic materials should be packaged and sent to `nlan@stu.edu.cn` under the naming requirements in the original notice.

The recommendation form requests personal information including name, gender, date and place of birth, major and cohort, identity-document details, height and weight, contact information, skills and leadership experience. Individual candidate information is not reproduced here.
