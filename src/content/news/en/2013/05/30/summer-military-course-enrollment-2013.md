STU's People's Armed Forces Department and Student Affairs Department have announced the **2013 summer-semester military-course** enrollment arrangements. Under the national syllabus for military courses at higher-education institutions, military skills training and military theory together form the compulsory Military Training and Military Theory course. Students in the 2012 cohort and students whose training was deferred in previous years must take the course this summer.

The course is offered in the summer semester, but actual instruction will take place within two weeks after the current semester ends because of the university's teaching plan and the training unit's operational duties. Enrolling in military theory will therefore not affect students' other summer-semester course selections.

## Enrollment Rules

Students must choose the class number of an instructor from their own school or college. Students in residential colleges must choose a residential-college instructor, while students who have changed majors must choose an instructor from their new school or college. ## Special Applications

- Students without mainland household registration may participate in military training after voluntary application and approval from their school or residential college and the People's Armed Forces Department. They must also register for the course in the credit-based academic system.
- Students with serious physical impairments, disabilities or illnesses may, with approval from their school or residential college and the People's Armed Forces Department, receive an appropriate exemption from unsuitable military-skills subjects. They must still enroll in and study military theory.
- Students in the 2012 cohort are generally expected to take the course this year. Those with special circumstances may apply for deferred training with approval from their school or residential college and the People's Armed Forces Department, and enroll again when they make up the training.

Special applications must be submitted in writing to the relevant school or residential college by **June 5**. The application form requests personal information including names, student numbers, majors, telephone numbers, email addresses and reasons for the application; it is not reproduced here. Applicants should follow the original OA notice.

## Military-Skills Class Grouping

A follow-up notice issued on **June 3, 2013** states that the year's military training will run from **July 1 to July 14**. Each school or residential college was asked to divide its participating students into military-skills classes of approximately **50 students per class** and submit the class rosters to the Student Affairs Office by **June 9** at `o_xsc@stu.edu.cn`. The student rosters are not reproduced here.
