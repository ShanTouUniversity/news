The Toward the Ocean public-welfare course group of STU&apos;s School of Science will hold a **Marine Ecological Protection Public-Welfare Day from June 1 to 2** at **Longhu Beach at Shantou CITIC Resort**. The theme is **“Go Toward the Ocean, Listen to the Voice of the Sea, and Keep Our Pledge to Protect the Sea.”**

The event is jointly organized by the Toward the Ocean course group&apos;s faculty and students, the Tourism Bureau of Haojiang District, the Haojiang District Urban Construction and Environmental Protection Bureau, the Haojiang District Marine Fisheries Bureau, and the Shantou CITIC Resort Hotel. It will promote marine ecological protection among the university community and the public.

## Program

The program will include stage performances, a thousand-person signature campaign, a base-unveiling ceremony, family games, a marine-knowledge competition and a checkpoint adventure. Faculty members and students from the course group will also prepare marine-knowledge activities and interactive games to help participants learn about the ocean and strengthen awareness of marine conservation.

Admission to the resort beach entrance will be free on June 1 and 2.

## Arrangements for Course Students

A subsequent arrangement issued by the School of Science states that the group-transport plan is for students enrolled in the **spring 2013 Toward the Ocean public-welfare course**: Class 67 will assemble at **7 a.m. on Saturday, June 1**, and Class 89 at **7 a.m. on Sunday, June 2**. Both groups will meet at the STU motto stele.

Students should bring a cup or water bottle, a hat, sunscreen and rain gear, and must wear a Toward the Ocean T-shirt. Sports trousers and athletic shoes are recommended. The course arrangement expressly prohibits course students from swimming during the two-day activity. Participants should protect the environment and take care of their own safety. In the event of a typhoon or other extreme weather, the activity will be cancelled automatically and a rescheduled date will be announced separately.

The follow-up notice listed course organizers Sun Zewei, Chen Yichun, Zhang Liting, Yang Mulan, Lin Yu, Wang Junying and Zhu Huibin as contacts; their personal mobile numbers are omitted here.
