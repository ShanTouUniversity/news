The South China Center of the Guangdong Education Network plans system maintenance at a regional node to support the national university-admissions network. The work will interrupt education-network connectivity.

## Maintenance Details

- **Time**: May 31, 2013, 12:30–1:30 p.m.
- **Impact**: STU campus-network users with education-network IP addresses will be unable to access external networks; off-campus education-network users will be unable to access STU&apos;s internal network.
- **Restoration notice**: The restoration time will not be announced separately.

The Network and Information Center asks affected users to share the notice and apologizes for the inconvenience.

For inquiries, contact the Network and Information Center at **86503400** (campus short number **883400**) or **stunic@stu.edu.cn**.
