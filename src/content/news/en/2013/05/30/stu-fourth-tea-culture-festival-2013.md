STU’s Tea Ceremony Club will hold the **fourth Tea Culture Festival from June 1 to 2** under the theme **“Promoting Traditional Chinese Tea Culture.”** The festival aims to introduce faculty members and students to Chaoshan tea culture and deepen their understanding of traditional Chinese tea arts through tea tasting and related activities.

The festival is organized by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the Shantou Tea Culture Association and the STU Student Societies Federation. It is co-organized by the STU Tea Ceremony Club and the STU–University of Calgary CDIO Exchange Program Team. Events will take place across the Shantou University campus.

## Schedule

| Activity | Time | Venue | Program |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sharing Tea Fellowship | June 1, 11 a.m.–1 p.m. | Golden Triangle and Pedestrian Street | Tea appraisal by tea experts, tea-knowledge displays and games |
| Getting Close to Tea | June 1, 4–5 p.m. | Stone benches behind the Gymnasium | Tea-guessing contest |
| Photography Competition | From the notice date until noon on June 1 | STU campus | Original tea-related photography, including images made before or during the festival; entries will be judged at the closing event |
| Tea Culture Festival Evening and Exchange Program with the University of Calgary | June 2, 7:30–9 p.m. | Science Lecture Hall | Chaoshan gongfu tea performance, performances by Canadian exchange students and a skit |

The original notice instructed participants in the tea-guessing and photography activities to submit personal names and contact details or short numbers through a student-club account. Those personal-submission fields and the personal account are omitted here. Participants should confirm the registration method through an official organizer channel.
