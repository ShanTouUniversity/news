STU Library has opened trial access to databases from the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) for faculty and students. The trial includes:

- ASME full-text current journals (ASME e-Journals)
- ASME full-text archival journals (ASME Archives)
- ASME Conference Proceedings
- ASME eBooks

Readers can access the resources through the library database page: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=278>. The trial runs through **June 30, 2013**.

Feedback on the trial can be sent to the Library&apos;s subject services team at **86503247**, **lqxie@stu.edu.cn** or **zhzeng@stu.edu.cn**.
