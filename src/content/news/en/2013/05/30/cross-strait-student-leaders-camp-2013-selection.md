It is reported that Shantou University has finalized its delegate for the 2013 Cross-Strait Student Leaders Camp and will send one student to represent the university.

Following the call for nominations issued by the Student Affairs Office, colleges (residential colleges) and relevant units actively responded, with 10 student leader candidates nominated. After careful evaluation by a review panel, the university decided to recommend Zhao HR, a 2010 cohort student majoring in International Economics and Trade at the Business School, to participate in the camp.

Themed "Youth · Innovation · Development — Dreams Lead Growth," the camp is sponsored by the Overseas Chinese Affairs Office of the State Council and the China Overseas Exchange Association, and organized by Jinan University, bringing together student leaders from universities across the cross-strait region.
