The MT Enterprise Innovation Club of STU&apos;s School of Engineering has opened its 2013 recruitment, mainly for **students in the 2012 cohort**, and welcomes eligible students from all schools. The organization&apos;s full name is the **Shantou University Modern Top Union**. Its enterprise innovation clubs operate in cooperation with the alumni companies Shantou Chuan Yue Computer Software Co., Ltd. and Nanjing Nanli Shiyou Software Technology Co., Ltd.

The organization documents describe MT as a project-practice, enterprise-exchange and training organization using a company-style project-management model. Its work includes business operations, technology development, publicity design, website development, software and mobile development. The documents trace MT&apos;s origins to a technology team formed in 2009, its development as an organization in 2010, and the establishment of two enterprise cooperation clubs at the School of Engineering&apos;s CDIO Innovation Practice Center on June 11, 2011.

## Recruitment Groups

The recruitment covers the following groups:

- **Project Development Department**: Technical Group and Design Group
- **E-Commerce Department**: E-Commerce Group
- **Integrated Planning Department**: Human Resources Group and Event Planning Group

### Requirements

| Group | Main requirements and target applicants |
| --- | --- |
| Technical | Clear and logical thinking, communication, learning and teamwork skills, and a strong interest in product design or network technology; project experience preferred. Mainly for 2012 cohort Computer Science students |
| Design | A solid art foundation, learning potential, proficiency with software such as Photoshop, imagination, modeling ability and color awareness. Mainly for 2012 cohort students in Arts and Journalism |
| Business | Responsibility, teamwork, an interest in e-commerce or management consulting, and public-relations and negotiation skills. Open to 2012 cohort students across the university |
| Human Resources | Organization and teamwork skills and the ability to organize and integrate materials. Open to 2012 cohort students across the university |
| Event Planning | Publicity skills such as poster design and production, an interest in event planning and some relevant experience. Open to 2012 cohort students across the university |

## Application and Interview

Applications close at **9 p.m. on June 4**. Applicants must complete the attached form and send it to the MT organization&apos;s email address, **moderntop@gmail.com**. The form requests personal details including name, school, major, telephone number and experience; the form and its personal-data fields are not reproduced here.

Interviews will be held in the **meeting room of the CDIO Innovation Practice Center**. The main notice lists interview times of **3 p.m. or 9 p.m. on June 5**. The application form lists 3–4 p.m., 4–5 p.m. and 9:15 p.m.; applicants should follow the recruitment organizers&apos; confirmed session.

Applicants for the Technical and Business groups must also indicate whether they can attend a two-week training program after military training. The organization materials describe MT projects involving websites, software, mobile applications, e-commerce platforms, publicity design and mobile games, with members working in project teams.
