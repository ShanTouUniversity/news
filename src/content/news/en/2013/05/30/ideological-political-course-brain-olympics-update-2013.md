To make ideological-political courses more engaging and help students master course content, STU&apos;s Department of Social Sciences has updated the question bank for four major required courses on the **Brain Olympics** website ([io.stu.edu.cn/web](http://io.stu.edu.cn/web/)).

The Network and Information Center has also updated the website&apos;s game rules. Previously, users had to accumulate 80 percent of the energy in **Brain Fuel Station** before entering **Intelligence Hurdles**, the simulated-examination game. After the update, users may play Brain Fuel Station and Intelligence Hurdles in either order according to their needs.

The Department of Social Sciences invites students to log in and use the updated question bank and games.
