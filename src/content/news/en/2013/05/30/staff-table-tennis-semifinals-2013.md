It is reported that the group stage of the Shantou University staff table tennis team tournament concluded on the afternoon of May 29, with all four semifinal berths now decided.

After fierce competition in Group A, the Administration Office team finished first with a perfect record, followed by the Library-Science-Media Research joint team in second place. In Group B, the College of Science team also went unbeaten to take the top spot, while the Resource Management team placed second. The semifinals will see the Administration Office team face the Resource Management team at Table 1, and the College of Science team take on the Library-Science-Media Research joint team at Table 2. All matches will be held in the table tennis room on the first floor of the university union building.

In addition, Power Dekor, the sponsor of this year's tournament, held a group-purchase consultation event on the first floor of the union building on Friday, May 31.
