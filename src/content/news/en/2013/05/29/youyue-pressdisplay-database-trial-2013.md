Shantou University Library is offering readers trial access to the Youyue Foreign-Language Digital Library and the PressDisplay newspaper database. The two resources provide foreign-language e-books and newspapers from around the world, with the following trial deadlines and access links.

## Youyue Foreign-Language Digital Library

The Youyue Foreign-Language Digital Library provides original-language e-books from Europe and North America in areas including science, technology, medicine and management. The notice says the platform covers publications from more than 5,000 European and American publishers and institutions and contains approximately **500,000 titles**. It identifies Baker & Taylor, Lybrary.com and MIT Press among the contributing publishers or institutions.

- **Trial ends**: August 22, 2013
- **Access**: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=370>

## PressDisplay Newspaper Database

PressDisplay, launched by DynoMedia, provides **more than 2,200 newspapers from 97 countries in 54 languages**. It reproduces the original layout with full text and images and provides access to newspaper content from the previous 90 days. The notice names *The Wall Street Journal*, *The Washington Post*, *USA Today*, *The Guardian*, *The Observer*, *Daily Express*, *China Daily* and *Far Eastern Economic Review* among the resources.

- **Trial ends**: July 27, 2013
- **Access**: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=359>

A November 2012 PressDisplay trial notice described the database as covering 95 countries and 55 languages. The current notice gives the figures as 97 countries and 54 languages; this article preserves both source-reported descriptions.

Readers may send trial evaluations and comments to the Library Subject Services team at `86503247`, `lqxie@stu.edu.cn` or `zhzeng@stu.edu.cn`.
