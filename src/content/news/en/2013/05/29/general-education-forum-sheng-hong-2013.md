The fourth lecture in Shantou University’s **2013 General Education Forum** series will take place at **7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, June 4** in the **Library Lecture Hall**. Professor Sheng Hong, director of the Beijing Unirule Institute of Economics and a professor at Shandong University’s Institute of Economic Research, will speak on **“Does the Marketplace of Ideas Fail?”**

## Speaker

Sheng Hong holds a doctorate in economics from the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences. His research covers the structure, origins and evolution of institutions, conflict and integration among civilizations, and international political economy. His published or edited works include *Division of Labor and Trade*, *Governing a Large Country as Cooking a Small Fish*, *Why Institutions Matter*, *China’s Transitional Economics* and *Seeking a Stable Form of Reform*, along with essays on the spirit of economics, culture and morality.

The Beijing Unirule Institute of Economics was founded in **July 1993** and studies economic institutions such as enterprises and markets, as well as political and cultural institutions. The source introduction lists Sheng Hong as its director and Professor Mao Yushi as its chair.

The lecture is organized by the Institute of Higher Education, the College of Liberal Arts and the Center for Christian Studies.

## Additional Lecture Details

The June 4 event notice gives the title as **“Will the Marketplace of Ideas Fail?”**, which differs slightly from the earlier preview's “Does the Marketplace of Ideas Fail?” This article preserves both wordings from the source notices. The lecture presents economics as a method for analyzing the production and dissemination of ideas. Treating ideas in society as products and services in a competitive marketplace, Professor Sheng Hong will discuss “markets,” “exchange,” “government regulation,” “externalities” and “monopoly”; how ideas are filtered through free competition; and how constitutions and governments can protect the marketplace of ideas and create mechanisms that support the production and dissemination of high-quality ideas.

Born in 1954, Sheng Hong holds a doctorate in economics from the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences. He is director of the Beijing Unirule Institute of Economics and a professor at Shandong University's Institute of Economic Research. His research includes the structure, origins and evolution of institutions, conflict and integration among civilizations, and international political economy. His representative works also include *Why Institutions Matter*, *The Spirit of Economics*, *Innovating at the Margin of Tradition* and *Creating Peace for All Generations*.

The Beijing Unirule Institute of Economics was founded in **July 1993** by economists including Mao Yushi, Zhang Shuguang, Sheng Hong, Fan Gang and Tang Shouning, together with Beijing Elephant Culture Co., Ltd. The notice describes the institute as working on economic institutions such as enterprises and markets, as well as political and cultural institutions, and supporting research on economic theory and social-economic issues to develop institutional solutions for reform practice.
