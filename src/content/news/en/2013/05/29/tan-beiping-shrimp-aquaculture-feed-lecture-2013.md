STU’s Institute of Marine Biology in the School of Science will host an academic lecture from **10 a.m. to noon on Friday, May 31, 2013**. Tan Beiping, dean of the School of Fisheries at Guangdong Ocean University and a Pearl River Scholar Distinguished Professor, will speak on **“The Current State of Shrimp Aquaculture and the Feed Industry, and New Advances in Nutrition and Feed Research.”** The lecture will take place in **Science and Technology Center Meeting Room 205**. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.

## Speaker Profile

Tan Beiping holds a PhD and is a Level II professor and doctoral supervisor. He is dean of the School of Fisheries at Guangdong Ocean University. His profile states that he was selected for the Ministry of Education’s New Century Excellent Talents Support Program in 2005, became a national-level trainee in the sixth cohort of Guangdong’s Qianbaishi Project in 2010, and was appointed a Pearl River Scholar Distinguished Professor in 2011.

Since 1990, Tan has worked on the nutritional physiology and nutritional immunology of economically important aquatic animals, as well as the development of safe, environmentally friendly feeds and new feed additives. His profile says that in the past five years he has led nearly 20 national, provincial or ministerial research projects, published more than 50 papers in Chinese and international journals, including more than 30 indexed by SCI, and received more than 20 authorized Chinese invention patents. He has supervised more than 20 doctoral and master’s students.

His reported honors include one second prize of the National Science and Technology Progress Award, two first prizes in Ministry of Education science and technology awards, and three second- or third-level provincial or ministerial science and technology awards.
