Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office and Mathematics Department mathematical-modeling coaching group have opened campus pre-registration for the 2013 National Undergraduate Mathematical-Modeling Contest. The contest is organized by the Ministry of Education’s Department of Higher Education and the Chinese Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics and aims to develop students’ ability to use mathematics and computer technology to solve practical problems.

In 2012, STU organized 17 teams and received one national first prize, two national second prizes, four Guangdong regional first prizes, seven regional second prizes and one regional third prize.

## Contest and Registration Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contest period | 8 a.m. Friday, September 13, to 8 a.m. Monday, September 16, 2013 |
| Applicants | Undergraduates university-wide |
| Team format | Self-organized teams of three |
| Pre-registration deadline | June 14, 2013 |
| Training | August 23 to September 12, 2013 |

Applicants should have a solid mathematics foundation, computer-programming skills, mathematical-application and innovation abilities, and a commitment to teamwork. The three members of each team should complement one another in mathematical application, software programming and written communication. All pre-registered teams must attend the campus training, through which the coaching group will select the teams representing STU in the contest.

The main notice gives the pre-registration period as **May 27 to June 14**, while the attached registration form gives it as **May 29 to June 14**. This article preserves the date difference. Applicants should download the form, submit it as required by the university, and sign for confirmation at Mathematics Department teacher Li Jian’s office, Gongbei Room 408-1. The registration contact is `lijian@stu.edu.cn`. The form requests team members’ names, year, major, gender, telephone, QQ, email and self-introduction; its contents are not reproduced here.

The national contest website is <http://www.mcm.edu.cn>, and the STU Mathematical Modeling Association website is <http://math.stu.edu.cn/modeling>.
