The Academic Planning Division of the Guangdong Federation of Social Sciences has opened a call for papers for the *Guangdong Local Culture Studies* collection. Shantou University’s Research Office forwarded the call on May 29, 2013. The collection is intended to present recent research on local cultures in Guangdong and support research and cultural development in the province. The original call is dated **May 13, 2013**.

The call is open to universities, Party schools, academies of social sciences, social-science federations and local-culture research bases. Papers should address current work in Guangdong local-culture studies and follow academic standards. Previously published papers may also be submitted, provided that the journal and publication date are identified.

## Submission Requirements

- Approximately **10,000 Chinese characters** per paper;
- A **200–300-character abstract** and **three to five keywords**;
- Footnotes with superscript numbering restarted on each page;
- The author’s institution, academic or professional title, telephone number and email address;
- No limit on the number of papers recommended by an institution, but no more than two papers per author. An editorial group will make the final selection.
