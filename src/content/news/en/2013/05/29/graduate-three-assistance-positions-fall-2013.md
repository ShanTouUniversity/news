Shantou University’s Graduate School has begun arranging graduate “three-assistant” positions for the **fall 2013 semester** and has published quotas for each unit. The attachments list teaching-assistant and administrative-assistant positions, with **85 teaching-assistant positions and 49 administrative-assistant positions** across the university.

Units at the main campus must establish positions according to their needs and submit the Graduate Teaching Assistant (Administrative Assistant) Position Plan to the Graduate School by **June 12, 2013**, together with an electronic copy. The forms require position titles, quantities and duties. Teaching-assistant plans must also include the course, instructor, course type, student group, planned class size, total hours and number of assistants.
