Shantou University has issued pre-appointment publicity for the proposed appointment of **Cai Zhengzhong**, an associate professor in the Law School’s Department of Public Administration, as the department’s deputy head. The proposal was considered at the **seventh 2013 meeting of the university leadership on May 28**.

Under STU’s Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres (STU Party document No. 汕大党字〔2009〕47), units and individuals with comments may submit written feedback to the university’s Human Resources Office by **June 4**. Feedback from units must bear their official seal, while individuals must sign their names.

Feedback: `86503390`; `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
>
> ⚠️ Cai Zhengzhong’s résumé and other attachment materials are available in the original OA notice.

### Formal Appointment

Shantou University issued the formal appointment notice on **June 5, 2013**, appointing Cai Zhengzhong as deputy head of the Law School's Department of Public Administration for a six-month probationary period. The decision was considered at the **seventh 2013 meeting of the university leadership on May 28**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Document No. 汕大发〔2013〕71)
>
> ⚠️ Cai Zhengzhong’s résumé and other attachment materials are available in the original OA notice.
