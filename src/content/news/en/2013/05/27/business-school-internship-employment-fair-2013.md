STU Business School is working with Shantou Nanyue Human Resources Market to hold the “2013 STU Business School Student Internship and Employment Fair.” The event is intended to expand employment opportunities and help companies develop talent pipelines. The notice says that about **50 companies** from eastern Guangdong, including large and medium-sized private, foreign-invested, joint-stock and listed enterprises, will participate and offer employment and internship positions in basic management, clerical work and fields related to students’ majors.

## Internships and Positions

Internships are scheduled to begin in **early July 2013** and last three months. Companies may extend the placements according to business needs, will provide certain allowances during the internship, and may offer meals and accommodation. Outstanding interns may be offered regular employment.

The fair is intended for **some undergraduates from the 2009 cohort and undergraduates from the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts** in the Business School. Applicants were asked to bring a printed résumé and work proposal to the fair; student résumés, work proposals and application materials are not reproduced here.

## Event Arrangements

The notice gives the event time as **9:00 a.m.–12:00 noon on Saturday, June 1, 2012**, at the second floor of Shantou Nanyue Human Resources Market, Jinlong Building, 104 Jinsha East Road, Shantou. Participants were asked to meet at the university motto monument by 8:00 a.m. for group transport. The notice scheduled return buses at 10:00 and 11:00, with Route 6 also available.

Participants were asked to dress neatly, wear the Business School uniform and follow the arrangements and discipline. The notice title and publication year identify the event as a 2013 program, and the internship period is also stated as beginning in July 2013, but the event date remains “June 1, 2012.” This source conflict is retained rather than inferred away; readers should verify the final date with the Business School.
