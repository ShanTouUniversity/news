The STU Communist Youth League Committee has announced that university Youth League organizations, cadres and members received **Shantou city-level honors for 2012**, including Outstanding May Fourth Youth League Committee, Outstanding May Fourth Youth League Branch (General Branch), Outstanding Youth League Cadre and Outstanding Youth League Member awards. These results are distinct from STU's previously published internal awards for outstanding Youth League organizations and individuals in 2012.

## Results

| Honor | Number of STU recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding May Fourth Youth League Committee | 1 organization |
| Outstanding May Fourth Youth League Branch (General Branch) | 2 organizations |
| Outstanding Youth League Cadre | 2 people |
| Outstanding Youth League Member | 6 people |

The attachment lists the specific organizations and individuals. This article reports only the award categories and totals and does not reproduce student names or the personal roster. Affiliated units named in the original list include the Affiliated School, Medical College, Zhicheng College, Graduate School, Law School, Science College, School of Journalism and Business School.

The university Youth League Committee encouraged the awardees to value the honor and continue their work, and called on the university community to learn from the recognized groups and individuals and advance Youth League and student-affairs work through innovation.
