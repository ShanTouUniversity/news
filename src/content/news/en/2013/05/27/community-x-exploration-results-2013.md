It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 27, 2013, announcing the results of the Student Community X Program *Exploring Behind the City — People on the Chao Journey*.

Held successfully in May, the program evaluated teams on their activity planning and presentation, with awards determined accordingly. The list of awardees is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here for privacy. Congratulations to the winners and thanks to all faculty and students for their support and participation.
