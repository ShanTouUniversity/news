Shantou University has issued its 2013 continuing-education arrangements for professional and technical staff. The program covers faculty and staff members of the university and its affiliated units. Under the Guangdong regulations cited in the notice, professional and technical staff should complete at least **12 days or 72 hours of continuing education each year**. Continuing-education participation is an important condition in annual assessment, appointment, reappointment, promotion of professional and technical qualifications, and renewal of professional qualifications.

## Subjects and Hours

The program is divided into public, professional and elective subjects:

| Subject | Hours and arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Public subjects | 20 hours, organized by the university, followed by an open-book assessment |
| Professional subjects | At least 42 hours, delivered through concentrated training, thematic lectures, workshops and similar activities organized by schools, colleges and administrative units |
| Elective subjects | At least 12 hours, selected in consultation with the individual’s employer or through approved external providers |

A 2013 notice from the Shantou Human Resources and Social Security Bureau lists **Ecological Civilization in Guangdong in the New Era** and **Information-Technology Development and Information Security** as the year’s public subjects. Additional topics on professional ethics for teachers and professional conduct in medical institutions are available for education and health-sector staff. The centralized-training period is **May 1–August 31** for staff pursuing professional-qualification assessment and **May 1–December 31** for staff undergoing appointment or reappointment procedures.

The notice also provides for crediting certain achievements toward the annual 42-hour professional-subject requirement. A person who led a research project or served as first or corresponding author may receive credit for a patent, a published monograph, a completed provincial-level or higher vertical project, or a paper in a provincial-level or higher professional journal. Other contributors may receive credit equal to 50% of the minimum professional-subject requirement, subject to the competent authority’s review.

## Registration and Verification

Professional and technical staff may log into the university training website at <http://training.stu.edu.cn> to select courses according to their needs. After completing the training and receiving individual course certificates, they should log into the Guangdong Professional and Technical Personnel Continuing Education Management System to register and submit their learning records. Existing users do not need to register again. Records include hours, subject, format, start and end dates, assessment results and teaching provider; the personal user guide explains the process.

The Continuing Education Center conducts an initial review of public- and professional-subject records and issues a continuing-education certificate of participation. The human resources and social security authority then reviews the records and issues a continuing-education certificate bearing the seal of the provincial Human Resources and Social Security Department. Public-subject training follows the required fee standard, listed in the notice as **RMB 60 per person**; professional courses offered by the university are free, while public-subject textbooks are ordered by the Continuing Education Center. Campus personnel use the entrusted-payment method specified by the university training system; no individual salary or payment information is reproduced here.

The attachments include the Guangdong regulations, the Shantou 2013 public- and professional-subject training notice, an individual course-registration process, the university’s own course schedule and a personal guide to the provincial management system. Details of the university’s own course schedule should be checked in the original OA attachment.
