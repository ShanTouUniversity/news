Zhicheng College has announced the results of its recruitment for student mentors for the 2013–2014 academic year. Following open recruitment, college assessment and interviews, the college selected **12 students**, including alternates, as prospective student mentors. The detailed student list is in the OA attachment and is not reproduced here.

Prospective mentors should report to **Room 204, E Staircase Building, at 1:50 p.m. on Sunday, June 2** for mentor training and the Zhicheng College annual review meeting for the 2012–2013 mentor work year.
