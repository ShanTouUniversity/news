The STU Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association have opened recruitment for the 2013 summer cultural, scientific and health service program, commonly known as the “Three Rural Areas” social-practice program. The service trip is planned for **mid-July** in **Dongshan County, Zhangzhou, Fujian**. Students in the 2012 cohort will take part after military training, and the university plans to select **50 volunteers** through interviews.

Applications close at **12:00 noon on May 31**. Interview registration and the interview time and venue will be announced separately.

## Service Teams

The activity-team introduction lists six service teams:

| Team | Main activities |
| --- | --- |
| Teaching | Short courses in cultural subjects, music, dance, arts and crafts; legal and self-protection education; and paired exchanges with local minors |
| Science and Technology | Free electrical-equipment inspection and repair for local residents, science-popularization activities and support for basic camp facilities |
| Medical | Visits to vulnerable local residents, health consultations, physical checks and health-awareness activities |
| Social Survey | Research on local agriculture, enterprises, industrial structure and compulsory education, followed by a written report |
| Publicity and Interviews | Documenting the service work, supporting publicity and producing related materials |
| Arts and Entertainment | Supporting teaching and arts education, organizing reading activities and staging performances |

Volunteer hours will be recorded under the registered-volunteer management rules according to the actual number of service days. Since 2011, the “Three Rural Areas” service program has counted as a public-service course. Students who complete the course and pass its assessment may receive **one credit** in the philosophy and social sciences category of the general-education curriculum.

Further information is available on STU’s “Three Rural Areas” blog at <http://blog.sina.com.cn/u/1779192437> and through the `@STU Youth Volunteers Association` Weibo account. Personal information requested by the application form, including birthplace, dormitory, student number, phone number, email address, personal skills and experience, is not published here. Application and interview arrangements should be taken from the original OA notice and attachments.

The final contact paragraph in the notice refers to the activity as STU’s 2012 summer “Three Rural Areas” practice, while the title, main body and attachments concern the 2013 program. This article follows the 2013 date supported by the main notice and attachments.

## First-Round Interview Arrangements

The Youth Volunteer Association and the Communist Youth League Committee issued a follow-up notice on **May 31, 2013**. The first-round interviews will be held on **Sunday, June 2**, in **Classroom D207**. Applicants must attend in the batches listed in the attachment and check in **10 minutes** before their assigned interview slot. On-site registration will also be accepted, and applications will remain open until noon on June 2.

Each applicant will give a **one-minute self-introduction**. Interviewers will ask questions based on the service team selected by the applicant, focusing on relevant knowledge and abilities. Arts and Entertainment applicants may bring instruments or accompaniment music, with performances limited to **one to two minutes**. The second-round Medical Team interview will include practical tasks such as blood-pressure measurement and bandaging. Applicants for the Publicity and Interviews Team may describe experience with image-editing software such as Adobe Photoshop or Adobe Illustrator, or with hand-drawn posters.

After the first round, the list of applicants advancing to the second round will be posted in the Youth Volunteer section of the BBS. The second-round interviews will be arranged separately by the service-team captains. The individual time-slot schedule and application form contain names, cohort and major information, student numbers, contact details and experience records; specific lists and submission addresses are not reproduced here.

### Volunteer List Announced

The STU Communist Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteer Association announced the selection results on **June 6, 2013**. Recruitment concluded through open interviews, democratic review and organizational recommendations. A total of **50 volunteers** were selected for the Teaching, Science and Technology, Medical, Social Survey, Publicity and Interviews, and Arts and Entertainment teams. Further arrangements would be announced separately.
