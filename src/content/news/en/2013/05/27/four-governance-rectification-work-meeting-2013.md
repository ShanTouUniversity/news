Shantou University will hold a **“Four-Part Governance” work meeting at 3:00 p.m. on May 28** in **Room 213 of the New Administration Center** to brief university units on the relevant work. The meeting follows the citywide campaign addressing **mediocrity, laziness, laxity and extravagance** and the implementation plan for building an integrity-focused Shantou.

Each university unit should send one person in charge to attend. The notice lists `86502346` as the contact telephone number.
