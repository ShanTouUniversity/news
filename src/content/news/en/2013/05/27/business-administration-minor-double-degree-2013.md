The Business Administration program at Shantou University Business School is accepting applications from **full-time undergraduates in the 2012 cohort** for a Business Administration minor or double degree. The program plans to admit **20 students**. Applications open with the notice and close on **September 12, 2013**.

## Eligibility and Selection

Applicants must have a first-year GPA of **2.7 or above** in their major courses and sufficient capacity for additional study. If applications exceed 20 places, applicants will be ranked by GPA from highest to lowest; strong foundations in English and mathematics will receive priority.

Applicants must complete the STU application form for a double degree or minor. After approval, they may complete course registration during the first week of the fall semester. Under the academic-affairs rules cited in the notice, students in the top 20 percent of their class who have passed all completed courses and have a GPA above 3 may register for up to **28 credits**.

## Curriculum and Graduation Requirements

The Business School curriculum includes management, economics, accounting, finance, statistics, marketing, organizational behavior, human resources, operations, strategy, logistics and supply-chain management, as well as management information systems, customer relationship management, project management, service-management courses and a graduation thesis. The curriculum plan lists **62 credits**, including a six-credit thesis; the 45-credit double-degree requirement excludes the thesis.

| Program | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Minor | At least 30 credits |
| Double degree | At least 45 credits plus a graduation thesis; the thesis is excluded from the 45 credits |
| Course fees | Course credits multiplied by RMB 100 per course; payment is made during registration |

Courses already completed in a student's major that duplicate courses in the minor or double-degree plan do not need to be repeated; students must take other courses in the plan to make up the credits. Certificates and study arrangements follow STU's implementation measures for undergraduate minors and double degrees.

## Submission

Applications must be submitted to the Business School Academic Affairs Office in **Room 204, Wenxi Building**. The notice identifies **Yang Min** as the campus contact, at `86502277`. Personal application materials such as application forms, course-registration forms and transcripts are not reproduced here.
