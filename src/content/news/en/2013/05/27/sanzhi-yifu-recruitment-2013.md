It is reported that the Department of Human Resources and Social Security of Guangdong Province has issued the Notice on Organizing Registration for the 2013 Guangdong College Graduates “San Zhi Yi Fu” Program, officially launching the 2013 plan to recruit graduates to work at rural grassroots level in education, agriculture, healthcare and poverty alleviation. The STU Student Affairs Office forwarded the notice on May 27 to organize registration.

## Program Overview

“San Zhi Yi Fu” refers to recruiting college graduates to serve at rural grassroots level in teaching, farming, medical care and poverty alleviation. Details are available on the official recruitment website for Guangdong 2013 “San Zhi Yi Fu”.

## Registration Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target | 2013 graduates of regular higher education institutions |
| Registration period | May 15 – May 31 |
| Registration method | Log in to the Guangdong “San Zhi Yi Fu” recruitment website http://www.szyf.org.cn |
| Campus submission deadline | Before June 4, 2013 |
| Submission location | Graduate Employment Guidance Center, Student Affairs Office (New Administration Building 106) |
| Requirements | Prepare materials as required by the official document (see http://www.szyf.org.cn/NewsInfo.asp?id=47); the university will centrally arrange registration |

The Student Affairs Office reminds interested graduates to complete online registration within the specified period and submit materials on time; late submissions will not be accepted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
