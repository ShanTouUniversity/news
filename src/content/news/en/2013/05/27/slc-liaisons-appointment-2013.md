It is reported that the Mutual Learning Center (SLC) of the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 27, 2013, announcing the interview results for liaisons for the 2013–2014 academic year.

Based on the requirements of the liaison positions and interview performance, 12 students were selected. To protect student privacy, the full list is withheld here and has been anonymized.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number selected | 12 |
| Colleges covered | College of Engineering (Mechatronics, Electronics, Computer, Civil), School of Science (Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Physics), School of Journalism, School of Art and Design, College of Liberal Arts |
| Academic year | 2013–2014 |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office, Mutual Learning Center |

The center thanked all applicants for their support. The full list is available on the OA system.
