STU Library has begun collecting recommendations for its **2014 foreign-language print-journal subscriptions**. Faculty and postgraduate students across the university are invited to recommend renewals and new subscriptions. Schools and colleges are asked to organize recommendations according to teaching and research needs and their disciplines' literature-support requirements.

## Recommendation Forms

The attachment has two sections:

- **Renewal recommendations:** A list of foreign-language periodicals subscribed to by the library in 2013, with a field for readers to indicate whether each title should be renewed for 2014. The list covers humanities and social sciences, business and economics, law, languages and literature, arts, mathematics, natural sciences, industrial technology and comprehensive subjects.
- **New subscriptions:** A form for recommending foreign-language print journals not included in the existing list, with fields for the title, original publication number and ISSN.

Prices in the form are for reference only. A note states that foreign-language print-journal prices increase by an average of approximately **7% per year**. The forms also request the recommender's identity and telephone number; personal submission fields are not reproduced here.

## Feedback

Faculty members and postgraduate students should send their recommendations to the library's subject-services department by **June 8**. Because print-journal subscriptions operate on a tight schedule, the library encourages readers to respond early.
