The STU Office for Campus Culture Development, also known as the Sanfeng Office, has released Academic Conduct Inspection Report No. **233**, covering week 13 of the 2012–2013 spring semester. Inspections were conducted in **Building E** on **May 20 and 21**, recording **four late arrivals** in total.

## Inspection Results

| Inspection time | Venue | Late arrivals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8:00 a.m., Monday, May 20, 2013 | Building E | Three students more than five minutes late |
| 2:00 p.m., Tuesday, May 21, 2013 | Building E | One student late by five minutes or less |

The report asks the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide guidance to students who did not observe university rules and to continue strengthening academic conduct. The attachment contains the relevant student list, which is not reproduced here along with any student personal information.
