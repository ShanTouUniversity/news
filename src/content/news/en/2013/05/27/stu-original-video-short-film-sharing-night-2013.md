The first STU Original Short-Video Sharing Night will be held from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on May 31, 2013**, at the **Library Student Activity Hall**. Under the theme “The Same Campus, Different Lives,” the event is open to students, faculty and staff. It is intended to enrich campus life, encourage exchange among original-video creators and support artistic appreciation and expression through campus video works.

## Submissions

The organizers are inviting original short videos created by members of the Shantou University community. Videos must be no longer than **20 minutes** and may take the form of narrative shorts, music videos, entertainment programs or original animation. Submissions close at **midnight on May 29**. Submitted works will be screened, and selected works will be shown during the sharing night.

The attached work-description form asks for the work title, creator, contact information, content summary and the work's theme or meaning. Selected creators will receive a set of hand-drawn pen postcards after the event. The form and its creators' contact details are not reproduced here; submission instructions should be taken from the original OA notice and attachment.

## Awards

The event will offer a “Most Popular Work” award and several participation awards, with corresponding prizes.

One organization name in the notice body is garbled. This article does not attempt to reconstruct it. The notice was issued by the STU Communist Youth League Committee.
