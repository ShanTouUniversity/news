The second lecture in STU's 2013 General Education Forum series will be held at **7:30 p.m. on Monday, May 13** in the Library Lecture Hall. Researcher Li Xiangping will speak on **“The Power of Imagination and Writing: Australia, Hong Kong, Taiwan Culture and Chinese Modernity.”** The lecture is organized by the Institute of Higher Education and the Center for Christian Studies in the College of Liberal Arts.

The lecture will examine imagination and writing as inner spiritual forces that can free people from the pressures of external forces such as money and power. It will consider how cultural imagination and writing in Macao, Hong Kong and Taiwan have influenced Chinese modernity, tracing the path from the early development of Chinese modernity to its vigorous growth and exploring the internal character of the three regions' cultures.

### Speaker profile

Li Xiangping is a researcher at the Institute of Literature of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, director of its Research Office for Literature and Culture of Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao, deputy editor-in-chief of *Literary Review*, professor and doctoral supervisor at the Graduate School of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, and vice president of the Chinese World Chinese-Language Literature Association. Her research focuses on modern and contemporary Chinese literature, literary theory, and literature and literary theory from Taiwan and Hong Kong. She has served as a visiting professor at Tsinghua University and Chung Hsing University in Taiwan, Ruhr University in Germany, Hitotsubashi University in Japan and Chosun University in South Korea. Her books include *The Melancholy of Taiwan* and *Literary Taiwan*.
