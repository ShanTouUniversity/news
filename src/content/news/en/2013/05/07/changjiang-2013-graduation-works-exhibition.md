Changjiang College of Arts and Design will hold its 2013 graduation works exhibition in four stages from **May 4 to 29**. The exhibition features the college's 2009 undergraduate cohorts and 2010 graduate cohort. Venues include the college's Art Museum, first-floor exhibition hall, Glass House 789, and campus and college public spaces.

### Exhibition schedule

| Stage | Classes | Dates | Venues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stage 1 | 2009 Product Design, Interface and Interaction Design, and Video and Animation Design | May 4–8 | Art Museum (Product Design), first-floor exhibition hall (Interface and Interaction), Glass House 789 (Video and Animation) |
| Stage 2 | 2009 Graphic Design and Public Art | May 11–15 | Art Museum; first-floor exhibition hall (Graphic Design); campus and college public spaces (Public Art) |
| Stage 3 | 2009 Architecture and Landscape Design and Interior Design | May 18–22 | Art Museum and first-floor exhibition hall |
| Stage 4 | 2010 graduate cohort | May 25–29 | Art Museum and first-floor exhibition hall |

Wang Shouzhi is the exhibition consultant; Han Ran, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Wu Xiangyong, Li Haoyu and Yang Hua are exhibition advisors; and Li Aiyi is exhibition director. Organized by Changjiang College of Arts and Design, the exhibition welcomes faculty members and students to visit during the relevant stages.

The original notice also lists the full exhibition-coordination, advisory and visual-design teams and provides information about the graduation works. Those materials contain student and staff information and are not reproduced here.
