STU Business School has established an MBA assessment working group to support the teaching-compliance assessment organized by the National MBA Education Steering Committee and to advance related MBA work. The document is **STU Business School Issuance No. 08 of 2013**. It is dated **May 6** in the formal document, while the OA archive page is dated **May 7**.

### Working group

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Leader | Xu Zongling |
| Members | Ouyang Feng, Chen Yinglin, Wang * (the source name is corrupted), Xie Shunlong, Lin Jiali, Zeng Qiang, Huang Wangming, Song Xiaobao, Qin Zhong, Ji Yun, Huang Xianglong, Zhang Xiaoxiao, Li Xiujuan, Yin Xiaoqing and Deng Lizhen |
| Secretaries | Yin Xiaoqing and Deng Lizhen |

The working group will coordinate the assessment-related work through STU Business School and the STU MBA Education Center. The document was sent to the STU MBA Education Working Committee and copied to the Graduate School. One member's name is corrupted in the source, and no reconstruction is attempted here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School)
