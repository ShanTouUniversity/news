To mark the 20th anniversary of STU's Campus Top Ten Singers competition, the Youth League Committee and Student Union will hold a special anniversary concert at **7:30 p.m. on Saturday, May 11** in the Great Hall. Ten former Campus Top Ten Singers will be invited to perform, giving the university community an opportunity to enjoy and exchange around campus music.

### Concert details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., Saturday, May 11, 2013 |
| Location | Great Hall |
| Performers | 10 former Campus Top Ten Singers |
| Admission | Ticket required; no assigned seating, first come, first seated |

The event will issue admission tickets through a charitable-donation arrangement. A donation of **at least 3 yuan** will qualify for one ticket, with no change provided. All proceeds will go to the Firefly Fund of the STU Youth Volunteers Association.

### Ticket channels

| Channel | Time and location |
| --- | --- |
| Weibo reservation | May 7–9 through `@汕头大学十大歌手`; reserved tickets will be distributed from 10:00 to 11:30 p.m. each evening from May 8 to 10 |
| SMS reservation | May 7–9 through the SMS channel specified in the original notice; reserved tickets will be distributed from 10:00 to 11:30 p.m. each evening from May 8 to 10 |
| On-site collection | May 8–10, 11:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m., at collection points in the Golden Triangle and Cafeteria 4, where donations will be accepted and tickets issued |

Weibo and SMS reservations require personal reservation details. This article does not reproduce names, dormitory numbers, telephone numbers or other personal information. Further details are available through `@汕头大学学生会` and the Student Union website: <http://stuu.stu.edu.cn/stuu2012/>.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
