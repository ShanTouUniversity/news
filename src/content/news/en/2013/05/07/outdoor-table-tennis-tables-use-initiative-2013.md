STU's Sports Department has issued an initiative concerning the outdoor table-tennis tables recently installed on the field beside the E and F student residence halls. The department is calling on faculty members and students to help keep the public sports facilities available for shared use.

### Use initiative

Faculty members and students are asked to use the tables in a civilized and orderly manner and avoid occupying them for long periods, so that more members of the university community can use them. The source notice contains only this readable requirement and begins its numbering at “2,” without providing the content of item “1.” This article reports only the verifiable information.
