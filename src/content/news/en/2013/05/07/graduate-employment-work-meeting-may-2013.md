STU Student Affairs held a graduate-employment work meeting at **4:30 p.m. on Tuesday, May 7** in New Administration Center Room 213 to coordinate graduate-employment work across the university.

### Meeting details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 4:30 p.m., Tuesday, May 7, 2013 |
| Location | New Administration Center Room 213 |
| Participants | Party general-branch secretaries or deputy secretaries from the schools and colleges, counselors responsible for employment, and staff from the Student Affairs Graduate Employment Guidance Center |

The meeting was intended for relevant university employment offices and personnel, who were asked to attend on time. The notice does not provide an agenda or graduate-employment data, and no additional details are inferred here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
