The Organization and United Front Department of the STU Party Committee has published the registration and course schedule for the 72nd Party basic-knowledge study class. The class is scheduled to begin on **May 12**. The attached list contains **121 participants** divided into nine groups, with courses scheduled through June 2.

### Registration

Participants were required to register with their group leaders from **May 9 to 10**, submit one frontal passport-style photograph and collect study materials. Group leaders were scheduled to meet at 1:00 p.m. on May 9 in Wenbei Room 206. Failure to register without a valid reason was treated as withdrawal from the class.

### Course schedule

| Date | Time and location | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 12 | 8:55 a.m., 10:40 a.m., 2:55 p.m. and 7:30 p.m., Lecture Hall 3 | Party-conduct development; *Forging* video, Part 1; duties and rights of Party members; the nature and mission of the Communist Party of China |
| May 19 | 8:55 a.m. and 10:40 a.m., Lecture Hall 1; 2:55 p.m. and 7:30 p.m., Lecture Hall 3 | Correcting the motivation to join the Party; *Forging* video, Part 2; the Party's organizational system and discipline; studying the Scientific Outlook on Development in depth |
| May 26 | 8:55 a.m. and 2:55 p.m., Lecture Hall 3; evening, group-designated locations | The Party's guiding ideology and university students; exchange with veteran members of the university's Committee for the Care of the Next Generation; group discussion |
| June 2 | Self-study; 7:30–9:00 p.m., Lecture Halls 1 and 3 | Self-study of *The Constitution of the Communist Party of China* and other materials; theoretical examination |

Written assignments were due on May 19, while the study summary and Participant Registration Form were due on May 26. Unexcused absences or failure to take the examination, or approved leave exceeding one-third of the scheduled teaching hours, meant that no completion certificate would be issued. Final results combined the theory examination, assignments and study summary, discussion, study attitude, Party conduct and attendance.

The class lists and the group leaders' names and telephone numbers contain personal information and are not reproduced here.
