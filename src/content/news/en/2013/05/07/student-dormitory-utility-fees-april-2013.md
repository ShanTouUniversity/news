STU's Resource Management Office has published the April utility bills for student residences. The latest meter reading was taken on **April 30**, following the previous reading on **March 31**. Students may log in to the STU utility-management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/> using their campus-network accounts to review their individual bills.

### Review and payment schedule

| Stage | Time or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Bill review and objections | Working hours, May 7–13 |
| Campus-card deductions begin | May 14 |
| Normal payment period | May 14–17 |
| Overdue handling | Under university rules from May 20 |

Students who identify an error or have a question may complete and sign the Utility-Bill Review Application Form and submit it to the Water and Electricity Center during the review period. The center is beside the Science and Technology Building, telephone `86502489`. Questions about a mismatch between residence information and student records may be directed to the Counselor's Office in Room B-106, telephone `86503355`.

The Network and Information Center will process deductions through campus cards; its telephone number is `86503545`. Students whose campus-card balances are insufficient should top up their cards promptly. Objections raised after the review period that require a correction after verification may take three to five working days to process.

The utility attachment contains individual bills and residence information. This article does not reproduce amounts, residence details or student data.
