The STU Student Affairs Office has arranged a workplace-learning visit to Guangdong Baicheng Human Resources Co., Ltd. on **May 8** for four classes in the courses Employment Guidance and Career Counseling and Training. The visit is for all students in the following classes:

| Course | Class numbers |
| --- | --- |
| Employment Guidance | 59587 and 59591 |
| Career Counseling and Training | 59588 and 59590 |

### Meeting arrangements

| Item | Time or location |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting time | 2:50 p.m., May 8 |
| Meeting point | University Motto Monument |
| Host company | Guangdong Baicheng Human Resources Co., Ltd. |
| Return time | 5:20 p.m., May 8 |

Students in the relevant classes are asked to share the notice and arrive at the University Motto Monument on time. The notice does not provide further details of the visit program, and no additional arrangements are inferred here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
