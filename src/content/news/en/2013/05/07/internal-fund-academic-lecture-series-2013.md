It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University, together with relevant colleges, will hold a series of academic lectures under the 2013 internal funding programs from May 8 to June 5. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

According to the university's regulations on Academic Innovation Team Building and National Fund Incubation Projects, Liberal Arts Research Funds, Youth Research Funds, and Research Start-up Funds, funded projects are required to conduct open academic exchanges during their implementation period. For Academic Innovation Team projects, the principal investigator or a core member is expected to deliver at least one university-wide lecture each year on the research topic. For projects funded by the Liberal Arts Research Fund, Youth Research Fund, National Fund Incubation Program, and Research Start-up Fund, the principal investigator is required to hold one public lecture on the project theme during the funding period.

The upcoming lecture series is organized to fulfill these requirements, showcase interim research outcomes, and provide a cross-disciplinary platform for exchange and innovation. Detailed schedules, speakers, and topics are available in the attached arrangements.
