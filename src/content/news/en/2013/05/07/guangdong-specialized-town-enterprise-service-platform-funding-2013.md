Guangdong has released the application notice and guidelines for the fifth batch of its 2013 provincial science and technology plan, covering a special program for **service platforms for small and micro enterprises in specialized towns**. STU's Research Office has forwarded the notice and asks interested faculty members to review the requirements and contact the office by **May 10**.

### Application topics

| Topic | Direction | Code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Topic 1 | Capacity improvement and functional development of specialized-town productivity-promotion centers | 0405 |
| Topic 2 | Technology-innovation service platforms | 0923 |
| Topic 3 | Inspection and testing service platforms | 0924 |
| Topic 4 | E-commerce and information-network service platforms | 0925 |
| Topic 5 | Modern service-product marketplaces in specialized towns | 0406 |
| Topic 6, Subtopic 1 | Innovation and development of service platforms for small and micro enterprises in specialized towns | 0926 |
| Topic 6, Subtopic 2 | Construction and management of specialized-town productivity-promotion centers | 0407 |

Application conditions, materials and procedures are governed by the notice and guidelines issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology: <http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/1367139794897-1799198799687877936.html>.

The university contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, telephone `86503271`. This article summarizes the public topic information and university contact channel and does not replace the formal application guidelines.
