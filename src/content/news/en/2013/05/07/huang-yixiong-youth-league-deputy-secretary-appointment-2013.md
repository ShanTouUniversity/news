The Communist Party of China Shantou University Committee has appointed **Huang Yixiong** deputy secretary of the STU Youth League Committee for a six-month probationary period. The decision appears in document No. 汕大党字〔2013〕第 16 号 and followed deliberation at the fifth 2013 meeting of the university Party Committee Standing Committee. The document is dated **May 7, 2013**.

Huang previously worked on student affairs and served as director of the School of Engineering Office at STU. The related pre-appointment publicity was issued on April 24, with the publicity period ending May 2. The formal appointment corresponds to the position described in that earlier notice.

The document was sent to all STU units and copied to the Organization Department of the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee and the Shantou Municipal Human Resources Bureau.
