STU has announced the results of its 2013 Advanced Undergraduate Education Teaching Reform Program, approving **172 projects**. The program follows the university's Comprehensive Reform Pilot Outline for Independent University Operation (2009–2017). Applications were organized by the university's units and reviewed by the Office of Academic Affairs to advance the goals of advanced undergraduate education.

### Project categories

| Category | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Professional comprehensive reform pilots | 9 |
| Featured majors | 8 |
| Experimental zones for innovation in talent-development models | 2 |
| Experimental teaching demonstration centers | 5 |
| Undergraduate practical-teaching bases | 7 |
| Teaching teams | 11 |
| High-quality video open course | 1 |
| High-quality resource-sharing courses | 20 |
| Teaching-reform research projects | 109 |
| **Total** | **172** |

### Funding and management

The first installment of funding will be allocated to the relevant teaching-reform units, including schools, departments and centers, and will account for 50 percent of the total funding. The Office of Academic Affairs has proposed recommended funding based on project category and review results, while also providing each unit with a discretionary matching allocation. The overall teaching-reform project leader in each unit may redistribute funding among its projects according to the unit's work priorities before final allocations are set.

Approved projects will be managed as university-level teaching-reform projects and will undergo interim reviews and final acceptance. The Office of Academic Affairs will organize reviews and acceptance work according to the different research periods. Projects that pass an interim review may receive the second funding installment.

Project leaders are expected to implement their promised outcomes in line with the university's 2013 advanced undergraduate education priorities. The notice highlights integrated thinking, adaptable professional training, integrated professional training, quality assurance and continuous improvement.
