The third College of Liberal Arts Academic Salon will be held from **2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on May 8, 2013** in ACC Room 3. The topic is **“Academic Freedom and Internationalization.”**

### Salon details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Academic Freedom and Internationalization |
| Time | 2:30–5:30 p.m., May 8, 2013 |
| Location | ACC Room 3 |
| Speakers | Associate Professor Peng Zhiheng and Associate Professor James McDougall |
| Host | Professor Li Jie, vice dean of the College of Liberal Arts |

Peng Zhiheng is an associate professor in the Department of Chinese Language and Literature, and James McDougall is an associate professor at the Center for International Studies, both within the College of Liberal Arts. Professor Li Jie, the college's vice dean, will host the academic exchange.
