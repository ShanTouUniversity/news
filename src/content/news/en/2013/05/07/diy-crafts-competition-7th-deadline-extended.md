It is reported that the registration deadline for the 7th "Hands-on DIY" competition at Shantou University has been extended to midnight on May 11 to give students more time to prepare their entries.

The extension was jointly announced on May 7 by the Communist Youth League Committee and the Science and Technology Association of Shantou University. Details about the competition are available in the *Notice on Holding the 7th Hands-on DIY Competition* published by the Youth League Committee on the campus office information network on April 27.

Interested students are encouraged to complete their registration and prepare their works before the new deadline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
