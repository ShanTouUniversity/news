STU Business School's **Transformative Talent Show** will hold its preliminary round on **May 11** and final round on May 19. The event is open to all STU students and uses the theme “Transformative Change” and the slogan “Stars in Motion, Art Reaches the Sky.” Participants may enter individually or as teams.

Participants may download the registration form through the campus office-information network or the official Weibo account `@汕头大学商学院才艺汇` and email it to `12klguo@stu.edu.cn` by the deadline. A paper form may also be submitted at the locations specified in the original notice. Registration through direct messages to the official Weibo account is also available. Names, student numbers, contact details, dormitory information and participant lists from the forms are not reproduced here.

### Competition format

The preliminary round uses a parallel-elimination format. Each entrant or team will complete the prompt “Because of transformation, I am _____” and present a talent performance of approximately three minutes, explaining the emotion or state expressed and its positive effect on their future plans. Performances should generally not exceed eight minutes. Eight judges will score the entrants and select the top 10 groups. If the final qualifying place is tied, the tied entrants will give one-minute performances in order, after which the judges will select the qualifier.

The first round of the final uses the theme “Transformative Change: Vitality.” The 10 groups will combine a randomly assigned STU landmark with the theme in an approximately five-minute performance, generally no longer than eight minutes. The top three by score will advance directly to the second round, while places four through seven will enter a revival round. Each revival-round entrant will have one minute to solicit votes, after which the audience will vote. The audience score will be calculated as “votes ÷ 50 × 100”; the scores from the revival round and first round will be combined, and the top two will advance.

The second round uses the partially open prompt “Transformative Change: _____.” Entrants will complete the theme and present a performance of no more than 10 minutes. Judges will determine the gold, silver and bronze awards by score. If the bronze-award score is tied, the judges will compare the criteria in sequence and confer if the tie remains.

The 10 STU landmarks named for the first final round are the eight-character motto at the University Motto Monument; “strive for excellence” at the Library; sweat on the athletics field; student clubs in motion on the Pedestrian Street; late-night conversations at the Reservoir; dreams setting sail at the Entrepreneurship Park; photographs and laughter beneath the phoenix trees; dancing at the Old Administrative Building; reading beside the Lotus Pond; and singing in front of the Great Hall.
