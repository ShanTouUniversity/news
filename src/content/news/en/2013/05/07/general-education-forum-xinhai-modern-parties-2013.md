The third lecture in STU's 2013 General Education Forum series will be held at **3:00 p.m. on Wednesday, May 15** in the Library Lecture Hall. Dr. Wu Xianning will speak on **“The Xinhai Revolution and Modern Chinese Political Parties.”** The lecture is organized by the Institute of Higher Education and the Center for Christian Studies in the College of Liberal Arts.

### Lecture details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The Xinhai Revolution and Modern Chinese Political Parties |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., Wednesday, May 15, 2013 |
| Location | Library Lecture Hall |
| Speaker | Dr. Wu Xianning |
| Organizers | Institute of Higher Education and Center for Christian Studies, College of Liberal Arts |
| Course arrangement | Also part of the continuing-education course “Public Lectures of the Center for Christian Studies in the College of Liberal Arts”; enrolled students must attend and sign in |

The lecture will examine the Xinhai Revolution's influence on China's modernization, the meaning of “modern” in modern political parties, the role of modern parties in the revolution's success, and the lessons from the rise of modern parties after the revolution. It will also discuss the historical and dialectical relationship between China's contemporary party system and political-party activities after the Xinhai Revolution.

Wu is a scholar, a doctor of literature and a member of a democratic party. He serves as a member of the Standing Committee and minister of the Publicity Department of the Revolutionary Committee of the Chinese Kuomintang, as well as executive council member of the Chinese Society for Studies of the Xinhai Revolution, secretary-general of the Sun Yat-sen Studies Association of the Revolutionary Committee of the Chinese Kuomintang, and executive council member of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference Theory Research Association. He is also a specially appointed professor at the Central Institute of Socialism and an adjunct professor at Shanghai Normal University. He served as a member of the 11th and 12th National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference and a member of its Proposals Committee. His research covers Sun Yat-sen and the Xinhai Revolution, consultative politics and multiparty cooperation, the theory of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, and ancient Chinese culture, literature and traditional culture.
