The STU Student Affairs Office will hold professional-image training for university students from **2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on May 8** in Room D304. The session is open to all STU faculty members and students and will feature Li Kexian and Sa Zhihui on professional image and public-relations etiquette.

### Training program

| Speaker | Topic or content |
| --- | --- |
| Li Kexian | “Building a Workplace Image”: workplace dress standards, creating an appealing professional image and a basic makeup demonstration |
| Sa Zhihui | Public-relations etiquette |

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 2:30–4:30 p.m., May 8, 2013 |
| Location | Room D304 |
| Organizer | STU Student Affairs Office |
| Audience | All STU faculty members and students |

Li Kexian graduated in fashion design from Shanghai China Textile University in 1992. She joined STU's Art Department at the end of 1993 and has taught foundational courses while pursuing design work. Her articles and works have appeared in publications including *装饰* and *江苏画刊*, and she published *Image and Mind: A New Perspective Sketching Training Course*.

Sa Zhihui teaches Public Relations, Public-Relations Planning, Public-Relations Social Etiquette, Foreign Cultural Knowledge and Chinese Government Public Relations. He serves as a council member of the Public-Relations Professional Committee of the Chinese Society of Education and the Guangdong Public Relations Association. His research areas include government public relations, interpersonal relations, and the skills and art of language communication.
