The Ministry of Education’s Department of Ideological and Political Work launched development of an Ideological and Political Education Research Library to publish outstanding books on the theory and practice of ideological and political education for university students. STU’s Research Office forwarded the call for submissions to in-service faculty members and administrators.

## Submission Requirements

Eligible works were Chinese academic books, either single-author monographs or co-authored works, with no restriction on discipline and encouragement for interdisciplinary research. Translations, software and other formats were excluded. Works were expected to meet publication standards, uphold the required political and academic orientation, have substantial academic and practical value, follow academic norms and demonstrate innovation.

The call encouraged research into frontier issues in the innovation and development of student ideological and political education from perspectives including fostering virtue through education, educational modernization, interdisciplinary study, cultivation of top innovative talent and the scientific development of ideological and political education. Each work, including charts and tables, was generally limited to 250,000 Chinese characters.

## Funding and Submission Process

Works selected for the funding plan after review would receive full publication funding and be published by People’s Publishing House. The funding would be paid directly to the contracted publishing institution rather than to the author’s institution or the author. The publisher would provide the author with 30 complimentary copies and generally would not pay royalties. Funded books would carry the relevant program attribution on the cover.

| Stage | Deadline and requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Form review | Send the application form to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` by August 30 |
| Materials submission | Submit two copies each of the qualified form and printed manuscript to the Research Office by September 2 |
| Submission quota | Guangdong Province could submit five books |
| Publication | The publisher would handle publication with authors under relevant regulations; authors would provide electronic manuscripts as requested |

STU contacts were Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun of the Research Office at `0754-86502563`. Applicants were to use the form in the notice attachments. This report does not reproduce applicants’ personal information or complete manuscripts.
