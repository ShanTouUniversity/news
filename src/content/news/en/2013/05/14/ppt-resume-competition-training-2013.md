The STU Student Affairs Office will hold pre-competition training for the second PPT electronic-resume competition to help participants develop résumé content and PPT-production skills. The training is scheduled for **3–5 p.m. on May 15, 2013**, in **E Lecture Room 202**. Su Junzhi and Wu Chao will serve as the trainers.

Students who have registered for the competition are asked to attend on time, and other students are welcome to join. This notice provides the specific time and venue for the “training and guidance” stage announced in the competition notice published on May 10.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Training | Pre-competition training for the second PPT Electronic-Resume Competition |
| Time | 3–5 p.m., Wednesday, May 15, 2013 |
| Venue | E Lecture Room 202 |
| Trainers | Su Junzhi and Wu Chao |
| Topics | Résumé content and PPT techniques |
| Participants | Registered competitors; other students are welcome |
| Organizer | STU Student Affairs Office |
