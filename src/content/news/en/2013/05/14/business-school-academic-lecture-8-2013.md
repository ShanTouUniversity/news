It is reported that the Business School issued a notice on May 14, 2013, that the 8th Academic Lecture of 2013 will be held on May 17.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 09:00–12:00, May 17, 2013 |
| Venue | Academic Seminar Room, 2nd Floor, Business School |
| Organizer | Business School |

## Topics and Speakers

| Speaker | Title |
| --- | --- |
| Liang Qiang | Selectively Performing Corporate Social Responsibility Issues: A Case of Entrepreneurial Firms in China |
| Long Yuee | Changes in Accounting Standards, Earnings Management and CPA Assurance Power |
| Zhang Ling | Does High-Quality Auditing Affect the Usefulness of Debt Contracts? |

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
