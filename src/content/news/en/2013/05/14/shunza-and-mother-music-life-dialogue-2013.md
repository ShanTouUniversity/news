Shantou University will host “Coming Home: Shunza and Her Mother Talk About Life” on May 15. European-based pianist Huang Ailian, Ni Qingqing, associate dean of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, and singer Shunza will meet at STU for a conversation on music, life and family.

The event was scheduled for 7:30 p.m. on Wednesday, May 15, in the STU Library Lecture Hall and was open to the university community.
