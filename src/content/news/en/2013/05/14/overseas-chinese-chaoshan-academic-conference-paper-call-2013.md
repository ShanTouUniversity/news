To support the integrated development of Shantou, Chaozhou and Jieyang, the Publicity Department of the Shantou Municipal Committee and the Shantou Federation of Social Sciences planned an academic conference on “Overseas Chinese and Chaoshan” for the second half of 2013. The conference organizers invited submissions from the public. STU’s Research Office forwarded the call to relevant university units and researchers.

## Suggested Topics

Chaoshan is a renowned homeland of overseas Chinese, whose communities have maintained extensive historical ties with local development, places of residence abroad and charitable traditions. Submissions could address the following areas, with authors free to choose their own titles:

1. Overseas Chinese and the development of Chaoshan;
2. The history and culture of overseas Chinese;
3. Economic activities of overseas Chinese and their places of residence and hometowns;
4. Studies of prominent overseas Chinese leaders;
5. Overseas Chinese and charitable culture;
6. Studies of qiaopi, or remittance-letter, culture.

Papers should have a clear theme and distinct arguments, with theoretical and practical value, and should generally be no longer than 5,000 Chinese characters. Submissions were to be sent as Word documents and include the author’s name, organization, position, telephone number and email address. The organizers would arrange expert review and select papers for the conference; the specific time and venue would be announced separately.
