Zhicheng College will hold a **Public Speaking Intensive Training Camp** on **May 18, 2013**, inviting public-speaking coach Wu Hao, general manager of the Shantou branch of Jucheng Co., Ltd., to provide professional training and guidance. The camp is open to residential-college students and university juniors or above, with **70 places** available.

### Schedule

The training will take place in morning and afternoon sessions in **Science Report Hall Meeting Room 205**. Fifty-five places are reserved for residential-college students and 15 for students from other residential areas. Participants must ensure that they have no other classes or activities during the training and must follow on-site management and training requirements.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Public Speaking Intensive Training Camp |
| Time | 8:30 a.m.–noon and 2–5:30 p.m., Saturday, May 18, 2013 |
| Venue | Science Report Hall Meeting Room 205 |
| Places | 70 |
| Allocation | 55 for residential-college students; 15 for students from other residential areas |
| Eligible participants | Residential-college students and university juniors or above |
| Application deadline | 2 p.m., May 16, 2013 |
| Admission notice | Results will be sent by email |

### Coach

Wu Hao is described as a senior lecturer with Jucheng Consulting Group, a youth entrepreneurship coach and an entrepreneur-speaking coach. He also advises several businesses on operations. His public biography says that he has delivered more than 500 speeches totaling over 1,000 hours and has provided diagnostic consulting to hundreds of businesses.

### Registration

Residential-college students should register through the relevant organization leaders. Other eligible students should download and complete the registration form in the OA attachment and send the electronic form to `ATP@stu.edu.cn`. The file must be labeled with the year, major, name and dormitory information as required by the notice; consult the original notice for the exact format.

The form contains personal fields including name, gender, dormitory, major and year, student number, email, mobile number, current position, political status and student-cadre experience. The form and candidate information are not reproduced here.
