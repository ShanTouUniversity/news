To advance undergraduate-education reform, STU will hold a briefing on the **“Thirty Points for Higher Education”** from **10 a.m. to noon on Friday, May 17, 2013**, in the **Library Report Hall**. The presentation is titled **“Implementing the Requirements of Connotative Development and Focusing on Improving Talent Cultivation.”** It will be delivered by Wu Aihua, director of the Science and Engineering Division of the Ministry of Education's Department of Higher Education.

Wu Aihua was one of the drafters of the Ministry of Education's *Several Opinions on Comprehensively Improving the Quality of Higher Education*. The briefing is open to the STU community. The university asks each unit to ensure the attendance of school or college deans and vice-deans, department heads, major coordinators or directors, holders of senior management posts, and faculty members and graduate students of the Institute of Higher Education. Attendees should enter the venue 15 minutes early.

### Policy overview

The attachment for the briefing is the Ministry of Education's *Several Opinions on Comprehensively Improving the Quality of Higher Education* (教高〔2012〕4号), issued on March 16, 2012. The document sets out 30 areas of work centered on connotative development and the quality of talent cultivation:

| Points | Policy directions |
| --- | --- |
| 1–5 | Connotative development; distinctive universities; talent-cultivation quality standards; discipline, major and talent-cultivation structures; innovation in talent-cultivation models |
| 6–10 | The foundation of undergraduate teaching; graduate-training reform; practice-based education; innovation and entrepreneurship education and employment guidance; ideological and political education |
| 11–15 | Education-quality assessment; collaborative innovation; university scientific and technological innovation; philosophy and social sciences; university research-management mechanisms |
| 16–20 | University social services; continuing education; cultural inheritance and innovation; admissions and examination reform; graduate funding |
| 21–25 | Modern university systems; pilot-college reform; shared high-quality education resources; provincial-government coordination; international exchange and cooperation |
| 26–30 | Faculty ethics; teacher professional and teaching ability; classified faculty management; university infrastructure; university funding |

The full document also gives specific reform measures for undergraduate teaching, practical education, quality assessment, research and innovation, international exchange, faculty development and financial management. This article summarizes the attachment; the original Ministry of Education document remains authoritative.
