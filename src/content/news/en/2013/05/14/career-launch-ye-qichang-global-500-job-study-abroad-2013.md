The 34th Career Launch forum at Shantou University will be held on May 17, 2013, under the theme “Job-Seeking Skills for Global 500 Companies and Successful Study-Abroad Experience.” Dr. Ye Qichang will discuss recruitment processes at foreign companies, interview preparation, study-abroad applications and ways to strengthen career competitiveness.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | May 17, 2013, Friday, 7:30–9 p.m. |
| Venue | Lecture Staircase E201 |
| Speaker | Dr. Ye Qichang |
| Topic | Job-seeking skills for Global 500 companies and successful study-abroad experience |

The session will cover how to stand out in foreign-company recruitment, common job-seeking mistakes, qualities sought by Global 500 companies and common interview questions. It will also address study-abroad application strategies, how overseas experience can support entry into major companies, and a STU student’s experience applying to universities ranked among the world’s top 25, including a Cambridge University waiting-list case.

## Speaker Profile

Dr. Ye Qichang is chief consultant at Shenzhen Boyang Education Consulting Co., Ltd. He previously worked in Hong Kong as a real-estate fund manager at LaSalle Investment Management and at Enright Partners, a U.S.-based strategy consulting firm, where he contributed to projects including an economic feasibility report for the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge and an economic-impact assessment of Hong Kong Airport’s third runway.

Ye graduated from the Business School of Shantou University and later received support from the British government’s Overseas Research Students Awards Scheme and the Cambridge Overseas Trust. He studied at Queens’ College, Cambridge, and King’s College London, earning a master’s degree in real-estate finance and a doctorate in economics. During his doctoral study, he participated in projects on China’s banking market at the Bank of England and HSBC.
