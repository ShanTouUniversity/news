The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University opened recruitment for university student volunteers for the 2013 Western Program and Mountain Program on May 14, 2013. Western Program volunteers would serve in township-level grassroots communities in western provinces and regions. Mountain Program volunteers would continue to work with township Communist Youth League organizations on youth and volunteer services in economically less-developed areas of eastern, western and northern Guangdong.

## Service Areas

The Western Program offered seven service areas: basic education, agricultural science and technology, healthcare, grassroots youth work, grassroots social administration, service in Xinjiang and service in Tibet. The Mountain Program focused on grassroots youth and volunteer services in economically less-developed areas of Guangdong.

## Application Process

| Stage | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Online information and registration | From the date of the notice through May 26, visit `www.gdvolunteer.org` for information and complete registration |
| Download the form | Western Program applicants were to download the 2013 Western Program Registration Form from `xibu.youth.cn`; Mountain Program applicants were to download the corresponding form from the “Downloads” section of the Guangdong Volunteer website |
| Submit materials | The form required a counselor’s or college Youth League official’s signature and the seal of the relevant school or college Party organization, and was due to the university project office by May 26 |
| Review | The university project office would verify the online information and enter its review comments in the Western Program information system |

STU’s University Student Volunteer Service Western Program Office was located in the university Youth League Committee and was responsible for recruitment and coordination for both programs. Inquiries: `0754-86502302`. Registration forms and application information contain personal data and are not reproduced here.
