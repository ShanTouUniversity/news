It is reported that the Resources Management Office of Shantou University has finalized the annual health checkup arrangements for 2009-cohort undergraduates and 2010-cohort graduate students at the main campus. The checkups will begin on **May 21**, and the arrangements have been communicated in advance to the youth league committees of all schools and to Zhi Cheng College.

According to the announcement, the detailed examination timetable was distributed as an attached schedule, and schools have been asked to cooperate actively in organizing students to attend their checkups on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
