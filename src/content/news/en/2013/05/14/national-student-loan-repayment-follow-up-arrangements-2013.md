Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office issued a supplementary notice on May 14, 2013. Under arrangements by Bank of China Shantou Branch, 2013 graduates with national student loans who had not yet signed repayment agreements were required to visit the Personal Finance Department of Bank of China Shantou Branch **by May 25** to sign a repayment agreement or apply for early repayment.

## Appointment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible group | 2013 graduates with national student loans who had not signed repayment agreements |
| Deadline | May 25, 2013 |
| Location | Personal Finance Department, Bank of China Shantou Branch, 98 Jinsha Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515041 |
| Office hours | Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m.–noon and 2–5:30 p.m. |
| Bank telephone | 0754-88949456 and 0754-88949407 |
| Fax | 0754-88266321 |

Participants were asked to bring the loan contract, identity document, repayment passbook and required copies in accordance with the bank’s requirements, as well as a black pen. Those who had lost their loan contracts could obtain copies from the Student Aid Management Center in Room 106 of the Administration Center. Repayment passbooks were to be checked at a bank branch in advance to confirm that they remained valid.

The notice also provided a National Student Loan Repayment Guide. Inquiries could be directed to the Student Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office at `0754-86504749` or `zjliu@stu.edu.cn`. This report does not reproduce the graduate list, identity documents, passbook account details, loan contracts or other personal financial records.
