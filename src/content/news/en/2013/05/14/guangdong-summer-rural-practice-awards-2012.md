Shantou University received multiple honors in Guangdong’s 2012 summer volunteer program for higher-education students, commonly known as the “Three Rural Areas” community-practice program. The university received an Outstanding Organization Award for its substantial program work. Its “Heart for the Rehabilitation Village” and “Passing on the Torch in Rural Communities” volunteer initiatives were named Outstanding Teams.

Ten STU faculty members and students were also named Outstanding Individuals by the province. Because the notice did not distinguish their faculty or student status, this report gives only the publicly stated total and does not reproduce personal names.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
