Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on May 14, 2013, opening the selection of projects for the 2013 National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program. The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education planned to recommend national-level projects from projects approved under the 2013 provincial program, and STU planned to submit 25 projects from its main campus.

## Selection Scope

The selection covered undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects approved at the provincial level in 2013. The eligible types were undergraduate innovation training, entrepreneurship training, and entrepreneurship practice. Each student could lead only one project. Each team was required to have no more than five members and clearly defined responsibilities.

## Key Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number to be recommended | 25 main-campus projects |
| Registration deadline | 10 p.m. on May 16, 2013 |
| Submission method | Complete the registration form and email it to `yjguo@stu.edu.cn` |
| Selection basis | The Academic Affairs Office would consider the projects’ 2013 provincial review results and select candidates competitively |
| Contact | Guo Yijia, Academic Affairs Office |
| Telephone | 86502736 (short number 882736) |

The notice included a list of provincially approved projects and a registration form. Because the attachments contain project-leader and application information, this report does not reproduce the names or personal fields.
