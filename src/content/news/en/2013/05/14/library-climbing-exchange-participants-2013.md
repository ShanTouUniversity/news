The Shantou University Library Student Activity Hall selected **12 participants** for a climbing exchange activity. Participants were asked to report to the Library Student Activity Hall at 4:57 p.m. on May 15, 2013, before taking part in climbing theory study and training.

If a participant could not attend, the organizers would fill the place from the remaining applicants. Because the notice included a participant list with student department information, this report does not reproduce names, departments or contact details.

| --- | --- |
| Number of participants | 12 |
| Check-in time | May 15, 2013, 4:57 p.m. |
| Check-in and activity venue | Library Student Activity Hall |
| Activities | Climbing theory study and training |
