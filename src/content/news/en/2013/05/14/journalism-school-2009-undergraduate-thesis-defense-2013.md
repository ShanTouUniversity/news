Shantou University’s Journalism School announced the undergraduate thesis defense schedule for the 2009 cohort on May 14, 2013. The defenses involved 158 students in six groups and were scheduled from May 20 to 28 in the Small Studio and Glass Room. The defense committee comprised Fan Dongsheng, Bai Jing, Wang Zongan, Yang Aili, Chen Yuefen and Cheng Jinfu, who served as group chairs.

## Defense Schedule

| Group | Students | Time | Venue |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | 26 | May 27, 1 p.m. | Glass Room |
| Group 2 | 23 | May 28, 8:30 a.m. | Glass Room |
| Group 3 | 27 | May 21, 8:30 a.m. | Small Studio |
| Group 4 | 28 | May 24, 10 a.m., and May 25, 2 p.m., in two sessions | Small Studio |
| Group 5 | 28 | May 22, 8:30 a.m. and 2:30 p.m., 14 students per session | Small Studio |
| Group 6 | 26 | May 20, 8 a.m.–noon and 1–4 p.m. in two sessions | Small Studio in the morning; Glass Room in the afternoon |

The defense procedure consisted of a thesis presentation, responses to questions and group deliberation on grades. The school assigned supervisors and recording staff to each group. The name of the Group 3 recorder appears garbled in the archived notice and cannot be reliably restored, so it is omitted here. Thesis titles and the defense order were included in an attachment; student names and student numbers are not reproduced.
