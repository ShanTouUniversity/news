Shantou University’s basic life-saving workshop received **34 registration forms**. After reviewing the applications under the published requirements, the organizers selected **20 faculty members and students** to attend. The first training session was scheduled for 1:30 p.m. on May 15 in Room D502.

The workshop focused on basic life-saving and water-related first-aid knowledge, with the aim of helping participants understand water safety and emergency-response skills. Because the original notice included a participant list containing student information, this report does not publish names, departments or personal contact details.
