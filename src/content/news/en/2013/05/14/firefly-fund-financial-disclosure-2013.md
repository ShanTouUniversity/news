The Firefly Charity Foundation of Shantou University’s Youth Volunteers published a periodic financial report on May 14, 2013, inviting supervision from the university community. Established on April 25, 2008, the foundation operates under the guidance of the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Youth Volunteers Association. It provides study assistance and emergency support for students and establishes special funds for designated groups.

## Financial Overview

The report stated that the foundation held **RMB 33,487.79** as of November 1, 2011. From November 2011 through February 2013, it raised **RMB 30,798.25** through used-goods sales, micro-public-welfare activities, and donations from individuals and organizations. During the same period, it spent **RMB 20,340.00** on programs including Ai Ban Gui Cheng, Xuehai Yingguang, a special fund for a leprosy rehabilitation village and individual assistance.

As of February 28, 2013, the foundation’s closing funds totaled **RMB 43,946.04**, all held in a designated account of the STU Finance Office.
