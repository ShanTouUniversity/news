To help students prepare for the English CET-4 and CET-6 examinations scheduled for June, Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) will hold two experience-sharing sessions on May 15 and 19. High-performing senior student tutors will share practical preparation methods.

## Schedule

| Session | Time | Venue | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CET-4 exam experience session | May 15, Wednesday, 3–5 p.m. | SLC 204 | Vocabulary memorization and reading strategies |
| CET-6 exam experience session | May 19, Sunday, 3–5 p.m. | SLC 204 | Developing interest in English and preparing through practice |

In addition to the two sessions, SLC would provide English CET-4 and CET-6 tutoring every Monday evening for continued examination support.
