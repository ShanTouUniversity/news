Zhicheng Residential College is recruiting **11 incoming mentors for the 2013–2014 academic year**, including four alternates. The mentors will live at Zhicheng College, assist with residential students' development and participate in college-organized training. Those who complete the training successfully will be formally appointed.

### Eligibility

The recruitment is open to Shantou University students who will be university juniors or above during their term of service, including graduate students. Applicants should identify with the Zhicheng philosophy, be willing to serve residential students, and have a strong sense of responsibility, student-organization experience, teamwork ability and communication skills. Academically, applicants must have ranked in the top 40 percent of their class in the 2011–2012 academic year and have no failing courses in the fall semester of 2012–2013. The notice allows appropriate flexibility for outstanding student cadres and requires graduate applicants to provide a supervisor's recommendation.

### Duties and support

The *Interim Regulations on Zhicheng College Mentor Work* define the role as a senior-student service position that supports residential students' development, encourages mutual support and strengthens their sense of belonging. Mentors are expected to use their free time to understand the academic and daily needs of assigned residents, assist counselors, help students navigate course selection under the credit system, maintain contact with faculty mentors, and support college activities and residential culture.

Under the regulations, each mentor generally works with about 30 residential students. A normal appointment lasts one year and may be renewed for one additional term. Mentors must complete college training before taking up their posts and may receive further training as needed. A mentor committee coordinates routine work, and the college conducts evaluations every six months. Mentors who pass evaluation may be reappointed for the following year; those with outstanding performance may receive recognition and certificates. The college provides a full accommodation subsidy to mentors, paid every six months.

### Application

Applicants should download and complete the *Shantou University Zhicheng College Mentor Application Form* in the OA attachment and send it to `10gzliu1@stu.edu.cn` by **noon on May 22**. The filename must identify the applicant's year, major and name as required by the notice.

The application form contains personal fields including name, gender, birth month and year, year and major, student number, dormitory, mobile number, email, political status, academic record, awards and activities, student-cadre experience, supervisor's comments, applicant signature and date. The form and personal application materials are not reproduced here.
