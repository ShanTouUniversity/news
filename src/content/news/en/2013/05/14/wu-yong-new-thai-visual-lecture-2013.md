### Speaker

Wu Yong is head of the Department of Graphic Design and a professor and master's supervisor at the STU Changjiang College of Arts and Design. He is a member of the Alliance Graphique Internationale (AGI). He graduated from the Book Arts Department of the Central Academy of Arts and Crafts in 1988 with a bachelor's degree and from the Department of Visual Communication Design at the Academy of Arts and Design, Tsinghua University, in 2008 with a master's degree.

Wu previously served as deputy director of the art-editing office at China Youth Press and as an arts adviser for UNICEF China. He is a committee member of the Design Education Committee of the Chinese Society of Higher Education and an expert reviewer for the Ministry of Education's humanities and social sciences programs. He has also taught courses at the Central Academy of Fine Arts and Tsinghua University's Academy of Arts and Design and served as a visiting professor at the Department of Graphic Design of Beijing Institute of Fashion Technology and at Korea's Asia Creative Academy.

His design work has received honors including the Asia-Pacific Gold Award at the Hong Kong Designers Association Biennial, the National Book Award, the exploration-category gold award at the Sixth National Book Design Art Exhibition, the “Most Beautiful Books in China” award and the China Publishing Government Award. Representative works listed in the public biography include *Selected Tobacco-Label Art from Dream of Red Mansions*, *The Essence of Stele Rubbings from the National Library*, *Ancient Chinese Doors and Windows*, *A Comprehensive Collection of Illustrations from Chinese Classics*, *Endless Voyage* and *A Tour of Chinese Cities*. In 2011, he was nominated for the Designer Award at the Shanghai Design Exhibition and received the SGDC Contribution of the Year Award.

Wu has been invited to deliver keynote or special presentations at design forums and book fairs in Spain, South Korea and Taipei, and participated in the invited-designer poster exhibition at the Taipei World Design Expo. In 2013, he was invited to the China–Japan–Korea Book·Architecture Exhibition, where he also spoke at a forum. He has published or co-published *Four Views on Book Design*, *Wu Yong Graphic Design: 2000 Plus and Minus* and *Something or Nothing: Dialogues with Contemporary Art*. His work and creative practice have also been featured in design publications including Japan's *IDEA*.
