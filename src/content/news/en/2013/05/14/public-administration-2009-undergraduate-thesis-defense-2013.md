Shantou University’s Public Administration Department announced the undergraduate thesis defense schedule for the 2009 cohort. A total of **75 students** applied to defend their theses. The defense was scheduled to begin at 2 p.m. on Saturday, May 18, 2013, with five groups working in Rooms D301, D302, D303, D304 and D306.

| Group | Students | Venue |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Group 1 | 15 | D301 |
| Group 2 | 15 | D302 |
| Group 3 | 15 | D303 |
| Group 4 | 15 | D304 |
| Group 5 | 15 | D306 |

Yan Zhigang chaired the defense committee, whose members were Li Ping, Qin Guozhu, Li Jiacai and Cai Zhengzhong. Wang Run served as secretary. Relevant faculty members served on the individual groups. Students were required to submit three copies of their theses before the defense. Supervisors were to collect the theses by May 8, group chairs were to assign theses and lead examiners by May 10, and the signed assessment materials were due to the secretary’s office by May 18.

## Procedure and Assessment

Each student was to introduce the thesis topic and significance, sources, main arguments and innovations within 10 minutes. The lead examiner would then ask two or three questions, followed by 15 minutes of preparation and the response. Other faculty members could ask additional questions, while a thesis supervisor could not serve as the lead examiner for that thesis. Each group could recommend outstanding theses at a rate of approximately 15 percent, subject to final approval by the defense committee. The notice recommended using PowerPoint presentations.

The assessment bands were 90–100 for excellent, 80–89 for good, 70–79 for average, 60–69 for pass and 59 or below for fail. A thesis found to involve serious plagiarism would automatically fail. All graduating students were required to attend; those who did not could not apply for a degree. Leave was not permitted during the defense period, and lateness, early departure or failure to attend the full defense could be treated as an absence or affect the defense-performance score.
