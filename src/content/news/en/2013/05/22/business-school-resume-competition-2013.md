Shantou University’s Business School would hold a resume-design competition from **May 25 to 29** to help students understand corporate recruitment expectations, the role of resume design and career preparation, while strengthening employment awareness and career planning.

The competition required **all students in the Business School’s 2010 cohort** to participate and welcomed students from other years.

## Competition Process

- **Preliminary round:** Participants had to submit electronic and paper resumes to their Business School class monitors by May 25.
- **Second round:** An on-site presentation would be held from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on May 29. The presentation format was open, and each participant had five to eight minutes.

The competition would include awards; details were to follow the Business School’s subsequent arrangements. Participants’ resumes and personal job-application information are not published here.
