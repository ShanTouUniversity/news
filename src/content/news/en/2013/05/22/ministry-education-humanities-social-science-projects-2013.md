The results of the 2013 Ministry of Education general humanities and social sciences program were recently announced. **Nine projects** from Shantou University received funding. STU had submitted 58 applications for planning-fund and youth-fund projects that year, achieving a **15.51%** funding rate, compared with the national average of **7.45%** for the Ministry of Education’s planning-fund, youth-fund and self-funded projects.

The funded projects were:

| Project number | Project | Unit | Principal investigator | Category |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13YJA740041 | Effects of multimodal factors in foreign-language classrooms on willingness to communicate | College of Liberal Arts | Peng Jiange | Planning Fund |
| 13YJC740007 | An interlanguage study of Chinese zai-constructions and an exploration of linguistic theory | College of Liberal Arts | Chen Fanfan | Youth Fund |
| 13YJA630049 | Multilevel influences and processes affecting knowledge-sharing intentions and behaviors | Business School | Lin Quan | Planning Fund |
| 13YJAZH128 | A university-student social-entrepreneurship learning model for opportunity exploration and “means-oriented rationality” decision contexts | Business School | Zeng Fanqi | Planning Fund |
| 13YJC630097 | Formation, effects and prevention mechanisms of shareholder collusion | Business School | Liu Wei | Youth Fund |
| 13YJA880035 | An empirical study of the dissemination and development of the CDIO engineering-education model in China | Institute of Higher Education | Kang Quanli | Planning Fund |
| 13YJC880076 | Assessment and diagnosis of undergraduate education quality based on learning-engagement theory | Institute of Higher Education | Wang Xueju | Youth Fund |
| 13YJAZH074 | Practice and research on the professional development of medical teachers in an internationalized context | Medical College | Qiu Xiuhua | Planning Fund |
| 13YJA710023 | Guiding youth values in the context of new media | Medical College | Lin Yuexin | Planning Fund |

The Research Office asked the principal investigators to organize their research carefully and ensure project quality, and encouraged faculty members across the university to continue applying for research programs.
