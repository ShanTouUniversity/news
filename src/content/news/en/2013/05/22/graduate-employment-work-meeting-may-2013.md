Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled a graduate employment work meeting for **10:30 a.m. on Friday, May 24**, in **Meeting Room 213 of the New Administration Center**.

Attendees included Party general-branch secretaries or deputy secretaries from the colleges and residential colleges, counselors responsible for employment work, and staff from the Graduate Employment Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office. The relevant staff members were asked to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
