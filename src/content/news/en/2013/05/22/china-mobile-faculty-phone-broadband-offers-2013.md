China Mobile introduced a special group-purchase phone promotion and a staff-dormitory broadband offer for Shantou University faculty and staff. Phone quantities were limited, with one purchasing phase arranged every three days. The notice said the phone promotion was provisionally scheduled to end on **May 30**.

## Group-Purchase Phones

Eligible numbers could send the text message `JTTG` to `10086` to check eligibility and promotion details. Staff could also consult the China Mobile service hall beneath **STU Canteen No. 2**. The attached offer listed the following special package prices and terms:

| Model | Group-purchase price | Offer term |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Samsung S7568 | RMB 449 | 24 months |
| HTC T329t | RMB 629 | 24 months |
| Motorola MT788 | RMB 899 | 24 months |
| Samsung I9268 | RMB 1,349 | 24 months |
| Samsung I9308 | RMB 2,699 | 24 months |
| Samsung GT-N7108 | RMB 2,879 | 24 months |
| Samsung I9508 | RMB 3,600 | 24 months |

The Samsung S7568 package specified a RMB 10 GPRS package and a TD 500 MB package. The attachment’s columns for monthly minimum-consumption levels, call-credit gifts and other package requirements were misaligned in the archived document; the original OA attachment should be consulted for those exact terms.

## Staff-Dormitory Broadband

The staff-dormitory broadband promotion ran from **April 1 through June 30, 2013**. After paying **RMB 599**, a customer received one year of 6 Mbps broadband service and one wireless router, together with mobile call-credit refunds:

- RMB 50 per month for months 1–8 beginning the month after application, totaling RMB 400;
- RMB 25 per month for months 9–12, totaling RMB 100.

Broadband service was free from the application date through the end of that month. The 12-month paid validity period began the following month. The account holder had to apply in person with the original identity card and a local China Mobile number at the China Mobile M-Zone service hall beneath Canteen No. 2. The offer also allowed family China Mobile numbers to receive a free ST City Pass IC card, subject to the service hall’s registration requirements.
