After the live-fire exercise in Shantou University’s 2012 student military training was completed, the university honored **14 students** who achieved outstanding results and granted them the title of “Top Shooter.” The scores listed in the public notice ranged from **40 to 47 rings**, with the highest score being 47.

The university encouraged the honorees to continue improving and called on all students to learn from the positive traditions and conduct developed during military training and apply them in their future study and work. The honorees’ names and the connection between individual students and scores are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
