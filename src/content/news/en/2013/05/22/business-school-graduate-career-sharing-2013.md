To help students plan university life and prepare to enter society, Shantou University’s Business School would hold a job-search experience session featuring graduates from the 2013 cohort. Nine Business School graduates would serve as speakers and use experience sharing and discussion to address job-search planning and practical skills.

The session was scheduled for **2:30–5 p.m. on May 24** in **E101 stepped lecture room**. It was open to Business School undergraduates in the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts, and students from other colleges were also welcome.

## Discussion Topics

The speakers would discuss job-search experiences, interview experience and techniques, and experiences applying to government agencies, foreign-invested companies, state-owned enterprises and private companies. The session would also include reflections on postgraduate entrance examinations and overseas study.
