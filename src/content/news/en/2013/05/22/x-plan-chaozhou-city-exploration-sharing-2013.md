The preliminary research stage of the Student Affairs Office’s X Plan activity “Exploring the City’s Hidden Stories: On the Chao Journey” had been completed. Teams made up of students from different majors and residential communities visited Chaozhou in May for field research into the stories behind the city.

To share the research results and communicate information about urban culture, the Student Affairs Office would hold a sharing session from **3 to 5 p.m. on Sunday, May 26**, in the **Student Activity Hall**. The research teams would present stories about Chaozhou from different perspectives and discuss what they learned from the fieldwork.

The session was open to all STU faculty members and students.
