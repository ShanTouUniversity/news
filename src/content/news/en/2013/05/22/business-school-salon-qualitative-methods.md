It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will hold the 119th session of its Economics Salon on the evening of Monday, May 27, featuring Dr. Alf H. Walle from the University of Alaska Fairbanks as the speaker.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Dr. Alf H. Walle (University of Alaska at Fairbanks) |
| Topic | Qualitative Methods: Tools of Business Research |
| Time | May 27, 2013 (Monday), 19:30–21:30 |
| Venue | Room E-204 (E Jie 204) |
| Language | English |

## Focus on the Rise of Qualitative Research

This session focuses on qualitative analysis in business research. According to the organizer, in an academic environment that favors quantitative and scientific methods, how to consolidate and practice qualitative research has become an issue that urgently needs discussion. Against this backdrop, Dr. Walle will present the value and application of qualitative methods as tools of business research.

## About the Speaker

Alf H. Walle is an anthropologist who specializes in methodological issues and cutting-edge qualitative approaches in marketing and management. He holds a doctorate in traditional culture, a master's degree in social anthropology, and postdoctoral training in cross-cultural studies. His latest book, *Rethinking Business Anthropology*, was published in May 2013.

He has an extensive publication record spanning business anthropology, marketing, management customs and tourism, with 15 published books. He has also served as editor of a trade journal and directed major cultural festivals and tourism events, including seven seasons of the Smithsonian Festival of American Folklife.

The salon is hosted by the Business School and primarily targets its undergraduate and graduate students, while all faculty and students of the university are welcome to attend.
