To implement the social-practice component of ideological-political theory courses and deepen teaching reform, Shantou University’s Social Sciences Department would organize field practice for all students taking *Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics* in the current term. The activities were scheduled for **May 25 and 26**.

## Group Arrangements

| Date | Field-practice site | Boarding time | Meeting point | Class numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| May 25 | Jinwei Beer Shantou Co., Ltd. | 8:50 a.m. | STU motto monument | 58966, 58967, 58968 and 58969 |
| May 26 | Niutianyang Agricultural and Sideline Products Base of the Guangzhou Military Region | 8:20 a.m. | STU motto monument | 58964, 58965, 58970 and 58971 |

Instructors and students from the listed classes were asked to arrive at the motto monument on time, line up by class and board in an orderly manner so that the activities would not be delayed.
