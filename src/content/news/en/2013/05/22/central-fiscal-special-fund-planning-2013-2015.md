Shantou University’s 211 Project Office began preparing the **2013–2015 Development Plan for the Central Fiscal Special Fund for Local Universities**. To encourage submissions from university units, the university placed no limit on the number of projects each unit could propose. The University Academic Committee would hear project presentations and organize screening.

## Project Areas

Proposals could cover six development areas: distinctive key-discipline development, provincial key-discipline development, teaching laboratory platforms, research platforms and professional-practice bases, public-service systems, and talent development and innovation teams.

Project plans were expected to describe the current situation, guiding principles, overall objectives, major construction content, funding sources and annual allocations, implementation organization and schedule, expected benefits and safeguards. The attached planning guide recommended a **3:2:1 ratio** for central fiscal, local fiscal and university self-raised funds; actual project funding would depend on review.

## Work Schedule

| Task | Responsible party | Deadline or start | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Submit project lists | Colleges and relevant units | By 4 p.m., May 22 | Submit centrally, identify project category, principal investigator and presenter |
| Present project PPTs | Principal investigators | From 2:30 p.m., May 23 | The University Academic Committee would review and screen projects; each presentation limited to 10 minutes |
| Submit materials for selected projects | Principal investigators | By 4 p.m., May 24 | Prepare materials according to the attached outline and requirements |
| Complete sections covering the university’s overall situation | 211 Project Office | By May 26 | Relevant units were asked to provide background information as needed |
| Complete the first draft of the plan | 211 Project Office | By May 26 | Project teams had to remain reachable for work coordination |
| Complete the final plan | Relevant personnel | May 27 | The university would organize the work |
| Submit the plan | 211 Project Office | May 27 | — |

Units were asked to treat the planning materials as a priority and complete each task on schedule. A proposed project that failed to complete a required task by the schedule would be deemed to have withdrawn its application.
