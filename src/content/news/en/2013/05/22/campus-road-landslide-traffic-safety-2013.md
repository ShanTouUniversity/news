Consecutive days of heavy rain caused a partial landslide on the motor-vehicle road west of Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design, on the side facing the reservoir. The infrastructure department was organizing emergency reinforcement and repairs.

Until the work was complete, temporary traffic controls applied to the road:

- Buses, other large vehicles and trucks were prohibited from entering;
- Parking was prohibited on nearby roads;
- Small vehicles had to travel slowly along the eastern side of the road.

The university reminded faculty members and drivers to proceed carefully through the area.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office, May 22, 2013)
