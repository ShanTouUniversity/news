The STU Labor Union has reported that Professor Lin Furong of the School of Science received the **Guangdong May 1 Labor Medal**. According to the notice, he traveled to Guangzhou on the morning of **April 27** to attend the provincial conference marking International Workers' Day and recognizing model workers, where he was received by provincial Party and government leaders and took part in the award presentation. On the afternoon of **April 28**, he also went on stage to receive an award at Shantou's conference marking International Workers' Day and promoting the “One Million Workers Building a Beautiful and Happy Shantou” initiative.

### Recognition events

| Date | Event | Details recorded in the notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning of April 27 | Guangdong conference marking International Workers' Day and recognizing model workers, Guangzhou | Lin Furong was received by provincial Party and government leaders and took part in the award presentation |
| Afternoon of April 28 | Shantou conference marking International Workers' Day and promoting the “One Million Workers Building a Beautiful and Happy Shantou” initiative | Lin Furong went on stage to receive an award |

The original notice records both events but does not explain the precise form or relationship of the two presentations. Lin Furong had previously been identified by the STU Labor Union as the university's preliminary nominee for the Guangdong May 1 Labor Medal; this report confirms that he received the provincial honor.
