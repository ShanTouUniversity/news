STU has issued Document No. 56 [2013] to open selection for the 2012–2013 **Li Ka Shing Foundation Distinguished Service Award**. The award recognizes academic support and administrative staff who have achieved notable results in routine service work and supported teaching, research and the university's development.

Relevant units must follow the award's guidelines and procedures and submit nominee lists and the required materials in both electronic and paper form **by May 15**.

### Award and eligibility

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Award | Li Ka Shing Foundation Distinguished Service Award |
| Annual places | No more than three |
| Allocation | No more than two for the main campus and one for the Medical College |
| Eligible employees | STU academic support staff, administrative staff and relevant contracted staff |
| Service requirement | At least 10 consecutive years at STU as of June 30 of the selection year |
| Previous-award restriction | Must not have previously received the same award |
| Performance requirement | Significant achievements in the employee's service duties; teaching and research achievements are not used as selection criteria |

Candidates may be nominated by a supervisor and confirmed by the department head, or jointly nominated by at least three STU faculty or staff members. The main criteria are commitment to work and performance, including diligent fulfillment of job responsibilities and sustained contributions to academic support, the campus environment, student and staff quality of life, university service systems and institutional development.

### Review and materials

The university will establish a temporary review committee under the Board of Trustees. The committee will have seven members representing academic support staff, administrative staff, faculty and students. It will rigorously review candidate materials, invite candidates to respond when necessary, and determine the recipients by secret ballot after discussion.

The required nomination materials are:

- Application form;
- Nomination form;
- The candidate's work statement explaining why the candidate meets the award requirements;
- Two letters of recommendation from colleagues.
