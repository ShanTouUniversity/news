It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will hold its 7th Fashion Creative Design Competition (FASHION SHOW) on May 4, 2013.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 7th Fashion Creative Design Competition |
| Time | 19:30, May 4, 2013 |
| Venue | Grand Auditorium |
| Admission | No ticket required; seats available on a first-come, first-served basis (except for the public jury seats) |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

The show aims to enrich campus cultural life and showcase students’ creative design talents. Further details are available via the school student union and the competition’s official Weibo accounts. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
