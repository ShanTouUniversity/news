STU's Resource Management Office has issued Document No. 2 [2013] to announce a personnel adjustment. Following consideration by the Resource Management Office, consent from the Human Resources Office and approval by the responsible university leader, **Cai Weizhong** was appointed deputy director of the Resource Management Office's Infrastructure Office and removed from the position of deputy director of its Campus Property Office.

### Appointment adjustment

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Appointee | Cai Weizhong |
| New position | Deputy director, Infrastructure Office, Resource Management Office |
| Previous position | Deputy director, Campus Property Office, Resource Management Office |
| Document | Resource Management Office Document No. 2 [2013] |
| Issuing unit | Resource Management Office |
| Date | May 3, 2013 |
