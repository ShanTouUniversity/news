It is reported that Shantou University has rescheduled its sixth campus Open Day. The event, which aims to give prospective students sitting the national college entrance exam and their parents an in-depth look at the university, was originally planned for May 12 and will now be held on June 10.

According to the report, the university has asked all departments to adjust their work arrangements and complete preparations for the Open Day.

The Open Day has long served as an important window for exam candidates and parents to experience the university's campus environment, academic programs and campus culture firsthand. The new date falls shortly after the conclusion of this year's entrance examination, allowing candidates to visit the campus in person afterwards for more direct reference when making application decisions.
