It is reported that Shantou University has rescheduled its sixth campus Open Day. The event, which aims to give prospective students sitting the national college entrance exam and their parents an in-depth look at the university, was originally planned for May 12 and will now be held on June 10.

According to the report, the university has asked all departments to adjust their work arrangements and complete preparations for the Open Day.

The Open Day has long served as an important window for exam candidates and parents to experience the university's campus environment, academic programs and campus culture firsthand. The new date falls shortly after the conclusion of this year's entrance examination, allowing candidates to visit the campus in person afterwards for more direct reference when making application decisions.

## Preparation Coordination

To advance preparations for the sixth Open Day, the university scheduled a coordination meeting for **10 a.m. on May 30** in Meeting Room 213 of the Administration Center. Relevant coordinators from the Party and Administration Office, Alumni Office, Student Affairs Office, University Youth League Committee, Admissions Office, Office of Resource Management, Academic Affairs Office, News Center, Network and Information Center, Library, Sports Department, English Language Center, Arts Education Center and the colleges or residential colleges were expected to attend.

The meeting agenda covers departmental progress reports, division and coordination of cross-department projects, and discussion of unresolved matters.

## Open Day Arrangements

The Party and Administration Office issued the formal event arrangements on **June 3, 2013**. The sixth Open Day is scheduled for **9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Monday, June 10**, across the Shantou University campus; on-campus working hours will be **8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.** The event is intended for students, parents, alumni and visitors from the wider community, offering a view of STU's institutional development and the work of its faculty and students.

The program includes outdoor choral and a cappella performances, a television filming experience, a pronunciation-training lecture, science experiments, an interactive-design workshop and student-society showcases. The Library, Grand Auditorium, Gymnasium and college laboratories will be open to visitors. On-site consultations will cover student overseas exchange, scholarships and financial aid, career development, employment, career planning and university-application choices. The university will also receive alumni, collect alumni information and process alumni-card applications.

The complete program and venue list is provided in the OA attachment.

## Choir and A Cappella Performance

Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Center for Arts Education issued a program update on **June 5**. The STU Choir will present *With You*, an outdoor choir and a cappella performance, at the Small Stage at **9 a.m. on June 10**. The program combines choral and unaccompanied vocal performances and includes more than 20 songs, among them *Bossa Nova in Sicily*, *Chrysanthemum Terrace*, *High*, *For You* and *Creep*.

The performance is open to faculty, students and visitors attending Open Day. No separate registration is required; visitors may go directly to the Small Stage.

## Weibo Interaction and Draws

The STU News Center also arranged a live Weibo broadcast for the sixth Open Day. Visitors who joined the topic **#汕大开放日2013#**, posted a photograph or reflection from the event, and mentioned at least one of the following official Weibo accounts could enter a draw for STU souvenirs:

- STU Undergraduate Admissions
- STU News Center
- Shantou Grassroots Broadcast
- Touda Encyclopedia
- STU Radio and Television Station

The on-site Weibo broadcast team planned to hold a draw every hour. The names of winning accounts would be pinned on the official STU News Center Weibo account. Visitors could ask for further details at the on-site Weibo broadcast booth.
