STU's Party and Government Office and Human Resources Office reported the result of the selection of faculty representatives for the university's ninth Board of Trustees on May 3. Under the Board of Trustees charter and the arrangements for the changeover, university units nominated and selected candidates, after which a university leadership meeting deliberated on the result. The university decided to recommend two faculty members and submit the recommendation to the Board of Trustees for consideration.

### Recommended representatives

| Candidate | Unit | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ma Fengqi | Institute of Higher Education Research | Submission to the Board of Trustees for consideration |
| Xu Jiezhou | Medical College | Submission to the Board of Trustees for consideration |

The notice records the result of the university's internal selection and recommendation process. It does not mean that the Board of Trustees had completed its deliberation or that a formal appointment had been made. The two recommendations had previously been publicized from April 25 through May 1.
