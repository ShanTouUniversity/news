The STU Labor Union has reported that Zheng Huaiping of the School of Science attended Shantou's conference marking International Workers' Day and promoting the “One Million Workers Building a Beautiful and Happy Shantou” initiative on the afternoon of **April 28**. Municipal leaders presented him with the **Shantou May 1 Labor Medal** at the conference.

### Recognition details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient | Zheng Huaiping |
| Unit | STU School of Science |
| Honor | Shantou May 1 Labor Medal |
| Ceremony | Shantou conference marking International Workers' Day and promoting the “One Million Workers Building a Beautiful and Happy Shantou” initiative |
| Presentation | Afternoon of April 28 |

Zheng Huaiping had previously been identified by the STU Labor Union as the university's preliminary nominee for the Shantou May 1 Labor Medal. This notice confirms his attendance at the municipal recognition event and receipt of the honor.
