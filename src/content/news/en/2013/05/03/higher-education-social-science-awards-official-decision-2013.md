The Ministry of Education has forwarded to Shantou University its **Decision on Presenting the Sixth Higher Education Scientific Research Outstanding Achievement Awards in Humanities and Social Sciences** (Decision No. 2 [2013] of the Department of Social Sciences). The decision states that the award process has been completed. Following expert review, public notification and review by the awards committee, **830 achievements** received awards.

### Award structure

| Award | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 45 |
| Second prize | 250 |
| Third prize | 518 |
| Achievement dissemination prize | 17 |
| Total | 830 |

The Ministry said the awards recognize outstanding achievements by scholars in philosophy and the social sciences at universities across China. The decision also calls on education authorities and universities to draw on the award process to improve innovation in higher-education philosophy and social sciences, including innovation in disciplinary systems, academic perspectives and research methods.

### STU-related achievement

The paper **“Does Knowledge Management Matter for Information Technology Application in China?”**, co-authored by Professor Danming Lin of STU Business School and colleagues, received a third prize in this round. A previous feature article provided the paper's co-authors, publication details and related context. This official decision provides the complete national award list and the overall award totals.

The attachment is the list of **830 award-winning achievements**, with fields including titles, achievement formats, publication or release information and creators. To avoid reproducing the lengthy list, this article summarizes the official decision and the STU-related achievement; the complete list is available through the original OA notice. The attachment states that it was issued on **March 26, 2013**.
