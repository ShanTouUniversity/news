STU's Graduate School has opened the first-half 2013 renewal of the rail-ticket discount function on graduate student IDs and the replacement of graduate student IDs. After registration and renewal are completed, eligible graduate students may purchase half-price train tickets in accordance with the applicable rules.

### Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Services | Rail-ticket discount-card renewal and graduate student-ID replacement |
| Deadline | By May 10, 2013 |
| Submission | Submit by class group |
| Location | Graduate School Youth League Office, Room 401, New Administration Center |
| Contact | Wang Fengqi |
| Telephone | `86504000` |

Students seeking card renewal must submit graduate student IDs bearing registration stamps for each semester. Registration is normally completed through the graduate office of the relevant school at the beginning of each semester. IDs without the required registration stamps cannot be used to purchase half-price train tickets. Students who need replacement IDs must also submit the relevant materials before the deadline; late applications will not be accepted.

The notice does not provide a detailed list of replacement materials. Applicants should confirm the requirements with the Graduate School Youth League Office.
