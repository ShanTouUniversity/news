STU's Infrastructure Office will demolish dangerous buildings **21, 26 and 37** in the Continuing Education School campus area. Shantou J Zhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. has been commissioned to carry out the work. Construction is scheduled to begin on **May 3, 2013**, and finish **before June 1**.

### Construction impact and safety

The work may produce substantial noise, vibration and dust. The university asks pedestrians to take care and prohibits entry to the construction site without permission from the relevant university offices and the contractor. People should also avoid staying near the site whenever possible.

The construction notice states a bid price of **RMB 82,000**. An earlier bid-result notice issued on April 27 listed the winning amount as **RMB 81,979.00**. The two notices use different amounts; this article preserves both source statements without conversion or speculation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
