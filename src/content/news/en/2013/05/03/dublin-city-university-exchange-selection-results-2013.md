STU's School of Liberal Arts has announced the selection results for its exchange program with Dublin City University in Ireland. Following a written test on the afternoon of **April 23** and subsequent interviews, the school proposed **five students** as formal nominees and selected **two students** as reserve candidates. Ten students had previously passed the preliminary selection.

### Selection summary

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Preliminary candidates | 10 |
| Written test | Afternoon of April 23 (Wednesday) |
| Formal nominees | 5 |
| Reserve candidates | 2, designated first and second reserve candidates |
| Review panel | Seven members, led by Terry Bodenhorn, dean of the School of Liberal Arts |
| Evaluation | English expression, analytical ability, leadership, personality and strengths, together with written-test reading and writing scores |

The notice lists the formal and reserve candidates by cohort and major, but the list contains student names. This article reports only the totals and does not reproduce the individual list. Final participation remains subject to the school's subsequent procedures and confirmation by the partner university.

### Publicity arrangements

The notice states that the publicity period ran from **May 23, 2013, to May 10, 2013**, an obvious conflict in date order. It also asks anyone with objections to submit written or email feedback to the School of Liberal Arts **by May 10** and requires feedback materials to carry the commenter's real name. Applicants and affected students should follow any later notice from the school.
