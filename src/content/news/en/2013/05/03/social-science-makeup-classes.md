It is reported that STU's Academic Affairs Office has arranged make-up sessions for two courses taught by Luo Shitian of the Social Science Department, whose classes were previously suspended. The sessions will be held on May 11 and 12.

## Makeup Schedule

| Course | Class No. | Suspended Sessions | Makeup Session | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics | 58968 | Week 8 Wednesday (Apr 17) periods 3–4; Week 9 Monday (Apr 22) periods A–B; Week 9 Wednesday (Apr 24) periods 3–4 | Week 12 Sunday (May 12), periods 2–3–4 and A, B, C | E406 |
| Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Basics of Law | 58955 | Week 8 Wednesday (Apr 17) periods A, B, C | Week 11 Saturday (May 11), periods A, B, C | E305 |

The Academic Affairs Office reminds students enrolled in these two courses to attend the sessions at the specified times and venues, and to pass word along to classmates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
