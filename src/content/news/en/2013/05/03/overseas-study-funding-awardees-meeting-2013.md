STU's Student Affairs Office has announced that it will hold a meeting for awardees of the 2013 Overseas Study Funding Program at **3:00 p.m. on May 6** in **Room 118 of the Administration Center**. Awardees should attend on time as required by the notice.

### Meeting details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | 2013 Overseas Study Funding Program awardees' meeting |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., May 6 |
| Location | Room 118, Administration Center |
| Participants | Students who received 2013 Overseas Study Funding Program awards |

The notice does not provide an agenda or list the awardees. Relevant students should follow subsequent arrangements from the Student Affairs Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
