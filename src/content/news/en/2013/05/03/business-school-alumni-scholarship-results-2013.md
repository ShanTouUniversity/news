STU's Student Affairs Office has issued Document No. 4 [2013] announcing the results of the Business School Alumni Scholarship for the 2011–2012 academic year. Following review by the Business School review committee, the notice states that **38 students** received awards of **RMB 2,500 each**.

### Award details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scholarship | STU Business School Alumni Scholarship |
| Academic year | 2011–2012 |
| Number stated in the notice | 38 students |
| Award | RMB 2,500 per recipient |
| Reviewing body | Business School review committee |
| Document | STU Student Affairs Document No. 4 [2013] |
| Issuing office | STU Student Affairs Office |
| Date | May 3, 2013 |

### Count discrepancy

In addition to stating a total of 38, the notice lists awardee names by cohort and major. Counting those entries individually produces 14 entries in the 2009 cohort, 15 in the 2010 cohort and 11 in the 2011 cohort, for **40 entries** in total; the 2009 cohort's Accounting section contains six entries. This count conflicts with the notice's stated total of 38 and with the summary table in the April 19 candidate-publicity notice. This article uses the explicitly stated total of 38 and does not infer the actual number of awardees from the entry count.

The awardees came from the 2009, 2010 and 2011 cohorts and the Business School majors of Business Administration, Accounting, International Economics and Trade, Marketing and Finance. Because the original notice contains student names, this article publishes the scholarship and the count discrepancy without reproducing the personal list.
