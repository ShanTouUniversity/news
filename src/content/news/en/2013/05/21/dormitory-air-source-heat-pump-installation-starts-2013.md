Following approval by the relevant authorities, Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office arranged the installation of air-source heat-pump hot-water equipment in student dormitories A–F. Shantou Kerun Energy Equipment Installation Co., Ltd. was the contractor. Construction was scheduled to begin on **May 22, 2013**, and finish by **August 2**.

The construction notice listed the contract price as **RMB 4.1496 million**. An April 26 bid-result notice for the same project had listed the winning bid as **RMB 4,357,041.36**. The two amounts differ, and the source did not explain the discrepancy.

Noise, vibration and dust could occur during construction. For safety, pedestrians were asked to take care around the site. Faculty and staff members were not permitted to enter the construction area without authorization from the university’s functional offices and the contractor, and were asked to avoid lingering nearby.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Infrastructure Office)
