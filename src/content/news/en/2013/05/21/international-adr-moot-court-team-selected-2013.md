Shantou University Law School completed the selection of its team for the 2013 International Alternative Dispute Resolution Moot Court Competition. Following preliminary screening and interviews by the law school’s selection group, the school selected a **six-student team** to represent STU in the competition.

The students’ names are selection-list information and are not published here.
