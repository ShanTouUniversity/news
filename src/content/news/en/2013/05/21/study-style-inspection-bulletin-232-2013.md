Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office issued its study-style inspection bulletin for week 12 of the spring 2012–2013 academic year. The study-style inspection group checked Buildings D and G at **8 a.m. on May 15 and 16** and recorded **seven students** who arrived up to five minutes late for class.

| Inspection date | Location | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 15, Wednesday | Buildings D and G | Four late arrivals of up to five minutes |
| May 16, Thursday | Buildings D and G | Three late arrivals of up to five minutes |

The Student Affairs Office asked the relevant colleges and residential colleges to educate the students involved and continue work on compliance with university rules and study-style development. The individual students’ names are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
