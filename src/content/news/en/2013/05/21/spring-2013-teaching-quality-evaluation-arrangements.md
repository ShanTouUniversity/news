Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office deployed the spring 2013 classroom teaching-quality assessment. Each teaching unit was asked to use the credit-system website to verify course information and complete the revision, submission and grouping of course-assessment indicators.

## Process

1. Teaching administrators at each unit had to log in to `http://credit.stu.edu.cn` and check the current term’s course schedule, including course names, instructors, student lists and enrollment numbers. Changes had to be compiled and sent to the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office.
2. Each unit had to revise the assessment indicators used by its course groups according to its courses.
3. Teaching administrators had to submit the unit’s course-assessment indicators and complete course grouping in the credit system.
4. By **May 28**, each unit had to print its reviewed assessment indicators and course groupings, obtain the signature of the unit head and submit them to the Teaching Reform and Assessment Section of the Academic Affairs Office.

The course-assessment attachment and student personal information in the course schedules are not reproduced here. Readers should follow the original Academic Affairs Office notice for the detailed operation.
