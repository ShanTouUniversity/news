In response to increased demand for literature-indexing certificates, the Shantou University Library decided to provide free **Literature Indexing Reports** to faculty members and students who needed them. Readers could search databases including SCI-E, SSCI, A&HCI and EI, complete the library’s template with information such as the literature title and authors, and send a draft report to Xie Luqing (`lqxie@stu.edu.cn`) or Zeng Zhaohong (`zhzeng@stu.edu.cn`) in the Subject Services Department.

The library would verify the draft, standardize the report information, print it and affix the library seal. Readers could collect the official report from the Subject Services Department within **two working days** after submitting the draft.

## Historical Web of Science Records

STU’s Web of Science database covered records from **2003 onward**. For a WOS report on literature published before 2003, the library would make every effort to contact Thomson Reuters for the original search results. Readers were asked to wait for notification by email or telephone before collecting the official report.
