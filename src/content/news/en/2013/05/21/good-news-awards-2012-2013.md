With support from university units, Shantou University’s campus homepage and news website had published nearly 600 news items since June 2012. To further improve news publicity, the university’s Communist Party Publicity Department and News Center launched the 2012–2013 Good News Awards and invited experienced media professionals from outside the university to serve as judges.

## Eligible Works and Criteria

Eligible works were written reports and photographs published by university units on the campus homepage or news website from **June 2012 through May 2013**. Articles written and photographs taken by the News Center were excluded.

- **Written reports:** They had to be truthful, newsworthy, well structured and concise, and have generated significant attention on campus or in society.
- **Photographs:** They had to be truthful, newsworthy, captured on location, vivid and clear. A submission could be a single photograph or a set from the same news item; a photo set counted as one entry.

## Submission

Each unit could submit no more than **five entries in each category**. Works had to be submitted electronically with the email subject “STU 2012–2013 Good News Awards Entry.” Unit correspondents were required to compile their entries, complete the entry form and send them to the News Center office at `o_pressoffice@stu.edu.cn` by **May 28**; late submissions would not be accepted. The office telephone number was 2368.

## Awards and Prizes

Written reports and photographs would each receive first-, second- and third-prize awards, with certificates and cash prizes:

| Category | First prize | Second prize | Third prize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Written reports | 3 entries, RMB 300 each | 5 entries, RMB 200 each | 10 entries, RMB 100 each |
| Photographs | 2 entries, RMB 300 each | 3 entries, RMB 200 each | 5 entries, RMB 100 each |

The entry form is submission material and is not reproduced here.
