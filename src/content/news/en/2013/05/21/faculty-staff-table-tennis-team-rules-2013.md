Shantou University’s Labor Union adjusted the rules for the 2013 faculty and staff team table-tennis tournament. The tournament would have eight teams. The 2012 champion, the Administration Team, and runner-up, the Resource Management Team, were assigned to Groups A and B respectively; the remaining teams would be grouped by drawing lots. Teams were asked to attend the draw at the university Labor Union office at **10 a.m. on May 22**.

## Teams and Eligibility

The teams represented the College of Science, College of Liberal Arts, College of Engineering, Law and Business joint team, Art/Social Sciences Continuing Education/Affiliated Middle School joint team, Administration Team, Research/News/Library joint team and Resource Management Team. All participants had to be current STU faculty or staff members.

A team from a unit with no more than 80 employees could register up to 12 players; a team from a unit with more than 80 employees could register up to 15. Male players could not enter more than one event, while female players could enter two events.

## Format and Schedule

Matches used a **best-of-three format with 11 points per game**. Each match had to include all five events, in this order: men’s singles, women’s singles, men’s doubles, mixed doubles and women’s doubles. The group stage used a single round-robin format: a win earned two points, a loss one point and a forfeit zero points.

Ties were resolved in the following order: when two teams had equal points, the winner of their match ranked higher; when three teams were tied in a win-loss cycle, rankings were determined by the number of matches won, net matches, net games and match points, with the higher figure ranking higher.

The tournament would begin on **May 27**, with matches starting at **4:30 p.m. each day**. Group-stage matches would be held in the sixth-floor multipurpose hall of the affiliated primary school and the university Labor Union table-tennis room. The semifinals and final would take place in the Labor Union table-tennis room.

## Awards and Requirements

The top four teams would receive awards, while the other teams would receive participation awards. Each participant would receive a commemorative gift. Players were required to arrive on time and follow the referees’ decisions.
