The editorial office of *Chinese-language Literature* at Shantou University will host a lecture titled “Contemporary Taiwanese Culture and Politics” from **3 to 5 p.m. on May 23, 2013**, in Meeting Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center. Professor Gu Yuanqing was the speaker, and faculty members and students across the university were welcome to attend.

The lecture would introduce and assess current Taiwanese party culture, media and publishing culture, literary education and important events in the literary world. It would also discuss Taiwan’s maritime cultural characteristics and related questions surrounding the “Taiwan-style Cultural Revolution.”

Gu is vice president of the Chinese New Literature Society, a professor and director of the Institute of Hong Kong and Taiwan Literature in the Chinese Department of Zhongnan University of Economics and Law. He has also served on doctoral-supervisor review work at Central China Normal University. He has led four projects funded by the Ministry of Education and the National Social Science Fund, and has published 28 monographs in China and abroad, including *A History of Contemporary Literary Theory and Criticism in Mainland China*, *A History of Contemporary Literary Theory and Criticism in Taiwan*, *A History of Contemporary Literary Criticism in Hong Kong*, *A History of Contemporary New Poetry in Taiwan* and *A History of Literary Relations across the Taiwan Strait*.
