Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold four outstanding-alumni sharing sessions from **9 a.m. on Sunday, May 19, 2013**. Faculty members and students across the university were welcome to attend. The sessions were organized by career or industry track:

| Track | Venue |
| --- | --- |
| Broad media | D303 |
| Print media | D301 |
| Non-media | D306 |
| Television | D302 |

The original notice directed readers to promotional posters for the specific topics and alumni biographies. Those poster images were not preserved in the local archive, so this report does not speculate about details that cannot be verified.
