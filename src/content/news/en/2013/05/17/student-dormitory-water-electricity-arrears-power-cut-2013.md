As of **May 17**, some student dormitories had not paid all water and electricity charges from April 2013 or earlier. Under university rules, STU would cut power to the affected dormitories beginning **May 20** and restore service after all outstanding charges were paid.

## Payment and Restoration Windows

From May 20 to 22, the Network Center would process two deductions through the campus-card system each day:

| Deduction | Restoration for dormitories that paid successfully |
| --- | --- |
| 10:30 a.m. | 2:30–4:30 p.m. |
| 4:30 p.m. | 5:30–8 p.m. |

A dormitory that paid after 4:30 p.m. would have power restored from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. the following day. After May 22, deductions would be processed once daily at 4:30 p.m.

Students whose campus-card balance was insufficient were asked to top up at a card-loading machine. Those with insufficient bank-account balances were asked to deposit enough funds during banking hours and complete the campus-card transfer. If a campus card was lost, payment could be made by setting up proxy payment through the water and electricity management system, or by paying cash into the university account at the Bank of China STU branch and submitting the receipt to the Finance Office for posting. For security reasons, the university account number is not reproduced here; the Finance Office did not accept cash directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office)
