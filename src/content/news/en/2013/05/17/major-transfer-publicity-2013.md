Under STU’s Interim Regulations on Credit-Based Student Status Management for Undergraduates (STU〔2005〕No. 67), Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office reviewed the assessment results and related materials for students who applied to change majors this semester and publicized the proposed approvals. The list covered **38 proposed transfers**.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce names, genders, student numbers or previous majors. It gives only the aggregate number proposed for each receiving major:

| Receiving major | Proposed transfers |
| --- | ---: |
| Law | 2 |
| Marketing | 1 |
| International Economics and Trade | 2 |
| Business Administration | 2 |
| Civil Engineering | 6 |
| Computer Science and Applications | 3 |
| Electronic Information Engineering | 2 |
| Communication Engineering | 1 |
| Applied Chemistry | 5 |
| Applied Physics | 1 |
| Mathematics and Applied Mathematics | 1 |
| Chinese Language and Literature | 1 |
| Radio and Television Journalism | 2 |
| Journalism | 1 |
| Clinical Medicine (five-year program) | 6 |
| Nursing | 2 |

The notice gives **May 23, 2012** as the deadline for feedback, which conflicts with its May 17, 2013 publication date. The applicable deadline should be confirmed in a subsequent Academic Affairs Office notice. Feedback could be submitted in writing, in person or by telephone to `0754-82902402`, or by email at `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn`.
