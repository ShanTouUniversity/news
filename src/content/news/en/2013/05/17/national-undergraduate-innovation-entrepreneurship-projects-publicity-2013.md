Following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University reviewed applications and the results of its 2013 provincial project assessment and proposed 25 main-campus projects for the 2013 National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Plan. The proposed projects were publicized for feedback.

The publicity period ran from **May 17 to May 22, 2013**. Objections could be submitted in writing to the Academic Affairs Office. The contact was Guo Yijia at `yjguo@stu.edu.cn` or `0754-86502736`.

10. “Changes in and Adaptation of Folk Religious Activities in Traditional Communities during Urbanization: The Case of the Parade and Festival in Yanzao Township, Chenghai District, Shantou, Guangdong”
