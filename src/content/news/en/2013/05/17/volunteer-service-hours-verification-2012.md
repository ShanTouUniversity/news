The Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association completed its summary of volunteer service hours recorded from **January through December 2012** and announced a verification process. Volunteers who found an error were asked to report it to the university association’s service-hours management group by **May 21**. Staff would verify the feedback and correct confirmed omissions or mistakes.

**May 23–26** was designated as the review period. Volunteers whose reported issues had not been corrected were asked to contact the staff again during that period. Individual service-hour records and student staff members’ personal contact details are not reproduced here; verification procedures should follow the original notice issued by the university Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Communist Youth League Committee and Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association)
>
> ⚠️ Individual volunteer-hour records and related attachments are available in the original OA notice through the OA system. Student personal information is not reproduced here.
