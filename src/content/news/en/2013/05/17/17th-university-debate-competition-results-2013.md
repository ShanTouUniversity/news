After preliminary rounds, the quarterfinals, semifinals, third-place match and championship match, the “Keep Moving, Keep Thinking” 17th Shantou University Student Debate Competition concluded. The School of Journalism won the championship, the Business School finished second and the School of Medicine finished third.

## Individual Awards

To protect student privacy, student names are shown as family names followed by the initials of the given name in pinyin.

| Stage | Best debater | Most popular debater |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary rounds | Yao RK (Zhicheng College), Wu ZZ (School of Medicine), Yu ZY (School of Arts qualifying team) and Xu JM (School of Law) | Meng L (School of Arts), Wu ZZ (School of Medicine), Xi Z (College of Liberal Arts qualifying team) and Chen B (College of Engineering) |
| Quarterfinals | Yao RK (Zhicheng College), Wu ZZ (School of Medicine), Huang JW (Business School) and Chen B (College of Engineering) | Yao RK (Zhicheng College), Zhao FD (Graduate School), Yu ZY (School of Arts qualifying team) and Zhang YT (College of Science) |
| Semifinals | Huang JW (Business School) and Wang MQ (School of Journalism) | Lin HL (School of Medicine) and Zhang YT (College of Science) |
| Third-place match | Cai XL (College of Science) | Wang J (School of Medicine) |
| Championship match | Wang RZ (Business School) | Wang RZ (Business School) |

The university Youth League Committee and Student Union congratulated the winning teams and students and thanked faculty members and students for supporting the competition.
