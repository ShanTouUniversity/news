The Shantou University Communist Youth League Committee and Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association launched the 2013 service-learning public-welfare project initiative under the theme **“Create Public Benefit Without Limits.”** The initiative was open to all faculty members and students, who were required to apply in teams of at least three people.

## Project Categories

Projects fell into three categories:

- **Public-welfare practice:** volunteer services for a particular social group.
- **Creative public welfare:** research and service activities based on a chosen creative topic, including small inventions or creations for service recipients, with an emphasis on solving practical problems.
- **Social research:** field research leading to a report or news story that reflected service recipients’ actual circumstances.

Teams could choose when to implement their projects from **June through December 2013**. The three categories would include no more than 10 approved projects in principle. Each project could request funding according to its needs, up to **RMB 2,000**.

Teams interested in applying were asked to complete the application form for the relevant category and email the electronic version to `volunteersofstu@163.com` by **midnight on May 22**. The required email subject was “2013 Shantou University Service-Learning Public-Welfare Project Application.” The organizers planned to hold a project defense on **May 25**, with the exact time to be announced separately.

The project application templates and other attachments are application materials and are not reproduced here. Applicants should follow the requirements in the original OA notice.
