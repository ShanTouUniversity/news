Shantou University’s School of Law will hold an experience-sharing and information session on the Jessup International Law Moot Court and Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot. The session was intended for faculty members and students interested in the competitions and in developing their general abilities, legal training and English skills.

The event was originally scheduled for **2:30–5 p.m. on Wednesday, May 22**, in Law School Room 213. A subsequent scheduling notice moved it 30 minutes earlier, to **2 p.m. in Room E208**. Attendees were asked to follow the revised time and venue.

## Competition Experience

During the fall and spring semesters of the 2012–2013 academic year, the School of Law’s teams took part in the Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot’s Beijing “CIETAC Cup,” Shanghai invitational and Vienna international competitions, as well as the Jessup International Law Moot’s Beijing China regional selection and international competition in Washington, D.C. The teams achieved what the notice described as historic breakthroughs. Their participation promoted the School of Law and STU while strengthening competitors’ professional and practical abilities.

The session would introduce the two competitions and share the teams’ experience and reflections. Gu Junfeng of the School of Law would provide guidance and share his perspective. Participation in the competitions could give students opportunities to exchange in Beijing, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Vienna, Austria and Washington, D.C., broaden their professional horizons and gain practical experience.
