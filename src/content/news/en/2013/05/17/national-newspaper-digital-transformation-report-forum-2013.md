A forum for the national newspaper digital-transformation survey will be held at Shantou University’s New Library Lecture Hall on **May 23, 2013**, following a notice from the Periodicals Division of the national press and publication authority. The forum will formally release the survey’s findings and conclusions and invite representatives of newspaper groups and guests from other sectors to share and discuss their experience with digital transformation.

The project was described as the first nationwide comprehensive survey of digital transformation in the newspaper industry and involved 35 newspaper groups. The forum was scheduled for **9 a.m. to noon** and **2:30 to 5 p.m.**

Invited speakers included Professor Fan Yijin, dean of Jinan University’s School of Journalism and Communication; Professor Fung Ying-him, dean of the School of Journalism and Communication at The Chinese University of Hong Kong; Professor Zhang Zhian, deputy dean of Sun Yat-sen University’s School of Communication and Design; and Zhang Lifen, deputy editor and editor-in-chief of the Chinese-language website of the *Financial Times* in the United Kingdom.

Faculty members and students could reserve a place at `http://activity.stu.edu.cn/` from the time of publication until **11 a.m. on Monday, May 20**. Attendance was limited to available places.
