Shantou University’s Finance Office began reimbursement work for selected treasury-funded projects and reminded project leaders to accelerate the execution of their public-finance budgets. Under the provincial rules on managing surplus and carried-over funds, balances from projects funded in the provincial budget would in principle be returned to the province’s general budget. Funds carried into the following year but still unused would also generally be treated as surplus and returned.

Projects with slow execution that were expected to produce substantial carry-over or surplus funds by the end of the year were asked to promptly apply to reduce the current-year budget or reallocate it to newly added priority expenditures during implementation, following the required approval procedures.

## Special Funds with Allocations Available

- 2009–2012 special funds for discipline development and quality engineering
- 2009–2012 undergraduate teaching-quality and teaching-reform projects
- 2011–2012 special funds for talent recruitment
- Central-government funds supporting the development of local universities
- 2012 provincial natural-science funds
- Industry-university research cooperation special funds

Projects whose historical allocations had not yet been issued were to await a subsequent notice.
