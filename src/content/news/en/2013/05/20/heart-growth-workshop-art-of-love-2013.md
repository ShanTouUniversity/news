Shantou University’s Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will hold an “Art of Love: How to Develop the Ability to Love” workshop in its “Heart Growth” series from **7 to 9 p.m. on May 25** in Room 736 of Building 789. Lin Rong would lead the session, which was limited to 12 participants.

The workshop would approach love through real-life relationships and emotional communication, asking how people can express, accept and decline love and resolve conflicts in romantic relationships. It was divided into three sections:

1. Meeting through love
2. On the road of love
3. Love tomorrow

Those interested were asked to download and complete the application form and email it to `o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn` by **noon on May 24**. Places would be assigned in order of registration, and the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center would notify selected participants by email. The application form is personal application material and is not reproduced here.
