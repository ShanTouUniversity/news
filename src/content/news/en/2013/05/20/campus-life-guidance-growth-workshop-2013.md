Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold a campus-life guidance “Growth” workshop from **8 to 10 p.m. on Sunday, May 26, 2013**. The workshop was open to undergraduates from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts, with 12 places available. If applications exceeded the quota, places would be assigned in registration order.

The workshop aimed to help students choose campus cultural activities according to their personal development needs, build skills and competencies through extracurricular activities, and plan and enrich their university lives. It included four sections:

1. **Self-awareness:** recognizing one’s own traits
2. **Meditation experience:** using focused experience to accept oneself
3. **Experience sharing:** invited faculty members and other mentors sharing their experience with campus cultural activities
4. **Ability awareness:** exploring how extracurricular activities can strengthen personal abilities and competencies

The application deadline was **noon on Friday, May 24**. The application form and submission procedure are personal registration materials; applicants should follow the requirements in the original Student Affairs Office notice. The form and applicants’ personal contact details are not reproduced here.
