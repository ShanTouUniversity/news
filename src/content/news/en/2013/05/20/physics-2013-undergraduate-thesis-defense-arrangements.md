Shantou University’s Physics Department announced the defense arrangements for its 2013 undergraduate theses. The defenses would begin at **2 p.m. on Wednesday, May 29**, continue until 4 p.m., pause for 10 minutes, and resume at 4:10 p.m. until completion.

## Groups and Committees

The defenses would be held in four groups:

| 1 | G403 | Qiu Guiming |
| 2 | G404 | Zhu Weian |
| 3 | G406 | Ma Wenhui |
| 4 | G407 | Chen Changjin |

Li Shaohui chaired the defense committee. Its members were Ma Wenhui, Chen Changjin, Qiu Guiming and Zhu Weian. Student group assignments and recorders’ personal information are not reproduced here.

## Preparation and Procedure

Students were asked to submit their thesis or design work to their supervisors early for review. Before the defense, the work had to be organized and bound as required, reviewed by the supervisor, and accompanied by the supervisor’s comments and score before being submitted to the defense-group chair. Students whose work had not arrived or whose supervisor had not approved them for defense could not enter the procedure.

Students had to prepare a PowerPoint presentation and practice in advance, keeping the thesis presentation to **no more than eight minutes**. They were asked to arrive early and copy all presentation files to the designated computer. Each defense consisted of a student presentation followed by questions from faculty members, with **two to three minutes** for answers. Afterward, students were asked to organize the questions and their responses and email them to the recorder for their group.

## Assessment

Supervisors and reviewers would each recommend a score, while the department defense committee or defense group would determine the final overall result. Thesis and design work would receive one of five grades with written comments:

- **Excellent:** The expected task was completed outstandingly, with original insights, a correct and well-structured plan, strong independent-work ability, clear concepts and accurate answers during the defense.
- **Good:** The expected task was completed comprehensively, with feasible views, a basically correct and well-structured plan, and generally correct answers.
- **Average:** The task was basically completed and the views were generally correct, although non-critical errors remained; the plan was broadly reasonable and the student could generally answer questions.
- **Pass:** The task requirements were completed only marginally, with unscientific or isolated non-critical fundamental errors and weak independent-work ability, but the student could answer the basic questions.
- **Fail:** The expected task was not completed, or was completed only with extensive dependence on the supervisor; substantial fundamental errors remained, concepts were unclear or major questions were answered incorrectly.

## Outstanding Thesis Review

Each defense group would recommend three outstanding theses, for a total of 12, for a second defense at **2:30 p.m. on May 31** in the college conference room. The defense committee would make the final selection of outstanding theses.
