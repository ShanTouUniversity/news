Shantou University will interview students participating in its Antarctic expedition project from **2:30 to 6:30 p.m. on Wednesday, May 22**, in ACC Room 2. The selection would use leaderless group discussions. Group assignments were provided in the original notice attachment and are not reproduced here.

## Selection Components

- **Nan’ao training-camp assessment: 30%**
- **Expedition-proposal assessment: 40%**, focusing on research feasibility, academic value and significance, the reasonableness of the research methods, and the project’s impact on STU faculty members and students
- **Comprehensive interview assessment: 30%**, including academic performance, participation in on- and off-campus activities, English proficiency and interview performance

## Interview Process

Each group’s interview would last **25 minutes**. Students would have one minute to prepare, followed by a 10-minute leaderless group discussion, 10 minutes of questions from the panel and five minutes of English questions, with one minute for each participant. The discussion topic would be selected by drawing lots immediately before the discussion.

Participants were asked to arrive at the ACC first-floor lobby 10 minutes before their assigned session to register and wait.
