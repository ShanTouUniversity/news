With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Shantou University launched an Antarctic expedition project in 2013. The project used an Antarctic expedition proposal competition, polar general-education courses and lectures, and a series of polar exhibitions to offer students varied learning experiences and strengthen their ability to apply their academic knowledge.

To increase awareness of the polar regions, oceans and global warming as public environmental issues and encourage environmentally responsible behavior and social responsibility, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Student Affairs Office, Institute of Marine Biology and Hong Kong Wild Direction Company jointly organized a series of polar exhibitions.

One exhibition, “Hello, Antarctica! Polar, Ocean, Exploration and Culture Knowledge Materials Exhibition,” was scheduled for **May 20 to June 29, 2013**, on the **first floor of the library**. It presented knowledge and exploration culture related to the polar regions, oceans, environment and culture.
