The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication announced its FILM 2013 series. The program included the “Television News: Stories Seen Through the Lens” talks and screenings, FILM FORWARD documentaries, and a sharing event for outstanding documentaries from the Media Convergence Laboratory’s first five years. Events were scheduled from **May 23 to 25** in the library lecture hall and Science Lecture Hall.

## Television-News Events

| Time | Program | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 23, 7:30–9:30 p.m. | “Television News: Stories Seen Through the Lens”; *Oriental Horizon* session; screening of episode 8, “Making an MTV,” from *Days That Lit the Ideal: The Legend of Oriental Horizon*; speakers Zhang Jie and Liang Bibo | Library lecture hall |
| May 24, 7:30–9:30 p.m. | “Television News: Stories Seen Through the Lens”; “Reflections of a Television News Professional on Historical Documentary Production”; speaker Hu Jincao | Library lecture hall |

At the time, Zhang Jie was a director with *Oriental Son* and former deputy director of the News Commentary Department. Liang Bibo was a director at the Expert Studio of Chengdu Television and director of the Liang Bibo Studio at China Central Television’s China Television Media Group. Hu Jincao was a director with the *News Probe* program in CCTV’s News Commentary Department.

## FILM FORWARD Screenings

The series also presented documentary and narrative films described in the notice as part of a “Sundance Film Festival” program. The screenings that can be confirmed from the film timetable were:

| Time | Library lecture hall | Science Lecture Hall |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 24, 9:30–11:30 a.m. | *Town of Runners* | — |
| May 24, 2:30–5:30 p.m. | *Bones Brigade: An Autobiography* | *For Once in My Life* |
| May 25, 9:30–11:30 a.m. | *The Loving Story* | *Burning the Future: Coal in America* |
| May 25, 2:30–5:30 p.m. | *Under the Same Moon* | *Chasing Ice* |

The attached FILM FORWARD film guide also listed *Valley of Saints*, but the detailed timetable did not show a separate screening slot for it. Its time and venue are therefore not inferred here.

The six FILM FORWARD films listed in the guide were:

- ** *Bones Brigade: An Autobiography* **, directed by Stacy Peralta: a group of young people who rejected mainstream culture turned to skateboarding and, mentored by a former world champion, formed one of the sport’s most influential teams.
- ** *Chasing Ice* **, directed by Jeff Orlowski: environmental photographer James Balog’s Extreme Ice Survey used multi-year time-lapse images to document vanishing Arctic glaciers and evidence of climate change.
- ** *Under the Same Moon* ** (*La Misma Luna*), directed by Patricia Riggen: when Carlitos’s mother went to the United States to seek a better life, their bond led him on an extraordinary journey to find her.
- ** *The Loving Story* **, directed by Nancy Buirski: the film examined the criminal trial and civil-rights struggle of an interracial couple in Virginia during the Jim Crow era.
- ** *Town of Runners* **, directed by Jerry Rothwell: the film followed three teenagers from Bekoji, Ethiopia, as they pursued their running heroes from school track to national competition; runners from the town had won 10 Olympic gold medals over the preceding 20 years.
- ** *Valley of Saints* **, directed and written by Musa Syeed: set around Kashmir’s Dal Lake, the film explored the relationship between two friends and a female researcher studying environmental degradation amid political unrest.

The timetable also listed two additional films:

- ** *Burning the Future: Coal in America* **, United States, 2011, 54 minutes, directed by David Novack. The film examined conflict between the coal industry and residents of West Virginia, including environmental damage from coal extraction and local resistance.
- ** *For Once in My Life* **, 2010, 94 minutes, directed by Jim Bigham. The film followed a band of singers and musicians with various physical and intellectual disabilities as they used their musical abilities to challenge prejudice and express themselves.

## STU Student Documentaries

“Our Documentaries” would present outstanding documentaries produced during the Media Convergence Laboratory’s first five years and invite viewers to share in STU students’ documentary work. The main text scheduled the event for **7:30–9:30 p.m. on May 25** in the **library lecture hall**, while the attached timetable listed **7:30–9 p.m.** The two source times conflict; attendees should follow a subsequent arrangement from the organizers.
