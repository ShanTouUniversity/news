Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced that the **2013 spring-term course final examination schedule** had been arranged and published. The main-campus teaching units were asked to notify their faculty members and students to log in to the credit-system website at `http://credit.stu.edu.cn/` to check the examination information and prepare in advance.

## Examination-Room and Proctoring Requirements

Proctors and students must arrive at the designated examination room **10 minutes early** in accordance with the university's proctoring rules and examination-room conduct rules. Before the examination begins, proctors should arrange seats, read the rules, count examinees and check identification. Candidates must bring their student cards, follow the seating arrangements, and complete their papers independently and honestly. Unless expressly permitted for a course, books, notes, reference materials, communication tools and other electronic devices may not be brought to the desk.

Candidates who are no more than 30 minutes late may enter only with the chief proctor's approval, and the examination time will not be extended. Candidates who are more than 30 minutes late, absent without authorization, or absent without an approved deferred-examination request will be treated as having missed the examination. Candidates may submit their papers and leave only after the first 30 minutes; leaving during the examination will be treated as submission. At the end of the examination, candidates must submit their papers as instructed and leave the room.

## Examination-Paper Management and Archiving

| Stage | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Question setting | Follow the STU Course Assessment Management Measures. Final examinations must prepare A and B papers with broadly comparable question volume and difficulty |
| Marking and grades | Instructors should generally complete marking and grade assessment within three days after the examination; courses with larger enrollments may take up to one week |
| Printing | Complete an examination-paper printing request and submit it with the questions and candidate list to the Academic Affairs Office printing center (Wendong 404) one week before the examination |
| Dragon Boat Festival adjustment | Courses scheduled for examination week 17 must submit their examination materials to the printing center in advance during the current week, week 15, to avoid disruption |
| Confidentiality and collection | Question setters must personally deliver papers for printing. Chief examiners should collect papers as required; papers for examinations beginning at 8:00 a.m. must be collected the previous afternoon. A proxy must present written authorization or certification from the chief examiner's unit |
| Archiving | After completing a self-check with the examination-paper inspection form, instructors must submit the examination materials and form to the academic administrator of the offering unit for storage |

The Academic Affairs Office asked all teaching units to follow the *STU Course Assessment Management Measures*, *STU Proctoring Rules*, *STU Examination-Room Conduct Rules* and examination-question confidentiality regulations.
