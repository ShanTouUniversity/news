It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 20, 2013, regarding an online prize quiz to be held during the “Polar, Ocean, Expedition and Culture Exhibition” and the “Marine Life and Expedition Equipment Exhibition” from late May to late June.

## Exhibition and Quiz Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exhibitions | Polar, ocean, expedition and culture exhibition; marine life and expedition equipment exhibition |
| Venues | Library and Administration Center |
| Period | Late May to late June 2013 |
| Questions | 10 questions per exhibition, 20 in total on polar topics |
| Eligibility | Current students |
| How to participate | Scan the QR code on the exhibition boards to access the quiz page, or visit http://expedition.stu.edu.cn/exhibit/mobile.aspx |
| Lucky draw | After the event, winners will be randomly drawn by computer from those who answer all questions correctly |
| Prizes | One commemorative quick-dry T-shirt from the Antarctic Expedition Project per winner |
| Number of winners | 50 |
| Deadline | 24:00, June 25, 2013 |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

> ⚠️ The QR code and quiz link are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
