The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host a lecture titled “Splendid and Exquisitely Crafted: Decoration in Chaoshan Vernacular Architecture” from **7:30 to 10 p.m. on Tuesday, May 21, 2013**, in the school’s stepped lecture room. Cai Haisong was the speaker, and faculty members and students across the university were welcome to attend.

Cai has researched traditional Chaoshan architecture for more than two decades and documented changes in the region’s traditional buildings through photography. He has published monographs including *Chaoshan Vernacular Architecture* and *Chaoshan Dwellings*. He also held the “Cai Haisong Photography Exhibition of Chaoshan Vernacular Architecture” at the school’s art museum in October 2002 and gave a related lecture in STU’s library lecture hall in October 2012.

This lecture would focus more specifically on the interior decoration of Chaoshan architecture. The research would be more detailed than in Cai’s previous lectures and would feature a larger selection of photographic examples, most of which were to be shown publicly for the first time.
