China Telecom Shantou and China Mobile Shantou had sent Shantou University the **April 2013 electronic bills for office telephones**, and the related data had been imported into the telephone-management system. Verification staff at each university unit could log in at `http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn` and check the “China Telecom landlines” and “China Mobile landlines” sections separately.

For bills without objections, staff were asked to click “Confirm selected bills.” If a bill detail did not match actual telephone use, objections had to be emailed by **May 24**: China Telecom landline issues to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn`, and China Mobile landline issues to `stunic@stu.edu.cn`.

After the verification period, bills for which no objection had been submitted or no confirmation action had been taken would be confirmed automatically by the system. After verification, the Finance Office would deduct the charges on **May 27** using the budget-funding numbers provided by each unit.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center and Resource Management Office)
