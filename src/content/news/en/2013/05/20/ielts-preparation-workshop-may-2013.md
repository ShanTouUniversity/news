It is reported that Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold an IELTS Preparation Workshop from **10 to 11:30 a.m. on Friday, May 24, 2013**, at the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL). Maria Nikologianni and Catherine Bai would lead the workshop.

Seats were limited. Interested participants could reserve a seat at `http://ieltsws.eventbrite.com`.
