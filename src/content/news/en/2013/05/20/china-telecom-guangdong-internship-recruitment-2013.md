China Telecom Guangdong was recruiting interns for 2013 from **university students graduating in 2014, including graduate students**. The company planned to recruit **1,159 interns** across Guangdong, mainly for sales, technology and internet-product operations positions.

## Internship Arrangements

The internship was scheduled in principle for **July 1 to August 31, 2013**. China Telecom Guangdong would determine each intern’s period according to the student’s actual vacation and academic-calendar dates and sign an internship agreement. The internship would generally last at least one month. Interns would receive a living allowance specified in the agreement, but housing would not be provided. Outstanding interns could receive priority consideration in China Telecom Guangdong’s 2014 campus recruitment.

Applications were submitted individually from **May 15 to May 31**. Interested applicants could visit China Telecom Guangdong’s recruitment website at `http://www.hr.gdtel.com.cn/` to review the positions and apply.

## Recruiting Company

China Telecom Group is a state-owned integrated communications and information-services company whose businesses include fixed-line telephone, mobile, satellite communications, internet access and applications. The recruitment letter said that China Telecom Guangdong had 21 city branches, 142 county- and district-level branches, 17 secondary centers and two specialized branches, with approximately 45,000 employees serving more than 50 million customers.

The recruitment letter was dated **May 10, 2013**, while STU’s Academic Affairs Office forwarded the internship information on **May 20**.
