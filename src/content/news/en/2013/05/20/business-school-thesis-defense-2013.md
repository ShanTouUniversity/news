It is reported that Shantou University Business School has announced arrangements for its 2013 undergraduate thesis defenses.

The defense committee is chaired by Xu Zongling, with members Huang Yan, Li Yue, Zeng Qiang, Ouyang Feng, Huang Wangming, Song Xiaobao, Liu Wei, Yao Mingan, Yan Bo, Qin Zhong, Chen Errui, Du Mian, and Ji Yun. Group assignments and the detailed timetable are provided in the attachment.

The defense procedure comprises seven steps: the group leader announces the arrangements; each candidate presents the thesis, covering main arguments, objectives, structure, and key features, generally within 10 minutes; committee members pose questions; candidates respond on the spot; teachers evaluate performance on the "Shantou University Undergraduate Thesis (Project) Evaluation Form (for Defense Committee)"; after all defenses in the group are completed, scores are tallied to determine the comprehensive grade; and the group submits comments and materials to the Academic Affairs Office for archiving.

Other requirements include a cap on excellent theses at no more than 15% of candidates per group, handling of failed defenses in accordance with relevant regulations, a one-week deadline for revisions when formatting does not meet standards, and referral of disputed matters to the school-level defense committee.
