Shantou University’s Graduate School has issued a notice on the evaluation of graduate assistantship positions for **spring 2013**. The university established **130 positions** during the semester, including 82 teaching-assistant positions and 48 administrative-assistant positions.

## Evaluation Arrangements

| Position type | Evaluation requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Teaching assistant | The instructor of the course hosting the position evaluates the graduate teaching assistant and completes the *Graduate Teaching Assistant Evaluation Form* |
| Administrative assistant | The host unit evaluates the graduate working in the position, completes the *Graduate Administrative Assistant Evaluation Form* and attaches proof that the unit paid the position allowance |

Relevant units must consolidate the materials and submit them to the **Graduate School, Room 401 of the New Administration Center, before the end of work on June 13**. Positions that are not evaluated, or whose host units cannot provide proof of allowance payment, will not be accepted for **fall 2013 graduate assistantship applications**.

The notice includes a position-information table and evaluation forms for teaching and administrative/research assistant positions. The attachments contain personal and position details such as graduate names, student numbers, courses, supervisors, contact information, work records and evaluations; these details are not reproduced here. The notice body counts 48 positions as administrative-assistant posts, while one attached form is titled for administrative/research assistant positions. This article preserves the distinction in the source wording.
