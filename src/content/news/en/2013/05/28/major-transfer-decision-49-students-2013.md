Shantou University has issued a decision on student major transfers under its undergraduate credit-based student-status regulations. After the relevant public-notice periods received no objections, the university approved major transfers for **49 students**, effective from the **summer semester of the 2013–2014 academic year**.

The decision covers **38 students** whose proposed transfers were posted by the Academic Affairs Office for one week, as well as **11 high-level athletes and arts students** whose transfers were posted through the Sports Department and the School of Arts. The formal university decision and OA attachments remain the authoritative sources.

To protect student privacy, this article does not publish names, genders, student numbers, previous majors or receiving majors. The complete rosters and individual student-record information are available in the original OA attachments.
