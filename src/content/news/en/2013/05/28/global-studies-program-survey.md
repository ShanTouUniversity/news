It is reported that the International Center of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University is preparing to establish a Global Studies major, together with a corresponding double-degree or minor option. In May 2013, it launched an anonymous questionnaire to collect ideas and suggestions from across the university.

## What Is Global Studies

According to the introduction, Global Studies examines the interrelationships among society, politics, economics, and culture in a globalized world. The program aims to provide students with a solid foundation to grow into global citizens who can work in a global economy, live in multicultural societies, and make decisions based on well-informed judgment.

## Double Degree and Minor Explained

The survey outlined the two study formats:

| Format | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Double Degree | A degree earned after completing two majors concurrently; STU requires at least 45 credits for the second major |
| Minor | A supplementary field of study pursued alongside a student's primary major; STU requires at least 30 credits for a minor |

## Anonymous Survey for Feedback

Participation in the survey is voluntary and responses are anonymous; the questionnaire is available through the Academic Affairs Office website. The College of Liberal Arts said that feedback from students and faculty will serve as an important reference for the program's development.
