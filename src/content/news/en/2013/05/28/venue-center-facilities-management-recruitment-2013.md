Shantou University’s Resource Management Office advertised **one Venue Center facilities-management position** on an auxiliary-contract basis. The role covers maintenance of venue electromechanical systems, technical support for university events and related facility-management work.

## Position Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Position | One Venue Center facilities-management staff member |
| Main duties | Daily management, repair and maintenance of lighting, central air-conditioning, elevators and other electromechanical facilities, fire-safety systems, security closed-circuit monitoring systems, special classrooms and event venues, together with technical support for university activities |
| Employment arrangement | Auxiliary contract; the personnel relationship will not be transferred to STU |
| Compensation | According to applicable university standards |

## Qualifications

Applicants should have sound professional conduct and teamwork skills, be no older than 35, be physically fit and be able to work **24-hour shifts**. The notice requires a technical-school or full-time junior-college qualification in electrical work, electromechanical equipment, electronic technology or a related field, together with a relevant vocational work certificate. Relevant experience or familiarity with ISO 9001 quality-management standards is preferred. The notice also lists other policy-based eligibility requirements; applicants should consult the original notice for details.

Application materials were to be sent by email or post to the General Office of STU’s Resource Management Office. The notice requests a résumé and copies of identity, education, professional-training and technical-qualification documents. Shortlisted applicants would be notified for an interview; personal application materials and form fields are not reproduced here.

The application address is **General Office, Resource Management Office, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong**, postcode 515063. The listed contacts are Hong and Chen, at `86502827` or `yhhong@stu.edu.cn`.

## Date Note

The archived and database notice date is **May 28, 2013**, but the body says that application materials were due **by June 8, 2012**. This source conflict is retained; the current status of the position and its application arrangements should be confirmed with the Resource Management Office.
