Shantou University will hold a **campus open day on June 10, 2013**, welcoming visitors from the wider community. To support reception and service work, the university is recruiting **248 student volunteers** from across the university to assist with visitor reception and on-site services.

## Recruitment Arrangements

Volunteers should have good communication and teamwork skills, be responsible and willing to follow arrangements, and be able to represent STU with courtesy. Applicants must also meet two scheduling requirements:

- Attend the open-day volunteer training in full from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on June 7**;
- Participate in the volunteer service throughout the **June 10 open day** and not join another volunteer activity that day.

Applications close at **noon on June 5**. Students may apply through <http://activity.stu.edu.cn/>. The university will select applicants according to the number of applications, with earlier applications taking priority in the event of equal conditions. The selected list will be posted on the university’s office-information website.

Under the relevant STU Youth Volunteer registration rules, the service hours credited to registered volunteers will be based on the actual hours served on the day of the activity. The notice also lists a personal student contact number for questions; it is omitted here.

## Volunteer Selection Results

The STU Communist Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association announced the Open Day volunteer selection results on **June 6, 2013**. As of June 5, the university had received **363 reservation applications**. Applying the principles of merit-based selection and earlier registration in the event of equal qualifications, it selected **188 official volunteers** and **20 alternate volunteers**.

Official volunteers were required to attend the Sixth Open Day training from **7:30 to 8:30 p.m. on Friday, June 7** in **Classroom E101**, and prepare for volunteer service on June 10. The earlier recruitment notice planned to recruit 248 volunteers, while the selection-results notice announced 188 official volunteers and 20 alternates; both notices' figures are retained here. The two notices also record different training periods: 7:30–9:30 p.m. in the recruitment notice and 7:30–8:30 p.m. in the selection-results notice.
