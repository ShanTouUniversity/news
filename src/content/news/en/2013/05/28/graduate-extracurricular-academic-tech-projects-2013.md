Shantou University’s Graduate School has opened its **2013 graduate extracurricular academic and technology project** program to all enrolled graduate students. Applications will be accepted from **May 29 through June 14**.

## Eligibility and Categories

Projects must be completed by students during their enrollment using their spare time and must be independent of the teaching plan. Graduation theses, graduation-design projects and academic-year papers are not eligible. Cross-disciplinary and cross-major teams are encouraged.

The notice body lists four project categories:

- Social-science survey reports and academic papers
- Literary and artistic works
- Business plans
- Invention and production works

The attached application form also includes a category for academic papers in the sciences, engineering, agriculture and medicine. This difference is preserved from the source; applicants should follow the categories in the application materials issued by the Graduate School.

## Application and Review Schedule

Applicants must complete the *Shantou University Extracurricular Academic and Technology Research Project Application Form* in duplicate, submit it to the **Graduate School, Room 401 of the New Administration Center, by June 14**, and send an electronic copy to `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn`. The notice names Wang Fengqi as the contact person.

The Graduate School will organize expert review, and the notice says approved projects are expected to be announced on the Graduate School website and through the OA system at the end of June. The attached progress schedule instead lists **June 16–23** for review and says the approved project list and funding should be announced **by June 25**. It schedules agreement signing for **June 26–July 2**, a midterm inspection in mid-September and the final review in early January of the following year. These source-level timing differences are retained.

The application form requests project names, research plans, budgets, member and contact information, supervisor comments and other application materials. Personal application information and form fields are not reproduced here; applicants should follow the original OA attachment for submission requirements.
