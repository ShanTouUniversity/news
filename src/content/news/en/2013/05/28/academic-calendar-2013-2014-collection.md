The **2013–2014 academic calendar** has been prepared. The Academic Affairs Office asks each unit at the main campus to count its current faculty, staff and student population and collect the calendar by **May 31** from the Academic Affairs service window on the **third floor of the New Administration Building**.

The count should include current faculty, staff and students but exclude students graduating that year and students admitted for 2013. Units should complete the count before collecting the calendars.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
