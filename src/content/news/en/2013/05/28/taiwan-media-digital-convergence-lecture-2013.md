The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will host a lecture from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, May 31, 2013**. **Guo Wuhuan**, director of the Overseas News Center at the Central News Agency, will speak on **“Taiwan Media amid Surging Digital Convergence.”** The lecture will take place in **ACC Meeting Room 3** and is open to the entire university community.

The lecture is expected to examine Taiwan media in the context of digital technology and media convergence. Further details will follow the on-site arrangements.
