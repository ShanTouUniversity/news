Shantou University Library has opened trial access to the **Global Product Database (GPD)** through **December 31, 2013**. Students and faculty can access it through the library database page: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=369>.

## Database Content

GPD organizes information from large manufacturing enterprises, including company information, product catalogs, product manuals, standard product images, technical materials, product CAD drawings, audiovisual resources and 3D models. Its coverage includes:

- General and specialized machinery
- Transportation equipment
- Electronics and communications
- Instruments and meters
- Electrical machinery
- Materials and supplies

Trial feedback and comments may be sent to the Library’s Subject Services Department at `86503247` or `yxu@stu.edu.cn`.
