The Shantou University Science and Technology Association is recruiting candidates from across the student body for its 13th Executive Committee, with the transition planned for **early June 2013**. Guided by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the association organizes extracurricular academic and scientific activities, science-popularization programs and related services, and supports the Challenge Cup and undergraduate extracurricular academic and scientific projects.

## Positions and Eligibility

The 13th Executive Committee is planned to have **nine executive members**:

- One president and two vice presidents
- One head each for academic competitions, the Science Popularization Group, the office, publicity, public relations and projects

Applicants should have strong academic and personal records, an interest in science and technology and its communication, organizational and social-activity skills, knowledge of the association, an innovative outlook and the ability to serve continuously for one academic year. Applicants for presidium positions must also have served at ministerial level or above in the association or another student organization.

## Selection and Scoring

The notice says that the transition committee will conduct interviews and produce **six presidium candidates** and **12 department candidates** for the final campaign defense. The association’s full membership will elect the new committee at the annual summary, commendation and 13th Executive Committee election meeting.

The attachment gives the following scoring rules:

- For the president and department-head selection, the total score consists of the work proposal (20%), work record (30%), first-round interview performance (30%) and transition-meeting performance (20%). For candidates who are not association members, the work-record component is zero and the first-round interview component becomes 60%.
- For the presidium-assistant selection, the total score consists of work record (40%), first-round interview performance (40%) and transition-meeting performance (20%). For non-members, the corresponding proportions become 0%, 70% and 30%, and the highest total score wins.
- The attachment also says that six people are admitted to the department-head selection by total score. This appears to describe a different stage from the notice’s 12 department candidates entering the final defense; the source does not clarify the relationship, so both statements are retained.
