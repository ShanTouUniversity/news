STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host the lecture **“LGBT People in China Enter Public Space” at 7:30 p.m. on June 1** in the **Library Science Lecture Hall**. LGBT-rights activists A Qiang and Dong Mama will speak with students and faculty about the lived circumstances of LGBT people, self-identification, family pressure and the development of LGBT-rights advocacy in China.

## Speakers

- **A Qiang**: Executive director and co-founder of Parents, Families and Friends of Lesbians and Gays China (PFALG China), A Qiang has worked in LGBT-rights advocacy and related media writing for many years and previously founded the New Perspective LGBT Reporting Watch Network.
- **Dong Mama**: Shenzhen coordinator of PFALG China, Dong Mama has long participated in LGBT public-interest activities. Since 2012, she has answered a parent hotline and provided related consultation and discussion through a blog, Weibo and QQ.

The notice frames the discussion around an estimated **40 million LGBT people in China**. This figure and the related wording are retained as source-reported content rather than independently inferred or revised. The lecture is open to the university community.
