Shantou University’s Law School plans to hold its **Eighth Youth League Congress and Seventeenth Student Congress** at **7 p.m. on June 1, 2013**, in **Lecture Hall 3**. The congresses will review the work of the school’s Seventh Youth League committee and Sixteenth Student Union executive committee, elect the next Youth League committee and Student Union executive committee, and discuss future work priorities.

## Delegate Selection

The congresses are expected to have approximately **95 delegates**, representing about **15 percent** of the Law School’s student population. Delegates must be Law School members of the Communist Youth League of China or the Communist Party of China, demonstrate sound academic and personal performance, maintain contact with fellow students and have the ability to participate in deliberations. Classes will select delegates through democratic evaluation and elections under the principle of democratic centralism.

The preparatory group for the two congresses is responsible for the arrangements. Questions may be directed to `0754-86503908` or `wangbin@stu.edu.cn`.
