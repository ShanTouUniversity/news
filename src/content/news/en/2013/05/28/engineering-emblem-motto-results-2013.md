Shantou University’s School of Engineering has announced the results of its logo and motto collection, which was open to the entire university community. The activity sought to present the school’s identity, strengthen its cohesion and promote its overall image. A review panel examined the submissions before the results were released.

## Results

The logo competition had three prize tiers: **one first-prize work, one second-prize work and three third-prize works**. The original work numbers and author information are not reproduced here; the designs are available in the OA attachment.

The notice clearly identifies **one first-prize, one second-prize and one third-prize motto entry**. It also lists **two additional motto entries**, but the archived body does not clearly associate them with a prize tier. This article retains the prize structure that can be confirmed from the source and does not infer the missing labels.

The collection included logo and motto submissions from faculty, staff and students. To protect student privacy, winner names, work numbers and other identifying information are not published here.
