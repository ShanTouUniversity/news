The 126th New Century Humanities Forum of Shantou University's School of Liberal Arts will be held from **7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on May 30, 2013**, in **ACC Meeting Room 3**. Professor Wang Yao, dean of Soochow University’s School of Liberal Arts, will deliver a talk titled “On the ‘Transitional State’ of Modern Chinese Literary History.”

## Forum Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | On the “Transitional State” of Contemporary Chinese Literary History |
| Speaker | Wang Yao, professor and dean of the School of Liberal Arts, Soochow University |
| Time | 7:00–9:00 p.m., May 30, 2013 |
| Venue | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Organizer | Shantou University School of Liberal Arts |

Wang holds a doctorate in literature and also serves as dean of Soochow University’s Tang Wenzhi Academy. He is the principal investigator of a major National Social Science Fund project and has been a Harvard–Yenching visiting scholar. He has served on the editorial board of *Literary Review* of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and works mainly on contemporary Chinese literature and intellectual culture. His publications include *A History of Modern Chinese Prose* and *Living and Dying in the Chinese Language*; he has also edited collections including *The Cultural Revolution Literature Series* and *The Series of Contemporary Chinese Literary Criticism*.
