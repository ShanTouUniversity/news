Shantou University’s Party and Administrative Office and Office of Supervision and Audit have forwarded Shantou’s related documents on a campaign addressing **lax discipline, dereliction, inefficiency and extravagance** and on building an incorruptible Shantou. The documents are Shantou Party Office Notice Nos. 73 and 74 of 2013; the university notice refers to the first as the **“Four Conducts” plan** and has launched a corresponding campus campaign.

## Work Schedule

| Stage | Period | Main work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-review | June 1–30, 2013 | Each unit organizes all faculty and staff to review their work against the 40 rectification items in the “Four Conducts” plan, including conduct, capabilities, performance of duties and discipline |
| Feedback collection | June 1–30, 2013 | Faculty, staff and students may submit comments and suggestions about workplace conduct at the university, units and individual level |
| Rectification | July 1–November 15, 2013 | Units analyze issues identified through self-review and feedback collection and implement corrective measures |
| Summary and improvement | November 16–December 10, 2013 | Units summarize outcomes, experience, shortcomings and future directions and complete the campaign summary form |

Faculty, staff and students may report conduct concerns or submit comments and suggestions about the university, units or individual workplace practices by calling **86502346** or emailing `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`.

## Reporting Deadlines

Each unit must designate a “Four Conducts” information officer and submit the officer’s name to the Office of Supervision and Audit by **May 30**. Self-review reports are due on **July 1**, rectification reports on **November 16**, and the completed campaign summary form on **December 10**.

The original notice attaches the two Shantou implementation documents and the campaign summary form. This article publishes the university-level schedule only; the detailed administrative items and form fields remain in the original OA attachments.
