STU’s Zhicheng College plans to run a summer rural service-learning public-service course in **mid-July 2013, after military training**, recruiting **40 students** from across the university through interviews. The course is included in the university’s public-service course system; students who complete the course and pass the assessment may receive **one credit**.

## Course Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service period | Seven days, provisionally scheduled for mid-July |
| Service location | Xintian Village, Jiantou Town, Dongyuan County, Heyuan |
| Activities | A personal-development summer camp for local primary and secondary students, a social survey, and a community evening event supporting rural and family harmony and local cultural life |
| Eligible applicants | All STU students |
| Intake | 40 students selected through interviews |

The course is intended to strengthen communication, teamwork and leadership skills and to develop social responsibility, a spirit of service and cooperation. Applicants should follow the public-service course rules, be willing to work hard, cooperate with others and follow unified arrangements.

## Application

Applications close at **5:00 p.m. on June 3**, and interviews are provisionally scheduled for the **afternoon of June 5**. Interested students must download and complete the application form for the Zhicheng summer rural service-learning public-service course, include their cohort and major in the file name as instructed, and send the form to the Zhicheng College course mailbox at `ATP@stu.edu.cn`.

The application form requests personal information such as name, cohort and major. The form fields and personal application materials are not reproduced here; interview details should be confirmed in subsequent notices from Zhicheng College.

## Interview Arrangements

By the application deadline, **104 students** had applied. Following an initial review of the application materials, the Zhicheng College public-service course team selected **65 students** for interviews. Interviews were scheduled for **4:10 p.m. on Wednesday, June 5** in Lecture Room **J-354** at Zhicheng College. The attachment contains the list of interviewees; their names, cohorts and majors are not reproduced here.
