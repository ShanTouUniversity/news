The 2013 provincial foreign-language examination for bachelor’s degrees in adult higher education will be held on **Sunday, June 2**. Faculty members assigned as invigilators must attend pre-exam training at **7:50 a.m.** that day in **E408**.

Invigilators are required to review the examination documents in the OA attachments before the training session. The attachments include examination procedures and room instructions, examinee rules, procedures for handling unexpected incidents and an invigilator list. This article publishes only the exam and training arrangements; personal information from the invigilator list and examination documents is not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The complete examination documents are available in the original OA attachments.
