Shantou University’s Student Community X Plan has announced the final results of its **Shantou History and Traditional Culture Exploration** project, which was carried out in May. Based on the teams’ project-planning processes and final presentations, the organizers awarded **one first prize, one second prize, one third prize and three excellence awards**, for **six winning teams** in total.

## Research Themes

To protect student privacy, team names, leaders, years and majors are not published here. The research themes that can be confirmed from the results notice are:

- Following the footprints of the Teochew city
- Shantou inlaid-porcelain culture
- Chen Cihong’s Former Residence
- Exploring Shantou’s charitable culture
- Historical sites and local customs in the old urban district
- Traces of the old district

The themes address Shantou’s old-city heritage and local customs, inlaid-porcelain traditions, historic architecture and charitable culture, reflecting the Student Community X Plan’s focus on local history and traditional culture.
