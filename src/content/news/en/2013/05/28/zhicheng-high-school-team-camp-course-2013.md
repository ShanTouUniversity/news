STU’s Zhicheng College has introduced the public-service course **“Entering University, Beginning the Journey: A Team-Development Summer Camp for Secondary-School Students.”** The seven-day course will run from **July 8 to 14, 2013**. University students will lead a personal-development summer camp for high-school students from the Chaoshan region. Students who complete the course and pass the assessment may receive **one credit**.

## Course and Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service recipients | High-school students from the Chaoshan region |
| Service period | July 8–14, seven days |
| Activities | Sharing and practicing university values, team development, research-based learning methods, project planning and implementation, Chinese-culture learning and leadership practice |
| Eligible applicants | All STU students |
| Intake | 24 students; a random draw will be used if applications exceed the quota |

The course is intended to support the physical and personal development of secondary-school students while giving participating university students experience in planning, organizing, receiving participants and running learning-oriented workshops. It is also designed to strengthen teamwork, communication, leadership, confidence, independence and social responsibility.

Applicants should follow the public-service course rules, be willing to learn and undertake service work, cooperate with others and follow unified arrangements. Applications close at **5:00 p.m. on June 4**.

## June 6 Admission Update

Zhicheng College reported on June 6 that some places remained available and interested students could enroll through the credit-based system. The exact course schedule was to be announced by the Academic Affairs Office. Because the course runs from **July 8 to 14**, overlapping with the university's military training period, applicants from the 2012 cohort could not be admitted. All other students who had already applied were admitted.

## Application

Interested students must download and complete the campaign form for the Zhicheng secondary-school summer-camp public-service course, include their cohort and major in the file name as instructed, and send it to the official Zhicheng College email address `o_zhicheng@stu.edu.cn`. Personal information and application materials from the form are not reproduced here.
