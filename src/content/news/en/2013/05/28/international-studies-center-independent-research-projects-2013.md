STU’s Center for International Studies in the School of Liberal Arts is continuing to offer **RSP0900 Independent Research Projects** in the fall semester of the 2013–2014 academic year. The course is open to undergraduates in the **2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts** across the university. Students may propose independent projects in history, philosophy, political science, anthropology, film studies, American studies or cultural studies, complete the research under supervision, and submit a report or paper for assessment. Students who pass the assessment receive **one credit**.

## Course Structure

Projects run for **10–12 weeks and approximately 40–50 hours**. The language of supervision may be English or Chinese, as agreed by the student and supervisor.

| Week | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Students contact a supervisor in a field of interest and discuss a feasible project |
| Week 2 | The supervisor selects students through an interview or meeting and sets the project plan and meeting schedule |
| Weeks 3–11 | Students carry out reading, investigation and other project work under supervision |
| Week 12 | Students submit a final report or paper as required and complete the project assessment |

Interested students should contact a relevant supervisor and develop a project **before week 17 of the current semester**. The supervisor will confirm the final student list and the credit-system record will then be completed.
