As part of its 10th-anniversary activities, the STU Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication will hold the alumni career forum **“Getting A JOB IN CHINA TODAY.”** Professor Peter Arnett will moderate the event, which is scheduled for **7–9 p.m. on May 17, 2013**, in the **Library Science Report Hall**.

Five alumni will introduce their industries and career experiences. The notice gives the following public professional information:

| Alumni | Employer and role |
| --- | --- |
| Gao Wenhuan | Financial planning consultant, FT Mergermarket of the Financial Times Group |
| Peng Xiangfeng | Corporate Culture Department, Baosteel Zhanjiang Iron & Steel Co., Ltd. |
| Yang Qiongzhen | Chinese-language editor, *Island Travel* magazine of Liantai Enterprise |
| Pang Yanjie | Office affairs, Zhoukou Municipal Commission for Discipline Inspection |
| Wei Wenyi | Reporter, Foshan Daily Group |

The forum will focus on employment in China and career development and is open to members of the university community interested in journalism, communication and employment.
