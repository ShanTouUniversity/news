The STU School of Science has opened recruitment for the summer Hong Kong collaboration class of its *Toward the Ocean* service-learning course. The class will select **20 undergraduates** from across STU and recruit **four student volunteers**, working with 20 exchange students from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Applications close on **May 25, 2013**.

Student volunteers must have taken the *Toward the Ocean* course before the summer. Students from the 2012 cohort must ensure that the course does not conflict with freshman military training; the notice also welcomes students from the 2010 and 2011 cohorts.

### Course schedule

The formal course title is *Marine Territorial Awareness and Marine-Ecosystem Conservation Outreach*. It is a general-education service-learning course carrying **48 hours and one credit**. The course syllabus places the implementation period on **July 1–9**, with the following plan:

| Stage | Main content |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–2 | Chinese marine-territorial awareness; service-learning and public-service course models; project design and social-survey methods; basic marine knowledge and the state of and strategies for protecting China's coastal ecosystems |
| Day 3 | Marine biological-resource conservation, sharks and other endangered marine species; project-design presentations, review and revision |
| Days 4–7 | Social practice at schools and coastal fishing villages in Shantou, including marine-knowledge education, public service and research, for 32 hours |
| Day 8 | Assessment, summary and experience sharing |

Fieldwork locations include **Yun'ao Middle School in Nan'ao County, Nan'ao fishing villages and Shantou urban areas**. Partner organizations include Shantou marine and environmental authorities, nearby fishing villages, primary and secondary schools and public activity venues. Sun Zewei is the course coordinator, with five STU instructors assigned to the course.

### Learning and safety

The course combines lectures, group discussion, interactive sharing and social practice. Students will participate in project design, marine-knowledge outreach, social surveys and practice reports. Grades consist of instructor and teaching-assistant evaluations of the learning process (70 percent) and evaluations of team performance from service partners and beneficiaries (30 percent). Within the instructor component, classroom performance accounts for 20 percent, service practice 50 percent and course summary and sharing 30 percent.

During fieldwork, participants must follow instructions and travel in groups, and must not swim in the sea or take part in other dangerous activities. During home visits and surveys, they should respect local customs, avoid embarrassing or unpleasant questions, accept no gifts, and pay attention to food hygiene, heat, sun exposure and personal belongings.

### Interview Arrangements

By the time of the follow-up notice, **45 students had applied**, and the course team had selected **30 students for interviews** after an initial review. Interviews were scheduled for **12:30 on Monday, June 3**, in the School of Science conference room. Interviews would begin at 12:30, with each group allotted about 10 minutes; students in the next group were asked to arrive 10 minutes early. Students who did not attend would be considered to have withdrawn. The notice includes an image listing the interview candidates; the list is not reproduced here.

### Interview Results

The School of Science interviewed 30 students who met the application requirements at **12:30 p.m. on June 3** in the Biology Department conference room. The interview consisted of a self-introduction and a leaderless group discussion, with five course instructors scoring each participant. In the end, 20 students were selected for the summer *Toward the Ocean* course, and the course team recruited three student volunteers. The original notice includes lists of the selected students and volunteers; their names, year groups and majors are not reproduced here.
