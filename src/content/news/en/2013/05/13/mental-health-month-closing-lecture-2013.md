The closing ceremony of STU's eighth Mental Health Awareness Month will be held from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on May 16, 2013**, in the **Science Report Hall**. The program will include an awards presentation and a psychology lecture. Psychologist Wei Zhizhong will speak on **“Expectations Filled with Happiness: Setting Out.”** All members of the university community are welcome.

### Speaker

Wei Zhizhong is a national Level II psychological counselor and a national psychological counseling supervisor. He has worked full-time in psychological counseling and psychological research, with research interests including group psychology, art psychology, cultural psychology, educational psychology and psychological techniques.

His public biography says that he has founded psychological counseling, vocational-training and research organizations as well as a graduate program in applied psychological techniques. He has also helped develop group psychotherapy, group counseling and experiential group-education models. His current public roles include council member of the National Forum on Applied Psychological Techniques, specially appointed researcher at the Wuhan University Research Center for Modern Psychology, invited psychological commentator for China National Radio, and specially appointed psychological expert for the Guangdong Committee of the Communist Youth League.
