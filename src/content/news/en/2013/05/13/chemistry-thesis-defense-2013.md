It is reported that the Department of Chemistry of the School of Science issued a notice on May 13, 2013, announcing the thesis defense arrangements for the Class of 2013.

## Defense Committee

Chair: Tong Qingxiao; Members: Huang Xiaochun and 14 others (see OA notice).

## First Round

| Group | Members | Chair | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | Huang Xiaochun, Tong Qingxiao, Yin Yegao, Peng Rong | Tong Qingxiao | 15:00–17:00, May 20 | E403 |
| Group 2 | Zhang Xin, Chen Hanjia, Shi Xuhua | Zhang Xin | 09:30–11:30, May 23 | D504 |
| Group 3 | Lu Fusheng, Xu Kaitian, Zhou Xiaoping, Zhan Shunze | Lu Fusheng | 14:30–16:30, May 22 | D202 |
| Group 4 | Gao Wenhua, Chen Zhanguang, Song Yibing | Gao Wenhua | 13:50–15:50, May 23 | D202 |
| Group 5 | Fang Yiwen, Lu Feng, Chen Guanghui | Fang Yiwen | 09:30–11:30, May 24 | G306 |

Student lists for each group are available in the OA notice and are withheld here for privacy.

## Second Round

The chairs of the first-round groups will serve as reviewers for the second round, scheduled for 15:00–17:00 on May 27 in Room E410.

Evaluation criteria and procedures are available in the OA attachment.
