STU and Ningbo University have signed an Undergraduate Exchange Training Agreement and plan to begin undergraduate exchange study in the **fall semester of 2013**. Students may exchange for **one semester or one academic year**. The program is intended to give students experience at a second campus, broaden their campus and cultural experience, and provide access to additional teaching resources.

### Eligibility and selection

The program is open to **main-campus students from the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts**. Applicants should have a positive record of political and personal conduct, observe the law and university rules, maintain good conduct and have no record of university disciplinary action. They should also demonstrate learning and analytical ability, have clear academic goals, maintain good academic performance and have no failed courses.

Applicants must complete the undergraduate exchange application form and apply through their school or college by **May 27, 2013**. Each school or college will conduct a comprehensive review under the published conditions, select and publicize a preliminary list, and submit the list to the Academic Affairs Office by **5:30 p.m. on June 7** after the publicity period. The Academic Affairs Office will review and approve the final list before publication.

| Stage | Time or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Student application to school or college | By May 27, 2013 |
| Preliminary review and publicity | Conducted by each school or college |
| School or college submission to Academic Affairs | By 5:30 p.m., June 7, 2013 |
| Final selection | Published after Academic Affairs review and approval |

### Exchange arrangements

During the exchange, students must observe Ningbo University rules and follow its administration. Tuition is paid to the STU Finance Office at STU rates. Accommodation fees are collected by Ningbo University at its rates. Students are responsible for transportation and living costs, while medical expenses are handled under relevant STU rules. Course selection and grade recognition will follow STU's *Measures for Exchange-Student Course and Grade Recognition*.

The attached profile describes Ningbo University as a provincial comprehensive key university in Ningbo, Zhejiang, covering 11 fields including economics, law, education, literature, history, science, engineering, agriculture, medicine, management and the arts. It is jointly established by the State Oceanic Administration and the Ningbo municipal government. The profile also says that the university has 19 schools, 72 undergraduate majors and broad international exchange partnerships. Further information was available at `http://zsb.nbu.edu.cn/`.

The exchange application form contains fields for name, gender, student number, telephone, email, school or department, major and entry year, requested host school and major, exchange period, self-assessment, and school and Academic Affairs approvals. Individual application information is not reproduced here.
