The STU Office for Campus Culture Development, also known as the Sanfeng Office, has released Academic Conduct Inspection Report No. **231**, covering week 11 of the 2012–2013 spring semester. Inspections were conducted in **Building E** on **May 6 and 7**, recording 12 late arrivals in total.

### Inspection results

| Inspection time | Venue | Late arrivals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 p.m., Monday, May 6, 2013 | Building E | Eight students more than five minutes late |
| 2 p.m., Tuesday, May 7, 2013 | Building E | Three students late by five minutes or less; one student more than five minutes late |

The report asks the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide guidance to students who did not observe university rules and to continue strengthening academic conduct. The attachment contains the relevant student list, which is not reproduced here along with any student personal information.
