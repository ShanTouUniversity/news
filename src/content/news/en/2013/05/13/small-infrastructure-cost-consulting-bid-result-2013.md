STU's Infrastructure Office has published the bid result for cost-consulting services for the university's small and medium infrastructure projects. Following the review and recommendation of the university bidding leadership group under STU's infrastructure tendering regulations, **Guangdong Lizhen Construction Engineering Cost Consulting Co., Ltd.** is listed as the winning bidder.

The service fee is calculated at **75 percent** of the standard in the Guangdong Price Bureau's *Reply on Adjusting the Fees for Construction-Engineering Cost-Consulting Services in Guangdong* (粤价函〔2011〕742号), representing a 25 percent reduction from that standard. The public-notice period is **May 13–15, 2013**.
