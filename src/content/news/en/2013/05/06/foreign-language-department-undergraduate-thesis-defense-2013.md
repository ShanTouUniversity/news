The STU Foreign Languages Department will hold undergraduate thesis defenses for its 2009 cohort from **2:30 to 6:30 p.m. on Wednesday, May 15**, the Wednesday of Week 12. The defenses will be conducted in English by four panels. The numbers listed in the notice total 58 students.

### Defense arrangements

| Panel | Chair | Panel members | Room | Listed students |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Panel 1 | Luo Qianjun | Peng Jiane and Chen Jie | D303 | 15 |
| Panel 2 | Li Renzhi | Wen Bing, Huang Jiayi and James I. McDougall | D304 | 14 |
| Panel 3 | Zhu Wang | An Ning and Melvin Sterne | D403 | 14 |
| Panel 4 | Li Jie | Chen Huayu, Yuan Guangtao and Karsten Krueger | D404 | 15 |

The defense committee will be chaired by Chen Huayu. Each student will give an approximately five-minute thesis introduction, answer questions for approximately five minutes, and then receive an approximately two-minute evaluation from the panel.

The attachment contains student group lists and therefore includes student names and other personal information. Those lists and the defense recorders' information are not reproduced here.
