STU's Discipline Inspection Office has announced that the **fourth plenary meeting of the fourth University Discipline Inspection Commission in enlarged format** will be held at **9:00 a.m. on Friday, May 10, 2013** in **Room 201 of the New Administration Center**.

### Meeting arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | Fourth plenary meeting of the fourth University Discipline Inspection Commission, enlarged format |
| Time | 9:00 a.m., Friday, May 10, 2013 |
| Location | Room 201, New Administration Center |
| Participants | Members of the university and Medical College Discipline Inspection Commissions, and discipline-inspection, supervision and audit staff from the main campus, Medical College and affiliated hospitals |

Relevant participants are asked to attend on time. The notice does not provide an agenda or other discussion details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office)
