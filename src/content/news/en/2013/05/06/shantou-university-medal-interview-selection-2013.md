The STU Student Affairs Office has announced the interview arrangements for the 2013 Shantou University Medal selection. After a preliminary review of application materials recommended by the schools and colleges, the selection committee advanced **13 of 20 applicants** to the interview stage.

### Interview arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Interview | 9:00 a.m., Thursday, May 9, 2013 |
| Location | Administration Building Room 221 |
| Order draw | 3:00 p.m. on Wednesday, May 8, Administration Building Room 105 |
| Shortlisted applicants | 13 of 20 applicants |
| Self-introduction | In Chinese, no more than one minute |
| Judges' questions | No more than nine minutes |
| Maximum interview time | 10 minutes per applicant |

The interviews are a subsequent stage in the 2013 STU Medal selection. The shortlist contains student names and other personal information, which are not reproduced here.
