The happiness workshop in STU's Heart Growth series will be held from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, May 11, 2013** in Activity Room 726 of Building 789. Titled **“Who Stole Your Happiness?”**, the workshop will be led by Fang Ying and has 12 places.

### Event details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Who Stole Your Happiness? |
| Time | 3:00–5:00 p.m., Saturday, May 11, 2013 |
| Location | Activity Room 726, Building 789 |
| Facilitator | Fang Ying |
| Capacity | 12 participants |
| Registration deadline | By 11:00 a.m. on May 10, 2013 |
| Registration email | `o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn` |

The workshop includes three sections: “If Life Were Only Like the First Meeting,” “What Is Happiness?” and “A Joyful Farewell.” Because places are limited, the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will notify registered students by email. Applicants should monitor their email for updates.

The registration form contains personal information and is not reproduced here.
