The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication's Wednesday Movie Night will screen **Citizen Kane** from **7:00 to 10:00 p.m. on Wednesday, May 8, 2013** in the Library Lecture Hall. The session will be moderated by Peter Herford. In addition to the 150 seats used by the class, 50 seats will be open to the public on a first-come, first-served basis. Students must present their campus cards to enter.

### Screening details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | Citizen Kane |
| Time | 7:00–10:00 p.m., Wednesday, May 8, 2013 |
| Location | Library Lecture Hall |
| Moderator | Peter Herford |
| Public seats | 50, first come, first served |
| Admission | Students enter with their campus cards |

Citizen Kane is a 1941 American drama film directed by and starring Orson Welles and his first feature film. It follows the life of newspaper magnate Charles Foster Kane through a newsreel reporter's investigation of Kane's final word, “Rosebud.” Kane is partly based on figures including American newspaper publisher William Randolph Hearst. The film won the Academy Award for Best Writing (Original Screenplay).

All STU faculty members and students are welcome. Public seating is limited and will be allocated on site.
