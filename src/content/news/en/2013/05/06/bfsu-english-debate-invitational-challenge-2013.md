Two members of the English Debate Society (EDS), a student activity organization of the STU English Language Center, **He Y** and **Ren HY**, were invited to the inaugural BFSU English Debate Invitational Challenge (1st BFSU IV). The competition was held at Beijing Foreign Studies University from **April 27 to May 2** and used the British Parliamentary debate format.

### Competition result

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Field | 48 teams from universities in China and abroad |
| Preliminary rounds | Ninth after six rounds of points competition |
| Elimination stage | Advanced to the semifinals and finished among the top eight teams |
| Individual honor | He Y ranked seventh in individual points and was named Best Debater |

The invitational challenge brought together English debaters from Peking University, Tsinghua University, Beijing Foreign Studies University, Beihang University, Woosong University in South Korea and STU, among other institutions. After six preliminary rounds, the STU team advanced through the elimination stage and reached the semifinals, placing among the top eight teams overall.

In accordance with the privacy rules for student names, this article uses “He Y” and “Ren HY” rather than the students' full names or other personal information.
