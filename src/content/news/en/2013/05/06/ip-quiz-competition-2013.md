It is reported that, to raise awareness of intellectual property creation and protection and to foster a positive IP protection atmosphere, Shantou University will hold a team-based Intellectual Property Knowledge Quiz Competition centered on the theme of the 13th World Intellectual Property Day, "Creativity: The Next Generation." The event is organized by the Research Office and co-organized by the STU Intellectual Property Protection Association.

## Format

The competition is open to all faculty and students. Teams of four, including one captain, will compete, with each participant limited to one team. A total of 16 teams will advance through three rounds:

| Round | Format |
|-------|--------|
| Round 1 | 16 teams divided into 4 groups of 4; group winners advance |
| Round 2 | 4 advancing teams split into 2 groups; winners reach the final |
| Round 3 | 2 finalists compete for the championship |

The competition uses a live Q&A format, with the highest-scoring team advancing. Questions are drawn mainly from a designated question bank.

## Time, Venue and Prizes

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Time | May 18, 2013, 19:30–21:30 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Champion | RMB 600 |
| Runner-up | RMB 400 |
| Other teams | Commemorative gifts |

Interested participants should complete the registration form and submit it to 11ltzeng@stu.edu.cn by May 15.
