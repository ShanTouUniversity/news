STU's School of Engineering has published the initial-review result for the OFILM Innovation Club's project-improvement plans and themed projects. The projects completed initial defenses and review by a judging panel, with final results provided in the notice attachment. Relevant teams were asked to proceed according to their plans, and a midterm defense was planned for **September**.
