STU's University-Affairs Disclosure Oversight Group has begun a **2012–2013 review of the implementation of university-affairs disclosure items**. The review covers functional departments on the main campus and asks them to compare their actual implementation with the disclosure items registered in the previous October.

### Review arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Units covered | Functional departments on the main campus |
| Academic year | 2012–2013 |
| Reference | University-affairs disclosure registration form submitted in October 2012 |
| Materials | Review report and implementation registration form |
| Written submission | Office of Supervision, Audit and Inspection, Room 422, New Administration Center |
| Electronic submission | `o_jj@stu.edu.cn` |
| Deadline | By May 17, 2013 |

Departments are asked to verify whether relevant items have been disclosed as required. Items that should have been disclosed but were not must be explained in the remarks section, while newly added disclosure items should also be identified. The template includes fields for disclosure scope, disclosure method and implementation status.

The University-Affairs Disclosure Oversight Group will consolidate the departmental reports and publish the results on the internal office-information website for review by faculty, staff and students. The attachment is an internal reporting template; this article does not reproduce its form-filler contact or signature fields.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Supervision, Audit and Inspection)
