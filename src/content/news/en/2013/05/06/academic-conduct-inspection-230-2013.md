It is reported that the Office for Campus Culture Development (Sanfeng Office) of Shantou University issued Academic Conduct Inspection Report No.230 on May 6, 2013, covering inspections in week 10 of the 2012–2013 spring semester.

## Inspection Results

| Time | Venue | Late arrivals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00, May 3, 2013 | Building E | 14 students late within 5 minutes, 5 students late over 5 minutes |
| 14:00, May 3, 2013 | Building E | 4 students late within 5 minutes |

Students who failed to observe university discipline were notified, and colleges (residential colleges) were asked to provide guidance and continue strengthening academic conduct. The full list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The student list is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Office for Campus Culture Development)
