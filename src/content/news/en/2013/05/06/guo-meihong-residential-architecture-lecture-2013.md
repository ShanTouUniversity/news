The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host an architecture lecture at **7:30 p.m. on Thursday, May 9** in the STU Library Lecture Hall. Titled **“On Residential Architectural Design,”** the lecture will be given by Guo Meihong, a registered first-class architect. All STU faculty members and students are welcome.

### Speaker profile

According to the attached profile, Guo has been a partner and chief architect at Lanshen Architectural Design Co., Ltd. since 2007. She served as design director at Aipusdun Investment Consulting Co., Ltd. in the United States from 2003 to 2006, architectural department manager at Shenzhen Vanke International Enterprise Services Co., Ltd. from 2000 to 2003, chief architect at Tsinghuayuan Architectural Design Co., Ltd. from 1997 to 2003, and architect at Beijing Wuzhou Engineering Design Co., Ltd. from 1991 to 1994. The dates in the original profile overlap, and are retained here as stated.

Guo received a master's degree in architecture from Tianjin University in 1997 and a bachelor's degree in architecture there in 1991. Her selected projects from the previous decade include the urban design of the area in front of Baoji Railway Station (4 square kilometers), planning for the Taizhou Biopharmaceutical Industrial Park in Jiangsu (2 square kilometers), urban design for the Ancient Yellow River scenic belt in Suqian, Jiangsu (300,000 square meters), and urban design for Haikou's West Coast (20 square kilometers). Her architectural and residential-planning work also includes the B and C student-residence areas of Suzhou University Town (200,000 square meters) and planning for the Changsha Tianjian Furong Road residential area (1 million square meters).
