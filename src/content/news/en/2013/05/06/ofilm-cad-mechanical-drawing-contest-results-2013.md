STU's School of Engineering has reported that the second CAD Mechanical Drawing Contest, organized by the OFILM Innovation Club, has concluded and published the results. The contest awarded **one first prize, three second prizes and 10 third prizes**, for 14 prize places in total.

### Awards

| Award | Places | Prize |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First prize | 1 | RMB 200 and a certificate |
| Second prize | 3 | RMB 100 each and certificates |
| Third prize | 10 | RMB 50 each and certificates |

The earlier contest notice also stated that all participants would receive a commemorative gift. The first-prize recipient's name is corrupted in the result notice and is not reconstructed here; the names of the other student winners are also not reproduced.
