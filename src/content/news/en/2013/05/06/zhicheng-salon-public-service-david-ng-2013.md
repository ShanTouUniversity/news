The fifth Zhicheng Salon will be held at **7:30–9:00 p.m. on May 8** in Lecture Room J-354 at Zhicheng College. The session is titled **“Sharing on public service”** and will feature Dr. David Ng Ka-sing, former head of the Hong Kong Police Public Relations Branch and former police spokesperson. The event is organized by the college-level student association.

### Event details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Sharing on public service |
| Time | 7:30–9:00 p.m., May 8, 2013 |
| Location | Lecture Room J-354, Zhicheng College |
| Speaker | Dr. David Ng Ka-sing |
| Registration | Email `ATP@stu.edu.cn` by 4:00 p.m. on May 8 |

Ng joined the Hong Kong Police in 1977 and later led its Public Relations Branch and served as a police spokesperson responsible for crisis response. He subsequently led Interpol Hong Kong, handling international affairs and liaison on cross-border crime. He received the Police Meritorious Service Medal in 2005 and the Police Distinguished Service Medal in 2011, and has served as an adjunct lecturer at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong Open University and other institutions.

Students are welcome to attend. The personal information requested in the registration email is not reproduced here.
