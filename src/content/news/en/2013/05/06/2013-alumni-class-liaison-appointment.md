STU's Alumni Affairs Office has opened the appointment process for **class alumni liaisons from the 2013 graduating cohort**. The liaisons will help maintain long-term communication among the university, its alumni association and alumni from their class. Each school will generally select one liaison for each graduating class.

### Position details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible group | Classes in the 2013 graduating cohort |
| Recommendation | Self-nomination and school recommendation |
| Allocation | Generally one liaison per class |
| Registration deadline | By May 10, 2013 |
| Electronic submission | `o_xyh@stu.edu.cn` |
| Appointment | The Alumni Affairs Office will issue appointment certificates |

Candidates are generally expected to have experience as a student leader or student-organization leader, good organizational and communication skills, an interest in the university and alumni affairs, and a willingness to serve. Responsibilities include facilitating communication among classmates and between class alumni and the university and local alumni associations, assisting with class reunions and local alumni activities, and reporting alumni activities, development and suggestions to the alumni association.

Liaisons may receive alumni publications and university information from the alumni association on a priority basis and may receive priority access to activities organized by the association. Schools must complete the registration form for the 2013 class alumni liaisons and send it to the designated email address; the university will issue appointment certificates based on the selections made by the schools.

The registration form contains liaison and class contact information. This article does not reproduce the attachment or personal contact details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office)
