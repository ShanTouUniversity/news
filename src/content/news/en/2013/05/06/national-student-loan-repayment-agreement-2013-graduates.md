The STU Student Affairs Office, following arrangements by Bank of China Shantou Branch, will organize repayment-agreement signing for **2013 graduates whose national student loans remain outstanding**. The session begins at **2:15 p.m. on May 13** in Lecture Staircase E101. The relevant schools and colleges will notify the graduates concerned.

### Session schedule

| Time | Content or schools |
| --- | --- |
| 2:15–2:30 p.m. | Sign-in and distribution of repayment-agreement materials |
| 2:30–3:00 p.m. | Student Affairs Office briefing on repayment procedures and precautions |
| 3:00–4:00 p.m. | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Business School, School of Law and School of Engineering |
| 4:00–5:00 p.m. | School of Science, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, College of Liberal Arts and Zhicheng College |
| Location | Lecture Staircase E101 |

### Procedures and subsequent rules

Participants should prepare their national student-loan contract, identity document, Bank of China repayment passbook and necessary copies, together with a black pen. The repayment passbook should be checked at a bank branch in advance to confirm that it is valid. Graduates who have already repaid their loans do not need to sign an agreement; they should submit the loan-settlement notice to the Student Aid Management Center in Room 106 of the Administration Center for filing. Those wishing to repay early before graduation may submit a request at the session.

The repayment guide states that the first repayment date is **August 1, 2013**, followed in principle by repayment on the first day of each month under the contract, using equal principal-and-interest payments. Early repayment requires bank approval, and the approval and debit process takes approximately 10 working days. Graduates continuing to domestic postgraduate study or a second degree may apply to the bank to extend the loan term by **June 30**; those unable to graduate normally because of financial hardship must submit a written application to the Student Aid Management Center by **June 15**. Overdue repayment may lead to collection measures, legal action and credit-report consequences.

Graduates unable to attend will receive further arrangements after the Student Aid Management Center consults with the bank based on this signing session. Inquiries: `0754-86504749`; email: `zjliu@stu.edu.cn`.

This article does not reproduce the list of graduates with outstanding loans, identity information, passbook account details or other personal loan records.
