The Office of the University Performance Assessment Leading Group has announced a re-assessment of the 2011–2012 performance results for some teachers in STU's Physical Education Department. Following a decision by the Shantou University Faculty and Staff Appeals Committee, the original results for the relevant teachers were revoked, and the university performance-assessment leading group agreed that the department should conduct a new assessment.

After completing the re-assessment, the Physical Education Department recommended **Zhang Xiaohong** and **Li Yuejin** for the 2011–2012 annual excellence rating. The recommendations have been publicized for comment.

### Publicity details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Re-assessing unit | Physical Education Department |
| Academic year | 2011–2012 |
| Recommended candidates | Zhang Xiaohong and Li Yuejin |
| Rating | Annual excellence rating |
| Comment deadline | By 5:30 p.m. on May 10, 2013 |
| Basis | Chapter VII, Article 29 of the Provisional STU Faculty and Staff Performance Assessment Measures |
| Issuing office | Office of the University Performance Assessment Leading Group |
| Date | May 6, 2013 |

Those with differing opinions may submit a written appeal or complaint under the applicable measures before the deadline. The notice does not provide details about the people whose original results were revoked, and none are inferred here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Performance Assessment Leading Group Office and Human Resources Office)
