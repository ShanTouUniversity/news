A team representing the Shantou University Business School won **third place** in the 2013 International Collegiate Business Strategy Competition (ICBSC). The 49th edition of the competition was organized by California State University, Long Beach.

### Competition details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | 49th International Collegiate Business Strategy Competition (ICBSC) |
| Organizer | California State University, Long Beach |
| Start date | January 23, 2013 |
| Format | A 10-week remote stage and a three-day in-person stage |
| In-person location | Anaheim, California, United States |
| STU result | Third place |

The ICBSC uses business-simulation software to model company operations. Teams also prepare business plans and annual reports and compete with university teams from around the world by reporting their work to a simulated board of directors. The STU Business School team completed both stages and finished third overall.

The notice includes a chart on the net-profit performance of the competing company, but it does not provide verifiable text data for further interpretation. The notice does not publish the participating students' names, which are not reproduced here.
