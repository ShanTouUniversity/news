The Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the schedule for **Dance Performance** (class number **59123**) because instructor Yu Duanrong will travel to Jieyang to serve as a judge at a dance competition.

### Class adjustment

| Item | Original arrangement | Revised arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week | Tuesday of Week 11 | Monday of Week 11 |
| Date | May 7 | May 6 |
| Periods | Suspended during Periods 8–9 | Makeup class during the AB periods |
| Location | Dance Rehearsal Room STU102 | Dance Rehearsal Room STU102 |

Students enrolled in the course are asked to share the update and attend the makeup class at the revised time.
