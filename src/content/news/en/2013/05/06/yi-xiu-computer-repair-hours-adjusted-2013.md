The Office of Student Affairs has announced that the Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee will continue its Yi Xiu computer-repair service under the Community Care Plan. Based on the service's earlier operation, appointments have changed from all-day acceptance to a concentrated schedule of **8:00–10:00 p.m. every Thursday, Friday and Saturday**. The location remains the committee office in **Building C, Room 411, Student Dormitory**.

### Service arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service | Yi Xiu computer-repair appointments |
| Appointment hours | 8:00–10:00 p.m. every Thursday, Friday and Saturday |
| Location | Dormitory Self-Management Committee office, Building C, Room 411 |
| Holidays | Major national holidays are excluded |
| Appointments and inquiries | Direct message or inquire through Sina Weibo “@汕大宿委会” |

During the activity period, the committee will also hold occasional small lectures on common computer troubleshooting, basic computer knowledge, and routine maintenance for desktop and laptop computers. The latest arrangements will be posted on dormitory-area committee bulletin boards.

The notice also states that laptops booked for repair earlier had all been serviced. Students who had not collected them could contact “@汕大宿委会” to arrange a pickup time. The notice does not give a specific end date for this round of activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Student Affairs)
