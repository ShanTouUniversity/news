It is reported that, in accordance with the requirements of Shantou University's Notice on the Assessment of the 2013 National Scholarship, National Inspirational Scholarship and National Grants, the College of Science of Shantou University carried out its college-level review for the 2013 National Scholarship.

After students applied on their own initiative and the college's Youth League Committee conducted a preliminary review, 10 students, including Guo LC, advanced to interviews. On October 14, 2013, the College of Science Scholarship Assessment Committee interviewed the shortlisted candidates and made a comprehensive assessment based on academic performance, professional competence, awards received and contributions to the university and the college. The committee plans to recommend Su WF and Li HY as recipients of the 2013 National Scholarship. The Youth League Committee of the college issued a public notice on October 15 to publicize the nominees.

Those who hold different views on the above nominees are asked to submit them in writing to the Youth League Committee of the College of Science before 12:00 noon on Saturday, October 19. Materials submitted by an organization must bear its official seal, and those submitted by an individual must be signed with the person's real name to show accountability. Submissions may be delivered to the Youth League Committee Office of the College of Science, and inquiries may also be made by telephone.
