It is reported that the Publicity Department, the Student Affairs Office and the Youth League Committee forwarded a notice on October 15, 2013, regarding a speech contest on practicing core socialist values for teachers and students at Shantou’s colleges.

All faculty and students, including those from the Medical College, are eligible. Application forms should be sent to the Youth League Committee email by October 20, with preliminary interviews scheduled for the afternoon of October 21. Details are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The application form and email are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department, Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee)
