It is reported that the Discipline Inspection Committee Office of Shantou University released Issue No. 1 of the *2013 Shantou University Discipline Education Month Briefing* on October 15.

During this year's Discipline Education Month, the university's Discipline Inspection Committee will compile and issue briefings based on how activities are carried out, so as to report in a concentrated way on how units, Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches conduct discipline education and study. The committee office had earlier invited units to submit activity notes, with a deadline of 12:00 on October 12 and submissions sent to o_jj@stu.edu.cn.

The current issue describes the practices and progress of discipline education and study activities in various units. The full text is available in the original notice on the OA system.
