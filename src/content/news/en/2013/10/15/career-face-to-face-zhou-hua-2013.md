It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold a talk on October 16 under the theme "From Good to Great: Becoming Irreplaceable," part of its Career Face-to-Face lecture series, with Zhou Hua, brand director of aigale, as the speaker.

The talk will run from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. that day at E204. Zhou Hua holds a bachelor's degree in e-commerce from the School of Management at Jilin University. He previously served as a brand manager at ASUS, where he was responsible for promoting the company's high-end sub-brand ROG (Republic of Gamers) in China, and is currently brand director of aigale. The talk will cover sharing his growth experience, making good life plans, and seven tips for becoming irreplaceable.

The event is hosted by the Student Affairs Office and organized by the Student Career Planning and Development Association. All faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
