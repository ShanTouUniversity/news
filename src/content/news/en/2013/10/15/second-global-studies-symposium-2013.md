The Center for International Studies of Shantou University will host the Second Global Studies Symposium from **October 18 to 20, 2013**, under the theme “Developing New Global Studies Programs in China: Challenges and Opportunities.”

Building on last year’s discussion of “New Directions in Global Studies in the 21st Century,” this year’s symposium will focus on the conceptual foundations and planning arrangements for a proposed Global Studies program (at both master’s and undergraduate levels) at STU. Leading scholars from Global Studies programs in the United States, India, Japan and China have been invited. They will share their professional experience in developing such programs, offer their understanding of Global Studies, and discuss what the field means for China and, in particular, for Shantou University. The visiting scholars will also examine how a Global Studies program can equip students with professional skills to meet the new demands of market competition in a globalizing world.

The Center for International Studies will also present the background and preparations for its proposed Global Studies program, together with the difficulties and challenges it faces.

## Main Schedule

A public lecture, “What is Global Studies?: Meeting the Needs of the Times,” will be held in **ACC #5 from 10:10 to 11:40 a.m. on October 18**. The speaker will be David Wank of Sophia University, Japan, and the lecture will be delivered in Chinese.

In the afternoon, **starting at 2:30 p.m. on October 18 in ACC #3**, the program will include opening remarks by Terry Bodenhorn, Dean of the College of Liberal Arts; an address by Vice President Lin Danming; an introduction to Shantou University by Liao Xuequan, Director of the International Cooperation and Exchange Office; and presentations by the scholars.

All faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
