It is reported that, in accordance with the Notice on the Collection of Basic Faculty and Staff Information Across the Province issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Human Resources Office of Shantou University has rolled out a university-wide collection of basic information on faculty and staff members in October 2013. Training for staff responsible for the collection work in each unit was completed on October 14.

## Work Arrangement

The collection covers all units on the main campus. Under the arrangement, each unit is required to organize its faculty and staff to complete the information forms, conduct an initial review of the data, and submit the results as scheduled.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Training Date | October 14, 2013 (completed) |
| Deadline | Before October 22, 2013 |
| Submission | Paper and electronic copies of collection forms |
| Paper Copy | Human Resources Office |
| Email | o_rsc@stu.edu.cn |
| Responsible Units | All units on the main campus; Human Resources Office |

## Background

The province-wide initiative aims to build a complete and accurate database of basic information on faculty and staff across Guangdong's higher education institutions. The Human Resources Office urged all units to complete the collection and initial review on time to keep the university's overall submission on schedule.

Relevant forms and filling instructions have been distributed to each unit as attachments to the notice.
