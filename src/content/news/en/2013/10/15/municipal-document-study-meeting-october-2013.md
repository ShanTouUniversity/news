It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on October 15, asking all units to report the names of the leading cadres who will attend a municipal meeting on studying a central document.

Acting on a notice from the General Office of the Shantou Municipal Party Committee, the Shantou Municipal Party Committee will hold a meeting at 10:00 a.m. on Thursday, October 17, at Tuodao Cinema to convey and study the spirit of a relevant central document. According to the requirements of the meeting, participants are leading cadres at or above the deputy division level (including the deputy division level).

The notice asks all units to report their lists of participants to the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee before the end of the afternoon on October 15, 2013. The contact telephone number is 86502345, and the email address is o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.

The university will provide shuttle buses for participants. Those attending are asked to board the bus at the University Motto Monument at 9:00 a.m. or at Anju at 9:10 a.m. on the morning of October 17 for the ride to Tuodao Cinema.
