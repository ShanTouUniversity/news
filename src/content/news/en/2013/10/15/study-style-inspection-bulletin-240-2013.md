It is reported that the Study Style Construction Office of Shantou University issued Inspection Report No. 240 on study style, covering checks of teaching buildings in week 4 of the 2013–2014 fall semester.

The inspection team checked Building E at 08:00 on October 9, 2013 (Wednesday), recording 6 students late by no more than 5 minutes and 2 students late by more than 5 minutes. A second check at the same venue at 08:00 on October 10 (Thursday) recorded 4 students late within 5 minutes and 1 student late by more than 5 minutes. The two days of checks totaled 13 late arrivals.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Semester and week | Week 4, fall semester of the 2013–2014 academic year |
| Time and venue | 08:00, October 9, 2013 (Wednesday) and 08:00, October 10, 2013 (Thursday), Building E |
| Late arrivals | October 9: 6 students late within 5 minutes, 2 late by more than 5 minutes; October 10: 4 students late within 5 minutes, 1 late by more than 5 minutes; 13 arrivals in total over the two days |
| Follow-up | Colleges (residential colleges) were asked to provide criticism and guidance to the students concerned and to keep up sustained efforts on study style |

The names of students who failed to observe university discipline were sent to their colleges, which were asked to counsel the students involved and to continue strengthening study style. The list is not reproduced here in order to protect student privacy.
