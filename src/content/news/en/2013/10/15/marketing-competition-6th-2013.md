It is reported that the Business School issued a notice on October 15, 2013, to recruit students for the 6th National University Marketing Competition (Social Sciences Award).

Students wishing to compete must first pass the written examination for the “Assistant Marketing Manager of China” qualification; only those who pass are eligible. Students not competing may also take the examination and, if successful, apply for the qualification certificates.

## Exam and Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration deadline | October 18, 2013 |
| Exam date | November 23, 2013 |
| Registration fee | 20 yuan |
| Registration contact | Student Zou KX, contact details available on the OA system |
| Inquiries | Mr. Huang Wangming, phone 8650-2786, email wmwong@stu.edu.cn |
| Certification | Those who pass may apply for the *Assistant Marketing Manager of China* certificate jointly issued by the National Education Examinations Authority and the China Marketing Association, and the *Assistant Marketing Manager of the US* certificate issued by the American Marketing Association; certification fee 350 yuan per person (for both certificates) |
| Issued by | Business School |
