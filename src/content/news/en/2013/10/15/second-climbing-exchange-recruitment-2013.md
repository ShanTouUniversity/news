It is reported that, to enrich sports activities for faculty members and students at Shantou University, the STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association will hold the second climbing exchange activity at the Library Student Activity Hall in October 2013 and is now recruiting participants from across the university. Combining fitness, entertainment and competition, climbing is known as "ballet on the cliff face".

The program consists of two parts. The theory exchange covers the origin and development of climbing and other basic knowledge, an introduction to climbing equipment, an introduction to indoor climbing (bouldering), and basic techniques for indoor climbing (bouldering). The second part is practice and training in indoor climbing (bouldering).

The activity plans to admit 15 participants; if applications exceed the quota, places will be determined by lottery. The participation fee is 15 yuan per person. The activity takes place in the Climbing Room, Student Activity Hall, first floor of the Library, and the application deadline is 10 p.m. on October 17.

Applicants are expected to meet the following requirements: be enthusiastic about outdoor activities and ready to endure hardship; be modest and eager to learn, follow instructions in all actions, and treat everyone with a smile; value the opportunity after joining the training and not drop out without a valid reason; and follow the coach's instructions during the activity, strictly observe the schedule and not leave the training venue without authorization.

Those interested should download and complete the attached "STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association Second Climbing Exchange Activity Registration Form", add "grade + major + name" to the front of the file name, and email the electronic form to snowleopardstu@163.com. Applications that do not follow the requirements will not be accepted. For other questions, applicants may consult the email address above.
