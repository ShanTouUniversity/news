It is reported that the Shantou University Library opened trial access to the Hantang Modern Newspapers Database on October 15, 2013. Faculty and students can use the resource through the library's database page until the trial ends on December 14, 2013.

The Hantang Modern Newspapers Database is another large-scale integrated online resource from the Hantang company, following its Hantang Ancient Books Database. By 2012 the collection had grown to 5.5 million entries, together with nearly one million frames of high-resolution images of modern newspapers and periodicals, each matched to its corresponding text.

The database covers representative newspapers from across China: the Shuntian Times in Beijing; Ta Kung Pao and Yi Shi Bao in Tianjin; the Shanghai News, Shen Bao and Republican Daily in Shanghai; the Central Daily News in Nanjing; and the Xinhua Daily in Chongqing. Richly illustrated, it draws on both northern and southern titles and offers a well-balanced body of content. At present, Shen Bao and Yi Shi Bao are fully included, while Tianjin's Ta Kung Pao and Beijing's Shuntian Times are being added at an accelerated pace.

In addition, the collection brings together more than 300 periodicals published from the late Qing dynasty to the early Republic, including the Gezhi Huibian, Xia'er Guanzhen, Liangyou Pictorial and The Eastern Miscellany, as well as the Provisional Government Gazette issued in 1912, when Sun Yat-sen established the Provisional Government of the Republic of China in Nanjing, and the English-language Chinese Repository.
