It is reported that, to enrich the cultural and sporting life of faculty and staff and to improve their fitness, the Shantou University Labor Union will again provide badminton facilities for staff this semester, beginning this week and running through to the end of the term.

According to the union, the badminton sessions are held every Wednesday evening from 18:00 to 20:00 at the badminton courts in the gymnasium. All faculty and staff are welcome to take part.

The union also reminded staff that the gymnasium courts have been re-planned: the former basketball area has been turned into badminton courts, so basketball cannot be played there during these sessions.
