It is reported that, in accordance with the Notice on the Review of Outstanding Graduate Students for 2013 issued by the Graduate School of Shantou University, and after student applications, recommendations by academic programs and review by the college, the College of Science of Shantou University plans to recommend 18 students to the university as candidates for the 2013 Shantou University Outstanding Graduate Scholarship. The results of the recommendation are now being publicized.

The recommended candidates are Liu JM, Zheng XW, Xie DZ, Li C, Zhang Y, Liu A, Li HY, Li W, Liao XB, Chen Y, Huang ZJ, Tang W, Li J, Xie ZQ, Zhao FD, Mei HJ, Liu XS and Wang C.

Those with different opinions on the above candidates are asked to submit them in writing to the College of Science before 5 p.m. on October 19, 2013. Materials submitted by a unit must bear the official seal of the unit, and those submitted by an individual must be signed with the person's real name to show accountability. Submissions may be delivered to the Graduate Academic Affairs Office of the College of Science in Room Li-353, and inquiries may also be made by telephone or email.
