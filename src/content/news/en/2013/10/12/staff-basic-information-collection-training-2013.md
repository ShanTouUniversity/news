It is reported that, in accordance with the Notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on the Collection of Basic Information of Staff and Faculty across the Province, and with the requirements of the provincial training session on this work held by the department in Guangzhou on October 10, 2013, basic information of staff and faculty at schools of all types and levels nationwide is to be entered into the database by November 15 this year. To carry out the collection of basic information of its staff and faculty, Shantou University will hold a training meeting from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Monday, October 14, 2013, in Meeting Room 201 of the New Administration Center.

The training will cover the following: arrangements for the university-wide collection of staff and faculty basic information; an explanation of the scope and indicator system of the collection; and guidance on the collection template, data collection tools and collection methods. Participants are the staff members designated by all units on the main campus.

The university noted that the collection of staff and faculty basic information is of great significance and is a task with a tight schedule and a heavy workload. All units are asked to attach great importance to it, designate a staff member in charge, and promptly organize relevant personnel to attend the training meeting and make arrangements for data reporting. Units should send the list of participants to o_rsc@stu.edu.cn before 12:00 noon on October 14, 2013.
