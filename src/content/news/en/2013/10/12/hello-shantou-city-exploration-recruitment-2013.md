It is reported that, to help 2013 freshmen better integrate into university life, feel the care and joy of the campus, and strengthen their sense of belonging, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and STU Youth will host "Hello, Shantou," a city exploration event as part of the 2013 Freshman Youth League Day activities.

## Activity Content

The event will be conducted in teams. Based on a theme drawn by lot (covering architecture, food, customs and more), a hand-drawn map and clues, freshmen will freely explore the urban area of Shantou.

## Time and Venue

The exploration will take place in Shantou's urban area from October 18 to 20. A sharing and awards session will be held at 3:00 PM on October 23; the exact venue will be announced separately.

## Eligibility and Requirements

The event is mainly open to all 2013 freshmen. Registration is accepted for teams or individuals: each team should have 5 to 6 members, with no more than 2 members from outside the 2013 freshman cohort; individual applicants will be randomly grouped by STU Youth staff according to the circumstances. After registering, each team must submit a brief activity plan based on the theme it has drawn. Shortlisted teams must complete their exploration between October 18 and 20 and attend the sharing and awards session on the afternoon of October 23.

## Registration Method and Deadline

The registration deadline is 12:00 noon on October 15. Those interested may download the registration form from the campus office information network, fill it in and send it to the designated registration email address (see the OA notice), naming the file "Name + Registration Form." They may also register in person at the promotional site (Golden Triangle) at noon on October 12, or fill in a paper registration form (available at the promotional posters or on site) and submit it to the collection point beside the posters before 12:00 noon on October 15.

## Seed Funding and Awards

Based on the exploration plans submitted by each team, the organizers will select 12 projects to fund, each receiving 100 yuan in seed funding. After the event, first, second and third prizes as well as a Most Popular Team award will be presented, with corresponding cash prizes.
