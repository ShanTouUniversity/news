It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on October 12, 2013, to hold a Community Tutor Training Camp on October 19–20.

## Training Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | All members of the Community Tutor Committee |
| Dates | October 19–20, 2013 |
| Venues | Room D503 and Student Activity Hall |
| Objectives | Enhance overall quality of the tutor team, improve service, strengthen residential community bonds, invigorate community culture and support self-growth |

## Notes

1. Attendance will be recorded; absence, lateness or early leave is not allowed without approval from the building counselor;
2. Phones must be turned off or set to silent, and participants should listen attentively and take notes.

All members are asked to attend on time. Details are in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ Training schedule is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
