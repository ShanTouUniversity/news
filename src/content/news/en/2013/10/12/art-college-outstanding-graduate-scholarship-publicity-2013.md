It is reported that, in accordance with the Notice on the Selection of Outstanding Graduate Students for 2013 issued by the Graduate School of Shantou University, the 2013 Shantou University Outstanding Graduate Scholarship selection committee of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has completed its review and proposed four graduate students as recipients of the 2013 Shantou University Outstanding Graduate Scholarship. The results are now being publicized.

The proposed recipients are Xiao JL, Lu J, Wang JH and Xu WS.

Units and individuals with different opinions are asked to submit them in writing to the Academic Affairs Office of the college before October 17, 2013. Materials submitted by a unit must bear the official seal of the unit, and those submitted by an individual must be signed with the person's real name to show accountability.
