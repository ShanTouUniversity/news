It is reported that, in line with the requirements of a notice from the Shantou Municipal Social Insurance Bureau, enrollment procedures have been completed for 2013-cohort students who applied to join Shantou's resident basic medical insurance through the university. On October 12, the Shantou University Medical Insurance Leading Group set out the details of the related insurance arrangements.

The list of students approved by the social insurance bureau to begin paying contributions in October is provided in Attachment 1. All participating students are enrolled under Tier One at a contribution standard of RMB 30 per person per year, and RMB 7.50 is deducted this time for the remaining three months of insurance. The coverage period runs from October 1 to December 31, 2013. Contributions are deducted on the university's behalf by a bank entrusted by the Finance Office from students' individual bank accounts, so participating students should deposit sufficient funds.

## Hospital Settlement and Reimbursement Outside Shantou

Before social security cards are fully issued and used across Shantou, participating students hospitalized at designated hospitals in Shantou may present their personal ID card so that the hospital can verify their contribution status and settle the bill directly as required.

Participating students who are hospitalized for an acute illness at hospitals outside Shantou during holidays, internships, practicums or leaves of absence may apply to the Jinping Branch of the Shantou Municipal Social Insurance Bureau for reimbursement within six months after discharge. The required documents are: the original invoice for inpatient medical expenses, an itemized list of medical expenses (totaling summary with the hospital's official seal), a diagnosis certificate (with the hospital's official seal), the inpatient medical record and discharge summary (with the hospital's official seal), a copy of the personal ID card, and a copy of a bank passbook or bank card.

## Renewal and Exemption Applications

Students who need coverage at the university from January 1, 2014 should complete an application form from November 15 to 30 at the Student Medical Insurance Office on the third floor of the university hospital; applications submitted after the deadline will not be processed. Postgraduate students who progressed from the university and already hold insurance through the university may apply to renew their coverage. Students with Shantou household registration who are already covered through their families and have not cancelled that coverage may not enroll again at the university.

Students applying to be exempted from personal contributions should submit a copy of the Minimum Living Guarantee Certificate between November 15 and 30; the certificate must carry a record confirming it remains valid beyond December 2013. Applications for exemption submitted after the deadline will not be processed, and the standard contribution of RMB 30 per person per year will apply.

The above arrangements were issued by the Shantou University Medical Insurance Leading Group on October 12, 2013.
