It is reported that the "Guangdong-HK Tech Youth Startup Program," jointly organized by the Hong Kong Cyberport Entrepreneurship Centre and the Guangdong Software Industry Association, aims to train young people's business skills, provide cooperation opportunities for youths from Guangdong and Hong Kong, and support youth entrepreneurship. To help Shantou University students learn more about youth startups and take part in the program, the university will hold a briefing session for the second edition of the program on October 16, 2013, and students from all colleges are welcome to attend.

The briefing will run from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon in the library lecture hall, with speakers from Hong Kong Cyberport Management Company Limited and the Guangdong Software Industry Association.

Looking back at the first edition, held in 2012, 29 STU students took part in the program, projects by 21 students were selected, 7 students won awards, and the winning projects received an award of HK$100,000.

According to reports, the program is supported by the Cyberport Creative Micro Fund (CCMF). It features non-repayable cash grants, promotes exchanges and cooperation between young people in Guangdong and Hong Kong, and offers entrepreneurship training. The program lasts six months, with total cash funding of HK$100,000 for developing prototype products.
