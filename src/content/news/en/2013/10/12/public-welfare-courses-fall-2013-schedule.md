It is reported that the class times and venues have been set for the fall 2013 public welfare courses **Shuguang Service Learning** and **Oral Histories of Leprosy Rehabilitated Villagers** at Shantou University. Both courses will proceed in four stages: service-site visits, course training, public service, and a final sharing session.

The service-site visit will take place on October 13, 2013, while the visit for the course Oral Histories of Leprosy Rehabilitated Villagers will be announced separately. Course training is scheduled for October 16, 23, 27 and 30. Public service will run from October 20 to December 15, and the final sharing session will be held on December 18. Detailed class times and venues are provided in the notice attachments.

## Course Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Courses | Shuguang Service Learning; Oral Histories of Leprosy Rehabilitated Villagers |
| Service-site visits | October 13, 2013 (announced separately for Oral Histories of Leprosy Rehabilitated Villagers) |
| Course training | October 16, 23, 27 and 30, 2013 |
| Public service | October 20 – December 15, 2013 |
| Final sharing | December 18, 2013 |

Students enrolled in the courses are reminded to make the necessary preparations and attend each stage on time.
