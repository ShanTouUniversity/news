It is reported that, after collection by the colleges and processing by the Admissions Office, the collection of archives for 2013 undergraduate freshmen at Shantou University is essentially complete, with the archives of the vast majority of freshmen already received. A small number of freshmen have yet to submit their archives; the list of those students is available in the attachment.

The Admissions Office has asked the relevant colleges to notify their freshmen who have not yet submitted archives to deliver them to the Admissions Office by October 29, 2013.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target | 2013 undergraduate freshmen who have not yet submitted their archives |
| Deadline | October 29, 2013 |
| Submission Location | Room 107, First Floor, New Administration Center (Admissions Office) |
| Contact Numbers | 86503666, 86502403 |
| Consequences of Late Submission | Submissions without valid reason will no longer be accepted, and students will bear the consequences of incomplete archives themselves |

Freshmen who genuinely cannot submit their archives on time due to special circumstances must explain the situation to the Admissions Office.

> ⚠️ The list of students yet to submit archives is available in the OA notice attachment.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Admissions Office)
