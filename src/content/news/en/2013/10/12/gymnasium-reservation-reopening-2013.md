It is reported that the relocation of facilities and the re-planning of courts at the Shantou University gymnasium have been completed, and the venue will resume open bookings for faculty, staff and students from Monday, October 14.

According to the Physical Education Department, teaching and training for sports teams will be given priority, and bookable hours for faculty, staff and students will be arranged on that basis. The specific times available for booking are those published on the department's website, which users can check at <http://www.spo.stu.edu.cn/custom.asp?id=12&type=0&main_id=4>.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Reopening date | Monday, October 14, 2013 |
| Who may book | All faculty, staff and students |
| Condition for booking | Hours are arranged on the basis of meeting teaching and sports team training needs |
| Where to check booking times | Physical Education Department website <http://www.spo.stu.edu.cn/custom.asp?id=12&type=0&main_id=4> |
