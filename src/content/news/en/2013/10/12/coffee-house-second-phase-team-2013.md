It is reported that the Student Entrepreneurship Center of the Student Affairs Office announced on October 12, 2013, the results for the second phase of operation of the Student Entrepreneurship Training Site (now known as the Coffee House).

The center issued a call for proposals on September 17, receiving eight business plans from student teams by the deadline. On October 10, a panel including Chen Zhulin, Lu Xiaohua, Zhuang Shaoming, Yao Mingan, Zeng Fanqi, Yu Hong and Su Junzhi reviewed the plans and selected the MUSCLE Team (members: Liu HL, Wu AD, Wang LX, He ZQ) as the winning team.
