It is reported that the selection process for the 2012 China College Student Self-Improvement Star campaign, organized by the Communist Youth League Central Committee and the All-China Students' Federation, has concluded. Xie ZQ, a 2011-cohort graduate student majoring in Materials Physics and Chemistry at Shantou University, received a Nomination Award in the selection and a prize of RMB 2,000.

The Youth League Committee of the university extended warm congratulations to the award recipient.

The campaign is open to full-time undergraduate and graduate students at higher-education institutions across China, with a focus on students who have outstanding records in patriotic dedication, moral conduct, scientific and technological innovation, self-reliant entrepreneurship, volunteer service, or perseverance in the face of disability. Universities recommend candidates for the selection under allocated quotas.
