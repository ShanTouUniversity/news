It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University has publicized its proposed recipients of the 2013 National Scholarship. In accordance with the university notice "On Conducting the 2013 National Scholarship, National Inspirational Scholarship and National Grant Review," and after assessment through interviews by the Business School 2013 National Scholarship Selection Committee, the school has proposed two 2010-cohort undergraduates as recipients of the 2013 National Scholarship.

The two nominees come from International Economics and Trade and from Business Administration respectively.

## Proposed Recipients

| Name | Cohort | Major |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feng Y | 2010 cohort | International Economics and Trade |
| Yang X | 2010 cohort | Business Administration |

## How to Raise Objections

During the publicity period, anyone who disagrees with the proposed recipients may submit comments in writing to the Business School Scholarship Review Committee before 5:00 pm on October 18, 2013. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with the sender's real name to take responsibility for the submission.

Submissions are received at the Business School Youth League Committee, second floor of Wenxi Building. The contact telephone number is 0754-86502725 and the email address is o_stw@stu.edu.cn.
