It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University will hold the 2013 Student Volunteer Summer "Three Rural Areas" Social Practice Review Meeting at 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, October 16, in Room 118 of the New Administration Center. Instructors of teams that have applied to become 2013 Guangdong college and secondary school student summer social-practice teams are required to attend on time and to give a summary presentation at the meeting.

According to the organizers, the meeting will have the applying teams report on how this summer's social practice was carried out and exchange their experience and methods. A total of 11 teams will take part in the presentations:

- STU "Three Rural Areas" Service-Learning Activity in Dongshan, Fujian
- Shuguang Educational Aid Work Camp at Yingfeng Primary School, Chaozhou
- "Passing on the Torch in Rural Communities" Caring Action Work Camp in Longxi
- "Passing on the Torch in Rural Communities" Caring Action Work Camp in Xiaguo
- "Heart for the Rehabilitation Village" Caring Action Work Camp in Linghou
- College of Science "Toward the Ocean" Public-Welfare Course Summer Outreach Team
- College of Science "Water Resource Protection" Education Activity
- Medical College Medical Poverty Alleviation Service-Learning Team
- Medical College National Defense Education Practice
- Medical College Students' Hometown Social Practice Activity
- Zhicheng College "Changqingteng" (Ivy) Rural Teaching Service Team
