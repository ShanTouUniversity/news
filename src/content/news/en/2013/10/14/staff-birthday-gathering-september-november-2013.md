It is reported that, to strengthen cohesion among faculty and staff, foster closer ties between them and help build a harmonious campus, the Shantou University Labor Union, with support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, will hold a birthday gathering for faculty and staff born in September, October and November at **6:30 p.m. on Saturday, October 26** at the **Junhua Hotel western restaurant**. Details of the event are set out in the attached activity plan.

Faculty and staff who wish to take part should register through the booking system, entering the channel for their own birth month.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 6:30 p.m., Saturday, October 26, 2013 |
| Venue | Junhua Hotel western restaurant |
| Booking period | 12:00 noon, October 14, to 12:00 noon, October 21 |
| Fee deduction (payment) period | October 21–23, 2013 |
| Ticket collection | 9:30 a.m.–12:00 noon, October 25 |
| Booking system | <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/>, using your campus internet account as the username and its password |
| Please note | Tickets cannot be refunded after payment; admission by ticket; children must be accompanied by a parent; take care with safety and keep personal belongings secure; do not waste food; travel is self-arranged |

## Booking and Payment

The booking system is at <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/>. Faculty and staff log in with their own campus internet account as the username and the same account password. Anyone who has trouble using the system can contact the Network Center on 3548 ext. 8842 or 3551 ext. 8842.

Faculty and staff who choose to pay in cash may follow the procedure to deposit the amount due into Shantou University's bank account at the Bank of China (account details are given in the original OA notice), then exchange the receipt issued by the bank for an event ticket at the Labor Union.

## Notes

- Tickets cannot be refunded once faculty and staff have registered and paid.
- Faculty, staff and their family members may take part on presentation of a ticket.
- Children must be accompanied by at least one parent. Parents should look after them well during the event, guide them to follow staff instructions and take care with safety, and keep personal belongings secure.
- Please do not waste food.
- The university will not arrange transportation; participants should travel independently and take care on the road.

> ⚠️ The event plan and payment instructions are available in the OA notice attachments.
