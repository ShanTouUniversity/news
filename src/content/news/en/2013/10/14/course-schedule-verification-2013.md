It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a notice stating that the course plan for the 2013 fall semester has been formally released and will not be adjusted further. All course instructors are asked to log in to the system to check and print the lists of students enrolled in their classes so that they can become familiar with their students for the semester.
