It is reported that the English Festival 2013 of Shantou University will take place from December 13 to 15. Organized by the English Language Center, this year's festival features three major intercollegiate events — a speech contest, a singing contest and a debate competition — with recruitment of undergraduate contestants for the speech and singing contests currently under way.

## The Three Contests at a Glance

| Contest | Key arrangements |
| ------- | ---------------- |
| Intercollegiate English Speech Contest | Submit a speech script of up to 3 minutes on the theme "My Life in Our World" to nchow@stu.edu.cn by 6:00 p.m., October 28; followed by essay review, interview and stage rounds |
| Intercollegiate English Singing Contest | Preliminaries on November 3, 3:30 p.m., at the English Activities Center (EAC); solo or duo entries with a one-minute performance each; top singers advance to the semifinal on November 24; inquiries via sarap@stu.edu.cn or the EAC front desk in Building 789 |
| Intercollegiate English Debate Competition | Open to students who enjoy debating hot topics; contact debate club teachers Anny (ngao@stu.edu.cn) or Collin (ctham@stu.edu.cn) to sign up, with a series of workshops to follow |

According to the organizers, the intercollegiate speech contest offers undergraduates the chance to communicate and compete with speakers from other universities, and the first step is to write a three-minute English speech on the theme "My Life in Our World".

For the singing contest, each contestant will have one minute in the preliminary round and must bring background music on a USB drive; the top singers will move on to the semifinal round.

As one of STU's signature campus events, the English Festival draws wide student participation every year, and the organizers look forward to more students joining this year's competitions.
