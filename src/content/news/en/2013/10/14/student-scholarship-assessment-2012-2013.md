It is reported that Shantou University will soon begin the assessment of its academic excellence scholarships, special scholarships and outstanding student scholarships for the 2012-2013 academic year. The assessment is open to undergraduates at the main campus and follows the Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate Scholarships of Shantou University (STU document [2010] No. 125).

## Application Period and Method

From October 14 to October 18, eligible students may apply online through the scholarship website (http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn) and log in to the Student Affairs System (http://sao.stu.edu.cn) to print the application form (no application form is required for the academic excellence scholarship). Materials must be submitted to the student's college (residential college) Youth League committee before 5:00 pm on October 18.

## Eligibility

The academic excellence scholarship is open to students whose GPA percentage in the assessment year ranks in the top 30 percent of their year and major and who have no failing courses. The outstanding student scholarship is open to students who rank in the top 10 percent by the same measure, have no failing courses, take an active part in campus cultural activities, public-welfare activities and social practice, and have received recognition at or above the university level.

The following four special scholarships may not be applied for at the same time: the Outstanding Contribution Award in Academic Science and Technology, the Outstanding Contribution Award in Sports and Culture, the Outstanding Contribution Award in Social Service, and the Outstanding Leadership Award. Likewise, the four active-participation special scholarships—in academic science and technology, campus cultural activities, social service, and campus service—may not be applied for at the same time.

## Screening and Selection Arrangements

From October 21 to October 25, the Student Financial Aid Management Center will screen applications for the academic excellence scholarship, determine the shortlist for each prize tier, and publish the winners after a three-day publicity period on the internal office information system. Students may check the results by logging in to the Student Affairs System.

Special scholarships are divided into award places and nomination places, which are allocated proportionally to colleges (residential colleges), the University Youth League Committee and other relevant units (see the attachments for details). Nomination places for the outstanding student scholarship are determined by student numbers and set at 1.5 times the number of award places.

By October 25, colleges (residential colleges) and the University Youth League Committee must submit their nomination lists and application forms for the Outstanding Contribution Award in Academic Science and Technology, the Outstanding Contribution Award in Sports and Culture, the Outstanding Contribution Award in Social Service and the Outstanding Leadership Award to the Student Financial Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office. By October 30, colleges (residential colleges) must complete the preliminary selection for the outstanding student scholarship and report their nominees to the center.

The university's special scholarship review sub-committee will select the recipients of the four special scholarships in mid-November. In late November, colleges (residential colleges) will complete the selection for the four active-participation special scholarships and report the results to the Student Affairs Office, and the university scholarship review committee will determine the recipients of the outstanding student scholarship.

## Other Matters

- Colleges (residential colleges) must establish their scholarship review panels and report the membership lists to the Student Affairs Office before October 18.
- After submitting an online application, students must log in to the Student Affairs System to check and confirm that their application was submitted successfully.
- If an application form mentions an award at or above the university level, a copy of the award certificate must be provided.
- Students who encounter problems with the online application may email xyli@stu.edu.cn for advice.
- Any change to the assessment schedule will be announced separately on the internal office information network or the scholarship website.
