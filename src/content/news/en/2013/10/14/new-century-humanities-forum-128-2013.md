It is reported that the 128th session of the New Century Humanities Forum of Shantou University will be held on the afternoon of October 16. Professor Wang Rumei of Jilin University has been invited to give a lecture titled "From Jin Ping Mei to Dream of the Red Chamber: The Historical Evolution of Full-Length Ming-Qing Novels of Manners." The forum is organized by the College of Liberal Arts of Shantou University.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

| Speaker | Wang Rumei, professor at Jilin University |
| Time | 3:00–5:00 p.m., October 16, 2013 |
| Venue | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Organizer | College of Liberal Arts, Shantou University |

## About the Lecture

According to the organizer, the lecture will take Jin Ping Mei and Dream of the Red Chamber as its thread, discussing the historical evolution of full-length novels of manners and tracing the development of this genre during the Ming and Qing dynasties.
