It is reported that, in preparation for the 2014 Cross-Strait College Students Market Survey Competition, the Business School of Shantou University began recruiting students interested in the contest on October 14.

As the competition centres on market research, participating students must have completed or be currently taking two of the following courses: Marketing Management, Marketing Research and Statistics.

Students who wish to register or ask for further information may contact Mr. Huang Wangming of the Business School.

## Key Facts

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | 2014 Cross-Strait College Students Market Survey Competition |
| Who can apply | Students interested in the market survey competition |
