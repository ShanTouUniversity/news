It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University released the internship assignments for 2010-cohort law students in Shantou on October 14. A total of 19 students applied, and the internships will begin on October 21.

After the Law School issued its notice on Shantou-area internships for the 2010 cohort, students signed up in large numbers. Based on the students' stated preferences and the number of internship places offered by each host organisation, and through coordination by the Career Development Center, the final allocation was determined.

## Students Assigned by Host Organisation

| Host organisation | Students assigned |
| --- | --- |
| Guangdong Guoyuan Lingdong Law Firm | 2 |
| Shantou Intermediate People's Court | 10 |
| Shantou Longhu District People's Court | 6 |
| Shantou Legislative Affairs Office | 1 |
| **Total** | **19** |
