It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Club Federation will hold the first student club presidents' meeting and project mobilization conference of the fall semester of the 2013–2014 academic year from 14:30 to 17:00 on Wednesday, October 16, 2013, in Room D502. The meeting is intended for the heads of all student clubs and will mobilize work related to this semester's club activity project applications.

Club leaders are asked to prepare in advance and attend on time.
