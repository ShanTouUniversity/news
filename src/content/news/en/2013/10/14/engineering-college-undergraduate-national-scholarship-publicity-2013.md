It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University has publicized its proposed recipients of the 2013 National Scholarship. In accordance with the university notice "On Conducting the 2013 National Scholarship, National Inspirational Scholarship and National Grant Review," and after assessment through interviews by the Engineering College 2013 National Scholarship Selection Committee, the college has proposed three 2010-cohort students as recipients of the 2013 National Scholarship.

The three nominees come from Mechatronics, Electronics and Civil Engineering respectively.

## Proposed Recipients

| Name | Cohort | Major |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qiu HC | 2010 cohort | Mechatronics |
| Zeng XJ | 2010 cohort | Electronics |
| Yang J | 2010 cohort | Civil Engineering |

## How to Raise Objections

During the publicity period, anyone who disagrees with the proposed recipients may submit comments in writing to the Engineering College Scholarship Review Panel before 5:00 pm on October 18, 2013. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with the sender's real name to take responsibility for the submission.

Submissions are received at the Engineering College Youth League Committee, Gongnan 210. The contact telephone number is 0754-86502986 and the email address is ydshen@stu.edu.cn.
