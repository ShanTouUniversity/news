It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a notice on major transfer applications for undergraduates of the 2012 cohort. The work is carried out in accordance with the "Shantou University Undergraduate Student Status Management Regulations" (Shan Da Fa [2013] No. 67). The office has released information on the relevant programs, including the number of places open to students transferring in from other majors, eligibility requirements, and the times and venues for application and assessment.

Undergraduates of the 2012 cohort who wish to transfer majors may, according to the published requirements, fill in the "Shantou University Student Major Transfer Application Form" (Attachment 1), have their transcripts printed by the academic affairs officer of the department offering their current major, obtain the signed approval of the department head and the dean, register with the department offering the intended major, and attend the assessment at the time announced by that department.

Departments receiving transfer students must submit students' assessment results, their application forms and the "Shantou University Course Plan for Transfer Students" to the Academic Affairs Office in the tenth week. The Academic Affairs Office will review the transfer applications and other relevant materials and publish the list of students it proposes to approve for transfer. The university will announce its decisions on major transfers in the twelfth week, and approved students will transfer to their new majors from the spring semester.
