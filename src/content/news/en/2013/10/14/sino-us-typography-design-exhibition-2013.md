It is reported that "Typeface: Sino-US Typography Design Exhibition 2013" will tour eight cities in China, and that the Shantou stop, co-hosted by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design (CKAD) of Shantou University and the Shantou Graphic Design Club, will be held at the CKAD Gallery from October 18 to 30, 2013.

The exhibition brings together more than 200 typographic poster works by over 180 outstanding designers from China and the United States. The participating designers include internationally renowned design masters, outstanding teachers in the field of design education, and outstanding young and middle-aged designers active in poster design. The exhibition is organized by and premiered at Gallery 5 in Richmond, Virginia, in the United States, and is then touring eight cities in China. The CKAD faculty members taking part are Kan Tai-keung, Wu Yong, Zhang Yi, Lin Guobiao and Cai Qizhen.

The opening ceremony of the Shantou stop will be held at 5 p.m. on October 18, 2013 at the CKAD Gallery, Shantou University. The exhibition is organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design of Shantou University and the School of Design and Art of the Beijing Institute of Graphic Communication, and co-organized by the Shantou Graphic Design Club.
