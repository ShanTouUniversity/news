It is reported that, with the active cooperation and joint efforts of part-time archivists in all units, Shantou University has basically completed the archiving of documents and materials for 2012. The university recently issued a report on this round of archiving work, in which 43 archiving units across the university transferred a total of 13,136 items.

## Archiving Results

The report shows that the quality of documents and the accuracy of information entry for 2012 improved compared with the previous year, and that duplicate archiving decreased. Units that did relatively well included the Academic Affairs Office, the Graduate School, the School of Continuing Education, the Alumni Affairs Office, the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation, the Scientific Research Office, the Resource Management Office (Equipment Office and Capital Construction Office), the Youth League Committee, the Student Affairs Office, the Library, the University Press, the Editorial Office of the University Journal, the Publicity Department (including the News Center and the University Newspaper Editorial Office), the College of Liberal Arts, the Business School, and the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication.

The report also noted that the Law School, the College of Science and the College of Engineering were among the units whose archives were incomplete.

## Existing Problems

- A few units did not archive all of their documents, and some colleges lacked graduation group photos;
- Some units had quality problems such as incomplete signatures and seals;
- A few units recorded basic document information incorrectly, did not load electronic files, or submitted only electronic files without printed paper materials; some of the uploaded electronic files contained errors and omissions;
- A small number of duplicate archives were found.

## Restated Archiving Requirements

The university restated specific requirements for archiving work in the report:

1. Part-time archivists should carefully study the "Scope and Retention Periods of Archives of Shantou University" so that archived materials are collected completely, systematically and comprehensively;
2. They should proactively collect and organize all kinds of materials, especially Party and government electronic official documents, and archive them promptly, filing them as soon as they are handled, as required by the Notice on Archiving Party and Government Official Documents issued by the Party and Government Office on July 2, 2013;
3. Before archiving, they should check transferred archives in the system to avoid duplicate archiving;
4. Documents and materials should be archived in both electronic and paper form; electronic files kept for short periods (non-official documents) need not be submitted on paper, while printed paper materials must be legible;
5. Each photo should have its occasion, time, photographer and location clearly noted in the archives management system; photos should be at least 4000×3000 pixels and about 4MB in size, and a few representative, well-taken photos should be selected for archiving for each activity;
6. Units with incomplete archives should finish supplementary archiving by October 30.
