It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University publicized its proposed 2013 National Scholarship recipients on October 14. In accordance with the university's notice on the assessment of the 2013 National Scholarship, National Inspirational Scholarship and National Grants, the School's Youth League Committee conducted a preliminary review of applications and shortlisted seven students, including Huang B, for interview.

The Law School scholarship assessment panel then interviewed the seven students and proposed two of them, Yang HY and Zhuang SL, as the Law School's 2013 National Scholarship recipients. The list has been publicized.

During the publicity period, students with objections may raise them with the Law School Youth League Committee in writing, in person or by telephone before Friday, October 18. The committee said that materials submitted on behalf of an organisation must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with a real name so that responsibility can be established.
