It is reported that the Graduate Volunteer Team of Shantou University's Graduate School issued a notice on October 14 recruiting graduate students as volunteers for the “Fun Tour of STU, Joyful Learning at Zoutang” activity. The activity will be held on October 19, with about 16 volunteers to be recruited.

According to the notice, a university is a place children long to see yet know little about. STU is about to welcome a group of lovely children who look forward to meeting volunteers; students are welcome to join and become “guides for their dreams”.

## Activity Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Fun Tour of STU, Joyful Learning at Zoutang |
| Time | 8:00–15:00, Saturday, October 19, 2013 |
| Venue | Shantou University |
| Volunteers needed | About 16 |
| Program | Guiding primary school pupils around campus on the morning of October 19; English games at the English Language Center in the afternoon to develop their interest in English |
| Reward | Each volunteer's service hours for the day will be recorded |
| Application deadline | 17:00, October 17, 2013 |

## How to Apply

Interested students should apply by email, using the format “Volunteer + Name + Major + Contact Details”; the application email address is given in the original OA notice. Places are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

> ⚠️ The application email address is available on the OA system.
>
