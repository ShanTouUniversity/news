The Logistics Management Office of Shantou University has announced the utility fees for staff dormitories for September 2013. The latest meter readings were taken on October 1 and 2, following the previous readings on September 1.

Faculty members can log in to the [Shantou University Utility Management System](http://power.stu.edu.cn) to check the amount due, using their campus-network accounts. Those who have questions about the charges may visit the Water and Electricity Center for a review between October 14 and 16; the center can be reached on 2489. If no objection is raised by the end of that period, the Finance Office will deduct the fees from individual October 2013 payroll accounts.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | September 2013 |
| Meter readings | October 1–2, 2013 (previous readings on September 1) |
| Inquiry system | Shantou University Utility Management System, http://power.stu.edu.cn (log in with a campus-network account) |
| Review period | October 14–16, 2013 |
| Contact | Water and Electricity Center, 2489 |
| Deduction | Deducted by the Finance Office from individual October 2013 payroll accounts if no objection is raised |
