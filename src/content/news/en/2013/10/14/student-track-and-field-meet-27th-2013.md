It is reported that, in order to promote the development of sports at the university and raise students' awareness of sports participation, the 27th Shantou University Track and Field Meet will be held over two days, December 7 and 8, 2013, at the university track and field ground.

According to the organizers, the meet aims to boost campus sports and encourage students to pursue the ideal of "exercise one hour a day, work healthily for 50 years and enjoy a happy life for a lifetime". All colleges and residential colleges are asked to publicize the event widely, organize their participation carefully and take an active part, so as to help ensure the event runs smoothly.

| --- | --- |
