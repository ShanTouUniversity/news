It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Club Federation announced on October 14 the results of the 2012–2013 spring semester Student Club Project Showcase. The showcase was held on September 25 in Room 304 of Building D, with eight student clubs taking part: the Guitar Association, the Sport Dance Association, the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association, the Sign Language Club, the Go Association, the Photography Association, the Finance Association and the Roller Skating Enthusiasts Association.

In accordance with the Regulations on Student Club Activity Applications of the STU Student Club Federation, and after careful review by the judging panel and approval by the University Youth League Committee, the ranking, score and award of each club project were finalized. The results were originally released as an attachment to the notice and are summarized below.
