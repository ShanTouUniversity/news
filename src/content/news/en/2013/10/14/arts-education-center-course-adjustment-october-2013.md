It is reported that, because Yu Duanrong of the Arts Education Center will be away on a business trip, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has adjusted the class times of three courses offered by the Arts Education Center. The three sessions originally scheduled for week 6 — Dance Art Appreciation, Chinese Folk Dance and Ballet Body Training — will not be held, and makeup classes have been arranged in week 5. All classes will continue to meet in the dance rehearsal room, STU102.

The Academic Affairs Office asks students enrolled in these courses to pass the word to one another and to attend the makeup classes on time.

## Key Information

| Class number | Course | Cancelled session (time and venue) | Makeup session (time and venue) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 62493 | Dance Art Appreciation | Periods 8–9, Tuesday of week 6 (October 22, 2013), dance rehearsal room STU102 | Periods A–B, Tuesday of week 5 (October 15, 2013), dance rehearsal room STU102 |
| 62494 | Chinese Folk Dance | Periods 3–4, Monday of week 6 (October 21, 2013), dance rehearsal room STU102 | Periods 6–7, Wednesday of week 5 (October 16, 2013), dance rehearsal room STU102 |
| 62495 | Ballet Body Training | Periods 8–9, Monday of week 6 (October 21, 2013), dance rehearsal room STU102 | Periods A–B, Wednesday of week 5 (October 16, 2013), dance rehearsal room STU102 |
