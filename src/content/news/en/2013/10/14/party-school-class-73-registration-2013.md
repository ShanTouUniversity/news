It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee issued a notice on October 14, 2013, that the 73rd Introductory Party Knowledge Class is scheduled to open on October 20.

## Registration and Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | October 20, 2013 |
| Registration | October 17–18 (Thursday to Friday) |
| Requirements | Bring one one-inch frontal bareheaded photo (with unit and name on the back) to the group leader to register and collect materials |
| Group leaders’ meeting | 13:00, October 17, Wenbei 206 |
| Leave | Those needing leave must complete the *Party School Leave Approval Form*, reviewed by the general Party branch and approved by the Party School Office; those absent without reason will be disqualified |
| Forfeiture | Failure to register without valid reason is considered forfeiture |
| Issued by | Organization and United Front Work Department |

The participant list, group leader contacts and course schedule are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The participant list and schedule are available on the OA system.
