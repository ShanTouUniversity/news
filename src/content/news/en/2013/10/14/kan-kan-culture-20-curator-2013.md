It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold Kan Kan Culture 20 (Dialogue on Culture 20) at **7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, October 15** in the **school's first-floor lecture hall**. The theme is **“Why is it so difficult to find a good curator?”**

The dialogue will bring together Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Oscar Ho, programme director of the Master of Arts in Cultural Management at the Chinese University of Hong Kong. Ho is a Professional Consultant and Associate Professor of Practice in Cultural Management. The event is organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design.
