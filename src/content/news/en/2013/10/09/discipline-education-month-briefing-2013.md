It is reported that during this year's Discipline Education Month, the Discipline Inspection Commission of Shantou University will compile and issue the "Shantou University Discipline Education Month Briefing" based on the activities carried out across the university. To report on discipline education work in a timely manner, the office of the commission is calling on all units, Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to submit briefs on their activities.

According to the requirements, all units, Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches should submit their activity briefs to the office of the Discipline Inspection Commission by 12:00 on October 12 (Saturday), sending them by email to o_jj@stu.edu.cn with "Discipline Education Month Briefing" as the subject line.

## Submission Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Content | Briefs on the discipline education activities carried out by each unit |
| Who should submit | All units, Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches |
| Deadline | 12:00, October 12 (Saturday) |
| How to submit | By email, with "Discipline Education Month Briefing" as the subject line |
| Email | o_jj@stu.edu.cn |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office)
