It is reported that, in accordance with the university notice on extracurricular academic and technology projects for graduate students and the requirements of the project development schedule, the Graduate School will soon conduct a mid-term review of the graduate extracurricular academic and technology projects that have received funding, in order to learn about the progress of each project.

The Graduate School requires that all project leaders submit a printed mid-term review form, including the comments and signature of the supervisor, to the Graduate School (fourth floor of the new Administration Center) by October 14, 2013, and send an electronic version of the form to o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Projects under review | Graduate extracurricular academic and technology projects that have received funding |
| Materials | Printed mid-term review form (with the supervisor's comments and signature) and an electronic version |
| Deadline | Before October 14, 2013 |
| Submission location | Graduate School (fourth floor of the new Administration Center) |
| Email | o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn |
| Consequences of non-submission | Projects that fail to submit the mid-term report as required will be regarded as abandoned, and the start-up funding will be recovered |

> ⚠️ The mid-term review form is available in the OA notice attachment.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
