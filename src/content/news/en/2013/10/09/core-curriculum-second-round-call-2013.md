It is reported that, following a decision by the university Academic Committee and its teaching-reform arrangements, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has opened a second round of proposals for core courses. From the 2013 undergraduate cohort onward, the university will use new core courses as the main route for general education and plans to build a more complete core-curriculum system over two to three years. In the spring 2013 semester, the university issued a first call to teaching units and faculty, which drew a broad and enthusiastic response; after review by the Core-Course Expert Group, 45 core courses were selected and received appropriate funding from the university. The new round is open to all teaching units and faculty, and the proposed courses are expected to open in the spring 2014 semester.

## Curriculum structure and priorities

The core-curriculum system the university plans to build comprises three fields and 13 course groups. Proposed courses must be assigned to a specific course group and match its objectives; the composition of the three fields and 13 groups, together with guidance on course design and teaching methods, is set out in the *Core Curriculum Application Guide*.

Approved core courses are unevenly distributed across the three fields and the 13 groups. The current call particularly welcomes courses in the following areas:

| Priority | Details |
| --- | --- |
| First field | Courses related to “Citizen and Society” |
| Second field | History of Chinese Philosophy within “Culture and Values” |
| Third field | Quantitative reasoning and philosophy of science |
| Interdisciplinary courses | Such as “Energy” and “Genetically Modified Organisms” |

## “Erya” course resources

An off-campus educational media institution provides course resources to the university on a paid basis, including lecture videos and assignments. The university has selected the following courses from that offering, and faculty interested in teaching them may use the resources provided by the institution. To preview the resources, faculty may email Mr. Zhang at the Academic Affairs Office (zzhang@stu.edu.cn).

1. Traditional Chinese Thought: Dialogue with Pre-Qin Philosophy
2. The Beauty of Chinese Poetry
3. Introduction to Literary Anthropology
4. Principles of Aesthetics
5. Contemporary Chinese Government and Politics
6. Mathematical Ways of Thinking

## Application and funding

Proposed core courses that pass review by the university Core-Course Expert Group will be listed in the university Core Course Directory and receive funding of a certain amount. Courses listed in the directory and funded by the university should be offered every semester.

Applicants must submit a core-course application form (template in Attachment 2) and a course syllabus (template in Attachment 3; Attachment 4 provides reference material for writing a core-course syllabus).

One paper copy of the materials should be submitted to the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office, and an electronic copy should be emailed to zzhang@stu.edu.cn. The Core-Course Expert Group will review the proposals as soon as possible and provide revision comments to the syllabus authors.

## Timeline

To allow the core courses to open in the spring 2014 semester, faculty applying for a core course should submit the above materials to the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office by Friday, November 1, 2013.
