Shantou University has launched its **2013 housing subsidy** application process. Under provincial and municipal housing subsidy policies, eligible newly transferred employees must submit their housing subsidy materials to the University Property Office by **October 22**. After consolidating the materials, the office will submit the applications to the relevant provincial and municipal authorities by the **end of October**. Those who have already applied for the subsidy in previous years do not need to apply again.

## Who May Apply

The housing subsidy is intended for staff holding formal posts at institutions directly administered by Guangdong Province whose personnel relationship is registered with Shantou University. Applicants and their spouses must also meet all of the following conditions:

- Neither has purchased a housing-reform residence, a hardship-relief residence, or taken part in employer-organized collective housing construction.
- Neither has received a government-recognized housing subsidy.
- Both have stopped receiving current housing-reform or low-rent benefits and are paying rent at cost or commercial rates.

## Documents Required

Applicants must first complete one copy of the *Housing Subsidy Personal Information Form* (Attachment 1); the formal form is to be completed only after the University completes its preliminary review. For applicants whose household registration is not in Shantou, the formal form must also be reviewed by the housing-reform department at the place of registration. Depending on their circumstances, applicants must additionally provide:

1. The original ID cards of both the applicant and the spouse; the originals are returned after the Property Office makes copies.
2. For newly graduated appointees, the original *Assignment Introduction Letter for Graduates of Secondary and Tertiary Institutions*, borrowed from the University Human Resources Office, to be copied and returned; a photocopy of the *Guangdong Province Public Institution Recruitment Review Form* must also be provided.
3. For transferred employees, the original *Personnel Transfer Letter* and *Cadre Introduction Letter*, borrowed from the Human Resources Office, to be copied and returned; a photocopy of the *Shantou Municipal Public Institution Personnel Transfer Approval Form* must also be provided.
4. Transferred employees must also provide certificates issued by the housing-reform office at their former place of household registration and by their former employer, confirming that neither they nor their spouses have purchased housing-reform, hardship-relief or collectively funded housing.
5. Divorced applicants must provide the original *Divorce Agreement* of both parties or the *Divorce Agreement* embodied in a court judgment; the originals are returned after copying.

## Registration of Changes in Professional Title or Position

Employees who already receive the housing subsidy monthly and whose professional title or position has changed must complete the *Professional Title (Position) Change Registration Form* (Attachment 2) and submit it to the University Property Office by **October 22**. After verification by the University's human resources or organization office, the information will be reported to the relevant provincial and municipal authorities. The new subsidy standard is calculated from the month in which the national salary adjustment has been paid.

> ⚠️ The application forms are available in the OA notice attachments.
