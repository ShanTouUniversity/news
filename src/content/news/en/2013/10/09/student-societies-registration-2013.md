It is reported that, in accordance with Article 9 of Chapter II of the *Regulations on the Management of Shantou University Student Societies* and in light of actual working conditions, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Student Societies Union have launched the 2013-2014 student society registration. Every student society is required to complete both its annual materials submission and its *Society Permit* registration **before October 16**.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | Before October 16, 2013 |
| Materials to submit | A 2012-2013 annual work summary, a summary of recruitment in the autumn semester of 2013, a 2013-2014 work plan and the 2013-2014 basic member registration form — four documents in total |
| Electronic submission | Send as a compressed archive to the email address of the Societies Union Information Department (see the OA notice) |
| Paper submission | Room 731, Building 7, from 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. daily from October 9 to 15 (except Saturday and Sunday) |
| Permit registration | Apply to register the *Society Permit* at the Societies Union Information Department after completing the annual materials submission |

## Annual Materials Submission

Under the requirements, each society must submit four documents:

1. A summary of its work in the 2012-2013 academic year, covering activities and the use of funds;
2. A summary of the society’s recruitment in the autumn semester of 2013, stating the number of new members recruited and the collection of membership fees;
3. A work plan for the 2013-2014 academic year, including planned activities and a plan for the use of membership fees;
4. The association’s 2013-2014 basic member registration form, which societies can download from the materials download section of the Societies Union homepage.

Materials may be submitted electronically or on paper. Electronic materials should be sent as a compressed archive to the email address of the Societies Union Information Department (see the OA notice); paper materials should be handed to staff of the department at Room 731, Building 7, **from 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. every day from October 9 to 15, except Saturday and Sunday**.

## Society Permit Registration

After completing the annual materials submission, each society must go to the Societies Union Information Department to apply for and register its *Society Permit*.

The notice states that the annual materials submission and the *Society Permit* registration are important parts of the management of student society organizations and an indicator of the standardized development of societies at the university. The university attaches great importance to this work and asks all societies to carry it out diligently.

> ⚠️ The member registration form and contact details are available in the OA notice.
