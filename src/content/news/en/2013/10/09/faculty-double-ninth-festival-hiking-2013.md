It is reported that, in order to enliven the sports and cultural life of faculty and staff, improve their fitness, promote exchanges among them and foster a healthy and harmonious campus atmosphere, the Shantou University Labor Union will hold a Double Ninth Festival hike for faculty and staff on the afternoon of Saturday, October 12. The event is open to faculty and staff on the main campus.

Participants will assemble and set off at 3:00 p.m. in front of the **school motto monument**. The route runs along the campus road from the monument toward the Basic Medical Sciences Division of the Medical College, passes through the wall opening opposite the graduate student apartments, joins Nongmin Road for about 10 metres, then continues up the hill to the summit finish at the **Cixin Pavilion**, where participants can collect a hiking souvenir coupon, before returning to campus along the same route.

## Notes for Participants

1. The activity takes place on mountain paths. Staff are asked to decide whether to join based on their own physical condition and provided it does not affect their work, and to set their own pace and manage their energy during the climb.
2. The activity carries a certain level of risk; those with hypertension or cardiovascular and cerebrovascular conditions are discouraged from participating.
3. Participants should gather in front of the school motto monument before 2:55 p.m. and collect bottled water there.
4. Please wear loose clothing (long trousers and long sleeves are preferred) and sports shoes, and take precautions such as sun protection. Take care on the trail, walk with others as much as possible, do not leave the group at will, and racing is discouraged.
5. Smoking and the use of open flames are strictly prohibited during the hike. Please protect the environment and dispose of rubbish in bins or at designated collection points.
6. Medical service points staffed by the university hospital will be available midway up the hill and at the summit finish.
