It is reported that Shantou University Library has released the seventh issue of its featured-literature series, focusing on the renowned contemporary writer Qin Mu. The library holds more than 90 titles related to Qin Mu, and 14 representative books have been selected for this issue. The complete bibliography is available in the attachment.

## Life and Career

Qin Mu (1919–1992), originally named Lin Juefu, was born in Hong Kong and traced his family roots to Chenghai, Guangdong. At the age of three he moved to Singapore with his parents and received his primary education there. He returned to China in 1932 and studied in Chenghai, Shantou and Hong Kong. In the spring of 1938 he was engaged in anti-war publicity work in Guangzhou, and later moved between Guangdong, Guangxi and Sichuan provinces, working as a battlefield worker and a newspaper editor. In 1949 he entered the Dongjiang liberated area.

After the founding of New China, Qin Mu served successively as director of the Guangzhou editorial office of Zhonghua Book Company, vice chairman of the Guangdong Branch of the Chinese Writers Association, and deputy editor-in-chief of Yangcheng Evening News. From 1978 onward, he held posts including deputy director of the Guangdong Provincial Literary and Art Creation Office, editor-in-chief of the magazine *Works*, director of the Chinese Department of Jinan University, vice chairman and executive chairman of the Guangdong Federation of Literary and Art Circles, member of the China Federation of Literary and Art Circles, and council member of the Chinese Writers Association.

## Writing and Influence

Over the past half century, Qin Mu produced a large body of work in many literary forms, including prose, fiction, drama, poetry and literary theory, and was best known for his distinctive prose. Hailed as a master of contemporary prose, he holds an important place in the history of contemporary Chinese literature.

According to the feature, Qin Mu was a writer of strong individuality. He adhered to the spirit of realism, sincerely confronting reality and life, while bringing a broad vision and philosophical reflection to his understanding of life. He was skilled at choosing distinctive and informative subjects and at articulating fresh and profound perceptions of life and insights, embedding ideas and philosophy in wide-ranging discussions of the world and of past and present. With the appeal of thought, knowledge and beauty, his works have nourished the minds of generation after generation of readers and have exerted a deep and wide influence at home and abroad.
