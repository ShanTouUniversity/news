Shantou University's Logistics Management Office has released the **September 2013 student-dormitory utility bills**. The latest meter reading was taken on **September 30**, following the previous reading on **August 31**. Students can check their individual bills on the Shantou University utility-fee management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/> using their own campus-network accounts.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | September 2013 |
| Meter readings | Latest reading on September 30; previous reading on August 31 |
| Query system | Shantou University utility-fee management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/>, accessed with campus-network accounts |
| Review period and location | Working hours, October 9-15, at the Utility Center (beside the Science and Technology Building, tel. `86502489`) |
| Deduction and payment dates | Campus-card deductions by the Network Center begin October 16; regular payment window October 16-18 |
| Relevant telephone numbers | Utility Center `86502489`; Counselor Office `86503355`; Network Center `86503545` |

## Review and Payment Arrangements

Students who find errors, omissions or anything unclear may complete and sign a *Utility-Fee Review Application Form* and submit it to the Utility Center during working hours from **October 9 to 15**. The Utility Center is located beside the Science and Technology Building and can be reached at `86502489`. If the dormitory listed does not match the student roster, students should contact the Counselor Office in Building B, Room 106, tel. `86503355`.

If no objections are raised before the review period ends, the Network Center (tel. `86503545`) will begin deducting the fees through the campus card from **October 16**. The regular payment window is **October 16-18**; those who fail to pay in time will be handled in accordance with University regulations from **October 21**.

In addition, because the billing process involves several departments, objections raised after the review period that require correction after verification will take **three to five working days** to complete. Students whose campus-card balance is insufficient are advised to top up promptly.

> ⚠️ The review application form is available in the OA notice attachment.
