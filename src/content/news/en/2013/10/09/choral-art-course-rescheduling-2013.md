It is reported that, because Liao Wenlan of the Arts Education Centre will be away on a business trip to attend a meeting, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has changed the class time of the general education course Choral Art and Works Appreciation.

The course (class number 62470) was originally scheduled for periods 6 and 7 on Monday of week 5 (October 14) in the music rehearsal room, STU101. That session will not be held; the makeup class will take place during periods 6 and 7 on Saturday of week 5 (October 19), in the same venue, the music rehearsal room STU101.

Students taking the course are asked to pass the word to one another and to attend the makeup class on time.
