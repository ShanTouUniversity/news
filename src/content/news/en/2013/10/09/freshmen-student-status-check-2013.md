It is reported that, in line with arrangements by the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Ministry's CHSI (chsi.com.cn) platform has opened its enrollment verification function for 2013 freshmen. Undergraduates admitted to Shantou University in 2013 may verify their registration and student status records online between October 9 and October 24, 2013.

## How to Check

Students can visit the CHSI inquiry page (http://xjxl.chsi.com.cn/xscx.action) and enter their ID number, name and verification code to look up their admission and registration status. Points to note:

| Item | Instruction |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate number | Not required (it differs from the gaokao admission ticket number) |
| Letters in ID numbers | Enter in uppercase |
| Students from Hong Kong and Macao | Use half-width parentheses in ID numbers |

## About Real-Name Registration

The Academic Affairs Office noted that completing the verification above is sufficient for now. Since real-name registration of a CHSI archive account relates to future verification of bachelor's degree certificates, students are advised to register in their junior year to avoid forgetting credentials while still on campus.

Those who do wish to register now must ensure that the name and ID number entered match their enrollment records exactly, using uppercase for any letters; students from Hong Kong and Macao should select "Other" as the document type and enter their ID number accordingly. The account is the sole credential for querying enrollment records on the national platform and verifying degree certificates after graduation, so usernames and passwords should be kept safe.

The university emphasized that it cannot resolve problems such as incorrect information entered during registration, forgotten credentials, or records registered by others due to leaked personal data — affected students must contact the CHSI platform's customer service directly.
