It is reported that Shantou University will conduct a psychological test for all 2013 freshmen on Sunday, October 13, in order to build psychological records for the cohort. The test uses three instruments — the University Personality Inventory (UPI), the Cattell 16 Personality Factor Questionnaire (16PF) and the Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90). With support from the Computer Teaching Center, students may complete the test free of charge in Computer Rooms E101 and E102 of Teaching Building E within the designated period.

## Test arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Sunday, October 13, 2013 |
| Participants | All 2013 freshmen |
| Instruments | University Personality Inventory (UPI), Cattell 16 Personality Factor Questionnaire (16PF), Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90) |
| Venue | Computer Rooms E101 and E102, Teaching Building E |
| System availability | Opens after 6 p.m. on October 12 |
| Organizer | STU Student Affairs Office (supported by the Computer Teaching Center) |

## Test steps

1. Visit the Psychological Counseling Center website at http://xjbx.stu.edu.cn/psytest6.
2. Select “Student” as the user type, sign in with the student number as the username and the initial password provided in the notice, then change the original password and complete the basic personal profile.
3. Click “Start Test” and complete all three scales in full.
4. After all three scales are finished, click “My Report” to view the test results.
5. Click “Appointments and Counseling” to submit questions a student would like to discuss and to select a counselor. While studying at STU, students may sign in to the website with their student number and password to check appointment and counseling messages issued by the Center.

## Reminders

Each student has 60 minutes to complete the test. Students should read the “Instructions” before answering the questions carefully.
