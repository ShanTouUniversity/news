It is reported that, because Sun Zewei of the Institute of Marine Biology will be away on a business trip, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has adjusted the schedule of the general education course Ocean Biology Discovery.

The session (class number 62794) was originally a week 4 Monday class that had been moved to Saturday, October 12, periods 6 and 7, in Room E207 under the National Day holiday schedule. It will now not be held, and the makeup time has yet to be decided.

Students taking the course are asked to pass the word to one another and to watch for follow-up information from the Academic Affairs Office.
