It is reported that, to better put environmental protection into practice, the office of the Youth Volunteers Association of Shantou University (YVA) will launch a “green office” supply application system starting October 10, 2013.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Effective date | October 10, 2013 |
| Eligible applicants | The seven major university-level organizations and college youth volunteer associations (not open to individuals or other teams for the time being) |
| Application process | Send the supply application form to the YVA email two days in advance; duty staff review it and reply within two days; sign for and collect supplies at Room 721, Building 7 on the collection day; return them on time and sign to confirm |
| Collection point | Room 721, Building 7 |
| Supplies available for loan | Display stands, power strips, vests and more |

## Application Process

Applicants should send the YVA Supply Application Form (see Attachment 1) to the YVA email address volunteersofstu@163.com two days in advance, and duty staff will review the application and reply by email within two days. On the collection day, the applicant should go to Room 721, Building 7 at the appointed time to sign for and collect the supplies. The supplies should be returned at the agreed time, with the applicant signing to confirm that they have been returned. The detailed process is set out in Attachment 2, “Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association Supply Application.”

## Rules for Applicants

The YVA accepts supply applications from the seven major university-level organizations and the youth volunteer associations of the colleges. Heads of these organizations and associations must use their campus email accounts to send the application form to the YVA email; applications from individuals and other teams are not accepted for the time being. Organizations that lose or damage supplies will be recorded by the YVA and required to pay compensation at the item’s price or repair the damaged supplies. If no compensation is made, the organization may not apply for supplies for one semester.

## Supplies Available for Loan

Supplies available for loan include display stands, power strips and vests, among others. See Attachment 3 for details.

Anyone with questions can contact the YVA office (contact details are in the original OA notice).
