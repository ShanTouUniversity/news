It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design of Shantou University has completed its college-level review for the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship. In accordance with the requirements of the notice issued by the Shantou University Graduate School on the review of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship, and following assessment by the college's Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee, the college proposes to recognize **Lu YL** and **Ren KN**, two 2011-cohort graduate students, as recipients of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship. The review results are now open for public comment.

## How to Submit Comments

According to the notice, units and individuals who disagree with the review results may submit their comments in writing to the college's Academic Affairs Office before **October 16, 2013**. Materials submitted on behalf of a unit must bear the unit's official seal, while materials submitted by an individual must be signed with the person's real name so that responsibility can be established.

Telephone: `86502419`; Email: `lilq@stu.edu.cn`.
