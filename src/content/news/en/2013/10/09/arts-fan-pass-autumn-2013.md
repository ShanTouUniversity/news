It is reported that the seventh Colorful Arts Season has begun recruiting arts fans for the new season, offering 200 places to faculty and students of all colleges at Shantou University. According to the organizers, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Art Education Center, the Art Education Center's Colorful Arts Season arts fans will join the Creative Arts Ambassadors of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design in introducing an innovative form of admission at the Zhao Muzi piano recital on the evening of October 11.

The organizers said that no tickets are required for the performance: members of the audience only need to follow the Weibo account of the Creative Arts Ambassadors and show the follow page to staff on the night of the performance to enter and enjoy the concert. An introduction to the Creative Arts Ambassadors is available in the notice attachment.

## How to Become an Arts Fan

1. Attend the Zhao Muzi piano recital on the evening of October 11, 2013. Audience members interested in becoming arts fans should queue at the entrance of the Auditorium from 19:00, where the organizers will distribute arts fan information registration forms to the first 200 audience members.
2. After the performance, drop the completed form into the collection box in the lobby. The organizers will collect the forms and enter the information into their records.

## Benefits and Requirements for Arts Fans

1. Membership is limited to 200 arts fans.
2. The programme is open to faculty and students of all colleges at STU.
3. Each place comes with one Arts Fan Pass; the collection time will be announced through the office automation system.
4. Arts fans may collect performance tickets ahead of other audiences, and the organizers will make sure that every arts fan who comes to collect a ticket receives an excellent seat.
5. Arts fans can take part in a lucky draw at the end of the semester for the chance to win generous prizes.
6. Arts fans are expected to take an active part in performances and observe audience etiquette.
