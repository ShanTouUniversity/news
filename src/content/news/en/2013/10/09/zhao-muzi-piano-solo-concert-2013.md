It is reported that the first show of the second Young Piano Artists series, also known as the Performing Arts Rising Star series, will be a piano recital by Zhao Muzi, held at 19:30 on October 11, 2013 in the Auditorium. The series is fully supported by the Foundation. The organizers, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Art Education Center, have specially invited a young piano artist from The Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts to perform at Shantou University, and audiences are advised to arrive and take their seats 10 minutes early.

Admission is simple: audience members only need to follow the Weibo account of the Creative Arts Ambassadors and show the follow page to staff on the night of the performance. In the recital, 14-year-old piano prodigy Zhao Muzi will tell the story of his dreams through a selection of famous Chopin works, including polonaises, impromptus, brilliant variations, preludes and etudes.

## About the Performer

Zhao Muzi, a 14-year-old piano prodigy, studies at Shenzhen Middle School. He began learning the piano at the age of four and was admitted at nine to the Junior Music Programme of The Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts, where he studies under Professor Gabriel Kwok, Head of Keyboard Studies. At the age of 11, he gave his own piano recital, *My Music Dream — Zhao Muzi New Year Piano Recital*. He has won numerous honours in domestic and international competitions since childhood:

## Audience Notes

1. Please arrive and take your seats 10 minutes before the performance begins.
2. Please dress neatly and attend in formal attire.
3. Eating, drinking and smoking are not allowed in the venue.
4. Please do not bring in items that may cause noise, and switch off mobile phones and other sound-making devices after entering.
5. Once the performance begins, please do not walk around the venue or make any noise.
6. After the performance begins, latecomers may only enter at an appropriate moment, and those who leave in the middle of the performance will not be allowed to re-enter.
7. Photography, video recording and audio recording are strictly prohibited.
8. Children under 12 will not be admitted.
