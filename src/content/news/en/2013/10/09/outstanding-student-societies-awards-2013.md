It is reported that, to better guide the development of student societies and to commend outstanding student societies, society presidents and society workers, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University decided to launch its 2012-2013 awards selection in October. The three awards are the “Shantou University Outstanding Student Society”, the “Shantou University Outstanding Student Society President” and the “Shantou University Outstanding Student Society Worker”.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Awards | Shantou University Outstanding Student Society, Outstanding Student Society President and Outstanding Student Society Worker |
| Academic year | 2012-2013 |
| Recommendation deadline | Before October 13, 2013 |
| Materials to submit | The *Shantou University Outstanding Student Society Self-Assessment Form*, the *Shantou University Outstanding Student Society President and Worker Application Form* and related materials (electronic versions) |
| Organizing body | Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University; the Societies Union and its affiliated student societies handle the scoring and recommendation |

## Scoring and Recommendation Process

Under the requirements, the Societies Union and its affiliated student societies must carry out the scoring and recommendation work in accordance with the *Selection Plan for Shantou University Outstanding Student Societies* and the selection regulations for “Shantou University Outstanding Student Society Presidents” and “Shantou University Outstanding Student Society Workers” provided in the attachments.

Societies must send the electronic versions of the *Shantou University Outstanding Student Society Self-Assessment Form* and the *Shantou University Outstanding Student Society President and Worker Application Form*, together with related materials, to the designated email address (see the OA notice) **by October 13**. Societies that have already submitted assessment materials this semester do not need to submit the same materials again.

> ⚠️ The selection criteria, application forms and contact details are available in the OA notice attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
