The Graduate School at Shantou University has released the midterm review results for the 2013 Extracurricular Science and Technology Projects for Undergraduates. Additional funding totaling 6,150 RMB has been allocated across two tiers — 150 RMB and 200 RMB — to support projects that demonstrated strong progress.

The midterm review aimed to ensure that all projects are advancing according to plan and to provide additional resources to high-performing teams. Projects that excel in this review will be prioritized as candidates for the National College Student "Challenge Cup" competition.
