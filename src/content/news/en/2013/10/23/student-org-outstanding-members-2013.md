The University Youth League Committee at Shantou University has published a list of outstanding members from university-level student organizations for the 2012-2013 academic year. The selection was based on self-evaluation, internal assessment, and review by the Youth League Committee.

The public notice period runs until October 25 at 12:00 p.m. Any concerns or objections regarding the list should be reported in writing or by phone to the Youth League Committee during the notice period. Contact: Mr. Jin, Tel: 86502302.

This assessment covers all members of university-level student organizations, aiming to encourage active participation and standardized organizational development.
