The STU Student Union has announced that the annual "Intramural Cup" basketball tournament will take place from October to November 2013. The All-Star Game and three-point contest prelims will kick off on October 26.

The men's tournament is divided into two groups: Group A includes the Colleges of Engineering, Medicine, Arts, Liberal Arts, and Graduate Studies; Group B includes the Schools of Business, Science, Journalism, Law, and Zhi Cheng College. The women's tournament similarly splits into Group A (Business, Medicine, Arts, Engineering) and Group B (Law, Liberal Arts, Journalism, Science, Zhi Cheng College).

Schedule highlights: group stage matches run Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays from October 27 to November 10 (3:00–5:30 p.m.); the semifinals take place November 13–17; the third-place game is on November 20; and the championship final will be held at the University Gymnasium on the evening of November 23.
