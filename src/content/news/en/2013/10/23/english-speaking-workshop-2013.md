The English Language Center (CILL) at Shantou University will host the third session of its "How Do I Improve My English" workshop series on October 30 (Wednesday), from 9:30 to 11:00 a.m. at CILL. The session, titled "Improve Your English Speaking through Self-Practice," will be led by Ms. Rita Zheng and Sohani Gandhioke.

Seats are limited. Interested students are encouraged to register in advance via Eventbrite. Those unsure of their availability are asked not to register; registered participants are expected to attend.
