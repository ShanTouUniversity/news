The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University has announced that Coventry University (UK) will hold a program information session on October 29 (Tuesday), from 2:30 to 4:00 p.m. in Room D-307.

The session will introduce the 2+2/3+1 bachelor's degree and 4+1 master's degree collaborative programs between STU and Coventry University. Ms. Judy Finlay, International Projects Manager at Coventry University, will attend in person to answer questions about curriculum, admission requirements, and study costs.

Students interested in studying abroad through a structured pathway between STU and a UK university are encouraged to attend.
