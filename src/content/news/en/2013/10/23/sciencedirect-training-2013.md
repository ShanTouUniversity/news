It is reported that the Library of Shantou University will hold a training session on the ScienceDirect database on the evening of October 25, 2013.

## Session Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Using ScienceDirect to Access Cutting-Edge Academic Information and Elsevier Journal Submission Tips |
| Speaker | Li Zihui, Trainer, Elsevier |
| Time | 19:30, October 25, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Subject Services Department (New Library, 2F Reference Desk) |
| Organizer | Library |

ScienceDirect, produced by Elsevier, contains over 25% of the world’s scientific, technical and medical articles, with SCI-indexed journals accounting for one-quarter of core journal titles. Covering 24 disciplines with more than 9 million full-text articles and over 10,000 e-books, it is one of the most widely used full-text databases. The session will cover search techniques, tracking of latest and most popular articles, and key points for submitting to international journals.
