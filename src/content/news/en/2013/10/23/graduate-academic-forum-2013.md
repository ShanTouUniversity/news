The Graduate School at Shantou University has launched the 2013 Graduate Academic Forum Project call, open to all graduate students across campus.

Proposals must include no fewer than 10 outstanding academic papers, with at least 3 selected for oral presentation. The forum must attract no fewer than 40 participants and be supervised by at least two faculty advisors from relevant fields. Approved projects will receive funding support based on their submitted budgets, reimbursed on an actual-expense basis.

Applications are due by November 20. Please send materials to o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn. The initiative aims to promote academic exchange among graduate students and foster a vibrant research culture on campus.
