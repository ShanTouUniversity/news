The third Campus Camping and Outdoor Film Exchange Event, co-hosted by the Student Affairs Office and the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association of Shantou University, will be held from 16:00 on October 26 to 07:00 on October 27 on the lawn in front of the Great Auditorium.

Activities include wilderness survival skills demonstrations and exchanges, outdoor experience sharing, outdoor film screenings, and on-campus camping. The initial exchange session has no capacity limit; on-campus camping is limited to 30 participants. Registration closes at 12:00 on October 25 via email to snowleopardstu@163.com (draw if over-subscribed).

Please note: protect the environment, follow arrangements, and do not bring valuable items. For details, follow the Weibo account @汕头大学雪豹户外协会.
