The Business School issued a notice on October 23, 2013, announcing a sharing session about the Singapore Exploration Project on October 26.

Initiated by Dennis Lee, a Singaporean professor, the project set off in July 2013 and lasted eight days. The entire journey was independently planned and executed by a student team, which engaged with local entrepreneurs, government officials, and leaders of social organizations in Singapore.

The sharing session will detail the experiences and takeaways from the exploration project. All students interested in overseas exploration programs are welcome to attend.
