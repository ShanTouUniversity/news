Veritas College at Shantou University has announced the launch of its newly redesigned website at http://veritas.stu.edu.cn. The new site features an upgraded interface and enhanced functionality to provide staff and students with better access to college information and services.

The previous version of the site (http://veritas.stu.edu.cn:8089) will remain accessible for a transitional period. Veritas College expressed gratitude to the university community for their support over the past five years since the college's establishment.
