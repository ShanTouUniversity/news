The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University has announced that the Western Sweden University will hold a program information session on October 30 (Wednesday), from 4:00 to 5:30 p.m. in Room E-201-2.

The session will focus on the 3+1 bachelor's and 3+1+1 dual bachelor's/master's degree collaborative programs between STU and the Western Sweden University. Dr. Anna Jonlund and Ms. Holly Xiao from the university's International Affairs Office will be on hand to provide details about the Swedish higher education system, admission requirements, and cost of living.

Sweden is widely recognized for its high-quality, low-tuition higher education, making it an attractive destination for students seeking to study in Europe.
