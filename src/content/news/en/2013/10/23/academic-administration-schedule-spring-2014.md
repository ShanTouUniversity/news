The Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University has announced the launch of teaching arrangement work for the Spring 2014 semester. The teaching period spans 16 weeks, with each student limited to a maximum of 25 credit hours.

Applications for general education courses, new courses, bilingual teaching, and faculty teaching across departments must be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office by November 15. Course plans must be entered into the credit system by November 22. Public welfare course plans are due by December 6. Textbooks must be ordered centrally through the Academic Affairs Office, and instructors must complete textbook information entry by December 6.

| Item | Deadline |
|------|---------|
| General Ed, New Courses, Bilingual & Cross-Department Applications | Before November 15 |
| Course Plan Entry into Credit System | Before November 22 |
| Public Welfare Course Plan Submission | Before December 6 |
| Textbook Information Entry | Before December 6 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
