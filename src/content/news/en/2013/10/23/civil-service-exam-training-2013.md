The Law School at Shantou University will organize a national civil service exam preparation course from October 31 to November 5 at the Moot Court, open to second- and third-year law students as well as second-year students from other colleges (limited spots, first-come-first-served).

Mr. Wang Renfa will teach the Administrative Aptitude Test over four days, and Mr. Guo Wulin will teach the Essay Writing (申论) component over two days. The training is designed to help students systematically prepare for the national civil service examination and improve their test-taking skills.

Applications close on October 25 at 12:00 p.m. Send registration information to cdclaw@stu.edu.cn.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Course | National Civil Service Exam Preparation |
| Dates | October 31 – November 5 |
| Venue | Moot Court, Law School |
