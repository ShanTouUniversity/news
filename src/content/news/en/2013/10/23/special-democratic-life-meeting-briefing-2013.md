The Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee at Shantou University has announced that the briefing on the special democratic life meeting will be held on October 24 at 14:30 in Conference Room 3, Academic Exchange Center.

University leaders, heads of departments and offices, deans of colleges, and secretaries of party branches of all colleges are expected to attend on time. The attendee list should be submitted to the Secretariat of the Leading Group Office for Educational Practice Activities before close of business on October 23. Contact: 86502345, email o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.
