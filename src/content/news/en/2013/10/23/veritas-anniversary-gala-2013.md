Veritas College at Shantou University will hold its fifth anniversary "College Day" gala on October 27 (Sunday) at 7:30 p.m. at the University Auditorium. The event, themed "in Our Prime" (风华正茂), will feature a variety of performances showcasing the vitality and talent of Veritas College students.

All faculty and students are cordially invited to attend. Seats for college tutors, distinguished guests, and alumni have been reserved in rows 6, 7, and 8. Other attendees may be seated according to on-site guidance. The five-year milestone offers an opportunity to reflect on the college's contributions to general education and holistic student development.
