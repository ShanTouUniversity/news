It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on October 10 the list of senior student recipients of the 2013 alumni grant. A total of 35 financially disadvantaged senior students, including Liu SY, will each receive 2,500 yuan.

## About the Grant

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Funded by | Donations from dedicated alumni |
| Recipients | STU students from financially disadvantaged families (senior students this round) |
| Number of recipients | 35 |
| Amount | 2,500 yuan per person |

According to the announcement, the grant aims to carry forward the alumni spirit advocated by Mr. Li Ka-shing — "self-improvement and mutual support; giving back to the alma mater and contributing to society" — and to help financially struggling students overcome difficulties in study and daily life.

## Selection Process

Candidates were recommended by their colleges (residential colleges) after assessment, then reviewed by the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Affairs Office before the final list of 35 senior students was confirmed.

The university hopes the recipients will keep striving and study diligently, shaping themselves into ambitious, knowledgeable, persevering and capable talents, and repay the care and support of alumni and all sectors of society with outstanding achievements.
