It is reported that, in order to ensure the accuracy of student records and to support teaching management, scholarship and financial aid assessment and more accurate, prompt and efficient services, the Academic Affairs Office and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on October 10 to review the student records of 2013 undergraduates. Students of the 2013 cohort must complete their personal information in the Student Affairs System between now and November 10, 2013.

## Filing Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Who must file | 2013 undergraduates |
| Filing period | From now until November 10, 2013 |
| System | Student Affairs System, http://sao.stu.edu.cn |
| Information to fill in | Basic information, study experience, immediate family members, main social relations, awards and penalties, etc. |
| Key details to check | Name, gender, date of birth and ID number (under the "My Information" menu) |
| Enquiry channels | Academic Affairs Section, Academic Affairs Office: 86502623; Education Management Section, Student Affairs Office: 86503398, lslin@stu.edu.cn |

## Filing Procedure

After logging in to the Student Affairs System at http://sao.stu.edu.cn, students should go to the "Fill in Student Records" page under the "My Affairs" menu, fill in their basic information, study experience, immediate family members, main social relations, awards and penalties as prompted, and submit the form after confirming that it is correct, then print the Shantou University Student Record Card. As printing requires a plug-in, students are advised to use Internet Explorer or the compatibility mode of their browser. The printed card must be signed in black fountain pen, collected by class officers and submitted to the college academic affairs office for archiving by major and class.

## Checking Key Information

Students can check their name, gender, date of birth and ID number in the key record information under the "My Information" menu. If any information is incorrect, they should contact the Academic Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office (Academic Affairs Section, Academic Affairs Office, third floor of the Administration Center; telephone 86502623) about the procedures for making changes. As for political affiliation, the Academic Affairs Office will update it uniformly in the students' fourth year based on data provided by the Youth League Committee, the Organization and United Front Work Department and other units, so students do not need to call the Academic Affairs Office about incorrect political affiliation.

## Other Matters

In accordance with the regulations of the higher education authorities, institutions of higher education must register and manage the records of enrolled students strictly in line with the information contained in the admissions data and the registration procedures, and may not change without approval the basic student information already determined at the time of admission (including name, gender and date of birth). Those whose applications are not approved will keep the information recorded at the time of admission.

Students with questions during the verification or supplementary filing process may call or email the Education Management Section of the Student Affairs Office (86503398, lslin@stu.edu.cn). Counselors of each college (residential college) may log in to the Student Affairs System, open the "Student Information List" under "Information Query" to see how students in their college (residential college) have submitted their records, and urge those who have not filed on time to do so as soon as possible.
