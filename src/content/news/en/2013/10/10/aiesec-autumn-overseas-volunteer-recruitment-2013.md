It is reported that STU AIESEC (AIESEC Shantou University) issued a notice on October 10, opening recruitment for its 2013 autumn overseas volunteer program to all students, with applications closing on October 23, 2013.

## About AIESEC

AIESEC is a global, non-political, independent, non-profit organization run by students and recent graduates of institutions of higher education. It now spans 124 countries and regions across five continents, is the world's largest student organization with more than 80,000 members at over 2,400 universities, and has its global headquarters in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, with national committees in the major regions.

As one of AIESEC's main programs, the Overseas Volunteer Program addresses a wide range of social issues, including cultural exchange, HIV prevention, community and rural development, environmental protection, language education, microfinance and women's rights. While taking part in projects, volunteers can experience local life and culture, discuss project development and operation with local AIESEC members, and expand their social circle while gaining valuable experience.

## Who Can Apply

The recruitment is open to all STU students, with no restriction on major or year.

| Event | Time and Venue | Content |

| Event 1: Global Night | 20:00–22:00, Sunday, October 13, EAC, Building 9 | Introduction to the Overseas Volunteer Program and AIESEC culture |

## How to Apply

Applicants may choose any one of the following methods:

| Method | Details |
| ------ | ------- |
| On-site (Global Night) | Collect an application form at the Global Night event and apply on site |
| On-site (JinSanJiao) | Collect an application form at the AIESEC booth at JinSanJiao, October 16, 11:30–14:00, and apply on site |
| By email | Send a Chinese and English resume, or a completed application form (see attachment), to aiesec.volunteer.stu@gmail.com |

In addition, drop-off points for application forms are available in the dormitory area and at the poster boards of the Second and Fourth canteens; see the original OA notice for details. Students with questions may contact AIESEC staff (contact details are available in the original OA notice).
