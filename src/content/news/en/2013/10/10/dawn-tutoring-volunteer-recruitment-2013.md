It is reported that the Shuguang Tutoring Team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association will carry out the fourth phase of the “Chaoyang Action” from mid-October to early December 2013, and is now recruiting volunteers across the university.

## Background

The initiative is part of Guangdong Province's special program for children of migrant workers. In Shantou, schools with concentrated populations of migrant workers' children are mainly located in the northeast of Longhu District, Chen Cuohe and Jinping District, where teaching conditions, especially faculty resources, are relatively limited. The team has successfully completed three previous phases.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Caring for Young Seedlings, Continuing Dreams — “Learning Through Fun” |
| Period | Mid-October to early December 2013, every Saturday morning |
| Content | Three featured classes — “Innovative Classroom,” “Vitality Competition” and “Handmade DIY” — plus themed quality-development activities, delivered in large-class format to enhance creativity, physical fitness and hands-on ability |
| Quota | 12 volunteers, selected via interview |
| Target | All students |
| Criteria | Passion for public service, responsibility and patience, willingness to work hard, and good communication and teamwork skills |
