It is reported that, in line with the spirit of the Measures for the Administration of Textbook Development and Publication Subsidies of Shantou University (Shanda Fa [2009] No. 148), the Academic Affairs Office will soon organize the application and review process for the 2013 fall textbook development and publication subsidy.

Faculty members who plan to publish textbooks in the second half of 2013 and meet the subsidy requirements may fill out the Application Form for Textbook Development and Publication Subsidy of Shantou University according to the management measures and prepare the relevant materials. The materials, in triplicate, must be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office by November 8, 2013, and the electronic version of the application form should be sent to the designated email address.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Basis | Measures for the Administration of Textbook Development and Publication Subsidies of Shantou University (Shanda Fa [2009] No. 148) |
| Applicants | Faculty members who plan to publish textbooks in the second half of 2013 and meet the subsidy requirements |
| Materials | Application Form for Textbook Development and Publication Subsidy of Shantou University and related materials, in triplicate |
| Campus deadline | Before November 8, 2013 |
| Submission location | Academic Affairs Office (service window on the third floor of the new Administration Building) |
| Contact | Zhou Mengyi, tel: 86502736, email: myzhou@stu.edu.cn |

> ⚠️ The management measures and application form are available in the OA notice attachments.
