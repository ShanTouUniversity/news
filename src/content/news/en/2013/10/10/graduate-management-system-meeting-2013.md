It is reported that the Graduate School will hold a working meeting on the launch of the graduate comprehensive management system from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on October 15, 2013, in Room 213 of the New Administration Center.

Convened by the Graduate School, the meeting requires the attendance of leaders in charge of graduate affairs and graduate program secretaries from all colleges (departments and institutes), who will discuss arrangements for putting the new management system into use.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Working meeting on the launch of the graduate comprehensive management system |
| Time | 3:00–5:00 p.m., Tuesday, October 15, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administration Center |
| Participants | Leaders in charge of graduate affairs, graduate program secretaries and other relevant staff of all colleges (departments and institutes) |
| Convening unit | Graduate School |
