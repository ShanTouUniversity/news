It is reported that on October 10 the Academic Affairs Office set out arrangements for the major transfer process for the 2012 undergraduate cohort, asking departments to report by October 12 the number of students from other majors they will admit and their specific requirements for incoming students.

According to the spirit of the Regulations on the Administration of Undergraduate Student Status of Shantou University (Shanda Fa [2013] No. 67), each department must publish, before the fifth week of every semester, the number of students from other majors it will admit and the specific requirements for incoming students. Departments are therefore required to complete the Report on Admitting 2012 Undergraduate Students Transferring Majors and submit it to the Academic Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office by October 12.

It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office will publish the reported numbers and requirements to students.
