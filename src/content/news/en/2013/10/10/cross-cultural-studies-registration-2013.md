It is reported that registration for the Cross-Cultural Studies program track for the fall 2013 semester is now open. The track, jointly established by the International Studies Center together with the Department of Chinese and the Department of Foreign Languages, is designed to let students explore questions beyond the framework of traditional disciplines and to strengthen their awareness of and sensitivity to multiple cultural perspectives. Students interested in the track may consult the course plan in the Study Guide to learn about its rules and requirements.

Registration this time is mainly for students of the 2012 cohort in the Department of Chinese and the Department of Foreign Languages. Applicants should complete the Cross-Cultural Studies Program Registration Form (see attachment), submit one paper copy to the office of the International Studies Center in Room 202 of the East Wing of the Liberal Arts Building (Wendong 202) by October 14 (next Monday), and send the electronic version to mycis202@gmail.com.

To help students learn about the track, the International Studies Center will hold an information session on selecting the Cross-Cultural Studies track from 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. on October 11 (this Friday) in E202-1. Undergraduate students of the College of Liberal Arts from the 2012 and 2013 cohorts are welcome to attend.
