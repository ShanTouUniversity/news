It is reported that the STU Labor Union plans to hold a faculty and staff basketball tournament starting Wednesday, October 23. Participating teams are asked to complete their pre-tournament preparations and send their registration forms to the Labor Union's email address by October 16, using "Faculty and Staff Basketball Tournament Registration" as the email subject.

Seven teams have been formed for the tournament: a joint Liberal Arts and Business team, the College of Science team, the College of Engineering team, a joint Law / Research / Journalism / Library team, a joint Arts / Social Sciences / Continuing Education / Affiliated Middle School team, the Administrative Offices team and the Resource Management team. Faculty and staff of the Physical Education Department who are not in the sports specialty may join the arts joint team.

The tournament will run from October 23 to November 5, starting at 16:30 on Tuesdays and Fridays on basketball courts 5, 6 and 8.

It is reported that some of the commemorative items and competition supplies for the tournament are sponsored by the Shantou Guotai store of Shengxiang Flooring.
