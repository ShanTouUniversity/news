The Center for International Languages and Linguistics (CILL) at Shantou University is offering the third installment of its "How do I improve my English" workshop series, featuring three themed sessions. Registration is required via Eventbrite for each workshop.

| Topic | Instructors | Date & Time |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| TOEFL iBT Listening Skills and Strategies | Ms. Gena Rhoades & Kristine Adams | Fri, Nov 1, 15:30–17:00 |
| How to Improve English Listening Skills | Ms. Qian Zhou & Anthea Tse | Mon, Nov 4, 17:00–18:30 |
| The Path to Wisdom: Vocabulary | Ms. Shirley Humphrey & Catherine Bai | Tue, Nov 5, 14:30–16:00 |

All workshops will be held at CILL. Seats are limited; registered participants are expected to attend.
