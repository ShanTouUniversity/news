Alumni of the Physics Department at the College of Science, Shantou University, have initiated the establishment of the "STU Physics Department Alumni Scholarship" to encourage outstanding students to apply to the department, motivate current students to study diligently and strive for progress, and help alleviate financial difficulties in their studies and daily lives.

The scholarship rewards full-time undergraduate students in the Physics Department who demonstrate good character and academic performance along with financial need. Twenty recipients are selected each year, including eight first-year students, receiving 2,500 yuan each.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Annual recipients | 20 (including 8 first-year students) |
| Award amount | 2,500 yuan per recipient |
| Application | Complete the application form (double-sided print and sign) |
| Deadline | Before 16:00 on November 4 |
| Submission location | Youth League Committee, College of Science (Room 354, Science Building) |
