It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on October 29, 2013, that three courses of the Art Education Center will be rescheduled due to instructor Zhou Zhihong’s business trip.

## Rescheduling

| Class | Course | Cancelled | Make-up | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 62596 | Modern Percussion Performance | Week 7, Wed (Oct 30) periods 3–4 | Week 8, Sun (Nov 3) periods 6–7 | Percussion Rehearsal Room STU109 |
| 62597 | Western Percussion Performance | Week 7, Thu (Oct 31) periods 6–7 | Week 8, Sun (Nov 3) periods 8–9 | Percussion Rehearsal Room STU109 |
| 62598 | Chinese Percussion Performance | Week 7, Thu (Oct 31) periods 8–9 | Week 8, Sun (Nov 3) periods A–B | Percussion Rehearsal Room STU109 |

Students enrolled are asked to inform each other.
