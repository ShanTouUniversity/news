It is reported that the Juilliard School will hold an information session on the 2014 Chinese Judicial Examination on Wednesday, October 30, from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m. at the Moot Court of Shantou University. The event is open to second- and third-year law students as well as graduate students, aiming to provide professional guidance and preparation information for those interested in taking the 2014 examination.

The session will cover an introduction to the judicial examination and its characteristics, systematic review strategies for the 2014 exam, a review of the 2013 exam trends, and an analysis of predictions for the 2014 examination. The organizers encourage students to plan ahead and focus on key study areas.
