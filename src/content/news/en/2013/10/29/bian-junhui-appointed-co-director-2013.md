Shantou University's Teacher Development and Educational Evaluation Center has announced a personnel adjustment, with Biàn Jūnhuī appointed as co-director, sharing leadership responsibilities with center director Guān Chāorán.

The appointment was approved at the 12th university leadership meeting held on October 15, 2013. The Teacher Development and Educational Evaluation Center is a key institution within the university, responsible for faculty professional development training, teaching assessment, and academic consulting services. Biàn Jūnhuī's appointment will strengthen the center's management capacity and support the advancement of its programs.
