The Research Office at Shantou University has released a revised draft of the *Academic Conduct Regulations of Shantou University* for public comment, aiming to standardize academic behavior, improve academic integrity, and promote the healthy development of scientific research on campus.

The draft was published on October 29, with the comment period closing on November 11. Comments may be sent to the Secretariat of the University Academic Standards Committee via email at o_kyc@stu.edu.cn or by phone at 86502928.
