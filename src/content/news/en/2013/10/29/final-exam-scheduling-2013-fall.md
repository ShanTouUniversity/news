It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on October 29, 2013, asking academic units to submit final exam schedules for the fall 2013 semester.

According to the *Shantou University Course Assessment Management Measures* (STU Document [2007] No. 78) and the 2012–2013 academic calendar, final exams for the semester are uniformly scheduled in weeks 17 and 18. Details are as follows:

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Uniform exam period | Weeks 17 and 18 |
| Applicable courses | All courses scheduled through week 15 or beyond |
| Other courses | Require submission of the *STU Final Exam Report* |
| Exam duration | Generally 120 minutes, adjustable with approval from the unit head for special cases |
| Responsibilities | Exam courses are filled by the instructor’s academic unit; courses from the Department of Social Sciences and other general courses are uniformly arranged by the Academic Affairs Office; others are arranged by academic administrators in the credit system |
| Submission deadline | November 5, 2013 (Tuesday of week 8) |
| Submission | After signing by the unit head, submit to the Academic Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office and email to xhx@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

The form and instructions are available in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The exam report form is available on the OA system.
>
