The Graduate School at Shantou University has collected the employment documents for the 2014 graduating cohort, including *Employment Agreements*, *Graduate Registration Forms*, and *Employment Recommendation Letters*, from the university's Employment Guidance Center. These materials are now being distributed to individual students through class monitors.

Each Employment Agreement is printed with a barcode linked to the student's identification number; class monitors are reminded to verify carefully before distribution. Regarding the *Graduate Registration Form*: comments from the grade director should be filled in by the class monitor, advisor comments by the advisor, department/school opinions by the respective training unit with seal, and university-level opinions by the Graduate School with seal.
