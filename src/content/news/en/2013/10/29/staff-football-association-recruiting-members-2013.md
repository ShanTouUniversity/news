It is reported that Shantou University's Staff Football Association is recruiting new members from all faculty and staff. The association is composed of university faculty and staff who are football enthusiasts. With the support of the university trade union, the team holds regular training sessions every Wednesday afternoon and represents the university in friendly matches against external organizations on an irregular basis, playing a positive role in building cohesion among employees and enriching their extracurricular cultural and sports life.

Faculty and staff who are passionate about football are welcome to join. Interested individuals may fill out a membership registration form and send it to the designated email address. Contact: Luo Yingguang, Tel: 86503271.
