The Law School at Shantou University has announced the enrollment list for its 2014 civil service exam training program. A total of 131 students applied following the release of the registration notice, and the final admission list is available as an attachment.

The training schedule is as follows:

| Course | Instructor | Dates |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Administrative Aptitude Test | Wang Renfa | Oct 31, Nov 1–3 |
| Essay Writing (Shenlun) | Guo Wulin | Nov 4–5 |

Students admitted to the program must bring a recent one-inch photograph (with their college and name written on the back) to their department training coordinator by 10:00 a.m. on October 30. They will then collect their admission passes at the Law School Career Development Center (Room 309, Wenbei Building).

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School)
