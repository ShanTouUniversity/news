The Shantou University 2013 themed Youth League Day activity campaign, "Dialogue with Faith, Build the Soul for Dreams," ran from May to September across campus. A summary and awards ceremony will be held on October 30 at 14:30 in Seminar Room 30A, Library.

During the campaign, various Youth League organizations at all levels carried out diverse and rich activities tailored to their characteristics, achieving notable results. The initiative aimed to educate and guide young league members to arm themselves with the Party's innovative theories, deepen their understanding of the spirit of the 18th CPC National Congress, the Second Plenary Session of the 11th Provincial Party Committee, and the 6th Plenary Session of the 16th Central Committee of the Communist Youth League, and translate the aspiration of realizing the "Chinese Dream" into concrete action.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
