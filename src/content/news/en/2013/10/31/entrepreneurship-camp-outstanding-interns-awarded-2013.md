The Youth Communist League Committee at Shantou University has announced the results of the 2013 Entrepreneurship Practice Program summary and commendation. During the summer vacation this year, the committee launched the STU Entrepreneurship Practice Program in conjunction with the Guangdong Provincial Committee's "展翅计划" (Wings Plan). Throughout the program, students worked diligently across various positions at host enterprises, demonstrating strong professional qualities and practical abilities, and earning widespread recognition from enterprises and the broader community.

Thirteen students, including Zhōng Yōu, were awarded the title of "Outstanding STU Intern" in recognition of their exceptional performance during the program.
