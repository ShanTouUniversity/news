It is reported that the Shantou University Staff Basketball Friendly Match will tip off on Friday, November 1 at 4:30 p.m. The matchups have been announced: the Resources Management team will face the Arts United team, while the Administrative Corps will take on the Liberal Arts & Commerce United team. The match is organized by the University Labor Union to enrich staff recreation and foster camaraderie across departments.

All faculty, staff, and students are welcome to come and cheer on the players.

| Match Details | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Staff Basketball Friendly Match |
| Time | November 1, 2013 (Fri), 16:30 |
| Match 1 | Resources Management vs. Arts United (Court 6) |
| Match 2 | Administrative Corps vs. Liberal Arts & Commerce United (Court 8) |
| Organizer | University Labor Union |
| Admission | Open to all faculty, staff, and students |
