The Human Resources Office at Shantou University has announced the results of the 2012-2013 academic year staff performance assessment. Following evaluations by respective departments and university leadership, 149 faculty and staff members, including Liu Yuzhou, have been designated as outstanding.

Objections may be submitted in writing to the Human Resources Office (Room 315, New Administrative Center) before 17:30 on November 7.

|------|---------|
| Assessment Period | 2012-2013 Academic Year |
| Number of Outstanding Designees | 149 |
| Notice Deadline | November 7, 2013, 17:30 |
| Objection Submission | Human Resources Office, Room 315, New Administrative Center |
| Issuing Body | Human Resources Office |
