The English Language Center (ELC) at Shantou University will host an interview techniques workshop on November 1 from 10:30 AM to 12:00 PM in Room 301, Building E. The workshop, themed "What do you want to know about Interview Techniques?", will be led by instructors Chanchal and Sohani.

The workshop aims to help faculty and students master essential interview skills, with a Q&A session for interactive learning. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
