It is reported that Zhi Cheng College at Shantou University announced the admission list for its sixth Psychological Outreach Camp on October 31, 2013. A total of 21 applications were received, and all applicants have been admitted. The camp will be held on November 2 at 8:30 a.m. in room J-354 of Zhi Cheng College.

The camp is themed "Harmonious Mind, Healthy You and Me," and aims to promote communication and mental well-being among students through interactive activities. All admitted participants are asked to wear sporty casual attire.

This term's camp had a quota of 24 places: 18 for Zhi Cheng College residents and 6 for residents of other dormitory areas. Since the number of applicants did not exceed the quota, all were admitted. The original recruitment notice was published on October 25.
