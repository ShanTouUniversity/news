It is reported that Shantou University's Yangtze River College of Press and Communication has completed the major allocation for the 2012 cohort in accordance with the "2012 Cohort Major Allocation Plan." The final allocation list will be posted on the college bulletin board from October 31 to 12:00 p.m. on November 8.

During the public comment period, the college welcomes feedback from students regarding the allocation results. Students must provide their real names when submitting comments. For any concerns, please contact the college through the following channels:
