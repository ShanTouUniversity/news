The Youth Communist League Committee at Shantou University has announced the list of outstanding members from university student organizations for the 2012-2013 academic year. The assessment covered all members across university-level student organizations and went through three stages: self-evaluation, internal organizational assessment, and final review by the Youth League Committee.

The committee stated that the assessment aims to motivate student organization members to actively participate in student work and serve as role models, thereby advancing the development of campus student organizations. The full list of outstanding members is available in the attached document.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Assessment Period | 2012-2013 Academic Year |
| Participants | Members of university-level student organizations |
| Assessment Process | Self-evaluation → Internal assessment → Committee review |
| Issuing Body | Youth Communist League Committee |
| Announcement Date | October 31, 2013 |
