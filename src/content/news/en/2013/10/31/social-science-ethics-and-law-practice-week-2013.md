It is reported that Shantou University's Department of Social Sciences will organize an "Ethics and Law Practice Week," aiming to enhance the moral cultivation and legal awareness of students enrolled in the "Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Legal Basis" course through practical activities.

The ethics practice week is open to students taking the "Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Legal Basis" course. Tasks will be assigned in the eighth week, followed by a practice session in the ninth week. Students are required to complete three activities: "do one good deed, correct one violation, and copy one wise saying." In the tenth week, students must submit a reflection essay of no less than 800 words, which will be reviewed and recognized by the course instructor.

The law practice week targets students enrolled in the law track of the same course. It also features a practice session in the ninth week, requiring students to identify real-life legal cases, analyze them, and submit written reports in the tenth week.

Both practice activities are integrated with the relevant course content, emphasizing the combination of theory and practice and guiding students to incorporate moral standards and legal awareness into their daily studies and life.
