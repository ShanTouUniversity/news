It is reported that Shantou University held the summary sharing and recognition meeting for its "With Faith in Dialogue, Building Souls for Dreams" themed Communist Youth League Day activities on October 30 in Seminar Room 30A of the University Library. The activities ran from May to September across all Youth League branches on campus, with active participation from organizations at every level and a diverse array of rich and meaningful events that achieved effective educational outcomes.

Following evaluation, the Youth League Committees of the School of Medicine and the College of Science were awarded the "Excellent Organization Award." The awards were distributed as follows:

| Prize | Winning Unit / Project |
|-------|----------------------|
| First Prize | College of Science — "Sing Our Class Song, We're Wild About Science" Class Song Competition |
| Second Prize | School of Medicine — "Youth China Dream" Themed League Day Activity |
| Second Prize | Business School — Nianyangyang Base Visit and Essay Competition |
| Third Prize | Yangtze River College of Art and Design — "Setting Out from the Heart, Achieving Dreams" |
| Third Prize | (Several other winning projects) |

The themed League Day activities were organized under the leadership of the University Youth League Committee and covered all Youth League organizations across campus, aiming to strengthen ideological guidance among young League members through diverse practical activities and inspire students' patriotism and sense of youthful mission.
