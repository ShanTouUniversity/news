The Human Resources Office at Shantou University will host a "Teaching Methods for Higher Education" training program on November 2 and 9, aimed at enhancing the teaching capabilities of all faculty members. Newly hired teachers are required to attend.

The program features a diverse curriculum delivered by several senior professors on campus, covering core topics in teaching methodology, learning motivation, course design, and strategies for stimulating student engagement.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Training Dates | November 2 and November 9 |
| Participants | All faculty (new hires required) |
| Organizer | Human Resources Office |
| Course 1 | Teaching Principles and Methods (Prof. Wang Weilian) |
| Course 2 | Learning Motivation and Teacher Psychology (Prof. Chen Xiaohong) |
| Course 3 | Single Course Design for Higher Education (Prof. Cai Yinghui) |
| Course 4 | Effectively Stimulating Student Initiative in Learning (Prof. Bian Junhui) |

Prof. Wang Weilian is a former vice president and Level II Professor at STU. Prof. Bian Junhui is the Executive Dean of the STU Medical College and Co-Director of the Teacher Development and Educational Assessment Center.
