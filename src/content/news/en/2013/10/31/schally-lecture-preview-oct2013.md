The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University has announced that Andrew Schally, recipient of the 1977 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, will deliver a public lecture titled "Hypothalamic Hormones: From Neuroendocrinology to Treatment of Tumors and Other Diseases" on the morning of November 6 at the STU Auditorium.

Professor Schally has made distinguished contributions to the field of neuroendocrinology. The lecture is open to all faculty and students. Prior registration has already been arranged through the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation; students and faculty who have successfully registered are encouraged to attend.
