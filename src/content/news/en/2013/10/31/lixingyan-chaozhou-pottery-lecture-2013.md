The University Library, in partnership with the Chaozhou Ceramic Culture Promotion Project of the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication, will host an academic lecture by Li Bingyan on November 1 from 14:30 to 16:30 in the Library Auditorium. The lecture is titled "Traditional Craftsmanship of the Fengxi Chaozhou Kiln in Modern Times."

Mr. Li Bingyan serves as director of the Yitaoxuan Ceramic Art and Culture Research Institute in Chaozhou and curator of the Chaozhou Yitaoxuan Chaozhou Kiln Ceramic Museum. He was recognized as one of the Top Ten Distinguished Figures in China's Cultural Heritage Protection for 2013. The lecture will systematically cover Fengxi porcelain production techniques—including shaping, body and glaze formulation, forming, decoration, styling, and firing—as well as the decorative arts of the Fengxi kiln, encompassing ceramic sculpture, pierced porcelain, colored glaze porcelain, and painted porcelain. The session will also explore the broader significance of the modern development of the Fengxi porcelain industry to Chinese ceramic culture.
