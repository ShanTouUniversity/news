The College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University will host an academic conference on philology and Chinese culture on November 2–3, bringing together scholars from home and abroad to exchange research findings and foster academic collaboration.

The conference schedule is as follows: On November 2, the opening ceremony and plenary sessions will run from 8:30 to 11:50 in the Science Lecture Hall, followed by panel discussions from 15:00 to 17:50 in Rooms 1–5 of the Academic Exchange Center. On November 3, plenary sessions will continue from 8:30 to 12:00, concluding with the closing ceremony.
