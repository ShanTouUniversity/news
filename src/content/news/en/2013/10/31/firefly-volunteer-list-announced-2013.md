The "Firefly" Love Action Brigade, under the Youth Volunteer Association of Shantou University, has announced the volunteer list for the 2013 "Care for Leprosy Rehabilitation Village" program. The recruitment drive, which opened on October 16, sought 20 volunteers and received a widespread response from the student body. The recruitment has now concluded successfully.

The "Firefly" Love Action program is a signature volunteer service initiative of the STU Youth Volunteer Association, providing long-term support to residents of leprosy rehabilitation villages through medical aid, living assistance, and companionship services. The new batch of volunteers is listed in the attached document.
