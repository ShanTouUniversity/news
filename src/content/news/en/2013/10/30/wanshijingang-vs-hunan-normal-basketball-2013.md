It is reported that in preparation for the upcoming Guangdong Provincial University Basketball League, the Shantou University "Wanshijingang" team, with generous support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, will host the Hunan Normal University men's basketball team on Saturday, November 2, at 7:30 p.m. at the STU Gymnasium.

Hunan Normal University's men's basketball team is a regular in the CUBA top-eight quarterfinals and a powerhouse in the Southwest Division. Having undergone intensive training and competitive matches in the United States during the summer, the Wanshijingang team has transformed into a squad known for aggressive defense and fast-break offense.

Admission is free. Fans are invited to enter the venue in an orderly manner from 7:00 p.m. A 40-seat cheerleading zone will be available on a first-come, first-served basis, and each spectator will receive two inflatable sticks. The Dance Club will also perform during the halftime break.
