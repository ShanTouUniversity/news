It is reported that the University Youth Communist League Committee and the Student Union of Shantou University, together with New Oriental Education & Technology Group, will hold the "Easy come, easy pass" English Exam Lecture Series from November 1 to 3. The series includes a motivational lecture and preparation guidance for multiple English proficiency tests, including TOEFL, GRE, GMAT, and the College English Test.

The motivational lecture, titled "Believe in the Future," will be delivered by Yang Tao and Xiu Rui on the evening of November 1 from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. at the Science Lecture Hall. On November 2 and 3, the University Library Lecture Hall will host three additional sessions: Yang Tao will present TOEFL preparation advice at 3 p.m., Xiu Rui will cover GRE/GMAT strategies at 4 p.m., and You Wenfeng will deliver a session on IELTS winning strategies from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. On November 3, You Wenfeng will also give a session on achieving high scores in the CET exams from 3 to 5 p.m.
