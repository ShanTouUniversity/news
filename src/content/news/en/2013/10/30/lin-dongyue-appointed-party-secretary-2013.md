It is reported that the Shantou University Party Committee issued a notice on October 30, 2013, announcing appointments to key positions across several colleges and departments.

The adjustments involve three posts: the Student Affairs Director and Party Secretary of the College of Art and Design, the Deputy Director of the Student Affairs Office, and the Interim Party Branch Secretary of the Graduate School.

## Appointments

| Name | Appointed Position |
| --- | --- |
| Lin Dongyue | Student Affairs Director & Party Secretary, College of Art and Design |
| Lin Shihong | Deputy Director, Student Affairs Office |
| Lin Lushui | Interim Party Branch Secretary, Graduate School |

The personnel adjustments aim to strengthen student work across colleges and improve the university's party organization management system.
