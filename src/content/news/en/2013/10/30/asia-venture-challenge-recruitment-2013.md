It is reported that the 2014 ASIA VENTURE CHALLENGE (AVC) recruitment has officially begun. Known as one of Asia's longest-running startup competitions, AVC 2014 boasts a total prize pool of USD 18,000. The top prize of the year is particularly notable: the winning team will earn the right to represent Asia at the Venture Labs investment competition in Austin, Texas, with an opportunity to win up to USD 100,000 in seed funding.

Competition rounds include a 60-second elevator pitch among other challenges, comprehensively evaluating teams' product ideas, business models, and presentation skills. The organizers welcome students from the College of Business Administration and related colleges to form teams and seize this valuable hands-on experience.
