It is reported that the Women's Research Center at Shantou University's College of Liberal Arts, with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, will continue to offer its 2013 Special Research Program on "Chinese Women's Participation in Rural Development" and is now accepting applications from faculty across the university. Interdisciplinary teams are encouraged, and students are welcome to participate.

Each project may receive up to 20,000 RMB in funding, with five projects planned for this year's cycle. The research period spans one year, commencing in January 2014, with final results due by December 2014.
