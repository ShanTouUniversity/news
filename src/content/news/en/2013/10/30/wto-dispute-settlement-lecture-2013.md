It is reported that Shantou University Law School will host two lectures on "Recent Developments in WTO Dispute Settlement" featuring Professor Zhang Yuejiao as the keynote speaker. Professor Zhang is currently a professor at Shantou University Law School and previously served as a judge on the WTO Appellate Body, becoming the first mainland Chinese judge on the body. She has also held prominent positions including Legal Advisor-General to China's WTO accession negotiations and Assistant Legal Counsel at the Asian Development Bank, and is widely respected in the field of international economic law.

The first session, open to MPA students and graduate students in law, will be held on November 3 from 4:00 to 5:00 p.m. in Room 202, Lecture Hall E. The second session, open to undergraduate students, will take place on November 4 from 2:45 to 3:45 p.m. in the University Library Lecture Hall. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
