It is reported that Shantou University issued an official document (STU Fa [2013] No. 125) on October 30, 2013, announcing the establishment of a "Leadership Group for Program Accreditation and Undergraduate Teaching Review," aimed at better preparing for and completing the accreditation of relevant programs as well as the university's undergraduate teaching review.

According to the document, the group is headed by Gu Peihua, with Wen Xiaobo, Lin Danming and Bian Junhui serving as deputy heads. Its members include heads of administrative departments, schools and other units, among them Chen Shaogang, He Wenbiao, Lu Xiaohua, Wu Ping, Zou Zhibo, Hu Zhong, Yu Hong, Liao Xuequan, Lin Bingfeng, Zeng Rui, Zhu Lun, Yang Minghua, Ma Fengqi, Cai Yinghui, Lou Zengjian, Chen Yan, Lyu Yuan, Wang Shouzhi, Fan Dongsheng, Huai Xiaofeng and Chen Jian, as well as Terry Bodenhorn, Michael Palmer and Don Snow. Cai Yinghui also serves as secretary of the group.
