The Party and Government Office at Shantou University announced on October 30 the schedule for the upcoming leadership reception day. This regular channel allows students and faculty to convey concerns and suggestions regarding academics, campus life, and other matters directly to university leadership.

Vice President Li Dan will preside over this session on the afternoon of October 31 (Thursday) at 3:00 p.m. Faculty and students who wish to attend are encouraged to call the预约 line at 86502306 in advance to schedule an appointment.
