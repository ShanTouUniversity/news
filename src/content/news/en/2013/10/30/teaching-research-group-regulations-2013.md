Shantou University officially released the Interim Regulations on Teaching and Research Groups at Shantou University on October 30. Approved at the 13th meeting of the university leadership on October 29, 2013, the regulations aim to promote teaching exchange, improve faculty teaching capabilities, implement advanced undergraduate education principles, and further strengthen the university's teaching quality assurance and enhancement mechanisms.

The publication of the regulations marks an important step in standardizing teaching and research group development and advancing the organization of grassroots teaching units, providing institutional guarantees for the continuous improvement of teaching quality across the university.
