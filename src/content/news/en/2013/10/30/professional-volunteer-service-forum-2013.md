It is reported that the "Act Together, Share the Future — 2013 China University Professional Volunteer Service Forum" will be held from November 22 to 24 at Shenyang Medical College. The forum is jointly hosted by Shantou University, the China Youth University of Political Science, and Shenyang Medical College, with the Medical Poverty Alleviation Office of Shantou University Medical College serving as the organizing body.

The forum agenda includes award ceremonies for outstanding volunteer service projects, keynote speeches by authoritative figures in the public welfare sector, specialized presentations on university volunteer services, sub-forums for public welfare exchange, and an exhibition of university公益 achievements. The organizers hope the forum will promote the exchange and development of professional volunteer services across Chinese universities.
