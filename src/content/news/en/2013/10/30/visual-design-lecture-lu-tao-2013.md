It is reported that the Changjiang College of Art and Design at Shantou University will invite renowned designer Mr. Lu Tao to deliver a special lecture on visual design on Friday, November 1, from 7:30 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. in the lecture hall on the first floor of the college.

The lecture will be divided into two parts. The first part will explore the significance of visual writing and the operational process of editorial design, using the magazine *COVER* as a case study. The second part will examine the trajectory of modernist design through 80 years of visual development at Penguin Books, and discuss the evolving role of the "book" as a reading medium.
