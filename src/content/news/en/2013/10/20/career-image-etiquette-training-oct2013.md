The School of Law will host a career image and social etiquette training on October 23 (Wednesday), from 15:00 to 16:30 in Lecture Hall 2, according to a notice issued on October 20, 2013.

The session will be led by Associate Professor Sa Zhihui of the School of Law, aiming to help university students understand professional image building and social etiquette basics, thereby improving personal qualities and employability.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Topic | Career Image and Social Etiquette Training |
| Time | October 23 (Wednesday), 15:00–16:30 |
| Location | Lecture Hall 2 |
| Speaker | Sa Zhihui (Associate Professor, School of Law) |
| Audience | All students |

The organizers encourage all students to attend.
