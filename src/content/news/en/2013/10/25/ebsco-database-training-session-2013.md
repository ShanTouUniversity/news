The University Library of Shantou University will host an EBSCO database training session on Monday, October 28, from 19:30 to 21:00, in the Subject Services Department (inside the reference desk, 2nd floor, New Library Building). Mr. Xu Yunfei from EBSCO will deliver the presentation, and all students and faculty are welcome to attend.

The session will cover the Academic Search Premier (ASP) multidisciplinary full-text database, Business Source Premier (BSP) business and economics full-text database, and Communication & Mass Media Complete (CMMC) communication and linguistics full-text database. It will also introduce EBSCOhost search functionalities, including well-known journal searches, full-text queries, SmartText Searching, My EBSCOhost personalization, and Journal/Search Alert features. A comparison between Google Scholar and the Library's knowledge discovery platform and an overview of Open Access resources will also be presented. A prize quiz session will be held during the event.
