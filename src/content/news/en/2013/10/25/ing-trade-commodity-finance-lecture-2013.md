The Business School at Shantou University will host a lecture on trade and commodity finance by ING Bank on Tuesday, November 5, from 19:00 to 21:00, in the Library Auditorium.

The lecture, titled "Trade and Commodity Finance – an ING Perspective and its Competitive Advantages & Public Service of Singapore," will feature Catherine Low, Country Manager of ING Bank Singapore and Head of Asian International Trade and Export Financing, and Jaime Teong, Senior Researcher at the Singapore Civil Service College. ING Bank was recently awarded Best Commodity Finance Bank at GTR Asia Leaders in Trade 2013. Catherine Low will share ING's competitive advantages and professional insights in international commodity trade finance.

Undergraduate and postgraduate students of the Business School, as well as all students and faculty, are welcome to attend.
