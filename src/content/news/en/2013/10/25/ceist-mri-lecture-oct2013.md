It is reported that the School of Engineering of Shantou University hosted an academic lecture on Friday, October 25, in Meeting Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building. Professor Phillip Zhe Sun from the Department of Imaging at Massachusetts General Hospital, Harvard Medical School, delivered a presentation titled "CEST MRI: Current Work and Future Directions."

Professor Sun has been engaged in medical imaging research and teaching. The lecture focused on the latest research progress and future development directions of Chemical Exchange Saturation Transfer (CEST) magnetic resonance imaging technology, and shared the latest research findings from his group's work in the CEST MRI field. A question-and-answer session with faculty and students followed.

The lecture was organized by the School of Engineering and open to faculty and students of the Department of Electronic Engineering, with all interested parties welcome to attend.
