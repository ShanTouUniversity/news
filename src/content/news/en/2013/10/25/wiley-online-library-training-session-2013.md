The University Library of Shantou University will host a training session on the Wiley Online Library database on the evening of Tuesday, October 29, at 19:30, in the Subject Services Department (inside the reference desk, 2nd floor, New Library Building). A Wiley account representative will deliver the presentation, and all students and faculty are welcome to attend.

The session will cover an introduction to John Wiley & Sons Inc., an overview of the Wiley Online Library platform and its top-tier SCI journals, hands-on guidance for conducting research literature searches, and practical tips for Chinese authors looking to submit manuscripts to Wiley journals.

Wiley Online Library is a next-generation knowledge platform serving scholars, researchers, scientists, clinicians, business professionals, and information specialists, offering access to a rich collection of academic resources including numerous leading journals.
