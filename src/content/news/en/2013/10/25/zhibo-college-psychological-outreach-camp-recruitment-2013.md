Zhi Cheng College at Shantou University will host its sixth Psychological Outreach Camp on November 2, under the theme "Harmonious Mind, Healthy You and Me," aiming to promote communication and mental well-being among students through interactive activities.

The camp is open to residents of Zhi Cheng College and select other dormitory areas on a voluntary basis. A total of 24 participants will be admitted: 18 from Zhi Cheng College residents and 6 from other dormitory areas. If the number of applicants exceeds the quota, selection will be determined by lottery. The registration deadline is October 30 at 14:00. Interested students must download and complete the registration form, with the filename formatted as "Grade + Major + Name," and email it to the designated address. Applications that do not comply with the requirements will not be accepted.

|------|---------|
| Theme | Harmonious Mind, Healthy You and Me |
| Date | November 2 (Full Day) |
| Eligibility | Zhi Cheng College and other dormitory residents |
| Capacity | 24 participants (18 from Zhi Cheng, 6 from other areas) |
| Registration Deadline | October 30, 14:00 |
| How to Apply | Email completed form to atp@stu.edu.cn |
