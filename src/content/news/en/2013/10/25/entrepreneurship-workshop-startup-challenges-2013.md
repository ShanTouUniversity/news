The Student Entrepreneurship Center and the Student Entrepreneurship Park Management Office, under the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University, will co-host an entrepreneurship workshop on the evening of Monday, October 28, at 19:00 in Conference Room ACC-5. The theme is "Start-up Challenges and Strategies."

Mr. Du Yongzhu, Chairman of Gaoxin International Limited, will be the keynote speaker. Drawing on his own entrepreneurial experience, he will share insights on the challenges encountered by startups and practical strategies for overcoming them. The event is open to all students with an interest in entrepreneurship.
