The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold the second session of the "Campus Culture Forum" on Sunday, November 3, from 15:00 to 17:30, at the Student Activity Hall. The theme for this session is "Brand Activities: A Journey of Growth."

The forum will take the form of a casual tea party, inviting university faculty and staff with rich experience in campus cultural activities to engage in free discussions with registered students about the development and branding of campus activities. The event is open to all students, with a limit of 40 participants; spots will be allocated based on registration order once the quota is filled.

Information booths and a message wall will be available at the Golden Triangle and the Library from October 26 to 30 for interested students.
