It is reported that the Youth League Committee, the Student Affairs Office and the Student Union of Shantou University will hold the “Navigating the Job Sea” mock interview, part of the 13th “My Career Dream” mock interview series, on November 2, 2013.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Navigating the Job Sea — Mock Interview |
| Participants | All students |
| Content | Career interviews, English interviews and civil service interviews |
| Time | 9:00–11:45 and 14:30–17:30, November 2, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Activity Hall |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office, Student Union |

## How to Join

1. **Resume submission**: Submit a resume (indicating preferred employer) via the designated drop-off points or email addresses listed in the OA notice before October 31;
2. **On-site registration**: Bring a resume to the venue on the day of the event.

Outstanding interviewees will have the opportunity to appear on the STU version of the “You’re Hired” interview show and gain access to more internship opportunities. Enterprise profiles, positions, resume templates and schedules are in the OA attachments and are withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ Enterprise details and registration contacts are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office, Student Union)
