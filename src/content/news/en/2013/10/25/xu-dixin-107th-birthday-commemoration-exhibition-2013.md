The University Library at Shantou University has launched a special exhibition to commemorate the 107th anniversary of the birth of Xu Dixing, the institution's founding president, showcasing his life story and academic thought. Over 50 related volumes from the library's collection are available for reading.

Xu Dixing (1906–1988), born Xushengwen in Mianhu Town, Jieyang, Guangdong, was a proletarian revolutionary, a renowned economist, and the founding president of Shantou University. He studied at Sun Yat-sen University, Xiamen University, and the National Labor University in Shanghai before joining the Chinese Communist Party in 1933. During the Anti-Japanese War, he worked at the editorial board of *Xinhua Daily* in Wuhan and Chongqing. After the war, he served as editor-in-chief of *Masses* magazine in Hong Kong and founded *Economic Leader*.

After the founding of the People's Republic of China, Xu Dixing held several key positions, including Deputy Minister of the United Front Work Department of the CPC Central Committee, Director of the State Administration for Industry and Commerce, academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Vice President of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences. He was a delegate to the First and Third National People's Congresses and a member of the Standing Committee of the Fifth and Sixth National People's Congress. In 1981, at the age of 75, Xu Dixing was appointed as the founding president of Shantou University, dedicating himself wholeheartedly to the university's establishment and its first enrollment.
