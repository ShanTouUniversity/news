The Business School at Shantou University will host Dr. Donald Finlay, Dean of the Business School at Coventry University, UK, for a lecture titled "Brands, Quality and Adding Value" on the afternoon of Tuesday, October 29, from 14:30 to 16:00, in ACC Room 3.

Dr. Finlay also serves as a visiting professor at Zhejiang University of Finance and Economics and Guangdong University of Foreign Studies. He earned his PhD in Entrepreneurship Education from the University of Nottingham and brings extensive teaching experience and external funding expertise to his work. He has published multiple articles in journals such as the *International Journal of Technology Management* and authored several professional books. His lecture will explore the strategic significance of brand understanding and value creation.

Undergraduate and postgraduate students of the Business School, as well as all students and faculty, are welcome to attend.
