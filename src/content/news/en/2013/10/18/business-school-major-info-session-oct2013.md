The Business School issued a notice on October 18, 2013, regarding the arrangements for the 2012 cohort major information session.

The session will be held uniformly on October 23 (Wednesday) at 14:30 in Lecture Hall 1. Students from other colleges who are interested in transferring to the Business Administration program (International Business Management track) are invited to attend.

The deadline for submitting cross-major selection application forms has been changed to 15:00 on October 25 (Friday).

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Session time | October 23 (Wednesday), 14:30 |
| Location | Lecture Hall 1 |
| Application deadline | October 25 (Friday), 15:00 |
| Target program | Business Administration (International Business Management) |
| Issuing office | Business School |
