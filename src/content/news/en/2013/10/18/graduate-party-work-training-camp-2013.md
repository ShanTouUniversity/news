It is reported that the Party Branch of the Graduate School will hold the 4th Party affairs training camp on October 19–20, 2013.

The camp aims to improve the political theory and Party work capabilities of graduate Party affairs workers, familiarize them with relevant procedures and promote standardization and institutionalization of student Party work. Secretaries (and vice secretaries), organization members and publicity members of graduate Party branches are requested to attend on time. Details are available in the OA attachment.
