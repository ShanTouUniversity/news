It is reported that as of October 18, some student dormitories had not yet cleared water and electricity bills for September 2013 and earlier. The Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office jointly announced that power will be suspended for those dormitories starting October 21, with supply to be restored once all arrears are paid.

From October 21 to 23, the Network Center will make two deductions per day via the campus card; after October 23, one deduction per day. The schedule is as follows:

| Time | Arrangement |
|------|-------------|
| 10:30 | First deduction; successful payments reconnected 14:30–16:30 the same day |
| 16:30 | Second deduction; successful payments reconnected 17:30–20:00 the same day |
| Payment after 16:30 | Reconnection 14:30–16:30 the next day |
| After Oct. 23 | One deduction per day at 16:30 |

Students with insufficient campus-card balance are advised to top up promptly at self-service transfer machines; those with insufficient bank account balance should deposit sufficient funds during banking hours and complete the transfer. Students who have lost their campus card may pay via the "Utility Management System" by setting up "proxy payment," or deposit cash to the Shantou University account at Bank of China Shantou University Branch (Account: 628857760147) and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for posting. The Finance Office does not accept cash directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office)
