The Finance Office of Shantou University has received notice from China Construction Bank (CCB) that the first-generation resident ID cards previously submitted for faculty salary accounts are gradually expiring.

Faculty members are reminded to bring their second-generation ID card originals along with their bank books (or debit cards) to any CCB branch for identity verification. Proxy verification is permitted. Faculty who have already completed identity verification at any CCB branch using a second-generation ID card need not take further action.
