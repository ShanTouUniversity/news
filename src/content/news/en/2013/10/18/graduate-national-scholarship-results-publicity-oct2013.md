It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on October 18, 2013, publicizing the results of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship selection.

A total of 56 graduate students received the National Scholarship this year, including 52 master's students and 4 doctoral students. The full list is available in the OA attachments and is withheld here for privacy.

The public comment period runs until 17:00 on October 24, 2013. Objections should be submitted in writing to the Graduate School (Room 401, Administration Center) within the comment period.

> ⚠️ The获奖名单 is available on the OA system.
