The College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University will host the second session of the 2nd Global Studies Seminar on October 19 (Saturday), from 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. in Room ACC #3, according to a notice issued by the college. The event is organized by the International Research Center.

Three distinguished speakers will share their perspectives on global studies: Professor David Wank from Sophia University in Japan and Harvard PhD in Sociology will discuss Japan's role in globalization; Guo Changgang, Director of the Global Studies Center at Shanghai University and PhD from Fudan University, will present China's approach to global studies; and Yang Yishan, screenwriter and producer at Baiqiang Channel Co., Ltd., will explore cultural expression of global issues through film and television.

The seminar will be moderated by Kasten Krueger from the International Research Center. The series aims to build an interdisciplinary platform and promote academic collaboration in global studies at STU.
