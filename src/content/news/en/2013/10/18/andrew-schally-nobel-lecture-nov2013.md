It is reported that the International Exchange and Cooperation Office issued a notice on October 18, 2013, announcing that Professor Andrew Schally, recipient of the 1977 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, will deliver a public lecture at Shantou University.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Speaker | Professor Andrew Schally (1977 Nobel Laureate in Physiology or Medicine) |
| Topic | Hypothalamic Hormones: From Neuroendocrinology to Therapy of Cancer and other Diseases |
| Time | November 6 (Wednesday), 10:30 a.m. |
| Location | Auditorium |
| Language | English |

In addition to seats reserved for Faculty of Medicine faculty and students, a limited number of remaining seats are available for reservation. The registration deadline is 12:00 on October 23. Please visit http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm to register.

> Source: STU OA Notice (International Exchange and Cooperation Office)
