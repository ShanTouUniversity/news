The Student Affairs Office announced on October 18, 2013, that Sun Guanghui, Deputy Secretary of the Shantou Municipal Committee, will deliver a special lecture for the 2013 freshman cohort on the evening of October 21.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Speaker | Sun Guanghui (Deputy Secretary, Shantou Municipal Committee) |
| Topic | If I Could Re-Experience University |
| Time | October 21 (Monday), 19:30 |
| Location | Auditorium |
| Audience | All 2013 freshmen |
| Admission begins | 18:50; gates close at 19:25 |

All faculty and students are welcome to attend. Seating arrangements are available in the OA attachments.
