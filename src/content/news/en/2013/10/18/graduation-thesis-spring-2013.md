The Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University has issued a notice outlining the schedule for undergraduate thesis (design) work in the spring semester of the 2013-2014 academic year.

According to the notice, colleges must launch thesis work by the sixth week of the semester. By the seventh week, colleges must review student eligibility — students must have completed at least 85% of their required credits. By the ninth week, a headcount statistic must be submitted. Thesis topic approval must be completed by the thirteenth week, system-based topic selection by the fourteenth week, and thesis assignment letters distributed by the sixteenth week.

The notice emphasized that the undergraduate thesis is a crucial component of teaching and that all colleges should ensure strict adherence to the schedule to maintain thesis quality.

| Item | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Launch thesis work | Before Week 6 |
| Review eligibility | Before Week 7 (85% credits required) |
| Submit headcount | Before Week 9 |
| Topic approval | Before Week 13 |
| System topic selection | Before Week 14 |
| Distribute assignments | Before Week 16 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
