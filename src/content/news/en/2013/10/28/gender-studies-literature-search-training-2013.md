The Women's Research Center of the School of Arts and the Library's Subject Services Department at Shantou University will co-host a literature search workshop on gender and women's studies on October 31 from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. in the Library First Floor Meeting Room. The workshop is open to all STU students.

The session will feature two speakers. Li Shujun, a lecturer at the Women's Research Center of the School of Arts and a Ph.D. graduate from the Department of Taiwanese Literature at National Cheng Kung University, will introduce gender and women's studies resources held in the library collection. Xu Yong, a subject librarian and associate research librarian at the University Library, will present an overview of library resource retrieval and utilization. Topics covered include the library's gender and women's studies collection, electronic book querying and downloading, and literature search techniques related to gender and women's studies.
