The Shantou University Science and Technology Association will hold its second "Starry Sky" astronomy event series throughout November, offering three activities open to all students: a stargazing night, a visit to the Shantou Meteorological Bureau, and an astronomy-meteorology knowledge competition.

On the evening of November 2 from 7:00 to 10:00 p.m., the "Let's Watch the Stars Tonight" stargazing event will be held on the university sports field. Astronomical telescopes will be set up for students to observe celestial phenomena and explore the mysteries of the night sky. The event will also feature prize-filled games, and six lucky participants will be drawn at random to receive gifts after the event concludes.

On the afternoon of November 6 from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m., the association will organize a trip to the Shantou Meteorological Bureau for a tour and exchange session. Only 40 spots are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Registration closes at 10:00 p.m. on November 4.

The astronomy-meteorology knowledge competition will hold its preliminary round on the evening of November 9 from 7:00 to 10:00 p.m. in Classroom E-101, with the finals taking place on the afternoon of November 10 from 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. in the Science Report Hall. Teams of four students may enter. Registration also closes at 10:00 p.m. on November 4.
