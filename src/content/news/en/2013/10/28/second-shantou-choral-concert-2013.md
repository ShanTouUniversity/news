The Second Shantou Choral Concert, co-organized by the Yangtze School of Art and Design and the Art Education Center at Shantou University, will take place on November 3 (Sunday) at 7:30 p.m. at the STU Auditorium.

The concert will bring together 11 of Guangdong's most distinguished choral ensembles, including the STU Chorus, to perform classic Chinese and Western choral works such as "The Great River Eastward," "A Pot of Good Tea, A Pot of Moonlight," and "The Blue Danube." Distinguished conductors Wang Jun and Tao Wenhua have been invited to guide the performance. Wang Jun, formerly chorus conductor of the Central Philharmonic Orchestra, later served as a conducting professor at the University of Wisconsin. Tao Wenhua is a celebrated conductor with the Guangdong Southern Symphony Orchestra. Under their direction, the Guangzhou Elderly Cadets Philharmonic Chorus and the Shenzhen Autumn Whisper Chorus will also take the stage.

Members of the STU community can pick up free tickets at Room 309, Art Education Center, by presenting their campus ID card. Each card is limited to one ticket. Tickets are available on October 30 (Wednesday) from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and 2:30 to 5:00 p.m.
