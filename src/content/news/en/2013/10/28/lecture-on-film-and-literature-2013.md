The University Library at Shantou University will host a lecture by Shi Hengtan, associate researcher at the Institute of World Religions, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, on October 30 from 4:00 to 5:30 p.m. in the Library Lecture Hall. The talk is titled "Suffering and Hope: Films '1942' and 'Les Misérables'."

Shi Hengtan, originally from Changsha, Hunan, holds a Ph.D. in Philosophy from Peking University and was a visiting scholar at the University of California, Berkeley. He is a recipient of the 30th Tang Qing Literary and Art Award in Hong Kong and has served as a lecturer for Superstar Digital Library's "Famous Teachers Forum." He is the author of *Seeing Life Through Film* and *Film and Television and Life*, and has delivered over 300 lectures across China and abroad on his *Analects and Bible* comparative lecture series. His work has been widely covered by media outlets including China Education Daily, China International Radio, the United States *Qiaobao*, and Malaysia's *Asia Times*.

The lecture will examine the narratives of suffering and the underlying themes of hope present in both *1942* and *Les Misérables*.
