The Office of the Three Style Construction at Shantou University released its 242nd bulletin on study style inspection on October 28, recording instances of tardiness at campus teaching buildings during Week 6 of the fall semester, academic year 2013–2014.

According to the bulletin, on October 21 (Monday) in Building E, two students arrived less than five minutes late and one student arrived more than five minutes late. On October 22 (Tuesday), three students arrived less than five minutes late and three students arrived more than five minutes late. The office has circulated the relevant student lists to all colleges, requesting that each college conduct批评教育 (criticism and education) for the affected students.
