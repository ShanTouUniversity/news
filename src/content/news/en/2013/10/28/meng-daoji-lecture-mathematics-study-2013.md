The College of Science at Shantou University will host Professor Meng Daoji from Nankai University's School of Mathematics for an academic talk titled "On Mathematical Learning" on October 30, open to all students and faculty.

Professor Meng is a distinguished expert in Lie groups and Lie algebras and serves as a doctoral supervisor at Nankai University. He graduated from Peking University with a graduate degree in 1966, received the State Council Special Allowance in 1995, and was appointed as an expert reviewer for the National Science and Technology Award in 2007. Professor Meng has published 13 mathematics monographs; his textbook *Advanced Algebra and Analytic Geometry* (Vol. I & II) was recognized as a national quality textbook in 2007, and the course was named a national quality course in 2004. In addition to his duties at Nankai University, he has lectured at Peking University, Nanjing University, University of Science and Technology of China, Sichuan University, Northeast Normal University, and other institutions, as well as at the National Summer School for Graduate Students in Mathematics.
