Shantou University Library has introduced the Springer electronic books and eJournals database to help faculty, staff, and students better understand and utilize this resource platform.

Through the SpringerLink system, users can access nearly 8,000 English eBooks published under the 2012 and 2013 copyright years. The collection spans a wide range of disciplines, including life sciences, medicine, agriculture, chemistry and materials, mathematics and statistics, computer science, physics, economics, business and management, humanities and social sciences, law, education, and philosophy. Notable series include *Lecture Notes in Computer Science*, *Lecture Notes in Mathematics*, *Lecture Notes in Physics*, and *Lecture Notes in Earth Science*.

Additionally, the library highlighted the SpringerProtocols Laboratory Guide database, which serves disciplines such as biochemistry, molecular biology, and biomedicine.
