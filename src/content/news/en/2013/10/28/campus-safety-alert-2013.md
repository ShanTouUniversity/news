The Security Department at Shantou University has issued a campus safety reminder, drawing attention to a recent surge in fraudulent activities targeting university students. Several students at the university have already suffered significant financial losses after being scammed during online shopping transactions.

The department urges all faculty, staff, and students to strengthen their awareness and ability to identify scams. Members of the university community are advised not to readily believe unverified information and to exercise caution whenever financial matters are involved. If any suspicious circumstances arise, individuals should promptly contact the relevant university departments or report to the police.

Campus 24-hour alarm and emergency hotline: 82904110. Tuipu Police Station alarm line: 82540966.
