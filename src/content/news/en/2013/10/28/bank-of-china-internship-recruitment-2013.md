Bank of China Shantou Branch has announced the recruitment of 10 interns from Shantou University to support its talent pipeline initiative. The internship runs from November 6 to December 31, 2013.

The position is open to full-time undergraduate and graduate students currently enrolled at STU. Applicants are expected to abide by laws and regulations, demonstrate a proactive attitude and strong sense of responsibility, have no record of misconduct, and be in good health with quick reflexes and a pleasant demeanor. Selected interns will be assigned to the branch headquarters or its subordinate offices, where they will assist with organizing and maintaining non-confidential materials.

Interns will be required to sign an internship agreement with Bank of China Shantou Branch and comply with all relevant rules, labor discipline, and confidentiality regulations. Working hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. and 2:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m.
