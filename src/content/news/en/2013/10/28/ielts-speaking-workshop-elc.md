It is reported that the English Language Centre (ELC) of Shantou University will hold the second event in its IELTS Preparation Workshop Series on Wednesday, October 30, from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m. at the Centre for Independent Language Learning (CILL), under the theme "Developing Speaking Skills."

The session will cover speaking skills training as well as mock interviews conducted by ELC teachers in the format of the IELTS Speaking test. Seats are limited, and interested students are required to reserve a place through Eventbrite. The organizer also reminded students who are unsure about their availability not to sign up, and those who register must attend.
