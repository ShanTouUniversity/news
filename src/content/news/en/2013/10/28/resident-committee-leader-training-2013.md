The Student Affairs Office at Shantou University will host the 8th Resident Committee General Assembly and Leader Training Program from November 1 to 3, 2013. The program aims to improve the work and planning capabilities of Resident Committee members, strengthen team cohesion, cultivate high-quality student leaders, and advance the goals of self-governance and self-service within student communities.

The event is divided into two phases: Phase 1, held from November 1 to 2 in Teaching Building D203, features intensive training sessions; Phase 2, on November 3, takes place at Sangpu Mountain for outdoor team-building activities. All members of the 8th Resident Committee are eligible to participate.
