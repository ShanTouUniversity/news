Funded by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the Truth Bell — a 22-meter iconic tower on the Shantou University campus — has been completed and will enter trial operation from November 4 to December 8. All currently enrolled undergraduate and graduate students will be given one opportunity to ring the bell in person during their time at the university.

The tower's design blends Eastern and Western elements: the metallic curve draws inspiration from Chinese calligraphy brushstrokes, the stone structure symbolizes the balance between analytical and intuitive thinking, and embedded lighting represents bit-level program design, pointing toward technology and innovation.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Trial period | November 4 – December 8 |
| Daily ringing hours | 7:30–8:00, 12:10–12:50, 18:00–18:30 |
| Reservation window | 13:00 on Oct 28 to 24:00 on Oct 31 |
| Reservation system | http://booking.stu.edu.cn |
| Opening ceremony | 7:30 a.m. on November 4 |

Enrolled undergraduate and graduate students may submit their applications through the online reservation system. The inaugural ringing ceremony for the trial period will be held at 7:30 a.m. on November 4.
