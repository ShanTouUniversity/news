It is reported that the English Language Centre (ELC) of Shantou University will hold the third session of its IELTS Preparation Workshop Series on Wednesday, October 30, from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m. at the Centre for Independent Language Learning (CILL), under the theme "Developing Speaking Skills."

The workshop will cover speaking skills training and a mock interview component, where participants can practice their speaking under the guidance of ELC teachers and experience the IELTS Speaking test format, receiving professional feedback.

| Event Details | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Series | IELTS Preparation Workshop Series, Third Session |
| Theme | Developing Speaking Skills |
| Time | October 30, 2013 (Wed), 4:00–6:00 p.m. |
| Venue | CILL |
| Registration | Online via Eventbrite |
| Availability | Limited seats, first come, first served |

The organizer reminded students who are unsure about their availability not to sign up, and those who register must attend.
