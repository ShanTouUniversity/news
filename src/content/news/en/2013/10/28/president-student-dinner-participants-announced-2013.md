The participant list for the third Shantou University Faculty-Student Dinner has been officially released, with 32 students selected through a screening process to attend this year's event. The dinner is designed to foster dialogue between university leadership and students, strengthen campus community bonds, and promote campus culture.

The event will be held at 18:00 on October 30 in Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant. Applications and selection were managed by the Student Affairs Office. University officials stated that the Faculty-Student Dinner will continue as an important platform for student-leadership interaction and will be held regularly in the future, offering more students the opportunity to engage in face-to-face conversations with university administrators.
