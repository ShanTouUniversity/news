The College of Science at Shantou University held the defense session for its Fall 2013–2014 semester extracurricular activity projects on the afternoon of October 23 at the Science Report Hall.

Following the presentations and evaluations by the review committee, ten projects including the "Life Tree – Understanding Life" series were approved for funding, with a total allocation of 7,500 yuan in Renminbi. The Youth League Committee reminded all approved organizations to organize their activities in accordance with the College of Science Extracurricular Activity Project Application and Management Measures, ensuring the quality and impact of each event.
