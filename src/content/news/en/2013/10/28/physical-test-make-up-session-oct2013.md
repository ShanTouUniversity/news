The Sports Department at Shantou University announced on October 28 that a make-up physical fitness test session will be held on the afternoon of October 30. The regular testing was completed on October 26, and most students had already finished on time. However, some students missed the test due to various reasons. To ensure no impact on individual records and university reputation, the department organized this make-up session.

Test items and venues: seated forward bend at the fitness testing room under the track field stands, 50-meter dash on the track field, standing long jump at the finish line of the track field, male pull-ups beside the small football field outside the track, female one-minute sit-ups under the track field grandstand, and male 1000-meter / female 800-meter runs on the track field.

| Event | Venue |
|-------|-------|
| Seated forward bend | Fitness testing room under track field stands |
| 50-meter dash | Track field |
| Standing long jump | Finish line of track field |
| Male pull-ups | Beside small football field outside track |
| Female 1-min sit-ups | Under track field grandstand |
| Male 1000m / Female 800m | Track field |
