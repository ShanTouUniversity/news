The English Language Center of Shantou University will host the second session of its IELTS Preparation Workshop Series on October 23 (Wednesday), from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. at CILL, according to a notice issued by the center.

The series' first session was held in September under the theme "Getting to Know the Exam." The second session will focus on developing speaking skills for the IELTS Speaking module.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | Developing Speaking Skills |
| Time | October 23 (Wednesday), 4:00-6:00 p.m. |
| Location | CILL |
| Organizer | English Language Center |
| Registration | Eventbrite (seats limited) |

The organizer noted that seats are limited and registration must be completed through the Eventbrite platform. Students who are unsure whether they can attend are asked not to register; those who do register are expected to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Center)
