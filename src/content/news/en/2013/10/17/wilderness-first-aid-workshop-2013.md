It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association of Shantou University will hold wilderness first aid workshops outdoors on October 19, 20 and 26, 2013.

In response to frequent outdoor safety accidents, instructors of the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association who recently passed the Wilderness Medical Associates International WAFA training during the National Day holiday will share knowledge and concepts.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | All faculty and students |
| Content | Based on the Wilderness Medical Associates WAFA curriculum, combining lectures with outdoor scenario simulations to learn the patient assessment system and proper response, assessment and emergency handling for wilderness illnesses and injuries |
| Venue | Outdoors |
| Schedule | Over three days; detailed class arrangements and outline are in the OA attachment |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office, Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |

The second-stage integrated class is only open to those who complete the first stage, with assessment of overall performance and a written test; 30 participants will advance. If oversubscribed, selection will be by lottery.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | 14:00, October 18, 2013 |
| Requirements | Approach with a sincere learning attitude, ensure full participation and leave no trace on the environment |
| How to apply | Download and complete the application form, rename the file as “Grade + Major + Name” and send it to snowleopardstu@163.com |
| Inquiries | Follow Sina Weibo @Snow Leopard Outdoor Association for updates |

The application form and detailed outline are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy.
