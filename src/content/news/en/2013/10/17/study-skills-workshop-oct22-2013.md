The English Language Center of Shantou University will host a workshop titled "How Do I Improve My English?" on October 22 (Tuesday), from 2:00 to 3:30 p.m. at the Campus Integrated Language Laboratory (CILL), according to a notice issued by the center.

The session, part of the Study Skills series, is led by Ms. Amy Chastain and Jiin Yap. It will provide strategies on how to study more effectively, with a focus on reflecting on aspects such as learning environment, personality, and study methods.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | How to Study More Effectively |
| Time | October 22 (Tuesday), 2:00–3:30 p.m. |
| Location | CILL |
| Organizers | Ms. Amy Chastain, Jiin Yap |
| Registration | Eventbrite (https://studyskillsws.eventbrite.com) |

The organizer reminded students that seats are limited and asked those who are unsure about attending not to register; registered participants are expected to attend.
