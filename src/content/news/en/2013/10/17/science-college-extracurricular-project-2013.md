The Youth League Committee of the College of Science at Shantou University has announced the launch of the Fall 2013 semester extracurricular activity project application, aimed at promoting holistic education and fostering students' confidence, independence, social responsibility, and teamwork abilities.

Applications are open to the College Student Union, Volunteer Association, Mingli Newspaper, Radio Club, each department's Youth League branch, class committees, and any student-formed teams within the college. Six thematic categories are available: public welfare, sportsmanship, career development, humanistic literacy, leadership, and innovation.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Eligible Applicants | College Student Union, clubs, department Youth League branches, class committees, and student teams |
| Themes | Public Welfare, Sportsmanship, Career Development, Humanistic Literacy, Leadership, Innovation |
| Application Deadline | October 20, 20:00 |
| Defense Date | Tentatively October 23, 14:30 |
| Contact | Email: 11ywmo@stu.edu.cn |

Applicants must download and complete the "Shantou University College of Science Extracurricular Activity Project Application Form" and send the electronic version to 11ywmo@stu.edu.cn. The committee will conduct preliminary reviews in accordance with the College of Science Extracurricular Activity Project Application and Management Measures.
