Yueqian Education, a subsidiary of Yueqian Shares, will host an embedded system technology experience event on October 19 (Saturday), from 2:30 to 5:00 p.m. at Building E, Room 303, according to a notice issued by the College of Science.

The session will be led by Deng Renming, a senior engineer and experienced Linux development expert with eight years of embedded systems experience. The event is open to students majoring in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, Applied Physics, Computer Science and Technology, Electronic Information Engineering, and Communication Engineering who have a foundational background in C programming or electronics.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | Embedded System Tech Experience: S5Pv210 Platform GAME BOX Case Study |
| Time | October 19 (Saturday), 2:30–5:00 p.m. |
| Location | Building E, Room 303 |
| Speaker | Deng Renming (Senior Engineer, Linux Development Expert) |
| Eligibility | Students from the College of Science and related programs with C language or electronics background |

Topics will include an overview of Yueqian Education, embedded systems fundamentals, product development workflows, hands-on GAME BOX case practice, industry trends, and interactive Q&A.
