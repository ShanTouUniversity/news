The draw for Shantou University's annual staff basketball tournament has been completed, and the group allocations are as follows:

| Group | Teams |
|-------|-------|
| Group A | Arts/Business Combined, Law/Research/News/Library Combined, Resource Management |
| Group B | Arts/Social Sciences/Continuing Ed/High School Combined, Administrative Staff, College of Science, College of Engineering |

Matches will take place on Tuesdays and Fridays from October 22 to November 5, starting at 4:30 p.m., at courts 5, 6, and 8 on campus. Students are advised to use other facilities during these times.

The event is partially sponsored by Shengxiang Flooring Shantou Guotai Store, which will also hold a promotional sales consultation at the basketball court entrance (near the volleyball court) on October 22 and 25 from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. For details, refer to the attached notices.

Full match schedules are available in the attached document "Staff Basketball Tournament Schedule."
