The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education Student Financial Aid Management Center has announced a province-wide essay contest themed "Financial Aid, Building Dreams, Cultivating People," aimed at showcasing the achievements of Guangdong's financial assistance programs for economically disadvantaged students and inspiring gratitude and resilience among recipients.

The contest slogan is "Chinese Dream — Whose Youth Doesn't Struggle?" It is primarily open to students across the province who have received national financial aid, encouraging them to write about their own stories of奋斗 and growth. Essays should be narrative in style, no more than 2,000 words, with content that is truthful, emotionally sincere, and positive in tone.

Prizes include five Special Awards, 15 Youth Inspiration Awards, 10 Distinguished Awards, and 20 Excellence Awards. Outstanding entries will be featured in media publications and compiled into an anthology, with selections forwarded to the national contest.
