The "Firefly" Love Action Brigade, under the Shantou University Volunteer Association, has launched a recruitment drive for long-term volunteers for the 2013–2014 academic year. Volunteers will be dispatched to leprosy rehabilitation villages in three locations across eastern Guangdong: Linghou in Chaozhou, Ganshijing in Puning, and Dongxikeng in Jieyang.

The recruitment quota is as follows:

| Village | Volunteers Needed |
|---------|------------------|
| Chaozhou Linghou | 6 |
| Puning Ganshijing | 8 |
| Jieyang Dongxikeng (one-day) | 15 |
| Jieyang Dongxikeng (two-day) | 6 |

Applications can be submitted in two ways: download the electronic form from the campus intranet and email it, or pick up a paper form at the Second or Fourth Cafeteria and submit it at designated collection points. The application deadline is October 19 at 9:00 p.m.

Interviews will be held on October 18 (7:00–9:30 p.m.) and October 19 (8:30–11:30 a.m., 2:30–5:00 p.m., 7:00–9:30 p.m.). The venue will be notified via SMS, as will the admission results.

For inquiries, please email 13ljzhang@stu.edu.cn.
