The Shantou Municipal Technical Standards Strategic Joint Conference Office has announced the opening of applications for the 2013 Shantou Technical Standards Strategic Special Fund, which supports organizations and individuals engaged in technical standards initiatives.

Eligible application categories include:

- Technical standards projects
- Standardization research projects
- New secretariats or working groups of international, national, or provincial professional technical standards committees
- Forums, annual meetings, or academic seminars hosted under commission from standardization organizations at various levels
- Pilot projects for implementing technical standards strategies

Detailed information is available in the attached notice. Interested parties may contact Luo Yingguang at ygluo@stu.edu.cn or by phone at 86503271 for inquiries.
