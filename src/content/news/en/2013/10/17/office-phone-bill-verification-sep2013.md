The Network and Information Center and the Resource Management Office of Shantou University have jointly launched an online phone management system, enabling all university units to verify their office phone bills (including China Telecom landlines and China Mobile landlines) through a unified web platform.

The September 2013 billing data has been imported from China Telecom Shantou Branch and China Mobile Shantou Branch. Unit coordinators are asked to log in to the system at http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn and select either "China Telecom Landline" or "China Mobile Landline" to review their bills.

For bills with no discrepancies, coordinators should click "Confirm Selected Bills" to finalize verification. If any billing details do not match actual usage, feedback must be submitted by October 24 via email: issues regarding China Telecom landlines should be sent to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn, and issues regarding China Mobile landlines to stunic@stu.edu.cn.

Units that fail to raise objections or complete confirmation within the specified period will be deemed to have approved their bills automatically. After verification is complete, the Finance Office will deduct the corresponding charges from each unit\'s allocated funds on October 29.
