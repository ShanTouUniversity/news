The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education has issued a notice (Yuejiao Han [2013] No. 15) launching the 2013 Outstanding Doctoral and Master's Thesis Selection across the province. Shantou University has been allocated 2 nomination slots for outstanding doctoral theses and 3 for outstanding master's theses.

Eligible theses are those defended between September 1, 2011 and August 31, 2012 at degree-granting institutions in Guangdong. Under special circumstances, theses defended between September 2007 and August 2011 (doctoral) or September 2010 and August 2011 (master's) may also qualify if jointly recommended by three external supervisors.

The allocation per college is as follows:

| College | Doctoral Slots | Master's Slots |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Medical College | 2 | 3 |
| College of Science | 2 | 2 |
| College of Engineering | 1 | 1 |
| Other colleges | — | 1 each |

Colleges must submit nomination lists to the Graduate School by October 21, and supporting materials by October 23. Materials include seven printed copies and corresponding electronic versions.
