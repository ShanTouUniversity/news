It is reported that the fifth session of the "Ai Zhi Workshop" hosted by the Department of Social Sciences of Shantou University will be held at 7:00 p.m. on October 21 (Monday) in Lecture Room 204, Building E. Themed "Academic Research and Paper Writing and Publication", the session will be delivered by Jian Chuanjin, PhD in Philosophy from Peking University, research fellow at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, and Director of the Editorial Department of Philosophical Researches.
