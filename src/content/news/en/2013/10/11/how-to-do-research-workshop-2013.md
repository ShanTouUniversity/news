It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will host a "How to Do Research" academic workshop. Through interaction with an experienced international scholar, the workshop aims to strengthen the ability of the school's young faculty members and graduate students to publish their research in high-level international academic journals.

## Mentor and Participants

The workshop will be led by Professor Hui Tak-Kee of the National University of Singapore and is open to Business School students and faculty.

## Registration and Presentations

Business School graduate students and faculty who are interested in receiving comments from Professor Hui should register by emailing huayelin@stu.edu.cn before October 16, and send their prepared presentation slides to the same address before October 28. The slides will be forwarded to Professor Hui by the school for review, after which the final list of presenters will be determined. Participants selected to present at the workshop will receive in-depth comments and guidance from Professor Hui.

## Presentation Requirements

To improve the effectiveness of the workshop, participants are encouraged to base their presentation on a research project they are currently working on, or a research topic they plan to pursue in the near future, and to address one of the following themes. Each participant may give only one presentation, and each presentation is limited to 10 minutes.

### Theme 1: My Research Design (no more than 10 slides)

- My research title (1 slide)
- The research question (1 slide)
- Reasons for choosing the research question, such as gaps in existing research on the issue (1 slide)
- The relevant theoretical framework (1 slide)
- The research model (1 slide)
- Proposed hypotheses (1 slide)
- Research methods planned (1 slide)
- The expected significance of the research for theory development (1 slide)
- References (1 slide)

### Theme 2: My Research Methods (no more than 10 slides)

- Research title and research question (1 slide)
- Proposed hypotheses (1 slide)
- The research model (1 slide)
- Research methods planned (1 slide)
- Proposed data sources and collection methods (1 slide)
- Proposed data analysis methods (2 slides)
- Comparison of the strengths and weaknesses of my data analysis methods (1 slide)
- Expected analysis results (1 slide)
- References (1 slide)
