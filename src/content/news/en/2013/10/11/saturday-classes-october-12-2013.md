It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a reminder that, in accordance with the Notice on Holiday and Class Adjustment Arrangements for the University in 2013 (Shantou University Party and Administrative Office Document [2013] No. 3), undergraduate students on the main campus will follow the Monday timetable on this Saturday, October 12, 2013, attending the classes scheduled for Monday, October 7, 2013.

All faculty and students are asked to remind one another and arrange their time according to the adjusted timetable so that no class is missed.
