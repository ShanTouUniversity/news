It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts publicized its proposed 2013 STU Outstanding Graduate Scholarship recipients on October 11, 2013. In accordance with the Graduate School’s notice on the 2013 selection of outstanding graduate students, and following assessment by the College of Liberal Arts 2013 STU Outstanding Graduate Scholarship Review Committee, three graduate students have been proposed as recipients of the 2013 Outstanding Graduate Scholarship. The proposal is now open for public comment.

## Proposed Recipients

| No. | Name |
|-----|------|
| 1 | Tang YJ |
| 2 | Li N |
| 3 | Chen X |

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Review basis | STU Graduate School notice on the 2013 selection of outstanding graduate students |
| Review body | College of Liberal Arts 2013 STU Outstanding Graduate Scholarship Review Committee |
| Proposed recipients | 3 graduate students |
| Deadline for comments | October 17, 2013 |
| How to comment | Submit written comments to the college review committee; submissions from organizations must bear an official seal, and those from individuals must be signed with the writer's real name |
| Contact | Graduate Management Office, Liberal Arts South 203; Tel 86503631; Email rmwang@stu.edu.cn |

Anyone who disagrees with the proposed recipients is invited to submit written comments to the College of Liberal Arts 2013 STU Outstanding Graduate Scholarship Review Committee before October 17, 2013.
