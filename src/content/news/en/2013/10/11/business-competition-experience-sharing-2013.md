It is reported that the 2013 Business Competition Experience Sharing Session will be held from 14:30 to 16:30 on October 16 in the Library Lecture Hall. Organized by the International Business Case Club (IBCC) of the Business School, the event will invite teams that competed in case competitions in the United States, Singapore, New Zealand and Thailand, as well as the STU Enactus team and the team that entered the national market research competition, to share their competition experience.

According to the organizers, participants will learn what preparation is needed for high-level business competitions, what the competitions are like, and the experience and insights of teams that have won world-class awards and national championships. The organizers say the opportunity is a rare one and welcome all faculty members and students to attend.
