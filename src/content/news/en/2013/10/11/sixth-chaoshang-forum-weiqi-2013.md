It is reported that the Sixth Chaoshang Forum of the Shantou University Business School will be held from **10:10 to 12:00 on October 18, 2013** in the Library Lecture Hall. Themed "Oriental Management Wisdom as Seen Through Weiqi," the forum will feature Cai Xufeng, vice chairman of the Charoen Pokphand Group and president of the World Chinese Weiqi Federation, as the keynote speaker. All faculty members and students of the university are welcome to attend.

## Background

It is reported that the forum was originally scheduled for June 15, 2013 but was later cancelled. After the school confirmed a new theme and keynote speaker, the event was rescheduled for October 18.

The Business School said that faculty members and students across the university are welcome to attend.
