It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design of Shantou University has released its recommendation list for the 2013 National Scholarship, proposing two 2010-cohort students as recipients of the award for that year and opening the list for public comment.

In accordance with the Guidelines for National Scholarship Work in Regular Higher Education Institutions and the Shantou University Guidelines for National Scholarship Selection, the college's 2013 National Scholarship Selection Committee interviewed and assessed the candidates, and proposed **Wei BL** and **Liao ZY**, two 2010-cohort students, one from the product design programme and one from the public art programme, for the recommendation list.

## How to Raise an Objection

The college says that anyone who disagrees with the list may submit an objection before 12:00 on October 17 in writing, in person or by telephone to the Youth League Committee of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, or by email to `o_ystw@stu.edu.cn`. The committee's telephone number is `86502187`.
