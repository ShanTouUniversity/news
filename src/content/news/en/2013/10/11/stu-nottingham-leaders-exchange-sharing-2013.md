It is reported that the Leader Exchange Program jointly run by Shantou University and the University of Nottingham Ningbo China has entered its third year. The student representatives who traveled to Ningbo this year will hold a sharing session on the evening of October 13 to tell the university community about their exchange experience and what they gained from it.

According to the organizers, STU selected 10 outstanding student representatives for a three-week exchange at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China this year. During the exchange, the students took part in preparing and running the university's orientation activities and observed and experienced the work and life of students there.

## Session Arrangements

The sharing session will invite these 10 students who studied at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China to share what they saw, thought, felt and gained during their stay. An audience interaction segment will also be held at the event, and all faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
