It is reported that Susan Desmond-Hellmann, Chancellor of the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), will give an English lecture titled Becoming a Leader at 7:30 p.m. on Friday, October 18, in the Science Lecture Hall.

The lecture will be delivered in English, and all faculty and students are welcome to attend.
