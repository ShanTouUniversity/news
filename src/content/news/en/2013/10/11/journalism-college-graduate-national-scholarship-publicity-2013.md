It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication of Shantou University has publicized its proposed recipient of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship. One graduate student has been named as the nominee, and the publicity period runs until 5:00 pm on October 17.

The review was carried out in accordance with the notice "On Conducting the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review" issued by the Graduate School of Shantou University. Candidates were first reviewed by the school's 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee and then given final approval by the Graduate School, after which one graduate student was proposed as the recipient of the award. The nominee is Deng X.

## How to Raise Objections

The school stated that anyone who disagrees with the nominee may submit comments in writing to the school's 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee before 5:00 pm on October 17, 2013. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with the sender's real name to take responsibility for the submission.

Submissions are received at Room 301, Academic Affairs Office, Journalism School. The contact telephone number is 86504068 and the email address is mingranli@stu.edu.cn.
