It is reported that the College of Science of Shantou University has publicized its proposed recipients of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship. In accordance with the notice "On Conducting the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review" issued by the Graduate School of Shantou University, and after assessment by the College of Science 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee, 11 master's students and three doctoral students have been proposed as recipients of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship.

## Proposed Recipients

| Category | Number | Proposed recipients |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Master's students | 11 | Wang YL, Liu Y, Huang L, Wang GJ, Wang ZH, Lu X, Liu SS, Zhu S, Yang YL, Ma YY, Sun GD |
| Doctoral students | 3 | Shen YF, Wang QR, Liu HL |

## How to Raise Objections

During the publicity period, anyone who disagrees with the proposed recipients may submit comments in writing to the College of Science 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee before 5:00 pm on October 17, 2013. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with the sender's real name to take responsibility for the submission.

Submissions are received at the College of Science Graduate Academic Affairs Office, Room Li-353. The contact telephone number is 86503011 and the email address is weiwang@stu.edu.cn.
