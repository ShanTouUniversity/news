It is reported that the project approval defense session for student projects under the Student Community X Plan for the 2013-2014 academic year **will be held from 3:00 to 5:00 pm on October 16, 2013** in **Room 726 of Building 789**. The order in which the projects will be defended was released in the attachment to the notice.

## Defense Arrangements

According to the organizers, each project will be defended in turn, and every defense consists of two segments: a presentation of the activity plan, followed by questions from the judges. The activity plan must be presented with slides. Each segment lasts five minutes, so a single project's defense runs for 10 minutes in total.

## Reminders for Participants

The notice sets out three requirements for students taking part in the defense. First, all participants must sign in at 2:45 pm on the day of the defense and load their slides or other materials onto the computer in the venue. Second, during the defense they should listen carefully to the judges' questions and answer them concisely and to the point, and they must not interrupt the judges while they are asking questions. Third, participants may not leave early and must remain until the results are announced.

Project leaders were also asked to prepare thoroughly for the defense and to attend on time.
