It is reported that, with approval from the relevant authorities, Shantou University's Infrastructure Office will build a halal dining room on the first floor of the Fourth Canteen and has commissioned Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. to carry out the work. The scope of the project is the interior demolition and rebuilding of the former staff dining room on the first floor of the Fourth Canteen.

## Project Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Halal dining room construction at Shantou University (first floor of the Fourth Canteen) |
| Scope of work | Interior demolition and rebuilding of the former staff dining room on the first floor of the Fourth Canteen |
| Contractor | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Winning bid price | RMB 79,900 |
| Schedule | Starts October 14, 2013; to be completed by November 12, 2013 |
| Safety reminder | Stay clear of the construction area; do not enter the site without permission |

The university said the construction will cause some noise, vibration and dust, and asked for understanding for the inconvenience caused. For safety reasons, passers-by are asked to take care. Faculty members are also asked not to enter the construction site without permission from the relevant university department and the contractor, and to avoid lingering near the site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Logistics Management Office)
