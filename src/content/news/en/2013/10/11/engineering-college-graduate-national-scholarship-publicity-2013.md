It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University has publicized its proposed recipients of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship. In accordance with the notice "On Conducting the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review" issued by the Graduate School of Shantou University, and following assessment by the college's Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee, **eight graduate students** have been proposed as recipients of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship. The review results are now open for public comment.

## How to Raise Objections

The college stated that any organization or individual with objections should submit comments in writing to the college office before **October 16, 2013**. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with the sender's real name to take responsibility for the submission.

The contact telephone number is `86504586` and the email address is `xiaoling@stu.edu.cn`.
