It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has announced the list of student union cadres rated outstanding for the 2012-2013 academic year. The assessment was carried out in accordance with the Shantou University Regulations on the Management of Student Cadres and took into account the work performance of student union cadres at each college (school) over the past academic year.

According to the notice, the process started with self-assessment by each cadre, followed by evaluation organized by each college (school), and the results were then reviewed by the Youth League Committee before the final list of outstanding members was determined. The list was released as an attachment to the notice; students and staff may view the original notice and its attachment in the OA system.
