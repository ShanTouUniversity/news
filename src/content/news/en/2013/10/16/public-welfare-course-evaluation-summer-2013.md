It is reported that, in order to keep abreast of how public welfare courses are taught and to improve their teaching quality, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University is once again organizing students, through the credit system, to evaluate the teaching of public welfare courses in the summer term of the 2013–2014 academic year.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Courses to be evaluated | Public welfare courses in the summer term of the 2013–2014 academic year |
| Evaluation period | October 16 to October 31, 2013 |
| How to evaluate | Log in to the credit system, open “Course Evaluation,” view the public welfare courses awaiting evaluation, and evaluate the summer-term 2013–2014 courses |
| If the deadline is missed | A written explanation must be submitted; otherwise, the student will not be able to view their public welfare course grades |

The Academic Affairs Office reminds students to complete their public welfare course evaluations within the specified period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
