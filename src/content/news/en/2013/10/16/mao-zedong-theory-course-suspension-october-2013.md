It is reported that, because Shen Haijun of the Social Sciences Department is on leave, two sections of Shantou University's *Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics* course will be suspended. Sections 62643 and 64462 are affected, involving four class meetings in total, and make-up times have yet to be decided.

| Section | Suspended session | Original location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 62643 | Thursday, Week 5 (October 17, 2013), periods 6–7 | E408 |
| 62643 | Tuesday, Week 6 (October 22, 2013), periods A–B | E408 |
| 64462 | Thursday, Week 5 (October 17, 2013), periods A–B | E Lecture Hall 204 |
| 64462 | Tuesday, Week 6 (October 22, 2013), periods 6–7 | E Lecture Hall 204 |
