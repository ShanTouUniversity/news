It is reported that, in accordance with the notice of the STU Graduate School on the review of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship, and following review by the Business School 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee, the Business School has proposed two students as recipients of the 2013 Graduate National Scholarship and publicized the outcome on October 16.

The proposed recipients are as follows (names have been anonymized to protect students' privacy):

| No. | Name |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Hu MZ |
| 2 | Wang YF |

## Comments and objections

Anyone with objections to the names above should submit them in writing to the Business School 2013 Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee before 17:00 on October 22, 2013. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with a real name so that responsibility can be established.

- Location: Business School Graduate Office, Room 210, Second Floor, Wenxi Building
- Telephone: 86503732
- Email: fcmeng@stu.edu.cn

The notice was issued by the Business School of Shantou University and dated October 16, 2013.
