It is reported that, under the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education's notice on the 2013 funding program for overseas study visits by young and mid-career teachers at higher education institutions in Guangdong (Yue Jiao Shi Han [2013] No. 109), the province is running the program from 2012 to 2016, supporting a group of young and mid-career teachers each year to study at well-known overseas universities and research institutions so as to broaden their academic horizons and raise their teaching and research standards. Shantou University's Human Resources Office has forwarded the notice and opened applications, with materials due by October 21.

## Eligibility

- Applicants must be in-service professional and technical staff at ordinary higher education institutions in Guangdong (excluding universities affiliated with central ministries and commissions). They should be dedicated to higher education, law-abiding and of good political integrity and professional ethics, have an outstanding record at work, and have the commitment and sense of responsibility to return after completing their studies and serve their university's development.
- Candidates should be teaching and research backbones or academic (discipline) leaders at their university, be no older than 50, and undertake overseas visits of more than three months.
- Applicants must be staff selected by their university in 2013 for collaborative research or study visits at high-level universities or research institutions overseas or in Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan (based on the actual date of going abroad), in a field the same as or close to their own specialty.
- Those who received this funding in 2012 and are still on an overseas study visit may not apply again.

## Funding standards and requirements

- The funds are allocated by the provincial treasury from the special fund for the "Strong Teacher Project", mainly to help universities expand the scale of sending teachers on overseas study visits; universities may not reduce their own annually planned funding for such visits because of this program.
- Support is generally capped at RMB 10,000 per person per month for overseas visits and RMB 5,000 per person per month for Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan; funding is calculated for 3 to 12 months, with visits shorter than three months not funded and those longer than 12 months counted as 12 months at most.
- Those who have already received full government funding (including national project funding) are no longer eligible. Where government funding was partial, this program's support will be reduced by the amount already received. Extension periods are not counted as funded time, and where a participant returns early, the corresponding portion of funds already allocated to the university will be deducted from the next round of funding.
- The funds are managed as a whole by the sending university and used for its visiting teachers' round-trip travel, overseas study or research costs and living expenses; they must be used for their designated purpose only, and any savings this year may be carried over to next year.
- All funds received by universities must be used entirely to support their teachers' overseas study visits and are included in the performance assessment of fiscal funds; the provincial departments of education and finance will monitor, evaluate and review each university's use of the funds.

## Application materials and submission

Materials include the university's application report, the summary form (Attachment 1) and the applicant's documents (copies of the invitation letter from the host institution, the sending letter from the applicant's unit, the overseas study agreement and the passport pages bearing entry and exit stamps). These must be bound into one set and submitted in triplicate. The full notice content is provided in Attachment 2.

Eligible teachers are encouraged to apply. Relevant units should submit application materials to the Human Resources Office (Room 315, New Administrative Center) before October 21, 2013; applications received after the deadline will not be accepted. The contact is Chen Ying, telephone 86502340, email wing@stu.edu.cn.
