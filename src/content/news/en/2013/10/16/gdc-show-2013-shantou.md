It is reported that GDC Show 2013 in Shantou will be held on October 18 and 19 at the lecture hall on the first floor of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Shantou University. Co-hosted by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Shenzhen Graphic Design Association, the event focuses on graphic design, advertising creativity and design education, and will invite local designers and creative professionals in Shantou to join the sharing and exchange.

GDC Show is one of the most important parts of GDC (Graphic Design in China) 2013. Through this platform, the organizers aim to integrate various resources and carry out project practices in cross-disciplinary cooperation and public participation, making exhibition, exchange, discussion and interaction the core content of GDC 13. According to the organizers, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design is highly representative among Chinese universities and design peers and has cultivated many design talents, which is why the Shenzhen Graphic Design Association has joined hands with the school to present this event, centered on graphic design, advertising creativity and design education, under the name "GDC Show in Shantou".

## The Open Plan Strategy

GDC 13 has adopted an open strategy called the "GDC Show Open Plan" for the event. According to the organizers, the plan holds that exchange activities can happen at any time, with informal gatherings held from time to time; the format of activities changes at any moment and is adjusted according to the actual needs of each event, with professional exchange and public education as the main purpose; it also explores creative cases and discussions beyond design or connected with daily life; everyone is a leader, and all members of the public may contribute, cooperating with various social resources and similar projects in a more open manner and returning the right of speech and the scope of discussion to the public. In terms of venue, GDC Show is no longer confined to art museums but reaches every corner of life.
