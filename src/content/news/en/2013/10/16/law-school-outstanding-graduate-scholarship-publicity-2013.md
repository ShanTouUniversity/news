It is reported that, in accordance with the notice of the STU Graduate School on the 2013 outstanding graduate review and following student applications, recommendations by each program and college review, the Law School has proposed six students as candidates for the 2013 Shantou University Outstanding Graduate Scholarship and has publicized the recommended results.

The nominees are:

| No. | Name |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Chen Y |
| 2 | Jiang YK |
| 3 | Gao WR |
| 4 | Sun JH |
| 5 | Gan QD |
| 6 | Huang XB |

## Comments and objections

Anyone with objections to the nominees above should submit them in writing to the Law School before 17:00 on October 18, 2013. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with a real name so that responsibility can be established.

- Location: Law School Office, Wenbei 203
- Telephone: 86503904
- Email: shuang@stu.edu.cn

The notice was issued by the Law School and dated October 16, 2013.
