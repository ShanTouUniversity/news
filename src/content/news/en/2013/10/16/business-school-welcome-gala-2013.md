It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will hold the China Unicom WeChat Wopai 2013 STU Business School Welcome Gala at the Grand Auditorium at 7:30 pm on Saturday, Oct 19, 2013. Faculty members and students of the school are invited to attend.

According to the organizers, seats in rows 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13 have been reserved for faculty members, guests and alumni. All faculty members and students of the university are also welcome to join.

## Key Facts

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | China Unicom WeChat Wopai 2013 STU Business School Welcome Gala |
| Time | 7:30 pm, Saturday, Oct 19, 2013 |
| Venue | Grand Auditorium, Shantou University |
| Seating | Rows 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13 for faculty, guests and alumni |
| Attendees | Business School faculty and students; all members of the university community are welcome |
