It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University has opened registration for the 2013 second-half China Marketing Association (CMA) qualification exams. The exams will be held nationwide on Nov 23, 2013, covering six subjects in total; the specific subjects and the exam schedule are listed in the attachment.

According to the notice, only candidates taking the China Marketing Manager Assistant Qualification Exam are required to pay a registration fee of 20 yuan, while no fee is charged for the other exams.

The Business School also noted that the written test of the Sheke Award National College Marketing Competition will continue to be merged with the China Marketing Manager Assistant Qualification Exam. Students taking part in the sixth National College Marketing Competition must sit the China Marketing Manager Assistant Qualification Exam, and only those who pass the written test are eligible to compete.

## Registration Steps

1. Register your exam information at kaoshi.cmat.org.cn; online registration must be completed by candidates themselves before Oct 18, 2013.
2. Submit one photocopy of your student ID card and one photocopy of your ID card, along with four recent 2-inch color photos.
3. Candidates taking the China Marketing Manager Assistant Qualification Exam pay a 20-yuan registration fee; no fee is required for the other exams.

Photocopies, color photos and the registration fee should be submitted to the designated contact person by Oct 23, 2013 (contact details are available in the original OA notice).
