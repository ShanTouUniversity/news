It is reported that the Shantou University Library has launched a trial of the Wanfang Data knowledge service platform for all students and faculty, which will run until December 31, 2013. Users can access the platform through the library website.

## Three Resource Collections at a Glance

| Resource | Coverage | Update Frequency |
| -------- | -------- | ---------------- |
| Journal Articles | Over 7,400 Chinese journals published since 1998, including more than 2,900 core journals; over 20 million papers in total | Twice weekly; nearly 2 million papers added annually |
| Dissertations | Over 2.2 million master's and doctoral theses from Chinese universities since 1980, covering more than 90% of degree-granting institutions | About 300,000 added annually |
| Conference Papers | Over 2 million papers from academic conferences held by national-level societies and associations, involving nearly 3,000 major conferences each year | Monthly updates; over 200,000 full texts added annually |

According to the report, most of the journal collection consists of core journals listed in the Ministry of Science and Technology's statistical sources. In the dissertation database, papers from Project 211 universities account for over 70% of the total, making it the broadest-coverage dissertation database currently available. The conference papers are provided by the Institute of Scientific and Technical Information of China.

During the trial period, readers are invited to evaluate the resource and send feedback to the library's subject services department (Tel: 86503247, Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn).
