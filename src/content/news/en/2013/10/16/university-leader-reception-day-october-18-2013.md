It is reported that Shantou University's university leader reception day this week is scheduled for 3 p.m. on Friday, October 18, 2013, when Deputy Party Secretary Luo Nianchao will be on duty to meet visiting faculty members and students.

The Party and Government Office reminded faculty members and students who wish to visit to make a phone appointment in advance on 86502306.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Leader on Duty | Luo Nianchao, Deputy Party Secretary |
| Time | 3 p.m., Friday, October 18, 2013 |
| Appointment | By telephone in advance |
| Appointment Phone | 86502306 |
| Note | Please make an appointment before visiting |
