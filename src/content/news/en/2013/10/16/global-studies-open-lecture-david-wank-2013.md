It is reported that, as part of "Global Studies at STU: The 2nd Symposium — Developing New Global Studies Programs in China: Challenges and Opportunities" (The 2nd Global Studies Symposium at Shantou University), the Center for International Studies of Shantou University's College of Liberal Arts will hold a public lecture on Friday, October 18, 2013.

The lecture will run from 10:10 to 11:40 a.m. at ACC #5 Conference Room in the Academic Exchange Service Center. The speaker will be David Wank of Sophia University, Japan, and the session will be moderated by Wuming Zhao of the Center for International Studies at Shantou University.
