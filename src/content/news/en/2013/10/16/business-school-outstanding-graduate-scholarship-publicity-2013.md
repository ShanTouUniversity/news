## Comments and objections

Anyone with objections to the nominees above should submit them in writing to the Business School before 17:00 on October 18, 2013. Materials submitted on behalf of an organization must bear an official seal, while materials submitted by individuals must be signed with a real name so that responsibility can be established.

- Location: Business School Graduate Office, Room 210, Wenxi Building
- Telephone: 86503732
- Email: fcmeng@stu.edu.cn

The notice was issued by the Business School of Shantou University and dated October 16, 2013.
