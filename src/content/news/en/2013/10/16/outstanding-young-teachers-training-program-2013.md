It is reported that, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education's notice on implementing the Guangdong Outstanding Young Teachers Training Program and the 2013 selection (Yue Jiao Shi Han [2013] No. 108), Shantou University has opened its 2013 selection work, with relevant units required to submit application materials to the Human Resources Office by October 21.

## Program goals

The Outstanding Young Teachers Training Program aims to cultivate reserve talent for academic (discipline) leaders at ordinary higher education institutions in Guangdong. Under the provincial plan, about 500 outstanding young university teachers under the age of 35 will receive focused support from 2013 to 2016, so as to guide young university teachers to set an example through virtue, devote themselves to their posts and to teaching, actively innovate talent development models and explore scientific problems, speed up the training of reserve discipline and academic leaders, raise the overall quality of university teaching staffs, and advance the scientific development of higher education in the province.

## Eligible candidates and program scale

The program targets outstanding young teachers working in-service on the front lines of teaching and research at ordinary higher education institutions across the province. Those who have already been selected for provincial-level or higher talent programs, including provincial and national participants in the "Thousand-Hundred-Ten Talent Training Project," are not covered by this program.

The program runs from 2013 to 2016. About 200 teachers are to be supported in 2013, and about 100 each year from 2014 to 2016.

## Application requirements

- Hold a higher education teaching certificate, a doctoral degree and a senior professional title of associate professor or above; be under 35 years old (as of January 1 of the application year); and be in good health.
- Demonstrate noble teaching ethics and a strong sense of dedication, rigorous working habits and a cooperative spirit.
- Have been selected as a university-level participant in the "Thousand-Hundred-Ten Talent Training Project," or have been included in the university's reserve training plan for academic (discipline) leaders with key university support.
- Possess solid theoretical knowledge of the discipline, have conducted in-depth research in a particular direction within the field, and show strong development potential.
- Have systematically taught one core course or at least two courses in the discipline over the past five years with good teaching results, and have won a university-level or higher teaching award, or been rated excellent in at least two annual teaching quality assessments, or guided students to a third-class prize or above in a provincial-level or higher professional (special) competition.
- Have led, or served as a key member of, university-level or higher quality courses, bilingual teaching demonstration courses, online education quality courses, teaching teams, experimental teaching demonstration centers, and key discipline development projects over the past five years.
- Have led a provincial or ministerial-level or higher research project over the past five years, and published at least three high-level papers in the field as first author (or corresponding author) in core academic journals, or achieved considerable economic or social benefits through technology development, industry-university-research cooperation or other social service work.
- Other conditions being equal, master's and doctoral supervisors will be given priority for recommendation.

## Support measures

The provincial departments of education and finance provide project-based talent funding to selected teachers: RMB 300,000 to 500,000 for natural sciences and RMB 150,000 to 300,000 for humanities and social sciences. The training period is three years, and the funds are allocated in a single payment.

The university will set up a training guidance group to formulate a sound training plan in light of its disciplinary (program) development and the selected teachers' professional backgrounds and to strengthen training. It will also support selected teachers in attending academic activities and forming academic teams, give them priority in arranging or coordinating the use of laboratories, instruments and equipment, and library materials, and regularly seek their opinions and suggestions.

## Materials and submission

Application materials include: three original copies of the application form (see Attachment 2, bearing the university seal, printed double-sided on A4 paper and bound on the left with a cover), which must be completed carefully according to the instructions; one copy of supporting materials (copies of certifying documents such as ID card, academic qualifications, professional title, teaching certificate, papers and projects); and electronic versions of the application form in Word and PDF formats. Written materials and electronic data must be kept consistent. Full details of the notice are provided in Attachment 1.

Eligible young teachers at the university are encouraged to apply, and relevant units should handle the application work carefully. Each unit may recommend no more than three candidates and should deliver the application materials to the Human Resources Office (Room 315, New Administrative Center) before October 21, 2013; late submissions will not be accepted. The contact is Chen Ying, telephone 86502340, email wing@stu.edu.cn.
