It is reported that, to develop and strengthen the leadership abilities of young students and to help them grow in knowledge, skills and experience, Shantou University offers the FLY training program, which serves different groups through three tracks: Fengyi (beginner), Shiyi (intermediate) and Zhanyi (advanced). The program aims to help students grow, while paying attention to society and caring for others, into future leaders who are socially responsible and able to serve society. Since its launch in 2011, the program has run eight training sessions.

On October 23, the university will launch the fifth Shiyi session of the FLY training program. The session is hosted by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and organized by the Shantou University Student Union. Training will be held on October 23, 26, 27 and November 2 and 3 — five days in total — with the detailed arrangements set out in the attached training timetable for the 2013 FLY Shiyi session.

## Training content

According to the organizers, the session includes the “Shiba Ban Wuyi” outdoor practice activity, a special lecture on Integrative Thinking in student activities, a project management lecture, a human resources management workshop, a time management workshop and a “You & Youth” roundtable, among other activities.

## How to apply

Students who wish to take part may download the attached application form for the 2013 FLY Shiyi session, complete it and send it as required to the designated email address (see the original OA notice), naming the file in the format “year + major + name”. Applications close at 12:00 noon on Monday, October 21, 2013.

The notice was issued by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Student Union on October 16.
