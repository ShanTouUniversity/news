It is reported that the groups for the STU faculty and staff basketball tournament have been decided. The joint Liberal Arts and Business team, champion of the 2012 tournament, goes into Group A, while the joint Arts / Social Sciences / Continuing Education / Affiliated Middle School team, the 2012 runner-up, goes into Group B. The remaining teams will be drawn to determine their groups.

Under the arrangement made by the Labor Union, the College of Science team, the College of Engineering team, the joint Law / Research / Journalism / Library team, the Administrative Offices team and the Resource Management team are asked to send their team leaders or representatives to the Labor Union office at 16:00 on October 16 for the draw.

The Labor Union also corrected the starting date: the tournament will begin on the afternoon of next Tuesday, October 22, with matches played on Tuesday and Friday afternoons. The match schedule will be released after the draw.
