It is reported that the 2013 university-level “Collaborative Innovation (Development) Centers / Education Platforms” construction projects have now been announced, after applications from the various disciplines, review by the research committee and final approval at a meeting of university leaders.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number of projects | 14 centers/platforms |
| Funding granted | 1 million yuan in total, from 40,000 to 120,000 yuan per project |
| Nature of the funding | Subsidy funding for building and cultivating the 2013 university-level collaborative innovation (development) centers and education platforms, coordinated by each project leader |
| Permitted spending | Hosting academic conferences, forums and workshops; travel and lecture fees for outside experts visiting the centers/platforms; travel costs for the centers'/platforms' own faculty conducting research visits with partner units; joint training and exchange for postgraduate and undergraduate students |
| Disbursement procedure | Each center/platform draws up an annual work plan and budget, and half of the funding is disbursed once the Research Office has approved them; a mid-term review follows in January 2014, after which projects making smooth progress receive the remaining funding |

## Use and Disbursement of the Funds

The funding serves mainly as a subsidy for building and cultivating the 2013 university-level collaborative innovation (development) centers and education platforms. It is allocated to each center or platform and coordinated by its leader, and is to be used for four purposes:

- Hosting academic conferences, forums and workshops;
- Travel and lecture fees for outside experts visiting the center or platform;
- Travel costs for the center's or platform's own faculty conducting research visits to partner units;
- Joint training and exchange for postgraduate and undergraduate students.

Under the arrangements, each center or platform is to draw up an annual work plan and a budget as soon as possible; once the Research Office has reviewed them, half of the funding is disbursed first. A mid-term review will be held in January 2014, and projects that are making smooth progress will receive the remaining funding.
