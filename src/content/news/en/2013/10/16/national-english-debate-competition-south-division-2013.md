It is reported that the South China division of the 17th FLTRP–Dangdang Cup National English Debate Competition was held at South China Agricultural University from Sept 27 to 29. The Shantou University team finished first among the second-prize winners in the division and qualified to compete in the national finals in Beijing.

The FLTRP Cup National English Debate Competition is co-hosted by the Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press, the National College Foreign Language Teaching Advisory Board of the Ministry of Education, and the National Advisory Board for Teaching English Majors of the Ministry of Education. It is one of the largest and most prestigious spoken English competitions in China and aims to promote debating among Chinese university students.

More than 120 debaters from 56 higher education institutions — including Shantou University, Sun Yat-sen University, Xiamen University, Jinan University, South China University of Technology, South China Agricultural University, and Shenzhen University — took part in the South China division. All of the competitors had come through their own campus selection rounds. The competition also invited well-known trainers and judges from China, the United States, Singapore, Malaysia and other countries, underlining its international and diverse character.

According to the English Language Center, Shantou University sent two debaters, accompanied by the center's Anny Gao (Gao Nan) and Collin Tham, who served as coaches and judges. After two days of intense competition, the STU team performed strongly, finishing ninth overall and tied for seventh in the individual debaters' total score ranking. The team placed first among the second-prize winners in the South China division and will travel to Beijing in late October for the national finals of the 17th FLTRP–Dangdang Cup National English Debate Competition.
