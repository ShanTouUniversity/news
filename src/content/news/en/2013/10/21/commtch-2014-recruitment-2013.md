The Business School at Shantou University has launched recruitment for CommTECH 2014, a cultural exchange program hosted by Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) in Surabaya, Indonesia. The program runs from August 25 to September 2, 2014, lasting approximately nine days.

The program focuses on community development and technology issues and has already attracted participants from 15 countries. STU's Business School has secured several free attendance slots (excluding airfare and visa costs), with estimated personal expenses of approximately 4,000 RMB.

Applications close on October 28. Interested students may contact Ms. Ye Dongni (dnye@stu.edu.cn) or Mr. Huang Wangming (wmwong@stu.edu.cn) for more information.
