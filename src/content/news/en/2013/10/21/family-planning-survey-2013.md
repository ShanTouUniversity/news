The Resource Management Office at Shantou University has announced that a family planning survey and pregnancy check for married female staff will be conducted from October 28 to 30 at the University Hospital.

The check will take place from 2:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily in the ultrasound room on the second floor of the hospital. All married female staff under the age of 49 are required to participate. Male staff whose spouses work at other institutions must fill out an investigation form and return it within one month.

The notice noted that this family planning survey is an initiative to care for staff health, and completion of the survey will be linked to the distribution of family planning bonuses.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Target | All married female staff under 49 |
| Check dates | October 28–30, 2:30–5:00 p.m. daily |
| Location | Ultrasound Room, 2nd Floor, University Hospital |
| Male staff (spouse elsewhere) | Fill out form, return within one month |
| Note | Survey completion linked to bonus distribution |
