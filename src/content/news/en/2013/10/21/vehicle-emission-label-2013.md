The STU Labor Union and Resource Management Office will jointly host a centralized processing session for vehicle environmental inspection label applications to assist university staff.

Only vehicles registered under STU staff members are eligible. Staff who wish to apply must prepare the required documents and register with their respective units by October 23, with units submitting all materials to the Labor Union by October 25.

Required documents include copies of the owner's ID, vehicle registration certificate, and driver's license. For a full list of materials and contact details, see the OA notice attachment.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Service | Vehicle environmental inspection label application |
| Registration deadline | Before October 23 (at respective units) |
| Submission deadline | Before October 25 (at Labor Union) |
| Eligibility | Vehicle owner must be an STU staff member |
| Required documents | ID copy, vehicle registration, driver's license copy |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union, Resource Management Office)
