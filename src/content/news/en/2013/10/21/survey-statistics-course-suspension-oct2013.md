The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced that two sections of the Survey and Statistics course will be suspended due to the leave of Dr. Wang Xueju from the Higher Education Research Institute. Makeup session dates are yet to be determined.

The suspended sessions are as follows:

| Class No. | Suspended Date | Original Location |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| 64486 | Monday, Oct 21 (Week 6), Periods 5–6–7 | Computer Lab E102 |
| 64487 | Tuesday, Oct 22 (Week 6), Periods 8–9–0 | Computer Lab E102 |

Students enrolled in these courses are reminded to stay informed of further updates regarding makeup sessions.
