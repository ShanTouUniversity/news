The Finance Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently released the 2012 electricity fee usage report for all units on the main campus.

Units that exceeded their allocated electricity quota will have the excess charges deducted from their designated funding accounts. Units with surplus allocations may carry the unused quota forward to cover 2013 expenses, or opt to receive half the surplus amount as a supplement to their daily operating funds.

Units that wish to specify a particular funding account for deduction purposes must notify Ms. Liang via email at susaleung@stu.edu.cn or by phone at 86502477 before October 25. In the absence of such notification, the Finance Office will deduct charges from the unit's general or public funds by default, with any surplus carried forward for 2013.
