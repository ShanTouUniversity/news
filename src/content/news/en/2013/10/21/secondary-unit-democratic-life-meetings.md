It is reported that, in accordance with arrangements from higher authorities and the university's campaign to implement the Party's mass line, Shantou University has recently issued a deployment for special democratic life meetings at its secondary units, requiring all Party general branches and directly affiliated branches on the main campus to hold high-quality meetings before November 15.

## Key Arrangements

The preparation and follow-up work is divided into several stages with clear deadlines:

| Stage | Main Content | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Study sessions | In-depth study of General Secretary Xi Jinping's key speeches; deepen understanding of the seriousness and harm of the "four undesirable work styles" | Before Nov 10 |
| Soliciting opinions | Collecting feedback on leadership teams' work styles through discussions, research and comment forms; compiling and reporting opinions as they were received | Before Oct 27 |
| Heart-to-heart talks | General branch secretaries holding one-on-one talks with each team member and mid-level Party cadres to exchange views and resolve misunderstandings | Before Nov 10 |
| Self-examination materials | Leadership team members and mid-level cadres drafting self-examination materials for review by the university supervision group before the meetings | Before Nov 4 |
| Holding the meetings | Carrying out criticism and self-criticism in the spirit of rectification, achieving "unity — criticism — unity" | Before Nov 15 |
| Rectification | Making immediate improvements, maintaining rectification ledgers with public disclosure within an appropriate scope, and building long-term mechanisms against the "four undesirable work styles" | Ongoing |

## Supervision and Guidance

The university supervision group will attend all secondary units' special democratic life meetings throughout the process, reviewing work plans beforehand, evaluating the conduct of criticism and self-criticism during the sessions, and urging each unit to report meeting outcomes and rectification measures afterwards. Units or individuals whose opinion solicitation falls short, whose problem identification lacks depth, or whose rectification measures are vague will be sternly advised and required to make revisions.

In addition, the supervision group of the university's education campaign leadership office has set up a public opinion box for secondary units on the first floor of the Administrative Center, along with an email address, o_jj@stu.edu.cn, for collecting comments and suggestions.
