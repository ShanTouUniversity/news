It is reported that the Youth League Committee and STU Youth announced on October 21, 2013, the results of the third Hello Shantou city exploration tour under the 2013 freshman League Day activities.

Organized by the Youth League Committee and undertaken by STU Youth, the program received 25 team proposals. After review, 10 teams were approved to carry out the city exploration tour.

## Selected Teams

| No. | Team |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Miracle |
| 2 | Rainbow |
| 3 | Simba team (辛巴小分队) |
| 4 | Ai Cha Yi (Love Tea Art) |
| 5 | Jia Yi Bing Ding |
| 6 | Qiang Qiang Food Tour |
| 7 | Taishan People at STU |
| 8 | Communication Detectives |
| 9 | Wo He Wo De Xiao Huo Ban Men Dou Chi Dai Le |
| 10 | Xiao Chan Mao (Little Foodies) |

Selected teams are asked to make preparations, schedule the Shantou city exploration tour, and attend the sharing session on October 30.
