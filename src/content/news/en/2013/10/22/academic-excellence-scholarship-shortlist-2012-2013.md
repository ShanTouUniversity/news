It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on October 22, 2013, publicizing the shortlist for the 2012–2013 Academic Excellence Scholarships.

To encourage diligent study, the university offers Academic Excellence Scholarships at 1,500 yuan per year for first prize, 1,000 yuan for second prize and 500 yuan for third prize. Under the *Measures for Undergraduate Scholarships of Shantou University* (STU Document [2010] No. 125), students ranking in the top 5% of their cohort and major by GPA qualify for first prize, the top 15% for second prize and the top 30% for third prize.

After student applications and university review, the following candidates are now publicized:

| Award | Candidates |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | 169 |
| Second prize | 430 |
| Third prize | 616 |
| Total | 1,231 |

The public comment period runs until 17:00 on October 24, 2013. Objections should be submitted in writing to the Education Management Section, Room 106, Administration Center, with an official seal for organizations and a real-name signature for individuals. Candidates may check results via the Student Affairs System at http://sao.stu.edu.cn using their campus network account.

The full lists are available in the OA attachments and are withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The shortlists are available on the OA system.
