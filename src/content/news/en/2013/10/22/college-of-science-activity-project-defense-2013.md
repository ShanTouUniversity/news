The Youth League Committee of the College of Science at Shantou University has announced the preliminary review results for extracurricular project applications. Out of 16 submitted applications, 14 have passed and are eligible to present at the defense session.

The defense will be held today, October 23, from 14:00 to 17:00 in Room 205, Science Building. Each project has 3 minutes for presentation and 2 minutes for Q&A. Project leaders are asked to arrive 15 minutes early to submit their PPT files.

|------|---------|
| Projects Passed Review | 14 (out of 16 submissions) |

| Presentation Time | 3 minutes per project |
| Q&A Time | 2 minutes per project |
