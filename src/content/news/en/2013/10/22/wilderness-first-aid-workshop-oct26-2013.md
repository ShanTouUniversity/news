The Student Affairs Office issued a notice on October 22, 2013, announcing the second session of the inaugural Wilderness First Aid Workshop, to be held on October 26.

Instructors from the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association will share knowledge and principles of wilderness first aid, aiming to enhance students' emergency response skills and safety awareness in outdoor settings.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Date | October 26 (Saturday) |
| Time | 8:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00 |
| Spots available | 30 |
| Registration deadline | October 25 (Friday), 14:00 |
| Registration email | snowleopardstu@163.com |
| Organizer | Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |

Students interested in attending are encouraged to register before the deadline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
