The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced that the makeup session for the Polar Course, organized by the Marine Biology Research Institute, originally scheduled for October 26 (Saturday), Period 12, in Building E, Room 308, has been cancelled due to a scheduling conflict with an invited expert lecture.

The makeup session has been rescheduled to October 24 (Thursday), Periods AB (7:10–9:10 a.m.), in Building D, Room 503. Students enrolled in the course are reminded to note the change and attend the rescheduled session.
