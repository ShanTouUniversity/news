The Personnel Office issued a notice on October 22, 2013, publicizing the applicant information for the 2013 Guangdong Province Higher Education Outstanding Young Teachers Training Program.

The public comment period runs from October 22 to October 30, 2013. Objections may be submitted in writing or by email to the Personnel Office during this period.
