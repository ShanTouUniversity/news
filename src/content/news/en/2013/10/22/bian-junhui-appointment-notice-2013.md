The Personnel Office of Shantou University has announced a public notice regarding the proposed appointment of Bian Junhui as co-director of the Teacher Development and Education Assessment Center, who will share responsibilities with current director Guan Chaoran.

The public notice period runs until October 28, 2013. Any objections should be submitted in writing to the Personnel Office during the notice period. Contact: 86503390, email rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
