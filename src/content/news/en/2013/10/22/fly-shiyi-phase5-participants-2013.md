It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Union issued a notice on October 22, 2013, announcing participants for the 5th Shiyi (intermediate) phase of the FLY training program.

The FLY program aims to develop leadership among young students through three stages — Fengyi (beginner), Shiyi (intermediate) and Zhanyi (advanced). Following the call released on October 16, 47 students applied, and 35 ministers from various student organizations were selected.

## Participants

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | 47 |
| Selected | 35 |
| Scope | Ministers of student organizations |
| List | 35 participants; full list in OA notice, withheld here to protect privacy |

Selected participants are asked to prepare and attend the training on time.
