The Research Office of Shantou University recently released its latest work bulletin, summarizing key research activities across the university.

Among the highlights, the first batch of 2013 National Natural Science Foundation project approvals was announced, along with updates on humanities and social science project applications and completions.

A notable research achievement featured in the bulletin is a paper published by the STU Health Science Center in the journal Nature.

The bulletin also covered several academic events: the 2013 Summer International Seminar on Humanities and Theology held at STU, the 2013 International Topology Conference in Shantou, a report by Prof. Don H. Madison on theoretical research into electron impact ionization of molecules, participation by faculty from the Taizhou Journalism and Communication College in the AEJMC 2013 Annual Convention, and attendance by Prof. Wang Yi of the Business School at the Academy of Management (AOM) Annual Conference.

Additional highlights include a visit by Prof. Liu Shaoying from the Department of Information Science at Hosei University, a report by Prof. Xu Yuesheng on fixed-point algorithms for emission computed tomography reconstruction, and the establishment of a industry-academia-research strategic partnership between STU and Chao'an County.
