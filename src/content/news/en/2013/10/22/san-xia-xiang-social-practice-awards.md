It is reported that during the summer of 2013, Shantou University organized 11 teams with nearly 1,000 volunteers who traveled to impoverished areas of Guangxi, Fujian and Guangdong for the summer "San Xia Xiang" (culture, science and technology, and health to the countryside) social practice program, themed "Practicing Youth Dreams, Continuing the Dedication to South Guangdong." Guided by a service-learning model, student organizations carried out a wide variety of community services at the grassroots level.

The program received strong recognition from both the Communist Youth League Guangdong Committee and the Central Committee. In the selection of national and provincial key teams, the "Changqingteng" (Ivy) Rural Teaching Service Team of Zhicheng Residential College was named a national key team, while the "Toward the Ocean" summer outreach team from the College of Science, run as a public welfare course, was named a provincial key team.
