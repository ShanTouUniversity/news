The School of Law will host a lecture on October 25 (Friday) featuring Professor Guan Chaoran, director of the Shantou University Teacher Development and Evaluation Center.

The lecture, titled "Concepts and Practice of Case Teaching in Higher Education," will run from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. in Room 213 of the School of Law. The presentation will be in Chinese, and all faculty and students are welcome to attend.
