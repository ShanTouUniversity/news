The Personnel Office and University Labor Union issued a joint notice on October 22, 2013, announcing orientation training for newly hired faculty members.

The training will include both conference-style sessions and team-building activities. Please submit the training participant list to wing@stu.edu.cn by end of day on October 23.

Additionally, university-level "Pedagogy" course training is scheduled for November 2 and November 9. Registration is available at http://training.stu.edu.cn.

|------|---------|
| New faculty training | October 26–27 |
| Training location | Conference Room 3, ACC |
| Participant list deadline | October 23 (end of business day) |
| Pedagogy course training | November 2 and November 9 |
| Pedagogy registration | http://training.stu.edu.cn |
