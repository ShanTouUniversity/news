The Infrastructure Office of Shantou University has commissioned Shantou Yueli Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. to carry out repair work on the stairwell of Faculty Dormitory Building B3. The scope of work includes wall and ceiling painting, installation of aluminum alloy windows, and replacement of lighting fixtures.

The approved contract value is 13,900 yuan. Construction began on October 22 and is expected to be completed by November 21. The university reminded residents and passersby to exercise caution during the construction period, as noise, vibration, and dust are expected.
