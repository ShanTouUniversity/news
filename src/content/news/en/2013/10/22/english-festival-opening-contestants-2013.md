It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University issued a call for contestants on October 22. The 2013 English Festival Opening Night will be held at 19:00 on Friday, December 13, and is seeking undergraduate students from the main campus for an original English short film contest and a talent competition.

## Original Short Film Contest

Participants should create a three- to six-minute English short film based on this year's English Festival theme, “My Life in Our World.” Submitted films will be reviewed; selected films will be screened during Opening Night and considered for awards. Interested students may email `englishfestival@stu.edu.cn` for further details.

## Talent Competition

Auditions for the Opening Night talent competition will be held at 15:00 on November 9 in the EAC. Registration closes on November 4. The competition accepts all types of performances, including dance, music, juggling and magic. Selected contestants will compete during Opening Night for the relevant awards. Registration is available by email at `englishfestival@stu.edu.cn`.
