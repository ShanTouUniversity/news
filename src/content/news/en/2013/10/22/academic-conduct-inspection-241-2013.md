It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued Academic Conduct Inspection Report No.241 on October 22, 2013, covering inspections in week 5 of the 2013–2014 fall semester.

## Inspection Results

| Time | Venue | Late arrivals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:00, October 14, 2013 | Building E | 2 students late within 5 minutes, 5 students late over 5 minutes |
| 14:00, October 15, 2013 | Building E | 2 students late within 5 minutes, 5 students late over 5 minutes |

Students who failed to observe university discipline were notified, and colleges (residential colleges) were asked to provide guidance and continue strengthening academic conduct. The full list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The student list is available on the OA system.
