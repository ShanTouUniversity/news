The Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on October 22, 2013, launching the 2013 themed League day activity project applications under the theme "Strengthen Study Atmosphere, Achieve Better Growth."

Each college's Youth League committee may recommend up to two projects for the university-level defense, which is tentatively scheduled for mid-November. Approved projects will receive funding at three levels: 2 A-grade, 3 B-grade, and 5 C-grade projects.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | Strengthen Study Atmosphere, Achieve Better Growth |
| Application period | October 22–28, 2013 |
| Defense date | Mid-November (tentative) |
| Projects per college | Maximum 2 |
| Funding levels | A: 2 projects, B: 3 projects, C: 5 projects |
| Issuing office | Youth League Committee of Shantou University |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
