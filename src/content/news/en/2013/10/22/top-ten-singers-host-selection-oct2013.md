The Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced on October 22, 2013, that host auditions for the 21st Campus Top Ten Singers Competition are now open.

Only 2013 incoming freshmen are eligible to apply. The application deadline is 17:00 on October 24. Interviews will be held on October 26, with final selections on October 30.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Eligibility | 2013 incoming freshmen |
| Registration deadline | October 24 (Thursday), 17:00 |
| Registration email | 13htzhong@stu.edu.cn |
| Interview time | October 26 (Saturday), 9:00–11:30 and 14:00–17:00 |
| Selection time | October 30 (Wednesday), 14:00–17:00 |
| Interview location | Room 726, Building 7 |

Interested students are encouraged to apply promptly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
