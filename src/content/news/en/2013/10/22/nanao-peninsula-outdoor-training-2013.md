It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association of Shantou University will hold an introductory outdoor training on the eastern peninsula of Nan’ao Island from November 2 to 3, 2013. The program is open to all faculty and students and aims to foster interest in the outdoors, teamwork, communication and environmental awareness.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | November 2–3, 2013 (two days and one night) |
| Location | Eastern peninsula, Nan’ao Island |
| Activities | Hiking traverse, field camping, team development and wilderness survival training |
| Places | 25 (lottery if oversubscribed) |
| Participants | All faculty and students |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office, Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | 20:00, October 31, 2013 |
| How to apply | Download and complete the application form, rename the file as “Grade + Major + Name” and send it to the designated email address |
| Costs | Transport 30 yuan per person and field meals around 20 yuan per person, borne by participants |
| Support | Organizers will purchase personal accident insurance and provide camping equipment |

Applicants should be confident in their physical fitness, enthusiastic about the outdoors, willing to learn and endure hardship, cooperative, able to follow arrangements and stay with the group, free on November 2–3, and optimistic and open to exploration.

The application form, email address and full requirements are available in the OA attachment. Updates are also posted on the association’s Sina Weibo account.
