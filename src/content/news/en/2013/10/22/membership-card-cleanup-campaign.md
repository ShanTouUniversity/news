It is reported that the Discipline Inspection Office of Shantou University has launched a special campaign to clear membership cards among university officials, in accordance with a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Education Work Committee (Yue Jiao Gong Wei Ban Han [2013] No. 24).

## Scope and Targets

The campaign covers Party members in leadership positions at division level or above at the university, as well as officials newly transferred into the discipline inspection system after the nationwide membership card cleanup concluded in June this year.

The targets are cards received by these individuals from any unit or individual other than immediate family members. Membership cards under the notice include vouchers issued by businesses such as entertainment venues, gyms, beauty salons, travel agencies and restaurants, as well as shopping malls, clubs, hotels and other establishments — any card carrying value, credit or consumption counts used for member identification and entitling holders to purchases, free services or discounts.

## How to Comply

Cardholders may either return the cards to the giver or make a personal declaration that they will no longer use them. All individuals in scope must self-examine, clear and report their situation using the official report form; even those holding no such cards are required to file a report.

Each unit will collect the individual reports and submit them together to the Discipline Inspection Office (Room 422, New Administrative Center) no later than November 4.
