It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has adjusted part of the schedule for the course History of Western Philosophy because instructor Bai Youjin of the Social Sciences Department is away on a business trip.

## Rescheduling Details

The two sessions originally set for week six — Wednesday, October 23 and Thursday, October 24 during periods AB — have been moved to the same time slots in week nine: November 13 (Wednesday) and November 14 (Thursday). The make-up classes will take place in the original classroom, Room 401 of Building G.

| Session | Original Time | New Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First class | Week 6, Wednesday (Oct 23), periods AB | Week 9, Wednesday (Nov 13), periods AB |
| Second class | Week 6, Thursday (Oct 24), periods AB | Week 9, Thursday (Nov 14), periods AB |

Students enrolled in the course are advised to take note of the new schedule so as not to miss the make-up sessions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
