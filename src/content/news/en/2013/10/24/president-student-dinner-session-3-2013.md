The Student Affairs Office at Shantou University has announced the third session of the President–Student Dinner, scheduled for October 30 at 18:00 in Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant.

Four to five university leaders and department heads will join 32 student representatives for a free-flowing exchange on campus issues of concern to students. Interested students should register online at http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm by October 27. Students may also submit questions gathered from their peers in advance for discussion. Meal expenses are covered by the university.
