The Institute of Higher Education and the Center for Christian Studies at the College of Liberal Arts, Shantou University, will co-host a lecture by Xu Youyu, researcher at the Institute of Philosophy, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, on the topic of "China\'s Social Transformation and Contemporary Intellectual Landscape."

Born in 1947 in Chengdu, Sichuan, Xu Youyu has long researched linguistic philosophy, contemporary Western political philosophy, and contemporary Chinese social thought. He has served as a visiting scholar at prestigious universities across multiple countries.

The lecture will be held on Tuesday, October 29, at 19:30 in the Library Auditorium. All students and faculty are welcome to attend.
