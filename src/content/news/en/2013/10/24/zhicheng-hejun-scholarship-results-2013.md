Zhicheng College at Shantou University has announced the results of the 2013 Hejun Business Internship Scholarship selection. Following the summer internship phase, the review committee has determined the recipients for this year.

Five students have been awarded the Hejun Business Internship Scholarship: Zhang Shengfu (Mathematics, Grade 2011), Hu Jingwen (International Trade, Grade 2010), Rong Zihao (Accounting, Grade 2011), Peng Junwen (English, Grade 2010), and Bao Mengting (Mathematics, Grade 2011).

| Award | Recipients |
|------|---------|
| Hejun Business Internship Scholarship | Zhang Shengfu, Hu Jingwen, Rong Zihao, Peng Junwen, Bao Mengting |
