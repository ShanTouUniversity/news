Zhicheng College at Shantou University will hold its fifth anniversary "College Day" alumni sharing event on October 27, from 10:00 to 12:00, in lecture room J-354 of the college. The theme is "Zhicheng and Me."

Five alumni have been invited to return to campus and share their personal growth and academic journeys. The guest alumni include: Wu Haihong (Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Class of 2009), Li Qiuting (Journalism, Class of 2010), Shi Yangen (Broadcasting and Television, Class of 2010), Xie Dawei (Chinese Language and Literature, Class of 2010), and Tan Xuan (Art, Class of 2013).

Students interested in attending should register on the sign-up sheet at the college lobby before 22:00 on October 26.
