The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced that the Clarinet Performance course, taught by Mr. Liu Guanghui of the Arts Education Center, has been temporarily cancelled on October 28 (Monday), periods 8–9 (7:10–9:10 a.m.) and AB, due to his participation in an external competition.

Makeup sessions have been scheduled for November 3 (Sunday): Period 12 for class 64351 and Period 34 for class 62600, both held in Music Classroom (3), STU203. Students enrolled in the course are reminded to attend the makeup classes as scheduled.
