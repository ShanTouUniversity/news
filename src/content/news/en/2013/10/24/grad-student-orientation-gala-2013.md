The Graduate School at Shantou University will host the "Starlight闪耀硕 博芸盛宴" 2013 Graduate Student Orientation Gala on October 25 at 19:00 in the Science Report Hall.

The gala features over ten performances including song and dance, comedy sketches, magic shows, and stage drama. Mystery gifts will be distributed to audience members. Faculty and guest seating is arranged in rows 3 and 4 (center area). All students and faculty are welcome to attend.
