The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced that the Polar Course (class number 64323), organized by the Marine Biology Research Institute, will be suspended on October 26 (Saturday), Period 34, at Building D, Room 503, due to the unavailability of the invited expert.

A makeup session will be scheduled at a later date. Students enrolled in the course are reminded to stay informed of further updates.
