The College of Business at Shantou University has announced the opening of registration for the CIMA Business Elite International Challenge, initiated by the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA) UK. The competition aims to identify and nurture student teams with outstanding business acumen.

Participants must register as a team of four, with all members coming from the same university. Registration is online at http://gbc.cncima.com/city.jsp and closes on October 31, 2013.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Organizer | CIMA (Chartered Institute of Management Accountants) |
| Eligibility | Undergraduate students |
| Team Requirement | 4 members, all from the same university |
| Registration Deadline | October 31 |
| Registration URL | http://gbc.cncima.com/city.jsp |
| Contact | Student Huang, 13794135751 (ext. 695751) |
