The core course "Marine Biology Exploration" at Shantou University will hold its outdoor classroom session, "Into Niutianyuan," an ecological survey by bicycle, on Sunday, October 27, from 13:30 to 17:30.

The activity starts from the University Motto Monument. The supervising instructor is Sun Zewei from the Institute of Marine Biology (phone: 13923998896 / ext. 698896). All participants must bring their own bicycles and are responsible for their own drinking water, food, and protective gear.

Environmental protection is emphasized; any damage to the ecosystem may result in disqualification. The event will be automatically canceled in case of rain or other extreme weather.
