The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a commendation notice for outstanding members of the 2012–2013 Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee.

Six students have been awarded the title of Outstanding Student Cadre of the Student Community: Luo Cong (Computer Science, Grade 2010), Huang Weipeng (Public Administration, Grade 2010), Hu Huicong (Electronic Information, Grade 2011), Fang Jiayi (English, Grade 2011), Yang Caiqi (Public Management, Grade 2011), and Liang Anyi (Clinical Medicine, Grade 2010). Four students received the Outstanding Staff title: Tang Wan (Public Management, Grade 2012), Chen Yidong (Chemistry, Grade 2012), Li Zawei (Clinical Medicine, Grade 2012), and Wang Xiaoyu (Medical Imaging, Grade 2012).

| Award | Recipients |
|------|---------|
| Outstanding Student Cadre of the Student Community | Luo Cong, Huang Weipeng, Hu Huicong, Fang Jiayi, Yang Caiqi, Liang Anyi |
| Outstanding Staff of the Student Community | Tang Wan, Chen Yidong, Li Zawei, Wang Xiaoyu |
