The Resource Management Office of Shantou University has announced road closure on the north side of Building G to accommodate renovation work at the entrance of the dome basketball training hall.

Construction will proceed in three phases: pavement demolition on the afternoon of October 25, base clearing on October 26, and concrete pouring on October 27. During the construction period (October 25–27), the road will be temporarily closed; pedestrians and vehicles are advised to detour.

After concrete pouring, a two-week curing period is required (October 27 to November 10). Motor vehicles must continue to detour during this curing phase until the pavement fully sets.
