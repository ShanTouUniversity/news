The College of Arts and Design and the Arts Education Center at Shantou University have issued a supplementary notice regarding the Hong Kong Modern Dance Troupe "Jiashiuo Dance Studio" performance of *Chopin VS Ca Bang*.

Since all tickets have already been distributed, additional walk-up seating is being arranged to accommodate more students and faculty. Those without tickets may queue at the designated area outside the main entrance of the Great Auditorium at 19:00 on October 24. If vacant seats remain at 19:30 before the performance begins, staff will guide attendees to their seats. Seating is limited and subject to availability.

The performance is open to audiences aged 12 and above only.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Performance | *Chopin VS Ca Bang* Dance Theater |
| Troupe | Hong Kong Modern Dance Troupe "Jiashiuo Dance Studio" |
| Queue Start Time | 19:00, October 24 |
| Final Seating Cut-off | 19:30 (before performance starts) |
| Queue Location | Designated area outside Great Auditorium main entrance |
| Age Restriction | 12 years and above |
