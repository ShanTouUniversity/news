## About the Speaker

Prof. Xiong Guangming received a master's degree in biochemistry from Bethune Medical University in Changchun in September 1982 and a doctoral degree in molecular biology from the PLA Veterinary University in Changchun in September 1986. In October 1987, he received a Humboldt Foundation fellowship and went to the University of Giessen in Germany for postdoctoral research. After 1989, he taught at the University of Giessen and the University of Marburg in Germany, and since 2003 he has served as a professor at the Institute of Pharmacology and Virology, Kiel University. He is also a visiting professor at Peking University, Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University, and Changchun University of Science and Technology.

The College of Science welcomes interested faculty and students to attend.
