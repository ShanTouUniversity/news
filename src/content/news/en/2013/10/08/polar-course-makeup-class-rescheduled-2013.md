It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a notice changing the makeup class time for the Polar Course. The course is offered by the Institute of Marine Biology; because it has invited an expert to give a lecture on campus, the class arrangements must be adjusted to fit the expert's schedule, and the previously planned makeup class is therefore changed.

According to the notice, the makeup class originally scheduled for periods 3 and 4 on Wednesday of week 4 (October 9, 2013) in E308 has been cancelled. It will instead be held during periods 1 and 2 on Saturday of week 6 (October 26, 2013), with the venue remaining E308. Students taking the course are asked to pass the word to one another.
