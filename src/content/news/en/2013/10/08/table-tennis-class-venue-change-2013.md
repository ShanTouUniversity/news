It is reported that the Physical Education Department has issued a notice adjusting the venue of table tennis classes for undergraduates at the main campus. With immediate effect, the classes previously held in the Auditorium will take place in the Gymnasium, and students enrolled in the course are asked to take note.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Table Tennis (main-campus undergraduates) |
| Previous Venue | Auditorium |
| New Venue | Gymnasium |
| Effective | With immediate effect (notice issued on October 8, 2013) |
| Issued By | Physical Education Department |

The Physical Education Department reminds students taking table tennis classes to inform one another and attend at the adjusted venue so as to avoid going to the wrong place.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
