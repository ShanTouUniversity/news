It is reported that the Office of Academic Affairs of Shantou University will hold an online course lecture at 4:00 p.m. on Wednesday, October 9, in Room 409 of Building E. Mr. Feng Zhaolin will be the speaker.

## Lecture Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Sharing experience with online courses (MOOCs), based on the MITx online course 7.00x "Introduction to Biology – The Secret of Life" |
| Speaker | Mr. Feng Zhaolin |
| Time | 16:00, Wednesday, October 9, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 409, Building E |
| Duration and language | About 40–50 minutes; delivered in English, with staff available to provide interpretation when necessary |
| Organizer | Office of Academic Affairs, Shantou University |

## Sharing First-Hand Learning Experience

In the spring semester of 2013, Mr. Feng Zhaolin took the online course 7.00x "Introduction to Biology – The Secret of Life", offered by the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) through the MITx platform. At the lecture, he will draw on his own learning experience to help faculty members understand how massive open online courses (MOOCs) are organized and how they are studied, laying a foundation for the university's teaching reform in large-scale online courses.

## MIT Practice as a Reference for STU

The lecture will also describe how the faculty and staff at MIT devoted extra effort to the online course to ensure that online learners had a unique interactive experience and to collect meaningful data on their learning behavior. According to the Office of Academic Affairs, the course's overall approach can serve as a reference for STU faculty members developing MOOCs and/or blended courses.

The lecture will last about 40–50 minutes and will be delivered in English, with staff on hand to provide interpretation when necessary. Faculty members interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
