It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will host an image design and makeup lecture for female students from **3:00 to 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, October 9, 2013**, in **E Staircase 202**. The speakers will be senior professional trainers from Shantou Qianwei Hair Design Chain Co., Ltd.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Image design and makeup for female students |
| Time | 3:00–5:30 p.m., Wednesday, October 9, 2013 |
| Venue | E Staircase 202 |
| Speakers | Senior professional trainers from Shantou Qianwei Hair Design Chain Co., Ltd. |
| Content | Training for female attendees on makeup and skincare, everyday hairstyles and outfit coordination, and interview attire |
| Interactive gift | Attendees will have the chance to take part in on-site interactive sessions and receive a student card (hairdressing discount card) provided by the company |

The lecture will provide training for female attendees in makeup and skincare, everyday hairstyles and outfit coordination, and interview dressing, among other topics.

A friendly reminder from the organizers: students attending the lecture may bring their own hair clips and hair-tying accessories so that they can take part in the interactive activities more easily. All female students are welcome to attend.
