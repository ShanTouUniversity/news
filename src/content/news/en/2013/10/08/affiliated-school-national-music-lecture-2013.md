It is reported that the Affiliated School of Shantou University will host a lecture titled “Cherishing the Traditions of National Music,” delivered by Prof. Kui Fu, a nationally recognized outstanding expert in social science popularization, from 4:10 to 5:20 p.m. on Wednesday, October 9, in E Staircase 203.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Cherishing the Traditions of National Music |
| Speaker | Prof. Kui Fu, a nationally recognized outstanding expert in social science popularization |
| Time | 4:10–5:20 p.m., Wednesday, October 9, 2013 |
| Venue | E Staircase 203 |
| Audience | Faculty members, students and parents |

The Affiliated School said faculty members, students and parents are all welcome to attend.
