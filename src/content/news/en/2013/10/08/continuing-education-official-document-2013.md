It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication issued a notice on October 8, 2013, that its continuing education course *Official Document Writing* will soon begin and enrolled students are asked to attend on time.

## Course Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Official Document Writing* |
| Instructor | Du Shimin |
| Time | 10:00–12:00, October 11, 2013 |
| Venue | Room D304, Teaching Building |
| Registration | Log in at http://training.stu.edu.cn with campus network account and password |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
