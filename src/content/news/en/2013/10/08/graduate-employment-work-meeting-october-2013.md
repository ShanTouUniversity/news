It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on October 8, 2013, announcing that the university will hold a graduate employment work meeting on the morning of October 9 (Wednesday) to coordinate employment-related work for graduates.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | Graduate Employment Work Meeting |
| Time | 9:00, Wednesday, October 9, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administration Building |
| Participants | Party branch secretaries (or deputy secretaries) of all colleges and residential colleges, counselors responsible for employment work, and staff of the Graduate Employment Guidance Center, Student Affairs Office |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office, Shantou University |

The notice asks those listed above to attend on time. The original notice did not release the meeting agenda or other specific topics, and this report makes no additions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
