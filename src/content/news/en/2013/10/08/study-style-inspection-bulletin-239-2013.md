It is reported that the "Three Styles" Development Office of Shantou University, which works on teaching style, study style and school spirit, has issued Bulletin No. 239 on study style inspections for week 3 of the fall semester of the 2013-2014 academic year. Members of the inspection team checked the teaching buildings that week. At 8:00 a.m. on September 29, six students were found wearing slippers to class, six bringing breakfast into the classroom and two arriving late by less than 5 minutes at Teaching Building E; at 2:00 p.m. on September 30 (Monday), three students were found late by less than 5 minutes in the same teaching building.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bulletin number | No. 239 |

| Time and place | 8:00 a.m., September 29, and 2:00 p.m., September 30 (Monday), both at Teaching Building E |
| Findings | September 29: 6 students wearing slippers to class, 6 bringing breakfast into the classroom and 2 late by less than 5 minutes; September 30: 3 late by less than 5 minutes |
| Follow-up | The list was sent to all schools (residential colleges), which are asked to criticize and educate the students concerned and to keep working on the study style |
| Issuing office | STU "Three Styles" Development Office |

The bulletin forwarded the list of students who failed to observe university rules and regulations to each school (residential college), asking them to criticize and educate the students concerned and to keep consistently improving the study style.
