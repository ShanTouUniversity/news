Shantou University launched its 2013–2014 school-affairs disclosure reporting process on November 14, 2013. Functional departments were asked to identify the matters they were responsible for disclosing, the scope and format of publication, and the officers in charge.

Departments were to complete a disclosure-items registration form and submit it to the School Affairs Disclosure Office and its oversight group by November 20, using `o_dwb@stu.edu.cn` and `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`, respectively. The work was conducted under the university’s disclosure rules to support faculty and staff access to information, participation, and oversight.
