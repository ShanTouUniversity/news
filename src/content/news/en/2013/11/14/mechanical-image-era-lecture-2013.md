It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a lecture titled *The Mechanical Image Era* on November 15, 2013, featuring Chen Shen.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | *The Mechanical Image Era* |
| Speaker | Chen Shen, editor and former vice president and deputy editor-in-chief, China Photography Publishing House |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, November 15, 2013 |
| Venue | ACC Conference Room 3 |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

## Content

The lecture will trace the beginning of the mechanical image era and the advent of the digital image age, introducing technical platforms such as daguerreotype, calotype, wet collodion, ambrotype/tintype and dry plate processes, and noting that Guangdong was the cradle of China’s mechanical photography era.

## Speaker

Born in Shanghai in 1949, Chen Shen has long engaged in publishing management, photography history research and teaching. He is a member of the Theory Committee of the China Photographers Association and a member of the China Theatre Association, and serves as a graduate supervisor and visiting professor at the Beijing Film Academy, Renmin University of China and the Graduate School of the Chinese National Academy of Arts. He has served as a judge for the China Photography Golden Statue Awards and as an academic committee member for major international photography exhibitions, and has authored *A History of Chinese Photography (1840–1937)* and *A History of Chinese Photographic Art*.
