It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has initiated the reporting of the 2014 domestic undergraduate enrollment plan, along with the preparation of promotional materials and recruitment outreach for the 2014 admissions cycle.

According to the安排, the proposed total domestic undergraduate quota for 2014 is 1,770 students. Each college is required to submit a preliminary plan based on its 2013 enrollment execution and institutional development goals. The university will then coordinate the final allocations across all colleges in light of the overall 2014 plan.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| 2014 Proposed Domestic Quota | 1,770 students |
| College Submission | Submit preliminary plans based on 2013 execution and development goals |
| University Coordination | Final allocation based on the 2014 total plan |
| Promotional Materials | Production of 2014 undergraduate admission brochures underway |

All colleges are urged to complete their submissions promptly to ensure a smooth admissions outreach process.
