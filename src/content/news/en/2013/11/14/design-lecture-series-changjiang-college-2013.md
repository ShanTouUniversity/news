The Changjiang College of Art and Design at Shantou University will host a series of design lectures on the evening of November 16 (Saturday) in the Level 1 Lecture Hall. The three guest speakers are designers Xu Tiantian, Zhu Decai, and Li De Geng.

The lecture schedule is as follows:

| Time | Speaker | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 19:30-20:30 | Xu Tiantian | On Space |
| 20:30-21:30 | Zhu Decai | Adaptive Design |
| After 21:30 | Li De Geng | My Relationship with Design in an Era of Change |

The lecture series will explore topics including spatial design, adaptive design, and the role of designers amid societal transformation, providing cross-disciplinary exchange opportunities for faculty and students in design and the arts. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
