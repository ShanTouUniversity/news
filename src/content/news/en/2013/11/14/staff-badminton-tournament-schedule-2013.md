It is reported that the Shantou University Trade Union has made adjustments to the schedule for the second phase of the faculty and staff badminton tournament.

The first phase group stage was completed recently. Given the significant changes in team rosters over the past two years, the second phase will now feature the top four teams from each group advancing to a cross-elimination bracket, ensuring both fairness and competitiveness.

Additionally, due to the large number of registrants from the College of Engineering, two separate teams were formed: Engineering College Team A (Group A) and Engineering College Team B (Group B). All other participating teams compete as single units representing their respective colleges.

The elimination round matchups will be announced separately by the Trade Union.
