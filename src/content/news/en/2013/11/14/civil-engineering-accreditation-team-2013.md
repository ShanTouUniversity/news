It is reported that the Civil Engineering program at Shantou University will undergo the Ministry of Education's professional accreditation in the near future. To ensure the smooth progress of the accreditation, a working group has been established following recommendations from relevant departments and approval by the STU Accreditation and Undergraduate Teaching Audit Assessment Leadership Group.

## Working Group Members

| Role | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Leader | Chen Yan |
| Deputy Leaders | Bao Nengsheng, Lin Peng |
| Secretaries | Deng Yong, Cai Lin |
| Members | Several (alphabetically sorted by Chinese surname) |

The working group will be responsible for coordinating accreditation document preparation, self-assessment report writing, and related liaison work, aiming to promote continuous improvement in program quality and enhance student development outcomes.
