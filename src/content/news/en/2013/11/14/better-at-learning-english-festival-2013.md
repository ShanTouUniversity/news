The first "Better at Learning, Better at English" English Festival, organized in collaboration between the Youth Volunteer Association of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University and Shantou Dongxia Middle School, will be held on November 16 (Saturday) at Dongxia Middle School.

The event aims to showcase the spirit of STU students serving society and keeping pace with the times, while also promoting the cultural atmosphere of the Shantou University campus. Activities include a Language and Grammar Masterclass and other interactive segments designed to help middle school students improve their English learning interest and abilities through engaging and fun methods.

The English Festival provides STU students with a valuable opportunity to apply their knowledge to social practice, while strengthening exchanges and connections between universities and middle schools. Faculty and students from across campus are welcome to attend and experience the charm of language learning.
