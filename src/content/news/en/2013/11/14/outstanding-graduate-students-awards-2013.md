The Graduate School of Shantou University recently announced the results of its 2013 Outstanding Graduate Student evaluation. After initial reviews by each graduate training unit and final adjudication by the Graduate School, Cao Qian and 59 other students were selected as recipients of the 2013 Outstanding Graduate Student Second Prize at Shantou University.

The evaluation process followed established procedures strictly, with each graduate training unit conducting initial recommendations based on allocated quotas, followed by a comprehensive final review by the Graduate School to ensure the process was open, fair, and impartial. The public notice period runs from November 14 to 17:00 on November 20, 2013. Anyone with objections may submit written inquiries or make telephone calls to the Graduate School.

The Outstanding Graduate Student award at Shantou University aims to recognize graduate students who demonstrate excellence in academic research, social practice, and moral character, and to inspire all graduate students to study diligently and develop comprehensively.
