It is reported that the Shantou University Library has recently featured *Annotated Complete Collection of Chaoshan Dialect Folk Songs* in its series of recommended new books on Chaoshan literature. Compiled by Lin Chaohong and Lin Lunlun over two years and through four drafts, the book is regarded as a rescue collection of increasingly endangered Chaoshan dialect folk songs.

## Cultural Value of Chaoshan Folk Songs

Chaoshan dialect folk songs are an important vehicle of Chaoshan cultural heritage, drawing on the artistic essence of poetry, classics, music, and ballads across the ages. They carry distinctive regional and ethnic characteristics, hold literary, historical, and folklore value, and belong to the "folk literature" category of intangible cultural heritage.

For centuries, these songs were passed down orally. It was not until 1929 that Chaoshan scholar Qiu Yulin published the first collected volume, *Chaozhou Folk Songs*, after which other collections followed. However, because the Chaoshan dialect lacks a standardized character list and due to regional variations, discrepancies exist among different editions — the very problem that motivated the compilers to spend two years producing this work.

## Scope and Scholarly Merit

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Title | Annotated Complete Collection of Chaoshan Dialect Folk Songs |
| Compilers | Lin Chaohong, Lin Lunlun |
| Songs collected | 1,003 folk songs |
| Coverage | Minnan-speaking areas of Chaozhou, Shantou, Jieyang, and Haifeng (Shanwei) |
| Sources | 17 song collections and other books published between 1929 and 2010, plus recent popular internet songs |

Divided into eight sections, the book proposes principles and methods for collecting and compiling folk songs from the combined perspective of language, culture, and folklore, standardizing the written characters used for all 1,003 songs. Annotations based on dialectological research give the volume high reference value. With its clear structure and precise phonetic notation, the book is considered significant for preserving the Chaoshan dialect, promoting its folk songs, and advancing Chaozhou culture at large.

## About the Compilers

**Lin Chaohong**: Senior lecturer at the Chaozhou Normal Branch of Hanshan Normal University, holds a master's degree in dialectology and is a national-level Putonghua proficiency tester. She authored *A Course in Putonghua Phonetics and Proficiency Testing* and published papers including "On the Prosodic Beauty of Chaoshan Folk Songs" and "On the Principles and Methods of Collecting and Compiling Chaoshan Dialect Folk Songs." She has been named an "Outstanding Teacher of Guangdong Province" and an outstanding tester of the province.

**Lin Lunlun**: Second-grade professor of Chinese language and literature and president of Hanshan Normal University, he serves as vice president of the Guangdong Association of Chinese Linguistics. He has long engaged in research on local dialects and culture, authoring more than 20 monographs and over 100 papers, and has received awards including the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences Young Linguist Award and the Guangdong Outstanding Achievement Award in Philosophy and Social Sciences.
