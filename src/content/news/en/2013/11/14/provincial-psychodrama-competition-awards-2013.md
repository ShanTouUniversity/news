The results of the 4th Guangdong Province University Student Psychodrama Competition were recently announced, with two productions from Shantou University standing out among entries from 44 universities across the province, winning second prize and third prize respectively.

"Growing Up Again" (《重新长大》), created and performed by Chen Xiaoling and seven other students from the Class of 2012, won the second prize, demonstrating the university's strong foundation in psychodrama creation and performance. "Nightmare" (《梦魇》), created and performed by five students from the Class of 2011 led by Fan Yanling, won the third prize. Both works focused on college students' mental health, using drama to portray the challenges and breakthroughs faced by contemporary youth during their development, earning high praise from the judges.

Hosted by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, this competition is one of the most influential branded events in university mental health education in the province. It aims to promote mental health work in higher education and guide students to pay attention to their psychological well-being through the artistic form of psychodrama.

The awards reflect the achievements of Shantou University in mental health education and student comprehensive quality development, as well as the artistic talent and creativity of STU students.
