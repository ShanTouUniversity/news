It is reported that the first makeup class for the "Polar Course" (Class No. 64323), offered by the Institute of Marine Biology at Shantou University, has been scheduled. The session will take place on Sunday, November 17 (Week 10), Period 34, in Room 503, Building D.

The assessment method for the course will be announced separately. Students enrolled in this course are encouraged to share this information with their peers and attend the makeup class on time.
