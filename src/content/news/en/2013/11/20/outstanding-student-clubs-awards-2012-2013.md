The Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the 2012–13 student club awards on November 20, 2013. Following a review organized by the STU Student Clubs Federation and approved by the committee, 10 clubs received the Outstanding Student Club title. The results also named 12 outstanding club presidents and six outstanding student club contributors.

The recognized clubs were the Science and Technology Innovation Association, Taekwondo Association, Sports Dance Association, Riddle Lovers Association, Psychological Growth Association, Tea Ceremony Club, Calligraphy and Painting Association, Xingchao Association, Roller Skating Association, and Animation Technology and Game Development Association.
