It is reported that the 2013 National University Counselor Excellent Paper Competition has officially launched, inviting outstanding academic papers from counselors and researchers engaged in ideological and political education at universities across the country.

The competition aims to summarize and exchange research findings and practical experiences of university counselors in the field of ideological and political education, and to promote the scientific development of student affairs work on campus. Eligible counselors and researchers may submit their papers for evaluation.
