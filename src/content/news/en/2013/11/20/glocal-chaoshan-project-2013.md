It is reported that the School of Literature at Shantou University will hold its first "Glocal Chaoshan" comprehensive project on November 22–23, 2013.

The centerpiece of the project is a symposium titled "Interdisciplinary Perspectives on Glocal Chaoshan Culture," bringing together scholars from within and outside the university to explore Chaoshan culture through multiple disciplinary lenses. In the 21st century, global diversity is accelerating, yet globalization is always realized through localization. This project was conceived to better understand the interplay between global and local forces and to examine the role and influence of the Chaoshan region and its culture within this dynamic interaction.
