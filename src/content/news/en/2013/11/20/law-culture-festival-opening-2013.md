It is reported that the School of Law at Shantou University will hold the opening ceremony and mock trial of its Ninth Legal Culture Festival on the evening of November 22, 2013, at the Science Report Hall. The event uniquely combines a humorous Chinese comedy sketch with a full English-language Anglo-American adversarial trial, presented with both Chinese and English subtitles, allowing the audience to experience the charm of law in an engaging atmosphere.

## Mock Trial Case Background

The trial is based on a true case: in April 2001, American "Emmy Award" winner Robert Blake was suddenly embroiled in a murder scandal involving his wife. Facing first-degree murder charges, he endured a grueling four-year legal battle that left him emotionally and financially drained. Yet, the criminal and civil courts delivered strikingly different verdicts, creating a dramatic contrast.
