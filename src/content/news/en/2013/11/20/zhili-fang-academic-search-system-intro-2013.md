It is reported that Shantou University Library has recently promoted the Zhili Fang system to faculty and students, aiming to help them access and utilize academic resources more efficiently.

The Zhili Fang system integrates multiple types of document resources and provides one-stop search and full-text access services. The specific resource types include:

| Resource Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Chinese and Foreign Journals | Full-text and index coverage of domestic and international academic journals |
| Dissertations and Theses | Full text of domestic and international master's and doctoral dissertations |
| Conference Papers | Collections of papers from important academic conferences |
| Patents | Domestic and international patent information retrieval |
| Monographs | Chinese and foreign academic monographs |
| Standards | Domestic and international technical standards and specifications |
| Scientific Achievements | Scientific reports and technology transfer information |
| Product Samples | Industrial product samples and technical materials |
| Scientific Reports | National scientific research reports |
| Policies and Regulations | Relevant laws, regulations and policy documents |

The library reminds the university community that Zhili Fang can be accessed via the campus network, and users can utilize its powerful search capabilities to quickly locate the academic resources they need and improve their research efficiency.
