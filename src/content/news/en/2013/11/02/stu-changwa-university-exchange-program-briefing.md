The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University will host an informational session on the exchange program to Chang Jung Christian University in Taiwan, providing students with an opportunity to learn about this academic exchange opportunity.

The program sends students to Chang Jung Christian University for a one-semester exchange from February to June 2014. Eligible applicants are second- and third-year undergraduate students (excluding medical majors) who maintain good academic standing, have no failing grades in the past year, demonstrate good moral character, and have not received any university-level disciplinary sanctions.

Courses taken during the exchange will be recognized by Shantou University, and credit transfer arrangements will be processed upon the student's return. All expenses, including tuition, accommodation, living costs, and round-trip travel, are the responsibility of the participating students.

Students interested in applying should submit their application materials to their respective college academic offices before November 15, 2013. Late applications will not be accepted.
