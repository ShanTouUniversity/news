The Business School at Shantou University recently announced the opening of registrations for the 2nd PEMT National Marketing Planning Competition and the 2013 H2 Marketing Professional Certification Exam (PEMT).

PEMT (Professional Marketing Executive Training) is one of the important professional certifications in the marketing field in China, administered by the National Marketing Professional Qualification Examination Office and the National Marketing Committee. Earning a PEMT certificate can enhance one's professional competitiveness in marketing and provide an advantage in job hunting.

Students interested in participating should submit their completed application forms to 12kxzou@stu.edu.cn by November 11, 2013, with the email subject line formatted as "Name + Major and Year + PEMT Exam/PEMT Competition." Students registering for the PEMT certification exam also need to independently register at http://www.pemt.org/bm.asp. For any questions, please contact Student Zou (extension 632134).

Students interested in the marketing field are encouraged to apply and take advantage of this opportunity to boost their career competitiveness.
