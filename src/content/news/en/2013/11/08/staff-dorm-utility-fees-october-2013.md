Shantou University’s Resource Management Office released the October 2013 utility bills for staff dormitories on November 8. The latest meter readings were taken on November 1–2, following the previous reading on October 1. Staff could check their individual bills through the university utility-management system.

Staff with questions could visit the Utility Center for a review from November 11 to 13 (telephone extension 2489). After the review period, charges without objections were scheduled to be deducted by the Finance Office from November 2013 payroll accounts.

> ⚠️ Individual dormitory bill details are not reproduced; they can be checked through the university utility-management system. Related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
