The "Artistic Direction" welcome party, jointly organized by the Youth League Committees of the Changjiang School of Art and Design and the School of Liberal Arts at Shantou University, will be held on the evening of November 8, 2013, at 7:30 PM in the Shantou University Auditorium. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

Audience seating is arranged in rows 6, 7, and 8. Please arrive 15 minutes early to find your seats and welcome the new students together.
