It is reported that Shantou University has completed the selection of candidates for the 2012–2013 National Encouragement Scholarship and the 2013–2014 National Grant. The Student Affairs Office completed a review after each school or residential college organized its selection under the relevant administrative measures and according to the principles of openness, fairness, impartiality and merit-based selection.

## Announcement Details

The announcement covers 291 National Encouragement Scholarship candidates and 1,300 National Grant candidates, for a total of 1,591 candidates. The names and related materials are included in the OA attachments; this report does not reproduce students’ personal information.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| National Encouragement Scholarship | 2012–2013 academic year; 291 candidates |
| National Grant | 2013–2014 academic year; 1,300 candidates |
| Issuing office | Student Affairs Office |
| Announcement date | November 8, 2013 |

## Objection Submission

Organizations or individuals with objections should submit written feedback to the Student Financial Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office by 9:00 a.m. on Tuesday, November 12, 2013. Submissions from organizations must bear an official seal, while individuals must provide their real names.

Written feedback should be delivered to Room 106 of the Administration Center. The OA announcement and its attachments remain the authoritative source for the candidate lists and related materials.

> ⚠️ The candidate-list attachments for the National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant are available on the OA system.
