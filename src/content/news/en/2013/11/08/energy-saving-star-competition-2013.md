The second "Energy-Saving Star" evaluation activity at Shantou University has officially launched, hosted by the Green Ribbon Environmental Volunteer Team under the University Youth Volunteer Association. Running from November 2013 to January 2014, the campaign's theme is "Every Drop of Conservation Makes a Low-Carbon Expert."

Participating dormitories will be evaluated based on per capita electricity consumption. The three dormitories with the lowest per capita electricity usage will be awarded the "Energy-Saving Star" Excellence Award. The activity aims to guide students in developing good habits of energy conservation and promote a frugal lifestyle, contributing to a green campus community.
