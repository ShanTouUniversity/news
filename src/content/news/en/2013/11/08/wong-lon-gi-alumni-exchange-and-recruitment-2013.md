It is reported that Guangzhou Wang Lao Ji Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. will hold a series of activities at Shantou University from November 19 to 26, including an alumni exchange, campus recruitment fair, and marketing competition, aiming to strengthen ties between the company and the university while providing employment opportunities for graduating students.

## Event Schedule

| Date | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| November 19 | "Wong Lon Gi President & STU Class of '88 Alumnus Fang Guanghong" 25 Years of Friendship with Alma Mater Alumni Exchange |
| November 20–26 | Wang Lao Ji 2014 STU Campus Recruitment Fair |
| Same period | "Wang Lao Ji Rising Star" STU Marketing Elite Practical Competition |

The alumni exchange will invite Wong Lon Gi president and STU alumna Fang Guanghong (Class of 1988) to return to campus and share professional experiences with fellow alumni. The campus recruitment fair will offer multiple positions for the graduating class of 2014, while the marketing competition provides current students with a platform to showcase their practical abilities.

All students are welcome to participate actively.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Alumni Affairs Office)
