Shantou University's 2013 faculty badminton tournament will begin on November 18, with nine representative teams competing. The participating teams are: Law/Business School Joint Team, Research/Graduate School/Library Joint Team, Physical Education Department, School of Science, College of Engineering, Administrative Staff Team, Resource Management Office, Chinese Language/Literature and Journalism School Joint Team, and Arts/Social Sciences/Continuing Education/Primary School Joint Team.

Team registrations must be submitted to the University Labor Union email by November 14. In the first stage, teams will be seeded and divided into Groups A and B using a serpentine draft format.
