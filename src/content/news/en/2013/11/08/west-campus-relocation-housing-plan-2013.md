It is reported that the General Affairs Office of the Resource Management Office issued a notice on November 8, 2013, to solicit comments on the housing selection plan for west campus relocation households.

Two draft documents — *Specific Arrangements for Lot-Drawing Housing Selection* and *Housing Selection Plan for West Campus Resettlement (Transitional) Housing* — are attached. Comments from all faculty and staff will be collected before submission to the university leadership for approval.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Documents for comment | Draft housing selection plan and lot-drawing arrangements |
| Deadline | 17:30, November 13, 2013 |
| How to comment | Send email to o_ssc@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | General Affairs Office, Resource Management Office |

Details are available in the OA attachments.
