Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced a schedule change on November 28, 2013, for Class 65142 of the Chinese Department’s *Culture and Humanity in Folk Literature*. The class originally scheduled for Periods 1–2 on Monday, December 2 (Week 12), was canceled and rescheduled for Periods 1–2 on Saturday, December 7, in Room G306.

This was a separate change from an earlier make-up arrangement for the same course, involving different class dates.
