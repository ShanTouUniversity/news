It is reported that after the Law College of Shantou University issued a notice for the court observation and exchange activity at Longhu District People's Court, a total of 34 students applied and were selected.

## Activity Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Activity Name | Court Observation and Exchange Activity at Longhu District People's Court |
| Assembly Time | 8:10 AM, December 4 (Wednesday) |
| Assembly Point | Motto Monument |
| Number of Participants | 34 students |
| Organizer | Law College |

Selected students are required to dress neatly, observe discipline, and follow arrangements.
