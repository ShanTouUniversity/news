Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office announced the results on November 27, 2013, for three small construction and repair projects. Following review by the university’s tendering group, separate contractors were selected.

| Project | Contractor | Award |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replacement of electric window openers at the New Library | Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 21,420 |
| Minor works at the West Campus courtyard | Shantou Residential Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. | RMB 99,225 |
| Mechatronics Department interactive virtual-reality lab renovation | Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 21,603.75 |

The public notice period ran from November 27 to 29, 2013. The Infrastructure Office listed 0754-86502490 for inquiries and the Supervision and Audit Office listed 0754-86502346 for complaints.
