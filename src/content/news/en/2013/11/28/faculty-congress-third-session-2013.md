It is reported that the Trade Union issued a notice on November 28, 2013, that the third session of the 5th Faculty Congress and the 7th Trade Union Congress of Shantou University is scheduled for December 4.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 14:30–17:30, December 4, 2013; please check in 15 minutes early |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Academy of Sciences |
| Agenda | See OA attachments for detailed content and schedule |
| Delegations | See OA attachments for the delegation list |
| Seating | Row 1 (A) center: university leaders and invited delegates; Row 2 (B) center: non-voting attendees; Rows 3–11 (C–L) center: official delegates |
| Requirements | Units should not arrange business trips or conflicting activities for delegates during the meeting; delegates with special circumstances must request leave from their delegation head or department trade union chair; delegations must report attendance by 12:00 on December 2 to the Trade Union |
| Contacts | Trade Union, phone 82902983 / 86502983, email o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Trade Union |
