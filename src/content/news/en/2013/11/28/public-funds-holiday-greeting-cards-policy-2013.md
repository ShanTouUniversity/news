Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Commission relayed rules issued by central, provincial and Shantou authorities in November 2013, directing university units not to use public funds to buy, print, mail or give away New Year cards, postcards or calendars. The rules also barred arranging such purchases through associations, enclosing the items in official correspondence or placing holiday greeting advertisements.

Necessary cases involving foreign affairs, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan or overseas Chinese affairs were exempt, but thrift was still encouraged. Costs could not be shifted to others or reimbursed with public funds. Finance staff were asked to review invoices, while disciplinary and audit offices would conduct oversight.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Discipline Inspection Commission)
