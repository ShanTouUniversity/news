It is reported that the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University announced the launch of the 2013 scholarship review for students from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao and overseas Chinese on November 28.

## Award Details

| Award | Quota | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Prize | 2 | RMB 5,000 per person |
| Second Prize | 3 | RMB 4,000 per person |
| Third Prize | 4 | RMB 3,000 per person |

## Eligibility

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible Students | Junior and senior undergraduates from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao and overseas Chinese |
| Application Deadline | Before 17:00 on December 4 |
| Organizer | Office of International Exchange and Cooperation |

Interested students should submit their application materials before the deadline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
