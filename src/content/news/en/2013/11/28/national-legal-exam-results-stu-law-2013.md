It is reported that the results of the 2013 National Judicial Examination were recently released, and students from the School of Law at Shantou University achieved outstanding results. Among 111 second-year law students who sat for the exam, 68 scored above the passing mark of 300 points, achieving an overall pass rate of 61.3%.

Among those who passed, 18 students scored 400 points or above, accounting for 26% of all test takers. This result demonstrates the teaching quality and effectiveness of the School of Law at STU in cultivating legal professionals.

The National Judicial Examination is a nationwide professional qualification exam for the legal sector, serving as a mandatory requirement for those seeking to become lawyers, judges, or prosecutors. The exam is known for its rigor, with a passing score of 300 out of a total of 600 points.
