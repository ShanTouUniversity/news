It is reported that the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University announced the opening of registration for the 2014 graduate program at the University of West England (Sweden) on November 28.

## Program Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program Name | University of West England 2014 Graduate Program |
| Eligible Applicants | Current STU students |
| Program Website | http://www.hv.se/internationalstudent |
| Scholarship | Students admitted to the program are eligible to apply for scholarships from the University of West England |
| Note | Registration for the exchange student and 3+1 dual degree programs will open at the start of next semester; specific dates to be announced |

Interested students may visit the program website for more details or refer to the 2014–2015 admissions brochure.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
