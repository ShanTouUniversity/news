Shantou University’s Graduate School scheduled a meeting on November 29, 2013, to coordinate applications for authorization to offer professional master’s degree programs. The meeting followed a notice from the Guangdong Academic Degrees Committee and was intended for leaders and application coordinators from graduate-training units.

It was set for 3–4 p.m. in room 213 of the New Administration Center.
