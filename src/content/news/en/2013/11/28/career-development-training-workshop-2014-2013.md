It is reported that the School of Liberal Arts at Shantou University has launched a Career Development Training Workshop for its 2014 undergraduate students, designed to help them positively face the increasingly competitive job market, build employment confidence, and enhance their professional skills and workplace competitiveness.

The workshop will cover a range of activities, including career mobilization meetings, company information sessions, legal guidance on employment contract signing, interview skills and etiquette training, and an alumni career growth forum. Detailed schedules and venues for each event will be announced separately by the school.

The School of Liberal Arts expressed its hope that through these systematic and multi-layered career development activities, graduating students will gain a better understanding of current employment policies and trends, face job-search challenges with confidence, and make a smooth transition from campus to the workplace.
