It is reported that, in accordance with a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced the launch of the intra-university selection round of Guangdong's first College Student "Industry-Major-Employment Talent Demand Analysis" competition on November 28.

## Competition Objectives

The competition aims to effectively improve employment guidance and service levels, cultivate students' career development awareness and social research investigation abilities, and continuously enhance college students' innovation, entrepreneurship capabilities, and employability.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition Name | Guangdong's First College Student "Industry-Major-Employment Talent Demand Analysis" Competition |
| Stage | Intra-university Selection Round |
| Launch Date | November 28, 2013 |
| Organizing Unit | Student Affairs Office |

Students interested in participating may register through their respective college student affairs offices for the selection process.
