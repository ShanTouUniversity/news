It is reported that the "Green Ribbon" Environmental Volunteer Service Team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association and the Science Popularization Team of the Science and Technology Association are jointly organizing the fifth session of the "Green Sprout Action" environmental education class and recruiting volunteers campus-wide.

## Background

The "Green Sprout Action" environmental class aims to help primary school students establish correct environmental concepts from a young age, pay attention to environmental protection details in daily life, and carry forward the volunteer spirit of "dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress."

## Recruitment Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | Fifth Session of "Green Sprout Action" Environmental Class |
| Organizers | "Green Ribbon" Environmental Volunteer Service Team; Science Popularization Team of STU Science and Technology Association |
| Target Recruits | All university students |
| Number of Volunteers | Several |
| Spirit | Dedication, Friendship, Mutual Assistance, Progress |

Students interested in participating may register through their college Youth Volunteer Associations or related organizations.
