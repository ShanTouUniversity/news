It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University published the list of 32 students invited to the 4th Student Dinner with University Leadership on November 27. The event will be held at Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant on November 28 at 18:00.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | 4th Student Dinner with University Leadership |
| Date and Time | November 28, 2013, 18:00 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Number of Participants | 32 students |

Students who have been selected are asked to arrive 45 minutes early (by 17:15) with their campus ID cards.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
