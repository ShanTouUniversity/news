It is reported that the Shantou University Sports Department has released the program booklet for the 27th Athletics Meet, which is now available for team leaders, coaches, and athletes to consult.

The booklet provides detailed information on the competition schedule, events, venue assignments, and rules of competition, serving as an essential reference for all participating units ahead of the meet.

The athletics meet is expected to take place in the coming days, with representative teams from all colleges across campus set to compete.
