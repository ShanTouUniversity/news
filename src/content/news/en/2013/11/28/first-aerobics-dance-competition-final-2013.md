It is reported that the 1st Shantou University Aerobics Dance Competition final will take place on November 30 (Saturday) from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM at the STU Gymnasium. Hosted by the Student Affairs Office and organized by the Student Community X Project, the competition aims to enliven the student community atmosphere, build a vibrant campus life, enrich students' extracurricular experiences, and provide a stage for dance enthusiasts to showcase their talents.

Teams will perform a variety of dance styles, including standard aerobics dance, Latin dance, and hip-hop, delivering a spectacular visual and auditory experience. All faculty and students are welcome to attend and cheer for the competitors.

Aerobics dance is a mass dance form that combines fitness, artistry, and social interaction, and is widely popular among university students. This competition marks the first time STU has organized a dedicated aerobics dance event on campus, offering a professional competitive and exchange platform for students passionate about dance.
