It is reported that the "When Little Fire Companions Meet Fire Extinguishers" fire safety knowledge activity, organized by the Volunteer Association of the College of Science at Shantou University, will take place from 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM on November 30 (Saturday) at the Jin San Jiao area and the adjacent basketball court.

Professional instructors have been invited to provide students with hands-on fire extinguisher training and fire escape method instruction. The event includes on-site fire extinguisher operation demonstrations, fire safety knowledge quiz with prizes, and a creative fire-scene DIY art activity open to passersby.

The College of Science Volunteer Association hopes that this fire safety awareness activity will enhance students' fire safety consciousness, equip them with basic firefighting skills and fire escape techniques, and contribute to a safe and harmonious campus environment.
