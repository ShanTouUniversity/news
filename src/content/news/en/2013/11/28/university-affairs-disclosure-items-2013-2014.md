Shantou University’s University Affairs Disclosure Office published the items registered by functional departments for the 2013–2014 academic year on November 28, 2013. The release aimed to support staff and students’ right to information, participation and oversight under the university’s rules on affairs disclosure.

The register covered offices including the Party and Administration Office, Organization and United Front Work Department, Publicity Department, university labor union and Supervision and Audit Office. It listed disclosure items, intended audiences, publication methods and responsible project contacts. Topics included university operations and decisions, cadre appointments and Party affairs, emergency response, follow-up on faculty-congress proposals and other departmental business. Methods included the university website, internal information network, meetings, documents and media.

The notice listed the oversight telephone number as `86502346` and email as `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`.
