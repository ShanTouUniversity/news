It is reported that in response to the call for the 7th National Million Students' Sunshine Sports Winter Long-Distance Running Campaign, Shantou University will continue its winter long-distance running program from November 2013 to April 2014, with all colleges and residential schools organizing students to participate.

The specific implementation plan includes the "Integrity Run, Challenge 100" event—a 1000-meter long-distance run—designed to encourage students to leave their dormitories, head to the sports fields, develop healthy exercise habits, improve physical fitness, and cultivate perseverance and determination.

The university's Physical Education Department stated that the winter running program is an important measure to implement the principle of "health first," and hopes that through sustained efforts, the campus will foster a culture of enthusiasm for sports and a positive, active attitude toward physical well-being.
