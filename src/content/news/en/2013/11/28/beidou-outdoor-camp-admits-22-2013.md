The Beidou Outdoor Development Association at Shantou University’s Zhicheng College selected 22 students for its first Beidou Outdoor Experience Camp. In a notice issued on November 28, 2013, the association scheduled a pre-trip briefing for 10:10 p.m. the following day, November 29, at Room J354 in Zhicheng College, where participants would also collect equipment.

Participants were asked to wear casual sports clothing. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce names or major information from the original roster.
