Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced make-up arrangements on November 28, 2013, for two History of Science classes offered by the Institute of Higher Education. Class 64489 was scheduled for the evening AB periods on Sunday, December 1, in Lecture Hall E201; Class 64490 was scheduled for the evening AB periods on Saturday, December 7, in Room E204.
