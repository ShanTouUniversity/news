It is reported that the General Party Branch and Youth League Committee of the College of Liberal Arts issued a notice on November 28, 2013, to hold an employment mobilization meeting for the Class of 2014.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:00, November 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Room E203, Building E |
| Theme | Helping graduates understand the employment landscape, find their positioning and better face the job market |
| Organizers | General Party Branch and Youth League Committee, College of Liberal Arts |

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Liberal Arts)
