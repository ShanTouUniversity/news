It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University issued a notice on November 29, 2013, that the midterm defense for student science and technology projects will be held on December 6.

## Defense Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 14:30, December 6, 2013 |
| Venue | Multimedia Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Format | Presentations in the order of the defense list, 5 minutes per project with PPT |
| Requirement | All projects must submit a written midterm review form in duplicate (with supervisor’s comments and signature) to the Youth League Committee Office of the School of Science by 17:00 on December 4, and send an electronic version to o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn; failure to submit on time will be regarded as abandonment and the start-up funding will be recovered |
| Issued by | Leadership Group for Extracurricular Academic Activities, School of Science |

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> ⚠️ The defense list and midterm review form are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science)
