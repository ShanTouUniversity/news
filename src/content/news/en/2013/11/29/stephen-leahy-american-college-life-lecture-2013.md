It is reported that American scholar Stephen M. Leahy will deliver an academic lecture from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM on December 3, 2013, in room E201-1 of the School of Liberal Arts at Shantou University. The lecture is titled "Having Fun and Changing the World: College Life in America from 1945 to the Present."

Starting from the end of World War II in 1945, Professor Leahy will trace and analyze the development of American college life over the past seven decades, exploring the cultural, social, and educational transformations of U.S. higher education. The lecture aims to offer faculty and students a vivid and in-depth perspective on American academic culture.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
