Shantou University’s Publicity Department announced on November 29, 2013, that Party general branches and directly affiliated branches at the main campus should collect theory-study materials. Each unit was asked to send a representative to Room 315 of the New Administration Center by December 2 to pick up items including *Study Outline on the Scientific Outlook on Development* and *Face to Face on Current Theoretical Issues*.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department)
