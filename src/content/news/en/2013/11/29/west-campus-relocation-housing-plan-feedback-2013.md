Shantou University’s Resource Management Office reopened consultation on a housing-selection plan for staff affected by west-campus demolition and construction on November 29, 2013. The office said it had revised the draft after considering staff feedback and comments from relevant university departments, and would submit a further revised version to university leadership for review after collecting responses.

Staff could email comments or proposed additions to `o_ssc@stu.edu.cn` by 5:30 p.m. on December 3. Employees seeking to change their housing type or submit a supplementary application because of the revisions were asked to file a new housing application form. The final selection rules, time and location were to be announced separately. This report covers the consultation process only and excludes personal housing information in the attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
> ⚠️ The draft housing plan and forms are available as attachments in the OA system; personal housing information is not reproduced here.
