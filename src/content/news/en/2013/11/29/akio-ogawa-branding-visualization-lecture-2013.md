It is reported that the Changjiang School of Art and Design at Shantou University will host Japanese scholar Akio Ogawa for an academic lecture on the evening of December 1, 2013 (Sunday) at 7:30 PM in the First Floor Lecture Hall. The lecture, titled "Visualization of Message Connoted in Branding," will explore how implied brand information can be visually represented.

Professor Ogawa is a well-known scholar in the field of brand design in Japan. His lecture will delve into how brand imagery conveys deeper messages through visual language, offering new perspectives on brand design theory and practice for attending faculty and students.

The lecture will be delivered in Japanese, with live translation provided by Dr. Zhang Zhigang from Guangdong University of Foreign Studies. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
