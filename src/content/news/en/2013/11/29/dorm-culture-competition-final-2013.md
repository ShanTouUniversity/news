It is reported that the third STU "My Dorm, My Show" Dorm Culture Competition final will be held on November 30 at 19:30 in the Science Report Hall, under the theme "Sparkling Dorms, Show Your Style."

After preliminary rounds, six teams have advanced to the final. During the competition, each team will showcase their dormitory culture through living space presentations, talent performances, and roommate chemistry challenges, highlighting the achievements and youthful spirit of their respective dorms.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, University Youth League)
