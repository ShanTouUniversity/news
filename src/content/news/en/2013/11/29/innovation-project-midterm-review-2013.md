Shantou University’s Academic Affairs and Student Affairs offices began midterm reviews in November 2013 for student innovation and entrepreneurship projects approved that year. Teams were asked to submit a review form, progress report and evidence of interim results. Project lists and forms were supplied as OA attachments.

| Project track | Submission location and method | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Innovation training | Academic Affairs service counter, third floor of the New Administration Center; two printed copies and an electronic copy to yjguo@stu.edu.cn | Dec. 20 |
| Entrepreneurship training and practice | Student Entrepreneurship Center, room 106; two printed copies and an electronic copy to luolie@stu.edu.cn | Dec. 20 |

Projects that did not pass were required to make corrections before continuing and accessing the next installment of funding; projects that still failed would be terminated. Contacts were Guo Yijia at 86502736 for innovation projects and Luo Lie at 86502255 for entrepreneurship projects.

> ⚠️ Project lists and review forms are available on the OA system.
