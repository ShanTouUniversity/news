The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University has announced the interview arrangements for the Whitman College exchange program. Following a preliminary review, eight students, including Mai Yunhua, have advanced to the interview stage.

Interviews will be held on Wednesday, December 4, at 4:30 PM in Conference Room 213 of the Administration Center. The interview will take the form of a defense, with each candidate delivering a five-minute presentation covering self-introduction and their personal essay topic, followed by approximately eight minutes of questions from the panel.

Candidates are encouraged to prepare thoroughly and attend on time.
