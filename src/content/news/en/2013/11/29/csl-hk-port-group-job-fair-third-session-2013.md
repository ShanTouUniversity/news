It is reported that the third CKI Group Shantou University Career Fair will be held from December 4 to 6 at Shantou University. Hutchison Ports Group will be the participating employer, offering recruitment opportunities to graduating students and current undergraduates at the university.

The career fair will include company presentation sessions, exhibition booths, and multiple rounds of interviews. The Student Affairs Office reminds all students attending the company presentation, exhibition, and second-round interviews to dress in business attire, in order to present a professional image and demeanor.

The university encourages graduating students to prepare in advance for their job search and to attend the relevant events on time to make the most of the recruitment opportunities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
