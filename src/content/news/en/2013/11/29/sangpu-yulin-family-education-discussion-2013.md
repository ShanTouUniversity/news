The Graduate School at Shantou University will host a "Sangpu Yulin" event on the evening of Sunday, December 1, from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM in the atrium on the second floor of the Y Building. The session's theme is "Family Education Perspectives from 'Dad, Where Are We Going?'"

The event aims to explore parent-child interaction and family education through discussions of the popular television show "Dad, Where Are We Going?", sharing family education experiences and examining the relationship between social phenomena and family upbringing. It encourages graduate students to reflect more deeply on the topic of family education.

The event is open to all graduate students and everyone is welcome to attend.
