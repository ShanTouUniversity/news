Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced on November 29, 2013, that Shantou Longhua Construction General Company had won the contract to upgrade water pipes in the staff housing area. The tendering group recommended the company with a bid of RMB 293,454.

The public notice period ran from November 29 to December 2. The Infrastructure Office listed 0754-86502490 for inquiries and the Supervision and Audit Office listed 0754-86502346 for complaints.
