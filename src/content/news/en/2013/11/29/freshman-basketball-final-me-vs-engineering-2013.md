It is reported that the championship match of the 2nd STU "Freshman Cup" basketball tournament will be held on December 1 at 19:30 at the STU Gymnasium. The medical school and engineering school freshman teams have both advanced from a large field of competitors to meet in the final.

Hosted by the Sports Department and organized by the Basketball Club, this year's tournament spanned nearly a month with two rounds of intense competition. The medical and engineering freshman squads emerged victorious through outstanding performances to set up the ultimate showdown for the "Freshman Cup" title.
