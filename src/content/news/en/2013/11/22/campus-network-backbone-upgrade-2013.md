Shantou University’s Network and Information Center planned to upgrade the system versions on two campus-network backbone devices from midnight to 6 a.m. on November 23, 2013. Services such as email and the university homepage could be briefly interrupted, while ordinary internet access was expected to remain available.

No separate restoration time was announced. The center listed 0754-86503400 and stunic@stu.edu.cn for inquiries.
