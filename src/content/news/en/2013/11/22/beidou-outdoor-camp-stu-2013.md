It is reported that the first outdoor experience camp of the "Big Dipper" Extension Association at Zhi Cheng College, Shantou University, will take place from the afternoon of November 30 to the morning of December 1, 2013.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Afternoon November 30 – morning December 1, 2013 |
| Location | Shuzhai Cave, Sangpu Mountain |
| Activities | Camping experience, outdoor skills training |
| Capacity | 20 participants |
| Organizer | "Big Dipper" Extension Association, Zhi Cheng College |

The event aims to enrich outdoor activities for college residents while fostering environmental awareness and teamwork spirit. All residents of Zhi Cheng College are welcome to sign up.
