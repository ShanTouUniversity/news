It is reported that the Career Development Center of the Shantou University Law School plans to organize students to attend court proceedings and engage in exchange activities at the Longhu District People's Court on the morning of December 4, 2013. The activity aims to help students better understand court trial procedures and strengthen their legal practice abilities. All university students are welcome to apply.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Date | Morning of December 4, 2013 |
| Venue | Longhu District People's Court |
| Activities | Courtroom observation, interactive exchange |
| Organizer | Career Development Center, Law School |
| Target participants | All university students |

## Registration

Interested students should send an email with the subject line "Longhu District People's Court Observation and Exchange Activity" to cdclaw@stu.edu.cn before 12:00 on November 25. The email must include college, class, name, student ID, and contact information. Applicants must have no scheduled classes or other activities during the event period.
