It is reported that, following deliberation at the 14th Shantou University leadership meeting on November 15, 2013, Lin Ling has been appointed as disciplinary inspector for a senior management position, with a probation period of six months.

## Appointment Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Appointee | Lin Ling |
| Position | Disciplinary Inspector for Senior Management |
| Probation period | Six months |
| Decision | 14th university leadership meeting, November 15, 2013 |
