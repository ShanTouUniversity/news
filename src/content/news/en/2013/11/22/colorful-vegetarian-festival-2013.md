It is reported that the second Colorful Vegetarian Festival, hosted by the STU Student Affairs Office as part of the Student Community X-Program, will take place from November 30 to December 1. The event aims to enrich campus cultural life, deepen understanding of vegetarian cuisine and develop hands-on cooking skills.

The festival comprises two events. The first, "Learn Vegetarian Cooking from Chefs," will be held from 14:30 to 18:00 on November 30 at Chen's Grandma Vegetarian Restaurant in Shantou, where participants can observe the full process of vegetarian dish preparation. The second, the "Vegetarian Cooking Showdown," will run from 9:00 to 11:40 on December 1 at Jin San Jiao, featuring two segments: "Discover Green — Vegetarian Knowledge Quiz" and "Create Green — Show Your Skills" cooking competition.

## Registration

Teams of 2 to 4 members may apply, with 6 to 8 teams accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. Applicants should submit the completed registration form to the designated email address by 22:00 on Thursday, November 28. Cooking stoves will be provided by the organizers, and ingredients will be supplied free of charge by Chen's Grandma Vegetarian Restaurant. All faculty and students are welcome to watch, and audience members can join interactive games for a chance to win prizes. For inquiries, contact Student Chen.
