Shantou University’s Student Entrepreneurship Center and Entrepreneurship Park Management Office announced the list of projects approved for the 2014 university Student Entrepreneurship Fund on November 22, 2013. Applications were reviewed by the fund’s evaluation committee and approved by the Entrepreneurship Park Management Committee.

The notice body did not specify project names, the number of awards or funding amounts; those details are in the original project list.
