It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University will hold the first lecture of the Peer Growth Learning Plan on Saturday, November 23, at 7:30 PM in the Student Activity Hall on the first floor of the Library.

The lecture, themed "How to Balance Academics, Organizations, and Activities," features senior student Guo Yan, who will share his experiences and time-management strategies for navigating campus life. Twenty-three students who successfully registered are required to arrive 10 minutes early; other interested students are welcome to attend as auditors.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
