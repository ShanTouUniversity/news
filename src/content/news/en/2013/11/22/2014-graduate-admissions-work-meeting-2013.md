Shantou University’s Graduate School announced a meeting on 2014 master’s admissions work on November 22, 2013. It was scheduled for 3 p.m. on November 26 in Room 201 of the New Administrative Center and was intended for graduate-work leaders from schools, departments, institutes and master’s programs.

Those unable to attend were asked to notify the Graduate School in advance and arrange for another faculty representative. The notice listed 82902424 for inquiries but did not provide meeting outcomes or subsequent admissions developments.
