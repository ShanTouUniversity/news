It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University is launching a themed activity titled "Chaoshan Tea and Food Culture Exploration," organized by the Tea Culture Society and open to all students.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Chaoshan Tea and Food Culture Exploration |
| Host | Student Affairs Office |
| Organizer | Tea Culture Society |
| Registration Period | November 23–29 |
| Format | Interviews, field trips, literature research,成果分享 |

The activity aims to guide students across campus to gain a deeper understanding of Chaoshan's local food culture through various approaches including interviews, field investigations, and literature research, culminating in a shared presentation of findings.
