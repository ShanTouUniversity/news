It is reported that the Law School at Shantou University will host an academic lecture on the morning of Monday, November 25, from 10:00 to 11:00 AM in Room 213 of the Law School building, with Czech scholar Tomas Hulle as the guest speaker.

The lecture is open to all faculty and students of the university. Those interested are welcome to attend. A brief biography of the speaker is available in the original notice.
