It is reported that the Center for International Language Learning (CILL) at Shantou University will hold debate qualifiers on November 23 and the speech and singing semifinals on November 24 as part of the 2013 English Festival.

## Schedule

| Competition | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Debate Qualifiers | 9:00, November 23 | CILL |
| Speech Semifinals | 14:00, November 24 | — |
| Singing Semifinals | November 24 | — |

The theme of this year's English Festival is "My Life in Our World." Competitors who advance from these semifinals will vie for spots in the December finals, where they will compete alongside participants from other domestic and international universities for the English Festival championship.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Center for International Language Learning)
