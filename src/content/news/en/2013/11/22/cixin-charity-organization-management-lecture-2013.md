It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University will host an academic lecture on the afternoon of November 24 from 16:00 to 17:30 in Lecture Hall E202, featuring Cai Mutong, president of the Shantou Cixin Charity Association, who will speak on "Organization Management of Cixin Charity Association."

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:00–17:30, November 24, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E202 |
| Speaker | Cai Mutong (President, Shantou Cixin Charity Association) |
| Topic | Organization Management of Cixin Charity Association |

Cixin Welfare Hall, a branch of the Shantou Charity Federation, was officially approved as such by the Shantou Municipal Civil Affairs Bureau and Social Organization Administration on July 1, 2003. Under the leadership of President Cai Mutong and his team, the organization has grown into a well-established charitable organization and is one of the most influential grassroots charities in the Chaoshan region.
