It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on November 22 that the 4th President's Dinner with Students will take place at 18:00 on November 28 in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant, and registration is now open to all students across campus.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Event name | 4th President's Dinner with Students |
| Time | 18:00, November 28, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

The event aims to encourage students to offer suggestions and participate in university development, promoting in-depth communication between university leaders and young students. Four to five senior leaders, along with representatives from relevant departments and faculty, will attend to engage in face-to-face dialogue with students and listen to their opinions on university development.

Students interested in participating may register through their respective college counselors. Spaces are limited and registration will close once the quota is filled.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
