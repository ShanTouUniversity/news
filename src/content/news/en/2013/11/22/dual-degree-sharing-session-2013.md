It is reported that the Shantou University Student Learning Center (SLC) will hold a dual degree study experience sharing session on the afternoon of November 23, inviting four outstanding students pursuing a second degree in business administration to share their academic experiences, covering topics such as academic performance and time management. The event is open to students interested in enrolling in a dual degree program.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Date | Saturday, November 23, 2013, 15:00–17:00 |
| Venue | Room 301, Building E |
| Organizer | Student Learning Center (SLC) |
| Target audience | Students interested in pursuing a dual degree |

## Speakers

| Speaker | College/Major |
|---------|--------------|
| Luo Yacong | School of Journalism and Communication, Class of 2011 |
| Zhang Shiwen | College of Electronics and Information Engineering, Class of 2010 |
| Miao Qing | College of Electronics and Information Engineering, Class of 2011 |
| Sun Jifeng | Business School, Finance, Class of 2011 |

The sharing session will cover practical topics including academic planning, time allocation, and stress management in the context of dual degree studies, providing valuable reference for students considering a second degree.
