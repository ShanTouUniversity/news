It is reported that the College of Sciences at Shantou University will host an academic talk on the evening of Saturday, November 23, at 19:00 in Lecture Hall E202, featuring Dr. Wang Shijun, general manager of Beijing Weidoushitong Technology Co., Ltd.

## Talk Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 19:00, November 23, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E202 |
| Speaker | Dr. Wang Shijun |
| Topic | Mathematical Techniques and Image Processing Applications |
| Bio | Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics, Institute of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (1998); currently general manager of Beijing Weidoushitong Technology Co., Ltd. |

The talk will introduce the basic concepts of mathematical technology applications through concrete examples from public security criminal investigation, particularly focusing on the application of digital image processing in forensic evidence analysis.
