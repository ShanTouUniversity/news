In November 2013, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced a Visual DNA tracking test for students admitted in 2010. The activity was intended to help participants understand themselves and consider career plans. It ran from November 25 to December 8, and the test took about 30 minutes.

## Test and Data Offer

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Students admitted in 2010 |
| Test period | November 25–December 8, 2013 |
| Platform | Shantou University psychological testing website |
| Test duration | About 30 minutes |
| Completion benefit | An additional 5 GB of campus network data |

After completion, the system provided a brief report and a data-claim code, and emailed a detailed report to each participant. According to the attachment, students could activate the data through the campus payment platform after the Network Center recorded completion, a process expected to take 48 hours. The data remained valid until June 1 of the following year. No individual test results are reproduced here.
