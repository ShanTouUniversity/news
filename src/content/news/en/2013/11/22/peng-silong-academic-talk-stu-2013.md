It is reported that the College of Sciences at Shantou University will host an academic talk on the afternoon of Monday, November 25, at 16:15 in Lecture Hall E202, featuring Prof. Peng Silong, who holds a Ph.D. from the Institute of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (1998), and has been deputy director of the National Special Integrated Circuit Design Engineering Research Center since 2000.

## Talk Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:15, November 25, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E202 |
| Speaker | Prof. Peng Silong |
| Topic | In the Prime of Youth (恰同学少年) |
| Bio | Graduated from the Department of Mathematics, Anhui University (1993); Ph.D. in Science, Institute of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (1998); IEEE and IEEE Signal Processing Society member |
