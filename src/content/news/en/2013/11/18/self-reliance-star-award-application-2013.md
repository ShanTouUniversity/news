It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University has launched two initiatives: the 2013 search for "China College Students Self-Reliance Star" and the nomination process for the "New Oriental Special Scholarship for West China Students in Difficult Circumstances."

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Activity 1 | 2013 Search for "China College Students Self-Reliance Star" |
| Activity 2 | "New Oriental Special Scholarship for West China Students in Difficult Circumstances" Nomination |
| Registration Deadline | November 22, 2013 |
| Registration Method | Register via the official activity website |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Shantou University |

Students interested in participating in the selection activities should register via the official website by November 22, 2013, in accordance with the requirements specified in the notice. For detailed information and the registration link, please refer to the original notice on the OA system.

> ⚠️ The registration link and attachments are available on the OA system.
