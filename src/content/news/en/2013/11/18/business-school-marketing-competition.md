It is reported that the Business Practice Center of Shantou University's Business School will hold the second "Step by Step" Marketing Competition, with preliminary rounds scheduled for November 30 to December 1, 2013, and the finals on December 7.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizer | Business Practice Center, Business School |
| Preliminary Rounds | November 30 – December 1, 2013 |
| Finals | December 7, 2013 |
| Participants | All STU students |
| Theme | Learning marketing knowledge, spreading marketing concepts |

The "Step by Step" Marketing Competition aims to enrich students' extracurricular lives, help them deepen their understanding of marketing专业知识, promote marketing concepts, and improve practical operational skills. This year's competition provides a platform for all STU students to showcase their marketing talents and develop teamwork abilities.
