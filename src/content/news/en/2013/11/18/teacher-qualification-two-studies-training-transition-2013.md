Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education and Academic Affairs Office outlined a transition plan for the Education and Psychology courses used in teacher-certification training on November 18, 2013. Under the reform plan at the time, Guangdong intended to implement a unified national teacher-certification examination and periodic registration in 2015, ending the previous university-organized training route for non-teacher-education students.

Spring 2014 was to be STU’s final term of the existing training program, open only to undergraduate and graduate students graduating in 2014. Other undergraduates could still take the courses as electives through the credit system, but would receive no training certificate. The notice also said existing course certificates would become invalid once the unified examination system took effect.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Institute of Higher Education and Academic Affairs Office)
