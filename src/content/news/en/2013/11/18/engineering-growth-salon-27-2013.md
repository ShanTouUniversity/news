It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the College of Engineering of Shantou University will hold the 27th Growth Salon on November 22, 2013, under the theme “Sharing with You, Helping You Soar.” The event is open to all students of the College of Engineering.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Sharing with You, Helping You Soar |
| Guest speakers | Chen Chao, Huang Xiaoyu, Huang Keli, Qiu Huachuan, Zhu Pan |
| Hosts | Zhou Yanhua, Zhou Dan |
| Participants | All students, College of Engineering |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, November 22, 2013 |
| Venue | Room E-201 |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, College of Engineering |

## Registration

Students interested in attending may register by email, providing name, grade, major and contact information. The registration deadline is 17:30 on November 20, 2013. The designated email address and attachment are available on the OA system.

All interested students are welcome to participate.
