It is reported that the Institute of Marine Biology at Shantou University's School of Science is recruiting 60 long-term volunteers from across campus for Chinese white dolphin conservation.

## Activities

Volunteers will engage in the following activities:

- Distributing survey questionnaires
- Planning awareness campaigns on Chinese white dolphins and the Shantou coastal protection zone
- Conducting field surveys by boat in Shantou waters

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Host | Guangdong Provincial Ocean and Fisheries Bureau |
| Organizer | Institute of Marine Biology, Shantou University |
| Positions Available | 60 |
| Registration Deadline | November 27, 2013, 12:00 PM |
