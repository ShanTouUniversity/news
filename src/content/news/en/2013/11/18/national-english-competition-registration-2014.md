It is reported that registration for the 2014 National College Students English Competition has officially opened. The competition consists of two stages: the preliminary round and the final round.

## Competition Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | 2014 National College Students English Competition |
| Preliminary Round | 9:00–11:00, Sunday, April 13, 2014 |
| Preliminary Venue | Shantou University |
| Final Round | 9:00–11:00, Sunday, May 11, 2014 |
| Final Venue | Guangzhou |
| STU Graduate Categories | Category B, Category C |

Graduate students at our university may register for Category B or Category C competitions. Interested students are encouraged to complete their registration in accordance with the notice requirements.

> ⚠️ Detailed registration procedures and attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
