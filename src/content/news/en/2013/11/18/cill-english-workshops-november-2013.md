Shantou University’s Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL) scheduled three English workshops for late November 2013. The sessions focused on interview communication, TOEFL iBT speaking and public speaking. Places were limited, and interested participants were asked to reserve through the pages listed in the original notice.

| Topic | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Using English to Your Advantage: Excel at an Interview (Chanchal Singh and Sohani Gandhioke) | Nov. 22, 10:30 a.m.–noon | CILL |
| TOEFL iBT: Tips & Strategies for Speaking (Gena Rhoades and Kristine Adams) | Nov. 22, 3:30–5 p.m. | CILL |
| Public Speaking Skills (Sohani Gandhioke and Rita Zheng) | Nov. 27, 9:30–11 a.m. | CILL |

The notice listed reservation pages for the interview session (https://excelatinterview.eventbrite.com), TOEFL speaking session (https://toeflibt3.eventbrite.com) and public-speaking session (https://pspeakingws.eventbrite.com). Participants were advised to register only if they could attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Center for Independent Language Learning)
