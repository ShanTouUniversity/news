It is reported that the Mental Health and Counseling Center under the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University will launch a psychological group activity themed "Warm Up Happiness" this semester, recruiting 40 undergraduate students on a voluntary basis.

## Activity Content

The activity aims to enhance students' happiness and mental health literacy, helping them better understand themselves and others, and cultivate positive psychological qualities such as confidence, tolerance, and gratitude. The program covers six modules: Meeting as a Song, Gratitude Between Us, Unspoken Tolerance as We Grow, Knowing and Accepting Oneself, The Meaning of Life, and Looking Ahead — Optimism and Hope.

## Activity Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Warm Up Happiness |
| Date | Saturdays, November 23 and 30, 2013, 9:00-12:00 or 14:00-17:00 |
| Venue | Room 203, Building E |
| Slots | 40 participants (20 female, 20 male) |
| Selection | Lottery draw if applications exceed slots |

Participants are expected to fully engage in all sessions and have no conflicting coursework or activities during the activity periods. Students with left-behind childhood experiences (living independently for over three years before age 16 due to parents being away) are also welcome to apply.
