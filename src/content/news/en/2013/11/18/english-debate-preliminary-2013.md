It is reported that the English Language Center issued a notice on November 18, 2013, that the preliminary round of the 2013 English Festival Debate will be held on November 23 at CILL (Language Learning Center, 1st floor, Library).

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2013 English Festival Debate — Preliminary Round |
| Registration deadline | 24:00, November 19, 2013 |
| Preliminary date | November 23, 2013 (Saturday) |
| Venue | CILL (Language Learning Center, 1st floor, Library) |
| Eligibility | All undergraduate students, teams of two recommended or individual entry |
| Format | British Parliamentary Format, four teams per debate, two debaters per team |
| Preparation | Debate positions drawn 20 minutes before the debate, topic announced 15 minutes before, each speech 7 minutes |
| Judging | Judges score eight debaters per debate, selecting the best debater and the winning team |
| Follow-up | Selected debaters will enter the final round robin and join pre-competition workshops organized by the English Debate Society |
| Registration | Download the application form in the attachment and send it to the designated email address |
| Organizer | English Language Center |

The British Parliamentary Format divides teams into Opening Government, Closing Government, Opening Opposition and Closing Opposition. The best debater and highest-scoring team per debate will be recognized. Details, the application form and contact emails are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The application form and detailed rules are available on the OA system.
