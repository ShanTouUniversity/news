It is reported that registration and preliminary scheduling for Shantou University’s 27th Track and Field Meet have been completed. A total of 10 teams and 311 athletes have registered, including 165 male athletes and 146 female athletes. The detailed competition schedule and entries are provided in the attachment issued by the Physical Education Department.

## Pre-Meet Schedule

The Physical Education Department reminded participating units that registered athletes and events generally may not be changed or added. If a unit has questions about event groupings or finds an error in an athlete’s name, its team leader should compile the corrections and email them to Mr. Zhang at zhangy@stu.edu.cn by 16:00 on Wednesday, November 20.

| Item | Time | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coordination meeting | 16:00, Wednesday, November 27 | Participating units should attend as required; entry order will be decided by drawing lots at the meeting |
| Opening ceremony rehearsal | 16:00, Wednesday, December 4 | Each school or residential college should send 36 to 60 people to assemble at the center of the basketball court in six-column formation; the organizing committee will arrange guides |
| Competition session | 14:00, December 7 | Competition begins |
| Opening ceremony and competition | 08:30 and 09:30, December 8 | The opening ceremony begins at 08:30, followed by competition at 09:30 |
| Competition session | 14:00, December 8 | Competition begins |

## Relay Documentation

Under the competition rules, each participating unit may select four athletes from its registered competitors for relay events. The required relay order sheets, in seven copies for each event, must be submitted to the call room according to the following schedule:

- Men’s and women’s 4×400-meter relay order sheets: by 09:00 on December 8;
- Men’s and women’s 4×100-meter relay order sheets: by 13:30 on December 8.

The relay order sheets are included in the OA attachments and must be printed by the participating units. Units should also complete their pre-meet preparations and submit an introduction of no more than 120 Chinese characters for their entry in the opening ceremony to Mr. Yan at dlyan@stu.edu.cn before the November 27 coordination meeting.

For inquiries about the meet, contact the Physical Education Department office at 86503727.

> ⚠️ The meet scheduling verification sheet, relay order sheets and other event attachments are available on the OA system.
