Shantou University’s Party Committee study group scheduled a session on the decision concerning comprehensive reform, adopted at the Third Plenary Session of the 18th Communist Party of China Central Committee, for November 20, 2013, from 15:00 to 16:00. The meeting was set for Room 330 of the Administration Center, with Professor Huang Yan listed as speaker.

The notice invited university Party Committee members and heads or representatives of relevant Party branches, offices, the news center, Student Affairs Office, Social Sciences Department, labor union, and Youth League Committee.
