Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office published the midterm schedule for MAT1901A Calculus C-I on November 18, 2013. All three classes were scheduled to sit the exam during periods 1–2 on Thursday, November 21 (Week 10), covering 484 students in total.

## Examination Schedule

| Class | Venue | Enrollment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 64128 | Rooms D503 and D504 | 163 |
| 64129 | E Building Tiered Classroom 101 | 159 |
| 64130 | Lecture Halls 1 and 3 | 162 |

Candidates were instructed to sit in alternate seats in their assigned rooms. Students approved for audit status were also required to attend, and invigilators would direct seating.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
