It is reported that the 8th Guangdong College Campus Culture Art Festival "Guangfa Securities Cup" New Media Competition has officially opened. Students interested in participating should submit their entries by 11:00 on December 11, 2013.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 8th Guangdong College Campus Culture Art Festival "Guangfa Securities Cup" New Media Competition |
| Submission Deadline | 11:00, December 11, 2013 |
| Submission Method | Deliver the application form and competition disc to the Youth League Committee Office |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Shantou University |

Students wishing to participate should follow the requirements in the notice and deliver the completed "New Media Competition Application Form" along with their competition disc to the Youth League Committee Office before the deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.

> ⚠️ The application form template and detailed rules are available in the OA attachment.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
