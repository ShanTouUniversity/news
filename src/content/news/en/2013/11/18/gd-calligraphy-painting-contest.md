It is reported that, as part of the overall arrangements for the 8th Guangdong College and Vocational Student Campus Culture and Arts Festival, the Communist Youth League Guangdong Provincial Committee, the Provincial Department of Education, the Provincial Department of Culture, and the provincial students' federation will jointly hold the 8th Calligraphy and Painting Art Festival works contest. The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has forwarded the notice and is collecting entries from students across the university.

## Submission Deadline Set for December 4

Students who wish to compete must prepare a registration form and 4R color photographs of their works in accordance with the contest notice, and deliver them to the university Youth League Committee office by December 4, 2013. Registration forms and other documents are available through the Youth League Committee.

The contest is one of the components of the Guangdong Campus Culture and Arts Festival for college and vocational students, providing a platform for students across the province's universities to showcase their calligraphy and painting talents. STU students may take this opportunity to compete alongside peers from other institutions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
