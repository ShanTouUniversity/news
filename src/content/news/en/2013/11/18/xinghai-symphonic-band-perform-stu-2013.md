It is reported that the Xinghai Conservatory of Music Symphony Wind Band will perform at the Shantou University Auditorium on November 22, 2013 at 19:30. The ensemble, comprising 56 professional student musicians, is the largest and the only symphony wind band in South China formed exclusively by students from a professional music conservatory.

## Concert Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Performing Ensemble | Xinghai Conservatory of Music Symphony Wind Band |
| Time | 19:30, November 22, 2013 |
| Venue | Shantou University Auditorium |
| Musicians | 56 |
| Main Repertoire | Joe Hisaishi symphonic suites: *Valley of the Wind*, *My Neighbor Totoro*, *Kiki's Delivery Service* and more |

The concert will feature classic works by Joe Hisaishi, the renowned composer behind Studio Ghibli films, including symphonic suites from *Valley of the Wind*, *My Neighbor Totoro*, and *Kiki's Delivery Service*, offering STU faculty and students an unforgettable symphonic experience.
