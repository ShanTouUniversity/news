It is reported that the Communist Youth League Guangdong Provincial Committee, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Culture, and the Guangdong Provincial Students' Federation have decided to jointly hold the 3rd "Touching Campus of Southern China" annual figure selection. The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has forwarded the relevant notice.

## Event Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizers | CYL Guangdong Provincial Committee, Provincial Dept. of Education, Provincial Dept. of Culture, Provincial Students' Federation |
| Event | 3rd 'Touching Campus of Southern China' Annual Figure Selection |
| Purpose | Showcase the spirit of contemporary Southern China youth and highlight exemplary role models |
| Requirements | Outstanding typical figures with先进性 in specific fields |

The selection aims to集中展现 the spiritual outlook of contemporary youth in Southern China, discover and promote advanced role models, and inspire students across the province to strive for excellence. Universities are encouraged to attach great importance to the selection, conduct in-depth outreach, and recommend exemplary figures who demonstrate outstanding achievements in their respective fields.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
