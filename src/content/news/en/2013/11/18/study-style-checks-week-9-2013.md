Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office issued Bulletin No. 245 on November 18, 2013, summarizing two classroom checks conducted during the ninth week of the fall 2013–14 semester. The checks took place in Building DG on the afternoon of November 11 and Building E on the afternoon of November 14.

| Date and location | Late by no more than five minutes | Late by more than five minutes | Other record |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| November 11, Building DG | 12 instances | 5 instances | — |
| November 14, Building E | 2 instances | 3 instances | 1 instance of wearing slippers in class |

The bulletin asked the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide follow-up education. Its attachment listed individual students; this report includes only aggregate figures and omits identifying information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Three-Style Development Office)
