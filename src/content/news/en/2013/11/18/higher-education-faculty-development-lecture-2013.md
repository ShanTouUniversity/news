Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education scheduled a lecture on “Ecological Approaches to and Issues in University Faculty Professional Development” for November 20, 2013. Professor Qin Guozhu was listed as the speaker. The talk was set for 14:30–16:30 in Room G201 and was open to faculty, staff, and students.
