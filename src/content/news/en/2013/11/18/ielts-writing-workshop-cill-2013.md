Shantou University’s Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL) scheduled the third workshop in its IELTS preparation series for November 20, 2013. The session focused on developing writing skills and was set for 4–6 p.m. at CILL. Places were limited; reservations were available at https://ieltsworkshopseries3.eventbrite.com.

The center advised participants to register only if they could attend.
