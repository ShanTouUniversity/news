Shantou University’s Physical Education Department arranged supervised pre-meet training for students registered in the javelin event, with safety as a key consideration. Sessions were scheduled at the athletics field every Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday from November 25 to December 6, 2013.

| Day | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | 3–4 p.m., instructor Zou |
| Tuesday | 7–8 a.m., instructor Ding |
| Wednesday | 3–4 p.m., instructor Li |

Participants were asked to meet the instructor at the field during the corresponding session. The department listed 86503727 and o_tyb@stu.edu.cn for inquiries.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
