Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced the 2013–14 corporate- and individual-funded scholarship process on November 21, 2013. The programs planned to award 141 places, with a total allocation of RMB 705,000 stated in the notice. The standard award was RMB 5,000 per student, while the Liang Zhaohan Medical College scholarship provided RMB 2,000 per student.

| Program | Places | Main eligibility or arrangement |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Outstanding Female Students from Outside Guangdong | 25 | Selection had been completed at the start of the semester |
| Hakka Student Scholarship | 2 | Main-campus students in year three or above with GPA of at least 3.0 |
| Medical College Freshman Scholarship | 1 | For a new student at the Medical College |
| Outstanding Senior Student Scholarship | 113 | Main-campus or Medical College students in year three or above with GPA of at least 3.0 |

Students could not apply for both the Hakka and Outstanding Senior Student programs. The Liang Zhaohan Medical College scholarship excluded sixth- and seventh-year students in the seven-year clinical medicine program and reserved one place for nursing. One Chen Houfu Scholarship place was designated for interior design.

Applicants were asked to submit an academic transcript and a personal essay of no more than 1,200 Chinese characters; previously published work was optional, and scans of awards at university level or above were also required. The deadline was 5 p.m. on November 28, 2013. After applying online, students were asked to check the generated form in the Student Affairs System to confirm submission. The full allocation plan and application channel were provided in the OA attachments. Inquiries could be sent to xyli@stu.edu.cn.
