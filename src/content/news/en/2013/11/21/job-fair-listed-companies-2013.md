It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University, together with the Shantou Municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau, will hold a campus recruitment fair on the morning of November 29, 2013, at the Student Activity Hall on the first floor of the New Library.

Co-hosted by Shantou Job Network (stzp.cn), the fair aims to provide a direct employment platform connecting graduating students with listed companies and major enterprises in Shantou. Graduating students are advised to make necessary preparations and attend on time.

## Fair Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | Shantou Listed & Major Enterprises Campus Recruitment Fair 2013 |
| Time | 9:00 AM, Friday, November 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Student Activity Hall, 1st Floor, New Library |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office, Shantou University; Shantou Municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau |
| Co-organizer | Shantou Job Network (stzp.cn) |

Graduating students are encouraged to attend promptly and make the most of this opportunity.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
