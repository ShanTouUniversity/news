It is reported that the First Club Culture Month at Shantou University will take place from November 23 to December 23, 2013. Hosted by the Youth League Committee, the event aims to showcase the vibrant culture of student clubs on campus, encourage more students to participate in diverse club activities, broaden their professional knowledge, and enhance their comprehensive abilities.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | STU First Club Culture Month |
| Duration | November 23 – December 23, 2013 |
| Opening Ceremony | 11:00, November 23 |
| Opening Venue | Golden Triangle |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Shantou University |

A series of themed activities will be held during the month, including "Protect Intellectual Property, Inspire Knowledge Innovation." All students are welcome to participate and experience the魅力 of club culture.
