Shantou University’s Business School announced on November 21, 2013, that on-site registration would be available for the China Marketing Manager Assistant Certificate Examination. The entry-level test was scheduled for the morning of November 23 in Room D503. The organizers and school opened the additional registration after students requested another opportunity, with places limited to the first 50 applicants.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Examination | China Marketing Manager Assistant Certificate Examination, entry level |
| Time | 9–10:30 a.m., November 23, 2013 |
| Venue | Room D503 |
| On-site registration | Arrive 30 minutes before the test; RMB 20 fee; 50 places |
| Required documents | Student ID or campus card, plus the original and a copy of national ID |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School)
