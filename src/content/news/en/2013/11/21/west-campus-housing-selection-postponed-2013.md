It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on November 21, 2013, that the unified housing selection for west campus relocation households will be postponed.

The earlier draft arrangement for lot-drawing housing selection, released on November 8 via the campus office information network, had planned a unified selection on November 24 in a tiered classroom of Building E. As the comment period has not yet concluded, the selection will be rescheduled. The confirmed time will be announced in advance on the campus office information network.

The office asked concerned faculty and staff to follow updates on the campus office network and apologized for any inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
