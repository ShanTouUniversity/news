It is reported that the MT Enterprise Innovation Club at the School of Engineering, Shantou University, will host its third "MT-IDEAS" Creative Design Competition, inviting project proposals from all students. Participants form teams of 2–5 members, elect a team captain responsible for naming and coordination.

The competition consists of registration and submission phases. The deadline for registration and project submission is 11:59 PM on November 26, 2013.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | 3rd "MT-IDEAS" Creative Design Competition |
| Organizer | MT Enterprise Innovation Club, School of Engineering |
| Format | Team entry, 2–5 members per team, 1 captain |
| Registration Deadline | November 26, 2013, 24:00 |

All students are welcome to participate and showcase their innovative thinking and teamwork skills.
