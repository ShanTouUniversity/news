Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced a Guangzhou travel subsidy for the Class of 2014 on November 21, 2013. Main-campus undergraduates facing financial hardship could seek reimbursement for round-trip travel to Guangzhou between November and December to take civil-service examinations or attend job fairs organized by the Guangdong Graduate Employment Guidance Center. Reimbursement was capped at RMB 160 per student.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | Financially disadvantaged main-campus undergraduates graduating in 2014 |
| Eligible travel | Guangzhou civil-service examinations or designated job fairs |
| Subsidy | Reimbursement against official round-trip tickets, up to RMB 160 |
| Application submission | Colleges and residential colleges to submit consolidated applications by December 5, 2013 |
| Follow-up claim records | Tickets and claim forms due by December 30 after results were publicized |

Advisors collected applications for review by each school or residential college. Application and claim forms were provided in the OA attachments; recipient lists and personal financial information are not reproduced. A separate payment notice followed in January 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
