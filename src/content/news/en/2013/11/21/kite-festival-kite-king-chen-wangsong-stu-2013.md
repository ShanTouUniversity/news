It is reported that Shantou University will host an autumn kite festival titled "The Wind Is Rising, Let's Fly Kites Together" on November 23 starting at 14:30 on the university's track field.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | From 14:30, November 23, 2013 |
| Venue | STU Track Field |
| Special Guest | Chen Wangsong ("Kite King" holding a Guinness World Record) |
| Activities | Kite performance showcase, student and faculty kite flying |

The event has specially invited Chen Wangsong, a Guinness World Record-holding "kite king," to perform live for students and faculty. Participants are also encouraged to bring their own handcrafted kites to enjoy a pleasant autumn day together.
