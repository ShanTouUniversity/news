Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced a schedule on November 21, 2013, for eligible resettlement households to view renovated homes and assess their location, orientation, layout and size. The two group visits were arranged for the afternoon of November 22 and the morning of November 23 at Longquanyuan and the West District Courtyard.

| Session | Time | Locations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | 4:30–6 p.m., November 22 | Longquanyuan and West District Courtyard |
| Second | 9:30–11 a.m., November 23 | Longquanyuan and West District Courtyard |

Staff who could not attend either session could visit at another time, with Resource Management Office staff available to open the properties. No individual household information is included.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
