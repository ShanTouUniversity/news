It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Chuaik Network will hold the “Dust-Free Week” computer maintenance event from November 23 to 29, 2013, to provide centralized repair and dust-removal services and promote computer maintenance knowledge.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Dust-Free Week: Stay Away from Dust |
| Period | November 23–29, 2013 |
| Participants | All faculty and students |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Chuaik Network |

## How to Request Service

| Group | Method | Period | Contact / Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Faculty and staff | Phone appointment for on-site service | November 25–29 during working hours | Call 13790730025 |
| Students | On-site registration or fill in on-site service form | November 23–24 | Service sites: Jin San Jiao and Zhicheng College hall |

Faculty may call the duty staff for appointments, with technicians dispatched according to the scheduled time; students may bring computers to the service sites or fill in a form for on-site visits in order of registration. Further updates are available on Weibo @Shantou University Chuaik Network.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
