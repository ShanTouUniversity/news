It is reported that Qiu Jie, a 1993 alumnus of the Department of English Language and Literature at Shantou University, will hold a career experience sharing session on the evening of November 25, 2013, at Lecture Hall 204, Building E, to exchange insights on career development and personal growth with current students.

Qiu Jie joined Yantian International Container Terminals in 1997, earned a Master's degree in Human Resource Development from the University of Manchester in the UK in 2003, and became a professional training instructor in 2004. To date, he has trained over 20,000 participants, specializing in communication and expression, team management, and leadership development.
