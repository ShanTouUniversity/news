It is reported that the "Shantou University International Poetry Festival & Hong Kong International Poetry Night 2013 Shantou Station," co-hosted by the Li Ka Shing Foundation and the College of Liberal Arts, will be held on November 25–26 at Shantou University.

## Event Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | November 25–26, 2013 |
| Venue | Shantou University |
| Theme | Rooted in the Land, Igniting the Soul |
| Organizers | Li Ka Shing Foundation, College of Liberal Arts |
| Special Guests | Bei Dao and four renowned poets from around the world |

This year's poetry festival carries the theme "Rooted in the Land, Igniting the Soul," aiming to awaken slumbering hearts through the power of words and unlock the vast potential of international poetry cultural exchange. Renowned poet Bei Dao will join four distinguished poets from around the globe for this international poetry event at STU.
