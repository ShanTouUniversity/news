It is reported that the Changjiang College of Art and Design at Shantou University will host a design dialogue titled "Peking VS Canton" on the evening of November 21, 2013, at the first-floor auditorium of the college.

The event features Professor Wang Shouzhi, Dean of the Changjiang College of Art and Design, in conversation with renowned graphic designer Liu Zhizhi. The two will explore the differences and intersections between Northern (Peking) and Southern (Canton) design philosophies and cultural connotations.
