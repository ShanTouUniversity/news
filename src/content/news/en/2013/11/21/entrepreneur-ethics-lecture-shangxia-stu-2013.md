It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will host a lecture on professional ethics and qualities of outstanding entrepreneurs on the afternoon of November 23.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 14:30–16:30, November 23, 2013 |
| Venue | Room E305, Building E |
| Speakers | Han Rongkui (General Manager), Li Yue (Associate Professor) |
| Attendees | All student party members and league members, Business School |

Han Rongkui is an outstanding alumnus who graduated from the Business School in 2003. He currently serves as general manager of Shantou Sunship Network Co., Ltd., which focuses on human resources services including online recruitment, on-site recruitment, labor dispatch, and headhunting, and has established well-known platforms such as the Shantou Recruitment Network.
