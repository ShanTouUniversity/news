It is reported that the School of Business at Shantou University will host a Business Case Analysis Competition, open to all students across campus. The competition aims to enhance students' business analysis skills and teamwork abilities, providing a platform for those interested in pursuing careers in business analysis.

For registration details, please contact King Wu (email: kingkng@stu.edu.cn).

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | Business Case Analysis Competition |
| Organizer | School of Business, Shantou University |
| Registration | Contact King Wu (kingkng@stu.edu.cn) |

All students interested in business case analysis are encouraged to register.
