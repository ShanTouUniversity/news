It is reported that the Students' Learning Center (SLC) under the Shantou University Student Affairs Office will offer introductory multimedia software courses during weeks 11 through 13, open to full-time undergraduate students on the main campus with a maximum of 8 students per class.

Course schedule:

| Course | Time | Location |
|--------|------|----------|
| Photoshop | Wednesday 15:00–17:00 | SLC 202 |
| Illustrator | Saturday 19:30–21:30 | SLC 202 |
| Office | Thursday 19:30–21:30 | SLC 202 |

All sessions will be held in SLC Room 202 during weeks 11–13. Students are required to bring their own laptops. Interested students may contact the SLC for registration details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
