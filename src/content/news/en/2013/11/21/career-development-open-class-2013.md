It is reported that the free career development workshop "Unlock Your Unlimited Career Potential," co-sponsored by Dell and Enactus (formerly SIFE), will be held on November 23, 2013, from 9:30 to 11:30 AM in Room 303, Building E. The workshop is hosted by the School of Business at Shantou University and features a senior HR manager from China Unicom Shantou Branch as the keynote speaker, providing systematic guidance on resume writing and other essential job-seeking skills.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | "Unlock Your Unlimited Career Potential" Workshop |
| Time | 9:30–11:30 AM, Saturday, November 23, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 303, Building E |
| Speaker | HR Manager, China Unicom Shantou Branch |
| Content | Resume writing and job-seeking skill guidance |
| Organizers | Dell, Enactus, School of Business, Shantou University |

All students interested in improving their career competitiveness are welcome to attend.
