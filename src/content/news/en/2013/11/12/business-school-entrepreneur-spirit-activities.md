It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the Business School has launched a themed series on learning from outstanding entrepreneurs, running from November 11 to 27 on campus under the theme "Excellence in Business, Vision for the World." The program is open to all Youth League members in the Business School.

The initiative aims to help students appreciate business ethics and professional character through the study of exemplary entrepreneurs, encouraging stronger engagement with academic learning and fostering well-rounded development.

## Program Schedule

| Session | Dates | Details |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Lectures and self-directed learning | Nov. 11–27 | Thematic lectures and independent study with compilation of reading materials on entrepreneurial spirit |
| Essay competition | Nov. 11–22 | Reflections of 500–3,000 words based on the study materials; each Youth League branch nominates 5 entries by Nov. 22; prizes include 1 first prize, 2 second prizes, 3 third prizes, and 14 merit awards |
| Speech and sharing session | Nov. 27 | Each branch nominates 1–3 contestants by Nov. 25; presentations are delivered without notes using PPT, limited to 5 minutes each; prizes include 1 first prize, 2 second prizes, 3 third prizes, and 6 merit awards; venue: teaching building |

Expert scholars, Party branch secretaries, and Youth League advisors serve as judges for both competitions.

Youth League branches have drawn up activity plans and are rolling out the themed events as scheduled.
