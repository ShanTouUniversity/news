The STU Academic Affairs Office has announced that the final exam for Calculus B-I in the first semester of the 2013-2014 academic year will be held on Friday, Nov. 15, during the 12th class period.

Exam venues are assigned by section number: class 64124 (158 students) will take the exam in Lecture Hall E-101; class 64125 (132 students) in Lecture Hall 5; and class 64126 (150 students) in the Moot Court. Students are reminded to arrive on time and follow the seating arrangement with alternate seats occupied.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
