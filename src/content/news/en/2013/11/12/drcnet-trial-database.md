It is reported that the Shantou University Library opened a campus-wide trial of DRCNet — the information network of the Development Research Center of the State Council — on November 12. All faculty and students can access the resource until the trial ends on December 31, 2013.

DRCNet is a well-known large-scale Chinese economic information platform. Backed by the rich resources and expert base of the State Council's Development Research Center, and in close cooperation with renowned economic research institutions and business information providers at home and abroad, it aggregates experts and research findings in China's macroeconomy, financial research and industry economics.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Database | DRCNet (Development Research Center of the State Council network) |
| Trial start | November 12, 2013 |
| Trial end | December 31, 2013 |
| Access | Library database page (lib.stu.edu.cn) |

The database provides information on China's economic environment, business opportunities and management cases, as well as policy directions, investment climate and overall economic trends in China's macroeconomic and sectoral spheres.

The library encourages staff and students to make active use of the resource. Feedback on the trial may be sent to the Library's subject services division (tel 86503247, lqxie@stu.edu.cn).
