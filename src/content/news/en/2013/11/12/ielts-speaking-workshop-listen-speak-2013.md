It is reported that the Center for International Languages (CILL) at Shantou University will host an IELTS speaking workshop titled "Listen To Me, Then Speak" on Monday, November 18, 2013, from 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM. The session will be led by Ms. Ana Rosseti and Lynn Chen.

The workshop aims to help students improve their IELTS speaking skills and master effective communication techniques. Due to limited seating, interested students are required to register in advance via Eventbrite. Those who are uncertain about their availability are asked not to reserve a spot; once registered, attendance is expected.
