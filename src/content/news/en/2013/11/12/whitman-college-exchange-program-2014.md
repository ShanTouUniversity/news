It is reported that the Office of International Exchange at Shantou University has opened the selection process for the 2014 Fall exchange program at Whitman College in the United States. One student will be selected to spend a semester studying at Whitman College in Fall 2014.

Eligible applicants must be full-time undergraduate students from the main campus, enrolled in the class of 2011. Applicants are required to rank in the top 5% of their cohort based on the 2012–2013 academic year GPA, and achieve Level 4 proficiency in English. Selection criteria include active participation in campus and community activities, leadership abilities, strong analytical and comprehension skills, and solid English listening, reading, and writing abilities.

Applicants must submit an application form along with a 300–500 word English essay (choose from two provided topics). Qualified students with interest in international exchange are encouraged to apply.
