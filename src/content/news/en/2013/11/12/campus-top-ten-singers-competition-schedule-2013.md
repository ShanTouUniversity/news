The Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Union announced the schedule for the 21st Campus Top Ten Singers Competition on November 12, 2013. The notice listed activities from October 26 through December 21, including freshman host auditions, community rounds, selections by schools and residential colleges, the preliminary round and the final. Some earlier dates had already passed when the schedule was issued.

## Schedule

| Activity | Scheduled date | Venue or arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freshman host interview | October 26 | Meeting Room 726, Building 7 |
| Freshman host selection | Afternoon of October 30 | Meeting Room 726, Building 7 |
| Freshman host training | November 3 | Meeting Room 726, Building 7 |
| Community auditions | Afternoon of November 10 | Golden Triangle |
| School and residential-college selections | By November 15 | Arranged by each unit |
| Community qualifying round | November 16 | Small Stage |
| Preliminary round | November 30 | Great Hall |
| Popularity vote | December 7–21 | Across the STU campus |
| “Heart and Voice” singing activity | December 7–20 | Across the STU campus |
| Final | December 21 | Great Hall |

The 10 participating units—Wen, Law, Business, Science, Engineering, Arts, Medicine, Journalism, Graduate Studies and Zhicheng Residential College—could each nominate two teams. Entries could be solo, duet or group performances; bands could not register independently. Students could also enter the community track based on their residential area. Five contestants would advance to the preliminary round through community auditions and a qualifying round. If a team qualified both through a unit nomination and as one of the five community qualifiers, it would advance as a community qualifier, and its unit could nominate a replacement team.

All preliminary-round contestants were to receive the Outstanding Singer title. Ten teams would advance to the final and receive certificates. The final awards comprised champion, runner-up and third place, as well as Best Costume, Best Stage Presence and Most Popular Contestant. Scores were allocated as follows: song content, 10%; vocal performance, 70%; stage presence—including accompaniment, costumes and stage performance—15%; and audience response, 5%.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of Shantou University and STU Student Union)
