It is reported that Shantou University's first Aerobics Dance Competition has been officially launched. Hosted by the Student Affairs Office and organized by the Aerobics Dance Club, the competition is open to all students and faculty members.

The competition features two categories: the Professional Aerobics Dance Group (mandatory or self-chosen routines, divided into beginner and intermediate levels) and the Colorful Dance Styles Group (other group dance formats). Each team may register one team leader and one coach, with 5 to 16 athletes (male and female participants both welcome).

The preliminary rounds will be held on November 20, and the final on November 30. In the Professional Aerobics Dance Group, the first prize of 600 yuan will be awarded to one team, with additional second prizes to be determined.

Registration closes at 23:59 on November 16. Interested participants should send their registration information to 12twu2@stu.edu.cn. Students and faculty with a passion for dance are encouraged to form teams and showcase the vibrant spirit of STU.
