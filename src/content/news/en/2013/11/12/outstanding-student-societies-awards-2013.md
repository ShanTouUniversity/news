The STU Youth League Committee recently announced the results of the 2012-2013 Outstanding Student Societies evaluation. Following review by the Student Associations Federation and approval by the committee, ten student organizations, including the Psychology Growth Association, are recommended for the "Outstanding Student Society of STU" title.

Additionally, 12 students including Hou Jinhui are recommended for the "Outstanding Student Society President of STU" title, and 6 students including Zhang HARong are recommended for the "Outstanding Student Society Worker of STU" title. The results are subject to a public review period until 12:00 on November 15, 2013. Any objections should be submitted in writing or by phone to the Youth League Committee.
