It is reported that the "Charity 1+1" poverty alleviation and student aid fundraising sale, hosted by the Youth League Committee of Shantou University's School of Business and co-organized by the School's Volunteer Association, will be held on November 23, 2013, at Shantou Zhongshan Park. The theme of this year's event is "Love Fills the Sky, Warmth Reaches Wuqiao."

The event has a 16-year history. Through charity sales and performances, funds are raised to support students in financial need. Each semester, approximately 21 disadvantaged students are sponsored: high school students receive 500 yuan each, middle school students 300 yuan, and primary school students 200 yuan.

Participating teams are required to operate with integrity, donating over 50% of their profits to charity. Students interested in joining are welcome to register as a team and use their youthful energy to spread love and warmth.
