It is reported that Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has announced the midterm exam arrangements for the course MAT1240 Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics (Engineering). The exam is scheduled for periods 3-4 on Friday of week 9 (November 15), with classes split across two exam rooms.

## Exam Room Arrangements

| Class No. | Course | Instructor | Class Location | Exam Location | Students | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 63564 | MAT1240 Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics (Engineering) | Lin Xiaoping | Lecture Hall 3 | E Staircase Classroom 101 | 140 | Seating as directed by the instructor |
| 63565 | MAT1240 Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics (Engineering) | Li Junde | Building D, Room 503 | Moot Court | 113 | — |

The Academic Affairs Office reminded students to sit in alternate seats in the assigned rooms, and that students who have applied for class exemption must still take the exam on time.
