It is reported that the 2013 Shantou University "Intra-College Cup" basketball tournament was held from October 26 to November 23, concluding successfully after nearly a month of intense competition.

## Tournament Results

| Category | Honor | Winner |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Men's | Champion | Business School |
| Men's | Runner-up | College of Engineering |
| Men's | Third place | Medical College |
| Women's | Champion | School of Journalism |
| Women's | Runner-up | College of Arts |
| Women's | Third place | Law School |
| Men's | MVP | Li Yusheng (Business School) |
| Women's | MVP | Bi Ying (School of Journalism) |
| Three-point Contest | Champion | Xu Mingwei (College of Sciences) |

Hosted by the University Youth League Committee, this year's tournament showcased the vigorous spirit and teamwork of STU students.
