It is reported that the PE Department of Shantou University announced on November 26 that the second tennis intercollegiate ranking tournament will take place at the STU tennis courts from December 11 to 15. The event features two categories: men's singles and women's singles, with individual registration.

The tournament adopts a single-set knockout format. Matches are scheduled for Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays. Interested students and staff members must register by December 10.
