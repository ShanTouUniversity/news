It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University announced on November 26, 2013 that, upon approval by the university, the Center for International Studies has been officially renamed the Center for Global Studies.

The renaming reflects the college's intention to broaden the center's research scope from an international perspective to a global one, underscoring its commitment to cross-cultural and cross-regional scholarship in an era of increasing globalization. Existing research programs and academic activities of the center will continue uninterrupted.
