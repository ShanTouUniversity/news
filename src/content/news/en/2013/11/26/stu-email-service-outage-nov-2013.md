A server fault disrupted access to Shantou University email at 10:45 a.m. on November 26, 2013. The Network and Information Center said it was working urgently to resolve the problem, but had not announced a restoration time; further updates would follow.

The center listed 86503400 and stunic@stu.edu.cn for inquiries.
