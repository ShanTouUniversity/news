It is reported that the Shantou University Youth League Committee will hold the 2013 "Youth Environmental Cycling Day" on Saturday, November 30, and is currently recruiting volunteers from across campus.

The event, themed "Waste Sorting Starts with Me," features a cycling route starting from Shantou University and ending at Huaqiao Park. The activity aims to raise environmental awareness among students and the public, promote knowledge of waste classification, and uphold the volunteer spirit of "dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress."

All faculty and students interested in environmental volunteering are encouraged to sign up and contribute to a cleaner campus and city.
