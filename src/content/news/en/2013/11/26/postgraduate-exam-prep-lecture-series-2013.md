It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University has officially launched a series of postgraduate entrance exam preparation lectures for graduating students, aiming to help those interested in further study fully understand the examination process and plan their preparation strategically.

The opening lecture, titled "Why Pursue a Postgraduate Degree?", will be delivered by Vice President Wu Lanhasi on Wednesday, November 27, from 19:30 to 20:00 in the Science Building Lecture Hall. Subsequent sessions will feature Professor Cheng Jiaming and senior instructors from New Oriental, covering English exam review strategies and comprehensive preparation guidance.

The lecture series will span the entire preparation journey—from understanding motivations to mastering practical study techniques—providing systematic support for students planning to take the postgraduate entrance exam.
