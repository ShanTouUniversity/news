It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee issued a notice on November 26, 2013, announcing personnel appointments following a study session at the 14th meeting of the university's Party Standing Committee in 2013.

According to the notice, Comrade Huang Guzi has been appointed as Vice Chair of the Trade Union at Shantou University Medical College, with a six-month probationary period. Comrade Qin Dainian will no longer serve as Vice Chair of the Trade Union at Shantou University Medical College.

The adjustment was made by the university Party Committee in accordance with work requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee)
