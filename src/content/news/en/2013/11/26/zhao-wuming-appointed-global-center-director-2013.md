It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University announced on November 26, 2013 that, upon approval by the university, Zhao Wuming has been appointed as the director of the Center for Global Studies.

The appointment was made in accordance with the operational needs following the renaming of the former Center for International Studies. Zhao Wuming will be responsible for the daily administration, academic planning, and external engagement of the center.
