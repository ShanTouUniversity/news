It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced on November 26 the results of the "Strengthen Study Style, Promote Personal Growth" theme day activity projects. The initiative aims to strengthen academic atmosphere on campus and help college students achieve better personal development. After review, a total of 13 projects were approved, with 10 receiving funding support.
