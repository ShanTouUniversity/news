Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office scheduled a training session on using a thesis similarity-checking system for November 27, 2013. The session was intended to familiarize academic administrators with the system’s functions and operation. At the time, the university planned to purchase the system and use it the following semester to check theses by the 2014 graduating class.

The session was aimed at academic administrators from schools and departments, with other interested faculty members also welcome. It was set for 3–4 p.m. in room E207 of the E Teaching Building.
