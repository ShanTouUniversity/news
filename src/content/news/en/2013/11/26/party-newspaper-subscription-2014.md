Shantou University’s Publicity Department announced its 2014 subscription plan for Party newspapers and journals on November 26, 2013. The university planned to coordinate publicity funds to meet a quota assigned by Shantou and provide designated publications to units at its main campus and to students.

Each school and residential college was assigned one copy each of *People’s Daily*, *Guangming Daily* and *Nanfang Daily*. Administrative units were assigned publications by staff size: units with up to 10 employees were to receive *People’s Daily* and *Nanfang* magazine; those with 11–50 employees would also receive *Nanfang Daily*; units with more than 50 employees would additionally receive *Shantou Daily*. Each Party general branch was assigned two copies of *Nanfang* magazine and one each of *Qiushi* magazine and *Guangming Daily*. Directly affiliated Party branches were assigned one copy of each of the latter three publications. Student subscriptions were set at one copy each of *Nanfang Daily* and *Shantou Daily* per 90 students.

The School of Business, Finance Office and Resource Management Office were assigned two, one and two copies of *Economic Daily*, respectively. Units operating under economic contracts were responsible for their own subscription costs. Units were asked to email their information forms to `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn` by November 28, specifying staff or student numbers as applicable.
