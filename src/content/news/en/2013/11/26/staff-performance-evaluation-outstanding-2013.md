It is reported that the Personnel Office at Shantou University issued a notice on November 26, 2013, announcing the results of the 2012-2013 academic year performance evaluation for faculty and staff.

Following the evaluation process, 149 individuals, including Liu Yuzhou, were assessed as receiving outstanding ratings for the 2012-2013 academic year. The full evaluation results for all faculty and staff will be distributed through their respective units in due course.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
