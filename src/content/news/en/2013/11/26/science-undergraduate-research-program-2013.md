Shantou University’s College of Science opened applications in 2013 for its 2013–14 Student Research Programmes (SRP), designed to develop students’ critical thinking, innovation and research skills through inquiry-based work.

Applicants had to be first- to third-year undergraduates in the college, with the project lead more than a year from graduation. Each project could include one to five students, with cross-year and cross-discipline teams encouraged; each student could join only one project. Topics could be research papers in science, engineering, agriculture or medicine, or invention projects. Students could also propose a topic and seek an adviser.

Project leads were to submit printed and electronic applications to their departments by 5:30 p.m. on December 2. Department contacts were Lu Peiyu (math@stu.edu.cn) for Mathematics, Wang Jiangyong (wangjy@stu.edu.cn) for Physics, Zhang Shilin (o_lxyhxx@stu.edu.cn) for Chemistry and Yang Mulan (mlyang@stu.edu.cn) for Biology. Departmental reviews were due by December 9. Topic lists and application forms were available in the OA attachments.
