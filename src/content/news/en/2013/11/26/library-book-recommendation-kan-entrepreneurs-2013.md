It is reported that the Shantou University Library recommended a new book, "Kans: The Entrepreneur's Path to Growth," on November 26, 2013. The book examines the various obstacles and challenges entrepreneurs encounter on their journey, analyzing why many businesses and individuals struggle to achieve success despite their efforts, and offering insights into how to overcome the critical "kans" (hurdles) along the way to build more mature and competitive enterprises.

The book is suitable for faculty, staff, and students interested in entrepreneurship management, entrepreneurial spirit, and corporate development strategy. Interested readers may borrow it from the library.
