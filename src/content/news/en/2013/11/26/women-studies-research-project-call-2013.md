It is reported that the Women's Studies Research Center at the College of Liberal Arts, Shantou University, issued a research proposal call on November 26, 2013, aiming to advance scholarship on women in Eastern Guangdong culture and to encourage faculty and students to pay closer attention to female artisans in the region.

The initiative invites students to participate individually or in teams, designing and implementing research plans focused on female artisans within Eastern Guangdong's local culture. Proposed studies will examine their handicraft works, cultural contributions, and relevant historical and social backgrounds, seeking to illuminate the cultural value and contemporary significance of these women through field research.

The center expressed hope that such projects would stimulate student interest in women's studies and broaden the scope of regional cultural research in Eastern Guangdong. Interested students may contact the Women's Studies Research Center at the College of Liberal Arts for detailed information and application forms.
