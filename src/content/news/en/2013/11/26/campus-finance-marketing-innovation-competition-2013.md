It is reported that the 1st Shantou University Club Culture Month Campus Finance Marketing Innovation Competition has been officially launched, open to all students across campus.

The event consists of two parts: first, a themed information session titled "Finance and Life: If You Don't Manage Your Money, Your Money Won't Manage You" will be held on Sunday, November 24, from 15:00 to 17:00 in Room E208. Subsequently, on Wednesday, November 27, from 11:00 to 13:30, a consultation and registration booth will be set up at the Golden Triangle, inviting interested students to learn more and sign up.

This competition is one of the key events of STU's inaugural Club Culture Month, aiming to guide students to pay attention to financial knowledge, develop money management awareness, and enhance their innovative thinking and marketing skills through practical competition.
