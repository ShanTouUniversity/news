It is reported that the Network and Information Center at Shantou University issued a notice on November 26, 2013, announcing a training session on the new collaborative office system scheduled for Monday, November 25, from 3:00 PM to 4:00 PM in Room 506, Building E Teaching Building.

The training is open to information officers from all university units who have been designated as system contacts. Topics covered include an introduction and demonstration of the new system's functions, basic operations such as document submission and approval workflows, Q&A sessions, and methods for collecting departmental information, process, and document knowledge directories, along with follow-up arrangements.
