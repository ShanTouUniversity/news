Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department initiated a self-review of Party and government officials’ part-time or full-time positions in enterprises on November 26, 2013. The department circulated rules from the Organization Department of the CPC Guangdong Provincial Committee and asked university units to organize reviews against the requirements.

The review covered serving administrative officials at deputy division level or above; former officials at that level who had not completed retirement procedures; and officials who had resigned from public service or retired. Those in scope were asked to complete a form on enterprise positions and related cleanup measures and submit it to the university department by November 28 for consolidation and reporting to the provincial department.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ The self-review form and circulated policy are available as attachments in the original OA notice.
