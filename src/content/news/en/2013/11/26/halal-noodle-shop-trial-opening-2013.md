It is reported that the halal noodle shop at Shantou University will begin trial operations on December 2. Located at the East Gate Bridge, at the intersection of Zhicheng College and the Fourth Canteen, the shop will provide a dining option for faculty and students who follow halal dietary practices.

## Trial Operations

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Start date | December 2, 2013 |
| Location | East Gate Bridge, at the intersection of Zhicheng College and the Fourth Canteen |
| Opening hours | 10:00–13:30; 16:00–20:00 |
| Issuing unit | Resource Management Office |
| Issue date | November 26, 2013 |

The Resource Management Office welcomes faculty and students to visit and asks diners to respect relevant customs and follow the dining room's rules. Bringing outside non-halal food into the dining room, smoking and drinking are prohibited. Diners are also asked to keep quiet and avoid making loud noises.
