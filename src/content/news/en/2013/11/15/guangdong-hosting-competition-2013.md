To leverage the educational and formative function of campus culture and inspire young students to contribute to the construction of a prosperous Guangdong and a culturally strong province, the Communist Youth League Guangdong Provincial Committee has announced the launch of the 8th Guangdong College Student Hosting Competition, with registration now open.

Students interested in participating should submit their application forms and competition DVDs to the University Youth League Committee office by 11:00 on November 18, 2013. The University Youth League Committee will conduct selective recruitment based on the number of applicants and form an STU representative team.

The Guangdong College Student Hosting Competition is one of the most influential campus cultural events in the province's universities, aiming to discover and cultivate outstanding hosting talent, showcase the spirit and abilities of contemporary college students, and inject new energy into campus culture. Students with passion and aspiration are encouraged to apply.
