The results of the 17th FLTRP Cup National College Student English Debate Competition were recently announced, with Yang Kaiying from the Law School and Huang Yilin from the News School of Shantou University winning third prize at the national finals.

The national finals were held in Beijing from October 27 to November 3, 2013, featuring top debaters from six regional zones including Tsinghua University, Peking University, Fudan University, Zhejiang University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Sun Yat-sen University, and Beihang University. The two students advanced from the intense university selection contest in May to the regional competition in September, earning their spot at the national finals, where they demonstrated strong English debating skills and critical thinking abilities.

Hosted by Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press, the FLTRP Cup is one of the most prestigious national English debate competitions in China. The excellent performance of the two STU debaters highlights the solid professional foundation and comprehensive abilities of Shantou University students.
