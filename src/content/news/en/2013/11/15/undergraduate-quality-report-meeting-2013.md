The General Administration Office and Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on November 15, 2013, that a meeting on annual institutional data collection and the undergraduate teaching quality report was scheduled for November 18. It was intended for staff involved in STU’s talent-development quality report, with relevant departments and units able to send representatives.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | 2013 institutional data collection and undergraduate teaching quality report |
| Time | 3 p.m., November 18, 2013 |
| Venue | Administrative Center, Room 201 |
| Issuing offices | General Administration Office and Academic Affairs Office |

The notice did not provide meeting outcomes or subsequent work results; this report records only the arrangement announced at the time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Administration Office and Academic Affairs Office)
