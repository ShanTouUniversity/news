Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office published the evaluation result for its West-Campus flood-drain pipeline installation project on November 15, 2013. Following review by the university’s bidding and tendering leading group, Shantou Jinhai Construction Co. was selected with a contract price of RMB 63,105.
