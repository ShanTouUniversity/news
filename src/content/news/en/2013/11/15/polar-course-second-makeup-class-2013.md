Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced the second make-up session for the Institute of Marine Biology’s Polar Course (Class 64323) on November 15, 2013. The class was scheduled for periods A–B on November 18 (Monday of Week 10) in Room D502.

This was separate from the first make-up session, which had been scheduled for November 17 in Room D503. Students enrolled in the course were asked to share the update.
