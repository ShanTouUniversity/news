It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Union will hold the community qualifier for the X-Project “Campus Top 10 Singers” competition on November 16, 2013, to enliven residential community life and select contestants for the 21st Campus Top 10 Singers preliminary round.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Community qualifier for the X-Project Campus Top 10 Singers Competition |
| Time | 19:30–22:00, November 16, 2013 |
| Venue | Small Stage |
| Contestants | See OA attachment |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Student Union |

## Awards

| Award | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Top 5 | Five contestants will advance to the 21st Campus Top 10 Singers Competition |
| Outstanding Contestants | All entrants in the qualifier will receive the title of Outstanding Contestant and a certificate |

All faculty and students are welcome to attend. The contestant list is available in the OA attachment.
