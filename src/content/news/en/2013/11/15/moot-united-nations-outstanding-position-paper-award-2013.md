It is reported that the 10th China Model United Nations Conference was held at Guangdong University of Foreign Studies from November 8 to 10, 2013, attracting 446 delegates from 116 universities across Australia, Hong Kong, Macau and mainland China — a record-breaking scale for the competition. The delegation from Shantou University, dispatched by the Law School under the guidance of instructor Lai Yulin, won the Outstanding Position Paper Award.

## Competition Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

| STU Award | Outstanding Position Paper Award |
| Winning Students | Yu Zhuochen (Public Administration, Class of 2011), Zhang Wenlang (Law, Class of 2012) |
| Faculty Advisor | Lai Yulin |

This year's conference was the largest in the event's history, with delegates from universities across multiple countries and regions gathering in Guangzhou to discuss international issues. The Shantou University team distinguished themselves through outstanding position paper writing, earning recognition from the judges.
