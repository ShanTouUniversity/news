It is reported that the College of Journalism and Communication of Shantou University will host an academic lecture by Farland Chang on November 18, 2013, at the University Library Lecture Hall.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture | Farland Chang Academic Lecture |
| Time | 19:00–21:00, November 18, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | College of Journalism and Communication |

## About the Speaker

Farland Chang previously served as a national correspondent for NBC Network News and as an economic news anchor for CNN International. He is one of the few Asian Americans who has secured a place in the American television broadcasting industry. He has won the Emmy Award, the highest honor in American television, a distinction comparable to the Academy Awards in film and the Grammy Awards in music.

All faculty, staff and students are welcome to attend.
