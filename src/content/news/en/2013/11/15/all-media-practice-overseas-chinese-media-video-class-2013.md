Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication scheduled a Chinese-language video open class, “Overseas Chinese Media in the All-Media Era,” for November 20, 2013. The session was set for 15:00–16:00 in E Building Tiered Classroom 203.

The class was to discuss the convergence of traditional and new media, digital transformation among overseas Chinese-language newspapers, and the wider media-fusion environment. A representative of Korea’s Xinhua Daily was scheduled to join faculty and students by video. The session was open to the university community.
