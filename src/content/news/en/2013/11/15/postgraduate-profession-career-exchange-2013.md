It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University will hold an exchange event on the evening of November 16, 2013, under the theme "Will Your Major and Career Hold Hands?" The event aims to encourage graduate students to actively reflect on the relationship between their academic programs and career paths, enrich their extracurricular lives, foster mutual understanding, and enhance cultural literacy and personal cultivation.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Theme | Will Your Major and Career Hold Hands? |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, Saturday, November 16, 2013 |
| Organizer | Graduate School, Shantou University |

All graduate students are welcome to attend and engage in discussions on the relationship between academic study and career development.
