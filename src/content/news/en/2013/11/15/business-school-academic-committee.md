It is reported that due to personnel changes and work needs, the Business School of Shantou University decided at an academic committee meeting on November 8, 2013 to adjust the membership of its academic committee. The decision was announced in a document (STU Shang Fa [2013] No. 14) issued on November 15.

## Adjusted Composition

| Position | Members |
| -------- | ------- |
| Advisors | Xu Erming, Xu Deqi |
| Director | Lyu Yuan |
| Secretary | Hu Shaodong |

The committee has nine members in total, listed in order of surname stroke count, including Bu Xiangzhi, Tian Guang, Song Xiaobao, Lin Danming, Ouyang Feng, Xu Zongling, Huang Yan and Zeng Qiang.

The document also specifies that the current committee was established in June 2012 for a three-year term. It was addressed to the Academic Committee of Shantou University.
