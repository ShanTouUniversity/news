It is reported that Shantou University's Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Committee issued a pre-appointment publicity on November 15. Following a study session at the 14th meeting of the university's Party Standing Committee in 2013, the university plans to appoint Comrade Huang Guzi as Vice Chair of the Trade Union at Shantou University Medical College. The appointment is currently in its public notice stage.

In accordance with the Trial Measures for Pre-appointment Publicity of Cadres at Shantou University, the university has published basic information about the nominee and is soliciting feedback from all campus units during the publicity period. Units or individuals with differing opinions on the proposed appointment may report them to the department before the deadline.

## Feedback Channels and Requirements

According to the notice, reports must meet specific formal requirements:

| Reporter | Requirements | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit | Written form, stamped with official seal | Before November 21, 2013 |
| Individual | In person, by phone, or in writing; real name required | Before November 21, 2013 |

The university stated that if no issues affecting the appointment are found when the publicity period ends, appointment procedures will proceed accordingly. The contact phone number listed is 0754-86502345, and the email address is o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.
