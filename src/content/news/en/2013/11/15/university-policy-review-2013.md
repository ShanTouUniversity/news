Shantou University launched a university-wide review of institutional policies in November 2013. Each unit was asked to examine its current rules and identify which should remain in force, be revised or be retired. The initiative followed requirements from the provincial education-practice office and the University Party Committee.

## Review priorities

Units were also asked to propose new policies, including measures on allocating educational resources, assessing risks in major decisions, connecting Party officials with the community, preventing academic misconduct and evaluating officials’ work style. Plans were to include implementation timelines.
