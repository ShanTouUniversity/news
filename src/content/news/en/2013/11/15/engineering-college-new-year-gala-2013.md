It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University will hold its 2013 Welcome Gala on November 17, 2013, under the theme "New Dreams Set Sail, Engineering Creates Brilliance." All faculty, staff and students are welcome to attend.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | College of Engineering 2013 Welcome Gala |
| Theme | New Dreams Set Sail, Engineering Creates Brilliance |
| Time | 19:30, November 17, 2013 |
| Venue | University Auditorium |
| Audience | All faculty, staff and students |

The welcome gala is an annual tradition of the College of Engineering, aimed at welcoming new students and showcasing the talents and spirit of engineering students through cultural performances, helping newcomers build a sense of belonging and identity with the college.
