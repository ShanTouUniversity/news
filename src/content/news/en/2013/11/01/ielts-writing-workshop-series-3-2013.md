It is reported that the Center for Intensive English Language Learning (CILL) at Shantou University will host the third workshop in its IELTS preparation series, titled "Developing Writing Skills," on November 6 from 16:00 to 18:00, aiming to help students improve their IELTS writing abilities.

The workshop is open to students preparing for the IELTS exam who wish to raise their writing band score. Due to limited seating, participants must register in advance via Eventbrite. Those uncertain about their ability to attend should refrain from booking; once registered, attendance is expected.
