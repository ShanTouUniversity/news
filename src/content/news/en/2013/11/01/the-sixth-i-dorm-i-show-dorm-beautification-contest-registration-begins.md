Shantou University's 6th "I-Dorm, I-Show" Dormitory Beautification Contest has officially opened for registration. This year's contest, themed "Sparkling Rooms, Show Your Dorm," is hosted by the Student Affairs Office and co-organized by the Residence Counselor Office and the Dormitory Student Committee, open to all undergraduate students.

The contest aims to enrich campus cultural life, promote an eco-friendly, healthy, and harmonious community atmosphere, and encourage students to creatively decorate their living spaces while showcasing youthful energy and team spirit.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Contest Theme | Sparkling Rooms, Show Your Dorm |
| Preliminary Round | November 23 |
| Preliminary Venue | 5th Floor, Building D |
| Finals | November 30 |
| Finals Venue | Science Report Hall |
| Host | Student Affairs Office |
| Co-organizers | Residence Counselor Office, Dormitory Student Committee |

Students can register through multiple channels: on-site registration on November 4, paper forms collected at bulletin board drop-boxes across campus residences, or by downloading the electronic form and emailing it to the designated address.
