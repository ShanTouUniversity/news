The pre-registration for the student resonance activity at Shantou University's iconic Truth Bell has closed. After a selection process, 764 students have been chosen to participate in this symbolic campus cultural event.

The university stated that the Truth Bell is an important cultural landmark of STU, and the trial resonance activity offers students a unique opportunity to engage with this meaningful campus tradition. Selected students are reminded to bring their campus ID cards and arrive 5 minutes early to complete the resonance under staff guidance.

|------|---------|
| Activity | Truth Bell Trial Run Student Resonance |
| Number of Selected Students | 764 |
| Required Item | Campus ID Card |
| Arrival Time | 5 minutes early |
| Issuing Body | Student Affairs Office |
