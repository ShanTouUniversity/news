The Volunteer Association of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University launched the "Clean Plate Campaign" on November 2, responding to the national call for building a resource-saving campus. The initiative encourages students and faculty to practice civilized dining, reduce food waste, and say no to waste on the tip of the tongue.

On the day of the event, volunteers set up information booths at the Second Cafeteria, Third Cafeteria, and Jin San Jiao, handing out advocacy flyers and guiding students to develop the habit of finishing their meals.
