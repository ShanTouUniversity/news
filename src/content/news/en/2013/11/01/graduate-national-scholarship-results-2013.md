It is reported that the Graduate School issued Document No. 05 [2013] on November 1, 2013, announcing the results of the 2013 Postgraduate National Scholarship at Shantou University.

Following the September 30 call for review, the process involved individual applications, preliminary recommendations by colleges (departments/institutes), review by the university’s Postgraduate National Scholarship Review Leading Group and a public announcement without objection. The final list is available in the OA attachment and withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The final list is available on the OA system.
