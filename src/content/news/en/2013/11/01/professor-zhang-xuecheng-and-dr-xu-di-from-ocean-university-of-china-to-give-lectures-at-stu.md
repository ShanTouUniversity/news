The College of Marine Life Sciences at Ocean University of China will send Prof. Zhang Xuecheng and Dr. Xu Di to Shantou University for academic talks on November 4 from 10:00 to 11:30 AM in Room 504 of the Science and Technology Building. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

Prof. Zhang Xuecheng is a Level II Professor and doctoral supervisor at Ocean University of China. He previously served as vice chairman of the Chinese Phycological Society, and his research focuses on seaweed biology and genetic breeding. Dr. Xu Di is a lecturer at the same college, with postdoctoral training at the University of Kochi in Japan and visiting scholar experience at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln in the United States.
