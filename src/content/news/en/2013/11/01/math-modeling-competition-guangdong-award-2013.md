The results of the 2013 National College Student Mathematical Modeling Contest Guangdong Division have been announced, and students from Shantou University delivered outstanding performances. Sponsored by the Ministry of Education, this competition is one of the largest extracurricular science and technology events for college students in China.

STU sent six teams to compete in the Guangdong division. The results are as follows: six teams won first prize, five teams won second prize, two teams won third prize, and eight additional teams received the Certificate of Participation. Professors Wèi Cáimǐn and Lǐ Jiàn from the College of Science served as coaches, leading the students through intensive preparation and ultimately achieving remarkable results.
