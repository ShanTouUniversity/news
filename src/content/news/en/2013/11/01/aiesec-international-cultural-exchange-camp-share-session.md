This past summer, the Shantou University AIESEC team worked alongside overseas volunteers from Egypt, Italy, Germany, and Vietnam to run an International Cultural Exchange Summer Camp at Dongxia Middle School in Shantou City. The program featuredspecial cultural teaching, Teochew culture tours, hands-on Teochew cultural experiences, and parent-child interactive activities. The camp was a great success and attracted coverage from local media including the Shantou Daily, Shantou Special Zone Evening News, and Jinri Shixian.

A results sharing session will take place on November 3 from 19:30 to 20:50 in the Library Auditorium, where camp participants will share their experiences and Chinese volunteers will present their reflections.
