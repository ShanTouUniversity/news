The Shantou University Physical Education Department issued a cheer campaign notice on November 1, 2013, encouraging spectators attending the upcoming men's basketball friendly between the "Wanshijingang" team and Hunan Normal University on November 2 to dress in team colors.

Orange and black are the primary colors of the Wanshijingang team logo. The department called on students and faculty members to wear clothing featuring these colors to create a vibrant home-court atmosphere. The appeal complements the event announcement published on October 30.

## Event and Cheer Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Matchup | Shantou University Wanshijingang vs. Hunan Normal University Men's Basketball |
| Time | November 2, 2013 (Sat), 19:30 |
| Venue | STU Gymnasium |
| Admission | Free |
| Doors open | 19:00 |
| Dress code | Orange or black attire to support the team |
| Cheerleading seats | 40 temporary seats, first come first served |
| Early seating | Spectators wishing to sit near the court should be seated by 18:45 |
| Halftime show | Performance by the Dance Club |
| Issued by | Physical Education Department |

The 40 cheerleading seats are limited. Interested spectators should arrive early to secure a spot. Staff will provide a brief orientation before the game to help maximize the cheering impact.
