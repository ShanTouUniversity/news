The Chinese Research Institute of Polar Studies will send Prof. He Jianfeng, director of the Planning and Science-Technology Division and former station master of the Yellow River Arctic Station, to Shantou University for an academic lecture on polar bioecology on November 2 from 10:10 AM to 12:00 PM in Room D503. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
