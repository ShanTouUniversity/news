In preparation for the 2014 winter holiday travel season, the railway authority will activate a verification system for student train ticket discount cards. Cards that do not meet the required information standards will not be eligible for discounted tickets. The Academic Affairs Office has outlined the following arrangements.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Freshmen Information Entry Deadline | Before November 6 |
| Freshmen Card Processing Start Date | November 11 |
| Freshmen Card Fee | 7 RMB per card |
| Freshmen Process | Collection by major for unified data entry |
| Other Grades Recharge Period | November 5-6 |
| Recharge Location | 3rd Floor, Administrative Center, Academic Affairs Office Window |
| Service Hours | 8:30-11:30, 14:45-17:00 |
| Recharge Fee | Free (once per year) |

New freshmen (class of 2013) must log in to the Student Affairs System to register their travel route information before November 6. Other grades of students need only bring their student IDs to the Academic Affairs Office on the 3rd floor of the Administrative Center for free recharge during the designated hours.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
