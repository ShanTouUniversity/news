The Business School at Shantou University will conduct a transfer major assessment for its International Business Management program on November 13, selecting up to 15 students from across campus.

Applicants are required to have a good English proficiency and no failed or retaken courses in their completed studies. The assessment consists of an English oral test and a business knowledge component, conducted through a review and interview process, with admission decided on merit.

The International Business Management track is a distinctive program within the Business School, emphasizing international perspective and business practice. Students in this program have opportunities to study at partner universities abroad and receive priority consideration for international competitions and exchange programs.

Students interested in applying should register at the Business School Academic Affairs Office (Room 204, Wenxi Building) by 3:00 PM on November 8. The assessment will take place at 2:30 PM on Wednesday, November 13.
