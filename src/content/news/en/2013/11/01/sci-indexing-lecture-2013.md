It is reported that Shantou University Library will hold an SCI lecture on November 4. Lin Xiao, a senior trainer at Thomson Reuters, will discuss the use of research information in topic selection, literature retrieval and academic publishing.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | November 4, 2013 (Monday), 19:30–21:00 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Speaker | Lin Xiao, senior trainer at Thomson Reuters |
| Organizer | Shantou University Library |

## Topics

Using specific research topics as examples, the lecture will introduce applications of SCI in research, including:

- Selecting research topics with SCI, understanding the origins and development of research fields, and developing research ideas;
- Identifying highly productive researchers and institutions in a field through literature data and following research frontiers;
- Tracking the latest developments related to a research topic and staying informed about international research trends;
- Managing and making effective use of research information accumulated over time;
- Selecting suitable academic journals for submission based on a research direction;
- Quickly and accurately generating in-text and reference-list citations in line with journal requirements, reducing rejections caused by formatting problems.

According to the notice, Lin Xiao has long provided training in scientific information retrieval, technical literature analysis, academic writing and submission, and patent analysis and research.
