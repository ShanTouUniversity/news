The 2013 International Robotics Competition (IRC) was held from October 24 to 27 in Gyeonggi Province, South Korea. Shantou University's School of Science robotics team participated as part of China's national delegation, achieving excellent results.

Zhang Jiaxun, a student from the Department of Mathematics at the School of Science, along with team members guided by Professor Gu Minqiang, won first prize in the Robotic Object Transport Competition (individual event) and took runner-up in the International Championship team competition, emerging as one of the top performers on the Chinese delegation.

The IRC is the largest robotics competition in South Korea, established in 2006 and co-hosted by the Korean Ministry of Knowledge Economy and the Korean Robot Industry Development Association. Each year, more than 4,000 teams from around the world compete.
