It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University, in coordination with the STU Hospital, has launched the 2013 autumn flu vaccination campaign. The Immunization Room of the Health Care Department at the STU Hospital is now accepting appointments.

## Vaccination Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Vaccination Period | November 7–12, during regular working hours |
| Location | Immunization Room, Health Care Department, STU Hospital |
| Target Group | Children aged 6 months and above, and adults |
| Dosage | One dose (0.5ml/dose) for children above 3 years old and adults |
| Contact | 82902318 |

The flu vaccine can effectively prevent seasonal influenza and protect the health of faculty and students. All members of the STU community are encouraged to get vaccinated and jointly maintain public health on campus.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
