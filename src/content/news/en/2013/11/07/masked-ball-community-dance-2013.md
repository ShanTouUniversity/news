Shantou University's third community masked ball will be held on the evening of November 10, 2013, from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM on the basketball court. The event is themed "An Encounter with You" and is open to all faculty and students on campus.

The ball features nine carefully curated segments including Time Freeze, Encounter and Hand in Hand, Five-Fruit Party, Rose Promise, Jelly Wine Tasting, Waltz for All, Fantasy Magic Show, Latin Dance Fever, and the much-anticipated "I Want to Confess" segment, offering a diverse and engaging program designed to foster a relaxed and enjoyable platform for students to connect and strengthen community bonds on campus.
