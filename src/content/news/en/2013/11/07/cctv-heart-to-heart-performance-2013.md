It is reported that the reservation for the China Central Television (CCTV) "Heart to Heart" art troupe outreach performance has closed. The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University released the list of 50 successful applicants on November 7. The performance will be held at 15:00 on Friday, November 8 at Huaqiao Park in Shantou.

## Reservation Result

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | CCTV "Heart to Heart" art troupe outreach performance |
| Result | 50 students confirmed, list available in attachment |
| Time | November 8, 2013 (Friday) 15:00 |
| Venue | Huaqiao Park, Shantou |
| Issuing unit | Student Affairs Office |
| Issue date | November 7, 2013 |

## Boarding and Entry Guidelines

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Boarding time | November 8 (Friday) 13:45 |
| Boarding location | Open area in front of the School Motto Monument |
| Dress code | Dress neatly and board on time |
| Belongings | Avoid carrying briefcases, handbags or metal items to ensure quick entry |
| Prohibited items | Umbrellas, drinks, food and promotional materials |

The Student Affairs Office reminds successful applicants to gather on time and enter in an orderly manner.

> ⚠️ The list of 50 successful applicants is available as an attachment on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
