Distinguished Professor Hsiao Xiyan from National Taiwan Ocean University will deliver a lecture titled "Aquaculture Research and Paper Publication — Experience Sharing" on November 11, 2013, from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM in Room 419 of the Science and Technology Building at Shantou University.

Professor Hsiao holds the title of Lifetime Distinguished Professor at Taiwan's Ministry of Education, one of only 31 such appointments across Taiwan. He also serves as a Lifetime Distinguished Professor at Providence University and is a member of two of the most important international committees in the world shrimp and fish nutrition field. Professor Hsiao earned his undergraduate degree from National Taiwan Ocean University and later received his master's and doctoral degrees from Texas Tech University, establishing an internationally recognized reputation in the field of aquaculture and nutrition.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
