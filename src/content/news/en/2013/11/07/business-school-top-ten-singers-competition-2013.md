The Business School at Shantou University has officially launched its "New Voices Carnival" Top Ten Singers Competition, aiming to enrich campus cultural life, promote spiritual civilization, and showcase the youthful spirit of Business School students.

The competition consists of preliminary and final rounds. All undergraduate students of the school are eligible to register for the preliminaries. After selection through the preliminary round, fifteen finalists will advance to the finals, where the Business School's Top Ten Singers will be crowned. The two outstanding performers will be chosen to represent the Business School in the university-level Top Ten Singers preliminary competition.

All students are encouraged to participate and showcase their talents.
