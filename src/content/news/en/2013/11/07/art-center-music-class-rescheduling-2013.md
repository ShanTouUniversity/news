Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced schedule changes for instrumental classes at the Arts Education Center on November 7, 2013. Four oboe and clarinet sessions were rescheduled because instructor Liu Guanghui was traveling for work. All make-up classes were arranged for November 24 in Music Room STU203.

## Revised Schedule

| Class | Course | Original session | Make-up session |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 62602 | Oboe Performance | November 13, periods 3–4 | November 24, periods 1–2 |
| 62602 | Oboe Performance | November 20, periods 3–4 | November 24, periods 3–4 |
| 64351 | Clarinet Performance | November 18, periods 8–9 | November 24, periods 5–6 |
| 62600 | Clarinet Performance | November 18, periods A–B | November 24, periods 7–8 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
