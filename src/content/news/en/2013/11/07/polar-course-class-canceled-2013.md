The Academic Affairs Office announced on November 7, 2013, that a session of the Institute of Marine Biology’s Polar Course (Class 64323), scheduled for periods 3–4 on November 9 in Room D503, had been canceled because an invited expert became unavailable. No make-up date had been set when the notice was issued.
