The National Natural Science Foundation of China-sponsored Symposium on Inorganic Chemistry for Young Scholars, hosted by Shantou University, will be held in Shantou, Guangdong from November 11 to 12, 2013. The conference will invite distinguished experts, including academicians of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, as well as young scholars actively working at the frontier of inorganic chemistry and related fields, to discuss the development trends and key scientific issues in modern inorganic chemistry.

The opening ceremony and plenary lectures will take place on the morning of November 11 at 8:45 AM in the Auditorium of the Shantou University Library. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
