It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has recently announced the project approval results for the 2013–2014 Student Community X Plan. After reviewing project proposals and defense presentations, a total of nine projects were approved for support, including the "Community Masquerade Ball."

The Student Community X Plan aims to enrich campus community cultural life and develop students' organizational coordination skills and innovation awareness. Selected project teams will organize various activities throughout the new academic year, contributing to the development of student community culture and adding vibrancy to campus life.

All approved project teams are encouraged to prepare carefully and ensure smooth implementation of their activities.
