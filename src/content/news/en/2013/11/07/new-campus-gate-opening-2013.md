Shantou University's new campus gate construction project has successfully passed acceptance inspection as of November 1, 2013, and is scheduled to officially open on November 11.

The new campus gate features east and west entrances. The west entrance is now open for both pedestrian and vehicular traffic. The east entrance will remain temporarily closed pending completion of some facilities. A traffic signal system at the new campus gate has also been activated; motor vehicle drivers are required to follow campus traffic regulations and slow down.

Bicycle parking spaces near the new gate are limited and intended only for temporary use by faculty and students. Long-term parking is not permitted. The old main gate will be closed simultaneously with the opening of the new gate.
