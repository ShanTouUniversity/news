Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced the first make-up schedule for three suspended History of Science classes offered by the Institute of Higher Education on November 7, 2013. Each session was set for periods A–B, on November 10, 16 or 17.

## Make-Up Schedule

| Class | Date | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 64488 | November 16 (Saturday, Week 9) | Room E208 |
| 64489 | November 10 (Sunday, Week 9) | E Building Tiered Classroom 201 |
| 64490 | November 17 (Sunday, Week 10) | E Building Tiered Classroom 201 |

Students were asked to check the schedule for their class number and share the information with classmates.
