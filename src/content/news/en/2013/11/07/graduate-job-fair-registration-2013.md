It is reported that in accordance with the requirements of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University is launching the 2014 graduate employment supply-and-demand meet-and-greet events, with the national graduate employment online platform fully rolling out network recruitment and诚信 (integrity) resume services.

## Key Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration URL | http://www.gradjob.com.cn/cms/index.html |
| Submission Deadline | Before November 18, 2013 |
| Review Responsibility | Employment counselors at each college |
| Review Focus | Ensuring resume information is truthful and accurate |

All 2014 graduating students are required to log in and complete their personal resumes before the deadline. Employment counselors at each college will review submissions to ensure accuracy. Students are encouraged to seize this employment opportunity.
