It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University has issued a notice reminding undergraduate students who selected the (4407) Shantou City Admissions Office as their exam registration point—including those admitted through recommendation—to pay attention to the following on-site confirmation arrangements.

## Confirmation Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmation Period | November 10–14, 2013; 8:30 AM–11:30 AM and 2:30 PM–5:00 PM daily |
| Location | Shantou Education Bureau, 1st Floor (Block 35, Fengze Village, Longhu District, Shantou) |
| Required Procedures | In-person identity verification, electronic photography, and exam fee payment |

Applicants must appear in person to complete all confirmation procedures. Substitutions are not permitted. Students are advised to plan their schedules accordingly and arrive at the designated location on time.
