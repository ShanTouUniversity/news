It is reported that the opening ceremony for the Wanbian Jingang Air-Membrane Basketball Training Hall, hosted by the Sports Department of Shantou University, was held recently. A total of 40 currently enrolled students applied through campus recruitment to participate in the event.

## Venue Overview

Located next to Building G dormitory, the training hall adopts pod-type air membrane technology and can accommodate approximately 2,000 spectators, providing a new indoor sports training facility for students and faculty.

## Event Schedule

Participating students gathered at the main gate at 15:30 and entered the venue in an orderly manner.
