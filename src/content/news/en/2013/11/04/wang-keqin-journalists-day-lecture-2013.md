In commemoration of China's 14th Journalists' Day on November 8, the Chen Chang Jiang School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University has invited renowned investigative journalist Wang Keqin to deliver a lecture titled "Why Do We Do Journalism" on the morning of November 6 at the Library Auditorium.

Widely regarded as "the most impactful nuclear submarine" in Chinese journalism, Wang is known for his investigative reports that have repeatedly sparked public discourse and driven institutional progress on key social issues. In this lecture, he will share his extensive professional experience and explore with students the ideals and social responsibilities of the journalism profession.

The lecture is open to all faculty and students. Those interested in journalism are encouraged to attend.
