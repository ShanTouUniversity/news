The Shantou University Youth League Committee will host "Career Battle," a mock interview reality show, on the evening of November 8 at 7:30 PM in the Library Auditorium, as part of the 13th annual "Face Your Future" Mock Interview Series.

Inspired by popular television job-hunting programs, the event incorporates STU campus elements and aims to provide students with a fresh, practical interview experience. Participants will face a panel of interviewers from diverse industries, receiving valuable career advice and interview feedback.

The event is open to all faculty and students. Everyone is encouraged to attend and gain hands-on interview experience in a simulated professional environment.
