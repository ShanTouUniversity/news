It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on November 4, 2013, regarding the purchase and recharge of student train discount cards for postgraduates, in line with the Ministry of Education’s notice that the railway department will activate a verification system during the 2014 winter vacation. Cards without properly entered information will not be eligible for discounted tickets.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicable to | 2013 postgraduates who need to purchase a discount card |
| Information entry | By November 6, log in to the Student Affairs System (“My Affairs” → “Fill in Academic Information”) to enter the boarding station (train station at family address) and fill in the travel section on the postgraduate student ID (must match the system entry) |
| Card processing | From November 11, by program class to the Graduate School Youth League Committee, 4th floor, Administration Center |
| Consequence of missing entry | Cards without entered information cannot be processed |
| Issued by | Graduate School |

Regulations from the Ministry of Railways and the Ministry of Education on student discounted tickets are in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The attachments are available on the OA system.
