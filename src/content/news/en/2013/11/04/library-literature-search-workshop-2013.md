The Shantou University Library will host a lecture on "Literature Review, Literature Search, and Statistical Analysis" on November 5 from 4:00 PM to 5:30 PM in the Library Lecture Hall. The session, led by librarian Xie Luqing, is part of the library's "Subject Literature Services" lecture series and is open to all faculty and students.

The lecture focuses on practical demonstrations using major databases such as CNKI (China National Knowledge Infrastructure), Engineering Village, and Web of Science. It aims to help graduate students and senior undergraduates master systematic literature search strategies and learn to utilize bibliometric and statistical analysis tools provided by these databases to conduct macro-level assessments of research trends in their fields.
