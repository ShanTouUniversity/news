It is reported that the English Language Center (ELC) of Shantou University will launch a series of English proficiency workshops in November, including sessions on TOEFL iBT writing skills and resume writing, aimed at enhancing students' academic English abilities and career competitiveness.

## Workshop Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| TOEFL iBT Writing Skills Workshop | November 8, 15:30–17:00, CILL, presented by Ms. Gena Rhoades & Kristine Adams |
| Resume Writing Skills Workshop | November 12, 10:00–11:30, CILL, presented by Ms. Catherine Bai |
| Registration | Via Eventbrite online |
| Note | Limited seats available; attendees must attend after registration |

Both workshops will be held at the CILL (Center for International Language Learning). Participants are required to register in advance through the Eventbrite platform. Due to limited seating, those who have registered are expected to attend; please cancel your registration promptly if you are unable to attend.
