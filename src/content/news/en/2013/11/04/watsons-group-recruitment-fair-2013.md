It is reported that the Cheung Kong Holdings (Watsons Group) 2013 Campus Recruitment Fair will be held at Shantou University in mid-November 2013. Notable companies including Hutchison Whampoa Property Group will attend the fair, offering employment opportunities to fresh graduates.

## Fair Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Cheung Kong Holdings (Watsons Group) 2013 Campus Recruitment Fair |
| Participating Companies | Hutchison Whampoa Property Group and others |
| Time | Mid-November 2013 |
| Venue | Shantou University |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

All graduating students are encouraged to prepare thoroughly and make the most of this valuable employment opportunity. Interested candidates are advised to learn about the requirements of target companies in advance and attend the fair on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
