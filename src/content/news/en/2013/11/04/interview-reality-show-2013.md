It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University will hold the finale of its 13th Mock Interview Series — a career reality show titled "Battle for Careers" — on the evening of November 8, 2013, at the Library Lecture Hall.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | "Battle for Careers" Interview Reality Show |
| Series | 13th "Facing My Future" Mock Interview Series |
| Time | 19:30, Friday, November 8, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee |

The event draws inspiration from high-rated television job-hunting programs and incorporates campus elements unique to Shantou University, providing students with an innovative platform for interview practice. Participants will face interviewers from diverse enterprises simultaneously, gaining valuable workplace advice and interview experience. The event is open to all faculty and students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
