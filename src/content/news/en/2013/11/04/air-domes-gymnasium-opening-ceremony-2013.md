Shantou University will hold the official opening ceremony for its new "WanBian JinGang" air dome gymnasium on the afternoon of November 7, marking an important step in enhancing the university's sports infrastructure. The Sports Department is recruiting 60 student spectators from the entire student body to witness the occasion.

Students interested in attending should submit their registration forms via email to o_tyb@stu.edu.cn. The registration deadline is 5:00 PM on November 5. The list of selected spectators will be announced in the order in which applications are received.
