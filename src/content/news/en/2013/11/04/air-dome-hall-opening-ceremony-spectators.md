It is reported that Shantou University will hold the opening ceremony of the "Transformers" air-dome hall on the afternoon of November 7. To meet the needs of the event, the university is recruiting 60 student spectators from among all currently enrolled students.

Students who wish to attend should fill in the registration form and send it by email to the Physical Education Department at o_tyb@stu.edu.cn. Registration closes at 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday, November 5, 2013. The Physical Education Department will finalize the list in order of registration time and publish it afterward.

## Spectator Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Opening ceremony of the air-dome hall |
