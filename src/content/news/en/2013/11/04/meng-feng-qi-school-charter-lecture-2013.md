It is reported that Professor Meng Fengqi from the Higher Education Research Institute of Shantou University will deliver a lecture titled "University Charter Drafting Progress" on the afternoon of November 5, 2013, open to all faculty and students.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | University Charter Drafting Progress |
| Speaker | Prof. Meng Fengqi (Higher Education Research Institute) |
| Time | 14:30–16:30, Tuesday, November 5, 2013 |
| Venue | Room G201 |
| Organizer | Higher Education Research Institute |

The university charter is a foundational institutional document for governing the university according to law and standardizing academic operations. This lecture aims to inform the university community about the progress and key contents of the charter drafting process. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
