The Student Affairs Office at Shantou University will host the first Chekiang Holdings Group 2013 Campus Recruitment Fair from November 11 to 13, featuring company representatives from Watsons Group and other well-known employers.

The event is open to all graduating students as well as sophomore and junior students, providing a valuable platform for face-to-face exchanges with companies, industry insights, and internship and job opportunities.

Attendees planning to participate in company presentations, exhibition tours, or second-round interviews are reminded to dress in formal attire. Students are encouraged to prepare in advance and attend on time.
