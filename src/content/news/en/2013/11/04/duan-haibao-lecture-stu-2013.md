The College of Sciences at Shantou University will host Researcher Duan Haibao from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, for an academic lecture titled "Classification Problems in Geometry — Form and Number" on Wednesday, November 6, at 3:00 PM in Room 201, Building E. The lecture is open to all faculty and students.

Researcher Duan received his Ph.D. from Peking University in 1987. He subsequently conducted postdoctoral research and taught at Autonomous University of Barcelona and the University of Edinburgh. Returning to China in 1991, he joined Peking University as an associate professor. In 2001, he joined the Chinese Academy of Sciences under the "Hundred Talents Program" at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science.

His main research areas include algebraic topology, differential topology, and algebraic geometry. He has received several prestigious awards, including the Second Prize of the State Education Commission Science and Technology Progress Award (1997), the Chern Prize in Mathematics (2005), and the Second Prize of the National Natural Science Award (2010).
