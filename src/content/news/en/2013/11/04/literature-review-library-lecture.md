It is reported that Shantou University Library will hold a lecture titled "Literature Review, Literature Retrieval and Statistical Analysis" as part of its 2013 subject literature service series. The lecture will take place at the library lecture hall on the afternoon of November 5 (Tuesday), targeting graduate students and senior undergraduates, and all faculty and students are welcome.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Literature review, literature retrieval and statistical analysis |
| Speaker | Xie Luqing |
| Time | November 5 (Tuesday), 16:00 – 17:30 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |

Writing a literature review depends on effective literature retrieval. Based on live demonstrations of and discussions around CNKI, Engineering Village and Web of Science, the lecture aims to help attendees quickly master and choose the right retrieval strategies to achieve optimal recall and precision. It will also show how to use the bibliometric functions of literature databases for statistical analysis to effectively assess the current state of a research topic, leading institutions and authors, development trends and hot topics.
