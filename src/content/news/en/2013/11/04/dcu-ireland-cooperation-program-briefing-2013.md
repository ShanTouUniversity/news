The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University will host an informational session on the cooperation program with Dublin City University (DCU) in Ireland on November 7 from 2:30 PM to 3:50 PM in Room 307, Building G.

Ms. Sylvia Schroeder, Director of the International Office at DCU, and Ms. Xiaoxia Wang, Chief Representative of DCU's China Office, will be present to introduce the 2014 exchange student program and joint training opportunities between the two universities. Attendees will have the chance to ask questions directly to the guest speakers.

Students interested in studying in Ireland are encouraged to attend and take advantage of this opportunity to learn more about the program details and application process.
