The 21st Shantou University Campus Top Ten Singers Competition community auditions have officially launched, organized by the University Youth League Committee and open to all enrolled students who are passionate about music and eager to showcase their talents.

Students register by dormitory area (Areas A, B, C, D, E, F, G, J, L, Y, etc.). The competition features two rounds of selection: the community auditions followed by a qualifying round. The top five finalists from these rounds will earn the right to compete in the preliminary round of the 21st Campus Top Ten Singers Competition.

Solo, duet, and ensemble performances are all welcome, and bands may participate in an accompaniment format. The organizers encourage all music-loving students to sign up and shine on stage.
