Shantou University’s Student Entrepreneurship Center opened applications in November 2013 for places in the student business park. Eligibility was limited to teams that had received the university’s 2014 entrepreneurship fund and wished to move into the park.

Teams were asked to complete an application form and submit it to the Student Entrepreneurship Center, room 106 of the New Administration Building, by noon on November 28. The center listed 86502255 for inquiries.
