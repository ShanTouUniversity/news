It is reported that the Shantou University Trade Union will hold its first faculty photography salon at 4:00 PM on November 28 in the Activity Room on the third floor of the Trade Union building, with an open invitation to all interested faculty and staff.

The guest speaker for this session is Mr. Ling Xuemin. Born in Myanmar, Mr. Ling is a Taiwanese photographer based in Japan who graduated from the Tokyo Photo Art College in Japan, specializing in advertising photography and digital photography art. He holds a Japanese Certified Photographic Technician qualification and worked as a professional photographer in Japan for many years. His works have won numerous international awards, including the Italy Photography Grand Prize.

The Trade Union plans to hold faculty photography salons on an irregular basis in the future, providing a platform for faculty and staff with an interest in photography to exchange ideas and learn from one another.
