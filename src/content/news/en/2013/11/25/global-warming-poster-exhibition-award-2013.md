It is reported that the opening and awards ceremony of the "Global Warming 2013" public welfare poster design exhibition was held on the afternoon of November 21 at the Changjiang College of Art and Design, Shantou University. Professor Li Dan, Vice President of STU, Ms. Mai from the Hong Kong & Mongolia Foundation, Secretary Lin Binfeng of the University Youth League Committee, Professor Wang Shouzhi, Dean of the College of Arts, and Secretary Lin Dongyue of the Party General Branch were among the guests in attendance.

In his address, Dean Wang Shouzhi reviewed the three exhibitions organized this year by the College of Arts in support of the Antarctic scientific expedition project. Vice President Li Dan, in her speech, affirmed the importance and academic value of the Antarctic exhibition curated by the College of Arts.

Centered on the theme of "global warming," the公益 poster design exhibition echoes the series of exhibitions previously held by the College of Arts for the Antarctic expedition, showcasing the commitment of art students to environmental protection and social responsibility.
