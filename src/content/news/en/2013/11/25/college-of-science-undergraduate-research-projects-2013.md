It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University announced the results of the 2012–2013 Undergraduate Research Project selection on November 25, approving a total of 42 projects. Following final defense evaluations, 5 projects were awarded first prize, 6 second prize, and 8 third prize, while 20 were rated as passing. Three projects were deemed non-compliant due to the teams' failure to attend the defense, and the corresponding project funds will be recovered. Five faculty advisors were also recognized as outstanding instructors.

## Project Awards

| Award | Count |
|-------|-------|
| First Prize | 5 |
| Second Prize | 6 |
| Third Prize | 8 |
| Passing | 20 |
| Failing (did not attend defense) | 3 |
| Total | 42 |

## Outstanding Instructors

| Award | Count |
|-------|-------|
| Outstanding Instructor | 5 |

The Undergraduate Research Project program is an important platform for cultivating students' research abilities and innovative thinking within the College of Science, aiming to enhance academic literacy and practical skills through systematic research training. The selection process included project applications, preliminary review, and final defense presentations.
