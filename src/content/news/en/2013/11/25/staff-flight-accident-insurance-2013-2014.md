Shantou University’s Finance Office announced on November 25, 2013, a group flight accident insurance plan provided through China Pacific Life Insurance Co., Ltd., Shantou Central Branch. Staff who expected to fly during the year beginning December 1 and did not need to buy separate flight insurance could opt in. Coverage was RMB 500,000, with a premium of RMB 40 per person.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Coverage period | One year from December 1, 2013 |
| Insured amount | RMB 500,000 |
| Premium | RMB 40 per person |
| Reimbursement | One claim per year; the same type of premium could not be reimbursed again during the coverage period |
| Enrollment deadline | November 27, 2013 |

Each unit was to consolidate enrollment information for submission to the Finance Office. Staff could choose whether reimbursement was handled personally or through their unit; family members could also enroll voluntarily at their own expense. The notice listed sbai@stu.edu.cn and office telephone 2101 for inquiries, with Bai Sheng as the contact.
