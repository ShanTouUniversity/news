It is reported that the Shantou University Handicraft Club will hold its first "Waste into Crafts" recycling exchange event on November 30 at Jin San Jiao, calling on faculty and students across campus to bring various discarded items that can be exchanged for handmade crafts on site. The event aims to put environmental protection concepts into practice and raise ecological awareness among university students.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | Waste into Crafts |
| Time | November 30, 2013, 11:30 AM – 5:30 PM |
| Venue | Jin San Jiao |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee Handicraft Club |

## Accepted Items

| Category | Requirements |
|----------|-------------|
| Aluminum cans | Clean and recyclable |
| Various bottles | Must be washed clean |
| Paper towel rolls | Intact |
| Cardboard boxes, old magazines | Clean and tidy |
| Pen refills | — |
| Scrap fabric, old clothes | Clean, no odor |

Club members will display a variety of exquisite handmade works crafted from recycled materials on site. All faculty and students are welcome to bring the above-mentioned discarded items to participate in the exchange and embrace a green, low-carbon lifestyle together.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
