Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office began renovating the faculty office of the Center for Independent Language Learning on November 25, 2013. The project involved a complete interior rebuild of Wen Nan 408 and was carried out by Shantou Longhua Construction General Company.

The project cost RMB 147,500 and was scheduled for completion by December 29, 2013. The university noted that construction could cause noise, vibration and dust, and advised nearby staff to take care around the site.
