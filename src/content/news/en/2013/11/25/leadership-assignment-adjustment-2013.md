It is reported that Shantou University and its Party Committee jointly issued Document No. 135 [2013] on November 25, 2013, to adjust the division of duties among some senior leaders and assistants to the president to meet work needs.

## Adjusted Duties

| Leader | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Xu Xiaohu, President | Oversees overall university affairs |
| Song *Zhen, Party Secretary (one character garbled in original) | Oversees overall Party affairs, assists with overall administration, in charge of publicity and united front work |
| Gu Peihua, Executive President | Assists the president with overall administration, in charge of personnel, finance and the preparation for Guangdong Technion-Israel Institute of Technology |
| Luo Nianchao, Vice Party Secretary | In charge of organization, ideological and political education, student employment, trade union, discipline inspection, supervision and auditing, and ideological education in the Department of Social Sciences |
| Wen Xiaobo, Vice President | In charge of research, teaching, admissions, arts education, teaching and general education in the Department of Social Sciences, the Central Laboratory, academic journals and university-run enterprises |
| Li Dan, Vice President | In charge of student development, residential colleges, the Sports Department and the basketball team |
| Lin Danming, Vice President | Assists with daily administration and GTIIT preparation, in charge of the President’s Office, foreign affairs, resources, security, and network and information |
| Wulan Hasi, Vice President | In charge of discipline development, Project 211, postgraduate education, continuing education and the library |
| Huang Dongyang, Assistant to the President | Assists with the School of Medicine and its relocation to the main campus |
| Xu Zongling, Assistant to the President | In charge of alumni affairs |
| Chen Shaogang, Assistant to the President | Assists with resource management |
