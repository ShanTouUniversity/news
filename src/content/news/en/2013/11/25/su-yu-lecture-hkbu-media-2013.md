It is reported that the Changjiang College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will invite Prof. Su Yu, Dean of the Department of Communication and Professor of Journalism at Hong Kong Baptist University, as well as Executive Dean of the Graduate College of HKBU, to deliver an academic lecture on November 27, 2013, titled "Hong Kong Media and Media Education."

## Lecture Schedule

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Topic | Hong Kong Media and Media Education |
| Speaker | Prof. Su Yu |
| Speaker Title | Dean, Department of Communication; Professor of Journalism; Executive Dean, Graduate College, Hong Kong Baptist University |
| Time | Wednesday, November 27, 2013, 19:30–21:30 |
| Venue | Room 204, E-Step Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Changjiang College of Journalism and Communication |
| Audience | Faculty and students of the university |

Prof. Su Yu has long been engaged in teaching and research in the field of journalism and communication, with profound academic expertise in Hong Kong media ecology and media education. The lecture will provide an in-depth discussion on the current development of the Hong Kong media industry and educational philosophy in media studies. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
