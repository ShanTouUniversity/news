It is reported that the "Practicing Core Socialist Values" speech competition, jointly organized by the Shantou Municipal Party Committee Publicity Department, the Shantou Municipal Education Bureau, and the Communist Youth League Shantou Municipal Committee, has completed its campus preliminary round. Ten students have advanced and will compete in the citywide semi-finals on November 13, 2013, split into two sessions.

## Background

The speech competition aims to showcase university faculty and students' profound understanding and reflection on worldview, outlook on life, and values, while guiding them to connect their personal futures and destinies with those of the nation and the Chinese nation, further cultivating and practicing core socialist values.

## Selection Results

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Organizers | Shantou Municipal Party Committee Publicity Department, Shantou Municipal Education Bureau, Communist Youth League Shantou Municipal Committee |
| Campus participants | 15 |
| Advancers to semi-finals | 10 |
| Semi-final date | November 13, 2013 (two sessions) |
| Semi-final scope | Citywide, Shantou |

Following rigorous evaluation in the campus preliminary round, ten students earned the right to represent the university at the citywide semi-finals. The semi-finals will be judged by a panel of experts appointed by the joint organizing bodies, with results to be publicly announced.
