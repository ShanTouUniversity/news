It is reported that the Centre for International Languages and Linguistics (CILL) at Shantou University will host an English workshop titled "Using English to Your Advantage: Excel at a Social Event" on Friday, November 29, from 10:30 AM to 12:00 PM. The session will be led by instructors Ms. Chanchal Singh and Sohani Gandhioke.

The workshop aims to help students and faculty develop practical English communication skills for social situations. Due to limited seating, participants are required to register in advance through Eventbrite.
