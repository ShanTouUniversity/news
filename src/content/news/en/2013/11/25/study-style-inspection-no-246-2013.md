Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office issued Bulletin No. 246 on November 25, 2013, summarizing two checks at the E Teaching Building during Week 10 of the fall semester. The bulletin recorded 14 instances of students arriving late; the student names in the original attachment are not reproduced.

| Check date and time | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
|------|------:|------:|
| November 19 (Tuesday), 8 a.m. | 5 instances | 3 instances |
| November 20 (Wednesday), 8 a.m. | 6 instances | Not specified |

The office said the findings were shared with the university’s schools and residential colleges for follow-up study-style education. To protect student privacy, this report includes only aggregate figures and no individual names.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Three-Style Development Office)
