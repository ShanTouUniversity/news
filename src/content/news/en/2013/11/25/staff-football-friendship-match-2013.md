It is reported that the Shantou University Trade Union will organize a friendly football match on the afternoon of November 22, 2013, between the university's faculty football team and a military team stationed in Shantou, aiming to promote university-military exchange and strengthen mutual friendship.

## Match Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Match type | Friendly match |
| Opponents | STU Faculty Football Team vs. military team stationed in Shantou |
| Match time | Afternoon of November 22, 2013 |
| Venue | Football field of the military unit, Shantou |
| Departure time | 4:00 PM, November 22 |
| Departure point | In front of the University Motto Monument |
| Contact person | Luo Yingguang |
| Phone | 86503271, 693448 (short number) |

Faculty football team members are requested to gather at the University Motto Monument at 4:00 PM on November 22 for bus departure to the venue. All faculty and students are welcome to attend and cheer for the team.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union)
