It is reported that the results of the calligraphy finals for the 8th "Xinghe Cup" Chinese Painting and Calligraphy Competition have recently been announced. At this competition, Zheng Sixuan, a ninth-grade student from STU Affiliated Middle School, won Third Prize in the junior high division, while Chen Cang (eight Grade) and Zhong Heyu (seventh Grade) from the same school received Excellence Awards.

The competition was organized by the ChaoShan Youth Painting and Calligraphy Competition Committee and opened to young people across the three ChaoShan cities. In total, competitors from the ChaoShan region won one First Prize, three Second Prizes, and five Third Prizes.
