It is reported that the Graduate School at Shantou University has recently announced the results of the 2012-2013 academic year Outstanding Graduate Student selection. Following initial evaluation and recommendation by various graduate training programs and final review by the Graduate School, 60 students including Cao Qian were awarded Second Class Honors for Outstanding Graduate Students for the 2012-2013 academic year.

The Graduate School has encouraged the honored students to continue their excellent work, study diligently, strive for innovation, and achieve further accomplishments. It has also called on all graduate students to learn from the awardees, hold themselves to higher standards, and make greater progress in their studies and research going forward.
