## Contest Background

The contest was held from September 13 to 16, attracting 23,339 teams from 1,326 institutions across 33 provinces and territories in China, as well as from Singapore, India, Malaysia, and other countries. The coaching advisors for our university's teams were Wei Caimin and Li Jian.
