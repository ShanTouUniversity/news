It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has announced the first STU Table Tennis Individual Open, scheduled for December 1 at the gymnasium (specific start time to be announced separately). The tournament features men's singles and women's singles, with awards for the top eight finishers in each category, as well as a Best Referee award.

Interested participants must register by 23:00 on November 28.
