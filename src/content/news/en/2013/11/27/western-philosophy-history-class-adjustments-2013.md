Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced three schedule changes in November 2013 for *History of Western Philosophy* (section 63309). The original notice gave the reason as “46-level examinations”; this report preserves that wording without interpreting it. All make-up sessions were scheduled in room E203.

The office announced two additional schedule changes on December 12, with both make-up sessions assigned to room E207.

| Original class date | Make-up date and periods |
| --- | --- |
| Dec. 4 (week 12, Wednesday, ABC periods) | Dec. 8 (week 13, Sunday, periods 234) |
| Dec. 6 (week 12, Friday, periods 678) | Dec. 15 (week 14, Sunday, periods 234) |
| Dec. 14 (week 13, Saturday, periods 234) | Dec. 22 (week 15, Sunday, periods 234) |
| Dec. 13 (week 13, Friday, periods 678) | Dec. 14 (week 13, Saturday, ABC periods), E207 |
| Dec. 18 (week 14, Wednesday, ABC periods) | Dec. 21 (week 14, Saturday, ABC periods), E207 |
