It is reported that the Education Committee of the Chinese Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics has decided to hold its 2013 annual meeting at Shantou University on December 1, 2013, co-hosted by Jinan University and Shantou University.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | 2013 Annual Meeting, Education Committee, Chinese Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics |
| Time | 14:00–17:00, December 1, 2013 |
| Venue | ACC Conference Room 3 |
| Co-hosts | Jinan University, College of Engineering, Shantou University |

## Afternoon Lectures

| Speaker | Title |
| --- | --- |
| Hong Jiazhen | *Resource-Sharing Course Development for Theoretical Mechanics at Shanghai Jiao Tong University* |
| Sui Yunkang | *Demystifying Innovation and Sharpening the Edge: Cultivating Innovation Begins with Basic Courses* |
| Xiong Guangjing | *CDIO-Based Engineering Education System* |

All faculty and students are welcome. Lecture materials are in the OA attachments.
