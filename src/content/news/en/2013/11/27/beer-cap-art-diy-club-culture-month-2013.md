The Edge Design Association at Shantou University planned a beer-cap art and craft DIY event at the campus Golden Triangle on December 8, 2013, as part of the university’s first Club Culture Month. The event was open to students and staff and was scheduled to run from 10:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

The association planned to provide tools and technical support so participants could design and make their own pieces, which they could keep. The activity aimed to promote environmental awareness through reused-cap art and encourage hands-on creativity. The original notice also described a social-media interaction organized by the association.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
