It is reported that Shantou University has completed the evaluation for the 2012-2013 University Scholarships and has released the proposed winners list for public comment across colleges (residential colleges).

In accordance with the Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate Scholarships of Shantou University (STU Fa [2010] No. 125), the selection went through college recommendations, individual applications, and reviews at various levels, covering Outstanding Student Scholarships, Academic Excellence Scholarships, Special Contribution Awards, and Activity Enthusiast Awards.

Twenty students including Nie BM are proposed for the Outstanding Student Scholarship after college recommendations and interviews by the University Scholarship Review Committee. For Academic Excellence Scholarships, 169 students including Huang T are proposed for First Prize, 430 including Chen Q for Second Prize, and 616 including Deng CR for Third Prize, following individual applications and review by the Financial Aid Management Center. For Special Awards, 10 students including Liu GR are proposed for Outstanding Contribution in Academic Science and Technology, 10 including Chen Y for Outstanding Leadership, 10 including Ji H for Outstanding Contribution in Social Service, and 10 including Zhang YD for Outstanding Contribution in Culture and Sports, selected by the Special Scholarship Sub-committee. In addition, 45 students including Wang LL are proposed for Academic Science and Technology Activity Enthusiasts, 147 including Zhou ZR for Campus Culture Activity Enthusiasts, 131 including Ye WT for Social Service Enthusiasts, and 136 including Li HJ for Campus Service Enthusiasts, selected by individual colleges.

The list is now open for public comment. Any unit or individual with objections may submit written comments to the Financial Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office, Room 106, Administration Building, before 12:00 noon on Friday, November 29. Submissions from units should bear an official seal, while individual submissions must include the person's real name. The full list is available in the attachment.
