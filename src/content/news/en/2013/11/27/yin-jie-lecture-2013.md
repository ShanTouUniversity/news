It is reported that Yin Jie, Director of Public Relations at Guangzhou Automobile Group, will deliver a lecture titled "Media Applications in Public Relations" at Shantou University's College of Journalism and Communication on November 29.

## About the Speaker

Yin Jie previously served as Deputy Director of the News Center of Guangzhou TV, Director of its English Channel, and Director of the Advertising Management and Brand Marketing Center. He was once one of the most well-known television news anchors in Guangzhou, having received the Guangdong Provincial Host Golden Microphone Award and the China Golden Microphone Charity Award. He was also selected as Champion of Guangzhou City Image Ambassador and one of the Top Ten Outstanding Youth of Guangzhou's Eighth Session, and held a position during the 2010 Guangzhou Asian Games.
