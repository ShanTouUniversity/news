It is reported that Colin Sparks, Chair Professor at Hong Kong Baptist University, will deliver a lecture titled "What Future for Journalism" at Shantou University on the evening of November 28.

## About the Speaker

Colin Sparks is a British scholar specializing in communication and media studies. From 1974 to 2011, he taught at University of Westminster, where he served as Dean of the Faculty of Arts and former Director of the Centre for Chinese Media Research—the first such center in the UK. His published works cover the BBC, newspapers, communication, and media studies, and he is currently a leading expert on media globalization.
