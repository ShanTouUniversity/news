It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has announced the first Club Culture Month travel guide activity, which will run from November 23 to December 8 in the Jin San Jiao area. The event invites all faculty and students to submit travel-related entries across six award categories.

## Award Categories

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Best Travel Guide | Outstanding travel planning proposals |
| Best Travel Route | Recommended itinerary routes |
| Best Travel Destination | Recommended destinations |
| Best Travel Food | Featured culinary guides |
| Best Travel Savings | Budget-friendly travel plans |
| Excellence Award | Other outstanding works |

One winner will be selected per category.
