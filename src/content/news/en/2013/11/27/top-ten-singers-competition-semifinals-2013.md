It is reported that the semifinals of the 21st Shantou University Campus Top Ten Singers Competition will be held on November 30 (Saturday) at 7:30 PM at the Grand Auditorium. Sponsored by the Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the competition provides a stage for vocal enthusiasts to showcase their talents.

A total of 25 teams have registered for this year's semifinals. The first round will feature ensemble performances, with all 25 teams divided into five groups according to the drawing order. There is no admission fee; audience members may enter on a first-come, first-served basis. Faculty and students are welcomed to attend and enjoy the performances.

The Campus Top Ten Singers Competition is one of the most influential campus arts events at STU, having attracted widespread attention and participation from faculty and students across its history, fully showcasing the youthfulness and artistic talents of STU students.
