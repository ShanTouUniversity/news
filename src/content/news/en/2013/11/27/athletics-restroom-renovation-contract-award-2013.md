Shantou University’s Resource Management Office published the result on November 27, 2013, for renovations to the athletics field public restrooms and the Social Sciences office. Following review by the university’s tendering group, Shantou Longhua Construction General Company won the two projects with a combined bid of RMB 269,887.21.

The notice period ran from November 27 to 29. Inquiries could be directed to the Infrastructure Office at 0754-86502490; complaints were handled by the Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346.
