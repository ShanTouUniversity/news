Shantou University’s Center for International Language Learning (CILL) scheduled the fourth workshop in its IELTS preparation series for November 27, 2013. The session was to focus on developing listening skills and, according to the announcement at the time, run from 4 to 6 p.m. at CILL.

Seats were limited. Students could reserve a place through the Eventbrite page provided by the organizers at the time: <https://www.eventbrite.com/e/ielts-41-listening-tickets-9194568201>. The notice asked those who registered to attend.
