Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced in November 2013 that the December 3 class of *Moral and Legal Foundations (Ethics)*, section 64455, would not take place. The class had been scheduled for periods AB in room D504 during week 12. The notice said instructor Zeng Dabin would be away, and no make-up date had yet been set.
