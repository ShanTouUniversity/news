It is reported that the Shantou University Campus TV will commence trial broadcasting on November 28. The station currently operates five programs: *Campus News*, *People Focus*, *STU in Pictures*, *Campus Walk*, and *Your Shoot, My Show*, capturing campus life, highlighting faculty and student achievements, and disseminating university information through a student-driven media lens.

Programs produced by the Campus TV will be published online and made available to the university community and the public. Viewers can access the station's website directly through the "Featured Topics" section on the homepage of the university's main website (http://www.stu.edu.cn).

The establishment of the Campus TV aims to create a student-led media platform for planning, filming, and producing campus content, enriching the channels for campus cultural dissemination and providing richer in-house information for faculty and students.
