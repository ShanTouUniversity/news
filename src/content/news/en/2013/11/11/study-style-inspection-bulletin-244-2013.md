The Office of Three Styles Construction at STU has released bulletin no. 244 summarizing tardiness incidents in Building E during Week 8 of the fall 2013-2014 semester.

According to the bulletin, on Nov. 4 there were 2 students arriving late (within 5 minutes) and 1 student arriving late (over 5 minutes) in Building E. On Nov. 6, there were 4 students arriving late (within 5 minutes) and 2 students arriving late (over 5 minutes). The office has forwarded the list of students who failed to comply with school discipline regulations to their respective colleges, urging stronger attention to academic conduct.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Three Styles Construction)
