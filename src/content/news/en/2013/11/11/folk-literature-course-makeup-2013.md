Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced a make-up session for Culture and Humanity in Folk Literature on November 11, 2013. The class (No. 65142), offered by the Chinese Department, had been canceled during periods 1–2 on November 4. It was rescheduled for periods 3–4 on Saturday, November 16 (Week 9), in Room G306, Building G.
