Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled the second workshop on integrative thinking in student activities for November 12, 2013. The meeting was set for 15:00–17:30 in Meeting Room 330 of the Administration Center, with project-leading faculty invited to attend.

The session continued the university’s earlier discussion of integrative thinking in student activities. The notice asked participants to arrange their work in advance and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
