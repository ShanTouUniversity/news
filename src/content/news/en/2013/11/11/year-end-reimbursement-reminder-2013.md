Shantou University’s Finance Office issued a year-end reimbursement reminder to campus staff in November 2013. To reduce queues that could result from a rush of requests at the end of the year, the office advised staff to plan reimbursement matters early.

The reminder did not specify a deadline or detailed procedure; subsequent arrangements from the Finance Office would apply.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office)
