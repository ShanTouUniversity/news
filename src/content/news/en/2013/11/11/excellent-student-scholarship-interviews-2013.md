Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled interviews for its 2012–2013 Excellent Student Scholarships for 15:00 on November 13, 2013, in Meeting Room 213 of the Administration Center. The interview used a leaderless group-discussion format and involved 30 candidates divided into five groups of six.

## Interview Format

| Stage | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Introduction | Up to one minute per candidate |
| Discussion topic | Selected by drawing lots |
| Preparation | Two minutes |
| Group discussion | Ten minutes |
| Questions from judges | Eight minutes |

Candidates were asked to bring name cards and attend on time. Candidate names and group assignments are omitted to protect student privacy.

> ⚠️ Interview group assignments and other attachments are available in the original OA notice.
