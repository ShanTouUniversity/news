It is reported that the Shantou University Staff Badminton Tournament will begin on November 19, 2013. Nine representative teams from across the university will compete in the tournament.

The participating teams include: Law School/School of Business Joint Team, Research/Graduate School/Library Joint Team, Physical Education Department, School of Science, School of Engineering, Administrative Staff Team, Resources Management Office, School of Humanities/School of Journalism Joint Team, and Arts/Social Sciences/Continuing Education/Affiliated Primary & Middle School Joint Team.

The seeded teams are the Research/Graduate School/Library Joint Team, Physical Education Department, School of Science, and School of Engineering.

Pre-tournament training sessions have been arranged: the University Labor Union has booked badminton courts 4–9 in the gymnasium for staff practice on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Fridays from 17:30 to 18:30. Wednesday sessions will continue as usual.
