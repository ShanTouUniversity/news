Cheung Kong School of Art and Design posted a public notice on November 11, 2013, concerning two proposed major transfers by 2012 public-art undergraduates. After discipline assessments, the students were proposed for transfer to the architecture and landscape-design track in Art and Design. The five-working-day notice period ran from November 11 through 15.

The proposal was based on Shantou University’s undergraduate academic-status rules and the school’s major-transfer implementation plan. Objections could be submitted in writing or raised in person or by phone with the school’s academic office at 86502258. Student names and ID numbers are omitted.
