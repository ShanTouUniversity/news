It is reported that the "Student Community X-Program: Repair Storm" campaign, hosted by the STU Student Affairs Office and the STU Committee of the Communist Youth League, will launch on November 13 and run through December 13, offering free small-appliance repair services to all faculty and students.

Jointly organized by the STU Science Creation Association and the Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee, the initiative aims to help members of the university community who lack repair skills or tools to fix everyday appliance malfunctions.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Campaign | Student Community X-Program: Repair Storm |
| Target | All STU faculty and students |
| Schedule | Nov. 13 – Dec. 13, 2013, every Wednesday and Friday, 16:00–18:00 (excluding statutory holidays) |
| Venue | Activity Room 236, Building C, Student Dormitory |
| Hosts | Student Affairs Office, STU Committee of the Communist Youth League |
| Organizers | Science Creation Association, Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee |

## Repair Scope

The service covers commonly used dormitory appliances, including desk lamps, fans, speakers, power strips, electric kettles, rice cookers, hair dryers, radios, induction cookers, and other small household appliances.

## How to Participate

Those in need may bring appliances directly to Activity Room 236, Building C during service hours. Outside those hours, advance appointments are available. For appointments and inquiries, contact Student Yang or send a private message via Weibo to @STU_Science Creation Association or @STU Dormitory Committee.
