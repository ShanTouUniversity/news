Shantou University’s Resource Management Office published a proposed addition to the New Administration Building window-shield procurement and installation project on November 11, 2013. During construction preparation, the planned stainless-steel security grille, measuring about 6.7 by 2.7 meters, was found too large to pass through an office doorway. The proposal was to bring it indoors as three separate sections and assemble them on site.

The work would also require moving a rail-mounted file cabinet in the Comprehensive Archives Room and returning it after installation. The reviewed cost of the addition was about RMB 3,800, less than 10% of the original contract amount. Written comments could be submitted to the Audit and Supervision Office or the Key Construction Projects Office by 17:30 on November 14.
