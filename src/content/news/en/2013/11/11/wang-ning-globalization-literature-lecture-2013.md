It is reported that Professor Wang Ning from the Department of Foreign Languages and Literatures at Tsinghua University will deliver an academic lecture titled "Globalization, World Literature and Translation" on the afternoon of November 13, 2013, from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM in Room ACC 3, Shantou University.

Professor Wang graduated from Peking University in 1989 with a Ph.D. in English and Comparative Literature. He later received a postdoctoral fellowship from the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences to conduct research at Utrecht University. After returning to China in 1991, he served as an associate professor in the Department of English at Peking University and was promoted to full professor in 1992. In 2000, he joined Tsinghua University as a professor and director of the Center for Comparative Literature and Cultural Studies. He was elected an academician of the Academia Latina de Ciencias in 2010 and selected as a Changjiang Scholar in 2012.

The lecture will cover four main aspects: the theoretical construction of globalization, the evolution and connotation of the concept of world literature, the construction and evaluation standards of world literature, and Chinese literature and its translation in the context of world literature. The aim is to deepen understanding of literary studies in the era of globalization.
