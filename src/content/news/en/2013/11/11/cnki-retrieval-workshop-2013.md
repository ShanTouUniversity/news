The STU Library will host a workshop on retrieving Chinese e-journals in science and engineering on Tuesday, Nov. 12, from 19:30 to 21:00, at the Subject Services Department on the 2nd floor of the new library building. Zeng Zhaohong will lead the session.

The workshop will cover: discovering new features of CNKI (China National Knowledge Infrastructure) with retrieval strategies for its academic journal full-text database; an introduction and retrieval tutorial for EI Village (Engineering Index); an overview of electronic journals from major professional societies including ACM, ACS, AIP, APS, ASCE, IEL, IOP, RSC, and MathSciNet, along with full-text download rankings; and an introduction to Open Access Journals.
