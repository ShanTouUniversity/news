It is reported that Kang Hua, a professional mountaineering coach and technical advisor of the Tibet Mountaineering School, will deliver a lecture titled "The Exploration History of Mount Everest" on the evening of November 15, 2013, from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM in Lecture Hall 201, Building E, Shantou University.

Kang Hua is a ski-mountaineering and rock climbing coach with the Chinese Mountaineering Association. He authored the "2012 Mount Everest Summit Report" and has conducted in-depth research on the mountain. From 2002 to 2006, he was responsible for technical training and event operations at the Tibet Mountaineering School, and from 2006 to 2011 he served as a climbing instructor for the Chinese Advanced Mountaineering Talent Training Program. He has summited more than 40 peaks and routes across China, Europe, and North America, and has undergone professional guiding training through the American Mountain Guides Association and the French National Ski Mountaineering School.

The lecture will explore the history of human exploration of Mount Everest, sharing firsthand mountaineering experience and observations, and presenting a vivid portrait of the grand adventures on the world's highest peak.
