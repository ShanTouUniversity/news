It is reported that the Career Workshop of the School of Science will hold a lecture on November 13, 2013, to help students understand postgraduate study and career development in Hong Kong.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Give Your Future More Choices — What You Must Know About Further Study and Career Planning; Hong Kong Postgraduate Study Guide |
| Speaker | Dr. Zhang Zhijian, Lecturer in the Department of Asian and Policy Studies and Programme Director of Greater China Studies, Hong Kong Institute of Education; PhD in Politics, University of Manchester |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, November 13, 2013 |
| Venue | Science Report Hall, Shantou University |
| Highlights | Overview of Hong Kong postgraduate education and employment prospects; guide to popular majors and application strategies; success case sharing; advantages of studying in Hong Kong |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, School of Science |

All interested students are welcome to attend.
