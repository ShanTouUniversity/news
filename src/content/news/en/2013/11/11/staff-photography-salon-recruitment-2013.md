The STU Labor Union will host a photography salon for faculty and staff, aiming to enliven extracurricular life and enhance aesthetic appreciation. The event will feature sharing and guidance from teachers with photography experience. Specific time and venue details will be announced separately.

Employees interested in participating are asked to fill out a registration form and send it to o_xgh@stu.edu.cn by November 15.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
