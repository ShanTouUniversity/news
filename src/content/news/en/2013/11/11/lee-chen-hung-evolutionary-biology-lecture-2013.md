The College of Sciences at STU has announced that Professor Li Chen-Hung of Shanghai Ocean University, a Shanghai Oriental Scholar, will deliver an academic lecture on campus.

The lecture, titled "Capturing protein-coding genes across divergent species and its implication in evolutionary biology," will take place on Wednesday, Nov. 13, from 10:00 to 11:30, in ACC Room 3.
