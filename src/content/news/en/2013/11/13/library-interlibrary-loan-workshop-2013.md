The Subject Services Department of the Shantou University Library will hold a workshop on interlibrary loan and document delivery services as part of its "Subject Bibliographic Services" lecture series on November 14 (Thursday), 19:30-20:30, at the Library's Subject Services Office (inside the Level 2 reference desk). The speaker is Mr. Xu Yong from the library.

Interlibrary loan and document delivery are important academic resource access channels provided by the library to faculty and students. When the university library does not hold a particular journal article or book, readers can borrow materials from other university libraries through interlibrary loan or obtain copies through document delivery. This workshop will provide a detailed introduction to the application processes, usage methods, and important considerations for both services, helping faculty and students make more efficient use of library resources to support their research.

Faculty and students are welcome to attend and improve their utilization of academic resources.
