The STU Graduate Volunteer Group will hold the "Green Campus, DIY" environmental activity series on Nov. 17 and 27, 2013, aiming to enrich campus life and raise environmental awareness among students and staff.

The event consists of three phases: a collection phase (Nov. 12–23, 22:00), during which students and faculty are invited to submit DIY works made from waste materials; a showcase phase (Nov. 23–26), featuring exhibitions and public voting at the entrance lobby of Graduate Apartment Block L; and an outdoor activity phase (Nov. 27, Thanksgiving Day), with a ring-toss game at the "Golden Triangle" on the pedestrian street, where participants can win the displayed DIY artworks for free.
