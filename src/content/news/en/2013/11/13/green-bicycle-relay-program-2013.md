The second phase of the "Green Relay" Campus Public Bicycle Program at Shantou University will officially commence on November 18, 2013, and run through June 18, 2014 (excluding winter and summer vacations).

Hosted by the Student Affairs Office and organized by the Counselor Office of Student Residential Areas, with operations managed by the student team "Light in the Wheels," the program is open to all enrolled students. The initiative aims to conserve public resources, promote recycling and reuse, raise awareness of environmental protection and low-carbon energy saving among faculty and students, and assist with the disposal of old bicycles on campus.

The first phase of the Green Relay program achieved notable success by collecting and processing discarded campus bicycles, which not only improved the campus environment but also enhanced students' environmental awareness. The second phase will continue with the same proven operational model, and all faculty and students are encouraged to participate actively in building a greener campus.
