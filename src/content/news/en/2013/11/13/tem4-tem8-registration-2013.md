The STU Academic Affairs Office has announced that registration for the 2014 Test for English Majors-Band 4 (TEM4) and Band 8 (TEM8) is now open.

Eligible candidates for TEM4 include second-year English majors enrolled in 2012, as well as 2011-level students who took the 2013 TEM4 but did not pass (retake). Eligible candidates for TEM8 include fourth-year English majors enrolled in 2010, as well as 2009-level students who took the 2013 TEM8 but did not pass (retake).

The TEM8 exam is scheduled for 8:30 AM on March 22, 2014; the TEM4 exam will be held at 8:30 AM on April 19, 2014. Online registration runs from Nov. 14 to 21. Students must log into the credit-system special website to complete the registration process. The registration fee is 40 RMB for TEM4 and 45 RMB for TEM8.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
