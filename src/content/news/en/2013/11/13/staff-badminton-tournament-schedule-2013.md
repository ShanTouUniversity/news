The 2013 Staff Badminton Tournament at Shantou University, hosted by the University Labor Union, will kick off at 16:30 on November 19 (Tuesday). Matches will be held every Tuesday and Friday throughout the tournament. Drinking water will be provided by the union.

The staff badminton tournament is a traditional annual sports event organized by the STU Labor Union, aiming to enrich the recreational lives of faculty and staff, promote physical fitness, and foster a positive and active campus sports culture.
