It is reported that the results of the 2013 FLTRP Cup National English Speech Contest Guangdong Regional Semi-Finals were recently announced. Jin Xiaoyi, a 2010-level English major student at Shantou University, stood out among 57 outstanding contestants from across the province and won the Special Championship with excellent results. This marks the first time an STU contestant has claimed the provincial championship in the FLTRP Cup Speech Contest, representing a historic breakthrough.

In addition, Ren Heyin, a 2012-level Journalism and Communication major student, was awarded the Honorable Mention prize in the same competition. Jin Xiaoyi will represent Guangdong Province and Shantou University at the National Finals in Beijing in December.

It is understood that beginning in early September, Teacher Ma Rui from the Center for International Languages commenced the selection and pre-competition preparation work, working alongside Teachers Zhang Li and Chanchal Singh to provide intensive coaching for the contestants.

## Competition Results

| Award | Name | Grade & Major |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Special Championship | Jin Xiaoyi | 2010 English Major |
| Honorable Mention | Ren Heyin | 2012 Journalism & Communication |

Contestants from 45 universities across the province participated in the semi-finals, including prestigious institutions such as Sun Yat-sen University, Jinan University, and Guangdong University of Foreign Studies.
