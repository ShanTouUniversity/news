It is reported that the Art Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will hold the *Beautiful Friday – Zhang Yue Piano Solo Concert* on the evening of November 8, 2013, as the second event of the 2nd “Rising Stars” series.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Beautiful Friday – Zhang Yue Piano Solo Concert |
| Performer | Zhang Yue, young pianist of the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts |
| Time | 19:30, November 8, 2013 (please arrive 10 minutes early) |
| Venue | Grand Auditorium |
| Tickets | Paired seats are available; holders can enjoy a buy-one-get-one-free ice cream cone at Ajimi |
| Issued by | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center |

Zhang Yue began studying the piano at the age of seven and a half, studied under Prof. Guo Jiate, Head of the Department of Keyboard, Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts, since 2008, and was admitted to the academy in 2010. He has performed with the Shenzhen Symphony Orchestra, the Xiamen Opera House Symphony Orchestra and the Hong Kong Pan Asia Symphony Orchestra, and has appeared in the “Beautiful Sunday” concert at the Shenzhen Concert Hall and the New Year Concert at the Shenzhen Grand Theater. He won fourth place in the youth group of the 5th Asian Youth Music Competition and the silver prize in the open group of the 14th Tokyo Asia Chopin International Piano Competition.
