It is reported that the Art Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will hold the "Beautiful Friday – Zhang Yue Piano Solo Concert" on the evening of November 8, 2013, as the second event of the 2nd "Rising Stars Series."

## Concert Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Beautiful Friday – Zhang Yue Piano Solo Concert |
| Performer | Zhang Yue, young pianist of the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts |
| Time | 19:30, November 8, 2013 |
| Venue | Grand Auditorium |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center |

Zhang Yue began studying the piano at the age of seven and a half and studied under Prof. Guo Jiate, Head of the Department of Keyboard at the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts, since then. He was admitted to the academy in 2010. Zhang has won fourth place in the youth group of the 5th Asian Youth Music Competition and the silver prize in the open group of the 14th Tokyo Asia Chopin International Piano Competition. He has also performed with the Shenzhen Symphony Orchestra, the Xiamen Opera House Symphony Orchestra, and the Hong Kong Pan Asia Symphony Orchestra.
