It is reported that Professor Wang Shouzhi, Dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, will hold a dialogue with renowned Hong Kong graphic designer Hon Bingwah (韩秉华) on the evening of November 7, 2013, under the theme "Aesthetic Life — Flat and Dimensional · Art and Design."

## Dialogue Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Aesthetic Life — Flat and Dimensional · Art and Design |
| Speakers | Wang Shouzhi (Dean, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design; distinguished design educator and scholar), Hon Bingwah (renowned Hong Kong graphic designer) |
| Time | 19:30, Thursday, November 7, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, 1st Floor, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Format | Dialogue |

The two speakers will explore the intrinsic connections between aesthetics and daily life, between flat and three-dimensional art, and the profound impact of design and art on life. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
