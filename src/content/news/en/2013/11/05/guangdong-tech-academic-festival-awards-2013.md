The 7th Guangdong University Student Science and Technology Academic Festival was held across universities in Guangdong Province from May to July 2013, jointly organized by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology, and the Guangdong Provincial Students'\'' Federation.

Shantou University students achieved outstanding results across 11 competitions, including the 12th "Challenge Cup" Guangdong College Student Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Works Competition, the Guangdong College Student Biochemistry Experiment Skills Competition, and the Guangdong College Student English Comprehensive Skills Competition. The detailed award list is available in the attached document.

The festival is one of the most influential university science and technology competitions in Guangdong Province, providing students with a platform to showcase their innovation abilities and academic achievements.
