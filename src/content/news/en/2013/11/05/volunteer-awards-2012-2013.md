It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University has announced the 2012–2013 "Volunteer Pioneer" and "Outstanding Youth Volunteer" awards, honoring students who have made significant contributions through the university's service-learning educational model.

## Award Recipients

| Honor | Number of Recipients |
| --- | --- |
| Volunteer Pioneer | 23 (including Wu Jinhu et al.) |
| Outstanding Youth Volunteer | 48 (including Mao Muyi et al.) |

The awards aim to promote campus culture and spiritual civilization, carry forward the spirit of volunteerism, and inspire more students to participate in volunteer activities and grow through practice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
