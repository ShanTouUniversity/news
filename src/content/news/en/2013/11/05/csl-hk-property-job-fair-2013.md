It is reported that the Cheung Kong Holdings (Group) 2013 Campus Recruitment Fair (Session 2) will be held at Shantou University from November 13 to 15, 2013. Hutchison Whampoa Property Group will participate as one of the recruiting companies, seeking fresh graduates.

## Fair Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Cheung Kong Holdings (Group) 2013 Campus Recruitment Fair (Session 2) |
| Dates | November 13–15, 2013 |
| Participating Company | Hutchison Whampoa Property Group |
| Application Process | Download and fill out the "Position Application Form" from the Hutchison Whampoa Property Group website by November 13; submit in person at the fair on November 14, 9:00–12:00 |

All graduating students are encouraged to prepare thoroughly and attend the fair. Interested candidates should visit the Hutchison Whampoa Property Group website and search for "2014 Shantou University Recruitment" to obtain the application form in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
