The Second Conference on Regional Christianity and Chinese Civilization and Social Development was held on November 2-3, 2013, at the Science Lecture Hall and Academic Exchange Center of Shantou University. Hosted by the College of Humanities, the conference carried forward the achievements of the inaugural session and aimed to advance the study of regional Christianity in China from multiple perspectives.

The theme of this year'\''s conference was "Christianity and Sino-Western Cultural Exchange." Over 30 scholars from universities across China attended the conference, engaging in in-depth discussions on topics such as the regional spread of Christianity in China and the history and current state of Sino-Western cultural exchange.

The academic conference provided a valuable platform for scholars in the field and contributes to the further development of humanities and social science research at STU.
