It is reported that the staff basketball championship final, organized by the university trade union, will be held on Thursday, November 7, 2013, at 17:30 in the Air Dome. The Liberal Arts & Commerce team will face the Resources Management team in the final match.

## Final Match Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Staff Basketball Championship Final |
| Matchup | Liberal Arts & Commerce Team vs. Resources Management Team |
| Time | 17:30, Thursday, November 7, 2013 |
| Venue | Air Dome (opposite Student Dormitory Block G) |
| Admission | Limited to 60 spectators; tickets issued at the venue |
| Dress Code | Proper rubber-soled sports shoes required for entry |

Due to the limited capacity of the Air Dome, only 60 spectators will be admitted, with entry tickets distributed at the door. All attendees are required to wear proper rubber-soled sports shoes. Smoking and littering inside the venue are strictly prohibited.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Trade Union)
