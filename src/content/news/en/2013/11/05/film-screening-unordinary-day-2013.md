It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University will screen the micro-film *The Extraordinary Day Before* on the evening of November 9, 2013, at the Library Lecture Hall. Produced by Shantou Diyuan Media and a team of post-90s filmmakers, the film explores the challenges faced by fresh graduates as they confront the tension between their ideals and the realities of the job market.

## Screening Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *The Extraordinary Day Before* |
| Time | 19:00, November 9, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee |

Students interested in attending are requested to register via the campus activity booking system (http://booking.stu.edu.cn/) by 12:00 on November 8.
