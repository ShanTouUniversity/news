It is reported that the Subject Services of the Shantou University Library will host a Springer literature retrieval lecture on the evening of November 6, 2013. Xu Xi, a senior trainer from Springer, will present on the theme "Writing Papers and Conducting Research — Let Springer Soar Your Academic Wings."

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Writing Papers and Conducting Research — Let Springer Soar Your Academic Wings |
| Speaker | Xu Xi (Senior Trainer, Springer) |
| Time | 19:30–21:00, Wednesday, November 6, 2013 |
| Venue | Subject Services, Library (2nd floor, new building, inside the reference desk) |

The lecture will cover an introduction to Springer, its electronic resources, literature retrieval methods on the Springer platform, and tips for journal submission. The Springer e-journal database includes nearly 1,500 English electronic journals published since 1997, spanning 11 disciplines including natural sciences, technology, engineering, and medicine. Faculty and students engaged in academic research are welcome to attend.
