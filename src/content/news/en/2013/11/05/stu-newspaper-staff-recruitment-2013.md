The STU Newspaper is currently recruiting student editors from across the university, including positions for both writing and graphic design. Interested students are welcome to join the newspaper editorial team.

The STU Newspaper is a four-page broadsheet, with pages one and four in full color. Published biweekly, the newspaper reaches faculty and students on campus, government agencies, other universities, alumni, and parents of new students, serving as one of Shantou University'\''s important external communication platforms.

| Position | Requirements |
|----------|--------------|
| Writer/Editor | Strong planning and writing skills, good news sense |
| Graphic Designer | Strong planning ability, graphic design skills |

Students interested in joining the editorial team are encouraged to contact the STU Newspaper office for more details.
