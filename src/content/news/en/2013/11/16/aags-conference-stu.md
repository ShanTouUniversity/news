It is reported that the 9th International Conference of the Association of Asian and Global Studies (AAGS) will be hosted by Shantou University on March 22–23, 2014. The conference is organized by AAGS and co-hosted by the International Research Center of the School of Liberal Arts at Shantou University.

## Conference Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Conference | 9th AAGS International Conference |
| Organizer | Association of Asian and Global Studies (AAGS) |
| Co-organizer | International Research Center, School of Liberal Arts, STU |
| Dates | March 22–23, 2014 |
| Venue | Shantou University |
| Theme | Ethical Debates and Dilemmas in a Globalized World |

This year's conference centers on the theme "Ethical Debates and Dilemmas in a Globalized World," focusing on ethical issues and theoretical debates in the process of globalization. The conference is currently accepting paper submissions and registrations from the university community. Faculty and students interested in attending or presenting may contact the International Research Center at the School of Liberal Arts for further details.
