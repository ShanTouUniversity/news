It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on November 19, 2013, announcing bidding results for three campus construction projects, as recommended by the university bidding leading group.

## Winning Bids

| Project | Winning Bidder | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teaching area public restroom renovation | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. | 378,681.98 yuan |
| Flood drainage ditch/culvert construction | Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. | 313,588.00 yuan |
| West zone substation construction | Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. | 150,306.00 yuan |
