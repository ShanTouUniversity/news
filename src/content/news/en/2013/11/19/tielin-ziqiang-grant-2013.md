It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on November 19, 2013, to launch the selection for the Shantou Tielin Charity Association’s “Ziqiang” (Self-Strengthening) Grant.

Established at Shantou University by the Tielin Charity Association to support undergraduates from Anhui with financial difficulties, the grant embodies the spirit of benefiting from and giving back to society.

## Grant Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Undergraduate students from Anhui with financial difficulties |
| Quota | Up to 20 recipients |
| Amount | 2,500 yuan per person |
| Conditions | Funds for academic and living expenses only; recipients should actively participate in public welfare activities; after graduation and when financially able, recipients voluntarily donate back to the charity; recipients must sign a commitment letter |
| Deadline | 17:00, November 26, 2013 |
| Submission | Student Aid Management Center, Student Affairs Office (Room 106, Administration Center) |
| Selection | Student submits application form (with proof of volunteer service) → Student Aid Center reviews → Tielin Charity Association decides |

The application form and commitment letter are available in the OA attachments.
