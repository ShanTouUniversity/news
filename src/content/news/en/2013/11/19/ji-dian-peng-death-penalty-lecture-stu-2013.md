It is reported that Ji Dianpeng, director of Beijing Jidian Law Firm, will deliver an academic lecture titled "Death Penalty and China's Judicial Reform" at the School of Law, Shantou University, on the evening of November 19.

## Guest Speaker

Ji Dianpeng is the director of Beijing Jidian Law Firm, a part-time professor at Southwest University of Political Science and Law, and a specially appointed graduate tutor at the Guanghua School of Management, Peking University. He is also a member of the Criminal Professional Committee of the All China Lawyers Association and director of the China Criminal Defense Theory Research Center. He has lectured at over 50 universities across China, including Peking University, Tsinghua University, and Renmin University of China, as well as Columbia University, Cornell University, and the University of Chicago in the United States. With rich practical experience and solid theoretical foundation, he primarily handles criminal cases and has served as defense counsel in numerous high-profile murder and corruption cases.
