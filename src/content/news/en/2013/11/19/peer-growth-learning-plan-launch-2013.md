It is reported that the Peer Growth Learning Program, hosted by the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University, has officially launched. The program aims to help students better plan their university life through experience sharing from senior students.

## First Session Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Balancing Academics, Organizations, and Activities |
| Speaker | Guo Yan (Law, Class of 2011) |
| Time | November 23, 19:30 - 21:30 |
| Venue | Student Activity Hall, Library |
| Capacity | 25 participants |

Interested students can register via booking.stu.edu.cn.
