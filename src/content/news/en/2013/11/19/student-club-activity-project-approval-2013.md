It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced on November 19 the results of the fall 2013–2014 semester Student Club Activity Project Defense. The defense was held on October 30, with 18 student clubs taking part and 11 projects ultimately approved for implementation.

## Approved Projects

| No. | Club |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Yuyin Music Society |
| 2 | Track and Field Association |
| 3 | Jianqiu (Shuttlecock) Association |
| 4 | Handicraft Society |
| 5 | Science and Technology Creation Association |
| 6 | Table Tennis Association |
| 7 | Sign Language Club |
| 8 | Calligraphy and Painting Association |
| 9 | Photography Association |
| 10 | Xingchao Society |
| 11 | Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |
