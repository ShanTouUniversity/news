It is reported that the Shantou University 2013 "Inter-School Cup" Basketball Tournament has been underway for nearly a month. The three-point contest preliminary round, group stage matches, and semi-finals have all concluded, and four teams have emerged to compete in the championship game on November 23.

## Tournament Recap

Hosted by the Youth League Committee, this year's tournament attracted active participation from students across all schools. The competition progressed through the three-point contest, group stage, and semi-finals, with intensity building at each stage.
