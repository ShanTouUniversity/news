The 168th Shantou University Engineering Research Forum was scheduled for November 22, 2013, on research and applications involving high-strength and high-performance steel. The speaker, Wang Zhan, was a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Civil Engineering and Transportation of South China University of Technology.

The event was set for 2:30–4 p.m. in the College of Engineering’s Academic Exchange Room and was open to interested faculty members and students.
