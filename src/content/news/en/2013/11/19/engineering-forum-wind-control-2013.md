The 169th Shantou University Engineering Research Forum was scheduled for November 22, 2013. The topic was wind-induced vibration control and field measurements for super-tall buildings. The speaker, Xie Zhuangning, was a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Civil Engineering and Transportation of South China University of Technology.

The forum was set for 4–5:30 p.m. in the College of Engineering’s Academic Exchange Room and was open to interested faculty members and students.
