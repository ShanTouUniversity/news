Shantou University’s Research Office announced its 2013 research startup fund closeout schedule on November 19. Project leaders covered by the review were asked to complete the university’s closeout report and submit the materials to their schools by November 30. Schools would review the submissions and forward them to the Research Office, with an electronic copy also required.

Supporting records could include papers, books, patents, awards, evidence of research commercialization and newly approved projects. Two printed copies were required. The list of projects due for closeout and the report form were provided in the original OA attachments.
