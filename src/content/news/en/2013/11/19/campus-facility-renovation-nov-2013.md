Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office began renovation work in November 2013 on the Resource Management Office and staff dormitory near the student canteen. Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. of Shantou was engaged to carry out the work, covering the interior of Building 1 of the former Jingwei Clinic and the former shower room west of the Third Canteen.

The provisional project cost was RMB 600,000, with a winning bid reduction rate of 24.10 percent. Work was scheduled to start on November 20, 2013 and finish by January 18, 2014. The university warned that construction could cause noise, vibration and dust, and advised people not to enter the worksite without authorization.
