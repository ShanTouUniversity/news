The selection for the seventh cohort of interns at Bank of China Shantou Branch, a program run in cooperation with Shantou University's Business School, has concluded. It is reported that the positions drew an enthusiastic response from students upon release, and after discussions between the school and the bank's Shantou branch, ten students including Lin YS were confirmed for the cohort.

As scheduled, the selected students reported to Bank of China Shantou Branch on November 6 for pre-service training and the start of their internships.

The school expressed its gratitude to all students who took part and reminded those not selected to keep an eye on upcoming announcements from the Business School, as similar opportunities with the bank are expected to continue given the strong response to previous cohorts.
