Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced a schedule change for Employment Guidance, Class 63538, on November 6, 2013. The session, originally scheduled for periods 3–4 on Wednesday of Week 8 in Room G307, was canceled, and no make-up date had been set at the time of the notice.

The notice named Su Wenji and Wang Bin as instructors and asked enrolled students to share the update.
