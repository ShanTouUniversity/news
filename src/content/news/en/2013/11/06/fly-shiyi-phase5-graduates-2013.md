The FLY (Foreign Language YOUTH) Training Program Phase 5 "Shiyi" Plan held its graduation ceremony on November 3 at Shantou University. After rigorous assessment, 32 cadets, including Hao Aize, Liu Yongchuan, He Xiaowen, and Wang Kaibing, were granted graduation certification, with 9 outstanding cadets receiving special recognition for their exceptional performance.

The FLY Training Program is an English language development platform organized by the University Youth League Committee, designed to enhance students' English speaking skills and cross-cultural communication abilities. Since its inception, the program has successfully conducted multiple sessions, providing rich English learning and exchange opportunities for students.

The conclusion of the "Shiyi" Plan marks a successful end to the fifth session and lays a solid foundation for future sessions.
