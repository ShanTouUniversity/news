It is reported that in order to help freshmen of the class of 2013 better integrate into university life and strengthen their sense of belonging, the STU youth organization held a freshman League day in October under the theme "Hello, Shantou". A results showcase took place on November 2 in Room E204, where awards were decided through on-site voting.

## List of Winners

| Award | Winning teams |
| ----- | ------------- |
| First prize (1) | Wo He Wo De Xiao Huo Ban Men Dou Chi Dai Le |
| Second prize (2) | Xiao Chan Mao; Taishan People at STU |
| Third prize (3) | Communication Detective; Qiangqiang Food Tour; Rainbow |
| Popularity award (1) | Qiangqiang Food Tour |

Judging by the team names, most entries approached the theme through food and city exploration, guiding freshmen to discover the city of Shantou in teams while making new friends and experiencing the warmth of university life.

The organizers expressed their gratitude to all teachers and students for their attention and support, and congratulated the winning teams.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Committee of the Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
