Shantou University's Resource Management Office announced the review and payment arrangements for student-dormitory utility bills for October 2013 on November 6. The latest meter readings had been taken on October 30 and 31, following the previous reading on September 30. At the time, students could check their individual bills through the university's utility management system using their campus-network accounts.

## Review and Payment Schedule

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | October 2013 |
| Meter readings | October 30–31; previous reading on September 30 |
| Bill inquiry | Shantou University utility management system (<http://power.stu.edu.cn/>); log in with a campus-network account |
| Review period | November 6–12, 2013, during business hours |
| Review submission | Complete and sign the Utility Fee Review Application Form and submit it to the Utility Center beside the Science and Technology Building; tel. 86502489 |
| Dormitory and student-roster discrepancies | Contact the Counselor Office in Building B, Room 106; tel. 86503355 |
| Campus-card deductions | The notice scheduled Network Center deductions from November 13 if no objection was raised during the review period; tel. 86503545 |
| Regular payment period | November 13–18, 2013; overdue bills were subject to university regulations from November 19 |

The notice also reminded students to top up their campus cards if the balance was insufficient. It said objections submitted after the review period that required corrections following verification could take three to five working days to process.

> ⚠️ Individual dormitory bill details are not reproduced; students can check them through the university's utility management system. Related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
>
