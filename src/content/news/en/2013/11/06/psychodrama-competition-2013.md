Shantou University's fourth college student psychodrama competition has officially launched, with the theme "Let Your Dreams Take Flight, Set Sail from the Heart." The competition is open to all students on campus and aims to promote mental health knowledge and innovate approaches to mental health education.

Participants are required to create original psychodrama performances based on common psychological challenges faced by college students. Themes may include conflicts between ideals and reality, balancing self-awareness with interpersonal relationships, weighing romantic relationships against academic pursuits, and career planning during college years. Hosted by the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center of the Student Affairs Office, the competition encourages students to express their insights on mental health through artistic performance.
