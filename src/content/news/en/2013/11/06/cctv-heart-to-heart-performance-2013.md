The CCTV "Heart to Heart" Art Troupe will hold a goodwill performance on November 8 (Friday) at 3:00 PM at Shantou华侨 Park in Shantou City. Shantou University will organize 80 students to attend the event.

Registration opened at 9:00 PM on November 6 and closed at 4:00 PM on November 7. Students were required to book through the university's reservation system (http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm). If more than 80 students registered, a random lottery system would determine the final participants. The confirmed list was published on the university's internal office information network.

On the day of the performance, university shuttle buses will transport students to and from the venue.
