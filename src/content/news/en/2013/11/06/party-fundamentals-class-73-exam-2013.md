The theoretical examination for Shantou University Party School’s 73rd Party Fundamentals Study Class was scheduled for November 10, 2013, from 19:30 to 21:00. Candidates from the 73rd class, together with approved make-up candidates from the 72nd class, were assigned to examination venues by school or college.

## Examination Arrangements

| Venue | Schools and colleges |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture Hall 1 | College of Liberal Arts, College of Science, College of Engineering, and Law School |
| Lecture Hall 3 | Business School, College of Journalism and Communication, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Zhicheng College, Graduate School, and faculty and staff participants |

Candidates were instructed to use the assigned venue and sit with alternate seats left between them. Those unable to attend for exceptional reasons had to obtain approval from their Party general branch or directly affiliated branch and apply to the Party School office for a deferral by November 8. The notice said unexplained absences would result in cancellation of class-member status.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
