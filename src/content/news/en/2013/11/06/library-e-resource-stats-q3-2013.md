The Shantou University Library has released the Q3 2013 e-resource usage statistics report, aimed at understanding database usage patterns and optimizing resource allocation.

The data shows that the CNKI China Academic Journals Network Publishing Database led all databases with 91,047 downloads during the quarter, followed by the VIP Chinese Scientific Journals Database with 19,555 downloads, and the CNKI China Master's Theses Full-Text Database with 15,851 downloads.

The statistics provide data support for the university to further optimize its e-resource procurement strategy and improve resource utilization efficiency.
