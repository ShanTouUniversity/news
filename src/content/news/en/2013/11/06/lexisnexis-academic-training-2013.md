The STU Library will hold a training session on the LexisNexis Academic database on Thursday, Nov. 7, from 19:30 to 21:00, at the Subject Services Department on the 2nd floor of the new library building. Wang Juesui, a product application consultant from LexisNexis, will be the presenter.

The session will cover how to search for journal articles, academic papers, company archives, and industry reports in fields such as finance, accounting, economics, foreign languages, news, business, law, medicine, and biology. It will also demonstrate how to locate laws, court cases, and international treaties using the database, as well as how to access news and business information from international newspapers, news agencies, and TV/radio programs. Additionally, attendees will learn how to retrieve news in languages including French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish, and Dutch, and discover tips to improve search efficiency and track research hotspots.
