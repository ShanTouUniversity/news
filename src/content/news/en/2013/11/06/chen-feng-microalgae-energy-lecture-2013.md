Professor Fēng Chén, Vice Dean and Endowed Chair Professor at the College of Engineering, Peking University, will deliver a lecture titled "Microalgae as Sustainable Bioenergy and Bioresources" on November 8 (Friday) at 2:30 PM in ACC Room #5.

Professor Chen is a recipient of the National "Thousand Talents Program" and was formerly a Chang Jiang Scholars Program Distinguished Professor. He was selected into the Chinese Academy of Sciences "Hundred Talents Program." He received his Ph.D. in Biochemical Engineering from the University of Queensland, Australia, in 1993. Previously, he served as a tenured professor at the University of Hong Kong, concurrently holding positions as Head of the Department of Botany and Executive Dean of the Faculty of Science. He currently directs the Institute of Food and Bioresource Engineering at Peking University's College of Engineering, with research focuses on microalgae biotechnology and bioenergy.

The lecture is hosted by the College of Science and is open to all students and faculty members.
