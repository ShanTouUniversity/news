Professor Lǐ Zhìxián, Deputy Head of the Department of Chinese at the National University of Singapore (NUS) and Coordinator of the Southeast Asia Studies Cluster, will deliver a lecture titled "Reflections on Disputes Among Singapore Teochew Societies" on November 7 (Thursday) from 10:20 AM to 12:00 PM at the University Library Auditorium.

Professor Lǐ holds a Ph.D. in History from NUS and a Master's degree in Higher Education from Ohio University, USA. He previously served as a lead lecturer at SIM University and currently holds positions as Vice President of the Nanyang Association and Academic Committee Member of the International Teochew Research Association.

The lecture is hosted by a relevant academic unit at Shantou University and is open to all students and faculty members.
