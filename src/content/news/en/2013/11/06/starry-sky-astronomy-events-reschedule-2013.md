Due to weather conditions, several events under the "Starry Sky" astronomy series organized by the Shantou University Youth League Committee have been rescheduled.

The stargazing event originally planned for November 2, titled "Let'\''s Stargaze Tonight," has been postponed to November 10 from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM on the university football field. The "Meteorological Bureau Tour" activity has finalized its participant list with 39 students, including Wang Zhenyu. Additionally, the registration deadline for the "Astronomy and Meteorology Knowledge Competition" has been extended to 12:00 PM on November 7.

The "Starry Sky" astronomy series is a popular science outreach brand activity at STU, aimed at stimulating students'\'' interest in astronomy and meteorology and popularizing scientific knowledge.
