It is reported that, after consultation with the cooperating troops, Shantou University has scheduled a live-fire shooting session for this year's military training on July 6. All students taking part in the training — the Class of 2012 — are in principle required to participate, as the military training course is compulsory and live-fire shooting is a key component of it.

## Bus Schedule by College

To keep the activity safe and orderly, the university will provide buses to take students to the shooting range. Students will gather in front of the School Motto Monument and depart according to the following schedule:

| Departure Time | Unit |
| -------------- | ---- |
| 7:30 | College of Engineering |
| 8:10 | College of Liberal Arts |
| 8:40 | College of Science |
| 9:10 | Law School |
| 9:40 | Business School |
| 14:30 | College of Journalism |
| 15:00 | Zhicheng College |
| 15:30 | Academy of Arts |

Students will board in order by college, and those not yet called should wait in place.

## Safety and Discipline Requirements

According to the report, participants must wear their military training uniforms when entering the range and arrive at the designated gathering point strictly on time. Inside the shooting range, students must follow the instructions of the drill instructors. In addition, each college has arranged for counselors to accompany the students and assist with organization to ensure safety throughout the exercise.

> Source: STU OA Notice (People's Armed Forces Department)
