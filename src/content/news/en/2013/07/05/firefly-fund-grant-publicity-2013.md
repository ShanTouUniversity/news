It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University, together with the Youth Volunteers Association and the Firefly charity fund, issued a notice on July 5, 2013, publishing the proposed recipients of the Study Light student grant.

The Study Light grant was established by the youth volunteer Firefly charity fund. This round received 20 applications, and after review by the fund board, 17 students are proposed for funding. The individual proposed recipients are listed in the public-notice attachment.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Grant | Study Light student grant |
| Established by | Youth volunteer Firefly charity fund |
| Applications received | 20 |
| Proposed recipients | 17 students |
| Objection deadline | 17:00, July 10 |
| How to comment | In writing or by phone; organizations should affix an official seal, individuals should sign with real name |
| Fund email | fireflyfundofstu@163.com |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee of Shantou University, Youth Volunteers Association, Firefly charity fund |
| Date | July 5, 2013 |

During the publicity period, anyone with objections to the proposed results may comment in writing or by phone before 17:00 on July 10. Details of the recipient list are contained in the OA attachments and are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
> ⚠️ The recipient list and other attachments are available on the OA system.
