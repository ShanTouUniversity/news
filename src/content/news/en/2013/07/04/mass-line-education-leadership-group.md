It is reported that the CPC Committee of Shantou University issued a document on July 4 (STU Party Document [2013] No. 20): after deliberation, the committee decided to formally establish a leadership group for the Party's mass line education practice campaign, charged with coordinating related work across the university.

## Composition of the Leadership

According to the document, the group is led by top members of the university's leadership:

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Head | Song Yaozhen |
| Deputy Heads | Xu Xiaohu, Luo Nianchao, Shi Ganggang |
| Members | Li Dan, Lin Danming, Wulanhasi, and 26 others (29 in total) |

## Office and Four Working Groups

A leading office is established under the group, with Luo Nianchao serving as office director and Zeng Dabin, He Wenbiao, Sun Fenqiang, and Bai Youjin as deputy directors. The office oversees four working groups:

| Working Group | Head |
| --- | --- |
| Secretariat | Zeng Dabin |
| Comprehensive Affairs | He Wenbiao |
| Publicity | Bai Youjin |
| Supervision | Sun Fenqiang |

The establishment of this body marks the entry of Shantou University's mass line education practice campaign into a stage with dedicated organizational coordination. The document has been distributed to all units across the university.

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Committee)
