The 2013 Shantou high school entrance exam results have been released, bringing good news from the Affiliated High School of Shantou University. It is reported that the school's 42 candidates performed strongly overall, with a class average of **702 points**.

The score breakdown shows a concentration of high scores: **4 candidates scored above 800, 14 above 780, and 24 above 700**, meaning more than half of the class reached the 700-point mark.

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Total candidates | 42 |
| Above 800 | 4 |
| Above 780 | 14 |
| Above 700 | 24 |
| Class average | 702 |

It is reported that the school congratulated the graduating class on their outstanding performance.
