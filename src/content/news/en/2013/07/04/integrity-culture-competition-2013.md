It is reported that the Discipline Inspection Commission Office issued a notice on July 4, 2013, organizing the university's participation in the 2nd National Campus Integrity Culture Competition. The event follows Guangdong Education Department Document Yue-Jiao-Si-Ban-Han [2013] No. 27 and Ministry of Education Document Jiao-Si-Zheng-Ting-Han [2013] No. 15, calling for integrity-themed works from universities nationwide.

The competition covers four categories, spanning stage performance, visual arts and new media.

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Performing arts | Integrity-themed stage performance works |
| Painting, calligraphy and photography | Calligraphy, painting and photographic works |
| Artistic design | Integrity-themed design works |
| Online and new media | Online and new-media works |

For on-campus submission, entrants should prepare application forms, works and discs according to the requirements of each category and submit them by August 30. The university Commission for Discipline Inspection will organize expert review and then recommend selected entries to the provincial education department.
