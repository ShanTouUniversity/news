It is reported that Shantou University will convene a meeting of its Party Standing Committee on July 5 to discuss and plan the university's Mass-Line Education Campaign.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 3:00 PM, Friday, July 5, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, Administration Center |
| Theme | Studying central and provincial directives and planning the university's education campaign |
| Issued by | Organization and United Front Work Department, General Administration Office |

The session will serve as both the 9th Party Standing Committee meeting of 2013 and a meeting of the university's education campaign leading group. Participants will be university leaders and heads of relevant departments, totaling about 30 people.

Those unable to attend shall ask for leave in advance by phone at 86502345 or by email at o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, General Administration Office)
