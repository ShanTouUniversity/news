It is reported that the Office of International Affairs issued a notice on July 18, 2013, that a second round of recommendations is open for the 2013 Outstanding Undergraduate International Exchange Program.

According to a letter from the China Scholarship Council, Shantou University may recommend an additional 10 students for CSC study-abroad scholarships, allocated as follows:

| Program | Places |
| --- | --- |
| College of Engineering – University of Calgary | 5 |
| Business School – University of Manitoba | 3 |
| Business School – Catholic University of Portugal | 2 |

Interested second- and third-year students of the Business School and the College of Engineering are advised to take foreign language examinations as early as possible and obtain admission letters or invitation letters from the relevant foreign universities before the online application deadline. Further arrangements will be announced by the respective colleges, with details in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ Details are available on the OA system.
