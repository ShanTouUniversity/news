It is reported that Shantou University has issued Document No. 92 [2013] to release the 2013 annual budget for the main campus to all relevant units for implementation. The budget was approved at a university leadership meeting.

The budget was not only thoroughly reviewed by the Budget Committee, but relevant items were also discussed by the Teaching Committee, the Research Committee, and the Laboratory Affairs Committee at the Budget Committee's request, with review comments provided. This arrangement further enhanced the scientific rigor of the budget review and created favorable conditions for securing support from the foundation.

## Guidance on Lump-Sum Funds for Teaching Units

To ensure teaching units at the grassroots level have adequate resources, the university offered the following guidance:

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Student-related funds | Each teaching unit should earmark a portion of its lump-sum funds for student internships and student activities; student activity funds earmarked by colleges are to be approved by the person in charge of student affairs as authorized by the college head |
| Allocation to grassroots units | At least 50% of the remaining funds should be allocated to departments, teaching and research sections, and other grassroots teaching units for teaching purposes |
| Distribution method | Priority is given to essential low-value consumables for grassroots units; the remainder is distributed among grassroots units based 50% on faculty headcount and 50% on teaching hours undertaken in the previous budget year |

All units are required to observe national financial discipline, arrange expenditures rationally, practice economy, eliminate waste, and improve the efficiency of fund use to support the university's development and the realization of its eight-year plan.
