It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently published a notice on additional costs for the renovation of the Building E teaching building (Phase II).

The project began on July 1, 2013. To meet fire safety acceptance standards and ensure the safe use of the building, the original scope of work needs to be supplemented: the seriously rusted fire hydrant cabinets in the public restrooms on the third and fourth floors will be replaced, 20 dry powder fire extinguishers and five extinguisher storage cabinets will be provided, and 50-square-millimetre grounding wire will be laid anew. The additional work is preliminarily estimated at about RMB 26,000, keeping the cumulative increase within the 10% limit set by the university.

In accordance with the Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering Management of Capital Construction Projects, the Infrastructure Office has published the additional costs for comment.

Those with objections may submit written comments to the Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office before the deadline.
