It is reported that the Resources Management Office of Shantou University has published the **June 2013 utility bills for faculty and staff housing**, and staff can now log in to the utility-management system to review them. Any objection may be raised at the Water and Electricity Center from July 15 to 17; charges that remain unopposed at the end of that period will be deducted from July salaries by the Finance Office.

## Billing and meter-reading details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | June 2013 |
| Current meter readings | July 1 and 2, 2013 |
| Previous meter reading | June 1, 2013 |

## How to check and verify charges

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| System | STU utility-management system |
| Web address | http://power.stu.edu.cn |
| Login account | Campus network account |
| Verification period | July 15 to 17, 2013 |
| Verification location | Water and Electricity Center |
| Telephone | 2489 |

Staff can log in to the system to view the June utility charges for their own housing. Those who find a discrepancy with actual usage may visit the Water and Electricity Center during the verification period to check the records.

## Payment arrangements

| Case | How it is handled |
| --- | --- |
| No objection after the verification period | Deducted from July salaries by the Finance Office |
| Staff without a salary account | Pay in person at the Finance Office |

The Resources Management Office has reminded affected staff to check their charges promptly so as not to miss the objection and verification period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
