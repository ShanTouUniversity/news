Shantou University has opened campus selection for the 6th National College Student Innovation-Entrepreneurship Annual Meeting, and will recommend 2 projects chosen from its 2012 and 2013 national-level training program projects.

It is reported that, under the national innovation training program schedule, the Department of Higher Education of the Ministry of Education plans to hold the 6th annual meeting at Central South University in mid-to-late October. The meeting will consist of an academic session, a project exhibition, and an entrepreneurship roadshow.

| Session | Scale and format |
| --- | --- |
| Academic session | Student academic presentations from innovation projects; about 90 papers to be selected nationwide, discussed in 5 groups, 15 minutes per presentation; 10 papers to be named outstanding, of which 5 will be presented at the plenary session |
| Project exhibition | Display via posters plus physical exhibits; about 120 projects to be selected nationwide, with a "My Favorite Project" award as well as innovation project and entrepreneurship project awards |
| Entrepreneurship roadshow | About 30 projects to be selected nationwide, 15 minutes per pitch |

STU has a quota of 2 recommended projects, drawn from its 2012 and 2013 national-level training program projects. Teams wishing to apply should submit the recommendation form together with the corresponding paper or exhibition-board materials, by category, to `wtyang@stu.edu.cn` before 17:00 on July 11.

Detailed application requirements and templates for the recommendation form, papers, and exhibition boards are provided in the compressed attachment to the OA notice.
