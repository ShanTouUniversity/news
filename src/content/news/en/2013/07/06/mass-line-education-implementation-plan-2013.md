It is reported that the CPC Committee of Shantou University issued the Implementation Plan for Carrying Out the Mass-Line Education Campaign (STU Dang Zi [2013] No. 21) on July 6. Under the arrangement of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee, Shantou University was designated as one of the first-batch participants in the provincial campaign, which runs from July 1 to December 31.

According to the plan, the guiding thinking is to hold high the great banner of socialism with Chinese characteristics, follow Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the Three Represents and the Scientific Outlook on Development, implement the spirit of the 18th CPC National Congress and the series of important speeches by General Secretary Xi Jinping, stay focused on preserving the Party's advanced nature and purity, take serving the people, pragmatism and integrity as the main content, give priority to the university leadership and leading cadres, highlight work-style building, resolutely oppose formalism, bureaucratism, hedonism and extravagance, and address prominent problems strongly felt by faculty and students, so as to provide a strong guarantee for the healthy and rapid development of the university.

Throughout the campaign runs the general requirement of looking in the mirror, Tidying attire, taking a bath and treating illness. Looking in the mirror means measuring gaps in purpose awareness, work style and integrity against the Party Constitution, integrity codes, style requirements, public expectations and role models; tidying attire means enforcing discipline and correcting conduct in line with the requirement of serving the people with pragmatism and integrity; taking a bath means conducting criticism and self-criticism in the spirit of rectification to achieve self-purification and self-improvement; treating illness means upholding the principle of learning from past mistakes to avoid future ones and curing the sickness to save the patient, educating, investigating and addressing work-style problems according to their circumstances.

## Schedule

| Phase | Period | Key tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Study, education and opinion solicitation | July 1–September 11 | Set up leading bodies, finalize the plan, hold the mobilization meeting on July 6, organize intensive study, and widely solicit opinions |
| Problem identification and criticism | September 12–October 31 | Carry out the Take the Lead in Changing Work Style campaign and the Five-Ones actions, hold the special democratic meeting of the university leadership on October 16, and hold special organizational meetings in grassroots Party branches |
| Rectification, implementation and institution building | November 1–December 31 | Publish rectification task lists for public supervision, conduct targeted governance, strictly educate and manage cadres, and strengthen institution building |

The plan states that the campaign covers all Party members whose Party organizational ties are with the university, with the university leadership and cadres at all levels taking the lead. To strengthen organization and leadership, the university set up a leading group for the campaign headed by Party Secretary Song Yaozhen, with President Xu Xiaohu, Deputy Party Secretary Luo Nianchao and Medical College Party Secretary Shi Ganggang as deputy heads, plus an office with secretarial, general, publicity and supervision teams.

The plan also sets out requirements: clarifying responsibilities with the Party Secretary as the first responsible person; strengthening publicity through the campus newspaper, website, radio, e-bulletins and WeChat platforms; dispatching supervisors to grassroots Party organizations for whole-process oversight; and ensuring the campaign and daily work reinforce each other by integrating the campaign with duty performance, Party building and teaching quality improvement. Each grassroots unit is required to formulate its own working plan in line with local conditions and file it with the university leading group before July 15.
