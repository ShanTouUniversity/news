It is reported that the CPC Committee of Shantou University issued a document (STU Dang Zi [2013] No. 22) on July 6, circulating the speech delivered by Party Secretary Song Yaozhen at the university's mobilization meeting for the mass-line education campaign held the same day, and requiring all units to organize Party members and cadres to study, discuss and implement it.

In the speech, Song noted that carrying out the campaign, whose main content is serving the people with pragmatism and integrity, was a major decision made at the 18th CPC National Congress. General Secretary Xi Jinping issued multiple important instructions on the campaign, the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau and the Political Bureau held repeated deliberations, the central authorities convened a working meeting on June 18, the Political Bureau held a special meeting from June 22 to 25 to examine the implementation of the central Eight-Point Regulation, and the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee held its provincial mobilization and working meetings on July 1. Under the provincial arrangement, Shantou University, as a first-batch participant, formally launched the campaign on July 1.

The speech explained the significance of the campaign from four angles: it is essential for achieving the goals set at the 18th CPC National Congress, since only by rooting the Party among the people can the nation pool strength to realize the Chinese Dream; it is essential for preserving the Party's advanced nature and purity and consolidating its governing foundation and status, which hinges on following the mass line and staying close to the people; it is essential for resolving prominent problems strongly felt by the public, requiring a thorough overhaul of misconduct and focusing on formalism, bureaucratism, hedonism and extravagance; and it meets the university's own need to strengthen Party building, with all Party organizations and members aligning their thinking and actions with the central and provincial decisions.

The speech summarized the guiding thinking, general requirements, basic principles, main tasks and steps set out in the university's implementation plan: the campaign runs from July 1 to December 31 in three phases covering study and opinion solicitation, problem identification and criticism, and rectification with institution building. It stressed that whether the university leadership and cadres can identify and resolve their prominent problems will be the key yardstick of the campaign's quality and effectiveness, calling for accurate problem identification, profound self-analysis and solid resolution.

On organization and leadership, the speech noted that the university has established a leading group headed by Song Yaozhen with Xu Xiaohu, Luo Nianchao and Shi Ganggang as deputy heads, supported by an office with four working teams. Grassroots Party organizations are to set up corresponding bodies and practical working plans, leading cadres are to oversee the campaign in the units under their charge, and discipline inspection, organization and publicity departments are to work closely together, alongside stronger publicity, whole-process supervision, and coordination so that the campaign and daily work reinforce each other. Li Xiufeng, head of the 22nd Provincial Supervision Group, attended the mobilization meeting with guidance remarks.
