It is reported that the MOFET Institute in Israel, a center for the professional development of educators and teacher educators, is organizing a nine-day study tour, MOFETeach 2014 - Education for Sustainability, to be held in Israel from January 20 to 28, 2014, and invites interested participants to apply.

The study tour focuses on sustainability in the education system and public outreach. Participants will learn about Israel's work on education for sustainability, covering topics such as energy, water, desert agriculture, green building, flora and fauna, and human adaptation to desert conditions, while visiting educational institutions and their support centers to see study programs and field projects first-hand.

## Itinerary

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | MOFETeach 2014 - Education for Sustainability study tour |
| Dates | January 20–28, 2014 (9 days) |
| Location | Israel |
| Route | The South - the Dead Sea - Jerusalem - Tel Aviv - Galilee in the north |

According to the announcement, the tour will run from the south of Israel through the Dead Sea, Jerusalem and Tel Aviv up to Galilee in the north, combining professional visits with an encounter with the history of Israel.

One day of the program will be devoted to a mini-conference at which participants and Israeli guests may present and discuss their own projects and research on education for sustainability. Another day will be devoted to a visit to the Cleantech 2014 international exhibition, the 18th International Summit and Exhibition for Renewable Energy and Water Technologies, Recycling and Environmental Quality, Infrastructure and Green Building.

## Who May Join and How to Inquire

The tour is aimed at heads of faculties and lecturers at academic and educational institutions, professionals and policy makers in education and sustainability, teacher educators, curriculum developers, and those working on corporate social responsibility and sustainable development programs. The program is led by Bilha Cohen.

For cost and further information, contact the tour coordinator Bilha Cohen at international@macam.ac.il. An English introduction is attached to the original notice, and a detailed program will be published separately.
