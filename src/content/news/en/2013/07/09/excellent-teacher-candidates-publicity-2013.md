It is reported that the Human Resources Office issued a publicity notice on July 9, announcing the university's proposed nominees for the 2013 Shantou Excellent Teacher and Excellent Educator awards.

The nomination was conducted in accordance with document Shanshi Jiao [2013] No. 42. It follows the on-campus nomination process launched on July 2. The candidates were recommended by various units, reviewed by functional departments, and selected by the university evaluation panel.

Four candidates are proposed, as follows:

| Award | Proposed candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Shantou Excellent Teacher | Chen Zhimin |
| Shantou Excellent Teacher | Wu Fan |
| Shantou Excellent Teacher | Yu Jin |
| Shantou Excellent Educator | Huang Yumei |

Objections to any of the above candidates may be submitted in writing to the Human Resources Office by July 15. Details are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Objection deadline | Before July 15 |
| Location | Room 315, New Administration Center (Human Resources Office) |
| How to submit | In writing; submissions from units should bear an official seal, and submissions from individuals should be signed with the real name |
| Phone | 86502340 |
| Email | rscgs@stu.edu.cn |
