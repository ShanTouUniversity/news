The "China Dream" Youth Entrepreneurship Contest is now open for entries, and interested STU students should sign up before July 15.

It is reported that the contest is jointly held by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League and partner organizations, running from June to December 2013. Entries fall into three categories: animation design, e-commerce, and cultural creativity. The corresponding document is Tuan Yue Ban Fa [2013] No. 42.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Categories | Animation design, e-commerce, cultural creativity |
| Contest period | June to December 2013 |
| Startup Star Dream Class | Based in Guangzhou; participants advance directly to the preliminary round |
| Youth online-merchant nominations | Each city and university recommends at least 2 young online merchants with annual sales above 1 million yuan, with entries due by July 15 |
| Entrepreneur profile nominations | At least 1 representative young entrepreneur to be nominated, with materials due by July 15 |

On campus, students who wish to take part should register before July 15. After registering, they may contact Chen Zepeng of the Student Entrepreneurship Center at `86502256` or `zpchen@stu.edu.cn`.

Detailed contest rules, entry procedures, and nomination forms are provided in the OA attachments.
