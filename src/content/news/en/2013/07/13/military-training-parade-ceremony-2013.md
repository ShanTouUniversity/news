It is reported that Shantou University will hold its 2013 Student Military Training Parade and Awards Ceremony at 4:30 p.m. on July 14 at the university athletics field. The event will review this year's military training program and honor outstanding groups and individuals.

All participating students and counselors from each college are required to assemble at the athletics field by 4:00 p.m. Party branch secretaries of each college (residential college) will be seated on the rostrum to observe the parade.

The training program is organized by the People's Armed Forces Department of the university. Following the parade, the awards segment will announce the list of advanced collectives and individuals recognized during this year's training.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
