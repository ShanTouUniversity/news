It is reported that Shantou University has launched an open opinion solicitation on work-style issues of the leadership and offices of the Organization Department of the Provincial Party Committee, in support of the ongoing mass-line education campaign.

The solicitation focuses on the "four winds": formalism, bureaucratism, hedonism, and extravagance. University units, faculty, and staff are invited to submit comments and suggestions on the leadership group and its members, as well as individual offices, of the Provincial Organization Department.

## How to Participate

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Subject | Work-style issues of the Provincial Organization Department leadership, its members, and its offices |
| Method | Email to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn |
| Recipient | Organization and United Front Work Department of the STU Party Committee |
| Deadline | Before July 23 (Tuesday) |

A dedicated opinion form is attached to the original notice as a reference for submissions.
