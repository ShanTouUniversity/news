It is reported that the Continuing Education Center of Shantou University released the 2013 orientation training schedule for university teachers on July 11. The program consists of five courses, all taught by faculty members from Hanshan Normal University.

The schedule is as follows:

| Course | Lecturer | Title and affiliation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Higher Education Psychology | Sun Yueliang | Associate Professor, Department of Education, Hanshan Normal University |
| Higher Education | Wang Liying | Associate Professor, Department of Education, Hanshan Normal University |
| Introduction to Higher Education Laws and Regulations | Lin Tianwei | Associate Researcher, School of Ceramics, Hanshan Normal University |
| Professional Ethics for Teachers | Li Jing | Associate Professor, Department of Education, Hanshan Normal University |
| Modern Educational Technology | Huang Yingling | Associate Professor, Department of Education, Hanshan Normal University |

Participating teachers may attend the courses according to the schedule above.
