It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Youth League Committee and nine other departments have jointly launched the 2013 Zhanchi Program, a Guangdong-wide campaign to improve university students' employment and entrepreneurship skills, under the theme of spreading wings in the workplace and setting sail toward dreams. Applications are now open, and internship postings close at 12:00 on July 15, 2013.

The program serves current university students, fresh graduates and unemployed graduates from recent cohorts, offering internships, probation placements and year-round employment and entrepreneurship services. Internships are concentrated in July and August 2013, while probation and employment services run throughout the year.

## Three Internship Tracks

| Track | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Provincial workplaces | Internships at provincial-level government bodies, public institutions and enterprises, featuring "five ones": joining an exchange seminar, being paired with an internship mentor, taking part in Party or Youth League activities, interviewing a workplace leader, and learning one area of professional work |
| Outstanding private firms | Internships at top private enterprises, high-tech firms and promising small and medium-sized enterprises, offering technical, management and marketing posts |
| Social service organizations | Internships at social groups, foundations, private non-enterprise entities and community public service centers, introducing trends in social governance innovation |

## How and When to Apply

Students may register and select posts on the Zhanchi Program homepage at http://zcjh.gdcyl.org or via the mobile platform. Applications close at noon on July 15, with random lotteries to assign posts held on July 12 and July 15.
