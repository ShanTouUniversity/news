It is reported that the power distribution expansion project in the student dormitory area of Shantou University will break ground on July 27. The Infrastructure Office has commissioned Guangdong Yuedong Electric Power Engineering Co., Ltd. for the construction, with a contract value of RMB 2.38 million. The project is scheduled for completion before September 4.

## Construction Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contractor | Guangdong Yuedong Electric Power Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Scope | High-voltage outgoing wiring at Switch Station No. 1, incoming and outgoing cables for box-type substations, incoming and outgoing cables for dormitories, and installation of new distribution boxes |
| Contract value | RMB 2.38 million |
| Duration | Starting July 27, 2013, to be completed before September 4 |

The university notes that construction will generate noise, vibration and dust, and appreciates the understanding of teachers and students. Pedestrians near the construction area are advised to stay safe and not to enter the site without permission.
