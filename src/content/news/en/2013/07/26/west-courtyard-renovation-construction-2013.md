It is reported that the renovation project for the west campus courtyard buildings will officially break ground on July 29, 2013, with completion scheduled before October 6.

The project is undertaken by Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd., covering the interior and exterior renovation of four two-story courtyard buildings in the west campus. The winning bid offered a 24.325% reduction, with a provisional price of 3 million yuan.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Interior and exterior renovation of four two-story courtyard buildings in the west campus |
| Contractor | Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Provisional price | 3 million yuan (24.325% bid reduction) |
| Start date | July 29, 2013 |
| Scheduled completion | Before October 6, 2013 |

Noise and dust are expected during construction, and nearby students and staff are asked for their understanding. All are advised to stay clear of the construction area for safety.
