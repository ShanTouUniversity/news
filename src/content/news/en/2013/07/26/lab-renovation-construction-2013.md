It is reported that the laboratory renovation project across six departments will officially break ground on July 26, 2013, with completion scheduled before September 13.

The project is undertaken by Shantou Jian'an Decoration Corporation, covering the interior demolition and reconstruction of laboratories in the Departments of Physics, Biology, Chemistry, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. The winning bid offered an 18% reduction, with a provisional price of 1 million yuan.

| --- | --- |

| Contractor | Shantou Jian'an Decoration Corporation |
| Provisional price | 1 million yuan (18% bid reduction) |
| Start date | July 26, 2013 |
| Scheduled completion | Before September 13, 2013 |

Noise and dust are expected during construction, and nearby students and staff are asked for their understanding. All are advised to stay clear of the construction area for safety.
