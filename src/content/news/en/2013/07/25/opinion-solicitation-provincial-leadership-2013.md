It is reported that Shantou University has launched an opinion solicitation on strengthening the work style of the leadership group and its members of the Provincial Party Committee.

The solicitation follows a notice from the Office of the Provincial Leading Group for Education and Practice Activities. University units are asked to gather views broadly and compile them into written reports before submitting them together.

## How to Participate

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Subject | Work style of the Provincial Party Committee leadership and its members |
| Format | Written report compiled by each unit |
| Method | Email to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn |
| Deadline | Before July 29 (Monday) |

Explanatory materials are attached to the original notice for reference in organizing discussions and drafting reports.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
>
