It is reported that applications for the 2013 projects under Guangdong's 12th Five-Year Plan for Philosophy and Social Science have started. No topic guide is issued this year, and applicants may choose their own topics.

## Categories and funding

| Category | Number and funding |
| -------- | ------------------ |
| General projects | About 100 projects |
| Youth projects | About 40 projects, funded at 30,000, 40,000 or 50,000 yuan |
| Follow-up funding projects | About 10 projects, 40,000 yuan each |

General projects are expected to be completed within 2 to 3 years. A monograph should exceed 100,000 characters, a research report should exceed 30,000 characters, and a collection of papers should be based on at least 3 publicly published papers in the series.

## Eligibility

- Applicants without an associate senior title or a doctoral degree need recommendations from two peer experts with full senior titles;
- For youth projects, both the applicant and all team members must be no older than 35 (before September 11, 2013);
- Each principal investigator may apply for only 1 project and may not join any other project as a member;
- Those leading ongoing national, ministerial or provincial projects may not apply as principal investigators, but may join as members, with each person joining no more than 2 projects.

## Submission method and deadlines

| Category | Method and deadlines |
| -------- | -------------------- |
| General and youth projects | Submit online at http://www.gdpplgopss.gov.cn from July 15 to September 2; after review, submit 6 hard copies before 12:00 noon on September 11 |
| Follow-up funding projects | No online submission required; send the application form and a brief introduction of the results to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn before September 9, and submit hard copies before September 12, with all unit and personal names removed from the results |

It is reported that results submitted for follow-up funding must be anonymized, and the application templates are attached to the OA notice.
