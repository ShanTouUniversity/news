It is reported that applications for the 2013 Shantou Science-Technology Plan have started. Applicants at the university are asked to follow the unified schedule arranged by the Research Office for formal review and online submission.

This year's plan covers industrial research, agricultural science and technology, social development research, science-technology capacity and environment building, and key medical and health projects. It also includes special programs for industry-university-research cooperation, international science-technology cooperation, the innovation fund for small and medium-sized enterprises, and the cultivation of high-tech enterprises for listing.

## Eligibility restrictions

Eligibility for this round is subject to several restrictions, mainly:

- Principal investigators with 2 or more ongoing municipal-level projects are generally not eligible;
- Those with major violations found in audits are not eligible;
- Projects already approved and funded shall not be submitted again (except cases requiring matching local funds or carry-over projects);
- Incomplete materials, multiple submissions, or submissions without recommendation from the competent department will fail the qualification review.

## Required attachments

Applications must include the following:

| No. | Document |
| --- | -------- |
| 1 | Business license (public institutions provide the institution certificate) |
| 2 | Organization code certificate |
| 3 | Project feasibility report |
| 4 | Balance sheet of the previous year (with finance seal) |

## Three key deadlines

| Deadline | Task |
| -------- | ---- |
| Before August 1 | Send the application form to cszhu@stu.edu.cn for formal review by the Research Office |
| Before August 6 | Submit online via the Research Office account at http://sbsys.shantou.net.cn |
| Before August 9 | After passing the online review, print two hard copies of the PDF and submit them to the Research Office |

It is reported that online submission must be made through the Research Office account, and no separate individual registration is required. Missing any step by its deadline may affect subsequent recommendation.
