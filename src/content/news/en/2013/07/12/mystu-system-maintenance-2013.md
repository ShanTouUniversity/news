It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University will perform scheduled maintenance on the MYSTU and E-Learning systems from 00:00 to 06:00 on July 13, 2013. Related online learning services will be temporarily unavailable during the maintenance period.

## Maintenance Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance period | 00:00–06:00, July 13, 2013 |
| Affected systems | MYSTU and E-Learning systems |
| Affected services | All E-Learning applications |
| Affected URLs | my.stu.edu.cn, digiu.com.cn and digiu.cn |
| Issuing unit | Network and Information Center |

The listed websites and all E-Learning applications will be shut down during maintenance. No separate notice will be issued when the systems return to service, so users are advised to make arrangements in advance.

## Contact Information

For inquiries about the maintenance, contact the Network and Information Center:

| Channel | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Landline | 0754-82503551 |
| Email | help@stu.edu.cn |

The Network and Information Center apologizes for any inconvenience caused during the maintenance period and asks users to inform one another.
