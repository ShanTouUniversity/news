It is reported that the Network and Information Center will carry out a scheduled data backup for the MYSTU system, during which the platform will be temporarily taken offline.

## Maintenance Schedule

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Downtime | July 13, 2013, 00:00-06:00 |
| Purpose | MYSTU system data backup |
| Scope | All university users; all MYSTU functions will be unavailable |
| Affected URLs | my.stu.edu.cn, digiu.com.cn, digiu.cn |
| Recovery | To be restored after maintenance (no separate notice) |

Users are advised to inform each other and plan accordingly. The center apologizes for any inconvenience.
