It is reported that the air-dome basketball training arena will undergo indoor floor concrete pouring on July 4, with a construction period of one day. During the work, concrete pump trucks will travel via the campus gate, the area in front of the university motto monument, the Engineering Building, and the Structure and Wind Tunnel Laboratory (Medical Teaching Building), before parking on the road northwest of Building G. The road section will be closed for one day, and passing vehicles and pedestrians should take a detour.

| --- | --- |

| Closed section | Road northwest of Building G (pump truck operating area) |
| Travel advisory | Road closed for one day; vehicles and pedestrians should detour |
