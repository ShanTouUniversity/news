It is reported that, in preparation for the 2013 general university admissions work, Shantou University will hold an admissions meeting at 10:00 a.m. on Friday, July 5, in Meeting Room 213 of the Administration Center. All members of the admissions leadership group and admissions staff recommended by each unit will attend.

| Key Information | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 10:00 a.m., Friday, July 5 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 213, Administration Center |
| Attendees | All members of the admissions leadership group and admissions staff recommended by each unit |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Admissions Office)
