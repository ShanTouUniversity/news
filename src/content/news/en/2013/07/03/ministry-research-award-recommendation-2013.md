Nominations for the 2013 Higher Education Outstanding Research Achievements Awards in Science and Technology of the Ministry of Education began on July 3. It is reported that Shantou University's Research Office has issued on-campus nomination arrangements, and all eligible research achievements may apply.

The award categories are:

| Category | Coverage |
|----------|----------|
| Natural Science Award | Achievements in basic and applied basic research |
| Technological Invention Award | Major technological inventions in products, processes, or materials made by applying scientific and technological knowledge |
| Science and Technology Progress Award | Including technological development, social welfare, and national security categories |
| Patent Award | Granted patents that have produced returns |

In addition, projects that have already won a second prize or above at the provincial or ministerial level and are to be recommended by the Ministry of Education for the 2014 National Science and Technology Awards may directly apply for the national awards as required.

The main eligibility requirements for each category are summarized as follows:

| Category | Key Requirements |
|----------|------------------|
| Natural Science Award | Representative publications must have been published openly before October 31, 2012 |
| Technological Invention Award | Must have been applied in practice for more than one year and have been granted an invention patent |
| Science and Technology Progress Award | Should generally have passed appraisal, been applied for more than one year, and produced economic or social benefits |
| Patent Award | Must be a single granted invention or utility model patent whose exploitation has produced notable returns |

The on-campus nomination procedure and deadlines are:

| Date | Step |
|------|------|
| By July 10 | Prospective applicants email their name, phone number, and project title to the Research Office to receive a filing account |
| By August 20 | Research achievements must first complete achievement registration, with registration materials submitted to the Research Office |
| By August 23 | Complete online filing via the comprehensive science and technology evaluation and management service platform and submit |
| By August 30 | Submit three printed sets of the nomination form plus electronic materials |

For the "Opinion of the Recommending Institution" section of the nomination form, the Research Office has drafted reference wording that applicants may use directly. Attachments to the notice include the award application requirements, the filing handbook, and the expert recusal application form.

The campus contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, reachable at 86503271 or ygluo@stu.edu.cn.
