The Shantou Intellectual Property Office has launched the selection for the 5th Shantou Patent Awards. It is reported that the awards cover five categories, and Shantou University's Research Office has issued on-campus application arrangements.

The award categories are:

| Category |
|----------|
| Patent Gold Award |
| Patent Excellence Award |
| Industrial Design Gold Award |
| Industrial Design Excellence Award |
| Outstanding Patent Inventor (Designer) Award |

To be eligible, a patent for invention, utility model, or industrial design must have been granted on or before May 30, 2013, based on the date of the authorization announcement. Its inventor (or designer) must hold Shantou household registration or work in Shantou.

Application materials include the application form, the patent certificate and gazette documents, proof of patent validity, proof of market access for the patented product, proof of economic returns, photos of the physical model or prototype for design patents, and documents relating to the right to exploit the patent. Applicants for the Outstanding Patent Inventor (Designer) Award must additionally submit identification documents and proof of employment.

Applications combine online and paper filing. Applicants must log in to the filing system (http://zlj.stipo.gov.cn), scan the application materials page by page into JPG files of less than 1M each for uploading, and print the application form from the system for binding. Three bound paper copies must be submitted to the Research Office by July 19, 2013, and the Research Office will forward them together. Six attachments are provided with the notice, all serving as application form templates for the various award categories.

The campus contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, reachable at 86503271 or ygluo@stu.edu.cn.
