It is reported that, in line with the unified arrangements of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee, the Shantou University Party Committee will hold a mobilization meeting for the Mass-Line Education Campaign on July 6, marking the official launch of the campaign at the university.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 9:00 AM, Saturday, July 6, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Science Center |
| Theme | Mobilization and arrangements for the Mass-Line Education Campaign |
| Issued by | Organization and United Front Work Department |

## Participants and Arrangements

Participants will include members of the university leadership, secretaries of general Party branches and directly affiliated branches, administrative officials at deputy-division level and above, all Party member officials in administrative offices, retired comrades who formerly held principal university posts, and heads and deputy heads of schools and colleges on the main campus.

Each unit shall submit its attendee list to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn before the end of the workday on July 5. Attendees are requested to be seated 15 minutes in advance.

After the mobilization meeting, the Provincial Party Committee supervisory group will hold individual talks with members of the university leadership and other relevant personnel.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
