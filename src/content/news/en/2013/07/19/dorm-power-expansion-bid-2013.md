It is reported that Shantou University has completed the bid evaluation for the student dormitory area power distribution expansion project, and the result was publicized on July 19. Guangdong Yuedong Electric Power Engineering Co., Ltd. won the bid with an amount of RMB 2,497,245.39.

## Publicity Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Student Dormitory Area Power Distribution Expansion Project |
| Winning bidder | Guangdong Yuedong Electric Power Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Bid amount | RMB 2,497,245.39 |
| Publicity period | July 19-22, 2013 |

Objections to the evaluation result may be raised with the relevant departments during the publicity period. The tender contact is the Infrastructure Office (Yao Yijia and Lin Yuqi), tel: (0754)86502490, fax: (0754)86502314. Complaints are handled by the Supervision and Audit Office, tel: (0754)86502346, fax: (0754)86503663.
