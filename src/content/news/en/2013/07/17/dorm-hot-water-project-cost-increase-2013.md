It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently published a notice on additional costs for the air-source heat pump hot water supply system project serving student dormitories in Buildings A to F, covering equipment supply, design and installation.

The project began on May 20, 2013. During construction, it was necessary to connect the system to the campus water supply pipeline and to add two 300-litre pressure tanks, generating costs beyond the original contract. The additional work is preliminarily estimated at about RMB 38,000, keeping the cumulative increase within the 10% limit set by the university.

In accordance with the Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering Management of Capital Construction Projects, the Infrastructure Office has published the additional costs for comment.

## Public Notice Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Equipment supply, design and installation of the air-source heat pump hot water system for student dormitories in Buildings A to F |
| Start date | May 20, 2013 |
| Additional items | Connection to the campus water supply pipeline; two 300-litre pressure tanks |
| Estimated additional cost | About RMB 38,000 |
| Cost range | Within the 10% limit set by the university |
| Feedback deadline | 5:30 p.m., July 19, 2013 |
| Offices receiving feedback | Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office |

Those with objections may submit written comments to the Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office before the deadline.
