The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, together with the Shantou University Student Associations Union, opened registration on July 2, 2013, for the second batch of internships under the university's 2013 Student Entrepreneurship Practice Action. The program is open to all STU students, including graduate students, and is intended to help participants gain practical experience in entrepreneurship and employment while developing innovation, entrepreneurship and career skills.

The action is aligned with the Guangdong Youth League's Wings Plan, a Guangdong university student internship service initiative. Participants may receive an internship certificate or assessment certificate issued by the Guangdong Youth League, while outstanding participants may be recommended by the university for the Outstanding Individual selection under the provincial internship service action. Detailed positions are provided in the registration attachments, and the practice period is generally two weeks to two months.

Interested students were required to submit an internship application form and a resume by 24:00 on July 3. The registration materials and submission method are subject to the OA attachments.
