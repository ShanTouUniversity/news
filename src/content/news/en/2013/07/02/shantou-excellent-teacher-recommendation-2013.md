It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University issued an on-campus recommendation plan on July 2 for the 2013 Shantou Excellent Teacher and Excellent Education Worker awards, in accordance with document Shan Shi Jiao [2013] No. 42 of the Shantou Municipal Education Bureau. The University (including the Medical College) may recommend three Excellent Teachers and one Excellent Education Worker to the city.

Key information for the on-campus process is as follows:

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Basis document | Shan Shi Jiao [2013] No. 42, Shantou Municipal Education Bureau |
| University quota | 3 Excellent Teachers and 1 Excellent Education Worker |
| On-campus submission deadline | July 8 |
| Submission venue | Human Resources Office, Room 315, Administration Center |
| Email for electronic copies | wing@stu.edu.cn |
| Quota per unit | No more than 2 Excellent Teacher candidates and no more than 1 Excellent Education Worker candidate per unit |
| University-level selection | A review panel composed of representatives from teaching units and relevant functional departments will select the University nominees |

Each unit is expected to publicize the eligibility criteria among teachers and education workers, organize applications and recommendations, and finalize its candidates after deliberation.

## Municipal Award Background

The awards are organized by the municipal Party committee and government, with a commendation planned around Teachers Day 2013: 20 Excellent Principals, 100 Excellent Teachers and 30 Excellent Education Workers. The selection covers Shantou University (including its Medical College, affiliated high school, affiliated primary school and kindergarten), whose preliminary quota is three Excellent Teachers and one Excellent Education Worker.

Eligibility thresholds set at the municipal level include generally at least eight years of teaching experience. Excellent Teacher candidates are required to have worked continuously on the teaching front line since 2009 with at least six class hours per week, plus two county-level or above papers or a city-level or above research project under their charge. Those who have received municipal-level or above honorary titles since 2009 are generally not to be nominated again.

Under the municipal document, districts, counties and relevant institutions are to submit materials to the Municipal Education Bureau by July 25. The contact person at the Municipal Education Bureau is Cai Yuezhong, tel. 88853404, email ssjrsk@163.com. This contact is for the municipal submission stage; on-campus applicants should follow the Human Resources Office arrangement.

## Application Materials

Applicants are required to submit both hard copies and electronic files. Hard-copy materials include the recommendation form (16 copies in A4, double-sided, with originals and photocopies as required), an approximately 2,000-word biographical account written in the third person (two original copies), endorsed and stamped copies of graduation certificates, professional title certificates, teaching certificates issued since 2009, teacher qualification certificates and honorary certificates, representative works and performance records since 2009, and a summary sheet of recommended candidates. Electronic versions of the recommendation form, the biographical account and the summary sheet should be sent to the on-campus email address above. Certificate and performance documents must be verified, signed and stamped by the applicant's unit.
