The Office of Party and Administration Affairs at Shantou University issued a notice on July 2 announcing the work arrangements for administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff during the 2013 summer vacation. Relevant units were asked to follow the university's established summer work system and ensure timely and effective services for students, faculty and staff during the vacation.

## Vacation Service Arrangements

The arrangements cover administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff in the relevant university units. The specific staff roster and duty schedule are set out in the notice attachment. Each unit is expected to coordinate its positions and service coverage so that necessary administrative, teaching support and logistics services remain available during the summer vacation.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Staff covered | Administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff in relevant units |
| Governing basis | Relevant provisions of STU Party and Administration Office Document No. 8 (2012) |
| Detailed roster | The attachment issued through the OA system |

The university also required all relevant units to implement the applicable rules strictly, maintain orderly vacation operations and respond promptly to service needs from students, faculty and staff.

>
