It is reported that Shantou University's Graduate School recently completed its 2013 review of student research-project applications. Following recommendations and reviews by the relevant schools, 37 applications were approved. The projects include research on factors affecting firm boundaries and small and medium-sized enterprises in eastern Guangdong; detailed project information is provided in the related attachments.

## Next Steps

The Graduate School plans to conduct midterm evaluations in early October. Decisions on whether to provide additional funding, as well as the amount of any additional funding, will be based on each project's progress. Project leaders have also been asked to organize their teams and begin the research work promptly.

To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce project-leader names, student lists, or personal information contained in the attachments. The complete project list, review results, and related attachments are available in the original STU OA notice.
