It is reported that the Shantou University–Guangdong Aerospace Satellite Technology Co., Ltd. Engineering Practice Education Center, proposed by Shantou University, has been selected for the national university student off-campus practice education base development program. The result was announced in a notice forwarded by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education from the Department of Higher Education of the Ministry of Education concerning university student off-campus practice education bases under the “Undergraduate Teaching Project.”

The base is jointly developed by the STU College of Engineering and Guangdong Aerospace Satellite Technology Co., Ltd. to provide undergraduates with an engineering practice education platform. Following its selection for the national development program, the base will improve its operational management and teaching functions in line with its proposal, strengthen its teaching staff, establish an open and shared access mechanism, and protect students’ lawful rights and interests during practical training.

Under the relevant arrangements, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance will provide unified funding for selected national practice base development projects through the “Higher Education Innovation and Strengthening Project.” Shantou University will track and manage the project in accordance with requirements from the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, and cooperate with relevant authorities in inspections of its development.
