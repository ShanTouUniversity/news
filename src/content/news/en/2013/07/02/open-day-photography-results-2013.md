It is reported that Shantou University's Student Affairs Office announced the results of the 2013 Open Day photography competition on July 2. The competition received 254 entries, and after evaluation by professional teachers, works by six participants received first, second or third prizes.

The winning works have been displayed on the Open Day website at http://openday.stu.edu.cn/news3.htm. Winners may collect their certificates and prize money from the Student Affairs Office in Room 106 of the Administrative Center during working hours from July 4 to 5, carrying an identity card or campus card.

To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce the names or full list of student winners. The list of winners and related attachments are available in the original STU OA notice.
