It is reported that Shantou University has finalized the list of student representatives for the third Student Leaders Exchange Program, run in partnership with the University of Nottingham Ningbo China to develop students' leadership skills. After a selection notice was issued on June 21, 19 candidates recommended by various units advanced to interviews, and 10 students — including Huang PS from the Student Union and Chen ST from the School of Business — were ultimately chosen.

## Selected Students

| Recommending Unit | Selected Students (Year & Major) |
| --- | --- |
| Student Union | Huang PS (Class of 2011, Public Administration), Chen ST (Class of 2011, Accounting) |
| STU Youth | Yang ZY (Class of 2012, Journalism) |
| Student Societies Federation (incl. societies) | Zheng YN (Class of 2011, English) |
| Zhicheng College | Zhong JT (Class of 2011, Law) |
| Business School | Rong ZH (Class of 2011, Accounting), Xiao HM (Class of 2011, Accounting) |
| College of Engineering | Zhang Y (Class of 2010, Civil Engineering) |
| Law School | Guo Y (Class of 2011, Law) |
| FLY Training Program | Yu SQ (Class of 2011, Computer Science) |

According to the organizers, the selected students were asked to make the necessary preparations and stay in contact with program staff ahead of the exchange at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China.

The program is part of the cooperation between the two universities on student leadership development, aiming to broaden participants' organizational skills and international perspectives through mutual exchange.
