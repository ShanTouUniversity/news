It is reported that the Trade Union issued a notice on July 2, 2013, that faculty and staff have actively participated in the Guangdong Poverty Alleviation Day donation drive.

As of July 2, 2013, a total of 30,220 yuan (thirty thousand two hundred and twenty yuan) has been received. Details are available in the attachment *Summary of Donations for Guangdong Poverty Alleviation Day*.
