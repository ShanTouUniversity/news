It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University released the "Trial Measures on Basic Requirements and Procedures for Faculty Applying to Supervise Graduate Students" (Document No. Shan Da Li Fa [2013] No. 1) on July 2, effective immediately.

The measures were formulated in accordance with the university-level "Regulations on Faculty Applications to Supervise Graduate Students" (September 2012) and tailored to the specific circumstances of the College of Science. They were deliberated and approved by the College's Degree Evaluation Subcommittee on June 7, 2013.

The document sets out detailed requirements and review procedures for faculty members seeking to supervise graduate students, aiming to standardize qualification management and ensure the quality of graduate education. Full details are available in the attachment issued by the College.
