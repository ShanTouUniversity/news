It is reported that Shantou University School of Law recently announced the results of its 2013 internship program with Grandall Law Firm in Shanghai. Following interviews conducted by the School of Law selection panel, two students from the Class of 2011 law program were selected to participate.

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Host organization | Grandall Law Firm, Shanghai |
| Number selected | 2 |
| Selected students | Students from the Class of 2011 law program (names omitted) |
| Selection method | Interviews conducted by the School of Law selection panel |

The program will give law students an opportunity to gain practical experience at a law firm, deepen their understanding of legal practice, and build relevant experience.
