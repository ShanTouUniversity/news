On July 2, the General Office of the Party Committee and Administration of Shantou University issued a notice on archiving Party and administrative documents, clarifying the responsibilities and procedures of university units.

According to the notice, the requirements were formulated in accordance with the Shantou University Measures for Handling Party and Administrative Documents and the university's related regulations on archives management and the filing and administration of electronic records. Each unit is responsible for archiving the Party and administrative documents it produces that fall within the scope of archival records.

For electronic documents, filing is required as soon as the documents are issued. Units must enter the basic information of each document into the archives management system and upload the electronic file together with its attachments. Paper drafts and official paper documents should be printed and stamped with the unit's official seal when issued. They may then be transferred to the Comprehensive Archives Room either at any time or on a regular schedule.

The notice is intended to standardize the archiving process for Party and administrative documents, support the simultaneous preservation of electronic and paper records, and facilitate the centralized management and subsequent use of university archives.
