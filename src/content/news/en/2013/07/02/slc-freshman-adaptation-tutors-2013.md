It is reported that the Student Learning Center (SLC) announced on July 2, 2013 the admission results for the 2013-2014 academic year freshman adaptation tutoring program, with 27 students selected as peer tutors.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Issuing unit | Student Affairs Office, Student Learning Center (SLC) |
| Academic year | 2013-2014 |
| Position | Freshman adaptation program peer tutor |
| Number admitted | 27 |
| Selection criteria | Post requirements, interview performance, and recommendations from school/department liaisons |

It is reported that the 27 selected tutors will support the 2013-2014 freshman adaptation tutoring program and help new students adjust to university study. The SLC expressed its appreciation to all students for their active participation and support.
