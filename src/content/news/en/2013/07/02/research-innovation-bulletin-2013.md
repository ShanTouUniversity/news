Shantou University’s Office of Research recently released the second 2013 issue of its Research and Innovation Bulletin, the 28th issue overall. The bulletin reviews recent developments in research projects, research achievements, academic activities, and services for the local community.

## Research Projects and Achievements

The issue reports on the university’s 2013 research-project applications, the approval of selected humanities and social-science projects, and improvements in the quality of humanities and social-science research. The Office of Research at the Medical College also organized a midterm review of ongoing clinical research projects to support subsequent implementation and project management.

The bulletin highlights important research published by a Medical College team in the international journal *Science*. It also notes the overseas publication of a monograph by Professor Qin Zhong of the Business School and the renewed selection of a print by Lin Dexiong of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design for an international print exhibition. These achievements reflect the university’s continuing work in medicine, humanities and social sciences, and art and design.

## Academic Conferences and Exchange

The Law School hosted an international symposium on international economic law, dispute-resolution procedures, and related reforms, while the College of Science organized a workshop on strong-field physics. An international conference on computational methods and function theory and an international symposium on sustainable urban development, urban administration, and legal systems were also held at Shantou University.

The university also welcomed scholars and professionals from different countries and regions. Former U.S. appellate prosecutor Irene Solet, American scholar David Smith, Professor Tan Beiping, Professor Ai Chunxiang, Professor Chen Jialiang, Professor Qu Yongjin, and Dr. Huang Wangming visited the university, delivered talks, or attended academic conferences. Professor Lu Zhenghui, researcher Zhou Ningyi, Professor Kan Tai-keung, Li Yongquan, and Professor Wang Shouzhi presented lectures or took part in discussions on the aesthetics of Tang and Song poetry, the microbial degradation of aromatic hydrocarbons, Hong Kong design, and related professional topics. Professor Qin Zhong of the Law School attended the 2013 Europe-America subject conference organized by an international journal on management and the arts and sciences in France. Professor Huo Xia of the Medical College attended a working meeting at the World Health Organization headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland. The Medical College also launched a series of lectures on medical frontiers.

## University-Local Cooperation and Public Service

For local service and technology cooperation, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design participated in the 15th China Chenghai International Toys and Gifts Fair. The university held industry-university-research cooperation activities with Chaonan District and signed a strategic industry-university-research cooperation agreement with Chaozhou. The Law School assisted social workers in Chenghai District, Shantou, with examination preparation and held a ceremony with Longhu District People’s Court to inaugurate a legal practice education base.

The issue also records the establishment of Shantou University’s intellectual-property protection volunteer team. The Shantou Municipal Committee of the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference, the Shantou Municipal Committee of the Revolutionary Committee of the Chinese Kuomintang, and the Shantou Municipal Committee of the China Democratic League visited the university for thematic research.
