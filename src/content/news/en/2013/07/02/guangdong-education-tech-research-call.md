According to the Office of Research Affairs at Shantou University, applications are open for the 2013 Education Information Technology Research Special Projects under the 12th Five-Year Plan for Guangdong Education Science. Shantou University may submit two projects this year.

The special projects are self-funded. Proposed topics should follow the annual project guidelines, address new trends and developments in educational technology and education informatization, and demonstrate originality, pioneering value, and relevance to Guangdong. Final outputs may take the form of academic papers or research reports, which should generally be completed within one to two years. A monograph should generally be completed within two to three years. Projects whose final output is a research report must provide proof of adoption by the relevant organization when applying for appraisal.

Applicants must have completed the final review of any National Social Science Fund project, National Education Sciences Planning project, Guangdong Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning project, or Guangdong Department of Education research project they previously undertook. Each applicant may submit one project only and may not participate as a member of another project team in the same call.

**Application Schedule**

Applications will be submitted through the online system. Applicants must complete and submit the online application by September 10, 2013. After approval, they must print two copies of the PDF generated by the application system by September 16 and submit them to the Office of Research Affairs, printed double-sided on A4 paper.

The contacts at the Office of Research Affairs are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun. The office telephone number is 86502563.
