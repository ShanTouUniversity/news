It is reported that the long-awaited dormitory air conditioning is finally on its way for Shantou University students. On July 30, the university's Resources Management Office, jointly with the Student Affairs Office, the Graduate School, and Zhicheng College, issued a construction notice announcing that Suning will carry out air conditioner installation across all student dormitories from August 1 to 30.

## Three Daily Work Shifts

Given the tight schedule, installation work will proceed in three shifts every day:

| Shift | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Morning | 8:00–12:00 |
| Afternoon | 13:30–18:00 |
| Evening | 19:00–22:00 |

## Students Asked to Secure Belongings and Cooperate

According to the notice, installation workers will need to enter every dormitory room during construction. To protect students' interests, installers will wear Suning uniforms or badges when entering rooms, which students can use to verify their identities.

The university also reminded students to organize and safeguard their personal belongings in advance and to cooperate with the installation crews, so as to avoid disputes over damaged or lost items.

As the construction schedule is tight, some disruption to students' daily lives is expected during the works, and the university hopes students will understand.
