It is reported that, in line with the arrangement of the Guangdong Provincial Leading Group Office for the Mass-Line Education and Practice Campaign, Shantou University organized study of the book "New Starting Point, New Development: A Guangdong Reader for the Party's Mass-Line Education and Practice Campaign" in order to consolidate the learning phase, enrich its content, and improve its effectiveness.

Each unit was asked to collect copies of the reader from the Organization and United Front Work Department, mobilize Party members, faculty, students, and the public to study it, and report back on the study progress.

## Distribution Scope

| Recipients | Copies |
| --- | --- |
| Members of the university leadership team | One copy each |
| Middle-level and above cadres | One copy each |
| Secretaries of general Party branches and directly affiliated branches | One copy each |
| Each grassroots Party branch | One copy |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
