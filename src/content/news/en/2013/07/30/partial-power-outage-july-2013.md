It is reported that parts of the Shantou University campus will have their power temporarily cut off from **8:30 to 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, July 31**, as the air-dome basketball training hall is connected to the main power supply. The affected areas are the G Teaching Building, the Structure and Wind Tunnel Laboratory, the zoo, the cold storage and the D Teaching Building.

| Key information | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 8:30-9:30 a.m., Wednesday, July 31 |
| Reason | Connecting the air-dome basketball training hall to the main power supply |
| Affected areas | G Teaching Building, Structure and Wind Tunnel Laboratory, zoo, cold storage, D Teaching Building |

The notice was issued by the Resources Management Office on July 30, and faculty, staff and units in the affected areas can make arrangements in advance accordingly.
