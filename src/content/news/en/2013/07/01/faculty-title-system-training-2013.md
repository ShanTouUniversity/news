It is reported that the STU Personnel Office will hold training on the online application system for faculty professional technical qualifications at 15:30 on July 3 in Room 102 of Teaching Building E. The session is intended to help faculty members applying for 2013 professional titles in the university series become familiar with the submission process.

## Training Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:30, July 3, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 102, Teaching Building E |
| Audience | Faculty members planning to apply for a university-series professional title this year |
| Content | Application-system training and discussion of title-application questions |
| On-site arrangement | Applicant accounts and passwords will be distributed |

The training is arranged in accordance with the university's Notice on 2013 Professional-Technical-Qualification Application and Review (STU [2013] No. 89). Prospective applicants are expected to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
