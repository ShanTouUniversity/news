The Shantou University gymnasium will be closed during the summer vacation, according to information released by the university. The closure covers the indoor badminton courts, fitness room and gymnastics room. Any subsequent opening arrangements will be subject to further notices from the university.

The arrangement follows the university's notice on the summer vacation schedule. Faculty members and students are advised to follow further updates from the Department of Sports.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Department of Sports)
