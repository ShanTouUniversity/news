It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office issued a construction notice on July 1, 2013, regarding renovation of the public restroom in stairwell No.1.

## Project Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Renovation of the public restroom in stairwell No.1 |
| Contractor | Shantou Chaoyang No.1 Construction and Installation Co. |
| Winning bid | 288,700 yuan |
| Schedule | July 2 – August 29, 2013 |
| Scope | Complete interior demolition and reconstruction, including aluminum alloy ceiling, wall and floor tiling, partition boards, basins, washstands, steel entrance doors and water and electrical wiring |
| Impact | Noise, vibration and dust are expected during construction |
| Issued by | Infrastructure Office |

For safety, pedestrians are advised to take care and not to enter the construction site without permission from university functional departments and the contractor.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
