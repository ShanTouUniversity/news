It is reported that Shantou University issued its Measures for Handling Party and Government Documents on July 1, 2013, to further standardize document handling across university units. The measures were formulated with reference to the Regulations on the Handling of Party and Government Official Documents issued by the General Office of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the General Office of the State Council, while taking the university's circumstances into account. They were reviewed and approved at the university's fifth leadership meeting of 2013 on April 23.

The new measures took effect upon publication and simultaneously repealed the Measures for Handling Administrative Documents of Shantou University, issued in 2001 as Shantou University document No. 106. The university stated that official documents serve as an important means of communicating national policies, arranging university work, issuing institutional rules, and handling affairs and exchanging information within and beyond the university. All units are expected to follow the new measures in their related work.
