Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office and Student Affairs Office announced on July 1 that the entrepreneurship training course under the SYB program for graduating students of the 2010 cohort had been canceled. The course was originally scheduled to take place in Building D from August 15 to 31.

The university said that enrollment had not reached the minimum required to open the course, so it would not proceed as planned. The notice also apologized to students who had previously booked a place and asked them to follow any subsequent arrangements.
