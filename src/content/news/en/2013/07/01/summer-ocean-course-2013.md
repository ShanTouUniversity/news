Shantou University’s Science College announced on July 1 the class arrangements for the 2013 summer public course Exploring the Ocean and reminded relevant students to prepare in advance and attend the course on time.

The notice states that the specific class times and venues are listed in the accompanying attachment. Since the detailed timetable was not included in the published notice, the original notice and attachment in the Shantou University OA system should be consulted for the official arrangements.
