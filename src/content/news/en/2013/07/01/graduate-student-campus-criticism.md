According to a Shantou University OA notice, the School of Science and the Graduate School recently issued a decision concerning a student dormitory-management violation.

Following a review by the relevant units, the university decided to issue a university-wide public criticism in accordance with the *Shantou University Student Dormitory Regulations* and the *Shantou University Regulations on Disciplinary Actions for Student Misconduct (Trial)*. To avoid disclosing personal or behavioral details, this report does not reproduce the specific time, conduct or identifying information involved.

**Decision Details**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Matter concerned | Student dormitory-management violation |
| Decision | University-wide public criticism |
| Responsible units | School of Science and Graduate School |
| Basis | *Shantou University Student Dormitory Regulations* and *Shantou University Regulations on Disciplinary Actions for Student Misconduct (Trial)* |

The university called on graduate students to comply with university regulations and help maintain a positive campus culture and academic environment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science and Graduate School)
