It is reported that Zhicheng College of Shantou University has published the 2013 Issue No. 4 of its journal *Zhicheng Life*. The issue reviews the college's activities from June to July, covering mentor training, annual summaries, student union transition and awards, a high-school summer camp, and visits from outside guests.

## Mentor training: five-day intensive program for the sixth cohort

A major feature of the issue is the sixth mentor training camp held from July 1 to 5. Incoming mentors took part in a five-day intensive program, including lectures on the college's history and the mentor's bridging role, video-based sessions on communication and personal development, outdoor team-building exercises at a base in Meizhou, and five short plays written, directed and performed by the mentors themselves about residential college life. The program concluded with sessions for sharing reflections.

## Summaries and awards: fifth mentor cohort and student union transition

On June 2, the college held a closing meeting for the fifth cohort of mentors. Mentors from different residential areas reviewed their work over the past year and shared advice with incoming mentors. Some mentors said that despite the heavy workload, they had gained friendship and personal growth, adding that they would continue to support their successors. Vice Dean Chen Wenbin spoke highly of the cohort's contribution to the college.

On the evening of the same day, the college held its student union summary and transition meeting alongside an awards ceremony. The fifth student union reviewed its work and handed over to the sixth, whose new president delivered an inaugural speech. A number of residents were recognized: 28 outstanding student leaders, 7 outstanding mentors, 45 active contributors to student affairs, and 6 outstanding volunteers. Dean Li Dan encouraged more residents to grow through the college's platforms and contribute to its development.

## Summer camp: 57 high-school students experience college life

From July 10 to 14, the college continued its team-building summer camp for high-school students, which was included in the university's service-learning curriculum. A total of 57 students from Senior Year 1 and Year 2 took part, coming from schools including Jinshan High School, Shantou No. 1 High School, Shantou Overseas Chinese High School, Niehuai High School, Dongxia High School, Jinping No. 2, No. 4 and No. 12 High Schools, Jieyang No. 2 High School and Puning No. 2 High School. Through experiential activities, the camp helped participants build self-awareness, motivation and teamwork, while university students serving as organizers practiced leadership and service.

## Visits: three delegations from home and abroad

On June 3, a five-member delegation led by Vice President Yang Shihong of Gansu Normal College for Nationalities visited the college, where Vice Dean Chen Wenbin and colleagues introduced its history and educational philosophy. On June 18, an eight-member delegation led by Dr. Brian Hughes of the National University of Ireland Galway toured the college's facilities and learned about its outreach courses during a visit to Shantou University. On June 28, a four-member group from the UK-based branding consultancy Winkreative visited the college.

The issue also briefly covers the farewell ceremony for the Class of 2013, a summer work retreat, and the military training completed by the 2012 cohort of residents.
