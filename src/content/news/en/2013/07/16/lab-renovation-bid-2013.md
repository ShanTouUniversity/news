It is reported that the bidding review for the laboratory environment renovation project at Shantou University has been completed, with the winning bidder announced on July 16.

## Winning Bid

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Project name | Laboratory Environment Renovation Project |
| Winning bidder | Shantou Jian'an Decoration Engineering Corporation |
| Bid reduction rate | 18.00% |

## Publicity Period

The publicity period for the bid result runs from July 16 to July 18. Objections to the evaluation result may be raised with the relevant departments during this period.
