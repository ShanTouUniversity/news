It is reported that the bidding review for the West Courtyard renovation project on the west side of the Shantou University campus has been completed, with the winning bidder announced on July 16.

## Winning Bid

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Project name | West Courtyard Renovation Project |
| Winning bidder | Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Bid reduction rate | 24.325% |

## Publicity Period

The publicity period for the bid result runs from July 16 to July 18. Objections to the evaluation result may be raised with the relevant departments during this period.
