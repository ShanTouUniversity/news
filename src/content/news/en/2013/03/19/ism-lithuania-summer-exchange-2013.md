STU Business School and Lithuania’s ISM University of Management and Economics signed a cooperation agreement in December 2012 and will launch a summer exchange in 2013. The Business School plans to select **four students** to attend ISM Summer University in the Baltics in Vilnius from **July 1 to 19**. Courses are taught in English.

Available courses include International Project Management, International Marketing, International Business and Economics, and Corporate Finance. Participants may apply for one or two courses, each worth six ECTS credits. The program also includes company visits, cultural activities, accommodation in Vilnius’s Old Town, weekday breakfast and lunch, and a weekend activity on the Lithuanian coast.

### Selection and costs

Applicants must be Business School undergraduates in their second year or above; preference will be given to students who have not previously taken part in a university or school exchange. Applicants must have strong English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills and submit evidence of TOEFL, IELTS, CET-4, CET-6, or STU ELC results by **April 15**. They should also have strong academic and personal records, a GPA of at least 3.0, an interest in public and volunteer service, and the ability to promote STU externally.

The school will conduct an initial review of written materials and select the top four candidates through a comprehensive assessment. The four nominated students will have their ISM tuition waived; eligible students ranked below the first four may also apply but must pay tuition themselves. Students cover application and other fees, round-trip travel, accommodation and meals, books and materials, insurance, and medical expenses. ISM materials list a hospitality fee of **EUR 800** for accommodation and activities; course charges for students without a tuition waiver vary by course load and applicant category and should be confirmed against the university’s published rates.

Application materials include the Business School overseas-exchange application form, English-language evidence, an academic transcript, and an approximately 300–500-word English essay on the applicant’s purpose and overseas study plan. Submit paper and electronic materials to the Business School Foreign-Exchange Projects Office, Wenxi 211, by **noon on April 15**; email electronic materials to `10zzhou@stu.edu.cn`. Contact Ye Dongni at `86503382`.
