STU and Coventry University have launched joint-degree programs covering relevant disciplines in the School of Engineering, Business School, School of Art and Design, and J-School. A program information session will be held from **9:30 to 11:00 a.m. on Saturday, March 23** in Room 207, Building D. Coventry representatives will introduce the programs and answer questions.

### Program structure

- **2+2 bachelor’s dual degree**: Open to STU second-year students. Students study two years at STU and two years at Coventry and may receive bachelor’s degrees from both universities after meeting the requirements;
- **3+1 bachelor’s dual degree**: Open to third-year students. Students study three years at STU and complete the final undergraduate year at Coventry, leading to degrees from both universities after meeting the requirements;
- **4+1 bachelor’s-to-master’s pathway**: Open to current and former STU undergraduate graduates. Students complete one year of master’s study at Coventry and may receive a Coventry master’s degree after meeting the requirements.

Partner disciplines include electronic and information engineering, mechatronics, computer science, civil engineering, international economics and trade, accounting, business administration, marketing, finance, landscape architecture, interior design, graphic design, industrial design, interface design, animation and imaging, journalism and media, and advertising. Consult the program prospectus for exact program matching.

### Requirements and costs

Current students and recent graduates must have an average major grade of at least 75. The language requirement for the second-year pathway is IELTS 6.0 with no component below 5.5 or TOEFL 76 or above. Third-year and graduate pathways generally require IELTS 6.5 with no component below 5.5 or TOEFL 88 or above. Application materials include an application form and personal statement, language results, passport information page, recommendation letters, Chinese and English graduation or enrollment certificates, and Chinese and English transcripts. Art applicants must also submit a portfolio.

Reference tuition for Coventry’s 2013–2014 undergraduate and master’s programs is **GBP 10,080–12,000 per year**, with estimated living expenses of **GBP 6,000–8,000 per year**. STU students who pay within the required period receive a GBP 500 tuition reduction; strong applicants may apply for a GBP 1,000 partnership scholarship, and students meeting the language conditions may receive a free four-week pre-sessional English course. The programs begin in fall 2013. Applicants should submit materials through their academic schools in late March and plan ahead for visas, accommodation, and proof of funds.

For program inquiries, contact STU’s International Exchange and Cooperation Office at `86503520`, `86502316`, or `wscicd@stu.edu.cn`, Room 240, second floor, New Administration Center. Confirm disciplines, tuition, application materials, and procedures against the OA attachments and Coventry’s latest information.
