STU’s 24th World Engineers Forum will be held from **7:30 to 10:00 p.m. on Friday, March 22** in the Science Building Lecture Hall. The forum is titled **“Thriving in Employment and Entrepreneurship.”** Wang Guoyu, chair and general manager of Hong Kong Jinxin Decoration Group and Shanghai Jinxin Decoration Co., Ltd., will share his experience and reflections on career choices, entrepreneurship, and business development.

Wang has a background in architecture and business management. He has founded and managed companies in the decoration and business-resources sectors and has spoken at universities on employment and entrepreneurship.

Faculty members and students may reserve a place through <http://activity.stu.edu.cn/> by **11:00 a.m. on March 21**.
