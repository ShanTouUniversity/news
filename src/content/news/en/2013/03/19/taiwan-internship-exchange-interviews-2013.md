STU’s J-School has announced its 2013 selection arrangements for a summer Taiwan internship and an exchange program in the following fall semester. **Forty-nine students** applied for the summer Taiwan internship, and **27** have been selected for interviews. One graduate student also applied for a self-funded exchange program.

The interviews will be held at **4:00 p.m. on Thursday, March 21** in the school’s glass room. Selected applicants who do not attend on time will be considered to have withdrawn. Students not included in this interview round may still pursue other routes to Taiwan, including self-funded semester study, academic conferences, and other exchange activities. Follow announcements from the International Exchange and Cooperation Office and the school website for future opportunities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (J-School)
