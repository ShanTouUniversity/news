STU’s Personnel Office has forwarded the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security notice on recommendations for the 2013 leading-talent category of the national special-support plan’s Hundred-Thousand-Ten-Thousand Talent Project and for national-level candidates under the Hundred-Thousand-Ten-Thousand Talent Project. The university has opened its internal application process.

Eligible faculty members should use the information-collection tools supplied in the notice attachments, print and sign two copies of the candidate information form, and prepare two copies of the attachment package, including the attachment index, identity document, academic certificates, professional qualification certificates, and evidence of achievements or honors.

Applicants must submit paper materials to their units by **April 2** and email the database file generated by the software and a PDF of the attachments to `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn`. Each unit should prepare its recommendation and submit it, together with the applicant’s materials, to the Personnel Office, Room 315, New Administration Center, by **April 5**. After university review, STU will submit the selected recommendations to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
>
> ⚠️ Application materials contain identity, academic, professional-qualification, and achievement records. The information tools and application attachments are not reproduced here.
