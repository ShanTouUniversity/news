STU’s Board of Trustees is scheduled for renewal in 2013. The university plans to recommend one faculty representative from the main campus and one from the Medical College to serve as directors on the new board. The Medical College will conduct its own nomination process; the main campus has now opened nominations.

Candidates should have high professional ethics, a strong sense of responsibility, and a commitment to STU’s development. They must hold a senior professional and technical title, work in teaching or research, and have strong academic achievements. Candidates must also have worked at STU for at least three years, have been born after 1957, and be in good health. Relevant achievements include a STU Outstanding Teaching Award, serving as a principal contributor to a provincial- or ministerial-level science or teaching award in the past five years, leading a national teaching or research project in the past five years, or receiving a provincial- or ministerial-level honor in the past five years.

Main-campus units should seek faculty and staff input and recommend the strongest candidate, with no more than one nomination per unit. Each nominee must complete 10 copies of the Faculty Representative Recommendation Form and submit them to the Personnel Office, Room 315, Administration Center, by **the end of the workday on April 8**. The Personnel Office will organize discussions among relevant unit leaders and submit recommendations for university leadership review. The selected representative will then be publicly announced.
