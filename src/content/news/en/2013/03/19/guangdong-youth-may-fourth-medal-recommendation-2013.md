The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has opened recommendations for candidates and collectives for the 15th **Guangdong Youth May Fourth Medal**. The award is presented by the Communist Youth League of Guangdong Province and the Guangdong Federation of Youth and includes individual and collective honors. It is a major recognition for young people and youth collectives in Guangdong.

### Recommendation criteria

Individual candidates should be 14–40 years old, hold a Guangdong household registration or have studied or worked in Guangdong for at least one year, support the leadership of the CPC, observe the law, maintain good ethical standards, and have made outstanding contributions in their work. They should also have received a city-level or higher award or commendation since 2011, or have won important recognition for China or Guangdong in a major competition or selection.

A candidate collective should be primarily composed of young people aged 14–40 who live or work in Guangdong. Its members should observe the law and maintain good ethical standards. Since 2011, the collective should have played a key role in a major project, key initiative, or widely influential event and achieved notable results. Its Party and Youth League organizations should play an active role and the collective should demonstrate strong cohesion and team spirit.

Application materials include an individual or collective recommendation form, a statement of achievements, and photographs. Collective applications also require a member roster or a description of the collective’s composition. Materials must be reviewed and approved by the Party organization of the candidate’s unit as required.

STU nominees must submit paper application forms and related materials to the STU Youth League Committee Office by **March 24** and email electronic copies to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. The provincial notice sets **April 5** as the deadline for recommending organizations to submit complete materials to the Organization Department of the provincial Youth League Committee; STU will announce the detailed submission arrangements separately.
