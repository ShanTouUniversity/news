STU’s Higher Education Institute will hold its first academic salon of 2013 from **7:00 to 9:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 20** in the institute’s conference room. Faculty members and students are welcome.

The salon will feature two topics: Mao Jinde will discuss “The Historical Evolution and Generative Logic of Professorial Power, with Reflections on Chinese University Reform”; Lan Faguo will present “Rationality and Passion among Graduate Students: A Cycling Journey in Western China.” Kang Quanli will be the guest speaker.
