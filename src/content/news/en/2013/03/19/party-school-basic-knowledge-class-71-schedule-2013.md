The 71st Basic Party-Knowledge Class of the CPC STU Party School will open on **March 24**. It has **185 participants** divided into nine study groups. Participants must report to their group coordinators from **March 21 to 23**, submit one passport-style photo, and collect study materials. Faculty and staff participants should report to Room 315, New Administration Center. Group coordinators will meet at 1:00 p.m. on March 21 in Room 206, Wenbei Building.

### Course schedule

Most sessions will be held in Lecture Hall 3 and will cover Party conduct, members’ duties and rights, the nature and mission of the CPC, Party-joining motivation, Party organizational systems and discipline, and the Party’s guiding principles in relation to university students. The program also includes lectures, video sessions, group discussions, and an exchange with members of the university’s working committee for retired faculty and staff.

| Date | Main arrangements |
| --- | --- |
| March 24 | Lectures, video sessions, and thematic study at 8:50 a.m., 10:40 a.m., 2:50 p.m., and 7:30 p.m. |
| March 31 | Lectures, video session, thematic lecture, and submission of written assignments |
| April 14 | Lecture, exchange with retired faculty and staff, group discussions, and submission of study summaries and participant forms |
| April 21 | Self-study of the *Constitution of the Communist Party of China* and related materials; theory examination from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. |

Failure to report without a valid reason constitutes voluntary withdrawal. Participants who need leave must complete a leave-approval form, obtain review from their Party general branch, and submit it to the Party School Office for approval. Unexcused absences or failure to take the examination may result in disqualification. Attendance is recorded for all scheduled classes, reports, self-study sessions, discussions, video sessions, and visits; participants who take leave for more than one-third of the planned instructional hours or are absent without authorization will not receive a completion certificate. Final assessment combines the theory examination, written assignments, study summary, discussion participation, study attitude, and attendance.
