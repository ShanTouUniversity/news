STU’s Three-Style Development Office has published the results of study-style checks for the third week of the 2012–2013 spring semester. Inspectors checked Building E at **10:00 a.m. on March 13** and found no violations of university rules. At **2:00 p.m. on March 15**, checks in Buildings D, E, and G found two students arriving no more than five minutes late and one student arriving more than five minutes late.

The university will refer the results to the relevant schools and colleges for criticism and education and will continue its study-style and classroom-conduct work.
