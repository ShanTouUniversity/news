STU plans to publish a campus anthology tentatively titled *That Year, When the Golden Phoenix Bloomed*. The Publicity Department and Alumni Office are inviting current students and alumni to submit original works about campus study and life, memorable events, personal growth, overseas study and visits, social practice, and student-organization activities.

Submissions may take the form of essays, miscellany, reflections, notes, short pieces, or poetry. Works must be original, concise, clear, and no more than **1,500 Chinese characters**.

The contest will award two first prizes of RMB 400 each, four second prizes of RMB 200 each, six third prizes of RMB 100 each, and 10 honorable-mention awards. Winners will receive certificates from the STU Publicity Department and copies of the anthology.

The deadline is **April 5**. Email electronic submissions to the Shantou University News editorial office at `o_xb@stu.edu.cn`. Submissions must include the author’s school or department, name, telephone number, email address, and other requested details; contributors’ personal data is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department and Alumni Office)
