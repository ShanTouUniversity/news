Eight faculty members and students from STU’s J-School traveled to India for reporting from **January 23 to February 6, 2013**, observing local society and culture at close range and completing a reporting-practice project.

The school will host the **STU Journalists in India** sharing session from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, March 22** in the STU Library Lecture Hall. The participants will discuss their reporting experiences and reflections. All STU students are welcome. Event video: <http://v.youku.com/v_show/id_XNTI4ODQyNDk2.html>.
