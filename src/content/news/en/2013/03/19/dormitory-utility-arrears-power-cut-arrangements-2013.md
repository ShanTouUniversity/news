As of **March 19**, some student residences had not paid their utility charges for February 2013 and earlier. Under university rules, STU will cut power to residences with outstanding balances from **March 20** and restore supply after all arrears are paid.

### Collection and restoration windows

From March 20 to 22, the Network Center will process two Campus Card deductions each day:

| Deduction | Power restoration |
| --- | --- |
| 10:30 a.m. | 2:30–4:30 p.m. the same day |
| 4:30 p.m. | 5:30–8:00 p.m. the same day |

Residences that pay after the 4:30 p.m. deduction will have power restored from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. the following day. From March 22 onward, one deduction will be processed daily at 4:30 p.m.

Students with insufficient Campus Card balances should use a self-service deposit machine to top up. Students with insufficient bank-account balances should deposit enough funds during banking hours and complete the transfer to the Campus Card. Students whose cards are lost may set up proxy payment through the utility-management system or pay cash at the Bank of China STU branch and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for posting; the Finance Office does not accept cash directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ This article does not list residences in arrears, individual payment information, or the university’s bank-account details.
