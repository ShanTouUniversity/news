It is reported that Professor Li Ping of the Shantou University Law School has been awarded the honorary title of "National Advanced Individual for Safeguarding the Rights and Interests of Women and Children" by the All-China Women's Federation, upon recommendation by the Shantou Municipal Women's Federation.

The award, conferred by the All-China Women's Federation, recognizes individuals who have made outstanding contributions to protecting the legitimate rights and interests of women and children. Professor Li has long been engaged in legal education and research, with notable achievements in the field of women and children's rights protection. The honor reflects high recognition of her professional contributions.
