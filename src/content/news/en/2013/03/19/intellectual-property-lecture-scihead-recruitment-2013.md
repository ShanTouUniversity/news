STU will host a lecture on **technology innovation and intellectual-property protection from 10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Saturday, March 23** in Conference Room 205, Science Building. Scihead IP Group will conduct campus recruitment at the same event. The session is organized by the Guangdong Intellectual Property Training Base at STU and co-organized by the Shantou Intellectual Property Rights Protection Assistance Center and Shantou Patent Protection Association.

The speaker, Wen Xu, is a senior intellectual-property expert, vice chair of the China University Intellectual Property Research Association, and a principal founder of Guangzhou Scihead Patent Agency Co., Ltd. Drawing on case studies, he will discuss how technology innovations can become independent intellectual-property assets and how such assets can be managed and protected.

### Recruitment

The Shantou branch of Guangzhou Scihead Patent Agency plans to recruit **10 graduating students** for two types of positions:

- **Patent engineer**: Full-time undergraduate or higher qualification in a science, engineering, or law field; biology and chemistry are preferred. Applicants should demonstrate initiative, responsibility, spatial reasoning, and clear logical communication;
- **Intellectual-property litigation lawyer, senior assistant**: Full-time undergraduate or higher qualification in law or a related intellectual-property field. Applicants with a science or engineering background, passing the judicial examination, or holding a patent-agent qualification are preferred; CET-4 English or above is required.

Relevant experience is preferred. The positions are based in Longhu District, Shantou, with a base salary plus commission, social insurance, and medical insurance. Applicants may email résumés to `vikey1329@scihead.com` or consult the company at Room 1508, Hengze Building, Shantou High-Tech Zone.
