With the relevant approvals in place, STU’s Network and Information Center is coordinating the winning contractor’s construction of the campus wireless Wi-Fi coverage project. The project is scheduled for completion by **May 30**.

The first phase has completed cabling and equipment installation in the DG and E teaching buildings and is undergoing system testing. The second phase will proceed in sections across office areas and outdoor hotspots, including the Arts and Humanities Building, Engineering Building, Science Building, Audio-Visual Education Building, Arts and Design Building, Science Building, Mechanical and Electrical Building, Medical Foundation Building, Teaching Building C, Multidisciplinary Building, union building, infrastructure building, Self-Study Center, Arts Education Center, Building 789, the Equipment Office, Water and Electricity Center, campus hospital, wind-tunnel laboratory, dining hall, residence-hall activity rooms, gymnasium, experts’ apartments, affiliated middle and primary schools, library, and pedestrian street.

Work includes site surveys, cabling, equipment installation, and commissioning. Construction may cause limited noise and dust. Pedestrians should take care near work areas and support the units’ construction arrangements.

Construction team: `86503548`, Ms. Huang.
