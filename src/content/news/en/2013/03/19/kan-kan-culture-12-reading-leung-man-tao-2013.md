STU’s 12th Kan Kan Culture event, titled **“Talking about Reading,”** will be held from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 20** in the Science Lecture Hall. Wang Shouzhi of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will speak with Hong Kong cultural figure Leung Man-tao about reading and cultural observation. Faculty members and students are welcome.

Leung graduated from the Department of Philosophy at Chung Chi College, the Chinese University of Hong Kong. He has worked in cultural criticism, writing, publishing, radio and television, and cultural promotion, and has hosted and provided commentary for numerous media platforms.
