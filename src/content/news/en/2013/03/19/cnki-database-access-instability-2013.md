CNKI is upgrading its Guangdong data-service node, and some universities are experiencing unstable access, failed automatic IP-based login, and problems downloading full text during the upgrade. CNKI is working to resolve the issue; service recovery will depend on the system’s actual status.

For urgent Chinese-language literature searches, STU Library recommends using the VIP database or the Duxiu Knowledge Base temporarily.
