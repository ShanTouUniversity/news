| 58478 | Choir and Conducting | Week 5, Wednesday, March 27, periods 3–4, STU101 | Week 6, Wednesday, April 3, periods 8–9, STU101 |
| 59257 | Choir Training | Week 5, Saturday, March 30, periods 2–3, STU101 | Week 6, Wednesday, April 3, periods 6–7, STU101 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
