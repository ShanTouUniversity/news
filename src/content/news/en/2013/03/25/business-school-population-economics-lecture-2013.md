Shantou University’s Business School will hold its second academic lecture of 2013 from **10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on March 27** in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of Wenxi Building. Dr. Song Jinfang of the Chinese Academy of Sciences will speak on “Guangdong Population Development from a Population-Economics Perspective,” chaired by Professor Qin Zhong.

The lecture will analyze the relationships among fertility and income, population growth, population structure, population quality and economic development, using Guangdong as a case for empirical study. It will consider population-development strategies aligned with economic growth.

Dr. Song’s research interests include resources, environmental and sustainable development, and international investment and capital operations. The lecture is open to the entire university community.
