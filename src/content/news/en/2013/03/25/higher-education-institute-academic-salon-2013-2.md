Shantou University’s Higher Education Institute will hold its second 2013 academic salon from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on March 28** in the institute’s conference room. Ma Fengqi is invited to join the event, which welcomes faculty and students from different disciplines.

The salon will feature two topics. Mao Jinde will lead “Reconsidering the Relationship between Academic Freedom and Teaching Freedom,” examining the legal foundations and starting points of the two forms of freedom as universities’ functions have diversified. Chen Bingyun will lead “A Discussion of Educational Freedom,” addressing graduate education, the relationship between education and research, and the direction of educational research.

The organizers invite participants to share different views and connect the discussion with questions arising from their study and research.
