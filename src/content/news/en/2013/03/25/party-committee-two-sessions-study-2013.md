Shantou University’s Party Committee central group will hold a study session on the National Two Sessions at **3:00 p.m. on March 27** in New Administration Center Room 213. The session will study the spirit of the First Sessions of the 12th National People’s Congress and the 12th National Committee of the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference, including the new national leadership bodies and major reforms affecting economic and social development.

The session will also convey and study a speech delivered by Xi Jinping on **January 5, 2013**. Professor Cheng Jiaming and Party Publicity Director Bai Youjin will lead the two presentations, respectively.

Members of the university Party Committee, secretaries of general and directly affiliated Party branches, and responsible officials from the Discipline Inspection Commission, General Office, Organization and United Front Department, Publicity Department, News Center, Student Affairs Office, Social Sciences Department, University Labor Union and University Youth League Committee are expected to attend.
