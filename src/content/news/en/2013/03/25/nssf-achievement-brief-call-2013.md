Shantou University’s Office of Research has forwarded the revised call from the National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences for the National Social Science Fund’s research-brief series. Eligible faculty and social science researchers are invited to submit work.

### Submission requirements

The call primarily targets holders of National Social Science Fund projects, while welcoming other social science researchers. Submissions should address major practical issues requiring attention or solutions in current or future national work, offer analysis and policy recommendations, demonstrate strategic and substantive thinking, and remain within **2,500 Chinese characters**. Authors must ensure that submissions raise no intellectual-property disputes.

The research-brief office accepts submissions year-round and generally notifies authors within one week when a submission is under consideration. Authors should indicate whether the work is a result of a National Social Science Fund project and provide the main author’s institutional affiliation and professional information as required. Send electronic manuscripts to `ghbcgc@vip.163.com`; confidential material should be submitted on a disc according to the notice.

### Recognition

Annual National Social Science Fund projects whose results are adopted by the research-brief series at least once, and major projects whose results are adopted at least twice, may apply for an appraisal-exempt completion review under the relevant rules. A first author whose work is published in the series may enter the annual project conference review directly. If a result is adopted by central decision-making departments, the relevant office will send a commendation letter to the author’s institution and the relevant provincial, autonomous-region or municipal Party publicity department.

Sample briefs are available in the “Selected Briefs” section of the website of the National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences. For STU inquiries, contact Huang Yumei or Zhuo Yanchun in the Office of Research at `86502563`. The National Office’s Results Division can be reached at `010-63094651`.
