It is reported that, in accordance with the Guangdong Department of Education's notice on selecting candidates for the "12th Five-Year Plan" National Experimental Teaching Demonstration Centers (Yue Jiao Gao Han [2013] No. 27), Shantou University plans to nominate the "CDIO Experimental Teaching Demonstration Center" of the College of Engineering for the national program. The nomination is now open for public comment.

The University stated that the recommendation was made after comprehensively evaluating the infrastructure, distinctive strengths, achievements, and voluntary applications of its existing provincial-level experimental teaching demonstration centers.
