Shantou University’s Office of Research has opened applications for the 2013 National Social Sciences Excellent Works Library. Established by the National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences, the library highlights frontier-level social-science research and promotes exemplary scholarship and scholars.

### Eligibility and works

Applicants should generally be full-time researchers, including retired researchers, hold an associate-senior or higher professional title, and be the first author of the proposed work. The work must be complete but unpublished, free of intellectual-property disputes, original and forward-looking, academically rigorous, and significant for social and economic development or disciplinary development.

Eligible formats are Chinese academic monographs, thematic collections of papers and thematic research reports, generally **200,000 to 600,000 Chinese characters**. Textbooks, translations, individual papers, reference books, data compilations, popular works and software are not accepted. A work may be a National Social Science Fund project result rated “good” or above at completion, or an unfunded research result. No more than 10% of its content may overlap with a published book, and it may not be published during review. A doctoral dissertation must have passed its defense **at least two years earlier**.

The call covers all 26 National Social Science Fund disciplines, including the separately listed fields of education, arts and military science. Interdisciplinary works should use the code for the primary discipline and must be recommended in writing by two peers with senior professional titles or by an eligible publishing institution.

### Materials and deadlines

Applicants must complete the application and submit materials to the Office of Research for a formal review by **June 14**. Materials that pass review must be submitted by **June 19**, including:

- Four A3, double-sided, center-bound copies of the application;
- Seven printed sets of the work;
- Nine copies of a summary containing the title, contents, a 5,000- to 10,000-character description and main references, with English translations of the title and contents;
- Three discs containing the application, manuscript, summary and all supporting data.

The manuscript and summary must not directly or indirectly reveal the applicant’s name, institution or background. Doctoral dissertations and works previously submitted but not selected require detailed revision statements. Materials are confidential and will not be returned. Selected works will be published and publicly recognized by the National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences; eligible works may also receive project approval or funding under the applicable rules.

Fabrication, plagiarism, intellectual-property infringement or submission of an already published work will result in public criticism and a five-year ban on applications for National Social Science Fund projects. Selection will be revoked and certificates recovered where applicable.
