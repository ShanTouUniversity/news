The “Colorful” Art Education Center Activity Poster Exhibition is being held at the museum of Shantou University’s Changjiang School of Art and Design from **March 20 through 31**. The exhibition is open to faculty, staff and students. It brings together posters from the center’s past performances and arts activities, most designed by students and faculty of the Changjiang School of Art and Design, highlighting campus arts programming and graphic-design practice.

STU’s arts education began in 1993. The former arts education teaching office was renamed the Art Education Center in 2001 and incorporated into the Changjiang School of Art and Design in 2008. The center offers more than 30 arts courses across the university, with approximately 3,200 students taking its courses in 2012. Its student arts groups include choirs, dance groups and a wind band, with members from schools and colleges across STU.

The center launched the “Colorful Arts Season” in 2010 and had held the series for five consecutive semesters by the time of the exhibition. The season had presented 19 performances to more than 13,000 audience members, and had invited professional conservatory ensembles, pianists, Chinese-huqin performers, dance companies and theater groups to STU.
