Shantou University’s Office of Research has forwarded a Guangdong call for publication support for works by retired senior experts. The call is open to retired STU experts who conducted social-science research and held an associate-senior or higher professional title, and seeks **completed but unpublished academic books**.

Works should follow sound academic principles, demonstrate academic value, intellectual substance and innovation, contribute positively to Chinese and Lingnan culture, comply with academic standards and raise no intellectual-property disputes.

Application materials include four copies of the application form for Guangdong outstanding social-science books by senior experts, along with 21 copies each of the manuscript’s table of contents and a Chinese summary of no more than 1,000 characters. Printed materials are due to the Office of Research by **April 7**; send the electronic version to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn`. STU contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun at `86502563`.
