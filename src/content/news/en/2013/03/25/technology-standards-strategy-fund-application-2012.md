Shantou University’s Office of Research has forwarded the call for applications for the 2012 Shantou Special Funding Awards for Implementing a Technology-Standards Strategy (Shantou Technology Standards Joint Office [2013] No. 1). Faculty members interested in applying should prepare the application form and supporting materials according to the municipal notice and submit them to the Planning Section of the Office of Research by **March 28**.

The municipal eligibility rules, award categories and documentation requirements are governed by the forwarded notice. Because the archived PDF is an unreadable scan, applicants should consult the original OA attachment or confirm the requirements with the Planning Section of the Office of Research.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Research)
