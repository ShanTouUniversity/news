STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has issued Bulletin 224 on inspections conducted during week four of the 2012–2013 spring semester. Both inspections took place in the **E Teaching Building**:

| Inspection | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| 2:00 p.m., March 19 | One student arrived within five minutes after class began |
| 8:00 a.m., March 22 | One student arrived within five minutes late, and two arrived more than five minutes late |

The bulletin asks the relevant schools and colleges to educate the students involved and continue strengthening study-style development. The attachment contains a student list and is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (“Three Styles” Development Office)
>
> ⚠️ The student list is not publicized. This article reports only anonymized inspection times, location and counts.
