It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts will hold a meeting between the Chief Representative of Dublin City University (Ireland) in China and students from 16:30 to 17:30 on March 25 in Room 201, Wen Dong.

The session will focus on introducing exchange programs and the 3+1+1 joint training program offered by Dublin City University and address questions of interest to students. All interested students are welcome to attend.
