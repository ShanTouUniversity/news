As the Qingming holiday approaches, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office reminds students to monitor possible short-term strong winds, heavy rain, lightning and hail in Guangdong and check weather warnings before traveling. During severe weather, students should enter a safe building and stay away from trees, billboards and temporary structures. The university encourages respectful tomb-sweeping practices without carrying candles, paper offerings or firecrackers into mountain areas.

The university also advises students to:

- Follow traffic rules, avoid unlicensed or overloaded vehicles, and never drive after drinking;
- Report forest fires in areas such as Sangpu Mountain rather than attempting to fight them. The Jinyuan District Forest Fire Prevention Command Office can be reached at `88201153` or `88215860`;
- Check safety advisories when traveling, choose reputable tour operators, protect cash and valuables, and travel with companions where possible;
- Follow residence and safety rules when staying on campus, do not house visitors from outside or admit strangers, and switch off and unplug electrical appliances before leaving;
- Avoid open flames and high-power appliances such as immersion heaters in residences, and keep fire exits clear;
- Do not fish or swim in the university reservoir or other dangerous waters, carry open flames into mountain areas, or light fires or barbecue in restricted areas. Do not climb walls, railings or fruit trees;
- Conduct on-campus activities safely, without lighting candles or setting off fireworks and without disturbing others.

Report unusual situations during the holiday. The Student Community Management Service Center is available at `86502661`, and the campus emergency number is `8290 4110`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
