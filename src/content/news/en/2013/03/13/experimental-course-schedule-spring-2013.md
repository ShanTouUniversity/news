It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on March 13, 2013, requesting the submission of lab course schedules for the spring semester of 2012/2013.

Teaching units are asked to organize undergraduate teaching laboratories to complete the forms and submit them by March 20, 2013, first as electronic documents and then as printed copies.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Content | Lab course schedules for spring 2012/2013 |
| Deadline | March 20, 2013 |
| Electronic copy | Send to yzchen@stu.edu.cn |
| Hard copies | Two copies; laboratory directors collect and submit one to the Academic Affairs Office (3rd floor, New Administration Building) and keep one with the department teaching secretary |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

The template is available in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The schedule template is available on the OA system.
>
