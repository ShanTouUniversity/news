Shantou University’s J-School has opened applications for summer 2013 Taiwan internships and exchange study during the fall 2013 semester. The programs are open to third-year J-School students. Eligible students may apply for both programs but can be selected for only one. Students who have received university or school funding, have already participated in or plan to participate in overseas internships, reporting, or exchange study may not apply. Hong Kong and Taiwan internships are also mutually exclusive.

### Taiwan internships

The summer Taiwan internship runs from **July 30 through September 9**. Students will intern with media organizations from **August 1 through 31**, with places in news agencies, newspapers, online media, and television. The planned distribution is four news-agency places, four newspaper places, one online-media place, and two television places, for approximately 11 interns; the actual number depends on media allocations. The school will provide round-trip airfare and visa fees and subsidize accommodation and insurance. Students cover their living expenses in Taiwan. From September 1 through 9, participants may take a self-funded visit and must travel as a group.

The selection plans to include 11 interns: eight writing, one photography, and two video positions. Selection will consider media experience, GPA, reporting and writing ability, and submitted work.

### Exchange program

The fall 2013 exchange program offers **two places** at the College of Journalism and Communication of Chinese Culture University in Taiwan. The exchange lasts up to four months, through the end of the semester. Exchange students pay no tuition in Taiwan, may take up to six courses across the college’s four departments, and must meet the credit requirements for transfer to STU. The school will provide round-trip airfare, visa fees, and semester insurance but no living-expense subsidy.

The school is also accepting graduate applications for a self-funded research stay at the Graduate Institute of Journalism at Chinese Culture University during fall 2013. The number of places will be determined separately. The four-month semester costs approximately **RMB 30,000**, including tuition, living expenses, and accommodation; graduate applicants may not apply for the Taiwan internship at the same time.

### Application arrangements

Application forms and works must be submitted to the student assistant of Professor Wang Zongan by **5:00 p.m. on March 16**. The school will screen applicants for interviews, scheduled for **4:00 p.m. on March 21** in the J-School’s Glass Room. Exchange applicants must also submit a statement explaining their application; graduate research applicants must provide an application statement and research plan.
