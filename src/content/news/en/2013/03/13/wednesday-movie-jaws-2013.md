STU’s **Wednesday Movie** will screen Steven Spielberg’s **Jaws** from **7:00 to 10:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 13** in the Library Theater. Peter Herford will moderate the screening, and faculty and students across the university are welcome.

The course has 150 seats, with an additional 50 seats available to the public on a first-come, first-served basis. Students must present their campus cards for entry.

Jaws is a 1975 American thriller directed by Steven Spielberg and adapted from Peter Benchley’s novel. It follows the hunt for a great white shark attacking a fictional seaside resort. The film won three Academy Awards at the 48th ceremony: Best Film Editing, Best Original Dramatic Score, and Best Sound.
