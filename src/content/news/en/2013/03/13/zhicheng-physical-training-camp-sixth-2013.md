Shantou University’s Zhicheng College will hold its sixth **Physical-Development Training Camp** from **March 23 through 24**. The two-day program includes a trek across Sangpu Mountain, outdoor camping, team development, leadership training, and wilderness-survival training to build fitness, cooperation, and communication skills.

The camp is open to residential students of Zhicheng College, with **20 places** planned. If applications exceed the quota, participants will be selected by drawing lots. The program includes arrangements on the afternoon of **March 20** and activities on March 23–24. Applicants must have no conflicting classes or activities and be willing to follow coaches’ instructions, schedules, and team rules.

Participants cover the two-day, one-night meal cost of **RMB 50 per person**; students with financial hardship may apply for a reduction. Applicants should download and complete the form and email it to `ATP@stu.edu.cn` by **10:30 p.m. on March 18**. The camp is co-organized by Zhicheng College’s Beidou Development Association.
