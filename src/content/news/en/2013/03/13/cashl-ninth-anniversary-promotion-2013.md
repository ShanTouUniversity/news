It is reported that the Library issued a notice on March 13, 2013, that the China Academic Social Sciences and Humanities Library (CASHL) will launch its first nationwide promotion of the year on March 15 to celebrate its ninth anniversary.

## Promotion

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Period | March 15 – April 15, 2013 |
| Eligible users | All new and existing CASHL users |
| Offer | Free CASHL journal articles: 100 for regular users, 200 for advanced users and 300 for senior users; unused quotas will not be extended after the deadline |
| Scope | Only CASHL journal holdings searchable via “Open WorldCat” with a specific holding library; excludes book loans, copies and non-electronic delivery requests |

## How to Use

| Step | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Register | Register under “Personalized Services” on the CASHL website (http://www.cashl.edu.cn) |
| Confirm | Send registration username, name, school, year and campus card number to yxu@stu.edu.cn for account confirmation |
| Apply | Log in with the confirmed account and search for journals or articles to submit requests |

Users who have already registered and been confirmed may log in directly with their existing credentials. Those with account issues may email for reactivation. User level can be viewed at the bottom left after logging in to “Open WorldCat.” Guides and the library database page are available in the OA notice.
