The 30th **STU Career Launch Forum** will be held from **7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on March 14** in E Lecture Hall 204. The session, titled **“Intensive Preparation from Start to Finish: Succeed in Guangdong,”** is open to faculty and students across the university.

The lecture will focus on the 2013 Guangdong civil-service recruitment examination, including career choices for university students in a competitive employment environment, the latest examination arrangements, recurring and difficult topics, key knowledge areas, preparation strategies under the new recruitment policies, and an intensive 40-day review plan.

Speaker Xu Zhimin teaches civil-servant administrative aptitude tests for central government agencies and local governments. He is an invited expert on the Sina and Dayang education-channel teaching teams and has delivered civil-service examination lectures at universities across China.
