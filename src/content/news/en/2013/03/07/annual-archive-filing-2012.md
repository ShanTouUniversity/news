Shantou University’s General Office has organized the collection, arrangement, and filing of records and documents from 2012 to strengthen standardized and institutionalized archives management. The filing scope also includes research institutions and laboratories affiliated with each unit.

Units must enter the basic information of records into the university archives-management system, load the corresponding electronic files, and generate receipt forms. They should collect all required materials and ensure that audiovisual archives are clear and labeled with the time, location, content, and responsible parties. Physical records and internal publications should also be collected, organized, and filed as required.

Each school, affiliated middle school, and affiliated primary school must file its annual graduate group photographs and provide the associated information as required by the archives rules. Units without a group photograph should file individual headshots under the applicable requirements. Because the Comprehensive Archives Room is moving to the New Administration Center, all filing work is due by **May 31, 2013**. The university will report completion by unit in **August**.

For archives questions, contact the Comprehensive Archives Room at `86502878`. The archives-management system is available at <http://efms.stu.edu.cn>.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
>
> ⚠️ Photographs and personal information in archival records are internal materials. Handle them through the university archives system and do not reproduce them on public pages.
