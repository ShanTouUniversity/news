The **2013 Test for English Majors Band 4 (TEM-4) speaking test** will be held at **8:30 a.m. on Saturday, May 18**. Under arrangements by the National Advisory Committee on Foreign Language Teaching in Higher Education’s TEM speaking-test office, the test is open only to **English-major undergraduates who entered in 2011**.

Online registration runs from **March 7 through 17** through the credit-based academic system. The registration fee is RMB 80 per student. The Finance Office will authorize the Network Center to deduct the fee from registrants’ campus-card accounts on **March 18**. Students should ensure sufficient balance; an unsuccessful deduction will result in failed registration.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office)
