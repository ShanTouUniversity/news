The Publicity Department of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee has opened applications for research grants on **strong and distinctive discipline development**, and Shantou University’s Research Office is organizing applications. Applicants should develop their own topics based on STU’s strengths and distinctive disciplines and the discipline-development requirements of the *Guangdong 12th Five-Year Plan for Philosophy and Social Sciences*. Each selected project will receive **RMB 90,000**.

The principal investigator must be a field leader with strong professional integrity, substantial scholarly expertise, and research experience and may lead only one application. A project team should generally have no more than five members, and each member may participate in no more than two projects. Members must have relevant prior research results, and the proposed discipline should be among the leading disciplines in Guangdong.

Selected projects will run from **April through October 2013**. Completion requires all of the following: at least one paper published in a core journal, a formal research report of at least 20,000 Chinese characters and a roughly 6,000-character abstract, and one academic discussion session related to the project.

Because of the urgent schedule, applicants must email an electronic application form to `ymhuang@stu.edu.cn` for formal review by **March 12**. By the end of the working day on **March 14**, submit five A4 paper copies, printed and bound on the left, to the Research Office and send the final electronic version. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhu Yanchun at `86502563` and `82902563`.
