Shantou University’s School of Law has opened applications for its 2013 Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship Program. Applications are accepted from **March 7 through 17**. Eligible applicants include:

- Primarily 2010 law students in the Law Department, with applications also open to 2011 law students. The 2011 cohort will not receive professional internship credit;
- 2010 students in public administration or administrative management in the Department of Public Administration.

Applicants should complete the Law School’s Hong Kong summer internship scholarship application form and email it to `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn`, using “department+name+Hong Kong summer application form” as both the subject line and file name. The form requests personal information and is not reproduced here.

The school has selected **46 students** for interviews under the previously announced screening criteria: 21 from Public Administration, 12 from the 2010 law cohort and 13 from the 2011 law cohort. Because the attached list contains student names and one damaged text entry, the names are not reproduced here.

Interview arrangements:

| Group | Time | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public Administration | 2:00 p.m., Wednesday, April 10 | Law School Room 206 |
| 2010 law cohort | 9:00 a.m., Friday, April 12 | Law School Room 206 |
| 2011 law cohort | 2:30 p.m., Friday, April 12 | Law School Room 206 |

Interviews will follow the order in the list and last approximately 10 minutes per person. Participants should wear formal attire.
