The Business Case Competition Steering Committee of Shantou University’s Business School will hold a briefing on the **CommTECH 2013 exchange at 3:00 p.m. on March 13** in the Business School Seminar Room on the second floor of Wenxi. The session is open to all Business School students. A student who attended the previous program will share experience, and program organizers will answer questions about the exchange.

CommTECH 2013 is organized by Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) in Indonesia and will take place in Surabaya, Indonesia’s second-largest city. The program includes cultural, academic, and social activities. Its official website is <http://io.its.ac.id/commtechcamp2013/>.

The notice body gives the program dates as **August 26 to September 23** and describes it as lasting 10 days. The attached English introduction gives **August 26 to September 3, 2013, for 10 days**. These dates conflict; prospective participants should rely on later confirmation from the organizer for application and travel arrangements.
