Shantou University Hospital will begin **2013 annual health examinations on March 13** for staff members who selected the on-campus option. Examinations will take place at the University Hospital, with detailed group schedules arranged internally.

The examination includes routine internal-medicine, surgery, ophthalmology, otolaryngology, dental, and gynecological checks; height, weight, blood pressure, and pulse measurements; blood and urine tests; liver and kidney function, blood-glucose, and blood-lipid tests; and chest imaging, electrocardiograms, and ultrasound examinations. Women’s examinations will follow the hospital’s arrangements.

Laboratory and ultrasound checks begin at **7:30 a.m.** each day, while other examinations begin at **8:00 a.m.** The hospital provides free breakfast for staff receiving laboratory and ultrasound checks. People with hypertension should bring medication as directed by their doctors. The contact number is `82902318`.
