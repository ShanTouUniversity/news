As part of its International Women’s Day event, Shantou University’s Trade Union will take participants to Dabu to view rapeseed flowers on **Saturday, March 9**. The transport schedule is:

| Time | Boarding point |
| --- | --- |
| 7:00 a.m. | University Trade Union |
| 7:10 a.m. | Anju STU Garden |
| 7:20 a.m. | Yidu Cinema |
| 7:30 a.m. | New World Furniture City |

Participants should board the assigned vehicle, return on the same vehicle, and arrive on time. Wear sports shoes or flat shoes, protect valuables, stay away from dangerous areas while taking photographs, and avoid traveling alone. Participants should travel in groups according to their physical condition. Because of the large group, children should not be brought to the event.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union)
