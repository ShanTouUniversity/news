The 26th Engineering Growth Salon at Shantou University will explore **“The Power of Music”** at **4:00 p.m. on Friday, March 15**, in E202-1. Lin Xiaoling will lead the session, which is open to faculty and students across the university.

Through singing practice, video, explanation, and live demonstrations, the session will introduce folk, bel canto, popular, and original singing styles and compare representative works, vocal-production methods, and techniques to help participants develop vocal appreciation.

Registration closes at **noon on March 13**. Interested participants may email `12jwlu1@stu.edu.cn`; if applications exceed the room’s capacity, places will be assigned in registration order.
