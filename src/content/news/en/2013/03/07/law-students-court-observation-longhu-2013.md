The Career Development Center of Shantou University’s School of Law will take students to **Longhu District People’s Court on March 12** to observe proceedings and participate in an exchange. The activity is intended to help students understand court procedures and strengthen practical legal skills.

The activity has **45 places** for Law School students. Applicants should email `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` by **March 10**, using “Longhu District People’s Court observation and exchange activity” as the subject. Places will be assigned on a first-come, first-served basis.

Under the subsequent arrangements, 45 students were selected on a first-come, first-served basis. They should assemble at the university motto monument at **8:10 a.m. on March 12** for boarding, dress neatly, observe the activity rules, and follow the organizers’ arrangements.
