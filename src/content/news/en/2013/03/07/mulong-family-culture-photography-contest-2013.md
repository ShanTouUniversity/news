The first **Mulong Family Culture Photography Competition** invites photographers to document family life and traditional customs during the Spring Festival, including honoring ancestors and elders, expressing gratitude and good wishes, family reunions, welcoming the new year, and prayers for a good harvest. The competition highlights the values of joy, harmony, filial respect, and happiness in Spring Festival family culture.

The competition runs from **February 1 through March 31** and is open to photographers and photography enthusiasts in China and abroad. Participants may submit entries online at <http://i.mulong.com>. Images must be JPG files no larger than 500 KB each, with a long edge of 600–1,000 pixels. Color and black-and-white photographs are accepted, submissions are unlimited, and series photographs are not accepted.

The competition offers one first prize of an iPhone 5, two second prizes of an iPad 4, and three third prizes of Mulong family matching homewear gift sets valued at RMB 1,200. Winners will also be invited to the award ceremony and receive certificates.

The competition is organized by Guangdong Mulong Garment Co., Ltd. and co-organized by STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, with media support from China Network Television, CCTV.com, and Shenzhen Satellite TV.
