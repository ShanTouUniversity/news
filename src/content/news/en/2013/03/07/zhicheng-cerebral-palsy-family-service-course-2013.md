Shantou University’s Zhicheng College has selected **24 students** for its public-service course, the “Family Tutoring Service Action for Children with Cerebral Palsy.” The course will run from **March through May**, with its first theory session scheduled for **March 20 during class periods 8 and 9** in Zhicheng College J-354. All enrolled students should attend on time.

The course aims to support families of children with cerebral palsy through tutoring service while providing students with practical experience in public service and service learning.
