Shantou University’s public-service course SER3015, the **Dawn Service-Learning Course**, has published its spring 2013 schedule. Based on service learning, the course combines tutoring-service training, community practice, and final evaluation, helping students develop skills in lesson design, educational communication, and teamwork through service.

### Schedule

| Component | Dates | Content or location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service-site visits | March 10 | Visits to the relevant service sites |
| Course training | March 13, 20, and 27, and April 3 | G301 and G302; topics include service learning, lesson design, teaching methods, instructional planning, team development, emergency response, and safety |
| Public-service practice | March 17–May 26 | The two classes will provide tutoring service at Tongping Middle School and Fuping No. 1 Primary School, and at Chang’an Primary School and Xinfu Subdistrict, respectively |
| Final sharing and evaluation | May 29, 3:00–5:00 p.m. | G301 and G302 |

Training also covers lesson-plan preparation, communication and interaction with young people, team teaching and one-to-one tutoring, instructional-plan presentations and feedback, and discipline and safety requirements for public service. At the end of the course, students must prepare project reports, present their work, share their experiences, and complete a multi-party evaluation.
