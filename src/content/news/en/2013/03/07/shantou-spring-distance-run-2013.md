The China Life Insurance Cup Shantou Spring Distance Run will begin at **7:20 a.m. on Saturday, March 23**, and Shantou University will form a team to participate. The race starts at Overseas Chinese Park, follows Haibin Road to Shipaotai Park for the turnaround, and finishes at the entrance to Overseas Chinese Park.

The event includes men’s and women’s 5-kilometer primary-school and middle-and-senior-age categories, 10-kilometer middle-school categories, and 10-kilometer adult categories. STU faculty and students interested in joining should email their registration information to the Physical Education Department at `o_tyb@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on March 10**. The department will organize a selection, with details to be announced separately.

Participants must be in good health and regularly engage in running or training. They are responsible for accident insurance, and people with conditions unsuitable for strenuous exercise may not participate. Participants must submit a registration form, voluntary participation liability statement, and identity or student documents and cover their own participation costs. A joint meeting for team leaders, coaches, and individual participants will be held at **3:30 p.m. on March 20** in the seventh-floor conference room of the Shantou Sports Bureau.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
>
> ⚠️ The registration form and voluntary participation statement request identity, contact, and health information. Consult the original OA attachments or official race channels.
