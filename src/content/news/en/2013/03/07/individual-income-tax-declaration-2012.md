It is reported that the Finance Office forwarded a notice on March 7, 2013, from the Shantou Local Taxation Bureau regarding self-declaration for individual income tax for those earning over 120,000 yuan in 2012.

Staff meeting the threshold should complete self-declaration by March 31, 2013. The declaration is to be made online via the Guangdong Local Taxation Bureau portal (http://www.gdltax.gov.cn), logging in through the “Personal Login” section of the Tax Service Area with ID number, login password and verification code, and confirming under the “Self-Declaration for Annual Income Over 120,000 Yuan” sub-item in the “Individual Income Tax Declaration and Inquiry” area. Those who do not know their login password may call Wu Shaoming (2988) or Li Peijie (2446) at the Finance Office.

After successful declaration, please print the “Individual Income Tax Declaration Form,” sign in the taxpayer field and submit it to Wu Shaoming or Li Peijie at the Finance Office.
