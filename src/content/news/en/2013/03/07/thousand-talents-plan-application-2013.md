Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has circulated the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education notice on preliminary candidates for selected 2013 **Thousand Talents Plan** projects and begun the internal review and recommendation process.

Units must report preliminary information on proposed candidates and program categories to the Human Resources Office by the end of the morning on **March 8**. Recommended candidates for the Innovative Talent Long-Term Program, Innovative Talent Short-Term Program, and Young Thousand Talents Program must submit paper and electronic application materials by **March 21**. Materials for the Top Talent and Innovation Teams program have no single fixed deadline; units with eligible candidates should submit complete materials promptly.

Application forms and instructions are available in the OA attachments. For questions, contact Li Zhiwei at the Human Resources Office at `86502364`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
