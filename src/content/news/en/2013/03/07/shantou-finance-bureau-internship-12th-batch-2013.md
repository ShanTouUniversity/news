Shantou University’s Business School has announced the 12th internship cohort for the Shantou Municipal Financial Affairs Bureau. Following student applications and consultation with the bureau, **four students** were selected for the internship.

The Business School thanked students for their participation and encouraged those not selected to follow later activities and opportunities announced by the school.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School)

> ⚠️ The original notice contains student-identifying information. The selected-student list is not reproduced here; follow Business School notices for subsequent internship arrangements.
