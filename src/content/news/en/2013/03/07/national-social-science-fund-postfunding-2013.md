Applications are open for the 2013 National Social Science Fund post-publication support program. The program primarily funds outstanding, substantially completed but unpublished basic research in the humanities and social sciences, especially academic monographs, with limited support also available for academically valuable source collections and reference works. Typical funding is **RMB 150,000–180,000** per project; applicants may request an amount based on actual research needs.

### Eligibility

All 26 National Social Science Fund disciplines are eligible, including the separately listed fields of education, art, and military science. Applicants must be Chinese citizens with an intermediate or higher professional rank or a doctoral degree. The work should be at least 80 percent complete, or at least 60 percent complete for retired researchers, and must be an unpublished Chinese academic monograph, academic source collection, or reference work. It must have a sound political orientation, originality or pioneering value, and an advanced level in its field.

A work must be recommended in writing by three peers with senior professional titles or by a designated publisher approved by the National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences. Works with a signed publishing contract must be recommended by the relevant publisher. Each applicant may submit only one work at a time, and a work not selected may not be resubmitted for one year.

Popular nonacademic books, applied research, papers and collections, research reports, textbooks, software, and translations are ineligible. A doctoral dissertation or postdoctoral research report is ineligible if it was completed less than two years earlier or has not been substantially revised. Works involving intellectual-property disputes, revisions of published books or more than 10 percent overlap with published works, results from certain uncompleted national projects, or works whose applicants do not agree to unified publication by the National Office are also ineligible.

### Schedule and materials

The program holds centralized reviews in **June and November**. Applicants must email electronic copies of the application, manuscript, and results summary to `yczhu@stu.edu.cn` for formal review by **March 18 or August 16**, respectively, and submit qualified materials to the Research Office by **March 21 or August 21**.

Materials include four copies of the application, eight copies of the results-introduction sheet, and manuscripts and results summaries submitted according to the recommendation route. Manuscripts and summaries must be anonymized as required and may not show the applicant’s name or institution. Three electronic discs containing all application data are also required.

The National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences will arrange publication of supported results. Applicants may not publish submitted or approved works without authorization during evaluation or before completion. Application and recommendation materials must be truthful. Fraudulent applications or recommendations may be publicly criticized, and the applicant and recommender may be barred from National Social Science Fund applications for three years.
