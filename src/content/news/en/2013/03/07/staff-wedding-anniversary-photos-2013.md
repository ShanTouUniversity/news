Shantou University’s Trade Union will hold its **“Beauty of the Years” anniversary wedding-portrait event** in March. Staff members who married in **1988**, marking a silver anniversary, or in **1983**, marking a pearl anniversary, may receive a free formal anniversary portrait and one 20-inch crystal print.

Applications are due by **March 14**. Eligible staff should complete the application form and email it to the University Trade Union at `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`. Participants will use an invitation letter to attend the session at Vman Vision outdoor wedding photography in Shantou. Department-level unions may collect invitation letters from the University Trade Union on **March 15**.

The event is organized by Vman Vision outdoor wedding photography, available at <http://www.vmhlly.com>. For inquiries, contact Ye at short number `696166` or office number `86503197`.
