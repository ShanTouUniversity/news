It is reported that the 114th Economic Salon of the Business School of Shantou University will be held on Tuesday evening, March 12, at the new library lecture hall. The salon has invited Mr. Christian Nothhaft, CEO of Watsons Personal Care Stores China, to deliver a talk titled "Retail from a CEO's Perspective."

**Event Details**

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Speaker | Christian Nothhaft (CEO of Watsons China) |
| Topic | Retail from a CEO's Perspective |
| Date | March 12, 2013 (Tuesday) |
| Time | 20:30-22:30 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall |
| Language | English |

Since taking over Watsons China's operations in early 2007, Mr. Nothhaft has led the company into a phase of rapid growth. By the time he was promoted to CEO of Watsons China in 2011, he had expanded its store network in China from 230 to more than 1,100 outlets.

A graduate of Munich, Germany, Mr. Nothhaft began his career in management consulting, chain enterprise development (F&B and retail), and real estate. After moving to Asia in 1997, he built extensive chain operations for a listed company across Indonesia, Singapore, South Korea, and Hong Kong, and also ran a food manufacturing business in China. He joined AS Watson Group in 2003, serving successively as General Manager of Watsons Wine Cellar and Managing Director of Fortress.

The salon is primarily intended for undergraduate and postgraduate students of the Business School and is open to all faculty and students of the university.
