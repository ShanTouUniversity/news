Shantou’s event commemorating the 103rd anniversary of International Women’s Day and showcasing women’s health and achievements was held at the Municipal People’s Square on the morning of March 6. The event announced the city’s “Women’s Civilization Post” awards, recognizing 44 groups.

The laboratory management team of the Physics Department at Shantou University’s School of Science received the “Women’s Civilization Post” honor for its work in managing and supporting teaching and research laboratories.
