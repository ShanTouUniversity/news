The 120th New Century Humanities Forum of Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will be held from **7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on March 29** in ACC Meeting Room 3. Professor Wolfgang Kubin, a German scholar, sinologist and chair professor at STU’s School of Liberal Arts, will speak on “Looking Back at 1933 from 2013: The Holocaust and the Burden of Those Born after World War II.”

The lecture will look back at 1933 from the perspective of 2013 and discuss the Holocaust in Germany and the historical memory and sense of responsibility it shaped for generations born after World War II.
