Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has opened the 2013 application process for new undergraduate majors. Under Ministry of Education requirements, the annual deadline for undergraduate-major applications and filings is **July 31**; STU schools and departments must complete internal review and submit materials earlier.

A proposed major should align with the university’s mission and development plan, address talent needs arising from Guangdong’s economic and social development, use educational resources efficiently, reflect STU’s institutional and disciplinary characteristics, and comply with the 2012 Catalogue of Undergraduate Majors and the 2012 Rules on Undergraduate-Major Establishment. A new major should not duplicate a direction that an existing major can expand to cover.

Applicants must have a related disciplinary foundation, stable demand for graduates, a sound training plan, a full-time teaching and support team, and the funding, classrooms, library resources, equipment, internship bases and institutional safeguards needed for sustainable operation.

Required materials include the previous three years of disciplinary and program-development planning, the undergraduate-major application form, a training plan, a feasibility report, a basic faculty-information table, documentation of operating conditions and supplementary materials. Schools and departments must conduct student-source and employment-market research and ensure that submissions accurately reflect the research process and results.

Printed materials should be ordered, bound, printed on A4 paper and submitted in two copies. Printed and electronic materials are due to the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office by **May 31**. Contact Jiang Songqi at `sonky@stu.edu.cn` or `86502416`.
