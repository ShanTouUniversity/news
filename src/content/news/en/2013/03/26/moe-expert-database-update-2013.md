It is reported that the Research Office forwarded a notice on March 26, 2013, from the Center for Science and Technology Development of the Ministry of Education regarding updating the expert database for research funds and science and technology awards.

## Update Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Associate professor or above; priority for academicians, Changjiang Scholars, NSFC Distinguished Young Scholars, full professors, frontier/emerging discipline experts and outstanding mid-career contributors |
| Focus | Supplement experts in certain disciplines (see Attachment 2) |
| Existing members | Log in to the MOE Expert Information System (http://211.68.23.118/zjk) with existing account by April 5 to update personal information, especially discipline codes, titles and secondary unit affiliation |
| New applicants | Email cszhu@stu.edu.cn by April 2 for an account and complete the system entry by April 5 to apply for inclusion |
| Contacts | Hu Yunxiang, Zhu Changsheng; phone 86502813 |
| Issued by | Research Office |

The existing expert list and discipline details are in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ Attachments are available on the OA system.
