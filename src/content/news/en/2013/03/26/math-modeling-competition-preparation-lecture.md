It is reported that the 135th session of the "Approaching Modern Mathematics" lecture series, organized by the School of Science, will be held from 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 27, at the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412).

The lecture, titled "How to Prepare for Mathematical Modeling Competitions," will be delivered by Li Jian of the Department of Mathematics, School of Science. The talk will introduce the background of mathematical modeling activities, analyze modeling cases, and share strategies for preparing for competitions, highlighting how students can apply mathematical knowledge and skills to solve real-world problems.

Li Jian received his Ph.D. in Science from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2012 and joined the Department of Mathematics at Shantou University in June 2012. In the 2012 National Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling, eight STU teams under his supervision won one national first prize, two national second prizes, three first prizes and five second prizes in the Guangdong division.

The 2013 STU Undergraduate Mathematical Modeling Competition is scheduled for April 12–19, and students are encouraged to sign up. Details are available on the STU Mathematical Modeling website at http://math.stu.edu.cn/modeling/shownews.asp?id=21.
