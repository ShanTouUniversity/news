The fifth Guangdong University Chemistry and Chemical-Engineering Laboratory Skills Competition is scheduled for **June 2013** at Sun Yat-sen University. STU’s School of Science will hold an internal selection to choose student representatives for the provincial competition and facilitate exchange with students from universities across Guangdong.

The selection is open to second- and third-year Applied Chemistry students. The preliminary round is a **90-minute written test** from **3:00 to 4:30 p.m. on April 3** in E208. The 10 highest-scoring students will advance to the final, which will test laboratory operations; details will be announced later.

Registration closes on **March 29**. Send the registration form to the School of Science at `o_lxyhxx@stu.edu.cn`. The form contains personal information and is not reproduced here.
