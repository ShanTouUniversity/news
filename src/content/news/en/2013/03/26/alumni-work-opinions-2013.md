Shantou University has issued the *Opinion on Strengthening Alumni Affairs* (STU Document [2013] No. 36), setting out the organization and priorities for alumni affairs at the university and school levels. Since the STU Alumni Association was founded in 1999, the university has established **22 municipal and prefectural alumni chapters**.

The opinion describes alumni affairs as an integral part of the university’s work and calls for a network led by a university leader, coordinated by the Alumni Affairs Office, implemented by the schools and supported by relevant departments. The university office will organize university-wide activities, develop platforms, manage information exchange, and guide and serve local chapters and schools. Each school should establish an alumni office, co-located with its school office, designate a leader, director and part-time staff, and provide funding and accountability.

### Priorities

The university and its schools will focus on:

- Alumni liaison, information collection, database maintenance and daily services, including two annual issues of *STU Alumni* and a monthly electronic newsletter;
- Publicizing alumni and university development through the university newspaper, website, radio and television, and developing alumni-culture projects such as alumni gardens;
- Building an alumni network platform for publicity, liaison, exchange, donations and future expansion;
- Organizing return visits and field-specific reunions around enrollment and graduation anniversaries, including five- and ten-year milestones;
- Developing students’ alumni awareness, connecting alumni with current students, and helping graduates contact local alumni chapters near their workplaces;
- Supporting alumni further study and helping alumni organizations with recruitment, training and technology consultation;
- Expanding giving through the education fund and encouraging alumni and alumni enterprises to establish scholarships, grants and student entrepreneurship funds, while supporting admissions, internships and employment;
- Building platforms for alumni cooperation in teaching, research, campus development and student career development.

The opinion calls on alumni-affairs staff to strengthen professional learning, improve the working mechanism through practice and build an alumni-affairs system with STU characteristics.
