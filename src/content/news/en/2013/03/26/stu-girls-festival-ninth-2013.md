Shantou University’s ninth Girls’ Festival will run **from March 27 through April 14**. The Student Union has organized six activities focused on campus culture, student interaction and women’s development:

- **“Wish Umbrella”**: From March 27 to April 12 at Golden Triangle, female students may hang wishes on an umbrella while members of the university community may volunteer to help fulfill them. A number-guessing draw will also be held on March 27.
- **“The Most Beautiful Scenery in My Eyes”**: Street photographs will be collected from March 27 to April 3, followed by voting on Weibo and the Girls’ Festival website from April 4 to 12. The top three will be selected based on votes and reposts.
- **“Meeting Yourself”**: On March 30, “Beauty at the Tip of the Pen” will introduce makeup and beauty tips from 3:00 to 4:30 p.m. in Room 726 of Building 7. “Speak Out Your Charm” will discuss women’s social roles, careers, relationships and personal experiences from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. in the Library Lecture Hall.
- **“The Girls in Our Class over the Years”**: Classes will organize Girls’ Festival activities from March 27 to April 14, prepare records, photographs, videos or satisfaction surveys, and submit electronic results by April 10. A class-results exhibition will be held on April 14, with activity funding for the top three classes.
- **“Caring Breakfast Bus”**: Breakfast will be offered to female students on April 2 at Golden Triangle and Zhicheng Bridge.
- **Food Festival**: Schools and colleges will operate food stalls together on the Pedestrian Street on April 13. Participants who collect five stamps by playing games at the stalls may enter a prize draw.

Follow the Weibo account “STU Ninth Girls’ Festival” for further information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee and STU Student Union)
