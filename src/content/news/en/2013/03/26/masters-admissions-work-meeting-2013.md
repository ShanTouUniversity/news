Shantou University will hold its 2013 master’s admissions work meeting at **3:00 p.m. on April 1** in Science Building Room 205. The meeting will convey the Guangdong Provincial Education Examination Authority’s graduate-admissions guidance, review admissions progress and outstanding issues in each master’s discipline, and organize STU’s admissions interviews, medical examinations and proposed-admission work.

Graduate-work coordinators and admissions staff from each master’s discipline should attend on time.
