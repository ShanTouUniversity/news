It is reported that the signature arts program "Colorful Arts Season" returns for Spring 2013, with the Arts Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design inviting Wanma Dance Theatre to perform on campus.

## Performance Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Troupe | Wanma Dance Theatre |
| Date | March 30, 2013 (Saturday) |
| Time | 19:30 |
| Venue | Grand Auditorium |
| Organizer | Arts Education Center, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

Further information about the program is available in the attachment.

## Ticket Collection

Tickets are free and distributed on a first-come, first-served basis, available only to those aged 12 and above:

- **March 28 (Thu), 14:30–16:30**: Room 309, Arts Education Center
- **March 29 (Fri), 12:15–13:30**: Golden Triangle
