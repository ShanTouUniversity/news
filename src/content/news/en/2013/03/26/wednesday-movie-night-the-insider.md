It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host its Wednesday Movie Night on March 27 (Wednesday) with a screening of *The Insider* (1999). All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

## Screening Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Film | *The Insider* (1999), directed by Michael Mann |
| Time | March 27, 2013, 19:00–22:00 |
| Venue | Theater in the Library |
| Seats | 200 (150 for students plus 50 open to the public, first-come first-served) |
| Admission | Students admitted with campus card |

## About the Film

Based on Marie Brenner's Vanity Fair article "The Man Who Knew Too Much," the film tells the true story of tobacco industry whistleblower Jeffrey Wigand and CBS *60 Minutes* producer Lowell Bergman. After Wigand is dismissed by a major tobacco company, Bergman enlists him to interpret a report showing the health harms of cigarettes. Facing surveillance, threats, a confidentiality agreement and a gag order, Wigand ultimately decides to go public — recording a *60 Minutes* segment and testifying in Mississippi — while Bergman fights to get the story aired despite resistance from CBS management.

In 2000, the film was nominated for seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Director, Best Adapted Screenplay, Best Actor, Best Cinematography, Best Film Editing and Best Sound.
