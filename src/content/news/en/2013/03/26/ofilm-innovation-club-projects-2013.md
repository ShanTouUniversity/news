To support the School of Engineering’s CDIO education reform, Shantou University’s OFILM Innovation Club has opened project-training and project-improvement plans for students across the university, encouraging participation in university-enterprise collaboration and project development.

There are two categories:

- **OFILM Innovation Club themed projects**: These include projects developed jointly by OFILM and the School of Engineering. Students may apply individually or as teams; students applying individually will be assigned to teams by the club. Teams may have no more than five members, each student may join only one project, and interdisciplinary, cross-major and cross-year collaboration is encouraged.
- **Project-improvement plan**: This is for completed projects that have passed their original completion defense but still have room for improvement. Applications must include a clear improvement plan.

Each project is normally eligible for no more than **RMB 2,000** in support and must have one or two faculty advisers. The themed projects will be advised by club technical consultant Wu Fupei. Projects will run for **six months to one year**, and project leaders must complete them before graduation and departure from the university.

Applications close on **April 12**. The themed-project process includes registration, a project overview, team formation and an initial defense. The improvement-plan process includes registration, submission of a proposal and an initial defense. Initial-defense times and locations will be announced later. Obtain the registration form, improvement proposal and project materials from the original OA attachments.

Project funds are earmarked and may not be repurposed. Teams must submit an expenditure list and explanation at completion; delayed projects receive no additional funding, and projects terminated midway will not receive remaining funds.

### Initial review result

On **May 6, 2013**, the School of Engineering reported that the OFILM Innovation Club project-improvement plans and themed projects had completed their initial defenses and review by the judging panel. The final results are provided in the notice attachment. Selected teams were asked to proceed according to their plans, and a midterm defense was scheduled for **September**.

Because the results attachment contains student names and individual or team mappings, this article does not reproduce the detailed list.
