Shantou University’s Graduate School has recognized outstanding graduate projects based on the 2012 graduate extracurricular technology-project review and results from the ninth Challenge Cup student academic and technology competition.

The awards include **two first-prize projects**, **three second-prize projects** and **two third-prize projects**, for seven projects in total. The Graduate School encourages the recognized projects to continue developing their results and urges graduate students to participate actively in extracurricular academic activities and strengthen their research and innovation skills.

The original OA attachment contains the awarded project and author list. Because it includes graduate students’ personal information, this article reports only the award counts and does not reproduce the list.
