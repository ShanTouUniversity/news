The Women’s Studies Center of Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will join partner organizations in holding a Guangzhou forum from **April 12 to 14** at Sun Yat-sen University and South China University of Technology. The center is recruiting **10 STU students** to participate.

The forum will use lectures and on-site discussion and focus on “Gender and Media” and “Gender and Law.” It is organized by STU’s Women’s Studies Center and the Sun Yat-sen University Gender Education Forum, with support from the South China University of Technology Gender Research Center, New Media Women’s Network, Sinner-B and the Women’s Rights and Lesbian Group.

Applications are open until **noon on April 5**. Send the registration form to `cws@stu.edu.cn` using the subject format specified in the original notice. Consult the OA notice for the detailed itinerary and recruitment requirements.

A subsequent notice reported that the organizers received **42 registration forms** and selected **10 students** to participate in the Guangzhou event from April 12 to 14. The Women’s Studies Center publishes further information through its Weibo account at <http://weibo.com/stucws> and website at <http://cws.stu.edu.cn>.
