The opening event of Shantou University’s 2013 General Education Forum lecture series will be held at **7:30 p.m. on March 29** in the Library Lecture Hall. Professor Qianfan Zhang of Peking University will speak on “Constitutional Democracy as the Highest National Interest.” The lecture is organized by STU’s Higher Education Institute.

Professor Zhang researches constitutional law and political institutions. His books include *Legal Regulation of the Market Economy*, *The U.S. Constitution and Government Structure*, *The Western Constitutional System* and *The Federal Constitution of the United States*, alongside translated works, papers and commentary. Further information is available on his [blog](http://const123.fyfz.cn/blog/const123/) and [Weibo page](http://weibo.com/qfzpku).
