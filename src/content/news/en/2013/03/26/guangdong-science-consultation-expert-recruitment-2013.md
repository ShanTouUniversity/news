Guangdong’s science authorities are recruiting experts from multiple fields and updating the existing science-consultation expert database to support consultation, review and acceptance of science and technology projects. The database includes technical experts, soft-science and science-policy experts, international-cooperation management experts, and science-finance management experts.

Applicants should be committed to science consultation, impartial, honest and self-disciplined, familiar with research, technology transfer, industry developments and science-and-economy policy, compliant with relevant rules, and in good health. The general age limit is 60. Each expert category has additional requirements for research, science administration, international cooperation or financial-management experience; for example, technical experts generally need at least eight years of relevant research, an associate-senior or higher professional title, a master’s degree or above, and experience leading a provincial- or ministerial-level project.

### How to apply

New applicants should use the Guangdong Science and Technology Business System at <http://pro.gdstc.gov.cn>, select “Apply to enter the expert database” and complete the required submission. Applicants who already have a system account may apply through that account. Those without an account may email their name, email address and department to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn` to request activation, specifying “Guangdong science-consultation expert database.” Existing experts may use their accounts, switch their role to “science-consultation expert” and update their records under personal settings before submitting for review.

The deadline is **April 8**. Approved applicants will be eligible as candidate reviewers for the 2013 science and technology plan, and the system will remain open for applications. STU contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at `86502813`.
