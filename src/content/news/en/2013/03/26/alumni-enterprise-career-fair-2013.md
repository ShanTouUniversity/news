Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office and Alumni Affairs Office will hold the **2013 STU Alumni-Enterprise Career Fair at 9:00 a.m. on March 29** in the New Library Student Activity Hall. **Twenty-five companies**, including Shenzhen Shun Dian Chain Co., Ltd., Suning Appliance, Xinyi Semiconductor Co., Ltd. and Guangdong Xianle Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., will attend the recruitment event.

Schools and colleges are asked to notify graduating students to attend on time and learn about employment opportunities with alumni enterprises. Consult the original OA attachment for the complete company and position list.
