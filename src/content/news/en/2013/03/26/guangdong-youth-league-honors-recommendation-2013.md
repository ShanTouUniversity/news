Guangdong’s Communist Youth League system plans to recognize outstanding members, cadres and May Fourth Red Flag organizations during the 2013 May Fourth period. Shantou University’s Youth League Committee has opened its internal recommendation process. STU has **two provincial recommendation places** this year: one for a Guangdong Outstanding Youth League Member and one for a Guangdong May Fourth Red Flag Youth League Committee.

### Recommendation criteria

An Outstanding Youth League Member candidate should observe the League constitution, participate actively in League activities, perform well in study or work, serve young people, take part in volunteer service, and have received a city-level or higher Youth League honor or award since 2008. Full-time League cadres and Party members retaining League membership are not eligible for this category.

A May Fourth Red Flag Youth League Committee should have a sound organizational structure and orderly internal work, conduct member education and member recommendations for Party membership, serve and protect young people, and achieve clear results in key activities. It should also have received a city-level May Fourth Red Flag Youth League Committee or related organizational honor since 2008. An organization recommended as a provincial May Fourth Red Flag committee may not simultaneously recommend a subordinate branch for this selection.

The provincial preliminary notice also lists honors for outstanding Youth League cadres, county-level Youth League secretaries, branch secretaries and May Fourth Red Flag branches. Consult the OA attachments for the detailed requirements. Recommendations should be factual and merit-based, focus on grassroots and frontline candidates, and take account of views from local Party and League organizations and members.

### Submission arrangements

Candidates and organizations must complete online submissions at the [Guangdong Youth League Pioneering and Excellence website](http://cxzy.gdcyl.org) by **April 1**. Submit two paper copies of the relevant form and achievement materials, together with electronic copies, to the STU Youth League Committee Office. Send electronic materials to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`.
