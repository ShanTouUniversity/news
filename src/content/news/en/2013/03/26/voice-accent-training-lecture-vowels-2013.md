The first lecture in Shantou University’s 2013 Voice and Accent Training Lecture Series will be held from **2:15 to 3:45 p.m. on March 27** in E Lecture Room 101. Pragya Vijay will speak on “Sounds of English: Vowels.”

The lecture is open to the entire university community.
