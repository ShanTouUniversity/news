Shantou University’s Graduate School has announced the results of the 2012 National Graduate Scholarship review. Following student applications, preliminary recommendations by schools, departments and institutes, review by the university’s graduate scholarship panel, and an uncontested public notice, **52 master’s students and four doctoral students** received awards.

The master’s awards by unit were: 24 from the Medical College, 10 from the College of Science, eight from the School of Engineering, two each from the School of Law, Business School, School of Liberal Arts and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and one each from the Institute of Higher Education and Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication. The doctoral awards included two from the College of Science and two from the Medical College.

The review was conducted under the relevant Guangdong provincial finance and education authorities’ graduate-scholarship measures and review notices. The original OA attachment contains the award list; student names are not reproduced here.
