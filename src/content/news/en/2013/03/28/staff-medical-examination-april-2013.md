The Health Management Center of STU First Affiliated Hospital will conduct medical examinations in batches for faculty and staff who selected the hospital, on **April 3 and April 7–12**. Participants should arrive **fasting at 7:40 a.m.** on the date assigned to them.

The former STU First Affiliated Hospital Medical Examination Center has been renamed the Health Management Center and moved to the third floor of the Science and Technology Building at **52 Dongxia South Road**, opposite the outpatient building beside Lantian Pharmacy. Consult the OA attachment for individual batch arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)

> ⚠️ The examination schedule contains staff names and health-related personal information and is not reproduced. Consult the original OA notice for individual arrangements.
