With approval from China Education Television, the STU station of China Education Television has been established. Shantou University Campus TV plans to launch a campus-news program and is recruiting student hosts, writers, camera reporters and designers, offering opportunities in campus communication, internships and portfolio development.

### Positions

- **Hosts**: Open to students in the 2011 and 2012 cohorts. Applicants should have standard Mandarin and an interest in broadcasting; related experience or competition experience is preferred. The station plans to recruit five women and five men.
- **Writers**: Open to students in the 2011 and 2012 cohorts. Applicants should be enthusiastic, conscientious, responsible, fluent writers with sufficient free time for interviews and editing assignments.
- **Camera reporters**: Open to students in the 2011 and 2012 cohorts. Applicants should be able to use camera equipment and produce video; experience is preferred, but beginners are welcome to apply in teams.
- **Designers**: Open to students in the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts. Applicants should be able to arrange studio lighting or design program settings and backdrops.

Through its cooperation with China Education Television, the station may submit student-produced programs for credited broadcast on the network. Broadcast videos may also serve as portfolio pieces for internships and job interviews. Each position will use a corresponding assessment process; interview times and locations will be sent by email after applications are collected.

Send a résumé to `o_gbds@stu.edu.cn` or submit it to Campus TV in Room 408, Xingxi, by **April 6**. Consult the original OA notice for detailed requirements.
