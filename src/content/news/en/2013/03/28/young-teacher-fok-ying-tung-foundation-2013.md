The Fok Ying Tung Education Foundation has opened applications for its 2013 university young-teacher research funds and Young Teacher Award. STU’s Office of Research is organizing internal recommendations for:

- **Basic research projects under the Young Teacher Fund**: STU may recommend two projects and the internal process is quota-limited;
- **Applied research projects under the Young Teacher Fund**: No quota applies, subject to the foundation’s application guide;
- **Young Teacher Award**: STU may recommend one candidate.

Applicants or nominees must be **35 or younger**, meaning born on or after March 15, 1977, and must hold a doctoral degree or an associate-professor or higher professional title.

The basic-research projects and Young Teacher Award are quota-limited. Interested faculty should apply through their schools, which will select one candidate and submit the candidate list and the nominee’s research and teaching record from the past five years to the Office of Research by **April 2**. Applied-research applicants may submit without a quota and should email the 2013 application form to `yxhu@stu.edu.cn` by **April 10**.

Consult the original OA attachments for discipline quotas, application guides and forms.
