Tickets for *Shambhala*, a dance-theater performance by the Beijing Wanma Dance Theatre on **March 30**, have all been distributed. To accommodate faculty members and students who did not obtain tickets but wish to attend, the organizers will arrange standby seating:

- Ticketless attendees may queue in the designated area outside the Auditorium’s main entrance from **7:00 p.m. on March 30**;
- If seats assigned to distributed tickets remain empty **before the 7:30 p.m. curtain**, the organizers will treat those seats as forfeited and staff will guide standby attendees into them;
- Seats are limited, entry will stop when the venue is full, and queuing does not guarantee admission.

Ticket holders should take their seats 10 minutes before the performance as required by their tickets.

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Arts Education Center)
