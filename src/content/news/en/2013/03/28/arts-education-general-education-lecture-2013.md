Shantou University’s Arts Education Center will host an arts-education lecture from **7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on March 29** in the Science Lecture Hall. Professor Yongjie Wang of Guangdong University of Technology will speak on “Reflections on Universal Arts Education in Higher Education in the Context of Cultural Heritage and Innovation.”

Professor Wang is a professor and deputy director of the university’s General Education Center. His research focuses on arts-education theory in higher education and music criticism. He has published more than 20 papers in related academic journals, edited or co-edited five books and received Ministry of Education awards. The lecture is open to the entire university community.
