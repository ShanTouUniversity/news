Because public-security authorities across Guangdong are updating their population-information systems, Shantou University’s household-registration services will pause in stages:

- **March 29**: Household-registration staff at the local police station will suspend household-migration services for training;
- **6:00 p.m. on March 30 to 8:00 a.m. on April 8**: Household registration, household migration, household-record changes, resident identity cards and residence permits, along with other related services, will be suspended;
- **After 8:00 a.m. on April 8**: Services will resume normally.

Faculty members, staff and students who need these services should plan ahead and follow subsequent notices from the Security Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office)
