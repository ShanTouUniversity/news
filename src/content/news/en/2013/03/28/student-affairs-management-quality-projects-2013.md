The Guangdong University Student-Work Professional Committee has opened the second selection of “Exemplary Student-Affairs Management Projects” to advance scientific and professional student affairs and ideological and political education. STU’s Student Affairs Office will organize an internal review and recommend **one candidate project** to the provincial committee.

Eligible projects must be run by a school or department counselor or a university- or school-level student-work team, have operated continuously for **at least three years**, produced clear educational results and gained influence on or beyond campus. Long-running student-work practices will be assessed for their philosophy, operating mechanism and educational outcomes.

Each candidate must submit an application form and an approximately **5,000-character** project document covering the project name, philosophy, operation model, participants, target groups, time and space, content, procedures, support, technical methods, results and distinctive features.

Schools and colleges must submit electronic and printed copies to the Student Education and Management Section in Administrative Center Room 106 by **April 30**. Name and package electronic materials as “school or college name + exemplary project” and email them to `lslin@stu.edu.cn`. Follow the original OA attachments for the required forms and formatting.

### Selection results

The Guangdong University Student-Work Professional Committee recognized **30 Exemplary Student-Affairs Management Projects** and **45 Outstanding Student-Affairs Management Projects**. STU’s recommended *Advanced Outdoor Development Project* was selected as an exemplary project. The university’s student-affairs system will use the recognition to continue professionalizing and strengthening student-affairs management.
