Shantou University and the National University of Ireland Galway (NUI Galway) will establish an undergraduate exchange program based on credit transfer, with the first intake planned for **September 2013**. The program aims to promote academic exchange, broaden students’ international perspectives and strengthen their overall and employment-related skills.

The program offers **10 places** to STU second- and third-year undergraduates, excluding students of the Medical College. Exchange students may apply to any of NUI Galway’s four colleges other than the College of Medicine, Nursing and Health Sciences. The basic requirements are:

- A GPA of at least 3.0 on a four-point scale or an average grade of at least 75;
- IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 80;
- Required materials such as a transcript, a copy of a birth certificate, a faculty recommendation letter, academic-contact information and a personal statement.

### Fees and applications

For 2013–2014, the notice lists non-EU tuition as EUR 6,000 per semester and EUR 12,000 per year for arts, business and law. For science and engineering it lists EUR 7,750 per semester and records the annual amount as “EUR 15,000 per semester,” an inconsistent unit in the source. If six or more STU students are admitted in a semester or academic year, tuition may be reduced to EUR 5,000 per semester and EUR 10,000 per year. Average living costs, including accommodation, are estimated at EUR 1,000 per month.

The application deadlines differ between two OA postings: the earlier version lists **April 20** for the full academic year or first semester and **October 21** for the second semester, while the follow-up version issued on March 27 lists **April 13** and **October 14**, respectively. Applicants should confirm with the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation and follow the latest OA notice. Email electronic materials to `wscicd@stu.edu.cn` with “NUI Galway exchange program application” in the subject line. Course and application information is available on NUI Galway’s [international-student page](http://www.nuigalway.ie/international-students/study-abroad-at-nuigalway.html).
