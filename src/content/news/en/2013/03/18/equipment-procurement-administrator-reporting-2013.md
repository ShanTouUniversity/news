STU’s Equipment and Materials Management Office previously asked each school, department, and administrative unit to recommend one equipment-procurement administrator to coordinate routine small purchases with the office. Some units have not yet completed the recommendation process and are asked to submit their administrator’s information.

An engineer from Guangzhou Ways Information Technology Co., Ltd. will provide **online bidding-system training at 3:00 p.m. on Friday, March 22** in Room 501, Building E. Each unit must report its administrator’s name and contact details to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by **March 20**.

Submit information at the former Reception Room or contact `86502558`, Ms. Huang, `zxhuang@stu.edu.cn`.
