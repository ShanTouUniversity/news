The STU union will hold a faculty and staff poker competition with men’s and women’s divisions. The number of groups advancing will be determined by the number of registrations.

The preliminary round is scheduled for **8:00–10:30 p.m. on Thursday, March 28**, and the final for **8:00–10:30 p.m. on Tuesday, April 2**, both on the third floor of the union building. Interested faculty and staff should complete the registration form and email it to `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn` by **Friday, March 22**, using “Poker Competition Registration” as the subject. For questions, call `86503197` or contact Ms. Ye.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Union)
