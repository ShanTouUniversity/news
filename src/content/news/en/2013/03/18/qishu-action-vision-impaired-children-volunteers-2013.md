The Dawn Education Project of the STU Youth Volunteers Association has launched the second **Qishu Action, “Let Smiles Remain in the Sunshine,”** and is recruiting **17 volunteers** from across the university to provide educational support for children with visual impairments in Shantou.

The service will run from **late March through late May on Wednesday afternoons** at the Shantou Disabled Persons’ Activity Center. Working with the Minghui Basic Education Class for Children with Visual Impairments, volunteers will use group teaching or one-to-one tutoring and offer “second classroom” activities such as storytelling and performance, music, handicrafts, science activities, and exercises.

Applicants should care about public service, be willing to commit time, and demonstrate responsibility, patience, perseverance, communication skills, and teamwork. Volunteers will be selected through interviews. Interviews are scheduled for **2:00–6:00 p.m. on March 20 in Room 202, Building D**, and **7:00–9:00 p.m. in Room 201, Building D**.

Registration closes at **6:00 p.m. on March 20**. The electronic form is available through the Office Automation system or the Youth Volunteers Association section of the BBS and should be submitted through the activity’s designated email channel. An information and on-site registration desk will operate at the Golden Triangle from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. on March 18 and 19.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association)
