STU Library has issued its first 2013 and third overall featured-literature bulletin, focusing on Li Ka-shing and his connection with Shantou University. The library holds more than 100 related books and has selected eight representative works for this issue. Readers can search the library catalog for additional materials: <http://202.192.155.48:83/opac/searchresult.aspx?title=%c0%ee%bc%ce%b3%cf&dt=ALL&cl=ALL&dept=ALL&sf=M_PUB_YEAR&ob=DESC&page=1&dp=20&sm=table>.

The recommended titles are:

1. *A Biography of Li Ka-shing*, Lan Chao, Mingliu Publishing House, Hong Kong, 2000;
2. *The 12 Obstacles Li Ka-shing Overcame*, edited by Wang Junyun, China Overseas Chinese Publishing House, 2007;
3. *Li Ka-shing and Shantou University*, compiled by the former State Education Commission of the People’s Republic of China, 1992;
4. *The Business Wisdom of Li Ka-shing*, edited by Liang Xiaogang, China Social Publishing House, 2004;
5. *Li Ka-shing’s Wealth Notes*, Shangguan Zhongyuan, People’s Daily Press, 2011;
6. *Li Ka-shing’s Course on Happiness in Life*, Zhang Xiaoheng, Beijing University of Technology Press, 2011;
7. *10 Pieces of Advice from Li Ka-shing for Young People*, Zhao Wenming, Jincheng Press, 2007;
8. *Knowledge Changes Destiny: Selected Speeches by Mr. Li Ka-shing*, edited by Li Yuguang, 2005.

The collection approaches Li Ka-shing’s life and work through biographies, business thinking, life experience, public speeches, and his relationship with STU, offering faculty and students a focused route into the library’s holdings.
