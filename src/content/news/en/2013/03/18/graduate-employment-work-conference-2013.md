It is reported that Shantou University will convene a graduate employment work conference at 3:00 p.m. on Tuesday, March 19, 2013, in Room 213 of the new administrative center, to coordinate employment efforts for the Class of 2013.

Participants included Party general branch secretaries or deputy secretaries of each school (residential college), counselors responsible for employment work, and staff from the Graduate Employment Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office. The conference was organized by the Student Affairs Office and aimed to advance the implementation of various employment-related measures.
