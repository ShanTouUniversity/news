STU’s 2013 **Test for English Majors Band 8 (TEM8)** will begin at **8:30 a.m. on Saturday, March 23** and last **195 minutes**. The sections are scheduled as follows:

| Part | Content | Time |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Part I | Listening comprehension: note-taking and gap-filling, plus Sections B and C | About 35 minutes |
| Part II | Reading comprehension | 30 minutes |
| Part III | General knowledge | 10 minutes |
| Part IV | Proofreading and error correction | 15 minutes |
| Part V | Translation | 60 minutes |
| Part VI | Writing | 45 minutes |

Section A of Part I is completed on Answer Sheet 1. Sections B and C and Parts II–IV are completed on Answer Sheet 2. Parts V and VI are completed on Answer Sheets 3 and 4 respectively.

### Examination requirements

Candidates arriving more than 15 minutes after the start may not enter. Once the examination has begun, candidates may not leave the examination site before the end. Candidates may submit after the first 60 minutes; those who submit early will be supervised in a designated rest room, where mobile phones and other communication devices are prohibited. Candidates may not submit during the final 30 minutes.

Use a 2B pencil for multiple-choice answers, selecting one answer per question. Use a black-ink pen with a tip of at least 0.5 mm for other answers, and stay within the marked areas. Do not write the university name or your own name in blank areas of the answer sheets. Failure to submit on time cancels the examination result.

Candidates must bring their **admission card, student ID, and identity card**. Candidates without complete identification may not sit the examination.
