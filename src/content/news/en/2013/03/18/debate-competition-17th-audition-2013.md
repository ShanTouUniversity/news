It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Union of Shantou University issued a notice on March 18, 2013, that the 17th University Debate Competition will soon be held. In addition to college and residential college teams, this year’s competition will open an audition channel for open teams to compete alongside college teams.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Requirements | No restriction on major or grade, at least four members per team; open teams represent only themselves and not college results |
| Deadline | March 22, 2013 |
| How to apply | Send member names, team leader, phone, email, major and grade to the designated email address or via private message to the Academic Planning Department’s Weibo account |

Contact details are withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The registration contacts are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Student Union)
