The STU Mathematical Modeling Association will hold its 2013 undergraduate mathematical-modeling competition for enrolled undergraduates across the university. The competition aims to help students understand how mathematics can address practical problems, connect learning with application, and identify strong participants for national mathematical-modeling competitions.

The competition will be held in the Mathematics Department laboratory. The main notice lists the competition period as **April 12–19**. Registration runs from **March 17 through midnight on April 2**, with related training during the registration period. Participants register in teams, generally with three members responsible for areas such as writing, programming, and modeling. The association will coordinate teams with fewer members; teams of more than three will be considered improperly formed.

The attached instructions state that competition questions will be released at **8:00 a.m. on April 15** through the Mathematical Modeling Association website, forum, and group file-sharing area. Because this release date differs from the main notice’s competition period, participants should follow subsequent arrangements from the association.

The competition offers first-, second-, and third-place awards with certificates, prize money, and award documents, as well as a successful-participation award. The registration form requests team members’ names, year, major, gender, telephone numbers, and email addresses. Consult the Mathematical Modeling Association website at <http://math.stu.edu.cn/modeling/index.asp> and QQ group `171331093` for the form and detailed requirements.
