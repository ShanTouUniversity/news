The STU Business School’s 115th Economic Salon will be held from **9:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Saturday, March 23** in the New Library Lecture Hall. Feng Haifa, director of the Rural Affairs Bureau of the Central Policy Research Office, will give a talk titled **“An In-Depth Analysis of the 18th Party Congress Report.”** From an economic perspective, the session will examine policies in the report and the likely direction of China’s domestic economy.

Feng Haifa is director of the Rural Affairs Bureau of the Central Policy Research Office. He has served as assistant director, professor, and doctoral supervisor at the Institute of Agricultural Economics of the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences, and his research has focused on areas including macroeconomics.
