STU’s J-School will host a **Daniel Ng advertising series on March 19–20**, open to journalism and communication students and the wider university community.

| Activity | Time | Location | Main audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lecture: “What Is Advertising?” | March 19, 7:30–9:30 p.m. | Library Science Lecture Hall | University community |
| Seminar: “Advertising Account Management” | March 20, 10:10 a.m.–12:00 | ACC Meeting Room 3 | 2011 advertising students in the J-School and the university community |
| Workshop: “The Qualities of an Advertising Professional” | March 20, 7:10–9:00 p.m. | E406 | Students interested in advertising |

Daniel Ng is an associate professor at the Gaylord College of Journalism and Mass Communication at the University of Oklahoma’s Norman campus. He previously served as a senior account executive at international advertising agencies including McCann, Ogilvy & Mather, BBDO, and Grey Worldwide, managing brand accounts including Unilever, Nestlé, Johnson & Johnson, Apple, Procter & Gamble, and Wrigley.
