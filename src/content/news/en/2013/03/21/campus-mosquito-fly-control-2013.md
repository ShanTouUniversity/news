To improve campus environmental sanitation, Shantou University’s Resource Management Office will conduct **mosquito and fly control from March 22 to 26** across the campus, Longquan Garden and the School of Continuing Education. Faculty, staff and students are asked to take precautions and note the area schedule:

| Date | Areas |
| --- | --- |
| March 22 | Around the campus security office, campus gates, campus roads and the perimeter of the New Library |
| March 23 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, student residence areas, cafeterias and the Administration Building |
| March 24 | The affiliated middle and primary schools through the Mental Health Center, the A–B faculty residence area and Building 789 |
| March 25 | The Medical Science Building, New Teaching Building and G, H, I and J student residences |
| March 26 | Longquan Garden and the School of Continuing Education |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
