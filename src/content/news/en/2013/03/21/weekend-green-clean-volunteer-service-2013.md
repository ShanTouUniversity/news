The Shantou Youth League Committee, Municipal Urban Management Bureau and Shantou Young Volunteers Association have launched the “Big Hands Hold Little Hands: Greener and Cleaner Together” volunteer service. The family-volunteer activity invites parents and children to help maintain greenery and clean public spaces as Shantou works toward national ecological-garden-city and forest-city designations.

The activity will run **from March 16 through the end of 2013**, with environmental-cleaning work held at Times Square from **4:00 to 5:00 p.m. every Saturday**. STU faculty, staff, students and other interested volunteers do not need to register in advance. They should arrive by **3:30 p.m. on Saturdays** at the Times Square Management Office on Jinhuan Road, south of Galaxy Building, to check in and collect tools.
