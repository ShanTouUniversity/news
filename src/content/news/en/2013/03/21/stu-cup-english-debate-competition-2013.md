STU’s English Language Center will hold the inaugural STU Cup English debate competition for students across the university. The competition will use the **British Parliamentary format**. Students may register in pairs or individually and be assigned to a team by the organizers.

The competition will take place in the E Teaching Building on **April 13 and 14**. Three preliminary points rounds will be held throughout April 13, followed by another points round, semifinals and the final on April 14. After four points rounds, the top eight teams will advance to the semifinals. The competition will award one champion, one runner-up and two third-place teams. Four teams and eight debaters will compete in each debate; motions will be announced 30 minutes before each debate, and each speech will last seven minutes.

The champion team will receive **RMB 300**, the runner-up will receive **RMB 200**, and the third-place teams will receive commemorative gifts. Every participating team will receive a certificate.

Registration closes at **11:59 p.m. on March 31**. Participants should complete the registration form and email it to `11kyyang@stu.edu.cn`. For inquiries, contact the English Language Center at `10yhe@stu.edu.cn`.
