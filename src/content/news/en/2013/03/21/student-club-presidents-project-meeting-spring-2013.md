The Shantou University Youth League Committee and Student Clubs Federation will hold the **spring 2012–2013 student-club presidents’ forum and activity-project launch meeting from 2:30 to 4:10 p.m. on Saturday, March 23, 2013**. The meeting will take place in Room 406 of the E Teaching Building.

Student-club leaders are asked to prepare in advance and attend on time. The meeting will introduce and discuss activity-project planning for the spring semester.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee)
