Shantou University’s Physical Education Department will hold its inaugural table-tennis trials to select players for the Guangdong University Student Table Tennis Championship. The trials are open to full-time undergraduate and graduate students with formal enrollment at the main campus. Graduate students must have graduated from Shantou University as undergraduates; students from the Medical School are excluded from this trial because the school fields its own team for the provincial championship.

The events are men’s singles and women’s singles. Participating units include the schools of Liberal Arts, Science, Engineering, Law and Business, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, the Graduate School and Zhicheng College. The venue is the STU gymnasium. The main notice schedules the event for **March 30 and 31**, while the attached competition regulations list **March 30** as the competition date. Participants should follow the final schedule issued by the Physical Education Department.

The competition will have a group round-robin stage followed by cross-bracket elimination and use a best-of-five format under the latest rules approved by the Chinese Table Tennis Association. The top two players in each singles event will qualify to represent STU at the 2013 Guangdong University Student Table Tennis Championship. The head coach will determine other team-member qualifications according to the team’s needs. The draw is scheduled for **1:00 p.m. on March 28**. Players must report to the venue 15 minutes before their scheduled matches; a 10-minute late arrival counts as a forfeit.

Registration closes at **noon on March 26**. Consult the original OA attachments for the registration form and detailed submission instructions.
