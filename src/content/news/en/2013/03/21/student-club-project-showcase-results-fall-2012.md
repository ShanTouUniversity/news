Shantou University held its fall 2012–2013 student-club activity project showcase on **March 20** in the multimedia meeting room in Room 726 of Building 7. Nine clubs took part in the defenses: the Tea Ceremony Club, Calligraphy and Painting Association, Chess Association, Finance Association, Guitar Association, Science and Technology Creation Association, Xingchao Club, Sign Language Association and Table Tennis Association. After review by the judges and approval by the university Youth League Committee, the results were as follows:

| Rank | Project | Club | Score | Award | Activity funding (RMB) |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 1 | “Autumn Ink Cup” Campus Calligraphy and Painting Competition | Calligraphy and Painting Association | 369 | First Prize | 1,000 |
| 2 | Christmas Unplugged Concert | Guitar Association | 365 | Second Prize | 500 |
| 3 | Chess Competition | Chess Association | 354 | Second Prize | 500 |
| 3 | “Taste of Chaoshan” Series | Xingchao Club | 353 | Not listed | Not listed |
| 5 | Hand Dance, Heart Language: First Sign Language Promotion Week | Sign Language Club | 346 | Not listed | Not listed |
| 6 | Finance Promotion Week | Finance Association | 338 | Not listed | Not listed |
| 7 | 15th Table Tennis Singles Open | Table Tennis Association | 335 | Not listed | Not listed |
| 8 | Community Care Plan: Yixiu Service Initiative | Science and Technology Creation Association | 333 | Not listed | Not listed |
| 9 | Fragrant Tea on Campus | Tea Ceremony Club | 327 | Not listed | Not listed |

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee)
