In accordance with Shantou University’s Qingming Festival holiday schedule, the Graduate School has adjusted classes for graduate students at the main campus as follows:

- **Classes are suspended on Thursday, April 4**.
- **The Friday, April 5 schedule will be exchanged with the Sunday, April 7 schedule**.

Graduate training units should organize teaching and notify students accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
