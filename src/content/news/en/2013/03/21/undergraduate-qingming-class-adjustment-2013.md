In accordance with Shantou University’s Qingming Festival holiday schedule, the Academic Affairs Office has adjusted classes for undergraduate students at the main campus as follows:

- **Classes are suspended on Thursday, April 4**.
- **The Friday, April 5 schedule will be exchanged with the Sunday, April 7 schedule**.

Teaching units should organize undergraduate classes and notify students accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
