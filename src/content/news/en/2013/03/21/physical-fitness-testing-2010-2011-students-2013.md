Shantou University’s Physical Education Department will administer the National Student Physical Health Standard tests to **all students in the 2010 and 2011 cohorts at the main campus**. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan students, international students, and students whose incomplete identity information prevents data reporting are temporarily excluded from this round under the notice.

The six tests are height, weight, vital capacity, standing long jump, grip strength, and a 1,000-meter run for men or 800-meter run for women. Students enrolled in physical education this term will receive their testing times from their instructors. Students not enrolled in physical education this term will take the tests on **May 4 and 5, 2013**; their specific time slots are listed in the attachment. Testing will take place in the physical-fitness testing room beneath the athletics stadium stands and on the track.

Students will complete height, weight, vital capacity and grip-strength tests in the testing room, followed by the standing long jump and distance run on the track. Missing any of the six data points invalidates the test. Students must attend at their assigned time, wear sports clothing and shoes, warm up beforehand, and confirm with the tester that all six results have been recorded.

Students unable to take the required tests for health reasons should follow the university’s exemption procedure and submit the application and supporting documentation to the designated mailbox at the gymnasium office by **May 12**.
