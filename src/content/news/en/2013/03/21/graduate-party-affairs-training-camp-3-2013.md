The Party General Branch of Shantou University’s Graduate School will hold its **third graduate party-affairs training camp from March 23 to 24**. The camp is intended for graduate student party-branch secretaries, deputy secretaries, organization committee members and publicity committee members. It is designed to strengthen participants’ theoretical knowledge and administrative skills and promote standardized party-affairs work.

### Schedule

| Time | Venue | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| March 23, 9:00–9:20 a.m. | D203 | Opening ceremony |
| March 23, 9:30–10:30 a.m. | D203 | Introduction to graduate-party-member development procedures, led by Wang Fengqi |
| March 23, 10:50 a.m.–noon | D203 | Branch reports and discussion of branch activities, member development and education |
| March 23, 2:30–5:30 p.m. | Not listed | Outdoor team training |
| March 24, 9:00–10:30 a.m. | E202-2 | Screening of video materials including *Loyalty and Betrayal* |
| March 24, 10:40 a.m.–noon | E202-2 | Group discussion on effective mechanisms for member development |
| March 24, 3:00–4:30 p.m. | E202-2 | Group reports, sharing and comments |
| March 24, 4:30–5:30 p.m. | E202-2 | Graduate party-affairs lecture, led by Lin Dongyue |
| March 24, 5:30–5:50 p.m. | E202-2 | Closing ceremony |

Participants are expected to attend on time, follow the sign-in and leave procedures, keep phones on silent or switched off, and take notes throughout the camp.
