Shantou University’s Party Committee has issued a notice on implementing the central eight-point rules. The notice said that the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection circulated **six typical cases of violations on March 19, 2013**, emphasizing the need to strengthen discipline and ensure implementation.

The university asks every unit and department to recognize the importance of the rules, avoid a wait-and-see attitude or attempts to merely “wait out the scrutiny,” and take active responsibility for implementation. Units should learn from the reported cases, practice thrift, strictly observe integrity requirements for public officials, and reject ostentation, comparison of extravagance and wasteful spending.

Leaders at every level are expected to lead by example and ensure that the requirements are implemented throughout the organization.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Party of Shantou University Committee)
