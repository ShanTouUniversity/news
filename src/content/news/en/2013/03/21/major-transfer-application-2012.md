It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on March 21, 2013, regarding applications for major transfers for undergraduates of the Class of 2012, in accordance with the *Interim Regulations on the Credit System for Undergraduates of Shantou University* (STU Document [2005] No. 67).

The notice announces quotas for receiving students from other majors, eligibility, and the schedule for registration and assessment.

## Procedures

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Undergraduates of the Class of 2012 who wish to transfer |
| How to apply | Complete the *Application Form for Major Transfer* (Attachment 1), obtain a transcript from the academic officer of your current department, obtain signatures from the department head and dean, then register at the department of the intended major and attend the assessment at the announced time |
| Department review | Receiving departments will submit assessment results together with the *Application Form* and the *Course Plan for Transfer Students* to the Academic Affairs Office in week 12 |
| University review and publicity | The Academic Affairs Office will review the materials, publicize the proposed list and announce the final decision in week 14 |
| Effective term | Approved students will transfer to the new major from the summer 2013 semester |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Quotas, eligibility, registration and assessment schedules for each major are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The quotas and application forms are available on the OA system.
