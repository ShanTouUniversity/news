Shantou University’s AIESEC team has opened recruitment for summer overseas volunteers. Projects address social issues including cultural exchange, HIV prevention, community or rural development, environmental protection, language education, microfinance and women’s rights. Volunteers may choose projects in countries and regions where AIESEC operates, take part in **six- to eight-week** overseas service experiences, and work with local AIESEC members on project implementation and team collaboration.

Applicants should be committed to public service, respectful of cultural diversity, willing to learn proactively, and able to communicate in basic English. Applications close at **11:30 p.m. on March 24**. Applicants may email Chinese and English résumés to `aiesecstuvolunteer@gmail.com` or complete the registration form and send it to the same address.

AIESEC will also hold a returnee sharing session from **8:00 to 10:00 p.m. on March 23** at the Coffee Bookshop. Volunteers who worked in India and Ukraine will discuss their overseas work, cultural experiences and cross-cultural adjustment, followed by a question-and-answer session.

For more information, follow the “AIESEC STU” official Weibo account or its [official blog](http://blog.sina.com.cn/s/profile_2566597763.html).
