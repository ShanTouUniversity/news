It is reported that, under its campus development plan, Shantou University will build two new student residential colleges on the west campus. According to the project preparation schedule, construction will begin before the end of 2013, and six groups of existing buildings — the 60-unit and 72-unit blocks, the 24-unit and 11-unit blocks, and buildings C8 and C9 — must be demolished before the end of August.

To arrange resettlement properly and fully hear residents' opinions and suggestions, the university will hold two forums, both on the third floor of the Labor Union building. The first is set for 5:00 p.m. on March 22 (Friday) for residents of the 60-unit and 72-unit blocks; the second takes place at 5:00 p.m. on March 25 (Monday) for residents of the 24-unit and 11-unit blocks as well as buildings C8 and C9. Faculty members unable to attend their assigned session may join the other one.
