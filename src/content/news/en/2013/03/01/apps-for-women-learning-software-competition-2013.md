Supported by the Li Ka-shing Foundation China Women’s Project and hosted by the Women’s Studies Center of Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts, the **Apps for Women Learning Shantou University Student Mobile-Software Design Competition** will run at STU from **March through June 2013**. The competition encourages students to use their expertise to serve rural women and communities. It is expected to produce about 50 tablet or mobile-phone applications related to rural women’s daily lives, work, and learning, with outstanding works potentially deployed for use by rural women.

### Competition areas

Current undergraduate or graduate students may form interdisciplinary teams or teams with students from other universities. Each team should have 3–6 members. Design areas include:

- **Information and news**: tools that help users learn or receive social information, such as natural-disaster alerts and electronic books on dispute mediation;
- **Village administration**: tools for surveys, data statistics, office work, reminders, contact directories, and holiday greetings;
- **Interactive learning**: tools supporting learning, discussion, communication, and information sharing among rural women;
- **Other areas**: applications that support the comprehensive development of rural women.

### Schedule and awards

| Date | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| March 20 and 22 | First and second competition briefings |
| March 20–April 17 | Complete registration and submit software proposals |
| April 17–24 | Reviewers select 15 outstanding proposals for encouragement awards |
| April 24–June 5 | Teams develop and submit software; tablets may be borrowed upon request |
| June 5–12 | Final software review |
| June 14 | Awards ceremony and exhibition of outstanding works |

Each of the 15 finalist teams will receive an RMB 500 finalist award. The Technology Implementation, Cross-University Cooperation, and Visual Design awards each carry RMB 2,000, while the Overall Excellence Award carries RMB 3,000.

Download the registration form at `http://cws.stu.edu.cn` and email registration materials and proposals to `cws@stu.edu.cn`. Questions may also be directed to that email address or website.
