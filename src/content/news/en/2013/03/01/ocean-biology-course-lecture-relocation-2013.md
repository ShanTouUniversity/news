The **periods 6–7 session on the afternoon of March 4** for Shantou University’s *Ocean Biology* course will be replaced by the Antarctic Expedition Project lecture **“World Biogeography and Its Relevance to South China.”**

The lecture runs from **2:00 to 4:00 p.m.** in the New Library Lecture Hall. Students enrolled in the course should inform one another and attend according to the revised arrangement.
