Shantou University’s cooperation programs with the University of Northampton have begun. In fall 2013, Northampton will receive STU students for its “3+1+1” dual-degree and bachelor’s-to-master’s program and tuition-reduction exchange program.

To answer student questions, a representative of the University of Northampton China Office will hold a program briefing at STU from **3:00 to 4:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 13**, in **Room 207, Building D**. Because of a change to the guest’s flight, the briefing has been moved one hour earlier than originally scheduled. Students who wish to attend should book online in advance at <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm>.

The briefing will cover program formats, application requirements, tuition reductions, and arrangements for study in the United Kingdom. Consult the previously published program notice and the original OA attachment for complete information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
