It is reported that applications for the Ministry of Civil Affairs' 2013 research projects on social work and volunteer service have opened. Shantou University's Research Office recently forwarded the application requirements to relevant campus units and is organizing researchers to take part. The projects are open to researchers directly engaged in related work who possess the foundation needed to undertake major research projects.

## Eligibility

According to the arrangements, applicants must choose topics based on the research tasks listed in the Research Guide for the 2013 Projects. Experts who previously undertook Ministry of Civil Affairs projects on social work or volunteer service that remain unfinished may not serve as principal investigators this time. Joint applications by universities, research institutions, local civil affairs departments, and practice organizations are encouraged.

## Timeline and Requirements for Research

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Completion | Reports submitted by August 20, 2013 |
| Ownership | Results jointly owned by the Ministry's Social Work Department and the authors; publication requires consent |
| Methods | Combined quantitative and qualitative approaches |
| Length | Generally no fewer than 30,000 words (excluding appendices or supporting materials) |
| Policy recommendations | At least one-fifth of the full text |
| Funding | Generally no more than 30,000 yuan |

Winning projects will sign a Research Task Agreement with the Ministry's Social Work Department and receive funding support. Projects that fail to complete their research or do not pass acceptance review will not be concluded.

## Internal Schedule at STU

Per the Research Office's requirements, applicants must complete the project application form and the research justification document, submitting drafts to the office by March 15 for formal review, followed by four qualified paper copies plus electronic versions by March 20.
