A total of **151 projects** were approved and received university funding under Shantou University’s 2012 student extracurricular academic and technology project program. To review project completion and assess program performance, the University Student Extracurricular Academic and Technology Activities Leading Group has asked project leaders and relevant units to complete the wrap-up process.

Key deadlines are:

- Project leaders must submit completion reports under their school’s requirements. Schools will consolidate the reports, complete the project summary form, and file it with the university.
- Schools must complete final reviews and submit results to the university Youth League Committee by **March 11**.
- Schools must complete project expense reimbursement by **April 1**.
- School Youth League committees must prepare detailed expenditure statements and performance-evaluation reports and submit the 2012 program summary report to the university Youth League Office by **April 5**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
>
> ⚠️ The project summary form and other attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
