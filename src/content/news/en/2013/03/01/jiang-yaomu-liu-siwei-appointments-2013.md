Following deliberation at the second 2013 meeting of the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee, the university appointed:

- **Jiang Yaomu** as head of the Party Organization, Publicity, and United Front Department of Shantou University Medical College, with a six-month probationary period;
- **Liu Siwei** as Party branch secretary, at deputy-division level, of the Shantou International Eye Center jointly established by Shantou University and The Chinese University of Hong Kong, also with a six-month probationary period.

The appointment notice was also copied to the Organization Department of the Communist Party Committee of Shantou and the Shantou Municipal Human Resources Bureau.
