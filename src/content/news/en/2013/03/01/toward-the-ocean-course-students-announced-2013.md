Shantou University’s School of Science has announced the roster for the 2012–2013 spring-semester *Toward the Ocean* public-service course. After adding 20 places to the original plan of 60, the course has confirmed **80 students**, with some applicants placed on a waitlist.

Selected students should check the class schedule in the credit-based academic system. The notice states that theory study will begin on **Friday afternoon of Week 3**. The detailed schedule attachment lists theory classes on Wednesday periods 6 and 7 in Room D304 during Weeks 3–9, followed by a summary and experience-sharing session in Week 10. Practical activities are scheduled for weekends in Weeks 5, 8, and 9, with locations to be announced. Students should follow the latest schedule in the academic system and course-group notices because the notice text and detailed attachment differ on the first class session.

Practical work includes on-campus photography or surveys, public-welfare photography, and service activities at a primary school or the Cunxin Shantang factory. The course team also announced plans for a summer *Toward the Ocean* special class with The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, expected to select 20 STU students and 20 PolyU students for joint study and public-service practice.
