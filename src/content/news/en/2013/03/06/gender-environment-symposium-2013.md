Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts and Center for Women’s Studies will host a symposium on **“Gender and the Environment” from 2:00 to 5:30 p.m. on March 8** in ACC Room 3. Faculty and students across the university are welcome.

Three scholars will give keynote presentations:

- Dr. Judith Shapiro of the School of International Service at American University: **“China’s Environmental Challenges: A Special Role for Women”**;
- Professor Tih-Fen Ting of the University of Illinois: **“Seeking Rachel Carson in China: Environmental Contamination and Health”**;
- Researcher Zhao Qun of the Yunnan Academy of Social Sciences: **“Gender in Environmental Issues.”**
