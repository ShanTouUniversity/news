Shantou University has published provisional *Management Measures for Undergraduate Exchanges with Domestic Universities*. Undergraduates may gain a second-campus experience and access teaching resources and campus culture at partner universities in mainland China. Exchanges may last for one spring or fall semester or one academic year.

### Ningbo University exchange plan

STU has signed an undergraduate exchange agreement with Ningbo University, with exchange education scheduled to begin in the **fall 2013 semester**. Each academic school must propose the year levels and number of students that each program can receive based on its teaching resources and faculty capacity. Because the two universities are expected to send roughly equal numbers of students, receiving quotas should generally correspond to the number sent out. Schools must complete the *Domestic Exchange Student Receiving Plan* form, submit the paper copy to the Teaching Office’s Student Affairs Section by **April 1**, and email the electronic copy to `yd@stu.edu.cn`. For questions, call `86502402`.

### Eligibility and selection

Applicants must be full-time STU undergraduates in their second year or above, with good conduct, no disciplinary record, strong learning ability and overall qualities, good academic performance without failed courses, and good physical and mental health.

After the Teaching Office publishes the partner universities’ programs and exchange quotas, each academic school organizes applications and conducts a comprehensive assessment. Schools select and publicize a preliminary list before submitting it to the Teaching Office for review and approval. A student will generally be eligible for only one exchange during their time at STU.

During the exchange, students remain registered at STU with their domestic-exchange status recorded. They must comply with the laws and rules of both universities and follow the host university’s administration. They must return to STU and register on schedule when the exchange ends. Course selection and credit recognition follow the Teaching Office’s relevant procedures. Students may not apply for overseas study during the exchange and should purchase accident insurance.

Students continue to pay tuition according to STU’s program-based rates. The host university collects accommodation fees according to its own standards, while students cover transportation and living expenses. Students generally receive only one exchange opportunity and must complete the required return and registration procedures after the exchange.

### Incoming students

STU will process incoming exchange students under its new-student admission procedures, issue admission notices, campus cards, and temporary student cards, assign student numbers, and enter the relevant information into the credit-based academic system. After the exchange, STU will issue a “second-campus experience certificate” and transcript. Except for participation in STU awards and honors, incoming exchange students are generally managed and educated under the same arrangements as STU students.
