The 11th China selection for the Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition was held in Beijing from February 25 to 28. The Shantou University School of Law team placed **fifth overall** among 38 teams and won a **national first prize**. It also received the Best Performance Award, the Best Applicant Memorial Award, and the Best Respondent Memorial Award, for four team and individual honors in total.

Organized by the International Law Students Association, the Jessup Moot is an international English-language legal advocacy competition for law students. This year’s China selection included teams from leading law schools such as Peking University, Renmin University of China, China University of Political Science and Law, and Xiamen University.

Following the team’s performance, the China and international organizing committees invited the STU team to attend the international preliminary event in Washington, D.C., as observers, where it could watch leading international teams and take part in academic exchange.
