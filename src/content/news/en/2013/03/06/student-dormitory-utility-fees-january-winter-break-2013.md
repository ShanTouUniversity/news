Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has released utility charges for student dormitories for January 2013 and the winter break and has arranged online viewing, discrepancy reviews, and payment.

### Metering and review

The January metering period was **January 1–23**. Under the winter-break residence arrangements, meters were read on **February 7 and February 22**. Students may view the relevant information through STU’s utility-management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/>.

Students who find an error or have questions may complete and sign a utility-charge review form and submit it during working hours from **March 7 to 13** to the Water and Electricity Center beside the Science and Technology Building. The center’s telephone number is `2489`. Students whose occupancy information does not match the actual residence list should contact the counselor’s office in Building B, Room 106, at `3355`.

If no objection is raised before the review period ends, the university will begin collecting the charges through campus cards on **March 14**. The regular payment period is **March 14–18**; unpaid charges after the deadline will be handled under university rules on **March 19**. A discrepancy raised after the review period that requires correction after verification generally takes three to five working days to process. Students should top up campus cards with insufficient balances in advance.
