The Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and Department of Finance have opened the second round of applications for the **2013 provincial science and technology plan**. Shantou University’s Research Office is organizing internal applications. The round covers the provincial strategic emerging-industry core-technology program, the provincial strategic emerging-industry LED program, and the numerical-control-generation mechanical-product innovation and application demonstration program.

### Application priorities

Projects must align with national and Guangdong 12th Five-Year science and technology plans and the relevant program guidelines. Priority will go to projects expected to produce substantial patent results during implementation, especially invention patents or international PCT patents. Applicants must commit to provide matching funds. Enterprise applicants must also cover any shortfall if the requested government funding is not fully approved so that total project investment remains unchanged.

A principal investigator with more than two ongoing provincial science-plan projects is generally ineligible for a new application, and each principal investigator may generally submit only one new project in 2013. Applications may fail eligibility review if required financial statements, cooperation agreements, matching-fund commitments, novelty-search reports, or other important attachments are missing; if the same project is submitted more than once under changed titles; or if its principal content has already received provincial science-plan approval.

Applicants must use the Guangdong science and technology administration system at <http://pro.gdstc.gov.cn> to submit the application, feasibility report, and supporting materials. Existing accounts remain valid. First-time applicants should email their name, institution, and project category to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn` to request an account and password.

### Deadlines and materials

- **March 18**: Send an electronic draft of the application to `yczhu@stu.edu.cn` for preliminary review;
- **March 27**: Complete online submission of the application and attachments and notify the Research Office for review;
- **April 5**: Upload revisions responding to review comments;
- **April 9**: Submit two paper copies of the approved application, matching the online version. Joint applications must bear the partner institution’s official seal.

Typical attachments include institutional registration and organization-code certificates, partner certificates, cooperation agreements, matching-fund commitments, patent or appraisal certificates, novelty-search documentation, evidence of research capacity, and testing reports. Applicants should follow the specific program guidelines.

See the [Guangdong science-plan notice](http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/13623894998497457137889690597072.html), [LED program guidelines](http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/kjyw/tzgg/1362389125910-5795628166526515018.html), and [numerical-control-generation guidelines](http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/kjyw/tzgg/13623891250283537929428458735105.html). Contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at `86502813`.
