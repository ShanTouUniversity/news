Following review at the second 2013 university leaders’ meeting on March 5, Shantou University has appointed **Su Shaohui** deputy director of Finance for a **six-month probationary period**.

Su has worked in STU’s Finance Office for many years. He has served as head of the General Finance Section and the Fund Operations Section and was previously an assistant to the director of Finance. He holds a senior accountant qualification and a master’s degree in business administration.
