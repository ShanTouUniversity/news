The first 2013 academic lecture of the Shantou University Business School will be held from **10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Monday, March 11**, in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of Wenxi. Dr. Chi Qishui, an associate professor at Beijing Institute of Petrochemical Technology, will present **“The Impact of Structural Changes in Money Supply on Prices.”** Professor Du Mian will chair the lecture, which is open to the university community.

The presentation will examine how changes in the structure of the money supply affect prices. Using monthly data from December 1999 to April 2012, Chi will test structural changes in China’s money supply and combine the CGPI price index with Fisher’s exact test to analyze their effects. The study finds that different levels of money supply are piecewise stationary and uses eight endogenous breakpoints to assess price trends, offering another approach to understanding money-supply effects on prices and evaluating monetary-policy effectiveness.

Chi Qishui is an associate professor at Beijing Institute of Petrochemical Technology. He holds a doctoral degree in economics from Central University of Finance and Economics and previously conducted visiting research in finance at the State University of New York at Buffalo. His teaching includes macroeconomics and public finance.
