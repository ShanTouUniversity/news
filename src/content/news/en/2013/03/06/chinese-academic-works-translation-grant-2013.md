Applications are open for the 2013 National Social Science Fund Chinese academic translation program. The program primarily supports the translation of outstanding works by Chinese scholars that have been published in China and their publication by authoritative overseas presses. Works written in a foreign language for which more than 60 percent of the content is complete but that have not yet been published may also apply.

### Funding and eligibility

The program currently supports English, French, Spanish, Russian, German, Japanese, Korean, and Arabic. Eligible works include outstanding results of National Social Science Fund projects, outstanding contemporary Chinese philosophy and social-science research, and academically or culturally significant Chinese philosophy and social-science works produced since the 20th century. Priority areas include Marxism and the theoretical system of socialism with Chinese characteristics, China’s development path and experience, contemporary Chinese economic, political, cultural, legal, and social issues, traditional Chinese culture, and other works suitable for international presentation.

Funding is approximately RMB 100,000 per 100,000 Chinese characters. The manuscript generally may not exceed 300,000 characters, and total funding is capped at RMB 500,000. Applicants should request funding according to translation and overseas-publishing costs.

Eligible applicants include researchers with strong subject expertise and bilingual writing skills, Chinese research institutions with close academic ties to overseas institutions, and domestic publishers with experience in international co-publication. Researchers and research institutions may apply independently or jointly with overseas researchers or institutions, but the domestic applicant must be named project leader. Domestic publishers must apply independently. Current National Social Science Fund project leaders, works already published overseas, and works already funded by designated Chinese international-promotion programs are ineligible.

### Application requirements

A project may be a single monograph or a series. It must be published independently by an authoritative overseas press or jointly by Chinese and overseas presses and distributed through mainstream overseas channels. Translations must remain faithful to the original while following the language conventions of overseas readers. Works should generally contain at least 100,000 Chinese characters; works longer than 300,000 characters should be appropriately revised and shortened. Published works must state that they were funded by the “Chinese Fund for the Humanities and Social Sciences.”

Applicants must sign a publishing contract with an authoritative overseas press before applying and resolve copyright issues. Applicants who are not the original author or publisher must attach authorization from the author and original publisher. Required materials include the application form, original work, translation or a translation sample of at least 20,000 characters from core chapters, copyright documentation, the overseas publishing contract, and legal documentation for the overseas publisher. Printed materials and electronic copies must be submitted in the required format.

The program plans centralized reviews in **June and November**. First-round materials are due to the Research Office by **March 21**, with an electronic draft of the application due to `yczhu@stu.edu.cn` by **March 18**. The second-round deadlines are **August 21** for complete materials and **August 16** for the electronic draft. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhu Yanchun at `82902563` and `86502563`.
