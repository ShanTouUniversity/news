The 134th seminar in the **Modern Mathematics** series will be held at **3:00 p.m. on March 8** in Mathematics Laboratory Gongbei 412. Dr. Jiang Zhengjun of the United International College of Beijing Normal University and Hong Kong Baptist University will present **“Optimal dividend policy when risk reserves follow a jump-diffusion process under Markov-regime switching.”**

Faculty and students across the university are welcome to attend.
