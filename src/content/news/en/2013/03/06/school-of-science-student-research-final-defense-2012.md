The final defenses for 2012 extracurricular student academic and science projects at Shantou University’s School of Science will begin at **2:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 13**, in the school’s Multimedia Conference Room (Science 352). Faculty and students across the university are welcome to attend.

Project teams must submit completion reports in the required format by **5:00 p.m. on Monday, March 11** and email electronic copies to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn`. The school’s attachments provide the team list and defense order; each team should prepare in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science)
>
> ⚠️ The project list, defense order, and completion-report template are available in the original OA attachments.
