Shantou University’s volunteer-service work received recognition in the selection of outstanding youth volunteer-service groups and individuals organized in February by the Shantou Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Shantou Youth Volunteer Association.

The Dawn Student Aid Team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association was named an **outstanding Shantou youth volunteer-service group**. Five STU students received individual honors: one received the Shantou Youth Volunteer “Golden Phoenix Award,” and four were named outstanding young volunteers. The recipients came from the Medical College, School of Law, and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design.

The University Youth League Committee encouraged the recognized group and students to build on the honor and called on students across STU to promote the volunteer spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress.
