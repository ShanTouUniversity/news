Shantou University’s School of Engineering will hold the 24th World Engineer Forum at **7:30 p.m. on March 22** in the Science and Technology Lecture Hall. The event is open to the university community. A book giveaway by Mr. Wang Guoyu will take place before the lecture, with books distributed on a first-come, first-served basis.

Registration for the session has closed, and the School of Engineering has published the list of successful reservations. Because the list contains personal information, it is not reproduced here; other faculty, staff and students may attend according to the on-site arrangements.
