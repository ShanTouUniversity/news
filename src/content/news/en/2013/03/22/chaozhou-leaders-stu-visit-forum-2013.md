More than 20 representatives from Chaozhou’s government and education, science, technology and health sectors will visit Shantou University and attend an exchange forum on **March 26**. The meeting will begin at **10:10 a.m.** in Meeting Room 3 of the STU Academic Exchange Center, chaired by President Xu Xiaohu.

The agenda includes introductions of the attending leaders and guests, presentations on STU’s profile, academic specialties, research, professional master’s programs and graduate courses, and reports from the university’s five affiliated hospitals. The program will continue with discussion, remarks by a representative of the Li Ka-shing Foundation and speeches by Chaozhou officials, followed by a working lunch.

The General Office asks the relevant schools, departments and affiliated hospitals of the Medical College to send one responsible person each. Units scheduled to speak should keep their remarks to five minutes. Participant names should be sent to `o_xzb@stu.edu.cn` by the end of the morning on **March 25**. The contact is Lin Yang of the General Office at `8650 2305`.
