Shantou University’s Christian Studies Center in the School of Liberal Arts has launched a research fund on “Christianity, Life Education and Religious Culture” and is establishing an academic team for research on religious culture and life education. The initiative aims to build a research platform, support young scholars, advance research on religious culture and whole-person life education, and promote Chaoshan cultural studies.

### Eligibility and research scope

The fund is open to STU faculty and staff, including teaching-support, administrative and retired staff. There are no restrictions on academic degree, discipline or rank, and interdisciplinary proposals are encouraged. Applicants may submit relevant published or unpublished papers or an ongoing research plan for the review committee’s reference. Projects may use methods from the natural sciences, social sciences or humanities to conduct theoretical or empirical research on Christianity, life education or other religious cultures.

Projects will run for **18 months**, with funding capped at **RMB 30,000 per project**. Funding is approved once and paid in two installments: 50% within about one month after the selected projects are announced, and the remaining amount within about one month after the midterm review is approved. Projects must submit a progress report and interim results six months after approval, followed by a completion report and final results.

### Applications and deliverables

Applicants must complete the special research-fund application and submit a curriculum vitae with the application to the Christian Studies Center. A review committee will assess proposals and score project reports anonymously before selecting research-team members and funded projects. Each project must produce at least a **10,000-character academic paper** and publish it at an academic conference or in a formal publication. If the work has not been published at completion, an acceptance notice may be submitted. Published results must acknowledge support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation.
