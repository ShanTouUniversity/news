To improve the student-residence environment, a cleaning company will conduct comprehensive cleaning of the common areas in **residences A, B, C, D, E and F**. Students should remove shoes, shoe racks, buckets, cardboard boxes and other items from corridors in advance or move them to the storage rooms on the top floors of the residences.

Items removed during cleaning will be stored centrally by the Student Community Management Service Center for **one week**. Students who need to reclaim an item should go to the duty room of their residence during that period. Unclaimed items will be treated as abandoned property and removed by the cleaning company after the storage period.

Consult the work schedule in the original OA notice for the detailed cleaning times and residence arrangements.
