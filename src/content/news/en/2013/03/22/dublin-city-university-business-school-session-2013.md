The principal representative of Dublin City University in Ireland will meet Shantou University Business School students at **2:30 p.m. on March 25** in **E Lecture Hall 203**. The representative will introduce DCU exchange-student and joint-training programs.

Business School students are welcome to attend and learn about study and exchange opportunities at Dublin City University.
