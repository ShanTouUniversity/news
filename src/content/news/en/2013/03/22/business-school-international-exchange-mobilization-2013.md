Shantou University’s Business School will hold an international exchange information session at **2:00 p.m. on March 27** in **E Lecture Hall 203**. The session is intended for Business School undergraduates in their second year and above.

The program will include an overview of the school’s recent international exchange projects by a Business School faculty member, an experience-sharing session with students who have studied abroad, and a presentation by faculty member Kong Ying on planning and preparing for overseas study. Eligible students are welcome to attend.
