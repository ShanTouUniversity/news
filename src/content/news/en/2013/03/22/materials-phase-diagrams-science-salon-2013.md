Shantou University’s School of Science will host a Science Salon at **2:40 p.m. on March 26** in the School of Science Lecture Hall (Science 352). Professor Du Yong of the Materials Design and Application Science Center at Central South University’s State Key Laboratory of Powder Metallurgy will speak on “Phase Diagrams, Phase Transitions and Alloy Design.”

The talk will examine how thermodynamic and kinetic databases and quantitative descriptions of microstructural evolution support improved alloy performance and the development of new materials. Professor Du will also discuss research on phase-diagram thermodynamics, diffusion and interfacial reactions, materials-property measurement and simulation, microstructural-evolution modeling, hard alloys and wear-resistant coatings.

Professor Du’s research focuses on phase-diagram thermodynamics, diffusion and interfacial reactions, materials-property measurement and simulation, microstructural evolution and graded-structure coating technology. His work has included projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the national 863 and 973 programs, and international collaborations.
