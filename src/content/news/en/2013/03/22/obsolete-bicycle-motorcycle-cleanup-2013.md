To improve the use of limited bicycle-shelter and parking space, Shantou University will clear **abandoned bicycles and unlicensed, discarded motorcycles** across the campus. The notice was issued jointly by the Security Office, Student Affairs Office, Zhicheng College, Resource Management Office and Graduate School.

The notice says that the detailed cleanup schedule is provided in the original table. Because the schedule is embedded as an unreadable image in the archived page, this article does not speculate about its dates or areas. Vehicle owners should consult the original OA notice for the applicable arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
