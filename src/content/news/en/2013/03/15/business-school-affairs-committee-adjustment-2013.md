STU’s Business School has adjusted the membership of its school-affairs committee in response to organizational and personnel changes, following the procedures in the *Business School School-Affairs Committee Working Regulations*. **Xu Zongling** is chair and **Huang Xianglong** is secretary.

The notice lists Chen Yinglin, Huang Wangming, Huang Yan, Ji Yun, Ouyang Feng, Qin Zhong, Song Xiaobao, Zeng Qiang, and Zhou Xiaoe as committee members, arranged according to the pinyin of their Chinese surnames. One additional member’s name is corrupted in the archived text and has not been reconstructed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School)
