STU’s Research Office has forwarded the Ministry of Science and Technology notice on the **2014 project guidelines for the National Basic Research Program (973 Program) and Major Research Programs** (Guo Ke Fa Ji [2013] No. 53). Faculty interested in applying should prepare applications according to the guidelines and application-writing outline.

The 973 Program, including the Major Research Programs, targets major national needs and supports basic research in nine areas, including agricultural science, as well as major research programs in six directions, including nanoscience. Applications must be submitted online through the National Science and Technology Program Project Application Center. The ministry’s online submission window is **8:00 a.m. on March 15 through 5:00 p.m. on March 25, 2013**; late applications will not be accepted.

STU applicants must submit their materials to the Research Office by **March 22**. Consult the National Science and Technology Program Project Application Center at <http://program.most.gov.cn/> for the guidelines, application-writing outline, and online submission arrangements.
