Oklahoma State University Vice President Lee Bird and Associate Provost Sheryl Tucker will present the university’s graduate programs at STU from **9:00 to 10:00 a.m. on Monday, March 18**. The session will be held in Multipurpose Lecture Hall 103 of the Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center, the Multidisciplinary Building, and is open to students across the university.

The presentation will cover Oklahoma State graduate programs, application procedures, scholarships, and financial aid, followed by a question-and-answer session.

Founded in 1890, Oklahoma State University offers approximately 350 degree programs at the undergraduate and graduate levels and is a public comprehensive research university in the United States.
