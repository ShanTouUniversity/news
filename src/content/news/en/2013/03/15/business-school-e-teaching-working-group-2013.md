STU’s Business School has adjusted the membership of its e-teaching implementation working group in response to organizational and personnel changes. **Xu Zongling** is chair; Ouyang Feng and one member whose name is corrupted in the archived text are deputy chairs; and Chen Xin is secretary.

The listed members are Chen Chaorong, Huang Wangming, Ji Yun, Qin Zhong, Song Xiaobao, Wu Jingqiang, Zeng Fanqi, and Zeng Qiang, arranged according to the pinyin of their Chinese surnames. The corrupted name has not been reconstructed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School)
