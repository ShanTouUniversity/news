STU’s School of Science has completed its final review of 2012 student extracurricular technology projects. The school approved **20 projects**, of which **19 completed their work and participated in the final defense**. The expert panel awarded **two first prizes**, **three second prizes**, and **five third prizes**; another **nine projects** were rated satisfactory.

One project did not attend the final defense and was treated as voluntarily withdrawn. It received an unsatisfactory result, and its project funding will be recovered. Consult the School of Science’s OA attachment for project titles and detailed results.

To encourage scientific exploration and innovation, the school will award **RMB 600 per first-prize project**, **RMB 400 per second-prize project**, and **RMB 200 per third-prize project**, together with certificates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science)
