The STU Youth League Committee and Students’ Union have launched the 17th University Student Debate Competition, **“Keep Moving, Keep Thinking.”** The competition aims to develop students’ critical-thinking abilities and cultural literacy while enriching campus life. Debate-team registration runs from **March 15 to 22**, and debate-topic submissions run from **March 13 to 24**.

### Competition schedule

| Stage | Date | Matches |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Opening ceremony | March 30 | “One STU Talk” 17th Debate Competition opening |
| First preliminary round | April 6 | 2 |
| Second round | April 13–14 | 4 |
| Semifinals | April 20 | 2 |
| Championship and third-place matches | May 4–5 | 2 |

Matches will take place in the Science Lecture Hall and Moot Court. Participants include school-level debate teams and teams that win the campus selection. The competition has three knockout stages, with draws determining matchups and sides. The previous champion and runner-up will enter the first preliminary round directly as seeded teams. Each match lasts 42 minutes, with four starting debaters per team; judges determine the winner based on performance.

Students may register or submit debate topics through the STU Students’ Union Academic Planning Department’s Sina Weibo account, `@STU学术策划部`, and follow that account for submission information.

### Awards

Each match will recognize one Best Debater and one Most Popular Debater. The awards include certificates, and the popularity award also includes a commemorative gift. The competition will recognize a champion, runner-up, and third-place team with certificates, trophies, and commemorative gifts. Three topic-submission winners will receive commemorative gifts. Audience members who ask or answer questions on site may also receive prizes. The Most Popular Debater will be selected through on-site and online voting, which will not affect the Best Debater evaluation.
