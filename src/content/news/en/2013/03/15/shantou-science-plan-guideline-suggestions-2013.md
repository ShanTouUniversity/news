STU’s Research Office is soliciting suggestions from university units for the **2013 Shantou Municipal Science and Technology Plan Project Guidelines**. The solicitation follows Shantou’s science-plan management rules and annual work arrangements. Suggested topics should support industrial upgrading, public services, and social and livelihood-oriented science and technology while balancing technical advancement, practical value, ecological development, and sustainability.

### Suggested directions

Researchers are encouraged to propose topics related to high-tech industries, the upgrading of traditional industries, strategic emerging industries such as LED, science and technology services, and social and livelihood-oriented technology. Suggestions may address key common technologies, frontier technologies, and the coordination of domestic and international innovation resources supporting modern industry and public services. Proposals framed from an industry or sector-wide perspective are encouraged, and proposals jointly submitted by multiple units will receive priority consideration.

Proposers must complete the *2013 Shantou Municipal Science and Technology Plan Project-Guideline Suggestion Form*, covering the proposed topic or project name, technology fields, development stage, implementation content, expected goals, implementation period, and supporting rationale. Submit one paper copy to the Research Office and email the electronic document to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn` by **noon on March 18**. The quality and number of suggestions submitted by each unit will be considered in future evaluations of municipal science-and-technology administration and resource allocation.
