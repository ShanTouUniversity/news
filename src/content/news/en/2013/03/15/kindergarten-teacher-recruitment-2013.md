STU’s kindergarten, under the Resources Management Office, is recruiting **two kindergarten teachers** from the public. One of the positions also includes administrative-assistant duties. Applicants with early-childhood education training and relevant experience are welcome.

### Requirements and duties

Applicants should be hardworking, caring, responsible, and willing to follow work assignments. They must be no older than 40, in good health, and compliant with family-planning regulations; hold at least a secondary vocational qualification in early-childhood education or have completed formal early-childhood teacher training; and possess a kindergarten-teacher qualification certificate and an occupational health certificate. Kindergarten experience is preferred.

Teachers prepare care-and-education plans based on children’s ages and individual differences, conduct classroom activities, manage children’s health and safety, participate in professional learning and teaching research, and communicate with parents. The administrative assistant/teacher also handles administrative, teaching-support, and logistics work; assists with meals, food, drinking water, and environmental hygiene; manages supplies and equipment and teaching materials; supports child health work and accounting; teaches part of the program; and performs assigned duty shifts.

### Employment and application

The positions are auxiliary contract positions; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation follows the university’s applicable standards. Applicants must submit a résumé and copies of their diploma, kindergarten-teacher certificate, health certificate, professional-training certificate, family-planning certificate, identity card, and other relevant credentials by **5:00 p.m. on March 24**. Materials may be emailed or mailed to the STU kindergarten. Shortlisted applicants will be contacted for interviews and must present originals for verification.

Mailing address: STU Resources Management Office Kindergarten, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong, postal code `515063`. Email `o_yey@stu.edu.cn`; telephone `0754-82902088`. Mark the subject or envelope for the kindergarten-teacher or administrative-assistant/teacher position.
