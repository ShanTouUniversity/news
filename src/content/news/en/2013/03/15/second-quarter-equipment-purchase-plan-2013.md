STU’s Equipment and Materials Management Office has opened the application process for the university’s **second-quarter 2013 equipment-purchase plan**. Under STU’s provisional government-procurement measures, each unit must prepare its plan and submit paper and electronic materials by **April 15**. Late submissions will be deferred to the third quarter of 2013.

### Submission requirements

- Plans totaling **RMB 20,000 or less** require the plan form;
- Plans totaling **more than RMB 20,000** require both the plan form and plan document and must follow the applicable funding-approval process;
- A plan including equipment priced at **more than RMB 100,000 per item** also requires a feasibility report for large, precision, or high-value instruments;
- Computer purchases require a computer-purchase registration form; air-conditioner purchases must state the installation location and condition of existing equipment in the remarks field;
- Materials must include the government-procurement catalogue code, equipment name, model, specifications, and technical parameters. Budgets should include taxes, transport, warranty, and after-sales service, and specifications should avoid designating a brand wherever possible.

The Equipment and Materials Management Office is located in the former Reception Room. Contact `86502558`, Ms. Huang, or `zxhuang@stu.edu.cn`.
