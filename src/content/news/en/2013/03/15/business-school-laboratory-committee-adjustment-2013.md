STU’s Business School has adjusted its laboratory working committee in response to organizational and personnel changes. **Ouyang Feng** is chair and **Chen Xin** is secretary.

The listed committee members are Chen Yinglin, Huang Wangming, Ji Yun, Lin Jiali, Qin Zhong, Song Xiaobao, Zeng Fanqi, and Zeng Qiang, arranged according to the pinyin of their Chinese surnames.
