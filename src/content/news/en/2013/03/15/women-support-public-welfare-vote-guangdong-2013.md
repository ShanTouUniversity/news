The **Public Service for a Happy Guangdong: Women-Support Plan**, jointly funded by the Guangdong Provincial People’s Government and the Li Ka-shing Foundation and implemented by the Guangdong Women and Children’s Affairs Committee, is holding a public vote. The plan supports community services and women’s development through public participation and social organizations. A total of **779 women-support public-service projects** across Guangdong are participating, including **29 shortlisted projects from the Chaoshan region**.

Voting runs from **March 8 through 22**. Voters may visit <http://care.gdwomen.org.cn>, register with a mobile number, browse projects, and click to vote. They may also call the free voting hotline at `400-1096-028` and enter a six-digit project application number following the voice prompts. Project pages can be shared on Weibo, and voters can follow the “Jisi Public Service Supports Women” accounts.
