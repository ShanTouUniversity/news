STU’s Party Organization and United Front Department has forwarded the Guangdong Provincial Organization Department’s notice, Yue Zu Tong [2012] No. 96, relaying five Central Organization Department documents on long-term mechanisms for the initiative to excel, including the system for Party members to make and fulfill commitments. The notice is addressed to all Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches and asks them to implement the documents in light of local conditions.

Units should strengthen organizational leadership for the long-term mechanisms, expand publicity, and promptly summarize and promote effective practices and experience. Related materials should be submitted to the university Party Organization and United Front Department at Administration Center 315 or `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Department)
