STU’s J-School has opened applications for its summer 2013 Hong Kong internship program. The school plans to send high-performing third-year students to Hong Kong media organizations for approximately **seven weeks**, from **early July through late August**. Final participants will be selected through applications and interviews and announced in ranking order; places may be adjusted or offered to alternates if selected students cannot participate.

Applicants must speak fluent Cantonese and have strong English skills. The school will arrange accommodation management and work guidance during the internship, while students cover their own living expenses. Students who apply for the Taiwan internship may not apply for the Hong Kong internship at the same time.

Interested students must submit their application materials by **4:00 p.m. on March 18**. The school will organize interviews through a selection committee and announce the interview time separately. The application form asks about preferred media type, language ability, academic ranking, practical experience, software and equipment skills, and work samples; consult the original school form for details.

Contact Professor Guo Zesheng for further information.
