It is reported that the Business School issued Document Shan Da Shang Fa [2013] No. 04 on March 15, 2013, announcing an adjustment to the membership of its Teaching Committee in accordance with the relevant provisions and procedures of the *Business School Teaching Committee Working Regulations*, due to organizational and personnel changes.

## Adjusted Membership

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Huang Yan |
| Members (listed by pinyin order of Chinese surnames) | Huang Wangming, Ji Yun, Li Yue, Lin Quan, Long Yue'e, Ouyang Feng, Qin Zhong, Song Xiaobao, Wang * , Xu Zongling, Zeng Qiang |
| Secretaries | Yang Min, Yin Xiaoqing |

*Note: The given name of the member surnamed Wang is garbled in the original OA document and is therefore rendered vaguely here.

The document was copied to the Academic Affairs Office.
