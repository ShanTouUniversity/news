STU’s Research Office has forwarded the Guangdong Education Department’s call for proposed topics for the **2013 Guangdong Higher-Education Ideological and Political Education Project Guidelines**. The university is collecting suggestions from relevant units to support preparation of the annual guidelines.

Topics should reflect policies on improving higher-education quality and practice-based education, offer broad guidance and academic value, and address real problems in improving the relevance and effectiveness of ideological and political education. They should connect with the development of Marxist theory as a discipline and current research frontiers, demonstrate originality or an exploratory character, and avoid duplicating previous topics. Units should organize expert discussions to refine proposals and ensure that they are scientific, forward-looking, useful, and feasible under existing conditions and within the prescribed period.

The call covers higher-education ideological and political theory teaching, ideological and political education and student services, counselor-team development, publicity and ideological work, career guidance, student financial aid, and campus safety and stability.

Units must complete the *2013 Guangdong Higher-Education Ideological and Political Education Project Topic-Suggestion Summary Form* and email it to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` by **April 10**. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun, `82902563`.
