STU will hold its first counselor meeting of 2013 at **10:30 a.m. on Monday, March 18** in Multipurpose Lecture Hall 103 of the Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center, the Multidisciplinary Building. Oklahoma State University Vice President Lee Bird will give a lecture titled **“Overview of Core Mission and Best Practices of American Student Affairs.”**

Secretaries of the university’s schools and colleges, all counselors, and staff of the Student Affairs Office and Youth League Committee should make the necessary arrangements and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
