The STU Youth League Committee and STU’s “Chai—Path to Success” website will hold the sixth Internet Culture Festival and seventh esports competition from **March 15 through 31**. The festival is open to faculty and students and includes esports, a Weibo writing contest, an annual-keyword selection, and cybersecurity outreach.

### Esports competition

The esports competition includes **League of Legends (LOL)** and **Counter-Strike 1.6** team events. Registration runs from **March 15 to 20**, preliminary matches are scheduled for **March 23–24**, and the semifinals and final will be held on **March 31**, followed by the awards ceremony that evening. Both LOL and CS use five-versus-five team formats. Maps, servers, bans, disconnection procedures, and referee decisions follow the attached competition rules.

Team awards include RMB 200 and five mice for first place, RMB 150 for second place, and RMB 100 for third place, with certificates for the winning teams. Recreational competitions include ranking events for Fruit Ninja and Temple Run, with prizes such as wireless keyboard-and-mouse sets, wireless mice, and laptop sleeves. The “Education Committee of the Celestial Empire” quiz uses a challenge format with 10 randomly selected questions; participants who complete it receive a small gift.

### Online activities

The Weibo writing contest asks participants to post a short description of a close friend under the theme **“Best Mischievous Friend,”** mentioning the friend and the official activity account. The main notice gives the submission and repost-evaluation period as **March 13–28 until 9:00 p.m.**, while the attached schedule gives **March 15–28**; both starting dates are retained. First place receives RMB 50, a 16G USB drive, and a certificate; second place receives RMB 30, a 16G USB drive, and a certificate; third place receives a 16G USB drive and a certificate. Awards will be presented on March 31.

The **2012 STU Annual Keywords** activity will collect keywords at the Golden Triangle on **March 17**, select 10 candidates on March 18, submit them to the Youth League Committee for approval on March 19, and hold a Weibo vote from **March 20 to 27**. The five keywords with the most votes will become the university’s annual keywords, with results announced on March 28. One lucky participant from the on-site collection and one from the Weibo vote will each be drawn on March 31 to receive a 16G USB drive.

Cybersecurity outreach will take place at the Golden Triangle on **March 17**. The main notice lists follow-up display-board publicity as **March 18–29**, while the attachment lists **March 18–27**; follow on-site announcements for the final arrangement.
