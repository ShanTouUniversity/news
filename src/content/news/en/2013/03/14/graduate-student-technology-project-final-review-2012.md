STU’s Graduate School has completed the final review of the university’s **2012 graduate student extracurricular technology projects**. **Twenty-nine projects underwent final review**: **28 passed**, and **one received approval for an extension**. **Four additional projects were abandoned before completion**. The attached Graduate School materials contain the detailed project results.

Project leaders whose projects passed review must visit the Graduate School in New Administration Center 401 on **March 25 or 26** to collect their funding books and complete reimbursement procedures. No late processing will be accepted. Leaders should prepare and organize receipts in advance, with the project leader and the actual user signing the back of each invoice.

### Funding and reimbursement

- Social-science survey-report and academic-paper projects receive RMB 200 in start-up funding and a proposed RMB 300 in additional funding;
- Invention-and-production and entrepreneurship-plan projects receive RMB 500 in start-up funding and a proposed RMB 500 in additional funding;
- Funding is released in midterm and completion stages after the corresponding reviews are passed;
- Social-science survey-report and academic-paper projects process reimbursement on **March 25**, while invention-and-production projects process reimbursement on **March 26**. Field-research projects must provide round-trip travel itineraries; otherwise, travel reimbursement will not be processed.
