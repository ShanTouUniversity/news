To advance undergraduate education and program development, STU’s Business School has established five program-director positions for business administration, marketing, accounting, international economics and trade, and finance. The school will no longer maintain the previous program teaching-research-section director positions.

Following review by the School Affairs Committee, the appointments are:

| Program | Director |
| --- | --- |
| Business Administration | Zeng Qiang |
| Marketing | Huang Wangming |
| Accounting | Song Xiaobao |
| International Economics and Trade | Qin Zhong |
| Finance | Ji Yun |

The directors will perform their duties under the *Trial Responsibilities for Business School Program Directors*, with a **30 percent reduction in teaching workload**.
