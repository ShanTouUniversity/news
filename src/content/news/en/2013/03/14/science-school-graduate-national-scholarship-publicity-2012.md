Following the Graduate School’s arrangements for the 2012 graduate national-scholarship review, the School of Science’s review committee has completed its assessment and proposed **10 master’s students and two doctoral students** for the 2012 national graduate scholarship. The proposed candidates are being publicized for review.

Comments should be submitted in writing to the School of Science review committee by **5:00 p.m. on March 18**. The receiving office is the Graduate Academic Affairs Office, Science 353. Contact `86503011` or `weiwang@stu.edu.cn`. Submissions from organizations must carry an official seal, while individual submissions must include the writer’s real name.
