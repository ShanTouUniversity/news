It is reported that, as female faculty and staff celebrated International Women's Day on March 8, the results of the 2012 national commendation for advanced collectives and individuals in women's workplace achievement were announced. Professor Xu Zongling of Shantou University was awarded the honorary title of "National Women's Meritorious Service Pacesetter."

The award recognizes outstanding individuals and collectives who have made remarkable contributions in their workplaces. This honor affirms Professor Xu's dedication to teaching, research, and her professional duties.
