Shantou University’s School of Science will hold a Science Salon lecture from **9:00 to 10:30 a.m. on Monday, March 25** in the School of Science Lecture Hall (Science 352). The topic is **“H2 Evolution by [FeFe]-H2ase Mimics: Reactivities of [HFeFe] Hydrides toward Protons.”** Faculty and students are welcome.

The speaker, Dr. Wang Wenguang, is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. His research covers the design, synthesis, and evaluation of hydrogen-production catalysts based on nonprecious-metal complexes; electron transfer and energy transfer in photocatalytic hydrogen-production and carbon-dioxide-reduction systems; and photochemical reactions of metal-hydride complexes. He has published more than 10 papers in international chemistry journals including the Journal of the American Chemical Society and Chemistry—An Asian Journal.

Wang graduated from Wuhan University’s School of Chemistry and Molecular Sciences in 2005 and received his doctorate from the Institute of Physical Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2010. He has conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Illinois since then.
