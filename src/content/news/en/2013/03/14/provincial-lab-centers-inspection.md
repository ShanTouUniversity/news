It is reported that, per requirements of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, an inspection and acceptance review will be conducted for provincial experimental teaching demonstration centers (construction projects) established between 2004 and 2010. Five projects at Shantou University's main campus are included in this round.

## The Five Projects Under Review

| Center | School / Department | Year Established | Project Leader |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Modern Electrical & Electronic Experimental Teaching Center | Electronic Engineering, College of Engineering | 2006 | Li Xutao |
| CDIO Experimental Teaching Demonstration Center | College of Engineering | 2010 | Xiong Guangjing |
| Computer Fundamentals Key Laboratory | Computer Science, College of Engineering | 2004 | Cai Weihong |
| Journalism & Communication Experimental Teaching Center | College of Journalism | 2009 | Fan Dongsheng |
| Art & Design Experimental Teaching Center | Art Academy | 2009 | Han Ran |

## Materials and Timeline

As required by the province, each center must log into the provincial quality project management system to submit a self-evaluation report, acceptance data sheets, achievement lists, and development plans, along with its website address, plus one paper copy. The university has arranged a phased schedule:

| Period | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Mar 15 – Apr 15 | Centers complete self-evaluation materials and submit them to the Academic Affairs Office |
| Apr 16 – Apr 26 | University experts review materials and conduct on-site checks with improvement suggestions |
| May 6 – Jun 20 | Centers refine their work based on expert feedback |
| By Sep 30 | Formal submission through the provincial system |
| To be announced | On-site inspection by the provincial department |

The Academic Affairs Office urges leaders of the relevant schools to take the inspection seriously and prepare thoroughly so the projects pass acceptance smoothly.
