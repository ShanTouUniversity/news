STU’s Teaching Office has organized major-transfer intake for the **2012 undergraduate cohort** under the university’s provisional credit-based student-status regulations. Before the fifth teaching week of each semester, every major must publish the number of students it can accept from other majors and the applicable requirements for students to review.

Each major must complete the *2012-Cohort Major-Transfer Intake Form*, covering the number of places, eligibility requirements, assessment content and method, admission principles, and the time and location of registration and assessments. Forms are due to the Teaching Office’s Student Affairs Section by **Tuesday of the fourth teaching week**. The Teaching Office will publish the detailed information based on the places and requirements submitted by each major.

The attachment states that registration closes in the **10th teaching week** and assessments should be scheduled in the **11th teaching week**. Final places, requirements, and assessment arrangements will be provided in the Teaching Office’s subsequent announcement.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office)
>
> ⚠️ The major-transfer form is an internal administrative document. Consult the Teaching Office’s subsequent notice for specific places and assessment requirements.
