The STU Youth League Committee has forwarded arrangements from the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League and the All-China Students’ Federation for the 2012 **China College Student Self-Improvement Star** campaign and recommendations for the **New Oriental Special Aid for Students in Extreme Financial Hardship in Western China**. Eligible students are invited to register and submit a printed form and personal achievements to the STU Youth League Committee Office.

### Self-Improvement Star registration

The campaign theme is **“Youth Self-Improvement, Realizing the Chinese Dream.”** It is open to full-time undergraduate, junior-college, and graduate students at regular higher-education institutions. Applicants should have good academic performance, integrity, and a commitment to public service, and should have outstanding achievements in areas such as public service, moral leadership, scientific and technological innovation, entrepreneurship, volunteer work, or overcoming physical challenges. Applicants whose stories had been reported by campus or public media by March 20, 2013, and had made a significant campus impact would receive priority. Previous recipients of the “Model China College Student Self-Improvement Star” or “China College Student Self-Improvement Star” awards may not enter again; previous nominees remain eligible.

Eligible students must register at <http://star.xiaomei.cc> by **4:00 p.m. on March 20** and complete the required online profile. Successful applicants must also post a link to their profile under the relevant Tencent Weibo topic, seek at least 50 reposts, and publish at least five posts presenting their stories. Campus publicity is scheduled for **March 25–29**. University recommendation materials are due to the provincial Youth League authorities by **April 5**, provincial recommendations are due by **April 15**, and final results are planned for early May.

The campaign plans to recognize 10 model self-improvement stars, 100 self-improvement stars, and 800 nominees. Each group will receive an honorary certificate and a New Oriental Self-Improvement Scholarship of **RMB 10,000, RMB 5,000, and RMB 2,000**, respectively.

### Western-region special aid

The New Oriental special aid program is for full-time undergraduate and junior-college students enrolled at regular higher-education institutions in 12 western provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities or the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps. Applicants must have less than **RMB 200 per month** in family-provided living expenses, good academic performance, and good conduct. Candidates selected by their school Youth League or student union must be publicized for at least seven days, and recommendation materials must be submitted by **April 5**. The program plans to recommend **750 students** in the relevant regions.
