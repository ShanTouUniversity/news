The **Sunshine Community** service project of Shantou University’s Youth Volunteer Association Firefly team is recruiting volunteers from across the university. Since its establishment in 2007, the project has provided community services for socially vulnerable groups, including visits to older people living alone, single-parent families, and others needing support. Services include household assistance, emotional support, volunteer repairs and health consultations, academic guidance, and public-information outreach.

### Registration

- **Deadline**: March 15;
- **On-site information and recruitment**: March 14–15, 11:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m. and 5:00–7:00 p.m.;
- **Location**: outside Dining Hall 2;
- **How to register**: Complete a form on site or submit the electronic form. The project encourages electronic submissions as part of its paperless-work initiative.

Project information is available through the Firefly website at <http://firefly.stu.edu.cn>, the Sunshine Community blog at <http://blog.163.com/sun_shine2009/>, and its official social-media accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteer Association)
