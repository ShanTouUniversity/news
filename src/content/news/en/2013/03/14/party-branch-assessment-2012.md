Under the university Party Committee’s work plan, Shantou University will conduct its **2012 assessment of grassroots Party branches**. Each Party general branch and directly affiliated Party branch must organize self-assessment under the *Trial Standards for Grassroots Party-Branch Development at Shantou University* and prepare the required materials.

### Assessment areas

The standards and assessment forms examine grassroots Party-branch development in the following areas:

- **Organizational development**: branch committees, member development, member education and management, and members’ exemplary role;
- **Ideological and political education**: theoretical study, contact with the broader community, ideological work, and social service;
- **Basic work**: work plans and summaries, meetings and organizational-life systems, Party-dues collection, document retention, and activity records;
- **Innovative activities**: target management and distinctive, innovative branch activities.

The first three sections of the assessment total 100 points, with 10 additional points for innovative activities. A branch needs at least 80 points to meet the standard and at least 90 points to be rated excellent. Separate assessment forms are provided for staff and student Party branches.

Party general branches and directly affiliated branches must submit assessment forms, materials from participating branches, and assessment summaries to the Party Organization and United Front Department by **April 15**. The submission office is Administration Center 315. Contact `86502345` or `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.
