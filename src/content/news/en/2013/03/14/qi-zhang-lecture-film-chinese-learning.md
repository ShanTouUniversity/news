It is reported that the 118th session of the New Century Humanities Forum of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University will be held from 8:00 pm to 9:00 pm on March 17 in Hall 3 of the Academic Exchange Centre (ACC). The speaker will be Dr. Qi Zhang of Dublin City University, Ireland, whose talk is titled "The impact of film and film-based activities on the attitudes of secondary-school students towards Chinese."

While foreign-language films can provide language learners with authentic exposure to the target language in a relatively natural context, few studies have examined the use of cinema in teaching Chinese language and culture. This research piloted two film sessions with three groups of secondary-school students of Chinese, investigating the influence of Chinese cinema on students' attitudes from three perspectives: the perceived importance of language study, motivational change and cultural awareness.

Comparing pre- and post-event evaluations, correlation tests showed that integrating film into the classroom can indeed increase motivation to learn Chinese and improve students' understanding of Chinese culture. Interestingly, compared with their female counterparts, male participants consistently reported lower motivation and demonstrated less cultural awareness after the film sessions. The researcher suggests that the cultural elements conveyed by foreign-language films and audiences' comprehension could serve as a starting point for intercultural exchange, offering British students a rare window into a culture that is difficult to access in their immediate environment.

Dr. Zhang holds an M.A. in Translation from Durham University and a Ph.D. in Linguistics from Newcastle University, UK. She joined Dublin City University as coordinator of Chinese in 2011, having previously taught at Manchester Metropolitan University. Her research covers language attitudes, motivation and cultural awareness.
