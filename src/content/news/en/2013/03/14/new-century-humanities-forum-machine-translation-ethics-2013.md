The 119th **STU New Century Humanities Forum** will be held from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on March 18** in E Building, Room 303. Dr. Dorothy Kenny of Dublin City University will give a lecture titled **“The Ethics of Machine Translation.”**

The lecture will introduce statistical machine translation and examine ethical questions concerning how translators are recognized and compensated in machine-translation research and workflows, how future translators are trained, and the ethical positions adopted in the development, popularization, and teaching of machine translation.

Dorothy Kenny is a senior lecturer in the School of Applied Language and Intercultural Studies at Dublin City University. Her research interests include corpus linguistics, translation theory, machine translation, computer-aided translation, scientific translation, and terminology. She serves on the editorial boards of *The Translator* and *The Interpreter and Translator Trainer* and contributes to the European Master’s in Translation and the International Association for Translation and Intercultural Studies.
