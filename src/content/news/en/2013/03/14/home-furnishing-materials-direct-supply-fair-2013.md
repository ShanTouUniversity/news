The third **Shantou Home-Furnishing Materials Factory Direct-Supply Fair** will be held at **12:30 p.m. on March 23** on the third floor of Lin Baixin Convention and Exhibition Center. Huihuang Plumbing Group will provide STU employees with free VIP direct-supply cards and a free group shuttle from in front of the STU Post Office at **noon** on the day of the fair.

Employees who are interested may collect VIP cards and ask questions outside the STU Union building from **9:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on March 17**.

For inquiries, contact Mr. Mai of Huihuang Sanitary Ware at `15889255205` or `0754-89925699`.
