Shantou University’s Human Resources Office, Teaching Office, and Publicity Department have launched a campus campaign to “Find a Zhang Lili Around You,” inviting faculty and staff to recommend outstanding teachers and further strengthen professional ethics in teaching.

The campaign focuses on teachers who demonstrate dedication to education, sustained scholarly work, teaching and mentoring, care for students, and selfless service. Units should organize recommendations and use stories, presentations, and exchanges with role-model teachers to promote their exemplary influence. Based on recommendations from institutions across Guangdong, the provincial education authority plans to select outstanding teachers for media coverage and recommend some role models for national publicity activities.

Each unit must complete the advanced-teacher recommendation form, attach an approximately **3,000-character** report on the candidate’s achievements, and submit the stamped materials to Human Resources Office Room 315 in the Administration Center by **March 13**. Send the electronic version to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. The campus contact number is `86502349`.

The attached Guangdong Provincial Department of Education notice asks each relevant university to recommend one representative teacher and gives a provincial submission deadline of **March 9**. This date is earlier than STU’s internal March 13 deadline; units should confirm the reporting arrangements with the Human Resources Office.
