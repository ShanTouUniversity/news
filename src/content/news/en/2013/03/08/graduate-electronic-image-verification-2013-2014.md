Shantou University’s Graduate School reminds students graduating in 2013 and 2014 that their images for the online academic registration system are available. Students who participated in the graduation-image collection, whether on or off campus, should log in to the China Higher-education Student Information and Career Center (CHSI) at <http://www.chsi.com.cn/> and verify their graduation information.

Students should confirm that their graduation photograph and academic and registration information are accurate and request corrections through the applicable CHSI and university procedures. Online verification and corrections must be completed by **March 31**.

Students photographed at an off-campus collection site should also complete the online verification and send or deliver the printed photograph to Wang Qiao at the Graduate School by **the end of April**. CHSI accounts must be registered with real-name information matching university records. Students should protect their account usernames and passwords.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
