It is reported that Shantou University has switched its treasury payment agent bank from ICBC Jinhu Branch to Bank of China Shantou University Branch starting in 2012, and the Finance Office has issued guidance on applying for new Bank of China official cards.

Since December 2009, the university has implemented the provincial reform requiring budget units to adopt official cards for treasury payments, with some faculty having previously opened cards at ICBC. Following the change of agent bank, those ICBC cards are no longer valid for official-card settlement, and holders must open new cards at Bank of China.

Under the provincial directive Yue Cai Ku [2012] No. 20 on accelerating the reform of the official-card settlement system, treasury payments — apart from transfer checks and remittances — must in principle be settled via official card. Faculty with treasury-funded special budgets and any staff whose expenses are paid through treasury funds are required to hold an official card for small, sporadic expenditures such as office supplies, travel, and hospitality.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Agent Bank Change | From ICBC Jinhu Branch to Bank of China Shantou University Branch (since 2012) |
| Impact | Former ICBC official cards no longer valid; holders must reapply at BOC |
| Who Must Apply | Faculty with treasury-funded special budgets and anyone spending treasury funds |
| Settlement Rule | Treasury payments other than transfer checks and remittances must use the official card in principle |
| Typical Uses | Office supplies, travel expenses, hospitality and other small incidental purchases |
| How to Apply | Submit copies of both sides of ID card and campus card to the Finance Office to complete application forms; the Finance Office will process applications at BOC collectively |
| Inquiries | 86502100, 86502467 |
| Policy Basis | Yue Cai Ku [2012] No. 20 |

The Finance Office advises affected faculty and staff to apply promptly to avoid disruption to treasury payment settlement.
