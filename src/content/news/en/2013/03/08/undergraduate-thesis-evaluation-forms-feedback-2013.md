Shantou University’s Teaching Office is again seeking feedback from schools and departments on revised evaluation forms to be used for the assessment of **2013 undergraduate theses**. A draft was previously circulated university-wide, and the Student Evaluation Subcommittee of the Teaching Committee has reviewed it. The Teaching Office has now incorporated the latest revisions.

The consultation covers three forms: an evaluation form for supervisors, one for reviewers, and one for thesis-defense committees. Units with comments or suggestions should email them to `yzchen@stu.edu.cn` by **March 15**.

The contact person is Chen Yangzhou, telephone `86503202`.
