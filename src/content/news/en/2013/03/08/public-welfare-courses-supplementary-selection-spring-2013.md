Shantou University’s Teaching Office has announced supplementary enrollment for spring public-service courses. After initial selection and add/drop adjustments, the following three courses still have places for students who have not yet selected a public-service course:

| Course code | Course | Schedule | Places remaining |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| SER0410 | “Meeting the Blue Sky” Creative Service Learning | Theory training and service-site coordination on Saturdays in Weeks 2–5, 12 hours total | 28 |
| SER1711 | Caring Tutoring Service | Recruitment in early March; service-site visit on March 16 or 17; theory classes Wednesday afternoons in Weeks 3 and 4; practice Saturday afternoons in Weeks 5–12; final sharing on June 5, 42 hours total | 4 |
| SER1710 | Shantou Traffic Quality Improvement Project | Group training on Wednesday afternoons and Saturday mornings in Weeks 3 and 4, 12 hours total | 85 |

Interested students must email their registration information to `gykc@stu.edu.cn` by **9:00 a.m. on March 11**. Places are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis until full, and each student may select only one course. For questions, contact Liu at `82902416`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office)
>
> ⚠️ Registration requires cohort, student number, name, contact details, and email address. Submit them through the designated mailbox; personal information is not reproduced here.
