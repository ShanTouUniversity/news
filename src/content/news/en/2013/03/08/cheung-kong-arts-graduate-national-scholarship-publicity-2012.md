The Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee of Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design has completed its review for the 2012 National Graduate Scholarship and proposed **two graduate students from the 2010 cohort** as recipients. The results are now being publicized.

Organizations and individuals with comments should submit them in writing to the school’s Academic Affairs Office by **March 15**. Materials submitted by organizations must bear an official seal, while individuals must sign with their real names. The contact number is `86502419`, and the email address is `lilq@stu.edu.cn`.
