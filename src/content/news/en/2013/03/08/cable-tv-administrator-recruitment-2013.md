It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on March 8, 2013, to openly recruit one cable TV administrator.

## Position and Responsibilities

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Position | Cable TV Administrator, Resource Management Office, 1 vacancy |
| Responsibilities | Monitor, inspect, maintain and debug the TV front-end reception and playback system and repair equipment; maintain outdoor closed-circuit TV lines, amplifiers, splitters, distributors and end users campus-wide; liaise with local cable TV authorities; report monthly on operation, inspection and maintenance; cover shifts and other tasks as assigned |
| Employment | Auxiliary contract worker; personnel file not transferred to STU; remuneration per university regulations |

## Requirements

1. Good political integrity, strong dedication, upright conduct, diligent and rigorous, with teamwork and devotion;
2. Major in electromechanical engineering, electrical/electronic or building electrical; secondary vocational (or technical school) education or above;
3. Practical experience in high/low voltage and water supply/drainage equipment maintenance;
4. Age no more than 35, in good health, compliant with family planning policy.

## Application and Contacts

Applicants should send a resume and copies of ID, graduation certificate, training and technical qualification certificates and family planning certificate by sealed letter before March 20, 2013. Shortlisted candidates will be notified for interview and must present originals.
