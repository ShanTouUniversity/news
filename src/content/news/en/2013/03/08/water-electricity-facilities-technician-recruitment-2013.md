For operational needs, Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is publicly recruiting **one water and electrical facilities management technician**. The position will be hired as a support-service contract worker; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow applicable university rules.

### Requirements

Applicants should have sound professional ethics, a serious and careful work style, and a spirit of teamwork and service. They should hold a college diploma in mechanical and electrical engineering, building electrical engineering, or a related field and have practical experience maintaining high- and low-voltage and water-supply and drainage equipment and facilities. Applicants must be no older than 35, in good health, and meet the applicable family-planning requirements in effect at the time.

### Responsibilities

The position will:

- inspect the high-voltage switch stations in Buildings 1 and 2, conduct preliminary analysis of high-voltage-system faults, and develop initial maintenance responses;
- inspect pump rooms in the eastern and western campus areas, conduct preliminary analysis of variable-frequency pressurized-water-system faults, and develop initial maintenance responses;
- compile and manage university water and electrical facilities records, manage departmental fixed assets, and prepare annual maintenance plans and related rules;
- cover duty shifts and complete other assigned work.

Applicants must send sealed copies of a résumé, identity document, diploma, professional training certificates, technical qualification certificates, family-planning certificate, and other relevant credentials to the Resource Management Office Water and Electrical Center by **March 20**. Shortlisted applicants will be notified for interviews and must present original documents for verification.

Mail applications to the Resource Management Office Water and Electrical Center, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Contacts are Cai Weizhong and Chen Shenhao at `0754-82902018` and `82902489`; fax `0754-82522397`; email `o_sdk@stu.edu.cn`. Use “Application for Water and Electrical Facilities Management Technician” as the subject.
