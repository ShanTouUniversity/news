Shantou University’s Teaching Office will hold final defenses for selected 2010 and 2011 undergraduate innovation projects at **2:30 p.m. on March 13**. According to the defense schedule, **58 projects** will participate. The projects cover the School of Journalism and Communication, Business School, College of Liberal Arts, College of Arts and Design, School of Law, College of Science, and School of Engineering and will be presented in five groups in classrooms on the fourth floor of the G Teaching Building.

Project teams must arrive for sign-in at **2:00 p.m.** and copy their presentation slides to the lectern computer. Each project has 12 minutes: seven minutes for the presentation and five minutes for questions from experts. Presentations should focus on completed work and results. Teams should bring physical models, software, or other supporting materials for demonstrations; photographs or video may be used when a result cannot be brought to the classroom.

Each team must designate a member to record questions and answers and email the record to `yjguo@stu.edu.cn` after the defense. Evaluation results will be classified as Excellent, Good, Pass, or Fail. Projects rated Excellent, Good, or Pass will receive two research-innovation credits, and the final-defense result will be entered into the credit-based academic system as a course grade. A project that does not attend the defense will be considered to have withdrawn.
