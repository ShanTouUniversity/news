To prepare for the 2014 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC)–Guangdong Provincial Government Natural Science Joint Fund, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology is soliciting suggestions for application-guideline topics. STU’s Research Office invites interested faculty to submit suggestions that do not duplicate Guangdong Joint Fund guidelines officially published from 2006 through 2013.

The call covers five areas:

| Area | Example topics |
| --- | --- |
| Agriculture | Higher yields and disease prevention for economically important plants and animals in South China, excluding chickens, pigs, and rice; disease prevention in distinctive South China Sea and Beibu Gulf aquaculture |
| Resources and environment | Prevention of saltwater intrusion in Guangdong; typhoon-climate mechanisms; pollution control and contaminated-site remediation during industrial relocation |
| Population and health | Thalassemia prevention and treatment; modernization of Lingnan traditional Chinese medicine; development and use of South China Sea marine medicines |
| Materials and engineering | Biomaterials, LEDs, electric vehicles, and South China Sea marine exploration equipment |
| Electronic information | Medical devices, network and information security, and cloud computing |

Interested faculty should email an electronic guideline-suggestion form to `yxhu@stu.edu.cn` by **March 12**.
