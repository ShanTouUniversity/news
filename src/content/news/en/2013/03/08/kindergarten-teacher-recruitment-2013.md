For operational needs, Shantou University is publicly recruiting **two kindergarten teachers**, one of whom will also serve as an administrative assistant. The positions will be hired as support-service contract workers; employment relationships will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow applicable university standards.

### Requirements

Applicants should be hardworking, caring, responsible, and willing to follow work arrangements. They must be no older than 40, in good health, and meet applicable family-planning requirements. Applicants should have at least a secondary vocational diploma in preschool education or formal preschool-teacher training, a kindergarten-teacher qualification certificate, and an industry health certificate. Kindergarten experience is preferred.

Teachers will develop care and education plans based on children’s ages and individual differences, conduct class activities, protect children’s safety, manage daily care and health, communicate with parents, participate in professional learning and teaching research, and help prepare classroom environments and activity facilities. They may also be assigned substitute, regular, holiday, and vacation duties.

The administrative-assistant teacher will also handle administrative, teaching-support, and logistics work; help arrange children’s meals; inspect food, drinking water, and environmental hygiene; manage kindergarten property and equipment; prepare teaching materials; support health-care work; perform accounting duties; and take scheduled duty shifts and other assigned work.

Applicants must submit a résumé and copies of their diploma, kindergarten-teacher qualification certificate, health certificate, professional-training certificates, family-planning certificate, identity document, and other credentials by **5:00 p.m. on March 14** by email or letter to the STU Resource Management Office Kindergarten. Shortlisted applicants will be notified for interviews and must present originals for verification.

Mail applications to the Resource Management Office Kindergarten, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Contacts are Deng Xiaming and Yu Ruohua. Email `o_yey@stu.edu.cn` and use “Application for Resource Management Office Kindergarten Teacher or Administrative-Assistant Teacher” as the subject.
