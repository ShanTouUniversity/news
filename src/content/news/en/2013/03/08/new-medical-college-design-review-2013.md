After more than two years of joint work, the preliminary design for Shantou University’s new Medical College project has been completed. The design was developed by Herzog & de Meuron of Switzerland and CSCEC International (Shenzhen) Design Consultants Co., Ltd.

As required by the construction process, the Shantou Municipal Housing and Urban-Rural Development Bureau will organize experts and relevant municipal departments to review the design at **2:30 p.m. on Monday, March 11**, in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center. STU’s General Office, Publicity Department, Supervision and Audit Office, Finance Office, Project Coordination Office, Medical College, Resource Management Office, and Key Construction Projects Office will attend or send representatives.
