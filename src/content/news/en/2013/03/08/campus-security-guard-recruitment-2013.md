For operational needs, Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is publicly recruiting **several campus security officers**. The positions will be hired as support-service contract workers; employment relationships will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow applicable university standards.

### Requirements and duties

Applicants should have sound professional ethics, a serious work style, and a spirit of teamwork and service; at least a middle-school education; be male, no older than 50, in good health, and meet applicable family-planning requirements; and be able to work 24-hour shifts. Experience in security or fire safety, or military service, is preferred.

Officers will maintain order in teaching, residential, and public areas; guard campus gates and patrol the campus; protect the safety and property of faculty, staff, and students; assist with fire and theft prevention, security-dispute resolution, and protection of key areas; identify and respond to hazards and emergencies; assist relevant offices in maintaining campus safety; and operate and maintain surveillance and fire-safety equipment. The position uses a 24-hour duty rotation and requires strict compliance with shift, handover, and reporting procedures.

Applicants must submit a résumé and copies of their identity document, family-planning certificate, diploma, certificate of no criminal record, professional-training certificates, technical qualification certificates, and other credentials by **April 8** by email or letter. Shortlisted applicants will be notified for interviews and must present originals for verification.

Mail applications to the Campus Security Office, Resource Management Office, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Contact Mr. Chen at `0754-86503439` or `o_xwd@stu.edu.cn`. Use “Application for Campus Security Officer” as the subject.
