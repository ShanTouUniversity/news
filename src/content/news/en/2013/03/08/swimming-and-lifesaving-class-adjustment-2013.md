Because Physical Education Department instructor Zhang Yi will be away for a meeting, Shantou University’s Swimming and Lifesaving classes scheduled in the Multimedia Classroom of the Gymnasium on **Monday of Week 3, March 11**, are canceled. The change affects class 58293 during periods 6–7 and class 58294 during periods 8–9.

The make-up classes will be held during the same periods on **Saturday of Week 3, March 16**, in the Multimedia Classroom of the Gymnasium. Students enrolled in the classes should note the change and attend at the revised time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office)
