The Central Committee of the Communist Youth League plans to recognize 2012 National Outstanding Youth League Members, National Outstanding Youth League Cadres, National May Fourth Red Flag Youth League Committees, and National May Fourth Red Flag Youth League Branches during the 2013 May Fourth period. STU’s Youth League Committee has forwarded the provincial call and is organizing recommendations through the Youth League committees of its schools and colleges and its directly affiliated branches.

### Eligibility

National Outstanding Youth League Members should support the leadership of the Party, observe the law, demonstrate strong ethics and outstanding performance in study, work, or social life, fulfill their member duties, and have received an honor from a provincial Youth League committee. National Outstanding Youth League Cadres generally must have at least three cumulative years of Youth League work, outstanding performance, and a provincial Youth League honor. National May Fourth Red Flag committees and branches must have standardized structures, sound systems, distinctive activities, and strong results in member education and grassroots organizational development, and must previously have received the corresponding provincial honor.

Relevant units may recommend one or two eligible candidate individuals or organizations under the call. Candidates and organizations will undergo a full review, including consultation with the relevant Party organization.

Youth League committees of schools and colleges and directly affiliated branches must submit two paper copies and an electronic copy of each application form and achievement statement to the STU Youth League Committee office by **March 14**. Electronic files should be sent to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. Paper achievement statements may be no longer than 2,000 Chinese characters; electronic submissions must also include a version of no more than 300 characters. Late submissions will be treated as withdrawals. The provincial Youth League Committee requires university materials to be consolidated and submitted to its School Department by **March 15**.
