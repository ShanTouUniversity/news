Shantou University AIESEC is opening its 2013 spring recruitment to students across the university, regardless of major or year. AIESEC is a global, non-political, independent, and nonprofit organization run by university students and recent graduates. Its overseas volunteer programs offer opportunities in more than 60 countries and address topics including Chinese culture, HIV/AIDS prevention, community and rural development, environmental protection, language education, and microfinance.

### Recruitment activities

STU AIESEC will hold **AIESEC Night from 8:00 to 10:00 p.m. on March 16** on the third floor of the Trade Union building. The session will introduce overseas volunteer programs and AIESEC’s internal departments and include an experience camp. Registration forms will be available at the event.

Recruitment has three stages: résumé screening, group interviews, and individual interviews. Later arrangements will be announced by text message. Team recruitment closes at **11:30 p.m. on March 17**; send a Chinese and English résumé to `aiesecstumember@gmail.com`. Overseas-volunteer recruitment closes at **11:30 p.m. on March 23**; send materials to `aiesecstuvolunteer@gmail.com`.

For more information, follow the Sina Weibo account “AIESEC STU” or visit the official blog at <http://blog.sina.com.cn/u/2566597763>.
