Shantou University’s School of Engineering will hold the **149th Engineering Research Forum** from **3:00 to 4:00 p.m. on March 15** in the school’s Academic Exchange Room. The forum will examine **“Intelligent Human-Computer Interaction Technology and Practice,”** and faculty and students are welcome.

Professor Jin Lianwen of South China University of Technology will discuss the history, related fields, and development trends of intelligent human-computer interaction. The presentation will demonstrate speech, gesture, handwriting, motion-sensing, touch, and visual interaction technologies and their applications. It will also use a Kinect-based in-air handwriting system to explain the design and implementation of a real-time visual motion-sensing interaction system and present related research results. Jin will also discuss grant-application writing with faculty and students.

Jin is a professor and doctoral supervisor at South China University of Technology. He has been selected for the Ministry of Education’s New Century Excellent Talents program, the provincial training program of Guangdong’s “Hundreds and Thousands of Talents Project,” and the Guangdong Pearl River Scholar Distinguished Professor program. His research focuses on intelligent information processing, human-computer interaction, and related fields.
