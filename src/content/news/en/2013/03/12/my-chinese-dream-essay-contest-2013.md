Following the Guangdong Department of Education’s initiative, Shantou University is inviting faculty and students to enter a **“My Chinese Dream”** essay competition. Participants are encouraged to connect personal aspirations and real experiences with the country’s development.

Entries must address the theme and be credible, original, and free of plagiarism or fabrication. There are no restrictions on genre or length. Submissions are made under the author’s real name: send the electronic version and contact details to `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn`, and deliver two printed copies to the Publicity Department in Room 315 of the New Administration Center.

The submission deadline is **April 8**. The university will organize an awards review, and outstanding essays may be published in the university newspaper.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department of the CPC STU Committee)
