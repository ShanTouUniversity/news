Shantou University has forwarded the Guangdong Department of Education’s call for applications for the **2013 Guangdong higher-education high-level talent projects** and is organizing eligible faculty applications.

Applicants must be currently serving faculty members at Guangdong universities who have not previously received funding under a Guangdong higher-education high-level talent project and who are either Pearl River Scholar Distinguished Professors or national- or provincial-level participants in the seventh cohort of the “Hundreds and Thousands of Talents Project.” Units should organize applications under the stated procedures and arrange a feasibility review by **at least five peer experts** for each project, including at least two external experts, with written review comments.

Submit the following materials to the Human Resources Office by **March 25** and send electronic copies to `o_rsc@stu.edu.cn`:

- Eight copies of the Application Form for Guangdong Higher-Education High-Level Talent Projects;
- One Summary Form for Guangdong Higher-Education High-Level Talent Projects;
- One Summary Form for Young Faculty to Be Trained under Guangdong Higher-Education High-Level Talent Projects.

The Human Resources Office and Research Office will review and select applications before submitting them to the Department of Education for approval.
