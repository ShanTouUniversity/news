Shantou University’s School of Engineering has announced the final review results for its 2012 student technology innovation projects. **Thirty-four projects** passed the midterm review; four requested deferred completion and two were abandoned without explanation. After review by the school’s expert panel, **16 projects passed the final review, four were deferred, and 12 failed the defense**.

Project leaders whose projects passed review and whose completion reports have been submitted must complete project-expense reimbursement by **March 29**. Students from projects that passed the final defense or received a completion deferral must attend a summary meeting at **5:00 p.m. on March 13** in D406; each project team must send at least one representative.
