The final round of the 12th Association of International Accountants (AIA) “Job King” competition will be held at the Sanshui campus of Guangdong University of Finance on **April 14**. Shantou University’s Business School will send one undergraduate contestant and is collecting Chinese and English speeches from its undergraduates for selection.

Selection interviews will begin at **9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, March 26** in the Business School’s second-floor Seminar Room. The interview will assess Chinese and English pronunciation, speaking ability, on-stage presence, and spontaneous response; the manuscript will be secondary. Applicants must have passed English Band 3 or above, speak standard Mandarin and English, and demonstrate strong communication and adaptive-response skills. Send bilingual manuscripts to `dnye@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on March 25**, together with the requested registration information.

The competition consists of an English speech and a Mandarin speech, each limited to five minutes, followed by questions from the judges and a one-minute response. The Mandarin topic concerns HSBC Holdings’ 2012 results, regulatory penalties, and ways to restore investor confidence. The English topic discusses Boeing 787 battery-safety concerns, Airbus A350 design choices, and an international traveler’s views on air safety, service, choice, and fares.
