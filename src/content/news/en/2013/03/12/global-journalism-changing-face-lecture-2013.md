Shantou University’s J-School will host the open lecture **“The Changing Face of Global Journalism”** from **7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 13** in ACC Conference Room 3. Faculty and students are welcome.

The speaker is Andrew Beck of Coventry School of Art and Design at Coventry University in the United Kingdom. He has more than 35 years of experience as an educator, consultant, author, examiner, and curriculum designer. His research interests include communication and design education, cultural work, unpopular music and film, and the effect of the environment on consciousness. He also works with the joint research center for Design, Innovation, and Management and curates Coventry University’s Alan Berry Atrium Gallery.

Beck has served as chief examiner for UK business, professional, and academic examining bodies, contributed to the UK Curriculum 2000 initiative by writing the AQA “A” Level Communication Studies specification, and published or contributed to books on communication studies, cultural work, social work, and intercultural research.
