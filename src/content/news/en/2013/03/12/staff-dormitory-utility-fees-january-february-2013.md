Shantou University has posted the utility-fee arrangements for staff dormitories for **January and February 2013**. January meter readings were taken on February 1 and 2, and February readings were taken on March 1. Relevant staff members may check their individual charges at the STU utility-management system, <http://power.stu.edu.cn>, using their campus-network account.

Questions or objections may be reviewed at the Utility Center from **March 12 through 14**. The contact number is `2489`. If no objection is raised by the deadline, the Finance Office will deduct the charges from March salary accounts under the applicable arrangements. Staff members without salary accounts should pay the Finance Office directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ Utility records and salary deductions are personal financial information. Check individual bills through the campus system; no personal billing data is published here.
