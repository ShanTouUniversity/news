It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University have jointly launched the application process for the 2013 Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program, which is open to all undergraduates.

## Three Types of Projects

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Innovation training | Individuals or teams independently complete innovative research design, implementation and academic exchange under faculty supervision |
| Entrepreneurship training | Student teams prepare business plans, conduct feasibility studies, simulate business operations and write entrepreneurship reports under faculty supervision |
| Entrepreneurship practice | Student teams, co-supervised by academic and corporate mentors, launch market-oriented ventures based on prior innovation outcomes |

## Application Requirements

Team-based applications are preferred, though individuals may also apply. Innovation training teams are limited to five members, each with a clear division of roles. Each student may participate in only one project, with no cross-applications, and may not apply for a new project before completing an existing one. Interdisciplinary, inter-major and inter-year collaborations are encouraged, with team-based and interdisciplinary projects given priority in equal circumstances.

Each project must have one to two supervising teachers, and each teacher generally supervises one project. Projects run for one and a half years; leaders of innovation and entrepreneurship training projects must complete them before graduation, while entrepreneurship practice project leaders may be replaced after graduation or continue as student entrepreneurs.

## Timeline and Review Process

- **Innovation training projects**: Colleges organize applications and reviews from March 12 to April 5, 2013, then submit recommended projects to the Academic Affairs Office for final approval;
- **Entrepreneurship training and practice projects**: Reviewed centrally by the university, with materials due at the Student Affairs Office by April 1.

Guo Yijia of the Academic Affairs Office (phone: 86502736) handles material collection and inquiries for innovation training projects.

## Funding and Management

Funding is generally RMB 10,000 per project. Fifty percent is disbursed upon approval, with the remainder released after passing the mid-term review. The funds are earmarked and cannot be repurposed; total spending must not exceed the allocated amount, and teams must submit an itemized spending report upon completion.

The university will conduct mid-term inspections requiring progress reports on project advancement, future plans and fund usage. Projects delayed or terminated without valid reason will lose funding, with team members barred from applying for two years and supervisors losing their mentoring eligibility for the same period.
