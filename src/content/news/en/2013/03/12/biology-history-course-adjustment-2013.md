Shantou University has adjusted the schedule for **Biology History and Mysteries of Life** (class number `59285`). The sessions originally scheduled for **March 13** and **April 3**, periods 1 and 2 in D303, are canceled. Both sessions will be made up on **Saturday, June 8**, periods 1 through 4, in the same room, D303.

Students enrolled in the course should note the change and attend the make-up class.
