Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has reported disciplinary action involving an Engineering student. During the **Structural Mechanics II examination on January 14**, the student brought and used materials related to the examination and was caught by an invigilator.

Under the Regulations on the Management of Students in Regular Institutions of Higher Education, STU’s student-discipline regulations, and its undergraduate credit-based enrollment-management rules, the university issued a **warning** and recorded a **zero** for the examination course.

Under STU’s degree-award rules, students disciplined for examination cheating during their enrollment are generally ineligible for a bachelor’s degree. A student who demonstrates strong improvement after the disciplinary action, receives a university second-class scholarship or higher, or graduates with a total GPA of at least 3.5 may apply under the prescribed process. Following review by the school, recommendation by the degree-assessment subcommittee, and university approval, the student may be considered for a degree.

The university reminds all students to observe examination rules and help maintain a sound teaching environment and academic culture.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The student’s name, year, and major are not published to protect personal privacy.
