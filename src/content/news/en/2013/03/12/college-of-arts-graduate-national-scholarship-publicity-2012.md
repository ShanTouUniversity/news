Following the Graduate School’s arrangements for the 2012 graduate national-scholarship review, the College of Liberal Arts’ 2012 review committee has completed its assessment and proposed **two graduate students** for the 2012 national graduate scholarship. The proposed candidates are being publicized for review.

Comments should be submitted in writing to the College of Liberal Arts review committee by **5:00 p.m. on March 18**. The receiving office is the Graduate Student Administration Office, Wenan Building Room 203. Contact `86503631` or `blsun@stu.edu.cn`. Submissions from organizations must carry an official seal, while individual submissions must include the writer’s real name.
