The spring 2013 College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 exams will be held on **June 15**. Band 4 runs from **9:00 to 11:20 a.m.**, and Band 6 runs from **3:00 to 5:20 p.m.** Main-campus graduate students who wish to register should do so through their school’s graduate-program secretary from **March 11 through 15**.

Graduate students applying for Band 6 must verify a passing Band 4 certificate or official score report. Graduate-program secretaries must consolidate registration information and submit it to the Graduate School by the end of the working day on **March 15**; electronic registration forms should be sent to `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn`. Refer to the Teaching Office’s related registration notice for eligibility, fee deductions, and other requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
>
> ⚠️ Registration and eligibility verification involve individual examination information. Register through the graduate-program secretary; personal data is not reproduced here.
