Under the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education’s 2013 arrangements for higher-education quality-engineering projects, Shantou University has completed its internal selection of projects from the main campus. Following expert review, STU plans to recommend projects including **“Spectral Analysis Classroom Teaching Using Auxiliary Molecular Simulation Tools”** for the provincial application, and is now publicizing the proposed recommendations.

The publicity period runs from **March 11 through 16**. During this period, comments may be submitted by telephone or in writing to the Teaching Office. The contact number is `86502736`; the contact person is Yang Weitao, and the email address is `wtyang@stu.edu.cn`.
