The 15th China Chenghai International Toy Fair will host a **student toy and animation creative-design exhibition and competition** from **April 20 through 23**. Shantou University students are invited to submit original toy and animation designs.

### Submission requirements

Entries may be hand-drawn works, computer works, or physical objects and must be created by the student without infringing another party’s intellectual-property rights. Hand-drawn works should be in formats from 8-kai to 4-kai. Computer drawings must be no larger than 15 MB, and animations must run from 30 seconds to one minute. Physical works may use any form or material. Entries must include a creative description; physical works must also describe their materials and production process.

The submission deadline is **April 3**. Email entries are dated by the sending date, while registered mail and courier entries are dated by the postmark. The competition offers one first prize, three second prizes, and nine third prizes.

Organizer submissions should be sent to Chenghai Vocational and Technical School, north side of the middle section of Huancui Road, Guangyi Subdistrict, Chenghai, Shantou, Guangdong 515800. Contact Xie Xiuyu at `15913995505` or `525426265@qq.com`. The STU contact is Deng Yong at `86503271` or `dengyong@stu.edu.cn`.
