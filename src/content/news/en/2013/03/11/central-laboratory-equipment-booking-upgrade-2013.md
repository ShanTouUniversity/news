To improve large-instrument booking management and the use of instrument time, Shantou University Central Laboratory has upgraded its booking system to add supervisor confirmation for student experiment requests.

Under the new process, a student submits an instrument-use request and the system automatically emails the details to the student’s supervisor. If the supervisor approves, they confirm through the link in the email; if they do not approve, no action is required. A student booking remains valid for **three days, including holidays**. If the supervisor does not confirm within that period, the request expires automatically. Instrument administrators will review only requests confirmed by supervisors. Students can check the review result in the system and visit the laboratory for analysis and testing at the booked time.

The upgraded system is now in pilot operation and will be **officially implemented on April 1, 2013**. Students should verify that their supervisor’s name and email address are complete before submitting a booking. For questions, call `86502740`.
