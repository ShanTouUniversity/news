To strengthen Youth League dues collection, management, and use and reinforce grassroots organizational development, Shantou University’s Youth League Committee asks the Youth League committees of its schools and colleges and its directly affiliated general branches and branches to follow the applicable dues rules, standardize procedures, and designate staff to oversee implementation.

Youth League organizations must complete **2012 dues collection** and conduct a self-review. The 2012 dues and self-review report must be submitted to the STU Youth League Committee office by **March 29**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University Committee)
