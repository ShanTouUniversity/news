Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) begins free spring academic-consultation services for all undergraduates on **March 11**. SLC is open **3:00–10:00 p.m. daily** and offers support in English, mathematics, physics, C programming, journalism and communication, law, engineering, and other subjects. Consult SLC’s latest schedule for exact sessions.

### Services

**Fixed consultation sessions** do not require registration. Students may go to the SLC front desk and sign in during the relevant course session. Fixed sessions include English pronunciation, writing, grammar, speaking, listening, TEM-4 preparation, advanced mathematics, linear algebra, calculus, and general physics. Some major-specific subjects are available by appointment.

Students whose schedules conflict with fixed sessions, or who want one-to-one support, may book at <http://booking.stu.edu.cn>. Appointments must be made at least 20 hours in advance, last up to two hours, and are limited to three per week. Cancellations must be made at least 12 hours in advance. Users must sign in and out at the front desk.

SLC also provides group-discussion rooms and self-study space. Discussion rooms are for groups of at least two people and must be booked at least four hours in advance; each session may last up to four hours, with a limit of six bookings per week. Individual self-study does not require a reservation and uses available rooms on a first-come, first-served basis. Students having substantial difficulty with a course or seeking a new course may use the SLC website forms to request one-to-one tutoring or an additional consultation subject.

For the latest schedule and user guide, visit the SLC website at <http://slc.stu.edu.cn>, its Weibo account at <http://weibo.com/stuslc>, or the student-affairs displays on campus.
