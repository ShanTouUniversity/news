Shantou University’s Trade Union will host **“Focus on Personal Image, Build a Beautiful Career: Clothing Image and Color Coordination”** from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on March 13** in E507 of the New Teaching Building. The lecture is open to women faculty and staff.

The session will examine skin tone, face shape, body type, and personal style and introduce clothing, color systems, and professional-image coordination to help participants identify styles suited to them. Participants may bring favorite clothing, scarves, or accessories for interactive discussion.

Speaker Li Kexian graduated from Donghua University’s fashion-design program in Shanghai and studied under image expert Yang Qingqing. Li teaches at STU’s Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design, teaches the courses Sketching and Color, and offers the university elective Personal Image Design.
