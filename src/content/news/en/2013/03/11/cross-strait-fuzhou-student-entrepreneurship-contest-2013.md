The first Cross-Strait Fuzhou Student Entrepreneurship and Innovation Competition is being organized by the Fuzhou Municipal People’s Government, Fujian Provincial Civil Service Bureau, Fujian Provincial Department of Education, Communist Youth League Fujian Provincial Committee, and other partners. Running from **November 2012 through May 2013**, the competition invites projects from students at full-time universities in mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan, as well as Chinese students and recent graduates from overseas universities.

### Eligibility and registration

Projects should relate to the 12th Five-Year Plan for national economic and social development and may involve inventions, patented technology, creative design, or conceptual products and services with development potential. Teams must hold the relevant intellectual-property or usage rights. Each team may have no more than eight members, with no more than three core members participating in the defense, and may invite up to two faculty or external advisers.

Teams must submit an entrepreneurship plan. Online registration runs from **November 23, 2012 through March 23, 2013**, at <http://gwy.fuzhou.gov.cn/cyds>; each team may enter one project. After the registration is approved, teams must print the form online and complete the required signatures. Five paper copies of the entrepreneurship plan and the original registration form must be delivered or mailed to the organizing office by **March 31**, and the electronic plan must also be uploaded to the registration system.

### Schedule and awards

The competition has preliminary and final rounds. From April through May 2013, an expert panel will review the written plans and select the **top 50 teams** for the final round. Finalists will present and defend their projects, and the top six teams will enter a public grand-final defense. The competition will award two special prizes, four first prizes, eight second prizes, and 16 third prizes, with awards of RMB 50,000, 30,000, 20,000, and 10,000 respectively.

The organizers will also connect promising projects with mentors, venture-capital institutions, university entrepreneurship parks, and incubators. Policy support may include startup funding, entrepreneurship-park space, technical platforms, and market-development opportunities. Out-of-town finalist teams, university leaders, and advisers may receive transport subsidies and accommodation under the event arrangements.

For registration questions, call `0591-83322607` or `0591-83337727`, or email `fzcyds@126.com`. The organizing office is at the Fuzhou Graduate Employment Guidance Center, second floor, Personnel Building, 12 Qianjie, Wuyi Xincun, Wuyi Zhong Road, Fuzhou, Fujian 350004.
