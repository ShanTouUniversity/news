The spring 2013 College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 exams will be held on **June 15**. Band 4 runs from **9:00 to 11:20 a.m.**, and Band 6 from **3:00 to 5:20 p.m.** Main-campus STU undergraduates may register online from **March 11 through 19**. The written-exam fee is **RMB 36 per person per exam**.

### Eligibility

Band 4 is open to undergraduates placed in English Level 2 or above this semester. Arts undergraduates must have completed ELC EA3 or passed the English Application Ability Test. Students in the 2012 cohort of English majors may also register.

Band 6 applicants must hold a Band 4 certificate or a Band 4 score of at least 425; hold an English major Band 4 certificate; or hold a Band 6 certificate or score of at least 425 and be applying to retake Band 6 no more than twice.

### Registration and payment

Undergraduates should log in to the Credit-Based Education website and complete **Course Selection → Eligibility Examination Registration**. The Finance Office, through the Network Center, will deduct the fee from registered students’ campus-card accounts on **March 20**. If that deduction fails, the Finance Office will arrange a second deduction from the tuition account on the morning of **March 21**. Students should ensure sufficient balances in the relevant accounts; failed payment may result in registration failure.

Non-enrolled students may not register. Candidates must bring their identity card, student card, and admission ticket to the exam; anyone missing one of these documents will not be admitted.
