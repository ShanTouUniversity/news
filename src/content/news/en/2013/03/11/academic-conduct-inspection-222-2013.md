Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office has released Bulletin No. 222 on academic-conduct inspections in Week 2 of the 2012–2013 spring semester. The inspections covered Building E.

| Inspection | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| March 4, 2:00 p.m. | 3 | 1 |
| March 8, 8:00 a.m. | 2 | 2 |

The two inspections recorded **eight late arrivals** in total. The bulletin asks relevant schools and colleges to provide criticism and education to students who did not follow university rules and to continue strengthening academic-conduct development.
