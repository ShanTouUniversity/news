The Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee of Shantou University’s School of Engineering has completed its review for the 2012 National Graduate Scholarship and proposed **eight graduate students** as recipients. The results are now being publicized to all Engineering graduate students.

Comments should be submitted in writing to the School of Engineering’s 2012 Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee by **5:00 p.m. on March 17**. Materials submitted by organizations must bear an official seal, while individuals must sign with their real names. The office is in Gongnan 301. The contact number is `86504586`, and the email address is `xjhuang@stu.edu.cn`.
