Shantou University’s Resource Management Office asks relevant staff members to collect their health-insurance manuals and medical-insurance IC cards from the Staff Medical Insurance Office on the second floor of the University Hospital during normal working hours. The notice concerns **six staff members**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ The original notice contains staff names and insurance information. The list is not reproduced here; relevant staff should follow the internal collection notice.
