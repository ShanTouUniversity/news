The Shantou Youth League Committee will recognize outstanding Youth League cadres, outstanding Youth League members, May Fourth Red Flag Youth League committees, and May Fourth Red Flag Youth League branches during the 2013 May Fourth period. STU’s Youth League Committee has forwarded the call and opened internal recommendations through the Youth League committees of its schools and colleges and its directly affiliated branches.

### Recommendation scope

Outstanding Youth League cadres must have served in a grassroots Youth League position for at least one year and demonstrate strong ethics, performance, and organizational work. Outstanding Youth League members must fulfill their duties and show strong academic or professional performance and must be under 28 as of March 2013. May Fourth Red Flag committees and branches will be assessed on leadership, branch development, member education and management, themed activities, organizational systems, activity venues, and funding.

Under the allocation issued by the Shantou Youth League Committee, STU, including STU Medical College, may recommend two outstanding cadres, six outstanding members, two May Fourth Red Flag Youth League committees, and two May Fourth Red Flag branches. Recommendations must follow the assigned quotas and eligibility requirements.

Units must submit recommended candidates and related materials to the STU Youth League Committee office by **March 17** and send electronic files to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. After review and consolidation, the relevant forms, materials, and electronic files must be submitted to the Organization Department of the Shantou Youth League Committee by **March 20**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University Committee)
