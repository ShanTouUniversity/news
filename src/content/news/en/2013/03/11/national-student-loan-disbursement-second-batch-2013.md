Following bank approval, **48 students** were included in the second batch of Shantou University’s 2012–2013 national student-loan disbursements. The loans will offset tuition and accommodation fees. Any remaining balance will be deposited into the recipient’s personal Bank of China account under the applicable arrangements.

Recipients may log in to the Student Affairs System and check “My Information → My Personal Information → Tuition and Miscellaneous Fee Payment” to review their individual payment status. Students should pay any remaining balance through the Finance Office. For questions, contact the Student Financial Aid Management Center at `86504749`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
