To improve equipment and materials procurement and reduce costs, Shantou University’s Equipment and Materials Management Office will introduce an online bidding system. Once active, **small purchases below RMB 20,000** may be submitted online directly by each unit and purchased after online review by the Equipment Office, Finance Office, and Supervision and Audit Office, shortening the approval process.

Each school, department, and administrative unit must recommend **one equipment-procurement coordinator** to work with the Equipment and Materials Management Office on routine small purchases. Units must submit the coordinator’s name and contact details by **March 15** to the Equipment and Materials Management Office in the former reception room.

For questions, call `86502558`, contact Huang, or email `zxhuang@stu.edu.cn`.
