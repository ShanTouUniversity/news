The 2013 CCS Whole-Person Life Education lecture series will present **“My Social-Work Dream: Twenty-Five Years of Participation in Social-Work Development in Mainland China”** at **7:30 p.m. on Friday, March 15**, in the Library Lecture Hall. Ruan Zengyuanqi, vice president of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, will share her experience supporting social-work education and professional services in mainland China, as well as the motivations and life reflections behind that work.

Ruan has been involved in social-work education since the 1980s. In 1988, she began working with Peking University to promote the development of social work in China, and in 2000 she helped establish the first master’s program in social work on the mainland. She has served as president of the International Association of Schools of Social Work, was appointed a Justice of the Peace in Hong Kong, and received the Bronze Bauhinia Star.

The lecture also forms part of the continuing-education course “Public Lectures of the Centre for Christian Studies” at the College of Liberal Arts, and students enrolled in that course should attend. Faculty and students may reserve seats at <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm> by **noon on March 15**. Seating is limited and will be assigned in reservation order.
