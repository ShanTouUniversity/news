It is reported that the first half of the 2013 National Computer Rank Examination will be held on March 30. To verify the operation of the exam software and system environment and ensure a smooth official test, Shantou University will conduct a pre-exam mock test on Wednesday, March 13, in computer Room E101.

The mock test is divided into two sessions by exam format:

| Time | Test Format | Levels |
| ---- | ----------- | ------ |
| March 13, 14:30-15:30 | Traditional format test | Level 1 & Level 3 |
| March 13, 15:30-16:30 | Paperless format test | Level 2 |

Students registered for the examination are reminded to attend the mock test at the designated computer room on time and familiarize themselves with the operating procedures of their respective exam formats in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
