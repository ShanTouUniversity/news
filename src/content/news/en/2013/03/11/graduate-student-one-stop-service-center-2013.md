Shantou University’s Graduate School has established a one-stop student affairs service center for all graduate students enrolled at the main campus. The center will support students facing difficulties in personal, academic, or daily life, provide guidance on university procedures, and help connect students with relevant offices. When necessary, it may work with the Mental Health and Education Counseling Center to provide individual consultation and psychological support.

The center promises courteous service without passing students from office to office or delaying responses. It will keep matters involving students’ personal safety and dignity confidential. Matters within the Graduate School’s authority that it can handle independently should be processed within **three working days**. Matters requiring other offices will be transferred or referred within three working days, while cases that do not comply with regulations or cannot be resolved will receive an explanation within three working days.

The Graduate School will hold an introductory session at **3:00 p.m. on March 13** in classroom G401. Graduate students are welcome to attend. During normal working hours, students may visit the Graduate School Student Affairs Office, call `86504000`, or email `ASC@stu.edu.cn`. The center also provides a blog at <http://blog.sina.com.cn/u/3202511384> and a Weibo channel.

The notice body lists the office as Room 401 of the New Administration Center, while the attached charter lists Room 405. The notice gives QQ group `295450987`, while the charter gives `295420987`. These details conflict; confirm the current address and group number with the Graduate School before using them.
