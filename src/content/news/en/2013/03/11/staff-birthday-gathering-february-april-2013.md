With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, Shantou University’s Trade Union will host a birthday gathering for staff members born in February, March, and April at the western restaurant of Junhua Hotel at **6:30 p.m. on Sunday, March 24**. Staff members celebrating birthdays that month may register voluntarily, while other staff may attend as friends or colleagues; family members may participate with tickets.

Reservations run from **noon on March 11 to noon on March 18** through the STU reservation center at <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/>. Campus-card deductions will take place from March 18 to 20, and tickets may be collected from **9:30 a.m. to noon on March 22**. After the system closes, reservations cannot be canceled and payments will not be refunded. Failed campus-card deductions or incomplete cash payments will be treated as withdrawal.

Under the event plan, the cash price for an adult ticket or registration is RMB 110, or RMB 100 when paid by campus card. Children’s tickets cost RMB 60 in cash or RMB 55 by campus card. Staff members celebrating birthdays that month may bring a spouse and one child under 12 free of charge; other relatives, friends, or colleagues must purchase tickets. No university transport will be provided, so participants should arrange their own travel and take care.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union)
>
> ⚠️ Cash payments must follow the Trade Union’s designated procedure. The university bank-account details in the notice are not published here.
