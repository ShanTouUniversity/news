Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design will host **“An Overview of Commercial Animation Production Lines: The Contemporary U.S. Animation Industry as a Case Study”** from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on March 14** in the school’s Lecture Theater. Faculty and students across the university are welcome.

The speaker, Wang He, graduated from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and is currently a project manager at Capstone Entertainment in the United States. He will introduce commercial animation production lines and related workflows through the contemporary U.S. animation industry.
