With support from the Resource Management Office, the Communist Youth League of Shantou University Committee, the Youth Volunteer Association, and the Green Ribbon service-learning environmental volunteer team will hold a series of tree-planting activities from **March 13 through 16** under the theme **“March Rendezvous, Boundless Green, Boundless Love.”** The campaign promotes volunteer service and a greener campus.

### Campus tree planting

From 4:00 to 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 13, the Youth Volunteer Association will organize STU volunteers and students from STU Affiliated Middle School to plant trees beside the campus artificial lake. The activity has 46 volunteer places. Registration closes at **11:00 p.m. on March 12**, with places assigned in registration order. Results will be announced by text message, telephone, and the volunteer section of the BBS.

### Recycling for potted plants

From 10:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. on Saturday, March 16, the Youth Volunteer Association will hold “Turn Waste into Greenery, Refresh the Dormitories” at the Golden Triangle. Students may exchange any five items from waste cardboard, beverage bottles, or used batteries for one potted plant. Each person may receive one plant; supplies are limited and will be distributed first come, first served. A campus card or other identification is required for registration at exchange.

Collected cardboard and bottles will be sold for recycling, with all proceeds donated to the Firefly Charity Foundation. Used batteries will be transferred to Huanrong for environmentally safe processing.
