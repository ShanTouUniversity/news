Shantou University will hold its **14th Student Sports Festival from March 23 to April 28, 2013**, at sports venues across campus. The festival is organized by the Physical Education Department and co-organized by the Zhicheng College, School of Engineering, and School of Law student unions and relevant student sports clubs. It aims to enrich campus sports culture, promote mass participation in sports, and improve competitive performance.

### Events

The festival features 12 events: tug-of-war, 20×50-meter relay, 10×800-meter relay, martial arts, roller skating, shuttlecock, basketball, volleyball, football, table tennis, badminton, and tennis.

Teams may represent the School of Liberal Arts, School of Science, School of Engineering, Medical College, School of Law, Business School, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Graduate School, or Zhicheng College. All registered full-time STU undergraduates and graduate students who are healthy and fit for their chosen events may enter through their school or college. Students residing in Zhicheng College may represent only Zhicheng College, while students not selected by Zhicheng College may not enter on behalf of another unit.

### Scoring and awards

The top three places in each event will receive awards, as will the top eight delegations by total score. Basketball, volleyball, football, and the 10×800-meter relay award 18, 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, and 2 points to the top eight places. Other events award 9, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1 points. In a tie, the tied places share the average of the corresponding points. If delegations remain tied on total points, rankings will be decided by the number of first-place finishes, then second-place finishes, and so on.

Participating units should organize registration according to the detailed rules for each event issued by the Physical Education Department.
