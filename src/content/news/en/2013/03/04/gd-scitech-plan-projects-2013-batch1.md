It is reported that, in accordance with the Notice on Organizing Applications for the First Batch of 2013 Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Program Projects (Yue Ke Han Gui Hua Zi [2013] No. 186) jointly issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and the Provincial Department of Finance, applications for the first batch of 2013 provincial sci-tech program projects have opened. The Research Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a notice organizing applications from relevant university units.

## Project Categories

This batch covers two types of special-fund projects:

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Guangdong Provincial Industry-Academia-Research (Provincial-Ministerial) Cooperation Special Fund Projects | For provincial-ministerial industry-academia-research cooperation |
| Guangdong Provincial CAS Comprehensive Strategic Cooperation Special Fund Competitive Allocation Projects | For comprehensive strategic cooperation with the Chinese Academy of Sciences |

## Application Limits

Under the rules, project leaders with more than 2 ongoing provincial sci-tech program projects may in principle not apply for new projects; a single leader may in principle apply for only 1 new project in 2013.

## Key Dates

Applicants must submit their application forms and feasibility reports through the business management system website of the Provincial Department of Science and Technology, keeping these deadlines in mind:

| Date | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| By March 27 | Submit application forms and attachments online; notify the Research Affairs Office for review |
| By April 5 | Re-upload revised applications based on review feedback |
| By April 9 | Submit two printed copies of the application form to the Research Affairs Office |

## Application Notes

- Applicants with existing system accounts may log in with them; first-time applicants should email their name, unit, and project category to the Research Affairs Office to obtain an account and password.
- Attachments must follow the specific guidelines of each project category, generally including the university's legal-person certificate and organization code certificate, cooperation agreements, funding commitment letters, patent certificates, novelty search reports, testing reports, and research capability credentials.
- Applications jointly submitted with external units require signed cooperation agreements; projects with partner units must bear the partners' official seals.

The Research Affairs Office will review applications online; those that do not pass will be returned with revision suggestions, and applicants are advised to track their application status closely.
