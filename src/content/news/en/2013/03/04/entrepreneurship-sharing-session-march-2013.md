Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will host an entrepreneurship-sharing session from **3:30 to 4:30 p.m. on March 4** in Science Lecture Hall 205. The session is open to the university community and will feature Li Longmei and Zhang Jihong discussing technology research and development, business management, and the commercialization of scientific and technological achievements.

Li Longmei has research and management experience in mechanical design, rapid prototyping, reverse engineering, and computer-integrated systems. She has participated in Canadian natural-sciences and engineering projects and international corporate collaborations and previously served as a senior technology vice president at a major enterprise. Zhang Jihong has worked for many years on land-based positioning, GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, and BeiDou satellite-navigation technologies, including research and development of vehicle autonomous-navigation equipment, products, and industrial applications.
