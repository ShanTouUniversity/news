It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee forwarded a notice on March 4, 2013, on extensively carrying out the campaign to learn from Luo Yang, an outstanding member of the Communist Party of China.

Party general branches and directly affiliated branches are asked to study and implement the important instructions of General Secretary Xi Jinping, mobilize Party members and teachers and students to carry out the learning campaign, and report the progress to the Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department at o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. The full notice and attachment are available on the OA system.
