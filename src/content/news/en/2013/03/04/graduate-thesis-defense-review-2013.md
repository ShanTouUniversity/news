It is reported that the Graduate School issued Document Shan Da Yan Zi [2013] No. 01 on March 4 to arrange the 2013 thesis defense and degree conferral review for graduate students, requiring all graduate training units to strictly ensure training quality and complete the defense and review in accordance with regulations.

## Key Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Issuing unit | Graduate School |
| Issue date | March 4, 2013 |
| Target groups | All graduate training units, including doctoral and master's candidates and those applying for master's degrees with equivalent academic qualifications |
| Basis | Program training plans, individual training plans and university degree regulations |

## Five Requirements

| No. | Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ensure training quality | Units should strictly follow training plans and individual plans |
| 2 | Clarify application procedures | Procedures are detailed in Attachments 1 and 2; candidates must meet university and discipline-specific requirements before applying for defense |
| 3 | Follow the schedule | Progress for defense and review is shown in Attachments 3 and 4 |
| 4 | Standardize application materials | Catalog is in Attachment 5; application materials are long-term archives and must be filled out accurately by the student, supervisor, secretary and academic staff without omissions or alterations and submitted on time, otherwise they cannot be submitted to the University Degree Committee, with responsibility borne by relevant personnel |
| 5 | Deadline for equivalent-qualification applicants | Those applying with equivalent academic qualifications must submit defense applications to the Graduate School by April 15, 2013, in accordance with the *Implementation Rules for Conferring Master's Degrees on Persons with Equivalent Academic Qualifications* and related notices |

## Attachments

The notice includes five attachments covering procedures, schedules and material catalogs. Please refer to the OA system for the full documents.
