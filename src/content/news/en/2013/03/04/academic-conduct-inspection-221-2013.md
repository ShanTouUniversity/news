Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office released Academic Conduct Inspection Bulletin No. 221, reporting the results of teaching-building inspections during Week 1 of the 2012–2013 spring semester.

| Inspection time | Late by up to 5 minutes | Late by more than 5 minutes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2:00 p.m., February 26 | 4 | 1 |
| 10:00 a.m., February 28 | 12 | 3 |

The two inspections recorded **20 instances** of late arrival. The bulletin asked the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to students who failed to follow university rules and to continue strengthening the academic atmosphere.
