Shantou University will hold its **2012 assessment and democratic review of the university leadership team and cadres at 3:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 6**, in the Science and Technology Center Lecture Hall. The process follows the requirements of the Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee for annual assessments of university leadership teams and cadres and will be conducted together with the “one report, two evaluations” review of cadre appointments.

The leadership team’s work summary, cadre performance reports, and performance reports from newly appointed cadres have been posted on the university’s internal office information network. Comments and suggestions concerning the leadership team and cadres may be sent to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` by the end of the working day on **Thursday, March 7**.

Participants include university leaders, cadres at or above the middle-management level, representatives of senior professional staff and the Party and faculty congresses from the main-campus schools, principal representatives of democratic parties, retired staff representatives, the presidents of the Student Union and Graduate Student Union, and relevant representatives from the Medical College and affiliated hospital. Participating units must submit their attendee lists to the Party Organization and United Front Department by the end of the morning on **March 5** and ask attendees to arrive 15 minutes early.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Department)
