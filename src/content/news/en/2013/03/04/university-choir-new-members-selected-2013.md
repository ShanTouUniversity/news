The Shantou University Choir has completed its new-member auditions, selecting **10 students** to join the choir. The selection considered choral-form requirements, the choir’s needs, and the vocal qualities of the applicants.

The selected students should gather at **1:50 p.m. on Wednesday, March 6**, in the choir rehearsal room on the first floor of the Arts Education Center, the same location where the auditions were held.

The choir thanked all students who auditioned and encouraged them to continue pursuing their passion for music.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Arts Education Center)
