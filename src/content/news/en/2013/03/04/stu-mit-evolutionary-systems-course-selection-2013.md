Shantou University and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) will launch a joint exchange-course project in April and May 2013. The pilot course, **“Evolutionary Processes and Systems, Pilot Learning Module,”** will be taught in English, with its main instruction scheduled for **May 6–10, Week 11**, over one week. Students who complete the course will receive one academic credit and an achievement certificate. They may also have the opportunity to join a research project conducted by STU and MIT Professor Una-May O’Reilly on medical diagnosis and prediction using evolutionary algorithms and data mining.

### Selection

The course will select **20 students** from the first- and second-year cohorts of STU’s Schools of Science and Engineering. Applicants should have strong English grades, speaking and writing skills, and a foundation in scientific English, as well as good App Inventor programming skills. They should also have sufficient time for the course, good academic performance, teamwork skills, and strong communication abilities.

STU faculty will offer three or four lectures on evolutionary algorithms in April. An MIT professor will teach the main course from May 6 to 10. With approval from the Teaching Office, selected students may request leave from other classes during Week 11 to attend the program.

The university will conduct an initial screening, identify candidates for interviews, and make final selections based on interview results. Applications are due by **5:00 p.m. on Friday, March 8**. Interested students must complete the application form and email it to `jzhang@stu.edu.cn`.

### Selection result

Following English interviews and review of students’ semester GPAs, the university selected **25 students** for the exchange course. Selected students will first study App Inventor and evolutionary algorithms and await further training arrangements. The names in the selection list are not reproduced here.
