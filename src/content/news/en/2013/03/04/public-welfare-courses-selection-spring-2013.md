Beginning with the 2012 cohort, public-service courses became a compulsory general-education requirement for Shantou University undergraduates. The university is offering 11 courses with **606 places** in spring 2013. The courses are classified under Philosophy and Social Sciences and carry one credit after successful completion. Each course consists of 48 hours covering service-learning study, training, public-service practice, reflection and sharing, and assessment.

Courses are scheduled mainly on Wednesday afternoons or weekends during the spring semester. Service groups, schedules, and assessment requirements vary. Students should read the relevant syllabus before selecting a course and consider whether they can meet the time commitment. The course themes include work with rural children, rural health promotion, educational support, traffic-quality improvement, community services, legal practice, marine conservation, and public-welfare photography.

### Enrollment schedule

- **Initial selection**: 9:00 a.m. March 5 to 9:00 a.m. March 6;
- **Add/drop period**: 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. on March 7. No enrollment or schedule-change requests will be accepted afterward;
- **How to enroll**: Log in to the credit-based academic system, go to “Guided Courses” and “Select a Topic,” and choose an available topic beginning with course code `SER` for the 2012–2013 spring semester;
- **Selection rules**: Places are assigned first come, first served. Each student may select one course. Students already selected through interviews should not enroll in this round.

Students who enroll but fail to participate as scheduled will receive a zero under the unified assessment rules. Course information is available at <http://gykc.stu.edu.cn>. For enrollment questions, contact the Teaching Office at `86502416` or `86503202`.
