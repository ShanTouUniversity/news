It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on March 4, 2013, to announce the appointments for Graduate Assistantship (“San Zhu”) positions for the spring 2013 semester.

Submitted by appointing units and reviewed by the Graduate School, the appointment list has been finalized and is withheld here to protect student privacy. Details are available in the OA attachment. Appointing units are asked to manage, supervise and evaluate the appointed graduate students in accordance with university regulations.

> ⚠️ The appointment list is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
