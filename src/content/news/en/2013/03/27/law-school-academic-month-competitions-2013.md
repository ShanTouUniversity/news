Shantou University’s Law School will hold its 15th Academic Month from **March through April**, featuring research-design and paper-writing competitions for all students. The program promotes social-science research and extracurricular academic activities.

### Research-design competition

Research-design entries may be submitted individually or by teams. Topics may address society, law, management, economics or education and should have practical value and significance while demonstrating scientific, advanced and original thinking. Eligible formats include outlines, plans and research designs for social investigations or academic papers.

Submit one electronic and two paper copies of the research-design application by **April 18**. Paper entries must be reviewed and signed by a faculty adviser. The competition offers first, second and third prizes and an excellence award, with first-, second- and third-prize places accounting for approximately 10%, 20% and 30% of entries. Research-design entries may also enter the technology-project program, with research-funding support from the school. Projects earning second prize or above may be recommended for STU’s undergraduate extracurricular academic and technology project program; third-prize projects will enter the Law School’s academic-technology program.

### Paper-writing competition

Papers should address a topic related to the student’s major, be approximately **3,000 Chinese characters** and include an abstract of approximately **300 Chinese characters**. Follow the original OA attachments for formatting and activity rules. Submit the required electronic and paper copies by **April 18**, with the faculty adviser’s review and signature. The competition offers the same prize categories, and outstanding papers will be compiled.

Obtain the submission email, collection locations and contact information from the Law School’s original OA attachments.
