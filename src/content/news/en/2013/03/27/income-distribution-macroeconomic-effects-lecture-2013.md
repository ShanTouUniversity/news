The third 2013 academic lecture of Shantou University’s Business School will be held from **10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on March 29** in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of Wenxi. Dr. Songtao Wang of South China Normal University will speak on “A Study of the Macroeconomic Effects of Income Distribution among Chinese Residents.” Professor Zhong Qin will chair the lecture.

The talk will introduce a framework for analyzing how income distribution affects macroeconomic indicators and use it to examine the theoretical and empirical effects of inequality in China’s resident income distribution on aggregate consumption, consumption structure, national labor income and national welfare.

Dr. Wang’s research focuses on macroeconomic theory and policy. He has published more than 20 papers in related fields and participated in numerous externally funded and commissioned projects. The lecture is open to the entire university community.
