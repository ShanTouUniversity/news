Shantou University’s School of Engineering will hold an Alumni Face-to-Face career-planning forum from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on March 29** in E Lecture Room 203. With the theme “Reflecting on Personal Growth,” the event will feature alumni Songjiang Lin and Yunfei Peng from Architectural Engineering and Shaoneng Chen from Electronic Information Engineering.

Lin graduated from STU’s Architectural Engineering program in 1993 and is now general manager and design director of Shantou Anjian Decoration Co., with qualifications as a national first-class registered constructor and senior engineer. Peng graduated from Architectural Engineering in 1995 and is president of design at Pengxiao International Construction Design Co., with national first-class registered architect, second-class registered structural engineer and senior-architect qualifications. Chen graduated from Electronic Information Engineering in 1995 and is general manager of Guangzhou Hongshi Electronic Technology Co., working in video-surveillance technology research and the production and sales of related products.

Reservations have closed. The forum will still be held at **7:30 p.m. on March 29** in E Lecture Room 203. Faculty members and students with confirmed reservations should arrive 10 minutes early; others should follow on-site arrangements.
