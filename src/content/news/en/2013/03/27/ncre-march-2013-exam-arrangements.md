The first-half 2013 National Computer Rank Examination will be held on **Saturday, March 30**. STU’s Academic Affairs Office has issued the following arrangements:

| Component | Time and location |
| --- | --- |
| Written test | From 9:00 a.m. on March 30; follow the assigned room arrangement |
| Computer test | From 12:30 p.m. on March 30 in batches in E101, according to the admission-ticket time |
| Computer-test seat draw | Gather in E204 30 minutes before the computer test to draw seat numbers |

Level 2 uses a paperless computer examination lasting 120 minutes for 100 points: 40 points for multiple-choice questions and 60 for practical computer tasks. A total of 60 points earns a passing certificate; candidates who do not earn one must register as new candidates for the next examination. Level 1 is computer-based, Level 3 combines written and computer tests, and Level 4 is written.

Candidates must bring their identity card and admission ticket and place them at the upper-left corner of their desks for inspection. Written-test candidates arriving more than 15 minutes late may not enter; candidates may leave only after 45 minutes. Late arrivals may not enter once the computer test has formally begun. Written-test candidates should bring a 2B pencil, eraser and a blue- or black-ink pen. Books, communication devices and other prohibited items may not enter the examination room.

STU undergraduate students may collect admission tickets from their school or college academic administrators. Other candidates should bring their identity cards to the Student Affairs window of the Academic Affairs Office. For questions, call `86502402`. Candidates should read the examination-room rules in the OA attachment before the test.
