Shantou University Library has issued its third 2013 electronic-resources bulletin, highlighting database training and trial resources to support literature searches, academic writing and research-information analysis.

### Online courses

The **2013 spring Web of Knowledge online lecture series** runs from March 21 to June 25 with five topics and 24 sessions covering:

- Topic selection, proposal development, citation analysis, ESI subject evaluation and trend analysis with Web of Science;
- Search-query writing and life-science and engineering-information research in specialist databases;
- English academic writing, submission strategies and EndNote-assisted writing;
- Using information resources for funding applications and preparation for overseas study;
- Conference literature, patent information and patent-map processes for engineering and applied research.

Course details are available at <http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/2013WOKOnline/>.

The IEEE/IET Electronic Library (IEL) online classroom introduces journals, conference papers and standards in electronics, electrical engineering, communications and computer science. See the [IEEE Chinese website](http://cn.ieee.org/online_training.html) for details.

### EBSCO resources

Six EBSCO subject full-text databases covering arts, education, law, humanities, applied technology, and library and information science are available for trial on campus through **May 15**. Access them at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=151>.

EBSCO’s 2013 spring online training runs from March through June on Mondays and Thursdays at 7:00 p.m. Registration and the schedule are available at <https://ebsco-chinese.webex.com/>.
