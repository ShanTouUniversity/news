The Communist Party of China Shantou University Committee has released its 2013 theoretical-study plan. Beginning in **March**, the Party Committee’s central study group will generally address one topic each month, with adjustments based on developments and university needs. The Publicity Department will coordinate the program, while Party general branches and directly affiliated branches will implement it.

### Study priorities

The annual plan covers six areas:

1. Studying and implementing the spirit of the 18th National Party Congress, with outreach and research connected to higher education;
2. Studying the spirit of the annual national legislative and consultative sessions, education issues and domestic and international developments to support university reform;
3. Examining the socialist core value system and advancing teacher ethics, campus culture and moral education;
4. Continuing study of national and Guangdong long-term education-reform and development plans, implementing STU’s development plan and improving educational quality;
5. Advancing legal-education and Party-conduct and clean-government study, including education laws, law-based governance and integrity-risk prevention;
6. Exploring new approaches to Party affairs and ideological and political education, including grassroots organization building, Party-member development, work-style improvement and student development.

The plan calls for varied learning formats, including lectures, keynote presentations, discussion sessions and audiovisual education. Central study-group members should attend collective study sessions, keep study notes, prepare for discussions and connect theory with university and unit-level practice, problem solving and decision-making.

Party general branches and directly affiliated branches should also organize monthly study sessions for their members and faculty and staff, then summarize the semester’s activities and results.
