Guangdong Biosoul Health Technology Co., Ltd. in Shantou has announced university-industry research needs. The company develops natural functional health and cosmetic products based on herbal extracts and bioactive factors and is seeking cooperation on:

- **Tea oil**: Developing a method for complete emulsification and studying the stability of the tea-oil emulsion in shampoo;
- **Tea-seed extract**: Analyzing its composition and studying potential applications. The original notice uses the Chinese term “茶籽箍”; this article does not infer a more specific material name;
- **Chitin**: Studying and evaluating anti-radiation properties.

Interested schools or faculty members may contact the Technology Achievement and Development Section of STU’s Office of Research for coordination. Contacts are Luoyingguang and Deng Yong at `0754-86503271` or `ygluo@stu.edu.cn`. Company website: <http://www.biosoul.cc>.
