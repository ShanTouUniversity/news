Shantou University’s Working Committee for Caring for the Next Generation has published its 2013 priorities. Under the leadership of the university’s Party and administrative authorities, the committee will focus on youth development and moral education, strengthen university- and school-level committees, and expand related programs.

Key priorities include:

- Studying and implementing the spirit of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, conducting education on the socialist core value system and holding a related study session in **May**;
- Guiding student organizations and social-practice activities, encouraging students to read classic works, participating in the “Blue-and-Green Project,” and supporting professional-ethics development among young faculty members;
- Implementing guidance on work-with-the-next-generation committees in the national education system, involving more retired faculty members, strengthening school-level committees, continuing the “Five Good Committee Organization” initiative and gradually forming working groups;
- Supporting student Party affairs with school Party organizations through Party-education sessions, conversations with students, attendance at student branch meetings and assistance with branch activities;
- Organizing exchanges, including a visit to nearby universities in **May** and an experience-sharing meeting in **October**, where outstanding units and individuals will be recognized.
