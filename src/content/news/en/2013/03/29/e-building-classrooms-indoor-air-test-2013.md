STU’s Infrastructure Office reported that classrooms 203 and 204 in the U-shaped section of E Building passed completion acceptance for renovation on **February 28** and entered teaching use on **March 4**. After students reported an odor during classes, the university commissioned the Shantou Environmental Protection Monitoring Station to conduct indoor-air testing on **March 12**.

Technicians brought professional equipment to the site on **March 20**. The results showed that indoor **TVOC, formaldehyde, radon and electromagnetic radiation field strength** in both classrooms met the applicable standards. The full testing report is available in the original OA attachments.
