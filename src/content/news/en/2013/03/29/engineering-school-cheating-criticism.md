It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University has issued a college-wide public criticism of two graduate students who violated examination rules during a final exam.

According to the college's announcement, Jiang and Ou, both 2012-cohort graduate students from the Department of Electronic Engineering, were caught peeking at other candidates' answer sheets during the Graduate English Tier 1 final exam on January 6, 2013. Invigilators discovered the misconduct on the spot and confiscated their papers. The two students later acknowledged their mistakes and submitted written self-criticisms.

Given that the violation was relatively minor and the students showed sincere remorse, the college decided — in accordance with Article 5 of the Shantou University Regulations on Student Disciplinary Actions — to issue a college-wide public criticism rather than a heavier penalty.

The college urged all graduate students to take this case as a warning, strictly observe university regulations, and work together to foster a positive academic atmosphere.
