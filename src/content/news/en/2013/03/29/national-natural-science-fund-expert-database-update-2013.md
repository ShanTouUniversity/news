The 2013 National Natural Science Foundation of China review has entered its assessment stage. The foundation’s scientific departments will select external-review experts according to research fields, professional titles and funded projects. Project leaders with senior professional titles, prior National Natural Science Foundation funding and strong academic reputations are priority candidates.

Relevant project leaders should use their existing usernames and passwords to log in to the foundation’s ISIS system at <http://isisn.nsfc.gov.cn> and update their expert profiles by **April 20**. Key information includes a current email address, professional title, research fields and personal biography. Contact the STU Office of Research if the password has been forgotten.

STU contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at `0754-86502813`.
