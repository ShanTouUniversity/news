The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host an exhibition of work from its **99 Cents Design Challenge** workshop at **7:00 p.m. on March 31** in the school’s Art Museum. The workshop is led by Brian, and faculty members and students in industrial and graphic design are invited to attend.
