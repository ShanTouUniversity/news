The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host a lecture titled “User-Experience Processes and Applications” at **7:30 p.m. on March 31** in the Art and Design College Lecture Classroom. Guest speaker **雅寅耳** will discuss iterative development in user research, interaction design and visual design. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

雅寅耳 holds a master’s degree in social and cultural psychology from the University of Chicago and is the user-experience director and Beijing office director of Tang Consulting. Her work covers end-to-end user-experience solutions for domestic and international projects involving e-commerce, websites, mobile communications, software, medical devices and automotive navigation systems.
