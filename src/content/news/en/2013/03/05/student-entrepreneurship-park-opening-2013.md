Shantou University’s Student Entrepreneurship Park will hold its opening ceremony at **4:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 6**, at the Student Entrepreneurship Park in Building 789, the former Sanlian Bookstore.

All student venture teams in the first group to receive support from the **2013 STU Entrepreneurship Fund** must attend. Faculty and students across the university are welcome to participate. For more information, contact Chen Zepeng at `86502256`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
