Following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University has launched an internal selection process for the **12th Five-Year national experimental-teaching demonstration-center program**. The initiative is intended to build on the university’s strengths and distinctive programs to strengthen practical education, students’ practical and innovation skills, and overall teaching quality.

### Priorities

The construction program will focus on integrating laboratory resources and teaching teams, establishing a clearly targeted experimental-teaching system with sound assessment, and strengthening interaction between teaching and research. It will also promote the transfer of research outcomes into laboratory instruction and explore cooperation among universities, research institutes, industries, and enterprises in talent development, linking professional experiments with research, engineering training, and social applications.

Applicants must already be provincial experimental-teaching demonstration centers or provincial construction units. They should have advanced teaching concepts, a clear reform plan, substantial practical resources, an established experimental team, an effective management system, and distinctive, strong results among comparable centers.

### Selection and submission

Eligible schools or departments may apply voluntarily. STU will organize expert review, select **one recommended unit**, publish the result internally, and submit it to the provincial education authorities. Units intending to apply should notify the Teaching Office by **March 8**.

Required materials include the national demonstration-center application form, supporting materials documenting the center’s overall profile, construction results, and management and operation, and a contact-information form for the selection process. Submit two A4 paper copies, printed double-sided and bound, and email the Word version to `yzchen@stu.edu.cn`. Materials are due to the Teaching Office by **March 21**. Applicants must also build an independent website covering the center’s application, management and operation, achievements, and construction results.

The Teaching Office contact is Chen Yangzhou at `0754-86503202` or `yzchen@stu.edu.cn`.
