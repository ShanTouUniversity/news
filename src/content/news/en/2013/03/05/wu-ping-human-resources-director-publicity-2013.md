Following review by the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee and approval from the Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee, the university has appointed **Wu Ping** as director of Human Resources for a **six-month probationary period**.

Wu holds a doctoral degree and is a professor. She taught at STU’s School of Science for many years and served as director of its Department of Physics. Before the appointment, she was deputy director of Human Resources and had served as acting head of the office since January 2013.
