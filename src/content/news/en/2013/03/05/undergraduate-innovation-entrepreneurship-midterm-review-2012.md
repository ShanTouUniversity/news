Shantou University’s Teaching Office will soon conduct a midterm review of **undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects approved in 2012**. The review follows relevant Guangdong higher-education teaching-quality and reform-project requirements and STU’s project-approval notice, providing a basis for later research and completion assessments.

Each project team must submit:

- The *STU Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Project Midterm Review Form*;
- The *STU 2012 Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Project Progress Report*, including experiment records and meeting minutes kept according to the project’s actual progress;
- Materials describing interim results, such as papers or developed prototypes, with images and written explanations.

Teams must submit two paper copies and an electronic version. Forms and interim-results materials are due by **March 22** at the Teaching Office service window on the third floor of the New Administration Center. Teams may also contact Guo Yijia at `86502736` or `yjguo@stu.edu.cn`.

Projects that pass the midterm review will use the result as an important basis for completion assessment. Projects that fail must submit a written corrective-action plan by the specified deadline. They may continue implementation and use the second installment of project funding only after passing expert review of the corrective action; projects that remain below the required standard will be terminated.
