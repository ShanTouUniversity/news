Shantou University’s School of Engineering is organizing internal project selection for the MIT CDIO Academy, the National Undergraduate Energy Conservation and Emission Reduction Social Practice and Science and Technology Competition, and the Central and Southern China Undergraduate Mechanical Innovation Design and Manufacturing Competition. The selection will proceed through project design, production of shortlisted entries, and selection of outstanding works. The first-stage project-design defense will be held at **7:30 p.m. on March 7** in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room.

The selection offers four themes:

- **Mechanically powered lighting**: Build a lamp using mechanical structures that can operate without an external power source or battery, with an output of more than 5 W and dimensions no larger than 1.5 m × 1.5 m × 1 m. Entries will be judged primarily by lighting duration.
- **Rescue robot for fire scenes**: The second assigned problem of the 2013 Central and Southern China, Hong Kong, and Macao Undergraduate Mechanical Innovation Design and Manufacturing Competition.
- **Pendulum-and-ball track “perpetual-motion device”**: The first assigned problem of the same competition.
- **Low-cost, environmentally friendly mechanical design and manufacturing**: The form of the work is open, encouraging students to consider resource and environmental challenges and develop innovation and engineering-practice skills.

Students who registered for the selection during the previous semester should attend the defense on time. On-site registration and defenses will also be accepted.
