The 71st Party basic-knowledge study class at Shantou University is scheduled to begin in **March**. The Party Organization and United Front Department has asked Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to recommend candidates, conduct preliminary reviews, and submit the required materials.

Candidates must have submitted a Party-membership application at least one year earlier and have been designated as applicants receiving key development support. After review by the relevant Party organization, the application form must be signed and stamped and submitted collectively to the Party School Office in Room 315 of the New Administration Center by **March 14**. The consolidated student list should also be emailed to `o_dxb@stu.edu.cn`. The Party School Office will conduct a further review before confirming the final class list.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Department)
