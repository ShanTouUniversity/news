To recognize graduate students who performed strongly in academic activities and competitions, Shantou University’s Graduate School reviewed recommendations from graduate-training units and recognized students with outstanding results in **domestic and international academic activities and competitions during 2012**.

The recognition includes **three first prizes, one second prize, and 14 third prizes**. The Graduate School previously published the relevant list for public comment from **March 5 to 8**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
>
> ⚠️ The published list contains student names and is not reproduced here. Consult the original OA attachment for the list.
