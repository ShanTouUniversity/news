The National Association for Research on Ideological and Political Education in Higher Education and the editorial office of *Research on Ideological Education* will jointly hold a national forum on innovation and development in university ideological and political education in **mid-May**. With the theme “Comprehensively Improving the Quality of Ideological and Political Education for University Students,” STU’s Publicity Department is inviting papers from the university community.

Topics include cultivating and practicing core socialist values, innovation in ideological and political education in an internationalized environment, interdisciplinary research, the influence of social trends and responses, online ideological and political education, mechanisms for fostering virtue through education, the professionalization of counselor teams, approaches to “the Chinese Dream” education, and new methods, platforms, and approaches shaped by changes in the educational environment.

Papers should generally be about 6,000 Chinese characters and include an author biography and detailed contact information, following the submission requirements of *Research on Ideological Education*. Email electronic manuscripts to `xcb@stu.edu.cn` by **April 5**. File names should follow the format “征文+author name+full article title.” Submit two printed copies to Room 315, New Administration Center.

STU’s Publicity Department will recommend five papers for submission to the forum. The organizers will select outstanding papers based on quality and send formal participation notices to their authors. Selected papers will be compiled for publication, and particularly original papers may receive priority consideration for publication in *Research on Ideological Education*.
