Shantou University Library has begun collecting **2012 library-materials statistics** from reference rooms on the main campus. Each reference room should use the attached template to report new additions during 2012 and cumulative holdings by document type as of **December 31, 2012**.

Categories include Chinese books, foreign-language books, bound Chinese periodicals, bound foreign-language periodicals, and other materials. Quantities should be reported in volumes or items. Completed electronic forms are due by **March 15** and should be emailed to `gnweng@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Library)
>
> ⚠️ The statistics template is available in the original OA attachment. Internal form-filler and telephone information is not reproduced here.
