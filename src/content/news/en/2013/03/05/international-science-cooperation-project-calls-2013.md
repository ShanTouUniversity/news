Shantou University’s Research Office has circulated recent calls from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology for international science and technology cooperation proposals. The calls are intended for faculty members and research teams interested in the relevant partnerships.

| Program | Internal submission deadline | Public notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2013 China–Netherlands industrial innovation cooperation program (PoIC) | April 18 | [View notice](http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/1362109525187-5161487353502861713.html) |
| 46th meeting of the China–North Korea intergovernmental science and technology cooperation committee | March 13 | [View notice](http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/1362109524080-1186168068766414514.html) |
| 10th China–Singapore joint research program | April 18 | [View notice](http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/13623691321201828845830697009405.html) |
| Seventh China–South Africa joint research program exchange projects | April 18 | [View notice](http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/13623691308068755529540032705557.html) |

Prospective applicants should review the requirements in each program notice and contact the Research Office before the relevant internal deadline. Contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at `86502813`.
