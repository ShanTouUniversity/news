Shantou University’s Teaching Office asks **2014 undergraduate graduates** to log in to the China Higher-education Student Information and Career Center website (CHSI, <http://www.chsi.com.cn>) and verify their degree-registration photographs and related enrollment information.

### Verification schedule

Main-campus undergraduates who had their graduation images collected on campus during the previous semester must complete online verification and corrections by **March 31, 2013**. Students who fail to verify by the deadline and later discover errors in their photographs will be responsible for the resulting consequences.

Students who had their photographs taken at a non-STU collection site and are graduating this year must also complete the online verification after receiving their photographs and submit the printed photographs to the Teaching Office’s Student Affairs Section by **April 30**. Late submission may affect diploma production.

Students must create a real-name CHSI account before using the image-verification function, carefully check the displayed photograph and enrollment information, and submit their verification result. Registration involves personal information such as identity-document details, email addresses, and passwords; students should verify these details themselves and protect their account credentials. If a photograph is incorrect, contact Xu at the Teaching Office’s Student Affairs Section at `86502623` so the office can review and correct the record before the student verifies it again.

The notice gives “March 31, 2012” for the in-person verification arrangement for account problems, which conflicts with the notice year and the main-campus deadline of March 31, 2013. Students needing in-person assistance should confirm the exact date and hours with the Teaching Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office)
>
> ⚠️ The verification process involves personal identity and account information. Use CHSI and official Teaching Office channels, and do not submit or reproduce identity documents on public pages.
