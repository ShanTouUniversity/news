According to the 2012 list released by the Guangdong Academic Degrees Committee and Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, **four master’s theses by Shantou University graduates** were named outstanding master’s theses in Guangdong.

The recognized work covers inorganic chemistry, marine biology, medicinal chemistry, and pharmacology:

- Design, synthesis, and characterization of phenanthroimidazole derivatives and their applications in organic electroluminescent devices, supervised by Tong Qingxiao;
- A preliminary study of the mechanism behind carotenoid enrichment in orange individuals of *Chlamys nobilis*, supervised by Zheng Huaiping;
- Star-block copolymer carriers for drugs and insulin and pH-sensitive release, supervised by Liu Daojun;
- Extraction of volatile components from black coral, also known as “sea willow,” and their biological activity, supervised by Shi Ganggang.
