Following arrangements by the Shantou branch of Bank of China, Shantou University will organize signing for the **second batch of 2012–2013 national student-loan contracts from 2:30 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 13**, in **Lecture Staircase E, Room 101**. Students concerned should attend at the time assigned to their school or college and sign their contracts with the bank.

| Arrival time | Schools and college |
| --- | --- |
| 2:20 p.m. | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, School of Law, School of Engineering |
| 3:20 p.m. | School of Science, Business School, School of Liberal Arts, Zhicheng College |

Students should bring their identity document, student card, Bank of China debit card, black pen, and other required materials. Witnessing teachers from each school or college will attend to witness the signing for their students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
