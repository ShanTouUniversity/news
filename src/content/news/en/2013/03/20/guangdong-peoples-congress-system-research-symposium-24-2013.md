The Guangdong People’s Congress System Research Association plans to hold its **24th symposium in the third quarter of 2013**. STU’s Organization and United Front Work Department is soliciting papers from researchers whose work is relevant to the symposium and will assist with recommendations.

Submit **seven paper copies** to the Organization and United Front Work Department by **April 20** and email an electronic copy to `o_tzb@stu.edu.cn`. Consult subsequent notices from the association and the OA attachments for the symposium’s detailed topics and submission requirements.
