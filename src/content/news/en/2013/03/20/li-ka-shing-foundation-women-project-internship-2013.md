STU’s Li Ka-shing Foundation China Women’s Project Office is recruiting **three student interns**:

- **Two research assistants**: Applicants should be responsible, conscientious, proactive, able to use statistical-analysis tools such as Excel and SPSS, and able to work independently. Duties include collecting and organizing project materials, analyzing data, and supporting project activities. Interns must work in the project office for at least eight hours per week;
- **One web-technology assistant**: Applicants should understand basic website administration and maintenance, be familiar with web design and graphics and text editing, and know HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Duties include daily back-end maintenance and updates for the project website and assistance with website design and development.

The internship location is Room 102, first floor, former Student Affairs Office. Submit the electronic version of the “Li Ka-shing Foundation China Women’s Project Office Student Intern Application Form” to `sphuo@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on March 26**. Compensation follows STU’s work-study position standards. Contact Ms. Huo at `86502694` or `sphuo@stu.edu.cn`.
