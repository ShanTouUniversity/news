Shantou University’s Office of International Exchange and Cooperation will continue organizing the **2013–2014 self-funded Taiwan study program**. The program is open to first- and second-year students enrolled at STU, except students in medicine. Applicants should have good academic performance, no failed courses in the previous year, and no university disciplinary record at or above the level of an official notice of criticism.

### Program information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Study period | September 2013 to June 2014, with an option to attend for one semester |
| Host universities | Chinese Culture University, I-Shou University and Chaoyang University of Technology in Taiwan; Chinese Culture University also accepts graduate research students from journalism and communication |
| Costs | Students are responsible for tuition, accommodation, living expenses, round-trip travel and other costs; see the program guide for details |
| Credits | STU recognizes courses taken abroad, and students may apply for course substitutions and credit transfer when the courses meet their STU program requirements |

Applicants should submit their materials to their school or department academic office by **March 28**. Each school will complete its recommendation process and send its recommendation list to the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at `icd@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on April 3**. Schools may set their own recommendation numbers based on teaching arrangements and applications, but recommendations should generally not exceed 25% of the students in the relevant major so that normal teaching is not disrupted.

For inquiries, call `86503520` or `86502316`. The host universities’ websites are [Chinese Culture University](http://www.pccu.edu.tw/), [I-Shou University](http://www.isu.edu.tw/) and [Chaoyang University of Technology](http://web.cyut.edu.tw/).
