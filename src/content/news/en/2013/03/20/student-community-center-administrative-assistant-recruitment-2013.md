STU’s Student Community Management Service Center, under the Resource Management Office, is recruiting **one administrative assistant** from the public. Applicants must hold at least a bachelor’s degree, be no older than 40, be in good health, have organizational, coordination, official-writing, and communication skills, be able to use office and drafting software, and be willing to work shifts.

Experience with office-network systems, construction engineering, property equipment maintenance and supervision, property administration, or ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 integrated-system certification and operation is preferred.

Responsibilities include assisting the center director with daily work arrangements and follow-up, handling routine matters and reporting conditions, checking cleaning and landscaping, drafting and organizing documents and work logs, collecting and addressing student comments and complaints, organizing weekly meetings, preparing staff shift schedules, tracking attendance, distributing documents, and coordinating with university offices.

The position is an auxiliary contract-worker appointment; the employment relationship will not transfer into STU, and compensation follows relevant university standards. Submit application materials by **March 31** by email or post to the Student Community Management Service Center. Candidates who pass the initial review will be invited to an interview.

Postal address: Student Community Management Service Center, Residence Hall C Office, Resource Management Office, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Mark submissions “Student Community Center Administrative Assistant.” Contact Ms. Lin at `0754-86502661` or `dorm@stu.edu.cn`, using the position title in the email subject.
