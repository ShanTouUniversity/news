STU Radio is recruiting students from all years for the following positions:

- Two broadcasters and four reporters for *Campus Focus*;
- Four male voice actors for the radio-drama group;
- Weibo administrators.

Broadcasters should enjoy observing and thinking, communicate well, and have reasonably standard Mandarin. Reporters should be responsible, logical, interested in interviews, strong writers, and available for campus reporting. Voice actors should have expressive voices, standard Mandarin, and some performance ability. Weibo administrators should be comfortable with online applications and online language and should be responsible.

Interviews will be held from **2:30 to 5:00 p.m. on Sunday, March 24** at the Radio Station Office, West 408, Old Administration Building. Students interested in interviewing should complete the application form and submit it according to the station’s notice by March 24. Selected members will gain a platform for campus-media practice, experience, and creative projects.
