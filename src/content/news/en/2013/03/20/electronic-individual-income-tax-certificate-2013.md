Shantou University’s Finance Office forwarded a notice from the local tax authorities about electronic individual income-tax payment certificates. Guangdong launched the service on **March 15, 2013**. Taxpayers can visit the Guangdong Local Taxation Bureau website at [www.gdltax.gov.cn](http://www.gdltax.gov.cn), enter the tax-service area through “Individual Login,” and then use the “Individual Income Tax Zone” to print a payment certificate and tax record.

The electronic certificate is generated from the provincial tax administration system and has the same evidentiary effect as the standard Chinese individual income-tax payment certificate. Anyone submitting income or tax proof to an overseas institution, including an institution in Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan, should apply to the competent local tax authority for a paper certificate. The electronic version may be used for other purposes. The provincial tax authority will no longer centrally print and mail certificates for the previous tax year after introducing this service.
