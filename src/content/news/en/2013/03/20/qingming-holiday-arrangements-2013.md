Shantou University’s General Office has announced the 2013 Qingming Festival schedule: **the university will take a three-day adjusted holiday from April 4 through 6, and work will resume on Sunday, April 7**. The Academic Affairs Office and Graduate School will make separate arrangements for teaching.

Before the holiday, each unit should inspect safety facilities in its offices, close windows and doors, shut off water and electricity, and complete other safety precautions. The security office will maintain campus security and order during the holiday.

The General Office, Resource Management Office, Student Affairs Office, schools, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and the D, E and G teaching buildings must arrange holiday duty coverage. They should email their duty arrangements to the General Office by **March 28** at `o_jdk@stu.edu.cn`. The office telephone is `86502305`.
