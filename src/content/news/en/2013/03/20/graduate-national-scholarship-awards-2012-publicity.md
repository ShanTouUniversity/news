According to a public notice issued by Shantou University’s Graduate School, the university completed its review for the 2012 national graduate scholarship in accordance with relevant provincial notices and management measures. After student applications, preliminary recommendations and publicity by the training units, and approval by the university’s graduate scholarship review leading group, **56 graduate students** were selected: **52 master’s students** and **four doctoral students**.

Organizations or individuals with objections may submit written materials to the Graduate School by **5:00 p.m. on March 26, 2013**. The receiving office is Room 401, Administrative Center. Submissions from organizations must carry an official seal, while individual submissions must be signed with the writer’s real name.
