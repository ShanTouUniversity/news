STU Library has announced the spring schedule for the Web of Knowledge online lecture series. Classes will run from **7:00 to 8:00 p.m. every Tuesday and Thursday from March through June 2013**. The series uses WebEX, so participants need an Internet-connected computer and a headset.

Course website: <http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/2013WOKOnline/courses.htm>. All classes require online registration before they begin. Participants may register for individual sessions or register once for the full semester. Courses marked “video” can be viewed from **March 21**, with questions and answers handled on the scheduled course date.

Read the WebEX operating guide before joining: <http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/WOKtraining/Webex.pdf>. Additional help is available through the WOK Online Lecture Weibo group: <http://q.weibo.com/569008>. The course site provides the WebEX viewer download and registration links; consult the site for current technical requirements.
