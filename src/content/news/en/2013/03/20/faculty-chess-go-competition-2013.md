Shantou University’s labor union will hold **faculty Chinese chess and Go competitions from April 1 to 20**. Faculty members interested in either game are invited to register. The competitions will take place in the labor union’s second-floor meeting room, and the format will be determined by the number of registrations.

Matches will be scheduled during evenings, weekends and other free time. Opponents may arrange their own match times. Participants should complete the registration form and email it to `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn` by **March 28**. For inquiries, contact Teacher Ye at the labor union at `86503197`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)

> ⚠️ The registration form and personal registration information are not reproduced here. Consult the original OA notice attachment to register.
