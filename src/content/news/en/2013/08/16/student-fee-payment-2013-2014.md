It is reported that the Finance Office and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced arrangements on August 16 for student fee payments in the 2013-2014 academic year. The notice cites Article 54 of the Higher Education Law, which requires higher-education students to pay tuition as prescribed by the state, and sets out the payment method, arrangements for students with student loans, and the application process for paying by semester.

## Payment Deadline and Method

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Payment deadline | Before September 1, 2013 |
| Fees covered | 2013-2014 tuition and accommodation fees (including arrears from previous years) |
| Deposit account | Personal Bank of China account |
| Deduction method | The university entrusts the bank to deduct the fees |

Students who have changed their individual bank deduction account, or who have not registered their fee deduction account with the university's Finance Office, must complete registration at the Finance Office as soon as possible to avoid affecting the deduction.

## Payment Arrangements for Students with Student Loans

Students who have obtained a 2013-2014 student loan may defer payment of the tuition and accommodation fees equal to the loan amount, but must deposit the difference into their personal Bank of China account. Students whose 2013-2014 loan amount has been cancelled due to academic performance or other reasons must deposit the full amount of their tuition and accommodation fees into their personal Bank of China account.

Students applying for a government-subsidized student loan from their home region must bring their Loan Acceptance Certificate, Loan Contract and other materials to the Student Financial Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office (Room 106, Administration Center) to complete the relevant procedures.

Students who have obtained a student loan or a home-region credit-based student loan must also log in to the Student Affairs System to report their installment payments. The difference is to be paid by September 1, 2013, and the loan-covered portion by May 10, 2014.

## Applying to Pay by Semester

Students from financially disadvantaged families may apply to pay by semester. They must log in to the Student Affairs System (http://sao.stu.edu.cn) between September 1 and 15, 2013 to submit an application. Counselors of each college (residential college) will review the applications on the system from September 16 to 20.

## Handling of Non-Payment

Those who fail to complete payment, including arrears from previous years, will be handled in accordance with the Measures for the Administration of Fee Payment and Registration of Undergraduate Students of Shantou University (Document No. 汕大发〔2013〕82号).
