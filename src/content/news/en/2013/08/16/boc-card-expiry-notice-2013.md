It is reported that the Finance Office of Shantou University circulated an explanatory letter from the Bank of China on August 16 regarding the expiry of combined debit-credit cards. According to the letter, the combined debit-credit cards that some students had previously obtained for tuition deduction have recently expired.

## Bank's Explanation

The Bank of China said it has sent text messages about card replacement to the affected students, reminding them not to deposit tuition into the credit card accounts. Because a system upgrade makes it impossible to issue replacement cards of the same type, students may go to the Bank of China Shantou University Branch to close their accounts or apply for a new "Great Wall Global" credit card. The bank also asked the university to help explain the matter to students who inquire and to advise them to complete the above procedures at the branch.

## University's Reminder

The university reminds students to avoid depositing tuition into the expired credit card accounts of their combined debit-credit cards, so as not to affect the deduction of tuition. The linked debit card account remains valid, and tuition deposited into that debit card account can still be deducted normally.
