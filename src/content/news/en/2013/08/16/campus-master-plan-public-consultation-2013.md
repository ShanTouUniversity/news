It is reported that a revision of the Shantou University campus master plan went on public display on campus on August 16, with comments and suggestions invited from faculty and students. The revision was prepared by the Shantou Urban Planning and Design Institute, and the consultation period runs until August 26.

## Planning Content

The revision focuses on the functional positioning of the campus front area, the west zone and Maotoushan, the student dormitory area, and the area around the reservoir. The planning period covers 2009 to 2020, with a near-term phase from 2009 to 2016 and a long-term phase from 2017 to 2020. The near-term student population is set at 10,000. The main campus covers 108 hectares (1,620 mu), with a total floor area of 450,000 square meters.

## Major Near-Term Projects

According to the plan, the major near-term construction projects include a new gymnasium, a new medical college building, residential colleges Phase I and Phase II, high-rise staff apartments, and a concert hall and arts education center.

## Display Channels and Exhibits

During the consultation period, faculty and students can view the online display on the university's internal office information network, or visit the on-site displays at the union information window in front of the post office, the information window on the first floor of the Administration Center, and the bulletin board on the plaza in front of the school motto monument. A total of 13 exhibits are on display, including a location analysis map, an existing-conditions map, the general layout plan, a planning structure diagram, a comprehensive transport plan, a landscape structure analysis diagram, and engineering plans for water supply, rainwater, sewage, electricity, telecommunications, gas, and earthquake disaster prevention and mitigation.
