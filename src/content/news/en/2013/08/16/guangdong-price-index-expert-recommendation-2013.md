It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University has forwarded a letter from the Guangdong Provincial Price Bureau (Yue Jia Han [2013] No. 752) regarding the recommendation of experts for the Guangdong Price Index. Interested faculty members should prepare the relevant materials as required by the letter and submit them to the Human Resources Office by September 9.

## Application Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Issuing authority | Guangdong Provincial Price Bureau |
| Document number | Yue Jia Han [2013] No. 752 |
| Receiving office at STU | Human Resources Office |
| Submission deadline | September 9, 2013 |
| Submission requirements | Prepare and submit materials as required by the letter |
| Contact | Yu Peisong |
| Telephone | 86503390 |
| Email | o_rsc@stu.edu.cn |

The attachments circulated with the notice include the letter from the Guangdong Provincial Price Bureau and the expert recommendation form.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
> ⚠️ The letter, the recommendation form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
