It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office has issued a notice that one session of the Philosophy of Life course will be suspended. The class will not meet on Tuesday, August 20, the first week of the summer session, because instructor Ma Fengqi of the Institute of Higher Education will be away attending a conference.

## Suspension Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Philosophy of Life (class number 63667) |
| Instructor | Ma Fengqi |
| Suspended session | Periods ABC, August 20, 2013 (Tuesday of the first week of the summer session) |
| Venue | Room E-203 (stepped classroom) |
| Make-up class | To be determined |

Students taking the course are requested to inform one another and to watch for further notice on the make-up session.
