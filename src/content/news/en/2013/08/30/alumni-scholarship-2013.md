It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Office of Shantou University issued a notice on August 30, 2013, launching the evaluation for the 2013 Alumni Scholarship.

To carry forward the alumni spirit advocated by Mr. Li Ka-shing — "self-improvement and mutual support; giving back to the alma mater and contributing to society" — alumni donated 125,000 yuan to establish this year's Alumni Scholarship, which helps students from families with financial difficulties with their studies and daily life. The scholarship will support 50 students this year, including 15 freshmen and 35 continuing students, at 2,500 yuan per recipient.

According to the notice, students who violate laws, regulations or campus rules, or who spend wastefully or breach public morality and cause a negative impact, are not eligible for the scholarship.

## Assessment Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | Students from families with financial difficulties |
| Number of recipients | 50 (15 freshmen and 35 continuing students) |
| Amount | 2,500 yuan per recipient |
| Freshman applications | Submit applications to the Youth League Committee of their college (residential college) by 17:00, September 4; colleges will publicize the candidates and submit them to the Student Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office by 17:00, September 9 |
| Continuing-student applications | Apply by 17:00, September 20; colleges will publicize the candidates and submit them by 17:00, September 27 |
| Selection criteria | Quotas allocated in the attachment, with recommendations based on family financial status, moral character, academic performance and compliance with campus rules |

According to the notice, recipients of the 2012 Alumni Scholarship must submit a signed summary of their study and campus life over the past academic year (printed on A4 paper) to the Student Aid Management Center by 17:00, September 30, and send a digital copy to o_xyh@stu.edu.cn.

> ⚠️ Quotas and application forms are available on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Alumni Office)
