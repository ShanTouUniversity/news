It is reported that, in order to strengthen the management of student dormitories and better serve students, the opening hours of the dining hall passages in Buildings A and D of Shantou University will be adjusted starting September 1, with the passages open in three time slots each day.

The adjusted opening hours are as follows:

| Time Slot | Opening Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Morning | 07:00 — 09:00 |
| Noon | 11:00 — 13:00 |
| Afternoon | 16:40 — 19:40 |

Students are reminded to note the change in the passages' opening hours and to plan their entry and exit from the dormitories accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
