It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a course adjustment notice on August 30, 2013, changing the schedule of the *Health Education I* course.

Because the instructor will be away at a conference, the session scheduled for Wednesday of week 1 of the fall semester (September 18), periods 0ABC, in Lecture Hall 1 is cancelled. The make-up session will be held on Saturday of week 1 (September 21), periods 6789, in the same venue, Lecture Hall 1.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Health Education I* (Class 62240) |
| Cancelled | Wednesday of week 1 of the fall semester (September 18), periods 0ABC |
| Original venue | Lecture Hall 1 |
| Make-up | Saturday of week 1 (September 21), periods 6789 |
| Make-up venue | Lecture Hall 1 (unchanged) |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students enrolled in the course are asked to inform each other.
