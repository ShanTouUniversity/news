It is reported that Shantou University has announced its arrangements for the 2013 graduate orientation. All new graduate students of the 2013 cohort will register on September 1, and orientation work will run from September 1 to September 15, covering reception, registration, accommodation, meetings with supervisors and orientation education.

## Off-campus Reception Stations

On September 1, the university will set up three reception stations across the city of Shantou, each run by a designated office.

| Reception Station | Responsible Unit |
| --- | --- |
| Exit of Shantou Railway Station (including the Coach Terminal) | Resources Management Office |
| Bus stop at the university gate | STU Student Union |
| Shantou General Bus Station | Medical College |

## On-campus Enquiries and Registration

During the orientation period, the Graduate Student Union will set up enquiry and guidance points at the university motto monument and the graduate dormitories, offering directions and information to new students. The Graduate School will set up a registration point from 8:00 to 22:00 on September 1 on the ground floor of the New Administration Center, below the Business School, to handle registration for new graduate students.

New students must bring their admission notice, identity documents, student records and household registration transfer certificate, among other materials. After collecting their accommodation slips, they should go to the duty room of the graduate dormitory building to complete check-in.

## Orientation Education and School Activities

During the orientation period, each school and department will arrange activities such as meetings between new students and their supervisors, program introductions and laboratory visits, helping new students get familiar with their academic and research environment as soon as possible.
