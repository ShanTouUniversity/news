It is reported that Shantou University issued a notice on August 30, 2013, opening the 2013 Outstanding Freshman Scholarship selection for full-time undergraduate freshmen who chose STU as their first choice and enroll on time.

## Eligibility

Applicants must meet at least one of the following conditions:

- A gaokao score, excluding policy-based bonus points, at least 30 points above the first-batch admission cutoff in their province;
- A third prize or above in a national or international competition during high school, such as an Olympiad, a youth innovation competition, a science and engineering competition, or a biology and environmental science practice competition;
- A National Grade II Athlete title or above earned during high school, with the provincial unified test standard met.

## Awards

| Award | Places and amount |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | Five per year, 20,000 yuan per recipient |
| Second prize | 25 per year, 5,000 yuan per recipient |
| Outstanding Female Freshman from Outside Guangdong Award Plan | 25 recipients, 5,000 yuan each |

The Outstanding Female Freshman from Outside Guangdong Award Plan requires no student application and is selected by the university based on the year's admissions situation and donation plans. The awards cannot be combined.

## Application and Selection Process

Application forms and eligibility details were sent with the admission letters. Eligible freshmen must complete the online application on the Student Affairs System at `http://sao.stu.edu.cn` before 12:00 noon on September 4, and submit the printed application form together with original and photocopied award certificates, portfolios and other materials to the Student Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office (Room 106, Administration Building).

A selection committee established by the university will determine the awardees through preliminary selection and interviews.
