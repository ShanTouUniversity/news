It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University has issued a pre-appointment public notice proposing Lyu Yuan as dean of the Business School. The proposal followed deliberation at the 4th University Leadership Meeting of 2013, held on April 9.

The notice is issued under the *Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres of Shantou University* (STU Party Committee Document No. 47 of 2009). During the publicity period, units and individuals with objections may submit them in writing to the university's Human Resources Office before September 5. Statements from units must bear the unit's official seal, while individual statements must be signed with the submitter's real name.
