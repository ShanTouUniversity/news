It is reported that, due to the installation of air-conditioning distribution wiring for student dormitory Buildings A to G, power supply to Buildings E and F at Shantou University will be cut off on the afternoon of August 30. The Student Community Management Service Center advises dormitory residents to switch off electrical appliances in their rooms in advance to avoid accidents when power is restored.

## Power Outage Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Outage time | August 30, 2013 (Friday), 13:30-18:30 |
| Affected area | Student dormitory Buildings E and F |
| Reason | Installation of air-conditioning distribution wiring for dormitory Buildings A to G |
| Reminder | Dormitory residents are advised to switch off electrical appliances in advance |
