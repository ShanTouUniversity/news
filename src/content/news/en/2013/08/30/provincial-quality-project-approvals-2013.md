It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education published the approval results of the 2013 Guangdong Higher Education Teaching Quality and Teaching Reform Projects on August 28 (Document No. Yue Jiao Gao Han [2013] 113). Shantou University had 34 projects approved.

## Approved Projects

The 34 approved STU projects fall into eight categories: comprehensive specialty reform pilot projects, excellent video open courses, excellent resource-sharing courses, experimental teaching demonstration centers, off-campus practical bases for college students, higher education teaching reform projects, university teacher teaching development centers and pilot colleges. The notice also specifies construction periods for the different types of projects:

| Construction period | Project categories |
| --- | --- |
| 3 years | Comprehensive specialty reform pilot projects, excellent video open courses, excellent resource-sharing courses, experimental teaching demonstration centers, off-campus practical bases for college students, university teacher teaching development centers and pilot colleges |
| 2–3 years | Higher education teaching reform projects |
| 2 years | College student innovation and entrepreneurship training projects |

## Follow-up Management

According to the Academic Affairs Office, the university will track and manage the approved projects and will support the provincial department's inspections, acceptance reviews and promotion of the results.
