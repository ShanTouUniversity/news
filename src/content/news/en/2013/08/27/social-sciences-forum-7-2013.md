It is reported that the Social Sciences Department will hold the seventh Social Sciences Forum on the afternoon of August 29, 2013, with Prof. Chen Jinlong, dean of the School of Marxism at South China Normal University, as the speaker.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

| Speaker | Prof. Chen Jinlong, Dean and Professor of the School of Marxism, South China Normal University; doctoral supervisor; PhD in History |
| Time | 15:00–17:00, Thursday, August 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Meeting Room of the Social Sciences Department (Room G209, Academic Building) |

## About the Speaker

Chen Jinlong holds a PhD in history and is dean, professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Marxism, South China Normal University. His research focuses on the history of the Communist Party of China, modern Chinese history, and the sinicization of Marxism. He also serves as a council member of the China Society for the Study of CPC Historical Figures, the National Society for the Study of Mao Zedong Thought and Life, and the National Society for the Study of Mao Zedong's Philosophical Thought, as well as an executive council member of the National Society for the Study of the History of the People's Republic of China, and vice president of the Guangdong Society for the Study of CPC History and the Guangdong Society for Scientific Socialism.

In research, he has led six National Social Science Fund projects and three major and general projects of the Ministry of Education's humanities and social sciences program, published four monographs of his own, and authored nearly 100 papers in journals such as Marxist Studies, Nationalities Research and CPC History Studies, several of which were reprinted in full by Xinhua Digest. His achievements have won the second prize of the Ministry of Education Award for Outstanding Achievements in Humanities and Social Sciences in Chinese Universities and the first and second prizes of the Guangdong Provincial Award for Outstanding Achievements in Philosophy and Social Sciences. He was selected for the Ministry of Education's "New Century Excellent Talents Support Program" in 2007 and the "Four Batches" talent program of the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee in 2009, and was named one of the first "Guangdong Outstanding Social Scientists" in 2011.

All teachers and students are welcome to attend.
