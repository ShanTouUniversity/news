As the new academic year began, the Network and Information Center of Shantou University launched Internet account renewal for faculty and staff as well as graduate students.

It is reported that the renewal is handled centrally by each unit and college. Units are required to compile and choose the Internet package type for their members for the period from October 2013 to September 2014, and then submit the name list in one batch before the deadline.

Under the arrangement, each unit's renewal list must be delivered to the One-Card Service Window on the ground floor of the Network and Information Center by September 24. The list must bear the official seal of the unit or college, and both the original and one photocopy must be submitted for verification and record keeping at the window.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Renewal period | October 2013 to September 2014 |
| Submission deadline | Before September 24, 2013 |
| Submission location | One-Card Service Window, ground floor, Network and Information Center |
| Required documents | Renewal list bearing the official seal of the unit or college, original and one photocopy |
| Contact telephone | `86503400-2` |
| Contact email | `cxfang@stu.edu.cn` |

The account application procedure and the Internet package table are provided in the attachment to the notice.
