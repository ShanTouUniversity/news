It is reported that *Desire Garden*, a Broadway classic and the annual flagship production of the National Theatre of China, will be staged at the Grand Hall of the Lin Baixin Convention Center in Shantou at 8:00 p.m. on September 1, 2013. It will be the theatre's first visit to Shantou.

## Performance Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Play | *Desire Garden* |
| Time | 8:00 p.m., September 1, 2013 |
| Venue | Grand Hall, Lin Baixin Convention Center |
| Troupe | National Theatre of China |
| Starring | Hu Ke, Na Wei, Jia Ni, Niu Piao and others |

## About the Troupe and the Play

The National Theatre of China was formed by merging the former China Youth Art Theatre and the Central Experimental Drama Theatre, and is a national-level performing arts group. *Desire Garden*, written by American playwright Edward Albee, is a classic black comedy that explores the conflict between money and human nature. It is directed by Han Jie.

## Tickets and How to Buy

Tickets are priced at 80, 180, 280, 380, 480 and 680 yuan, with a limited number of 880-yuan VIP seats. STU students and staff can enjoy a 20 percent discount by presenting their campus card.

On-campus sales will be held from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on August 30 downstairs at the STU Labor Union building. Buyers in the city may purchase tickets at Kangstand Advertising on the 28th floor of the Friendship International Building on Jinsha Road. Performance inquiry hotline: 0754-88630100.
