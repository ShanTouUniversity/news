It is reported that, to strengthen the management of League member records, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University made arrangements on August 27 for the transfer of Youth League organizational relations for the 2013 freshman class, requiring each college Youth League committee to assist with member registration, the collection and sorting of records, and the updating of grassroots League data.

## Collecting and Submitting Materials

By August 30, each college Youth League committee must collect the "Shantou University League Member Registration Form" from the university Youth League Committee. The committees should organize the 2013 freshmen by major to collect their League member records and membership cards, and, after checking and sorting them, deliver the original registration forms together with the membership cards and member records to the university Youth League Committee Office by September 13. A copy of each registration form is to be kept on file by the college committee.

## Records Handling

Member records should be taken out of their envelopes, marked in pencil with the last three digits of the student ID number in the upper right corner of the cover, and arranged in order of student ID number.

## Data Update

Each college committee must also update the grassroots Youth League organization data-collection system by deleting information on the 2009 graduating class and adding information on the 2013 freshmen, and send the updated data to the university Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by September 13.

Questions may be directed to the university Youth League Committee Office at 86502302.
