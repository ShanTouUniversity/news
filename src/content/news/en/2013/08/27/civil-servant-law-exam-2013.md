It is reported that Shantou University is carrying out the 2013 law knowledge exam for public employees on campus, with the work advanced by the Party Committee Publicity Department.

A "2013 Public Employees Law Knowledge Exam Unit Information Form" was issued together with the notice, and relevant units are asked to fill it in. The form covers the unit name, the number of people in the unit, the number of examinees, and the contact person together with their contact details, so that information on participants from all units can be compiled in a unified way.

The specific time, venue and organizational arrangements for the exam are subject to the original OA notice, and this article does not reproduce the form.

| Form fields (excerpt) | What to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Unit name | Full name of the reporting unit |
| Number of people | Registered headcount of the unit |
| Number of examinees | Number of people to take part in this law knowledge exam |
| Contact person and details | The staff member responsible for this work and their contact details |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Publicity Department)
> ⚠️ The 2013 Public Employees Law Knowledge Exam unit information form is available in the OA system.
