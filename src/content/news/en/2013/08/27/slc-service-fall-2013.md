It is reported that Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) has announced its service schedule for the new academic year, with academic support services opening to undergraduates across the university in stages.

## Service Start Dates

| Date | Services |
| --- | --- |
| From September 1 | Group-discussion services, open to all undergraduates |
| From September 8 | University adaptation tutoring classes, for new students |
| From September 29 | Academic consultations and all other services |

## Opening Hours and Location

The SLC is open daily from **3:00 to 10:00 p.m.** and is located beside the STU post office. For details, visit the center's website at http://slc.stu.edu.cn or call 86502250 (campus extension 882250).
