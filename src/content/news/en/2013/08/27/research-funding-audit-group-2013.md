It is reported that, in accordance with the requirements of the Notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on the Special Audit and Investigation of Research Fund Management of Provincial Universities (Yue Jiao Shen Han [2013] No. 15), Shantou University issued a document on August 27 (STU Fa [2013] No. 96) to establish the Leading Group for the Special Audit Self-Inspection of Research Fund Management.

## Composition of the Leading Group

| Role | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Head | Luo Nianchao |
| Deputy Head | Wen Xiaobo |
| Members | Sun Fenqiang, Hu Zhong, Zou Zhibo, Yao Mingan, Qiu Jingfeng |

## Office and Contact

The office of the leading group is located at the Supervision and Audit Office and is responsible for organizing and coordinating the self-inspection work.
