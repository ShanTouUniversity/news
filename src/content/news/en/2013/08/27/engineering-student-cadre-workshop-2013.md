It is reported that, in support of the university's EIP-CDIO engineering education reform, the College of Engineering of Shantou University will hold a workshop for student leaders on August 28 to strengthen their sense of responsibility and their awareness of the bigger picture, improve their theoretical grounding and organizational and coordination abilities, and enhance the cohesion and effectiveness of their teams.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | August 28, 2013 |
| Venue | D401 |
| Participants | Departments of the College of Engineering Youth League Committee, the Youth Volunteer Association, the College of Engineering Student Union, the College of Engineering Science and Technology Development Center, the Career Development Center, and volunteer counselors for the 2013 freshman class |
| Content | Communication skills, etiquette training, team-building and more |

## Notes

- Sign in 15 minutes before the session begins;
- Attend on time; absence, lateness or leaving early is not permitted;
- Keep mobile phones on silent during the workshop;
- Take notes carefully and keep quiet.
