It is reported that the Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee of Shantou University, which works under the guidance of the residential-counselor office of the Student Affairs Office to promote self-management and self-service in the dormitory areas, issued a recruitment notice on August 26 for student leaders. The committee says it is committed to serving fellow students and building a harmonious community.

## Positions Available

| Department | Positions |
| --- | --- |
| Project Department | One director and one to two deputy directors |
| Public Relations Department | One director and one to two deputy directors |
| Secretariat | One director and one to two deputy directors |
| Publicity Department | One director and one to two deputy directors |

## Requirements

Applicants should have a proper attitude, a certain level of management and leadership ability, good communication and coordination skills and a spirit of dedication, and should be passionate about the residential community. The recruitment is open to all undergraduate students living in university housing.

## Application and Interview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Application period | From now until noon on August 31 |
| How to apply | Complete the application form (see attachment), name the file "name + application" and send it to the designated address — the application email is available in the original OA notice |
| Interview time | 7:30 p.m. on August 31 |
| Interview venue | Room 411, Building C, men's dormitory building (Dormitory Committee office) |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The application form and other attachments, along with related contact details, are available in the original notice on the OA system.
