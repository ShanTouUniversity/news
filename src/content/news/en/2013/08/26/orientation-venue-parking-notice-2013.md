It is reported that, under Shantou University's 2013 orientation arrangements, the open ground floors on the south, north and east sides of the Administration Building and under the Business School will be used as check-in points for new students during registration.

## Open Ground Floors Set Aside for Check-in

The open ground floors on the south, north and east sides of the Administration Building, as well as the one under the Business School, will serve as reception areas for orientation. The university said the move is intended to ensure that new students can register smoothly and in an orderly manner.

## No Parking from August 31 to September 1

To keep these areas clear during registration, motorcycles, bicycles and electric bikes may not be parked there from August 31 to September 1. Vehicle owners are asked to move their vehicles to nearby parking spots in advance and to pass on the message.

The arrangement was issued jointly by the General Administration Office, the Student Affairs Office and the Resources Management Office.
