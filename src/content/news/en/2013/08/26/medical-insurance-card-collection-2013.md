It is reported that the Staff Medical Insurance Section of the STU Campus Hospital issued a notice on August 26, asking 16 faculty and staff members to collect their medical insurance booklets and IC cards during working hours at the section on the second floor of the campus hospital.

## Collection Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Working hours |
| Location | Staff Medical Insurance Section, second floor, Campus Hospital |
| Items to collect | Medical insurance booklet, IC card |
| Who should collect | 16 faculty and staff members |

The notice advises those affected to contact the hospital's Staff Medical Insurance Section if they have any questions. The full list and further details are available in the original notice on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
> ⚠️ The collection list and other attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
