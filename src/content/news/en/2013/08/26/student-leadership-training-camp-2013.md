It is reported that the Law School issued a notice on August 26, 2013, to hold a student leadership training camp to improve the comprehensive quality and working ability of student leaders and foster teamwork and service awareness.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | August 26–28, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 213, Law School |
| Participants | Members of the Law School Student Union, Law Society, Youth Volunteers Association, debate team and volunteer counselors for the 2013 freshmen |
| Organizer | Law School |

## Notes

1. Wear the school uniform neatly;
2. Arrive 15 minutes early to check in;
3. Attend on time without absence, lateness or early departure;
4. Set phones to silent and take notes carefully;
5. Keep the venue quiet.
