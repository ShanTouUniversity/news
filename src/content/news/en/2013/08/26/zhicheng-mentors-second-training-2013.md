It is reported that Zhicheng Residential College of Shantou University will hold the second pre-service training session for its 2013–2014 peer mentors on August 31, in support of the university's goals of internationalization and finely tailored talent development, and in a bid to further strengthen mentors' overall competence and the quality of their work.

## Training Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | August 31, 2013 (Saturday) |
| Participants | All peer mentors of the college for the 2013–2014 academic year |
| Venue | Lecture room J-354 |
| Course session | Begins at 9:00 a.m. |

According to the college, the training is designed to help mentors better know themselves, serve as role models and strengthen their sense of responsibility so that they can carry out their mentoring duties effectively. The specific training requirements are set out in the attachment, and all mentors are asked to prepare accordingly.
