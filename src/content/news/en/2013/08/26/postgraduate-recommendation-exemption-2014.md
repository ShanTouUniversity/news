It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on August 26, 2013, launching the 2014 work of recommending outstanding undergraduate graduates for postgraduate study without entrance examination. The work is carried out in accordance with the "Administrative Measures of Shantou University for Recommending Outstanding Undergraduate Graduates for Postgraduate Study without Entrance Examination" (Shan Da Fa [2009] No. 119).

## Organization and Principles

Each school is required to establish a recommendation working group led by school (department) leaders and joined by faculty representatives to organize and implement the work. The work adheres to the principles of openness, fairness and impartiality, evaluates candidates comprehensively in moral, intellectual and physical terms, selects the best, and highlights the assessment of innovative spirit and professional competence.

## Types and Quota

The 2014 recommended students fall into two types — academic degree and professional degree — with an introduction to professional degrees provided in Attachment 1 of the notice. As the Ministry of Education has not yet released the quota, the university will not allocate quotas to schools for the time being, and each school must rank students separately for the academic degree and professional degree types.

## Schedule

| Time | Task |
| --- | --- |
| By September 6 | Students intending to apply fill in the application form (see Attachment 2) and submit hard and electronic copies to their school |
| By September 13 | Each school completes its recommendation work |
| By 5:00 p.m. on September 13 | Each school submits the ranked candidate list (see Attachment 3) together with official transcripts to the Academic Services Section of the Academic Affairs Office |

Electronic materials including application forms and ranked lists should be sent to yd@stu.edu.cn. For inquiries, contact the Academic Services Section of the Academic Affairs Office at 86502402.

> ⚠️ The introduction to professional degrees and attachments such as the application form and ranked list are available on the OA system.
