It is reported that the results of the 2013 Guangdong Junior High School Biology League, organized by the Guangdong Provincial Biology Competition Committee for Secondary School Students, have recently been released. Ten students from the Affiliated High School of Shantou University won awards, including two second prizes and eight third prizes.

## Award List

| Prize | Students | Number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Second Prize | Huang S, Xie LH | 2 |
| Third Prize | Yu ZT, Zhang QR, Chen J, Su WZ, Zhang JY, Xu YX, Huang D, Chen X | 8 |

In addition to the student awards, the school's tutor Huang Xiaoyan was also commended by the competition committee.

The Affiliated High School announced the good news on August 26, congratulating the award-winning students and their tutor.
