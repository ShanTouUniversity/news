It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold the 160th Engineering Research Forum on the morning of August 30, 2013, with Prof. Liu Shaoying of Hosei University, Japan, as the speaker.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Formal Engineering Methods for Software Quality Assurance |
| Speaker | Prof. Liu Shaoying, Hosei University, Japan |
| Time | 10:00, Friday, August 30, 2013 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering |
| Participants | Postgraduate students and related faculty of the College of Engineering, with other interested teachers and students welcome |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |

Prof. Liu Shaoying is a well-known computer scientist in Japan. Postgraduate students and related faculty of the College of Engineering are invited to attend the forum, and other interested teachers and students are also welcome.
