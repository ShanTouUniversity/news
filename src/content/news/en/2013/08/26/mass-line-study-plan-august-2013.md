It is reported that the Party Committee Publicity Department issued a notice on August 26, announcing that Shantou University will hold intensive study sessions for the mass-line education campaign from August 27 to 29. The sessions combine keynote reports, the screening of a warning film and group discussions, and are designed to help Party members and cadres study in depth and identify problems.

## Study Programme

| Date | Time | Venue | Activity | Chair / Speaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aug 27 | 9:00–11:00 | Science Lecture Hall | Keynote report: The Party's Mass Line and Current Hot Issues in Party Building | Chair: Luo Nianchao; Speaker: Deng Jianguang |
| Aug 27 | 15:00–17:00 | Meeting rooms of each unit | Group discussions by Party general branches and directly affiliated branches on conveying the spirit of the campaign | — |
| Aug 28 | 9:00–10:00 | Science Lecture Hall | Screening of the warning film "Raising the Sharp Weapon to Resolutely Curb Unhealthy Trends: Typical Cases from Guangdong's Work-Style Undercover Inspections" | Chair: Sun Fenqiang |
| Aug 28 | Afternoon | Meeting rooms of each unit | Group discussions by Party general branches and directly affiliated branches on identifying problems | — |
| Aug 29 | 9:00–11:30 | Science Lecture Hall | High-level forum on the campaign: opening address, keynote speech and interactive exchange | Chair: Song Yaozhen; Speaker: Prof. Chen Jinlong, Dean of the School of Marxism, South China Normal University |
| Aug 29 | Afternoon | Meeting rooms of each unit | Group discussions on each unit's report on the outcomes of its study and practice | — |

## Participants

The intensive study sessions are intended for university leaders, cadres at or above the division level, staff in senior positions, and Party and administrative heads of teaching units. The participants in the August 28 morning film screening are the same as those in the August 27 morning keynote report. The afternoon group discussions are organized by Party general branches and directly affiliated branches, with Party members and cadres as well as administrative leaders at or above the deputy-section level taking part.

The notice requires each unit to compile a report on the outcomes of its study and practice after the sessions and submit it to the office of the university's leading group for the mass-line education campaign.
