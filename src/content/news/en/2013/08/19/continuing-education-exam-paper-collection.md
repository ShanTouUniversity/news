It is reported that the Continuing Education Center of Shantou University has recently issued a dedicated notice on the collection of exam papers for the 2013 continuing education public-required course ("gongxu" course), with the arrangements communicated to faculty and staff through the university's Human Resources Office.

The public-required course is a mandatory component of continuing education for professional and technical staff, with study and assessment organized annually at the provincial and municipal levels. The distribution of exam papers directly affects staff members' completion of their annual continuing education hours, and this notice sets out arrangements for that stage.

Specific collection times, locations, and handling requirements are subject to the center's official notice, and those concerned may contact the Continuing Education Center to confirm the details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
