It is reported that, according to the project announcements issued by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC), Shantou University had secured a total of 49 NSFC-funded projects for 2013 as of August 16, 2013, with total funding of 25.51 million yuan.

## Breakdown by Program Category

The 49 funded projects fall into three categories: 26 General Program projects, 17 Young Scientists Fund projects, and 6 International (Regional) Cooperation and Exchange projects.

| Program category | Number of projects |
| --- | --- |
| General Program | 26 |
| Young Scientists Fund | 17 |
| International (Regional) Cooperation and Exchange | 6 |
| Total | 49 |

The Research Office also released the list of funded projects for the year, and the list is the authoritative source for the details of each project.
