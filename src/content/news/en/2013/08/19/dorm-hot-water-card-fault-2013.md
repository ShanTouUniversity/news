It is reported that STU's Student Community Management Service Center issued a notice on August 19, 2013, stating that a lightning strike on the afternoon of August 17 caused faults in the hot-water card system serving dormitory Buildings A through F, with multiple cascaders and management computers failing one after another.

The Network Center immediately organized emergency repairs. By the time the notice was issued, the card systems in Buildings E and F as well as Buildings C and D had been restored and were working normally. In Buildings A and B, 24 cascaders remained out of service and are expected to take several days to repair.

As a result, hot water supply in Buildings A and B is temporarily unavailable. The Student Community Management Service Center advises students in those buildings to use the hot-water facilities in Buildings C and D.
