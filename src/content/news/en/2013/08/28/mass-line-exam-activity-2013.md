It is reported that, in accordance with the deployment of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee and the university's work arrangements for the Party's mass-line education and practice campaign, Shantou University has organized a study assessment for the campaign, aiming to guide Party members and cadres in in-depth study and improve learning outcomes.

## Assessment Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Those who took part in the university's intensive study sessions (see the attachment for the name list) |
| Collecting exam papers | August 28 (Wednesday) |
| Submission deadline | Before 12:00 on August 30 (Friday) |
| Collection venue | Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Committee (Room 315, New Administration Center) |

The list of participants is subject to the attachment to the notice and is not reproduced here. Those involved are required to collect and submit their exam papers by the times above.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ The attachment containing the list of participants is available on the original notice on the OA system.
