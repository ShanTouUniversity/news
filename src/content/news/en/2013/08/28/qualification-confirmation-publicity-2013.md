It is reported that, in accordance with the *Interim Measures of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security on the Confirmation of Senior Professional Titles Obtained by Personnel from Outside Guangdong* (Yue Ren She Fa [2010] No. 306), Shantou University began publicizing on August 28 the application materials of Liu Guanghui from the Art Education Center for the confirmation of a senior professional title obtained outside the province. The publicity period runs from August 28 to September 5.

Anyone with objections to the materials may submit them in writing to the university's Human Resources Office before September 5. Objections raised on behalf of a unit must bear the unit's official seal, while those raised by individuals must be signed with the person's real name.
