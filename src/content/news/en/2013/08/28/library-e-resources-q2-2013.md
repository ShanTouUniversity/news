Shantou University Library has released second-quarter 2013 electronic-resource usage statistics in the seventh 2013 issue of its Electronic Resources Bulletin (13th issue overall). The figures cover Chinese- and foreign-language databases and record downloads, searches, clicks, plays, transferred pages, books read and tracks listened to; the measures differ by database.

### Usage of major databases

| Database | Usage | Measure |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| CNKI China Academic Journals Full-text Database | 148,907 | Downloads |
| Kuke Digital Music Library | 147,037 | Tracks listened to |
| Duxiu Knowledge Base | 76,365 | Pages transferred |
| Superstar Academic Video | 53,388 | Plays (1,640 on the on-campus server) |
| Superstar Million-volume eBook Library | 36,297 | Books read |
| VIP Chinese Science and Technology Journals Database, full-text edition | 35,916 | Downloads |
| CNKI China Master's Theses Full-text Database | 32,886 | Downloads |
| Huike News Database | 32,037 | Clicks |
| Yinfu Examination Question Bank B1 | 28,291 | Clicks |
| Elsevier ScienceDirect | 24,982 | Downloads |
| EI Village | 22,300 | Searches |
| JSTOR Western Backfiles Full Text | 18,958 | Downloads |
| Wiley Online Library | 12,997 | Downloads |
| Web of Science (SCI/SSCI/A&HCI) | 11,677 | Searches |

### Chinese databases

Among the other Chinese resources, the CNKI China Doctoral Dissertations Full-text Database recorded 10,241 downloads this quarter, and 12,622 books were read in the STU Superstar eBook Library. China InfoBank recorded 18,397 downloads, the PKU Lawyee Database 13,121 clicks, the RESSET Financial Database 4,884 downloads, the GTA CSMAR Database 805 downloads, and Westlaw China 3,624 searches.

### Foreign-language databases

Among the other foreign-language databases, Springer recorded 9,988 downloads, American Mathematical Society MathSciNet 10,682 searches and IOP 506 downloads, and the bulletin also reports usage of the remaining foreign-language resources.
