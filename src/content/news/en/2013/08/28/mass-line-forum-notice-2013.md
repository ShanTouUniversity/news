It is reported that, as arranged under the Party's mass-line education and practice campaign, Shantou University will hold a high-level forum on the morning of August 29, 2013, featuring a lecture by Professor Chen Jinlong, dean of the School of Marxism at South China Normal University.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 09:00, August 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Science Report Hall |
| Speaker | Professor Chen Jinlong, Dean of the School of Marxism, South China Normal University |
| Participants | Cadres at the intermediate post level and above of all units; open to all Party members, cadres and members of the public across the university |
| Issued by | Party Committee Publicity Department and the Office of the Leading Group for the Education and Practice Campaign |

According to the arrangements, cadres at the intermediate post level and above of all units are required to attend on time and to enter the venue 10 minutes in advance. The forum is also open to all Party members, cadres and members of the public across the university.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Publicity Department)
