It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the College of Liberal Arts will hold a training workshop for 2013 orientation volunteers and freshman counselors on August 29, 2013.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Hearts Connected, Harmony Composed |
| Time | 09:00–17:00, August 29, 2013 |
| Venue | Tiered Classroom E204, Building E |
| Participants | Orientation volunteers and freshman counselors |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, College of Liberal Arts |

The workshop aims to prepare volunteers and counselors to serve new students from a people-oriented perspective, improving their service capabilities and providing warm and thoughtful support to freshmen and their parents. Participants are asked to observe discipline, arrive on time, follow arrangements and complete all assigned tasks, without unauthorized absence or leave.

Relevant personnel are asked to attend on time.
