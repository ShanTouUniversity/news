It is reported that, to complete the transfer of Party organizational relations and the verification of Party member status for the 2013 freshman class, the Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on August 28, asking the Party general branches of each college and residential college to register new Party members and collect their materials after enrollment.

## Materials to Be Collected

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target | 2013 freshman Party members |
| Materials | A valid letter of introduction for Party organizational relations; Party membership files such as the *Application for Joining the Communist Party of China* |
| Verification | Where membership files are sent separately together with a student's academic file, their arrival must be verified |
| Submission | The materials above together with the statistics form of freshman Party members |
| Deadline | Before September 12 |
| Venue | Organization and United Front Work Department (Room 315, New Administration Center) |

The notice requires that the collected materials and the statistics form of freshman Party members be submitted together to the Organization and United Front Work Department before September 12, at Room 315 of the New Administration Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ Attachments such as the statistics form of freshman Party members contain students' personal information; please refer to the original notice on the OA system.
