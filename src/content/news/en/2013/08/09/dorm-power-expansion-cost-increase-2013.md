It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University published a notice on August 9, 2013, setting out additional items for the student dormitory power distribution expansion project.

The project broke ground on July 27. During construction, cable transition boxes and copper busbars were added to supply power to the heat pump units, while waterproofing and drainage facilities were added for the outdoor box-type substations. Both items fall outside the original contract. The additional cost is preliminarily estimated at about RMB 25,000, keeping the cumulative increase within the 10% limit set by the university.

In accordance with the Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering Management of Capital Construction Projects, the Infrastructure Office has published the additional items for comment.

## Public Notice Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Student dormitory power distribution expansion project |
| Start date | July 27, 2013 |
| Additional items | Cable transition boxes and copper busbars; waterproofing and drainage facilities for outdoor box-type substations |
| Estimated additional cost | About RMB 25,000 |
| Cost range | Within the 10% limit set by the university |
| Feedback deadline | 5:30 p.m., August 12, 2013 |
| Offices receiving feedback | Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office |
| Issued by | Infrastructure Office |
| Date of notice | August 9, 2013 |

Those with objections may submit written comments to the Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office before the deadline.
