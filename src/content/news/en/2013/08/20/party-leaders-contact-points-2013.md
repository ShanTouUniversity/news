It is reported that, in line with higher-level arrangements and to give full play to the exemplary role of Party member leaders, the Leading Group for Shantou University's Mass Line Education and Practice Campaign decided to establish contact points for university-level Party leaders and announced the arrangements on August 20, 2013.

## How University Leaders Will Guide the Contact Points

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Activity guidance | Guide the contact point in carrying out education and practice activities |
| Democratic life meetings | Attend and guide the leadership team's special democratic life meeting and hold heart-to-heart talks with its principal officials |
| Rectification guidance | Follow up on the contact point's progress as appropriate and guide rectification |

## Requirements for Contact Points

- Connect with the assigned university leader as soon as possible and report on activities on their own initiative;
- Submit the activity implementation plan and materials on the special democratic life meeting and rectification work as required;
- Implement the guidance given by the leader and report on progress in a timely manner.

The specific assignment of contact points for each university-level Party leader is set out in the attachment to the notice.
