It is reported that the Network and Information Center and the Resources Management Office issued a notice on August 20, 2013, starting the verification of office telephone bills for June and July 2013. The electronic bills cover fixed-line services provided by China Telecom Shantou Branch and China Mobile Shantou Branch; the two carriers have sent the bill data to the university, where it has been imported into the Telephone Management System.

## Verification Procedure and Schedule

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Log in to the Telephone Management System at http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn |
| 2 | Open the "China Telecom Fixed Lines" and "China Mobile Fixed Lines" sections to review the bills |
| 3 | Click "Confirm the selected bills" if a bill is accurate |
| 4 | Report any discrepancy between the bill and actual use by August 26 |
| 5 | After verification, the Finance Office will deduct the charges on August 28 using each unit's funding account number |

Units that find discrepancies between bill details and actual use can give feedback by email: questions about China Telecom fixed-line bills can be sent to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn, and questions about China Mobile fixed-line bills can be sent to stunic@stu.edu.cn.

The notice states that bills for which no objection is raised or no confirmation is submitted by the end of the verification period will be treated as confirmed by the system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center, Resources Management Office)
