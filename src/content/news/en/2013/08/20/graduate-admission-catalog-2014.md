It is reported that the STU Graduate School issued a notice on August 20, 2013, starting work on the 2014 doctoral and master's graduate admissions program catalog. Heads of graduate studies in each discipline are required to carry out the work through the STU Graduate Admissions Management System (http://www.gs.stu.edu.cn/zs/), with login and operating procedures detailed in an attachment to the notice.

## Items to Be Compiled

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Admitting programs | Code, name and level of each program |
| Planned enrollment | Based on actual enrollment in 2013 |
| First-round examination catalog | Research direction codes and names, supervising faculty, examination subjects |
| First-round syllabus | Syllabi for self-designed examination subjects |
| Second-round examination | Codes, names and formats of second-round subjects and their syllabi |

## Requirements for Self-Designed Subjects

Under Ministry of Education rules, self-designed examination subjects must state the scope of content covered and must not specify reference books, and each syllabus must be kept within 300 characters. New or revised content-scope descriptions must be emailed to the Graduate School in the format of Attachment 5 for centralized entry; codes for new subjects are also assigned by the Graduate School.

Disciplines may consult the 2013 admissions catalog on the Graduate School website as a reference (doctoral programs: doctor_zsjz.htm; master's programs: master_zsjz.htm).

## Schedule

| Time | Task |
| --- | --- |
| By the end of the working day on Monday, August 26 | Complete and submit entries in the Graduate Admissions Management System |
| August 27 | The system closes |
| By the end of the working day on Wednesday, August 28 | Deliver one signed and sealed printout to the Graduate School for the record (Room 401, fourth floor, New Administration Center; phone 2424) |

The catalog in this version will be reported to the Ministry of Education.
