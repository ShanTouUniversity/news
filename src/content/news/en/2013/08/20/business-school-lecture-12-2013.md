It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will hold an academic lecture from 10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Friday, August 23, on the topic *A Study of the Evolution Model of Chinese Corporate Culture: An Analysis Based on Grounded Research Methods*. The lecture will be chaired by Professor Zeng Qiang.

### Lecture details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | A Study of the Evolution Model of Chinese Corporate Culture: An Analysis Based on Grounded Research Methods |
| Speaker | Wang Shaojie (postdoctoral researcher at South China University of Technology, PhD from City University of Macau) |
| Time | 10:00–11:30 a.m., Friday, August 23 |
| Venue | Business School Academic Seminar Room (second floor, Wenxi Building) |
| Chair | Professor Zeng Qiang |

### About the lecture

The lecture will use grounded research methods to analyse the evolution model of Chinese corporate culture, discuss national character and individuality in corporate culture, and explain the implications of the research for localised management practice.

### About the speaker

Wang Shaojie is a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Business Administration, South China University of Technology. His research focuses on the governance of Chinese family businesses and strategic human resource management, and he has published nine papers in journals including *Management World*. Since 2007 he has mainly used qualitative research methods, and he has served as a consultant and provided institutional design and management training for more than 160 companies.

He is a member of the China Democratic National Construction Association. In 2012 he received the third prize for outstanding social science achievement from the Guangdong Federation of Social Sciences and the Publicity Department of the CPC Guangdong Provincial Committee, and was named an outstanding individual in participation in state affairs by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the association. He also received related awards from the association's provincial committee in 2011 and 2010. In addition, he is the principal investigator of one general project funded by the research fund for central universities.
