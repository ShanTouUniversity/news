It is reported that STU's Student Community Management Service Center issued a power outage notice on August 20, 2013. To accommodate low-voltage panel upgrades and cable installation, power to dormitory Building G will be cut on the afternoons of August 20 and 21.

The outages will run from 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. on both days, interrupting the electricity supply to the building. The center advises students to switch off all appliances in advance to prevent equipment damage or other accidents caused by a sudden loss of power.

## Outage Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Reason | Low-voltage panel upgrade and cable installation |
| Affected area | Dormitory Building G |
| Outage time | Aug 20, 2:00-4:00 p.m.; Aug 21, 2:00-4:00 p.m. |
| Reminder | Students should switch off appliances in advance |
| Issued by | Student Community Management Service Center |
| Date signed | August 20, 2013 |
