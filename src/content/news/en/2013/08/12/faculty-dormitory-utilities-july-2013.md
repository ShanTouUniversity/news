It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University announced arrangements on August 12 for querying and paying July 2013 utility bills for faculty and staff dormitories. The latest meter readings were taken on August 1 and 2, while the previous readings were taken on July 1.

## Query and Review Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible residents | Faculty and staff dormitory residents of Shantou University |
| Billing month | July 2013 |
| Latest meter-reading dates | August 1–2, 2013 |
| Previous meter-reading date | July 1, 2013 |
| Query method | Log in to the Shantou University utility management system at power.stu.edu.cn |
| Objection review period | August 12–16, 2013 |
| Review location | Water and Electricity Center |
| Telephone | 2489 |

The Resource Management Office advises eligible faculty and staff to visit the Water and Electricity Center during the specified period if they have questions or objections regarding their utility bill information. After the review period, charges without objections will be deducted by the Finance Office from the relevant August salary accounts. Faculty and staff without salary accounts should pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
