Staggered power outages will affect student dormitory Buildings EF and CD on Sept 2, it is reported, as part of the installation of the air-conditioning distribution wiring for Dormitory Buildings A to G at Shantou University. The Student Community Management and Service Center has issued a reminder asking residents of the affected buildings to prepare in advance.

## Outage Schedule

| Building | Date | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EF | Sept 2, 2013 | 8:30 a.m. – 12:00 noon |
| CD | Sept 2, 2013 | 1:30 p.m. – 6:30 p.m. |

The Student Community Management and Service Center advises students to switch off electrical appliances before the outage begins for their building, so as to avoid damage to equipment or other accidents caused by an abrupt loss of power or by the restoration of supply.
