It is reported that the Human Resources Office, the University Labor Union and the Resources Management Office of Shantou University jointly issued an obituary notice on August 14, making arrangements related to the farewell ceremony.

According to the notice, units and individuals wishing to send wreaths are asked to contact the Office for Retired and Veteran Faculty and Staff (tel: 82902660) or Anju (tel: 82517190) before 4:00 p.m. on August 14.

## Shuttle Schedule for August 15

The notice also released the pick-up points and departure times for the shuttles on August 15:

| Pick-up Point | Departure Time |
|---------------|----------------|
| Outside the Reception Room on campus | 13:00 |
| Entrance of Anju | 13:10 |
| Entrance of the Fourth Hospital in Dajiao | 13:20 |
| University bus stop at Baiheyuan | 13:30 |

The notice was issued jointly by the Human Resources Office, the University Labor Union and the Resources Management Office, and dated August 14, 2013.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office, Labor Union, Resources Management Office)
