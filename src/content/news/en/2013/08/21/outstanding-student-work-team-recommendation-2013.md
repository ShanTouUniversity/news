It is reported that the Guangdong Professional Committee for University Student Affairs has launched its 2013 selection of outstanding student work teams and individuals. The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on August 21 to organize recommendations from colleges and residential colleges.

## Eligibility

| Award | Eligible candidates |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Team | Student education and management departments, or student work teams of faculties and departments, at member universities of the committee |
| Outstanding Individual | Student affairs staff at member universities who actively take part in committee activities and pay membership dues, including faculty counselors, deputy party secretaries and staff of relevant functional departments |

## Criteria

An outstanding team should have a correct political orientation, a united leadership with sound systems, and achievements recognized by awards at or above the provincial level. An outstanding individual should have high political quality, a passion for student work, more than three years of experience in student affairs, and outstanding performance. Frontline counselors, as well as those who have published papers or undertaken research projects at or above the university level, will be given priority.

## Procedure

The selection proceeds through university-level preliminary review, review by the professional committee, and recognition at the 2013 annual meeting.

## Submission

In principle, each college or residential college may recommend one team and one individual. Materials must be submitted by October 10: hard copies to the Education Management Section (Room 106, Administration Center) and electronic copies to lslin@stu.edu.cn, packaged with the file name "Shantou University + name of the individual".

| Category | Required materials |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Team | Recommendation form and award certificates, plus team members' papers and research project materials |
| Outstanding Individual | Recommendation form, copies of certificates, a JPG photo of daily life (no larger than 500K), and papers and research project materials |

> ⚠️ The recommendation forms and other attachments are available on the OA system.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
