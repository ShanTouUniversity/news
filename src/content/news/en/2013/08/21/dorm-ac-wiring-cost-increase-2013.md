It is reported that the air-conditioning wiring installation project for student dormitories A to G at Shantou University officially began on August 2. During construction, to meet the electricity demand of the dormitory air conditioners, the user departments requested additional air conditioning units, which in turn required more power cables in the corridors and indoor distribution lines.

According to estimates by the Capital Construction Office, the above changes will increase the project cost by about 65,000 yuan, which is within the 10 percent limit stipulated by the university.

In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Measures for the Implementation of Management of Bidding for Capital Construction Projects, the Capital Construction Office publicized the cost adjustment on August 21. Any department or individual within the university who has objections may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Capital Construction Office before 5:30 p.m. on August 23.
