It is reported that, as part of the university's Party mass line education and practice campaign, the Labor Union of Shantou University will hold a staff representative forum at 3:00 p.m. on Friday, August 23, in the Labor Union meeting room to hear faculty and staff opinions on the university's relevant work.

## Participation Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., Friday, August 23 |
| Venue | Labor Union meeting room |
| Participants | Each departmental labor union sends representatives: two from unions with 60 or more members and one from those with fewer than 60 |
| Name list | Submit to the University Labor Union on the morning of August 23 |
| Contact | Tel: 86502983, 86503197; Email: o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
