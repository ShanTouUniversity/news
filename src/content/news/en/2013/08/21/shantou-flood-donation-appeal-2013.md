It is reported that Shantou has been hit by exceptionally heavy rainstorms in recent days, with severe flooding in Chaoyang, Chaonan and other areas. Li Ka-shing donated HK$10 million to the disaster areas after learning of the situation. On August 21, the General Administration Office, the Labor Union and the Youth League Committee of Shantou University jointly issued an urgent notice calling on all faculty, staff and students to donate and help the flood victims.

## How to Donate

| Donor | Arrangements |
| --- | --- |
| Faculty and staff | Pledge donations by phone or email to their department union; department unions are to compile the pledges on August 21 and report them to the university Labor Union before 11:00 am on August 22 (email `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`) |
| Pledged amounts | Deducted from salaries by the Finance Office |
| Students | Those who wish to donate are to deliver their donations to the Youth League Committee office before 5:30 pm on August 21 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Administration Office, Labor Union, Youth League Committee)
