It is reported that the kindergarten run by the Resources Management Office of Shantou University is recruiting **two kindergarten teachers** from the public, one of whom will also serve as an administrative assistant. Successful candidates will be hired as auxiliary contract staff; their personnel relationship will not be transferred to STU, and their pay and benefits will follow the university's relevant standards.

## Requirements

Applicants should be hardworking, caring and responsible; be aged 40 or under and in good health, in line with family-planning policy; and hold at least a secondary vocational qualification (or above) in early-childhood education, together with a kindergarten teacher qualification certificate and an industry health certificate. Kindergarten teaching experience is preferred, as is a full-time bachelor's degree in early-childhood education.

## Duties

| Duty | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Teaching and care | Draw up care and education plans and organize class activities |
| Child safety | Protect children's safety while they are at the kindergarten |
| Daily life and health care | Manage children's daily routines and support health-care work |
| Home-kindergarten communication | Keep in touch with parents and report on children's progress |
| Research and environment | Take part in teaching-research activities and help decorate the kindergarten |
| Duty shifts | Serve on holiday duty rosters |

The teacher who also serves as administrative assistant will additionally handle administrative, teaching-support and logistics tasks; guide and supervise children's meals; keep custody of property and facilities; inspect equipment safety; manage teaching materials; and take charge of the kindergarten's accounting and duty arrangements.

## How to Apply

Applicants should send a résumé and photocopies of their academic certificate, teacher qualification certificate, health certificate, training certificate, family-planning certificate and ID card to the STU kindergarten under the Resources Management Office by email or post **before 5:00 p.m. on September 10**. Shortlisted candidates will be notified separately for an interview, where the originals of the above documents must be presented for verification.
