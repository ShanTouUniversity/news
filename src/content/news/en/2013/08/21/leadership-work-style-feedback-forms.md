It is reported that, as part of the "study and education, listening to opinions" stage of the first batch of the Party's mass line education campaign, Shantou University is soliciting opinions on the work style of its leadership team from all general Party branches and directly affiliated Party branches.

According to arrangements by the Discipline Inspection Commission Office, each branch should send representatives to Room 422 of the New Administrative Center to collect the feedback forms **on Wednesday, August 21**, and return them **by 5:00 p.m. on Friday, August 23**. Inquiries may be made at 86502346.
