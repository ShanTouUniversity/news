It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on August 21, scheduling a graduate employment meeting for 3:00 p.m. on Thursday, August 22, in Room 213 of the New Administration Center. It is the second such meeting in a week, following the one held on August 16.

## Participants

- Party branch secretaries (and deputy secretaries) of all colleges and residential colleges
- Counselors responsible for employment work
- Staff of the Graduate Employment Guidance Center under the Student Affairs Office

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
