It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department issued a notice on August 21, 2013, outlining arrangements for the Mass Line Education and Practice Campaign.

To advance the campaign, Party branches are asked to complete two tasks by September 11:

## Tasks

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Hold a special democratic life meeting |
| 2 | Hold an organizational life meeting by grassroots Party branches to study documents on the Mass Line campaign (university Party leaders will join their respective branches); branches will set their own time and venue |
| Deadline | Before September 11, 2013 |
| Reporting | Promptly report progress to the Office of the Leading Group for the Education Campaign, email o_zzb@stu.edu.cn, phone 86502345 |
| Issued by | Leading Group for the Education Campaign, Organization and United Front Work Department |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
