It is reported that Shantou University has entered the materials display stage of its 2013 professional title application process. From **August 21 to 29**, the university is displaying this year's professional title application materials in Meeting Room 330 of the New Administration Center, and faculty and staff are welcome to review them.

The materials are on display daily from **8:30 to 11:30 a.m. and 2:30 to 5:30 p.m.**, excluding Saturdays and Sundays.

The university said that anyone with objections to the displayed application materials may report them by phone or email to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Human Resources Office **before August 29**. Reports must be made under the reporter's real name.
