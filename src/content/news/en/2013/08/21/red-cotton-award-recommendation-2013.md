It is reported that, according to a document of the Guangdong Professional Committee for University Student Affairs (Yue Gao Xue Wei [2013] No. 6), the 2013 Guangdong Red Cotton Award for student affairs has opened for nominations. The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on August 21 to organize recommendations from colleges and residential colleges.

## Eligibility and Criteria

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible candidates | Full-time student affairs staff at universities who have long worked on the frontline with a cumulative 20 or more years of service, including deputy party secretaries of faculties and departments, counselors, and staff of student affairs functional departments |
| Criteria | Firm political orientation, dedication to student work, creative conduct of ideological and political education for university students, and outstanding performance |
| Quota | In principle, each college or residential college may recommend one candidate |

## Procedure

The selection proceeds through university-level preliminary review, review by the professional committee, and recognition at the 2013 annual meeting.

## Submission

Recommendation materials must be submitted by 5 p.m. on October 10: hard copies to the Education Management Section (Room 106, Administration Center) and electronic copies to lslin@stu.edu.cn.

| Material | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Recommendation form | Must bear an official seal |
| Personal profile | About 100 words |
| Photo of daily life | JPG format, no larger than 500K |
| Reflections on student work | Written in the first person, about 2,000 words |

> ⚠️ The recommendation form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
