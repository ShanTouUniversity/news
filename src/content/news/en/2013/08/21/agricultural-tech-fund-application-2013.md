It is reported that, starting in 2013, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and the Provincial Department of Finance began implementing a special program for commercializing agricultural science and technology achievements. The Research Office of Shantou University has arranged this year's application work, organizing university units to submit materials in line with the provincial notice and the 2013 Guidelines for Applications to the Guangdong Provincial Agricultural Sci-Tech Achievements Commercialization Fund.

The program applies a quota system, and Shantou University may submit two projects this year. The university will organize recommendations through open application, expert review, and selection of the best candidates for submission.

## Application Materials

Applicants are required to submit a Project Application Form and a Feasibility Study Report (prepared according to the attached outline), together with supporting documents:

- Copies of the legal-person certificate and organization code certificate
- Proof of achievements
- Certification of intellectual property ownership and authorized use
- Audit reports and financial statements
