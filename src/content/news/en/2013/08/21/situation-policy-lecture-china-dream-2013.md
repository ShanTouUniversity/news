It is reported that, in line with the teaching requirements for Situation and Policy education in the first half of 2013, the Social Sciences Department of Shantou University has made the Chinese Dream a unified teaching topic and will hold a special study lecture from **9:00 to 11:00 a.m. on Saturday, August 24**, at the University Auditorium. Professor Nie Wenjun, who holds a PhD, will speak on **"The China Dream and You and Me."**

All students taking the *Situation and Policy Education* course in the 2013 summer semester will attend the lecture. They must enter the venue between **8:45 and 8:55 a.m.**, and course instructors will handle organization and attendance.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | "The China Dream and You and Me" |
| Speaker | Professor Nie Wenjun, PhD |

| Venue | University Auditorium |

| Organization | Course instructors will handle organization and attendance |
| Assessment | Each student must write a paper related to the lecture, using the unified template (see attachment), and submit it to the course instructor; the paper is the main basis for course assessment |
