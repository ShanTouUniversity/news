It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on August 21, 2013, to organize students to participate in the *Integrity Heritage* 2013/14 team competition invited by the Youth and Moral Education Section of the Hong Kong Independent Commission Against Corruption (ICAC). Jointly organized by the ICAC and Hong Kong tertiary institutions and youth service organizations, the program focuses on integrity.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Categories | (A) Promotion proposal to promote integrity among youth; (B) Research report on either: handling integrity challenges and making wise choices, or youth views on money and a fulfilling life |
| Team size | 2–4 members per team, one entry per team in either category A or B |
| Requirements | 2,500–3,000 words (Chinese or English, excluding appendices etc.), A4 single-sided printing, page numbers, 2.5 cm margins, font size 13, total word count at the end |
| Deadline | October 10, 2013 |
| Submission | Hard copy to the Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center) and electronic copy to zjliu@stu.edu.cn with subject “Integrity Heritage Youth Program” |
| Selection | University review before submission to the organizing committee; up to 10 teams per category will be shortlisted to give a 10-minute presentation in English and 5-minute Q&A for final judging |
| Awards | Shortlisted teams may receive scholarships and funded participation (including return airfare and accommodation) in exchange activities and the Youth Summit in Hong Kong from April 24 to 27, 2014 |
| Contact | Mr. Liu Zaijun, Student Aid Management Center, phone 86502249, email zjliu@stu.edu.cn; website www.icac.org.hk.yip.html |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |
