It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold a graduate employment meeting at 9:00 a.m. on Friday, August 16, in Room 213 of the New Administration Center.

## Participants

- Party branch secretaries (and deputy secretaries) of all colleges and residential colleges
- Counselors responsible for employment work
- Staff of the Graduate Employment Guidance Center under the Student Affairs Office

The meeting will focus on graduate employment work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
