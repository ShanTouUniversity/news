It is reported that Shantou University's Laboratory Work Committee, Academic Affairs Office and Research Office have jointly launched the reporting of 2012/2013 laboratory information statistics, covering all colleges, departments, centers and divisions of the university. The work follows the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education's notice on reporting laboratory information statistics for higher education institutions in the 2012/2013 academic year (Yue Jiao Zhuang Bei Han [2013] No. 6).

## Reporting Scope

The reporting covers laboratories at all levels and of all types, including teaching laboratories, research laboratories and other laboratories.

## Reporting Contents

Units must complete five types of forms, templates for which are provided as attachments to the notice:

| Form | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Form 1 | Laboratory Directory |
| Form 2 | Experiment Delivery Records |
| Form 3 | Laboratory Personnel |
| Form 4 | Laboratory Basic Information |
| Form 5 | Laboratory Funding |

## Statistical Period and Deadline

The data cover the 2012/2013 academic year, comprising its three semesters — the summer, fall and spring semesters. Units must complete their submissions by September 10, 2013.

## Submission Method and Receiving Offices

Materials should be submitted first in electronic form and then as printed copies, with printed forms collected by each laboratory director. Materials for teaching laboratories go to the Academic Affairs Office, contact Chen Yangzhou, email yzchen@stu.edu.cn, telephone 86503202. Materials for research and other laboratories go to the Research Office, contact Chen Jinshan, email chenjs@stu.edu.cn, telephone 86502583.

## Notes

The two offices reminded units to read the instructions carefully before filling in the forms, to carry out the reporting conscientiously and to strengthen data review so as to ensure completeness and accuracy.
