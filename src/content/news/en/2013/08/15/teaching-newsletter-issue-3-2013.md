It is reported that the 2013 Issue No. 3 (No. 16 Overall) of *Teaching Newsletter*, Shantou University's publication for exchanges on undergraduate teaching, has been released. Running 25 pages, the issue features sections on major news, academic affairs developments, teaching information, student achievements and education trends.

On May 17, Wu Aihua, Director of the Engineering Division of the Department of Higher Education at the Ministry of Education, was invited to campus to give a special report on the ministry's "Several Opinions on Comprehensively Improving the Quality of Higher Education," commonly known as the "30 Articles on Higher Education Quality." The session was chaired by Vice President Wen Xiaobo, with Executive President Gu Peihua delivering closing remarks. Wu gave a systematic interpretation covering the strategic plan for raising quality, the requirements of connotative development and reform of the talent-training system; Gu said the report offers valuable guidance for the university's pursuit of connotative development and better talent training. College leaders and faculty and students of the Institute of Higher Education attended the session.

Public-service courses were a key focus of the issue. The first "Public Service Course Practice Bases" were unveiled in succession with two partners of the course *Toward the Ocean* — Danyang Middle School in Shantou and CITIC Resort. On May 25, STU teachers and students held an education activity at Danyang Middle School, where Associate Professor Sun Zewei presented the base plaque to Principal Jiang Wenhao; on June 1-2, more than 100 teachers and students visited CITIC Longhutan and joined district authorities in holding a "Marine Ecological Protection" public-service day, during which the base was officially unveiled. In addition, the fourth training program for public-service course instructors opened on April 17, and the courses were also showcased at the campus open day on June 10.

On teaching information, the issue announced the results of the 2012-2013 Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Teaching Award, with Associate Professor Yu Jin of the College of Engineering receiving the award in the science and engineering category, as well as the results of textbook development and publication subsidies for the first half of 2013. It also reported on the completion review of 83 student innovation experiment and innovation-and-entrepreneurship training projects, of which 62 passed and five were rated excellent.

In the student achievements section, the Business School team won third place at the 49th International Collegiate Business Strategy Competition, while the College of Engineering team took second prize in Project II (the figure-eight track group) at the national finals of the National Undergraduate Engineering Training Comprehensive Ability Competition. The education trends section examines the online education changes prompted by Tsinghua University's joining of the US online platform edX, and the teaching of critical thinking in American gifted education.
