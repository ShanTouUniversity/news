It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University has issued a construction notice for repairs to sections of the concrete road in the west campus. Work began on August 15, 2013, and is scheduled for completion before September 5.

The project has been contracted to Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. The repair covers sections of the road running from in front of Building 5 to the 36 staff dormitories.

## Work Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | West campus concrete road repair |
| Contractor | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Scope | Sections of the road from in front of Building 5 to the 36 staff dormitories |
| Start date | August 15, 2013 |
| Planned completion | Before September 5, 2013 |

Noise and dust may affect nearby areas during the work, and the university asks for the understanding of staff and students. Pedestrians and vehicles are advised to take care when passing the work area.
