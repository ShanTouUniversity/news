It is reported that the Affiliated School of Shantou University announced arrangements for its fall 2013 Grade-7 freshmen. New students were to register at 8:30 a.m. on Friday, August 30, in the Grade-7 classrooms on the second floor of the Affiliated School, and attend an orientation session from 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. that afternoon.

Freshmen were asked to bring a copy of their household registration (only the pages for the head of household and the student) and four one-inch color photos, with the photos to be handed to the class teacher after the semester began.

## Registration Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | 8:30 a.m., Friday, August 30 |
| Orientation | 3:00 p.m.–5:00 p.m. on the same day |
| Venue | Grade-7 classrooms, second floor, Affiliated School |

## Required Materials

| Material | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Household registration copy | Pages for head of household and student only |
| One-inch color photos | Four copies, to be submitted to the class teacher after school starts |
