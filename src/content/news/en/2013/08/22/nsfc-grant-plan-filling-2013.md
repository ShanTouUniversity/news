It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University has arranged the submission of grant project plans for projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) in 2013. Project leaders whose projects received funding can log on to the NSFC Internet-based Science Information System (ISIS, http://isis.nsfc.gov.cn), enter the "Grant Project Plan" section, obtain the approval notice and the plan template, and complete the form as required.

## Filling in and Submitting the Plan

| Milestone | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Before September 6 | Project leaders fill in and submit the plan; the Research Office reviews it and forwards it to the NSFC |
| Same period | The NSFC reviews the electronic plan, based on the version first submitted |
| Before September 13 | Those not approved revise the plan as instructed and submit it again |
| Before September 23 | Those approved print two paper copies (double-sided) and deliver them to the Research Office |

The Research Office notes that the content of the paper version must match the electronic version.
