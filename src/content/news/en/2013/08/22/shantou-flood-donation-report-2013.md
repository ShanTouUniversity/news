It is reported that the General Administration Office and the Labor Union of Shantou University released a report on August 22 on donations for the Shantou flood relief. As of 4:00 pm on August 22, faculty and staff across the university had pledged a total of 160,420 yuan, with a detailed list of the donations attached.

According to the notice, any additional donations can be compiled and reported to the university Labor Union before 11:00 am on August 23 at `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Administration Office, Labor Union)
>
> ⚠️ The donation details and other attachments are available in the original OA notice.
