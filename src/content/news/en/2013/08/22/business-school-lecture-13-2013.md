The Shantou University Business School will host an academic lecture from **10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Monday, August 26**, in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of Wenxi. Dr. Huang Huiling, who holds a doctoral degree in economics from Peking University, will speak on **“US Influence on the Trade-Related International Intellectual Property System: The ACTA Perspective.”** Professor Qin Zhong will chair the lecture.

The talk will review international initiatives to raise intellectual property protection and examine how the United States and other developed countries have pushed for stronger protection through free trade agreements and regional economic agreements. It will also analyze the international competitive strategy challenges facing companies and explain the relationship between innovation, trade, and intellectual property and how that relationship works.

Huang holds a PhD in economics from Peking University and researches the world economy and intellectual property law. With innovation, trade, and growth as her main threads, she studies cross-strait international competition, the global economy, and trends in intellectual property law from a globalization perspective. She has published five papers in journals including Asia-Pacific Economy.
