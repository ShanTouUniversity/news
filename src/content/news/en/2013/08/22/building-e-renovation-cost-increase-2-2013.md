It is reported that the renovation project of Building E, Phase II, at Shantou University started on July 1. During construction, reservoir water supply pipes were added to save water in the restrooms.

In addition, to improve the appearance of the building and protect its finishes from rain damage, the project added artificial marble slabs at the corridor doors and window sills of the B staircase on both sides, laid polished floor tiles in the corridors, repaired and replaced the stainless steel plates of the expansion joints, and refurbished the copper strips on the steps of the B staircase. At the request of the user department, the contractor also changed the aluminum alloy louver doors for the outdoor air-conditioning units and added ceiling lighting circuits in the student activity area.

According to estimates by the Capital Construction Office, the above changes will increase the project cost by about 45,000 yuan, and the cumulative increase together with previous changes remains within the 10 percent limit stipulated by the university. The Capital Construction Office publicized this cost adjustment on August 22.

In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Measures for the Implementation of Management of Bidding for Capital Construction Projects, any department or individual within the university who has objections may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Capital Construction Office before 5:30 p.m. on August 26.
