It is reported that Shantou University has started processing applications for the 2013 reward and subsidy for the high school entrance exam (Zhongkao) results of children of in-service faculty and staff. Employees whose children studied in the Shantou urban area and took this year's Zhongkao are eligible to submit materials to the Resources Management Office.

## Materials Required

Applicants should provide the original and a photocopy of their child's Zhongkao admission ticket, high school admission notice and Zhongkao score report, and note the employee's own name, work unit and contact information on the materials.

| Material | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Zhongkao admission ticket | Original and photocopy |
| Admission notice | Original and photocopy |
| Zhongkao score report | Original and photocopy |
| Employee information | Name, work unit and contact details |

## How to Submit

The materials should be delivered to the General Office of the Resources Management Office **by September 15**. For inquiries, employees may call `86502827`.
