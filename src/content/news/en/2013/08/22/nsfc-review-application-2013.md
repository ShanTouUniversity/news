It is reported that the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) has released its 2013 review results. Under the Regulations on the National Natural Science Foundation of China, applicants who disagree with a decision not to fund their projects may file a reconsideration application within the prescribed period.

## Application Procedure

| Step | Time and requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Online application | From August 22 to September 6, applicants obtain a username and password from the Research Office, log on to https://isis.nsfc.gov.cn, fill in the reconsideration application form online and confirm submission |
| Paper materials | Print two paper copies matching the electronic version, sign them in person and deliver them to the Research Office by September 2 |
| Processing | Applications are handled by the relevant science departments of the NSFC |

## Cases Not Accepted

According to the Research Office, the following cases will not be accepted: disagreement with the academic judgment of review experts; applications filed by someone other than the project applicant; applications submitted after the deadline; and incomplete procedures, such as failure to submit through the ISIS system, a missing paper application form, or a missing signature.
