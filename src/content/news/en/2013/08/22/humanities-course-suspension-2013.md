It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office has issued a suspension notice: one session of the *Humanities Topics* course will not meet on Saturday, August 24, the first week of the summer session, in Lecture Hall 1 because instructor You Xilin of the Institute of Higher Education is on sick leave. The session was scheduled for periods 2–4.

## Suspension Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Humanities Topics* (Class 63308) |
| Instructor | You Xilin (Institute of Higher Education) |
| Suspended session | Periods 2–4, Saturday, August 24, 2013 (Week 1 of the summer session) |
| Venue | Lecture Hall 1 |
| Reason | Instructor on sick leave |
| Make-up class | To be determined |

Students taking the course are requested to inform one another and to watch for further notice on the make-up session.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
