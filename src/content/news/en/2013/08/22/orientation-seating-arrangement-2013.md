As part of efforts to prepare freshman orientation education for 2013, Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has released the auditorium seating arrangements for this year's orientation activities. The activities include an introduction to Shantou University, a presentation on the English enhancement program, a briefing on the credit system, security and confidentiality education, instruction on university rules and discipline, library orientation, a lecture on basic knowledge of the Communist Party of China, and distinguished-guest lectures.

The Student Affairs Office has asked each college to organize its freshmen to attend these activities on time. Seating for the opening ceremony will be announced separately, and detailed seating assignments for each activity are provided in the attachment to the notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The orientation seating attachment is available in the original notice on the OA system.
