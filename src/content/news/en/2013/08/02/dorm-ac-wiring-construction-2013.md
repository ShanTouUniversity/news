It is reported that the Infrastructure Office issued a construction notice on August 2, 2013, announcing that the air-conditioning power wiring installation for Student Dormitory Blocks A through G would break ground the same day and be completed before September 4.

The project is undertaken by Guangdong Runmin Jianan Engineering Co., Ltd., with a winning bid of 675,200 yuan. The work covers laying cables from new distribution boxes to each floor, modifying incoming lines for floor meter boxes, and installing air-conditioning outlets and wiring in all rooms across Blocks A through G.

| Start date | August 2 |
| Planned completion | Before September 4 |
| Scope | New distribution box to floor cables, floor meter box incoming-line modifications, AC outlets and wiring in all rooms |
| Issued by | Infrastructure Office |

Noise and dust are expected during construction, and nearby students and staff are asked for their understanding. Pedestrians passing the work areas should watch for safety notices and follow on-site management.
