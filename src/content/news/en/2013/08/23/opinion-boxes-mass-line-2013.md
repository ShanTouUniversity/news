It is reported that, in accordance with provincial arrangements and the university's campaign to implement the Party's mass line, Shantou University has opened dedicated channels for faculty and students to offer comments and suggestions on strengthening the work style of the university leadership and its members.

## Two Ways to Submit Feedback

Two channels are available, one on site and one online:

| Channel | Details |
| --- | --- |
| On-site opinion boxes | Two drop boxes are placed on the first floor of the New Administration Center — one for comments addressed to the 22nd Provincial Supervision Group, the other for comments addressed to the university's own supervision group |
| Online mailbox | The university's special website for the mass line education and practice campaign (http://mt.stu.edu.cn:8015/index.aspx ) provides a feedback mailbox for submissions online |

## Channels Open to the Whole University

The two boxes respectively serve the opinion-collection work of the provincial supervision group and the university supervision group. Faculty and students may raise views on the work style of the university leadership and its members, or report related issues and suggestions through the mailbox on the campaign website. The university said it welcomes active participation and input from faculty and students as it works to improve its conduct and work style.

The arrangements were made by the office of the university's education and practice campaign leading group.
