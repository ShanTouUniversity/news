It is reported that Shantou University has been installing air conditioners in all student dormitories, including graduate dormitories, during the summer break, and that the air-conditioning units and the related power distribution lines are expected to be fully completed in early September. The university has issued several reminders about electricity use before and after the air conditioners come into service.

## New Distribution Lines in Service on September 6

Because the existing lines cannot handle the load of the air conditioners, dormitories whose power has not yet been connected are asked not to connect it themselves. The university advises these dormitories to wait until the new power distribution lines come into service on September 6, or until they receive a notice from the university, before using the air conditioners, so as to avoid line failures and safety hazards.

## Remote Controls to Be Distributed in Early September

The university tentatively plans to distribute air-conditioner remote controls at each dormitory area in early September. The exact collection points and times will be announced separately on the campus information network (http://notes.stu.edu.cn ). Students are advised to watch for the announcements.

## Plea for Energy Saving and Safe Electricity Use

The university also reminded students to save energy, conserve electricity and use power safely, and welcomes suggestions from faculty and students on saving electricity.
