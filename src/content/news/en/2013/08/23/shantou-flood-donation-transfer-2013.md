It is reported that the General Administration Office and the Labor Union of Shantou University released a report on August 23 with the latest figures for donations to the Shantou flood-hit areas. As of 11:00 am on August 23, faculty and staff across the university had pledged a total of 162,170 yuan, and the money was transferred to the account of the Shantou Charity Federation that morning. A detailed list of the donations is attached.

Earlier, on August 21, the General Administration Office, the Labor Union and the Youth League Committee of Shantou University jointly issued an urgent notice calling on all faculty, staff and students to donate to the flood-hit areas in Chaoyang, Chaonan and other parts of Shantou, which had been struck by exceptionally heavy rainstorms. Li Ka-shing donated HK$10 million to the disaster areas after learning of the situation. A progress report released on August 22 showed that faculty and staff had then pledged 160,420 yuan.
