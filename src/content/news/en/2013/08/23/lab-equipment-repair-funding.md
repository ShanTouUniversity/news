It is reported that Shantou University has activated the equipment-repair portion of its 2013 "Undergraduate Laboratory Teaching Support and Improvement" special fund, which subsidizes teaching units in maintaining laboratory instruments and equipment for undergraduate teaching.

## Application Arrangements

According to the Academic Affairs Office, teaching units with faulty equipment that genuinely requires university-funded repair may submit an application form to the office **before September 16**. The university will review all applications and provide funding where appropriate.

In addition, the university plans to open a second round of repair-funding applications in the fourth quarter of this year for equipment that breaks down later.

## University Requirements

The office also stressed that each unit should cover equipment maintenance costs on its own whenever possible, and apply for university support only when the costs exceed its own capacity.
