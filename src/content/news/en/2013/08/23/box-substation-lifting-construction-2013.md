It is reported that, under the construction plan for the capacity expansion project of the power distribution facilities serving student dormitories Blocks A to F, Shantou University will hoist box substations on August 26. Because the schedule is tight, the contractor will complete the transport and lifting work on that day, and some campus roads will be temporarily closed during the operation.

## Working Hours and Transport Route

The hoisting work is scheduled for August 26 from 7:00 to 17:00. The transport route runs as follows: campus gate → in front of the university motto monument → Engineering Building → Science Building → in front of Graduate Dormitory Block L (Third Canteen) → open ground behind student dormitories ABC.

Vehicles will stop and carry out work at two locations: in front of Block L and on the open ground behind dormitories ABC.

## Two Road Sections Closed for One Day

Affected by the hoisting work, the roads at the two stopping points will be temporarily closed for one day. The university advises vehicles and pedestrians passing through these sections to take other routes, follow on-site directions and pay attention to safety.

The construction arrangement was issued by the Capital Construction Office on August 23.
