It is reported that STU's Student Community Management Service Center issued a power outage notice on August 23, 2013. Because low-voltage panel upgrades and cable installation work is required in dormitory Building G that day, power to the building will be cut on the afternoon of August 23.

The outage will run from 2:00 to 6:00 p.m., interrupting the electricity supply to the building. The center advises students to switch off all appliances in advance to prevent equipment damage or other accidents caused by a sudden loss of power. On the afternoons of August 20 and 21, the building had already lost power for the same construction project.
