It is reported that the Network and Information Center issued a notice on August 1, 2013, announcing air-conditioning repairs for the area housing the library network equipment room from August 2 to 4, during which campus network service in the area may be affected.

According to the notice, room temperatures may rise during the repairs, and core network equipment could malfunction, potentially interrupting network service across the area in severe cases. The center stated it would take emergency measures to keep the network running as steadily as possible.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Repair period | August 2 to August 4 |
| Work | Air-conditioning repairs for the area housing the library network equipment room |
| Affected area | Library and nearby teaching and office buildings |
| Recovery | Service will return to normal gradually after the work, with no separate notice |
| Phone | 86503400 (campus short number 883400) |
| Email | stunic@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Network and Information Center |

Users in the affected buildings are advised to plan ahead and contact the center if network problems occur during the repairs.
