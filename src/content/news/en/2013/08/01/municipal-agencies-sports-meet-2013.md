It is reported that the 1st Shantou Municipal Agencies Sports Meet, jointly organized by the Municipal Agencies Work Committee and the Municipal Sports Bureau, is about to kick off. Shantou University will field teams in five events: table tennis, badminton, tennis, Chinese chess, and Go. Registration is coordinated by the university labor union.

Each event has its own registration window, start date, and venue, as follows:

| Event | Registration Period | Start Date | Venue |
|-------|---------------------|------------|-------|
| Tennis | Aug 1 – Aug 6 | Aug 22 | Xinghu Tennis Court |
| Chinese Chess | Aug 15 – Aug 25 | Sep 14 | Municipal Social Sports Instruction Center |
| Go | Aug 15 – Aug 23 | Sep 14 | Municipal Social Sports Instruction Center |
| Badminton | Sep 10 – Sep 18 | Oct 19 | National Table Tennis Training Base |
| Table Tennis | Sep 23 – Sep 30 | Nov 9 | Chaoshan Gymnasium Annex |

Faculty and staff wishing to compete are required to submit a medical insurance certificate, a copy of their second-generation ID card along with the original, and a one-inch color photo (including a digital copy). If entries for any event exceed the quota, the team leader for that event will coordinate the final selection.

Detailed regulations for each event are provided in the attachments. Inquiries about registration can be directed to the university labor union at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, Labor Union)
>
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
