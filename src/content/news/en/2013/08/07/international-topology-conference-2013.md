It is reported that the 2013 International Topology Conference, hosted and organised by Shantou University, will be held at Shantou University from August 10 to 16. The conference chair is Liu Yingming, an academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and a professor at Sichuan University.

During the conference, Professor Jan van Mill of Vrije Universiteit in the Netherlands will give a series of lectures totalling about 11 hours. A number of well-known Chinese topologists will also attend and present their research.

The academic talks will take place in Room 205 of the College of Science, from the morning of August 11 to the morning of August 15. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.

The conference handbook has been published on the STU Department of Mathematics website and is available at http://math.stu.edu.cn/_admin/uploads/huiyishouce.pdf.
