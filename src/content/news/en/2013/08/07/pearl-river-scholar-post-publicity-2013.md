It is reported that, after a recommendation by the university and an expert review by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University plans to establish a Pearl River Scholar post in the discipline of chemistry and to appoint Professor Huang Xiaochun as a Pearl River Scholar Distinguished Professor. The matter is open for public comment from August 7 to 15.

## Details Under Public Notice

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Discipline | Chemistry |
| Post type | Guangdong higher education Pearl River Scholar post |
| Proposed appointee | Professor Huang Xiaochun |
| Proposed position | Pearl River Scholar Distinguished Professor |
| Public notice period | August 7–15, 2013 |

## Raising Objections

During the public notice period, anyone with objections to the proposed post or appointee may raise them by telephone or in writing with the Teacher Affairs Division of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education or the Human Resources Office of Shantou University. Those raising objections must give or sign their real name and provide specific facts.
