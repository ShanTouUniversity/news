It is reported that the College of Science of Shantou University will host a "Science Salon" seminar at **10:00 on Tuesday, August 6, 2013** in the College Lecture Hall (Li-352), delivered by Distinguished Professor Don H Madison of the Department of Physics, Missouri University of Science and Technology.

## Event details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Theoretical advances in electron-impact ionization of molecules |
| Speaker | Prof. Don H Madison, Missouri University of Science and Technology, USA |
| Time | 10:00, Tuesday, August 6, 2013 |
| Venue | College of Science Lecture Hall (Li-352) |

The talk will review how research on single ionization of molecules by electron impact has evolved. Over the past two decades, studies using high-energy electron collisions, notably electron momentum spectroscopy, have served to test the accuracy of molecular wave functions. More recently, measurements of collision cross sections at low energies have posed greater challenges for theory. The seminar will focus on the current state of comparison between experiment and theory for single ionization of small and large molecules at low and intermediate energies.

Prof. Madison received his PhD from Florida State University in 1972 and has worked on atomic and molecular collision theory for more than four decades. He is recognized as a pioneer in the international quantum collision theory community, with nearly 300 publications in journals including Nature, Nature Physics, Physical Review Letters and Physical Review A, as well as more than 60 invited talks at international conferences.
