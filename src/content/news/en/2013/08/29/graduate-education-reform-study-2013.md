It is reported that the Graduate School has recently forwarded the full text of the Guidelines on Deepening Graduate Education Reform, issued jointly by the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Finance and the National Development and Reform Commission, asking all graduate training units to organize study and discussion sessions.

## Study Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Study material | Full text of the Guidelines on Deepening Graduate Education Reform issued by the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Finance and the National Development and Reform Commission |
| Organized by | All graduate training units |
| Participants | Graduate supervisors, graduate administrators and graduate students |
| Requirements | Study the document and discuss how to further advance graduate education reform and improve training quality in light of the actual circumstances of the university and each unit |
| Issued by | Graduate School |

The guidelines are a programmatic document for deepening graduate education reform at the national level. According to the notice, all graduate training units are required to organize graduate supervisors, graduate administrators and graduate students to study the document carefully, and to hold discussions on further advancing graduate education reform and improving the quality of graduate training in light of the actual circumstances of the university and each unit. The full text is available in the notice attachment.
