It is reported that, in order to prepare for the 2013 orientation and carry forward the spirit of volunteer service, the Graduate School of Shantou University held the 2013 Orientation Staff and Volunteer Training Camp on August 29. The camp was conducted in two sessions, one for orientation staff and the other for orientation volunteers.

## Training Schedule

| Session | Participants | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First session | Orientation staff | Aug 29, 15:00—18:00 | Meeting Room 426, New Administration Center |
| Second session | Orientation volunteers | Aug 29, 20:00—21:30 | Teaching Building D |

## Discipline and Work Requirements

The Graduate School asked participants to observe discipline, follow work arrangements, take part in all training sessions earnestly and complete their assigned tasks on time, so as to ensure the smooth running of the 2013 orientation.
