It is reported that applications for the 2014 National Torch Program, the Key New Products Program and the Soft Science Research Program are now open, and the Research Office of Shantou University has recently begun organizing submissions from relevant campus units and faculty members. In line with the notice of the Ministry of Science and Technology (Document No. Guo Ke Fa Ji [2013] 528), applications for all three programs are submitted through the unified online system of the National Science and Technology Program Application Center (http://program.most.gov.cn).

## Application Arrangements

The Soft Science Research Program is subject to a quota, and the university will select the strongest applications to recommend; the supplementary requirements for the Torch Program and the Key New Products Program are set out in the attachments to the notice. The campus deadlines are as follows:

| Program | Deadline for mailing materials and online recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| National Torch Program and Key New Products Program | 5 p.m., September 1, 2013 |
| National Soft Science Research Program | 5 p.m., September 5, 2013 |

## Contact

The Research Office asks faculty members who intend to apply to contact it as soon as possible so that their applications can be organized and recommended in time. Contacts: Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng; telephone: `86502813`.
