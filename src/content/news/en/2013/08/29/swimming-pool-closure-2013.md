It is reported that the swimming pool of Shantou University will be closed from Saturday, August 31 to Tuesday, September 3 due to equipment maintenance.

Students and staff who plan to swim are advised to take note of the change in opening schedule and to arrange their exercise plans accordingly, avoiding visits during the closure period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
