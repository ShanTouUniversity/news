It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University has issued a notice launching the 2012-2013 academic-year performance review for faculty and staff. The review is carried out under the *Measures for the Assessment of Staff of Public Institutions in Guangdong Province (Trial)* (Yue Ren She Fa No. 125 of 2011) and the *Trial Measures for Performance Assessment of STU Faculty and Staff* (STU Document No. 158 of 2012). The assessment period runs from August 1, 2012 through July 31, 2013, and 22 assessment units at the main campus are taking part, including teaching units as well as support units such as the University Library and the Network and Information Center, and administrative offices.

## Who Is Assessed

The review covers full-time employees who worked at the university for at least half of the assessment period. They fall into five categories: full-time teachers, academic-unit leaders, administrative staff, professional support staff, and skilled service personnel. Assessment records and results are placed in the employees' personnel files.

In addition to the regular assessment, full-time teachers must complete the *STU Annual Teacher Work Sheet*, which can be filled in through MYSTU or at https://zhidao.stu.edu.cn.

## Rating Caps

To limit the distribution of ratings, the review sets caps on the top levels: the number of employees rated "Very Outstanding" may not exceed 15 percent of a unit's participants, and "Very Outstanding" and "Exceeds Expectations" combined may not exceed 30 percent.

## New Staff Evaluation Stage

Unlike in previous years, the university has added a staff evaluation stage this year for personnel assessed by university leaders.

## Submitting Materials

By September 26, each unit must deliver its assessment rules, the roster of its assessment group, and the performance summary table (all bearing the unit's official seal), together with all assessment registration forms, to the Human Resources Office (Room 315, New Administration Center). Electronic versions should be sent to gqzhang@stu.edu.cn.
