It is reported that, to support the 2013 orientation program and help freshmen settle into campus life more quickly, Shantou University had recruited orientation volunteers from across the university, drawing an enthusiastic response from students. Following selection, the university has now confirmed 180 students as volunteers for this year's orientation work.

## Recruitment at a Glance

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment scope | Students across the university |
| Confirmed number | 180 students |
| Service | 2013 orientation volunteering |
| Next steps | Training and work arrangements to be announced separately |
| Issued by | Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and STU Youth Volunteer Association |

Selected students are reminded to get ready in advance, as volunteer training and specific work arrangements will be announced separately later. The full list is available in the attachment on the OA system.
