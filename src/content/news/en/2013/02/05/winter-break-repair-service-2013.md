During the 2013 winter break, from **February 9 through 15**, the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University’s Resource Management Office will provide repair and maintenance duty coverage for carpentry, metalwork, fan repair, clearing services, and emergency repair requests.

University units with facilities problems should use the duty contacts listed in the original OA notice to reach the appropriate repair team or emergency-duty staff.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ The duty numbers are personal mobile contacts and are available in the original notice on the OA system.
