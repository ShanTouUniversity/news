Applications are open for 2013 Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects. Shantou University’s Research Office announced arrangements for five categories: research teams, outstanding young researchers, key projects, open applications, and postdoctoral research start-up projects.

### Main rules

Research-team, key, and open-application projects are subject to application limits, with each school allocating its quota and organizing selection. Outstanding-young-researcher and postdoctoral start-up projects are not subject to quota limits. An applicant may submit only one application in the year. Applications and active Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects generally may not exceed three in total; no more than two may be general projects, and the combined total of key, outstanding-young-researcher, and research-team projects may not exceed one.

Applicants for research-team and key projects must hold senior academic titles and have experience undertaking national or provincial basic-research projects. Research-team coordinators must be no older than 55, and core members must have an average age of no more than 45. Applicants for open projects must be no older than 55. Postdoctoral start-up applicants may not hold an associate-senior or higher title, may not be over 40, must have received their doctorate within the previous three years, and must not have led a provincial-level or higher research project. Outstanding-young-researcher applicants must be no older than 35 and hold a doctoral degree or an associate-senior or higher title.

Average funding levels are RMB 2 million for research teams, including RMB 1.2 million for management projects; RMB 300,000 for key projects; RMB 1 million for outstanding-young-researcher projects; RMB 50,000 to RMB 80,000 for open projects; and RMB 30,000 for postdoctoral start-up projects. Projects begin on October 1, 2013, with durations of five years for research teams, three years for key projects, four years for outstanding-young-researcher projects, and two years for open and postdoctoral start-up projects.

### Schedule

| Date | Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| March 1–21 | Applicants prepare drafts and submit them to their units; units organize expert selection |
| March 22 | Units submit stamped application lists; the Research Office assigns accounts and passwords |
| March 23–31 | Applicants complete and submit applications through the provincial system |
| April 1–6 | The Research Office conducts a formal review and returns comments |
| April 7–8 | The Research Office reviews and submits applications |
| April 9–19 | The provincial foundation office conducts online review |
| April 20–21 | Applicants print, sign, and submit two formal copies to their units |
| April 22 | Units submit two copies of each application |

Applicants should consult the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation website at `http://gdsf.gdstc.gov.cn/` for the project-management and financial-management rules and prepare budgets accordingly. Key-project applications must include a novelty-search report.
