Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) will continue providing academic support during the spring semester. Group-discussion services open on **March 4**, followed by academic consultations on **March 11**.

The SLC offers academic consultations, group discussions, and study classes. It is open daily from **3:00 to 10:00 p.m.** beside the campus post office. Visit `http://slc.stu.edu.cn` for details or call `0754-86502250`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
