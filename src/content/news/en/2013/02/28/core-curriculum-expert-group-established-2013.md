To advance core-curriculum reform, Shantou University’s Teaching Office has established an expert group for core curricula. Ma Fengqi serves as chair, with Cai Yinghui, Huai Xiaofeng, Huang Yan, Liu Xirui, Yin Yegao, Wei Chiju, and Yang Qingjie as members.

The group will discuss academic issues arising during implementation of the core-curriculum plan, review individual core courses and propose revisions, and assess the qualifications of external instructors for core courses and experts invited to the General Education Forum.
