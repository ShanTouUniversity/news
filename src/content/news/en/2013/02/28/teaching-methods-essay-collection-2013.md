It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on February 28, 2013, to launch a collection of papers on teaching methods among all teachers.

Following a university-wide teaching methods exchange meeting, the office aims to encourage the sharing of teaching practices and continuous innovation.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Period | March 1–30, 2013 |
| Theme | Summaries and reflections on course teaching methods and practices |
| Submission | Send to the Academic Affairs Office at o_jwc@stu.edu.cn with subject “Teaching Methods Paper” |
| Phone | 86502416 |
| Follow-up | Selected papers will be compiled into *Shantou University Teaching Methods Collection*, reviewed by experts and awarded with certificates |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

All teachers are welcome to contribute.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
