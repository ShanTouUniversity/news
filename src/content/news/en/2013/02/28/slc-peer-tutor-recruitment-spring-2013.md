To support its academic consultation courses, Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) is recruiting peer tutors for spring 2013. Tutors will share study methods and experience, answer course questions, organize discussions, and provide support based on the learning progress of students they serve.

### Eligibility and schedule

Applicants must be second-year or higher undergraduates at STU’s main campus. They should be responsible, diligent, friendly, and able to communicate effectively. As a general rule, they must rank in the top 20 percent of the assessment for the course they will support; exceptional cases require a statement from the course instructor or another knowledgeable faculty member. Courses seeking tutors include English, French, calculus, linear algebra, advanced mathematics, general physics, data structures, computer organization, numerical methods, assembly programming, signals and systems, circuit theory, engineering graphics, mechanics of materials, automatic control, engineering mechanics, soil mechanics, mathematical analysis, advanced algebra, digital electronics, structural chemistry, organic chemistry, specialized criminal law, broadcasting and hosting, Photoshop, poster design, Illustrator, and InDesign.

Tutors generally serve from Week 3 through Week 16 for two hours per week and receive compensation under the university’s work-study peer-tutor standard. Contact the relevant school or department SLC student liaison for course-specific requirements.

Applicants must submit an application through the Student Work-Study Application System at `http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/` by **10:00 p.m. on Tuesday, March 5**. The recruitment schedule is: applications from February 28 through March 5, interviews on March 6, selection on March 7, unit verification from March 8 through 15, and service beginning March 11.

The SLC can be reached at `0754-86502250` (campus extension `882250`), `slc@stu.edu.cn`, or <http://slc.stu.edu.cn>.
