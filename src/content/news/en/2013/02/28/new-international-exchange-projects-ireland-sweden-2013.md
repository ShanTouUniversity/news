Following its agreement with the University of Northampton in the United Kingdom, Shantou University has signed new university-level cooperation agreements with Dublin City University in Ireland and University West in Sweden. Both agreements will begin operating in **September 2013**.

### Dublin City University

The first intake includes:

- **“3+2” dual-degree/bachelor’s-to-master’s program**: The September 2013 intake is limited to Business School students, with 10 places. After completing three years at STU and meeting English and related requirements, students may complete their undergraduate study at Dublin City University. Eligible students may continue into a one-year master’s program. Students who meet the requirements receive bachelor’s degrees from both universities, while those completing the master’s program receive the relevant Dublin City University master’s degree. STU students receive a 15 percent reduction in the non-EU tuition rate during their study at Dublin City University.
- **Exchange program**: The first intake is open to second- and third-year students in the Business School and School of Liberal Arts, with five places. Students will study at Dublin City University for one academic year with tuition waived by both institutions.

The School of Liberal Arts will select **five** exchange students from its full-time 2011 and 2012 cohorts, corresponding to second- and third-year undergraduates in fall 2013. Applicants should have strong academic records and English skills, maturity, independence, teamwork, and the ability to introduce and promote STU. Exchange students continue to pay tuition at STU and are exempt from Dublin City University tuition, but must cover international travel, living expenses, accommodation, laundry, books, local travel, and personal spending. Estimated expenses are **EUR 700–900 per month (approximately RMB 6,000–8,000)**.

Application materials include the exchange-program application form, GPA and class-ranking evidence, and a copy of English-language test results if available. Submit paper and electronic materials to the School of Liberal Arts Youth League Office, South 201, Liberal Arts Building, by **the end of the workday on April 15**; email electronic materials to `twwxy@stu.edu.cn`. The school will use a written-materials review, written test, and interview to select candidates, then submit them to the partner university for confirmation. Applicants may request one or two semesters; selecting “or Both” on the application indicates willingness to accept either arrangement.

### University West

- **“3+1” or “3+1+1” program**: The program is open to business administration, computer science, and mechanical engineering, with five places per major. Students spend their first three years at STU and the fourth year at University West to complete the undergraduate stage. Students choosing the “3+1+1” option may continue for one year of graduate study at University West. Credits are mutually recognized, and successful students receive degrees from both universities. The fourth-year tuition at University West is SEK 125,000; graduate tuition is subject to the rate published for the 2014 academic year.
- **Exchange program**: Four places are available for STU second- and third-year students to study at University West for one semester with tuition waived. Students must complete 30 credits per semester. Eligible graduate applicants may also apply for eight full scholarships offered by University West.

### March 19 application arrangements

The internal STU application deadline for the University West programs is **April 1**. Applicants must email the “UV Internal Application Form,” a scan of English-language evidence, and a scan of an English transcript to STU’s International Exchange and Cooperation Office at `wscicd@stu.edu.cn`, using “University West Exchange Program Application” as the subject. The office will submit materials to University West for review; nominated and admitted applicants must then complete an online application at <http://www.studyinsweden.se>.

The notice’s main text lists IELTS 6.5 or an equivalent level and CET-6 for the 3+1/3+1+1 programs, while the program attachment lists IELTS 6.0 or an equivalent level for those programs. The sources also differ on exchange eligibility: the main text says second-, third-, and fourth-year students may apply, while the attachment says second- and third-year students. Confirm the applicable requirements with the International Exchange and Cooperation Office before applying.

The University West materials list **April 15** as the external application deadline. Self-funded graduate applicants may apply for eight full University West scholarships, also due April 15. Applicants beyond the tuition-waived exchange quota may study at their own expense; the attachment gives a reference fee of SEK 15,000 for one 7.5-credit course, subject to the rate published for fall 2013.

The School of Liberal Arts will hold a separate briefing on the Dublin City University exchange project from **7:00 to 8:00 p.m. on March 17** in ACC Hall 3. University West representatives will hold a program presentation from **4:00 to 5:30 p.m. on Monday, March 18** in D201. Dr. Anna Jonlund, Ms. Holly Xiao, and Mr. Carl-Fredrik Mile will introduce the programs and answer questions. Further details of the Dublin City University programs will be announced separately.
