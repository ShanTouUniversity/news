Following the application period, Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication has confirmed **40 students** for the spring-semester Public-Welfare Photography course and released a waitlist. The waitlist reflects the fact that some students may enroll in two public-welfare courses and is intended to use course places effectively.

The course runs from Week 3 through Week 10:

- **Weeks 3–5**: Wednesday periods 6 and 7 in Room D304, covering service-learning models, public-welfare photography, team development, photography fundamentals, communication for fieldwork, and social-survey methods;
- **Saturday of Week 5**: eight hours of on-campus photography practice and topic research;
- **Week 6**: portrait-photography instruction and practice;
- **Week 7**: group discussion, public-welfare photography planning, presentations, and feedback;
- **Week 8**: candid and posed photography and appreciation of outstanding works, followed by 16 hours of practical photography work on Saturday and Sunday;
- **Saturday of Week 9**: eight hours of public-welfare photography practice at a primary school or Cunxin Shantang factory;
- **Week 10**: discussion, presentation of results, and experience sharing.

Practical locations other than Room D304 will be announced according to the activity arrangements.
