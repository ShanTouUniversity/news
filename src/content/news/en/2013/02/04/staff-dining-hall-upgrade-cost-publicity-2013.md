The staff dining hall upgrade project at Shantou University began on January 19, 2013. Based on site conditions, the project proposes additional or revised work for air-conditioning equipment and power lines, a bar and hanging cabinets, lightweight-brick filling in a public-bathroom floor structure, serving-counter materials, an upper-wall finish, and aluminum ceilings in private dining rooms.

The Infrastructure Office’s preliminary estimate puts the additional cost at approximately **RMB 40,000**, within the university’s prescribed 10 percent limit for additional items. The university is publicizing the proposed additions. Written comments may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by **5:30 p.m. on February 6**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office, Resource Management Office)
