Shantou University will build a parking area for fleet buses on vacant land west of the swimming pool and north of the seismological station entrance. The Infrastructure Office is organizing the project, which has been awarded to Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. at a winning contract price of **RMB 36,400**.

Work began on **February 4, 2013**, and is scheduled to finish by **February 9**. Construction may generate noise, vibration, and dust. Pedestrians should take care near the site, avoid entering the construction area without authorization from the relevant university office and contractor, and minimize time spent near the site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office, Resource Management Office)
