Shantou University’s Personnel Office launched a process to recommend review experts for the China Scholarship Council, following the council’s request (CSC Letter No. 3002 [2013]). Units were asked to organize recommendations based on national key disciplines, key laboratories, national engineering or technology research centers, key discipline-development programs, and the university’s distinctive strengths.

Each secondary discipline may generally recommend no more than three candidates. Disciplines with a clear national advantage or with limited offerings at Chinese universities and research institutions are exempt from the quota. The process combines individual applications with school-level recommendations.

Units must submit the *Recommended Expert Information Form* to Room 315 of the Administration Center by **February 25, 2013**, and email the electronic version to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. The contact is Fan Jibin at `0754-86502349`.
