Shantou University opened its nomination and recommendation process for candidates for membership of the Chinese Academy of Engineering and the Chinese Academy of Sciences, following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security (Guangdong Human Resources and Social Security Letter No. 229 [2013]).

The process follows the relevant notices of the two academies on candidate nomination, recommendation, and preliminary selection. Eligibility criteria, nomination procedures, and forms can be found on the Chinese Academy of Engineering website at `www.cae.cn` and the Chinese Academy of Sciences website at `http://www.casad.ac.cn/`. Applicants should use the relevant academy’s election system to print the required materials.

Forms must be completed in the required format, printed on A4 paper in **20 copies** with a CD, and accompanied by one copy of representative publications, technical reports, papers, design plans, research reports, and copies of major award certificates. Materials are due at Room 315 of the Administration Center by **February 28, 2013**. For inquiries, call `0754-86502349`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
>
> ⚠️ Consult the OA system and the official websites of the Chinese Academy of Engineering and Chinese Academy of Sciences for detailed eligibility criteria and templates.
