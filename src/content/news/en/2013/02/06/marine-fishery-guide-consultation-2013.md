It is reported that the Research Office issued a notice on February 6, 2013, to solicit comments on the *2013 Guangdong Marine Fishery Technology Extension Program Application Guide (Draft for Comments)*.

To advance the 2013 program more effectively, the Guangdong Oceanic and Fishery Administration drafted the guide. The Research Office asks relevant faculty members to review the draft carefully and submit revisions and suggestions, with a focus on major and key project proposals, to yxhu@stu.edu.cn by February 15, 2013.
