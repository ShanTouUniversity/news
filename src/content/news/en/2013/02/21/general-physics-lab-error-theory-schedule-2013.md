Shantou University’s Physics Department announced the schedule for this semester’s General Physics Laboratory. Students will study error theory during the first week and begin laboratory experiments in the second week.

| Regular laboratory day | Error-theory class | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | March 2, Saturday, 9:00 a.m.–noon | E303 |
| Tuesday | March 2, Saturday, 2:00–5:00 p.m. | E303 |
| Thursday | March 3, Sunday, 9:00–11:00 a.m. | E303 |
| Friday | March 3, Sunday, 2:00–5:00 p.m. | E303 |

Students enrolled in the course should share the notice and attend at the time corresponding to their regular laboratory day.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physics Department and Teaching Office)
