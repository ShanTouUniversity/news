It is reported that Wu Tong, Youth League Branch Secretary of the Affiliated Middle School of Shantou University, was named an "Outstanding Seconded Youth League Cadre for 2012 University Youth League Cadres Seconded to County-Level Youth League Committees in Guangdong" by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League in February 2013.

In 2012, Wu responded to the call of the Central Committee and the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League and took up a secondment at the Chenghai District Youth League Committee in Shantou. Over the course of a year, she rooted herself at the grassroots level, integrated university-local resources, innovated working methods, and actively advanced grassroots Youth League organization building — earning high recognition from the Provincial Youth League Committee.

The STU University Youth League Committee extended congratulations and expressed hope that she will continue to play an exemplary role in organizing, serving, and guiding young people.
