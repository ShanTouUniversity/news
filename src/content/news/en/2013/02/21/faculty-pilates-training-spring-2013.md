At the request of faculty and staff who enjoy Pilates, Shantou University’s labor union will continue classes on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays during the spring semester. Classes run from **5:35 to 6:35 p.m.** The first sessions are February 25 for the Monday class, February 27 for the Wednesday class, and March 1 for the Friday class.

Returning participants must confirm their class time by **February 24**. Those who cannot attend or need to change classes should notify the association in advance. Participants who do not confirm by the deadline will be considered absent for the semester, and open places will be offered to new participants on a first-come, first-served basis.

For registration and confirmation, call `2654` or campus short number `678222`. Participants should bring a yoga mat and wear loose clothing. Classes are automatically suspended on public holidays.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
