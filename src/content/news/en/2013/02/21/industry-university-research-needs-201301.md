Guangdong Sunfun Toys Industrial Co., Ltd. has presented Shantou University with two technology needs for industry-university research cooperation. The Research Office is inviting interested university units and faculty members to explore partnerships. The company website is `www.sunfuntoys.com`; proposed cooperation models include joint development, commissioned development, and technology services.

### Technology needs

**2.4 GHz wireless control.** The company seeks to improve the functionality and precision of conventional remote-control toys, reduce interference and cross-frequency problems associated with traditional 27 MHz and 49 MHz products, and lower module costs so that the technology can be used more widely in ordinary remote-control toys.

**Stop-motion software.** The company seeks an integrated photography and production system that automatically converts still photographs into animation and supports post-production dubbing. The target system would offer greater automation, stronger processing, and more professional results, connect directly with phones, tablets, and household cameras, and generate animation directly.

Units and faculty members interested in cooperation may contact the Technology Achievements and Development Division of STU’s Research Office. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong at `0754-86503271` or `ygluo@stu.edu.cn`.
