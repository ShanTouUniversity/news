Following a Guangdong Provincial Federation of Trade Unions notice, Shantou University’s labor union opened recommendations for the National May Day Labor Certificate, National May Day Labor Medal, and National Pioneer Worker designation, along with the Guangdong May Day Labor Medal and Guangdong Pioneer Worker designation.

The All-China Federation of Trade Unions plans to recognize outstanding groups and workers before May Day 2013 for contributions to economic, political, cultural, social, and ecological development. The Guangdong federation will also recognize outstanding workers and groups that have contributed to the province’s economic and social development.

Eligible units and individuals should complete the relevant forms according to the attachments and submit materials to the university labor union by **February 25**. Electronic copies should be sent to `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.
