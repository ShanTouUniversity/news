Following a Guangdong Provincial Federation of Trade Unions notice, Shantou University’s labor union opened recommendations for the National May Day Labor Certificate, National May Day Labor Medal, and National Pioneer Worker designation, along with the Guangdong May Day Labor Medal and Guangdong Pioneer Worker designation.

The All-China Federation of Trade Unions plans to recognize outstanding groups and workers before May Day 2013 for contributions to economic, political, cultural, social, and ecological development. The Guangdong federation will also recognize outstanding workers and groups that have contributed to the province’s economic and social development.

In a subsequent publicity notice, the university labor union committee determined that **Professor Lin Furong** of the School of Science meets the basic requirements for a Guangdong May Day Labor Medal candidate and plans to recommend him. Any objections should be submitted to the university labor union by **March 22**. Unit submissions must bear an official seal; individual submissions must include the submitter’s real name and accept responsibility for the information. Contact `86502983` or `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.


### Subsequent result

On **May 3, 2013**, the STU Labor Union reported that Professor Lin Furong of the School of Science had received the **Guangdong May 1 Labor Medal**. He traveled to Guangzhou on the morning of **April 27** to attend the provincial conference marking International Workers' Day and recognizing model workers, where he was received by provincial Party and government leaders and took part in the award presentation. On the afternoon of **April 28**, he also went on stage to receive an award at Shantou's conference marking International Workers' Day and promoting the “One Million Workers Building a Beautiful and Happy Shantou” initiative. The original notice records both events; this article does not infer the precise relationship between the two presentations.
