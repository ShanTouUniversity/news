It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University issued a notice on February 21 to organize the 2013 registration filing of graduate course programs in accordance with Document Yue Xue Wei Ban [2013] No. 8. Graduate training units must prepare the required materials and submit them to the Graduate School, which will sign off on the university's position before forwarding the materials to the Guangdong Provincial Academic Degrees Committee Office for review.

## Submission Requirements

Units planning to offer graduate course programs in 2013 must submit the following materials to the Graduate School by March 26. Late submissions will not be accepted, and all materials must be printed on A4 paper:

| Material | Copies and requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Overview Form for Offering a Graduate Course Program | Three copies |
| Proposed teaching plan for the program | Two copies |
| Summary Form for Applying to Offer a Graduate Course Program | One copy |
| Data disk for the graduate course program | One copy; see Attachment 4 for data-entry requirements |

## Cooperative and Out-of-Province Programs

Programs held outside the applicant unit that require cooperation with another institution must include a joint-program agreement and a statement describing the cooperating institution's operating conditions. Programs held outside Guangdong Province must also submit an *Opinion Solicitation Form for an Out-of-Province Program* (Attachment 3), reviewed and stamped by the local provincial authority responsible for academic degrees and postgraduate education.

All units must complete and submit the materials accurately and in full. After the Graduate School signs the university's opinion, it will forward the materials to the Guangdong Provincial Academic Degrees Committee Office for review. The provincial office is expected to publish the list of graduate course programs approved for filing by May 31. Information will be available through the website of the Guangdong Academic Degrees Committee Office at http://xwb.gdhed.edu.cn/.

> ⚠️ The four attachments, including the program overview form, summary form, out-of-province program opinion form and data-entry requirements, are available in the original notice on the OA system.
