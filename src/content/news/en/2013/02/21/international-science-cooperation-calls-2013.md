The Guangdong Department of Science and Technology has issued calls for international science and technology cooperation projects with Canada, Belgium, Israel, Finland, Croatia, and the European Union. Shantou University’s Research Office forwarded the calls to university units and invited interested faculty members to review the opportunities.

| Cooperation program | Internal deadline |
| --- | --- |
| 2013 China–Quebec, Canada intergovernmental science and technology cooperation | March 18 |
| 2013 China–Canada intergovernmental science and technology cooperation | March 18 |
| 2013 China–Belgium (Wallonia–Brussels) intergovernmental science and technology cooperation | March 29 |
| 2014 China–Israel industrial technology R&D cooperation and calls for cooperation needs | April 19 |
| 2013 China–Finland Nano Innovation Center | April 19 |
| Projects for the 6th meeting of the China–Croatia Science and Technology Cooperation Committee | April 19 |
| 2013 China–EU science and technology cooperation special program | April 19 |

Faculty members interested in applying should review the relevant call and contact the Research Office before the applicable deadline. Contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at `0754-86502813`.
