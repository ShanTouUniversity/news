The Guangdong Education Department has issued a call for suggestions on guidelines for the development of university engineering technology research and development centers. Shantou University’s Research Office is collecting suggestions from interested units and faculty members.

Interested units and faculty should prepare their suggestions according to the provincial notice and submit them to the Research Office by **the end of February 22, 2013**. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong at `0754-86503271` or `ygluo@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
