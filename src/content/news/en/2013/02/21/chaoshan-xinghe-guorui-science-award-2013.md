The 7th Chaoshan Xinghe Guorui Science and Technology Award is accepting applications. Established with a donation from Zhang Zhangsun, chairman of Shantou Garden Group, the award recognizes technology workers in the Chaoshan region who have made outstanding achievements in scientific and technological invention and innovation.

### Eligibility

Applicants must have worked in science and technology in Shantou for at least three years, meaning they began the relevant work by December 31, 2009, and must have made outstanding achievements in technological invention and innovation. The award focuses on technology-development and public-interest work in fields including engineering, agriculture, and medicine; theoretical research is outside its scope.

Technology-development work includes products, technologies, processes, materials, designs, and biological varieties with significant market value and technological innovation, as well as contributions to the transfer, promotion, and industrialization of new achievements. Public-interest work includes achievements and applications in health care, environmental protection, natural-resource surveys and use, disaster monitoring and prevention, and related areas. Major inventions with a leading position in China or internationally and significant economic benefits may be submitted for the special award under the detailed rules.

The recommendation form and supporting materials must be submitted in **six copies**, with one marked as the original. Applicants must submit materials to the Technology Achievements and Development Division of the Research Office by **the end of February 26, 2013**. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong at `0754-86503271` or `dengyong@stu.edu.cn`.
