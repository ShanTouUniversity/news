To promote the volunteer spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress, the Shantou Youth League Committee and Shantou Youth Volunteers Association will recognize outstanding groups and individuals in youth volunteer service. Shantou University’s Youth League Committee forwarded the call to school Youth League committees and youth volunteer organizations for nominations.

Units may recommend eligible volunteer-service groups and individuals according to the selection notice. Electronic recommendation materials are due to the university Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` by **February 26**; printed copies should be delivered to the committee office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
>
> ⚠️ Consult the original attachment on the OA system for eligibility criteria, forms, and award categories.
