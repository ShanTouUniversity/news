Shantou University’s Teaching Office announced arrangements for undergraduate theses and design projects in the 2012–2013 spring semester. Schools and departments must complete proposals, supervision and writing, defenses, and grading by the end of **Week 14**.

| Work stage | Latest completion time |
| --- | --- |
| Students complete proposal reports | End of Week 2 |
| Students write theses or complete designs | End of Week 11 |
| Departments establish defense committees and groups | Weeks 11–12 |
| Supervisors and reviewers assess theses or designs | Weeks 12–13 |
| Defense committees conduct defenses and grade; academic staff enter grades | Weeks 13–14 |

Schools and departments must follow the university’s revised undergraduate thesis and design regulations and writing standards and maintain quality control. Students must complete their work independently and on time, may not plagiarize or reuse others’ achievements, must follow the required formatting, printing, and binding rules, and must attend the defense on schedule.

Before formally submitting a thesis or design project, each student must sign an academic-integrity commitment and bind it into the final copy. During Weeks 15–16, academic staff must collect the thesis or design assessment forms and submit them to the Student Affairs Office. Templates are available from the Teaching Office website at `http://jwc.stu.edu.cn/plus/list.php?tid=15`.
