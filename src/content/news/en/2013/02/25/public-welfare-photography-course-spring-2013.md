The spring-semester Public-Welfare Photography course offered by Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication still has places available. The course is open to undergraduates university-wide and will admit **40 students** in one class. It combines photography instruction with community service, encouraging students to observe and document people in need and use images to promote care and social responsibility.

The course is included in the university’s public-welfare course system and awards **one credit** after students complete the course and pass the assessment. Theory classes meet during periods 6 and 7 on Wednesday afternoons in Weeks 3–9. A summary session is scheduled for periods 6–9 on Wednesday of Week 10. Practical sessions are scheduled for Saturday of Week 5, Saturday and Sunday of Week 8, and Saturday of Week 9. Service locations include STU and nearby areas, rural primary schools, and facilities such as the Cunxin Shantang factory.

Students who enjoy photography and are committed to public service are welcome; students with single-lens reflex cameras receive priority. Applicants must complete the course form and email it to `11zcguo@stu.edu.cn` by **the end of February 27, 2013**.
