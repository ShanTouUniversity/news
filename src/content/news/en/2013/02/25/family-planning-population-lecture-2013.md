It is reported that the Center for Women’s Studies of the College of Liberal Arts will hold a public lecture on the evening of February 27, 2013, featuring Dr. Liu Hongyan.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | China’s Family Planning Policy and Population Change |
| Speaker | Dr. Liu Hongyan, Deputy Director and Researcher, China Population and Development Research Center |
| Time | 19:30, February 27, 2013 |
| Venue | Conference Room 3, Academic Exchange Center |
| Organizer | Center for Women’s Studies, College of Liberal Arts |

## Synopsis

The lecture will introduce the background and historical development of China’s family planning policy, the population changes since its implementation, major current population issues, and future trends.

## Speaker

Dr. Liu Hongyan holds a bachelor’s and master’s degree in medicine from Harbin Medical University and a doctorate in law from Peking University. Her research focuses on reproductive health, epidemiology and demography. She is Deputy Director and Researcher at the China Population and Development Research Center, Deputy Director of the Seventh Cycle Project Office of the UNFPA and the National Population and Family Planning Commission, and has been selected for the National “New Century Hundred-Thousand-Ten Thousand Talents Project.” She has authored more than ten books and published over 50 papers and reports.
