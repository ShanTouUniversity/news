To enrich faculty and staff recreational life and promote fitness, Shantou University’s labor union will continue providing badminton courts during the spring semester.

Activities run from this week through the end of the semester every Wednesday from **7:00 to 9:00 p.m.** at the badminton courts in the gymnasium. Faculty and staff are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
