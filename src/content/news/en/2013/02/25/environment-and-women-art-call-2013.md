The Women’s Studies Center of Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts and Sun Yat-sen University’s Gender Education Forum will launch the **Gender Equality @ Environmental Protection STU Station** on **March 8**. The organizers are inviting students and faculty to submit poems, paintings, and photographs on the theme “Environment and Women,” promoting low-carbon environmental action and gender equality.

Submissions may be original works or materials collected through public online sources. The submission period runs from **February 23 through March 7**. Representatives should bring works to the Student Activity Hall in the Library by **7:00 p.m. on March 8**. On-site selection will take place from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m.**, with attendees voting for the winning works.

Five “Most Appreciated Work” prizes will be awarded: one first prize, two second prizes, and two third prizes, with awards of RMB 200, RMB 100, and RMB 50 respectively. Additional awards include Most Creative, Best Environmental Concept, and Best Participation. Student awardees may also be recommended for related gender-equality new-media activities in Guangzhou.

Those interested should register through the official channels of the Women’s Studies Center; consult the original notice for registration details.
