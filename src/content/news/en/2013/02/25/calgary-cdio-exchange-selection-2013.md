Shantou University’s School of Engineering completed student selection for its CDIO exchange program with the University of Calgary in Canada. Following an initial review, interviews, and confirmation by the students, **20 students** were selected for the current exchange cohort. They represent civil engineering, mechanical and electronic engineering, electronic engineering, computer science, communications, physics, biology, and related majors.

The program is intended to broaden students’ engineering-education experience and professional outlook through international exchange.
