Shantou University’s Teaching Office reminded faculty members who have not yet collected their teaching textbooks for the current semester to bring their textbook-use forms to the Teaching Office service window in the New Administration Center by **March 1**. The procedure is intended to ensure timely access to teaching materials.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office)
