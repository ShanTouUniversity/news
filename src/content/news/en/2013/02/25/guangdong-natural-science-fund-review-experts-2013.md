To support the 2013 review of Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects, Shantou University’s Research Office is collecting additional reviewer information and will enter eligible experts into the provincial reviewer database.

### Eligibility

Applicants must meet all of the following conditions: work in Guangdong and hold a full-time position at a legal entity in the province; be currently employed; have completed, passed formal appraisal or acceptance, or formally concluded national, provincial, or overseas basic-research projects they previously led; hold an associate-senior or higher professional title; and be no older than 60. Academicians are exempt from the age limit.

Eligible faculty must complete the *Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Project Reviewer Information Registration Form* and email it by **March 22** to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn`. After the Research Office reviews and submits the information and the provincial foundation committee approves it, the office will return a watermarked form. Experts must print one double-sided copy, sign and stamp it, and submit it to the Research Office by **March 31**.
