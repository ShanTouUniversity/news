The Ministry of Education and China Scholarship Council approved Shantou University undergraduate exchange programs with the Catholic University of Portugal and the University of Manitoba as Outstanding Undergraduate International Exchange Programs. Together with the previously approved exchange program between the School of Engineering and the University of Calgary, STU has **13 nationally funded places** for undergraduate international exchange in 2013: five at Calgary and four each at the Catholic University of Portugal and the University of Manitoba.

Selected students will receive support for one round-trip international fare and living expenses during their study period. The two business-school programs also waive tuition at both institutions, reducing the financial burden on participating families.

In addition, the School of Science’s undergraduate summer research program with the University of Texas at El Paso was approved for a Ministry of Education China-U.S. Humanities Exchange special scholarship. Beginning in 2013, U.S. exchange students arriving at STU may receive subsidies for tuition, accommodation, and living expenses.
