It is reported that the Communist Youth League Central Committee and the All-China Youth Federation will hold the 17th China Youth May Fourth Medal presentation during the May 4 period of 2013. The medal is the highest honor awarded to outstanding young people in China, intended to showcase the spirit and values of contemporary youth and to inspire young people across the country to strive toward building a moderately prosperous society.

## Campus Nomination Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Award | 17th China Youth May Fourth Medal |
| Selection rules | Measures for Selection and Recognition of the China Youth May Fourth Medal (trial), CYL document [2010] No. 2 |
| Quota | One candidate per college league committee or directly affiliated branch |
| Deadline | Materials due at the university Youth League office by March 4, 2013 |

According to the notice forwarded by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, each college league committee and directly affiliated league branch should carefully nominate one candidate in strict accordance with the eligibility requirements. By March 4 they must submit two copies of the application form with original supporting materials (plus electronic versions), proof of highest academic degree, and copies of award certificates to the university Youth League office.

The university Youth League noted that the selection is open to young faculty and students campus-wide; prospective candidates may consult their college's league committee for detailed criteria and material requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
