As part of Shantou University’s 2014 Antarctic Expedition Project lecture series, a lecture titled **“World Biogeography and Its Relevance to South China”** will be held from **2:00 to 4:00 p.m. on March 4, 2013**, in the Library Lecture Hall.

The speakers are Professor James (Skip) Lazell and Professor Lu Wenhua of the Rhode Island-based conservation institute in the United States. Lazell is the founder and president of The Conservation Agency. His research covers speciation, geographic variation, biogeography, and conservation biology, with long-term attention to biodiversity on islands in the South China Sea. Lu’s work focuses on insect ecology and biodiversity.

The lecture will address Planet Earth, glaciation and continental drift, land connections and dispersal, speciation in isolation, Grayian Distribution, and Austro-boreal Disjunction. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
