Shantou University’s School of Engineering has released Issue 2 of its 2012 *Engineering School Work Newsletter* (No. 7 overall), reviewing developments in undergraduate teaching, research, academic exchange, and student development.

### Teaching and discipline development

The newsletter reports that the School held its 2012 undergraduate teaching conference and continued advancing EIP-CDIO engineering-education reform. The “Wind Engineering and Wind Turbine Design Technology” project passed acceptance for the third phase of the Guangdong 211 Project, while the “Mechanical Design and Theory” discipline was approved as a key Guangdong discipline in the ninth round. The School also held a science and technology innovation camp for secondary-school students and co-hosted the Ministry of Education’s advanced training program on the CDIO engineering-education model, attended by more than 200 faculty representatives from 48 universities nationwide.

Delegations from Xiamen University, Xi’an Jiaotong University, the Technion–Israel Institute of Technology, and Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology visited the School to discuss engineering-education reform and educational cooperation. In 2012, the School recruited 10 doctoral faculty members, seven of whom had overseas experience. Doctoral holders accounted for 67.5 percent of professional faculty.

### Research and academic exchange

Six Engineering School faculty members were approved for the second 2012 Guangdong Enterprise Science and Technology Commissioner Program, and three were selected as first-batch high-level members of the Chaoshan IT Think Tank. In 2012, the School received awards from the Guangdong Science and Technology Awards, the Ministry of Education’s Higher Education Outstanding Scientific Research Achievement Awards, the Shantou Science and Technology Awards, and the Shantou Patent Awards. It also worked with companies and research institutes to submit more than 10 provincial and ministerial industry-university-research collaboration applications.

The School hosted the World Engineers Forum, the Engineering Research Academic Forum, and a Guangdong Academicians Forum. Speakers and visiting scholars included Erik D. Goodman, Sun Yu, Huang Jiwu, Wang Jiaqi, and Jin Lianwen.

### Student development

An STU student team received a High Commendation of the CDIO Academy at the eighth International CDIO Conference student-works competition. The 12th EIP-CDIO development camp used communication, teamwork, and project-design activities to develop engineering students’ collaborative abilities. Graduate students received awards related to the Green Wind Energy Outstanding Graduate Thesis program, and two students received China Scholarship Council support for exchange study in Canada. Students from the Department of Electronic Engineering won multiple awards in the National University Electronic Information Innovation Works Selection and the Guangdong Undergraduate Electronic Design Competition’s Smart City special competition.
