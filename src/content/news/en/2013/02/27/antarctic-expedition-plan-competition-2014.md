With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, Shantou University plans to conduct an Antarctic Expedition Project during the 2014 winter vacation and select **16 students and four faculty members**. Participants will visit China’s Great Wall Station, experience the polar environment, and conduct field activities related to environmental protection.

Selection will proceed through an Antarctic proposal competition, preliminary judging, physical-fitness testing, a training camp in Nan’ao, and final judging. The proposal competition is intended to encourage the university community to learn about Antarctica and promote marine environmental awareness.

### Competition categories

- **Category A**: Participants enter the proposal competition and the Antarctic project selection. It is open to current faculty and staff and undergraduates in the 2010, 2011, and 2012 cohorts, including Medical College students. Students who took part in the 2011 or 2012 advanced outdoor programs and completed the final projects may not enter. A student selected for the Antarctic project will lose eligibility if they have a failing course in either the fall or spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year.
- **Category B**: Participants enter only the proposal competition. It is open to faculty, staff, and students university-wide, including graduate students who cannot enter the Antarctic project selection but are interested in polar research and exploration.

An outstanding proposal may still be implemented in Antarctica by one of the final 20 faculty and student project members even if its author cannot travel there.

Participants must use a campus email account and password to submit proposals through the competition website by **midnight on March 13**: <http://expedition.stu.edu.cn/vote/Index.aspx>. Proposals may be revised until the deadline.

A total of **167 faculty members and students** successfully submitted proposals. The public vote runs from **March 15 through midnight on March 20**. Faculty and students must use their campus email accounts and passwords to vote at <http://expedition.stu.edu.cn/vote/ToVote.aspx>. Each person may vote only once and cast up to three votes: two for student proposals, which must be different proposals, and one for a faculty proposal. Voting does not distinguish between competition categories and cannot be changed after submission; ties will be decided by judges based on the proposal content.

An earlier extension notice gave the voting period as **March 14–20**, while this March 15 voting notice specifies **March 15–20**; both dates are recorded here. Voting results will determine the competition winners and serve as an initial screening reference for Category A participants advancing to physical-fitness testing in the Antarctic project selection.

Awards are based on vote totals without separating faculty and students: one first-prize winner receives an iPad Mini (16G), two second-prize winners receive Lenovo LePad A2109 (8G) tablets, three third-prize winners receive iPod nano 7 (16GB) devices, and **10 shortlisted entries** receive two buffet coupons from Shantou Junhua Hotel. One hundred participating voters will also be selected at random to receive RMB 100 in campus-card credit, of which RMB 50 is restricted to purchases at the Library Student Activity Hall.

For questions, email `expedition@stu.edu.cn` or call `0754-86502246`.
