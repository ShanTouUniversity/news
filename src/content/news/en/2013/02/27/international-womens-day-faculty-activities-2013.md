For International Women’s Day, Shantou University’s labor union will organize an outing, a photography competition, and anniversary portrait sessions for female faculty and staff under the theme “Approach Nature and Embrace Health.”

### Outing

The outing is scheduled for **Saturday, March 9**, and is open to female faculty and staff university-wide. Participants may not bring relatives or children. The route includes Sanheba in Dabu, the Pingshan terraced fields and rapeseed-flower area, and Huaqilou. Boarding locations and the detailed itinerary are provided in the original attachment. Participants should register through the female-workers representative of their department union and pay the **RMB 30 per-person** registration fee. Department unions must submit participant lists and fees to the university labor union by the end of **March 4** and email an electronic copy to `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.

### Photography competition

The “Spring Melody” photography competition will use the March 9 outing as its theme. Participants may photograph the terraced rapeseed fields, Sanheba, Huaqilou, and other moments from the trip. First, second, and third prizes will be awarded. Each person may submit one photograph by **March 20** to `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.

### Anniversary portraits

The labor union will provide free festive portraits for faculty and staff married in 1988, marking their 25th wedding anniversary, and those married in 1983, marking their 30th anniversary. The time and location will be announced separately.

The attached travel itinerary lists a group package price of RMB 150 per person, which differs from the registration-fee amount stated in the notice. The final fee arrangement should be confirmed with the university labor union.
