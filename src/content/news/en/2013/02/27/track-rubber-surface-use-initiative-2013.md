It is reported that the Sports Department and the Resource Management Office issued a joint proposal on February 27, 2013, calling for proper use of the track field’s rubber surface.

The two semicircular rubber areas of the track had been severely damaged and were renovated during the winter vacation and have now reopened. As playing football on the rubber surface can damage it and accelerate aging, the departments call on all faculty and students to use football fields for football activities and jointly care for and make rational use of campus sports facilities.
