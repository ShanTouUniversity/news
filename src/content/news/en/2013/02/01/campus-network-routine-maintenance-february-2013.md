Shantou University’s Network and Information Center will perform routine maintenance on the campus network core and gateway systems from **12:00 midnight to 6:30 a.m. on February 2, 2013**. All campus network services and internet access may be briefly interrupted, affecting all campus network users.

The restoration time will not be announced separately. For inquiries, call `0754-82903413-100` or `0754-86503401`, or email `stunic@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
