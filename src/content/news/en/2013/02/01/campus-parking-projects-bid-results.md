It is reported that, upon the recommendation of the Shantou University bidding leadership group, the bid results for two campus parking facility projects were published on February 1. The projects cover the construction of a parking lot for the university bus fleet and the laying of curbed-brick parking spaces across the campus.

## Award Results

According to the announcement, two local Shantou construction companies won the contracts:

| Project | Winning Bidder | Award Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bus fleet parking lot construction | Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. | 36,349.00 yuan |
| Curbed-brick parking spaces project | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. | 284.00 yuan (comprehensive quote per 4 square meters of curbed brick) |

The public notice period runs from February 1 to February 3, 2013, lasting three days.

## Inquiries and Complaints

During the notice period, questions about the bid evaluation may be directed to the tenderer, the STU Infrastructure Office, while complaints may be filed with the university's Supervision and Audit Office.
