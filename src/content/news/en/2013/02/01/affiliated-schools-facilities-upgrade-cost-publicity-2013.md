The facilities-upgrade project for Shantou University’s affiliated middle and primary schools began on January 17, 2013. During demolition work in a bathroom, the original reinforced-concrete floor slab was found to be seriously deteriorated with exposed reinforcement, creating a safety concern. The project therefore proposes additional lightweight-brick filling, bathroom partitions, and water-supply pipes using reservoir water for toilet flushing.

The Infrastructure Office’s preliminary estimate puts the additional cost at approximately **RMB 27,000**, within the university’s prescribed 10 percent limit for additional items. The university is publicizing the proposed additions. Written comments may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by **5:30 p.m. on February 3**.
