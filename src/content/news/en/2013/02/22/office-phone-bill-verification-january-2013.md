Electronic bills for office telephone charges incurred by Shantou University units from January 1 through 31, 2013, have been imported into the telephone management system. Unit reviewers may log in at `http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn` to view and verify the details. Bills without objections should be confirmed by clicking “Confirm selected bill.”

If bill details do not match actual use, send an email to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` by **February 27** so the issue can be consolidated and reported to China Telecom’s Shantou branch. After the verification period, bills with no objection and no confirmation action will be treated as confirmed by the system. The Finance Office will deduct charges by each unit’s funding account on **February 28**.

Questions or suggestions about the telephone management system may also be sent to the same email address.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
