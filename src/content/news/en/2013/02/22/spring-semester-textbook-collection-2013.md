Shantou University’s Teaching Office asked each major to arrange for class representatives to collect textbook-use forms and textbooks for the 2012–2013 spring semester.

Collection will take place on **February 23 and 24** at **Xueyuan Bookstore in Building 8 at 789**. Majors should arrange representatives to complete the collection.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office)
