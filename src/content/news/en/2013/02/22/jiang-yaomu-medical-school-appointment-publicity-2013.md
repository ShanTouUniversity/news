Following deliberation at the second 2013 meeting of the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee, the university proposed appointing Jiang Yaomu as head of the Organization, Publicity, and United Front Department of the Medical College. The university released the information under its pre-appointment publicity system.

Jiang holds a bachelor’s degree, a master’s degree, and the professional title of associate chief physician. He has worked at the First Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University Medical College for many years, including as deputy director of the hospital Party Committee Office and assistant to the hospital president. His previous work also included palliative-care services and hospital administration.

Comments may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the university Party Committee’s Organization and United Front Department by **February 28, 2013**. Contact the department at `0754-86502345` or `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. Organizations must stamp written submissions, and individuals must provide their real names.
