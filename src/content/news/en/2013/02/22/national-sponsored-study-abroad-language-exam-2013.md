Shantou University’s Personnel Office forwarded the Guangdong Education Department Office’s notice on the 2013 schedule for the national foreign-language examination for government-sponsored study-abroad personnel. Faculty members at the main campus who need to take the examination were reminded to register promptly according to the notice.

The examination is one of the foreign-language assessments used in connection with government-sponsored study abroad. Detailed dates, registration procedures, and eligibility requirements are subject to the original notice issued by the Ministry of Education Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
>
> ⚠️ Consult the original attachment on the OA system for the examination schedule and registration details.
