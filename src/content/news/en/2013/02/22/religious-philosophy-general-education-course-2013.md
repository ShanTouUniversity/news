At the invitation of Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education, He Guanghu, a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Philosophy of Renmin University of China, will visit STU in mid-to-late March to teach the general-education elective **Religion and Philosophy** (course code **HED3001**). The course is open to students university-wide and carries **two credits**.

Classes will meet during Weeks 3–5 on Monday periods ABC, Wednesday periods 6–8, and Saturday periods 2–4 in Room 401, Building D. Teaching will combine lectures and discussion. Interested students may enroll through the credit-based academic system during the first-week course-adjustment period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Institute of Higher Education and Teaching Office)
