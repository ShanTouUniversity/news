The China Scholarship Council has opened applications for its 2013 Outstanding Undergraduate International Exchange Program. Shantou University’s Business School received approval for exchange programs with the Catholic University of Portugal and the University of Manitoba, with **four fall placements** at each institution.

### Eligibility and costs

Applicants must be full-time second-year or higher students enrolled in the Business School. They should have strong academic and personal records and rank in the top 10 percent of their major cohort at the time of application. Applicants who have not previously taken part in a university or school exchange program will receive priority. Applicants must also demonstrate English proficiency through qualifying study in an English-speaking country, a passing National Foreign Language Examination (WSK), completion of an advanced English program at a Ministry of Education-designated training center, or an IELTS score of at least 6.5 or TOEFL score of at least 95. The University of Manitoba specifically requires IELTS 6.5 or higher.

Both partner universities waive tuition, but students are responsible for fees, round-trip travel, accommodation, meals, books, materials, insurance, and medical costs. Students approved by the China Scholarship Council will receive one round-trip international fare and the prescribed living stipend.

Applicants must submit the Business School overseas-exchange application form, proof of English proficiency, an academic transcript, and a 300–500-word English essay describing their purpose for joining the program and overseas study plan. The school will conduct a written-materials review followed by interviews consisting of a PowerPoint presentation and questions, with approximately 10 minutes per applicant.

Applicants must submit printed and electronic materials by **12:00 noon on March 18** to the office of Ye Dongni in Room 204, West Liberal Arts Building. Electronic materials should be sent to `dnye@stu.edu.cn`. For inquiries, call `0754-86502277`.
