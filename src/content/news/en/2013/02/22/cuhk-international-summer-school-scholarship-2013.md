With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation and a donation from Texhong Enterprises, Shantou University will continue sending students to the Chinese University of Hong Kong International Summer School. The university received applications from **15 eligible students** and, after interviews by a university review panel, confirmed **10 students** for the 2013 program.

### Program details

Eligible applicants are full-time undergraduates from the 2010 and 2011 cohorts at the main campus, excluding the Medical College. Applicants should have strong academic records, learning and analytical abilities, experience in campus or community activities, and leadership potential. They must also demonstrate English proficiency through one of the following: TOEFL 71 or higher, IELTS 6.0 or higher, CET-4 550 or higher, or CET-6 520 or higher. Applicants should be able to introduce STU to international audiences and show interest in public affairs and volunteer service.

The program runs from **July 2 through August 5, 2013**. Students will select one morning and one afternoon course, earning three credits per course and six credits in total. Courses include International Finance, Principles of Management, Human Resource Management, Introduction to International Business, Global Enterprise Management, Asian Business, Marketing Management, Engineering Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Technology and Life, Energy and Green Society, General Psychology, Mainstream Chinese Philosophical Thought, Chinese Culture and Society, Fundamentals in Chinese Medicine, Government and Politics of China, and Chinese Foreign Policy.

The total cost is approximately HKD 27,000 per student, covering tuition, accommodation, and living expenses. Selected students receiving support will receive a **HKD 15,000** scholarship; students will cover the remaining costs.

Applicants must submit an application form and an English essay of no more than 300 words, choosing between “How would the CUHK International Summer School enhance your global perspective?” and “What are your academic goals, and how would the CUHK International Summer School help you to achieve them?” All materials must be in English.

Printed and electronic materials are due by **11:00 a.m. on March 1** at the Student Financial Aid Management Center, Room 106 of the Administration Building. Email electronic materials to `xyli@stu.edu.cn`. The university will select final scholarship recipients through a written-materials review and interview.

### Interview arrangements

Shortlisted students will be interviewed from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 13**, in Administration Center Room 221. The interview uses a leaderless group-discussion format: each student gives a self-introduction in English lasting no more than one minute, followed by two minutes of preparation, a 10-minute group discussion, and a 10-minute panel-question period. Shortlisted students should arrive by **2:40 p.m.** and prepare a name card in advance.
