Because of curriculum reform, the Computer Teaching Center at Shantou University will stop offering the public required course Computer Application Skills (COM1011), previously available to the 2009–2011 cohorts, and Computer Application Fundamentals (COM1014), previously available to the 2012 cohort, after the 2013–2014 academic year.

To help students who have not passed the courses complete their requirements, STU will offer retake sections in spring 2013. Students from the 2009–2011 cohorts may enroll in Computer Application Skills, section **60696**; students from the 2012 cohort may enroll in Computer Application Fundamentals, section **60697**. Eligible students should log in and enroll during next week’s course-adjustment period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Teaching Office and Computer Teaching Center)
