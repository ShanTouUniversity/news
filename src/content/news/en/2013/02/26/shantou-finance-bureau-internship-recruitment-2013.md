The Shantou Municipal People’s Government Finance Work Bureau is recruiting **four interns** from Shantou University’s Business School. Eligible applicants are Business School graduate students and undergraduates in the 2009 and 2010 cohorts. Applicants should have strong writing skills and a basic understanding of current economic development and reform.

The internship begins on **March 11, 2013**, lasts approximately three and a half months, and follows regular working hours. Pre-internship training is scheduled for **3:00 p.m. on March 7** in the Business School seminar room on the second floor of the West Liberal Arts Building. The Finance Work Bureau will provide an appropriate work allowance; other arrangements follow the Business School’s internship-base rules.

Applicants must email the intern information form and an internship work proposal to `chenyl@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on March 5**. For inquiries, call `0754-82903496`.
