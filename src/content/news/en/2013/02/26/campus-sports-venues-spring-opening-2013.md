At the start of the new semester, Shantou University sports venues resumed opening and reservations for students, faculty, and staff while continuing to support physical-education classes and sports-team training.

Detailed opening hours are available on the Sports Department website: <http://www.spo.stu.edu.cn/custom.asp?id=12&type=0&main_id=4>. The fitness center opens on **March 5, the second week of the semester**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
