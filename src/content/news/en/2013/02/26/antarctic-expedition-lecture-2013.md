It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold a lecture of the Antarctic Expedition Series (under the 2014 Antarctic Expedition Program) titled “Antarctic Scientific Expedition” on the evening of March 5, 2013, to raise environmental awareness and help students improve their expedition proposals for the first-round selection competition.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Antarctic Scientific Expedition |
| Speakers | Dr. Rebecca Lee (first Chinese woman to reach the Three Poles, founder of the Polar Museum Foundation) and Feng Jiayi (School of Architecture, The University of Hong Kong) |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, March 5, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

Dr. Lee has participated in the Chinese Antarctic Expedition Team since 1985, wintered four times at Antarctic stations, traveled six times to the Arctic, reached the North Pole with an international expedition in 1993 and climbed the northern slope of Mount Everest. Feng Jiayi joined the “Wild Expedition” high school Antarctic team in 2010 and later participated in Arctic and Taklamakan Desert expeditions. They will share Antarctic research experiences and insights on drafting and implementing expedition proposals.
