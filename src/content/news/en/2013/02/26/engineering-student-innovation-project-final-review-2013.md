To prepare for the ninth Shantou University “Challenge Cup” and the twelfth Guangdong “Challenge Cup” competitions for extracurricular academic and technological works, STU’s School of Engineering will conduct final reviews and defenses during Week 1 of the 2012–2013 spring semester for projects that passed the midterm review.

### Submissions and defenses

Project leaders must submit printed and electronic completion reports to their department Youth League branch by **February 28**. Department branches must submit the collected materials to the university Youth League Office by **11:30 a.m. on March 1**. Materials may be sent to Yao at `yaoyi@stu.edu.cn`. Projects that cannot be completed by the deadline but will not be abandoned must submit a postponement report, a feasible progress plan and completion date, together with the supervisor’s signature and comments.

Final defenses will take place in the relevant department:

| Department | Time | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electronic Engineering | 3:00 p.m., March 2 | Department conference room |
| Mechanical and Electrical Engineering | 3:00 p.m., March 1 | Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Building |
| Computer Science | 3:00 p.m., March 1 | Room 302, West Liberal Arts Building |
| Civil Engineering | 3:00 p.m., March 1 | Department conference room |

Project leaders must prepare a five-minute PowerPoint presentation, the physical work or photographs of it, and materials demonstrating completion. Projects that fail to attend the final review or submit required materials will be treated as abandoned and their project funds will be recovered. Abandoned projects must also submit an explanation. Physical works, papers, software, and other results must be submitted by department Youth League branches by **March 1**.

Projects that pass review and have submitted completion reports must complete reimbursement by **March 29, 2013**.
