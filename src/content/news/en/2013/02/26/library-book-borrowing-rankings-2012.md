The Shantou University Library released its 2012 university-wide book-borrowing rankings. The second volume of *The Ordinary World* ranked first with 74 loans, *Demi-Gods and Semi-Devils* ranked second with 53, and the first volume of *The Ordinary World* ranked third with 49.

| Rank | Loans | Title |

| 1 | 74 | *The Ordinary World*, Volume 2 |
| 2 | 53 | *Demi-Gods and Semi-Devils* |
| 3 | 49 | *The Ordinary World*, Volume 1 |
| 4 | 48 | *The Second Sex* |
| 5 | 46 | *Collected Works of Eileen Chang* |
| 6 | 45 | *Love or Money?* |
| 7 | 43 | *The Catcher in the Rye* |
| 8 | 40 | *Friends*, also known as *F.R.I.E.N.D.S.*, Seasons 1–10 |

| 18 | 32 | *The Thorn Birds* |
