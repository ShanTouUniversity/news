The **15th China Chenghai International Toy and Gift Expo** will be held at the Chenghai Exhibition Center from **April 20 to 23, 2013**. Its toy-technology innovation showcase will include displays of university and research-institute results, technology lectures or presentations, university-industry research matching, and student toy-concept design displays and competitions.

Shantou University’s Research Office is inviting relevant units to submit results for exhibition. Eligible areas include:

- new materials, processes, and technology applications in production materials, packaging and printing, and mold manufacturing;
- industrial product and appearance design;
- photo, audio, electrical-control, and wireless interconnection technologies;
- automated product design, manufacturing equipment, and related technologies;
- research papers, reports, and other results on industrial innovation, development, and product-marketing structures.

Results may be presented through display boards, PowerPoint projection, computer demonstrations, physical exhibits, or compiled materials. Units should prepare visual materials and physical exhibits and submit them to the Research Office by **March 15**.
