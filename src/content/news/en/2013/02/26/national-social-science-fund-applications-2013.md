Shantou University has completed the 2013 application process for National Social Science Fund projects. The university submitted **27 applications**, excluding projects under the National Social Science Fund programs for arts and education.

| Applicant unit | Applications | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Business School | 10 | Including two from the Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation Research Institute |
| Law School | 7 | Including three from the Institute of Local Government Development |
| School of Liberal Arts | 4 | |
| Department of Social Sciences | 2 | Including one from the Institute of Local Government Development |
| School of Journalism and Communication | 2 | |
| Institute of Higher Education | 1 | |
| School of Arts and Design | 1 | |
| Total | 27 | |

The Research Office thanked teaching and research staff across the university and encouraged units to support further applications, preliminary research, and project design in order to improve the success rate for National Social Science Fund awards.
