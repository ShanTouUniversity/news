It is reported that the Shantou University Chorus is about to launch its recruitment for the spring semester of the 2012-2013 academic year, welcoming new members from across the university. The drive is jointly organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center, and any student who loves singing and wants to join the choral community may apply.

## Audition Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Who May Apply | All students (no special music background or instrument skills required) |
| Time | Saturday, March 2, 2013, 9:00-12:00 and 14:00-16:00 |
| Venue | Music Rehearsal Hall, 1st Floor, STU Building, Arts Education Center |
| Registration | On-site registration before auditioning |

## Flexible and Varied Audition Format

The audition consists of two parts: pitch-matching exercises and a self-selected song excerpt. There are no restrictions on song genre. Students skilled in an instrument may bring it for a live demonstration, and self-accompaniment is allowed. According to the Arts Education Center, anyone with a passion for singing is welcome to join and help create beautiful choral music together.

Students seeking more information can follow the STU Chorus official Weibo account (weibo.com/stuchorus).

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Arts Education Center)
