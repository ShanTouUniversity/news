In accordance with requirements issued by the Academic Degrees Committee of the State Council and the Ministry of Education, Shantou University has opened qualification review, online registration, and on-site confirmation for 2013 applicants seeking master’s degrees through the equivalent-credentials pathway. The arrangements cover programs associated with the Business School, Institute of Higher Education, and Medical College.

### Registration and confirmation

Applicants must register, enter their basic information, and upload an electronic photograph through the national information platform for applicants seeking degrees through the equivalent-credentials pathway: <http://www.cdgdc.edu.cn/xwyyjsjyxx/zzgs/tdxltk/>. STU’s online submission period runs through **March 20, 2013**. Applicants who completed on-site confirmation last year do not need to repeat it.

On-site confirmation is scheduled for **March 7 and 8, from 9:00 a.m. to noon**, in Conference Room 426 of the New Administration Center. The contact number is `0754-86504745`. Applicants must bring original and copied degree certificates, identification, the qualification-review application form, and platform registration information, and complete image and fingerprint collection, form verification, and signature procedures as required.

The qualification review also requires a personal degree-application report, relevant materials supplied by the applicant’s employer, required forms, and recent photographs. The qualification-review fee is **RMB 150**. Consult the original attachments for the required formats and complete list of forms.

Applicants must complete on-site confirmation within the scheduled period; otherwise, they may be unable to take the national examination for applicants seeking master’s degrees through the equivalent-credentials pathway on time.
