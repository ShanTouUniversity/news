Shantou University’s Graduate School has launched the review of 2012 graduate national scholarships. Under the relevant Guangdong provincial rules, **56 awards** are available at STU this year: four for doctoral students and 52 for master’s students.

### Eligibility and award standards

Currently enrolled, full-time, non-employed master’s and doctoral students in at least their second year and within the standard program length may apply. Applicants must comply with laws and university regulations, demonstrate integrity and good conduct, achieve strong academic results, show significant research ability, and have clear development potential.

The award is RMB **30,000 per doctoral student per year** and RMB **20,000 per master’s student per year**.

### Review schedule

Students apply voluntarily, graduate-unit review committees conduct preliminary reviews and recommendations, and the university Graduate National Scholarship Review Leading Group makes the final determination. Key dates are:

- **By March 6**: Applicants submit the application form and supporting materials to their school, department, or institute review committee, with a supervisor’s or recommender’s signature.
- **By March 8**: Units with fewer than one allocated recommendation place submit materials for a joint review convened by the Graduate School.
- **March 7–18**: Graduate units conduct preliminary reviews and publicize results for at least five working days.
- **March 19**: Schools, departments, and institutes submit review results, summary forms, and review reports to the Graduate School Student Affairs Office. Electronic materials should be sent to `mphuang@stu.edu.cn`.
- **March 20–27**: The Graduate School submits recommendations to the university review leading group and publicizes the approved results university-wide for at least five working days.
- **By March 29**: The Graduate School reports the year’s review process and results to the competent authorities.

During the publicity periods, applicants may submit appeals to the relevant unit or university review body according to the established procedure.
