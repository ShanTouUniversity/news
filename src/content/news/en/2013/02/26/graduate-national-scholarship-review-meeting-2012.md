To support the review of 2012 graduate national scholarships, Shantou University’s Graduate School will hold a review meeting at **3:00 p.m. on February 27, 2013**, in Room 330 of the New Administration Center.

The leaders responsible for graduate affairs and graduate secretaries from each school, department, and institute should attend on time.
