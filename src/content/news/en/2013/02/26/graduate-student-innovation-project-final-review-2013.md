Shantou University’s Graduate School will soon conduct the final review of graduate extracurricular academic and technology projects approved in 2012. Leaders of all approved projects must submit a written completion report and project results by **March 6, 2013**.

The completion report must include the supervisor’s evaluation and signature. Results may include published research papers, business plans, patents, completed models, survey reports, proposals, and other achievements already obtained or expected from the project. Submit printed materials to Wang Fengqi in Room 401 of the New Administration Center and email electronic materials to `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn`.

Projects that fail to submit completion reports as required will be treated as abandoned, and their additional project funding will be recovered.
