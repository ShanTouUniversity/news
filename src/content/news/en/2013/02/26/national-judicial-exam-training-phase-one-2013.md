The first phase of Shantou University Law School’s 2013 national judicial-exam training program will begin on **March 2** in the Moot Court. The past-question analysis course covers civil law, criminal law, commercial and economic law, legal theory, civil procedure, criminal procedure, administrative law, and the laws of the three jurisdictions.

| Date | Morning | Afternoon | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| March 2 | 8:30 a.m.–noon | 2:30–5:30 p.m. | Civil law and criminal law |
| March 3 | 8:30 a.m.–noon | 2:30–5:30 p.m. | Commercial and economic law; legal theory |
| March 9 | 8:30 a.m.–noon | 2:30–5:30 p.m. | Civil and criminal procedure |
| March 10 | 8:30 a.m.–noon | 2:30–5:30 p.m. | Administrative law; laws of the three jurisdictions |
