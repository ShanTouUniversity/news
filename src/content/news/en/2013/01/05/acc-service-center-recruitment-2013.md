The STU Academic Exchange Service Center (ACC) is recruiting restaurant, guest-service, kitchen, and security personnel, with applications closing on January 20, 2013. The positions will be filled as auxiliary contract staff, without transferring an employment relationship to Shantou University. Salary and benefits will be discussed with applicants.

### Open positions

| Position | Openings | Summary of requirements |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Restaurant shift supervisor | 1 | High-school education or above, under 40, able to work shifts |
| Housekeeping shift supervisor | 1 | High-school education or above, under 40, able to work shifts and communicate with clients |
| PA shift supervisor | 1 | Junior-high education or above, male, under 40; manages public-area cleaning |
| Front-desk receptionist | Multiple | High-school education or above, under 30, able to work 24-hour shifts |
| Restaurant service staff | Multiple | Junior-high education or above, under 30, able to work shifts |
| Housekeeping service staff | Multiple | Junior-high education or above, under 33, able to work 24-hour shifts |
| Wok cooks, food-preparation staff, and kitchen assistants | Multiple | Junior-high education or above, under 35; kitchen cooking, preparation, and support duties |
| Security officers | Multiple | Junior-high education or above, male, under 45; a valid security certificate is preferred |

The notice states that applicants generally should be responsible, hardworking, healthy, and able to work shifts. Some roles require computer skills and basic spoken and listening English; relevant work permits or technical certificates are preferred.

### Application process

Applicants should submit a résumé, copies of graduation and identity certificates, and copies of other relevant certificates by email or mail before the deadline. Candidates who pass the initial screening will be invited to an interview. Original identification and certificates must be presented at the interview and will be returned after review. Materials will be used only for this recruitment; applicants should keep their own copies.
