STU Resource Management Office announced the review and payment arrangements for student-dormitory utility charges for December 2012. The latest meter readings were taken on December 30 and 31, following the previous reading on November 30.

Students may check their charges through the STU utility-fee management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/>. Students who find an error or have questions should complete and sign a Utility-Fee Review Application Form and submit it to the Utility Center beside the Science and Technology Building during working hours from January 5 to 10, 2013. The center’s telephone is `2489`. Students whose accommodation information does not match the student records may contact the Residential Area Counselor Office in Building B Room 106 at `3355`.

### Payment schedule

| Item | Date or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Review and objections | January 5–10 |
| Campus-card deductions begin | January 11 |
| Regular payment period | January 11–15 |
| Overdue handling | Under university rules from January 16 |

Objections submitted after the review period that require correction after verification may take three to five working days to process. The Network Center will deduct the charges through campus cards; students with insufficient balances should top up promptly.
