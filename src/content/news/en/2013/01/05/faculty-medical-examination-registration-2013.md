STU Resource Management Office announced arrangements for the university’s 2013 routine faculty and staff health examinations, scheduled for March through May.

The examination covers all retired staff, personnel with senior associate professional titles, deputy-division-level personnel, and in-service staff aged 40 or older. Auxiliary contract workers aged 40 or older will be examined at the university hospital. Eligible faculty and staff may choose among the university hospital, STU First Affiliated Hospital, and STU Second Affiliated Hospital.

Units should register the selected examination hospital for their in-service personnel, while the Retired Staff Affairs Section will register retired personnel. Because individual examination records must be established, participants should avoid changing their selected hospital without a special reason.

### Registration submission

The Health Examination Registration Form must bear the unit’s official seal and be submitted to the University Hospital Health Care Section by January 18, 2013, with an electronic copy sent by email.
