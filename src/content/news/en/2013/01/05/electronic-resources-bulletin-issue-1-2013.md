STU Library issued the first 2013 edition of its Electronic Resources Bulletin, highlighting new video, test-bank, and reference-book resources as well as off-campus access services during the winter break.

### New resources

| Resource | Content and access |
| --- | --- |
| Yinfu Exam Simulation Database B12 | 655 new sets covering civil-service and graduate-entrance examinations; on-campus access: <http://192.168.64.108:8083/newyfB12> |
| Online Lecture Hall | 1,663 new videos across subject areas, plus 589 open-course videos and 221 culture and classic-film resources; on-campus access: <http://192.168.64.17/> |
| Chaoxing Academic Video | 1,108 videos in 15 series covering engineering, basic sciences, history, language and literature, economics and management, and social sciences; on-campus access: <http://192.168.64.40:8080/> |

The new Yinfu content includes civil-service aptitude tests, essay writing, public fundamentals, and interview materials, as well as English, politics, and mathematics papers for graduate entrance examinations.

### Winter access and trials

Kuke Digital Music Library enabled winter-break account roaming for 2013. Readers who had registered Kuke personal accounts within the campus IP range could access Kuke and its spoken-audio resources off campus from January 1 through February 28 using their personal accounts: <http://www.kuke.com>.

CNKI’s versions of the *Grand Chinese Dictionary* and *Kangxi Dictionary* were available for free trial without an account or password at <http://hd.cnki.net/kxhd>.
