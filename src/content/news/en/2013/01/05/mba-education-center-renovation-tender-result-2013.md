STU Resource Management Office announced the winning bid for the renovation project at the Business School MBA Education Center. Following the review and recommendation of the university tendering and bidding leading group, Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. was selected with a bid of RMB 63,034.39.

The result is open for public comment from January 5 through January 7, 2013.
