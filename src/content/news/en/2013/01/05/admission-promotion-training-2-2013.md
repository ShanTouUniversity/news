It is reported that the Admissions Office issued a notice on January 5, 2013, to hold the second training session for the 2013 winter vacation admission outreach campaign “Remember the Source and Pass on the Dream.”

Following the first training, additional students registered, bringing the total to 453 across colleges (residential colleges). To ensure clear and consistent messaging, the office will hold a second training for those who missed the first session.

## Training Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 19:00–20:00, January 6, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 101, Building E |
| Participants | Students who did not attend the first training |
| Content | Work arrangements and reminders for the outreach campaign, plus Q&A |
| Organizer | Admissions Office |

Relevant students are asked to attend on time.
