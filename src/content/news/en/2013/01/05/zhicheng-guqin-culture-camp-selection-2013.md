Zhicheng College announced the selected participants for its seventh Guqin Culture Camp. A total of 41 students applied before the deadline, and 20 were selected under the camp’s application requirements.

The camp will be held from February 18 through 24, 2013, with sessions from 9:00 a.m. to noon and from 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. each day. Participants must collect the camp materials and pay a RMB 100 deposit at the Zhicheng College affairs office in Room J-338 by the end of working hours on January 7. An opening ceremony will be held at 8:00 p.m. on February 17 in the college’s lecture room in J-354.
