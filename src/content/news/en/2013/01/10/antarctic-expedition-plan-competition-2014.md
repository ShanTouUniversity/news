With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, Shantou University planned an Antarctic expedition project for the 2014 winter break. The university would select 16 students and four teachers for a project team that would visit China’s Great Wall Station and carry out scientific exploration and environmental-protection activities. The project theme is “scientific exploration,” and the selection process consists of a proposal competition, preliminary panel review, a physical-fitness test, a training camp in Nan’ao, and a final panel review.

The proposal competition serves as the project’s first selection round and is also open to faculty, staff, and students who are interested in polar exploration but cannot enter the later project selection. The competition will take place in spring 2013: participants will submit proposals through the online platform during weeks 1 and 2, followed by an online vote among all STU faculty, staff, and students in week 3 to determine competition winners and candidates for the next project-selection stage.

### Competition categories

| Category | Eligibility and notes |
| --- | --- |
| Proposal competition only | STU faculty, staff, and students, including graduate students. People interested in polar exploration but unable to enter the Antarctic project selection may also participate; awards will be offered. |
| Competition plus project selection | STU faculty and staff, and undergraduates from the 2010, 2011, and 2012 cohorts, including School of Medicine students. Students who took part in the 2011 or 2012 Advanced Outdoor Exploration projects and completed the projects may not participate. |

A student ultimately selected for the Antarctic project will lose eligibility if they have a failing grade in either the fall or spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. A proposal competition winner who cannot travel to Antarctica may still have the winning proposal carried out there by the 20 students and teachers who ultimately reach the final project roster. Competition winners will be determined by vote totals without separating faculty and students; candidates for the next project-selection stage will be ranked separately as students and teachers.

### Proposal format

The template requires a proposal of no more than three single-sided A4 pages, using text no smaller than Chinese fifth-point type and single spacing. Each proposal must include a topic of no more than 20 Chinese characters, an explanation of no more than 50 characters, an abstract of no more than 150 characters, a practical plan and methods, the equipment needed and its purpose, and the project’s significance for the STU community. Diagrams may be added where useful.

Detailed rules and online-submission arrangements will be announced separately through the campus office information network. Questions about the project may be sent to the Student Affairs Office Outdoor Exploration Office at `expedition@stu.edu.cn`.
