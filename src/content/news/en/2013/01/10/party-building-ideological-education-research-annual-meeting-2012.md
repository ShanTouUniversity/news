The STU Party Committee will hold the 2012 annual meeting of the Party Building and Ideological and Political Education Research Society at 3:00 p.m. on Tuesday, January 15, 2013, in the Science Lecture Hall. The theme is “Opportunities and Challenges: New Reflections on Tripartite Co-construction.”

### Participants

The meeting is intended for members of the university Party Committee and Discipline Inspection Commission, secretaries of Party general branches and directly affiliated branches, and heads of the Party and Administrative Office, Organization and United Front Work Office, Publicity Department, News Center, Supervision and Audit Office, Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, University Union, and Social Sciences Department. The School of Medicine will send 10 representatives, and paper authors are also expected to attend.

Participants should arrive 10 minutes early. Anyone who needs to request leave must report to the Publicity Department by the end of the morning work period on January 14 at `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn`.
