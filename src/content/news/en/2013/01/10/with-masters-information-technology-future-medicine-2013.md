The second event of the Walking with Masters program will be held at Shantou University from February 18 to 20, 2013. The event is funded by the Li Ka-shing Foundation, supported by the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial People’s Government, co-organized by the Chinese Society of Education, and hosted by STU. Its theme is “Information Technology and Future Medicine,” and six mentors in computational biology and medical research will visit the university to give lectures.

### Mentors

| Mentor | Institution and position |
| --- | --- |
| Erol Gelenbe | Dennis Gabor Professor and professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London, United Kingdom |
| Haifan Lin | Professor of Cell Biology and Genetics and director of the Yale Stem Cell Center, Yale University School of Medicine, United States |
| Jill P. Mesirov | Associate director and chief informatics officer of the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, and director of Computational Biology and Bioinformatics, United States |
| Isidore Rigoutsos | Director of the Computational Medicine Center and professor at Thomas Jefferson University, United States |
| Edward M. “Eddy” Rubin | Director of the U.S. Department of Energy Joint Genome Institute and director of the Genomic Science Department at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, United States |
| Cathy Huey-Hwa Wu | Edward G. Jefferson Chair of Bioinformatics and Computational Biology, director of the Center for Bioinformatics and Computational Biology and the Protein Information Resource, and professor at the University of Delaware, United States |

Faculty, staff, and students can follow the event website at <http://www.prometheus.stu.edu.cn> and participate through the official Weibo account “@2013与大师同行.”
