Guangdong recently announced the results of its ninth-round key-discipline evaluation. Eight Shantou University disciplines were approved as Guangdong key disciplines: one Peak discipline, four Advantage disciplines, and three Distinctive disciplines. The Provincial Department of Education announced the results on December 19, 2012.

| Category | Approved disciplines |
| --- | --- |
| Peak discipline | Basic Mathematics (second-level discipline) |
| Advantage disciplines | Chemistry, Clinical Medicine, and Basic Medicine (first-level disciplines); Mechanical Design and Theory (second-level discipline) |
| Distinctive disciplines | Marine Biology, Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, and Pharmacology (second-level disciplines) |

The ninth-round application process began in January 2012 and included university applications, online publicity, correspondence review by peer experts, and on-site defense evaluation. The university said the results represented a breakthrough in the number and level of its provincial key disciplines and laid a foundation for further pursuing national key-discipline status and a “2011 Collaborative Innovation Center.”

STU has continued to promote discipline development and interdisciplinary innovation. The university previously received approval for one national key discipline and four Guangdong key disciplines in 2007, entered the third phase of Guangdong’s Project 211 key-discipline development program in 2008, received authorization for one first-level doctoral discipline and eight first-level master’s disciplines in 2010, and saw Chemistry and Clinical Medicine enter the top 1% globally in ESI in 2012.

The university said it would strengthen the development and management of the ninth-round key disciplines in accordance with the Provincial Department of Education’s requirements, expand their leading role, and support the university’s overall development.
