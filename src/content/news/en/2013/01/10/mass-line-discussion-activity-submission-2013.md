The STU Organization and United Front Work Office forwarded the Shantou Municipal Organization Department’s notice on the “Mass Line Discussion” activity, Document Shanzu〔2012〕No. 80, and arranged its implementation on campus.

Party general branches and directly affiliated branches are asked to organize activities according to their local circumstances and submit activity reports and outstanding reflection articles to the university Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Office at `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.

The archived attachment is the forwarded document, but its image-based version does not provide other requirements that can be read reliably. This article reports only the submission requirements stated in the university notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office of the Party Committee)
