The STU Organization and United Front Work Office of the Party Committee issued Document Shanda〔2013〕No. 2, a pre-appointment public notice. Following deliberation at the first 2013 meeting of the university Party Committee Standing Committee, STU proposed appointing Zeng Yang as director of the School of Medicine Party and Administrative Office and adjusting the administrative ranks of 18 other employees.

### Publicized candidates

| Proposed action | Individuals |
| --- | --- |
| Director of the School of Medicine Party and Administrative Office | Zeng Yang |
| Administrative rank: deputy department level | Su Shaohui, Chen Wenguang, Du Shimin, Chen Wenzheng, and Zheng Zaixin |
| Administrative rank: regular section level | Weng Luyin |
| Administrative rank: deputy section level | Zhang Guihua, Lu Zhuobin, Lan Na, Yao Yi, Zhuang Xiangkun, Tang Can, Li Shengzhi, Yang Tao, Zhang Sanyun, Li Jing, Zhang Hong, and Ji Rongxin |

The name Zeng Yang appears garbled in the notice body but is clearly shown as “曾旸” in the attached basic-information document. This article reports the proposed appointments and ranks only and does not reproduce personal birth, education, or employment-history details from the attachment.

Organizations and individuals with differing opinions may submit written feedback, visit, or call the Organization and United Front Work Office by January 16, 2013. Organizational submissions must bear the official seal, while individual submissions must include the signatory’s real name. The telephone number is `82902345`, and the email address is `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office of the Party Committee)
