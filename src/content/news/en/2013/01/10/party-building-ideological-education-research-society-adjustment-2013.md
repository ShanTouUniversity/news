Following personnel changes, the STU Party Building and Ideological and Political Education Research Society adjusted its membership after deliberation by the society’s council and approval by university Party leaders. The new structure is as follows:

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chairs | Song *Zhen and Xu Xiaohu |
| Executive vice chairs | Luo Nianchao and Shi Ganggang |
| Vice chairs | Cheng Jiaming, Zeng Dabin, He Wenbiao, and Chen Maohuai |
| Secretary-General | Bai Youjin |
| Deputy secretaries-general | Yu Hong, Liu Yuangen, and Huang Guzi |

Council members are Huai Xiaofeng, Li Haotao, Chen Yiyuan, Chen Chuqun, Liu Yuzhou, Chen Wenbin, Su Wenji, Chen Yinglin, Du Shimin, Zheng Zaixin, Chen Wenguang, Zou Xuandong, Lin Dongyue, Gao Jian, Zhu Lun, Wang Hewen, Yang Jian, Li Youquan, Li Xiangxin, Sun Fenqiang, Yu Shanyan, Chen Zhulin, Huang Shaobin, Lin Bingfeng, Ou Limin, Yang Wei, Cai Guisheng, Yao Xitao, Chen Liqun, Li Liping, Yang Mianhua, and Zhang Jianjun.

Part of the chair Song *Zhen’s name is garbled in the original and has not been reconstructed speculatively.
