The 2013 Shantou University–Hua Hai New Year Cup football tournament, hosted by Shantou University and organized by the STU Faculty Football Association, will be held at the STU football field on January 12, 13, and 19. The participating teams are STU Faculty Football Team, Shantou Customs Football Team, Huaneng Shantou Power Plant Football Team, and Huaneng Haimen Power Plant Football Team. Faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend and support the teams.

### Match schedule

| Date | Time | Match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January 12 (Saturday) | 1:00–2:45 p.m. | STU (yellow) vs. Huaneng Shantou Power Plant (red) |
| January 12 (Saturday) | 3:00–4:45 p.m. | Shantou Customs (white) vs. Huaneng Haimen Power Plant (blue) |
| January 13 (Sunday) | 1:00–2:45 p.m. | Shantou Customs (blue) vs. Huaneng Shantou Power Plant (red) |
| January 13 (Sunday) | 3:00–4:45 p.m. | STU (yellow) vs. Huaneng Haimen Power Plant (blue) |
| January 19 (Saturday) | 1:00–2:45 p.m. | STU (yellow) vs. Shantou Customs (blue) |
| January 19 (Saturday) | 3:00–4:45 p.m. | Huaneng Haimen Power Plant (blue) vs. Huaneng Shantou Power Plant (red) |

The tournament is a men’s 11-a-side competition using a single round-robin format and the latest International Football Association Board Laws of the Game. Each match lasts 90 minutes, with two 45-minute halves. All registered players who meet the eligibility requirements may play; substitutions are unlimited, but substituted players may not return to the match. Each team may register up to 30 in-service employees who are physically fit to play.

### Awards

Awards include champion, runner-up, third place, Outstanding Organization, and Top Scorer. The first three teams and the Outstanding Organization recipient will receive trophies and cash prizes of RMB 1,800, RMB 1,200, RMB 600, and RMB 400, respectively. The Top Scorer will receive a certificate.

The organizing committee may change the match dates in case of severe weather or other force majeure. The regulations set the registration deadline at 5:00 p.m. on January 10, 2013.
