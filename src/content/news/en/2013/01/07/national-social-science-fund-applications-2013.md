The STU Research Office opened applications for 2013 National Social Science Fund of China projects. This year’s projects are divided into key, general, and youth projects, with funding of RMB 250,000–300,000 for key projects and RMB 150,000–180,000 for general and youth projects. Applicants should prepare budgets according to their research needs.

### Eligibility and application limits

Applicants for key and general projects must hold an associate-senior professional title or an equivalent or higher qualification. Youth-project applicants and project-team members must be no older than 39; the notice specifies that applicants should have been born after March 1, 1974. A youth-project applicant without an associate-senior or higher title must have recommendations from two peers holding full-senior professional titles.

A project leader may submit only one application in the same year and may not also join another application. A team member may participate in no more than two applications. Applicants must conduct substantive research and take responsibility for organizing implementation, while participants and recommenders must give consent and sign. Leaders of active National Social Science Fund or National Natural Science Foundation of China projects may not apply, except where the project completion certificate is dated before March 1, 2013.

A National Social Science Fund project leader may not apply in the same year for an NSFC project or another national science and technology plan. Team members may not lead an NSFC or other national science and technology plan application with the same or a similar topic. Applicants for a 2013 Ministry of Education general humanities and social sciences project may also apply, but receiving a National Social Science Fund award before the Ministry project is approved will cancel the Ministry project’s eligibility. Applicants found to have falsified information in a National Social Science Fund application within the previous three years, or bribed review experts within the previous five years, are ineligible.

### Topics and research requirements

Applicants may design their own topics within the research scope, directions, and priorities of the Project Guide, but topics should identify a clear research subject or problem. Self-designed topics that meet the guide’s basic requirements receive the same review procedures, standards, project quotas, and funding intensity as listed topics. Interdisciplinary projects should select one closest primary discipline. Education, art, and military studies are organized separately.

Basic-theory research normally lasts three years and may be extended when necessary. Applied policy research should reflect the timeliness of the problem and normally last at least one year. Basic research should be original and exploratory, while applied research should be targeted and operational. Final results are subject to anonymous correspondence review and, except in special circumstances, should be reviewed before publication. Applications based on a doctoral dissertation or postdoctoral completion report must explain the relationship in the application and submit the original dissertation or report at final evaluation.

### Process and deadlines

Applicants must use the 2013 version of the National Social Science Fund application and complete it truthfully without intellectual-property disputes. The application materials, Project Guide, and data-code table can be obtained from the relevant National Social Science Fund website or the original OA attachments.

| Deadline | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| By January 20, 2013 | Send application materials to `ymhuang@stu.edu.cn` for the Research Office’s formal review |
| By February 19, 2013 | Print the qualified application and the project argumentation sheet double-sided on A3 paper, bind at the center fold, submit seven copies of each, and send electronic copies |
| Late submissions | Not accepted by the university |

The project argumentation sheet may not exceed 4,000 Chinese characters and should list relevant prior research results and references rather than only stating the number of publications. Applicants should preferably specify one expected-result format and use the same final format at project completion. Application forms and argumentation sheets must not contain false information about the applicant or team members or directly or indirectly reveal the applicant’s background. The 2013 National Social Science Fund uses application limits, with unified preliminary screening by experts organized by the provincial planning office.
