STU Graduate School began arranging graduate “three-assistance” positions for spring 2013 and published quotas for each unit. The attached quota table lists teaching-assistant and administrative-assistant positions, with 85 teaching-assistant positions and 50 administrative-assistant positions across the university.

Units at the main campus should establish positions according to their needs and submit a Graduate Teaching Assistant (Administrative Assistant) Position Plan to the Graduate School by January 11, 2013. Electronic copies should be sent to `fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn`.

### Position quotas

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| School of Science, including Multidisciplinary Studies and the Institute of Marine Sciences | 36 | 12 |
| School of Engineering | 24 | 7 |

| Graduate School | 0 | 2 |
| Business School | 6 | 3 |
| Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication | 2 | 2 |
| Academic Affairs Office | 0 | 1 |
| Central Laboratory | 0 | 2 |
| Publicity Department of the Party Committee | 0 | 3 |
| **Total** | **85** | **50** |

The quotas were allocated according to each unit’s position arrangements in fall 2012 and the number of graduate students in the relevant training units, and were designated as fixed quotas for future “three-assistance” positions. Position plans should specify the position title, number, and duties. Teaching-assistant plans should also list the course, instructor, course type, student group, class size, total hours, and number of assistants.
