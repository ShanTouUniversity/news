The second Guangdong Universities Pearl River Scholar Forum will be held for one day at Southern Medical University on January 12, 2013. The forum is organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and Southern Medical University, with operational support from Southern Medical University’s Human Resources Office and Nanfang Hospital. Hou Jinlin, a professor at Southern Medical University, will chair the forum.

The forum theme is “Genomics of Infectious and Liver Diseases and Personalized Treatment.” Keynote presentations and thematic discussions will bring together academicians, Guangdong Pearl River Scholars, and other specialists to discuss developments in the field, including hepatitis-virus gene research, mathematical models of viral evolution, and genome-sequencing technology.
