STU Resource Management Office announced the winning bid for the affiliated middle and primary schools facilities-upgrade project. Following review and recommendation by the university tendering and bidding leading group, Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. was selected with a bid of RMB 270,896.

The result is open for public comment from January 7 through January 9, 2013.
