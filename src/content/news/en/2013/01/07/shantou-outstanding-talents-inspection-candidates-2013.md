Following a review, Bao Nengsheng, vice dean of the STU School of Engineering; Hu Zhong, director of the STU Research Office; and Zheng Huaiping, a professor at the STU School of Science, were selected as candidates for inspection under Shantou’s thirteenth outstanding professional and technical talent program. The inspection was scheduled to begin shortly after the announcement.

The inspection group was organized by the Organization Department of the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee. Its members were Hu Dong, Wu Weijia, Deng Baoguo, and Guo Mingxiang.
