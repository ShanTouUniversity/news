It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold the 133rd session of the “Towards Modern Mathematics” lecture series on January 7, 2013, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412).

## Schedule

| Session | Speaker | Title | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Prof. Bai Zhongzhi (Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences) | Modulus-Based Matrix Splitting Iteration Methods for Linear Complementarity Problems | 15:00, January 7, 2013 | Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412) |
| 2 | Prof. Yang Qixiang (Wuhan University) | Multipliers, New Function Spaces and Harmonic Analysis Techniques in Differential Equations | 16:00, January 7, 2013 | Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412) |

## Speakers

**Bai Zhongzhi** is a researcher at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, CAS. He received his bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Lanzhou University and his doctorate from Shanghai University of Science and Technology, followed by postdoctoral research at Fudan University. His honors include the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, the Feng Kang Prize for Scientific Computing, the CAS Young Scientist Award, selection for the New Century National Hundred-Thousand-Ten Thousand Talents Project, and the State Council Special Allowance.

**Yang Qixiang**, born in June 1966 in Yiyang, Hunan, is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Wuhan University. He studied at Université Paris-Dauphine under Prof. Y. Meyer, a founder of wavelet analysis, and conducted postdoctoral research at Sun Yat-sen University from 1995. From 2002 to 2003 he was a Distinguished Professor at Xi’an Jiaotong University. His research focuses on wavelet analysis and its applications to function spaces, operator theory and harmonic analysis.
