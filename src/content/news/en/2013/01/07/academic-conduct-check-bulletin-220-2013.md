STU’s Three-Style Development Office issued academic-conduct inspection bulletin No. 220, covering inspections of teaching buildings during week 16 of the fall 2012–2013 semester.

Both inspections took place in Building E. The inspection at 2:00 p.m. on December 31, 2012, recorded three students arriving within five minutes after class began and one student arriving more than five minutes late. The inspection at 8:00 a.m. on January 4, 2013, recorded six students arriving more than five minutes late.

The university asked colleges and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to the students concerned and continue improving academic conduct. The original attachment contains the student list; student names and other personal information are not reproduced.
