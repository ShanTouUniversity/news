STU Student Affairs Office issued safety guidance for the 2013 winter break, asking students to prepare for winter weather, travel during the Spring Festival period, and increased gatherings during the holiday.

### Travel and health

Students traveling should choose appropriate transportation, purchase insurance, check destination weather in advance, and take suitable precautions. Those traveling outdoors or visiting relatives and friends should go in groups where possible and avoid exploring undeveloped areas. Students leaving campus should buy tickets through legitimate passenger stations and avoid unlicensed vehicles. In an emergency, remain calm and contact the relevant local authorities promptly.

Cold weather and increased holiday gatherings may increase the spread of respiratory infections. Students should maintain good hygiene, get sufficient sleep, exercise regularly, avoid close contact with people showing flu-like or pneumonia symptoms, wash their hands frequently with soap and clean water, and avoid crowded places where possible. Students at home should maintain a regular routine, pay attention to food safety, follow the law, and stay away from feudal superstition and cult activities.

### Dormitory and residence safety

Students should close doors and windows and turn off water and electricity before leaving their rooms or going to sleep. They must follow the dormitory regulations and may not accommodate people from outside their own dormitory or visitors from off campus. Violations will be handled under university discipline.

Students remaining on campus must follow university safety rules, may not carry open flames into the hills or hold barbecues, and must ensure that on-campus recreational activities are safe. Fishing or swimming is prohibited in the university reservoir and other closed rivers or lakes to prevent drowning, traffic accidents, and other incidents.

### Part-time job fraud prevention

Students seeking winter-break part-time work should use legitimate employment agencies and watch for fraudulent recruitment that requests deposits in exchange for job placement or withholds identity cards. Students attending interviews should tell friends or family where they are going and keep their phones available.

Students remaining on campus should report unusual situations promptly to the Student Community Management and Service Center at `86502661` or the campus security duty line at `82904110`.
