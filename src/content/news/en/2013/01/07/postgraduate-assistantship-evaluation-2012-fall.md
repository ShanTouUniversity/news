It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on January 7, 2013, to evaluate graduate assistantship (“San Zhu”) positions for the fall 2012 semester.

A total of 133 positions were established, including 84 teaching assistant and 49 administrative assistant posts. In accordance with the *Measures for Graduate Assistantship Positions of Shantou University*, arrangements are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Positions | 133 assistantship posts (84 teaching, 49 administrative) |
| Teaching assistant evaluation | Instructors evaluate teaching assistants and complete the *Graduate Teaching Assistant Evaluation Form* |
| Administrative assistant evaluation | Host units evaluate administrative assistants, complete the *Evaluation Form* and attach proof of allowance payment |
| Deadline | Before the end of work on January 11, 2013 |
| Submission point | Graduate School (Room 401, New Administration Building) |
| Note | Positions without evaluation or without proof of payment will not be considered for spring 2013 applications |
| Issued by | Graduate School |

Forms are available in the OA attachment.
