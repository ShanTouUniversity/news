The STU Security Office issued a fraud-prevention safety reminder. According to information from public-security authorities, fraudulent activity had become more active, with university students among the frequent victims. STU had also received reports of students losing money in online-shopping scams.

The office urged faculty, staff, and students not to trust false information because of promised small benefits or convenience. When encountering suspicious people or matters involving money, community members should remain alert, verify information calmly, and avoid acting on unverified claims. Suspicious situations should be reported promptly to a relevant university office or the public-security authorities.

The campus 24-hour emergency and police-assistance telephone is `82904110`.
