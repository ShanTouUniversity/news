The STU Engineering School Youth League Committee opened a 2013 internal selection for design and technology innovation projects. Selected teams may represent the university in competitions including the MIT CDIO Project Competition, the National Undergraduate Energy Conservation and Emission Reduction Social Practice and Science and Technology Competition, and the Central-South Regional Hong Kong and Macao Universities Mechanical Innovation Design and Manufacturing Competition.

The internal selection has three stages: project-scheme selection, prototype production by shortlisted teams, and selection of outstanding works for the relevant competitions. Scheme defenses are planned for the second week after the next semester begins, after which prototypes will be produced according to the schedules of the external competitions.

### Themes

| Theme | Summary of requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanical lighting lamp | Use a mechanical structure to provide lighting without an external power supply or battery; output above 5 W, simple one-person start-up, and overall dimensions no greater than 1.5 × 1.5 × 1 m; the internal contest is judged by lighting duration |
| Fire-scene rescue robot | Based on Problem 2 of the sixth Central-South Regional Hong Kong and Macao Universities Mechanical Innovation Design and Manufacturing Competition; detailed rules govern the project |
| Pendulum-and-ball track “perpetual-motion” device | Based on Problem 1 of the same competition; detailed rules govern the project |
| Low-cost, environmentally friendly machinery | Design and build a machine related to resource and environmental issues; the specific work is open-ended |

Participants must be Engineering School students. Each student may join only one project, and each team must have three members. Mechanical-innovation entries must submit design, engineering-management, manufacturing-process, and cost-analysis reports. The pendulum-and-ball project also has specific size, material, on-site manufacturing, and performance-test requirements.

### Registration

The Engineering School plans to hold a project briefing before January 18. Interested students must complete the registration form and email it by January 15 to `10zxu@stu.edu.cn`. The subject should read “XXX Team Design and Technology Competition Registration Form,” with XXX replaced by the team leader’s name.
