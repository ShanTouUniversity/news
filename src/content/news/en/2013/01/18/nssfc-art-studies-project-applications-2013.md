Shantou University’s Research Office announced application arrangements for the 2013 National Social Science Fund of China Art Studies projects. The program includes key, general, and youth projects under the National Social Science Fund of China, as well as cultural and arts science research projects administered by the Ministry of Culture. Relevant units and researchers may prepare applications using the annual project guide.

### Eligibility and research areas

Applicants for key projects must hold an associate-senior or higher professional title and have led a completed research project in the same field at the provincial or ministerial level or above. General-project applicants must hold an associate-senior or higher professional title and have published three relevant papers in core journals. Youth-project applicants and team members must be no older than 39 on the stated eligibility date, meaning they must have been born after March 10, 1974. Applicants without an associate-senior or higher professional title must be recommended by two peers holding senior professional titles.

The guide covers art theory, drama, film and new media arts, music, dance, fine arts, design arts, and integrated arts and culture studies. Applications should address major theoretical or practical issues in cultural and arts development, combine basic and applied research where appropriate, and encourage original, forward-looking, and interdisciplinary work.

### Submission schedule

Applicants must complete the *Application and Review Form for National Social Science Fund of China Art Studies Projects* and the project-argumentation sheet. Electronic materials for formal review must be sent to `ymhuang@stu.edu.cn` by **March 4, 2013**. After review, applicants must submit paper materials to the Research Office by **March 12**, including three copies of the application and review form, two copies of evidence for completed provincial- or ministerial-level research projects, and six copies of the project-argumentation sheet. Electronic copies must be sent at the same time.

The online application platform was expected to open around January 30, 2013, on the Ministry of Culture website (`www.mcprc.gov.cn`). Online submission was being piloted that year and was scheduled to become mandatory in 2014, when paper-only submissions would no longer be accepted.
