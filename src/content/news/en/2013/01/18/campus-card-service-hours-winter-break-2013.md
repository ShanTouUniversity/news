Shantou University’s Network and Information Center announced winter-break service arrangements for campus card windows. The two service locations will close during different periods.

| Service window | Closure | Reopening |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campus card window at the Expert Apartments | February 6–17, 2013 | February 18 |
| Campus card recharge window below the No. 2 Dining Hall | January 23–February 24, 2013 | February 25 |

During the closures, users may recharge through the integrated self-service deposit-transfer and balance-query terminals. Lost-card reports and balance queries are available through the voice self-service line at `82903414` (campus extension `3414`). During the Spring Festival period, users may call `82903413-200` or email `support@stu.edu.cn` for assistance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
