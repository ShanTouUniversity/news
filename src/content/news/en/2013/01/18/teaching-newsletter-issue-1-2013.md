Shantou University’s Teaching Office published the first 2013 issue of its *Teaching Newsletter* (No. 14 overall), reviewing developments in teaching quality, undergraduate education reform, public-service courses, and student innovation during 2012.

### Teaching quality and reform

In December 2012, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education announced approved undergraduate teaching-quality and reform projects. STU received 31 projects across seven categories: high-quality open video courses, high-quality resource-sharing courses, experimental teaching demonstration centers, provincial teaching-reform projects, comprehensive major-reform pilots, undergraduate practice bases, and innovation laboratories for talent-development models. The university also received recommendations for 100 provincial undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects and RMB 5.32 million in funding during the year.

STU’s 2013 teaching-reform initiative was organized as a university-level quality-engineering program. It covered eight categories, including comprehensive major reform, distinctive majors, talent-development model innovation laboratories, open courses, teaching teams, experimental teaching demonstration centers, undergraduate practice bases, and teaching-reform projects. A total of 191 projects were submitted, including 102 university-level teaching-reform projects.

### Teaching activities

STU opened its 2012 undergraduate innovation-experiment project program to undergraduates and approved 76 projects. Of these, 43 were recommended for provincial status and 33 were university-level projects. The Teaching Office also organized a teaching-methods exchange on December 21–22, 2012. Thirty-nine teacher representatives discussed lessons from the Medical College–Stanford School of Medicine basic-science teaching program, integrated-thinking instruction, and other curriculum reforms.

A 24-member STU public-service-course delegation traveled to Hong Kong in November to attend the first University Social Responsibility Summit. On December 29, the university’s public-service-course working group held its 2012 summary meeting to review course implementation and evaluation and discuss future development.

### Textbooks and student achievements

The Teaching Office organized the second-half 2012 textbook development and publication subsidy program. Two textbooks from the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication and the Business School received university support: *Campus News Interviewing and Practice* and *Business Anthropology*.

The newsletter also reviewed STU student achievements in the National Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling and the China Intelligent Robot Competition. STU teams won a national first prize and multiple national second prizes in mathematical modeling. The Mathematics Department’s robot team won the humanoid-robot all-around championship under the international rules at the China Intelligent Robot Competition and received second prizes in two other events.
