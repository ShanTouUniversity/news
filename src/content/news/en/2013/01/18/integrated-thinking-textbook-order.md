It is reported that Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has opened ordering for the main reference book of the Spring 2013 course [CIS2031] "Integrated Thinking". Students enrolled in the course can place their orders through the university's credit system.

## Textbook Details

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Principles and Methods of Critical Thinking (《批判性思维原理和方法》) |
| Author | Dong Yu |
| Publisher | Higher Education Press |
| Publication Date | September 2010 |
| Price | RMB 37.5 |

## Ordering Arrangements

Students who wish to purchase the book should log in to the credit system and complete the order on the "Textbook Ordering" page. The ordering window opens at 8:00 on January 18 and closes at 21:00 on January 21; requests submitted after the deadline will not be accepted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
