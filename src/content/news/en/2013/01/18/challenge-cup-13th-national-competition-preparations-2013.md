Shantou University forwarded the notice for the 13th Challenge Cup National College Students Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Competition, jointly organized by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League of China, China Association for Science and Technology, Ministry of Education, All-China Students’ Federation, and the Jiangsu Provincial People’s Government. Relevant units at STU are beginning preparations for participation.

Suzhou University and Suzhou Industrial Park will host this edition. Entries are expected to open in early June 2013, and the final review and competition will take place in Suzhou in the second half of the year. Eligible works include academic papers in the natural sciences, social investigation reports and academic papers in philosophy and social sciences, and scientific or technological inventions.

Held every two years, the Challenge Cup competition encourages students to value science, pursue knowledge, and develop innovative abilities while supporting extracurricular academic and scientific activities at universities. The STU Youth League Committee asked colleges and relevant units to study the competition documents and organize preparations in advance.
