Following the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education’s notice on 2013 higher-education quality-engineering projects, Shantou University opened its internal application process for the provincial program.

### Categories and planned recommendations

The program covers comprehensive major-reform pilots, experimental teaching demonstration centers, off-campus undergraduate practice bases, higher-education teaching-reform projects, open courses, undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects, pilot colleges, and teacher-development centers. Applicants must have a university-level project foundation. The program emphasizes collaborative innovation throughout talent development, including cooperation among universities, government, industries, and enterprises for comprehensive major-reform projects.

STU’s main campus planned to recommend two comprehensive major-reform pilots, two experimental teaching demonstration centers, four off-campus undergraduate practice bases, ten higher-education teaching-reform projects, and one or two open courses. Existing provincial-level quality courses could apply for transformation without using an additional quota.

### Application process

Projects with a university-level teaching-reform foundation could apply for provincial higher-education teaching-reform status. Off-campus practice bases would be selected from projects submitted to STU’s 2013 teaching-reform initiative. Recommendations for comprehensive major-reform pilots, experimental teaching demonstration centers, and open courses would be determined from the university-level review, after which the Teaching Office would notify project leaders to complete the provincial materials. Arrangements for undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects, pilot colleges, and teacher-development centers would be announced separately.

Applicants must submit two paper copies to the Teaching Office by **February 25, 2013**, and email electronic materials to `wtyang@stu.edu.cn`. Contact Yang Weitao at `86502736` (campus extension `882736`).
