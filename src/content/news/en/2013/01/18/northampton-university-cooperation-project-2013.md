Shantou University and the University of Northampton (UON) opened applications for their 2013 inter-university cooperation programs. The partnership uses credit recognition to support undergraduate exchange and a 3+1+1 dual-degree or bachelor’s-to-master’s pathway.

### Program structure

| Program | Eligible students | Planned places | Study period |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 3+1+1 dual-degree/bachelor’s-to-master’s pathway | STU third-year undergraduates | 20 | September 9, 2013–July 11, 2014 |
| Exchange program | STU second-year undergraduates | 30 | September 9–December 13, 2013, or one academic year |

The first 3+1+1 cohort accepts students from the STU Business School to study at the UON Business School. Students from the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design may submit portfolios for assessment and apply for relevant UON Arts courses; applications from other schools will also be considered individually. Students who complete three years of study at STU and meet the language requirements may transfer to UON for the final undergraduate year. Students who meet UON’s requirements may then continue into a one-year master’s program.

The exchange program selects second-year STU undergraduates each year. Students may apply for courses in UON’s Business, Social Sciences, Science and Technology, Arts, Education, or Health schools. Exchange participants are eligible for tuition reductions under the cooperation program, with details subject to the program materials.

### 2013 application arrangements

Applications close on **March 29**. Applicants must email the electronic University of Northampton application form to STU’s International Exchange and Cooperation Office at `wscicd@stu.edu.cn`, using “Northampton University Exchange Program Application” as the subject. Required materials include an English personal statement, Chinese and English transcripts, Chinese and English proof of enrollment, one recommendation letter, a passport, IELTS or TOEFL results or participation in the University of Northampton’s internal test, and a portfolio for applicants to art, architecture, and related programs.

Items 2–8 that are temporarily unavailable by the deadline may be supplemented according to each applicant’s situation. If STU cannot issue English transcripts or certificates, applicants may translate them during the academic matching stage and arrange the required translation during the visa stage. Applicants are encouraged to plan early for language testing, academic matching, admission, visas, accommodation, and airport pickup.

### Requirements and estimated costs

Applicants must complete the required courses in China and maintain good academic standing. The stated language requirement is IELTS 6.0 or a pass in UON’s internal language test. Students who do not meet the requirement may take a language course based on their level.

Based on 2013 estimates, tuition and ordinary living costs were approximately GBP 2,700 and RMB 44,000 respectively for one-semester exchange students; about RMB 110,000 for one-year exchange students; RMB 160,000 for the undergraduate pathway; and RMB 170,000 for the master’s pathway. Costs may change according to the university’s current published rates.

The suggested timeline was to improve English in January and February, submit IELTS results and take the internal language test in March, apply for admission and pay the tuition deposit in mid-April, complete supplementary materials and prepare visa documents in May, arrange accommodation and airport pickup in June, and travel to the United Kingdom in September for major courses.
