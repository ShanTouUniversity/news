It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has completed the disbursement of travel subsidies for financially disadvantaged graduates of the Class of 2013. The funds have been transferred to the students' Bank of China accounts, and colleges and residential colleges have been notified to inform recipients to check their accounts.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Recipients | Graduating students of the Class of 2013 with financial difficulties |
| Disbursement channel | Students' Bank of China accounts |
| Responsible office | Student Affairs Office |

The subsidy aims to ease the travel burden on students from economically disadvantaged families as they return home after job hunting. The Student Affairs Office reminded recipients to check their account balances and to consult their college or the office with any questions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
