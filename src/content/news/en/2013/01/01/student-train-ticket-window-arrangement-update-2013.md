Because of problems with the railway ticket machines, STU Student Affairs Office revised the arrangements for the temporary student train-ticket sales and free-collection window for winter break 2013. The university window could not print tickets, including bullet-train tickets, that had been successfully booked through the Railway Customer Service Center website (12306).

### Revised purchasing and collection arrangements

The original centralized ticket-collection period from 1:30 to 2:30 p.m. on January 2, 2013, was cancelled. The revised arrangements are as follows:

- Students who neither joined the university pre-registration nor bought tickets online, as well as students whose trial “no-waiting purchase” failed or who missed their original queue position, may queue at the window on a first-come, first-served basis with their identity and student cards.
- Students who successfully booked tickets through 12306 and need to collect them at the university window must submit their identity card, student card, and ticket order number to on-site staff by 4:00 p.m. on January 2, and register a telephone number.
- When an order number covers multiple passengers, the documents for the corresponding passengers must be provided individually.

All other ticket-sale arrangements remain unchanged. Students with questions may consult on-site staff or contact Lu at 86502247 (campus short number 882247).
