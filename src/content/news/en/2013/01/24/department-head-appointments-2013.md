Following deliberation at the first university leadership meeting of 2013, Shantou University issued Document No. 19 [2013] announcing several department-head appointments.

Chen Ruilin was appointed head of the Law Department in the School of Law; Niu Xiaodong was appointed head of the Department of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering; Fan Zhen was appointed head of the Department of Electronic Engineering; and Cai Hao was appointed head of the Department of Computer Science in the School of Engineering. Each appointment carries a six-month probationary period.

The university also announced that Xiong Jincai, Cheng Xiyun, Li Xutao, and Sun Haojun would no longer serve as heads of the Law, Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Electronic Engineering, and Computer Science departments, respectively.
