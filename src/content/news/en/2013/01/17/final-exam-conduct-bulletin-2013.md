The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office issued a bulletin on the fall 2012–2013 final examinations, covering examination-management and conduct issues identified between January 7 and 16.

During the Probability and Mathematical Statistics (Economics and Biology) examination in E305 from 8:00 to 10:00 a.m. on January 16, a Business School invigilator arrived 15 minutes after the examination began. Under Article 3, Item 2 of the *STU Trial Measures for Identifying Undergraduate Teaching Incidents*, the office classified the conduct as an ordinary teaching incident.

During the Structural Mechanics II examination in E303 from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on January 14, a student in the 2009 Civil Engineering cohort was found carrying unauthorized materials. The bulletin classified the conduct as cheating by carrying materials, stated that the course would receive a score of zero, and said the student would face administrative disciplinary action and might be unable to receive a bachelor’s degree upon graduation. The student’s name, student number, and other personal information are not published.

The Academic Affairs Office also received an email at 10:39 p.m. on January 16 reporting that some invigilators had failed to perform their duties diligently, allowing students to copy from one another without effective intervention. The bulletin asked invigilators to follow the *STU Invigilation Rules* (Shanda Teaching〔2011〕No. 2), arrive on time, and perform their duties properly. Students were likewise reminded to observe examination rules and act honestly.
