Following discussion at the first 2013 university leadership meeting on January 16, Shantou University appointed Zhu Lun director of the Alumni Affairs Office. Lin Shihong no longer serves as director of the office.

The personnel decision was issued in Document Shanda〔2013〕No. 10 on January 17, 2013.
