To carry out faculty appointments under the annual-salary system, the Shantou University Engineering School adjusted its appointment committee in accordance with the *STU Interim Measures for Appointing Faculty to Annual-Salary Positions* (Shanda〔2007〕No. 43) and the *STU Engineering School Interim Measures for Faculty Assessment*, taking the school’s circumstances into account. The committee is responsible for faculty appointment matters within the school.

### Committee members

| Role | Member(s) |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Chen Yan |
| Members | Wang Yingzi, Chen Shaoke, Zhang Jie, Lin Peng, Zheng Zaixin, Liu Ping, Jiang Yongquan, Cheng Xiyun, Cai Hao, and Xiong Guangjing |
| Secretary | Huang Yixiong |

The members are listed in order of the number of strokes in their surnames. The document was copied to the Human Resources Office.
