The Shantou University Resource Management Office announced that electronic bills for office telephone charges incurred from December 1 to 31, 2012, had been submitted by China Telecom’s Shantou branch to the university telephone office and entered into the Telephone Management System. Verification officers in each unit can log in to the [Telephone Management System](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) to review the itemized bills.

Units with no objections should click “Confirm the selected bills” in the system. If an item does not match actual telephone use, the unit should email `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` by January 22, 2013, so the university can report the discrepancy to China Telecom. After the verification period, bills for which no objection was raised and no confirmation was made will be treated by the system as confirmed.

After verification, the Finance Office will deduct the charges by internal transfer against each unit’s designated funding number on January 29. Questions or suggestions about the system may also be sent to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
