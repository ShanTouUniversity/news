The first 2013 meeting of the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee confirmed the administrative ranks of 13 mid-level management appointees after their probation periods ended. The appointees completed duty reports, performance assessments, democratic evaluations, and organizational reviews.

### Administrative ranks

| Rank | Person(s) | Effective date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section-chief level | Weng Luyin | January 2012 |
| Deputy section-chief level | Zhang Guihua, Lu Zhuobin, Lan Na, Yao Yi, Zhuang Xiangkun, Tang Can, and Li Shengzhi | December 2011 |
| Deputy section-chief level | Yang Tao, Zhang Sanyun, and Li Jing | May 2012 |
| Deputy section-chief level | Zhang Hong and Ji Rongxin | June 2012 |

The decision was made under the *Trial Measures for the Appointment of STU Management Cadres* (Shanda Party〔2009〕No. 47) and related rules. The document is Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee Document〔2013〕No. 3.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee)
