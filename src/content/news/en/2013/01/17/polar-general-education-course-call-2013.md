With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, Shantou University planned an Antarctic scientific exploration project for the 2014 winter break. The project was expected to select 16 students and four faculty members to visit China’s Great Wall Station and conduct exploration activities including environmental protection work. To support the project, the Academic Affairs Office and Student Affairs Office invited faculty proposals for polar-themed general-education courses.

### Course proposal requirements

Proposed courses must relate to the polar regions, but may fall within any category, including mathematics and natural sciences, philosophy and social sciences, or languages, literature, and the arts. Courses may carry one or two credits and may be offered in the spring, summer, or fall 2013 semester. Spring courses are preferred, and courses are generally expected to continue for at least three years. Teaching formats are flexible as long as the required class hours are met.

Instructors must complete the attachment and obtain review comments from their teaching unit before submitting it to the Student Affairs Office Outdoor-Expansion Office for initial review. That office would determine the amount of funding support before submitting the proposal to the Academic Affairs Office for approval. The *STU General-Education Course Application Form* should include the requested budget in the “Conditions for Offering the Course” section.

Applications are due by 5:00 p.m. on February 25, 2013. Questions may be sent to the Student Affairs Office Outdoor-Expansion Office at `expedition@stu.edu.cn`.
