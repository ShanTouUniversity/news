Shantou University issued Document Shanda〔2013〕No. 9, changing the name of the “Shantou University Alumni Association Office” to the “Shantou University Alumni Affairs Office.”

The university document was issued on January 17, 2013, and circulated to all university units.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
