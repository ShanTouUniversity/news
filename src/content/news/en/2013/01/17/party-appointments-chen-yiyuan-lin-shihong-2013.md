Following discussion at the first 2013 university leadership meeting and the second meeting of the 2013 Standing Committee of the University Party Committee, the Shantou University Party Committee issued Document Shanda Party〔2013〕No. 3 announcing two personnel changes:

| Person | Appointment change |
| --- | --- |
| Chen Yiyuan | Appointed secretary of the general Party branch for retired faculty and staff; no longer secretary of the general Party branch of the Changjiang School of Arts and Design |
| Lin Shihong | Appointed student-affairs coordinator and deputy secretary of the general Party branch of the Changjiang School of Arts and Design, presiding over the branch’s work, with a six-month probation |

The document was issued by the CPC Shantou University Committee and Shantou University on January 17, 2013. It was sent to the CPC Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee and Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, and copied to the CPC Shantou Municipal Organization Department and Shantou Municipal Human Resources Bureau.
