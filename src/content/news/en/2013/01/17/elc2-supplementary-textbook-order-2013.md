The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office and English Language Center reminded students enrolled in ELC2 in spring 2013 that the course uses two books: *New Standard College English: Viewing, Listening and Speaking 2* and *New Standard College English: Synchronous Test 2*. The second book was not linked to the credit-system portal when the course plan was prepared and is now available through a supplementary order.

The book, edited by Wen Qiufang and published by Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press in May 2010, costs RMB 14.90. Students who need the book should log in to the credit-system portal and place the order through its “Textbook Ordering” page.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Ordering period | January 17, 10:00 a.m. to January 21, 9:00 a.m. |
| Ordering channel | “Textbook Ordering” page in the credit-system portal |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office and English Language Center)
