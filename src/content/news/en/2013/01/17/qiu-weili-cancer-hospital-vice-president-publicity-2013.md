Following discussion at the second 2013 meeting of the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee, the university proposed appointing Qiu Weili vice president of the affiliated cancer hospital of its Medical College. The appointment was publicized under the *STU Trial Measures for Pre-Appointment Publicity of Cadres*.

Qiu is currently director of the affiliated cancer hospital office. Since 2004, he has also served as chair of the hospital’s ethics committee for clinical drug trials, and since 2010, as head of its infrastructure office. His professional titles include chief physician, labor economist, and lecturer.

### Publicity feedback

Organizations and individuals with differing opinions may submit feedback in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Organization and United Front Work Department of the STU Party Committee by January 23, 2013. Organizational submissions must bear the official seal, and individual submissions must be signed with the submitter’s real name.
