The first 2013 meeting of the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee confirmed deputy department-level administrative ranks for five senior-management appointees after their probation periods ended: Su Shaohui, Chen Wenguang, Du Shimin, Chen Wenzheng, and Zheng Zaixin.

The five completed duty reports, performance assessment, democratic evaluation, and organizational review before the decision.

| Person(s) | Effective administrative-ranking date |
| --- | --- |
| Su Shaohui and Chen Wenguang | November 2011 |
| Du Shimin | March 2012 |
| Chen Wenzheng and Zheng Zaixin | May 2012 |

The decision was made under the *Trial Measures for the Appointment of STU Management Cadres* (Shanda Party〔2009〕No. 47) and related rules. The document was copied to the CPC Shantou Municipal Organization Department and Shantou Municipal Human Resources Bureau.
