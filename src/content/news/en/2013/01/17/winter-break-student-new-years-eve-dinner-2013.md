With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office arranged a Lunar New Year’s Eve dinner for students staying on campus during the 2013 winter break.

### Event details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Reservation period | January 18, 4:00 p.m. to January 25, 4:00 p.m., 2013 |
| Dinner | February 9, 2013, at 5:30 p.m. |
| Venue | First-floor Western Restaurant, Junhua Hotel |
| Reservation system | [activity.stu.edu.cn](http://activity.stu.edu.cn) |

Students staying on campus during the break who wished to attend were asked to register through the reservation system using their campus internet accounts. To prevent food waste, students who made a reservation but failed to attend without a valid reason would be responsible for their individual meal cost. The confirmed participant list and detailed arrangements would be announced separately.

Questions and suggestions could be sent to `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn` or submitted by telephone at `82903355`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
