The Shantou University Party Publicity Department forwarded the Shantou Municipal Party Publicity Department’s call for applications for the 2012 Shantou Literature and Arts Awards (Shantou Publicity〔2013〕No. 3). The call invited university units and individuals to submit eligible projects. The awards include Excellence, Creativity, and Achievement categories, covering drama, literature, fine arts, music, dance, folk arts, photography, calligraphy, acrobatics, quyi, film and television, and arts theory.

### Eligibility and categories

Applicants, groups, and works must be by people from Shantou or people who have lived and worked in Shantou for an extended period, and their creations. Outstanding works and individuals or groups that received awards in provincial-level or higher arts competitions organized by government, publicity and culture departments, or recognized professional bodies may apply. Works within the scope of the “Five-One Project” awards may also apply if they have contemporary and distinctive artistic qualities, significant positive social, economic, or cultural influence, and public recognition.

The Excellence Award targets works or stage-art programs that won provincial-level or higher arts awards or were published, performed, or broadcast by major media or important arts platforms. The Creativity Award targets works that break with convention, use forward-looking concepts or methods, or represent a new direction in cultural development. The Achievement Award targets arts practitioners, arts teachers, and arts theorists with substantial accomplishments and domestic or international influence who have worked in the cultural and arts field for at least 40 years and remain active in it.

### Submission schedule

STU units must consolidate project materials and the statistics form and submit them to the Party Publicity Department in Room 315 of the New Administration Center by February 25, 2013. Electronic documents should be emailed to `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn`. The STU contact telephone number is `86502343`.

The original municipal notice set February 28, 2013, as the deadline for applicants and March 5 as the deadline for preliminary-review recommending organizations. Municipal questions may be directed to the Arts Division of the Municipal Party Publicity Department at `88437314` or the General Affairs Division of the Shantou Federation of Literary and Art Circles at `88525211`.
