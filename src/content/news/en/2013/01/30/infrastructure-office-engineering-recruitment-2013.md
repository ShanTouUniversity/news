To support campus infrastructure work, the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is recruiting one repair-system administrator and one civil-engineering management engineer.

### Positions

| Position | Main responsibilities and requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Repair-system administrator | Manage routine repair and maintenance requests for campus buildings and structures; assist with maintenance rules, annual plans, tenders, contracts, inspections, and emergency response. An associate degree or above is required; applicants with construction, civil engineering, or related backgrounds, technical qualifications, or repair-management experience are preferred. Familiarity with Windows, Word, and Excel is required. |
| Civil-engineering management engineer | Manage civil-engineering technical work for construction projects; participate in project preparation, design, tendering, site management, material inspection, schedule and quality tracking, quantity review, and acceptance inspections. An associate degree or above in civil engineering or construction engineering is required, along with an intermediate or higher construction-engineering qualification. Project-management and site-construction experience, and familiarity with CAD and office software, are preferred. |

Both positions require communication, coordination, teamwork, responsibility, and the ability to work in engineering-management settings. The positions are auxiliary contract roles, with compensation determined under relevant university rules; the employment relationship will not be transferred to Shantou University.

Application materials must be mailed in a sealed package or emailed to the Infrastructure Office by **February 10, 2013**. Contact the office at `o_jjb@stu.edu.cn`, `0754-86503216`, `0754-86502490`, or fax `0754-86502314`; the postal code is `515063`. Shortlisted applicants will be contacted for interviews.
