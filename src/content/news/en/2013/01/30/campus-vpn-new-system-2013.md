Shantou University’s Network and Information Center will launch a new campus VPN system at `https://vpn.stu.edu.cn` on **February 1, 2013**. The service will give faculty and staff a secure way to access designated campus resources from off campus. It supports Windows, Mac, PC, iPad, and Android devices, offers Chinese and English interfaces, and supports multiple carrier networks. The existing campus WebVPN service will remain available.

Accounts previously approved for WebVPN will transfer their permissions and validity periods directly to the new VPN system. Faculty and staff who need access but do not yet have an account may apply at `http://netms.stu.edu.cn`. The center advises users not to share account passwords and to follow the university’s computer-network management rules.

For assistance, email `stunic@stu.edu.cn` or call `0754-86503400-1`.
