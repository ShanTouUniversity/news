Shantou University’s Network and Information Center announced campus network service arrangements for the 2013 Spring Festival period. From **February 6 through 16**, on-duty staff will focus on monitoring and protecting the core campus network and key services.

Routine services and individual repair requests during this period must be logged through the self-service platform at `its.stu.edu.cn`. On-duty staff will schedule responses as conditions permit, but cannot guarantee resolution within 24 hours. Normal self-service procedures will resume on **February 17**. For urgent failures, call the center’s duty line at `0754-86503400-1` or email `stunic@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
