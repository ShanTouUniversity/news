To support key construction projects, the Key Construction Projects Office of Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is recruiting three staff members: one electrical management engineer, one civil-construction management engineer, and one construction-permit clerk.

### Positions

- **Electrical management engineer**: Bachelor’s degree or above in an electrical field. Construction-site management experience and at least three years of relevant work experience are preferred, as is an intermediate or higher technical title in an electrical field. Duties include electrical technical management, coordination of water and electricity at construction sites, inspection of materials and project progress, quantity review, and completion acceptance.
- **Civil-construction management engineer**: Bachelor’s degree or above in civil engineering, construction engineering, or a related field. Construction-site management experience and at least three years of relevant work experience are preferred, as is an intermediate or higher technical title in construction engineering. Duties include civil-engineering management, pre-construction preparation, material and progress inspections, quantity review, and acceptance work.
- **Construction-permit clerk**: Associate degree or above, preferably in civil engineering, a science field, or a related discipline. At least two years of construction-permit or related management experience is preferred. Duties include organizing permit procedures and documentation, preparing application materials, processing internal university approvals, coordinating outstanding issues, and maintaining project records.

All three positions require communication, writing, office-software, coordination, and teamwork skills. The general age limit is 50 for the engineering positions and 55 for the construction-permit clerk position.

### Employment and applications

The positions are auxiliary contract positions, and the employee’s personnel relationship will not be transferred to STU. Compensation will follow applicable university rules. Application materials must be submitted by **January 29, 2013**, by sealed letter or email to the Key Construction Projects Office. Candidates who pass the initial screening will be contacted for interviews.

Address: Key Construction Projects Office, Resource Management Office, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Telephone: `0754-86503890` or `0754-86502890`; fax: `0754-86505111`; email: `o_jjc@stu.edu.cn`. Applicants should identify the position in the subject line or on the envelope.
