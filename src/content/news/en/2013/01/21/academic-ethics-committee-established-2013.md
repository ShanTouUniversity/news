Following deliberation at a Shantou University Academic Committee meeting on January 9, 2013, the university established an Academic Standards Committee to regulate academic conduct, strengthen academic ethics, and support healthy and sustained development of research.

Li Dan was appointed chair. The committee includes members from relevant teaching, research, and administrative units, and its secretariat is located in the Research Office. One name in the original roster is garbled in the archived source; it has not been reconstructed speculatively, and the full roster is not reproduced here.
