It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 16 [2013] on January 21, 2013, to announce the members of the subcommittees under the Academic Committee.

In accordance with the *Working Regulations of the Academic Committee of Shantou University* (STU Document [2013] No. 14), the proposal was deliberated and approved by the Academic Committee on January 10. The subcommittees are as follows (in no particular order):
