It is reported that the Student Affairs Office announced on January 21, 2013 the results of the 2012 “Golden Phoenix Award” for the student affairs system of Shantou University.

Established in 2012 to commend outstanding staff and strengthen the student affairs team, the award was decided through individual applications and selection by a review committee. The winner of the 2012 Golden Phoenix Award is Zhang Yan.

The office encourages the awardee to continue striving for excellence and calls on all student affairs staff to uphold a student-centered approach and improve education, management and service.
