The Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology is seeking suggestions for the 2014 application guide of the National Natural Science Foundation of China–Guangdong provincial government joint fund, known as the Guangdong Joint Fund. STU’s Research Office forwarded the call to faculty members interested in proposing research topics.

### Research areas

| Area | Suggested directions |
| --- | --- |
| Agriculture | High-yield production and pathogen control for economically important animals and plants in South China, excluding chickens, pigs, and rice; aquaculture and disease control for distinctive species in the South China Sea and Beibu Gulf |
| Resources and environment | Salt-tide control in Guangdong; typhoon and climate mechanisms; pollution control and contaminated-site remediation during industrial relocation |
| Population and health | Thalassemia prevention and treatment; modernization of Lingnan traditional Chinese medicine; development and use of marine medicines from the South China Sea |
| Materials and engineering | Biomaterials; LEDs; electric vehicles; marine exploration equipment for the South China Sea |
| Electronic information | Medical devices; cybersecurity and information security; cloud computing |

Proposals should not duplicate topics in Guangdong Joint Fund guides officially published by the National Natural Science Foundation of China from 2006 through 2013. Electronic suggestions should be sent to `yxhu@stu.edu.cn` by **March 12, 2013**.
