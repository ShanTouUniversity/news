Shantou University published revised *Regulations of the Shantou University Teaching Committee*. The Academic Committee approved the revision on January 9, 2013. The revised text is dated November 21, 2012, and is intended to promote more scientific and democratic decision-making in teaching affairs.

### Role and composition

The Teaching Committee is a body under the Shantou University Academic Committee. Entrusted by the Academic Committee, it reviews matters concerning undergraduate teaching and submits policy recommendations, while providing professional advice on undergraduate teaching management and quality improvement.

The committee comprises the university leader responsible for teaching, the director of the Teaching Office, associate deans or heads of centers and departments responsible for teaching, the director of the Faculty Development and Assessment Center, and elected professor representatives. It has one chair and may appoint one or two vice chairs. Its secretariat is attached to the Teaching Office, and the director of the Teaching Office serves as secretary-general. Committee membership must be confirmed by the Academic Committee and announced by the university.

### Subcommittees and procedures

The regulations establish six subcommittees:

1. Teaching Planning and Development;
2. Teaching Quality and Educational Assessment;
3. Student Assessment;
4. Major Development;
5. Teaching Conditions;
6. General Education.

The Teaching Committee must hold at least two full meetings each year. A resolution is valid when at least two-thirds of all members attend. Matters involving university-wide teaching policy must be reviewed by the Teaching Committee and then submitted to the Academic Committee for approval.
