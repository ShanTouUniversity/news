Shantou University’s Research Office forwarded recent calls from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology for international science and technology cooperation projects involving China and Slovenia, Switzerland, Denmark, Belarus, and the Commonwealth of Independent States.

The forwarded calls cover intergovernmental science and technology cooperation with CIS countries, the 2013 China–Switzerland Green Innovation Partnership, a China–Denmark research and demonstration program in energy, a China–Belarus intergovernmental high-technology cooperation program, and projects for the 10th meeting of the China–Slovenia Commission for Scientific and Technological Cooperation.

Faculty interested in applying should review the requirements of each individual call and contact the Research Office before the relevant application deadline. The forwarded notice does not list the specific deadlines in its body.

Contacts: Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng, `86502813`.

The original title refers to Slovenia, Switzerland, Denmark, and CIS countries, while one country name in the body is rendered as “Sweden” when the individual calls are listed. This article preserves the source discrepancy rather than correcting it without supporting evidence.
