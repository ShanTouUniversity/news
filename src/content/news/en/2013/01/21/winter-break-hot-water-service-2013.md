To support students who remain on campus during the winter break, Shantou University will operate hot-water service points from **January 26 through February 21, 2013**, daily from **3:00 to 11:00 p.m.**

Male students may use the point at L118, the duty room, for hot water and showers. Female students may use the restroom beside the duty room in Building E and Room L116. Students must bring their campus cards and register their actual water usage at the duty room in the building where they receive service.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Student Affairs Office)
