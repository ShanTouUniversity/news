Following personnel changes affecting some members, the Shantou University Academic Committee reviewed an adjustment to its membership at a meeting on January 9, 2013. The university issued Document No. 12 [2013] to announce the revised composition.

Gu Peihua was appointed chair, Lin Danming vice chair, and Hu Zhong secretary-general. The committee includes members from different teaching, research, and administrative units, listed in the original notice in order of the number of strokes in their surnames. The roster also includes international members Don Snow, Michael Palmer (Peng Wenhao), and Terry Bodenhorn.

Two names in the original roster are garbled in the archived source. They have not been reconstructed speculatively, and the full roster is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
