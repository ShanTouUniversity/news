Following recommendations from university units and review by the Shantou University Academic Committee under the *Regulations of the Shantou University Teaching Committee* (STU Document No. 14 [2013]), the university adjusted the composition of its Teaching Committee. The revised membership was announced in STU Document No. 15 [2013].

Gu Peihua was appointed chair, Wen Xiaobo vice chair, and Lu Xiaohua secretary-general. The committee includes members from multiple schools, teaching centers, and relevant administrative units. The roster is ordered alphabetically by the pinyin of Chinese surnames and includes Don Snow and Peng Wenhao (Michael Palmer).

Two names in the original roster are garbled. They have not been reconstructed speculatively, and the full roster is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
