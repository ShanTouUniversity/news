It is reported that Shantou University launched the application and selection process for its 2012 student innovation experiment projects in November 2012. Following review, recommendation by each college, and public online announcement, a total of 76 projects have been approved as STU's 2012 student innovation experiment projects.

The approved projects are divided into two levels:

| Level | Number | Representative Project |
| ----- | ------ | ---------------------- |
| Provincial-level projects | 43 | Small Underwater Prospecting Robot |
| University-level projects | 33 | Fractal Structure of the Generalized Sierpinski Carpet |

According to the Implementation Measures for Student Innovation Experiment Projects of Shantou University (Trial) (STU Fa [2010] No. 169), each approved team is required to make full use of the teaching facilities provided by the university, conduct research according to the schedule set out in the application, and strengthen project management to ensure smooth implementation.

The Academic Affairs Office said the university will track the progress of all projects and organize mid-term inspections and final acceptance reviews to safeguard project quality.
