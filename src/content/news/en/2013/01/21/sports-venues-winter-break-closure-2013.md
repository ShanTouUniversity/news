Shantou University’s gym and indoor badminton courts will close during the winter break in accordance with the university holiday schedule. The badminton courts will reopen in the first week of the spring semester, while the gym will reopen in the second week.

The notice does not provide specific calendar dates. Students and faculty should check facility arrangements after the spring semester begins.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
