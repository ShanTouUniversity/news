The 261st lecture in the Shantou University Law School series will be held on January 13, 2013. Chen Zhenming, dean, professor, and doctoral supervisor at Xiamen University’s School of Public Affairs, will speak on “Frontier Issues in Public-Policy Research.” Faculty and students across the university are welcome.

### Lecture details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | January 13, 2013, 8:30–10:30 a.m. |
| Venue | Law School Room 213 |
| Speaker | Professor Chen Zhenming, dean of Xiamen University’s School of Public Affairs |
| Topic | Frontier issues in public-policy research |

Chen also serves as dean of Xiamen University’s Institute of Marxism. He is a Changjiang Distinguished Professor appointed by the Ministry of Education and an adviser to the Fujian Provincial People’s Government, and holds positions with academic and educational bodies in public administration and Master of Public Administration education. His research and teaching focus on public administration, public policy, and political theory. He has led projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the National Social Science Fund, and provincial and ministerial programs, published works including *Understanding Public Affairs* and *Competitive Government*, and written nearly 200 academic papers.
