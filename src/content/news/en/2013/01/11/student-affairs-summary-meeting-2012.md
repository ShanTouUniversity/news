It is reported that Shantou University will hold its 2012 Annual Student Affairs Summary Meeting on Thursday, January 17, from 2:30 pm to 5:30 pm in ACC Meeting Room 5, to review the university's student affairs work over the past year.

The Student Affairs Office stressed that the meeting is of great importance and asked all colleges and residential colleges to arrange their work in advance, attend punctually, and prepare for their presentations.
