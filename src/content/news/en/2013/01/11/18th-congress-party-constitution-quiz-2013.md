The STU Organization and United Front Work Office forwarded the Shantou Municipal Organization Department’s notice on the study quiz for the report of the 18th National Party Congress and the Party Constitution, Document Shanzu〔2013〕No. 4. It asked student and faculty Party members and activists seeking Party membership to take part in the study and quiz.

Party general branches and directly affiliated branches should organize study and participation according to local circumstances, then submit participation reports in writing or electronically to the university Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Office by the first day of each month at `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. Quiz questions and detailed arrangements were published on the Communist Party Member Network, Guangdong Organization Work Network, Guangdong Party Member and Cadre Distance Education Network, Nanfang.com, Shantou Organization Work Network, and Dahua Online.

Units could collect the *Compilation of Documents of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China* and the *Constitution of the Communist Party of China* from the Organization and United Front Work Office in Room 315 of the Administration Center from January 14 through 18.

The quiz was jointly organized by the Party Member Education Center of the Central Organization Department, People’s Publishing House, and the Communist Party Member Network. Outstanding individual and organization awards would be presented, and the Guangdong Provincial Organization Department would also select outstanding organizations based on participating Party-member numbers and organizational activity.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office of the Party Committee)
