It is reported that the College of Engineering issued a notice on January 11, 2013, to select students for the 2013 STU–University of Calgary College of Engineering exchange program.

The program will run for four weeks in May 2013, taught in English by professors dispatched by the University of Calgary. The university will send 20 students to study alongside 20 STU students. The theme is *Solving Global Problems: Innovation and Leadership in Renewable Energy*, with two courses: *Innovation & Entrepreneurship in Renewable Energy* and *Renewable Energy Practicum* (2 credits each). Participants will earn 4 credits and a joint certificate from both universities.

## Selection

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 20 |
| Duration | 4 weeks in spring 2013 (May) |
| Eligibility | Primarily 2010 Mechanical Engineering students of the College of Engineering; also open to 2009–2011 students of the College of Engineering and School of Science (excluding Mathematics); 2009 students need supervisor consent; strong English proficiency, good academic performance and teamwork required |
| Process | Shortlisting followed by interview |
| Deadline | 17:00, January 15, 2013 |
| How to apply | Complete the form, rename as “Grade + Major + Name” and send to designated email |

The form and email are in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy.
