The China Scholarship Council opened applications for its 2013 outstanding-undergraduate international exchange program. Four Engineering School majors at Shantou University that participate in the Excellence Engineer Training Plan received five placements. Selected students will begin a 12-month course of study at the University of Calgary in Canada in fall 2013.

### Eligibility

Applicants must be full-time Engineering School undergraduates in the 2010 or 2011 cohort, have strong academic and personal records, and rank in the top 10% of their major and year when applying. They must hold Chinese citizenship, be in good physical and mental health, have no record of violations, and intend to return and contribute after completing their studies. Students who have received but not yet used a China Scholarship Council award, or who are currently studying or working abroad, are not included in this selection.

Applicants must meet one of the following English-language conditions:

- At least 8–12 months of study in an English-speaking country;
- A passing result in the National Foreign Language Proficiency Examination (WSK);
- Completion of an advanced English course at a Ministry of Education-designated overseas-study training center;
- IELTS or TOEFL scores meeting the China Scholarship Council standard of IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 95. The University of Calgary separately requires IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 83.

### Placements and funding

| Major | Placements |
| --- | ---: |
| Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation | 2 |
| Electronic Engineering | 1 |
| Communications Engineering | 1 |
| Computer Science and Technology | 1 |

Students approved by the China Scholarship Council will receive one round trip of international travel and the stipulated living scholarship. The school will reimburse the English-test fee once for students who pass the test. Students must pay their own tuition during the exchange, estimated in the notice at approximately CAD 15,000. Students who return to STU on schedule may receive a reward of RMB 6,000–50,000 based on academic performance. If no candidate is selected from a major, its placement will be transferred to another major.

The total selection score consists of major GPA, English score, and comprehensive qualities, weighted at 50%, 30%, and 20%, respectively. The Engineering School Youth League Committee will organize a review panel to assess the comprehensive qualities of candidates who pass the English test.

Applicants must register by March 31, 2013, with their IELTS or TOEFL score report at the academic-affairs office of their major in Gongnan Building Room 303.
