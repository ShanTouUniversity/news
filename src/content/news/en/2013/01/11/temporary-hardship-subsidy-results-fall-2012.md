Under the Provisional STU Undergraduate Temporary Hardship Assistance Measures, the schools and colleges reviewed students’ submissions. After the Student Affairs Office conducted a further review and publicity process, STU announced the results for fall semester 2012–2013: 163 undergraduate students received assistance.

The full list is included in the original Student Affairs Office attachment and is available to the relevant schools and colleges. To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce names, student numbers, or individual assistance details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Consult the original OA attachment through the OA system for the temporary hardship-assistance list; student personal information is not reproduced on the news page.
