The Shantou University Continuing Education Center for Professional and Technical Personnel began distributing certificates for 2012 continuing-education courses. Office staff from units at the main campus must collect the materials from the Human Resources Office in Room 315 of the Administration Center by January 16. Faculty and staff who took the courses should then collect their individual certificates from their unit offices.

### Registration and review

After receiving the certificate, faculty and staff must log in to the [Guangdong Professional and Technical Personnel Continuing Education Management System](http://59.41.8.110/vsappgdhr/gdhr/login_gdjx.aspx) and complete personal registration and course-record reporting. Required information includes continuing-education hours, course content, learning format, start and end dates, assessment result, and provider.

Each course certificate must first be photographed or scanned and saved as a file, then uploaded during the application process. Applications without uploaded proof cannot pass review. Course type, dates, and hours must be entered strictly according to the certificate. The assessment result should be selected as “Pass,” and the higher-education *Teaching Methods* course in an advanced training program should be registered as a professional course.

After submission, the Continuing Education Center will review the information online and submit it to the human resources and social security authorities for approval. Faculty and staff can view and print certificates obtained through the system.

For inquiries, call `86502349` or contact Fan Jibin.
