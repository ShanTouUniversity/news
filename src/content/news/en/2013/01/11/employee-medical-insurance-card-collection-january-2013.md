The Shantou University Resource Management Office issued a notice asking 16 employees to collect their employee medical-insurance manuals and IC cards during working hours from the employee medical-insurance office on the second floor of the campus hospital.

To protect personal medical-insurance information, this article does not reproduce the names in the notice. The relevant employees should use the internal notice to complete collection at the designated location.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
