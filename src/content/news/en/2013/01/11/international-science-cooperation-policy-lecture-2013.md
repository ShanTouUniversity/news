Shantou University’s Research Office will hold a lecture on policy interpretation and application guidance for international and regional science-cooperation projects on January 14, 2013. Zeng Lu, director of the Science and Technology Exchange and Cooperation Division of the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology, will deliver the lecture.

### Lecture details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | January 14, 2013, 9:00–11:30 a.m. |
| Venue | ACC Room 3 |
| Speaker | Zeng Lu, director of the Science and Technology Exchange and Cooperation Division, Guangdong Department of Science and Technology |
| Topic | Policy interpretation and application guidance for international and regional science-cooperation projects |

The session is primarily intended for the deans, associate deans, and department heads of the School of Science and Engineering; faculty with overseas study or exchange experience; faculty planning to apply for international science-cooperation projects of the Ministry of Science and Technology or the provincial department; leaders of active national or provincial international science-cooperation projects; and faculty recruited within the past three years. Faculty and students across the university are welcome to attend.
