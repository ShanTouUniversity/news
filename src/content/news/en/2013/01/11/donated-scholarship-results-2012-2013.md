Shantou University announced the results of its 2012–2013 enterprise- and individual-funded scholarship selection. Under the Provisional Measures for the Administration of Enterprise- and Individual-Funded Scholarships, the selection committee completed its review and the management committee approved a total of 144 student awardees.

| Award program | Students |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Senior-Student Award Program | 140 |
| Chenghai-Native Student Award Program | 2 |
| Hakka Student Award Program | 2 |

The university encouraged the awardees to continue making progress and to excel in their studies, work, sports, cultural activities, and social practice. It also called on students across the university to learn from the awardees and maintain high standards in their studies and activities.

To protect student personal information, this article does not reproduce names, student numbers, or other personal details from the original attachments.
