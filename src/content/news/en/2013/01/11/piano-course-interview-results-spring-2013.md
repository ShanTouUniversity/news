The STU Arts Education Center announced the interview results for the spring 2012–2013 piano course, taught by Dong Xuemin. Based on a count of the original attachment, 66 students passed the interview: 18 assigned to Sunday, 16 to Monday, 16 to Tuesday, and 16 to Wednesday.

To protect students’ names and student numbers, this article does not reproduce the personal list or identification numbers in the attachment. Relevant students may consult the original OA notice for their individual results.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Arts and Design and Arts Education Center)
>
> ⚠️ Consult the original OA attachment through the OA system for the interview-pass list; student names and student numbers are not reproduced on the news page.
