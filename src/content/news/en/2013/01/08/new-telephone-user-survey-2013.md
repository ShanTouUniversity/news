The STU Network and Information Center invited faculty and staff to provide feedback on the new telephones by completing an online questionnaire at <http://vote.stu.edu.cn/>. The survey takes approximately 10 minutes and closes on January 11, 2013.

The manufacturer will use the survey results to plan targeted improvements and training. Further notice will be issued when the training arrangements are available.
