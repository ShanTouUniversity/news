STU Infrastructure Office will renovate the Business School MBA Education Center in Room 206 of the Science and Technology Building. Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. has been commissioned to carry out the work. The awarded construction price is RMB 63,000, and construction is scheduled to begin on January 8, 2013, with completion by January 27.

Noise, vibration, and dust may occur during the work. People passing through the construction area should take care, must not enter the site without permission from the relevant university office and the contractor, and should avoid staying near the site where possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office, Resource Management Office)
