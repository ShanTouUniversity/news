It is reported that the Personnel Office and the Labor Union of Shantou University issued an obituary on January 8, 2013.

## Arrangements

The main obituary content is presented as an image in the original OA notice. Arrangements outside the image are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Wreaths | Units or individuals wishing to send wreaths should contact the Office for Retired Faculty and Staff (Tel. 82902660) or Baiheyuan (Tel. 88626402) before 16:00 on January 9 |
| Transportation (January 10) | 07:40 at the reception room entrance on campus, 07:50 at the Anju entrance, 08:00 at the Fourth Hospital entrance in Dajiao, 08:10 at the school bus stop in Baiheyuan |
| Issued by | Personnel Office, Labor Union |

Please refer to the OA image for the full obituary text.
