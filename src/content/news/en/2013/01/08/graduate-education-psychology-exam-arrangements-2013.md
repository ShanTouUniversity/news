STU Academic Affairs Office announced the final-exam arrangements for this semester’s graduate Education and Psychology courses. Both examinations will take place in Lecture Room 101 of Building E.

| Exam class | Course | Time | Chief examiner | Invigilators | Listed students | Additional graduate students |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 9659 | HED1830 Education | January 8, 2013, 4:00–6:00 p.m. | Ma Fengqi | Chen Xiaohong | 60 | 29 |
| 9665 | HED1811 Psychology | January 15, 2013, 4:00–6:00 p.m. | Chen Xiaohong | Wang Xueju and Xu Fei | 70 | 28 |

The Education exam is the fourth session on Tuesday of week 17, and the Psychology exam is the fourth session on Tuesday of week 18. The Academic Affairs Office asked the relevant graduate students to inform one another.
