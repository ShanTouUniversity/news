It is reported that Shantou University has completed the review of temporary hardship grants for undergraduates in the fall semester of the 2012-2013 academic year. On January 8, the Student Affairs Office released the list of recipients for public comment, with a total of 163 undergraduates receiving the grant.

The grant was administered in accordance with the Trial Measures of Shantou University for the Management of Temporary Hardship Grants for Undergraduates. Under the procedure, students applied individually, and their submissions were reviewed by the respective colleges (residential colleges) before final confirmation by the Student Affairs Office.

## Public Comment and Feedback

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number of recipients | 163 |
| Deadline for feedback | Before 5:00 pm, January 10 (Thursday) |
| Office handling comments | Student Aid Management Center, Student Affairs Office (Room 106, Administrative Building) |
| Method of feedback | Written form |

According to the announcement, any unit or individual with objections to the list may submit written comments to the Student Aid Management Center before the deadline. Comments from units must bear the unit's official seal, and comments from individuals must be signed with the commenter's real name to confirm responsibility.
