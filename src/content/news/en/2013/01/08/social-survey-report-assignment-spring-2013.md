Following its teaching plan, the STU Social Sciences Department asked all main-campus students taking *Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics* in spring 2013 to conduct a social survey during the winter break and write a report.

The survey forms part of the course’s social-practice component, and the report will count toward the course’s regular-assessment grade. The theme is “My Hometown, My Home,” with the specific subject chosen by each student.

### Report requirements

Reports should be approximately 3,000 Chinese characters and generally include the background of the topic, survey purpose, survey content, survey procedures and methods, and conclusions. Reports must be submitted to the course instructor by the end of April 2013. A template is provided in the original attachment, titled STU Social Sciences Department Social Survey Report.
