STU Student Affairs Office announced the collection arrangements for student train tickets for winter break 2013. Following the January 1 supplementary notice, all collection requests registered at the university on January 1 and 2 had been issued.

Students who registered for collection may retrieve their personal documents and train tickets during working hours on January 4 at the Residential Area Counselor Office in Building B Room 106. Personal collection details and order information are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
