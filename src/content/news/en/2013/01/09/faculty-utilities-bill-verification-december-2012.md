The STU Resource Management Office published faculty and staff water and electricity records for December 2012. The latest meter readings were taken on January 1 and 2, 2013, and the previous reading was taken on December 1, 2012.

Faculty and staff can use their campus-network credentials to log in to the STU utility-management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/>. Questions or objections may be checked in person at the Water and Electricity Center from January 9 through 14. The contact telephone is `2489`.

After the review period, charges without objections will be deducted by the Finance Office from January 2013 salary accounts. People without salary accounts must pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
