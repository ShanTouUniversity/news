To give research groups time to collect, organize, and summarize their materials, the STU Research Office issued a preliminary notice for 2013 science and technology award applications and invited interested groups to begin preparations early.

### Planned award programs

| Award program | Expected period |
| --- | --- |
| Shantou Science and Technology Awards | February–March; exact dates pending |
| Guangdong Science and Technology Awards | April–May |
| Higher-Education Outstanding Research Achievement Awards | July–August |

The main award categories are the Natural Science Award, Technology Invention Award, and Science and Technology Progress Award.

### Evaluation and registration

Research results must be evaluated before an award application is submitted. Evaluation methods include expert appraisal, acceptance, application assessment for authorized patents, academic-committee or peer evaluation, and industry approval documents. Applied technology results may use expert appraisal, covering new products, technologies, processes, materials, designs, and biological varieties. Typical materials include a research summary, technical summary, novelty-search report, application evidence, relevant project contracts, representative papers, and other supporting documents.

Basic-theory results may use academic-committee or peer evaluation. Typical materials include a research summary, technical summary, evidence of paper indexing and citations, relevant project completion or acceptance documents, representative papers, and representative papers citing the work. Results should be registered with the competent higher-level science and technology authority within one month after evaluation.
