It is reported that Shantou University's Research Office has made unified arrangements for the 2013 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) project applications, covering the schedule, eligibility limits, and submission methods. The university will forward all applications to NSFC on March 16.

## Application Schedule

| Date | Item |
| --- | --- |
| From January 8 | Offline applicants download the 2013 version of the proposal form; online applicants use existing accounts (contact the Research Office if not activated) |
| By February 25 | Colleges conduct formal review and expert evaluation, returning proposals to applicants for revision |
| February 27 | Colleges submit draft proposals (paper and electronic versions) to the Research Office |
| February 27 – March 8 | The Research Office conducts centralized formal review, checks over-quota applications, and gives feedback |
| March 8 – March 12 | Proposals returned for final revisions |
| March 13 | Colleges submit final proposals in duplicate (including one original) with electronic versions to the Research Office (individual submissions not accepted) |
| March 13 – March 15 | The Research Office re-checks, stamps, and uploads data |
| March 16 | The university forwards all proposals to NSFC |

## Eligibility Limits

According to the notice, the main limits for 2013 include:

- Starting from 2013, project leaders who received NSFC funding in the previous year (2012), excluding one-year projects, may not apply for the same type of project that year;
- An applicant may apply for only one project of the same type per year (principal investigators only);
- Personnel with senior professional titles may have no more than three ongoing-plus-applied projects in total across types such as General Programs, Key Programs, Major Programs, Major Research Plan projects, Joint Funds, and Young Scientists Fund projects;
- Young Scientists Fund, Excellent Young Scientists Fund, and National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars are each limited to one award per leader;
- Personnel without senior professional titles are limited to one project in total as applicant or leading project leader;
- Innovative Research Groups, the National Basic Science Talent Cultivation Fund, Joint Scholar Programs with overseas and Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan scholars, Tianyuan Fund for Mathematics, and international exchange programs are exempt from the three-project limit.

In addition, project team members may not be replaced after proposals are submitted on February 27; where replacement is truly necessary, a written report must be submitted to the Research Office before March 4 and approved.

## Submission Methods and Final Material Requirements

For this round, General Programs (excluding Youth-to-General continuous funding projects) and Young Scientists Fund projects use offline writing; applicants must download the latest templates after January 1, 2013 and delete all old template files. All other types, including Key Programs, Major Programs, Excellent Young Scientists Fund, and National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, use online writing.

Final materials require two paper originals consistent with the electronic version number (hand-signed) plus one electronic copy. Attachments such as postdoctoral commitment letters, expert recommendation letters, supervisor consent letters, and ethics review certificates must be provided as originals as required.

## Special Notes

The Research Office highlighted two points: first, starting from 2014, applicants who failed to obtain General Program funding for two consecutive years (2012 and 2013) will be suspended from applying for General Programs for one year; second, NSFC will continue strengthening content-comparison checks on proposals this year to prevent academic misconduct and duplicate funding.
