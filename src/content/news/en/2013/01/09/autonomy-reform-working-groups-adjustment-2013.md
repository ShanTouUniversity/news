Following personnel changes, Shantou University adjusted the personnel of the working bodies for its comprehensive self-governance reform pilot after deliberation at the 16th university leadership meeting on December 26, 2012. The arrangement was issued as Document Shanda〔2013〕No. 4.

### Leading group

The leading group for the comprehensive self-governance reform pilot is headed by Xu Xiaohu and Song *Zhen, with Gu Peihua as deputy head. Its members are Luo Nianchao, Wen Xiaobo, Li Dan, Lin Danming, Wulanhasi, Shi Ganggang, and Huang Dongyang. Part of Song *Zhen’s name is garbled in the archived source and has not been reconstructed speculatively.
