As the year-end period approached, the STU Security Office reported that, according to a local police briefing, burglaries and other incidents around the campus had increased. The university security team strengthened its prevention and response work and reminded faculty, staff, and students to remain alert.

Before leaving campus or resting at night, residents should check that doors and windows are secure and that water, electricity, and gas valves are closed. Vehicles should be secured with anti-theft locks when parked. Anyone who notices an unusual situation should contact campus security or call the police.
