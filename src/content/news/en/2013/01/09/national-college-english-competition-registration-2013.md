STU will participate in the 2013 National College English Competition, which consists of preliminary and final rounds. The preliminary round will take place at STU from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. on Sunday, April 21, 2013. The final will take place in Guangzhou from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. on Sunday, May 12.

Students may register voluntarily for one of three categories: Category A for graduate students, Category B for English majors, and Category C for non-English majors. Both rounds carry a maximum of 150 points, including 90 points for subjective questions, 60 for objective questions, and 30 for listening.

The competition offers four award levels: special prize, first prize, second prize, and third prize. The second and third prizes are determined through the preliminary round, while the special and first prizes are determined through the final by the provincial-level competition organizers based on final-round scores.

### Registration and payment

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Registration period | January 9–February 20, 2013 |
| Registration fee | RMB 20 per student |
| Undergraduate students | Log in to the credit-based education website and complete the qualification-exam registration process |
| Graduate students | Register at the Graduate School Student Affairs Office |
| Automatic payment | The fee will be deducted from the registrant’s campus-card account on February 21 |

Registrants must keep sufficient funds in their campus-card accounts during the deduction period. Registration will fail if the deduction is unsuccessful.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
