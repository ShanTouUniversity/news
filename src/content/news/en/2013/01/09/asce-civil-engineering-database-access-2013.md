STU Library opened access to the database of the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE), inviting faculty, staff, and students to use the resource.

Founded in 1852, ASCE is a major publisher of civil-engineering information and knowledge. The notice states that ASCE’s 2012 publications included 33 professional journals, proceedings, books, committee reports, practice manuals, standards, and monographs. Its ASCE Online Proceedings collection contains more than 320 volumes of international civil-engineering conference literature, offering research on emerging and frontier technologies for civil-engineering practice and scholarship.

The database covers aerospace, architectural design, coastal and ocean engineering, construction, energy, engineering mechanics, environmental engineering, geotechnical engineering, hydraulics, infrastructure, materials, engineering management, professional issues, structural engineering, transportation, urban planning, water resources, and computing in civil engineering.

Faculty, staff, and students can access the resource through the library database detail page: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=323>.
