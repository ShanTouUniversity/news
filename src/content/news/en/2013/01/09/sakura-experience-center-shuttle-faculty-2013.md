The STU University Union shared a faculty and staff service arrangement: Sakura Company and Sakura Experience Center will provide a shuttle from the university Union entrance at 1:00 p.m. on Sunday, January 13, 2013. The shuttle will take interested faculty and staff to the experience center to view and order products.

Participation is voluntary.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Union)
