The Shantou University Youth League Committee and Student Associations Federation announced the assessment results of the 2012 student entrepreneurship-practice program. During the 2012 summer vacation, students worked at university-affiliated entrepreneurship-practice bases in production, operations, management, and marketing roles, gaining professional experience and testing their academic preparation.

Based on the performance of 51 participating students and feedback from the practice bases, the Youth League Committee awarded seven students an “excellent” rating. One student who made an outstanding contribution to the program received the title of Outstanding Individual in Student Entrepreneurship Practice.

The program aims to broaden students’ abilities through practical experience. The university Youth League Committee called on students to learn from the recognized participants and support continued development of the program.
