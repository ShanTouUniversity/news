Shantou University’s Research Office published issue 4 of its 2012 *Research and Innovation Newsletter* (No. 26 overall), reviewing research projects, awards, academic exchange, local services, and technology cooperation during the year.

### Projects and achievements

The university’s total research funding reached RMB 81.35 million in 2012. In the natural sciences, STU received approval for 60 National Natural Science Foundation of China projects with RMB 29.301 million in funding, and 27 Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects with RMB 3.34 million. In the humanities and social sciences, faculty members received 59 research projects, including 19 at the provincial or ministerial level and above, as well as three annual National Social Science Fund projects.

The university’s research awards included three third prizes in the Guangdong Science and Technology Awards, one Ministry of Education higher-education natural-science award, 14 Shantou Science and Technology Awards, one Ministry of Education award for outstanding humanities and social-science research, and 17 Shantou philosophy and social-science achievement awards.

### Teaching resources and academic exchange

STU received three awards at the 2012 Guangdong Higher-Education Educational Technology Annual Conference. Xie Lin, an associate professor at the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, received a second prize in the article category of the 8th China Federation of Literary and Art Circles Literary Criticism Awards. The university’s Analysis and Testing Center passed a special supervision inspection commissioned by the national accreditation authorities. Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, was appointed a preliminary judge for the China Design Exhibition.

The newsletter also reported lectures and exchanges with scholars from Kiel University, the University of California, Irvine, and Huazhong University of Science and Technology, along with an international symposium hosted by the College of Liberal Arts International Studies Center and a biomedical-engineering exchange between Jinan University and STU.

### Local service and cooperation

STU signed a technology cooperation agreement with the Jieyang Oceanic and Fisheries Bureau covering marine biodiversity conservation, cultivation of new marine species, and marine and island environmental protection. Professors Peng Wenhao and Deng Jianguang of the School of Law were appointed members of Shantou’s first Legal Consultation Expert Committee. The School of Law also organized an outdoor legal consultation activity for local residents.
