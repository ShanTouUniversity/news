To enrich the winter-break experience of students remaining on campus and help them plan university life and explore future careers, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office and Communist Youth League Committee will hold a forum titled “Spring Festival Conversation and Future Outlook.”

The forum will take place from **3:00 to 4:30 p.m. on January 30, 2013**, in the activity room on the first floor of Student Residence D. Chen Zijie, operations director of the e-commerce system of Qiya Group and of Yasu Information Technology Co., Ltd., will join the discussion as guest speaker.

Students interested in attending may email `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn` by **January 27**. Personal information requested for registration is not reproduced on this news page.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office and Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
