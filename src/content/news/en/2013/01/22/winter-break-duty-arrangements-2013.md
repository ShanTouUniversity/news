Shantou University’s General Office announced duty arrangements for administrative, teaching-support, and logistics staff during the 2013 winter break. Relevant units are required to follow university rules and maintain timely services for students and employees.

The schedule covers January 23 through February 20, 2013, and includes the General Office, International Exchange and Cooperation Office, Alumni Affairs Office, Teaching Office, Library, Graduate School, schools and colleges, Student Affairs Office, Research Office, Zhicheng College, Sports Department, Network and Information Center, Finance Office, and Resource Management Office, among other administrative, teaching, and service units.

During the Spring Festival period, the General Office, Resource Management Office, Student Affairs Office, schools and colleges, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and D, E, and G teaching buildings were also required to arrange duty coverage.
