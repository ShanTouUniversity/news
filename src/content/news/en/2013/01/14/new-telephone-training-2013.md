To help faculty and staff become familiar with new telephone equipment, the Shantou University Network and Information Center invited the equipment provider to conduct user training. The sessions will take place in E Staircase Room 201 on January 16, 2013: the first from 9:30 a.m. to noon and the second from 3:00 to 5:30 p.m. Participants should bring their campus cards to sign in.

Each unit must designate a telephone-use coordinator and email the coordinator’s name to `stunic@stu.edu.cn` by noon on Tuesday, January 15. For questions, call `86503400`, extension line 1, or email the same address.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
