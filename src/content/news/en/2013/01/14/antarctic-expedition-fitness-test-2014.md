With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Shantou University planned to carry out an Antarctic expedition project in 2014, with selection beginning in the spring 2013 semester. The process will include an Antarctic expedition proposal competition, preliminary judging, a fitness test, a training camp in Nan’ao, and final judging. It will ultimately select 16 students and four faculty members for the project team.

To help faculty and students begin targeted winter-break training, the university announced arrangements for the fitness test. The notice says that the same requirements apply to students and faculty and that the test is tentatively scheduled for late April 2013. Specific test items and standards are provided in an image embedded in the original notice.

Questions may be sent to the Student Affairs Office Outdoor Development Office at `expedition@stu.edu.cn`.
