The China Law Society opened a public call for topic suggestions for its 2013 ministerial-level legal-research projects. STU’s Research Office forwarded the announcement and invited university units and faculty members to complete the *2013 China Law Society Ministerial-Level Legal-Research Topic Suggestion Form*.

### Topic focus and requirements

Suggestions should support study and implementation of the spirit of the 18th National Party Congress and the broader work of the Party and the state. They should address major legal questions and current challenges with broad, strategic, or forward-looking significance, and provide theoretical and policy support for advancing the rule of law and building a socialist country under the rule of law. Suggestions should be specific, practical, and effective, and should avoid duplicating already approved project topics. Unit-submitted suggestions must bear the official seal; individuals should draw on their own research expertise.

The Research Department of the China Law Society will organize expert review of the suggestions, establish a research-topic guide, and publish an open call for project bids after approval by the society.

### Submission schedule

| Submission route | Deadline and method |
| --- | --- |
| STU-coordinated submission | Email the completed electronic form to `ymhuang@stu.edu.cn` by March 13, 2013 |
| Direct individual submission to the China Law Society | Submit by mail, fax, or email by March 15, 2013 |

Individuals may submit the form directly according to the announcement. Email subject lines to the China Law Society should use the format “Discipline (Topic),” for example, “Criminal Law (Empirical Study of Bribery Cases).”
