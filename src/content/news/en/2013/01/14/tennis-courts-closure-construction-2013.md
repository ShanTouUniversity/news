Under a construction notice issued by the Infrastructure Office, Shantou University’s tennis courts and surrounding fencing, lighting, seating, and related facilities closed for renovation from January 14, 2013. The courts, including the student courts, are temporarily unavailable to the campus community.

The Sports Department asked faculty and students to note the closure. The original notice does not provide a construction completion date or a date for reopening.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
