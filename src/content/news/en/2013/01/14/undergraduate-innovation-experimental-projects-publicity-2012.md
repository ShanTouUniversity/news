Shantou University publicized the proposed project approvals for its 2012 undergraduate innovation-experiment program. The program opened for applications and evaluation in November 2012. After project-team applications, school-level recommendations, and university review, 76 projects were proposed for approval.

| Project category | Proposed projects |
| --- | ---: |
| Proposed for provincial recommendation | 43 |
| University-level projects | 33 |
| Total | 76 |

Examples named in the public notice include “Small Underwater Mineral-Prospecting Robot” and “Fractal Structure of the Generalized Sierpinski Carpet.” The complete project list and team information are provided in the Academic Affairs Office attachments. To protect student names and student numbers, this article does not reproduce team rosters.

The publicity period runs from January 14 through 17, 2013. Objections to the project approvals may be submitted orally or in writing to the Academic Affairs Office. The telephone number is `86502736`; the contact person is Yang Weitao, and the email address is `wtyang@stu.edu.cn`.
