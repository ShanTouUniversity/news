It is reported that the Center for Women’s Studies, College of Liberal Arts, will hold a sharing session on the Taiwan study tour for women’s oral history on January 14, 2013, in Room 205 of the Science Lecture Hall.

The session is part of the Center’s Women’s Oral History Publication Project, which aims to promote research methods on women’s oral history and related public welfare culture and will publish a collection of research by STU faculty and students. Previous activities included public lectures, methodology workshops and seminars on gender, culture and charity in the Chaoshan context. From November 16 to 20, 2012, six faculty and student representatives completed the study tour in Taiwan.

## Session Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00–17:00, January 14, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 205, Science Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Center for Women’s Studies, College of Liberal Arts |
| Theme | Sharing of the Taiwan study tour on women’s oral history |

Presentations will cover the overall study tour and six special topics: Prof. Hou Kunhong on interviews with Buddhist women practitioners in Taiwan, Prof. Song Jinxiu on fieldwork and fetal culture, Prof. Xu Xueji on dialogues with female doctors, Prof. Shen Huaiyu and Prof. Chen Jinjin on oral history research techniques, and Prof. Xie Guoxing on Taiwan corporate history, as well as Prof. You Jianming’s comments on related works. Remarks will follow from Prof. Fang Lian and Ms. Ke Qianting of Sun Yat-sen University, followed by an interactive Q&A. Interested faculty and students are welcome to attend.
