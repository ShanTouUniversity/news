According to the schedule of the provincial education authorities, applications for the 2013 provincial quality-engineering projects will open soon. The STU Academic Affairs Office will hold a mobilization meeting on January 16 to explain application schedules, development requirements, and precautions for the various project categories.

### Meeting details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | January 16, 2013, 3:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Science and Technology Center Room 205 |
| Contact | Yang Weitao |
| Telephone | `86502736` |

The year’s provincial quality-engineering categories include pilot comprehensive major reform projects, experimental teaching demonstration centers, off-campus undergraduate practice-teaching bases, higher-education teaching-reform projects, high-quality open courses including high-quality resource-sharing courses and open video courses, undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects, pilot colleges, and teacher teaching-development centers.

The meeting is intended for school leaders responsible for teaching and relevant staff, as well as prospective applicants for 2013 provincial quality-engineering projects. Other interested faculty members are also welcome.
