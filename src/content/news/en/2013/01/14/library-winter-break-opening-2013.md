Following the university’s winter-break schedule, the Shantou University Library announced its opening arrangements for the break.

### Opening hours

During the administrative and service-support units’ staff-rotation period, from January 23 through February 20, 2013, the library will open on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The specific opening dates are January 23, 25, 28, and 30, and February 1, 4, 6, 8, 18, and 20.

During the student winter break, from January 20 through February 23, the special-collections area, reading hall, and seminar rooms will be temporarily closed to students. The library will resume normal opening from February 21.
