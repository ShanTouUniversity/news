Shantou University announced the first batch of student entrepreneurship projects approved for 2013 funding. Eleven student ventures passed review by the Entrepreneurship Fund Funding Application Review Committee and were approved by the Entrepreneurship Park Management Committee.

### Project names

1. Mobile Internet Applications and Games Provider
2. Machine-Vision Automation Service Solutions
3. E掌通 Enterprise Mobile Service Platform
4. Shantou Chaoyun Network Technology Co., Ltd.
5. MT-Viewer Mobile-Terminal Network Video Conferencing System
6. Shantou Baizhuo Cultural and Creative Team
7. Series of Mobile-Terminal Applications Based on a Semi-Closed Model
8. Red Square Studio
9. STU Ji-Nian Team Souvenir Project
10. Pelican Social-Entrepreneurship Website
11. Xueduofen Interactive Teaching Platform

To protect student personal information, this article does not reproduce the project leaders’ names, student numbers, or other personal details from the original notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Entrepreneurship Park Management Office and Student Entrepreneurship Center)
