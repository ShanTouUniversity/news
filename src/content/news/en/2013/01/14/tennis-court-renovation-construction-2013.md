With the relevant approvals, the STU Infrastructure Office arranged renovation of the tennis courts and surrounding fencing, lighting, and seating. Guangzhou Shili Sports Equipment Co., Ltd. was commissioned to carry out the work. The winning contract value was RMB 490,200, with work scheduled to begin on January 14, 2013, and finish by February 24.

The construction could generate noise, vibration, and dust. The university asked pedestrians to exercise caution, prohibited entry to the construction site without permission from the university’s relevant offices and the contractor, and asked people to avoid lingering near the site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office, Resource Management Office)
