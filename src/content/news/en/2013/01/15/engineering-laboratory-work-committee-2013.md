Following discussion and approval by its school affairs meeting, the Shantou University Engineering School established a laboratory work committee. The committee will support teaching reform, strengthen and improve laboratory work, promote standardized management and reform of experimental teaching, and guide laboratory development and management.

### Committee members

| Role | Member(s) |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Bao Nengsheng |
| Deputy chair | Chen Shaoke |
| Members | Fang Ruoyu, Chen Shaobo, Chen Liqiang, Chen Zheng, and Xiong Guangjing |
| Secretary | Huang Yixiong |

The members are listed in order of the number of strokes in their surnames. The establishing document is Shanda Engineering〔2013〕No. 1, copied to the Academic Affairs Office.
