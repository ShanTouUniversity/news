To conserve electricity, the Shantou University Library reading hall will be closed during the student winter break, from January 20 through February 23, 2013. The library asked readers to remove personal belongings stored in the reading hall by January 20.

The library will clear items left behind, place them in boxes, and store them in the printing rooms on the second and third floors until March 5. Readers may claim their belongings in those areas during library opening hours from January 21 through March 5. Items still unclaimed after that period will be discarded as unclaimed property.
