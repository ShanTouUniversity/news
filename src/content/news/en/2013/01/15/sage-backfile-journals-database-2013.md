Shantou University Library opened access to the SAGE journal backfile database, giving faculty and students access to historical full-text journal resources.

### Database coverage

- Full text from 381 high-quality academic journals;
- Coverage from 1998 back to Volume 1, Issue 1 of each journal;
- More than 35,000 volumes, 476,000 articles, and 5.2 million pages;
- Subject coverage including education, psychology, sociology and social work, law and criminology, economics and business management, tourism and hospitality, communications and news media, politics and international relations, environment and geography, urban planning and studies, music, history, philosophy, materials science, mechanical engineering, control and automation, food science, life sciences, pharmacology and toxicology, public health, and clinical medicine.

Access the [SAGE journal backfile database](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=344). The original notice recommended using Internet Explorer and opening the link in a new window.
