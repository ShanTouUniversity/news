It is reported that the Research Office forwarded a notice on January 15, 2013, regarding training for the 2013 National Natural Science Foundation of China (Guangdong region) applications.

Organized by the Department of Science and Technology of Guangdong Province and the Office of the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong Province, the training will help applicants prepare for the 2013 call.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Training for 2013 NSFC Projects (Guangdong region) |
| Time | 09:00–11:00, January 18, 2013 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, 3rd Floor, Auditorium, Guangdong University of Technology (No. 100 Waihuan West Road, Higher Education Mega Center, Panyu, Guangzhou; on the right side of the main gate) |
| Organizers | Department of Science and Technology of Guangdong Province, Office of the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong Province |
| Issued by | Research Office |

Interested teachers may attend on their own. Materials are available in the OA attachment.
