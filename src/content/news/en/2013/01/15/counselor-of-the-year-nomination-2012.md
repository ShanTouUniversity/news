Following Document Yuejiaosi〔2013〕No. 1 from the Guangdong Education Department, Shantou University opened nominations for candidates for the 2012 National University Counselor of the Year selection. The call is for full-time counselors who are formally employed and work directly in schools or departments on students’ day-to-day ideological and political education. Counselors who have received a National University Counselor of the Year title or nomination award in a previous year are not eligible.

Candidates should be committed to counselor work, care for students, maintain high ethical and professional standards, and develop student work in line with the patterns of student ideological education and personal development. They should have achieved notable results in 2012 in carrying out counselor responsibilities, innovating student services, and supporting student growth, and should have received a university-level or higher award.

### Nomination process

Schools and colleges should organize applications and email the electronic application form, a statement of achievements of no more than 3,000 Chinese characters, and one ID photo and one informal photo to `lslin@stu.edu.cn` by 5:00 p.m. on February 15, 2013. The statement should contain a personal résumé, work record, and awards received.

The Student Affairs Office will conduct the university selection and submit the result for approval by the university Party Committee before reporting the candidate to the Guangdong Education Department. The provincial department will organize an expert panel to select 40 counselors from those reported by universities, publicize them on the Nan Yue Counselors Online website, and recommend them for the national selection.
