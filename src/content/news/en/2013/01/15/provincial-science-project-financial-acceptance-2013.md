The Guangdong Department of Science and Technology issued a notice requiring financial acceptance for all provincial science and technology projects from January 1, 2013. The STU Research Office forwarded the notice and asked relevant units and faculty to follow it.

### Acceptance requirements

| Project award amount | Financial-acceptance requirement |
| --- | --- |
| RMB 200,000 or more | An accounting firm must issue a project-completion financial-acceptance audit report |
| Less than RMB 200,000 | The host institution must prepare a project-completion financial-acceptance final funding statement |

The provincial department established standard formats for the *Audit Report for Financial Acceptance at Completion of Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Projects* and the *Final Funding Statement for Financial Acceptance at Completion of Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Projects*. The audit-report template covers the project profile, budgets and execution of dedicated and self-raised funds, problems and recommendations concerning fund management and use, and the audit opinion. The final-statement template is used to report project-funding settlement in the prescribed format.

Questions about implementation should be directed to the Research Conditions and Finance Office of the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology. The notice is Document Yuekehan Caizi〔2012〕No. 1836, issued by the provincial department on December 29, 2012.
