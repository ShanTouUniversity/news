It is reported that, in order to strengthen communication between the Academic Affairs Office and academic administrative staff across teaching units and to further implement its "Advanced Undergraduate Education" initiative, Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office plans to hold a symposium for academic secretaries (teaching secretaries) of all teaching units on the main campus on the afternoon of Friday, January 18.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Time | January 18, 2013 (Friday), 15:30–17:20 |
| Venue | Multipurpose Hall 330, New Administrative Center |
| Participants | Academic secretaries (teaching secretaries) of all teaching units on the main campus |
| Organizer | Academic Affairs Office |

According to the office, the symposium aims to explore ways to improve the quality of teaching management services so as to better support the university's teaching reform with high-level service. All participating academic secretaries have been asked to attend on time.
