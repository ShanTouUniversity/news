It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has opened applications for the Student Startup Park to teams that received funding from the 2013 Student Entrepreneurship Fund. Interested teams should submit materials in line with the STU Student Entrepreneurship Fund Management Measures (Trial) and the Interim Measures for the Management of the STU Student Startup Park.

## Application Details

Eligible teams need to complete the STU Startup Park Application Form and deliver it to the Student Entrepreneurship Center (Room 106, New Administration Building) before 12:00 noon on January 16, 2013.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Teams funded by the 2013 Student Entrepreneurship Fund |
| Required material | STU Startup Park Application Form |
| Deadline | 12:00 noon, January 16, 2013 |
| Submission venue | Student Entrepreneurship Center (Room 106, New Administration Building) |

The application form template is attached to the original notice, and teams may contact the Student Entrepreneurship Center for further information.

> ⚠️ The application form attachment is available on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
