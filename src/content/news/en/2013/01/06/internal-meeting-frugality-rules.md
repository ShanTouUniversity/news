It is reported that the Administrative Office and the Discipline Inspection Office of Shantou University have jointly issued a notice reaffirming the rules for internal university meetings, urging all departments to continue scheduling work meetings in line with frugality requirements.

The move came after a front-page report in People's Daily on January 5, 2013, revealing that more than 80 officials from Hongshan Subdistrict in Wuxi, Jiangsu Province, flew to Xiamen for a four-day "brainstorming retreat" during which they spent less than five hours in actual meetings — a violation of the central government's "Eight-Point Regulations." According to the report, the Wuxi municipal authorities suspended Zhou Rong, Party secretary of the subdistrict, from duty pending investigation, and ruled that apart from the venue rental fee in Xiamen, members of the leadership team must personally bear all their own travel expenses.

## Meeting Arrangement Requirements

University leaders took the incident seriously and asked all departments to draw lessons from it. The notice sets out two clear requirements:

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Venue | Meetings convened by campus units for work purposes should be held on campus wherever possible |
| Off-campus meetings | Internal meetings held off-campus for well-founded reasons should generally not go beyond the eastern Guangdong region and require prior approval from the responsible university leader |

The notice also stresses that department heads organizing off-campus meetings bear leadership responsibility for personnel safety and related expenses.

In fact, provincial and municipal governments have repeatedly stressed frugality and the control of meeting expenditure in recent years, and the university's finance and audit departments had already issued a document regulating internal meetings on March 27, 2012.
