The ninth STU Challenge Cup Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Competition is scheduled for early April 2013. The Communist Youth League Committee issued preparations for the event.

Each college must complete its final review of 2012 student extracurricular academic and scientific projects by March 11. Printed copies of the summary report, benefit-analysis report, project summary table, and other materials must bear the official seal and be submitted to the University Youth League Committee Office. Electronic copies should be sent to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`.

Teams that received an advancement qualification in the eighth Challenge Cup competition should use the winter break to improve their projects. These projects will join the ninth Challenge Cup entries for the defense and completion review of the improved work.
