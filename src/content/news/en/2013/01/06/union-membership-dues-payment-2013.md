The STU Union arranged collection of 2013 membership dues. Union members should pay their dues to their departmental union before the winter break. Each departmental union will consolidate the payments and submit them to the university union, together with printed and electronic copies of the Membership-Dues Detail Sheet.

Electronic detail sheets should be sent to the university union at `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.

The original attachment is a membership-dues detail sheet. Individual names and payment amounts are not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Union)
>
> ⚠️ The membership-dues detail sheet contains personal information. Consult the original OA attachment through the OA system.
