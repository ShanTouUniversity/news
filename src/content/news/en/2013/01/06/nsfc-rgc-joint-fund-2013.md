It is reported that under the agreement establishing the Joint Research Scheme between the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) and the Research Grants Council of Hong Kong (RGC), the two bodies co-fund collaborative research projects between researchers in mainland China and Hong Kong each year. The 2013 call for proposals has now opened.

## Program Overview

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Priority areas | Information science, biological science, new materials science, marine and environmental science, medical science, management science |
| Duration | 4 years |
| Mainland funding | Up to 900,000 yuan per project |
| Number of projects | About 23 |
| Hong Kong funding cap | HK$1.125 million per project starting from 2013 |

The scheme supports research plans in natural sciences jointly proposed by mainland and Hong Kong researchers, managed under NSFC's regulations for international (regional) cooperative research projects. Funds from each side are, in principle, restricted to local use. Mainland funding may cover research expenses, hosting costs for Hong Kong collaborators visiting the mainland, round-trip travel for mainland personnel to Hong Kong, and other NSFC-approved expenditures.

## Eligibility and Limits

Applicants must be formally employed by a mainland research institution with an appointment covering the project period, hold a senior professional title, and be currently leading or have previously led an NSFC project of three years or longer. Cross-institutional and cross-disciplinary applications between mainland and Hong Kong researchers are allowed. Researchers from outside the two regions may join as project team members on either side, and Hong Kong partners must be full-time researchers at public universities.

As an international cooperative research program, the scheme limits each senior-title researcher to applying for or participating in no more than one such project per year. Leaders and senior-title participants of ongoing international cooperative projects may not apply again. The general limit of three projects for senior-title holders does not apply to this scheme.

## Application Arrangements

Applicants must submit a preliminary application form through the Science Fund online information system by selecting the NSFC-RGC form under "International Cooperation Pre-application," then confirm submission and notify the Research Office by phone for review. The form must be completed by **January 18, 2013**. Titles on both sides' forms and the names of both applicants must match exactly, and the research plan should demonstrate complementary strengths. Hong Kong applicants should follow the RGC deadline.

The evaluation process includes preliminary review, formal proposal submission, peer review by both agencies, and a joint final review, with results released jointly. Faculty may contact Hu Yunxiang or Zhu Changsheng at the Research Office (tel: 82902813) for inquiries.
