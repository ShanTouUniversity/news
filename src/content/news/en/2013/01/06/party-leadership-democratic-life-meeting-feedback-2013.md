The STU Party Committee will hold a democratic life meeting for university-level Party-member leaders on Wednesday, January 16, 2013. In accordance with relevant policy documents, the Organization and United Front Work Department is soliciting comments and suggestions from all university units, faculty, staff, and students regarding the university leadership team and its members.

Comments and suggestions should be sent to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` by January 15.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
