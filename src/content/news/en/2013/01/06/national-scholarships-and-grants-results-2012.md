STU Student Affairs Office announced the recipients of the 2011–2012 National Scholarship and National Inspirational Scholarship and the beneficiaries of the 2012 National Student Grant. The three programs together accounted for 1,610 awards and grants.

Under relevant Ministry of Education and Guangdong Provincial Department of Education announcements, 19 STU students received the 2011–2012 National Scholarship, with RMB 8,000 per recipient and a certificate of honor. Another 291 students received the National Inspirational Scholarship, worth RMB 5,000 per recipient. Following nominations by colleges and residential colleges, university review and public display, and approval by the provincial education department, 1,300 students received the 2012 National Student Grant, worth RMB 3,000 per recipient.

| Program | Recipients | Standard amount |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| National Scholarship | 19 | RMB 8,000 per recipient, plus a certificate of honor |
| National Inspirational Scholarship | 291 | RMB 5,000 per recipient |
| National Student Grant | 1,300 | RMB 3,000 per recipient |

The detailed award and beneficiary lists were released as attachments to the notice. Student names and other personal information are not reproduced.
