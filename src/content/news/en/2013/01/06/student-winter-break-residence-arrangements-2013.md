STU announced arrangements for students remaining on campus during the 2013 winter break. Under the university holiday schedule, the winter break runs from January 20 through February 23, 2013. Students who need to remain on campus for academic research, public-service activities, graduate-entrance examination preparation, or other necessary reasons must apply online through the Student Housing Management System at <http://dorm.stu.edu.cn/> by January 20. The application requires the residence period, telephone number, and reason for staying.

### Utilities and hot water

Students whose residence applications are approved are responsible for their dormitory utility charges and will not receive the holiday utility subsidy. Charges will be calculated in two periods: January 24 to February 7 and February 8 to February 21. A stay covering one period will be charged for that period; a stay spanning both periods will be charged for the full winter-break period. Roommates should agree on how to share and pay charges according to actual use.

Dormitories without an application or with an unapproved application will have electricity cut off on January 23, with power restored to all dormitories on February 22. Students who leave early after receiving approval, where no other resident will remain, should register for a power shutoff at the Student Community Management and Service Center.

Hot-water service for undergraduate dormitories will be extended through January 25. From January 20 through 25, the service hours will be 5:00–7:00 p.m.; normal service will resume on February 22. The university will also set up temporary hot-water points during the winter break, with details to be announced separately.

### Safety and access

Students remaining on campus should close doors and windows and turn off water and electricity when leaving their rooms. Any unusual or emergency situation should be reported to the building attendant or university security. Residents must follow the STU Student Dormitory Regulations, cooperate with housing staff, and not accommodate outside visitors. Students without approved residence applications will lose access authorization. Approved residents must carry their campus cards and swipe them when entering or leaving the residence area.
