The STU Research Office opened applications for the 2013 National Natural Science Foundation of China international or regional cooperation and exchange projects. The program supports cooperation between Chinese and foreign researchers through information exchange, personnel exchanges, and joint experiments. Funding focuses on personnel-exchange costs, international travel for Chinese researchers conducting cooperative work abroad, and living expenses in China and related domestic travel for foreign collaborators visiting China.

Applicants must be principal investigators or key participants on active National Natural Science Foundation projects lasting at least three years. The cooperation project may not extend beyond the 2013 funding year, and requested funding is RMB 30,000–50,000. Application materials must be submitted at least three months before the start of the cooperation project’s execution period.

### Application process

Project leaders should sign in to the NSFC management system at <http://isis.nsfc.gov.cn/portal/index.asp> with their NSFC account and password. Select “International Cooperation Project Application,” then “Apply for an International Cooperation Project,” “Cooperation and Exchange Project,” and “Cooperation and Exchange Project (Non-inter-organizational Agreement).” Applicants should select the active project providing the support, complete and submit the application, generate a PDF, and print it.

Applicants must also provide a signed cooperation agreement or other written confirmation from both parties. The documents should cover the invitation or confirmation and visit dates, the research topic and objectives, detailed information about both project leaders and key participants, the work plan and schedule, intellectual-property arrangements for research results, and the division of cooperation costs.

### Timing and materials

Applications are accepted year-round, except during the concentrated review period from February 15 through March 20. After submitting online, applicants should telephone the Research Office for review. Once approved, they must submit two printed copies of the system-generated PDF application, together with the cooperation agreement and invitation or confirmation letter.
