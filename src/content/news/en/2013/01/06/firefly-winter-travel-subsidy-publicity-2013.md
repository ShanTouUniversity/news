The STU Young Volunteers Firefly Love Foundation announced the review results for its “Love Accompanies the Journey Home” winter-travel subsidy program. The foundation received 11 applications and, after review by its council, proposed nine students as beneficiaries. The original plan had been to support 20 students.

Organizations or individuals with objections may submit comments to the University Youth League Committee in writing, in person, or by telephone by 3:00 p.m. on January 9, 2013. Organizations must affix their official seal, while individuals must provide their real names when submitting comments.
