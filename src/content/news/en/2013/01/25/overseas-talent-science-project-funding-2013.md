Shantou University’s Personnel Office forwarded the Guangdong Foreign Experts Affairs Bureau’s notice on applications for 2013 preferential funding for science and technology projects by overseas-trained personnel (Guangdong Foreign Experts Affairs Letter No. 4 [2013]). Relevant units and eligible faculty members were asked to prepare applications in accordance with the provincial notice.

Applicants must submit their materials to the Personnel Office, Room 315 of the New Administration Building, by **February 18, 2013**. The university contact is Fan Jibin, reachable at `0754-86502349` or `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
>
> ⚠️ Consult the original provincial attachment on the OA system for eligibility criteria, required materials, and funding rules.
