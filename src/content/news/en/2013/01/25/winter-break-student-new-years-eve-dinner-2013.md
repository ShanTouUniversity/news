With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, Shantou University will host a buffet-style New Year’s Eve dinner for students remaining on campus during winter break. Thirty-one students successfully reserved places.

The dinner will begin at **5:30 p.m. on February 9, 2013**, at the Western Restaurant on the first floor of Junhua Hotel. The university will provide transportation to the hotel. Participants must gather at the university motto monument at **4:20 p.m.** for boarding; they are responsible for transportation back to campus after the dinner. A campus card is required for card-based entry.

Students who cannot attend must email `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn` by **4:00 p.m. on January 29** to cancel their reservations. Students who fail to cancel on time and do not attend will be responsible for their individual meal costs.
