Shantou University’s Network and Information Center will upgrade and maintain the campus internet gateway firewall from **12:30 to 5:00 a.m. on January 26, 2013**. Email, website, and campus internet services may be briefly interrupted during the work, with the expected impact lasting approximately 20 minutes.

For assistance, contact the Network and Information Center at `0754-86503400` or email `stunic@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
