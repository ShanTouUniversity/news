Following deliberation at the second 2013 meeting of the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee, Qiu Weili was appointed vice president of the Affiliated Cancer Hospital of the Shantou University Medical College. The appointment carries a six-month probationary period.

The appointment document was issued by the Communist Party Committee of Shantou University and copied to the Organization Department of the Shantou Municipal Party Committee, the Shantou Municipal Human Resources Bureau, and the Shantou Municipal Health Bureau.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Party Committee of Shantou University)
