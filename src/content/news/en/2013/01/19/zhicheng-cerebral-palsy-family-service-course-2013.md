Zhicheng College at Shantou University will offer the “Family Education Service for Children with Cerebral Palsy” public-service course from March to May 2013. The course is designed to give students practical experience while developing communication, teamwork, and community-service skills. It is included in STU’s public-service-course program, and students who pass the assessment will receive one academic credit.

The course will admit 24 students from across the university. Service sessions will take place on Wednesday and Saturday afternoons from March 20 through May 15 in urban Shantou. Activities will use team teaching or one-to-one tutoring and include music, English, handicrafts, science outreach, psychological support, and related physical rehabilitation exercises. Students will also help parents carry out rehabilitation activities at home.

Applications will use an electronic form and a screening process. The deadline is **5:00 p.m. on February 27, 2013**. Applicants should follow the public-service-course rules, be willing to cooperate and serve others, and be prepared for sustained practical work. Application materials should be sent to `atp@stu.edu.cn`.
