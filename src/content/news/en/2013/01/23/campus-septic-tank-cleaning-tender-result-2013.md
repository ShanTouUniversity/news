Following a review by Shantou University’s tendering and bidding leadership group, the university announced the winning bid for its campus septic-tank cleaning project. Shantou Huanrong Cleaning Services Co., Ltd. won the contract at a price of **RMB 65,000**.

The public-notice period runs from **January 23 through January 25, 2013**. The project’s procuring office is the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University, which can be reached at `0754-86502490` by telephone or `0754-86502314` by fax. Complaints are handled by the university’s Supervision and Audit Office at `0754-86502346`, with fax service at `0754-86503663`.
