Following a review by Shantou University’s tendering and bidding leadership group, the university announced the winning contractors for its minor repair projects. The selected companies are Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd.; Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd.; Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd.; Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd.; Shantou Residential Construction Engineering Co., Ltd., selected for Project 2; Shantou Yueli Construction Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd.; and Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd.

The projects will be assigned through work orders, with a **10 percent** bidding reduction rate. The public-notice period runs from **January 23 through January 28, 2013**. The procuring office is the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University, reachable at `0754-86502490`; complaints are handled by the university’s Supervision and Audit Office at `0754-86502346`.
