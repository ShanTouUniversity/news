It is reported that the 2013 selection of the "STU Overseas Study Grant Program," funded by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, has begun, open to final-year undergraduates across all schools. Launched in 2007, the program aims to support academically outstanding students with commitment and potential to study at leading universities outside mainland China; applications are currently limited to undergraduates pursuing master's degrees abroad.

The program plans to award 20 students this year in three tiers — A, B and C — worth 80,000, 50,000 and 30,000 yuan per person respectively.

Applicants must be final-year STU undergraduates without a master's degree or above, possess strong English skills meeting the language requirements of overseas institutions, and demonstrate excellent academic performance. Selection considers academic results, foreign-language proficiency, the quality and academic standing of the target institution and program, as well as overall qualities such as leadership and participation in public affairs, community service and extracurricular activities.

Applicants must submit an online application on the university's scholarship website by 4 p.m. on March 8 and deliver written materials to the Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Building). Required materials include the online application form, an academic transcript confirmed by the school, two sealed reference letters (one from the dean), and an English essay of up to 500 words on personal goals and overseas study plans.

The university reminded students that they may apply to no more than three schools, which must be drawn from the candidate list, with names and programs written in full English without abbreviations; choices cannot be changed once submitted. Incomplete applications, whether missing the online form or written materials, will be deemed invalid.
