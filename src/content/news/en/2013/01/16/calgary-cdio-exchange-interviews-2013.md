The 2013 Shantou University–University of Calgary CDIO exchange program received 41 applications by January 15. Five applicants withdrew from the interview process, leaving 36 candidates to advance to interviews.

The interviews were held at 7:30 p.m. on January 16, 2013, in the third-floor meeting room of the Engineering School. Candidates were asked to check in at 7:20 p.m. in the third-floor Academic Exchange Room.

The interview used a leaderless-group format, with topics selected by drawing lots on site. The process consisted of an individual statement of no more than 30 seconds, a leaderless-group discussion of no more than 15 minutes, questions from the judges as appropriate, and the conclusion of the interview.

To protect student personal information, this article does not reproduce the interview list in the attachment.
