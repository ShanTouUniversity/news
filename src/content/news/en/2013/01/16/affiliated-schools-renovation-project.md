It is reported that, with relevant approvals in place, the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University will carry out renovation work on restrooms and other facilities at the university's affiliated middle school and primary school. The contractor is Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd.

## Project Overview

The renovation project was awarded at a cost of 270,900 yuan and is scheduled to begin on January 17, 2013, with completion expected before February 19, 2013.

| --- | --- |
| Scope | Renovation of restrooms and other facilities at affiliated schools |
| Contractor | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Award amount | 270,900 yuan |
| Start date | January 17, 2013 |
| Completion | Before February 19, 2013 |

## Safety Reminders

The Infrastructure Office noted that construction may generate noise, vibration and dust, and asked for understanding of any inconvenience. For safety reasons, pedestrians are advised to yield and stay alert near the site, and should not enter the construction area without permission from university departments and the contractor, nor linger nearby.
