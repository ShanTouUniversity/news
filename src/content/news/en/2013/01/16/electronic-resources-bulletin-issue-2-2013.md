The Shantou University Library issued Electronic Resources Bulletin No. 2 for 2013, the eighth issue overall, covering new and updated academic resources available to the university community.

### New databases

From 2013, STU formally opened access to Thomson Reuters’ Science Citation Index (SCI), Social Science Citation Index (SSCI), and Arts & Humanities Citation Index (A&HCI). The donated Index Chemicus (IC), Current Chemical Reactions (CCR), and Medline databases also became available. Access the [Web of Knowledge](http://webofknowledge.com). The library also provided links to product training, application tips, and online lectures.

The SAGE journal backfile database became available online from January 1, 2013. It includes full text from 381 journals, more than 35,000 volumes, 476,000 articles, and 5.2 million pages, covering the humanities and social sciences as well as engineering and medicine. Coverage extends from 1998 back to Volume 1, Issue 1 of each journal. Access [SAGE Online](http://online.sagepub.com/).

Access to American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) journals and proceedings also opened. Founded in 1852, ASCE served more than 130,000 professionals from 159 countries. Its 2012 publications included 33 professional journals, proceedings, books, committee reports, practice manuals, standards, and monographs. ASCE Online Proceedings included more than 320 volumes of international civil-engineering conference literature. Access the [ASCE Library](http://ascelibrary.org/).

### SpringerLink redesign

The redesigned SpringerLink platform improved page speed, mobile-device support, navigation, and search, and preselected content by subject relevance to help researchers browse electronic books and journals. The bulletin said pages could be delivered at approximately three times the previous speed and that the platform supported desktop computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

Personal SpringerLink accounts could not be migrated automatically. Users with accounts on the former platform would need to register again. The new address is [SpringerLink](http://link.springer.com/).
