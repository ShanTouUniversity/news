Following Document Yuezu〔2013〕No. 2 from the Guangdong Provincial Organization Department, Shantou University organized the centralized reporting of 2013 personal affairs by leaders at department level and above. Leaders at deputy department level and above should collect the *Leadership Personal-Affairs Report Form* from the Organization and United Front Work Office in Room 315 of the Administration Center and complete every item under the first-report requirements.

The form must be completed by hand, sealed and signed in a C4 envelope, and returned to the Organization and United Front Work Office by February 22, 2013. Personnel meeting specified reporting conditions must also complete the relevant supplementary form.

To protect the privacy of cadres and their family members, this article does not reproduce the forms, reporting fields, or any personal-affairs information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office of the Party Committee)
