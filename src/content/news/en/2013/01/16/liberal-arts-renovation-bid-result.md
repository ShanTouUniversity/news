It is reported that the bidding result for the renovation project of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University has entered its public notice stage, with Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. named as the winning contractor based on the evaluation and recommendation of the university's tendering leadership group.

## Bid Result

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Project | STU College of Liberal Arts Renovation |
| Winning Contractor | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Contract Amount | RMB 247,009.46 |

The public notice period runs from **January 16 to January 18, 2013**. Any objection to the result may be filed with Shantou University's Discipline Inspection and Audit Office at (0754) 8650-2346.

The tendering party is the STU Infrastructure Office, reachable at (0754) 8650-2490.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
