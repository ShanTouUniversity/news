Following a recommendation from the Academic Affairs Office and discussion by the Changjiang School of Arts and Design leadership, the school established a laboratory work committee. The committee will strengthen and improve laboratory operations, promote standardized management and reform of experimental teaching, and guide laboratory development and management.

### Committee members

| Role | Member(s) |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Han Ran |
| Members | Li Aiyi, Zhuang Wei, Chen Qisheng, and Chen Rui |
| Secretary | Pan Aiting |
