The Shantou University Business School opened recruitment for its 2013 business-management summer course with McGill University’s Desautels Faculty of Management in Canada. The program is scheduled for July 2–26 at McGill and is open to outstanding undergraduates and graduate students, including working graduate students and MBA students. It primarily targets business students, while interested students from other majors may also apply.

Taught in English by professors from McGill’s Desautels Faculty of Management, the course includes modules on “The Art of North American Management” and “Global Brand Strategy,” along with extracurricular activities and local visits. Students who complete the program will receive six credits. Tuition is CAD 7,500.

### Requirements and process

Applicants must be enrolled undergraduates or graduate students with a foundation in economics, strong academic performance with no failing courses, and strong English results and communication skills. The Business School will conduct an initial screening and determine the final participants based on the applications received.

Required materials include a university English transcript, an English résumé, an English personal statement, and a copy of an IELTS or TOEFL score report if available. Interested applicants should first register by emailing `10zzhou@stu.edu.cn`, then submit the application materials to the Business School Foreign-Affairs Program Office in Wenxi 211. Materials are due by noon on February 28, 2013.

The contact is Zhou Zhou, at the office telephone number `82903382` or `10zzhou@stu.edu.cn`. Further information is available on the [McGill Desautels international summer program page](http://www.mcgill.ca/desautels/programs/international-summer).
