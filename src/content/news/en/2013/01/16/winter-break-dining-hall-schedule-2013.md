Shantou University Resource Management Office adjusted dining-hall operations for facility repairs and renovations during the winter break:

| Dining hall | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| First floor of the Fourth Dining Hall | Closed January 19–23; reopens January 24 and operates through the start of the new semester |
| Second-floor staff restaurant in the Fourth Dining Hall | Closed for construction January 19–February 20; reopens to faculty and staff on February 21 |
| Second and Third Dining Halls | Closed January 24–February 14; reopen February 15 |

While the staff restaurant is closed, faculty and staff may use their campus cards at the designated staff-service counter on the first floor of the Fourth Dining Hall.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
