The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office invited faculty members and graduate students who passed the first, second, or third service-learning teacher-training session to select teaching roles for spring 2013. Eleven service-learning courses were offered, requiring instructors and teaching assistants to support classroom training, community service, and review and assessment.

### Course needs

| Course | Staffing and schedule | Service location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “A Date with Blue Sky” creative research service | Three people needed, with one additional person still needed; training on Saturdays in Weeks 2–5, service on alternate Saturdays in Weeks 3–13, review in Week 14 | Guangda Experimental School and other Shantou schools |
| Toward the Ocean: marine-territory awareness and marine-ecology education | Seven people needed, with two additional people still needed; lectures and discussions on Friday afternoon periods 6–9 in E303 during Weeks 3–12, with weekend practice | Fishing villages, primary and secondary schools, and public venues around Shantou |
| Rural Health Promotion Plan | Five people needed, with two additional people still needed; training on March 20, service on March 23, 24, and 30, and review on April 10 | Longjiang Town, Huilai County (planned) |
| Love and Care in Education | Six instructors and two teaching assistants, with two additional teaching assistants still needed; March site visit and classes, Saturday practice in Weeks 5–12, review on June 5 | Shantou Aixin School |
| Shantou Transportation Quality Improvement Project | Five instructors and five teaching assistants, with five additional teaching assistants still needed; training in Weeks 3–4, practice in Weeks 6, 8, 10, and 12, and review in Week 12 | Shantou |
| Legal Clinic | One person needed and already confirmed; training, practice, and assessment from late March to early June | Determined by the course arrangement |
| Sunshine Action: community-correction service and research in Chaoshan | Two people needed and already confirmed; Wednesdays afternoons or Saturday mornings from March to June | Shantou |
| Public-Welfare Photography | Four people needed and already confirmed; lectures in Weeks 3–9, practice in Weeks 5, 8, and 9, and review and exhibition in Week 10 | Rural primary schools or Cunxin Shantang factory |
| Caring for rural left-behind children | Seven people needed and already confirmed; survey, team training, home-visit support, and outcome assessment | Xiacuo Village, Leiling Town, Chaoyang District, Shantou |
| Shuguang service-learning course | Four to six people needed, recruited by the course; training, Sunday practice, and review from March to May | Primary schools or communities in urban Shantou |
| Family tutoring and service for children with cerebral palsy | Three people needed and already confirmed; Wednesday-afternoon activities from March 20 to May 15 | Chaoshan region |

Faculty members undertaking course duties were asked to complete the “Service-Learning Course Faculty Selection Information Form” and email the Excel file as an attachment to Liu Xiangling at `gykc@stu.edu.cn` by 5:00 p.m. on January 18, 2013. The filename should follow the required “Service-Learning Course Faculty Selection Information-Name” format. Participants should select courses according to the staffing needs and schedules; detailed service plans could be adjusted as circumstances required.

To protect the personal information of trained faculty members and graduate students, this article does not reproduce the qualified-personnel lists, student numbers, or individual selection forms.
