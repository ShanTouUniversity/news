The Shantou University Graduate School opened “three-assistance” positions for all graduate students on the main campus for the spring 2013 semester. The positions cover course teaching assistance, laboratory support, school and institute administration, research and teaching support, records management, student affairs, and campus information management. Detailed positions and duties are provided in the Graduate School attachments.

Teaching assistants will help instructors grade assignments and examinations, provide tutorial support, prepare and supervise laboratory work, and organize course projects and teaching materials. Administrative assistants are placed in schools, institutes, laboratories, and university offices, where they support instrument maintenance, laboratory management, teaching and research administration, student affairs, and office operations.

### Application schedule

Interested graduate students must complete the *STU Graduate Three-Assistance Position Application Form (Spring 2013)* and submit it to the responsible teacher at the hiring unit, such as a major school or administrative office, by noon on January 21, 2013. Applicants should contact the hiring unit about the position requirements. Each hiring unit must submit its consolidated applicant list to the Graduate School by 5:00 p.m. that day and email the electronic version to `fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn`.

The contact is Wang Fengqi, at `86504000` or `fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn`. To protect graduate-student personal information, this article does not reproduce names, contact details, student numbers, campus-card numbers, adviser or hiring-unit opinions, or the appointment rosters.
