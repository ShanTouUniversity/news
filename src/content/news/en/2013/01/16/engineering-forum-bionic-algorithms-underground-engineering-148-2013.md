The 148th Shantou University Engineering School research forum will be held on January 17, 2013. Gao Wei, professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Geotechnical and Road and Bridge Engineering at Wuhan University’s School of Civil Engineering and Architecture, will discuss “Applications of Bio-Inspired Algorithms in Underground-Engineering Research.” The session is open to interested Engineering School faculty and students.

### Forum details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | January 17, 2013, 10:00–11:00 a.m. |
| Venue | Engineering School Academic Exchange Room |
| Speaker | Professor Gao Wei |
| Topic | Applications of bio-inspired algorithms in underground-engineering research |

Gao’s research and teaching cover stability analysis and construction control for underground engineering, landslide and slope hazard prevention, foundation and road engineering, design and control of large underground caverns, and civil-engineering disaster control. He has published nearly 100 academic papers, written three monographs and one textbook, and participated in national research projects and major engineering projects.
