Following discussion at the first 2013 university leadership meeting, Shantou University publicized four proposed department-head appointments. The appointments were made under the Trial Measures for the Appointment of STU Management Cadres (Shanda Party〔2009〕No. 47).

| Proposed position | Candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Head of the Law Department, Law School | Chen Ruilin |
| Head of the Department of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Engineering School | Niu Xiaodong |
| Head of the Electronic Engineering Department, Engineering School | Fan Zhen |
| Head of the Computer Department, Engineering School | Cai Hao |

Organizations and individuals with differing opinions may submit written feedback to the university Human Resources Office by January 22, 2013. Organizational submissions must bear the official seal, and individual submissions must include the signatory’s real name. The telephone number is `86503390`, and the email address is `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.

Fan Zhen’s name appears garbled in the notice body but is clearly recorded as “范衠” in the attached résumé. This article uses that verified name and does not reproduce the attachment’s birth, education, or employment-history details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
