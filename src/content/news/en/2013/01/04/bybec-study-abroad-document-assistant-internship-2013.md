Shenzhen Bybec Information Consulting is recruiting two study-abroad document-assistant interns from current undergraduate students majoring in English. Applications close on January 18, 2013.

### Position details

Interns will assist document consultants with collecting and analyzing school information, communicating with clients about applicants’ backgrounds, designing initial study-abroad plans, and translating, organizing, and proofreading application materials. They will also help with follow-up matters such as visas, air tickets, and overseas accommodation.

Applicants should have strong English reading and writing skills. Preference will be given to candidates with TEM-8 qualifications or experience with IELTS, TOEFL, or GRE examinations. Strong writing, learning, and communication skills and proficiency in Excel, Word, and PowerPoint are required.

The position offers a base salary of RMB 1,000 per month, performance commission, and personal health insurance. Internships during winter and summer breaks are full-time, with a five-day workweek and flexible working times and locations.
