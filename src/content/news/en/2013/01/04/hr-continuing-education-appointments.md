It is reported that, following deliberation at the 16th university leadership meeting held on December 26, 2012, Shantou University has adjusted several mid-level management positions, announcing the decisions in Document Shanda Fa [2013] No. 2.

## Appointment Decisions

According to the meeting's decisions:

| Person | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Li Yue | Appointed Deputy Director of the Human Resources Office, with a six-month probation period |
| Liu Mingqiang | Appointed Vice Dean of the School of Continuing Education, with a six-month probation period; relieved of his post as Deputy Director of the HR Office |
| Wulanhasi | No longer serves concurrently as Director of the Human Resources Office |
| Wu Ping | As Deputy Director of the HR Office, presides over the office's work |

All appointments carry a six-month probation period. Following the reshuffle, daily operations of the Human Resources Office will be led by Deputy Director Wu Ping.
