It is reported that the Institute of Higher Education, the Center for Teacher Development and Educational Assessment and the Academic Affairs Office will jointly hold a Teacher Development Lecture on January 4, 2013.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | “Four Qi” on the Grassroots Expression of Master Teacher Growth |
| Speaker | Huang Huiyang, Director of Shantou Municipal Education Bureau |
| Time | 15:00–17:00, January 4, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 205, Science and Technology Building |
| Organizers | Institute of Higher Education, Center for Teacher Development and Educational Assessment, Academic Affairs Office |

All faculty, students and parents interested in Shantou’s educational development are welcome to attend.
