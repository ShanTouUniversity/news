The “Dream Road” interview and premiere activity, part of STU’s Little Dreams micro-public-welfare project, raised RMB 10,913. The full amount was transferred to the STU Youth Volunteers’ Firefly Charity Fund.

The total included RMB 5,433 raised on campus, of which RMB 925 came from an additional film screening at the Library Lecture Hall on December 23, 2012, and RMB 5,480 raised in urban-area activities.

The Communist Youth League Committee and Student Volunteer Association said that faculty and students with questions about the fundraising activity or its amount could contact the University Youth League Committee.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Student Volunteer Association)
