STU Communist Youth League Committee and Student Volunteer Association appointed 30 students as volunteer supervisors for product quality and safety and formed the STU University Student Product-Quality and Safety Volunteer Team.

The initiative follows the State Council’s Outline for Quality Development (2011–2020). It is intended to encourage social participation in product-quality and safety work, expand university student volunteer service, and support campaigns addressing quality and safety risks and prominent ethical issues. The team’s detailed duties will be announced separately.
