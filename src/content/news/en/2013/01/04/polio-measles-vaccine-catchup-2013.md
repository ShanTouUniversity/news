It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on January 4, 2013, regarding supplementary and catch-up vaccinations for polio and measles-containing vaccines in Guangdong Province, to be carried out at the campus clinic.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target group | Children born on or after January 1, 2009 |
| Dates | January 5, 6, 10 and 11, 2013 |
| Venue | Planned Immunization Room, Campus Clinic (3rd floor) |
| Interval requirement | At least 28 days between polio/measles-containing vaccines and other live attenuated vaccines |

## Scope

- **Polio supplementary immunization**: All children aged 2 months to 3 years without contraindications receive one dose regardless of prior history or residence;
- **Measles-containing vaccine catch-up**: Children aged 8 months to 3 years who have no history at ≥8 months or fewer than two doses at ≥18 months receive one dose if without contraindications;
- **Measles-containing vaccine supplementary immunization**: In designated cities, counties or townships, all children aged 8 months to 3 years without contraindications receive one dose regardless of prior history.

The list of designated areas and contraindication details are available in the original OA notice.
