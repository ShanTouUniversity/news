STU Human Resources Office announced that the 2012 pre-service training certificates and teacher qualification certificates had been returned. Relevant units should send a representative to collect their personnel’s certificates from Room 315 of the Administrative Center by January 6.

For personnel who passed the pre-service training, the Human Resources Office would handle reimbursement of the training fees centrally. The reimbursements were expected to be deposited into individual Bank of China accounts in mid-to-late January. The names in the certificate attachment are not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
>
> ⚠️ Consult the original OA attachment through the OA system for the certificate collection list.
