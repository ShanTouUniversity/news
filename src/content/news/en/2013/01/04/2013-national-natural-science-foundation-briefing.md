STU Research Office will hold a briefing on 2013 National Natural Science Foundation project applications at 3:30 p.m. on January 7, 2013, in Room 205 of the Science and Technology Center. The meeting will outline the year’s application work.

The agenda includes updates from the foundation’s annual meeting. Liang Bin, PhD, will discuss his experience as an adjunct appointee of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and explain the review process. Li Yuanyou, professor, will share experience from the second-round review and project applications.

The briefing is intended for prospective 2013 foundation applicants, principal investigators of active foundation projects, personnel recruited within the previous three years, and associate deans and research secretaries responsible for research at each school. Postdoctoral researchers and graduate students are also welcome.
