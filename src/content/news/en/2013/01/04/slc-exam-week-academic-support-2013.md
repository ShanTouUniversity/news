STU Student Affairs Office announced the examination-week service schedule for the Student Learning Center (SLC). Regular academic-inquiry services will end after Saturday of Week 16. From Week 17 through Thursday of Week 18, January 6–17, 2013, all SLC discussion rooms will remain open for academic inquiries, group discussion, and self-study.

### Academic-inquiry schedule

| Course | Service type | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calculus I | Fixed service | Wednesday evenings in Weeks 17 and 18, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Probability and Mathematical Statistics | Appointment service | Saturday of Week 17 and Sunday of Week 18, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Communication Principles | Fixed service | Wednesday evening of Week 18, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Organic Chemistry | Fixed service | Monday evening of Week 18, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |

Students may sign in at the SLC front desk during fixed-service periods without an appointment. Appointment-based services require booking at <http://booking.stu.edu.cn> at least 20 hours in advance. Tutor names and class information are not published here.
