STU Network and Information Center reported the service status following the integration upgrade between MYSTU and the mail system on January 4, 2013. Foxmail, Outlook, and other mail clients could send and receive messages normally after the upgrade, but Webmail access remained unavailable.

The center planned to take the related services offline from midnight on January 4 until 6:00 a.m. on January 5, during which all functions would be suspended for campus users. Messages from January 4 could be viewed through the former mail system at `http://oldmail.stu.edu.cn`; the site provided search access only and could not send mail.
