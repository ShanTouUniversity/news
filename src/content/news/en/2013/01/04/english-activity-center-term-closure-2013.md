The STU English Language Center announced that its extracurricular English activities for the semester concluded in Week 15. The English Activity Center (EAC) would close from January 4, 2013.

The opening schedule for the next semester will be announced separately. The English Language Center thanked faculty and students for supporting the semester’s activities.
