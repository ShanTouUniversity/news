The Hong Kong Chinese Orchestra will headline a performance in **Colorful Arts Season 2013 Spring** at **7:30 p.m. on Thursday, April 25, 2013**, in the Shantou University Auditorium. The program features ensemble works for xiao, yangqin, huqin and other traditional Chinese instruments.

### Program

1. **Calm**, a dongxiao, yangqin and huqin quartet by Doming Lam;
2. **The Sky and the Well**, a huqin quartet by Zheng Bing;
3. **Ran Shao Xi Wang (Burning Hope)**, a huqin quartet by Robert Zollitsch;
4. **Cloud and Wind**, a huqin quartet by Wang Se;
5. **Skyland Ascended (Excerpts)**, a huqin quartet by Joshua Chan;
6. **Xiao-Ban**, a string quintet by Xu Jianqiang;
7. **Leaping Dragon**, a string quintet by Hu Dengtiao.

The program notes describe *Calm* as combining a neoclassical style with a Latin dance rhythm. *The Sky and the Well* uses a new musical language to explore the imagination of the sky and the well. *Burning Hope* examines polyphony, speed and rhythm in a huqin quartet, while *Cloud and Wind* evokes hopes for the future through the meeting of clouds and wind. *Skyland Ascended* draws on Li Bai&apos;s poem and uses three sections to express dream, landscape and freedom. *Xiao-Ban* and *Leaping Dragon* incorporate musical materials associated with Chinese opera, including the xiao-ban mode of Yue opera and Ninghai pingdiao.

### Orchestra and tickets

Founded in 1977, the Hong Kong Chinese Orchestra is a large professional Chinese orchestra with 85 professional musicians. Its instrumentation includes bowed strings, plucked strings, winds and percussion. The orchestra presents concerts, arts-education activities, forums and competitions, and has commissioned or arranged more than 2,100 works. More information is available at <http://www.hkco.org>.

### Audience guidance

Audience members should arrive 10 minutes early and dress neatly or formally. Food, drinks, smoking and noisy items are prohibited in the venue. Phones and other sound-emitting devices should be turned off, and audience members should not walk around after the performance begins. Anyone leaving during the performance will not be readmitted. Photography, video recording and audio recording are prohibited unless approved in advance by the organizers. All audience members must also follow STU&apos;s rules for public venues.
