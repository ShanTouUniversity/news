Shantou University&apos;s English Language Center will host **“Writing in the Sand of Memory: Vocabulary Building”** at CILL from **10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Saturday, April 27, 2013**. The workshop will be led by Tian Xu.

The workshop will present strategies for remembering a large number of English vocabulary words in a limited amount of time, including the “word root” strategy, and will discuss how such methods can help with exam preparation.
