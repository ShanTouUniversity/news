With the Labor Day holiday approaching, Shantou University Student Affairs has issued safety guidance covering weather, transport, travel and staying on campus.

### Travel and health

- Guangdong has entered a period with frequent rainstorms and severe convective weather. During lightning, strong winds or hail, students should immediately enter a safe building and stay away from trees, billboards and temporary structures;
- In response to the H7N9 avian-influenza cases reported at the time in Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui, Henan and Beijing, the notice recommends reducing travel to areas with concentrated cases and avoiding close contact with poultry;
- Follow traffic regulations when traveling, do not use unlicensed or overloaded vehicles, and do not drive after drinking;
- Follow safety guidance at destinations, use regulated travel companies and choose food-service establishments with reliable hygiene. During hiking, rock climbing or outdoor expeditions, follow local safety guidance and do not climb unapproved mountains or enter officially prohibited hazardous areas;
- Safeguard cash and valuables, travel in groups where possible, avoid displaying valuables in public, tell relatives, family members or teachers about the itinerary and leave a contact number, and return to campus on schedule.

### Staying on campus

Students remaining on campus must follow university schedules and safety-management rules. They must not accommodate visitors or people of the opposite sex overnight and should not allow strangers into residence halls. Open flames and high-power appliances such as immersion heaters are prohibited in student residences. Students should turn off appliances and unplug them before leaving, and should not store items in or block fire-escape routes.

Students should not fish or swim in dangerous waters such as the campus reservoir, lakes or beaches. On-campus recreational activities must be conducted safely. Candles and fireworks are prohibited, and activities should not disturb other students. Open flames and barbecues are prohibited in fire-restricted areas, and students must not carry open flames into the hills.
