Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will host a media salon from **7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on Tuesday, April 23, 2013**, in the Science Lecture Hall. University of Southern California professor Adam Clayton Powell III will speak in English on **“Cities Get Bigger, Screens Get Smaller: Urbanization, Mobile Phones and Digital-Media Development.”**

The salon will examine urbanization, the role of mobile phones in the development of digital media, and how digital media can help people access detailed information about their cities. Faculty and students interested in digital-media development are welcome to attend.
