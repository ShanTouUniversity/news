It is reported that the Research Office, the Social Sciences Department, and the Journal Editorial Office of Shantou University have jointly invited Yu Yang, editor-in-chief of *Xinhua Wenzhai*, to speak at the university. The lecture, themed "The Art of Writing," will take place at 3:00 p.m. on Thursday, May 2, in Meeting Room 205 of the Science Center, followed by a discussion session. All students and staff are welcome.

## About the Content

According to the organizers, good writing values innovation above all, prizes refinement, and excels through its spirit and rhythm. Although many people work with written articles every day, their understanding of what makes a piece of writing remains unclear. The lecture will address questions such as what defines an article and how to evaluate the quality of writing in the humanities and social sciences.

Yu will focus on the criteria *Xinhua Wenzhai* applies when selecting articles for reprinting, and will offer an in-depth critique of problems found in recent articles published in well-known journals.
