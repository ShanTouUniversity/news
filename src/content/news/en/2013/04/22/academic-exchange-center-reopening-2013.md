It is reported that the Academic Exchange Service Center of Shantou University issued a notice on April 22, 2013, that the facade renovation of the annex fire escape has been completed.

The second-floor restaurant and Meeting Room No.5 will resume normal operation on April 24, 2013. The center thanked all units for their support.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Exchange Service Center, Resource Management Office)
