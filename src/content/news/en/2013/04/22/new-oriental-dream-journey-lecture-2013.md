A New Oriental Dream Journey motivational lecture for university students will be held at the Shantou University Auditorium at **7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, April 23, 2013**. The theme is **“English Learning and Striving in Life.”** New Oriental speakers Xia Peng, Li Xu and Zhao Erdi will share perspectives on English learning, life planning and personal development with the STU community.

Dream Journey is a nationwide public-welfare lecture tour launched by New Oriental in 2005 and jointly organized by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League School Department, the All-China Students&apos; Federation Office and New Oriental Education & Technology Group. It uses discussions of English learning and personal growth to encourage young students to set goals and develop their overall abilities.

### Speakers

- **Xia Peng**: Assistant to the president of New Oriental&apos;s Shenyang School and a lecturer for the New Oriental “Believe in the Future” and “Dream Journey” national public-welfare tours. Xia earned a bachelor&apos;s degree in English Language and Literature from Nanjing University and an MBA from Cheung Kong Graduate School of Business. His listed honors include the 2005 International English Speech Contest championship, the championship and Most Potential Award at the 10th 21st Century·FLTRP Cup National College English Speaking Contest, and third place in the CCTV Cup National College Student Speaking Contest.
- **Li Xu**: One of New Oriental Education & Technology Group&apos;s first-generation speakers and an oral-English teaching specialist. He previously worked as a radio host and was a visiting scholar in the English Department at the University of Arizona. He has participated in the “Believe in the Future” and “Dream Journey” public-welfare tours and serves as a group trainer.
- **Zhao Erdi**: A graduate of Nankai University&apos;s Department of International Economics who later earned a master&apos;s degree from the University of Aberdeen. He previously worked for China National Arts and Crafts Group, the head-office business department of CITIC Bank and Nortel. His New Oriental roles have included teacher in the Beijing middle-school division, supervisor at the group&apos;s fund-management center, assistant to a vice president, and principal of the Chengdu and Guangzhou schools.

Zhao has received New Oriental awards for corporate culture, outstanding teams and outstanding senior management. Li was listed as the top speaker in the 2011–2012 Dream Journey national public-welfare lecture tour.

Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
