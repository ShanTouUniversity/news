It is reported that, in accordance with the requirements of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and the Provincial Department of Finance for the third batch of 2013 provincial science and technology program projects, the Research Office of Shantou University has arranged on-campus applications for the 2013 Guangdong Laboratory System Construction projects.

## Two Application Tracks

Applications fall into two tracks. The first is the **provincial key laboratory construction project**, which follows an organized application process. Interested units should complete a project proposal according to the application guide. The provincial department will select units capable of completing the construction tasks based on the discipline and industry layout of the province's laboratory system, taking into account Guangdong's science and technology development and industrial restructuring needs.

The second track is the **Guangdong Laboratory System Construction supporting project**, which focuses on supporting provincial key laboratories and other platforms in tackling core and generic technologies, and provides conditions for pilot testing and industrialization of scientific achievements with major promotion value.

| Track | Focus | Required Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Key laboratory construction | Laboratory system construction | Project proposal |
| Supporting project | Core/generic technology R&D, pilot testing and industrialization | Supporting project proposal |

## Timeline

Proposals must be sent to the designated Research Office email by **May 7**. The application guide and proposal templates are available as attachments to the original notice.

> ⚠️ The application guide and related attachments are available on the OA system.
