Interviews for the 2013 Shantou University Sportsmanship Medal will be held at **9:00 a.m. on April 24** in Administrative Center Meeting Room 221. Shortlisted students should attend on time. The interview has two stages:

1. A self-introduction in Chinese, limited to one minute;
2. Questions from the judges, limited to nine minutes.

The shortlist was distributed as an attachment to the original notice. Student names and other personal information are not reproduced here; eligibility is governed by Student Affairs&apos; original notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The original attachment contains the shortlist of students. Personal names are not reproduced; only the interview process and venue are provided.
