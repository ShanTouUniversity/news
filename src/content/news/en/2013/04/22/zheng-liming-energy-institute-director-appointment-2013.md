Shantou University Engineering College has appointed **Associate Professor Zheng Liming** as director of the Energy Research Institute. **Professor Liu Xiong** no longer serves as director.

The appointment notice, numbered STU Engineering College Document No. 7 (2013), was dated **April 22, 2013**.
