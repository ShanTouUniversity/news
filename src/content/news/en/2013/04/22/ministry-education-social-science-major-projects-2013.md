The Ministry of Education has opened bidding for its 2013 Major Research Projects in Philosophy and Social Sciences. The call includes **50 projects**, with funding of **RMB 300,000 to 800,000 per project in principle**. Shantou University Academic Research asks relevant units and faculty members to prepare proposals according to the bidding guide.

### Eligibility

The notice requires bidders to hold a full senior professional or technical title and to be able to organize and guide the research. Only one person may bid for each project, and a bidder may not also serve as a subproject leader or research-group member on another bid in the same call. Bidding is not permitted in the following cases:

- The applicant is responsible for an unfinished major National Social Science Fund project;
- The applicant is responsible for an unfinished major project of a Ministry of Education Humanities and Social Sciences Key Research Base or an unfinished major post-funding project in the Ministry of Education philosophy and social-sciences program;
- The applicant is the chief expert of an ongoing Ministry of Education major philosophy and social-sciences project and had not submitted an application for final-results appraisal by May 10, 2013.

The attached project-management provisions further state that a bidder must be a university professor guaranteed by a legal-person institution. Projects use a chief-expert responsibility system and encourage collaboration across disciplines, universities, departments and regions, with one university serving as the lead bidding institution. Bids must use the exact title in the bidding guide and may not change the title or add a subtitle.

### STU submission schedule

| Stage | Deadline and requirement |

| Formal review | By May 24, send the electronic version of the Proposal Evaluation Book to `ychzuo@stu.edu.cn` |
| Paper submission | By May 31, submit the qualified final version and supporting materials; the main notice requests eight copies of each, printed on A4 paper and bound as required |
| Electronic final materials | By May 31, send electronic materials matching the paper submission to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` |

The main notice and the attached filing instructions contain two details that require confirmation. The main notice requests eight copies of the final proposal and supporting materials, while the attached instructions require the Proposal Evaluation Book to be printed in seven copies, bound into seven sets and placed in seven file envelopes. The formal-review address is `ychzuo@stu.edu.cn`, while the final-submission address is `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn`. Applicants should confirm the copy count and email addresses with Academic Research before submitting.

The Proposal Evaluation Book and its attachments request information on research significance and value, the state of research, the overall framework and objectives, subproject structure, research methods and fieldwork plans, key challenges and innovations, research conditions, task allocation, schedule, output formats, budget, cooperation and substantive international academic exchange. Research records and research-group information should be prepared according to the forms; individual application materials are not reproduced here.
