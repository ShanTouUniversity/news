The Shantou University Three-Style Development Office has issued Study-Style Inspection Bulletin No. 228. During week 8 of the 2012–2013 spring semester, the inspection group conducted two class-attendance checks at the E Teaching Building and recorded **seven student lateness cases**: five cases involving lateness of five minutes or less and two cases involving lateness of more than five minutes.

### Inspection results

| Inspection time | Location | Five minutes or less | More than five minutes | Total |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 08:00, Tuesday, April 16 | E Teaching Building | 4 | 1 | 5 |
| 08:00, Thursday, April 18 | E Teaching Building | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| **Total** |  | **5** | **2** | **7** |

The bulletin states that the relevant colleges and residential colleges will provide criticism and education to students who did not follow university rules and will continue their study-style development work. The original bulletin includes student names; this article publishes only the anonymized totals and does not reproduce the personal list.
