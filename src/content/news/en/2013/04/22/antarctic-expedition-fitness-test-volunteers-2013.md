To support fitness testing for Shantou University&apos;s 2014 Antarctic Expedition Project, Student Affairs is recruiting **30 student volunteers**. Students who have qualified for the fitness test are not eligible for these volunteer positions.

Volunteers should be responsible, cooperative, able to follow instructions and committed to work discipline. They must have **no other classes or activities scheduled for the morning of April 30**; relevant volunteer experience is preferred. The service period is **8:00 a.m. to noon on April 30**, and the main duty is to assist with the fitness-testing activities.

### Application details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 30 volunteers |
| Eligible applicants | All STU students except fitness-test qualifiers |
| Service time | 08:00–12:00, April 30 |
| Duties | Assist with fitness-testing activities |
| Deadline | 9:00 a.m., April 25 |
| Submission | Download and complete the form and email it to `expedition@stu.edu.cn` |

The application form requests student personal information, and file naming must follow the original notice. This article does not reproduce form fields, student names, year and major, or contact details; the Student Affairs notice governs the detailed recruitment requirements.
