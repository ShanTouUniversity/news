Shantou University Academic Research has forwarded the 2014 solicitation guidelines for prospective projects under the National High-Tech Research and Development Program (863 Program) and the National Science and Technology Support Program. The guidelines are being released by field, and teaching and research units are asked to study the relevant requirements when preparing project or task proposals.

### Solicitation fields

The notice covers 11 fields:

- New materials;
- Transport;
- Advanced energy;
- Advanced manufacturing;
- Information technology;
- Earth observation and navigation;
- Resources and environment;
- Biology and medicine;
- Population and health;
- Marine technology;
- Modern agriculture.

The notice states that the guideline for rural areas will be added after it is issued. Faculty members interested in submitting proposals should study the applicable field guideline and contact Academic Research for follow-up arrangements. The notice does not provide a single internal STU submission deadline; prospective applicants should contact the office promptly for current requirements.
