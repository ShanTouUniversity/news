The 72nd Shantou University Party basic-knowledge study class is scheduled to begin in **May 2013**. The Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee has asked Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to nominate applicants, conduct preliminary reviews and submit materials. Nominees must have submitted a Party membership application at least one year earlier and have been designated as **key development candidates**. The Party School Office will determine the final class list after a further review.

| **Total** | **About 160** |

### Submission arrangements

After nomination and preliminary review, each unit must have its general branch or directly affiliated Party branch sign and stamp the application form and submit the materials together to the Party School Office by **April 30** at New Administrative Center Room 315. The electronic version of the consolidated student list should also be sent to `o_dxb@stu.edu.cn`. The Party School Office will finalize the list after rechecking the submissions.

The application form requests personal information including name, date of birth, place of origin, school or department, address and telephone number. The consolidated list also contains names and telephone numbers. This article publishes only the nomination requirements, quotas and submission arrangements and does not reproduce the forms or list.

### Registration and course arrangements

A subsequent notice confirms that the 72nd study class was scheduled to begin on **May 12**. The attached class list contains **121 participants** divided into nine groups. Participants were required to register with their group leaders from **May 9 to 10**, submit one frontal passport-style photograph and collect study materials. Group leaders were scheduled to meet at 1:00 p.m. on May 9 in Wenbei Room 206.

The course schedule was as follows:

| Date | Time and location | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 12 | 8:55 a.m., 10:40 a.m., 2:55 p.m. and 7:30 p.m., Lecture Hall 3 | Party-conduct development; *Forging* video, Part 1; duties and rights of Party members; the nature and mission of the Communist Party of China |
| May 19 | 8:55 a.m. and 10:40 a.m., Lecture Hall 1; 2:55 p.m. and 7:30 p.m., Lecture Hall 3 | Correcting the motivation to join the Party; *Forging* video, Part 2; the Party's organizational system and discipline; studying the Scientific Outlook on Development in depth |
| May 26 | 8:55 a.m. and 2:55 p.m., Lecture Hall 3; evening, group-designated locations | The Party's guiding ideology and university students; exchange with veteran members of the university's Committee for the Care of the Next Generation; group discussion |
| June 2 | Self-study; 7:30–9:00 p.m., Lecture Halls 1 and 3 | Self-study of *The Constitution of the Communist Party of China* and other materials; theoretical examination |

Written assignments were due on May 19, while the study summary and Participant Registration Form were due on May 26. Failure to register without a valid reason was treated as withdrawal. Unexcused absences or failure to take the examination, or approved leave exceeding one-third of the scheduled teaching hours, meant that no completion certificate would be issued. Final results combined the theory examination, assignments and study summary, discussion, study attitude, Party conduct and attendance.

The class lists and the group leaders' names and telephone numbers contain personal information and are not reproduced here.
