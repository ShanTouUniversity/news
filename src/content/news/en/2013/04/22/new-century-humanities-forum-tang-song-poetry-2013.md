New Century Humanities Forum No. 122 will be held from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on April 24, 2013**, in ACC Meeting Room 3 at Shantou University. Professor Lü Zhenghui will speak on **“The Aesthetic Features of Tang and Song Poetry.”**

### Speaker

Since the 1990s, Professor Lü has focused his research on modern Taiwanese literature, examining its development and the ideological issues in literary works primarily from the perspective of literary sociology. His research covers Taiwanese literature before and after the Second World War. His published collections of essays on modern literature include *Fiction and Society*, *The Experience of Postwar Taiwanese Literature*, *Literary Classics and Cultural Identity* and *The Scars of Colonialism*. He also co-edited *An Outline History of Trends in New Taiwanese Literature* with mainland scholar Zhao Xiaqiu.
