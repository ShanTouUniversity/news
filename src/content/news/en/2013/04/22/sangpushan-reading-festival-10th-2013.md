It is reported that the Youth League Committee, the Library and the Student Societies Federation of Shantou University will hold the 10th Sangpushan Reading Festival from April 20 to May 10, 2013, under the theme “Fragrant Books.”

## Program

| Section | Time | Venue | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening ceremony | April 20 | Pedestrian Street, Jin San Jiao | Student societies showcase and interactive games |
| Calligraphy competition | April 22–May 10 (submissions April 22–May 3) | Pedestrian Street, Library | Reading-themed soft-brush works; 30% advance to final; final works exhibited May 6–10; awards on May 5 in Pedestrian Street |
| Book recommendations | April 21–28 | Second Canteen, Weibo | Recommend favorite books via board and Weibo; Top 10 favorites announced; 20 lucky participants win a book |
| Discover the Library | April 19–24 (activity April 24, 15:00) | New Library | Department visits and work experience, suggestion reports (best 3 awarded); riddle quiz with prizes |
| Morning reading on Sangpu Mountain | April 22–May 10 (climb May 4, 08:00 from the motto monument) | Sangpu Mountain | Recitation of classics and discussion with teachers; 5 best reflections awarded a book |
| Riddle guessing | April 20–24 | Pedestrian Street, Jin San Jiao | Guess lantern riddles; 10 lucky winners receive a book |
| Classical literature lecture | To be announced | — | Reading Festival lecture; details to follow |

Registration methods and contact emails are in the OA notice and are withheld here for privacy.
