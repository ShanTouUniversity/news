Because Arts Education Center instructor Liao Wenlan will be away on business, the schedule for **Choral Training**, section **59257**, has been adjusted as follows:

| Item | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original session, suspended | Week 9, Saturday, April 27, periods 2–3 | Music Rehearsal Hall STU101 |
| Make-up session | Week 9, Wednesday, April 24, periods 6–7 | Music Rehearsal Hall STU101 |

The source notice adds a parenthetical note after the April 27 session stating that the Labor Day unified schedule adjustment moves it to April 29. It does not further explain how that note relates to this suspension. The note is retained here as a source discrepancy; the stated make-up arrangement is **periods 6–7 on April 24**, at the same venue.

Students enrolled in the course should inform one another and attend according to the make-up arrangement.
