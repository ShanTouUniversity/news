In accordance with the university&apos;s Labor Day holiday notice, the Graduate School has adjusted the class schedule for graduate students on the main campus:

| Date | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Wednesday, May 1 | Classes suspended |
| Monday, April 29 and Saturday, April 27 | The two days&apos; class schedules are exchanged |
| Tuesday, April 30 and Sunday, April 28 | The two days&apos; class schedules are exchanged |

Main-campus graduate-training units and graduate students should arrange teaching and study according to the exchanged schedules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
