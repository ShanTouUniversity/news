Undergraduate thesis and graduation-project work for the 2012–2013 spring semester is entering the defense and grade-assessment stage. Shantou University Academic Affairs requires each college or department to complete defenses, grade assessment and grade entry by the **end of week 14** of the semester, while supervisors must ensure that submitted work meets quality and formatting requirements.

### Submission and defense

When submitting the paper final version of a thesis or graduation project, each student must sign an academic-integrity pledge and bind the original in the following order: cover; integrity pledge; Chinese and English titles, abstracts and keywords; contents; main text; references; appendix, if any; supervisor scoring sheet; reviewer scoring sheet; thesis supervisory committee scoring sheet; back cover; and relevant drawings, if any. Students must also submit a Word document corresponding to the paper final version to the teaching administrator of their college or department. The college or department will collect the electronic files and submit them to Academic Affairs. Electronic filenames must follow the original notice; examples containing student numbers and names are not reproduced here.

Each department should establish a thesis supervisory committee, which may form several defense groups. The department committee determines the final grade for each thesis or graduation project. One week before defenses begin, departments should publish the committee and defense-group members, student defense times and venues, and group assignments through the OA system. Supervisors and reviewers must provide proposed grades and specific comments on the work they supervise or review before the defense begins.

### Grade assessment

Academic Affairs requires grades to reflect students&apos; actual performance, with consistent standards and a reasonable distribution across grade levels. The reference ranges are:

| Grade | Reference share of students |
| --- | ---: |
| Excellent | 0–15% |
| Good | 35–60% |
| Average | 15–40% |
| Pass | 0–15% |
| Fail | No fixed limit |

Beginning in 2013, teaching units will use new undergraduate thesis and graduation-project scoring sheets in three versions: for supervisors, reviewers and thesis supervisory committees. Scores must be entered on a computer so that the average is calculated automatically. Each type of sheet must be completed in duplicate and bound, as required, in the thesis original and the appraisal book.

### Appraisal-book submission

The teaching administrators of colleges and departments must collect the thesis or graduation-project appraisal books and submit them to Student Affairs by **the end of week 16**. The appraisal book should be bound in this order: cover and summary, supervisor scoring sheet, reviewer scoring sheet, and thesis supervisory committee scoring sheet. Students complete the cover and summary, which must be printed double-sided; the summary is limited to one page.
