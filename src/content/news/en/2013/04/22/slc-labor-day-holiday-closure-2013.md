In accordance with Shantou University&apos;s Labor Day holiday schedule, the Student Learning Center (SLC) will close to the public from **Monday, April 29 through Wednesday, May 1, 2013**.

Its academic-consultation course services will be adjusted as follows:

| Original date | Rescheduled arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Monday, April 29 | Moved to Saturday, April 27 |
| Tuesday, April 30 | Moved to Sunday, April 28 |
| Original arrangements for April 27 and 28 | Services suspended |

Faculty and students who need academic consultations should plan around the adjusted dates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
