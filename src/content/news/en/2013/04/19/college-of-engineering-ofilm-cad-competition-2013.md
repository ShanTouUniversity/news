It is reported that the O-Film Innovation Club of the College of Engineering will hold its 2nd CAD Mechanical Drawing Contest, open to all students.

## Contest Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Organizers provide an identical mechanical part drawing and a computer with AutoCAD 2004; participants must independently complete the CAD drawing within 30 minutes |
| Scoring | Drawing accuracy accounts for 70% and time used for 30%, with a total of 100 points, judged by instructors |
| Deadline | Before May 3, 2013 |
| How to apply | Download the registration form, rename it as “CAD Mechanical Drawing Contest Form + Grade/Major + Name,” and send it to the designated email address |
| Awards | 1 first prize (200 yuan), 3 second prizes (100 yuan each), 10 third prizes (50 yuan each), plus certificates; all participants receive a gift |

Details on awards and the registration email are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The registration form and contacts are available on the OA system.
