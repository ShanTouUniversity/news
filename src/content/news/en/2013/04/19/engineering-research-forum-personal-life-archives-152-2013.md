The 152nd Engineering Research Forum at Shantou University will be held at **10:00 a.m. on April 24, 2013**, in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room. Cathal Gurrin, a lecturer in the School of Computing at Dublin City University, will deliver a talk titled **“Personal Life Archives, a new search challenge.”**

### Talk overview

The talk will introduce the concept of a Personal Life Archive: a searchable digital record of a person&apos;s experiences created through sensing, software and storage technologies, and designed to function as an auxiliary memory in cooperation with human memory. It will consider advances in data sensing, data storage, information retrieval and data mining, as well as the possibilities created by personal-scale search engines, wearable devices and environmental sensors.

The presentation will also examine potential applications in healthcare, learning and productivity, possible social consequences of widespread Personal Life Archives, and the progress of related prototype systems and research.

### Speaker

Cathal Gurrin is also a visiting researcher at the University of Tromso in Norway, director of the Human Media Archives research group at Dublin City University and a collaborating investigator at the CLARITY Centre for Sensor Web Technologies. His research interests include information access to Personal Life Archives, multimodal human-computer interaction and information retrieval.

According to the public biography, he has been an active lifelogger since mid-2006, has accumulated more than 10 million SenseCam images and develops search and organizational technologies for Personal Life Archives. He has authored more than 100 academic publications, and his research has been featured by the BBC and Discovery Channel.
