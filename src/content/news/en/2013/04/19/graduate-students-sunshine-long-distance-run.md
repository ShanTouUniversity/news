In accordance with requirements from the National Sunshine Sports Leading Group Office and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, together with the university's own arrangements, the Graduate School of Shantou University will hold the sixth Sunshine Sports Winter Long-Distance Run at 4 PM on Wednesday, April 24, with all of its graduate students taking part.

It is reported that the event carries the theme "Patriotism Starts with Physical Exercise" and consists of one lap around the campus ring road. Teachers from the university's Physical Education Department and the school's Youth League Committee, along with heads of the sports section of the Graduate Student Union, will lead the run and photograph the event.

The Graduate School welcomes broad participation and reminds runners to gather at the University Motto Stone ten minutes ahead of the start.
