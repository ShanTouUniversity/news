It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on April 19, 2013, announcing the results of the mid-term review for 2011 Teaching Reform Research Projects and the acceptance review for 2009 deferred projects.

## Review Summary

A total of 49 projects were reviewed: 38 mid-term reviews, seven early completions, two deferred completions and two discontinued projects. The outcomes were:

| Category | Count | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early completion | 7 | 3 completed as planned and approved for closure; 4 did not meet the standard and were treated as having passed the mid-term review to continue |
| Deferred completion (2009) | 2 | Both completed as planned and approved for closure |
| Mid-term review | 38 | All met project requirements, passed and will receive the second phase of funding |
| Discontinued | 2 | Discontinued |

Detailed results are available in the OA attachment.

Projects that passed the mid-term review are asked to continue research, while those that passed acceptance are encouraged to promote and apply their results to advance teaching reform and improve teaching quality.
