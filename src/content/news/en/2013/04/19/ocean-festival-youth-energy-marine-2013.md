Under the initiative of the School of Science Youth League Committee, the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association will join the *Toward the Ocean* public-welfare course group to organize the first **“Youth and Ocean” Marine Festival**. Open to the entire STU community, the festival runs from **April 20 to June 15, 2013**, and aims to strengthen awareness of the ocean and marine ecological conservation.

### Festival program

| Series | Dates | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marine Festival image exhibition and opening | April 20–June 15 | Opening ceremony and marine-themed exhibition |
| Maritime-territory awareness and marine-conservation works call | Registration and submission by midnight, May 18 | Handicraft and visual-art works |
| Environmental cycling activity | To be announced | Environmental cycling from a meeting point in Niutianyang; no registration required |
| Awards ceremony and closing | June 9–15 | Awards, closing event and the second *Toward the Ocean* public-welfare course sharing session |

### Works call

Individuals, teams and organizations may participate; teams may have no more than **three members**. Eligible works include marine-themed handicrafts and visual works such as cartoons, drawings, sketches and graffiti designs. Materials are unrestricted, but each work must relate to the ocean and include a statement giving its theme and an explanation of no more than **100 Chinese characters**.

Each work will be assessed through judges&apos; scores and public voting, weighted **60%** and **40%**, respectively. The planned public-voting method is an anonymous vote after the works are displayed in a public area.

### Awards

The following awards will be offered separately in each work category:

| Award | Places | Prize |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First prize | 1 | Marine Festival souvenir, certificate and RMB 200 |
| Second prize | 2 | Marine Festival souvenir, certificate and RMB 120 |
| Third prize | 3 | Marine Festival souvenir, certificate and RMB 80 |
| Finalist award | 10 | Marine Festival souvenir and certificate |

Registration and work-submission channels will be governed by notices on the campus OA network, the School of Science Youth Volunteers Association&apos;s public channels and subsequent Marine Festival announcements. The registration process requests student names, student numbers, year and major, contact details and dormitory information; personal data and submission locations are not reproduced here.
