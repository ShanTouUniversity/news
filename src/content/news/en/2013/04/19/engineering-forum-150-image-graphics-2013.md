It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold its 150th Engineering Research Forum on the afternoon of April 20 (Saturday) at the Lecture Hall of the Science Building, themed on image and graphics related technologies and their applications, with two experts from Sun Yat-sen University as speakers.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | 150th Engineering Research Forum, College of Engineering |
| Theme | Image and Graphics Related Technologies and Their Applications |
| Time | April 20 (Saturday), 15:30–17:00 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Science Building |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |
| Issue date | April 19, 2013 |

## Speakers

| Speaker | Profile |
| --- | --- |
| Prof. Ni Jiangqun | Director, Department of Electronic and Communication Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University; Executive Director and Secretary-General, Guangdong Image and Graphics Society |
| Assoc. Prof. Zheng Weishi | Hundred Talents Program (2011) scholar; Core member, Computational Science Research Team of the Guangdong Introduced Innovative Research Team |

Undergraduate and postgraduate students and faculty of the Department of Electronic Engineering are expected to attend; other interested faculty and students are welcome.
