To support student-society development, the Shantou University Student Societies Federation, under the guidance of the Communist Youth League of Shantou University Committee, is organizing its third student-society incubation action. The campus-wide action runs from **April 14 to May 15, 2013**.

Teams may apply to lead an existing society or propose a new society. Each team must have at least **three members** and submit a society introduction and basic plan of at least 150 Chinese characters. The application lists Economics, Skateboard, Health, Drift Board, Football, Speech and Inspiration, Tourism and Global Study Abroad societies as possible directions. Students may also propose societies focused on flowers, handicrafts, kites, yoga or other interests.

### Schedule

| Stage | Dates |
| --- | --- |
| Application submission and review | April 14–27 |
| New-society showcase | April 28 |
| Society defense session | May 4 |
| Training workshop | May 5 |
| Submission of complete materials | May 5–15 |

Applications close at **midnight on April 27**. A new society established through the incubation action may receive up to **RMB 600** in start-up activity funding, subject to the review results and subsequent notices.

The application form requests member names, year and major, telephone numbers, dormitory information and email addresses. These personal details and contact points are not reproduced here. Students should obtain the form, submission instructions and subsequent notices through the official channels of the Student Societies Federation.
