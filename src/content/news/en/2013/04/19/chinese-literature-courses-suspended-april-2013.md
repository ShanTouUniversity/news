| Class | Course | Suspended session | Original room | Make-up time |

| 58807 | Lectures on Literary Classics | Week 9, Monday, April 22, periods 6–7 | E308 | To be announced |
| 58808 | Lectures on Literary Classics | Week 9, Monday, April 22, periods 8–9 | E308 | To be announced |
| 58809 | *The Dream of the Red Chamber* and Buddhist Cultural Wisdom | Week 9, Tuesday, April 23, periods 6–7 | E401 | To be announced |
| 58810 | *The Dream of the Red Chamber* and Buddhist Cultural Wisdom | Week 9, Tuesday, April 23, periods 8–9 | E401 | To be announced |

Students enrolled in the affected courses should inform one another and follow subsequent Academic Affairs notices for make-up arrangements.
