The Shantou University Youth League Committee has forwarded the call for the **“Build Guangdong, Youth Maxims”** microblog writing contest, part of the seventh Guangdong University Student Science and Academic Festival. The contest is open to full-time students at higher-education institutions across Guangdong, with no restriction on major. It is organized by the Communist Youth League of Guangdong Provincial Committee, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and the Guangdong Students&apos; Federation, and hosted by the Communist Youth League of South China Normal University Committee.

### Entry requirements

Entries must address the theme “Build Guangdong, Youth Maxims,” remain healthy and positive, and show clear ideas and genuine feeling. Each entry must be no more than **140 Chinese characters**. Entries must be original and first published by the participant; each person may submit only one entry. Plagiarism and multiple submissions will result in disqualification.

Suggested directions include reflection on the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China and related speeches, Guangdong&apos;s people, history and cityscape, family, friendship and teacher-student relationships, students&apos; diligence and innovation, and personal views on happiness.

### Submission

Participants must publish the following complete content on Sina Weibo:

- Hashtag: `#筑梦广东·青春箴言#`
- Original text of no more than 140 Chinese characters
- `@2013广东省红段子大赛` and `@广东学联`

The same entry must also be sent as a Word document to the dedicated contest mailbox, `scnu2013hongduanzi@163.com`. Both the microblog post and the email submission are required; otherwise the entry is invalid. The STU internal deadline is **midnight on May 6, 2013**, and participants should follow the detailed registration requirements in the Youth League Committee&apos;s forwarded notice.

### Review and publication

Judges will consider scientific value, creativity and inspiration, along with thematic relevance, originality, depth of thought and flexibility of expression. Entries should reflect young students&apos; concern for society, willingness to innovate and pursuit of truth, goodness and beauty. The contest will award first, second and third prizes, each accompanied by an honorary certificate. After expert review, the winners will be publicized through `@2013广东省红段子大赛`, the Communist Youth League of Guangdong Provincial Committee and university Youth League websites.

The organizers retain the right to use, edit and compile entries for event promotion, exhibitions and public-welfare publicity without additional remuneration. Contest information and answers to common questions are available through the contest&apos;s official Weibo account and information platforms.
