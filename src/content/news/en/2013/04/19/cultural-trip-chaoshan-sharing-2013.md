It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University will hold a sharing session on the “Cultural Trip: Exploring Chaoshan Culture” study visit on April 19, 2013. The session will help students learn more about Chaoshan culture and review the learning outcomes of the visit.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:00, April 19, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 330, New Administration Center |
| Format | Presentations and group discussions |
| Participants | All students |
| Organizer | Graduate School |

The session will share learning outcomes from the study visit through presentations and group discussions. All students are welcome to attend.
