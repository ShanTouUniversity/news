Following a review by the Shantou University Business School Alumni Scholarship Review Committee, **38 candidates** have been identified for the Business School Alumni Scholarship for the 2011–2012 academic year. The candidates come from the 2009, 2010 and 2011 cohorts and five majors: Business Administration, Accounting, International Economics and Trade, Marketing and Finance.

### Candidate summary

| Cohort | Business Administration | Accounting | International Economics and Trade | Marketing | Finance | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2009 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 0 | 1 | 12 |
| 2010 | 7 | 5 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 15 |
| 2011 | 7 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 11 |
| **Total** | **16** | **9** | **8** | **1** | **4** | **38** |

During the publicity period, comments may be submitted in writing, in person or by telephone to the Business School Youth League Committee by **the end of the workday on Thursday, April 25, 2013**. Submissions should be signed as required by the notice for verification and accountability.

- Telephone: 86502725

The original publicity list contains student names. This article provides only the cohort and major totals; the Business School's original notice governs the detailed candidate results.

### Subsequent result

The Student Affairs Office issued Document No. 4 [2013] on **May 3, 2013**, reporting that, following review by the Business School review committee, awardees of the Business School Alumni Scholarship for the 2011–2012 academic year would receive **RMB 2,500 each**. The notice states that there were **38 awardees**.

The decision notice lists 38 name entries when counted individually by cohort and major: 12 in the 2009 cohort, 15 in the 2010 cohort and 11 in the 2011 cohort. The breakdown therefore matches the notice's stated total of 38 and the summary table in this earlier publicity article. This article does not reproduce the individual awardee list.
