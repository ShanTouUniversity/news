Following a review by the Shantou University Business School Alumni Scholarship Review Committee, **38 candidates** have been identified for the Business School Alumni Scholarship for the 2011–2012 academic year. The candidates come from the 2009, 2010 and 2011 cohorts and five majors: Business Administration, Accounting, International Economics and Trade, Marketing and Finance.

### Candidate summary

| Cohort | Business Administration | Accounting | International Economics and Trade | Marketing | Finance | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2009 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 0 | 1 | 12 |
| 2010 | 7 | 5 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 15 |
| 2011 | 7 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 11 |
| **Total** | **16** | **9** | **8** | **1** | **4** | **38** |

During the publicity period, comments may be submitted in writing, in person or by telephone to the Business School Youth League Committee by **the end of the workday on Thursday, April 25, 2013**. Submissions should be signed as required by the notice for verification and accountability.

- Telephone: 86502725

The original publicity list contains student names. This article provides only the cohort and major totals; the Business School&apos;s original notice governs the detailed candidate results.
