To enrich campus life, Shantou University Student Affairs is organizing an X Plan project on the exploration of Shantou history and traditional culture. The project invites students to research local history and culture, including distinctive attractions, buildings and food. It is open to all STU students and runs from **April 22 to May 27, 2013**.

Each team must have **three to five members**. Teams will be reviewed on their research plans and ability to collaborate, with **six participating teams** selected. Each team may apply for up to **RMB 500** in activity funding. An approved team will receive one-third of its approved budget as start-up funding, with the balance issued after it submits its research results.

### Schedule

| Stage | Dates |
| --- | --- |
| Team registration | April 22–27 |
| Team defense and review | Evening of April 27 |
| Field research | April 29–May 7 |
| Materials organization and report writing | May 8–15 |
| Research-results review and awards | May 18 |
| Results showcase | May 19–27 |

Registration closes at **noon on April 27**. Teams must submit an application form describing their name, research location and subject, research plan, feasibility, innovation and proposed use of funds. Final results must include at least **a five-minute video, 20 photographs and one research report**.

### Awards

| Award | Places | Prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| First prize | 1 | RMB 400 |
| Second prize | 1 | RMB 300 |
| Third prize | 1 | RMB 200 |
| Merit award | 3 | RMB 100 |

Students should obtain the application form and submission instructions through the official channels of Student Affairs and the Student Community X Plan. The form requests team leaders&apos; and members&apos; names, year and major, contact details and other personal information; personal data and dormitory submission information are not reproduced here.
