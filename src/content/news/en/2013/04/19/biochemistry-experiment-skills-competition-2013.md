The seventh Guangdong Student Biochemistry Experiment Skills Competition, part of the Guangdong University Student Science and Academic Festival, is open to full-time undergraduate and junior-college students at universities across Guangdong. The competition has separate undergraduate and higher-vocational and junior-college divisions. It is organized by the Communist Youth League of Guangdong Provincial Committee, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and the Guangdong Students&apos; Federation, and hosted by South China Agricultural University.

The Shantou University Youth League Committee will select and recommend teams based on internal registration. Students interested in participating must send the application form, experimental-design proposal and other materials to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` by **April 23, 2013**. The provincial competition accepts entries and experimental-design proposals from **April 2 through April 24**; the STU internal deadline is earlier and should govern university submissions.

### Competition schedule

| Stage | Time and venue |
| --- | --- |
| Preliminary submission and review | April 2–24; experimental-design proposals are reviewed |
| Finalist announcement | By May 5 |
| Preliminary experiment | May 25, 09:00–17:00; laboratories open for teams to familiarize themselves with the venue and pre-process materials, instruments or reagents |
| Final | May 26, 09:00–17:00, Wushan Campus of South China Agricultural University |

Approximately 70 teams are expected to reach the final, including 50 undergraduate teams and 20 higher-vocational and junior-college teams. Each university may enter no more than **10 teams**. Finalists must conduct the experiment in the laboratory according to their proposal and complete an experiment report on site. The schedule may change with the competition process; the competition website will publish the operative schedule.

### Works and safety requirements

Experimental designs may address material extraction, material testing, measurement of material content, exploration of a substance&apos;s application, comparison of the value of materials from different brands, or another biochemistry topic of interest. Proposals should be scientific, practical, innovative and safe, with clear real-world relevance and workable procedures.

Toxic or dangerous reagents and explosive apparatus are prohibited. Each team may submit only one experimental-design proposal, and previously submitted works may not be entered again. The laboratory will provide common instruments and reagents; teams must list the required quantities in their application materials and prepare any special items themselves. The organizers will not provide experimental materials.

### Assessment and awards

The final score combines the two stages: the preliminary experimental-design score accounts for **40%**, while the final score for experimental skills, records, results and report accounts for **60%**. The preliminary review considers content value, creativity, feasibility and the quality of the written design. The final review considers basic operations, instrument use, experimental results and reporting, as well as orderly and compliant conduct during the competition.

Finalists will be ranked for first, second and third prizes, excellence awards and outstanding supervisor awards. Individual awards such as Best Experimental Operation and Best Experimental Design will also be offered.

Competition information is available from the Communist Youth League of Guangdong Provincial Committee School Department at `http://www.gdcyl.com/xxb/` and the South China Agricultural University College of Life Sciences competition page at `http://life.scau.edu.cn/news-bioCompete.asp`. STU&apos;s application form and experimental-design materials contain participant information; this article does not reproduce form fields, student contacts or personal experimental proposals.
