The 151st Engineering Research Forum at Shantou University will be held from **9:00 to 10:30 a.m. on Monday, April 22, 2013**, in ACC Meeting Room 3. Lai Jianhuang, professor, doctoral supervisor and dean of the School of Information Science and Technology at Sun Yat-sen University, will present a talk titled **“New Nonlinear Clustering Methods and Their Applications.”**

### Talk overview

Beginning with the significance and framework of nonlinear-clustering research, the talk will introduce algorithms developed by Lai Jianhuang&apos;s research group, including the frequency-sensitive online learning kernel clustering algorithm (COLL), multi-exemplar affinity propagation (MEAP), graph-based multi-exemplar competitive learning clustering (GMPCL) and position-regularized support-vector clustering (PSVC).

Against the backdrop of big data, the talk will also discuss the challenges that rapidly growing data volumes create for data capture, management, processing and analysis, and the role of clustering methods in extracting useful information and supporting decision-making.

### Speaker

Lai Jianhuang is a professor, doctoral supervisor and dean of the School of Information Science and Technology at Sun Yat-sen University. He also serves as chair of the Academic Committee of the Ministry of Public Security&apos;s Key Laboratory of Video and Image Intelligent Analysis and Applications, chair of the Guangdong Society of Image and Graphics, an executive council member and academic-committee member of the Chinese Society of Image and Graphics, and a member of the Artificial Intelligence and Pattern Recognition Committee of the China Computer Federation.

Lai received his bachelor&apos;s degree from Sun Yat-sen University in 1986, his master&apos;s degree there in 1989 and his doctorate there in 1999. His research covers video and image analysis, biometric recognition, digital image processing, pattern recognition and machine learning. He has published more than 150 academic papers in international journals and at relevant conferences.
