It is reported that the STU Youth League Committee announced on April 27 a campus-wide program to commemorate the 94th anniversary of the May Fourth Movement and the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Youth League of China. Running from April through May under the theme "Dialogue with Faith, Building the Soul for Dreams," the program comprises 14 events organized by college-level Youth League committees.

## Background

The year 2013 marks 91 years since the founding of the Communist Youth League and 94 years since the May Fourth Movement. The series aims to mobilize Youth League members to study the spirit of the 18th CPC National Congress, the Second Plenary Session of the 11th Guangdong Provincial Party Committee, and the Sixth Plenary Session of the 16th CYL Central Committee, carry forward the May Fourth spirit, and translate the aspiration of the "Chinese Dream" into concrete action.

## Requirements

College Youth League committees are asked to stay focused on the central theme, tailor activities to youth needs, publicize through Weibo, the Youth League website, *STU Youth* newspaper and mobile bulletins, and submit summaries to the University Youth League Committee office by May 25.
