Beijing Hejun Consulting Co., Ltd. has donated **RMB 50,000** to establish the Hejun Business Internship Scholarship at STU's Zhicheng College. The scholarship recognizes Zhicheng College residents who perform outstandingly in business internships. Zhicheng College has published the review rules and opened the 2013 selection process.

### Award

The scholarship is awarded annually to **five outstanding interns**. Each recipient receives **RMB 2,000** and an honor certificate bearing the name of the STU Zhicheng College Hejun Business Internship Scholarship.

### Eligibility and criteria

Eligible applicants are Zhicheng College residents who take part in a business internship during the current year's summer. The internship may be found and secured by the student or recommended or arranged by the college or the student's academic school. Projects that have already received a university award or grant are excluded.

The review rules require applicants to:

- approach the internship seriously, work diligently and connect study with practice;
- innovate based on the position and academic specialty, analyze and solve practical problems, and achieve notable internship results;
- demonstrate professional ethics, follow internship arrangements and comply with university and host-organization rules;
- show teamwork, good interpersonal relationships, collaboration, organizational ability and social-activity skills;
- maintain complete, high-quality internship records, finish the assigned plan on time and achieve excellent results;
- complete work logs, submit a work summary and relevant photographs after the internship, and truthfully record the work and reflections; and
- complete an internship lasting at least one month.

### Selection process

Eligible residents must submit a written application and supporting materials to the Zhicheng College Affairs Office by the end of May each year. The deadline for the 2013 selection is **5:00 p.m. on May 31**.

The scholarship review committee includes one representative of Hejun Consulting, two Zhicheng College teachers, one mentor and one or two student representatives. A working group formed from student representatives and college-affairs teachers collects, checks and verifies application materials.

In early June, the committee will select 8–10 candidates from the applications, depending on the number of applicants that year. After their internships, candidates must submit summary materials in mid-September and take part in a public defense. The committee will then select and publicize five outstanding interns. The full review and award process is to be completed by the end of October each year.

The rules require the selection to be fair, impartial and open. Any falsification results in disqualification.

The original application form requests names, student numbers, identity numbers, internship organizations, positions, locations, dates and application statements, as well as summary reports and other supporting materials. No personal application data or internship records are reproduced here.
