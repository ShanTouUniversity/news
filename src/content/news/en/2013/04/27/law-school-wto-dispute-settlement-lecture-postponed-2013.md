STU Law School has announced that its “WTO Dispute Settlement” lecture, originally scheduled for **April 29** in Law School Room 213, was canceled and postponed because of a change in the speaker's travel schedule. A new time and venue have not been announced.

The earlier notice identified the event as Lecture 262 in the Law School series, originally scheduled for 9:30–11:00 a.m. on April 29. Zhang Yuejiao, a professor at STU Law School and a member of the World Trade Organization Appellate Body, was scheduled to speak. The Law School's latest notice will govern the rescheduled event.
