STU's Graduate School has announced that the university's Graduate Student Congress and Student Union transition election is scheduled for **7:30 p.m. on Sunday, May 5** in the Science Report Hall of the New Library. The congress will review the work of the 18th Graduate Student Union Executive Committee, recognize outstanding members and elect the presidium and department heads of the 19th Graduate Student Union.

### Congress agenda

The agenda has three parts:

- hear and review the work report of the 18th Graduate Student Union;
- recognize outstanding members of the 18th Graduate Student Union; and
- elect members of the presidium and the heads of the departments of the 19th Graduate Student Union.

The notice says the new Student Union is expected to serve as a bridge between graduate students and the university or Graduate School, while carrying out self-education, self-management and self-service functions.

### Graduate representatives

The congress is expected to have approximately **120 representatives**, selected at a ratio of about 10% of the graduate-student population. Representatives must be enrolled graduate students who perform well academically and ethically, can serve as role models in their work, and have a degree of representativeness and deliberative ability.

Each major and class is to elect representatives through a meeting of all graduate students in the class under the principle of democratic centralism. Classes must consolidate their representative lists and registration forms and submit them for qualification review by **5:00 p.m. on May 2**. The original notice describes May 2 as “next Tuesday,” which does not match the calendar date; this article retains the stated date and time without correcting the source wording.

### Student Union positions

The notice says the 19th Student Union Executive Committee will consist of the presidium and seven committee members, with the following positions:

| Position | Number or selection method |
| --- | --- |
| Presidium | One president and two vice presidents; presidium candidates run as three-person teams |
| Department heads | One each for the Secretariat, Academic, Publicity, Arts, *Fangyuan* Editorial, Life and Sports departments; candidates register individually |
| Executive Committee | The presidium and seven committee members |

Presidium teams must meet the requirements in the STU Student Cadre Regulations and have relevant student-work experience and social-activity ability. Department-head candidates must also show initiative, creativity and the ability to serve in a relevant department. After registration and qualification review, candidates are to be selected by the congress through a contested election.

The original attachments include a graduate representative registration form, a presidium team application form and an executive-committee candidate application form. They request student names, birth information, birthplace, political status, dormitory, contact details, team-member information and personal work plans. Student lists, candidate lists and personal application fields are not reproduced; applicants should follow the Graduate School's or OA system's original procedures.

### Numbering note

The notice title describes the event as the 10th Graduate Student Congress and the transition election for the 18th Graduate Student Union, while its agenda explicitly calls for the election of the 19th Student Union presidium and department heads. This article uses the 19th designation for the elected body and preserves the discrepancy in the original title.
