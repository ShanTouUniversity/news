STU's Resource Management Office has published the bid result for the construction contractor of its dangerous-building demolition project. Following the review and recommendation of STU's bidding leadership group under the university's infrastructure tendering rules, Shantou J Zhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. is listed as the winning bidder at **RMB 81,979.00**.

### Public-notice details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | STU dangerous-building demolition project |
| Winning bidder | Shantou J Zhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Bid amount | RMB 81,979.00 |
| Publicity period | April 27–May 2, 2013 |
| Tenderer | STU Infrastructure Office |
| Address | 243 Daxue Road, STU campus, Shantou |
