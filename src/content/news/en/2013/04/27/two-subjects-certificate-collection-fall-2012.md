Following review by the Guangdong Provincial Education Department Teacher Continuing Education Guidance Center, STU has received the certificates for **non-teacher-education students** who passed the Education and Psychology course training for teacher qualification recognition in the **fall 2012 semester**. The Academic Affairs Office has asked eligible participants to collect their certificates at the designated service window.

### Collection details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate | Certificate for Education and Psychology course training for teacher qualification recognition |
| Training term | Fall 2012 |
| Eligible recipients | Non-teacher-education students who passed the training |
| Collection location | Academic Affairs service window, third floor, New Administration Center |
| Personal collection | Present the recipient's valid identity document |
| Collection by proxy | Present valid identity documents for both the proxy and the certificate holder |

The Academic Affairs Office asked eligible participants to share the collection information with other students who took the training. The eligible-student list is included as an attachment; names and other student information are not reproduced here.
