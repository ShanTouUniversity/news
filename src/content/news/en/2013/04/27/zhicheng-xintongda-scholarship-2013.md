Guangdong Xintongda Steel Pipe Factory Co., Ltd. has donated **RMB 100,000** to establish the Xintongda Scholarship at STU's Zhicheng College. The scholarship recognizes Zhicheng students who demonstrate outstanding commitment to the college's “Sincerity, Respect, Humility and Harmony” motto. Zhicheng College has published the review rules and opened the 2013 selection process.

### Eligibility and award

The scholarship is open to **Zhicheng College residents who have lived in the college for at least two years**, including graduate students. It is awarded annually to **no more than four recipients**, with each recipient receiving **RMB 2,500** and an honor certificate bearing the name of the STU Zhicheng College Xintongda Scholarship.

### Applications and nominations

Eligible residents may submit applications themselves, be nominated by a Zhicheng student organization or be jointly nominated by **at least three students**. For the 2013 selection, application or nomination forms must be submitted to the Zhicheng College Affairs Office by **5:00 p.m. on May 31**. The attached form says submissions are due “before May 31”; this article retains the specific deadline stated in the notice body.

### Review criteria

The review rules identify the following main considerations:

- helping others, showing compassion, honesty, perseverance, self-discipline and a diligent approach, with notable personal moral qualities;
- making a clear and sustained academic effort, understanding the Zhicheng motto of “Sincerity, Respect, Humility and Harmony,” and demonstrating it through notable examples; and
- actively contributing to Zhicheng College development and earning recognition from fellow residents.

### Review organization

The scholarship is selected once a year. The review committee consists of one Xintongda representative, two Zhicheng College teachers, one mentor and one or two student representatives. A working group made up of student representatives and college-affairs teachers collects, checks and verifies information about applicants and nominees.

The review and award process must be completed by the end of **June** each year. The rules call for strict oversight and a fair, impartial and open selection process; falsification results in disqualification.

The application and nomination forms request names, gender, academic year and major, length of residence, birthplace, political status, contact details and previous awards or grants, along with application or nomination statements. Student names, joint-nomination information, contact details and personal application materials are not reproduced here.
