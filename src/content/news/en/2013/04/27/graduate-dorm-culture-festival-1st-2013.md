It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Graduate Student Union of the Graduate School will hold the first “Gathering as Family, Hello Roommates” Dormitory Culture Festival from late April to mid-May 2013, open to all postgraduate students by dormitory.

Themed “Gathering from All Corners, Happy as a Family,” the festival aims to strengthen emotional ties among dorm mates and create a home-like living environment.

## Program

| Event | Time | Venue | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Men’s dormitory 3x3 basketball | Afternoon, May 8 | Basketball court | 3-player knockout, 15 minutes per match, draw to determine fixtures |
| Women’s dormitory fixed-point shooting | Afternoon, May 9 | Basketball court | 10 shots per team, highest score wins |
| Three-person four-leg relay | Afternoon, May 14 | Football field | Ranked by time |
| Debate: Talking about My Dormitory | Evening, May 11 | Zhongting, Graduate Association | Flexible debate with on-site comments |
| Chess and card tournament | Evening, May 12 | Zhongting, Graduate Association | Five-in-a-row, Chinese chess and Dou Dizhu, divided by gender |
| Essay contest: My Roommate / My Dormitory, My Home | Late April – May 30 | — | Open topic, 1,500–2,000 words, A4 printed and electronic version |
| Initiative | Final stage | — | Call for building harmonious dormitories |

All competitions offer first, second and third prizes. Registration closes on May 3 (send form to o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn); essays are due by May 30 to the same address. Details are in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The registration form is available on the OA system.
