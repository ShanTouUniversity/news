Under STU's Party Central Group study plan, the university's Publicity Department has issued the April study arrangement. The focus is implementing the central Eight Rules and studying General Secretary Xi Jinping's guidance on practicing frugality and opposing waste.

### Study focus

The study will address:

- the Central Party leadership's emphasis on improving work style;
- the main content of the Eight Rules issued by the Political Bureau of the Central Committee;
- the new deployments, measures and requirements for improving work style;
- strengthening responsibility, mission and risk awareness, and making work-style improvement and close contact with the public basic standards of conduct; and
- taking the lead in practicing frugality and fostering a clean and upright campus atmosphere through a pragmatic work style.

### Format and participants

Members of the Party Central Group are to arrange their own time for self-study. Party general branches and directly affiliated branches are asked to organize study for Party members and to implement the relevant requirements in accordance with STU's implementation measures on the central Eight Rules, document No. 12 of 2013.

The recommended reference is the China Youth Network special topic on practicing frugality and opposing waste: [news.youth.cn/zt/qjff](http://news.youth.cn/zt/qjff/).
