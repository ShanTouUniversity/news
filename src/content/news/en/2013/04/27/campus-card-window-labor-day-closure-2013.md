STU's Network and Information Center has announced its campus-card service arrangement for the Labor Day holiday. The **campus-card window at the expert apartment** and the **window below the Second Dining Hall** will be closed from **April 29 through May 1** and will **reopen on May 2**.

### Holiday service options

- Top up cards through the self-service transfer, inquiry and top-up machine.
- For card loss reports and balance inquiries, call the voice self-service line at `82903414`, or dial `3414` from campus phones.
- For other issues, call the campus-card hotline at `86503420` or email `support@stu.edu.cn`.

The notice also lists a mobile contact number for the holiday period. Because it is not necessary to reproduce a personal mobile contact, it is omitted here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
