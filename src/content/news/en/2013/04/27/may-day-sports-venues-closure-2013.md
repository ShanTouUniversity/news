Under the university's Labor Day holiday schedule, STU's Sports Department has reminded faculty, students and staff that sports venues will be closed during the holiday.

The closure covers:

- indoor sports facilities;
- tennis courts; and
- the gym.

The venues will be closed from **April 29 through May 1**. Any reopening arrangement will follow a subsequent notice from the Sports Department.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
