With support from Shantou University and its foundation, the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design’s back-hill wall graffiti project will formally launch next week after a short period of paint testing. The school will organize faculty members and students to design and paint the wall, adding to the campus art environment and reflecting STU’s open approach to art.

During the work, people passing the site should take fire and personal-safety precautions, keep away from faculty members and students working at height, and observe the site’s no-open-flame rule. The university community is asked to protect the completed artwork and not damage it.

Other STU faculty members and students interested in creating work may contact Qisheng Chen of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at `qschen@stu.edu.cn`.
