To facilitate contact among university units during the 2013 Qingming holiday, STU’s General Office has compiled the duty arrangements for university departments and offices. Relevant units may consult the original OA attachment for duty information and use it for holiday work coordination.

Questions about the arrangements may be sent to the General Office at `o_jdk@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
>
> ⚠️ The duty roster contains personal names and telephone numbers and is not reproduced. Consult the original OA notice for detailed arrangements.
