Campus-card service windows at the Faculty Apartments and beneath Dining Hall 2 will close from **April 4 through 6** for the Qingming holiday and reopen on **April 7**.

During the holiday, faculty members, staff and students may use the card-transfer and balance-query machines for self-service top-ups. To report a lost card or check the balance, call the voice self-service line at `0754-82903414` (campus extension 3414). For other issues, contact the Network and Information Center hotline at `0754-86503420` or email `support@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
