STU’s Wednesday Movie Night will screen the Disney musical *Mary Poppins* from **7:00 to 10:00 p.m. on Wednesday, April 3** in the Library Theater. The venue has **150 seats**, including 50 seats open to the public on a first-come, first-served basis. Students should present their campus cards for entry.

Directed by Robert Stevenson and starring Julie Andrews, Dick Van Dyke, Glynis Johns and Hermione Baddeley, the film follows Mary Poppins as she helps a family rediscover affection and the value of life beyond money. The film received 13 Academy Award nominations and won five awards, including Best Actress, Best Film Editing, Best Original Music Score, Best Original Song and Best Visual Effects.

The entire university community is welcome.
