The second phase of the National Judicial Examination training program organized by the Career Development Center of STU Law School, the civil-and-criminal-law preparatory class, will continue on **April 12**. Students who registered for the training should bring their class passes and attend according to the timetable.

Consult the timetable issued by the Law School for detailed course arrangements.
