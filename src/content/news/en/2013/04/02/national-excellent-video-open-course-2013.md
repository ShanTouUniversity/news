It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on April 2, 2013 to launch the selection and recommendation for the 2013 National Excellent Video Open Courses, following documents from the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Department of Education.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Category and quota | Scientific and cultural quality education courses, one course to be recommended university-wide |
| Eligibility | Completed video open courses meeting the 2013 filming and production technical standards; avoid duplicating courses published on iCourse in the past two years or already submitted for review; prioritize high-impact, broad-audience cultural quality courses and lectures; encourage Chinese traditional culture courses taught in English or Chinese with bilingual lyrics |
| Procedure | Contact the Academic Affairs Office by April 10 to verify eligibility and submit materials by April 30; the university will organize expert review and publicize the recommended list before reporting to the department |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

## Application Materials

1. Course application form (Attachment 4, three copies);
2. Complete course video discs (including video and lyric files);
3. Paper course metadata file (one copy);
4. Disc file including metadata, catalog and recommendation statement;
5. Electronic application sent to 09myzhou@stu.edu.cn.

Contact: Zhou Mengyi, tel 86502736. Standards and forms are in the OA attachments.
