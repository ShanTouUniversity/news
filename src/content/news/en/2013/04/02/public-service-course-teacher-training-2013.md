Public-service courses are an important part of STU’s talent-development reform. The university has opened its fourth public-service course teacher-training program and, from 2013, will count the training as continuing education for faculty. Faculty members who plan to teach a public-service course and graduate students who plan to serve as teaching assistants must attend the training; instructors qualify to teach after completing the training and taking on a course.

### Participants and requirements

- **Academic instructors**: Full-time faculty members with a graduate degree or a mid-level or higher professional title, an interest in public-service activities and experience leading students in planning and organizing activities;
- **Practice instructors**: Administrative or teaching-support staff with at least a bachelor’s degree, an interest in public-service activities and experience leading students in planning and organizing activities;
- **Graduate teaching assistants**: Since 2011, public-service course assistantships have been assigned to STU graduate students. Assistants may choose either one teaching-practice credit with certification from the Office of Academic Affairs or a subsidy of RMB 300 per course under the “Three Assistantships” standard.

Under STU’s public-service course implementation plan, full-time faculty members with fewer than 280 annual hours in specialized courses or 360 annual hours in general courses, as well as faculty members recruited within the past three years, are in principle required to teach a public-service course. As public-service courses transition from electives to required courses, schools and colleges should arrange for more faculty members to attend the training.

| April 17 | 2:30–5:30 p.m. and 7:30–9:20 p.m. | Experiential education; the vision and implementation of STU public-service courses; the mentor’s role; public-service project design and planning |

The training totals **14 class hours**, and the order of sessions may be adjusted. The listed instructors and discussion leaders include Yu Chen, Yinghui Cai, Qin Yao, Yuan Feng, Yichun Chen, Dongsheng Guan, Jun Xie, Zhigang Yan, Lanlan Xu, Yingfan Wu, Hong Yu and Run Wang.

Faculty members should register at `training.stu.edu.cn` by selecting “Specialized Courses > Office of Academic Affairs > Public-Service Course Teacher Training.” Graduate students should complete the registration form and email it to `gykc@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on April 12**.
