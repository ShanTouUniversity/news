The 19th Kangteng National University Student Business Case Competition has opened. Authorized by the Chinese Economics Annual Conference and organized or run by the Hubei Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, Wuhan University and the Kangteng Practice Center, the event is open to university students from mainland China, Hong Kong and Taiwan. It uses business cases to connect theory with practice and promote academic exchange. STU’s Business School is organizing student participation.

### Schedule

- **April 10**: Registration closes;
- **By 10:00 p.m. on April 14**: Electronic preliminary-round case texts due;
- **April 15**: National preliminary round;
- **April 24**: National semifinal;
- **7:00 p.m. on April 26**: National final.

Each team may have up to **five members**, including no more than one graduate student; the remaining members must be undergraduates. Each team may invite one faculty adviser. Students from all Business School cohorts may register. After forming a team, members should designate a captain to coordinate with the organizing committee and organize the team.

### Registration and submission

Teams should register through the Kangteng Practice Center website at <http://www.content.org.cn> between **March 23 and 10:00 p.m. on April 10**. Send the preliminary submission to `case19th@content.org.cn` by **10:00 p.m. on April 14**; submissions cannot be revised afterward. The required preliminary material is a business case analysis report, while research videos and photographs are optional. Follow the original competition rules attachment for detailed requirements.

### Awards

One grand prize carries RMB 2,000 per team and a provincial award certificate; one first prize carries RMB 1,000 per team; two second prizes carry RMB 500 per team; and four third prizes carry provincial award certificates. Several excellence awards and one Most Popular Team award will also be offered, with prizes as announced by the organizers.

For questions, email `10lironghua@gmail.com`. Consult the original OA notice for official websites, social-media accounts and other channels.
