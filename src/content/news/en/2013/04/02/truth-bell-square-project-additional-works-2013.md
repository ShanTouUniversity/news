During construction of the Truth Bell Square project at STU’s new main gate, the university proposes adding low-voltage conduits and utility pits to connect the area’s network, television and telephone fiber-optic trunks with the university’s external access. The work is intended to avoid reopening the road and damaging facilities in a later project.

The unit prices for the proposed additions have been reviewed by the university audit office. The estimated additional cost is approximately **RMB 244,390**, keeping the added cost within the university’s prescribed 10% limit. The university is publishing the proposed additional cost under its infrastructure tendering and bidding measures.

Written comments should be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office, the Key Projects Office of the Resource Management Office or the Project Coordination Department by **5:30 p.m. on April 7**.
