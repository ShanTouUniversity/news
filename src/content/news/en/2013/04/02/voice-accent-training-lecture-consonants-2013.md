The second lecture in the English Language Center’s 2013 Voice and Accent Training Lecture Series will be held from **2:15 to 3:45 p.m. on Wednesday, April 3** in E Lecture Room 101. Ravi Dutt will speak on “Sounds of English: Consonants.” The lecture is open to the entire university community.
