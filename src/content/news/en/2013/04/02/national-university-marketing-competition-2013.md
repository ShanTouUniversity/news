The **2013 Social Science Award National University Marketing Competition**, jointly organized by the Chinese Marketing Association of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, the National Education Examinations Authority and the China Association of International Trade’s SME cooperation body, has opened. STU’s Business Case Competition Steering Committee is organizing student participation.

The competition develops marketing practice, innovation, entrepreneurship and teamwork. Teams will work with real enterprise products on market research, marketing plans, sales and brand promotion. The competition lasts approximately **five to six months** and consists of university preliminaries, provincial or regional semifinals and a national final.

### Preliminary round and fees

The preliminary round includes a written examination and research planning. Students must take the 2013 first-half written examination for the competition and the Chinese Marketing Assistant (CMAT) qualification certificate; only those who pass may enter the later competition stages. Successful candidates may form teams of up to **five people**, complete market research and a report on a designated enterprise under faculty guidance, and take part in sales practice and business simulations.

- Competition registration: **RMB 20 per person**;
- Reference textbook: the new two-volume Chinese Marketing Assistant qualification textbook, **RMB 68 per person**;
- CMAT certification: successful examinees may apply for the Chinese Marketing Assistant qualification certificate jointly issued by the National Education Examinations Authority and the Chinese Marketing Association, as well as the American Marketing Assistant certificate issued by the American Marketing Management Association. The application fee is **RMB 340**. The certificate-application deadline is **June 25**; applicants who pay the certification fee before the written-examination registration deadline may have the written-examination registration fee waived.

Interested students should submit application materials as required by the Business School to `10lironghua@gmail.com` by **April 10**. Written-examination registration closes **April 20**. Follow the Business School and organizing committee for email-subject formatting, textbook and certification options, and the detailed registration process.

Competition website: <http://www.cmat.org.cn/web/index.html>. The original OA notice provides links to the written-examination notice, official social-media account and previous competitions. The title sponsor, final rules and awards had not yet been confirmed and will be announced by the organizing committee.
