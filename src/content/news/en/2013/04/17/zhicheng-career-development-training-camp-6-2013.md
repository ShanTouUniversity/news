To help students plan their careers, understand social needs and strengthen job-search skills, Shantou University&apos;s Zhicheng College will hold its sixth **Career-Development Training Camp** from **April 29 to 30, 2013**. The camp is primarily for second- and third-year students and will admit **40 participants**, including five places for students from other residential areas. If applications exceed the quota, participants will be selected by drawing lots.

### Camp content

- mock interviews, including role-play and leaderless group discussions;
- on-site résumé guidance and job-search techniques;
- experience sharing by outstanding graduating students on employment and graduate study;
- an alumni forum with a sales director and a human-resources manager; and
- an enterprise visit and field exchange.

### Application requirements

Applicants should be willing to learn and develop themselves, accept intensive training, value the opportunity and follow the activity arrangements. Participants must be free of other classes and activities throughout **April 29 and the morning of April 30**.

Students should email their applications to `ATP@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on April 23, 2013**, following Zhicheng College&apos;s required format and providing the requested grade, major and residential-area information. The registration form is authoritative for the full application requirements.
