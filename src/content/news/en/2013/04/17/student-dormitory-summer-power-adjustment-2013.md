Under STU&apos;s spring electricity-adjustment rules, the university will begin the summer power schedule early because the Shantou forecast indicated minimum temperatures of at least 20°C from April 16 to 18.

From **April 18, 2013**, residence halls A, B, C, D, E, F, G and J will have electricity all day under the summer schedule. This arrangement will continue through **October 31**, excluding the summer vacation period.

### Facility adjustment

The Logistics Office and Student Community Management Service Center will adjust electricity facilities in the eight residence halls from **3:00 to 6:00 p.m. on April 18**. Each hall is expected to experience an outage of about 15 minutes. A bell will sound 15 minutes before the outage so students can prepare.
