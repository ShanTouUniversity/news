Shantou University held its student-club activity project defense for the 2012–2013 spring term on **April 10**. Following panel review and approval by the STU Youth League Committee, the university granted project support to **11 student-club projects**.

The approved projects cover outdoor development, culture and arts, sports competitions, career development and financial learning. Projects that can be reliably identified in the attachment include:

- the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association&apos;s intermediate outdoor-development activity for a round-island hike on Nan&apos;ao Island;
- the STU Sports Dance Association&apos;s 25th-anniversary gala;
- the STU “243 Music Festival”;
- an activity by the Sign Language Club;
- an activity by the Photography Association;
- an activity by the Go Association;
- an activity by the Career Planning and Development Association;
- a “Traditional Culture” themed puzzle event;
- the eighth STU roller-skating competition; and
- a finance training camp.

Some project titles in the legacy XLS attachment are affected by encoding damage, and one or more titles cannot be identified reliably. This article does not infer missing titles or reproduce project-leader names.
