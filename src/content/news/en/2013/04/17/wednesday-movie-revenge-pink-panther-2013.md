Shantou University&apos;s Wednesday Movie Night will screen **Revenge of the Pink Panther** at the Library Theater from **7:00 to 10:00 p.m. on Wednesday, April 17, 2013**. All STU faculty and students are welcome.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *Revenge of the Pink Panther* |
| Release year | 1978 |
| Time | 7:00–10:00 p.m., Wednesday, April 17, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Theater |
| Seating | 150 seats, including 50 open to the public |
| Admission | Students enter with their campus cards; public seats are first come, first served |

The sixth film in the *Pink Panther* series stars Peter Sellers and was directed by Blake Edwards. It continues the comic adventures of Inspector Clouseau and was the last film in the series to feature Sellers.
