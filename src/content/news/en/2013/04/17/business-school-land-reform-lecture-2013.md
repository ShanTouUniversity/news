The Shantou University Business School will hold its fourth academic lecture of 2013 from **10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Monday, April 22**. Zhang Hudong will speak on **“Lessons from Traditional Institutional Resources for Current Rural Land Reform,”** with Professor Huang Yan as chair.

| Venue | Business School Academic Seminar Room, second floor of Wenxi |
| Participants | STU faculty and students |

### Lecture focus

The lecture will consider traditional China&apos;s economic achievements and land institutions, examining institutional factors alongside technology, markets and policy. It will revisit land transactions and land-rights allocation in traditional society, review four major changes in China&apos;s land institutions over the past century and discuss their effects on economic and social structures.

Drawing on rural land-reform experiments from the past decade, the lecture will explore how historical institutional resources may help explain current reform challenges. It will also argue that understanding present land issues requires renewed examination of past concepts, theories and basic assumptions.

### About the speaker

Zhang Hudong holds a doctorate in economics from Tsinghua University. He graduated from the STU Law School in 1999 and received a master&apos;s degree in political science from Fudan University in 2005. He has been a visiting scholar at the Catholic University of Leuven (K.U.Leuven) and The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

He has worked as a researcher at the Shanghai Institute of East Asian Studies, director of the research division of the Guangzhou Public Opinion Research Center and a contributing writer for the Southern Media Group. He also served as an assistant to the director of the Haidian District Statistics Bureau in Beijing. His research fields include public economics, institutional economics, surveys and statistics, and he currently focuses on China&apos;s land issues in collaborative research with the Development Research Center of the State Council.
