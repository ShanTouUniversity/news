Shantou University Library has opened a trial of the **Books24x7 IT and business e-book collections** through **June 9, 2013**. Faculty, staff and students are invited to use the collections and provide feedback.

Books24x7 provides electronic books in information technology and business. To enable its full personalized-service features, the system asks first-time users to set a personal username and password. Users should follow the library&apos;s guide for the setup process.
