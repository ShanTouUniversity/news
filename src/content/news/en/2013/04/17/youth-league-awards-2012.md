It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on April 17, 2013, to commend Outstanding Youth League Branches (General Branches), Outstanding Youth League Cadres and Outstanding Youth League Members for the year 2012 during the upcoming May Fourth period.

## Eligibility

| Category | Main Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Branches | Branch evaluation score ≥90 with no disciplinary cases; for General Branches, all affiliated branches ≥70 and 70% ≥90 |
| Outstanding Members | Uphold Party line and policies, observe league discipline and university regulations, no violations in two years, actively participate in league activities and play an exemplary role; student members must have good academic performance and no retakes or failures within a year |
| Outstanding Cadres | Meet all criteria for Outstanding Members, demonstrate diligence, strong organization and responsibility, innovation and solid work, with notable achievements; if serving as branch committee member, the branch score must be ≥90 |

## Quotas and Procedures

Quotas are set at about 10% of branches for Outstanding Branches (General Branches), 3% of registered members for Outstanding Members, and 5% of league cadres for Outstanding Cadres.

Units should guide branches to conduct truthful evaluations and recommend based on results: Branches (General Branches) are recommended by college Youth League Committees with a recommendation form and a ~1,000-word report; Outstanding Members are recommended democratically by branches and reviewed by college committees; Outstanding Cadres are recommended through democratic procedures coordinated by college committees. Graduate selections are reviewed by the Graduate School Youth League Committee, and affiliated hospitals follow the same principles via the Medical College Youth League Committee.

## Requirements

College committees and directly affiliated branches must uphold objectivity and fairness, strictly review candidates and publicize recommendations. Application forms (in duplicate) with supporting materials must be submitted to the Youth League Committee office by April 23. The committee will conduct a final review and commend the awardees around May Fourth.

> ⚠️ Application forms and attachments are available on the OA system.
