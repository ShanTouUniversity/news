The Ministry of Education has announced the first group of projects approved in 2013 under its **Hong Kong-Mainland University Faculty and Student Exchange Plan**. **Seven Shantou University projects** were approved, covering social practice, service learning, cultural preservation, entrepreneurship exchange and medical poverty-alleviation activities.

### Approved projects

| Project | Applying unit |
| --- | --- |
| Rural Service Practice | Zhicheng College |
| Enactus Practice Exchange | Business School |
| Preservation and Inheritance of Public-Art Culture in Shantou | School of Art and Design |
| “Connecting with the Rehabilitation Village” | Student Affairs Office and Youth League Committee |
| “Passing the Torch to Rural Communities” | Student Affairs Office and Youth League Committee |
| “Toward the Ocean” | Academic Affairs Office and School of Science |
| Medical Poverty Alleviation and Service Learning | Poverty Alleviation Office |

The Hong Kong-Mainland University Faculty and Student Exchange Plan is a Ministry of Education initiative to advance educational exchange between the two regions. Under the relevant statement of intent on deepening Hong Kong-mainland university education exchange and cooperation, the central government began providing dedicated funding in 2013. The plan supports cooperation between mainland and Hong Kong universities through annual project applications and grants.

Funding supports Hong Kong university faculty and students in coming to mainland China for study, internships, social practice, field visits, service and research. The plan is intended to help participants gain a deeper understanding of mainland China&apos;s political, economic, historical, cultural and social development, while strengthening exchange and cooperation between universities in Hong Kong and the mainland.
