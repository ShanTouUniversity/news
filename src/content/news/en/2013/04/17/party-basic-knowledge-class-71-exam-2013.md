The Shantou University Party School will hold the theory examination for **class 71 of its Party Basics course** from **7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, April 21, 2013**. Students from class 70 who have been approved for a deferred or make-up examination will also attend according to the arrangements.

### Examination halls

| Student group | Hall |
| --- | --- |
| School of Liberal Arts, Law School, Business School, School of Journalism, and Zhicheng College | Lecture Hall 1 |
| School of Science, School of Engineering, School of Art, Graduate School and faculty/staff participants | Lecture Hall 3 |

Students must attend the assigned hall and sit with one seat between examinees.

Students unable to attend for special reasons must obtain approval from their Party general branch or directly affiliated branch and complete deferral procedures at the Party School office. Unexcused absence will result in cancellation of student status in the class. Students who fail or receive an approved deferral may take one make-up examination in the next class only.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office)
