Following comments from faculty affected by relocation and taking teaching and working schedules into account, the Shantou University Resource Management Office will open the Yulanyuan and other courtyard homes in the west campus for eligible faculty to view before selecting housing.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Viewing locations | Yulanyuan and other courtyard homes, west campus |
| Dates | April 17 and 18, 2013 |
| Hours | 4:30–6:30 p.m. each day |
| Participants | Eligible faculty selecting housing |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
