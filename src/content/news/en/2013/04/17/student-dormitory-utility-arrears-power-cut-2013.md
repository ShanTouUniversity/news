As of **April 17, 2013**, some student residences had not fully paid utility bills for March or earlier. Under university rules, power to residences with arrears will be cut from **April 18** and restored after all outstanding charges have been paid.

The Network and Information Center will debit campus cards twice daily on April 18, 22 and 23. Restoration windows are as follows:

| Debit time | Power-restoration window for residences with successful payment |
| --- | --- |
| 10:30 a.m. | 2:30–4:30 p.m. the same day |
| 4:30 p.m. | 5:30–8:00 p.m. the same day |
| Payment after 4:30 p.m. | 2:30–4:30 p.m. the following day |

From **April 23 onward**, debits will be made once daily at 4:30 p.m., with restoration following the applicable service arrangement.

### Payment reminders

Students whose campus-card balance is insufficient should recharge it through a card-loading machine. Those with insufficient bank balances should deposit enough funds during banking hours and complete the card-loading process. If a campus card is lost, payment may be made by setting up delegated payment in the water-and-electricity management system or by making a cash payment to the university account at the Bank of China STU branch and submitting the receipt to Finance for posting. The Finance Office does not accept cash directly.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Residences subject to cut-off | Student residences with unpaid March or earlier utility fees as of April 17 |
| Cut-off begins | April 18 |
| Twice-daily debit dates | April 18, 22 and 23 |
| Debits after April 23 | Once daily at 4:30 p.m. |
| Restoration condition | All arrears paid and debit successfully completed |

This article does not identify residences with arrears or publish student names, individual balances or the university&apos;s receiving-account number. Payment records should be checked through the official systems of Finance, Student Affairs and Resource Management.
