The seventh Guangdong Student Science and Academic Festival Simulated Business Negotiation Contest has opened. Shantou University will conduct a campus selection and recommend one team to represent the university in the provincial competition. The contest theme is **“Gathering Youthful Dreams, Demonstrating Business Style,”** and the competition is scheduled for **May 2013**.

The contest is organized by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and Guangdong Students&apos; Federation. It is hosted by Guangdong Polytechnic Normal University, with China Mobile Guangdong and the university&apos;s School of Economics and Trade as co-organizers.

### Eligibility and teams

Participants must be full-time students at higher-education institutions in Guangdong. All majors are eligible. Participants should have a foundation in business administration, marketing or related subjects, together with communication, presentation, teamwork and independent-practice skills.

- Registration is by university, with one team allowed per institution;
- each team has four students and no substitutes;
- teams may assign roles such as planner, technical adviser and legal adviser, and may also register one team leader and one instructor; and
- individual registration is not accepted.

### Campus selection and provincial schedule

The STU campus deadline differs from the provincial submission deadline. Teams should follow the deadline for each stage:

| Stage | Time and requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Campus registration | Form a four-member team, complete the registration form and email it to the STU Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` by April 29 |
| Campus preliminary round | Each university holds its own preliminary round by May 10 and selects one team for the provincial competition |
| Preliminary works | Submit the electronic negotiation plan and a 5–10-minute DVD/PPT presentation to the organizing committee by May 10; each accounts for 50% of the assessment |
| Second-round list | The committee selects eight teams, with the list announced on May 15 |
| Provincial second round | May 23: eight teams compete in four on-site negotiations, with four advancing to the semifinals |
| Semifinals | Morning of May 24: four teams compete in two matchups, with two advancing to the final |
| Final | Afternoon of May 24: the two remaining teams compete for first and second place |

The provincial second round, semifinals and final will take place at the main campus of Guangdong Polytechnic Normal University. The organizing committee will provide the preliminary case. Teams must conduct preliminary research and explain their understanding of the case, analysis, negotiation strategy and goals, as well as their team structure and division of labor.

### Awards and conduct

The contest will offer multiple first-, second- and third-place awards and one “Best Negotiator” award. Participants must follow the organizers&apos; and moderator&apos;s arrangements, respect opponents and judges, dress appropriately for formal negotiations and communicate courteously. Disrupting the competition or deliberately creating a false negotiation may lead to disqualification from the contest and awards. Submitted works must be original and may not be plagiarized.

The organizing committee will provide the registration form, undertaking, schedule, competition process and scoring standards. The registration form requests student identity and contact information; the form and personal data are not reproduced here. Provincial materials should be submitted through the channels designated by the organizing committee, as specified in its notice.
