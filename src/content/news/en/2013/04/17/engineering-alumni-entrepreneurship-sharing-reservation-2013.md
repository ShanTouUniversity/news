The reservation period for the Shantou University Engineering School alumni face-to-face entrepreneurship session has ended, and the school has published the list of faculty and students with successful reservations. The event will take place at **7:30 p.m. on April 17, 2013**, in Science and Technology Lecture Hall 205. Students with successful reservations should arrive 10 minutes early.

All STU faculty and students are welcome to attend.
