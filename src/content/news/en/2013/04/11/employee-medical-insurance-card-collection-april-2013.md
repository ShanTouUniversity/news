Shantou University&apos;s Resource Management Office announced arrangements for collecting employee medical insurance handbooks and IC cards. The original notice concerned **13 employees**. The relevant employees may collect the materials during working hours from the employee medical insurance section on the second floor of the campus hospital.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | Employee medical insurance handbook and IC card |
| Location | Employee medical insurance section, second floor of the campus hospital |
| Service time | Working hours |

The names listed in the original notice are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ The original notice contains a collection list related to employees&apos; medical insurance coverage. The names are not reproduced here; relevant employees should follow the internal notice to complete collection.
