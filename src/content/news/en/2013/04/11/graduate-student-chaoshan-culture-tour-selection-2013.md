After registration for the Shantou University Graduate School&apos;s “Cultural Journey: Exploring Chaoshan Culture,” **55 graduate students** had signed up. The school selected **40 participants** on a first-come, first-served basis and announced the meeting arrangements.

The activity is provisionally scheduled for **Saturday, April 13, 2013**. Participants must assemble at the university motto monument at **8:15 a.m.** All participants must follow the school&apos;s arrangements and may not leave the group without permission. The school recommends wearing sportswear and bringing an umbrella, food and other necessities.

If weather conditions are unsuitable on Saturday, the activity will be postponed. Participants should watch for further notices. The selected-participant list contains student names and majors and is not reproduced here.
