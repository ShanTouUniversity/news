The results of the 2012–2013 Guangdong university Communist Youth League work research program have been announced. A total of **444 projects** were submitted by **73 universities** across the province, and expert review approved **150 projects**. **Three projects from Shantou University** were included among the approved projects.

### Approved STU projects

1. “An Empirical Study of the Psychological Health Education Function of University Student Organizations in Online Environments”
2. “The Core Socialist Values System and University Student Growth and Development: A Theoretical Perspective Based on the 18th National Congress”
3. “The Impact of Medical Poverty-Relief Activities on Professional Ethics Education for Medical Students”

The program aims to deepen understanding of the spirit of the 18th National Congress, advance university Communist Youth League work under new conditions and strengthen the theoretical research capacity of League officials. The project-team list contains personal names and is not reproduced here.
