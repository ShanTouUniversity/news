The Guangdong Department of Education and the China Scholarship Council have launched the 2013 local cooperation project for national government-sponsored study abroad. The project will select outstanding teaching and research staff employed by ordinary universities in Guangdong, excluding universities directly administered by ministries, for visiting study at renowned overseas universities and research institutions. The provincial education document sets a total quota of **50 participants**; the quotas for senior research scholars and visiting scholars will be determined according to applications.

### Scope and eligibility

The project prioritizes disciplines and fields identified in Guangdong&apos;s medium- and long-term talent development plan and its 12th Five-Year Plan for science and technology development, as well as fields in the humanities and social sciences.

Applicants should be outstanding in teaching or research, have a foundation of research and relevant results, and not have previously received funding from the China Scholarship Council. Applicants must contact an overseas supervisor and host institution independently, meet the foreign-language standards set by the Council, and satisfy basic health and other requirements.

Visiting-scholar applicants must hold at least an intermediate professional qualification, be under 45 and have worked at the recommending university for at least three years. Senior research scholar applicants must be under 50 and meet the basic requirements in the relevant China Scholarship Council selection guide. The online application guide states that senior research scholars may apply for 3–6 months, while visiting scholars and postdoctoral researchers generally apply for 6–12 months; participants in group-supported or local subprojects may apply for 3–12 months.

### Application and review

The selection process consists of individual application, institutional recommendation, expert review and merit-based admission. Applicants must complete online registration in the [National Government-Sponsored Study Abroad Information Management System](http://apply.csc.edu.cn) between **April 6 and April 25**; the system will close after the deadline. Applicants must then submit the application form, institutional recommendation form, and supporting documents including professional-title, education, award, overseas invitation, foreign-language and identity documents. Personal application materials are not reproduced here.

The Guangdong Department of Education will recommend candidates to the China Scholarship Council at 120% of the planned quota. The two bodies will then conduct a joint review combining document review and interviews before determining the final admissions list. The list is expected to be announced in **October**, when admission notices will be issued.

### University and provincial deadlines

After completing online registration, Shantou University applicants must submit written materials to Room 315 of the Administrative Center by **April 26** and send the electronic version to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. The university will review the submissions and forward recommended materials to the provincial education authority.

The provincial education document requires each university to submit an official letter and the applicants&apos; written materials to the Department of Education by **April 30**. The written materials should be prepared in three sets, each held together with paper clips rather than bound. Applicants should follow the university&apos;s final internal instructions for submission.

### Dispatch and administration

Before departure, admitted participants must use their institution-approved overseas invitation, translation and foreign-language qualification documents to complete the consent-to-departure procedures. They must also sign the *Agreement on Sponsored Study Abroad* with the China Scholarship Council and complete notarization, security-deposit and *International Travel Health Certificate* procedures. After departure, participants must follow the applicable national rules and accept administration by the education office or group at the relevant Chinese embassy or consulate.
