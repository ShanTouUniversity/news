The Shantou Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning Office has opened applications for its 2013 social science planning projects. Relevant units at Shantou University may develop proposals around Shantou&apos;s current priorities, including building a new platform for the Special Economic Zone, strengthening the central city of eastern Guangdong, upgrading industries, advancing Shantou–Chaozhou–Jieyang integration, and promoting ecological development.

### Research directions

Applicants may formulate their own titles under the following 20 directions:

1. Industrial clustering and transformation in Shantou
2. Enterprise-led innovation strategies
3. The Shantou Bay New Area and industrial development
4. Strategy for establishing an overseas Chinese economic and cultural cooperation pilot zone
5. Building a regional center for overseas Chinese asset management
6. Overseas Chinese communities and Shantou&apos;s economic and social development
7. Contemporary Teochew merchants and Shantou&apos;s development
8. Cooperation mechanisms for Shantou–Chaozhou–Jieyang integration
9. The eastern Guangdong city cluster in the context of integrated development
10. Improving urban management and environmental quality in Shantou
11. New urbanization throughout the Special Economic Zone
12. Improving urban and rural environments and building an ecological Special Economic Zone
13. Preservation and revitalization of historical sites from Shantou&apos;s port-opening period
14. Consolidating Shantou&apos;s position as a regional cultural center
15. The history and culture of overseas Chinese from the Chaoshan region
16. Teochew charitable organizations and innovative social governance
17. A multi-level social security system and the elderly-care and health industries
18. Improving healthcare and building a medical service center for eastern Guangdong
19. Shantou&apos;s market supervision framework and regulatory system
20. Standardized credit administration and a social credit system

### Eligibility and expected results

The project leader should hold a senior professional title. The main notice requires applicants without the relevant title to obtain written recommendations from two peer experts holding full senior professional titles. Each leader may apply for only one project and may not participate as a member of another application; each project team should have at least three members.

After approval, project leaders should organize research and drafting promptly. A final paper or research report must be submitted by **October 11, 2013**, with an electronic copy sent to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn`. The final research product must be a paper or research report of at least **10,000 Chinese characters**.

### Internal deadlines

Applicants should download and complete the *Shantou 2013 Social Science Project Application Form* and submit it to the Research Office for formal review by **May 8**. Qualified forms must be submitted to the Research Office by **May 13**, with an electronic copy sent to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn`. The main notice requires six A4, double-sided copies; the instructions in the attached application form require five copies. Applicants should confirm the applicable number with the Research Office before submission.

The application form requests personal information about project leaders and team members, including contact details, education, research history and previous results. The form and personal application materials are not reproduced here.

## Project Results

After Shantou announced the approved 2013 philosophy and social sciences planning projects, **eight STU projects** were approved, including **five funded projects**. STU had submitted 17 projects, representing 34% of all submissions in Shantou. The city approved 25 projects in total, including 13 funded projects, for a citywide funding rate of 52%. Eight of STU's 17 submissions were approved, and five of its eight approved projects were funded, a rate of 62.5% among STU's approved projects.

### Differences between the notice and form

The main notice requires two full-senior-title peer recommendations for leaders without the required senior title, while the attached form says that the two recommenders should hold associate-senior or higher titles. The two documents also differ on the number of printed copies, six versus five. Applicants should follow the latest requirements confirmed by the Research Office.
