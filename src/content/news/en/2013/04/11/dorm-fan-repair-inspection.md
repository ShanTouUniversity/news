It is reported that as summer approaches and temperatures climb, Shantou University expects a sharp rise in repair requests for dormitory fans. To prevent delays in repairs from disrupting students' study and daily life, the Student Community Service Center issued a reminder on April 11 urging students to inspect the fans in their dormitories as soon as possible and to submit repair requests through the online repair system (http://repair.stu.edu.cn) if any faults are found.

## Why an Early Check

The center explained that fan repairs take relatively long, and during peak periods some work orders cannot be completed on time. Checking and reporting issues early helps students avoid the rush and reduce waiting times.

## Safety Tips

| Situation | Action |
| --------- | ------ |
| Fan not turning smoothly | Stop using immediately and file an online repair request |
| Strange noises during operation | Stop using immediately and file an online repair request |
| Unusual smells | Stop using immediately and file an online repair request |

The center stressed that if any of these problems occur, students must stop using the fan right away and report it, so as to prevent fire or other accidents.
