Following arrangements by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University has opened applications for the 2013 Guangdong discipline-construction special fund for natural-science projects. The university will organize applications and select proposals for recommendation in technology innovation, young innovative talent development, research-platform construction, engineering research centers and international, Hong Kong or Macao, and Taiwan cooperation platforms.

### Project categories and requirements

| Category | Main requirements and funding | Internal quota |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology innovation | The leader should generally hold a senior professional title or doctorate and generally be no older than 50; projects should address scientific frontiers and the economic, social and technological needs of Guangdong; funding up to RMB 80,000 per project, with university matching funds of at least 1:1; normally completed within two years | 10 university-wide |
| Excellent Young Innovative Talent Development Plan | Applicants must not have received national or provincial/ministerial research funding, be under 35, hold at least a master&apos;s degree and have no higher than an associate-senior title; team members should generally be under 39; funding up to RMB 30,000 per project, with matching funds of at least 1:1; projects start in January 2014 and normally finish within two years | 4 university-wide |
| Rolling support for research platforms | Intended for key laboratories, engineering research or technology-development centers, and industry–university–research demonstration bases; platforms that have failed acceptance or projects inconsistent with platform directions or objectives are ineligible; funding up to RMB 500,000 per project, with matching funds of at least 1:1; each eligible platform may submit one project | One per eligible platform |
| Engineering research or technology-development center construction | A proposed center should have cooperated with at least two enterprises and completed at least two engineering R&D and commercialization projects in the previous three years, with actual research funding of at least RMB 2 million and at least 30% from horizontal sources; it should also have relevant patents, provincial or ministerial awards and a technical innovation team of at least 25 people; funding up to RMB 1 million per platform, with university matching funds of at least 1:1.5 | 1 university-wide |
| International, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan cooperation innovation platform | The applicant must have an established platform with at least one identified overseas partner and at least two years of cooperation; the leader should have an associate-senior or higher title or doctorate, at least five years of relevant experience and generally be no older than 55; funding up to RMB 500,000 per project, with the university and partner each providing matching funds of at least 1:1 | 1 university-wide |

The notice describes rolling support for research platforms as comprising “four categories,” but later lists only key laboratories, engineering research or technology-development centers, and industry–university–research demonstration bases. This discrepancy is retained from the source.

Projects that duplicate existing national or provincial funding, and applicants who are still responsible for unfinished Department of Education platforms or projects, must comply with the restrictions for each category. An engineering-center proposal may not duplicate a national, ministry or provincial platform already established in the same principal discipline.

### Internal submission process

1. Applicants must send materials to the relevant contact by **April 19** for university selection and recommendation.
2. Applicants selected for recommendation must log in to the [Guangdong Department of Education Research Digital Service Platform](http://202.116.224.17) with an account assigned by the Research Office and upload the application between **April 20 and 21**.
3. The Research Office will conduct online review on **April 22**.
4. Applicants must submit printed materials to the Research Office on **April 23**, printed double-sided on A4 paper in two copies.

The attached forms, except the general special-fund application form, are for reference during project development and should not be used as the final submission forms. Applicants should download the final versions from the platform&apos;s “Application Form Download” area after **April 13**.

The application forms request personal details, research histories and results of project leaders and team members. The forms and personal materials are not reproduced here.
