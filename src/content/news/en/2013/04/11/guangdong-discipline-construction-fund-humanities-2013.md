The Guangdong Department of Education has opened applications for the 2013 discipline-construction special fund for humanities and social sciences. Relevant units at Shantou University may submit proposals in research, education studies, journal development, policy consulting, research platforms and international cooperation. Applications are submitted online through the [Guangdong Department of Education project portal](http://202.116.224.17), which is open from **April 12 to April 25**.

### Categories and funding

| Category | Main requirements | STU internal quota | Maximum funding |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Major humanities and social sciences research | The leader should be an in-service employee with a full senior professional title; renowned scholars and cross-institutional or interdisciplinary collaboration are encouraged; leaders of unfinished Department of Education projects may not apply for new projects | 2 | RMB 300,000 per project, with university matching funds of at least 1:1 |
| General humanities and social sciences research | Address current social, economic, cultural and research-management issues; the research-management subcategory is for young and mid-career research-management staff, and the head of the research-management office should generally not apply | 4; one additional research-management project | RMB 30,000 per project, with university matching funds of at least 1:1 |
| Excellent Young Innovative Talent Development Plan, humanities and social sciences | Applicants must not have received national or provincial/ministerial research funding, must have been born after January 1, 1978, hold at least a master&apos;s degree and have no higher than an associate-senior title; team members should generally be under 39 | 5 | RMB 30,000 per project, with university matching funds of at least 1:1 |
| Major education research | The leader should hold a senior professional title and have academic standing, research foundations and sufficient time; each leader may submit one project, and leaders of unfinished key or major provincial education projects are ineligible | 1 | RMB 300,000 per project, with university matching funds of at least 1:1 |
| Key philosophy and social sciences laboratory construction | Must be based on a provincial key discipline and be a university-level research platform operating and open to the public for more than two years, with a stable team, fixed premises and sound research conditions | 1 | RMB 800,000 per platform, with university matching funds of at least 1:1 and at least RMB 300,000 in annual operating funds |
| Excellent philosophy and social sciences journal development | The journal must be publicly published by a Guangdong university with a unified serial publication number and must be or have been included in the CSSCI source-journal list or its extended list, or in the Peking University core-journal list | 1 | RMB 150,000 per project, with university matching funds of at least 1:1 |
| Policy consulting | The leader should generally hold a senior professional title and have practical experience; the project should address current higher-education and social-development issues, be organized by a relevant research association and have an association recommendation letter | Not specified | RMB 300,000 per project |
| International, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan cooperation innovation platform | Must cooperate with at least one recognized overseas university, research institution or enterprise for at least two years and submit a formal Chinese and foreign-language agreement; the leader should hold an associate-senior or higher title or doctorate, have at least five years of relevant experience and generally be no older than 55 | 1 | RMB 500,000 per project, with the university and partner each providing matching funds of at least 1:1 |

Major research projects should formulate their own titles within the application-guide framework, with clear objectives and priorities and results of academic and social significance. General projects emphasize theoretical advancement in basic research and social service in applied research. Young-talent projects start in January 2014 and normally last two years; major education projects normally last no more than three years; laboratory construction normally lasts three years; and policy-consulting projects last no more than two years, with research reports as the main result.

### Application requirements

Applicants must submit one application or bid document to the Research Office for formal review by **April 17**. By **April 24**, they must complete online uploading and submit double-sided A4 printed materials to the Research Office. Major research, general research, young-talent, major education and international cooperation projects generally require two copies each of the category-specific form and the general discipline-construction special-fund form. Key laboratory and excellent-journal projects require six copies of the category-specific form; applicants should follow the relevant materials list for final quantities.

The online process requires applicants to obtain an account and the relevant form from the project portal, complete it offline, check the contents, set password protection and upload the protected document. Applicants must use the prescribed format and must not delete or alter the required report text.

### Key project notes

A key laboratory should address frontiers in philosophy and social sciences and important areas of Guangdong&apos;s economic and social development, support innovative-talent development, and maintain advanced, open equipment and research methods. An international cooperation platform must submit a specific project together with the platform proposal. The agreement must state the foreign contribution, intellectual-property ownership, cooperation model, research content and expected objectives, and include at least one overseas team member; email or letter confirmations cannot replace a formal agreement.

Policy consulting projects should be practice-oriented and support decision-making, with clear policy recommendations, likely policy impacts and timely results. International cooperation proposals must also be prudent, controllable and supported by formal agreements and continuing contributions from both sides.
