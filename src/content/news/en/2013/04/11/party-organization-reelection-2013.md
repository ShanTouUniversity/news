Shantou University has launched the reelection of its Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches. The terms of the university&apos;s Party general branches and directly affiliated branches, elected in January 2006, have expired. The Standing Committee of the University Party Committee approved the reelection in accordance with the Party Constitution.

### Grassroots Party organizations

The university will have 12 Party general branches: the College of Liberal Arts, College of Science, School of Engineering, Law School, Business School, School of Arts and Design, School of Journalism and Communication, Graduate School, Zhicheng College, university offices, Resource Management Office and retired faculty and staff.

It will also have eight directly affiliated Party branches: the Department of Social Sciences Teaching and Research, Department of Physical Education Teaching and Research, School of Continuing Education, Research Office, Library, Affiliated Middle and Primary Schools, Student Residence Areas and Students with Deferred Employment.

### Committee composition

Committee size will be determined by the number of Party members. A general-branch committee with at least 50 members will have 5–7 committee members and one secretary or deputy secretary; a general branch with fewer than 50 members will have 3–5 committee members and one secretary or deputy secretary. The retired faculty and staff general branch will have one serving employee as secretary, one serving employee as deputy secretary and one retired employee as deputy secretary.

A directly affiliated branch with at least seven members will have a three-person committee consisting of one secretary and two committee members. A directly affiliated branch with fewer than seven members will have only one secretary.

### Schedule

| Time | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Early April | The University Party Committee issues the documents, holds a meeting for secretaries of general and directly affiliated branches, and deploys the work |
| By April 15 | Each branch submits a request for reelection to the University Party Committee and begins preparations after approval |
| April 16 to May 5 | Discusses and recommends preliminary candidates for committee members, secretaries and deputy secretaries, then submits them for review and approval |
| May 6 to 25 | Holds formal elections; committee members are elected with competitive candidacies, while secretary and deputy-secretary candidates are elected without competition, all by secret ballot |
| By the end of May | Submits election results to the University Party Committee for approval |

The University Party Committee requires grassroots organizations to use the reelection process to organize Party members&apos; study of the spirit of the 18th National Congress, the Party Constitution and relevant Party documents, and to follow internal rules strictly. Relevant Party Committee offices will provide guidance, inspection and supervision to support completion of the reelection.
