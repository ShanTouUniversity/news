The Guangdong Department of Education has opened applications for 2013 Pearl River Scholar positions at universities. Units at Shantou University may propose Pearl River Scholar positions and recommend candidates according to their discipline-development needs. Materials must be submitted to Room 315 of the New Administrative Building by **April 18**, with an electronic copy sent to `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn`.

### Position types and scope

The Pearl River Scholar program has two position types: **Distinguished Professor** and **Chair Professor**. It follows a system of needs-based positions, open recruitment, expert review, merit-based appointment, strict assessment and dynamic management. Positions are generally established in provincial or higher-level key disciplines, key laboratories, engineering research centers, key humanities and social sciences research bases, and emerging interdisciplinary fields with significant breakthrough potential. Each position may appoint one Distinguished Professor or Chair Professor.

Distinguished Professors are expected to teach core courses, supervise doctoral and master&apos;s students, lead major frontier research and build academic teams. Chair Professors are expected to offer advanced courses or lectures, conduct collaborative research, advise on discipline development and help build innovative teams.

### Eligibility

A Distinguished Professor candidate should generally hold a doctorate, be under 45 and work at the front line of teaching and research. The candidate should have a strong academic record, organizational and team-leadership abilities, and be able to work full-time during the appointment. Domestic candidates should generally work at a recognized university or high-level research institution, hold a full senior professional title and supervise doctoral students; overseas candidates should generally hold an associate-professor or assistant-professor position, or an equivalent position, at a high-level university.

A Chair Professor candidate should work at the front line of teaching and research and have substantial academic influence and achievements recognized by peers. Domestic candidates should generally hold a full senior professional title and supervise doctoral students; overseas candidates should generally hold an associate-professor or assistant-professor position, or an equivalent position, at a high-level university, and work at the appointing university for at least three months each year during the appointment. Candidates for both types should demonstrate strong academic ethics and be in good health.

### Appointment and support

Distinguished Professor appointments last five years, divided into an initial three-year term and a two-year renewal term. Chair Professor appointments last no more than three academic years. Distinguished Professors receive a provincial allowance of **RMB 120,000 per year**, while Chair Professors receive **RMB 15,000 per month** for the actual months worked.

The provincial department also provides research funding aligned with the appointed discipline: **RMB 300,000–2 million** for natural-science Distinguished Professor projects and **RMB 100,000–500,000** for humanities and social sciences projects. The university must provide matching funds at a ratio of at least 1:1. The appointing university should support the scholar in building an academic team and provide suitable working and living conditions.

### Review and assessment

The university should investigate and evaluate candidates, obtain written assessments from at least three well-known external peers, and form a comprehensive recommendation through its academic committee. The recommended candidate&apos;s materials must be publicized for 10 working days. After provincial review, the proposed appointees will be publicized for another 10 working days before the university signs an appointment contract with candidates without objections.

The university must establish annual reporting and appointment-term assessment procedures. A Distinguished Professor whose initial-term assessment is satisfactory may be renewed for two years; an unsatisfactory result leads to no renewal. Fraud, academic misconduct or failure to meet the required time on site may result in withdrawal of the title and allowance and termination of the appointment contract.

### Submission materials

Units should prepare the position application, candidate application, discipline classification materials and supporting documents required by the provincial notice. Candidate materials contain personal information including education, employment history, research projects, publications, patents, awards and contact details and are not reproduced here.

The university notice requires the materials and attachments in **two copies** by April 18. The instructions for the *Guangdong University Pearl River Scholar Application Form* require the form in **10 copies**, together with supporting evidence of achievements, for submission to the provincial education authority. Because the two documents differ on the number of copies, units should confirm the final requirement with the Human Resources Office.
