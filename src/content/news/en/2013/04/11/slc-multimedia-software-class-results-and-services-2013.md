After registration for the Shantou University Student Learning Center (SLC) beginner multimedia software class, **20 students** were successfully registered. The class will meet from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on Sundays during weeks 8–11 in the School of Arts computer lab, on April 14, April 21, April 28 and May 5. The registration list is included in the original OA attachment and is not reproduced here.

Students who were not admitted or who need more advanced multimedia software instruction may visit the SLC directly or use the [Reservation Center](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/) to book a consultation with a peer tutor.

### Multimedia software services

The SLC main center also offers fixed and appointment-based services. Fixed services require neither advance reservation nor registration; users may go directly to the SLC front desk to sign in. Appointment-based services must be booked through the Reservation Center at least 20 hours in advance.

| Software or course | Service mode | Service time or appointment slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photoshop image processing | Fixed service | Tuesdays, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Illustrator graphic design | Appointment | Mondays, 3:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Photoshop image processing | Appointment | Fridays, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Poster design | Appointment | Tuesdays, 3:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Poster design | Fixed service | Wednesdays, 3:30–5:30 p.m. |
| Illustrator graphic design | Appointment | Wednesdays, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| InDesign layout design | Appointment | Fridays, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
