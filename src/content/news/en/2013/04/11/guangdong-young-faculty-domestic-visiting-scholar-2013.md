The Guangdong Departments of Education and Finance are implementing a domestic visiting-scholar plan for young and mid-career university faculty. Starting in 2013, the plan will send in-service faculty from universities in Guangdong to leading universities or research institutions in China for further study. According to the Shantou University Human Resources Office notice, the university plans to recommend **8–10 teachers** this year.

The plan runs from 2013 to 2016. The provincial “Strong Teacher Project” special fund will support approximately **400 young and mid-career teachers** each year to spend one year at universities in the “985 Project” or “211 Project” or at high-level research institutions. Universities will also organize shorter domestic visiting and training opportunities.

### Recommendation requirements

Applicants should follow the code of professional ethics for university teachers, have a strong sense of responsibility and good professional conduct, and generally have at least five years of teaching or research experience in higher education. They should have a solid professional foundation, strong teaching and research abilities, good teaching evaluations and the potential to become academic leaders or key faculty members. University-level candidates in the “Thousand-Hundred-Ten Talent Development Project” receive priority.

Applicants should generally hold at least an intermediate professional qualification and a master&apos;s degree, be no older than 45 and be in good health. Teachers with excellent professional ethics or outstanding innovative ability may, under the implementation plan, be exempted from some requirements concerning years of experience, professional title or academic degree.

### Study arrangements and funding

Host institutions generally include key universities in the “985 Project” or “211 Project,” high-level research institutions and nationally exemplary higher vocational colleges. Priority is given to advantageous disciplines such as national key disciplines, key laboratories, engineering research centers, key humanities and social sciences research bases and postdoctoral research stations. The plan may extend the discipline requirement to doctoral programs for English, arts, physical education and ethnic studies.

Visiting scholars will develop a study plan with a host-school supervisor. Their work will focus on research, while they may also assist with graduate supervision, participate in teaching or undertake other educational work. The provincial subsidy is **RMB 25,000 per person per year**, administered by the sending university. During the visit, training fees, accommodation and two round trips may be reimbursed according to the applicable rules. Universities may not reduce their existing faculty-development budgets because of this subsidy.

### Application and recommendation

Schools should recommend teachers who urgently need training and show development potential, based on their faculty-development plans. Applicants may contact potential supervisors at host institutions in advance. Host universities, disciplines and supervisors can be searched in the “Domestic Visiting Scholars” section of the website of the [Wuhan Center for the Training and Exchange of University Faculty](http://train.whu.edu.cn/).

Recommended candidates must complete the *Recommendation Form for Young Key University Faculty as Domestic Visiting Scholars (Guangdong Project)* in three copies. After review and stamping by the school or department, the forms must be submitted to Room 315 of the Administrative Center by **April 18**, with an electronic copy sent to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`.

The provincial document requires universities to submit recommended names and materials to the Department of Education by **April 22**. STU applicants should therefore complete their school-level submission by April 18; the university will handle the subsequent provincial submission.

Under the provincial schedule, the proposed subsidy list will be publicized for seven days, the final list will be announced in July, supported visiting scholars will report to their host universities in September with admission notices, and funding will be allocated in October according to actual registrations. Host universities will conduct completion assessments, and successful participants will receive a Ministry of Education *Domestic Visiting Scholar Certificate*. The sending university will also assess fulfillment of the training agreement.
