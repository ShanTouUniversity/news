Shantou University received **43 applications** for its introductory outdoor activity at Shuzhai Cave in Sangpushan and selected **20 students** by drawing. Selected participants must attend the activity briefing and theory training.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | Introductory outdoor activity at Shuzhai Cave, Sangpushan |
| Applications received | 43 |
| Selected participants | 20 students |
| Briefing and theory training | Saturday, April 13, 2013, 7:00 a.m. |
| Venue | Multimedia Classroom 726 |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office and STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |

The notice and its attachment contain the selected students&apos; names and class information. The personal list is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office and STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association)

> ⚠️ The selected-participant list contains student names and class information. Please contact the Student Affairs Office or Snow Leopard Outdoor Association for individual arrangements; the list is not reproduced here.
