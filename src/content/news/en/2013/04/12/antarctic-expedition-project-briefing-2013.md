Shantou University will hold a briefing for faculty, staff and students shortlisted in the physical-test selection for the 2014 Antarctic expedition project. Hong Kong Wild Direction Co., Ltd., the project partner, will introduce the research vessel and detailed itinerary for the Antarctic trip, helping participants revise and improve their expedition plans.

### Briefing details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Wednesday, April 17, 2013, 3:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Lecture Room 101, E Building |
| Presenters | Chen Jiajun and Li Minting of Hong Kong Wild Direction Co., Ltd. |
| Participants | Faculty, staff and students shortlisted in the Antarctic project physical-test selection |
| Leave-request email | `expedition@stu.edu.cn` |
| Leave-request deadline | Before 5:00 p.m. on April 16 |

After the Nan&apos;ao training-camp selection, people who enter the final interview will need to submit revised and improved expedition plans. The topic of the plan will remain unchanged. Anyone unable to attend should send a leave request by email before the deadline.

The attachment is a shortlist for the physical-test selection and contains personal information about students and employees. The list and related personal data are not reproduced here.
