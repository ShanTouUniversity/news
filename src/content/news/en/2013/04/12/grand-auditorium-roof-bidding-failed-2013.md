It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office issued a notice on April 12, 2013, that the bidding for the Grand Auditorium roof steel plate replacement and maintenance project was declared unsuccessful.

The project was publicly tendered and opened on March 29 at Shantou University. Upon review, some bidders made major pricing errors, such as under-reporting and omitting items, rendering their bids invalid. With fewer than three qualified bidders, the tender did not meet relevant bidding regulations and was handled as an unsuccessful bidding.
