The finals of Shantou University&apos;s seventh esports competition and the awards ceremony for the sixth Network Culture Festival have been rescheduled because of weather and venue conditions. The event will now take place on **April 14** at the east entrance of the New Administrative Center. In addition to the esports finals, mobile-game challenges will be open to the university community.

### Mobile-game activities

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
