Shantou University Library has opened a trial of the **ProQuest Dissertations and Theses database (PQDT)** through **May 8, 2013**. The database contains high-quality master&apos;s and doctoral dissertations, mainly from more than 2,000 universities in Europe and North America, across the humanities, sciences, engineering, agriculture, medicine and other fields.

### Resource details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Database | ProQuest Dissertations and Theses (PQDT) |
| Trial period | From the notice date through May 8, 2013 |
| Access | [pqdt.calis.edu.cn](http://pqdt.calis.edu.cn/Default.aspx) |
| Library database page | [lib.stu.edu.cn](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=364) |
| Coverage | Dissertations from more than 2,000 recognized universities, mainly in Europe and North America |
| Collection size | More than 405,000 dissertations |
| Fields | Humanities, sciences, engineering, agriculture, medicine and others |

The database is built through collaborative selection and purchase by Chinese university and institutional libraries. Readers at participating institutions share the acquired resources. The notice states that the trial provides access to basic information such as authors and institutions and to abstracts; actual full-text availability depends on the access scope shown on the trial page.
