Shantou University&apos;s School of Engineering has opened applications for its 2013 extracurricular student technology innovation projects. The program is open to current engineering students and is designed around the school&apos;s EIP-CDIO engineering-education reform, developing project development, design and implementation skills as well as innovation, communication, leadership and teamwork.

### Application details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Current students of the School of Engineering |
| Application period | April 12 to May 3, 2013 |
| Project period | Normally six months to one year |
| Printed materials | Three copies each of the project application and development agreement |
| Electronic material | Electronic project application |
| Email | `11lzyu@stu.edu.cn` |

Each student may generally participate in only one project. Students who abandoned a previously approved project without justification may not apply. Cross-disciplinary and cross-year undergraduate teams are encouraged. Project leaders must complete the application and submit the printed application, agreement and electronic application to their class monitor by **10:00 a.m. on May 3**. Class monitors will consolidate the materials and forward them to their department-level Party branches. Those branches must deliver the printed materials to the School of Engineering Youth League Office by **4:00 p.m. on May 3** and send the electronic version by email.

### Suggested topics

The attachments provide suggested topics by discipline; applicants may also design other projects meeting the program requirements:

- **Mechanical innovation**: Under the theme “Dream Classroom,” design and build classroom equipment or teaching aids that make lessons richer and more engaging.
- **Energy conservation and environmental protection**: Design and manufacture low-cost, environmentally friendly machinery while addressing resource and environmental issues.
- **Architectural design**: Use building information modeling and collaborative design under the theme “Building an Architectural Stage Together, Opening a Professional Life.”
- **Software applications and development**: Work on website design, database applications and scientific computing.
- **Computer music, digital media and software services**: Develop innovative work in computer music, digital-media design and software and service outsourcing.
- **Electronic engineering**: Suggested projects include a free-swinging-platform control system, an intelligent car, a simple digital-signal transmission performance analyzer, a simple automatic resistance tester, and a waveform acquisition, storage and playback system.

### Project management

The application asks for the research significance, content, implementation plan, budget, project members and supervising teacher. Applicants may also request access to the EIP-CDIO Innovation Practice Center laboratory. After approval, the Youth League and relevant departments will coordinate research conditions and provide support with laboratory access and reference materials.

Project authors must complete the work as planned and may not change the project content arbitrarily or discontinue it without justification. A project that must be terminated or redirected requires a written request to the University Youth League, followed by approval. Misuse of project funds or deliberate failure to complete the project as planned may lead to contractual liability and repayment of the relevant funds.

The application and agreement contain personal information about students, supervising teachers and project members. Specific forms, rosters and application materials are not reproduced here.
