Shantou University has adjusted the electricity schedule for undergraduate residences. Based on the local Shantou weather forecast, the university will switch to the summer schedule early when spring temperatures rise significantly, supporting students&apos; rest and use of fans and other daily facilities.

### Electricity schedule

| Situation | Service hours |
| --- | --- |
| Summer, May 1 to October 31 | Electricity available all day |
| Winter, November 1 to April 30 of the following year | 6:00 a.m.–11:30 p.m. |
| Early warm weather in spring | If the forecast shows minimum temperatures of at least 20°C for two consecutive days, the summer all-day schedule will begin early |

The schedule was issued by the Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office and applies from spring 2013.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office)
