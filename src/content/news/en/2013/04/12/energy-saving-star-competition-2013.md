It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Green Ribbon Service-Learning Environmental Volunteer Team will hold the “Advocating Thrift, Building a Harmonious Campus” Energy-Saving Star competition from April 12 to June 3, 2013, to cultivate electricity-saving habits.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Advocating Thrift, Building a Harmonious Campus |
| Period | April 12–June 3, 2013 |
| Eligibility | By dormitory |
| Evaluation | Based on March electricity consumption, with power-saving rankings posted in early May and early June |
| Formula | Total saving rate = (March – April)/March + (March – May)/March; the 10 dormitories with the highest total rate will be awarded |
| Award | Plaques and prizes |

## Registration

| Method | Deadline | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | 22:00, April 17 | Download and complete the application form and send to the designated email addresses |
| On-site | 11:00–13:00, April 13 | On-site registration during the Jin San Jiao interactive activity |
