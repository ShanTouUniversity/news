Shantou University will continue providing free alumni cards to graduating students. The card allows alumni to enter the campus, visit and study at the Library, and receive relevant offers from alumni-owned businesses.

### Arrangements

Each college&apos;s alumni office will collect the information by graduating class and prepare the *2013 Graduate Alumni Card Registration Form* and electronic ID photographs. The materials must be sent to `o_xyh@stu.edu.cn` by **May 10**. Cards are expected to be distributed in **mid-June**; the exact collection time will be announced separately.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible group | 2013 graduating class |
| Fee | Free |
| Collection method | Consolidated by graduating class through each college |
| Submission deadline | May 10, 2013 |
| Submission email | `o_xyh@stu.edu.cn` |
| Expected distribution | Mid-June; exact time to be announced |

The registration form and photographs contain graduates&apos; names, student numbers and other personal information. These files and personal data are not reproduced here.
