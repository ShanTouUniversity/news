The Party general branch of Shantou University&apos;s Zhicheng College will take new Party members to an educational activity at the Shenxi Patriotic Education Base in Chaonan District. The program will include an oath ceremony for new members, a renewal of the oath by existing members, a visit to the memorial and discussion to strengthen responsibility and communication among the college&apos;s Party branches.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Date | April 14, 2013 |
| Assembly time | 8:20 a.m. |
| Assembly point | Zhicheng College lobby |
| Activity venue | Shenxi Patriotic Education Base, Chaonan District, at the Martyrs Memorial Monument |
| Participants | Newly admitted Party members of Zhicheng College this year, together with existing members taking part in the oath renewal |
| Theme | Remembering martyrs, recognizing responsibility and strengthening Party cultivation |

The activity will also use current issues as a basis for discussion, helping participants understand the university&apos;s educational goals of commitment, knowledge, perseverance and achievement, while strengthening social responsibility and the cohesion of the college Party organization.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party General Branch Committee of Zhicheng College, Shantou University)
