Under the *STU Course Assessment Management Measures* and the 2012–2013 academic calendar, Shantou University will schedule final assessments for the spring semester in **Weeks 17 and 18**. The Academic Affairs Office asks teaching units to report course-assessment information so that examination times and venues can be coordinated.

### Assessment arrangements

Courses scheduled to meet through Week 15 or later will have their final assessments arranged in Weeks 17 and 18. Other courses must be entered on the *STU Final Assessment Report*. The teaching unit of the course instructor is responsible for reporting examination courses, and academic administrators must verify course offerings and class numbers to avoid omissions or errors.

The Department of Social Sciences Teaching and Research, Computer Teaching Center and Institute of Higher Education, together with courses such as General Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Calculus, and Probability and Statistics, will have examination times and venues arranged centrally by the Academic Affairs Office. Academic administrators in the instructor&apos;s teaching unit will arrange other courses in the credit-based system.

### Reporting requirements

The assessment method should reflect the course and be recorded as an open-book examination, closed-book examination, oral examination, course paper or a combination of these methods. The normal examination duration is **120 minutes**. When a course cannot be completed within 120 minutes, the instructor must request approval from the teaching-unit head; after approval, the academic administrator records the required duration. Instructors may not lengthen or shorten an examination without approval.

The Academic Affairs Office and teaching-unit academic administrators will jointly complete the examination schedule in the credit-based system. After the report is completed and approved with the unit head&apos;s signature, each teaching unit must submit it to the Academic Affairs Office Student Affairs Section by **April 18, 2013, Thursday of Week 8**, and email the electronic version to `xhx@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)

> ⚠️ The final-assessment report is an internal teaching-management document. Final courses, class numbers, examination times and venues are subject to the schedule ultimately issued by the Academic Affairs Office and teaching units.
