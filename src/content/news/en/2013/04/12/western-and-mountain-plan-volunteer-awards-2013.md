Shantou University has decided to reward two graduates who joined the **2012 College Student Volunteer Service Western Plan and Mountain Plan**. The volunteers traveled in July 2012 to Nyingchi, Tibet, and Haifeng County, Shanwei, respectively. During their service, they completed their assigned work and also devoted time to charitable activities.

Under the university&apos;s relevant award provisions, the university will provide:

| Volunteer | Program and service location | Award |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Huang HY (Chinese Language and Literature, College of Liberal Arts) | Western Plan, Nyingchi, Tibet | RMB 6,000 |
| Li F (Applied Physics, College of Science) | Mountain Plan, Haifeng County, Shanwei | RMB 3,000 |

The university recognized the graduates&apos; commitment, resilience and spirit of service, and encouraged them to continue learning and improving while supporting local education, poverty alleviation and other social initiatives during their service.
