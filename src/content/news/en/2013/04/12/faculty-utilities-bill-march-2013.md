Shantou University has published the utilities charges for faculty and staff for **March 2013**. The current meter readings were taken on **April 1 and 2**, following the previous readings on **March 1**. Faculty and staff may log in to the [STU utilities management system](http://power.stu.edu.cn) to review their individual charge details.

### Verification and payment

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | March 2013 |
| Current readings | April 1 and 2 |
| Previous readings | March 1 |
| Query method | Log in to the utilities management system |
| Dispute period | April 12–17 |
| Verification location | Utilities Center |
| Telephone | 2489 |

Questions or objections should be checked at the Utilities Center between **April 12 and 17**. After the verification period, charges without objections will be deducted according to the stated procedure from April 2013 salary accounts. Faculty and staff without salary accounts must pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ The utilities management system uses individual campus-network accounts. Personal bills, account details and salary deductions are not reproduced here; please use the official system and follow the notice.
