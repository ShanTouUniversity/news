Shantou University units may log in to the telephone management system to verify electronic office telephone bills incurred from **March 1 to March 31, 2013**. China Telecom Shantou sent the bills to the university telephone office, which has imported the data into the system for unit reviewers to inspect.

### Verification and deduction schedule

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| From the notice date | Unit reviewers log in to the [telephone management system](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) and review bill details |
| After verification | Click the system confirmation command for the selected bills |
| By April 24 | Email discrepancies between bill details and actual use |
| April 25 | The Finance Office deducts charges using each unit&apos;s designated funding account number |

Units that find discrepancies must email `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` by **April 24** so that the information can be consolidated and sent to China Telecom. At the end of the verification period, bills for which no objection has been raised and no confirmation action has been taken will be treated by the system as confirmed.

Questions or suggestions about the telephone management system may also be sent to the same address.
