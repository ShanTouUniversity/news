STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has reported its study-style inspection for Week 5 of the spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. The inspection team checked E Building on **March 29**:

- At **8:00 a.m.**, one student was found to have arrived up to five minutes late for class;
- At **2:00 p.m.**, four students were found to have arrived up to five minutes late.

The inspection recorded five late-arrival cases. The original OA attachment contains the student list, which is not reproduced here. Schools and colleges will provide corrective education and continue strengthening study-style development and classroom order.

> Source: STU “Three Styles” Development Office
>
> ⚠️ The attachment contains student names and other personal information. This article reports only aggregate figures and does not reproduce the list.
