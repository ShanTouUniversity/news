It is reported that the Business School issued a notice on April 1, 2013, to launch selection for its 2013 “3+2” dual-degree/master’s and exchange programs with Dublin City University (DCU), Ireland.

## Programs

| Program | Places | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3+2 Dual-degree/Master’s | 10 | Complete three years at STU and meet English requirements to transfer to DCU for the final undergraduate year; DCU recognizes STU credits, graduates receive dual bachelor’s degrees; those meeting master’s requirements may continue for one year to earn a DCU master’s degree |
| Exchange (non-degree) | 5 | Study at DCU Business School, requiring at least one year of study at the home university and meeting partner admission criteria |

## Selection

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Full-time undergraduates of the Business School, Classes of 2010 and 2011 |
| Academic | GPA ≥3.0; priority for those who have not previously joined university or college exchange programs |
| English – 3+2 | IELTS ≥6.5 (Writing and Speaking ≥6.5, Listening and Reading ≥6) or TOEFL ≥92 (each section ≥21) |
| English – Exchange | Provide CET-4/6 or IELTS/TOEFL certificate at application; IELTS ≥6 or TOEFL ≥80 required for visa (per Irish Embassy) |
| Costs | Exchange: DCU tuition waived; 3+2: 15% reduction on DCU non-EU tuition; other expenses (travel, living, accommodation, books, insurance) about 700–900 euros/month (approx. 6,000–8,000 yuan) borne by students |
| Deadline | 12:00, May 20, 2013 |
| Materials | Application form, English proficiency certificates, academic transcript, and a 300–500-word English essay on purpose and overseas study plan |
| Submission | Hard copy and electronic version to the International Projects Office, Wenxi 211, Business School, electronic to dnye@stu.edu.cn (Ms. Ye Dongni) |
| Process | Document screening → interview on language, communication and overall quality → final candidates recommended to partner university |
| Contact | Ms. Ye Dongni, tel. 86503382, email dnye@stu.edu.cn |

For non-EU students, undergraduate tuition at DCU is about 11,300 euros/year and master’s about 12,800–17,000 euros/year, see http://www.dcu.ie/finance/fees/schedule.shtml. The application form is available in the OA attachment.
