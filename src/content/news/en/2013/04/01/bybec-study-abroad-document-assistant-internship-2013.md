Shenzhen Boyang Information Consulting Co. is recruiting **two undergraduate interns** as study-abroad documentation assistants. The role supports consultants with university research and analysis, client-background communication, preliminary study-abroad planning, and translation, organization and proofreading of application materials. It also assists with follow-up arrangements such as visas, flights and overseas accommodation.

### Requirements

Applicants may be from any major and should have strong English reading and writing skills and have passed CET-6. TEM-8 qualification and experience with IELTS, TOEFL, GRE or GMAT are preferred. Applicants should also have English-writing, learning and communication skills, be familiar with Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Photoshop, be interested in education and be able to handle pressure.

### Internship terms

The role emphasizes practical skills and experience and offers a **base salary of RMB 1,000 per month, performance commission and personal health insurance**. The summer internship is full-time, five days a week, with flexible working hours and location. BYBEC is an officially recognized STU student internship base, and interns will work with an overseas-education consulting team.

Send an English résumé to `hr@bybec.com` by **April 30**. Confirm the employer’s location and detailed terms with the recruiter.
