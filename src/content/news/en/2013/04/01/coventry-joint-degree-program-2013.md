Shantou University and Coventry University in the United Kingdom have opened applications for a joint-degree cooperation program. Students interested in the program may obtain the application form and other materials from STU’s Office of International Cooperation and prepare their applications according to the stated procedure.

Send application materials to `wscicd@stu.edu.cn` by **noon on Friday, April 5**. Consult the Office of International Cooperation for the application form, process and detailed program requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Cooperation)
>
> ⚠️ The original notice provided application materials through an internal file-transfer service. Access credentials are not reproduced; obtain the official materials from the Office of International Cooperation.
