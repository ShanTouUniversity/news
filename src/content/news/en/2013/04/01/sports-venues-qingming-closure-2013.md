It is reported that the Sports Department issued a notice on April 1, 2013, that sports venues will be temporarily closed for the Qingming Festival in accordance with the university’s holiday arrangement.

## Closure Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Closure date | April 4, 2013 (Thursday, Qingming Festival) |
| Venues closed | Indoor venues, tennis courts and fitness center |
| Issued by | Sports Department |

Please be advised of the adjustment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
