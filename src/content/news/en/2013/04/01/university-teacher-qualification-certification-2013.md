Under arrangements from Guangdong education authorities, Shantou University has opened its 2013 university-teacher qualification certification process. Applicants must meet the requirements of the Teachers Law and the Regulations on Teacher Qualification, be below the statutory retirement age as of **May 15**, and currently teach in a university position. The scope includes full-time staff teachers, clinical teaching personnel at affiliated hospitals and long-term non-establishment faculty members. Consult the latest provincial requirements and OA attachments for detailed conditions.

### Timeline and process

- **By April 13**: Applicants register through their units. Each unit must submit the basic-information overview to the Office of Human Resources in New Administration Center Room 315 and email it to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`; consolidated, reviewed application materials are also due that day.
- **April 10–13**: Applicants log in to the Guangdong Teacher Qualification website at <http://jszg.gdhed.edu.cn>, complete the application form in the “Online Registration” section, print two double-sided copies on 80-gram A3 paper and submit them to the university’s personnel office for review.
- **Medical examination**: The Office of Human Resources will arrange examinations at a university-teacher qualification hospital designated by the Guangdong Department of Education. Details will be announced separately.
- **By May 23**: Degree-certificate verification documentation may be submitted to the Office of Human Resources by this later deadline.
- The university’s Teacher Qualification Expert Review Committee will review and decide the approved list. After a seven-day public notice, the list will be submitted to the provincial office for approval and certification.

Units should review the ideological-and-ethical assessment form and other materials as required by the attachments. Since 2010, applicants for professional titles in schools at all levels have been required to hold the corresponding teacher qualification.

Contact Fan at `0754-86502349` or `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. Application forms, conditions, materials checklists and the registration guide are available in the original OA attachments.
