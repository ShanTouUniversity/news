Under the university's schedule, hot-water service in STU student dormitories will follow separate summer and winter hours. Dormitories A, B, C, D, E, F, G and J will adopt the summer schedule from **May 1**.

### Hot-water hours

| Schedule | Applicable period | Supply hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer | May 1–October 31, excluding summer vacation | 5:00–6:30 p.m.; 10:00–11:00 p.m. |
| Winter | November 1–April 30 of the following year, excluding winter vacation | 4:30–10:30 p.m. |

Students in the affected dormitories should use hot water during the applicable hours. Problems may be reported at `82902670`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Logistics Office and Student Community Management Service Center)
