Because of maintenance on an electrical cabinet in a student-dormitory power-distribution room, selected STU dormitories and dining halls will be without power from **2:00 to 3:30 p.m. on Tuesday, April 30**.

### Affected areas

- Student dormitories A, B, C, D, Y and L;
- the Second Dining Hall; and
- the Third Dining Hall.

The Resource Management Office has asked affected faculty, staff and students to make arrangements in advance and apologized for the inconvenience caused by the maintenance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
