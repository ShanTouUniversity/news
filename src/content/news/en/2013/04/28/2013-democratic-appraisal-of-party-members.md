It is reported that, with the approval of the university Party Committee, Shantou University will carry out the 2013 Democratic Appraisal of Party Members on its main campus from late April to early June. The campaign follows the guidelines set out in the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee Directly-Affiliated Organs Work Committee's Opinions on Conducting the 2013 Democratic Appraisal of Party Members (Shan Zhi Fa [2013] No. 10), adapted to the university's context.

## Requirements

All general Party branches and directly-affiliated Party branches are asked to implement the appraisal conscientiously in line with the Shan Zhi Fa [2013] No. 10 document, combined with activities to study and implement the spirit of the 18th CPC National Congress.

## Deadlines

| Item | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| For Party members proposed for handling, submit Form 2 (Disposition of Unqualified Party Members) with supporting materials to the university Party Committee for approval | Before May 30 |
| Submit a summary of the appraisal work along with Forms 1, 3, and 4 (one copy each) to the Organization and United Front Work Department (New Administration Center, Room 315); send electronic copies to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn | Before June 7 |

## Forms

- Attachment 1: Shan Zhi Fa [2013] No. 10 document
- Form 1: Preliminary Survey on the 2013 Democratic Appraisal
- Form 2: Disposition of Unqualified Party Members
- Form 3: Statistics on the 2013 Democratic Appraisal
- Form 4: Unqualified Members and Other Disciplinary Actions
- Form 5: Self-Evaluation Registration Form for Party Members (to be printed individually)

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee)
> ⚠️ The notice attachments and forms are available on the OA system.
