To facilitate contact among university departments during the Labor Day holiday, STU's General and Administrative Office has compiled and published the **May 1, 2013 duty roster** for reference by university units.

The roster lists duty personnel, duty locations and telephone numbers by unit, covering the General and Administrative Office, News Center, Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office, Science and Technology Center, academic schools, colleges, residential colleges, Network Center and related teaching-service units. To protect duty personnel's personal information, this article does not reproduce names, mobile numbers or individual duty assignments.

Questions about the roster may be sent to the General and Administrative Office at `o_jdk@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General and Administrative Office)
