STU Student Affairs Office has announced volunteer arrangements for fitness testing for the Antarctic Expedition Project. The selection tests will take place **April 29–30**, and the notice says that **40 volunteers** will assist with the testing.

### Fitness-test schedule

| Date and time | Location | Test activities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April 29, 08:30 a.m.–noon | Deep-water pool | 100-meter swim and two minutes of treading water |
| April 29, 2:00–5:00 p.m. | Track and field | One minute of sit-ups, one minute of push-ups, one minute of pull-ups and a weighted 2,400-meter run |
| April 30, 8:00 a.m.–noon | Campus | 10 game activities |

### Volunteer training and meeting points

The 15 volunteers assisting with the **April 29** fitness tests must attend training at 4:00 p.m. on April 28 in Lecture Classroom 204, E Teaching Building. They must then meet at the swimming pool at 8:00 a.m. and at the track and field at 2:00 p.m. on April 29.

The 37 volunteers assisting with the **April 30** fitness tests must attend training at 8:00 a.m. on April 30 in Lecture Classroom 204, E Teaching Building.

The notice gives a total of 40 volunteers but lists 15 and 37 volunteers for assistance on the two days. Because the source does not say whether the groups overlap, these figures are retained as assignment counts and are not added together as a total. An April 22 recruitment notice had planned to recruit 30 volunteers for April 30; this selection notice gives 37, and the archive does not explain the difference.

The attachment is a volunteer list containing student personal information. Names, contact details and the list itself are not reproduced. Questions should be directed through Student Affairs' official channel.
