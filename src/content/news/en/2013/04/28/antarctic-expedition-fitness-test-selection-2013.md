STU Student Affairs Office has announced that fitness-test selection for the Antarctic Expedition Project will take place **April 29–30**. The selection is open to participating faculty, staff and students and includes water-based tests, track-and-field activities and campus games.

### Test schedule

| Date and time | Location | Sequence or activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April 29, 08:30 a.m.–noon | Deep-water pool | 100-meter swim and two minutes of treading water |
| April 29, 2:00–5:00 p.m. | Track and field | One minute of sit-ups, one minute of push-ups, one minute of pull-ups and a weighted 2,400-meter run |
| April 30, 8:00 a.m.–noon | Campus | 10 game activities |

Participants testing on the morning of April 29 must check in with their campus cards at the swimming pool at 8:30 a.m. Participants testing in the afternoon must check in at the track and field at 2:00 p.m. Participants taking part in the games on April 30 must attend a briefing at 8:00 a.m. in Lecture Classroom 204, E Teaching Building. The game rules will be announced there, and participants must show their campus cards while completing the games.

### Groups and scoring

The 100-meter swimming test will follow the groups in the attachment. Groups for later tests will be announced on site after the 100-meter swim. The notice establishes a fitness-test points system and provides scoring rules for the April 29 events. The tables were embedded as images in the webpage, but the local archive does not contain those image files; the exact scores cannot be verified and are not supplemented or inferred here.

### Safety guidance

The 100-meter swim and two-minute treading-water test will take place in the deep-water pool. Participants should assess their swimming ability carefully and treat safety as the first priority. The notice advises people who cannot swim to withdraw from the water tests. Because weather conditions may change, participants should keep warm after completing the water-based tests.

The grouping attachment contains participant names. No student list or other personal information is reproduced. The original Student Affairs notice and on-site staff instructions govern the detailed arrangements.
