STU Business School issued Document Shan Da Shang Fa [2013] No. 07 announcing the establishment of a new faculty appointment group. The group is responsible for faculty appointments to annual-salary positions at the Business School. The decision followed discussion by the school's affairs committee and was copied to the STU Faculty Appointment Committee.

### Group membership

| Position | Appointee or members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Xu Zongling |
| Members (listed by Chinese-character stroke order) | Wang * (given name garbled in the original), Ji Yun, Song Xiaobao, Chen Yinglin, Ouyang Feng, Huang Yan, Huang Wangming, Qin Zhong and Zeng Qiang |
| Secretary | Huang Xianglong |

The original OA text renders the given name of one member surnamed Wang as corrupted characters. It cannot be reliably identified and is not reconstructed here.
