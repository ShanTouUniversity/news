STU Student Affairs Office, Student Entrepreneurship Park Management Office and Student Entrepreneurship Center have announced that the university will organize students to take part in the second “Help You Start a Business, Win in Guangzhou” university student entrepreneurship competition. STU will select and recommend **three to five outstanding startup projects** for the provincial semifinal.

The competition is jointly organized by the Guangzhou Human Resources and Social Security Bureau and the Guangdong Provincial Graduate Employment Guidance Center. It runs **from April through September 2013** and aims to promote student innovation and entrepreneurship, develop awareness and ability to start a business, and give students opportunities for practical startup experience.

### Campus entry requirements

Entries must be business plans, and the competition is open to current STU students. Participants must enter as startup teams. Each team should normally have no more than **10 members**; no more than **three core team members** may take part in the defense. Projects that won one of the top six places in the first “Win in Guangzhou” university student entrepreneurship competition in 2012 are excluded.

Projects may address any topic, but their products or services must be feasible and have a degree of technical support. Participants must hold legal intellectual-property rights or rights of use for the project. Based on broad market research and analysis, each business plan must explain the project's technology, market prospects, operating strategy, funding needs, future planning and team information.

For the STU campus selection, projects must be submitted by **noon on May 17, 2013**:

- submit the paper version to the Student Entrepreneurship Center, Room 106 of the New Administration Building; and
- send the electronic version to `zpchen@stu.edu.cn`.

The campus contact is Chen Zepeng, a teacher at the Student Affairs Office, at `86502256`. After the campus selection, STU will recommend three to five projects for the semifinal. Recommended teams and works must be uploaded to the designated competition website by **May 31** under the provincial competition schedule.

### Provincial process and scoring

Universities will organize their own preliminary rounds and recommend projects. In the provincial semifinal, a judging panel including university professors, senior managers from venture-investment institutions and successful entrepreneurs will select **30 startup teams** for the final.

| Stage | Time or rule |
| --- | --- |
| Overall competition | April–September 2013 |
| Campus launches, entrepreneurship-story events, project exhibitions and preliminary rounds | April 1–May 31, organized by each university |
| Universities upload semifinal teams and project materials | By May 31 |
| Semifinal review and announcement of finalists | June 1–30 |
| Entrepreneurship training and project revision | July–August |
| Online voting | Early September, worth 10% of the total score |
| On-site final and awards | Late September |

The on-site final consists of project presentations and an open defense. A project presentation may use slides, a physical display or a simulated operation, lasts **five minutes** and accounts for **40%** of the total score. The open defense lasts **eight minutes**; judges will ask about areas including market potential and operating strategy, and the defense accounts for **50%** of the total score.

### Awards

| Award | Number | Prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| First prize | 2 | Certificate and RMB 30,000 per project |
| Second prize | 5 | Certificate and RMB 20,000 per project |
| Third prize | 8 | Certificate and RMB 10,000 per project |
| Excellence award | 15 | Certificate and RMB 5,000 per project |

The 30 finalist teams will receive priority consideration for the Guangzhou startup-project database and the corresponding project-collection award. The top 15 projects may qualify to enter relevant startup incubators in Guangdong and receive their support measures. Projects with investment potential may be recommended to venture-capital institutions for further assessment. Eligible teams may apply under applicable rules for self-employment subsidies, small guaranteed loans and personnel-file management services.
