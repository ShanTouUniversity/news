Following organizational and personnel changes, STU Law School adjusted the membership of its affairs committee after approval at a **Law School president's office meeting on April 23, 2013**. The decision was issued as Document Shan Da Fa Fa [2013] No. 02.

### Adjusted membership

| Position | Appointee or members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Michael Palmer (Peng Wenhao) |
| Members (listed by Chinese-character stroke order) | Tian Zhirong, Liu Xiaoyong, Yan Zhigang, Su Wenji, Wang Qu, Chen Xueyi, Chen Ruilin, Cai Zhengzhong and Tan Kanglin |
| Secretary | Zheng Shunqin |
