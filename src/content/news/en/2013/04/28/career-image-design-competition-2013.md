STU Student Affairs Office, together with China Everbright Bank Shantou Branch and the STU Career Planning and Development Association, is organizing a career image design competition. The event runs **from April 28 through late May** and is open to all STU students. It is intended to help students understand image expectations in different professions and strengthen their professional skills and job-search preparation.

### Entry format

The competition is team-based. Each team must have **two or three members**, who form their own team and select a captain and team name. Registration is open from **April 28 through May 14**.

### Competition process

During the **training session**, a personal-image design teacher will be invited to give a lecture on career image design and analyze university students' job-search attire and current career-image practices. The time and venue will be announced separately, and all faculty, staff and students are welcome.

For the **preliminary round**, each team must choose a career of interest, design a personal image for that career and take a career-image photograph. Teams must also prepare an analysis report using sources such as literature research, market research, interviews with company personnel or interviews with university teachers. The report should explain the design concept and analyze the relationship between career image and personal career development.

Preliminary works must be submitted by **May 14** to `imagescdda@163.com`. The electronic analysis report must be in PDF format, with the career-image photograph integrated into the report. The stated formatting requirements are small-four type, SimSun font, 1.5-line spacing and no more than six pages.

The **final round** is scheduled for **late May**, with eight teams advancing to the final presentation. The final will include presentations of design concepts, etiquette questions and answers, and questions exchanged among contestants. The venue has not been announced.

The registration form requests entrants' academic year, major, names, campus extension and short numbers, dormitories and email addresses. Student registration information and personal contact details are not reproduced here.
