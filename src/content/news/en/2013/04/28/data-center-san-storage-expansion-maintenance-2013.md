To meet the growing storage needs of campus information services, STU Network and Information Center will upgrade and expand the data center's SAN storage system under its annual plan. Applications using the storage system will be unavailable during the maintenance.

### Maintenance details

- **Maintenance window:** 12:30–6:00 a.m. on April 29, 2013;
- **Expected outage:** three to four hours;
- **Scope:** all campus users.

Affected services include the university email system, the English version of the campus homepage, school and departmental websites, the internal office-information website, the MySTU platform, Chaoxing electronic books, the online lecture hall and Chaoxing academic videos.

Network and Information Center asks users to arrange work and study plans in advance. Questions may be directed to `86503400` or `stunic@stu.edu.cn`. The original notice also lists a duty mobile number, which is not reproduced here as a personal duty contact.
