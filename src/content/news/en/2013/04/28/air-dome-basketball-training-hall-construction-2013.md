To support physical-education reform, improve students' athletic abilities and build a high-level basketball team, STU is constructing an air-supported basketball training hall on the **vacant land northwest of G Dormitory**. The project, which includes the air-supported hall and related facilities purchased with a foundation donation, will operate as a fully enclosed construction site.

### Project and schedule

Beijing Yuedun Air-Supported Building Technology Co., Ltd. is the general contractor. The consortium members and their responsibilities are:

| Organization | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Beijing Yuedun Air-Supported Building Technology Co., Ltd. | General contracting |
| Shantou No. 2 Architectural Design Institute | Civil-design work |
| Shantou Longhua Construction General Corporation | Civil construction |
| Dalian Qiansen Sports Facilities Engineering Co., Ltd. | Wood-floor supply and installation |

The contract period runs from **April 23 through August 30, 2013**. Site work is scheduled for **7:00 a.m.–noon**, **2:00–6:00 p.m.** and **7:00–10:00 p.m.**

The original notice gives areas for the air-supported hall and auxiliary rooms, but the area units and figures are corrupted in the database text. The attachment is a construction-site layout drawing, so no unverifiable area figures are added here.

### Safety and access

The construction site will be fully enclosed, and construction vehicles will enter and leave through Nongmin Road on the north side of the site. Because of space limitations, the construction fencing is close to the southwest campus walkway. Vehicles and pedestrians passing the area should keep to the outer side of the route indicated on site.

Students, faculty, staff and other people may not enter the construction site or remain nearby without permission from Resource Management Office personnel and the construction contractor. The arrangement is intended to protect safety and limit the project's impact on nearby teaching and residential activities.
