STU's Graduate School has announced recruitment for the 10th National Graduate Mathematical Modeling Competition, open to master's students, doctoral students and admitted incoming graduate students at the main campus. The university will organize campus registration and selection and provide each participating team with its entry fee and a living subsidy during the competition.

The 10th competition is scheduled for **late September 2013** and will be hosted by Central South University. Founded in 2004, the National Graduate Mathematical Modeling Competition became a theme event of the National Graduate Innovation Practice Series in 2013 under the sponsorship of the Ministry of Education's Degree and Graduate Education Development Center.

In 2012, the competition attracted 2,507 teams from 251 graduate-education institutions in 29 provinces, municipalities and autonomous regions, with 7,521 student participants, including 384 doctoral students. An STU team won a third prize in the 2012 competition.

### Eligibility and teams

Master's and doctoral students at STU may register, and admitted incoming graduate students are also included among the prospective participants named in the notice. Teams consist of **three graduate students**. Students may register individually or as an existing team. Priority will be given to students who have taken part in the National Undergraduate Mathematical Modeling Competition or conducted research involving mathematical models or data processing.

### Registration schedule

| Date | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| By June 10 | Send the registration form to `fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn` |
| By June 13 | Complete team formation; each team has three graduate students |
| June 18–August 20 | Each team member logs into the competition website and completes online registration; late registration results in loss of eligibility |
| August 26 | The Graduate School reviews participants' enrollment status |
| Late September | The organizing committee releases the problems online; participants develop mathematical models in a four-day competition |
| Mid-October to early November | Papers are reviewed and award results are published online |
| Early December | Award ceremony and results-exchange conference at Central South University |

Pre-competition training and practice arrangements will be announced separately. Teams receiving a third prize or above will receive a corresponding university award under later arrangements; the notice does not specify the amount.
