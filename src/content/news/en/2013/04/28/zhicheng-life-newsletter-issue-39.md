It is reported that Zhicheng Residential College of Shantou University has recently published the second issue of 2013 (issue No. 39 overall) of its internal newsletter, "Zhicheng Life," distributing it to faculty and students through the university's OA system.

"Zhicheng Life" is a publication run by Zhicheng Residential College that documents the daily life and activities of the college's students and staff. The new edition is the second issue for 2013 and the thirty-ninth since the newsletter was founded.

The full issue is available as a PDF attachment on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng Residential College)
