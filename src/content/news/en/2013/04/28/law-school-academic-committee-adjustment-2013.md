Following organizational and personnel changes, STU Law School adjusted the membership of its academic committee after approval at a **Law School president's office meeting on April 23, 2013**. The decision was issued as Document Shan Da Fa Fa [2013] No. 03.

### Adjusted membership

| Position | Appointee or members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Michael Palmer (Peng Wenhao) |
| Members (listed by Chinese-character stroke order) | Deng Jianguang, Yan Zhigang, Li Guanghui, Li Jiacai, Chen Ruilin, Fan Yaodeng, Qin Guozhu, Sa Zhihui and Xiong Jincai |
| Secretary | Deng Linqiang |
