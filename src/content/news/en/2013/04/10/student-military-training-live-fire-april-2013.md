After consulting the supporting training unit, STU has scheduled live-fire training for the **2011 cohort on the morning of Sunday, April 14**. Students who took military training in 2012 are generally expected to participate, as live-fire training is an important part of the required military-training course.

The university will provide transportation from the monument commemorating the university motto to the firing range. Colleges and Zhicheng College will board in the following groups:

| Departure | Units |
| --- | --- |
| 7:30 a.m. | School of Engineering and School of Liberal Arts |
| 8:15 a.m. | School of Science and School of Law |
| 8:45 a.m. | Business School and School of Journalism |
| 9:15 a.m. | Zhicheng College and School of Art and Design |

Participants must wear military-training uniforms, assemble at the designated location according to schedule and follow instructors’ directions at the range. Each school or college will assign counselors to accompany students and assist with organization; students awaiting their boarding time should remain at the meeting point.

> Source: STU OA Notice (People’s Armed Forces Department)
