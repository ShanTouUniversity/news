STU Research Office is organizing applications for the fourth batch of 2013 Guangdong provincial science-plan projects. Under the relevant notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and Department of Finance, the category suitable for university applicants is the Industrial Technology Research and Development Fund. Projects should comply with national and Guangdong “12th Five-Year” science and technology plans and the relevant special-program guidelines; priority will be given to projects expected to produce substantial patent results during implementation.

A project leader with two or more ongoing provincial science-plan projects is generally ineligible to submit a new project. Each leader may generally submit only one new project in 2013. Applications involving external partners must include a cooperation agreement specifying responsibilities, funding arrangements and ownership of results.

Applicants must submit an application, feasibility report and supporting materials through the Provincial Science and Technology Department’s integrated management system at <http://pro.gdstc.gov.cn>. Existing account holders may continue to use their accounts. First-time applicants should email their name, organization and project category to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn` to request system access through the Research Office. Applications and attachments must be submitted online for STU review by **April 30**; revised materials returned for changes must be uploaded by **May 7**. Approved projects must ensure that the printed application matches the online version. Projects with partners must bear the partner’s official seal and submit two printed copies to the Research Office by **May 10**.

Supporting materials generally include proof of the applicant organization’s legal-person status and organizational code, financial statements, partner qualifications, cooperation agreements, matching-fund commitments, patent or appraisal documents, novelty-search reports, research-capacity evidence and testing reports. Requirements depend on the relevant project guidelines. Contacts: Yunxiang Hu and Changsheng Zhu at `86502813`. The general notice is available from the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology at <http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/1365500848864-3398670657511824719.html>; the Industrial Technology Research and Development Fund guidelines are at <http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/kjyw/tzgg/1365554727540-8816384524847545295.html>.
