To meet undergraduate demand for multimedia software instruction, Shantou University&apos;s Student Learning Center (SLC) is offering a beginner class taught by SLC peer tutors. The class is open to enrolled undergraduates on the main campus.

The course covers Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and poster design. It will be held from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on Sundays during weeks 8–11, in the computer lab of the School of Arts.

| Course information | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | April 14, April 21, April 28 and May 5 |
| Time | Sundays, 7:00–9:00 p.m. |
| Venue | School of Arts computer lab |
| Eligibility | Enrolled undergraduates on the STU main campus |

Students must register by **10 p.m. on April 11** through the [Reservation Center](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/), under “SLC Reservations – Beginner Multimedia Software Class.” Registration is required only once, for the April 14 session, and covers all four classes. Places are offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Registered students are expected to attend each weekly class on time and may not be absent without reason. The list is scheduled to be published on the campus office information network on April 12.

Students seeking more advanced instruction may use the Reservation Center&apos;s “SLC Reservations – Academic Consultation” service to book guidance from an SLC peer tutor.
