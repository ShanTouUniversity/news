Following the **fourth leadership meeting on April 9**, STU has publicized Mei Zhiping’s proposed appointment as head of the Biology Department in the School of Science.

The publicity is conducted under STU’s Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres. Organizations and individuals with comments should submit written feedback to the Human Resources Office by **April 17**. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must include the writer’s real name. Phone: `86503390`; email: `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.
