Shantou University&apos;s School of Engineering has adjusted the composition of its Research and Discipline Development Committee following organizational and personnel changes. The adjustment was recommended at a school affairs meeting on March 21, 2013, and approved by the school&apos;s academic committee.

| Role | Personnel |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Xiong Guangjing |
| Committee members | Niu Xiaodong, Yan Jingwen, Lin Peng, Zhao Xiaohua, Jiang Yongquan, Cai Hao, Cheng Xiyun, Cai Weihong, Xiong Guangjing, and one member whose name is garbled in the original text |
| Secretary | Huang Xuejia |

One committee member&apos;s name appears as corrupted text in the original notice and has not been reconstructed here.
