STU Academic Exchange Service Center will introduce its **2013 spring discounted banquet meals on April 15**. The previous discounted offering will end on the same date.

The service is available to units or projects with valid charge-account arrangements and to faculty, staff and students using a valid campus card. Cardholders must have sufficient balance; cash is not accepted for discounted banquet meals, and diners must attend for the full meal. The center also asks diners not to bring outside food or beverages.

Reservation hotlines: campus extensions `2668` and `4138`; outside-campus numbers `82905103` and `82905104`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Academic Exchange Service Center)
