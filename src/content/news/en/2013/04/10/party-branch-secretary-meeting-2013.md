It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee issued a meeting notice on April 10, 2013, to hold a meeting of general Party branch and directly affiliated Party branch secretaries.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | General and directly affiliated Party branch secretaries meeting |
| Time | 16:00, April 11, 2013 |
| Venue | Room 201, Administration Center |
| Agenda | Arrangements for the election of new terms |
| Issued by | Organization and United Front Work Department |

Relevant personnel are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
