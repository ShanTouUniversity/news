Following the **fourth leadership meeting on April 9**, STU has publicized three proposed appointments: Ruifeng Wen as director of the Campus Security Office, Jibin Fan as assistant director of the Human Resources Office, and Jian Gao concurrently as deputy director of the editorial office of *Shantou University Journal*.

Wen is currently deputy director of the Resource Management Office, deputy director of the Campus Security Office and director of the Campus Security Administration Office. Fan works in a management position in the Human Resources Office and holds a master’s degree in management and the title of senior economist. Gao is deputy director of the Research Office and secretary of its direct Party branch, and holds a master’s degree in higher education and the title of associate research fellow.

The publicity is conducted under STU’s Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres. Organizations and individuals with comments should submit written feedback to the Human Resources Office by **April 17**. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must include the writer’s real name. Phone: `86503390`; email: `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.
