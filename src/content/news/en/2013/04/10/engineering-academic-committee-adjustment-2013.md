It is reported that the College of Engineering has adjusted the membership of its Academic Committee in response to institutional and personnel changes. The adjustment was nominated at the college affairs meeting on March 21, 2013 and approved by the Academic Committee, and was issued as Document No. 3 [2013] of the College of Engineering.

## New Roster

| Role | Members |
|------|---------|
| Chair | Chen Yan |
| Members (ordered by stroke count) | Wang Yingzi, Zhuang Lihong, Sun Haojun, Chen Shaoke, Li Fenlan, Li —ping, Zhang Chengtian, Liu Ping, Jiang Yongquan, Zhao Xiaohua, Cheng Xiyun, Cai Weihong |
| Secretary | Zheng Zaixin |

> Note: One member's name was affected by a character-encoding issue in the original notice and is rendered approximately here.

The reconstituted Academic Committee will continue to oversee academic affairs of the college.
