Shantou University&apos;s School of Engineering has adjusted the composition of its school affairs committee in response to organizational and personnel changes. The recommendation was discussed at a school affairs meeting on March 21, 2013, and approved by the school&apos;s academic committee.

| Role | Personnel |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Chen Yan |
| Committee members | Niu Xiaodong, Bao Nengsheng, Lin Peng, Zheng Zaixin, Huang Yixiong, Cai Hao, Xiong Guangjing, and one member whose name is garbled in the original text |
| Secretary | Huang Yixiong, concurrently |

One committee member&apos;s name appears as corrupted text in the original notice and has not been reconstructed here.
