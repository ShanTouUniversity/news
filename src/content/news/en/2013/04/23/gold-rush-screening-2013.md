It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a film screening on the evening of April 24 in the Library Theater, featuring Charlie Chaplin’s classic silent comedy *The Gold Rush*.

## Screening Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *The Gold Rush* (1925), written, produced, directed by and starring Charlie Chaplin |
| Time | 19:00–22:00, April 24, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Theater |
| Host | Peter Herford |
| Seats | 50 public seats available on a first-come, first-served basis in addition to 150 class seats; students present campus card for entry |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

Regarded as Chaplin’s most cherished work and a masterpiece blending laughter and tears, the film follows a prospector’s hardships in Alaska. All faculty and students are welcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
