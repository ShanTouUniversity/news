It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Youth Volunteers Association of Shantou University issued a notice on April 23, 2013, to organize a donation and prayer campaign for the 7.0-magnitude earthquake that struck Ya’an, Sichuan on April 20, with its epicenter in Lushan County.

Upholding the tradition of mutual support in times of hardship, the university called on students to show care for the affected people and help them rebuild confidence.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Ya’an, Stay Strong |
| Prayer vigil | 19:30–20:00, April 26, 2013, in front of the Grand Auditorium; candlelight choral prayer with on-site donations; participants should dress simply, remain solemn and follow staff guidance, and practice the choral pieces in the attachments in advance |
| Donation times | 11:00–13:00 and 17:00–19:00, April 24–25, downstairs of Canteens 2 and 4; and at the prayer site on April 26 |
| Participants | All students |
| Use of funds | All proceeds will be remitted to the Guangdong Youth Development Foundation’s special fund for Ya’an youth disaster relief |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Youth Volunteers Association |

> ⚠️ The choral pieces and attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Youth Volunteers Association)
