Shantou University Business School has opened recruitment for a Singapore cultural visit scheduled for **July 1–8, 2013**. Through visits and exchanges involving public institutions, private-sector organizations and community groups, the program will introduce students to Singaporean society and help them develop an international perspective. It is a self-funded program expected to include **12 to 15 students** and one faculty advisor.

### Information session and eligibility

The Business School will hold an information session from **3:30 to 4:40 p.m. on Wednesday, April 24**, in E203 to answer questions about the program.

The selection is open to full-time Business School undergraduates in the classes of 2010, 2011 and 2012; full-time graduate students in the classes of 2011 and 2012; and MBA and course-program participants. Applicants should have good spoken and written English, demonstrate team spirit and have an active record of public-service or volunteer participation.

A selection group will conduct an initial review of the written applications. Applicants who pass the initial review will have one-on-one interviews with faculty advisor Dennis Lee.

### Indicative program

The English itinerary attached to the notice proposes accommodation at **Angsana College, NUS University Town**. It includes:

- Visits to Singapore&apos;s Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA) and Housing and Development Board (HDB) on urban planning and public housing;
- A session on Singapore public transport and related policy questions with Jeremy Yap, Group Director for Policy and Planning at the Ministry of Transport;
- A visit to Marina Barrage to discuss Singapore&apos;s water-management strategy;
- Visits to Little India, Chinatown and Arab Street to explore the history of Singapore&apos;s migrant communities;
- Visits to Esplanade, the Supreme Court and Parliament House;
- A session on critical success factors in the telecommunications business with Keith Leong, Head of Enterprise Data Managed Services at Singtel;
- A session titled “Understanding Singapore: Meritocracy, Multi-Racialism and Money” with Dr Tan Lai Yong;
- A visit and meeting with Ng Kian Seng, General Secretary of the YMCA of Singapore, an NGO;
- A session on culture and communication led by Dr Tan Lai Yong;
- Sessions on leadership and communication, a conversation with a young successful entrepreneur and the ingredients of success with a business leader. Some speakers were still to be confirmed in the itinerary;
- A full-day activity on Singapore&apos;s flora and fauna, community visits and interaction, and an observation of Singapore nightlife;
- Visits to the Asian Civilizations Museum and the National Museum, a performance of *Songs of the Sea*, free time, a final gathering, reflection and journaling, and a barbecue.

The itinerary also lists flights between Jieyang Airport and Singapore Changi Airport: an outbound flight at 6:15 p.m. arriving in Singapore at 10:15 p.m., and a return flight listed at 1:35 p.m. to Shantou with arrival in Jieyang at 5:35 p.m. Flight details, the order of activities and costs were subject to final confirmation.

### Application arrangements

Applicants should submit their materials to the Business School Foreign Affairs Project Office in Wenxi 211 by **noon on May 3**. MBA and course-program participants may send electronic materials to `dnye@stu.edu.cn`. The application requires evidence of English proficiency and one English résumé; personal application materials are not reproduced here.

The program is self-funded. The attached budget lists accommodation, meals, transportation, airfare, travel insurance, speaker gifts, museum admission and activity costs, including some provisional amounts. The final itinerary and cost were to be confirmed by the school.
