Shantou University will hold an awards and showcase event for the **Apps for Women Learning Student Mobile-Software Design Competition** from **2 to 4 p.m. on Wednesday, April 24**, in the Library Student Activity Hall. The event will recognize outstanding design proposals and encourage participating teams to complete the subsequent software-development work.

The event will present and honor **15 shortlisted proposals**. Competition teams may attend, and all students are welcome to view the showcase. Judges, advisors and participating students are expected to attend, with approximately 50 people anticipated.

For inquiries, contact `cws@stu.edu.cn` or Teacher Zheng at `86502965`.
