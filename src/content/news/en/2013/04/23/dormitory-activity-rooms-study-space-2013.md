To make better use of residence-area space and support a stronger study environment, Shantou University has added study-space functions to the **three existing activity rooms on the first floors of residence halls AB, CD and EF**. The study-space function took effect upon publication of the notice.

### Opening hours and use

During the spring and fall semesters, the rooms are open daily from **10:30 p.m. to 8:30 a.m. the following day**, as well as during other unoccupied periods. Access-card readers will be improved as supporting facilities are completed. Students should swipe their cards when entering and leaving and follow residence-area rules.

Users should conserve electricity and take care of the desks, chairs and electrical equipment. Desks and chairs may not be taken outside. Report violations to the residence-area counselor office in the Student Affairs Office at B106 or email `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn`.
