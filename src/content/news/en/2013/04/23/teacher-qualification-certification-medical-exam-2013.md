Shantou University Human Resources has announced the 2013 medical examination arrangements for teacher-qualification applicants. Teachers applying for certification must arrive at the Medical Examination Department on the third floor of the West Building at Shantou Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine at **7:30 a.m. on Friday, April 26**. They should bring an identity card and the examination form, collect the laboratory-test form, pay the fee and complete the examination.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Examination time | 7:30 a.m., Friday, April 26 |
| Venue | Medical Examination Department, third floor, West Building, Shantou Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine |
| Required materials | Identity card and examination form |
| Fee | RMB 200 |
| After the examination | Return the examination form to the hospital |

The attachment is the Guangdong Physical Examination Form for Teacher-Qualification Applicants, revised in 2011. It contains identity-card, health and examination-conclusion fields, which are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
>
> ⚠️ The teacher-qualification examination form contains personal identity and health information. Obtain or consult the original form through the Human Resources Office.
