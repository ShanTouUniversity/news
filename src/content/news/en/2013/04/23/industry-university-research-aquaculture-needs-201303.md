Shantou Hongtai Technology Aquaculture Co. has announced a university-industry research need and is seeking research partners at Shantou University and other research institutions to study production of South American white shrimp.

Established in **2007**, the company focuses on South American white-shrimp aquaculture. It has built a **450-mu elevated shrimp-farming base** and employs seven technical personnel and 90 workers. The company is seeking research on the deterioration of shrimp seed and poor water quality in coastal farming areas. It hopes that scientific assessment and targeted investment can improve seed quality, address water pollution in the Guang&apos;ao Bay sea area, support safe aquaculture production and increase yields.

Interested university units and faculty members should contact the Technology Achievement and Development Section of STU Research by **April 28** to discuss possible cooperation. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, telephone `86503271`, email `ygluo@stu.edu.cn`.
