Registration has opened for the **UFIDA Cup Enterprise Management Simulation Sand-Table Competition**, part of the seventh Guangdong University Student Science and Technology Festival. The event is organized by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and Guangdong Student Federation; the Guangzhou University Committee of the Communist Youth League is the host, with China Mobile Group Guangdong and UFIDA Xindao Technology as co-organizers. The event expects participation from 40 undergraduate institutions and 50 higher vocational institutions in Guangdong.

With the theme “Taking on the business battlefield and managing a wealth of life,” the competition introduces ERP fundamentals and enterprise-management simulation. It emphasizes fair competition, honest transactions, teamwork and innovation. Teams will operate simulated companies in an ERP environment and develop practical business thinking, collaboration and problem-solving skills.

### Schedule and venue

| Stage | Date | Venue or details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration | April 23–30 | Universities conduct campus selection before registering |
| Observation and training | May 11 | UFIDA Xindao will provide professional instruction; the detailed program will follow |
| Provincial check-in | Afternoon of May 24 | Guangzhou University University Town Campus |
| Provincial competition | May 25–26 | Guangzhou University University Town Campus |

The competition has undergraduate and higher-vocational divisions. Teams will be grouped by drawing lots and compete in a two-day ERP sand-table contest. Rankings will be based on each team&apos;s simulated six-year operating performance. The first through fourth operating years will be conducted on May 25; the fifth year, sixth-year market selection and sixth-year operations will be conducted on May 26, followed by closing activities.

### Participation requirements

Participants must be university teachers or enrolled students whose fields are relevant to the competition, including logistics, enterprise management, business administration, accounting and economics. They should have solid professional knowledge, a broad understanding of enterprise information management, good communication and oral-expression skills, a strong team orientation, collaborative ability, creativity and initiative.

Registration is by university. Each university may register only one team, consisting of **five contestants** and **one or two instructors**. Every team must send one or two instructors or contestants to the pre-competition observation and training session. The competition charges no registration or participation fee, but participants must pay their own meals, accommodation and transportation. Teams needing accommodation reservations should state the number of rooms and nights on the registration form.

### Awards and advancement

Team awards include first, second and third prizes and merit awards. The exact number of awards will be determined based on the final number of participating teams. Students on first-prize teams will receive priority recommendations for internships with UFIDA partner companies. The top four teams in each of the undergraduate and higher-vocational divisions will advance directly to the ninth UFIDA Cup National University Student Entrepreneurship Design and Sand-Table Business Simulation Competition final.

### STU registration

STU students interested in competing must send their registration information to the University Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` by **April 28**. The committee will select and recommend teams based on actual registrations. The provincial notice sets April 23–30 as the formal registration period and requires selected teams to complete the application form, obtain the university Youth League Committee seal and return the form by fax and email to the provincial organizing committee by **April 30**. April 28 is therefore the STU campus deadline, while April 30 is the provincial organizing-committee deadline.
