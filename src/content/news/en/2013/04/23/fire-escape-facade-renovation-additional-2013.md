It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office issued a notice on April 23, 2013, regarding additional costs for the Academic Exchange Center fire escape facade renovation.

The project started on March 1, 2013. During construction, additional costs were incurred to cover the originally exposed fire hydrant boxes and power conduits on the wall with light steel keel and calcium silicate board. A preliminary estimate puts the additional cost at about 8,000 yuan, keeping the cumulative increase within the university’s 10% threshold.

In accordance with the *Management Measures for Infrastructure Project Bidding*, the additional cost is now publicized. Those with objections may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 17:30 on April 25, 2013.
