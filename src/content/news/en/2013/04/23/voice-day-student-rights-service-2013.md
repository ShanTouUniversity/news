The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Union will hold the fifth **Voice Day** rights and feedback event from **11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. on Friday, April 26**, at the Golden Triangle. The event will provide students with a channel to raise concerns, seek advice and communicate with the Student Union.

A service booth will display the Student Union&apos;s complaint channels, issue-handling process and processing deadlines. It will receive student complaints and inquiries, present progress on student-rights work and collect opinions and suggestions. The event is open to all STU students.

> Source: STU Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Union OA Notice
