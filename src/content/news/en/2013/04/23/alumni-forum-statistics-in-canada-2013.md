The STU Alumni Forum lecture series will hold a session from **3 to 5 p.m. on April 26** in E Lecture Room 203. Chen Jian will speak on **“Applications of Statistics Across Canadian Social and Professional Sectors.”**

The lecture is organized by the Shantou University Alumni Affairs Office and is open to all STU faculty members, staff and students. The original notice provides no further biographical information about the speaker.
