Shantou University&apos;s Voyage Plan workplace series will host a lecture on legal risks in employment from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on April 26** in Lecture Room E101. The lecture is intended to help students recognize traps and legal risks that may arise during job searches and employment and prepare appropriate responses.

The lecture will be led by **Associate Professor Huang Xuelan**. Topics include tripartite agreements, social insurance and housing provident funds, employment contracts, common legal issues in the employment process and labor-law knowledge students should understand before seeking or starting a job. The lecture will also interpret the Labor Contract Law and possible employer responses and discuss employment-related legal risks.

The lecture is open to all STU students.
