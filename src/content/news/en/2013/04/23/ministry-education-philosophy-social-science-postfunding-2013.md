The Ministry of Education has opened applications for its 2013 post-funding projects in philosophy and social sciences. The program supports university teachers whose research results are already substantially developed, including foundational research, original theoretical research, documentary research, translated works, reference books and important non-paper research outputs.

### Project categories and funding

| Category | Funding | Basic description |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Major project | RMB 200,000 | A landmark result expected to make an important contribution to academic development and achieve major academic value |
| Key project | RMB 150,000 | A research result with important academic value |
| General project | RMB 100,000 | A research result with significant academic value |

At least **70% of the research task** must have been completed when an application is submitted. Applicants must provide the completed portion of the manuscript or non-paper result. Post-funding support does not include publication funding; it covers compensation for earlier research and subsequent research work. The Ministry of Education and relevant publishers will organize publication of the final result, which must prominently identify it as a “Ministry of Education Philosophy and Social-Science Research Post-Funding Project.” Projects should normally be completed within **one to two years**. With unanimous agreement from the review experts, an extension to three years may be granted when necessary.

### Eligibility and restrictions

Applicants must be full-time, in-establishment teachers of the university, including foreign teachers and teachers from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. Foreign, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan applicants must attach a certificate from Human Resources confirming their in-establishment, active-service status. Applicants must have sound political and professional qualities, the ability to conduct and organize independent research, and be able to serve as the actual project leader while undertaking substantive research.

The title requirements are:

- Major-project applicants must hold a full senior professional or technical title;
- Key-project applicants must hold a senior professional or technical title;
- General-project applicants must hold an intermediate or higher professional or technical title. Applicants with an intermediate title must also hold a doctoral degree.

Each applicant may submit only one project in each round. A project must be recommended by the academic committee of the applicant&apos;s unit and signed by two experts with full senior professional or technical titles who are not members of the project team. No more than one recommender may be from STU. A project does not have to include a research team, although joint research is encouraged. A team member may participate in no more than two project applications at the same time, and every listed member must consent in advance and take part in substantive research.

The following results are ineligible:

- Results submitted by a person currently responsible for a Ministry of Education Humanities and Social Sciences project, or by a person who has also applied for another category of 2013 Ministry of Education Humanities and Social Sciences project;
- Results that have received research or publication funding from any institution or department;
- Results for which a publication contract has already been signed with a publisher;
- Results involving an intellectual-property dispute;
- Doctoral dissertations or postdoctoral exit reports defended or completed after January 1, 2008;
- Revised editions of published books, or results with more than 10% overlap with a published book.

The project-management provisions additionally exclude results that have received support from a Ministry of Education major philosophy and social-science project, a major project of a Ministry Humanities and Social Sciences Key Research Base, the National Social Science Fund or the National Natural Science Foundation of China. Applicants responsible for unfinished Ministry of Education research projects within the prescribed period are also ineligible.

### Scope and review

Eligible outputs include foundational research that could significantly advance scholarship, original theoretical research, documentary research, translated works and reference books with important academic value and social influence, and important academic results presented in non-paper formats such as data models or databases. Papers, collections of papers, textbooks, research reports and software are excluded from the documentary-research, translated-work and reference-book scope.

The Ministry of Education will conduct eligibility checks and organize expert review. Review follows a recusal rule, and experts will not be appointed from the applicant&apos;s university. Review will consider originality and exploratory value, theoretical or practical value, intellectual-property status and the reasonableness of the requested budget. Applications are submitted through a limited institutional recommendation process rather than directly by individuals. A project may close only after all research is complete and it passes an expert appraisal organized by the Ministry of Education.

| Stage | Deadline and requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Formal review | By May 13, send the electronic *2013 Post-Funding Project Application and Evaluation Book* to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` |
| Paper materials | By May 16, submit the paper materials to Academic Research |
| Electronic materials | By May 16, send electronic materials matching the paper submission to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` |

The paper submission includes seven A4, double-sided copies of the application; four sets of the completed manuscript or non-paper result; two copies each of the title page and copyright page of relevant research results listed in the application; two copies of adoption certificates for research consultation reports; and two copies of evidence concerning the social impact of relevant research results.

The application-form instructions separately state that the form should be printed in six A4 copies, which conflicts with the seven copies required by the application notice. An attached FAQ states that where the application notice differs from the project-management provisions, the application notice controls; matters not addressed by the notice follow the project-management provisions. Applicants should confirm the required number of copies and binding instructions with Academic Research before submission.
