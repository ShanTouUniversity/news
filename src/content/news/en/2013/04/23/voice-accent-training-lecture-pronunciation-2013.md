The fourth lecture in Shantou University English Language Center&apos;s 2013 Voice and Accent Training Lecture Series will be held from **2:15 to 3:45 p.m. on Wednesday, April 24**, in E Lecture Room 101. **Sohani Gandhioke** will lead the session on **“Techniques to Improve Pronunciation.”**

The lecture is open to the entire university community.
