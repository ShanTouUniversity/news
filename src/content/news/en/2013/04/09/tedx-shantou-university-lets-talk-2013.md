It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Zhicheng College and its Ivy Service-Learning Association will hold the TEDx Shantou University talk event on April 13, 2013.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | LET’S TALK |
| Time | 19:27–21:00, April 13, 2013 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee of Zhicheng College, Ivy Service-Learning Association |
| Format | Locally organized TEDx event licensed by TED to share ideas worth spreading and showcase local talent |

Six speakers will share their stories and explorations: Hu ZW, a graduate student of the Law School and core member of a youth public welfare center who served the Asian Games; Liu GZ, a student of the Business School and vice head of the university choir; Guo XZ, an alumna who joined the Teach For China program in Yunnan and whose class ranked first in the county; Associate Professor Xie Lin of the School of Journalism; Associate Professor Cai Zhengzhong of the Law School (social sciences research); and Chen Shuping, known on campus as the “Handstand Brother” and a career counselor.

TED, known for Technology, Entertainment and Design, gathers outstanding figures in science, design, literature and music. TEDx events are locally organized TED-style gatherings. Details are available at http://www.ted.com/tedx.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
