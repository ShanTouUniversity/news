The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Student Clubs Federation will hold the 2012–2013 spring-semester student-club project-selection defense from **2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, April 10** in Building 789, Room 726. Twelve club projects will participate.

Projects include the Shantou University 243 Music Festival, a traditional-culture riddle event, the eighth Shantou University roller-skating competition, a legal-hot-topics discussion, a Go Club open-day showcase, the “Light and Shadow Variations: Meizhou Ancient Town” photography activity, “Impressions of Chaoshan,” a finance training camp, the Sports Dance Association’s 25th-anniversary gala, the “Professional Image” career-image design competition, and the “Hands Speak, Hearts Connect” sign-language week. The Snow Leopard Association’s project title remains listed as to be determined in the attachment.

Students participating in the defense must dress neatly and check in by **2:00 p.m.** They should listen carefully to judges’ questions, answer concisely, avoid interrupting, remain until the total score is announced and prepare in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and Student Clubs Federation)
