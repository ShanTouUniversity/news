STU Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host a Chinese-language media salon on **North Korea’s nuclear crisis and media-reporting strategy from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on April 12** in the Library Science Report Hall.

The speaker is Dr. Chengxian Li, director of the China Research Center at *The Korea Times*. He researches Northeast Asian security and has served as a current-affairs commentator on North Korea for several media organizations. The university community is welcome.
