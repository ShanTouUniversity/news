It is reported that the Department of Social Sciences issued a notice on April 9, 2013, to hold a Moral and Civilization Practice Week to deepen teaching reform for the *Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics* course.

## Schedule

| Section | Item | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Participants | All undergraduates taking *Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics* this semester |
| Schedule | Time/Venue | Weeks 9–11, on campus |
| Content | Content | “Five Ones”: do one good deed, correct one violation, copy one maxim, write one reflection and join one discussion |
| Organizer | Organizer | Department of Social Sciences |

## Detailed Arrangements

| Week | Arrangements |
| --- | --- |
| Week 8 | Instructors brief students on arrangements and requirements |
| Week 9 | Practical class: students go beyond the classroom to do a good deed, correct a violation and copy a maxim on campus, and write a reflection of at least 800 words (for a prize essay) to be submitted in week 10 |
| Week 10 | Each class holds a discussion session on moral and civilization |
| Week 11 | Department organizes a prize essay contest, selecting several reflections per class for evaluation, with awards for outstanding participants |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Department of Social Sciences)
