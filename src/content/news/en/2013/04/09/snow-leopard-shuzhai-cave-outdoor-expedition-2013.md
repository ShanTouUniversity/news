To enrich campus sports and develop environmental awareness, teamwork and communication, STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association will organize an introductory outdoor expedition to Shuzhai Cave in Sangpu Mountain from **April 13 through 14**. The event is open to enrolled undergraduates, with **20 places** available; a lottery will be used if applications exceed the quota.

The route starts at Liantang, passes the Tropic of Cancer marker and continues toward Shuzhai Cave via Huamei Mountain for camping. Participants will hike back to STU the next day. Activities include trekking, outdoor camping, team development and wilderness-survival training.

Participants should enjoy outdoor activities, be prepared for physical exertion, cooperate with and help others, follow the coach’s instructions and remain with the group. They must have no other classes or activities scheduled for April 13 and the morning of April 14. Participants cover their own meals for the two-day, one-night event at **RMB 20 per person**. The organizers will purchase personal accident insurance and provide appropriate camping equipment.

Register by **midnight on April 11**. Download and complete the official form and email it to `12zxzhang@stu.edu.cn`, naming the file with the required cohort, major and name format.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office and Snow Leopard Outdoor Association)
