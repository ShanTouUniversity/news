STU’s Mathematics Department formed teams for the 2013 Mathematical Contest in Modeling for the first time. Under the guidance of Jian Li, 24 undergraduates competed in eight teams and won **one Meritorious Winner award and three Honorable Mentions**.

The international contest has been organized since 1985 by the Consortium for Mathematics and Its Applications and other organizations and attracts students from universities around the world. Following strong results in China’s national mathematical modeling contest, this achievement highlighted the students’ mathematical foundation, academic English, analytical ability and teamwork.

The Mathematics Department said it will continue organizing participation in mathematical modeling competitions to strengthen students’ ability to use mathematical models and computer technology to solve practical problems and to develop innovation and collaboration.
