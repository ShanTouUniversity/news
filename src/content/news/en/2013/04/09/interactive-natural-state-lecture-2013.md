STU’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host the lecture **“The Natural State of Interaction” at 7:30 p.m. on April 10** in the Art School Tiered Classroom. The speaker is Cheng Zhang.

The lecture will address the aesthetics of interaction, affordance in art and design, and new considerations for design in new-media environments. Zhang graduated from the China Academy of Art and the École nationale supérieure d’art de Bourges in France. He is artistic director of Shanghai LUMI Studio, works in contemporary art, researches sound, light and space, and is involved in commercial display, communication, projection, holographic and interactive-installation projects.

The university community is welcome.
