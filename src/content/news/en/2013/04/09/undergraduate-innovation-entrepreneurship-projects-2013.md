STU Academic Affairs Office has publicized the proposed 2013 undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects. Following team applications, expert reviews and recommendations by academic units, and university review, the university proposes **53 projects** for the university program and will recommend them for the provincial undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training program.

The list includes 37 innovation-training projects across the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, School of Art and Design, Business School, School of Law, School of Engineering and School of Science. The 16 entrepreneurship-training and entrepreneurship-practice projects come from schools and colleges across the main campus. Topics include local culture and dialects, public communication, education and social surveys, art and design, regional economics and employment services, legal and social issues, green buildings and materials, engineering structures, automation, information systems, mathematics and physics, food and biotechnology, and business ideas involving campus services, education, cultural products, food and tourism.

The feedback period is **April 9–14**. Objections may be submitted in writing to Academic Affairs Office on the third floor of the New Administration Center. Phone: `86502736`; email: `wtyang@stu.edu.cn`; contact: Weitao Yang.

Project titles and leaders are listed in the Academic Affairs Office attachment. Personal names are not reproduced here.
