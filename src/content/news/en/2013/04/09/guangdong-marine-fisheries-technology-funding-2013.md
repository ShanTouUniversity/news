Following the application guidelines issued by the Guangdong Ocean and Fisheries Bureau and Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance, STU Research Office is organizing applications for the 2013 Guangdong Marine and Fisheries Technology Promotion Special Program. The program supports research, development, demonstration and technology transfer by universities, research institutes, extension organizations, enterprises and relevant organizations.

### Program areas

Research topics cover pearl breeding and aquaculture, efficient and healthy aquaculture, marine biomedicine and functional products, marine and fisheries equipment, marine-resource development and conservation, improved varieties and aquaculture technology, and studies of science and technology’s contribution to the marine and fisheries economy and technology transfer.

Promotion topics cover demonstrations of modern fisheries technologies and models, improved varieties and methods, and modern fisheries equipment, facilities and technologies. Research projects are divided into major, key and general categories, with guideline funding ranges of RMB 1.2–1.5 million, RMB 800,000–1 million and RMB 300,000–500,000 respectively. Promotion projects are divided into key and general categories, with ranges of RMB 800,000–1 million and RMB 200,000–400,000.

Applicants should have relevant research or demonstration foundations, facilities and technical teams; industry–university–research collaboration is encouraged. Major research projects must involve a university or research institute and an enterprise. Project leaders must meet the guideline’s requirements on title, relevant work experience and age. A leader may submit only one project in the same year, and duplicate or overlapping applications on the same research content are not allowed.

### Materials and deadlines

STU applicants must bind the application, feasibility study and supporting materials and submit **nine copies** to the Research Office by **May 8**, while emailing electronic documents to `yxhu@stu.edu.cn`. Contacts are Yunxiang Hu and Changsheng Zhu at `86502813`.

The provincial guidelines require applicants to download the application and feasibility-study formats from the Guangdong Ocean and Fisheries Bureau website at <http://www.gdofa.gov.cn>, submit **eight copies** to its Department of Science, Technology and Exchange Cooperation, and email an electronic version to `kwc@gdofa.gov.cn`. The provincial deadline is **May 15**. The internal STU requirements differ from the provincial guideline in copy count and deadline; applicants should meet both the university’s internal schedule and the formal provincial requirements.
