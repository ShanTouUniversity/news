STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has issued Bulletin 226 for Week 6 of the 2012–2013 spring semester. Inspection teams checked E Building at **2:00 p.m. on April 2** and **8:00 a.m. on April 3**.

| Inspection date | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| April 2 | Five students arrived within five minutes after class began, one arrived more than five minutes late, and two wore slippers in class |
| April 3 | Five students arrived within five minutes after class began |

The bulletin asks schools and colleges to educate the students involved and continue strengthening study-style development. The student list in the attachment is not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Study-Style Development Office)
>
> ⚠️ The inspection list contains student names and is not reproduced; only aggregate findings are reported.
