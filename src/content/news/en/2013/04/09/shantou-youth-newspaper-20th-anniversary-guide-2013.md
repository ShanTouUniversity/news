Founded in 1992, *Shantou Youth* has released a commemorative issue marking its 20th anniversary. The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and *Shantou Youth* published a preview reviewing the newspaper’s development and looking ahead, inviting the university community to follow and participate in campus media.

The issue covers generations of student reporters, guidance and assessments from the university Youth League Committee, and changes in coverage of recurring campus topics such as dormitories, English and the internet. It also introduces the Shantou Youth Online news website, Shantou University Youth Weibo account and mobile newspaper, and reviews anniversary activities including a “History Corridor,” “Blessing Wall” and a “20th Birthday: An Appointment with You” party.
