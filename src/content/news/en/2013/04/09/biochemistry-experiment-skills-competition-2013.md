The Guangdong Undergraduate Biochemical Experiment Skills Competition, part of the seventh Guangdong Student Science and Technology Academic Festival, will be held **May 25–26** at the School of Life Sciences of South China Agricultural University. STU Science School is selecting enrolled undergraduates to participate; applicants should have completed relevant biology or chemistry laboratory courses.

Teams must include one faculty advisor and no more than three members, and cross-cohort teams are encouraged. Submit a registration form and experimental-design document to the Biology Department Office, Science Building Room 444, or email the electronic registration form to `mlyang@stu.edu.cn`.

The notice gives the registration deadline as **April 20, 2012**, with assessment results due by April 23 and the provincial preliminary round on April 24. These dates conflict in year with the 2013 competition schedule and are retained as written; confirm the current arrangements with Science School. Selection is based primarily on written review of the experimental design, with other assessment methods to be announced.

The final round is an eight-hour laboratory session. Organizers will provide commonly used biochemical reagents and instruments; teams may bring items not on the provided list. Experimental designs should cover objectives, principles, equipment and materials, procedures, results and calculations, precautions and a cost estimate.
