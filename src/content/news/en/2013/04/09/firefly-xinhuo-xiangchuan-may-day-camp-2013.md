STU Youth Volunteers Association’s Firefly team will run the **Xinhuo Xiangchuan rural-school May Day work camp from April 29 through May 1** at Xiazuo Primary School in Leiling Town, Chaonan District, Shantou. The team plans to recruit **20 students** from across the university through interviews.

| Team | Main work | Planned places |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Teaching | Teach basic Chinese, mathematics and English and support a school fair | 8 |
| Health service | Assist with basic health checks, health education and everyday-medicine services | 3 |
| Repair | Repair household appliances and share maintenance knowledge | 2 |
| News and editorial | Document the camp through writing, photography, video and Weibo | 2 |
| Logistics | Manage meals, supplies and operational support | 3 |
| Arts and recreation | Organize a school fair and film screenings | 2 |

Online registration closes at **noon on April 12**. On-site registration will be available at consultation booths near Dining Halls 2 and 4 at designated times from April 10 through 12. The notice also gives the overall registration deadline as **noon on April 14**; because the two deadlines differ, applicants should confirm the applicable deadline with the organizers. Interviews are scheduled for April 12 from 7:00 to 9:30 p.m.; April 13 from 8:30 a.m. to noon, 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. and 7:00 to 9:30 p.m.; and April 14 from 8:30 a.m. to noon, in D401 and D402. Changes will be announced separately.

Name the electronic registration form as required and email it to `12pluo@stu.edu.cn`. Volunteer-service hours will be calculated according to the actual number of camp days. More information is available on the Xinhuo Xiangchuan blog: <http://blog.sina.com.cn/fireflyxhxc>.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and Youth Volunteers Association)
