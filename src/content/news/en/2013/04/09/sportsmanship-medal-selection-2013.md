STU has opened its 2013 selection for the **Sportsmanship Medal**, established by the Li Ka Shing Foundation to recognize outstanding graduating students who demonstrate sportsmanship and sports ethics, make significant contributions to university athletics and develop broadly.

The award includes **one medalist** and **two finalists**. The medalist will receive **RMB 8,000** and be honored during the annual commencement period; each finalist will receive a certificate and **RMB 1,000**. The selection committee will identify no more than 10 applicants for interviews before determining the medalist and finalists.

Applicants should demonstrate fair play, teamwork, perseverance and other strong athletic qualities; leadership and team awareness; a significant influence on university sports; participation representing STU in provincial-level or higher competitions or outstanding performance in major campus competitions; and good academic performance.

Applicants must apply through the scholarship website at <http://sholarship.stu.edu.cn> by **4:00 p.m. on April 17** and deliver written materials to the Student Affairs Office Student Aid Management Center, Administration Building Room 106. Materials include an application form, an article of no more than 1,200 Chinese characters on the applicant’s sporting experience and contributions, and two recommendation letters. Published work is optional. The application materials require recommendations from the applicant’s school or college and the Physical Education Department.
