STU Liberal Arts School will open its 10th Student Academic Activity Month with its first academic lecture at **3:00 p.m. on Wednesday, April 10** in E Building Tiered Classroom 203.

Professor Furen Wang, a doctoral supervisor, will speak on **“Learning, Historical Facts and Courage.”** The opening ceremony and lecture will take place at the same time. The university community is welcome.
