STU Science School’s “Modern Mathematics” academic lecture series will present **“Mathematical-Modeling Competition Paper Writing” from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, April 10** in the Mathematics Laboratory, Engineering North 412. The speaker is Jian Li.

Using examples, Li will explain how to write a strong mathematical-modeling competition paper. He received a PhD in science from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2012 and is a faculty member in STU’s Mathematics Department. Teams he coached won multiple awards in China’s 2012 national mathematical modeling contest and the 2013 Mathematical Contest in Modeling.

STU’s 2013 undergraduate mathematical modeling competition will run from **April 12 through 19**. Registration details are available at the university’s modeling website: <http://math.stu.edu.cn/modeling/shownews.asp?id=21>.
