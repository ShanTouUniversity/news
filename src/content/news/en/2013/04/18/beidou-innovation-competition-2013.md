It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, the Department of Education, the Department of Science and Technology, and the Provincial Students' Federation will jointly hold the inaugural Beidou Application Technology Innovation Design Competition as part of the 7th Guangdong University Student Science and Technology Festival. The Youth League Committee of Shantou University forwarded the notice on April 18 to organize campus registration.

## Background and Objectives

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | Inaugural Beidou Application Technology Innovation Design Competition of the 7th Guangdong University Student Science and Technology Festival |
| Organizers | Communist Youth League Guangdong Committee, Department of Education, Department of Science and Technology, Provincial Students' Federation |
| Objectives | Build a platform for youth science and technology exchange, enhance innovation and practical abilities, inspire patriotism, create a well-known youth science brand, and boost the construction and application innovation of the Beidou system |

## Campus Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | All students |
| Campus deadline | Before May 30 |
| Paper submission | Youth League Committee office |
| Electronic submission | o_tw@stu.edu.cn |
| Selection | The campus Youth League Committee will select the best entries for the provincial final based on registrations |

> ⚠️ The attachment of the competition notice is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
