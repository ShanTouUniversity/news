The Shantou University School of Science will hold a two-talk edition of its **Science Salon** at **9:00 a.m. on Monday, April 22, 2013**, in Science and Technology Center 205. The program will examine polar microorganisms and their diversity as well as synthetic biology. Faculty and students are welcome.

| Talk | Speaker | Institution and position | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talk 1 | Dr. Zeng Yinxin | Researcher, Biology and Ecology Laboratory, Polar Research Center of China and Key Laboratory of Polar Science, State Oceanic Administration | Research on Polar Microorganisms and Their Diversity |
| Talk 2 | Dr. Qi Qingsheng | Professor, Shandong University | Synthetic Biology - Synthesis to Understand |

### Topics

Zeng Yinxin&apos;s talk will introduce research on polar microorganisms and their diversity. Qi Qingsheng&apos;s talk will use engineering theory and the design and construction of basic biological components to discuss the synthesis of complex biological functions, systems and artificial life. Drawing on examples of microbial biosynthesis, it will explain how “synthesis to understand” can deepen understanding of biological processes. The synthetic-biology applications discussed include the production of specific chemicals, biomaterials manufacturing, gene therapy and tissue engineering.

### Speakers

Zeng Yinxin is a researcher at the Polar Research Center of China and a master&apos;s supervisor at Jimei University. His research interests include polar and marine microorganisms, microbial molecular ecology and environmental microbiology. His work focuses on microbial community structure and diversity, microbial ecological functions and enzymology. He has participated in scientific expeditions in the Arctic, Antarctic and other polar regions and has led or contributed to projects on polar marine microbial diversity, ecological functions and environmental assessment.

Qi Qingsheng is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Shandong University, a researcher at the National Engineering Research Center for Glycoengineering and a director of the Shandong University Institute of Microbiology. He has received support as a Ministry of Education New Century Excellent Talent. His research focuses on metabolic-pathway design in industrial microorganisms, metabolic engineering and synthetic biology. He has led projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, key Ministry of Education programs, the National 863 Program and subprojects of the 973 Program.
