The Shantou University Shuguang Student Aid team will run the first camp of its 2013 rural-school support program from **April 29 to May 1** at Yingfeng Primary School in Fengtang Town, Chaozhou. With the theme **“Holding Your Hand as We Walk the Road to Dreams,”** the camp is recruiting **20 student volunteers** from across the university.

### Service activities

Volunteers will teach courses for students at a township primary school, with the course content and format designed and delivered by the volunteers. They will also survey local students&apos; learning interests and reading habits to inform follow-up support.

### Application and interviews

Applications close at **10:00 p.m. on April 20**, including on-site registration. Consultation and on-site registration will be available at the Golden Triangle from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on April 19 and 20, with an additional 5:00–7:00 p.m. session on April 19.

Applicants must attend an interview on **April 20** during one of three periods: 9:00–11:30 a.m., 2:30–5:30 p.m. or 7:00–10:00 p.m. The interview location will be announced separately. It will focus on applicants&apos; ideas for teaching-camp courses. Volunteers will be selected on the basis of the interviews; Shuguang volunteers will receive priority when candidates are otherwise equal.

Applicants should have good physical fitness and communication, organizational and emergency-response skills. They should be responsible, hardworking, willing to follow arrangements and able to work in a team. Participants must commit to the full volunteer service from April 29 to May 1 and have enough time to prepare course designs beforehand.

Applications require student names, majors, telephone numbers and email addresses. These materials and student contact details are not reproduced here. The original notice governs the designated application channel and interview location.
