Shantou University Academic Affairs conducted the **April 2013 midterm review** of projects approved under the 2012 Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Plan and has published the results. **All 72 projects** that participated in the review passed, and Academic Affairs will continue to provide second-tranche project funding.

### Project categories

The midterm-review attachment classifies the 72 projects as follows:

| Project category | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Innovation training | 45 |
| Entrepreneurship training | 9 |
| Entrepreneurship practice | 17 |
| Innovation practice | 1 |
| **Total** | **72** |

Academic Affairs reviewed the project midterm-review forms and progress reports, focusing on research progress, interim results, problems and responses, next-stage plans and use of funds.

### Review findings and next steps

The review found that most projects were progressing well, had reasonable next-stage plans and showed good member participation, broadly meeting their expected training objectives. Some teams had prepared draft papers or reports, completed preliminary physical or software designs, filmed videos or built websites, or accumulated experimental data and images. Some projects had established cooperation with companies, while others were preparing patent applications or had received recognition in competitions.

For projects progressing slowly, Academic Affairs recommended adjusting the research schedule and accelerating the work. Teams with questions about research ideas or methods were advised to consult their supervisors and improve their experimental methods. All teams should continue accumulating results and prepare publications and patent applications in line with their original plans, so that projects can be completed on schedule and with improved quality.

The midterm-review forms and progress reports contain project leaders, student members and funding information. The names and personal details are not reproduced here; the official attachment governs the detailed project results.
