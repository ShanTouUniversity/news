The public-service advertising design contest of the **seventh Guangdong University Student Science and Technology Festival** is open to full-time students at regular universities in Guangdong. STU students interested in participating must submit the registration form and work to the STU Youth League Committee by **May 15, 2013**; electronic files should be sent to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. The university will select works for the provincial finals according to the number and quality of submissions.

With the theme **“Innovation Creates the World, Public Service Spreads Civilization,”** the contest invites students to create original public-service advertisements about morality, environmental protection, health, hygiene, education and safety. It is organized by the Guangdong Provincial Youth League Committee, Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and Guangdong Students&apos; Federation; the Youth League Committee of Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts is the host institution, with China Mobile Guangdong as a supporting organization.

### Schedule

| Stage | Dates | Arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promotion and mobilization | April 15–May 15 | Universities promote the contest and collect registrations and works |
| University submissions | May 16–20 | University Youth League committees organize and submit works centrally |
| Expert review | May 21–June 5 | The organizing committee arranges expert judging and award selection |

### Categories and requirements

**Graphic design**: Submit an output-ready electronic file measuring 700×1,000 mm, at 300 dpi in CMYK and JPEG-compressed format. Each file should be 4–5 MB and include an explanatory statement of no more than 300 Chinese characters. Hand-drawn works must be submitted as scanned electronic files with the same explanatory statement.

**Video**: Any genre is accepted, including animation. Videos should be 15, 30 or 45 seconds long, may be shot with a professional or home digital camera, and must be submitted on DVD at a resolution of at least 720×576 pixels.

Works must be original and must not have been fully published in commercial media outside media designated or authorized by the organizing committee before the final award list is released. Entries should have a positive concept, a clear theme, healthy content, standardized language and both social and artistic value. Each participant may submit one work in each category and may enter multiple categories; a separate registration form is required for each work.

### Participation notes

The committee will not charge entry or judging fees, and submitted works will not be returned. Each category will offer first, second and third prizes and additional special awards as determined by the actual contest. Winning individuals and organizations will receive certificates or plaques. Submitting a registration form constitutes acceptance of the contest rules and authorizes the organizers to use entries for noncommercial contest promotion, including exhibitions, performances and publications.

STU students should follow the university Youth League Committee&apos;s internal submission process. Registration forms contain names, school information and contact details and are not reproduced here. The official contest rules govern the final submission requirements.
