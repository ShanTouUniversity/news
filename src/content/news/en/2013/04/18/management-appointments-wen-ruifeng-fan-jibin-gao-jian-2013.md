Shantou University has issued Document No. 48 [2013], announcing the following management appointments:

- Wen Ruifeng was appointed director of the Security Office for a six-month probationary period.
- Fan Jibin was appointed assistant director of the Human Resources Office for a six-month probationary period.
- Gao Jian was appointed concurrent deputy director of the editorial office of *Shantou University Journal*.

The university also announced that Chen Shaogang no longer serves concurrently as director of the Security Office. The decisions were considered at the fourth STU leadership meeting of 2013 on April 9 and formally issued on April 18.

### Professional backgrounds

According to the public biographies, Wen Ruifeng previously served as deputy director of STU&apos;s Resource Management Office and deputy director of the Security Office, as well as head of the Security Affairs Office. He has worked in campus security and resource management for many years. Fan Jibin previously worked in STU&apos;s Human Resources Office and holds a master&apos;s degree in management and the title of senior economist in human-resources management. Gao Jian previously served as deputy director of STU&apos;s Office of Scientific Research and secretary of its directly affiliated Party branch. He holds a master&apos;s degree in higher education and is an associate research fellow.
