Shantou University Academic Affairs has announced the midterm examination arrangements for **[MAT1210] Advanced Mathematics II**. The examination will be held during **periods 1–2 on Wednesday of week 9, April 24, 2013**. The notice provides examination rooms and listed student numbers for some sections.

| Class number | Instructor | Examination room | Listed students |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 58724 | Lin Xiaoping | Lecture Hall 1 | 98 |
| 58725 | Xie Changzhen | Moot Court | 96 |
| 58726 | Tan Chaoqiang | Residence Hall D, Room 503 | 95 |
| 59856 | Li Junde | E Tiered Classroom | 101 |
| 58723 | Chen Yanming and Li Junde | Not specified in the notice | Not specified in the notice |

Students must attend in the assigned rooms and sit with one seat between examinees. Students who have applied for an exemption from attending the course must also take the examination on time. The current notice does not provide the room or student number for section 58723; those students should follow any later official arrangement from Academic Affairs.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
