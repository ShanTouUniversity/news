Shantou University has issued Document No. 47 [2013], appointing **Mei Zhiping** as head of the Biology Department in the School of Science for a **six-month probationary period**. The university also announced that **Zhang Yueling** no longer serves as head of the department.

The appointment was considered at the fourth STU leadership meeting of 2013 on April 9, and the university formally issued the appointment document on April 18. STU had previously publicized Mei Zhiping&apos;s proposed appointment through a preappointment notice.

### Mei Zhiping&apos;s background

According to the public biography attached to the document, Mei Zhiping was born in May 1967 in Chongming, Shanghai. He holds a doctorate and is a professor and master&apos;s supervisor. From 1997 to 2002, he pursued doctoral studies at Laval University in Canada. He later conducted postdoctoral research at Old Dominion University in the United States, the University of Quebec at Rimouski in Canada and Mount Allison University, and served as an assistant research scientist at the Horn Point Laboratory of the University of Maryland Center for Environmental Science.

Mei Zhiping has been a professor at STU&apos;s Institute of Marine Biology in the School of Science since October 2011.
