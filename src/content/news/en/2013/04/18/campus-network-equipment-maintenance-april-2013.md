Under the campus-network upgrade plan, the Shantou University Network and Information Center will upgrade network equipment in two maintenance windows. Gateway equipment will be serviced early on April 19, followed by aggregation equipment serving regional network backbones early on April 21.

| Maintenance | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Gateway equipment upgrade | 12:00–6:00 a.m., April 19, 2013 |
| Aggregation-equipment access upgrade | 12:00–6:00 a.m., April 21, 2013 |

During each window, external internet access for all campus-network users and external services provided by campus systems will be interrupted. The notice does not give a separate restoration time; users may resume service when the network becomes available.

For network-service inquiries, contact the Network and Information Center:

- Telephone: 0754-86503400-1
- Email: `stunic@stu.edu.cn`
