Because of the university&apos;s May Day holiday arrangements, the Shantou University Law School judicial-examination training scheduled for **April 26, 27 and 28, 2013** will be suspended.

The notice states that the subsequent training schedule will be adjusted, but it does not provide a new resumption date or timetable. Registered students should follow the Law School&apos;s later notice, which will govern the updated arrangement.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Change | Judicial-examination training suspended |
| Suspension dates | April 26, 27 and 28 |
| Reason | University May Day holiday arrangements |
| Resumption | Not specified in the current notice; to be announced by the Law School |

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Law)
