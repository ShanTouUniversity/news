The Shantou University **Voyage Program: 2013 Career Paths** event will take place from **2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on April 24, 2013**, at the Coffee Book House. Open to all STU students, the event will select **40 participants** from résumé submissions and give students an opportunity to discuss career experience and planning with business managers and working professionals.

### Format

The event will use a coffee-chat format. Students will be divided into eight groups of five according to major. Each group will meet with one business manager or alumnus, who will comment on students&apos; résumés and share workplace experience. An open networking session will follow.

Applicants should prepare their own résumés and email them to `12ljin@stu.edu.cn` or `12xxren@stu.edu.cn` by **midnight on April 22**. Staff will randomly select 40 participants from the submissions, publish the final list through the office-automation system and contact those selected.

The event is intended to help students understand workplaces through direct conversation, hear from business managers and working professionals, expand career networks and plan their careers.
