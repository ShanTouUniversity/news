Following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University Graduate School is coordinating applications for **Guangdong&apos;s 2013 demonstration bases for jointly training graduate students**. The application is open to universities in Guangdong authorized to provide graduate education. Eligible partners may include technology-innovation enterprises, regional authorities in areas with concentrations of such enterprises, industry-management authorities and research institutes.

Applicant universities and partners should have a sound foundation for jointly training graduate students or conducting collaborative research. Applications should clarify each party&apos;s responsibilities, establish the base&apos;s operating and management mechanisms, and explore innovative approaches to talent development in light of the partners&apos; strengths and needs.

### Application materials

Relevant schools and applicants must complete the *Feasibility Demonstration Report for Guangdong Joint Graduate-Training Demonstration Bases*, the *Application Form for Guangdong Joint Graduate-Training Demonstration Bases* and the application summary table. All paper materials must be printed double-sided on A4 paper. The report and application form must be bound separately, with **10 copies** of each; the summary table requires **one copy**.

Materials must reach the Graduate School by **May 10, 2013**. Electronic documents should be sent to `xwb@stu.edu.cn`; late submissions will not be accepted.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Program | Guangdong 2013 joint graduate-training demonstration bases |
| Applicants | Guangdong universities authorized to provide graduate education and eligible partners |
| Partner types | Technology-innovation enterprises, regional or industry authorities, research institutes and similar organizations |
| Paper materials | 10 copies each of the report and application; 1 summary table |
| Deadline | May 10 |
| Submitting office | STU Graduate School |
| Email | `xwb@stu.edu.cn` |

Applications should explain the need for and feasibility of the base, overall construction goals and plan, management and operating mechanisms, joint-supervisor development, research collaboration, resource sharing, graduate-training goals and training plan. The forms contain partner-leader, supervisor, funding and research data, and are not reproduced here.
