Following preliminary screening and interviews, Shantou University&apos;s School of Law has announced the final results of its **2013 Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship Program**. **Twenty-eight students** were selected, and **two Public Administration students** were placed on the alternate list.

The selected students include **13 students from the 2010 Public Administration cohort**, **eight students from the 2010 law cohort** and **seven students from the 2011 law cohort**. Because some Public Administration internship places were still being confirmed, the school also named one first alternate and one second alternate.

| Group | Selected students |
| --- | ---: |
| 2010 Public Administration cohort | 13 |
| 2010 law cohort | 8 |
| 2011 law cohort | 7 |
| **Total selected** | **28** |
| Public Administration alternates | 2 |

The final results followed the earlier application and interview stages. The Law School had previously selected 46 students for interviews: 21 from Public Administration, 12 from the 2010 law cohort and 13 from the 2011 law cohort.

Because the original notice publishes student names and includes one damaged text entry, the selected and alternate lists are not reproduced here. Students should check their individual results through the Law School or OA system. The school&apos;s subsequent notice will govern the internship arrangements.
