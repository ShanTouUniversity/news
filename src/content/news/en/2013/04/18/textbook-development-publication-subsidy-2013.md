Shantou University Academic Affairs has opened the application and review process for its **first-half 2013 textbook development and publication subsidies**. Current or retired staff at the main campus who plan to publish a textbook in the first half of 2013 and meet the university&apos;s conditions may apply.

Applicants must submit three copies of the materials to the Academic Affairs service window on the third floor of the New Administration Building by **May 17, 2013**, and send an electronic copy of the application form to `09myzhou@stu.edu.cn`. The contact person is Zhou Mengyi, telephone 86502736.

### Eligibility

Applicants must be the first author or editor-in-chief of a textbook included in a publisher&apos;s publication plan, with financial difficulty in textbook development or publication. Before formal publication, the textbook should have been used as an internal textbook at least twice with positive results. Textbooks or related projects that have received a provincial teaching-achievement award or the university&apos;s first prize for teaching achievements receive priority.

A textbook that has already been published without approved university subsidy is not eligible for a new application. A non-new textbook being republished may qualify for a publication subsidy based on its innovation and value, but not for a development subsidy. A textbook that has already received an STU development or publication subsidy will not be considered again.

### Subsidy categories and standards

| Category | Main use or eligibility | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Textbook development subsidy | Pre-publication development, including CD or software production, printing and textbook appraisal | Up to RMB 5,000 |
| Class A publication subsidy | Textbooks linked to national projects, major provincial or ministerial projects, national-level publishers or joint funding by national funds | Up to RMB 5,000 per 100,000 Chinese characters |
| Class B publication subsidy | Textbooks judged valuable for publication but not meeting the Class A conditions | Up to RMB 3,000 per 100,000 Chinese characters |

The combined ceiling for the two subsidies is **RMB 20,000**. Development subsidies do not cover travel, computer equipment or network-platform expenses. Publication subsidies may be used only to pay the publisher; applicants must cover amounts exceeding the approved subsidy.

A subsidized textbook must state on its cover, title page or preface that its publication received an STU textbook subsidy and identify STU as the workplace of the first author or editor-in-chief.

### Materials and review

Applicants should first confirm the publisher, publication plan and required funding with the publisher. They must complete the Academic Affairs application form, obtain review and recommendation from their school and signatures and seals from the responsible leader, and submit a complete manuscript meeting the “complete, clear and final” standard, appraisal letters from two peer professors including at least one from another university, the publisher&apos;s final-review opinion and a copy of the author&apos;s contract with the publisher. Applicants seeking only a development subsidy may temporarily omit the contract but must provide a proposed-publication agreement.

Academic Affairs will organize a review and submit subsidy recommendations for approval by the responsible vice president before publishing the approved textbook information. Applications and reviews are generally accepted in April and October each year; late applications are not accepted. The application form contains detailed applicant, publication-contract and funding information and is not reproduced here.
