The Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host an **Architecture, Landscape and Interior Design Sketch Exchange Exhibition** from **April 19 to 25, 2013**. The exhibition will be held in the school&apos;s first-floor exhibition hall, and faculty and students are welcome to visit.

Organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, the exhibition features works supplied by the Lushan Sketch Training Camp. It will display design sketches by students of the school and students from across China, covering interior design, architecture and landscape design, along with selected works from professional classrooms.
