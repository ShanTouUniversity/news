To support the preparation of university graduate-employment plans and recommendation and dispatch procedures requested by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University Student Affairs has begun collecting employment-resource information for the 2014 graduating cohort.

**2014 main-campus undergraduate graduates and all graduate students** must log in to `http://gdc.stu.edu.cn` and submit their personal information by **May 8, 2013, inclusive**.

### Access steps

After logging in to the designated platform, graduates should select the “on-campus graduating student” entry, open the “Personal Management Center” and use the “Basic Information” menu on the portal homepage to enter and check their information. Information such as place of origin, which affects graduate dispatch, should be verified carefully. Students should provide supporting documents through the designated channel when requested by the platform or their school.

The collected information will be used to prepare graduate-employment plans and support subsequent recommendation and dispatch work. The attachment includes sensitive instructions concerning accounts, household registration, place of origin, identity documents and personal data. Specific fields, examples and document requirements are not reproduced here.

For questions, contact the Graduate Employment Guidance Center:

- Email: `job@stu.edu.cn`
- Telephone: 0754-86502252
