STU has launched its 2013 Li Ka Shing Foundation Distinguished Teaching Award selection. Established in 2003, the award recognizes faculty members who successfully combine teaching and research and demonstrate outstanding teaching achievements and contributions to the university’s teaching mission.

The 2013 Selection Committee is chaired by Xiaobo Wen. Its named members are Fengqi Ma, Ping Li, Zengjian Lou, Yan Huang and Wang Zhu; one additional member’s name cannot be reliably reconstructed from the corrupted source text. Relevant units must submit nominee names and required materials by **May 3** under the *Li Ka Shing Foundation Distinguished Teaching Award Guidelines and Procedures*, amended in 2007. Contact: Mengyi Zhou, `86502736`, `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn`.

### Eligibility and review

Under the amended guidelines, faculty members generally qualify for nomination if they have taught at least eight regularly scheduled courses during the previous four semesters and at least one undergraduate course each academic year. Reduced teaching-load thresholds apply to deans, administrative leaders, associate deans and department or center leaders who also hold management responsibilities. Recipients may not be nominated again for five years, and nominations for faculty on leave or away from campus are deferred until they return.

Criteria include command of the subject, continued scholarly growth, organization and presentation of course material, course design and redesign, inspiring independent thinking, encouraging intellectual interest and creative work, enthusiasm for teaching and learning, and supervision of student research.

The process has two stages. In Stage One, departments submit a nomination letter, a chronological list of courses taught during the previous four semesters and a summary of teaching evaluations. Finalists in Stage Two submit a teaching statement, résumé, teaching responsibilities outside class, letters of support, course evaluation and grade-distribution data, and course materials, and are observed teaching by at least two committee members. Candidate materials and evaluation data are submitted through the university process and are not reproduced here.

### Award arrangements

The annual notice refers to selecting recipients from the Sciences, Humanities and Medicine, with the Medical College conducting its own selection. The amended guidelines specify one recipient each in Sciences and Humanities at the main campus, with the Medical College running a separate selection. Both descriptions are retained; the final number of recipients follows the current-year arrangements of the Selection Committee and Li Ka Shing Foundation.

The amended guidelines state that the two main-campus recipients receive a Li Ka Shing Foundation gift and an all-expenses-paid seven-day academic visit or exchange in a foreign country of their choice. Results should generally be announced no later than the end of June, with the award ceremony forming part of Convocation.
