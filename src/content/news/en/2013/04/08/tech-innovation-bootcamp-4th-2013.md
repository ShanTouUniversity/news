It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Science and Technology Development Center of the College of Engineering will hold the 4th Tech Innovation Bootcamp on April 13–14, 2013.

The bootcamp aims to implement the EIP-CDIO engineering education reform goals, enhance students’ innovation capabilities and foster a culture of hands-on practice and practical innovation.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Green Technology, Harmonious Innovation |
| Time | April 13–14, 2013 |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, College of Engineering |
| Co-organizer | Student Science and Technology Development Center |
| Participants | First- and second-year students intending to apply for 2013 innovation projects |
| Program | See Attachment 1 in the OA notice |

## Registration

Download and complete the application form (Attachment 2), rename the file as “Grade + Major + Name” and send it to the designated email address. The deadline is 24:00 on April 11, 2013. The form and email are available in the OA attachment.

Participants must follow the organizer’s arrangements, complete the form carefully and dress neatly (male students must wear long trousers; no slippers allowed).
