To strengthen standardized grassroots development, summarize annual work and improve organizational cohesion, capacity and creativity, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University is conducting a **2012 assessment of grassroots Youth League organizations**.

The assessment covers annual evaluations of Youth League branches and school or college Youth League committees. Branch evaluation includes ideological education, organizational development, second-classroom activities and members’ exemplary role, using branch self-assessment and reviews by general branches and school committees. School and college committee evaluation covers political and ideological education, organizational development and standardized administration, and services supporting Youth League members’ growth. The university committee will conduct that review in April, with the exact date to be announced.

Youth League organizations should review and summarize their annual work objectively, address omissions and complete assigned tasks on time. Each school or college committee must complete branch assessments by **April 16** and submit stamped assessment forms and supporting evidence to the university committee for filing.

Assessment results will be an important basis for awards and recognition within the Youth League system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
>
> ⚠️ Assessment forms and supporting materials are completed internally by Youth League organizations and are not reproduced.
