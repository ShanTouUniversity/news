STU Graduate School plans to organize a one-day **“Cultural Tour: Exploring Chaoshan Culture”** on **April 13** for all graduate students. The activity has **40 places**, allocated in order of registration.

The tour is intended to help graduate students experience and understand Chaoshan’s cultural traditions and to encourage them to organize their observations using their academic expertise. Participants will be divided into four cross-school and cross-discipline groups. After the study visits, each group must submit a summary report containing learning outcomes from different disciplines and present its findings.

Participants must follow group instructions, may not act alone, and should demonstrate teamwork and willingness to help others. Register by **3:00 p.m. on April 10** by sending the form as directed by Graduate School to `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn`. Use the required format, “cohort + major + name + mobile number + Cultural Tour,” as the email subject.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
