In response to the first confirmed human H7N9 avian-influenza cases reported in China at the time, STU’s General Office, Student Affairs Office and Office of Resource Management issued public-health guidance, urging the university community to take precautions during the seasonal transition. This is a historical health notice published in 2013; follow current guidance from health authorities for present-day health concerns.

The notice recommended balanced nutrition, adequate sleep, sufficient water intake and avoidance of excessive fatigue. It also advised regular handwashing, covering the nose and mouth when coughing or sneezing, keeping indoor air moving and avoiding poorly ventilated places.

People with fever, coughing, a runny nose or other respiratory symptoms should seek medical care promptly. Avoid close contact with poultry and do not touch dead or sick wild birds. Chicken and other poultry products should be cooked thoroughly, and undercooked eggs should be avoided. When visiting crowded places or traveling, take appropriate precautions; people with underlying conditions should carry necessary medication and seek care if they feel unwell.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office, Student Affairs Office and Office of Resource Management)
