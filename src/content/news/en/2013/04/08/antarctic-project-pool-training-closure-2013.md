Following arrangements by the Student Affairs Office’s Outdoor Development Office, the STU campus swimming pool will be used for fitness training and testing for the 2014 Antarctic Expedition Project from **April 8**. It will temporarily be unavailable to faculty members and students outside the project.

The date when the pool will reopen to the university community will be announced separately. Please follow subsequent notices.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
