STU’s Office of Resource Management is recruiting **one water and electrical facilities management technician** from the public. The position is an auxiliary contract position; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow university regulations.

### Requirements

Applicants should have sound professional ethics, initiative, responsibility, rigor, teamwork and dedication; a college diploma in electrical and mechanical engineering, building electrical engineering or a related field; and practical experience maintaining high- and low-voltage equipment, water-supply and drainage equipment and related facilities. Applicants should be no older than 35, in good health and compliant with family-planning policy.

### Duties

The technician will inspect the university’s No. 1 and No. 2 high-voltage switchgear stations, provide preliminary analyses of high-voltage-system faults and maintenance recommendations, inspect pump rooms in the east and west campus areas, and provide preliminary analyses and recommendations for variable-frequency pressurized-water systems. Other duties include maintaining campus water and electrical facilities records, managing departmental fixed assets, preparing annual maintenance plans and related rules, and covering shifts or other assigned work.

### Application

By **April 20**, send a sealed mailing with a résumé and copies of identity, graduation, professional-training, technical-qualification and family-planning documents to the Water and Electrical Center of STU’s Office of Resource Management. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of interviews and must bring originals for review. Originals will be returned after review; other materials will not be returned. Applicants should retain their own copies.

Address: STU Water and Electrical Center, Office of Resource Management, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong, postal code `515063`. Contacts: Weizhong Cai and Shenhao Chen.
