STU Liberal Arts School will hold its 10th Student Academic Month from **April 10 through May 8**. The program includes academic lectures, a writing contest, a dubbing competition and the “Knowledge Expert” general-knowledge quiz, open to all enrolled students.

### Academic lectures

| Date | Speaker | Topic | Location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| April 10 | Professor Furen Wang | Contemporary cultural trends | E Building Tiered Classroom 203 |
| April 24 | Professor Wang Zhu | Modern Western thought and modern British literature | To be announced |

### Writing contest

The writing contest has fiction, drama, prose, poetry, criticism and English-creative-writing categories. Fiction and drama should generally be no longer than 8,000 Chinese characters or words, prose no longer than 5,000, and criticism no longer than 5,000. Works must be original and unpublished in public newspapers or periodicals. Each participant may submit one work in each Chinese category and no more than two English works.

The activity plan gives the submission deadline as **11:00 p.m. on April 7**, with judging from April 9 through 20 and results announced April 22. Submit both a printed copy and an electronic copy; printed copies must not contain author information, while electronic submissions should include the required contact information. First, second and third prizes carry RMB 500, RMB 300 and RMB 100 respectively, with certificates. Winning drama works may be considered for the Liberal Arts School’s original drama gala. Follow the school’s notice for the submission method.

### Dubbing competition

Registration runs from **April 7 through 14**. The preliminary round is April 14 and the semifinal is April 21 in E Building Classroom 207. Participants may enter individually or in teams of two to four. Videos may be in Chinese or English and should have positive subject matter; preliminary videos must be 5–8 minutes long in MP4 or FLV format. Finalists will dub live and be scored by judges. The competition offers two first prizes, two second prizes, two third prizes and six excellence prizes, with certificates and gifts.

### “Knowledge Expert” quiz

Registration runs from **April 12 through 19**. The quiz will be held from **2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on Sunday, April 21** in E Building Tiered Classroom 202 and is open to all enrolled students. If there are no more than 30 participants, they will be randomly divided into Groups A and B. The four rounds are required-answer questions, a challenger round, audience questions and a final battle for the title. Prizes include an e-book worth about RMB 200 for the “Knowledge Expert,” a dictionary or book set worth about RMB 100 for the runner-up, and books worth a combined RMB 60 for third through fifth places.
