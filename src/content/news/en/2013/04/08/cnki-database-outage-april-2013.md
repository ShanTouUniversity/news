STU Library has reported that the CNKI database servers are experiencing a failure and are temporarily unavailable. The provider is carrying out emergency repairs; a restoration time was not given.

While repairs continue, readers needing Chinese-language literature may use the VIP database at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=135> or the Duxiu Knowledge Base at <http://www.duxiu.com>.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Library)
