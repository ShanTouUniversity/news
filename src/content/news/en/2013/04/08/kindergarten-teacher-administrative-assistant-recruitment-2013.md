STU is recruiting **one kindergarten teacher and administrative assistant** from the public. The position is an auxiliary contract position; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow relevant university standards.

### Requirements

Applicants should be hardworking, caring, responsible and willing to follow work arrangements; no older than 40 and in good health; and compliant with family-planning policy requirements. Applicants should have at least a secondary vocational qualification in early-childhood education or formal early-childhood teacher training, plus a kindergarten-teacher qualification certificate and an industry health certificate. Kindergarten experience is preferred.

### Duties

Duties include kindergarten administration, teaching support and logistics; some teaching; management of children’s meals, food, drinking water and environmental hygiene; care of property and facilities; regular safety checks of large play equipment, grounds, buildings, furniture and related facilities; management of teaching materials; support for child health services; accounting; and early-morning, evening, holiday and other assigned shifts.

### Application

By **5:00 p.m. on April 20**, send a résumé and copies of education, teacher-qualification, health, professional-training, family-planning and identity documents by email or mail to the STU kindergarten under the Office of Resource Management. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of interviews and must bring originals for review. Materials are for this recruitment only; originals will be returned after review, while other materials will not be returned. Applicants should retain their own copies.

Address: STU Kindergarten, Office of Resource Management, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong, postal code `515063`. Contacts: Teachers Deng and Chen. Email: `o_yey@stu.edu.cn`. Phone: `0754-82902088` (Office of Resource Management and Human Resources Office). Mark the position in the email or mailing subject.
