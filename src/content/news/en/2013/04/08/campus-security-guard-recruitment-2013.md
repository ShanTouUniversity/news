STU’s Office of Resource Management is recruiting several campus security officers from the public. The positions are auxiliary contract positions; employment relationships will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow university standards.

### Requirements

Applicants should have at least a junior-middle-school education; be male, no older than 50 and in good health; comply with family-planning policy; and be able to work a 24-hour shift system. Experience in security or fire protection, or military-service experience, is preferred. Applicants should also be responsible, disciplined, cooperative and willing to follow work arrangements.

### Duties

Officers will guard campus gates, patrol the campus and protect key areas and public spaces; maintain order in teaching, residential and other activity areas; protect the lives and property of faculty, staff and students; assist with fire and theft prevention and emergency response; help university departments and public-security authorities address conduct endangering campus safety; mediate internal security disputes; and prevent security threats and the infiltration or disruption of campus by illegal religious groups.

Officers will work a 24-hour rotation and must follow duty, reporting and shift-handover rules, remain properly dressed and not leave their posts. They must also know how to operate and maintain surveillance and fire-protection equipment.

### Application

By **April 20**, send a résumé and copies of identity, family-planning, graduation, non-criminal-record, professional-training and technical-qualification documents by email or mail to the campus security office of STU’s Office of Resource Management. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of interviews and must bring originals for review. Originals will be returned after review; other materials will not be returned. Applicants should retain their own copies.
