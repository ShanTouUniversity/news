STU Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication’s Wednesday Movie Night will screen David Lean’s **The Bridge on the River Kwai** from **7:00 to 10:00 p.m. on Wednesday, April 10** in the Library Theater.

Set against the history of the Burma–Thailand railway and the bridge built by the Japanese military during World War II, the film explores conflict among a prisoner-of-war camp, bridge construction and an Allied sabotage mission. It won seven awards at the 30th Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Director and Best Actor.

The screening has 150 seats, with 50 seats open to the public on a first-come, first-served basis. Students must present their campus cards. The university community is welcome.
