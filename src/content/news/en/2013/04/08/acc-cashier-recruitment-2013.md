STU’s Academic Exchange Service Center, under the Office of Resource Management, is recruiting **one cashier** from the public. The position is an auxiliary contract position; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation is negotiable.

Applicants should have at least a high-school education, an accounting qualification certificate and computer skills; be under 40 and in good health; comply with family-planning policy; and be responsible, diligent and cooperative.

The cashier will handle daily cash receipts and internal reimbursements, weekly check payments, cash-operation records, month-end reconciliation and adjustments, monthly statement organization and verification, settlement follow-up with university departments, and asset and equipment additions or disposals, along with other assigned duties.

By **April 18**, send a résumé and copies of graduation, identity and other relevant certificates by email or mail to STU Academic Exchange Service Center. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of interviews and must bring originals for review. Originals will be returned after review; other materials will not be returned. Applicants should retain their own copies.

Address: STU Academic Exchange Service Center, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong, postal code `515063`. Contacts: Teachers Weng and Li. Phone: `0754-82902668`. Email: `stuacc@stu.edu.cn`. Mark “ACC cashier application” in the email or mailing subject.
