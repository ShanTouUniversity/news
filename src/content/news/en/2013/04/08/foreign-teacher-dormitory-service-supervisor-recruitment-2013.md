STU’s Office of Resource Management is recruiting **one customer-service supervisor for foreign-faculty housing**. The position is an auxiliary contract position; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow university standards.

### Requirements

Applicants should have a college diploma or above, be no older than 40 and in good health, and comply with family-planning policy. They should have organizational and coordination skills, official-document writing and verbal communication skills, basic proficiency with office software, and enough spoken English to communicate directly with foreign faculty.

### Duties

The supervisor will organize, inspect and follow up daily housing services; handle routine matters; process move-in and move-out procedures and confirm furniture and appliances; receive and follow up repair requests for room equipment and shared areas; coordinate concentrated construction repairs and painting during winter and summer breaks; assist with emergencies such as theft, gas leaks, lost keys and accidental lockouts; and help resolve resident complaints and disputes such as noise issues.

### Application

By **April 20**, send a sealed résumé and copies of identity, graduation, technical-qualification, professional-training and family-planning documents by email or mail to the Property Office of STU’s Office of Resource Management. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of interviews and must bring originals for review. Originals will be returned after review; other materials will not be returned. Applicants should retain their own copies.

Mailing address: Property Office, Office of Resource Management, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong, postal code `515063`. Contacts: Teachers Chen and Huang. Phones: `0754-86502491`, `0754-86502494`. Fax: `0754-82903697`. Email: `o_ssc@stu.edu.cn`. Mark the position in the email or mailing subject.
