It is reported that Shantou University's Resource Management Office, together with the Personnel Office, has announced the recruitment of one maintenance technician for its Equipment and Materials Management Office. Applications must be sent by email or post by April 20.

## Position Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Post | Maintenance technician, Equipment and Materials Management Office (1 opening) |
| Employment | Auxiliary contract staff; personnel relations not transferred to STU; pay per university standards |
| Deadline | April 20, 2013 |
| Contact | Teacher Hong (0754-82902827) |

## Requirements

Applicants should hold a technical secondary school degree or above, have experience repairing electronic instruments and household appliances, and possess an operation or qualification certificate in electromechanical or electronic equipment. Candidates must be no older than 40 and in good health, be able to use computers, read English materials at a basic level and communicate simply in English. ISO training is preferred.

## Responsibilities

The technician will repair teaching, laboratory and administrative equipment as well as appliances in student and staff dormitories, maintain the online repair-request system, follow up after-sales service and maintenance of campus equipment, provide technical appraisals and fault analyses of equipment slated for disposal to inform procurement plans, and keep maintenance records in accordance with ISO procedure documents.

## How to Apply

Applicants must submit a detailed résumé, copies of diplomas and professional certificates, and a statement of work plans (no more than 1,000 words). Emails should be marked "Application — Maintenance Technician, Equipment and Materials Management Office." Shortlisted candidates will be notified of interviews separately.
