It is reported that the Resource Management Office and the Personnel Office issued a recruitment notice on April 8, 2013, to openly recruit two administrators for the Student Residential Community Management Service Center.

## Position Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Vacancies | 2 |
| Requirements | Good political quality, strong dedication, integrity, diligence and teamwork; ability to work 24-hour shifts; under 40 years old; associate degree in property or hotel management, or vocational school degree in electrical work with an intermediate technician qualification or above |
| Responsibilities | Duty and patrol in the residential area, facility inspection and repair follow-up, greening and cleaning checks, handling student inquiries and reception of visitors, handling emergencies and completing tasks assigned by superiors |
| Employment | Auxiliary contract worker, personnel relationship not transferred to Shantou University, treatment per university standards |

## Application

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Period | April 8–22, 2013 |
| How to apply | Complete the *Shantou University Open Recruitment Application Form*, and send it with resume and scanned copies of graduation certificate, family planning certificate, technical qualification certificate and ID card to dorm@stu.edu.cn with subject “Application for Community Center Administrator” |
| Screening | Shortlisted candidates will be notified for written examination and interview; materials will not be returned, so applicants should keep copies |
| Contact | Ms./Mr. Lin |
| Phone | 0754-86502661 |
| Email | dorm@stu.edu.cn |
| Address | Office, Building C, Student Dormitories, Student Residential Community Management Service Center, Resource Management Office, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, 515063 |
| Issued by | Resource Management Office, Personnel Office |
