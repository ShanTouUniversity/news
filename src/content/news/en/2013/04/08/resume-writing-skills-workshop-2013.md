STU English Language Centre will host a **resume-writing workshop from 10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Friday, April 12** at the Centre for Independent Language Learning. The workshop will be led by Bai Xian.

The workshop will discuss how to present individual strengths and communicate a positive attitude in a concise résumé, helping participants improve the résumé skills needed to obtain interviews. Reserve a seat at <http://resumews.eventbrite.com>.
