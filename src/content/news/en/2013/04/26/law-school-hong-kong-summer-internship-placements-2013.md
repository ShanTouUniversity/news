STU's School of Law has announced the placement arrangements for its 2013 Hong Kong summer internships. According to the notice, **28 selected students** will undertake summer internships at **16 organizations in Hong Kong** from **July 4 through July 31, 2013**, a period of 28 days.

### Numbers and dates

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Internship location | Hong Kong |
| Internship organizations | 16 |
| Selected students | 28 |
| Law department | 15 students |
| Public Administration department | 13 students |
| Internship period | July 4–31, 2013, 28 days |

### Placement details

The original notice uses two images to list the placements for 15 students from the law department and 13 from the Public Administration department. The archived directory does not contain those images, and the original image address currently returns a web-proxy page rather than image data. The organization names and individual student-to-organization mappings therefore cannot be read reliably and are not inferred here.

The earlier final-results notice identified 28 formal selections and two Public Administration alternates. This follow-up notice adds the internship period, geographic scope and department totals for the selected students. Individual placements should be confirmed through the School of Law or the original OA notice.
