STU's University Union has issued a supplement to the Shengxiang Flooring group-purchase arrangements. Shengxiang Flooring's Shantou Guotai store will hold the group-purchase event for STU faculty and staff from **10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Saturday, May 4**. A dedicated shuttle will take faculty and staff who have registered or are interested in attending to the event venue.

### Shuttle schedule

| Pickup point | Time |
| --- | --- |
| University union entrance | 9:15 a.m., May 4 |
| Entrance to Anju Shantou University Garden | 9:25 a.m., May 4 |

The supplement also states that Shengxiang Flooring will continue consultation from **9:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. on April 27** on the first floor of the university union office. Interested faculty and staff may visit the office to learn more about the group-purchase arrangements.

### Promotional terms

The terms supplied with the supplement state that all products will be offered at 77% of the listed price. A RMB 200 earnest deposit paid on the consultation and registration day may become RMB 400 in purchase credit; the deposit is refundable if no order is placed, and a commemorative gift is provided. Factory accessories such as skirting boards and metal transition strips are offered at 51% of the listed price.

Groups reaching 50, 80 or 100 participating households receive an additional 1%, 2% or 3% reduction on top of the 77% offer. Customers whose new homes have not begun renovation may reserve products early and receive a one-year price guarantee and related per-square-meter reductions under the stated conditions. Whole-home wood-flooring orders or orders covering at least 60 square meters also receive additional offers.

The supplied terms state that the offers may be combined, although a small number of special-price products are excluded. Final discounts, group thresholds, deposit terms and product conditions should be confirmed at the event and with Shengxiang Flooring.

### Consultation

The Shengxiang Flooring Shantou Guotai store service hotlines are `88164541` and `88787820`. This article does not contain faculty or staff registration or transaction details.
