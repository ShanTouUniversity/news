STU's Resource Management Office has announced the renewal of the accident-insurance contract for all full-time students studying on the main campus, including graduate students. The renewal is intended to strengthen student safety protection and reduce the costs and administrative burden of separate insurance purchases by university units and student organizations for activities.

### Insurance arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Covered population | All full-time students on the main campus, including graduate students |
| Coverage period | May 1, 2013 to April 30, 2014 |
| Maximum amount under the renewed coverage | RMB 50,000 |
| Combined maximum including the previous coverage | RMB 60,000 |
| Geographic scope | Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan |

The renewed coverage has a maximum amount of RMB 50,000. Together with the RMB 10,000 previously purchased, the combined maximum is RMB 60,000. Actual claims remain subject to the insurance contract and the insurer's review.

### Overseas travel and claims

The geographic scope covers mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. Students traveling to other countries must report to the insurer in advance and, as required by the notice, provide the student's name, destination, dates and study content. The Resource Management Office's logistics unit will assist with the procedures. No student travel-reporting information is published here.

Any insured incident must be reported to the insurer within **24 hours**.

For overseas-travel procedures, contact Huang Shaoxiong at the Resource Management Office logistics unit at `86502115`. The notice lists Huang Fengguang, `13902726995`, as the contact for reporting an insured incident.
