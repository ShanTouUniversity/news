STU's Resource Management Office has published the proposed winning bid for the equipment supply, system design and installation project for an air-source heat-pump hot-water system in student dormitories A–F. Based on the recommendation of bid-evaluation experts from the Shantou Construction Project Trading Center and STU's infrastructure tendering rules, Shantou Kerun Energy Equipment Installation Co., Ltd. is listed as the proposed winning bidder.

### Bid information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Equipment supply, system design and installation for an air-source heat-pump hot-water system in student dormitories A–F |
| Proposed winning bidder | Shantou Kerun Energy Equipment Installation Co., Ltd. |
| Bid amount | RMB 4,357,041.36 |
| Publicity period | April 26–28, 2013 |
| Tenderer | STU Infrastructure Office |
