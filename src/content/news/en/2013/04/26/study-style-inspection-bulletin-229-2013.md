STU's Three-Style Development Office has issued Study-Style Inspection Bulletin No. 229. During week 9 of the 2012–2013 spring semester, the inspection group conducted two checks at the E Teaching Building. The bulletin covers food brought into class, slippers worn in class and student lateness.

### Inspection results

| Inspection time | Location | Reported records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8:00 a.m., Monday, April 22 | E Teaching Building | 5 cases of bringing breakfast into class; 3 cases of lateness of five minutes or less; 4 cases of lateness of more than five minutes |
| 2:00 p.m., Wednesday, April 24 | E Teaching Building Room 207 | 1 case of wearing slippers in class; 7 cases of lateness of five minutes or less; 2 cases of lateness of more than five minutes |

Adding the categories listed in the bulletin gives 22 reported records across the two inspections: 16 lateness records, 5 records of breakfast being brought into class and 1 record of slippers being worn in class. This is a sum of records across different categories, not a deduplicated count of individual students.

The bulletin asks the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to students who did not follow university rules and to continue their study-style development work. The original notice includes a student list; this article does not reproduce names or other personal identifiers.
