The Communist Youth League Committee of STU, the STU Library and the STU Student Societies Federation will hold a Sangpushan Reading Festival literary lecture at **7:30 p.m. on April 27** in the Library Lecture Hall. The topic is “The Young Lu Xun,” and Wang Furen, a lifelong professor in STU's College of Liberal Arts, will be the speaker.

### Speaker

Wang Furen was born in 1941 in Gaotang, Shandong, and graduated from the Department of Foreign Languages at Shandong University. He began teaching in the Chinese Department of the Zhuhai campus of Beijing Normal University in 2002 and was appointed a lifelong professor in STU's College of Liberal Arts in 2003.

Wang is a member of the China Writers Association and serves on the councils of the Society for Modern Chinese Literature, the Lu Xun Research Society and the Wen Yiduo Research Society. His main research areas include Lu Xun studies, Chinese culture, modern and contemporary Chinese literature, and Chinese left-wing literature and culture. In recent years, he has promoted the modern Chinese “academic-culture” concept known as “New Sinology.”

### Festival numbering note

This lecture notice describes the event as a literary lecture of the **ninth STU Sangpushan Reading Festival**. Another STU notice issued in the same year for the overall reading-festival program calls the 2013 festival the **tenth**. The two notices use different numbering; this article retains the designation in the lecture notice and does not resolve the discrepancy.
