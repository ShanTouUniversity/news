To improve the accuracy of academic records and support teaching administration and student-aid assessments, STU's Academic Affairs Office and Student Affairs Office have asked **undergraduates in the 2010–2012 cohorts** to complete and verify their academic-record information.

### Submission process

From the date of the notice through **May 10, 2013**, students in the relevant cohorts must log in to the student affairs system at [sao.stu.edu.cn](http://sao.stu.edu.cn), open “My Affairs” and select “Complete Academic Record Information,” then follow the system instructions to complete, confirm and submit the information.

After online submission, students must print the STU Student Academic Record Card and sign it in black ink. Class student leaders will collect the signed cards by major and class and submit them to the academic administration office of the relevant school. The notice says that printing requires an additional component and recommends Internet Explorer or a compatible browser mode.

### Verification and changes

Students must also use “My Information” to verify key academic-record data. If any information is incorrect or incomplete, they should visit the Academic Affairs Office service window on the third floor of the Administration Center to ask the Academic Affairs Student Services Section about the procedures for changes.

University academic-record administration must follow admissions data and registration procedures. Without approval, the university may not independently change basic information established at admission, and information-change requests that are not approved leave the original admissions information unchanged.

The submission process involves personal academic-record data, including basic information, educational history, family members, social relationships and disciplinary or award records. No student data is reproduced here.
