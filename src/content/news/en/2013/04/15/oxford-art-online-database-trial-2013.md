Shantou University Library has opened a trial of **Oxford Art Online** through **June 30, 2013**. Faculty, staff and students are invited to use the database and provide feedback.

### Database contents

Oxford Art Online contains the complete content of the 34-volume print reference work *The Dictionary of Art*, as well as a large collection of articles and newly added bibliographic information available through the online database. It also includes:

- *The Oxford Companion to Western Art*;
- *Concise Oxford Dictionary of Art Terms*;
- *The Encyclopedia of Aesthetics*.

These resources support research into art history, art concepts, terminology and aesthetics.
