To standardize assessment and comments for undergraduate theses and graduation designs, the Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has developed **Version 1.0 of the Undergraduate Thesis and Graduation Design Assessment Form**. The form was reviewed and approved by the Student Assessment Working Subcommittee of the University Teaching Committee and will be piloted from **spring semester 2013**.

Three versions are provided for supervisors, reviewers and defense committees, allowing thesis and design outcomes and quality to be assessed more objectively and comprehensively.

| Form | Users |
| --- | --- |
| Supervisor assessment form | Thesis and graduation-design supervisors |
| Reviewer assessment form | Thesis and graduation-design reviewers |
| Defense committee assessment form | Undergraduate thesis and graduation-design defense committees |

Because disciplines and majors differ, teaching units may adjust the assessment items and standards according to their professional training standards. Any revised form must be reviewed and approved by the relevant school teaching committee and filed with Academic Affairs by **May 10**.
