To improve campus sanitation and control plant pests, the Shantou University Resource Management Office will conduct plant pest-control and “Four Pests” spraying from **April 19 to 25** in relevant areas of the campus and the School of Continuing Education. The work will cover green spaces, plants, fruit trees and drainage areas. Faculty, staff and students should take care when passing through the affected areas.

### Daily schedule

| Date | Work areas |
| --- | --- |
| April 19 | Artificial Lake area and sports field |
| April 20 | Around the dining hall, Administration Building and Administrative Center |
| April 21 | From the affiliated middle and primary schools to the Mental Health Center |
| April 22 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, P Building, student-residence areas, reservoir dam, Building 789 and A–B faculty and staff residences |
| April 23 | Medical Building, New Teaching Building, and G, H, I and J student residences |
| April 24 | Around the Campus Security Team office, campus entrance, campus roads and outside the New Library |
| April 25 | School of Continuing Education |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
