Shantou University&apos;s Business School will hold **Economic Salon 117** from **9:30 a.m. to noon on Monday, April 22** in the New Library Lecture Hall. The topic is **“Some Interesting Questions in Financial Research.”** The speaker will be Sun Qian, head of the Department of Finance at Fudan University&apos;s School of Management and a distinguished professor.

### Speaker

Professor Sun Qian earned a bachelor&apos;s degree in Economics from Peking University, an MBA from William Paterson College and a PhD in Economics from Arizona State University. Sun previously taught at Nanyang Technological University in Singapore and Xiamen University and is currently head of Fudan University School of Management&apos;s Department of Finance and a distinguished professor.

Sun&apos;s research focuses mainly on emerging markets. Sun has published in international academic journals including *Journal of Financial Economics*, *Journal of Banking & Finance*, *Financial Management*, *Journal of International Money and Finance* and *Journal of Empirical Finance*. Sun is also a board member of the Asian Finance Association and a member of the Asian Shadow Financial Regulatory Committee. Sun has taught corporate finance, international finance and investment management and has provided consulting services to several organizations.
