In accordance with documents from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and Shantou Municipal Education Bureau, the 2013 teacher-certification process for non-education graduates entering rural teaching has opened. Eligible Shantou University graduates must complete online application, in-person confirmation and university registration.

### Eligible applicants and requirements

Eligible applicants include full-time graduates of ordinary universities who majored in a non-education discipline and have signed an intention letter to teach in rural areas. The process also accepts graduates with an associate degree or above who are under deferred employment or who have completed service under the “Three Supports and One Assistance” program with a passing assessment.

Applicants must also:

- achieve at least Level 2-B in the national Putonghua proficiency standard;
- pass the physical examination under the 2011 revised standards for teacher-certification applicants;
- complete make-up courses in education and psychology at the relevant level and obtain the related training certificate; and
- submit the required intention letter signed with a county or district education bureau. Applications without the letter will not be certified.

| STU registration deadline | By April 28 at the STU Academic Affairs Office |

After certification is completed, the university will collectively submit verified copies of graduates&apos; diplomas and collect the teacher-certification certificates from the certifying authority for return to the applicants. The relevant forms can be downloaded from the [Guangdong Teacher Certification Website](http://www.gdhed.edu.cn).

### Fees

Under the relevant provincial pricing rules, the teacher-certification examination fee is **RMB 240 per person**, collected by the certifying authority. Physical-examination and Putonghua-test fees are not included and must be paid separately as required.

Application materials include diploma copies, the rural-teaching intention letter, an ideological and ethical assessment form and a physical-examination form. The forms, identity documents and health information are not reproduced here.
