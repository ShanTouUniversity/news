Shantou University&apos;s fourth public-welfare course teacher training program will open on **April 17**. The program consists of three afternoon sessions and two evening sessions, totaling 14 instructional hours. Topics include public-welfare course delivery, project design, social research, student development and emergency response.

### Training schedule

| Date | Sessions and topics | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April 17 | Opening remarks; STU&apos;s public-welfare course vision and implementation; the mentor&apos;s role in public-welfare projects; public-welfare project design and planning, including theory and experience sharing | ACC 3# and E410 |
| April 19 | Operation and communication of university student public-welfare projects; student participation in public welfare and capacity development; social research methods and report writing, including theory and experience sharing | ACC 3# and E410 |
| April 24 | Developing and guiding student leadership and teamwork, including theory and experience sharing; methods and skills for handling emergencies, including theory and experience sharing | Not separately specified in the schedule |

Sessions will be taught or led by Song Yaozhen, Cai Yinghui, Yao Zhen, Feng Yuan, Chen Yichun, Feng Shifeng, Xie Xinni, Xu Lanlan, Wu Yingfan, Yu Hong and Wang Run. Examples include work by the Anti-Domestic Violence Network, the “China at the Grassroots” student social-research project of the Liao Bingxiong Foundation and Guangzhou Sunshine Social Work Service Center.

### Attendance and teaching qualification

Attendance will be recorded. Teachers whose teaching duties conflict with the program are encouraged to adjust their classes. Trainees who cannot attend because of an unavoidable conflict must request leave from the Academic Affairs Office in advance. Missing more than one-third of the program, or 5 instructional hours, constitutes failure.

Completing the training is a prerequisite for the Public-Welfare Course Teaching Qualification Certificate. Trainees must also undertake one public-welfare service project and receive a passing evaluation before they can obtain the certificate.

For questions about attendance and the training program, contact Liu Xiangling at the Academic Affairs Office: `86502416`, `lxl0726@stu.edu.cn`.
