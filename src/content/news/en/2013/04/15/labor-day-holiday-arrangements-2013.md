The Shantou University General Office has announced the adjusted 2013 Labor Day schedule: **the university will close from April 29 to May 1 for three days, while Saturday, April 27, and Sunday, April 28, will be working days**. Academic Affairs and the Graduate School will issue separate teaching arrangements.

Before the holiday, each unit must inspect safety facilities in its offices, close doors and windows, shut off water and electricity, and complete other precautions. Campus security will maintain campus safety and order during the holiday.

### Duty arrangements

The General Office, Resource Management Office, Student Affairs Office, schools, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and the D, E and G teaching buildings must arrange duty coverage. Duty information must be emailed to the General Office by **April 24**.
