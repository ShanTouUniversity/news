To celebrate World Book Day, China National Knowledge Infrastructure (CNKI) is offering free access to six tool-oriented resources. Shantou University Library has shared the offer with faculty, staff and students. The trial runs from **April 12 to May 12, 2013**.

### Six trial resources

| Resource | Main content |
| --- | --- |
| Statistical data | CNKI China Economic and Social Development Statistical Database, with economic and social-development statistics |
| Yearbooks | Online yearbook resources with regularly updated yearbook materials and search functions |
| Reference books | Multiple reference-book resources for common knowledge, specialist concepts and research materials |
| *Hanyu Da Cidian* and *Kangxi Dictionary* | Searchable Chinese-language dictionary and character-reference content |
| Academic images | A specialist academic image database for browsing and searching scholarly images |
| Law database | Legal resources including laws and regulations, representative cases and law papers for legal research and study |

Details are available on the CNKI activity page: [q.help.cnki.net/active.aspx](http://q.help.cnki.net/active.aspx).
