Shantou University&apos;s eighth Mental Health Awareness Month will feature a **“Letters to the Future”** activity. Faculty, staff and students may write a letter to themselves or another person one year in the future. The organizers will collect and seal the letters and distribute them to the writers after one year, giving participants a way to record their current feelings and hopes for the future.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | April 20, 2013, 3:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Locations | Golden Triangle on the Pedestrian Street and G Building |
| Participants | University faculty, staff and students |
| Supplies | Paper, envelopes, glue, colored pens and other materials |
| Delivery | Letters will be collected, sealed and distributed after one year |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Activity letters are participants&apos; private correspondence; their contents are not reproduced here.
