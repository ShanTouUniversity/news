The writing and selection contest for Shantou University&apos;s campus anthology *That Year, the Golden Phoenix Blossomed* has concluded. Organized by the University Party Committee Publicity Office and Alumni Affairs Office, the contest received **102 submissions**. A panel of professional teachers and student representatives conducted an anonymous review and selected **23 winning works**.

### Winning works

| Award | Works |
| --- | --- |
| First prize (2) | *递烟*; *雨夜随笔* |
| Second prize (4) | *旅程*; *那一年，金凤花开*; *在赛扶卖地瓜的这一年*; *四季絮语* |
| Third prize (6) | *不倦的岁月*; *花开自悦然*; *亲爱的2012，再见*; *讲七的鸟*; *汕大的围墙*; *像海不曾怀疑天的蓝* |
| Merit prize (11) | *每一朵花都有她的故事*; *此心安处是吾校*; *2012的那个夏天*; *花开花落*; *那年夏天，我们一起支教的日子*; *一步一脚印*; *大学生活点滴*; *凤于九天*; *在路上*; *刘建高作品选*; *年少无知* |

Commissioned contributions were excluded from the contest. The winning-author list contains personal information about students and alumni, so names, years and majors are not published here.
