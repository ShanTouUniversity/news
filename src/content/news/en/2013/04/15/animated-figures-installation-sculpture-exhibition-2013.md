An exhibition of animated-figure installation sculptures will be held at the Art Museum of Shantou University&apos;s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design from **April 17 to 23**. It will be open to the university community, with the opening ceremony scheduled for **10:00 a.m. on Wednesday, April 17**.

The exhibition is created by Liu GD, a graduate student at the school. Using plaster and xuan paper, the works transform familiar animated figures into sculptures and installations. Some plaster figures are placed on white bases of different sizes, while xuan-paper figures hang from a production line and move continuously. The exhibition also presents the artist&apos;s working process, techniques and sketches, inviting visitors to reconsider familiar animated forms and everyday memories from a different perspective.
