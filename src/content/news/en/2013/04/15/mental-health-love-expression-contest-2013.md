Shantou University&apos;s eighth Mental Health Awareness Month will hold a **“Record Us and Speak Your Love”** contest, inviting the university community to submit original works expressing gratitude. Participants may enter individually or as teams.

### Work requirements

Entries may combine photographs, Weibo or QQ screenshots, audio or video from the participant&apos;s study and daily life with another person. They must be submitted in one of the following formats:

- a PowerPoint presentation of no more than 15 slides;
- an electronic photo album; or
- an audio or video work no longer than five minutes.

Works should be truthful and concise, express sincere feelings vividly, and convey a positive, optimistic spirit.

### Schedule and awards

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | University faculty, staff and students |
| Submission period | April 15–22, 2013 |
| Deadline | Midnight on April 22 |
| Display and judging | April 26, 2013, 7:30–9:00 p.m. |
| First prize | One winner, RMB 300 and certificate |
| Second prize | One winner, RMB 200 and certificate |
| Third prize | Two winners, RMB 100 and certificate each |

The original notice asks participants to submit works through a designated organizer mailbox; that mailbox and the student consultation contacts are not published here because they contain personal information. Entries may include photographs, social-media content and audio or video involving individuals, so their content is not reproduced here.
