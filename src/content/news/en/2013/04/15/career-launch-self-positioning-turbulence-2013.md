The 32nd **Career Launch Forum** at Shantou University will be held from **7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on April 17** in E Staircase 201. Titled **“Finding Yourself amid Turbulence,”** the session is open to the university community.

The speaker, Chen Zilong, is a project director for Hutchison Whampoa&apos;s Delta Ports project. Chen previously worked as a teaching and research assistant at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and in analysis and management roles in the air-transport and shipping industries. Chen holds a bachelor&apos;s degree in Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management and a master&apos;s degree in Industrial Engineering and Logistics Management from HKUST, as well as a master&apos;s degree in Accountancy from the Chinese University of Hong Kong.

Drawing on cross-sector study and professional experience, the session will examine self-understanding, career choices and professional direction in a changing environment, helping students consider how to find direction and continue building capabilities amid career uncertainty.
