Shantou University&apos;s Business School will select **four undergraduates** for an exchange at **ISM University of Management and Economics in Lithuania** beginning in September 2013. The universities signed a cooperation agreement in December 2012, and the program will launch in 2013.

### Eligibility

The program is open to second-year and above Business School undergraduates. Priority will be given to students who have not previously joined a university or school exchange program. Applicants should have strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, strong academic and personal records, a GPA of at least 3.0, an active record of public and volunteer service, and a willingness to represent Shantou University externally.

Applicants must submit proof of English proficiency by **April 30**, such as TOEFL, IELTS, CET-4, CET-6 or the university ECL result.

### Application and selection

Application materials include the Business School overseas-exchange application form, proof of English proficiency, an academic transcript and an English essay of approximately 300–500 words explaining the applicant&apos;s purpose for joining the program and overseas study plan. A school selection group will conduct a preliminary review and comprehensive assessment of the written materials before identifying the four final candidates.

If the application is successful, the student may be exempted from ISM tuition. Students remain responsible for fees, round-trip travel, accommodation and meals, books and materials, insurance, medical expenses and other costs during the exchange.

The application form requests student names, student numbers, identity and passport details, contact information, English results and a personal photograph. The form, transcript and personal identity information are not reproduced here.
