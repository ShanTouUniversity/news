Shantou University&apos;s 2013 Test for English Majors Band 4 (TEM4) will begin at **8:30 a.m. on Saturday, April 20** and last approximately **135 minutes**. The Academic Affairs Office reminds candidates in the School of Liberal Arts to prepare the required identification and stationery and follow the examination rules.

### Sections and answer cards

| Section | Content | Time | Answer location |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| I | Dictation, Tape A | 15 minutes | Answer Card 1 |
| II | Listening Comprehension, Tape B | 20 minutes | Answer Card 2 |
| III | Cloze | 15 minutes | Answer Card 2 |
| IV | Grammar and Vocabulary | 15 minutes | Answer Card 2 |
| V | Reading Comprehension | 25 minutes | Answer Card 2 |
| VI | Writing | 45 minutes | Answer Card 3 |

The writing section consists of Section A (35 minutes) and Section B (10 minutes). The dictation is played four times, followed by two minutes for checking. Answers for the dictation, listening, cloze, grammar and vocabulary, and reading sections must be entered on the designated answer cards; answers written in the test booklet do not receive credit. Candidates do not need to write the dictation title on Answer Card 1.

### Examination rules

- Dictation and writing must be completed with a black-ink pen of at least 0.5 mm; multiple-choice answers must be marked with a 2B pencil, with only one answer selected per question.
- Answers must remain within the designated areas. Candidates may not write the university or their names in blank areas of the answer sheets; violations result in a score of zero. Correction fluid is not allowed.
- Candidates arriving more than 15 minutes after the official start may not enter. No candidate may leave the test site before the examination ends.
- Candidates may submit after 60 minutes have elapsed, but those who submit early will be supervised in a designated rest room and may not use mobile phones or other communication devices there. Submission is not permitted during the final 30 minutes.
- A candidate who fails to submit the paper on time will have the examination result canceled.
- Mobile phones and other communication devices may not be brought into the examination room. Dictionaries, including electronic dictionaries, and other reference books may not be used.

Candidates must enter with their **admission card, identity card and student card**. Candidates missing any required document will not be admitted.
