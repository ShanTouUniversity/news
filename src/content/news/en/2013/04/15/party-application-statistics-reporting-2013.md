To complete first-half 2013 Party statistics and database entry, the STU Organization and United Front Work Office has asked each Party general branch and directly affiliated branch to consolidate and submit information on people who applied to join the Party.

Each unit must designate a staff member to verify the data and ensure that database entries are accurate, complete and truthful. The report covers the initial database of Party applicants and statistics on people identified in the first half of 2013 as activists or development candidates.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting units | Party general branches and directly affiliated branches |
| Deadline | By April 26, 2013 |
| Printed materials | Submit to the Organization and United Front Work Office |
| Printed submission location | Room 315, Administrative Center |
| Electronic materials | `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` |

The forms contain personal information and lists of Party applicants, activists and development candidates. Forms, rosters and personal data are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office)
