Shantou University&apos;s School of Engineering will host an alumni face-to-face session titled **“Entrepreneurship: Pursuing Dreams.”** Four engineering entrepreneurs will discuss their paths from project practice to company formation and exchange ideas about mobile internet, mobile applications, interactive devices and e-commerce.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | April 17, 2013, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Conference Room 205, Science and Technology Report Hall |
| Theme | Entrepreneurship: Pursuing Dreams |
| Speakers | Yuan YY, Lin SQ, Li JJ and Pan HC |
| Reservation | Reserve through [activity.stu.edu.cn](http://activity.stu.edu.cn/) |
| Reservation deadline | Before 11:00 a.m. on April 17 |
| Participants | University faculty, staff and students |

### Topics

- Yuan YY graduated from the School of Engineering&apos;s Computer Science and Technology program in 2012 and founded Shantou Chenggu Network Technology Co., Ltd. with partners that June. One of the companies in the STU Entrepreneurship Park, Chenggu explores mobile internet and intelligent life-service platforms.
- Lin SQ graduated from the School of Engineering&apos;s Computer Science and Technology program in 2012. After winning a gold award and first place in the first and second national “MM Million Youth Entrepreneurship Plan” competitions, respectively, Lin formed a team and founded Shantou One-Minute Network Technology Co., Ltd. to provide internet services.
- Li JJ is a 2009-level Electronic Information Engineering student and head of the Engineering School MT Team. Li was preparing to establish Shantou Zhichi Technology Co., Ltd., focusing on mobile-application development, mobile interactive devices and mobile e-commerce marketing.
- Pan HC is a 2010-level Computer Science and Technology student with experience in multiple projects. Pan was exploring entrepreneurship in mobile terminals, e-commerce and mobile internet.
