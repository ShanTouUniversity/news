Shantou University has completed project applications, expert review and recommendation, and online publicity for its 2013 Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Plan. The university has formally approved **53 projects** for the main campus and recommended them for review under the provincial undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training plan.

The list includes 37 innovation-training projects across the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Business School, Law School, School of Engineering and School of Science, plus 16 entrepreneurship-training and entrepreneurship-practice projects from schools across the main campus.
