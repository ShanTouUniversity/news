Following organizational and personnel changes, Shantou University&apos;s Law School has adjusted its Teaching Committee. The adjustment was discussed and approved at the **Law School dean&apos;s office meeting on March 27, 2013**. Peng Wenhao (Michael Palmer) has been appointed chair of the Teaching Committee.

| Position | Appointee |
| --- | --- |
| Chair, Law School Teaching Committee | Peng Wenhao (Michael Palmer) |
