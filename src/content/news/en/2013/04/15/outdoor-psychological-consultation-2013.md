The Psychological Health Education and Consultation Center of Shantou University&apos;s Student Affairs Office will offer an outdoor psychological consultation activity during the eighth Mental Health Awareness Month. Open to the university community, the activity will promote mental-health knowledge and provide an informal space for consultation and interaction.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | “I&apos;ll Help Untie Your Heart&apos;s Knots” |
| Time | April 20, 2013, 3:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Golden Triangle on the Pedestrian Street |
| Participants | University faculty, staff and students |
| Activities | On-site consultation, music relaxation, wish wall, games, quiz prizes and “Su Shu Chuan Qing” |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
