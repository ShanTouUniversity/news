Shantou University&apos;s School of Engineering will hold its **10th Science and Technology Festival** from **April to May**, under the theme “Technology, Practice and Innovation.” Connected to the school&apos;s EIP-CDIO engineering-education reform, the festival is open to all current students and uses competitions and training to develop project development, design, implementation, communication, leadership and teamwork skills.

### Program

| Activity | Time | Venue | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Festival opening ceremony | April 13 | E Staircase 204 | Festival history and achievements, this year&apos;s program and interaction with senior students |
| Fourth Student Technology Innovation Training Camp | April 13–14 | E Staircase 204 | Introductory training for first- and second-year students interested in extracurricular technology projects |
| ACM Programming Competition | April 20–21 | West Humanities Building 302 | Programming contest following ACM standards |
| Electronic Design and Production Competition | April 20–21 | Engineering School CDIO Innovation Laboratory | Electronic-design and production contests in Groups A and B |
| Civil Structure Design Competition | May | To be announced | “Bridge with Newspaper” contest |
| Festival closing ceremony | May | To be announced | Training-camp certificates, competition results, awards and project showcase |

### ACM Programming Competition

The competition will accept **18 teams** of three. Each team will use one standardized computer in a Windows XP environment and solve 10 practical problems using C++, C or Pascal. Top teams may have an opportunity to join the university ACM team.

Registration is first come, first served and closes at **noon on April 19**. Teams must complete the registration form and email it to `12jhhe3@stu.edu.cn`; team names may not exceed eight Chinese characters or 16 characters.

Books, manuals and program listings may be brought as references, but contestants may not bring software or data that can be processed by a computer, private calculators, electronic dictionaries, laptops, floppy disks or portable hard drives. Teams are ranked by the number of problems solved; ties are decided by total time plus penalties, with 20 minutes added for each incorrect submission.

### Electronic Design and Production Competition

The two groups use first-come, first-served registration and close registration at **noon on April 19**. Registration materials should be emailed to `12zrweng@stu.edu.cn`.

- **Group A** is limited to first-year students. Teams have 2–4 members, and 14 teams will be accepted. Using components, tools and instruments supplied by the organizers, each team must assemble a superheterodyne radio within the allotted time. Scores cover completion time, completeness, production quality and an on-site question, worth 25, 40, 20 and 15 points respectively. Choosing a printed circuit board results in a 20-point deduction from the final score.
- **Group B** is open to students in all years. Teams have 2–4 members. The organizers will set the task and provide selected materials, and teams must complete their work within the allotted time.

Teams may not bring their own components, tools or instruments, and may not begin production before the official start. A team arriving more than 20 minutes late will be disqualified. Every team must be scored by the judges at the end, whether or not it has completed its work; otherwise it will be treated as having withdrawn.

### Organizers

The festival is organized by the Engineering School Youth League Committee, operated by the Engineering School Student Technology Development Center, and supported by the school&apos;s department-level Youth League branches, ACM Training Team and m@x Electronic Practice Center team. Prizes and certificates will be offered in each competition.

Registration forms contain student names, majors, years and contact information. The forms and participant lists are not reproduced here.
