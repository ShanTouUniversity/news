The first Guangdong Model United Nations Conference will be held on **May 10–11** at the University City campus of Guangdong University of Foreign Studies. The conference will simulate the UN Human Rights Council, with **“Racial Discrimination”** as its theme and English as the working language. Shantou University plans to send **one or two teams**, each consisting of two conference delegates and two MPC representatives.

The event will provide training and an exchange opportunity for Guangdong universities preparing for the 10th China Model United Nations Conference, scheduled for **November 8–10, 2013**. That conference will also be held at Guangdong University of Foreign Studies and is expected to involve universities from China and overseas.

### Eligibility and application

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | STU graduate students |
| Application period | April 15–18, 2013 |
| Deadline | Before 5:00 p.m. on April 18 |
| Application email | `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn` |
| Conference venue | University City campus, Guangdong University of Foreign Studies |
| Conference dates | May 10–11, 2013 |
| Working language | English |

Applicants should have strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, present themselves appropriately, and have debate and negotiation abilities. They must also be able to set aside sufficient time to prepare. The application asks applicants to write in English about human-rights issues and Model United Nations and to confirm their availability for preparation.

### STU selection

STU will use an English-language unstructured group discussion for selection. The interview time and venue will be announced separately. The selection will include:

1. Self-introductions and a three-minute English speech on the conference theme;
2. An eight-minute unstructured group discussion after candidates draw a topic and prepare for two minutes;
3. Eight minutes of questions from the judges.

Applicants must email the form to `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn` before **5:00 p.m. on April 18**.
