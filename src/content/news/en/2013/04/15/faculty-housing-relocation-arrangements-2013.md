Under the campus construction plan, the first phase of Shantou University&apos;s new student colleges will include three student-residence buildings, two in the West Campus and one near the post office. The construction land covers several faculty and staff residences and campus buildings, and the university is coordinating relocation, replacement housing and repairs.

### Affected residences and relocation principles

The affected buildings include the 60-suite, 72-suite, 24-suite and 11-suite residences, C8, C9 and D-type faculty and staff residences, as well as the old Publishing House building, the Resource Management Office buildings and the Learning Mutual-Aid Center.

| Current residence | Relocation arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| 24-suite, 11-suite, C8, C9 and D-type faculty and staff residences | Generally relocated to C-type faculty and staff residences in Longquan Garden |
| 60-suite and 72-suite residences | Residents may choose from three categories of replacement housing |
| Family households currently in the 60-suite and 72-suite single residences | May apply for C-type residences in Longquan Garden |

Residents of the 60-suite and 72-suite residences may choose among collective dormitories converted from commercial housing in Longquan Garden, collective dormitories converted from Anju fund-raising housing, and single rooms or similar one-bedroom units converted from courtyard residences such as Yulan Garden and Huichun Garden in the West Campus. The university has provided housing-location, floor-plan and layout information for reference.

### Applications and consultation

To incorporate residents&apos; needs and begin replacement-housing repairs, affected households must complete the replacement-housing application and send it to the public mailbox `AZF@stu.edu.cn` by **April 20**. Written comments and suggestions may also be sent to this mailbox.

The university plans to hold its third relocation-household consultation meeting at **3:00 p.m. on Tuesday, April 16**, on the third floor of the Trade Union building, to hear residents&apos; comments and suggestions.

The replacement-housing application and floor-plan materials contain personal housing information. The forms, resident lists and individual housing details are not reproduced here.
