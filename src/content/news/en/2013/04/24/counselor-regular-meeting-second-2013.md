STU's Student Affairs Office announced that the university's second counselor meeting of 2013 will be held at **10 a.m. on Saturday, April 27** in Room 330 of the Administrative Center. All counselors and relevant personnel in the student-affairs system are asked to make the necessary work arrangements and attend on time.

The notice does not provide an agenda or a list of attendees.
