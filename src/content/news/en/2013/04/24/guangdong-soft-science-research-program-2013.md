STU's Research Office announced that the university is organizing applications for the 2013 Guangdong Soft Science Research Program. Under the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology and Department of Finance notice for the fourth batch of 2013 provincial science and technology programs, STU units may apply under Topics 3 and 4. The university may ultimately recommend **one project**.

### Basic requirements

A principal investigator with an ongoing Guangdong Soft Science Research Program project or an overdue project whose completion has not been accepted may not apply for a new project. A 2011 project is treated as overdue if its completion-acceptance application has not been submitted. Projects should in principle be carried out in Guangdong and must be completed by **December 31, 2014**; early completion and an early acceptance application are encouraged.

Research should connect theory with practice, investigate and analyze current problems, and use relevant theory, data or cases to develop practical recommendations suited to Guangdong and useful for government decision-making. Applicants must ensure that their materials are accurate and free of intellectual-property disputes, and should use the latest available data. Publications or award applications based on a funded project must acknowledge support from the Guangdong Soft Science Research Program and include the project number. Budgets should be reasonable; if the actual award is lower than the requested budget, the difference should in principle be covered by the applicant or matched by the managing department.

### Eligible topics

#### Topic 3: General open-topic projects (0703c)

This topic allows applicants to propose their own project titles on issues affecting overall scientific and technological progress, or on technology-related issues in national economic and social development. The applicant chooses the project title, but it may not duplicate any major or key research topic listed in the attachment.

Funding for an individual project is generally capped at **RMB 50,000**. At completion, projects must provide a decision-consulting report of approximately **3,000 Chinese characters** for use by relevant departments and a general research report of at least **30,000 Chinese characters**. Applicants should select “General open-topic project” as the project type and choose one of the following 10 research directions:

1. Science and technology development strategy and major issues in economic and social development
2. Public science and technology policies and regulations
3. Major issues in regional innovation systems
4. Technology-enterprise innovation and industrial transformation and upgrading
5. Innovation in science and technology finance
6. Major issues in using scientific and technological innovation to improve social well-being
7. Public science and technology administration and related services
8. Innovation and entrepreneurship talent and science popularization
9. Science, technology and intellectual-property creation
10. Innovation in soft-science research and scientific decision-making

#### Topic 4: Young PhD start-up projects under the general open-topic category (0704c)

Topic 4 is limited to applicants who received their doctorates within the previous three years and who meet all Topic 3 requirements. The principal investigator must be familiar with the relevant field; as of **January 1, 2013**, the doctorate must have been awarded no more than three years earlier and the applicant must be no older than **35**. The doctoral degree certificate must be uploaded as an attachment to the application. Applicants with ongoing national- or provincial-level projects should not apply, and each applicant may undertake this type of project only once.

Topic 4 has the same requirements for an approximately 3,000-character decision-consulting report and a general research report of at least 30,000 characters. Funding is generally capped at **RMB 50,000** per project. Applicants should select “Young PhD start-up project under the general open-topic category” as the project type and choose from the 10 directions listed under Topic 3. Titles are proposed by applicants and may not duplicate major or key topics.

### Topics excluded from duplication

The attachment states that open-topic projects may not duplicate the following major or key research topics.

**Topic 1: Major research topics**

1. Theory and practice of using an innovation-driven strategy to promote an innovative economy in Guangdong
2. Development of enterprise and industry research and development centers in Guangdong
3. Policy system and institutional design for science and technology finance in Guangdong
4. Innovation strategy and transformation and upgrading of Guangdong's marine economy
5. Development and promotion of a standardized archive-management system for high-technology enterprises and private technology enterprises in Guangdong

**Topic 2: Key research topics**

1. Development of a supervision system for science and technology funds in Guangdong
2. Plan for establishing a Guangdong Institute of Science and Technology Services
3. Financial systems for high-technology enterprises listed on the NEEQ
4. Reform of state-asset disposal in the commercialization and industrialization of job-related innovation achievements
5. Theory and practice of building pre-incubators at universities in Guangdong
6. Models and policy recommendations for government procurement of science and technology services in Guangdong
7. Dynamic monitoring of Guangdong provincial science and technology program projects
8. Industrial competitiveness of the Pearl River Delta city cluster
9. Standards system for the science and technology services industry
10. Standardized financial administration of research and development activities at technology enterprises

### Internal submission

The university has a quota of one recommended project. Interested faculty members must complete the *Guangdong Soft Science Research Program Joint Project Application Form* and send the electronic document to `yxhu@stu.edu.cn` by **April 29**. The university will select a project for recommendation. The notice lists Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng as contacts and gives the telephone number `86502813`.

The application form contains fields for the principal investigator's and researchers' identities, positions, education, contact details, assignments, budget, bank-account information and expert recommendations. Individual application materials and account details are not reproduced here.
