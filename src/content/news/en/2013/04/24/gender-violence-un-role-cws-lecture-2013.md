STU's Center for Women's Studies in the College of Liberal Arts will hold the second event in its 2013 CWS Public Lecture Series from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, April 26** in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center. Titled “Gender-Based Violence: He or She, You and Me — Triple Dimensions of the Individual, Political and International on the Role of the United Nations,” the event will feature Professor Feng Yuan as the keynote speaker and Dr. Xu Lanlan as the dialogue guest.

### Discussion

The lecture will use the 57th session of the United Nations Commission on the Status of Women as a starting point. Held in New York from **March 4 to 15, 2013**, the session brought delegations from more than 130 countries together, with more than 6,000 representatives of non-governmental women's organizations registered to attend.

The discussion will consider the factors behind this major international conference on violence against women and girls, the consensus and tensions inside and outside the meeting, and the achievements and concerns reflected in its agreed conclusions. It will also examine how these international discussions relate to national policies and laws, as well as to the everyday lives of people of different genders and ages.

### Speakers

Feng Yuan is a professor at the Center for Women's Studies at STU's College of Liberal Arts, a council member of the China Women's Research Society and a board member of the Anti-Domestic Violence Network / Beijing Fanbao. Her long-term work covers action, training and research on women, gender and development, including gender and development, gender, women, culture and media, and the prevention and intervention of gender-based violence.

Xu Lanlan is a lecturer in the Department of Public Administration and Service at STU Law School. Her research interests include gender studies, microcredit and rural finance, public policy and rural development. She teaches Rural Gender Studies and Gender and Public Policy, among other courses.
