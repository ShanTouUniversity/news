Changjiang School of Arts and Design completed the major-assignment process for its 2012 cohort and announced the final results on April 24, 2013. The school's Teaching Committee had previously reviewed and approved the assignment plan. After the results were posted on the school bulletin board without objections, the final assignments were announced to all students.

The assignments formed part of the transition into specialized study following the school's broad-based admissions reform. Under the educational approach of broad foundations and solid fundamentals, students in the 2012 cohort entered the specialized training stage after completing first-year foundational courses. The school organized preference submission, selection and reassignment around students' stated interests and a process intended to be open and fair.

### Assignment principles and selection

Each student was required to complete the school's unified major-preference form and rank all available choices. The school first assigned students according to their first choices. When applications for a direction exceeded its planned capacity, the relevant interview or comprehensive-assessment criteria were applied; students not admitted to their first choice then proceeded through their subsequent choices. Students who failed to submit the form on time, submitted no preferences or were not admitted to any of their listed choices were regarded as accepting reassignment by the school.

Industrial Design applicants had to submit a personal portfolio, which could include hand-drawn sketches, photography, graphic works or photographs of three-dimensional models. When applications exceeded capacity, interviews assessed overall competence together with the portfolio. For Graphic Design, an interview and several personal works were required even when second-choice applications did not reach the stated limit. For other directions, interviews focused on overall competence when applications exceeded capacity; in some directions, introductory-course, foundational-course and English results were considered as references.

### Dates recorded in the plan attachment

The plan attachment listed dates for the mobilization meeting, submission of preference forms, interviews and adjustment, and internal publicity as March 13, March 19, March 22–31 and April 4–6, 2011, respectively. Because these dates precede the 2012 cohort and the April 24, 2013 announcement, this article preserves the dates as recorded in the attachment and does not speculate about or correct them.

The final assignment spreadsheet contains student identification numbers and individual major assignments. The individual list is not reproduced here; the original OA attachments remain the authoritative source.
