STU Union plans to hold a faculty and staff basketball tournament in **early May 2013**. Seven combined teams are being formed by school and administrative unit:

- College of Liberal Arts and Business School combined team;
- School of Science team;
- School of Engineering team;
- Law School, Research Office, News Center and Library combined team;
- Art Education Center, Social Sciences Department, Continuing Education College and Affiliated Schools combined team;
- Administrative Offices team; and
- Resource Management team.

Faculty and staff members in non-sports specialties from the Department of Physical Education may join the Art Education Center and other units' combined team.

### Registration

Each team must send its registration form to the university union email address, `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`, by **April 28**. The notice does not give a specific game date, schedule or group draw; subsequent arrangements will be subject to information issued by the union.

Shengxiang Flooring's Shantou Guotai store is sponsoring some tournament commemorative items and equipment. The store will also visit the campus on **April 26** for a consultation and group-purchase event.

The XLS file supplied with the notice is a registration form, but its format could not be read reliably. Faculty and staff registration details are not reproduced here.
