STU Business School announced that the 2013 “Shangshan Ruoshui” charity marketing competition will take place on **May 11** at the campus Golden Triangle and pedestrian street. The event theme is “Wings of Charity,” with the slogan “Business Wings Carry Charity.” Open to students, faculty and staff, the event will use charity sales and on-site donations to provide living support for disadvantaged students in Wuqiao, Shantou, while giving participants practical experience in marketing and hands-on work.

### Participation and donations

Individuals and teams may enter, and organizations or classes may also register. The attachment rules specify one to six people per team. Participants must source their own goods and bear their own profits and losses while operating sales stalls on the event day. Each stall must donate **at least 50% of its profits** as a charitable contribution, and participants may voluntarily increase the donation ratio. The event will also provide a central donation box, donation boxes at game stalls and mobile fundraising activities for voluntary contributions from campus visitors.

The competition also provides a consignment-sales option. Individuals with unused items may entrust them to staff without charge during the publicity-stall activity for sale on their behalf. All turnover from consignment stalls on the competition day will be donated to disadvantaged students in Wuqiao. Whether unsold items will also be donated is to be negotiated between staff and the owners.

### Schedule and rules

Registration runs from **April 24 through midnight on May 3**. Individuals and teams may download the electronic application form through the campus office-information network or the official “Shantou University Shangshan Ruoshui” Weibo account and submit it as instructed. The registration address listed in the notice belongs to a student contact and is not reproduced here. The organizing committee will set up a consultation and on-site registration stall at the Golden Triangle on April 26.

The attachment rules state that participants will receive training on May 5, and applicants who pass review must attend and follow the training requirements. During the publicity-stall activity on May 8, buyers may enter a drawing for discount coupons. The event provides 25 coupons worth RMB 2 each; they may be used at face value for purchases on the competition day.

Goods sold by participants may not be flammable or explosive, toxic or harmful, unhealthy, pornographic or violent. Food vendors are responsible for food safety and must look after their goods during the event. The rules also require participants to sign an agreement with the organizers and state that participants bear responsibility for food-safety problems and lost goods.

### Games and awards

Five games will be available on the competition day. Participants may join by using a game ticket obtained through a donation at the main base or by donating at a game stall. The games use a points system, with up to two points available from each game. Participants who accumulate 10 points may exchange them for gifts or discount coupons.

Awards will be based on four measures: donating at least 50% of profits, worth 50% of the total score; voluntarily increasing the donation ratio for remaining profits, worth 20%; total charitable donations, worth 20%; and on-site popularity, worth 10%. Popularity will be measured by the number of customer stamps on the “Stalls that left an impression” feedback board.

The top three individuals or teams will receive the titles “Yingshan Champion,” “Yingshan Runner-up” and “Yingshan Third Place,” together with gifts and certificates. Places four through ten will receive certificates corresponding to the titles “Yingshan Jinshi,” “Yingshan Huiyuan,” “Yingshan Gongshi,” “Yingshan Jieyuan,” “Yingshan Juren,” “Yingshan Xiucai” and “Yingshan Scholar,” respectively. The detailed application form and participant agreement contain personal and team contact fields, which are not reproduced here.
