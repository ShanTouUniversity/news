Shantou University Union shared information about a May Day group purchase at Shengxiang Flooring's Shantou Guotai store for STU faculty and staff. The on-site event is scheduled for **10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Saturday, May 4**. Interested faculty and staff may visit the first floor of the university union office for consultation and registration from **10 a.m. to noon and 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on April 26**.

The retailer will provide a shuttle to the event. Pickup on May 4 is scheduled for 9:15 a.m. at the university union entrance and 9:25 a.m. at the entrance to Anju Shantou University Garden. The group-purchase venue is Shengxiang Flooring's Shantou Guotai store.

A union supplement issued on April 26 states that Shengxiang Flooring will continue consultation from **9:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. on April 27** on the first floor of the university union office. Interested faculty and staff may visit the office to learn more about the group-purchase arrangements.

### Offers

The promotional terms supplied with the notice state that:

- all products are offered at 77% of the listed price;
- a RMB 200 earnest deposit paid on the consultation and registration day may become RMB 400 in purchase credit; the deposit is refundable if no order is placed, and a commemorative gift is provided;
- Shengxiang factory accessories, including skirting boards and metal transition strips, are offered at 51% of the listed price;
- on top of the 77% offer, groups reaching 50, 80 or 100 participating households receive an additional 1%, 2% or 3% reduction, respectively; the retailer should be consulted about the number of participating households;
- customers whose new homes have not yet begun renovation may reserve products early and receive a one-year price guarantee, together with an additional RMB 5 per square meter reduction for laminate flooring and RMB 8 per square meter for solid-wood flooring under the stated conditions; and
- whole-home wood-flooring orders, or orders covering at least 60 square meters, receive an additional RMB 6 per square meter reduction for laminate flooring and RMB 10 per square meter for solid-wood flooring. A whole-home solid-wood order also receives a decorative golden-elephant ornament valued at RMB 1,288.

The supplied terms state that the offers may be combined, although a small number of special-price products are excluded. Final discounts, group thresholds, deposit terms and product conditions should be confirmed at the event and with the retailer.

### Consultation

The Shengxiang Flooring Shantou Guotai store service hotlines are `88164541` and `88787820`. This article does not contain faculty or staff registration or transaction details.
