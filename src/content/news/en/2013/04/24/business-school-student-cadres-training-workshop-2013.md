STU Business School will hold the second session of its 2013 student-leader development workshop from **2:00 to 5:10 p.m. on April 26**. The workshop is intended to strengthen student leaders' overall capabilities, teamwork and execution in support of student-affairs work.

### Participants

The session is for members of the Business School Student Union, Business Practice Center, Young Volunteers Association, *Qipan* magazine editorial department and Business School debate team, as well as class committee members from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts.

### Format and requirements

The workshop will use keynote presentations, interactive games, situational analysis and case discussions. Participants are asked to arrange their schedules and attend all training activities on time, dress neatly and appropriately, observe discipline and follow on-site arrangements. They must arrive at the activity venue within the required time to sign in and may not be late, leave early, leave without permission, take unapproved leave or be absent without cause. Special circumstances require leave to be requested according to the stated procedure.
