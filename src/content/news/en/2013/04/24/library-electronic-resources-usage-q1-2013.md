The Shantou University Library has released its first-quarter 2013 electronic-resource usage statistics. The report covers Chinese- and foreign-language databases and records downloads, searches, clicks, plays, thumbnail views, transferred pages, books read and music tracks listened to. The measures differ by database, and some resources provide data only for January or January–February.
