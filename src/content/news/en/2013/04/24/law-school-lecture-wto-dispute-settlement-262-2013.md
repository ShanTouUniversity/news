The 262nd lecture in STU Law School's series was originally scheduled for **9:30–11:00 a.m. on April 29, 2013** in Law School Room 213. Its topic is “WTO Dispute Settlement.” A follow-up notice issued by the Law School on April 27 said that the event was canceled and postponed because of a change in the speaker's travel schedule. A new time and venue have not been announced.

### Lecture details

| --- | --- |

| Topic | WTO Dispute Settlement |
| Speaker | Zhang Yuejiao, WTO Appellate Body member and STU Law School professor |
| Original time | 9:30–11:00 a.m., April 29, 2013 |
| Original venue | Law School Room 213 |
| New time and venue | To be announced |

### Speaker

Zhang Yuejiao is a professor at STU Law School and a member of the World Trade Organization Appellate Body. She was the first judge from mainland China to serve on the WTO Appellate Body. She previously served as director-general of the Treaty and Law Department of China's Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade, a representative in China–United States intellectual-property negotiations and chief legal adviser for China's accession negotiations. She also served as assistant general counsel, chair of the Appeals Committee and director of the European Department at the Asian Development Bank.

Her other previous roles include division director in the Treaty and Law Department of China's Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade, member of the China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission and legal counsel in the Legal Department of the World Bank.
