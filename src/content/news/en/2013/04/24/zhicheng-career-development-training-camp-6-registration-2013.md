Thirty-one students registered for the sixth Zhicheng College Career Development Training Camp at Shantou University by the registration deadline.

Registrants must check in at **8:20 a.m. on April 29** in Room J-354 of Zhicheng College and attend the training sessions. Male participants are required to wear formal attire, while female participants are asked to dress in a dignified and understated style. Participants should prepare in advance.

The original notice also listed the registrants' names, year groups and majors. Because the list contains student personal information, this article reports only the aggregate number and does not reproduce or indirectly identify individual registrants.
