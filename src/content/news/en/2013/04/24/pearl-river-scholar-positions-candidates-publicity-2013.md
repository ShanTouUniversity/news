STU's Human Resources Office announced that, following applications from university units and review by the University Academic Committee, the university plans to apply for new Pearl River Scholar positions in **chemistry, mechanical design and theory, information and communication engineering, and industrial economics**. The university also recommended candidates for Pearl River Scholar Distinguished Professor positions.

The publicity period runs from **April 24 through April 28, 2013**. Units or individuals with comments may submit them in writing or by email to the Human Resources Office by April 28. The publicity is based on the Guangdong Department of Education's notice on 2013 applications for university Pearl River Scholars, document No. 粤教师函〔2013〕30号, and the relevant implementation measures for the position program.
