The Organization and United Front Department of the Communist Party of China Shantou University Committee issued a pre-appointment notice, document No. 汕大组字〔2013〕第 8 号, stating that Huang Yixiong is proposed for appointment as deputy secretary of the STU Youth League Committee. The proposal followed deliberation at the fifth 2013 meeting of the university Party Committee Standing Committee. The publicity is conducted under STU's trial rules for pre-appointment publicity of management cadres, and comments are due by **May 2, 2013**.

### Candidate profile

Huang Yixiong was born in June 1978 in Quanzhou, Fujian. He holds a bachelor's degree and is an economist. He joined the Communist Party of China in June 2000 and began working in July 2001.

He studied in the Chinese Department of Shantou University from September 1997 to June 2001 and has worked at the university since July 2001. His previous roles include political counselor and Youth League secretary in the School of Engineering; he was placed at deputy-section level in July 2004. From February 2007 to February 2008, he took part in Guangdong's “Ten, Hundred, Thousand and Ten Thousand Cadres to the Countryside” program and served as head of the working group and deputy secretary of the Party branch in Wushi Village, Longtian Town, Chaonan District, Shantou.

He became director of the School of Engineering Office in September 2008 and was appointed to an intermediate management post in July 2010.

### Subsequent appointment

On **May 7, 2013**, the STU Party Committee issued document No. 汕大党字〔2013〕第 16 号, formally appointing Huang Yixiong as deputy secretary of the STU Youth League Committee for a six-month probationary period. The appointment corresponds to the position described in the earlier pre-appointment publicity.

### Comments

Units or individuals with comments about the proposed appointment may contact the Organization and United Front Department of the university Party Committee in writing, in person or by telephone by **May 2**. Units must affix their official seal to written submissions, while individuals must provide their real names.
