It is reported that, under the unified arrangement of the National Advisory Committee for Foreign Language Teaching in Higher Education, the preliminary round of the 2013 National English Competition for College Students (NECCS) will be held from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. on Sunday, April 14, 2013. One week ahead of the contest, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued exam reminders to schools and candidates to ensure a smooth process.

## Exam Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contest | 2013 NECCS Preliminary Round |
| Time | April 14, 2013 (Sunday), 9:00-11:00 (120 minutes) |
| Full Score | 150 points (90 subjective, 60 objective) |

## Entry and Exit Rules

The office stressed that candidates must enter the exam room on time — no admission 30 minutes after the official start. Leaving the room is not allowed during the first 30 minutes (9:00-9:30) nor during the final 10 minutes (10:50-11:00). Between 9:30 and 10:50, those wishing to submit early must leave their answer sheets on their desks and wait for invigilators to collect them before exiting.

## Documents and Stationery

Candidates are required to present both the admission ticket and the student ID upon entry; anyone with incomplete documents will be denied access. As for stationery, participants had to bring their own 2B pencils and black pens, while books and electronic materials of any kind were banned from the exam room. Making any marks on the test paper would be treated as cheating.
