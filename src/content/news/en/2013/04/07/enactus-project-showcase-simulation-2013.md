To mark its fifth anniversary, the STU Enactus team, formerly known as Sai Fu, will hold its **2013 project showcase and simulated competition from 3:00 to 4:30 p.m. on Wednesday, April 10** in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Service Center (ACC).

The event will present innovative social-enterprise projects operated by the team. Representatives of the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Business School leaders, team advisors, academic advisors and business advisors will attend. The Enactus team invites the university community to exchange ideas and work together on social-enterprise projects that benefit more people.

Faculty members and students are welcome.
