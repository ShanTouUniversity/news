The Ministry of Education has announced the results of the sixth Higher Education Scientific Research Outstanding Achievement Awards in Humanities and Social Sciences. The paper **“Does Knowledge Management Matter for Information Technology Application in China?”**, co-authored by Professor Danming Lin of STU Business School and colleagues, won third prize. It is STU’s first paper-based achievement to receive this award.

Presented every three years since 1995, the award is regarded as a major and influential recognition in China’s humanities and social sciences. This round evaluated achievements publicly published between **January 1, 2008, and December 31, 2010**.

The paper was written by Danming Lin, Qiang Liang, Zongling Xu, Rundian Li and Weimin Xie and published in the ninth issue of the 2008 *Asia Pacific Journal of Management*. The journal is the official publication of the Asia Academy of Management. Its 2011 impact factor was 3.062, ranking 21st among 168 management journals indexed by SSCI.
