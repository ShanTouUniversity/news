STU’s ninth Board of Trustees first meeting is scheduled for **June 27–28**. Board meetings, commencement and activities for invited guests will take place during the period.

To support these events, the Grand Hall, Science Report Hall, Library Lecture Hall, gymnasium and Academic Exchange Service Center, along with related facilities, will be reserved and unavailable for other activities during the meeting. Units planning events should adjust their arrangements in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office of the Party Committee and Administration)
