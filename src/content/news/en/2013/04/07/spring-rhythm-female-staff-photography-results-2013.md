STU Labor Union has announced the results of the 2013 “Spring Rhythm” photography contest for female staff, featuring the rapeseed terraces of Dabu. The contest received nearly **30 entries**. Expert judges awarded **28 prizes**: two first prizes, four second prizes, 10 third prizes and 12 commemorative prizes.

Award recipients may collect their prizes from the university Labor Union from **April 7 through 10**. The attached award list is not reproduced; winners should contact the Labor Union for their individual results.
