STU Engineering College will host a lecture and demonstration on **Tuesday, April 9, at 3:00 p.m.** in the Engineering College Academic Exchange Room.

The lecture will cover the **features, operation and applications of the EON 8 virtual-reality system**, followed by demonstrations of a 3D display system, gesture-based interaction and user cases. The speaker is Pontus Appelqvist, vice president of sales for Asia-Pacific at EON Reality.

Faculty members and students interested in the topic are welcome.
