STU’s General Office and Academic Affairs Office will host the Chinese-language lecture **“Interpreting STU’s Annual Report on Social Demand and Training Quality”** from **2:30 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, April 10** in the Science Report Hall.

In 2012, STU commissioned MyCOS Data to survey the training quality of undergraduate graduates from the class of 2009 onward and prepare a report. The lecture will interpret the findings, helping the university understand strengths and weaknesses in its training process and advance undergraduate education reform.

The speakers are Lingbo Zhou, deputy director of the MyCOS Research Institute, and Professor Boqing Wang, chief expert at MyCOS. Wang helped develop the China Higher Education Follow-up Evaluation System and is the principal writer of the *Chinese College Graduate Employment Report*. Zhou researches higher education and its informatization and has led or contributed to projects including the employment report, an undergraduate-major selection system and online higher-education services.

University leaders, heads of Party and administrative units, associate professors and above, senior administrators, teaching secretaries and academic staff in teaching units, and Academic Affairs Office staff should attend. Other faculty members and students are also welcome. Designated participants unable to attend because of classes or travel should email the list of absences to `o_xzb@stu.edu.cn` by the end of the morning on **April 9**. Phone: `86502317`.
