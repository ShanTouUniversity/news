STU’s Office of Resource Management has posted student-dormitory utility charges based on meter readings through **March 31**; the previous reading was taken on February 22. Students can check their charges at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/> using their individual campus-network accounts.

Students who find an error or have questions may complete and sign the Utility Fee Verification Application and submit it to the Utility Center beside the Science and Technology Building during working hours from **April 7 through 12**. The center’s phone number is `2489`. If residence information does not match the student list, contact the counselor’s office in Building B, Room 106, at `86503355`.

After the verification period, charges without objections will be deducted from campus cards beginning **April 15**. Regular payment runs from **April 15 through 17**; unpaid charges will be handled under university rules on April 18. Objections raised after verification that require correction after review may take three to five working days. Students should top up campus cards with insufficient balances.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
