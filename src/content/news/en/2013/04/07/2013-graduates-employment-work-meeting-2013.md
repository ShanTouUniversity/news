STU Student Affairs Office will hold an employment-work meeting for the **2013 graduating class at 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, April 9** in the Science Report Hall. Party secretaries and counselors from each school and college must attend and notify graduating-class students to arrive on time.

The meeting will cover:

- Current employment conditions and related advice;
- Employment policies;
- Employment procedures.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
