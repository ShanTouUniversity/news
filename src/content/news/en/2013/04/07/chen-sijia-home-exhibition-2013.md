**Home**, an exhibition of new works by U.S.-based artist Chen Sijia, will be held at the STU Art and Design Museum from **April 8 through 14**. The opening ceremony will take place at **3:00 p.m. on April 8**. The exhibition is organized by STU’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Shantou Artists Association, and the university community and public are welcome.

Chen works in painting, photography and installation art and has exhibited in several countries. She also works in contemporary-art curating and cultural exchange between China and the United States. The exhibition presents her recent creative work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Shantou Artists Association)
