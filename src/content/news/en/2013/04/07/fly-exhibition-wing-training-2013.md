The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Union have launched the third “Exhibition Wing” program under the FLY training project. It is designed for student-organization decision-makers and students who have completed the “Trial Wing” program, with modules on learning, decision-making, creativity, influence and professional conduct. Since its launch in 2011, the FLY project has held seven training sessions.

The program plans to recruit **25 participants**. Eligible applicants include leaders of the STU Student Union, Student Societies Federation, Path to Success, Youth Volunteer Association, *STU Youth*, Student Association for Science and Technology, Graduate Student Union, school-level organizations and campus societies, as well as students who completed the Trial Wing program. If applications exceed the quota, participants will be selected by random draw.

The notice body summarizes the training as four days, April 20, April 21, May 4 and May 11, while the detailed attachment separately lists social-research practice from April 22 through May 5. Follow the organizers’ final arrangements.

Applicants should complete the Exhibition Wing application form and email it to `12ylwang@stu.edu.cn` by **midnight on April 16**. Participants must wear formal attire except during outdoor activities and attend all activities and complete assigned tasks. A participant who is late or absent without authorization once, or takes leave more than once, will not receive a completion certificate.
