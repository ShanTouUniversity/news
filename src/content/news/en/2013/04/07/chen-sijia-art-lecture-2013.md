The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at STU and the Shantou Artists Association will host an art lecture at **7:30 p.m. on Monday, April 8** in the first-floor tiered classroom of the school. The speaker is China-born U.S.-based artist Chen Sijia.

Chen has worked in painting, photography and installation art and has exhibited in China, the United States, Switzerland, Austria, Mexico and Italy, including solo exhibitions in the United States. She also works in contemporary-art curating and cultural exchange between China and the United States. The lecture will discuss her artistic path and creative experience. The university community is welcome.
