STU Library will host a lecture on the Web of Science database from **4:00 to 5:30 p.m. on Monday, April 8** in the Library Lecture Hall.

The lecture will be led by Xiao Lin, a senior trainer at Thomson Reuters. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.
