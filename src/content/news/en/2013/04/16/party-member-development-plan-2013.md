Under the latest deployment and the 2013 Party-member development plan discussed by the municipal working committee for organs, Shantou University has been assigned a 2013 development plan of **324 Party members**. The university developed 457 Party members in 2012, making the 2013 plan approximately **70%** of the previous year&apos;s actual total.

The STU Organization and United Front Work Office has asked each Party general branch and directly affiliated branch to follow the principles of controlling total numbers, improving quality and standardizing management. Units should implement the plan while strengthening process management and quality review for Party-member development.

The attachment provides each grassroots unit&apos;s 2012 total and 2013 plan. This article publishes only the university-wide total and does not reproduce unit-level details or personal lists.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office)
