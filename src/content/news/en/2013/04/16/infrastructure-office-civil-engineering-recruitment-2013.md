To support campus infrastructure projects, the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University&apos;s Resource Management Office is publicly recruiting **one civil-construction management engineer**.

### Requirements

- A college diploma or above in civil engineering, building construction or a related field, plus an intermediate or higher technical title in construction engineering; a relevant registered professional qualification is preferred;
- knowledge of project management and on-site construction management experience; three or more years of relevant experience is preferred;
- familiarity with CAD and Windows, Word and Excel;
- organizational, coordination, planning and teamwork skills, together with strong writing and communication skills;
- a strong sense of responsibility, professional commitment, ability to adopt advanced management methods and an innovative outlook; and
- age under 45, good health, professional integrity, no record of misconduct and compliance with family-planning requirements.

### Main duties

The position will manage civil-engineering technical work for project teams; support project preparation, design and tendering; coordinate pre-construction preparations; inspect the quality of materials and components on site; track the quality and progress of important works; review budget quantities and site certifications; and participate in concealed-work and completion inspections.

### Employment and application

The position will be hired as an auxiliary contract worker. The employment relationship will not be transferred to Shantou University, and compensation will follow relevant university rules. Applicants must send sealed application materials to the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office by **April 23, 2013**. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of an interview and must present original identity and qualification documents at that time.
