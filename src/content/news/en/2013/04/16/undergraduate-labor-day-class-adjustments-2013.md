Following the university&apos;s Labor Day holiday schedule, STU Academic Affairs has announced the following class adjustments for main-campus undergraduates:

| Date | Teaching arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| April 27 (Saturday) | Courses exchange with April 29 (Monday) |
| April 28 (Sunday) | Courses exchange with April 30 (Tuesday) |
| April 29 (Monday) | Courses exchange with April 27 (Saturday) |
| April 30 (Tuesday) | Courses exchange with April 28 (Sunday) |
| May 1 (Wednesday) | Classes suspended |

The arrangement applies to main-campus undergraduates. Teaching units should organize classes and communicate the schedule accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
