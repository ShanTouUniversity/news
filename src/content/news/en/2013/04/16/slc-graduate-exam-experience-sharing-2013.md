Shantou University&apos;s Student Learning Center (SLC) will hold a **“Pursuing Dreams”** graduate-exam experience session. More than 20 prospective graduate students from five schools will share experiences of graduate entrance examinations, recommendation-based admission and overseas applications with current students, helping them plan further study.

The session will combine presentations and small-group discussions so students can exchange more targeted advice based on their majors and further-study plans.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | April 20, 2013, 7:30 p.m. |
| Venue | E Building Teaching Block 204 |
| Speakers | More than 20 prospective graduate students from five schools |
| Topics | Graduate entrance examinations, recommendation-based admission, overseas applications and further-study planning |
| Information-sharing platform | SLC graduate-exam experience QQ group: 280126276 |
| Participants | All STU students |
