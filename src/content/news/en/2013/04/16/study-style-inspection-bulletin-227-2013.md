The Shantou University Three-Style Development Office has issued bulletin 227 on study-style inspections during week 7 of the 2012–2013 spring semester. Two inspections in E Building recorded **22 instances of student lateness**: nine instances within five minutes and 13 instances exceeding five minutes.

| Inspection time | Location | Within 5 minutes | More than 5 minutes |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 8:00 a.m., Wednesday, April 10, 2013 | E Building | 7 | 5 |
| 2:00 p.m., Thursday, “the 11th day of 2013” | E Building | 2 | 8 |

The second inspection date appears in the original notice as “the 11th day of 2013 (Thursday)” without a month. This article preserves the source wording and does not infer or correct the missing month.

The university asked schools and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to students who did not observe university rules and to continue strengthening study-style development. The attachment contains a student list; this article publishes only aggregate figures and does not reproduce personal names.
