Shantou University Library has opened a trial of the **2013 Oxford Journals Collection** through **June 30, 2013**. Faculty, staff and students are invited to use the collection and provide feedback.

### Collection overview

The Oxford Journals Collection contains **255 electronic journals**, about 80% of which are indexed by SCI, SSCI or AHCI. It includes many academic journals and articles with high international citation rates and covers six subject areas:

- life sciences;
- medicine;
- mathematics and physics;
- law;
- social sciences; and
- humanities.
