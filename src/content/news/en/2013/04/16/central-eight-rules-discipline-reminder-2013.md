The Shantou University Discipline Inspection Office has reiterated the need to implement the central eight-point rules, urging all units and officials to observe discipline, practice frugality and prevent extravagance or improper use of public funds.

The notice, citing a *Nanfang Daily* report on five cases notified by the provincial discipline authorities, describes the following violations:

- organizing publicly funded tourism for primary and secondary school principals under the name of training and inspection. Responsible personnel received Party-discipline sanctions, and travelers were required to bear the travel costs personally;
- distributing publicly funded New Year “opening-work” cash gifts to cadres and staff. The money was fully recovered, and responsible personnel received Party-discipline sanctions;
- organizing publicly funded spring banquets and prize draws. Responsible personnel received Party-discipline sanctions; and
- using public funds for a banquet and gifts at an institutional anniversary celebration. The responsible official received a Party-discipline sanction, and attending leaders bore the banquet costs.

The cases involved units in Yangjiang, Yunfu, Fogang and Chao&apos;an. The first case involved publicly funded trips to Kunming, Xishuangbanna, Nanjing, Shanghai and Hangzhou. The notice does not reproduce the names of the individuals involved.

### University requirements

Units and officials must draw lessons from the cases and strictly implement the central eight-point rules. The university called for an end to extravagance, publicly funded eating and entertainment, publicly funded tourism, unauthorized distribution of money or goods, and improperly organized celebrations, ensuring that the rules are reflected in routine administration and official activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office, citing a report by *Nanfang Daily*)
