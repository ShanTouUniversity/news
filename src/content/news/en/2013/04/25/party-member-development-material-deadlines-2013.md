To support the departure arrangements for members of the Communist Party of China in the 2013 graduating class and first-half party statistics, STU's Organization and United Front Department has announced deadlines for party-member development, probationary-member regularization and related materials.

### Arrangements for graduating students

Beginning **May 24, 2013**, the university will stop accepting 2013 graduating students as probationary Party members, with the date of the branch assembly used as the reference. Graduating students whose probationary periods end before July 2013 must complete regularization procedures before leaving the university.

### Material deadlines

Each unit must submit first-half materials for accepting probationary Party members, processing regularization and seeking approval from the university Party Committee to the Organization and United Front Department by **June 13**. Materials involving the 2013 graduating class are due earlier, by **May 31**, so that approval procedures and the transfer of student files can be completed before students leave.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| End of acceptance of 2013 graduating students as probationary Party members | From May 24, 2013; based on the branch-assembly date |
| Graduating students whose probation ends before July 2013 | Complete regularization before leaving the university |
| Materials involving the 2013 graduating class | Due by May 31 |
| Other first-half development and regularization materials | Due by June 13 |
| Submission location | Organization and United Front Department, Room 315, New Administrative Center |

The notice is addressed to Party committees and directly affiliated Party branches and does not include personal lists or public registration materials.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department)
