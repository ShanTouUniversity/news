STU's General Office has announced interview arrangements for Administrative Center reception assistants. Interviews are scheduled for the **afternoons of Friday, April 26, and Saturday, April 27** in Administrative Center Room 221. Shortlisted students should assemble outside the room at their assigned times and attend the interview.

### Interview details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Position | Administrative Center reception assistant |
| Interview time | Afternoons of April 26 and April 27 |
| Interview venue | Administrative Center Room 221 |
| Assembly point | Outside Administrative Center Room 221 |
| Individual time slots | See the interview schedule |

For special circumstances, candidates may contact Ms. Lin at `86502305` or campus extension `882305`.

The two XLS files supplied with the notice assign interview slots to shortlisted students, but their contents could not be read reliably. Because the schedules contain student names and individual interview information, the lists, time slots and spreadsheet contents are not reproduced here.
