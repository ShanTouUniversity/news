STU's Human Resources Office announced that, following nominations by university units, discussion by relevant unit heads and deliberation at a university leadership meeting, the university proposes Ma Fengqi from the main campus and Xu Jiezhou from the Medical College as trustee nominees for its ninth Board of Trustees.

The publicity period runs from **April 25 through May 1**. Units or individuals with comments about the nominees may submit them in writing to the Human Resources Office during the publicity period.

### Nominees

| Representing unit | Nominee | Proposed role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main campus | Ma Fengqi | Trustee nominee for the ninth Board of Trustees |
| Medical College | Xu Jiezhou | Trustee nominee for the ninth Board of Trustees |

### Comments

The notice gives `86503390` as the telephone number for comments. It does not provide additional personal biographies for the two nominees, and none are inferred here.

### Subsequent result

On **May 3**, the university reported that, under the Board of Trustees charter and the arrangements for the changeover, Ma Fengqi and Xu Jiezhou had been recommended as faculty representatives for the ninth Board of Trustees after nominations and selection by university units and deliberation at a university leadership meeting. The recommendation would be submitted to the Board of Trustees for consideration; this article does not describe it as a completed appointment.
