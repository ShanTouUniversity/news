STU Graduate School will hold a graduate employment experience exchange from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on April 28** in Room 410 of Building E. Titled “A Difficult Graduation Season: His Path, Your Path and My Path,” the event is open to all graduate students on the main campus. Student or recent-graduate speakers with experience in government administration, a public institution and an enterprise will discuss different paths after graduation and through the job search.

### Speakers

| Anonymized identifier | Academic background | Employment direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Su XW | Class of 2009, public administration | Shantou Municipal People's Government Office; civil servant and government agency |
| Wu TF | Class of 2010, computer application technology | Shantou Air Traffic Management Station of the Civil Aviation Administration of China; public institution |
| Deng ZF | Class of 2010, mechanical design and theory | Ningde New Energy Technology Co., Ltd.; foreign-invested enterprise |

The original notice gave the three speakers' full names. Because they are students or recent graduates, this article uses the family name followed by the initials of the given name in pinyin. Their employers and employment categories are retained from the notice to describe the career directions represented in the exchange.

### Registration

Interested graduate students must register by email by **5:30 p.m. on April 27** and follow the notice's instruction to include their year group, major, name and the phrase “employment experience exchange.” The registration address is `mphuang@stu.edu.cn`; participants should follow subsequent arrangements from the Graduate School. Names, majors and other personal registration data are not published here.

The exchange will address how to identify suitable recruitment information, prepare for written tests and interviews, and recognize individual strengths in a competitive employment market. It is intended to give graduate students practical perspectives on different employment paths.
