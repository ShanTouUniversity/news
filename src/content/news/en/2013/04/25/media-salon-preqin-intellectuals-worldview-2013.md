STU's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a media salon from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on April 27** at the Science Report Hall. The topic is “How Pre-Qin Intellectuals Understood the World.” Professor Dai Weihua, a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Chinese Language and Literature at South China Normal University, will speak.

### Speaker

Dai Weihua holds a doctorate in literature and is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Chinese Language and Literature at South China Normal University. He also serves as director of the Institute of Chinese Literature and Culture, head of Guangdong's first-level key discipline in Chinese language and literature, and academic leader of the authorized first-level doctoral discipline in Chinese language and literature. He is vice chair of the Chinese Tang Dynasty Literature Society and receives a special allowance from the State Council.

Dai has published eight monographs, including *唐方镇文职僚佐考* and *地域文化与唐代诗歌*, and nearly 100 academic papers in journals including *Social Sciences in China*. He has led one “Eighth Five-Year Plan” social-sciences project of the former State Education Commission, three projects under the National Social Sciences Planning program and multiple projects of the Ancient Books Committee of Chinese Universities.

His work has received one third prize in the humanities and social sciences awards for Chinese universities, three first prizes in the Guangdong provincial government philosophy and social sciences awards, two third prizes in the Jiangsu provincial government social sciences awards and other honors.
