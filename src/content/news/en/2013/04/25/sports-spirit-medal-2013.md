It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 52 [2013] on April 25, 2013, announcing the results of the 2013 Li Ka Shing Foundation STU Sportsmanship Medal.

Established to carry forward Mr. Li Ka-shing’s educational philosophy of “Aspiration, Knowledge, Perseverance and Achievement” and to honor outstanding graduates who demonstrate noble sportsmanship and contribute to the university’s sports development, the medal was awarded after strict selection by the evaluation committee.

## Awardees

| Award | Recipient (anonymized) | College | Program | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sportsmanship Medal | Chen HC | Business School | Business Administration, Class of 2009 | 8,000 yuan |
| Nomination Award | Wang JH | School of Science | Applied Chemistry, Class of 2009 | 1,000 yuan |
| Nomination Award | Xie MF | School of Science | Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, Class of 2009 | 1,000 yuan |
