Shantou University Enactus, formerly known as SIFE, will open its 2013 entrepreneurship week on **April 26**. The opening public lecture, titled “What Makes Me Fit to Start a Business?”, will feature Jiang Zhu, CEO of Meiwei Media, sharing entrepreneurial experience and practical reflections. The event is organized by STU Business School and co-organized by the STU Enactus team.

### Schedule

| Event | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “What Makes Me Fit to Start a Business?” public lecture | 3:00–5:00 p.m., April 26 | Science Lecture Hall |
| Entrepreneurship discussion | 8:00 p.m., April 26 | Student Activity Hall |

The events are open to students, faculty and staff. The notice also allowed same-day registration and provided an advance-registration channel. Advance registration requested names, majors, mobile numbers and email addresses; personal contact details and registration data are not reproduced here.

### Speaker

Jiang Zhu was born in 1974 and graduated from Solent University in the United Kingdom. He began his career in marketing, sales and management in the fast-moving consumer-goods sector and later held positions at Jiucheng Group, Youpeng Pule and Baidu's video website iQIYI. He founded Meiwei Media in 2012 to work on cross-media distribution of television programs.

The entrepreneurship week aims to bring experienced entrepreneurial mentors to campus, helping students develop entrepreneurial awareness and ability while providing references for career choices, practical preparation and risk assessment.
