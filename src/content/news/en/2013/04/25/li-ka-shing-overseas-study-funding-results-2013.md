STU's Student Affairs Office has announced the results of the 2013 Li Ka Shing Foundation-funded STU Overseas Studies Funding Scheme. After review by the selection committee and approval by the management committee, **19 students** received awards in three categories: six in Category A, six in Category B and seven in Category C.

### Awards

| Category | Recipients | Award per recipient | Category total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 6 | RMB 80,000 | RMB 480,000 |
| B | 6 | RMB 50,000 | RMB 300,000 |
| C | 7 | RMB 30,000 | RMB 210,000 |
| Total | 19 | — | RMB 990,000 |

Established by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the scheme supports academically outstanding STU students with commitment and potential to pursue master's degrees at leading universities outside mainland China. It aims to develop graduates with international experience and a global perspective. The scheme has been open to students across STU's schools since 2007 and currently accepts applications only for overseas master's study.

### Selection process

The 2013 selection notice was issued on January 16, and **37 eligible students** applied. After an initial review of the application materials, the selection committee chose 25 students for second-round interviews. The interviews on April 11 assessed academic performance, foreign-language ability, the academic quality and standing of the proposed universities and programs, and broader qualities including participation in public affairs, community service and extracurricular activities.

According to the selection report, award recipients must obtain admission to a university approved by the management committee before receiving support to pursue a master's degree abroad. The attachment states that, unless otherwise agreed, students should obtain admission to an approved university and master's program by **the end of March 2014**. It also says that the scholarship category varies according to the selected university.

### University information and source discrepancy

The attachment lists proposed universities and master's programs associated with individual award recipients, covering institutions in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Hong Kong, Canada, Sweden, the Netherlands, Switzerland and Singapore. Because these details are linked to individual students' application combinations, this article does not reproduce names, departments, majors, university-program mappings or no-offer information.

The main notice names Yale University, the University of Cambridge, Columbia University and Boston University as examples of overseas institutions. The attached selection report instead names a London university in place of Boston University, while the English annex lists specific options including SOAS, University of London. The source documents therefore differ in how they describe the institutions. This article does not infer any individual's final admission or funding destination.

The document is numbered **STU Student Affairs Document [2013] No. 3** and dated April 25, 2013.
