In accordance with higher-education requirements for student registration and degree-certificate information, STU Academic Affairs Office is conducting an **enrollment-record review for the 2014 undergraduate graduating class**. Academic staff in each school should collect the “2014 Undergraduate Graduate Enrollment Information Verification List” and distribute it to students who entered in 2010 for item-by-item verification.

Students must verify and sign the list themselves. If all information is correct, check the “Correct” option and sign. If personal fields such as name, sex, date of birth, national ID number or ethnicity do not match, mark “Incorrect,” identify the fields in error and sign, then bring the original ID card and household register to the Student Affairs Section on the third floor of the New Administration Building to request a correction. Records will not be changed without completing the correction process or if approval is denied.

The list is based on admissions information recorded at enrollment and will serve as the basis for student records and graduation and degree certificates. Schools must collect the verification materials and submit them to the Student Affairs Section by **June 20**. As a general rule, applications from students who entered in 2010 to change these key fields will not be accepted afterward.

For questions, contact the academic staff in the relevant school or Ms. Xu at the Academic Affairs Office Student Affairs Section: `86502623`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The verification list contains student names, national ID numbers and other personal information. Lists and forms are not reproduced; complete verification and correction procedures through the school and Academic Affairs Office.
