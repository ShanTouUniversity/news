The Guangdong Provincial Bureau of Retired Cadres has recognized STU’s Organization and United Front Work Department and Office for Retired Faculty and Staff as an **Outstanding Unit for 2012 Retired-Cadre Statistical Reporting**.

The recognition reflects the departments’ standardized work in retired-cadre information statistics, administration and services.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department and Office of Human Resources)
