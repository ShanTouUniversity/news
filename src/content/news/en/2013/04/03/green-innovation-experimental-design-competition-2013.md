The Youth League Committee of STU’s College of Science has launched the Green Innovation experimental-design competition, organized by the college’s Student Union and Radio Club. Under the theme “Greenery, Technology and Life,” the competition invites undergraduates to develop experimental verification, product designs and open experimental proposals related to campus sustainability, strengthening practical application, innovation and hands-on skills.

### Requirements

- Participants must be enrolled STU undergraduates;
- Teams may have **two to five members**, with College of Science students making up at least half of each team. Each team should designate a leader and may invite no more than two faculty advisers;
- Projects must address campus sustainability and demonstrate innovation, energy efficiency, environmental value, practicality, simple structure and low cost;
- Entries must be original. The verification track uses experiments to test a selected topic; the design track develops an experimental design and physical work. Both preliminary and final rounds use defenses, with short films or videos permitted as supporting presentation materials.

The preliminary round assesses design principles, innovation, environmental and energy performance, practicality and feasibility. The final assesses craftsmanship, demonstration, on-site defense, environmental and energy performance and practicality. Final criteria are subject to the final-round notice.

The verification track offers one first prize of RMB 300, one second prize of RMB 200 and several third prizes of RMB 100 per team. The design track offers one first prize of RMB 500, one second prize of RMB 300 and several third prizes of RMB 200 per team.
