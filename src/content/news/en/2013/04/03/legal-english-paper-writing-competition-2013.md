The Career Development Center of STU Law School has announced a legal-English paper competition to build an academic environment, encourage research and innovation on legal issues, and strengthen students’ English academic writing.

### Submission requirements

- **Submission period: April 3–May 20**;
- **Participants**: All Law School undergraduates. Public Administration students may also participate if their papers address a legal issue;
- Papers must present a legal perspective in English on a current focal, emerging or difficult issue. Topics are open;
- Papers should be **3,000–5,000 characters or words in length**, as specified by the original notice;
- Submit two printed copies and one electronic copy. Name the electronic file “paper title + author name.”

Submit printed copies to the designated collection point in Law School Room F310 and email the electronic copy to `12pemo@stu.edu.cn`. Contact details in submissions are used only for competition communication and are not reproduced here.

The competition will award first, second and third prizes, with certificates issued by the Law School. Outstanding papers may be recommended for the national legal-English competition.
