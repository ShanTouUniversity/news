Zhicheng College will hold its second “Etiquette and Culture Learning Camp” from **8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on April 13**. The camp is open to Zhicheng residents and students living in other university residences who volunteer to apply. It plans to admit **32 students**, including 26 from Zhicheng and six from other residences; if applications exceed the quota, participants will be selected by lottery.

Topics include *Changli Juyao*, Chinese etiquette culture, key approaches to etiquette learning, the relationship between etiquette and the individual, family and society, social interaction through everyday details, and communication and personal presentation. The program combines lectures, expert videos, group discussion, individual study and centralized learning.

Applicants must be able to attend the full session and demonstrate a willingness to learn and work in teams. Complete the application form and email it to `ATP@stu.edu.cn`; follow the original OA attachment for the required filename format and application instructions.

The notice gives the application deadline as **5:00 p.m. on April 8, 2012**, which conflicts with the April 13, 2013 event date. This source discrepancy is retained; interested students should confirm the actual deadline with Zhicheng College.

A subsequent notice reported that **30 students** registered and were all selected for the camp. Participants must check in at **7:50 a.m. on April 13** in the Zhicheng College J-354 lecture room and attend the learning sessions.
