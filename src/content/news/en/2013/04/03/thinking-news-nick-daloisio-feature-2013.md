The second issue of STU’s “Thinking News” recommends a feature published in Hong Kong’s financial newspaper *Hong Kong Economic Journal* on **April 3**, written by Kaixuan Zhou, a director of the Li Ka Shing Foundation. The feature introduces the growth and entrepreneurial experience of Nick D’Aloisio.

D’Aloisio became the world’s youngest venture-capital entrepreneur at age 15. The Publicity Department recommends the profile as a reference for students interested in technology entrepreneurship, innovation and personal development.
