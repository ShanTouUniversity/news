Following the 2013 call from the Office of the Guangdong Science and Technology Awards Evaluation Committee, STU Research Office has launched the university’s nomination process. The awards include the Outstanding Contribution Award and project awards in natural sciences, technological invention and applied technology.

### Eligibility

Outstanding Contribution Award candidates must conduct independent innovation work in Guangdong and have made major contributions. Nominated projects should hold independent intellectual property rights. Natural-science results should be supported by publicly published and peer-recognized papers or books; inventions must have authorized patents; other intellectual-property rights must also be supported by valid documentation.

The publication date of representative papers and books, the application date of technological inventions and the overall application date of applied-technology results should generally be no later than **May 31, 2011**. All nominated projects must undergo a science-and-technology achievement evaluation and complete Guangdong registration by **April 20**, obtaining a Science and Technology Achievement Registration Certificate.

### Schedule and materials

Applicants should follow the *2013 Guangdong Science and Technology Awards Recommendation Handbook*, log in to the Guangdong Science and Technology Business Integrated Management System at <http://pro.gdstc.gov.cn>, complete the recommendation form and upload supporting materials. Key dates:

- **April 20**: Deadline for science-and-technology achievement registration;
- **April 15–May 17**: Online completion and review by recommending units and experts;
- **May 31**: Deadline for submitting printed materials.

Interested faculty members should contact the Research Office promptly to complete evaluation, registration and preparation. Contacts: Yingguang Luo and Yong Deng, `86503271`. Follow the provincial department and original OA attachments for the handbook, recommendation form and detailed requirements.
