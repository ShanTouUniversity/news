STU’s Second Affiliated Hospital has notified the university that faculty and staff who selected the hospital for their medical examination will attend at the Second Affiliated Hospital Medical Examination Center on **April 9 and 10**. Participants should arrive fasting at **8:00 a.m.** according to their assigned schedule; the Office of Resource Management’s attached table provides individual time slots.

Teachers unable to attend because of class commitments may complete the examination before **April 19**, during working hours and before 8:00 a.m. People with hypertension should bring prescribed blood-pressure medication.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
>
> ⚠️ Individual examination schedules and health information are not published. Obtain personal arrangements from the Office of Resource Management.
