STU Business School has launched an undergraduate visiting-student program with the Gustavson School of Business at the University of Victoria in Canada. The school will select **five** candidates from its 2010 and 2011 undergraduate cohorts.

### Eligibility and costs

Applicants must:

- Be full-time Business School undergraduates in at least their second year; students from the 2010 and 2011 cohorts may apply;
- Have a cumulative GPA of **at least 3.0**;
- Independently take an IELTS or TOEFL test and meet one of the following thresholds: IELTS 6.5 overall with no component below 6.0, or TOEFL 90 overall with no component below 21;
- Students who have not previously participated in a university or school exchange program will receive priority.

Students are responsible for application fees, tuition, student-community fees, one round-trip international journey, living expenses, accommodation, insurance and other related costs.

### Materials and selection

Required materials include STU Business School and University of Victoria study-abroad application forms, the University of Victoria course-preference form, IELTS or TOEFL evidence, an academic transcript and an English essay of approximately 300–500 words on the purpose of joining the program and an overseas study plan. Application materials are not reproduced.

The Business School will first review written materials and determine interview candidates. Its review panel will then select the final candidates through interviews. Submit printed and electronic materials to the Business School International Programs Office in Wenxi Building Room 211 by **noon on April 25**. Email electronic materials to `dnye@stu.edu.cn`.

Contact: Dongni Ye, `86503382`, `dnye@stu.edu.cn`. Course information is subject to the latest materials provided by the University of Victoria and the Business School.
