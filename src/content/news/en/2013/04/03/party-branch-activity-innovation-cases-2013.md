Following the Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee’s call for innovative activity cases from grassroots university Party branches, STU’s Organization and United Front Work Department is organizing a university-wide solicitation among Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches.

Each unit must recommend **at least one innovative Party-branch activity case** and prepare the case materials according to the provincial requirements. Submit materials to the Organization and United Front Work Department in Administrative Center Room 315 by **April 13**, and email an electronic copy to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.

Consult the original notice from the Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee for detailed case requirements and evaluation criteria.
