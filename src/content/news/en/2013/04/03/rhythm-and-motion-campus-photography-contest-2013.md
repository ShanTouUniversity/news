The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and “STU Youth” have launched the “Rhythm and Motion on Campus” photography contest. Open to all enrolled students, the contest seeks photographs centered on music and sports while encouraging photography skills, musical appreciation, physical activity and engagement with campus life.

### Schedule and requirements

- **April 16**: Entry deadline;
- **April 17–20**: Online voting;
- Entries must address the “sound and motion” theme through music, sports or both, with positive content reflecting the energy and civility of university life;
- Entries must be original works by the student and may be a single photograph or a series. A series counts as one entry and may contain no more than six images;
- Digital editing must not alter the original image or add a border. Basic cropping, brightness and contrast adjustments are permitted, but extensive Photoshop processing is not.

### Entry methods and awards

The contest has two tracks: “Microblog Snapshots” and “Online Entries.” Microblog Snapshots are not judged, and participants will receive two specially made postcards. Online Entries will be scored by online voting and professional judges, weighted at 40% and 60%, respectively.

For the online track, place the original JPG files and entry form in one folder, compress it and email it to `12xcjiao@stu.edu.cn`. For Microblog Snapshots, post the photograph and an approximately 20-character description on Sina Weibo, then follow and mention “STU Youth.” The contest offers one first prize, two second prizes and three third prizes.

The organizers may use selected works for public-interest publicity, albums and postcards. Authors retain attribution rights, but no remuneration will be paid for use.
