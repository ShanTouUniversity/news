STU Central Laboratory launched an upgraded large-instrument booking system on **April 1**. The system publishes instrument availability in real time, helping campus users plan testing and maintain orderly operations. The upgrade adds a supervisor-confirmation step.

### Booking and confirmation

A student’s supervisor should normally confirm an experiment booking. A supervisor may authorize one faculty or student member of the research group to serve as the confirmation contact. Supervisors who need to authorize a contact should email the authorization information to `yjlin@stu.edu.cn`, after which the laboratory will update the relevant confirmation contact.

Users can select instruments and book time at <http://zxsys.stu.edu.cn/ShowInstrList.php> and consult the “Notice on the Upgrade of the Central Laboratory Large-Instrument Booking System” at <http://zxsys.stu.edu.cn/ShowNotice.php?recno=104>.

Users must book time truthfully and in advance and arrive at the scheduled time. Cancel at least one working day in advance. Cancellations within one working day require a phone call to the instrument supervisor. More than three no-shows will result in a one-month suspension of booking privileges.

The person booking instrument time must be the person who uses the instrument; users may not book through their own accounts for someone else. In exceptional cases requiring an urgent sample insertion, contact the instrument supervisor first and submit a form signed by the supervisor or authorized confirmation contact afterward.

### Instrument-time limits

Because some instruments have limited availability, the laboratory may limit the number of bookings a user can make in one week according to actual demand. Users may consult the relevant instrument group before booking and should choose a reasonable period based on their samples, preferably an hour or half a day. Repeated discrepancies of more than one hour between booked and actual use may result in restrictions on later bookings.
