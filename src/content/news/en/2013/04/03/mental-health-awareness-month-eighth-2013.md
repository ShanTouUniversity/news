STU’s Student Affairs Office has launched its eighth Mental Health Awareness Month, inviting the university community to join a series of mental-health education and campus activities. The notice lists:

1. An opening ceremony;
2. Mental-wellness workshops;
3. The “Say It Out Loud” creative-works contest;
4. “STU Slow Mail”;
5. Outdoor psychological counseling;
6. A closing ceremony.

### Mental-wellness workshop

The awareness month’s mental-wellness workshop, **“Return to Adolescence and Break Self-Limitation,”** will be led by psychologist Sixuan Tao from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on April 12** in the Library Student Activity Hall. Through guided activities, participants will explore their inner selves and support personal growth.

The workshop seeks 10 volunteers and 40 participants on a first-come, first-served basis. Submit the application form to `o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn` by **5:00 p.m. on April 11**. Volunteers must attend advance training from 1:30 to 2:30 p.m. on April 12. Participants are asked to prepare clothing representing different stages or roles in their lives; consult the Student Affairs Office form for details.

### Opening ceremony

The awareness month opening ceremony will take place at **7:30 p.m. on April 12** in the Science Report Hall, followed by the lecture **“Seeing Through Your Unknown Mind: Secrets of the Subconscious,”** delivered by psychologist Sixuan Tao. The university community is welcome.
