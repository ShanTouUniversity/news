STU Library has published its fourth electronic-resources bulletin of 2013, recommending the following study and research resources:

- **CNKI E-Learning**: Organize literature by learning unit and map knowledge through citations, references and related materials at <http://yanshi.cnki.net/eln>;
- **Duxiu identity-verification program**: Submit verification materials from an on-campus valid IP range. Successful verification provides 10 years of off-campus roaming access at <http://www.duxiu.com/nameauth/identifyindex.jsp>;
- **CASHL foreign humanities and social-science journals**: The library provides a free public-account service through **April 15**. Enter a valid email address when requesting an article to receive the document at <http://www.cashl.edu.cn:8000/search/jnlsearch.asp>;
- **Chinese Academy of Sciences Scientific Journals Open-Access Platform**: Provides open-access journal links, document access, citation analysis and subject consultation for personal teaching, research and study at <http://www.oaj.cas.cn/cn/default.aspx>;
- **Hans Publishers open-access Chinese journals**: Covers mathematics and physics, life sciences, chemistry and materials, engineering, information and communications, humanities and social sciences, and other fields. Articles are available for free at <http://www.hanspub.org>.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Library)
>
> ⚠️ CASHL public-account credentials are not reproduced. Consult the library’s original notice for current access instructions and follow each platform’s usage rules.
