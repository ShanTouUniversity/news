It is reported that the Supervision and Audit Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice aiming to further standardize and strengthen the management of leadership signature seals, following an investigation in the second half of 2011 into a case where an employee at a certain unit used a signature seal without authorization for personal gain.

The notice requires all units that use signature seals to integrate seal management into their ISO management systems, establish usage and management procedures, clarify the scope of authorization, and maintain proper handover registration for custodians. Each use of a signature seal must be recorded with the date, purpose, number of copies, and method of authorization, and must be signed by both the applicant and the custodian.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Supervision and Audit Office)
