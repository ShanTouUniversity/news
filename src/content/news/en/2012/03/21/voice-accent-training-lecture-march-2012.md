It is reported that the English Language Center at Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the first lecture in the 2012 Voice Training Series will be held on Thursday, March 22 from 7:10 to 8:40 PM in E-Lecture Hall 101.

The speaker for this session is Chanchal Singh, presenting on the topic "Correct Voice & Accent for Intelligible Communication - Formal Greetings and Social Etiquette." Faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Center)
