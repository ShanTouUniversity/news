It is reported that the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently issued a supplemental notice providing additional guidance on the "2011 Annual Large-Scale Instrument and Equipment Usage Survey Form."

The notice clarifies that the "instrument manager" and "instrument archive materials" fields are required only for large-scale instruments and equipment valued at 100,000 RMB or above. The survey period for instrument usage spans from January 1, 2011 to December 31, 2011.

For inquiries, please contact Wang Shudi at 82903142 or sdwang@stu.edu.cn.
