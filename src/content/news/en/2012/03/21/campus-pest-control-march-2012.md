It is reported that the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing pest control and disease prevention spray operations across campus green spaces, fruit trees, and drainage ditches (including Longquan Garden and the Continuing Education College) from March 25 to April 1, 2012.

The schedule is as follows:

| Date | Area |
|------|------|
| March 25 | Affiliated Middle School, Affiliated Primary School to Mental Health Center |
| March 26 | Artificial Lake area, Sports Field |
| March 27 | Around Academic Exchange Center, Building P, Student Dormitory Area |
| March 28 | Reservoir Dam, Buildings 789, Staff Residential Area A-B |
| March 29 | Medical Building, New Teaching Building, Student Dormitories G/H/I/J |
| March 30 | Perimeter of Campus Security Office, Main Gate, all campus roads, perimeter of New Library |
| March 31 | Cafeteria, around Administration Building |
| April 1 | Longquan Garden, Continuing Education College |

The university urges all faculty, staff, and students to exercise caution and avoid the spray operation areas during this period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
