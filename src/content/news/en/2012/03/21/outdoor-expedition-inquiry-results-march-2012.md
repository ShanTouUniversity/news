It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University published the list of teams selected for the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Expedition Inquiry Review on the campus intranet on March 21. Prior to this, the office completed the collection of project applications on March 16, and the review committee conducted a written evaluation to determine which teams would advance to the inquiry review stage.

The inquiry review is scheduled to take place on March 22 from 3:00 to 6:00 PM in ACC Conference Room 2.
