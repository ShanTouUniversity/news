It is reported that the Communist Youth League Shantou University Committee recently issued a notice announcing the launch of an annual assessment of grassroots league organizations across campus, effective from 2012. The initiative aims to promote the overall work standards of the university's league organizations and strengthen standardized construction at the grassroots level.

The assessment comprises two parts: annual evaluations of league branches and annual evaluations of college/school league committees. League branch assessments cover ideological education, organizational development, second-course activities, and the exemplary role of league members. College/school league committee assessments include three aspects: ideological and political education, organizational development and standardized management, and services supporting the growth and development of league members and youth.

The Committee plans to organize the assessment work in April 2012.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Shantou University Committee)
