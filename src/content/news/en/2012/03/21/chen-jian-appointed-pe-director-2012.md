It is reported that the Human Resources Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice stating that, following deliberation at the third university leadership meeting held on March 20, 2012, Chen Jian has been proposed for appointment as Director of the Physical Education Department.

The public notice period runs from March 21 to March 27, 2012. Any objections may be submitted in writing. For inquiries, please contact 82903390 or psyu@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
