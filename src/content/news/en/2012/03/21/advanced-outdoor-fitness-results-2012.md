It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on March 21, 2012, announcing the results of the fitness test for the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Program.

With active participation from faculty and students, the fitness test was successfully completed. The list of qualified participants is now published. To protect student privacy, the full list is withheld here; details are available in the OA attachments.

The university thanked all participants for their support.

> ⚠️ The full list is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
