It is reported that the Party and Government Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that, to enhance the service and execution capabilities of university administrative staff, a special lecture will be held on Friday, March 23 at 3:00 PM in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center. Professor Hui King Man, Vice President of the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), has been invited to deliver a lecture on the principles and experiences of university administrative operations.

University leaders, Medical College leadership, directors and deputy directors of all departments and offices at the main campus, as well as leaders of all colleges are invited to attend. All units are requested to submit their confirmed attendance lists to the Secretariat of the Party and Government Office by 5:00 PM on March 22. Contact: Qiu Saiqing, Tel: 8290 2317, Email: o_xzb@stu.edu.cn.
