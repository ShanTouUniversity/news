It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 STU PPT Electronic Resume Competition will be held from late March to May 2012.

The competition is open to all currently enrolled students and is individual-based. Registration can be done by submitting paper application forms to Rooms A235, C318, E147, or G542, or by registering in person at Jin San Jiao on April 7. The event is co-organized by China Unicom Shantou Branch and Shantou Recruitment Network, and hosted by the STU Career Planning and Development Association.

Students who advance to the finals will have the opportunity to secure internship positions provided by China Unicom Shantou Branch and Shantou Recruitment Network.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
