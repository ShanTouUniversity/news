It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the special research project "Fast-Track Sharing of Scientific Papers in the Network Era," first launched by the Science and Technology Development Center of the Ministry of Education in 2007, has received active responses from researchers nationwide. To further promote fast-track sharing of scientific papers and better regulate and develop the "China Academic Papers and Documents Online" (www.paper.edu.cn) platform, the center has released the 2012 project guidelines.

This special topic covers 12 research directions. Applicants may propose topics freely based on the "Project Guidelines for Fast-Track Sharing of Scientific Papers in the Network Era (2012)" and their own expertise. Each project\'s application budget is capped at 80,000 yuan. The research period is one year. Applicants should download the application form from the China Academic Papers and Documents Online website or the Ministry of Education Science and Technology Development Center website, and submit the electronic draft of the application by early April.
