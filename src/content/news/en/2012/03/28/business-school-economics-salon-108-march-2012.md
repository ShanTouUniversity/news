It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Business School Economics Salon (VOL.108) will be held on March 31, 2012 (Saturday) from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM in the New Library Auditorium.

The speaker is Dr. Yuan Yue, who holds a PhD in Sociology from Peking University, is Chairman of the Zero Point Research and Consulting Group, and serves as Chairman of the YES Zero Point Youth Public Welfare Entrepreneurship Development Center. The lecture topic is "The Service Revolution and Service Industry Entrepreneurship." The lecture will be delivered in Chinese. Business School undergraduate and graduate students are requested to attend; all faculty and students are welcome.
