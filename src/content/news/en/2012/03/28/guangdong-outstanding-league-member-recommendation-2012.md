It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League\'s "Notice on Applying for 2011-2012 Guangdong Outstanding Communist Youth League Members, Outstanding League Cadres, May Fourth Red Flag League Organizations, and Advanced Collectives in "Two New" Organizations" (团粤办发〔2012〕22号) and the recommendations from various units, after discussion, it is proposed to recommend the Basic Department League Branch of the STU Medical College for the "Guangdong May Fourth Red Flag League Branch (General Branch)" and Chen Jianhui (College of Engineering) for the "Guangdong Outstanding League Member."

Units or individuals with objections may report in writing or in person/by phone to the University Youth League Committee before 4:00 PM on March 31, 2012. (Unit submissions must bear the official seal; individual submissions must include the real name for responsibility purposes.) Tel: 0754-82902302.
