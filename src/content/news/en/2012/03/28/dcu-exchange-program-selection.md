It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts of Shantou University and the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences at Dublin City University, Ireland, have preliminarily reached a four-year student exchange agreement: starting from the fall semester of 2012, each side will select five undergraduates per semester to study courses at the partner institution.

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Quota | 5 students |
| Eligibility | Full-time CLA undergraduates of the 2010 and 2011 cohorts (second or third year as of September 2012) |
| Deadline | 11 a.m., April 25, 2012 |
| Submission | Youth League office (Room 201, South Liberal Arts Building); electronic copies by email |

Applicants must complete an exchange program application form and provide GPA and ranking certificates along with copies of English proficiency proof. A selection panel will first review written materials to determine candidates for the written test.

## Exchange Conditions

According to the announcement, exchange students pay normal tuition to STU while DCU waives their tuition. However, students must cover their own international travel as well as living expenses abroad — approximately 700 to 900 euros per month, about 6,000 to 8,000 yuan.

## Selection Criteria

The published criteria include:

- Outstanding academic performance;
- Adequate financial means;
- Strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills;
- Maturity, independence and team spirit;
- Recognition of STU and its educational features, with the ability to promote them abroad.

In addition, the program offers one-semester and two-semester options; selecting "or Both" on the application form indicates willingness to be assigned either option, and the college may adjust arrangements based on actual circumstances.

According to the college, this cooperation marks a promising start to its overseas study plan for undergraduates, will encourage graduates to pursue further studies, and will enhance Shantou University's international influence.
