It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently issued a notice that, to celebrate the 8th anniversary of CASHL (China Academic Journals and Societies Literature) services, all new and existing CASHL users can obtain a certain number of free journal papers. Specifically: senior users receive 100 papers, advanced users 70, and普通 users 50. Users who register successfully before April 30 will become "普通 users" and can immediately enjoy CASHL\'s quality services and rich literature resources.

Usage instructions: New users should visit the CASHL website (http://www.cashl.edu.cn), click "Register" under "Personalized Services" in the lower-left corner, then send registration information via email to the contact (Email: yxu@stu.edu.cn, Tel: 3247), specifying the registered username, name, department, grade, and campus card number. This promotional service ends on April 30; any unused free paper allocations will not be extended.
