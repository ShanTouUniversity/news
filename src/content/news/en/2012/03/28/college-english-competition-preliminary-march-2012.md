It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "2012 National College English Competition Notice" from the Teaching Guidance Committee for College Foreign Languages of Higher Education, the preliminary round of the 2012 National College English Competition will be held on April 8 (Sunday) from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

To ensure the smooth conduct of the exam, candidates should note the following: the preliminary written exam is out of 150 points (90 subjective, 60 objective); the exam duration is 120 minutes, and candidates must enter the venue on time—no entry is permitted 30 minutes after the exam begins; candidates may not leave the venue during the first 30 minutes (9:00-9:30) or the last 10 minutes (10:50-11:00); candidates must bring their own 2B pencils, black ink pens, and headphones—no books or electronic materials are allowed; the exam at STU is divided into Categories A, B, and C, with Category C requiring headphones.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
