It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Press of South China University of Technology will hold a premium textbook exhibition on campus. Sample textbooks required by faculty will be available for free registration, and those with publishing intentions can communicate with editors on-site.

The exhibition will be held on March 29, 2012 (Thursday), from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM, at the area below Teaching Buildings D and G (in front of the Medical College Basic Department). All faculty are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
