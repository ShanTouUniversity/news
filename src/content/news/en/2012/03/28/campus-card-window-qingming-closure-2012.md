It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the campus card windows at the Expert Apartment and beneath the Second Cafeteria will be closed from April 2 to 4 during the Qingming Festival, resuming operations on April 5.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
