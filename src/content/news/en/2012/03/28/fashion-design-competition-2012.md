It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 6th FASHION SHOW Clothing Design Competition of 2012 is about to begin.

The theme of this year\'s competition is "Yi" (Change). "Yi" is an attitude—daring to break conventions and turn the ordinary into the extraordinary; "Yi" is transformation—presenting colors, styles, and designs in ever-changing forms; "Yi" is a posture—infusing emotion into clothing to make it approachable; "Yi" is culture—the timeless I Ching philosophy, containing natural laws and wisdom that can be woven into fashion.

The competition is open to all STU students, individually or in teams. The preliminary round will feature PPT presentations of design sketches, concepts, and explanations, judged by professional faculty. Prizes: 1st prize — 1,000 RMB plus trophy and certificate; 2nd prize — 500 RMB plus trophy and certificate; 3rd prize — 300 RMB plus trophy and certificate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
