It is reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the academic lecture "Gender and Space: Starting from the Phenomenon of Female Students Occupying Men\'s Restrooms" will be held on March 30 (Friday) from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM in the New Library Auditorium. The speaker is Assoc. Prof. Fang Lian.

The lecture will use the "female students occupying men\'s restrooms" street art action that took place in Guangzhou on February 19 and spread to Shenzhen and Beijing as a starting point. On that day, several female volunteers occupied men\'s restrooms to allow women waiting in long lines at women\'s restrooms to use the empty male stalls优先. The movement, aimed at addressing the imbalance in male-to-female restroom ratios, attracted widespread attention from media, lawyers, scholars, and the public.

The lecture will analyze gender discrimination phenomena permeating daily life, explore the relationship between gender, space, and rights from a social gender perspective, and examine how gender-based demand differences are overlooked under male-centric "fairness" standards. It will also discuss how public participation can drive changes in public policy and law to improve the allocation of public spaces and resources.
