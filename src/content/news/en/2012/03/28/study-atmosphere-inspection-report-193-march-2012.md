It is reported that the "Three Styles" Construction Office of Shantou University recently issued Study Atmosphere Inspection Report No. 193. During the 4th week of the spring semester of the 2011-2012 academic year, the study atmosphere inspection team conducted inspections of teaching buildings. The findings are reported as follows:

1. Inspection time: March 20, 2012 (Tuesday) at 10:00 AM; location: Building E; result: No violations of school discipline or regulations found.

2. Inspection time: March 21, 2012 (Wednesday) at 2:00 PM; location: Building E; result: No violations of school discipline or regulations found.

> Source: STU OA Notice ("Three Styles" Construction Office, Shantou University)
