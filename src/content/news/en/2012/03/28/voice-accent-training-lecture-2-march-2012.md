It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the second lecture in the 2012 Voice and Accent Training Lecture Series will be held on March 29 (Thursday) from 7:10 PM to 8:40 PM at the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL).

The topic is "Assertiveness," and the lecturer is Deepali Anand. All are welcome.
