It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the STU 2012 Graduate Employment Work Conference will be held in the near future.

The meeting will cover analysis of the current employment situation and job-seeking strategies for graduating students. Relevant personnel from all colleges and residential colleges are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
