It is reported that the Shantou University Research Office recently issued a notice that the State Council Overseas Chinese Affairs Office will release its 2013-2014 project guidelines this year, and is currently soliciting opinions and suggestions from across campus.

The notice requires faculty members to guide their input by the spirit of the Sixth Plenary Session of the 17th CPC Central Committee and the 2011 National Conference on Overseas Chinese Affairs Work, while taking into account the current domestic and international situation and the characteristics of overseas Chinese affairs. Suggestions should be sent to ljwang@stu.edu.cn by March 28.
