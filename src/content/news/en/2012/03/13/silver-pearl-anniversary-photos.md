It is reported that the Shantou University labor union will launch the "Beauty of Years" wedding anniversary photo event in March, creating lasting visual memories for faculty and staff who have walked side by side for decades.

## Eligible Participants

The event is open to two groups of faculty and staff:

| Commemoration | Year of Marriage | Years Married |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| Silver jubilee | Married in 1987 | 25 years |
| Pearl jubilee | Married in 1982 | 30 years |

Eligible participants will receive a free formal-attire anniversary photo session, along with one complimentary 16-inch photograph.

## Registration Details

- **Deadline**: March 20
- **How to apply**: Fill in the registration form and send it to the union's email address
- **Inquiries**: Call 82902983

According to the announcement, the photo sessions will be undertaken by V Man Visual, an outdoor wedding photography studio based in Shantou.
