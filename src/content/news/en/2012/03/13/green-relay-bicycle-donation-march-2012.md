It is reported that the Shantou University Student Affairs Office recently issued a notice launching the "Green Relay" bicycle circulation program to conserve public resources and promote recycling. Used bicycles collected campus-wide will be refurbished and made available for free lending to students in need.

Registration is open from March 14 to 20. Students wishing to donate a bicycle should send their name, student ID, dormitory number, email address, phone number, and a preferred time for doorstep pickup to 11zjli1@stu.edu.cn or call 675507. The event is organized by the Zhicheng College Student Union and the Student Self-Management Committee.
