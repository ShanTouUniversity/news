It is reported that the Shantou University Sports Department recently issued a notice that, starting today, the tennis courts and indoor badminton and basketball courts at the gymnasium are now open for booking by faculty and students, subject to prior scheduling for physical education classes and athletic team training.

Reservations must be made at least one day in advance by emailing o_tyb@stu.edu.cn. Payment is accepted only through the campus IC card or internal bank transfer. Starting this week, the indoor gymnasium venues will be free of charge for faculty and staff every Wednesday from 19:00 to 21:00 and will not be available for external booking during that period. Smoking is strictly prohibited in all sports venues.

## Venue Booking Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Open venues | Tennis courts, indoor badminton courts, indoor basketball courts |
| Eligible users | All faculty and students |
| Reservation requirement | At least one day advance notice via email |
| Payment methods | Campus IC card or internal bank transfer |
| Staff benefit | Free gym access every Wednesday 19:00–21:00 |
| Note | No smoking permitted in sports venues |
| Booking email | o_tyb@stu.edu.cn |
