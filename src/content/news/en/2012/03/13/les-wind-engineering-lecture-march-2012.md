It is reported that the Shantou University College of Engineering recently issued a notice that Dr. Liu Zhe from the Department of Civil Engineering at the University of Nottingham will deliver an academic lecture on March 14 from 10:00 to 11:00 in the College of Engineering Academic Exchange Room, titled "Large Eddy Simulation (LES) Applications in Computational Wind Engineering."

Dr. Liu's research focuses on computational wind engineering and fluid dynamics simulation. The lecture will introduce recent advances in Large Eddy Simulation methods applied to computational wind engineering. Faculty and students from the Civil Engineering Department and others interested are welcome to attend.
