It is reported that the Shantou University Organization and United Front Work Department recently issued a notice requiring leaders at or above the deputy-division level to centrally report their personal matters for 2012 in accordance with relevant regulations.

Deputy-division-level and above cadres are instructed to collect the "Leading Cadre Personal Matters Report Form" from the Organization and United Front Work Department (Room 405, Xingdong Building). Reports must be submitted as an initial filing, with truthful and detailed information; simply marking "no changes" is not acceptable. The form must be filled out by hand, sealed in a C4 envelope, signed by the applicant, and returned to the department by March 30.

## Reporting Arrangement

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible cadres | Deputy-division-level and above |
| Form collection | Organization and United Front Work Department (Room 405, Xingdong) |
| Reporting form | Initial report — must be filled out truthfully and in detail |
| Submission requirement | Hand-filled, sealed in C4 envelope, signed by applicant |
| Deadline | March 30, 2012 |
