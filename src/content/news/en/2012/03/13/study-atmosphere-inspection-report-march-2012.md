It is reported that the Shantou University "Three Styles" Construction Office recently issued its 191st bulletin on study atmosphere inspection. On the mornings of March 6 (Tuesday) and March 9 (Friday) 2012 at 8:00, inspection team members conducted checks at E Building.

The results showed that on March 6, six students arrived late (within 5 minutes) and five students arrived late (beyond 5 minutes). On March 9, ten students arrived late (within 5 minutes) and four students arrived late (beyond 5 minutes).

The bulletin called on relevant colleges (residential colleges) to critique and educate students who failed to abide by school discipline and rules, in order to further foster a positive study environment.
