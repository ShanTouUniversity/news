It is reported that the Shantou University Academic Affairs Office recently issued a notice that, in order to implement the "advanced undergraduate education" goal outlined in the *Shantou University 2009–2017 Development Plan Outline*, the university will conduct a revision of the undergraduate curriculum plan for the 2012 cohort.

The curriculum plan serves as the guiding document for undergraduate talent cultivation. This revision will be carried out in conjunction with teaching reform research and on the basis of scientific inquiry. Each teaching unit is required to revise the curriculum plan by major, completing the first draft by the end of May 2012, and to determine the syllabus for each course based on the new curriculum plan.

## Revision Arrangement

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Basis | "Advanced undergraduate education" goal, *STU 2009–2017 Development Plan Outline* |
| Principle | Integrated with teaching reform research, grounded in scientific inquiry |
| Responsible units | All teaching units (organized by major) |
| Draft deadline | End of May 2012 |
| Follow-up | Determine course syllabi based on the new curriculum plan |
