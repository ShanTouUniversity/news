It is reported that the College of Science of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that HORIBA Scientific, one of the world's leading manufacturers of Raman spectrometers (Jobin Yvon Spectroscopy Technology), will host a symposium on the latest advances and applications of Raman spectroscopy on March 22 at 8:30 AM in the College of Science Lecture Hall (Science-352).

HORIBA Scientific is a globally renowned enterprise in spectroscopy technology, with its Jobin Yvon spectral technology enjoying a distinguished reputation in the scientific research community. The symposium will introduce the latest research developments in Raman spectroscopy and its wide-ranging applications across various disciplines.

Registration for the event must be completed online by March 19. The College of Science encourages all faculty and students to participate actively in this academic exchange.
