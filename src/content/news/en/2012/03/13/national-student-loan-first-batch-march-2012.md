It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice stating that, per notification from the Bank of China Shantou Branch, a total of 657 students will receive the first batch of national student loans for the 2011-2012 academic year.

The notice reminds students who have received national student loans to log into the student payment information query system to check their personal payment status and promptly pay any outstanding balances at the Finance Office. For students without plans for early repayment, loan repayment agreements will be signed with the bank prior to graduation, including repayment confirmation procedures and a structured repayment plan.

The notice specifically pointed out that any current students who received SMS reminders requiring loan repayment starting from March 1 should be aware that these messages were sent in error by the Bank of China. Students are encouraged to share this information with their peers to avoid confusion.
