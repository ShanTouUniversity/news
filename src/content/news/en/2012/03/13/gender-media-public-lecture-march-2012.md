It is reported that the Women Research Center of the Shantou University College of Liberal Arts recently issued a notice that Professor Feng Yuan will deliver a public lecture on March 14 from 19:30 to 21:30 in the New Library Auditorium, titled "Gender and Media: Starting from the Coverage of the Li Yang Domestic Violence Case."

Professor Feng Yuan is the director of China's Anti-Domestic Violence Network, a council member of the Chinese Women's Research Association, and a visiting professor at the Women Research Center of STU's College of Liberal Arts. She has long been engaged in action, training, and research on women, gender, and development. The lecture will use media coverage of the Li Yang domestic violence incident as a starting point to analyze the triangular relationship between journalistic professionalism, market orientation, and gender awareness, and to explore ways to improve the standard of reporting on women's rights issues.
