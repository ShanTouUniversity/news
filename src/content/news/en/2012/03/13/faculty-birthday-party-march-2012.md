It is reported that the Shantou University Labor Union recently issued a notice that, to strengthen faculty cohesion and promote a harmonious campus community, the Union will host a birthday celebration for faculty members born in March and April on Saturday, March 24, at 18:30 in the Western Restaurant of the Junhua Hotel, with generous support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation.

Reservation opens at 12:00 on March 13 and closes at 24:00 on March 18. Fee deduction will be processed from March 19 to 21. Tickets can be collected on March 23 from 10:00 to 12:00. Faculty and their family members may attend with tickets; children must be accompanied by at least one parent.
