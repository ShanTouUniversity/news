It is reported that the Shantou University Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office jointly issued a notice stating that as of March 13, some dormitories still have unpaid utility bills for February 2012 and earlier periods. In accordance with university regulations, power will be cut to those dormitories starting March 14 and restored once all outstanding fees are settled.

## Deduction and Restoration Schedule

| Date | Deduction Times | Power Restoration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| March 14 | 1st: 10:30; 2nd: 16:30 | Paid dorms restore power 14:30–16:30 (after 1st deduction) or 17:30–20:00 (after 2nd deduction) |
| March 15 | Same as 14th | Same as above |
| March 16 | Same as 14th | Same as above |
| After 16th | Once daily at 16:30 | Dorms paid after 16:30 restore power the following day 14:30–16:30 |

Students with insufficient IC card balance should top up promptly at the campus card machines.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office)
