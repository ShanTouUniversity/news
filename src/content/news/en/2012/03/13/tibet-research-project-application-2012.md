It is reported that the Shantou University Research Office recently issued a notice that applications for the 2012 National Social Science Fund special commission project "Comprehensive Research on Tibet History and Contemporary Conditions" are now open. The project carries an average funding of 100,000 RMB. Applicants may select a topic within the scope of the project guidelines or propose their own title.

Applicants whose National Social Science Fund projects have not been concluded, or whose previously concluded projects are identical to the proposed topic, are ineligible to apply. Guidelines must be obtained and preliminary application forms submitted for review at the Research Office by March 22, with qualified materials (five copies plus electronic version) due by March 26 to ljwang@stu.edu.cn.
