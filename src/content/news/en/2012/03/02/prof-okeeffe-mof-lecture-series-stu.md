It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently announced a lecture series featuring Prof. Michael O'Keeffe, Regents' Professor of Chemistry at Arizona State University, as part of its "Science Salon" program.

Prof. O'Keeffe is a world-renowned crystallographer famous for his research on the topological structures of metal-organic frameworks (MOFs).
