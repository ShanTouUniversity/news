It is reported that the "211 Project" Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing a meeting on the 9th round of Guangdong Province Key Discipline applications.

The meeting will be held on Tuesday, March 6, 2012, at 3:30 PM in Room 205 of the Science Building. Attendees include discipline heads of applying units, the main document preparer from each unit (1 person), and the leadership in charge of discipline construction at the Medical College.
