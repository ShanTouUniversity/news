It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee recently issued a notice announcing the 68th session of the Party Basic Knowledge Training Class, scheduled to begin in March 2012.

Each unit must conduct preliminary review and submit nominations. Eligible candidates must have submitted a Party membership application more than one year ago and be listed as key cultivation targets. The application form, signed by the Party general branch or directly affiliated branch and stamped with the official seal, must be submitted to the Party School Office (Room 401, Xingdong Building) by March 15, 2012. The class roster summary should also be emailed to o_dxb@stu.edu.cn. Admission notices will be issued after the Party School Office completes its review.
