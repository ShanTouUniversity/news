It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently announced the opening of applications for the 6th Ministry of Education Higher Education Scientific Research Excellence Award (Humanities and Social Sciences).

The award covers 24 disciplines, including Marxism/ideological and political education, philosophy, logic, religious studies, linguistics, Chinese and foreign literature, art, history, archaeology, economics, management, political science, law, sociology, ethnic and cultural studies, journalism and communication, library and information science, education, psychology, statistics, Hong Kong-Macau-Taiwan studies, international studies, and interdisciplinary studies.

Award categories include monographs, papers, research reports, and popularization achievements, with levels of special prize, first prize, second prize, and third prize. A total of approximately 800 awards will be granted, with allowances for vacancies in any category.

Eligibility: Faculty and researchers whose employment records are with STU (including retired personnel). For collaborative research, only the first-listed author may apply, with no more than 5 participants. Each person may submit only one application.
