It is reported that the Affiliated Middle School of Shantou University recently announced the recruitment of 6 instructors for the Leadership Adventure Program (LAP), aimed at guiding junior high school students.

The LAP program begins in March 2012. Instructors are required to participate in training sessions on Wednesdays (March 14, 21, and 28) and lead activities from March 31 to June 16, every Saturday, 3:00–5:00 PM.

Requirements:
1. Current undergraduate student at Shantou University;
2. Good team communication and collaboration skills;
3. Strong management and leadership abilities;
4. Enjoy working with children and adolescents;
5. Good physical condition;
6. Appreciate physical exercise and outdoor activities.

Application process: Interested students should fill out the attached form and send it to 10qhpeng@stu.edu.cn by 5:00 PM on March 10. A shortlist of 20 candidates will be emailed on March 13, and candidates must attend the first-round training on March 14. Ultimately, 6 instructors will be selected and compensated according to the university's work-study standards.
