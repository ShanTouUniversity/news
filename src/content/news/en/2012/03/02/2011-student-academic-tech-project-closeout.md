It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice deploying the close-out work for the 2011 Student Extracurricular Academic Technology Projects.

Since the program began in 2011, 181 projects have been approved and funded across all units, with 154 projects passing the mid-term review.

The Youth League Committee required the following tasks:

1. All project leaders must complete wrap-up work according to their college's requirements, submit summary reports on time, and have the college compile project summary tables for university filing;
2. Each college must complete final reviews and submit results to the Youth League Committee by March 16;
3. Each college should process expense reimbursements promptly, completing all reimbursement by April 1, 2012;
4. Each college Youth League committee must prepare detailed expense reports and benefit assessment reports, and submit the 2011 annual summary of student academic technology projects to the Youth League Committee office by April 5, 2012.
