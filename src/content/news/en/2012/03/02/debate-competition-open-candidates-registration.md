It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently announced that the 16th STU University Debate Competition will accept open-registration teams, broadening participation opportunities.

Open-registration teams will compete alongside college-delegation teams. The requirements are as follows:

- No restriction on major or year; each team must have at least 4 members
- Registration deadline: March 11, 2012
- How to register: Send member names, team leader info, phone, email, major and year to 11ljhuang1@stu.edu.cn, or via the STU Academic Planning Department's Sina Weibo

Rewards: Each open-registration team will receive a精美的礼品 and an opportunity to meet Mr. Huang Zhizhong (a renowned Chinese debater and coach).
