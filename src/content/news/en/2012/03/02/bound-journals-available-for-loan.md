Shantou University Library has adjusted its borrowing policy: bound journal volumes, which were previously restricted to in-library reading, can now be taken out by readers with immediate effect.

According to the library, these bound volumes are housed in the foreign periodicals area on the first floor. Under the new arrangement, loan periods and quantity limits for bound journals are identical to those for regular books, and no additional procedures are required.

The change means faculty and students no longer need to consult back issues within opening hours alone — they may now take the volumes they need out of the library. Details of the borrowing rules are available at the first-floor periodicals area or from library staff.
