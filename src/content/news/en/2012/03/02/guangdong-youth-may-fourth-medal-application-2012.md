It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Guangdong Provincial Federation of Youth Associations will award the 14th Guangdong Youth May Fourth Medal (individual and collective categories) during the 2012 May Day period.

The Guangdong Youth May Fourth Medal is the highest honor bestowed by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Guangdong Provincial Federation of Youth Associations upon outstanding young people and youth collectives in the province. The award includes both individual and collective categories.

This year, the application process adopts a self-nomination method. Qualified outstanding individuals or collectives are encouraged to apply according to the notice requirements.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Award | 14th Guangdong Youth May Fourth Medal |
| Categories | Individual Award, Collective Award |
| Application Method | Self-nomination |
| Award Ceremony | May Day period, 2012 |
| Issuing Office | Youth League Committee, STU |
