It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently announced that Founder Group Zhuhai PCB Research Institute will hold a dedicated recruitment talk for STU graduate students on Wednesday, March 7, 2012, from 2:00 to 5:30 PM in Room G301.

In addition to signal-related majors mentioned in the recruitment brochure, the institute is also seeking graduate students in materials, chemistry, and electronics. Students from relevant majors or those interested in the industry are encouraged to attend. Resumes will be accepted on-site, followed by interviews and hiring.
