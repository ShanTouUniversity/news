It is reported that the Shantou University Chorus recently announced its spring 2012 recruitment, open to all students on campus.

Eligibility: All current students; no special musical background or instrumental requirement needed — only a passion for singing.

Audition Dates: Saturday, March 10, 2012, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM and 2:00–4:00 PM.

Audition Venue: Music Rehearsal Hall 101, Arts Education Center Building.

Audition Content: Pitch-matching exercises or self-selected song excerpts (any genre accepted); students with instrumental talents may bring their instruments for live performance.

Registration:
- Option 1: Download the application form from the attachment and email it to 10jrliu@stu.edu.cn by 12:00 PM on March 9.
- Option 2: Register in person at the audition venue by 4:00 PM on March 10.

Option 1 is recommended to reduce waiting time.
