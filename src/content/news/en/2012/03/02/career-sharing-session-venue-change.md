It is reported that the 9th Career Planning Sharing Session, jointly organized by Chizheng College and the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University, has been moved due to exceeding the capacity of Lecture Hall 205.

The venue has been changed to E-Hall 201; the time remains unchanged: 10:00 AM–11:30 AM on March 3, 2012. Registered attendees are asked to arrive 10 minutes early.

|------|---------|
| Event | 9th Career Planning Sharing Session |
| New Venue | E-Hall 201 |
| Time | March 3, 2012, 10:00–11:30 AM |
| Organizers | College of Liberal Arts, Chizheng College |
