It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing the results of the 2011 Outstanding Graduate Student awards.

In accordance with the "Shantou University Outstanding Graduate Student Evaluation Measures," after preliminary recommendations from each graduate training unit and review by an expert panel appointed by the Graduate School, the following results were determined:

- First Prize: Qiao Jie and 6 other students
- Second Prize: Guo Rongfang and 41 other students

The results are published for public comment. Anyone with objections may submit written comments or contact the Graduate School in person or by phone before 5:00 PM on March 7, 2012. (Individual complaints must include the complainant's real name.) Contact: 82904000.
