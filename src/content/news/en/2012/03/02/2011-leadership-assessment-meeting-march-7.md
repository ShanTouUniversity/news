It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee recently announced the 2011 annual assessment evaluation meeting for the university leadership team.

The meeting will be held on Wednesday, March 7, 2012, at 3:00 PM in the Science and Technology Center Auditorium. Attendees include university leadership, middle-level and above cadres, two senior faculty representatives and one each of Party congress and faculty congress representatives from each college, leaders of democratic parties, 10 retired faculty representatives, the presidents of the Student Union and Graduate Student Union, and relevant leaders from the Medical College and affiliated hospitals.

Each unit must submit its meeting attendee list to the Organization and United Front Work Department (o_zzb@stu.edu.cn) by close of business on March 5, and notify attendees to arrive 15 minutes early.

To enhance transparency, leadership work reports are publicly displayed on the university's internal information website. Suggestions and comments regarding the leadership team should be sent to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn by March 8.
