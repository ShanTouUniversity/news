It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced the opening of formal registration for the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Expansion Program.

Student teams (including those who previously submitted pre-registration forms) must complete a project plan and send it as an attachment to liran@stu.edu.cn. The registration deadline is 5:00 PM on March 16, 2012. Email and attachment subjects must include: "2012 STU Advanced Outdoor Expansion Program Plan (Project Name)."

Faculty members must print and fill out the faculty registration form, obtain approval and signature from their college or department head, and submit it to Room 205, Student Affairs Office.

To help faculty and students better understand the program, an information session will be held on the evening of March 4 at 7:00 PM in Room 726, 2nd Floor, Building 789. Attendance is limited to team project leaders due to space constraints.
