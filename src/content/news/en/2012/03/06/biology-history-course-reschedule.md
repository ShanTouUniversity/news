It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice regarding a schedule change for the course "History of Biology and Secrets of Life" (Course Code: 51729).

Due to Professor Huang Bingxin's participation in the university's International Women's Day faculty event, the class originally scheduled for Wednesday of Week 2, periods AB, in Room E203 has been moved to periods BC. The venue remains unchanged.

Students enrolled in this course are advised to note the change.

|------|---------|

| Venue | Room E203 (unchanged) |
| Issuing Office | Academic Affairs Office |
