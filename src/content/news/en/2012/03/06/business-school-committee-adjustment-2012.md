It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice adjusting its Faculty Affairs Committee membership due to personnel changes.

Qin Zhong, newly appointed Director of the Economics and Trade Teaching and Research Section, has been added to the committee. Yao Hongxin will no longer serve on the committee.

The adjusted committee members are:
Chair: Xu Zongling
Members: Ouyang Feng, Huang Yan, Chen Yinglin, Li Yue, Du Mian, Wang Ti, Bu Xiangzhi, Liu Wei, Kong Ying, Zhou Xiao'e, Qin Zhong
Secretary: Chen Mingqin
