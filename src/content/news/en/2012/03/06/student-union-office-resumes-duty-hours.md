It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently announced the resumption of regular duty hours at the Student Union office.

Effective Wednesday, March 7, 2012, the office will be staffed Monday through Friday, 4:15–5:15 PM.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Resumption Date | Wednesday, March 7, 2012 |
| Duty Hours | Monday–Friday, 16:15–17:15 |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Student Union |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, STU)
