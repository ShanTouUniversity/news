It is reported that some arts courses of the Art Education Center for the spring semester of the 2011-2012 academic year still have places available. On March 6, the center released enrollment information to all students and welcomed interested students to register before 12:00 noon on March 13.

| Flute Performance | 52468 | Lin Weiliang | 2 | Tuesday, periods 89 |
| Clarinet Performance | 52849 | Liu Guanghui | 2 | Wednesday, periods AB |
| Bass Brass Performance (Advanced) | 53659 | Lin Weijie | 2 | Sunday, periods AB |
| Woodwind Performance (Advanced) | 52469 | Lin Weiliang | 2 | Tuesday, periods AB |
| Woodwind Performance (Advanced) | 52731 | Xiao Hui | 2 | Monday, periods 34 |
| Bassoon Performance | 52846 | Liu Guanghui | 2 | Monday, periods 34 |
| Chorus and Conducting | 52448 | Liao Wenlan | 2 | Wednesday, periods 34 |

## How to Enroll

- Send your name and student ID by email to o_ysjyzx@stu.edu.cn, stating the class number and course name;
- Or register in person at the Art Teaching Office, Room 309, Art Education Center.

For inquiries, call 82903775 or email o_ysjyzx@stu.edu.cn.
