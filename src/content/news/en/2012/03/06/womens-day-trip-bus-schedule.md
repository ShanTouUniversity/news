It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice with the bus schedule and important reminders for the March 7 Women's Day outing to Danspring Eco Park and the seaside greenway.

Boarding: 1:40 PM on Wednesday, March 7, at the University Motto Stone (no other pickup points will be available).

Reminders:
1. Board according to your assigned bus number; return on the same bus at the scheduled time
2. Wear sports shoes or flat shoes; keep valuables secure
3. Pay attention to safety when taking photos; avoid dangerous areas
4. Do not wander off alone; choose walking routes according to your physical condition
5. Due to the large number of participants, please do not bring children
6. Dinner will be held at a Chaozhou restaurant in the city center (Danspring Eco Park has limited capacity)

|------|---------|
