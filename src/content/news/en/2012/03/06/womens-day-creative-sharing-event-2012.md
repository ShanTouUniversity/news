It is reported that the Women's Research Center and the International Research Center at the College of Liberal Arts, Shantou University, are jointly hosting a creative sharing event titled "The Woman I Admire Most — 2012 International Women's Day Creative Showcase, Sharing, and Discussion" on the evening of March 8 at 7:30 PM in the Science Lecture Hall.

The event encourages students to express their admiration for a woman through creative works, viewed from a youthful perspective. Twelve works will be presented in various formats, including video clips, PDFs or PPTs, personal items, and musical or theatrical performances.

Experts in gender studies will interact with participants and provide interpretations of the creative works, aiming to deepen students' understanding and appreciation of diverse female experiences.

The first 100 attendees will receive a complimentary bouquet of flowers.
