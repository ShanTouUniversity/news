It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced the opening of registration for the first-half 2012 National College English Test (CET-4 and CET-6), in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Examination Authority.

Exam schedule:
- CET-4: Saturday, June 16, 2012, 9:00 AM–11:20 AM
- CET-6: Saturday, June 16, 2012, 3:00–5:20 PM

Eligibility:
CET-4: Undergraduate and graduate students with English placement at Level 2 or above; art majors who have completed ELC EA3 or passed the English Application Ability Test; 2010-cohort English majors.

CET-6: Students who have passed CET-4 or scored 425 or above; students who have passed the English Major Band 4 test; graduate and undergraduate students who have previously passed CET-6 or scored 425 or above (undergraduates must have completed ELC 4).
