It is reported that the Publicity Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee, along with the Youth League Committee, Discipline Inspection Office, and Student Affairs Office, jointly announced that two works from STU received honors at the Guangdong provincial anti-corruption art program competition.

The competition, organized by the Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee, Provincial Department of Education, and Provincial Education Discipline Inspection Committee, ran from May to October last year under the theme "Bright Red Flag — Celebrating the 90th Anniversary of the Founding of the CPC."

Results:
- Skit "Running for Office" (Performers: Hong Weihe, Fang Bingran, Zheng Shiyue, Meng Xian) — Second Prize
- Tea Art Performance "Kung Fu Tea — Cultivating Integrity Through Tea" (Performers: Feng Bingzhi, Chen Yinbin, Zheng Shaoshan, Yuan Yuan) — Excellence Award

The university congratulated the student performers and encouraged continued participation in cultural activities to showcase the spirit of STU students.
