It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee recently issued a public notice proposing Du Shimin for appointment as Deputy Secretary of the Party Committee (presiding over committee work) and Head of Student Affairs at the Changjiang College of Journalism and Communication.

Du Shimin's background: Female, born December 1974, native of Shantou, Guangdong. Holds a master's degree in literature. Currently a lecturer and economist. Joined the CPC in December 1995 and began working in July 1998. She studied Chinese at the College of Liberal Arts, STU (September 1994 – June 1998), served as political counselor, Youth League secretary, and Party branch secretary at the College of Liberal Arts (July 1998 – November 2003), and has been working in the STU Party and Government Office since December 2003, serving as deputy section chief (December 2003) and section chief (May 2006) of the Party Affairs Section.

Public notice period: March 6–13, 2012. Any concerns should be submitted in writing or by phone to the Organization and United Front Work Department during this period (organization submissions require an official seal; individual submissions require a real name).
