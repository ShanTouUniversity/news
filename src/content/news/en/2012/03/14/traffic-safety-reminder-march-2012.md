It is reported that the Resources Management Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice reminding all faculty and students to pay attention to traffic safety at the university gate.

Previously, the municipal traffic police department installed traffic signal lights at the intersection in front of the main gate. After a period of observation, certain safety hazards remain at this intersection, primarily due to some through-traffic drivers on Daxue Road not obeying the traffic signals. In particular, cars traveling from Jieyang toward Shantou and motorcycles traveling in both directions have been observed running red lights at high speed.

The university has reported these issues to the municipal traffic police department and plans to strengthen management and improve safety facilities. The Resources Management Office urges all faculty and students to obey traffic signals while also carefully observing the movement of oncoming vehicles when passing through the intersection, to ensure personal safety.
