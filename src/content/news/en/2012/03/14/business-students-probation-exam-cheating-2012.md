It is reported that the Shantou University Student Affairs Office recently issued its 2012 No. 2 bulletin on student disciplinary violations. Two students from the 2008 cohort of Business Administration at the Business School, Ma M and Wang WW, were found to have hired substitutes to take the "Computer Application Skills" course exam on their behalf on January 7.

In light of the fact that both students voluntarily confessed their wrongdoing to the invigilator near the end of the exam, the university decided to place Ma M and Wang WW on disciplinary probation for one year, and the grade for the compromised course was recorded as zero.

| --- | --- |
