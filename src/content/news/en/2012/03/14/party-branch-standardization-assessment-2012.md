It is reported that the Shantou University Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the university Party committee's work schedule, the 2011 grassroots Party branch standardization assessment will be carried out.

Each general Party branch and directly affiliated Party branch is required to carefully organize its subordinate branches to conduct self-assessment and self-evaluation according to the *Shantou University Grassroots Party Branch Construction Standardization Criteria (Trial)*. Assessment forms, materials from participating branches, and assessment summaries must be submitted to the Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department (Xingdong 405) by April 13. Contact: Tel. 82902345, Email: o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.

## Assessment Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
