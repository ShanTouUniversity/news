It is reported that the Central Laboratory at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Chen Tao, Application Engineer for Gene Analyzers in Southern China at ABI, will deliver an academic lecture on "Principles, Applications, and Current Status of Gene Sequencing Technology" at 4:00 PM on March 15 in Conference Room 205 of the Science Building.

The lecture will focus on the applications of gene analysis instruments in the biological field, covering techniques such as sequencing, DNA methylation detection, fragment analysis, STR detection, and SNP analysis, as well as their broad applications in genetic breeding, phylogenetics, diversity analysis, and variety quality identification.

Chen Tao stated that gene sequencing technology is one of the core tools in contemporary biological research, with rapid development in recent years, and has played important roles in medical diagnostics, forensic science, agricultural breeding, and many other fields. He expressed hope that through this lecture, more STU students would gain insight into the latest developments in this area.
