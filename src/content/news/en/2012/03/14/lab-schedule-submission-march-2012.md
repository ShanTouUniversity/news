It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice requiring all teaching units to organize undergraduate teaching laboratories to complete the submission of laboratory schedules for the spring semester of the 2011-2012 academic year.

According to the notice, all teaching units must submit their schedules by March 20. The process requires first submitting an electronic document to yzchen@stu.edu.cn, followed by two printed copies to Room 406, Wendumeng, Academic Affairs Office. The Office emphasized that laboratory schedules are a crucial foundation for ensuring the orderly conduct of experimental teaching, and all units must treat this task seriously and submit on time.
