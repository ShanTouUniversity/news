It is reported that the College of Arts at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the English Theater Society will hold its first meeting of the spring 2012 season on Saturday, March 17 at 10:00 AM at CILL, recruiting new members from across the university.

The Society is actively seeking screenwriters, actors, directors, designers, and various volunteers to participate in the creation and rehearsal of short plays in preparation for next semester's performances. The Society emphasized that no prior drama experience is necessary, and welcomed students interested in drama and the English language to join.

The English Theater Society aims to provide a platform for all students to improve their English communication skills and stage performance abilities through theatrical practice, and looks forward to welcoming new members.
