It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following a faculty council meeting on March 7, the members of the college's Teaching Committee have been adjusted.

The new Teaching Committee is chaired by Fan Dongsheng, with Bai Jing serving as convener. Other members include Song Jihong, Wang Zongan, Cheng Jinfu, Xie Lin, Hu Lufeng, and Fan Linjun. Lin Yan serves as secretary.
