It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently issued a notice that the Dacheng Historic Journal Full-Text Database has commenced a trial period effective immediately, running through May 30, 2012.

The database contains over 6,800 periodicals published in China during the nearly 80 years from the late Qing dynasty to 1949, comprising more than 130,000 issues and over 1.5 million articles. Items are classified according to the Chinese Library Classification periodical taxonomy, covering 22 major categories including philosophy and economics, politics and military affairs, industry and agriculture, transportation, arts and sciences, history and geography, astronomy, and medicine. All periodicals are high-definition scans of original copies, holding significant value for historical, scientific, and literary research.

Access link: http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=345 (please open in a new window using Internet Explorer). For usage evaluations and acquisition recommendations, please contact the Library Subject Services Department at ext. 3247, or via email at lqxie@stu.edu.cn or yxu@stu.edu.cn.
