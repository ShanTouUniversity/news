It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing the reorganization of the Leadership Group for Student Extracurricular Academic and Technology Activities and the departmental working groups.

The newly adjusted Leadership Group is headed by Lou Zengjian, with members including Liu Yuzhou, Li Yuanyou, Huang Xiaochun, Yang Shouzhi, Li Shaohui, Tong Qingxiao, Zhang Yueling, Zhang Liting, and Lin Yu. The heads of the departmental working groups are: Ye Ruisong (Mathematics), Wang Jiangyong (Physics), Zhang Xin (Chemistry), and Chen Meizhen (Biology).

This reorganization aims to further improve the organizational management mechanism for student extracurricular academic and technology activities at the college, promoting the enhancement of students' innovative and practical capabilities.
