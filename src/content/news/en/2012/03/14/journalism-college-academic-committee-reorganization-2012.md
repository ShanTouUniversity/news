It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following a faculty council meeting on March 7, the members of the college's Academic Committee have been adjusted.

The new Academic Committee is chaired by Fan Dongsheng, with Cheng Jinfu serving as convener. Other members include Wei Yongzheng, Chang Weimin, Chen Yuefen, Yang Aili, and Bai Jing. Li Mingran serves as secretary.
