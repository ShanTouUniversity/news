It is reported that the Shantou University Student Affairs Office recently issued a notice that the Guangdong Provincial Committee for Higher Education Student Work has organized the selection activities for the 3rd Guangdong Higher Education Student Work "Kapok Award" and the 2011 Guangdong Higher Education Student Work Advanced Individual honors. After evaluation, Teacher Chen Yiyuan was awarded the 3rd Guangdong Higher Education Student Work "Kapok Award," and Teacher Lin Ling was named a 2011 Guangdong Higher Education Student Work Advanced Individual.

## Award Recipients

| Name | Award |
| --- | --- |
| Chen Yiyuan | 3rd Guangdong Higher Education Student Work "Kapok Award" |
| Lin Ling | 2011 Guangdong Higher Education Student Work Advanced Individual |
