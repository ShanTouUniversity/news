It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to personnel changes and following a faculty council meeting on March 7, the members of the college's Dean's Council have been adjusted.

The new Dean's Council is chaired by Fan Dongsheng, with members including Bai Jing, Chang Weimin, Cheng Jinfu, and Du Shimin. Li Jing serves as secretary.

## Dean's Council Composition

| Position | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Chairman | Fan Dongsheng |
| Member | Bai Jing |
| Member | Chang Weimin |
| Member | Cheng Jinfu |
| Member | Du Shimin |
| Secretary | Li Jing |
