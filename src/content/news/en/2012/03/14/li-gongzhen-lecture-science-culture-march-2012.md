It is reported that the Shantou University Party Committee Propaganda Department and the Student Affairs Office recently issued a joint notice that Professor Li Gongzhen of the School of History at Wuhan University, a doctoral supervisor, will deliver an academic lecture titled "The Migration of World Scientific and Cultural Centers" on the evening of March 19 (Monday) at 20:00 in the New Library Auditorium.

Professor Li Gongzhen was born in December 1952 in Wuhan and is originally from Fengshun, Guangdong. His main research areas are German historical culture and modernization issues. His representative works include *The German Path: A Study of the Modernization Process*, *Cultural Exile: Research on European Intellectual Refugees during the Nazi Era*, and *Thirteen Lectures on Modern German History: From the Weimar Republic to the Third Reich*. He has published over 40 related papers in important academic journals such as *Historical Research* and *World History*.

Registration: From now until 17:00 on March 16, log in to http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ to reserve a spot. Faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend.
