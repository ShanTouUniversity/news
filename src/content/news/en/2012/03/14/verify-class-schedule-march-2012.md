It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice requiring all colleges to conduct a comprehensive verification of the class schedules for the spring semester of the 2011-2012 academic year, ensuring that teaching arrangements are accurate and error-free.

The schedule verification covers all majors and classes across the university, aimed at safeguarding the smooth operation of teaching activities at the start of the new semester. The Academic Affairs Office reminded all colleges to attach great importance to this task and promptly confirm all details in the course arrangements, addressing any issues with the Office as soon as they are discovered.
