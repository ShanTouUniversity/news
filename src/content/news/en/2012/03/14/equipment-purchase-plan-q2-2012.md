It is reported that the Shantou University Resources Management Office recently issued a notice that the second quarter 2012 equipment procurement plan is now open for submission. All units are required to submit both hard copy and electronic documents to the Equipment and Materials Management Office (former reception room) by April 6, 2012.

Late submissions will be deferred to the third quarter of 2012. Units should note the following requirements when filling out plans: equipment under RMB 20,000 requires only the "Plan Form"; equipment over RMB 20,000 requires both the "Plan Form" and "Plan Proposal," submitted according to approval authority; equipment with a unit price over RMB 100,000 requires a "Feasibility Report for Large Equipment Purchase"; computer purchases require the "Application Form for Computer Purchase Registration"; air conditioner purchases must indicate the installation location and the condition of existing units in the remarks column.

Contact: Mr./Ms. Huang, Tel. 82902558, Email: zxhuang@stu.edu.cn.

## Submission Guidelines

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | April 6, 2012 |
| Submission format | Hard copy and electronic document |
| Equipment under RMB 20,000 | "Plan Form" only |
| Equipment over RMB 20,000 | "Plan Form" and "Plan Proposal," per approval authority |
| Unit price over RMB 100,000 | "Feasibility Report for Large Equipment Purchase" required |
| Computer purchases | "Application Form for Computer Purchase Registration" required |
| Air conditioner purchases | Indicate installation location and existing AC condition in remarks |
