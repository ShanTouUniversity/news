It is reported that the Committee of the Communist Party of China at Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing personnel appointments. Following the decision of the second leadership meeting and the Standing Committee of the University Party Committee in 2012, Comrade Du Shimin has been appointed as Director of Student Affairs and Deputy Secretary of the Party General Branch of the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University (in charge of the Party General Branch work), with a probationary period of six months. Comrade Chen Yuefen will no longer serve as Secretary of the Party General Branch of the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication.

This personnel adjustment is an important measure undertaken by Shantou University to optimize the organizational structure of grassroots Party building in higher education institutions, aiming to strengthen Party building work and student affairs management at the college level.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Committee of the Communist Party of China at Shantou University)
