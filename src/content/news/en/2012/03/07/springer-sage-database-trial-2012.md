It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently announced trial access to the Springer e-books database and SAGE Premier journal database from March 7 to May 15, 2012.

**Springer E-Books**: Through the SpringerLink system, users can access nearly 8,000 English e-books published by Springer in 2010–2011, covering life sciences, medicine, agriculture, botany, chemistry and materials, mathematics and statistics, resources and environment, computer science, physics, economics, business and management, psychology, humanities and social sciences, law, education, and philosophy, including renowned series such as Lecture Notes in Computer Science, Lecture Notes in Mathematics, Lecture Notes in Physics, and Lecture Notes in Earth Science.

**SAGE Premier Journal Database**: Founded in 1965 in the United States, SAGE Publishing has branches across North America, Europe, South Asia, and the Asia-Pacific region. It partners with over 250 associations worldwide.

Access link: http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=343 (please use Internet Explorer and open the link in a new window).
