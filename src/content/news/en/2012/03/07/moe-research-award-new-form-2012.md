It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing the adoption of a new application form for the 6th Ministry of Education Higher Education Research Excellence Award (Humanities and Social Sciences), to standardize data submission and processing.

Faculty members intending to apply should download and complete the new application form. Other requirements published by the Research Office on March 2 via the OA system remain unchanged.

The new application form is a self-extracting executable file. After downloading, double-click to extract, then open the Word file (MOEAward2012.doc) in the extracted directory and follow the instructions to complete the form. Ensure the Word file and supporting program files (const.dat and support.dll) remain in the same directory throughout the process. Upon completion, protect the Word file; the program will automatically generate a file version number in the footer. Finally, print and submit.

Note: The version number on the submitted paper form must match the electronic version. Only the Word file needs to be submitted electronically; supporting files are not required.

Contact: Huang Yumei or Wang Liju, Tel: 82902563.
