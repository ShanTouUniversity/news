It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice arranging academic tutoring sessions for students throughout the semester.

To help students resolve difficulties in their daily studies and exam preparation, and to strengthen interaction between academic advisors and students, each department has arranged tutoring hours for students to consult with their advisors. Please refer to the attachment for detailed schedules by department.

Students are encouraged to prepare in advance and make the most of these opportunities to communicate with faculty.
