It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University has announced arrangements for graduate students to register for the first-half 2012 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 (CET-4/CET-6), with online registration being piloted starting this year.

Graduate students wishing to take the examinations may log in to the Graduate School website (http://www.gs.stu.edu.cn/) between March 8 and 14, click "Graduate Management System," select "Unified Authentication" in the pop-up window, enter their credentials, and then choose "Exam Registration — I Want to Register" to complete the process. The Student Affairs Office of the Graduate School will compile and review all registrations before the end of the workday on March 16, and submit the stamped rosters and electronic data to the Academic Affairs Division of the Academic Affairs Office.

Eligibility requirements, payment methods, and other notes are detailed in the notice titled "Notice on the Registration for the First-Half 2012 National CET-4/CET-6 in Guangdong Province" issued by the Academic Affairs Office on the campus OA system.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Examination | First-half 2012 National CET-4 and CET-6 |
| Eligible Participants | All graduate students |
| Registration Period | March 8–14, 2012 |
| Registration Method | Online via the Graduate Management System |
| Compilation Deadline | Before end of workday, March 16 |
| Issuing Unit | Graduate School |
