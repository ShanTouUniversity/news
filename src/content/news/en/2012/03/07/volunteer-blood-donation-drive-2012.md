It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently announced a voluntary blood donation event organized by the STU Youth Volunteer Association, aiming to address the critical blood supply shortage in Shantou and to enhance college students' sense of social responsibility.

Event details:
Date: Saturday, March 10, 2012, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM
Location: In front of the Second Cafeteria (opposite the supermarket)

Eligibility requirements:
1. Age between 18 and 55 years
2. Minimum weight: 50 kg for males, 45 kg for females
3. Good health with no infectious diseases; no cold, fever, or medication within one week before donation
4. Adequate rest the night before; eat normally on the day of donation — do not donate on an empty stomach
5. Bring ID card and blood donation certificate (first-time donors need not bring a certificate)
6. Those who have donated blood within the past six months prior to March 10, 2012 are not eligible to participate
