It is reported that the "Science Salon" lecture series of the College of Science at Shantou University recently announced an upcoming talk by Professor Chen Changjin from the Department of Physics.

Title: Theoretical Research on Laser-Induced Recollision Processes

Date & Time: Friday, March 9, 2012, 10:10–11:00 AM

Venue: College of Science Lecture Hall (Room Li-352)

Abstract: The interaction of lasers with matter is one of the important frontier topics in atomic and molecular physics. The behavior of atoms and molecules in strong laser fields can be roughly divided into two processes: direct ionization and recollision. In direct ionization, an atom or molecule absorbs enough photons to directly eject an outer-shell electron. In recollision, the electron absorbs energy greater than the ionization potential from the laser field and moves outward without being directly ionized; it is then pulled back by the changing electric field and collides with the parent ion. This recollision can lead to a rich variety of physical processes, including high-harmonic generation (HHG), high-order above-threshold ionization (HATI), and non-sequential double ionization (NSDI). In recent years, Professor Chen has developed a theoretical model to describe laser-induced recollision processes, from which structural information about atoms and molecules can be extracted. This talk will provide a brief introduction to the development of this theoretical model.
