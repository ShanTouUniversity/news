It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently published the January and February 2012 utility bills for faculty and staff.

Meter readings for January were taken on February 1–2, and for February on March 1. Faculty members can check their bills at the university's utility management system (http://power.stu.edu.cn) using their campus network credentials.

For any questions or discrepancies, please visit the Water and Electricity Center between March 7 and March 12 to verify (Tel: 2489). If no objections are raised by the deadline, the amount will be deducted from each person's March 2012 salary account. Those without a salary account should pay directly at the Finance Office.
