It was reported that the Youth Communist League Committee of Shantou University and the Resource Management Office jointly announced that, to welcome the annual Arbor Day and enhance students' environmental awareness, a series of activities themed "Meet on Arbor Day, Approach the Apple Tree" will be held throughout March 2012.

The first event, "Hand in Hand, Planting Together," is scheduled for 10:00–11:30 on March 12 beside the campus artificial lake. Volunteers will plant trees alongside students from STU Affiliated Middle School, with a limit of 40 participants. Registration closes at 12:00 on March 11.

The second event, "Approaching the Newton Apple Tree," is scheduled for 16:30–17:30 on March 14, during which students will visit the Newton Apple Tree in the greenhouse. Chen Shaogang, Director of the Resource Management Office, will serve as the guide.
