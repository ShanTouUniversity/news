It was reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently announced that the final of the 11th AIA (Association of International Accountants) "Job Hunt King" Competition will be held on April 8, 2012, at the Central University of Finance and Economics in Beijing. The competition aims to help university students recognize the important role that language expression skills play in job hunting and career development. The Business School will select one participant to represent the university.

The interview will be held at 10:00 AM on March 12 in the Seminar Room on the second floor of the Business School building. It will primarily assess candidates' pronunciation in both Chinese and English, their public speaking ability, and their stage presence. Competitors will deliver five-minute speeches each in English and Mandarin on assigned topics, followed by a one-minute Q&A with the judges. Speech manuscripts must be submitted by 10:30 PM on March 11 to tracyxu@stu.edu.cn, or can be submitted in person at the interview. Topics involve issues such as the adjustment of economic growth targets in Premier Wen Jiabao's Government Work Report and their national and global implications.
