It was reported that the Guangdong Province 8th "Challenge Cup" College Student Entrepreneurship Competition Shantou University Selection, hosted by the STU Leading Group for Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities and organized by the Communist Youth League Committee at STU, has received 14 business plan projects proposed by 68 students since its launch on December 6, 2011.

The defense session is scheduled for 7:00 PM on Saturday, March 17, 2012, at the Science Lecture Hall. Competitors are required to wear formal attire and arrive 15 minutes early for sign-in. The defense process includes a 5-minute PPT presentation by each contestant followed by a 3-minute Q&A session with judges.

Awards: First Prize (1 winner), Second Prize (3 winners), Third Prize (6 winners), and Best Popularity Award (1 winner). Projects winning first, second, and third prizes will represent the university at the Guangdong provincial competition.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee, Shantou University)
