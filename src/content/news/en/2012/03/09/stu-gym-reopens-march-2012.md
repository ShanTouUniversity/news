It was reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently announced that, to meet the fitness needs of students and staff, the campus gym has reopened effective immediately.

Operating hours are as follows: Tuesday through Friday, 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM; Sunday, 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM. The gym is closed on Mondays and Saturdays (Mondays are reserved for equipment cleaning and maintenance). Users will be charged a fee of 5 yuan per session, payable via the campus one-card system. Starting this semester, discounted options including per-visit cards, monthly cards, and annual cards will be gradually introduced to better serve the diverse fitness needs of the university community.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
