It was reported that the Financial Office of Shantou University recently announced that, in accordance with the notice issued on November 21, 2011 regarding the payment of basic medical insurance for residents by enrolled students for the 2012 academic year, multiple attempts have been made through the Bank of China to deduct medical insurance fees from students' bank accounts (including previously unsuccessful deductions). However, fees for some insured students could not yet be processed.

The Financial Office will conduct a supplementary deduction from students' campus one-card accounts on March 15. Affected students are advised to ensure that their campus card accounts contain sufficient funds before that date to guarantee successful deduction of the medical insurance premiums.
