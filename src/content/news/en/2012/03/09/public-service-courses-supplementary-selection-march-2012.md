It was reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced that, following the initial selection and supplementary selection process, a small number of seats remain available in the spring semester public service courses, and students who have not yet enrolled are welcome to apply.

Available courses include: the School of Art's [SER2211] "Happy Children's Day for Migrant Workers' Children" (open to 2009, 2010, and 2011 undergraduate cohorts), the Law Department's [SER0310] "Our Common Future — Environmental Education Public Service for Primary School Students in Shantou" (open to 2010 undergraduates), the Medical School's [SER3311] "Gratitude Creative Service" (open to 2010 and 2011 undergraduates), and the University Youth League Committee's [SER3015] "Shuguang Volunteer Teaching Service Learning Course" (open to 2010 and 2011 undergraduates).

Interested students should submit their registration forms to lxl0726@stu.edu.cn by 5:00 PM on March 9. Selection will be on a first-come, first-served basis, with a limit of one course per student. For inquiries, please call 82902416 to reach Mr. Liu.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
