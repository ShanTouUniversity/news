It was reported that the gallery of the Cheung Kong School of Art & Design at Shantou University recently announced that the "Emotion in Sound and Color: Second American Movie Poster Exhibition" will be held from March 10 to 16 at its gallery.

The exhibition will showcase original American movie posters, offering students and faculty a unique glimpse into the art of cinema and visual design.
