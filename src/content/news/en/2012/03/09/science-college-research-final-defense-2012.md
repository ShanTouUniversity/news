It was reported that the Extracurricular Science and Technology Leading Group at the College of Science, Shantou University, recently issued a notice that the final defense for 2011 student extracurricular academic science and technology projects will be held at 2:00 PM on Wednesday, March 14, 2012, in the College of Science multimedia conference room (Room L352).

Each project team is requested to submit their final reports on time before 5:00 PM on Tuesday, March 13, and send electronic versions to o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
