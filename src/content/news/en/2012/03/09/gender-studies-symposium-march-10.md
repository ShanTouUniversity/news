It was reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently announced that the "STU Women's Day Event — Gender Studies Faculty-Student Symposium" will be held from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM on Saturday, March 10, 2012, in Room 201 of the Science Reporting Hall. The event welcomes faculty and students from across campus to share and discuss experiences and achievements in gender research.

Contact: felix@stu.edu.cn, cws@stu.edu.cn.
