It was reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University recently announced that a total of 33 students applied for the third batch of national student loans for the 2011-2012 academic year. After review by the Student Financial Aid Management Center, the list is now published for public comment. Any objections may be submitted in writing or via visit/phone to the Student Financial Aid Management Center before Wednesday, March 14. Contact number: 82904749.

Additionally, the Bank of China Shantou Branch has notified that due to system upgrades, students applying for student loans must additionally provide the account number linked to the bank card listed on their loan application form. Applicants are requested to bring their bank cards to the Bank of China counter before March 14 to obtain the account information and submit the receipt to the Student Financial Aid Management Center (Office 102, Student Affairs Office).
