It was reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a decision to honor the "Outstanding Graduate Students" of 2011. Following preliminary evaluations by various graduate training units and review by the evaluation committee, a total of 49 students were selected — 7, including Qiao J, received first-class honors, and 42, including Guo RF, received second-class honors.

The university hopes that the honored students will continue to excel, and that all graduate students will learn from their example, continuously improving their academic standards and making greater contributions to the construction and development of Shantou University.
