It was reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently announced that, in care of female staff health, the STU Hospital will conduct free breast health screenings using far-infrared breast diagnostic equipment for all registered female staff members from March 9 to 16.

The screening aims to raise health awareness among female staff, enable early detection of breast diseases, and safeguard the physical and mental well-being of female faculty and staff. Interested staff members may register at the STU Hospital.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
