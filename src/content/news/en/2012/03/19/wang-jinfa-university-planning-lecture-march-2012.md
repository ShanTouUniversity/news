It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office at STU recently announced that the first lecture in the Education Ideology Discussion series, titled "Planning Your University Experience," will be held on March 21 at 7:30 p.m. in the Ke Bao Hall. The speaker is Prof. Wang Jinfa from Sun Yat-sen University.

Prof. Wang currently serves as Director of the National Biology Experimental Teaching Demonstration Center, Professor, and doctoral supervisor. He was honored as the first national teaching master and a distinguished teacher of Guangdong Province, and receives the State Council special government allowance.
