It is reported that the Law School at STU recently announced that, to help students preparing for the 2012 National Judicial Examination organize their review plans effectively, the Career Development Center will host a judicial exam experience sharing session on March 21 at 3:00 p.m. in Room 303, Building E.

Students who scored highly on last year's judicial exam from the Class of 2008 will share their study plans, test preparation strategies, mindset adjustment techniques, and exam-taking methods. Attendees will also receive a free copy of Weiguo's "2012 Judicial Exam Preparation: Methods First" booklet.
