It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee issued a notice on March 19, 2012, that the 68th Introductory Party Knowledge Class is scheduled to open on March 25.

## Registration and Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | March 25, 2012 |
| Collection of admission letters | Party branches should send representatives to collect admission letters by March 20 |
| Registration | 13:30–17:00, March 23, 2012 (open at noon) |
| Venue | Party School Office (Room 401, East Administration Building) |
| Requirements | Bring the admission letter and one one-inch frontal bareheaded photo (with unit and name on the back) |
| Group leaders’ meeting | 21:00, March 23, Room 311, East Administration Building |
| Leave | Those needing leave must complete the *Party School Leave Approval Form*, reviewed by the general Party branch or Youth League Committee and processed at the Party School Office; those absent without reason will be disqualified |
| Issued by | Party School of the CPC Shantou University Committee, Organization and United Front Work Department |

The course schedule, participant list, leave form and student rules are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The participant list and schedule are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
