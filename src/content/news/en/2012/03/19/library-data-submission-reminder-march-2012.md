It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a reminder for various departments to submit their 2011 library data statistics.

As of now, several departments including the School of Journalism and Communication, Social Sciences Department, College of Science, Physical Education Department, Cheung Kong School of Art & Design, Law School, and College of Arts and Letters have submitted on time. Remaining departments are requested to submit their data by March 21 via email to gnweng@stu.edu.cn.
