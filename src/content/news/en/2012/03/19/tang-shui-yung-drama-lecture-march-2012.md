It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art & Design and the Arts Education Center of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that the third lecture in the Cross-Strait Drama Series will be held at 7:30 PM on March 23 at the Science Lecture Hall.

The lecture features renowned Hong Kong theater director Prof. Tang Shui Yung, who serves as Artistic Director of the Tang Shui Yung Theatre Studio. He holds a Master degree in Drama from the Universite de la Sorbonne Nouvelle in Paris and formerly served as Dean of the Drama Academy at the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts. Known for his work in physical theater and minimalist aesthetics, he has been hailed by the media as the "Alchemist of Minimalist Theater."

It is also reported that Tang will be invited to participate in the World Shakespeare Festival hosted by London Globe Theatre in May 2012, making him the only Hong Kong artist selected for this event.
