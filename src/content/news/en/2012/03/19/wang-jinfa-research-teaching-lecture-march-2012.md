It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office at STU recently announced that the second lecture in the Education Ideology Discussion series, titled "Standing at the Podium — Research-Based Teaching Concepts and Design," will be held on March 21 at 3:00 p.m. in the Ke Bao Hall. The speaker is Prof. Wang Jinfa from Sun Yat-sen University.

Prof. Wang has long been engaged in teaching and scientific research, and advocates innovative teaching concepts and models such as "research-based teaching philosophy," "open and research-based teaching modes," and the "Four Famous" project construction. Heads of teaching units and all faculty members are welcome to attend.
