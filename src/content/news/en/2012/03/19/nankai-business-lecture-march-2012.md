It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2nd academic lecture of 2012 will be held on March 21 from 3:00 to 4:30 PM in the Business School Level 2 Academic Seminar Room.

The lecture will be delivered by Dr. Zhang Huiyu from the Business School of Nankai University, with the topic "Analysis of the Effect Mechanism of New Enterprise Social Network Construction: An Empirical Study Based on Efficiency and Effectiveness." All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
