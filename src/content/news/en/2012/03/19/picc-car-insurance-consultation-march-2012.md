It is reported that the Labor Union at STU recently announced that People's Insurance Company of China (PICC) will bring a "one-stop door-to-one-stop service" program for staff and faculty-owned vehicles and will host a car insurance consultation event on campus on March 21 (Wednesday).

The event will run in two sessions: 9:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. and 2:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., located in the lobby of the Labor Union Building (first floor). On-site services include free vehicle and vessel tax processing, annual ticket assistance, traffic violation fine payment, and vehicle inspection代办. All staff and faculty who attend the consultation will receive a Sinopec fuel discount card.
