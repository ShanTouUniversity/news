It is reported that the Party Publicity Department and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the academic lecture by Prof. Li Gongzhen of Wuhan University, titled "The Migration of World Scientific and Cultural Centers," will be held at 8:00 PM on March 19 in the New Library Lecture Hall.

The list of successfully reserved attendees has been published. Successful reservers are required to bring their campus ID cards and swipe in before 7:50 PM.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Publicity Department, Student Affairs Office)
