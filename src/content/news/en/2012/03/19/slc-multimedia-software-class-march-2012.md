The Student Affairs Office at Shantou University recently announced that to meet the growing demand for multimedia software course services, the Peer Learning Center (SLC) is offering introductory multimedia software classes, open to undergraduate students on the main campus.

Class A will be held on the evenings of March 25, April 8, April 15, and April 22 from 19:30 to 22:00. Class B will be held on the evenings of April 29, May 6, May 13, and May 20 from 19:30 to 22:00. Both classes will take place at the School of Art computer lab. Registration closes at midnight on March 22. Please send the registration form to slc@stu.edu.cn with the subject line "Registration-Class A/Class B."
