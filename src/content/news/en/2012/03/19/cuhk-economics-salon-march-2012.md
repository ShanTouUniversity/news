The Business School at Shantou University recently announced that Prof. Hui King Man, Vice President of the Chinese University of Hong Kong and Chair Professor of Marketing, will deliver the 107th session of the Business School Economics Salon on Friday, March 23, from 19:30 to 21:30 in the New Library Lecture Hall. The lecture theme is "Hong Kong: The Service Centre of the Pearl River Delta." The lecture will be conducted in Chinese.

Students from the Business School, both undergraduate and graduate, are welcome to attend, as are all faculty and staff members of the university.
