It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice to circulate the announcement on the 2012 Guangdong provincial township civil servant selection. To implement the central and provincial party committee directives on valuing grassroots orientation and to optimize the experience structure of township cadre teams, the Organization Department of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the CPC and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security have decided to openly select township civil servants.

The university urges eligible and interested graduates to actively participate.
