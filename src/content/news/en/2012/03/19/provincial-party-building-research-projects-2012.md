It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 application for Guangdong Provincial University Party Building Research Projects has officially opened.

This year, the projects are divided into key projects (10) and general projects (35, including 20 government-funded). The research period is原则上 one year. Applicants may only submit one project per application, and those whose 2010 or 2011 projects have not yet been concluded are not eligible to apply.

Units are requested to submit the approved project applications to the Party School Office (East 401, Administrative Building) by March 23, with electronic copies sent to o_dxb@stu.edu.cn.
