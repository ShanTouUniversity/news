It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at STU recently issued a notice stating that, with the approval of school leadership, a meeting of the Psychological Crisis Intervention Leading Group will be held on March 20 (Tuesday) at 4:00 p.m. in Conference Room 5 of the ACC Building.

The notice requires members of the school's Psychological Crisis Intervention Leading Group as well as counselors from all colleges, residential schools, and dormitory areas to attend the meeting.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
