It is reported that the Three Atmospheres Construction Office of Shantou University recently issued its 192nd report on study atmosphere inspection, highlighting student tardiness at the E teaching building.

According to the report, on March 13 (Tuesday) at 8:00 AM, 9 students arrived up to 5 minutes late and 6 students more than 5 minutes late at the E building. On March 16 (Friday) at 8:00 AM, 6 students were up to 5 minutes late and 1 student was more than 5 minutes late.

The report urged relevant colleges and residential schools to provide批评 education to students who failed to observe school rules and regulations, in order to promote a good study atmosphere.
