It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to meet annual fire safety inspection requirements and with approval from relevant municipal authorities, the university will construct a配套 kitchen gas room at ACC.

The project, undertaken by Shantou Gas Construction Company, includes gas pipeline installation, fire and explosion alarm system setup, and construction of the new gas room. The工程 is scheduled to commence on March 20 and is expected to be completed by the end of May.

The Resource Management Office reminded faculty and students not to enter the construction site without permission from relevant departments and the construction unit, to ensure safety and campus order.
