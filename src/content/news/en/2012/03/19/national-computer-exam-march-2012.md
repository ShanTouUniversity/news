It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the first half of 2012 National Computer Rank Examination will be held on Saturday, March 24.

The written test begins at 9:00 AM, and the computer-based test starts at 12:30 PM in batches at the E102 computer lab. Candidates must bring their ID cards and admission tickets. Those arriving more than 15 minutes late for the written test will not be admitted. Candidates for the computer-based test must assemble at the E204 classroom 30 minutes early to draw lots for seating arrangements.

Undergraduate students of STU can collect their admission tickets from their respective college academic affairs offices, while other candidates may obtain them from the Academic Affairs Office Student Affairs Section (West 402, Administrative Building), contact number 82902402.
