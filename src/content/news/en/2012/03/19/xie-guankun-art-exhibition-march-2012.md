It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at STU recently announced that the exhibition "Water Journey - Xie Guankun Solo Exhibition" will be held at the college's art museum from March 20 to 27.

The opening ceremony is scheduled for 3:00 p.m. on March 20, with a works auction following at 3:20 p.m. The exhibition will run through March 27, and all faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
