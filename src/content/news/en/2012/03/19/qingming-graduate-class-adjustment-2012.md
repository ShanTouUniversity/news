It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice regarding class adjustments for graduate students in accordance with the university's Qingming Festival holiday schedule.

The adjustments are as follows: classes on April 4 (Wednesday) will be suspended; classes on April 2 (Monday) and March 31 (Saturday) will be swapped; classes on April 3 (Tuesday) and April 1 (Sunday) will be swapped.
