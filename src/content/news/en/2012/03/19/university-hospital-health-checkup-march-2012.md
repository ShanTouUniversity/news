The Resources Management Office at Shantou University recently announced that the 2012 routine health checkup for faculty and staff has been launched, covering all retired staff members. Personnel who choose to have their checkup at the University Hospital are scheduled to begin on March 21.

The examination items include routine checkups and laboratory tests (liver function and blood routine). Staff aged 40 and above or with intermediate titles or above will additionally undergo blood glucose and blood lipid tests. Female participants will have uterine ultrasound and cervical smear tests added, while male participants will have prostate ultrasound added. The University Hospital provides free breakfast for those undergoing lab tests and ultrasounds. Staff with hypertension are advised to bring their own antihypertensive medication.
