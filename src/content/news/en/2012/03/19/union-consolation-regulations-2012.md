It is reported that the Education Labor Union of STU recently released the "STU Labor Union Consolation Activity Management Regulations." The regulations specify that consolation recipients are union members currently employed at the main campus.

The consolation standards are as follows: 1,000 yuan for long-term living difficulties; 500–1,000 yuan for hospitalization due to serious illness; 1,000 yuan during recovery from serious illness; 500 yuan for the death of a direct relative; and 2,000 yuan for the death of a serving staff member or their spouse. The regulations take effect from the date of publication.
