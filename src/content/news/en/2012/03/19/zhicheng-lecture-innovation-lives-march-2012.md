The Zhicheng College at Shantou University recently announced that the 12th edition of the "Zhicheng Lecture Series" invites Luo Donghong, Deputy General Manager and Senior Engineer of China Unicom's Shantou Branch, to deliver a keynote lecture titled "Innovation, Changing Lives."

The lecture will be held from 19:30 to 21:30 on March 25 in Lecture Room J-354, Zhicheng College. It will cover the national 12th Five-Year Plan's strategy in the communications and information sectors, as well as innovative applications in mobile internet, the Internet of Things, cloud computing, and the evolution of industry technologies within smart city development.

Luo holds a Master of Engineering Management (MEM) degree from the University of Technology Sydney, Australia. He also serves as Vice President of the Shantou Information Services and Software Industry Association and as an expert consultant to the Shantou Municipal Government Information Leading Group. Registration closes at 17:00 on March 22. Please visit http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ to register.
