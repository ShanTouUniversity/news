It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 graduate student academic electronic registration images are now available online. Graduates who participated in the image collection are urged to log in to CHSI (http://www.chsi.com.cn/) and click on the "Academic Electronic Registration Image Verification" link to carefully check their graduation photo, name, ID number, gender, ethnicity, date of birth, student ID, and major.

The notice emphasized that the information verified will appear on graduation and degree certificates. Students who fail to verify and suffer consequences affecting their certificate issuance will bear full responsibility.
