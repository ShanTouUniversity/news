It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at STU recently announced that the first lecture in the "Kankan Culture" series will be held on March 20 at 7:30 p.m. The dialogue will feature Prof. Wolfgang Kubin, a distinguished Sinologist, thinker, and visiting professor at STU's School of Arts and Letters, and Prof. Wang Shouzhi, Dean of the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design.

Titled "Reflections on Contemporary Chinese Culture," the event is hosted by the college and open to all faculty and students.
