It is reported that the Graduate School at STU recently announced the results of the final review for the 2011 extracurricular science projects, with 32 projects passing the review after fair and careful evaluation by an expert panel. Additionally, 11 projects were abandoned midway and did not complete their final defense.

According to the notice, project leaders who passed the review are required to visit the Graduate School (Room G-204) at 9:00 a.m. on March 23, 26, and 27 to collect their funding books and complete the project reimbursement process.
