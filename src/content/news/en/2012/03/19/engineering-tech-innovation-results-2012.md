The Engineering College at Shantou University recently announced that out of 37 student science and technology innovation projects that passed the mid-term review in 2011, 12 projects chose not to proceed to the final defense while 25 projects underwent the final review. After fair and careful evaluation by the college review panel, 19 projects—including "The Future of 'Homestead Land' Use in Small Towns from the Perspective of Stakeholders"—passed the final review, and 6 projects including "Design and Development of an Expandable Pedestrian Arch Bridge" were deferred.

The notice requests that project leaders whose projects have passed review and submitted final reports complete relevant summary work and finish project expense reimbursement by March 30.
