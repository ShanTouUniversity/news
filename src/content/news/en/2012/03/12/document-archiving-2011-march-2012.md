It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice requiring all units to complete the archiving of all 2011 annual documents and any accumulated backlog from previous years by April 30, 2012.

The scope of archiving covers affiliated research institutions and laboratories, including data entry into the archives management system, organization of audio-visual archives, and archiving of physical materials. The university will circulate a progress report on archiving completion in June.

For business inquiries, please contact the Comprehensive Archives Office at 82902878.

This deployment aims to standardize the university's archival management, ensure the integrity and traceability of important documentary materials, and lay the foundation for preserving and utilizing the school's historical records.
