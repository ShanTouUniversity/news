It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University will hold the first session of its public service course SER3014, "Sunshine Action — Community Correction Service and Research in the Chaoshan Region," from 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm on March 14, in Room 213 of the Law School building.

Combining service and research, the course organizes students to take part in community correction-related services in the Chaoshan region, reflecting the school's exploration of integrating professional study with public welfare. Students enrolled in the course have been asked to attend the first session on time.
