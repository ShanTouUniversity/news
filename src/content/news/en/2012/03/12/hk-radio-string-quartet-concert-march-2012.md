It is reported that the Arts Education Center of the Yangtze College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently announced that the opening performance of the "Colorful Art Season 2012 Spring" will take place on March 16 at 19:30 in the Science Lecture Hall. The Hong Kong Radio String Quartet has been specially invited to perform classical string quartet works by Haydn and Shostakovich. The concert will be broadcast by Hong Kong Radio Television Programme 4.

Tickets can be collected on March 14 from 9:30–11:30 and 14:30–17:00 at Room 309, Arts Education Center, with registration using the STU smart card. Children under 12 are not admitted.

The Arts Education Center stated that this performance serves as the opening act of the "Colorful Art Season 2012 Spring," aiming to bring a high-caliber classical music experience to faculty and students and enrich campus cultural life.
