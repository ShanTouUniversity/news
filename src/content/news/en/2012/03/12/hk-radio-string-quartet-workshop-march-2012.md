It is reported that the Arts Education Center of the Yangtze College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently announced that, as a prelude to the "Colorful Art Season 2012 Spring," the Hong Kong Radio String Quartet will host a workshop for faculty and students.

The workshop will take place on March 16 from 15:30 to 16:45 in the Science Lecture Hall, hosted by Mr. Chung Tsz Hou, a well-known host of the Hong Kong Radio. To apply, participants should send their name, grade and college, student ID, mobile number, and email to o_ysjyzx@stu.edu.cn. Applications close at 4:00 PM on March 14. Participation is limited to 30 people on a first-come, first-served basis.

The Hong Kong Radio String Quartet is one of the most renowned chamber music ensembles in Hong Kong. This workshop offers faculty and students a rare opportunity to learn string quartet performance techniques up close.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Yangtze College of Art and Design, Arts Education Center)
