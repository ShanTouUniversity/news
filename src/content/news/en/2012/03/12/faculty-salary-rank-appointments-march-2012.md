It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing proposed appointments under the university's annual salary system, based on the evaluation results of the Faculty Appointment Working Committee held on March 9.

The proposed appointments cover multiple ranks: five faculty members including Lou Zengjian are proposed for Professor T1-level positions; eight faculty members including Yang Zhongqiang are proposed for Professor T2-level positions; fourteen faculty members including Zhu Wang are proposed for Professor positions; and seventeen faculty members including Chen Fanfan are proposed for Associate Professor positions.

The notice stated that the public consultation period runs until March 15, 2013. Any objections may be submitted in writing. The contact number is 82902364, and the email is o_rsk@stu.edu.cn.

This announcement represents an important step in Shantou University's ongoing reform of its personnel system and the implementation of the annual salary appointment scheme, aimed at attracting and cultivating high-caliber teaching and research faculty.
