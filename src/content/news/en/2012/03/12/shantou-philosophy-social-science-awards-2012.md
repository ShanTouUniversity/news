It was reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently announced that, in support of the 2010–2011 Shantou Philosophy and Social Science Excellent Achievements Awards organized by the Shantou Municipal Propaganda Department and the Municipal Federation of Social Science Circles, calls for submissions are now open across campus.

Eligible submissions include formally published books and papers from 2010 to 2011, as well as unpublished theoretical works, papers, and research consultation reports that have been adopted by party committees or governments at or above the county level, or by municipal bureau-level units or above. Each individual may submit up to one entry; collaborative projects involving two or more participants may submit an additional entry.
