It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the Peer Learning Center (SLC) will open to all undergraduate students starting March 12.

The SLC will be open Sunday through Friday from 3:00 PM to 10:00 PM each week. Key services include academic advising, group discussion spaces, and tutoring sessions.

The Peer Learning Center is an important platform established by the Student Affairs Office to provide academic support to undergraduate students, helping them improve their learning abilities, develop independent study habits, and foster a positive academic atmosphere.

The Student Affairs Office stated that the opening of the SLC will effectively supplement academic resources beyond the classroom, offering students more flexible and diverse learning support services.
