It is reported that the Yangtze College of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center of Shantou University recently announced the results of the university choir auditions. Nine students successfully passed the interview and will join the STU University Choir as new members.

Selected members are required to assemble at the choir rehearsal hall on the first floor of the Arts Education Center at 13:30 on March 14 to begin the new semester's training.

The selection process comprehensively considered the requirements of choral music, the choir's practical needs, and the vocal qualities of each candidate, ensuring that new members would strengthen the ensemble's sectional structure and overall performance level.

The STU University Choir is one of the most prominent student arts organizations on campus, regularly performing at both internal and external cultural events throughout the year.
