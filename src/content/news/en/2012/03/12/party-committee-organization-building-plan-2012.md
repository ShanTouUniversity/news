It is reported that the Communist Party Committee of Shantou University recently issued the "Implementation Plan for Deepening Grassroots Organization Building Year Activities within the Framework of the Excellence Creation and Competition Campaign." The document requests the Medical College Party Committee, all party general branches, and directly affiliated party branches to implement the plan in accordance with their respective circumstances.

The document specifies that 2012 has been designated as the Grassroots Organization Building Year, requiring all party organizations at every level to use the excellence creation and competition campaign as a driving force to further strengthen grassroots organization building and enhance the cohesion and combat effectiveness of party organizations.

The implementation plan requires all units to formulate specific action plans based on their actual conditions, ensuring that all tasks and measures for the Grassroots Organization Building Year are properly executed and yield tangible results.
