It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently circulated a notice from the Social Sciences Federations of Guangdong and Hainan provinces, which have decided to hold the inaugural China (Guangdong-Hainan) Reform and Innovation Forum. The first forum is planned for May or June 2012 in Hainan Province.

The forum's theme is "Regional Cooperation among Guangdong, Qiongan, Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan and the Development of the South China Sea Economy." Discussion topics include regional cooperation and economic development mode transformation, as well as marine economy development and South China Sea development cooperation.

Papers are required to center on the forum theme, combining theory with practice, with a recommended length of approximately 6,000 Chinese characters. Submissions should be sent to the contact email at the Academic Planning Department of the Guangdong Provincial Social Sciences Federation by May 20.

Contact persons: Huang Yumei and Wang Liju, telephone 82902563.

The forum is co-hosted by the Social Sciences Federations of both provinces, aiming to promote research on regional economic cooperation and innovative development. Scholars worldwide are encouraged to submit their papers.
