It was reported that the General Office of the STU Party Committee and Administration recently issued a bulletin stating that on March 9, the Ministry of Civil Affairs announced the shortlist for the 7th China Charity Award via its official website and the China Charity and Philanthropy Web. The "Li Ka Shing Foundation — STU Medical School Medical Poverty Alleviation Project" has been shortlisted for the "Most Influential Charity Project" category. Online voting closes at 2:00 PM on March 16.

The medical poverty alleviation initiative began in May 1998. By the end of 2011, the Li Ka Shing Foundation had cumulatively donated over 21 million yuan, mobilizing matching funds and facility investments from the STU Medical School and its affiliated hospital. Over fourteen years, the project has established 31 poverty alleviation bases and 120 outreach sites across 14 provinces and municipalities in China, dispatched medical teams 1,356 times, provided free medical care and medicines to over 500,000 impoverished patients, donated pharmaceuticals worth over 10 million yuan, performed 12,313 free surgeries of various kinds, established China's first rural child rehabilitation model, and provided free rehabilitation to 544 children with cerebral palsy.
