It was reported that the School of Chinese Language and Literature at Shantou University recently announced that the renowned German sinologist Professor Wolfgang Kubin has been appointed as a distinguished professor at the university, with his appointment effective from 2012. The appointment ceremony and his first academic lecture, titled "Jin Yong and the Crisis of Contemporary Chinese Literature," will be held on the evening of March 15 from 7:00 to 9:30 PM in the Science Lecture Hall.

Professor Kubin is a preeminent German scholar and widely regarded as a leading figure in Chinese literature and cultural studies in Europe, as well as one of the most prominent sinologists in the world today. He has published over 50 monographs and translated works in German, English, Chinese, and other languages. Among his most celebrated works are "The Evolution of the Concept of Nature in Chinese Literature," "A History of Classical Chinese Poetry," and "A Literary History of China in the Twentieth Century." Fluent in over a dozen languages including German, Chinese, English, French, Russian, and Japanese, Professor Kubin has made outstanding contributions to Sino-Western literary translation and cultural exchange.
