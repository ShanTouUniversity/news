It was reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced that the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Expedition Project application review process has officially commenced. After collecting project proposals from student teams by March 16, the review committee will conduct a written evaluation, and teams advancing to the inquiry review will be announced on the campus intranet on the afternoon of March 21.

The inquiry review is scheduled for 3:00–6:00 PM on March 22 in ACC Conference Room 2. Selected team representatives are required to arrive 15 minutes early for preparation.

Evaluation criteria include: Challenge (25%), Feasibility (20%), Team Capability (20%), Safety (20%), and Research Value (15%).
