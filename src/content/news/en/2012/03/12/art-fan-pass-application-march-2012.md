It is reported that the Arts Education Center of the Yangtze College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently announced the relaunch of the "Art Fan Pass" application program for the new semester, following enthusiastic response from the previous season.

The "Art Fan Pass" is limited to 200 holders, who will receive priority ticket access and guaranteed prime seating at performances. "Super Art Fans" who attended all performances of the previous art season can exchange directly for this season's pass without registration. The pickup dates are March 14, 9:30–11:30 and 14:30–17:00, at Room 309, Arts Education Center, on a first-come, first-served basis.

The Arts Education Center stated that the "Art Fan Pass" program aims to reward faculty and students who have long supported the university's arts activities, enrich campus cultural life, and encourage greater participation in high-quality artistic performances.
