It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the Student Club Federation office (Room 731, Building 7) will resume regular duty hours starting March 12. The office hours are Monday through Friday, 16:15 to 17:30.

The notice also mentioned that the management regulations for the office and equipment lending have been partially revised, and the new regulations will take effect this semester.

The resumption of regular office hours aims to better serve all student clubs on campus, streamline daily administrative processes, and improve the efficiency of equipment lending services.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee, STU)
