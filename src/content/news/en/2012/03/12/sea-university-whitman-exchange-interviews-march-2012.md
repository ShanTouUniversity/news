It is reported that the Student Affairs Office, International Exchange and Cooperation Office, and English Language Center of Shantou University jointly issued a notice announcing that interviews for the 2012 "Sea University" and Whitman College exchange program will be held on March 14 at 4:00 PM in Room E202.

A total of 19 students applied for the program. After initial screening, 8 students including Zhang H advanced to the interview stage. Shortlisted students must draw lots at the Student Affairs Office conference room by 4:30 PM on March 13 to determine their interview order, and submit their interview PPT to xyli@stu.edu.cn by 11:00 AM on March 14.

The interview will be conducted in a defense format, featuring a 5-minute PPT presentation followed by an 8-minute Q&A session with the panel. The interview is open to all faculty and students who wish to observe.

The "Sea University" program is a key academic exchange mechanism between Shantou University and Whitman College, providing students with valuable opportunities for further study at the U.S. institution.
