It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing a campus-wide survey on undergraduate teaching laboratories. The initiative aims to assess the current situation of teaching laboratories, analyze existing problems and challenges, and provide constructive recommendations for strengthening and improving laboratory work across the university.

All colleges and departments are asked to cooperate fully, organizing experimental teaching staff to engage in in-depth discussions and produce objective, comprehensive, and accurate data statistics and reports. Submission of both printed and electronic versions of the survey materials is due to the Academic Affairs Office by March 26, 2012.

Contact: Chen Yangzhou, telephone 82903202, email yzchen@stu.edu.cn.

The survey is intended to comprehensively evaluate the current state of undergraduate teaching laboratories at STU, providing a foundation for future laboratory construction planning and optimized resource allocation.
