It was reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the STU Party Committee recently announced that the mobilization meeting for the in-depth implementation of the Grassroots Organization Building Year activity within the framework of the strive-to-be-excellent campaign will be held on March 14 at 9:00 AM in Room 205 of the Science and Technology Center. Members of the university's leading group for the Grassroots Organization Building Year activity, representatives from the Party Committee of the Medical School, general party branches of the main campus, and heads of directly affiliated party branches will attend the meeting.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee)
