It was reported that the Youth Communist League Committee of Shantou University recently announced the class schedules for two public service courses: SER3015 "Shuguang Volunteer Teaching Service Learning Course" (Class No. 53720) and SER3014 "Sunshine Community Service Learning Course" (Class No. 53721).

For the "Shuguang Volunteer Teaching Service Learning Course," training sessions are scheduled for March 14, 21, and 28; social practice runs from March 11 to May 13; and the summary and sharing session will be held on May 16.

For the "Sunshine Community Service Learning Course," training sessions are scheduled for March 14, 21, 28, and April 4; social practice runs from March 17 to May 13; and the summary and sharing session will be held on May 16.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth Communist League Committee of STU)
