It was reported that the Propaganda Department of the STU Party Committee recently announced that the Shantou Fourth Civil Servant Calligraphy Exhibition will take place in late April. The university is currently accepting submissions from calligraphy enthusiasts across campus. Interested faculty and staff should submit their works to the Propaganda Department (Room 402, Xingdong Building) by March 23, following the submission guidelines, for the university to compile and forward.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Propaganda Department of the Party Committee)
