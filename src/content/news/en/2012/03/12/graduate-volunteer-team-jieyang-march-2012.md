It is reported that the Graduate Student Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice launching a volunteer recruitment campaign during the "Lei Feng National Volunteer Service Action Month." A one-day event themed "Bringing Knowledge and Technology to Rural Areas" will be held on March 25, alongside regular teaching support activities at Zoutang Primary School in Fengmei Village, Jieyang City.

The recruitment, open to all graduate students, includes positions in elementary teaching (10 spots), legal aid (2–3 spots), aquaculture technology (2–4 spots), psychological counseling (2–3 spots), and free medical consultations (10 spots).

The Graduate Student Union stated that the initiative aims to leverage the professional strengths of graduate students to deliver knowledge and technology to rural communities, contributing youthful energy to local development.
