It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice that in 2011, a total of 28 extracurricular science projects were approved, of which 18 completed their work and participated in the final review defense. After rigorous evaluation by the review expert panel, 2 first prizes, 3 second prizes, and 4 third prizes were awarded; 9 projects were rated as qualifying. Ten projects did not participate in the defense and were deemed to have自动放弃, resulting in an unqualified rating and recovery of project funding.

To encourage and promote students\' spirit of exploration and innovation, the college has decided to award bonuses to projects with outstanding results: 600 yuan for first prize, 400 yuan for second prize, and 200 yuan for third prize, along with certificates of achievement.
