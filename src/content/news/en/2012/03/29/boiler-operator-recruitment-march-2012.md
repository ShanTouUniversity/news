It is reported that the Personnel Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University jointly issued a notice recruiting several boiler operators for the Student Community Management Service Center.

Requirements include: good political and ideological qualities, strong sense of responsibility, upright conduct; under 50 years old, in good health. Responsibilities include ensuring the normal operation and daily maintenance of the dormitory hot water supply system (fuel oil boiler, solar thermal, and air-source heat pump systems), conducting daily inspections and recording operational data, regularly inspecting and reporting repairs for hot water system pipelines, and managing oil storage safety. Applications should be submitted by April 25, 2012, via email or mail to the Student Community Management Service Center, Resource Management Office, Shantou University.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office, Resource Management Office)
