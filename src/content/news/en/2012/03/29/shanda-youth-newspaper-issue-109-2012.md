It is reported that, with the support of the University Youth League Committee and the efforts of all the paper's editors, Issue 109 of the *STU Youth* newspaper has been completed and released to students and faculty, who are welcome to follow it and offer guidance.

The issue spans eight pages, covering student activities, personal stories, in-depth reporting and campus life.

## Highlights

| Page | Section | Focus |
|------|---------|-------|
| A2 | Youth League Updates | Creative themed Youth League day projects close to everyday life; college youth volunteer associations beautifying the campus through the "service street" programme; and the X Programme helping female students develop inner and outer qualities |
| A3 | Stories | As graduation season approaches, more seniors are travelling to job fairs nationwide; outstanding students share tips on standing out |
| A4 | In-Depth | After a theft at the university library, rumours spread across campus; the report asks whether the case was real, whether security work is adequate, and who should be responsible for campus safety |
| B1 | Investigation | After the 2011 New Arts orientation gala, staff celebrated at the East Gate in the early hours, disturbing residents of Zhicheng College; STU Youth reporters investigated the sources of the noise and the related complaints |
| B2 | Opinion | "STU People Ask STU People" — addressing canteen price rises, slow campus internet and holiday scheduling |
| B3 | Life | A look inside the postgraduate community, a group rarely in the campus spotlight |
| B4 | Culture | Memories of the old library and the last of the century-old erythrina trees, reflecting on the past while looking ahead |

*STU Youth* welcomes contributions and engagement from the university community.
