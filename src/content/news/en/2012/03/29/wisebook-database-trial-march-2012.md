It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently issued a notice that the WISEBOOK foreign original books database has begun its trial period, running until June 30, 2012.

The WISEBOOK database is a professional platform for foreign original electronic books, covering industrial technology, bioscience, medicine, economics and finance, literature, history, geography, and other disciplines. It is provided by Beijing Weisi Bowen Technology Co., Ltd., an authorized distributor in mainland China for two major American e-book distribution groups (CyberRead and OverDrive) and the DynoMedia Group. The database contains a large collection of high-quality foreign original electronic books spanning 21 professional disciplines.

Access link: http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=346 (please use Internet Explorer and open in a new window). Faculty and students are encouraged to make full use of this resource. Feedback and acquisition suggestions may be sent to the Library Subject Service Department, Tel: 3247, Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn or zhzeng@stu.edu.cn.
