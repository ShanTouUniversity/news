It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following the leadership meeting on March 12, 2012, the college has adjusted the work division among its leadership team. The adjustments are as follows:

Dean Xu Zongling: Overall responsibility for all college operations, in charge of discipline construction and development, MBA center work, administrative work, personnel work, and financial work.

Vice Dean Ouyang Feng: Oversees undergraduate, graduate, MBA, and graduate course extension program teaching implementation, the experimental training center, and website construction.

Vice Dean Wang [name obscured]: Assists the dean with international accreditation, foreign faculty recruitment, and student international competition work; oversees instructional design, external exchange and cooperation, academic lectures, and English website content management.

Deputy Secretary Chen Yinglin (Student Affairs Director): In accordance with university regulations, oversees student ideological and political education, admissions and employment, alumni management, and Party building; also oversees the MBA Center EDP (executive short-term training) program.
