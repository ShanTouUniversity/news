It is reported that the Personnel Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University jointly issued a notice recruiting two water and electricity management engineers for the Infrastructure Office, due to construction work needs.

Requirements include: bachelor\'s degree or above in water and electricity-related fields, with priority given to those holding intermediate or above technical titles or professional registration qualifications; certain knowledge of engineering management and on-site construction experience, with preference for those with 3+ years of relevant work experience; proficiency in CAD drawing and office software; strong organizational, coordination, and teamwork skills; under 45 years old, in good health, with good professional ethics. Responsibilities include technical management for the project team and assisting in project筹备, design, and engineering bidding management.
