It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Club Federation recently issued a notice that the 2011-2012 Spring Semester Student Club Activity Project application process has begun.

The application period runs from March 29 to April 11. Management measures and application procedures for project approval are detailed in the attachment.各社团请于4月11日前把相关申报资料发至社联FTP（地址，帐号shelian，密码association）. All student clubs are encouraged to actively participate and submit applications to promote the development of club culture at STU.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
