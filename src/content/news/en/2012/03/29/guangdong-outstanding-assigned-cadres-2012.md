It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that in 2011, Lin Xiaoling (College of Engineering) and Zhang Yanzhen (Medical College) of STU actively responded to the calls of the Communist Youth League Central Committee and the Guangdong Provincial Committee, and were assigned to the Longhu District and Jinping District Youth League Committees in Shantou City, respectively, for grassroots work.

Over the past year, both have stayed close to the people, rooted in the grassroots, integrated university and local resources, innovated working methods, and actively promoted the construction of grassroots league organizations. Their work has received high recognition from the Provincial Youth League Committee. In March this year, the Provincial Youth League Committee awarded both Lin Xiaoling and Zhang Yanzhen the title of "2011 Guangdong University Youth League Cadres Outstanding Assigned Cadres at County-Level League Committees."
