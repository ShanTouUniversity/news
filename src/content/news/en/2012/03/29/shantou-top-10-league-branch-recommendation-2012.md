It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the Shantou Municipal Committee of the Communist Youth League\'s "Notice on Applying for the 2011 Shantou Top 10 Grassroots League Committee Secretaries, Outstanding League Cadres, Outstanding League Members, and May Fourth Red Flag League Organizations" (Shan Shi Tuan Zi〔2012〕No. 9) and the recommendations from various units, the proposed list of advanced collectives and individuals is hereby published for public consultation.

Units or individuals with objections may report in writing or in person/by phone to the University Youth League Committee before April 1, 2012. (Unit submissions must bear the official seal; individual submissions must include the real name for responsibility purposes.) Tel: 0754-82902302.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
