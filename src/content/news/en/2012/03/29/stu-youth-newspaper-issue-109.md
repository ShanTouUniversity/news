It is reported that Issue 109 of the *STU Youth* newspaper, produced with support from the University Youth League Committee, has been completed and released to students and faculty.

The issue spans eight pages, covering student activities, personal stories, in-depth reporting and campus life.

## Highlights

| Page | Section | Focus |
|------|---------|-------|
| A2 | Youth League Updates | Creative themed Youth Day projects, volunteer service street with college youth associations beautifying campus, and the X Program helping female students develop inside and out |
| A3 | Stories | Graduating seniors navigating job fairs, with outstanding students sharing tips on standing out |
| A4 | In-Depth | Investigating rumors around a library theft and examining campus security and the role of the security office |
| B1 | Investigation | Noise complaints after a new arts orientation party late-night celebration, and following discussions on BBS and Weibo, and a broader survey of campus noise sources |
| B2 | Opinion | "STU People Ask STU People" — addressing questions on canteen prices, internet speed and holiday scheduling |
| B3 | Life | Spotlight on postgraduate students, a less visible but integral campus community |
| B4 | Culture | Memories of the old library and the century-old erythrina tree, reflecting on the past while looking ahead |

*STU Youth* welcomes contributions and engagement from the university community.
