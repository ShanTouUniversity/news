It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Club Federation recently issued a notice that the 2011-2012 Autumn Semester STU Student Club Activity Project Showcase was successfully held on March 25.

Eight clubs participated in the showcase: Photography Association, Guitar Association, Track and Field Association, Tea Culture Society, Lantern Riddle Association, Xingchao Society, Anime Technology and Game Development Association, and Finance Association. In accordance with the "STU Student Club Federation Student Club Activity Application Regulations," after careful review by the judges and approval by the University Youth League Committee, the results of the project showcase are now published.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
