It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the requirements of the Organization Department of the Shantou Municipal Working Committee of the CPC, the "Preliminary Candidate List for Guangdong Delegates to the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China" is hereby published for public consultation.

Comments and suggestions regarding the preliminary candidates for the 18th CPC National Congress delegates should be submitted to the Organization and United Front Work Department by April 5. Tel: 82902345, Email: o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, Party Committee)
