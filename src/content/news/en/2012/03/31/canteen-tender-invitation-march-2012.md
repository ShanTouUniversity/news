It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the university will conduct a public tender for the operation of the Fourth Cafeteria and the Second-Floor Faculty Dining Hall, inviting qualified catering companies to submit bids.

Bidders must hold a business license with food service as the main business scope, possess considerable financial strength and scale, and have valid food industry hygiene qualification certifications. Registered bidding units must apply and submit relevant qualification documents (including business license, hygiene permit, company credentials, legal representative\'s ID, and contact information) to the Diet Supervision Center Office, Resource Management Office, by 5:00 PM on April 10. The university will review the qualifications of bidding units and determine eligible participants. Qualified bidders will be notified to collect tender documents, receive a briefing on the tender process, and conduct a site visit at the Diet Supervision Center. The deadline and location for submitting bid documents will be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
