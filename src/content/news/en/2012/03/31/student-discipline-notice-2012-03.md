It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued the *Student Discipline Notice (No. 3, 2012)* on March 31, 2012, addressing a violation involving unauthorized stays in the female dormitory of Building E, and decided to give a demerit to Zhou M, a 2011 undergraduate majoring in Law in the Law School.

## Incident

| Time | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Afternoon, March 10 | An off-campus student registered at the duty office of Building E under a false name to visit Zhou M, left luggage in Zhou M's dormitory, left the female dormitory at 14:30, and stayed off campus that night. The university has notified the student's home institution |
| Noon, March 11 | Zhou M and Ye M entered the female dormitory together when it was crowded and stayed overnight |
| Morning, March 12 | A counselor from the dormitory area discovered a male student brushing teeth in the bathroom during a routine visit to the female dormitory of Building E, and the facts were subsequently verified |

## Basis and Decision

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Violation | Arranging for off-campus and fellow students to stay overnight in the female dormitory in violation of dormitory management regulations |
| Basis | Article 14 of the *Shantou University Regulations on Student Disciplinary Sanctions (Trial)* |
| Decision | After university review, Zhou M was given a demerit |
| Persons involved | Zhou M (sanctioned), Ye M (accompanying stay), off-campus student (notified to home institution) |

The notice called on all students to take this as a warning, consciously abide by dormitory management regulations, and jointly maintain normal teaching and living order to promote the development of school spirit and academic atmosphere.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
