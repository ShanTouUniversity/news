It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 18th National University Student Business Case Analysis Competition (Kent Cup) national round has begun as of March 15. The competition is authorized by the Chinese Economics Annual Conference, hosted by the Communist Youth League Hubei Provincial Committee, and open to students from universities across the Taiwan Strait region.

The competition schedule is as follows: registration closes April 5; preliminary case electronic submissions due April 10; semi-finalist list announced April 12; semi-finals on April 21–22; national finals on May 12–13. Eleven teams (excluding the Central China region) will advance to the national semi-finals based on preliminary review, and eight teams will advance to the national finals. Business School students interested in participating are encouraged to register.
