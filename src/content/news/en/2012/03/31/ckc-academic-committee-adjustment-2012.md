It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to further strengthen academic development and management, the college has decided to adjust the members of the Academic Committee / Degree Evaluation Sub-committee. The updated roster is as follows:

Director: Wang Shouzhi
Members: Han Ran, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Qian Fenggen, Chen Biru, Wu Xiangyong, Cui Dewei, Li Haoyu, Yang Hua, Kuang Zhiwen
Secretary: Li Lanqing

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
