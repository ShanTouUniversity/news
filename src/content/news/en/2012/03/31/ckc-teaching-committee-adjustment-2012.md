It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to strengthen teaching management and improve teaching quality, the college has decided to adjust the members of the Teaching Committee. The updated roster is as follows:

Director: Wang Shouzhi
Members: Han Ran, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Qian Fenggen, Chen Biru, Wu Xiangyong, Cui Dewei, Li Haoyu, Yang Hua, Kuang Zhiwen, Lin Wen, Liu Guanghui
Secretary: Fang Ruohong

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
