It is reported that the CPC Shantou University Committee recently issued a notice distributing the "Shantou University Party Committee Central Group 2012 Theory Study Plan."

The notice requests the Medical College Party Committee, all general Party branches, and directly affiliated Party branches to combine the actual conditions of their respective units and earnestly implement the plan.

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Committee)
