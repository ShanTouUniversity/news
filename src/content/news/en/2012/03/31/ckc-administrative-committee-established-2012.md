It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to better manage the college\'s daily affairs, the college has decided to establish an Administrative Committee. The committee members are as follows:

Director: Wang Shouzhi
Members: Chen Yiyuan, Li Aiyi, Han Ran, Zhuang Wei, Li Lanqing, Lin Jinxiang
Secretary: Xu Xiangna

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
