It is reported that, according to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the annual deadline for university program applications and filings is June 30. In preparation for this year's round, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has arranged the 2012 application and filing of new undergraduate programs. Schools and departments intending to apply must submit their materials to the office's Teaching Section **by May 4**.

## Orientation: Driven by Social Demand

New programs must align with the university's positioning and development plan and respond to talent demands arising from Guangdong's economic and social development, guided by social demand while optimizing the discipline and program structure. The Academic Affairs Office emphasized that new programs should be grounded in each school's actual conditions, balance teaching capacity with demand, and properly handle the relationships between quantity and quality, tradition and distinctiveness, short-term and long-term needs. Proposals should also highlight STU's educational characteristics and give full consideration to applicant pools and graduate employment rates. Where existing programs can meet social needs by broadening their directions, no new program should be added.

## Basic Requirements for Application

According to the announcement, applying for a new program requires meeting the following basic conditions:

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Social demand | Relatively stable demand for graduates; enrollment of at least 60 students per year in principle (special programs such as arts excepted) |
| Training plan | A program development plan, a training scheme aligned with objectives, and other necessary teaching documents |
| Discipline support | Support from related disciplines, with distinctive strengths, a complete curriculum, supporting textbooks, and an adequately structured faculty |
| Operating conditions | Necessary laboratories, equipment, library resources, internship sites and other basics |

## Materials and Timeline

Applicants must submit: the school's three-year discipline and program development plan; the National Application Form for Adding Undergraduate Programs; the professional training scheme; a feasibility report (including rationale, talent-demand forecast, market research, expert review and reviewer list); a faculty information table; a description of operating conditions; and supplementary materials.

The Academic Affairs Office noted that program establishment affects the whole cycle of enrollment, training and employment. Relevant schools should carefully survey applicant pools and the job market, ensure faculty strength can support the new program's teaching needs, and make sure submitted materials truthfully reflect the research process and results.

Printed materials must be bound in order into booklets — seven copies printed on A4 paper — and submitted together with electronic versions to the Teaching Section by May 4 (Contact: Jiang Songqi, Tel: 82902416).
