It is reported that the "Three Styles" Construction Office of Shantou University recently issued Study Atmosphere Inspection Report No. 194. During the 5th week of the spring semester of the 2011-2012 academic year, the study atmosphere inspection team conducted inspections of teaching buildings. The findings are reported as follows:

1. Inspection time: March 28, 2012 (Wednesday) at 8:00 AM; location: Building E; number of students arriving late (within 5 minutes): 2.

2. Inspection time: March 29, 2012 (Thursday) at 8:00 AM; location: Building E; number of students arriving late (within 5 minutes): 9; number of students arriving late (over 5 minutes): 1.

The names of students who failed to strictly observe school discipline have been reported to all colleges. Each college and residential college is urged to provide critical education to the relevant students and to persistently strengthen study atmosphere construction.

> Source: STU OA Notice ("Three Styles" Construction Office, Shantou University)
