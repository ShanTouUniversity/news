It is reported that Zhicheng College at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 5th "Physical Training Camp" received a highly positive response, with 96 students registering by the deadline—far exceeding the enrollment quota of 45. In accordance with the registration notice, the camp organizing committee conducted a computerized lottery on the evening of March 30 and selected 45 students, including Zhou Xiaoping, as campers.

All selected students are required to attend the kick-off ceremony and course orientation at 7:30 PM on April 6 in Room E204, Building E Teaching Block. The camp will feature approximately 20 kilometers of hiking; campers are advised to make appropriate preparations. The college thanks all participants and plans to continue offering the Physical Training Camp in future semesters. Students not selected are encouraged to留意 future college activity announcements.
