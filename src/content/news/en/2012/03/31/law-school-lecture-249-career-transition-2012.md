It is reported that the Law School at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Law School Lecture Series No. 249 will be held on April 11, 2012 (Wednesday) from 8:00 PM to 9:40 PM in Room 213, Law School Building.

The lecture topic is "How to Quickly Transition from Campus to Workplace." The speaker is Chen Litong, a partner at Shanghai Yuanda Law Firm (a strategic partner of McDermott Will & Emery, USA), licensed to practice in both China and New York State, member of the Economic Committee of the All-China Lawyers Association, Secretary-General of the Shanghai Economic and Commercial Mediation Center, and member of the Arbitration Association of Switzerland.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
