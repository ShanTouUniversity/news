It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice recruiting two part-time counselors open to current undergraduate students of the college.

The recruitment aims to implement the spirit of the CPC Central Committee and State Council's opinions on strengthening and improving college students' ideological and political education. Applicants must be senior undergraduate Party members (second year or above) of the College of Science. Compensation will follow university standards. Interested applicants should submit completed application forms to the College of Science Youth League Committee Office (Room 354, Science Building) by close of business on March 26, or send electronic versions to o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn. Interview time and location will be announced separately.
