It is reported that the Study and Learning Center (SLC) of Shantou University recently issued a notice that after the registration for the multimedia software introductory class closed, a total of 148 students had signed up. Based on the application forms, SLC selected 50 students to participate in the class, and the admission list is available in the attachment.

The SLC multimedia software introductory class provides students with入门 guidance from peer tutors. Students who wish to deepen their knowledge of multimedia software or seek one-on-one services may log in to the booking center (http://booking.stu.edu.cn/) to schedule appointments with tutors.
