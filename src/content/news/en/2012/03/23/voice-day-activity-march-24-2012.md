It is reported that the Shantou University Student Union recently issued a notice that the third "Voice Day" activity will be held on March 24 (Saturday) from 10:30 AM to 2:00 PM at Jin San Jiao (Golden Triangle).

The event, themed "Voice Day -- Listening to Your Voice," is open to all students. Booths will be set up at Jin San Jiao to receive student complaints and feedback, while also displaying student rights protection procedures and related information. The Student Union will address hot issues raised by students through face-to-face exchanges with the university president during the "President Reception Day," aiming to enhance communication between the university and students and promote solutions to concerns.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
