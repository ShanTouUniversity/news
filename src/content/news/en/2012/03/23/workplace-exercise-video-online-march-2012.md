It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that to facilitate faculty and students in learning workplace exercises, tutorial videos have been uploaded to the university video network. The link is available at: http://focus.stu.edu.cn/Viewer/MoveVideoInfo.aspx?SheetNo=2012032213251312.

Previously, the department issued an initiative to launch workplace exercise activities for all faculty and students, focusing on the ninth set of broadcast gymnastics published by the General Administration of Sport of China. Campus radio will play the gymnastics music during break time (9:50-10:10 AM) from Monday to Friday starting in the fifth week.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
