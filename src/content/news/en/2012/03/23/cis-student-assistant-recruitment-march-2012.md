It is reported that the Center for International Studies (CIS) at the College of Arts and Letters, Shantou University, recently issued a notice recruiting two student assistants open to all STU students.

Applicants must meet the following two requirements: (1) having completed at least two CIS general education courses, including Prof. Waiman Kwok's Integrative Thinking course and CIS Independent Study Projects; (2) having attained ELC Level 3. Interested applicants are requested to send their resumes to the designated email address with the subject line "CIS Student Assistant Application."
