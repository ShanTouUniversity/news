It is reported that Zhicheng College issued a notice on March 23, 2012, that the 12th Zhicheng Forum, themed “Innovation Changes Life,” has been rescheduled due to a change in the speaker’s itinerary.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | Zhicheng Forum No.12: Innovation Changes Life |
| New time | 16:00, March 25, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Room J-354, Zhicheng College (unchanged) |
| Check-in | Attendees with reservations are asked to enter by 15:50 |
| Organizer | Zhicheng College |

The college apologizes for any inconvenience caused by the change.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
