It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University is preparing for its eleventh Original Drama Night. Following strict review by the judging panel, "The Red Dress in the Wheat Field", first-prize winner of the script solicitation competition, has been selected as this year's stage play, and the production team is now openly recruiting actors across the university.

The original drama tradition at the college spans a decade. Over the past ten years, the College of Liberal Arts has presented ten drama productions to the STU community, including "The Thirty-first Step of Black Jack's Staircase", building "Liberal Arts Original Drama" into a widely recognized campus brand and drawing enthusiastic participation from student theater lovers.

Key arrangements for the recruitment are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Eligibility | All STU undergraduates and postgraduates; students from the College of Liberal Arts and those with stage experience are preferred |
| Registration deadline | March 23, 22:00 |
| How to apply | Submit a paper or electronic registration form |
| Interview time | March 24 (Saturday), 9:00–11:00 and 14:00–17:00 |
| Interview venue | Classrooms D403 and D404, Teaching Building D |

Paper registration forms may be submitted to Zhong WJ, Huang CF, Chen ZJ, Sun W or Fan JW; electronic forms may be sent to the organizing committee's email address. The registration form and play synopsis can be obtained from the College of Liberal Arts.
