It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that employees who chose the Affiliated Hospital No. 2 for health checkups are scheduled from March 28 to 31, with specific arrangements detailed in the attached table. Affected individuals are asked to arrive fasting at 8:00 AM on their scheduled date at the health checkup center of the Affiliated Hospital No. 2, bringing their own urine samples and antihypertensive medication if applicable.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
