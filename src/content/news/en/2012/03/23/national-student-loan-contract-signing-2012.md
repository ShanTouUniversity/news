It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on March 23, 2012, regarding the signing of contracts for the third batch of national student loans for the 2011–2012 academic year.

As notified by the Shantou Branch of the Bank of China, the signing is scheduled for 15:00 on March 27, 2012, in Room E101.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00, March 27, 2012 |
| Venue | Room E101 |
| Participants | Students on the list in the attachment |
| Required documents | ID card, student ID, Bank of China debit card or loan-integrated card, black pen, among others |
| Requirement | Arrive 15 minutes early to verify contracts and loan receipts |
| Phone | 0754-82904749 (Student Aid Management Center) |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

The list of participants is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The signing list is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
