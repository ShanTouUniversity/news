It is reported that the Student Affairs Office announced on March 23, 2012, the results of the inquiry review for the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Program. The review concluded on March 22, with eight projects advancing.

## Results and Next Steps

The list of advancing projects is available in the OA attachment.

| Stage | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Field survival camp | April 2–4 on Nan’ao Island, with members of the eight teams and teachers who passed the fitness test |
| Team size | Maximum 6 members per team; adjust as required, recruiting from students who passed the fitness test if needed |
| Coaching | 19:30, March 28, Room 726, 2F, Building 789 |
| Public defense | 19:30, April 11, Science Lecture Hall, with PPT presentations by the eight teams and open voting by all students |
| Requirement | Participants must attend each stage on time; absence is considered forfeiture |

Questions may be directed to expedition@stu.edu.cn. The full list is in the OA attachment.
