It was reported that the Communist Youth League Committee at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the public service course SER3015 "Shuguang Volunteer Teaching Service Learning Course" will organize students to conduct a field study at the service site on March 11, 2012.

To ensure the smooth progress of the field study, a communication meeting is scheduled from 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM on March 9 in classroom E207. Students registered for this course (five classes in total) are required to attend on time. Old volunteers who participated in the Shuguang助学 Project teaching service during the fall semester of 2011 are exempt from attending this meeting.

For any inquiries, please send an email to stusgren@163.com.
