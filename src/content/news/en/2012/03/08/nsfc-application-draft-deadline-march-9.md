It was reported that the Research Office at Shantou University recently issued an urgent notice stating that strict application limits apply to the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) projects, and applications exceeding the limits will not be accepted.

The NSFC over-limit inspection system will close on March 10. Faculty who have not yet submitted the initial draft of their application must send the inspected and protected draft (with confirmed research team members) to cszhu@stu.edu.cn before the close of business on March 9. Submissions after the deadline will not be accepted.
