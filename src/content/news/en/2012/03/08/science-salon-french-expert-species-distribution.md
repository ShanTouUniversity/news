It was reported that the "Science Salon" series of academic lectures hosted by the College of Science at Shantou University announced recently that Professor Soven LEK from the University of Toulouse, France, will deliver an academic report at the Science and Technology Center on the afternoon of March 9.

## Report Information

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Title | Models Predicting Species Distributions in the Changing World |
| Time | 3:30 PM - 5:00 PM, Saturday, March 9, 2012 |
| Venue | Room 205, Science and Technology Center |
| Speaker | Professor Soven LEK, Distinguished Professor, University of Toulouse, France |

## About the Speaker

Professor Soven LEK is a distinguished French professor and a renowned fish ecologist. He currently serves as Director of the Laboratory of Evolution and Biodiversity at the University of Toulouse, Dean of the Doctoral School of Life Sciences, Chairman of the Ecology Expert Committee, and Head of Asian Affairs at the International Office.

He has been engaged in fish community ecology research for a long time and enjoys considerable international reputation in the field of ecological modeling. The method he pioneered of "using artificial neural networks to establish ecological data models" has laid an important foundation for his position in fish ecology research and ecological model research. He has published over 100 papers in internationally prestigious journals such as Nature, Ecology Letters, Journal of Applied Ecology, and Conservation Biology.
