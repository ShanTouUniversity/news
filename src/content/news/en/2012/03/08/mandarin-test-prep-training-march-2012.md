It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced pre-test training for the March 2012 Mandarin Proficiency Test (Putonghua Shuiping Ceshi), to be held on March 11 on campus.

Due to high enrollment, the training is divided into three sessions to ensure quality:

| Session | Start Time |
|---------|-----------|
| Batch 1 | 8:00 AM |
| Batch 2 | 10:00 AM |
| Batch 3 | 2:00 PM |

Venue: E-Hall 101
