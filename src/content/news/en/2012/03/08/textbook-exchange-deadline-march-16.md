It was reported that the Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice that SDS Bookstore will close its textbook pickup and return services for the current semester on March 16.

Faculty and students who have not yet picked up their textbooks, as well as those needing to return textbooks, are advised to complete the relevant procedures before the deadline. No further processing will be accepted after the deadline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
