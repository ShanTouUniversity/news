It was reported that the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently announced the official launch of the 2012 routine health examination for employed staff. In accordance with the spirit of the president's office meeting, the scope of this year's health checkup has been expanded to include all retired employees.

The examination venues remain the same as previous years: the University Hospital, the First Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University, and the Second Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University. Faculty and staff may voluntarily choose their preferred hospital. Health checkups for auxiliary contract workers will be conducted at the University Hospital without prior registration.

Each department is requested to submit the completed and stamped "Health Examination Registration Form" to the Healthcare Section of the University Hospital before March 14, along with an electronic version sent to o_bjk@stu.edu.cn. Contact number: 82902318.
