It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently announced the 119th session of its Engineering Research Academic Forum.

Title: Vibration Balancing and Lubrication — Practical Approaches to Extending Equipment Service Life

Speaker: Li Chuan, Postdoctoral Researcher, Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Ottawa

Date & Time: Friday, March 9, 2012, 11:00 AM–12:00 PM

Venue: Engineering College Academic Exchange Room

Faculty and students from the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering department and all interested students are welcome to attend.
