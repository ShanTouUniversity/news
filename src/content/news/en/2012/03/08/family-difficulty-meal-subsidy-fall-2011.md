It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced the distribution of a temporary meal subsidy for economically disadvantaged undergraduate students, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance notice (Yue Cai Jiao [2011] No. 591).

The provincial government has allocated a one-time subsidy fund of 390,000 RMB to help ease the financial pressure on students caused by rising prices.

Eligibility: Full-time undergraduate students from economically disadvantaged families
Duration: 5 months (fall 2011 semester)
Amount: 60 RMB/month × 5 months = 300 RMB per student
Disbursement: One-time payment to the recipient's campus card account

Application process:
Students wishing to apply must register online at the STU scholarship website (http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn/chinese/applyIntro2.aspx) by 8:00 AM on March 19, 2012. After submission, students can log in to the Student Affairs System to confirm their application. For questions, email s_xtchen1@stu.edu.cn or call 82904749.
