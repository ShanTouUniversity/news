It was reported that the Academic Exchange Service Center under the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently announced the launch of the 2012 Spring Discount Banquet starting from March 12, while the previous discount banquet service will be simultaneously discontinued.

The service is available to faculty, staff, and students holding a valid "One-Card." For reservations, please call the hotline: extensions 2668 and 4138 on campus, or 82905103 and 82905104 off campus.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
