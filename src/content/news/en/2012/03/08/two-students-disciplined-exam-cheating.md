It was reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued the 2012 First Bulletin on Student Disciplinary Violations.

The bulletin revealed that Chen M-mou, a 2011-level student majoring in Journalism and Communication at the Yangtze River News and Communication College, was caught bringing and using exam-related materials during the "Introduction to Journalism" course examination on January 9. Luo M, a 2010-level student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at the College of Engineering, was similarly caught bringing related materials into the "Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics" course examination on January 12.

According to relevant regulations, the university has decided to issue warnings to both students, and the scores for the courses in which they cheated will be recorded as zero.
