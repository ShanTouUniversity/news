It was reported that the Physical Education Department at Shantou University recently announced that the 13th Student Sports Festival will be held from March 24 to April 29, 2012 at various athletic venues across campus.

This year's sports festival, guided by the spirit of thrift and economy, aims to be civilized, enthusiastic, exciting, and successful. The general competition rules, individual event regulations, and registration forms have been distributed. All colleges and residential colleges are encouraged to actively organize teams to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
