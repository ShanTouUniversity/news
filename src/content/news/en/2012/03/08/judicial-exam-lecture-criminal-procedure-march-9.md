It was reported that the Law School at Shantou University recently announced that Yang Yang, an exclusive签约 teacher from Zhonghe Education, will deliver a judicial exam preparation lecture on March 9 from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM at the STU Moot Court. The lecture topic is "Amendment to the Criminal Procedure Law."

Dr. Yang is a doctoral graduate from China University of Political Science and Law, specializing in civil and criminal procedure law. His teaching style is lively and emphasizes graphical organization of knowledge points, with a keen ability to grasp examination trends. Materials such as "Preparing for the Judicial Exam: Methods First" will be distributed free of charge at the event. There will also be a lucky draw, with the first prize being a set of "2012 Real Exam Classification Interpretation - Five Volumes."
