It was reported that the Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice stating that the "Pipa Performance" course (Course ID: 52904), originally scheduled for periods 8-9 on Friday, March 9 in classroom STU213, will be suspended once due to Teacher Yang Qing from the Art Education Center's business trip for a conference. The make-up class time is yet to be determined.

Students enrolled in this course are advised to inform each other and stay tuned for subsequent arrangements regarding the make-up session.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
