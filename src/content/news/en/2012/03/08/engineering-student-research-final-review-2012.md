It was reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to monitor the progress of student extracurricular research projects approved in 2011, the College's Student Science and Technology Innovation Activity Leading Group has decided to conduct final reviews for projects that have passed the mid-term inspection.

Project leaders are required to submit both physical and electronic versions of their final reports to their respective department Youth League committees by March 15. Each department's Youth League committee must consolidate and submit all reports to the Youth League Committee office by 11:30 AM on March 15. The review work will be conducted within each student's home department, rather than through a college-wide defense session. Projects that fail to participate in the final review as stipulated will be considered as voluntarily withdrawn.
