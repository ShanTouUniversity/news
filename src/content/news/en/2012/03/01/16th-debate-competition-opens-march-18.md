It is reported that the Youth League Committee and Student Union of Shantou University jointly announced the launch of the 16th STU University Debate Competition, themed "Truth · The Strongest Voice." The opening ceremony will be held on March 18, 2012.

The schedule is as follows:

| Date | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Feb 26 – Mar 11 | Debate team registration |
| Mar 10 – Mar 18 | Topic征集活动 |
| Mar 18 | Opening ceremony: "Three Strong Contending the Debate Arena" |
| Mar 24 – Apr 15 | Official debate matches |
| Apr 7 | "STU Time" forum |

Registration: Send email to 11ljhuang1@stu.edu.cn or via the STU Academic Planning Department's Sina Weibo.

|------|---------|
