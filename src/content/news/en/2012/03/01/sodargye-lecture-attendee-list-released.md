It is reported that Chizheng College at Shantou University recently published the list of successfully registered attendees for the 11th session of its lecture series titled "The Root of Happiness is in the Mind."

The lecture was held on the evening of March 2, 2012, at 7:30 PM in the New Library Auditorium, featuring renowned scholar Khen Sodargye as the speaker. The college reminded all attendees to bring their campus cards and enter the venue before 7:20 PM for card scanning.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Lecture Theme | The Root of Happiness is in the Mind |
| Date & Time | Saturday, March 2, 2012, 19:30 |
| Venue | New Library Auditorium |
| Entry Requirement | Campus card required, enter by 19:20 |
| Organizer | Chizheng College |
