It is reported that the wind ensemble of the Arts Education Center at Shantou University's Changjiang College of Art and Design won second prize in the instrumental music category at the 3rd National College Student Art Performance held in Hangzhou, Zhejiang on February 11, 2012.

The competition, held triennially, brought together 30 ensembles from 28 universities across China, including Peking University, Tsinghua University, Fudan University, and Zhejiang University. STU's ensemble was the only group composed entirely of non-specialty students, and earned consistent recognition from judges for their performance of the wind ensemble piece "Legend of the Ancient Hero."

To prepare for the competition, ensemble members and instructors returned to campus early during the winter break and underwent a nine-day intensive training starting February 1. Over the half-month period from training to the competition in Hangzhou, the ensemble members overcame various difficulties, supported and learned from one another, improved their overall playing ability, and strengthened team cohesion, ultimately delivering an outstanding performance that received high praise from the judges.

Students expressed that they felt honored to represent the university at a national-level competition and that observing other teams' performances broadened their musical horizons.
