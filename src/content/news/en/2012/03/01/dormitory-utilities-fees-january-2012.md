It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently published the January 2012 dormitory water and electricity fees for students.

Meter readings cover January 1–18. Two winter vacation meter reads were conducted on February 7 and February 23. Students can check their bills at the university's utility management system (http://power.stu.edu.cn/) using their campus network credentials. If any discrepancies are found, please fill out a "Utility Fee Verification Application" and submit it to the Water and Electricity Center (next to the Science Building, Tel: 2489) between March 2 and March 8. For any issues regarding dorm assignments, contact the counselor office. Auto-deduction via campus card will begin on March 9. Normal payment period is March 9–13.
