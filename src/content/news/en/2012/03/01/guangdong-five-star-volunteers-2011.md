It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently announced that three of its students have been recognized as 2011 Guangdong Province Five-Star Volunteers by the Guangdong Provincial Volunteer Federation.

The recognized students are:

| Name | Year | Major |
|------|------|-------|
| Li Lianjian | 2008 cohort | Computer Science and Technology |
| Yu Shengyang | 2008 cohort | Electronic Information Engineering |
| Ma Guangcheng | 2008 cohort | Electronic Information Engineering |

The Youth League Committee encouraged the newly recognized volunteers to remain humble and continue their excellent service. It also called on all student volunteers to learn from their example and actively contribute to social development.
