It is reported that the "211 Project" Office of Shantou University recently announced the launch of the review process for the 9th round of Guangdong Province Higher Education Key Discipline application materials.

Two review teams have been established: the overall review team, led by Vice President Wen Xiaobo, with members including heads of each applying discipline; and the basic data review team, comprising representatives from the Research Office, Personnel Office, Graduate School, Equipment Office, and Academic Affairs Office, with the 211 Office as the lead unit.

The university emphasized that key discipline applications are crucial to institutional development. Review units are urged to work responsibly and ensure that no errors in basic content or data could jeopardize any discipline's application. Due to resource constraints, the overall review team will conduct form-level reviews only, focusing on obvious issues in the submitted materials.
