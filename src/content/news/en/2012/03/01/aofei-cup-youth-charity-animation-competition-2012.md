It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University forwarded a notice on March 1, 2012, regarding the Shantou City Aofei Cup Youth Charity Animation Competition.

To solemnly commemorate the 90th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Youth League and to encourage young people to champion innovation, municipal authorities including the Publicity Department of the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee, the Youth League Committee, the Education Bureau, Shantou Special Economic Zone Newspaper and the Radio and Television Station will jointly hold the competition to promote moral education through internet and animation technologies.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | Shantou City Aofei Cup Youth Charity Animation Competition |
| Organizers | Publicity Department of CPC Shantou Municipal Committee, Youth League Committee, Education Bureau, Shantou Special Economic Zone Newspaper, Radio and Television Station |
| How to apply | Register on the official website (Shantou Youth League http://www.styouth.com or Chaoren Network http://dm.crtv.hk) with real and valid information, confirm registration and submit works as required |
| Deadline | Before April 30, 2012 |
| Eligible participants | Individuals and organizations interested in participating |
| Forwarded by | Youth League Committee of Shantou University |
