It is reported that the third edition of the "STU Consumer Culture Investigation Report Award," organized under the Student Community X Plan, has entered its midterm interview phase.

Since November 2011, participating student groups have conducted preliminary research and clarified their investigation directions through plan writing workshops. To further help students deepen their understanding of their projects, Mr. Wang Guancheng and Mr. He Qinghui from the Education Department of the Hong Kong Consumer Council, along with Mr. Liu Weide from the Lee Shau Kee Foundation, will conduct midterm interviews with each group from March 2 to March 4.

Students are asked to attend their scheduled interviews according to the timetable. For any questions or schedule changes, please contact the group advisor directly.
