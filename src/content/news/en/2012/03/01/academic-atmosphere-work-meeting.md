It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced a special meeting on academic atmosphere building to be held on March 1, 2012.

The meeting will bring together party secretaries of each college's student affairs and relevant leaders from the Student Affairs Office.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | Academic Atmosphere Building |
| Date & Time | Thursday, March 1, 2012, 15:00 |
| Venue | Room 311, Xingdong Building |
| Attendees | Party secretaries of colleges' student affairs, Student Affairs Office leaders |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
