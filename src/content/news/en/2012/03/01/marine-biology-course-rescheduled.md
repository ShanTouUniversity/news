It is reported that the Office of Academic Affairs at Shantou University has announced a class adjustment: because instructor Zheng Huaiping of the Marine Biology Research Institute is away attending a conference, the course Introduction to Marine Biological Resources (class number 51365), originally set for periods 3-4 on Tuesday of week 2 (March 6) in Room 303 of Building E, will be suspended for that session.

## Make-up Session Arrangement

| Item | Original Session | Make-up Session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time | March 6 (Tuesday), periods 3-4 | March 8 (Thursday), periods 3-4 |
| Venue | Room 303, Building E | Room 504, Building D |

The Office of Academic Affairs asks students enrolled in the course to spread the word and attend the make-up session at the new location on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Academic Affairs)
