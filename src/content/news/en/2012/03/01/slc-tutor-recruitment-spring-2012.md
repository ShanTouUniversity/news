It is reported that the Student Learning Center (SLC) at Shantou University recently announced the recruitment of tutors for the spring 2012 semester.

Tutor recruitment is led by student coordinators from each department, and faculty members are encouraged to recommend outstanding students. Requirements for tutors include:

1. Current undergraduate students at the main campus;
2. Strong sense of responsibility, serious work attitude, and good communication skills;
3. Excellent academic performance, with grades in the served course ranking in the top 20% of the class. Special circumstances require a certificate from the course instructor.

Tutor responsibilities include: promoting independent learning, sharing study methods and experiences, patiently answering students' questions, organizing and guiding academic discussions, understanding students' course progress and needs, and proactively providing appropriate support.

The general working period is weeks 3–16 of each semester, 2 hours per week, with compensation according to the university's work-study program standards.
