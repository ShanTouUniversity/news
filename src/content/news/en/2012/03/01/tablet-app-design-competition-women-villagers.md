It is reported that the Women's Research Center at the College of Liberal Arts, Shantou University, has launched a tablet software design competition titled "Apps for Women Learning," inviting all enrolled students to submit creative proposals.

The competition theme focuses on designing tablet software for female village officials. Design directions include: health knowledge, agricultural knowledge, an online interactive community for women village officials, official document templates, and other tools to assist women village officials in their work and learning.

Requirements: Android 3.0 or above tablets. Eligible participants are cross-disciplinary teams of current undergraduate or graduate students, with 3–6 members per team.

Key dates:
- By March 12: Submit registration form and software design proposal to cws@stu.edu.cn
- March 16: Top 5 proposals and teams selected; each team receives a tablet for development
- April 16: Final software submission
- April 20: Award ceremony and exhibition of outstanding designs

The competition advisor is Professor Chen Biru, Associate Dean of the Design Department at the Changjiang College of Art and Design.
