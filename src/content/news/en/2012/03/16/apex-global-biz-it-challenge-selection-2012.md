In a recent notice, the Business School of Shantou University announced that it would select students to participate in the 2012 APEX Global Biz-IT Challenge, with selections taking place in March.

Eligible applicants must be second-, third-, or fourth-year undergraduate students majoring in Computer Science from the Business School or Engineering College. They must have completed or be currently enrolled in Business School courses such as Management Information Systems, Business Intelligence, Economics, or Enterprise Management. Applicants must maintain a GPA of at least 3.0 and an average score of no less than 80 in ELC courses.

Registration is open from March 16 to 19. Interested students should register via the "International Case Competition Registration" link on the Business School website. Shortlisted candidates will receive interview notifications.

The APEX Global Biz-IT Challenge is an international competition for university students worldwide, aiming to cultivate cross-disciplinary thinking and team collaboration skills.
