The Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University recently announced that due to a business trip by Professor Zheng Huaiping of the Marine Biology Research Institute, the Marine Biology Resources course (Course No. 51365), originally scheduled for Tuesday, March 20, Periods 3-4 in Room E303, has been temporarily suspended.

After coordination by the Academic Affairs Office, the make-up class will be held on Thursday, March 22, Periods 3-4, in Room D504. Students enrolled in the course are advised to note the schedule change and attend the make-up session on time.
