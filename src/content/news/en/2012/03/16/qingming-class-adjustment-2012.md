The Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University recently announced that, in accordance with the school's Qingming holiday schedule, the class adjustment arrangement for undergraduates on the main campus is as follows: classes are suspended on Wednesday, April 4; the classes scheduled for Monday, April 2 and Saturday, March 31 are swapped; and the classes scheduled for Tuesday, April 3 and Sunday, April 1 are swapped.

All colleges are asked to implement the adjustment work in accordance with the unified deployment of the Academic Affairs Office to ensure smooth implementation of the teaching plan during the Qingming holiday period.
