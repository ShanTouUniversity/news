The Human Resources Office at Shantou University recently announced that, to help all faculty and staff—especially newly hired employees—better understand the school's management systems, the university has decided to undertake a comprehensive revision of the STU Employee Handbook.

The notice requests that each relevant unit select its own rules and regulations for inclusion in the handbook and provide valuable opinions and suggestions. The catalogue of rules and regulations intended for inclusion should be submitted to Room 416, Xingdong Building, Human Resources Office, by March 27, with an electronic version sent to psysu@stu.edu.cn.
