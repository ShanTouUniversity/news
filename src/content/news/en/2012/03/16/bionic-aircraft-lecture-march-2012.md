In a recent notice, the Engineering College of Shantou University announced that Dr. Zhang Xingwei, a doctoral researcher in Fluid Machinery and Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, would deliver the 121st Lecture of the Engineering Research Academic Forum on March 19 from 10:00 to 11:00 a.m. in the Engineering College Academic Exchange Room.

The lecture, titled "Numerical Study on Aerodynamic Characteristics of Flapping Wings of Bionic Micro Air Vehicles," focuses on numerical simulation and analysis of flapping wing aerodynamic performance in the field of bionic flight vehicles. Faculty and students from the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering department and those interested in the field are welcomed to attend.
