In a recent notice, the Engineering College of Shantou University announced that the 2012 Application for the China Scholarship Council (CSC) Excellent Undergraduate International Exchange Program had officially commenced.

Five selection spots have been approved for four majors in the Engineering College that are part of the "Excellent Engineer Training Program." Accepted students will study at the University of Calgary in Canada for one year starting this autumn.

Selection criteria include: Chinese citizenship, love for the motherland, good health, and no record of disciplinary violations; applicants must be full-time second-year undergraduates in the Engineering College (Class of 2010); outstanding academic and moral standing with professional grades ranking in the top 10%; and English proficiency meeting IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 95 (the University of Calgary requires IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 83).

The scholarship covers one round-trip international airfare and stipend for living expenses as prescribed by the CSC.
