In a recent notice, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced that the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Expedition physical fitness test would be held on March 17-18, divided into team and individual components.

The team test starts from the track field, passing through the Motto Stone, Building E, the Fourth Cafeteria, and the Wind Tunnel Laboratory, then follows the roadway to the reservoir, circles around the reservoir through the woods to the beach opposite ACC, and finishes at the College of Art. The individual test includes shuttle sprint 4x50 meters, one-minute push-ups, and one-minute sit-ups. Faculty members were required to complete a 5000-meter timed run.

The notice specified that the test would proceed regardless of weather conditions, with no makeup opportunities provided. It also emphasized that individuals with heart disease, asthma, hypertension, anemia, or other medical conditions were strictly prohibited from participating.
