The Resources Management Office at Shantou University recently announced that, based on work requirements and after discussion and approval by the office with concurrence from the university's supervising leadership, Liu Hansheng has been appointed as director of the Medical Technology Department at the University Hospital.

This appointment aims to further strengthen the medical technology workforce at the university hospital, enhance the level of medical technical services, and better safeguard the physical health of all faculty, staff, and students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
