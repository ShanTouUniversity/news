In a recent notice, the Law School of Shantou University announced the interview list for the 2012 Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship program, with 40 students advancing to the interview stage.

Twenty-two students from the Public Management department and 18 students from the Law department will attend the interview on March 19. The Public Management department interviews begin at 8:30 a.m., while the Law department interviews begin at 2:00 p.m. Both are held in Room 206 of the Law School. Each interview lasts approximately 10 minutes and proceeds in the order listed.

The Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship program aims to provide law school students with practical opportunities in Hong Kong's legal and related fields, helping them broaden their international perspective and enhance their professional skills.
