In a recent notice, the Resources Management Office of Shantou University announced that the February 2012 office phone electronic bills for all units had been uploaded to the "Phone Management System." Unit verification personnel are urged to log in to http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn/ to review their bills.

Bills with no objections should be confirmed by clicking "Confirm Selected Bills." If discrepancies are found between the bill details and actual usage, an email should be sent to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn by March 23. Bills that neither raise objections nor receive confirmation will be automatically deemed confirmed by the system. After verification is complete, the Finance Office will deduct charges from the designated fund accounts on March 26.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
