The School of Arts at Shantou University recently announced that the STU Qipu Plan tablet software design competition received a total of 45 software design proposals. After careful review by the evaluation panel, 8 team projects ultimately advanced to the finals.

The finalist teams include: Infinity Team (work "Data Village"), lv7 Team (work "showU"), MT-Viewer Team (work "Qipu Online"), Dacha Fan Team, Einstein Group (work "Contacts + FT"), and 5+? Team (work "Simple Pass"), among others.
