In a recent notice, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced that the opening ceremony of the 16th STU University Debating Competition would be held on March 18 from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. at the Science Report Hall.

This year's competition, themed "Three Powers Compete for Supremacy in the Debate Arena," features a unique three-sided debate format. Former outstanding debaters are invited to participate as competitors, with faculty members serving as judges. The opening debate topic is "In university education, the most critical aspect is enhancing students' moral cultivation, knowledge level, and practical ability."

Awards include the winning side and best debater selected by the judges, the most popular debater voted by the audience, and prizes for audience members who ask questions during the event.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
