In a recent notice, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced that the electronic registration images for the 2012 and 2013 graduating undergraduate classes had been uploaded to CHSI (http://www.chsi.com.cn/). Students who previously participated in the graduation photo collection are urged to log in to CHSI and click the "Degree Electronic Registration Image Verification" link to carefully review their graduation photos.

The university's verification period will be completed before March 31. Students who fail to verify within the deadline and suffer consequences from incorrect photo information will be held responsible. Students who had their photos taken at non-university collection sites must also verify their information online and submit their printed photos to the Academic Affairs Office Student Services Section by the end of April.

Graduate electronic registration images are an essential component of degree certificate electronic registration, and ensuring accuracy is crucial for graduates to obtain their degree certificates smoothly.
