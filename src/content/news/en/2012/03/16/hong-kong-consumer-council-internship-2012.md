In a recent notice, the Law School of Shantou University announced that the Hong Kong Consumer Council would accept two 2010-level Public Management students for an internship from July 5 to August 1, 2012.

During the internship, students will participate in the preparatory work for the 13th Hong Kong Consumer Culture Research Report Award Ceremony. After the internship, they will also be required to continue supporting the organizing committee for one year. The primary working language is Cantonese, and overtime work may be required on weekends or holidays.

The school will provide a scholarship covering round-trip travel, accommodation, and living allowances. Applicants must submit their application forms to cdclaw@stu.edu.cn by March 20.

The selection process consists of an initial screening (50%) and an interview (50%). The initial screening evaluates academic performance, service practice experience, and event planning capabilities. The interview assesses work attitude, personality, communication skills, Cantonese proficiency, and understanding of the Consumer Culture Research Report Award.
