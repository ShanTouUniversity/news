It is reported that the Personnel Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice publishing the list of Shantou University recipients selected for the 2012 Guangdong Provincial University Talent Introduction Program.

The Guangdong Provincial University Talent Introduction Program aims to attract outstanding high-level talents to join provincial universities and is an important initiative to strengthen faculty development in Guangdong's higher education sector. The announcement of Shantou University's selected recipients reflects positive progress in the university's talent recruitment efforts and will further enhance the institution's teaching and research capabilities.
