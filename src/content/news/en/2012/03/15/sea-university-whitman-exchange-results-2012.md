It is reported that the STU Student Affairs Office recently announced the results of the 2012 "Sea University" and Whitman College exchange program selection. After initial review and interviews of 19 applicants by the review panel, the following students were selected:

| Program | Student | College / Major | Year | Departure |
|---------|---------|-----------------|------|-----------|
| Sea University Scholarship | Jin XY | College of Arts and Letters / English | 2010 | Fall 2012 semester |
| Sea University Scholarship | Lin JQ | College of Engineering / Mechatronic Engineering | 2010 | Spring 2013 semester |
| Whitman College Exchange | PYM | College of Law / Public Administration | 2010 | Fall 2013 |

Jin XY and Lin JQ will respectively participate in the 2012 Fall and 2013 Spring semesters of the "Sea University" program. PYM will spend a half-year exchange study at Whitman College in the United States beginning in Fall 2013.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Student Affairs Office)
