It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice, in accordance with the "Provisional Regulations on Credit-Based Student Status Management for Undergraduates at Shantou University," officially launching the 2011 undergraduate major transfer program.

The notice specifies that each major must publish, before the fifth week of each semester, the number of transferred undergraduate students it can accept and the specific requirements for incoming students. All majors are required to complete the "2011 Undergraduate Major Transfer Acceptance Form" and submit it to the Student Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office by Wednesday of the fourth week. The Academic Affairs Office will then announce to students the number of available positions and specific requirements for each major.

The major transfer program provides students with more flexible academic choices, and those interested in applying are advised to closely monitor the specific requirements and deadlines for each department.
