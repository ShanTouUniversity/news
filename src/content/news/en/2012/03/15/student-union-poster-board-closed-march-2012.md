It is reported that the STU Communist Youth League Committee recently announced that, due to renovation of the posters boards, the STU Student Union Office will suspend applications for the posters boards at the 2nd and 3rd cafeterias and the ABCDEF dormitory areas starting March 15. The date for resuming applications will be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Communist Youth League Committee)
