In a recent notice, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the launch of candidate recommendations for the 2011 Shantou City Outstanding Youth League honors, in commemoration of the 93rd anniversary of the May Fourth Movement.

According to the deployment by the Shantou Municipal Committee of the Communist Youth League, the 2011 Shantou City Top Ten Grassroots Youth League Secretaries, Outstanding Youth League Cadres, Outstanding Youth League Members, and May Fourth Red Flag Youth League Organizations will be collectively honored during this year's "May Fourth" period. The initiative aims to inspire young students to carry forward the spirit of the May Fourth Movement and promote grassroots youth league organization building.

The notice requested all college Youth League committees and directly affiliated Youth League branches to organize the recommendation process in accordance with the document requirements, and submit the recommended candidates and supporting materials to the University Youth League Committee Office by March 23.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
