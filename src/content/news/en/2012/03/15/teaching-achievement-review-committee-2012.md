It is reported that the STU Academic Affairs Office recently announced the formation of the 2011 STU Teaching Achievement Award Review Committee to conduct the review of teaching achievement awards for 2011.

The review committee is composed of the following members:

| Position | Name |
|----------|------|
| Chair | Wang Weilian |
| Members | Du Hong, Ma Fengqi, Huang Xiaochun, Feng Shang, Chen Yuefen, Han Ran, Huang Yan, Yan Zhigang, Sun Haojun |

The committee is responsible for ensuring a fair, just, and transparent review process for the 2011 campus-level teaching achievement awards.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
