It is reported that the STU Communist Youth League Committee recently announced that, to enrich campus cultural life and reflect the university's spirit of respecting and caring for female students, the STU Student Union will host the 8th STU Women's Day Festival.

The series of events will take place from March 14 to April 8, 2012, featuring the following activities:

| Activity | Dates | Location / Notes |
|----------|-------|-----------------|
| "Heartfelt Wishes" | March 14 – April 8 | Golden Triangle Wish Tree |
| "Love on a Unicycle" | March 19 – 21 | Male students give female students rides |
| "Self-Defense Taekwondo Women's Session" | March 24 – 25 | Registration: March 19 – 23 |
| "Her in My Eyes" Photo Contest & Voting | March 19 – 31 | Photo submissions and voting |
| "Love Express Bus" | March 29 | Breakfast delivery for female students |
| "Messages from Above" | During the festival | See on-site notices |

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Communist Youth League Committee)
