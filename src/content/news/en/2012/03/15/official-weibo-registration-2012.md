It is reported that the Publicity Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee recently issued a notice stating that, in order to meet the needs of external publicity in the internet age and strengthen information monitoring, all units that have opened and certified official Weibo accounts are required to report their information to the University Publicity Department for registration and filing; units planning to open Weibo accounts should also report in a timely manner.

All units must complete the attached form by March 30, send the electronic version to o_xcb@stu.edu.cn, and submit one stamped paper copy to the Publicity Department office (Room 402, Xingdong). Contact person: Chen Laoshi; Telephone: 2343.

This filing initiative marks an important step in standardizing new media management at Shantou University, helping to coordinate the university's online publicity resources and improve external communication efficiency.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee)
