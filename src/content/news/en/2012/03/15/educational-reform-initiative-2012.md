It is reported that STU recently issued the "Notice on Vigorously Advancing Educational Reform Across Campus." The document noted that in 2011, STU's independent operation comprehensive reform pilot was officially designated as a provincial comprehensive reform pilot project. The provincial Party committee and government expect the university to become a world-renowned, domestically leading high-level university by 2017.

Mr. Li Ka-shing has set three goals for the university to achieve within five years: cultivating first-class undergraduate students nationally, building the STU Medical College into one of the top five medical schools in China, and establishing an advanced educational system of world standard.

The notice requires all units to thoroughly study the spirit of national and provincial education reform outlines, and in conjunction with the university's implementation guidelines for independent operation comprehensive reform and President Gu Peihua's speeches on advanced undergraduate education and teaching reform, to actively research, formulate, and implement educational reform measures within their respective units.
