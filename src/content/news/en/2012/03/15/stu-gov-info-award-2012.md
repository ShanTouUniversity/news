It is reported that STU was recently honored with the title of "Advanced Unit in 2011 Shantou Municipal Government Information Work." The Shantou Municipal Government Office conducted the evaluation based on the quantity and quality of information submissions from various units throughout 2011, recognizing outstanding organizations and individuals.

The evaluation was organized by the Shantou Municipal Government Office, using the adoption rate of government information reports in 2011 as the primary assessment criterion, and commended advanced units and individuals who made positive contributions to government information work.
