It is reported that the STU Party and Government Office recently announced the 2012 Qingming Festival holiday schedule. The holiday runs from April 2 to 4, totaling three days. Makeup workdays are scheduled for March 31 (Saturday) and April 1 (Sunday). Teaching arrangements will be made by the Academic Affairs Office and the Graduate School.

The notice also reminded all units to inspect safety facilities in their offices before the holiday and to close windows, doors, and water and power sources. The Party and Government Office duty phone number is 8290 2305.

| Item | Schedule |
|------|----------|
| Qingming Holiday | April 2 – 4 (3 days) |
| Makeup Workdays | March 31 (Saturday), April 1 (Sunday) |
| Duty Phone | 8290 2305 |
