It is reported that the STU Library recently announced that, in furtherance of the China Academic Library and Information System (CALIS) Dissertation Project, a "Chinese Dissertation Document Delivery Discount Activity" will be held across Chinese universities from March 12 to April 13, 2012. During the activity period, any full-text request for Chinese dissertations successfully retrieved through the CALIS Dissertation Center Service System or CALIS-eRead and submitted via the CALIS Document Delivery System will receive full subsidy.

The system contains over 3.84 million doctoral and master's dissertation records, including approximately 1.72 million Chinese dissertations, primarily sourced from more than 80 major universities across the country.
