It is reported that the STU College of Arts and Letters recently announced that Associate Professor Wen Bing of the Foreign Languages Department will hold a lecture titled "Weaving Your Dreams — Academic and Career Planning for English Majors" on the evening of March 17 from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. in Room 204, E-Step Lecture Hall.

The lecture aims to help English majors clarify their learning goals, stimulate their enthusiasm for study, and address common concerns regarding the postgraduate entrance exam in English, studying abroad, and employment directions. The college welcomes English majors, students considering switching to the English major, and those interested in pursuing careers related to English.
