It is reported that the CPC Shantou University Committee recently issued a notice distributing the "2012 Party Building Work Points of the CPC Shantou University Committee" and the "2012 Work Points for Deepening the Advanced Creation Activity of the CPC Shantou University Committee."

The notice requests the Medical College Party Committee, all general Party branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches to combine the actual conditions of their respective units and earnestly implement the work points.

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Committee)
