It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 STU Master\'s Graduate Admissions Work Meeting will be held on April 1 (Sunday) at 4:15 PM in Room 401, Building G Teaching Block.

The meeting will convey the spirit of the provincial education examination authority\'s graduate admissions work conference, review each discipline\'s admissions progress and existing issues, and deploy the 2012 master\'s graduate admissions work including复试, medical examinations, and proposed admission lists. Responsible persons for graduate work at all master\'s program disciplines and relevant staff handling graduate admissions matters are requested to attend on time.
