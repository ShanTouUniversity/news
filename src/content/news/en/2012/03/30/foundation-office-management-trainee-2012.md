It is reported that the STU Foundation Office is openly recruiting two management trainees, with applications due by April 13, 2012.

## Qualifications

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Age | Under 30, in good health |
| Education | Master's degree or outstanding bachelor's degree, any major |
| Skills | Strong Chinese and English writing, organizational and communication skills; good English proficiency; adept at Excel data processing; able to work independently |
| Attributes | Diligent, hardworking, collaborative, with strong teamwork, innovative mindset, and dedication |

## Responsibilities

- Assist the Foundation advisor with administrative and outreach planning tasks;
- Handle daily administrative affairs of the office;
- Coordinate and implement outreach project operations;
- Complete other tasks assigned by office leadership.

## Employment Terms

Positions are offered under a personnel agency arrangement; personnel files will not be transferred to STU. Compensation is negotiable.

## Application Process

Applicants should submit the following materials by April 13, 2012, via email or post to the STU Foundation Office. Shortlisted candidates will be notified for interviews; those not notified need not visit. Originals and copies of ID, diplomas, and other certificates must be presented at the interview; originals will be returned after verification, while other materials will not be returned.

- Resume;
- Copies of graduation certificate, degree certificate, ID card, and other relevant certificates (e.g., computer and English proficiency certificates).
