It is reported that the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation of Shantou University recently issued a notice that last year, 13 STU students studied at I-Shou University and Chaoyang University of Technology in Taiwan. A survey of returning students showed that the vast majority affirmed the value of the program, reporting improvements in academic vision, resource acquisition, interpersonal skills, and hands-on abilities, which benefited their future academic development.

Based on this positive feedback and with university leadership approval, the Office will continue to organize students for Taiwan study programs this year. Eligibility: currently enrolled first- and second-year students (excluding medical majors). The program runs from September 2012 to June 2013, or students may participate for one semester only. All costs—including tuition, accommodation, living expenses, and round-trip airfare—are self-funded. Tuition is approximately 10,000 RMB, varying by host institution and major. Living and accommodation expenses are generally twice those in mainland China. Host institutions include Chaoyang University of Technology, I-Shou University, and Chinese Culture University. Courses completed in Taiwan will be recognized by STU for credit transfer upon return.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
