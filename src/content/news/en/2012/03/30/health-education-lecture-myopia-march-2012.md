It is reported that the News Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Health Education Lecture Series will be held on March 30 (Friday) at 7:00 PM in the STU Science and Technology Building.

The lecture, titled "How Bright Are Your Eyes Determines How Bright Your Future Is: Focusing on Myopia, Decoding Visual Fatigue — Using Black Eyes to Search for Light," will be delivered by Dr. Sun Lixia, an optometry expert at the Shantou International Eye Center. The lecture will focus on college students\' vision health and the latest advances in myopia treatment, helping students understand how to prevent and treat myopia and other common vision problems, and guiding them to protect their eyesight correctly. The concept of "medical optometry" and how scientific eyeglass prescription can slow myopia progression will also be explained.
