It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Local Government Development Institute at STU will hold a symposium on "Cultural System Reform and Local Government Cultural Development" at the university in June 2012.

Topics include: innovation in cultural management systems and mechanisms; reform of comprehensive cultural law enforcement systems; cultural industry creativity and intellectual property protection; development, utilization, and management of cultural resources; local government cultural policies; and Chaozhou–Shantou–Shanwei (Chaoshan) cultural characteristics. Conference papers should be approximately 6,000–8,000 Chinese characters and submitted as Word document attachments via email to the conference筹备 group. Paper format and citation guidelines are in the attachment. Please indicate "文化体制改革与地方政府文化建设" in the email subject line and include the author\'s name and contact information in the email body. The full paper submission deadline is June 1, 2012. The venue is ACC Meeting Room 3, Academic Exchange Building, STU. No conference fee will be charged.
