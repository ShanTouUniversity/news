It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that since the launch of the 3rd Campus Entrepreneurship Challenge STU Division on December 1, 2011, the competition has received widespread support and响应 from students. To date, 17 business plan projects submitted by 76 students have been received.

The on-campus simulated defense will be held on March 31 (Saturday) at 9:30 AM in Room 726, Building 789. The main agenda includes: simulated defenses by two teams—"Green Ocean" and "Maimaotong Patent"—each following the final competition format (8-minute presentation per team, 10-minute Q&A); judge commentary by Prof. Li Yue and Prof. Zeng Fanqi; experience sharing by winners of the Bangkok Global Business Plan Challenge; and experience sharing by winners of the 2nd Entrepreneurship Challenge. Students and faculty interested in entrepreneurship competitions are welcome to attend as observers.
