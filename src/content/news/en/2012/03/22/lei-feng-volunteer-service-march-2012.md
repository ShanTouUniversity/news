It is reported that the Shantou University Volunteer Association will hold a "Learn from Lei Feng National Volunteer Service Action Month" city service street event on March 24 (Saturday) from 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM in Shipaotai Subdistrict, Jinping District, Shantou City.

The event, themed "Building a Happy Shantou, Youth Take the Lead," includes home visits, free health checks (blood pressure, blood sugar, color blindness, height and weight), quiz games, food safety education, community photography and interactive games, art displays including calligraphy, portrait sketching, and Chinese knot making, as well as charity sales.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
