It is reported that the Communist Youth League Shantou University Committee recently issued a notice announcing that the 2011 "Moral Starts from the Heart, Culture Is Passed on by Me" Featured League Day Project Evaluation and Commendation Ceremony was held on March 21 in Multimedia Classroom 726, Building 7.

Following careful and fair evaluation by the defense panel judges, the results are as follows:

| Prize | Project Name | Unit |
|-------|-------------|------|
| First Prize | "Artistic Heart, Cultural Heritage" | Art School 2011 League Branch |
| Second Prize | "Let's Eat Differently — Environmental Public Welfare Action" | Zhicheng College 4th and 7th League General Branches |
| Third Prize | "Chaoshan Commerce" | Business School 2011 Class 1A League Branch |

Units awarded are requested to submit the name of the person collecting funds (with student ID for students) and contact information to 10zhpan@stu.edu.cn by 11:00 AM on March 23.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Shantou University Committee)
