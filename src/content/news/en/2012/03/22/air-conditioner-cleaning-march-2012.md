It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that a professional air conditioning company will conduct unified cleaning and maintenance of all non-central air conditioning units on campus from March 25 to April 25, 2012.

The initiative aims to ensure optimal air conditioner performance, improve indoor air quality, reduce energy consumption, and extend the lifespan of equipment.各单位 are requested to confirm suitable construction times and provide contact information, filling out the registration form and sending it to dlu@stu.edu.cn before March 23. Contact: Lu Dun, Tel: 82903142.
