It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Club Federation recently issued a notice that the 2011-2012 academic year spring semester student club project exhibition and club president symposium will be held on March 25 (Sunday) from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM in Room E204. Club leaders are requested to make necessary preparations and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
