It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the "STU 2012 Alumni Enterprise Special Recruitment Fair" will be held on March 30 at 9:00 AM in the Student Activity Hall of the New Library.

Nineteen companies, including Huizhou Taiji Enterprise Group Co., Ltd., Guangdong Chaohongji Industry Co., Ltd., Xinli Semiconductor Co., Ltd., Guangdong Maike Medical Technology Co., Ltd., O-Film Technologies Co., Ltd., and Guangdong Songfa Ceramics Co., Ltd., will participate in the recruitment event. Colleges and residential colleges are urged to notify graduating students to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
