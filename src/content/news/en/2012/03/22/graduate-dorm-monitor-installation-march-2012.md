It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice stating that recent dormitory inspections revealed that Building L lacks surveillance equipment on floors above the second, posing potential safety risks.

To better protect the personal and property safety of graduate students, the school plans to apply to relevant university departments to install surveillance cameras at both ends of the stairwells on each floor of Building L, following the same arrangement as Building Y. Students with objections are invited to email o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn before March 29.
