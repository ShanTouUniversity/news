It is reported that the evaluation results of the Guangdong Province 8th "Challenge Cup" College Student Entrepreneurship Plan Competition STU Preliminary have been announced. The competition is hosted by the STU Student Extracurricular Academic and Technological Activities Leading Group and organized by the Youth League Committee of Shantou University.

Since the registration notice was issued in December 2011, a total of 68 students have submitted 14 business plan projects to the organizing committee. After careful and fair evaluation by the defense panel, the project "Zhichi Technology Company" won the first prize, three projects including "MELODY Media Design Company" won the second prizes, six projects including "Hengli Thermal Insulation Building Materials Company" won the third prizes, and "Jia - Herbal Products Company" won the Best Popularity Award.
