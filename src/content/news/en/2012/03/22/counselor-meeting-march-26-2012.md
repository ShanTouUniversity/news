It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the first counselor regular meeting of 2012 will be held on March 26 (Monday) at 3:00 PM in Room 726, Building 789. Counselors from all colleges and residential colleges, community counselors, and relevant staff from the Student Affairs Office are requested to make necessary arrangements and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
