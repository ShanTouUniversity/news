It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice regarding the transfer major procedures for 2011 undergraduate students, in accordance with the "Provisional Regulations on Credit-based Student Status Management of Shantou University."

Students wishing to transfer majors may fill out the "STU Student Transfer Major Application Form," obtain their academic transcripts from the academic affairs office of their department, and have the form signed by the department head and dean. They should then register at the department of their intended major and participate in the assessment according to the announced schedule.

The receiving departments will submit student assessment results and relevant materials to the Academic Affairs Office by the 12th week for review and public announcement of proposed approvals. The university will publish transfer major decisions at the 14th week, and approved students will begin their new program from the 2012 summer semester.
