It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold the second STU Industry (Major, Position) Analysis Competition from March to April 2012, aimed at helping students establish correct views on achievement and employment, deepen their understanding of industries, and learn basic methods of career planning.

The competition schedule is as follows: registration from March 22 to March 29; training and writing guidance sessions to be announced separately;作品submission from April 1 to April 12, with hard copies submitted to the Employment Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office and electronic copies sent to kalaszhixie@163.com or hyfx2012@163.com; preliminary selection from April 13 to April 20; and the finals tentatively scheduled for April 29 from 7:30 PM to 10:30 PM, including project presentations and public defenses. Specific arrangements will be announced separately.
