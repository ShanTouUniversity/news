It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that according to feedback from the Financial Affairs Office, some students failed to have their 2012 first-half CET exam fees deducted due to insufficient balances in their campus cards or bank accounts.

Affected students are requested to deposit the exam fee into their campus cards before 10:00 AM on March 23, after which the finance office will request the campus card center to retry the deduction. Failure to do so will result in failed registration.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
