It is reported that the Party and Government Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the weekly leadership reception day will be held on Thursday, March 22, at 3:00 PM, with Vice President Lin Danming on duty.

The leadership reception day serves as an important channel for the university to hear feedback from faculty and students and address their concerns. Those wishing to attend are encouraged to make an appointment in advance by calling 8290 2306.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
