It is reported that the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the western pharmacy at the campus hospital has begun issuing medical insurance IC cards to relevant faculty and staff members.

Eligible colleagues may collect their IC cards at the western pharmacy on the second floor of the campus hospital during working hours.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
