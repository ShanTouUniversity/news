It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with relevant announcements from the Ministry of Education and after review, 19 students including Xu JY have been awarded the 2010-2011 National Scholarship, receiving 8,000 RMB each along with a certificate of honor.

Pursuant to requirements from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, and after selection by individual colleges, university review and public notice, and approval by the provincial department, 291 students including Zhang LL have been awarded the 2010-2011 National Motivational Scholarship, receiving 5,000 RMB each. Additionally, 1,300 students including Lin JN have been awarded the 2011 National Grant, receiving 3,000 RMB each.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
