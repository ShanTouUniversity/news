It is reported that the Human Resources Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing the list of projects approved for the 2012 Guangdong Provincial University Talent Introduction Special Fund.

The Guangdong Provincial University Talent Introduction Special Fund aims to attract outstanding high-level talents to universities and colleges and is an important initiative to strengthen faculty development in the province. The announcement of STU's approved projects reflects the university's positive progress in recruiting high-caliber talents and will further enhance its teaching and research capabilities.

Details are available in the attached document.
