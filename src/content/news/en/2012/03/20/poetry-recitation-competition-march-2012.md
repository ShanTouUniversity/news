It is reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the preliminary round of the 9th Student Academic Festival Chinese and English Poetry Recitation Competition will be held on Saturday, March 24, from 7:30 PM to 10:00 PM in Classroom D.

All currently enrolled students are welcome to participate. Interested students should send their registration information to 10gjhuang@stu.edu.cn by noon on March 22.

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Arts and Letters)
