It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the 2011 "Featured League Day" project evaluation and commendation conference will be held on Wednesday, March 21, at 2:30 PM in Multimedia Classroom 726, Building 7.

Following project proposal defenses, 18 projects were approved for funding, including "Artistic Heart, Cultural Heritage" from the College of Arts and 10 projects from the Business School, including "The Interesting World of Bartering." Project leaders from each college will present their league day activities to compete for six Excellence Awards: one first prize, two second prizes, and three third prizes. Presentations using PowerPoint or other formats are encouraged and should not exceed eight minutes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Shantou University)
