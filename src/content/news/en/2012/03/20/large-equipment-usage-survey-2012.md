It is reported that the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice to conduct a comprehensive survey of the usage of large-scale instruments and equipment valued at 50,000 RMB or more during the year 2011.

The survey covers equipment used for teaching, scientific research, and discipline development, spanning from January 1, 2010 to December 31, 2011. All units are requested to submit both an electronic and a paper copy of the completed forms to the Equipment Office of the Resource Management Office by March 26. Paper forms must be stamped with the unit official seal and signed by the supervisor. Contact: Wang Shudi, Tel: 82903142, Email: sdwang@stu.edu.cn.
