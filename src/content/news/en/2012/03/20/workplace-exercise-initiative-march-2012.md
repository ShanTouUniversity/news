It is reported that the Physical Education Department at Shantou University recently issued an initiative to promote workplace exercise activities for all faculty and students. The initiative has been discussed and approved by university leadership.

The workplace exercises will primarily feature the Ninth Set of National Broadcast Gymnastics, published by the General Administration of Sport of China. Starting from Week 5 (next Monday), the campus radio will play gymnastics music every weekday from 9:50 to 10:10 AM during the break period, allowing faculty and students to follow along. The PE Department will distribute one learning DVD of the Ninth Set of Broadcast Gymnastics to each college and administrative department, and has arranged for PE instructors to learn the latest routine. Guidance will also be provided to departments and individuals interested in learning the exercises.
