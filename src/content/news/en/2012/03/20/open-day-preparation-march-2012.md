It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the university is preparing to conduct exchange activities with local high schools, with the Fifth STU Open Day scheduled for May 13, 2012. The event will invite high school teachers and students to visit the campus.

The university plans to send delegations to high schools between April and June to carry out academic exchanges. Colleges and departments are encouraged to actively recommend projects and send participants. Recommended projects include academic lectures or science popularization talks, laboratory and workshop visits, interactive activities such as student club performances, talent shows, skills demonstrations, and art exhibitions, as well as exchange sessions that can assist high school students with academics, exams, and college admissions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
