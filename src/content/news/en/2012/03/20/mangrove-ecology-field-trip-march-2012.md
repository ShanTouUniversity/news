It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice inviting students enrolled in Marine Biology Exploration and Marine Ecology courses to voluntarily participate in a mangrove ecosystem field investigation. The event, themed "Survey of the Mangrove Ecosystem in Su'ai Bay, Shantou," aims to give students hands-on experience studying the distribution and current state of mangrove ecosystems in the Chaoshan region.

The activity will take place on Sunday, March 25, with participants gathering at 7:40 AM at the University Motto Monument. The field trip will be led by Mr. Sun Zewei from the Marine Biology Research Institute. Students bringing their own bicycles will cycle from the gathering point to the entrance of Chaoshan Gymnasium, located to the left of Shantou People's Square. Those needing to rent bicycles will take Bus Route 17 to the terminus at Chaoshan Gymnasium. Participants are reminded to follow safety guidelines, obey the tour leader's instructions, and bring their own drinking water and snacks.
