It is reported that the Personnel Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University jointly issued a notice recruiting three student community management center administrators.

Basic requirements include: good political and ideological qualities, strong sense of responsibility, upright conduct; ability to adapt to a 24-hour shift system; under 40 years old, in good health, compliant with family planning policies; college diploma or above in property management or hotel management, or graduation from a vocational technical college in electrical engineering (with intermediate technician qualification or above). Responsibilities include dormitory area duty and patrol, facility inspection and repair follow-up, greening and cleaning inspections, student consultations and visitor reception, emergency incident handling, and other tasks assigned by superiors.

Applications should be submitted by April 25, 2012, via email or mail to the Student Community Management Center of the Resource Management Office, including a personal resume, copies of graduation certificate, family planning certificate, professional technical qualification certificate, ID card, and other relevant documents. Qualified candidates will be notified for a written test.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office, Resource Management Office)
