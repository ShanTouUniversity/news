It is reported that the Personnel Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University jointly issued a notice that, due to work needs, the university is recruiting several campus security personnel for the Resource Management Office from the general public.

Basic requirements include: good political and ideological qualities, strong sense of responsibility, upright conduct, serious and meticulous work style, teamwork spirit, and dedication; high school education or above; male, under 50 years old, in good health, and compliant with family planning policies; able to adapt to a 24-hour shift system; priority given to those with security, fire prevention experience, or military veterans. Responsibilities include maintaining campus political stability and public order, protecting the life and property of faculty and students, assisting with fire and theft prevention, and preventing infiltration and sabotage by hostile forces and illegal religious groups.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office, Resource Management Office)
