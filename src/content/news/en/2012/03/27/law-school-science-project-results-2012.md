It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2011 Law School Undergraduate Extracurricular Science Project final review has been completed. A total of eight projects underwent final review. After fair and careful evaluation by the college expert panel, eight projects including "Investigation and Research on Emergency Mechanisms for Sudden Water Pollution Incidents -- A Case Study of the Han River in the Chaoshan Region" passed the final review.
