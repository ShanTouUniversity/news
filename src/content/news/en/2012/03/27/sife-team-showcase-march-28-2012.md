It is reported that the SIFE (Students In Free Enterprises) team at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to celebrate the team\'s fourth anniversary and give back to all colleges for their support over the past four years, the STU SIFE 2012 Annual Project Showcase will be held on March 28 (Wednesday) from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM in ACC Meeting Room 3. The event will showcase the team\'s innovative social entrepreneurship projects. Representatives from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Business School leadership, team advisors, academic consultants, and business consultants will attend.
