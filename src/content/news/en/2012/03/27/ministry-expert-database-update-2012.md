It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the Ministry of Education Science and Technology Development Center\'s "Notice on Updating Expert Information for Scientific Research Funds and Science and Technology Awards," the Ministry of Education expert database information update work has begun.

Expert qualifications require at least associate professor rank or above, with priority given to academicians, Chang Jiang Scholars, National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars recipients, full professors, experts in emerging and interdisciplinary fields, and mid-career experts with significant contributions. A new secondary discipline "Marine Science" has been added in 2012, with 13 tertiary disciplines under it; expert information for these disciplines should be prioritized. Registered experts are urged to log in to the "Ministry of Education Expert Information System" (http://211.68.23.118/zjk) by April 6 to supplement and完善 their personal information, especially discipline codes,职称, and secondary unit affiliation. Faculty meeting expert qualifications who are not yet members are requested to email yxhu@stu.edu.cn by April 2 to apply for membership.
