It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in anticipation of the 12th World Intellectual Property Day (April 26, 2012), the university will hold a series of activities during Intellectual Property Awareness Week 2012 to strengthen faculty and students\' awareness of creating and protecting intellectual property rights.

One of the key activities is a drama and sketch competition themed around intellectual property protection. The competition is open to all faculty and students, individually or in teams. Scripts must be original or adapted from IP protection cases and current hot-topic discussions, effectively reflecting the theme of protecting and promoting awareness of intellectual property rights. Performance formats are unrestricted, with a duration of approximately 10–15 minutes. Props should be prepared by participants. Please submit scripts to 10zyyang@stu.edu.cn by April 9, along with detailed contact information (name, phone, email, dormitory). Contact: Yang Zongyang, Tel: 13794119274.
