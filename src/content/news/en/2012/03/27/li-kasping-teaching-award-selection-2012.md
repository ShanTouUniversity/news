It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to honor STU faculty members who have successfully combined teaching and research roles, the university established the "Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Teaching Award" in 2003, selecting one recipient annually from the fields of science and engineering, humanities, and medicine (the Medical College conducts its own selection).

This year\'s selection for the main campus is about to begin. In accordance with the "Guiding Principles and Selection Procedures for the Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Teaching Award (Revised Draft)," university leadership has approved Ulanhas as chair of the selection committee, with Yang Qingjie, Han Ran, Yan Zhigang, Ouyang Feng, Xiong Guangjing, and Huang Xiaochun serving as committee members. Relevant units are requested to submit candidate lists and required materials by April 20, in accordance with the guiding principles and procedures.
