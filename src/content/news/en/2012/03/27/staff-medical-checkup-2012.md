It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University forwarded a notice from the First Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University on March 27, 2012, setting out medical checkup arrangements for staff members who selected the hospital. Checkups will be conducted in batches on March 31 and April 1, 5, 6 and 9, with specific dates subject to the attached schedule.

## Checkup Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Staff members choosing the First Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University for their checkups |
| Checkup dates | March 31; April 1, 5, 6 and 9 |
| Arrival time | 7:40 a.m. on the assigned date, according to the attachment |
| Requirements | Arrive fasting and bring a urine sample |
| Organizing unit | Resource Management Office |

## New Location

The hospital's medical checkup center has been renamed the **Health Management Center** and relocated to the third floor of the Science Building at No. 52 Dongxia South Road. It is opposite the outpatient building and beside Lantian Pharmacy. Staff members should visit the new location on the date assigned in the attached schedule.

The specific participant and date arrangements are subject to the original OA attachment. This article does not reproduce personal health information or the checkup list.

> ⚠️ The participant list and specific date arrangements are available in the original attachment on the OA system.
>
