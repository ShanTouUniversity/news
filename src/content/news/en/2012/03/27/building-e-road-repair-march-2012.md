It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that due to subsidence and uneven pavement on the road and sidewalk curb in front of Building E, repair work will begin on March 28, 2012. The construction period is 20 calendar days, with completion planned by April 16. The contracting company is Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd.

During the construction period, warning signs will be placed in the work area. Pedestrians and vehicles are advised to exercise caution or take detours.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
