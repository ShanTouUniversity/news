It is reported that the Publicity Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that, to implement the spirit of the 10th Shantou Municipal Party Congress, the Municipal Party Committee Publicity Department and the Shantou Special Economic Zone Daily Newspaper Society have decided to launch an essay competition on building a happy Shantou. A special column titled "Golden Ideas for a Happy Shantou" will be set up in *Shantou Daily* and on the Dahua Network to collect opinions and suggestions from cadres, the public, and all sectors of society on building a happy Shantou.

Each submission should focus on one policy proposal, providing specific measures and practices with high reference value for decision-making on building a happy Shantou. Each entry should be within 600 characters, with a submission deadline of May 10. After the collection period ends, the organizers will select first, second, and third prizes with corresponding rewards.各单位 are encouraged to actively submit entries via email to xfst321@163.com, copying the Party Committee Publicity Department at o_xcb@stu.edu.cn.
