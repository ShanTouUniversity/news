It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the university will conduct physical fitness standard tests for the 2009 and 2010 cohorts this semester. Exemptions apply to second-degree students, Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan and international students, students with incomplete ethnic information, and students with incomplete ID numbers.

The six test items are height, weight, vital capacity, standing long jump, grip strength, and men\'s 1000m/women\'s 800m. Students currently enrolled in PE classes will take the test during their PE sessions arranged by their instructors. Students not enrolled in PE classes must attend the unified test scheduled for May 5-6, 2012 (Week 10 weekend), at the fitness testing room under the stadium stands and the athletic field. Specific scheduling can be found in the attachment "Unified Test Schedule for Students Not Enrolled in PE Classes."
