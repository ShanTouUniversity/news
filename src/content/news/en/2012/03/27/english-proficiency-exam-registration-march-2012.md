It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the arrangements of the Guangdong Provincial Higher Education Teaching Examination Management Center\'s "Notice on Doing a Good Job in the Registration for the 1st English Proficiency Exam of 2012," the registration for the 1st English Proficiency Exam of 2012 has begun.

The exam will be conducted via computer network on June 17, 2012. Eligible applicants are sports students and students from the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design. The registration fee is 16 yuan per person. Online registration is open from March 27 to March 31. Applicants should log in to the credit-based course system, complete "I Want to Select Courses → Qualification Exam Registration." The registration fee will be deducted from the campus card account by the Finance Office through the Network Center. Applicants are urged to ensure sufficient balance in their campus card accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
