It is reported that the Finance Office and Supervision and Audit Office of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice stating that in recent years, provincial and municipal governments have issued multiple documents emphasizing the need to practice frugality and explicitly calling for control of meeting expenditures.

Accordingly, the university has decided that internal meetings of all campus units should be held on campus whenever possible. If there are compelling reasons to hold a meeting off-campus, it should generally not exceed the Eastern Guangdong region, and prior approval from the competent university leader is required. The department head will bear leadership responsibility for personnel safety and related expenses during off-campus meetings.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Supervision and Audit Office)
