It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, based on the published preliminary scoring criteria, the following nine students have been selected to participate in the 2012 Hong Kong Consumer Council internship interview: Zhang Huiwen, Liang Yinyan, Deng Suping, Zhou Mozhu, Zhao Yunping, He Shuang, Liang Yuandi, Tang Susan, and Zhao Jiaxin.

The interview will be held on March 28 (Wednesday) at 3:00 PM in Room 206, Law School Building. Each interview will last approximately 10 minutes. Students are urged to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School)
