It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with Document No. 40〔2012〕of the Shantou Municipal Party Committee Office on the selection of Shantou Model Workers and Advanced Collectives, Shantou City has decided to hold a commendation conference for Shantou Model Workers and Advanced Collectives before the 2012 International Workers\' Day ("May Day").

The university has been allocated two Shantou Model Worker quotas (one for frontline workers and one for enterprise/institution heads) and one workshop/team/section advanced collective quota. Frontline worker candidates will be selected from the main campus, while enterprise/institution head candidates and advanced collective candidate units will be selected from the Medical College and its subordinate units. For the main campus selection, candidates will be chosen from all colleges, departments, and administrative offices; bureau-level cadres are not eligible to participate. Each unit is requested to submit the preliminary candidate list and nomination forms (20 copies each) to the Staff Union by close of business on March 30 (Friday).
