It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice that, upon discussion at the 3rd university leadership meeting on March 20, 2012, Chen Jian has been appointed as Director of the Physical Education Department at Shantou University, with a six-month probation period. Qu Xiaofeng is no longer serving as Director of the Physical Education Department.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
