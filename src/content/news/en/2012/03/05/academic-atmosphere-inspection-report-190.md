It is reported that the "Three Atmospheres" Construction Office of Shantou University recently issued Academic Atmosphere Inspection Report No. 190, summarizing findings from the first week of the 2011–2012 spring semester.

Inspection results on February 27:

**8:00 AM — Building E:**
- Late arrivals (within 5 minutes): 3 students
- Late arrivals (over 5 minutes): 3 students
- Bringing breakfast into classrooms: 1 student

**2:00 PM — Building G:**
- Late arrivals (within 5 minutes): 3 students
- Late arrivals (over 5 minutes): 1 student

The report urges relevant colleges and residential colleges to conduct criticism and education for students who failed to observe school discipline, and to persistently strengthen academic atmosphere building.
