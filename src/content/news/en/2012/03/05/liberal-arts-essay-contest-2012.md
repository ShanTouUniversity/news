It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University recently announced the 9th Student Academic Month Essay Contest, aiming to promote student literary creation and inspire academic engagement.

Eligible participants are all enrolled undergraduate students. The submission deadline is 10:00 PM on March 31, 2012. Manuscript review will take place from April 2 to April 17, with awards announced on April 20.

Categories:
- Fiction, Drama, Prose, Poetry (modern and classical), Criticism, English Creative Writing

Requirements:
- Fiction and drama: preferably under 8,000 characters; prose: limited to 5,000 characters
- Criticism may cover Chinese and foreign literature, film/TV works, or online literature; analysis must be in-depth with original insights, max 5,000 characters
- All works must be original and previously unpublished in any public periodical (university newspapers with unified ISSN numbers count as public; internal publications such as "Luming," "STU Youth," and "Qipan" do not)
- One entry per category per student; English creative writing allows up to two entries

Submissions require two copies: one printed and one electronic. Printed copies should be delivered to Room B320 (Teacher Yang).
