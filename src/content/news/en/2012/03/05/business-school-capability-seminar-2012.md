It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently announced a student capability development seminar to be held on March 14, 2012.

The seminar aims to explore the construction of a business student competency development model under EPAS international accreditation, aligned with the university's talent cultivation objectives. The format combines keynote speeches with group brainstorming sessions.

Participants include representatives from the student union, Business Practice Center, Youth Volunteer Association, Qipan Magazine editorial board, campus radio, debate team, and class committees from the classes of 2009, 2010, and 2011. Business School students serving in university student organizations are also welcome.

Discussion topics include:
1. What capabilities define an outstanding business professional?
2. Beyond common business skills, what special competencies do different business majors need?
3. What特色 activities has the Business School conducted in recent years, and what capabilities do students develop through them?
4. What activities from other departments or institutions can be learned from for student capability development?
5. What shortcomings exist in current Business School student work, and how can they be improved?

Speakers must submit their presentation materials (PPT) to o_stw@stu.edu.cn by March 9.
