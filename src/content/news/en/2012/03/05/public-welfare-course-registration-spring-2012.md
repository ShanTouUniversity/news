It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced that public welfare courses will be offered during the regular semester starting this spring. Seven courses are available with a total of 375 seats.

Public welfare courses are part of the university's general education curriculum. Students who pass the course will earn 1 credit, classified under "Philosophy and Social Sciences." Each course comprises 48 contact hours, including service learning, training, public welfare practice activities, reflection sharing, and evaluation.

Registration schedule:
- Initial selection: March 6, 9:00 AM – March 7, 9:00 AM
- Add/drop period: March 8, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Students are advised to review the course syllabi in the attachment and select courses and instructors of interest through the credit system.
