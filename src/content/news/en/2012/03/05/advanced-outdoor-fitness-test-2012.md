It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on March 5, 2012, that the fitness test for the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Program will be held on March 17 and 18, with reservations now open.

Students wishing to participate should log in to the university activity system at http://activity.stu.edu.cn and make a reservation before 22:00 on March 11, 2012.

All students are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
