It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced a sharing session on the "Sea Academy," University of Melbourne, and Whitman College exchange programs.

The session will be held on Friday, March 9, 2012, at 7:30 PM in Room E203. Three students — Cai Siqi, Chen Chen, and Ye Zijian — will share their overseas study experiences and provide tips for the interview process.

All interested students are welcome to attend.
