It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced that all 2011–2012 semester student loans from the China Development Bank have been disbursed, with a total of 68 students receiving funds.

Students who received loans are advised to log in to the student fee inquiry system to check their personal payment status and promptly pay any outstanding balance at the Finance Office. For questions regarding the Alipay loan account, please refer to the attachment "Student Loan Alipay Usage Instructions and FAQ."

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Lending Bank | China Development Bank |
| Academic Year | 2011–2012 |
| Recipients | 68 students |
| Fee Inquiry Portal | http://cwc.stu.edu.cn/StudentChargeQuery/frmStuentChargeQuery.aspx |
| Issuing Office | Student Affairs Office |
