It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently released the 2011 Most-Borrowed Books Ranking (Top 20), showcasing the most popular titles among students.

The top five are as follows:

| Rank | Title | Author | Borrows |
|------|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Ordinary World (Part 2) | Lu Yao | 80 |
| 2 | Qin Empire | Sun Haohui | 55 |
| 3 | Collected Works of Lu Yao | Lu Yao | 35 |
| 4 | Ordinary World (Part 1) | Lu Yao | 51 |
| 5 | The Catcher in the Rye | J.D. Salinger | 45 |

"Ordinary World" leads with a combined 125 borrows across both parts, while Lu Yao's collected works also ranked in the top three. Other popular titles include "Chrysanthemum and the Sword," "To Live," "Dream of the Red Chamber," and "One Hundred Years of Solitude."
