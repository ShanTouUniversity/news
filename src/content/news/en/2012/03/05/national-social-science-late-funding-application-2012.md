It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently announced the opening of applications for the 2012 National Social Science Fund Late-Stage Funding Project.

This project primarily supports outstanding academic成果 in basic humanities and social science research that are nearly complete but not yet published. Funding mainly supports academic monographs, and also supports a small number of high-value compiled materials and reference works. The typical funding amount is 150,000 RMB.

Eligibility requirements:
1. The applicant must be a citizen of the People's Republic of China;
2. The applicant must hold a mid-level or higher professional title or a doctoral degree;
3. The proposed work must be at least 80% complete and unpublished, in Chinese;
4. The application must be endorsed in writing by three senior experts with full professorial titles, or by a publishing house designated by the National Social Science Fund Planning Office.

Each applicant may submit only one project at a time. Projects not funded in the current cycle cannot be resubmitted within one year.
