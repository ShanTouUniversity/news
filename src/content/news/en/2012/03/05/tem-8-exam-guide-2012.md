It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the 2012 Test for English Majors-Band 8 (TEM-8) will be held on Saturday, March 10, 2012, at 8:30 AM, with a duration of approximately 195 minutes.

The exam sections and time allocation are as follows:

| Section | Content | Duration |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Part I | Listening Comprehension (Section A: Note-taking & Gap-filling; Sections B & C) | ~35 min |
| Part II | Reading Comprehension | 30 min |
| Part III | General Knowledge | 10 min |
| Part IV | Proofreading & Error Correction | 15 min |
| Part V | Translation | 60 min |
| Part VI | Writing | 45 min |

Answer sheet distribution:
- Part I (Section A) → Answer Sheet 1
- Part I (Sections B & C), Part II, Part III, Part IV → Answer Sheet 2
- Part V and Part VI → Answer Sheets 3 and 4 respectively

Relevant colleges are reminded to notify students of the above arrangements and ensure proper preparation.
