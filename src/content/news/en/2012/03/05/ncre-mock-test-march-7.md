It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced a mock test for the first-half 2012 National Computer Rank Examination (NCRE). The actual exam will be held on March 24, 2012.

The mock test is scheduled for 4:30 PM on March 7 in the E102 computer lab, to verify the examination software and system environment. Due to limited resources, only about 40 seats are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

Students registered for the NCRE are encouraged to attend the mock test to familiarize themselves with the exam system.
