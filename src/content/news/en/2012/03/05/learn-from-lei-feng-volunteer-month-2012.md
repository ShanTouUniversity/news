It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently announced a series of volunteer service activities throughout March, themed "Learn from Lei Feng —全民志愿服务行动月," in celebration of the 25th anniversary of volunteer service in Guangdong and the 13th China Youth Volunteer Service Day (March 5).

Key activities include:

**"Love Fills the Community" Series**
Time: March 2012
Locations: Guanghua Mingzhu Street Office, Wuqiao Street Office, Anju Engineering Community, Qiaotou Community, and primary/secondary schools in Shantou
Activities:
1. City Service Street — the University Youth Volunteer Association, together with college-level volunteer organizations, will provide volunteer services across Shantou
2. "Sunshine Community" Service — volunteers will serve impoverished communities through home visits, housekeeping, tutoring, health checks, legal education, cultural performances, and other activities
