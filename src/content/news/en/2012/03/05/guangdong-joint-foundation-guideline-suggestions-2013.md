It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice calling for guideline suggestions for the 2013 Guangdong Provincial Natural Science Joint Foundation (co-sponsored by NSFC and the Guangdong Provincial People's Government).

Suggested guidelines should cover the following five areas:
1. Agriculture
2. Population and Health
3. Materials and Engineering
4. Resources and Environment
5. Electronic Information

Proposed guidelines should not duplicate content from the Guangdong Joint Foundation guidelines officially published by NSFC between 2006 and 2012.

Faculty members interested in submitting suggestions should send electronic copies of their proposal by March 12 to yxhu@stu.edu.cn.
