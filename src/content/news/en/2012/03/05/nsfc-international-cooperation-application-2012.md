It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently announced the opening of applications for the 2012 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) International (Regional) Cooperation and Exchange Projects.

NSFC international cooperative research projects support collaborative research between Chinese scientists holding NSFC grants and foreign scientists through data exchange, personnel visits, and joint scientific experiments. Cooperative research is a key focus of NSFC international funding.

Funding covers personnel exchange costs during the project, including international travel for Chinese scientists to conduct collaborative research abroad and living expenses in China plus domestic travel for foreign collaborators during their research visits.

Applicants must complete and submit applications online. Steps: Log in to the NSFC management system (http://isis.nsfc.gov.cn/portal/index.asp), click "International Cooperation Project Application" → "Apply for International Cooperation Projects" → "Cooperative Exchange Project" → select "Cooperative Exchange Project (Non-Intergovernmental Agreement)" and the hosting active project → fill in the application → submit → generate PDF and print.
