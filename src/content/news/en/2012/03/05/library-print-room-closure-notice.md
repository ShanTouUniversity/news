It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently announced the temporary closure of the print room on the 3rd floor due to other arrangements. The reopening date will be announced separately.

The copying machine previously located on the 3rd floor has been relocated to the 2nd floor print room and continues to serve readers.

|------|---------|
| Closed Location | 3rd Floor Print Room, Library |
| Alternative Service | 2nd Floor Print Room |
| Reopening | To be announced |
