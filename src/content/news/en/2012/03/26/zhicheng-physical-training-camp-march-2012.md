It is reported that Zhicheng College at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 5th "Physical Training Camp" will be held on April 7, 2012, featuring a hiking trek and cross-country traversal of the Sangpu Mountains, ending at the beautiful Dongshan Lake Scenic Area in Chaozhou.

The camp aims to enrich residential student physical activities, exercise endurance and willpower, promote physical health and self-protection awareness, and cultivate the spirit of not giving up and teamwork. Activities include approximately 20 km hiking, basic survival drills, lectures, group discussions, and experience sharing. The camp is open to Zhicheng College residents and students from other residential areas, with a target enrollment of 45 (40 from Zhicheng College and 5 from other residential areas). If applications exceed the quota, a lottery system will be used.
