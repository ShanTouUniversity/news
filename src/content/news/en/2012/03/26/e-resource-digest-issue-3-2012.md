It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently issued its E-Resource Digest for Issue 3 of 2012 (General No. 3). The digest highlights that the Royal Society of Chemistry (RSC) added two new journals in 2012: *Biomaterials Science* and *Toxicology Research*. Additionally, Ebrary e-books have seen a significant increase in new titles.

*Biomaterials Science* is an international high-impact journal discussing mesoscale interactions and applications between molecules and biomaterials, co-published by RSC and the Institute for Integrated Cell-Material Sciences at Kyoto University. *Toxicology Research* is a cross-disciplinary journal co-published by RSC, the British Toxicology Society, and the Chinese Society of Toxicology, covering pharmaceutical toxicology, food toxicology, mechanisms, and risk/safety assessment.
