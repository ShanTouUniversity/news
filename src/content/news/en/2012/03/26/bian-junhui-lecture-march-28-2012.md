It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to practice the concept of "advanced undergraduate education" and guide faculty to transform their educational perspectives and learn advanced teaching methods, the university has invited Prof. Bian Junhui, Director of the Faculty Growth Center at STU Medical College, to deliver the third lecture in the Educational Thought Discussion series.

Prof. Bian joined STU Medical College in 2009 and currently serves as Director of the Faculty Growth Center and Assistant to the Dean, responsible for teacher training in basic and clinical medicine. He spent 21 years studying and working in the United States and has deep insights into both Chinese and American education. The lecture, titled "Standing at the Podium -- Student-Centered Teaching Behavior," will be held on March 28 (Wednesday) at 3:00 PM in the Science Lecture Hall. Heads of teaching units and all faculty members are requested to attend.
