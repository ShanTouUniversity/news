It is reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the opening ceremony of the 9th Student Academic Festival and the first academic lecture will be held on March 28 (Wednesday) afternoon in Lecture Hall 201, Building E.

The opening ceremony will begin at 3:00 PM. The first academic lecture will be delivered by doctoral supervisor Prof. Wang Furen, with the topic "China\'s Path to Democracy," starting at 3:15 PM. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
