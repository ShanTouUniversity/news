It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that some campus cards printed with the old printer have faded and become unclear after long-term use. With university approval, free replacements are now available.

Cardholders can bring their cards to the first-floor window of the Network Center for registration. After verification and confirmation, a replacement will be issued, and the old card will be recycled and destroyed according to regulations. Cards with bends, breaks, water damage, or人为 scratches are not eligible for free replacement. The free replacement service will end at the end of April; affected users are urged to visit the window as soon as possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
