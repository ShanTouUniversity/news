It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the admissions office organized the 2012 winter break "Remembering Our Roots, Passing on Dreams" high school alma mater visit program to strengthen ties with secondary schools across the country and better promote STU.

A total of 386 students actively participated in the program, visiting their alma maters during the winter break to conduct admissions publicity work, achieving excellent results. To summarize and share experiences, a summary and commendation conference will be held on March 28 (Wednesday) at 3:00 PM in the Library Auditorium. Outstanding students and teams will be recognized and rewarded. All participants are expected to attend; all students are welcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
