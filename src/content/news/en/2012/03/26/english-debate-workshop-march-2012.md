It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 STU English Debate Workshop will be held from March 30 to April 1. The center has invited renowned English debate training coach Loke Wing Fate to visit the campus.

The workshop will adopt the British Parliamentary Format, with training content including theoretical instruction and group competition practice. Thirty-two undergraduate students will be selected through a competitive application process. The center is also recruiting volunteers to assist with judge and external participant reception and venue setup. Interested students may register by emailing 09yjzhou12@stu.edu.cn.
