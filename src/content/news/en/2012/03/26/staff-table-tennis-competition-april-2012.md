It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the staff table tennis team competition will be held on April 9. Eight teams will participate: College of Science, College of Arts and Letters, College of Engineering, Law and Business Joint Team, Arts/Social Sciences/Continuing Education/Attached Schools Joint Team, Administration Team, Research/News/Library Joint Team, and Resource Management Team.

The match format includes men\'s singles, women\'s singles, men\'s doubles, mixed doubles, and women\'s doubles. Each team is requested to organize pre-competition training according to the rules and submit the registration form to o_xgh@stu.edu.cn by April 1. The draw ceremony will be held at the Staff Union at 10:00 AM on March 29 (Thursday).
