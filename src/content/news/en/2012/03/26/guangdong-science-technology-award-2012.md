It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "Notice on Recommending 2012 Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Awards" published by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology, the university will now accept applications for the 2012 Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Awards.

The 2012 Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Awards include the Outstanding Contribution Award and Project Awards (Natural Science, Technology Invention, and Applied Technology categories). Candidates and projects must comply with the "Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Awards Measures," its implementation rules, and the provisional measures for the Outstanding Contribution Award review.
