It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently issued a notice that the Longyuan Journal database trial period has been extended for STU readers until June 30, 2012.

Access link: http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=332 (please use Internet Explorer and open the link in a new window). Faculty and students are encouraged to make full use of this resource. Feedback and acquisition suggestions may be sent to the Library Subject Service Department, Tel: 3247, Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn.
