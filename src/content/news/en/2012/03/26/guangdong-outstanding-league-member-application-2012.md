It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to commemorate the 90th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Youth League and inspire grassroots league organizations and league members and cadres to deeply carry out the campaign of creating advanced achievements and excelling, and to guide young people to actively participate in accelerating economic transformation and building a happy Guangdong, the Provincial Youth League Committee has decided to hold集中 commendations during the 2012 "May Fourth" period for the 2011-2012 academic year\'s Guangdong Outstanding Communist Youth League Members, Outstanding Communist Youth League Cadres, May Fourth Red Flag League Organizations, and advanced collectives in the "two new" organizations.
