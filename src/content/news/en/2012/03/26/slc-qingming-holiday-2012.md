It is reported that the Study and Learning Center (SLC) of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the university's Qingming Festival holiday arrangement, the SLC will be closed from April 2 (Monday) to April 4 (Wednesday), 2012.

The academic consultation service originally scheduled for April 2 (Monday) has been rescheduled to March 31 (Saturday), and the service for April 3 (Tuesday) has been moved to April 1 (Sunday). Students are advised to plan accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
