It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that since the "Notice on Verifying Electronic Image Information of 2012 and 2013 graduating undergraduate students" was published on March 16, over 800 students have still not verified their photos online.

The notice reminds students that the photos will be used on graduation certificates, and urges them to prioritize the verification work to detect and correct errors early, avoiding issues such as photo-person mismatch.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
