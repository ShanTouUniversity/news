It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the qualified certificates for the 2011 fall semester pedagogy and psychology course training (teacher certification) for non-normal major students have been received at the university, following review and confirmation by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education Teacher Continuing Education Guidance Center.

Students whose names appear in the attached list with qualifying scores are requested to collect their certificates from the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office (Room 413, Xingxi Building). Valid ID must be presented when collecting; those collecting on behalf of others must present both their own and the certificate holder\'s valid IDs. Students who participated and qualified are urged to notify each other.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
