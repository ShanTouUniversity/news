It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the university's Qingming Festival holiday arrangement, the library will be closed on April 4, 2012 (Qingming Festival).

Additionally, the Longyuan Journal database has extended its trial period for STU readers until June 30, 2012. Access link: http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=332 (please use Internet Explorer and open the link in a new window). Feedback and acquisition suggestions may be sent to the Library Subject Service Department, Tel: 3247, Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn.
