It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently released the final selection list of its 2012 Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship Program. Following a preliminary screening and interview process conducted by the school's selection panel, 21 students in total were formally admitted: 9 from the Department of Public Administration and 12 from the Department of Law.
