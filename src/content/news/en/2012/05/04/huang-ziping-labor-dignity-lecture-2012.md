It is reported that a lecture in the New Century Humanities Forum series will be held from 19:30 to 21:30 on May 7 in ACC Room 3. Huang Ziping, a distinguished professor in the Department of Chinese Language and Literature at Peking University and an honorary professor at Hong Kong Baptist University, will discuss “Labor” and “Dignity” in contemporary literature.

Huang is from Meixian, Guangdong, and has long worked in contemporary Chinese literature and literary criticism. He has conducted visiting research at Columbia University, the University of Chicago, and the University of Tokyo. His major works include “The Meditative Old Tree Spirit,” “The Meaning of Literature,” “Revolution, History, and the Novel,” and “Literature of the Survivors.”
