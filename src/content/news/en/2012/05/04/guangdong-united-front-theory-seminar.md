It is reported that the 12th Guangdong Province University United Front Theory Research Seminar will be held in mid-to-late October 2012, organized by the Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department of Shaoguan University. To organize campus participation, Shantou University's Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department has made arrangements on paper topics and submission requirements, inviting submissions of united front theory research papers from across the university.

## Broad Range of Research Topics

According to the arrangements, united front theory research across Guangdong universities in 2012 will center on the "One Heart" concept and the core task of "accelerating transformation and upgrading, building a happy Guangdong," summarizing 90 years of theoretical and practical experience of the Communist Party of China's united front work. The seminar offers 28 reference topics spanning several research directions:

| Category | Representative Topics |
| --- | --- |
| Theory and Brand | "One Heart" concept, brand, and culture studies; summary of 90 years of united front theory and practice |
| Guangdong Characteristics | Research on the patterns and culture of united front work with local Guangdong features; advantages and paths for Taiwan-related united front work |
| Social Governance | The role of the united front in coordinating social interests; community-level mechanisms; the impact of online democracy on China's party system |
| Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and Overseas | Review of Hong Kong united front experience over 15 years since the handover; relations among patriotic associations; united front work with overseas Chinese developing businesses in Guangdong |
| Higher Education | Research on university democratic parties; institutional mechanisms of university united front work; work with non-party intellectuals |

## Submission Requirements and Selection

Papers should generally be about 5,000 words, while research reports written in the name of a research group or papers of considerable depth may not exceed 8,000 words. All papers must use the Song typeface, with the author's institution, name and postal code listed under the title, an author biography footnoted on the first page, an abstract (within 300 words) and keywords in size-5 font, and references and notes placed at the end.

Faculty members who wish to submit must email their papers to the university's Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department (hsb@stu.edu.cn) by August 31, 2012, and confirm receipt. The department will forward the papers to the organizers for review before September 10. Excellent papers that pass expert review will be compiled into a published proceedings volume, with awards given by the Guangdong Province University United Front Theory Research Association and submission to the Provincial United Front Work Department.
