It is reported that Shantou University is conducting its selection of students for the Chinese University of Hong Kong summer courses and has issued supplementary arrangements for course choices and application materials. The university plans to select 10 students on May 9. Selected students must choose their courses and complete the Hong Kong Immigration Department application form for study in Hong Kong on that day.

The course places are allocated as follows:

| Session | Course code | Course | Places |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Morning | MGNT1020A | Principles of Management | 3 |
| Morning | MGNT2040A | Human Resource Management | 2 |
| Morning | UGEC2722 | Asian Business and Management | 5 |
| Afternoon | IASP2591 | Special Topics in International Studies II: Regional Cooperation | 5 |
| Afternoon | UGED2205 | Chinese Culture from a Gender Perspective | 5 |

Applicants must submit an application form, an English essay of no more than 300 words, the Hong Kong Immigration Department study application form, and a copy of a Home Return Permit, passport, or identity card. They must also provide a copy of their STU Bank of China passbook, a transcript certified by the Academic Affairs Office, and a copy of an English proficiency test report. Personal information must be consistent across all forms, and the applicant’s account balance must meet the stated minimum of HK$13,000.

The English essay may address either how the CUHK International Summer School would broaden the applicant’s global perspective, or the applicant’s academic goals and how the program would help achieve them. Paper materials, the electronic application form, and the essay must be submitted to the Student Affairs Office’s Education Administration Section by 11:00 on May 7. Electronic documents should be sent to `xyli@stu.edu.cn`. For inquiries, contact the Student Affairs Office Student Aid Management Center at 82903398 or consult Teacher Li.

> ⚠️ The application forms and visa materials are available in the original OA attachments.
