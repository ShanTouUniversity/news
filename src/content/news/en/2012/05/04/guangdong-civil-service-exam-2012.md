It is reported that the Organization Department of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security will organize the 2012 civil service recruitment examination for government bodies at county level and above. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has forwarded the information to graduating students.

The recruitment follows the Civil Servant Law of the People’s Republic of China, the Provisional Regulations on the Recruitment of Civil Servants, and the Guangdong Measures for the Recruitment of Civil Servants. It is based on the principles of openness, equality, competition, and merit. Graduates who are interested and meet the requirements may consult the full announcement for positions, eligibility, registration, and examination arrangements.

The announcement is available on the website of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security: `http://www.gdrst.gov.cn/info/Information.asp?InfoID=201205026399472891`.
