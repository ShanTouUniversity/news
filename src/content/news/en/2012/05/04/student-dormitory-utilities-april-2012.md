It is reported that Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has published utility charges for student dormitories for April. The latest meter readings were taken on April 29 and 30, following the previous reading on March 31. Students may check their charges through the STU utility management system at `http://power.stu.edu.cn/`.

Students who find errors or have questions may complete and sign a Utility Charge Review Application Form and submit it to the Water and Electricity Center beside the Science and Technology Building during working hours from May 4 to 10. The regular payment period is May 11 to 15. The Network Center will deduct payments through campus cards beginning May 11; unpaid charges after the deadline will be handled under university regulations on May 16.

Students whose accommodation information does not match the student roster may contact their counselor’s office for verification. Requests raised after the review period that require correction after verification may take three to five working days. Students should also top up campus cards with insufficient balances.

The Water and Electricity Center can be reached at 2489, the counselor’s office at 2903355, and the Network Center at 3547-8842.
