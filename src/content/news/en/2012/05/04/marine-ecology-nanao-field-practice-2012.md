It is reported that the Nan’ao Island field practicum for Shantou University’s Marine Ecology course has been rescheduled because the instructor had an unexpected engagement. The group will now meet at the STU motto stele and depart at 12:30 on May 5, rather than the originally planned 10:00 departure. Participants will take public buses between STU and Laiwu Ferry Terminal, using Routes 17 and 105.

The practicum schedule includes the following activities:

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 12:30, May 5 | Depart from STU for Nan’ao Island |
| Afternoon, May 5 | Survey intertidal marine species, vertical distribution, ecological succession, geological types, environmental protection, and the effects of gravel beaches on marine life |
| 19:30, May 5 | Observe nocturnal intertidal marine life at the STU Nan’ao Station |
| Morning, May 6 | Study nearshore aquaculture and its effects on shallow-sea ecosystems; visit the Marine Biology Institute to learn about graduate thesis experiment design; conduct aquaculture-environment surveys and interviews with fishers |
| Afternoon, May 6 | Visit Nan’ao Haiwang Marine Biology Aquaculture Base Co., Ltd., survey the Zhuxiwan beach ecosystem, and return to STU at 17:00 |

Students must stay with the group, follow the instructors’ directions, and must not leave without permission or swim in the sea. They should also prepare rain and sun protection, wear slip-resistant shoes, and observe environmental and interview etiquette.
