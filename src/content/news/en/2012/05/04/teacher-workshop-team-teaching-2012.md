It is reported that Shantou University’s Education Discussion has moved from its initial expert-lecture phase into a series of faculty workshops. The first workshop will take place at 15:00 on May 9 and focus on team teaching. Myra Ingmanson, Muriel Walker, Lee Linghon, and Felix Giron will lead the workshop, which will be conducted in both Chinese and English.

Faculty members and administrative staff may register voluntarily through their units. Units should submit the names of interested participants to the Academic Affairs Office by the end of the working day on May 7 at `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn`. The venue will be determined according to the number of registrations and announced separately.

> ⚠️ Registration materials and related documents are available in the original OA attachments.
