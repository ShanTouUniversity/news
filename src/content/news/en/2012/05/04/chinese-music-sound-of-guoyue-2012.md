It is reported that “The Sound of Chinese Music,” the closing performance of the 2012 Spring Colorful Arts Season organized by the Arts Education Center of STU’s Changjiang College of Arts and Design, will be held at 19:30 on May 11 in the Science Lecture Hall. The Xinghai Chinese Orchestra, with guests including Meng Xiaoxu, will perform works such as “Liu Qingniang,” “The Charm of the Flute,” “Scent of a Woman,” and “Reflections on Plum Blossoms.”

Arts-fan pass holders may collect tickets from 09:30 to 11:30 or from 14:30 to 17:00 on May 7. Other students and staff may collect tickets from 14:30 to 17:00 on May 8. Tickets are available at Room 309 of the Arts Education Center. Arts-fan pass holders should register with their pass, while other students and staff should register with their campus card. Tickets are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Children under 12 will not be admitted.
