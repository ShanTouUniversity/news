It is reported that, in accordance with Guangdong Education Working Committee document No. 27 of 2012, the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee has finalized nominees for the pre–July 1 Excellence-Striving commendations after democratic recommendations and committee discussion. The university is now soliciting comments from Party members and the wider university community.

STU has proposed the Party general branch of the Medical College Student Affairs Office as an advanced grassroots Party organization of the Guangdong Education Working Committee. It has also proposed two individuals, anonymized here as Zhao XH and Wu P because the notice does not establish whether the nominees are students, for recognition as outstanding Communist Party members of the committee.

Comments may be submitted to the Organization and United Front Work Department by May 8. The contact telephone number is 82902345 and the email address is `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.
