It is reported that the final of Shantou University’s second Industry, Major, and Position Analysis Competition will be held from 14:30 to 17:30 on May 9 in the New Library Lecture Hall. Organized by the Student Affairs Office, the competition is intended to help students understand industries, majors, and positions, learn basic career-planning methods, and strengthen their overall abilities.

The competition received 66 team submissions. After judging, eight teams advanced to the final. Finalists should attend on time, and all students and staff are welcome to observe.
