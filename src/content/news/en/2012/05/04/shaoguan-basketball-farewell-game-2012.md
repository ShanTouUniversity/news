It is reported that the men’s basketball team of Shaoguan University will visit Shantou University for an invitational game against STU’s Wabian King Kong team at 19:30 on May 5 in the STU Gymnasium. The game will serve as a farewell event for graduating players.

Because seating is available, the game is open to all students and staff. Faculty members and students who have reserved tickets through the booking system may enter directly. Spectators should arrive 15 minutes early and observe venue rules.

The event staff and cheer squad were selected by drawing lots, and their roster was included with the OA notice. The roster is not reproduced here to protect student privacy.
