It is reported that a ceremony marking the 90th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Youth League of China will be held at 15:30 on May 4. Hu Jintao, then General Secretary of the Communist Party of China, will attend and deliver an important speech.

China Central Television, China National Radio, China Radio International, People’s Daily Online, Xinhua News, and CCTV.com will provide live coverage. Shantou University’s Communist Youth League Committee has asked the Youth League committees of each college and residential college to organize students and Youth League cadres to watch or listen to the broadcast.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
