It is reported that the plenary session of the Shantou University Party Committee has recommended Wen Xiaobo as a candidate in the public recommendation, competitive selection, and quantitative evaluation process for the president of Zhongkai University of Agriculture and Engineering.

The university’s Organization and United Front Work Department is soliciting comments on the recommendation. Units submitting objections must affix their official seal, while individuals must sign with their real names. Comments may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the department by the end of the working day on May 4.

The contact telephone number is 82902345 and the email address is `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.
