It is reported that Shantou University’s Business School has completed its preliminary review of 2011 students applying to transfer into the school and has released the interview roster and arrangements.

The interviews will begin at 14:30 on May 9 in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of the west section of the Wenxi Building. Students on the roster should attend on time. The roster is available in the original OA attachment and student names are not reproduced here.
