It is reported that STU’s Party Organization and United Front Affairs Office has forwarded Shantou’s 2012 notices for the open selection of department-level leaders in municipal units. Eligible faculty and staff are invited to review the opportunity. The selection includes positions open nationwide for principal and deputy department-level roles in municipal units and positions open across Shantou for deputy department-level roles in municipal government offices.

Applicants for the citywide selection of deputy department-level leaders in municipal government offices must deliver their materials to STU’s Party Organization and United Front Affairs Office, Xingdong-405, by the end of the workday on May 28. Applicants for the nationwide selection of principal and deputy department-level leaders in municipal units must submit their materials by the end of the workday on June 19.

The original OA attachments provide the positions, eligibility requirements, application materials, and selection procedures. Inquiries: `82902345`.
