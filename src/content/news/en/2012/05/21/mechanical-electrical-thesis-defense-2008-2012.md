It is reported that the 2008 cohort of STU’s Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Department will hold undergraduate graduation-design defenses in several sessions from May 22 to 25. The work will be overseen by Cheng Xiyun as committee chair; detailed panels and student groups are provided in the original OA attachments.

| Time | Venue |
| --- | --- |
| May 22, from 08:30 | Department meeting room, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Building |
| May 24, from 08:30 | Meeting room, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Building |
| May 25, from 13:00 | Department meeting room, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Building |

Before the defense, faculty members will inspect the graduation designs. During the formal defense, each student will have five to ten minutes to present the main arguments, purpose, structure, and features of the thesis, followed by five to ten minutes of questions from the panel and on-site responses. A recorder will document the questions and answers. The defense will also assess students’ command of relevant theories, knowledge, and skills and their ability to analyze and solve problems. Students must prepare a PowerPoint presentation.

Students attending the defense must complete and print the undergraduate thesis or design defense record form and the Engineering School thesis or design grading form in advance and bring them to the venue. Outstanding-thesis defenses will be scheduled after the group defenses according to circumstances; if the number of participants does not exceed the university quota, a separate outstanding-thesis defense may not be held.

> ⚠️ Defense groups, record forms, grading forms, and detailed arrangements are available in the original OA attachments. Student rosters are not reproduced here.
