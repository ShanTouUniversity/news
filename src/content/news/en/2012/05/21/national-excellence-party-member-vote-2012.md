It is reported that, under arrangements from the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee Education Working Committee and Shantou authorities, STU will organize the Party Committee of the Medical School, the general Party branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches to encourage Party members and members of the university community to participate in the online recommendation vote for national outstanding Party members.

The activity will run from May 22 through 30. Units should follow the voting process, collect participants’ reflections, comments, and suggestions, and report the information to the university’s Excellence Campaign Leading Group Office by 11:30 each day during the activity.

Voting is available through the People’s Daily Online special page at <http://cpc.people.com.cn/toupiao/GB/243702/index.html> and the Xinhua News Agency special page at <http://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/zt/2012qgcxzyyxgcdypx/index.htm>. The university office can be reached at `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` or `82902345`.
