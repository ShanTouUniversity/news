It is reported that the Science School’s Science Salon at STU will host Professor Huang Weiyang of Hong Kong Baptist University from 09:00 to 10:00 on May 25 in the Science School lecture hall, Room Li-352. His presentation is titled “Metallopolyynes and Metallophosphors: Multifunctional Materials with Emerging Applications.”

The presentation will introduce the functional properties of metallopolyynes and metallophosphors and discuss their potential applications in emerging fields. The speaker’s biography and abstract are included in the original OA attachments. Students and staff interested in the topic are welcome.
