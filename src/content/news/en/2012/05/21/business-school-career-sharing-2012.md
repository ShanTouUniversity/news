It is reported that STU’s Business School will hold a job-hunting experience-sharing session from 14:30 to 17:00 on May 23 in Room E201. Graduates who have entered employment or further study will share their job-search experiences and interview advice, helping current students plan their studies, career choices, and employment earlier and more effectively.

The discussion will cover job searches in government agencies, foreign-invested companies, state-owned enterprises, and private companies. Speakers will also discuss applications for overseas study, followed by an interactive question-and-answer session. Guests come from Cranfield University in the United Kingdom and organizations including AB InBev China, Unilever, COFCO, the Foshan Tax Bureau, KPMG Huazhen in Shenzhen, Esquel Group, Shenzhen Airlines, and Chow Tai Fook Jewellery. Student speaker names are not reproduced here.

Students and staff are welcome to attend.
