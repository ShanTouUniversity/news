It is reported that the 13th Extracurricular Activity Project Defense of STU’s Science School will be held from 14:30 to 16:30 on May 23 in the conference hall of the Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research. Eleven proposals, including a discussion on employment or postgraduate study, have passed the preliminary review by the Science School Youth League Committee and will proceed to the defense.

Under the Science School’s rules for reviewing extracurricular activity proposals, each project defense will include a three-minute presentation of the activity plan followed by two minutes of questions from the panel. Project contacts should prepare their materials in advance. If a PowerPoint presentation is used, it should be sent to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn` by 20:00 on May 22.
