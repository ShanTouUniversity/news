It is reported that the 2012 “Dear Ones” concert by the Shantou University Choir will be held at 19:30 on May 26 in the Grand Auditorium. The program will center on full-choir performances and also feature small-group choral works and a cappella singing, with music inspired by youth and campus memories.

The Shantou University Choir will perform, conducted by Liao Wenlan and accompanied on piano by Feng Yuebing. Students and staff are welcome to attend.

Tickets will be distributed at Room 309 of the Arts Education Center on May 23 from 09:30 to 11:30 and from 14:30 to 17:00. Ticket collection requires registration with an individual campus card and is available while supplies last. Children under 10 will not be admitted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Arts and Design and Arts Education Center)
