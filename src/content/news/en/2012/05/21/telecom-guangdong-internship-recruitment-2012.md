It is reported that China Telecom Guangdong has opened its 2012 internship recruitment for students expected to graduate in 2013, including third-year undergraduates and second-year postgraduate students. STU’s Academic Affairs Office has forwarded the recruitment information and encourages eligible students to review the opportunity and apply.

The application period, application method, internship arrangements, and other requirements are provided in the original OA attachments. Interested students should consult the complete information and submit materials as required.
