It is reported that STU’s Student Affairs Office will hold a growth-story sharing session for recipients of the STU Medal and Sportsmanship Medal from 19:00 to 21:00 on May 24 in the New Library lecture hall. The event will share awardees’ stories and university experiences and encourage students to reflect on their academic paths, life goals, and personal development.

The program will include keynote speeches, interviews, and audience questions. Interview guests will include recipients and nominees of the STU Medal and Sportsmanship Medal, with discussion of their growth stories, university experiences, and reflections. All students and staff are welcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
