It is reported that STU has published the final examination schedule for courses in the spring semester of 2012. The Academic Affairs Office asks teaching units on the main campus to notify relevant faculty members and students to log in to the credit-system portal and check their detailed examination information and prepare in advance.

The portal is available at <http://credit.stu.edu.cn/>. Units should assist with examination arrangements and help ensure that the final examinations proceed smoothly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
