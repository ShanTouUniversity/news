It is reported that STU’s Interim Rules on the Management of Reserved Funds for Discipline Development and Research Enhancement were approved by the STU Research Committee on May 18 and published by the Research Office for relevant university units.

The rules establish a framework for managing reserved funds under the Discipline Development and Research Enhancement initiative, and relevant units should implement them accordingly. The full provisions and scope of application are provided in the original OA attachment.
