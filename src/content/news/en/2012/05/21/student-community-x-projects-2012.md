It is reported that STU has opened applications for 2012 projects under the Student Community X Program. Since its launch in November 2010, the program has organized community activities to develop students’ initiative, independence, creativity, communication, responsibility, and teamwork, supporting the development of a people-centered, innovative, energetic, and harmonious residential community.

Faculty and staff may apply individually or in self-organized teams. Undergraduates from the residential communities may also form teams. Activities should serve undergraduates in the residential communities and run from June through December 2012. Teams may design innovative and practical projects on health, planning, learning, time, interpersonal relations, or emotions, provided the activities are substantive and appealing to students.

Applicants must complete the 2012 Student Community X Project Application Form, and each team must designate a project contact. Faculty and student projects will be evaluated and approved separately. After a project ends, its contact must submit a news brief and photographs within one week and a work memorandum, digital album, or video within one month for filing.

Materials must be delivered to the residential-community counselor office beside Room G238 by 17:00 on June 1, with electronic copies sent to `ymfan@stu.edu.cn`. Inquiries: `82903118`.
