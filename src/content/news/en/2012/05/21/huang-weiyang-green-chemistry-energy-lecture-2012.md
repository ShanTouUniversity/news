It is reported that the Science School’s Science Lecture Series at STU will host Professor Huang Weiyang of Hong Kong Baptist University from 19:30 to 21:00 on May 25 in Lecture Hall 3 of the Academic Conference Center. His presentation is titled “Go Green! Get and Save Energy with Chemistry.”

The lecture will examine the relationship between chemistry and everyday life, as well as the links among scientific literacy, chemistry research, and the global energy challenge. It will also consider how scientific knowledge and chemical research can support energy production and conservation and increase public appreciation of green chemistry.

The speaker’s biography and abstract are provided in the original OA attachment. Students and staff interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
