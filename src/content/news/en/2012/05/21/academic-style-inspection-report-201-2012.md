It is reported that STU’s “Three Atmospheres” Development Office has issued Report No. 201 on academic-style inspections during Week 12 of the spring semester of 2011–2012. Inspection teams checked class attendance in teaching buildings at 08:00 on May 17 and 18.

| Date | Location | Late by up to 5 minutes | Late by more than 5 minutes |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| May 17 | Building E | 1 | 4 |
| May 18 | Buildings D and G | 1 | 2 |

The report asks the relevant schools and colleges to provide criticism and education to students who failed to follow university rules and to continue strengthening the academic atmosphere. The student roster is provided in the original OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.

> ⚠️ The student roster and inspection records are available in the original OA attachment. Student personal information is not reproduced here.
