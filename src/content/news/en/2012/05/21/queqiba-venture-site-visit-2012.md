It is reported that STU’s Entrepreneurship Center will arrange a site visit and consultation session on May 22 for student teams interested in operating Queqiba. The activity is intended to help teams understand the venue, equipment, and operating conditions.

| Time | Activity | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 17:00–17:30 | Visit the Queqiba site and inspect its equipment | Queqiba, Building 789 |
| 17:30–18:00 | University representatives collect team feedback and suggestions | Room 726, Building 789 |

Student venture teams interested in the opportunity are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office Entrepreneurship Center)
