It is reported that the STU Youth League Committee has opened recruitment for the 2012 Volunteer Service Western Program and Mountain Program. Western Program volunteers will continue to serve in Guangxi, Guizhou, Xinjiang, and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps, with a special service initiative in Tibet this year. Mountain Program volunteers will continue to work in townships in economically less-developed areas of eastern, western, and northern Guangdong, supporting local youth work and volunteer services.

Applicants must review the programs and complete online registration by May 25 through the Western Program website, China Volunteers website, or Guangdong Volunteers website. They should then download and print the appropriate application form from the Guangdong Volunteers website. The form must be signed by a counselor or school Youth League official, stamped by the Party organization of the applicant’s school or college, and delivered to the STU project office by May 25.

After receiving the forms, the university project office will verify the online information and enter its review comments in the Western Program information system. STU’s Western Program project office is located in the university Youth League Committee. Inquiries: `82902302`.
