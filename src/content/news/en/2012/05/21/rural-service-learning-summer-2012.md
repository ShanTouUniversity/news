It is reported that Zhicheng College will hold its Rural Service-Learning summer course in mid-July 2012, now open for registration.

Listed under the university’s service-learning curriculum, the 7-day course offers 1 credit upon passing and aims to develop communication, teamwork, leadership and social responsibility through rural service.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Mid-July 2012 (after military training), 7 days |
| Venue | Lubi Community, Quxi Town, Jiedong County, Jieyang |
| Activities | Summer camp for local primary and secondary students, social survey and a joint evening gala |
| Places | 40, selected via interviews |
| Eligibility | All students |

## Requirements

Applicants should observe service-learning regulations, be eager to learn, hardworking, cooperative and disciplined, and treat others with a smile. Applications require downloading the form, naming it as “Grade+Major+Name” and sending it to the designated email address by 17:00 on May 27, 2012.

> ⚠️ The application form is available on the OA system.
