It is reported that, as the 2012 graduating class prepares to leave campus, STU’s Party Organization and United Front Affairs Office has arranged the transfer of graduating student Party members’ organizational relations. Under the principle of local administration, graduates should transfer their relations to the Party organization of their employer or to an organization in their place of residence. Where an employer has no Party organization, graduates may contact a talent-exchange center, the neighborhood Party working committee where the employer is located, or a neighborhood, township, or town Party committee in the residence area of the graduate or the graduate’s parents.

Graduating members who have not secured employment, have deferred employment, and genuinely cannot identify a receiving organization may apply to retain their relations temporarily in the university’s directly affiliated branch for no more than 24 months. Applicants must submit a request to their school or college Party general branch by June 8. After review and consolidation, the materials must be reported to the Party Organization and United Front Affairs Office by June 15.

Schools and colleges must verify each graduate’s receiving organization and the addressee on the introduction letter and submit electronic and printed materials by June 15. Introduction letters will be issued centrally from June 21 to 22, with detailed arrangements in the OA attachments. After paying Party dues through June 2012, graduates must personally collect their letters from their school or college Party general branch.

Members should report to the receiving local Party committee within the required period. Those transferring outside Guangdong must first complete the transfer through the Shantou Municipal Party Organization Department at Building 8 in the Shantou Municipal Party compound, 8 Haibin Road; telephone `0754-88276114`. Introduction letters should be kept securely. If the receiving organization changes within three months, the member must return with the original letter and identification document to process the change.

Files for full members, probationary members, and applicants for Party membership will be organized, sealed, and transferred under the relevant rules. Schools and colleges must also provide training on Party-relation transfers and education on organizational awareness.

> ⚠️ The transfer register, temporary-retention application, departure-work schedule, and related materials are available in the original OA attachments.
