It is reported that STU’s Law School Career Development Center will hold a civil-service interview experience session from 15:00 to 16:30 on May 30 in the School of Law conference room 213. The session is titled “Civil-Service Interview Skills.”

The speakers will be Gui Bo of the Shantou Chaoyang District People’s Court and Lan Pengzhe of the Shantou Municipal Administration for Industry and Commerce. They will discuss the format and features of the Guangdong civil-service interview, answer techniques and approaches, and details to manage in the examination room. Both speakers have experience with civil-service examinations or work in public-sector institutions.

Students and staff of the Law School are welcome to attend.
