It is reported that STU’s School of Law will hold a lecture on amendments to the Criminal Procedure Law from 19:00 to 22:00 on May 23 in Room 213 of the School of Law, also known as Wenbei-213. Associate Professor Chen Ruilin will be the speaker.

The session is included in STU’s continuing-education program and counts toward continuing-education credit under the relevant arrangements. Teachers who have registered are asked to attend on time.
