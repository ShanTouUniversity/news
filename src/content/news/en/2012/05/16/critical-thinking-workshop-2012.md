It is reported that, to advance “advanced undergraduate education” and the development of integrated thinking, Shantou University will host a critical-thinking workshop from May 21 to 31, excluding weekends, with Dr. Dong Yu from Canada as the speaker. Sessions will run from 08:30 to 11:30 and 14:30 to 17:30 each weekday in Room E-410 of the Teaching Building, unless otherwise specified.

Teaching units will designate staff members for full-time participation, and the workshop will also count as a continuing-education course. Students and staff across the university may audit the sessions and receive a copy of *Principles and Methods of Critical Thinking: Toward New Cognition and Practice*. The detailed schedule and preparatory materials are provided in the original OA attachments.

Born in 1956, Dr. Dong Yu studied science and engineering and philosophy as an undergraduate and graduate student at Huazhong Institute of Technology and Wuhan University. Dong later studied scientific methodology at the London School of Economics and researched logic, scientific methodology, and critical thinking at McMaster University in Canada, where Dong earned a doctorate. Dong has taught critical thinking, scientific methodology, logic, and epistemology for many years and has conducted related research, teaching, and practical work.
