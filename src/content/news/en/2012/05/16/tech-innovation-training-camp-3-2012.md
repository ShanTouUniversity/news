It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Science and Technology Development Center of the College of Engineering will hold the 3rd Tech Innovation Training Camp from May 20 to 27, 2012, to implement the EIP-CDIO engineering education reform and foster a culture of hands-on innovation.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Approaching Technology, Creating Miracles |
| Time | May 20–27, 2012 |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, College of Engineering |
| Co-organizer | Student Science and Technology Development Center |
| Participants | First- and second-year students intending to apply for 2012 innovation projects |
| Program | See Attachment 1 |

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | 17:00, May 18, 2012 |
| How to apply | Download and complete the registration form, rename as “Grade + Major + Name” and send to the designated email |
| Notes | Participants must follow arrangements and dress neatly (male students in long trousers, no slippers); unexplained absence may affect future applications |

Details and the form are available in the OA attachment and the submission email is withheld here for privacy.
