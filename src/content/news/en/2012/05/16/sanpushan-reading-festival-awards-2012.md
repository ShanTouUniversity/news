It is reported that the ninth Shantou University Sanpushan Reading Festival began on April 18, and its series of activities concluded on May 5. The program received support and participation from students and staff, and the organizers have now announced the award results for its activities.

Staff members will contact award recipients and distribute prizes in the near future. Detailed awards, recipient rosters, and arrangements are provided in the original OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.
