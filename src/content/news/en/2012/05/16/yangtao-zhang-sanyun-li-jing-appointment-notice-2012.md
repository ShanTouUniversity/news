It is reported that, following discussion at the sixth 2012 meeting of the Shantou University leadership on May 15, the university is proposing to appoint Yang Tao, Zhang Sanyun, and Li Jing to intermediate management positions. The university has published the notice in accordance with its Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres.

Units or individuals with comments on the proposed appointments may submit them in writing to the Personnel Office by May 22. Units must affix their official seal, while individuals must provide their real names.
