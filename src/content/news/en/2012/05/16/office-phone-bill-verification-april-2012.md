It is reported that China Telecom Shantou Branch has sent electronic bills for office telephone charges incurred by Shantou University units from April 1 through 30, 2012, to the university telephone office. The data has been imported into the Telephone Management System. Designated staff from each unit should log in at `http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn` to review the billing details and confirm whether they match actual usage.

For bills without discrepancies, staff should click “Confirm the selected bill” in the system. Any discrepancy between billing details and actual use must be emailed to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` by May 24 so the university can report it collectively to the telecom company. Bills for which no objection or confirmation is submitted before the review deadline will be treated by the system as confirmed by default.

The Finance Office will deduct the charges on May 25 using the funding account numbers provided by each unit. Questions or suggestions concerning the Telephone Management System may also be sent to the same email address.
