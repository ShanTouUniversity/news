It is reported that the final of Shantou University’s 2012 PPT Resume Design Contest was held on May 13 in the New Library lecture hall. The contest awarded one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes, five merit awards, and one popularity award. In addition, 11 students received company internship placements.

The awards and placements highlighted the contest’s focus on resume presentation, career planning, and practical employment experience. The internship opportunities included four placements at Shantou Recruitment Network and seven at China Unicom’s Shantou branch.

The Student Affairs Office has published the complete award and internship rosters. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
