It is reported that, to improve campus environmental hygiene, Shantou University will conduct a pest-control operation from May 19 to 23 across the campus, Longquan Garden, and the Continuing Education College. The operation will focus on controlling mosquitoes and flies. The Resource Management Office has reminded students, faculty, and staff to take care in affected areas.

| Date | Areas |
| --- | --- |
| May 19 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, student dormitories, dining halls, and Administration Building |
| May 20 | Affiliated Middle School, the route from the Affiliated Primary School to the Mental Health Center, A-B faculty dormitories, and Building 789 |
| May 21 | Medical Building, New Teaching Building, and student dormitories in Buildings G, H, I, and J |
| May 22 | Around the Campus Guard office, campus gates, campus roads, and outside the New Library |
| May 23 | Longquan Garden and the Continuing Education College |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
