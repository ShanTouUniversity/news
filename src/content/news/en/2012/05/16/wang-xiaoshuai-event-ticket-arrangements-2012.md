It is reported that 460 students and staff registered for the “Memories of Youth” exchange jointly organized by the Publicity Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee and Zhicheng College. After the reservation system closed, the first 400 reservations were approved.

Approved attendees must bring their campus cards to the Zhicheng College Student Union office, Room J-405A, from 16:15 to 17:30 each day from May 16 through 18 to collect admission tickets. The exchange will take place on May 19, and ticket holders must enter at 13:10 that day. Anyone who does not collect a ticket by the deadline will be considered to have given up the place.

Acclaimed director Wang Xiaoshuai will speak at the exchange. The approved roster and entrance arrangements are provided in the original OA attachment; personal rosters are not reproduced here.
