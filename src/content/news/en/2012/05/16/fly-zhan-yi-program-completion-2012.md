It is reported that the second “Wings” session of Shantou University’s FLY training program concluded on May 13. Following assessment across several areas, 24 participants were approved to complete the program.

Six participants received Outstanding Participant awards, five received Best Cinematography awards in the short-film activity, and five received Best Popularity awards. The university maintains the detailed recipient record; student names are not reproduced here.

The organizers encouraged participants to continue improving and apply the knowledge and abilities developed through the program to their future study, work, and daily life, becoming ambitious and competitive young people prepared for an increasingly international environment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union)
