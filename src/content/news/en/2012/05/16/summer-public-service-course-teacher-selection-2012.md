It is reported that Shantou University will offer 11 public-service courses in the 2011–2012 summer semester, most during the first part of the summer vacation. The Academic Affairs Office is recruiting qualified teachers and graduate students who have completed public-service-course teacher training to serve as instructors or teaching assistants. Trained graduate students may fill teaching-assistant positions.

Participants who passed the first, second, or third public-service-course teacher-training sessions may select courses according to the instructor and teaching-assistant demand table, course syllabi, and requested staffing levels, then complete the Public-Service Course Teacher Selection Information Form. The original OA attachments provide the course offerings, staffing needs, and completion requirements.

Teachers and graduate students who wish to undertake a course assignment must send the information form as an email attachment to Liu Xiangling of the Academic Affairs Office at `lxl0726@stu.edu.cn` by 09:00 on May 21.

> ⚠️ The course syllabi, staffing table, qualified-participant roster, and information form are available in the original OA attachments.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
