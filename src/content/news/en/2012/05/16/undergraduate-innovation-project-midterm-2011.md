It is reported that, under STU’s implementation measures for undergraduate innovation projects, the Academic Affairs Office will conduct a midterm review of selected projects approved in 2011 with one-year implementation periods.

Project leaders must complete the midterm review form. Project teams must submit a progress log containing experiment records and meeting minutes, together with images or written descriptions of interim results such as papers and developed products. Two printed copies and an electronic copy are required. Teams must submit the materials to the General Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office by May 25.

Projects that pass the review will use the result as an important basis for their later completion assessment. Projects that do not pass must make corrections within a specified period. Their written improvement plans must pass expert review before the projects may continue and use second-stage funding; projects that remain below standard will be terminated.

Contact: Zhai Liu, `82902736`, `zhailiu@stu.edu.cn`.

### Midterm-review results

The Academic Affairs Office subsequently reviewed the materials from the participating projects. Of **22 projects**, **21 passed and one was suspended**. Projects that passed will continue to receive second-stage project funding and undergo completion review in **June 2013**. Members of the suspended project will receive a failing project grade, and the university will stop releasing further funds.

The review recommends that project leaders and members clarify their responsibilities and next-stage schedule, communicate proactively with advisers, keep more standardized research records and use project funds appropriately. The original OA attachment contains the detailed project list and research materials; they are not reproduced here.
