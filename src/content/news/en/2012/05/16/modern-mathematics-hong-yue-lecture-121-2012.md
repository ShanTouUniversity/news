It is reported that the 121st lecture in the School of Science’s “Toward Modern Mathematics” series will be held at 15:00 on May 18 in the Mathematics Laboratory, Room Gongbei-412. Dr. Hong Yue of Trine University in Indiana will present “Some Characterizations of Local bmo and h¹ on Metric Measure Spaces.”

Working in the setting of doubling metric measure spaces, the lecture will examine the local bmo space and Goldberg’s Hardy space h¹. It will introduce a John–Nirenberg-type inequality for local bmo and two proofs based on a good-lambda inequality and duality. The presentation will also address the boundedness of the Hardy–Littlewood maximal function from bmo to bmo and characterizations of bmo and h¹ through alternative mean-oscillation and moment conditions.

Dr. Hong Yue earned a bachelor’s degree in computational mathematics from Wuhan University and later studied and worked at Wuhan University, Concordia University in Montreal, and the University of Oulu in Finland. Hong has been an assistant professor at Trine University since 2009, with research interests in Fourier analysis and function spaces and their applications to partial differential equations and geometric measure theory.
