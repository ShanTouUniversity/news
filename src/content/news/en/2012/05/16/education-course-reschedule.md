It is reported that Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office issued a rescheduling notice on May 16: Cai Yinghui of the Institute of Higher Education will take part in the university's critical thinking workshop, so her two sections of the Education course will be temporarily suspended on May 21 (Monday of Week 13), with a unified makeup time already confirmed.

## Rescheduling Details

| Item | Original Schedule | Makeup Session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time | Monday of Week 13 (May 21), periods 3–4 and 8–9 | Saturday of Week 13 (May 26), periods 1–2 |
| Venue | Room E202 and Room E204 | Room E303 |

The suspension affects the two Education sections under course numbers 52602 and 52603. After the May 21 classes are called off, all students enrolled in the course will complete the makeup session together on May 26 at the time and location listed above.

The Academic Affairs Office asked students taking the course to spread the word so that no one misses the makeup class. Students who need further confirmation may check with their college or the Academic Affairs Office.
