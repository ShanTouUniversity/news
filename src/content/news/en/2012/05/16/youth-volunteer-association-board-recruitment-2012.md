It is reported that the Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association plans to begin its transition to an 18th board in mid-May and is recruiting candidates from across the student body. Guided by the STU Communist Youth League Committee, the association promotes the goal that everyone can be a volunteer, uses a service-learning model, and organizes social-service activities for people in need.

The 18th board is planned to have five members: one president and four vice presidents. Applicants should have strong academic and personal records, an interest in volunteer service, experience in organizing activities and social work, a detailed understanding of the association’s development, a solid grasp of service learning, an innovative outlook, and the ability to serve continuously for one academic year.

Candidates must submit the board-candidate registration form and a work statement of at least 1,000 Chinese characters for the position sought. Materials should be packaged as instructed and sent to `volunteersofstu@163.com` by 18:00 on May 20. The transition will include registration, interviews, and speeches. Formal candidates will give public speeches at the 18th association transition conference, where volunteer representatives will elect the new board.
