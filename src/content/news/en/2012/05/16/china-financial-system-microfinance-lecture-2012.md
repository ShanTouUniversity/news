It is reported that the STU Research Office and Business School will hold a lecture at 10:00 on May 21 in Room 205 of the Science Building. Dr. Li Huamin, a professor at Guangdong University of Finance, director of the Guangdong Regional Financial Policy Research Center, economic-review expert for the Guangdong Social Science Fund, and special researcher for the Development Research Center of the Guangdong Provincial Government, will speak on “Establishing China’s New Financial System and the Challenges Microfinance Institutions Must Overcome.”

The lecture is open to students and staff interested in social-science fund project evaluation and financial studies.
