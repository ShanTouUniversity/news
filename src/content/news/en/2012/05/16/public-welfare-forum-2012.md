It is reported that, marking the 18th anniversary of the Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association, STU’s first public-welfare forum will be held from 15:00 to 17:00 on May 19 in the New Library lecture hall. The theme is “Public Welfare Makes Life Better.”

The forum will include keynote speeches and panel discussions on the role of student public-welfare organizations, volunteers’ growth experiences, volunteer service in Wenchuan, service in western China, and the use of creativity and technology in public-welfare projects for rural communities. Additional sessions will examine the continuation of the volunteer spirit, support for volunteer organizations, creative approaches to improving lives, and personal value through service. Representatives of public-welfare organizations from the Chaoshan region will discuss the development of social nonprofit organizations.

Students and staff may reserve a place at `http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ActivityBooking.aspx` by 22:00 on May 17. The organizers will publish the successful reservation list on May 18. Student speakers’ and interview guests’ names are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association)
