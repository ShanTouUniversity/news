It is reported that construction for STU’s new administration-building renovation project damaged an underground water main and caused a leak. Water service will therefore be suspended in several campus areas from 18:30 to 22:30 on May 30 while the pipe is repaired.

The affected areas include the Second and Third Dining Halls, student residences in Buildings L and Y, the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Building, the Science Building, the Science School Building, and the General Physics Laboratory. The Resource Management Office asks affected users to store water in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
