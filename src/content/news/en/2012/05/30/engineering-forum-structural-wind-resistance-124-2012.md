It is reported that STU’s 124th Engineering Research Academic Forum will be held from 09:00 to 10:30 on June 5 in Room 205 of the Science Building. Professor Gu Ming will present “Fundamental Scientific Issues and Research Progress in Structural Wind Resistance.”

Gu is a recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars and a distinguished professor under the Chang Jiang Scholars Program. The forum is open to Engineering faculty members and students interested in the topic.
