It is reported that the Sports Department issued a notice on May 30, 2012, to announce the overall results of the 13th Sports Festival.

Ten teams, including the College of Liberal Arts, competed in eleven events over more than a month. The full scorebook is available in the OA attachment.

Colleges (residential colleges) are asked to arrange for collection of prizes at the Sports Department office in the Gymnasium during working hours from May 31 to June 6.

> ⚠️ The scorebook is available on the OA system.
