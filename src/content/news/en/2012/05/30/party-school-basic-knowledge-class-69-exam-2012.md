It is reported that the theory examination for STU Party School’s 69th Basic Party Knowledge Study Class will be held from 19:30 to 21:00 on Sunday, June 3. Students from Class 68 who have been approved for a deferred or make-up examination must also attend on schedule.

The examination venues are assigned as follows: students from the Schools of Liberal Arts, Law, Business, Journalism and Communication, and Zhicheng College will sit in Lecture Hall 1; students from the Science and Engineering schools, Arts and Design, the Graduate School, and faculty and staff participants will sit in Lecture Hall 3. Examinees must use their assigned venue and sit with one seat between them.

Students unable to attend for special reasons must obtain approval from their school or college Party general branch or directly affiliated branch and complete the deferral procedure at the Party School office. An unexcused absence will result in cancellation of student status. Students who fail or receive an approved deferral may take one make-up examination in the next study class.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Organization and United Front Department and Party School)
