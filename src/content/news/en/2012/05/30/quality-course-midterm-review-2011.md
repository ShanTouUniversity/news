It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office will conduct a midterm review of university-level quality-course projects approved in 2011 to strengthen project management and ensure the quality of course development. The detailed course list is provided in the original OA attachments.

Project leaders must complete the *STU University-Level Quality Course Midterm Review Form* by June 15 based on the original course-development plan and the construction and updating of course resources. The printed form and supporting materials should be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office’s General Affairs Section, Room 401 on the west side of the Administration Building. An electronic copy should be sent to `wtyang@stu.edu.cn`.

Courses that pass the review will receive the second installment of course-development funding. Funding for courses that do not pass may be deferred or discontinued. Contact: Yang Weitao, `82902736`.

> ⚠️ The quality-course list, review form, and supporting-material requirements are available in the original OA attachments.
