It is reported that leaders of selected STU university-funded projects will hold an academic lecture from 15:00 to 17:30 on May 31 in Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building. The event is open to students and staff across the university.

The original notice does not specify the lecture topics or the speakers’ names. Further details should follow the arrangements issued by the Research Office and Business School.
