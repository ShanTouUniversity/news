It is reported that, to strengthen academic exchange across the Taiwan Strait and broaden access to scholarly resources, STU Library has established a resource-sharing and document-delivery partnership with the library of Taiwan’s Chaoyang University of Technology. The partner library can provide journal or monograph materials from its holdings by email at no charge to STU full-time faculty members and postgraduate students.

Requested materials must be purely academic and may not concern political matters prohibited by relevant laws and regulations. The number of pages delivered must comply with copyright law. Library subject-services contact: `yxu@stu.edu.cn`; telephone `82903247`.
