It is reported that the 108th New-Century Humanities Forum will be held at 19:30 on June 1 in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic and Cultural Center. Lin Lunlun, president of Hanshan Normal University and vice president of the Guangdong Society of Chinese Linguistics, will present “New Words and Social Culture.” The event is organized by STU’s School of Liberal Arts.

The lecture will examine new-word phenomena and their relationship with social culture. Students and staff across the university are welcome to attend.
