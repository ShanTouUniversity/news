It is reported that STU has scheduled military training for students in the 2011 cohort from July 2 through 15. Some students remain enrolled in public-service courses that conflict with the training period. The Academic Affairs Office asks affected students to withdraw from the conflicting courses and choose another time slot by 09:00 on May 31. Records for students who do not withdraw on time will be deleted by the university.

The affected courses include “Shuguang Study-Aid Service” (project no. 56847), “Passing the Torch: Rural Community Care” (project no. 56850), “Hearts Together in Kangfu Village” (project no. 56853), and “Quality-Education Outreach Service for Students at Private Schools.” The relevant student roster is provided in the original OA attachment and is not reproduced here.

The Academic Affairs Office also reminds all students in the 2011 cohort to avoid military-training dates when selecting public-service courses.
