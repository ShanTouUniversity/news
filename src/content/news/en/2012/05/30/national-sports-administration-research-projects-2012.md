It is reported that the Science and Education Department of the General Administration of Sport of China has opened applications for 2012 research projects in key areas. Proposed topics must follow the *Outline for Medium- and Long-Term Sports Science and Technology Research of the General Administration of Sport (2011–2012)* and the 2011–2015 guide to major research projects. STU may submit no more than three proposals.

The priority areas are improving national fitness, raising competitive-sports performance, promoting the sports industry, and supporting distinctive research by key laboratories of the General Administration of Sport. Specific topics include scientific fitness and fitness monitoring, youth fitness, community sports, training theory and technology, prevention and rehabilitation of sports injuries, sports fatigue, anti-doping tests, sports marketing, technology transfer, and the development of fitness and training equipment.

Applicants must be Chinese citizens who comply with relevant laws and regulations, hold a senior professional title or a doctoral degree, and directly undertake project organization and implementation. The first applicant may not be leading more than two provincial- or ministerial-level or higher research projects. Applications must be free of intellectual-property disputes. Funding is generally capped at RMB 300,000 for key projects and RMB 150,000 for general projects, with a usual research period of two years.

Prospective applicants must email `ljwang@stu.edu.cn` by June 1 to request an online-application account, complete the online submission by June 6, and submit six printed copies of the application to the Research Office. Contacts: Huang Yumei and Wang Liju; telephone `82902563`.
