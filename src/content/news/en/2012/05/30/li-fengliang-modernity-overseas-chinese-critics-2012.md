It is reported that the 109th New-Century Humanities Forum will be held at 19:00 on June 2 in Meeting Room 5 of the Academic and Cultural Center. Li Fengliang, vice president of Shenzhen University and director of its Institute of Cultural Industries, will present “Modernity on the Other Shore: Insights from Overseas Chinese Critics.” The event is organized by STU’s School of Liberal Arts.

Li is also a doctoral supervisor at Jinan University and has been a visiting scholar at the University of Southern California. He is a chief expert on a major National Social Science Fund project and a participant in the Ministry of Education’s New-Century Excellent Talents Support Program. His research covers Chinese-language literature, foreign literary theory, comparative poetics, and cultural industries. He has led three National Social Science Fund projects and six provincial or ministerial projects, published nine books, and written more than 80 academic papers and nearly 200 cultural reviews.

Students and staff interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
