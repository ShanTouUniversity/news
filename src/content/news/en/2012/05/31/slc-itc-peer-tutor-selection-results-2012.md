It is reported that STU’s Student Learning Center (SLC) selected three students as ITC peer tutors for the autumn 2012 semester. The decision was based on the position requirements and applicants’ performance in the computer-application skills test and interviews.

To protect student privacy, the selected students’ names and student numbers are not reproduced here. SLC thanked students for their support.
