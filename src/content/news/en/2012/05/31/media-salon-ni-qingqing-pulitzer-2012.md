It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism will hold a Media Salon on June 4, 2012, featuring Ching-Ching Ni, former China correspondent of the Los Angeles Times, Harvard Nieman Fellow and Pulitzer Prize winner.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Finding My Story, Raising My Voice |
| Speaker | Ching-Ching Ni (倪青青), former China correspondent, Los Angeles Times; Harvard Nieman Fellow; Pulitzer Prize winner |
| Time | 19:30, June 4, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, New Library |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

Ni will share her family and personal growth story on how she went from a shy young immigrant who was afraid to speak to a senior Chinese-American journalist covering international news for world-leading media. All interested faculty and students are welcome to attend.
