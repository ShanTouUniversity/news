It is reported that STU’s University Hospital will offer faculty and staff the continuing-education elective “First Response to Accidental Injuries.” Yang Fengxia will teach the course from 09:00 to 12:00 on June 7 in Room 204 of the E Building.

Faculty and staff who enrolled in the course should attend on time.
