It is reported that 86 students applied for STU Zhicheng College’s summer rural service-learning public-service course. After a preliminary review of the application materials and interviews held on the evening of May 30, the project team selected 45 students for the course.

All selected students must log into the credit-system platform and complete course enrollment by 22:00 on May 31. Operating instructions have been sent to the students by email. The participant roster is provided in the original OA attachment; student names, years, and majors are not reproduced here.
