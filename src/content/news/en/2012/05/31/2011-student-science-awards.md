It is reported that on May 31, Shantou University issued a document commending individuals and groups with outstanding achievements in extracurricular academic and sci-tech activities in 2011. The commendation was made under the Regulation of Shantou University for Evaluating Student Performance in Sci-Tech Competitions and covers three award categories.

## Overview of Awardees

| Award | Recipients |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Supervisor | Li Yuanyou and 5 other teachers (6 in total) |
| Student Research Innovation Award | Researchers of 16 student research projects, including Lin SQ |
| Outstanding Organization Award | College of Engineering and 2 other units (3 in total) |

The Outstanding Supervisor title recognizes teachers who provided dedicated guidance in students' extracurricular academic and sci-tech activities, while the Student Research Innovation Award went to the research teams behind 16 student projects. The Outstanding Organization Award honored the College of Engineering and two other units for their distinguished work in organizing the activities.

In the document, the university expressed its hope that all faculty and students would learn from the commended individuals and groups, keep striving for innovation, and contribute further to cultivating high-caliber talent described as "aspiring, knowledgeable, persevering and accomplished," as well as to the development of Shantou University.
