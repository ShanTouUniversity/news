It is reported that the eighth Guangdong Challenge Cup Undergraduate Entrepreneurship Competition, organized by the Guangdong Youth League Committee, Department of Education, Department of Science and Technology, Association for Science and Technology, and Students’ Federation, has concluded. Following expert review, seven STU entries received awards.

The project “Zhichi Technology Co., Ltd.” won a silver award. Six projects received bronze awards: “Xueduofen Interactive Education Platform,” “Shantou Wangtuo E-Commerce Co., Ltd.,” “Shantou Deep Blue Software Co., Ltd.,” “Greenshell Green Shell Co., Ltd.,” “Maima Patent Link,” and “Hengli Thermal-Insulation Building Materials Co., Ltd.”

The university Youth League Committee congratulated the award-winning students and supervising faculty and thanked the university community for its support. Student team members’ names are not reproduced here to protect their privacy.
