It is reported that Shantou University is offering 11 public welfare courses for the summer term of the 2012-2013 academic year, open to all undergraduate students. Course selection is being conducted through the university's credit-based system, with initial selection and add/drop windows scheduled between May 30 and June 1.

## Selection Schedule

| Stage | Time |
| ----- | ---- |
| Initial selection | May 30, 9:00 a.m. — May 31, 9:00 a.m. |
| Add/drop period | June 1, 9:00 a.m. — 5:00 p.m. |

## Course Overview

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Number of courses | 11 |
| Seats offered | 710 |
| Category | Philosophy and Social Sciences (general education) |
| Credits | 1 credit upon passing assessment, counted toward total credits |
| Study hours | 48 hours in total |
| Schedule | Mainly in July or during the summer term |

According to the introduction, each public welfare course consists of four components: service learning, training, hands-on community service activities, and reflection sharing with assessment. All courses are taught by trained university faculty members. Students can browse the courses (course codes beginning with "SER") offered by various teaching units under "Guided Courses – My Topic Selection" and choose the course and instructor they are interested in.

As seats are limited, enrollment follows a first-come, first-served principle until all seats are filled, and each student may take only one course. The university reminded students that no course changes will be accepted after the add/drop period, and those who fail to attend after enrolling will receive zero credit under the unified grading policy. Questions may be directed to the Academic Affairs Office at 82902416 (Ms. Liu) or 82903202 (Mr. Chen).
