It is reported that, because the final examination period is approaching and student-organization activities are declining, the STU Student Union office will suspend office duty and approval of materials requests from Monday, June 4, Week 15.

The suspension covers applications for audio equipment, other equipment, poster boards, and related materials. After June 4, student organizations with urgent materials needs should contact the Student Union through its official channels. A student’s personal contact information included in the notice is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Union)
