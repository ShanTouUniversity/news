It is reported that STU’s Student Entrepreneurship Center invited students to submit operating teams for the Queqiba project on May 17. By the submission deadline, 14 student entrepreneurship teams had provided business-management proposals.

On May 30, the center organized a review panel to assess the proposals and selected the MUSCLE team as the winner. The team will take responsibility for the project’s subsequent operations and management. Team members’ names are not reproduced here to protect student privacy.

The Student Entrepreneurship Center thanked all participating teams and congratulated the winner.
