It is reported that the Shantou municipal government will soon select and commend advanced collectives and individuals in spiritual-civilization development. STU is inviting eligible units and individuals from the main campus to submit recommendations.

Applicants should prepare materials under Shantou Party Committee Office Notice No. 71 (2012) and submit two printed copies and an electronic version to the Party Committee Publicity Department before the end of work on June 3. The detailed application document and forms are provided in the original OA attachments.

Consultation: `2343`; email `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn`.

> ⚠️ Eligibility requirements, application materials, and forms are available in the original OA attachments.
