It is reported that STU’s Institute for Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation has issued its 2012 open-fund project guide. Established in 2010, the institute is a key humanities and social-sciences research base among Guangdong universities and focuses on Guangdong-Taiwan enterprise cooperation. Professor Xu Zongling is its director.

This year’s topics are models of Guangdong-Taiwan enterprise cooperation and models of Guangdong-Taiwan industrial cooperation. Applications will undergo eligibility review by the institute’s administrative committee and a decision by its academic committee under principles of fair competition and merit-based support. Successful researchers will sign agreements and become part-time or full-time research members of the institute.

The institute plans to approve three projects, each funded at RMB 15,000 to 20,000. Projects will last one to two years, from July 2012 through June 2014. Each project must publish at least two to three papers in CSSCI core journals with the institute as the first institutional affiliation and acknowledge support from the open fund.

Applicants must email the electronic application to `sdhu@stu.edu.cn` by June 8, using “Open Fund Application + Applying Institution” as the subject. Three printed copies, stamped by the applicant’s institution, must be mailed to the institute by June 10, with the postmark determining timeliness. The printed and electronic versions must be identical.
