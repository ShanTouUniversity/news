It is reported that STU Student Affairs will host an X Program Muse Music Community session at 20:00 on June 3 in the Science Center Lecture Hall. The event aims to strengthen students’ creative abilities and ecological awareness while enriching campus culture. Singer-songwriter Zeng Yan will speak with students, with Shantou Radio DJ Bai Yang serving as moderator.

Zeng has received the Best Original Film Song award at the 46th Golden Horse Awards, a Best Creation award at the second Best Record Chart Awards, and the Best New Mandarin Female Singer award at the 11th Chinese Music Media Awards. She will discuss her creative journey and inspirations, travel stories and the relationship between travel and music, and ecological ideas expressed through music. She will also perform her own songs and lead students in learning the “Hand Sign of Love.”

The session will run from 20:00 to 21:00, and attendees should enter 30 minutes early at 19:30. Reservations are open through 16:30 on June 1 at <http://activity.stu.edu.cn/>.
