It is reported that STU’s second student club incubation program has completed its evaluation. Six newly established clubs were assessed through club presentations, online voting, and on-site voting.

The results were: Chao Shan Food Club, first; Cantonese Culture Association, second; Vegetarian Culture Association, third; Billiards Association, fourth; Astronomy Enthusiasts Association, fifth; and Chinese Classics Society, sixth.

The university Youth League Committee will provide RMB 600, RMB 400, and RMB 200 to the top three clubs, respectively, and encourage the recognized clubs to continue contributing to campus club culture.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Club Federation)
