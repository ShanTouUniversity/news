It is reported that STU’s Zhicheng College completed open recruitment, assessment, and interviews for its 2012–2013 peer-mentor program and selected 16 students as incoming peer mentors, including alternates.

Zhicheng College will hold peer-mentor training and a summary meeting for the 2011–2012 program at 19:00 on June 2 in the New Library Student Activities Center. All incoming mentors must attend on time. The selection roster is provided in the original OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.
