It is reported that STU’s Zhicheng College will hold a summary meeting for the 2011–2012 peer-mentor program at 19:00 on June 2 in the New Library Student Activities Center. All current peer mentors and incoming mentors for the 2012–2013 academic year must attend on time.

The meeting will review the year’s peer-mentor work and provide an opportunity for outgoing and incoming mentors to exchange experience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
