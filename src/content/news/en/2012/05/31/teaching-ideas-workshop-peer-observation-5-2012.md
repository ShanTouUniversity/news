It is reported that STU’s fifth Teaching Ideas Discussion faculty workshop will be held at 15:00 on June 6. Terry Bodenhorn will present “Peer Classroom Observation System: Design & Implementation.” The workshop will use both Chinese and English, and its venue will be determined separately according to the number of registrations.

The event is intended for faculty members and administrative staff interested in undergraduate-education reform and changes in teaching ideas and methods. Those wishing to attend should send their names to the Academic Affairs Office at `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn` before the end of work on June 4.

> ⚠️ Workshop materials and the final venue are available in the original OA attachments and subsequent notice.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
