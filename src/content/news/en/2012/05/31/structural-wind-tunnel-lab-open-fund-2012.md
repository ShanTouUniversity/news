It is reported that the Guangdong Key Laboratory of Structures and Wind Tunnels, hosted by STU, has issued its 2012 open-project fund guide. The laboratory is inviting universities, research institutes, and other domestic research organizations engaged in basic or applied basic research to submit collaborative projects.

Projects must fit the laboratory’s research directions, including structural health inspection and monitoring, theories of structural strengthening and repair, structural wind engineering, and related basic and applied basic research. Supported projects will be selected by the academic committee under principles of fair competition and merit-based support.

Each project will receive RMB 20,000 to 30,000 and last two years, from July 2012 through June 2014. Applications close on June 30. Applicants must complete the open-project fund application, email the electronic version to the designated address, and submit three printed copies bearing comments from the applicant’s academic authority, the signature of the institutional or school leader, and the official seal. The printed and electronic versions must be identical; printed copies should be mailed or couriered to the laboratory.
