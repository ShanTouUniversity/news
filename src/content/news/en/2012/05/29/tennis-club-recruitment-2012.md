It is reported that the tennis club of STU’s Physical Education Department is recruiting students as one of the university’s pilot projects for advanced undergraduate-education reform. The club will offer tennis training and exchange activities, provide a learning platform for tennis enthusiasts, and allow participants to earn physical-education course credit under the applicable arrangements.

Applications are open from May 29 through 17:00 on June 4, with approximately 70 places available on a first-come basis. Applicants may choose either Monday periods 8–9 or Tuesday periods 8–9.

Students should complete the application form in the OA attachment and email it to `dlyan@stu.edu.cn` with “Tennis Club Application” as the subject.

> ⚠️ The tennis-club application form is available in the original OA attachment.
