It is reported that STU’s “Three Cultures” Development Office organized inspections of classroom conduct during Week 13 of the spring semester of the 2011–2012 academic year.

At 08:00 on May 24, inspectors found two students bringing breakfast into classrooms in the E Teaching Building. At 08:00 on May 25, they found two students arriving late for class, by no more than five minutes. The relevant student roster was sent to the students’ schools or residential colleges through an OA attachment for criticism and education as part of continuing study-culture work.

> ⚠️ The relevant student roster is available in the original OA attachment. Student personal information is not reproduced here.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (STU “Three Cultures” Development Office)
