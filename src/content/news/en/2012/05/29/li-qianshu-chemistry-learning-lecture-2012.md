It is reported that STU’s Science School will hold a Science Lecture Series event at 10:00 on June 4 in the Science School Lecture Hall, Room Li-352. Li Qianshu, a professor at the School of Chemistry and Environment of South China Normal University, will present “The Current Development of Chemistry and How to Learn Chemistry Well.”

The lecture will review developments in chemistry and discuss approaches to studying the subject. Students and staff interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
