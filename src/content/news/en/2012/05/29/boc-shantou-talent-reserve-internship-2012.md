It is reported that Bank of China Shantou Branch and STU’s Business School are recruiting talent-reserve interns from full-time undergraduate and postgraduate students currently enrolled at STU. The program plans to recruit 50 interns per group. Positions include lobby-manager assistants at the branch and its affiliated institutions, as well as the organization, entry, and maintenance of non-confidential banking materials.

Lobby positions involve greeting customers, guiding them through services, explaining self-service equipment, maintaining order, answering questions, and helping with forms. Materials positions involve processing non-confidential information as required. Interns must follow the bank’s attendance, work-discipline, and confidentiality rules.

Internships will run from July 2, 2012, through March 31, 2013. They may be arranged in groups, with each placement lasting at least three months. Working hours are Monday through Friday, 08:30–12:00 and 14:00–17:30. Interns must sign an internship agreement and complete pre-service and on-the-job training, as well as training, routine, and end-of-placement assessments. The bank will assign specific institutions and may adjust positions according to operational needs.

Interns with strong assessment results may enter Bank of China Shantou Branch’s campus-recruitment talent pool. The internship allowance is RMB 40 per day. The bank will provide lunch and purchase group commercial accidental-injury insurance for interns.

Applicants must send the *Bank of China Shantou Branch Talent-Reserve Internship Registration Form* to `chenyl@stu.edu.cn` by 17:00 on June 8. Interviews are scheduled for 15:00 on June 12 in the Business School seminar room on the second floor of the west section of the Wenke Building. Applicants must present their identity card and student card and submit copies when beginning the internship.

Contact: Chen; telephone `82903496`.
