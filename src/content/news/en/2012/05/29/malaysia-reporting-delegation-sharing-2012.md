It is reported that STU’s Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication will hold a sharing session by a Malaysia reporting delegation at 19:30 on June 1 in the New Library Lecture Hall. The event is open to students and staff across the university.

The session will share experiences from reporting in Malaysia and offer an opportunity to discuss news reporting and cross-cultural observation in the region.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication)
