It is reported that 2012 graduates who have not yet signed their national student-loan repayment agreements must collect the agreements and related materials from STU’s Student Aid Management Center, Room 102 of the Student Affairs Office. By June 20, they must personally visit the Personal Finance Department of Bank of China Shantou Branch to sign a repayment agreement or submit an early-repayment application. The relevant graduate roster is provided in the original OA attachment and is not reproduced here.

Applicants must bring the original national student-loan contract and copies of the specified pages, the original second-generation identity card and a copy with both sides on one A4 sheet, the original Bank of China repayment passbook and a copy, the repayment agreement, and a black pen. The passbook account should be checked at a bank branch in advance. If the account is no longer valid, applicants should inform bank staff when signing the agreement.
