It is reported that STU assessed students from the 2011 cohort who applied to change majors under the university’s relevant undergraduate credit-system and student-records regulations. Each major submitted a preliminary list of students it would accept, and the Academic Affairs Office published the proposed transfer list for one week. No objections were received during the notice period.

The university has approved major transfers for 43 students, who will begin studying in their new majors in the summer semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. The complete list is provided in the original OA attachment; student names and personal information are not reproduced here.
