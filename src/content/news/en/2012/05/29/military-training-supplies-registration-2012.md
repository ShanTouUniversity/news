It is reported that STU plans to conduct its 2012 student military training within two weeks after the spring semester ends. The specific dates and detailed training plan will be announced separately. The People’s Armed Forces Department has opened size registration for training supplies.

All participants must consult the *Student Military-Training Uniform and Shoe Size Chart* and submit their sizes through the supply-registration system at <http://gdc1.stu.edu.cn/junxun/> by 18:00 on June 3. Students must select their school or college accurately. Students in residential colleges should select Zhicheng College, and students who changed majors should select their new school or college.

Each set of supplies includes a military cap, camouflage uniform, web belt, and military shoes and costs RMB 49. The university will provide a 50 percent subsidy, leaving students responsible for RMB 24.50. Students who complete registration must deposit sufficient funds in their campus-card accounts by June 10 for unified collection of the fee.

> ⚠️ The military-training uniform and shoe size chart and registration details are available in the original OA attachment.
