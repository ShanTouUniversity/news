It is reported that, at STU’s invitation, Shen Zuyao, president of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, will meet with STU university leaders and administrators from relevant departments to discuss education and administrative reform. The meeting will take place from 15:00 to 17:30 on May 30 in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center.

Deans of schools and colleges, heads of teaching units, and heads of administrative offices will attend the exchange.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
