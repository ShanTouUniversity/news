It is reported that STU will host a lecture on critical thinking from 15:00 to 17:30 on May 31 in the Science Lecture Hall. Canadian scholar Dr. Dong Yu will speak to students and staff across the university as part of efforts to advance undergraduate education and strengthen students’ integrative thinking.

Dong studied science and engineering, philosophy, scientific methodology, logic, and critical thinking at Huazhong Institute of Technology, Wuhan University, the London School of Economics, and McMaster University, earning relevant master’s and doctoral degrees. After graduation, he taught critical thinking, scientific methodology, logic, and epistemology at a Canadian university and published books and articles on critical thinking, the philosophy and methodology of science, modern Western philosophy, and comparisons of social thought and culture in China and the West.
