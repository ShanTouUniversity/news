It is reported that STU’s Arts Education Center is offering faculty and staff the continuing-education elective “Vocal Performance and Voice Care.” The course began on May 29 and will run for four weeks through June 19.

Feng Yuebing will teach the course from 19:00 to 21:00 on Tuesdays in the Choir Rehearsal Hall, Room 101 of the STU Building. Faculty and staff who enrolled should attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Arts and Design)
