It is reported that 86 students applied for Zhicheng College’s summer rural service-learning public-service course at STU. After a preliminary review of the application materials, the project team selected 55 students for interviews.

The interviews will begin at 22:10 on May 30 in Lecture Room J-354 of Zhicheng College. To protect student privacy, the names, years, and majors of interviewees are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
