It is reported that the 2012 Campus English Cross-Country Race will be held on May 26. Under the theme “Mystery in Different Cultures,” participants will complete English-language challenges and team tasks around campus, exploring stories from different countries and cultures while developing cooperation and problem-solving skills.

The race is open to all enrolled students. Teams must have four members, and up to 15 teams will be accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. The competition will run from 09:00 to 11:00. Teams should gather at the EAC at 08:30 before starting the course, which includes 12 checkpoints around campus.

Registration closes at 12:00 on May 23. First, second, and third prizes are RMB 160, RMB 120, and RMB 80 respectively, and every participating team will receive a commemorative gift. The application form and detailed rules are provided in the original OA attachments.

The event is organized by English Lounge, Creative Expression Club, English Corner, and English Debating Society.
