It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 18, 2012, to hold the Creative Dormitory Contest to foster creative thinking, hands-on skills and dormitory community spirit.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Creative Thinking for a New Community Ethos |
| Period | May 18 – June 4, 2012 |
| Venues | Student residential area, Jin San Jiao |
| Participants | All students (by dormitory or joint dormitory teams, no individual entries) |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office, Counselors’ Office for Residential Areas, and the Self-Governance Committee for Student Dormitories |

## Registration and Categories

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | 24:00, May 26, 2012 |
| How to register | Collect forms at Jin San Jiao and dormitory promotion points and drop them at designated boxes (Block A 208, Block C 126, Block E 108, Block G 119); or download the electronic form from OA and send it to the designated email addresses |
| Categories | Electronic (videos, albums, micro-films, games, dubbing, dorm songs); Handicraft (dorm models, inventions, cross-stitch, paper-cutting, DIY); Talent (fruit platters, mini concerts); Money-saving tips (travel and daily life strategies) |
| Requirements | Works must match the theme and category, be completed on time, and be submitted as photos or videos with descriptions |

## Awards

| Award | Places | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best Work Award | 4 (one per category) | 300 yuan |
| Most Popular Award | 4 (one per category) | 100 yuan |
| Best Creativity Award | 2 | 100 yuan |
| Excellence Award | 5 | 50 yuan |
| Lucky Audience Award | 10 | Gift |

Contact details and the QQ group/Weibo are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy.

> ⚠️ The registration form is available on the OA system.
