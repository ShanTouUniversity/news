It is reported that selected laboratories and offices of the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Department in STU’s Engineering School will undergo renovation. The work covers Rooms 104–106, Room 108, and Room 301 of the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Building, as well as the ground floor of the Science and Technology Building. Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation won the contract through a public tender.

The work includes removing and building brick walls, opening and sealing wall openings, installing aluminum-alloy windows and steel doors, plastering and painting walls, laying wall tiles, pouring terrazzo flooring, and installing parts of the electrical wiring. Construction is scheduled to start on May 21 and finish by the end of July.

Noise, vibration, and dust may occur during construction. The university asks students and staff to take care near the site, refrain from entering without permission from the relevant university office and contractor, and avoid lingering nearby.
