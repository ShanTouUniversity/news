It is reported that STU’s Media Salon will host Huang Zongliang, professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of International Studies at Peking University, from 19:30 to 21:30 on May 22 in the New Library lecture hall. His lecture will address “Several Issues in Current Chinese Political and Cultural Development.”

Drawing on China’s major challenges in reform and development and on historical experience, the lecture will discuss the relationship among reform, development, and stability, as well as the relationship among the Party, the people, and the law. It will also examine the relationship among China’s outstanding traditional culture, Chinese culture, and Marxism in the effort to build China into a strong cultural nation.

Huang previously served as director of the Institute of World Socialism at Peking University’s School of International Studies. He is currently executive deputy director of the Peking University Russia Research Center and vice president of the Chinese Association for the History of International Communism. He lectured on strengthening the Party’s governing capacity at a collective study session of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee in 2004, and has written or edited 10 books and published about 100 academic papers.

The lecture is included in STU’s continuing-education program. Participants may register for the course under university arrangements and receive continuing-education credit.
