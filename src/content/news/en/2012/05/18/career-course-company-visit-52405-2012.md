It is reported that the classroom schedule for STU’s Career Guidance and Training class 52405 has been changed to a company visit. Students enrolled in the class should gather at the university motto monument at 10:00 on May 24 before traveling together to Shantou Recruitment Network for an educational visit.

The visit forms part of the course and is intended to help students learn about recruitment platforms and related corporate work. The Student Affairs Office asks enrolled students to inform one another and arrive on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
