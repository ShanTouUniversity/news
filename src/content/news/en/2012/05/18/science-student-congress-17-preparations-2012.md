It is reported that the 17th Student Congress of STU’s Science School is tentatively scheduled for 14:30 on June 3 in the Science Lecture Hall. The congress will review the work of the 16th Student Union Executive Committee, elect the 17th Executive Committee, and set priorities for the next phase of student work.

The congress is expected to have about 100 delegates, representing roughly 13% of the school’s student population. Delegates must be Science School students with firm political convictions, good academic and personal records, close contact with their peers, and the ability to represent others and deliberate. Classes will elect delegates through all-class student meetings. Department Youth League general branches must consolidate the names and send them by 17:00 on May 28, while delegate registration forms should be submitted to the preparatory group for eligibility review.

The 17th Student Union Executive Committee will have nine members, including a president, vice president, and heads of the secretariat, publicity, arts, public relations, student life, sports, and learning departments. Students interested in serving may self-nominate or be recommended by their classes. After interviews and assessment by the school, candidates will be submitted to the congress for a competitive election. Candidate application forms are due to the school Youth League Committee by 17:00 on May 18.

The original OA attachments provide the delegate registration form, candidate application form, delegate summary form, and detailed preparations. The Science School Youth League Committee can be reached at `82902779` or `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn`.
