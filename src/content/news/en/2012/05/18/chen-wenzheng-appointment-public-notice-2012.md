It is reported that, following discussion at the sixth STU university leadership meeting of 2012 on May 15, Chen Wenzheng is proposed for appointment as director of the Infrastructure Office under the Resource Management Office. The appointment is now subject to public notice under STU’s Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres.

Units and individuals with comments may submit them in writing to the university’s Human Resources Office by May 22. Materials submitted by units must carry an official seal, while individual submissions must include the commenter’s real name.

Telephone: `82903390`; email: `psyu@stu.edu.cn`.
