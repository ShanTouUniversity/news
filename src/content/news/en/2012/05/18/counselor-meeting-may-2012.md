It is reported that the University will hold its third counselor meeting of 2012 at 3:00 p.m. on Monday, May 21, at Room 726, Building 789.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Meeting | 3rd Counselor Meeting of 2012 |
| Date & Time | May 21, 2012, 3:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Room 726, Building 789 |
| Attendees | All counselors and relevant student affairs personnel |

The Student Affairs Office reminds all relevant personnel to make appropriate work arrangements and attend on time. In addition, the "Counselor Skills Enhancement Workshop," offered as a specialized course under the University's continuing education program, requires all counselors to register via the STU Training Portal (http://training.stu.edu.cn).

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
