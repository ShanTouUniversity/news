It is reported that STU’s Zhicheng College is recruiting 13 prospective resident tutors from across the university for the 2012–2013 academic year, including four alternate places. Selected students will live in Zhicheng College and receive training related to resident-tutor work; those who complete the training successfully will be formally appointed.

Applicants should be undergraduate students who will be in their third year or above during the appointment, or first- or second-year postgraduate students. They should support the college’s educational philosophy, be willing to serve residential students, and demonstrate responsibility, communication and teamwork skills, as well as experience in student organizations. Generally, applicants must have ranked in the top 40% of their class by GPA in the previous academic year and have no failed courses in the fall semester of the current academic year. Outstanding student leaders may receive case-by-case consideration, while postgraduate applicants need a supervisor’s recommendation.

Selection includes preliminary review of application materials, interviews, training, and final appointment. Applicants must complete the resident-tutor application form and submit it by 13:00 on May 26. The original OA attachments provide the form and detailed requirements; personal application contact details are not published here.
