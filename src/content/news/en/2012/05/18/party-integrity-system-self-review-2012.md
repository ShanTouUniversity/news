It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee Education Working Committee and the Provincial Department of Education plan to inspect STU’s implementation of relevant Party integrity and anti-corruption systems in June. STU’s Discipline Inspection Office has asked all university units to conduct self-reviews in accordance with the higher-level notice, improve related work, and prepare for possible spot checks.

The inspection will cover implementation of systems concerning supervision and management of major issues in higher education, the “three important and one major” decision-making system, and integrity requirements for university leaders. Units should review their implementation against the relevant documents, address gaps, and prepare supporting materials.

The detailed inspection requirements and policy documents are provided in the original OA attachments.
