It is reported that STU’s Publicity Department and News Center have launched the 2011–2012 “Good News” awards to improve the quality of university news publicity. External senior journalists will be invited to participate in the judging. Eligible entries are text reports and photographs published by university units on the campus homepage or news website from June 1, 2011 through May 31, 2012. Articles written and photographs taken by the News Center itself are excluded.

Text reports should be accurate, newsworthy, well structured, concise, and well received on or off campus. Photographs should be accurate, newsworthy, captured on location, vivid, and clear. A photo entry may be a single image or a set covering the same news event.

Each unit may submit no more than five entries in each category, with a photo set counted as one entry. Unit correspondents must consolidate their entries, complete the registration form, and email the electronic materials to the News Center office by June 1. Late submissions will not be accepted.

| Category | First prizes | Second prizes | Third prizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text reports | 3, RMB 300 each | 5, RMB 200 each | 10, RMB 100 each |
| Photographs | 2, RMB 300 each | 3, RMB 200 each | 5, RMB 100 each |

Certificates and prize money will be issued for all awards. Contact: `8099`; email `o_pressoffice@stu.edu.cn`.
