It is reported that 76 students and staff successfully reserved places for Shantou University’s first public-welfare forum by 22:00 on May 17. The forum will be held from 15:00 to 17:00 on May 19 in the New Library lecture hall under the theme “Public Welfare Makes Life Better.”

Organized by the STU Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association, the forum will feature keynote speeches and interviews on volunteer service, public-welfare organization development, and service practice. All students and staff are welcome.

The reservation roster and related arrangements are provided in the original OA attachment; personal rosters are not reproduced here.
