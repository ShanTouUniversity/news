It is reported that, under STU’s Interim Regulations on Credit-Based Academic Status Management for Undergraduate Students, the university’s departments evaluated students who applied to change majors during the current semester and proposed receiving lists. The Academic Affairs Office reviewed the departmental evaluation results and related materials and has now published the proposed approval roster.

The proposed transfers involve programs including Public Administration, Marketing, International Economics and Trade, Business Administration, Civil Engineering, Biotechnology, Applied Chemistry, Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, English, Chinese Language and Literature, Editing and Publishing, Radio and Television Journalism, and Journalism. The complete roster is provided in the original OA notice; student names, identification numbers, genders, and previous majors are not reproduced here.

Units or individuals with comments on the notice may submit them in writing or contact the Academic Affairs Office in person or by telephone by May 25. Comments must be accompanied by the commenter’s real name.
