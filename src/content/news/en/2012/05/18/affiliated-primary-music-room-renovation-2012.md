It is reported that, to support reform of primary-school music education and broaden its teaching content, STU Affiliated Primary School will convert its existing fourth-floor music classroom into a combined music and dance room. Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. was selected through a price comparison process. The work will include interior-wall painting, floor surfacing, installation of dance mirrors, and electrical wiring.

Construction is scheduled to start on May 19 and finish by the end of June. Noise, vibration, and dust may occur. Work likely to affect routine teaching, as well as the transport of construction materials, will be arranged on Saturdays and Sundays where possible to reduce disruption.

Teachers and parents are asked to strengthen safety education for pupils. Students must not enter or remain near the construction site without permission.
