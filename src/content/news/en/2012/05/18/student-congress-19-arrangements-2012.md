It is reported that Shantou University’s 19th Student Congress will be held on May 20 in the Science Lecture Hall. Delegates must check in at the entrance to the lecture hall between 08:10 and 08:30, and the meeting will begin at 08:40.

The congress preparatory committee has asked each delegation to notify and organize its delegates and help ensure that the congress proceeds smoothly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Preparatory Committee of the 19th Student Congress)
