It is reported that the Shantou University Guitar Association will hold “Kangu Frog” themed music events from May 19 to 20, creating a platform for musical exchange as graduating students prepare to leave campus and enter society. The program will include a music workshop and a themed concert, with professional musicians, campus bands, and returning alumni bands invited to perform or exchange ideas.

The music workshop will begin at 14:30 on May 19 at the Labor Union. The themed concert will begin at 18:30 on May 20 at the Reservoir Steps. Postcards will be distributed while supplies last on a first-come, first-served basis. The event will also give away ukuleles and small guitars sponsored by Shantou Xierqin Musical Instrument Store.

Organizers have reminded participants to pay attention to safety around the reservoir and cooperate with the on-site arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee, Student Organizations Federation, and Guitar Association)
