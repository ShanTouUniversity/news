It is reported that the Shantou University Science and Technology Association will begin its transition to a 12th Executive Committee in late May and is recruiting candidates from across the student body. Guided by the STU Communist Youth League Committee, the association promotes extracurricular academic and scientific activities, develops students’ creativity, and supports stronger performance in the Challenge Cup and undergraduate extracurricular research programs.

The 12th Executive Committee is planned to have 13 executive members, with positions including president, three vice presidents, heads of academic competitions, science popularization, office, information and publicity, public relations, and three project departments. Applicants should have strong academic and personal records, an interest in science and technology and its communication, organizational and social skills, knowledge of the association, an innovative outlook, and the ability to serve continuously for one academic year.

Candidates must submit the relevant campaign form and a statement of work for the position or department sought. Materials should be packaged as instructed and submitted by 22:00 on May 22. The submission method and address are provided in the original OA notice.

The transition will include registration, interviews, and a public defense. A preparatory committee will invite Youth League teachers and representatives of the 11th Executive Committee to serve as interview judges. The interviews will produce six Presidium candidates and 15 department candidate teams for the annual summary, commendation, and 12th Executive Committee election. Association members will then elect the new committee.

> ⚠️ Campaign forms, work-statement requirements, submission instructions, and position descriptions are available in the original OA attachments. Student names and personal email addresses are not reproduced here.
