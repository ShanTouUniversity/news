It is reported that the Department of Chemistry of the School of Science issued a notice on May 11, 2012, announcing arrangements for the 2012 undergraduate thesis defenses.

## Defense Committee

The defense committee is chaired by Tong Qingxiao, with 15 members including Huang Xiaochun and others.

## Schedule

| Group | Chair | Time | Venue | Students |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | Tong Qingxiao | 15:00–17:00, May 23 | D302 | 10 |
| Group 2 | Yin Yegao | 15:00–17:00, May 23 | D207 | 9 |
| Group 3 | Zhang Xin | 15:00–17:00, May 23 | D301 | 10 |
| Group 4 | Chen Guanghui | 09:30–11:30, May 25 | D307 | 10 |
| Group 5 | Lu Fusheng | 15:00–17:00, May 23 | D303 | 10 |
| Group 6 | Fang Yiwen | 15:00–17:00, May 27 | D301 | 10 |

The second-round defense, judged by the chairs of the first-round groups, will be held from 10:00 to 12:00 on May 28 in Room D403. The grouping list and grading criteria are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy.
