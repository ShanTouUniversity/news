It is reported that the 2012 selection for the Li Ka Shing Foundation Shantou University Overseas Study Support Program has been completed. The program supports students with strong academic performance and the potential to contribute to innovation and development, helping them study at outstanding overseas universities and gain international experience and perspective.

Following review by the 2012 program selection committee and approval by the management committee, 19 students received support: three Category A awards of RMB 80,000 each, 11 Category B awards of RMB 50,000 each, and five Category C awards of RMB 30,000 each. The recipient roster is included in the original OA attachments; student names are not reproduced here.

Provided they receive admission offers from universities approved by the management committee, the recipients will pursue master’s degrees at institutions including the University of Edinburgh, the University of London, the National University of Singapore, and the University of Hong Kong.
