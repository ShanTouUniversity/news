It is reported that the third "Me and Tea" essay contest, organized jointly by the Guangdong Tea Culture Research Institute, the Guangdong Federation of Social Sciences, and the Guangdong Federation of Students, will be held to promote traditional Chinese tea culture and enhance moral and aesthetic awareness. The contest is open to university students across Guangdong, including those at Shantou University.

The Youth League Committee of Shantou University has forwarded the contest notice and is organizing student participation on campus. Interested students should prepare their entries according to the contest requirements and send their essays to the committee's email at o_tw@stu.edu.cn before May 25.
