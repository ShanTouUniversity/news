It is reported that the Shantou University Student Club Development Action will include a “Student Club Cup” three-on-three basketball tournament and a “Student Club Cup” Mini Fun Sports Meet. The action is organized by the STU Student Organizations Federation to strengthen exchange and cooperation among student clubs and support campus culture.

The “Harbin Beer 3X3 Cool Basketball Battle” will be held on May 19 at the STU basketball court. Teams of four will be recruited from student clubs, with at least one member of each team belonging to an STU club. The tournament will use an elimination format to determine the top three teams, and a Weibo vote will select the most popular team. Winning teams will receive cash prizes. Registration closes at 24:00 on May 16, and the basketball event accepts male participants only.

The Mini Fun Sports Meet, themed “Youthful Energy, Joyful Fellowship,” will take place on May 20 at the STU athletics field. Teams will complete a series of fun games, with points determining the top three teams, which will receive cash prizes. Each team must include at least one club member and at least two female participants. Registration closes at 24:00 on May 16.

A club may enter multiple teams in either event. Registration forms and submission instructions are provided in the original OA attachments.

> ⚠️ Registration forms, competition rules, and submission instructions are available in the original OA attachments. Student names and personal email addresses are not reproduced here.
