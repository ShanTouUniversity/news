It is reported that Shantou University will hold a photography contest to record memorable scenes from its fifth Open Day. Students and staff are invited to submit related photographs. Each participant may submit no more than 10 images. Entries must be in JPG format and measure at least 1,024 by 768 pixels, with captions identifying the work and its author and affiliated unit.

Entries may be submitted by email, optical disc, or USB drive. Electronic files may be sent to the Admissions Office at `o_zsb@stu.edu.cn` or delivered to Room 406, west section of the Administration Building. The submission deadline is May 18. Detailed submission and file-naming requirements follow the university’s arrangements; students’ personal phone numbers are not reproduced here.

The contest will award one first prize worth RMB 300, two second prizes worth RMB 200 each, and three third prizes worth RMB 150 each. Awards will be determined according to the quality of the entries and may be left vacant. For submission questions, contact Huang or Qiu at `82903666`.
