It is reported that the second 2012 session of the Business School MBA Lecture series will be held from 09:00 to 12:00 on May 12 in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC). Xie Zesheng, a member of the Standing Committee of the Shantou Municipal Party Committee and secretary of its Political and Legal Affairs Commission, will discuss “Prevention and Response to Stability-Related Emergencies.”

The session will examine prevention mechanisms and response measures for emergencies that may affect social stability, offering an exchange for students, faculty, and community members interested in public governance and risk management.
