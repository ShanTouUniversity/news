It is reported that Shantou University’s Youth Volunteer Firefly Charity Fund, established in 2008, is recruiting three student representative directors. Guided by the STU Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association, the fund raises donations and operates the Firefly Scholarship, Firefly Assistance Fund, and special-purpose funds to support students and other designated groups in need.

The recruitment is open to students in the 2010 and 2011 cohorts and graduate students who will not be members of the Youth Volunteer Association in the next academic year, except volunteers. Applicants should have an interest in public service, relevant experience, and the ability to supervise the use of donated funds, participate in decisions, and perform their duties fairly.

The board of directors reviews the fund’s management rules, spending plans, annual financial reports, and work reports; supervises daily operations and use of funds; considers aid recipients; and decides other major matters.

Applications are open from May 11 through 19:00 on May 18. Students may download the form through OA or the Firefly website and email it to `fireflyfundofstu@163.com`. Paper forms may also be placed in designated collection envelopes or delivered to staff at publicity booths below Dining Hall 2 and outside Dining Hall 4 on May 15. Interview time and location will be announced separately.

> ⚠️ The registration form and fund-management materials are available through OA or the Firefly website. Applicants should not include unnecessary personal information such as their full name in email subject lines.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Firefly Charity Fund)
