It is reported that the fifth Overseas-Chinese Universities Debate Invitational will be held at Shantou University’s Science Lecture Hall from May 12 to 13. By 12:00 on May 11, 104 students and staff had successfully reserved seats for the preliminary round, and 153 had successfully reserved seats for the final.

Those with successful reservations must collect tickets from Room 721 of Building 7 between 09:00 and 12:30 on May 12, presenting a campus card or student ID. Those who did not reserve successfully may enter after 19:15 on the evening of the relevant event, subject to available seating.

Teams from Huaqiao University, Jinan University, Ningbo University, and Shantou University will participate. The detailed schedule was announced previously.
