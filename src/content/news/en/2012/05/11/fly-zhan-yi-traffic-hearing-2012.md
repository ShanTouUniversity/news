It is reported that the second “Wings” session of Shantou University’s FLY training program will hold a simulated hearing from 09:30 to 11:30 on May 13 in the Science Lecture Hall. The hearing will address traffic-safety problems in Shantou caused by inadequate road infrastructure.

Representatives from the Shantou Construction Bureau, Shantou Traffic Police Detachment, Shantou Television, and Shantou Public Transportation Corporation have been invited. Twenty-four participants from student organizations will form groups representing transportation authorities, traffic police, news workers, public transportation companies, and residents. They will propose solutions to the traffic-safety issue and present and defend their proposals at the hearing.

The activity is designed to encourage students to engage with social and public-livelihood issues and develop their ability to learn, identify problems, and find solutions through practice. All students and staff are welcome.
