It is reported that the final of Shantou University’s second Industry, Major, and Position Analysis Contest was held on May 9 in the lecture hall of the New Library. After the competition, the organizers awarded one first prize, one second prize, three third prizes, three finalist awards, and one popularity award.

The Dream Catcher team received the second prize. Guidance, Give Me Five, and Four-Person Gang received third prizes. Blue Sea, Five Strings Unlimited, and Sunny Skies received finalist awards, while Four-Person Gang also won the popularity award. The first-prize team and the complete award details are provided in the original OA attachment; part of the first-prize team name is corrupted in the collected text and is not reconstructed here.

The organizers congratulated the winning teams and thanked students and staff for supporting the contest.
