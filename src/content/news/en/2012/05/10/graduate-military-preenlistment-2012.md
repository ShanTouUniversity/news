It is reported that, under arrangements by the Guangdong Provincial Conscription Office and Department of Education, Shantou University has begun pre-enlistment for 2012 graduating students at regular universities. Eligible graduates are encouraged to consider military service.

From April 20 through July 20, graduating students may log in to the university student pre-enlistment systems at `http://zbbm.chsi.com.cn` or `http://zbbm.chsi.cn`, complete and print the Registration Form for Prospective Graduate Enlistees and the Application for Tuition Compensation and National Student Loan Repayment for University Graduates Enlisting in the Military, and submit them to Room 205 of the Student Affairs Office. After online registration, military authorities and the university will conduct preliminary political and physical screenings. Qualified applicants will be designated prospective enlistees.

Prospective enlistees must, by the end of October, take the reviewed and stamped forms to the county-level military service authority at their household-registration or place-of-origin location to apply for enlistment. That authority will give them priority under applicable rules. Applicants should accurately enter their permanent household-registration location, contact the relevant local military authority, and complete any required household-registration transfer procedures.

For questions, contact the Student Affairs Office at `82903299`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (People’s Armed Forces Department and Student Affairs Office)
