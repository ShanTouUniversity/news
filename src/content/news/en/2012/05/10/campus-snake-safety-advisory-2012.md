It is reported that Shantou University is surrounded by hills on three sides and has extensive woodland and shrubbery. As the weather warms, snakes and other insects have been seen more frequently along campus roads, in vegetation, and near buildings. University departments will soon clear overgrown vegetation and the boundary areas between buildings and wooded hills, and will use measures such as sulfur powder to deter snakes.

Southern Chinese snakes are generally active from April through November, with activity often increasing from July to September. STU has advised students and staff to limit activity in dense vegetation and avoid walking alone in isolated areas at night. Anyone who sees a snake on campus should keep away and avoid disturbing it or vibrating the surrounding ground and plants. Anyone bitten should stay calm, rest at the scene, call for help promptly, and remember the snake’s features if possible so medical staff can identify appropriate treatment.

The 24-hour Campus 110 emergency number is `82904110`, and the university hospital emergency number is `82902651`. The outpatient clinic of the Chaozhou Institute of Medical Research can advise on snake-antivenom serum at `0768-2297007`.
