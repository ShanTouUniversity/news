It is reported that the third lecture in Shantou University’s 2012 English Language Center Faculty Lecture Series will be held at 19:30 on May 10 in the Science Lecture Hall. Elena, John, and Jiin will lead the session on “University Life.”

The lecture is open to the university community and is designed to help participants learn about the topic while improving their English listening and speaking skills.
