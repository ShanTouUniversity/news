It is reported that the ABC-period class of Bridges, Architecture, and Culture, course number 51851, scheduled for Monday of Week 13, May 21, in Staircase Room E-201, has been cancelled because Wu Ming of the Department of Civil Engineering is traveling.

The make-up class will be held during the third and fourth class periods on Saturday of Week 12, May 19, in the same room. Students enrolled in the course should inform one another of the change.
