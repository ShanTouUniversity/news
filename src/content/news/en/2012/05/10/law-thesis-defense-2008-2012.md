It is reported that undergraduate thesis defenses for the 2008 cohort of Shantou University’s Law Department will be held on May 19 in six groups. The groups will use Rooms D-201, D-202, D-203, D-204, D-206, and D-207, with sessions from 08:00 to 11:30 and 14:00 to 17:00.

Michael Palmer will chair the defense committee. Members include Xiong Jincai, Deng Jianguang, Nie Shuo, Temur Guli, Ren Yue, and Li Guanghui. Students must submit three copies of their theses. Supervisors should collect the theses by May 11 and give them to the relevant group chair; group chairs should assign papers to reviewers, designate the primary examiner for each student, and set the defense order by May 15.

During the defense, each student must introduce the purpose, structure, main arguments, and innovations of the thesis within 10 minutes, then answer two or three questions from the primary examiner and any follow-up questions from other teachers. A student’s supervisor may not serve as the primary examiner for that thesis. Students should use PowerPoint for their presentations. Each group will recommend outstanding theses at a rate of approximately 15 percent, subject to final approval by the defense committee.

Scores are based on six components: 90–100 is excellent, 80–89 good, 70–79 fair, 60–69 pass, and 59 or below fail. A thesis involving serious plagiarism will automatically receive a failing grade. All graduating students must attend the defense to apply for a degree. Students may not request leave during the defense and must attend the entire process; lateness or early departure may affect the performance score.
