It is reported that the College of Science will hold a "Science Salon" lecture on the morning of May 14, featuring Prof. Michael Depledge of the European Centre for Environment and Human Health.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|

| Topic | Marine and Ocean Environment and Health |
| Time | May 14, 2012, 10:00–11:30 |
| Venue | Science Center, Room 205 |

## About the Speaker

Prof. Michael Depledge holds the Chair of Environment and Human Health at the Peninsula College of Medicine and Dentistry (University of Exeter, UK). He is Visiting Professor at the Department of Zoology, Oxford University (2007–present) and at University College London (2011–present), and previously served as Commissioner of the Royal Commission on Environmental Pollution and Chief Scientist of the Environment Agency of England and Wales.

He has published more than 380 peer-reviewed papers in leading international journals and books. In recognition of his contributions to comparative and medical toxicology, he was awarded a Doctor of Science (DSc) by the University of London (1996) and the Poulsson Medal of Honour by the Norwegian Society of Pharmacology and Toxicology (2009). An expert advisor on marine pollution to the United Nations, he has worked in Brazil, Costa Rica, India, Vietnam and other countries to develop the RAMP (Rapid Assessment of Marine Pollution) programme for UNEP's Global Oceans Observing System (GOOS), and has served as an expert advisor to the World Health Organization since 2001. He was Honorary Visiting Professor at the Harvard School of Public Health (1998–2004) and at Imperial College London (2003–2009), and currently leads the European Centre for Environment and Human Health's research on health and wellbeing derived from the natural environment.

All interested faculty and students are welcome to attend.
