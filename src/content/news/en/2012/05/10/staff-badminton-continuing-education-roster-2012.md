It is reported that the Sports Department at Shantou University has assigned participants to its continuing-education badminton elective for university faculty and staff. Because demand was high, the roster was determined on a first-come, first-served basis, with 40 participants in the class.

In response to requests from faculty and staff, the department plans to add another 12-hour badminton elective. Details of the new course will be announced separately. The confirmed participant roster is provided in the original OA attachment.
