It is reported that the fifth Overseas-Chinese Universities Debate Invitational will be held from May 12 to 13 at Shantou University’s Science Lecture Hall. Debate teams from Huaqiao University, Jinan University, Ningbo University, and Shantou University will compete to strengthen friendship and cultural exchange among overseas-Chinese universities.

The preliminary round will begin at 19:30 on May 12. Ningbo University will face Huaqiao University in the first debate on whether the rapid rise of television entertainment programs benefits or harms the contemporary television industry. Shantou University will face Jinan University in the second debate.

The final will begin at 19:30 on May 13. The motion will ask whether shaping a city’s cultural appeal should place greater emphasis on promoting local culture or absorbing outstanding outside cultures. The five-member judging panel will include three invited judges from Anhui Normal University, Anhui University, and Fujian Normal University, together with the coaches of the two teams not competing in that round. One judge will serve as panel chair.

Students and staff may reserve seats for either or both rounds at `http://activity.stu.edu.cn/` from May 10 until 12:00 on May 11.
