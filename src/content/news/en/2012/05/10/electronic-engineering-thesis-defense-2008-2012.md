It is reported that undergraduate students from the 2008 cohorts of Electronic Information Engineering and Communications Engineering in the Engineering School’s Department of Electronic Engineering will defend their graduation projects from May 18 to 19. The defenses will be organized into five regular groups and an outstanding-defense group. The original OA attachments provide the group assignments and committee rosters.

The five regular groups will meet on May 18 during the third and fourth class periods in the morning and the sixth through ninth periods in the afternoon. Their venues are Rooms E-303, E-406, E-407, E-408, and E-409, respectively. The outstanding-defense group will use the same morning and afternoon periods on May 19 in Room G-306.

Before each defense, the group will inspect the student’s completed graduation project. During the defense, each student should spend about five minutes introducing the thesis’s main arguments, purpose, structure, and features, answer committee questions, and prepare presentation materials in PowerPoint. A recorder will document the questions and answers. Students who self-nominate for the outstanding-defense group must give a complete presentation of their project or thesis in no more than 10 minutes, followed by about five minutes of questions.

Defense groups will determine grades by considering the project inspection, defense performance, supervisor’s recommendation, and peer-review recommendation. The five-level grading system consists of excellent, good, fair, pass, and fail. After the defense, students must revise and bind their theses according to the feedback and university requirements, then submit them to the department by May 24. Students must remain available until their final grades are confirmed so that errors can be corrected promptly.

> ⚠️ Group assignments, committee rosters, grading criteria, and detailed arrangements are available in the original OA attachments.
