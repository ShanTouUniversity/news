It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a Media Salon at 19:30 on May 20 in the lecture hall of Shantou University’s New Library. Cai Zhenting, a senior documentary producer in the Television Division of Radio Television Hong Kong, will speak on “The Truth, Goodness, and Beauty of Documentaries: Beginning with the Master Ji Xianlin.” The lecture is open to the STU community and counts toward continuing-education credits.

Cai graduated from the Journalism and Communication Department of the Chinese University of Hong Kong. From 1988 to 1990, Cai worked in London for BBC World Services as a radio host and producer. Most of Cai’s career has been spent in the Television Division of RTHK, where Cai is currently deputy director of the Public Affairs Group and leads documentary and television commissioning projects. At the invitation of the Asia-Pacific Broadcasting Union, Cai produced the co-produced documentary series “After the Tsunami” and “Avian Influenza Outbreak.”

Cai’s documentaries have received multiple festival awards. “Kangqiang Ji: Life After the War” on postwar Cambodia won the News Documentary Gold Award at the Chicago Television Festival. “Outstanding Chinese: Ji Xianlin” won a Gold Award in the cinematography category and a Silver Award in the biography category at the same festival. “Outstanding Chinese: Bai Xuexian” received the jury’s Grand Award in the 2005 Hong Kong Television Appreciation Index survey.

Continuing-education participants may register at `training.stu.edu.cn` using their personal campus-network usernames and passwords.
