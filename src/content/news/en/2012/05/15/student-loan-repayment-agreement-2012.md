It is reported that, at the request of Bank of China Shantou Branch, Shantou University will organize the signing of repayment agreements for 2012 graduates with outstanding national student loans. The session will begin at 14:15 on May 24 in Staircase Room E-101. Colleges and residential colleges will notify the relevant graduates according to the roster in the original OA attachment.

Graduates must bring the original national student-loan contract and copies of the specified pages, the original second-generation identity card and a copy with both sides on one A4 sheet, the original Bank of China repayment passbook and a copy, and a black pen. Repayment passbooks should be checked at a bank branch in advance; account holders whose accounts are invalid should notify bank staff at the session.

The schedule is as follows: check-in and collection of agreement materials from 14:15 to 14:30; an introduction to repayment procedures and requirements by the Student Affairs Office from 14:30 to 15:00; agreement signing for relevant graduates from the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design, Law School, and Engineering School from 15:00 to 16:00; and signing for relevant graduates from the School of Science, Business School, College of Liberal Arts, and Zhicheng College from 16:00 to 17:00.

Graduates who have already repaid their loans do not need to sign an agreement, but must submit their loan-settlement notice to the Student Aid Management Center, Student Affairs Office, Room 102, for filing. Those wishing to repay early may apply at the session. The center will coordinate further arrangements with the bank for anyone unable to attend.

For questions, contact the Student Aid Management Center at `0754-82904749` or `s_xtchen1@stu.edu.cn`.

> ⚠️ The attendee roster, repayment guide, and related materials are available in the original OA attachments. Student rosters and personal identity information are not reproduced here.
