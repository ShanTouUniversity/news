It is reported that the previously cancelled sessions of the Department of Social Sciences course Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics, sections 52217 and 52218, will be made up on Saturday of Week 12, May 19.

Section 52217 will meet during the first and second periods in Room E-407 of the Teaching Building. Section 52218 will meet during the third and fourth periods in Room E-202. Students enrolled in the relevant sections should inform one another and attend on time.
