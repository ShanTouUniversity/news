It is reported that the undergraduate thesis (design) defense for civil engineering students who enrolled in 2008 at Shantou University's College of Engineering will be held on May 25. The defense is overseen by a committee chaired by Lin Peng, with members including Zhao Xiaohua, Dong Ping, Xie Xinlu, Liu Jinwei, Wu Ming, Huang Diyu, Xiong Guangjing, Lu Xiaohua and other faculty members.

## Defense Procedure

In line with the requirements of the university and the college, each student will have 15 minutes (including handover time). Students are required to complete and print the defense record form and scoring form in advance and bring them to the venue. Beyond questioning students on issues related to their thesis (design), the committee will assess each student's grasp of the fundamental theories, knowledge and skills closely related to the project, as well as their ability to analyze and solve problems. Each defense consists of a student presentation of no more than 8 minutes, followed by roughly 7 minutes of questions from committee members.

## Schedule

| Session | Time | Venue | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regular defense | May 25, 8:30–12:00 | Rooms D304, D403, D504, G307 | All students attend by assigned panel |
| Outstanding-performance session | May 25, 14:30–18:00 | Room D502 | For students applying for a grade of "excellent" |

The regular defense is organized into four panels, led respectively by Dong Ping, Xie Xinlu, Liu Jinwei and Wu Ming as panel chairs. The projects cover a wide range of topics, including long-span prestressed concrete industrial plant structures, metro transfer station structural design, high-rise residential building and structural design, steel-structure office buildings, interchange planning and design, and preliminary bridge design with construction organization.

The outstanding-performance session is chaired by Zhao Xiaohua with the full defense committee attending; Huang Diyu serves as secretary. Participants in this session are self-nominated and recommended by their supervisors.
