It is reported that the first-round undergraduate thesis defenses for the 2012 cohort of Shantou University’s Biology Department will begin at 14:30 on May 24 in four groups. The original OA attachments provide the student and thesis-topic assignments.

| Group | Venue | Chair and members | Secretary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lecture Hall 1 | Liu Yang (chair), Chen Shanwen, Li Shengkang, Huang Tongwang, Wu Danqi | Huang Bingxin |
| 2 | Lecture Hall 2 | Liu Zhu (chair), Hu Zhong, Lun Jingsheng, Du Hong, Zhong Mingqi | Xiong Jixian |
| 3 | Lecture Hall 3 | Chen Meizhen (chair), Zhang Yueling, Xie Liling, Yu Jie, Zhu Yankun | Xiao Xiang |
| 4 | Science and Technology Center 504 | Liu Wenhua (chair), Zheng Huaiping, Sun Zewei, Ding Lanping, Chen Weizhou | Li Ping |

The second defense for outstanding theses will be held at 14:30 on May 29 in Room D-401. Committee members are Zhang Yueling, Chen Meizhen, Liu Yang, Liu Zhu, and Liu Wenhua, with Yang Mulan as secretary. The original OA attachments provide the grading criteria and defense procedures.

> ⚠️ Student groups, thesis topics, grading criteria, and defense procedures are available in the original OA attachments. Student rosters are not reproduced here.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Biology Department, School of Science)
