It is reported that Shantou University’s faculty and staff basketball tournament will be held at 16:30 on May 18 on Courts 5, 6, and 8. Participating teams should arrive on time according to the schedule and email their player rosters to the university labor union at `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn`.

The draw produced two groups. Group A includes the Resource Management team, the Arts joint team, and the Business and Liberal Arts joint team. Group B includes the Administration team; the joint team of the Law School, School of Science, Library, School of Journalism, and Graduate School; the School of Science team; and the Engineering School team.

> ⚠️ The detailed schedule and roster-submission requirements are available in the original OA attachment.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Labor Union)
