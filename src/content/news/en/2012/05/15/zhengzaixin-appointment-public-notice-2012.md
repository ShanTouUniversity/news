It is reported that, following discussion at the sixth 2012 meeting of the Shantou University leadership and the fourth 2012 meeting of the Party Standing Committee, STU is proposing to appoint Zheng Zaixin as head of student affairs and deputy secretary of the Engineering School Party General Branch, with responsibility for presiding over the branch’s work. The university has published the information under its pre-appointment publicity procedures.

Zheng Zaixin was born in February 1971 in Jieyang, Guangdong, holds a bachelor’s degree, and is an economist. Zheng joined the Communist Party of China in June 1993 and studied civil engineering in the Engineering School at STU from September 1991 to June 1995. From July 1995 to March 1997, Zheng served as a political counselor for students and secretary of a student Party branch in the Engineering School. Zheng has worked in STU’s Organization Department since April 1997, serving as an organization officer, head of the Organization Work Section and Party branch secretary, and was appointed to an intermediate-level management position in July 2010.

Units or individuals with comments on the proposed appointment may contact the university Discipline Inspection Commission in writing, in person, or by telephone by May 22. Units must affix their official seal, while individuals must provide their real names.
