It is reported that the fourth event in Shantou University English Language Center’s 2012 Faculty Lecture Series will be held at 19:30 on Wednesday, May 16, in the Science Lecture Hall. Sandra, Basia and David will speak on the theme “Culture.”

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Series | 2012 English Language Center Faculty Lecture Series |
| Session | Fourth event |
| Theme | Culture |
| Speakers | Sandra, Basia and David |
| Time | 19:30, Wednesday, May 16, 2012 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | English Language Center |

The English Language Center said the event is intended to help participants learn about a new topic while improving their English listening and speaking skills. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
