It is reported that a Shantou University campus photography contest received 69 entries after opening on April 16. Following evaluation by professional teachers and a student vote, seven students received honors including first, second, and third prizes and the top-popularity award.

The organizers announced the results and winning roster, with detailed award information and works provided in the original OA attachment. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.

> ⚠️ The complete recipient roster and work details are available in the original OA attachment. Part of the contest title is corrupted in the collected text, so this article uses a generic title based on the confirmed information.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Shantou Youth newspaper)
