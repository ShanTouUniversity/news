It is reported that the 2012 Holistic Life Education lecture series of the College of Liberal Arts’ Centre for Christian Studies will be held at 19:30 on May 21 in the New Library lecture hall. Professor Leung Yuen Sang, head of Chung Chi College, dean of the Faculty of Arts, and chair professor in history at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, will speak on “Seeking Across East and West, Heaven and Earth: Thought, Scholarship, and Life.”

Professor Leung will draw on his life experience and scholarly journey to reflect on the boundaries between East and West, tradition and modernity, and life and scholarship. The discussion will consider how people can learn from and remind one another through sharing the different stages of their life journeys.

Professor Leung’s research focuses on modern Chinese history, the relationship between Confucianism and Christianity, overseas Chinese communities, and urban cultures in Shanghai, Hong Kong, and Shenzhen. His representative works include *Christianity and China*, *Modern China in Transition*, *Studies in the History of Singapore’s Chinese Society*, and *The Cross and the Lotus: Essays on Christianity and Chinese History and Culture*.
