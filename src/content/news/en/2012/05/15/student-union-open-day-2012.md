It is reported that the Shantou University Student Union will hold its first Open Day from May 16 to 20 to strengthen communication with the university community and present its collaborative, efficient, and innovative work.

The program has three parts. “Impressions of the Student Union” will run from May 16 to 20 at the Golden Triangle, featuring an annual-work exhibition and distribution of an annual report booklet. “All for You on Weibo” will use the official Shantou University Student Union account to report on the year’s work through posts and short videos during the same period. “Meet You at the Golden Triangle” will take place there on May 19, bringing the Student Union’s 10 departments and four teams shortlisted in the new Presidium election together for face-to-face exchange with students and staff.

All students and staff are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union)
