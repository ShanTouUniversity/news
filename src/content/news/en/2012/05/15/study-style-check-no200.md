It is reported that STU's "Three Styles" Construction Office has released its No. 200 study-style inspection bulletin. During week 11 of the spring semester of the 2011–2012 academic year, the inspection team carried out two unannounced checks at Teaching Building E, finding a total of 18 instances of students arriving late to class.

## Inspection Results

| Time | Venue | Findings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 8 (Tue), 2:00 p.m. | Building E | 3 students less than 5 minutes late |
| May 11 (Fri), 8:00 a.m. | Building E | 9 students less than 5 minutes late; 6 more than 5 minutes late |

According to the bulletin, the names of students who failed to observe university rules have been forwarded to their respective colleges, which are asked to provide criticism and education while continuing to strengthen work on academic atmosphere.

The office indicated that study-style inspections will continue as routine work, and urged students to observe classroom discipline and arrive on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU "Three Styles" Construction Office)
