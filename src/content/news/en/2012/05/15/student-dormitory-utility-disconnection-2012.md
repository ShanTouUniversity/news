It is reported that student dormitories that had not paid all utility bills for April 2012 and earlier by May 15 will have their power suspended from May 16. Power will be restored after all outstanding charges have been paid.

From May 16 to 18, the Network Center will process two deductions from campus cards each day. The first deduction will take place at 10:30, with power expected to return to successfully paid dormitories from 14:30 to 16:30. The second deduction will take place at 16:30, with restoration expected from 17:30 to 20:00. Dormitories paying after 16:30 will have power restored from 14:30 to 16:30 the following day. From May 18 onward, one deduction will be made daily at 16:30.

Students with insufficient campus-card balances should top up promptly. Those with insufficient bank-account balances should deposit enough funds during banking hours and complete the transfer to their campus card. Students who have lost their campus cards may use the utility-management system to set up proxy payment, or deposit cash into the designated Shantou University account at the STU branch of Bank of China and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for posting. The Finance Office does not accept cash directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office)
