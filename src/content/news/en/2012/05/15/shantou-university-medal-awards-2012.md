It is reported that Shantou University has announced the results of the 2012 Li Ka Shing Foundation Shantou University Medal selection. The award recognizes outstanding graduating students and reflects Li Ka-shing’s educational philosophy of being ambitious, knowledgeable, persistent, and accomplished.

Following recommendations from colleges and residential colleges and review by the University Medal Selection Committee, Qiu H, an accounting student in the Business School’s 2008 cohort, received the University Medal and RMB 10,000 from the Li Ka Shing Foundation. Six other students received nomination awards of RMB 2,000 each.

| Award | Recipient (privacy-protected) | College and program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| University Medal | Qiu H | Business School, 2008 Accounting cohort |
| Nomination award | Pan Y | Engineering School, 2008 Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation cohort |
| Nomination award | Zhang MS | Law School, 2008 Law cohort |
| Nomination award | Zhai LH | Medical College, 2007 Clinical Medicine cohort |

| Nomination award | Yang B | Business School and Zhicheng College, 2008 International Economics and Trade cohort |
| Nomination award | Qiu SQ | Medical College, 2005 Clinical Medicine, seven-year integrated bachelor’s and master’s program |
