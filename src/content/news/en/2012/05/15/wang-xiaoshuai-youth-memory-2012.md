It is reported that acclaimed director Wang Xiaoshuai will give a presentation titled “Memories of Youth” from 13:30 to 14:30 on May 19 in Shantou University’s Science Lecture Hall. Students and staff may reserve a place at `http://activity.stu.edu.cn/` by 11:30 on May 18.

Wang graduated from the Directing Department of the Beijing Film Academy and received the French Order of Arts and Letters in 2010. His film *The Days* was named by the BBC as one of the 100 greatest films since the birth of cinema and was the only Chinese film on the list. *Bian Dan, Guniang* was selected for the Cannes International Film Festival; *Beijing Bicycle* won the Silver Bear at the Berlin International Film Festival; *Shanghai Dreams* won the Jury Prize at Cannes; *In Love We Trust* won a Silver Bear in Berlin; and *Chongqing Blues* received the Directors’ Association of China’s Director of the Year award.

The film title in the original notice is truncated in the collected text and is not reconstructed here.
