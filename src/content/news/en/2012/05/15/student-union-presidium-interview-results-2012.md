It is reported that the first-round interviews for the Shantou University Student Union Presidium concluded on the afternoon of May 9. Since the university-wide selection began on April 26, the Secretariat of the Student Congress had received six preliminary candidate registration forms. Four campaign teams passed the first round and qualified to deliver second-round campaign speeches at the 19th Student Congress.

The advancing teams are Rainbow, Lemon Juice, Ranran, and V. The university’s published arrangements provide the detailed team information. Team-member names are not reproduced here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union)
