It is reported that the sixth “Bai Cao Ji Cup” Shantou University Mindful Hands DIY Activity recently concluded. Themed “Caring Action, Green Living,” the activity included the “Turning Waste into Treasure” and “I Am a Director” competition categories. After preliminary and final rounds, the organizers announced awards across the clothing and non-clothing divisions.

The non-clothing division received one first prize, two second prizes, two third prizes, and three merit awards. The clothing division received one first prize, one second prize, one third prize, and one top-popularity award. The complete list of winning works and recipients is provided in the original OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.

The activity was organized by the STU Communist Youth League Committee and STU Science and Technology Association and attracted broad attention and participation from the university community.
