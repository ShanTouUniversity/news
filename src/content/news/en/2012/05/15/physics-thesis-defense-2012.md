It is reported that undergraduate thesis defenses in Shantou University’s Physics Department will begin at 08:30 on May 30, pause for 10 minutes at 10:00, and resume at 10:10 until completion. The defenses will be held in four groups in Rooms D-502, D-503, Staircase Room E-201, and Rooms E-401 and E-408.

Li Shaohui will chair the defense committee, whose members are Su Jianxin, Ma Wenhui, Wang Jiangyong, Chen Changjin, and Yang Weifeng. The defense groups will consist of graduation-thesis supervisors; the original OA attachment provides the detailed groups and student assignments.

Students should submit their thesis or design work to their supervisors for review as early as possible and complete binding, review, and grading before the defense. Students whose papers have not been submitted or whose supervisors have not approved them for defense may not enter the defense process. Students must also prepare and practice a PowerPoint presentation of no more than eight minutes, arrive early, and copy all presentation files to the assigned computer.

Each student will give an approximately eight-minute presentation and answer committee questions for two to three minutes. Afterward, students must organize the questions and answers and submit them as instructed. Final grades will be determined by the defense committee based on recommendations from supervisors and reviewers. The six evaluation areas are topic background, research novelty, presentation ability, responses to questions, overall qualities, and thesis writing.

Each defense group will recommend three outstanding theses, for a total of 12, to a second defense at 14:30 on May 31 in the school’s conference room. The defense committee will make the final selection.

> ⚠️ Student groups, thesis-submission requirements, and detailed defense arrangements are available in the original OA attachment. Student rosters and personal contacts are not reproduced here.
