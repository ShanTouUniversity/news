It is reported that Shantou University's Research Office, together with relevant colleges, will hold a series of campus fund academic lectures from May 15 to June 10. All faculty members and students are welcome to attend.

According to the Research Office, the series is organized in accordance with the requirements of several university research fund management measures, including the Management Measures for Academic Innovation Team Construction and National Fund Cultivation Projects, the Management Measures for Humanities and Social Sciences Research Fund, the Management Measures for Young Researchers' Research Fund, and the Management Measures for Research Start-up Funds.

Under these regulations, all ongoing funded projects are required to conduct open academic exchanges on their research topics:

| Project Type | Lecture Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Academic Innovation Team Construction Projects | During the project period, the principal investigator or other core members shall give at least one university-wide academic lecture each year |
| Humanities and Social Sciences Research Fund Projects | During the project period, the principal investigator shall give one public lecture on the research topic |
| Young Researchers' Research Fund Projects | During the project period, the principal investigator shall give one public lecture on the research topic |
| National Fund Cultivation Projects | During the project period, the principal investigator shall give one public lecture on the research topic |
| Research Start-up Fund Projects | During the project period, the principal investigator shall give one public lecture on the research topic |

The Research Office stated that the lecture series aims to promote exchange and innovation across related disciplines. Detailed schedules are available in the attachments.
