It is reported that Elizabeth Cannon, president of the University of Calgary, will give an English-language public lecture at 19:30 on May 26 in the lecture hall of Shantou University’s New Library. The topic is “Building an Innovative University of the 21st Century: The University of Calgary Experience.”

Students and staff who wish to attend should reserve a seat online at `http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ActivityBooking.aspx` by 12:00 on May 21. Seating is limited and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
