It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a screening of “Gains and Losses” documentaries at 19:30 on May 18 in the lecture hall of Shantou University’s New Library. The event is open to the STU community.

The program includes four works: *The End*, which follows two cancer patients at the end of life; *Qiaopi*, which explores the history and culture of remittance letters; *Can This Dream Wait?*, about young gymnasts in Shantou; and *One Person’s Leprosy Rehabilitation Village*, which records the life of the village’s last remaining leprosy patient.
