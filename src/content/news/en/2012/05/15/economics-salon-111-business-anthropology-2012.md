It is reported that the Business School will hold Economics Salon Vol. 111 on May 16 in Conference Room 3 of the Exchange Building. The event also counts as part of the continuing education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice I”.

## Salon Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Series | Economics Salon Vol. 111 |
| Speaker | Ann T. Jordan, Professor of Anthropology, University of North Texas |
| Topic | What Business Anthropology Has to Offer in the Age of Globalization? |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, May 16, 2012 |
| Venue | Conference Room 3, Exchange Building |
| Language | English |
| Organizer | Business School |

Faculty who wish to have the participation counted for continuing education may register by email to o_sxy@stu.edu.cn; on-site attendance will be recorded and cross-checked. Undergraduates and postgraduates of the Business School and all faculty and students are welcome. A speaker profile is available in the OA attachment.
