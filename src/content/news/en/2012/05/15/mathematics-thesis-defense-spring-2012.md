It is reported that the spring-session undergraduate thesis defenses for the 2012 cohort of Shantou University’s Mathematics Department will be held from 15:00 to 18:00 on May 25 in four groups, in Rooms G-402, G-403, G-404, and G-407.

Ye Ruisong will chair the defense committee. Committee members are Xie Changzhen, Tan Chaoqiang, Yu Chengjie, Li Pengtao, Xu Fei, Lin Xiaoping, Wei Caimin, Yang Zhongqiang, Lin Furong, Chen Rixiang, and Gu Minqiang. The group arrangements are:

| Group | Venue | Defense panel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | G-402 | Xie Changzhen (chair), Tan Chaoqiang, Yu Chengjie |
| 2 | G-403 | Ye Ruisong (chair), Li Pengtao, Xu Fei |
| 3 | G-404 | Lin Xiaoping (chair), Wei Caimin, Yang Zhongqiang |
| 4 | G-407 | Lin Furong (chair), Chen Rixiang, Gu Minqiang |

The original OA attachments provide the student assignments, thesis topics, grading criteria, and defense procedures. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
