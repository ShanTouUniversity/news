It is reported that the 21st World Engineers Forum and Career Planning Forum will be held from 19:30 to 21:30 on May 18 in Staircase Room E-201. The theme is “Dreams and Reality: Career Planning for Science and Engineering Students.”

The speaker, Li Qing, holds a master’s degree in science and serves as project director at CLC Multimedia Engineering in Canada. Li will draw on academic and professional experience to discuss career choices for science and engineering students and ways to connect personal aspirations with practical development.

All students and staff are welcome.
