It is reported that STU has reached an agreement with Jieyang County Donghai Hotel Co., Ltd. on discounted accommodation, dining, and meeting services. The agreement applies to university students, faculty, staff, and units and is valid from May 21, 2012 to May 21, 2013. The hotel is in Jieyang County, about five kilometers from the airport.

Dining and meeting discounts include 10% off at the Chinese and Western restaurants, 12% off the Western restaurant buffet, and 10% off at the coffee shop. Special dishes, seafood, tobacco, and alcohol are excluded from the relevant discounts. Meeting rooms are available at 30% off; projectors, banners, and name boards are charged separately.

Accommodation prices are based on the rates in effect when rooms are booked:

| Room type | Regular price | Discounted price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| East Building standard twin | RMB 599 | RMB 238 |
| East Building standard single | RMB 636 | RMB 258 |
| East Building standard suite | RMB 1,000 | RMB 400 |
| East Building ministerial suite | RMB 1,800 | RMB 728 |
| South Building business twin | RMB 699 | RMB 278 |
| South Building business single | RMB 736 | RMB 298 |
| South Building executive room, single or twin | RMB 900 | RMB 360 |
| South Building business suite | RMB 1,500 | RMB 600 |
| South Building royal suite | RMB 14,600 | RMB 5,880 |

The agreement rates cannot be combined with other discount cards. Guests may receive breakfast according to the hotel’s standard complimentary-breakfast policy. Holiday rates are subject to the prices in effect at the hotel. Reservations are generally held until 18:00 each day, and checkout is at 13:00 the following day. Changes or cancellations should be reported to the hotel in advance.

Reservation and inquiry numbers: `3905888-3188` and `3905888-8188`; fax `0663-3219999`.
