It is reported that the 2008 cohort of STU’s Journalism and Communication School will hold undergraduate thesis defenses in six groups, involving 154 students, from May 28 through June 2. The defense process will include a thesis presentation, responses to questions from the panel, and evaluation by the defense group.

| Group | Students | Time | Venue |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | 28 | June 2, 08:30–17:30 | Small Studio |
| 2 | 28 | May 28 and 29, 08:30–12:30 | Glass Room |
| 3 | 25 | May 31, 08:30–16:30 | Small Studio |
| 4 | 20 | May 30 and June 1, 14:30–17:30 | Small Studio |
| 5 | 26 | June 1, 14:30–17:30 and 19:00–22:00 | Glass Room |
| 6 | 27 | May 30, 14:30–17:30, and May 31, 08:30–12:30 | Glass Room |
