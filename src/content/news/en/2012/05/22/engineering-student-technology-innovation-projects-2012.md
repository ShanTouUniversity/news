It is reported that STU’s Engineering School has opened its 2012 extracurricular technology-innovation project program for enrolled students. The program applies the EIP-CDIO engineering-education reform approach and explores practical, open innovation experiences to develop students’ project development, design, implementation, innovation, communication, leadership, and teamwork abilities.

Applications are open from May 22 through 31, and projects generally last six to twelve months. Each student may generally participate in only one project, while students who abandoned a previously approved project without justification may not apply. Cross-disciplinary and cross-year teams of undergraduates are encouraged. The school will also arrange “Face-to-Face with Mentors” sessions to support dialogue about project topics and proposals.

Project contacts must complete the application form and submit two printed copies and an electronic copy to their class monitor by 10:00 on May 31. Classes will consolidate the materials and send them to their department Youth League general branch. Each general branch must deliver the materials to the Engineering School Youth League Committee by 16:00 that day and send the electronic version to `yaoyi@stu.edu.cn`.
