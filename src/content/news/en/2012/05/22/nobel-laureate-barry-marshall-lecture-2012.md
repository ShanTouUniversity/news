It is reported that Nobel laureate Barry Marshall will visit Shantou University from May 30 to June 1 and deliver a keynote lecture.

Marshall, who shared the 2005 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Robin Warren for discovering the bacterium Helicobacter pylori and its role in gastritis and peptic ulcer disease, will speak at the honorary professorship conferment ceremony. His lecture, titled "Man versus Helicobacter," will be held from 19:30 to 21:00 on Thursday, May 31, in the University Auditorium and will be delivered in English.

A limited number of seats have been made available to faculty and students on the main campus. Reservations can be made at http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ActivityBooking.aspx until 12:00 on May 27 on a first-come, first-served basis.
