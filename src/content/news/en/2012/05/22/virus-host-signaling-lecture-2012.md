It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on May 25. Dr. Chen Honglin of the Department of Microbiology, Faculty of Medicine, the University of Hong Kong, will discuss Cellular Signaling Pathways in Virus–Host Interaction.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | *Cellular Signaling Pathways in Virus–Host Interaction* (Cellular Signaling Pathways in Virus–Host Interaction) |
| Speaker | Dr. Chen Honglin, Department of Microbiology, Faculty of Medicine, the University of Hong Kong |
| Time | 15:00, May 25, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |

## Research Areas

According to the speaker profile, Dr. Chen's research focuses on the pathogenesis of influenza and Epstein–Barr virus, the molecular basis of host–virus interaction and cross-species transmission, the molecular basis of influenza virus antigenic variation, and novel influenza virus vaccines. He has published more than 100 papers in international journals including *Nature* and *PNAS*.

The lecture is open to interested faculty and students. A detailed profile of Dr. Chen is available in the OA attachment.
