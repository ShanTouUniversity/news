It is reported that the Science School’s Science Lecture Series at STU will host Chen Honglin of the Department of Microbiology at the University of Hong Kong at 10:00 on May 25 in the New Library lecture hall. His presentation is titled “Infectious Disease in a Changing World: From SARS to Bird Flu.”

The lecture will use the transmission and evolution of infectious diseases in a changing world as a starting point. It will introduce research in molecular virology, including the mechanisms of influenza and Epstein–Barr virus pathogenicity, host-virus interaction and the molecular basis of cross-species transmission, antigenic variation in influenza viruses, and new influenza vaccines. SARS, avian influenza, and related public-health issues will also be discussed.

Chen earned his first postgraduate degree from Xiamen University in 1986 and later pursued doctoral study at the University of Hong Kong with support from a Li Ka Shing Scholarship. He conducted postdoctoral research at the Royal Postgraduate Medical School in the United Kingdom and Johns Hopkins School of Medicine in the United States, and has worked at the University of Hong Kong since 2003. He previously taught and conducted research in STU’s Biology Department on two occasions and has maintained research collaborations with STU’s Medical School and Biology Department. He has published more than 100 papers in international journals.

Students and staff interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
