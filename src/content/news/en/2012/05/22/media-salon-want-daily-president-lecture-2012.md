It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host a Media Salon on May 26, featuring Huang Qinglong, President of Taiwan's Want Daily, as the keynote speaker.

The lecture, titled "Media and Taiwan's Political Development," is scheduled for 10:00 to 12:00 on May 26, 2012, at E-Lecture Hall 101.

Huang is a veteran journalist in Taiwan with 25 years of experience, having served as reporter for United Daily News, news director of Independence Evening Post, chief editorial writer of China Times Express, and editor-in-chief and vice president of China Times. From 2006 to 2008, he was a visiting scholar at the East Asian Institute of Columbia University in New York and the Center for Northeast Asian Policy Studies at the Brookings Institution in Washington, D.C. After returning to Taiwan following the second party rotation in May 2008, he was entrusted with launching Want Daily, which debuted in August 2009 as Taiwan's first newspaper dedicated to cross-strait and mainland China news. In October 2010, he also launched Want Daily English Edition, guided by the motto "Taiwan Spirit, Chinese Perspective, Global Vision." His publications include *Will Newspapers Still Have a Tomorrow? Observations on Media Development in the US, Japan and China*. Want Daily operates under the philosophy of "understanding mainland China and grasping the world's future," serving as a bridge across the strait.
