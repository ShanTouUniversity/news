It is reported that, following recent reports of late returns and activities such as computer use during rest hours that disturbed other residents, STU’s Student Affairs Office has reiterated relevant requirements in STU’s Residence-Hall Rules. Students must return to their residence halls before 23:00 each night and may not go out freely afterward. They should also follow rules on public order, security, and safe use of electricity.

The university will conduct inspections of late returns, overnight absences, and visits by people of the opposite sex in the residence areas. Students are expected to cooperate. Violations that continue after education and are serious in nature will be handled under STU’s Trial Regulations on Disciplinary Actions for Student Violations.

Students may report or provide information about violations in the residence areas at `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn` to help maintain a civilized, safe, and orderly living environment.
