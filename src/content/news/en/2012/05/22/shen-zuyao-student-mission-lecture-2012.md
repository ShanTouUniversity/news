It is reported that the fourth 2012 CCS Whole-Person Life Education public lecture will be held at 19:30 on May 30 in the STU Grand Auditorium. Professor Shen Zuyao, president of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, will present “Finding My Cheese: The Mission of University Students in the 21st Century.”

Using the story from *Who Moved My Cheese?* as a starting point, the lecture will consider how students can plan and make choices amid change and what responsibilities and mission they carry as they prepare for the future. Drawing on his work as a physician, scientist, professor, and university president, Shen will discuss aspirations, responsibility, dedication, and enthusiasm for public service.

Faculty and students on the main campus may reserve seats through the university activity-booking system. Reservations close at 12:00 on May 27, and seats will be assigned on a first-come, first-served basis while available.
