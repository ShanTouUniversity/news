It is reported that the 13th Standing Committee of the Shantou University Student Organizations Federation is nearing the end of its term. The 14th Federation Council Congress is scheduled for June 2 and will conduct the election for the next Standing Committee. Nine student candidates will be recruited from across the university: one president, two vice presidents, and six committee directors, with specific portfolios assigned according to candidates’ preferences.

Candidates should have strong academic and personal records, support student organizations and campus cultural development, be able to represent and protect organizations’ interests, and help coordinate relations among student organizations, the university, and society. They should also understand the federation’s current development, demonstrate initiative, have student-work and community-activity experience, and be able to serve continuously for one academic year.

Applicants must complete the preliminary candidate registration form and submit at least 300 Chinese characters describing their plans for the position. Materials are due by 23:00 on May 26. The preparatory committee plans to hold interviews on the evening of May 27. Formal candidates will give public speeches at the June 2 council congress and be selected through a competitive election.

The registration form, submission requirements, and subsequent arrangements are provided in the original OA attachments. Personal contact details are not published here.
