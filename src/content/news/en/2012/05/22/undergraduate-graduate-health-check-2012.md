It is reported that annual health checks for STU’s 2008 undergraduate and 2009 postgraduate cohorts will begin on May 24. The Resource Management Office has notified the Youth League organizations of the schools and Zhicheng College and asks the relevant units to cooperate and organize students’ participation according to schedule.

The detailed times, venues, and arrangements for each group are provided in the original OA schedule.
