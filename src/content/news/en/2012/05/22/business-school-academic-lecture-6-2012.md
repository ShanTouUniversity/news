It is reported that the sixth 2012 academic lecture of STU’s Business School will be held from 14:30 to 17:30 on May 24 in the academic seminar room on the second floor of the Wenxi section of the Business School. The event will discuss modern economic-management issues including corporate governance, external auditing, the open economy, and low-carbon technology transfer.

| Speaker | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| Liu Wei | Research on the Effects of Equity Governance in Listed Companies |
| Zhang Ling | External Auditing, Accounting Information, and Debt Financing |
| Wang Yuhua | Transformation of China’s Open-Economy Development Model in the Post-Crisis Era |
| Zhao Peihua | Evolutionary Game Analysis of International Low-Carbon Technology Transfer Strategies |

Business School postgraduate students are expected to attend, and students and staff from across the university are welcome. The event also forms part of the staff continuing-education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice I.” Teachers seeking credit may register as required and sign in at the venue. Registration email: `o_sxy@stu.edu.cn`.
