It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on May 24, 2012, announcing course selection and textbook ordering for the summer and fall 2012 semesters.

## Course Selection

| Stage | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Initial selection | 09:00, May 25 – 17:00, May 27, 2012 (unchanged selections carry over as final) |
| Re-selection | 12:30, June 1 – 17:00, June 3, 2012 |
| Add/drop | 12:30, June 15 – 17:00, June 20, 2012 |
| Adjustment | 09:00, September 17 – 17:00, September 21, 2012 (fall semester only, first week of the semester) |

Credit limits are 6 credits for the summer semester and 25 credits for the fall semester (excluding public service courses). Change requests must be submitted via the credit system with instructor signatures.

## Textbook Ordering

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Ordering period | 09:00, May 25 – 17:00, June 3, 2012 (aligned with initial and re-selection) |
| Method | Order via the course selection system and confirm the list by June 3 |
| Supplier | Guangdong Xueyuan Cultural Development Co., Ltd. (30% discount except for ideological and political theory textbooks, at 76% of list price) |
| Notes | Courses with multiple optional textbooks default to all; remove unwanted items via the workflow; prices are estimates and may change |

Students are advised to read the course selection rules and consult advisors to complete selection and ordering on time.

> ⚠️ The course selection rules and ordering workflow are available on the OA system.
>
