It is reported that STU’s Science School Youth League Committee will hold its Career Workshop series on May 27 for second- and third-year undergraduates. The sessions are designed to strengthen employability, improve career planning, and prepare students for employment and entrepreneurship. All sessions will take place in the lecture hall of the Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research, formerly the Multidisciplinary Center.

| Session | Time | Topic and speaker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 08:30–10:30 | “Winning Strategies for Resumes and Interviews,” Zhang Xiuling, assistant manager in terminal human resources at Chao Hong Ji |
| 2 | 10:45–11:45 | “My Job-Hunting Journey,” alumni guests from China Guangfa Bank Shenzhen Branch, Chow Tai Fook Jewellery Shenzhen, Beijing Bei’ao Group, Xiamen Tianma Microelectronics, and Watsons Juice and Drinks |
| 3 | 14:30–16:30 | “Have You Found an Internship Yet?”, Chen Zijie, manager of the education division at Qiya Group |

The first session will explain resume writing, written tests, and interviews from a corporate recruitment perspective. The second will share job-search experiences from graduates in different fields. The third will discuss the role of internships in career planning and preparation and how employers assess interns.

The Science School Youth League Committee will continue to provide internships of different types, and students who complete the full Career Workshop program this year may receive priority consideration. Participants should dress formally, arrive 15 minutes early, attend the sessions in full, and sign out at the end. Student alumni names are not reproduced here.
