It is reported that STU’s 2012 undergraduate thesis defenses in the Business School have been divided into 11 groups, assigned to Rooms D201, D202, D203, D204, D401, D402, D403, D206, D207, D406, and D407. The schedule involves 289 students. Four additional students were approved to defer their defenses to June 3 for special reasons; detailed times will follow the school’s arrangements.

The defense process includes an announcement by the group chair, a thesis presentation of no more than 10 minutes, questions from faculty, student responses, faculty evaluation, and calculation of the group’s overall results. Afterward, each group will complete the committee comments and submit the materials to the Academic Affairs Office for filing.

The number of outstanding theses in each group may not exceed 15% of its students. Students who fail the defense will be handled under university regulations. Papers that do not meet formatting requirements must be revised within one week, and disputed matters will be referred to the school’s thesis defense committee. Student names and individual panel rosters are not reproduced here.
