It is reported that the seventh 2012 academic lecture of STU’s Business School will be held from 09:00 to 12:00 on May 28 in the academic seminar room on the second floor of the Business School. Professor Dai Daochuan will present “Examining the Historical Trajectory of Globalization.”

The lecture will examine globalization and its evolution from a historical perspective, offering students and staff an academic framework for understanding global economic and social connections. The event also forms part of the staff continuing-education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice I.” Teachers seeking credit may register as required and sign in at the venue. Registration email: `o_sxy@stu.edu.cn`.
