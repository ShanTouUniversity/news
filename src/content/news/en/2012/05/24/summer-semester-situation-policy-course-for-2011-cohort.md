It is reported that the Office of Academic Affairs and the Department of Social Sciences have reminded students of the 2011 cohort at the main campus to enroll in the "Situation and Policy Education (Theory)" course offered in the 2012 summer semester.

Under regulations from the Ministry of Education and the Provincial Department of Education, "Situation and Policy Education" is a compulsory course for university students, consisting of a theory component and a practice component totaling 2 credits. In line with the university's summer semester teaching plan — where the summer term is a formal semester under the trimester system with total teaching hours at roughly 15%–20% of a spring or autumn semester and typically offering general compulsory courses such as ideological and political theory courses — the Department of Social Sciences will offer the 1-credit theory course in the 2012 summer semester. The course is designed specifically for the 2011 cohort and will run 20 classes with sufficient places available.

Students who do not enroll in this offering may face delayed graduation due to unfulfilled credit requirements.

The companion 1-credit "Situation and Policy Education (Practice)" course requires students to study the annual "Current Affairs Report (Student Edition)" and the key points for situation and policy education in higher education during their second, third and fourth years. Materials are available for download on the "Ideological and Political Education Online" platform. In the fourth year, students will complete a critical review of a current affairs report based on topics assigned by their supervisors and informed by three years of study and observation of major domestic and international events. Enrollment for that course will be announced in semester six, with completion in semester seven.
