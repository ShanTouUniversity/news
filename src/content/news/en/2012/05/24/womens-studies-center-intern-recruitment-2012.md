It is reported that the Center for Women’s Studies of the College of Liberal Arts issued a recruitment notice on May 24, 2012, for one Website Technical Assistant intern.

## Position

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Position | Intern — Website Technical Assistant |
| Number | 1 |
| Requirements | Basic knowledge of website management and maintenance; familiarity with web design and graphic editing; ability to work independently |
| Responsibilities | Daily backend maintenance and updates of the center’s website; assisting with graphic processing and publishing of event reports; completing other tasks assigned by the center |
| Allowance | Paid according to the university’s work-study standards |

## Application

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | 17:00, May 27, 2012 |
| How to apply | Send the electronic *Application Form for Interns at the Center for Women’s Studies* to cws@stu.edu.cn, with subject “Application for Intern at Center for Women’s Studies” and file named after the applicant (e.g., Zhang San) |
| Interview | Shortlisted candidates will be notified for an interview; please bring original or electronic portfolios; results will be notified by phone |
| Contact | Ms./Mr. Huang |
| Phone | 82903082 |
| Email | cws@stu.edu.cn |
| Website | http://cws.stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Center for Women’s Studies, College of Liberal Arts |
