It is reported that the English Language Center announced on May 24, 2012 that, as the publisher has discontinued the JieJin series, it has updated textbooks for the following courses. Students are asked to select courses and order the corresponding textbooks.

## Textbook Changes

| No. | Course | Textbook | Author | Publisher |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ELCF | New College English Integrated Course 1 | Wang Haixiao | Higher Education Press |
| 2 | ELC1 | New Standard College English Integrated Course 1 (1st Edition) | Chief Editor: Wen Qiufang | Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press |
| 3 | ELC2 | New Standard College English Viewing, Listening and Speaking Course 2 | Chief Editor: Wen Qiufang | Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press |
| 4 | ELC3 | New Standard College English Viewing, Listening and Speaking Course 3 | Chief Editor: Wen Qiufang | Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press |
| 5 | ELC4 | Academic English (Social Sciences) | Ji Peiying / Zhang Ying | Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press |
