It is reported that STU’s Science Lecture Series will host Lin Junda, professor at the Florida Institute of Technology and director of its Vero Beach Marine Laboratory, at 09:00 on May 28 in Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building. He will present “Seahorse Reproduction and Aquaculture.”

The presentation will introduce research on the reproductive biology and aquaculture technology of seahorses, a distinctive and valuable marine fish. Lin will also draw on his personal experience to discuss scientific research and questions about life paths with younger students.

Lin earned a doctorate in ecology from Rutgers University in 1985 and a doctorate in oceanography from the University of North Carolina in 1989. He conducted postdoctoral research at the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center, taught at the Florida Institute of Technology from 1996, and became a professor in 2002. His research covers sexual selection and evolution, marine ecology and resource conservation, and aquaculture. He has published more than 60 papers and served on editorial boards or as a reviewer for journals in aquaculture and marine biology.

Students and staff interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
