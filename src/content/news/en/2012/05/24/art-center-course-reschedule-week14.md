It is reported that eight courses taught by Liu Guanghui, Yu Duanrong, and Dong Xuemin of Shantou University's Art Education Center will be rescheduled, mainly involving class sessions in week 14. The Academic Affairs Office has published the arrangements and asked students enrolled in these courses to spread the word.
