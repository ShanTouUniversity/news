It is reported that STU’s Business School will hold its fourth Chaoshan Business Forum from 09:30 to 11:30 on May 27 in the New Library lecture hall. Yang Yuzhi, president of the Bank of China Shantou Branch, will present “The Path of Commercial-Bank Innovation and Integrated Operations.”

The lecture will review the development of China’s commercial banks since the beginning of reform and opening up, analyze the current competitive and operating environment, and discuss the need for and current state of banking innovation and integrated operations. Drawing on the Shantou branch’s experience, it will also cover innovation in products and services, operating and management concepts, and efforts to expand the local economy.

Yang will offer advice to university students on innovation, broad knowledge, strong conviction, and entrepreneurial practice and share three lessons from the workplace. The event also forms part of the staff continuing-education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice I.” Teachers seeking credit may register as required and sign in at the venue. Registration email: `o_sxy@stu.edu.cn`.
