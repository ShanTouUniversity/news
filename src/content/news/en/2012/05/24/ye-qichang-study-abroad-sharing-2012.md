It is reported that the 25th session of STU’s Career Launch Forum, an alumni study-abroad experience-sharing event, will be held from 19:30 to 21:30 on May 27 in the New Library lecture hall. STU alumnus and PhD holder Ye Qichang will discuss his experiences in the United Kingdom, the UK education system, and skills, procedures, and considerations for overseas-study applications.

Ye is currently chief consultant at Shenzhen Boyang Education Consulting Co., Ltd. He previously worked at LaSalle Investment Management’s real-estate fund and Enflet Partners, contributing to projects including the economic feasibility report for the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge and an economic-benefit assessment of Hong Kong International Airport’s third runway. After earning an international-finance bachelor’s degree from STU’s Business School, he received a UK government scholarship and a full Cambridge Overseas Trust scholarship. He studied at Queens’ College, Cambridge, and King’s College London, earning a master’s degree in real-estate finance and a PhD in economics.

During his doctoral study in the UK, Ye also participated in projects on China’s banking market at the Bank of England and HSBC. Students and staff are welcome to attend.
