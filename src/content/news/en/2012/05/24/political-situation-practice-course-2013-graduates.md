It is reported that STU’s Social Sciences Department plans to offer the Policy and Current Affairs Education Practice course in fall 2012 for undergraduates graduating in 2013 from the main campus. This primarily covers students in the 2009 cohort graduating in the first half of 2013, along with some graduates from other cohorts. The university-wide compulsory course carries 16 class hours and one credit.

Students must log in to the credit-system portal during the enrollment period and select the course under “Guided Courses.” The course does not require regular classroom attendance. With guidance from their instructors, students will write a review paper on major domestic and international current-affairs issues from the previous three years. Papers must follow the template in the attachment, contain 3,000 to 5,000 Chinese characters, and be submitted to the selected class instructor by December 31, 2012. Students must pass to receive the credit; otherwise graduation may be affected.

Students should study the past three years of *Current Affairs Report (Student Edition)* and the Ministry of Education’s teaching points for higher-education Policy and Current Affairs Education. Materials may be purchased or accessed through “Ideological and Political Online” at <http://skbonline.stu.edu.cn/asp>.

The course is offered only once each fall for graduating students. The 2013 graduating class must enroll during the current or supplementary enrollment period; late enrollment may not be possible. Students graduating early must also submit a printed application. Later graduating cohorts will follow a similar approach.

> ⚠️ The teaching points, paper template, and course arrangements are available in the original OA attachments.
