It is reported that STU’s Science School has opened applications for its 2012 extracurricular academic and scientific projects. The program is open to enrolled undergraduates. Projects must be completed by students in their spare time while at university and may not be part of the teaching plan; course assignments, graduation theses, graduation designs, and annual papers are ineligible.

Projects generally fall into five categories: social-science survey reports and academic papers; academic papers in science, engineering, agriculture, and medicine, open only to undergraduates; inventions and productions; literary and artistic works; and business plans. Cross-disciplinary teams are encouraged, with clear division of responsibilities. Each student may participate in only one project and may not submit overlapping applications.

Students may choose their own topics and seek faculty guidance or select topics proposed by faculty members. Each supervisor should generally guide no more than two projects. Applicants must submit two printed copies of the research-project application to the Science School Youth League Committee office by 17:30 on May 31 and send an electronic copy to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn`. The school will arrange expert review separately.
