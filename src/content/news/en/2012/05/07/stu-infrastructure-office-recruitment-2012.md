It is reported that, to support its infrastructure work, Shantou University’s Human Resources Office and the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office are recruiting eight engineering management staff members from the public. The openings cover water and electrical management, civil construction management, project tendering, project approvals, and maintenance engineering.

| Position | Places | Main requirements and responsibilities |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Water and Electrical Management Engineer | 2 | A bachelor’s degree or above in a related field and experience in engineering and site construction management; technical management, water and electrical coordination, quality and progress checks, quantity review, and acceptance |
| Civil Construction Management Engineer | 3 | A bachelor’s degree or above in civil engineering, construction engineering, or a related field and relevant project experience; technical management, early site preparation, quality and progress checks, quantity review, and acceptance |
| Project Tendering Supervisor | 1 | A bachelor’s degree or above and at least two years of tendering-management experience; organization of tenders, document preparation, registration, information release, bid review, clarification, and archiving |
| Project Approval Supervisor | 1 | A bachelor’s degree or above and at least two years of project-approval experience; preparation of application materials, internal approvals, coordination, follow-up, and archiving |
| Maintenance Engineering Supervisor | 1 | A college degree or above and at least three years of building maintenance-management experience; maintenance plans and budgets, project applications, design, tendering, contracts, progress, settlement, and inspections |

Applicants for the engineering positions should be familiar with CAD and common office software and have organizational, communication, and teamwork skills. Relevant intermediate professional titles or professional registration qualifications are preferred for some positions. The notice generally requires applicants to be under 45, in good health, professionally ethical, free of adverse records, and compliant with the stated family-planning policy; detailed conditions are subject to the recruitment notice.

The positions will be hired as auxiliary contract staff. Employment relationships will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow university regulations. Applicants must submit a résumé and copies of identification, academic credentials, professional training certificates, and technical qualification certificates by May 27. Materials may be sent in a sealed letter to the Infrastructure Office of STU’s Resource Management Office or emailed to `o_jjc@stu.edu.cn`. Candidates who pass the initial review will be contacted for interviews and must bring original documents for verification.

Applications should be addressed to the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University’s Resource Management Office, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Contact: Teacher Liu; telephone 0754-82903890 or 0754-82902890; fax 0754-82510511. Email subjects should state “Application for XXXX.”
