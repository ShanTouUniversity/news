It is reported that the Shantou University Student Organizations Federation is holding its second Student Organization Incubation Program on campus from May 5 to 20. The program is open to the entire university community and provides a platform for registration, training, presentation, and defense for new student organizations.

The program schedule includes:

| Stage | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Registration form submission | May 5–11 |
| Preliminary review | May 11 |
| Training workshops | May 12–16 |
| Complete materials submission | May 5–18 |
| Online voting | May 13–19 |
| New organization popularity showcase | May 19 |
| Organization incubation defense | May 20 |

Registration closes at 24:00 on May 11. Applicants should download and complete the electronic registration form and send it to `11zyxue@stu.edu.cn`. Student organizations established through the program may receive up to RMB 600 in initial activity funding.

> ⚠️ The registration form and activity materials are available in the original OA attachments. Personal consultation contacts are not reproduced here.
