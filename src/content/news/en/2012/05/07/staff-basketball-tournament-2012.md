It is reported that Shantou University’s 2012 staff basketball tournament is scheduled to begin on Friday, May 18. Seven teams will participate: the College of Liberal Arts and Business School joint team; the School of Science; the School of Engineering; a joint team from the Law School, Research Office, News Center, and Library; a joint team from the Arts Education Center, Department of Social Sciences, School of Continuing Education, and Affiliated Primary and Secondary School; the administrative offices; and the Resource Management Office.

The 2011 men’s basketball champion and runner-up, the Resource Management Office and the administrative offices, will be seeded into Groups A and B respectively. The remaining teams will be assigned by drawing lots. Each team should send a representative to the university Labor Union at 10:00 on Wednesday, May 9, for the draw.
