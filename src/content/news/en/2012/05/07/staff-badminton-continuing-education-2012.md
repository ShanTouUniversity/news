It is reported that, to support the university’s development goals and improve service and teaching quality, Shantou University’s Sports Department will offer a continuing-education elective, Badminton Training, for university staff.

The course will run for four weeks from May 16 to June 6, every Wednesday from 19:00 to 21:00, for a total of 12 class hours. It will be held in the Gymnasium. Experienced Sports Department instructors will use explanations and demonstrations to help participants improve badminton techniques and tactics, learn training methods, and exercise.

Interested staff members may register through `http://training.stu.edu.cn` using their personal campus-network account information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office and Sports Department)
