It is reported that, under the Shantou Municipal Party Committee and Shantou Municipal Government decision on honoring labor models, outstanding workers, and advanced collectives, several Shantou University faculty members and an office have received municipal honors.

Ma Weiping, an associate professor in STU’s Sports Department, and Yang Wei, Party secretary of the Second Affiliated Hospital of the Medical College, were named Shantou labor models. The Mental Health Center’s Office for the Prevention and Treatment of Mental Disorders was named a Shantou advanced collective.
