It is reported that the Youth League Committee issued a notice on May 7, 2012, that the final of the 8th Challenge Cup University Student Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Works Competition is scheduled for May 9.

## Background

The competition was announced on April 20, with 49 entries received by May 2: 12 natural science papers, 17 philosophy and social science reports and papers, and 20 inventions. Shortlisted entries for the final will be notified by phone.

## Final Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 14:30, May 9, 2012 |
| Venue | Main venue: Science Lecture Hall; Invention category: Science Lecture Hall; Social Science category: Room 205; Natural Science category: Room 504, Marine Biology Research Center |
| Procedure | Opening ceremony → 5-minute PPT presentation (plus 1 minute for enhancement projects) → 5-minute Q&A with judges → judges’ comments → announcement of results |
| Requirements | Formal dress, check in 15 minutes early, bring inventions for the relevant category; enhancement projects with value will receive funding and may represent the university at the provincial 12th Challenge Cup |

All faculty and students are welcome to attend. Details are available in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The list of entries is available on the OA system.
>
