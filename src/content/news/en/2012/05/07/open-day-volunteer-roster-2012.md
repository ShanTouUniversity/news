It is reported that the fifth Shantou University Open Day volunteer recruitment received 328 applications. Based on merit-based selection and application time as the tie-breaker for otherwise equal candidates, the university’s Communist Youth League Committee selected 170 students as volunteers for the 2012 STU Open Day and placed another 20 students on the waiting list.

The Open Day is intended to introduce STU to the wider community and increase awareness of the university among nearby residents, particularly high school students. Selected volunteers should prepare for their service; training and detailed arrangements will be announced separately.

The detailed roster is available in the original OA attachment. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy. Students not selected for this activity may continue to follow other volunteer opportunities at the university.
