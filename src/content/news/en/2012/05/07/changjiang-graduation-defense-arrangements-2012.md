It is reported that graduation design and thesis defenses for all majors in the 2008 cohort of STU’s Changjiang College of Arts and Design will be held from May 8 to 14.

Because the OA notice did not list the detailed arrangements for each major, the students and faculty concerned should consult the original attachments for the specific times, locations, and group assignments and attend according to the college’s schedule.

> ⚠️ Detailed defense schedules and group assignments are available in the original OA attachments.
>
