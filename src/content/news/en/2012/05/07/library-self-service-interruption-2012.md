It is reported that Shantou University Library’s servers for its self-service printing, copying, scanning, and computer-room management systems require hard-drive replacement. The related services will therefore be briefly interrupted from 15:00 to 17:00 on May 7.

During the maintenance period, self-service printing, copying, and scanning will be unavailable. Public computer terminals for reader searches may also be unstable, including login failures or automatic restarts. The Library advises readers to avoid using the search computers during this period where possible. Those who need to use them should save important data frequently. The restoration time will be announced separately.

For inquiries, contact 82902953 ext. 6210 or `cfdong@stu.edu.cn`.
