It is reported that the 2012 National English Major Test Band 4 oral examination will be held at 08:30 on Saturday, May 12, in the audio-visual room of Building E at Shantou University. The Academic Affairs Office has asked the College of Liberal Arts to notify the candidates concerned.

Examinees must bring their admission card, identity card, and student card. Before the examination, they should read the Candidate Instructions and Examination Guide for the Band 4 English oral test issued by the national office for English oral examinations and familiarize themselves with the requirements and procedures.

> ⚠️ The candidate instructions, examination guide, and related materials are available in the original OA attachments.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
