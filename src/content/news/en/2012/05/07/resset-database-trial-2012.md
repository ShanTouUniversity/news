It is reported that Shantou University’s Library has opened a trial of the RESSET/DB financial research database from May 7 to August 6, 2012.

RESSET/DB supports empirical research and model testing. Its coverage includes stocks, fixed income, funds, macroeconomics, industries, economics and law, Hong Kong stocks, foreign exchange, futures, and gold. The platform contains nearly 100 databases, hundreds of Chinese and English tables, and nearly 20,000 fields, supporting research in economics, finance, accounting, and investment.

Students and staff may access the database through the Library database page at `http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=350`. The login method and browser requirements are provided in the Library’s original notice. Feedback and acquisition recommendations may be sent to the Library’s subject services department at 3247 or `yxu@stu.edu.cn`.
