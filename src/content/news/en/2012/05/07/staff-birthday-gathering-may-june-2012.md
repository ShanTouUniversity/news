It is reported that, with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Shantou University’s Labor Union will hold a birthday gathering for staff with May and June birthdays at 18:30 on May 19 in the Western Restaurant of Junhua Hotel. Staff members and their families may attend with tickets.

Registration will run from 12:00 on May 8 through 24:00 on May 13. Payment will take place from May 14 to 16, and tickets can be collected from 10:00 to 12:00 on May 18. Staff members may register at `http://booking.stu.edu.cn/` using their campus-network account and password, selecting the month of their own birthday. Those paying in cash should follow the activity plan and exchange the bank receipt for an admission ticket at the Labor Union.

Tickets cannot be refunded after registration and payment. The university will not arrange transportation, so participants should travel independently and take care. Parents bringing children must supervise them and ensure their safety. Participants are also asked to avoid wasting food.

For booking-system problems, contact the Network Center at 3548 ext. 8842 or 3551 ext. 8842.

> ⚠️ The activity plan and payment instructions are available in the original OA attachments.
