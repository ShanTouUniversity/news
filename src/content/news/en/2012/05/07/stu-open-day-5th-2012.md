It is reported that Shantou University’s fifth Open Day will be held on campus from 09:00 to 16:00 on May 13, with campus working hours from 08:30 to 16:30. The event is open to students, parents, alumni, and members of the wider community and is intended to showcase the university’s reform and development while strengthening communication between STU and society.

Activities will include a team development camp for secondary students, a voice-training lecture, science experiments, arts workshops, student organization performances, and interactive games. Visitors will be able to observe and experience aspects of university students’ extracurricular life. The Library, Grand Auditorium, Gymnasium, and laboratories of the colleges will also be open for tours.

The event will provide information about STU’s educational features, reform and development, overseas exchange, scholarships and financial aid, career development, and employment. On-site consultations will cover career planning and college-entrance examination preference selection. Alumni reception, alumni information collection, and alumni-card registration will also be available.
