It is reported that Shantou University’s Graduate School and Resource Management Office will conduct a comprehensive inspection of electric water heaters in graduate dormitories on the main campus from May 9 to 11. Each room must arrange for one student to remain present during the inspection and assist the maintenance staff, supporting a safe residential environment.

The schedule is as follows:

| Date | Time | Area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 9 | 08:00–12:00 | Floors 1–3 of Building L |
| May 9 | 14:00–18:00 | Floors 4–5 of Building L |
| May 10 | 08:00–12:00 | Floors 1–3 of Building Y |
| May 10 | 14:00–18:00 | Floors 4–7 of Building Y |
| May 11 | 08:00–12:00 | All floors of Building B13 |
| May 11 | 14:00–18:00 | All floors of Building B14 |

Graduate students should arrange for someone to be present in their rooms and cooperate with the inspection.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School and Resource Management Office)
