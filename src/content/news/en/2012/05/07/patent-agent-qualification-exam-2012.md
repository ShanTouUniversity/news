It is reported that the State Intellectual Property Office has released its notice on the 2012 National Patent Agent Qualification Examination. Shantou University’s Research Office has forwarded the information to students and staff.

Those interested in applying may consult the State Intellectual Property Office website for detailed eligibility, registration arrangements, and examination requirements. After registering, applicants should submit their application information to the Research Office for record-keeping.

The announcement is available at `http://www.sipo.gov.cn/zwgs/gg/201204/t20120425_679334.html`. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong at `dengyong@stu.edu.cn` or 82903271.
