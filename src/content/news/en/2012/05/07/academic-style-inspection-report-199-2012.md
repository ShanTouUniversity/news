It is reported that Shantou University’s Office for the Development of the Three Campus Cultures recently reported the results of academic-style inspections during Week 10 of the 2011–2012 spring semester. The inspection team checked class attendance at Building E at 14:00 on May 3 and 08:00 on May 4.

The two inspections recorded 24 instances of students arriving late, all by no more than five minutes. Nineteen instances were recorded on May 3 and five on May 4. The university distributed the relevant student list with the notice to colleges and residential colleges, which will provide educational guidance and continue efforts to strengthen academic conduct.

> ⚠️ The student list and related attachment are available in the original OA notice.
