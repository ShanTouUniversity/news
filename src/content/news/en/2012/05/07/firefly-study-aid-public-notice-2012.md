It is reported that the Shantou University Youth Volunteer Firefly Fund has completed its review of applications for the Xuehai Yingguang Student Aid program. The program received 32 applications. Following review by the fund’s board, 20 students have been proposed as aid recipients, and the proposal is now open for public comment.

The detailed proposed recipient roster is included in the original OA attachment. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy. Units or individuals with comments may submit them to the fund in writing or by telephone before 17:00 on May 7. Units must affix their official seal, while individuals must sign with their real names.

The fund’s email address is `fireflyfundofstu@163.com`.
