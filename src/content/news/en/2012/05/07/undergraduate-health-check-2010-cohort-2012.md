It is reported that health checks for Shantou University’s 2010 main-campus undergraduate cohort will begin on the afternoon of May 9. This year’s check consists only of a chest X-ray. Each major has been assigned two possible times, allowing students to choose one according to their class schedules.

The Resource Management Office has sent the detailed schedule to the Youth League committees of the colleges and Zhicheng College and asked the units concerned to assist with the organization. Students should confirm their assigned major’s arrangements in advance; the detailed sessions are provided in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The schedule for each major is available in the original OA attachment.
