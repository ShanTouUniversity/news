It is reported that, in accordance with relevant documents from the Shantou Municipal Education Bureau, the STU Affiliated Primary School will register eligible first-grade children of Shantou University staff members. The registration applies to children who turn six in 2012, meaning those born on or before August 31, 2006.

Parents should register at the principal’s office of the STU Affiliated Primary School at 15:00 on May 8, so that the school can arrange textbook orders. They should bring the child’s birth certificate and household register, together with the parent’s work certificate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and STU Affiliated Primary School)
