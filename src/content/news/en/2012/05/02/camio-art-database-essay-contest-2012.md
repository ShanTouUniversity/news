It is reported that OCLC’s Beijing Representative Office has launched an essay contest for users of the CAMIO Art Museum Images Online database. Shantou University’s Library recently forwarded the information.

CAMIO contains art materials from more than 20 world-class museums, including images of more than 100,000 works dating from 3,000 BCE to the present. Its collection covers paintings, sculptures, buildings, jewelry, clothing, textiles, and printed materials, and is intended for teaching, research, and appreciation. The contest is open to faculty members and students who use CAMIO. Entries may discuss users’ experiences, the database’s impact on teaching or learning, or suggestions for improvement, and should contain at least 800 Chinese characters.

Entries must be sent to `China@oclc.org` by June 10, 2012. The prizes are one first prize of an iPad 2, three second prizes of 320 GB portable hard drives, six third prizes of wireless mice, and several merit prizes of gift items.

Founded in 1967, OCLC is a nonprofit cooperative and research organization providing electronic-resource and library services. Its services are used by more than 72,000 libraries in 171 countries and regions.
