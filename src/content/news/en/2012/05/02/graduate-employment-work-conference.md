It is reported that on May 3, 2012 (Thursday) at 4:00 p.m., the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will convene a graduate employment work meeting in Room 311 of the East Administration Building to coordinate employment efforts for that year's graduating class.

Those called to attend included Party general branch secretaries or deputy secretaries of each college and residential college, counselors responsible for employment work, and staff from the Career Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | May 3, 2012, 16:00 |
| Venue | Room 311, East Administration Building |
| Organizer | Career Guidance Center, Student Affairs Office |
| Attendees | College/residential college Party secretaries (deputies), employment counselors, career guidance staff |

Falling at a critical stage of the job-hunting season for graduates, the meeting will bring together employment coordinators from across the university's colleges, reflecting the institution's emphasis on graduate employment.
