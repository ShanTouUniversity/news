It is reported that Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the schedule for Career Counseling and Training because Wang Bin of the Student Affairs Office will attend advanced outdoor development training.

Class 52548 was scheduled to meet during periods 8 and 9 in Room G-304 on Monday, May 7, the 11th week of the semester. That class has been cancelled. A make-up class will be held during the same periods and in the same room on Monday, May 21, the 13th week. Students enrolled in the course are asked to inform one another of the change.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
