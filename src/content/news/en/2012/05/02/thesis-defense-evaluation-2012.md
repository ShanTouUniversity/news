It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on May 2, 2012, regarding the defense and grading for undergraduate graduation theses (design projects) for the spring semester of 2011–2012.

With the thesis work entering its final stage, the office set out the following arrangements to ensure smooth completion:

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | Complete defenses, grading and entry by the end of week 14 |
| Integrity | Students must sign a Letter of Commitment to Academic Integrity and bind it into the original thesis; supervisors are responsible for checking |
| Supervision | Supervisors must ensure theses meet quality and formatting requirements |
| Regulations | *Shantou University Regulations for Undergraduate Graduation Theses (Revised)* (STU Document [2006] No. 108) |

## Defense and Grading Standards

1. Theses must receive comments and recommended grades from both the supervisor and the reviewer before entering the defense, subject to review by the department defense committee;
2. Comments from supervisors, reviewers and the defense committee must be specific and substantive;
3. Grades must truthfully reflect students’ actual performance, with a reasonable distribution across excellent, good, medium, pass and fail;
4. Department committees may establish defense groups for specific defenses. When members disagree, the group should reach a recommended grade through discussion or voting, and the recommended comments should reflect differing views; the final comments and grade are determined by the department committee;
5. One week before defenses begin, departments should publish the committee and group members, as well as the schedule and grouping, via the office automation system and file them with the Academic Affairs Office (electronic documents to yzchen@stu.edu.cn).
