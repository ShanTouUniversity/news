It is reported that Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department has announced the schedule for submitting Party membership materials in the first half of 2012. The arrangement is intended to support the departure of graduating student Party members and the university’s first-half Party statistics.

Beginning May 22, the university will stop accepting materials for probationary Party members among the 2012 graduating cohort, with the date of the relevant branch meeting used as the basis. Graduating students whose probationary periods end before July 2012 should complete regularization procedures before leaving the university. Materials for the first-half intake of probationary members and regularization cases must reach the Organization and United Front Work Department by June 15. Materials concerning 2012 graduates are due earlier, by June 1, so that approval and archive transfer can be completed before departure.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
