It is reported that Shantou University’s Business School will hold its fourth academic lecture of 2012 from 10:00 to 11:30 on May 4 in the Business School Academic Seminar Room. Mao Shangyi, a doctoral researcher at Xiamen University’s Wang Yanan Institute for Studies in Economics, will discuss the factors that shape older Chinese adults’ happiness.

The lecture will examine how living with children and grandchildren may affect older adults’ well-being. The study uses data from the 2005 Chinese General Social Survey and applies an instrumental-variable method to analyze the causal relationship between living arrangements and happiness. It finds that, among older adults living with grandchildren, living with children has a negative effect on happiness. It also finds that older adults who live with grandchildren report greater happiness than those who do not.

All students and staff are welcome.
