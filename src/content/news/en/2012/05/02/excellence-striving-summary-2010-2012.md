It is reported that the Office of Shantou University’s Leading Group for the Excellence-Striving Campaign has begun organizing a summary of the campaign conducted from 2010 to 2012. The Party Committee of the Medical College, Party general branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches are expected to review the campaign and summarize the experience and results of developing advanced grassroots Party organizations and outstanding Party members.

Each report should cover the campaign’s implementation, practices and experience, major achievements, remaining problems, and suggestions for the university’s future work. Reports should be focused and supported by concrete facts and reliable data, with a length of approximately 3,000 Chinese characters.

Units must submit printed reports to Room 405, Xingdong Building, by May 20 and send electronic copies to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. The contact person is Ji Rongxin, at 82902345.
