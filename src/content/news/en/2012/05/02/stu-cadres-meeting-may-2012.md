It is reported that Shantou University will hold a cadres’ meeting at 15:00 on Thursday, May 3, in the Lecture Hall of the Science and Technology Center. The meeting will bring together university leaders, heads of university-level departments, heads of teaching and support units, and selected representatives of faculty, the Party Congress, the Staff Congress, and retired staff. Members of the Medical College leadership and relevant representatives from its affiliated hospital will also attend.

The attendance arrangements include university leaders; heads of university-level departments; heads of teaching and support units, including departments, centers, and institutes; two representatives each of senior-title faculty, the Party Congress, and the Staff Congress from each relevant college; heads of grassroots democratic parties; and five representatives of retired staff. The Medical College leadership, the president and Party secretary of its affiliated hospital, and four representatives each of senior-title faculty, the Party Congress, and the Staff Congress from the Medical College will also attend.

University units must submit their attendee lists to Room 405, Xingdong Building, or email them to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` by the end of the working morning on May 3. Participants should arrive 10 minutes early.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
