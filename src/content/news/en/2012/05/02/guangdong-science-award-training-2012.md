It is reported that the Guangdong Awards Office will hold a science award recommendation training session in Shantou on May 8. Shantou University’s Research Office recently forwarded the arrangements. The session is intended mainly for researchers with scientific, technological invention, or applied-technology achievements, as well as staff who manage scientific and technological achievements at relevant enterprises and public institutions.

The training will cover Guangdong’s science award recommendation requirements, the appraisal and registration of scientific and technological achievements, and the operation of the Guangdong Integrated Science and Technology Management System. It will take place from 14:30 to 17:30 on May 8 at the Shantou Science Museum, 12 Haibin Road.

Interested faculty members should send the registration reply to `ygluo@stu.edu.cn` by the end of the working day on May 4. A shuttle will be arranged for participants on the day of the training. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, at 82903271.
