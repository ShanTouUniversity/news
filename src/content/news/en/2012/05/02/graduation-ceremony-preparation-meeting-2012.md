It is reported that Shantou University will hold the first preparatory meeting for its 2012 graduation ceremony and awards ceremony at 15:30 on Thursday, May 3. The meeting will take place in Conference Room 2 of the University Exchange Center.

Responsible officials from the Party and Administrative Office, Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, Academic Affairs Office, Graduate School, Publicity Department and News Center, Resource Management Office, Security Office, Network and Information Center, Sports Department, Center for Arts Education, and the Student Affairs Office of the Medical College are expected to attend. The meeting will coordinate preparations for the two ceremonies.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office)
