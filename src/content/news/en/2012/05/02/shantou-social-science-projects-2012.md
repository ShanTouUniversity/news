It is reported that the Office of the Shantou Social Science Planning Leading Group has opened applications for its 2012 social science planning projects. Shantou University’s Research Office recently forwarded the information. Proposals should address current priorities such as Shantou’s development and integration among Shantou, Chaozhou, and Jieyang, and may focus on the following areas:

| Area | Area | Area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shantou’s development trends and strategy | Emerging strategic industries | Transformation of traditional industries |
| Blue-ocean development and the marine economy | Shantou’s role in Shantou–Chaozhou–Jieyang integration | Regional resource integration and collaboration |
| Improving livelihoods and regional cooperation | Transport systems and regional integration | Comprehensive marine tourism development |
| National e-commerce demonstration city development | Social organization development | Innovation in social administration and public services |
| Balanced development of education | Integrated urban-rural social security | Cultural development and cultural soft power |
| Cultural-system reform and cultural industries | Inheritance and innovation of Chaoshan culture | Urban renewal and historic-building preservation |
| The Chaoshan people and China’s modernization |  |  |

Project leaders should hold a senior professional title. Applicants without one must provide written recommendations from two peer experts holding full senior professional titles. Each leader may submit only one project and may not participate as a member of another project team. Each team must include at least three people. Once approved, a project should begin research and writing promptly and submit a paper or research report of at least 10,000 Chinese characters by September 10.

Applicants must submit the application form to the Research Office for a formal review by May 22. Materials that pass the review must be printed double-sided on A4 paper, submitted in five copies by May 28, and accompanied by an electronic copy sent to `ljwang@stu.edu.cn`. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Wang Liju, at 82902563.
