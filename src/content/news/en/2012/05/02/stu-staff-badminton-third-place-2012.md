It is reported that the Shantou University staff badminton team competed in the Qiangli Cup May Day team invitational tournament for Shantou’s education, culture, health, and sports system from April 27 to 28. The team finished third in the competition.

The result reflects the university staff’s active participation in sports and inter-organizational activities.
