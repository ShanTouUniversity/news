It is reported that, following the university-wide call to select the Shantou University Student Union Presidium, the Secretariat of the Student Congress received six preliminary candidate registration forms by May 7. The first-round interviews will be held at 14:00 on May 9 in Room 726, with six campaign teams participating.

The interview panel will consist of teacher representatives from the Communist Youth League Committee and the current Student Union Presidium. The process will include a 30-minute presentation of each team’s members and work plan, a 10-minute reciprocal question-and-answer session based on the teams’ plans, and a 10-minute panel question session. Teams should check in 10 minutes early and send a representative to Room 721 of Building 789 at 21:30 on May 8 for the interview-order draw.

To protect student privacy, team-member names are not reproduced here. Detailed candidate-team information follows the university’s arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union)
