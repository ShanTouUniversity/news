It is reported that Shantou University’s Ninth Graduate Student Congress and Graduate Union election will be held at 19:30 on May 18 in the Science Lecture Hall. The agenda includes hearing and reviewing the work report of the 17th Graduate Union, recognizing outstanding members of the 17th Graduate Union, and electing the Presidium of the 18th Graduate Union.

Members of the 18th Graduate Union Presidium will be selected through a candidate campaign. Graduate students may download the electronic registration form from the OA attachment or the Graduate Union website and send it to `STU_YH2011@163.COM`. They may also submit a paper form in person at Room Y604. Registration closes at 12:00 on May 12.

All students and staff are welcome to attend and observe. Personal contact details are not reproduced here to protect privacy.

> ⚠️ The registration form and congress materials are available in the original OA attachment.
