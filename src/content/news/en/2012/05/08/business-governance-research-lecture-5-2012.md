It is reported that the fifth 2012 academic lecture of Shantou University’s Business School will be held from 10:00 to 11:30 on May 10 in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor. Guo Liang, who holds a PhD in business administration from Lincoln University in New Zealand, will discuss new developments in corporate governance research.

Business School graduate students are expected to attend, and all students and staff are welcome. The speaker’s biography is provided in the original OA attachment.
