It is reported that Shantou University’s Communist Youth League Committee has decided to recognize groups and individuals who performed well in the university’s Youth League work in 2011. Over the past year, the university’s Youth League organizations advanced work in ideological education, organization building, skills development, social practice, technological innovation, entrepreneurship practice, and campus culture, while promoting organizational innovation and excellence-striving efforts.

The university will recognize 25 outstanding Youth League branches or general branches, 47 outstanding Youth League cadres, and 263 outstanding Youth League members. The detailed roster is included in the original OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.

The Youth League Committee encouraged the recognized groups and individuals to value the honor and continue improving. It also called on Youth League organizations and members to learn from them and contribute to campus academic and ethical culture and the university’s development.
