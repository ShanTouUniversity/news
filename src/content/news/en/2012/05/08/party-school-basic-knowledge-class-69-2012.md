It is reported that the 69th Basic Party Knowledge Class of the Shantou University Party School will begin on May 13. Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches should designate representatives to collect admission notices by May 10 and notify the participants concerned.

Participants must report from 13:30 to 17:00 on May 11 to the Party School office in Room 401, east section of the Administration Building, bringing their admission notice and one one-inch, front-facing ID photograph. Group leaders must attend a meeting at 21:00 that day in Room 311, east section of the Administration Building. Participants who need leave should complete a leave-approval form, obtain approval from their Party general branch, and process it at the Party School office. Unexcused absences from classes or examinations will result in cancellation of enrollment.

The course schedule, participant roster, leave form, and participant rules are included in the OA attachments. The roster is not reproduced here to protect personal information.
