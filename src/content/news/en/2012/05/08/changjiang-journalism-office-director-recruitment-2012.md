It is reported that, to meet staffing needs, Shantou University has opened an internal recruitment process for one director of the Office of the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication.

Applicants should hold a graduate degree and have passed College English Test Band 4 or an equivalent level. They should be conscientious, team-oriented, and able to communicate effectively. The position also requires strong written and spoken English, proficiency with office software, administrative-management and official-document experience, organizational and coordination skills, and at least three years of work experience within the university.

The director will oversee the office’s general affairs, seal and certification management, and support for teaching and research. Responsibilities include drafting annual plans, development plans, work summaries, biweekly reports, and annual reports; reviewing information for the school website and office automation system; assisting with committee meetings, minutes, and follow-up; coordinating requests and reports; and supporting procurement, personnel, publicity, and ISO-related work.

Applications and eligibility review will take place from May 8 to 10, with interviews scheduled for May 11. The interview time and location will be announced separately. Applicants should submit a résumé and a statement of their work plans for the position to Room 403, Xingdong Building, Human Resources Office. Contact Teacher Zhang at 82902344 or `gqzhang@stu.edu.cn`.
