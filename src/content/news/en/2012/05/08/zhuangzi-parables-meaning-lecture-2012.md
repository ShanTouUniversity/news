It is reported that STU’s Department of Social Sciences will hold an academic lecture from 15:00 to 17:00 on May 10 in Room E-408 of the Teaching Building. Dr. Gao Qingrong will speak on “The Meaning of Zhuangzi’s Parables.”

Using close analysis of the parables in Zhuangzi as its starting point, the lecture will consider their value for understanding and interpreting Zhuangzi’s thought and the intuitive features of traditional Chinese ways of thinking. The lecture is part of STU’s continuing-education program and counts for three class hours. All students and staff are welcome, and participants may register at `http://training.stu.edu.cn`.
