It is reported that the Qiaotou Street dragon boat team representing Jinping District will conduct pre-competition training at Shantou University’s Riyuekeng Reservoir from May 9 to 22 in preparation for the 2012 Shantou Dragon Boat Race. The reservoir is located west of ACC, and training will take place daily from 17:30 to 19:00.

The Security Office asks students and staff not to gather around the reservoir to watch the training during this period, in the interest of safety.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office and Resource Management Office)
