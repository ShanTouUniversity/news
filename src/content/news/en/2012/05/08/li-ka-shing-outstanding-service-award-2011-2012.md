It is reported that Shantou University has begun selecting recipients of the 2011–2012 Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Service Award. The award recognizes teaching-support and administrative staff members who have achieved notable results in their day-to-day service work.

Relevant university units should follow the Guiding Principles and Selection Procedures for the Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Service Award and submit nominee lists together with the required electronic and printed materials by May 18. Detailed criteria and application requirements are provided in the original OA attachment.

Contact: Luo Feijun of the Party and Administrative Office; telephone 82902306; email `fjluo@stu.edu.cn`; address: Secretariat Section, central hall, fourth floor, east section of Xingdong Building.
