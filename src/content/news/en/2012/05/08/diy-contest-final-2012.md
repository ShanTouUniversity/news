It is reported that the preliminary round of the Baicaoji Cup Shantou University Sixth DIY Craft Competition concluded on May 5. Following evaluation of the entries’ creative concepts, methods, and substance, 19 teams advanced to the final. The final and awards ceremony will be held at 14:30 on May 12 in the Science Lecture Hall.

The competition will award first, second, and third prizes, merit awards, a popularity award, and first, second, and third prizes in the clothing category, for a total of 12 award places. The popularity award will be decided through a combination of on-site audience voting and online voting.

Each team will have three minutes to present and explain its work with PowerPoint. Teams must send a PowerPoint introduction to their work to `10wjsu@stu.edu.cn` by 23:00 on May 10. Finalists should arrive 30 minutes early for preparations. After the competition, all entries will be transferred to the Firefly Fund for a charity sale.
