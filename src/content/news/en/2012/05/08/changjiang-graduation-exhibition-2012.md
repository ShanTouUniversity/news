It is reported that Changjiang College of Arts and Design will hold its 2012 graduation works exhibition in two stages, open to all students and staff.

The first stage will run from May 10 to 30 and feature graduation works by the 2008 cohorts in graphic design, environmental design, product design, digital media, and public art. Venues include the Changjiang College Art Museum, the first-floor exhibition hall, the rear exhibition hall, Space 8 in Building 789, and selected outdoor areas. The second stage will run from June 4 to 13 at the college’s Art Museum and feature work by the 2009 graduate cohort.

The exhibition is organized by Changjiang College of Arts and Design. Wang Shouzhi is the exhibition consultant; Han Ran, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Wu Xiangyong, and Chen Biru are exhibition advisors; Li Aiyi is exhibition director; and Zhang Yi is responsible for exhibition coordination and visual design.
