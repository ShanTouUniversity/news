It is reported that, to mark the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China and prepare for the 18th National Party Congress, the Shantou University Party Committee has organized commendations for the 2010–2012 Excellence-Striving Campaign. The university plans to recognize advanced grassroots Party organizations and outstanding Communist Party members before July 1, highlighting their work in fulfilling responsibilities and serving development.

The selection will include 36 advanced grassroots Party organizations, with 16 from the main campus and 20 from the Medical College, as well as 180 outstanding Communist Party members. Recommendations will emphasize grassroots positions in teaching, research, and healthcare.

Advanced grassroots Party organizations should demonstrate strong leadership, Party membership, work mechanisms, performance, and public recognition. Outstanding Party members should lead in learning and improvement, achieving strong results, serving the community, observing discipline and law, and promoting positive conduct.

Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches on the main campus should make recommendations through democratic consultation and step-by-step review. Candidate organizations must submit a recommendation form and an approximately 1,000-character statement of achievements. Candidate Party members must submit a recommendation form and a similar statement. The Medical College Party Committee will organize its own selection. All units must submit recommendation reports, rosters, forms, achievement statements, and electronic copies to Room 405, Xingdong Building, Organization and United Front Work Department, by May 28.
