It is reported that to encourage students' innovation awareness and foster an innovative atmosphere, the "College of Engineering – O-Film Enterprise Innovation Club" will hold its first mechanical creativity competition, featuring two modules: a mechanical creativity contest and a CAD mechanical drawing contest, open to all students.

## Mechanical Creativity Contest

Participants need to conceive a creative idea and conduct feasibility and principle analyses with illustrations, covering required technologies, processing difficulty and budget estimates, then present it in a PPT report on presentation day. Each team's defense lasts no more than 5 minutes.

**Registration deadline**: May 27, 2012

| Award | Quota | Prize |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| First prize | 1 | 200 yuan |
| Second prize | 2 | 100 yuan |
| Third prize | 3 | 50 yuan |

Winners will also receive certificates.

## CAD Mechanical Drawing Contest

The contest consists of two parts:

| Track | Format | Time limit | Scoring |
| ----- | ------ | ---------- | ------- |
| Timed race | Individual only | Draw a uniform part drawing in 30 minutes on site | Accuracy 70% + time 30% |
| Difficulty challenge | Teams of up to 2 | Complete one of three assembly drawings graded A/B/C within 60 minutes | Difficulty 40% (A up to 40 pts, B up to 35 pts, C up to 30 pts) + accuracy 60% |

Due to limits on equipment and venue, only 30 slots are available for the timed race and 15 for the difficulty challenge, allocated on a first-come-first-served basis according to the order in which registration forms are received by email.

**Registration deadline**: May 13, 2012

For prizes, the top three of the timed race will receive 100, 80 and 50 yuan respectively; the difficulty challenge offers 200, 150 and 100 yuan, with certificates for all winners.

Interested students may download the registration form from the OA system and send the completed form to the designated campus email address.
