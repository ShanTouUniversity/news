It is reported that the Youth Volunteers Association of Shantou University, together with the Guitar Association, will hold the “Small Dreams” micro-charity concert on May 12, 2012, combining music and philanthropy to broaden participation in public welfare. All proceeds will be donated to the Firefly Love Foundation.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 19:30, May 12, 2012 (admission 18:50–19:30) |
| Venue | Grand Auditorium |
| Admission | Entry with a love ticket; no assigned seats, first-come first-served |
| Donation | 3 yuan or more per ticket (no change given) |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Youth Volunteers Association, Guitar Association |

## How to Get Tickets

| Method | Period | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weibo reservation | May 8–11 | Private message via the “STU Youth Volunteers Association” Weibo |
| Love hotline (SMS) | May 8–11 | Send required information to designated hotline numbers (SMS only, no phone calls) |
| On-site | May 9–12, 11:00–13:30 | Donate and collect tickets at booths at Jin San Jiao and the Fourth Canteen |

Hotline reservations will be delivered nightly from 22:00 to 23:30 on May 9–11. Reservation details, contacts and delivery arrangements are available on the OA system.
