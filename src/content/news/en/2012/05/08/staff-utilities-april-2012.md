It is reported that Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has published staff utility charges for April 2012. The latest meter reading was taken on May 1, following the previous reading on April 1. Staff members may check their charges through the STU utility management system at `http://power.stu.edu.cn`, using their personal campus-network accounts.

Questions or objections may be taken to the Water and Electricity Center from May 9 to 14 for verification. After the review period, charges without objections will be deducted by the Finance Office from staff members’ May 2012 salary accounts. Staff members without salary accounts should pay the charges directly at the Finance Office.

For inquiries, contact the Water and Electricity Center at 2489.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
