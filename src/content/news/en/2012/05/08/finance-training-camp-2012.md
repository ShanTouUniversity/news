It is reported that Shantou University’s Finance Association will hold a finance training camp from May 16 to 26. Open to all STU students, the camp will combine learning and practical activities in securities, insurance, and futures, and will select outstanding participants for summer internships at financial companies based on their performance.

The camp will include the following activities:

| Stage | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Opening and study | An opening ceremony on May 16 followed by a workshop on basic futures and stock-market knowledge led by professionals from Haitong Securities |
| Simulated stock trading | Online simulated investment and practical competition using designated trading software |
| Teamwork | Team development activities incorporating stock and futures knowledge to strengthen collaboration |
| Thematic interviews | Student-led discussions with securities professionals and university teachers on basic stock and futures knowledge and market developments |
| Insurance scenario simulation | Small-group exercises in insurance marketing and cold-call visits under the guidance of business guests |
| Internship selection | Based on company requirements and participant performance, approximately 20 students will be selected for one- to two-month internships at financial companies during the summer vacation |

Electronic registration forms should be sent to `11yzli@stu.edu.cn` by 12:00 on May 12. Paper forms may be submitted at the designated collection points, with locations provided in the original notice. Participants who complete the camp will receive certificates. Ten outstanding participants and one outstanding team will be selected; the outstanding team will receive RMB 100 and prizes.
