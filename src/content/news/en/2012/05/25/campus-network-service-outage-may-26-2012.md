It is reported that, because of the Resource Management Office’s construction-related power interruption, the network equipment room serving the Wenke Building’s east and west sections will stop operating from 08:00 to 13:00 on May 26. Campus-network service in the affected areas will be suspended during that period.

The affected locations include the Wenke Building, Audio-Visual Education Building, west side of the Administration Building, West Lecture Hall, and General Physics Laboratory. The Network and Information Center will shut down network equipment in stages before the power interruption and restart it in stages after power is restored to protect equipment stability. No separate service-restoration notice will be issued.
