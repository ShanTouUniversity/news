It is reported that, under STU’s Annual Student Science and Technology Competition Assessment Regulations, the university has published the results achieved by student groups and individuals in science and technology competitions at university level and above in 2011. An annual commendation meeting will be held to recognize groups and individuals who performed strongly in the previous year’s student research-initiation and competition activities.

Units and individuals with comments may submit them in writing or by telephone to the university Youth League Committee by 17:00 on May 29. Unit submissions must carry an official seal, while individual submissions must include the commenter’s real name.

Telephone: `82902302`; email `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. The complete results are provided in the original OA attachments; student names and rosters are not reproduced here.

> ⚠️ The student competition results and related materials are available in the original OA attachments. Student personal information is not reproduced here.
