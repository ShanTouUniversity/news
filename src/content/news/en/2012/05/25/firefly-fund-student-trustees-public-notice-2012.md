It is reported that STU’s Youth Volunteer Firefly Fund interviewed seven students who applied to serve as student representative trustees on the evening of May 20. After considering their application information and interview performance, the fund proposes appointing Zeng CX, Lu MJ, and Shen ZX as new student representative trustees.

The public notice is issued under the Firefly Fund Management Regulations. Units and individuals with comments may submit them in writing or contact the fund by 12:00 on May 29. Unit submissions must carry an official seal, and individual submissions must include the commenter’s real name. The fund’s official email is `fireflyfundofstu@163.com`. Student personal contact details are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee, Youth Volunteer Association, and STU Youth Volunteer Firefly Fund)
