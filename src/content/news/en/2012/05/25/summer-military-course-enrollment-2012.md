It is reported that STU will offer the military course in the summer semester of 2012 under the revised national syllabus for military courses at higher-education institutions. Military skills training and military theory together form a compulsory course. Students in the 2011 cohort and students whose training was deferred in earlier years must take the course this year.

The course is scheduled for the summer semester, but military skills training will actually take place within two weeks after the current semester ends because of teaching arrangements and the training unit’s operational duties. Enrolling in military theory will not affect students’ other summer-semester course selections.

When enrolling, students must choose the class number of a teacher from their own school or college. Students in residential colleges should choose a class taught by a residential-college teacher, while students who changed majors should select a teacher from their new school or college. Students who fail to enroll or choose the wrong class number will be unable to have their grades recorded.

Students without mainland household registration may participate after voluntary application and approval from their school or college and the People’s Armed Forces Department. Students with serious physical impairments, disabilities, or illnesses may receive approved exemptions from unsuitable military-skills subjects but must still enroll in and study military theory. Members of the 2011 cohort with special circumstances may apply to defer training and enroll when they make up the training. These applications must be submitted in writing to the relevant school or college by June 1.
