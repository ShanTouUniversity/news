It is reported that STU’s Local Government Development Institute has opened applications for its 2012 open-fund projects. Established in 2004, the institute is a key humanities and social-sciences research base among Guangdong universities and focuses on local-government development. It is inviting researchers in relevant fields across China to undertake collaborative research.

This year’s topics include integrated governance and new rural public-service systems; social development and innovation in social management; pluralistic dispute-resolution mechanisms; and special-administrative-region systems and national governance structures. Applications will undergo eligibility review by the institute’s administrative committee and a decision by its academic committee under principles of fair competition and merit-based support. Successful applicants will sign agreements and become part-time or full-time research members of the institute.

The institute plans to approve three projects, each with funding of RMB 15,000 to 20,000. Projects will last one to two years, from July 2012 through June 2014. The program is results-oriented: each project should publish at least two to three papers in CSSCI core journals, and research outputs must acknowledge support from the STU Local Government Development Institute Open Fund.

Electronic applications must be emailed by May 30. Three printed copies, stamped by the applicant’s institution, must be mailed to the institute by June 1, with the postmark determining timeliness. The printed and electronic versions must be identical.
