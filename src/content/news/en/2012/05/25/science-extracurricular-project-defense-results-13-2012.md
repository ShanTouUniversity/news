It is reported that STU’s Science School held the 13th Extracurricular Activity Project Defense on the afternoon of May 23 in the large conference hall of the Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research. The review panel evaluated 11 proposals that had passed the preliminary review. Following its review, the school approved seven projects for support, with total project funding of RMB 5,000.

Organizations whose projects were approved should implement their activities under the Science School’s Measures on Applications and Management of Extracurricular Activities. The projects are intended to advance project-based management of student activities and support campus cultural development.
