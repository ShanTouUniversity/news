It is reported that STU’s Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association have opened recruitment for the 2012 summer cultural, scientific, and health service program, commonly known as the “Three Rural Areas” social-practice program. The service trip is planned for mid-July in Dongshan County, Zhangzhou, Fujian. Students in the 2011 cohort will take part after military training. The university plans to recruit 50 volunteers through an interview-based selection process.

Applications close at 12:00 on May 31. Students should download the electronic application form from the OA system, complete it as required, and submit it. Interview registration and the interview time and venue will be announced separately. Volunteer hours will be recorded under the registered-volunteer management rules according to the actual number of service days.

Since 2011, the “Three Rural Areas” service program has counted as a public-service course. Students who complete the course and pass its assessment may receive one credit in the philosophy and social sciences category of the general-education curriculum. Further information is available on STU’s “Three Rural Areas” blog at <http://blog.sina.com.cn/u/1779192437>.

Student personal application contacts are not published here; the OA notice provides the application form and detailed requirements.
