It is reported that STU’s Science School will hold a Science Salon from 09:00 to 12:00 on May 27 in Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building. Professors Chen Liqiao of East China Normal University and Ji Hong of Northwest A&F University will present two research talks.

| Speaker | Topic | Research area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chen Liqiao, professor and doctoral supervisor at East China Normal University | Chinese mitten-crab aquaculture and feed technology | Aquatic-animal nutrition, aquatic biology, and germplasm genetics |
| Ji Hong, professor and doctoral supervisor at Northwest A&F University | Grass-carp lipid metabolism | Economic-aquatic-animal nutrition, aquaculture, and aquatic biology |

Chen has long worked on aquatic-animal nutrition and aquaculture and has led numerous national, ministry, and municipal research projects. His related work has received a second-class National Science and Technology Progress Award and first-class Shanghai Science and Technology Progress Awards. Ji studies lipid-accumulation regulation in freshwater fish, aquaculture models, and biological regulation of aquatic ecosystems. He has led multiple research projects and published more than 70 papers.

Students and staff interested in the topics are welcome to attend.
