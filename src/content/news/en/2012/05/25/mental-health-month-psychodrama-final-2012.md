It is reported that STU’s seventh Mental Health Publicity Month, themed “Passing Friendship On,” is approaching its conclusion. Six teams have reached the final of the psychodrama competition after the application and selection stages. The closing ceremony and final will be held at 19:30 on May 25 in the Science Lecture Hall, and students and staff are welcome.

The awards include one first prize of RMB 1,000 and a certificate, two second prizes of RMB 600 and certificates, and three third prizes of RMB 300 and certificates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office Mental Health Education and Counseling Center)
