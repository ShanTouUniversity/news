It is reported that the STU faculty and staff basketball semifinals will be held at 16:30 on May 25. Two games will take place at the campus basketball courts: the Liberal Arts and Business joint team will face the Engineering School on Court 6, while the Administration team will face the Arts and Design joint team on Court 8.

The matches are organized by the university labor union. Students and staff are welcome to watch and support the participating teams.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
