It is reported that STU’s Science School will hold a lecture on mathematical modeling from 16:30 to 17:30 on May 28 in Classroom E309. Professor and doctoral supervisor Liu Shenquan of South China University of Technology will present “Mathematical Modeling and Mathematical Modeling Competitions.”

Liu earned a doctorate in science from Beihang University in 1998 and visited the University of Sydney in 2003. He is currently vice chair of the Guangdong Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics. He has led three National Natural Science Foundation of China projects, published more than 60 related international papers, and was recognized in 2011 as an outstanding instructor in the National Undergraduate Mathematical Modeling Competition.
