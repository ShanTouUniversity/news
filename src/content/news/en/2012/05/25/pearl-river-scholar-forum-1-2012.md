It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education will hold the first Guangdong University Pearl River Scholar Forum at South China University of Technology on May 29. The forum will examine how Pearl River Scholars can provide leadership, momentum, and broader influence in talent development and academic research.

STU units with faculty members interested in attending must send their expressions of interest to the designated Human Resources Office email by 11:30 on May 28. STU will consolidate the names and submit them to the Human Resources Office of South China University of Technology.

The forum notice is available on the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education website at <http://www.gdhed.edu.cn/main/www/170/2012-05/330072.html>. Registration email: `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
