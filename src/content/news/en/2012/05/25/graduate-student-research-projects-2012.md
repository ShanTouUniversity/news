It is reported that STU’s Graduate School has opened applications for its 2012 postgraduate extracurricular academic and scientific projects. The program is open to all enrolled postgraduates on the main campus from May 25 through June 4. Projects must be completed by students in their spare time while at the university and may not be part of the teaching plan; graduation theses, graduation designs, and annual papers are ineligible.

Project categories include social-science survey reports and academic papers, literary and artistic works, business plans, and inventions and productions. Cross-disciplinary and cross-major teams are encouraged.

Applicants must complete the STU Student Scientific Research Project Application and submit it to the Graduate School. The school will organize expert review and plans to publish approved projects on its website and in the OA system in June. Two completed printed copies of the Student Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Research Project Application must be delivered to the Graduate School, Room G204, by June 4, with an electronic copy sent to `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn`.

> ⚠️ The postgraduate extracurricular academic and scientific project application forms and requirements are available in the original OA attachments.
