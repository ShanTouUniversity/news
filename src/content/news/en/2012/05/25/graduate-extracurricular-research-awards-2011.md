It is reported that the 2011 postgraduate extracurricular academic and scientific project program at STU produced a number of strong results. Based on expert opinions from final reviews of completed projects and the results of STU’s eighth Challenge Cup Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Contest, the Graduate School will recognize members of outstanding project teams.

The awards include one first prize, three second prizes, and three third prizes for research innovation. The Graduate School hopes that recognized teams will continue their research and study, while other postgraduate students will learn from the projects, participate actively in extracurricular academic and scientific activities, and strengthen their academic abilities.
