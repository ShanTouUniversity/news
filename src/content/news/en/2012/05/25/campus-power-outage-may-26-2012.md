It is reported that STU will suspend power in several areas from 08:30 to 12:00 on Saturday, May 26, to support cable-connection work at the power distribution room of the new administration-building project.

The affected areas include the Wenke Building, Audio-Visual Education Building, the west side of the Administration Building, the West Lecture Hall, and the General Physics Laboratory. The Resource Management Office asks relevant units to make arrangements for the interruption in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
