It is reported that STU’s Changjiang School of Arts and Design will host architect Xu Tiantian for a presentation titled “DnA Design and Architecture Works” at 19:30 on May 29 in the school’s first-floor stepped classroom. The presentation will share perspectives on design and architectural practice.

Students and staff from across the university are welcome to attend.
