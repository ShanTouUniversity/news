It is reported that undergraduate thesis defenses for the 2008 cohort of Shantou University’s Chinese Language and Literature program will be held on May 25. The defense committee includes Chen Zhanshan, Zhang Huimin, Yang Weigang, Xu Dancheng, and Yang Qingjie.

The defenses will be organized in groups and held in morning and afternoon sessions on May 25. The original OA attachment provides the group assignments, student rosters, and specific times and locations. Students scheduled to defend should attend according to their group arrangements.

Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.

> ⚠️ Group assignments, student rosters, and detailed times and locations are available in the original OA attachment.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Chinese Language and Literature Department, College of Liberal Arts)
