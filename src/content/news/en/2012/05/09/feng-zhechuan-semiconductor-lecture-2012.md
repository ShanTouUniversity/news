It is reported that the School of Science at Shantou University will host a Science Salon lecture at 16:00 on May 14 in its lecture hall, Room Li-352. Professor Feng Zhe Chuan of the Institute of Photonics and Department of Electrical Engineering at National Taiwan University will present “Interdisciplinary Studies on Advanced Semiconductors, Oxides and Nano Structures.”

The presentation will cover III-nitride materials, quantum-well light-emitting diodes and nanostructures, silicon carbide structures, MOCVD- and PLD-grown materials using zinc oxide substrates, and advanced silicon-based materials for integrated circuits below 45 nanometers. It will also address ferroelectric and high-k materials, solar-cell semiconductors, and III-V and II-VI semiconductor materials.

Professor Feng studied at Peking University and the University of Pittsburgh and has conducted research at universities, institutes, and companies in several countries. He joined National Taiwan University in 2003 and focuses on wide-bandgap semiconductors, oxides, silicon carbide, zinc oxide, and nanomaterials and devices. He has published approximately 500 scientific and technical papers, authored review books on advanced compound semiconductors and microstructures, and participated in international academic organizations and university collaborations.
