It is reported that the 123rd Engineering Research Academic Forum will be held from 15:00 to 16:00 on May 10 in the Engineering School’s Academic Exchange Room. Dr. Yan Hongcheng, affiliated with Clarkson University and Purdue University in the United States, will present “The Path of Teaching Reform: An Introduction to Trends in Teaching Reform at Chinese Universities.”

The lecture is open to Engineering School faculty members and students with an interest in the topic. It will introduce current directions in teaching reform across Chinese universities.
