It is reported that the English Language Center at Shantou University has arranged two continuing-education elective courses for university faculty and staff. Because demand was high, participants were assigned in registration order to support effective teaching. Each class has 25 participants, and the detailed rosters are included in the original OA attachments.

To support faculty and staff members preparing applications for professional titles, the center also plans to offer a 12-hour intensive English-speaking course during the short semester. Further arrangements will be announced separately.

> ⚠️ The course rosters and related materials are available in the original OA attachments.
