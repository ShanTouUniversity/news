It is reported that the Suning Shantou Campus Elite Marketing Contest opened at Shantou University on May 9 and will run through June 8. Open to all STU students, the contest aims to develop skills in marketing-plan design, market analysis, practical marketing, event organization, and on-the-spot response, while encouraging exchange between students and industry.

Teams will consist of two to five students. Main activities include an opening ceremony and training session on the evening of May 18, a marketing plan for Suning 3C products, brand promotion and practical marketing, and a final presentation. Teams must submit their marketing plans by May 22. After evaluation, 10 teams will be selected to promote Suning and its 3C products through Weibo and put their plans into practice. Finalists will present their team profile, practical results, and marketing plan at the final.

The contest will award one first prize, one second prize, and one third prize, with prizes of RMB 1,000, RMB 500, and RMB 300 respectively, along with certificates and gifts. Additional merit awards will be given. The top 10 teams will receive priority interview opportunities for Suning’s “1,200 Interns” program.

Students may download the registration form through OA and submit it as instructed. Detailed registration arrangements and updates will be posted through STU OA, the Business School BBS forum, and the Business Practice Center’s Weibo account. Student registration details are not reproduced here.

> ⚠️ The registration form, marketing-plan guidelines, and event arrangements are available in the original OA attachments.
