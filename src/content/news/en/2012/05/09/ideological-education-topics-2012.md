It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education has launched an open call for research topics to support the compilation of the 2012 Guangdong Higher Education Ideological and Political Education Project Guide, with Shantou University's Research Office coordinating submissions on campus.

## Scope and Requirements

The call focuses on all areas of ideological and political education in higher education, with an emphasis on problem-oriented and practice-relevant research. Key requirements include:

- Aligning with the Ministry of Education's opinions on improving higher education quality (Jiao Gao [2012] No. 4) and on strengthening practical education (Jiao Si Zheng [2012] No. 1), as well as national and provincial education plans and the Higher Education Quality Project;
- Reflecting the current state of ideological and political education across domains, addressing real-world problems and exploring ways to enhance relevance and effectiveness;
- Connecting with the development of Marxist theory as a first-level discipline and related fields, reflecting frontier trends and pursuing originality while avoiding low-level duplication;
- Taking into account institutional characteristics such as educational level and faculty resources to ensure feasibility and practical impact within the given timeframe;
- Avoiding overlap with topics from previous years, with submissions solicited by category.
