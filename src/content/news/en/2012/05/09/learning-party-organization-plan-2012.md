It is reported that the Implementation Plan for Building Learning-Oriented Party Organizations at Shantou University was reviewed and approved at the fourth meeting of the STU Party Standing Committee on May 4 and issued on May 9.

The university’s Party Committee has asked the Medical College Party Committee, Party general branches, and directly affiliated Party branches to implement the plan in light of their local circumstances and advance the development of learning-oriented Party organizations. The accompanying materials also provide the membership of the leading group for this work.

> ⚠️ The implementation plan and leading-group roster are available in the original OA attachments.
