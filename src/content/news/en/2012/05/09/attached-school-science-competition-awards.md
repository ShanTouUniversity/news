It is reported that the results of the 2012 national junior high school chemistry, physics and mathematics competitions were recently announced. Students from the Affiliated Middle School of Shantou University achieved good results, with three students winning awards.

## Awards

| Student | Competition | Award |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Li Y | National Junior High Chemistry Quality and Experiment Ability Competition (Shantou Division) | Second Prize |
| Ma KR | National Junior High Applied Physics Knowledge Competition (Shantou Division) | Third Prize |
| Lin JY | National Junior High Mathematics Competition (Shantou Division) | Third Prize |

The awards demonstrate the students' solid foundation in science subjects such as chemistry, physics and mathematics, and have brought honor to the school.
