It is reported that “Kan Kan Culture 4: The Self and Others in the Weibo Era” will be held at 19:30 on May 12 in the lecture hall of Shantou University’s New Library. The event is organized by Changjiang College of Arts and Design.

Ma Jiahui, a media professional, columnist, and cultural critic, will speak with Wang Shouzhi, dean and professor of Changjiang College of Arts and Design. The dialogue will explore personal expression, social interaction, and the relationship between the self and others in the Weibo era.

All students and staff are welcome.
