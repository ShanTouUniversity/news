It is reported that the ABC-period class of Fundamentals of Marxism, course number 52187, scheduled for Monday of Week 12, May 14, in Room E-207 of the Teaching Building, has been cancelled because Cheng Jiaming of the Department of Social Sciences is traveling.

A make-up time has not yet been set. Students enrolled in the course should inform one another and watch for further arrangements from the Academic Affairs Office.
