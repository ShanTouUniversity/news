It is reported that, under arrangements by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University’s 2012 graduates must submit employment-destination information by May 31. Graduates should log in to `http://gdc.stu.edu.cn` to complete the submission, enabling the university to prepare employment plans and process graduate dispatch documents and academic-record transfers.

Graduates applying for deferred employment, further study, or study and work overseas should follow the instructions in the relevant attachments. Because employment-plan preparation and dispatch procedures affect graduate records and household-registration processing, graduates should carefully verify and complete their submissions.

Questions may be sent to `o_bys@stu.edu.cn` or directed to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center at `0754-82902357`.

> ⚠️ Instructions for submitting employment information and notices concerning deferred employment, further study, and overseas study are available in the original OA attachments.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
