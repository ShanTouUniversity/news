It is reported that, to complete Party statistics for the first half of 2012, Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department has asked Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to submit the First-Half 2012 Party Applicant Management Form for database entry.

The summary form covers applicants entering the database for the first time, as well as the people identified as probationary Party members or development candidates during the first half of 2012. Each unit should designate a staff member to verify the statistics and ensure that the information is accurate, authentic, and complete.

Materials must be submitted to Room 405, Xingdong Building, Organization and United Front Work Department, by May 24, with electronic copies sent to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. The detailed forms are provided in the original OA attachment; no personal roster is reproduced here.
