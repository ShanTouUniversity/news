It is reported that Chen JH, a student in Shantou University’s School of Engineering, was named an “Outstanding Communist Youth League Member of Guangdong” in the province’s 2011 selection of outstanding Youth League members, cadres, and May Fourth Red-Flag Youth League organizations.

The STU Communist Youth League Committee congratulated the recipient and encouraged Chen to value the honor, continue making progress, and contribute to the development of the university’s Youth League work. It also called on Youth League organizations, cadres, and members to learn from the recognized individual, combine study with practice, and contribute to the university’s reform and development.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
