It is reported that the 2012 STU PPT Resume Design Contest received 47 submissions. After judging, 20 contestants advanced to the final, which will take place from 19:00 to 21:30 on May 13 in the lecture hall of the New Library. Finalists should attend on time, and all students and staff are welcome to observe.

The contest aims to help students learn PPT resume-design skills and understand the content of electronic resumes while developing creativity, independent learning, and overall competence. It is organized by the STU Student Affairs Office, co-organized by China Unicom’s Shantou branch and Shantou Recruitment Network, and undertaken by the STU Career Planning and Development Association.

The finalist roster is not reproduced here to protect student privacy.
