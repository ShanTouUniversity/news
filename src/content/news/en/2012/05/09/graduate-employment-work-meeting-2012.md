It is reported that Shantou University will hold a graduate employment work meeting at 17:00 on May 10 in Room 726 of Building 789. The meeting will convey the remarks and policy guidance from Vice Governor Chen Yunxian and Department of Education Director Luo Weiqi at an employment work conference.

Party branch secretaries or deputy secretaries of each college and residential college, counselors responsible for employment, and staff members of the Student Affairs Office Graduate Employment Guidance Center should attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
