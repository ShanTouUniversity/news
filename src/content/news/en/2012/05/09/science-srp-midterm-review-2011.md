It is reported that the midterm review of the 2011 Undergraduate Research Program (SRP) at Shantou University’s School of Science has been completed. Departmental review panels evaluated 43 previously approved projects through presentations and discussion, considering each project’s progress, difficulty, and funding needs.

Following discussion by the school, 38 projects were approved for additional funding totaling RMB 89,300. The detailed evaluation results are provided in the original OA attachments. The School of Science will track project progress and plans to organize final defenses in September 2012.

Project members are encouraged to continue their research, while supervising faculty are asked to strengthen guidance during the later stages and help the projects achieve stronger results.
