It is reported that the second Faculty Workshop in STU’s Education Ideas Discussion series will be held at 15:00 on May 16. Titled “Implementation of Shantou University’s Advanced Undergraduate Education Reform,” the workshop will be led by Lu Xiaohua. The venue will be determined according to the number of registrations.

The workshop will introduce the core concepts, reform arrangements, and implementation requirements of advanced undergraduate education. Through examples and interactive discussion, participants will examine the development of integrative thinking, adaptable talent, integrated training models, and undergraduate quality-assurance systems. The workshop will also collect suggestions from faculty and administrative staff for future improvements.

Faculty and administrative staff interested in undergraduate teaching reform, teaching concepts, training plans, or teaching methods may register voluntarily by sending the participant list to the Academic Affairs Office at `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn` before the end of the workday on May 14.
