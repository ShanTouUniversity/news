It is reported that the Implementation Plan for the “Three Loves and One Dedication” Themed Education and Practice Program at Shantou University was reviewed and approved at the fourth meeting of the STU Party Standing Committee on May 4 and issued on May 9.

The university’s Party Committee has asked the Medical College Party Committee, Party general branches, and directly affiliated Party branches to organize and implement the program in light of their local circumstances, integrating the themed education with university work and grassroots Party-organization development.
