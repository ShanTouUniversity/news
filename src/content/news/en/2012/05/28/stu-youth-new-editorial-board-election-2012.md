It is reported that the term of the 19th editorial board of *STU Youth* will end in late May. The university Youth League Committee will hold the newspaper’s 19th annual meeting and election of the 20th editorial board at 14:00 on May 30 in Classroom E201 of the E Lecture Hall.

The meeting will hear and review the 19th editorial board’s annual work report, recognize outstanding workers and departments from 2011, and elect the 20th editorial board. The new board is planned to have 10 members. Candidates were openly recruited from students across the university through school and college league recommendations or voluntary applications, followed by assessment interviews and review by the university Youth League Committee. Twelve candidates will stand for election.

The 12 candidates will give speeches and present their plans at the annual meeting. All members of the 19th editorial board will vote, and 10 members will be selected based on vote totals and interview results. After the annual meeting, the new board will hold its first meeting to elect an editor-in-chief and deputy editors-in-chief.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
