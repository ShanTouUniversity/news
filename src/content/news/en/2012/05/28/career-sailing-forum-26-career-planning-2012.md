It is reported that STU’s Career Sailing Forum 26 will be held from 19:30 to 21:30 on May 29 in the lecture hall of the New Library. Yang Mei, regional deputy director of human resources at Guangdong Chuanghong Group’s Jieyang operations, will present on “Career Planning and Management.”

Yang holds a master’s degree in human-resources studies from the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and is a certified human-resources manager and corporate trainer. She has more than 10 years of experience in group-company human-resources management and has held management positions at Piaoying Group and Logan Group. Her other training topics include quantitative human-resources management, interview skills, performance assessment, time management, communication, and improving personal execution.

Students and staff from across the university are welcome to attend.
