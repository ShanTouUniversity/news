It is reported that STU’s Student Learning Center (SLC) has selected student-liaison applicants for interviews based on registrations through May 27 and the center’s recruitment needs. Interviews will take place at SLC on the afternoon of Friday, June 1, in staggered sessions at 14:45, 16:00, 17:10, and 18:00.

Applicants who advanced to the interview stage should attend at their assigned time. Questions may be directed to SLC. To protect student privacy, the interview roster, student numbers, and individual major information are not reproduced here.

SLC official telephone: `82901482`; email: `slc@stu.edu.cn`.
