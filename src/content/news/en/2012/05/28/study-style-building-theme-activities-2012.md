It is reported that STU’s Youth League Committee has launched the theme “Strengthen Study Culture for Better Growth and Development” for activities involving young members across the university. The program is intended to promote sound views of learning, personal development, and employment, and to encourage diligent study and rigorous academic attitudes.

The overall program will run from June through November 2012, while the themed league-day component will run from May through November. League organizations will design activities around their academic specialties. All league members, especially league cadres, are expected to take the lead in studying seriously and creating a positive learning environment.

The program also includes discussions on study culture. School and college league committees will use class meetings and league-day activities to organize forums, discussions, lectures, and speeches on topics such as “What can I learn from STU Medal recipients?” and “What is worth learning from Harvard students?” The university Youth League Committee plans a summary and exchange meeting in mid-to-late November to encourage grassroots organizations and their members to put study-culture principles into practice.

The committee asks school and college league organizations to keep activities focused and practical, respond to the needs of different student groups, publicize the program through channels including microblogs, QQ, the Youth League website, *STU Youth* newspaper, and its mobile publication, and submit activity summaries on schedule.
