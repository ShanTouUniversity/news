It is reported that the STU faculty and staff basketball final will be held at 16:30 on May 28 on Court 8 of the campus basketball courts. The Liberal Arts and Business joint team will face the Arts and Design joint team for the championship.

The game is organized by the university labor union. Students and staff are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
