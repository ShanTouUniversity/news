It is reported that two courses at STU’s Arts Education Center will be rescheduled because the instructor will be away. The sessions originally scheduled for May 30 will be suspended and made up on June 10.

| Class | Course | Original schedule | Make-up schedule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 52850 | Woodwind Performance, advanced class | May 30, periods 3–4, STU203 | June 10, periods 6–7, STU203 |
| 52848 | Saxophone Performance | May 30, periods 8–9, STU201 | June 10, periods 8–9, STU203 |

The Academic Affairs Office asks enrolled students to inform one another and attend according to the revised schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
