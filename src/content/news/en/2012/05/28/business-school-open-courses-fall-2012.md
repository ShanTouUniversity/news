It is reported that 17 courses in STU’s autumn undergraduate teaching plan will be open to students across the university. Students outside the Business School may take them as general-education courses. The offerings cover economics, entrepreneurship, securities and investment banking, marketing, human-resources management, and risk management.

| Course code | Course |
| --- | --- |
| BUS3623 | Intermediate Microeconomics |
| BUS3250 | Entrepreneurship Management |
| BUS3723 | Computational Economics |
| BUS1261 | Econometrics |
| BUS3188 | Business Decision-Making |
| BUS3722 | Securities Issuance and Investment Banking Regulation |
| BUS2723 | Securities Trading and Securities Market Regulation |
| BUS3640 | World Economy |
| BUS3186 | Business Case Analysis |
| BUS3214 | Green Marketing |
| BUS3215 | Innovative Marketing Management |
| BUS3209 | Brand Management |
| BUS3213 | Nonprofit Organization Marketing Management |
| BUS3212 | Marketing Channels Management |
| BUS3217 | Cross-Cultural Consumer Behavior |
| BUS1070 | Human Resources Management, Friday AB periods |
