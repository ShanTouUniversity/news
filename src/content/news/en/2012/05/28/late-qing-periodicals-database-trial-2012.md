It is reported that STU Library has opened trial access to the *Complete Late Qing Periodicals Database (1833–1911)*. The database contains 302 periodicals published between 1833 and 1911 and provides source material for research on late Qing history and related academic topics.

Students and staff can access it through the library’s database page at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=351>. Usage evaluations and acquisition recommendations may be sent to the library’s subject-services department at telephone `3247` or email `yxu@stu.edu.cn`.
