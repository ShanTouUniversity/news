It is reported that STU’s Science School will hold a lecture on postgraduate admissions and training at 15:00 on May 30 in Classroom E305 of the Teaching Building. Sun Haiwei, associate professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Macau, will be the speaker.

The lecture will introduce the University of Macau and its mathematics department, explain master’s and doctoral recruitment, outline application requirements, and describe postgraduate training models and benefits. It is intended to provide information for students and staff considering further study in Macau.

Sun’s research focuses on numerical algebra and numerical partial differential equations. He has conducted postdoctoral research at universities in the United States, led multiple Macau Natural Science Foundation projects, and published more than 20 papers, including 20 indexed by the Science Citation Index Expanded.

Students and staff interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
