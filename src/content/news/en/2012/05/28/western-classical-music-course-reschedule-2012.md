It is reported that STU’s Arts Education Center has revised the schedule for Class 52453, Western Classical Music Appreciation, because the instructor will be away at a meeting. The session originally scheduled for Wednesday of Week 14, May 30, during the AB periods in Lecture Hall 3 will be suspended. The make-up session will take place during periods 8–9 on Sunday of Week 16, June 10, in the same lecture hall.

The Academic Affairs Office asks enrolled students to inform one another and attend according to the revised schedule.
