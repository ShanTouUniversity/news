It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee Publicity Department, together with Nanfang Daily, Yangcheng Evening News, Guangzhou Daily, Nanfang Magazine, and Nanfang.com, has opened the “Discuss Practicing the Guangdong Spirit” essay contest. Entries should address the contemporary Guangdong spirit of “valuing virtue, honoring integrity, and acting with initiative.” Writers may explain its meaning theoretically, discuss lessons from personal experiences and people around them, or offer practical proposals for strengthening civic ethics.

Titles are open and genres are unrestricted except for novels and plays. Entries should present clear views, healthy and authentic content, fluent language, and original work that has not been publicly published. Each entry should be no longer than 2,000 Chinese characters. Submissions may be made through the [Nanfang.com forum](http://bbs.southcn.com/forum-139-1.html) or by post to the Theory Division of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee Publicity Department, Hegun Sanma Road, Guangzhou, 510082. The deadline is July 31, with mailed entries determined by postmark. Personal information should be provided only as required by the organizers and is not reproduced here.

Selected entries may be published and will be reviewed by experts. Awards include five first prizes, 20 second prizes, 30 third prizes, and multiple excellence awards, together with prizes and certificates.
