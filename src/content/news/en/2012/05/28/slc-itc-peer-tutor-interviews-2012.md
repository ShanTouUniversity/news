It is reported that STU’s Student Learning Center (SLC) has selected ITC peer-tutor applicants for interviews based on the results of the computer-application skills test held on the evening of May 25. The interviews will begin at 15:00 on Wednesday, May 30, at SLC.

Applicants who advanced to the interview stage should attend on time and contact SLC in advance if they have questions. To protect student privacy, the interview roster, student numbers, and personal contact details are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
