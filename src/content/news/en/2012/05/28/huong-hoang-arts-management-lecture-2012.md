It is reported that STU’s Changjiang School of Arts and Design has invited Huong Hoang, general manager of the U.S.-based Mark Morris Dance Group, to give a lecture on arts management. The invitation follows the company’s first visit to China and performances at the Guangzhou Opera House.

The lecture will be held from 19:30 to 21:00 on May 28 in the first-floor stepped classroom of the Changjiang School of Arts and Design and is open to students and staff across the university.
