It is reported that the 107th New-Century Humanities Forum will be held from 15:00 to 17:00 on May 30 in the New Library Lecture Hall. Wang Chuming, professor and doctoral supervisor at the Research Center for Foreign Linguistics and Applied Linguistics of Guangdong University of Foreign Studies, will present “How to Improve Foreign-Language Learning Efficiency.” The event is organized by STU’s School of Liberal Arts and Research Office.

The lecture will examine the relationship between imitation and creative language use in foreign-language learning, discuss how interaction between language comprehension and production supports learning, and introduce methods that combine imitation, study, and practical use to inform improvements in language learning and teaching.

Wang’s research focuses on second-language acquisition and its application to foreign-language teaching. His contributions include the “writing to length” approach and the context-deficiency hypothesis. He has received two second-class National Teaching Achievement Awards and four first-class Guangdong Teaching Achievement Awards.
