It is reported that the 36th National Computer Rank Examination will begin on September 22, 2012. The written examination will start at 09:00 that day, while computer-based tests will be conducted in batches from September 22. At STU, registration will run from May 30 through June 6 at the Academic Affairs Office’s Academic Affairs Section, Room 402 on the west side of the Administration Building.

The examination has four levels. Level 1 is MS Office. Level 2 covers C, Visual Basic, Visual FoxPro, Java, Access, C++, and Delphi. Level 3 covers PC Technology, Information Management Technology, Network Technology, and Database Technology. Level 4 covers Network Engineer, Database Engineer, and Software Testing Engineer. Applicants are not restricted by age, occupation, education, or household registration, but may apply for only one level in each examination cycle.

Applicants must bring identification. Minors without identity cards may register with a household register, and active-duty military personnel may use a military identity card. Candidates from the 35th examination who passed only the written or computer-based component may use their previous score report to register for the component they did not pass. A qualifying result is retained only once; candidates who do not present the score report and enter the previous admission-ticket number at registration will be treated as having waived the retained result.

According to the fee table in the notice, the full examination fee for Levels 1 and 4 is RMB 137. For Levels 2 and 3, the written component is RMB 66 and the computer-based component is RMB 71, for a total of RMB 137. Consultation: `82902402` and `82902623`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
