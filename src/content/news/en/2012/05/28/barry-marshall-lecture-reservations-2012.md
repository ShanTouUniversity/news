It is reported that 718 students and staff successfully reserved places for a public lecture by Nobel Prize-winning physician Barry Marshall at Shantou University. The lecture will begin at 19:30 on May 31 in the Grand Auditorium and will be delivered in English. All students and staff are welcome.

The successful-reservation roster is provided in the original OA attachment; personal names are not reproduced here.
