It is reported that STU plans to offer Education and Psychology training courses in the autumn semester of 2012 for non-teacher-training students preparing to apply for teacher qualification certification. Students who pass the examinations may apply under the relevant rules for the Guangdong Teacher Qualification Certification Office’s Certificate of Completion for Education and Psychology Training. The certificate is a required condition for applying for teacher qualification and is valid within Guangdong Province.

Undergraduates should log into the credit-system platform and enroll during the designated autumn course-selection period, following the course-selection notice. Their course information will count toward general-education credits. Postgraduates and medical-school students must bring identification and register with the Teaching Affairs Section, Room 413 on the west side of the Administration Building, from 09:00 on May 29 through 17:00 on June 29 on working days. They should confirm the class they intend to take. Each course carries two credits and 36 class hours.

The available classes are:

| Course | Classes and arrangements |
| --- | --- |
| Education | 55712: Wednesday periods 3–4, E201; 55713: Tuesday periods 3–4, E306; 55714: Tuesday periods 8–9, E306; 55748: Monday periods 8–9, E207 |
| Psychology | 55705: Thursday periods 3–4, G301; 55706: Friday periods 3–4, G301; 55707: Monday periods 6–7, E202; 55708: Tuesday periods 6–7, E202 |

The notice first states that the training is for the 2012 graduating class, then lists the 2013 and 2014 graduating classes as the target group in its notes and says that 2013 graduates receive priority in course selection. Because the original notice contains this discrepancy, prospective applicants should follow the latest information from the Academic Affairs Office. Registration will close after June 29, and all related classes will be closed in the credit system with no add/drop processing afterward.

Students must purchase the textbooks themselves, including *Foundations of Pedagogy* and *Contemporary Educational Psychology*.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
