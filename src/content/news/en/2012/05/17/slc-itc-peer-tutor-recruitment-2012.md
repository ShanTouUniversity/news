It is reported that Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) plans to recruit six to nine students as ITC peer tutors for the fall 2012 semester. The program is open to main-campus undergraduates in the 2010 and 2011 cohorts who have excellent computer application skills examination results, a strong technical foundation, effective study methods, communication skills, and patience, and who want to help classmates improve.

During Weeks 1 through 8 of the fall semester, ITC tutors will work in Computer Rooms E-101 or E-102, guiding students in the 2012 cohort and students who have not yet passed the computer application skills examination. Tutors will help students address difficulties encountered during self-study and will receive the standard ITC tutor payment.

Applicants must submit the application form through the student work-study application system at `http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/` by 17:00 on May 24. A computer-based test will be held at 19:00 on May 25 in Room E-102. SLC will select interview candidates based on test scores; the interview date will be announced separately.
