It is reported that the month-long eighth Challenge Cup Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Contest at Shantou University has concluded. Students from across the colleges submitted 49 entries, which went through preliminary and final rounds before a panel of competition experts completed the evaluation.

The prizes were distributed as follows:

| Category | First prizes | Second prizes | Third prizes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Natural-science academic papers | 1 | 3 | 6 |
| Philosophy and social-science survey reports and academic papers | 1 | 3 | 6 |
| Scientific and technological inventions | 1 | 3 | 6 |

Nine additional entries qualified for advancement: three natural-science academic papers, two philosophy and social-science survey reports or academic papers, and four scientific and technological inventions. The complete lists of winning and advancement-qualified works are provided in the original OA attachments; student names are not reproduced here.
