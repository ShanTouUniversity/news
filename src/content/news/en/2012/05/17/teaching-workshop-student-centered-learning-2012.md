It is reported that the third Shantou University faculty workshop will be held on May 23, 2012. Organized by the Academic Affairs Office, the workshop will focus on “Student-Centered Active Learning” and is open to faculty and administrative staff interested in teaching philosophy, training programs and teaching-method reform.

| Venue | To be determined based on the number of registrants |
| Organizer | Academic Affairs Office |
| Participants | Faculty and administrative staff |

## Course Reform Case

The workshop will introduce a teaching reform case from the Engineering Mechanics I course. Drawing on the School of Engineering’s EIP-CDIO engineering education reform, the course instructors explored introducing student-centered active learning into classroom teaching. The approach enables students to experience an active learning process while mastering core scientific knowledge and concepts.

The practice emphasizes stimulating students’ curiosity, developing an active attitude toward inquiry, encouraging discussion and collaboration among students, and supporting the development of positive learning attitudes. The workshop will use this case to discuss innovations in teaching methods and reflections on their outcomes.
