It is reported that the Centre for International Studies in the College of Liberal Arts and the CIS Student Symposium will hold a bilingual “Dialogues Across Difference” symposium in Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building on May 19. The event is designed to promote academic exchange between students and faculty, with particular attention to dialogue across disciplines and colleges.

The symposium will examine difference through varied interpretations, values, ideologies, and power relations, considering how difference can create new possibilities while also generating conflict, dialogue, and exploration. Topics may include gender and sexuality studies, class, language, rural-urban relations, economic systems, law, regional and national studies, politics, religion, education, research methods, media, epistemology, time, space and memory, aesthetics, science, and technology.

The detailed program, abstracts, and poster are provided in the original OA attachments. Students and faculty are welcome to attend and join the discussion.
