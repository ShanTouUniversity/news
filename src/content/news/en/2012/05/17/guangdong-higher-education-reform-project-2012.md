It is reported that, to deepen higher-education reform and improve talent development, Shantou University will organize applications for Guangdong’s 2012 higher-education teaching reform projects. The projects will focus on quality improvement, reform and innovation, and changes to talent-development models, addressing practical teaching problems, developing educational achievements, and promoting transferable experience.

The undergraduate projects will focus on areas covered by Guangdong’s pilot comprehensive education-system reforms and quality-engineering projects. Priority will go to projects benefiting a broad range of students, developing innovation and practical abilities, and addressing urgent teaching issues in higher education. The projects fall into three categories: funded projects based on pilot comprehensive education-system reforms, other funded reform-practice projects, and approved but unfunded projects.

Applicants should present advanced concepts, a solid foundation, a well-structured team, and an innovative, practical, and workable implementation plan with clear potential benefits and demonstration value. Projects with the same name or substantially similar content from one unit may not be approved more than once. General individual projects, theoretical studies unrelated to teaching reform, projects already approved at the same level, and projects exceeding application quotas will not be accepted. Each project leader may submit only one project and may not join another application. A leader of an unfinished provincial teaching-reform project may not apply as a leader again this year.

The project period is two years. Applicants must submit three printed copies of the Application Form for Education and Teaching Reform Projects at Guangdong Undergraduate Institutions and send an electronic copy to `zhailiu@stu.edu.cn`. Materials are due at the General Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office, Room 401, west section of Xingdong Building, by 16:00 on May 31.

Contact: Zhai Liu, `82902736`.
