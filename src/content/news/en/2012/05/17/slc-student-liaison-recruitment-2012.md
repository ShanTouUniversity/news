It is reported that Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) is recruiting 10 student liaison officers for the 2012–2013 academic year from the 2010 and 2011 undergraduate cohorts. The positions include one each in the Engineering School’s Mechanical and Electrical, Electronic, Computer, and Civil Engineering departments; the Science School’s Chemistry Department; the School of Arts; the Law School; and the Business School; and two in the English Department of the College of Liberal Arts.

Applicants should be responsible, conscientious, able to communicate and coordinate effectively, and familiar with faculty members and students in their departments. Liaison officers will help SLC provide academic inquiry services, recruit and manage suitable peer tutors, and promote SLC services within their assigned departments.

The appointment will cover the 2012–2013 academic year. Applicants must submit an application through the student work-study application system at `http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/` by 22:00 on May 27. The interview roster will be announced on May 28.

SLC contact: `82901482`; email: `slc@stu.edu.cn`.
