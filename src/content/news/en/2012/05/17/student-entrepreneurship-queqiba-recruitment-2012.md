It is reported that, to develop students’ entrepreneurial spirit and practical abilities while meeting the consumption needs of faculty and staff, Shantou University’s Student Entrepreneurship Center is recruiting a student entrepreneurship team to operate Queqiba. The team should provide a clean and convenient environment, safe and good-quality food, reasonable prices, and attentive service, while expanding services in response to demand.

Because of the site’s limitations, Queqiba may only heat finished products or process semi-finished products. Frying, cooking, open-flame barbecuing, and other rough processing are prohibited. The operator must follow the Food Safety Law and school catering-hygiene rules, accept supervision by the campus dining-hygiene center, pay its own utility costs, pay rent under the contract, and follow the Queqiba Operations and Service Guide.

Competing teams must submit an operating plan covering the business model, management responsibilities, site and equipment layout, processing flow, hygiene facilities, food-safety rules, health certificates for participating staff, and food-safety-manager training. A review panel will consider the team’s capabilities, previous entrepreneurial practice, investment, and service commitments, and may hold an on-site defense before selecting the winning team.

The winning team must apply to the municipal food and drug administration for a catering license and may sign the Paid-Use Agreement for Site 789 (Queqiba) with the university after receiving the license. Interested teams must submit three sealed copies of their proposal to the Student Entrepreneurship Center by the end of the workday on May 24.
