It is reported that the Engineering School at Shantou University will launch its third service-learning project program, running from May through October 2012. The program will use team-based projects as its main format and encourage students to conceive, design, practice, and operate their own service-learning activities while applying the Engineering School’s EIP-CDIO education-reform principles.

Following the ideas of learning while serving and learning through service before feeding learning back into service, the projects will help students apply knowledge, develop practical abilities, serve society, enrich campus culture, and strengthen professional ethics. The original OA attachment provides the applicant scope, project organizers, and material requirements.

Interested teams must complete the required materials and email them to `10zahuang@stu.edu.cn` by 23:00 on May 27.
