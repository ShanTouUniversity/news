It is reported that some university units have recently posted posters and other publicity materials directly on the glass doors and walls below Shantou University’s Administration Building. The practice affects campus appearance and makes removal difficult. The university will remove the relevant materials on May 21.

The Publicity Department and Resource Management Office have reminded all units not to post posters on Administration Building walls or glass doors from the date of this notice. Units with publicity needs may use movable displays such as roll-up banners, secure them properly, and remove them promptly after the event. The relevant offices will supervise and clear publicity installations.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department and Resource Management Office)
