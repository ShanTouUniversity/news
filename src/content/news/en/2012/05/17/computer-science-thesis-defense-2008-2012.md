It is reported that undergraduate thesis defenses for the 2008 cohort of Shantou University’s Computer Science Department will be held in three groups from May 23 to 26, involving 77 students. Each group will complete its graduation-project inspection before the defense at a time and place agreed upon by the panel. The outstanding-thesis defense will be scheduled according to circumstances after the group defenses.

| Group | Time | Venue | Students |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | May 24, 08:30–12:00 | Gongxi-302 Laboratory | 24 |
| 2 | May 23, 08:00–12:00 | Wenxi-302 Laboratory | 25 |
| 3 | May 26, 08:30–12:00 | Wenxi-302 Laboratory | 28 |

Sun Haojun will chair the thesis defense committee, with Cai Lin as secretary. Committee members are Sun Haojun, Cai Weihong, Zhang Jie, Li Xin, Xiong Zhi, Yu Jin, Zhu Shisheng, Jiang Dazhi, Chen Qinwu, and Cai Lingru. The detailed group chairs, secretaries, and faculty members are provided in the original OA attachments.

Students should arrive at the assigned laboratories early to install their presentation files. The defense-group roster, defense record form, and grading form are included in the OA attachments; student names are not reproduced here.
