It is reported that, to deepen higher-education reform and improve talent development, Shantou University will organize applications for Guangdong’s 2012 undergraduate practice-education bases. The bases will provide off-campus practical education and promote joint talent development between universities and industry, enterprises, research institutes, and legal and public-security agencies, helping students develop innovation, practical ability, social responsibility, and employability.

Applicant bases must work with partner organizations to establish systems for organization, teaching operations, student management, and safety; reform off-campus practical education; jointly set training objectives and plans, develop course content, implement the training process, and evaluate its quality. They must also build a mixed faculty of university teachers and professional or management staff from partner organizations, establish an open-sharing mechanism, and strengthen safety, confidentiality, and intellectual-property education and protection.

Projects may be proposed as liberal-arts practice-education bases, science practice-education bases, engineering practice-education centers, agricultural education and industry cooperation bases, legal-education practice bases, comprehensive clinical-skills training centers, or pharmaceutical practice-education bases. The construction period is two years. Project review, funding, and financial management will follow requirements for experimental-teaching demonstration centers.

Applications must include a construction plan, application form, and supporting materials. Three printed copies of each item and an electronic copy are required. Materials must be submitted to the General Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office, Room 401, west section of Xingdong Building, by 16:00 on May 31.
