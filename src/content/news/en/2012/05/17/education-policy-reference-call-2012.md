It is reported that the Office of the National Education Sciences Planning Leading Group is inviting submissions to *Education Policy Reference* from relevant institutions and individuals, including researchers responsible for national education-sciences planning projects and other education researchers. The publication focuses on major theoretical and practical issues in education reform and development and values applied research with policy and practical relevance.

Submissions should be strategic, forward-looking, and targeted, addressing important, urgent, or emerging education issues and widely discussed challenges. They should offer substantive analysis, reliable evidence, practical and feasible recommendations, and new data or cases. Manuscripts must have clear arguments, sound logic, concise language, be no more than 4,000 Chinese characters, and contain no intellectual-property disputes.

Under the stated incentives, results from national education-sciences planning projects adopted by the publication may qualify for an exemption from appraisal at project completion. Projects with multiple influential publications may receive priority for continued support, and principal investigators of other projects whose results are adopted may receive priority when applying for similar national projects. Published results may also enter an annual award program, with results announced through the relevant website.

Submissions should state whether the work resulted from a national education-sciences planning project and identify the main author’s institution, title or position, and contact details. Electronic manuscripts should be sent to `qgb@cnier.ac.cn`. Confidential material should be submitted on an optical disc. Address: Office of the National Education Sciences Planning Leading Group, 46 North Third Ring Middle Road, Beijing 100088; telephone `010-62003308`.
