It is reported that the Party General Branch of Shantou University’s Zhicheng College will take students who joined the Communist Party this year to the former Honggong-Hongchang site in the revolutionary area of Mount Danan, Chaonan, on May 20. Themed “Remembering the Martyrs, Remembering Responsibility; Striving for Excellence and Serving Society,” the program will include an oath ceremony and study visit to mark the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China and the 18th National Party Congress.

New Party members will take the Party oath, while veteran members will renew it. Through the visit, study of revolutionary figures, and discussion of current issues, the program will encourage members to remember their responsibilities, strengthen their abilities and social responsibility, and deepen their understanding of STU’s educational goals of ambition, knowledge, perseverance, and achievement. Participants will also exchange with Hongchang Primary School pupils, helping them develop aspirations, while strengthening communication among the college’s Party branches.

Participants should gather in the college lobby at 08:20 on May 20 before departure.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College Party General Branch)
