It is reported that the Engineering School at Shantou University will launch its 2012 “Happy Family, Face-to-Face with Mentors” project program, creating a platform for academic mentors and undergraduates to communicate and work together. Applications are open from May 15 to 27, the project defense is tentatively scheduled for the afternoon of May 30, and activities will run from May through December.

Projects will be submitted by undergraduate teams guided by academic mentors. There is no strict topic restriction, and teams are encouraged to design activities that are innovative, substantive, and practical. Each team must complete the project application under a mentor’s guidance, designate a project contact, and send its electronic materials to `10yjfu@stu.edu.cn` by 22:00 on May 27.

Every applicant team must attend a defense. The school will select projects based on the application materials and defense performance. After activities are completed, project contacts must submit a completion-acceptance form under the academic-mentor funding guidelines and provide a news brief, photographs, or video to the Youth League Committee for filing. Mentors must record the project activities in their work handbooks.

> ⚠️ The project application, funding guide, and completion materials are available in the original OA attachments. Student project contacts are not identified here.
