It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office will hold a series of “Education Ideas Discussion” faculty workshops from May 23 to June 20. The sessions will examine teaching methods, student assessment, peer exchange among teachers, and general-education reform.

| Date | Topic | Speaker | Language and credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| May 23 | Student-centered active learning | Wang Xiaohong | Chinese; may count as continuing education |
| May 30 | Student academic assessment | Ma Fengqi | Chinese; may count as continuing education |
| June 6 | Design and Implementation of a Peer Classroom Observation System | Terry Bodenhorn | Chinese and English |
| June 20 | General-education curriculum reform | Cai Yinghui | Chinese; may count as continuing education |
