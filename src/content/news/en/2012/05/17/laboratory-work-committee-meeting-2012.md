It is reported that Shantou University’s Laboratory Work Committee will meet at 15:00 on May 18 in Room 205 of the Science and Technology Center. The agenda includes development of a shared platform for major research instruments and equipment, standardization of procedures for establishing, adjusting, or closing undergraduate teaching laboratories, and other laboratory matters.

The meeting will be chaired by Wen Xiaobo, director of the Laboratory Work Committee. Deputy directors Du Hong, Chen Yaowen, Qiu Jingfeng, and Huang Jingxia, together with the committee members, are expected to attend on time.
