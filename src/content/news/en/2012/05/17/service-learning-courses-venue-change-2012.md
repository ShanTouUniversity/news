| Dawn Volunteer Teaching Service-Learning | (2) | Su Bingdian, Yao Yi | Room G401 |

| Dawn Volunteer Teaching Service-Learning | (5) | Fang Ying, Zhuo Yanchun | Room G402 |
| Sunshine Community Service-Learning | (1) | Yao Qin, Lin Xiaoling | Room G403 |
