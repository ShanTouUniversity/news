It is reported that the Li Ka Shing Foundation and the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design jointly issued a notice on May 17, 2012, to launch a logo design contest for the LAP Leadership Adventure Project.

## Contest Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | All postgraduate and undergraduate students of Shantou University |
| Requirements | Submit A4, 300dpi, CMYK, JPG design in electronic form with design description and contact details (phone, email) |
| Submission | Send to 11rdai1@stu.edu.cn |
| Deadline | 17:00, May 31, 2012 |
| Selection | Expert panel organized by the organizer; criteria are creativity, thematic relevance and execution |
| Award | One Excellence Award, prize 1,000 yuan |
| Organizer | Li Ka Shing Foundation |
| Co-organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

All submissions must be original; plagiarism or intellectual property disputes will result in disqualification and the participant shall bear consequences. Questions may be sent to ckad@stu.edu.cn. Details are in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ Details are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
