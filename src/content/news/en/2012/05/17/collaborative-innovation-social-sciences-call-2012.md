It is reported that the Ministry of Education’s Department of Social Sciences will hold the 2012 China University Forum on the Development of Philosophy and Social Sciences in November under the theme “Collaborative Innovation and the Prosperity and Development of Philosophy and Social Sciences.” The STU Research Office has forwarded the call for papers to relevant units and welcomes submissions from qualified scholars and research administrators.

The forum is open to member institutions of the National Association for Research Administration in University Social Sciences. Suggested topics include the scientific meaning, requirements, and pathways of collaborative innovation; ways to improve university innovation capacity; the role of philosophy and social sciences in collaborative innovation; and platforms, models, mechanisms, and systems for collaboration.

Member institutions should organize experts and research administrators to select and recommend high-quality papers. Chair and vice-chair institutions must submit one to two papers. Abstracts of no more than 500 Chinese characters are due by July 30, and full papers of 6,000 to 15,000 Chinese characters are due by September 10. Both must be submitted electronically.

Authors of selected papers will be invited to the forum, and outstanding papers may be edited and published. Formatting and submission requirements are provided in the original OA attachment.
