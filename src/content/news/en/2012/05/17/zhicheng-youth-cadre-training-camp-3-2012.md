It is reported that the third Youth Cadre Training Camp of Shantou University’s Zhicheng College will be held on May 19. The program aims to improve student Youth League cadres’ overall qualities, operational abilities, service awareness, and innovative thinking, while strengthening their sense of social responsibility and enthusiasm for their work and supporting the development of medium-sized teams within the college.

Newly elected secretaries and deputy secretaries of the fifth Youth League general branch and secretaries of Youth League branches should gather in the lobby on the first floor of Zhicheng College at 07:40 on May 19.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College Youth League Committee)
