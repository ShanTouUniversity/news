It is reported that STU’s Research Office has forwarded the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology’s call for applications to the 2012 Special Fund Database for the Numerical-Controlled Generation Mechanical Product Innovation and Application Demonstration Project. Relevant university units and faculty members are invited to prepare applications according to the official guidelines.

Projects must comply with the special-fund application guidelines. Where the guidelines require government matching funds, the application must include a commitment letter from the relevant government department or agency. Joint applications that integrate scientific and technical resources from multiple units are encouraged. A principal investigator with more than two ongoing provincial science and technology projects is generally ineligible for a new application. Each principal investigator may generally submit only one new project in 2012, and duplicate applications under altered project titles are prohibited.

Interested faculty members must submit six printed copies of the application, including any matching-fund commitment letter, to the Research Office by June 11 and send an electronic copy as well. The original OA attachments provide the application guidelines, forms, and detailed requirements.
