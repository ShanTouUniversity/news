It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a sharing session on May 19, 2012, for students who participated in exchange programs in Taiwan in the previous semester.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, May 19, 2012 |
| Venue | Room E201 |
| Speakers | Six students who studied in Taiwan (names anonymized to protect privacy) |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

The session will feature sequential sharing by each student, followed by informal exchange after the formal program; no Q&A during the session. Presentation materials and notes on studying in Taiwan will be uploaded to the college’s electronic platform after the session.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
