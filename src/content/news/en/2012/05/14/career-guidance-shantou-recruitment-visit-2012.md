It is reported that, under the teaching schedule, Class 52403 of Shantou University’s Career Guidance course will replace its classroom session, originally scheduled for the third and fourth periods on May 15 in Room D-407, with a company visit.

Students enrolled in the class should meet at the university motto monument at 10:00 on May 15 and travel to Shantou Recruitment Network for an educational visit. The activity will be led by Su Junzhi and Zhu Guang and will help students learn about employment services and company practice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
