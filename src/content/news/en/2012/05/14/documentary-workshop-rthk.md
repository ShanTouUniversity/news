It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will hold a workshop titled "Documentary Production: Truth and Innovation" on May 19 (Saturday) in classroom E307. The guest speaker is Choi Ting Tsoi, Deputy Director of the Public Affairs Unit at RTHK Television. The workshop is open to faculty and students of the journalism school, with limited seats. Interested participants should submit a completed registration form by May 16, and selections will be made based on applications.

## Workshop Content

According to the organizer, the workshop aims to introduce the concepts and methods of shooting news documentaries. Through case analysis and interactive exchange, participants will understand and grasp the fundamentals of the entire documentary production process — including pre-production, production, and post-production — covering the attitudes, techniques, and knowledge involved, so as to improve their professional skills in documentary filmmaking.

## About the Speaker

Choi Ting Tsoi graduated from the School of Journalism and Communication at the Chinese University of Hong Kong. Apart from a stint from 1988 to 1990 at BBC World Services in London as a radio host and producer-director, she has spent most of her career at RTHK Television, where she currently serves as Deputy Director of the Public Affairs Unit, focusing on documentaries and outsourced television projects.

In 2005 and 2006, at the invitation of the Asia-Pacific Broadcasting Union, she twice served as executive producer of the co-produced documentary series "After the Tsunami" and "Bird Flu Outbreak." Her own documentaries have won repeated awards at international festivals: "Hong Kong Connection: Surviving the Aftermath of War," depicting postwar Cambodia, won the Gold Award for news documentaries at the Chicago International Television Festival; "Outstanding Chinese Series: Ji Xianlin" won both the Gold Award (cinematography) and Silver Award (biography) at the Chicago festival; and "Outstanding Chinese Series: Pak Suet Sin" received the Grand Jury Prize in the 2005 territory-wide TV Programme Appreciation Index Survey in Hong Kong.
