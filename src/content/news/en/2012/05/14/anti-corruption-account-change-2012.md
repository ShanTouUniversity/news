It is reported that Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Office has forwarded the Shantou Discipline Inspection Commission’s Notice on Changes to the Shantou Integrity Account. The notice reminds university units and officials to strengthen integrity awareness and firmly refuse and return “red envelopes” offered for any reason.

When an envelope cannot be returned immediately for objective reasons, it must be deposited into the municipal integrity account within one month. The account number is `35581`; the account is held at the Jianshe Branch of China Construction Bank Shantou Branch, located at 15 Changping Road, Shantou.

> ⚠️ Detailed implementation requirements are available in the original Shantou Discipline Inspection Commission notice attached in OA.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office; forwarded from the Shantou Discipline Inspection Commission)
