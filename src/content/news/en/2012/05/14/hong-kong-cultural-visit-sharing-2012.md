It is reported that, with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, 10 Shantou University students traveled to Hong Kong from May 4 to 6 to attend a Lady Gaga concert and visit exhibitions and museums including the Hong Kong Museum of Coastal Defence, the Hong Kong Museum of Medical Sciences, and the Hong Kong Museum of Art.

To share the results of the cultural visit, STU will hold a themed sharing session from 15:00 to 16:30 on May 16 in Room 726 of Building 789. The participating students will form three groups and draw on their academic specialties to discuss their visits, observations, and experiences from different perspectives.

All students and staff are welcome. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
