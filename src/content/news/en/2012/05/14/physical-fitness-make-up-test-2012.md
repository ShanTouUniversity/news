It is reported that STU’s Sports Department will hold a final make-up session on Sunday, May 20, for undergraduate students in the 2009 and 2010 cohorts who were not enrolled in physical-education classes this semester and missed or did not complete the National Student Physical Health Standards test. The make-up roster and individual arrangements are provided in the original OA attachment.

Arrival times are 14:00 for the Engineering School, 14:20 for the Business School, 14:30 for the College of Liberal Arts, 14:40 for the School of Science, 15:00 for the School of Journalism, 15:15 for the Law School, and 15:30 for the School of Arts. Height, weight, vital capacity, and grip strength will be tested in the physical-fitness testing room below the stands; standing long jump and long-distance running will take place on the athletics field.

The test sequence is height, weight, vital capacity, grip strength, standing long jump, and long-distance running. Students must ensure that all six scores are recorded on the instructor’s form, wear sportswear and athletic shoes, and warm up before testing. Class monitors and physical-education representatives should check the attachment and notify students who missed tests.
