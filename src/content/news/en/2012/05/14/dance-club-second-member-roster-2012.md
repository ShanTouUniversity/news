It is reported that the second recruitment round for Shantou University’s dance club has concluded with 50 new members, bringing membership from the first and second rounds to 72. Members who identify errors in the roster should contact teacher Yi Zheyun by May 16 for verification.

Club activities for the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year will begin in the first week and take place in the gymnasium’s aerobics room. Performance-team members recruited in the first round will rehearse from 14:00 to 15:30 on Wednesdays, while second-round members will meet from 18:00 to 19:30 on Thursdays.

The student roster is not reproduced here to protect privacy.
