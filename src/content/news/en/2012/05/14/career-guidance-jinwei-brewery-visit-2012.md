It is reported that, under the teaching schedule, Class 52404 of Shantou University’s Career Guidance course will replace its classroom session, originally scheduled for the eighth and ninth periods on May 17 in Room D-406, with a company visit.

Students enrolled in the class should meet at the university motto monument at 16:00 on May 17 and travel to Shantou Jinwei Brewery for an educational visit. The activity will be led by Su Wenji and is intended to connect career guidance with practical exposure to a company.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
