It is reported that, under relevant Guangdong regulations and STU’s implementation measures, the Academic Affairs Office will soon review selected undergraduate innovation projects approved in 2010 with 18-month implementation periods. Completion will involve both written-material review and a public defense.

Project teams must submit the completion report, research or survey reports, patent applications, software or physical products, papers and publication copies, other evidence of results, and the supervisor’s work handbook to the General Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office, Room 401, west section of Xingdong Building, by 16:00 on June 4. Three printed copies are required, and an electronic copy should be sent to `zhailiu@stu.edu.cn`. Detailed material and file-naming requirements are provided in the original OA attachments.

The public defenses are tentatively scheduled for the afternoon of June 13, with venues and groups to be announced separately. Each project will receive 10 to 20 minutes, including a five- to 10-minute presentation and five to 10 minutes of expert questions. All team members must arrive 30 minutes early. Project leaders should present in PowerPoint and bring supporting materials such as physical products and programs; photographs or video may be used when an item cannot be brought to the classroom.

Results will be rated excellent, good, pass, or fail. Projects receiving one of the first three ratings may earn two research-innovation credits. Projects needing an extension must submit a written request signed by the supervisor. Projects implemented inadequately, delayed without a concrete improvement plan, or involving improper use of funds may lose further funding or part or all of their existing support.

Contact: Zhai Liu, `zhailiu@stu.edu.cn`, `82902736`.
