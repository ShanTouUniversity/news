It is reported that Fulbright scholar and Michigan State University Professor Jim Detjen will give two lectures at Shantou University on May 21 in Lecture Hall 3 of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC).

The first lecture, “Climate Change: How It Will Affect China, America and the World,” will run from 16:00 to 17:30 and be moderated by Dr. Terry Bodenhorn, dean of the College of Liberal Arts. The second, “An Overview of How American Journalism Is Changing in 2012,” will run from 20:00 to 21:30 and be moderated by Professor Peter Herford of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication.

Professor Detjen is the Knight Professor of Journalism and director of the Knight Center for Environmental Journalism at Michigan State University. He teaches environmental, science, and health reporting and is the founding president of the U.S. Society of Environmental Journalists. He previously taught at Nankai University in Tianjin as a Fulbright scholar during the spring 2002 term. During this visit, his interests include Chinese environmental issues and exchange with journalists and educators.
