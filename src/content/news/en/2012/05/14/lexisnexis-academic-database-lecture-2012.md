It is reported that the eighth session in Shantou University Library’s Subject Literature Services series will be held from 19:30 to 21:00 on May 15 in the Subject Services Department inside the consultation desk area on the second floor of the New Library. Liu Yulong, a LexisNexis trainer, will introduce the LexisNexis Academic Universe database.

Designed by a committee of American library experts and curated through professional librarian evaluation, LexisNexis Academic Universe covers news, business, law, and other fields, including more than 2,000 journals and over 9,000 independent resources. The lecture will introduce resource categories and case studies, as well as methods for finding company financial and performance reports, legal cases, legal commentary, and leading international news.

The training will also demonstrate wildcards, connectors, Shepard’s and Dossier functions, people searches, and tools for saving and emailing search results. Students and staff are welcome, and participation prizes will be available.
