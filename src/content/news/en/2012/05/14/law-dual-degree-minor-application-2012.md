It is reported that, to help undergraduates systematically study knowledge across disciplines and broaden their academic and career options, Shantou University’s Law Department will continue accepting applications for double-degree and minor programs in the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year.

Interested students must complete the STU Application Form for Students Pursuing a Double Degree or Minor and submit it to the Law School Academic Affairs Office, Room Wenbei-202, by May 25. After approval, students may complete course registration and enroll during the fall-semester registration period. Students who meet the relevant program requirements will receive a minor-program certificate or double-degree certificate from the university.

The applicable implementation rules, application form, and program requirements are provided in the original OA attachments.
