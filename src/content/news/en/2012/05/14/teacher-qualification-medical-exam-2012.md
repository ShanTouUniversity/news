It is reported that medical examinations for Shantou University’s 2012 teacher-qualification applicants have been scheduled. Faculty and staff applying for certification must go to the third floor of the west building of Shantou Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital at 08:00 on May 17, bringing an ID card and medical-examination form. They will collect laboratory forms, pay the RMB 196.22 examination fee, and complete the examination there.

The medical-examination form must be returned to the hospital after the examination. Relevant university units may collect forms from Room 406, east section of Xingdong Building, Personnel Office, on May 15.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
