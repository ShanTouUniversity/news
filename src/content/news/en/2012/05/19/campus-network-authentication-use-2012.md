It is reported that STU’s Network and Information Center recently detected several campus-network accounts using unauthorized client software to repeatedly attack the Internet authentication system. The activity caused abnormal system load and affected normal campus-network operations. The authentication system has provided campus-network users with stable Internet access services since its launch in July 2010.

The center reminds users not to use or attempt to use unauthorized software to log in to or attack the authentication system. Accounts confirmed to have violated the rule will be blocked and handled under campus-network regulations, with the matter reported to the Student Affairs Office and the relevant school.

Users can consult the Network and Information Center’s guide to proper authentication-system use at <http://uptime.stu.edu.cn/gaikuang1/10070901.html>. The center also asks users to inform one another and help monitor compliance.
