It is reported that the seventh lecture in Shantou University’s Voice and Accent Training series will be held from 19:10 to 20:40 on May 3 in Lecture Room 101 of Building E.

Titled “Interview Techniques – The Art of Communication at a Job Interview,” the lecture will be delivered by Chanchal Singh. Students and staff are welcome.
