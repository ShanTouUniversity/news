It is reported that the 14th session of Shantou University’s Business School Alumni Lecture will be held from 15:00 to 17:00 on May 4 in ACC Room 5. Liu Jianbing, a 2004 e-commerce alumnus of the Business School, will speak with students and staff under the theme “Share, Pass On, and Create Value.”

Liu Jianbing is currently a market operations analysis supervisor at the Dongguan branch of China Mobile Guangdong. He has eight years of experience in data analysis and also serves as an internal trainer for China Mobile Group and a senior part-time lecturer for China Mobile Guangdong. He has participated in volunteer service for seven years and currently leads the Dongguan Green Sheep Volunteer Service Team. His honors include recognition as an outstanding individual during the Guangzhou Asian Games and Asian Para Games, a Guangdong Volunteer Service Silver Award, the Guangdong Five-Star Volunteer title, and recognition as a Dongguan moral model.

All students and staff are welcome.
