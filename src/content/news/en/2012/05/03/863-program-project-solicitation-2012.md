It is reported that guidelines for soliciting candidate projects for the 2013 National 863 Program and National Science and Technology Support Program have recently been released by field. Shantou University’s Research Office has forwarded the information.

The National 863 Program focuses on 10 high-technology fields, including information technology, while the National Science and Technology Support Program focuses on 11 fields, including energy. Faculty members interested in applying should prepare project or subproject recommendation documents according to the guidelines and submit two copies of the materials to the Research Office by May 17.

The detailed guidelines and requirements for recommendation documents are subject to the information issued by the Ministry of Science and Technology and Guangdong science and technology authorities, together with the original OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The project guidelines and recommendation documents are available in the original OA attachments.
