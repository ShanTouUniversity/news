It is reported that Shantou University will hold a campus open day on May 13 for visitors from the community. To support reception and visitor services, the university’s Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association are recruiting 170 student volunteers.

Applicants should have strong communication skills and be able to provide warm, careful, and efficient reception services. They should also be responsible, willing to work hard, follow arrangements, work well in teams, and participate in the full volunteer service on the open day. Applicants should maintain courtesy and ensure that they have no other volunteer activity scheduled that day.

Applications close at 12:00 on May 6. Students may apply through `http://activity.stu.edu.cn/`. The university will select volunteers according to the number of applications, using application time as the tie-breaker. The selected roster will be published on the university office information website. Registered volunteers will receive service-hour records based on their actual service time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association)
