It is reported that the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center of Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold an outdoor consultation activity at 15:00 on May 5 at the Golden Triangle on the pedestrian street. The activity is part of the university’s seventh Mental Health Awareness Month.

The activity is open to all students and staff and will offer on-site counseling, scale-based assessment, sandplay, music relaxation, and short psychological tests. It is intended to help members of the university community learn about mental health and access support.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Mental Health Education and Counseling Center, Student Affairs Office)
