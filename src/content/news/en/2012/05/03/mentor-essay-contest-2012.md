It is reported that Shantou University’s Graduate School will hold its second “My Ideal Supervisor” essay contest from May to August 2012. The theme is “A Beacon Across the Sea of Learning, Nurturing Growth Like Spring Rain.” The contest is intended to present, from graduate students’ perspectives, the teaching, mentoring, and professional dedication of outstanding supervisors while promoting communication between supervisors and students.

The contest is open to current master’s and doctoral students at STU. Graduates may also enter if their supervisors continue to supervise graduate students at STU. Entries should address the theme, describe real events, and be original, with a recommended length of 1,000 to 3,000 Chinese characters. Relevant images or DV materials may be submitted. Outstanding entries will receive recognition, prize money, and certificates.

Electronic entries must be sent to `xjlin@stu.edu.cn` by August 20. Based on the submissions, the Graduate School will also organize activities such as profiles of distinguished supervisors, supervisor interviews, and “Meet an Excellent Supervisor.” The contact person is Lin Xiaojun, at 82903637.
