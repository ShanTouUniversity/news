It is reported that Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office, and Security Office will clear bicycles that appear to have been left unused for a long period around student residential areas and nearby roads, in order to improve the use of bicycle shelters.

From May 4 to 10, a temporary abandoned-bicycle area will be set up near the student residences in Buildings A through G, the bicycle shelters serving Zhicheng College, and surrounding roads. Bicycles showing clear signs of prolonged disuse, such as severe rust, heavy dust, or deflated tires, will be moved there. Owners who believe their bicycles were moved by mistake may reclaim and remove them from the temporary area during this period.

Bicycles still unclaimed after May 10 will be moved to an open area beside SLC and kept there through June 10. Owners may contact the Security Office during this period. Bicycles remaining unclaimed after June 10 will be treated as unowned property; some will be used in the Green Relay bicycle recycling program, while the remainder will be handled by the Security Office according to relevant rules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office, and Security Office)
