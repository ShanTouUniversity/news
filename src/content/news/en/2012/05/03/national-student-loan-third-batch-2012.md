It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 3, 2012, regarding the third batch of national student loans for the 2011–2012 academic year.

According to the Bank of China Shantou Branch, 33 students in this batch have received loans. The full list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here to protect privacy. Recipients may log in to the Student Payment Information System to check their payment status and settle any outstanding balance at the Finance Office.

After offsetting tuition and accommodation fees, any remaining loan balance will be transferred to the individual Bank of China account, expected to arrive on May 4.
