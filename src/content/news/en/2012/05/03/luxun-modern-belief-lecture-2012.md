It is reported that a lecture titled “Lu Xun and Modern Belief,” part of Shantou University’s 2012 Sangpushan Reading Festival, will be held at 19:30 on May 5 in the Library Lecture Hall.

Professor Peng Xiaoyan, a literature scholar and professor at STU’s College of Liberal Arts, will be the speaker. The lecture will examine Lu Xun’s spiritual journey and intellectual changes, his encounters with existential emptiness and the hardships of reality, and the significance of modern belief in his work.

All students and staff are welcome.
