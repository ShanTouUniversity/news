It is reported that the 250th lecture in the Law School series of Shantou University will be held on the evening of May 4, 2012. Wang Zhanming, an associate professor at the School of Law of Hangzhou Normal University, will deliver a talk on medical liability from an interpretive perspective. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Medical Liability from an Interpretive Perspective |
| Speaker | Wang Zhanming, associate professor at the School of Law of Hangzhou Normal University |
| Time | May 4, 2012 (Friday), 19:50–21:00 |
| Venue | Room 213, Law School |
| Organizer | Law School |

The lecture will examine medical liability through an interpretive perspective and provide faculty and students with an opportunity to discuss related legal issues.
