It is reported that Shantou TV’s Chaoshan Wind will broadcast an interview with Wang Jian, a faculty member of STU’s Changjiang College of Arts and Design.

The first broadcast is scheduled for May 6 at 21:25 on Shantou TV Channel 3 and at 23:45 on Channel 2. Rebroadcasts are scheduled for May 7 at 08:12 on Channel 1 and at 12:24 on Channel 3. Students and staff may watch according to the channel and time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang College of Arts and Design)
