It is reported that the 2012 Shantou University–University of Calgary CDIO exchange project is being held at STU from April 29 to June 2. To improve young faculty members’ teaching skills and quality, the Engineering College will organize faculty observation of classes taught by Professor Ronald Hugo, the project’s main lecturer, followed by teaching discussions.

The names of participating faculty members and the observation schedule are provided in the original OA attachment.
