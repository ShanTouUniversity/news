It is reported that, to support the university’s internationalization goals and improve staff members’ capacity for external communication, Shantou University’s English Language Center will offer two continuing-education electives from May 9 to June 13. The courses are intended for faculty and administrative staff seeking to improve their English communication skills.

| Course | Time | Room | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic English Communication Skills | Wednesdays, 14:00–16:00 | E201 | Social conversations, telephone calls, email, and reading and writing current news |
| English Pronunciation and Oral Communication | Wednesdays, 16:00–18:00 | E208 | Pronunciation and intonation adjustment, and oral communication skills |

Both courses count toward continuing-education credits. Faculty and staff may register through `training.stu.edu.cn` using their personal campus-network account and password.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office and English Language Center)
