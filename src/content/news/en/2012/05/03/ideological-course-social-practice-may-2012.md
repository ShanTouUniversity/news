It is reported that Shantou University’s Department of Social Sciences will organize an off-campus social practice activity on May 5 for students taking Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics this semester. The activity is intended to help students learn about society and China beyond the campus.

The course groups will visit Jinwei Beer Shantou Co., Ltd. and the Niutianyang Agricultural Products Base of the Guangzhou Military Region. The corresponding course sections are 52219, 52220, 52221, 52222, and 52223 for Jinwei Beer Shantou, and 52217, 52218, 52224, and 52225 for the Niutianyang base.

Participants should gather at the STU motto stele at 07:50 on the day of the activity for departure. Instructors and students in the listed sections are asked to arrive on time.
