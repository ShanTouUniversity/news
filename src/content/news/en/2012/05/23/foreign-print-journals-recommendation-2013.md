It is reported that STU Library has begun collecting recommendations for its 2013 foreign-language print-journal subscriptions. Faculty and postgraduate students across the university are invited to recommend renewals and new subscriptions. Schools and colleges are asked to organize recommendations according to teaching and research needs and the literature-support requirements of their disciplines.

Faculty members and postgraduate students should complete the 2013 Foreign-Language Print Journal Renewal and New-Subscription Recommendation Form and send their suggestions to the library’s subject-services department by June 8. Because print-journal subscriptions operate on a tight schedule, the library encourages readers to respond early.

Contact: Wu Xiaoxing; email `xxwu@stu.edu.cn`; telephone `3247`.
