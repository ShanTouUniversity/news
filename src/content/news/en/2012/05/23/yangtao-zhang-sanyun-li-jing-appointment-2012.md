It is reported that, following discussion at the sixth STU university leadership meeting of 2012 on May 15, the university has appointed Yang Tao of the English Language Center, Zhang Sanyun of the Graduate School, and Li Jing of the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication to intermediate management positions. The appointments carry a six-month probation period.

The appointments follow the earlier public-notice process. The relevant units will carry out position placement and probation management under the university’s regulations on the appointment of management cadres.
