It is reported that, under STU’s Measures on Applications and Management of Student Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Projects, the university has launched its 2012 project program. The project cycle runs from the date of the notice through March 1, 2013.

Each school or college will set its own application schedule according to local circumstances, organize outreach and applications, provide guidance, and arrange review by an expert panel. Within one week after completing the review, each unit must submit to the university Youth League Committee a list of proposed projects, one printed and one electronic copy of each application, an analysis of expected outcomes and benefits, and a work schedule.

The STU Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities Leading Group will approve projects and allocate corresponding project-initiation funds under the management measures. After projects conclude, units must organize expert evaluation and submit completion reports, consolidated explanations of fund use, and work-performance evaluation reports.

> ⚠️ The project application, management measures, and related materials are available in the original OA attachments.
