It is reported that Zhang YS, a student in the Mathematics Department of STU’s Science School, has been selected for the 2012 fourth Graduate Mathematics Foundation Enhancement Program of the Beijing International Mathematical Research Center after application, expert recommendation, and review by the center’s expert committee. Zhang was admitted by exception as a third-year undergraduate and is among the few students selected from provincial universities.

The Beijing International Mathematical Research Center has offered the program since 2009, bringing together mathematics-education resources from China and abroad to train students with strong interest and ability in mathematics research. The 2012 program recruited nationally, primarily from fourth-year undergraduates and first- and second-year postgraduate students. It enrolled about 30 students for one semester.

Zhang YS is currently undertaking intensive professional study with 30 students from 17 universities across China. Established with national funding, the center aims to build a high-level mathematics research platform, train mathematicians, promote applications of mathematics in technology, and advance mathematics research and education.
