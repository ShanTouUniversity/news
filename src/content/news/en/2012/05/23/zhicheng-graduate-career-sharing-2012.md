It is reported that the “Resident Tutors for You” series at STU’s Zhicheng College will hold a session from 21:00 to 22:30 on May 25 in Room J354. Eight graduating students will share their preparation and choices in four areas: overseas postgraduate applications, domestic postgraduate study, employment, and civil-service careers.

The guests’ destinations include the University of Edinburgh, City University of Hong Kong, Xiamen University, China University of Political Science and Law, Fudan University, the Shenzhen General Station of Entry-Exit Frontier Inspection, KPMG, and Chow Tai Fook Jewellery. The session will help younger students learn about study planning, job preparation, and civil-service examinations. Student speaker names are not reproduced here.

All STU students are welcome to attend.
