It is reported that, following discussion at STU’s fourth university leadership meeting on April 10 and sixth meeting on May 15, the university has established a Key Construction Projects Office under the Resource Management Office and adjusted related management positions.

Liu Jinhua has been appointed assistant director of the Resource Management Office and director of the Key Construction Projects Office. Chen Wenzheng has been appointed director of the Infrastructure Office under the Resource Management Office, with a six-month probation period. Following the adjustment, Liu will no longer serve as director of the Infrastructure Office.

The organizational and personnel changes are intended to strengthen the management of major construction projects and infrastructure work at the university.
