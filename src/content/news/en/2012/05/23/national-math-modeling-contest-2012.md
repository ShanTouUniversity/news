It is reported that Shantou University will once again field teams for the 2012 National College Mathematical Contest in Modeling, with registration open from May 23 to June 11.

## About the Contest

The contest is jointly organized by the Higher Education Department of the Ministry of Education and the China Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics. It aims to motivate students to learn mathematics and to develop their ability to build mathematical models and apply computing skills to real-world problems. Over nearly two decades, the contest has grown steadily in influence, and participants' creativity and hands-on abilities are widely recognized by employers. STU has participated with solid results in recent years.

## Registration Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Undergraduates in year 2 or above |
| Team format | Self-organized teams of three |
| Registration period | May 23 – June 11, 2012 |
| Training | August 1 – September 6 |
| Contest time | 8:00 a.m., Friday, Sept 7 – 8:00 a.m., Monday, Sept 10 |

Applicants should have a strong foundation in mathematics, software programming skills, mathematical application and innovation capabilities, plus perseverance and teamwork spirit. Each team's three members should complement one another in mathematical application, programming and written communication.

Applicants should download the registration form and email it to cmwei@stu.edu.cn. After registration closes, the university's mathematical modeling mentoring group will select participants before the semester ends, followed by rigorous training during the summer to field the strongest teams for the contest.

More information is available on the official website at http://www.mcm.edu.cn.
