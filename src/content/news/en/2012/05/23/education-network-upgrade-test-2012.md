It is reported that, under arrangements from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the province’s education network will undergo an upgrade in the near term to support network services during the national college entrance examination and online admissions. STU’s education-network outlet may experience interruptions or unstable connections beginning May 23.

During the upgrade, users with education-network IP addresses may be unable to access external websites, and off-campus users may be unable to reach campus resources on the education network. STU’s Network and Information Center will stay in contact with the provincial information center and seek to minimize interruptions. No separate restoration notice will be issued.

The center asks users to make arrangements in advance and inform one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
