It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office has begun preparations for the spring 2012 classroom teaching quality evaluation. Teaching units are asked to log in to the credit-system portal to verify the semester’s course schedule and update evaluation information.

Units should check course titles, instructors, student rosters, and student numbers and report any changes to the Teaching Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office. They must also revise course-group evaluation indicators according to their course offerings and complete the submission of indicators and course grouping in the system.

Reviewed evaluation indicators and course-grouping materials must be printed, signed by the head of the teaching unit, and delivered to the General Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office by June 1. The credit-system portal is available at <http://credit.stu.edu.cn>.

> ⚠️ The course-group evaluation indicators and related work forms are available in the original OA attachment.
>
