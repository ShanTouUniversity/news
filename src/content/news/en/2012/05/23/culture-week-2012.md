It is reported that STU’s English Language Center will hold Culture Week from May 25 to 27. The program will introduce students and staff to films, food, languages, and customs from different countries through screenings, a campus race, cultural booths, and language lessons.

| Activity | Time | Venue and content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Movie Night | May 25, 19:30 | Classrooms E201, E202, E203, E204, E207, and E208; six films from France, Brazil, the United States, Germany, Iran, and India, followed by discussion |
| Cross Country Race | May 26, 09:00–11:00 | A 12-station campus challenge themed “Mystery in Different Cultures”; participants meet at EAC at 08:30 |
| Cross Country Booths | May 26, 10:00–13:00 | Golden Triangle; Spanish chicken rolls, Italian spaghetti, Polish souvenirs, and a British-accent activity |
| Language and Culture Lessons | May 27, 09:00–17:00 | EAC, the English Language Center Activity Center in Building 9; yoga, hula, Korean, Spanish and salsa, Russian, and Hindi sessions |

Movie Night will screen *The Class*, *Central Station*, *Sister Act*, *The Lives of Others*, *A Separation*, and *Like Stars on Earth*. The language-and-culture sessions will run at set times, allowing participants to choose topics of interest.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Center)
