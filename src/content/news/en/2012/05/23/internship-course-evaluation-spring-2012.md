It is reported that STU’s internship-course evaluation for the spring semester of 2011–2012 began on May 23 and will continue through June 4. The evaluation covers internship courses offered this semester and applies to students who selected topics for those courses.

Students must log in to the credit-system portal, select “Course Evaluation,” then “View Courses Pending Evaluation,” and choose the spring 2011–2012 internship course to complete the evaluation. Students who miss the deadline must submit a written explanation to the Academic Affairs Office; those who fail to complete the required process will be unable to view their internship-course grades.

Academic administrators in each school or department should remind relevant students to complete the task on time. The portal is available at <http://credit.stu.edu.cn/>.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
