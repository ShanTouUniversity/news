It is reported that the first 2012 internal-fund academic lecture of STU’s College of Liberal Arts will be held from 15:00 to 17:00 on May 24 in Room Wendong-201. The session will bring together research and teaching perspectives on Chinese dialects, linguistics, and university English education.

| Topic | Speaker |
| --- | --- |
| The origins of Cantonese and the history of its written form | Associate Professor Deng Xiaoqin |
| Research on models for identifying speech acts | Associate Professor Wen Bing |
| An analysis of the current state and strategies of autonomous university English learning based on constructivist theory | Lecturer Su Li |

Students and staff from across the university are welcome to attend.
