According to the Office of Scientific Research, in line with the notice of the Guangdong Provincial Ocean and Fisheries Bureau on issuing the application guide for the 2012 Guangdong marine fishery sci-tech promotion program (Document No. YUE HAI YU [2012] 84), the university is organizing applications for the 2012 program.

Details of the application content are available in the application guide. Application materials (application form, feasibility study report and attachments) should be bound into 13 copies and submitted to the Office of Scientific Research by September 5, with electronic copies sent to yxhu@stu.edu.cn. Contacts: Hu Yunxiang, Zhu Changsheng; tel. 82902813.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Scientific Research)
