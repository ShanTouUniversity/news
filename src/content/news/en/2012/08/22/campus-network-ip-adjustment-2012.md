It is reported by the Network and Information Center that, in line with the campus network upgrade plan, user access IP addresses in the teaching, office and staff dormitory areas will be repartitioned and adjusted with immediate effect, during which the network in the relevant areas will be briefly interrupted (about 5 minutes) and users' computers will automatically obtain new IP addresses.

Units with static addresses such as private servers or shared printers need to configure them manually according to the new IP range before they can be used. The adjustment runs from August 23 to 31, 2012, affecting the teaching, office and staff dormitory areas of the main campus (the student dormitory area has already been adjusted).

If users cannot connect normally after the brief interruption, they are advised to save their files, restart the computer, and confirm that the network configuration is set to obtain IP addresses automatically; if problems persist, contact the center. The restoration time will not be separately announced; users are asked to pass the word around.
