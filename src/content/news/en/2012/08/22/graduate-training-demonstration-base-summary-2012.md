According to the Graduate School, in line with the notice of the Guangdong Provincial Academic Degrees Committee on reporting the construction summary materials of provincial demonstration bases for joint graduate training (Document No. YUE XUE WEI BAN [2012] 35), the three provincial demonstration bases approved at Shantou University need to submit their summary materials on the joint training construction.

The summaries should cover the overall situation of university-enterprise joint graduate training over the past three years, the main achievements and successful experience, the main problems and difficulties, ideas for the next steps, and relevant opinions and suggestions. They should focus on institutional innovation in the construction and operation of the demonstration bases and model innovation in graduate training, fully explore their own features and highlights, and distill measures and experience that can be referenced or promoted.

Each demonstration base submits one separate set of materials, concise, well-organized and well-founded, no more than 5,000 words each. The bases should submit their summaries to the Graduate School by September 24, 2012, with electronic copies sent to xwb@stu.edu.cn.

The three provincial demonstration bases approved at STU and their partner institutions are as follows:

| Discipline (specialty) | Partner institution |
|---|---|
| Mechatronic Engineering; Mechanical Design and Theory | Shantou Light Industry Equipment Research Institute |
| Marine Biology; Environmental Science | Tuolian Subdistrict, Jinping District, Shantou |
| Internal Medicine; Obstetrics and Gynecology | Yuebei People's Hospital; Shantou Central Hospital |
