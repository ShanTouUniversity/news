It is reported that the Publicity Department of the CPC Guangdong Provincial Committee and the Guangdong Federation of Social Sciences will hold the 2012 Guangdong Social Science Annual Meeting in October and November. The Office of Scientific Research has forwarded the call for papers to units across the university, inviting interested scholars to submit papers as required.

The overall theme of the annual meeting is "deepening reform, transformation and upgrading", with five thematic sessions: transformation and upgrading of cultural development (hosted by the Provincial Party School); innovative economy and transformation and upgrading (hosted by the Dongguan Federation of Social Sciences, Dongguan University of Technology and the Dongguan Academy of Social Sciences); financial support for industrial transformation and upgrading (hosted by Guangdong University of Finance); expanding domestic demand and building a modern circulation system (hosted by Guangdong University of Business Studies); and institutional change and development transformation (hosted by Shenzhen University). Contact persons and submission email addresses for each session are available in the original OA notice.

Submitted papers should closely address the overall theme and the five sessions, follow academic norms, be no more than 8,000 words, and not have been previously published. Each paper should indicate at the front the thematic session it belongs to, the author's name, gender, affiliation, position/title, postal address, telephone number and email address. Papers should be sent directly to the email address of the corresponding session, and multiple submissions of the same paper are not permitted. After screening, the host institutions will invite selected authors to attend the corresponding thematic conferences.

Each session will award two first prizes, four second prizes and six third prizes. Winners will be invited to attend the annual meeting plenary, where certificates and prize money will be presented. The deadline for submissions is October 1, 2012.
