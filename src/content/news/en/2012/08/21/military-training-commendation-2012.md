In order to commend excellence and encourage students to strive for better results, the university's military training leading group has decided to honor the collectives and individuals who achieved outstanding results in the various appraisals of the 2012 student military training. Following strict selection by the 2012 STU student military training regiment, the results were recently announced.

The outstanding collectives (9 classes) include: Class 1 and Class 6 of the College of Engineering, Class 2 of the Cheung Kong Art and Design College, Class 1 of the Law School, Class 4 of the Business School, Class 1 of the School of Journalism, Class 1 of the College of Liberal Arts, Class 3 of the College of Science, and Class 1 of Zhicheng College.

There are 10 outstanding instructors from various battalions and the model formation, and 70 outstanding individuals across colleges including the College of Engineering, the Cheung Kong Art and Design College, the College of Liberal Arts, the College of Science, Zhicheng College, the Business School, the School of Journalism and the Law School.

The leading group hopes the commended collectives and individuals will guard against arrogance and impatience, keep improving, and achieve even greater results.
