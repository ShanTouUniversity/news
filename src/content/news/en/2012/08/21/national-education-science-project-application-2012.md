According to the Office of Scientific Research, applications for the 2012 National Education Science Planning projects have opened.

By category and funding level, the key, general and youth topics under the National Social Science Fund's education science receive funding of 200,000-250,000 yuan, 120,000-150,000 yuan and 100,000-120,000 yuan respectively; the Ministry of Education's key topics and youth special topics receive 30,000 yuan and 20,000 yuan respectively. Applicants may propose a funding amount based on their actual research needs and the funding standards, making a reasonable budget in line with the Measures for the Management of Funds for National Social Science Fund Projects.

Application requirements include: applicants must hold a professional title at or above the associate senior level (or equivalent); applicants for youth topics (including team members) must be no older than 39 (born after September 30, 1973); those without an associate senior title or above must be recommended by two peer experts with senior professional titles; in the same year, each principal investigator may apply for only one of the five project categories, and each applicant may apply for only one topic per year; principals of ongoing National Education Science Planning topics may not apply for new topics; those whose topics or projects have received funding from government fund projects (including the NSFC, the National Social Science Fund and Ministry of Education humanities and social science projects) may not apply again for National Education Science Planning topics; and researchers already leading two or more ongoing projects from other sources are not supported in new applications. Applicants must fill in materials truthfully and ensure there is no intellectual property dispute; anyone found to have committed fraud will be barred from applying for three years, and approved projects will be cancelled and criticized.

The National Education Science Planning covers 14 disciplines, and applicants may select topics based on the research fields listed in the Topic Guide. Forward-looking and innovative research that responds to national needs and international trends is encouraged. Starting in 2012, all applications will undergo preliminary review by peer experts and centralized re-review at expert meetings, with the preliminary review using an anonymous detached-sheet method. The program has limited application quotas.

On timing, applicants should submit the draft application to the Office of Scientific Research by September 7, and by September 17 submit 7 paper copies of the qualified "Topic Application and Review Form" printed double-sided on A3 paper and stitched at the center, plus 8 copies of the "Topic Design and Justification" detached sheets, together with electronic copies to ljwang@stu.edu.cn.
