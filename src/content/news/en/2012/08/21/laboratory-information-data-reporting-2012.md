According to the Academic Affairs Office and the Office of Scientific Research, in line with the notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on reporting laboratory information statistics for the 2011/2012 academic year (Document No. YUE JIAO ZHUANG BEI HAN [2012] 17), the university is organizing the reporting of laboratory information statistics for the 2011/2012 academic year.

The reporting covers all laboratories at all levels (teaching, research and others). Five types of forms must be completed: the laboratory overview form; the experiment-record form; the laboratory personnel form; the laboratory basic information form; and the laboratory funding form. The statistics cover the 2011/2012 academic year (three semesters: summer, fall and spring).

All colleges (departments)/centers/offices must complete the data by September 11, 2012, submitting electronic documents first and printed copies afterwards; printed forms are to be collected uniformly by each laboratory director. Materials for teaching laboratories should be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office (contact: Chen Yangzhou, email yzchen@stu.edu.cn, tel. 82903202); materials for research and other laboratories should be submitted to the Office of Scientific Research (contact: Xin Qiting, email qtxin@stu.edu.cn, tel. 82902583).

The university reminds units to read the instructions carefully before filling in the forms, do the work conscientiously, and strengthen data review to ensure completeness and accuracy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office, Office of Scientific Research)
