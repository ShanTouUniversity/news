According to the library, the Genius Finance Service Platform (GFP), a web-based real-time financial information and data platform built on the Genius Finance Database, is now open for trial use by all faculty and students.

The platform covers stocks, funds, bonds, warrants, indices, Hong Kong stocks, macroeconomics and industries, offering functions including information retrieval, newspaper reading, data customization, comparative analysis, securities screening, securities pricing and user-defined features.

Trial access link: http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=353. Username and password are both 82903247. The trial ends on September 19, 2012. All faculty and students are welcome to try it. Feedback on the database and purchase recommendations may be sent to the library's Subject Services Department (Tel: 3247; Email: yxu@stu.edu.cn).
