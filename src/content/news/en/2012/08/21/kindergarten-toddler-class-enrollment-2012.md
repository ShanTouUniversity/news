According to the STU kindergarten, in response to strong requests from some faculty members and with the approval of university leaders, the kindergarten will resume its toddler class starting this semester, enrolling children of campus faculty and staff aged over 2 and under 3.

Parents of eligible children of campus staff (born before August 31, 2010) who have not yet registered should bring the originals and photocopies of their work certificate, household registration booklet and the child's birth certificate to register at the second floor of the kindergarten between 9:00 and 11:00 a.m. on August 27, 2012.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Facilities Management Office)
