According to the Office of Scientific Research, the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) has published the review results of project applications received centrally in 2012. Under the Regulations on the National Natural Science Foundation, applicants who disagree with the decision not to fund their projects may apply to the foundation for a re-review.

Review applications run from August 22 to September 3. The procedures are as follows: applicants obtain a username and password from the Office of Scientific Research to log in to the Scientific Foundation Network Information System (https://isis.nsfc.gov.cn); log in to the ISIS system to fill in the review application form online, then confirm and submit it; after submitting the electronic form in the ISIS system, print 2 paper copies of the application form, ensure the two copies are consistent, sign them, and hand them to the Office of Scientific Research by September 4.

The science departments of the NSFC are responsible for accepting review applications. Review applications will not be accepted under any of the following circumstances: the application is made due to disagreement with the academic judgment of review experts; the application is not made by the project applicant; the application is submitted after the prescribed deadline; or the application procedures are incomplete (e.g., the form has not been submitted in the ISIS system, paper copies are missing, or the applicant has not signed).
