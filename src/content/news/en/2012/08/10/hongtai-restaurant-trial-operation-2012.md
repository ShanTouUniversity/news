It is reported that Hongtai Restaurant, located on the second floor of Canteen 4, will run a trial operation from August 16 to 31, during which it will offer a variety of dining services to faculty, staff and students.

For breakfast, the restaurant mainly serves Cantonese-style dim sum and morning tea. For lunch, it offers selected fast food, wok-fired dishes, seafood and Western food made to order, banquet reservations of various sizes, plus light business lunches and casual afternoon tea. For dinner, it serves wok-fired dishes, seafood and Western food made to order and accepts banquet reservations, while during the late-night period it offers desserts, kung fu tea, coffee, cocktails, fresh fruit juices, various grilled items, smoothies, ice cream and assorted fruit platters.

Service hours are as follows: breakfast 6:40–9:20; lunch 11:00–14:00 (Monday to Friday lunch is reserved for faculty and staff); dinner 16:00–19:00; late night 19:00–23:00.

During the trial period, all items are offered at a 20 percent discount for campus-card holders, with the discount credited back at the end of the month.
