It is reported by the Network and Information Center that, in line with the campus network upgrade plan, the university needs to optimize and cut over its exit lines, during which external network services will be interrupted.

The cutover window runs from 12:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. on August 11, 2012. Internet access for all campus users and external services of campus systems will be affected. The restoration time will not be separately announced; users are asked to pass the word around.

For enquiries, contact the Network and Information Center at 0754-82903413 ext. 100, mobile 13592895188 (campus short number 665188), or stunic@stu.edu.cn.
