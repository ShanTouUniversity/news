It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University issued a notice to all campus network users on August 8, announcing an adjustment to the outbound line of the campus network.

The body of the notice did not specify the exact timing, scope, or impact of the adjustment. Users should refer to the center's original announcement and its attachments for complete information, and may follow further explanations from the center if they encounter problems during use.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
> ⚠️ The original notice and details of the adjustment are available on the OA system.
