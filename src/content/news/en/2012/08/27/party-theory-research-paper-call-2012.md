Shantou University’s Research Association for Party Building and Ideological and Political Education has selected “Opportunities and Challenges: New Reflections on Three-Party Co-Construction” as the theme of its 2012 annual meeting. The association is inviting papers from the university’s School of Medicine Party Committee, campus Party general branches, and directly affiliated Party branches. The call follows the June 29, 2012 signing at STU of the co-construction agreement among the Ministry of Education, the Guangdong Provincial People’s Government, and the Li Ka Shing Foundation, which created new opportunities and challenges for the university’s Party building and ideological and political education work.

Papers related to the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China and the university’s work are also welcome. Submissions should have a clear theme and argument, academic and practical value, sound academic standards, rigorous logic, and fluent, concise writing. Papers should generally be 3,000 to 8,000 Chinese characters and include a summary of no more than 200 Chinese characters, keywords, and the author’s department and contact information. References should use endnotes; the OA attachment provides the detailed formatting requirements.

## Submission Arrangements

Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches are responsible for mobilizing contributors, organizing submissions, and making recommendations. Each Party general branch should recommend at least three papers, and each directly affiliated Party branch at least one. The School of Medicine Party Committee should recommend at least five papers; its Organization, Publicity, and United Front Department will collect and conduct an initial review before submitting them to the University Party Committee Publicity Department.

Units must submit five hard copies of their faculty members’ papers to the University Party Committee Publicity Department by **September 28, 2012**. They must also send a bundled electronic version to `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn`, with “征文” (call for papers) in the email subject line. Experts will conduct a collective review and select first-, second-, and third-prize papers as well as several outstanding papers. The association will decide whether to publish the papers formally or in an internal edition based on their overall quality.
