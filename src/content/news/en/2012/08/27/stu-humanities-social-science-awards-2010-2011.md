The results of the 2010–2011 Shantou Philosophy and Social Sciences Outstanding Achievement Awards were recently announced. Seventeen humanities and social sciences research works from Shantou University received awards: four first prizes, six second prizes, and seven third prizes. Across the city, the selection produced four first prizes, 10 second prizes, and 26 third prizes.

## Prize Schedule

The Shantou awards provide prizes according to award level and work type: RMB 12,000 for first-prize books and RMB 4,000 for first-prize articles; RMB 7,000 for second-prize books and RMB 2,000 for second-prize articles; and RMB 4,000 for third-prize books and RMB 1,000 for third-prize articles.
