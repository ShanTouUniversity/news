The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education is seeking comments on the *Draft Management Measures for Science and Technology Innovation (Key) Projects at Universities in Guangdong*. Shantou University’s Office of Scientific Research has circulated the material and is collecting feedback from research faculty and research administrators on campus.

Contributors may review the draft and submit proposed revisions in a paper copy with revisions visibly marked, commonly called an annotated or “redline” draft. The paper must be delivered to the Office of Scientific Research **by September 14**. Hu Yunxiang is the contact person; the telephone number is 82902813.

The PDF containing the draft management measures is not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for the detailed provisions and attachment.
