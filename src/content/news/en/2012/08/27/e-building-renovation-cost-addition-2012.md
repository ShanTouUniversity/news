Shantou University’s E Building renovation project began on **August 1, 2012**. After removing the existing concrete floor underlay and lecture platform in the seminar rooms, the Resource Management Office’s Infrastructure Development Office found that the original floor was approximately 5 centimeters below the designed level. A fine-stone concrete leveling layer and other related adjustments are therefore required based on site conditions.

The proposed additions include expanding the painted area along the seminar-room corridor, relocating four fire cabinets and associated pipes, adding pipes at the entrance to preserve floor-drainage functions after the floor is raised, installing stainless-steel guardrails, and adding fire-exit and evacuation lighting systems. The preliminary estimate for these additions is approximately **RMB 19,000**, keeping the increase within the university’s prescribed 10% limit.

Under the university’s regulations on construction-project tendering and bidding, the proposed cost additions are being published for public comment. Written objections may be submitted to the Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Development Office by **5:30 p.m. on August 29, 2012**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office, Infrastructure Development Office)
