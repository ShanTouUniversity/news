It is reported that the School of Science will hold a Science Salon lecture on August 30, 2012, featuring Prof. Zhou Jianying of the State Key Laboratory of Optoelectronic Materials and Technologies, Sun Yat-sen University.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Micro-Nano Imaging and Display Optics |
| Focus | Fundamentals and applications of micro-nano imaging (micro-nano lithography, generation and measurement of light fields at extremely small scales) and full-HD glasses-free 3D display systems (principles, technology and development) |
| Speaker | Prof. Zhou Jianying, State Key Laboratory of Optoelectronic Materials and Technologies, Sun Yat-sen University |
| Time | 10:10, August 30, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |
