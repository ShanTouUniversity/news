Shantou University’s Office of Scientific Research has reminded principal investigators whose projects received 2012 funding from the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) to complete their funded-project plans in accordance with NSFC requirements.

Principal investigators should log in to the Scientific Foundation Network Information System ([ISIS](http://isis.nsfc.gov.cn)) and use the “Funded Project Plan” section to obtain the relevant notice and the *NSFC Funded Project Plan*. The plan must be completed and submitted online by **September 4**.

After review and approval by the Office of Scientific Research, investigators must submit **two hard copies** of the plan to the office by **September 5**. The hard-copy and electronic versions must be identical. Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng are the contacts; the telephone number is 82902813.
