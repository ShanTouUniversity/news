According to the STU Continuing Education Center, in line with its arrangement, all units at the main campus must collect the exam papers for the 2012 continuing education public courses from the HR Office (Room 403, Xingdong Building) before August 17, 2012, and organize open-book exams for trainees.

The collected exam papers must be returned to the HR Office (Room 406, Xingdong Building) before August 28, 2012.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Continuing Education Center)
