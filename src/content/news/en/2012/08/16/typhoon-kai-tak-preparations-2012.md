According to the university's "Three Defenses" headquarters, the Shantou meteorological station has forecast that under the influence of the outer circulation of Typhoon Kai-tak, coastal land areas of Shantou will see easterly to southeasterly winds of force 5 with gusts of force 7, and a process of moderate to heavy rain with local downpours from the night of the 16th to the 18th.

All units are asked to notify faculty, staff and students on campus and make preparations against the typhoon and rainstorms. Key measures include promptly closing and reinforcing doors and windows of offices, laboratories, classrooms, dormitories, corridors and adjacent public areas and switching off power; and, in the event of heavy rain, flash floods or landslides, giving top priority to the personal safety of faculty, staff and students, with specific measures detailed in the "STU Typhoon Prevention Plan".

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU "Three Defenses" Headquarters)
