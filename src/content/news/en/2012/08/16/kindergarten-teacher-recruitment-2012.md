Due to operational needs, Shantou University is recruiting one kindergarten teacher and one childcare worker for the kindergarten under the Facilities Management Office.

Basic requirements for applicants include: the ability to endure hardship, with care and a sense of responsibility, and a willingness to follow work arrangements; age no more than 40, good health, holding an industry health certificate and complying with family planning policy; secondary (or high school) education or above with vocational training in preschool education or childcare; and preference is given to holders of kindergarten teacher qualification certificates or childcare worker certificates, or those with kindergarten work experience.

In terms of duties, teachers are to formulate education and care plans based on the "Kindergarten Education Outline" and the age characteristics and individual differences of children in their class, organize class education and care activities, manage children's daily life and health care, maintain regular contact with parents, and take part in professional learning and teaching research activities. Childcare workers are to assist teachers with class care work, keep the classrooms, equipment, environment and assigned areas clean, and strictly implement the kindergarten's safety rules.

The position is a contract auxiliary post; personnel relations will not be transferred to Shantou University, and remuneration follows the university's relevant standards.

Applicants should send their resume and photocopies of their education certificate, health certificate, professional training certificates, family planning certificate and ID card, among other materials, by email or post to the kindergarten under the Facilities Management Office before 5:00 p.m. on August 23, 2012 (postal address: STU Kindergarten, Facilities Management Office, No. 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong, 515063; please note "application for kindergarten teacher (or childcare worker)" on the envelope). Email: o_yey@stu.edu.cn (with the subject "application for teacher (or childcare worker) at the Facilities Management Office kindergarten"). Those who pass the initial screening will be notified for an interview and must present the original ID card and relevant certificates. Tel: 0754-82902088. Contacts: Deng Xiaming, Yu Ruohua.
