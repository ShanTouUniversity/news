According to the Student Affairs Office, the university will hold a graduate employment work meeting at 3:00 p.m. on Friday, August 17, 2012, in Room 311 of the Xingdong Building.

Party committee secretaries (and deputy secretaries) of all colleges (residential colleges), counselors in charge of employment work, and staff of the Graduate Employment Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office are required to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
