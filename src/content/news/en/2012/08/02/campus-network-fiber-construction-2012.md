It is reported that, to support the campus network upgrade project, some campus fiber-optic pipelines need to be expanded. Construction will run from now until August 14 along the roadside areas from the new administration building, past the pedestrian street, to the Academic Exchange Center.

The main works include road excavation, pipeline laying and road backfilling. Pedestrians and vehicles passing through these sections during the construction period are advised to stay alert.

For enquiries, contact the Network and Information Center at 82903413 ext. 100.
