It is reported that Shantou University has launched the selection of 2012 national undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects. The university will select and recommend 50 national-level projects from those approved under the 2012 Guangdong Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program.

The program is part of the Ministry of Education’s undergraduate education quality initiative and is designed for undergraduates. It aims to strengthen innovation and entrepreneurship training and support the development of innovative talent. Projects are organized at national, provincial, and university levels; projects recommended for the national level will be submitted by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education to the Ministry of Education for approval and record.

## Project Types

| Type | Main activities |
| --- | --- |
| Innovation Training | Individuals or teams, under faculty guidance, complete research design, implementation, report writing, and achievement sharing |
| Entrepreneurship Training | Teams prepare business plans, conduct feasibility studies, simulate business operations, and write entrepreneurship reports |
| Entrepreneurship Practice | Under university and enterprise mentor guidance, teams use earlier innovation results to pursue a product- or service-based venture |

## Registration and Selection

Interested teams must complete the registration form and email it to `wtyang@stu.edu.cn` by September 10, 2012. After registration, teams should prepare a project presentation based on their existing provincial-level application. The university will organize expert defense reviews and recommend the strongest projects for the national level; the defense time and venue will be announced separately.

Projects upgraded to the national level will be managed under the same arrangements as provincial projects and will receive funding at the level of their original provincial project support, without duplicate funding. Enquiries may be sent to `wtyang@stu.edu.cn` or made by telephone at 82902736.
