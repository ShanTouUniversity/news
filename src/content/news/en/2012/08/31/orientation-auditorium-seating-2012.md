It is reported that Shantou University has published an auditorium seating plan for orientation-related activities for the 2012 incoming class. The activities include an introduction to Shantou University, an introduction to the English Enhancement Program, a credit-system briefing, security and confidentiality education, school rules and regulations education, library orientation, a lecture on basic Party knowledge, and a lecture by a distinguished guest, for a total of eight activities.

## Seating Plan

The attachment divides auditorium seating among eight schools: the School of Engineering, School of Law, School of Science, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Business School, School of Liberal Arts, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Medical College. The total number of new students is 1,775.

| School | New students | Auditorium seats |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| School of Engineering | 360 | Rows 1–9; seats 1–32 in row 10 |
| School of Law | 172 | Seats 33–43 in row 10; rows 11–13; seats 1–26 in row 14 |
| School of Science | 199 | Seats 27–47 in row 14; rows 15–17; seats 1–37 in row 18 |
| Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication | 164 | Seats 38–45 in row 18; rows 19–21; seats 1–17 in row 22 |
| Business School | 254 | Seats 18–47 in row 22; rows 23–26; seats 1–39 in row 27 |
| School of Liberal Arts | 158 | Seats 40–46 in row 27; rows 28–30; seats 1–13 in row 31 |
| Cheung Kong School of Art and Design | 175 | Seats 14–38 in row 31; rows 32–34; seats 1–6 in row 35 |
| Medical College | 293 | Seats 7–48 in row 35; rows 36–40; seats 1–21 in row 41 |
| **Total** | **1,775** | |

Schools should organize their new students to attend the relevant activities on time. The seating plan for the opening ceremony will be announced separately; the detailed seat ranges are subject to the OA attachment.
