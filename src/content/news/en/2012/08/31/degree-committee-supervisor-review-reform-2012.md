It is reported that the first meeting of Shantou University’s Eighth Degree Evaluation Committee adopted a resolution on August 31, 2012. The resolution adjusts supervisor management and the committee’s meeting mechanism to support graduate education and discipline development.

## Two Institutional Decisions

The committee decided to abolish the qualification review system for graduate supervisors and to introduce a review of supervisors’ admission conditions beginning in 2013. Under the new arrangement, supervisors’ eligibility to enroll graduate students will be confirmed through an admission-conditions review.

The university’s Degree Evaluation Committee will also hold two plenary meetings each year from 2012 onward, scheduled for June and December.

| Matter | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Supervisor management | Abolish the qualification review system for graduate supervisors |
| Effective date | Introduce supervisor admission-conditions reviews from 2013 |
| Plenary meetings | Twice a year, in June and December |
| Resolution document | STU Degree Evaluation Document No. 7 (2012) |

The measures are intended to improve graduate education and further advance the university’s disciplines.
