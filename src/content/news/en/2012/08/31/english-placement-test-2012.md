It is reported that Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold an English proficiency placement test for the 2012 on-campus undergraduate cohort on September 4, 2012. The test consists of a composition-based written test and a computer-based oral test. All freshmen will take the written test from 8:30 to 9:00 that morning, while the oral test will begin at 10:00 and be administered in batches.

## Test Instructions

Candidates must take both parts at the time and in the venue specified on their English Placement Test Notice and arrive at the assigned venue 15 minutes before the scheduled start time. They must bring the test notice, a pen, their student ID card, and their identity card.

| Item | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Written test | September 4, 8:30–9:00; all freshmen take it simultaneously |
| Oral test | From 10:00 on September 4; administered in batches |
| Placement results | Published on the campus office information network on September 11 |
| Follow-up | Freshmen purchase the corresponding textbooks based on their placement results |

The detailed venues and batch arrangements for the written and oral tests are subject to the OA attachments. Candidates should attend on time according to their notices.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office and English Language Center)
> ⚠️ The written- and oral-test arrangement attachments are available on the OA system.
