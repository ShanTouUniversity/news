It is reported that Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold the English placement test for the 2012 on-campus doctoral and master’s cohort on September 4, 2012. Master’s students majoring in English Language and Literature are exempt. The test consists of a written test and a computer-based oral test.

## Test Schedule

The written test will run from 9:30 to 10:30 on September 4, with all candidates taking it simultaneously. The oral test will begin at 3:30 p.m. and be administered in batches. Detailed venues and batches are provided in the OA arrangement attachments.

| Component | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Written test | September 4, 9:30–10:30; all candidates take it simultaneously |
| Oral test (computer-based) | From 3:30 p.m. on September 4; administered in batches |
| Listening frequency | FM73.2 |
| Placement results | Published on the campus office information network on September 12 |

Candidates should arrive at the assigned venue 15 minutes before the scheduled time and bring a photo-bearing identity document, headphones and batteries, a 2B pencil, a pen, and an eraser. The university will provide a listening broadcast test from 3:00 to 3:30 p.m. on September 3 in the E Teaching Building; candidates may bring their own headphones to test them at their assigned venue. Books, paper, bags, recording equipment, electronic organizers, and communication devices may not be taken into the examination room.

Candidates must follow invigilators’ instructions when completing answer-sheet information and each test component. No make-up test will be arranged. Students who miss the examination must begin with the lowest-level English course. After the results are released, candidates should purchase the corresponding textbook: *Global English Course 3* Student’s Book for Graduate Level 1 and *Global English Course 4* Student’s Book for Graduate Level 2, both published by Shanghai Foreign Language Education Press. Master’s students majoring in English Language and Literature are exempt from English courses offered by the English Language Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School and English Language Center)
