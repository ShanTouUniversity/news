It is reported that Shantou University’s tendering and bidding leading group completed its review and recommendation of contractors for three campus renovation projects. The university announced the results on August 31, 2012. The projects involve a model public restroom in the teaching area, glass roofs over public stairwells, and repairs to Lecture Hall 7.

## Winning Bids

| Project | Winning bidder | Bid amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Model public restroom renovation in the teaching area | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 297,103.00 |
| Public stairwell glass-roof renovation (seven stairwells) | Shantou Longhua Construction General Co., Ltd. | RMB 351,651.64 |
| Lecture Hall 7 classroom repair project | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. | RMB 78,200.00 |

The amounts include owner-controlled provisional sums of RMB 14,147.76, RMB 16,745.32, and RMB 3,725.00, respectively. The results will be publicized from August 31 through September 2.
