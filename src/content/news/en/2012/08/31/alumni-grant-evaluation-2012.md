It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Office issued a notice on August 31, 2012, to launch the evaluation for the 2012 Alumni Grant.

To carry forward the alumni spirit advocated by Mr. Li Ka-shing, alumni donated 125,000 yuan to support 50 students in financial need (15 freshmen and 35 continuing students), at 2,500 yuan per recipient.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Students from families with financial difficulties |
| Exclusions | Violations of laws/regulations or campus rules, or wasteful lifestyle or misconduct causing negative impact |
| Freshmen application | Apply to college/residential college Youth League Committee by 17:00, September 5; colleges to publicize and submit recommendations by 17:00, September 10 |
| Continuing students | Apply by 17:00, September 21; colleges to submit by 17:00, September 28 |
| Criteria | Family financial status, moral character, academic performance and discipline, with reference to quota in the attachment |

Recipients of the 2011 Alumni Grant are required to submit a signed summary of their 2011–2012 academic year (printed on A4) to the Student Aid Management Center by 17:00, September 28.

> ⚠️ Quotas and application forms are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Alumni Office)
