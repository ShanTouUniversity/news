It is reported that Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office will distribute enrollment materials for the 2012 freshman cohort to the university’s colleges on August 31, 2012. The materials include student handbooks, academic guides, and academic calendars.

## Collection Arrangements

Each college should send a staff member to collect the materials at the Academic Affairs Office service window on the third floor of the New Administration Building between 3:00 and 4:00 p.m. on August 31. The colleges will then distribute the materials to incoming students to support their orientation and academic planning.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Collection time | August 31, 2012, 3:00–4:00 p.m. |
| Collection location | Academic Affairs Office service window, third floor, New Administration Building |
| Collectors | Staff members designated by each college |
| Materials | Student handbooks, academic guides, and academic calendars |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
