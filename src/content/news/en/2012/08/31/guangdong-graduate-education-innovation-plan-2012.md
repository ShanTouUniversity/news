It is reported that Shantou University’s Graduate School has begun accepting applications for the 2012 Guangdong Degree and Graduate Education Innovation Plan. The application covers graduate academic forums, degree and graduate education reform research projects, and graduate model-course development projects.

## Projects and Quotas

| Project | STU quota | Materials and deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graduate academic forums | 2 projects | Eight paper copies and one electronic copy of the application, plus one paper and one electronic copy of the summary form; submit by September 25 |
| Degree and graduate education reform research | 6 projects, including no more than 2 key projects | Eight paper copies and one electronic copy of the application, plus one paper and one electronic copy of the summary form; submit by October 10 |
| Graduate model-course development | 4 projects | Eight paper copies and one electronic copy each of the application and summary form, plus one disc containing at least 30 minutes of classroom teaching footage; submit by October 25 |

Graduate academic forum projects are open to regular higher-education institutions authorized to confer master’s degrees or above. Reform research projects are open to graduate-education providers; applicants may follow the project guide or choose their own topics, with the guide highlighting issues such as graduate training models, quality assurance, supervisor teams, and financial-aid systems. Model-course projects are open to regular higher-education institutions authorized to confer master’s degrees or above, and applications must follow the relevant interim implementation and management measures.

## Reform Research Eligibility

Leaders of key projects must be active employees, hold an associate-senior or higher professional title, and have led a project at or above the provincial or ministerial level. Leaders of general and self-funded projects must be active employees, hold an intermediate or higher professional title, have participated in a provincial- or ministerial-level project or above, and be able to undertake and organize the project. Applicants may not have an unfinished project of the same type.

From the date of approval, key reform research projects should generally be completed within two years, while general and self-funded projects should generally be completed within one year.

All materials should be submitted to the Graduate School. Enquiries can be made at 4745 or `xwb@stu.edu.cn`.
