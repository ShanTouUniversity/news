The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Student Union have announced that applications for poster boards at the Second Canteen, Third Canteen, and the A, B, C, D, E, F, and G dormitory areas will resume on **Monday, September 17, 2012**, after renovation is completed.

Posters displayed on the renovated boards must use a standardized format of **40 cm × 60 cm**. Student organizations seeking to use the listed boards should follow the Student Union’s application process. Please consult the OA attachment for the required materials and detailed procedures.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, Shantou University Student Union)
>
> ⚠️ The required application materials and procedures for the poster boards are available on the OA system.
