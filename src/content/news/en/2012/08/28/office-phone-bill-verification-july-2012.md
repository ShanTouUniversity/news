China Telecom’s Shantou branch has sent electronic bills for Shantou University units’ office telephone charges incurred from **July 1 through July 31, 2012** to the university telephone office. The itemized data has been uploaded to the Telephone Management System, where designated unit reviewers can log in at [lrmo.stu.edu.cn](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) to check the bills.

Bills without discrepancies should be approved by clicking “Confirm the selected bill” in the system. Any difference between the bill and actual telephone use must be emailed to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` **by August 31** so the university can report it collectively to the telecom provider. Bills that receive neither an objection nor a confirmation by the end of the verification period will be treated as confirmed by default.

After verification, the Finance Office plans to deduct the charges from each unit’s designated fund number on **September 3**. Questions or suggestions about the Telephone Management System may also be sent to the same email address.
