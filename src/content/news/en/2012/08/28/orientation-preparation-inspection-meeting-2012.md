Shantou University will hold a meeting to inspect preparations for 2012 student orientation at **10:00 a.m. on August 30, 2012**, in Conference Room 213 of the New Administration Center. The meeting will coordinate and review preparations before new students arrive. Each relevant unit is required to send one person in charge.

Participating units include the Party general branches of all colleges, Zhicheng College, the Party and Government Office, the Student Affairs Office (including the Admissions Office and the Student Residential Area Counselor Office), the University Youth League Committee, the Organization and United Front Department, Academic Affairs Office, Finance Office, Resource Management Office (including the University Hospital, Student Community Property Management Service Center, and Water and Electricity Center), Security Office, Library, Network and Information Center, English Language Center, Arts Education Center, Computer Teaching Center, Physical Education Department, International Studies Center of the School of Liberal Arts, and Student Affairs Office of the School of Medicine.

| Meeting time | Venue | Attendance requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 a.m., August 30, 2012 | Conference Room 213, New Administration Center | One person in charge from each relevant unit listed above |
