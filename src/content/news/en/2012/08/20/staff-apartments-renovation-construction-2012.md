It is reported that, with the approval of the relevant authorities, the university's Capital Construction Office will renovate 18 staff apartments, with the work contracted to Shantou Zhenqiao Decoration Engineering Corporation.

The main scope of the repair includes replacing wooden doors and aluminum windows and doors and stainless steel mosquito screens; removing and re-rendering and repainting the ceiling and wall plaster; laying polished tiles on floors and repairing and refreshing terrazzo floors; and installing part of the power, weak-current and water supply piping.

The contract price of the renovation is 391,100 yuan. The work is scheduled to start on August 21, 2012, and to be completed by September 30. Some noise, vibration and dust are expected during the construction period.

The Facilities Management Office reminds passers-by to stay alert for safety. Without permission from the university's functional departments and the construction unit, people should not enter the construction site, and are advised to avoid lingering nearby.
