It is reported that, with the approval of the relevant authorities, the university's Capital Construction Office will repair the roof insulation bricks of student dormitories A to E, with the work contracted to Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. The main scope of the repair is replacing the roof insulation bricks.

The contract price of the project is 57,500 yuan. The repair is scheduled to start on August 21, 2012, and to be completed by September 10. Some noise, vibration and dust are expected during the construction period.

The Facilities Management Office reminds passers-by to stay alert for safety. Without permission from the university's functional departments and the construction unit, people should not enter the construction site, and are advised to avoid lingering nearby.
