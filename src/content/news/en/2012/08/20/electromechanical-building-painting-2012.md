It is reported that, having been in use for many years and affected by humid weather, some corridor walls and restroom doors of the Electromechanical Building have deteriorated (mainly on the ground floor and the third floor). At the request of the electromechanical department, the university has arranged to repair and repaint the corridors in parts of the ground floor and the third floor as well as the restroom doors. The work started on Monday, August 20, 2012.

The main scope includes removing the original paint layer, repairing hollow wall plaster, fixing restroom doors and repainting the corridor walls. The project is scheduled for completion by August 28. Some noise, vibration and dust will be generated during construction, causing inconvenience to students and teachers using these public corridors.
