According to the Supervision and Audit Office and the HR Office, the application materials of those applying for professional technical titles in 2012 will be on display in the academic exchange room, Room 312 of the Gongnan Building, from August 21 to 29, 2012. Faculty and staff are welcome to review them.

The display is open from 8:30-11:30 a.m. and 2:30-5:30 p.m. on weekdays (excluding Saturdays and Sundays). Those with questions or objections about the materials filled in by applicants or the achievements submitted should place a written opinion in the suggestion box in the display room before August 29, or call 0754-82902346, with the Supervision and Audit Office handling the complaints. Written or telephone opinions should be signed with, or accompanied by, the real name of the person raising them as a sign of responsibility.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Supervision and Audit Office, HR Office)
