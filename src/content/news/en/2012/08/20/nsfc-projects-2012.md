According to the Office of Scientific Research, in line with the announcement of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) on the review results of 2012 applications, the university had won a total of 57 NSFC projects in 2012 as of August 20, with funded amounts totaling 26.136 million yuan.

By category, the approved projects comprise 33 general projects, 16 Young Scientists Fund projects and 8 international (regional) cooperation and exchange projects. The detailed list of approved projects is available in the attachment published by the Office of Scientific Research.
