It is reported that, with the approval of the relevant authorities, the university's Capital Construction Office will renovate the 36 staff dormitories in the West Zone, with the work contracted to Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation.

The contract price of the renovation is 2.4188 million yuan. The work is scheduled to start on August 21, 2012, and to be completed by November 3. Some noise, vibration and dust are expected during the construction period.

The Facilities Management Office reminds passers-by to stay alert for safety. Without permission from the university's functional departments and the construction unit, people should not enter the construction site, and are advised to avoid lingering nearby.
