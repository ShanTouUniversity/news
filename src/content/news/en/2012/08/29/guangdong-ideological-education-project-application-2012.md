Applications have opened for the 2012 Guangdong university ideological and political education projects. The program includes four categories: key funded, key self-funded, general funded, and general self-funded projects. A total of 151 projects are planned, including 49 key projects and 102 general projects. Key projects normally run for two years, while general projects normally run for one year.

Applicants must select topics from the *2012 Guide to Ideological and Political Education Projects at Universities in Guangdong*. Key projects may not use self-defined titles; general projects may add a subtitle to refine the research direction under the main title.

## Eligibility and Limits

Applicants must be full-time personnel currently working in the university’s ideological and political education field. The “Development of the University Counselor Workforce” topic is limited to front-line full-time counselors; approved projects will receive guidance from designated mentors. Each principal investigator may submit one application and participate in no more than two additional applications as a non-principal participant. Applicants with unfinished Guangdong university ideological and political education projects under way are ineligible. STU has been allocated a maximum of 12 submissions.

## Submission Schedule

Applicants must submit one completed copy of the *Guangdong University Ideological and Political Education Project Application* to the Office of Scientific Research for a formal review **by September 6**. By **September 13**, they must upload the final application through the online system and submit three A4-printed copies to the Office of Scientific Research.

The project guide, application form, and related attachments are not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for the detailed topics, forms, and online submission requirements. Huang Yumei and Wang Liju are the contacts; the telephone number is 82902563.
