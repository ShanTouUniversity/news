To strengthen the management of member records, Shantou University’s Youth League Committee has arranged the transfer of Youth League organizational relations for the 2012 freshman class. Each college Youth League committee is responsible for registration, collecting records, and updating grassroots Youth League data.

By **August 31**, each college Youth League committee must collect the *Shantou University Youth League Member Registration Form* from the university Youth League Committee. It should organize the 2012 freshmen by major, collect their membership records and membership cards, check and arrange the materials, and deliver the original registration forms, cards, and member records collectively to the university Youth League Committee Office. Each college Youth League committee should retain a copy of the registration form for its records.

Each college Youth League committee must also update the grassroots Youth League organization data-collection system by removing information for the 2008 graduating class and adding information for the 2012 freshmen. The updated data and the collected member materials must be sent to the university Youth League Committee by **September 15**. Updated data should be emailed to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. Questions may be directed to the university Youth League Committee Office at 82902302.

Information from students’ individual records is not published here; detailed material-arrangement requirements should follow the Youth League Committee’s instructions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
