To support 2012 student orientation and help new students settle into campus life, the Shantou University Communist Youth League Committee and Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association have announced the selection of **180 students** for orientation volunteer service.

The university previously recruited orientation volunteers from across the student body, receiving an active response. Selected volunteers should prepare for their service; the organizers will announce the training schedule, positions, and detailed work arrangements separately.

The volunteer-list attachment contains students’ personal information, so the complete list is not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for selection details and subsequent arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association)
>
> ⚠️ The complete orientation volunteer list and subsequent arrangements are available on the OA system.
