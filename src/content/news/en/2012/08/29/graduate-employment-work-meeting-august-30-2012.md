Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold a graduate employment work meeting at **3:00 p.m. on Thursday, August 30, 2012**, in Conference Room 213 of the New Administration Center. The meeting will discuss and advance work related to graduate employment.

Party general-branch secretaries or deputy secretaries of each college and residential college, counselors responsible for employment work, and staff from the Graduate Employment Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office are required to attend on time.

| Meeting time | Venue | Participants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3:00 p.m., August 30, 2012 | Conference Room 213, New Administration Center | Party general-branch secretaries or deputies of each college or residential college, employment counselors, and Graduate Employment Guidance Center staff |
