Shantou University has begun its recommendations for the 2012 Guangdong Outstanding Thesis Awards under an arrangement from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. STU has received quotas for **one outstanding doctoral thesis and five outstanding master’s theses**. The Graduate School will organize an expert review of candidates recommended by each degree program and determine the final nominees.

## Scope and Exclusions

Eligible theses are primarily doctoral and master’s theses for which degrees were awarded by a degree-granting institution in Guangdong between September 1, 2010, and August 31, 2011. A thesis by a doctoral graduate who received the degree between September 1, 2006, and August 31, 2010, or by a master’s graduate who received the degree between September 1, 2009, and August 31, 2010, may also be considered if it is demonstrably outstanding, has not previously received the Guangdong Outstanding Thesis award, and is jointly recommended by three external peer supervisors.

The competition will not accept theses whose authors held an associate-senior or higher professional title before the thesis defense; theses that have already been entered twice in the Guangdong Outstanding Thesis selection (from 2013 onward, any thesis previously entered will be ineligible); doctoral theses by authors who did not publish a qualifying paper in a high-level journal in their discipline during doctoral study; or classified doctoral or master’s theses.

## Recommendation Schedule

Each degree program must submit one copy of its doctoral and master’s thesis recommendation forms and supporting materials to the Graduate School Degree Office **by September 3, 2012**. Each program may recommend no more than one doctoral thesis and one master’s thesis. After the Graduate School determines the final nominees, the recommendation materials must be submitted **by September 12**.

The final package includes the thesis matching the archived original, printed or copied double-sided and bound in seven copies; the recommendation form, a Chinese and English abstract of about 3,000 Chinese characters for a doctoral thesis or 1,000 for a master’s thesis, and supporting evidence bound in seven sets; seven copies of the author and supervisor information form in a separate packet; one copy of the recommendation summary; and, where required, two copies of the joint-expert recommendation form.

Electronic materials include the thesis in PDF, the recommendation form and Chinese and English abstracts in WORD, the author information form in WORD, and a summary database in DBF. The recommendation form may list no more than five representative achievements closely related to the thesis and demonstrating its quality, with supporting copies of relevant articles, books, awards, or patents. Discipline codes and names must match the degree awarded and follow the 1997 national catalogue of disciplines and specialties.

The recommendation forms, abstract templates, summary forms, and other attachments are not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for the detailed formats and materials list. The contact telephone number is 4745 and the email address is `xwb@stu.edu.cn`.
