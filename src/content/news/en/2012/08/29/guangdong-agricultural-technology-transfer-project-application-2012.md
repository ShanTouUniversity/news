The Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance launched the Guangdong Agricultural Science and Technology Achievement Transfer Fund special program in 2012. Shantou University’s Office of Scientific Research is organizing internal applications. STU may recommend a maximum of **two projects** this round, using open applications, expert review, and merit-based selection before submission.

## Application Materials

Applicants must prepare the following materials according to the *2012 Guidelines for Guangdong Agricultural Science and Technology Achievement Transfer Fund Projects*:

- *Guangdong Agricultural Science and Technology Achievement Transfer Fund Project Application*;
- *Guangdong Agricultural Science and Technology Achievement Transfer Fund Project Feasibility Study Report*;
- Supporting documents, including a copy of the applicant institution’s legal-person certificate and organization code certificate, proof of the achievement, documentation establishing project intellectual-property ownership or authorization to use it, audit reports, and financial statements.

The paper materials listed above must be bound into **seven copies** and delivered to the Office of Scientific Research **by September 6**. Electronic materials must be sent at the same time to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn`. Faculty members interested in applying should contact the Office of Scientific Research promptly. Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng are the contacts; the telephone number is 82902813.

The project guidelines, application form, and feasibility-study outline are not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for the detailed scope and formatting requirements.
