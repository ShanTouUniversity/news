To complete the transfer of organizational relations and verification of Party-member status for the 2012 freshman class, Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Department has asked the Party general branches of each college and residential college to register Party-member information and collect the relevant materials after new students enroll.

Each unit must collect and verify the following:

- A valid letter of introduction for Party organizational relations;
- Party-member records such as the *Application to Join the Communist Party of China*. If these records are sent separately with a student’s academic file, the unit must verify their arrival.

Each unit must submit these materials together with the *Freshman Party-Member Summary Form* to the Organization and United Front Department **by September 12**. The submission venue is Room 315 of the New Administration Center. The summary-form attachment contains students’ personal information, so individual names and data are not reproduced here; please consult the original OA notice for the form.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department)
>
> ⚠️ The *Freshman Party-Member Summary Form* and other attachments containing students’ personal information are available on the OA system.
