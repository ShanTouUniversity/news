Shantou University’s Graduate School has announced its 2012 graduate orientation arrangements. Graduate students entering in 2012 will register on **September 2**, and orientation activities will run from September 2 through **September 16**. The Graduate School has also arranged orientation education to help new students complete registration and prepare for study before formal classes begin.

## Registration and Reception

| Arrangement | Time | Responsible unit or venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exit of Shantou Railway Station, including the intercity bus terminal | September 2 | Resource Management Office |
| Public-bus stop at the university gate | September 2 | Student Union |
| Shantou Central Bus Station | September 2 | School of Medicine |
| On-campus information and guidance points | From September 2 according to schedule | Graduate Student Union at the university motto monument, graduate residences, and other locations |
| On-campus registration site | 8:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m., September 2 | Graduate School at the pedestrian street, for registration and enrollment procedures |

New students should bring their admission notice, identity documents, academic records, household-registration transfer certificate, and other required materials. After collecting a housing assignment form, they should complete check-in at the duty room of the graduate residence building. Each graduate training unit must arrange meetings with supervisors, program introductions, and laboratory visits during orientation.

The attachment containing the graduate freshman orientation education schedule is not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for the detailed schedule.
