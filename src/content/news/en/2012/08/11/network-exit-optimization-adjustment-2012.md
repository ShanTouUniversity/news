It is reported by the Network and Information Center that, to improve the reliability, security and speed of internet access for campus network users, the university will optimize and adjust its campus network exit during the coming days (August 10 to 15).

If campus users experience a prolonged interruption of internet access, they should contact the Network and Information Center promptly and faults will be fixed at the earliest opportunity. Fault reporting phone: 0754-82903413 ext. 100; mobile 13592895188 (campus short number 665188); email stunic@stu.edu.cn.
