It is reported by the Campus Card Center that, in order to standardize the issuance of temporary cards and ensure their safe use, a daily spending limit will be introduced for campus-card consumption at the university swimming pool, requiring total daily spending to stay below 20 yuan.

Temporary campus cards are mainly used by faculty, staff and their family members for dining in campus canteens and shopping at campus stores. Temporary cards are registered under real names and may only be used by the cardholder. If lending or abnormal consumption is detected, the Campus Card Center has the right to suspend use of the card.
