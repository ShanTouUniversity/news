It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office issued a notice on August 15, 2012, regarding additional costs for the renovation of the Continuing Education College’s multimedia classrooms, meeting room and evening college office.

The project commenced on July 17, 2012. During construction of the multimedia classroom floor, the original cement mortar screed was found to be laid over sandy soil without a concrete bedding. Additional work was required to add power supplies, enlarge window openings after removing the original wooden windows, refurbish the exterior environment and walls, and add water supply and drainage pipes for the meeting room. After on-site consultation with the user unit, a preliminary estimate puts the additional cost at about 7,000 yuan, keeping the cumulative increase within the university’s 10% threshold, with the cost to be covered by the user unit’s maintenance budget.

In accordance with the *Shantou University Infrastructure Project Bidding Management Measures*, the additional cost is now publicized. Those with objections may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 17:30 on August 17, 2012.
