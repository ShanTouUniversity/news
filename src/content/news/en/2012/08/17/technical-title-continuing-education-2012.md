According to the HR Office, in line with the arrangement of the human resources and social security department and the education department, applicants for teacher-series professional technical titles this year must complete the continuing education course training for 2010 and 2011. The university's June notice on application and review of professional technical titles for 2012 (STU Document [2012] No. 86) already set out this requirement.

Applicants for professional technical titles this year are asked to complete the continuing education course study before August and log in to the continuing education management system (http://59.41.8.110/vsappgdhr/gdhr/) to register and record their courses.

For those making up 2010 or 2011 continuing education courses, the study year entered in the system should be changed to 2010 or 2011, with a note in the remarks stating clearly that the course is a make-up course. After personal information is submitted and approved, the human resources and social security department will issue the annual certificate of completion before the end of August.

> Source: STU OA Notice (HR Office)
