It is reported that, to support the university's first public-welfare course publicity exhibition and help 2012 freshmen understand how public-welfare courses are run, the university is recruiting 3 volunteers from the School of Journalism.

Applicants should be proficient in image-editing software such as Photoshop, familiar with documentary production and skilled in photography; possess strong communication skills and be able to organize the exhibition-board publicity work enthusiastically, thoughtfully, meticulously and efficiently; be conscientious, hard-working and good team players; and be able to report back to campus before August 24, without heavy clashes of courses or other activities during the exhibition period (September 4-16).

The work runs from August 25. Eligible students should send their application form to 10qxchen@stu.edu.cn, noting their specialties in the personal profile section; those with classes during the exhibition period should also list their class schedules. The application deadline is 5:00 p.m. on August 24.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
