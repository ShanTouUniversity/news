It is reported that the ninth congress of the Guangdong Society of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology was held from August 17 to 20, 2012, at the Jinhaiwan Hotel in Shantou. The conference focused on academic exchange and discussion of the latest progress in biochemistry and molecular biology made by universities and research institutes in Guangdong in fields such as medicine, marine science and botany.

The conference was hosted by the biology department of Shantou University and the Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Molecular Biology of High-Incidence Tumors in the Chaoshan Coastal Region, with more than 150 renowned experts from across the country attending and taking part in the discussions. Seventeen plenary talks were arranged during the conference.

The hosts noted that the conference was large in scale and high in standard, and that its success would significantly promote the university's discipline development and research level. Faculty and students were welcomed to attend.
