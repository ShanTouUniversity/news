Following the review and recommendation of the STU bidding and tendering leadership group, the results of the bidding for the construction units of the renovation of 36 staff dormitories in the West Zone and 18 staff apartments have been publicized.

The results of the two projects are as follows:

| Project | Winning unit | Winning amount |
|---|---|---|
| Renovation of 36 staff dormitories in the West Zone | Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation | RMB 2,418,838.79 (including provisional sum of RMB 115,182.80) |
| Renovation of 18 staff apartments | Shantou Zhenqiao Decoration Engineering Corporation | RMB 391,100.00 (including provisional sum of RMB 18,600.00) |

The publicity period runs from August 17 to 19, 2012. The tenderer is the STU Capital Construction Office; contacts: Mr. Chen, Mr. Lu; tel. (0754) 82903216; fax (0754) 82902314. Complaints are accepted by the STU Supervision and Audit Office, tel. (0754) 82902346, fax (0754) 82903663.
