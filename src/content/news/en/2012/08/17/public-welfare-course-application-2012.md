It is reported that, following a decision of the university teaching committee, public-welfare courses will become compulsory general-education courses for all undergraduates starting with the 2012 intake, with every undergraduate required to take at least one credit of public-welfare courses during their studies. To further expand course resources, and in line with the Notice on Publishing the Implementation Plan for STU Public-Welfare Courses (STU Document [2010] No. 7), the university is continuing to organize applications from all colleges to offer public-welfare courses in the fall semester of the 2012-2013 academic year.

The application deadline is September 13, 2012. Requirements include: the proposed course must meet the basic conditions listed in the notice above, covering the course outline, the talent-training objectives the course serves, course implementation, course evaluation, and the teachers responsible; the course will be offered in the fall semester of the 2012-2013 academic year; the course applicant must be qualified to teach public-welfare courses, namely having completed the public-welfare course teacher training and taught public-welfare courses before; given the seat requirements of compulsory courses, each college should in principle offer at least one public-welfare course; and, as required by the document, teachers whose teaching load is below the required level (280 class hours per academic year for specialized courses, 360 for public courses) and teachers recruited in the past three years should in principle take on public-welfare course teaching.

As for the application and review procedures, teaching units may begin selecting, recommending and applying for public-welfare courses immediately upon receiving the notice; applicants must fill in the "STU New Course Application Form (Public-Welfare Course)". Teaching units should complete their review by September 13 and submit the application forms to the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office, with electronic copies sent to gykc@stu.edu.cn.
