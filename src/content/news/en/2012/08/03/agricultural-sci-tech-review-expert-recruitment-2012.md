It is reported that, in response to the letter from the Rural Affairs Division of the provincial Department of Science and Technology on supplementing review experts for the Agricultural Sci-Tech Achievement Transformation Fund, the Office of Scientific Research is now recruiting review experts for the fund from within the university.

According to the notice, recommended experts should meet the following basic requirements: a high level of academic attainment, keen technical insight and strong academic judgment, with familiarity with the technical fields and research directions related to the fund; a rigorous scientific attitude and sound professional ethics; a professional title at or above the associate senior level and rich practical experience in the transformation and application of agricultural sci-tech achievements; and the time and energy to take part in project review, evaluation and technical consultancy and related activities. Candidates should in principle be no older than 55, be in good health, and be proficient in using computers for internet access and office work.

Interested faculty should send the completed "Information Form for Recommended Experts for the Agricultural Sci-Tech Achievement Transformation Fund" to cszhu@stu.edu.cn before August 20. Contacts: Hu Yunxiang, Zhu Changsheng.
