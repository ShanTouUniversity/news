It is reported by the Network and Information Center that, in line with the campus network upgrade plan, the university needs to upgrade the core equipment of the library network system, during which the library, the current administration building and the teaching areas will be unable to connect to the network.

The upgrade window runs from 10:00 p.m. on August 4 to 6:00 a.m. on August 5, 2012. Users in the library, the current administration building, the science building, the engineering building, the liberal arts building and the teaching building (DGE) will be affected. The restoration time will not be separately announced, and users are asked to pass the word around.

Contact the Network and Information Center at 0754-82903413 ext. 100, mobile 13592895188 (campus short number 665188), or stunic@stu.edu.cn.
