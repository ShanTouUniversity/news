It is reported that, to support new students’ online study needs at the beginning of the academic year, Shantou University will provide all new students with a free Campus Network Package 1 from September 2 to 30 for trial use. The package has a daily traffic limit of 0.8 GB.

New students may also log in to the campus-network package reservation system at [netpay.stu.edu.cn](http://netpay.stu.edu.cn) to reserve packages for October and subsequent months in advance. Package fees will be deducted monthly from the campus card, and the selected package will take effect after a successful deduction. Users should ensure that their cards have sufficient funds.

## Campus-Network Packages

The account-management and fee regulations issued by the Network and Information Center set daily traffic limits of 2 MB, 0.8 GB, 1.5 GB, and 2 GB for the basic account, Package 1, Package 2, and Package 3, respectively. The monthly fees for the three paid packages are RMB 9, RMB 30, and RMB 60, respectively. The university provides basic accounts free of charge to enrolled students and active faculty members, and subsidizes Packages 1 and 2.

Monthly package deductions generally begin on the 26th for the following month. After a successful deduction, the package takes effect and cannot be changed or suspended. If the deduction fails, the system sets the following month’s service to the basic account and attempts a supplementary deduction during the first three working days of that month. Users must add funds to their campus cards themselves; the Network and Information Center does not issue separate reminders.

The complete regulations on campus-network accounts, fees, and traffic packages are available in the OA attachment.
