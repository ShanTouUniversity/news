It is reported that applications are now open for the Guangdong Sports Bureau’s 2012 sports soft science projects. The program includes funded and self-funded projects, with key, general, and youth projects under each category.

## Eligibility and Research Periods

Leaders of funded key and general projects must hold an associate-senior or higher professional title, or an administrative post at or above the department level. Leaders of funded youth projects must hold at least a master’s degree and be no older than 39. Eligibility requirements may be relaxed for self-funded projects. Leaders of ongoing projects are not eligible to apply. Each applicant may submit only one project application and may not join another application as a project-group member; project-group members may not participate in two or more applications simultaneously.

Key projects generally run for one to one and a half years. General, youth, and self-funded projects generally run for eight months. Applicants found to have committed fraud will be barred from applying for three years.

## Submission Schedule

Applicants must complete the application form according to the *Administrative Measures for Guangdong Sports Bureau Sports Soft Science Research Projects* and submit materials according to the following schedule:

| Deadline | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| September 21 | Submit a draft application to the Science Office for a formal review |
| September 28 | Submit three copies of the qualified application to the Research Office and email an electronic copy to `ljwang@stu.edu.cn` |

Contacts are Huang Yumei and Wang Liju. The contact telephone number is 82902563.
