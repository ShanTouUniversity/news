It is reported that Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) will hold an orientation open day from September 10 to 13, offering new students an opportunity to visit the center and seek information.

## Open Day Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | September 10–13 |
| Location | Beside the Shantou University post office |
| Audience | New students |
| Website | [slc.stu.edu.cn](http://slc.stu.edu.cn) |
| Enquiries | 82901482 |

New students may visit the site during the open days and make enquiries about relevant matters.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
