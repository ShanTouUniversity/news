It is reported that the Shantou Disabled Persons' Federation will conduct a survey of students with disabilities and children from impoverished families with disabilities who are enrolled at universities and technical schools in Shantou. The effort aims to help students with disabilities choose suitable majors for further study and better support them in completing their education. STU's colleges and Zhicheng College will assist with publicity and information collection.

## Survey Arrangements

Eligible students should complete the *Registration Form for Enrolled Students with Disabilities and Children from Impoverished Families with Disabilities* and provide supporting documents as required by the Federation's aid programs. Materials collected by college counselors must be submitted to Room 106 of the Administrative Center (Student Education and Management Section) by October 12, 2012, with scanned copies sent to the designated email address.

## Aid Programs

**Program One: Student Grants**

Students with disabilities, or children from families of persons with disabilities holding low-income (minimum living allowance) status, may apply once per academic year:

| Level of Study | Grant |
| --- | --- |
| Postgraduate and above | RMB 5,000 each |
| Undergraduate | RMB 3,000 each |
| Junior college | RMB 2,000 each |

**Program Two: Assistive Device Subsidy**

Assistive devices with an average value of RMB 10,000 per person will be provided for people with disabilities who are studying or employed. Applicants must be currently studying or working, have a family income below the local urban-rural minimum living standard or face relative financial hardship, and be able to provide relevant proof.
