It is reported that Shantou University’s Central Laboratory has recently completed the installation, commissioning, and acceptance of a newly purchased Hitachi F-7000 fluorescence spectrophotometer. The instrument has been added to the laboratory’s online reservation system and is available for use by university faculty.

## Instrument Information

The instrument has an RMS signal-to-noise ratio of 800 and a maximum scanning speed of 60,000 nm/min. It supports three-dimensional measurement, wavelength scanning, three-dimensional scanning measurement, time scanning measurement, and photometric measurement. Fluorescence spectrophotometry can be applied to industrial materials such as organic electroluminescence and liquid crystals, environmental fields such as water-quality analysis, pharmaceutical fields such as fluorescent-reagent synthesis and development, and biotechnology applications such as measuring intracellular calcium-ion concentrations.

Faculty can consult the instrument’s technical specifications, features, and functions and make reservations through the Central Laboratory’s online reservation system at [zxsys.stu.edu.cn/ShowInstrList.php](http://zxsys.stu.edu.cn/ShowInstrList.php#).
