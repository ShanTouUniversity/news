It is reported that the Institute of Higher Education Science at Shantou University has begun enrolling students for its 2012 graduate course program in Higher Education, with approval from the Guangdong Provincial Degree Office. The program aims to strengthen the management theory and research capabilities of in-service young faculty and administrators. Applications are being accepted now on a limited-quota basis.

The program offers two research directions — Educational Principles and Educational Management — over a two-year course of study, taught through a combination of face-to-face sessions and self-study on weekends or during winter and summer vacations.

**Certificates and Degree Pathway**

Upon completing all courses with passing grades, participants receive a Completion Certificate for the graduate course program issued by Shantou University. According to the university, the certificate is printed under the unified standards of the Guangdong Provincial Degree Committee Office, enjoys wide social recognition, and serves as evidence of equivalent graduate-level academic qualification that some employers accept as a reference for professional title evaluation. Those who complete the courses, pass the master's degree examination for equivalent academic qualifications, and successfully defend their thesis will be awarded a nationally recognized master's degree certificate produced and numbered by the Ministry of Education.

**Program Overview**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Directions | Educational Principles; Educational Management |
| Duration | Two years |
| Format | Face-to-face teaching + self-study, weekends or vacations |
| Applicants | In-service personnel with a bachelor's degree or equivalent (employer approval required) |
| Tuition | 8,000 yuan per academic year, 16,000 yuan in total |
| Application Venue | Institute of Higher Education Science, STU |
| Inquiry Lines | 0754-82902974, 82903301 (Ms. Shi) |

Applicants must submit one copy each of their ID card, diploma, and degree certificate, four recent one-inch color photos with white backgrounds plus matching digital photos, and complete the enrollment registration form. Fees for thesis supervision and defense related to the master's degree application are charged separately according to university standards.
