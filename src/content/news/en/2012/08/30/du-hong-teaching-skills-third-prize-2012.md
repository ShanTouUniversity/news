It is reported that the first Guangdong Higher Education Young Teachers’ Teaching Fundamentals Competition was held in Guangzhou from July 7 to 9. The event was organized by the Guangdong Federation of Trade Unions and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. It featured science, engineering, and humanities and social sciences categories, held respectively at South China Agricultural University, South China University of Technology, and Jinan University.

A total of 124 young teacher competitors from 104 higher-education institutions across Guangdong took part. Du Hong, PhD, a young teacher at Shantou University, competed with a section from the Genetics course titled “Mendel’s Laws and Extensions: Gene Action and Its Relationship with the Environment” and won third prize in the science category.
