It is reported that Shantou University issued a document (STU Fa [2012] No. 101) on August 23, 2012, setting out arrangements for the internal acceptance stage of its "Project 211" Phase III key discipline construction programs. The review follows a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Project 211 Coordination Group Office on the Phase III acceptance work.

With support from the Guangdong provincial government, relevant authorities, and the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the university's Project 211 Phase III discipline programs had already made positive progress. According to the university, this acceptance review is both a final test of the programs' investment returns and a matter of whether the university can enter the next round of Project 211 construction.

## Key Milestones and Division of Work

| Item | Arrangement |
| ---- | ----------- |
| Leadership | Work conducted under the unified leadership of the university's Project 211 Phase III acceptance leadership group |
| Draft Summary Reports | Due by September 5 for each key discipline program |
| Data Support | Offices for equipment, infrastructure, personnel, and finance to compile data immediately |

## Full Cooperation Required

Under the document, members of the acceptance working group must organize, coordinate, and guide their units' acceptance work while managing routine duties, completing all tasks according to the schedule. Leaders of units hosting reviewed programs are required to treat the review as a near-term priority and provide full human and material support to program teams.

In addition, during the review period, units should minimize activities unrelated to the review while keeping teaching and research in normal order, and generally avoid arranging travel for program team members.
