It is reported that, in line with the teaching content of the "Situation and Policy" course for the first half of 2012, the Social Science Department of Shantou University has designated "Enhancing China's Cultural Soft Power" as unified teaching content and will hold a themed lecture on Saturday, August 25, from 9:00 to 11:30 a.m. at the university auditorium. Dr. Gao Qingrong will deliver the lecture, and all students taking the "Situation and Policy Education" course in the 2012 summer term are required to attend.

## Lecture Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Aug 25 (Saturday), 9:00–11:30 a.m. |
| Venue | University Auditorium |
| Speaker | Dr. Gao Qingrong |
| Audience | All students enrolled in the summer-term course |

The department reminds students to enter between 8:45 and 8:55 a.m., while course instructors are responsible for organization and attendance.

## Course Assessment

The lecture is a key component of the course. All enrolled students must submit an essay related to the lecture's content — using a standard template — to their instructor, serving as the primary basis for course assessment.
