Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office and Network Center will provide training on the MYSTU digital teaching platform for academic affairs coordinators and teaching faculty from each teaching unit. MYSTU uses single sign-on to bring together e-learning, scheduling, campus notices, internal email, and personal information management. Its new electronic teaching system is scheduled to replace the existing virtual classroom system from the fall semester.

In addition to file storage and sharing, the new system supports assignment management, quizzes, discussion areas, feedback, and a knowledge wiki. The training will help academic affairs coordinators learn the system and conduct follow-up training within their units, while giving teaching faculty an overview of its functions before the fall semester.
