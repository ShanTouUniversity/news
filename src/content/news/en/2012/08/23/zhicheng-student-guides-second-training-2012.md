According to Zhicheng College, in support of the university's goal of cultivating internationalized, refined talent with distinctive characteristics, and to strengthen the overall competence of the college's student guides, consolidate their work, help them better understand themselves, set an example and enhance their sense of responsibility, the college will hold the second pre-service training for student guides of the 2012-2013 academic year on Thursday, August 30, 2012.

All student guides of the college are required to attend the course training at 2:30 p.m. in Lecture Room J-354 as arranged.
