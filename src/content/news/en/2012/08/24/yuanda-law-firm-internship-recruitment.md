Yuanda Law Firm plans to recruit several interns from the 2009 law cohort of Shantou University’s Law School. Applicants must email their resumes to `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` **by September 10, 2012**, and follow the notice’s instructions for including their year, major, name, and the firm in the email subject line.

The firm plans to visit the Law School for interviews in late September. The specific time and venue will be announced separately. Yuanda Law Firm’s website is [www.mwechinalaw.com](http://www.mwechinalaw.com/).

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School)
