Changjiang College of Art and Design at Shantou University will host the lecture “Profitable Design 3.0: From Pursuit to Acquisition, and Then to Attracting Followers” at **7:30 p.m. on Monday, August 27, 2012**. The lecture will take place in the college’s first-floor lecture classroom and is open to the STU community.

Gong Kai will deliver the lecture. Gong is a founding partner of JIHE Brand Design Consultants (China), a director of Transworld International Brand Management Co., Ltd., and a council member of the Southern Brand Strategy Promotion Center. He has extensive experience in brand creation and management consulting, with work spanning brand strategy, brand identity design, communications, marketing, and innovation.

Gong is also known for developing the “Living Brand” brand-growth identity theory and for research and practice related to brand gene sequencing and sustainable brand-profit strategies. The lecture will explore brand design and the creation of brand value. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
