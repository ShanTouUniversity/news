The Shantou University Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Shantou University Student Union will hold the third FLY “Trial Wings” program to develop student leadership and support the leadership-student exchange program between Shantou University and the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. The program is scheduled for August 31, September 5, September 9, and September 12, 2012, over four training days.

The program plans to enroll 30 participants: 12 exchange students from the University of Nottingham Ningbo China and 18 students from STU. The on-campus places are primarily intended for presidents or vice presidents of student organizations. Participants will support reception and work exchanges connected with the two universities’ visits and attend activities including an off-campus practical session titled “A New Journey,” project-management case analysis, a human-resources management workshop, and a time-management lecture.

## On-Campus Places

Of the 18 on-campus places, 12 are designated reception places: two each for the Shantou University Student Union, Student Association Federation, Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association, and Zhicheng College; and one each for the College of Science, Business School, College of Engineering, and STU SIFE team. Recommended participants from these organizations will also help receive exchange students from the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. The remaining six places are open to student organizations across the university. If applications exceed the available places, participants will be selected by random draw.

## Application Information

Both organization recommendations and individual applications require the application form. Applicants for organizational recommendation should submit their materials to their organization’s president or chair, which will determine the recommended list and send it collectively. Individual applicants may email the completed form directly. Materials must be sent to `11lchen2@stu.edu.cn` by **24:00 on Monday, August 27, 2012**.

The training plan, application form, and other activity attachments are not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for these materials.
