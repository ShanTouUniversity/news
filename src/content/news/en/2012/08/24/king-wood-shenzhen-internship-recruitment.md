King & Wood Mallesons’ Shenzhen office plans to recruit several interns from the 2009 law cohort of Shantou University’s Law School. The Law School’s Career Development Center will arrange interviews separately depending on the number of applications; the interview time and venue have yet to be determined.

Applicants must complete the application form and email their materials to `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` **by August 29, 2012**. The firm’s website is [www.kingandwood.com](http://www.kingandwood.com/).

The application form is not reproduced here. Please consult the original OA notice for the attachment.
