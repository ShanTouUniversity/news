It is reported that the July utility fees for faculty and staff have now been published (this meter reading took place on August 1 and 2, and the previous reading on July 1).

Faculty members can check their fees by logging into the Shantou University utility fee management system (http://power.stu.edu.cn) with their campus network accounts. Those with questions or objections should go to the Utilities Center for verification from August 13 to 17, tel. 2489.

After that period, fees without objection will be deducted from the August 2012 salary accounts by the Finance Office; those without salary accounts should pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Facilities Management Office)
