It is reported that the Office of Scientific Research has forwarded the notice on recommending review experts for the 2011 Collaborative Innovation Center issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education (Document No. YUE JIAO KE HAN [2012] 82), asking all colleges to study it carefully and actively recommend qualified experts.

Recommenders should fill in the "Recommendation Form for Review Experts for the 2011 Plan" and send the electronic version to yxhu@stu.edu.cn before August 16. Contact: Hu Yunxiang, tel. 13502738123.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Scientific Research)
