It is reported by the Network and Information Center that, in line with the annual work plan, the university will replace aging network switches in several areas, during which the network in the relevant areas will be interrupted for about 30 minutes.

The affected areas and schedule are as follows:

| Affected area | Replacement date and time |
|---|---|
| Science Building, Mathematics Building | Aug 7, 2012, 09:00–17:00 |
| Student Dormitory E, east and west sides | Aug 8, 2012, 09:00–17:00 |
| Student Dormitory E, south and north sides | Aug 9, 2012, 09:00–17:00 |
| Student Dormitory F | Aug 10, 2012, 09:00–17:00 |
| Student Dormitory D, east and west sides | Aug 13, 2012, 09:00–17:00 |
| Student Dormitory D, south and north sides | Aug 14, 2012, 09:00–17:00 |

The restoration time will not be separately announced; users are asked to pass the word around. For enquiries, contact the Network and Information Center at 0754-82903413 ext. 100, mobile 13592895188 (campus short number 665188), or stunic@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
