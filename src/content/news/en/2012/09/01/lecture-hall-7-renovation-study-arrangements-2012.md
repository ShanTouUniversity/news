It is reported that Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office plans to renovate Lecture Hall 7. Work is scheduled to begin on September 4, 2012, and is expected to finish by September 23. Lecture Hall 7 will close at 6:00 p.m. on September 3 and will not be available for self-study during construction.

## Alternative Study Space

The university will temporarily open Lecture Hall 1 as an overnight self-study room. Students must leave if Lecture Hall 1 is needed for regular classes. They are also responsible for their personal belongings while studying there and must take their belongings with them when leaving.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture Hall 7 closure | 6:00 p.m. on September 3 |
| Renovation | Begins September 4; expected to finish by September 23 |
| Temporary study space | Lecture Hall 1, open overnight |

Students studying in Lecture Hall 7 should clear their personal belongings before 6:00 p.m. on September 3. Items left in the room after the deadline will be treated as abandoned property and removed by classroom administrators. During overnight study, students should follow classroom rules, comply with staff instructions, and pay attention to personal safety.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office and Network and Information Center)
