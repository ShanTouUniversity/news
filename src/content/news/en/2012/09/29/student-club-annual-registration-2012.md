The Shantou University Youth League Committee and Student Association Union have begun the 2012–2013 annual reporting and Club License registration process for student clubs. The process updates club information under the student-club regulations and supports more standardized administration.

Each club must submit four items: a 2011–2012 annual work report covering activities and fund use; a report on fall 2012 recruitment, including recruitment numbers and membership-fee collection; a 2012–2013 work plan covering activities and membership-fee use; and a basic member information registration form for 2012–2013. The member form is available from the materials-download area of the Student Association Union website.

Electronic materials should be sent as a compressed file to `992238914@qq.com`. Paper materials may be submitted to the Club Information Department staff office, Room 731, from 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. on working days from October 8 to 12. All annual materials must be submitted by October 15, 2012.

After submitting the annual materials, clubs must visit the Student Association Union Information Department to apply for and register their Club Licenses. The Youth League Committee and Student Association Union said that annual reporting and license registration are important parts of student-club administration and club standardization.
