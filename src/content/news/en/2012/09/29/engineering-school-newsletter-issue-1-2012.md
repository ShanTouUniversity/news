Shantou University Engineering School has published its first 2012 work bulletin, the sixth issue overall. The bulletin presents recent work in engineering education reform, academic research, international exchange and student development.

## Contents

The “School Highlights” section covers a visit by the provincial education discipline-inspection authorities, participation by university leaders in engineering education seminars, a visit by a Hong Kong University of Science and Technology scholar to the World Engineers Forum, student participation in the National Undergraduate Mechanical Innovation Design Competition, faculty and student exchange at MIT, and the STU–University of Calgary CDIO exchange program. It also reports on the school's annual summary and recognition meeting and honors received by faculty members.

The “Work Updates” section is divided into undergraduate teaching, academic research and student affairs. Teaching items address engineering education reform exchanges, teaching reform meetings, professional accreditation research and teaching seminars. Research items cover industry–university collaboration bases and projects, an engineering technology research center committee meeting, a young faculty research-fund forum and an international academic conference. Student-affairs items address entrepreneurship and career-planning exchanges, scholarships, technology training, programming and mechanical-design competitions, STU–MIT exchange activities, the student congress and the science and technology festival.
