Shantou University Human Resources Office has circulated the application arrangements for the second cohort of the Hundred Outstanding Talents of South Guangdong Training Program and is organizing applications from eligible members of the university community. The university will review the applications, determine its recommended candidates and submit them to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security.

## Eligibility

Applicants should be patriotic, law-abiding, ethical and in good health, with a strong sense of purpose and substantial academic achievements and influence in their disciplines. Applicants should generally hold a senior professional title, be no older than 55 and have worked full-time in Guangdong for at least four years. The age limit is extended to 58 for effective candidates for membership of the Chinese Academy of Sciences or the Chinese Academy of Engineering.

Applicants must also meet at least one of the following criteria: be an effective candidate for membership of either academy; rank among the first three principal contributors to a second-prize-or-higher National Science and Technology Award or be the first contributor to a provincial first-prize science and technology award; serve as leader of a national 863 Program expert group or chief scientist of a 973 Program project; be selected for the national Thousand Talents Plan, the Chinese Academy of Sciences Hundred Talents Program, the Ministry of Education Changjiang Scholars Program or the national New Century Talent Project; have received the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars or lead a major National Natural Science Foundation of China project; lead a national laboratory, key laboratory, engineering laboratory or engineering technology research center; or be a leading professional urgently needed by an industry prioritized for support in Guangdong.

## Materials and deadlines

Applicants should read the Implementation Measures for the Guangdong Hundred Outstanding Talents of South Guangdong Training Program, complete the application form and prepare copies of diplomas and degrees, identity documents, professional qualification certificates, and evidence of achievements or honors.

| Date | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| By October 12, 2012 | Submit materials to the applicant's unit and email the electronic version to `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn` |
| By October 16, 2012 | Each unit consolidates materials, completes its recommendation and submits them to the Human Resources Office, New Administration Center, Room 315 |
| After university recommendation | Submit additional copies of certificates and supporting documents, bound in A4 format in 19 copies, and email the electronic version |
