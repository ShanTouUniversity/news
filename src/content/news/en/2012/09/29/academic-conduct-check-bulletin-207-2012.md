The Shantou University Office for the Three Styles has issued Academic Conduct Check Bulletin No. 207, reporting the results of classroom checks in Week 2 of the 2012–2013 fall semester. The inspection team conducted attendance checks in Building E on September 25 and September 27 and recorded lateness on both occasions.

| Check date | Location | Time | Lateness recorded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| September 25, 2012 | Building E | 8:00 a.m. | 6 students late by up to 5 minutes |
| September 27, 2012 | Building E | 2:00 p.m. | 6 students late by up to 5 minutes |

The names of the students concerned were sent to the colleges and residential colleges in the bulletin attachment. The office asked the relevant units to provide criticism and education and continue their work on classroom discipline and academic conduct.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office for the Three Styles)
