The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has announced the fall opening schedule for the Student Learning Center (SLC). The SLC will open to all STU undergraduates from Monday of Week 4, October 8, 2012, and provide learning-support services.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible users | All undergraduates |
| Opening date | October 8, 2012 |
| Hours | Daily, 3–10 p.m. |
| Services | Academic consultations, group discussions and study classes |

Students may visit the SLC during opening hours. The detailed academic-consultation schedule and service guide are provided in the attachments issued by the Student Affairs Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the academic-consultation schedule and SLC service guide.
