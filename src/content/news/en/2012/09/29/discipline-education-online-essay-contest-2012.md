The Shantou University Commission for Discipline Inspection Office is organizing faculty members and students to participate in Shantou's online essay campaign for Discipline Education Month. The campaign invites submissions related to integrity culture and ideological and ethical development.

The campaign runs from August 10 to November 10, 2012. Submissions may present materials and stories related to integrity culture in formats such as news stories, essays, cartoon commentary, poetry or prose, with a limit of 1,000 Chinese characters. The campaign also invites reflection articles from Party members and officials and reviews of the film *Loyalty and Betrayal*.

Contributors may submit their work through the campaign website at <http://www.dahuawang.com/ztxw/jiweiedu2012/> and send a copy to the Commission for Discipline Inspection Office at `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`, using “Shantou Discipline Inspection Essay” as the email subject. Enquiries can be made at `82902346`.
