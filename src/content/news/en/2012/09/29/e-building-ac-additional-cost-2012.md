It is reported that the Equipment Office of the Resource Management Office issued a notice on September 29, 2012, regarding additional costs for the E-Building air-conditioning removal and installation project.

The project started on August 1, 2012. During construction, additional work was required for the installation of air-conditioning units in the U-shaped tiered classrooms and research rooms, including piping from the control boxes to the main distribution room downstairs and lightning protection facilities. A preliminary estimate puts the additional cost at about 18,000 yuan, with the cumulative increase remaining below the university’s 10% threshold.

In accordance with university regulations, the additional cost is now publicized. Those with objections may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Equipment Office before 17:30 on October 8.
