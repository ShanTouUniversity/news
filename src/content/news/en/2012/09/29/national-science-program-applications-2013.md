Shantou University Research Office has circulated the Ministry of Science and Technology's application arrangements for the 2013 National Spark Program, Torch Program, Key New Products Program, Soft Science Research Program and Science and Technology for the People Program. The notice organizes applications from relevant university units and faculty members.

Applications will be submitted through a unified online system and will be subject to limits on the number of submissions. The Research Office asks interested faculty to prepare materials according to the requirements of each program and contact the office before the relevant deadline. The university will make recommendations based on the applications received.

| Program | Deadline for mailing materials and online recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Science and Technology for the People Program | 5 p.m., October 10, 2012 |
| Torch Program and Key New Products Program | 5 p.m., October 12, 2012 |
| Soft Science Research Program | 5 p.m., October 19, 2012 |
| Spark Program | 5 p.m., November 10, 2012 |

Faculty members may contact Hu Yunxiang or Zhu Changsheng at `82902813`. The Ministry of Science and Technology notice, program guidelines and application materials in the attachments govern the detailed requirements.
