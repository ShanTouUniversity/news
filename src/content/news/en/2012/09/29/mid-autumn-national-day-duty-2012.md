It is reported that the President’s Office issued a notice on September 29, 2012, summarizing the duty arrangements for the 2012 Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holiday.

To facilitate contact among departments during the holiday, the consolidated duty roster is now available for departments to check. Details are in the OA attachment. Questions may be sent to the President’s Office at o_jdk@stu.edu.cn.

> ⚠️ The duty roster is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (President’s Office)
