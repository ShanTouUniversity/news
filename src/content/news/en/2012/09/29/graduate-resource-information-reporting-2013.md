To support the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education's preparation of employment plans and recommendation and placement work for 2013 university graduates, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office has begun collecting 2013 graduate employment information.

Students scheduled to graduate in 2013 must log in to the Shantou University graduate employment information system at <http://gdc.stu.edu.cn/> and complete their personal information by October 23, 2012. The Student Affairs Office noted that basic graduate information will be used for subsequent employment placement work and asked students to check and submit their information carefully.

Colleges and residential colleges will notify the relevant graduates. Detailed operating instructions are available in the attachment to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)

> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachment for operating instructions for submitting graduate employment information.
