The Shantou University Commission for Discipline Inspection Office is organizing faculty members and students to participate in Shantou's second Integrity Reading Month. The activity encourages the university community to contribute to integrity-culture development through reading and writing.

The theme is “Read Integrity Books, Promote Upright Culture.” Contributors may write on this theme in connection with preparations for the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, study and practice of the new Guangdong spirit, or the development of a happy Guangdong. Titles and genres are open, and submissions must not exceed 3,000 Chinese characters. Entries must be original, unpublished in newspapers, online media or other outlets, and may not be submitted to more than one venue.

The submission deadline is October 20, 2012. Contributors should email an electronic copy to the Commission for Discipline Inspection Office at `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`, using “Award Essay on Integrity Book Review” as the subject, and submit an A4 printed copy to Room 422, Commission for Discipline Inspection Office, New Administration Center. Enquiries can be made at `82902346`.
