The Shantou University–Chinese University of Hong Kong Joint Research Centre for Christian Studies will host the “Chaoshan Christianity and Women” roundtable on October 11, 2012. Scholars from mainland China, Hong Kong, the Netherlands and the United States will discuss the spread of Christianity and women&apos;s experiences and gender relations in Chaoshan and East Asia.

The roundtable will take place from 9:00 to 11:30 a.m. and from 1:30 to 6:00 p.m. in Meeting Room 205, Science and Technology Center, Shantou University. Topics will include the influence of Christianity&apos;s arrival in China on traditional gender relations and gender consciousness, as well as how Chaoshan Christians understand and express religious and cultural identities.
