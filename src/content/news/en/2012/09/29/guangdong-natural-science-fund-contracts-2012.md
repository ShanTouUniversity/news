The 2012 Guangdong Natural Science Fund projects, excluding the Provincial Distinguished Young Scholars projects, have been announced. Shantou University Research Office has arranged contract signing for the funded projects. Project leaders must sign project-performance contracts with the Office of the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation and complete online submission and paper filing according to the schedule.

The main research content, objectives and principal researchers in each contract must match the project application. The common contract start date is October 1, 2012. Project leaders should first read the contract terms and the Guangdong Natural Science Fund project administration and funding-administration measures.

| Date | Task |
| --- | --- |
| October 1–15 | Log in to the Guangdong Natural Science Fund website, complete and submit the contract for review by the university Research Office |
| October 22–26 | Online review by the Office of the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation |
| By October 29 | After the status shows “Accepted by the Foundation Committee,” download the contract, print four double-sided A4 copies and submit them to the Research Office |
| December | Expert review comments for projects not approved are expected to be posted online for applicants |

Project leaders can complete the relevant procedures through the Guangdong Natural Science Fund website at <http://gdsf.gdstc.gov.cn/>. Enquiries may be directed to Hu Yunxiang or Zhu Changsheng at `82902813`. The list of funded projects is provided in the OA attachment.
