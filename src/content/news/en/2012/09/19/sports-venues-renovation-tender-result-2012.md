Following the recommendation of Shantou University’s Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, the winning contractor for the Sports Department sports venue renovation project was announced for public review from **September 19 to 21**. Shantou Yueli Construction and Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. won the bid for **RMB 92,853.76**, including a **RMB 4,421.61** provisional sum fully controlled by the project owner.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Sports Department sports venue renovation |
| Winning bidder | Shantou Yueli Construction and Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Winning bid | RMB 92,853.76 |
| Provisional sum | RMB 4,421.61 |
| Public-review period | September 19–21 |

The tendering entity is the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University. Contacts are Mr. Chen and Mr. Lu, at `0754-82903216`; fax: `0754-82902314`. Complaints are handled by the university’s Supervision and Audit Office at `0754-82902346`; fax: `0754-82903663`.
