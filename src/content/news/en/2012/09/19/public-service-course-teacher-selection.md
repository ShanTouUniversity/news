According to the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University, five public-service courses will be offered in the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. The university is inviting teachers and graduate students who have passed the public-service course instructor training to select teaching assignments based on the course requirements and syllabi.

## Course Requirements

| Course | Coordinator | Requirement and status | Schedule and service location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family Tutoring Service for Children with Cerebral Palsy | Zhang Yan | 3 people, filled | October 10 to November 28, Wednesday and Saturday afternoons; Chaoshan region |
| Shuguang Volunteer Service-Learning Course | Yao Qin | 6 people, 5 still needed | Schools or communities in urban Shantou; includes a site visit on September 23, theory classes in October, practice on Sunday mornings from October 14 to December 2, and a summary on December 5 |
| Sunshine Community Service-Learning Course | Yao Qin | 4 people, 2 still needed | Some disadvantaged communities in Shantou; the schedule is the same as the Shuguang course |
| Rural Health Promotion Plan | Xu Longshui | 5 people, filled | Promotion and course selection in September, centralized training on October 24, and field service on October 27, 28, and November 6 in Baita Town, Jieyang County |
| March Toward the Ocean: Publicity on Maritime Territory Awareness and Marine Ecological Protection | Sun Zewei and Chen Yichun | 5 people, filled | Classes from Weeks 4 to 10; service at primary and secondary schools and public venues in the Shantou area |

Those wishing to undertake a course assignment must complete the Public-Service Course Teacher Course Selection Information Form. The Excel file should be named “Public-Service Course Teacher Course Selection Information Form-Name” and sent as an attachment to Liu Xiangling of the Academic Affairs Office at lxl0726@stu.edu.cn by 5 p.m. on September 20. The course requirements table, syllabi, qualified trainee list, and selection form are available in the original notice on the OA system.
