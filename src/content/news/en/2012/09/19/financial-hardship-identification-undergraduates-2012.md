Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has begun its 2012–2013 assessment of financial hardship among undergraduate students. Each school or residential college will establish an assessment working group, while classes, year groups, or Youth League branches will organize review panels to handle applications, reviews, approvals, and public announcements under the university’s relevant regulations.

## Assessment timeline

| Stage | Schedule and arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Student application | Students wishing to apply must log in to the [Student Affairs System](http://sao.stu.edu.cn) by October 9 and submit an application, then provide the Student Family Financial Circumstances Survey Form and other supporting materials to the review panel |
| Class, year-group, or Youth League branch review | October 10–17; review application materials and determine the list and hardship category |
| School or residential college review and public announcement | October 18–25; review the results, announce them among the relevant students, and confirm the assessment results |

Financial-hardship assessment is conducted once each academic year. Students previously recognized as financially disadvantaged who apply again without a significant change in their family finances only need to submit an application through the system and do not need to provide another family financial circumstances survey form.

For problems with the online application, contact Ms. Chen at the Student Aid Management Center at `82904749` or `s_xtchen1@stu.edu.cn`. Application instructions, the survey form, and review materials are included in the OA attachments.
