Shantou University’s Research Office has circulated a September 2012 industry-academia cooperation opportunity from Guangdong Aihua New Optoelectronics Technology. The company is seeking research support for a “high-power LED floodlight,” defined as a product with power above 2 kilowatts, and plans to pursue technical cooperation in the following areas.

| Area | Research need |
| --- | --- |
| Thermal management | Compact, high-efficiency heat dissipation system design |
| Optical system | High-power LED optical system design |
| Control system | Power-line carrier control system design |
| Materials | Development of corrosion-resistant materials for outdoor lighting |

STU units and faculty members interested in cooperation should contact the Science and Technology Achievement and Development Section by **the end of the working day on Friday, September 21** to learn more about the project. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, at `82903271` or `ygluo@stu.edu.cn`.
