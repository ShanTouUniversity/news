According to the School of Engineering at Shantou University, the third National Undergraduate Engineering Training Comprehensive Ability Competition will be held at Dalian University of Technology in June 2013. The competition is open to undergraduates at universities nationwide and consists of university, provincial or regional, and national levels. The provincial or regional preliminary competition is to be completed by March 20, 2013.

The School of Engineering is accepting student teams for its campus selection. Teams will consist of three students, with no restrictions on year or major. The unified competition theme is the “Carbon-Free Vehicle Obstacle-Crossing Competition.” The campus process includes team registration, training in areas such as mechanical processing, a campus preliminary round, and selection of teams for the Guangdong provincial and national competitions.

## Registration

Teams must prepare a preliminary design proposal and send it together with the registration form as email attachments to 10zxu@stu.edu.cn. The email subject should follow the format “XXX Group Engineering Competition Registration Form,” with XXX representing the team leader. Registration closes on September 29, 2012. An expert panel will decide and announce the teams selected for the provincial competition based on the registration situation and design proposals.

The competition notice, registration form, design proposal requirements, and other attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
