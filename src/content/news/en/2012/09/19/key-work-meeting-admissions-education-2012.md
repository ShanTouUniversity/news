Shantou University plans to hold a meeting on key initiatives for the main campus, including admissions, advanced undergraduate education, and student employment and entrepreneurship, on **Saturday, September 22**. The one-day meeting will take place at Jieyang Donghai Hotel. Participants will gather at the university motto monument at **8:10 a.m.** that day before departing.

## Participants

The meeting will include university leaders; the deans, vice deans responsible for teaching, and Party branch secretaries or deputy secretaries of each undergraduate professional school; one principal representative from each office and other teaching or administrative unit; and deputy-division-level personnel from the Teaching Affairs Office and the Student Affairs Office and Youth League Committee.

Units must finalize their attendance lists and send them to the Secretariat Section of the Party and Government Office by **5:30 p.m. on September 20**. The contact is Qiu Saiqing, at `sqqiu@stu.edu.cn`.
