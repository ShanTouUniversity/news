According to the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University, the university will soon organize applications and reviews for textbook development and publication subsidies for the second half of 2012. Those planning to publish textbooks during that period and meeting the subsidy requirements may prepare an application under the university's relevant administrative measures.

## Application Information

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | People planning to publish textbooks in the second half of 2012 who meet the subsidy requirements |
| Deadline | October 15, 2012 |
| Printed materials | Three copies of the application form and supporting materials, submitted to the Academic Affairs Office service counter on the third floor of the New Administration Building |
| Electronic material | Send the electronic application form to zhailiu@stu.edu.cn |
| Contact | 82902736; contact: Zhai Liu |

Applicants are required to complete the application form under the *Administrative Measures for Shantou University Textbook Development and Publication Subsidies* and prepare the supporting materials. The administrative measures, application form, and other attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
