According to information issued by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Centre for Arts Education, the Shantou University Choir will recruit new members from across the university and hold auditions on September 22. Applicants do not need special musical training or instrumental skills; any student interested in singing may participate.

## Audition Details

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 22, 2012 (Saturday), 9:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Music Rehearsal Room, first floor, STU Building, Centre for Arts Education |
| Eligible applicants | All STU students |
| Registration | On-site registration on the audition day |

The audition will include pitch imitation or a presentation of a self-selected song excerpt. There are no restrictions on song type. Students with instrumental skills may bring an instrument for an on-site presentation and may also bring accompaniment. More information is available through the choir's official Weibo account.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Centre for Arts Education)
