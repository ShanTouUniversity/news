According to the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University, the Basic Principles of Marxism class taught by Cheng Yingzi will be suspended because the instructor will be away attending a meeting. The class, course section number 54242, was scheduled for the ABC periods on Friday of Week 1 (September 21) in Room 208, Building E. A make-up time has not yet been arranged, and enrolled students are asked to inform one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
