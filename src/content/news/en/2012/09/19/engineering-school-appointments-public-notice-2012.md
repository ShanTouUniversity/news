Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has issued a pre-appointment announcement for three proposed Engineering School administrative appointments. The proposals were considered at the 10th university leaders’ meeting on September 18: Bao Nengsheng is proposed as associate dean of the Engineering School, Zhang Jie as deputy head of its Department of Computer Science, and Zhang Kaidong as director of its Computer Teaching Center.

| Proposed appointee | Proposed position |
| --- | --- |
| Bao Nengsheng | Associate dean, Engineering School |
| Zhang Jie | Deputy head, Department of Computer Science, Engineering School |
| Zhang Kaidong | Director, Computer Teaching Center, Engineering School |

The announcement is made under the Trial Measures for the Appointment of STU Administrative Cadres (STU Party Document No. 47, 2009). Organizations and individuals with comments should submit them in writing to the university’s Human Resources Office by **September 26**. Materials from organizations must bear an official seal, while individuals must sign their real names. The office can be reached at `82903390` or `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.
