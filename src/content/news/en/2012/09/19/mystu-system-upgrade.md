According to a notice from Shantou University's Network and Information Center, the MYSTU system will undergo an upgrade, during which the related website will be unavailable for maintenance. The notice does not specify the maintenance period, and campus network users should watch for the service to resume.

## Affected Website

- [MYSTU](http://my.stu.edu.cn)

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
