It is reported that the exchange program between the Business School of Shantou University and the Asper School of Business at the University of Manitoba has entered a new round of selection. The two schools signed a cooperation agreement in September 2010, and the program officially launched in 2011. Under the current arrangement, the school will select two undergraduate students for a four-month exchange (January to April) at the University of Manitoba beginning in January 2013.

## Selection Criteria

According to the selection standards released by the school, applicants must be second-year or above undergraduate students of the Business School, with priority given to those who have not yet participated in any university or school-level exchange programs. Applicants are required to have a GPA of 3.0 or above and demonstrate strong English proficiency in listening, speaking, reading and writing, submitting test scores such as TOEFL or IELTS by September 30. Candidates who excel academically, are outgoing, actively engage in public affairs and volunteer service, and are good at promoting Shantou University will be preferred.

## How to Apply

Interested students must submit both printed and electronic application materials to the school's International Programs Office (Room 211, Wenxi Building) **before 12:00 noon on September 27**. The required materials include:

| Material | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Overseas Exchange Student Application Form | See OA attachment |
| Academic Transcript | Transcript covering studies at STU |
| English Essay | 300–500 words, on the purpose of joining the program and overseas study plan |

A selection panel will review the written materials and determine the final two candidates through comprehensive assessment.

## Costs

The University of Manitoba will waive tuition fees for exchange students. However, students are responsible for their own expenses during the exchange, including administrative fees, round-trip travel, accommodation and meals, books and materials, as well as insurance and medical costs.

For further inquiries, please contact the International Programs Office of the Business School (Contact: Xu Xiaojing, Tel: 82903382).
