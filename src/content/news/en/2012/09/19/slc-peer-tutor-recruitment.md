According to the Student Affairs Office, the Shantou University Student Learning Centre (SLC) is recruiting peer tutors from among upper-year undergraduates at the main campus for the fall 2012 semester. The tutors will support academic inquiry courses and peer-learning services, with recruitment for individual courses coordinated by SLC student liaisons in the relevant schools or departments.

## Position Information

Applicants must be second-year or above undergraduates at STU's main campus and demonstrate responsibility, strong communication skills, and a friendly and conscientious attitude. Their grade in the course they plan to support should normally rank within the top 20% of that course's assessment results. Special cases require certification from the course instructor or another teacher familiar with the circumstances.

Peer tutors will share study methods and experience, answer students' questions, organize course-related discussions, and provide assistance based on the learning progress and needs of the students they serve. The position generally runs from Weeks 3 to 16 of the semester for two hours per week. Compensation follows the university's standard for peer tutors in work-study positions.

## Application

Applicants should consult the SLC student liaison in the relevant school or department about the requirements for their intended course. Applications must be submitted through the [Student Work-Study Application System](http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/) by 10 p.m. on September 25 (Tuesday), with the electronic application form also sent to the relevant liaison.

SLC contact information: 82901482 and slc@stu.edu.cn; website: [slc.stu.edu.cn](http://slc.stu.edu.cn). The recruitment process, course list, and liaison contact details are available in the attachments to the original notice on the OA system.
