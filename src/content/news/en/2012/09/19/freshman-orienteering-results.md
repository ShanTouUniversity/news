According to the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the 2012 freshman orienteering event began on September 10 and has now concluded. The organizers said that the event received active participation from the freshman class and congratulated the winning students.

## Prize Collection

Winning teams may collect their prizes from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on September 19 and 20 at Room 731, Building 7. The list of winning teams is included in the attachment to the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
>
> ⚠️ The list of winning teams and other event attachments are available on the OA system.
