The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has completed stamping the student cards submitted by new students and announced the collection arrangement. Colleges should notify freshmen to collect the cards by class from the Academic Affairs Office service window on the third floor of the New Administration Center.

The notice does not specify a separate centralized collection time; classes may collect the cards according to arrangements made by their colleges.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
