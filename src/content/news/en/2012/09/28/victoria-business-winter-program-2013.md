Shantou University Business School has opened applications for a 2013 winter business management program at the University of Victoria (UVic) in Canada. The school plans to select several outstanding undergraduates, full-time or part-time graduate students and MBA participants. The program is open only to students of the Business School, and classes will be taught in English by professors from the UVic Business School.

## Program details

The program will run from February 17 to March 2, 2013. Its curriculum includes business communication, international business practices, entrepreneurship and business innovation, and service-industry practices. UVic will also arrange extracurricular activities and local visits. Students who complete the program will receive six credits.

## Application and selection

Applicants should be Business School students with a foundation in economics, strong academic performance and no failed courses. They should also have strong English results and communication skills. The Business School will conduct an initial screening and determine the final participants according to the applications received.

Applicants must email a university English transcript and an English résumé to `10zzhou@stu.edu.cn` by noon on October 15, 2012. Further information is available in the course introduction issued with the notice or on the relevant UVic program webpage. Questions may be directed to Xu Xiaojing in the Business School's Office of International Affairs Programs at `82903382`.
