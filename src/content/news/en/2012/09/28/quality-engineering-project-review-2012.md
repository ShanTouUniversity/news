The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has launched a review and acceptance process for higher education teaching quality and teaching reform projects. National and provincial projects launched by STU since the 11th Five-Year Plan period will undergo either an interim review or completion acceptance to assess their progress and strengthen their demonstration role.

## Scope and review areas

The process covers specialty programs, including key programs, high-quality courses, including online and bilingual demonstration courses, experimental teaching demonstration centers, teaching teams, experimental zones for innovation in talent-development models and teaching reform projects. Projects that had completed their full construction cycle by August 2012 will undergo completion acceptance; projects still within their construction cycle will receive an interim review. A specialty program with both national and provincial approvals will be assessed according to the date of its national approval.

The review will examine achievement of construction goals and tasks, progress and innovative practices, landmark outcomes and demonstration effects, problems and proposed responses, and the matching and use of project funds.

## Materials and assessment

Project leaders should complete the review registration form against the original application or task document and provide supporting evidence. Projects under interim review should submit a progress report, while projects seeking completion acceptance should submit a project summary report. Projects that require websites, such as high-quality courses and experimental teaching demonstration centers, should provide functioning links and keep their content updated. Provincial teaching reform projects should use the designated registration form; specialty programs and experimental teaching demonstration centers should prepare their materials first and await further instructions after the provincial education authority issues its dedicated forms.

The university will organize reviews by internal and external experts. Interim review projects will be examined at meetings, while completion projects will undergo a defense in which the project leader presents with slides and answers expert questions. The Academic Affairs Office will place project self-review materials in the quality-project section of the university website for public display and review by provincial education experts.

Interim review conclusions will be classified as passed, deferred or not passed. Completion acceptance results will be classified as excellent, passed, deferred or not passed. Projects receiving a deferred result will be given a deadline for corrective work and reviewed again. The results will also inform future project applications, evaluations and recommendations.
