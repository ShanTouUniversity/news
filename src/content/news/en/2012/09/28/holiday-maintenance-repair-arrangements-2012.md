Shantou University Resource Management Office has announced campus maintenance and repair arrangements for the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holidays. During the holiday period from September 30 to October 7, university units may contact the duty staff in the Infrastructure Office when facilities require repair.

| Service | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Carpentry, metalwork and fan repairs | Mr. Wu: `13509888389`; Mr. Zhuang: `13556454895` |
| Drainage and clearing services | Mr. Chen: `13502753952` |
| Emergency repairs | `13556337078` |

The Resource Management Office asks units to contact the appropriate duty staff according to the repair needed. The emergency repair number should be used for urgent situations.
