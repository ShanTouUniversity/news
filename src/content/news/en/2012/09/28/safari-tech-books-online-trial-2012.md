Shantou University Library is offering students and staff trial access to Safari Tech Books Online. The database is a joint service from O'Reilly Media and Pearson Technology Group and contains a large collection of technology books from the two publishers for information technology learning, teaching and research.

Topics include certification, enterprise computing, Java, Linux/Unix, web development, Windows and XML. Readers can access the resource through the library database page at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=354>. The notice advised users in 2012 to use Internet Explorer and open the link in a new window.
