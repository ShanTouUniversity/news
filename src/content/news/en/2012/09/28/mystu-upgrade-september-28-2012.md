The Shantou University Network and Information Center will upgrade the MYSTU system. During the maintenance period, the MYSTU website and all system functions will be unavailable to users across the university.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance period | 22:00 on September 28 to 06:00 on September 29, 2012 |
| Affected URL | [my.stu.edu.cn](http://my.stu.edu.cn) |
| Scope | All campus users; all MYSTU functions will be suspended |
| Restoration notice | No separate notice will be issued when service resumes |

For questions during the maintenance period, contact the Network and Information Center at `0754-82903547-8842`, mobile `13643058622` (campus short number `698622`), or `sunrise@stu.edu.cn`.
