Hongtai Restaurant at Shantou University has introduced individual Chinese and Western set meals in response to dining needs expressed by students and staff. The restaurant said the meals were developed by its chefs and would be available from September 28, 2012.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meal types | Individual Chinese and Western set meals |
| Available from | September 28, 2012 |
| Menu details | See the restaurant menu |

Students and staff may visit Hongtai Restaurant to learn about and order the set meals. The complete dishes and prices are listed in the menu attached to the original OA notice.
