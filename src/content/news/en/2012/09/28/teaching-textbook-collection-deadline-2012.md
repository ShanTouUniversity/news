The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has announced the collection arrangements for teaching books for the current semester. Faculty members who have not yet collected their books are asked to complete the process promptly so that the materials will be available for teaching.

Faculty members must bring their teaching-book collection forms to the Academic Affairs Office service window at the New Administration Center by October 12, 2012. Colleges will notify the relevant faculty members.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
