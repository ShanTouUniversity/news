Shantou University has released the Interim Management Measures for the Shantou University Student Entrepreneurship Fund, establishing an institutional basis for administering and using the fund. The measures were discussed and approved at the 10th university leadership meeting on September 18, 2012, and were formally issued for implementation on September 28.

The document is numbered Shantou University Document [2012] No. 118. Relevant units and participants in student entrepreneurship projects should conduct related work in accordance with the interim measures. The official document governs the detailed requirements for applications, use and administration of the fund.
