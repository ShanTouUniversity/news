Shantou University has released the Interim Management Measures for the Shantou University Student Entrepreneurship Park, establishing an institutional basis for the park's development and administration. The measures were discussed and approved at the 10th university leadership meeting on September 18, 2012, and were formally issued for implementation on September 28.

The document is numbered Shantou University Document [2012] No. 117. Relevant university units and participants in the student entrepreneurship park should conduct related work in accordance with the measures; the official document governs the detailed requirements.
