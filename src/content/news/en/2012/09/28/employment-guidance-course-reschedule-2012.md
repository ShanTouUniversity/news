It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 28, 2012, regarding the rescheduling of the *Employment Guidance* course (Class No. 55191).

## Rescheduling Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Employment Guidance* (Class No. 55191) |
| Originally scheduled | Saturday of Week 2 (September 29), periods 3-4 |
| Original venue | Room D203 |
| Adjustment | Suspended |
| Makeup time | Friday of Week 13 (December 14), periods 3-4 |
| Makeup venue | Room D203 (unchanged) |
| Reason | Instructor Zhu Guang on business trip |

The Student Affairs Office reminds students enrolled in the course to inform each other and attend the makeup class at the adjusted time in the original venue.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
