Shantou University will review implementation of university affairs disclosure by its functional departments in the 2011–2012 academic year and collect information on matters to be disclosed in 2012–2013. The work is intended to protect the rights of faculty, staff and students to know, participate and exercise oversight, while advancing democratic administration and supervision.

## Materials required from departments

Each functional department should complete the following by October 9, 2012:

- Check the University Affairs Disclosure Registration Form for Functional Departments for 2011–2012 submitted in November of the previous year, update any newly added matters, submit a written copy to the Supervision and Audit Office for filing, and email an electronic copy to `o_dwb@stu.edu.cn` and `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`;
- Review implementation of disclosure matters since the previous November, complete the Implementation Registration Form for University Affairs Disclosure by Functional Departments for 2011–2012, explain any items that were not disclosed, submit the written materials to the Supervision and Audit Office and email an electronic copy to `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`;
- Complete the University Affairs Disclosure Registration Form for 2012–2013, including the matters to be disclosed, disclosure scope, disclosure method and responsible person for each item, submit the written materials to the University Affairs Disclosure Oversight Group and email an electronic copy to `o_dwb@stu.edu.cn` and `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`.

Written materials should be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office in Room 422 of the Administration Center. The University Affairs Disclosure Oversight Group will consolidate the submissions for both academic years and publish them on the internal office information network for review by the university community.
