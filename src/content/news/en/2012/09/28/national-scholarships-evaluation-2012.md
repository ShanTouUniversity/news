It is reported that Shantou University has launched the 2012 evaluation of three national financial aid programs for full-time undergraduates: the National Scholarship (8,000 yuan per recipient), the National Encouragement Scholarship (5,000 yuan per recipient) and the National Grant (3,000 yuan per recipient).

According to arrangements released by the Student Affairs Office, the National Scholarship targets second-year or above undergraduates with outstanding academic and moral performance. Students in combined bachelor's-master's or master's-doctoral programs are in principle no longer eligible from their sixth year after enrollment. Applicants should rank within the top 10 percent of their cohort academically, with no failed courses. The National Encouragement Scholarship goes to financially disadvantaged yet high-performing students in the same year levels, while the National Grant covers full-time undergraduates from economically difficult families. The National Scholarship and the National Encouragement Scholarship cannot be held together; recipients who also won a 2011–2012 "Outstanding Student Scholarship" will receive only the higher-valued award.

Students applying for the National Scholarship must complete an online application on the university's scholarship website by 4 p.m. on Wednesday, October 10, print the application form and personal statement double-sided, sign them in black ink, and submit them to their school's (residential college's) counselor before the end of that working day. The other two programs require no printed application forms.

As quota allocations from Guangdong Provincial Department of Education have not yet been issued, each school's quotas will be announced separately. Schools are required to set up evaluation committees composed of school leaders, faculty representatives, counselors and student representatives, select National Scholarship winners through interviews, confirm results with committee signatures, and publicize them within the school for five working days. Relevant materials are due at the Student Aid Management Center by October 22. Candidates for the other two programs must in principle pass the poverty-status verification, with materials due by October 31.
