The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University has announced a schedule change for the continuing education course I Want to Go to School: Xie Hailong Documentary Photography Lecture, originally set for September 30, 2012. Photographer Xie Hailong will deliver the lecture. Teachers who enrolled in the course should attend according to the revised schedule.

| Item | Revised details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | I Want to Go to School: Xie Hailong Documentary Photography Lecture |
| Time | 14:30–17:00 on October 17, 2012 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall |
| Speaker | Xie Hailong |
| Participants | Teachers who enrolled in the course |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
