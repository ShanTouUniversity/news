Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has announced the university's 2012 projects for the National Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program for College Students. In line with arrangements from the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, and based on the allocation of places and the approval of provincial projects, STU selected 72 projects for recommendation to the national program.

The projects cover innovation training and entrepreneurship training. A project list was issued with the OA notice. The university requires each project team to follow its proposed plan, strengthen project administration and ensure smooth implementation.

The Academic Affairs Office will track project progress and organize interim reviews and completion acceptance. The official OA project list governs the specific project titles and leader information.
