It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee issued a notice on September 17, 2012, that the 70th Introductory Party Knowledge Class is scheduled to open in October.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled opening | October 2012 |
| Eligible applicants | Applicants who submitted a Party membership application more than one year ago and have been designated as key training targets |
| Submission requirements | The *Application Form*, signed by the general Party branch or directly affiliated branch and stamped, must be submitted to the Party School Office (Room 315, New Administration Center) by September 28, 2012; the *Participant List Summary* should be sent to o_dxb@stu.edu.cn |
| Quotas | See OA attachments |
| Admission | Admission letters will be issued after review by the Party School Office |
| Issued by | Organization and United Front Work Department |

Quota allocation and forms are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ Quota and forms are available on the OA system.
