Shantou University’s Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office reported on September 17 that some student dormitories still had unpaid utility bills for August 2012 or earlier. Under university rules, dormitories with outstanding balances will **lose power from September 18** until all arrears have been paid.

## Debit and power restoration schedule

From September 18 to 20, the Network and Information Center will process two One-Card debits each day. Dormitories with successful payments will have power restored according to the following schedule:

| Debit time | Expected power restoration |
| --- | --- |
| 10:30 a.m. | 2:30–4:30 p.m. |
| 4:30 p.m. | 5:30–8 p.m. |
| Payment made after 4:30 p.m. | 2:30–4:30 p.m. the following day |

After September 20, only one debit will be processed each day, at 4:30 p.m. Students should top up their One-Card balances promptly. Those with insufficient bank-account balances should deposit enough funds during banking hours and complete the transfer to their cards.

Students who have lost their One-Cards may use the utility management system to set up “proxy payment.” Alternatively, they may deposit cash into the university’s account at the Bank of China STU Branch (account: `8030-15331308094001`) and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for recording. The Finance Office does not accept cash directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office)
