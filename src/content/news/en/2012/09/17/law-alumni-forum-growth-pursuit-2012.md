The 12th alumni forum of Shantou University Law School will be held from **7:30 to 9 p.m. on September 19** in Room 213 of the Law School. The forum, titled “Growth and Aspiration,” will feature Cai Zeyun, a 2006 law graduate, and is open to all faculty members and students.

Cai currently serves as manager of the Compliance Department at Industrial Securities (Hong Kong) Financial Holdings Limited. She previously worked in the Financial Services Department of Deacons in Hong Kong and also serves as deputy secretary-general of the China Children Safety Foundation. She will draw on her educational and professional experience to discuss personal growth and career aspirations.
