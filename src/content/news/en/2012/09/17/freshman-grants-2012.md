Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced its 2012 freshman grant arrangement on September 17. To ease the financial pressure faced by freshmen from financially disadvantaged families at the beginning of their university studies, the university will provide grants to **260** such freshmen, at **RMB 500 per student**.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | 2012 freshmen with financial difficulties |
| Number of recipients | 260 |
| Grant amount | RMB 500 per student |
| Announcement date | September 17, 2012 |

The list of recipients and related information are provided in the OA attachment. The Student Affairs Office encouraged recipients to make good use of the support and continue their studies diligently.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
