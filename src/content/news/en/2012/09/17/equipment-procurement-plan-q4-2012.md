Shantou University has opened applications for its fourth-quarter 2012 equipment procurement plan, according to the university’s Resource Management Office. Each unit must submit its written and electronic materials to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by **October 10, 2012**. Late submissions will be deferred to the first quarter of 2013.

## Application requirements

| Procurement situation | Required materials or approval |
| --- | --- |
| Total plan value of RMB 20,000 or less | Complete the Plan Form |
| Total plan value above RMB 20,000 | Complete the Plan Form and Plan Proposal, and obtain the required approvals under the university’s expenditure approval rules |
| Including equipment priced above RMB 100,000 per unit | Also complete the Feasibility Report for Large-Scale Equipment Procurement |
| Computer purchases required | Also complete the Computer Purchase Registration Form |
| Air-conditioner purchases required | State the installation location and condition of the existing air conditioner in the Plan Form’s remarks field |

Units should complete every field of the Plan Form, use the Government Centralized Procurement Catalogue to enter the relevant equipment code and name, and specify models, specifications, and technical parameters. Budgets should include taxes, transportation, warranty, and after-sales service costs; brands should generally not be specified.

Materials should be submitted to the Equipment and Materials Management Office (the former reception room). The office can be reached at `82902558`; the contact is Ms. Huang, and the email address is `zxhuang@stu.edu.cn`. The relevant forms and Government Centralized Procurement Catalogue are available in the OA attachments.
