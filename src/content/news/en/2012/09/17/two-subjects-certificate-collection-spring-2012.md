It is reported that, after review by the Guangdong Teacher Continuing Education Guidance Center, Shantou University has received completion certificates for its spring 2012 Education and Psychology training for non-teacher-training students seeking teacher qualification certification. Students who passed may collect their certificates at the Academic Affairs service window on the third floor of the New Administration Center.

## Collection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate | Completion certificate for Education and Psychology training for teacher qualification certification |
| Training semester | Spring 2012 |
| Eligible recipients | Non-teacher-training students who passed the training |
| Collection venue | Academic Affairs service window, third floor, New Administration Center |
| Identification | Recipients must show valid identification; representatives must show valid identification for both themselves and the certificate holder |

The Academic Affairs Office asks students who attended the training and passed to share the collection information with one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The list of students who passed is available on the OA system.
