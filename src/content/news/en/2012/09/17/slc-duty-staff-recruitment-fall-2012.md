Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) has opened recruitment for the new academic year and plans to hire **four to six** first-year undergraduate students from the main campus who have financial difficulties. Applicants should demonstrate a service-oriented attitude, responsibility, teamwork, planning and organizational ability, and basic computer skills.

## Position details

Duty staff will handle SLC reception and duty shifts, plan and organize activities, assist with center administration and service-quality monitoring, promote SLC services, and undertake temporary assignments. Working hours for the 2012–2013 academic year will be arranged through discussion. Compensation will follow the university’s work-study rate for student positions.

Eligible students must log in to the [Student Work-Study Application System](http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/) and submit a fully completed application form by **10 p.m. on Sunday, September 23**. If applications exceed the available positions, SLC will select some applicants for interviews. The interview list is expected to be posted on the internal office information network on Monday, September 24; applicants who are not selected will not receive separate notice.

SLC is located beside the Shantou University post office. For inquiries, contact `82901482` or `slc@stu.edu.cn`; its BBS ID is `SLC` and its website is <http://slc.stu.edu.cn>.
