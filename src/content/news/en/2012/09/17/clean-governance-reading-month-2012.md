Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Office has circulated arrangements from the Shantou Discipline Inspection Commission and the Municipal Bureau of Culture, Radio, Television, Press and Publication for the city’s second Integrity Reading Month. Faculty members and students are invited to participate in the reading program and its essay competition.

Participants in the prize essay competition for reflections on integrity-themed books should prepare their work according to the Implementation Plan for Shantou’s Second Integrity Reading Month. By **October 17**, they must email an electronic copy to the Discipline Inspection Office at `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`, using “Integrity Book Reflection Prize Essay” as the subject, and submit one A4-printed copy to the office in Room 422 of the New Administration Center.

For inquiries, call `82902346`. Detailed essay requirements are provided in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office)

> ⚠️ The implementation plan for Integrity Reading Month and other attachments are available on the OA system.
