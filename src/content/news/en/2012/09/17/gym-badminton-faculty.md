It is reported that the labor union of Shantou University will provide badminton courts for faculty and staff throughout the current semester, as part of efforts to enrich their cultural and sports life.

According to the arrangement, the sessions begin this week and will continue through week 18, taking place every Wednesday evening from 19:00 to 21:00 at the gymnasium badminton courts. All faculty and staff members are welcome to take part.

The opening of this regular court time offers busy staff members a fixed opportunity for exercise and social interaction, and stands as one of the union's standing initiatives to enrich campus employees' leisure life.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Labor Union)
