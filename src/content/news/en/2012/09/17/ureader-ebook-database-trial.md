It is reported that the Shantou University Library announced on September 17 the launch of trial access to the UReader foreign-language e-book database for all faculty and students, with the trial running until December 31, 2012.

The database brings together foreign-language e-books from nearly one hundred publishing organizations, including INGRAM LYBRARY.COM, OverDrive, US-based DynoMedia (a member of the US Digital Publishing Alliance), as well as McGraw-Hill, MIT Press, University of California Press, University of Michigan Press, Jones and Bartlett ebooks, and Cisco Press.

The library encourages faculty and students to make active use of the database. Feedback and comments on the trial may be sent to the Library's Academic Services Department (Tel: 3247, Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn or zhzeng@stu.edu.cn).
