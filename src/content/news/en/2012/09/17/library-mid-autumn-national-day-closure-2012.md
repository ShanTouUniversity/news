In accordance with Shantou University’s holiday arrangements issued by the Party and Government Office, the STU Library will be **closed from September 30 to October 3** and **resume regular opening hours on October 4**. Faculty members and students should plan borrowing and study activities accordingly.

| Dates | Opening arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| September 30–October 3 | Closed |
| From October 4 | Regular opening hours |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Library)
