Shantou University Business School has opened selection for students to represent the university in an international business case competition scheduled for the first half of **2013**. Training and candidate selection will take place in mid-to-late September 2012.

## Eligibility

The selection is open to full-time Business School undergraduates in the 2009, 2010, and 2011 cohorts, corresponding to the second through fourth years. Applicants must also meet these requirements:

- Have completed courses in economics, business administration, or related subjects;
- Demonstrate strong English listening, speaking, and reading skills;
- Have an average score of at least 80 in ELC Levels 1–5 courses taken.

Interested eligible students must register from **September 17 to 21** through the “International Case Competition Registration” quick link on the Business School website or directly through the [registration system](http://kingwu.stu.edu.cn/CaseCompetitions/Login.aspx). Shortlisted candidates will be notified for interviews, and the final team list will be published on the Business School website.

For inquiries, contact Ms. Wu at `kingkng@stu.edu.cn`. The competition calendar is included in the OA attachment.
