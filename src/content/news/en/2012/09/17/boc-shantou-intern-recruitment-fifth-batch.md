It is reported that Bank of China Shantou Branch is recruiting 20 fifth-batch reserve talent interns from full-time undergraduate and graduate students currently enrolled at Shantou University. Applicants should abide by the law, be proactive and responsible, have no record of misconduct, and be in good health with quick responses and a presentable appearance. Business School students will receive priority under otherwise equal conditions.

## Positions and Requirements

Internships will be arranged at the headquarters and subordinate institutions of Bank of China Shantou Branch. Positions include lobby manager and the organization and maintenance of non-confidential materials.

Lobby manager interns will receive and arrange services for customers with appointments, guide customers in using the intelligent queueing system, including remote ticketing and SMS reminders, and assist them with self-service equipment. They will also guide customers in the lobby, maintain order, answer inquiries, help customers complete forms, receive feedback, and promptly report complaints, emergencies and unusual conditions, including safety hazards, to the responsible supervisor.

Interns assigned to materials organization and maintenance will organize, enter and maintain relevant non-confidential banking information and materials as required by their host institution. All interns must keep banking information and materials confidential.

## Internship Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number of interns | 20 |
| Internship period | October 10 to December 31, 2012 |
| Working hours | Monday to Friday, 8:30–12:00 and 14:00–17:30 |
| Internship location | Bank of China Shantou Branch headquarters and subordinate institutions |
| Work agreement | Interns must sign an internship work agreement with Bank of China Shantou Branch |

Interns must comply with the branch's attendance, leave, labor discipline and confidentiality requirements and follow work arrangements and position duties. The branch's Human Resources Department will assign host institutions according to applicants' qualifications and the bank's staffing needs, and may adjust positions during the internship as required.

Interns must complete pre-service and on-the-job training and participate in training, routine and end-of-internship assessments. Pre-service training will be organized by the Human Resources Department, while host institutions will arrange on-the-job training and implement the assessments. If an assessment confirms that an intern does not meet internship requirements, is unsuitable for banking work or has violated the bank's rules, the Human Resources Department may terminate the internship and, where necessary, pursue corresponding responsibility. At the end of the internship, the host institution or department will inspect and hand over the work, and the Human Resources Department will record the completion of the internship.

## Benefits

Interns who perform well in assessments may enter Bank of China Shantou Branch's reserve talent pool for campus recruitment. Those who apply under the requirements of the bank's head office campus recruitment, pass the written examination and complete the relevant procedures may receive priority for formal employment under otherwise equal conditions. During the internship, interns will receive a subsidy of 40 yuan per day, with individual income tax withheld and paid by Bank of China Shantou Branch in accordance with regulations. Host institutions will provide lunch, and Bank of China Shantou Branch will purchase group commercial personal accident insurance for interns and cover the premiums.

## Application and Pre-service Training

Applicants should submit the *Bank of China Shantou Branch Reserve Talent Internship Applicant Information Form* to the Communist Youth League Committee of the Business School by 17:00 on September 27, 2012. Bank of China Shantou Branch and the Business School will determine the internship roster based on applications and notify selected applicants about pre-service training. Applicants who do not receive a notification should not attend the training.

Pre-service training will be held at 15:00 on October 8, 2012, in the Business School Seminar Room on the second floor of the Wenxi building. Before beginning the internship, interns must present the originals of their identity cards and student cards and submit one photocopy of each.

The internship will be carried out in accordance with the Internship Base Cooperation Agreement signed by Bank of China Shantou Branch and the Shantou University Business School. Submission contact details, the information form and other requirements are available in the original OA attachment.
