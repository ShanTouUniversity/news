It is reported that the STU Project 211 Office has published the *Handbook for the Internal Acceptance Meeting of Shantou University Project 211 Phase III Construction Projects*, finalized after comments were solicited. The handbook will guide the internal acceptance work for Phase III construction projects.

## Implementation Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Document | *Handbook for the Internal Acceptance Meeting of Shantou University Project 211 Phase III Construction Projects* |
| Issuing office | Project 211 Office |
| Applicable parties | Project 211 project teams and relevant units |
| Requirement | Complete assigned tasks according to the work plan and schedule in the handbook to support a successful acceptance meeting |

The specific tasks and schedule are subject to the original handbook.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Project 211 Office)
> ⚠️ The internal acceptance meeting handbook is available on the OA system.
