Shantou University’s Network and Information Center reported on September 17 that the university had prepaid RMB 100 to the One-Card accounts issued to 2012 undergraduate and graduate freshmen, allowing the cards to be used upon arrival. The Finance Office commissioned Bank of China to conduct the first batch debit on September 12, but the debit was unsuccessful for 128 freshmen.

## Second debit arrangement

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Affected students | 2012 undergraduate and graduate freshmen whose first debit failed |
| Prepayment | RMB 100 per student |
| First debit | September 12, conducted in batches by Bank of China for the Finance Office |
| Second debit | September 24, to be commissioned by the Finance Office |

The university asks the affected freshmen to deposit RMB 100 into their Bank of China accounts from now on so that the second debit can be completed successfully. The list of students whose debits failed is available in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
>
> ⚠️ The list of students with unsuccessful debits and other attachments are available on the OA system.
