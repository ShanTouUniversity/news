It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 17, 2012, to organize applications for the Xinqiaowei Grant.

The grant, established this year in Guangdong by Fujian Xinqiaowei Shoes & Apparel Co., Ltd., supports disadvantaged freshmen admitted to key universities.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Outstanding students from disadvantaged families among the 2012 freshmen |
| Places | 5 (30 province-wide) |
| Support | Full tuition for the duration of study plus 500 yuan per month for living expenses; tuition transferred before each academic year and living expenses monthly to the student’s account |
| Required materials | Application form and a copy of the STU Family Economic Situation Form |
| College recommendation | Each college (residential college) recommends 2 candidates |
| Student deadline | 17:00, September 20, 2012, to the college Youth League Committee |
| College submission deadline | 12:00, September 25, 2012, to the Student Aid Management Center (Administration Center, Room 106) |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

The application form is available in the OA attachment.
