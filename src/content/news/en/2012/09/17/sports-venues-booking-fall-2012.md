It is reported that, at the start of the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year, Shantou University sports venues will be available for booking by faculty and students after accommodating physical education classes and athletic-team training.

## Venue Schedule

| Venue | Fee | Opening hours |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Indoor badminton courts | RMB 10/hour | Monday–Friday 11:30–13:30; Saturday 7:30–8:30 and 11:30–15:30; Sunday 7:30–8:30 and 11:30–18:00 |
| Tennis courts | RMB 20/hour | Monday, Tuesday and Saturday 8:00–15:30; Wednesday–Friday 11:30–13:30; Sunday 8:00–18:00 |
| Fitness center | RMB 5/visit | Opens from the second week; Tuesday–Friday 18:00–22:00 and Sunday 15:00–17:00; closed for maintenance and cleaning on Monday and Saturday |

## Booking and Use Requirements

Tennis courts and indoor badminton courts must be reserved at least one day in advance by emailing o_tyb@stu.edu.cn. Weekend use must be booked before the end of working hours on Friday. Requests should state the venue needed, usage time, contact person and contact details.

Payment is accepted only by campus card or internal university transfer, and users must carry proof of payment. Faculty and students must follow venue-management rules and staff instructions during opening hours. Non-opening hours are reserved for physical education classes and athletic-team training. Sports venues will not open to external users during statutory public holidays.
