It is reported that the Shantou University Wind Ensemble is recruiting new members from across the university for the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year and will hold auditions on Wednesday, September 19, 2012. Applicants of all genders are welcome. Students without prior music training may also audition if they enjoy music and are willing to maintain a regular rehearsal commitment.

## Audition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Audition time | 14:00–20:00, Wednesday, September 19, 2012 |
| Venue | Second floor of the Arts Education Center building, beside Building 789 |
| Registration | On-site registration |
| Audition content | A piece selected by the applicant |
| Instruments | Flute, piccolo, clarinet, oboe, bassoon, saxophone, French horn, trumpet, trombone, euphonium, tuba and percussion |

Students who play other instruments but none of those listed are also welcome to audition. The Arts Education Center will provide instruments for the audition, although applicants may bring their own.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang College of Art and Design and Arts Education Center)
