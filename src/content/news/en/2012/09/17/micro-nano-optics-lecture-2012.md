It is reported that the School of Science will hold a Science Salon lecture on September 19, 2012, featuring Researcher Chen Linsen, Chairman of Suzhou SVG.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Micro-Nano Optics: Demand-Driven, Frontier Technologies and Industrial Applications |
| Content | Overview of current demand and trends in micro-nano fabrication, frontier micro-nano optics technologies (micro-nano patterning, flexible nanoimprinting), mechanisms of nanostructured interface engineering (structural color, light guiding and conductive properties, polarization), key issues in cross-scale micro-nano structure formation and efficient nano direct writing, platform technologies and solutions, and prospects in light of the 12th Five-Year Plan |
| Speaker | Researcher Chen Linsen, doctoral supervisor, Chairman of Suzhou SVG (SVG Optronics) |
| Time | 16:00, September 19, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |
