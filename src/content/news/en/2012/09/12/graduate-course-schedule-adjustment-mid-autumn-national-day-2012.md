According to Shantou University's holiday arrangements for the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day, the Graduate School has adjusted the academic schedule for graduate students on the main campus. Classes scheduled for Saturday, September 29, 2012, will follow the Friday, October 5 schedule, while classes on October 5 will follow the September 29 schedule.

The adjustment applies to all graduate training units on the main campus. Graduate students and their training units should make corresponding arrangements for classes and coursework.

## Schedule Adjustment

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Applicable group | Graduate students and graduate training units on the main campus |
| Saturday, September 29 | Exchange with the classes scheduled for Friday, October 5 |
| Friday, October 5 | Exchange with the classes scheduled for Saturday, September 29 |
| Issuing office | Graduate School, Shantou University |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
