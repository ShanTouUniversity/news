It is reported that Shantou University has completed the selection for its 2012 Outstanding Freshman Award Program, with the proposed list of recipients now open for public comment starting September 12.

In accordance with the Detailed Rules of the STU Outstanding Freshman Award Program, following student applications and interviews conducted by the selection committee, the university proposes the following awards:

| Award | Number | Representative Nominee |
|-------|--------|------------------------|
| Outstanding Freshman First Prize | 5 | Zhang Y, et al. |
| Outstanding Freshman Second Prize | 25 | Ouyang XG, et al. |
| Outstanding Out-of-Province Female Student Donor Scholarship | 26 | Wu ZH, et al. |

The Outstanding Out-of-Province Female Student awards, funded by corporate and individual donations, were determined in line with relevant regulations and this year's enrollment profile.

Members of the university community who wish to raise concerns may submit written comments to the Student Financial Aid Management Center of the Office of Student Affairs before 10:00 a.m. on Thursday, September 13. Submissions from units should bear an official seal, and individual submissions should include the submitter's real name.
