Shantou University has published a public notice concerning the seventh batch of university-level candidates for the Guangdong Higher Education “Qianbaishi Project.” The notice states that 16 teachers, including Cai Lingru, Cai Zemin, Chen Yindong, Fan Lisheng, Gao Qingrong, Jiang Dazhi, Li Pengtao, Song Xiaohong, Wei Chuliang, Wu Fupeng, Yu Chengjie, Yu Ying, Yuan Ye, Zhang Xiaoni, Zheng Muqiang, and Lin Quan, meet the relevant conditions.

The group includes applicants who had sought provincial-level selection but were not approved and were subsequently considered for university-level development. During the public-notice period, organizations or individuals with differing opinions may submit written feedback to the university's Human Resources Office by September 16, 2012.
