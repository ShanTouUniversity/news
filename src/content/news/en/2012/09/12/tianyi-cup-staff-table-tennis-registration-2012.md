The 2012 Shantou “Tianyi Cup” Amateur Table Tennis Tournament, organized by the Shantou Sports Bureau, will take place from October 18 to 23 at the Chaoshan Gymnasium Annex. Shantou University's Staff Union is inviting interested employees to register and will form a team based on the registration response.

Employees who wish to participate should bring a photocopy of their identity card to the STU Staff Union office. Registration closes on the morning of Monday, September 17. Inquiries may be directed to Teacher Ye at 82902983.
