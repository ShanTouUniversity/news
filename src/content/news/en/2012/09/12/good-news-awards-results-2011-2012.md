Shantou University's Publicity Department and News Center have announced the results of the 2011–2012 “Good News” awards. The selection received 75 entries in the news-text category and 44 entries in the photo-series category. Following anonymous evaluation by external experts, 28 awards were presented in total.

The news-text category received three first prizes, five second prizes, and 10 third prizes. The photo-series category received two first prizes, three second prizes, and five third prizes. The prize amounts were 300 yuan per first-prize entry, 200 yuan per second-prize entry, and 100 yuan per third-prize entry. The specific winning works and units are listed in the results attachment on the OA system.

Correspondents from the winning units were asked to collect certificates and prize money from the News Center office on September 14. The News Center can be reached at 8099 for inquiries.
