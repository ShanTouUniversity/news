According to Shantou University's holiday arrangements for the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day, the Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the undergraduate academic schedule. Classes scheduled for Saturday, September 29, 2012, will follow the Friday, October 5 schedule, while classes on October 5 will follow the September 29 schedule.

The adjustment applies to all undergraduate teaching units. Undergraduate students and their teaching units should make corresponding arrangements for classes and coursework.

## Schedule Adjustment

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Applicable group | Undergraduate students and teaching units at Shantou University |
| Saturday, September 29 | Exchange with the classes scheduled for Friday, October 5 |
| Friday, October 5 | Exchange with the classes scheduled for Saturday, September 29 |
| Issuing office | Academic Affairs Office, Shantou University |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
