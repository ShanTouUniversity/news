It is reported that, to improve students’ English ability and interest in professional English, Shantou University’s Law School will hold its 5th Legal English Speech Contest. The contest is open to all undergraduate and graduate students in the Law School. The preliminary interview will select the top 10 contestants for the final. Winners of the final may also have the opportunity to participate in the 5th National Legal English Competition.

## Contest Schedule

| Stage | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary interview | 3:00–5:30 p.m., Wednesday, September 26, 2012 | Wenbei 403, Law School |
| Final | 3:00–5:00 p.m., Wednesday, October 10, 2012 | To be announced |

Contestants must choose one of the following topics and deliver a read or memorized speech at the preliminary interview. Judges will assess pronunciation, expressive ability, and language organization, among other criteria:

- *Solving the “Minor Property” Problem in China*
- *The Role of Law in Economic Development*

Submissions must be 200–500 words. Contestants should complete the registration form and email the materials to cdclaw@stu.edu.cn by noon on September 20, 2012. Final-round requirements will be announced separately.
