Shantou University's Department of Physics in the College of Science has announced the schedule for this semester's General Physics Experiment course. The first week will be devoted to error theory, and laboratory work will begin in the second week. Students should attend the session corresponding to their laboratory section in Room D504.

Students enrolled in the Monday General Physics Experiment section will attend the error theory class during the evening ABC periods on Monday, September 17. Students enrolled in the Tuesday section will attend during the evening ABC periods on Wednesday, September 19. Students taking the course should share the information and attend on time.
