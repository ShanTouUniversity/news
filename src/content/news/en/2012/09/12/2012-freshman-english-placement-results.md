Shantou University's English Language Centre (ELC) has released the English placement results for the university's 2012 main-campus undergraduate freshmen following a comprehensive assessment. The specific placement results are listed in an attachment on the OA system.

The 2012 main-campus undergraduate freshmen should select the appropriate courses and purchase the corresponding level of textbooks according to their placement results. Colleges should remind freshmen to check their individual levels and complete the subsequent academic arrangements.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicable group | 2012 undergraduate freshmen on the main campus |
| Assessing office | English Language Centre (ELC), Shantou University |
| Results | English placement levels for freshmen |
| Follow-up | Select courses and purchase textbooks for the assigned level |

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Centre)
>
> ⚠️ Individual placement results and related attachments are available on the OA system.
