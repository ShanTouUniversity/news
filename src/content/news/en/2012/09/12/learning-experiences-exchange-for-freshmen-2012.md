Shantou University's Student Affairs Office will hold an exchange event themed “Different Learning Experiences” from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Thursday, September 13, in the Library Lecture Hall. The event is intended for members of the 2012 freshman class and will feature four student representatives sharing their university learning experiences.

The speakers include two recipients of the 2011 Sea Academy Scholarship, one 2011 Waseda University exchange student, and one recipient of a 2012 Chinese University of Hong Kong summer-course scholarship. The Student Affairs Office welcomes 2012 freshmen to attend.
