Shantou University's Student Affairs Office and Alumni Affairs Office have announced the recipients of the 2012 Alumni Grant for freshmen. Established through donations from supportive alumni, the grant assists students facing financial difficulty with their study and living costs. Fifteen freshmen will receive support this year.

Following recommendations from colleges and residential colleges and a review by the Student Affairs Office and Alumni Affairs Office, each recipient will receive 2,500 yuan. The full recipient list is provided in an attachment on the OA system.

The university encouraged the recipients to continue working hard, develop their abilities, and respond through their achievements to the care and support of alumni and the wider community.
