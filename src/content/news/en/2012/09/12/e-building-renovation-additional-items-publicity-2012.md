Shantou University's Capital Construction Office under the Resources Management Office has published a notice on proposed additional items for the renovation of Teaching Building E. The project began on August 1, 2012. During construction, the office found that the existing power-line diameter for the seminar-room air-conditioning units was insufficient for the load of the new units. The proposed work therefore includes laying approximately 900 meters of new power conduit from the floor electrical rooms to the seminar rooms and adding air circuit breakers.

Because the whiteboard areas also lacked sufficient illumination, eight light panels and related wiring are proposed at the request of the user unit. The preliminary estimate for the additional items is approximately 18,000 yuan, keeping the cumulative increase below the 10 percent limit set by the university.

Organizations or individuals with differing opinions may submit written feedback to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Capital Construction Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 14, 2012.
