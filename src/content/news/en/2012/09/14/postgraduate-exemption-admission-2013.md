It is reported that the STU Graduate School has published its 2013 rules for accepting outstanding graduating undergraduates recommended by their institutions for admission to master's programs without taking the entrance examination. Applicants must have recommendation-based exemption eligibility from their current institutions, complete procedures and supporting materials, good conduct, good health and no disciplinary record while enrolled.

All STU disciplines and fields except the Master of Business Administration and Master of Public Administration may accept exempted candidates. Academic degree programs may accept only candidates recommended for academic degree programs. When other conditions are similar, priority will be given to applicants who were principal contributors to relevant provincial-level or higher academic competition awards, or who independently authored or were the first or second author of relevant academic papers published in domestic or overseas publications.

## Awards and Support

| Applicant group | Award or support |
| --- | --- |
| Non-medical exempted candidates | A first-year scholarship covering tuition plus a RMB 5,000 per student per academic year award |
| Non-medical exempted candidates | A transport subsidy based on travel distance |
| Non-medical candidates from “985” universities | Eligible to apply for the STU Main Campus Outstanding New Master's Student Scholarship, first prize: RMB 8,000 per person |
| Non-medical candidates from “211” universities | Eligible to apply for the same scholarship, second prize: RMB 5,000 per person |

## Application Arrangements

Applicants should first visit the [STU Graduate Admissions website](http://www.gs.stu.edu.cn/yjszs/) to consult the *2013 STU Master's Program Catalogue*, select a receiving school and field, and download and complete the application form. The applicant must sign it, and the recommending institution's academic affairs office must affix its official seal.

| Applicant category | Submission method and deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Non-medical programs | Send scanned copies of the application form, proof of exemption eligibility and recommendation type, transcripts for all years, and CET-4/CET-6 results to o_yjsc@stu.edu.cn by October 12, 2012. Use “Name–Exemption Application” as the subject and call 0754-82902424 to confirm receipt |
| Medical programs | Mail one copy of each item above by October 12, 2012 to the Graduate Affairs Section of Shantou University Medical College, 22 Xinling Road, Shantou, Guangdong, postal code 515041; recipient: Cao Jun |

Copies of awards, representative academic papers, publications or original research work are optional supporting materials for review; originals will be checked during the second-round examination. Late applications will not be accepted.

## Subsequent Procedures

STU will conduct a preliminary review of application materials and notify eligible applicants by October 17, 2012, about the second-round examination and medical examination. The Graduate School website will publish the examination list and schedule. The proposed admission list will be posted on the website for seven days, after which acceptance letters will be issued if no objections are received.

Proposed candidates must also complete online registration, on-site confirmation and other formal procedures required by the Ministry of Education. They must complete the *Registration Form for National Recommended Exempted Master's Candidates (Direct PhD Candidates)* and mail it by November 10, 2012 to the STU Graduate School, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong, postal code 515063; recipient: Li Moyan. Candidates who fail to complete formal registration or send the form on time will not be admitted. Proposed exempted candidates may not register for the unified entrance examination; doing so will cancel their exemption eligibility. Eligibility will also be cancelled if a candidate has not obtained a bachelor's degree or undergraduate diploma before enrollment, or has received disciplinary action.

The Graduate School can be reached at 0754-82902424 and o_yjsc@stu.edu.cn. The Medical College Graduate Affairs Section can be reached at 0754-88900206 and o_yjshb@stu.edu.cn. Applicants must ensure that their exemption eligibility and all application materials are truthful and accurate.
