It is reported that the STU Project 211 Office will hold a meeting at 16:00 on Monday, September 17, 2012, in Room 213 of the New Administration Center to discuss and arrange the internal acceptance of Project 211 Phase III construction projects.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:00, Monday, September 17, 2012 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administration Center |
| Topic | Planning and arrangements for internal acceptance of Project 211 Phase III construction projects |
| Participants | Leaders and secretaries of key-discipline construction projects, plus relevant personnel from the main campus and Medical College |

The notice asks all relevant participants to attend on time. The named participants from the main campus and Medical College are Huang Dongyang, He Wenbiao, Chen Shaogang, Zou Zhibo, Hu Zhong, Yao Mingan, Huo Xia, Gao Chuangbin, Wu Jianhui, Fang Ye, Liu Jinhua, Qiu Jingfeng, Zeng Jianping and Weng Luyin.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Project 211 Office)
