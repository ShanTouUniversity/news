It is reported that the Shantou University Dance Troupe is recruiting new members from across the university for the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. Students of any gender who enjoy dance are welcome to apply, including those without formal dance training.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Shantou University Dance Troupe |
| Applicants | Students across the university |
| Semester | Fall semester, 2012–2013 academic year |
| Eligibility | Interest in dance; all genders welcome, and no prior dance training is required |

Specific application arrangements and requirements are provided in the attachments to the original notice.
