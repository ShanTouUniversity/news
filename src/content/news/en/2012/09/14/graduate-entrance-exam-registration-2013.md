It is reported that registration for the 2013 National Graduate Entrance Examination consists of online registration and on-site confirmation. The STU Graduate School reminds graduating undergraduates and candidates recommended for admission without examination who plan to take part in the examination to complete registration as required, provide accurate personal information and truthfully report any penalties received during national education examinations.

## Registration Schedule

| Stage | Time and notes |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-registration for graduating undergraduates | September 28–29, 2012, 9:00–22:00 daily; valid pre-registration information does not need to be submitted again |
| Formal online registration | October 10–31, 2012, 9:00–22:00 daily; late registration and subsequent changes to registration information are not permitted |
| On-site confirmation | November 10–14, 2012; late confirmation will not be accepted |

Online registration is available through the public website of the [China Graduate Admissions Information Network](http://yz.chsi.com.cn) or its education website at [yz.chsi.cn](http://yz.chsi.cn). Applicants must read and follow the online announcements issued by the Ministry of Education, the provincial education examination authority where they are located, the selected examination site and the admitting institution.

## Examination Sites and Reminders

Graduating undergraduates at Shantou University should in principle select the nearby 4407 Shantou Admissions Office as their examination site. Candidates recommended for admission without examination should select an examination site according to the online-registration announcement of the Guangdong Provincial Education Examination Authority. Applicants are responsible for any inability to take the examination or gain admission caused by failure to meet registration requirements, inaccurate information or false statements. False applications will be handled under the relevant national examination and graduate-admission regulations.

Applicants may consult the [STU 2013 Master's Admissions Prospectus](http://www.gs.stu.edu.cn/yjszs/master_zsjz.htm) for further admissions information.
