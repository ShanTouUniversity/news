It is reported that applications for the 2012 Shantou Science and Technology Plan have opened. Organized by the Shantou Science and Technology Bureau, the plan covers research, development and industrialization in agriculture, high-tech industry and social development, as well as special plans for industry–university–research cooperation and small and medium-sized enterprise innovation funds. Agricultural projects are divided into key and guiding projects.

## Eligible Plans

The accepted categories include the Torch Plan, Industrial Research Plan, Spark Plan, Agricultural Research Plan, Social Development Research Plan, Soft Science Research Plan, Industry–University–Research Cooperation Plan, SME Innovation Fund Plan and the special plan for cultivating high-tech enterprises for listing. The industrial categories are the Torch and Industrial Research plans; agricultural categories are the Spark and Agricultural Research plans; and social development and medical research projects are accepted under the Social Development Research Plan.

## Application Process

| Stage | Requirement and deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Prepare application | Download and complete the Shantou Science and Technology Plan Project Application (Task) Form; prepare a cooperation agreement for collaborative projects |
| Formal review | Send the application and, if applicable, cooperation agreement to cszhu@stu.edu.cn by September 27 for review by the Research Office |
| Online submission | After passing review, use the account assigned by the Research Office to submit the application and materials through the [Shantou Science and Technology Plan Project Application System](http://sbsys.shantou.net.cn/) by September 29 |
| Paper submission | After online approval, download and print two copies of the PDF application generated by the system and submit them with other attachments to the Research Office by October 10 |

Key medical and health projects must be submitted online; general medical and health projects do not require online submission. The participating colleges are the project-hosting units, and Shantou University is the recommending unit.

Researchers who lead more than two ongoing municipal science and technology projects may not apply for a new project. Each applicant may submit only one new project in the year. Applicants whose provincial or municipal projects from the past three years failed to complete acceptance or appraisal on schedule are also ineligible.

Research Office contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 82902813.
