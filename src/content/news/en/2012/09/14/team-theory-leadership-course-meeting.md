It is reported that Zhicheng College will hold a meeting for all students enrolled in *Team Theory and Leadership Practice* at 17:00 on Tuesday, September 18, 2012, in Lecture Room J-354 at Zhicheng College. The two-credit general education course is designed around Shantou University\'s general education goals and aims to develop students\' team spirit and leadership abilities.

## Meeting and Course Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | All students enrolled in the course |
| Time | 17:00, Tuesday, September 18, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Room J-354, Zhicheng College |
| Credits | Two |
| Leave requests | liumq@stu.edu.cn |

Students unable to attend for special reasons should complete the attached student information form in advance and send it to the designated email address. Students who neither attend nor request leave will be considered to have withdrawn from the course.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
> ⚠️ The enrolled-student list and student information form are available on the OA system.
