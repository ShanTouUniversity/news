It is reported that Shantou University has launched its internal recommendation process for the 13th selection of Shantou outstanding professionals, following the Shantou Municipal Party Committee Organization Department\'s notice (Shantou Organization Circular [2012] No. 54). University units are asked to mobilize eligible applicants and complete recommendations in accordance with the required procedures.

## Recommendation Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Submission deadline | September 26, 2012 |
| Receiving office | Party Organization and United Front Work Department |
| Application form | Five copies of the Shantou Outstanding Professionals Application Form; the “opinion of employing unit” section need not be completed or stamped |
| Achievement statement | No more than 800 Chinese characters, prepared by the employing unit and covering political conduct, work and research, and honors |
| Supporting materials | Copies of diplomas, degrees, professional qualifications, awards and honors, plus evidence of major research achievements |
| Electronic submission | o_zzb@stu.edu.cn |
| Contact | Ji Rongxin, 82902345 |

Recommendation materials must use the prescribed format and be printed double-sided on A4 paper; copies must also be on A4 paper. After reviewing the copies, each unit must have the verifier sign them and affix the official seal. Late submissions will not be accepted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ The selection notice, application form and other recommendation templates are available on the OA system.
