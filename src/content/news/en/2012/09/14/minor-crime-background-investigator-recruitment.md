It is reported that Shantou University's Legal Aid Research Center is recruiting students in their second year or above majoring in law to serve as investigators for a juvenile crime background survey project. Investigators will undertake background surveys commissioned by the Longhu District People's Court, prepare investigation reports and read them in court, while also taking part in the center's duty shifts, legal consultations and public-service activities.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Law students in their second year or above |
| Application period | September 15–20, 2012 |
| Selection | Written test and interview; candidates will be selected on merit, with the schedule to be announced separately |
| Application materials | Download and complete the attached application form |
| Submission email | laclaw@stu.edu.cn |
| Email subject | Year + name + investigator application |
| Enquiries | 0754-82903457 |

Applicants should submit the application form as required. The form and related attachments are available through the original notice.
