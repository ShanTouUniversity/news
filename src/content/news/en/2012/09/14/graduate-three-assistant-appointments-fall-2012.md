It is reported that Shantou University\'s Graduate School released the appointment list for graduate “three assistant” positions for the fall 2012 semester on September 14, 2012. The list was submitted by units that established the positions and reviewed by the Graduate School.

The university requires each host unit to handle the appointment, day-to-day administration and assessment of graduate students taking up the positions in accordance with relevant regulations.

## Position Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Semester | Fall 2012 |
| Appointments | Graduate “three assistant” positions |
| Review process | Submission by host units and review by the Graduate School |
| Follow-up responsibilities | Host units handle appointments, administration and assessments |

The specific appointment list is provided in the attachment to the original notice.
