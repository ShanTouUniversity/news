It is reported that the STU Graduate School completed its assessment of the Outstanding New Master's Student Scholarship for the 2012 cohort and announced the results on September 14, 2012. The assessment followed the *STU Measures for Assessing Outstanding New Master's Student Scholarships* and consisted of applications from graduate students, recommendations by graduate training units and review by the Graduate School.

## Results

| Award | Recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 12 students |
| Second prize | 54 students |
| Total | 66 students |

The specific recipient list is provided in the attachment to the original notice issued by the Graduate School.
