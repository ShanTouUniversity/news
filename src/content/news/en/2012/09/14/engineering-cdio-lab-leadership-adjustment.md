It is reported that the Engineering School of Shantou University adjusted the leadership appointments of its CDIO Innovation Practice Center and Computer Science laboratory on September 14, 2012, following discussion and approval by the school affairs committee in response to development needs.

## Appointment Changes

| Person | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Sun Mei | Appointed director of the Engineering School CDIO Innovation Practice Center |
| Fang Ruoyu | Appointed director of the Computer Science laboratory |
| Bao Nengsheng | No longer serves as director (concurrent) of the CDIO Innovation Center |
| Lin Yiwen | No longer serves as deputy director of the CDIO Innovation Practice Center |
| Zhang Kaidong | No longer serves as director of the Computer Science laboratory |

The appointment changes were issued by the Engineering School for implementation among all faculty and students.
