It is reported that the second-half 2012 National Computer Rank Examination will be held on September 22. To test the examination software and system environment, Shantou University will conduct mock tests on September 18 in Room E102.

## Test Schedule

| Examination format | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traditional examination | September 18, 13:00–14:00 | Room E102 |
| Paperless examination | September 18, 14:00–15:00 | Room E102 |

Students registered for the examination should attend the mock test corresponding to their examination format on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
