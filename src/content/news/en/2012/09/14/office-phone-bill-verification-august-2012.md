It is reported that China Telecom\'s Shantou branch has sent Shantou University units\' electronic bills for office telephone charges incurred from August 1 through 31, 2012, to the university telephone office. The itemized data has been uploaded to the Telephone Management System for designated reviewers to check.

## Verification and Deduction Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Billing period | August 1–31, 2012 |
| Verification system | [Telephone Management System](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) |
| Discrepancy deadline | Send to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn by September 21, 2012 |
| Unified deduction date | September 26, 2012 |
| Deduction basis | Funding account numbers provided by each unit |

Bills without discrepancies should be confirmed in the system by selecting “Confirm verified bills.” Bills for which no discrepancy is reported and no confirmation is made by the end of the verification period will be deemed confirmed by the system. Questions or suggestions about the system may also be sent to the same email address.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
