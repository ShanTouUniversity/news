It is reported that Shantou University will adjust the swimming pool opening schedule from Monday, September 17, 2012, to accommodate swimming classes in the new semester.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Effective date | September 17, 2012 |
| Opening hours | 17:45–19:15 |
| Issuing unit | Physical Education Department |

Students and staff should take note of the new opening hours when visiting the pool.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
