It is reported that Shantou University will hold its campus selection for the 2012 FLTRP Cup English Speech Contest from 14:00 to 17:00 on September 26, 2012, at the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL). The selection will choose two contestants to represent STU in the Guangdong regional semifinal in mid-to-late October. Regional winners may advance to the national semifinal and final in December.

In-service postgraduate students, contestants who have reached the final of a previous FLTRP Cup or CCTV Cup National English Speech Contest, and contestants who have won an overseas-study award in the FLTRP Cup National English Debate Contest are not eligible to compete.

## Contest and Scoring

Contestants will compete in groups of three. Fifteen minutes before appearing, the three contestants in each group will draw confidential topics simultaneously and prepare separately. They will then deliver two-minute impromptu speeches in draw order. After all three speeches, each contestant will answer one judge’s question for one minute in speech order. They will then give one-minute extension speeches in reverse order.

The judges will remove the highest and lowest scores and average the remaining scores. The total is 100 points: impromptu speech, 60% (content 20%, organization and effectiveness 20%, delivery 20%); response to questions, 20% (content 10%, organization and effectiveness 5%, delivery 5%); and extension speech, 20% (content 10%, organization and effectiveness 5%, delivery 5%).

Volunteers will assist with venue setup, timekeeping and scorekeeping. Enquiries may be directed to Rui Ma (Ray, ruima@stu.edu.cn) or Paul (pauld@stu.edu.cn).
