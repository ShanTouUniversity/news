It is reported that seven Shantou University faculty members have received 2012 honors as Guangdong Nan Yue Outstanding Teachers, Shantou Outstanding Teachers or a Shantou Outstanding Education Worker.

Under the Decision on Commending Guangdong 2012 Nan Yue Outstanding Teachers and Nan Yue Outstanding Education Workers (Yue Jiaogongwei [2012] No. 4), issued by the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee Education Working Committee, the Guangdong Department of Education, the Guangdong Department of Human Resources and Social Security, and the Guangdong Federation of Trade Unions, Bao Nengsheng, Chen Jian, Han Ran and Huang Xiaochun from the main campus received the title of Guangdong Nan Yue Outstanding Teacher.

Under the Decision on Commending Outstanding Principals, Outstanding Teachers and Outstanding Education Workers in Shantou (Shanfa [2012] No. 11), issued by the Shantou Municipal Party Committee and the Shantou Municipal People\'s Government, Du Hong and Lin Peng received the title of Shantou Outstanding Teacher, while Li Xiangxin received the title of Shantou Outstanding Education Worker.
