It is reported that Shantou University’s Law School judicial examination training has entered its final preparation stage. On September 6, 2012, the Career Development Center will invite Gao Huiyun, a judicial examination expert from Wanguo Judicial Examination Training, to provide in-person pre-examination coaching for participating students.

## Coaching Details

The coaching will take place at the Moot Court in two sessions: 8:30 a.m. to noon and 2:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. The session will focus on answering techniques for Paper IV of the judicial examination.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 6, 2012, 8:30 a.m. to noon and 2:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Moot Court |
| Speaker | Gao Huiyun, judicial examination expert from Wanguo Judicial Examination Training |
| Focus | Answering techniques for Paper IV |

The coaching is intended for students participating in the Law School’s judicial examination training. Participants may attend at the Moot Court during the sessions above. Related attachments are available on the OA system.
