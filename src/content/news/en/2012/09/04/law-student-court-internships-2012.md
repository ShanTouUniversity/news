It is reported that Shantou University’s Law School plans to arrange teaching internships for students in the 2009 law cohort in mid-October 2012. Four courts have confirmed that they will accept interns: the Dongguan Intermediate People’s Court, Dongguan No. 1 People’s Court, Dongguan No. 2 People’s Court, and Huizhou Intermediate People’s Court. The internship hosts will provide free accommodation.

## Internship Arrangements

The Dongguan Intermediate People’s Court and Dongguan No. 1 People’s Court will host interns for two months each. The Dongguan No. 2 People’s Court will host interns for two and a half months. The specific internship duration at the Huizhou Intermediate People’s Court will be confirmed in subsequent university arrangements.

| Host court | Known arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Dongguan Intermediate People’s Court | Two-month internship; free accommodation |
| Dongguan No. 1 People’s Court | Two-month internship; free accommodation |
| Dongguan No. 2 People’s Court | Two-and-a-half-month internship; free accommodation |
| Huizhou Intermediate People’s Court | Accepts interns; free accommodation |

Interested students must complete the application form, identify their preferred court, and email the materials to `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` by September 16. The Career Development Center will determine interview arrangements based on the number of applications. The application form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
