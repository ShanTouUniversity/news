Shantou University’s Network and Information Center has opened the annual renewal process for faculty, staff and graduate student internet accounts. The renewal period covers 12 months, from October 2012 through September 2013; graduate students expected to graduate the following year should subscribe only through their graduation month.

## Renewal Arrangements

Units and schools should select packages according to the internet needs of faculty, staff and graduate students, then compile renewal lists, package types and funding-account indicators. Schools will compile renewal requests for graduate student accounts. A monthly package cannot be changed once it takes effect, and package fees will be transferred by the Finance Office after the funding-account indicator is supplied.

Units and schools should first email the subscription form to `cxfang@stu.edu.cn` for review. After receiving confirmation by email from the Network and Information Center, they should print the renewal form for signature by the funding officer and affix the official unit or school seal. By September 24, 2012, one original and one photocopy of the stamped subscription form must be submitted to the One-Card Service Window on the first floor of the Network and Information Center.

The center asks each unit to verify and update personnel names and amounts, and to complete the funding-account indicator for every account. The internet package subscription form is available on the OA system.
