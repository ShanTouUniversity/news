At the start of the new academic year, the Shantou University SIFE team opened recruitment for new members among students across the university, with no restrictions on major or year of study. Guided by a commitment to serving communities, the team has carried out business projects to help disadvantaged groups develop their capabilities and won first prize nationally at the China round of the 2011 SIFE World Cup.

## Recruitment Activities

The recruitment process consists of a roadshow, a themed information session and interviews:

| Activity | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruitment roadshow | September 6, 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. | Golden Triangle and No. 4 Dining Hall |
| Themed information session, “Develop Business Leaders and Create Social Value” | September 8, 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. | Science and Technology Report Hall |
| Recruitment interviews | September 10 to 11, 7:00 to 11:00 p.m. | Academic Seminar Room, second floor of the Business School |

Interested students may complete the application form and email their materials to `stusife@stu.edu.cn` by midnight on September 10. Forms may also be collected at the roadshow or information session, or completed at the interview venue. The application form and recruitment guide are available on the OA system.
