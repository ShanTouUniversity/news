It is reported that the Law School issued a notice on September 11, 2012, that three institutions intend to recruit a number of interns from the 2009 law program in mid-October.

## Openings

| Host | Duration | Number | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zhongshan People’s Procuratorate | 2 months | 2–3 | Submit resume, arrange accommodation independently |
| Shantou Justice Bureau | 2 months | 2 | — |
| Shantou Legislative Affairs Office | 2 months | 2 | — |

Interested students should email cdclaw@stu.edu.cn by September 18, with the subject line “Class + Name + Host Organization.”
