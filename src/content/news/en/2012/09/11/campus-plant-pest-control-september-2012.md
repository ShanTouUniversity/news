It is reported that, to improve campus sanitation and strengthen plant pest control, Shantou University’s Resources Management Office will carry out zone-by-zone spraying on greenery and fruit trees from September 15 through 21, 2012, across campus, including Longquan Garden and the Continuing Education College. Faculty, students, and staff are advised to take care around the work and must not pick or eat campus fruit.

## Zone Schedule

| Date | Work areas |
| --- | --- |
| September 15 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, and student dormitories |
| September 16 | Affiliated Middle School and Primary School through the Mental Health Center |
| September 17 | Reservoir dam, Buildings 7, 8, and 9, and the A–B faculty and staff housing area |
| September 18 | Medical Building, New Teaching Building, G/H/I/J student dormitories, areas around the Security Team office, main gate, campus roads, and outside the New Library |
| September 19 | Around the dining hall and Administration Building |
| September 20 | Artificial Lake area and sports field |
| September 21 | Longquan Garden and Continuing Education College |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
