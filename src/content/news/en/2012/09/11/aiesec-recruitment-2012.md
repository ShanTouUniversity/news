It is reported that the Shantou University AIESEC team has launched a campus recruitment drive open to all STU students, regardless of major or year. AIESEC is a global, non-political, independent, and not-for-profit organization operated by university students and recent graduates. It has more than 60,000 members across more than 1,000 universities in 110 countries and regions on five continents, with its global headquarters in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. The STU chapter was founded on June 18, 2012, with support from the Business School and attention and development support from AIESEC Mainland China.

## Recruitment Events

| Event | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-site consultation | 11:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m., September 11, 2012 | JinSanJiao |
| Recruitment information session | 2:30–4:30 p.m., September 12, 2012 | Science Report Hall |
| Recruitment interviews | Time and venue will be sent by text message after résumé screening | To be announced |

The recruitment process has three stages: résumé screening, group interviews, and individual interviews. Applicants should email their résumés to stuaiesec2012@gmail.com by September 17, 2012.

According to the team, joining AIESEC offers opportunities to take part in international volunteer service, overseas internships, and international exchange, while developing global networks and youth leadership skills.
