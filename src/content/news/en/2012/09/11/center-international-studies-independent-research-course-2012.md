It is reported that the Center for International Studies at Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts will continue offering the one-credit RSP0900 Independent Study course in the fall semester of 2012. The course is open to undergraduates from all majors in the 2009, 2010, and 2011 cohorts. Students may choose from history, philosophy, political science, anthropology, film studies, American studies, and cultural studies. The supervision language may be Chinese or English, as agreed by the supervisor and student.

## Course Plan

During Weeks 1–2 of the fall semester, students should contact a supervisor in their field of interest and develop an independent project. Supervisors will determine the list of students they guide through interviews or meetings and confirm it through the credit-system platform. Students will carry out their projects under supervision in Weeks 3–11, then submit a report or paper in Week 12 and complete a comprehensive assessment. Students who pass the assessment will receive one credit.
