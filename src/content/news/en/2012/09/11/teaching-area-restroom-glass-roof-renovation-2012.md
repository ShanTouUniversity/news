It is reported that, following the required approvals, Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has begun renovation of the model public restroom in the teaching area and the glass roofs over public stairwells. Work started on September 11, 2012, and is scheduled for completion by November 20. Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. and Shantou Longhua Construction General Company have been commissioned to carry out the respective works.

## Project Information

| Work | Contractor | Contract amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Renovation of the model public restroom in the teaching area and the restroom at Stairwell 4 | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 297,100 |
| Complete removal and reconstruction of glass roofs over Stairwells 1–4 and 6–8 | Shantou Longhua Construction General Company | RMB 351,700 |

The relevant work areas will be closed separately during construction, which may cause noise, vibration, and dust. The university reminds pedestrians to take care and prohibits entry to the construction sites without permission from the university’s functional departments and the contractors. People are also advised to avoid lingering near the sites.
