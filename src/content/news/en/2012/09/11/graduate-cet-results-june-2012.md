It is reported that results from the June 2012 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 have been posted in Shantou University’s Graduate Management System. Relevant graduate students may log in to check their results.

## How to Check Results

Visit the Graduate School homepage and select “Graduate Management System.” In the pop-up window, select “Unified Authentication,” enter the account and password, and then open “Exam Registration” followed by “Results Query” to view the results.
