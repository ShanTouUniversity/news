It is reported that Shantou University has revised six regulations to further standardize research management. The revisions were reviewed by the STU Research Committee and Academic Committee and approved for discussion at the ninth university leadership meeting of 2012 on September 4. The university published and implemented the revised regulations on September 11, requiring units on the main campus to follow them.

## Revised Regulations

1. *Management Measures for Shantou University Academic Innovation Team Development and National Fund Cultivation Projects*
2. *Management Measures for the Shantou University Young Research Fund*
3. *Management Measures for the Shantou University Humanities Research Fund*
4. *Management Measures for the Shantou University Academic Works Publication Support Fund*
5. *Management Measures for the Shantou University Academic Exchange Support Fund*
6. *Management Measures for Shantou University Intellectual Property*

The revised regulations and related documents were issued as attachments to the notice. The attachment texts provide the detailed management requirements.
