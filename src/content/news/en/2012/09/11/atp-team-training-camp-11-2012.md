It is reported that Zhicheng College will hold the 11th ATP (Action-Teamwork-Passion) Team Development Training Camp from the evening of September 14 to 16, 2012, for all freshmen of the class of 2012 and relevant volunteers.

The camp aims to help freshmen integrate into university life, plan their four-year studies and enhance comprehensive competencies, fostering social responsibility, confidence, independence, communication, collaboration and leadership.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 11th ATP Team Development Training Camp |
| Dates | September 14–16, 2012 |
| Participants | All freshmen of the class of 2012 at Zhicheng College (required for residential students) and volunteers |
| Credit | Listed under general education management; 1 credit upon completing coursework and assessment |
| Organizer | Zhicheng College |

The camp is specifically designed for the class of 2012. Freshmen are asked to attend the opening ceremony and coursework as scheduled, and volunteers are asked to prepare accordingly.
