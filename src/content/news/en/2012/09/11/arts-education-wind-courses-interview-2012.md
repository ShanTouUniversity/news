It is reported that Shantou University’s Arts Education Center will offer *Trumpet Performance*, *French Horn Performance*, and *Saxophone Performance* in the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. Students interested in enrolling must attend an audition. All three courses will be taught by Xiao Hui and carry 2.0 credits each.

## Course Schedule

| Class number | Course | Weeks and periods | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 57294 (AED1041) | Trumpet Performance | Weeks 1–8; Monday and Friday, periods 3–4 | Music Classroom 1, STU201 |
| 57295 (AED1042) | French Horn Performance | Weeks 1–8; Monday and Friday, periods 6–7 | Music Classroom 4, STU204 |
| 57298 (AED1055) | Saxophone Performance | Weeks 1–16; Thursday, periods 6–7 | Music Classroom 4, STU204 |

The audition will take place from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on Friday, September 14, 2012, in Rehearsal Hall STU 213 at the Arts Education Center. No prior instrumental training is required. The audition will assess pitch, rhythm, and memory.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Arts Education Center)
