It is reported that Shantou University has released the August utility charges for faculty and staff. The latest meter readings were taken on September 1 and 2, following the previous readings on August 1. Relevant staff members may log in to the STU Utility Management System at http://power.stu.edu.cn using their campus-network accounts to check the charges.

Questions or objections may be taken to the Utility Center for verification from September 11 through 14. The center can be reached at 2489. After the verification period, the Finance Office will deduct charges without objection from individual September 2012 salary accounts. People without salary accounts must pay directly at the Finance Office.
