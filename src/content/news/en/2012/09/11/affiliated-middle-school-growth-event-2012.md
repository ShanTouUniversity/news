It is reported that the Affiliated Middle School of Shantou University will host a “Growth” themed event on the afternoon of September 12, 2012. The event is organized by members of China Education Action, a group made up of graduates of leading universities in China and the United States, and is open to parents of students at the affiliated middle school.

## Event Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Growth |
| Time | 3:30–5:10 p.m., September 12, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Members of China Education Action |
| Participants | Parents of STU Affiliated Middle School students |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office, STU Affiliated Middle School)
