It is reported that two fall-semester classes at Shantou University’s Arts Education Center will be suspended on Monday, September 17, 2012, because instructor Liu Dawei will be on leave. Make-up classes will be scheduled at a later date. The Academic Affairs Office asks students enrolled in the courses to inform one another and watch for follow-up arrangements.

| Class number | Course | Suspended session | Original venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 56024 | Guitar Performance | Week 1, Monday, periods 3–4 (September 17) | STU204 |
| 54831 | Chinese and Foreign Musical Masterpieces Appreciation | Week 1, Monday, periods 6–7 (September 17) | Lecture Hall 3 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
