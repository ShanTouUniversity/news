It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold the 130th Engineering Research Academic Forum on September 27, 2012.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Introduction to Cloud Computing and Enterprise Informatization Applications |
| Speaker | Prof. Jin Lianwen, Director of the Guangdong University Engineering Research Center for Audio-Video-Graphic Intelligent Information Processing and Deputy Director of the Guangdong Key Laboratory of Wireless Communication Networks and Terminals |
| Time | 10:00–11:30, September 27, 2012 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |

Faculty of the Department of Electronic Engineering and other interested teachers and students are welcome to attend. A speaker profile is available in the OA attachment.
