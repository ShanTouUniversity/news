The National Intellectual Property Administration has opened applications for its 2013 Soft Science Research Program and Patent Strategy Promotion Engineering projects. Shantou University’s Office of Research has forwarded the notice and is coordinating the university’s internal application process. Relevant units are expected to prepare and recommend projects in accordance with the national requirements.

## Application Arrangements

Applicants must submit four paper copies and an electronic copy of each project application to the Research Achievements and Development Section of the Office of Research by October 8. The office will review the materials and submit the applications collectively.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Application year | 2013 |
| Project categories | Soft Science Research Program; Patent Strategy Promotion Engineering |
| Paper materials | Four copies of the project application |
| University deadline | October 8 |
| Receiving office | Research Achievements and Development Section, Office of Research |

The application notice and related materials are available in the attachments to the original OA notice. For inquiries, applicants may contact Luo Yingguang or Deng Yong at `82903271` or `dengyong@stu.edu.cn`.
