Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office under the Resource Management Office has published a notice on additional work arising during the renovation of E Building. The second-floor seminar room and U-shaped lecture room require new air-conditioning installations, together with additional electrical boxes and power lines. The preliminary estimate for the additional work is approximately RMB 40,000, keeping the cumulative increase below the 10 percent threshold set by the university.

The renovation project began on August 1. The notice was issued under Shantou University’s Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering of Capital Construction Projects.

## Additional Work Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Shantou University E Building renovation |
| Reason for additions | Ceiling-mounted air conditioning in the second-floor seminar room and a small central air-conditioning system in the U-shaped lecture room |
| Main additions | Electrical boxes and power lines, among other work |
| Estimated increase | Approximately RMB 40,000 |
| Cost control | Cumulative additional cost remains below the university’s 10 percent threshold |
| Comment deadline | 17:30 on September 23 |

Those with different views may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by the deadline.
