Shantou University’s Trade Union has forwarded a notice from the Shantou Women’s Federation and Shantou Federation of Trade Unions and opened the university’s self-review and application process for municipal “Women’s Model Posts.” Eligible offices, sections, schools, departments and centers may prepare applications in accordance with the Administrative Measures for Shantou Municipal Women’s Model Posts.

## Application Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | Shantou Municipal Women’s Model Posts |
| Application basis | Administrative Measures for Shantou Municipal Women’s Model Posts |
| University deadline | October 16 |
| Receiving unit | Shantou University Trade Union |
| Issuing document | Shantou Women’s Federation document No. 42 (2012) |

Units intending to apply should complete a self-review against the administrative measures and submit their applications to the Trade Union by the deadline. The administrative measures and application form are available in the attachments to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union)
>
> ⚠️ The Administrative Measures for Shantou Municipal Women’s Model Posts and application form are available on the original notice in the OA system.
