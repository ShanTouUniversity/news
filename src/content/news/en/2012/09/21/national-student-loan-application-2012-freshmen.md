Shantou University’s Office of Student Affairs has opened national student loan applications for undergraduates admitted in 2012 to help students with financial difficulties complete their studies. A loan information session will be held at 19:30 on September 24 in Lecture Room 101 of Building E. Students planning to apply should bring their campus cards.

## Application and Review Schedule

| Stage | Time or location | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information session | 19:30, September 24; Lecture Room 101, Building E | Applicants should attend and bring their campus cards |
| Online application | September 24 to October 9 | Submit an application through “My Applications - Loan Application” in the Student Affairs System |
| School or college review | By October 26 | Counselors review the online application and loan materials |
| University submission | October 29 to November 9 | Materials are delivered in batches to the Student Aid Management Center, Administrative Center 106 |
| Supplementary materials deadline | By March 1, 2013 | For students who submit an online application before all materials are ready |

After submitting the online application, applicants should print the National Student Loan Application Form without signing it and provide the form together with the family financial circumstances survey, identification documents for the student and both parents, relevant copies of the student card, a copy of a Bank of China debit card and a “linked account inquiry” receipt to a counselor in their school or college. The linked-account receipt must be obtained at the Bank of China Shantou University branch counter with the debit card.

The submission schedule for schools and colleges is as follows: the Faculty of Law, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Zhicheng College will submit materials from October 29 to November 2. The School of Liberal Arts, School of Science, School of Engineering, and Business School will submit materials from November 5 to 9.

## Application Notes

Students who have already obtained a student loan from their place of origin may not apply for a national student loan at the same time. Applications must be submitted through the Student Affairs System, and all information must be accurate and complete. Materials that pass the preliminary review will become attachments to the loan contract and generally will not be returned. Students may keep a copy of the family financial circumstances survey for future financial-aid applications. The Student Aid Management Center will submit applications to the Shantou Branch of Bank of China in batches according to the applicable academic year and organize the signing of loan contracts.

For assistance, contact the Student Aid Management Center at `82904749` or email `s_xtchen1@stu.edu.cn`.
