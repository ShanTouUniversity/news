According to the Office of Research at Shantou University, the Administrative Committee of the Guangdong Outstanding Philosophy and Social Sciences Publication Fund is collecting outstanding philosophy and social sciences manuscripts from across the province. Manuscripts that pass expert review will receive full funding from the publication fund and be published and distributed by Guangdong People's Publishing House.

The call is open to people engaged in social sciences research in Guangdong, including professional researchers, teaching staff, and practitioners. Submissions must be completed, original Chinese academic monographs that have not been publicly published anywhere in the world in any language. They should fill a gap in scholarship or make an advance over existing research and follow academic conventions.

## Materials and Deadline

Applicants must submit:

- Four copies of the *Application Form for the Guangdong Outstanding Philosophy and Social Sciences Publication Subsidy Fund*, each an original bearing the official seal;
- Three bound printed copies of the manuscript;
- 31 copies each of the manuscript contents and a Chinese abstract of no more than 1,000 Chinese characters;
- Two peer-review recommendations and other written materials demonstrating the quality of the work.

Except for the detailed applicant information shown on the application form, the manuscript must not contain the applicant's name, workplace, alma mater, supervisor, or other personal information. Applicants must submit the materials to the Office of Research by September 28 and email the electronic materials to ymhuang@stu.edu.cn. Late submissions will not be accepted.

Contacts: Huang Yumei and Wang Liju; telephone: 82902563. The application form and other attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
