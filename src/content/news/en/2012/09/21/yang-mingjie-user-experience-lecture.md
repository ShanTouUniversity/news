Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host a lecture titled “Creating the Perfect User Experience” on September 26, 2012, with product designer Jamy Yang as the speaker. The lecture will take place from 19:30 to 21:30 in the school’s lecture classroom.

Yang holds a master’s degree in industrial design from Germany and previously worked at Siemens’ design headquarters in Munich. He has received dozens of design honors, including the Red Dot Design Award, iF Award, Japan’s G-Mark Award and the Design for Asia Silver Award. He has also served as a judge for several international design competitions, including the iF China Awards, and his work has been exhibited at design exhibitions and museums around the world.

## Design Practice

Yang founded YANG DESIGN in 2005. The consultancy works across product strategy and design, service design and brand integration, and has collaborated with brands including Schneider Electric, Boeing, Audi, Bosch, Rado, Absolut Vodka, DuPont, NATUZZI and Royal Selangor.

In 2007, he founded the design brand y-town, which conducts research into the environment, people and materials. The brand has launched experimental projects including the Recycled Project, the Jiu Jie collection and the Neo-craftsmanship movement, and has worked with organizations including Greenpeace, Show the World and the World Expo Museum on environmental protection and folk handicrafts.
