It is reported that, to enrich students' extracurricular life, enliven campus culture, and showcase the vibrancy of Shantou University's student clubs, the Communist Youth League Committee and the Student Club Federation will hold the "Club Tour" event **from 7:30 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. on Saturday, September 22** at the Pedestrian Street.

Student clubs will gather there for showcases and recruitment activities, and all faculty and students are welcome to attend. For students hoping to join a club that matches their interests, it is a great opportunity to learn about the various clubs in one place.
