Zhicheng College will offer the “Family Education Service for Children with Cerebral Palsy” public-interest course from October to November 2012, recruiting 24 students from across Shantou University. The course is included in the university’s public-interest course program, and students who complete the coursework and pass the assessment will receive one academic credit.

## Course Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service period | October 10 to November 28 |
| Sessions | Wednesday afternoons and Saturday afternoons |
| Service location | Urban Shantou |
| Places available | 24, selected through interviews |
| Eligibility | All Shantou University students |

The course will use team teaching and one-to-one tutoring to support children with cerebral palsy. Its activities will include music, English, handicrafts and science as supplementary learning opportunities, together with psychological support and relevant physical rehabilitation exercises. The program will also guide parents in carrying out rehabilitation activities at home.

## Application Information

Applicants should comply with the university’s public-interest course requirements, demonstrate a willingness to serve and work with others, and be prepared to follow team arrangements. Applicants should download and complete the application form, add “grade + major + name” to the file name, and email the spreadsheet to `atp@stu.edu.cn`.

Applications close at 17:00 on September 25. The interview schedule will be announced separately.
