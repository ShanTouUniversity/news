According to the Graduate School of Shantou University, the university will soon conduct midterm reviews of projects approved under its 2012 graduate extracurricular academic and technology project program, in accordance with the relevant project notice and research progress requirements.

Leaders of all approved projects must submit a written midterm review form by September 27. The form must include the supervisor's comments and signature and should be submitted to the Graduate School in Room 405 of the New Administration Centre. An electronic copy must also be emailed to o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn. Projects that fail to submit a midterm report as required will be deemed voluntarily abandoned, and the project initiation funds will be recovered.

The midterm review form and other project attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
