According to the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University, the school has added Professors Ni Qingqing and Wang Zongan as members of its School Affairs Committee to strengthen administration and advance the school's development. The revised committee is composed as follows:

- Chair: Fan Dongsheng
- Members: Bai Jing, Chang Weimin, Cheng Jinfu, Du Shimin, Ni Qingqing, and Wang Zongan
- Secretary: Li Jing
