It is reported that Shantou University will offer sports-facility trial services to freshmen from September 2 to 16, 2012. Freshmen may participate with the trial vouchers issued by the university, while each facility will operate on its designated schedule.

## Facility Schedule

| Facility | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Swimming pool | Daily, 5:00–6:30 p.m. |
| Fitness room | Daily, 4:00–6:00 p.m. |
| Gymnasium | Daily, 8:00 a.m.–noon; main entrance opposite Student Residence Halls E and F |
| Tennis courts | Daily, 8:00 a.m.–noon; advance reservation required |
| Other outdoor facilities | Open throughout the day |

Tennis-court users should make an advance reservation by emailing `o_tyb@stu.edu.cn`. Freshmen should follow facility rules and staff instructions when using the sports facilities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
