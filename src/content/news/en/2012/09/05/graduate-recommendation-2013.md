Shantou University has begun its 2013 process for recommending outstanding graduating undergraduates for admission to master’s programs without the standard entrance examination. The university requires each school to make recommendations openly, fairly and impartially, assessing students’ academic performance, research ability, innovative spirit, innovation skills and professional aptitude before selecting candidates.

## Recommendation Arrangements

Candidates will be considered in two categories: academic master’s programs and professional master’s programs. Because the Ministry of Education had not yet announced the year’s recommendation quotas for universities, schools will not set specific quotas at this stage and will rank applicants separately by category. Each school must establish a recommendation working group led by school leadership and including faculty representatives.

| Stage | Deadline and requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Student applications | Submit the application form to the relevant school by September 12 |
| School recommendations | Complete the recommendation process by September 17 |
| School submissions | By 5:00 p.m. on September 17, submit the ranked candidate list and official transcripts to the Student Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office, and email electronic materials to `yd@stu.edu.cn` |

The application form, professional master’s program information, ranked candidate list and related templates are available on the OA system. For inquiries, contact the Student Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office at `82902402`.
