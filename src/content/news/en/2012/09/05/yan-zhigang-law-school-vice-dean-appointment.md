Shantou University has proposed appointing Yan Zhigang as vice dean of the Law School. The proposal was considered at the ninth university leadership meeting on September 4, 2012, and is being publicized before appointment in accordance with the university’s interim measures for appointing management cadres.

## Public Notice and Feedback

Units or individuals with differing views may submit written feedback to the university’s Personnel Office by September 12, 2012. Materials submitted by a unit must bear its official seal, while individual submissions must include the writer’s real name for accountability.
