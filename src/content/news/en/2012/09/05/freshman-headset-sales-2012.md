Shantou University has arranged continued headset sales by the supplier because some new students have not yet purchased one. New students who still need a headset may purchase one beneath Teaching Building E on September 7, 2012.

## Sales Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible buyers | New students who have not purchased a headset |
| Date | September 7, 2012 |
| Time | 10:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. |
| Location | Beneath Teaching Building E |

Schools are asked to notify the relevant new students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
