Ahead of Teachers’ Day, the Shantou Xinghua Branch of China Construction Bank will hold financial services promotion events on the Shantou University campus and at the STU Garden residential compound. The events will provide faculty and staff with personal online-banking services and consultations. The bank will distribute personal online-banking USB security tokens free of charge, answer questions about its financial products and offer gifts.

## Event Schedule

| Session | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First session | September 7, 10:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. | First floor of the STU Labor Union building |
| Second session | September 8, 10:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. | STU Garden residential compound |

Faculty and staff seeking to process a related service must attend in person with a valid second-generation identity card and the relevant transaction account.
