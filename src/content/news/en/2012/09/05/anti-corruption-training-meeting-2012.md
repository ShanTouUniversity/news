The Guangdong Education Discipline Inspection Commission will hold a briefing on integrity and clean governance for leaders of key departments and positions at selected universities in eastern Guangdong on September 20, 2012, at Hanshan Normal University in Chaozhou. Zhao Kang, secretary of the commission, supervision commissioner and a member of the Party Leadership Group of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, will deliver a special report on combating corruption and promoting integrity.

Shantou University requires the heads of departments responsible for personnel, finance and assets, as well as admissions, examinations and employment, to attend. The university will arrange shared transportation to the venue. Participants should board at the university landmark stele at 7:20 a.m. that day.

## Participant Information

Relevant units must email the participants’ names, positions and mobile phone numbers to `o_jj@stu.edu.cn` by September 12, and make work arrangements in advance. The meeting notice attachment is available on the OA system.
