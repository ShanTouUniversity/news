Shantou municipal leaders will visit Shantou University in the near future for a Teachers’ Day visit and forum. To support a productive discussion, STU’s Party and Government Office is collecting speaking points, opinions and suggestions from all faculty members. Based on the submissions, the university will invite selected faculty members to attend and speak at the forum.

Faculty members should email their comments to the Secretariat Section of the Party and Government Office at `o_xzb@stu.edu.cn` by 3:00 p.m. on September 6, 2012.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
