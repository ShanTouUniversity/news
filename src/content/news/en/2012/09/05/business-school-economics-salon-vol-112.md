Shantou University’s Business School will hold its 112th Economics Salon on September 10, 2012, featuring Li Wenhai, vice president of Thailand’s Charoen Pokphand Group, who will speak on “Staying True to One’s Original Aspiration.” The Chinese-language event is open to Business School undergraduate and graduate students as well as the wider STU community.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Staying True to One’s Original Aspiration |
| Speaker | Li Wenhai, vice president of Thailand’s Charoen Pokphand Group |
| Time | September 10, 2012, 3:50 to 5:20 p.m. |
| Venue | Science and Technology Report Hall |
| Language | Chinese |

The salon will also count as part of the continuing education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice II.” Students enrolled in that course who wish to attend may register by emailing `o_sxy@stu.edu.cn`; staff at the venue will arrange sign-in confirmation. Event attachments are available on the OA system.
