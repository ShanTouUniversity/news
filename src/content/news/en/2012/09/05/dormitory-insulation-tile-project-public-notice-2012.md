The roof insulation-tile replacement project for Shantou University’s Dormitories A through F began on August 8, 2012. During demolition, the Resource Management Office found that some tiles that appeared intact on the surface had in fact deteriorated severely, prompting a preliminary estimate for additional replacement work.

Based on current findings, the project is expected to require approximately 200 additional square meters of insulation tiles. The Infrastructure Development Section of the Resource Management Office has issued a public notice on the proposed additional work.

## Feedback

Those with differing views may submit written feedback to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Development Section by 5:30 p.m. on September 7, 2012.
