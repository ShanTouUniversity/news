It is reported that Shantou University's Party and Administration Office has released the arrangement for this week's University Leader Reception Day, which will be held at 3 p.m. on Thursday, September 6. Luo Nianchao, Deputy Secretary of the University Party Committee, will be on duty to receive visitors.

The reception day serves as a regular channel for the university to hear opinions and concerns from faculty and students. Those who wish to raise issues with university leaders are required to make an appointment in advance by calling 8290 2306.
