For operational needs, Shantou University’s Student Community Management Service Center under the Resource Management Office is recruiting two administrators from the public. Responsibilities include duty and patrol work in residential areas, facility inspections and repair follow-up, greenery and cleaning checks, student consultations and visitor reception, and responding to emergencies in student residences.

## Position Information

Applicants should have good professional ethics, a strong sense of responsibility and teamwork skills, work carefully, be able to work rotating 24-hour shifts and be under 40 years old. Applicants should hold a junior college diploma in property management or hotel management, or have graduated from a technical and vocational institution in electrical work with at least an intermediate technician qualification.

The positions will be filled as auxiliary contract roles. The employment relationship will not be transferred to Shantou University, and compensation will follow relevant university standards.

## Application

By September 25, 2012, applicants must send a résumé, graduation certificate, family-planning certificate, professional qualification certificate, identity card and copies of other relevant certificates to the Student Community Management Service Center by email or mail. Candidates who pass the preliminary review will be notified separately about written tests and interviews. Materials will be used only for this recruitment and will not be returned; applicants should retain their own copies.
