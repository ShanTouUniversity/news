Shantou University has launched the MYSTU personal campus platform as a unified campus-account login for faculty, staff and students. The platform integrates electronic teaching, calendar management, campus notices, internal email and personal information management. Its “My Courses” module uses the former Digital Campus Moodle system and will gradually replace the existing Virtual Classroom beginning in the fall 2012 semester.

In addition to file storage and sharing, the Moodle course system supports assignment management, quizzes, discussion areas and knowledge wikis to strengthen interaction between teachers and students and among students.

## Transition Arrangements

| Milestone | Change and requirement |
| --- | --- |
| From September 17, 2012 | The Virtual Classroom will no longer allow file uploads; faculty and students may still download existing files. The Network and Information Center will back up existing files to the “Virtual Classroom File Backup” folder in MYSTU Moodle. |
| By February 27, 2013 | Faculty should use their personal campus accounts to view and download their files in MYSTU and complete the Virtual Classroom backup. |
| From the fall 2012 semester | The Network and Information Center will stop maintaining faculty FTP and disable uploads. |
| By February 27, 2013 | Faculty should back up FTP files independently and may then use “My Courses” in MYSTU to manage electronic teaching files. |

To ensure that materials are complete, faculty should finish backing up Virtual Classroom and FTP files before the start of the spring 2013 semester.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office and Network and Information Center)
