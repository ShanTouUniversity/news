The Art Education Center of Cheung Kong School of Art and Design is opening applications for the “Colorful Arts Season · Autumn 2012” fan pass to students and faculty across Shantou University. The season will issue up to 500 passes. Pass holders may collect tickets before other audiences and receive priority seating at each performance.

## Registration Arrangements

| Applicant group | Time | Location and method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Super arts fans” from the previous season | September 25, 09:30–12:00 and 14:30–17:00 | Bring the Spring 2012 pass to Room 309 of the Art Education Center for an exchange |
| Other students and faculty | The same periods, while supplies last | Bring a campus card to Room 309 of the Art Education Center to register |

People who attended every performance in the previous arts season may exchange their Spring 2012 pass for the new pass without registering again. The remaining passes will be offered to students and faculty university-wide in registration order. Pass holders may collect tickets in advance at the time announced through the university’s office information system before each performance.

## Opening Performance and Pass Benefits

Students and faculty who register and collect a pass on September 25 may receive a ticket to the season’s opening performance. “A Cappella,” an unaccompanied choral theater performance, will be held at 19:30 on September 28 in the University Auditorium. Tickets and priority seats are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

At each performance, staff will collect the ticket stub at the entrance. Pass holders who attend all three performances and collect all three stubs will qualify as “super arts fans” and may enter the final lucky draw.
