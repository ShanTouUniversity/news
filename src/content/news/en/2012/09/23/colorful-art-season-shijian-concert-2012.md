Hong Kong professional a cappella theater troupe Yi Pu Qing Chang will visit Shantou University to perform the 2012 special edition of *Shi Jian*. The performance will be held at 19:30 on September 28 in the University Auditorium and will open the “Colorful Arts Season · Autumn 2012,” giving students and faculty an opportunity to experience a live a cappella theater production.

## Performance and Ticket Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Production | *Shi Jian*, 2012 special edition |
| Performing group | Hong Kong professional a cappella theater troupe Yi Pu Qing Chang |
| Performance time | 19:30, September 28 |
| Venue | Shantou University Auditorium |
| Ticket collection | Room 309, Art Education Center |
| Collection times for other students and faculty | September 26, 09:30–12:00 and 14:30–17:00 |

Arts fan pass holders should follow the announcement on the university’s office information system for their ticket collection schedule. Other students and faculty may register with a campus card, while arts fans must present their “Colorful Arts Season · Autumn 2012” fan pass. Tickets will be distributed on a first-come, first-served basis until supplies run out.

The production introduction, program and other arrangements are available in the attachments to the OA notice. Children under 12 will not be admitted.
