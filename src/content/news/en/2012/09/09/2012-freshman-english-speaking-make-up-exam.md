Shantou University’s English Language Center reported that the original speaking tests for some 2012 freshmen on the main campus, including undergraduates and doctoral students, were invalid because the students either did not take the speaking test or did not operate the speaking-test software according to the instructions. The affected students are required to take a speaking make-up exam.

The make-up exam will be held on the morning of September 10, Monday. Affected students should arrive at their assigned room in Building E 10 minutes early, according to the room arrangements. The list of students required to take the make-up exam and the specific room assignments are provided in the English Language Center’s attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Center)
>
> ⚠️ The attachment containing the speaking make-up list and room assignments includes personal information and is available on the OA system.
