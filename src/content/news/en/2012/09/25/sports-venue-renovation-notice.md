Shantou University's Infrastructure Office has announced renovations to the sports venues of the Department of Physical Education. The project will be carried out by Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Company. Its awarded contract value is 92,900 yuan, with work scheduled to begin on September 25 and finish by October 20.

Noise, vibration, and dust may occur during construction. For safety, pedestrians should pay attention to the work area. Faculty and students may not enter the construction site without permission from the University's functional offices and the contractor, and should avoid lingering nearby. The University asks everyone to cooperate with the construction arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office, Office of Resource Management)
