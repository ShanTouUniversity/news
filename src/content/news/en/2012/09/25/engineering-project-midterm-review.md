Shantou University's School of Engineering has announced the midterm review schedule for student extracurricular science and technology innovation projects. The school received 51 preliminary applications and approved 44 projects after expert review. The school's student extracurricular academic and science-and-technology activities leading group will conduct the reviews by department on September 28 rather than hold a school-wide defense.

## Review Schedule

| Department | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Computer Science | September 28, 15:00 | Wenxi 302 |
| Electronics | September 28, 15:00 | Electronics Department Meeting Room |
| Civil Engineering | September 28, 16:00 | Civil Engineering Department Meeting Room |
| Mechanical and Electrical Engineering | September 28, 15:00 | Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Building |

Each project leader must prepare a five-minute PowerPoint presentation, the physical product or photographs of it, and materials demonstrating project progress. Every project team must also print and complete two copies of the written midterm review form and submit them to its departmental supervisor during the review. A project that does not participate as required will be considered withdrawn automatically. Expert reviewers, supervisors, and project leaders are expected to attend on time.
