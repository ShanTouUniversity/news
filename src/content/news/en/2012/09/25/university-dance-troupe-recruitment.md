The Shantou University Dance Troupe has completed its recruitment interviews and subsequent one-week assessment for the 2012–2013 fall semester. After screening and assessment, 38 students were selected to join the university dance troupe.

The Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design and the Center for Arts Education said the new members will take part in troupe activities and training. Students who were not selected were encouraged to continue pursuing their interest in dance. The detailed list of new members is included in the OA attachment and is not reproduced here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design and Center for Arts Education)
