The Shantou Intellectual Property Office has launched the selection and recognition program for the fourth Shantou Patent Awards, and Shantou University's Research Office has announced campus application arrangements. The awards cover five categories: Patent Gold Award, Patent Excellence Award, Industrial Design Gold Award, Industrial Design Excellence Award, and Outstanding Patent Inventor (Designer) Award.

## Eligibility and Materials

Inventions, utility models, or industrial design patents authorized and published by June 30, 2012, including that date, may be submitted if they meet the requirements of the *Shantou Patent Awards Regulations* and its implementation rules. Patent inventors and designers who hold a Shantou household registration or work in Shantou may also apply where eligible.

Applications for the patent awards require the relevant application form, patent certificate and publication documents, proof of patent validity, and evidence of the economic and social benefits of implementation. Industrial design entries must additionally provide the product, a model, or photographs; applicants who are not patent owners must provide documentation of their legal right to implement the patent. Applicants for the Outstanding Patent Inventor (Designer) Award must also provide valid identification, proof of employment, copies of relevant patent certificates, and other supporting materials.

## Application Process

Applications will be submitted online and in writing. Applicants must use the Shantou Patent Award Application and Selection System at [zlj.stipo.gov.cn](http://zlj.stipo.gov.cn) and upload scanned electronic documents in JPG format, with each page under 1 MB. Paper materials should use the relevant application form, be bound with supporting documents on A4 paper, and be submitted in triplicate to the Research Office by October 12, 2012, for the university's consolidated recommendation.

Because patent publication documents and proof of validity must be requested from relevant authorities in advance, prospective applicants should contact the Research Office by September 29. Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, at `0754-82903271` or `dengyong@stu.edu.cn`.
