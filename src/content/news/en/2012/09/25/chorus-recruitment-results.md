Shantou University's university choir has completed auditions for new members, with 37 students selected to join the choir. The selection considered the requirements of choral performance, the choir's current needs, and the vocal qualities of auditionees.

## Arrangements for Selected Students

Selected students must gather at 13:50 on Wednesday, September 26, in the university choir rehearsal room on the first floor of the Arts Education Center, which was also the audition venue. The detailed list of selected students is included in the related attachment.

The organizers said that some students who were not selected performed well, but their distinctive vocal qualities made it more difficult to blend with the choir. They thanked everyone who auditioned and encouraged students to continue cultivating their love of music.
