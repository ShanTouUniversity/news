Shantou University's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will screen Charlie Chaplin's *The Great Dictator* from 19:00 to 22:00 on Wednesday, September 26, 2012, at the Library Theater. Peter Herford will moderate the screening.

## Screening Information

The course has 150 seats, with an additional 50 seats available to the public on a first-come, first-served basis. Audience members must bring their campus smart card for entry. Set against the backdrop of World War II, the satirical comedy criticizes Nazism, anti-Semitism, and fascism. Released in 1940, the film received five nominations at the 1941 Academy Awards: Best Picture, Best Actor, Best Supporting Actor, Best Writing (Original Screenplay), and Best Music (Original Score). It was selected for the United States National Film Registry in 1997.

All faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
