The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University will organize a 2012 themed Youth League Day program for students who are League members from September through November. Held under the theme “Strengthening Academic Engagement for Better Growth and Development,” the program supports the University's undergraduate education initiatives and encourages students and League cadres to contribute to a stronger academic culture.

Youth League organizations at different levels will design activities around their academic disciplines, using varied formats to promote a positive learning environment and sound views on learning, personal development, and employment. School-level Youth League committees are expected to mobilize participants, organize activities, and publicize them through platforms including Weibo, QQ, Youth League websites, *STU Youth* newspaper, and its mobile newspaper. They will also conduct internal selections, recommend outstanding projects for the University-level selection, and summarize practices that can sustain the results.

## Material Submission

Each school-level Youth League committee must submit printed copies of its activity plan, summary report, and other materials to the University Youth League Committee office by November 5. Electronic copies should be sent to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. Projects recommended for the University-level Outstanding Themed Youth League Day Award must also submit two copies of the recommendation form and detailed materials of approximately 1,500 Chinese characters, with printed and electronic copies delivered through the same channels.

The University-level evaluation will feature project presentations and experience sharing. Project leaders may use PowerPoint or other formats to present their activity results, after which an assessment panel organized by the University Youth League Committee will select the award recipients.

| Award | Number and prize |
