According to Shantou University's Student Affairs Office, the university's fifth counselor meeting of 2012 will be held at 09:00 on Friday, September 28, in Meeting Room 330 of the Administration Center. Party general-branch secretaries and deputy secretaries of each school, all counselors, and relevant personnel in the student-affairs system are expected to arrange their work and attend on time.

The notice did not publish a detailed agenda for the meeting.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
