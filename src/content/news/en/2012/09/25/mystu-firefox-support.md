The Shantou University Library has issued a browser compatibility reminder for the MYSTU platform in response to reader feedback. The platform, launched by the Network Center on September 17 at [my.stu.edu.cn](http://my.stu.edu.cn), is not supported by Internet Explorer 7.0 installed on the library's reader search computers. Some readers also reported that the Chrome installer downloaded from the page did not respond after installation.

The library's technical staff found through testing that the Firefox browser supports MYSTU. An offline Firefox installer is available for readers who need it. It can be downloaded from the library homepage at [www.lib.stu.edu.cn](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn) by selecting “Services and Guides,” then “Online Library Services,” and “Software Downloads.”
