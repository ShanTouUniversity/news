Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has announced schedule changes for two Arts Education Center courses because instructors Liu Guanghui and Lin Weijie will be away at a meeting. Students enrolled in the courses should inform one another and follow the revised arrangements.

## Schedule Changes

| Course | Canceled class | Make-up class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saxophone Performance (class 54694) | Week 2 Wednesday, September 26, periods 3–4; original venue STU203 | Time and venue to be determined |
| Western Classical Music Appreciation (class 54700) | Week 2 Wednesday, September 26, periods A–B; original venue Teaching Building E, Room 303 | Week 4 and Week 5 Wednesdays, October 10 and 17, period C; Teaching Building E, Room 303 |

The make-up time and venue for Saxophone Performance will be arranged separately once confirmed. Western Classical Music Appreciation will meet at the make-up times listed above.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
