It is reported that Zhicheng College issued a notice on September 25, 2012, announcing the interview list for the “Cerebral Palsy Family Tutoring Service” public service course.

A total of 33 students applied, and 25 were selected for interviews after preliminary screening.

## Interview Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Cerebral Palsy Family Tutoring Service |
| Applicants | 33 |
| Selected for interview | 25 |
| Time | 16:10, September 26, 2012 |
| Venue | J-354, Zhicheng College |
| Issued by | Zhicheng College |

The list is available in the original OA notice and is withheld here to protect privacy.
