Shantou University's Discipline Inspection Office has forwarded a notice from the Publicity and Education Office of the Shantou Discipline Inspection Commission about an online essay contest held as part of Discipline Education Month. The university is asking its Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to encourage faculty, staff, and students to participate.

Participants should submit their essays through the contest website at [www.dahuawang.com/ztxw/jiweiedu2012/](http://www.dahuawang.com/ztxw/jiweiedu2012/) and send a copy to the university Discipline Inspection Office at `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`, using “Shantou Discipline Inspection Commission Essay” as the email subject. The submission deadline is November 10. Detailed requirements are set out in the attachment. For inquiries, call `0754-82902346`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office)
