Shantou University's Sports Department has announced a change to the swimming pool schedule. As the weather becomes cooler, the campus swimming pool will stop general access from September 29, 2012, and will remain available only for swimming classes. Faculty members and students should plan activities accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
