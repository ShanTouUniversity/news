According to a notice circulated by Shantou University's Office of Resource Management, a preliminary design review meeting for the University's new sports complex and related facilities will be held at 15:00 on Wednesday, September 26, in Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center. The meeting follows arrangements from the Shantou Municipal Housing and Urban-Rural Development Bureau for the related review.

## Attendance

Leaders or representatives from the Party and Government Office, Publicity Department, Supervision and Audit Office, Finance Office, Department of Physical Education, Network Center, Project Coordination Office, and the units under the Office of Resource Management are expected to attend. These units include the Security Office, Property Management Office, Equipment Office, Logistics Office, Key Projects Office, and Infrastructure Office.

The meeting will focus on the preliminary design review of the new sports complex and related facilities. Relevant departments should prepare for attendance in accordance with the meeting arrangements.
