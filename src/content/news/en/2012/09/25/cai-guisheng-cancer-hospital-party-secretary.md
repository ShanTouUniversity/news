Shantou University's Party Organization and United Front Work Office issued a pre-appointment public notice on September 25. Following deliberation at the seventh 2012 meeting of the University's Party Committee Standing Committee, the University proposed appointing Cai Guisheng as Party secretary of the Shantou University Medical College Affiliated Cancer Hospital.

## Comment Period

Organizations or individuals with differing views may submit comments to the University's Party Organization and United Front Work Office by October 9 in writing, in person, or by telephone. Comments from organizations must bear their official seal, while individuals must sign with their real names.

The office can be reached at 82902345 or `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. The candidate's basic information is provided in the public-notice attachment.
