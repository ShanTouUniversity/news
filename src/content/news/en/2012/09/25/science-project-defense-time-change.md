Shantou University's School of Science Student Extracurricular Academic and Science-and-Technology Activities Leading Group has announced a schedule change. Because of an unexpected scheduling issue involving a member of the review panel, the student science and technology project midterm defense originally scheduled for September 26 will now take place at 14:30 on Friday, September 28, in the Multimedia Lecture Hall (Li-352) of the School of Science.

Each project team must submit two paper copies of its written midterm review form, including the supervisor's comments and signature, to the School of Science Youth League Committee Office by noon on Thursday, September 27. An electronic copy should also be sent to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn`. Project teams should use this revised schedule for the defense and material submission.
