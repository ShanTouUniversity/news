It is reported that the 124th lecture in the *Modern Mathematics* series will take place at 16:00 on Tuesday, September 18, 2012, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). Professor Rauno Aulaskari of the University of Eastern Finland will speak on “Counterexamples on non-α-normal functions with good integrability.”

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Professor Rauno Aulaskari, University of Eastern Finland, Finland |
| Topic | *Counterexamples on non-α-normal functions with good integrability* |
| Time | 16:00, Tuesday, September 18, 2012 |
| Venue | Mathematics Laboratory, Gongbei 412 |
| Organizer | School of Science |

The abstract indicates that the lecture will use Blaschke products to construct concrete examples of analytic functions with good integrability but poor behavior of the spherical derivative. These examples demonstrate that, for 0 < *p* < 1 and 0 < α < 2, none of the classes *Mₚ* is contained in the α-normal class, suggesting that *Mₚ* is in a sense a larger class than *Qₚ*. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
