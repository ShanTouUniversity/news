It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office on September 20 publicized the proposed list of outstanding undergraduates of the Class of 2013 recommended for exemption-based admission to master's degree programs.

In accordance with the Administrative Measures for Recommending Outstanding Undergraduates for Exemption-Based Master's Admission of Shantou University (STU Fa [2009] No. 119), and following college recommendations and review by the University Working Group for Exemption-Based Admission, a total of 36 candidates were proposed, including 21 for academic master's programs and 15 for professional degree programs.

The publicity period runs until September 24. Any objections may be sent by email to the Academic Affairs Office at o_jwc@stu.edu.cn with the sender's real name.
