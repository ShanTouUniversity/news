The Fourth Chaozhou Opera Festival in Shantou will present two performances at Shantou University. At 7:30 p.m. on Monday, September 24, the Singapore Traditional Arts Centre, Singapore City God Arts Academy, and the Confucian Music Ensemble of the Johor Yingchuan Chen Clan Association in Malaysia will present *Selected Chaozhou Opera Scenes*. At 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, September 25, the Zhengzi Opera Troupe of Lufeng, Shanwei, will perform *The Battle Drum at Jinshan*.

## Performance Schedule

| Date | Performing group | Program | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| September 24 (Monday), 7:30 p.m. | Singapore Traditional Arts Centre, Singapore City God Arts Academy, and the Confucian Music Ensemble of the Johor Yingchuan Chen Clan Association in Malaysia | *Selected Chaozhou Opera Scenes* | STU Auditorium |
| September 25 (Tuesday), 7:30 p.m. | Zhengzi Opera Troupe of Lufeng, Shanwei | *The Battle Drum at Jinshan* | STU Auditorium |

Tickets will be available from 4 p.m. on September 20 and on September 21 at the Party Publicity Department (Room 315, New Administration Centre), the Centre for Arts Education, or the Communist Youth League Committee office (Room 110, New Administration Centre). The festival committee is the organizer, with STU's Party Publicity Department, Student Affairs Department, and Centre for Arts Education among the co-organizers.
