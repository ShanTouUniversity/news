It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on September 20, 2012, asking colleges to notify freshmen to handle the stamping of student ID cards by class.

Colleges are asked to collect the cards, have academic officers verify the information and submit them to the Affairs Window on the third floor of the New Administration Center by 16:00 on September 25 for centralized stamping.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
