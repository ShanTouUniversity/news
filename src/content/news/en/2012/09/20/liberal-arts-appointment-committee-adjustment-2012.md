It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts issued Document No. 6 [2012] on September 20, 2012, to adjust its Appointment Committee responsible for faculty appointments and annual evaluations, following a meeting of the college’s administrative committee on September 19 due to personnel changes.

## Adjusted Members

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Terry Bodenhorn |
| Members | Terry Bodenhorn, Chen Chuqun, Feng * (one character garbled in the original), Chen Zhanshan, Zhu Wang, Li Jie, Peng Xiaoyan, Yang Qingjie, Chen Huayu, Deng Xiaoqin, Wen Bing, Zhao Wuming, Karsten Krueger |
| Secretary | Qiu Dongyang |
