The School Affairs Committee of Shantou University's School of Liberal Arts met on September 19, 2012, and decided to adjust the membership of the school's Teaching Committee in response to personnel changes. The revised membership is as follows:

- Chair: Terry Bodenhorn
- Members: Terry Bodenhorn, Chen Chuqun, Li Renzhi, Zhang Yanyan, Zhu Wang, Li Jie, Feng Shang, Yang Qingjie, Chen Huayu, Zhang Weidong, Guo Weiwen, and Zhao Wuming
- Attending members: Zhou Zhiwei and Shao Yong
- Secretary: Zhou Zhiwei

The document is the *Notice on Adjusting the Members of the Teaching Committee of the School of Liberal Arts* (STU Liberal Arts Document No. 4, 2012), addressed to all departments and centres of the School of Liberal Arts.
