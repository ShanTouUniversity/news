Shantou University's Law School will host the 254th lecture in its series on the theme “From Regional Public Management to Regional Governance.” The speaker will be Chen Ruilian, a professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Public Administration at Sun Yat-sen University.

The lecture will be delivered in Chinese from 3 to 4 p.m. on September 22, 2012, in Room 213 of the Law School. All STU faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
