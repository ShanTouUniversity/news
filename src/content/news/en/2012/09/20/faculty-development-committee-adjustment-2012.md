It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 112 [2012] on September 20, 2012, to adjust the membership and responsibilities of its Faculty Development Committee due to personnel changes, following deliberation at the 10th university leadership meeting on September 18.

## Adjusted Membership

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Huai Xiaofeng |
| Members (ordered by stroke count) | Ma Fengqi, Yan Zhigang, Li * Ping (one character garbled in the original), Li Jie, Lu Xiaohua, Hu Zhong, Huang Xiaochun, Huang Yan, Cheng Jinfu, Han Ran |
| Office Director | Wu Ping |

## Responsibilities

1. Participate in formulating the faculty development plan and provide advice;
2. Review medium- and long-term faculty recruitment plans of teaching units and advise on high-level recruitment;
3. Review annual faculty recruitment plans of teaching units;
4. Review faculty recruitment applications of teaching units;
5. Recommend candidates for talent programs at various levels;
6. Recommend outstanding or model teachers at various levels.
