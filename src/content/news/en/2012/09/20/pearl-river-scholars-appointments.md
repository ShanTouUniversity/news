According to the Human Resources Office of Shantou University, the disciplines and candidates for the 2012 Guangdong higher-education Pearl River Scholars position plan have been confirmed following recommendations by schools, university selection, and a peer review organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. The university has now announced the results.

The relevant disciplines are to work with the Pearl River Scholars to set objectives and responsibilities based on discipline development and talent training needs, and prepare appointment contracts. The objectives should clearly cover position responsibilities, discipline or program development, talent training, research, and public service, with specific and measurable assessment requirements. Each unit is also expected to support the scholars' teaching and research work by creating a suitable working environment.

The relevant disciplines must submit draft appointment contracts to the Human Resources Office by September 26. Contracts will be formally signed after university approval.
