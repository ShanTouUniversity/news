According to the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University, the university has begun reviewing academic records for its 2013 graduating undergraduates. Academic administrators in each school will collect the review lists and distribute them to students who entered the university in 2009 for item-by-item verification. The approved admissions and enrollment information will serve as the basis for academic administration and for the information on graduation and degree certificates.

Students must personally verify basic information such as their name, gender, date of birth, identity card number, and ethnicity. If all information is correct, they should tick the “Correct” option and sign for confirmation. If personal information is incorrect, they should identify the erroneous field and sign, then apply for correction at the Academic Affairs Office's Academic Affairs Section with the original identity card and one copy. Information will not be changed if the correction procedure is not completed or the application is not approved.

## Schedule and Consultation

Academic administrators in each school must collect the completed review lists and submit them to the Academic Affairs Office's Academic Affairs Section by October 20. Students may consult their school academic administrator or the Academic Affairs Office Academic Affairs Section at the Student Service Counter on the third floor of the New Administration Building. The telephone number is 82902402.

Students are specifically reminded to check uncommon or easily confused characters in their names and confirm that their gender, ethnicity, date of birth, and identity card number are accurate and consistent. Applications from fourth-year students to change these key items will not be accepted after this review.
