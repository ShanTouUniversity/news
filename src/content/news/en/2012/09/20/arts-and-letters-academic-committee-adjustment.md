The School Affairs Committee of Shantou University's School of Liberal Arts approved an adjustment to the membership of the school's Academic Committee on September 19, 2012. The revised membership is as follows:

- Chair: Wang Furen
- Vice chair: Li Renzhi
- Members: Wang Furen, Feng Shang, Li Renzhi, Li Jie, Zhu Wang, Chen Huayu, Yang Qingjie, Deng Xiaoqin, and Zhao Wuming
- Secretary: Yang Qingjie, concurrently

The document is the *Notice on Adjusting the Members of the Academic Committee of the School of Liberal Arts* (STU Liberal Arts Document No. 5, 2012), addressed to all departments and centres of the School of Liberal Arts.
