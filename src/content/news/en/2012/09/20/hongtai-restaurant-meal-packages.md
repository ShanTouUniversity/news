According to information issued by Shantou University's Office of Resource Management, Hongtai Restaurant has introduced convenient discount Chinese meal sets for faculty and students who organize meals at the restaurant. The specific sets are listed on the restaurant menu, and members of the university community may order or reserve them.

At dinner, the restaurant also offers made-to-order dishes, seafood, and Western food, and accepts reservations for group meals. Late-night refreshments include desserts, kung fu tea, coffee, cocktails, freshly squeezed juices, flavored barbecue, smoothies, ice cream, fruit platters, and other tea-based drinks.

For reservations, call 82902023. The detailed menu for the discount Chinese meal sets is available in the attachment to the original notice on the OA system.
