According to the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University, registration has opened for this semester's National College Computer Proficiency Examination. The examination will use a computer-based network format and begin at 8 a.m. on January 5, 2013, in batches. Each subject in each batch will last 105 minutes.

## Examination Subjects

- Level 1: Computer Applications
- Level 2: Visual Basic Programming, Visual FoxPro Programming, C++ Programming, Access Database, Photoshop Image Processing and Production, Fundamentals of Web Design, and Flash Animation Production

Students must log in to the credit-based system from September 21 to 29 and select “Course Selection → Qualification Examination Registration” to register online. The registration fee is 38 yuan per person per subject. The Finance Office, through the Network Centre, will deduct the fee from each registrant's campus card account on October 8. Students should ensure that their accounts have sufficient funds; an unsuccessful deduction will result in registration failure.
