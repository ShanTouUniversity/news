The School Affairs Committee of Shantou University's School of Liberal Arts met on September 19, 2012, and decided to adjust its membership in response to personnel changes, with the aim of strengthening school administration and advancing the school's development. The new committee is composed as follows:

- Chair: Terry Bodenhorn
- Members: Terry Bodenhorn, Chen Chuqun, Wang Furen, Li Jie, Yang Qingjie, Chen Huayu, Li Linghan, Zhang Weidong, Chen Zhanshan, Zhao Wuming, and Qiu Dongyang
- Secretary: Qiu Dongyang, concurrently

The document is the *Notice on Adjusting the Members of the School Affairs Committee of the School of Liberal Arts* (STU Liberal Arts Document No. 3, 2012), addressed to all departments and centres of the School of Liberal Arts.
