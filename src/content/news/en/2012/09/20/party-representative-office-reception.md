According to the Party Organization and United Front Work Department of Shantou University, the university will arrange for members of its Party Standing Committee to receive grassroots party members and members of the public at the Party Representative Office from September 21 to 27. The meetings will take place in the multifunction room in Room 330 of the New Administration Centre, providing an opportunity to submit comments and suggestions.

Faculty and students who are party members, as well as other members of the university community, may make an appointment or attend in person. Faculty and staff who require an appointment should complete the appointment registration form and submit it in advance to the Party Organization and United Front Work Department in Room 315 of the New Administration Centre, or email it to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. The contact number is 82902345.

The detailed reception schedule and registration forms are available in the original notice attachments on the OA system.
