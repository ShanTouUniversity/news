To ease financial difficulties during the first period of enrollment, Shantou University is providing a freshman grant of 500 yuan per person to new students who entered through the green channel or completed a student-origin credit-based education loan. Following its review, the university has published the list of 2012 freshman grant recipients for public comment.

Organizations or individuals with objections may submit them in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Financial Aid Management Center by the end of the workday on Thursday, September 13. Reports from organizations must bear an official seal, while individuals must provide their real names. The center is located in Room 106 of the New Administrative Center and can be reached at 82904749.

The recipient list is provided in the public-notice attachment. To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce the names on the list.
