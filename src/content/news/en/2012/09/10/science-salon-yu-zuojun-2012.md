It is reported that Shantou University’s School of Science will hold two talks in its Science Salon series on September 13 and 14. Dr. Zuojun Yu will discuss ocean science and scientific writing. Both events will take place in Room 504 of the Science and Technology Building, and all STU students and faculty are welcome.

## Talk Schedule

| Time | Topic | Speaker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 a.m., September 13 | *On the oxygen minimum zones in the northern Indian Ocean* | Dr. Zuojun Yu, International Pacific Research Center, School of Ocean and Earth Science and Technology, University of Hawaii |
| 2:30 p.m., September 14 | *Scientific writing basics* and *How to write like a pro* | Dr. Zuojun Yu, freelance English editor based in Honolulu |

Dr. Yu’s résumé is available through the International Pacific Research Center website at the University of Hawaii. Faculty and students who wish to discuss topics with Dr. Yu individually may contact Mei Zhiping at zhipingmei@stu.edu.cn to make an appointment.
