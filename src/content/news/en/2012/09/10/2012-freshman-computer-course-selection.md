It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office and Computer Teaching Center at Shantou University have announced course-selection arrangements for the compulsory public courses “Introduction to Mobile Programming” and “Fundamentals of Computer Applications,” based on the computer placement examination results of the 2012 freshman cohort. The two courses are alternatives for students in non-computer majors. The student grouping list is available in the OA attachment.

## Course Grouping

| Course | Students assigned | Class numbers | Number of classes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to Mobile Programming | Students listed in Attachment 1 | 55087–55096 | 10 |
| Fundamentals of Computer Applications | All other students | 55076–55086 and 55572 | 12 |

Both courses are offered only in the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year and will not be offered in the spring semester. Because both courses include mobile programming content, students enrolled in either course must purchase the *Introduction to Mobile Programming* lecture notes.

The Academic Affairs Office asked academic administrators in each major to notify students in their classes of the arrangements.
