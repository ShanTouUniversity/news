It is reported that Shantou University’s Graduate School has issued supplementary arrangements for registration for the second-half 2012 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6. Because accounts for the 2012 graduate freshmen cannot yet be created in the graduate management system, freshmen who wish to take the examination must register through the graduate secretary of their school from now through the morning of September 16. Those applying for CET-6 must present a CET-4 certificate or official score report for verification.

## Registration Methods

| Applicant group | Registration method | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2012 graduate freshmen | Register with the graduate secretary of their school; CET-6 applicants must have their CET-4 certificate or score report verified | Morning of September 16 |
| Graduate students in other cohorts | Register online through the graduate management system under the Graduate School’s September 7 notice | September 16, 2012 |

Graduate secretaries at each school must submit their candidate lists to the Graduate School by September 16. After compiling, reviewing, and stamping the registration information from all schools, the Graduate School must submit the candidate registration form and electronic data to the Academic Affairs Office’s Student Affairs Section by the end of the morning workday on September 18.
