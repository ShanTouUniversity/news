Shantou University’s Party and Government Office and Student Affairs Office will hold the 2012 opening ceremony at 9:00 a.m. on Friday, September 14, in the auditorium. Each school will organize its 2012 freshmen to line up and enter early. Entry will begin at 8:25 a.m. and must be completed by 8:50 a.m.

Officials from schools and administrative departments who are scheduled to sit on the rostrum must gather in the South VIP Lounge of the auditorium by 8:50 a.m. before entering as arranged.

## Entry Reminders

All attendees must dress neatly and may not enter in slippers, undershirts, or shorts. They must follow the directions of event staff. Students carrying cameras may take photographs from their places but may not move around the venue. Attendees in the first row of the auditorium balcony should not place water cups or other items on the guardrail. Everyone in the venue must set mobile phones to vibrate mode during the ceremony.
