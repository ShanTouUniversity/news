It is reported that, in accordance with the arrangements issued by the relevant authorities, Shantou University will observe a combined Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holiday from September 30 through October 7, 2012, for eight days. Saturday, September 29, will be a working day. The Academic Affairs Office and Graduate School will make separate arrangements for teaching.

## Holiday Arrangements

The President’s Office, Resources Management Office, Student Affairs Office, all schools, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and Teaching Buildings D, E, and G must arrange personnel to be on duty during the holiday. By September 25, these units must email the names and telephone numbers of duty personnel to the President’s Office at o_jdk@stu.edu.cn. Questions may be directed to 82902305.

Before the holiday, each unit must inspect safety facilities in its office area, close windows and doors, shut off water and electricity, and complete other safety precautions. The Security Office must maintain campus security and order throughout the holiday.
