It is reported that, following review by the Guangdong Higher Education Teacher Qualification Recognition Expert Review Committee, Shantou University’s Personnel Office is publicizing the teachers proposed for higher education teacher qualification recognition. The publicity period runs from 8:30 a.m. on September 10 through 5:30 p.m. on September 17, 2012. The list of proposed recipients is available in the OA attachment.

Anyone who objects to the proposed recognition may submit a written report to the Guangdong Teacher Qualification Recognition Office of the Department of Education or the STU Personnel Office. Reports must state specific facts and bear the submitter’s real name; submissions without factual grounds or a signature will not be accepted.
