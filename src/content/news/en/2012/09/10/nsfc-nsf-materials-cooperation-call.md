It is reported that, under their scientific cooperation framework, the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) and the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) will jointly fund materials research collaborations between Chinese and U.S. scientists. Shantou University’s Research Office has circulated the call. The program focuses on energy and environmental materials sciences; NSFC will provide an average of approximately RMB 2 million per project. Projects will run for three years, from January 1, 2014, through December 31, 2016.

## Eligibility and Scope

Chinese applicants must hold a senior professional or technical title, be the principal investigator of a current or previously completed National Natural Science Foundation of China project lasting at least three years, and must not have applied in 2012 as an applicant, excluding participant status, for an NSFC international or regional cooperation research project carrying research funds.

The funding field is energy and environmental materials sciences. Applications focused on physical problems in advanced energy materials must use application code A04; other applications must use E02. Applicants must select one of these two codes or the application will be invalid. Chinese and U.S. scientists must have a strong cooperation foundation, submit applications separately to their respective national foundations after full consultation, and understand that a unilateral application is invalid. Joint applications by young Chinese and U.S. scientists are encouraged.

## Application Process

Chinese applicants must submit the preliminary application form by September 20, 2012, as an email attachment to Qin Wei of the Americas and Oceania Division of NSFC’s Bureau of International Cooperation at weiqin@nsfc.gov.cn, with a copy to yxhu@stu.edu.cn. NSFC will send preliminary review results to applicants after October 15, 2012; applicants must then inform the Research Office of the results.

Applicants who pass the preliminary review must use the ISIS science-fund system, select “International (Regional) Cooperation and Exchange Projects” and then “Cooperative Research (Inter-organizational Agreement Projects),” choose “NSFC-NSF Project (China-US)” under the cooperation agreement, and enter the approval number of the supporting fund project. In addition to submitting the Chinese application online, applicants must provide a copy of the English application submitted by the U.S. partner through NSF FastLane. Without this copy, the application will be invalid.

The application package must also include Chinese and English academic résumés for both applicants, with the U.S. applicant permitted to provide an English version only, lists of five recent representative publications from each applicant, and a copy of the cooperation agreement signed by both parties. After all materials have been completed, uploaded, and submitted online, applicants must notify the Research Office for review. Applicants whose materials pass review must print two copies of the system-generated PDF and deliver them, together with the attachments, to the Research Office by November 10, 2012.

Because of U.S. budget constraints, the program’s funding rate was relatively low in the previous two years, with two projects approved in 2010 and one in 2011. The notice also stated that the application process would span 2012 and 2013, and that applications submitted during the acceptance period would count toward applicable application limits. Applicants were advised to decide whether to apply according to their own circumstances.
