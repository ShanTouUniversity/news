It is reported that, with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Shantou University’s Labor Union will hold a birthday gathering for faculty and staff with birthdays from July through October at 6:30 p.m. on Sunday, September 23, 2012, in the Western Restaurant of Junhua Hotel. Eligible staff and their family members may attend with tickets.

## Event and Registration Schedule

| Item | Time or venue |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 6:30 p.m., Sunday, September 23, 2012 |
| Venue | Western Restaurant, Junhua Hotel |
| Reservation | 12:00 on September 10 through 24:00 on September 16, 2012 |
| Payment deduction | September 17–19, 2012 |
| Ticket collection | 10:00–12:00 on September 21, 2012 |
| Reservation system | `http://booking.stu.edu.cn/` |

Staff members should enter the reservation system through their own birthday month and log in with their campus-network account and password. Those paying in cash should follow the activity plan and exchange the bank receipt for an admission ticket at the Labor Union. Tickets cannot be refunded after registration and payment. For system-operation problems, contact the Network Center at 3548 ext. 8842 or 3551 ext. 8842.

Children must be accompanied by at least one parent, who should supervise them and safeguard personal belongings. The university will not provide transportation; participants should make their own travel arrangements and observe traffic safety. Participants are also asked to avoid wasting food.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
>
> ⚠️ The activity plan, payment account details, and other attachments are available on the OA system.
