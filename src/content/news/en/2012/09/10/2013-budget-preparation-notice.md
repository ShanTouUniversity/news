It is reported that Shantou University has begun preparing its 2013 annual budget. Each unit must prepare its budget according to the university schedule and submit the proposal to its supervising university leader for review. Budget preparation runs from September 10 through October 22, and proposals must be emailed to o_cwc@stu.edu.cn by October 22.

## Budget Priorities

The university will use unified budget management in 2013, without distinguishing between the university budget and the foundation budget. Units may apply for three types of special funding: teaching-reform projects, employment projects, and self-proposed projects. The Finance Office will calculate routine operating funds using fixed data such as employee numbers, student numbers, and teaching hours, so units do not need to apply for them. The Admissions Office will submit admissions-related funding applications centrally. Salary expenditures will be covered through the university-wide budget and do not need to be included in unit proposals.

The teaching-reform special fund supports advanced undergraduate education, with specific application arrangements to follow in an Academic Affairs Office notice. Units with approved teaching-reform projects must also submit a fact- and data-based report on the implementation and outcomes of their 2012 projects. Employment special-fund applications should address key performance indicators jointly set by the university and each unit. The university will separately establish a “Discipline Development and Research Enhancement” special fund, with application arrangements to be announced by the Research Office. Self-proposed funding is intended for other projects that support university development.

## Required Forms

| Form | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Form 1 | Basic data for the budget unit, based on authorized staffing and actual September headcount |
| Form 2 | 2011 targets and actual completion |
| Form 3 | 2012 targets and completion during the first three quarters |
| Form 4 | Analysis of tasks, target completion, and fund use for each 2012 budget project from January through September |
| Form 5 | 2013–2015 development plan and 2013 implementation priorities |
| Form 6 | Key performance indicators for employment special-fund applications |
| Form 7 | Key performance indicators for self-proposed special-fund applications |
| Form 8 | Summary of all 2013 budget applications, with comparison with and explanation of differences from the 2012 budget projects |

The notice emphasized that each unit’s performance in meeting budget targets in previous years would directly affect the budget support it might receive in 2013. Units were therefore asked to complete Forms 2, 3, and 4 carefully. The Medical College may prepare its budget using its previous method or by referring to the main campus model.
