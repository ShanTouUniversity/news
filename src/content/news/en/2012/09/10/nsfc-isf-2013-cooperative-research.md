It is reported that the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) and the Israel Science Foundation (ISF) will jointly fund substantive research collaborations between Chinese and Israeli scientists for three consecutive years beginning in 2013. Shantou University’s Research Office has circulated the call for 2013 joint research project applications to relevant units on campus.

## Project Overview

The 2013 call covers physics, chemistry, and agriculture. No more than 10 projects are expected to be funded. NSFC will provide up to RMB 2 million for each project, including research and collaboration-exchange expenses, while ISF will provide matching support for the Israeli scientists. Projects approved in 2013 will run for three years, from October 1, 2013, through September 30, 2016.

| Field | Designated application codes |
| --- | --- |
| Physics | A03, A04, A05 |
| Chemistry | B01, B03, B04, B05, B07 |
| Agriculture | C02, C13, C14, C15, C17 |

## Eligibility and Limits

Chinese applicants must hold a senior professional or technical title and must be currently undertaking, or have previously undertaken, a science-fund project lasting at least three years. Chinese and Israeli scientists must have an established basis for cooperation, and proposals should demonstrate substantive collaboration, complementary strengths, and high-level partnership.

The project is classified as an international (regional) cooperation research project. An applicant, excluding project participants, may submit only one application in this category in the same year. The combined number of projects applied for and undertaken by personnel with senior professional or technical titles, including applicants and key participants, must also remain within the three-project limit. Applicants were advised to consult the 2012 NSFC Project Guide for detailed restrictions.

## Application Arrangements

Chinese applicants must use the ISIS science-fund system to select “International (Regional) Cooperation and Exchange Projects,” then “Cooperative Research (Inter-organizational Agreement Projects),” and choose “NSFC-ISF Project (China-Israel)” under the cooperation agreement. They must complete the Chinese application as directed by the system. Applicants must also submit jointly with an Israeli partner and confirm consistency between the Chinese and English applications through the Israeli system: after the Israeli applicant locks the English application and generates its PDF, the Chinese applicant downloads it through the email link sent by the system, reviews it, and clicks the check confirmation. The Israeli applicant can submit only after this confirmation.

After completing the online submission, Chinese applicants must print two copies of the Chinese application PDF generated by the system and deliver them to the Research Office by October 29, 2012. Contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng; the telephone number is 82902813.
