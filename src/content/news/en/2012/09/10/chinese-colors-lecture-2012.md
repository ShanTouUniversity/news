It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design of Shantou University will hold a lecture on Chinese colors on the evening of September 11, 2012.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Chinese Colors: From the Distinction of Black and White to the Profound Yellow of Heaven and Earth |
| Speaker | Chen Yanqing, faculty member, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design; doctoral candidate, Academy of Arts and Design, Tsinghua University (supervisor Prof. Hang Jian) |
| Time | 19:30, September 11, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, 1F, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

## Abstract

Traditional Chinese colors have origins distinct from Western color systems. Archaeological finds and documents before the Qin and Han dynasties hint at an emerging Chinese color system; the confirmed color practices of the Qin and Han can be termed a color system, while the less clear pre-Qin practices may be called a pre-color system. The lecture will focus on uncovering the causes behind traditional Chinese color concepts. Born in 1972, the speaker researches art and design theory, design and art semiotics, and traditional Chinese color systems.
