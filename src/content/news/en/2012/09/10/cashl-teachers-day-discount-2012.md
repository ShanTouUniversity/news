The China Academic Social Sciences and Humanities Library (CASHL) is offering a Teachers’ Day promotion from September 10 to 23. During the promotion, users who register or submit document requests can receive a 100 percent subsidy on journal articles in the CASHL collection, making the requested articles free of charge.

## How to Use the Service

New users can visit the CASHL website (http://www.cashl.edu.cn), register under “Personalized Services,” and email their registration information to the Shantou University Library contact at yxu@stu.edu.cn to request account confirmation. After confirmation, users can log in, search for journals or articles, and submit document requests.

Previously registered and confirmed users can log in with their existing usernames and passwords. Users who cannot access an account because they have forgotten their credentials or because the account has expired may email the same contact to request reactivation. Further details and a user guide are available through the relevant Shantou University Library page.

CASHL currently holds more than 11,700 journals, over 700,000 print books, 340,000 electronic books, and 58 large special collections, providing foreign-language humanities and social sciences resources for research.

The promotion does not cover mediated searching, mediated borrowing, or book loans. Users should not submit requests for book loans, copying, or delivery of non-electronic documents through the CASHL system.
