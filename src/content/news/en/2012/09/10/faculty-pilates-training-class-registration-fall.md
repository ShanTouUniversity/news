It is reported that, in response to demand from Pilates enthusiasts, the Shantou University Labor Union will continue offering faculty and staff Pilates training classes on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays this semester. Returning participants must confirm their preferred session by 17:30 on September 13. Those who need to change sessions or will no longer attend should notify the association in advance. Participants who do not confirm by the deadline will be considered withdrawn, and their places will be offered to new participants.

## Class Schedule

| Session | First class | Regular time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | September 17, 2012 | Mondays, 17:35–18:35 |
| Wednesday | September 19, 2012 | Wednesdays, 17:35–18:35 |
| Friday | September 21, 2012 | Fridays, 17:35–18:35 |

Places are limited and will be assigned in registration order. Participants should bring their own yoga mat and wear loose-fitting clothing. Classes will be automatically suspended on public holidays.

For registration or confirmation, call 2654 or short number 678222.
