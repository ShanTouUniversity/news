It is reported that the Shantou University Youth League Committee and the STU Student Associations Federation will hold the 16th Clubs Night on Tuesday, September 11. The event is intended to showcase student associations and enrich campus cultural life. All STU faculty and students are welcome at the themed evening program in front of the auditorium.

## Event Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Shantou University 16th Clubs Night |
| Time | 8:00–10:00 p.m., Tuesday, September 11, 2012 |
| Venue | Auditorium forecourt |
| Organizers | STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Associations Federation |
| Theme | “Clubs Whirlwind Rises, Dreams Dance and Music Soar; We Await Your Support” |

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
