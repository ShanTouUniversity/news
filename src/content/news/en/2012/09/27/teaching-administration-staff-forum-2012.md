To advance its undergraduate education initiative, Shantou University will hold a forum for academic affairs officers and teaching secretaries from its teaching units. The forum will address the development of teaching administration systems and ways to improve teaching management and service quality, supporting future education reform.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:45–17:30 on Saturday, September 29, 2012 |
| Venue | Multipurpose Hall 330, third floor of the New Administration Building |
| Participants | Academic affairs officers, teaching secretaries and other relevant staff from the main-campus teaching units |
| Topics | Teaching administration systems, teaching service quality and support for education reform |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
