The 26th Shantou University Student Track and Field Meet will be held on December 8 and 9, 2012, at the university track. The event is intended to promote sports at STU, raise student awareness of participation in physical activity and encourage regular exercise.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 26th Shantou University Student Track and Field Meet |
| Date | December 8–9, 2012 |
| Venue | University track |
| Organizing units | Colleges and residential colleges |

The university has encouraged each unit to publicize the event, organize students and support active participation. The competition rules, registration materials and detailed events are available in the original OA attachments.
