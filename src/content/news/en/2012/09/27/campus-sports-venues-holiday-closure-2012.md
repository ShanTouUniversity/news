The Shantou University Sports Department has announced that several campus sports venues will close during the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holiday period. Students and staff should arrange their activities accordingly.

| Venue | Closure | Reopening |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indoor badminton court, tennis court and gym | September 30–October 7, 2012 | From October 8, 2012 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
