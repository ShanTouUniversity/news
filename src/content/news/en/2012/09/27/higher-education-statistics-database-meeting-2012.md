Shantou University will hold a meeting on September 29, 2012, to organize grassroots higher education statistics for the 2012–2013 academic year. The meeting will also cover basic database development and provide training in relevant statistical software. The units concerned are expected to send staff responsible for statistical work.

## Meeting details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 09:00 on September 29, 2012 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 213, New Administration Building |
| Topics | Grassroots higher education statistics, basic database development and statistical software training |

Representatives should be arranged by the Medical College, Graduate School, Academic Affairs Office, School of Continuing Education, Human Resources Office, Office of Research, Office of International Exchange and Cooperation, Party School Office, University Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office, Library, Network and Information Center, University Assets Office and Equipment Section.

## Main statistical categories

The work will cover the following information:

- Enrollment status and political affiliation of doctoral and master's students, regular undergraduate and junior-college students, and adult undergraduate and junior-college students;
- International students;
- The number and political affiliation of faculty and staff;
- Campus and building area, fixed assets and library holdings;
- Digital resources and information technology development;
- Research information, scholarships and grants, and employment rates;
- Full-time counselors and psychological counseling staff.
