Shantou University has asked eligible staff members to collect their medical insurance manuals and IC cards. Collection is available during working hours at the Employee Medical Insurance Section on the second floor of the university hospital.

The original notice includes a list of people eligible to collect the items. Because one name is garbled in the original list and “Fang Jinmei” appears twice, this article does not reproduce the complete list. Staff members should check the original OA notice to confirm whether they are included.
