Based on the decision of the 10th meeting of Shantou University leaders on September 18, 2012, STU has announced appointments and role changes in the School of Engineering. Bao Nengsheng, Zhang Jie and Zhang Kaidong have been appointed deputy dean of the School of Engineering, deputy head of its Department of Computer Science and director of its Computer Teaching Center, respectively. Each appointment carries a six-month probation period.

| Person | New role | Probation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bao Nengsheng | Deputy dean, School of Engineering | Six months |
| Zhang Jie | Deputy head, Department of Computer Science, School of Engineering | Six months |
| Zhang Kaidong | Director, Computer Teaching Center, School of Engineering | Six months |

Li *Ping is no longer deputy dean of the School of Engineering. Bao Nengsheng is no longer assistant to the dean of the School of Engineering, and Zhang Jie is no longer director of its Computer Teaching Center. One character in Li *Ping's name appears garbled in the original notice and has not been reconstructed speculatively.
