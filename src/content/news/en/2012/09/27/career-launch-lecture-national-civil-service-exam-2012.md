The 27th Career Launchers Lecture at Shantou University will offer guidance on preparing for the 2013 national civil service examination. The session is open to all STU students and staff and will be led by Chen Hong, a researcher at the Guangdong Huatu Teaching and Research Center. Chen has worked in teaching research and has experience in civil service interview training and analytical reasoning courses.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Guidance on preparing for the 2013 national civil service examination |
| Time | 19:00–21:00 on September 27, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Staircase E204 |
| Speaker | Chen Hong, researcher at the Guangdong Huatu Teaching and Research Center |
| Audience | All STU students and staff |
