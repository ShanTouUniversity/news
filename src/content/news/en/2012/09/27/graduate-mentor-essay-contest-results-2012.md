The Shantou University Graduate School has announced the results of the second My Ideal Supervisor essay contest. Following expert review, the contest awarded one first prize, two second prizes and three third prizes. The list of winners and the winning essays are included in the notice attachments; student names are not reproduced here.

| Award | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 1 |
| Second prize | 2 |
| Third prize | 3 |
| Total | 6 |

Winners may go to the Youth League office of the Graduate School on October 9, 2012, to collect their prizes and certificates. Please refer to the original OA attachments for the detailed list and winning works.
