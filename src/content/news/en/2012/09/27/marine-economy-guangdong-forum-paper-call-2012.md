The STU Office of Research has forwarded a call for papers for the Forum on Marine Economy and the Future Development of Guangdong. The forum will be jointly held in early December 2012 by the Publicity Department of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China and the Guangdong Provincial Federation of Social Sciences. It will also serve as the 2011 China (Southern) Think Tank Forum. Relevant officials and experts in marine economy research are expected to attend, and submissions are open to researchers and other members of the public.

## Theme and research areas

The forum theme is “Marine Economy and the Future Development of Guangdong.” Papers may address any of the following seven areas:

1. Lessons from marine economy development;
2. A comparison of marine economy development in Guangdong, Fujian, Shandong, Zhejiang and Hainan;
3. Challenges, opportunities and responses in building Guangdong into a strong marine economy province;
4. Prospects for strategic emerging marine industries;
5. Marine science and technology and the development of a modern industrial system;
6. Integrated marine management and institutional innovation;
7. Environmental challenges and responses in marine administration.

The organizers are seeking theoretical analysis grounded in Guangdong's recent marine economy practice. Submissions should examine major issues, risks and challenges arising as Guangdong builds a strong marine economy, advances transformation and upgrading, and pursues a blue rise, while proposing possible responses. Each paper should be about 5,000 Chinese characters.
