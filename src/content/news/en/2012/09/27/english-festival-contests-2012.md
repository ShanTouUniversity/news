The Shantou University English Language Center has announced that the 2012 English Festival will take place from December 7 to 9. The festival will feature the Intercollegiate English Speech Contest, Intercollegiate English Singing Contest and Intercollegiate English Debate Competition. Recruitment is currently open to undergraduates for the speech and singing contests.

## Speech contest

The speech contest will use the theme “Stories the World Should Hear.” Contestants must first submit a speech manuscript for a performance of no more than three minutes. An electronic copy must be emailed to `cindychen@stu.edu.cn` by 18:00 on October 7, 2012. Later stages will include an interview and a stage presentation.

## Singing contest

The Intercollegiate English Singing Contest is open to STU undergraduates. Entries may be performed individually or by pairs. The preliminary round will be held at 19:30 on October 14 at the English Activities Center (EAC). Each entry will have 30 seconds to one minute to sing. Contestants should prepare one minute of instrumental backing music without vocals and bring it on a USB drive on the day of the preliminary round. Top performers will advance to the semifinal on November 4.

## Debate competition

The English Debate Competition is open to students who want to discuss current topics and take part in formal debates with students from other universities. Interested students may contact Anny or Collin of the English debate club for the dates and details of the workshop series: Anny at `ngao@stu.edu.cn` and Collin at `ctham@stu.edu.cn`.

For questions about the singing contest, students may email `abuchal@stu.edu.cn` or visit the front desk of the English Activities Center in Building 789.
