The Shantou University Student Learning Center (SLC) has announced the results of its 2012–2013 duty staff selection. Based on position requirements and interview performance, the center selected five students to serve as duty staff.

To protect student privacy, student numbers are not published and names are presented as the surname followed by the initials of the given name:

- Ruan LJ
- Li JY
- Jia LJ
- Yu HW
- Zhu QW

SLC also thanked students for their support of the center. Detailed duty arrangements will be provided in a subsequent notice.
