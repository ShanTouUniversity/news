Following arrangements from the Guangdong Higher Education Teaching Examination Management Center, Shantou University has opened registration for the second English proficiency test of 2012. The computer-based online examination will be held on December 23, 2012. Eligible applicants are sports students and students of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Computer-based online examination |
| Examination date | December 23, 2012 |
| Eligible applicants | Sports students and students of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Online registration | September 27–October 12, 2012 |
| Registration fee | 16 yuan per person |

Eligible students should log in to the credit-system website and select “Course Selection → Qualification Examination Registration” to complete the process. The Finance Office will authorize the Network Center to deduct the fee from each applicant's student card account. Applicants should keep enough money in the account; an unsuccessful deduction will result in unsuccessful registration.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
