The Shantou University Network and Information Center has announced that the student card service windows at the Experts' Apartments and below Dining Hall 2 will close during the National Day holiday. Students and staff can use self-service equipment, telephone services or email for related matters.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service closure | September 30–October 7, 2012 |
| Affected locations | Student card windows at the Experts' Apartments and below Dining Hall 2 |
| Service resumes | October 8, 2012 |
| Self-service top-up | Use the integrated deposit-transfer, inquiry and top-up machine |
| Loss reports and balance inquiries | Call 82903414, campus extension 3414 |

During the holiday, users with student card questions may call `82903413-200`, contact the service mobile number `13556307566` (campus short number `677566`), or email `support@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
