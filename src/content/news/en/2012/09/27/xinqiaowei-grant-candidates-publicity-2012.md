The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has published the proposed recipients of the Xinqiaowei Student Grant. Following recommendations from colleges and residential colleges, review by the Student Affairs Office and approval by the university leader in charge, STU plans to designate five students as grant recipients. The detailed list is included in the notice attachment and is not reproduced here to protect student privacy.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed recipients | Five Xinqiaowei Student Grant recipients |
| Objection deadline | Before 09:00 on Friday, September 28, 2012 |
| Submission location | Student Aid Management Center, Room 106, Administration Center |
| Submission format | Written materials |

Units submitting objections must affix an official seal. Individuals must sign their real names when reporting information and assume responsibility for the report. Please consult the original OA attachment for the detailed list.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
