To advance undergraduate education and strengthen civic education for university students, Shantou University has established a leading group for Culture, Civility, Character and Care education. The four elements are represented by the abbreviation 4C: Culture, Civility, Character and Care. STU announced the organizational arrangement on September 27, 2012.

## Group membership

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Song *Zhen (one character appears garbled in the original notice) |
| Deputy chairs | Luo Nianchao and Li Dan |
| Members | Ma Fengqi, Huai Xiaofeng, Lu Xiaohua, Lin Bingfeng, Bai Youjin and Yu Hong |

The leading group will have an office attached to the Institute of Higher Education Research to coordinate related work. One character in Song *Zhen's name appears garbled in the original notice and archive and has not been reconstructed speculatively.
