The Shantou University Network and Information Center has reminded 2012 undergraduate and graduate freshmen that the student card prepayment is 100 yuan. The university made a second bank debit on September 24. Accounts for students whose second debit was unsuccessful will be frozen and may later be canceled according to the schedule below; the relevant list is available in the OA attachment.

## Payment schedule and consequences

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Amount due | 100 yuan |
| Payment method | Pay in cash at the student card service window of the Network and Information Center |
| Payment deadline | October 30, 2012 |
| Account freeze | From October 24, 2012, for students who have not completed payment |
| Late handling | Accounts still unpaid by the deadline will be reported to the university for cancellation |

A frozen student card cannot be used for purchases at campus cafeterias or stores, and cannot be used for deductions or subsidy payments. The payment window is open Monday through Thursday from 08:30 to 12:00 and from 14:30 to 16:15, and Friday from 08:30 to 12:00. Friday afternoons are reserved for settlement and do not offer window service. The Network and Information Center will also notify the students concerned by email.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
