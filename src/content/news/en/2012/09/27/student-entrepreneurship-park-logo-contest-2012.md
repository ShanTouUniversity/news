The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has opened a logo design contest for the student entrepreneurship park to all enrolled students and staff. The contest offers an opportunity to present creative work and take part in entrepreneurship-related practice. Entries may be submitted individually or by teams, and should reflect the park theme and the entrepreneurial and practical spirit of STU students.

## Entry and submission requirements

Entries must be original, positive and healthy in content, contemporary in character, creative, distinctive and easy to disseminate. Each entry must include an electronic JPG logo file and a Word document explaining the design concept and providing author information. Both files should be named with the author's name and sent to `zpchen@stu.edu.cn`; the email subject should use the format “author name + college or residential college.” The author information includes name, college or residential college and mobile number; this article does not reproduce personal information.

## Awards and use of the work

The contest will offer one selected-work award of 1,000 yuan and five finalist awards of 200 yuan each. The selected-work design will receive priority for adoption. Entries will be reviewed for form, creativity and meaning.

After a work is adopted, the student entrepreneurship park may propose changes in consultation with the author and will have the permanent right to use the logo.
