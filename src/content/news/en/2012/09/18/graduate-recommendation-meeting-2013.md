Shantou University’s working group for recommending outstanding undergraduates for admission to master’s programs without the national entrance examination will meet at **4 p.m. on Wednesday, September 19** in the Teaching Affairs Office conference room on the third floor of the New Administration Center. The meeting will discuss the university’s proposed 2013 recommendation list, taking into account recommendations from the schools and the 2013 quota allocated by the Teaching Affairs Department of the Ministry of Education.

Business, Liberal Arts, Law, Science, Engineering, Journalism and Communication, Art and Design, and Medical schools, as well as the Graduate School, must each send one representative to the meeting on time.
