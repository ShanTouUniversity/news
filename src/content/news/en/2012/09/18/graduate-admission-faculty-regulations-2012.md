Shantou University’s Graduate School announced that the Regulations on Faculty Applications to Admit Graduate Students have been reviewed and approved by the university’s Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee and are now officially issued. All graduate-training units must follow the regulations in their related work.

The full text of the regulations is provided in the OA attachment.
