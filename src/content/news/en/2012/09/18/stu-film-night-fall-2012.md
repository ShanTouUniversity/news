The Yangtze Journalism and Communication School is launching STU Film Night for the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. Screenings will take place at **7 p.m. every Wednesday** in the lecture hall of the New Library. The program is open to all faculty members, staff, and students and will feature English-language movies with Chinese subtitles. Admission requires a One-Card, and seating is first come, first served. Peter Herford will moderate the program.

The opening screening, *The Wizard of Oz*, will take place at **7 p.m. on Wednesday, September 19**. The planned film list also includes:

*The Great Dictator*, *Singin' in the Rain*, *The Three Musketeers*, *Henry V*, *High Noon*, *All the King's Men*, *Hamlet*, *National Velvet*, *Casablanca*, *The Bicycle Thief*, *The Third Man*, *The Greatest Show on Earth*, *Yankee Doodle Dandy*, *Mister Roberts*, and *Gone with the Wind*.
