Shantou University’s Graduate School has begun the eligibility review for faculty members applying to admit graduate students in 2013. Schools and disciplines should conduct the review under the university’s Regulations on Faculty Applications to Admit Graduate Students, supporting the upcoming admissions process and the publication of supervisor information in the admissions prospectus.

Each graduate-training unit must submit the following materials to the Graduate School by **the end of the working morning on Monday, September 24**:

- One paper copy of the Shantou University Faculty Graduate Student Admission Application Form;
- One paper copy of the summary form;
- One electronic copy of the summary form.

Paper materials should be submitted to Ms. Li at the Graduate School, and the electronic summary should also be sent to `galaxylee@stu.edu.cn`. For inquiries, call `82902424`. The Graduate School noted that 2013 graduating undergraduates will begin online pre-registration for the graduate entrance examination on September 28, with formal registration to follow.

The relevant regulations, application form, and summary form are included in the OA attachments.
