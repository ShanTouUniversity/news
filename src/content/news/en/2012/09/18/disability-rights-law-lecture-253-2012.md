The 253rd lecture in Shantou University Law School’s lecture series will be held from **8 to 9 p.m. on September 20** in Room 213 of the Law School. Dr. Cui Fengming, director of Harvard Law School’s China Project on Disability, will deliver a Chinese-language lecture titled “Legal Rights of Persons with Disabilities and the Role of their Organizations.” All faculty members and students are welcome.
