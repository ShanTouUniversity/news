As the new academic year begins, Shantou University’s Security Office has urged freshmen and their families to guard against telephone and online scams that exploit students’ limited social experience. Common schemes include fake prize or bargain-sale messages, requests for money disguised as pleas for help, calls from people impersonating university staff who claim that a student has been injured and needs an urgent transfer, and requests for bank-account information under the pretext of investigating prohibited items in a parcel.

The Security Office advises freshmen to discuss these warnings with their families, avoid trusting unfamiliar messages or verbal promises, and never provide account information or transfer money in pursuit of a small benefit. Anyone who encounters a suspected scam should contact a university department promptly or report it to the police.

The campus reservoir has complex hydrological conditions and has been the site of previous drownings. For personal safety, the university prohibits swimming, fishing, wading, and other water-related activities in the reservoir.
