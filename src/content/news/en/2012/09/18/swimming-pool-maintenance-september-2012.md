Shantou University’s Sports Department announced that the campus swimming pool will suspend regular access from **September 19 to 21** for maintenance and repairs to faulty equipment. During this period, the pool will remain available only for swimming classes; other campus access will be suspended.

| Period | Access arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| September 19–21 | Regular campus access suspended; pool available only for swimming classes |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
