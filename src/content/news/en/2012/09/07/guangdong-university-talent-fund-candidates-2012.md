According to the interim measures governing Guangdong’s special fund for talent recruitment in higher education, four recruited Shantou University teachers are proposed for support from the Guangdong University Talent Recruitment Special Fund following the university’s application and an evaluation by the Guangdong Education Department. The basic information of the proposed recipients has been released for public comment from September 7 to 14.

During the notice period, objections to the proposed recipients may be submitted to the Faculty Management Division of the Guangdong Education Department or the university’s Human Resources Office. Reports must include the reporter’s real name, either provided directly or by signature, and specific facts. Reports without a real name or supporting facts will not be accepted.
