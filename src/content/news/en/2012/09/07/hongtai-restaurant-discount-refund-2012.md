Hongtai Restaurant offered a 20 percent discount during its trial operation from August 16 to 31. The All-in-One Card Center has completed its review of the transaction records and refunded the corresponding 20 percent discount to consumers’ campus-card accounts.

The Resource Management Office reminds relevant consumers to check whether the funds have been credited through the campus-card service.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
