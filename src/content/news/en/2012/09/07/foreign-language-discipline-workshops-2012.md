Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will hold workshops on foreign-language discipline development and faculty development on September 10 in Lecture Hall 3 of the Academic Conference Center. The speakers will be Professor Yang Xinzhang, dean of the School of Foreign Languages at Xiamen University and a doctoral supervisor, and Professor Ran Yongping of Guangdong University of Foreign Studies, also a doctoral supervisor and editor-in-chief of Modern Foreign Languages. Faculty and graduate students interested in the topics are welcome.

## Program

| Time | Speaker and topic |
| --- | --- |
| September 10, 9:00–11:30 a.m. | Professor Yang Xinzhang: Foreign-language discipline development, research-project applications and approval |
| September 10, 3:00–5:30 p.m. | Professor Ran Yongping: Improving foreign-language research, academic writing standards, and publication requirements |

The workshops will discuss foreign-language discipline development, faculty research, research-project applications and approval, as well as topic selection, writing standards, and publication requirements for academic papers.

Ran is also a U.S.-China Fulbright Scholar, a recipient of the Ministry of Education’s New Century Excellent Talents program, and a distinguished Pearl River Scholar professor in Guangdong higher education.
