Shantou University’s School of Science will hold a freshman orientation lecture for biology students on the morning of September 10, featuring Professor Zhan Ximei of Sun Yat-sen University. His lecture, titled “From an Underprivileged Child to a Doctor, Professor, and National Teaching Scholar: Reflections on My Educational Journey,” will take place from 8:30 to 10:30 a.m. in Conference Room 205 of the Science and Technology Center. Undergraduates and graduate students in the 2012 biology cohort are expected to attend, and all faculty, staff, and students are welcome.

Zhan graduated from Sun Yat-sen Medical College in 1970, received a master’s degree from Sun Yat-sen University of Medical Sciences in 1981, and earned a doctorate in 1987. He pursued further study in molecular biology at the University of Wales in the United Kingdom in 1991. He became a master’s supervisor in 1994 and has supervised 12 master’s graduates; he became a doctoral supervisor in 1996 and has supervised 10 doctoral graduates. In 2008, Zhan was named a National Teaching Scholar in the fourth national selection.

Zhan has taught and conducted research in parasitology for more than 20 years and has published over 130 papers. His work has covered parasite ecology, taxonomy, epidemiology, ultrastructure, numerical taxonomy, image analysis, disease-control network systems, molecular biology, receptors, automated detection systems, and teaching reform. He also served as chief editor of the fifth edition of Human Parasitology, a national planning textbook for higher medical education, as well as the unified textbooks for seven-year and eight-year medical programs.
