Shantou University will build an open-air temporary parking lot west of the New Administrative Building, on the side facing the sports field. The project was approved by the relevant authorities and awarded through a public tender to Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation. Work is scheduled to begin on September 8 and finish in early October.

## Project Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Location | West of the New Administrative Building, beside the sports field |
| Project type | Open-air temporary parking spaces |
| Contractor | Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation |
| Main work | Site leveling and soil removal, sand filling for the ground base, and installation of curved-wave paving blocks |
| Schedule | Starts September 8, 2012; completion planned for early October |

During construction, no one may enter the site without permission from the university’s relevant administrative offices and the contractor. The university also reminds faculty, staff, and students to avoid lingering near the construction area in the interest of safety.
