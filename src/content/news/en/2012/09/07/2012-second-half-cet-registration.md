Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has announced registration arrangements for the 2012 second-half National College English Test (CET). CET-4 will be held from 9:00 to 11:20 a.m. on December 22, and CET-6 from 3:00 to 5:20 p.m. the same day. Non-enrolled candidates will not be accepted for this examination.

## Eligibility

For CET-4, eligible applicants include undergraduates placed in English Level 2 or above this semester, arts undergraduates who have completed ELC EA3 or passed the English Application Ability Test, and students in the 2011 English major cohort. For CET-6, eligible applicants include enrolled students who hold a CET-4 certificate or scored at least 425 on the CET-4, and enrolled students who hold an English major CET-4 certificate.

Enrolled graduate and undergraduate students who already hold a CET-6 certificate or scored at least 425 on the CET-6 may register for the test again. Undergraduates must have completed ELC4, and the number of repeat registrations after reaching 425 may not exceed two.

## Registration and Payment

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Online registration | September 8–18 |
| Undergraduate registration portal | Log in to the Credit-Based Education website and select “Course Selection” → “Qualification Exam Registration” |
| CET-4 fee | 16 yuan per person |
| CET-6 fee | 18 yuan per person |
| Campus-card deduction | The Finance Office, through the Network Center, will deduct fees on September 19 |
| Tuition-account deduction | If the campus-card deduction fails, the Finance Office, through Bank of China, will deduct fees on the morning of September 20 |

Applicants must maintain sufficient funds in their accounts during the deduction period; an unsuccessful deduction will result in failed registration. Candidates must bring their identity card, student card, and admission ticket to the examination. Anyone missing any one of these documents will not be admitted.
