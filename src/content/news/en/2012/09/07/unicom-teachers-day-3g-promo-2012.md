It is reported that the Trade Union issued a notice on September 7, 2012, that Shantou Branch of China Unicom will offer a special Teachers’ Day 3G handset bundle promotion for faculty.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 10–11, 2012, 10:00–12:00 and 14:30–15:30 |
| Venue | First floor, Trade Union |
| Required documents | Staff campus card and a copy of ID card (both sides) |
| Contact | Mr. Zheng 18675668688 |
| Issued by | Trade Union |

## Promotion

Selected handset bundles enjoy a 400-yuan discount: six models originally priced at 799 yuan are reduced to 399 yuan, including Coolpad 7019, Tianyu W619 and Lenovo A65; eight models originally at 999 yuan are reduced to 599 yuan, including ZTE V889D, Coolpad 7260 and Lenovo A780. Deposits, rebates and contract terms remain unchanged; see the OA attachment for details.

Faculty who purchase the bundle can also enjoy broadband at 40 yuan per month for 6M or 80 yuan per month for 10M.

> ⚠️ Details of models and tariffs are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union, Shantou Unicom)
