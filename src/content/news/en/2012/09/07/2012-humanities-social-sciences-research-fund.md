Shantou University’s Research Office has opened applications for the 2012 Humanities and Social Sciences Research Fund for relevant units on the main campus. The fund has two categories: priority-area projects and open-topic projects. Applications should generally be submitted through the applicant’s unit rather than directly by individuals.

## Areas and Eligibility

The priority areas are local government development research, Guangdong-Taiwan enterprise cooperation research, and Chaoshan history and culture research. Applicants may develop their own topics around these directions, but proposals must identify a clear research subject or problem. Applicants for open-topic projects may choose topics based on their research interests and academic work.

Applicants must be in-service, actively employed teachers in humanities and social sciences disciplines appointed by the main campus. They must not have served as principal investigators for provincial- or ministerial-level or higher research projects and must have prior research relevant to the proposed topic. Applicants who have taken part in applications for the National Social Science Fund in the past five years and passed peer review at least twice will automatically receive funding.

## Submission Materials

Applicants must complete the STU Humanities and Social Sciences Research Fund Application Form and the Project Certification Sheet and submit the following materials to their units:

| Material | Printed copies | Other requirement |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Project application form | 2 | A4, double-sided; electronic version also required |
| Project certification sheet | 4 | Electronic version also required |

Each applicant’s unit will review eligibility and application materials and sign its review comments as required. Units must deliver the printed materials to the Research Office by September 26 and send the electronic versions to ymhuang@stu.edu.cn. Late submissions will not be accepted.

The contacts are Huang Yumei and Wang Liju, reachable at 82902563.
