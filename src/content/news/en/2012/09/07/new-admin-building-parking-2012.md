It is reported that the Party and Government Office and the Resource Management Office jointly issued a notice on September 7, 2012, announcing parking arrangements for the New Administration Building, effective from September 10.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Elevated ground floor, east north-south passage | Vehicle passage and fire lane, no fixed parking spaces |
| South side of the elevated floor | Electric scooter parking point |
| East side of the elevated floor | Motorcycle and bicycle parking area |
| Parking area 1 | Both sides of the road north of the New Administration Building (about 60 spaces) |
| Parking area 2 | Parking lot on Road No. 10 west of the New Administration Building (about 40 spaces) |

To ensure fire safety, no fixed car spaces will be provided in the elevated ground floor area. All faculty, students and staff are asked to follow the security department’s management to maintain a good campus environment.

> ⚠️ The parking diagram is available on the OA system.
