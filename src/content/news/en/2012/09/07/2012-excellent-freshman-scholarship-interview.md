Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold the 2012 Outstanding Freshman Scholarship interviews on the morning of September 11 in Room 301 of Building E. The interviews will use a leaderless group discussion format. Shortlisted students must report to Room 309 of Building E at 8:00 a.m. that day to receive the relevant arrangements; the formal interviews will begin at 8:30 a.m.

## Interview Process

Participants will first give a self-introduction of no more than one minute each. They will then draw lots to determine the discussion topic and take part in a 10-minute leaderless group discussion. The topic will be drawn immediately before the discussion, with one minute provided for preparation. An eight-minute question-and-answer session with the judges will follow the discussion.

The Student Affairs Office reminds shortlisted students to arrive at the designated location on time.
