Shantou University’s Party and Government Office and Resource Management Office have received several complaints about vehicles honking at pedestrians on campus. The university reminds drivers to yield to pedestrians while driving on campus and never honk at them. Faculty, staff, and students are also asked to remind one another and help maintain orderly campus traffic.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office and Resource Management Office)
