The 2012 Freshman Orienteering Event, organized by the Communist Youth League of Shantou University and hosted by the Shantou University Student Organizations Federation, will take place from September 10 to 13, from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. each afternoon. All undergraduate freshmen except those in the medical school are required to participate; graduate students are not included in the event audience.

Participants must gather at the gymnasium at 1:30 p.m. on the day of the activity and wear a sleeve marker to identify their group. They should wear sportswear and athletic shoes, bring a water bottle, and prepare appropriately for the weather. Students from Zhicheng College should collect sleeve markers from their respective professional schools.

Schools may collect sleeve markers in Room 731 of Building 789 on September 9, from 10:00 a.m. to noon or from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. Detailed student groupings are provided in the event attachments.
