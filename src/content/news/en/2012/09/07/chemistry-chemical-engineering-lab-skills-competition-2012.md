The fourth Guangdong Intercollegiate Chemistry and Chemical Engineering Laboratory Skills Contest will be held at Dongguan University of Technology from November 16 to 18. Shantou University’s Department of Chemistry in the School of Science is organizing a campus selection to choose student representatives for the provincial contest and facilitate laboratory-skills exchanges with students from universities across Guangdong.

Students in any major or year who have completed both Analytical Chemistry Laboratory and Physical Chemistry Laboratory, with a score of at least 80 in each course, may apply. Applicants must complete the registration form and email it to o_lxyhxx@stu.edu.cn by September 19.

## Selection Schedule

| Stage | Date | Venue or format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration | By September 19 | Complete the attached form and email it |
| Written preliminary round | September 26 | Room 301, Building E; 60 minutes, with the top 16 selected by score |
| Experimental final round | October 13 | The Analytical Chemistry and Physical Chemistry laboratories in the Department of Chemistry |
| Provincial contest | November 16–18 | Dongguan University of Technology |

The experimental final will cover the spectrophotometric determination of trace iron using the o-phenanthroline method, as well as the determination and application of electromotive force.
