Shantou University’s Research Office has begun accepting applications for the 2012 Academic Innovation Team Development and National Fund Development projects. Eligible in-service teachers on the main campus may apply through their units. The application conditions and management requirements are set out in the Interim Measures for the Management of Shantou University Academic Innovation Team Development and National Fund Development Projects.

Teachers applying for an academic innovation team project must complete the A or B application form for the STU Academic Innovation Team Development Project. Teachers applying for a national fund development project must complete the STU National Fund Development Project Application Form. Priority areas have been established for humanities and social sciences national fund development projects: local government development research, Guangdong-Taiwan enterprise cooperation research, and Chaoshan history and culture research. Applicants may also propose open topics based on their research interests and academic work. All projects must identify a clear research subject or problem, and priority-area proposals must identify their designated area in the application.

## Submission Materials

Applicants must submit four A4 printed copies of the application form and an electronic version to their unit. Each unit will review its projects, complete the unit-opinion section of the application form, and affix its official seal. By September 26, units must deliver the application forms and application list to the Research Office and send the electronic documents in a consolidated submission. Natural sciences materials should be sent to cszhu@stu.edu.cn, while humanities and social sciences materials should be sent to ymhuang@stu.edu.cn. Late submissions will not be accepted.

The contacts for natural sciences are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 82902813. The contacts for humanities and social sciences are Huang Yumei and Wang Liju at 82902563.
