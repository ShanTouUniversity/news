Shantou University’s Department of Biology in the School of Science will hold a biology subject introduction and alumni and student sharing session on the morning of September 8 in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Service Center. All biology students are required to attend, and faculty, staff, and students across the university are welcome.

The session will bring together alumni and a current student from different cohorts to share their experiences. Guests include Xu FX, a 1987 biology alumnus and technical director at Kim Mui Group (Singapore); Li P, a 1999 biology alumnus and lecturer at STU’s Institute of Marine Biology; Lin XM, a 2006 biology alumnus and senior account manager at the Shantou office of Alibaba (China) Network Technology Co., Ltd.; and Wang SJ, a current biology student from the 2009 cohort.
