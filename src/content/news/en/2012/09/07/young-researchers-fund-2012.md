Shantou University’s Research Office has opened applications for the 2012 Young Researchers Fund for relevant units on the main campus. The fund is an exploratory internal fund and generally does not provide repeat funding; each person may receive the university’s Young Researchers Fund only once. Applications must be submitted through the supporting unit rather than directly by individuals.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be in-service, actively employed teachers in natural sciences disciplines appointed by the main campus and must meet all of the following conditions:

- Hold an intermediate or higher professional and technical position, or hold a master’s degree or higher;
- Be no older than 40, inclusive;
- Have not served as principal investigator for a provincial- or ministerial-level or higher research project;
- Be dedicated, responsible, and capable of conducting research independently.

## Materials and Deadline

Applicants must complete the STU Young Researchers Fund Application Form and submit one A4, double-sided printed copy and an electronic version to their unit. Each supporting unit will review applicants’ eligibility and materials and sign its review comments. Units must deliver the printed materials to the Research Office by September 26 and send the electronic version to cszhu@stu.edu.cn. Late submissions will not be accepted.

The contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng, reachable at 82902813.
