Shantou University’s Graduate School has opened online registration for the 2012 second-half National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 for graduate training units on the main campus and all graduate students. Eligible graduate students who wish to take the exams may log in to the Graduate School website from September 8 to 16 and complete registration through the Graduate Management System.

## Registration Procedure

Applicants should enter the Graduate Management System on the Graduate School website, select “Unified Authentication” in the pop-up window, and log in with their account and password. They should then select “Exam Registration” and “I Want to Register” to complete the online process. The Graduate School’s Student Affairs Office will compile the registration information by the end of the morning on September 18. After review and stamping, it will submit the candidates’ registration forms and electronic information to the Student Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office.

The exam schedule, eligibility requirements, payment deduction method, and other instructions are set out in the relevant notice issued by the Academic Affairs Office on the campus office information network. Graduate students should observe the registration deadline and follow the system prompts when submitting their applications.
