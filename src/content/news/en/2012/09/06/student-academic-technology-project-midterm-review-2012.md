Shantou University has begun the midterm review of its 2012 student extracurricular academic and technology projects. The review will assess current progress and interim results across participating units and support the projects’ subsequent implementation.

## Material Submission

Relevant units and project leaders should complete the midterm review and prepare a midterm summary together with the post-review project summary table. Electronic versions must be emailed to the University Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` by 5:00 p.m. on October 8, 2012. Paper copies bearing the official school seal must then be submitted to the committee for filing.

| Material | Submission requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Midterm summary | Email the electronic version by 5:00 p.m. on October 8 |
| Post-review project summary table | Email the electronic version by 5:00 p.m. on October 8; use the attached format |
| Paper materials | Affix the school’s official seal and submit to the University Youth League Committee for filing |
| Submission email | `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` |

The project summary table and other attachments are available on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee)
