The Shantou Youth newspaper has released a 2012 welcome special issue to help new students learn about Shantou University and adapt to campus life. The issue provides information on orientation activities, campus facilities and guest lectures, while also offering channels for the university community to contribute and follow its work.

## Issue Highlights

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Page 1 | An overview of 2012 orientation activities for students beginning campus life |
| Pages 2–3 | A campus guide to frequently visited locations including the Network Center, New Administration Center, library, reservoir and East Gate |
| Page 4 | A look back at guests who have spoken at STU over the previous two years, including *A Bite of China* executive director Ren Changzhen, Taiwanese writer Li Ao, architect Maya Lin and Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine laureate Barry Marshall |

Faculty and students may submit contributions to `stu.shanqing@gmail.com`. Further information is available through Shantou Youth Online (<http://shanqing.stu.edu.cn/>) and its Sina Weibo account (<http://t.sina.com.cn/shandaqingnianbao>).
