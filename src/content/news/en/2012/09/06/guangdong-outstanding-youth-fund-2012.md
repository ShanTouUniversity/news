The Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Management Committee has established the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Outstanding Young Scientists Fund to support the development of outstanding young researchers and cultivate talent with major original innovation capacity. Online applications for the 2012 program will open at 9:00 a.m. on September 10. Eligible STU researchers may apply through the university’s internal process.

## Application Process and Schedule

Applicants must first email their personal information to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn`. After the Research Office opens an application account, applicants may use the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation online application system (<http://gdsf.gdstc.gov.cn/>).

| Milestone | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| September 10, 9:00 a.m. | Online applications open |
| By September 20 | Complete the online application form |
| September 24–28 | The provincial fund office conducts centralized formal review of online applications |
| By October 10 | Download and print the form whose status is “Accepted by the Foundation Committee,” then submit two A4 paper copies and other supporting materials to the Research Office |

Supporting materials to accompany the paper application include original recommendation forms from two senior experts in the relevant field working in Guangdong and an academic-ethics certificate for the applicant; copies of the doctoral degree and professional-title certificates; and evidence including publications, research project contracts or completion documents, science and technology awards, patents and third-party retrieval reports on citations of representative papers. Each applicant may submit only one project in the year.

## Eligibility and Funding Priorities

The applicant must be the project’s actual leader, with one leader only, and the funding period is four years. Applicants must also meet all of the following conditions:

- Be committed to research and uphold sound academic standards and scientific ethics;
- Work in Guangdong and hold a full-time position at a legal-entity organization in the province;
- Be under 35 years old as of July 1, 2012;
- Hold a doctoral degree or an associate senior or higher professional title;
- Have experience leading or participating in a provincial-ministerial or higher-level research project and recommendations from two senior experts in the relevant field in Guangdong.

The 2012 program is expected to focus on six strategic emerging industries: advanced new electronic information, biotechnology, advanced equipment manufacturing, energy conservation and environmental protection, new energy, and new materials. Funding is RMB 1 million per person.

The following applicants are ineligible: recipients of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars or the National Science Fund for Excellent Young Scholars; chief scientists of the National 973 Program, Changjiang Scholars, Pearl River Scholars or recipients of the National Thousand Talents Program; coordinators or principal leaders of key National Natural Science Foundation projects or major national science and technology programs; coordinators of Guangdong Natural Science Foundation research-team projects; and leaders or leading talents of Guangdong introduced innovative research teams.

Applicants are encouraged to complete submissions early to avoid network congestion. For inquiries, contact Hu Yunxiang or Zhu Changsheng at `82902813`. Application documents and related attachments are available on the OA system.
