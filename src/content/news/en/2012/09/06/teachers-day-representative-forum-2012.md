Shantou University will hold a Teachers’ Day forum for faculty representatives on September 7, 2012, inviting relevant representatives from university units to exchange views on faculty work and university development.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 7, 2012, 3:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Conference Room 3, Academic Exchange Center |
| Arrival | Attendees should enter and take their seats 15 minutes early |
| Attendance confirmation deadline | September 7, 2012, 10:00 a.m. |
| Contact | Qiu Saiqing |
| Telephone | 82902317 |
| Email | `o_xzb@stu.edu.cn` |

Units should notify the people listed in the attachment and submit the confirmed attendance list to the Secretariat Section of the Party and Government Office by 10:00 a.m. on September 7. The meeting attachment is available on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
> ⚠️ The faculty representative list and other meeting attachments are available on the OA system.
