Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has announced the schedule for meter reading, review, and payment of student dormitory utility fees during the 2012 summer vacation. The arrangement takes account of changes in occupancy associated with graduating students’ departure, the end of military training for the 2011 cohort, the end of the summer vacation, and the arrival of the 2012 freshmen. Dormitory water and electricity use from early July through the end of August was therefore reviewed in stages.

## Schedule

| Stage | Date | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First meter reading | July 4 | After graduating students completed departure procedures |
| Second meter reading | July 16 | After the 2011 cohort completed military training |
| Third meter reading | August 17 | Before the end of the summer vacation |
| Fourth meter reading | August 31 | Before the 2012 freshmen arrived |
| Fee review | September 6–12 | During office hours on working days |
| All-in-One Card payment deduction | September 13–17 | Regular payment period |

Students may log in to Shantou University’s utility fee management system to check their records using their campus-network accounts. Students who find an error in the charges or have questions about their accommodation records may complete and sign the Utility Fee Review Application Form and submit it to the Water and Electricity Center during office hours on working days from September 6 to 12. The center is located beside the Science and Technology Building and can be reached at 2489. Students who find that their dormitory occupancy does not match the student list may contact the Counselor’s Office in Room 106 of Building B at 2903355.

If no objection is raised by the end of the review period, the university will deduct the relevant fees through the All-in-One Card from September 13. The regular payment period ends on September 17. Late payments will be handled under university regulations on September 18. Because corrections submitted after the review period require coordination among multiple departments, necessary amendments are expected to take three to five working days. Students whose All-in-One Card balance is insufficient are reminded to top it up promptly.
