Shantou University has announced course registration arrangements for its 2012 freshmen. Registration for students at the main campus will begin on September 13, 2012, and proceed in three stages: preliminary selection, re-selection and adjustments.

## Registration Schedule

| Stage | Time | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary selection | September 13, 9:00 a.m. to September 14, 4:30 p.m. | Students may select classes beyond available degree capacity; after the stage, over-capacity classes will be reduced by random deletion, applying only to 2012 freshmen |
| Re-selection | September 15, 9:00 a.m. to September 16, 5:00 p.m. | First come, first served within class capacity |
| Course adjustments | September 17, 9:00 a.m. to September 21, 5:00 p.m. | Students may adjust courses; those unable to make changes themselves may submit a course-change request |

After the adjustment period ends, the Academic Affairs Office will formally publish the class opening plan, and students’ course selections will no longer be changed.

## Textbook Orders

Because textbooks require advance ordering, the university has already ordered first-semester books for freshmen. These include general required courses such as public English, public mathematics, computer fundamentals, integrated thinking and ideological and political education, as well as most major-required courses. A corresponding textbook order will be placed by default when a freshman selects a course. The textbook order list will be based on the course registration list at 5:00 p.m. on September 16; later registration adjustments will not affect textbook orders.
