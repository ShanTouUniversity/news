Shantou University will hold a meeting on its 2012 teaching quality report on September 7, 2012. The meeting will address requirements for universities to publish annual teaching quality reports and organize STU’s related work. Deans of all schools and heads of all departments and offices are expected to attend.

## Meeting Details

The meeting will begin at 9:00 a.m. on Friday, September 7, in Conference Room 205 of the Science and Technology Report Hall.

The agenda includes a briefing by Lu Xiaohua, director of the Academic Affairs Office, on the Guangdong directors of academic affairs meeting and its notice regarding annual teaching quality reports; a mobilization address by Executive President Gu Peihua on preparing STU’s annual teaching quality report; and a work deployment by Vice President Lin Danming for the 2012 STU teaching quality report.
