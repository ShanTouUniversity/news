To help 2012 freshmen learn about the library and make effective use of its resources and services, the Shantou University Library has uploaded materials from its library orientation sessions held on September 5. The sessions were held in the Grand Auditorium for undergraduates and in the Science and Technology Report Hall for graduate students.

## Accessing the Materials

Faculty and students may visit the library homepage and select “Services and Guides” followed by “Library Resource Use Guide,” or open <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/accessory/2012> directly to download and view the materials.

The materials are available to new students and other interested members of the university community.
