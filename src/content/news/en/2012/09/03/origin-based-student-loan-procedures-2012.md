It is reported that 109 Shantou University students had applied for home-region credit-based student loans for the 2012–2013 academic year as of September 3, 2012. The Student Affairs Office reminds applicants whose contract status is “Receipt pending university entry” to complete the next stage of the process by the stated deadline.

## Procedure Details

Eligible students must bring their Home-Region Credit-Based Student Loan Acceptance Certificate and campus card to the Student Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office by September 20. The center is located in Room 106 of the New Administration Center.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | 109 students as of September 3 |
| Eligible applicants | Applicants whose contract status is “Receipt pending university entry” |
| Deadline | September 20, 2012 |
| Required documents | Home-Region Credit-Based Student Loan Acceptance Certificate and campus card |
| Location | Room 106, New Administration Center; Student Aid Management Center |

The detailed contract-status list and related attachment are available on the OA system; students do not need to disclose personal loan information on a public page.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The home-region student loan contract-status attachment is available on the OA system.
