It is reported that Shantou University’s Youth League Committee and Student Union will hold a showcase of university-level student organizations on September 4, 2012. The event will introduce freshmen to campus culture and student organizations, helping them choose activities that match their interests.

## Event Information

The showcase will take place in the Library Lecture Hall from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on September 4 and is open to all freshmen. It will give university-level student organizations an opportunity to present themselves and help new students learn about their work and activities.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | September 4, 2012, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Audience | All freshmen |
| Organizers | STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Union |

Freshmen are welcome to attend and learn about student organizations that suit their interests.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
