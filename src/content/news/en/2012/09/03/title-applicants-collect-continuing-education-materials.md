It is reported that the Human Resources Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice arranging the collection of continuing education documents for 2010 and 2011 by staff members applying for professional titles.

The materials are intended for faculty and staff preparing title applications. Continuing education records serve as an important basis for professional title applications, and eligible staff are expected to collect their documents promptly in accordance with the office's arrangements to ensure a smooth application process.

Details such as collection times, locations, and required procedures can be found in the original notice on the university's OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
