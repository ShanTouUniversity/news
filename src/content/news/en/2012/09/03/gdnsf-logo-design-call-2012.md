It is reported that the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Administration Committee is inviting logo designs that reflect its cultural identity and the character of basic research in Guangdong. Shantou University’s Research Office forwarded the call and shared the submission arrangements with relevant university units.

## Design Requirements

The call is open from September 1 to October 15, 2012. Designs should have a clear theme, a concise and classic form, strong visual appeal, and broad usability. They should remain clear at different sizes and be suitable for various publicity media. Each submission must be original and carry independent, lawful intellectual property rights.

The creator of a selected design will receive RMB 2,000 and an honorary certificate. Copyright and permanent exclusive property rights in submitted works will belong to the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Administration Committee, which may modify, combine, and develop the works for different applications.

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Submission | Logo design for the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Administration Committee |
| Deadline | October 15, 2012 |
| Design sheet | One color design printed on A4 paper |
| Concept statement | No more than 800 Chinese characters, covering the image, technical details, and design concept |
| Award | RMB 2,000 and an honorary certificate for the selected creator |

Participants should complete the Application Form for the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Administration Committee Logo Design Call, email the electronic version to `cszhu@stu.edu.cn`, and submit one color-printed paper copy to STU’s Research Office. The university contact is Zhu Changsheng, reachable at 82902813. The application form and other event attachments are available on the OA system.
