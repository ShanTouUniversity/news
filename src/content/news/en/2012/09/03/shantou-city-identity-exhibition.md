It is reported that the exhibition "Process and Methodology — An American Student's Design Proposal for Shantou City Identity" will be held from September 4 to 18, 2012, at the gallery of the Cheung Kong Institute of Art & Design (CKAD), Shantou University, presenting the full process of a city identity project for Shantou, from research to final proposal.

## Background of the Project

The project is the independent-study graduation work of Zhou C, an undergraduate graphic design student at the Art Center College of Design (ACCD) in Pasadena, who was born in mainland China and grew up in the United States. Under the guidance of Wang Shouzhi, dean of CKAD, she completed the project over a 12-week semester and held her graduation show at ACCD in August 2012.

Explaining why Shantou was chosen as the design subject, Wang cited three reasons:

- He works at CKAD himself and knows the city well;
- As a relatively underdeveloped medium-sized Chinese city, Shantou has barely begun its city identity design work, leaving great potential space;
- The institute's research on city identity and corporate branding only started in 2012, so this project could provide a systematic model for that new direction.

## From Process to Methodology

According to the introduction, ACCD was founded in 1930 and leads the United States and the world in fields such as industrial design, transportation design, graphic design, environmental design, photography, film and entertainment design. Its approach to urban and corporate identity follows a highly standard practice: market surveys, independent data collection and comparison, studying a city's characteristics, traditions and problems, forming concepts, proposing solutions, extending them into visual system designs, and finalizing after feedback.

Wang said he hopes the exhibition will have a direct impact on CKAD's teaching in city identity and corporate branding, and expects it to help build consensus with relevant officials of the Shantou municipal government, ultimately leading to cooperation between the two schools and the city to create an internationally competitive city image for Shantou.
