It is reported that following evaluation and recommendation by the Shantou University Bidding Leadership Group, the winning bid for the Nan'ao Coastal Experimental Station renovation project is now under public notice from September 3 to 5.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Project | Nan'ao Coastal Experimental Station Phase II Renovation, Shantou University |
| Winning Bidder | Nan'ao Youye Building Decoration Co., Ltd. |
| Winning Amount | RMB 262,843.00 (including a provisional sum of RMB 12,516.00 under full control of the employer) |
| Public Notice Period | September 3–5, 2012 |
| Tendering Entity | STU Infrastructure Office |
| Contacts | Mr. Chen, Mr. Lu |
