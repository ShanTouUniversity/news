It is reported that Shantou University has launched its 2012 Outstanding Freshman Scholarship selection. The program uses freshman applications and expert evaluation and is open to full-time undergraduate freshmen who applied to STU as their first choice, were admitted, and enroll on time.

## Eligibility and Awards

Freshmen may apply if they meet at least one of the following conditions: their gaokao score, excluding policy-based bonus points, is at least 30 points above the first-batch admission cutoff in their province or autonomous region; they received a third prize or higher in a national or international competition such as an Olympiad, youth innovation competition, science and engineering competition, or biology and environmental science practice competition during high school; or they hold a National Grade II Athlete title or higher and have met the relevant standard in the unified test organized by a provincial admissions authority.

| Award | Places and amount |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | Five awards per year, RMB 20,000 per recipient |
| Second prize | 25 awards per year, RMB 5,000 per recipient |
| Outstanding Female Freshman from Outside Guangdong Award Plan | 25 awards, RMB 5,000 per recipient; no student application required |

The scholarships cannot be combined. The Outstanding Female Freshman from Outside Guangdong Award Plan will be evaluated according to the year’s admissions situation, scholarship donations and use plan, and selection principles.

## Selection Process

Applicants must log in to the Student Affairs System at `http://sao.stu.edu.cn` by noon on September 5 to submit and print a signed online application. By the same deadline, they must submit the application form and supporting materials, including original and photocopied award certificates and portfolios, to the Student Affairs Office in Room 106 of the New Administration Building. Original award certificates must be available for verification.

The university will assess applicants’ academic performance and overall qualities through preliminary selection and interviews before determining the awardees. Interviews are tentatively scheduled for September 11, with detailed arrangements to follow. The application form and related material templates are available on the OA system.
