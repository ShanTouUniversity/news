Shantou University's administrative units have moved to the new Administration Building under a university-wide arrangement. To coordinate the use of the former offices and return them to service promptly, the property and equipment offices will recover keys and equipment from September 17 to 19 in stages. The Equipment Office will also inventory and transfer furniture, air conditioners, and other items that have not yet been moved, and recheck equipment transferred to the new administrative center.

Units should prepare equipment inventories in advance. If a scheduled time is inconvenient, they should contact Teacher Yan at the Property Office by this Friday at office telephone 2494 so that an individual adjustment can be arranged.
