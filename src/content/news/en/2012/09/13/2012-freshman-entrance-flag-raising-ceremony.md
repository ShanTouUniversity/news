Shantou University will hold a flag-raising ceremony for the 2012 freshman class on Monday, September 17. All 2012 freshmen, including graduate students, should arrive at the National Flag Platform by the University Motto Monument at 6:10 a.m. to form their lines. The ceremony will begin at 6:30 a.m.

Participants should dress neatly and assemble on time. Each college and residential college should bring its flag. Freshmen should also bring a pen to sign the oath and, after the ceremony, submit the signed oath in an orderly manner to the collection point designated at the venue. Each unit should count attendance and submit the final figure to the University's Youth League Committee.

## Ceremony Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Begins at 6:30 a.m., Monday, September 17, 2012 |
| Assembly time | 6:10 a.m. |
| Venue | National Flag Platform by the University Motto Monument |
| Participants | All 2012 freshmen, including graduate students |
| Organizers | Communist Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office, Shantou University |

## Program

The program will include the announcement of the opening, the national anthem and flag-raising, the freshman oath, a speech by university leaders, and the announcement of the closing.
