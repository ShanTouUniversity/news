The second-half 2012 National Computer Rank Examination will be held on Saturday, September 22. Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has released the written and computer-based test arrangements and examination requirements. Candidates should follow the information on their admission cards.

The written test begins at 9 a.m.; the room assignments are listed in the OA attachment. Computer-based tests begin at noon and will be conducted in batches in Room E102, with each candidate's time determined by the admission card. Computer-test seat numbers will be assigned by random draw. Candidates taking the computer test should gather in Room E204 30 minutes before their test to draw their seat numbers.

## Examination Formats

For 2012 freshmen taking the Level 2 VB, VFP, or ACCESS examinations, the paperless format replaces the traditional format. Candidates taking make-up examinations in these three subjects will continue to use the traditional format. In the paperless format, the written-test component is completed on a computer, while the content and requirements remain unchanged. The test lasts 130 minutes for 100 points: 40 points for multiple-choice questions and 60 points for practical computer tasks. A passing certificate requires at least 60 total points and at least 36 points on the practical tasks; no separate subject pass or score-retention arrangement applies to paperless subjects. Other Level 2 subjects will continue to use a combination of written and computer-based tests.

## Candidate Requirements

Candidates must bring both their identity cards and admission cards and place the documents at the upper-left corner of their desks for inspection. Candidates without complete documents will not be admitted. Candidates arriving more than 15 minutes late for the written test may not enter; candidates may leave only after 45 minutes of testing and may not return after leaving. Late candidates may not enter once the computer test has officially started.

Written-test candidates must bring a 2B pencil, eraser, and a blue- or black-ink ballpoint or signing pen. Books, communication devices, and other items are prohibited in the examination room; violations result in disqualification. STU undergraduates may collect admission cards from the academic affairs staff in their departments. Other candidates should present their identity cards at the Student Services counter of the Academic Affairs Office. The inquiry telephone number is 82902402.
