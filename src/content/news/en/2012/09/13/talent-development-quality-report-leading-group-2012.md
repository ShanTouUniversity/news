Shantou University's ninth leadership meeting of 2012, held on September 4, decided to establish a leading group for the university's annual talent development quality report and a working office affiliated with the Academic Affairs Office. The organization will coordinate and advance work related to the report on talent development quality.

The leading group is headed by Gu Peihua, with Lin Danming as deputy head. Its members are Wang Kanghua, Wang Shouzhi, Sun Fenqiang, Chen Yan, Chen Jian, Chen Shaogang, Chen Wenbin, Chen Zhulin, He Wenbiao, Huai Xiaofeng, Li *Ping (one character appears garbled in the original notice), Wu Ping, Lu Xiaohua, Yang Mianhua, Zou Zhibo, Fan Dongsheng, Lin Bingfeng, Bai Youjin, Hu Zhong, Lou Zengjian, Xu Zongling, Yu Hong, Zeng Rui, Zeng Dabin, Liao Xuequan, Donald Snow, Michael Palmer, and Terry Bodenhorn.

The working office will be directed by Cai Yinghui and Lin Feng. Its members are Wang * (one character appears garbled in the original notice), Ye Chengzhou, Jiang Songqi, Jiang Wenlin, Yang Weitao, Yang Qingjie, Yang Tao, Chen Yichun, Li Jing, Li Lanqing, Li Zhiwei, Li Shengzhi, Zhang Lingling, Zhang Jingping, Xin Qiting, Zheng Shaoyan, Zheng Shunqin, Lin Lushui, Luo Feijun, Bai Sheng, Hong Yinghua, Xu Lifang, Weng Guinan, Huang Rugan, Xie Yuanfang, Han Baocheng, and Cai Lin. Names are presented in the order of the original notice; the garbled entries have not been reconstructed speculatively.
