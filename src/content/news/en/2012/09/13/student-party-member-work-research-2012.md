Following a research arrangement from the Guangdong Provincial Committee of Education Work, Shantou University's Organization and United Front Work Department has launched a study of student Party member development, education, administration, and support. The study aims to review current practices and experience, identify shortcomings, and develop targeted recommendations for improving student Party member work in higher education.

The Party general branches of each college and residential college, together with the other relevant units, should prepare research reports based on their circumstances. Reports must contain at least 3,000 Chinese characters and be submitted to the University's Organization and United Front Work Department in Room 315 of the New Administration Center by the end of the working morning on September 18. Reports may be emailed to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. The contact person is Ji Rongxin, at 82902345.

## Research Topics and Responsibilities

| Research area | Responsible units |
| --- | --- |
| Practices and experience in student Party member development, education, administration, and support | Party general branches of all colleges and residential colleges |
| Motivations for joining the Party, and the quantity and quality of development | Party general branches of all colleges and residential colleges |
| Relevance of education and training and quality of organizational life | Party general branches of all colleges and residential colleges |
| Administration of Party members who defer employment after graduation or study abroad | Direct Party branch for graduates with deferred employment; college and residential-college Party general branches and Office of International Exchange and Cooperation as supporting units |
| Support mechanisms for financially disadvantaged and vulnerable student Party members | Student Affairs Office; college and residential-college Party general branches as supporting units |
| Countermeasures and recommendations | Party general branches of colleges and residential colleges, direct Party branch for graduates with deferred employment, and Student Affairs Office |
