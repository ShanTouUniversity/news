It is reported that Shantou University issued an appointment decision on September 13, 2012, appointing Yan Zhigang as vice dean of the Law School for a six-month probationary period.

## Appointment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Position | Vice dean of the Law School |
| Appointee | Yan Zhigang |
| Probationary period | Six months |
| Decision basis | Decision of the ninth university leadership meeting on September 4, 2012 |

The decision was issued by Shantou University for implementation by all university units.
