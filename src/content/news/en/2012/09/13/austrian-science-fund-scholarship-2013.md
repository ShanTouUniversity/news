The China Scholarship Council and the Austrian Science Fund (Fonds zur Förderung der wissenschaftlichen Forschung, FWF) have launched a cooperation program for 2013 scholarships. The program plans to select 30 outstanding students and scholars each year to pursue doctoral degrees, undertake joint doctoral training, or conduct postdoctoral research through FWF-funded projects and research teams in Austria.

The program is open to applicants in all disciplines. It is the first talent-development cooperation program signed between China's Ministry of Education and Austria. Established by the Austrian government and primarily funded by the Austrian Ministry of Science, the FWF supports basic research and international research cooperation.

## Funding and Application

For selected participants, the China Scholarship Council will cover one round-trip international airfare, visa fees, and the first year's scholarship living allowance. FWF will cover the registration fee during the first year. From the second year onward, the host research team will provide the scholarship living allowance, including medical and related social insurance, through an employment arrangement at the Austrian rate. The program provides 14 months of salary per year, while registration fees are paid by the participant.

Applicants should first contact a relevant Austrian research project or team and obtain an invitation letter. They should then apply during the next annual application period for China's government-sponsored study-abroad programs. Detailed program information and doctoral and postdoctoral openings provided by the Austrian side are available through the China Scholarship Council website.
