Shantou University's Institute of Local Government Development Research is inviting submissions from the public for Volume Seven of Local Government Development Research. The publication will present theoretical and practical work in local government development. Topics include urban-rural cultural development, the characteristics of Chaoshan culture, innovation in cultural-industry creativity and management systems, and government cultural policy.

Submissions must be unpublished. Authors should send Word documents as email attachments to 10swzhang1@stu.edu.cn. The email body should include the author's name and telephone number and state “Submission to Volume Seven of Local Government Development Research.” The deadline is December 15, 2012.

## Manuscript Requirements

Each manuscript should include a concise title, an abstract of no more than 300 Chinese characters, and three to five keywords. Notes should appear as footnotes. References must be formally published sources accurately cited in the manuscript and numbered in order of citation. Headings should follow the call's formatting requirements. The specified fonts are boldface SimSun in size three for titles, SimSun in size five for the main text, and SimSun in small size five for footnotes. Authors should also provide a mailing address, telephone number, and email address, and mark author names with corresponding indicators for the biographical notes in the footnotes.
