Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has reported on the submission of course grades for the 2012 spring semester. Its review found that most instructors had submitted grades through the credit-system platform. The Christian Studies Center of the College of Liberal Arts had still not submitted grades for “Death and Immortality: An Exploration of Eastern and Western Philosophical and Religious Traditions” despite repeated reminders from relevant college staff and leaders.

The class number for the course is 52185. Under the relevant provisions of Shantou University's rules for identifying undergraduate teaching incidents, failure to enter or report examination grades as required and continued failure to submit them five days after a reminder constitutes an ordinary teaching incident. The Academic Affairs Office issued the report and reminded teaching units and instructors to follow the course assessment rules and maintain orderly teaching.
