To better distribute dining traffic, Hongtai Restaurant on the second floor of Shantou University's Fourth Cafeteria has adjusted its weekday lunch arrangement since September 1. From 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. Monday through Friday, the restaurant is reserved for university staff. During that period, only staff cards can be used for Campus Card payment; student cards and temporary cards are restricted.

At other times, Hongtai Restaurant remains open to all students, faculty, and staff. Members of the university community should plan their meals according to the applicable service period.

## Service Arrangement

| Period | Eligible diners | Payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday–Friday, 10 a.m.–1 p.m. | University staff | Staff cards only |
| Other times | All members of the university community | Normal payment arrangements |
