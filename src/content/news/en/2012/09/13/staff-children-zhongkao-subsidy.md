It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University has begun accepting applications for the 2012 subsidy rewarding the high school entrance exam (Zhongkao) results of faculty and staff children. Employees whose children studied in the Shantou urban area and took this year's Zhongkao are eligible to apply.

## Required Materials and Deadline

Applicants should prepare the following materials and deliver them to the Resource Management Office by September 25:

| Material | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Exam admission ticket | Original and photocopy |
| Admission notice | Original and photocopy |
| Exam score report | Original and photocopy |
| Employee information | Name and workplace unit indicated |

The office can be reached at 82902827 for inquiries about the application.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
