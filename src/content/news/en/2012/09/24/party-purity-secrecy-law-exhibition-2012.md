To support Shantou University’s Discipline Education Month organized by the Party Committee, the Commission for Discipline Inspection Office, Publicity Department and Party and Administrative Office are jointly presenting an exhibition on “Maintaining the Purity of the Party” and “Publicity and Education on Secrecy Laws.” The exhibition is intended for faculty and students, with Party committees and directly affiliated Party branches organizing visits for their members and students.

## Exhibition Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Themes | “Maintaining the Purity of the Party” and “Publicity and Education on Secrecy Laws” |
| Dates | September 24 to October 31, 2012 |
| Location | Trade Union publicity board beside the post office |
| Organizers | Commission for Discipline Inspection Office, Publicity Department, and Party and Administrative Office |
| Audience | University faculty and students |

The Party purity section covers the ideological purity of Party members and officials, the purity of the membership, conduct, integrity, internal Party supervision and strict discipline. The secrecy education section focuses on the Law of the People’s Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets and confidentiality guidance for computers and office automation equipment.
