Shantou University's General Office and Human Resources Office announced the composition of the funeral committee for Luo Lie on September 24, 2012. The committee comprises relevant officials from Shantou University and Renmin University's School of Journalism and will coordinate related funeral arrangements.

## Committee Members

| Role | Member and position |
| --- | --- |
| Chairpersons | Xu Xiaohu, president of Shantou University; Song *Zhen, secretary of the CPC Committee of Shantou University |
| Vice chairpersons | Luo Nianchao, deputy secretary of the CPC Committee of Shantou University; Wulan Hasi, vice president of Shantou University; Ni Ning, executive dean of Renmin University's School of Journalism; Gao Gang, secretary of the CPC Committee of Renmin University's School of Journalism |
| Members | He Wenbiao, director of the General Office of Shantou University; Zeng Dabin, director of the University's Organization and United Front Work Department; Li Haotao, deputy director of the University's General Office; Liu Mingqiang, deputy director of the University's Human Resources Office; Zhang Jingyuan, secretary of the Party General Branch for Retired Faculty and Staff; Fan Dongsheng, acting dean of the University's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication; Luo Jianhui, deputy secretary of the CPC Committee of Renmin University's School of Journalism; Jiang Qiurong, chair of the labor union of Renmin University's School of Journalism; Yan Fang, director of the Party, League, and Student Affairs Office of Renmin University's School of Journalism |

The committee's contact numbers are `0754-82902660` and `0754-88626402`. One character in Song *Zhen's name appears garbled in the original notice; it has not been reconstructed speculatively here.
