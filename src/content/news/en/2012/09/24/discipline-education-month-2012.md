The CPC Committee of Shantou University has announced the program for its 2012 Discipline Education Month. Running from September 26 to October 26, the program will focus on “strengthening moral development and maintaining the Party's integrity,” with activities covering Party conduct and discipline, anti-corruption education, professional ethics for officials and educators, and confidentiality laws and regulations.

## Program Activities

The university will hold a mobilization meeting for all faculty and staff and student Party members on the afternoon of September 26, followed by a screening of *Loyalty and Betrayal*, a film produced by the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection. The university leadership will conduct a special CPC Committee theoretical study session on the theme, while primary-level Party organizations will arrange special study sessions and educational film screenings. The Youth League Committee will organize themed activities and discussions on academic conduct under the theme “Strengthen Academic Conduct for Better Growth and Success” across schools and residential colleges.

The Discipline Inspection Office will arrange an integrity guidance lecture for leaders of key departments and personnel in critical positions. It will also organize faculty and students to participate in an online essay activity hosted by the Shantou Discipline Inspection Commission and other organizations, as well as the city's second Integrity Reading Month. A weekly email campaign will send one integrity reminder each week to department- and section-level leaders. The office will also work with relevant departments on special exhibitions about maintaining Party integrity and confidentiality laws, organize a seminar on integrity-risk prevention in construction projects, and publish program bulletins. The Supervision and Audit Office's Qingfeng Songyuan website will open a dedicated section.

The program is intended for all faculty, staff, and students, with particular emphasis on all Party members, leaders at different levels, personnel in departments with concentrated authority, and staff in key positions involving people, finances, and materials. The university will also strengthen education on professional ethics, teaching and academic conduct, academic integrity, student integrity cultivation, and integrity-risk prevention.

## Organization and Reporting

The university has established a Discipline Education Month leading group chaired by Song *Zhen, secretary of the CPC Committee of Shantou University. Xu Xiaohu, deputy secretary of the CPC Committee and president, and Luo Nianchao, deputy secretary of the CPC Committee and secretary of the Discipline Inspection Commission, will serve as deputy chairs. The office is located in the university Discipline Inspection Office. The office can be reached at `0754-82902346` or `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`.

All units and Party organizations must report activity information promptly. Leaders at section level and above must submit study reflections by October 30, and Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches must submit written summaries and activity statistics by the same date. The university asked each unit to adapt the activities to its circumstances and turn the results of the education program into actions that improve work and address practical problems.
