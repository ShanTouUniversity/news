Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) has selected some duty staff applicants for interviews based on registrations through September 23 and the center’s staffing needs. The interviews will be held at SLC on Wednesday, September 26.

## Interview Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Interview date | Wednesday, September 26 |
| Venue | Student Learning Center (SLC) |
| Participants | Duty staff applicants selected for interview |

Selected applicants should arrive on time according to the notice. As the interview list contains students’ personal information, it is not reproduced here. Applicants with questions may contact SLC at `82901482` or `slc@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Student Affairs)
