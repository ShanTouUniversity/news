Shantou University's Finance Office, Discipline Inspection Office, Supervision and Audit Office, and Research Office have jointly launched a self-inspection of research project fund management, according to a university notice. The initiative responds to national and Guangdong requirements for strengthening the management of university research funds.

## Review Arrangements

All project leaders at the main campus are required to review every research project they have undertaken since 2009 against the requirements of the Guangdong Discipline Inspection Commission's *Notice on Further Strengthening the Management of Research Project Funds* (Yue Ji Fa [2012] No. 27) and related documents. They must complete the *Research Project Fund Management Self-Inspection Report* and submit it to the research secretary of their unit by the end of September 25, 2012. Units will consolidate the reports and forward them to the Research Office.

Inquiries may be directed to Xie Yuanfang at `0754-82902583`.
