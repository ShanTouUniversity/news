Shantou University’s Office of Research has forwarded the National Intellectual Property Administration notice on the Seventh China Design Patent Competition and is inviting entries from students and faculty across the university.

Eligible entries must meet both of the following conditions: they must be Chinese design patents authorized by September 20, 2012, or Chinese design patent applications filed by that date; and they must not have previously received an award in a China design patent competition.

## Registration Arrangements

| Item | Time or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Contact the Office of Research | By September 29 |
| Register with the competition organizing committee | By October 15 |
| Filing requirement | Submit the application materials to the Office of Research for record at the time of registration |
| Contacts | Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong |
| Telephone | 82903271 |
| Email | `dengyong@stu.edu.cn` |

Potential entrants should prepare their applications in accordance with the competition notice and complete the university contact, committee registration and filing steps by the applicable deadlines. The detailed requirements and application documents are available in the attachments to the OA notice.
