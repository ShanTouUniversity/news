Shantou University's School of Science will hold an open midterm review defense for its student science and technology projects at 14:30 on September 26, 2012, in the Multimedia Lecture Hall (Li-352) of the School of Science. Projects will be presented in the listed order, and each team must prepare a five-minute presentation with supporting PowerPoint materials.

## Submission Requirements

Every approved project must submit a written midterm review form. By 17:00 on September 25, project teams must deliver two paper copies, including the supervisor's comments and signature, to the School of Science Youth League Committee Office and email an electronic copy to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn`.

A project that fails to submit its midterm report as required will be considered to have withdrawn automatically, and its initial project funding will be reclaimed. Faculty members and students of the School of Science are welcome to attend.
