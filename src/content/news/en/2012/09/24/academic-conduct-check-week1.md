Shantou University's Academic Conduct Development Office has released Bulletin No. 206 on inspections of academic conduct during the first week of the 2012–2013 fall semester. Inspection team members checked teaching buildings on September 17 and 18, recording 11 instances of class lateness in total.

## Inspection Records

| Date and time | Location | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| September 17, 08:00 | Teaching Building E | 5 | 1 |
| September 18, 14:00 | Third floor, Teaching Buildings D and G | 1 | 4 |

The office asked each school and residential college to provide criticism and education to the students concerned and continue its academic conduct initiatives. The notice's attachment contains the relevant student list, which is not reproduced here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Conduct Development Office)
