Applications have opened for the 2012 Guangdong Soft Science Research Program joint projects, and Shantou University's Research Office has issued campus arrangements for applicants. The joint program is being piloted through Guangdong University of Foreign Studies' Guangdong Institute for International Strategies, the first Guangdong key research base for soft science, and is jointly established by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and the institute for implementation across the province. It aims to advance research on the internationalization strategy of science and technology in Guangdong and develop related talent. Eight projects are available this year; the project list is included in the OA attachments.

## Eligibility and Schedule

Applicants should be familiar with a relevant soft science research field and have achieved research results in that field. They should, in principle, hold an associate senior or higher professional title. Applicants without a senior title must be recommended by two peer experts who hold full senior professional titles. Applicants must also ensure sufficient time for substantive research, lead overall project implementation, and organize the research team. In the same year, a project leader or team member may submit or join only one new joint project; anyone with an ongoing joint project may not apply for another one.

Projects should normally be completed within one year, except where special requirements apply. Applicants must complete the *Guangdong Soft Science Research Program Joint Project Application Form* and submit seven paper copies of the form and supporting materials to the Research Office by October 16, 2012. An electronic copy should be sent to `yxhu@stu.edu.cn`. Contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng, at `0754-82902813`.
