According to a notice from Shantou University's Network and Information Center, a fault occurred on the main fiber-optic line of the provincial education network from 11:00 on September 24, 2012. During the outage, users on campus could not access education-network resources, while users outside the campus could not access campus-network resources.

## Impact and Response

The Network and Information Center said it was monitoring the fault and taking contingency measures. No definite restoration time was provided, and no separate restoration notice would be issued. Users may contact the center at `0754-82903413-100` or `stunic@stu.edu.cn` for information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
