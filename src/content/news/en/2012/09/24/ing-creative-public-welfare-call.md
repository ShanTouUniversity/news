Supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the InG public welfare project has been piloted at Shantou University since late 2010 and has opened a call for creative topics for the new semester. The project encourages students to identify production and daily-life challenges in society, especially in rural communities, that could potentially be addressed through innovative product solutions.

## Progress and Recognition

Over the past two years, the project has involved 37 multidisciplinary teams at STU and produced 18 public welfare designs. Six of those designs recently received national patent authorization. The project team will review the submitted questions, publish selected topics, and use some of them as research and development directions for new-semester teams.

Contributors to selected topics may receive recognition. If a topic is developed into a design and successfully receives project funding and a patent application, its contributor may share inventor honors with the developer. Contributors may also be selected as “Campus Public Welfare Innovators,” receive an interview, and share their experiences in an influential public welfare magazine. Further information on team recruitment will be announced separately.
