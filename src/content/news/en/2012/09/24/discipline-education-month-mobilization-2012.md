Shantou University’s Party and Administrative Office and Commission for Discipline Inspection Office will hold a mobilization meeting for the 2012 Discipline Education Month at 15:00 on September 26 in the University Auditorium. The meeting is for all university employees and student Party members. Its agenda includes mobilization for the month-long discipline education program and a screening of the film *Loyalty and Betrayal*.

## Attendance Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting time | 15:00, Wednesday, September 26 |
| Venue | University Auditorium |
| Attendees | All university employees and student Party members |
| Arrival deadline | 14:50 |
| Program | Discipline Education Month mobilization and screening of *Loyalty and Betrayal* |

Party organizations at all levels should organize faculty Party members and student Party members to attend on time. Attendees should sit in the central section of the auditorium according to the seating chart in the attachment; the side sections will not be assigned. Mobile phones should be switched off or placed on vibrate during the meeting.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office and Commission for Discipline Inspection Office)
>
> ⚠️ The auditorium seating chart and other meeting attachments are available on the original notice in the OA system.
