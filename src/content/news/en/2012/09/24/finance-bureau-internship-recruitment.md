The Shantou Municipal Financial Affairs Bureau plans to recruit four interns from Shantou University's Business School because of its staffing needs, according to a university notice. Eligible applicants are graduate students and undergraduates in the 2009 class at the Business School. The internship is scheduled to begin on October 10, 2012, and last three months.

## Position and Application

Applicants should have strong writing skills and a basic understanding of current economic development and economic reform. Applicants must submit the *Shantou Municipal Financial Affairs Bureau Intern Information Form* and an *Internship Work Plan* to the Business School Youth League Committee by 17:00 on October 8, 2012. The bureau and the Business School will select the interns from the applications and notify those selected about pre-internship training. Applicants who are not notified should not attend the training.

The pre-internship training is scheduled for 15:00 on October 9 in the Business School Seminar Room on the second floor of Wenxi Building. Interns will receive an appropriate labor subsidy from the bureau, while other arrangements will follow the relevant rules for the Business School's student internship base. For inquiries, contact the Business School at `0754-82902725`.
