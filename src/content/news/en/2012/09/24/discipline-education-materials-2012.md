To support the 2012 Discipline Education Month, the Education Commission for Discipline Inspection and the Publicity and Education Office of the Guangdong Commission for Discipline Inspection have jointly published the 2012 edition of the *Anti-Corruption Education Reader for the Guangdong Education System*. Shantou University’s Commission for Discipline Inspection Office will distribute the materials to relevant Party organizations and officials on campus.

## Collection Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | Party committees, directly affiliated Party branches, officials at or above the division level, and section-level officials working in personnel, finance, materials and related areas |
| Collection deadline | By 17:00 on September 29 |
| Location | Room 422, New Administration Center, Commission for Discipline Inspection Office |
| Collection method | Relevant units should send a representative to collect the materials |

The Commission for Discipline Inspection Office also has anti-corruption education videos covering exemplary figures and analyses of negative cases. Units may borrow the videos from the office and organize screenings if needed. The main study materials for the Discipline Education Month are provided in the attachments to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Commission for Discipline Inspection Office)
>
> ⚠️ The anti-corruption reader, education video materials and other study attachments are available on the original notice in the OA system.
