Students from the Shantou University Affiliated Primary School represented Jinping District at the 2012 Shantou Youth Fencing Championship and placed second in the team standings. According to the school, the delegation also won four individual titles across multiple weapons and age groups.

## Winning Events

| Group | Weapon | First-place finishes |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Group B | Épée | 1 |
| Group C | Épée | 2 |
| Group C | Foil | 1 |

All the winners were students at the affiliated primary school. Their names are not published here to protect the privacy of minors.
