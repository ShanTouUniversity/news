Shantou University's Office of Research Affairs is conducting a midterm review of 2012 Research Startup Fund projects under the University's Research Startup Fund Management Measures. The list of project leaders included in the review is provided in Attachment 1 issued by the office.

## Material Submission

Relevant project leaders must complete the *Shantou University Research Startup Fund Project Midterm Review Report* and submit three printed copies of the report and one copy of supporting evidence to the Office of Research Affairs by October 21. Electronic copies must also be submitted: natural-science projects should be sent to `yxhu@stu.edu.cn`, and humanities and social-science projects to `ymhuang@stu.edu.cn`.

After the midterm review, the University will decide whether to release the remaining funds based on experts' assessment of project progress. For projects that do not pass the review, the University may suspend further disbursement or withdraw funding, depending on the circumstances.

The contact for natural-science projects is Hu Yunxiang at 82902813; the contact for humanities and social-science projects is Huang Yumei at 82902563.
