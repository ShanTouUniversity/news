Shantou University's Office of Research Affairs has begun the completion process for 2012 Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects in accordance with a notice from the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Committee and relevant management rules. The detailed list of projects requiring completion is provided in the attachment. Project leaders must follow the acceptance procedure for their project type.

## Acceptance Methods

Team projects are expected to undergo a meeting-based assessment organized by the Provincial Foundation Office in mid-to-late November; further arrangements will be announced separately. Key projects will undergo a meeting-based acceptance review. Their leaders must submit the proposed review time, venue, and preliminary expert list to the Office of Research Affairs by October 26, after which the Provincial Foundation Office will conduct spot checks. General projects, including freely proposed and doctoral-startup projects, will be reviewed by correspondence.

Acceptance experts must be peer specialists with senior professional titles or above. No more than one expert may come from the same institution as the project leader, and experts must come from different institutions; different secondary units within the University are treated as the same institution. General projects require three acceptance experts, while key projects require five.

## Timeline

| Stage | Dates | Work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online submission | October 10 to November 10 | Project leaders enter and submit completion documents online |
| Internal review | November 11 to 18 | The Office of Research Affairs conducts a formal review |
| Provincial review | November 19 to 23 | The Provincial Foundation Committee reviews the materials |
| Printed-material submission | November 26 to 30 | Project leaders submit two printed copies of approved materials to the Office of Research Affairs |

Printed materials include the completion report with the original expert-panel opinion, a copy of the contract, published papers or monographs bearing the Guangdong Foundation acknowledgment, patent certificates, and other completion evidence. Leaders of projects requiring an extension must contact the Office of Research Affairs by October 12.
