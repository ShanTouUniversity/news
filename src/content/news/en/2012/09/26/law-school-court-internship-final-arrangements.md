Shantou University's School of Law has announced the final arrangements for court internships during the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. The notice states that 45 law students who entered the program in 2009 applied. The nine-week internships will run from October 8 to December 6 at the Dongguan Intermediate People's Court, Dongguan No. 1 People's Court, Dongguan No. 2 People's Court, and Huizhou Intermediate People's Court.

## Departure Arrangements

The school will provide a bus to transport participating students. Students must gather at the University motto monument at 8:00 a.m. on October 8 before departure. Students assigned to the Dongguan Intermediate People's Court, Dongguan No. 1 People's Court, or Dongguan No. 2 People's Court must bring quilts and other personal necessities themselves.

The original notice included placement lists for each court, but the stated number for the Huizhou Intermediate People's Court group does not match the number of names listed. This report does not reproduce student names or infer an alternative group size; the School of Law's original notice should be consulted for the detailed final list and placements.
