The Shantou University Student Union Office resumed its duty service on Wednesday, September 26, providing routine office support for student organizations.

Duty hours are 16:15–17:15 from Monday through Friday. Student organizations should arrange related matters during these hours.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
