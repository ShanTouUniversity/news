The Shuguang Student Aid Team of the Shantou University Young Volunteers Association will launch the first phase of its “Qishu Action: Education Support Service for Vision-Impaired Children” from October to December 2012. The team is recruiting 17 volunteers from across the University. The program will work with the Minghui Basic Education Class for Blind Children, established by the Shantou Federation of Persons with Disabilities, to provide learning support.

## Service Arrangements

Services will take place on Wednesday afternoons from mid-October to early December at the Shantou Activity Center for Persons with Disabilities. Volunteers will use group teaching or one-to-one tutoring to offer activities such as story-based performance, music, handicrafts, science education, and exercises as part of a “second classroom,” helping children learn and better understand their social environment.

Applicants should be committed to public service, responsible and patient, able to complete the service consistently, and capable of communicating and cooperating well with teammates. Seventeen volunteers will be selected through interviews scheduled for September 27, September 28, and October 10; the exact times and venues will be announced separately.

## How to Apply

Applications close at 18:00 on October 9. Students may download the electronic application form from the office automation system or the Youth Volunteers section of the BBS, complete it, and email it to `stusgren@163.com`. An in-person consultation and registration desk will also operate at the campus Golden Triangle from 11:30 to 14:30 on September 26.
