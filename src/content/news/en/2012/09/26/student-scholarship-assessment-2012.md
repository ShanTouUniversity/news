Shantou University is conducting its assessment of academic excellence, special and outstanding student scholarships for the 2011-2012 academic year. The process follows the university's Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate Scholarships (STU document [2010] No. 125).

## Application and assessment schedule

Undergraduates at the main campus may submit applications through the scholarship website from September 26 to October 10. Applicants for the academic excellence scholarship must rank in the top 40 percent of their year and major by GPA percentage and have no failing courses. The outstanding student scholarship is intended for students in the top 10 percent who have no failing courses and have received university-level or higher recognition for campus culture, public service or social practice. GPA statistics close on October 10, so applicants should ensure that all grades have been recorded.

Within each group, the following special scholarships cannot be applied for simultaneously: Outstanding Contribution in Academic Science and Technology, Outstanding Contribution in Sports and Culture, Outstanding Contribution in Social Service, and Outstanding Leadership; and the awards for active participation in academic science and technology, campus culture, social service and campus service.

| Stage | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Colleges, residential colleges and the University Youth League establish review groups and submit their names | By October 10 |
| Screening of academic excellence applications and publication of shortlisted names | Mid-October, for three days |
| Submission of nominations and application forms for four outstanding-contribution scholarships | By October 17 |
| Completion of the preliminary selection for the outstanding student scholarship | By October 22 |
| University review of the four outstanding-contribution scholarships and outstanding student scholarship | Late October to early November |
| Colleges and residential colleges select the four active-participation scholarships and submit results | Early November |

Special scholarship quotas and nomination quotas will be allocated among the relevant units. Nomination quotas for the outstanding student scholarship will equal 1.5 times the number of award places. Results will be posted through the student affairs system, the internal office information network or the scholarship website.

Applicants may contact `xyli@stu.edu.cn` with questions. The university noted that the scholarship system had previously been in testing; applications submitted after this notice are valid, while forms submitted during testing will be deleted.
