Shantou University's Office of Academic Affairs has announced registration arrangements for public-service courses in fall 2012. Beginning with the 2012 entry cohort, public-service courses will form part of the University's required general-education curriculum for undergraduates. Students who complete 48 class hours and pass the assessment will receive one credit in the “Philosophy and Social Sciences” category. Five courses are available this semester, offering 254 places in total, with most sessions scheduled on Wednesday afternoons or weekends.

## Registration Windows and Rules

Initial registration runs from 09:00 on September 27 to 09:00 on September 28. Add/drop registration runs from 09:00 to 17:00 on September 29. No registration or registration-change requests will be accepted after the add/drop period. Places will be assigned on a first-come, first-served basis. Each student may register for only one course, and students already selected through interviews should not register in this round.

Students should log in to the credit-based education system and go to “Guided Courses > Course Selection” to find courses offered by the relevant teaching units in fall 2012–2013 whose codes begin with `SER`. They should review each syllabus for course content, requirements, schedule, and assessment method, and select from the listed options rather than create a new project. A student who registers but fails to participate normally will receive a score of zero under the unified assessment rules.

Further information is available on the public-service course website at [gykc.stu.edu.cn](http://gykc.stu.edu.cn). For registration questions, contact Liu at 82902416 or Chen at 82903202 in the Office of Academic Affairs.
