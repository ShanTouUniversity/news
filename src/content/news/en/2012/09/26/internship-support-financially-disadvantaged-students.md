Shantou University's Office of Student Affairs has launched its 2012 internship support program for financially disadvantaged students. The program provides assistance to students taking employment-related internships, helping them gain practical experience and improve their employment competitiveness. It offers 400 yuan per student and has 500 grants available.

## Applications and Nominations

Interested students must apply online through the scholarship website at [scholarship.stu.edu.cn](http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn) by October 10 and attach an internship summary report and internship evaluation form. Each school or residential college will recommend supported students according to its allocated quota. Printed summary sheets must be submitted to the Student Aid Management Center of the Office of Student Affairs in Room 106 of the Administration Center by the end of the workday on October 22, with electronic copies sent to `zpchen@stu.edu.cn`.

The program is open to eligible students from schools and residential colleges at the main campus. The application forms and detailed material requirements are provided in the attachments issued by the Office of Student Affairs.
