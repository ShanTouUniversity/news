Grandall Law Firm (Shanghai) plans to recruit two 2009-entry law students from Shantou University's School of Law for internships expected to begin after the National Day holiday and last approximately three to four months. Interns will assist the firm's lawyers in traveling to Suzhou to work on an initial public offering (IPO) project for a client company.

## Position Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number of positions | 2 |
| Eligible applicants | 2009-entry law students |
| Location and work | Travel to Suzhou with lawyers to assist with a client company's IPO project |
| Duration | Approximately three to four months, beginning after the National Day holiday |
| Benefits | Accommodation and meals; a subsidy of 300 yuan per month for the first three months and 1,000 yuan per month from the fourth month onward |

Applicants are expected to be conscientious, dependable, and hard-working. Interested students must complete the internship application form in the attachment and send it together with a résumé to `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` by September 28. The Career Development Center will arrange interviews, with the time and venue to be announced separately. Grandall's website is [grandall.com.cn](http://www.grandall.com.cn/index.htm).
