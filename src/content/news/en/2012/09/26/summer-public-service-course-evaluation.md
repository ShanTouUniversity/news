Shantou University's Office of Academic Affairs will ask students to evaluate the teaching of public-service courses offered in the 2012–2013 summer term through the credit-based education system. The evaluation is intended to help the University understand course delivery and improve teaching quality. Ten summer public-service courses will be covered in two periods.

## Evaluation Schedule

| Period | Dates | Courses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First period | September 26 to October 12 | Nine summer public-service courses other than “Care for Children with Cerebral Palsy” |
| Second period | October 29 to October 31 | “Care for Children with Cerebral Palsy” |

Students should log in to the credit-based education system, select “Course Evaluation,” view the public-service courses awaiting evaluation, and choose courses from the 2012–2013 summer term. Students must complete the evaluation within the specified period. Those who miss the deadline must submit a written explanation; otherwise, they will be unable to view their public-service course grades.
