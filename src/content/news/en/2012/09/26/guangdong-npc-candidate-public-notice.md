Shantou University's Party Organization and United Front Work Office has published a public notice for a preliminary candidate for the 12th Guangdong Provincial People's Congress, following the recommendation plan approved by the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee. The candidate, Ulanhas, is a member of the Standing Committee of the Shantou University Party Committee and a vice president of the University. Ulanhas is Mongolian, a member of the Communist Party of China, was born in October 1960, and holds a doctorate and a professorship.

## Notice and Comments

The public notice runs from September 26 to October 3. During this period, faculty, staff, and other members of the University community may submit information about the candidate to the University's Party Organization and United Front Work Office by letter or telephone. Comments should be factual; real-name submissions are encouraged to facilitate verification.

The office can be reached at 82902345 or 82902341, or by email at `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.
