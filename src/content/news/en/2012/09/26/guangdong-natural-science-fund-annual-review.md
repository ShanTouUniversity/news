Shantou University's Office of Research Affairs has begun its annual review of 2012 Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects in accordance with a notice from the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Committee and relevant management rules. Project leaders must submit annual execution reports according to their projects' status.

## Projects Covered

The review covers three groups: projects funded in 2012 that are ongoing, projects funded in 2012 that have been completed, and follow-up projects completed in 2010 or 2011. Doctoral-startup projects are excluded from the follow-up group. The detailed project list is provided in the attachment issued by the Office of Research Affairs.

## Deadlines

Project leaders must log in to the relevant system and submit annual reports online between October 10 and November 10. After a report is approved, the leader must download and print one copy and submit it to the Office of Research Affairs by November 18.

The procedure is described in Attachment 1. Contacts at the Office of Research Affairs are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng, reachable at 82902813.
