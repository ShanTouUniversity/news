The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has circulated information about the third Hip Hop Dance Challenge to students across the University. The event is jointly organized by the Guangdong Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, and the Guangdong Dance Association. It aims to provide hip hop enthusiasts in Guangdong with a platform to showcase themselves and support the development of hip hop culture in a healthy and positive environment.

The University Youth League Committee invites interested students to register and compete according to the event notice. The competition schedule, eligibility requirements, registration process, and other participation details are provided in the event attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
>
> ⚠️ The event notice and related attachments for the third Hip Hop Dance Challenge are available on the OA system.
