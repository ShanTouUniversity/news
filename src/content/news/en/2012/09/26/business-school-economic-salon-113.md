The 113th Economic Salon of Shantou University's Business School will be held on September 28. Professor Zou Lan, deputy editor-in-chief of Hong Kong's *Economic Digest*, will speak on “Several Issues in the Succession of Chinese Family Businesses.” Zou previously worked as a researcher at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and the former State Commission for Economic Restructuring, and as an official with the United Nations Development Programme. His research has covered China's macroeconomy, regional development, international economic relations, family businesses, and corporate social responsibility.

The event will also form part of the continuing-education course “Theory and Practice of Modern Economic Management II.” Students enrolled in that course may register by emailing `o_sxy@stu.edu.cn`; staff at the venue will arrange signature confirmation. Business School undergraduates and postgraduates are expected to attend, and all STU faculty and students are welcome.
