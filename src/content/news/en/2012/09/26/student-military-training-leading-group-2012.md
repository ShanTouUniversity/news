The 10th meeting of Shantou University leaders decided on September 18, 2012, to establish a leading group for student military training and coordinate related university work. STU announced the group's membership on September 26.

## Organizational structure

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Luo Nianchao |
| Deputy chair | Yu Hong |
| Members | Liu Yuzhou, Su Wenji, Chen Wenbin, Du Shimin, Chen Yiyuan, Chen Yinglin, Yu Shanyan, Li Haotao, Chen Chuqun, Zheng Zaixin, Lin Bingfeng, Wen Ruifeng |
| Secretary | Zhang Guihua |

The leading group will oversee a student military training working group. Members of the working group for each year will be determined by the leading group. This arrangement provides a coordination mechanism for the university's annual student military training work.
