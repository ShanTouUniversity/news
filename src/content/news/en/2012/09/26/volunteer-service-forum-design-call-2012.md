Shantou University and the China Youth University for Political Sciences are inviting STU students to design the logo and event backdrop for the 2012 Service Society, Grow Through Service China University Volunteer Service Development Forum. The forum is organized by the Medical Poverty-Relief Office of the STU Medical College and is intended to create an open platform for exchange among university students, public-interest organizations, professionals and other people involved in volunteer service.

## Design call

The call covers two coordinated designs: a forum logo and an event backdrop. The two pieces should form a series with a consistent visual style and dominant color scheme. The logo will be used on forum publicity and printed materials. It does not have to display the forum name or year, but it should convey charity and volunteer service so that it can be reused in future editions. The backdrop should display the full forum name and year, while leaving space for the event time, venue and organizers.

Submissions should have a clear concept, a concise and polished form, vivid colors and a positive, energetic character. Each entry must include color and black-and-white versions of the logo as well as a written explanation, submitted in JPG format.

## Schedule and award

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Submission deadline | 00:00 on October 15, 2012 |
| Submission method | Email the JPG files to `o_map@stu.edu.cn` |
| Award for the selected work | 500 yuan |

The selected entrant will be notified separately to provide the original design files. Entrants should keep their own copies of the submitted work.

## Intellectual property terms

Entries must not infringe the intellectual property rights of others. An infringement will result in disqualification and recovery of any award already paid. The selected work will be treated as a commissioned work: all copyrights other than the right of attribution will belong to the Medical Poverty-Relief Office of the STU Medical College, and the 500-yuan award will serve as the design commission fee. Entrants are responsible for any costs incurred during submission, and submitted works will not be returned.
