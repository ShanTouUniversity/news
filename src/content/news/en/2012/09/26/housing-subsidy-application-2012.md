Shantou University's Office of Resource Management has announced arrangements for 2012 housing allowance applications. Under provincial and municipal housing policies, eligible newly transferred employees must submit their application materials to the Property Management Office by October 15. The University plans to consolidate the materials and submit them to the relevant provincial and municipal authorities by the end of October. Employees who applied for the allowance in previous years do not need to apply again.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be employees of provincial public institutions with personnel ties to Shantou University and must meet all of the following conditions:

- Neither the applicant nor their spouse has purchased a housing-reform or hardship-relief home, or participated in an employer's pooled-funding housing project.
- Neither the applicant nor their spouse has received a government-recognized housing allowance.
- Both the applicant and their spouse have stopped receiving current housing-reform or low-rent housing benefits and pay rent at cost-based or commercial rates.

Applicants should prepare the personal-information form and supporting documents concerning identity, employment, and housing status as required. The specific documents for applicants with different employment or marital circumstances are listed in the application attachments.

## Other Registration Requirement

Employees already receiving the housing allowance monthly whose professional title or position has changed must also submit a professional-title or position-change registration form to the Property Management Office by October 15. After verification by the University's personnel or organization office, the information will be submitted to the relevant authorities. The new allowance standard will be calculated from the month in which the national salary adjustment is paid.

The Property Management Office is in Room W313 of the Administration Building and can be reached at 82902494.
