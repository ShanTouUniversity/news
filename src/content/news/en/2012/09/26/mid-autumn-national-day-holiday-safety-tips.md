As the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holidays draw near, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has issued a set of holiday safety tips to all students, offering specific advice on travel, dormitory security, on-campus rules for those staying behind, and protection against scams.

## Prepare Before Traveling and Go in Groups

The office advises students planning trips to follow travel advisories from the tourism, foreign affairs, health, and meteorological authorities in advance, so as to learn about weather, health, transport, and public safety conditions at their destinations, and to keep emergency contact numbers at hand. Those joining package tours are urged to choose licensed travel agencies, sign travel contracts, and fully understand the risks and restrictions involved.

For outdoor excursions or visits to relatives and friends, the university recommends traveling in groups and paying close attention to personal and property safety. Handbags should be worn across the front of the body rather than left unattended, and pedestrians should keep away from motorized lanes.

## Dormitory Precautions Remain Essential

For both departing and remaining students, the office reminded everyone to close doors and windows and switch off water and electricity before leaving dormitories, and to stay alert to fire and theft risks. Outsiders may not stay overnight in student dormitories, and doors and windows should be locked when going out and before bedtime. Students were also warned not to trust strangers asking to borrow money or goods or selling items, so as to avoid personal injury or property loss.

## On-Campus Rules for Students Staying Behind

It is reported that students remaining on campus during the holidays must observe the university's schedules and safety management rules, and that recreational activities on campus may only take place under safe conditions. The tips explicitly prohibit the following:

| Prohibited Activity | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Open flames on hills | No open fires or barbecues on hills |
| Fireworks | No fireworks or sky lanterns on campus |
| Reservoir activities | No fishing or swimming at the university reservoir |

## Beware of Telecom and Online Scams

The office also urged students to stay vigilant against online misinformation during the holidays, including phone and text-message scams in which impostors pose as classmates, fellow townsmen, or relatives, as well as frauds requesting bank transfers through QQ or MSN chat. Students leaving campus should buy tickets only at legitimate passenger stations.

In case of emergencies, students on campus can contact the relevant departments: the Student Community Management Service Center at 82902661, and the University Security Section at 82904110.
