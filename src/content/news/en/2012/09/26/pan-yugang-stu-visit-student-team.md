Shantou University's School of Science is recruiting a student team to plan and organize activities during a visit by Pan Yugang, a prominent quantum chemist, tenured professor at Boston College, and honorary professor of Shantou University. Pan will visit STU from October 11 to 13, giving a lecture titled “The Best of Li Ao” and holding an on-site signing for his new book, *A Little More Li Ao*. All proceeds from the book sales will go to the Pan Yugang Scholarship.

## Team Applications

Students may form their own teams, with no fixed limit on team size. Each team should determine its membership according to the needs of the project. Responsibilities will include receiving Pan during his visit and planning, organizing, and publicizing the lecture and book-signing event. The School of Science will select the successful team based on the applications and project requirements.

Interested teams must complete the application form in the attachment and email it to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn` by noon on September 28. The subject line must state “Pan Yugang STU Visit + Team Name.”
