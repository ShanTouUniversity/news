It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing that travel subsidies for the Class of 2012 graduates in financial need have been credited to recipients' Bank of China accounts.

Colleges and residential colleges have been asked to notify eligible graduates to check their accounts. The detailed recipient list is provided as an attachment to the original notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The recipient list is available as an attachment on the OA system.
