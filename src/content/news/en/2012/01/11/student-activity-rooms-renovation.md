It is reported that, at the request of the Student Affairs Office and to enrich students' extracurricular life, Shantou University will renovate part of the ground floors of dormitory buildings A, D and E among the A–F residence halls for use as student activity rooms. Following a price-comparison tender, Shantou Yuesheng Construction Co., Ltd. was awarded the contract. Construction is set to begin on January 15, 2012, with completion planned by the end of February.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Location | Part of the ground floors of dormitories A, D and E |
| Start date | January 15, 2012 |
| Planned completion | End of February 2012 |
| Contractor | Shantou Yuesheng Construction Co., Ltd. (winner of price comparison) |
| Organizing departments | Resource Management Office / Infrastructure Office |

The main works include brick wall construction, wall plastering, removal and repair of interior wall and ceiling finishes, painting of walls and ceilings, exterior mosaic tiling, floor tile and skirting repairs, door and window renovation and installation, as well as installation of television, network and power lines.

Noise, vibration and dust are expected during construction. The university has asked passing students and staff to take care, to stay out of the site without permission from the relevant functional departments and the contractor, and to avoid lingering near the work areas.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
