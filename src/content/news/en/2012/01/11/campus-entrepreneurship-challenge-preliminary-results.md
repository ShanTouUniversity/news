It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing the preliminary results of the 3rd Campus Entrepreneurship Challenge.

Following evaluation by the competition's judging panel, 30 entries from participating universities were selected to advance to the semi-finals. Among the 17 submissions from Shantou University, eight were shortlisted. The shortlisted entries and the semi-final evaluation criteria are detailed in the attachments to the original notice.

## Competition Progress

| Stage | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Preliminary round | 30 entries from across universities advance to semi-finals |
| STU performance | 8 of 17 submissions shortlisted |
| Semi-final requirement | Shortlisted teams must submit a complete business plan to o_bys@stu.edu.cn by the close of business on March 5, 2012 |
| Business plan format | No more than 15 pages, may include charts, tables, data, and illustrations |

Colleges and residential colleges have been asked to notify shortlisted teams to submit their materials on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The shortlisted entries and semi-final evaluation criteria are available as attachments on the OA system.
