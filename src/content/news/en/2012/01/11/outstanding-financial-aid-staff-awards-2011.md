It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University circulated a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing the results of the 2011 Outstanding Individuals in Financial Aid Work among Guangdong's Regular Higher Education Institutions, with two STU staff members receiving the honor.

To strengthen the development of student financial aid teams and to recognize dedicated, selfless, and exemplary financial aid workers, the Guangdong Professional Committee for Scholarships and Financial Aid in Regular Higher Education Institutions, together with the Guangdong Department of Education's Student Financial Aid Management Center, organized the province-wide selection.

Following evaluation, Li Xiaoyan and Hu Junxian of Shantou University were named "2011 Outstanding Individuals in Financial Aid Work among Guangdong's Regular Higher Education Institutions."

| Awardee | Honor |
|---------|-------|
| Li Xiaoyan | 2011 Outstanding Individual in Financial Aid Work among Guangdong's Regular Higher Education Institutions |
| Hu Junxian | 2011 Outstanding Individual in Financial Aid Work among Guangdong's Regular Higher Education Institutions |

The Student Affairs Office extended congratulations to the awardees and called on staff across the student affairs system to draw inspiration from their example, faithfully implement the Party's financial aid policies, and continue to innovate in support of building a high-level university renowned both domestically and internationally.
