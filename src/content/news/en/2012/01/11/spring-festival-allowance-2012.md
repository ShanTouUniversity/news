It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University issued a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing Spring Festival allowances for all union members in celebration of the upcoming Spring Festival.

## Distribution Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Eligibility | Union members registered by December 31, 2011 |
| Amount | 200 yuan per person |
| Collection | Departmental unions are asked to send a representative to the university Labor Union on the morning of January 11 to collect the allowances |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
