It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on January 11, 2012, launching the selection process for the 2012 "STU Overseas Study Funding Program."

Funded by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the program supports academically outstanding STU students with commitment and potential to contribute to national and university innovation, enabling them to pursue studies at distinguished institutions outside mainland China and develop international experience and perspective. Open to students across all colleges since 2007, the program currently accepts applications only from undergraduates seeking master's degrees abroad. Starting in 2012, the university has adjusted the program to expand overseas exchange opportunities.

## Funding Scale

| Tier | Amount per Recipient |
|------|----------------------|
| Tier A | 80,000 yuan |
| Tier B | 50,000 yuan |
| Tier C | 30,000 yuan |
| Total awards | 20 |

## Eligibility and Selection Criteria

**Eligibility:**

- Graduating undergraduate students of Shantou University who have not yet obtained a master's degree or higher;
- Strong English proficiency meeting general language requirements for admission at overseas institutions;
- Excellent academic performance and good character.

**Selection criteria:**

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Academic performance | Overall academic record |
| English proficiency | Language competency |
| Target institution and program | Quality and academic standing of the chosen university and program |
| Overall competence | Leadership, engagement in public affairs, community service, and extracurricular activities |

## Application Materials

1. Application form for the Overseas Study Funding Program (to be submitted and printed via the scholarship website at http://scholarship.stu.edu.cn/chinese/applyIntro2.aspx);
2. Official academic transcript (printed on A4, verified by the college);
3. Two sealed reference letters, including one from the college dean, with the recommender's signature across the sealed flap. Letters should highlight the applicant's notable achievements, activities, and contributions to the university or wider community;
4. An English essay of no more than 500 words on life goals and the applicant's overseas study plan, to be uploaded as an attachment during the online application.

## Timeline and Important Notes

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Application deadline | By 4:00 p.m. on March 7, 2012 — complete the online application and deliver hard-copy materials to the Education Management Section of the Student Affairs Office |
| Choice of institutions | No more than three institutions, selected from the university-provided list of eligible overseas universities (see attachment). Names of institutions and programs must be written in full in English without abbreviations and cannot be changed once submitted |
| GPA information | Applicants should not fill in personal or program-average GPA; the Student Affairs Office will complete this |
| Completeness | Both online and hard-copy submissions are required; incomplete applications will be considered invalid |
| Inquiries | Email xyli@stu.edu.cn or call 82903398 |

The list of eligible overseas institutions, application forms, and other attachments are available with the original notice.
