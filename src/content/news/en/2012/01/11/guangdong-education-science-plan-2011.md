It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University circulated a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing the call for applications for the 2011 Guangdong Education Science 12th Five-Year Plan Projects.

The projects are self-funded. Applicants are expected to align their proposals with the goals of building a strong education province and establishing Guangdong as a leading education hub in southern China, focusing on major practical and theoretical issues in the province's education modernization and reform, and designing their own research topics accordingly.

## Application Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Topic orientation | Must be grounded in Guangdong, reflect local characteristics, demonstrate originality and innovation; submissions with papers and research reports as final outputs are encouraged |
| Limit per applicant | Each principal investigator may apply for only one project and participate in no more than two others as a team member |
| Ineligibility | PIs with unfinished projects under the National Social Science Fund, National Education Science Planning, Ministry of Education, Guangdong Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning, or Guangdong Department of Education programs may not apply |
| Duration | Papers/reports to be completed within 1–2 years; monographs within 2–3 years |
