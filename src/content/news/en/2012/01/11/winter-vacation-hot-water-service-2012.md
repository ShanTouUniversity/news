It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Resource Management Office of Shantou University jointly issued a notice on January 11, 2012, outlining hot water arrangements for students remaining on campus during the winter break.

## Service Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Service period | January 18 to February 22, 2012, daily 4:00 p.m.–8:00 p.m. |
| Hot water points for male students | L118 (duty room) |
| Hot water points for female students | Restroom next to Building E duty room and Room Y106 |
| Services | Collecting hot water or using hot water for showers |

Students are required to bring their campus smart card and register actual water usage at the duty room of the building where the service point is located.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office)
