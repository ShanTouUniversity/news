It is reported that the Sports Department issued a notice on January 11, 2012, regarding opening hours for sports venues during the winter break (January 18 – February 26).

## Schedule

| Venue | Hours | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Badminton courts | Thu and Sun, 09:00–12:00 and 15:00–17:00 | Closed from New Year’s Eve to the sixth day of the Chinese New Year |
| Tennis courts | Open normally | Closed from New Year’s Eve to the sixth day of the Chinese New Year |
| Fitness center | Closed | Reopens on March 6 |

Faculty and students needing to reserve badminton or tennis courts should email the Sports Department at o_tyb@stu.edu.cn one week in advance and swipe their campus card at the Sports Department to pay.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
