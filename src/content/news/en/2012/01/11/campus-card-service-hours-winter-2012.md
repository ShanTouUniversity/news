It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University issued a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing adjusted service hours for campus card counters during the winter break.

## Service Adjustments

| Service Point | Closure Period | Reopening |
|---------------|---------------|-----------|
| Expert Apartment campus card counter (Spring Festival) | January 18–29, 2012 | Resumes normal operation from January 30 |
| Canteen No. 2 recharge counter (winter break) | January 18–February 22, 2012 | Resumes normal operation from February 23 |

Students and staff are advised to plan card-related services around these schedules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
