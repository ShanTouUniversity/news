It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing the proposed list of 2011 Undergraduate Innovation Experiment Projects for public review.

In accordance with the notice on launching the 2011 application round and the university's interim measures for the program, the university began accepting applications in November 2011. Following submissions by project teams, preliminary expert reviews organized by each college, and university-level vetting, 110 projects were proposed for approval.

## Projects at a Glance

| Category | Number | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Proposed provincial-level projects | 57 | "A 50-Year Record of Land Reclamation in Shantou," among others |
| University-level projects | 53 | "A Study on Summer Internship Outcomes for Journalism Students at STU," among others |
| Total | 110 | — |

The public review period runs from January 11 to January 16, 2012. Objections may be raised orally or in writing with the General Section of the Academic Affairs Office (Administration Building West Wing, Room 401).
