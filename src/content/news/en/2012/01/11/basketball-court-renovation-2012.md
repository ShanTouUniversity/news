It is reported that the Sports Department issued a notice on January 11, 2012, announcing a renovation of the basketball court backboards during the winter break.

The existing basketball stands have been in use for many years and can no longer meet normal teaching and activity needs. The university will replace all wooden backboards with fiberglass ones.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Renovation of basketball court backboards, wooden to fiberglass |
| Start date | January 16, 2012 |
| Duration | About 15 working days |
| Timing | During the winter break to avoid impacting campus activities |
| Issued by | Sports Department |

Please be advised.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
