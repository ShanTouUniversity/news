It is reported that to ensure the safe and stable operation of the campus power supply system, the Hydropower Center of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University has scheduled preventive testing of high-voltage power equipment during the winter vacation, along with a corresponding outage plan.

In accordance with national electric power industry standards, regular preventive testing of high-voltage equipment is required to monitor the operating condition of power supply facilities. To minimize disruption to teaching, learning and work, the Hydropower Center has arranged the testing during the winter vacation, covering 10kV equipment at Switch Stations No. 1 and No. 2 and high-voltage side equipment in selected distribution rooms.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Testing | Preventive testing of 10kV power equipment |
| Scope | Switch Stations No. 1 and No. 2, plus high-voltage side equipment in selected distribution rooms |
| Period | 2012 winter vacation |
| Organized by | Hydropower Center, Resource Management Office |
| Outage schedule | See attachment |

The university apologizes for any inconvenience caused by the outages and appreciates the understanding of faculty, staff and students.
