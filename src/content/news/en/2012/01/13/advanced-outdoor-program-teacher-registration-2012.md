It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on January 13, 2012, opening registration for the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Program at Shantou University for faculty members.

## Program Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | All faculty members |
| Application deadline | 17:00, March 1, 2012 |
| Selection | Fitness test in late March, with final list determined by a review committee |
| How to apply | Download the program introduction and application form, complete and send to liran@stu.edu.cn with subject and attachment titled “2012 Advanced Outdoor Program Application — Name — Unit” |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

Further selection details will be announced via the office information network. The introduction and forms are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ Program materials and forms are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
