It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University has issued a notice requesting each departmental labor union to send a representative to the university Labor Union office to collect birthday gifts for faculty and staff whose birthdays fall in January and February.

Departmental unions concerned may proceed to the Labor Union office to complete the collection.
