It is reported that Shantou University has issued Document No. 8 [2012], announcing the revised *Shantou University Faculty and Staff Attendance Regulations*.

According to the notice, the regulations were approved at the first university leadership meeting on January 10, 2012, and take effect upon promulgation. The former *Interim Provisions on Faculty and Staff Attendance and Leave* (Document No. 03 [2007]) is repealed simultaneously.

The full text of the regulations is attached to the original notice.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Document No. | STU Fa [2012] No. 8 |
| Title | Shantou University Faculty and Staff Attendance Regulations |
| Approved | First university leadership meeting, January 10, 2012 |
| Effective | Upon promulgation |
| Repealed | Interim Provisions on Attendance and Leave (STU Fa [2007] No. 03) |
