It is reported that the Office of Research Affairs at Shantou University has issued a notice launching the internal selection for the 2012 Guangdong Soft Science Research Program. According to documents from the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology (Yue Ke Han Gui Hua Zi [2011] No. 1760) and the 2012 Program Application Guide, the program adopts a quota-based nomination system, with Shantou University allocated two nomination slots.

Applicants are required to submit their project applications to the designated email address (yxhu@stu.edu.cn) by February 10. The Office of Research Affairs will organize expert reviews to select the best candidates for nomination. Only shortlisted projects will be eligible to proceed to the subsequent online application stage.

## Key Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Program | 2012 Guangdong Soft Science Research Program |
| Basis | Yue Ke Han Gui Hua Zi [2011] No. 1760 and the 2012 Application Guide |
| University quota | 2 projects |
| Internal deadline | Before February 10, 2012 |
| Submission method | Email application to yxhu@stu.edu.cn |
| Selection process | Expert review organized by the Office of Research Affairs |

Application guidelines and templates have been attached to the original notice.
