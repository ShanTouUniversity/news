It is reported that Shantou University has recently introduced a series of measures to ensure campus traffic safety and maintain orderly traffic flow.

At the university's request, the traffic police department of Shantou has installed traffic lights at the main gate. Faculty, students, and staff driving in and out of campus are required to strictly follow the traffic signals and are prohibited from entering or exiting against the designated direction.

In addition, the university has added speed bumps on major roads across campus to control vehicle speeds and safeguard the lives and property of the campus community. Drivers are reminded to proceed slowly while on campus.

## Key Measures

| Measure | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Traffic lights at main gate | Installed by Shantou traffic police; drivers must follow signals |
| Speed bumps | Added on major campus roads to slow traffic |
| Traffic rule | No wrong-way entry or exit at the gate |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management, Security Office)
