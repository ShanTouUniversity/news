It is reported that the Postdoctoral Fellowship Program jointly run by the China Scholarship Council (CSC) and Georgetown University is now accepting applications, with a deadline of March 20, 2012. The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at Shantou University has forwarded the announcement.

Established in 2006 to promote academic exchange between Georgetown and leading Chinese universities, the program enables funded scholars to conduct 1–2 years of postdoctoral research at Georgetown, collaborating with experts in their fields and publishing in high-level English-language journals. Research areas for 2012 include chemistry, physics, biotechnology, computer science, neuroscience, pharmacology, and physiology.

## Eligibility

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Doctoral degree | Obtained between March 20, 2007 and March 20, 2012 from a Chinese university or research institution |
| Current status | Faculty member at a Chinese university/research institution or first-year postdoctoral researcher |
| Language | High level of English proficiency |
| Academic background | Strong academic foundation and research capability |
| Previous CSC funding | If previously funded by CSC, must have returned to China for at least 5 years |

## Funding

| Item | Coverage |
|------|----------|
| Living stipend | At least USD 30,000 per year |
| Health insurance | Georgetown faculty and staff medical insurance |
| Travel | One round-trip air ticket between China and the United States |
| Academic resources | Access to Georgetown libraries and other academic resources |

## Timeline

| Time | Stage |
|------|-------|
| By March 20 | Submit complete applications to both the Georgetown fellowship office and CSC (no need to contact supervisors in advance) |
| April | Georgetown conducts English interviews for shortlisted candidates; those who pass are exempted from the national foreign language proficiency test (WSK/PETS5) |
| May | Georgetown announces admission list and forwards it to CSC |
| April–July | CSC organizes expert review and announces final results |
| August–September | Admitted scholars complete departure procedures and begin research in Washington, D.C. |

Applicants should send the application form and two recommendation letters to cscfellowship@georgetown.edu for the Georgetown side and submit required materials to CSC as instructed. More details are available at http://china.provost.georgetown.edu/csc/.
