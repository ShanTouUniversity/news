It is reported that the Office of Resource Management at Shantou University has announced adjusted canteen hours for the winter vacation due to internal maintenance needs.

According to the notice, Canteen No. 2 will remain open as usual throughout the vacation. Canteen No. 3 will be closed for maintenance from January 16 to February 8 and resume normal operations on February 9. Canteen No. 4 will be closed from January 20 to February 20 and reopen on February 21.

## Winter Vacation Canteen Schedule

| Canteen | Schedule |
|---------|----------|
| Canteen No. 2 | Open as usual during the vacation |
| Canteen No. 3 | Closed Jan. 16–Feb. 8, reopens Feb. 9 |
| Canteen No. 4 | Closed Jan. 20–Feb. 20, reopens Feb. 21 |

The Office of Resource Management advised students and staff to plan their dining arrangements accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
