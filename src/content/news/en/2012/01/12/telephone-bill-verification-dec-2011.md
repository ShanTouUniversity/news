The Resource Management Office at Shantou University has issued a notice organizing verification of office telephone charges for December 2011.

## Verification Arrangements

China Telecom's Shantou branch has sent electronic bills for office telephone charges incurred from December 1 to December 31, 2011, to the university telephone office. The data has been imported into the "Telephone Management System" (http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn), where detailed statements are available for review.

| Item | Arrangement |
|------|-------------|
| How to Verify | Designated verifiers from each unit log in to the Telephone Management System to check bill details |
| Confirmation | For bills with no discrepancies, click "Confirm Selected Bills" |
| Disputes | If any charges do not match actual usage, email o_dhs@stu.edu.cn by January 18 |
| Deduction Date | The Finance Office will deduct charges on January 27 based on the funding codes provided by each unit |
| Default Rule | Bills with no objection or confirmation by the end of the verification period will be deemed confirmed |

Units that encounter issues or have suggestions while using the Telephone Management System are also encouraged to email o_dhs@stu.edu.cn so that relevant departments can further improve the system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
