Shantou University has issued Document No. 7 [2012] announcing the recipients of the Outstanding Undergraduate Teaching Award and the Outstanding Teaching Service Award for the 2010–2011 academic year.

The selection was conducted in accordance with the *Measures for the Selection of STU Outstanding Undergraduate Teaching Awards and Outstanding Teaching Service Awards* (STU Document No. 148 [2009]) and the relevant selection notice, following evaluation, ranking, and recommendation by each teaching unit and review by a university panel.

## Awardees

### Outstanding Undergraduate Teaching Award (20 recipients)

Zhang Chuanfang, Yang Weigang, Fan Linjun, Qiu Guiming, Xie Changzhen, Du Hong, Fang Yiwen, Cui Yan, Li Gengying, He Jun, Liu Ping, Tang Meiyun, Yan Bo, Chen Errui, Yan Zhigang, Li Wanli, Yang Hua, Wen Ping, Chanchal Singh, and Mark Robinson

### Outstanding Teaching Service Award (10 recipients)

Zhou Zhiwei, Lin Jinqing, Shi Yongjie, Yuan Yuyun, Zhang Xiaosong, Xiao Tongmin, Chen Xin, Ni Xiangdong, Fang Ruohong, and Yang Weitao

The university congratulated the awardees and encouraged them to continue their outstanding work, while calling on all faculty and staff to uphold exemplary teaching standards and a student-centered service ethos to further enhance undergraduate teaching quality and service management.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Document No. 7 [2012])
