It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University has organized a special training course to prepare students for an international law moot court competition. Based on the order of registration, 18 students have been admitted to the course.

The selected students are: Wan CS, Wang F, Chen MZ, Wu XW, Liao SF, Zhao JX, Zhang XF, Liu X, Lin ZY, Zhang WT, Wang CH, He Y, Dai C, Wu LP, Lao YW, Ou J, Lu XS, and Jiang BB.

The training consists of three sessions, all held in Room 213 of the Law School:

| Time | Topic |
| ---- | ----- |
| Jan 13, 9:00-12:30 | Overall competition strategy design |
| Jan 13, 14:30-18:00 | Preparation strategies for oral rounds |
| Jan 14, 9:00-12:00 | Techniques for handling oral arguments |

Covering the full spectrum from overall strategy to in-court response, the course aims to help participating students systematically master the methods and skills of moot court competitions. The school has reminded the selected students to arrive on time.
