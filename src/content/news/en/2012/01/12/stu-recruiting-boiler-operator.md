The Community Management Service Center of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University has announced an opening for a boiler operator.

## Responsibilities

The position is responsible for the operation and routine maintenance of the student dormitory hot water supply systems, including:

| Duty | Details |
|------|---------|
| System Operation | Operate and maintain three hot water systems: oil-fired boiler, solar, and air-source heat pump |
| Daily Inspection | Inspect all systems daily, log operating data, adjust parameters, and ensure timely hot water supply |
| Pipeline Maintenance | Regularly inspect hot water pipelines and arrange repairs |
| Fuel Depot Safety | Manage fuel depot safety, check daily fuel consumption and reserves, and refuel promptly |
| Standards Compliance | Implement ISO 9001 and ISO 14000 standards for boiler management |
| Emergency Response | Handle emergencies promptly, report to supervisors, and complete other assigned tasks |

## Qualifications

Applicants should demonstrate good political and ideological qualities, a strong sense of responsibility, integrity, diligence, teamwork, and dedication; be under 50 years of age, in good health, and compliant with family planning policies.

## How to Apply

Applications are due by **February 29, 2012**, via email or postal mail. Required materials include:

1. A personal resume;
2. Copies of academic credentials, ID card, professional qualification certificates, family planning certificate, and other relevant certificates.

| Application Info | Details |
|------------------|---------|
| Deadline | February 29, 2012 |
| Mailing Address | Community Management Service Center, Resource Management Office, Dormitory Building C Office, Shantou University, Shantou 515063 (mark "Application for Boiler Operator") |
| Phone | 0754-82902661 |
| Email | dorm@stu.edu.cn (subject line: "Application for Boiler Operator") |

Shortlisted candidates will be notified for an interview and must bring original ID and certificates for verification. All materials will be used solely for this recruitment; originals will be returned after verification, while other materials will not be returned.
