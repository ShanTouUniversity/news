The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation, the Student Affairs Office, and the English Language Center at Shantou University have jointly announced the selection process for the 2012 Semester at Sea and Whitman College (USA) exchange programs.

## Places Available

A total of **3 students** will be selected:

| Program | Arrangement |
|---------|-------------|
| Semester at Sea Scholarship | 2 places — one for the Fall 2012 voyage and one for the Spring 2013 voyage |
| Whitman College Exchange | 1 place — a semester-long exchange in Fall 2013 |

## Eligibility

Full-time undergraduates of the 2010 cohort on the main campus; English proficiency at Level 4 (including those currently enrolled in Level 4). Students below Level 4 with outstanding academic performance may apply with a recommendation letter from their course instructor.

## Selection Criteria

1. Active participation in on- and off-campus activities, with leadership potential and an outgoing, optimistic personality;
2. Strong analytical and comprehension abilities;
3. Strong English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills;
4. Ability to promote Shantou University and its distinctive educational features;
5. Excellent academic performance with clear learning goals;
6. Global perspective, active engagement in public affairs and volunteer service, and a spirit of dedication;
7. Where candidates are equally qualified, preference for those who listed STU as their first choice on the college entrance application and those with strong IELTS or TOEFL scores.

## Application Materials

| Material | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Application Form | As specified in the attachments |
| English Recommendation Letters | One from the dean (or department head) and one from a professor (or associate professor), stating reasons for recommendation and the applicant's achievements |
| English Essay | 300–500 words, choose one topic (see below) |

**Essay Topics:**

- **For Semester at Sea applicants** (choose one):
  - A. *How will your Semester at Sea voyage create (or enhance) your personal global perspective?* (reference at least one country on the itinerary)
  - B. *What are your academic goals, and how will Semester at Sea help you to achieve them?* (include at least two examples)
  - Details: https://www.ise.virginia.edu/asp/onlineapp.html
- **For Whitman exchange applicants** (choose one):
  - A. *How will the exchange program create (or enhance) your personal global perspective?*
  - B. *What are your academic goals, and how will exchange program help you to achieve them?* (include at least two examples)

All materials must be submitted in English.

## Selection Process

| Stage | Arrangement |
|-------|-------------|
| Application Deadline | By 11:00 AM, March 2, 2012 — submit hard copies and electronic files to the Education Management Section of the Student Affairs Office; email electronic versions to xyli@stu.edu.cn (recommendation letters are sealed and do not require electronic copies) |
| Initial Screening | A university selection panel reviews written materials and shortlists interviewees |
| Interview | Tentatively March 14, 2012, in defense format (PPT presentation + Q&A), about 10–20 minutes per candidate |
| Final Selection | The review panel selects the final 3 awardees based on interviews |

## Additional Notes

- Applicants may not apply for both programs simultaneously;
- Applicants should indicate in their email whether they accept reassignment between programs;
- Whitman College exchange students receive a tuition waiver, partial board and lodging subsidy from Whitman, and round-trip international airfare support from STU.
