The Personnel Office at Shantou University has circulated a notice from the Personnel Department of the Ministry of Education to launch applications for the 2011 Changjiang Scholars Program. The Ministry has implemented a renewed Changjiang Scholars Program starting in 2011, and the relevant documents — *Yue Jiaoshi Han [2012] No. 1* and the *Implementation Measures for the Changjiang Scholars Program* — have been forwarded to all units.

## Application Arrangements

The Personnel Office has set out the following requirements for on-campus applications:

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Eligibility | High-level talents meeting the program criteria |
| Organization | All units should attach great importance, actively scout and recruit candidates through multiple channels, and organize qualified applicants |
| How to Prepare | The candidate recommendation form must be completed, edited, and verified using the designated application software; supporting materials should be prepared strictly according to the instructions |
| Downloads | Software, templates, and formatting guidelines are available in the OA attachments or at the Changjiang Scholars Program website (www.changjiang.edu.cn) |
| Deadline | February 9 |
| Submission | 3 hard copies of the recommendation form and 2 bound copies of supporting materials to the Personnel Office, plus an electronic version to o_rsk@stu.edu.cn |

## Contact

For questions during the application process, contact Li Zhiwei at the Personnel Office, tel. 82902364, email o_rsk@stu.edu.cn.
