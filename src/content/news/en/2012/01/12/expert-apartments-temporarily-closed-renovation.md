The Resource Management Office at Shantou University has announced that the Expert Apartments will be temporarily closed for interior renovation.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Closure Period | January 18–31 |
| Reopening | February 1 |
| Reason | Interior renovation |

Faculty, staff, and visitors with accommodation needs are advised to make alternative arrangements in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
