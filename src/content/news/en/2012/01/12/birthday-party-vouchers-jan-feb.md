The Labor Union of Shantou University has announced arrangements for faculty and staff who registered for the January and February birthday gatherings to collect their event vouchers.

## Collection Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Eligible Recipients | Faculty and staff who registered for the January–February birthday gatherings |
| Collection Time | Friday, January 13, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM |
| Venue | University Labor Union Office |

## Reminders

- Tickets are non-refundable after registration and payment; faculty, staff, and their family members are admitted with vouchers;
- Children must be accompanied by at least one parent; parents should supervise their children, ensure they follow staff instructions, stay safe, and keep personal belongings secure;
- Food waste should be avoided;
- No shuttle bus will be provided; participants should arrange their own transportation and travel safely.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
