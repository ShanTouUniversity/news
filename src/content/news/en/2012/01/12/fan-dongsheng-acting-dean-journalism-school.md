Shantou University has issued Document No. 6 [2012] announcing a leadership change at the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication.

Dean Chen Wanying's term will expire on January 31, 2012, and she will no longer serve as dean of the school. Following deliberation at the 1st University Leadership Meeting on January 10, 2012, it was decided to appoint Fan Dongsheng as Acting Dean of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, effective February 1, 2012.
