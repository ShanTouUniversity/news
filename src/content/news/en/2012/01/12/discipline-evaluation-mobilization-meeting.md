To advance discipline development and prepare for the submission of materials for the third national discipline evaluation, the Graduate School of Shantou University has announced a mobilization meeting for the university's participation in the evaluation.

The Degree and Graduate Education Development Center of the Ministry of Education has recently launched the third round of the national discipline evaluation. With the approval of the university leadership, STU will hold a dedicated mobilization meeting.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Date & Time | Friday, January 13, 3:30 PM |
| Venue | Room 205, Science Building |
| Agenda | Convey relevant policy documents, introduce evaluation indicators, and assign tasks |

Heads of all graduate education units, department chairs, discipline leaders, and graduate secretaries are requested to attend on time. Supporting materials are available in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
