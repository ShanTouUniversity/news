It is reported that the Network and Information Center at Shantou University has issued a notice regarding routine maintenance of the backbone network equipment for the G-Building student dormitories.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Maintenance | Routine maintenance of G-Building dormitory backbone network equipment |
| Time | January 16, 2012, 09:00–12:00 |
| Affected area | G-Building student dormitory network (service interruption expected) |
| Restoration | Service will resume automatically after maintenance without further notice |

Users are advised to inform one another. For inquiries, contact Tel: 0754-82903413-100, Mobile: 13592895188 (campus short number: 665188), Email: stunic@stu.edu.cn.
