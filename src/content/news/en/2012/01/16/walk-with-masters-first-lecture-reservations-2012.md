It is reported that the reservation period for the open lectures of the inaugural "Walk with Masters" program at Shantou University has closed, and the list of successful reservations has been announced.

The Office of Student Affairs reminded confirmed attendees to arrive 15 minutes before each lecture. Any changes will be notified by email.

## Lecture Schedule

| Session | Time | Venue | Moderator | Speakers and Topics |
|---------|------|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| Session 1 | Feb. 9, 20:45–22:15 | Library Lecture Hall | Prof. Joan Steitz | Prof. Arthur Horwich — "Protein Folding in the Cell: The Final Step of Information Transfer"; Prof. Vann Bennett — "Building Fluid Cell Membranes for Better Life: Ankyrin Adaptors in Health and Disease" |
| Session 2 | Feb. 10, 14:30–16:00 | Library Lecture Hall | Prof. Trudi Schupbach | Prof. Philip Beachy — "Hedgehog Signaling in Growth, Disease and Regeneration"; Prof. Haifan Lin — "Exploring the New World of Genes" |

The full reservation lists are available in the attachments to the original notice.
