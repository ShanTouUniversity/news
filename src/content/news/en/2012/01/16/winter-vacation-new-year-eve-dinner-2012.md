It is reported that the Student Affairs Office announced on January 16, 2012 the reservation list for the New Year Eve dinner for students staying on campus during the 2012 winter break. With strong support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, this year’s dinner will be held as a buffet.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 17:30, January 22, 2012 |
| Venue | Western Restaurant (1F), Junhua Hotel |
| Assembly | Gather at the school motto monument at 16:40 on January 22; the university will provide buses to the hotel, with return transport self-arranged |
| Entry | Campus card required for check-in |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

## Notes

Students with successful reservations are expected to attend as scheduled in the spirit of thrift and against waste. Those who must cancel should email o_fdy@stu.edu.cn before 16:00 on January 18; otherwise absentees will bear their own meal costs.

The reservation list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here to protect privacy.
