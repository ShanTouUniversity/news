It is reported that Shantou University has issued Document No. 9 [2012], announcing the establishment of the Shantou University Laboratory Animal Ethics Committee.

According to the document, the committee was established following deliberation at the first university leadership meeting on January 10, 2012, with the aims of safeguarding laboratory animal welfare, regulating the professional conduct of personnel working with laboratory animals, and implementing national and Guangdong provincial regulations and policies on laboratory animal management.
