It is reported that the Office of Research Affairs at Shantou University has announced the call for applications for the 2012 Ministry of Education (MOE) Late-Stage Funding Program for Philosophy and Social Sciences Research.

## Categories and Funding

| Category | Funding | Scope |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Major Project | RMB 200,000 | Foundational research with significant impact on academic development or original theoretical research expected to yield landmark results |
| Key Project | RMB 120,000 | Research with important academic value |
| General Project | RMB 90,000 | Research with notable academic value |

## Eligibility and Requirements

Eligible projects include foundational research, original theoretical work, and literature studies, translations, and reference works with significant academic and social impact (excluding papers, collections, textbooks, reports, and software), as well as important non-paper-based research outputs. Projects must have completed at least 70% of the research, with manuscripts or non-paper outputs submitted for review.

Applicants must be on-staff faculty members with good political and ideological standing and independent research capability, capable of leading the project. Major projects require a senior (full professor) title, key projects require a senior professional title, and general projects require at least an intermediate title (those with an intermediate title must also hold a doctoral degree). Each applicant may submit only one project.

Projects are ineligible if they have already received research or publication funding, have signed a publishing contract, involve intellectual property disputes, or are doctoral dissertations or postdoctoral reports defended after January 1, 2007.

## Application Timeline

| Deadline | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| By Feb. 24 | Submit the Application Form to the Office of Research Affairs for review |
| By Mar. 1 | Submit 7 copies of the Application Review Form (including one original) and 4 sets of completed manuscripts/non-paper outputs, plus an electronic version to ljwang@stu.edu.cn |

Funded projects will be published by the Higher Education Press in a unified format. Exceptions regarding format or publisher require approval from the Department of Social Sciences after evaluation, with the designation "MOE Late-Stage Funding Program for Philosophy and Social Sciences Research" prominently displayed.
