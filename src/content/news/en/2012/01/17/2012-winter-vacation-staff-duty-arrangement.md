It is reported that the Party and Government Affairs Office of Shantou University has announced the duty arrangements for administrative, teaching support and logistics staff during the 2012 winter vacation.

The list of staff on duty for each relevant unit has been published alongside the notice. The university requires all units to strictly follow the provisions of Document No. 1 [2011] issued by the STU Party and Government Affairs Office, ensuring that services and support for faculty, staff and students remain timely and effective throughout the vacation period.
