It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has announced the list of approved projects for the 2011 Undergraduate Innovation Experiment Program, with a total of 110 projects selected.

Following the notice on launching applications for the 2011 program, STU initiated the application and selection process in November 2011. After written review, open defense and online public notice, 110 projects were confirmed, including 57 provincial-level projects such as *A 50-Year Record of Land Reclamation in Shantou* and 53 university-level projects such as *A Study on the Effectiveness of Summer Internships for Journalism Students at STU*.

Approved projects are required to make full use of the university's teaching facilities and resources, follow the timelines set out in their proposals, and strengthen project management in accordance with the *Implementation Measures for the Undergraduate Innovation Experiment Program of STU (Trial)* (STU [2010] No. 169) to ensure smooth implementation. The university will track project progress and organize mid-term inspections and final acceptance reviews.
