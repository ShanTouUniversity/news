It is reported that following deliberation at the 1st university leadership meeting on January 10, 2012, the university has appointed a faculty member surnamed Wang as Vice Dean of the Business School, with a six-month probationary period. The decision was issued as STU Document No. 10 [2012] on January 17, 2012.

## Appointment Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Document | STU No. 10 [2012] |
| Deciding meeting | 1st University Leadership Meeting, Jan. 10, 2012 |
| Position | Vice Dean, Business School |
| Probationary period | Six months |
| Issue date | Jan. 17, 2012 |
