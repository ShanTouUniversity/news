It is reported that the Logistics Office Fleet under the Resource Management Office of Shantou University is openly recruiting several drivers from the public.

## Eligibility

- Strong political and moral standing, sense of responsibility, and dedication to service with good teamwork
- Valid motor vehicle driving license of category B1 or above (A1 preferred), issued by the public security authority
- At least five years of driving experience
- Male, under 40 years old, in good health and in compliance with family planning policies

## Responsibilities

Provide transport services supporting the university's teaching, research and administrative operations; comply with all regulations and dispatch procedures, driving only with an approved Vehicle Request Form; strictly observe traffic laws and operational rules to ensure driving safety; perform regular vehicle inspections, maintenance and cleaning; deliver courteous and punctual service; and complete other tasks assigned by supervisors.

## Employment Terms

Positions are offered on an auxiliary contract basis. Personnel files will not be transferred to the university, and compensation follows relevant university standards.
