It is reported that Shantou University has announced a personnel appointment.

Following deliberation at the first meeting of the Standing Committee of the CPC Shantou University Committee in 2012, it has been decided to appoint Huang Qingjun as Director of the Mental Health Center of Shantou University, with a six-month probationary period. The decision was issued under Document No. 4 [2012] of the CPC Shantou University Committee.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Document No. | CPC STU [2012] No. 4 |
| Position | Director, Mental Health Center of STU |
| Appointee | Huang Qingjun |
| Probationary period | Six months |
| Decided by | Standing Committee of the CPC STU Committee, 1st meeting of 2012 |
| Date | January 17, 2012 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Committee)
