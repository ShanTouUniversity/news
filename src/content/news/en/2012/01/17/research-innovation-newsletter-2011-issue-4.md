It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University has released Issue 4, 2011 (No. 22 overall) of the *Research and Innovation Newsletter*, reviewing recent developments across research projects, awards, academic exchanges and community engagement.

## Research Projects

- STU achieved a strong harvest of research platform and key project approvals in 2011
- Updates on humanities and social sciences project applications and approvals
- The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design successfully concluded several National and Provincial Social Sciences Fund projects

## Research Achievements and Awards

- The College of Science won an Outstanding Paper Award at the 10th China International Nanoscience and Technology Symposium
- A major breakthrough was made in factory-scale cultivation technology for *Pyropia* (laver) seedlings
- A research paper from the College of Science was selected as a cover feature by an authoritative international journal
- The Institute of Higher Education was honored as one of the Outstanding Higher Education Research Institutions of China (3rd edition)

## Academic Exchanges and Conferences

- Vice President Lin Danming led a delegation to the 2011 Sino-Foreign University Partnership Fair
- Prof. Mimi Koehl, marine biologist from UC Berkeley, visited STU
- The Guangdong Social Sciences Annual Conference symposium on "Local Government Functions and Social Public Management" was held at STU
- The 2011 Annual Meeting of the Marine Ecology Committee of the Ecological Society of China was held at STU
- The 2011 International Conference on Environmental Omics was held in Guangzhou
- A Chaozhou business culture research meeting under the "Impression Chaoshan" series was held at STU
- The Sado Print Art Exhibition from Japan opened at STU, and the Business School held the third Chaozhou Business Forum
- A five-year review meeting on background investigations of juvenile delinquency was held in Chaozhou
- An academic symposium on the preservation and study of Chaoshan literature was held at STU

## Community Service and Technology Cooperation

- The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design completed the graffiti design and production for the seawall along Haibin Road in Shantou
- Leveraging the STU Institute for Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation, the Business School has actively served local economic and social development

## Other Research Activities

- The university convened a mobilization meeting for 2012 natural sciences project applications
- An on-campus research fund academic forum for 2011 was held
- The Guangdong provincial meeting on 2011 science and social sciences statistical annual reports for universities was held in Huizhou
