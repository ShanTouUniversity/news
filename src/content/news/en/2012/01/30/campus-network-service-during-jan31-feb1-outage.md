It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University has issued a notice regarding campus network services during the power outages scheduled for January 31 and February 1, 2012.

According to the outage plan from the Resource Management Office, all data center and related equipment rooms will be within the affected area on January 31 and February 1. The UPS backup power in the data center can support more than four hours of operation, during which outbound network access, internal application systems and website services will largely remain unaffected, while internet access for users in other campus areas will be affected.

The Network and Information Center stated that if the actual outage lasts more than four hours, non-essential services will be shut down to prioritize the core network and critical systems; if it exceeds seven hours, all services will be suspended. Services will be restored as soon as stable power supply resumes.

|------|---------|
