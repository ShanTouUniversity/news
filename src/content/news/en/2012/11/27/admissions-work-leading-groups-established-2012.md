STU has issued Document No. 148, establishing an admissions leading group and an admissions working group to coordinate the university’s admissions work. The decision was considered and approved at the 14th university leadership meeting on November 20, 2012.

### Organizational structure

The admissions leading group is headed by Xu Xiaohu and another chair whose name is garbled in the archived source. Gu Peihua serves as deputy head, with Luo Nianchao, Wen Xiaobo, Li Dan, Lin Danming, Wulanhas, Huang Dongyang, and Xu Zongling as members. The garbled name is not reconstructed speculatively.

The admissions working group is headed by Gu Peihua, with Wen Xiaobo and Xu Zongling as deputy heads. Its members are Bai Youjin, Terry Bodenhorn, Chen Jian, Chen Maohuai, Chen Shaogang, Chen Yan, Fan Dongsheng, He Wenbiao, Lin Bingfeng, Lou Zengjian, Lu Xiaohua, Michael Palmer, Sun Fenqiang, Wang Shouzhi, Yu Hong, Yu Shanyan, Zeng Rui, and Zou Zhibo. The working-group office will operate together with the Admissions Office, with Zeng Rui serving concurrently as office director.

The STU Admissions Work Committee will cease operations following the establishment of the new structure.

> Source: STU university document (No. 148, 2012)
