The STU union will hold a meeting of faculty congress delegation leaders and deputy leaders to solicit views on the draft trial Measures for Faculty and Staff Performance Evaluation and arrange related faculty congress work.

The meeting will begin at 4 p.m. on Wednesday, November 28, in the university union meeting room. Leaders and deputy leaders of all faculty congress delegations should attend on time.
