The STU union has announced the matchups for the faculty volleyball semifinals, scheduled for the afternoon of November 27. The School of Science will play the School of Engineering, while the Arts United Team will play the Library, Information Technology, Journalism, and Graduate Student United Team.

| Match | Teams | Court |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semifinal | School of Science vs. School of Engineering | Court 2 |
| Semifinal | Arts United Team vs. Library, Information Technology, Journalism, and Graduate Student United Team | Court 3 |
