STU’s Center for International Studies in the College of Liberal Arts will host a public lecture by Professor John Storey of the University of Sunderland, UK, titled “What Is Cultural Studies? What Does It Do? What Is It For?” The lecture is a companion event to the international symposium on new directions in Global Studies in the 21st century.

Storey is Professor of Cultural Studies and director of the Centre for Research in Media and Cultural Studies at the University of Sunderland. His work examines cultural studies, globalization, and everyday life. The lecture will be moderated by Wuming Zhao of the Center for International Studies.

The lecture will be held from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on Thursday, November 29, in E-Lecture Hall 201. All faculty members and students are welcome.
