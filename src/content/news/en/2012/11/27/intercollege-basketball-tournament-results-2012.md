STU’s 2012 intercollege basketball tournament concluded on November 25. The university Youth League Committee and Students’ Union have announced the results and congratulated the winning schools and individuals.

### Basketball results

| Division | Champion | Runner-up | Third place |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Men’s | School of Engineering | Business School | Medical College |
| Women’s | Business School | College of Liberal Arts | School of Law |

The men’s and women’s Most Valuable Player awards went to players from the School of Engineering and Business School respectively. The top three places in the three-point contest went to representatives of the Graduate School, School of Science, and School of Engineering. The fair-play referee award went to representatives of the Graduate School, School of Arts, and Zhicheng College. In accordance with student privacy requirements, individual student names are not reproduced.

The Youth League Committee and Students’ Union thanked faculty members and students for supporting and participating in the tournament.
