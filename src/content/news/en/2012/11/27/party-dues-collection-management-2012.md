STU’s Party Committee Organization and United Front Department has reiterated procedures for collecting and managing Party dues. In accordance with the Party Constitution and relevant regulations, all Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches should ensure that members pay dues on time and voluntarily and that branches and units keep proper records and make remittances.

After collecting dues, branch organization committee members should record each payment in the member’s Party dues certificate. In principle, each Party general branch or directly affiliated branch should remit dues to the university once every quarter. By the 15th day of the final month of each quarter, it should complete the Party Dues Collection Notice and remit the dues to the Party Committee Organization and United Front Department.

Each unit should also check its records and identify missing Party dues certificates while ensuring that each member has one certificate. Missing certificates should be collected from Room 315 of the New Administration Center by December 5.

The contacts are Ji Rongxin and Chen Ying, at 82902345.
