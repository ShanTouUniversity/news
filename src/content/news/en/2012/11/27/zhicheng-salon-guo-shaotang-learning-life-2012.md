STU’s Zhicheng College will hold its second Zhicheng Salon on November 29. Professor Guo Shaotang, visiting chair professor of social sciences at Hong Kong Baptist University and former executive vice president of the Beijing Normal University–Hong Kong Baptist University United International College, will speak with students on “Learning and Life.”

Guo worked at the Chinese University of Hong Kong for nearly 30 years, serving as dean of the Faculty of Arts, professor in the Department of History, and university counselor. He has extensive experience in whole-person education and earned his master’s and doctoral degrees from the University of California, Berkeley.

The salon will take place from 8 to 9:30 p.m. on November 29 in Lecture Room J-354 at Zhicheng College. It is organized by the College Society, the Chinese Classics Reading Club, and the Qianjing Inheritance Society. Students may register by sending their name, year and major, and contact information to `ATP@stu.edu.cn` by 6 p.m. on November 28.
