STU’s Office of Research has forwarded the application guide issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology and Department of Finance for the 2012 special funds supporting service-platform development for small, medium-sized, and micro enterprises in specialized townships. Relevant faculty members are invited to contact the townships concerned and explore joint applications.

Online applications must be submitted by 5 p.m. on December 8. Hard-copy materials must reach the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology’s application reception office by December 20. Applicants should prepare their materials in accordance with the application guide and follow the detailed requirements issued by the provincial department.

The STU research-office contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, at 86503271. The guide is available on the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology website: <http://www.gdstc.gov.cn/HTML/zwgk/tzgg/13534901044013875463396783606399.html>.
