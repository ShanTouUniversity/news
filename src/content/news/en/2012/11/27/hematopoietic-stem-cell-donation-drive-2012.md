The STU Business School Youth League Committee and Young Volunteers Association will hold a series of activities titled “Love in My Heart, Stem Cells in My Mind” from November 30 to December 2. The program will introduce hematopoietic stem-cell donation, provide information about the China Marrow Donor Program (CDMP), and arrange volunteer registration and blood-sample collection for interested members of the university community.

### Schedule

| Activity | Date | Venue | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Information display | November 30 | Golden Triangle | CDMP consultation, videos on blood diseases, banner signing, and a prize quiz |
| Blood-sample collection | December 2 | Below the Second Dining Hall | Blood-sample collection for stem-cell donor registration |

The activities aim to promote the volunteer spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress while helping students learn about leukemia, other blood diseases, and hematopoietic stem-cell donation. Participation requirements and the collection process will be explained by on-site staff.
