It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a Wednesday Movie screening on the evening of November 28, 2012, featuring *The Bicycle Thief*.

## Screening Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *The Bicycle Thief* (The Bicycle Thief, 1948) |
| Time | 19:00–22:00, November 28, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Host | Peter Herford |
| Seats | 50 public seats available on a first-come, first-served basis in addition to 150 class seats; students present campus card for entry |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

The film is a classic of Italian neorealism and the most renowned work of director Vittorio De Sica. Set in post-World War II Rome, it follows a father who has just found a job putting up posters but has his bicycle — essential for the work — stolen on his first day. He searches the city with his young son and, in desperation, attempts to steal another bicycle. Told with simple yet moving storytelling and natural performances by non-professional actors, the work vividly reflects postwar Roman society and has received an Honorary Academy Award and other honors.
