The 2012 National Unified Judicial Examination results show strong performance by the 2009 law cohort at STU’s Law School. Of the 112 students who registered for the examination, 74 passed by reaching at least 360 points, producing a 66.1% pass rate, six percentage points higher than the previous year.

Among those who passed, 23 students scored above 400 points, representing 31.1% of the successful candidates and a two-percentage-point increase over the previous year. In accordance with student privacy requirements, this report includes only the published aggregate figures and does not reproduce individual names.
