STU has issued Document No. 149, adjusting the organizational relationship of the Admissions Office and a related administrative duty. The decision was considered and approved at the 14th university leadership meeting on November 20, 2012.

Under the adjustment, the STU Admissions Office will no longer be affiliated with the Student Affairs Office. Zeng Rui, director of the Admissions Office, will no longer serve as deputy director of the Student Affairs Office.

> Source: STU university document (No. 149, 2012)
