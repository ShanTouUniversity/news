STU’s Party Committee Organization and United Front Department has asked grassroots Party organizations to study the exemplary deeds of Li Linsen in accordance with requirements from the provincial and municipal authorities and in connection with the university’s study of the spirit of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China.

All Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches should collect copies of the Party-building study reader *The Story of Li Linsen* from Room 315 of the New Administration Center by December 5. Each unit should organize Party members and other members of the university community to study the material and report its learning activities to the university Party Committee Organization and United Front Department.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Organization and United Front Department)
