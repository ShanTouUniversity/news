It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host the "Women in Media" forum on December 1, bringing together distinguished women representatives from leading global media organizations.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Women in Media — Distinguished Women from Leading Global Media Organizations |
| Chair | Associate Dean Ni Qingqing (Pulitzer Prize winner) |
| Main forum time | December 1 (Saturday) 19:30–21:30 |
| Main forum venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Additional sessions | Separate sharing sessions with individual guests will be held on the morning and afternoon of December 1 at ACC and the School's Glass House |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |
| Release date | November 27, 2012 |

The forum is open to students and faculty interested in television, newspapers, broadcasting, news agencies and public relations, with an open interactive session.

## Guest List

| Guest | Affiliation and Title |
| --- | --- |
| Marsha Cooke | Managing Editor, Asia-Pacific and Television News Producer, CBS (Columbia Broadcasting System) |
| Normandy Madden | Senior Vice President, Asia-Pacific, Thoughtful Media Group (TMG) |
| Mary Kay Magistad | Senior Foreign Correspondent, The World (Public Radio International) |
| Wang Xiaoqing | Host of major programs, TV program writer, director and producer, CCTV (China Central Television) |
| Carrie Hong (Hong Xiaoyan) | Bond News (Capital Markets) Reporter based in Hong Kong, Reuters |
| Helen Zia (Xie Hanlan) | Renowned Chinese-American writer, journalist, editor and civil rights activist |

For more details, please refer to the announcement on the School of Journalism and Communication website at http://media.stu.edu.cn/?p=15105.
