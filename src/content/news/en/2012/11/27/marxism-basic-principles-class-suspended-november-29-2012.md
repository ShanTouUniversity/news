The STU Academic Affairs Office has announced that one meeting of course section 54247, *Basic Principles of Marxism*, will be suspended because the instructor will be away. The class originally scheduled for periods A, B, and C on Thursday, November 29, in Room E204 will not be held. A make-up time has not yet been set.

Students enrolled in the course should inform one another of the change. The make-up arrangement will be announced in a subsequent university notice.
