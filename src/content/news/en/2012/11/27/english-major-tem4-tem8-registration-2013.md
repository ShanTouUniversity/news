STU’s Academic Affairs Office has opened registration for the 2013 Test for English Majors Band 4 (TEM4) and Band 8 (TEM8). Eligible English-major undergraduates must complete online registration within the announced period.

### Eligibility and examination dates

| Test | Eligible students | Examination time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TEM4 | Second-year English majors admitted in 2011; 2010-entry students who failed the 2012 TEM4 may take one make-up attempt | 8:30 a.m. on April 20, 2013 |
| TEM8 | Fourth-year English majors admitted in 2009; 2008-entry students who failed the 2012 TEM8 may take one make-up attempt | 8:30 a.m. on March 23, 2013 |

The make-up opportunity is limited to one attempt. Students who do not take it will be considered to have waived the opportunity, which will not be deferred. The TEM4 registration fee is RMB 40 per person, and the TEM8 fee is RMB 45.

### Registration arrangements

Online registration runs from November 27 to December 2, 2012. Current undergraduates should log in to the credit-based education website and complete the process through “Course Selection — Qualification Examination Registration.” The Finance Office will ask the Network Center to deduct the fee from registered students’ campus-card accounts on December 3, so students should ensure that sufficient funds are available in advance.

Candidates must bring both their identity cards and admission permits to the examination. The Academic Affairs Office asked the College of Liberal Arts to notify all eligible students and remind them to complete registration and prepare the required documents on time.
