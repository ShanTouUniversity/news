To support the development of a green campus and address the collection of hazardous waste from offices and laboratories, Shantou University’s Resource Management Office will collect and process the materials centrally.

Office waste covered by the collection includes toner, toner cartridges, ink cartridges, used batteries, correction fluid, spent fluorescent tubes, waste electronic components and empty medicine bottles. Expired, surplus or waste laboratory liquids are also included; the original notice’s attachment lists the specific liquid types.

### Packaging and handover requirements

Units should sort the waste, complete the *Shantou University Hazardous Waste Collection and Handover Form*, and complete a separate form for waste liquids. The forms should be printed in duplicate for the handover.

Each type of waste liquid must be placed in a separate bottle, sealed with a cap and labeled with the submitting unit, liquid name or composition, weight and collection date. Units must pack the bottles securely in cartons to prevent collisions. Waste liquids that do not meet the packaging and labeling requirements will not be accepted.

Laboratory waste must not contain toxic substances, explosives, especially explosive materials, radioactive substances, polychlorinated biphenyls or cyanides. If prohibited substances are found in a handover, the transferring unit will bear the corresponding economic and legal responsibility.
