*Listening to Water in Silence: Gao Fei Watercolor Exhibition* will be held from November 2 to 6, 2012, at the Art Museum of Shantou University’s Yangtze River School of Art and Design. The exhibition is organized by the school and is open to faculty members and students across the university.

The exhibition will present Gao Fei’s watercolor depictions of industrial environments and urban spaces, including old vehicles, mechanical tools, abandoned factory sites and overlooked city landscapes. The exhibition preface describes the works as combining fluid brushwork, weighty colors and plain narration while maintaining a sustained focus on industrialized landscapes.

The preface also places the exhibition within the history of watercolor’s transmission and development in Guangdong and considers Gao Fei’s work in the broader context of art addressing early industrial landscapes. It was written in Shantou on October 25, 2012, by Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Yangtze River School of Art and Design.
