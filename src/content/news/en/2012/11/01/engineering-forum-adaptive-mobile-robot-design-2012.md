The 135th Engineering Research Forum at Shantou University’s School of Engineering will be held on November 2, 2012. Dr. Li Yi will present “Application of Adaptive Design Methods to Mobile-Robot Design.” Faculty members in the Department of Electromechanical Engineering and interested students are welcome to attend.

Dr. Li graduated from the University of Calgary and researches adaptive design and its applications. He has published multiple papers in SCI- and EI-indexed journals and has conducted successful practical work in several application areas.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | Engineering Research Forum No. 135 |
| Topic | Application of Adaptive Design Methods to Mobile-Robot Design |
| Speaker | Dr. Li Yi |
| Time | 10:00 a.m., November 2, 2012 |
| Venue | Engineering School Academic Exchange Room |
| Audience | Faculty members in Electromechanical Engineering and interested students |

The presentation will introduce mobile robots and their uses, examine problems in existing mobile robots and new user needs, discuss product adaptability in mobile-robot design, outline adaptive-design processes and evaluation, and follow the path from laboratory prototype to product development.
