Shantou University’s Physical Education Department will administer the National Student Physical Health Standard test to all 2011 and 2012 undergraduate students. Students from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, international students, students with incomplete ethnicity information and students with incomplete identity-card numbers will be temporarily excluded from this round of testing.

The test has six components: height, weight, vital capacity, standing long jump, grip strength, and a 1,000-meter run for men or 800-meter run for women. Students enrolled in physical education this semester will receive their test schedule from their instructors. Students not enrolled in physical education will test on November 24 and 25, the weekend of Week 10; their specific time slots are listed in the attachment.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | All 2011 and 2012 undergraduates, except the groups listed as temporarily excluded |
| Components | Height, weight, vital capacity, standing long jump, grip strength, and 1,000-meter/800-meter run |
| Students taking physical education | Schedule arranged by physical-education instructors |
| Students not taking physical education | Centralized testing on November 24–25 |
| Location | Physical Fitness Testing Room beneath the track grandstand and the track |

### Procedure and notes

Students will complete height, weight, vital capacity and grip-strength tests in the testing room, then move to the track for the standing long jump followed by the distance run. Students with a recent physical examination may report their height and weight to the testing teacher. Participants should check and report each result after completing a component so that all six results are recorded; a missing result will make the test invalid.

Students must attend at the assigned time, wear sports clothing and shoes, and warm up in advance. Students with physical disabilities, chronic illness, heart disease, congenital developmental conditions or other conditions that make the required components unsuitable should complete the *Application for Exemption from the National Student Physical Health Standard*, attach a medical institution’s certificate and place the documents in the exemption-application box at the gymnasium office before Week 10. Testing methods and scoring rules are provided in the original attachments.
