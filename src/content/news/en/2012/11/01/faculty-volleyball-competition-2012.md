Shantou University’s Trade Union will hold a faculty volleyball competition on the afternoon of Monday, November 19, 2012. The participating teams will be divided by draw into Groups A and B. Faculty members may register through the trade union of their unit.

| Group | Teams |
| --- | --- |
| A | School of Science; Administrative Team; Library/Science and Technology Office/News Center/Graduate Union Team; School of Liberal Arts |
| B | School of Engineering; Art School/Physical Education (non-PE faculty)/Social Sciences Continuing Education/Affiliated Middle School Team; Resource Management Office; Law and Business Team |

Each team must email its registration form to the university trade union at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn by November 9, using “Volleyball Competition Registration Form” as the email subject. Teams should arrive on time according to the schedule to be issued separately. The Trade Union will provide drinking water.
