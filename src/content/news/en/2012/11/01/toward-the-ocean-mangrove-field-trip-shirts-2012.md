Shantou University’s *Toward the Ocean*, *Exploring Marine Life* and *Introduction to Marine Biological Resources* courses have announced the T-shirt collection arrangements for the Suae Bay mangrove field study. Students who registered for the study in class and recorded their T-shirt sizes should collect their shirts at the designated times and wear them as instructed during the fall 2012 field activity.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | Students in the related courses who registered for the study and recorded a T-shirt size |
| Collection times | 7:00–9:00 p.m. on November 1; 9:00–11:30 a.m. and 2:30–5:30 p.m. on November 2 |
| Collection location | Biology Department Meeting Room, Science Building 430 |
| Related activity | Fall 2012 Suae Bay mangrove field study |

The field study is part of the practical program for the *Toward the Ocean* public-service course. Registered students should follow the course team’s arrangements.
