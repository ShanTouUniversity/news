Shantou University will hold interviews for the 2011–2012 Outstanding Student Scholarship on November 7, 2012. The interview will take place in Science Building 205. Thirty candidates will participate and will be randomly divided into five groups of six.

The interview will use a leaderless group discussion format. Each group will begin with self-introductions of no more than one minute per member, followed by a drawing to determine the discussion topic. Students will have two minutes to prepare, then discuss the topic for 10 minutes, followed by eight minutes of questions from the judges.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Interview | 2011–2012 Outstanding Student Scholarship |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., Wednesday, November 7, 2012 |
| Venue | Science Building 205 |
| Candidates | 30, randomly divided into five groups of six |
| Format | Leaderless group discussion |
| Requirement | Shortlisted students should prepare name cards and arrive on time |

The group assignments are included with the original notice. Candidate names and other personal information are not reproduced here to protect student privacy.
