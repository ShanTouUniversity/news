It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design of Shantou University launched recruitment for the STU Creative Arts Ambassadors volunteer team on November 1 for the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. The team plans to recruit 20 undergraduate and postgraduate students from the 2012 cohort across the university.

## Team Mission

The STU Creative Arts Ambassadors is a volunteer team founded by students in the Department of Art Design’s Creative Industry Planning and Management program. Administrators and teachers from the school serve as advisers. The team focuses on arts and creative communication, organizing or assisting with creative arts activities and performances to help students understand event organization, develop teamwork and strengthen the creative arts atmosphere on campus.

In addition to helping organize arts activities, the team will periodically arrange performances, exhibitions, group sharing sessions and themed talks, offering students opportunities to engage with artistic creation and cultural exchange. The school noted that applicants do not need prior experience in arts performances or campus event organization, provided they have an interest in the arts and are willing to make a sustained commitment.

## Recruitment and Interview Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Target applicants | Undergraduate and postgraduate students from the 2012 cohort across the university |
| Number of recruits | 20 |
| Registration method | Complete an application form on site at the interview |
| Interview time | November 2, 2012 (Friday), 19:00–21:00 |
| Interview venue | Interactive Exchange Space, front building of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design (beside the first-floor staircase) |
| Interview focus | Candidates’ ability to respond and perform in the moment |

The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design advises interested students to attend the on-site interview and registration as scheduled. Further information about the team is available in the notice attachment.
