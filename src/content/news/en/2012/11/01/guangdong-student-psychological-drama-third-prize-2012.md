STU’s campus psychological drama *Dream Is Not Far Away*, created and performed by students, won third prize in the final of the third Guangdong University Student Psychological Drama Competition, which concluded recently. Twenty-two students took part in creating and performing the production. The university congratulated the students and thanked the university community for supporting the competition.

The competition was organized by the Guangdong Higher-Education Psychological Health Education and Counseling Professional Committee and received 65 entries from 42 universities. Experts organized by the committee conducted an anonymous review and selected first, second and third prizes as well as awards of excellence.

Student performer names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
