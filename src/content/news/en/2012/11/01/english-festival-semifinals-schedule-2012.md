The 2012 Shantou University English Festival semifinals will be held on November 3 and 4. Under the theme “Stories the World Should Hear,” contestants selected through the preliminary rounds will compete for places in the December finals. Finalists will also compete with participants from universities in China and abroad for English Festival honors.

| Competition | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inter-university English speech semifinal | 7:00 p.m., November 3 | Grand Auditorium |
| Inter-university English debate semifinal | 3:00 p.m., November 4 | Science Lecture Hall |
| Inter-university English singing semifinal | 7:00 p.m., November 4 | Grand Auditorium |

The debate competition will use the British Parliamentary format. Two debaters form a team, and four teams compete in each debate. Forty minutes before the debate, a draw will assign positions on the Government or Opposition side, with each side divided into Opening and Closing teams. The motion will be announced before the debate and teams will prepare at a designated location. The debate begins with the Opening Government; each speaker has seven minutes. Judges will score all eight speakers and select the best speaker and winning team for each debate.

The semifinal participant list is included with the original notice. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
