Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has announced the participant arrangements and study requirements for its advanced Teacher Training Methods course. The course will be taught on November 3 and 4, 2012. Participants must complete assignments as requested by the instructors and attend the full course.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Advanced Teacher Training Methods |
| Schedule | November 3–4, 8:30 a.m.–noon and 2:30–5:30 p.m. each day |
| Venue | Lecture Classroom E202, Teaching Building E |
| Requirements | Submit assignments as requested and attend the full course |
| Completion | Participants who pass will receive a Guangdong professional-technical personnel advanced-technology training completion certificate |

Certificates will be issued together after the course and assignments have been completed. The participant list is included with the original notice.
