The sixth Kan Kan Culture event at Shantou University’s Yangtze River School of Art and Design will be held on November 5, 2012. Professor Guo Qiyong of Wuhan University’s School of Philosophy and School of Chinese Classics will engage in a dialogue with Professor Wang Shouzhi, dean of STU’s Yangtze River School of Art and Design, on “Classical Learning and Personal Growth.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Kan Kan Culture 6 |
| Topic | Classical Learning and Personal Growth |
| Guests | Professor Guo Qiyong, Wuhan University; Professor Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Yangtze River School of Art and Design |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., Monday, November 5, 2012 |
| Venue | STU Science Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Yangtze River School of Art and Design |

Faculty members and students across the university are welcome to attend.
