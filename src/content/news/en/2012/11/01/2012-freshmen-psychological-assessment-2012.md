To establish psychological records for the 2012 freshman cohort, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will conduct psychological assessments on November 3, 2012. With support from the Computer Teaching Center, students may complete the assessment free of charge in Computer Rooms E101 and E102 of Teaching Building E during the designated period.

Each student will have 60 minutes to complete the assessment. After signing in to the Psychological Counseling Center’s assessment system, students should read the instructions, complete their basic profile and change the initial login information before entering “My Tests” and completing all three scales. Afterward, students can view their personal results in “My Report” and use “Appointments and Counseling” to submit questions, select a counselor and check appointment messages from the center.

### Participation note

Students should use the personal account assigned by the university, with login details governed by the university notice. Students may use their accounts during their time at STU to check appointment messages from the counseling center. Student numbers, passwords and psychological assessment results are not reproduced here.
