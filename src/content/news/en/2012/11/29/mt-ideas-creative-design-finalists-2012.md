The preliminary round of the second MT-IDEAS creative design competition, organized by the MT Enterprise Innovation Club of STU Engineering College, took place on November 25. Judges selected seven teams for the final round, which will be held at 7 p.m. on Saturday, December 1, at the CDIO Innovation Practice Center. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

### Finalist projects

| Team | Ideas in the final round |
| --- | --- |
| Boiling Point Creative Team | “Custom Password” |
| Papadi Team | “Shantou University Debate Timer”; “The Beginning of Love” |
| 123Hello CS Team | “Community Mutual-Aid System” |
| Be Team | “Micro Campus” |
| Travelers Team | “Navigation Software”; “Voice Search Software”; “Profanity Filter”; “New Application Marketplace” |
| Creative Flight Team | “Let Me Help You” |
| V Focus Team | “Ready? Go”; “Premium Communications” |

Each team should prepare a project-introduction presentation of no more than 10 minutes covering the team, idea, innovation, market application, and feasibility. Detailed project materials are also required. Teams may continue improving or rethink their ideas until noon on December 1, the deadline for collecting final-round works.
