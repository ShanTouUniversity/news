Volunteers from STU’s School of Science Toward the Ocean public-welfare course will visit Yun’ao Middle School and nearby fishing villages on Nan’ao Island, Shantou, from December 1 to 2. Under the theme “Toward the Ocean, Understand the Ocean, Protect the Ocean,” they will promote awareness of marine territory and environmental protection.

The outreach is organized through the Toward the Ocean public-welfare course with support from Yun’ao Middle School and the Guangdong Nanpeng Archipelago National Nature Reserve. Its audiences include students at Yun’ao Middle School and residents of nearby fishing villages. Volunteers will conduct marine-conservation education and carry out interviews with fishermen in small groups.

Volunteers should gather at the university motto stele at 7:30 a.m. on December 1 before traveling to Nan’ao. The supervising faculty members are Sun Zewei, Chen Yichun, Zhang Liting, Yang Mulan, and Wang Junying.

### Safety and service guidance

Participants must stay with the group, follow the supervising teachers, and must not leave independently or swim in the sea. Because the island is expected to be rainy and windy, participants should bring rain and cold-weather gear and wear non-slip shoes. Lodging will be basic, so participants should bring personal toiletries. Volunteers should also protect the environment and conduct household or group interviews courteously.
