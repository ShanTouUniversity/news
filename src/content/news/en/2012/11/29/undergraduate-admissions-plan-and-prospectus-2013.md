STU’s Admissions Office has begun preparing the 2013 domestic undergraduate admissions plan and promotional materials. Following review by the university leadership meeting, the provisional university-wide plan for 2013 is 1,770 domestic undergraduate students. The university will make further adjustments based on college submissions and the final annual total.

### Admissions plan submissions

Each college should prepare a preliminary plan based on its 2012 admissions implementation, development plan, and the university’s needs. Submissions should identify the proposed total, provincial and non-provincial quotas, quotas for high-level athletes, and program names. Programs admitted by category should use standardized category names. Colleges proposing new programs must also provide written proof that the new program has been reviewed and approved by the university Teaching Committee. Any college whose total plan changes from 2012 should provide a separate written explanation.

### College and program profiles

Each college should prepare its 2013 college and program profile with a clear emphasis on its distinctive features and concise writing, using charts and bullet points where possible. Colleges should also provide recent, clear, high-resolution electronic photographs showing teaching, research, academic exchange, and student activities, with brief captions, along with examples and testimonials from outstanding students.

The College of Liberal Arts, School of Law, Business School, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design should keep the text within 1,000 Chinese characters. The School of Science, School of Engineering, and Medical College should keep the text within 1,500 Chinese characters.

### Submission deadlines

| Material | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| 2013 college admissions plan form | December 20, 2012 |
| 2013 college and program profile | December 28, 2012 |

Colleges should submit paper materials to the Admissions Office and send electronic documents to `o_zsb@stu.edu.cn`. The office is on the first floor of the Administration Center and can be reached at 86502403 or 86503666.
