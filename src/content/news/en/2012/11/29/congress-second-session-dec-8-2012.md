It is reported that the second session of the Fifth Congress of Teaching Staff and the Seventh Congress of Labor Union Members of Shantou University will be held on Saturday morning, December 8.

## Conference Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 8:30 a.m. – 12:00 p.m., December 8 |
| Venue | Science College Lecture Hall |
| Check-in | Delegates should arrive 15 minutes early to sign in |

Details on the conference agenda and delegation lists are provided in the attached materials.

## Requirements

To ensure the smooth convening of the conference, the university labor union asked that delegating units not arrange business trips or conflicting activities for delegates during the conference. Delegates with special circumstances should request leave from their delegation leader or the union chair of their department.

Each delegation should compile its attendance figures and report them to the university labor union by December 5. The union can be reached by phone at 82902983 or 86502983, and by email at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn.
