STU Engineering College will hold an information session on the Guangdong-Hong Kong ICT Youth Entrepreneurship Program on December 5. The program is jointly promoted by the Hong Kong Cyberport Enterprise Development Center and the Guangdong Software Industry Association and offers young people business training, cross-border team collaboration, and startup support.

The session will take place from 3 to 5 p.m. in E-Lecture Hall 201 in Building E and is open to students across the university. The speakers will be Zhong Weiqiang, technical director of Cyberport Management Company, Lin Yuzhen, business development manager, and Lü Hui, secretary-general of the Guangdong Software Industry Association.

### Program overview

The program aims to develop young people’s business skills, create cooperation opportunities for young people in Guangdong and Hong Kong, and support entrepreneurship. Applicants must be at least 18 and under 30, be enrolled at a postsecondary institution registered in Hong Kong or Guangdong, or have graduated within the previous three years. Projects must involve creativity and information and communications technology, including emerging Internet services, Web 3.0, applications, cloud computing, mobile applications, digital entertainment, and creative digital lifestyles.

Supported by the Cyberport Creative Micro Fund (CCMF), the program lasts six months. Each selected team may receive a total cash grant of HK$100,000 to develop a prototype, with no repayment required. Selection will involve online applications, eligibility screening, review by a vetting committee, and matching of Guangdong-Hong Kong teams.

The grant will be released in three installments of HK$10,000, HK$45,000, and HK$45,000 after agreement signing, approval of the midterm report, and approval of the final report respectively. The Hong Kong Polytechnic University will provide student training in entrepreneurial thinking, ICT trends and business models, business plans, project management, presentations, and team formation.

The program website is <http://gdhkictyep.cyberport.hk>. Please follow the official website and OA attachments for detailed eligibility, application materials, and procedures.
