The STU Graduate School and Graduate Students’ Union will launch the first Sangpu Language Forest event on November 30. Under the theme “A Historical and Contemporary Discussion of Women’s Appeal,” the event aims to enrich the graduate academic and cultural atmosphere and encourage discussion of historical, cultural, and social questions.

The event will be held from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on November 30 in the Graduate Students’ Union central hall on the second floor of Building Y. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.
