It is reported that the Department of Law's course *Consumer Life and Consumer Rights Protection Law* (class numbers 54611 and 54612) will swap its classroom and field practice schedules due to teaching arrangements.

## Adjusted Schedule

| Week | Original Plan | Adjusted Plan |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Week 11, Friday (Nov 30), Periods 1-2 & 3-4, E303 | Classroom session | Field practice |
| Week 12, Friday (Dec 7), Periods 1-2 & 3-4 | Field practice | Classroom session (E303) |

The Academic Affairs Office reminds all enrolled students to inform each other and follow the updated schedule.
