STU’s Graduate School has announced the degree-certificate photo collection schedule for graduate students expected to graduate in 2013 and 2014. The collection will take place from 8:30 a.m. to noon on December 14 in the mock courtroom in Building E and covers all eligible graduate students on the main campus.

### Unit schedule

| Unit | Students | Time |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| College of Liberal Arts | 38 | 8:30–8:40 a.m. |
| School of Journalism and Communication | 12 | 8:40–8:45 a.m. |
| School of Art and Design | 46 | 8:45–8:55 a.m. |
| Institute of Higher Education | 26 | 8:55–9 a.m. |
| Department of Social Sciences | 9 | 9–9:05 a.m. |
| Business School | 146 | 9:05–9:35 a.m. |
| School of Law | 86 | 9:35–9:50 a.m. |
| School of Science | 205 | 9:50–10:50 a.m. |
| School of Engineering | 207 | 10:50–11:50 a.m. |
| Doctoral students | 21 | 11:50 a.m.–noon |

The on-campus collection fee is RMB 15 per student. Students expected to graduate should verify their graduation information and arrange on-campus collection according to the instructions of the Graduate School and their units. Students unable to attend on campus for special reasons may contact the Guangdong Image Collection Center and complete the process at the Photography Department, Room 205, 21 Fuqian Road, Guangzhou, at 020-83302077.

Students who completed degree-certificate photo collection in an earlier year do not need to participate again. Unit academic-affairs staff should organize attendance in advance. Please follow your unit’s instructions for individual schedules, identity verification, and subsequent material submission.
