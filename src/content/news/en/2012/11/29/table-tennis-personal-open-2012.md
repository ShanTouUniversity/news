The STU Table Tennis Association will hold its 15th personal open tournament to enrich student life and promote campus sports. The tournament is organized by the STU Youth League Committee and Students’ Association Union and hosted by the university Table Tennis Association.

The tournament will take place at the STU gymnasium from December 8 to 9 and is open to all students.

### Events and registration

The notice lists men’s singles, women’s singles, men’s doubles, and mixed doubles, and allows each participant to enter multiple events. Registration closes at noon on December 5, and the draw format is expected to be posted on the university BBS that evening. Please follow the association’s OA attachments and latest notice for the registration form and channels; personal email addresses, phone numbers, and individual collection details are not reproduced here.

The men’s singles event will award the top eight places, while the other groups are generally to award the top four. The original award clause separately lists women’s doubles, which does not match the competition list’s reference to mixed doubles. Participants should follow the organizers’ final arrangement.
