STU will host the Symposium on Several Frontier Problems in Mathematics from November 29 to December 2. The event is intended to strengthen academic exchange among researchers in China and abroad and encourage interaction among different areas of mathematics.

The symposium will take place at the STU Academic Exchange Center. The thematic talks on November 30 will be held in Conference Room 3, and faculty members and students are welcome to attend.

### Research topics

The talks will cover analytic functions and fractals, multivariate wavelets, boundary regularity for parabolic Monge–Ampère equations, Calderón reconstruction formulas and wavelet frames, link invariants from homotopy groups of the three-sphere, quantitative unique continuation for singular partial differential equations, and operator theory in quantum information and quantum computing.

Afternoon presentations will also address dynamical systems and computation, global regularity for coupled Navier–Stokes equations, reproducing-kernel Banach spaces with the l1 norm, Mean-CVaR portfolio selection, and partial quadratic eigenvalue assignment with time delay.

### Academic exchange

Participants will come from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, the Institute of Mathematics and Systems Science of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Peking University, Nanjing University, Nankai University, Dalian University of Technology, Xiamen University, Zhejiang Sci-Tech University, Beijing University of Technology, Xiangtan University, Shaanxi Normal University, Sun Yat-sen University, Shenzhen University, Guangdong University of Technology, STU, and other universities and research institutions. The symposium will use thematic presentations and peer discussion to exchange recent findings and future directions.
