STU’s Academic Affairs Office has opened applications for the 2012 Undergraduate Innovation Experiment Projects. The program is available to full-time undergraduates across the university and aims to encourage participation in research and invention while strengthening students’ innovation and practical skills.

### Application requirements

A project leader must have more than one year remaining before graduation. Projects should normally be submitted by teams, although individual applications are also permitted, and each team may have no more than five students. Each student may participate in only one project. Cross-disciplinary, cross-major, and cross-year collaboration is encouraged, and each project must have one or two faculty supervisors and normally be completed within one year.

Students should select topics, design plans, conduct the work, and prepare final reports independently under faculty guidance. Topics should be scientifically sound, innovative, and exploratory. Project members must observe academic standards and university financial rules and use project funds responsibly.

### Application and review

| Stage | Timing or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| College applications for project quotas | November 29–December 6 |
| College applications and reviews | December 3–January 4, 2013 |
| Application process | Propose a topic, form a team, appoint a supervisor and finalize the plan, complete the application, undergo college review, and submit the recommendation to the Academic Affairs Office |
| Materials | Submit three paper copies and one electronic copy to the Academic Affairs Office by January 4, 2013 |

The Academic Affairs Office will review and consolidate the projects recommended by the colleges and determine the provincial and university-level projects after a public notice period without objections. Project information will be entered into the credit-based education system and managed as a practical course.

### Management and funding

The university will conduct a midterm review. Project leaders must report progress, future plans, and fund use. At completion, they must submit an acceptance form and materials such as experimental samples, reports, papers, designs, patents, and fund-use records. Changes to the research content, project members, or completion date require a written request approved by the supervisor and reviewed by the Academic Affairs Office.

General funding is RMB 10,000 per science or engineering project and RMB 5,000 per humanities project. Half of the approved amount will be allocated at project launch, with the remainder released after a successful midterm review. Funds may cover books and reference materials, network services, experimental materials, simple instruments, design, research, and paper publication expenses related to the project and may not be diverted. Students who complete a project and pass the final review may receive two research and innovation credits.
