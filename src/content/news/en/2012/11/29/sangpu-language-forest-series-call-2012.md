STU’s Graduate School has launched the Sangpu Language Forest series and is inviting graduate students to propose presentation topics and discussion themes. The series is designed to reflect STU’s “4C” education philosophy, encourage active thinking and interdisciplinary exchange, broaden perspectives, and build a stronger graduate-school community.

### Series arrangements

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | Provisionally one session every two weeks |
| Time | Provisionally Fridays, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Graduate Students’ Union central hall, second floor of the graduate residence in Building Y |
| Presentation format | One speaker or one speaker team per session |
| Eligible participants | All graduate students |

Individuals or teams wishing to present should submit a topic to the Graduate Students’ Union. A topic may be scheduled after discussion and approval. Once approved, the speaker or team should communicate with the union’s Academic Department and submit the presentation content, slides, and other required materials at least two weeks in advance. Please contact the Academic Department for the current submission method.
