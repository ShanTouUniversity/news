| Class No. | Course | Instructor | Venue | Students | Notes |

| 55161 | Advanced Mathematics I | Lin Xiaoping | E-Building 203, 204 | 155 | Seating as directed by instructor |
| 55173 | Advanced Mathematics I | Xie Changzhen | Lecture Hall 1, Lecture Hall 3 | 152 | — |
| 55174 | Advanced Mathematics I | Xu Fei | E303, E409 | 138 | — |
