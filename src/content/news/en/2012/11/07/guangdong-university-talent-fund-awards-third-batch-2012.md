STU’s Office of Human Resources has announced the third-batch 2012 project approvals under the Guangdong University Talent Introduction Special Fund. Following the review by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, four Shantou University professors received support under the program. The announcement cites the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance notice on 2012 provincial education funds, Yue Cai Jiao [2012] No. 328.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Fund | Guangdong University Talent Introduction Special Fund |
| Year | 2012 |
| Batch | Third batch |
| STU recipients | Four professors |
| Reviewing body | Guangdong Provincial Department of Education |

The university asks the leaders of the supported projects to follow the Interim Measures for the Administration of the Guangdong University Talent Introduction Special Fund and ensure that the projects are completed successfully. The approved-project list is embedded in the original notice as an image that cannot be reliably read, so the individual names are not reconstructed here.
