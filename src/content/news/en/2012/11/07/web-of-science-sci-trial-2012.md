The Shantou University Library has opened a trial of the Web of Science database, including the Science Citation Index (SCI), through December 5, 2012. Web of Science is a major citation-indexing resource, and SCI covers high-quality academic journals in the natural sciences and engineering.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Database | Web of Science, including SCI |
| Trial end date | December 5, 2012 |
| Access | [Library database details page](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=317) |
| Coverage | More than 170 disciplines, over 8,000 high-quality academic journals, and nearly a century of data |
| Eligible users | STU faculty members and students |

The Library describes SCI as a citation index with a strong academic reputation based on its content, data quality, and long history. Faculty members and students may use the database during the trial and send comments to the Library’s Subject Services Department at `3247`, `lqxie@stu.edu.cn`, or `zhzeng@stu.edu.cn`.
