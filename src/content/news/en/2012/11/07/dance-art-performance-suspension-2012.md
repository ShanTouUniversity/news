It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on November 7, 2012, that the *Dance Art Performance* course will be cancelled due to the instructor’s business trip.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Dance Art Performance (Class 54834) |
| Originally scheduled | Weeks 8–9, Saturdays (November 10 and 17), periods 6–7 |
| Venue | Dance Rehearsal Hall STU102 |
| Adjustment | Cancelled; make-up time to be announced |
| Instructor | Dou Bo, Art Education Center |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students enrolled are asked to inform each other.
