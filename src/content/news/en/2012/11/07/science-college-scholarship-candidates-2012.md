The Shantou University College of Science has announced the 2012 review results for the Pan Yugang Scholarship and Sendeli Chemical Scholarship. After applications closed, the college received 51 applications for the Pan Yugang Scholarship and 39 for the Sendeli Chemical Scholarship. The review committee selected eight students as candidates for the two scholarships in accordance with the relevant review regulations.

| Item | Details |
| --- | ---: |
| Pan Yugang Scholarship applications | 51 |
| Sendeli Chemical Scholarship applications | 39 |
| Total applications | 90 |
| Candidates for the two scholarships | 8 |
| Feedback deadline | Before the end of working hours on November 8 |
| Receiving office | College of Science Youth League Committee |
| Telephone | 82902779 |

The candidate list appears in the attachment to the original OA notice. Students with comments may contact the College of Science Youth League Committee during the public notice period in writing, in person, or by telephone. Individual submissions must be signed with the submitter’s real name. To protect student privacy, the candidate names are not reproduced here.
