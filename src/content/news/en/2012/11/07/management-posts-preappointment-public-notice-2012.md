STU’s Office of Human Resources has published a pre-appointment notice for five mid-level management positions. Following discussion at the 13th university leadership meeting on November 6, 2012, Li Peijie, Chen Li, Huang Xianglong, Xu Xiaojing, and Wang Fengqi have been proposed for appointment to the positions.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Notice | Proposed appointments to mid-level management positions |
| Number of proposed appointees | 5 |
| Meeting | 13th university leadership meeting, November 6, 2012 |
| Notice deadline | By November 13, 2012 |
| Receiving office | STU Office of Human Resources |
| Telephone | 82903390 |
| Email | `rscgs@stu.edu.cn` |

The public notice is issued under the Trial Measures for the Appointment of STU Management Cadres (STU Party Document [2009] No. 47). Organizations or individuals with comments may submit them in writing to the Office of Human Resources during the notice period. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, while individual submissions must be signed with the submitter’s real name.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Human Resources)
