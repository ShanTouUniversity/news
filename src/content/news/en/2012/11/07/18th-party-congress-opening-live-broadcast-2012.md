The 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China will open at 09:00 on November 8 at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing. Hu Jintao will deliver a report to the congress on behalf of the 17th Central Committee. China National Radio, China Central Television, People’s Daily Online, Xinhua Net, China Network Television, and China.com will provide live coverage.

STU’s Publicity Department of the Party Committee, Student Affairs Office, and Youth League Committee will open the Science Lecture Hall for students and faculty members to watch the live broadcast of the opening session.
