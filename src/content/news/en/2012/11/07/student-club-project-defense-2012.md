The Shantou University student club project-defense session for the 2012–2013 fall semester will be held on the afternoon of November 7. Twelve student clubs, including the Guitar Association, are scheduled to present their activity proposals. The session is organized by the STU Youth League Committee and the STU Student Clubs Federation in E207 of the E Teaching Building.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Defense time | 14:30–17:30 on Wednesday, November 7 |
| Venue | E Teaching Building E207 |
| Projects | Activity proposals from 12 student clubs |
| Check-in | By 14:15 |
| Organizers | STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Clubs Federation |

Participants are asked to dress neatly and maintain a positive professional demeanor. During the defense, presenters should listen carefully to the judges’ questions, answer concisely and directly, and avoid interrupting. Presenters must remain until the total scores are announced, and project leaders should prepare in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
