It is reported that since the renovation of 36 faculty apartments on Shantou University's west campus began on August 21, 2012, a number of items not covered by the original design have emerged during construction. After a preliminary estimate, the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office calculated that these anticipated additions would cost approximately RMB 95,000 extra, keeping the cumulative increase within the 10% cap set by university regulations.

## Major Additional Items

| Category | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Exterior walls | After removal of glass mosaic, the original walls were found to be uneven with poor verticality, requiring a thicker plaster base layer |
| Flooring | A 6 cm height difference was found on ground-floor interior floors, requiring an added concrete leveling layer |
| Door and window fittings | The original design lacked thresholds, door stops, curtain boxes, and mosquito screens; granite thresholds, stainless steel door stops, plywood curtain boxes, and stainless steel mosquito screens were added |
| Safety protection | As kitchen windows sit on the stair landing, stainless steel anti-theft grilles were added for safety |
| Plumbing and electrical | Added wall openings for air conditioner outlets, exhaust oil vents, air conditioner drainage pipes, and exhaust ducts; added drainage pipes and floor drains at the stair landing; replaced PVC floor drains of insufficient height with stainless steel ones |
| Bathrooms | The original design had no paper holders, towel racks, or hot water piping; stainless steel paper holders, double-towel racks, and hot water angle valves were added; mixing faucets were added to showers and basins due to the new hot water lines |

In accordance with the Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering Management of Capital Construction Projects of Shantou University, the Infrastructure Office has publicized the estimated cost of these additions. Anyone with objections may submit written feedback to the Discipline Inspection and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 5:30 p.m. on November 12.
