Shantou University has issued a notice establishing the Center for Faculty Development and Educational Evaluation. Following discussion at the 13th university leadership meeting on November 6, 2012, the university decided that the center would operate jointly with the Institute of Higher Education Research.

Ma Fengqi, deputy director of the Institute of Higher Education Research, will concurrently serve as director of the new center. The document was issued as STU Document [2012] No. 141 on November 8, 2012.
