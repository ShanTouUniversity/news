The Shantou University Office of Academic Affairs has announced the arrangements for the MAT1120 Calculus I midterm examination. The examination is scheduled for the 12th class period on Thursday of Week 9, November 15, and covers four class sections.

| Class section | Instructor | Examination rooms | Students |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 55176 | Chen Yanming | E406 and E407 | 119 |
| 55177 | Gu Minqiang | E408 and E409 | 120 |
| 55178 | Tan Chaoqiang | Lecture Halls 1 and 3 | 122 |
| 55179 | Li Jian | D503 and D504 | 97 |

Students should go to the assigned rooms, sit with an empty seat between students as arranged, and follow their instructors’ directions for seating. Students who have applied for permission to audit the course without attendance requirements must still take the examination on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Academic Affairs)
