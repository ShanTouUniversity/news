The 12th “My Career Dream” mock-interview series at Shantou University will present “Competing in the Workplace: A Live Interview Show” on the evening of November 11. Inspired by the television job-search program *Job Interview*, the event incorporates STU elements and gives students an opportunity to practice in a simulated formal-interview setting.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event time | 19:30–21:30 on November 11 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Participants | All STU students |
| Student interviewees | 4 |
| Updates | Sina Weibo: “@汕头大学模拟面试” |

Interviewers from several companies will ask questions from different professional perspectives and offer career advice. The panel includes Hu Nanshan, general manager of GF Securities’ Jiexi branch; Zhang Xiuling, customer service manager at Guangdong Chao Hong Ji Industry Co., Ltd.; Yang Mei, deputy director of human resources and administration for Chuanghong Real Estate’s eastern Guangdong regional headquarters; and Xu Daisen, executive deputy director of Chao’an Haibao Development and Construction Co., Ltd.

The event is organized by the STU Student Affairs Office, STU Youth League Committee, and STU Student Union, and is open to all STU students. To protect student privacy, the names, majors, and year levels of the student interviewees are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
