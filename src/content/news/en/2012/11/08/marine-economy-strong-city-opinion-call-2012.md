STU’s Office of Research Affairs is seeking comments from relevant faculty members on the Draft Implementation Opinion of the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee and Shantou Municipal People’s Government on Accelerating the Development of Shantou as a Strong Marine-Economy City. The draft was prepared by the Shantou Municipal Policy Research Office together with the Municipal Ocean and Fisheries Bureau as part of Guangdong’s effort to build a strong marine-economy province.

Relevant faculty members may review the draft and submit proposed revisions to the Office of Research Affairs by November 14. The contact is Hu Yunxiang at `82902813` or `yxhu@stu.edu.cn`.
