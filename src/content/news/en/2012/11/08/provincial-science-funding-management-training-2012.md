The Guangdong Science and Technology Department will hold a training session on provincial science and technology program fund management at the Shantou Science Museum on November 22. The session is intended for principal investigators of active provincial science-program projects and faculty members interested in applying for provincial science-program funding. STU’s Office of Research Affairs has announced the registration arrangements.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Training time | 09:00 on November 22 |
| Venue | Academic Lecture Hall, fourth floor, Shantou Science Museum |
| Topics | Science-program fund management and accounting |
| Participants | Principal investigators of active provincial science-program projects and faculty members planning to apply for such projects |
| Registration deadline | By November 13 |
| Office email | `cszhu@stu.edu.cn` |

The training is intended to explain Guangdong’s science-program fund-management policies for the 12th Five-Year Plan period, strengthen financial oversight, and improve the use of government science funding. Faculty members from the College of Science and College of Engineering should submit the registration form to their college research secretary. Faculty members from other units may email the form to the Office of Research Affairs.
