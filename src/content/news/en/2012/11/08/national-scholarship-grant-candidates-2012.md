The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has announced the review results for the 2011–2012 National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant. In accordance with relevant Guangdong education authorities’ guidance, the university allocated quotas according to the number of eligible applicants at each college or residential college. Each unit organized its review under the applicable interim management measures. Following review by the Student Affairs Office, the university is publicizing 291 candidates for the National Encouragement Scholarship and 1,300 candidates for the National Grant.

| Program | Candidates |
| --- | ---: |
| National Encouragement Scholarship | 291 |
| National Grant | 1,300 |

The review followed principles of openness, fairness, impartiality, and merit. Organizations or individuals with comments may submit them in writing to the Student Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office by 09:00 on Monday, November 12. The receiving office is Room 106, Administration Center. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, while individual submissions must be signed with the submitter’s real name. To protect student privacy, the candidate lists are not reproduced here.
