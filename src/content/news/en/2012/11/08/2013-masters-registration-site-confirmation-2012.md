The Shantou University Graduate School has announced on-site confirmation arrangements for the 2013 master’s degree examination. Final-year undergraduate students, including recommended graduate candidates, who selected the Shantou Admissions Office (test-center code 4407) as their registration site must complete the confirmation process as scheduled.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmation period | November 10–14, 2012, 08:30–11:30 and 14:30–17:00 |
| Venue | First floor of the Shantou Education Bureau |
| Address | Fengze Village, Block 35, Longhu District, Shantou |
| Test center | Shantou Admissions Office (4407) |
| Shantou Admissions Office | 88863455 |
| Graduate School | 82902424 |

Applicants must appear in person with a second-generation identity card, a graduation certificate or student card, and their online registration number. They must verify their registration information, have an electronic photograph taken, and pay the examination fee. Information confirmed by an applicant cannot be changed during the examination, retest, or admission stages. False or inaccurate information may result in cancellation of examination eligibility, admission eligibility, or student status.

To assist applicants, STU will arrange transportation to the test center on November 11 for students who registered for the service, with current undergraduates receiving priority. Vehicles will depart in two groups at 08:30 and 14:30 from the STU motto stele beside the national flag. Applicants who did not register for the service must travel independently; public buses 21 and 39 serve the area.

Additional instructions from the Shantou test center are available through the relevant notices published by the Shantou Admissions Office and the China Graduate Admissions Information Network.
