Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Department has deployed renewal elections for grassroots Party branches on the main campus. The branches have reached the end of their terms, and the university Party Standing Committee has approved new elections in accordance with the Party Constitution and the Regulations on the Work of Primary-Level Party Organizations in Regular Institutions of Higher Education.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Grassroots Party branches on the main campus |
| Organizing units | Party general branches |
| Deadline | By the end of November 2012 |
| Follow-up | File the election results with the Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee |

Each Party general branch is responsible for organizing and leading the renewal election work of its affiliated branches, completing the elections on schedule, and filing the results with the Organization and United Front Department.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee)
