Shantou University has announced hepatitis B vaccination arrangements for the 2012 freshman cohort as part of its campus health program. The university recommends a three-dose hepatitis B vaccination course for students who have not previously received the vaccine and whose hepatitis B surface antigen and surface antibody tests are both negative.

Vaccination is voluntary and self-funded. Under the fee standard cited by the Shantou epidemic-prevention station, an imported hepatitis B vaccine costs RMB 78 per dose. Students must complete hepatitis B surface antigen and surface antibody tests before vaccination.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Testing location | Second-floor laboratory of the University Hospital |
| Blood-draw period | November 7–16, 08:30–09:30 |
| Weekends | No blood draws on Saturdays or Sundays |
| Result collection | The afternoon after the blood draw |
| Follow-up | Register and reserve a vaccination time after receiving the results |
| Vaccine fee | RMB 78 per dose for the imported vaccine |

The arrangement applies to 2012 freshmen who meet the stated conditions and choose to be vaccinated. No individual test results or health records are included in this report.
