The eighth 2012 academic lecture of the Shantou University Business School will be held on the morning of November 7. Professor Yun He of the School of Economics and Management at Zhejiang Sci-Tech University will speak on “Modern Economics: Frameworks, Misconceptions, and Writing Standards” in the Business School’s second-floor Academic Seminar Room.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 10:00–11:30 on Wednesday, November 7 |
| Venue | Business School, second-floor Academic Seminar Room |
| Speaker | Professor Yun He, School of Economics and Management, Zhejiang Sci-Tech University |
| Topic | Modern Economics: Frameworks, Misconceptions, and Writing Standards |

The lecture will introduce the meaning of modern economics, the spread of modern economics education in mainland China, and the field’s research framework. It will also examine common misconceptions in learning and research and discuss techniques and standards for writing economics papers.

The event is open to Business School graduate and undergraduate students as well as faculty members and students across the university.
