The Shantou University Academic Exchange Center is recruiting male students enrolled at the main campus for hourly positions as restaurant service staff and floor service staff. Located beside Riyue Lake, the center receives visiting leaders, scholars, and business guests from different regions and countries.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Positions | Restaurant service staff; floor service staff |
| Applicants | Male students enrolled at the STU main campus |
| Pay | Hourly wages |
| Application deadline | By November 10, 2012 |
| Application email | `stuacc@stu.edu.cn` |
| Contacts | Ms. Weng and Ms. Li |
| Telephone | `82902668`, `82904138` |
| Consultation hours | 08:30–18:00 |

Applicants should have strong spoken English and Cantonese skills. Passing the College English Test Band 4 is preferred. Applicants should also have social and situational skills, a neat appearance, and a courteous manner. Application materials include the job application form and a one-inch frontal photograph; interview details will be sent by email.

To protect applicants’ privacy, this report does not reproduce the original notice’s instruction concerning personal identity documents at the interview. Applicants may contact the Academic Exchange Center directly about the required interview materials.
