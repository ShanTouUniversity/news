The Shantou University Youth League Committee and Young Volunteers Association have announced their 2011–2012 volunteer-service recognition results. A total of 63 volunteers were recognized: 19 were named “Exemplary Young Volunteers,” and 44 were named “Outstanding Young Volunteers.”

| Title | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Exemplary Young Volunteers | 19 |
| Outstanding Young Volunteers | 44 |
| Total | 63 |

During the year, Party and Youth League organizations and volunteer groups carried out a range of volunteer activities on and off campus. The organizers said the volunteers developed their abilities, broadened their experience, and contributed to campus culture and civic-mindedness through service.

The recognized volunteers will receive public recognition at the first general meeting of the STU Young Volunteers Association for 2012–2013 and the STU Outstanding Young Volunteers Recognition Ceremony. The award list appears in the attachment to the original OA notice; student names are not reproduced here.
