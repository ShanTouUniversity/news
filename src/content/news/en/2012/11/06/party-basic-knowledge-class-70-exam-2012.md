The STU Party School will hold the theory examination for its 70th Party Basic Knowledge Study Class on the evening of November 11. Participants in the 70th class, together with 69th-class participants whose deferral or make-up examination has been approved, must attend the assigned examination venue.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Examination time | 19:30–21:00 on Sunday, November 11 |
| Lecture Hall 1 | Participants from the Colleges of Liberal Arts, Law, Business, Journalism, and Zhicheng College |
| Lecture Hall 3 | Participants from the Colleges of Science, Engineering, Arts, the Graduate School, and staff participants |
| Seating | One seat between examinees |

Participants unable to attend for special reasons must obtain approval from their Party general branch or directly affiliated Party branch and complete the deferral procedure at the Party School Office. An unexcused absence will result in cancellation of class membership. Participants who fail or receive an approved deferral may take one make-up examination in the next study class.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee)
