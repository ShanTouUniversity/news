The Shantou University College of Journalism and Communication has completed major assignments for its 2011 cohort and opened a public notice period. The college used its 2011 student major-assignment plan and students’ submitted preference forms, followed by discussion by the Teaching Committee and approval by the college Party and administrative leadership.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Notice | Major assignments for the 2011 cohort of the College of Journalism and Communication |
| Notice period | November 6 until 12:00 on November 11 |
| Ways to submit feedback | Written submission, in person, or by telephone |
| Telephone | 82904068 |
| Email | `ylin@stu.edu.cn` |

Students may submit comments on the assignment results during the public notice period. Feedback must be signed with the submitter’s real name for accountability. The college will consider the feedback received before confirming the final major-assignment list.

Because the attachment contains students’ names and individual major-assignment information, the list is not reproduced here.
