It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a screening of *Hamlet* on the evening of November 7, 2012, hosted by Peter Herford.

## Screening Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *Hamlet* (1948 British version, adapted and directed by and starring Sir Laurence Olivier; winner of the Golden Lion at Venice and Academy Awards for Best Picture and Best Actor) |
| Time | 19:00–22:00, November 7, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Library |
| Host | Peter Herford |
| Seats | In addition to 150 course seats, 50 public seats are available on a first-come, first-served basis; students should bring their campus card |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

The version is regarded as the most classic screen adaptation of Shakespeare’s *Hamlet*, with Olivier’s performance and cinematic staging breaking theatrical constraints, and Jean Simmons’s portrayal of Ophelia also highly acclaimed. All faculty and students are welcome.
