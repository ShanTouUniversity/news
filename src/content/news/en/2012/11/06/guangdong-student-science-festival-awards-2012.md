The sixth Guangdong College Student Science and Academic Festival has announced its competition results, with Shantou University receiving honors in several events. The festival, themed “I Start a Business, I Am Happy,” was jointly organized by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology, and the Guangdong Students’ Federation from April to June 2012. It featured 15 events in five areas: innovation and entrepreneurship, practical competitions, science forums, energy conservation and emissions reduction, and public science and social science education.

## STU Award Results

| Competition or project | STU result | Award |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eighth Challenge Cup Guangdong College Student Entrepreneurship Plan Competition | Shantou University | Outstanding Organization Award |
| Challenge Cup entrepreneurship plan competition | Zhichi Technology Co., Ltd. project | Silver Award |
| Challenge Cup entrepreneurship plan competition | Hengli Thermal-Insulation Building Materials Co., Ltd.; Shantou Wangtuo E-Commerce Co., Ltd.; Xueduofen Interactive Education Platform; Maimai Patent Link; Shantou Deep Blue Software Co., Ltd.; Greenshell Green Shell Co., Ltd. projects | Bronze Awards |
| College Student Biochemistry Experiment Skills Competition | “Extraction and Measurement of Crude Polysaccharides from Gracilaria and Production of Nutritional Jelly” | First Prize, undergraduate category |
