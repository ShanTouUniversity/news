The first Eastern Guangdong Software and Information Industry Expo will be held at the Lin Baixin International Convention and Exhibition Center in Shantou on November 10–11. The expo is jointly organized by the Guangdong Provincial Commission of Economy and Information Technology and the Shantou Municipal Government. It will also feature the second Eastern Guangdong Cloud Computing Industry Summit and a talent exchange event for the software and information-services sector.

The expo aims to showcase innovation and applications in the region’s software and information industries, promote regional economic integration, and advance the integration of information technology and industrialization. STU’s Office of Research Affairs is inviting relevant university units to recommend software projects for display and encouraging interested faculty members and students to attend the summit and talent exchange activities.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Expo dates | November 10–11 |
| Venue | Lin Baixin International Convention and Exhibition Center, Shantou |
| Related events | Second Eastern Guangdong Cloud Computing Industry Summit; software and information-services talent exchange |
| Project materials | A description of no more than 500 Chinese characters and 2–3 related images per project |
| Submission deadline | Before the end of working hours on November 7 |
| Submission office | STU Office of Research Affairs |

Units recommending projects should submit the materials to the Office of Research Affairs by the deadline so that the office can produce exhibition panels. Further details are available from the Shantou Software Industry Association at `http://www.stisa.org.cn/2012/Index.html`.

Contacts are Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong. The Office of Research Affairs can be reached at `82903271` or `ygluo@stu.edu.cn`.
