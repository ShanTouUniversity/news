The Shantou University Hospital has opened its influenza vaccination program. Faculty members, family members, and students may register at the Planned Immunization Room of the hospital’s Health Care Department during working hours from November 8 to 14. Influenza is primarily transmitted through respiratory droplets, and people considering vaccination should make an individual decision in consultation with medical staff.

## Vaccination Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration period | Working hours, November 8–14 |
| Registration location | Planned Immunization Room, Health Care Department, University Hospital |
| Eligible age | Children and adults aged six months and older |
| Consultation telephone | 82902318 |
| Children over 3 and adults | One 0.5 ml dose |
| Children aged 6–35 months | Two 0.25 ml doses, four weeks apart |
| Adult vaccine fee | RMB 81.50 per dose for children over 3 and adults |
| Pediatric vaccine fee | RMB 59.00 per person-dose for children aged 6–35 months |

The vaccine is generally administered by intramuscular or subcutaneous injection below the deltoid muscle of the upper arm. People with fever, acute illness, or a cold should postpone vaccination. People allergic to eggs or other vaccine components, or with a history of Guillain–Barré syndrome, should not receive the vaccine. Pregnant people should exercise caution and consult medical staff first.

A small number of recipients may experience redness, swelling, pain, bruising, or a lump at the injection site, as well as low-grade fever, dizziness, headache, chills, or sweating. The notice says these reactions generally require no special treatment and resolve on their own within one or two days; questions should be directed to the University Hospital.
