STU’s Business School will hold an information session on its exchange partnership with the University of Victoria in Canada. The session will introduce the university and the local area, as well as the International Summer Business Management program (ISIBM) and International Winter Business Management program (IWIBM), and will include a question-and-answer discussion.

The partnership between the two business schools began in March 2010, and STU’s Business School sends selected outstanding undergraduates each year. The session will help undergraduate and graduate students learn about the programs and potential application opportunities.

The event will take place from 8 to 9:30 p.m. on Thursday, November 29, in E-Lecture Hall 202 in Building E. The speaker will be Li Yuxuan, international programs manager in the University of Victoria’s Executive Management Programs.
