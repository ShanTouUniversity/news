STU’s General Office has asked all units on the main campus to update common contact numbers in the quick-access directory on the university homepage and on their own unit pages. The first four digits of most university office numbers have now changed to 8650; numbers beginning with 8290 will remain available during a transition period before being discontinued.

Units should update their common numbers and adjust the list of offices by the end of the workday on November 29, adding or removing offices as appropriate for internal and external searches. Updated information should be sent to `o_pressoffice@stu.edu.cn` for the News Center to update on the homepage. The university will publish an announcement about the number changes in *Shantou Daily* on November 30.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
