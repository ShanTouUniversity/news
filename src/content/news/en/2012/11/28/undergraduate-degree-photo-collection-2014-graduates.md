STU’s Academic Affairs Office has announced the schedule for collecting degree-certificate photographs for the 2014 graduating class. The collection will take place on December 13 in the mock courtroom in Building E and covers main-campus undergraduates in the 2010 cohort, as well as eligible students from the 2007–2009 cohorts who have not previously completed the collection.

### College schedule

| College | Students | Time |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| College of Liberal Arts | 131 | 8:30–9 a.m. |
| School of Arts and Design | 189 | 9–9:50 a.m. |
| Business School | 243 | 9:50–11 a.m. |
| School of Science | 203 | 11 a.m.–noon |
| School of Journalism | 153 | 2:10–2:50 p.m. |
| School of Engineering | 364 | 2:50–4:20 p.m. |
| School of Law | 195 | 4:20–5 p.m. |

The collection fee is RMB 15 per student. Students in the 2010 cohort must complete the collection on campus. Students from the 2007–2009 cohorts who cannot return because of internships or other special circumstances may contact the Guangdong Image Collection Center and complete the process at the Photography Department, Room 205, 21 Fuqian Road, Guangzhou, at 020-83302077. Students using the off-campus service should follow the Academic Affairs Office’s instructions for subsequent submission of materials.

Students who completed the degree-certificate photo collection in an earlier year should not participate again. College academic-affairs staff should organize students’ attendance and submit consolidated fee, headcount, and related-material information by December 11. Students should follow their college’s instructions, wait outside the south entrance of the mock courtroom during their assigned period, and cooperate with on-site staff.
