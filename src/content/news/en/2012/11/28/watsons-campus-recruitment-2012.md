Watsons Group, part of the Cheung Kong Group, will hold its third 2012 campus recruitment event at STU from December 3 to 5. The event is open to graduating students and undergraduates in the 2010 cohort and includes a company presentation, company exhibition, written test, and comprehensive assessment. The position list includes 103 openings in total.

### Recruitment schedule

| Stage | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company presentation | December 3, 3–5 p.m. | New Library Lecture Hall |
| Company exhibition | December 4, 9 a.m.–noon | New Library Student Activities Hall |
| Written test | December 4, 2–4 p.m. | Mock Courtroom, Building E |
| Comprehensive assessment, final round | December 5, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. | New Library Student Activities Hall |

### Positions

| Company | Department and position | Locations | Major and target group | Openings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Watsons Personal Care Stores | Operations store-management trainee (full-time) | Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Fuzhou, and other locations | Any major; fresh graduates | 51 |
| Watsons Personal Care Stores | Operations store-management trainee (internship) | Guangzhou, Fuzhou, Quanzhou, Dongguan, and other locations | Any major; third-year students | 33 |
| Guangzhou Watsons Food & Beverage Co., Ltd. | Finance assistant | Guangzhou | Finance-related majors; fresh graduates | 2 |
| Guangzhou Watsons Food & Beverage Co., Ltd. | Audit assistant | Guangzhou | Finance-related majors; fresh graduates | 2 |
| PARKnSHOP Supermarkets Limited, China | Headquarters/Operations management trainee | Guangzhou and Shenzhen | Any major; fresh graduates | 10 |
| PARKnSHOP Supermarkets Limited, China | Sales management trainee | Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Beijing, and Shanghai | Marketing, e-commerce, business administration, accounting, and related majors; fresh graduates | 5 |

Students attending the company presentation, exhibition, and comprehensive assessment should dress formally. Colleges and residential colleges will notify graduating students and eligible undergraduates to attend on time.
