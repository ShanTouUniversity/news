STU’s Law School will host a public lecture on recent developments in WTO dispute settlement. Professor Zhang Yuejiao, a member of the WTO Appellate Body and professor at STU’s Law School, will introduce current developments in the World Trade Organization’s dispute settlement mechanism.

The lecture will be held from 10 to 11:30 a.m. on November 30, 2012, in the Library Lecture Hall. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

Zhang Yuejiao is a legal expert who was among the first people from mainland China to serve on the WTO Appellate Body. She previously served as director general of the Treaty and Law Department of China’s Ministry of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation, China’s representative in China–US intellectual-property negotiations, and chief legal counsel for China’s WTO accession negotiations. She has also held positions with the Asian Development Bank, World Bank, and China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission.
