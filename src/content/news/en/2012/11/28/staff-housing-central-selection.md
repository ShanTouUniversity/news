It is reported that, due to a shortage of transition housing on campus, Shantou University earlier this semester arranged temporary accommodation in the expert apartments for 16 newly arrived teachers, while four other teachers chose to wait for the completion of a renovation project. With the 36 renovated staff apartments at the former Jingwei Center expected to be completed around early December, the Resource Management Office plans to hold a central room selection to make the leasing of transition housing fairer and more transparent.

## Scoring Registration and Verification

| Date | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Nov 28–29 | Newly hired teachers without formal housing assignments fill out forms and submit housing score documents at Room 313, West Wing, Old Administration Building |
| Nov 30 & Dec 3 | Scores verified by the personnel department and individual teachers |

Scoring follows the university's Staff Housing Allocation Measures (STU Document [1997] No. 186).

## Room Selection

The central selection is provisionally set for 3 p.m. on December 4 in the first-floor conference room of the Network Center (subject to further notice). Rooms will be chosen in descending order of teachers' scores; where scores tie, those with higher professional titles choose first.
