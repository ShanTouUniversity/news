Because the instructor from the Department of Biology will be away at a conference, STU’s Academic Affairs Office has adjusted course section 55166, *Global Change and Biology*. The class originally scheduled for periods A and B on Thursday of Week 11, November 29, in Room E208 will be suspended. Two make-up sessions will be held in the same room.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Global Change and Biology* |
| Section | 55166 |
| Original class | Periods A and B, Thursday of Week 11, November 29, Room E208 |
| First make-up | Period C, Thursday of Week 12, December 6, Room E208 |
| Second make-up | Period C, Thursday of Week 13, December 13, Room E208 |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in the course to inform one another of the change.
