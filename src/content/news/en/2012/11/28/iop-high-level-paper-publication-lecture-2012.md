STU’s School of Science will host a Science Salon lecture titled “How to Publish High-Level Research Papers in IOP Journals.” The speaker will be Professor Lu Mingfang, chief representative of the Institute of Physics Beijing Office and editor-in-chief for IOP China.

Drawing on the characteristics of IOP Publishing journals, the lecture will cover research-paper writing, submission, responses to peer review, and revision. It will offer guidance for researchers preparing submissions to international journals, including ways to improve scholarly presentation and communicate effectively through the editorial and review process.

The lecture will begin at 10:10 a.m. on Monday, December 3, 2012, in the School of Science Lecture Hall (Science 352).

### About the speaker

Lu Mingfang received a doctorate in engineering from Tsinghua University in 1995 and later conducted postdoctoral research at the Institute of Physics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, the Weizmann Institute of Science in Israel, and the Swedish Institute of Space Physics. His research has covered high-temperature, high-density plasma physics, controlled nuclear fusion, low-temperature plasma applications, and space physics. He became chief representative and editorial director of the Institute of Physics Beijing Office in 2002, took on the role of IOP China editor-in-chief in 2007, and was elected a Fellow of the Institute of Physics in 2003.
