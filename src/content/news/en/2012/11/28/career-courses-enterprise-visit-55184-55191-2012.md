STU’s Student Affairs Office has announced that course section 55184, *Career Development Counseling and Training*, and course section 55191, *Employment Guidance*, will be changed to a company visit as part of the course arrangements, giving students an opportunity to learn about an actual workplace.

The *Career Development Counseling and Training* class originally scheduled for periods 8 and 9 on Thursday of Week 11, November 29, in Room D206, and the *Employment Guidance* class originally scheduled for periods 3 and 4 on Friday of Week 11 in Room D302 will both be replaced by the visit.

Participating students should gather at the university motto stele at 8:25 a.m. on Saturday, December 1, and travel to Guangdong Andely Environmental Protection Technology Co., Ltd. Students enrolled in the relevant courses should inform one another of the change.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Career Development Counseling and Training | 55184 | Periods 8 and 9, November 29, Room D206 | Company visit on December 1 |
| Employment Guidance | 55191 | Periods 3 and 4 on Friday of Week 11, Room D302 | Company visit on December 1 |
