The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at STU will hold the ninth Kan Kan Culture session, bringing Hong Kong film producer and director Gao Zhisen together with Professor Wang Shouzhi for a discussion of artistic creation and cultural communication through film, television, and theater.

The event will be held from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, November 30, 2012. The notice does not specify a venue; please follow subsequent information from the organizers for the complete arrangements.

Gao Zhisen has worked in Hong Kong theater and film production since the 1990s and has held a range of public cultural and arts positions. He was awarded the Medal of Honour by the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Wang Shouzhi is a scholar of design history and theory whose research covers architecture, industrial products, graphic design, fashion, automobiles, urban planning, illustration, and modern art. He has taught at ArtCenter College of Design in the United States.
