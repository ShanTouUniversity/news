Because the instructor will be away, STU’s Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the schedules for two Arts Education Center courses. The Chinese Ethnic Dance and Ballet Body Conditioning classes will both be suspended on December 3, with make-up classes held in Room STU102 on December 23.

| Section | Course | Suspended class | Make-up class | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 56248 | Chinese Ethnic Dance | Periods 3 and 4, Monday of Week 12, December 3 | Periods 6 and 7, Sunday of Week 15, December 23 | STU102 |
| 56249 | Ballet Body Conditioning | Periods 8 and 9, Monday of Week 12, December 3 | Periods 8 and 9, Sunday of Week 15, December 23 | STU102 |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in these courses to inform one another of the changes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
