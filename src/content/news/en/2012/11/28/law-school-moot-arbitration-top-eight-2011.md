The STU Law School team performed strongly at the 10th CIETAC Cup International Commercial Arbitration Debate Competition, scoring 989.3 points to reach the national top eight. Team members also received the quarterfinal Best Debater Award and the Team Outstanding Contribution Award.

The competition was held in Beijing from November 19 to 23, 2011, with teams from 26 university law schools. During the round-robin stage, the STU team competed against the University of International Business and Economics, China University of Political Science and Law, Central University of Finance and Economics, and Guangdong University of Foreign Studies, demonstrating strong legal knowledge and English communication skills.

In the top-eight knockout round, the STU team faced Peking University and narrowly lost. Its composure and clear presentation nevertheless earned recognition from the arbitrators and audience. The team consisted of eight students and was coached by Dr. Gu Junfeng. In accordance with student privacy requirements, individual student names are not reproduced.
