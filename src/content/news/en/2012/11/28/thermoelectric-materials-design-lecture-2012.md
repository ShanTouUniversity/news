STU’s School of Science will host a Science Salon lecture titled “Thermoelectric-Material Design and Coordinated Control of Electrical and Thermal Transport” on November 30. The speaker will be Associate Researcher and research-group leader Luo Jun of the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Thermoelectric materials use the movement of charge carriers in solids to convert thermal energy directly into electrical energy and vice versa. The lecture will review recent research and draw on experiments involving materials such as Bi2Te3, AgSbTe2, and PbTe. It will examine how composition, crystal structure, and microstructure affect thermoelectric performance, as well as mechanisms for coordinated electrical and thermal transport, approaches to improving conversion efficiency, and designs for materials with high thermoelectric figures of merit.

The lecture will begin at 10:10 a.m. on Friday, November 30, 2012, in the School of Science Lecture Hall (Science 352).

### About the speaker

Luo Jun received bachelor’s and master’s degrees from the University of Science and Technology Beijing in 1999 and 2002 and a doctorate from the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2005. He later conducted Humboldt Scholar research in the Department of Physics at Freie Universität Berlin. His work focuses on the synthesis, phase diagrams, crystal structures, and structure–property relationships of thermoelectric and magnetic materials. He has led projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and Chinese Academy of Sciences talent programs.
