The Shantou Human Resources and Social Security Bureau and STU’s Student Affairs Office will host the “2012 Shantou Listed Companies and Major Enterprises Special Job Fair” to give graduating students opportunities to meet employers and explore careers.

The fair will begin at 9 a.m. on November 30, 2012, in the Student Activities Hall on the first floor of the New Library. Colleges and residential colleges will help graduating students review the recruitment information and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
