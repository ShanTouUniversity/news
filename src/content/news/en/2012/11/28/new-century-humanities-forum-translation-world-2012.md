The 115th New Century Humanities Forum at STU will feature a lecture titled “Entering the Colorful World of Translation” by Professor Wang Hong, director of the Translation Research Institute and an academic leader in translation studies at Soochow University’s School of Foreign Languages.

The lecture will be held from 4 to 6 p.m. on November 30, 2012, in the New Library Lecture Hall.

### Lecture topics

The lecture will have five parts: Wang’s connection with translation, questions and definitions of translation, the relationship between style and translation, domestication and foreignization, and the diverse world of translation practice. Wang will distinguish translation in the strict sense, as language conversion guided by the source text, from translation in the broader sense, as a cross-cultural communication activity shaped by sponsors, readers, translators’ purposes, and the target culture.

The lecture will also examine translation standards and strategies, emphasizing that translation involves more than transferring words and meanings. It also requires the recreation of tone, style, and cultural flavor, together with appropriate addition, omission, and artistic re-creation.

### About the speaker

Wang Hong is a professor, doctoral supervisor, director of the Translation Research Institute, and academic leader in translation studies at Soochow University’s School of Foreign Languages. His research focuses on translation theory and the English translation of Chinese classics. He has published 78 academic works, including English translations of *Brush Talks from Dream Brook*, *Mozi*, and *The Classic of Mountains and Seas*, as well as books on translation theory and practice.
