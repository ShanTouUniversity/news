STU College of Art and Design alumni Cai Kaisheng, Chen Sibi, and Zhang Jixiong will present the “3+3” painting exhibition at their alma mater’s gallery from December 1 to 8. Organized around the theme of “three painters, three directions,” the exhibition will show the artists’ continuing exploration and creative achievements.

Cai Kaisheng is a senior lecturer at Shantou Vocational and Technical College who studied mural painting at the Central Academy of Fine Arts and focuses on watercolor. Chen Sibi graduated from STU’s Department of Fine Arts in 1989 and is deputy director of the Shantou Chinese Painting Research Institute. Zhang Jixiong graduated from STU’s College of Art and Design in 1990, later studied mural painting at the Central Academy of Fine Arts, and now works on oil and works on paper in Songzhuang, Beijing.

The three artists have exhibited in watercolor, Chinese painting, and contemporary-art exhibitions. The exhibition will provide a presentation of their recent creative work and an opportunity for current students to learn about alumni careers and contemporary artistic practice.
