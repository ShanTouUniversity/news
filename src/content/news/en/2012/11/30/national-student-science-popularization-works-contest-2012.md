STU Youth League Committee is inviting students to submit works for the 2012 National College Student Science-Popularization Works Competition. Organized jointly by the China Association for Science and Technology’s science-popularization department, the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League’s school department, and relevant divisions of the Ministry of Education, the competition aims to encourage students to take part in science-communication practice and strengthen innovation and entrepreneurship skills.

Entries must be original science-popularization works with a clear theme and strong intellectual, scientific, artistic, and accessible qualities. Accepted formats include graphic works, audio or video works, and animation works. The animation category includes Flash, Flash-based web features, HTML5 resources, and science-popularization games.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | 2012 National College Student Science-Popularization Works Competition |
| Participants | All STU students |
| Entry requirements | Original works combining intellectual, scientific, artistic, and accessible qualities |
| Formats | Graphic, audio-video, animation, and science games |
| Campus submission deadline | December 24, 2012 |
| Creation period | July 2012 to December 31, 2012 |
| Preliminary review and recommendations | January 2013 |
| Final review and awards | February to March 2013 |
| Promotion of winning works | April to June 2013 |

Students interested in participating can consult the China Digital Science and Technology Museum at <http://www.cdstm.cn/> for details and submit their works to the Youth League Committee through the campus arrangement. Selected works will proceed through preliminary review, recommendations, final judging, awards, and later promotion.
