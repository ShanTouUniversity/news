It is reported that the Department of Social Sciences of Shantou University issued a notice on November 30, 2012, requiring 2013 undergraduate graduates who took the *Situation and Policy Education* (Practice) course in the fall semester of 2012 to submit their project papers.

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Authors | 2013 graduates on the main campus (mainly Class of 2009 graduating in the first half of 2013 and some non-2009 students) who took the course in fall 2012 |
| Topic | Complete the paper according to the topic selected in the credit system |
| Format | Follow the template in the attachment; handwritten or printed on A4, 3,000–5,000 words |
| Deadline | 17:30, January 4, 2013 (Friday of week 16) |
| Submission | Submit the hard copy in person to the supervisor’s mailbox |
| Location | Mailboxes of supervisors, 2nd floor, Block G, Teaching Building, Department of Social Sciences |

Topics and supervisors can be viewed in the credit system under “Guided Courses.” Students graduating early must also complete and submit the paper as required, otherwise normal graduation will be affected.

> ⚠️ The paper template is available on the OA system.
