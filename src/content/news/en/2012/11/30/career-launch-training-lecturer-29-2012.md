The 29th STU Career Launch Forum will feature a lecture titled “How to Become an Outstanding Training Instructor.” Hu Nanshan, an award-winning instructor at GF Securities, will share professional skills and career experience in training and instruction.

The lecture will be held from 7 to 9 p.m. on December 2, 2012, in the New Library Lecture Hall. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

Hu Nanshan is general manager of the Jiexi office of GF Securities in Jieyang. He has received company-level honors including the GF Securities President’s Award, Service Star, and Outstanding Communist Party Member and has long worked in fund sales and training. He studied at Jinan University and South China University of Technology and later participated in study programs at Northwestern University in the United States and the Canadian Securities Institute.
