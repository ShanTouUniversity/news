It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on November 30, 2012, that the hand-copied newspaper contest of the Cantonese Culture Festival, part of the Student Community X-Project, has been rescheduled due to weather.

The contest was originally scheduled for December 1–2 at Jin San Jiao and will now be held on December 5–6 at the same venue. The office apologized for the inconvenience and said students may continue to register and should contact the designated student organizer with questions.
