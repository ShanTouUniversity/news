It is reported that the 31st Anniversary and Freshmen Welcome Gala of Shantou University Law School will be held tonight.

Organized by the Law School, the gala is scheduled for 19:30 on November 30, 2012, at the University Auditorium. Themed "Setting Dreams Free, Continuing the Glory," the event celebrates the 31st anniversary of the school's founding and welcomes the incoming cohort of students.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Event | Law School 31st Anniversary and Freshmen Welcome Gala |
| Theme | Setting Dreams Free, Continuing the Glory |
| Time | November 30, 2012, 19:30 |
| Venue | University Auditorium |
| Organizer | Law School |

The evening is open to all faculty and students. The Law School has extended a warm invitation to the university community to attend and share in the celebration.
