STU Student Affairs Office has announced that 93 students passed review by the Student Aid Management Center for the second batch of national student-loan applications for the 2012–2013 academic year. The list is now being publicized within the university.

The publicity period ends on December 7, 2012. Organizations or individuals with objections may submit comments in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Student Aid Management Center before the deadline. Organizations must affix their official seal, while individuals must provide their real names when submitting comments.

According to arrangements by the Shantou branch of Bank of China, contracts for this batch of national student loans are scheduled to be signed at the beginning of the spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year.
