STU Youth League Committee has announced that the end-of-semester sharing session for the fall 2012 Shuguang Volunteer Teaching Service-Learning Course (SER3015) and Sunshine Community Service-Learning Course (SER3014) has been moved from 2:00–3:50 p.m. to 3:00–4:50 p.m. on December 5. The venues remain unchanged.

Both courses conduct public-service practice through off-campus service sites. Students enrolled in the courses should prepare their course summaries and presentations according to the revised schedule.
