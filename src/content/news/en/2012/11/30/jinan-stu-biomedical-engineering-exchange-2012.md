STU’s School of Science will host a Jinan University–STU academic exchange meeting on biomedical engineering. Scholars from the two universities will exchange research perspectives in biomedical engineering and promote collaboration across related science and engineering fields.

The meeting will take place on December 8, 2012, in the Lecture Hall on the first floor of STU’s Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research.

Participants include Zhou Changren, Party Committee secretary of the College of Science and Engineering at Jinan University; Xue Wei, director of its Department of Biomedical Engineering; Jiao Yanpeng, deputy director of its Department of Materials; and STU professors Wei Chiju, Chen Zhikai, and Xu Kaitian. The meeting will address biomedical engineering and related materials research.
