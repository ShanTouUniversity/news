It is reported that the Trade Union forwarded a notice on November 30, 2012, from the Shantou Federation of Trade Unions to launch nominations for the Shantou May Day Labor Medal.

To implement the spirit of the 18th CPC National Congress and encourage workers to contribute to the 12th Five-Year Plan, the municipal federation will commend advanced workers who have made outstanding contributions to economic, political, cultural, social, ecological and Party building endeavors. The city plans to award 20 medals.

## Nomination Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Frontline workers in enterprises, institutions and other social organizations below the level of workshop or section chief (below department/office level in colleges); leaders of enterprises and institutions are not eligible |
| Quota | 20 citywide, with allocation in Attachment 1 |
| Criteria | Those who faithfully implement Party policies, abide by laws, promote scientific development and have made outstanding contributions in areas such as industrial restructuring, strategic emerging industries, key project construction, independent innovation, disaster response and crisis management, science, education, culture, health and sports, harmonious labor relations, safety and environmental protection, or new rural construction |
| Deadline | Before the end of work on December 10, 2012 |
| Submission | Submit the nominee list and the *Shantou May Day Labor Medal Recommendation Form* to the university Trade Union, email o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Trade Union |

The recommendation form and allocation table are available in the OA attachments.
