It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on November 30, 2012, announcing the shortlist for the 2012–2013 Corporate and Individual Donor Scholarships.

## Applications and Screening

Following the November 20 call for applications, 559 students applied online by 09:00 on November 27:

| Program | Applicants | Awards | Screening | Shortlisted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outstanding Senior Student Award | 521 | 140 | Ranked by overall GPA percentage within each college, selected at 1:1.5 ratio | 212 |
| Hakka Student Award | 36 | 2 | Ranked by GPA percentage, top in each college | 7 |
| Chenghai Student Award | 2 | 2 | Selected per donor intent of “academic excellence and financial need” | 2 |

A total of 221 candidates were shortlisted; the list is in the OA attachment and withheld here for privacy.

## Materials Required

Shortlisted candidates should submit the following to the Student Financial Aid Management Center (Administration Center Room 106) by 17:00 on December 7, 2012:

1. *Corporate and Individual Donor Scholarship Application Form* (print via http://sao.stu.edu.cn);
2. *Recommendation Form for Shortlisted Candidates* (see attachment; college master fills the recommendation for residential college students);
3. A personal essay within 1,200 words (A4) on university learning experiences and how they shaped life goals;
4. Academic transcript (A4, stamped by the college).

Note: Students who have received the National Student Aid Grant and obtain this scholarship will receive only the higher amount, not both.

> ⚠️ The shortlist and forms are available on the OA system.
