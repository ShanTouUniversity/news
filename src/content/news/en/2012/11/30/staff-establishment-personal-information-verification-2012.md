In accordance with Guangdong’s verification of establishment records for provincial government and public institutions, STU’s Human Resources Office is conducting verification of personal information in the establishment management system. Each unit should organize staff members included in the establishment to collect and check the Guangdong public-institution personal-information verification form and sign it after confirming that the information is accurate and complete.

Units should collect the forms from the Human Resources Office on the morning of December 3 and distribute them to the relevant staff members for verification and signature. Errors or omissions should be corrected after supporting documents are provided and checked as required by the office. Signed forms must be returned to the Human Resources Office by December 5. The office is in Room 315 of the New Administration Building.

Because the forms contain staff identity and employment-establishment information, this report does not reproduce the form fields, personal data, or supporting-document details. Please consult the OA attachment and contact the Human Resources Office for completion instructions.
