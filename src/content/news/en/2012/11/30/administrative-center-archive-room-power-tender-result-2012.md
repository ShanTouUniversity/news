Following the review and recommendation of the STU Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, Shantou Longhua Construction General Corporation was named the winning bidder for the Administrative Center comprehensive archive-room power project. The contract amount is RMB 39,333.

The result is being publicized from November 30 through December 4, 2012. Relevant parties may raise objections during the publicity period through the project owner or the designated complaint-handling office.
