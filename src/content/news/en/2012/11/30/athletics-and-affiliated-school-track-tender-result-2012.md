Following the review and recommendation of STU’s Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, the university has announced the tender results for partial resurfacing of the athletics track and the rubber running tracks at the affiliated middle and primary schools. Shenzhen Aoshunda Industrial Co., Ltd. won both contracts.

| Project | Contract amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Partial resurfacing of the athletics track | RMB 477,907.30, excluding a 5% contingency |
| Rubber running-track project at the affiliated middle and primary schools | RMB 87,919.34 |

The public-notice period runs from November 30 to December 4, 2012. The tendering entity is the STU Infrastructure Office. Its contacts are Mr. Chen and Mr. Lu, at (0754) 86503216, with fax at (0754) 82902314.

Complaints may be submitted to the STU Supervision, Audit, and Inspection Office at (0754) 86502346, with fax at (0754) 86503663.
