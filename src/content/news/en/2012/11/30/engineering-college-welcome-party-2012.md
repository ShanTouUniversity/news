STU Engineering College will hold its 2012 welcome party at the University Great Hall on the evening of December 2, 2012. The event is themed “Ignite Passion, Pursue Excellence,” and all students and faculty are invited to attend.

The party will begin at 7:30 p.m. Audience members should follow directions from on-site staff and arrive 15 minutes early to take their seats. Rows 6 through 8 will be reserved for university leaders and guests.
