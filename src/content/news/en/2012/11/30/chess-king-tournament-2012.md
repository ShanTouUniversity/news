The STU Chess Association will hold the “Chess King Championship,” a student-club project for the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. The event is intended to enrich student life and encourage interest in Chinese chess and traditional Chinese culture.

The tournament will take place from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. on December 8 at the Golden Triangle and is open to all students.

### Format and awards

The event will have two areas. The formal competition area will use one-on-one knockout matches and require advance registration. The on-site open-play area will also use knockout matches but will not require advance registration. The formal men’s division will award the top eight places and the women’s division the top four, with additional special awards for the male and female chess champions.

The organizers are the STU Youth League Committee and Students’ Association Union, and the host is the STU Chess Association. The association will publicize the event and accept on-site registration at the Golden Triangle on December 1, moving below the Second Dining Hall in case of heavy rain. An on-site open-play area will also be available on the tournament day.

All registration channels close at midnight on December 6. Please consult the Chess Association’s official channels or the original OA notice for forms, registration methods, and participation requirements. Student numbers, personal contact details, and personal email addresses are not reproduced here.
