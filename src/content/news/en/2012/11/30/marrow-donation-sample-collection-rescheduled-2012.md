It is reported that due to weather conditions, the Business School Youth League Committee and the Business School Youth Volunteer Association have adjusted the schedule for the “Love and Marrow” activities. The booth activity and blood sample collection originally scheduled for November 30 have been rescheduled.

## Revised Schedule

| Activity | Date | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booth activity | December 7 | Jin San Jiao |
| Blood sample collection | December 9 | Outside the Second Dining Hall |

The Business School Youth League Committee and the Business School Youth Volunteer Association remind participants to note the revised dates and locations.
