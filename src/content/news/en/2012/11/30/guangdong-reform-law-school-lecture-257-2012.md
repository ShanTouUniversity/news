STU Law School will host its 257th series lecture on December 3, 2012, under the theme “Reform in Guangdong.” Professor Li Zhilian of the Department of Public and Social Administration at City University of Hong Kong and Professor Yue Fangmin of the Economics Department at the Party School of the Guangdong Provincial Committee will discuss reform governance and Guangdong’s industrial development.

Under the title “How Can Reform Be Consolidated and Sustained?”, Li Zhilian will address central-local relations, politics and public finance, government reform, institutional change, and accountable governance. Yue Fangmin will present “Accelerating Transformation and Upgrading to Build a Modern Industrial System in Guangdong,” drawing on research in industrial clusters, regional economics, public administration, and institutional economics.

Li is a professor in the Department of Public and Social Administration at City University of Hong Kong. She has worked in the Hong Kong Government and at the University of Hong Kong and was a Fulbright scholar at the University of California, Berkeley. Yue is a professor in the Economics Department of the Party School of the Guangdong Provincial Committee and Guangdong Administration Institute. She also serves on the councils of the Guangdong Economics Society and Guangdong Finance Society.
