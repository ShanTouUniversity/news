STU Business School has invited graduate student teams to participate in the 2013 Asia Venture Challenge (AVC). Hosted by Thammasat Business School, the competition will take place in Bangkok from March 5 to 7, 2013, with total prizes exceeding USD 18,000.

AVC is open to MBA and other graduate students and encourages them to develop new ventures based on their own ideas or technologies. It gives teams opportunities to present business plans and receive feedback from investors and professional judges. The competition includes a 60-second elevator pitch, a new-venture showcase booth, and a business-plan presentation. The Asian champion will be invited to the Venture Labs Investment Competition at the University of Texas at Austin from May 2 to 4, 2013, and may compete for USD 100,000 in seed capital.

### Eligibility and team rules

Eligible ventures must be student-created, student-managed, and student-owned seed-stage, startup-stage, or early-growth ventures seeking outside equity capital. Each team must include at least one graduate student and may have no more than five student members. Non-students may help plan a venture, but only students may formally present the plan and answer judges’ questions. The business plan must be prepared under faculty supervision and be the team’s original work.

Each university may submit no more than two summary new-venture plans, but may have no more than one representative team in the competition. Each country may have no more than four representative teams. Participating universities are responsible for international travel, incidental expenses, and accommodation costs for their teams.

| February 20, 2013 | Submit the complete new-venture business plan |

| May 2–4, 2013 | Venture Labs Investment Competition in Austin, Texas |

The complete plan must be submitted as a printable PDF of no more than 16 pages, including no more than 10 pages of text and six pages of appendices. It should include financial data, funding needs and use, a proposed investment structure, and potential exit strategies. Detailed rules and application materials are available at <http://www.asiaventurechallenge.org/>. For campus questions, please contact the Business School’s business-case competition working group.
