STU’s School of Science will host a Science Salon lecture titled “New Materials and Methods for Enzymatic Digestion and Biomarker Research” on December 4. The speaker will be Dr. Wu Shuaibin, a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University.

The lecture will be held from 9 to 10 a.m. on Tuesday, December 4, 2012, in the School of Science Lecture Hall (Science 352).

The presentation will address sample digestion, protein analysis, and subsequent identification in proteomics. It will introduce high-throughput technologies and methods designed to improve digestion speed and reduce interference with mass-spectrometry identification, and discuss biomarker discovery in translational-medicine research and its potential clinical value.

Wu Shuaibin conducted doctoral research in analytical chemistry at the Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and began translational-medicine research at Stanford University in March 2012. His main field is proteomics. He has published in international academic journals, received several patents, and won a first-prize scholarship from the Chinese Instrument and Control Society.
