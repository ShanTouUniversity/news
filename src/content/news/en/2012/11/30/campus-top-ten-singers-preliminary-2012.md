The preliminary round of STU’s 20th Top Ten Singers Competition will be held at the Great Hall on the evening of December 1, 2012. Organized by the STU Youth League Committee and Students’ Union, the competition is intended to identify student performing-arts talent and enrich campus cultural life.

The preliminary round will feature 25 teams. In the first stage, the teams will be divided by draw into five groups for collaborative performances in a group challenge. In the second stage, 15 advancing teams will perform selected songs in sequence, with 10 teams advancing to the final.

Every participant in the preliminary round will receive the title of “Outstanding Singer of STU’s 20th Top Ten Singers Competition.” The 10 finalists will receive the title of “STU Top Ten Singer.” The organizers will also present one award each for Most Promising Newcomer and Best Medley Collaboration, along with certificates and trophies.

Further information was to be released through the STU Students’ Union social-media account and website. Audience members should follow on-site arrangements; guest and judges’ seating will be designated by the organizers.
