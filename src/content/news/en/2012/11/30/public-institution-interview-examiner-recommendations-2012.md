STU Human Resources Office has forwarded a request from the Shantou Human Resources and Social Security Bureau to update its pool of interview examiners for public-institution recruitment. Campus units are being asked to recommend candidates on a voluntary basis, with no restriction on academic specialty.

Candidates should be in good health, fair-minded, law-abiding, and able to maintain strict confidentiality. They must hold an associate senior professional title and at least a graduate degree, and be under 58 years of age. Each campus unit should complete the “Summary Form for Recommended Interview Examiners for Shantou Public Institutions” and submit the electronic version to the Human Resources Office by December 5, 2012.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Positions | Interview examiners for Shantou public-institution recruitment |
| Recommendation scope | Campus units; all academic specialties |
| Main requirements | Associate senior title, graduate degree or above, under 58 |
| Other requirements | Healthy, fair-minded, law-abiding, confidentiality-conscious, and willing to serve |
| Deadline | December 5, 2012 |
| Responsible office | STU Human Resources Office |
| Consultation phone | 86502349 |

Campus units should complete the recommendation and submission process as required. The original OA attachment should be consulted for the exact format and submission instructions.
