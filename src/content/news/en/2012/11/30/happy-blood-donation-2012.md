The STU Youth League Committee and Young Volunteers Association will work with Shantou Central Blood Station to hold the 2012 “Happy Blood Donation” activities. The program will help faculty members and students learn about blood and blood-station operations and promote voluntary blood donation.

### Schedule

| Activity | Time | Venue | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blood-station visit | December 8, Saturday, 8–11 a.m. | Shantou Central Blood Station | Registration planned for 40 participants, with group transportation |
| On-campus blood drive | December 9, Sunday, 9 a.m.–6 p.m. | Below the Second Dining Hall, opposite the supermarket | Open to faculty members and students who pass the on-site medical screening |

Those interested in the blood-station visit should register in advance according to the organizers’ instructions. Registration closes at midnight on December 6, with places available on a first-come, first-served basis. Please follow Shantou Central Blood Station and the organizers for current health eligibility requirements, identity verification, and the on-site process.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
>
> ⚠️ Blood donation involves personal health and identity information. Health criteria and personal registration contacts are not reproduced here; please consult on-site staff and complete the medical screening.
