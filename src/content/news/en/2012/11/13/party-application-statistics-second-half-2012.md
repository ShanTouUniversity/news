To support its 2012 internal Party statistics work, the STU Organization and United Front Department has asked each Party general branch and directly affiliated Party branch to submit the Second-Half 2012 Party Applicant Management Form for database entry.

The submission includes the Initial Party Applicant Database Form and the list of individuals designated as Party activists or development targets during the second half of 2012. Each unit should appoint a staff member to handle the submission and ensure that the statistical data and database entries are accurate, truthful, and complete.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Submission | Second-Half 2012 Party Applicant Management Form |
| Contents | Initial Party applicant database information, and lists of Party activists and development targets |
| Paper submission | Room 315, Administration Center, Organization and United Front Department |
| Deadline | By November 23 |
| Electronic submission | `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` |

Units must deliver the consolidated materials to the Organization and United Front Department by the deadline and send the electronic document to the same office email.
