The Shantou University Engineering College will hold its second MT-IDEAS Creative Design Competition and offer a pre-competition workshop titled “Inspire New Ideas, Create the Future.” The workshop will help students develop innovative thinking and shape creative project concepts. It is open to the STU community and will take place in E Staircase 202.

The workshop speaker will be Yuan Yingliang, founder of the MT Enterprise Innovation Club, a 2008 Computer Science graduate of Shantou University, and founder of Shantou Chenggu Technology Co., Ltd.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | Second MT-IDEAS Creative Design Competition |
| Workshop | “Inspire New Ideas, Create the Future” |
| Workshop date | November 17 |
| Venue | E Staircase 202 |
| Registration deadline | 23:00 on November 21 |
| Project-submission deadline | November 23 |
| Preliminary round | November 25 |
| Final | December 1 |
| Organizers | Engineering College Youth League Committee and Engineering College MT Enterprise Innovation Club |

The competition aims to encourage students to develop an innovation mindset and strengthen their creative abilities. Detailed participation requirements are subject to the competition arrangements issued by the Engineering College.
