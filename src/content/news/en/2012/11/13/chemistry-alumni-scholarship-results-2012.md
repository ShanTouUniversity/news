The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has announced the results of the 2012 Chemistry Alumni Scholarship selection. Under the STU Regulations on the Review of the Chemistry Alumni Scholarship, the college review committee selected 20 Chemistry students for awards of 2,500 yuan each.

| Cohort | Recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| Chemistry Class of 2009 | 4 |
| Chemistry Class of 2010 | 4 |
| Chemistry Class of 2011 | 4 |
| Chemistry Class of 2012 | 8 |
| **Total** | **20** |

The university encouraged the recognized students to continue their studies with diligence and achieve stronger results in their future academic work and careers. To protect student privacy, individual recipient names are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
