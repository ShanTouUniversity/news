The STU Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will hold “Heart Messenger” mental-health training under the theme “Shape You and Me, Share Care.” The program is designed to use peer support to strengthen mental-health awareness and mutual-help skills, enabling the “Heart Messengers” to support their peers and themselves more effectively.

The training is intended for all members of the Psychology Growth Association and residence-hall floor leaders. It will run from November 17 to 18.

| Session | Date | Venue | Content and format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | November 17 | E208 | Clarifying group goals and methods |
| The role of peer counseling | November 17 | E208 | Understanding peer mental-health support; lecture |
| Peer counseling skills | November 17 | E208 | Learning mental-health knowledge; lecture |
| Outdoor development | November 17 | Small Stage | Building awareness of helping others and oneself through group games; group training |
| Case discussion | November 18 | E208 | Learning about common student concerns; video viewing and group discussion |
| Summary | November 18 | E208 | Reviewing training experience and outcomes |
