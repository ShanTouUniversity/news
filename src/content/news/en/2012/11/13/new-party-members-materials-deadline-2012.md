The STU Organization and United Front Department has begun the university’s 2012 internal Party statistics work and asked each Party general branch and directly affiliated Party branch to submit documentation for members accepted as probationary members or granted full membership during the year. The reporting scope covers cases approved by a branch members’ meeting by December 31, 2012.

| Item | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Documentation for new probationary and regularized members submitted for Party Committee approval | By November 30 |
| Initial information for cases unable to submit documentation on time | By November 30, using the attached format |
| Complete approval documentation for exceptional cases | No later than December 7 |
| Email | `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` |
| Contact | Ji Rongxin |
| Telephone | 82902345 |

Units unable to submit complete approval documentation by November 30 because of exceptional circumstances should first submit the relevant information using the formats for the STU New Party Members Register and STU Party Member Regularization Register, then submit the approval documentation by December 7.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the New Party Members Register and Party Member Regularization Register forms. Individual register information is not reproduced here.
