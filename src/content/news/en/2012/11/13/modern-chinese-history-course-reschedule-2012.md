The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the schedule for course section 54264, *An Outline of Modern Chinese History*, because the instructor will be away. The class originally scheduled for periods 8–9 on Thursday, November 15, in Room E203 will be suspended. It will be made up during periods 3–4 on Saturday, November 24, in the same room.

| Item | Original schedule | Make-up schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course | *An Outline of Modern Chinese History* | *An Outline of Modern Chinese History* |
| Section | 54264 | 54264 |
| Time | Periods 8–9, Thursday, November 15 | Periods 3–4, Saturday, November 24 |
| Venue | Room E203 | Room E203 |
| Reason | Instructor away | Make-up class |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in the course to inform one another of the change.
