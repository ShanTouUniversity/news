The Shantou University Labor Union has announced arrangements for the 2012 faculty volleyball tournament. The tournament is scheduled for 16:30 on Monday, November 19, at the volleyball court. Team leaders should organize their players to attend according to the tournament schedule, and the Labor Union will provide drinking water.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2012 STU Faculty Volleyball Tournament |
| Time | 16:30 on Monday, November 19 |
| Venue | Volleyball court |
| Team organization | Team leaders should organize players according to the schedule |
| Drinking water | Provided by the Labor Union |

The detailed matchups and rounds are subject to the 2012 STU Faculty Volleyball Tournament Schedule.
