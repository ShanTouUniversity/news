The Shantou University Engineering College will hold a career-planning forum on November 16 under the theme “Workplace Image and Social Etiquette.” Chen Chanyan, an etiquette expert, senior etiquette trainer, and member of the National Professional Talent Education Experts Committee’s etiquette expert panel, will speak about workplace presentation and social etiquette.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Workplace Image and Social Etiquette |
| Speaker | Chen Chanyan |
| Speaker credentials | Etiquette expert and senior etiquette trainer; member of the etiquette expert panel of the National Professional Talent Education Experts Committee |
| Time | 19:30–21:30 on Friday, November 16 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Participants | All STU students and faculty members |
