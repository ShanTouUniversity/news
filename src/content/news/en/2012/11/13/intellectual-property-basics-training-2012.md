The STU Office of Research Affairs and School of Law will offer a basic intellectual-property training course. In 2012, Shantou University was approved by the Guangdong Intellectual Property Office and Department of Education as a Guangdong intellectual-property training base. The course is open to all STU students and faculty members and aims to develop practical skills in intellectual-property matters.

The course will be held in E Staircase 101. There are 250 places, and registration is voluntary on a first-come, first-served basis while places last. Applicants should complete the registration form and submit it through the method specified in the original OA notice by November 19.

| Date | Time | Topic | Instructor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| November 24 (Saturday) | 09:00–12:00 | Patent systems and applications | Expert from the Shantou Intellectual Property Office |
| November 24 (Saturday) | 14:30–17:30 | Current intellectual-property issues | STU School of Law professor |
| December 1 (Saturday) | 09:00–12:00 | Entrepreneurship, innovation, creation, and intellectual property | Expert from the Shenzhen Intellectual Property Research Association |
| December 1 (Saturday) | 14:30–17:30 | Patent applications and examination | Course instructor |
| December 2 (Sunday) | 09:00–12:00 | Drafting patent claims | Course instructor |
| December 2 (Sunday) | 14:30–18:30 | Drafting patent specifications and examination | Course instructor |

Participants who complete the training and pass the assessment will receive a Guangdong Intellectual Property Training Completion Certificate from the Guangdong Intellectual Property Office.
