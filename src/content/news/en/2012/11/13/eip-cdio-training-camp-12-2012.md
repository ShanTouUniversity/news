The Shantou University Engineering College will hold its 12th EIP-CDIO student development camp under the theme “Sincere Communication.” The camp is part of the college’s implementation of EIP-CDIO engineering-education reform and its effort to develop students’ ethical, integrity, and professional qualities.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Camp | 12th EIP-CDIO Student Development Camp of the Engineering College |
| Theme | Sincere Communication |
| Dates | November 17–18 |
| Eligible students | Class of 2012 students in the Engineering College |
| Planned places | 40 |
| Registration deadline | 12:00 on November 15 |

Interested students should download and complete the Engineering College EIP-CDIO Training Camp Participant Registration Form and submit it according to the original OA notice. The notice requests that registration files be labeled with year, major, and name; the precise submission method is subject to the original notice.

The camp aims to strengthen students’ overall qualities and the professional capabilities expected by the engineering-education reform. The Engineering College provides the detailed activity plan separately.
