It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has announced the results of the 2012 Pan Yugang Scholarship and the Sundelly Chemical Scholarship, with eight undergraduates from the School of Science receiving recognition of RMB 2,500 each.

## Award Lists

The review was conducted in accordance with the university's evaluation regulations for both scholarships and confirmed by the college's review committee.
