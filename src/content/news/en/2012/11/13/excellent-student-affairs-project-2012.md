It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on November 13, 2012, to recommend candidates for the Guangdong University Excellent Student Affairs Program.

In line with central and provincial directives on strengthening ideological and political education for university students and advancing professional student affairs management, the Provincial University Student Affairs Professional Committee will evaluate “Excellent Projects.”

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Student affairs projects run by counselors or student affairs teams for at least three years with notable educational outcomes and wide influence |
| Procedure | Applicant submits to the Student Affairs Office → Office reviews and recommends one candidate to the province → Provincial committee evaluates and commends |
| Materials | One *Excellent Student Affairs Project Application Form* plus a ~5,000-word project report covering name, philosophy, operation, outcomes and features |
| Deadline | November 23, 2012 |
| Submission point | Student Education Management Section (Administration Center 106) |
| Electronic copy | Packaged as “College Name + Excellent Project” and sent to lslin@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

The form and formatting requirements are available in the OA attachment.
