The Wednesday Movie program at Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will screen *National Velvet* on the evening of November 14. Peter Herford will moderate the screening in the Library Lecture Hall. In addition to 150 seats reserved for the class, 50 seats will be open to the public.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *National Velvet* |
| Screening time | 19:00–22:00 on Wednesday, November 14 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Moderator | Peter Herford |
| Public seating | 50 seats, first come, first served |
| Admission | Students should present their campus cards |

*National Velvet* is a 1944 Technicolor sports film based on Enid Bagnold’s 1935 novel of the same name. It stars Mickey Rooney, Donald Crisp, and a young Elizabeth Taylor. The film won the Academy Awards for Best Supporting Actress and Best Film Editing at the 18th ceremony and received nominations for Best Director, Best Art Direction (Color), and Best Cinematography. In 2003, it was selected for preservation in the United States National Film Registry.

Public seating is limited and will be offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Students and faculty members are welcome to attend.
