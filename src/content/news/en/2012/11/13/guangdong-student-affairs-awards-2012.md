It is reported that the Student Affairs Office forwarded a notice on November 13, 2012, from the Guangdong University Student Affairs Professional Committee to recommend candidates for the 2012 Outstanding Student Affairs Individual and the 4th Hongmian Prize.

## Eligibility

| Category | Eligible Group | Main Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outstanding Individual | Student affairs workers who participate in committee activities and pay dues (counselors, deputy secretaries and functional department staff) | Firm political orientation, dedication to student work, at least 3 years of service with outstanding achievements and strong student recognition; frontline counselors with publications or research projects are preferred |
| Hongmian Prize | Full-time frontline student affairs workers with at least 20 years of cumulative service (deputy secretaries, counselors and functional staff) | High political integrity, deep commitment to student work, innovative practice in ideological and political education and notable contributions to student growth |

## Nomination

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quota | In principle, each college/residential college may recommend one candidate for each award |
| Procedure | University preliminary recommendation → Provincial committee review → Commendation at the annual meeting |
| Deadline | 17:00, November 23, 2012 |
| Submission point | Student Education Management Section (Administration Center 106) |
| Electronic copy | Packaged and sent to lslin@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

Outstanding Individuals should submit recommendation forms, award certificates and evidence of publications/projects; Hongmian Prize candidates should submit a form, a ~100-word bio, a ~2,000-word reflection on student work and a recent life photo (JPG, ≤500K). Forms are in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ Forms and attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
