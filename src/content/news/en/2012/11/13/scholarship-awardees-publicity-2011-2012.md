The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has published the proposed recipients of the university’s 2011–2012 scholarships. Under the STU Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate Scholarships, the university proposed 1,746 students for awards based on recommendations from schools and residential colleges, reviews by relevant award committees, and review by the Student Aid Management Center.

| Award category | Proposed recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Student Scholarship | 20 |
| First-class Academic Excellence Scholarship | 177 |
| Second-class Academic Excellence Scholarship | 422 |
| Third-class Academic Excellence Scholarship | 626 |
| Outstanding Contribution to Academic and Scientific Activities | 10 |
| Outstanding Leadership Ability | 10 |
| Outstanding Contribution to Social Service | 10 |
| Outstanding Contribution to Sports and Arts | 10 |
| Academic and Scientific Activities Initiative Award | 47 |
| Campus Cultural Activities Initiative Award | 150 |
| Social Service Initiative Award | 140 |
| Campus Service Initiative Award | 144 |
| **Total** | **1,746** |

Organizations or individuals with concerns should submit written materials to the Student Aid Management Center in Room 106 of the Administration Center by 12:00 on Friday, November 16. Detailed feedback requirements are subject to the original OA notice.
