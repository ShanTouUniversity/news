The seventh Kan Kan Culture event at Shantou University, titled “My Design Observations,” will be held on the evening of November 15. Design scholar Wang Shouzhi will speak with internationally renowned graphic designer Kan Tai-keung, former dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, about observations and experiences in design.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Kan Kan Culture 7 |
| Theme | My Design Observations |
| Conversation | Wang Shouzhi and Kan Tai-keung |
| Time | Starting at 19:30 on November 15 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall |
| Participants | All STU students and faculty members |

The event is open to the STU community.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
