The first Hong Kong Public Governance Experience Salon organized by the Shantou University School of Law will take place on November 18 under the theme “At the Edge of the Establishment: The Role, Function, and Circumstances of Hong Kong Nongovernmental Organizations in Social Development.” Lu Dale, chair and professor in the Department of Sociology at the University of Hong Kong, will speak with public-service veteran Chen Huangsui, with faculty members and graduate students in public administration offering on-site responses.

The discussion will compare the perspectives of a researcher and a public-service manager, reviewing policy design, public debate, implementation outcomes, and governance experience across the 15 years before and after Hong Kong’s return to China (1982–2012). Topics include the characteristics and role of Hong Kong NGOs, their relationship with government, policy changes, governance challenges, independence under resource constraints, the relationship between NGOs and civil society, and lessons for mainland Chinese NGOs.

Lu is chair and professor in the Department of Sociology at the University of Hong Kong and chair of the New Force Network, a civil policy research think tank. Chen is an honorary fellow of the Chinese University of Hong Kong, a former director general of the Hong Kong Consumer Council and leader of an international consumer organization, and currently chair of the Hong Kong Deposit Protection Board and a member of the University Grants Committee and Law Reform Commission.

The event is open to the STU community. Interested participants should follow the online-registration instructions in the School of Law’s original notice. The next salon, *Law and the Rule of Law*, is previewed for 16:00 on December 9 in the Science Lecture Hall, with barrister Tam Hee-chu as guest speaker.
