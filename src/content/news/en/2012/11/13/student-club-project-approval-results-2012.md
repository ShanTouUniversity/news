The student club project-defense session for the 2012–2013 fall semester at Shantou University was held on November 7. Following review by the judges and approval by the STU Youth League Committee, nine student club projects received project support.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Defense session | 2012–2013 fall-semester student club project-defense session |
| Defense date | November 7 |
| Projects approved for support | 9 |
| Review and approval | Review by judges and approval by the STU Youth League Committee |
| Example | Guitar Lovers Association’s Christmas Unplugged Concert |

The STU Youth League Committee and Student Clubs Federation asked the approved project leaders to organize their activities carefully and contribute to campus cultural development. The complete list of approved projects appears in the original OA attachment.
