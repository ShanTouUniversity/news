The College of Science at Shantou University will host a lecture in its Science Salon series on November 15. Professor Ji Hongbing of the School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University, who supervises doctoral students, will speak on “Metal-Porphyrin Enzyme-Mimicking Biomimetic Catalytic Oxidation.”

The lecture will also introduce the current state and development prospects of Guangdong’s petrochemical industry, including talent needs in related fields.
