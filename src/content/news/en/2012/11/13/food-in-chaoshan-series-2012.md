It is reported that the Youth League Committee, the Student Societies Association and the Xingchao Society of Shantou University will hold the “Food in Chaoshan” series as part of the 2012–2013 autumn student club funded program, open to all faculty and students.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Name | “Food in Chaoshan” Series |
| Participants | All faculty and students |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Student Societies Association, Xingchao Society |

### Schedule

| Session | Time | Venue | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chaoshan Food Culture Lecture | 14:30–16:30, Nov 14 | Room E207 | Speakers Zhang Xinmin, Xie Shunlong, Chen Haizhong on Chaoshan cuisine and its cultural significance |
| Thematic Training | 16:30–17:30, Nov 14 | — | Introduction to Chaoshan food varieties and cultural traditions |
| Field Observation | Morning, Nov 17 | Lin Baixin Plaza | Groups observe Chaoshan delicacies and submit photos and descriptions |
| Cooking Contest “Chao Chu Show” | 15:30–17:30, Nov 18 | STU Pedestrian Street | Groups bring utensils and ingredients for a cooking competition |

## Registration and Awards

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| How to apply | Download the form and send it to the designated email by Nov 14, naming the file “Xingchao Society Food in Chaoshan Registration” |
| Places | First-come, first-served |
| Awards | 1 first prize, 1 second prize, 2 third prizes and 3 merit awards |

The application form and email address are available in the OA attachment and withheld here for privacy.
