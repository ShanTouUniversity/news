The STU Student Affairs Office has arranged applications for temporary hardship subsidies for the 2012–2013 fall semester. The funding comes from a 2012 allocation by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance under central incentive funds related to national student loans. It is intended to support students who experience financial difficulty because of unexpected events and will be administered under the Trial Measures for Temporary Hardship Subsidies for STU Undergraduates.

### Application and review schedule

| Stage | Deadline and requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Student online application | By 11:00 on December 21; log in to the university application platform, apply, and upload supporting materials |
| School or residential-college review | Review applications and consolidate results |
| Consolidated form submission | Deliver to the Student Aid Management Center by 11:00 on December 28 |
| Eligible applicants | Undergraduates whose living situation has become difficult because of an unexpected event |

The application platform is the university scholarship and aid portal: <http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn/chinese/applyIntro2.aspx>. Applicants should provide materials that document the unexpected hardship according to the platform’s requirements. Schools and residential colleges will review the applications and submit consolidated forms through the required process.
