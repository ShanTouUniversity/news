It is reported that the Finance Office, the Student Affairs Office, and the Resource Management Office of Shantou University have jointly issued a notice regarding outstanding utility payments. As of November 16, 2012, some student dormitories had not yet settled their water and electricity bills for October and earlier months. In accordance with university regulations, power supply to those dormitories will be suspended starting November 19 until all arrears are paid.

## Deduction and Power Restoration Schedule

From November 19 to 21, deductions via the campus card will be processed twice daily:

| Deduction Time | Power Restoration Window |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| 10:30 (first deduction) | 14:30–16:30 the same day |
| 16:30 (second deduction) | 17:30–20:00 the same day |
| Payment after 16:30 | 14:30–16:30 the next day |

After November 21, deductions will be processed once daily at 16:30.

## Payment Methods

- Students with insufficient campus card balance should recharge at the card top-up machines promptly;
- Those with insufficient bank account balance should deposit adequate funds during banking hours and complete the transfer to the campus card;
- Students who have lost their campus card may use the "Utility Management System" to set up proxy payment, or deposit cash to the university account at the Bank of China STU Branch (Account No.: 628857760147) and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for crediting. The Finance Office does not accept cash directly.

The three offices remind affected students to settle outstanding bills in a timely manner to avoid disruption to their electricity supply.
