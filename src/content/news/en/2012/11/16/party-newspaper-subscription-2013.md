The Shantou University Publicity Department has set arrangements for 2013 subscriptions to Party newspapers and journals in accordance with the subscription task assigned by the Shantou municipal authorities. The publicity budget will coordinate the subscription costs. Units should complete registration according to organizational type and staffing or student numbers.

| Unit type | Subscription arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Schools and residential colleges | One copy each of *People’s Daily*, *Guangming Daily*, and *Nanfang Daily* |
| Administrative units with 10 or fewer staff | One copy of *People’s Daily* |
| Administrative units with 11–50 staff | One copy each of *People’s Daily* and *Nanfang Daily* |
| Administrative units with more than 50 staff | One copy each of *People’s Daily*, *Nanfang Daily*, and *Shantou Daily* |
| Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches | One copy each of *Qiushi*, *Nanfang*, and *Guangming* |
| Students in schools and residential colleges | *Nanfang Daily* and *Shantou Daily*, allocated at one copy per 90 students |
| Business School | Two copies of *Economic Daily* |
| Finance Office and Office of Resource Management | One copy of *Economic Daily* each |

Units operating under economic contracts must cover their own newspaper-subscription expenses. All units must submit the Party newspaper and journal subscription registration form to the Publicity Department by Tuesday, November 20, stating their staff or student numbers as required.
