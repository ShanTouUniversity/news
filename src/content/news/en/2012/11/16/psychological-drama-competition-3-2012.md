The STU Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will hold the third Undergraduate Psychological-Drama Competition under the theme “New Life, Happy Heart.” The competition will use common student psychological concerns as creative material and aims to promote mental-health knowledge on campus while enriching the formats of mental-health education.

### Participation and production requirements

The competition is open to all STU students and faculty members. Participants may form teams freely, with no more than 15 members per team. Entries must be original undergraduate-student creations with healthy content, a clear theme, psychological knowledge and counseling skills, engaging plots, and artistic presentation.

Permitted formats include musicals, plays, skits, mimes, and song-and-dance dramas. Performances should run approximately 10–15 minutes, with dialogue or narration mainly in Mandarin; English or dialects may be used as appropriate. Teams should prepare their own props, while finalists will receive an appropriate subsidy for props.

### Competition schedule and awards

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Registration and script submission | November 16–December 1 |
| Preliminary-round training | Selected teams receive training in scriptwriting, performance skills, and related areas |
| Preliminary round | Live performances on December 23 |
| Final | Held during the following semester |
| First prize | 1, 1,000 yuan and certificate |
| Second prize | 2, 600 yuan and certificate each |
| Third prize | 3, 300 yuan and certificate each |
| Best Popularity Award | 1, 200 yuan and certificate |

Paper scripts and electronic materials must be submitted according to the original instructions of the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center. The institutional email for electronic scripts and inquiries is `o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn`; the center’s telephone number is 82903380.
