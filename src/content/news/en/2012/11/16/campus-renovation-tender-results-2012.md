Following review and recommendations by the Shantou University Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, STU has publicized the winning contractors for two construction projects.

| Project | Winning contractor | Winning bid |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Academic Exchange Center fire-staircase facade renovation | Guangdong Junding Fire Protection and Electromechanical Engineering Co., Ltd. | 84,761.25 yuan |
| Student Entrepreneurship Park Management Office repair | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. | 40,683.42 yuan |

The public notice period runs from November 16 to 19, 2012. The STU Infrastructure Office is the tendering entity. Its tender inquiries telephone number is (0754) 82903216 and its fax number is (0754) 82902314.

Concerns about the results may be submitted to the STU Supervision, Audit, and Inspection Office at (0754) 82902346 by telephone or (0754) 82903663 by fax.
