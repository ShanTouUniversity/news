The College of Science at Shantou University will host a Science Salon lecture on November 23. Professor Su Min of the School of Pharmacy at the Third Military Medical University will speak on “Considerations in Research on Novel Sustained- and Controlled-Release Drug Formulations.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Professor Su Min |
| Affiliation | School of Pharmacy, Third Military Medical University |
| Topic | Considerations in Research on Novel Sustained- and Controlled-Release Drug Formulations |
| Time | 10:30–11:30 on November 23 |
| Venue | First-Floor Lecture Hall, Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center |

Drawing on the 2010 edition of the Chinese Pharmacopoeia, relevant U.S. Food and Drug Administration requirements, and the pharmaceutical field’s Quality by Design (QbD) concept, the lecture will discuss recent developments, characteristics, and requirements of novel sustained- and controlled-release formulations. It will also cover principles for formulation design, research, and release-technology studies, including the requirements and evaluation of sustained-release materials, with analysis based on recent clinical drug-preparation cases.
