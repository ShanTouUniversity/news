The STU Office of Research Affairs will host a lecture titled “Analyzing STU Academic Development and Research Performance: Based on Authoritative SCI, ESI, and InCites Data and Analytical Tools.” Ma Yapeng, a product and solutions expert with Thomson Reuters Intellectual Property and Science, will deliver the presentation.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Analyzing STU Academic Development and Research Performance: Based on Authoritative SCI, ESI, and InCites Data and Analytical Tools |
| Speaker | Ma Yapeng |
| Affiliation | Thomson Reuters Intellectual Property and Science |
| Time | 10:00–11:00 on Monday, November 19 |
| Venue | Science Building Conference Room 205 |
| Primary audience | Deans overseeing research, academic leaders and research fellows, key laboratory directors, staff in academic-development and research-administration offices, and school research secretaries |

The presentation will examine STU’s research output and influence, compare the university’s academic results with related institutions from multiple perspectives, and discuss opportunities and challenges in academic development and research. Other interested students and faculty members are welcome.
