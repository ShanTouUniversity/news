As part of the seventh Guangdong University Students Campus Culture and Arts Festival, the provincial Youth League, education and culture authorities, and the provincial student federation will organize the “Voices on Campus” Guangdong University Students Top Ten Singers Competition. The STU Youth League Committee has forwarded the competition notice and invited students across the university to register.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | “Voices on Campus” Guangdong University Students Top Ten Singers Competition, part of the seventh Guangdong University Students Campus Culture and Arts Festival |
| Participants | All STU students |
| Registration material | Registration form |
| Internal deadline | By November 19 |
| Submission email | `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` |
| Internal recommendation | The STU Youth League Committee will select candidates based on the actual registrations |

Interested students should complete the registration form according to the competition notice and submit it to the STU Youth League Committee by the deadline. Detailed rules, participation requirements, and the form format are subject to the original OA attachment forwarded with the notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the detailed competition notice and registration form.
