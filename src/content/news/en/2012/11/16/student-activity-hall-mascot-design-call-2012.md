To promote the Student Activity Hall and showcase student and faculty creativity, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office has invited mascot designs from the entire STU community. The Student Activity Hall management team is organizing the call, which will run from November 17 to 30.

### Design requirements

Entries must be original, positive, healthy, and easy to understand. They should reflect the Student Activity Hall’s functions, demonstrate creativity and strong recognizability, and be easy to promote. Participants should submit electronic materials including a JPG mascot file and a Word document explaining the design concept and providing author information. File-naming and submission channels are subject to the original OA notice.

### Evaluation and awards

| Award | Places | Prize |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Selected Design Award | 1 | Free annual pass for the Student Activity Hall |
| Outstanding Design Award | 3 | Free monthly pass for the Student Activity Hall |

Judges will evaluate entries based on form, creativity, and meaning. The results are expected to be published on the university’s office information network in December.

Once a design is adopted, the Student Activity Hall may propose revisions in consultation with the author and will hold permanent usage rights to the mascot.
