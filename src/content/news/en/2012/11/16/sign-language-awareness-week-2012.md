The STU Sign Language Club will hold “Hands Dance, Hearts Speak,” the first sign-language awareness week under the 2012–2013 fall-semester student club project program. The series will promote sign-language culture through games, lessons, and performances and is open to the STU community.

| Activity | Time | Venue | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activity 1 | 11:00–13:00, November 19 | Golden Triangle and the banyan tree area | Sign-language riddle and jigsaw games; live sign-language song performance |
| Activity 2 | 11:00–13:00, November 21 | Golden Triangle | Sign-language matching game, “Call Your Name” sign-language lesson, and sign-language song performance |
| Activity 3 | 10:00–14:00, November 24 | Golden Triangle and the area beside the tennis courts | “Most Dazzling Sign-Language Style” lesson, sign-language maze game, and sign-language song performance |
| Activity 4 | 19:30–21:00, November 24 | To be announced | Sign-language lesson for the song “University Questions” |

The series is organized by the STU Youth League Committee, STU Student Clubs Federation, and STU Sign Language Club. Students and faculty members across the university are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
