The Shantou University Student Affairs Office, Alumni Association Office, and Physical Education Department have released the *Schedule Booklet for the Student Selection Tournament of the First Student Community X Plan Alumni Cup Badminton Friendship Tournament*. The booklet has been finalized and printed, and its electronic version is now available as the basis for the competition arrangements.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Student selection tournament of the first Student Community X Plan Alumni Cup Badminton Friendship Tournament |
| Release | Electronic schedule booklet for the student selection tournament |
| Applicable to | Participating units, players, and related staff |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office, Alumni Association Office, and Physical Education Department |
| Release date | November 16, 2012 |

Participating units, players, and staff should consult the electronic booklet. The competition order and arrangements are subject to that document.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Alumni Association Office, and Physical Education Department)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the detailed tournament schedule, participation arrangements, and related forms.
