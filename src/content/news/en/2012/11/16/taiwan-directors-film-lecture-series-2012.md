The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will host a Taiwanese directors film lecture series with two sessions on production design and documentary filmmaking. Both sessions will be held in the Lecture Hall of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design.

| Session | Topic | Speaker | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | Hollywood Production Design History | Yang Zongying (Michael Young), production designer | 19:30–21:30 on November 19 |
| Second | Documentary Filmmaking | Wu Zhaojun (Marco Wu), executive producer | 19:30–21:30 on November 21 |

The series will give students and faculty members an opportunity to learn about film-industry practice through production design and documentary creation.

The archived notice’s English section repeats November 19 as the date of the second session, while the Chinese schedule clearly gives November 21. This article follows the Chinese date.
