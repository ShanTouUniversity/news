It is reported that to enliven cultural life in the residential areas and select outstanding contestants for the preliminary round of the 20th Campus Top Ten Singers Contest, the university Youth League Committee and the Student Union held community auditions under the Student Community X Program on November 10. After fierce competition, 20 students including Li S stood out and earned places in the qualifying round.

## Qualifying Round Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | November 17 (Saturday), 7:30 pm - 10:00 pm |
| Venue | Small Stage |
| Contestants | 20 students advancing from the community auditions |
| Advancing spots | Top five contestants |

Under the contest rules, the qualifying round will select the top five contestants of the qualifying stage for the 20th Campus Top Ten Singers Contest. In addition, all contestants who enter the qualifying round will be named "Outstanding Contestant of the Qualifying Round of the 20th Campus Top Ten Singers Contest" and receive a certificate.

The organizers welcome all faculty and students to attend and cheer for the contestants. The full list of contestants is available in the attachment on the OA system.
