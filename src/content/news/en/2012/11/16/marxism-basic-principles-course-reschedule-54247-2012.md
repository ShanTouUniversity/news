The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has adjusted two meetings of course section 54247, *Basic Principles of Marxism*, because the instructor will be away. Classes originally scheduled during periods A, B, and C on Thursdays, December 6 and 13, in Room E204 will be made up during periods 2, 3, and 4 on Sundays, November 18 and December 23, respectively. The venue remains unchanged.

| Original class | Make-up class | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Periods A, B, and C, Thursday, December 6 | Periods 2, 3, and 4, Sunday, November 18 | Room E204 |
| Periods A, B, and C, Thursday, December 13 | Periods 2, 3, and 4, Sunday, December 23 | Room E204 |

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Basic Principles of Marxism* |
| Section | 54247 |
| Reason | Instructor away |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in the course to inform one another of the change.
