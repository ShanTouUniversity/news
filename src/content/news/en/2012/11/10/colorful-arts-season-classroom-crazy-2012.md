The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center at Shantou University will present the main event of Colorful Arts Season 2012 Autumn: *Classroom Goes Crazy*, a dialogue-free physical comedy by Tang Shu-wing Theatre Studio. The performance will take place in the STU Auditorium on the evening of November 16, and audience members must be at least 12 years old.

| Performance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Production | *Classroom Goes Crazy* |
| Format | Dialogue-free physical comedy |
| Time | 19:30 on November 16 |
| Venue | STU Auditorium |
| Company | Tang Shu-wing Theatre Studio |
| Audience age | 12 and above |

### Ticket collection

Arts fans may register for tickets with their Colorful Arts Season 2012 Autumn Arts Fan Pass. Other students and faculty members may register with their campus card. Tickets can be collected at Room 309 of the Arts Education Center on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last.

| Group | Collection times |
| --- | --- |
| Arts fans | November 12, 09:30–12:00 and 14:30–17:00 |
| Other students and faculty members | November 13, 09:30–12:00 and 14:30–17:00 |

### Creative Arts Ambassador recruitment

The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design is also recruiting STU Creative Arts Ambassadors. Eligible applicants include first- and second-year undergraduates and postgraduate students from all colleges, with students outside the arts disciplines particularly welcome. Applications will be completed on site from 10:00–12:00 and 14:00–16:00 on November 11 in the Interactive Exchange Space beside the stairs on the first floor of the front building of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Arts Education Center)
