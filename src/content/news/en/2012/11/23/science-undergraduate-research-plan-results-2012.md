STU’s Science School has announced the results of its 2011–2012 undergraduate research plan. A total of 43 research projects were approved. Thirty-eight completed the final defense, while five did not attend the defense and were treated as voluntarily withdrawn. Those five projects were assessed as unsuccessful, and their initial project funding will be recovered.

### Project results

| Result | Number of projects |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 6 |
| Second prize | 7 |
| Third prize | 8 |
| Passed | 17 |
| Did not attend defense and unsuccessful | 5 |

The school’s departmental review panels also selected six outstanding supervisors. The school will recognize successful projects and outstanding supervisors and issue certificates; the detailed arrangements will be announced separately by the school’s Youth League Committee.
