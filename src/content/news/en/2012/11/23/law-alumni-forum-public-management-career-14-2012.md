STU’s Law School will hold the 14th session of its Alumni Forum series on “Public Administration and Career Development.” Alumni Chen Lihua, Zhong Jianfeng, Yan Shenshan, and Zeng Wei will draw on their professional experience to discuss study in public administration and career choices.

The speakers currently work at Lishui Town Government in Nanhai District, Foshan; the Organization Office of Gaoming District, Foshan; Mingcheng Town Government in Gaoming District, Foshan; and the Huangpu Port Branch of Guangzhou Port, respectively. The forum will begin at 8:30 p.m. on November 24 in Room 213 of the Law School. All faculty members and students are welcome.
