The 15th Business School Alumni Lecture at STU will explore “My Career Path.” Feng Jianqiang, a 1990 alumnus of the Business School’s International Trade program, will share his professional experience and business-development practice with current students.

Feng graduated from STU’s Business School in 1994 and later earned an MBA from South China University of Technology. Since 2001, he has served as the legal representative and general manager of Foshan Daming Lighting Appliance Co., Ltd.

The lecture will begin at 8:30 p.m. on Saturday, November 24, in the second-floor seminar room of the Business School.
