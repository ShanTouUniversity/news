The first lecture in STU’s 2012 CCS Visiting Scholar holistic life education series will examine humanistic education and cultural revival. Professor Guo Shaotang will discuss “The Meeting of Humanistic Education and Cultural Revival: The Innovation of Liberal Arts Education,” sharing reflections on liberal arts education and cultural development.

The lecture will begin at 7:30 p.m. on Monday, November 26, in the Library Lecture Hall. Guo previously served as a professor in the Department of History, university counselor, and dean of the Faculty of Arts at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. He also served as executive vice president of the Beijing Normal University–Hong Kong Baptist University United International College. His research interests include Chinese and Western cultures, comparative urban studies, and cultural and creative industries.
