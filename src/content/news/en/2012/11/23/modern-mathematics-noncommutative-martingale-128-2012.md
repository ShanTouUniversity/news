STU’s Science School will host the 128th lecture in its Modern Mathematics series. Professor Toerdebieke, a doctoral supervisor in the School of Mathematics and Systems Science at Xinjiang University, will present “Noncommutative Martingale Theory.” The discussion will relate to noncommutative analysis, operator theory, and space theory in functional analysis.

The lecture will begin at 4:30 p.m. on Monday, November 26, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). Faculty members, graduate students, and senior undergraduates are welcome.
