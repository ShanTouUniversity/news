The 2012 STU Inter-School Cup basketball tournament will reach its final stage on November 25 after the group round-robin and semifinal matches. Organized by the Student Union, the tournament aims to encourage interaction among students from different schools and promote teamwork and campus sportsmanship.

### Final arrangements

| Event | Match or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Men’s championship match | School of Business Administration vs. School of Engineering |
| Women’s championship match | School of Humanities vs. School of Business Administration |
| Three-point contest final | Qualifiers from the Science, Arts, Engineering, and Graduate schools |
| Time | 7–9:30 p.m., Sunday, November 25 |
| Venue | STU Gymnasium |

Awards will include first, second, and third places in the men’s and women’s divisions, most valuable player awards for both divisions, the top three places in the three-point contest, and the referee “Golden Whistle” award.

A “Date with the Inter-School Cup” activity will also be held at the Golden Triangle from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. on the day of the final, featuring tournament video and photo highlights, a slam-dunk game, and a championship prediction activity. After the final, the organizers will draw lucky spectators from the champion team’s ballot box and distribute prizes.
