STU’s Law School will hold the 13th session of its Alumni Forum series on “Legal Studies and Career Development.” Fourteen alumni from the Law and International Economic Law programs will draw on experience in government offices, judicial institutions, law firms, businesses, and universities to discuss legal studies and career choices.

The forum will begin at 8:30 p.m. on November 24 in Room E202. All faculty members and students are welcome.
