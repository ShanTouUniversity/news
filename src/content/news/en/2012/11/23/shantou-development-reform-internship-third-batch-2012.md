STU’s Business School has announced the third group selected for an internship program with the Shantou Development and Reform Commission. Following student applications and discussions between the school and the commission, three students were confirmed for the placement.

The Business School thanked students for their participation and encouraged those not selected to follow later activity notices from the school. Because the original notice contains students’ identifying information, the selected names are not reproduced here.
