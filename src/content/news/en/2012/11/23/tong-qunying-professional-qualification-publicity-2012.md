Following review by the Guangdong Professional Publishing Editor and Associate Editor Qualification Committee, Tong Qunying of STU has obtained the senior publishing-editor professional qualification through promotion. STU has opened the result for public comment from November 23 through 29.

During the public-comment period, objections may be submitted to the personnel authority of the Guangdong Press and Publication Administration, the STU Personnel Office, or the Office of Supervision and Audit. Individuals are encouraged to sign or identify themselves when submitting comments, while submissions from organizations should bear the organization’s official seal. Comments should be factual and must not be defamatory or maliciously accusatory.
