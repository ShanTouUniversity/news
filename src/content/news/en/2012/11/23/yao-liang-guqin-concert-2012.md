Zhicheng College’s “Spring Breeze” series will present a guqin concert by Yao Liang. The guqin performer will appear at 7:30 p.m. on Saturday, December 1, in the Science Lecture Hall. Faculty members and students from across the university are welcome.

Yao Liang studied with guqin performers Gong Yi and Wu Zhaoji and has worked for many years to perform and promote the instrument. She received an Excellence in Performance award at the Hangzhou International Guqin Invitational Competition and has participated in cultural events in China and abroad. She currently serves as a council member of the Guqin Professional Committee of the Chinese Orchestra Association and as a guqin examination judge, among other positions.

Reservations are required. The deadline is 1 p.m. on November 28, through the university activity system: <http://activity.stu.edu.cn/index.aspx>.
