STU’s Calligraphy and Painting Association will hold the Autumn Ink Cup Campus Calligraphy and Painting Competition under the theme “Ink Expresses Affection, Harmony Enriches Campus.” The competition aims to foster interest in traditional Chinese culture and provide a platform for artistic expression and exchange. It is organized jointly by the STU Youth League Committee, Student Organizations Federation, and Calligraphy and Painting Association.

The competition is open to all faculty members and students. Works should inherit and promote excellent Chinese traditional culture, and preliminary-round writing or drawing should relate to Chinese classics or quotations by Chinese and international figures. The competition includes calligraphy, covering brush and hard-pen work, and painting, covering Chinese painting, oil painting, pastel, printmaking, sketching, and quick sketches.

### Works and awards

Requirements for dimensions, mounting, and explanatory text are provided in the original OA attachment. Each participant may submit no more than two works or work groups in the calligraphy or painting categories; small works such as quick sketches must be assembled as required. A judging panel will conduct preliminary and final reviews. Each category will offer one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes, and several excellence awards. Award recipients will receive certificates and gifts, and first-prize winners may be recommended by the judges for membership in the Shantou Artists Association.

Submissions close on December 1. Winning works will be exhibited in the library together with works collected from alumni and universities in the Chaoshan region.
