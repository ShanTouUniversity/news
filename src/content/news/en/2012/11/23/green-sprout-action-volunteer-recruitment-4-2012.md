The Green Ribbon environmental service-learning team of the STU Young Volunteers Association will carry out its fourth Green Sprout Action. Volunteers will visit Xidong Primary School in Shantou and use talks, games, and other interactive activities to promote environmental awareness among primary-school pupils under the theme “Save Resources and Turn Waste into Useful Materials.”

The activity will be held from 2:30 to 4:10 p.m. on Wednesday, December 5. Nineteen volunteers will be recruited from among faculty members and students across the university. Applicants must attend both volunteer training from 8 to 9:30 p.m. on December 1 and the service activity on December 5, and should demonstrate environmental commitment, responsibility, communication skills, and teamwork. Applications close at midnight on November 29; volunteer interviews will also be arranged before the activity.

Applicants may submit materials as specified in the original OA attachment or register in person beneath Dining Halls 2 and 4 at noon on November 29. Volunteer service hours will be recorded in the university’s volunteer-service-hours system.
