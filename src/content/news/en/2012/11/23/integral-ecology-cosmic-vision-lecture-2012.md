STU’s School of Humanities will host a 2012 CCS Visiting Scholar lecture on holistic life education. Professor Guo Shaotang of Hong Kong will discuss “Integral Ecology, Cosmic Vision, Spiritual Health, and the Dilemma of the Self,” exploring connections among ecology, a cosmic perspective, spiritual well-being, and personal development.

The lecture will begin at 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, November 27, in the Library Lecture Hall. Guo previously served as a professor in the Department of History, university counselor, and dean of the Faculty of Arts at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. He also served as executive vice president of the Beijing Normal University–Hong Kong Baptist University United International College. His research interests include Chinese and Western cultures, comparative urban studies, and cultural and creative industries.
