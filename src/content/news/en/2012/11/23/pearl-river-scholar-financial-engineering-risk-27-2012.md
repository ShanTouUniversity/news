The 27th Guangdong Higher-Education Pearl River Scholars Lecture will take place at 3 p.m. on Thursday, November 29, in the second-floor lecture hall of the Yifu Science Building at South China University of Technology. Its topic is “Research on Selected Issues in Financial Engineering and Risk Management.”

The speaker, Professor and doctoral supervisor Zhang Weiguo of South China University of Technology, conducts research on the theory and applications of financial engineering and risk management. He is vice dean of the university’s School of Business Administration and holds academic responsibilities related to management science and engineering. He has received support including the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars and multiple provincial or ministerial-level research awards.

Faculty members at STU who wish to attend may register through the Personnel Office’s arrangements. The university will consolidate the list and submit it to the host institution. The internal registration deadline is the end of the workday on November 27.
