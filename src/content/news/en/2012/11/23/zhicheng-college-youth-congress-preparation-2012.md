With the approval of the STU Youth League Committee and Zhicheng College Party General Branch, the second Youth League Congress of STU’s Zhicheng College is scheduled for November 30. The congress will review the work of the first college Youth League Committee, elect the second committee, and discuss future priorities and approaches.

### Delegate and committee arrangements

Each branch will determine its number of delegates at 15% of its Youth League membership. Delegates must be members of the Zhicheng College Youth League or the Communist Party and must have representative and deliberative capacity. Branches must complete delegate elections and submit registration materials for qualification review by November 25.

The second Zhicheng College Youth League Committee is expected to have seven members. Candidates must meet the delegate requirements and demonstrate initiative, Youth League experience, and organizational and social-activity skills. Each branch and relevant organization may recommend one or two candidates. Candidate recommendations and registration must be completed by November 25, after which the congress will conduct a competitive election.
