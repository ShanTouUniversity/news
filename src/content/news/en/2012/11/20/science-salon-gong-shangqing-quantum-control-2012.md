It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on November 23, 2012, featuring Prof. Gong Shangqing of the School of Science, East China University of Science and Technology.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Controlling the Quantum World with Lasers: Quantum Control |
| Speaker | Prof. Gong Shangqing, East China University of Science and Technology |
| Time | 16:30, November 23, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |

## Abstract and Speaker

The lecture will introduce the basic features of controlling the quantum world with lasers, covering key issues in atomic and molecular physics and optics such as precision measurement, ultracold matter, ultrashort ultra-intense lasers and quantum information science, and will present recent work on non-demolition measurement.

Prof. Gong, born in 1963 in Tai’an, Shandong, received his degrees from Shandong University and the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and studied abroad with support from the Sino-British Fellowship and the Humboldt Foundation. He is a recipient of the Rao Yutai Prize for Basic Optics, a member of the CAS Hundred Talents Program, a Leading Academic of Shanghai, and a first-prize winner of the Shanghai Natural Science Award. He served as deputy director of the State Key Laboratory of Strong Field Laser Physics at the Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics, CAS, and as an editorial board member of several academic journals, and now serves as professor, doctoral supervisor and head of the Department of Physics at ECUST, with more than 140 SCI papers.

All interested faculty and students are welcome to attend.
