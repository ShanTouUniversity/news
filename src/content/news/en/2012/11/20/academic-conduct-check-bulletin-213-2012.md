The STU “Three Conducts” Development Office has released Bulletin No. 213 on academic conduct for the ninth week of the 2012–2013 fall semester. The inspection team checked classroom attendance in teaching buildings, focusing on student lateness.

| Inspection time | Location | Findings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14:00 on November 15 | E Teaching Building | 3 students were up to five minutes late; 7 were more than five minutes late |
| 19:00 on November 15 | Room E204 | 27 students were more than five minutes late |

The bulletin asks the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to the students concerned and to continue strengthening academic conduct and classroom-order management.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU “Three Conducts” Development Office)
