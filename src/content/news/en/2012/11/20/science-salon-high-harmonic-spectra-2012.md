The STU College of Science will hold a Science Salon lecture on November 23. Zhang Jingtao, a researcher at the State Key Laboratory of High Field Laser Physics, Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, will speak on “Atomic-Wavefunction Modulation of High-Harmonic and Photoelectron Spectra.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Researcher Zhang Jingtao |
| Affiliation | State Key Laboratory of High Field Laser Physics, Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences |
| Topic | Atomic-Wavefunction Modulation of High-Harmonic and Photoelectron Spectra |
| Time | 15:10 on Friday, November 23 |
| Venue | College of Science Lecture Hall, Room Li-352 |

The lecture will first introduce the background of ultrashort, ultraintense lasers, then review progress in research on interactions between such lasers and atoms and molecules, and finally present related results on high-harmonic generation by atoms in strong laser fields.

Zhang holds a doctorate in science and supervises doctoral students. His long-term theoretical research focuses on interactions between lasers and atoms and molecules. He has published papers in journals including *Physical Review Letters*, *Physical Review A*, and *Optical Express*, and has led several projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and Shanghai research programs.
