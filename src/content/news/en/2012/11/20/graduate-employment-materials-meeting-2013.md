The STU Graduate School will hold a meeting for class monitors of the 2013 graduating graduate-student cohort and distribute employment materials. Employment agreements, graduate-student registration forms, and other materials were collected from the university Career Guidance Center on November 19.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Class monitors of the 2013 graduating graduate-student cohort |
| Time | 15:00 on Tuesday, November 20 |
| Venue | Administration Center Room 330 |
| Agenda | Monitor meeting and collection of class employment materials |
| Agreements | One copy per graduate, in a four-part form |

Employment agreements are issued uniformly by the provincial authorities. Class monitors should verify and distribute the materials according to the university’s arrangements. If a monitor cannot attend, another student should be assigned to participate.

The sections of the graduate-student registration form are completed by the relevant parties: the class monitor records the student’s overall performance at the university, the supervisor writes the evaluation, each training unit completes and stamps its opinion, and the Graduate School completes and stamps the university or research-unit opinion before filing the form. Before graduation, class monitors must collect the completed forms and return them to their training units.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
>
> ⚠️ Employment agreements, barcodes, student numbers, registration forms, and other personal materials should be collected and stored according to Graduate School instructions. Student numbers and personal documents are not reproduced here.
