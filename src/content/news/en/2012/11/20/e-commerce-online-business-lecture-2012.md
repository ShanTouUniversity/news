The online-business lecture accompanying Shantou University’s first e-commerce practice project will be held on November 21, using Taobao’s “Double Eleven” phenomenon as a starting point to discuss e-commerce practice. The event is organized by the STU Business School and Engineering College and implemented by the Business Practice Center and ModernTop-Union.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture | Online-business lecture for the first e-commerce practice project |
| Topic | Starting with the Taobao “Double Eleven” phenomenon |
| Time | 14:00 on Wednesday, November 21 |
| Venue | E204 Lecture Room |
| Organizers | STU Business School and Engineering College |
| Implementing units | Business Practice Center and ModernTop-Union |

Guest speaker Chen Congying graduated from the STU Department of Computer and Information Engineering in 1993. After working for many years in Shantou’s telecommunications sector, he developed experience in e-commerce training and business development. He has served as executive director, chief e-commerce adviser, and trainer at the Shantou High-Tech E-Commerce Industrial Base (Shantou Boxun Network Technology Co., Ltd.), and has also held e-commerce advisory and training leadership roles at related companies.

The lecture is intended to help e-commerce practice participants understand online-business operations and e-commerce development. Interested students and faculty members are welcome.
