The “Fly Young Youth” Guangdong University Student Campus Microfilm Competition, part of the seventh Guangdong University Students Campus Culture and Arts Festival, has opened for registration. The provincial Youth League, education and culture authorities, and student federation are using the competition to enliven university culture, encourage students to express their individuality, record social and campus life, and present the positive spirit of young people.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | “Fly Young Youth” Guangdong University Student Campus Microfilm Competition |
| Festival | Seventh Guangdong University Students Campus Culture and Arts Festival |
| Creative direction | Use accessible filming tools to record social and campus life from different perspectives |
| Participants | STU students |
| Registration material | Registration form |
| Internal deadline | By December 9 |
| Submission email | `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` |
| Internal recommendation | The STU Youth League Committee will select entries based on actual registrations |

Interested students should complete the registration form according to the competition notice and submit it to the STU Youth League Committee by the deadline. Detailed production requirements, competition rules, and registration formats are subject to the original OA attachments forwarded with the notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the detailed competition notice, registration form, and production requirements.
