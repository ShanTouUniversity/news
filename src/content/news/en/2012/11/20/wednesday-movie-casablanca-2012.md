The STU Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication’s Wednesday Movie program will screen the classic film *Casablanca*. The screening will take place on the evening of November 21, and students and faculty members across the university are welcome.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *Casablanca* |
| Screening time | 19:00–22:00 on Wednesday, November 21 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Moderator | Peter Herford |
| Public seating | 50 seats in addition to the 150 seats reserved for the course |
| Admission | Public seats are first come, first served; students should present their campus card |

Set in Casablanca during World War II, the film follows nightclub owner Rick after his former lover Ilsa arrives with her anti-Nazi husband. Rick must choose among rekindled love, personal ethics, and helping the couple escape so the resistance leader can continue fighting the Nazis.

*Casablanca* won the 16th Academy Awards for Best Picture, Best Director, and Best Writing, Screenplay. It was also nominated for Best Actor, Best Supporting Actor, Best Cinematography in Black and White, Best Film Editing, and Best Music.
