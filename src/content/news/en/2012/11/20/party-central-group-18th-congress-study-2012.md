Shantou University will hold a study session of the STU Party Committee central group on the 18th National Party Congress. The session will focus on studying the congress and its report, “Advance Unswayingly Along the Path of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics and Strive to Complete the Building of a Moderately Prosperous Society in All Respects,” and on promoting implementation of the congress spirit across relevant university units.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | STU Party Committee central group study session on the 18th Party Congress |
| Time | 15:00 on Wednesday, November 21 |
| Venue | New Administration Center Conference Room 213 |
| Organizer | Party Committee Publicity Department |

Participants include members of the university Party Committee; secretaries of Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches; heads of the Discipline Inspection Commission, Party and Government Office, Organization and United Front Department, Publicity Department, News Center, and Student Affairs Office; the director of the Social Sciences Department; the vice chair of the university trade union; and the secretary of the university Youth League Committee. Attendees should arrive on time.
