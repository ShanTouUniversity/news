Because the mathematics instructor will be away, the STU Academic Affairs Office has adjusted course section 55469, *Probability and Mathematical Statistics (Economics and Biology)*. The class originally scheduled for periods 3 and 4 on Wednesday, November 21, in Lecture Hall 5 has been suspended. A make-up time will be announced separately.
