The STU Student Community will hold a Colorful Vegetarian Food Festival featuring lectures, a cooking demonstration, and hands-on activities. The series is intended to enrich campus life, help students and faculty members learn about vegetarian food and healthy diets, and encourage respect for life and nature. The activities are open to the STU community.

### Schedule

| Activity | Time | Venue | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vegetarian Heart Salon lecture | 19:30–21:30 on December 1 | Science and Technology Center Room 205 | Lecture on “Ten Junk Foods,” delivered by guest speaker Yang Zidong |
| Learn vegetarian cooking from chefs | 14:30–18:00 on December 1 | Jiexiyuan Vegetarian Food Court, Shantou | Observe and learn the vegetarian-cooking process |
| Vegetarian DIY | 09:00–11:40 on December 2 | Golden Triangle | Vegetarian-knowledge quiz and vegetarian-cooking competition |

The vegetarian DIY competition requires team registration, with 2–4 members per team. Six to eight teams will be accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. Awards include one first prize of 300 yuan, one second prize of 150 yuan, two third prizes of 100 yuan each, and one Best Popularity Award of 50 yuan. Award recipients will also receive commemorative gifts.

The organizers will provide the cooking equipment for the DIY activity. Teams must prepare their own ingredients and may receive a subsidy of up to 40 yuan per team for ingredients. On-site spectators may join interactive games and have opportunities to win gifts.

Activities 2 and 3 require advance registration. The registration form and submission method are subject to the original OA attachment, with registration closing at 18:00 on November 28.
