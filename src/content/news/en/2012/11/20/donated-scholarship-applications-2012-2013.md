The STU Student Affairs Office has opened the assessment process for scholarships funded by enterprises and individuals for the 2012–2013 academic year. The scholarships provide 171 awards totaling 840,000 yuan. The standard award is 5,000 yuan per recipient, while the Liang Zhaohan Medical College Student Scholarship provides 2,000 yuan per recipient.

### Awards and special programs

| Program | Places or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding students from outside Guangdong program | 26 places, selected at the beginning of the academic year |
| Medical College freshman program | 1 place |
| Hakka-student program | 2 places |
| Chenghai-student program | 2 places |
| Outstanding upper-year student program | 140 places |

The Hakka-student program is open to students in their third year or above at the main campus with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 who are of Hakka origin. The Chenghai-student program additionally requires applicants to face financial difficulty. The outstanding upper-year student program is open to main-campus and Medical College students in their third year or above with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0.

The Liang Zhaohan Medical College Student Scholarship is not open to sixth- or seventh-year students in the seven-year clinical medicine program. One place is reserved for a nursing student, and one Chen Houfu Scholarship place is reserved for an interior-design student. Students may not apply simultaneously for the Hakka-student, Chenghai-student, and outstanding upper-year student programs.

### Application

Eligible students should apply online through the STU scholarship website: <http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn/chinese/applyIntro2.aspx>. The deadline is 09:00 on November 27, 2012. Applications must include materials required by the system, including an academic transcript, a personal essay of no more than 1,200 Chinese characters, optional published work, and scans of awards received at university level or above.

The National Scholarship, National Encouragement Scholarship, Outstanding Student Scholarship, and enterprise- and individual-funded scholarships cannot be held concurrently. After submitting an online application, students must log in to the Student Affairs system at <http://sao.stu.edu.cn/> to view the application form and confirm successful submission. Enterprise scholarships established independently by schools and residential colleges will be assessed by those units under their own rules and reported to the Student Affairs Office.
