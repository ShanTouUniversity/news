The third Creative Public-Welfare InG project, supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, has extended its team-application deadline to 12:00 on November 21. The project began as a pilot at Shantou University in late 2010 and encourages students to design public-welfare products serving society, especially people in rural communities facing poverty. The first two rounds involved 37 multidisciplinary campus teams and produced 18 public-welfare designs, six of which recently received national patent authorization.

### Topics and team requirements

The third project will focus on the following eight directions and plans to recruit 10 campus teams:

1. Address drinking-water safety in remote rural areas;
2. Design an automatic seeding device for agricultural tools;
3. Design a rural household-waste treatment system;
4. Design more convenient travel solutions for older people in remote areas;
5. Improve school-meal arrangements for children in mountainous areas;
6. Design products that improve quality of life or study and work efficiency for people with visual impairments;
7. Address the problem of people with intellectual disabilities becoming lost;
8. Improve responses to earthquake disasters.

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Team size | 2–5 members |
| Disciplinary composition | At least two majors; teams including a product-design student are preferred |
| Project period | November 24, 2012–June 30, 2013 |

Teams may choose a topic based on their interests and expertise, conduct independent research and design, submit project plans and progress reports, and present and share their work as required. Some teams may attempt inter-university collaboration under arrangements by the project team.

### Support and schedule

The project team will provide training, technical guidance, dedicated studio space, project funding, and patent-application support. Outstanding teams may have opportunities to join a summer public-welfare design program, apply for follow-up promotion funding, and visit or share their work with other universities or overseas institutions. All participating teams will receive service certificates.

Applicants must submit the completed team application form by 12:00 on November 21. The project team will notify selected teams by November 21 to attend the November 24 training workshop. Detailed submission channels and application materials are subject to the original notice.
