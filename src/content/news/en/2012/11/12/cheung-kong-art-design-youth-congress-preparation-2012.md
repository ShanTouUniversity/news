With the approval of the STU Youth League Committee and the Party general branch of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, the school plans to hold its ninth Youth League Congress of Members’ Representatives in late November 2012 in the stairway lecture room of the school’s middle building. The congress will review the work of the eighth school Youth League committee, elect members of the ninth committee, and set future priorities.

### Student representatives

The number of student representatives will equal 15% of the Youth League membership in each branch. Representatives must be enrolled undergraduates of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design with a degree of representativeness and the ability to deliberate. Each branch should elect its representatives through a members’ representative meeting under the principle of democratic centralism, complete the election by November 20, fill in the Representative Registration Form, and submit it to the school Youth League Committee for qualification review.

### Committee-candidate recommendations

The school Youth League Committee is planned to have nine members. Candidates must meet the representative requirements and also demonstrate an innovative spirit, commitment to Youth League work, diligence, relevant Youth League experience, work ability, and social-activity skills sufficient to serve in a committee department.

Each Youth League branch and student organization will recommend one or two committee candidates. After review, consultation, and consideration by the school Youth League Committee and Party general branch, the candidates will be submitted to the congress for a competitive election. Branch-recommended candidates must complete the Candidate Registration Form and submit it to the school Youth League Committee by November 20.
