The STU Office of Academic Affairs has announced the examination arrangements for MAT1240 Probability and Mathematical Statistics (Engineering). Both class sections will take the examination in Lecture Hall 5 on Monday of Week 10.

| Class section | Instructor | Periods | Venue | Students |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 55214 | Yu Chengjie | A–B | Lecture Hall 5 | 139 |
| 55215 | Yu Chengjie | 3–4 | Lecture Hall 5 | 134 |

The examination covers the first three chapters of the textbook. It is closed-book, but calculators are permitted. Students must follow the instructor’s seating instructions and leave an empty seat between students. Students repeating the course and students who have applied for permission to attend without regular class attendance must also take the examination on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Academic Affairs)
