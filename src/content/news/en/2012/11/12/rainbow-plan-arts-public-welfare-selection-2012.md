The Guangdong Zhongyi Cultural Development Foundation will organize the Rainbow Plan Top Ten Public-Welfare Arts and Culture Selection under the guidance of the Guangdong Provincial Youth League Committee, Provincial Department of Culture, Provincial Federation of Social Sciences, and other organizations. The initiative is intended to encourage youth cultural creativity and innovation and support participation in cultural development through public-welfare activities.

Eligible organizations and individuals may apply through the Rainbow Plan official website, [www.mygy.com.cn](http://www.mygy.com.cn), by December 15.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | Rainbow Plan Top Ten Public-Welfare Arts and Culture Selection |
| Applicants | Eligible organizations and individuals |
| Application deadline | By December 15 |
| Application website | [www.mygy.com.cn](http://www.mygy.com.cn) |
| Organizer | Guangdong Zhongyi Cultural Development Foundation |
| Guiding organizations | Guangdong Provincial Youth League Committee, Provincial Department of Culture, Provincial Federation of Social Sciences, and others |

Specific eligibility requirements, application materials, and selection rules are subject to the official website and the original OA attachment.
