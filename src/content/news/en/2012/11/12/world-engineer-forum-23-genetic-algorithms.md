It is reported that the 23rd World Engineer Forum of the College of Engineering will be held on Wednesday, November 14, from 15:00 to 17:00 at the Science Lecture Hall, featuring a keynote by Prof. Erik D. Goodman.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Forum | 23rd World Engineer Forum, College of Engineering |
| Topic | Evolution in Action: An Overview of Single- and Multi-Objective Genetic Algorithms |
| Speaker | Prof. Erik D. Goodman |
| Time | November 14 (Wednesday), 15:00–17:00 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |

The talk will provide an overview of the evolution and applications of single- and multi-objective genetic algorithms. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
