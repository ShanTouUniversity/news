The STU Central Laboratory (Analytical Testing Center) has announced applications for dedicated funds under the Guangzhou Scientific Instrument Cooperation and Sharing Network. Shantou University is a member of the network. Eligible projects may apply to use network instruments for testing services and receive testing fees reduced to 70% or 60% of the standard rate.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Network | Guangzhou Scientific Instrument Cooperation and Sharing Network ([www.gzsin.cn](http://www.gzsin.cn)) |
| Applicants | STU research groups |
| Eligible projects | National, Guangdong, Chinese Academy of Sciences, or Guangzhou science and technology programs, including natural science foundation projects; key laboratories at or above the provincial level; provincial public laboratories; and joint technical research projects between network members and enterprises |
| Testing discount | 70% or 60% of the standard fee |
| Applications | Accepted on an ongoing basis by the network office |
| Processing time | Within five working days after a successful formal review |
| Office telephone | 82902740 |
| Office email | `yjlin@stu.edu.cn` |

Research groups should submit applications to the Office of the Network Management Committee, referred to as the network office. After a successful formal review, the office will issue a Dedicated Fund Use Card within five working days or return a stamped Preferential Testing Registration Form to the applicant unit. Detailed application instructions and procedures are provided in the original OA attachment.
