STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has issued Bulletin No. 212 on classroom-conduct checks. During Week 8 of the 2012–2013 fall semester, the attendance inspection team conducted two checks in the E Teaching Building, recording students who arrived late for class.

| Check time | Location | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late | Total late arrivals |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 14:00, November 5 | E Teaching Building | 4 | 3 | 7 |
| 14:00, November 8 | E Teaching Building | 4 | 2 | 6 |

The bulletin asks the relevant colleges and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to students who did not observe university rules and to continue strengthening academic conduct. To protect student privacy, the student list in the original attachment is not reproduced here.
