The STU Student Affairs Office has issued Student Discipline Bulletin No. 6 for 2012. According to the bulletin, on the evening of August 15, a Law student from the Class of 2009, identified only as Wu, assaulted a student from the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design without apparent cause on a campus road, causing facial injuries.

After receiving guidance from Law School faculty members, the student acknowledged the misconduct. Following review, the university imposed a demerit under Article 11 of the Trial Regulations on Student Disciplinary Actions of Shantou University.

The university urged students to learn from the incident, help maintain normal teaching and living order, and support the development of a positive campus and academic culture. To protect student privacy, the full name of the disciplined student and the identity of the injured student are not published here.
