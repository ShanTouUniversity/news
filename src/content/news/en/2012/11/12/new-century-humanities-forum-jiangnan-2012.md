The 113th New Century Humanities Forum at Shantou University will be held on November 14 under the theme “How Was Jiangnan Formed in Chinese History?” Professor Hu Xiaoming, a doctoral supervisor in the Chinese Department at East China Normal University, will discuss the historical construction and cultural meanings of Jiangnan.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | 113th New Century Humanities Forum |
| Topic | How Was Jiangnan Formed in Chinese History? |
| Speaker | Professor Hu Xiaoming |
| Time | 15:00–17:00 on November 14 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 3, Academic Exchange Center |

Hu is president of the Chinese Society for Ancient Chinese Literary Theory and deputy director and a research fellow at the Institute of Modern Chinese Thought and Culture, a key research base for humanities and social sciences under the Ministry of Education. The lecture will consider Jiangnan not only as a geographic region but also as a poetic, cultural, and psychological space, examining how the concept developed historically.
