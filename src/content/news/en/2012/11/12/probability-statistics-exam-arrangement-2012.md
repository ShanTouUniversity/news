The STU Office of Academic Affairs has announced the examination schedule for MAT2280 Probability and Mathematical Statistics (Economics and Biology). The examination will be held on Monday of Week 10, with both class sections assigned to Lecture Hall 2.

| Class section | Instructor | Periods | Venue | Students |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 55468 | Chen Yanming | 1–2 | Lecture Hall 2 | 149 |
| 55469 | Luo Jun | 3–4 | Lecture Hall 2 | 131 |

The examination is closed-book, but calculators are permitted. Students repeating the course and students who have applied for permission to attend without regular class attendance must still take the examination on time.
