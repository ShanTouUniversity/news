The Guangdong Provincial Youth League Committee is organizing the “Voice of Dreams” Guangdong Student Host Competition as part of the seventh Guangdong University Campus Culture and Arts Festival. The competition is open to university students and aims to identify student hosts. The STU Youth League Committee is inviting applications and will recommend participants based on the campus response.

Students who wish to participate should complete the Competitor Registration Form specified in the competition notice and email it to the STU Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` by November 14. The committee will make recommendations after reviewing the applications received.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | “Voice of Dreams” Guangdong Student Host Competition |
| Festival | Seventh Guangdong University Campus Culture and Arts Festival |
| Eligible participants | STU students |
| Registration material | Competitor Registration Form |
| Campus deadline | By November 14 |
| Submission email | `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` |
| Recommendation | The STU Youth League Committee will recommend candidates based on applications |

The event is intended to use campus culture to support student development, engage young people with advanced culture, and create a positive campus atmosphere.
