The first East Guangdong Wind-Band Arts Showcase of 2012 will be held at Shantou University on November 18. The STU Arts Education Center has invited wind ensembles from across eastern Guangdong to perform for students and faculty members.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Performance | 19:30 on Sunday, November 18 |
| Venue | STU Auditorium |
| Participating ensembles | 9 representative teams |
| Ticket distribution | November 13, 09:30–12:00 and 14:30–17:00 |
| Collection venue | Room 309, Arts Education Center |
| Distribution | First come, first served, while supplies last |

Participating ensembles include the Shantou Philharmonic Wind Ensemble, Shantou Customs Wind Ensemble, the Symphony Wind Ensemble of the Music Department at Hanshan Normal University, and the Jiaying University Music College Student Wind Ensemble, among others. Renowned conductors Yu Hai and Lin Yousheng, clarinetist Xi Weilong, flutist Yan Qi, and Hong Kong arts-management consultant Zheng Xinwen will serve as judges and offer on-site commentary.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Arts Education Center)
