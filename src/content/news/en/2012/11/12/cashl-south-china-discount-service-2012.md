It is reported that, according to the CASHL Management Center, a discounted service campaign will run from November 12 to 25 for users in South China (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan) and Northeast China (Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang). During the campaign, users in these regions can obtain CASHL document delivery services free of charge or at half price. The Library released a notice on November 12 detailing the discounts and application procedures.

## Campaign Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Period | November 12–25 |
| Regions | South China (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan) and Northeast China (Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang) |
| Eligible users | CASHL users in the two regions |

## Discounts

| Service | Subsidy |
| --- | --- |
| CASHL journal articles | 100% subsidized |
| CASHL large special collections | 50% subsidized |
| CASHL books (partial chapter delivery) | 50% subsidized |

Notes: Interlibrary loan agency services and book loans are not covered. Users should not submit book loan, copying or non-electronic delivery requests in the CASHL system; for special collections and books, please contact the coordinator by email.

## How to Use

### New Users

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Register | Visit http://www.cashl.edu.cn and click “Register” under “Personalized Services” at the bottom left |
| 2. Verify | Email registration information to yxu@stu.edu.cn for account verification, including username, name, school, grade and campus card number |
| 3. Request | Log in with the verified username and password, search for journals or articles, and submit a request |

### Existing Users

Log in with the existing username and password. If the account or password is forgotten or expired, email the coordinator to reactivate it.

## Collections

CASHL currently holds more than 11,700 journal titles, over 700,000 print books, 340,000 e-books and 58 large special collections, serving as a major source of foreign-language humanities and social sciences literature in China. For details and a user guide, see http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=189.
