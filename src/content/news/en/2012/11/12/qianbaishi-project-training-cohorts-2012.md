Shantou University has issued a formal document announcing its participants in three cohorts of Guangdong’s university “Qianbaishi Project”: the fifth-batch advanced individual, the sixth-batch continuing-training cohort, and the seventh-batch training cohort. According to the relevant notice from the Guangdong education authorities, 48 STU faculty and staff members were selected: one received the fifth-batch advanced-individual designation, 19 joined the sixth-batch continuing-training cohort, and 28 joined the seventh-batch training cohort.

| Item | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Fifth-batch advanced individual | 1 |
| Sixth-batch continuing-training cohort | 19 |
| Seventh-batch training cohort | 28 |
| Total | 48 |

### Fifth-batch advanced individual

Li Yuanyou received the designation of advanced individual among the fifth-batch Qianbaishi Project participants.
