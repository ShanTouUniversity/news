The opening ceremony and mock trial of the eighth Shantou University Law Culture Festival will take place in the Science Lecture Hall on the evening of November 17. The program will use a Chinese skit and an English-language trial with Chinese and English subtitles to present courtroom proceedings and legal advocacy. All STU students and faculty members are welcome.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Opening ceremony and mock trial of the eighth Law Culture Festival |
| Time | 19:30 on Saturday, November 17 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Formats | Chinese skit and English-language trial with Chinese and English subtitles |
| Organizer | School of Law |

The mock trial is based on the Casey Anthony case in the United States. The case involved the disappearance of Anthony’s daughter, Caylee, and subsequent judicial proceedings, which concluded with a verdict on July 5, 2011. Through the courtroom performance, the event will invite audiences to consider the relationship between evidence, judicial procedure, and verdicts.
