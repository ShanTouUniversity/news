The Shantou University Graduate School has issued its decision recognizing the 2012 Outstanding Graduate Students. Under the STU measures for selecting outstanding graduate students, the university conducted the review among students enrolled during the 2011–2012 academic year. After initial recommendations by graduate-training units and review by a panel of university experts, 50 graduate students were recognized.

| Award | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 11 |
| Second prize | 39 |
| Total | 50 |

The Graduate School organized the expert panel to review the candidates recommended by the graduate-training units. The university encouraged the recognized students to continue pursuing rigorous study and innovation and urged all graduate students to learn from their peers and strengthen their academic work.

The award list appears in the original OA attachment. To protect student privacy, individual student names are not reproduced here.
