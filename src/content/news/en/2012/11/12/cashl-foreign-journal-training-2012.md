The Shantou University Library will hold a session in its Academic Subject Services series on November 13 titled “How to Use CASHL to Obtain Foreign Humanities and Social Sciences Journal Articles for Free.” Xu Yong from the Library’s Subject Services Department will introduce the resources and services of the China Academic Social Sciences and Humanities Library (CASHL), known in Chinese as “Kaishi Lanwen.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | How to Use CASHL to Obtain Foreign Humanities and Social Sciences Journal Articles for Free |
| Speaker | Xu Yong |
| Time | 19:30–20:30 on Tuesday, November 13 |
| Venue | Subject Services Department, inside the consultation desk area on the second floor of the Library |
| Participants | STU students and faculty members |

The session will cover CASHL resources and services, the registration process, and practical usage methods. CASHL is a major component of the Ministry of Education’s initiative to promote philosophy and social sciences in higher education and provides nationwide collection and services for humanities and social-science literature.
