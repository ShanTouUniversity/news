It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University will convene a special meeting on the university's 2013 master's degree admissions work at 4:00 pm on Thursday, November 22, 2012, in Room 205 of the Science Building.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Work related to 2013 master's admissions |
| Time | November 22, 2012 (Thursday), 4:00 pm |
| Venue | Room 205, Science Building |
| Attendees | Leaders of each school (department/institute) in charge of graduate affairs; faculty coordinators of master's degree programs |

## Attendance Requirements

All units are required to notify their attendees in time. Given the importance of the meeting, anyone unable to attend must inform the Graduate School in advance and arrange for another relevant faculty member to attend on their behalf. Contact number: 82902424.
