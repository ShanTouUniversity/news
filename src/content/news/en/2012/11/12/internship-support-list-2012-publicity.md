It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has released the 2012 Internship Support List for public comment, soliciting feedback from all colleges (and residential colleges) on the main campus.

The list was compiled based on student applications and recommendations from each college (residential college) and is now open for review. Any unit or individual with objections may submit written comments to the Financial Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office, Room 106, Administration Building, before 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, November 14. Submissions from units should bear an official seal, while individual submissions must include the person's real name.
