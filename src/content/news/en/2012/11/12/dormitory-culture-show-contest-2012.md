The STU Student Affairs Office and Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee have opened registration for the fifth “My Dorm, My Show” dormitory-culture competition, under the theme “One Shared Goal, Let My Home Shine!” The competition is open to enrolled undergraduates and aims to enrich campus life, strengthen interaction among and between dormitories, and showcase student-community culture.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | “One Shared Goal, Let My Home Shine!” |
| Eligible participants | Enrolled undergraduates |
| Competition period | Through December 7, 2012 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Registration | November 12–16 |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office and Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee |

Entries must be submitted by teams of two to eight people. A team of fewer than four members may include residents of no more than three dormitory rooms. Formats are open and may include skits, talent performances, short plays, magic, cross-talk, dance, or choral singing. Content should be healthy and positive.

### Registration and awards

Students may register on site during the publicity event or complete a paper form and place it in the designated collection bags at the dormitory building committees’ publicity boards or at the Golden Triangle. The electronic form can be downloaded through the OA system; the original notice provides the applicable submission instructions.

| Award | Quantity and prize |
| --- | --- |
| Most Outstanding Performance | 1, up to RMB 650 |
| Most Popular Performance | 1, up to RMB 600 |
| Talent Performance Award | Multiple, up to RMB 500 |

Teams with performances lasting five to ten minutes may receive a complete commemorative competition disc. Updates are available through Sina Weibo at “@汕大宿委会”.
