It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on November 14, 2012, featuring Dr. Gu Lin of the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Toward Sub-Angstrom — 60 Years of High-Resolution TEM and Contemporary Aberration-Corrected Microscopy and Spectroscopy in Materials Science |
| Speaker | Dr. Gu Lin, Institute of Physics, CAS |
| Time | 16:10, November 14, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |

The talk will review the 80-year history of transmission electron microscopy, trace its 60-year path to sub-angstrom resolution, and discuss aberration-corrected microscopy and spectroscopy as a comprehensive platform for materials science, condensed matter physics and chemistry, including future impacts on research.

## Speaker

Born in 1979, Dr. Gu received his bachelor’s degree from Tsinghua University in 2002 and his doctorate from Arizona State University in 2005. He worked at the Max Planck Institute for Metals in Stuttgart and Tohoku University in Japan, contributing to energy-filter development and atomic-scale lithium imaging, and has been a Hundred Talents Program researcher at the Institute of Physics since late 2010. He has published over 90 SCI papers and received several international young scientist awards.
