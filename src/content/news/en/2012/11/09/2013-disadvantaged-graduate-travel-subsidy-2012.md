The STU Student Affairs Office has announced a travel subsidy for financially disadvantaged members of the Class of 2013. Main-campus undergraduate students who travel to Guangzhou between November and December to take civil-service examinations or attend job fairs organized by the Guangdong Graduate Employment Guidance Center may apply for reimbursement of their round-trip transportation costs.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | Financially disadvantaged main-campus undergraduates in the Class of 2013 |
| Eligible purposes | Civil-service examinations or Guangdong Graduate Employment Guidance Center job fairs in Guangzhou |
| Period | November–December 2012 |
| Required proof | Official round-trip tickets for the eligible period |
| Maximum reimbursement | RMB 160 for the round trip |
| Application materials deadline | December 7, 2012, inclusive |
| Tickets and claim form deadline | By December 31, 2012 |
| Receiving office | Graduate Employment Guidance Center |

Students who need support should complete the application form and submit it to their college or residential-college counselor. After review by the college or residential college, the application forms and subsidy list will be submitted collectively to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center. Following the university’s public notice of the subsidy results, each college or residential college must submit the transportation tickets and subsidy claim form by December 31.

To protect student privacy, applicant names, financial circumstances, and subsidy lists are not published here.
