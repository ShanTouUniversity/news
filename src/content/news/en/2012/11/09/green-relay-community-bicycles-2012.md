Shantou University has launched its Green Relay bicycle-circulation plan for students and faculty members. Sixty community bicycles are currently available for free use within the campus, and student volunteers will regularly organize and maintain the bicycles.

| Bicycle location | Location details |
| --- | --- |
| E Teaching Building | Across from the moot court |
| Area below the Engineering Building | Near the DG Teaching Building |
| Administration Center | Temporary bicycle parking area |
| Second Cafeteria | Area below the building |
| Library | Bicycle shelter |

The bicycles are managed on an open basis: they are not locked and are free to use, but may only be used on campus. Users should return bicycles to any designated location after use and take care of the shared equipment.

The plan will continue and will accept bicycle donations on an ongoing basis to expand the fleet. Faculty members and students interested in donating bicycles or discussing cooperation may contact the counselor’s office for the student residential area in Room B106 of the student residences at `82903355` or `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn`.
