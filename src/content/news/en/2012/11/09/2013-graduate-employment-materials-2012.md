The Shantou University Graduate School has issued an update on employment services for the Class of 2013. Because Guangdong’s employment agreements for university graduates are printed centrally by the provincial Graduate Employment Guidance Center, the agreements for postgraduate students had not yet been issued to universities as of November 5. STU will continue to follow up on their collection and distribution.

The employment recommendation forms for 2013 graduating postgraduates were collected from the university’s Employment Guidance Center on November 5. Class monitors may collect the forms from the Graduate School in Room 401 of the New Administration Center. After completing all required information and obtaining the supervisor’s comments, class monitors should collect the forms and submit them collectively to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center in Room 106 of the New Administration Center by November 30 for stamping.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible group | 2013 graduating postgraduates at the main campus |
| Recommendation-form collection | Graduate School, Room 401, New Administration Center |
| Form-submission office | Graduate Employment Guidance Center, Room 106, New Administration Center |
| Submission deadline | By November 30 |
| Employment-agreement status | Postgraduate agreements had not been issued as of November 5 |

Postgraduates who already have employment intentions and need to submit an employment agreement should explain the issuance delay to their prospective employers. Employers seeking verification may contact the Graduate School at `82904000`, or contact faculty members Wang Fengqi at `86504000` and Lin Dongyue at `86502425`. Students who need supporting documentation may request it from the Graduate School, which can also explain the situation directly to an employer if contact details are provided.
