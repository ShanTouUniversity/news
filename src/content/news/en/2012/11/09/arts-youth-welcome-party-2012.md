The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Shantou University College of Liberal Arts will hold an “Artistic Youth” themed welcome party on the evening of November 10. Organized jointly by the two schools’ Youth League committees and student unions, the event will take place in the STU Auditorium and is open to all students and faculty members.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | “Artistic Youth” |
| Time | 19:30 on Saturday, November 10 |
| Venue | STU Auditorium |
| Organizers | Youth League committees and student unions of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the College of Liberal Arts |

Audience members should follow the directions of on-site staff and take their seats 15 minutes before the event begins.

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Liberal Arts)
