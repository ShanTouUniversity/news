Shantou University will hold its second student-community masquerade ball on the evening of November 11 under the theme “Masquerade Shadows, My Heart Forever.” The event is open to all STU students and faculty members and will take place in the open area between Dormitory Building C to encourage community interaction and enrich students’ extracurricular life.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | “Masquerade Shadows, My Heart Forever” |
| Time | 19:00–21:00 on November 11 |
| Venue | Open area between Dormitory Building C |
| Participants | All STU students and faculty members |
| Attendance | Limited to 200 people |
| Organizers | STU Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office |

The program includes nine sections: “Fruits in Abundance,” “Rose Rendezvous,” “First Taste of Wine,” “Fairy Tales,” “Time Machine,” “A Feast for the Eyes,” “Games,” “Free Dance,” and “I Want to Confess.” Participants should arrive 10 minutes early, avoid wearing slippers, and wear long trousers if male. They should keep their belongings secure. Participants may also collect a pair of event logos from the promotional flyers and exchange them for a mask before the ball.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office)
