STU’s Office of Resource Management has announced the verification process for office telephone bills. Electronic statements for calls made by each unit from October 1 through October 31, 2012, have been imported into the Telephone Management System. Authorized reviewers may log in to the [Telephone Management System](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn), review the itemized charges, and confirm the statements.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bills covered | Office telephone charges from October 1–31, 2012 |
| System | [Telephone Management System](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) |
| Discrepancy deadline | By November 16 |
| Feedback email | `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` |
| Charge date | November 26 |
| Charging basis | Funding account numbers provided by each unit |

Units with no discrepancies should click the confirmation option in the system. If any item does not match actual telephone use, the unit should email `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` by November 16 so the relevant office can consolidate the feedback for China Telecom’s Shantou branch. After the verification period, statements with no objection and no confirmation action will be treated by the system as confirmed. Units may also use the same address to report system problems or offer suggestions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
