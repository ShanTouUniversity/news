It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will hold a recruitment drive for STU Creative Arts Ambassadors on November 11, 2012.

Founded by students of the Creative Industries Planning and Management program of the Department of Art and Design and advised by the school’s administrative director and faculty, the volunteer team focuses on art and creative communication. It initiates or supports creative and art events and performances to help students understand large-scale event organization, foster teamwork and build a sustainable event planning team. The team also organizes performance viewings, exhibitions, group sharing and lectures to energize the campus creative and artistic atmosphere.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | First- and second-year undergraduates and postgraduates from all schools; non-art students are especially welcome; no prior experience required, passion and perseverance are key |
| Time | 10:00–12:00 and 14:00–16:00, November 11, 2012 |
| Venue | Interactive Space, Front Building, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design (next to the first-floor staircase) |
| Method | On-site form filling |
| Interview | Assessment of on-site performance |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

The ambassadors have previously participated in a theater lecture by director Tang Shu-wing, a modern dance workshop with the Mark Morris Dance Group, and a post-performance exchange for the a cappella theater production *Rock Hard* by Hong Kong’s Yat Po Singers.
