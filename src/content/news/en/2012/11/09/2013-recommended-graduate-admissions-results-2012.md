The Shantou University Graduate School has reported the proposed admission results for students recommended to pursue master’s degrees without sitting the national entrance examination in 2013. The Ministry of Education allocated STU 36 recommendation quotas, including 21 academic-track places and 15 professional-degree places. With the work of the professional schools, Office of Academic Affairs, Graduate School, and the recommended students, receiving institutions proposed 37 students for admission, including one student whose admission does not use a recommendation quota.

| Item | Details |
| --- | ---: |
| Government recommendation quotas | 36 |
| Academic-track quotas | 21 |
| Professional-degree quotas | 15 |
| Students proposed for admission | 37 |
| Admissions outside the recommendation quota | 1 |

The reported destinations include Xiamen University (1 student), Huazhong University of Science and Technology (1), Hunan University (1), Jinan University (10), and South China University of Technology (12). In addition, one Biotechnology student was proposed for direct doctoral admission at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, while one Mathematics and Applied Mathematics student was proposed for admission to the University of Macau outside the recommendation quota.

The detailed proposed-admission table appears in the original OA attachment. To protect student privacy, individual names and row-by-row details are not reproduced here.
