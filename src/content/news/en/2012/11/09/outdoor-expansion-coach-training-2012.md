With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the STU Student Affairs Office will join the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association in organizing the university’s first outdoor-instruction course. The course will be taught by Li Wei of the Shenzhen Mountaineering Association and will provide classroom and field training for students and faculty members with outdoor experience, preparing participants to share outdoor skills and help organize related activities on campus.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Host | Student Affairs Office |
| Organizer | STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association |
| Instructor | Li Wei, Shenzhen Mountaineering Association |
| Eligible applicants | First- to third-year undergraduates and in-service faculty members |
| Application deadline | Before 17:00 on November 16, 2012 |

Applicants should have a strong interest in outdoor activities and substantial outdoor-sports experience, along with good physical and psychological fitness, teamwork, and a sense of responsibility. Applicants with relevant training experience will receive priority when other qualifications are equal. The course will consist of three full weekend sessions:

| Dates | Content |
| --- | --- |
| November 24–25 | Classroom instruction |
| December 15–16 | Field practice and instruction |
| January 19–20, 2013 | Field practice and instruction |

The course instructor will select participants by reviewing application materials against outdoor-instructor requirements and may arrange interviews depending on the number of applications. During their time at STU, participants must serve as “outdoor guides” and assist with the university’s advanced outdoor programs and Snow Leopard Outdoor Association activities at least once each semester. Updates are available through Sina Weibo at “@汕头大学雪豹户外协会”.
