The third Shantou University Chemistry Department Alumni Scholarship ceremony and a faculty-student forum will take place on November 11. Organized jointly by the College of Science and the STU Alumni Association Office, the events will bring Chemistry Department alumni back to campus for the ceremony and discussions with faculty members and students.

The Chemistry Department Alumni Fund is STU’s first department-level alumni fund. It provides awards and support for full-time Chemistry Department undergraduates who demonstrate strong academic and personal performance and face financial hardship. The fund aims to encourage outstanding students to apply to the department and support current students in their studies and personal development.

| Event | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Third Chemistry Department Alumni Scholarship ceremony | 09:30–11:30 | Library Lecture Hall |
| Chemistry faculty-student forum | 14:30–16:30 | ACC Meeting Room 5 |

Both events will be held on November 11, and faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
