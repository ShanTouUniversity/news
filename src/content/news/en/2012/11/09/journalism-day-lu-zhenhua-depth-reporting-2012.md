The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will hold a Journalism Day lecture on November 12 titled “How Is an Excellent In-depth Report Produced?” Lu Zhenhua, a senior editor in the international section of *21st Century Business Herald*, will discuss practical approaches to in-depth reporting, international news, and feature writing.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | How Is an Excellent In-depth Report Produced? |
| Time | 10:00–12:00 on November 12 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Speaker | Lu Zhenhua |
| Position | Senior editor in the international section of *21st Century Business Herald*, based in Beijing |

Lu has worked in investigative reporting, international news, and feature writing. He received the inaugural Guardian China Environmental Journalism Award in 2010 and holds a master’s degree in international journalism from Beijing Broadcasting Institute.
