The Shantou University Library has opened a trial of the Shushu database, a specialized search service for statistical and survey data provided by China Infobank International Co., Ltd. The trial will remain available through February 9, 2013.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Database | Shushu |
| Trial end date | February 9, 2013 |
| Access | [Library database details page](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=358) |
| Yearbooks | More than 5,604 |
| Statistical tables | 1.16 million |
| Time span | 1949 to the present |
| Geographic coverage | All 31 provincial-level regions in China, 254 prefecture-level cities and county-level areas, plus selected data from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao, and international regions |
| Industry coverage | 54 broad industry groups |

Faculty members and students may send trial comments to the Library’s Subject Services Department at `3247` or `yxu@stu.edu.cn`.
