Tickets for the series of Guangdong graduate employment fairs for the Class of 2013 have been distributed to the colleges at Shantou University. Graduating students at the main campus may collect tickets from their college counselors, while graduate students should obtain tickets through the Graduate School.

All tickets are free and may not be resold. Because only a limited number of tickets were distributed in advance, students who did not receive one may obtain a temporary ticket free of charge at the designated venue on the day of the employment fair by presenting a student card and employment recommendation form. Temporary tickets will be issued while supplies last.

| --- | --- |
