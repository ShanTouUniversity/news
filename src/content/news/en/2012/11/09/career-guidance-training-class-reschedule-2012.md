The STU Office of Academic Affairs has adjusted the schedule for class section 55186 of Career Guidance and Training because Student Affairs Office instructors Zhuo Yanchun and Zhang Yan will be away. The class originally scheduled in Lecture Hall 2 during Periods 3–4 on Thursday of Week 9, November 15, will be canceled and made up in Lecture Hall 2 during Periods 3–4 on Thursday of Week 13, December 13.

| Item | Original schedule | Make-up schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course | Career Guidance and Training | Career Guidance and Training |
| Class section | 55186 | 55186 |
| Time | Periods 3–4, November 15 (canceled) | Periods 3–4, December 13 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall 2 | Lecture Hall 2 |
| Reason | Instructors away | Make-up class |

Students enrolled in the course should inform one another and attend according to the revised schedule.
