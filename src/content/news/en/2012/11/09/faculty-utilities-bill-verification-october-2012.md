STU’s Office of Resource Management has announced the verification process for faculty members’ October water and electricity bills. The latest meter readings were taken on November 1 and 2, with the previous readings taken on October 1. Relevant faculty members may log in to the [STU Utility Management System](http://power.stu.edu.cn) to review their bills.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | October |
| Latest meter readings | November 1–2 |
| Previous meter reading | October 1 |
| System | [Utility Management System](http://power.stu.edu.cn) |
| Question period | November 9–14 |
| Verification office | Utility Center |
| Telephone | 2489 |
| Payment method | Deduction from November 2012 salary accounts; those without salary accounts should pay at the Finance Office |

Faculty members with questions or objections should visit the Utility Center during the stated period. If no objection is raised by the deadline, the Finance Office will process the charges as arranged. Personal login credentials and individual bill details are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
