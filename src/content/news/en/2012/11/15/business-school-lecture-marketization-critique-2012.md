The ninth 2012 academic lecture of the Shantou University Business School will be held on November 16 under the title “A Critique of Pan-Marketization: A Rational Reflection on China’s Economic-System Reform.” Professor Tian Guang will deliver the lecture.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture | Business School Academic Lecture No. 9, 2012 |
| Topic | A Critique of Pan-Marketization: A Rational Reflection on China’s Economic-System Reform |
| Speaker | Professor Tian Guang |
| Time | 19:00–21:00 on Friday, November 16 |
| Venue | Business School Academic Seminar Room, second floor |
| Participants | Business School graduate and undergraduate students and the wider STU community |

From historical and developmental perspectives, the lecture will review China’s contemporary economic-system reform and opening-up, discuss the changes brought by reform and the lessons that require careful reflection, and analyze the pan-marketization academic trend and its possible negative effects. It will also explore approaches to comprehensive economic and social development in China.
