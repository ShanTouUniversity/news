It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs will hold a "My View on Campus Culture" series from November 17 to 24, aiming to gather student perspectives on the current state of campus cultural activities and their expectations for personal development.

The series consists of three parts:

| Segment | Content | Time | Venue |
|---------|---------|------|-------|
| Campus Culture: Your Voice | Culture wall and information desk where students can freely share views on campus activities, with experienced student peers offering advice | Nov. 17–20, 11:30–13:30 | Jin Sanjiao |
| Campus Culture: In Conversation | One-on-one interviews to explore suggestions and needs regarding campus culture | Nov. 17–18 | — |
| My View on Campus Culture Forum | Forum bringing together 40 students from different years and majors with peers experienced in campus cultural activities to discuss the present and future of campus culture | Nov. 24, 15:00–17:30 | Conference Room 205, Science Report Hall |

Invited student representatives include peers from majors such as international trade, applied physics and journalism who have been active in campus cultural activities or have received academic scholarships, including a participant in the STU–University of Nottingham Ningbo China leadership exchange program.

Registration for the forum is available in two ways: download the application form (see attachment) and email it before 17:00 on November 20 with the subject line formatted as "Year/Major + Name + My View on Campus Culture Forum Registration" to the designated mailbox (see attachment for details), or register on site at Jin Sanjiao from 11:30 to 13:30 on November 17–18. Places are limited to 40 and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis if oversubscribed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Student Affairs)
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
