The Shantou University Personnel Office has begun planning applications for the 2013 High-Level Foreign Experts Program and the Education, Science, Culture, and Health Foreign-Talent Introduction Program. Relevant units should prepare materials under the requirements of the Guangdong Administration of Foreign Experts Affairs and focus projects on the university’s key disciplines, priority development areas, and major talent-training plans.

### Program conditions

Applicants for the High-Level Foreign Experts Program should be scholars holding positions equivalent to professor at prominent overseas universities or research institutions, or technical and management professionals holding senior positions at internationally recognized companies or financial institutions. Each applicant must work in Guangdong for a cumulative period of at least two months in 2013.

The Education, Science, Culture, and Health Foreign-Talent Introduction Program focuses on overseas high-level talent around key disciplines, priority development fields, and major talent-training plans. STU may submit no more than two applications.

### Materials and deadlines

| Program item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Submission deadline | November 26, 2012 |
| Paper materials | Five copies, submitted to Room 315, New Administration Building, Personnel Office |
| Electronic materials | One copy, sent to `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn` |
| High-Level Foreign Experts Program | Candidate summary form, application, evaluation-presentation PPT, and supporting materials |
| Education, Science, Culture, and Health program | Project application form and summary form |

The High-Level Foreign Experts Program application and supporting materials must be bound separately. Copies of certificates and supporting documents must be signed by the reviewer and stamped with the official seal. Proposed employment contracts require university approval before signing, and units should submit a separate draft of the employing-unit opinion in the application. Electronic files must match the paper submissions.

Applicants should read the instructions carefully, ensure that all information is truthful, and avoid changing the form format. Proposed projects must involve foreign experts whom the unit has already confirmed for introduction in 2013. Failure to implement an approved project, or insufficient time in China affecting implementation, may lead the provincial authority to suspend acceptance of the unit’s applications the following year.

For questions, contact Li Zhiwei at 86502364 or `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn`.
