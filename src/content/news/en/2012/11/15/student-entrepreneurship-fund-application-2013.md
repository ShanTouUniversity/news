To advance innovation and entrepreneurship education and develop students’ entrepreneurial awareness and practical abilities, Shantou University has opened applications for its 2013 Student Entrepreneurship Fund under the university’s regulations on entrepreneurship awards and aid. The fund supports student entrepreneurship teams and projects.

### Eligible applicants and conditions

Eligible applicants generally include full-time undergraduates in their third year or above, full-time undergraduates who graduated within the previous six months, and full-time senior graduate students. A proposed project must meet at least one of the following conditions:

- An entrepreneurship plan that has won an award in a university-level or higher competition;
- A plan based on a patent obtained by an individual applicant and recommended by at least two professors;
- A project that received an excellent evaluation in the STU Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program and was recommended by its adviser and at least two professors.

Applicants must comply with the law and university rules, act honestly, demonstrate good character, and be able to complete credits according to the credit-based academic schedule. Applicants and all team members must receive review and recommendation from their school or residential college; graduate students must obtain their advisers’ consent, and applicants must also obtain parental consent. Teams should have some initial funding and the ability to bear operating risks, agree to comply with university entrepreneurship rules, and accept the administration of the Student Entrepreneurship Park Management Committee.

### Fund uses and application process

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Fund uses | Student Entrepreneurship Park rent, initial startup funding, and operating-performance awards |
| Project application amount | 30,000–150,000 yuan |
| Step 1 | Submit the STU Student Entrepreneurship Project Proposal, STU Entrepreneurship Fund Application and Approval Form, and supporting materials to the Student Entrepreneurship Center |
| Step 2 | The Student Entrepreneurship Center reviews the proposal, approval form, and supporting materials |
| Step 3 | The materials go to the Entrepreneurship Fund Support Application Review Committee |
| Step 4 | The Student Entrepreneurship Park Management Committee approves the result and notifies applicants |

Supporting materials may include academic records or other academic proof, a graduate adviser’s consent, and parental consent. After approval, project leaders must carry out the project according to plan. Leaders initially advance expenses, and funding and review are handled under the university’s applicable regulations.

### Submission arrangements

Applicants must submit both an electronic application and sealed paper copies. Five paper copies should be delivered to the Student Entrepreneurship Center in Room 106 of the New Administration Center. Electronic materials should be submitted through the method specified in the original OA notice. The deadline is December 15, 2012. File names and email subject lines must follow the original notice’s instructions.
