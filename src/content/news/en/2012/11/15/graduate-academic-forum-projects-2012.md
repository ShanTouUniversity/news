To strengthen the graduate academic environment and improve graduate students’ overall qualities, the Shantou University Graduate School has invited proposals for graduate academic forums from training units and graduate students. Eligible applicants include training units, major classes, and project groups.

### Proposal requirements

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Academic papers | Collect at least 10 strong academic papers and select 3 for presentation |
| Forum size | At least 40 participants |
| Guidance and commentary | Two faculty advisers from relevant fields should attend as commentators |
| Proposal materials | Application form covering significance, date, format, implementation plan, budget, and other details |
| Submission deadline | Before the end of the workday on November 26 |

### Project administration

After approval, project leaders should promptly prepare a work plan and complete the forum within the planned period. The Graduate School will provide a certain amount of activity funding with reference to the proposed budget. Project leaders must initially advance expenses and may seek reimbursement within the approved funding amount after presenting valid invoices.

After the forum is completed, project leaders should submit a summary report, photographs, and other materials for review by the Graduate School Student Affairs Office. Funding may be reclaimed from projects that are not held or whose implementation is deemed ineffective.

Interested units and graduate students should complete the application form and send it to the Graduate School Student Affairs Office at `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn` by the deadline.
