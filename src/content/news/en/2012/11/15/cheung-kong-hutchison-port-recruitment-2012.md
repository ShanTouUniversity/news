The second 2012 Cheung Kong Group dedicated recruitment event at Shantou University will take place from November 21 to 23, with Hutchison Whampoa Ports Group participating. The Student Affairs Office has published recruitment arrangements and position information and asked graduating students to prepare and attend on time.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment event | Second 2012 Cheung Kong Group dedicated recruitment event at STU |
| Participating company | Hutchison Whampoa Ports Group |
| Dates | November 21–23 |
| Venue | New Library for the company briefing, exhibition, and comprehensive tests |
| Participants | STU graduates |
| Dress | Graduates attending activities in the New Library should wear formal attire |

The detailed recruitment schedule, positions, and requirements are subject to the original OA attachments issued by the Student Affairs Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the recruitment schedule and position list.
