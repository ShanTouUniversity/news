The Shantou University Business School, Student Affairs Office, and Alumni Association Office have opened the 2012 selection for the Business School Alumni Scholarship. The scholarship was initiated by Business School alumni and launched with a 200,000-yuan donation to support full-time Business School undergraduates who demonstrate academic and personal merit and face financial hardship.

The scholarship plans to recognize 40 students in 2012, with an award of 2,500 yuan for each recipient.

### Eligibility

Applicants must comply with university regulations, fulfill their student obligations, act honestly, and demonstrate strong character and overall qualities. They should show diligence and initiative in their studies, have a GPA of at least 3.0 for the selection year, and have no two or more failed examination or assessment courses. Applicants should also care about public affairs, take part in social practice, demonstrate a spirit of service and outstanding innovative awareness and ability, and come from financially disadvantaged families while maintaining a modest lifestyle.
