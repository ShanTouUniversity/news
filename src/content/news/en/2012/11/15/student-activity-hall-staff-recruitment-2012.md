The Student Activity Hall on the first floor of the Shantou University Library is recruiting undergraduate staff from across the university. The hall offers indoor activities including billiards, board games, fitness, climbing, and video games, can accommodate nearly 100 people at a time, and serves as a practical student-development platform through its operations and management.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Current undergraduates at the STU main campus |
| Availability | At least 8 hours of free time per week |
| Duties | Front-desk consultation, indoor equipment management, check-in and payment procedures, and other operational tasks |
| Hall hours | Monday–Friday 15:00–22:00; Saturday–Sunday 10:00–22:00 |
| Work arrangement | Training after selection; management team schedules about 8 hours per week during opening hours |
| Compensation | Paid under the university’s work-study standards |
| Application deadline | 19:00 on November 16 |

Applicants should truthfully complete the Student Activity Hall Staff Registration Form and submit it according to the method specified in the original OA notice. Applicants should also have strong verbal communication skills and demonstrate warmth, patience, responsibility, and a serious attitude.
