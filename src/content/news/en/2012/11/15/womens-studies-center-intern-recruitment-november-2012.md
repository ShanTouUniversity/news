The STU Center for Women’s Studies in the College of Liberal Arts is recruiting two interns for one Research Assistant position and one Project Assistant position. The opportunity is open to undergraduates and graduate students from the 2010–2012 cohorts. Allowances will follow the university’s work-study standards.

### Positions

| Position | Openings | Requirements | Main duties |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Research Assistant | 1 | Responsibility, a careful and proactive work attitude, proficiency with statistical-analysis software such as Excel and SPSS, and the ability to work independently | Assist with searching for and organizing materials; work in the center’s office at least 8 hours per week; complete other assigned tasks |
| Project Assistant | 1 | Responsibility, a careful and proactive work attitude, interest in women’s public-welfare work and student learning activities, and the ability to write event news releases independently | Assist with student activities; prepare event reports and communications; maintain a fixed duty schedule of at least 6 hours per week; complete other assigned tasks |

### Application

Applicants must send the electronic *Application Form for Interns at the STU Center for Women’s Studies* to `cws@stu.edu.cn` by 17:00 on November 20, 2012. The email subject should state “Application for Intern at the Center for Women’s Studies.” Shortlisted applicants will receive interview notices. They should bring original or electronic versions of relevant work samples, and selected applicants will be notified by telephone after the interviews.

The center’s telephone number is 82903020, and its website is <http://cws.stu.edu.cn>.
