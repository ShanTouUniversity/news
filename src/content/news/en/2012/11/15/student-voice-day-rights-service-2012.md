The STU Youth League Committee and Student Union will hold the fourth Voice Day student-rights service event, providing a channel for students to raise concerns, seek information, and offer feedback.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Fourth Voice Day rights-service event |
| Participants | All STU students |
| Time | 11:30–13:00 on Friday, November 16 |
| Venue | Golden Triangle |
| Organizers | STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Union |

A service booth will publish the Student Union’s rights-service complaint channels, issue-handling procedures, and processing time limits. It will accept student complaints and inquiries, present progress on rights-service work, and collect suggestions. The Student Union will also consolidate current concerns and help students communicate with relevant university departments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
