The Shantou University “Chua—Path to Success” network will hold “Dust Be Gone II—Kicking Out the Dust,” a weeklong computer repair and dust-removal service event. The event will provide repair and cleaning services for students and faculty members and promote computer maintenance knowledge. It will run from November 17 to 23 and is open to the STU community.

| Service group | Repair and service arrangement | Dates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Students | Bring computers to the Golden Triangle or Zhicheng College lobby for on-site registration | November 17–18 |
| Students | If a computer cannot be brought to the site, complete a home-service registration form; teams will schedule visits in registration order | November 17–18 |
| Faculty and staff | Make an appointment through the event duty channel; teams will provide service at the appointed time | November 19–23 during regular working hours |

Student repair sites are the Golden Triangle and Zhicheng College lobby. The faculty and staff service channel will operate on weekdays. Detailed duty channels and event updates are subject to information published by the Chua—Path to Success network.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
