The Shantou University Engineering College will hold an App Inventor mobile-programming competition with awards for students in different cohorts, graduate students, and course teaching assistants. The competition is designed to strengthen programming practice and innovation through mobile-application projects.

### Awards

| Award group | Eligible participants | Awards and places | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Freshman Programming Grand Prize | Registered STU undergraduates in the Class of 2012 | Special prize, 1 | 10-inch Samsung tablet and 16GB phone memory card |
| Freshman Programming Grand Prize | Registered STU undergraduates in the Class of 2012 | First prize, 2 | 7-inch Samsung tablet and 16GB phone memory card |
| Freshman Programming Grand Prize | Registered STU undergraduates in the Class of 2012 | Second prize, 7 | 7-inch Samsung tablet |
| Freshman Programming Grand Prize | Registered STU undergraduates in the Class of 2012 | Third prize, 40 | 16GB phone memory card |
| Professional Programming Grand Prize | Registered STU undergraduates and graduate students other than freshmen | First prize, 1 | 7-inch Samsung tablet and 16GB phone memory card |
| Professional Programming Grand Prize | Registered STU undergraduates and graduate students other than freshmen | Second prize, 2 | 7-inch Samsung tablet |
| Outstanding Contribution Award | Teaching assistants making important contributions to course services | 1 | 10-inch Samsung tablet and 16GB phone memory card |

The awards ceremony was tentatively scheduled for the evening of Friday, December 21, during Week 14. The winners would be announced and prizes distributed at the ceremony.

### Project submission

Projects must be submitted from 00:00 on December 15 through 23:59 on December 18. Each submission should include:

- A research report;
- Project source code;
- An executable APK file;
- A photographed signed copy of the originality statement.

Students in the *Introduction to Mobile Programming* course should use the course assignment-submission method. Students in the *Computer Applications Fundamentals* course should be recommended by their instructor, who would forward the submission. Other participants should use the method specified in the original OA notice. The signed original of the originality statement was due at the E102 computer room during working hours on December 17–18; detailed submission channels and forms are subject to the original OA notice.

Participants must declare that their award-winning projects are original. The mobile-programming teaching team would be authorized to cite award-winning software, source code, and design reports as case studies in classes and academic papers. Award-winning freshmen would also be expected, as needed, to participate in mobile-programming website management, service, and course-assistant work during the following two years. An evaluation panel would include external experts, course instructors, mobile-programming teaching assistants, and student representatives.
