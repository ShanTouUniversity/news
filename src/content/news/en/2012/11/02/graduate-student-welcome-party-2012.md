To welcome the 2012 graduate cohort, the Shantou University Graduate Student Union will hold a welcome party on the evening of November 2, 2012, bringing together new and continuing graduate students from the main campus. The event is intended to create a platform for exchange, help new students settle into their new academic and living environment, and identify and develop artistic talent by offering participants a stage to perform.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2012 Graduate Welcome Party |
| Time | 7:30–10:00 p.m., Friday, November 2, 2012 |
| Venue | STU Small Stage |
| Organizer | Shantou University Graduate Student Union |
| Participants | All graduate students on the main campus; the entire university community is welcome |
