It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University forwarded on November 2 a notice (Yue Jiao Ke Ban Han [2012] No. 29) from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on applications for the 2012 national Spark Program and other candidate projects, calling for interested faculty members across the university.

Under the arrangement, teachers intending to apply should contact the Research Office promptly, prepare materials according to the eligibility criteria of each project category, and deliver their application materials to the office by November 5. Details on project categories and requirements are available in the notice's attachments.
