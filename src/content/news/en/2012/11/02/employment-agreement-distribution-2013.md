It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on November 2, 2012, announcing that employment agreements for undergraduate graduates of the Class of 2013 have been distributed to the monitors of each graduating class.

Graduating students are asked to collect their agreements from their class monitors. Each agreement is barcoded and linked to the student’s ID and should be kept safely.
