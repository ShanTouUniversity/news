Shantou University’s Yangtze River Journalism and Communication School and School of Science will hold the “Xinli Hangkong—You Are in Our Hearts” welcome party at 7:30 p.m. on Friday, November 2, 2012, in the Grand Auditorium. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome to attend.

Audience members should arrive 15 minutes early and follow the on-site staff’s instructions for seating. Seats for university leaders and guests are arranged in Rows 6–8 of the auditorium.
