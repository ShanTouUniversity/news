Because of the opening of Jieyang Chaoshan Airport and special requirements from some airline agencies, Shantou University had planned to stop arranging its previous group air travel accident insurance after 2012. Following requests from several schools, the university is now offering supplemental 2012–2013 coverage.

Employees who expect to travel by air within one year from November 15, 2012, and do not need to purchase individual air travel accident insurance may choose to enroll. The insured amount is RMB 500,000 per person and the premium is RMB 40 per person. If the number of enrollees does not reach the group-insurance requirement of at least eight people by the deadline, this notice will be void.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Reference coverage period | One year from November 15, 2012 |
| Insured amount | RMB 500,000 per person |
| Premium | RMB 40 per person |
| Group requirement | At least eight enrollees |
| Submission deadline | Before November 11, 2012 |
| Submission method | Units complete the attached form and email it to sbai@stu.edu.cn |
| University inquiry contact | Bai Sheng, 2101 |

The insurance fee may be reimbursed only once during the year. During the coverage period, the university will not reimburse another premium for the same type of insurance; the individual or unit may determine the reimbursement channel. Other family members may purchase coverage voluntarily at their own expense. Units must complete the enrollment form collectively. For insured persons from outside mainland China, the date of birth must also be provided.
