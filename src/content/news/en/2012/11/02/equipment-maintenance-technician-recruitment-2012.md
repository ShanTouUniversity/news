For operational needs, Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is publicly recruiting one maintenance technician for its Equipment and Materials Management Office. The position will be hired as an auxiliary contract worker; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow relevant university standards.

### Qualifications and duties

Applicants should have at least a secondary vocational qualification, experience repairing electronic instruments and household appliances, and a work or qualification certificate in mechanical and electrical or electronic technology equipment. The position requires sound professional conduct, initiative, execution, teamwork and communication skills, computer literacy, and basic English reading comprehension and simple English communication. Applicants with ISO training are preferred. Applicants should be no older than 40, in good health and compliant with family-planning policy.

Duties include repairing teaching-laboratory, administrative-office, student-dormitory and employee-dormitory instruments and appliances; communicating with faculty members and students; maintaining and tracking the online repair-reporting system; following up on after-sales service and maintenance for university equipment; technically identifying equipment proposed for disposal and assessing faults to inform procurement planning; keeping repair records under ISO procedures; and completing other tasks assigned by the office.

### Application arrangements

Applicants must send a detailed résumé, copies of graduation, professional-technical qualification, identity and other relevant certificates, and a work proposal of no more than 1,000 Chinese characters to the STU Resource Management Office by email or mail before November 20, 2012. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of an interview and must bring original documents and certificates. Materials will be used only for this recruitment; originals will be returned after review, while other materials will not be returned. Applicants should keep their own backups.
