Shantou University’s schools and residential colleges have completed the initial review of National Grant applications. A small number of places remain, so the university has reopened the application system to broaden the grant’s coverage.

Students who wish to apply and meet the relevant requirements must submit an application through the scholarship website by noon on Monday, November 5, 2012: [STU National Grant Application System](http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn/chinese/applyIntro2.aspx).

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | National Grant |
| Applicants | Students who wish to apply and meet the relevant requirements |
| Deadline | Before noon, Monday, November 5, 2012 |
| Method | Online application through the STU scholarship website |
| Reason for reopening | A small number of places remained after initial review |

Eligibility and system instructions are governed by the scholarship website and the university’s relevant student-aid rules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
