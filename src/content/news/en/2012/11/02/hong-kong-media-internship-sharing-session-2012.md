During the 2012 summer vacation, two graduate students and 12 third-year undergraduates from Shantou University’s Yangtze River Journalism and Communication School completed two-month media internships in Hong Kong. The school will hold a Hong Kong media internship sharing session on November 9, 2012, where the 14 interns will discuss Hong Kong’s media environment, preparation for media work and their internship experiences.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2012 Hong Kong Media Internship Sharing Session |
| Speakers | 14 Hong Kong interns: two graduate students and 12 third-year undergraduates |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., November 9, 2012 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall |
| Media represented | Hong Kong China News Service, *Yazhou Zhoukan*, *Ta Kung Pao*, Sing Tao Newspapers, *Hong Kong Commercial Daily*, *Metro Daily*, Cable TV and Australia Asia Satellite Television |

The session will address how Hong Kong media operate, how students can prepare for media careers and how to make use of Hong Kong internship opportunities. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
