Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has announced the result of the review for the Student Activity Hall management team. Students responded actively after recruitment opened, and four teams took part in the defense review on October 31, 2012. After considering the teams’ overall performances, the judges selected Key as the next management team for its activity ideas, organizational ability and execution.

| --- | --- |
| Review | Student Activity Hall management-team selection |
| Defending teams | Four |
| Defense date | October 31, 2012 |
| Selected team | Key |
| Evaluation focus | Activity ideas, organization and execution |
