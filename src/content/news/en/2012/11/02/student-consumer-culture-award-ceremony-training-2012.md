The ceremony and award-winning-work presentation for the third STU Student Consumer-Culture Research Report Award under the Student Community X Plan will be held on November 3, 2012. To help the university community learn more about the award and exchange reflections after the ceremony, Wang Guancheng, director-general of the Education Department of the Hong Kong Consumer Council, and senior officer He Qinghui will lead an interactive discussion and civic consumer-responsibility training session on November 4.

| Event | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Award ceremony and presentation of winning works | 5:00–6:30 p.m., November 3 | Science Lecture Hall |
| Post-ceremony discussion and civic consumer-responsibility training | 2:00–5:00 p.m., November 4 | Science Lecture Hall Conference Room 205 |

Students who wish to attend the November 4 discussion and training should download and complete the registration form and email it to STUCCSA@163.com by 5:00 p.m. on November 3. The filename must identify the year, major and name as required by the original notice; student registration information is not reproduced here.
