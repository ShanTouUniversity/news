The STU Committee of the Communist Youth League and Student Union have opened registration for the community preliminary round of the Student Community X Plan’s 20th Campus Top Ten Singers competition. Students may enter through the identity of their dormitory area. Five finalists will emerge through the community preliminary round and a qualification round and will earn places in the preliminary round of the 20th Campus Top Ten Singers competition.

Solo, duet and group performances are accepted. Bands may not register as standalone competitors but may participate as accompanying performers. Students already recommended by a school or residential college may not enter the community preliminaries.

| Round | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community preliminary | 2:00 p.m., November 10, 2012 | Golden Triangle |
| Qualification round | The notice states November 17, 2011, at 7:30 p.m.; the year conflicts with the 2012 competition context | Small Stage |
| Registration deadline | Before 11:00 p.m., November 7, 2012 | Online or paper submission |

At the preliminary round, contestants will enter in time slots according to their numbers, give a brief self-introduction and perform a song of their choice. The two parts together must not exceed two minutes. Forms can be downloaded from the campus office-information network or Student Union website, or collected from dormitory bulletin boards. The form and an accompaniment audio file should be sent to top102012@163.com as required by the organizers; audio must be edited to two minutes or less.

Preliminary-round results will be announced on site after the competition and published through the Student Union website and the “STU Top Ten Singers” Weibo account. The year and schedule of the qualification round should be confirmed in a subsequent organizer notice. Dormitory drop-off points, student names, phone numbers and dormitory numbers are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
