Shantou University Library has opened a trial of the HeinOnline legal full-text database through November 30, 2012. Centered on legal literature and delivered in PDF full text, the database also covers politics, international relations and diplomacy.

The collection includes more than 2,000 legal journals from around the world, 675 volumes of authoritative international-law works, more than 100,000 cases, over 2,000 classic legal academic books, and full-text reports from the U.S. federal government, offering substantial academic reference value.

Faculty members and students can enter HeinOnline through the STU Library database portal, follow the sign-in prompts and select Law Journal Library or another relevant collection. To avoid publishing trial credentials, this article does not reproduce the username or password; access details should be obtained through the library’s original notice.
