It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the School of Science issued a notice on November 2, 2012, announcing the preliminary results for the 2012 Pan Yugang Scholarship and Senderi Chemical Scholarship.

From the release of the evaluation notice on October 24 to the deadline at 16:00 on October 29, the school received 51 applications for the Pan Yugang Scholarship and 39 for the Senderi Chemical Scholarship. After preliminary review under the respective regulations, 16 students, including a student surnamed Zhang from Mathematics Class of 2009, advanced to the second-round interview and defense.

## Interview Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | 10:00, November 6 (noted as 2011 in the original notice, presumed to be 2012) |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Requirements | Arrive 15 minutes early, prepare a 3-minute personal PPT and send it to the designated email; absence without leave is considered withdrawal |
| Objections | Raise by 11:30, November 5 at the Youth League Committee office |

The shortlist and email address are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here for privacy.
