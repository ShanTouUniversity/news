For operational needs, Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is publicly recruiting one key water and electrical facilities inspector and one water and electrical maintenance and duty worker. Both positions will be hired as auxiliary contract workers; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow relevant university rules.

### Positions and duties

The key facilities inspector should have sound professional conduct and teamwork skills, a junior-college qualification in mechanical and electrical engineering or building electrical engineering, and practical experience maintaining high- and low-voltage and water-supply and drainage equipment. Applicants should be no older than 35, in good health and compliant with family-planning policy. Duties include inspecting High-Voltage Switch Stations 1 and 2 and the water-pump rooms in the east and west areas of the main campus; analyzing high-voltage and variable-frequency pressurized-water-system faults; proposing maintenance measures; managing campus water and electrical facilities records and unit fixed assets; and preparing annual maintenance plans and related rules.

The maintenance and duty worker should have a secondary vocational or technical-school qualification in mechanical and electrical engineering or building electrical engineering and practical water and electrical maintenance experience. Applicants should be no older than 35, in good health and compliant with family-planning policy. Duties include staffing the high-voltage switch stations and pump rooms; performing minor repairs and inspections for water and electrical facilities across approximately 450,000 square meters of university buildings; inspecting and repairing 309 streetlights and outdoor sports-field lighting; and installing water and electrical facilities for major events and other temporary outdoor needs.

### Application arrangements

Applicants must send a sealed package by November 20, 2012, to the STU Resource Management Office Water and Electrical Center. The package should include a résumé and copies of an identity card, graduation certificate, professional training certificates, technical qualification certificates, family-planning certificate and other relevant credentials. Shortlisted applicants will be notified of interviews and must bring the original documents and certificates for review. Materials will be used only for this recruitment; originals will be returned after the interview review, while other materials will not be returned. Applicants should keep their own backups.
