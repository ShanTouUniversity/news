The 136th Engineering Research Forum at Shantou University’s School of Engineering will be held on November 4, 2012. Professor Gao Jiahong of Peking University’s Academy for Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies, and director of the Peking University Magnetic Resonance Imaging Research Center, will present “Neuronal Current MRI.”

Professor Gao is an expert recruited in the second cohort of the national Thousand Talents Program. Faculty members, graduate students and senior undergraduates in electronic engineering, biotechnology and physics, as well as other interested members of the university community, are welcome to attend.
