The Shantou University Business School and Engineering College are jointly organizing the university’s first e-commerce practice project, centered on operating plans and commercial practice for remote-control toys exported from Shantou. The project is designed to develop students’ innovation, teamwork, and social adaptability and to help them apply academic knowledge to market analysis and practical problems.

### Project and team requirements

Teams will study remote-control toy products such as cars and aircraft, analyze the spending levels and preferences of target consumers, and propose online marketing methods and operating strategies. Teams must have at least three members, with no restriction on year. Cross-school and cross-major collaboration is encouraged, and each team must include members from at least three different schools or residential colleges. Each team must appoint a captain and choose a team name.

### Schedule

| Stage | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | Through 23:00 on November 25 |
| E-Commerce Business Lecture | 14:00 on November 21 |
| Operating-plan submission | By 23:00 on December 2 |
| Team defense | 14:00 on December 5 |
| Finalist training | 19:30 on December 8 |
| Commercial practice | After training through May 2013 |

The top three teams whose plans pass the defense will enter the e-commerce commercial-practice stage. The first-ranked team will have priority to choose one of the following options: one operating Taobao shop and one operating Paipai shop, one operating Taobao shop and one operating Dangdang shop, or another promotion and sales approach. The practice period will last about six months, with the winning team determined by shop sales during the project. The online shops will provide products and logistics, while student teams will focus primarily on marketing, promotion, and customer service.

### Incentive plan

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Team sales reward | A commission equivalent to 10% of team sales |
| Above-target sales reward | A 40% commission on sales above the predetermined target |
| Internship recommendation | Strong performers may be recommended for e-commerce internships |
| Project completion | Teams that complete the project on schedule may receive formal internship certificates |

Interested participants should submit registration materials according to the organizers’ requirements. Detailed channels and formats are subject to the original OA notice. The organizers are the STU Business School and Engineering College; the implementing units are the Business Practice Center and ModernTop-Union.
