The STU Network and Information Center will perform scheduled routine maintenance on the campus wireless system. Wireless service in some areas will be interrupted during the maintenance window.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance window | 00:00–06:00 on November 18 |
| Affected areas | Wireless networks in areas including the Administration Center and ACC |
| Service restoration | No separate restoration notice will be issued |
| Inquiry numbers | 0754-82903413-100 and 86503401 (campus short number 883401) |
| Inquiry email | `stunic@stu.edu.cn` |

The Network and Information Center asked campus network users to inform one another and apologized for the inconvenience caused by the maintenance.
