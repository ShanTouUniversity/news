STU’s Student Affairs Office has announced a change to section 55187 of the Career Counseling and Training course. The class originally scheduled for periods 3 and 4 in room G404 on Friday, November 23, will be replaced by a company visit.

Students enrolled in the section should gather at the university motto monument at 9 a.m. on Sunday, November 25, and travel to Guangdong Alpha Animation and Culture Co., Ltd. The arrangement is subject to the Student Affairs Office notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
