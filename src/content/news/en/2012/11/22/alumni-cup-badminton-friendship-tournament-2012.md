STU’s first Student Community X Plan Alumni Cup Badminton Friendship Tournament will take place at the STU Gymnasium from November 24 to 25. The organizers have released a finalized electronic schedule booklet, and participating units, players, and related staff should follow it as the authoritative basis for the competition order.

The event is organized jointly by the Student Affairs Office, Alumni Association Office, and Physical Education Department. The original OA attachments contain the detailed schedule, participation arrangements, and related forms.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Alumni Association Office, and Physical Education Department)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the detailed schedule, participant list, and competition arrangements.
