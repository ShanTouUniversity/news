In accordance with arrangements from the Guangdong education authorities, STU’s Student Affairs Office is soliciting papers from its colleges and residential colleges for the 2012 National College Counselor Outstanding Paper Competition. The university will organize an internal expert review and recommend selected submissions to the provincial education authority.

### Eligibility and requirements

Submissions may be prepared individually or collectively by full-time counselors and must not have been previously published. Papers should be guided by Marxist theory and the theoretical framework of socialism with Chinese characteristics, address ideological and political education for university students, and demonstrate theoretical value, practical relevance, and originality. They must be well documented and rigorously structured; plagiarism and falsification are prohibited.

Each author may submit only one paper as first author. Collaborative work must be submitted under the names of the collaborators, while work by four or more collaborators must be submitted in the name of a research group. Internal submissions, including the paper and application form, should be sent through the method specified in the original OA notice by November 27.

STU will recommend selected papers to the Guangdong education authority. Outstanding submissions may be published in *College Counselor*, subject to the organizer’s copyright and usage rules.
