STU’s Academic Affairs Office has announced the textbook development and publication subsidy projects for the second half of 2012. Under the university’s textbook development and publication subsidy measures, two textbooks were approved for the program after applications from textbook authors or chief editors, college recommendations, and an expert review organized by the Academic Affairs Office.

Details of the approved projects are provided in the original Academic Affairs Office OA attachment.
