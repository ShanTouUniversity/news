STU’s Sports Committee has announced a change to the schedule of the 26th Student Track and Field Meet. The competition has been postponed from December 8–9 to December 15–16.

Each college and residential college must reorganize its registration and submit a new electronic registration form by 4 p.m. on Friday, November 23. A printed copy bearing the unit’s official seal must also be delivered to the Physical Education Department office by the same deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted, and registration entries cannot be changed or supplemented after submission.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the registration form and designated submission channel.
