STU has issued a decision to establish a committee for reviewing student-management regulations. The decision follows Guangdong’s administrative-approval reform, which removed the filing requirement for higher-education student-management regulations, and was approved at the 14th university leadership meeting on November 20, 2012.

### Composition and responsibilities

The committee will be chaired by the university leader in charge of the relevant area. Its members will include the heads of the Academic Affairs Office, Graduate School, Student Affairs Office, Office of Supervision and Audit, Office of Research Affairs, Finance Office, Resource Management Office, and Youth League Committee.

The committee will establish and improve the university’s student-management rules and review procedures. It will assess whether regulations comply with legal requirements and whether the drafting process solicits views from relevant parties, especially those whose interests are affected, thereby promoting more scientific and standardized institutional development.
