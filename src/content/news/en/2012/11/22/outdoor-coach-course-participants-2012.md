STU’s Student Affairs Office has announced the participant arrangements for its first outdoor-expedition coach training course. The program received 84 applications, including 19 from faculty and staff and 65 from students. After reviewing the application information, the course coaches selected 24 participants for the formal training: 10 faculty and staff members and 14 students.

To give more members of the university community access to outdoor activities, the program also invited another 16 faculty members and students to join its first outdoor practice, including seven faculty and staff members and nine students. The practice is tentatively scheduled for December 8–9 at a venue to be confirmed; further details will be announced separately.

Formal participants should attend all activities according to the training schedule. The participant list and schedule are included in the original OA attachments.
