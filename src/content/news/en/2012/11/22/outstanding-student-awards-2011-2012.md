STU has issued its decision on outstanding-student honors for the 2011–2012 academic year. The awards recognize students’ achievements in study, student work, technological innovation, social service, sports, and campus cultural activities. The evaluation was conducted under the university’s undergraduate scholarship measures.

### Award categories and numbers

| Award category | Number of recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Student Scholarship | 20 |
| First-Class Academic Excellence Scholarship | 177 |
| Second-Class Academic Excellence Scholarship | 422 |
| Third-Class Academic Excellence Scholarship | 626 |
| Outstanding Contribution to Academic and Scientific Activities | 10 |
| Outstanding Leadership Ability | 10 |
| Outstanding Contribution to Social Service | 10 |
| Outstanding Contribution to Sports and Arts | 10 |
| Academic and Scientific Activities Participation | 47 |
| Campus Cultural Activities Participation | 150 |
| Social Service Participation | 140 |
| Campus Service Participation | 144 |

Under the relevant rules, students who received the National Scholarship will not also receive the Outstanding Student Scholarship. The complete recipient list is included in the original university OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.
