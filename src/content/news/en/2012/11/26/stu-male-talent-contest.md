It is reported that Shantou University's Student Affairs Office will organize the Student Community X Plan — "Mr. STU Makeover" contest, aiming to enhance the overall quality of male STU students, help them discover their potential, strengthen exchanges among dormitory residents, and provide male students a stage to showcase their talents.

## Schedule

| Time | Stage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 26 – 29 | Registration | Participants needing video-shooting assistance should submit forms early |
| Nov 29 – 30 | Preliminary round | Video screening or direct interview |
| Dec 2, 14:00 – 17:00 | Cocktail-making training | Guided by professional bartenders |
| Dec 3 – 7 | Talent learning | On-campus clubs introduced for talent study |
| Dec 8, 19:30 – 22:00 | Final showcase | Cocktail making and other talent performances |

The event is open to all students: men compete while women can also take part by serving as public judges (men may apply as judges too), joining the cheering squad, or assisting with performances.

## Awards

| Award | Quota | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mr. STU Award | 1 | RMB 300 + certificate |
| Best Look Award | 2 | RMB 200 + certificate |
| Best Taste Award | 2 | RMB 100 + certificate |
| Cool Play Star | 3 | A gift |

## Registration

Registration closes on November 29. Participants may fill in the registration form and send it to the designated email address, or collect a form from the paper bag next to the promotional poster and hand it to any of the designated staff members. In addition to self-registration, the event encourages nominations: a "Hero Board" will be set up at the Golden Triangle where nominators can leave nominees' information, and staff will contact the nominees afterwards.
