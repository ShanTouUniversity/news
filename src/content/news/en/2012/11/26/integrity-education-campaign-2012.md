STU’s Student Affairs Office has announced an “Uphold the Guangdong Spirit and Practice Integrity” campaign running from December 2012 through January 2013. The program aims to strengthen students’ awareness of integrity, law, and social responsibility and foster a campus culture that values and practices honesty.

### Four activities

1. **“Rigorous Study and Integrity in Life” activities**: Each college or residential college will organize class or Youth League branch activities addressing integrity in academic research, technological innovation, qualification documents, student loans, and job seeking. Activity plans are due by December 10; activities will run from mid-December to mid-January, followed by summaries in late January.
2. **“What Do You Think About Integrity?” microblog essay contest**: Open to all students from November 26 through December 15, the contest asks participants to post microblog entries of no more than 140 characters. It will offer one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes, and five excellence awards, with prizes of RMB 300, 200, 100, and 50 respectively, plus certificates. Awards may remain vacant depending on the quality of submissions.
3. **Themed cartoon-design competition**: Open to individual students or teams from November 26 through December 15, the competition seeks original cartoons of any format or length with a clear theme, creative concept, meaningful message, and engaging presentation. It will offer one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes, and five excellence awards, with prizes of RMB 300, 200, 100, and 50 respectively.
4. **“Student Loans and Credit Help Others” education**: This activity targets students with national or home-place student loans and will promote financial-aid policies, personal credit reporting, and basic financial knowledge to support the sustainable development of student-loan and aid programs.

Cartoon submissions may be sent as specified in the notice to the Education Management Section at Room 106, New Administration Center. The office can be reached at 86504749 or `xtchen@stu.edu.cn`. Please follow the original Student Affairs Office OA notice for the official microblog account, hashtag format, and submission requirements.
