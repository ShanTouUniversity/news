The 13th Future Partner Cup China Intelligent Robot Contest was held at Nanjing University of Technology from November 16 to 18. STU’s Mathematics team won the championship in the international-format humanoid all-around event and qualified for the international humanoid all-around competition scheduled to take place in Seoul, South Korea, the following year.

The competition attracted more than 270 teams and over 1,000 students from 23 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities across China. STU’s six-member Mathematics team, coached by Gu Minqiang, competed in the humanoid all-around, robot firefighting, and Robot Olympic Tour events. In addition to the all-around championship, it won two second prizes, or silver awards, in the other two events.

The championship was one of eight university-division titles awarded at the competition and the first won by a mathematics student team in the event’s 13-year history. The Mathematics program has developed an intelligent robotics laboratory, offered robotics courses, and provided a competition training platform to strengthen practical skills and interdisciplinary learning.
