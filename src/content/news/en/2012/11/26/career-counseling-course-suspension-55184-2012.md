STU’s Student Affairs Office has announced that, because of a course adjustment, the class for Career Counseling and Training section 55184 originally scheduled for periods 8 and 9 in Building D, Room 206, on Thursday, November 29, has been suspended. A make-up time will be announced separately.

Students enrolled in the section should follow subsequent teaching announcements.
