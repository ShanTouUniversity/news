STU’s Science School will host a research lecture by Professor Douglas R. Tocher of the Institute of Aquaculture, School of Natural Sciences, University of Stirling. His presentation is titled “Recent Advances in n-3 LC-PUFA Biosynthesis in Fish Species.”

The lecture will introduce the desaturation and elongation processes involved in long-chain polyunsaturated fatty-acid biosynthesis in vertebrates, with attention to the metabolic pathways for producing arachidonic acid, eicosapentaenoic acid, and docosahexaenoic acid in fish. As declining fisheries increase aquaculture’s share of the human food supply, reliance on fish oil from reduction fisheries raises sustainability concerns. The presentation will examine the nutritional challenges of replacing fish oil with plant oils and how research into fatty-acid biosynthesis can help maintain the nutritional quality of farmed fish.

Professor Tocher’s research focuses on the biochemical, molecular, and genetic regulation of lipid and fatty-acid metabolism and nutrition in fish. His current interests include cell culture and post-genomic technologies applied to fish lipid metabolism and nutrition.

The lecture will take place from 10 a.m. to noon on Wednesday, November 28, in Science and Technology Center 504. Interested faculty members and students are welcome.
