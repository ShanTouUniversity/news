The STU Library has launched a featured-literature series, beginning with a focus on Chaoshan vernacular residences. The featured material introduces the large number of traditional buildings and villages preserved in the Chaoshan region, examining their inheritance of Chinese architectural traditions and their adaptation to local culture, climate, and building practices.

The literature describes a spatial system combining indoor and outdoor areas through halls, courtyards, corridors, and passageways. It also highlights the region’s refined craftsmanship, decorative traditions, and exchanges with outside architectural cultures, arguing that Chaoshan residences embody the area’s historical heritage and have historical, artistic, and scientific value.
