In response to complaints about the occupation of limited reading seats during examination periods, the STU Library will clear belongings left in the reading hall and book-reading areas. Readers are asked to remove their belongings before December 3 to prevent them from being collected.

Starting December 3, items left behind will be gathered by the library and placed on shelves inside the photocopy rooms on each floor. Readers may go there to reclaim them. The library is asking readers to help maintain a fair and orderly reading environment.
