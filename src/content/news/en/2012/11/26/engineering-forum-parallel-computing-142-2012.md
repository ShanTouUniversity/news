STU’s School of Engineering will host the 142nd Engineering Research Forum on “New Parallel Computing.” Professor and doctoral supervisor Wu Zhijian will present research on parallel-computing technologies and related topics.

The forum will begin at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, November 28, in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room. Faculty members and students from the school, as well as other interested members of the university community, are welcome.
