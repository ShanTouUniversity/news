STU’s 26th Student Track and Field Meet has completed formal registration, and the organizing committee has announced its pre-meet arrangements. Ten teams and 287 athletes have registered, including 156 men and 131 women.

### Schedule

| Stage | Time and venue | Main arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team leaders’ and coaches’ coordination meeting | 4 p.m. on December 5, Wednesday, Multimedia Classroom in the Gymnasium | Discuss competition matters and collect bibs; each bib requires a RMB 5 deposit |
| Opening-ceremony rehearsal | 4 p.m. on December 12, Wednesday, Track and Field | Each college or residential college sends 36–60 people to assemble and rehearse in six columns |
| First competition session | 3 p.m. on December 15 | Competition begins |
| Second competition session | Opening ceremony at 8:30 a.m. and competition at 9:30 a.m. on December 16 | Competition continues |
| Third competition session | 2 p.m. on December 16 | Competition continues |

Registration is closed, and units may not change or add athletes or events. Questions about event groupings or typographical errors in names should be submitted by team leaders through the method designated by the organizing committee before 4 p.m. on November 28. Each unit may select four registered athletes for the relay and must submit the running order at the coordination meeting. Units should also submit an opening-ceremony introduction of no more than 120 Chinese characters by December 5.

The detailed competition schedule is subject to the original OA attachment. The Physical Education Department office can be reached at 82903727.
