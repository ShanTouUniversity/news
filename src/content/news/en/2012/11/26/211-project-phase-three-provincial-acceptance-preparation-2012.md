The provincial acceptance review of Guangdong’s Phase III 211 Project development is approaching. The provincial authorities have scheduled an acceptance review of STU’s related development from December 10 to 12. STU’s 211 Project Office will hold a preparation meeting at 4 p.m. on November 29 in Meeting Room 201 of the New Administration Center.

### Meeting agenda

The meeting will convey the message of the provincial acceptance mobilization meeting and interpret the relevant acceptance notice. It will review the draft STU acceptance-work plan and the project teams’ plans for improving on-site conditions, and hear reports from the Finance Office and Equipment Office on project data consolidation and changes. University leaders will also deliver a mobilization address.

Participants will include leaders in charge from the Schools of Engineering, Science, and Medicine; project leaders and secretaries; and representatives responsible for relevant work from the General Office, Resource Management Office, Finance Office, Office of Supervision and Audit, Equipment Office, Infrastructure Office, and Academic Communication Center.
