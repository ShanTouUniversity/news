STU’s Student Affairs Office will hold its seventh counselor meeting of 2012 and include training in holistic life and spiritual guidance. Professor Guo Shaotang of Hong Kong will lead the training, offering the student-affairs team perspectives on life education and psychological support.

The meeting will begin at 3 p.m. on Wednesday, November 28, in Meeting Room 330 of the Administration Center. All counselors and relevant staff in the student-affairs system should make the necessary arrangements and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
