In accordance with relevant requirements, STU will arrange annual medical examinations at the university’s First Affiliated Hospital for serving and retired department-level officials. Retired officials will attend on the morning of December 4, while serving officials will attend on the morning of December 5.

The examinations will take place at the hospital’s Health Management Center, on the third floor of the Science and Technology Building at 52 Dongxia South Road, opposite the outpatient building and next to Lantian Pharmacy. Participants should arrive at the center at 7:30 a.m. on their scheduled date. The General Office and Office for Retired Staff will issue separate transportation and boarding arrangements.
