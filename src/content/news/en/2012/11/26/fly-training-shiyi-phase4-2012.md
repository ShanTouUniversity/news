It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the university Student Union will launch the fourth Shiyi (intermediate) phase of the FLY training program, with sessions scheduled for December 1, 2 and 8, 2012.

Launched in 2011, the FLY program aims to develop leadership among young students through three stages — Fengyi (beginner), Shiyi (intermediate) and Zhanyi (advanced) — helping participants grow in knowledge, skills and experience and become socially responsible future leaders. Six training sessions have been held to date.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee of Shantou University |
| Co-organizer | Student Union of Shantou University |
| Dates | December 1, 2 and 8, 2012 (3 days) |
| Main content | Off-campus practice “Discover Every Self,” project planning defense “You Choose the Topic,” innovative thinking workshop, project management lecture, human resource management workshop, time management workshop, among others |
| Target participants | Ministers and vice ministers of university- and college-level student organizations; presidents and vice presidents of student associations |
| Places | 30 (lottery if oversubscribed) |

## Registration

Applicants should download and complete the application form for the 2012 FLY Shiyi phase, rename the file as “Grade + Major + Name” and send it to the designated email address. The deadline is 24:00 on November 29, 2012. The form and email address are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The application form and submission email are available on the OA system.
