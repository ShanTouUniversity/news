The Center for Women’s Studies in STU’s College of Liberal Arts will host the fifth lecture in its 2012 CWS Public Lecture Series on “Trends in International Charitable Organizations.” Starting from recent public-trust crises involving charitable organizations in China and the United States, the lecture will examine how charities understand and implement accountability and monitoring, as well as how public relations, new media, and fragmented organizational work affect responsibility mechanisms in the nonprofit sector.

The speaker, Dr. Rollin A. Van Broekhoven, has served as a federal judge in Washington, D.C., and as chair of the International Committee on Fundraising Organizations. His research interests include fundraising, monitoring, and accountability in international charitable organizations, and he has worked in the charitable sector for many years.

The English-language lecture will begin at 7:30 p.m. on Thursday, November 29, in the Library Lecture Hall. It is organized by the Center for Women’s Studies and co-organized by the Public Administration Department of the Law School.
