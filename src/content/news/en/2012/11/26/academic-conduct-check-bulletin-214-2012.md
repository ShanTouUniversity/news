STU’s Three-Style Development Office has released bulletin 214 on academic-conduct inspections. During week 10 of the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year, the inspection team checked teaching buildings and recorded tardiness during morning classes in Building E.

| Date and time | Location | Tardiness recorded |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 8 a.m., Wednesday, November 21 | Building E | 11 students late by no more than five minutes |
| 8 a.m., Thursday, November 22 | Building E | 9 students late by more than five minutes |

The bulletin asks the relevant colleges and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to the students involved and continue strengthening compliance with university rules and academic-conduct development. The original OA attachment contains the individual list; student names are not reproduced here.
