With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, the STU union will host a birthday gathering for faculty and staff members born in November, December, and January 2013. The event will take place at 6:30 p.m. on Saturday, December 8, in the western restaurant of the Junhua Hotel. Faculty, staff, and their family members may attend with tickets.

### Reservation and ticket schedule

| Item | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Reservations | Noon on November 26 to noon on December 3 |
| Payment deduction or payment | December 3–5 |
| Ticket collection | 10 a.m.–noon on December 7 |
| Reservation system | <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/> |

Participants should enter the reservation system under their birth month. Tickets cannot be refunded after registration and payment. The university will not arrange transportation, so attendees should travel independently and observe traffic safety. Children must be accompanied by at least one parent.
