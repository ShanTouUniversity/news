STU’s Department of Social Sciences will host an Ai Zhi Workshop lecture by Professor Xie Xialing of Fudan University’s School of Social Development and Public Policy. Under the theme “Philosophy and Wisdom,” the lecture will examine what philosophy is, the nature of philosophical thinking, and the relationship between philosophy and wisdom, inviting the university community to reflect on philosophy’s contribution to wisdom in life.

Xie is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Fudan University’s School of Social Development and Public Policy and director of Fudan’s Research Center for Social Development. His teaching and research focus on Chinese and Western philosophy and sociological theory.

The lecture will begin at 7:30 p.m. on Monday, November 26, in the Science Lecture Hall. All faculty members and students are welcome.
