STU’s Infrastructure Office under the Resource Management Office has publicized provisional cost additions arising during the renovation of the public stairwell glass roof. The project began on September 11, and additional repairs identified during construction are estimated to add about RMB 18,000. The provisional additions remain below the 10% ceiling set by the university.

The additional work mainly covers repairs to damaged roof insulation tiles, rust removal and painting for roof ventilation louvers, repainting areas where the roof ventilation layer’s wall coating has peeled, and repairs to damaged exterior glass mosaics.

Comments on the provisional project cost may be submitted in writing to the Office of Supervision and Audit or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on November 28.
