It is reported that the General Education Forum and the CCS Whole Person Education Open Lecture will be held on the evening of November 8, 2012.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Life and Intellectual Pursuit: Meandering between this World and Eternity |
| Speaker | Prof. Zhuo Xinping, Director of the Institute of World Religions, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences; President, Chinese Association for Religious Studies |
| Time | 19:30, November 8, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Library |
| Organizer | Academic Affairs Office, College of Liberal Arts |
| English title | Life and Intellectual Pursuit: Meandering between this World and Eternity |

Born in March 1955 in Cili, Hunan, Prof. Zhuo received his master’s degree in philosophy from the Graduate School of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences in 1981 and his doctorate in philosophy from the University of Munich in Germany in 1987. He served as a member of the European Academy of Sciences and Arts and Vice President of the International Council for Philosophy and Humanistic Studies, among other posts, and is currently a Member of the Academic Division, CASS and Member of its Presidium. He has authored 25 monographs and published about 200 academic papers. A detailed profile is available in the OA notice.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
