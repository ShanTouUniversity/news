The Shantou University Party leadership study group will hold a study meeting at the New Administration Center on the afternoon of November 7. The program includes two sessions focusing on regulations concerning integrity in office for Party officials and the history of the Communist Party of China in the new period.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00 on Wednesday, November 7, 2012 |
| Venue | New Administration Center, Room 213 |
| Topic 1 | Guidance on implementing the Code of Integrity in Government for Party members and leading officials |
| Speaker | Sun Fenqiang, Deputy Secretary of the University Commission for Discipline Inspection |
| Topic 2 | Welcoming the 18th National Party Congress and reviewing Party history in the new period |
| Speaker | Bai Youjin, Director of the Publicity Department of the Party Committee |

The meeting is intended for members of the university Party committee, secretaries of Party general branches and directly affiliated branches, and responsible officials from the Commission for Discipline Inspection, General Office, Organization and United Front Department, Publicity Department, News Center, Student Affairs Office, Department of Social Sciences, University Labor Union, and Youth League Committee.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department of the Party Committee)
