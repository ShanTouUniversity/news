The 111th New Century Humanities Forum at Shantou University will be held on the afternoon of November 9. Ouyang Yu, a scholar, writer, translator, and editor-in-chief of the literary magazine *Hometown*, will give a lecture titled “Mo Yan, the Nobel Prize in Literature, and More” in the New Library Lecture Hall.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | 111th New Century Humanities Forum |
| Time | 15:00–17:00 on November 9, 2012 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall |
| Speaker | Ouyang Yu |
| Topic | Mo Yan, the Nobel Prize in Literature, and More |

Born in Huangzhou, Hubei, Ouyang earned a master’s degree in English and Australian literature from East China Normal University and taught at Wuhan University before moving to Australia in 1991. He is a member of the Australian Society of Authors, holds a doctorate in Australian literature from La Trobe University in Melbourne, and writes, translates, and publishes poetry in both Chinese and English. He also serves as editor-in-chief of *Hometown* literary magazine. By 2012, he had published 65 books and translations in Chinese and English, with work appearing in numerous Chinese- and English-language journals in countries including the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and Ireland.

According to the notice, Ouyang has received several creative-fund awards and twice been shortlisted for the New South Wales Premier’s Literary Awards. His English poetry was selected for *The Best Australian Poems* seven consecutive times from 2004, and his translations of contemporary Chinese poems were included in the 2012 edition.

The lecture will use Mo Yan’s 2012 Nobel Prize in Literature as a starting point to discuss the possibility of contemporary Chinese literature reaching wider international audiences, as well as international perspectives and debates surrounding literary prizes.
