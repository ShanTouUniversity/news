The STU selection contest for the fourth Anji Cup National College Student Logistics Design Competition will hold team defenses on the morning of November 10. Participating teams are asked to arrive at D204 in the Teaching Building by 09:00 for the defense.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | STU selection contest for the fourth Anji Cup National College Student Logistics Design Competition |
| Stage | Team defenses |
| Time | 09:00 on Saturday, November 10 |
| Venue | Teaching Building D204 |
| Organizer | STU Business School Case Competition Committee |

Participating teams may contact the teachers through the official university email addresses `xzpu@stu.edu.cn` and `qzeng@stu.edu.cn` with questions.
