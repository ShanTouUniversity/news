The 112th New Century Humanities Forum at Shantou University will be held on the evening of November 9. Professor Luo Yimin of Southwest University will give a lecture titled “Literature and Life” in Lecture Room E203. The event is open to faculty members and students across the university.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forum | 112th New Century Humanities Forum |
| Time | 19:30–21:30 on November 9, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Room E203 |
| Speaker | Professor Luo Yimin, Southwest University |
| Topic | Literature and Life |

Luo holds a doctorate from Peking University and has been a Fulbright research scholar at Columbia University. He is a professor and doctoral supervisor in philosophy and literature at Southwest University and deputy director of the university’s Research Center for Foreign Linguistics. He also serves as president of the Chongqing Shakespeare Research Association, vice president of the Chongqing Translators Association, and a council member of the Association for Sino-American Poetry and Poetics.

According to the notice, Luo has published 77 papers in 30 journals in China, the United States, and South Korea, and has published 20 books and other works. His research and publications cover Shakespeare, English literature, American poetry, and poetics. He has led projects funded by the National Social Science Fund and the Ministry of Education’s research-startup program for returned scholars, and has undertaken academic visits and research in the United States, Australia, South Korea, Taiwan, and the Czech Republic.
