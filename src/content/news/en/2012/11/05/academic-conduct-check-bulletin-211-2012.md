Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office has issued Academic Conduct Bulletin No. 211, reporting inspections conducted during Week 7 of the 2012–2013 fall semester. The inspection team visited the E Teaching Building on October 30 and November 1, recording 15 instances of classroom tardiness in total.

| Inspection time | Location | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| October 30, 14:00 | E Teaching Building | 5 | 3 |
| November 1, 08:00 | E Teaching Building | 4 | 3 |
| Total |  | 9 | 6 |

The bulletin states that the names of the students identified during the inspections were included in the attachment to the original OA notice. Colleges and residential colleges are asked to provide educational guidance and continue their efforts to improve academic conduct. To protect student privacy, the names and other personal information are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Three-Style Development Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachment for the list of students concerned.
