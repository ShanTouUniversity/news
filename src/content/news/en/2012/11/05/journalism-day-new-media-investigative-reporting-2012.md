The Shantou University College of Journalism and Communication will hold a Journalism Day lecture on the morning of November 7. The talk, titled “The Interaction Between Online Disclosures and In-depth Reporting in the New-Media Environment,” will take place in the Library Lecture Hall.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 10:00–12:00 on November 7, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Topic | The Interaction Between Online Disclosures and In-depth Reporting in the New-Media Environment |
| Guest profile | Guangzhou media professional, veteran online source, and “Outstanding Resident Journalist” at Sun Yat-sen University |

The guest has used blogs, Weibo, and other self-media platforms to follow major public-interest events. The notice credits the guest with first reporting on cases including Sinopec’s “expensive liquor” incident, the Lu Meimei father-and-daughter China-Africa Hope Project controversy, problematic food products, the Chongqing International Miss pageant controversy, the appointment of a convicted official in Funing, Jiangsu, and the Ministry of Railways 12306 ticketing website. These reports attracted nationwide public attention.

According to the organizers, the guest received honors including the 2010 Tianya Zatan Top Ten Internet Figures award, the 2011 Tencent Annual In-depth Reporter award, the Daily Economic News annual tribute award, the Aoyi.com Top Ten Netizens award, and a Caixin Media Outstanding Journalist honor.

The guest’s name is garbled in both the original notice and its archived page, so it is not reconstructed here.
