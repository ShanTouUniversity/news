Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has published student-dormitory utility charges for October 2012. Meters were read on October 30 and 31, following the previous reading on September 30. Students can sign in to the STU utility-management system to review their dormitory charges and raise questions within the designated period.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| System | [STU Utility Management System](http://power.stu.edu.cn/) |
| Review and objection period | Working hours, November 6–12 |
| Submission location | Water and Electrical Center, beside the Science and Technology Building |
| Water and Electrical Center | 2489 |
| Regular payment period | November 13–16 |
| Automatic deduction begins | November 13 |
| Late handling | Managed under university rules from November 19 |

Students who identify an error or omission should complete and sign the *Utility Fee Review Application Form* and submit it to the Water and Electrical Center during working hours from November 6 to 12. Students whose dormitory occupancy does not match the student list should contact the counselor’s office in B106 at 2903355. If no objection is raised by the end of the review period, the university will deduct the charges through the campus card beginning November 13. The regular payment period is November 13–16; late nonpayment will be handled under university rules.

When a matter involves verification by multiple departments and a correction is required, processing is expected to take three to five working days. Students should top up their campus cards in time to maintain a sufficient balance. Personal campus-network account information is not reproduced here.
