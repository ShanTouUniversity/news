The Shantou University College of Science has announced the results of the 2012 Chemistry Alumni Scholarship review. From the opening of applications on October 24 until the October 29, 16:00 deadline, the college received 50 applications. Following review by the scholarship committee, 20 students were selected as candidates.

The scholarship supports 20 full-time undergraduate chemistry students each year, with an award of RMB 2,500 per recipient. Eight of the annual places are reserved for first-year students. The candidate list covers chemistry students from the 2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012 cohorts and appears in the attachment to the original OA notice. To protect student privacy, the names are not reproduced here.
