The first Philosophy Workshop organized by Shantou University’s Department of Social Sciences will be held on November 7. Titled “Following the Steps of Great Thinkers: Hegel through Russell’s Eyes,” the session will be led by Professor Cheng Jiaming and is open to faculty members and students across the university.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00–17:00 on Wednesday, November 7 |
| Venue | Teaching Building E404 |
| Speaker | Professor Cheng Jiaming |
| Topic | Hegel through Russell’s Eyes |

The workshop will use Bertrand Russell’s interpretation of Hegelian philosophy as an entry point for exploring the development of these philosophical ideas.
