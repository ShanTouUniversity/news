The Shantou University Library’s Subject Resource Services series will hold a training session on the basic functions of the CNKI Knowledge Network Discovery Platform on the evening of November 6. Wang Yanna, a product manager at Tongfang Knowledge Network (Beijing) Technology, will lead the session in the Subject Services Department inside the second-floor consultation desk area of the Library.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Demonstration and introduction to the basic functions of the CNKI Knowledge Network Discovery Platform |
| Time | 19:30–20:30 on Tuesday, November 6 |
| Venue | Library Subject Services Department, inside the second-floor consultation desk area |
| Speaker | Wang Yanna, product manager at Tongfang Knowledge Network (Beijing) Technology |

The session will introduce CNKI database series and new-platform functions, explain how to create and use institutional and personal digital libraries, and include a live demonstration and reader Q&A. CNKI integrates journals, graduate theses and dissertations, conference papers, newspapers, yearbooks, reference books, patents, scientific achievements, and Chinese and international standards.

The session is open to faculty members and students across the university and will include a prize quiz during the interactive segment.
