Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has published a notice on proposed cost additions for the renovation of 36 staff residences on the west campus. The project began on August 21, 2012. Several design adjustments were identified during construction in response to actual site conditions. The preliminary estimate is an additional cost of about RMB 30,000, within the 10 percent limit specified by the university.

## Proposed Additions

| Adjustment | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Balconies and terrace parapets | Brickwork and glass-mosaic or ceramic-tile finishes were added where the original design had no facing layer |
| Safety doors | Stainless-steel safety doors were added at openings below the fourth-floor and roof-level sloped panels |
| Parapet and exterior ground levels | Plastering, glass-mosaic work, and integrated scaffolding were added because of incorrect elevations in the original drawings |
| Metal cable trunking | Separate low-voltage trunking was changed to combined high- and low-voltage trunking with a divider, creating a price difference |

Organizations or individuals with comments on the proposed cost additions may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 17:30 on November 7.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
