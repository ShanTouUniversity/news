Shantou University’s Tonghuashun simulated securities investment competition began on November 3 and is open to faculty members and students across the university. The competition will run through December 1, with three trading weeks on a simulated securities platform, investment-analysis training, and stock-market discussion sessions. It is intended to help participants understand financial markets, develop financial-planning awareness, and build risk tolerance.

## Schedule

| Stage | Time | Location or information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-person registration | November 7 | Golden Triangle |
| In-person registration | November 8 | Dining Halls 2 and 4 |
| Opening ceremony and training | November 10, 14:30–16:30 | Science Lecture Hall |
| Simulated trading | November 10–December 1 | Three trading weeks |
| Investment analyst training lecture | November 17 | Time and venue to be announced |
| Stock-market investment discussion | November 24 | Time and venue to be announced |
| Closing and awards ceremony | December 1 | Time and venue to be announced |

Registration is open from November 3 to 9. Participants may register individually in person or use the email channel provided by the organizers. The first 50 students registering in person at the Golden Triangle by November 7, and the first 30 students at each of Dining Halls 2 and 4 by November 8, will receive a commemorative gift. The competition offers cash awards, prizes, and certificates; detailed award arrangements will be issued by the organizers.

Updates will be posted through OA, the Business School section of the BBS, and the STU Business Practice Center’s Weibo account.
