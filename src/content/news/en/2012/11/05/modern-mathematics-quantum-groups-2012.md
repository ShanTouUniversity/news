The 127th lecture in Shantou University’s Modern Mathematics series will be held on November 6. Dr. Hu Naihong, a professor and doctoral supervisor in the Department of Mathematics at East China Normal University, will present “Introduction to Quantum Groups and Related Topics.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:00 on Tuesday, November 6 |
| Venue | Mathematics Laboratory, Gongbei Building Room 412 |
| Speaker | Dr. Hu Naihong |
| Topic | Introduction to Quantum Groups and Related Topics |

Hu’s research covers Lie theory, quantum groups, and representation theory. The lecture will offer a brief introduction to the basic concept of a quantum group, its historical background, and its relationship with the Yang–Baxter equations. It will also use examples to discuss quantum-group construction and related topics such as knot invariants.

The lecture is open to faculty members, graduate students, and senior undergraduates.
