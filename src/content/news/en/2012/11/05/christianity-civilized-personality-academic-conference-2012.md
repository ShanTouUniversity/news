The 2012 conference on regional Christianity and the development of Chinese civilization will be held at Shantou University on November 8–9. The conference theme is “Christianity and the Cultivation of Civilized Personality.” It is organized by the Center for Christian Studies of the STU College of Liberal Arts, with the Christian Studies Center of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences as co-organizer. Sessions will take place in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Communication Center (ACC).

The conference will run in morning and afternoon sessions: 08:30–12:00 and 14:00–18:00 on both days. Scholars from the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, Peking University, Fudan University, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and other universities and research institutions will discuss Christian and Chinese traditional understandings of personality formation; the relationship between Christianity, Chinese and Western value systems, and modern civilization; and the relationship between regional Christian charity, education, medical services, women’s work, and the cultivation of personality.
