The Shantou University AIESEC team has opened recruitment for its winter overseas volunteer program. The opportunity is available to students across the university, regardless of major or year level. According to the notice, the program offers more than 6,000 volunteer placements in over 60 countries, covering issues such as Chinese cultural exchange, HIV/AIDS prevention, community and rural development, environmental protection, language education, and microfinance.

## Recruitment Schedule

| Activity | Time | Location or information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information booth | November 6, 11:00–13:00 | Golden Triangle |
| Overseas volunteer program briefing | November 7, 14:30–16:30 | Lecture Room E204 |
| Recruitment interviews | Resume screening, group interview, and individual interview | Details sent by text message after resume screening |

The briefing will cover the application process and introduce destination countries and volunteer projects. AIESEC’s partner, Jingrui Education Consulting, will also provide information about studying overseas. Applicants may complete a form at the briefing or email a Chinese and English resume to the STU AIESEC team at `aiesec.stu.epcv@gmail.com`. Applications close at 23:30 on November 10.

AIESEC is an international, non-political, independent, and nonprofit organization operated by university students and recent graduates. The STU AIESEC team will also publish recruitment information through the Sina Weibo account “@AIESEC汕大”.
