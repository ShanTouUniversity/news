It is reported that Shantou University has recently issued an official document (STU Fa [2012] No. 137), announcing the appointment of Ni Qingqing as Vice Dean of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, with a probation period of six months.

The appointment was made following deliberation at the 12th university leadership meeting held on October 25. The document has been distributed to all units of the university.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
