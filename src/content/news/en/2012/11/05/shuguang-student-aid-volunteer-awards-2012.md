Shantou University’s Youth League Committee and Young Volunteers Association have announced the 2011–2012 recognition results for the Shuguang Student Aid Program. A total of 82 students were named “Active Contributors.”

Over the past year, volunteers in the program provided sustained tutoring and educational assistance for children from families facing financial difficulties in Shantou. Their work received positive recognition from local residents and relevant departments and contributed to cooperation between the university and the city in developing a culture of civic responsibility.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recognition | Active Contributors of the Shuguang Student Aid Program |
| Period | 2011–2012 |
| Number recognized | 82 students |
| Recognizing organizations | STU Youth League Committee and STU Young Volunteers Association |
| Main service | Tutoring and educational assistance for children from families facing financial difficulties |

The names of the recognized students appear in the attachment to the original OA notice. To protect student privacy, the individual names are not reproduced here. The Youth League Committee also called on students across the university to follow the example of the volunteers and promote the spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress.
