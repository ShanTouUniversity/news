Shantou University’s Graduate School has announced the results of the 2012 Outstanding Graduate Student review. After graduate training units completed their preliminary recommendations, the Graduate School organized a review by university experts. Eleven graduate students were selected as first-prize candidates and 39 as second-prize candidates, for a total of 50 candidates. The candidates represent graduate programs including inorganic chemistry, marine biology, computer application technology, structural engineering, journalism, and public administration.

| Category | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Graduate Student first-prize candidates | 11 |
| Outstanding Graduate Student second-prize candidates | 39 |
| Total | 50 |

The detailed list appears in the attachment to the original OA notice. Because the list contains students’ names, programs, year levels, and supervisors, this report gives only the category totals and does not reproduce the individual names.
