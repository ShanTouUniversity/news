Shantou University’s Engineering College has issued an updated division of responsibilities. The arrangement was considered and approved at a meeting of the college leadership on October 18, 2012, and covers administration, personnel, finance, academic development, research, teaching, student affairs, and international cooperation.

| Official | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Chen Yan, Dean | Overall responsibility for the college, including faculty development, administration, personnel, and finance |
| Xiong Guangjing, Vice Dean | Assisting the dean and overseeing academic development, provincial and ministerial key laboratories, research, graduate education, and engineering master’s education |
| Bao Nengsheng, Vice Dean | Teaching, engineering-education reform, the CDIO Innovation Practice Demonstration Center, and student competitions |
| Zheng Zaixin, Deputy Party Secretary and director overseeing student affairs | College administration, student ideological and political education, admissions and employment, alumni affairs, Party affairs, and publicity |
| Professor (name garbled in the original) | International cooperation and external exchanges |

The revised arrangement applies across the Engineering College. The name of the professor responsible for the final portfolio is garbled in the original notice and is not reconstructed here.
