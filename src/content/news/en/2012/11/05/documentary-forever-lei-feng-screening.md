It is reported that the Publicity Department of the Party Committee and the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University have jointly issued a notice organizing faculty and students across the university to watch the documentary series *Forever Lei Feng*.

The series is jointly produced by the General Political Department of the PLA, *Qiushi* Magazine, and the Military Programs Center of CCTV, and co-produced by the PLA Television Publicity Center, August First Film Studio, and the China Central Newsreel and Documentary Film Studio. Created to implement the spirit of the Sixth Plenary Session of the 17th CPC Central Committee and to advance the campaign of learning from Lei Feng, the documentary presents a relatable, everyday portrait of Lei Feng through innovative storytelling, bringing the Lei Feng spirit closer to contemporary life and the general public. It is considered highly significant for deepening Lei Feng activities and promoting the socialist core value system in the new era.

## Broadcast Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Title | *Forever Lei Feng* |
| Episodes | 6 episodes, 25 minutes each |
| Channels | CCTV General Channel, China Education Television |
| Producers | General Political Department of the PLA and partners |

The viewing campaign is organized in accordance with the *Notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education Forwarding the General Office of the Ministry of Education on Recommending the Documentary Forever Lei Feng* (Yue Jiao Si Ban Han [2012] No. 46). All Party general branches, directly affiliated branches, faculty, and students are encouraged to watch the series to enhance the effectiveness of Lei Feng activities and foster a positive atmosphere for the successful convening of the 18th CPC National Congress.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department of the Party Committee, Communist Youth League Committee)
