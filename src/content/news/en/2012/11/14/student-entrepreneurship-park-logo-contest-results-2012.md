The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has announced the results of the Student Entrepreneurship Park logo-design competition. The competition was launched among STU students on September 27 and received 26 design entries by the submission deadline. On November 1, the Student Entrepreneurship Center invited three faculty members to form a judging panel and review the entries.

| Award | Recipients | Prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Selected design award | 1 | 1,000 yuan |
| Finalist awards | 5 | 200 yuan each |
| Entries received | 26 | — |

The Student Affairs Office congratulated the recognized students and thanked all students who participated. To protect student privacy, individual recipient names and schools are not reproduced here.
