The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the schedule for course section 54246, *Basic Principles of Marxism*, because the instructor will be away. The class originally scheduled for periods A, B, and C on Tuesday, December 4, in Room E207 will be suspended. It will be made up during periods 2, 3, and 4 on Saturday, November 17, in the same room.

| Item | Original schedule | Make-up schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course | *Basic Principles of Marxism* | *Basic Principles of Marxism* |
| Section | 54246 | 54246 |
| Time | Periods A, B, and C, Tuesday, December 4 | Periods 2, 3, and 4, Saturday, November 17 |
| Venue | Room E207 | Room E207 |
| Reason | Instructor away | Make-up class |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in the course to inform one another of the change.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
