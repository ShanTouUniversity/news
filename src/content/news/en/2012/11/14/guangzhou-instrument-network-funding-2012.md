It is reported that the Central Laboratory issued a notice on November 14, 2012, regarding applications for dedicated funding from the Guangzhou Scientific Instrument Collaborative Network.

To facilitate applications, research groups intending to use instruments on the network should first complete an online application. After passing a formal review by the network’s management office, applicants should submit printed forms and related materials to the Central Laboratory, which will assist in processing the funding card.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | Research groups intending to use the Guangzhou network instruments |
| Deadline for paper materials | November 30, to Ms. Lin Yuejuan at the Central Laboratory |
| Handling unit | Central Laboratory |

Details are available on the campus office network and in the OA notice.
