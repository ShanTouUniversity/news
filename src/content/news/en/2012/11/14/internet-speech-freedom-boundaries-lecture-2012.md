The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will host a lecture titled “The Boundaries of Freedom of Online Speech” on the evening of November 15. The speaker will be Pu Zhiqiang, a lawyer and executive partner at Beijing Huayi Law Firm.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The Boundaries of Freedom of Online Speech |
| Speaker | Pu Zhiqiang |
| Time | 19:00–21:00 on Thursday, November 15 |
| Venue | E Staircase 204 |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

Pu holds a bachelor’s degree in history from Nankai University and a master’s degree in law from China University of Political Science and Law. He has represented clients in several defamation disputes, including the cases of Yu Qiuyu v. Xiao Xialin and Lu You v. Huang Jianxiang, and has been recognized by media organizations as a public-interest figure of the year.
