The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has issued a correction to the schedule for course section 54846, *Masterpieces of Chinese and Foreign Music*. Because the instructor will be away, the class originally scheduled for periods 6–7 on Thursday, November 15, in Room STU101 will be suspended. It will be made up during periods 3–4 on Saturday, November 17, in the same room.

| Item | Original schedule | Corrected make-up schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course | *Masterpieces of Chinese and Foreign Music* | *Masterpieces of Chinese and Foreign Music* |
| Section | 54846 | 54846 |
| Time | Periods 6–7, Thursday, November 15 | Periods 3–4, Saturday, November 17 |
| Venue | Room STU101 | Room STU101 |
| Reason | Instructor away | Make-up class |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in the course to inform one another of this correction.
