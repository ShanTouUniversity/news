The Shantou University Personnel Office has announced five appointments. Following review at the 13th University Leadership Meeting on November 6, 2012, the university appointed Li Peijie, Chen Li, Huang Xianglong, Xu Xiaojing, and Wang Fengqi to mid-level management posts, each with a six-month probationary period.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Appointed posts | Mid-level management posts |
| Number appointed | 5 |
| Decision-making meeting | 13th University Leadership Meeting |
| Meeting date | November 6, 2012 |
| Probationary period | Six months |
| Appointees | Li Peijie, Chen Li, Huang Xianglong, Xu Xiaojing, and Wang Fengqi |

The notice was issued as STU Personnel Document [2012] No. 3 on November 14, 2012.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
