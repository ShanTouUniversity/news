The Mathematics Department of the STU College of Science organized students to compete in the 2012 National Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling, where they achieved multiple awards. Under the guidance of faculty members Wei Caimin and Li Jian, STU entered 17 teams. One team won a national first prize and two teams won national second prizes; 12 additional teams received awards in the Guangdong division.

| Division and award | Teams recognized | Students represented |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| National first prize | 1 | 3 |
| National second prize | 2 | 6 |
| Guangdong first prize | 4 | 12 |
| Guangdong second prize | 7 | 21 |
| Guangdong third prize | 1 | 3 |
| **Total entered** | **17** | **45** |

The Ministry of Education-sponsored contest attracted entries from universities in provinces, municipalities, autonomous regions, Hong Kong, and Macao, as well as institutions in Singapore and the United States. The 2012 contest included 21,219 teams from 1,284 universities and more than 60,000 students. The notice states that national first prizes accounted for about 1% of participating teams and national second prizes for about 5.5%.

The College of Science said the results reflected progress in the Mathematics Department’s undergraduate education and teaching reform. Mathematical modeling competitions help students apply mathematics and computer technology to practical problems while developing innovation and teamwork.

To protect student privacy, individual participating and award-winning student names are not reproduced here.
