The STU Dance-Fitness Club, one of the Physical Education Department’s teaching-reform pilot projects, will hold recruitment activities on November 21. The program promotes dance fitness as an international activity combining dance, sport, and the arts, and all currently enrolled students are welcome to attend.

| Activity | Time | Venue | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Promotional performance and information session | 11:30–13:00 | Golden Triangle | Dance-fitness performance, on-site information, and commemorative gifts |
| Try-out class | 14:00 | Gymnasium aerobics room | Introductory dance-fitness lesson |

The Physical Education Department hopes the recruitment activities will introduce more students to the club and encourage them to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
