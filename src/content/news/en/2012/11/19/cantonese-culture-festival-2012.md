The STU Student Community X Plan will hold the “Cantonese in the Air” culture festival for currently enrolled students. The series is designed to enrich community life, encourage interaction, and give students an opportunity to demonstrate their Cantonese-language skills.

### Activities

| Activity | Time | Venue | Main rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STU “Guess the Lyrics” competition | 20:00 on November 24 | Small Stage | Team registration; three members per team, with at least one Cantonese speaker |
| Cantonese-culture poster contest | December 1 | Golden Triangle | Create an A3 poster on the theme of Cantonese cuisine; teams of 2–4 members |

The “Guess the Lyrics” competition will award first, second, and third prizes, as well as the Most Popularity Award and Best Voice Award. The first prize carries 300 yuan, the second prize 200 yuan, and the third prize 100 yuan; the two special awards each carry 100 yuan. Prize winners will also receive gifts or CDs as specified in the event arrangements.

The poster contest will select the top eight works by vote, with each receiving a 100-yuan cash award.

### Registration

Registration runs from November 19 to 22. On-site registration will be available beneath Dining Halls 2 and 4 on November 21. Both competitions require teams to submit team information in the prescribed format; detailed registration instructions and event rules are subject to the original notice attachment.
