The STU Academic Affairs Office is organizing applications for public-welfare courses for the 2012–2013 spring semester. Under a decision by the university’s Teaching Committee, public-welfare courses became a required general-education component for the 2012 cohort and later. Every undergraduate must complete at least one public-welfare course credit during their studies. The application process is intended to expand course resources and develop the service-learning model.

### Application requirements

Proposed courses must meet the basic requirements of the *STU Public-Welfare Course Implementation Plan* (STU document [2010] No. 7), including requirements for the course overview, educational objectives, implementation, assessment, and instructors. Courses must be offered during the 2012–2013 spring semester. Course leaders must have completed public-welfare-course instructor training and previously taught a public-welfare course.

As a general principle, each school should offer at least one public-welfare course. Teaching units must also coordinate course capacity so that the number of available course places for 2013 reaches at least two-thirds of the unit’s enrollment. Based on their disciplines and programs, units should encourage students to apply their academic knowledge to community service and encourage instructors to develop new public-welfare courses.

The university also requires relevant full-time instructors to undertake public-welfare-course teaching, including instructors whose annual teaching workload is below 280 hours for professional courses or 360 hours for public courses, and instructors recruited within the previous three years, who are in principle expected to teach a public-welfare course.

### Application process

Teaching units may begin their selection, recommendation, and application work upon receiving the notice. Applicants must complete the *STU New Course Application Form (Public-Welfare Course)*. By December 31, each unit must complete its review and determine its recommended course list, submit the paper form to the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office, and email the electronic version to `gykc@stu.edu.cn`.
