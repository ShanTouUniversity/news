The “Wuyi Three-Stroke Paintings: Gao Fei Chinese Painting Exhibition” will be held at the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design Gallery at Shantou University from November 19 to 28. The exhibition is organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and students and faculty members across the university are welcome to visit.
