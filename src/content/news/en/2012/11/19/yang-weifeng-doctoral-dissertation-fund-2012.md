The STU Graduate School has announced that faculty member Yang Weifeng has received support from the Special Fund for Authors of National Outstanding Doctoral Dissertations in Higher Education. Established by the Ministry of Education, the fund is intended to encourage authors of outstanding doctoral dissertations to continue producing innovative research.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient | Yang Weifeng |
| Project approval number | 201215 |
| Project | Applications of localized novel optical fields in ultrafast electronic-dynamics control |
| Project period | January 2012–December 2014 |
| Total support | 380,000 yuan |

The project will study localized novel optical fields and their applications in controlling ultrafast electronic dynamics.
