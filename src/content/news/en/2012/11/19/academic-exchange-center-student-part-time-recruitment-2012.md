The Shantou University Academic Exchange Service Center is recruiting students for part-time positions as restaurant service attendants and floor service attendants. The center provides hospitality services for campus visitors, giving the positions a practical service-work component.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Positions | Restaurant service attendant; floor service attendant |
| Pay | Hourly wage |
| Applicants | Male students currently enrolled at Shantou University |
| Application deadline | December 20, 2012 |
| Application email | `stuacc@stu.edu.cn` |
| Interview | Candidates will be notified separately by email |
| Inquiry numbers | 82902668 and 82904138, 08:30–18:00 |

Applicants should have strong spoken English and Cantonese, while applicants who have passed the national College English Test Band 4 will receive preference. They should also have social and adaptive skills, a neat appearance, and a courteous manner. Applications must include the job application form and a one-inch front-facing photograph, sent by email to the designated address. Applicants should bring their ID card and other relevant original documents to the interview as required.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachment for the job application form. Detailed interview requirements are subject to the recruiting unit’s notice.
