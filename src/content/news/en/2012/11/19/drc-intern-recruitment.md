It is reported that, due to work needs, the Shantou Municipal Development and Reform Commission will jointly recruit the third batch of interns with the Business School of Shantou University. There are 3 openings, open to Business School graduate students and undergraduate students who enrolled in 2009 and have been recommended for graduate admission.

## Internship Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Openings | 3 |
| Eligibility | Business School graduate students; Business School undergraduates who enrolled in 2009 (recommended for graduate study) |
| Requirements | Strong writing skills; basic understanding of current economic development and reform trends |
| Duration | Starting November 26, 2012, for 3 months |
| Application deadline | 17:00, November 22, 2012 |

Applicants must submit the "Registration Form for DRC Interns" and a "Draft Internship Work Plan" to the Business School Youth League Committee before the deadline. The Commission and the Business School will select interns based on applications and notify those selected to attend pre-job training.

Pre-job training is scheduled for 3:00 p.m. on November 23, 2012, in the Business School seminar room (second floor, Wenxi Building). Students who have not received a selection notice need not attend.

It is also understood that the Commission will provide appropriate subsidies to participating interns, and other matters will follow the relevant regulations of the Business School's student internship base.
