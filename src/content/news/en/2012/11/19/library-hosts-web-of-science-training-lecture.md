It is reported that the University Library will hold an SCI training lecture titled "The Application of Web of Science in Scientific Research" from 4:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. on Monday, November 19, at the Library Lecture Hall, delivered by Ma Yapeng, training specialist at Thomson Reuters.

The session will address key needs across the research workflow, including:

- How to quickly and accurately retrieve core literature related to a research topic
- How to derive new ideas and inspire innovative research from existing publications
- How to track the latest progress on a topic and stay current
- How to efficiently manage valuable references
- How to improve writing and submission efficiency and accelerate publication
- How to showcase research achievements on international academic platforms

The lecture aims to help graduate students preparing proposals, researchers seeking core papers and collaborators, and academic leaders looking to manage literature collections and streamline citation formatting — including how to retrieve collected references and automatically generate bibliographies in required styles.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
