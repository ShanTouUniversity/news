The STU Student Affairs Office is conducting a VisualDNA psychological follow-up assessment for students in the 2009 cohort. The assessment is intended to help students understand themselves and plan their careers. It will be available from November 19 through December 2, after which the system will close.

### Assessment arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Students in the 2009 cohort |
| Assessment period | November 19–December 2 |
| Platform | <http://xlcs.stu.edu.cn> |
| Usage | Each campus-network account may be used once |
| Time limit | Approximately 30 minutes |
| After completion | The system provides a brief assessment report and, through the stated process, a 5 GB traffic reward; a survey must then be completed |

Students should log in with their own campus-network accounts and read the informed-consent form and assessment instructions before answering. After the assessment and traffic operation are completed as instructed, the additional traffic will take effect after midnight the following day and may be transferred to another person under the system’s rules.
