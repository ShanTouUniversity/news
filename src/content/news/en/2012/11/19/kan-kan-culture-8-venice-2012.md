The eighth Kan Kan Culture event at Shantou University will explore “The Preservation and Development of Venice.” The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has invited Italian architect and professor Marino Folin to the event, where he will engage in a dialogue with Wang Shouzhi.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Kan Kan Culture 8 |
| Topic | The preservation and development of Venice |
| Dialogue participants | Wang Shouzhi and Marino Folin |
| Time | Starting at 19:30 on November 20 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall, Shantou University |

Marino Folin previously served as rector of the University of Venice and dean of its architecture school. He has conducted long-term research on cities and territories and has served as an adviser on cultural and architectural policy and to related foundations. The event will discuss Venice’s history and future from the perspectives of urban preservation, cultural development, and architectural policy.
