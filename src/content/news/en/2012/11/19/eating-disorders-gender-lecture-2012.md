Muriel Walker, PhD, an associate professor in the STU Foreign Languages Department, will deliver a lecture on “Gender and Eating Disorders.” The presentation will introduce the relationship between personal body image and dieting and discuss how eating-disorder issues affect women.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Gender Specificity of Eating Disorders |
| Speaker | Dr. Muriel Walker, associate professor |
| Affiliation | STU Foreign Languages Department |
| Time | 16:10–18:00 on Thursday, November 22 |
| Venue | E Lecture Room 204 |
| Audience | STU students and faculty members |

The lecture will discuss links among body image, dieting, and eating disorders. Students and faculty members are welcome to attend.
