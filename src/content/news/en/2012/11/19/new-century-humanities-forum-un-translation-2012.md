The 114th New Century Humanities Forum will focus on “A Discussion of United Nations Translation: The UNESCO Example.” Professor Zhu Gang, dean and doctoral supervisor of the School of Foreign Languages at Nanjing University, will draw on his experience at the translation service of UNESCO to discuss the connections among translation, politics, culture, and social knowledge.

Zhu also serves as secretary-general of the National Association for American Studies and as a member of the Ministry of Education’s Foreign-Language Teaching Advisory Committee. His teaching and research focus on British and American literature, critical theory, and translation studies. He has been a visiting scholar at several overseas universities and worked as a translator and reviser at UNESCO. The presentation will address the deeper cultural meanings behind literal language, a central concern of translation studies.
