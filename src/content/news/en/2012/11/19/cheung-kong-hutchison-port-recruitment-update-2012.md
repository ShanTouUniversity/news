The second 2012 Cheung Kong Hutchison campus recruitment session at Shantou University will be held from November 21 to 23. Under the updated arrangement, Hutchison Whampoa Port Holdings will participate, and recruitment information for Xiamen International Container Terminal Co., Ltd. has been added.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment event | Second 2012 Cheung Kong Hutchison campus recruitment session at STU |
| Dates | November 21–23 |
| Participating companies | Hutchison Whampoa Port Holdings; newly added Xiamen International Container Terminal Co., Ltd. |
| Audience | STU graduating-class students |
| Related sessions | Company presentations, company exhibition, and comprehensive tests |
| Related venue | New Library |

Graduating-class students should prepare according to the updated recruitment schedule and position list and attend on time. Students going to the New Library for company presentations, the company exhibition, and comprehensive tests are required to wear formal attire.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
