To strengthen its intellectual atmosphere and encourage academic discussion, the STU College of Liberal Arts will periodically hold open academic salons where faculty members and students can discuss topics of interest. The first salon will focus on “Chinese Modernity and the Spirit of Capitalism.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | First College of Liberal Arts academic salon |
| Topic | Chinese Modernity and the Spirit of Capitalism |
| Languages | English and Chinese |
| Time | 15:00–17:30 on Wednesday, November 21 |
| Venue | ACC Conference Room 3 |
| Audience | Interested students and faculty members |

Possible discussion topics include Western understandings of modernity, modernization, and capitalism; modern Chinese history; and modernity and capitalism in China. The discussion is not limited to these subtopics.
