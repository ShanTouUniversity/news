Zheng Yefu, a professor in the Department of Sociology at Peking University, will visit Shantou University to deliver a lecture titled “Understanding China through News Events.” The event is organized by the Academic Affairs Office and the Institute of Higher Education.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Professor Zheng Yefu |
| Affiliation | Department of Sociology, Peking University |
| Topic | Understanding China through News Events |
| Time | 19:30 on November 22, 2012 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall, Shantou University |
| Organizers | Academic Affairs Office and Institute of Higher Education |

Zheng is a sociologist who has worked at the Beijing Academy of Social Sciences, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, Renmin University of China, and Peking University. He previously hosted *Oriental Son* and served as chief planner for *Tell It Like It Is* on China Central Television. His books include *Like Our Ancestors*, *The Coming of the Post-Material-Desire Era*, *On Trust*, *On Costs*, *Resisting Monopoly*, *A Smoker’s Account*, and *Out of the Prisoner’s Dilemma*.
