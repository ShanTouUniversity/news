STU’s Finance Office has issued the schedule for the university’s 2012 year-end financial settlement. The Accounting Section will conduct the settlement from December 29, 2012, through January 11, 2013. Relevant units and faculty and staff members are asked to complete reimbursement, repayment, and document handover procedures according to the type of account.

### Routine accounts

Units should submit receipts and invoices already collected and used to the Accounting Section for settlement by December 28. Unused receipts from the year should also be returned. Units must repay borrowed petty cash by the same date, and faculty and staff who need reimbursement or repayment should complete the relevant procedures by December 28. The Accounting Section will suspend public-facing reimbursement services from December 29 through January 11. Special funding needs should be discussed with the Finance Office.

### Treasury-payment accounts

| Account type | Deadline or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Treasury-authorized payment | Complete reimbursement procedures by December 20 and withdraw the funds from the bank by December 30 |
| Treasury-direct payment and remittance | Complete reimbursement procedures by December 24 |
| Suspension of treasury-payment reimbursement | Reimbursement for these accounts will be suspended from December 24 until the provincial finance authority approves the 2013 budget |

The Finance Office listed the public contact numbers 86502446 and 86502492.
