STU’s Student Affairs Office will host a sharing session on outdoor experiences, inviting Cao Jun, vice president of the Shenzhen Mountaineering and Outdoor Sports Association, to discuss mountaineering experiences and life lessons under the theme “From Looking Up at Snow Mountains to Touching Glaciers.”

The session will also introduce the development of Peking University’s Shanying Mountaineering Club and practical knowledge and skills for outdoor activities. Organized by the Student Affairs Office and co-organized by the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association, the event will take place from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, November 23, in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Communication Center (ACC). All faculty members and students are welcome.
