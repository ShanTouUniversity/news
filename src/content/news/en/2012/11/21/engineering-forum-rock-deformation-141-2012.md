STU’s School of Engineering will host the 141st Engineering Research Forum. Professor Zhu Zhende, doctoral supervisor at Hohai University, will discuss the mechanical behavior of progressive rock deformation and failure in complex environments, presenting research related to rock deformation and failure processes.

The forum will take place from 3:30 to 5 p.m. on Tuesday, November 27, in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room. Faculty members and students from the school, as well as other interested members of the university community, are welcome.
