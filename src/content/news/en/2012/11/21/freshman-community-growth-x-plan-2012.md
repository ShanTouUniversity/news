To help first-year students adapt to university community life, STU’s Student Affairs Office is launching the “Very 3+1: A Complete Development Guide for First-Year Students” program under the Student Community X Plan. The program focuses on interpersonal communication, financial skills, and physical and mental well-being, while adding off-campus practice and group reflection.

### Program activities

- Workshop: developing and applying interpersonal communication skills
- Guest sharing: managing personal spending and living expenses
- Outdoor activities: exploring psychological and physical safety
- Off-campus practice: getting to know STU and Shantou communities
- Group sharing: discussing activity outcomes and personal reflections

The program is open to all first-year students and will be held on the afternoons of December 1, 2, and 5. Forty places are available. If applications exceed the quota, participants will be selected by lottery. Applications close at 6 p.m. on November 27.

Please follow the Student Affairs Office’s original OA attachment and subsequent community publicity for the registration form and submission instructions.
