Zhicheng College will hold its 2012 College Day gala under the theme “Spring Breeze.” The event is scheduled for 7:30 p.m. on Saturday, November 24, at the STU Grand Hall. Faculty members and students from across the university are invited.

Zhicheng College residents will enter and take their seats 15 minutes early under the guidance of area resident assistants and Communist Youth League branch secretaries. College mentors, guests, and alumni will be seated in rows 6 through 8.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
