STU’s union has circulated information about a home-building materials group-buying event for faculty and staff. Organized by a Shantou home-building materials brand alliance, the event will take place at 12:30 p.m. on November 24 in the fourth-floor conference hall of the Dihao Hotel. It offers a special arrangement for faculty and staff with home renovation or housing needs.

According to the event information, faculty and staff may receive an additional discount on top of the group-buying price by presenting a work ID or other relevant employment documentation, except for specially priced products. A deposit of RMB 1,000 per brand will be collected at the event. Refund arrangements, the validity period, and gifts are subject to the event organizer’s order terms and published rules.

Registration closes on November 23. Please consult the original OA attachment for the registration channel and complete discount terms.
