The final of the 20th STU Campus Top Ten Singers competition is approaching. To broaden campus participation, the STU Youth League Committee and Student Union are recruiting 55 public jurors from across the university. They will evaluate the final together with professional judges.

### Jury composition

| Source | Number and selection method |
| --- | --- |
| School-recommended representatives | Two from each school, recommended by the school student union; students active in school cultural and sports activities, with relevant awards or professional vocal training, will receive preference |
| School lucky representatives | Three from each school; students apply independently, after which the organizing committee reviews applications by school and draws representatives at random |
| Community preliminary-track representatives | Five, drawn at random from contestants who reached the qualifying competition but did not qualify for the preliminary round |
| Total | 55 |

Final contestants may not serve on the public jury. School lucky representatives should download and complete the registration form and send the electronic version to the designated organizing-committee email. Applications are open through 24:00 on December 2. Further information is available on the Student Union website: <http://stuu.stu.edu.cn/stuu2012/>.
