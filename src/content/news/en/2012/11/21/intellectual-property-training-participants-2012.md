STU’s Office of Research Affairs has announced the participant arrangements for the basic intellectual-property training course jointly organized with the School of Law. A total of 155 students and faculty members have been confirmed for the program. Sessions will be held in E Staircase 101, and participants should attend according to the course schedule.

Attendance will be required. Participants who fail to attend as required will be considered unsuccessful in the assessment and will not receive the Guangdong Intellectual Property Training Completion Certificate. The participant list and detailed course schedule are included in the original OA attachments.
