It is reported that to help the Class of 2012 freshmen better adapt to university life and strengthen their sense of belonging, STU Youth organized a freshman Youth Day program themed "Hello, Shantou." A showcase of the projects was held on November 3 at Jin San Jiao, with winners selected by on-site voting.

## Event at a Glance

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Theme | "Hello, Shantou" |
| Participants | Class of 2012 freshmen |
| Organizers | Communist Youth League Committee of STU, STU Youth |
| Showcase | Nov. 3, Jin San Jiao |
| Selection method | On-site voting |

## Winners

| Prize | No. of Teams | Teams |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| First Prize | 1 | STU – A Bite of Shantou |
| Second Prize | 2 | Footprints; Time Machine |
| Third Prize | 3 | Leisure – Team Ideal; Double Twins; Travelers |

The Youth League Committee congratulated the winning teams and thanked all faculty and students for their attention and support.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
