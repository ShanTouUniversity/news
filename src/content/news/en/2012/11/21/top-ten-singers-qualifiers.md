It is reported that, to enliven cultural life in the dormitory areas and select outstanding singers for the preliminary round of the 20th "Campus Top Ten Singers Contest," the university Youth League Committee and Student Union held the community qualifying round of the contest under the Student Community X Program at the Small Stage on November 17.

After fierce competition among 19 contestants, the list of five qualifiers has been confirmed:

| No. | Singer | Community |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Li JH | Community G |
| 2 | Liu XB | Community C |
| 3 | Luo RY | Community G |
| 4 | Li L | Community G |
| 5 | Huang ZL | Community F |

The five qualifiers will advance to the preliminary round of the "Campus Top Ten Singers Contest" on behalf of their communities. The Youth League Committee and the Student Union thanked all faculty and students for their support and participation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
