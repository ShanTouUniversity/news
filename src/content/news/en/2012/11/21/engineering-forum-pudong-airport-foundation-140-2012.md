STU’s School of Engineering will host the 140th Engineering Research Forum. Li Junshi, a senior professor-level engineer and deputy chief engineer, will share work on “Foundation Treatment and Analysis for the Three Runways at Shanghai Pudong International Airport,” covering related engineering practice and analysis for large airport runways.

The forum will take place from 4:10 to 5:40 p.m. on Monday, November 26, in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room. Faculty members and students from the school, as well as other interested members of the university community, are welcome.
