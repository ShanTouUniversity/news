STU’s Student Affairs Office has formally released the 2012 internship support list. The list was finalized after student applications, recommendations from colleges and residential colleges, and an earlier public-comment period, and has been distributed to the colleges and residential colleges on the main campus.

The selected participants are listed in the original Student Affairs Office OA attachment. Because the attachment contains students’ personal information, this article reports the release only and does not reproduce names or other identifying details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The internship support list contains students’ personal information. Please consult the original OA attachment through the OA system; the list is not reproduced here.
