STU’s School of Engineering will host the 139th Engineering Research Forum. Professor Xu Chao, doctoral supervisor at Tongji University, will discuss “Geosynthetic-Reinforced Soil Structures and Analysis of Representative Cases,” introducing related structures and engineering examples.

The forum will take place from 2:30 to 4 p.m. on Monday, November 26, in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room. Faculty members and students from the school, as well as other interested members of the university community, are welcome.
