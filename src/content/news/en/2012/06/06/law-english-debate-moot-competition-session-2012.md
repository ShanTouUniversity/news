It is reported that STU Law School will hold an information session on its English speech and debate team and the CIETAC Cup and Jessup competitions from 19:30 to 21:30 on June 6 in Meeting Room 213 of the Law School.

The session will introduce the newly formed English speech and debate team and include an experience-sharing discussion on the CIETAC Cup and Jessup competitions. Gu Junfeng, a Law School teacher, will provide guidance. Students interested in English communication or case competitions are welcome to attend.
