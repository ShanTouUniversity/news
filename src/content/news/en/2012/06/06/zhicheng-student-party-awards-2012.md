It is reported that Zhicheng College’s Party general branch has recognized 14 students as Outstanding Student Party Members and two students as Outstanding Party Affairs Workers after reviewing recommendations from the student Party branches.

The Party general branch called on all student Party members to learn from the recognized individuals, demonstrate exemplary conduct in study and work, contribute to college development, and pursue innovation and diligent learning.

Because all awardees are students, their names and major and year information are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College Party General Branch)
