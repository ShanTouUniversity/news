It is reported that new Grade Seven students entering STU Affiliated Middle School in fall 2012, who are children of STU faculty and staff, must register from 09:00 to 11:30 on June 26 at the Academic Affairs Office on the first floor of the affiliated middle school.

Families should bring the household registration booklet and copies showing only the head of household and the student, as well as the student’s primary-school evaluation handbook. Families unable to register on time for special reasons should contact the school’s Academic Affairs Office in advance. Failure to register by the deadline will be treated as voluntary forfeiture of the place.

For inquiries, call `82902041` and ask for Director Huang.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Affiliated Middle School and Resource Management Office)
