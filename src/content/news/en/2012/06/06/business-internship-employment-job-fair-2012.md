It is reported that STU Business School and Shantou Nanyue Human Resources Market will jointly hold the 2012 Student Internship and Employment Fair from 09:00 to 12:00 on June 17 at the second floor of Nanyue Human Resources Market in Jinlong Building, 104 Jinsha East Road, Shantou.

About 50 companies, including large and medium-sized private, foreign-invested, joint-stock, and listed enterprises in eastern Guangdong, will offer positions in basic management, clerical work, and fields related to students’ majors. Internships are expected to begin in early July and last three months, with extensions possible according to business needs. Outstanding interns may be offered regular employment. Companies will provide certain allowances, and some will provide meals and accommodation.

The fair is intended for Business School undergraduates from the 2009 cohort, 2010-cohort graduate students, and selected undergraduates from the 2010 and 2011 cohorts, involving about 300 students. Participants should bring a printed résumé and work proposal and meet at the university motto monument by 08:00 on June 17 for group transport. Return buses will leave at 10:00 and 11:00, and Route 6 is also available. Participants should dress neatly, wear their school uniform, and follow event arrangements and discipline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School and Shantou Nanyue Human Resources Market)
