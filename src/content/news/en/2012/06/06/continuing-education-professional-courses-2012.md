It is reported that STU’s Social Sciences Department has launched five continuing-education courses for the university’s professional and technical staff to improve service standards and teaching quality. The schedule is:

| Course | Instructor | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to Philosophy | Cheng Jiaming | June 9, 08:00–12:00 | D202 |
| Practical Analysis of the Labor Contract Law | Shen Yiyong | June 10, 08:00–12:00 | D202 |
| Research on Legislation in China’s Special Economic Zones | Shen Yiyong | June 10, 15:00–17:00 | D202 |
| Confucius: A Witness to Zhuangzi’s Transcendence of the Inexpressible | Gao Qingrong | June 13, 15:00–17:00 | D202 |
| Changes in the Law-Enforcement Environment Brought by the Administrative Enforcement Law | Shen Yiyong | June 15, 19:00–21:00 | D202 |

Interested faculty members may log into <http://training.stu.edu.cn> to select courses using their campus-network account credentials. Registered participants should attend at the scheduled times.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office and Social Sciences Department)
