It is reported that on June 6, Shantou University issued a document (STU Fa [2012] No. 74) announcing that, following deliberation at the 7th meeting of university leadership held on June 5, 2012, it had decided to appoint Li Rundian as Deputy Director of the Research Affairs Office, with a probation period of six months.

The appointment was announced to all units across the university. Under the document, Li Rundian will serve a probationary term of six months, after which formal appointment procedures will follow in accordance with relevant university regulations.
