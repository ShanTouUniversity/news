It is reported that STU Law School’s Party general branch has proposed six Outstanding Party Affairs Workers and 26 Outstanding Communist Party Members for recognition. The proposal follows work by the school’s Party branches and members to build learning-oriented and service-oriented Party organizations and participate in the “Strive for Excellence” campaign. The public notice was issued ahead of the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China.

Comments on the proposed awardees may be submitted to the Law School Party general branch in writing, in person, or by telephone by June 12. Feedback must include the commenter’s real name. Telephone: `82902215`. Because the list includes student Party members, individual names are not published here.
