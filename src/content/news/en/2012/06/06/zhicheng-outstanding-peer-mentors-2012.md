It is reported that STU’s Zhicheng College reviewed its peer-mentor work for the 2011–2012 academic year and recognized seven students as Outstanding Peer Mentors after considering work reports, defenses, self-assessments, and evaluations by residential students.

Zhicheng College encouraged the recognized students to value the honor and continue contributing to residential-community development and student services.

Because all awardees are students, their names and major and year information are not published here.
