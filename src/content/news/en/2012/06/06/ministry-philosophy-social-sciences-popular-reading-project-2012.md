It is reported that the Ministry of Education has launched a philosophy and social sciences popular-reading project to collect accessible works that bring academic research to wider audiences. The call focuses on readable works about the theory of socialism with Chinese characteristics, economic and social development, ecological civilization, education reform, Chinese civilization, China’s development path, and major advances in philosophy and social sciences.

Proposed works should demonstrate sound political direction, academic rigor, authority, clear language, and practical readability. Illustrated formats are encouraged, the usual length should not exceed 100,000 Chinese characters, and the publication must be a Chinese-language book. Project leaders must be university faculty members with senior professional titles, and each applicant may submit only one proposal at a time. Revised editions of published books are not eligible, and projects must have no academic-integrity or intellectual-property disputes.

Each project will receive RMB 150,000. The research and publication period is generally one to two years, and designated publishers of the Ministry’s Department of Social Sciences will provide unified publication funding. STU units must submit a draft plan to the Research Office by June 21 and submit seven printed copies of the final plan by June 25, together with an electronic copy.
