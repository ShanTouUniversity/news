| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Huang Yan |
| Members | Xu Zongling, Ouyang Feng, Du Mian, Qin Zhong, Wang * (one character garbled in the original), Bu Xiangzhi, Song Xiaobao, Li Yue, Lin Quan, Long Yue’e |
| Secretaries | Yang Min, Yin Xiaoqing |

All faculty and students of the Business School are asked to take note.
