It is reported that STU has announced the review arrangements for faculty and staff utility charges for May 2012. The latest meter reading was taken on June 1, following the previous reading on May 1. Faculty and staff may log into the [STU utility-management system](http://power.stu.edu.cn) with their campus-network accounts to check the charges.

Questions or objections may be taken to the Utility Center from June 6 through June 12; telephone: `2489`. If no objection is raised by the deadline, the Finance Office will deduct the charges from June salary accounts. Staff without salary accounts must pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
