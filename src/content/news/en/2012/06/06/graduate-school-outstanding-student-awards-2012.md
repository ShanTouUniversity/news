It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University has launched the selection and recognition of outstanding student leaders and Party affairs workers for the 2011–2012 academic year, open to all graduate students on the main campus.

The initiative aims to commend outstanding individuals, set role models, and encourage graduate student leaders and Party affairs workers to be innovative, dedicated, and service-oriented, while strengthening the role of graduate student organizations in self-governance, self-education, and self-service. Detailed evaluation criteria are provided in the *Trial Measures for the Selection of Outstanding Student Leaders and Party Affairs Workers of the Graduate School (2012)*.

## Submission Timeline

| Stage | Deadline | Requirements |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Democratic evaluation and nomination by graduate organizations | Before June 11 | Graduate student unions, class collectives, and Party/Youth League branches complete nominations and submit lists and application forms to the department office or the Graduate School Office (Room G204) for review |
| Consolidated submission by departments | Before end of work on June 13 | Academic coordinators of each training unit compile and submit all materials to the Graduate School Office |

Contact: Lin Xiaojun, Tel: 82904000.

Application forms for outstanding student leaders and outstanding Party affairs workers are available as attachments on the OA system.
