It is reported that STU has installed video equipment for real-time monitoring in multimedia classrooms to protect multimedia teaching facilities.

The Party and Government Office reminds students using the classrooms for self-study to take care of public teaching equipment and maintain appropriate, courteous conduct, helping preserve a positive learning environment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
