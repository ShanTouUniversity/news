It is reported that the CPC Shantou University Committee and Shantou University have announced a personnel decision concerning the College of Engineering.

According to Document No. 18 [2012] of the CPC Shantou University Committee (Shan Da Dang Zi [2012] No. 18), following deliberation at the 6th University Leadership Meeting of 2012 and the 4th Standing Committee Meeting of the University Party Committee in 2012, it has been decided that:

- Comrade Zheng Zaixin is appointed Student Affairs Director and Deputy Secretary of the Party General Branch of the College of Engineering (presiding over the Branch's work), with a six-month probationary period;
- Comrade Li Runtian will no longer serve as Student Affairs Director and Deputy Secretary of the Party General Branch of the College of Engineering.

The notice has been distributed to all university units, with copies sent to the Organization Department of the CPC Shantou Municipal Committee and the Shantou Municipal Personnel Bureau.
