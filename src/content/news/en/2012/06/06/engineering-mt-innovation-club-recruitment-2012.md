It is reported that STU Engineering School’s MT Enterprise Innovation Club has opened its 2012 recruitment. The club is supported by the Engineering School and alumni companies Shantou ChuanYue Computer Software Co., Ltd. and Nanjing Nanli Vision Software Technology Co., Ltd., and welcomes students from the 2010 and 2011 cohorts across the university.

The club is recruiting for technical, design, and business teams. Applicants should have a strong interest in product design or network technology; project experience is preferred. Specific competency requirements will follow the application materials. Registration closes at 20:00 on June 8, and the application form must be submitted according to the original notice by 17:00 that day.

Recruitment interviews will begin at 09:00 on June 9 in the meeting room of the CDIO Innovation Practice Center. To protect student privacy, the personal email address in the original notice is not published; application forms, submission instructions, and position requirements should be confirmed in the OA attachments.
