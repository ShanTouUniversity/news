It is reported that under the "Ten Thousand College Students in Work-Study, Supporting One Hundred Thousand Impoverished Primary and Secondary School Students" program jointly organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and China Mobile's Guangdong branch, Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has released a recruitment notice for the whole campus. Thirty students will be recruited for social practice positions at China Mobile's physical outlets, with students from financially disadvantaged families given priority.

## Positions and Compensation

The practice period runs from April 2012 to March 2013. Participants take part as social practice, receiving a service allowance of 9.3 yuan per hour, paid to each student by the 30th day of the month following their service.

Schedules are arranged flexibly around students' class time: students may submit a monthly work plan at the start of each month based on their courses, working no more than four hours per day and no more than 24 hours per week cumulatively, so that their studies are not affected. Shifts are divided into morning (8:00–12:00) and afternoon (2:00–6:00 p.m.) sessions. Practice venues include service halls, experience stores, and brand stores.

## Six Types of Positions

| Position | Main Duties |
| --- | --- |
| Reception | Greeting customers at service hall entrances and guiding them to queuing devices |
| Consultation | Answering inquiries in the lobby and guiding customers to self-service devices |
| New Service Experience & Promotion | Assisting customer trials and recommending new services |
| Customized Handset Sales | Introducing handset models, guiding agreement forms, and presenting data-service packages |
| Promotion & Marketing Events | Presenting promotional campaigns and recommending products on site |
| Campus, Community & Direct Sales | Selling products to potential customers on campus and in the community |

## How to Apply

Applicants must log on to the program website dxsx.st10086.com, click "2012 Paid Internship Program Registration," and after submitting the online form download the paper application form to bring to the interview (on-site registration is not accepted). The deadline is 12:00 noon on June 8; interview times and locations will be announced on the website on June 11.
