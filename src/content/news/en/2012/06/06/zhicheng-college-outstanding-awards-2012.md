It is reported that Zhicheng College issued Document No. 1 [2012] on June 6, 2012, to honor outstanding student contributors for the 2011–2012 academic year.

The college noted that student organizations made notable contributions to team building and self-governance over the past year. Following selection by the Student Union, Youth League branches, the Ivy Service-Learning Association and the editorial board of *Zhicheng*, and approval by the college Youth League Committee, the following honors were conferred:

## Awardees (anonymized)

| Award | Number | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outstanding Student Cadres | 25 | Students with outstanding performance in student organizations |
| Active Participants in Student Affairs | 28 | Students actively engaged in student affairs |
| Outstanding Volunteers | 8 | Students with outstanding volunteer service |

The full lists are available in the original OA notice and are withheld here to protect student privacy. Awardees were encouraged to cherish the honor and continue contributing to the college’s development.
