It is reported that STU’s 19th Student Congress was held on May 20 in the Science Lecture Hall. Of the 191 delegates expected, 132 attended. The congress approved the work report of the 18th Student Union Committee and elected a 12-member 19th Student Union Committee through a competitive ballot.

The congress also elected the new Student Union presidium: Huang KM as chair, and Xu JY, Zhao HR, and Zhao ZX as vice chairs. To protect student privacy, the full committee list is not published here, and student names are abbreviated under the applicable rules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
