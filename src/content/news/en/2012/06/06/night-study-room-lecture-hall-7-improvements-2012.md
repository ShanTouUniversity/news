It is reported that, following student suggestions about night-study rooms, STU has studied the issue with relevant departments and plans to designate Lecture Hall 7 as a night-study room because it is the only lecture hall without multimedia playback equipment. The university will address concerns about seat use, noise from the southern laboratories, and mosquitoes.

Tables will be placed near the podium for temporary book storage. Students who leave the hall for more than two hours should take their books with them, and staff will regularly clear items left on seats. During the summer break, the university plans to seal the southern windows, repair the soundproof door, add mosquito screens to the southern and northern doors and windows, replace damaged seats, refurbish walls, and install air-conditioning, fire-safety, and monitoring facilities.

If Lecture Hall 7 cannot meet demand for night study, the university will take further measures to add study space.
