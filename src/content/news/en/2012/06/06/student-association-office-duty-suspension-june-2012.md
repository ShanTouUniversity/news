It is reported that, as final examinations approach and student-organization activities decrease, Office 731 of the STU Student Association Federation will suspend duty service from Wednesday of Week 15, June 6. Approval for borrowing the office and equipment will also be suspended.

Student organizations with urgent requests during Weeks 15 and 16 should follow subsequent arrangements from the Student Association Federation and Youth League Committee. To protect personal privacy, the student contact details in the original notice are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Association Federation)
