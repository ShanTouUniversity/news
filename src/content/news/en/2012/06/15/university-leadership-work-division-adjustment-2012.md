It is reported that Shantou University has adjusted the division of responsibilities among its university leaders following changes in the leadership team. President Xu Xiaohu will oversee the university’s overall work. The Party secretary will oversee the Party’s overall work, assist with university administration, and supervise publicity, united front, and alumni affairs. The secretary’s name appears corrupted in the source notice and is not inferred here.

The other responsibilities are as follows:

| Leader | Main responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Executive President Gu Peihua | Assisting with university administration; teaching, finance, art education, and teaching and general education work in the Department of Social Sciences |
| Deputy Party Secretary Luo Nianchao | Organization, ideological and political education, student employment, the labor union, discipline inspection, supervision, auditing, and ideological and political education in the Department of Social Sciences |
| Vice President Wen Xiaobo | Research, discipline development, the “211 Project,” academic journals, and university-affiliated industries |
| Vice President Li Dan | Student development, admissions, residential colleges, the Physical Education Department, and the basketball team |
| Vice President Lin Danming | Party and administrative offices, international affairs, resources, security, networks, and information |
| Vice President Wulan Hasi | Human resources, graduate education, continuing education, and the library |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
