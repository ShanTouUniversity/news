It is reported that the STU Academic Affairs Office conducted completion reviews in June for selected undergraduate innovation projects approved in 2010. A total of 23 projects took part in the review. The notice also records two projects as terminated and one as deferred; all 23 projects participating in the review passed, including four rated excellent.

The review panel examined completion reports, project logs, and supporting materials such as papers, patents, physical products, videos, and photographs. It also considered each project’s objectives, implementation, innovations, students’ research experience, results, and use of funds during the defense. The office said that most projects followed their original plans and made good progress, while some achieved interim results including papers, patents, or awards.

Detailed project ratings and student information are available in the original OA attachment and are not reproduced here.
