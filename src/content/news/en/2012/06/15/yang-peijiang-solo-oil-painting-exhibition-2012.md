It is reported that “Sanwu Xiangcun: Yang Peijiang Solo Oil Painting Exhibition” will be held from June 18 to 30 at the Art Museum of STU’s Changjiang School of Arts and Design. The exhibition is organized by the Changjiang School of Arts and Design and is open to the university community and the public.

The exhibition will present Yang Peijiang’s recent oil paintings. Commentary on his work notes that it retains the foundations of traditional painting while using strongly expressive language to convey personal experience, forming a distinctive exploration between social themes and individual perception.

Born in Shantou in 1963, Yang Peijiang graduated from Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts in 1985. He is an associate professor and master’s supervisor at STU’s Changjiang School of Arts and Design, an adjunct professor at Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts, a member of the China Artists Association, and a council member of the Guangdong Artists Association. His work has appeared in national watercolor, pastel, and Chinese-painting exhibitions and is held by institutions including the National Art Museum of China and Guangdong Museum of Art.
