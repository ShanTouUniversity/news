It is reported that renovation of the STU New Administration Building has been completed. The contractor confirmed that the project meets relevant laws, construction standards, design documents, and contract requirements and submitted the completion report. Guangdong Construction Engineering Supervision Co., Ltd., the project’s supervision unit, verified that the project meets the conditions for completion acceptance.

The completion-acceptance meeting will be held at 15:00 on June 18 in the temporary venue under the south side of the first floor of the New Administration Building. Heads and relevant personnel from the project’s user, management, and supervisory units will attend. The process will include a meeting chaired by the developer; reports from the developer, survey, design, construction, and supervision units on contract performance and standards compliance; review of project archives; on-site quality inspection; and comments, formal acceptance opinions, and signatures from the professional and general acceptance groups.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Key Construction Projects Office)
