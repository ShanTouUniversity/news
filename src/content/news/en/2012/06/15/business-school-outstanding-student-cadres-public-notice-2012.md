It is reported that student cadres at STU Business School made strong contributions to student affairs and achieved positive results over the past year. Following recommendations by student organizations and classes and review by the school Party general branch and Youth League Committee, the school has proposed 47 students for the title of Outstanding Student Cadre.

Comments may be submitted to the Business School Youth League Committee in writing, in person, or by telephone by 17:00 on June 20. Feedback must include the commenter’s real name. Because all proposed recipients are students, their individual names are not published here. Telephone: `82902725`.
