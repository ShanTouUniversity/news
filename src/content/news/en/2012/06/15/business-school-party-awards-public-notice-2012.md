It is reported that the STU Business School Party general branch has proposed three students as Outstanding Party Affairs Workers and 13 students as Outstanding Communist Party Members based on their performance in study, work, daily life, and Party and non-Party activities. The proposal is now open for public comment.

Comments may be submitted to the Business School Party general branch in writing, in person, or by telephone by June 20. Feedback must include the commenter’s real name. Because all proposed awardees are students, their individual names are not published here. Telephone: `82903496`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School Party General Branch)
