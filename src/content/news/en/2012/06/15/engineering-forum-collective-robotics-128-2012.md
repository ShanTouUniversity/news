It is reported that the 128th STU Engineering Research Forum will be held from 10:00 to 11:00 on June 18 in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room. Dr. Kuyucu of the University of York in the United Kingdom will give the presentation.

The presentation will address “Autonomous collective robotics,” rendered in the source notice as “Autonmous collective robotics.” Engineering School Computer Science faculty members and other interested members of the university community are welcome to attend.
