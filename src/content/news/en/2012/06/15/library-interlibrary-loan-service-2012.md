It is reported that the Library of Shantou University issued a notice on June 15, 2012, to promote interlibrary loan and document delivery services.

To supplement the collection and better meet faculty and students’ needs, the Library has established cooperative relationships with multiple institutions across China. Details are available on the Library website under Services & Guidance → Interlibrary Loan / Document Delivery (http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/gjhj.aspx).

## Notes

1. Before submitting a request, please check the Library’s holdings (including print books and journals, databases and e-books) to confirm the needed material is not available, so as to avoid unnecessary waste;
2. Please comply with relevant national copyright laws and use materials appropriately.
