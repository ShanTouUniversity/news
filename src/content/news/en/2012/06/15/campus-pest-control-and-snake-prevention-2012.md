To strengthen campus plant pest control, STU Resource Management Office plans to spray designated green spaces, trees, fruit trees, and drainage areas across the campus, Longquan Garden, and the School of Continuing Education from June 18 through June 25. Faculty, staff, and students should take care around the work areas and must not pick or eat campus fruit.

| Date | Work areas |
| --- | --- |
| June 18 | Artificial lake area and sports field |
| June 19 | Area around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, and student residential areas |
| June 20 | Reservoir dam, Building 789, and Buildings A–B faculty residences |
| June 21 | Medical Building, New Teaching Building, and student residences G–J |
| June 22 | Area around the Campus Security Office, campus gates, campus roads, and outside the New Library |
| June 23 | Dining facilities and area around the Administration Building |
| June 24 | Route from the affiliated middle and primary schools to the Mental Health Center |
| June 25 | Longquan Garden and the School of Continuing Education |

The office will also place sulfur powder in residential areas in the coming days to further prevent snakes. People in the affected areas should follow on-site safety guidance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
