It is reported that the STU Graduate School Party general branch has proposed four Outstanding Party Affairs Workers and 25 Outstanding Communist Party Members for recognition following review by student Party branches and the college. The proposal is now open for public comment.

Comments may be submitted to the Graduate School Party general branch in writing, in person, or by telephone by 17:00 on June 20. Feedback must include the commenter’s real name. Because the proposed awardees come from student Party branches, their individual names are not published here. Telephone: `82904000`.
