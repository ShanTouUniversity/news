The first meeting of the Eighth STU Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee was held on June 15 to review master’s-degree applications from 744 graduate students. The applicants included 447 graduating graduate students, 268 professional-degree students, 20 candidates receiving degrees through equivalent academic qualifications, and nine in-service master’s candidates who were university teachers. In accordance with the national academic degree regulations, STU’s academic degree regulations, and related rules, the committee decided to confer the corresponding master’s degrees.

The degree categories and numbers are as follows:

| Degree category | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Master of Arts | 51 |
| Master of Education | 13 |
| Master of Laws | 4 |
| Master of Management | 60 |
| Master of Economics | 9 |
| Master of Science | 101 |
| Master of Engineering | 93 |
| Master of Medicine | 145 |
| Master of Business Administration professional degree | 13 |
| Professional degree in engineering | 25 |
| Engineering professional degree | 18 |
| Professional degree in clinical medicine | 212 |

Because the recipients are students, their individual names are not reproduced here.
