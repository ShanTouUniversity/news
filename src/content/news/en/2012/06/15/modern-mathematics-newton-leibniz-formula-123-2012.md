It is reported that the 123rd lecture in the STU Modern Mathematics Series will be held at 16:30 on June 18 in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). Professor Yin Jingxue of South China Normal University, a specially appointed professor under the Ministry of Education’s Changjiang Scholars Program, will speak on the Newton–Leibniz formula.

Professor Yin’s research focuses on nonlinear diffusion equations with degeneracy and other singularities. He has received the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars award and the Distinguished Young Scholar Award from the K. C. Wong Education Foundation, and serves as a member of a State Council Academic Degrees Committee discipline evaluation group. The lecture is open to the university community.
