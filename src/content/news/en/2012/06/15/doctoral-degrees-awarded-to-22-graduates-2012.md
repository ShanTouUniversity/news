The STU Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee decided on June 15 to award doctoral degrees to 22 graduating graduate students. The decision was issued as STU Academic Degrees Document No. 2 (2012).

Because the recipients are students, their individual names are not reproduced here. Other list information is available in the original document issued by the Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee)
