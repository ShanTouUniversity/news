It is reported that the Business School announced on June 15 the fourth batch of interns for Bank of China Shantou Branch.

The recruitment drew enthusiastic applications from postgraduate and undergraduate students. Following discussions with the leadership of Bank of China Shantou Branch, 48 students were selected for this round.

Selected students are required to gather at the University Motto Monument at 8:00 a.m. on July 2 for transport to the Bank for the internship. The Business School thanked all applicants and encouraged those not selected to stay tuned for further notices.
