It is reported that a provincial inspection team will visit Shantou University on June 19 to review the implementation of regulations related to Party conduct and clean government. A briefing and discussion will be held from 09:00 to 11:00 that morning in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center.

Each school and each administrative or teaching-support department is to arrange for one principal official to attend. Each school should also send one faculty representative. Attendee information must be submitted to the Discipline Inspection Office by the end of the morning on June 18. Contact: Huang Huan, `2346`, `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office)
