It is reported that, as part of its initiatives to promote excellence and develop learning-oriented Party branches, Changjiang School of Arts and Design has proposed four individuals for the school’s Outstanding Party Member title following a review by student Party branches. The recognition is intended to highlight exemplary Party members and their role in the university community.

Comments may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the school Party general branch by June 19. Feedback must include the commenter’s real name. Because the proposed awardees are student Party members, their individual names are not published here. Telephone: `82902187`.
