It is reported that Shantou University will conduct its 2012 student military training from July 2 through July 15. Under the university’s arrangements, each school and residential college is to assign participating students to military skills training classes of approximately 50 students each.

The completed participant rosters must be submitted to the Student Affairs Office by June 21 at `o_xsc@stu.edu.cn`. Individual student names are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
