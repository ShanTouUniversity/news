It is reported that the STU Admissions Office has selected 17 students as admissions consultation assistants after considering interview performance, individual abilities, and activity experience. They will support summer visits to students’ former secondary schools and related admissions outreach.

To ensure that admissions information is presented clearly, accurately, and consistently, the Admissions Office will hold training at 10:30 on June 17 in Lecture Room 201, Staircase Classroom E. Selected students are expected to attend on time and will receive the *Fieldwork Guide for Admissions Outreach* and other materials. Because all selected assistants are students, their individual names are not reproduced here.
