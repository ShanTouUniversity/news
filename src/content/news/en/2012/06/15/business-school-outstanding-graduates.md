It is reported that Shantou University's Business School has launched the selection of its Outstanding Graduate Scholarship for the Class of 2012, aimed at honoring graduates with excellent character and academics and encouraging students across the school to strive for excellence. Eligible graduates must submit their application forms by June 20; late applications will be treated as forfeiture.

## Selection Criteria

| Category | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Overall performance | All-round development in moral, academic and physical terms, with excellent grades |
| Scholarship record | Third-class scholarship or better for three consecutive years, including one first-class year or two second-class years |
| Thesis | Graduation thesis graded as excellent |
| Campus involvement | Served as a student cadre at college level or above (including class cadres) and participated in volunteer or public-welfare activities |

According to the notice issued by the Business School, all four conditions must be met simultaneously. Eligible students should send their application forms to the school's Youth League committee email within the deadline.

The school said the scholarship is intended to set role models and motivate current students to learn from outstanding peers. Class-of-2012 students who intend to apply but have not yet submitted materials are urged to do so promptly.
