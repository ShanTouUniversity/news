It is reported that STU Student Affairs Office plans to provide employment awards to 2012 main-campus undergraduate graduates who took proactive steps toward employment, in accordance with STU document No. 53 (2012). The related list is now open for public comment. The award standard and number of recipients are available only in the original OA attachment and are not inferred or reproduced here; student names and individual information are not published.

Units or individuals with comments may submit them in writing or in person to the Student Affairs Office Graduate Employment Guidance Center by 17:00 on June 20. Unit submissions must bear an official seal, while individual submissions must include the commenter’s real name. Telephone: `0754-82902357`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
