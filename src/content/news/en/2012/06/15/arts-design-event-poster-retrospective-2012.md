It is reported that Changjiang School of Arts and Design at STU will hold an activity poster retrospective from June 18 to 30 in the school’s first-floor exhibition hall. The exhibition is organized by Changjiang School of Arts and Design.

It will present 48 selected posters from the school’s exhibitions, seminars, workshops, lectures, and performances in recent years, showcasing the work of its design studios in the visual communication of campus cultural activities. Faculty members, students, and visitors are welcome.
