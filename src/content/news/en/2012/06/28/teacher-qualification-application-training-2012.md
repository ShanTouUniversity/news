It is reported that, to help faculty complete the application procedures for 2012 professional-title applications, the STU Personnel Office will hold training on the use of the teacher qualification application system at 15:30 on July 3 in Room 102 of Teaching Building E, with time set aside for discussion on title-application issues.

The training is arranged in accordance with the university's notice on 2012 professional-title application and review (STU [2012] No. 86). Applicant accounts and passwords will be distributed during the training. Faculty members planning to apply for a professional title this year are required to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
