It is reported that, with the approval of the relevant authorities, the College of Liberal Arts will renovate Room 307 in the Wenge Building. The project is commissioned by the university's Capital Construction Office to Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. The main works include removing the podium, installing aluminum alloy windows, patching and painting walls, patching and casting the terrazzo floor, and renovating parts of the power and low-voltage wiring.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Estimated budget | About 17,500 RMB |
| Planned schedule | Start on July 1, 2012; finish by August 20 |
| Contractor | Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Commissioning office | University Capital Construction Office |

The Resource Management Office and the Capital Construction Office remind the campus community that the construction may generate noise, vibration and dust. Pedestrians should stay alert, and no one may enter the construction site without the permission of the university's functional departments or the contractor, or linger nearby, for safety reasons.
