It is reported that, to commend graduates who excel in both character and scholarship, the College of Science of Shantou University has established an outstanding-graduate scholarship and carried out the selection of 2012 outstanding graduates in accordance with its evaluation rules. Following applications submitted by graduates, review by the college Youth League committee, and discussion by college leaders, 18 students have been proposed as the college's 2012 outstanding graduates, and the list is now being publicized within the college.

Any objections may be raised in writing, in person or by phone to the college or its Youth League committee before 10:00 on June 29 (individuals giving feedback are asked to sign their real names). Contact phone: 82902779; email: o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn.
