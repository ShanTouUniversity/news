It is reported that the STU Network and Information Center recently released a notice on the adjusted campus-card window hours for the summer vacation.

| Window | Arrangement |
|--------|-------------|
| Expert Apartments campus-card window | Open as usual on original working hours |
| Recharge window below the Second Canteen | Closed from July 5 to August 17, 2012; reopens August 20 |

Faculty, staff and students are reminded to use the self-service transfer machines for recharge. For card loss reporting and balance inquiries, they may call the voice self-service hotline at 82903414.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
