It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee has issued an appointment notice (STU Party Organization [2012] No. 7): following deliberation at the fifth meeting of the university Party Committee Standing Committee in 2012, the university has appointed Ji Rongxin to an intermediate-level management position in the department, with a six-month probationary period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, Party Committee)
