It is reported that, in accordance with the notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on assessing the fifth and sixth batches of "Thousand-Hundred-Ten Project" participants and selecting the seventh batch (Guangdong Teacher Letter [2012] No. 57), Shantou University has publicized its proposed nominees for the seventh batch of the project, which were determined through individual applications, unit recommendations, and deliberation and voting by the review committee. Feedback is now being solicited from units on the main campus.

| Level | Proposed nominees |
|-------|-------------------|
| National level (1) | Li *Ping (one character of the name garbled in the original notice) |
| Provincial level (6) | Bu Xiangzhi, Du Hong, Fan * (name partly garbled in the original notice), Hu Zhong, Lin Quan, Yang Weifeng |
| University level (15) | Cai Lingru, Cai Zemin, Chen Yindong, Fan Lisheng, Gao Qingrong, Jiang Dazhi, Li Pengtao, Song Xiaohong, Wei Chuliang, Wu Fupei, Yu Chengjie, Yu Ying, Yuan Ye, Zhang Xiaoni, Zheng Muqiang |

Units or individuals with objections may raise them in writing to the Personnel Office before July 4. Contact phone: 82902348; email: rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
