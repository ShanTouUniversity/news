It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts of Shantou University recently issued a document (Liberal Arts [2012] No. 2) announcing the selection and admission results of its exchange programme with Dublin City University (DCU) in Ireland.

To select participants objectively and fairly, the college set up a review panel headed by Dean Terry Bodenhorn, with seven members including Professor Don Snow, Director of the English Language Centre, college Party and administrative leaders, department heads and faculty representatives. On the afternoon of May 2, ten students who had passed the preliminary selection sat a written test organized by the college. The panel then assessed the candidates who attended the interviews on overall qualities such as English proficiency, analytical ability, leadership, personality and special strengths.

Combining the reading and writing results of the written test with the interview scores, the college selected four students from the 2010 English major cohort (Lu M, Peng QH, Zhou ZR and Zhu HX) and one student from the 2010 Teaching Chinese as a Foreign Language cohort (Xu D) as the officially shortlisted participants, with one student of the 2010 Chinese Language and Literature cohort (Xing SL) as the first alternate and another (Chen LF) as the second alternate.

On May 7, the college sent formal confirmation notices to the shortlisted students and collected their feedback, and later submitted the materials of the five shortlisted students to DCU for review in accordance with the relevant agreement. On June 15, DCU issued formal admission letters to the five students. They will travel to Dublin City University in the fall 2012 semester for a half-year exchange study.
