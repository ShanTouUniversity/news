It is reported that the Shantou University Library recently issued its E-Resource Digest for Issue 5 of 2012 (General No. 5), covering five service adjustments:

| Resource | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Duxiu (读秀) database | Summer roaming access enabled: new users should register a "My Library" account within the campus IP range and click the verification link, while existing users should log in on campus and click the verification link to bind their account; the account can then be used off campus during the summer via "Summer Account Login" |
| PKU Law (Educational Channel) | Trial of the "National Judicial Examination Online Mock Test System," which includes simulated exams, exam outlines, essential laws and regulations, and judicial documents; trial ends September 30, 2012 |
| RESSET Financial Research Database | Trial extended: the platform covers stocks, fixed income, funds, macroeconomics, industries, economic and legal information, Hong Kong stocks, foreign exchange, futures, gold and more; trial ends September 30, 2012 |
| KUKE Digital Music Library | Audio quality upgraded from 64K/128K to 192K, with formats switched from WMA to MP3; readers who register a personal account within the campus IP range can access the library off campus free of charge in July and August (downloads not available off campus) |
| "Knowledge View" Video Library | Trial extended: the library offers tens of thousands of hours of educational science videos organized by discipline, covering sciences, engineering, agriculture, medicine, history and literature; trial ends January 31, 2013 |

Access links for each database are available on the corresponding database detail pages of the library website.
