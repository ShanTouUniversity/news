To improve the student-dormitory environment and reduce the impact of mosquitoes, flies, rats, cockroaches, and other pests on study and daily life, STU Student Community Management Service Center is collecting reports from students.

Students who find pests in or around their dormitories may complete the *Student Dormitory Pest Registration Form* at their building’s duty room. They may also complete the electronic form and email it to `dorm@stu.edu.cn`. The Student Community Management Service Center will arrange professional pest-control treatment based on the reports.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Community Management Service Center and Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original notice in the OA system for the dormitory pest form and electronic template.
