The international forum for the Qipu Plan pilot project, “Rural Women’s Leadership: Experience and Policy,” will be held on June 28 in STU’s Library Lecture Hall. The forum is co-hosted by the All-China Women’s Federation and the Li Ka Shing Foundation and hosted by STU. It will exchange the Qipu Plan’s ideas and methods, draw on experience from China and abroad, and examine diverse ways to use technology and information to advance women’s empowerment and gender equality.

Topics will include policy-making and capacity building for rural women’s leadership, village governance and gender mainstreaming, information and communications technology for women’s empowerment, and resource development and practical pathways for women’s development and educational empowerment. Speakers will include representatives of UN Women and UNESCO in China, leaders of Indian research and participatory-development organizations, the World Vision China representative, All-China Women’s Federation officials, and Chinese experts in women’s studies.

The forum will run in two sessions, from 09:00 to 12:30 and from 14:40 to 17:40. It will be conducted in Chinese and English with simultaneous interpretation. STU has reserved 50 places for faculty members and students. Registration is available through the [STU Activity Booking System](http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ActivityBooking.aspx) until 15:00 on June 26.
