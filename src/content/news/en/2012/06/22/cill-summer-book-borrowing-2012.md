CILL, the Language Independent Learning Center under STU’s English Language Center, is located on the left side of the first floor of the New Library and holds more than 1,000 foreign-language books. Faculty members and students with a valid campus card may borrow the books, normally for a two-week period.

To support summer reading, books borrowed from June 22 through August 18 may be returned by September 1. The regular two-week loan period will resume after August 18. The university encourages faculty members and students to use CILL’s foreign-language collection during the summer.
