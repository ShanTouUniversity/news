STU Student Affairs Office has announced arrangements for employment procedures for 2012 graduates at the main campus. From June 25 to 29, graduates who applied for deferred employment should sign the deferred-employment agreement at the Career Guidance Center in Room 201 of the Student Affairs Office Building. Graduates who did not apply for deferred employment and are not continuing their studies should collect their employment registration certificates. Graduates continuing their studies, including overseas study, should promptly submit a file-transfer letter or admission notice and sign the *Guangdong Form for Graduates Continuing Study and Not Participating in Employment*.

| Date | Time | School or matter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 25 | 14:30–17:00 | School of Science |
| June 26 | 09:00–11:30; 14:30–17:00 | Engineering School; Engineering School and School of Humanities |
| June 27 | 09:00–11:30; 14:30–17:00 | Graduate School; Business School |
| June 28 | 09:00–11:30 | School of Journalism and School of Law |
| June 29 | 09:30–11:30 | School of Arts |

Graduates should check the information on their registration certificates and contact the Career Guidance Center immediately if they find an error. A certificate may be collected by an authorized person, who must promptly deliver it to the graduate. Graduates must report to the destination address within the prescribed period. Students who have not completed graduation, including those still taking courses or whose graduation status is not yet confirmed, should not collect a certificate; if one has already been collected, it must be returned to the Career Guidance Center, or another certificate may not be issued at normal graduation. These procedures affect the destination of graduate files and should be handled carefully.

Career Guidance Center: `0754-82902357`. The deferred-employment roster is not reproduced here.
