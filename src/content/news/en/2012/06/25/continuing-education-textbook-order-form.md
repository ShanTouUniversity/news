It is reported that Shantou University's Continuing Education Center issued a notice on June 25 through the university's Human Resources Office, arranging for faculty and staff to collect the textbook order forms for continuing education required courses.

Required (public-need) subjects are a compulsory component of continuing education for professional and technical personnel. The newly released order forms allow faculty and staff who need to order or collect required-course textbooks to check their details and complete the process.

Since the original notice did not include specifics such as collection times or locations, interested faculty and staff are advised to contact the Human Resources Office or the Continuing Education Center directly for further arrangements, and to watch for follow-up announcements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
> ⚠️ The original order form and collection details are available on the OA system.
