Following the first student-dormitory forum on June 20 and the on-site dormitory investigation and improvement-measure discussions on June 21, STU will hold a second forum to gather student feedback on the measures.

The forum will take place from 19:30 to 21:00 on June 26 in Science and Technology Building Room 205. In addition to the 50 students announced on June 21, the university will admit 20 more students. Students whose previous registrations were unsuccessful, as well as others who wish to participate, may register at the venue with their campus cards or student IDs. Admission will continue until all seats are filled.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office and Student Affairs Office)
