The Guangdong Provincial Government Development Research Center has launched the public tender for the 2012 major decision-consulting research projects of Guangdong Province. It is reported that five topics are on offer this year, each funded at 150,000 yuan, and Shantou University's Research Affairs Office has made arrangements for on-campus bids.

The five topics are:

| No. | Topic | Funding |
|-----|-------|---------|
| 1 | Research on Cultivating and Developing Social Organizations in Guangdong and Related Policies | 150,000 yuan |
| 2 | Trends in Foreign Capital Inflow to Guangdong During the 12th Five-Year Plan and Countermeasures | 150,000 yuan |
| 3 | Leveraging Guangdong's Agricultural S&T Resources to Advance Agricultural Modernization | 150,000 yuan |
| 4 | Ideas and Countermeasures for Promoting Guangdong's Economy Through Cross-Border RMB Investment and Financing | 150,000 yuan |
| 5 | Paths and Countermeasures for the Transformation and Upgrading of Guangdong's Circulation Industry | 150,000 yuan |

Any unit and its researchers with the relevant research capability and conditions may bid. On-campus applicants must complete the Application Form for the Social Tender of Guangdong Major Decision-Consulting Research Projects and the Research Outline, submit hard copies to the Research Affairs Office by July 15, 2012, and send electronic versions to ljwang@stu.edu.cn.

Campus contacts: Huang Yumei and Wang Liju, tel: 82902563.
