It is reported that the Department of Social Sciences issued a notice on June 25, 2012, assigning a summer social survey report.

The survey serves as a practical component of the *Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics* course, with the report counting toward the regular grade.

## Survey Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | My Hometown, My Home |
| Participants | Undergraduates of the Classes of 2009, 2010 and 2011 taking the course in fall 2012 |
| Period | Summer 2012, with reports due to instructors at the end of October 2012 |
| Requirements | Approximately 3,000 words on a self-chosen topic under the theme, formatted according to the attached template |
| Organizer | Department of Social Sciences |

Students concerned are asked to complete and submit the report on time.

> ⚠️ The report template is available on the OA system.
>
