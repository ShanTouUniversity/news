Under the *STU Engineering School Regulations on Awards for Student Extracurricular Scientific and Technological Innovation Activities (2011 Revision)*, the Engineering School Student Academic and Technology Works Leading Group has completed its review for 2011–2012. After discussion and approval by the school’s affairs committee, the school proposes to award Innovation Medals to selected students and confer the title of Outstanding Supervisor on 11 teachers.

The proposed honors are now open for comments. Objections may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Engineering School Youth League Committee before the noon workday deadline on June 26. Telephone: `82902986`. Student and teacher names are not reproduced here.
