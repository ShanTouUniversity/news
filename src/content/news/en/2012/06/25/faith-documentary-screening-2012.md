To strengthen education in the socialist core value system, with a focus on ideals and convictions, the STU Organization and United Front Department has circulated arrangements for viewing the three-part theoretical documentary *Faith*. The film was jointly produced by the Organization Department of the CPC Central Committee, the Central Compilation and Translation Bureau, the Party History Research Center, and China Central Television. Each episode is approximately 50 minutes long. STU units are asked to organize faculty and student Party members and Party applicants to watch it.

| Platform | Time or access method |
| --- | --- |
| CCTV General Channel | Episode 1 at 21:02 on June 24; Episodes 2 and 3 at 20:05 on June 25 |
| Guangdong Television News Channel | One episode daily at 13:00 from July 1 to 3 |
| Guangdong Party-member distance-education platform | Available on demand from July 1 under “Guangdong—Excellence Striving—Outstanding Communist Party Members” |
| Guangdong rural Party-member distance-education website | All three episodes available from July 1 at [gdycjy.gov.cn](http://www.gdycjy.gov.cn/) |

Broadcast times on China Education Television, the Chinese channel of Xinhua Television (CNC Chinese), Xinhua Network Television’s live channel, and People’s Daily Online should be checked through the National Party Cadre Distance Education website at [dygbjy.gov.cn](http://www.dygbjy.gov.cn). Units should report their viewing activities to the Organization and United Front Department at `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department)
