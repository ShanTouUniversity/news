To support graduate departure, STU Finance Office and Network and Information Center will settle and close the campus-card accounts of 2012 undergraduate and graduate students on July 6. Remaining balances will be returned to each graduate’s own Bank of China STU Branch account, with refunds scheduled to be completed by August 25.

Graduates whose previously registered bank account has been closed should log in to the [campus-card closure and refund website](http://smartcardcancel.stu.edu.cn) from June 25 to confirm and provide valid personal bank-account information and keep the account active until the refund is made. The university notes that student accounts do not need to be closed before graduation and will be automatically closed two years after the balance reaches zero. Passbook deposits at the Bank of China STU Branch can be accessed across Guangdong, while bank cards can be used nationwide.

Questions about campus-card balances: `82903413-200`; questions about refunds: Finance Office, `82902654`. Later university notices will prevail if the schedule changes. Personal bank-account information is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office and Network and Information Center)
