To prepare for the arrival of the 2012 freshman class, STU Student Community Management Service Center will begin cleaning and repairing vacant beds throughout the student dormitories on July 2. The work includes beds vacated by departing graduates.

Personal, non-university-provided items on vacant beds or within their projected areas will be removed. Students should place personal items, chairs, and movable cabinets beneath their assigned beds to prevent mistaken removal. Vacant beds have been marked and registered, and no one may change bed assignments without approval. Errors in bed markings should be reported to the management staff at `82902661`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Community Management Service Center and Resource Management Office)
