STU’s Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design will hold its 2012 graduation send-off and sixth Hanwei Taihe Design Award ceremony at 19:30 on June 26 in the school’s art museum. The event will celebrate graduating students and showcase their creative work.

The program will include performances and exchange activities. A panel formed by Hanwei Taihe representatives and alumni will make the final assessment of 19 shortlisted graduation works, with the award results announced during the reception. Dean Wang Shouzhi, school faculty, company representatives, and some alumni will attend.

The award is co-hosted by the Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design and Hanwei Taihe Brand Design Consulting Co., Ltd., and organized by the school’s Student Union. It selects outstanding graduation works by graduating students and supports exchange between design education and professional practice.
