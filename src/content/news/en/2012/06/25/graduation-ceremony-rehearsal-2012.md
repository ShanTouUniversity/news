It is reported that the President’s Office and the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 25, 2012, to hold the rehearsal for the 2012 Graduation and Awards Ceremony on June 28 in the gymnasium.

## Rehearsal Schedule

| Group | Time | Venue | Participants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | 16:30, June 28, 2012 | Gymnasium | Wind band, choir and etiquette team members and instructors, selected members of the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Training program, and volunteers |
| Group 2 | 19:30, June 28, 2012 | Gymnasium | Recipients of the Teaching Excellence Award, Outstanding Service Award, STU Medal and Sportsmanship Medal, doctoral degree recipients (in doctoral gowns), and deans of each college |
| Support | On time | Gymnasium | Relevant staff from the President’s Office, Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, News Center, Network and Information Center, Sports Department and Resource Management Office, as well as counselors responsible for graduation matters |

Relevant personnel are asked to assemble on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (President’s Office, Student Affairs Office)
