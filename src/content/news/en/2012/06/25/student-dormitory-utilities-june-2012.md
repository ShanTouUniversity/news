Under the 2012 graduate-departure schedule, STU Water and Electricity Center read student-dormitory meters on June 24. The measurement period for this bill is June 1–24, and the Network and Information Center plans to charge campus-card accounts on June 27. Utilities used after June 25 will be billed separately. Graduates must settle their utility charges as part of departure procedures on June 28–29.

Students may check bills through the [STU Utilities Management System](http://power.stu.edu.cn/) using their campus-network accounts. Those who identify an error or have questions should complete and sign the *Utilities-Bill Verification Application* and visit the Water and Electricity Center from June 25 to 26. The center is beside the Science and Technology Building and can be reached at `2489`. Students whose accommodation information differs from university records may contact the counselor office in Building B Room 106 at `3355`.

Students with insufficient campus-card balances should top up promptly, especially graduating students, to avoid disrupting departure procedures.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office, Water and Electricity Center, and Network and Information Center)
