STU Network and Information Center has announced arrangements for the campus-network accounts, unified authentication, and university email accounts of 2012 undergraduate and graduate students.

| Service | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Internet access through campus-network accounts | Disabled on July 6 |
| Unified authentication through campus-network accounts | Retained until August 20; after that, the units responsible for systems such as the credit-based and utilities systems will adjust graduate authentication permissions |
| University email | Closed on August 20, with stored messages deleted; graduates should transfer important messages in advance |
| Alumni email | Already available; graduates may register and confirm through the [alumni website](http://xyh.stu.edu.cn) |

Graduates who remain at STU for employment will have their campus-network accounts and university email handled through the personnel onboarding process. Support: `82903413-200`, `support@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
