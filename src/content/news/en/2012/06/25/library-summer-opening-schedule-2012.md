Under STU’s summer-break arrangements, the library will operate on a reduced schedule during the staff-rotation period from July 4 to August 15. It will open from 09:00 to 17:00 on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, specifically July 4, 6, 9, 11, 13, 16, 18, 20, 23, 25, 27, and 30, and August 1, 3, 6, 8, 10, 13, and 15.

To conserve electricity, the special-collections area, reading halls, and seminar rooms will be closed during the on-campus student summer period from July 1 to August 18. The original notice also states that normal opening begins on August 16, which overlaps with the stated closure through August 18 for those areas; readers should follow the library’s latest arrangement.
