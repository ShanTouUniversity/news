It is reported that registration for STU’s 2012 summer “Three Rural Areas” social-practice program has concluded. First-round interviews will be held on June 2 in Classroom D402, with Classroom D401 serving as the waiting area. On-site registration will also be accepted. Applicants must attend according to the staggered schedule in the attachment and check in 10 minutes before their assigned slot.

Each applicant will give a one-minute self-introduction, and interviewers will ask questions about the relevant team’s professional knowledge and abilities. Applicants for the arts and performance team may bring instruments or backing tracks, with performances limited to one or two minutes. Medical-team applicants will take a practical second-round interview involving blood-pressure measurement and bandaging. Publicity-team applicants may describe experience with image-editing software such as Adobe Photoshop or Adobe Illustrator, or with hand-drawn posters.

After the first round, the second-round roster will be published in the youth-volunteer section of the BBS. Team leaders will arrange the second-round interviews themselves. The detailed staggered schedule is provided in the original OA attachment; student personal contact information is not reproduced here.
