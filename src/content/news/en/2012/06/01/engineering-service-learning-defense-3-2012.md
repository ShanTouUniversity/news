It is reported that STU Engineering School’s third service-learning project defense will be held from 16:10 to 18:00 on June 1 in Room D403.

Each project must send at least two members to attend the defense and give a four-minute presentation using PowerPoint. Participants are asked to dress appropriately. Detailed project arrangements should follow the Engineering School’s notice.
