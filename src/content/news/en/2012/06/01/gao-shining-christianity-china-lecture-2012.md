It is reported that STU’s CCS Holistic Life Education public lecture series will host Gao Shining, a researcher at the Institute of World Religions of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, at 19:30 on June 7 in the New Library Lecture Hall. She will discuss the development and social situation of Christianity in contemporary China.

Starting from the more than two-century history of Protestant Christianity in China, the lecture will consider the number of Christians in contemporary China, the distribution and development of Christianity, Christians’ social position, and public understanding of the religion. It will offer a perspective on the relationship between religion and contemporary Chinese society.

Gao is among the earliest scholars in mainland China to translate and research the sociology of religion. Gao’s books include *Sociology of Religion* and *Christianity and Christians in Contemporary Beijing*, and Gao has translated multiple works on religious sociology and religious thought.
