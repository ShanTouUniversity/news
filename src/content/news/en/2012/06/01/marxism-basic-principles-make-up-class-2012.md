It is reported that Class 52187, Marxism Fundamentals, will hold a make-up session because the class taught by Cheng Jiaming of the Social Sciences Department was suspended during periods A–C on Monday of Week 12, May 14. The make-up class is scheduled for periods 8–10 on Wednesday of Week 15, June 6, in Classroom E207.

The Academic Affairs Office asks enrolled students to inform one another and attend according to the revised schedule.
