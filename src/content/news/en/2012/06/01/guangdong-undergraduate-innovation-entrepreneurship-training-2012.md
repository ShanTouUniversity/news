It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office and Student Affairs Office have opened applications for the 2012 Guangdong Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program to strengthen students’ innovation and entrepreneurship abilities. The program includes innovation training, entrepreneurship training, and entrepreneurial practice. Innovation training focuses on student-led research design, implementation, and exchange of results. Entrepreneurship training covers business plans, feasibility studies, simulated enterprise operations, and entrepreneurship reports. Entrepreneurial practice uses earlier innovation results to develop and test market-oriented products or services with guidance from university and enterprise mentors.

Projects are generally submitted by teams. An innovation-training team may have no more than five members, and each student may participate in only one project. Cross-disciplinary, cross-major, and cross-year cooperation is encouraged. Each project must have one or two supervising teachers and lasts 18 months. Leaders of innovation-training and entrepreneurship-training projects must complete them before graduation.

Schools and colleges will organize applications and reviews for innovation-training projects from June 1 through June 28. The university will review entrepreneurship-training and entrepreneurial-practice projects, with applications due by 17:00 on June 22. After a supervisor signs the application, innovation-training materials should be submitted through the relevant school or college to the Academic Affairs Office, while entrepreneurship-training and entrepreneurial-practice materials go directly to that office. Three printed copies and one electronic copy should be submitted; electronic copies go to `zhailiu@stu.edu.cn`. Consultation: `82902736`.

Projects generally receive RMB 10,000; entrepreneurial-practice projects may receive a higher budget according to their scale. The university will release 50 percent after approval and the remainder after a successful midterm review. Funds are earmarked and may not be used for other purposes. Projects must undergo midterm review and final acceptance, and delayed or terminated projects receive no additional or remaining funding.

The notice requires students to lead their projects and independently complete design, implementation records, and final reports. Changes to project content require a written application in advance. Projects delayed or terminated without valid reasons will lose funding; their members may not apply again for two years, and their supervising teachers may not supervise related projects during that period.
