It is reported that, with approval from the university Youth League Committee, STU’s Chuàng Path to Success Network will hold its 12th board election from 15:00 to 18:00 on June 6 in the stepped Classroom E201 of the Teaching Building. The meeting will review the 11th board’s annual work and elect members of the next board.

The 12th board is planned to include one chief executive officer, one chief technology officer, one chief operating officer, and one chief administrative officer. Candidates are being openly recruited from students across the university and will be selected through voluntary application, eligibility review, assessment interviews, and review by the Youth League Committee. Candidates will deliver public speeches, present their plans, and answer questions at the election. The 11th board’s full membership will vote, and the candidate with the most votes for each post will be elected.

Applications close at 22:00 on June 4. Applicants must submit an application form and personal work plan; the submission channel is provided in the original OA notice. Student personal email addresses are not reproduced here.
