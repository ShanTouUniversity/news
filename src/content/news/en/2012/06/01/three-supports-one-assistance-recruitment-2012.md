It is reported that the Guangdong Party Committee Organization Department, Department of Education, and seven other departments have jointly issued the 2012 Guangdong Implementation Plan for the “Three Supports and One Assistance” program. The plan recruits university graduates to provide teaching, agricultural, medical, and poverty-relief services at rural grassroots level.

STU graduates interested in applying should prepare materials under the implementation plan and submit them by June 6 to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center, Room 201 of the Student Affairs Office Building. The university will organize the applications centrally.

Detailed eligibility requirements, document lists, and implementation arrangements should follow the original OA attachment.
