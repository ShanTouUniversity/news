It is reported that the fifth session of STU’s Science School Career Workshop will be held from 19:30 to 21:30 on June 5 in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic and Cultural Center. The event will focus on entrepreneurship across majors and career planning.

Wang Danxu, an alumnus of STU’s 1989 foreign-language cohort and general manager of Shantou Andeli Environmental Protection Technology Co., Ltd., will share professional and entrepreneurial experience, including how to respond to setbacks, identify opportunities, and set a direction during an entrepreneurial career. Zhang Baozhong, a master of science from Xiamen University and a researcher at the China Zhouyi Fengshui Research Institute, will join the interactive discussion and offer career-planning commentary from a Zhouyi perspective.

The event is open to Science School students and faculty.
