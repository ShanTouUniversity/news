It is reported that the Student Affairs Office announced on June 1, 2012, the results of the 7th Mental Health Awareness Month at Shantou University.

Organized by the Student Affairs Office and undertaken by the Psychology Growth Association, the month-long campaign concluded successfully. Results were announced for three competitions: the “Voice Born from Faith” dubbing contest, the “Image Born from Heart” photography contest and the psychodrama competition. The full lists of winners, hosts and guest performers are available in the OA attachments.
