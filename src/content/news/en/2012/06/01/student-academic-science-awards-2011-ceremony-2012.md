It is reported that STU’s 2011 Student Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activity Summary and Awards Ceremony will be held at 19:30 on June 3 in the New Library Lecture Hall.

The meeting will review the year’s student extracurricular academic and scientific activities and recognize outstanding individuals and groups. It will also honor students who won awards in STU’s eighth Challenge Cup Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Competition and the STU selection round of the eighth Guangdong Challenge Cup Undergraduate Entrepreneurship Competition.

Members of the university Student Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities Leading Group, relevant school and college coordinators, Youth League officials, representatives of winning groups, individuals, and projects, and student representatives will attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Student Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities Leading Group and Communist Youth League Committee)
