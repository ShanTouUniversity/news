It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 1, 2012, announcing reservation results for the X-Project “Muse Music Community” exchange with Golden Melody award-winning singer-songwriter Zeng Yan.

Following the event announcement, 193 faculty and students successfully made reservations by the deadline; the list is in the OA attachment and withheld here for privacy.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | X-Project “Muse Music Community” — Exchange with Zeng Yan |
| Time | 20:00, June 3, 2012 (admission from 19:30) |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Science and Technology Center |
| Reservations | 193 faculty and students |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

Those with successful reservations are asked to attend on time.

> ⚠️ The reservation list is available on the OA system.
