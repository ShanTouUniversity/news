It is reported that the fourth session of STU’s Science School Career Workshop, “A Different Kind of University Experience: STU-UTEP China-U.S. Student Exchange Sharing,” will be held from 09:00 to 11:00 on June 3 in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic and Cultural Center. The event is open to first-, second-, and third-year Science School students.

Outstanding Science School graduates and exchange students from The University of Texas at El Paso will share experiences in academic study, research, and university planning from Chinese and U.S. student perspectives. The graduates’ paths include mathematics at the University of Macau, optical engineering at STU, and applied chemistry at STU. One graduate entered a direct doctoral program in biochemistry and molecular biology at China Agricultural University and was nominated for the 2012 STU Medal. UTEP exchange students will contribute to the cross-cultural learning exchange.

The event is organized by the Science School Youth League Committee as part of the university’s “Strengthen Study Culture for Better Growth and Development” program.
