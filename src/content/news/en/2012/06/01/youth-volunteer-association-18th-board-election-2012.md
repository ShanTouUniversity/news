It is reported that the STU Youth Volunteer Association held its 18th board election on the evening of May 26 in the Science Lecture Hall. The meeting approved the 17th board’s work report and elected the 18th board through a competitive election.

The new board includes Deng YL as president and Lei XT, Ji H, Hu YQ, and Jian YM as vice presidents. The student names are abbreviated under the privacy rules, and years and majors are not published.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association)
