It is reported that STU’s Muse Girl Self-Development project, part of the Student Community X Program, has completed three editions of training on self-awareness, skin care and makeup, color and clothing coordination, composure, breathing meditation, and psychological adjustment. The project’s sharing session will be held from 14:30 to 16:30 on June 6 in Meeting Room 205 of the Science Center.

Outstanding participants from earlier editions and representatives of the management team will discuss their growth experiences. Topics include “Confidence in the Ordinary,” “Accepting Imperfection,” “My University, My Choice,” and “How My Confidence Changed After the Training.” The student speakers are abbreviated as Li SJ, Hu YQ, Yan JJ, and Huang XF under the privacy rules.

Mary Kay senior trainer Xie Qiugui will provide on-site consultation, and Lan Na, a teacher at the Student Affairs Office Student Quality Development Center, will join the informal discussion. Students are welcome to attend.
