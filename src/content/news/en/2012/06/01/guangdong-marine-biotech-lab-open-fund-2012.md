It is reported that the Guangdong Marine Biotechnology Key Laboratory based at Shantou University issued its 2012 open project fund guide on June 1, inviting researchers from domestic and overseas universities and research institutes, especially young and middle-aged scholars, to apply.

Approved in April 1993 and passing acceptance in December 1995, the laboratory consists of a 2,000-square-meter on-campus facility and the Nan'ao Coastal Experimental Station covering over 130 mu of land and about 10 hectares of sea area. With advanced instruments valued at nearly 30 million yuan, it focuses on characteristic marine resources of eastern Guangdong.

## Research Directions

| Direction | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Marine economic animal biology | Biology, breeding, nutrition and feed, seedling propagation, disease control and immunology of economically important fish, shellfish and crustaceans |
| Seaweed biology and utilization | Taxonomy and diversity of large seaweeds, aquaculture engineering, ecophysiology, bio-invasion and intertidal vegetation restoration |
| Marine eco-environment and healthy aquaculture | Coastal ecosystem health assessment, bioremediation, conservation management, disease prevention, phytoplankton ecology and biodiversity |

## Application

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Proposal | Fill in the Open Project Application Form with clear objectives and innovative content; team must include 1–2 members of the key lab |
| Eligibility | Formal employee with intermediate title or above or a doctoral degree, with sufficient time for research |
| Encouraged | Use of the laboratory's instruments and facilities |
| Period | June 1–30, 2012, review completed by end of July |
| Funding | 20,000–30,000 yuan per project, 4–5 projects, duration 1–2 years |
| Submission | 3 printed copies with official seal plus electronic version |
