STU’s Student Affairs Office has published procedures for 2012 graduates with national student loans or outstanding fees to collect their graduation and degree certificates. Eligible graduates may visit the Student Aid Management Center in Room 102 of the Student Affairs Office from the afternoon of June 28 through June 29.

Graduates who have signed a repayment agreement with Bank of China should complete items 1 through 9 on the departure checklist and bring the original identity document to collect duplicate certificates, the relevant proof, and the repayment agreement. Those who have not signed an agreement should first contact the bank to complete repayment confirmation. Graduates with outstanding fees, including graduates whose national student loans have not covered all tuition due, must obtain university approval for deferred payment and sign a repayment agreement before collection.

Graduates who settled their loan or fees after June 20 may collect the original certificates with a completed departure checklist, original identity document, and settlement notice or receipt. Loan graduates who submitted a settlement notice to the Student Aid Management Center on or before June 20 may collect the originals from their schools. Graduates continuing to domestic graduate study or a second degree who need to extend their loan term must bring their admission notice, identity document, loan contract, repayment agreement, and other materials to Bank of China Shantou Branch by June 30 to sign a supplemental extension agreement. Otherwise, repayment will begin according to the contract.

Graduates who authorize another person to collect certificates must provide an authorization letter and copies of both parties’ identity documents. Student Aid Management Center: `0754-82904749`. Bank of China Shantou Branch Personal Finance Department: 98 Jinsha Road, Shantou; `0754-88949456` and `0754-88949407`.
