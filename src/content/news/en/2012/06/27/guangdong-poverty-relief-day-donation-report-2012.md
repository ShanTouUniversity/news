STU faculty members and students responded to the Guangdong and Shantou governments’ call to support Guangdong Poverty-Relief Day and took part in charitable giving. As of June 27, the university had received 62,358.6 yuan in donations.

The Staff Union said that department staff unions would continue collecting contributions from faculty and staff and consolidating them through the university union. The campaign promotes public service and support for people in need; detailed donation figures remain subject to the union’s records and subsequent announcements.
