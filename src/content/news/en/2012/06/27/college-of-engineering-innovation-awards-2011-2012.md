It is reported that the College of Engineering issued a commendation decision on June 27 to recognize individuals who achieved outstanding results in student extracurricular academic innovation activities during the 2011–2012 academic year.

Over the past year, faculty and students actively participated in competitions including the 5th National Undergraduate Mechanical Innovation Design Competition (Guangdong Division) and the 10th Guangdong Collegiate Programming Contest (GPCDC 2012), earning excellent results. In accordance with the Regulations on Rewards for Student Extracurricular Academic Innovation Activities of the College of Engineering (2011 Revision), and following review by the College's leadership group for student innovation works and approval by the College Affairs Committee, the College decided to award the "Innovation Medal" to students including Huang GS and to confer the title of "Outstanding Supervisor" on 11 faculty members including Chen Shaoke. Detailed lists are provided in the attachments.

The College called on all faculty and students to learn from the honorees and to continue striving to cultivate high-quality talent characterized as "aspirational, knowledgeable, persistent, and accomplished" and to contribute further to the College's development.
