It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a document (STU [2012] No. 88) laying out the arrangements for the 2012 reception of new students. Undergraduate freshmen of the 2012 intake will register on September 2, and the reception work will run from September 2 to September 16, with the detailed schedule available in the attachment to the notice.

**Reception arrangements**: On September 2, three reception stations will operate in the city, including the exit of Shantou Railway Station (covering the coach terminal), handled by the Resource Management Office; the bus stop at the campus gate, handled by the Student Union; and Shantou General Coach Station, handled by the Medical College. Colleges will set up reception points on the pedestrian street from 08:00 to 22:00 on September 2 to process registration (moved to the ground floor under the Administration Center in case of rain). Dormitories will accept freshmen from September 1, and the Student Community Management and Service Center will run an overnight duty station for new students.

Freshmen should bring their admission notice, ID documents, personal files and household-registration transfer certificate, then collect their dormitory assignment and check in at the duty room of their dormitory building; arrangements for collecting Party and Youth League membership materials will be announced separately. On September 2, a "green channel" for registration and a national student-loan consultation point will be set up on the pedestrian street, and a vendor arranged by the Academic Affairs Office will sell earphones for the English test in the lobby of Building E on a voluntary basis. Without permission, no individual or organization may set up stalls or hang purely commercial banners or posters on campus.

**Division of responsibilities**: The Party and Administration Office will coordinate the opening ceremony and university-wide events; the Student Affairs Office will coordinate the orientation schedule, education on school rules, and SLC visits; the Academic Affairs Office will handle credit-system briefings, course-selection guidance, computer placement tests and registration; the English Language Centre will arrange the English enhancement briefing and the freshmen English placement test (scheduled for September 4); the Security Office will handle household-registration transfers and security; the University Hospital will conduct physical examinations and provide emergency medical services; and the dormitory counselors' office will arrange accommodation. The Network and Information Center, Library, Department of Physical Education, Publicity Department, Organization and United Front Work Department, Youth League Committee, Admissions Office, colleges and residential colleges all have defined roles.

**Requirements**: Relevant units must enter the timetables for the English placement test, credit-system guidance, integrative-thinking training, library orientation, physical examinations and college activities into the student affairs system by August 24. Reception staff must wear work badges. Colleges should verify each freshman's admission notice, exam admission ticket, ID and file photo, and contact the Admissions Office promptly when any information is questionable. Anyone found to have obtained admission through fraud or impersonation will have their admission revoked according to regulations, and the admissions authority of the province where the student took the exam will be notified.
