To keep campus roads moving during the Board of Trustees period, STU’s security department will implement temporary traffic controls in accordance with the relevant arrangements. From 00:00 on June 28 through 18:00 on June 29, vehicle access will be restricted around the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Academic Exchange Center. Only authorized vehicles will be exempt.

A checkpoint will be set up beside Building P, and affected vehicles may park in front of the building. Motor vehicles may not park on the pedestrian street or at either of its entrances. Drivers and campus community members should follow on-site instructions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Security Office)
