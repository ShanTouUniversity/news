It is reported that the STU Party and Administration Office recently issued a document (STU Administration [2012] No. 5) informing all university units of the status of 2011 filing work.

**Overall status**: A total of 43 filing units handed over 12,899 items of documents and materials. The quality of the documents and the accuracy of information entry improved compared with the previous year, and duplicate filing decreased.

| Category | Units |
|----------|-------|
| Units doing well in filing | Academic Affairs Office, Resource Management Office (Capital Construction Office), Youth League Committee, Graduate School, Department of Physical Education, Journal Editorial Office |
| Units with the greatest improvement | Trade Union, Student Affairs Office, College of Engineering, University Press |

The report also noted that a few units failed to file complete document sets, lacking important materials such as meeting minutes and memos; some documents had quality problems such as missing drafting sheets or incomplete seals; and a few units made errors in basic information entry, failed to attach electronic files, or completed filing after the deadline.

In response, the report restated filing requirements: part-time archivists should carefully study the "STU Scope of Archiving and Retention Periods for Various Records," collect materials completely, and upload them in a timely manner for archiving as they arise; before archiving they should check records already transferred to avoid duplication; documents should in principle be archived in both electronic and paper form, though electronic files of short-term, non-official documents need not be submitted in paper; and archived photos should be annotated in the records system with details such as event, time, photographer and location, with a resolution above 4,000×3,000 pixels and a size of about 4 MB, selecting several representative, well-taken photos for each event.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Party and Administration Office)
