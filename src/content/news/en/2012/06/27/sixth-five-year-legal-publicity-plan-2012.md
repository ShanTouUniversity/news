It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice publishing its "Sixth Five-Year Legal Publicity Work Plan," which sets out the university's legal publicity work for the sixth five-year campaign period.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Document | Sixth Five-Year Legal Publicity Work Plan of STU |
| Document No. | STU [2012] No. 87 |
| Release date | June 27, 2012 |
| Issuing office | Shantou University |
| Requirement | All units concerned should organize and implement the plan earnestly |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
>
