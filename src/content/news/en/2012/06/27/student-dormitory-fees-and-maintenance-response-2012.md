STU’s Party and Government Office and Student Affairs Office have issued a public response to student questions about dormitory fees and living conditions raised through channels including the university president’s mailbox. The university said that all construction funding for student dormitories came from donations by the Li Ka Shing Foundation and that accommodation fees were used mainly for dormitory maintenance and repairs.

According to the university’s figures, since 2004, renovation of Buildings A through F cost approximately 35 million yuan, routine repairs approximately 1.62 million yuan, property management approximately 4.44 million yuan, and water and electricity fee reductions approximately 18.91 million yuan, for total related expenditure of approximately 59.97 million yuan. Accommodation fees collected for Buildings A through F during the same period totaled approximately 39.67 million yuan.

The response said the university had improved services by strengthening public-area sanitation management and would publish a work plan through the Resource Management Office based on issues collected through multiple channels. The current accommodation-fee standard was legally approved by the pricing authorities and the relevant documents had been published. The university said it would study students’ suggestions for adjusting dormitory prices in the near term. The dormitory-area table referenced in the source was not available in the notice and is not reproduced here.
