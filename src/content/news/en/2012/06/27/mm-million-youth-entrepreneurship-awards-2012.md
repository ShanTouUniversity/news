STU Engineering students won two awards at the national final of the second MM Million Youth Entrepreneurship Plan, held from March 25 to 27, 2012. Lin SQ received first place in the social paid-service group for “Jade Rabbit Moon Mission” and a prize of 100,000 yuan. Li JJ received an excellence award in the free-campus group for “Affinity” and a prize of 20,000 yuan.

According to the university report, STU has supported student innovation and entrepreneurship since 2010 through a student entrepreneurship association, an incubator, developer salons, entrepreneurship lectures, project competitions, and training sessions. Three applications had previously reached the national final, and two received awards in this edition. The report describes STU as the only university in the country to win a top prize in two consecutive editions and “Jade Rabbit Moon Mission” as the only Guangdong entry to win a top prize in this edition.
