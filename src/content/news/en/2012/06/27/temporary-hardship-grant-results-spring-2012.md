STU has announced 181 recipients of temporary hardship grants for the spring semester of the 2011–2012 academic year under the *Interim Measures for STU Undergraduate Temporary Hardship Assistance*.

The schools and colleges reviewed students’ application materials, after which the Student Affairs Office completed verification and publicized the results. The recipient list and specific assistance details remain subject to the original materials issued by the Student Affairs Office. Student names and other personal information are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
