It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University recently announced the winners of its 2011-2012 "Best Administrative Service Award" and "Best Teaching Service Award."

The selection process involved nominations by each department, recommendations by the college's faculty council, and final evaluation by the college's academic committee. Mr. Huang Xuejia of the college administration office received the 2011-2012 "Best Administrative Service Award," while Senior Experimentalist Chen Liqiang of the Department of Civil Engineering received the "Best Teaching Service Award."

The college noted that the award-winning staff had worked diligently over the past year to support teaching and research activities and to ensure the smooth progress of reforms such as the college's EIP-CDIO engineering education program. It expressed the hope that the winners would continue to excel and called on all faculty and staff to learn from them.
