STU’s 2012 graduation ceremony and awards presentation will be held at 07:50 on Friday, June 29, in the Gymnasium. The university will livestream the ceremony; parents without reserved Gymnasium seats may watch in the Science Lecture Hall from 07:30 to 09:30.

Ticketed parents should enter at 06:40 and sit in their designated areas. Holders of east-section tickets should use the Gymnasium’s main entrance, while west-section ticket holders should use the west entrance. School leaders seated on the platform should gather in academic regalia in the Gymnasium fitness room by 07:00. Graduate groups should be organized by 06:30 and enter from 06:35 under the leadership of their counselors.

All attendees should dress appropriately and may not enter in slippers, undershirts, or shorts. Everyone should follow staff directions and set mobile phones to vibrate. Students carrying cameras may take photographs from their seats but may not move around the venue. Children under 12 will not be admitted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office and Student Affairs Office)
