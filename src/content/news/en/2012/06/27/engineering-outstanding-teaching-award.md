It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University has announced the winners of its 2011-2012 Outstanding Teaching Award: Associate Professor Lin Peng from the Department of Civil Engineering and Professor Chen Shaoke from the Department of Mechatronics.

## Selected Through Multi-level Review

Candidates for the award were nominated by their departments, recommended by the college's executive committee, and finally evaluated by the college's academic committee. The college noted that over the past year, faculty members across the school had devoted themselves to teaching, contributing to the college's EIP-CDIO engineering education reform and teaching research. The award recognizes this outstanding work in teaching.

## A Call for Faculty to Follow Their Example

The college expressed its hope that the honored teachers will continue their efforts and achieve even better results in future work. It also called on all faculty and staff to learn from the awardees, actively advance various initiatives, and make greater contributions to the EIP-CDIO engineering education reform.
