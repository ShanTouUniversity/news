STU’s Student Affairs and People’s Armed Forces departments have published the arrangements for 2012 student military training. The training will take place from July 2 to 15, with military-skills sessions held on campus at the locations specified in the attachments.

Military-skills training will run from 07:30 to 10:30 and from 16:00 to 18:00 each day during the training period. Military-theory classes will be held in classrooms according to the training schedule. A mobilization meeting will take place at 08:30 on July 2 in the Auditorium, and military lectures will be arranged there during the training period. The concluding meeting is scheduled for the afternoon of July 15 at the athletics field.

The Student Affairs Office will organize military-training supplies by school for distribution. Students may collect them from Room 108 of the Student Affairs Office building from 09:00 to 12:00 on July 1. Participants should bring their own drinking water.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
