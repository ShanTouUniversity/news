It is reported that preparations for STU’s spring 2012 classroom teaching evaluation have been completed. All students enrolled in courses must log into the credit-system platform and complete their evaluations between June 4 and June 15, providing careful, objective, and fair feedback on their instructors.

The evaluation is available at <http://credit.stu.edu.cn> under “Course Evaluation.” Students enrolled in public-service courses must also open “View Public-Service Courses Awaiting Evaluation” and complete a separate evaluation for public-service courses from the 2011–2012 spring semester.

Students who do not complete the evaluation by the deadline must submit a written explanation of the reason. Otherwise, they will be unable to view their grades for courses taken this semester.
