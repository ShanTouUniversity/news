It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on June 4, 2012, that places remain for the summer semester service-learning courses after initial selection and supplementary rounds, and additional registration is now open to students who have not yet taken a service-learning course this summer.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Students who have not yet enrolled in a summer service-learning course |
| Deadline | 17:00, June 6, 2012 |
| How to apply | Send registration information to lxl0726@stu.edu.cn, including grade, student ID, name, course title (and project number), contact details and email |
| Principle | First-come, first-served until places are filled; each student may select only one course |
| Contact | 82902416 (Ms./Mr. Liu) |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

The list of available courses is as shown in the original OA notice.
