It is reported that STU’s College of Liberal Arts and English Language Center will host a workshop on college English teaching reform from 19:00 to 21:00 on June 6 in ACC Lecture Hall 5. Professor Chang Junyue, dean of the School of English Studies at Dalian University of Foreign Languages, will lead the session.

Titled “Curriculum Reconstruction and Practice Exploration Guided by Content-Based Instruction Theory,” the workshop will discuss the application of Content-Based Instruction (CBI) to English curriculum design and teaching practice.
