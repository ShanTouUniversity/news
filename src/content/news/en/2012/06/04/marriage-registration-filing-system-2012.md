It is reported that, under relevant rules of the Guangdong Population and Family Planning Commission, STU has established a marital-status registration system for faculty, staff, and students. The system is intended to standardize family-planning administration at universities and maintain accurate information on marriage and childbirth status.

STU faculty, staff, and enrolled female students who register a marriage must file their marital status with the Family Planning Office of the University Hospital within one month after the marriage. Violations of family-planning policy will be handled under the applicable regulations.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Family Planning Office)
