It is reported that STU’s Social Sciences Department and Network Center have expanded the Brain Olympics website’s “Brain Booster” and “Intelligence Hurdles” simulated-test programs to four courses: Marxism Fundamentals, Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics, Outline of Modern Chinese History, and Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law. The platform provides regular study and practice support for students in these courses.

Students at the main campus taking any of the four courses must first complete the Brain Booster exercises and reach 80% energy at every station before they are eligible to take the Intelligence Hurdles simulated test.

The university’s final examinations begin on June 18. The Social Sciences Department will restrict access to the Brain Olympics website from 08:00 on June 18 and begin compiling students’ Intelligence Hurdles scores for submission to course instructors as a reference in the overall assessment.
