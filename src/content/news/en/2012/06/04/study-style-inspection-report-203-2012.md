It is reported that STU’s Three-Style Development Office organized inspections of classroom attendance in Building E during Week 14 of the 2011–2012 spring semester. An inspection at 08:00 on May 29 found seven students arriving no more than five minutes late. A second inspection at 14:00 on May 31 found four students arriving no more than five minutes late and one student arriving more than five minutes late, for 12 instances in total.

The office has sent the relevant student list to schools and residential colleges and asked them to provide criticism and education while continuing efforts to improve study culture. To protect student privacy, individual names are not published in this article.
