It is reported that STU’s Party Committee Study Group will meet at 15:00 on June 6 in Meeting Room 311 of the Administration Building to convey the spirit of the 11th Guangdong Provincial Party Congress.

Members of the university Party Committee, secretaries of Party general branches and directly affiliated branches, and relevant officials from the Discipline Inspection Commission, Party and Government Office, Organization and United Front Department, Publicity Department, News Center, Student Affairs Office, Social Sciences Department, university labor union, and university Youth League Committee will attend.
