It is reported that STU has established its eighth Academic Degrees Evaluation Subcommittees after the term of the seventh subcommittees ended. The new structure is intended to support the university’s degree and graduate-education work and includes subcommittees for the College of Liberal Arts, Law School, Business School, Changjiang School of Arts and Design, Science School, Engineering School, and Medical College.

The appointed leaders are:

| Subcommittee | Chair | Vice chair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| College of Liberal Arts | Yang Qingjie | Fan Dongsheng |
| Law School | Peng Wenhao (Michael Palmer) | Not listed |
| Business School | Xu Zongling | Ouyang Feng |
| Changjiang School of Arts and Design | Wang Shouzhi | Han Ran |
| Science School | Lou Zengjian | Li Youyuan |
| Engineering School | Chen Yan | Xiong Guangjing |
| Medical College | Gu Jiang | Shi Ganggang |

The notice also lists committee members. Several member names contain character corruption in the source file, so names that cannot be confirmed are not reconstructed here.
