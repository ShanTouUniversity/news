It is reported that STU Law School’s graduate study and career sharing session will be held from 15:00 to 17:00 on June 6 in Meeting Room 213 of the Law School. Five graduating students will share their experience in planning for further study and careers.

The session will cover overseas applications, domestic postgraduate examinations, civil-service examinations, and employment after graduation. The graduates’ destinations include China University of Political Science and Law, McGill University in Canada, The University of Hong Kong, the Zhongshan State Taxation Bureau, and Johnson & Johnson Medical Devices China. The speakers’ names are not published here to protect student privacy.

Students who wish to attend may reserve a place by emailing `cdclaw@stu.edu.cn` with their class and name.
