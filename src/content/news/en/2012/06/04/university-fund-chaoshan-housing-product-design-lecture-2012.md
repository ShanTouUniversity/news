It is reported that leaders of selected STU university-funded projects will hold an academic lecture from 19:30 to 21:30 on June 5 in the stepped classroom of the middle section of the Changjiang School of Arts and Design. The session will share progress from projects supported by the liberal-arts research fund.

| Project | Leader | Lecture topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research on the Beauty of Chaoshan Ancient Customs in Contemporary Visual Culture | Lin Kailong | Chaoshan Vernacular Houses and Culture |
| Research on Product-Design Education with Chinese Characteristics | Li Haoyu | Eastward Movement of Western Ideas: Documenting Product-Design Practice |

The session will exchange perspectives on traditional Chaoshan residences and their cultural meaning, product-design education, and related research practice. Students and staff are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office and Changjiang School of Arts and Design)
