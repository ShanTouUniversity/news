It is reported that renovation of laboratories and offices in STU Engineering School’s Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering began on May 22. During construction, the user unit requested that wall tiles on the first floor of the Science Center and the rear building of the department use a style and installation method the same as or similar to those in the Innovation Center. The wall-tile specification was consequently changed.

The original bidding documents specified 330 × 400 mm Foshan first-grade tiles at RMB 70 per square meter, including labor and materials. The revised specification is 300 × 600 mm, with the contractor quoting RMB 118 per square meter, an increase of RMB 48. The relevant price difference has been reviewed by the Infrastructure Office, Finance Office, and Supervision and Audit Office. The total tiled area is approximately 250 square meters, producing an estimated additional construction cost of RMB 12,000.

The Resource Management Office and Infrastructure Office have published the cost variation for public comment. Comments may be submitted in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 17:30 on June 7.
