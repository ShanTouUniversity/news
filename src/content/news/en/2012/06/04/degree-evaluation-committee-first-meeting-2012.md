It is reported that the first meeting of STU’s eighth Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee will be held at 14:30 on June 22 in Meeting Room 205 of the Science and Technology Building. Schools and colleges should notify their committee members to attend on time. Members are asked to avoid scheduling business travel during the meeting so that a quorum can be reached.

Xu Xiaohu is chair of the eighth committee, with Gu Peihua, Wulanhasi, and Huang Dongyang as vice chairs. The committee also includes relevant university administrators and faculty representatives. Members who cannot attend for special reasons must notify the Degree Office by June 18.

Degree Office email: `xwb@stu.edu.cn`; telephone: `4745`.
