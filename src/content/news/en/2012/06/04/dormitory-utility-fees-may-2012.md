It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on June 4, 2012, regarding May dormitory utility fees.

Meter readings were taken on May 30-31, with the previous reading on April 30. Students may check fees via the STU Utility Management System at http://power.stu.edu.cn using their campus network account.

## Payment Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Verification period | June 4–8 during working hours; submit the *Utility Fee Verification Application Form* to the Hydropower Center (beside the Science and Technology Building, tel. 2489) |
| Dormitory-list mismatch | Contact the Counselors’ Office (Block B-106, tel. 2903355) |
| Deduction | Starting June 11 via campus card by the Network Center (tel. 3547-8842) |
| Regular payment window | June 11–13; please ensure sufficient balance |
| Overdue | From June 14, handled per university regulations |

Objections raised after the verification period, if requiring correction, will take 3–5 working days. Students are advised to check promptly and recharge as needed.

> ⚠️ The attachment is available on the OA system.
