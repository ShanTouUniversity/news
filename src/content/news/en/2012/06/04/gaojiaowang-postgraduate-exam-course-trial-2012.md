It is reported that STU Library has opened trial access to a set of interactive, high-definition postgraduate-exam preparation courses developed by Gaojiao, a brand of Beijing Yuzhi Xingbang Education Technology Co., Ltd., together with China Renmin University Press. The courses support preparation across foundation, intensive, and final-review stages.

The content covers politics, English I, English II, Mathematics I, Mathematics II, Mathematics III, and subjects for both national unified examinations and non-unified examinations. The trial will remain available through September 1, 2012.

Faculty and students can access the courses through the [library database page](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=352). The original notice recommends using Internet Explorer and opening the link in a new window. Questions may be sent to `yxu@stu.edu.cn`.
