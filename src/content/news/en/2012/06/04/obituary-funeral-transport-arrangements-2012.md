It is reported that STU has issued arrangements following an obituary. Organizations and individuals planning to send funeral wreaths must contact the Retired Faculty and Staff Office or Baiheyuan by 16:00 on June 4. Because the source notice does not provide the deceased person’s name or identity, this article reports only the logistical arrangements.

Transport on June 5 is scheduled as follows: campus passengers will board at the reception room entrance at 08:10; passengers from Anju will board at the Anju entrance at 08:20; passengers from Dajiao will board outside the Fourth Hospital at 08:30; and Baiheyuan passengers will board at the campus bus stop at 08:30.

Retired Faculty and Staff Office: `82902660`; Baiheyuan: `88626402`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office and Labor Union)
