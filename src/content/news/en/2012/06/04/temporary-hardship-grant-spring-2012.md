It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Finance Department allocated STU RMB 1.04 million in 2012 from a central student-loan incentive fund. The university has designated RMB 210,000 for temporary hardship grants to support students who experience financial difficulty because of unexpected events. The grants will follow the measures in STU document No. 4 of 2012.

For the current semester, students who need support must submit an online application through the [student aid application system](http://www.scholarship.stu.edu.cn/chinese/applyIntro2.aspx) by 11:00 on June 11, then deliver a printed application form and supporting documents to their school or residential college. After review, schools and residential colleges must submit the applications, supporting documents, and summary form to the Student Affairs Office Student Aid Management Center by 11:00 on June 15.
