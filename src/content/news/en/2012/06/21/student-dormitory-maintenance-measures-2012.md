At 15:00 on June 21, representatives of the STU Office of Supervision and Audit, Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office, and Network and Information Center conducted an on-site investigation in the student dormitories. They consulted the Student Community Service Center about repair requests and maintenance and met student representatives to hear their views on dormitory repairs and responses.

The university departments agreed on six measures: the Resource Management Office will publish dormitory repair procedures and service commitments for student supervision; sound fans will replace faulty fans where repairs have been delayed; ventilation and limited space in the clothes-drying room on the first floor of Building F will be addressed within one week; the Student Community Service Center will provide ant-killing products free of charge; the Network and Information Center will repair the hot-water card system during the summer; and the Resource Management Office will study solutions for other repairs inside dormitories and in public areas and announce them separately.

> Source: STU OA Report (Office of Supervision and Audit, Network and Information Center, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office)
