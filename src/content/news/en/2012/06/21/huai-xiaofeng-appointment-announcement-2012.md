It is reported that the 8th University Leadership Meeting on June 20, 2012, decided to propose the appointment of Huai Xiaofeng as director of the Social Sciences Teaching and Research Department. In accordance with the *Interim Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres of Shantou University* (STU Party Document [2009] No. 47), the Personnel Office issued a pre-appointment publicity notice on June 21.

The publicity period runs until June 26, 2012. Organizations or individuals with differing opinions are asked to submit written comments to the Personnel Office, with organizational comments stamped with an official seal and individual comments signed with real name.
