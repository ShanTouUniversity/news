The STU School of Humanities Party General Branch has honored student Party members for the 2011–2012 academic year, conferring the title of Outstanding Student Party-Affairs Worker on two students and Outstanding Student Party Member on six students.

The school said the honorees demonstrated exemplary service in study, work, daily life, and Party and community activities, making particularly positive contributions to the excellence-striving campaign and the practice of learning-oriented student Party branches. The Party General Branch called on all student Party members to learn from the honorees and continue improving their study and service. Student names are not reproduced here.
