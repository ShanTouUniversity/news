It is reported that, in response to questions raised by some students regarding the fee basis for student dormitories A through F, the STU Finance Office has released relevant charging documents and approval records for reference.

A total of four attachments covering the standards and approvals for student accommodation fees are available via the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office)
> ⚠️ The fee documents and approval records are available on the OA system.
