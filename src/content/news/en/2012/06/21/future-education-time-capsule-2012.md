To encourage the STU community to reflect on and envision the future of education, STU plans to create a “Our Hopes for Future Education” time capsule. Faculty members and graduating students will each be invited to write personal hopes for future education on cards.

The cards will be placed underground beneath the plaza in front of the New Administration Center and are scheduled to be opened 20 years later, in 2032. Teaching units should organize faculty members and graduating students to complete the cards and submit consolidated materials by June 25. Faculty cards should go to the Secretariat Office in the east central hall of the Administration Building, while graduating-student cards should go to the Student Affairs Office’s Career Guidance Center.
