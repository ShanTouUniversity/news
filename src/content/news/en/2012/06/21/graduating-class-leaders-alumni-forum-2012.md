STU will hold a forum for 2012 graduating-class leaders at 15:00 on June 26 in Conference Room 726 of Building 7 to coordinate alumni-related work for graduating classes.

The forum will introduce alumni work and alumni chapters in cities across the region, explain the functions of the alumni card, arrange unified collection of cards by graduating classes, and gather suggestions about alumni work. Graduating classes from the Graduate School and Medical School will also attend. Class leaders should participate on time; when a leader cannot attend, another class officer may attend instead.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Alumni Office and Party and Administrative Office)
