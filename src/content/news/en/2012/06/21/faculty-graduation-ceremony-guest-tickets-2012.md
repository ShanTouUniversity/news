STU’s 2012 graduation ceremony will be held in the university gymnasium on June 29. The university plans to invite 100 faculty and staff representatives as guests to share the occasion with graduating students.

Tickets will be allocated according to the number of faculty and staff members in each unit. The School of Humanities, School of Law, and Business School will each receive eight tickets; the Schools of Science, Engineering, and Medicine will each receive 12; the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will receive six; the Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design will receive eight; other teaching and teaching-support units will receive 10; and administrative and logistics departments will receive 16. Faculty and staff should collect tickets from their school office, while members of other teaching and teaching-support units should collect them from the Academic Affairs Office and administrative or logistics staff from the Labor Union.

Each unit must send a representative to collect tickets from the Confidential Affairs Section in Xingdong Building Room 417 by June 27. Collection is scheduled for the morning of June 27.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office)
