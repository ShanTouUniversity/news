It is reported that, in accordance with requirements from the Department of Science and Technology of China's Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education for the 2012 "New Century Excellent Talents Support Program," Shantou University has launched its internal application and selection process.

**Eligibility**: Applicants must meet the basic conditions listed in the program's implementation measures, hold Chinese nationality, and apply through their home institution (cross-institution applications are not accepted). Those already funded by the "Thousand Talents Plan," "Chang Jiang Scholars Distinguished Professor Program," "National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars," or the Chinese Academy of Sciences' "Hundred Talents Program" may not apply again. In terms of age, applicants in natural sciences should generally have been born on or after January 1, 1972, while those in philosophy and social sciences should generally have been born on or after January 1, 1967. Applications fall under 27 fields, including mathematics, physics, chemistry, electronic science and technology, and biomedical sciences.

**Internal selection**: The program operates under a quota system, and Shantou University may nominate only one project. The university will organize experts to review applications and recommend the best candidate to the provincial education department. Each unit must submit its proposed candidate list (limited to one per unit) **by June 27**, together with an introduction to the candidate's research over the past five years — covering research experience, projects, and achievements — in one paper copy to the Research Office and electronically to o_kyc@stu.edu.cn. Application forms and implementation measures can be downloaded from the Ministry's science and technology department website at www.dost.moe.edu.cn. For inquiries, call 82902928.
