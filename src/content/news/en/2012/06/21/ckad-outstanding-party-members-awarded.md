It is reported that the Party General Branch of the Cheung Kong School of Art & Design at Shantou University has decided to confer the title of "Outstanding Communist Party Member of the Cheung Kong School of Art & Design" on four students — Huang RC, Sun WY, Wei J and Li Q — in recognition of their performance during the 2011–2012 academic year.

## Grounds for the Award

According to the Party General Branch, over the past year student members of the school held themselves to high standards under the leadership of Party organizations at all levels. Centering on the goals of building "learning-oriented Party branches" and striving for excellence, they played an exemplary vanguard role in study, work, daily life and activities both inside and outside the Party, contributing actively to the school's development. To recognize exemplary individuals and set role models, the branch honored the four students.

## Expectations

The branch hopes that the honorees will cherish the recognition, guard against pride and impatience, and achieve further success. It also calls on all student Party members in the school to learn from these role models, strive to be pioneers, keep pace with the times, practice boldly, innovate, study hard and work diligently to contribute more to the university's and the school's development.
