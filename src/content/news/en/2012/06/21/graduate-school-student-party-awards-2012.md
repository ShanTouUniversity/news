The STU Graduate School Party General Branch has announced its 2011–2012 student Party-honor decisions. Following reviews by student Party branches and examination by the Graduate School Party General Branch, the school has named four graduate students Outstanding Party-Affairs Workers and 25 graduate students Outstanding Communist Party Members.

The school encouraged the honorees to value the recognition, continue serving as exemplary Party members, pursue innovation and study, and contribute to the university’s development. The individual award roster is not reproduced here.
