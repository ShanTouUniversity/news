To strengthen exchange through the Engineering School–Shenzhen OFILM Tech Co., Ltd. Innovation Club and give students practical experience in product and technology innovation, the Engineering School and OFILM will organize one- to two-week summer internships. The internships will take place during the summer at OFILM’s Nanchang branch.

The planned openings are two to three positions in embedded-microcontroller programming, one to two in circuit-board wiring and connection, and one in coordination. The program is open to Mechanical and Electrical Engineering students in the 2009 and 2010 cohorts. Outstanding members of the OFILM corporate innovation club and project teams will receive priority consideration. Applicants must have no failing courses in the 2010–2011 academic year.

Interested students must submit an application form according to the school’s requirements by June 25. Interview arrangements will be announced based on the number of applications. The application form and submission method are provided in the Engineering School notice; student personal email addresses and telephone numbers are not reproduced here.
