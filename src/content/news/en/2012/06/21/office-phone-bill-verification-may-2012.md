Electronic bills for office-telephone charges incurred by STU units from May 1 to 31, 2012 have been sent by China Telecom Shantou Branch to the university telephone office and uploaded to the [Telephone Management System](http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn). Unit reviewers may log in to inspect the itemized bills. Bills without objections should be confirmed in the system; discrepancies must be emailed to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` by June 28 so the university can consolidate feedback for the telecom provider.

After the review period closes, bills that have neither been challenged nor confirmed will be treated by the system as approved. The Finance Office will deduct the charges on June 29 using the funding-account numbers supplied by each unit. System problems and suggestions may also be sent to the same official email address.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
