STU Student Affairs Office has announced the results of its 2012 graduation-ceremony volunteer recruitment. The university received 612 applications between the recruitment launch on June 11 and the June 13 deadline, selecting 55 volunteers and six alternates.

Volunteers must participate throughout the graduation rehearsal beginning on the afternoon of June 28 and the graduation ceremony on the morning of June 29. Volunteer training will be held at 20:30 on June 25 in Room G304. The Student Affairs Office notice contains the selected and alternate rosters and detailed duties; student names and personal contact information are not reproduced here.
