It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University announced a provisional list of outstanding graduates for the Class of 2012 on June 21, 2012. The announcement was made under the *Business School Criteria for Selecting Outstanding Graduates* to recognize graduates with strong academic and overall performance.

## Announcement Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Announced group | Provisional list of outstanding graduates from the Business School's Class of 2012 |
| Number discrepancy | The first part of the original notice refers to 13 candidates, while a later section says 14 candidates are being publicized; the OA attachment should be consulted for the definitive list and final number |
| Objection deadline | Before June 24, 2012 |
| Issuing unit | Business School, Shantou University |
| Issue date | June 21, 2012 |

To protect students' personal information, this article does not reproduce the names in the provisional list.

## Selection Criteria

The criteria published by the Business School primarily covered the following areas:

- Well-rounded development in moral, intellectual and physical education, strong academic performance and no repeated courses;
- A record of receiving outstanding-student scholarships. For graduates enrolled in 2008, the criteria required annual awards at third prize or above, including either at least one first prize or two second prizes;
- An excellent undergraduate thesis result;
- Experience serving as a student leader at the school or higher level, including class leadership, or participation in volunteer and public-service activities.

## Feedback on the Public Notice

During the public notice period, objections could be submitted in writing or raised in person or by telephone to the Business School Party branch or Youth League Committee by June 24. Individuals submitting feedback were required to provide their real names.
