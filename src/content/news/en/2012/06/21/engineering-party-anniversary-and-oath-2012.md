STU’s Engineering School will hold a ceremony at 19:30 on June 24 in the Labor Union’s third-floor multipurpose hall to mark the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China and welcome new Party members. All faculty and student Party members of the Engineering School are expected to attend. The event will commemorate the anniversary and include an oath ceremony for new members.
