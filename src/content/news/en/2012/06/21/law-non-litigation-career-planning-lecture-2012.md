STU’s Law School will hold a lecture titled “Non-Litigation Practice and Career Planning” from 16:00 to 17:00 on June 23 in Room 213. Two 2006 law alumni will share their experience in legal practice.

The speakers are Ni H, who works at the Hong Kong office of Slaughter and May, and Xu QH, who works at the Hangzhou office of King & Wood Mallesons. The lecture is open to the STU community and will focus on non-litigation practice and career planning in the legal profession.
