STU’s School of Science has opened its selection of outstanding undergraduate graduates for 2012. The selection recognizes graduates with strong academic and personal records who have contributed to collective and public-welfare activities and supported the university’s graduation and employment work, helping promote a positive academic culture.

Applicants must respect the country, university, teachers, and community, comply with laws and regulations, demonstrate balanced development and good academic performance, and have no repeated courses. They should have participated actively in collective activities and taken part in volunteer or public-welfare service. Academically, applicants must have received the second-tier or higher Academic Excellence Scholarship at least twice, or received a university special scholarship at least once. Their graduation thesis must be rated good or above, and they must cooperate actively with the university’s graduation and employment arrangements and leave campus responsibly.

Eligible undergraduates must complete the application form and submit it to the School of Science Youth League Committee by 12:00 on June 25. A school selection committee will review the applications and determine the recipients. The school will then confer the Outstanding Graduate title and issue certificates and awards. The award for an outstanding undergraduate graduate is 800 yuan.
