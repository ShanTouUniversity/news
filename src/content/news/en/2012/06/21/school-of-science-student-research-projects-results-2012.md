The STU School of Science review panel for student extracurricular academic and technology works has completed its 2012 project review, approving 20 projects for school-level support. The projects cover student research topics including materials studies. The school asks each project group to organize its members and begin the research work promptly.

The School of Science will hold a project meeting at 15:00 on June 29 in its multimedia classroom, Room Li-352. Project leaders will sign the *STU Student Science and Technology Project Development Agreement*. All project members must attend on time, and the project leader’s presence is required. Failure to attend will be treated as giving up the project approval. The detailed project list is provided in the original school notice attachment.
