Academic activities connected with the third meeting of STU’s eighth Board of Trustees are now open for registration. Advisors Wei Yu and Cheng Jieming will each present a lecture. Interested members of the STU community may register through the [STU Activity Booking System](http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ActivityBooking.aspx).

Registration opens at 11:00 on June 21 and closes at 22:00 on June 25. The lecture titles, times, and detailed arrangements will follow the original notice attachments issued by the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original notice in the OA system for detailed arrangements and related materials for both lectures.
