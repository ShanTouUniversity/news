It is reported that in response to opinions and suggestions raised by students at a recent dormitory forum held in Room 205, Science Building, relevant departments of Shantou University have promptly formulated a series of improvement measures, aiming to give older dormitories a fresh look next semester.

According to a joint circular from the Party and Government Office and the Student Affairs Office, four immediate actions have been taken. First, led by the Supervision and Audit Office, a team including the Resource Management Department, the Student Affairs Office, the Student Counselor Office and student representatives inspected the dormitory maintenance service system on the afternoon of June 21 to help refine procedures and improve responsiveness, with follow-up measures to be announced soon. Second, the summer power supply schedule will be adjusted: instead of switching to summer hours on a fixed date of May 1, the university will in future make the switch when temperatures remain above a certain threshold for consecutive days after spring, with the specific threshold to be further discussed with students. Third, the Student Community Management Service Center will distribute canned spray insecticides to each floor to control cockroaches and improve living conditions. Fourth, the fee standards for student dormitories as approved by the pricing authority have been published on the OA system to enhance transparency.

In addition, the university convened a meeting of heads of resource management and other departments on June 21 to discuss further improvements in public hygiene, hot water supply, electronic access control, and laundry services.

The university encourages students to continue sharing feedback through contact information published on the university homepage, the President's Mailbox (o_xzxx@stu.edu.cn), the weekly Thursday reception day for university leaders, or by making appointments with leaders directly.

| Area | Measures |
|------|----------|
| Maintenance | Inspection of maintenance system led by Supervision and Audit Office; system improvements to ensure timely repairs |
| Power supply | Summer schedule no longer fixed to May 1; switch based on consecutive days above temperature threshold |
| Living environment | Canned insecticide sprays distributed floor by floor for cockroach control |
| Fee transparency | Approved dormitory fee standards published on OA system |
| Ongoing improvements | Review of hygiene, hot water, access control and laundry services |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office, Student Affairs Office)
