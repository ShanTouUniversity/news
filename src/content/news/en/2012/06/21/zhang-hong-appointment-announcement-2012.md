It is reported that the Personnel Office issued a notice on June 21, 2012, proposing to appoint Zhang Hong to an intermediate management post following deliberation at the 8th University Leadership Meeting on June 20, 2012.

In accordance with the *Shantou University Interim Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres* (STU Party Document [2009] No. 47), any objections should be submitted in writing to the Personnel Office by June 26, 2012. Submissions from units should bear an official seal, and those from individuals should be signed with the real name.
