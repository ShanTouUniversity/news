The STU Business School Party General Branch and Youth League Committee have reviewed and honored student-leader work for 2011–2012, conferring the title of Outstanding Student Leader of the Business School on 47 students.

The school said student leaders worked diligently and innovated throughout the year, helping student affairs progress and achieve positive results. The individual recipients’ names are not reproduced here.
