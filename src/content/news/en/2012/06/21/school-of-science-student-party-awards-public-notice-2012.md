To recognize student Party members who demonstrated exemplary service in study, work, daily life, and Party and community activities, the STU School of Science Party General Branch has opened its 2011–2012 student Party-honor recommendations.

The school proposes to confer the title of Outstanding Party-Affairs Worker on two students and Outstanding Communist Party Member on 10 students. The recommendations are being made public among the school’s student Party branches for comments. Comments may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone before the end of the morning work period on June 25. Telephone: `82902779`. Student names are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Public Notice (Party General Branch of the STU School of Science)
