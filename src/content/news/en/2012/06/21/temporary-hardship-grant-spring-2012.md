It is reported that the selection results for the Temporary Hardship Grants for the spring semester of the 2011–2012 academic year were announced on June 21.

Following the release of the notice on this semester's hardship grant program, a total of 453 students submitted applications. In accordance with the "Interim Measures for the Administration of Temporary Hardship Grants for Undergraduates at Shantou University (Trial)," colleges (and residential colleges) carefully reviewed the submitted materials. After a secondary review by the Student Affairs Office, 180 recipients were finalized.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Total applicants | 453 |
| Final recipients | 180 |
| Recipient list | See attachment from the Student Affairs Office |

Any objections may be submitted in writing to the Education Management Section of the Student Affairs Office (Room 104) before 5:00 p.m. on Monday, June 25. Submissions from organizations should bear an official seal, and those from individuals must include the real name of the submitter.
