Because of high enrollment, STU’s Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design has divided its Chinese-painting continuing-education course into two classes to support instruction.

Class 1 will follow the original schedule from June 25 to 28, with sessions from 09:00 to 12:00 and 15:00 to 17:30 each day. Class 2 will meet from June 29 to July 1 and on July 4. Both classes will use Room E507. The notice does not specify Class 2’s session hours; students should follow the college’s subsequent arrangements. The class assignments are provided in the original notice attachment.
