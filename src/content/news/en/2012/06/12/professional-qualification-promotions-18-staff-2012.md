It is reported that 18 STU faculty and staff members obtained professional qualifications after review by the relevant Guangdong professional-qualification committees and approval by the provincial Human Resources and Social Security Department. Fifteen obtained senior qualifications and three obtained intermediate qualifications.

| Qualification | Recipients |
| --- | --- |
| Professor | Bu Xiangzhi, Sun Lisha, Wang Jiangyong, Xiong Jincai |
| Researcher | Chen Xiaohong, Wei Chiju |
| Associate Professor | Fan Lisheng, Wang Jun, Yuan Ye, Wu Fupei, Tan Chaoqiang, Yu Chengjie, Ji Yun, Bai Wenjun |
| Associate Researcher | Gao Jian |
| Lecturer | Wang Xiaoli (English Language Center), Yang Junru, Zheng Huibin |

Bu Xiangzhi, Sun Lisha, Wang Jiangyong, Xiong Jincai, Chen Xiaohong, Wei Chiju, Bai Wenjun, and Gao Jian obtained their qualifications in November 2011. Fan Lisheng, Wang Jun, Yuan Ye, Wu Fupei, Tan Chaoqiang, Yu Chengjie, Ji Yun, Wang Xiaoli, Yang Junru, and Zheng Huibin obtained theirs in September 2011. Under the relevant regulations, professional qualifications and professional appointments are assessed and made separately; the qualifications are not directly linked to nationally prescribed salaries or other benefits.
