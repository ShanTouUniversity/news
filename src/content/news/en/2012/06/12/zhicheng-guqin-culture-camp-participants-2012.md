It is reported that 15 faculty members and students have been confirmed for the sixth STU Zhicheng College Guqin Culture Camp, including 12 students and three faculty or staff members. To protect student privacy, participant names and individual information are not published here.

Participants must collect camp materials from the college affairs office in J338 of Zhicheng College by the end of working hours on June 21 and pay a RMB 100 deposit. The camp will run from June 30 through July 6, with sessions scheduled from 09:00 to 12:00 and from 14:00 to 17:00 each day. All participants must attend the opening ceremony at 20:00 on June 29 in Lecture Room J354 of Zhicheng College.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
