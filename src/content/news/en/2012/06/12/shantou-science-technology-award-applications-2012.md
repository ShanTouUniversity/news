It is reported that STU Research Office is organizing applications for the 2012 Shantou Science and Technology Awards in accordance with a notice from the Shantou Science and Technology Bureau. Applicants must prepare three copies of the Recommendation Form for the Shantou Science and Technology Awards, 26 copies of the Project Summary Form, and one copy of the Project Application Status Form.

Industrial, agricultural, and medical-health projects require different supporting materials, including achievement-appraisal documents, research reports, novelty-search reports, user opinions, and intellectual-property documentation. Projects whose scientific and technological achievements have not been registered must also complete registration using the simplified version of the National Science and Technology Achievement Registration System V6.0 and submit the required printed and electronic materials.

Units must review the award materials and submit the printed set to the Research Office by July 5, while sending the electronic copy to `dengyong@stu.edu.cn`. Contacts: Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong; telephone: `82903271`. Detailed requirements should follow the Shantou Science and Technology Bureau notice and the original OA attachments.
