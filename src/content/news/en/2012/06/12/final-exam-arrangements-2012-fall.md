It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on June 12, 2012, confirming that final exam arrangements for the fall 2012 semester have been finalized.

## Exam Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Where to check | All exam courses are published on the credit system portal; faculty and students should log in to check their schedules; academic officers may generate printed schedules for their units |
| Invigilation | Invigilators and students should arrive 10 minutes early in accordance with invigilation rules and examination discipline |

## Paper Management

| Stage | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Setting and marking | Follow the *Course Assessment Management Measures* (STU Document [2007] No. 78); prepare two equivalent sets (A/B); complete marking within 3 days after the exam (up to one week for large courses) |
| Printing | Complete the *Exam Printing Application* with papers and the candidate list (printed by the chief examiner via the exam management system) and submit to the Academic Affairs Office printing center one week before the exam |
| Confidentiality | The setter must deliver papers in person for printing; the chief examiner collects papers on the exam day during working hours (or the afternoon before for 08:00 exams); a proxy must present authorization |
| Archiving | After self-checking against the *Exam Inspection Form*, submit materials to the academic officer of the offering unit for retention |

Related rules and forms are in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ Attachments are available on the OA system.
