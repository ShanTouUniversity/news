It is reported that STU will organize its third Guangdong Poverty Relief Day campaign under the theme “Help Those in Need and Give with Care.” The public-welfare campaign is open to all faculty members, staff, and students and supports poverty relief, coordinated regional development, and a more harmonious Guangdong.

Department labor unions will collect and register faculty and staff donations, while Student Affairs Office and the university Youth League Committee will collect and register student donations. All donations must be consolidated with the university labor union by June 20. The union will transfer the funds to the designated account in accordance with provincial and municipal requirements. The union will publish faculty and staff donor information, while Student Affairs Office and the Youth League Committee will publish student donor information; individual donation details are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office, Organization Department, Labor Union, Student Affairs Office, and Youth League Committee)
