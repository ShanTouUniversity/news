It is reported that the Personnel Office issued a notice on June 12, 2012, announcing the setup and application arrangements for professional technical posts in administrative departments.

## Post Setup

| Unit | Series / Category | Rank | Number | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finance Office | Accounting | Associate Senior (Support Series) | 2 |  |
| Party and Administration Office | Archives Management | Associate Senior (Support Series) | 1 |  |
| Supervision and Audit Office | Auditing | Associate Senior (Support Series) | 1 |  |
| Resource Management Office | Engineering | Associate Senior (Support Series) | 1 |  |

A total of five associate senior posts in the support series will be established across four administrative units.
