It is reported that the "Science Salon" lecture series at Shantou University's College of Science will host another session on the morning of June 14. The speaker this time is Dr. Sun Qunhui, chief scientist at Sinopec, who will give a themed talk on the design, preparation, and applications of polymer nanomaterials.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Polymer Nanomaterials: Design, Preparation, and Applications |
| Time | Thursday, June 14, 2012, 10:30–11:30 a.m. |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, First Floor, Institute of Biomedicine and Advanced Materials Research Center |
| Speaker | Dr. Sun Qunhui (Chief Scientist, Sinopec) |

According to the published abstract, morphology control of polymers is of great importance — whether in building novel controlled architectures that meet specific requirements, enhancing material performance, or ensuring good compatibility within polymer composite systems. Among related technologies, aligned-grown conductive polymer nanofibers play an important role in new electro-optic devices, supercapacitors, as well as anti-reflecting and anti-fog devices.

In this direction, the research team has developed newly aligned-grown polyaniline nanofiber arrays and explored their novel material properties. The lecture is expected to offer an industry-based research perspective to faculty and students engaged in materials research on campus. Interested members of the university community are welcome to attend in person.
