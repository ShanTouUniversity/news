It is reported that STU has opened applications for third-quarter 2012 equipment procurement plans. Units must submit printed and electronic materials to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by June 27. Late submissions will be deferred to the fourth quarter of 2012.

Projects with a total planned value of no more than RMB 20,000 require the Plan Form. Projects above RMB 20,000 require both the Plan Form and Plan Proposal and must complete the relevant approvals under the university’s financial-approval authority. Equipment priced above RMB 100,000 requires a feasibility report for major-equipment purchases. Computer purchases require a computer-purchase registration form, while air-conditioner purchases must state the installation location and condition of existing equipment in the remarks field.

Applications must include complete equipment names, models, specifications, technical parameters, budgets, funding sources, and government-centralized-procurement catalogue codes. Budgets should account for taxes, transportation, warranties, and other after-sales services, and brands should generally not be specified.
