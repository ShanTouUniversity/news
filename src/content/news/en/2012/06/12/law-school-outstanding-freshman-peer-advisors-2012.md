It is reported that STU Law School’s freshman volunteer advisors helped new students adapt to university life, answered questions, addressed difficulties, and supported healthy development during the past year. The work received positive feedback from students.

Following selection by the Student Union and review by the college Youth League Committee, the Law School has recognized 10 students as Outstanding Freshman Volunteer Advisors. Because all recipients are students, their individual names are not published here.
