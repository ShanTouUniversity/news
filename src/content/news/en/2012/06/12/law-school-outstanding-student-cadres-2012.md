It is reported that the STU Law School Party general branch and Youth League Committee have recognized 42 student cadres as Outstanding Student Cadres for 2011–2012 following a democratic selection process. The recognition honors work during the past year in student-organization development, student self-management, and extracurricular academic, cultural, sports, and social-practice activities.

The Law School encouraged the recognized students to continue contributing to college development and student services. Because all recipients are students, their individual names are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School Party General Branch and Youth League Committee)
