It is reported that STU College of Liberal Arts will hold a study-experience exchange at 15:00 on June 13 in its meeting room, Wendong 201. Under the theme “Learn from Outstanding Examples and Build a Positive Study Culture,” the event supports the university’s study-culture campaign and aims to encourage students to share learning methods and build an active academic atmosphere.

Class monitors and deputy monitors, Youth League branch secretaries, and all student Communist Party members are expected to attend. Other students are also welcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Liberal Arts Party General Branch and Youth League Committee)
