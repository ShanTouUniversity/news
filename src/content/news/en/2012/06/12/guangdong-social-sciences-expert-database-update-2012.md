It is reported that the Guangdong Social Sciences Planning Office is adjusting its provincial social-sciences expert database. STU Research Office is coordinating additions and updates for eligible university experts.

Eligible new experts are currently employed social-science researchers with senior or associate senior professional titles and strong political awareness, responsibility, scholarly expertise, professional reputation, conduct, and discipline. The database covers 20 disciplines established by the provincial social-sciences plan, with priority additions in education, psychology, arts, sports, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan studies, and overseas Chinese studies.

Eligible experts not yet in the database should first register and complete their profiles through the [Guangdong Social Sciences Expert Database](http://www.gdpplgopss.gov.cn/), download and print the expert recommendation form, and submit it to the Research Office by June 29. Experts already in the database should log in and update their information by the same date; no printed materials are required.
