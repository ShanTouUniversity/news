It is reported that STU Law School is recruiting 45 volunteer freshman advisors to help the 2012 freshman class adapt to its new environment and university life. Applicants may be undergraduates from the 2009, 2010, or 2011 cohorts or graduate students from the 2010 or 2011 cohorts. They should be enthusiastic about volunteer advising, responsible, willing to contribute, and experienced in student organizations, teamwork, and communication. Graduate applicants must provide a supervisor’s recommendation.

The Youth League Committee and Student Union will select and appoint advisors through preliminary review, interviews, training, and appointment. Applications close at 12:30 on June 13. Interviews are scheduled for 14:00 to 17:00 that day in Rooms D406 and D407. Because the original application email is a personal contact address, it is not reproduced here; the form and submission method should follow the original OA notice.
