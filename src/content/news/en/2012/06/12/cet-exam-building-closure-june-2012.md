It is reported that Buildings D, G, and E will be used as examination rooms for the National College English Test on June 16 and will temporarily restrict access. From 13:00 through the evening of June 15, all rooms except those used for regular classes will be closed. On June 16, the buildings will be managed as restricted examination areas from 08:00 to 18:00.

To provide students with alternative self-study space, the university will open Lecture Hall 1 from 13:00 to 22:00 on June 15 and from 08:00 to 18:00 on June 16.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
