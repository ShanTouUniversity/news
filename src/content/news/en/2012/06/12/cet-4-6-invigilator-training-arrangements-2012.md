It is reported that STU Academic Affairs Office will organize pre-examination training for invigilators on June 16 to support the first-half 2012 National College English Test. CET-4 will run from 09:00 to 11:20 and CET-6 from 15:00 to 17:20; invigilation sessions and rooms will be announced on the examination day.

By June 15, invigilators must review the Guangdong regulations for invigilators and candidates, invigilation procedures, room-record forms, unexpected-incident procedures, and classifications of violations and cheating. The examination site will conduct security checks on all invigilators as required, and mobile phones may not be carried into the examination room during invigilation.

CET-4 invigilators must report to Lecture Classroom E101 at 07:45 on June 16 for training and examination-paper collection, then return the papers there for counting after the test. CET-6 invigilators must report to the same room at 13:40 and follow the same post-examination procedure. The examination office is in E101; telephones: `2402` and `2623`.
