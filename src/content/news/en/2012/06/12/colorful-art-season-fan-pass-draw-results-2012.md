It is reported that the Colorful Art Season Spring 2012 program at STU has concluded and the lottery for art-fan passes has been completed, producing five student winners. Winners must bring their campus card and art-fan pass to Room 309 of the Art Education Center on the afternoon of June 14 or the morning of June 15 to confirm eligibility and learn how to claim their prizes.

To protect student privacy, the winners’ names, student numbers, and colleges are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Arts and Design and Art Education Center)
