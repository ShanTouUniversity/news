It is reported that the 122nd presentation in STU’s Modern Mathematics academic series will be held at 15:00 on June 13 in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). The speaker will be Dr. Zhu Huiling of the National University of Singapore.

Titled “Set Theory: An Overview,” the presentation will briefly review the development of set theory and its applications in other branches of mathematics. It will give particular attention to the forcing method and its role in obtaining results on the independence of mathematical statements. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
