It is reported that STU has deposited the spring 2012 national grants into the personal bank accounts of eligible students. The grant is RMB 1,500 per person, and eligible students should check their accounts.

For students with outstanding tuition or accommodation fees, the grant will first be used to offset those charges under university policy. Grants for Medical College students have been transferred to the college’s finance office, which will handle the disbursement.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office and Student Affairs Office)
