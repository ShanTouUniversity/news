It is reported that the STU Student Learning Center’s regular academic inquiry service for the semester will end on Friday of Week 16. During Weeks 17 and 18, all SLC discussion rooms will open from 15:00 to 22:00 Sunday through Friday, offering academic inquiries, group discussion, and self-study space. The rooms will be closed on Saturdays.

The list of courses covered by the academic inquiry service is available in the original OA attachment. Students are welcome to use SLC services during the examination period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original notice in the OA system for the course list.
