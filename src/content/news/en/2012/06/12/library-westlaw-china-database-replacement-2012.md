It is reported that STU Library’s subscribed iSinolaw database has been replaced by the Westlaw China bilingual database of Chinese laws and regulations. The change follows iSinolaw’s strategic partnership with Thomson Reuters and the transition of its information services to the Westlaw China platform.

Faculty members and students can access the new legal database through the [library database page](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=330).
