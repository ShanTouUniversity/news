It is reported that STU Social Sciences Department has rescheduled its continuing-education course New Changes in the Law-Enforcement Environment Arising from the Administrative Compulsion Law because the instructor will be traveling. The class originally scheduled for June 15 will now be held from 19:00 to 21:00 on June 20 in the same venue, Room 202 of Building D.

Enrolled participants should notify one another of the change. Faculty members interested in the course may register through the [continuing-education course system](http://training.stu.edu.cn/), using their personal campus-network account for login. Password details are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office and Social Sciences Department)
