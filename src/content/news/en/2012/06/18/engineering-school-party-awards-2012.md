On the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, the STU Engineering School Party general branch made its 2011–2012 recognition decision following recommendations from Party branches and review by the school.

The school awarded the title of Outstanding Party-Affairs Worker to 12 individuals and the title of Outstanding Party Member to 11 individuals. It said the awardees had maintained high standards in study, work, and daily life and had played exemplary roles in the “Striving for Excellence” initiative.

The Party general branch called on all Party members to learn from the awardees, pursue innovation, study diligently, and contribute to the development of the university and the school. Because the awardees may include student Party members, their individual names are not reproduced here.
