In accordance with arrangements by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, STU has begun assessments of “Thousand-Hundred-Ten Project” participants and the selection and recommendation of the seventh batch. The university will conduct end-of-term assessments for the fifth batch selected in 2008 and midterm assessments for the sixth batch selected in 2010. The process will combine personal summaries, school-level reviews, university reviews, and expert assessment organized by the education authorities.

Following the end-of-term assessment of the fifth batch, the education authorities will recognize advanced teams and individuals. The province plans to recognize five advanced teams and 20 advanced individuals. STU will also select and recommend candidates for the seventh batch under the relevant provincial requirements.

Each unit must compile and review the required materials and submit them to the Human Resources Office, Xingdong Building Room 406, by June 25. Electronic copies should be sent to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. Detailed assessment standards, recommendation forms, and material lists are provided in the original OA attachments.
