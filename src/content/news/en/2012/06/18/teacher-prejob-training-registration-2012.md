In accordance with Guangdong education regulations, STU’s 2012 higher-education teacher induction training will be organized by the Higher-Education Teacher Induction Training Center of Hanshan Normal University. A summer training class will be offered at Shantou Polytechnic, and registration is now open.

The training is intended for newly recruited teachers, education staff, and other personnel working in teaching, research, laboratory work, student ideological and political education, or Party and administrative management, as well as higher-education employees who entered the sector after the Higher Education Teacher Law took effect on January 1, 1994 but have not completed induction training. Graduates with a bachelor’s degree or above in specified education or psychology fields may apply to waive the Higher Education and Higher-Education Psychology courses if they have the required credits and submit supporting records when registering.

The fee is 420 yuan per person for five courses or 270 yuan for three courses, including 120 yuan for textbooks in either case. Participants must register through the [STU Training Website](http://training.stu.edu.cn) by June 26. Each unit must compile its registration materials and submit them to the Human Resources Office, Xingdong Building Room 406, by the same date. Training and textbook fees are paid at the training site on the first day. After completion and receipt of a certificate, STU will reimburse an appropriate portion of the training fee under applicable rules; other expenses are borne by participants.
