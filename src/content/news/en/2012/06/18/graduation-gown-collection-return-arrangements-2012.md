To prepare for the June 29 graduation ceremony, STU Resource Management Office has announced arrangements for collecting and returning academic regalia. Each school is to collect the gowns as a unit, distribute them to its departments and majors, and appoint a counselor to handle the procedure and help maintain order.

Collection will take place from 14:30 to 17:30 on June 25 in the Grand Auditorium foyer. The School of Journalism, School of Arts, and Graduate School are scheduled for 14:30–15:30; the School of Humanities, Business School, and School of Law for 15:30–16:30; and the School of Engineering, School of Science, and School of Continuing Education for 16:30–17:30. Gown quantities will be allocated according to statistics provided by the Academic Affairs Office and Graduate School, with sizes adjusted proportionally. The detailed allocation table should be followed.

Gowns must be returned to the Grand Auditorium foyer from 14:00 to 17:00 on June 29. Each school should check the quantities and sizes and return them on time. Damaged gowns or gowns not returned by 11:00 on July 2 will be charged at the original price or treated as lost. Contact: Hong, `82902827`.

For the conferral ceremony, gowns should be worn without additional outer clothing, over a white or light-colored shirt, with dark trousers or a dark, plain skirt and dark leather shoes. Men should wear ties and women may wear bow ties. Before a degree is conferred, the tassel should hang at the center of the right front side of the cap.
