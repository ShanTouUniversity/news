STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has reported the results of academic-conduct inspections during Week 16 of the 2011–2012 spring semester. Inspection teams checked teaching areas on the evenings of June 12 and June 14 and recorded 36 instances of lateness in total.

| Inspection | Location | Findings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 19:00 on June 12 | Teaching Building E | 12 students were up to five minutes late; 15 were more than five minutes late |
| 19:00 on June 14 | Room E301 | Nine students were more than five minutes late |

The university has sent the relevant student list to each school and residential college and asked them to provide criticism and education while continuing efforts to strengthen academic conduct and compliance with university rules. Student names are not reproduced here.
