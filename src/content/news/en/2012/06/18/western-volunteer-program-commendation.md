It is reported that the Xinjiang Project Office of the College Student Volunteer Service Western Program recently sent a letter of thanks to Shantou University, expressing gratitude for the university's support of the program and highly commending the volunteers' service in Xinjiang. The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has since forwarded the letter.

## Two Graduates Serve China's West

In July 2011, Li SP and Zhang XN, two fresh graduates of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University, answered the national call and were dispatched to impoverished areas of Xinjiang and Guangxi as volunteers. The Western Program is organized by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League, the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security in support of the talent-powered nation strategy, the Western Development strategy and the strategy of rejuvenating the country through science and education.

According to the report, during their service both volunteers performed their duties diligently and devoted considerable spare time to charitable and public-welfare activities, earning praise from local authorities and residents.

## A Call to Follow Their Example

In forwarding the letter, the Youth League Committee expressed the hope that all students will take the two volunteers as role models, learn from their youthful spirit, perseverance and dedication, study hard and strive for excellence, so as to make greater contributions to the country and society.
