STU Publicity Department has rescheduled the continuing-education course *Innovation in University News and Publicity in the Weibo Era*, originally planned for the morning of July 4. Professor Fan Dongsheng will teach the course from 09:00 to 10:30 on June 21 in Teaching Building D Room 201.

Teachers who selected the course should attend at the revised time and location.
