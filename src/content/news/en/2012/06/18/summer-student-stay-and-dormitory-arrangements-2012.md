STU’s 2012 student summer vacation runs from July 1 through August 18. Students who need to remain on campus for reasons such as military training, Medical School classes, academic research, public-welfare activities, or examination preparation must apply online from June 19 to 29 using their campus-network accounts through the [student dormitory system](http://dorm.stu.edu.cn/). Applications must include the stay period, a telephone number, and the reason for staying.

Students approved to stay must pay their dormitory utility costs during the summer; no subsidy will be provided. Utilities will be billed in two periods, July 4–16 and July 17–August 18. A stay spanning both periods will be billed for the entire summer period. Dormitories without an application or approval will have their power cut on July 4, with unified power restoration scheduled for August 17.

Dormitory areas will operate under 24-hour access control throughout the summer. Approved residents must carry their campus cards and register when entering or leaving. Outside visitors may not stay overnight. Students should secure doors and windows and turn off water and electricity when leaving. Students who leave early and are the only approved residents remaining in a dormitory should register for a power cut at the Student Community Management Service Center.
