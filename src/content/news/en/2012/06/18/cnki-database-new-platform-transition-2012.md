According to a notice from CNKI operator Tongfang Knowledge Network, updates on the old CNKI database platform are behind those on the new platform, and the old platform will gradually stop receiving updates and operating. The STU Library advises faculty members and students to become familiar with searching and downloading literature through the new platform.

The new platform can be accessed at:

- Public Internet: <http://epub.cnki.net>
- Education network: <http://epub.edu.cnki.net>

After entering the platform, readers can select “Academic Literature Database” for cross-database searches, or search individual collections including the “China Academic Journals Network Publishing Database,” “China Doctoral Dissertations Full-Text Database,” and “China Master’s Theses Full-Text Database.”
