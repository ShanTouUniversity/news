STU’s 2012 national college-admissions work is expected to begin in early July and conclude later that month. The Admissions Office has begun organizing staff recommendations to support the admissions process.

The Discipline Inspection and Audit Office, School of Humanities, School of Science, School of Engineering, School of Law, Business School, Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication, Changjiang School of Arts and Design, and Medical School should each recommend one person. The Academic Affairs Office and Network and Information Center should each recommend two people. The Resource Management Office, also responsible for security, should assign one leader to oversee security, water, and electricity services during admissions.

Recommended staff must act impartially, work carefully, maintain proper conduct, follow regulations, and be familiar with computer operations. They must have no immediate family member taking that year’s national college entrance examination and must be able to participate throughout the new-student admissions period. Units should complete the recommendation form and submit their lists to the Admissions Office by June 22.
