It is reported that, according to a notice from the Academic Affairs Office, students of the 2011 cohort who ordered the textbook Military Theory and Skills Training Course through the credit system should collect the books by class at the Academic Affairs Office's academic services section (Administration Building West 402) on the afternoon of June 29. The textbook fee of 22.73 yuan per copy will be charged through the campus card.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
