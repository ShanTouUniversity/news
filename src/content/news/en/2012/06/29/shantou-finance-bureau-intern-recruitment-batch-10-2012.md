It is reported that, due to work needs, the Financial Work Bureau of the Shantou Municipal People's Government is recruiting four interns from the Business School of Shantou University. The recruitment targets graduate students and 2009 undergraduates of the Business School. Applicants should have strong writing skills and a certain understanding of current economic development and reform trends.

The internship will start on July 11, 2012 and last for three months. Interested applicants should send the "Registration Form for Interns of the Shantou Municipal Financial Office" and their "Internship Work Plan" to Mr./Ms. Chen at chenyl@stu.edu.cn before 17:00 on July 6, 2012. Interviews will be held at 15:00 on July 10 in the college seminar room (second floor, Wenge Building West).

The bureau will provide interns with an appropriate labor subsidy, and other matters will follow the relevant arrangements of the Business School's student internship base.
