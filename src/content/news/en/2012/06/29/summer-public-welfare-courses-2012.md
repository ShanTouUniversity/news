It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice publishing the class times and locations of its 2012 summer public-welfare courses. The arrangements for the "Dawn Assistance Service," the "'Passing the Torch' Charity Action" and the "'Love Gathering in Rehabilitation Villages' Charity Action" are available in the attachments to the notice; locations for some courses not yet confirmed will be announced before July 3.

The committee reminds enrolled students to follow the campus office information network, make the necessary preparations, and attend the courses on time. The class time and location of the "'Three Rural Areas' Service-Learning Course" will be announced separately (initially scheduled before July 3), and faculty and students are advised to watch for the notice on the campus information network.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
>
> ⚠️ Please refer to the attachments in the OA system for the detailed class times and locations.
