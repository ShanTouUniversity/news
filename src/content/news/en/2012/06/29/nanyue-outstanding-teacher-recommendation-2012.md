It is reported that, in line with the Guangdong Department of Human Resources and Social Security notice on the 2012 selection and commendation of Nanyue Outstanding Teachers and Educators (Document Yue Ren She Fa [2012] No. 135), Shantou University (including its Medical College) may nominate 5 "Nanyue Outstanding Teachers" and 1 "Nanyue Outstanding Educator" this year.

Under the nomination procedure, each unit is asked to publicize the selection criteria and relevant rules among faculty and educators, encourage nominations and applications, and determine its own nominees after careful review. Each unit may nominate no more than 2 outstanding teachers and 1 outstanding educator. The university will form a review panel with representatives from teaching units and relevant functional departments to select the university's final nominees from the unit-level candidates.

Nomination materials must be submitted to the Human Resources Office (Administration Building East 406) before July 5, including the Guangdong 2012 Nanyue Outstanding Teacher/Educator Approval Form in 12 copies (6 originals, printed and copied double-sided), one copy of the Nominee Summary List, and one copy of relevant certificates and supporting documents. All materials must be printed double-sided on A4 paper, and the approval form and summary list must also be emailed to the designated address.
