It is reported that Shantou University's Staff Union will hold the 2012 staff parent-child garden party on campus from 4:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Sunday, July 8. Faculty and staff may collect activity tickets from the union office between 9:00 a.m. and 12:00 p.m. on July 2, with the detailed program provided in the attached activity guide.

The union reminds participants that faculty, staff and their family members can join the activities with valid tickets, and children must be accompanied by at least one parent. Parents should look after their children, choose appropriate games and stay safe during the event. Participants are asked to wear flat sports shoes and comfortable clothing, protect the campus environment, and take food from the buffet only as needed to avoid waste. The event is limited to STU main-campus staff and their families; tickets must not be transferred to people outside the university.
