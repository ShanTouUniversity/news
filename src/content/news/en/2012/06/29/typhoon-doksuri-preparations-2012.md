It is reported that the Shantou Meteorological Observatory issued a typhoon advisory on the morning of June 29. At 08:00 that day, Severe Tropical Storm Doksuri was centered at 20.1 degrees north latitude and 118.0 degrees east longitude, about 490 kilometers southeast of Shenzhen over the South China Sea, with maximum winds of force 10 near its center. Doksuri is forecast to move rapidly northwest at 25 to 30 kilometers per hour and make landfall along the coast between Huizhou and Zhanjiang from the morning to afternoon of June 30.

To safeguard the campus, STU's Flood, Typhoon and Drought Control Headquarters issued a notice the same day, asking all units to promptly inform faculty, staff and students and complete typhoon preparations. Key measures include: closing and securing doors and windows of offices, laboratories, classrooms, dormitories, passageways and adjacent public areas, and switching off power; in the event of torrential rain, flash floods or landslides, the top priority is the personal safety of faculty, staff and students, with specific actions to follow the STU Typhoon Emergency Plan.
