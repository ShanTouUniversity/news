It is reported that Shantou University will hold the 2012 student military training mobilization meeting at 8:30 a.m. on July 2 in the Grand Auditorium. According to the notice issued by the Student Affairs Office, each college must have its students seated at their designated places by 8:10 a.m., with the seating plan attached to the notice. Secretaries (or deputy secretaries) of each college (residential college) are to sit on the rostrum.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
