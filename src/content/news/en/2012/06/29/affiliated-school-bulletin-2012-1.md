It is reported that the Affiliated Middle School of Shantou University has recently published the first issue of its 2012 newsletter, "STU Affiliated Middle School Bulletin." The issue was distributed through the university's OA system and brings together recent updates on the school's education and teaching activities.

According to the report, this is the school's first bulletin of the year, intended to inform teachers, students and other followers of the school about its latest developments.

The full text of this issue was published as a document attachment; readers may refer to the original notice on the OA system for details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
