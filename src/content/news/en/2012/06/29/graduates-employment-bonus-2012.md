It is reported that employment bonuses for main-campus 2012 bachelor's graduates of Shantou University have been deposited into the graduates' Bank of China accounts. According to a notice from the Student Affairs Office, colleges are asked to notify graduates who received the bonus to check their accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
