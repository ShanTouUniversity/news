It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University has announced that its national judicial examination training will resume on June 30 and run full-time during the summer vacation, continuing until the examination.

This stage will complete all the remaining courses of this round of training consecutively, including the systematic intensive course (unfinished part), the statutory provisions review course, the mock-examination intensive course, and the pre-examination breakthrough course. Training is held from 08:30 to 12:00 in the morning and from 14:30 to 18:00 in the afternoon, with focused review from 19:00 to 22:00 in the evening.
