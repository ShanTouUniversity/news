It is reported that, to cultivate and enhance students' leadership abilities, Shantou University will run a 2012 leadership exchange programme with the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. Following the selection notice issued on June 19 and recommendations by units, 26 students have been shortlisted for interviews, which will be held on July 2.

The interviews will take place at 19:30 on Monday, July 2, 2012, in Multimedia Classroom 726 of Building 789, in the form of individual presentations. The process covers a self-introduction, an introduction of the exchange plan, an English test, and questions from the panel, lasting about seven minutes per person. The self-introduction and the introduction of the exchange plan are combined and may not exceed three minutes, while the English conversation and questions may not exceed four minutes.

The review panel will select 10 student representatives through the interviews, scoring candidates on five criteria — communication skills, adaptability, deportment, work experience and English proficiency — with 20 points for each (100 points in total). Selection will in principle follow the quota allocated to each unit, with quotas subject to appropriate adjustment when necessary. Candidates should arrive 15 minutes early to draw lots for the interview order.
