It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University recently announced the results of its 2012 review of student extracurricular science and technology innovation projects. A total of 51 projects applied for funding. Following review by the college's expert panel on student extracurricular academic and technological activities, 44 projects, including "Research on the Performance and Application of Rubberized Concrete Foundations," passed the preliminary defense and were approved.

The college hopes that the approved project teams will follow the requirements of their projects and carry out their research and development work carefully under the guidance of their supervisors.
