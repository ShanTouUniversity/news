It is reported that, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the "STU Implementation Measures for Undergraduate Innovation Experiment Projects (Trial)," the STU Academic Affairs Office organized a midterm review in June of undergraduate innovation experiment projects initiated in 2011 with a one-year implementation period, and recently published the results.

A total of 110 projects were initiated in 2011, and 82 participated in this midterm review. After the office reviewed the project materials and the expert panel examined the progress logs and midterm review forms with overall deliberation, 77 of the 82 projects passed the midterm review, while 5 projects require rectification.

For projects requiring rectification, the office requires the teams to send their rectification plans to the designated mailbox (zhailiu@stu.edu.cn) for review before 15:00 on July 5. Projects that fail the review or fail to submit plans on time will be automatically terminated, and the use of project funds will be suspended during the rectification period. The rectification plans must explain in detail the improvements to the original research approach and content and their feasibility, the schedule of the revised plan, and the safeguards to ensure timely completion; the project may continue only after the plan is approved by the office.

The expert panel also advised that project leaders and members should clarify task division and proactively communicate with their supervisors to keep the implementation on schedule, strengthen their professional learning while carrying out research, and control the use of funds more reasonably. For projects that passed the midterm review, the office will continue to provide the second installment of project funding. The acceptance of these projects is scheduled for December 2012.
