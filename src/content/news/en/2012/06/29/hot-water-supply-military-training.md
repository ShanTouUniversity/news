It is reported that Shantou University will extend the hot water supply period in student dormitories until July 15 to make campus life easier for students staying on during military training.

Specifically, from July 1 to July 15, hot water will be available in student dormitories from 18:30 to 19:30 each day. The arrangement was jointly announced by the Student Affairs Office and the Resources Management Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office / Resources Management Office)
