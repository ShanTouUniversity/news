It is reported that Shantou University's 2012 student military training will run from July 2 to July 15. According to a notice from the Student Affairs Office, military skills training will take place from 7:30 to 10:30 a.m. and from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. each day during the training period.

As the basketball courts will be used as training grounds, students are asked to arrange other physical exercise activities outside the training hours and not to use courts that are occupied by military training, so as not to disturb the training of the 2011 cohort. The university apologized for any inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
