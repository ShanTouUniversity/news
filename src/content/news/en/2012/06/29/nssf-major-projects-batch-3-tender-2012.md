It is reported that the Research Affairs Office of Shantou University recently forwarded the "Tender Announcement for the Third Batch of 2012 Major Projects of the National Social Science Fund," encouraging relevant units to organize applications.

Bidders may visit the website of the National Planning Office of Philosophy and Social Science (www.npopss-cn.gov.cn) or that of the Guangdong Provincial Planning Office of Philosophy and Social Science (www.gdpplgopss.gov.cn) for relevant information and to fill in the bid forms. Bid forms must be completed on a computer, printed on both sides of A3 paper and bound at the center. They must be submitted to the Research Affairs Office before August 17, 2012; late submissions will not be accepted.

The materials to be submitted include eight paper copies of the bid form, at least one of which must be the original, plus one electronic copy of each bid form in Word format. Contacts: Huang Yumei and Wang Liju; phone: 82902563.
