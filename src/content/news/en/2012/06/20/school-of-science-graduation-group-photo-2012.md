The STU School of Science will organize graduation group photographs for its 2012 graduating class from 09:15 to 10:00 on June 26 in front of the Administration Building. If it rains, a different location will be announced.

Each class should assemble 20 minutes early, wear academic regalia, and arrange its photo formation in advance. The schedule is as follows:

| Class | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2008 Biology | 09:15–09:25 |
| 2008 Mathematics | 09:25–09:35 |
| 2008 Physics | 09:35–09:45 |
| 2008 Chemistry | 09:45–09:55 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science Youth League Committee)
