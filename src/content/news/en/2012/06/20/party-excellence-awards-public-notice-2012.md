Under STU Party Document No. 15 (2012), the STU Party Committee plans to honor advanced grassroots Party organizations and outstanding Party members who emerged from the university’s 2010–2012 excellence-striving campaign before July 1. The recommendations were made democratically by grassroots units and reviewed by the Standing Committee of the University Party Committee. The recommended organizations and individuals are now open for comments from Party members and the wider university community.

Comments may be submitted to the Organization and United Front Department by June 25. Telephone: `82902345`; email: `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. Individual names from the attached recommendation list are not reproduced here.
