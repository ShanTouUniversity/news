STU Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the intensive-session arrangements for its summer public-welfare courses. Because the course “Perspectives on Life” now requires Class A students to attend a training module on public-welfare project design and planning, its intensive sessions on the mornings of June 30 and July 1 will be held in Lecture Hall E101. Enrolled students should follow this updated arrangement.

Under the course syllabus, students enrolled in “Shuguang Study-Aid Service” do not need to attend the module on social survey research methods and report writing. Other interested faculty members and students are also welcome to attend the relevant training.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
