To support STU’s internationalized and intensive talent-development goals, Zhicheng College will hold its fifth “Peer-Mentor Development Camp” from July 3 to 10. The seven-day program will focus on personal ability, communication, teamwork, and leadership to strengthen peer-mentor work.

Designated and alternate peer mentors for the 2012–2013 academic year should prepare for the program and attend the opening ceremony and course session at 19:30 on July 3 in Lecture Room J354 of Zhicheng College. Detailed course arrangements will be issued by the college.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
