It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 20, 2012, announcing the results of the 2012 Shantou University Open Day photo contest.

A total of 657 entries were received. After evaluation by professional teachers, five winners received first, second and third prizes. The list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here for privacy.

Winning works are displayed on the Open Day website at http://openday.stu.edu.cn/news3.htm. Winners on campus may collect certificates and prizes from the Admissions Office in Room 406, West Administration Building, during working hours from June 25 to 28, with their ID card or campus card.

> ⚠️ The full list is available on the OA system.
