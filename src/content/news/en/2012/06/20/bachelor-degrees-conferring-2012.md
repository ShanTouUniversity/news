The first meeting of the eighth Degree Evaluation Committee of Shantou University was convened on June 15, 2012, to review the proposal on conferring bachelor's degrees on the graduating class of 2012. On June 20, the committee formally announced its decision to award bachelor's degrees to 1,598 students, including Cai XL.

It is reported that these students met the requirements in moral conduct and physical fitness, completed all components of their programs' teaching plans, and earned the prescribed credits, thus satisfying the conditions set out in the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Academic Degrees and STU's own degree regulations.

The conferrals cover multiple program types:

| Program type | Degree | Number |
|--------------|--------|--------|
| Regular undergraduate | BA in Literature | 376 |
| Regular undergraduate | LLB in Law | 96 |
| Regular undergraduate | BBA in Management | 219 |
| Regular undergraduate | BSc in Science | 174 |
| Regular undergraduate | BEng in Engineering | 297 |
| Regular undergraduate | BEc in Economics | 93 |
| Regular undergraduate | BM in Medicine | 246 |
| Adult higher education | Literature/Law/Management/Engineering/Medicine/Science | 87 |
| Second bachelor's degree | Management | 10 |
| Double bachelor's degrees | Management and Economics | 13 |

The categories above total 1,611 degree conferrals. Of these, the 1,598 students named in the decision are graduates of regular undergraduate, adult higher education, and second bachelor's degree programs, while another 13 students earned double bachelor's degrees (not double-counted in that total). The complete list of degree recipients is available in the annex to the committee's document.
