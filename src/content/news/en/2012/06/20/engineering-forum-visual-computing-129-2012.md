The 129th STU Engineering Research Forum will take place from 09:30 to 10:30 on June 21 in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room. Dr. Zhou Jianlong of the Faculty of Information Technologies at the University of Sydney will present a lecture titled “Visual Computing and Beyond.”

The lecture is open to faculty members in the Engineering School’s Computer Science Department and other interested members of the STU community.
