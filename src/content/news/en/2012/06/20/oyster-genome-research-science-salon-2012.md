STU’s School of Science will hold a Science Salon lecture from 09:00 to 11:00 on June 25 in Science and Technology Building Room 205. Professor Zhang Guofan, a researcher and doctoral supervisor at the Institute of Oceanology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, will speak on advances in oyster genome research.

Professor Zhang directs the institute’s Center for Marine Biotechnology. His research focuses on shellfish aquaculture, aquaculture genetics, and breeding technologies, including shellfish aquaculture theory and technology, genetic improvement, and shellfish genomics. He helped initiate an oyster genome-sequencing project and works on genome-assisted and designed breeding. The lecture is open to interested faculty members and students.
