It is reported that the Standing Committee of the university's Party Committee, at its 5th meeting in 2012, proposed to appoint Ji Rongxin to a mid-level management position in the Party Committee's Organization and United Front Work Department. The department has issued a publicity notice (Document No. 6 [2012]) in accordance with the university's trial measures for pre-appointment publicity.

## Candidate Profile

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Name | Ji Rongxin |
| Gender | Male |
| Date of birth | July 1979 |
| Native place | Shantou, Guangdong |
| Education | Bachelor's degree |
| Party membership | Joined the CPC in June 2001 |
| Career start | July 2004 |

### Education and Work History

| Period | Experience |
|--------|------------|
| Sept. 1999–June 2002 | Multimedia Networking and Technology, College of Engineering, STU |
| Sept. 2002–June 2004 | Computer Science and Technology, College of Engineering, STU |
| July 2004–Mar. 2009 | Organization, Publicity and United Front Work Department, STU |
| Apr. 2009–present | Organization and United Front Work Department, STU Party Committee |

Those who wish to comment on the proposed appointment may submit written feedback or contact the university's Discipline Inspection Commission in person or by phone before June 27, 2012. Organization feedback should bear an official seal; individual feedback should include the sender's real name. Contact: 82902346, o_jj@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, STU Party Committee)
