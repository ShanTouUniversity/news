It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 20, 2012, that the archives for the Class of 2012 will soon be dispatched and relevant materials should be collected.

Units are asked to submit materials to be included in the archives to the Career Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office (Room 201, Student Affairs Building) by June 29, 2012, for filing.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
