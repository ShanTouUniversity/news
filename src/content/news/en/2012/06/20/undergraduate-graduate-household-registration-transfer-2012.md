To facilitate household-registration transfers for 2012 undergraduate graduates, STU Resource Management Office has coordinated with the local police station. Before diplomas are issued, graduates who moved their household registration to STU when they enrolled may begin the transfer process using the diploma number supplied by the university.

Eligible graduates must complete the procedure by July 4 with their household-registration card and identity card. The card can be collected from the Security Office on the second floor of the STU Post Office before the graduate visits the household-registration office of the local police station. Graduates transferring their registration to urban Shantou may collect the card and go directly to the police station responsible for the destination address. Those returning registration to their place of origin must provide the detailed destination address; those transferring it to an employer must provide a registration certificate or an acceptance letter from the employer’s personnel department.

A delegated applicant must provide a letter of authorization, copies of the identity cards of both parties, and a copy of the diploma stamped by the relevant school. After the transfer, graduates must register at the destination police station within the period stated on the transfer certificate; otherwise, the certificate cannot be returned for reprocessing. The certificate fee is four yuan.

Security Office: `82903436`; local police-station household-registration office: `82756003`. Processing may be delayed for graduates missing from the completed-student list supplied by the Academic Affairs Office; subsequent university notices will prevail.
