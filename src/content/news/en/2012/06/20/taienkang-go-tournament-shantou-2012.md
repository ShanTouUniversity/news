The Shantou Municipal Committee for Government Offices, Shantou Sports Bureau, and Shantou Go Association will jointly hold the “Taienkang Cup” Shantou Fifth Go Invitational from July 7 to 8 at the CPC Shantou Municipal Party School in Beishan Bay, Haojiang District.

The competition is open to Go players who are officials at deputy-division level or above in central or provincial organizations based in Shantou or local units, senior professionals such as associate professors, senior engineers, senior secondary-school teachers, licensed lawyers, and certified public accountants, and enterprise managers at deputy-general-manager level or above. Players will compete in three groups, for officials, senior professionals, and enterprise managers, through a five-round Swiss-style points competition. The latest rules of the Chinese Weiqi Association will apply. Each game will last two hours in total, one hour per player; exceeding the time limit results in a loss.

The notice circulated by the STU Labor Union lists the registration period as June 21–28, 2010, which conflicts with the 2012 event year. This article does not silently correct the date; prospective participants should verify the actual registration schedule with the organizer or university Labor Union.
