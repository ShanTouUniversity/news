It is reported that STU has renewed accident insurance for all full-time students enrolled at the main campus, including graduate students, to strengthen safety protection and reduce the cost and administrative burden of separate purchases by university units and student organizations. The coverage period is May 1, 2012 through April 30, 2013.

The maximum payout under the renewed policy is RMB 50,000. Combined with the RMB 10,000 purchased previously, the total maximum coverage is RMB 60,000. Coverage applies within mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan. Students traveling to other countries must report to the insurer in advance and provide their name, destination, travel dates, and study content; the Logistics Office of the Resource Management Office will assist with the procedures.

An insurance incident must be reported to the insurer within 24 hours. For procedures, contact Huang Shaoxiong at the Logistics Office of the Resource Management Office: `82902115`. To protect privacy, the personal mobile number in the original notice is not published here.
