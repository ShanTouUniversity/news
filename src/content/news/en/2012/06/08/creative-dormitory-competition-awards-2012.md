It is reported that STU’s second Creative Dormitory Competition, themed “Innovative Thinking and New Community Culture,” selected 12 winning teams based on online and on-site voting. Awards included Best Work, Popularity, Best Creativity, and Excellence.

Winning works included Assorted Eight-Treasure Rice, Memories, Enjoying Life, Those Years We Spent Together in the Dormitory, Money-Saving Tips, Mobile Phone Projector, Silk Cave, Dormitory Model DIY, Mosquito-Proof Window Screen, Green Dormitory Collection, The Other Side of the Paper Tube, and Showmanship. The entries covered talent, handicrafts, electronics, and creative ideas for residential life.

The competition aimed to develop students’ creative thinking and practical skills and strengthen communication among dormitory residents. The organizers thanked faculty and students for their participation and support.
