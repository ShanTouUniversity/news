It is reported that the 126th STU Engineering Research Forum will be held from 09:00 to 10:30 on June 11 in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room. Lai Jianhuang, professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Information Science and Technology of Sun Yat-sen University and deputy dean of its School of Information Science and Technology, will give the presentation.

Titled “Recent Advances in Face Recognition and Video-Surveillance Technology,” the presentation will discuss technology trends and applied research in these fields. Engineering School faculty members and interested students are welcome to attend.
