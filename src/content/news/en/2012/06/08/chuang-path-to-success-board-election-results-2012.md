It is reported that STU’s Chuang Path to Success Network held its 12th council election on June 6 in Lecture Classroom E201. The congress reviewed the work report of the 11th council and elected members of the 12th council by ballot.

The principal office assignments are:

| Position | Elected representative |
| --- | --- |
| Chief executive officer | Lin SQ |
| Chief operating officer | Xu ZG |
| Chief technology officer | Che YC |
| Chief administrative officer | Zhao SX |

The students’ names are abbreviated under the privacy rules. Their year and major information from the original notice is not published here.
