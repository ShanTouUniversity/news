It is reported that, because the university Party Committee had another scheduled assignment, STU’s meeting on recommendations for university-wide “Strive for Excellence” awards was postponed from June 11 to June 12 at 15:00. It will remain in Meeting Room 311 of the Administration Building. Leaders of the Medical College Party Committee, Party general branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches should attend on time. The meeting will coordinate the university-wide award-recommendation process.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee)
