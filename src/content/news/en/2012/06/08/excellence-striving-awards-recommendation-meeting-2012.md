It is reported that STU will hold a meeting on recommendations for university-wide “Strive for Excellence” awards at 15:00 on June 11 in Meeting Room 311 of the Administration Building. Leaders of the Medical College Party Committee, Party general branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches should attend on time. The meeting will coordinate the university-wide award-recommendation process.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee)
