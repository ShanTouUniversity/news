It is reported that interviews for the first batch of positions in STU’s student entrepreneurship practice program will be held on June 9 and 10 in Room 731 of Building 789. The interviews are for students who applied for internships with China Mobile Communications Group Guangdong Co., Ltd. Shantou Branch.

Applicants should follow the individual interview arrangements in the attachment, bring their application forms, and attend on time. To protect student privacy, the applicant list and individual interview slots are not published here; the original OA attachment controls the arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Association Federation)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original notice in the OA system for the applicant list and individual interview schedule.
