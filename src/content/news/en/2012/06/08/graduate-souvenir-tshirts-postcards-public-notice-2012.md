It is reported that STU plans to work with a student team to collect public orders for graduation souvenir T-shirts and campus-view postcards. Because little time remains in the semester, the university proposes extending the T-shirt design used for Campus Open Day to graduation souvenirs and adding a postcard project, rather than organizing a new design selection process this term.

The proposed “Because You Are Special” STU crew-neck T-shirt will cost RMB 35 each, and STU campus-view postcards will cost RMB 2 each. The student team will arrange production with a manufacturer according to the orders received. The proposal will apply only during the current semester.

Comments may be emailed to `o_xzxx@stu.edu.cn` by June 11. The university plans to organize an open call for souvenir designs and licensing proposals next semester.

To protect student privacy, the names of the student team members are not published here.
