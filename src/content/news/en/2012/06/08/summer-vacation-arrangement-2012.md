It is reported that the Administrative Office of Shantou University has released a notice (STU Administrative Document [2012] No. 4) detailing the schedule for the 2012 summer break and the upcoming summer term.

## Schedule for Students and Faculty

According to the academic calendar, the summer break for current students runs from July 1 (Sunday) to August 18 (Saturday), with military training for the 2011 undergraduate cohort to be arranged separately by the Student Affairs Office. Current students must register on August 19, and the summer term begins on Monday, August 20. Freshmen will report on September 2; registration for the autumn term takes place on September 16, with classes starting on Monday, September 17.

Faculty begin their summer break on July 4 (Wednesday) and return on August 17 (Friday) to prepare for the new term and advise freshmen on course selection under departmental arrangements, before starting summer-term teaching on August 20. Teaching units have been asked to make full use of the summer term for teaching and research activities and to arrange appropriate social practice opportunities for students not taking courses. Graduate schools and supervisors will schedule teaching, research and practice activities according to their training plans.

## Staying on Campus and Safety

Students staying in dormitories over the break must apply through the counselor's office of their residential area, which will arrange accommodation together with the Student Community Management Service Center. The university also requires colleges and relevant departments to provide safety education to students and faculty returning home, raising awareness of personal and property safety during the holiday.

## Staggered Leave for Administrative Staff

Under STU Administrative Document [2009] No. 08, administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff will continue the winter/summer working system: all administrative and service units must maintain normal services during the break. As fewer university-wide activities take place in summer, units may arrange staggered leave for staff between July 4 and August 15. During this period, their working hours are adjusted to 8:30–11:30 am and 3:00–5:00 pm, with staff on leave required to stay reachable and return to work as needed. From August 16, all administrative, teaching-support and logistics staff resume normal working hours.

Units must submit their staffing arrangements for the break to the Administrative Office by the end of the working day on June 26.
