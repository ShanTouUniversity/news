It is reported that, ahead of World Blood Donor Day on June 14, STU Youth Volunteers Association will hold a “Happy Blood Donation” drive from 09:00 to 16:30 on June 10. The event aims to promote voluntary blood donation and volunteer service.

The drive will take place in the open area in front of the shopping area below Dining Hall 2 and below Dining Hall 4. According to the event guidance, donors should be 18 to 55 years old, with a minimum weight of 50 kilograms for men and 45 kilograms for women, and should be in good health without infectious disease. Donors should not have had a cold or fever or taken medication during the preceding week, should rest adequately the day before, eat normally on the day, and avoid donating on an empty stomach. Blood donation should occur no more than once every six months.

Donors should bring an identity card or blood-donation certificate to protect donor rights and prevent substitution.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association)
