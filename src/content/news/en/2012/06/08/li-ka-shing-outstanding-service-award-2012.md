It is reported that STU has selected three recipients for the 2011–2012 Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Service Award following recommendations from university units and review by the award committee. The confirmed recipients include Xu Zhenxiong and Yang Jian. The source text corrupts the name of the third recipient, so it cannot be reliably reproduced.

The award recognizes individuals who have made outstanding contributions through their work and service to the university. The results have now been formally announced.
