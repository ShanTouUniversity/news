It is reported that STU College of Liberal Arts has opened applications for its 2012 extracurricular academic and science projects for undergraduates in the college. Applications will be accepted from June 11 through August 20.

Projects must be research projects completed by students in their spare time while enrolled. Graduation theses, graduation projects, and academic-year papers are not eligible. Categories include social survey reports and academic papers, literary and artistic works, and entrepreneurial practice. Interdisciplinary teams are encouraged.

Applicants must complete the STU Student Scientific Research Project Application Form and submit one printed copy and one electronic copy to the College of Liberal Arts Youth League Committee. The college Student Extracurricular Academic and Science Activities Leading Group will review the applications before approved projects are announced. Materials may be sent to `twwxy@stu.edu.cn`; detailed forms should follow the original OA attachments.
