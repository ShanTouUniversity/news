It is reported that STU Kindergarten plans to enroll new junior-class students in 2012. Under the applicable rules, children must be at least three years old, meaning they must have been born on or before August 31, 2009. Parents of eligible STU faculty and staff children should register from 08:00 to 11:00 or from 15:00 to 17:00 on June 14 at the kindergarten office on the second floor, bringing the original and copies of the child’s birth certificate, household registration booklet, and parent’s work ID.

Children of established STU main-campus faculty and staff, including agency-hired personnel, who were born on or before February 28, 2010 and are at least two and a half but under three years old may submit an application if they have a demonstrated need for enrollment. Parents should bring the documents above and an enrollment application report during the same hours. The competent authority will determine admissions under the university’s rules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Kindergarten and Resource Management Office)
