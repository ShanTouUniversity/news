It is reported that STU Engineering School’s 22nd World Engineers Forum will be held from 15:00 to 17:30 on June 13 in the New Library Lecture Hall. The speaker will be Li Zexiang, professor in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering and director of the Center for Intelligent Automation at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

Li has long worked in teaching and research in robotics, manufacturing science, and automation and has also focused on reforming graduate engineering education and developing systems for training innovative talent. He studied or worked at Carnegie Mellon University and the University of California, Berkeley, and currently chairs Googol Technology (HK) Limited. He also serves as a professor or doctoral supervisor at several universities.

The forum is open to the university community. Those interested may reserve a place at <http://activity.stu.edu.cn/> by 15:00 on June 11.
