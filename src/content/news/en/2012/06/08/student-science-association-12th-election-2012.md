It is reported that STU Student Science and Technology Association will hold its 11th annual summary and awards meeting and election of the 12th Executive Committee at 19:00 on June 10 in Lecture Classroom E202. The meeting will review the 11th association’s work, recognize outstanding members, and elect the 12th Executive Committee.

The 12th Executive Committee will have 11 members. Fifteen candidates were selected through open recruitment across the university, voluntary applications, assessment by the 11th Executive Committee, and approval by the university Youth League Committee. The candidates will give public speeches and answer questions at the election meeting. Members of the 11th association will vote, and the 11 candidates with the highest comprehensive scores will be elected. The new committee will then elect its presidium and ministerial group.

To protect student privacy, the candidate list and individual information are not published here.
