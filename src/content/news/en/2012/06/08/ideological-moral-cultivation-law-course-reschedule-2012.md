It is reported that STU has revised the schedule for Class 52204, Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law, because Shen Yiyong of the Social Sciences Department will be away at a meeting. The class originally scheduled for periods 6–8 on Friday of Week 16, June 15, in Classroom E208 will not meet. Its make-up session will take place during periods A–C on Monday of Week 16, June 11, in Classroom E301.

The Academic Affairs Office asks enrolled students to inform one another and attend according to the revised schedule.
