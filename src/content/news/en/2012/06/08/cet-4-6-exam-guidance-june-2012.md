It is reported that the first-half 2012 National College English Test will be held on June 16. CET-4 will run from 09:00 to 11:20, and CET-6 from 15:00 to 17:20. CET-4 candidates must enter the examination room by 08:45, while CET-6 candidates must enter by 14:45 and test their headphones. To ensure that candidates can receive the listening signal, the university will arrange a headphone test from 15:00 to 17:00 on June 13 in Buildings E, G, and D. Candidates should bring their own headphones to their examination building and tune them to FM 73.2.

Candidates must bring an identity card, student card, and admission ticket. A campus card cannot replace the student card. Candidates who have lost an identity document must bring certification issued by their department and signed by its academic-affairs staff member to the examination office in Lecture Classroom E101. Candidates who have forgotten an identity document must sign a statement at the same office. Papers may be handled according to the rules if the required document is not supplied.

Candidates should bring headphones, batteries, a 2B pencil, a black ballpoint pen, and an eraser. Books, paper, bags, recording devices, electronic organizers, and communication devices including MP3 players and mobile phones are prohibited. Headphones must be removed after the listening section. Late candidates will not be admitted after the examination begins, and candidates may not leave early or submit papers before the end.

The examination office is in Lecture Classroom E101 of the Teaching Building; telephones: `2402` and `2623`. Candidate regulations and detailed requirements should follow the original OA attachments.
