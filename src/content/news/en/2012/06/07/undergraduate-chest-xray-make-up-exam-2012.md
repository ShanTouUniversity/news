It is reported that some STU main-campus undergraduates in the 2010 cohort did not complete the chest X-ray during the May medical examination. The university has scheduled make-up chest X-rays from 15:00 to 16:50 on June 8 at the University Hospital.

The Youth League committees of the schools and colleges will notify the students concerned. Because the original attachment contains a list of individuals, the names are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original notice in the OA system for the list of students requiring a make-up chest X-ray.
