It is reported that STU established 139 graduate “three-assistant” positions in spring 2012, including 88 teaching-assistant positions and 51 administrative-assistant positions. The Graduate School has begun the corresponding assessment process.

Instructors whose courses include graduate teaching assistants must assess the assistants and complete the teaching-assistant position assessment form. Units hosting administrative assistants must assess the students in service and complete the administrative-assistant assessment form, attaching proof that the host unit reimbursed the administrative-assistant allowance.

Units must submit the compiled materials to Room G-204 of the Graduate School by the end of work on June 13. Positions without an assessment, or without proof of allowance payment, will not be accepted for 2012 fall-semester three-assistant applications.
