It is reported that STU’s Degree Office and Graduate School have opened applications for the Guangdong Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Author Funding Project. Eligible applicants are authors selected for a Guangdong Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation in 2011 who meet the requirements of the relevant management measures.

Each funded project will receive RMB 30,000 to 60,000. Applicants must complete the project application form. Relevant units must submit seven printed copies of the application form and two copies of the summary form to the Graduate School by June 27, and send an electronic copy to `xwb@stu.edu.cn`. Late submissions will not be accepted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Degree Office and Graduate School)
