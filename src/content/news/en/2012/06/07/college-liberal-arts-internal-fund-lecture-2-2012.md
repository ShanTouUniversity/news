It is reported that the second 2012 STU College of Liberal Arts internal-fund academic lecture will be held from 15:00 to 17:30 on June 7 in Room W201 of the Liberal Arts Building. The lecture is open to the university community.

The session will feature two presentations: Dr. Yang Weigang will discuss “Narrative Space and Spatial Narrative in the Li Yang Romance,” while Dr. Cai Qiuyan will present “We Are Night Owls”: *New Tide* in the New Culture Movement. The talks will approach narrative studies and modern cultural periodicals from different perspectives.
