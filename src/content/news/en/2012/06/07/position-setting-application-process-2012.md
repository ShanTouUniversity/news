It is reported that STU has launched its first position-setting and position-application process under Guangdong’s guidance for position management in public education institutions and STU’s Position-Setting and Position-Application Implementation Measures. The process covers employees in establishment-based internal positions; other personnel are outside its scope.

After appointments are completed, personnel will receive the national salary associated with the appointed position series and level, particularly the position-salary component. Personnel whose current national salary exceeds the salary for the appointed position may retain the current national standard. Position totals in each category will generally follow the number of currently serving establishment-based employees. Administrative departments will generally set management positions, while eligible departments may apply for senior professional positions in engineering, accounting, auditing, or records management as auxiliary series.

Administrative departments seeking auxiliary-series positions must apply to the Human Resources Office by June 11. Faculty and staff must submit the relevant position application form to their unit by June 13. After review and recommendation, units must submit the materials to the Human Resources Office by June 19 and send the specified electronic materials. University committees will review and publish the proposals before approval by the university leading group, after which the results will be filed with the Guangdong Department of Education and Department of Human Resources and Social Security.
