It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University has invited Huang Xiaozhen, an etiquette trainer from Hong Kong, to hold workplace image and social etiquette training for students who will take part in this year's summer internship program in Hong Kong, helping them prepare before departure.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Trainer | Huang Xiaozhen, etiquette expert from Hong Kong |
| Time | June 9, 14:30 – 17:30; June 10, 8:30 – 11:30 |
| Venue | Conference Room 213, Law School |

Participants taking part in this year's Hong Kong internship are required to attend in formal attire and arrive on time. The training will focus on professional image and social etiquette, laying the groundwork for the students' upcoming summer internships in Hong Kong.
