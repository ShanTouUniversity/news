It is reported that the sixth STU Teaching Ideas Discussion faculty workshop will be held at 15:00 on June 13 in Classroom E410 of the Teaching Building. The workshop will focus on undergraduate laboratory-teaching reform and will be led by Yin Yegao, Chen Jiehui, and Chen Shaobo.

The session will examine organic-chemistry laboratory reform grounded in holistic thinking, practical experience in laboratory teaching, and the opening, organization, and implementation of laboratory instruction. Faculty members and teaching-support staff interested in undergraduate laboratory reform, teaching methods, or teaching administration may register voluntarily by sending the attendee list to the Academic Affairs Office at `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn` by the end of work on June 11.
