It is reported that, following the decision of the seventh university leadership meeting on June 5, STU’s swimming pool will open to faculty members and students from June 9. The schedule is:

| Date | Opening hours | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 9–10 | 17:00–18:30 |  |
| June 11–15 | 17:45–19:15 | Physical-education classes run until 17:30 |
| From June 16 | 17:00–18:30 |  |

From 2012, swimming tickets will no longer be used. Access will be managed through campus-card payment at RMB 5 per visit. The Physical Education Department reminds swimmers to observe safety rules, take care of equipment, and maintain courteous conduct. The pool has been fitted with closed-circuit monitoring.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
