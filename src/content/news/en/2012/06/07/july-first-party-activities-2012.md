It is reported that, under the university Party Committee’s work plan, STU will not hold a university-wide commemorative event during this year’s July 1 period. The Medical College Party Committee, Party general branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches will organize themed activities in connection with the “Strive for Excellence” campaign and the Year of Grassroots Organization Development, including Party oath ceremonies for new members.

Units should notify the Party Committee Organization and United Front Department of their activity times in advance. The department will provide Party flags and the oath text and arrange department leaders to attend and lead the oath.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee)
