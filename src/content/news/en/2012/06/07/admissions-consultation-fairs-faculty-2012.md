It is reported that STU Admissions Office plans to organize faculty participation in regional admissions consultation fairs in mid-to-late June. Each school or residential college should recommend two to four teachers familiar with STU and experienced in admissions publicity, complete the personnel registration form, and send it to `o_zsb@stu.edu.cn` by June 12. The Admissions Office will form publicity groups according to its needs, and participants must follow the university’s unified arrangements and direction.

Participating teachers will introduce STU and its admissions information, answer questions from prospective students and parents, distribute publicity materials, and visit selected local secondary schools. The university’s admissions-publicity special fund will cover their travel expenses.

The university will not generally arrange out-of-province consultation fairs this year; individual cases requiring participation will be discussed separately. The Admissions Office plans to hold training for traveling admissions-publicity staff in mid-June, and proposed participants must complete the training before representing the university. Fair dates and venues should follow the original OA attachments. Telephone: `82902403`.
