It is reported that STU Admissions Office is recruiting 10 student assistants to support admissions publicity and consultation. Undergraduate and graduate students with strong academic and personal records, an understanding of STU’s educational philosophy and strengths, good computer and internet skills, clear written and spoken communication, patience, and a strong sense of responsibility may apply. Students with prior experience in school outreach, admissions publicity, or Open Day volunteering will receive priority under equal conditions.

The assistants will answer questions from prospective students, parents, and teachers through telephone, QQ, and Weibo; introduce STU and its admissions information; and monitor platforms including Baidu Tieba, Baidu Zhidao, Tencent Gaokao, and Sina Gaokao. Work will run from June 19 through July 3 in shifts from 08:30 to 12:00, 12:00 to 17:00, and 17:00 to 21:00 at the Admissions Office. Compensation follows the university’s work-study rate.

Applicants must submit an application through the [student work-study application system](http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn/) by 17:00 on June 12. Interviews will be held from 14:30 to 17:00 on June 13 in Classroom D301; applicants unable to attend may interview during office hours on June 14 at the Admissions Office in Xingxi 406 by prior appointment.

Email: `o_zsb@stu.edu.cn`; telephone: `82902403`. Application forms and detailed requirements should follow the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Admissions Office and Student Affairs Office)
