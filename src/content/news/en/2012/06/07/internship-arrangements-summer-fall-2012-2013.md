It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office has arranged student internships for the 2012–2013 summer and fall terms. Schools and colleges should follow the university’s undergraduate internship regulations and complete preparations, supervision, and assessment.

Schools and colleges must identify or review internship providers, assign enough supervisors, secure funding, prepare or revise course syllabi, develop internship plans, and hold orientation meetings on safety, discipline, and courteous conduct. Beginning with the 2009 cohort, internship records must be considered in the assessment of internship-course results. Units arranging providers centrally should create and publish project topics through their academic affairs staff; students who find their own providers may create and submit their own topics.

By June 20, schools and colleges must submit the previous term’s internship summary forms, the current summer and fall internship teaching plans, and records of internship-orientation meetings to the Academic Affairs Office. They must also organize students to complete project selection for the relevant internship and practical courses by June 22.
