It is reported that the defense session for the third round of service-learning project applications at the School of Engineering of Shantou University was held on June 1. Judges conducted fair and impartial reviews of the projects presented, and based on the results — together with project type and practical impact — the school decided to grant approval and funding to four projects.

## Approved Projects

| No. | Project Title |
| ---- | ---- |
| 1 | Walking with You, Helping You Move Forward (与你相伴 助你前行) |
| 2 | Promoting Tradition, Passing On Care (弘扬传统 传承关爱) |
| 3 | Warm Hearts and Fragrance, Free Haircuts with Love (暖心沁香 真情义剪) |
| 4 | Loving-Heart Volunteer Teaching Series (爱心义教系列活动) |

The school expressed the hope that each approved project team will organize its activities conscientiously, integrate the philosophy of service-learning, and complete all work according to plan.
