It is reported that, under the national pilot program for paperless examinations, new candidates for Level-2 VB, VFP, and ACCESS in Guangdong’s second-half 2012 National Computer Rank Examination will take paperless tests. Candidates retaking any of the three subjects will continue to use the traditional format.

The paperless test transfers the written section of the traditional examination to a computer while keeping the content and requirements unchanged. The test lasts 130 minutes and carries 100 points, including 40 points for multiple-choice questions and 60 points for computer-operation tasks. A passing certificate requires at least 60 total points and at least 36 points in the computer-operation section. Single-subject passes and score retention do not apply to paperless subjects.

The paperless examination will follow the written and computer-operation tests under the traditional format, and all examinations will be completed by September 22. Detailed times will be announced before the examination.
