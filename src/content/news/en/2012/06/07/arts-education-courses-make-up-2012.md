It is reported that STU’s Arts Education Center has announced make-up sessions for classes taught by Dong Xuemin that were suspended in Week 14. All sessions will be held in the original classroom, STU202:

| Class | Course | Make-up time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 52726 | Piano Art | June 9, periods A–B |
| 52727 | Basic Music Literacy (Staff and Simplified Notation) | June 9, periods 1–2 |
| 52728 | Fundamentals of Music Theory and Ear Training | June 9, periods 8–9 |
| 52729 | Piano Art | June 9, periods 3–4 |
| 52730 | Fundamentals of Music Theory and Ear Training | June 8, periods A–B |

The Academic Affairs Office asks students enrolled in these courses to inform one another and attend according to the revised schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office and Arts Education Center)
