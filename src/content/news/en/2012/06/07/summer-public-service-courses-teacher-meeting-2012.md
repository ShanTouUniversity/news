It is reported that STU will hold a meeting for instructors and teaching assistants of its 2012 summer public-service courses at 15:00 on June 8 in Meeting Room 726 of Building 789.

The meeting will cover four courses: Dawn Student Aid Service, the “Passing on Care from Hometown to Hometown” Initiative, the “Care for Kangfu Village” Initiative, and the “Three Rural Areas” Service-Learning Course. The meeting will coordinate teaching and service-learning arrangements for the summer courses.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
