It is reported that, as the university enters the final-examination revision period, STU campus radio will suspend its midday and afternoon programs from June 11. Broadcasting is scheduled to resume after the next semester begins.

Units with special broadcasting needs may contact Liu, campus radio; telephone: `2203`; email: `o_gbds@stu.edu.cn`.
