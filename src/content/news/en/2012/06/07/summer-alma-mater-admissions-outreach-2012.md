It is reported that STU’s Student Affairs Office has launched the 2012 summer “Remember Where We Came From, Pass on the Dream” admissions outreach program for undergraduate and graduate students. Volunteers will visit their former secondary schools during the summer, introduce STU’s recent development and reforms and their own study, work, and campus-life experiences, distribute admissions materials, and submit a feedback form after the activity.

The program will also organize students to accompany STU admissions staff at university-admissions consultation fairs in different locations. Applicants should demonstrate responsibility, enthusiasm, and communication skills, attend Admissions Office training, and follow coordinated assignments. Outstanding teams and individuals will receive recognition and awards and priority consideration for future student-assistant positions. Students participating in consultation fairs may receive work clothing and an appropriate travel allowance.

Applications will mainly be submitted by teams. Students without a team may also apply, after which the Admissions Office will arrange groups according to hometown and former-school distribution. Application forms must be sent to `o_zsb@stu.edu.cn` by June 14; the detailed activity schedule should follow the original OA attachments.
