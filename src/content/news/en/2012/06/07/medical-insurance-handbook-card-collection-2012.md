It is reported that STU has arranged the distribution of medical-insurance handbooks and insurance IC cards. Eligible recipients may collect them during working hours from the ENT Department on the second floor of the University Hospital.

Because the original notice contains a list of individuals, the names are not published here. Recipients should go to the designated location according to the notice.
