It is reported that the fifth STU Teaching Ideas Discussion faculty workshop will be held at 15:00 on June 6 in Classroom E404 of the Teaching Building. Terry Bodenhorn will lead the session, titled “Peer Classroom Observation System: Design & Implementation.”

The workshop will be conducted in both Chinese and English and is open to faculty members and administrative staff.
