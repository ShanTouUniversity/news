It is reported that STU Business School has adjusted the membership of its executive committee following organizational personnel and responsibility changes. Liu Wei has stepped down as head of the Accounting Teaching and Research Section for personal reasons, and Song Xiaobao has taken over the position.

Xu Zongling will chair the executive committee. Chen Yinglin, Ouyang Feng, a member whose name is corrupted in the source file, Huang Yan, Du Mian, Bu Xiangzhi, Song Xiaobao, Qin Zhong, and Zhou Xiao’e will serve as members, with Chen Mingqin as secretary.
