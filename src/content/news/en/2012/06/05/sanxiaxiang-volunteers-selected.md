It is reported that the recruitment of student volunteers for Shantou University's 2012 summer culture, technology and health "Three Rural Visits" (San Xia Xiang) social practice program has concluded successfully, with the complete list of selected members now published.

According to the report, selections were made in line with the program's tasks and team-formation principles through fair, just and open interviews together with democratic review, combined with recommendations from relevant organizations.

The university's Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteers Association have reminded selected members to begin their preparations; follow-up arrangements will be confirmed soon, with further notices to be issued.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
