It is reported that STU Business School has established a digital teaching working group to advance digital instruction and implement measures related to advanced undergraduate education, following deliberation by the school’s executive committee.

Xu Zongling will serve as group leader, with Ouyang Feng and a deputy whose name is corrupted in the source file serving as deputy leaders. Bu Xiangzhi, Qin Zhong, Du Mian, Song Xiaobao, Zeng Fanqi, Chen Chaorong, and Wu Jingqiang will serve as members, and Chen Xin will serve as secretary.
