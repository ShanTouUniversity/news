It is reported that two fifth-grade students from the Primary School Affiliated to Shantou University achieved excellent results at the 2012 Guangdong Primary School Mathematics "Seedling Cup" (育苗杯) correspondence contest, with students Lin HC and Tang ZS each winning a second prize.

The Seedling Cup is one of the major mathematics competitions for primary school students in Guangdong Province. The awards reflect the school's achievements in mathematics education.
