It is reported that STU’s graduate course evaluation system for the second semester of the 2011–2012 academic year is now open. Graduate students enrolled in Graduate English must use their campus accounts to evaluate their instructors’ courses between June 5 and June 11, providing careful and fair feedback.

The evaluation system is available at <http://demo.stu.edu.cn/postsurvey/>. Students with questions during the process may contact the graduate academic affairs coordinator in their school or college or the Graduate School.
