It is reported that the 14th Council Congress of STU’s Student Association Federation was held on June 2 in Room D502. The congress reviewed the work report of the 13th Standing Committee and elected the 14th Standing Committee by ballot. The new standing committee subsequently held its first meeting and elected the new presidium.

The results are:

| Position | Elected representatives |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Feng XF |
| Vice chairs | He JH and Luo ZJ |
| Standing committee members | Feng XF, He JH, Li QY, Liang SP, Luo ZJ, Mo T, Zou RY, Zhou WF, and Zhang HR |

The students’ names are abbreviated under the privacy rules. Their year and major information from the original notice is not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Association Federation)
