It is reported that STU’s Student Affairs Office has announced the list of 2012 graduates approved for deferred employment. Approved graduates should follow subsequent OA instructions on signing the deferred-employment agreement and complete the required procedures at the Student Affairs Office Graduate Employment Guidance Center.

The procedures will be handled in Room 201 of the Student Affairs Office Building. To protect graduate privacy, this article does not publish the student list from the attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original notice in the OA system for the deferred-employment graduate list and agreement materials.
