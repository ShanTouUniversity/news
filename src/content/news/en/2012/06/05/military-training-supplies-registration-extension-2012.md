It is reported that STU’s Student Affairs Office has extended military-training supplies registration to 18:00 on June 7 because some students in the 2011 cohort missed the original registration period. No late supplementary registrations will be accepted after the deadline.

Registration is available at <http://gdc1.stu.edu.cn/junxun/>. The university plans to collect payment for the supplies on June 11. Students who registered to purchase supplies must prepay the required amount through their campus-card accounts by June 10.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
