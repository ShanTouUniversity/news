It is reported that the STU Youth newspaper held its 20th election meeting on the afternoon of May 30. The meeting reviewed the work report of the 19th editorial board and elected the 20th editorial board by ballot.

The new board’s assignments are:

| Position | Elected representative |
| --- | --- |
| Editor-in-chief | Wang ZY |
| Deputy editors-in-chief | Liu J and Guo JJ |
| Editorial Department director | Hu XF |
| News Department director | Dong GP |
| Photography Department director | Jiang MY |
| New Media Department director | Guan SJ |
| Visual Design Department director | Li M |
| Public Relations and Planning Department director | Chen SL |
| Administrative Affairs Department director | Huang XM |

The students’ names are abbreviated under the privacy rules. Their year and major information from the original notice is not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Youth newspaper)
