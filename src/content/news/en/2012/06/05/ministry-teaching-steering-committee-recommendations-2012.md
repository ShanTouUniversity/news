It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office has forwarded Ministry of Education and Guangdong notices on recommending members for the new Higher Education Teaching Steering Committees and has begun the university’s internal recommendation process. Schools and colleges should identify candidates according to the stated requirements and encourage more university experts to participate in discipline-based teaching committees, supporting research, consultation, guidance, evaluation, and service for undergraduate education.

Schools and colleges must submit printed copies of the 2012 Ministry of Education Higher Education Teaching Steering Committee Recommendation Form and summary form to the General Section of the Academic Affairs Office by the end of work on June 12. Electronic copies should be sent to `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn`.

Contact: Shen Shaolong, Academic Affairs Office; telephone: `82902406`.
