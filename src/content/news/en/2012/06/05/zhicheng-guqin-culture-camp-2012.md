It is reported that STU’s Zhicheng College will host its sixth Guqin Culture Learning Camp during the summer break. Deng Weiqiang, a senior guqin instructor with the Chinese Nationalities Orchestra Society and director of Shantou Haiyunlin Guqin Art Museum, will teach the camp. The camp will run from June 30 to July 6 at Haiyunlin Guqin Art Museum, with sessions from 09:00 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 17:00 each day.

The curriculum will cover reading guqin abbreviated notation, plucking techniques, beginner pieces, guqin and ritual-music culture, and the instrument’s role in personal cultivation and wellness. The camp is open to STU faculty members and students and will admit 15 participants. Previously unsuccessful applicants will receive priority, with remaining places determined by drawing lots.

Deng Weiqiang and Haiyunlin Guqin Art Museum will provide most of the funding. The college and admitted participants will cover the remaining approximately 15 percent. Each admitted participant must pay a RMB 100 deposit and a RMB 300 learning fee. Applications close at 22:00 on June 9; submission instructions should follow the Zhicheng College notice.
