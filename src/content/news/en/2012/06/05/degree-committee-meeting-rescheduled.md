It is reported that the first meeting of Shantou University's eighth Degree Evaluation Committee, originally scheduled for June 22, has been rescheduled because the date falls on the national Dragon Boat Festival holiday. The meeting will now be held from 2:30 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. on June 15 in Room 205 of the Science Building.

## Key Meeting Details

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | First meeting of STU's Eighth Degree Evaluation Committee |
| Original date | June 22, 2012 (Dragon Boat Festival) |
| New time | June 15, 2012, 14:30–17:30 |
| Venue | Room 205, Science Building |
| Organizer | Degree Office |

## Committee Composition

According to the roster released by the Degree Office, the committee is chaired by Xu Xiaohu, with Gu Peihua, Wulanhasi and Huang Dongyang serving as vice chairs. Members include Wang Shouzhi, Wang Furen, Kong Kangmei, Deng Jianguang, Shi Ganggang, Li Dan, Li Yuanyou, Li Changping, Yang Zengming, Lu Xiaohua, Chen Yan, Lin Danming, Fan Dongsheng, Lou Zengjian, Xu Zongling, Wen Xiaobo, Han Ran, Xiong Guangjing and Tan Xuerui, as well as scholars including Terry Bodenhorn and Michael Palmer.

## Attendance Requirements

As a quorum is required for the meeting to proceed, the Degree Office has asked members to avoid arranging business trips on that day. Those unable to attend due to exceptional circumstances are requested to notify the office before June 12.
