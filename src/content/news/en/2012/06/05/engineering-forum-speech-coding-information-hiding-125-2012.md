It is reported that the 125th STU Engineering Research Forum will be held from 09:30 to 10:30 on June 8 in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room. Li Songbin, a postdoctoral researcher in information and communication engineering at Tsinghua University, will give the presentation.

Titled “Detection of QIM Information Hiding in Low-Rate Speech Coding Based on AbS-LPC,” the presentation will discuss low-rate speech coding, QIM information hiding, and detection methods. Engineering School faculty members and interested students are welcome to attend.
