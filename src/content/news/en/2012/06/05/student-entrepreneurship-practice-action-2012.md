It is reported that STU has launched its 2012 summer student entrepreneurship practice action and is accepting applications for the first group of practice positions from all students, including graduate students. The action combines theory with practical experience, supports youth employment and entrepreneurship, and advances the development of youth employment and entrepreneurship practice bases.

The first three practice bases are China Mobile Communications Group Guangdong Co., Ltd. Shantou Branch, Zhongshan Hanke Fine Chemicals Co., Ltd., and Shantou Fantawild Blue Water Star Tourist Resort. Applicants must complete an electronic application form and attach an electronic résumé with a photograph, then submit the materials according to the original notice by 24:00 on June 8.

To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce the personal contact details in the original notice. Position details, application forms, and submission instructions should be confirmed in the original OA notice and attachments.
