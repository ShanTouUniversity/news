It is reported that, in accordance with an upstream government notice, STU will observe a three-day public holiday from June 22 through June 24 for the 2012 Dragon Boat Festival. The Academic Affairs Office and Graduate School will arrange teaching matters separately.

Before the holiday, each unit must inspect safety facilities in its offices, close doors and windows, shut off water and electricity, and complete other precautions. The Security Office will maintain campus security and order during the holiday.

The Party and Government Office, Resource Management Office, Student Affairs Office, schools and colleges, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and the D, E, and G teaching buildings must arrange duty staff. Names and telephone numbers of duty staff should be emailed to the Party and Government Office by June 18 at `o_jdk@stu.edu.cn`; telephone: `82902305`.
