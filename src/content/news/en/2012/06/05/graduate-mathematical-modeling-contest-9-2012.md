It is reported that the ninth National Graduate Mathematical Modeling Contest will be held in late September 2012, hosted by Shanghai Jiao Tong University. STU’s Graduate School is accepting applications from master’s students, doctoral students, and admitted graduate students, with priority for students who have participated in mathematical-modeling competitions or researched mathematical models and data processing.

Registration runs from June 5 through June 25. The university will review applications and adjust team membership from June 25 to June 28. Teams will consist of three students, who may apply individually or as a group. The exact competition dates will follow the organizing committee’s announcement.

The Graduate School will provide entry fees and living subsidies during the competition. Teams receiving third prize or higher will receive corresponding awards. Training arrangements before the competition will be announced separately.

For registration and inquiries, contact Associate Professor Wei Caimin of the Mathematics Department at `82902732` or `cmwei@stu.edu.cn`.
