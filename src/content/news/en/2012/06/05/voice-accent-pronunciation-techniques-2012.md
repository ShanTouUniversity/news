It is reported that STU’s English Language Center will host a lecture on English pronunciation from 19:00 to 21:00 on June 7 in the E101 VA Lecture Hall. The lecture, titled “Techniques to Improve Pronunciation,” will be led by Sohani Gandhioke.

The session will focus on methods and practice techniques for improving English pronunciation. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
