It is reported that the Center for Women's Studies at Shantou University's College of Liberal Arts has adjusted the schedule of the summer-term service-learning course SER0212, "Women and Society in Chaoshan: Creative Study and Service". The first theory class has been moved to **9:00–12:00 on June 17** in Room G304.

## Course Content and Upcoming Sessions

The June 17 session will cover three modules: fundamental concepts of gender and women's development, needs identification and data collection methods, and Chaoshan folk customs and the local social service system.

Intensive classes are scheduled for **8:00–11:50 on June 30** and **8:00–11:50 on July 1**, both in Room D502.

The center noted that students unable to attend due to schedule conflicts may request leave in advance by emailing cws@stu.edu.cn and leaving contact details to facilitate group assignments. Enrolled students are encouraged to spread the word and attend on time.
