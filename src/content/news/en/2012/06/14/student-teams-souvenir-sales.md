It is reported that, in response to students' request to provide souvenir options for graduating seniors, the Party and Administration Office of Shantou University issued a notice in June 2012 temporarily authorizing two student teams to run souvenir projects using the university's logo during the current semester.

## The Two Teams and Their Products

As there was not enough time for a formal solicitation and review of proposals, but some individual student teams were willing to produce and sell souvenirs on short notice, the university collected feedback after public disclosure and confirmed one team led by Chen MJ and another newly added team led by Wu WR. The products offered by the two teams are:

| Team | Souvenirs |
| --- | --- |
| Team One | "Special Because of You" T-shirts; STU scenic postcards |
| Team Two | "Aspiration, Insight, Perseverance, Achievement" commemorative shirts; "I love STU" commemorative shirts |

## Scope of Authorization and Responsibilities

According to the notice, given the warm welcome expressed by many faculty members and students, the school decided to grant the two teams temporary authorization to use the Shantou University logo for the project this semester. The teams are required to ensure product quality, uphold the image of the university's logo, and offer prices as favorable as possible to staff and students.

The notice also clarified several key points:

- The souvenir service is **not provided by the university**. The attachments were published on behalf of the student teams at their request, and the related responsibilities rest with the teams themselves;
- Buyers and the student teams shall assess and bear the risks of ordering and pick-up on their own; civil relationships among buyers, teams, and manufacturers are to be resolved independently;
- All rights to the university logo belong to the university, and this authorization is valid only for the current semester. No one may use the logo without approval, and the student teams may not alter it without authorization.

## What Comes Next

The university will announce an application procedure for the use of its logo next semester, publicly launching the souvenir design solicitation and licensed operation process, and students will be welcome to take part at that time.
