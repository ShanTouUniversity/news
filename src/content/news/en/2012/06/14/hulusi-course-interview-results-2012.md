It is reported that STU Art Education Center has completed interviews for its fall 2012–2013 hulusi performance course. Twenty students passed the interview. The course will be taught by Lu Xiaoling.

Accepted students must meet at Lecture Hall 3 at 12:30 on June 15 to discuss arrangements for the autumn semester. The meeting is expected to last about 15 minutes. Because all accepted applicants are students, their names and student numbers are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Arts and Design and Art Education Center)
