It is reported that the 211 Project Office issued a notice on June 14, 2012, to hold a mobilization meeting for the acceptance of the 211 Project Phase III key discipline construction projects.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 16:00, June 18, 2012 |
| Venue | Room 205, Science and Technology Center |
| Theme | Convey the competent authorities' spirit on the acceptance of Phase III key discipline projects, with university leaders mobilizing and arranging upcoming acceptance work |
| Participants | Leaders and secretaries of all Phase III key discipline projects; heads of the Finance Office, Supervision and Audit Office, Infrastructure Office and Equipment Office; responsible person of the Medical College 211 Office |
| Issued by | 211 Project Office |

Relevant personnel are asked to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (211 Project Office)
