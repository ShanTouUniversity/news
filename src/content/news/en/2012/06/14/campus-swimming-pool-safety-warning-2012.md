It is reported that since the STU swimming pool opened to the university community on June 9, closed-circuit monitoring has recorded people climbing the railings and entering the pool during late-night and other non-opening hours. Incidents have included drinking and breaking bottles, disrupting pool management and creating serious safety risks.

The university reminded faculty members and students that the swimming pool is a venue for teaching and extracurricular activities. Everyone must follow pool-management regulations and safety measures, obey instructions from on-site teachers and lifeguards, and never enter without authorization during non-opening hours. Similar incidents will be handled seriously under the relevant rules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office, Physical Education Department, and Resource Management Office)
