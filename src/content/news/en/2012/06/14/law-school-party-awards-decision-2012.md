It is reported that, ahead of the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, the STU Law School Party general branch awarded the title of Outstanding Party Affairs Worker to six people and the title of Outstanding Communist Party Member to 26 people following democratic review and recommendations by the Party branches.

The Law School Party general branch called on Party members to learn from the recognized recipients, strengthen their ideals and convictions, and support the development of learning-oriented and service-oriented Party organizations. It also encouraged the recipients to remain modest and continue serving as role models. Because the list includes people with different identities, including students and faculty or staff, individual names are not published here.
