It is reported that the Law School issued a notice on June 14, 2012, to select outstanding students to represent the school in the 11th Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition (China) and the 10th CIETAC Cup International Commercial Arbitration Moot.

The competitions will be held in November this year (CIETAC Cup) and February next year (Jessup).

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Teams | Jessup: 5–8 members (5 on the official roster) plus 4 research assistants (mainly for second-year undergraduates next semester); CIETAC Cup: 6–8 members plus 2–6 researchers |
| Eligibility | All undergraduates and postgraduates of the Law School |
| Requirements | Solid legal foundation, strong interest and willingness to devote extracurricular time, English proficiency and legal reasoning and research skills |
| Selection method | Written essay (≤500 words in English, 50%) plus oral interview in English (50%, scheduled for mid-to-late September) |
| Application | Application form by June 18 to designated email; English essay by September 15 |
| Official websites | Jessup: http://www.ilsa.org/jessuphome; CIETAC Cup: http://www.cisg.law.pace.edu/vis.html |
| Issued by | Law School |

Interested students should submit materials as required. The form and topics are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The application form and topics are available on the OA system.
