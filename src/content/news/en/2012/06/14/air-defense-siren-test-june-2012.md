It is reported that, under a Shantou municipal government announcement, Shantou will conduct a 15-minute air-raid siren test at 11:00 on June 21, the anniversary of Shantou’s fall. All STU units should take note and make any necessary arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
