It is reported that, as part of a curriculum reform, the Department of Computer Science and the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University jointly announced that the course "Computer Application Skills" (COM1011) will be discontinued from the 2012-2013 fall semester and replaced by a newly established course, "Fundamentals of Computer Applications" (COM1014).

## Course Substitution Plan

| Item | Old Course | New Course |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course Name | Computer Application Skills | Fundamentals of Computer Applications |
| Course Code | COM1011 | COM1014 |
| Availability | Discontinued from 2012-2013 fall semester | Offered from 2012-2013 fall semester |

The university reminded students who have not yet passed "Computer Application Skills" that they need to enroll in "Fundamentals of Computer Applications" as a substitute to fulfill the corresponding credit requirement. Affected students were advised to pay attention to this change during fall course registration so as not to delay their academic progress.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
