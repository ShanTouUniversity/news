It is reported that the Student Community X Plan lecture “Critical Thinking in Progress” will be held from 19:30 to 21:00 on June 20 in the STU Academic Lecture Hall. The guest will be Ren Changzhen, executive director of A Bite of China and director of its first episode, “Nature’s Gifts.” Ren has also worked as a director for CCTV’s People and one of the executive directors of Keep the Craft, and served as executive director of the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games Organizing Committee’s sports presentation program.

The final lecture topic has not yet been determined. Options include Stories from the “Tip of the Tongue,” People, Food, and Nature, The Sour, Sweet, Bitter, Spicy, and Salty World Beyond the Lens, and From People to A Bite of China: My Path as a Director. The event is open to the university community. Registration, appointment, and question collection close at 15:00 on June 18. Participants can register through the [activity platform](http://activity.stu.edu.cn) and send questions to the university Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`.
