It is reported that the STU Student Science and Technology Association held its 11th annual summary and awards meeting and election of the 12th Executive Committee on the evening of June 10 in Lecture Classroom E202. The meeting heard a work report from Liang Wei, chair of the 11th association, recognized outstanding cadres and officers, and elected the new Executive Committee through a competitive election.

Eleven members were elected to the 12th Executive Committee. To protect student privacy, their names, year groups, and majors are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and STU Student Science and Technology Association)
