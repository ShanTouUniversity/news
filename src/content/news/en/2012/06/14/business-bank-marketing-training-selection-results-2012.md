It is reported that STU Business School has completed interviews for its marketing-practice internship training supported by China Construction Bank Shantou Branch. Six students have been selected to participate in the training.

The Business School thanked students who applied and reminded those not selected to continue following the school’s activity announcements. Because the selected participants are students, their names are not published here.
