It is reported that the 127th STU Engineering Research Forum will be held from 15:00 to 16:30 on June 15 in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room. Professor Deyi Xue of the Department of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering at the University of Calgary will give the presentation.

Titled “Advances in Design and Manufacturing: An Overview of Recent Research Programs,” the presentation will introduce recent research programs in design and manufacturing. Engineering School faculty members and other interested members of the university community are welcome to attend.
