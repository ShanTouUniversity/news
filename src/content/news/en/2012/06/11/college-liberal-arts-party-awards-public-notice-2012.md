It is reported that STU College of Liberal Arts’s Party general branch has proposed two Outstanding Party Affairs Workers and six Outstanding Communist Party Members for recognition, based on reviews by the student Party branches. The proposal supports the “Strive for Excellence” campaign and the development of learning-oriented Party branches.

Comments may be submitted to the College of Liberal Arts Party general branch in writing, in person, or by telephone by June 15. Feedback must include the commenter’s real name. Telephone: `82902234`. Because the proposed awardees come from student Party branches, their individual names are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Liberal Arts Party General Branch)
