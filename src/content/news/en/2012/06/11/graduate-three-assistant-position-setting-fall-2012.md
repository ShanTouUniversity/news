It is reported that STU Graduate School has begun setting graduate “three-assistant” positions for fall 2012. Units should set teaching-assistant and administrative-assistant positions according to the position quota and their actual needs and prepare position plans.

Units must submit the Graduate Teaching-Assistant and Administrative-Assistant Position Plan Form to the Graduate School by June 18 and send an electronic copy at the same time. Position quotas and forms should follow the original OA attachments.

Contact: Lin Xiaojun; telephone: `82904000`; email: `xjlin@stu.edu.cn`.
