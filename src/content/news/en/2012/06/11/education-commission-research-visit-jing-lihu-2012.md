It is reported that Jing Lihu, deputy secretary of the Guangdong Education Party Committee, led a delegation to STU for a research visit on the afternoon of June 11. A research forum was held from 15:30 to 17:00 in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center.

The visit examined the university’s overall reform and development and recent work in Party building, cadre development, ideological and political education, student affairs, and faculty development. Secretaries and deputy secretaries of Party general branches and directly affiliated branches, together with principal officials from the Party and Government Office, Organization and United Front Department, Publicity Department, Social Sciences Department, Student Affairs Office, Human Resources Office, university labor union, and Youth League Committee, attended the forum.
