It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 78 [2012] on June 11, 2012, announcing the winners of the 2011–2012 Li Ka Shing Foundation Teaching Excellence Awards.

Following recommendations by schools and a strict selection by the evaluation committee, Prof. Zhu Wang of the College of Liberal Arts and Prof. Yang Shouzhi of the School of Science were honored.
