It is reported that STU’s Changjiang School of Arts and Design has changed the venue for its continuing-education elective Chaoshan Houses and Culture because of a venue conflict with the College English Test. The class schedule remains unchanged.

The course, taught by Lin Kailong, will run on June 15 and 16 from 09:00 to 12:00 and from 14:30 to 17:30 each day. The new venue is the stepped classroom of the Changjiang School of Arts and Design. Faculty members enrolled in the course should attend at the revised venue and times.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Arts and Design)
