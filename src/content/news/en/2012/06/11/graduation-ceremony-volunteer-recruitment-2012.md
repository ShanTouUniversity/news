It is reported that STU Student Affairs Office is recruiting 55 student volunteers from across the university to support the 2012 graduation ceremony. Volunteers will assist with reception and related services and should demonstrate strong communication and organizational skills, provide warm and careful service, follow instructions, and observe venue rules.

Applicants must be free of other courses and activities on the afternoon of June 28 and the morning of June 29; detailed service times will be announced separately. Applications are open from June 11 through 20:00 on June 13. Student Affairs Office will select volunteers according to applications and activity needs, and service hours will be recorded under the university’s youth-volunteer registration measures.

Application forms and submission instructions should follow the original OA attachment. To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce the personal email address or application example in the notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
