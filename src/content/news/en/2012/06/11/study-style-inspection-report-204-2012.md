It is reported that STU’s Three-Style Development Office organized inspections of classroom attendance in Building E during Week 15 of the 2011–2012 spring semester. An inspection at 08:00 on June 5 found eight students arriving no more than five minutes late and four arriving more than five minutes late. A second inspection at 08:00 on June 7 found two students arriving no more than five minutes late, for 14 instances in total.

The office has sent the relevant student list to schools and residential colleges and asked them to provide criticism and education while continuing efforts to improve study culture. To protect student privacy, individual names are not published in this article.
