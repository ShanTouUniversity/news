It is reported that STU Business School will select six students for practical customer-manager training supported by China Construction Bank Shantou Branch. The program will introduce commercial-bank products and operating principles, expose students to banking-management requirements, and develop skills in identifying and serving customer groups, marketing, and communication.

The training will pair each participant with a bank business specialist for one-to-one guidance. One cycle will begin in early July 2012 and run for three months. The school and bank will arrange flexible training times to avoid disrupting regular study. Participants who perform well may enter an internship after the training, and allowances will be provided according to individual performance during the program.

Applicants must be Business School undergraduates from the 2010 cohort or first-year master’s students. They should be confident, responsible, honest, persistent, hardworking, interested in banking and marketing, and strong in communication, presentation, etiquette, and personal conduct. Applicants must send a résumé to `chenyl@stu.edu.cn` by 17:00 on June 12 and attend an interview at 15:00 on June 13 in the Business School seminar room on the second floor of the West Liberal Arts Building.

Telephone: `82903496`.
