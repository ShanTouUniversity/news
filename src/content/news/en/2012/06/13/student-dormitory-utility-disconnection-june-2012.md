It is reported that student dormitories with unpaid utility bills for May 2012 or earlier as of June 13 will have power disconnected from June 14 until all outstanding fees are paid.

On June 14 and 15, the Network and Information Center will process campus-card deductions twice each day. The first deduction will be at 10:30, with power expected to be restored between 14:30 and 16:30 for dormitories that pay successfully. The second deduction will be at 16:30, with restoration expected between 17:30 and 20:00. Dormitories paying after 16:30 will receive restoration service between 14:30 and 16:30 the following day. From June 15 onward, deductions will be processed once daily at 16:30.

Students with insufficient campus-card balances should recharge promptly. Those with insufficient bank-account balances should deposit sufficient funds during banking hours and complete the campus-card transfer. Students who have lost their campus cards may set up proxy payment through the utility-management system or make a cash payment at the Bank of China STU Branch and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for posting; the Finance Office does not accept cash directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office)
