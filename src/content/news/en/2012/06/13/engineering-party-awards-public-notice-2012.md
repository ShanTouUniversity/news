It is reported that the STU Engineering School Party general branch has proposed 12 Outstanding Party Affairs Workers and 11 Outstanding Communist Party Members for recognition following reviews by its Party branches. The public notice recognizes exemplary work and sets out to establish positive models.

Comments may be submitted to the Engineering School Party general branch in writing, in person, or by telephone by June 15. Feedback must include the commenter’s real name. Because the source does not provide sufficient identity context for the listed individuals, their names are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Engineering School Party General Branch)
