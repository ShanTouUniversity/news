It is reported that STU has begun processing home-origin student credit loans for the 2012–2013 academic year. The program is open to economically disadvantaged full-time students in regular undergraduate, associate-degree, higher-vocational, and second-bachelor programs. Processing dates and document requirements vary by locality, so students seeking new loans or renewals should consult the county-level education authority where their household registration is held.

New applicants generally need a stamped application form, identity documents for the student and co-borrower, an admission notice or student card, and copies of the relevant documents; household-registration books only need to be shown. Renewal applicants generally need a signed application form and a copy of the student card. If one borrower cannot attend, authorization procedures should follow local requirements. Students can review the process through the [China Development Bank home-origin student loan online service system](https://sls.cdb.com.cn/).

Home-origin student credit loans and university national student loans are mutually exclusive. Students who have successfully obtained a university national student loan may not apply for a home-origin loan again. Students who need university confirmation and a stamp may bring their materials to the Student Aid Management Center in Student Affairs Office 102. Information on participating provinces and handling banks is available in the original OA attachment.
