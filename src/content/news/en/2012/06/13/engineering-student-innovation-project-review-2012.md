It is reported that applications for STU Engineering School’s 2012 student extracurricular science and technology innovation projects have closed, with 51 projects applying for approval. The school will conduct departmental reviews to follow project progress and assess innovation and implementation.

| Department | Time | Venue | Reviewers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Civil Engineering | June 15, 10:00 | Civil Engineering meeting room | Li Gengying and Zhou Qi |
| Electronics | June 15, 15:00 | Electronics meeting room | Wang Jun and Lin Yiwen |
| Mechanical and Electrical Engineering | June 15, 14:30 | Mechanical and Electrical Engineering meeting room | Chen Shaoke and Zheng Liming |
| Computer Science | June 15, 15:00 | Wenxi 302 | Xiong Zhi and Yang Xiaoyi |

Project teams should prepare a five-minute PowerPoint presentation introducing their project. The detailed project list is available in the original OA attachment.
