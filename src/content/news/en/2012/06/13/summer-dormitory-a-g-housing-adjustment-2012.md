It is reported that STU has opened summer student-dormitory adjustments in Buildings A–G based on the housing plan and students’ needs. Eligible applicants for Buildings A–F include students extending their period of study, students whose rooms have only one remaining occupant, and others with a demonstrated need to move. Available vacancies and beds vacated by graduating students may be requested, excluding empty rooms. Eligible applicants for Building G include students extending their period of study, students seeking an internal move or sharing a room with only one remaining occupant, 2009-cohort students in Buildings E and F, students currently on the first floor of Building G, and others with a need to move to G.

Applicants should download the relevant form from the Student Affairs website’s common forms section and apply at the residential-college counselor offices: B106 for Buildings A–F and the office beside G238 for Building G. Applications are open through June 26, with the main processing period on July 3 and 4. Because Medical College examinations end later, applications for beds vacated by Medical College students may be submitted and processed on July 16.

Housing adjustments must follow the mixed-residency principle and be completed within the designated period; students may not change rooms or beds without authorization. If multiple applicants request the same room or bed, arrangements will follow a first-come, first-served principle while considering actual circumstances. First-floor Building G residents may request another floor, while residents of other buildings may request floors with a concentration of their cohort. Students should clean their rooms and protect their belongings during the process. Questions may be sent to the residential-college counselor office at `o_fdy@stu.edu.cn`.
