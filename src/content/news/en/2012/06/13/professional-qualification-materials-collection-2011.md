It is reported that STU Human Resources Office will distribute the original application materials for the 2011 professional-qualification review and qualification certificates for approved applicants on June 14 in Xidong 403.

The College of Liberal Arts, College of Science, Engineering School, Law School, Business School, Changjiang School of Arts and Design, English Language Center, Social Sciences Department, Physical Education Department, Institute of Higher Education, and Research Office should send representatives to collect the materials on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
