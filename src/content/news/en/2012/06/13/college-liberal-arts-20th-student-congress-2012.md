It is reported that the 20th STU College of Liberal Arts Student Congress will be held at 19:00 on June 15 in the Academic Lecture Hall. The congress will review the work of the 19th Student Union Executive Committee, elect the 20th Executive Committee, and discuss the new phase of the union’s tasks and direction.

About 80 representatives will attend, representing approximately 15 percent of the college’s student population. Representatives must be enrolled undergraduates with strong academic and personal records, representative standing, and the ability to deliberate. Each class will elect representatives through a meeting of all students under the principle of democratic centralism. Representatives and candidates must arrive by 18:45 to sign in and take their seats.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Preparatory Committee of the 20th College of Liberal Arts Student Congress)
