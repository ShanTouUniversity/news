It is reported that the STU academic calendar for 2012–2013 is ready. Main-campus units should count their faculty, staff, and students, excluding those graduating this year, and collect copies from the Academic Affairs Office Academic Services Section in Xixing 402 by June 15.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
