To develop student leadership, STU will continue its student leadership exchange program with the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. The program is tentatively scheduled for August 27 to September 14. Each university will send 10 student representatives to the other campus to help prepare and conduct new-student welcome activities and experience student life and work there.

Each university will cover its outgoing representatives’ transportation and provide free student-dormitory accommodation for visiting representatives. Students will cover their own living expenses during the exchange. Representatives will be selected through applications, unit recommendations, material review, exchange-project design, and interviews.

Applicants must have worked or previously worked in a relevant student organization for at least one year, have a strong record of responsibility and activity participation, maintain good academic performance, and possess strong English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. The quotas are two from the Student Union, two from the Youth Volunteers Association, two from the Student Organization Federation and its affiliated organizations, and four from schools or residential colleges, with no more than two from any one school or college. Each sending unit must also assign at least two students to host visiting representatives and support exchange activities.

Eligible students should complete the application form, obtain a recommendation from their school, residential college, or student organization, and submit it to the University Youth League Committee Office by 16:00 on June 27. Electronic copies should be sent to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. Interviews are tentatively scheduled for the evening of July 2 and will include a self-introduction, exchange-plan presentation, English test, and panel questions, lasting about 10 minutes per person. Interview arrangements and the interview list are scheduled to be announced on the afternoon of June 28. The review panel will select 10 student representatives.
