The third STU Consumer-Culture Research Report Award received applications from 102 teams. After a series of lectures, workshops, and interviews, 48 teams submitted entries. Judges then selected 13 winning teams through a fair and impartial review.

Teams receiving the first, second, or third prize, Best Topic Award, Outstanding Work Award, or Excellence Award may represent STU in Hong Kong from July 16 to 22 at the 13th Consumer-Culture Research Report Award ceremony and related training, jointly organized by the Consumer Council of Hong Kong and the Education Bureau of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. The relevant winning teams must attend a Hong Kong research-trip interview at 21:10 on June 20 in Room J354.

Student names and the detailed award list are not reproduced here; the original OA attachment should be consulted for the complete results.
