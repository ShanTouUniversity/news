STU will hold a coordination meeting at 16:30 on June 19 in Xingdong Building Room 311 to coordinate arrangements for 2012 graduate departure and the 2012 new-student welcome period.

Participants will include the Party general branches of all schools, Zhicheng College, and the Party and Administrative Office, Student Affairs Office, University Youth League Committee, Organization and United Front Department, Publicity Department, Academic Affairs Office, Finance Office, Resource Management Office, Library, Network and Information Center, English Language Center, Art Education Center, International Research Center of the School of Humanities, Computer Teaching Center, Physical Education Department, Alumni Office, and Medical School Student Affairs Office. Relevant units under the Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office, including admissions, employment guidance, dormitory counseling, the university clinic, student-community property services, utilities, security, and the transport fleet, will also attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office and Student Affairs Office)
