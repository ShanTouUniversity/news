Following Guangdong education-authority requirements for the later-stage management of “Yumiao Project” grants, STU Research Office has begun preparing completion reviews for natural-science projects approved in 2009 under the Guangdong Higher-Education Distinguished Young Innovative Talents Development Program.

Project leaders must complete the *Completion (Acceptance) Report for the Guangdong Higher-Education Distinguished Young Innovative Talents Development Program (Natural Sciences)*. The funding-settlement form must be reviewed and stamped by the Finance Office. Copies of research outputs, including monographs, papers, research reports, software, databases, patents, and appraisal certificates, must be attached to the report and identify the project as supported by the Foundation for Distinguished Young Talents in Higher Education of Guangdong, China, together with its approval number. Unmarked outputs may not be included in the completion materials.

Electronic and printed copies of the completion report and supporting materials, with two printed sets required, must be submitted to the Research Office by June 29. After review, STU will submit the materials to the provincial education authorities. Contacts: Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng, `82902813`.
