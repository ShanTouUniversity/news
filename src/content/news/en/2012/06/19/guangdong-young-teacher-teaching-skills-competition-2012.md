The Guangdong Provincial Federation of Trade Unions and Department of Education will jointly hold the first Guangdong University Young-Teacher Teaching Skills Competition. STU Labor Union and Academic Affairs Office have opened the internal recommendation process. Each teaching unit and the Medical School must recommend one eligible teacher, after which the university will select its representative based on the recommendations.

Units must submit their recommended teachers to the Academic Affairs Office by the end of the morning on June 21 at `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn`. The university is also inviting candidates for faculty and student juries. Faculty jurors should be professors with senior professional titles and extensive teaching or management experience; student jurors should be high-achieving students in their third year or above. The Academic Affairs Office will send eligible-teacher lists to units separately, while the Medical School will compile its own list.

The competition has science, engineering, and humanities and social sciences categories. Each category will include one first prize, seven second prizes, and 10 third prizes, with the remaining contestants receiving excellence awards. The competition will also include one Most Popular with Students Award and one Best Lesson Plan Award. Subject to a successful comprehensive assessment, each first-prize winner will be recommended for the Guangdong May 1 Labor Medal and represent Guangdong in the national young-teacher teaching competition. First- and second-prize winners will also receive relevant provincial honors under the applicable rules.
