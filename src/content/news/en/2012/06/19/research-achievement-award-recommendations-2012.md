STU Research Office has opened recommendations for the 2012 Higher-Education Research Achievement Awards in Science and Technology. It is also soliciting eligible projects for direct recommendation to the Ministry of Education’s selection of candidates for the 2013 National Science and Technology Awards. The higher-education awards include Natural Science, Technological Invention, Science and Technology Progress, and Patent awards.

Natural Science Award entries must be based mainly on papers or monographs published or issued through publicly distributed domestic or international academic journals by the end of October 2011 and supported by peer evaluation. Technological Invention Award entries must have been in practical use for more than one year and have an authorized invention patent; project teams generally may include no more than six principal contributors. Science and Technology Progress Award entries should generally have passed appraisal, been in practical use for more than one year, and generated clear economic or social benefits. Promotion-category entries must meet additional duration or recognition requirements. Patent Award entries must concern a single authorized invention or utility-model patent that has generated clear benefits after implementation.

Teachers with projects to recommend should send their names, telephone numbers, and project titles to the Research Office by July 1 to receive an online application account. Award entries must first be registered as scientific and technological achievements, and the registration and online recommendation materials must be completed by August 20. After review by the Research Office, three printed sets and an electronic copy of the recommendation materials must be submitted by August 27.

For guidance on achievement registration, see the [achievement-registration guide](http://www.cutech.edu.cn/cn/kjcg/cgdj/webinfo/2008/08/1216958741089790.htm). The online application system is available at [the application portal](http://211.68.23.93/xmsb). Contacts: Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong, `82903271`, `dengyong@stu.edu.cn`.
