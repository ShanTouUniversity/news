June 18 was the first day of STU’s 2012 spring-semester final examinations. University teaching supervisors and Academic Affairs Office staff inspected every examination room and session. The office said rooms were generally orderly, most proctors arrived on time, and students followed examination rules; inspectors nevertheless identified one late-proctor incident and five students involved in cheating.

The reported incidents were as follows: four students cheated during the ELC4 examination from 08:00 to 10:00 on June 18; one student cheated during the EA2 examination from 16:00 to 18:00; and a Business School teacher assigned to proctor the “Quantitative Methods in Management” examination in Room E303 arrived at 19:16 for the 19:00–21:00 session, 16 minutes late.

The Academic Affairs Office called on teaching units and proctors to verify examination times and locations and follow STU examination-management rules. It also reminded students to comply with examination regulations and sit exams honestly. The relevant materials will be sent to the schools for investigation. Individual names are not reproduced here.
