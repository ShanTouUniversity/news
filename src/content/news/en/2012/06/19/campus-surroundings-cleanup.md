It is reported that, in accordance with higher-level directives on improving the environment around schools, the Jinping District government and relevant municipal departments will launch a joint campaign to thoroughly clear temporary stalls and illegal structures in and around Shantou University.

## Bus Route Adjustment

As part of the campaign, the terminus of Bus Route 39, currently located at the university's main gate, will be relocated to the gate of the former Jingwei Center. After the adjustment, students and staff taking Route 39 will need to wait at the bus stops along both sides of Daxue Road.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Scope | Shantou University and its surroundings |
| Targets | Temporary stalls, illegal structures, etc. |
| Route 39 terminus | Relocated from the main gate to the former Jingwei Center gate |
| Boarding points | Bus stops along both sides of Daxue Road |

The university has called on all students and staff to support the campaign by the government authorities and to work together to maintain a clean and safe campus environment.
