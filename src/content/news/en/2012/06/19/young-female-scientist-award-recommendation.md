It is reported that, in accordance with the Guangdong Association for Science and Technology's notice on recommending candidates for the 9th China Young Women Scientists Award (Guangdong), the Research Office of Shantou University has recently arranged the university's nomination process, and all relevant units may apply freely.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Scientific spirit | Patriotic, upholding the spirit of "dedication, innovation, truth-seeking and collaboration" as well as the modern ethos of "self-respect, self-confidence, self-reliance and self-improvement," with sound academic conduct |
| Degree or title | Holding a doctoral degree or a professional title of associate researcher or above |
| Academic achievement | Major discoveries, major achievements or important innovative academic ideas in natural science research |
| Age and gender | Female citizen of the People's Republic of China aged 45 or below (born on or after January 1, 1967) |

## Quotas and Selection

According to the notice, no more than 10 recipients will be honored for the 9th China Young Women Scientists Award, at least one of whom works in western China. The university's nomination quota is one candidate; if more than one person applies, the university will select the strongest candidate.

## Submission Requirements

Applying units must submit three paper copies of the recommendation form for the award (without an additional cover), together with three bound sets of supporting documents including copies of award certificates; reports in non-academic newspapers and magazines are not accepted as supporting materials. All materials must be submitted to the Research Office before July 2, 2012.
