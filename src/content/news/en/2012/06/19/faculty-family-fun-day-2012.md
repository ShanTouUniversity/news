To strengthen community ties and promote family harmony, the STU Labor Union will hold a “Happy Family Fun Day” with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation. The event will take place on campus from 16:30 to 20:00 on July 8 and will include game challenges, a buffet, and family activities. Families that complete the challenges may receive a prize.

Faculty and staff at the main campus may bring family members and children. Registration runs from June 19 to 26 through the [online booking system](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/). Each employee may register up to four places, and total attendance is limited to 180 people. The event will be canceled if fewer than 100 people register. The fee is 55 yuan per person when paid through the campus card system or 65 yuan per person in cash. Payment is due June 27–28, and cash payers must exchange a bank receipt for an admission ticket at the Labor Union office from 09:00 to 12:00 on July 2.

Registrations may be canceled without charge before the booking system closes; cancellations and refunds will not be accepted afterward. Each child must be accompanied by at least one parent. Participants should wear flat athletic shoes and comfortable clothing, supervise children, avoid food waste, and protect the campus environment. The event is limited to main-campus faculty, staff, and their families.
