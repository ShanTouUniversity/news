STU’s 2012 undergraduate admissions publication has been printed. Schools, residential colleges, departments, faculty members, and students may collect copies from the Admissions Office, Xingxi Building Room 406, and distribute them to organizations or individuals outside the university to help prospective students and parents learn about STU.

For mailing to other locations, send the recipient’s name, address, and postal code to the Admissions Office, which will arrange shipment. A PDF version is available from the [STU Admissions Information Website](http://zs.stu.edu.cn) and may also be shared with people outside the university through instant-messaging tools.

Admissions Office: `82902403`, `o_zsb@stu.edu.cn`.
