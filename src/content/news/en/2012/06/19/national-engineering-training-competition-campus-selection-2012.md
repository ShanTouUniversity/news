The Third National Undergraduate Engineering-Training Comprehensive Ability Competition will be held at Dalian University of Technology in June 2013. Open to undergraduates at universities across China, the competition has university, provincial or regional, and national levels. Provincial or regional competitions, which serve as preliminaries for the national final, are scheduled to be completed by March 20, 2013.

STU Engineering School has opened registration for its campus selection. The unified challenge is the “Carbon-Free Vehicle Obstacle Course.” Applicant teams must submit a preliminary design proposal, and an expert panel will select the teams to advance to the provincial competition based on applications and proposals. Detailed eligibility requirements are provided in the original OA attachments.

Teams wishing to apply must send their materials to `10zxu@stu.edu.cn` by June 28, using “XXX Team Engineering Competition Application Form” as the email subject. The original attachments contain the detailed challenge description, application form, and competition requirements.
