During the Dragon Boat Festival, the STU campus-card service windows at the Expert Apartments and beneath the No. 2 Dining Hall will close on June 22 and resume service on June 25.

During the closure, faculty members and students may use the integrated deposit-transfer and inquiry machines for self-service top-ups. Card-loss reports and balance inquiries can be handled through the voice self-service line at `82903414`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
