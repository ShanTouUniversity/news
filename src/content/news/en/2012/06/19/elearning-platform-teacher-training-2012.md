STU Academic Affairs Office and Network and Information Center will hold an introduction and hands-on training session for the Elearning platform at 16:00 on June 21 in Computer Lab E102, Teaching Building E, to help faculty become familiar with the system.

Elearning is an online teaching-support platform developed by STU on Moodle. It is scheduled to gradually replace the existing virtual classroom system next semester. Faculty members are welcome to attend, with detailed operating materials to follow the training arrangements.
