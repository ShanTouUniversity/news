STU’s 2012 graduation ceremony will be held in the gymnasium on June 29. The university will reserve seats for parents invited by graduating students.

Graduates wishing to invite parents may register through the [STU Booking Center](http://activity.stu.edu.cn) from 10:00 on June 21. The system will close at 12:00 on June 22. Each graduate may in principle invite no more than two parents, while the actual number of tickets will depend on overall demand. Non-graduating accounts may not register.

Approximately 550 parent seats are expected to be available. After registration closes, the university will determine the ticket recipients and quantities according to the total demand and registration sequence and announce the results through the office-automation system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
