It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 84 [2012] on June 26, 2012, to establish leading and working groups for the acceptance of the 211 Project Phase III.

The acceptance will include an internal review in September 2012 and a review organized by the supervising authority in October. Following deliberation at the 8th university leadership meeting on June 20, groups were formed as follows:

## Groups

| Group | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Leading group head | Xu Xiaohu |
| Deputy heads | Gu Peihua, Wen Xiaobo |
| Members | Song *Zhen, Lin Danming, Li Dan, Wulan Hasi, Luo Nianchao, Shi Ganggang, Gu Jiang, Huang Dongyang |
| Working group head | Wen Xiaobo |
| Deputy heads | Huang Dongyang, Li *Ping (one character garbled in the original) |
| Members | He Wenbiao, Hu Zhong, Huo Xia, Zou Zhibo, Sun Fenqiang, Chen Shaogang, Zhang Kaisheng, Gao Chuangbin, Wu Jianhui, Fang Ye |
