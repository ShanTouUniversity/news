STU has opened its 2012 application and review process for professional qualifications. The process follows relevant measures from the Guangdong education and human-resources authorities and covers the application, recognition, and review of professional qualifications for university teachers and for laboratory, engineering, library-information, and related professional series.

Applications for intermediate and senior qualifications must follow current Guangdong requirements. Continuing-education evidence must cover courses completed in 2010 and 2011. High-level overseas returnees using the green channel must be applying for the first time after returning, submit achievements obtained overseas, and obtain recognition or recommendations from at least three domestic peer experts. Overseas academic credentials must also be certified as required. Faculty applicants will be assessed on professional ethics, teaching ability, teaching quality, and teaching outcomes. False information, academic misconduct, and serious teaching accidents will be handled under the applicable rules.

Applicants should first submit materials to their local unit. Applications for university-teaching professional qualifications, including both review and initial recognition, must be entered through the Guangdong Teacher Professional-Title Online Application System at [rsc.gdhed.edu.cn](http://rsc.gdhed.edu.cn). Local units and relevant university offices will review the materials, teaching work, and research record. The Personnel Office will also display application materials across the university for seven working days before review. Materials approved by the relevant discipline group and the University Teacher Professional-Qualification Review Committee will then be sent to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education for review or approval.

### Key Dates

| Date | Task |
| --- | --- |
| By July 2 | Applicants register with their units and submit the representative-paper application form |
| By July 3 | Units submit the personnel roster and representative-work copies to the Personnel Office; the university provides online-application training |
| July 4 | The Personnel Office sends representative works anonymously to external experts for assessment |
| By August 16 | Applicants submit completed online-application materials to their units |
| By August 18 | Units submit all materials to the Personnel Office |
| August 20–28 | Pre-review publicity and review of research, undergraduate-teaching, and graduate-teaching materials |
| From September | Discipline-group review and preparation of materials for submission to provincial authorities |

The review fee for a senior qualification is 780 yuan per applicant, including a 200-yuan representative-paper assessment fee. An additional 140-yuan defense fee applies when a defense is required. The intermediate review fee is 450 yuan, and the junior review or recognition fee is 280 yuan. Fees may be deducted from the applicant’s salary account under the stated procedure or paid at the Finance Office. Once materials are accepted, fees are non-refundable regardless of the review result. Materials should be submitted to the Personnel Office, Xingdong Building, Room 406.
