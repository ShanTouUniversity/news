Following its award-selection measures, STU Engineering School has publicized nominees for its 2011–2012 teaching and administrative-service awards. The candidates were recommended by departments, centers, and the school administration, then reviewed by the school affairs committee and assessed by the school academic committee.

| Award | Nominees and units |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Teaching Award | Associate Professor Lin Peng, Civil Engineering; Professor Chen Shaoke, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering |
| Best Teaching Service Award | Senior Experimentalist Chen Liqiang, Civil Engineering |
| Best Administrative Service Award | Huang Xuejia, Engineering School Administrative Office |

Comments may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Engineering School Office on the morning of June 27. Submissions must include the commenter’s real name. These are nominees rather than final award recipients; the final results will be announced separately by the school.
