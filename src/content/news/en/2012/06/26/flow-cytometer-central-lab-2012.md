It is reported that the Central Laboratory and the Marine Biology Institute jointly issued a notice on June 26, 2012, entrusting the Central Laboratory to manage a flow cytometer and open it for campus-wide services.

## Equipment

Purchased in November 2007 by the Marine Biology Institute, the BD FACSARIA flow cytometer (BD, USA) was transferred to the Central Laboratory (Analytical and Testing Center) to optimize resource allocation and improve utilization of large instruments, with approval from university leadership and the Equipment Office of the Resource Management Office. Flow cytometry enables rapid quantitative analysis of single cells and sorting of specific cell populations, supporting microbiological analysis, cell sorting, apoptosis and cell cycle analysis, intracellular and surface labeling and physiological studies in molecular and cellular biology.
