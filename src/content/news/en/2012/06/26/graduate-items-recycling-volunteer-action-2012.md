STU’s Youth Volunteers Association Firefly Charity Action Team and Green Ribbon Service-Learning Environmental Volunteer Team are jointly organizing the 2012 graduate-item recycling drive. The activity promotes reuse, an environmentally responsible campus, and an orderly departure for graduates while providing material support for the Firefly Fund.

Volunteers will collect donated used items and books from the AB, CD, EF, G, HI, J, L, Y, B13, and B14 dormitory buildings. Door-to-door collection will take place from June 27 to 29, during 09:30–12:30, 15:30–18:00, and 20:30–22:30 each day. From June 30 to July 2, collection will be arranged at flexible times according to graduates’ requests. The items are expected to be sold at a charity sale at the beginning of the fall semester, with proceeds supporting students facing financial hardship.

The organizers are also recruiting volunteers, with applications closing on June 26. The application form and submission channel should be confirmed through official University Youth League Committee or Youth Volunteers Association channels. Student personal telephone numbers, email addresses, dormitory numbers, and other registration details are not reproduced here.
