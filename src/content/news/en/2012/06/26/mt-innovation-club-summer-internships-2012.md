To strengthen exchange with companies and public institutions and give students practical experience in project management, product design, technology development, operations, and services, partners of STU Engineering School’s MT Innovation Club are recruiting summer interns. Participating organizations include the CPC Shantou Municipal Commission for Discipline Inspection, Guangdong Aerospace Technology Co., Ltd., Shantou Chenggu Technology Co., Ltd., and Shantou Chuanyue Computer Technology Co., Ltd.

Applicants should be familiar with web programming, preferably one of J2EE, PHP, C#, or Flex; have systems-analysis skills and be able to prepare technical documents; and demonstrate teamwork and communication abilities. The recruitment primarily targets Engineering School students in the 2010 and 2011 cohorts. Outstanding MT Innovation Club and project members will receive priority consideration, and applicants must have no failing courses in the 2010–2011 academic year.

Interested students must submit an application form by June 30. Interview arrangements will be announced based on the number of applications. Specific positions, the application form, and the submission channel should be confirmed through official Engineering School channels; student personal email addresses and telephone numbers are not reproduced here.
