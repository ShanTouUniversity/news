To coordinate with the library’s summer opening schedule, STU English Language Center has rescheduled its continuing-education course *Applied Basic English Communication Skills*. The course will be taught by Associate Professor Zhang Bei in the Language Independent Learning Center (CILL) on the first floor of the Library.

| Date | Time |
| --- | --- |
| July 2 | 14:00–18:00 |
| July 4 | 09:00–11:00 and 15:00–17:00 |
| July 6 | 09:00–11:00 and 15:00–17:00 |

Faculty members enrolled in the course should attend according to the revised schedule.
