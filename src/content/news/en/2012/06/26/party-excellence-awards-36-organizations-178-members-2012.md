Ahead of the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, STU’s Party Committee issued a decision honoring achievements from the university’s Excellence Initiative, which has been carried out by Party organizations and members across the university since May 2010. The decision aims to strengthen the role of primary Party organizations and the exemplary role of Party members.

The committee granted the title “Advanced Primary Party Organization in the Excellence Initiative” to 36 units, including the First Party Branch of the Medical School’s administrative office. It also granted the title “Outstanding Communist Party Member in the Excellence Initiative” to 178 members. The committee encouraged the honorees to build on the recognition and called on Party organizations and members across STU to learn from the examples and continue making contributions in their roles.
