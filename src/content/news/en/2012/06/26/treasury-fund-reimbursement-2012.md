It is reported that the Finance Office issued a notice on June 26, 2012, to launch reimbursement for treasury funds.

In line with the requirements of the provincial education and finance departments to accelerate the execution of fiscal project budgets and the *Interim Measures for the Administration of Provincial Fiscal Carryover and Surplus Funds*, surplus funds under the provincial budget are in principle fully reclaimed, and carryovers not fully used in the following year are treated as surpluses. Projects with slow progress that may form large carryovers or surpluses by year-end should promptly apply to reduce the current year’s budget or reallocate funds to new priority projects.

## Funds Covered

| Category | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Indicators arrived this time | Discipline and specialty development and quality engineering funds for 2007–2010; quality and reform funds for undergraduate teaching for 2009; funds for the graduate innovation training program |
| Already launched in April | 211 Project Phase III funds, central support funds for local universities, quality and reform funds for 2010, talent recruitment funds and funds issued in 2011 |

The Finance Office noted that managers of treasury-funded projects should pay close attention to execution progress. Historical indicators will be issued for the last time this year; those not yet issued will be notified separately.
