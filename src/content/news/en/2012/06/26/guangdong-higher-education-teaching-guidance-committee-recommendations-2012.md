STU’s Academic Affairs Office has begun the recommendation process for the new Guangdong higher-education professional teaching guidance committees. After reviewing recommendations from campus units, the university will select candidates and submit them to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education.

Under the forwarded document, the university may generally recommend one person to each professional teaching guidance committee. After selecting a candidate, each unit must complete the relevant materials in the document, submit the paper version to the General Section of the Academic Affairs Office by the end of the morning on Thursday, June 28, and send an electronic version at the same time. Because the number of places is limited, the university will review the candidates recommended by each unit.

The list of committees to be formed and the required recommendation format are set out in the attachments to the Academic Affairs Office’s forwarded document. Contact: Shen Shaolong, `82902406`.
