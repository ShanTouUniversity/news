STU Engineering School has opened campus selection and organizational work for the 2012 Guangdong Student Electronic-Design Competition. The provincial competition uses “Smart City” as its unified theme and runs from June through September. It aims to develop innovation, collaboration, hands-on skills, and engineering practice through electronic-information system design.

Teams must consist of three students. Except for graduate students, formally enrolled undergraduate and junior-college students who will remain enrolled by November 30, 2012, are eligible. Teams must independently select a topic and use their free time to design and build innovative, practical hardware and software electronic systems. Projects may involve mixed-signal circuits, microcontrollers, radio-frequency circuits, programmable devices, embedded systems, and software development, and must include hardware design, programming, system debugging, and a design report. Software-led projects must use an external hardware platform to implement a specific function.

Teams must submit an application form and preliminary design proposal. An expert panel will select the teams to represent STU based on the applications and proposals. Final works must be submitted by the specified deadline, demonstrated and defended before the expert panel, and accompanied by a statement explaining their innovations compared with similar products or applications. The application deadline is July 6; submission details should be confirmed through official Engineering School channels. Student personal email addresses and name requirements in the email subject are not reproduced here.
