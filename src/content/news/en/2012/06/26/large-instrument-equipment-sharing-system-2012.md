Following guidance from the Guangdong finance and science-and-technology authorities, STU plans to establish a sharing system for large instruments. The system will help faculty members and students understand the equipment available in laboratories, improve utilization and performance, and support collaboration across disciplines. The system is available at [zxsys.stu.edu.cn](http://zxsys.stu.edu.cn/).

STU is collecting information on individual large instruments valued at 200,000 yuan or more. Equipment managers should complete the *STU Instrument and Equipment Registration Card* and send it as instructed to `sdwang@stu.edu.cn`. The registration card, sample form, and large-instrument list will be issued by the Equipment and Materials Management Office.

Contact: Wang Shudi, `82903142`. The original notice does not state a submission deadline; the timing should be confirmed through subsequent arrangements from the Equipment and Materials Management Office.
