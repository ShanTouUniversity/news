To coordinate its 2012 national college-entrance-examination admissions work, STU has established an admissions leading group in accordance with guidance from the relevant higher authorities. The group will coordinate the university’s admissions and enrollment work for the 2012 examination cycle.

The group is headed by Li Dan. Sun Fenqiang, Lu Xiaohua, Yu Hong, and Zeng Rui serve as members. Further work arrangements will follow subsequent university admissions announcements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
