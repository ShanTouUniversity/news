The results of the Leadership Adventure Program (LAP) activity-logo contest have been announced. The contest received 36 submissions from across STU. After review by the judging panel, Ruan QL, a student from the 2008 cohort, received the Selected Award. The design will be adopted as the project’s activity logo, and the student will receive a prize of 1,000 yuan.

The organizers will contact the winning student separately about collecting the prize and thanked all students who submitted designs. The adopted work and prize arrangements remain subject to the organizers’ announcement.

The contest was organized by the Li Ka Shing Foundation and co-organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. To protect student privacy, the winning attachment and the student’s full name, student number, and other personal information are not reproduced here.
