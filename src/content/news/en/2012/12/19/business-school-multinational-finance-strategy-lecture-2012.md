The 14th 2012 Business School Academic Lecture at STU will be held from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Monday, December 24, in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of West Liberal Arts Building. The topic is “Financial Management Strategies for Multinational Enterprises.”

The guest speaker will draw on financial-management practice in large enterprises to discuss financial-management methods and the role of financial strategy in achieving corporate goals. The lecture will also examine the demands that globalization and the expansion of Chinese companies’ international business place on finance professionals, as well as market demand for high-level accounting talent.

The speaker’s public biography states that the guest holds a master’s degree in project financial management from the University of York’s Department of Economics, is an international accountant and an ACCA member, and currently serves as China-region chief financial officer of a British engineering-design company. The guest previously held financial positions at BBC Global and Temenos UK and has more than 10 years of financial-management experience with overseas enterprises. The speaker’s name is garbled in the archived page and is not reconstructed here.

The event also forms part of the continuing-education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice II,” whose students are expected to attend. Business School graduate students and interested faculty and students from across the university are also welcome.
