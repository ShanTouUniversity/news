STU Student Entrepreneurship Center has announced the results of its campus souvenir business-project competition. The competition received proposals from 11 student teams, and a five-member faculty judging panel organized by the center reviewed the proposals on December 10.

The “记・念” (“Remembrance”) team won the selected-project award and RMB 1,000. Five teams, “太阳语” (“Sun Language”), “小石头” (“Little Stone”), “Special,” “集智” (“Collective Wisdom”), and “回忆” (“Memories”), received finalist awards of RMB 200 each.

| Award | Team | Prize |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Selected-project award | “记・念” (“Remembrance”) | RMB 1,000 |
| Finalist award | “太阳语” (“Sun Language”) | RMB 200 |
| Finalist award | “小石头” (“Little Stone”) | RMB 200 |
| Finalist award | “Special” | RMB 200 |
| Finalist award | “集智” (“Collective Wisdom”) | RMB 200 |
| Finalist award | “回忆” (“Memories”) | RMB 200 |

Organized by Student Entrepreneurship Center, the competition solicited campus-souvenir business proposals and supported student entrepreneurship practice. Award-winning teams may apply for support under the university’s student entrepreneurship-fund and entrepreneurship-park regulations, subject to review by the responsible offices.
