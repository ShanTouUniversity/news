STU Science School will host an overseas university application and study sharing session from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, December 19, 2012, in E Staircase Lecture Room 202. The session is open to all current students in the Science School.

Students with experience applying to overseas universities or studying abroad will discuss application preparation, document planning, and their academic experiences outside mainland China. The session is intended to provide practical reference for students considering overseas study and help them prepare in advance.
