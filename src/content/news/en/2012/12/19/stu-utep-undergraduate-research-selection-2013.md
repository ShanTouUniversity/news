STU Science School and the University of Texas at El Paso will continue their summer undergraduate research program in 2013. The Science School will select three full-time students from the 2010 and 2011 Chemistry cohorts to take part in the exchange program.

Applicants should have participated in an undergraduate research program or extracurricular science and technology project. As a general requirement, applicants should have passed the National College English Test Band 6; applicants with excellent Band 4 results who have not passed Band 6 must provide a recommendation letter from an English instructor. Selection will consider academic performance, overall English ability, research participation and laboratory skills, teamwork, and the ability and willingness to introduce STU and its educational characteristics. Applicants with strong IELTS or TOEFL results will receive preference.

### Materials and selection

Applicants must complete the application form, provide proof of GPA, academic ranking, and English proficiency, and write an English essay of at least 500 words on their academic goals and how the exchange program would help them achieve those goals. Paper and electronic materials must be submitted to the Chemistry Department Office by 11:00 a.m. on Saturday, December 22, 2012. Electronic documents should be sent to `o_lxyhxx@stu.edu.cn`.

The Chemistry Department will first review the written materials and select candidates for a laboratory assessment and interview. The laboratory assessment date will be announced later. The interview will take the form of a defense: applicants will present the theme of their essay using PowerPoint or a similar format and answer questions from the panel, with approximately 10–20 minutes per person. The final three exchange students will be selected based on the laboratory assessment and interview.
