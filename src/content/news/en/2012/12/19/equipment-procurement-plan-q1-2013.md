It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University has launched the application process for the first-quarter 2013 equipment procurement plan. All university units are required to submit their plans by January 20, 2013; late submissions will be deferred to the second quarter.

The initiative is conducted in accordance with the *Interim Measures for Government Procurement of Shantou University*. Applications must be submitted in both hard copy and electronic form to the Equipment and Materials Management Office (former reception room). The contact person is Ms./Mr. Huang.

## Submission Requirements

Documentation requirements vary depending on the total budget and type of equipment:

| Scenario | Required Documents | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| Total plan amount ≤ RMB 20,000 | *Plan Form* only | — |
| Total plan amount > RMB 20,000 | *Plan Form* + *Plan Proposal* | Must be approved by the supervising university leader, the president, and the university leadership meeting per the *Regulations on Funding Approval Authority* before submission |
| Includes equipment priced ≥ RMB 100,000 per unit | Additional *Feasibility Report for Large Equipment Purchase* | — |
| Includes computers | Additional *Computer Purchase Application Registration Form* | — |
| Includes air conditioners | Specify installation location and condition of existing units in the "Remarks" column | — |

## Filling Instructions

The notice provides detailed guidance for completing the forms:

- The "Item (Equipment) Code" should be filled in by consulting the *Government Centralized Procurement Catalogue*;
- The "Item (Equipment) Name" field should contain only the equipment name, with other goods information entered in the "Remarks" column;
- Equipment models, specifications, and technical parameters should be clearly stated, preferably without specifying a brand;
- Budgets should account for taxes, transportation, warranty, and after-sales service costs;
- All columns must be completed in full.

Relevant forms — including the *Plan Form*, *Plan Proposal*, *Feasibility Report for Large Equipment Purchase*, *Computer Purchase Application Registration Form*, and the *Government Centralized Procurement Catalogue* — are available from the Equipment and Materials Management Office.
