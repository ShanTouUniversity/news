STU School of Science will hold a Science Salon lecture from 10:00 a.m. to noon on Friday, December 21, 2012, in Science Building Room 205. Dr. Liu Guoqing will speak on “From Population Genetics to Ecosystem Functions: Bioinformatics and Systems Biology Approaches.”

Liu will also take part in academic exchanges at the Institute of Marine Biology from December 20 through 22. Faculty and students interested in further discussion may contact Professor Mei Zhiping at `zhipingmei@stu.edu.cn`. The notice also provides a laboratory website related to the presentation.
