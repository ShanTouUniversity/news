STU Resource Management Office has announced that electronic office telephone bills for November 1–30, 2012, have been imported into the university’s telephone management system. Verification staff in each unit should log in, review the bill details, and complete the confirmation process.

If a bill is accurate, the verifier should confirm it in the system. If a bill does not match actual telephone use, the unit should email `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` by December 25 so that the university can provide consolidated feedback to China Telecom’s Shantou branch. After the verification period closes, bills that have neither been challenged nor confirmed will be deemed confirmed by the system.

Once verification is complete, Finance Office will process internal deductions on December 26 using the funding information provided by each unit. Problems or suggestions concerning the telephone management system may also be sent to the same email address.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Billing period | November 1–30, 2012 |
| Verification system | Telephone Management System: `http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn` |
| Objection deadline | December 25 |
| Feedback email | `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn` |
| Planned deduction date | December 26 |
| Issuing unit | STU Resource Management Office |

The notice does not publish bill amounts or unit funding details. Units should complete verification through the internal system and follow the university’s financial procedures for deductions.
