Because banks have notified the university that they will not handle corporate transactions on December 31, 2012, STU Finance Office has adjusted its year-end reimbursement schedule. The office must complete all business dated on or before December 27 by December 28 and will suspend external reimbursement processing from December 28.

Units on the main campus with reimbursement matters should arrange processing in advance according to these deadlines.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Bank arrangement | No corporate transactions on December 31, 2012 |
| Finance Office processing deadline | Complete business dated on or before December 27 by December 28, 2012 |
| External reimbursement suspension | From December 28, 2012 |
| Applies to | All main-campus units |
| Responsible unit | STU Finance Office |

> Source: STU OA emergency notice (Finance Office)
