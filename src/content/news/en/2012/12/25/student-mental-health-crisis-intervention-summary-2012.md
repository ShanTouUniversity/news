STU Student Mental-Health Crisis-Intervention Leading Group Office will hold its 2012 review meeting at 4:00 p.m. on Thursday, December 27, in New Administration Center Room 330. Members of the leading group are asked to attend, together with relevant staff from the colleges, residential colleges, and student residential-area counselor offices.

The leading group includes responsible officials from Student Affairs Office, the university Youth League Committee, General Office of Party and Administrative Affairs, Publicity Department, Resource Management Office, Party general branches of the colleges and residential colleges, student residential-area counselor offices, Mental Health Education and Counseling Center, Security Office, and University Hospital. The meeting will review the university’s student mental-health crisis-intervention work during 2012.

This article reports only the university’s student-support mechanism and meeting arrangements. It does not disclose student mental-health information, individual cases, or reasons for intervention.
