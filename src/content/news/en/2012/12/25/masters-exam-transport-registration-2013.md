To assist main-campus candidates taking the 2013 master’s entrance examination, Shantou University will arrange vehicles on the examination days for candidates who need transportation to the old campus of Shantou No. 1 Middle School, the Shantou test center. The venue is at 64 Zhongshan Road, Shantou, and candidates should arrive by 5:00 p.m. on January 4, 2013, to familiarize themselves with the examination venue.

### Transportation arrangements

| Examination date | Departure | Boarding location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January 5, 2013 | 7:10 a.m. | In front of the Shantou University motto monument |
| January 6, 2013 | 7:10 a.m. | In front of the Shantou University motto monument |

Candidates who need university-arranged transportation should confirm their requests through the Graduate School’s online registration system by 5:30 p.m. on December 30, 2012: `http://www.gs.stu.edu.cn/dzzw/busbook.jsp?oa_id=20`. Candidates are asked to register only when transportation is needed. Anyone who has registered but later decides not to use the vehicle should contact the Graduate School promptly to cancel the request and avoid wasting transportation resources.
