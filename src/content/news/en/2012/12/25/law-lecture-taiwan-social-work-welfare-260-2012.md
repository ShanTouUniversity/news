The 260th Law School lecture at Shantou University will be held on December 30. Professor Wang Xiulan of the Department of Sociology at Beijing Normal University at Zhuhai will speak on “The Current State of Social Work and Social Welfare in Taiwan,” covering the development of social work, social welfare, and nonprofit organizations. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

The lecture will use the localization of welfare in Chinese-speaking societies as its central theme and discuss the history and practice of social-work administration and social-welfare services in Taiwan, together with relevant theories and practices from Western and Chinese-speaking societies.

Wang holds a doctorate in social work from Tunghai University in Taiwan. She is a professor in the Department of Sociology at Beijing Normal University at Zhuhai, a board member of a Taiwan social-work association, and chair of the ethics committee of a Taiwan federation of social-work associations. She has worked in Taiwan’s social-welfare administration and related public-service institutions and has taught or provided training at universities and social-service organizations, bringing extensive public-sector experience in social-work administration and social-welfare services.
