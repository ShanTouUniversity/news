The 146th Engineering Research Academic Forum at Shantou University will be held on December 26. Professor Liang Yingchang, an IEEE Fellow and chief scientist at the Singapore Institute of Communications Research, will speak on “Signal Processing and Optimization for Cognitive Radio Networks,” introducing research developments in signal processing and optimization for cognitive radio and wireless communications.

The presentation will review research in cognitive radio networks and communications, with particular attention to cognitive-radio concepts and developments in femtocell networks, device-to-device (D2D) communications, TD-LTE in TV white spaces, and carrier aggregation in advanced LTE systems.

Liang was a visiting scholar at Stanford University and taught graduate courses at the National University of Singapore. He was elected an IEEE Fellow in 2011 and has received academic and engineering awards including an IEEE Communications Society paper award, a Best Paper Award from the European Wireless and Networks journal, and an Outstanding Engineering Achievement Award from the Singapore Academy of Engineering. He also serves on the editorial boards of relevant IEEE journals and was selected for the “Thousand Talents Program” at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2010.
