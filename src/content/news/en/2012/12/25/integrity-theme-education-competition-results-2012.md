STU Student Affairs Office has announced the results of the “What Do You Think About Integrity?” micro-essay competition and the “Promote the Guangdong Spirit and Practice Integrity” themed comic-design competition. The activities sought to promote the spirit of contemporary Guangdong, strengthen students’ awareness of integrity, and foster a campus culture that values and practices integrity.

### Micro-essay competition

| Award | Awardees, anonymized |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | Huang HY |
| Second prize | Li SM; Yan CM |
| Third prize | Wang JC; Lin JC; Wang T |
| Honorable mentions | Huang YC; Wang WX; Yuan XJ; Ye LK; Li YX |

### Themed comic-design competition

| Award | Work and authors, anonymized |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | “Prepare Guangdong Cuisine and Be an Honest Guangdong Businessperson”: Li SM |
| Second prize | “The Foundation of Integrity”: Wu XJ and Lin CL; “The Contest Between Integrity and Betrayal”: Wu XJ |
| Third prize | “The Virtue of Integrity”: Ke XL; “A Promise Worth a Thousand Jin”: Li YH and He XY; “A Little Story About Integrity”: Lin CL |
| Honorable mentions | “Healthy Living”: Ke XL; “Start with Small Things”: Liang B; “Waiting”: Chen QC; “People’s Livelihood”: Pan JY |

The university congratulated the awardees and thanked other participating students for their support. To protect student privacy, awardees are identified by surname and given-name initials; complete names, cohorts, and majors in the original notice are not reproduced.
