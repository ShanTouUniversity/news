To strengthen laboratory work, standardize experimental teaching management, and advance teaching reform, STU Business School established a laboratory committee. The committee will guide laboratory development and management within the school.

| Position | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Ouyang Feng |
| Committee members | Zeng Qiang, Song Xiaobao, Qin Zhong, Du Mian, Chen Yinglin, Lin Jiali, Zeng Fanqi, and one member whose name is garbled in the original |
| Secretary | Chen Xin |

The committee’s establishment supports the university’s broader work to enhance experimental teaching and is intended to improve the mechanisms for laboratory development, management, and teaching reform.

The original notice is dated December 25, 2012, while the OA page records the attachment on December 28. This article uses the date on the notice itself.
