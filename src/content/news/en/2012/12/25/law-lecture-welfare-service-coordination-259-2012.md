The 259th Law School lecture at Shantou University will be held on December 29. Professor Liu Liwen of the Department of Social Work at Tunghai University will speak on “Building Coordinated and Collaborative Welfare-Service Delivery Systems,” examining how organizational collaboration can respond to uncertainty in service environments and resources and improve welfare-service outcomes. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

The lecture will introduce relevant concepts, theories, and principles for action-strategy planning; examine coordination between government agencies and nonprofit organizations; and use a network-analysis perspective to discuss the complex connections between nonprofits and other organizations. It will also review Taiwan-related experience and the historical development of research methods for studying organizational relationships.

Liu holds a doctorate in social work from the University of Washington and is a professor in the Department of Social Work at Tunghai University. Liu has worked or taught at Taiwan child-welfare and family-support institutions, the University of Washington Mental Health Center and School of Social Work, and other institutions, and has been a visiting scholar at the University of Southern California’s Ethel Percy Andrus Gerontology Center, the University of Franche-Comté’s Faculty of Social Sciences, and the University of Singapore’s Department of Social Work. Research interests include social support and networks, nonprofit organizations, older-adult welfare services, service-delivery systems and resource-network management, disaster-prevention and relief resource systems, and service-program design and evaluation.
