STU Student Affairs Office has announced the queue and collection arrangements for student train tickets for winter break 2013. Since pre-registration opened on December 20, 604 students have registered, of whom 527 registrations were accepted. On the afternoon of December 25, the pre-registration list was randomly ordered in the presence of representatives from the Student Union Rights Department, Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee, and the student body. The resulting order will determine the queue for on-site ticket purchases.

Students whose registrations were accepted should bring valid identification to collect a ticket-purchase queue-number card at the Residential Area Counselor Office, Building B Room 106, from 8:30 a.m. to noon or from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on December 26. The original attachment provides the ordered list; student names, student numbers, colleges, and other personal information are not reproduced.

Because the 2013 Spring Festival train timetable was still being compiled and entered, ticket-sale times and specific train services remained subject to change. The university could not yet confirm the on-site ticket-sale schedule. Students should follow the internal university information network for updates.
