The 117th New Century Humanities Forum at Shantou University will be held on the evening of December 28. He Xiongfei, a publisher, speaker, and researcher of Jewish culture, will give a talk titled “Did You Ask a Question Today?” The session will examine the Jewish educational tradition of valuing questions and its possible implications for learning. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

The forum will use educational ideas and family-education experiences as a starting point to discuss the role of questions in learning and how encouraging questions can develop independent thinking. The background materials refer to Jewish education and Jewish Nobel laureates; the specific views should be understood in the context of the speaker’s presentation.
