The final of the Shantou division of the inaugural Guangdong University Students “Dream Wo Star” Workplace Challenge was held on December 25, 2012, at the STU Science Lecture Hall. A total of 26 students registered for the competition, and eight contestants advanced to the Shantou division final after review.

STU students Zhong XQ, Liang JY, and Kong ZY won first, second, and third prizes, respectively, and all three advanced to the Guangdong provincial final.

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Registered contestants | 26 |
| Contestants reaching the Shantou division final | 8 |
| First prize | Zhong XQ |
| Second prize | Liang JY |
| Third prize | Kong ZY |
| Advancement | The three prize winners advanced to the Guangdong provincial final |

The results were issued jointly by the STU Youth League Committee, STU Student Societies Federation, and STU Career Planning and Development Association. Contestant scores and complete judging information are available in the original OA attachment.
