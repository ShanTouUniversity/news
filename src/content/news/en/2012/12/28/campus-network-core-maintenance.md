It is reported that Shantou University's Network and Information Center has announced that, as part of the campus network upgrade plan, maintenance will be carried out on the campus network core equipment in the early morning of December 30. Brief interruptions to network services may occur during this period.

## Maintenance Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Content | Maintenance of campus network core equipment |
| Time | December 30, 00:00 – 06:00 |
| Affected services | Internet access for all users and external access to on-campus systems |
| Restoration | Services will resume automatically after maintenance; no further notice will be issued |

According to the Network and Information Center, the maintenance is part of the overall campus network upgrade plan. During the maintenance window, internet access for all users on campus as well as off-campus access to university systems may be affected.

The center reminded faculty and students to spread the word and make arrangements in advance, expressing apologies for any inconvenience caused.

For inquiries, users may contact the Network and Information Center at 0754-86503401 or by email at stunic@stu.edu.cn.
