As the semester approached its end and most student-organization and society activities had concluded, the STU Student Societies Federation office suspended duty service from December 24, 2012. Approval of materials applications was suspended at the same time.

During Weeks 15 and 16, student organizations and societies with urgent materials needs were advised to follow the Federation’s relevant channels for application arrangements. The original notice identified an individual student as the emergency contact; the student’s name and contact details are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Societies Federation)
