Starting in 2013, Shantou University established a special fund for undergraduate laboratory teaching enhancement. Managed through a project-based process, the fund supports improvements to laboratory conditions, the quality of experimental teaching, and laboratory development and reform. The Academic Affairs Office has opened applications for the 2013 projects.

### Project areas

Units may select an area according to their development needs:

1. Experimental teaching quality assurance, enhancement, and equipment purchases;
2. Laboratory information-platform development;
3. Experimental teaching teams;
4. Laboratory environment construction, renovation, and facilities;
5. Reform of laboratory development models, management systems, and operating mechanisms;
6. Self-proposed projects.

Projects should generally be submitted by departments. Where a department-level submission is unsuitable, the relevant school may submit an application. Planning and applications should be based on research, consultation, and faculty discussion, with clear development goals, phased implementation arrangements, and annual targets.

### Application schedule

| Stage | Requirement | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning | Complete the 2013–2015 development plan for undergraduate teaching laboratories and submit one electronic copy and one paper copy | February 27, 2013 |
| Project application | Select a project area based on the plan and submit the project application, electronic materials, two paper copies, and the project summary form | March 11, 2013 |
| Review | The STU Laboratory Committee will review applications and announce approved projects | To be announced |

Planning may cover reforms to experimental teaching systems and methods, teaching-achievement targets, laboratory personnel development, management mechanisms, information platforms, equipment procurement and maintenance, and laboratory environment and safety. Electronic application materials may be sent to `yzchen@stu.edu.cn`.

Academic Affairs Office contacts are Du Hong and Chen Yangzhou. The office can be reached at `86503132` and `86503202`, or at `hdu@stu.edu.cn` and `yzchen@stu.edu.cn`.
