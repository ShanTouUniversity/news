To strengthen laboratory work, standardize experimental teaching management, and advance related reforms, STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication established a laboratory committee following discussion by the school’s leadership.

| Position | Member |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Hu Lufeng |
| Committee members | Xu Shengjian, Xing Zhong, and Lin Yan |
| Secretary | Ye Huansheng |

The committee will provide guidance on laboratory development and management and support the standardization and reform of experimental teaching.
