It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on December 28 reminding all postgraduates to centrally handle the purchase and recharging of train discount cards and the stamping of registration seals, with a deadline of December 31.

## Background

Since the Graduate School issued a notice in October on the purchase and recharging of train discount cards, some postgraduates have continued to request the service. According to the Ministry of Railways, students admitted from 2011 onwards who need to purchase a discount card must register their preferential train stations and related information on the Ministry of Education's CHSI platform.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service 1 | Purchase and recharging of train discount cards |
| Service 2 | Stamping of registration seal on postgraduate ID |
| Target group | All postgraduates in need |
| Deadline | Before December 31; no further processing after the deadline |
| Method | Centralized by class |
| Locations | Graduate School Student Affairs Office (New Administration Center 401) for discount cards / respective professional schools for registration stamps |
| Prerequisite | Students admitted from 2011 who need a card must first register the discount section on CHSI |

The Graduate School reminded that postgraduates without a registration stamp will not be able to purchase half-price train tickets, and those concerned should submit their documents via their classes to their professional schools for stamping before the deadline.
