The STU Academic Affairs Office issued reminders for the fall 2012 final examinations, asking teaching units to help faculty and students check examination arrangements and follow university requirements for examination administration and the archiving of examination materials.

### Checking schedules and arriving at the venue

All course examination schedules had been published in the credit-system portal. Faculty and students were asked to log in and check their own schedules, while academic administrators could use the system to retrieve and print their unit’s examination schedule. Proctors and students should arrive at the examination venue 10 minutes early in accordance with the STU Proctoring Rules and Examination Room Conduct Rules.

### Examination-paper management

| Stage | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Question setting and grading | Follow the STU Course Assessment Management Measures. Each course assessment should prepare A and B papers with broadly comparable question volume and difficulty. Instructors should generally complete marking and grade assessment within three days after the examination; courses with larger enrollments may take up to one week |
| Printing | Instructors should complete an examination-paper printing request and submit it with the questions and candidate list to the Academic Affairs Office printing center one week before the examination |
| Confidentiality and collection | Question setters must personally deliver papers for printing. Chief examiners should collect papers from the printing center during working hours on the examination day; papers for examinations beginning at 8:00 a.m. should be collected the previous afternoon. A proxy must present written authorization or certification from the chief examiner’s unit |
| Archiving | Instructors should conduct a self-check using the examination-paper inspection form, then submit the examination materials and form to the academic administrator of the offering unit for storage |

The Academic Affairs Office also reminded units to follow the *Revised Regulations on Examination-Question Confidentiality* and other applicable documents so that examination papers and related materials would be handled securely and consistently.
