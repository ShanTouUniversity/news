Following discussion at Shantou University’s 16th university leadership meeting on December 26, 2012, Li Yue was nominated for appointment as deputy director of the Human Resources Office. STU opened pre-appointment publicity in accordance with its *Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres* (STU Party Document No. 47, 2009).

Organizations and individuals with comments may submit them in writing to the Human Resources Office by January 3, 2013. Submissions from organizations must bear an official seal, while individuals must sign their real names.
