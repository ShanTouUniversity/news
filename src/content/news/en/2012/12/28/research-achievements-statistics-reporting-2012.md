In accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education’s annual statistics arrangements, the STU Research Affairs Office began reporting and statistics for the university’s 2012 science and social-science achievements. The reporting period covers publications and academic exchanges from January 1 through December 31, 2012.

Faculty members should log in to the STU Research Management System at `http://sro.stu.edu.cn` to enter the relevant data. Original supporting materials should then be submitted to the faculty member’s unit for preliminary review before being consolidated and sent to the Research Affairs Office.

| Stage | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Faculty complete system entry | January 9, 2013 |
| Units complete review, consolidation, and submission | January 18, 2013 |

The office reminded the university that data in the research management system would be the basis for future reviews of research-related materials for annual assessments, the annual-salary system, and professional-title evaluations.
