Shantou University’s Zhicheng College will hold a guqin culture camp from February 18 to 24, 2013, and will admit 15 students from across the university. The camp will be taught by Deng Weiqiang, an advanced guqin instructor with the Chinese Orchestra Association and director of the Haiyunlin Guqin Art Museum in Shantou. It is intended to introduce students to guqin art and related traditional culture while supporting their artistic and humanistic development.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | February 18–24, 2013; 9:00 a.m.–noon and 2:00–5:00 p.m. daily |
| Venue | Haiyunlin Guqin Art Museum, Shantou |
| Eligibility | All STU students |
| Places | 15: 8 for Zhicheng College residents and 7 for students from other residential areas; drawing if applications exceed the quota |
| Registration deadline | 10:00 p.m., January 3, 2013 |
| Registration email | `o_zhicheng@stu.edu.cn` |

The program will cover guqin character tablature, plucking techniques, beginner pieces, the guqin and ritual and music culture, and the instrument’s role in self-cultivation. Applicants should be interested in guqin culture, able to study with concentration, and able to work well in a team. They must also have no conflicting courses or activities during the camp.

Most of the camp costs will be sponsored by the instructor and the Haiyunlin Guqin Art Museum. The remaining costs will be shared by the college and admitted students. Each admitted student must pay a RMB 100 deposit and RMB 300 in learning fees. Applicants should download and complete the registration form from the “Forms Download” section of the college website and email it to the designated address.

The original notice uses inconsistent numbering for the camp in its title and body; this article therefore does not assign a specific installment number. Personal information in the registration form is not reproduced.
