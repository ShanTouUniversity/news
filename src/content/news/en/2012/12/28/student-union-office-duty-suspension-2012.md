As the semester approached its end and student-organization activity declined, the STU Student Union office suspended office duty from December 30, 2012. It also suspended approval of applications for audio equipment, other materials, poster boards, and related resources.

After December 30, student organizations with urgent poster-board or materials requests were advised to follow the Student Union’s relevant channels for application arrangements. The original notice identified individual students as emergency contacts; their names and campus short numbers are not published here.

The original notice refers to December 30, 2012, as both a Monday in Week 16 and a specific calendar date, creating a weekday discrepancy. This article uses only the clearly stated calendar date.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Union)
