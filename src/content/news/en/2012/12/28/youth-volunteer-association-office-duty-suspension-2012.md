As the end of the semester approached and most student-organization activities had concluded, the STU Youth Volunteer Association office suspended duty service from December 28, 2012. Office service and materials-application approvals were suspended at the same time.

Student organizations were asked to return all outstanding association materials to Room 721 of Building 789 by 10:00 p.m. on December 28. The office would collect and organize materials during the end-of-term period. Organizations with urgent materials needs during Weeks 15 and 16 were advised to follow the association’s relevant channels for application arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteer Association)
