It is reported that the Personnel Office forwarded a notice on December 28, 2012, from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on funding for overseas visiting and training for young and middle-aged teachers in higher education institutions (Yue Jiao Shi Han [2012] No. 141).

Relevant units are asked to prepare applications in accordance with the notice and submit the materials to the Personnel Office (Room 315, New Administration Building) by January 15, 2013.

| Item | Details |
