The STU Youth League Committee organized themed Youth League activities on “Strengthening the Study Environment for Better Growth and Achievement” from September through November 2012. A summary and awards ceremony was held on December 26 in Meeting Room 726 of Building 7. Following review, two organizations received Outstanding Organization Awards and six activities received Outstanding Themed Activity Awards.

### Outstanding Organization Awards

- Zhicheng College Youth League Committee
- Graduate School Youth League Committee

### Outstanding Themed Activity Awards

| Prize | Activity | Organizing unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First prize | *Reading and Speech Competition on Li Ka-shing’s Professional Life* | Business School |
| Second prize | *Listen to Books Singing: Reading Exchange* | Zhicheng College |
| Second prize | *Strengthening the Study Environment for Better Growth and Achievement Series* | School of Engineering |
| Third prize | *A New Study Ethos and Artistic Pursuits* | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Third prize | *Ideal, Youth, and Future English Speech Competition Themed Youth League Activity* | Medical College |
| Third prize | *Discussing the Study Environment on the Road to Growth* | Graduate School |

The Youth League Committee said the activities were organized to support the university’s advanced undergraduate education initiative, raise students’ awareness of the importance of a strong study environment, and promote a positive learning culture. Personal identities and contact details required for prize-collection arrangements are not reproduced here.
