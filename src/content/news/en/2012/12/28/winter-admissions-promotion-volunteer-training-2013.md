The STU Admissions Office reported that 381 students had registered by the afternoon of December 28 for the 2013 winter “Remembering Our Roots, Passing on Dreams” admissions-promotion campaign. To align publicity information and clarify the work arrangements, the office scheduled a training meeting for registered students.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Training time | 10:00 a.m., Saturday, December 29, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Room 101, E Staircase |
| Topics | Campaign arrangements and reminders, experience sharing by previous outstanding publicity volunteers, and Q&A |
| Additional registration deadline | January 4, 2013 |

Attendance at the training session will count toward the campaign assessment. Students who cannot attend for a valid reason should contact the Admissions Office in advance at `o_zsb@stu.edu.cn` so that an alternative training arrangement can be made. Students who registered but cannot find their information in the attached table should also contact the Admissions Office for verification.
