STU’s Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication will screen *The Greatest Show on Earth* from 7:00 to 10:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 12, 2012, in the Library Lecture Hall. Peter Herford will moderate the evening.

Produced, directed, and narrated by Cecil B. DeMille, the 1952 film is set around the Ringling Bros. and Barnum & Bailey Circus. Its story follows the circus’s operation, competition among trapeze artists, and touring performances. The film combines real circus acts with behind-the-scenes scenes of a large touring production and won the Academy Award for Best Picture.

The film stars Betty Hutton, Cornel Wilde, and Charlton Heston, while James Stewart plays a mysterious clown who never removes his makeup. The 1951 Ringling Bros. and Barnum & Bailey Circus troupe also appears, presenting the performances and logistics of a large circus operation.

Beyond the 150 course seats, only 50 seats will be open to the public. Admission will close when those seats are filled. Faculty and students across the university are welcome.
