The facade of the fire stairway at the annex of the STU Academic Exchange Center will undergo renovation from March 1 through April 10, 2013. The second-floor restaurant and Meeting Room 5 will be unavailable during the construction period.

A small amount of noise may affect the surrounding environment while work is underway. Relevant units should adjust their arrangements in advance and follow on-site notices during construction.
