The first STU Physics Alumni Scholarship award ceremony will be held from 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. on December 16, 2012, in the multifunction meeting room of New Administration Center Room 330. The event is jointly organized by the STU School of Science and Alumni Office, and faculty and students are welcome.

The Physics Alumni Fund was established at the initiative of supportive alumni. It provides awards for full-time undergraduate students in the School of Science’s Physics Department who have strong academic and personal records and financial need, while helping eligible students with difficulties in their studies and daily lives. Physics alumni from across the country are expected to return to campus for the ceremony.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2012 first STU Physics Alumni Scholarship award ceremony |
| Time | 9:30–11:30 a.m., December 16, 2012 |
| Venue | Multifunction meeting room, New Administration Center Room 330 |
| Recipients | Full-time Physics undergraduates with strong records and financial need |
| Organizers | STU School of Science and Alumni Office |

The fund is intended to support student development, encourage Physics students to study diligently and pursue progress, provide alumni role models, and strengthen the department and its ties with alumni.
