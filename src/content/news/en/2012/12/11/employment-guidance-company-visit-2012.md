It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on December 11, 2012, to adjust the *Career Guidance* course.

The class originally scheduled for Friday of week 13 (December 14), periods 3–4 in Room D202 (Class 55190, instructors: Lin Xiaoling, Qiu Jiawen) is cancelled and will be replaced by a company visit.

Students enrolled are asked to gather at the university motto monument at 14:00 on December 19 and proceed to Guangdong Shantou Hehe Lexus Motor Sales and Service Co., Ltd. for the visit.
