It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on December 12, 2012, featuring Dr. Liu Yanyan, Senior Engineer at AGILTRON Inc., USA.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | RF/Microwave Photonics |
| Speaker | Dr. Liu Yanyan, Senior Engineer, AGILTRON Inc., USA |
| Time | 15:00, December 12, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |

The lecture will introduce the concept of RF/microwave photonics, a cross-disciplinary field bridging photonics and RF/microwave engineering that addresses challenges difficult to solve with conventional microwave systems. It will focus on microwave/terahertz generators with different structures, optical analog-to-digital converters, optical waveform generators and photonic RF/microwave filters, highlighting advantages in size, weight, power consumption, bandwidth, capacity, efficiency and electromagnetic compatibility.

A speaker profile is available in the OA attachment. Dr. Liu received his bachelor’s degree from Nanjing University in 1984 and his master’s and doctorate from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 1990 and 1993, where he later served as associate professor and professor and led multiple National 863 and other projects. Since 2001 he has worked in the United States on optical communication devices and photonics/microwave engineering, undertaking projects for NASA, NIST and the U.S. Department of Defense.
