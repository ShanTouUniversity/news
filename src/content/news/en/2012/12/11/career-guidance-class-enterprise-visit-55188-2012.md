Under its course syllabus, STU course section 55188, *Career Guidance*, will replace its scheduled class with a company visit. The class originally scheduled for Periods 8 and 9 on Thursday of Week 13, December 13, in E Building Staircase Classroom 202, will become a practical-learning visit to Guangdong Alpha Animation and Culture Co., Ltd.

Students enrolled in the section must meet at the campus motto monument at 9:20 a.m. on Sunday, December 16, 2012, and board the arranged transportation. The instructors are Su Wenji and Wang Bin.
