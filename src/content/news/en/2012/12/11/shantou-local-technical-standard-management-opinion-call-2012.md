The Shantou Bureau of Quality and Technical Supervision has prepared a draft *Measures for the Administration of Shantou Local Technical Standards* for public consultation. STU Research Office has circulated the draft and invited relevant faculty members to review it and submit comments.

The consultation aims to improve urban standardization management in Shantou, support an orderly market economy, and strengthen the role of standardization in serving society and the city’s development. Comments must be submitted to the STU Research Office by December 18.
