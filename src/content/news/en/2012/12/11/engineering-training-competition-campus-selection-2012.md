STU’s campus selection for the third National Undergraduate Engineering Training Comprehensive Ability Competition will be held from 2:00 to 6:00 p.m. on December 12, 2012, in the first-floor Student Activity Hall of the Library. The competition is open to current undergraduates, and faculty and students are welcome to observe.

Connected with engineering-training education, the selection will assess mechanical innovation design, manufacturing processes, practical skills, engineering management, and teamwork. The winning team from the campus selection will represent STU in the Guangdong Undergraduate Engineering Training Comprehensive Ability Competition.

The event aims to encourage students’ interest in research and engineering exploration, strengthen practical engineering skills, innovation, and collaboration, and give students an opportunity to apply engineering knowledge to practical problems.
