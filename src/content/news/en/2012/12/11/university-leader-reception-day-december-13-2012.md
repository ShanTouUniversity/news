STU’s Party and Administrative Office has announced the schedule for this week’s university-leader reception day. The reception is scheduled for 3:00 p.m. on Thursday, December 13, 2012. Faculty and students who wish to raise matters or offer suggestions may make an appointment in advance.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Thursday, December 13, 2012 |
| Time | 3:00 p.m. |
| Appointment phone | 86502306 |
| Requirement | Appointment required in advance |
| Issuing office | Party and Administrative Office |

The reception day provides a channel for faculty and students to communicate concerns and suggestions to university leaders. The original notice does not specify a reception venue; people with appointments may confirm the arrangements with the Party and Administrative Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office)

> ⚠️ One character in the duty leader’s name is garbled in the original notice and has not been reconstructed speculatively.
