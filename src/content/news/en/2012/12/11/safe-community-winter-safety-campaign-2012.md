As winter brings dry weather and increased electrical and fire risks in residential communities, STU Student Affairs Office and the Student Residence Self-Management Committee are organizing a Safe Community safety-awareness series. Under the theme “Safety: Visible to You and Me,” the activities will promote safe electricity use and accident prevention among current undergraduates.

| Activity | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sharing Safety Experiences | 11:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m., December 13, 2012 | Golden Triangle |
| Film Night: Take Safety Home | 7:30–10:15 p.m., December 15, 2012 | Activity Room, first floor, Building A |

The series will also distribute a safety reminder to each residence and display safety-themed posters or cartoons in residential areas. Organizers encourage students to share safety experiences, learn through films and publicity materials, follow safe electricity practices, and work together to maintain a safe community.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Safety: Visible to You and Me |
| Participants | Current undergraduates |
| Formats | Experience sharing, film night, residence reminders, and posters |
| Information channel | Sina Weibo, `@汕大宿委会` |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office and Student Residence Self-Management Committee |

Students can follow the relevant campus platforms for updates and participate according to the on-site instructions.
