It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism will hold a Media Salon on December 14, 2012, featuring Prof. Yang Pingyuan of Chinese Culture University.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Customer Relationship Management and Business Performance |
| Speaker | Prof. Yang Pingyuan, Chinese Culture University |
| Time | 16:10–18:00, December 14, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism |

Prof. Yang is a lecturer at the Chinese Association of Personnel Management and the Securities and Futures Market Development Foundation and an adjunct assistant professor at Chinese Culture University, with 25 years of corporate management experience specializing in cross-functional integrated management. All faculty and students are welcome.
