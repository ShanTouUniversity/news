The Business Practice Center of STU Business School will hold the Silk Road Marketing Competition on December 22, 2012, at the campus Walkway. The event will give students across the university a practical platform for sales and marketing, helping them develop planning, communication, and business skills.

Registration runs from December 5 through 14. Students may enter individually or in teams of no more than eight. The organizer will select entries based on the feasibility of the proposed products and sales plans, with 20 places planned. The final list will be announced separately.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | Silk Road Marketing Competition |
| Eligible participants | STU students |
| Competition date | December 22, 2012 |
| Venue | STU Walkway |
| Registration | December 5–14, 2012 |
| Entry format | Individual or team of up to eight |
| Places | 20 planned places, selected by proposal review |
| Organizer | Business Practice Center, STU Business School |

Applicants must submit a registration form describing the proposed product category, quantity, price range, and sales format. During the competition, participants will sell products from designated booths. Transaction volume generated on the Tao STU platform from December 16 through 22 will also count toward the final result. Teams must provide their cost prices and purchase documents to the Business Practice Center’s marketing-planning unit before the event.

Pre-competition training is scheduled for December 16, with the exact time and venue to be announced through Weibo, Fetion, and other channels. Awards include first, second, and third prizes of RMB 300, RMB 100, and RMB 50, respectively. Additional awards include Best Sales, Best Creative Design, Best Service Attitude, and Most Popularity, with certificates and gifts.

Interested students can download the registration form through the OA system and submit it as required. Further arrangements will be posted through OA, the Business School section of the BBS, and the Business Practice Center’s related platforms.
