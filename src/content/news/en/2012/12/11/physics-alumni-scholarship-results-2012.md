STU Student Affairs Office has announced the results of the 2012 Physics Alumni Scholarship. Following review by the School of Science selection committee, 20 Physics undergraduates received awards of RMB 2,500 each.

The recipients come from the 2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012 Physics cohorts. The 2009, 2010, and 2011 cohorts each account for four recipients, while the 2012 cohort accounts for eight. According to the Student Affairs notice, the scholarship is intended to encourage Physics students to study diligently and pursue progress.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Award | 2012 STU Physics Alumni Scholarship |
| Recipients | 20 |
| Amount | RMB 2,500 per recipient |
| Cohorts | Physics students from 2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012 |
| Selection body | School of Science selection committee |
| Issuing office | Student Affairs Office |
| Document | STU Student Affairs Document No. 11 of 2012 |

The university encouraged the recipients to maintain their motivation and continue achieving strong results in their studies and future work.
