STU Infrastructure Office has begun construction of a synthetic track at the university’s affiliated middle and primary schools. The project has been awarded to Shenzhen Aoshunda Industrial Co., Ltd. at a bid amount of RMB 88,000. Construction is scheduled to start on December 11, 2012, and finish by February 4, 2013.

Noise, vibration, and dust may occur during construction. The university asks passersby, especially students at the affiliated schools, to take care near the site. Faculty and other people must not enter the construction area without permission from the university’s responsible offices and the contractor and should avoid lingering nearby.
