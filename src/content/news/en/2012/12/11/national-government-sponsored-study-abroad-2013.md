The China Scholarship Council plans to select 18,000 students, scholars, and other participants nationwide for government-sponsored study abroad in 2013. STU International Exchange and Cooperation Office has circulated the selection schedule. Chinese citizens who meet the requirements of the *2013 Guide to Selecting Recipients of China Scholarship Council Funding for Study Abroad* and the relevant program guides may apply for funding through the prescribed procedures.

The planned categories include senior research and visiting scholars, including postdoctoral researchers; graduate students under the national program for building high-level universities; master’s students; outstanding undergraduate international exchange students; and overseas cooperation projects.

| Program | Planned places |
| --- | ---: |
| Senior research and visiting scholars, including postdoctoral researchers | 2,500 |
| Graduate students under the high-level-university program | 6,000 |
| Government-sponsored master’s students | 300 |
| Outstanding undergraduate international exchange | 2,000 |
| Overseas cooperation projects, including selected reciprocal scholarship plans | 950 |
| All categories | 18,000 |

### Guangdong schedule

The Guangdong receiving institution is located within the International Exchange and Cooperation Office of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. The Guangdong Education International Exchange Service Center has been entrusted with consultation and the receipt and organization of application materials. Consultations began in December 2012, and the application windows are as follows:

| Program | Application window |
| --- | --- |
| Senior research and visiting scholars, including postdoctoral researchers | March 1–20, 2013 |
| Graduate students under the high-level-university program | March 20–April 5, 2013 |
| Government-sponsored master’s students | March 20–April 5, 2013 |
| Special program for developing arts professionals | March 1–20, 2013 |

Applicants must apply online through the Government-Sponsored Study Abroad Information Management System at <http://apply.csc.edu.cn> and submit one paper copy to the receiving institution. Applications that are late or fail to meet the requirements will not be accepted. Results for the senior research and visiting scholar programs are expected in July 2013, while results for the high-level-university graduate and government-sponsored master’s programs are expected in May 2013. Arts, overseas cooperation, and other scholarship programs will follow their respective schedules.

Applicants can consult the China Scholarship Council website at <http://www.csc.edu.cn> for selection categories, priority disciplines, eligibility, and program guides. The Guangdong Education International Exchange Service Center is located on the second floor of 723 Dongfeng East Road, Guangzhou, and can be reached at (020) 37627213 or (020) 37628200.
