Under their course syllabi, two STU career-related course sections will replace their scheduled classes with a company visit to Guangdong Lafang Group. The sections are 55189, *Career Guidance*, and 55185, *Career Development Counseling and Training*.

Students enrolled in the two sections must meet at the campus motto monument at 1:30 p.m. on Saturday, December 15, 2012, and board the arranged transportation.

| Course | Original class | Instructors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 55189, *Career Guidance* | Periods A and B on Monday of Week 11, November 26, in D Building Room 304 | Wu Guangjiao and Wang Fengqi |
| 55185, *Career Development Counseling and Training* | Periods 3 and 4 on Thursday of Week 11, in D Building Room 206 | Lan Na and Zhang Xin |

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Revised activity | Company visit |
| Host company | Guangdong Lafang Group |
| Meeting time | 1:30 p.m., December 15, 2012 |
| Meeting point | Campus motto monument |
| Issuing office | Student Affairs Office |

Student Affairs asked students in both sections to share the information with classmates and meet at the campus motto monument at the stated time.
