STU’s Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication will host a Media Salon from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, December 14, 2012, in the Library Lecture Hall. Professor Yeh Ming-der, dean of Chinese Culture University’s College of Journalism and Communication, will discuss “Change and Continuity in Journalism and Communication in the Digital Age.”

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Media Salon |
| Topic | Change and Continuity in Journalism and Communication in the Digital Age |
| Speaker | Professor Yeh Ming-der |
| Position | Dean, College of Journalism and Communication, Chinese Culture University |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., December 14, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication |

Yeh holds a PhD in political science from the University of Pittsburgh. He has served as director of the Sun Yat-sen Institute at Chinese Culture University; professor and president of Toko University; professor and dean at National Chi Nan University; and researcher and director at National Chengchi University’s Institute of International Relations. His research areas include media history, government and politics in different countries, and international relations.

The lecture is open to the public, and faculty and students are welcome to attend.
