STU Research Office has issued instructions for the 2012 annual-progress and completion reports for National Natural Science Foundation projects. Leaders of active projects must submit annual progress reports, while projects scheduled to end at the close of 2012 must submit completion reports. All reports must be completed and submitted online through the National Natural Science Foundation of China’s ISIS project-management system.

Project leaders should log in at <http://isis.nsfc.gov.cn> with their existing ISIS usernames and passwords, complete the reports as required, and submit digitized supporting materials online. Research outputs must be collected through the foundation’s “Results Online” function.

| Report | Online deadline | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual progress report | January 4, 2013 | Online format review by STU Research Office; no paper copy required |
| Completion report | February 17, 2013 | After format review, download and print the final PDF in duplicate by February 22 and submit it to Research Office |

Completion reports should accurately describe the research plan and its implementation, major progress and results, talent development, collaboration and exchanges, funding use, and changes to the research plan. The report consists of the main text, research-output list, statistical data, signature and review pages, and supporting attachments. Output categories include awards, patents, monographs, journal papers, conference papers, and other results.

Research Office reminded project leaders that unpublished papers or papers lacking an acknowledgment of National Natural Science Foundation funding and the project approval number must not be included in completion reports. Paper text should not be copied directly into the report. After a project is approved for completion, the foundation may publish the completion report and application abstract on its website, so leaders should protect confidential information and intellectual property while ensuring that the content is factual and the data accurate.

Representative research-output attachments should be supplied as required. Materials that are difficult to digitize may be submitted with the paper completion report. The paper version must match the electronic version. Formal outputs published or released during the three years after completion should also be provided and archived in accordance with project-management requirements.
