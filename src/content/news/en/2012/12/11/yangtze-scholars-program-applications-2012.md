STU Human Resources Office has opened its 2012 application process for the Yangtze River Scholars Program, seeking high-level candidates in China and abroad. University units are asked to establish positions in line with STU’s development, discipline-building, and faculty-development plans and organize applications from qualified candidates.

The program supports universities in appointing distinguished professors and lecture professors. Distinguished professors teach core courses, guide young faculty and graduate students, lead discipline development, and undertake major research work. Lecture professors offer courses or lectures in frontier areas, guide young faculty and graduate students, and promote exchanges between universities in China and high-level academic institutions overseas.

### Eligibility

Distinguished-professor candidates should generally hold a doctoral degree and work on the front line of teaching and research. They should demonstrate high academic achievement, important recognized results, innovative and strategic thinking, team-leadership ability, and strong professional ethics. As of January 1, 2012, candidates in natural sciences and engineering should generally be no older than 45, while candidates in the humanities and social sciences should generally be no older than 55. They must work full-time at the appointing university during the appointment.

Lecture-professor candidates should generally work in teaching and research at a high-level university overseas and hold a professorial or equivalent position. They should have internationally recognized achievements and significant influence in their fields, maintain rigorous academic standards, and work at the appointing university for at least two months each year during the appointment. People already supported by the national Thousand Talents program are generally excluded, except participants in the Young Thousand Talents program.

STU will review candidates’ application materials, academic ethics, and political conduct, organize selection by experts or the Academic Committee, and publicize candidate materials on campus for one week. There is no quota limit for recommendations, but the university will apply strict review and selective recommendation standards. Confidential information must be technically processed in accordance with relevant rules.

Relevant Ministry of Education offices will receive electronic materials by January 18, 2013, and written materials plus an electronic disc by January 25. Detailed application formats and software are available from the Yangtze River Scholars Program website at <http://www.changjiang.edu.cn>.
