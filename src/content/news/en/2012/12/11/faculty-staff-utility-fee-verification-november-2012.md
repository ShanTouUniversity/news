STU Resources Management Office has posted faculty and staff utility charges for November 2012. Employees can log in to the STU water and electricity fee management system to review their charges. The latest meter readings were taken on December 1 and 2, following the previous readings on November 1.

Employees with questions or objections may visit the Water and Electricity Center for verification from December 11 through 14, 2012. Charges not challenged by the end of the period will be deducted from December 2012 salary accounts under university procedures. People without salary accounts must pay directly at the Finance Office.
