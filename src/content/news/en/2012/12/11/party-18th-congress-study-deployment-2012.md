The STU Party Committee has issued a plan for Party members to study and implement the spirit of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China. In line with requirements from the Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee, grassroots Party organizations will organize thematic study, organizational meetings, and practical activities from December 2012 through the end of March 2013, linking the work to the university’s development and progressive undergraduate education.

### Thematic study

Grassroots Party organizations will prepare study and training plans and organize symposiums, training sessions, lectures, and forums. Party members will study the 18th National Congress report and the Party Constitution, as well as speeches by Xi Jinping at the First Plenary Session of the 18th CPC Central Committee and Wang Yang at the Guangdong conference on implementing the Congress spirit. Study content and formats will be adapted to different types of Party members, while the Publicity Department will distribute and support the use of study materials.

Party members may also use related sections and exchange platforms of the Guangdong Organization Work website to study online and share reflections and work plans.

### Organizational meetings and improvement

By February 2013, the university plans to hold a special democratic life meeting for Party-member university leaders in conjunction with the 2012 annual meeting schedule. Grassroots Party organizations will hold special organizational meetings to review annual work, plan for the following year, and discuss approaches to institutional development.

The university-level meeting will include soliciting views before the meeting, criticism during the meeting, quality assessment afterward, and publicizing improvement results. Party-member leaders will also attend organizational meetings in their Party branches as required and take part in self-examination and criticism and self-criticism.

### Practical work and organization

STU will organize Party members and officials to take part in provincial “double study” activities, including online quizzes and the writing and reposting of “red messages” and “red microblogs.” Grassroots organizations will also develop practical activities suited to their circumstances, linking study outcomes to the university’s scientific and harmonious development, educational quality, and public-service work.

The Party Committee asked grassroots organizations to establish leadership responsibility systems, prepare implementation plans, and improve registration, assessment, and evaluation mechanisms for Party-member study. Units must report their progress to the university Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department, which will report regularly to the Organization Division of the Provincial Education Working Committee. The Publicity Department will use newspapers, radio, websites, and bulletin boards to share progress and results in Party-member education and grassroots Party building.
