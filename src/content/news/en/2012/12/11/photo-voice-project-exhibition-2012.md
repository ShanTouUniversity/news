STU College of Liberal Arts’s exhibition “Speaking Through Images: Photo Voice Project” will be held in the second-floor lobby of the STU Library from December 11 through 26, 2012. The exhibition presents selected photographs taken by project participants, offering views of daily life, identity, and their surroundings.

The Photo Voice project was proposed in December 2011 and formally implemented in March 2012 as a provincial undergraduate innovation project. It was supervised by Professor Karsten Krueger of the International Research Center in the College of Liberal Arts. Drawing on participant-centered anthropological research and visual-research methods, the project invited students at a Shantou school for the deaf to use cameras provided by the project to document their lives.

The project team did not intervene, direct, or comment during the photography process. Instead, it sought to use participants’ own images to present their lived experiences and perspectives as objectively as possible. The exhibition invites faculty and students to view and discuss the lives and future development of deaf students in Shantou.
