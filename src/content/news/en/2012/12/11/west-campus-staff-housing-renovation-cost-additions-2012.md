STU Infrastructure Office has publicized projected cost additions for the renovation project covering 36 staff residences on the West Campus. The project began on August 21, 2012. Based on construction conditions and requirements from the design institute and lightning-protection authority, the estimated additions total about RMB 55,000, keeping the cumulative increase below the university’s prescribed 10 percent limit.

| Projected addition | Reason or scope |
| --- | --- |
| Class-one surge protectors | Added at the request of the design institute and lightning-protection authority |
| Outdoor power cable | Actual construction required 30.4 meters more than the provisional design quantity |
| Outdoor drainage wells and pipes | Added according to construction needs |
| Outdoor water-supply pipes | Not included in the original design and added during construction |
| Concrete apron, sewage pipes, septic tanks, and concrete frontage | Existing facilities were damaged and had to be relaid or rebuilt |

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Renovation of 36 STU West Campus staff residences |
| Start date | August 21, 2012 |
| Estimated increase | About RMB 55,000 |
| Cumulative additions | Below the university’s 10 percent limit |
| Comment deadline | 5:30 p.m., December 13, 2012 |
| Comment method | Written submissions to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office |
| Issuing unit | Resources Management Office Infrastructure Office |

Organizations or individuals with comments on the projected additions may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office before the deadline.
