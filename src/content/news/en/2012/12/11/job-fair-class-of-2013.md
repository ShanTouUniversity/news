It is reported that Shantou University will hold the "STU Job Fair for the Class of 2013" on December 14, 2012, with approximately 60 employers expected on campus.

Participating organizations span multiple industries, including Shenzhen Tsingtao Brewery South China Marketing Co., Ltd., Suning Appliance, Shantou SEZ Ultrasonic Electronics Co., Ltd., Guangdong Huaxing Bank, Country Garden, the Shantou Border Defense Detachment, Wanjing Jincheng Apparel (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. and Guangdong Yashili Group Co., Ltd. The Student Affairs Office has advised graduating students to prepare in advance and attend on time.
