Yang Fengxia has passed the Guangdong Senior Professional Qualification Review Committee’s review for the qualification of chief physician in traditional Chinese internal medicine. In accordance with Guangdong’s rules for publicizing candidates who pass senior and intermediate professional-qualification reviews, the result has entered the publicity period.

The result will be publicized from 9:00 a.m. on December 11 through 9:00 a.m. on December 20, 2012, for a total of seven working days. Anyone with an objection to Yang Fengxia’s qualification may submit a signed written statement to the designated supervision body during the publicity period.

| --- | --- |
| Candidate | Yang Fengxia |
| Qualification | Chief physician in traditional Chinese internal medicine |
| Review body | Guangdong Senior Professional Qualification Review Committee for Health Professions |
| Publicity period | 9:00 a.m., December 11 to 9:00 a.m., December 20, 2012 |
| Duration | Seven working days |
| Objection method | Signed written submission |
