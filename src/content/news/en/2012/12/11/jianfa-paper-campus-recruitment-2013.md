Shantou Jianfa Paper Co., Ltd. and Xiamen Jianfa Paper Co., Ltd. will conduct their 2013 campus recruitment at STU on December 13, 2012. The companies will hold a presentation for Business School undergraduates and accept résumés from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. in E Building Room 303.

The recruitment includes business specialist and financial-accounting positions. Ten business specialists are sought in business administration or marketing, with locations including Shantou, Ningbo, Wuxi, Suzhou, Taizhou, Hangzhou, Wenzhou, and Yiwu. Two financial-accounting positions are available for accounting majors in Shantou. Both positions require a bachelor’s degree and CET-4-level English.

| Position | Major | Number | Education | English | Locations |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Business specialist | Business administration or marketing | 10 | Bachelor’s degree | CET-4 | Shantou, Ningbo, Wuxi, Suzhou, Taizhou, Hangzhou, Wenzhou, and Yiwu |
| Financial accounting | Accounting | 2 | Bachelor’s degree | CET-4 | Shantou |

### Recruitment schedule

The recruitment process consists of a company presentation, résumé submission, group interview, second interview, written test, preliminary offer list, online testing, company internship, contract signing, and onboarding. The presentation and résumé submission will take place in E Building Room 303 on December 13. The group interview, second interview, and written test are scheduled for December 14 at a venue to be announced.

Graduating students attending the recruitment should prepare a stamped transcript, one-inch photograph, foreign-language certificate, other award certificates, and graduate recommendation form.

According to the company profile, Xiamen Jianfa Paper was established in 1992 as a wholly owned subsidiary of C&D Inc. It operates in domestic and import-export trade involving paper, pulp, waste paper, board, wood chips, and paper products. New graduates will sign three-year employment contracts. The stated first-year monthly salary is at least RMB 3,400, excluding bonuses, with benefits including the statutory five-insurance-and-one-fund package. Final terms are subject to the company’s offer and employment contract.
