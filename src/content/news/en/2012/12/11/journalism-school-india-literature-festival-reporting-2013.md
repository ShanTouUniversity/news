STU’s Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication has opened recruitment for its 2013 India reporting project. The project will send students to cover a literature festival in India and examine the country’s development from a cultural perspective. It is tentatively scheduled for January 23 to February 4, 2013, with four students to be selected.

### Eligibility

The opportunity is open to Journalism School undergraduates in their third year or above and graduate students. The selection will consider GPA, reporting and photography skills, spoken English, media-internship experience, enthusiasm for the work, and the ability to work under pressure. Students who have previously taken part in overseas internships or reporting projects organized by the school will generally not be considered for this selection.

Interested students must complete the school’s application form and submit it through the designated school channel by 5:00 p.m. on December 18. A selection committee will determine the participants; if a selected student cannot travel, replacements will be taken in order.

The application form asks about English ability, academic ranking, overseas experience, equipment skills, and personal identification. Applicants should complete it truthfully as required by the school. The OA notice and subsequent school announcements govern the detailed submission channel and project arrangements.
