The STU–University of Calgary exchange program has been approved under the China Scholarship Council’s Outstanding Undergraduate International Exchange Program. Two initial funded undergraduates from STU’s Engineering School will begin a one-year period of study at the University of Calgary in Canada in January 2013.

The program was submitted through the university’s Outstanding Engineer Education and Training Program. Under its arrangement, the Engineering School may send five exchange students to the University of Calgary each year. China Scholarship Council funding covers one round-trip international airfare and living expenses during the study period.

The Outstanding Undergraduate International Exchange Program was established to guide public-sponsored study abroad, promote cooperation between Chinese universities and leading international institutions, and develop internationally minded talent with innovation, practical skills, and global competitiveness.

According to the notice, this was the first time the China Scholarship Council had funded undergraduate study abroad and the first time STU had successfully obtained approval for one of its projects. Other key disciplines at STU were also expected to pursue additional applications for funded exchange opportunities.
