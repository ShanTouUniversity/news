STU Resources Management Office has published utility charges for student dormitories for November 2012. Meter readings were taken on November 29 and 30. Students may log in to STU’s utility-fee management system to check their dormitory charges and verify the records within the specified period.

The review period is during working hours from December 6 through 12. Students who find errors or have questions should complete and sign a Utility-Fee Review Application Form and submit it to the Water and Electricity Center. Records without objections before the review period ends will be collected through the campus-card payment system as scheduled.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Billing month | November 2012 |
| Meter-reading dates | November 29–30, 2012 |
| Query system | STU utility-fee management system: <http://power.stu.edu.cn/> |
| Login | Individual campus-network account |
| Review and challenge period | Working hours, December 6–12, 2012 |
| Challenge material | Completed and signed Utility-Fee Review Application Form |
| Submission location | Water and Electricity Center, beside the Science Building |
| Regular payment period | December 13–17, 2012 |
| Collection method | Campus-card deduction |
| Late handling | Handled under university rules from December 18 |
| Water and Electricity Center phone | 2489 |

Students should top up their campus cards in time to ensure sufficient balance. Challenges raised after the review period that require correction after verification may take three to five working days to process. Cases in which dormitory information does not match the student roster may be reported to the counselor’s office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
