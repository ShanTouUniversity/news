Following the review and recommendation of the STU Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. was named the winning bidder for the environmental improvement project around 36 staff-housing units on the west campus. The bid amount is RMB 69,526.

The result is being publicized from December 6 through 10, 2012. Organizations or individuals with objections may contact the project owner or the designated complaint-handling office during the publicity period.
