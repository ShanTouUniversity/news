STU Network and Information Center has announced that maintenance related to an adjustment of Guangdong’s power-communications core network may cause brief interruptions to the university’s education-network connection.

The expected impact period is 1:00–7:00 a.m. on December 7, 2012. During the maintenance, campus-network users may be unable to access education-network resources, while users outside the campus may be unable to access campus-network resources. The Network and Information Center will monitor the connection and prepare emergency measures; no separate restoration notice will be issued.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Reason | Adjustment to the provincial power-communications core network |
| Expected impact | 1:00–7:00 a.m., December 7, 2012 |
| Scope | On-campus access to education-network resources and off-campus access to campus-network resources |
| Interruption | Brief interruption; restoration time will not be announced separately |
| Responsible unit | STU Network and Information Center |
| Service phone | 0754-86503400 |
| Service email | stunic@stu.edu.cn |

The Network and Information Center asked campus-network users to plan network use in advance and share the maintenance information with others.
