STU English Language Center will hold the 10th English Festival from December 7 to 9, 2012, under the theme “Stories the World Should Hear.” The festival aims to improve students’ English communication skills, broaden their international outlook, and provide opportunities for language, interpersonal, and intercultural practice through university exchange.

Seoul National University, Jinan University, South China Agricultural University, and Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University have been invited to participate. Their contestants will join STU students in the Intercollegiate English Speech Contest, Intercollegiate English Debate Competition, and Intercollegiate English Singing Contest.

| Activity | Time | Venue | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening ceremony and film screening | 7:30 p.m., December 7 | Grand Auditorium | *The Wizard of Oz* |
| Intercollegiate English Speech Contest | 7:30 p.m., December 8 | Grand Auditorium | English speech competition |
| Intercollegiate English Debate Competition | 3:00 p.m., December 9 | Grand Auditorium | English debate competition |
| Intercollegiate English Singing Contest | 7:30 p.m., December 9 | Grand Auditorium | English singing competition |

Organized by the English Language Center, the festival combines student and faculty participation. In addition to competing, students may gain practical experience in stage management, lighting, choreography, delegation reception, and event coordination. The speech contest will include prepared and impromptu speeches, the debate contest will use the British Parliamentary format, and the singing contest will include solo and duet performances.
