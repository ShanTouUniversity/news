In accordance with Guangdong’s review program for organizational staffing in provincial public institutions, STU is publicizing its organizational staffing information. The materials will be displayed in Meeting Room 312 of the New Administration Building from December 6 through 12, 2012.

Campus units and individuals with different opinions may submit written comments to the Human Resources Office during the publicity period. Materials submitted by units must carry an official seal, while individuals must sign their comments with their real names.
