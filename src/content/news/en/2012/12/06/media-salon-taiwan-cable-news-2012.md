The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at STU will host a media salon on December 7, 2012, under the theme “The State of Taiwan Cable News and Reflections.” Yang Hua, director of the international-news center at TVBS News, will draw on her experience in news production and international reporting to discuss Taiwan’s cable-news landscape and professional issues.

Yang previously served as deputy editor-in-chief of Era Television’s News Department, a political reporter for the Liberty Times, and a Los Angeles correspondent for the United Daily News Group’s World Journal. Her reporting has covered environmental and green issues and natural disasters, and she has extensive experience in international interviews and domestic broadcast coverage. Her honors include television international-reporting awards at the Excellence in Journalism Awards, the Xue Xue Foundation Green Fashion Influential Award, and the United Daily News Wang Tiwu Journalism Research Award.
