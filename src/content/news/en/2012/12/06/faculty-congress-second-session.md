It is reported that the second session of the Fifth Faculty Congress and the Seventh Labor Union Congress of Shantou University will be held at 8:30 am on December 8 (Saturday) in the Science Lecture Hall. The labor union has issued seating and attendance arrangements to all delegates.

## Meeting Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 8:30 am, December 8 (Saturday) |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Sign-in | Delegates should sign in 15 minutes in advance |

According to the seating plan, the center of the first row is reserved for university leaders and members of the presidium; the center of the second row for specially invited and observer delegates; and formally elected delegates will be seated in the central area from the third row onward.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
