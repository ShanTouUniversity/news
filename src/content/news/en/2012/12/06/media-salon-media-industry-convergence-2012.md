The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at STU will host a media salon on December 7, 2012, under the theme “Challenges of Convergence Effects in the Media Industry.” Dr. Yang Zhihong, founding dean of the College of Communication at Taiwan’s Ming Chuan University, will discuss the effects and challenges of media-industry convergence, with a focus on media management, creative and cultural industries, and digital media.

Yang is chair of the Taiwan Association of Communication Management and a research fellow at Peking University’s Institute for Cultural Industries. He served as founding dean of Ming Chuan University’s College of Communication and founding director of its Graduate Institute of Communication Management. He received the college’s Lifetime Achievement Award in 2011. His work has promoted research and teaching on digital multimedia platforms and explored how communication industries can develop through knowledge and how communication knowledge can support industry.

Yang has also served as a board member or executive at Taiwanese television companies, a telecommunications company, and a newspaper group, and participated in establishing radio stations, cable-television services, newspapers, and magazines. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
