STU Zhicheng College will hold Zhicheng Salon 3 on December 8, 2012, under the theme “Expectations and a Few Words of Advice for University Students.” Hong Kong deputy to the National People’s Congress and barrister Rita Fan will be the main speaker, with Chan Wong Sui serving as moderator. They will discuss student growth, responsibility, and choices during university.

The event will run from 8:40 to 10:00 p.m. in Lecture Room J-354 and will be hosted by a college-level student society. Students may register by emailing `ATP@stu.edu.cn` by 5:00 p.m. on December 7, following the requirements in the notice.

Rita Fan holds an honorary Doctor of Laws degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong. She is a Hong Kong deputy to the National People’s Congress and a member of the Hong Kong Basic Law Committee and the Basic Law Promotion Steering Committee. She has contributed to work related to the Basic Law and received the Grand Bauhinia Medal.

Chan Wong Sui is an honorary fellow of The Chinese University of Hong Kong and chairs the Hong Kong Deposit Protection Board. She also serves on bodies related to university grants, law reform, and charity law, and previously led the Hong Kong Consumer Council and other social-service organizations.

The salon will give students an opportunity to discuss public affairs, social responsibility, and personal development during university.
