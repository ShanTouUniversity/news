Business School Alumni Lecture 16 at STU will be held on December 8, 2012, under the theme “Ideas Shape Outcomes: Practical Experience in Power-Project Management.” Qiu Jianxi, a 2001 alumnus of the Business School’s International Accounting program, will draw on his professional experience to discuss investment and financing planning and project management in the power sector.

Qiu earned a bachelor’s degree in accounting from STU’s Business School and a master’s degree in management from Sun Yat-sen University. He holds professional qualifications including Certified Internal Auditor, International Certified Senior Financial Manager, and Economist. He currently works in project investment and financing planning and corporate-group treasury management, and serves as treasury supervisor at Guangdong Yuedian Finance Co., Ltd.
