STU’s School of Science will hold a Science Salon academic lecture on December 8, 2012. Professor Dong Wei of the School of Light Industry and Food Sciences at South China University of Technology will present “Microalgal Biotechnology: Basic Research and Applied Technologies.”

The presentation will introduce applications of microalgal biotechnology in biochemicals, biofuels, and aquaculture feed. It will also discuss transcriptomics and metabolomics for discovering microalgal biomarkers and metabolic networks, as well as biorefinery approaches for converting and adding value to biomass waste.

Professor Wei is a professor and doctoral supervisor at South China University of Technology and serves on the Biotechnology Division of the Chinese Society of Oceanology and Limnology. His research focuses on microalgal biotechnology, omics technologies, and biorefinery. He has conducted visiting research at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and The Ohio State University, led multiple projects supported by the Ministry of Science and Technology, the State Oceanic Administration, and Guangdong Province, and served as a reviewer for international journals.

The School of Science welcomes faculty and students interested in microalgal technology, bioengineering, and the use of biological resources.
