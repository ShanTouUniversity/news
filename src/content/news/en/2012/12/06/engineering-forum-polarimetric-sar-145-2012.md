STU Engineering Research Forum No. 145 will be held on December 7, 2012, under the theme “Polarimetric Synthetic-Aperture Radar.” Professor Yang Jian of the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University will review the development of polarization technology and synthetic-aperture radar, then introduce polarimetric SAR systems in China and abroad, their application prospects, and their advantages in feature extraction for target detection, classification, and recognition.

Polarimetric synthetic-aperture radar obtains target information by combining different polarization states for electromagnetic-wave transmission and reception. It is a major research topic in radar science. Drawing on multiple cases, the presentation will discuss the value of polarimetric SAR for extracting target information and supporting recognition.

Professor Yang is a professor and doctoral supervisor in Tsinghua’s Department of Electronic Engineering. His research covers polarimetric radar and SAR applications, including optimal polarization, clutter suppression, target decomposition, target-feature extraction and recognition, polarimetric SAR image processing, and array signal processing. He has published several papers in IEEE journals, led multiple funded research projects, received provincial- or ministerial-level science and technology progress awards five times, and delivered invited presentations at international academic conferences.

The Engineering School welcomes faculty and students interested in radar technology, signal processing, and SAR applications.
