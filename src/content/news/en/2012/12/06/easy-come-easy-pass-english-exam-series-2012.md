To help students prepare for CET-4/6, IELTS, TOEFL, and GRE examinations, the STU Youth League Committee and Students’ Union will hold the Easy Come, Easy Pass English-exam series. Instructors from New Oriental’s Guangzhou school will share preparation methods and examination strategies.

The series will have four sessions. The afternoon of December 8 will focus on CET-4/6 preparation, followed by an IELTS session that evening. The morning of December 9 will cover TOEFL and GRE preparation, and a Q&A session titled “Teachers, Let’s Talk” will be held that afternoon in Room 726 of Building 7.

| Session | Time | Venue | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3:00–5:00 p.m., December 8 | Science Report Hall | CET-4/6 preparation |
| 2 | 7:30–9:30 p.m., December 8 | Science Report Hall | IELTS preparation |
| 3 | 10:00 a.m.–noon, December 9 | Science Report Hall | TOEFL and GRE preparation |
| 4 | 3:00–5:00 p.m., December 9 | Building 7, Room 726 | “Teachers, Let’s Talk” Q&A |

The invited New Oriental instructors are Shuai Jianxiang, Fan Yi, and Lin Ruhai. Shuai focuses on vocabulary, pronunciation, and English speech coaching. Fan holds a master’s degree from New York University and has worked in financial consulting and interpreting. Lin is from Canada and teaches IELTS and TOEFL listening and speaking, with an emphasis on practical English.
