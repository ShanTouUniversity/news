It is reported that many faculty and staff members have recently inquired about the urban employee basic medical insurance outpatient fee co-ordination policy, prompting the Resource Management Office to release a clarification of the key policy points.

According to the Opinions on Implementing Outpatient Medical Expense Co-ordination under Urban Employee Basic Medical Insurance (Shan Ren She Fa [2012] No. 2), which was published on the university's internal office network on July 2, 2012, insured persons visiting designated medical institutions since July 1, 2012 are entitled to have the insurance pooling fund pay 50% of their basic outpatient medical expenses (including general consultation fees), capped at 20 yuan per person per month.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Policy basis | Shan Ren She Fa [2012] No. 2 |
| Effective date | From July 1, 2012 |
| Reimbursement rate | 50% of basic outpatient fees (incl. general consultation) |
| Cap | 20 yuan per person per month |
| Cumulative quota, Jul–Dec 2012 | 120 yuan |

It should be noted that any unused balance at the end of the year will be returned to the insurance fund and cleared, with no carry-over to the following year.

In addition, the campus hospital, as a grassroots designated medical institution in Shantou, provides basic outpatient services for faculty and staff whose costs fall within the scope of the insurance pooling fund, meaning staff can enjoy the reimbursement benefits directly when seeking treatment there.
