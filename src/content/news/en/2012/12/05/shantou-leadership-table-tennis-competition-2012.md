The Shantou Sports Bureau will hold the Guoshou Cup leadership table-tennis competition from December 22 to 24, 2012. STU Trade Union is collecting registrations from eligible campus personnel who wish to participate. The event will be held at the Shantou Table Tennis School.

The competition is open to current and retired officials at deputy-division level or above from districts and counties, municipal departments, central and provincial units based in Shantou, and military units stationed in Shantou. Events include team competition, men’s singles, women’s singles, and mixed doubles. Mixed-doubles pairs may cross organizational boundaries, but no more than one such cross-unit pair is allowed.

Each organization may enter three team players. Men’s singles will have groups for current and retired officials at deputy-department level or above and at deputy-division level or above, with the higher-level group eligible to enter the lower group. Women’s singles will not be divided into groups. The team event will use round-robin play in the first stage, followed by elimination and placement matches; singles and doubles formats will be determined according to registrations. All matches will be best of five games to 11 points under the Chinese Table Tennis Association’s 2011 rules.

The team event and each individual event will recognize the top eight finishers, with fewer places awarded when participation is below the threshold. Team prizes from first through eighth are RMB 1,000, 800, 700, 600, and 400 for fifth through eighth. In men’s singles, women’s singles, and mixed doubles, prizes from first through eighth are RMB 600, 500, 400, 300, and 200 for fifth through eighth. Participating teams are responsible for their own accommodation, meals, transportation, and related costs.

The OA notice describes the event as the third edition, while the attached competition regulations are titled the “New Year Fifth Guoshou Cup.” This article therefore does not assign a single edition number; the organizing committee’s formal release will govern.
