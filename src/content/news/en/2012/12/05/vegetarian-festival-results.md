It is reported that the "Colorful Vegetarian Festival," a Student Community X Program series organized by the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University and hosted by the Vegetarian Culture Association, was successfully held on December 1-2. The results of the "Vegetarian DIY" cooking contest, the highlight of the series, have now been announced.

The cooking contest consisted of two rounds: a vegetarian knowledge quiz and on-site dish preparation. After the competition, one first prize, one second prize, two third prizes, and one Most Popular Award were presented.

## Awards

| Prize | Number |
| --- | --- |
| First Prize | 1 team |
| Second Prize | 1 team |
| Third Prize | 2 teams |
| Most Popular Award | 1 team |
