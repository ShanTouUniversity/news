STU Student Affairs Office has launched the 2012 selection for the Jinfeng Award for its student-affairs system. The award recognizes outstanding work, strengthens the student-affairs workforce, and supports improvements in student development. The selection will follow the university’s Jinfeng Award selection measures for the student-affairs system.

Eligible personnel include staff members of the Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, and People’s Armed Forces Department, as well as student-affairs workers in colleges and residential colleges. Applicants must have worked continuously at STU for more than three years, must not have previously received the same award, must submit their own application with confirmation from a supervisor, and must have achieved notable results in their work.

The selection will focus on commitment and performance, including exemplary fulfillment of duties, sustained dedication to student work, and outstanding or continuing contributions to student development, services, and support.

| Service requirement | More than three consecutive years at STU |
| Other conditions | No previous same award, notable performance, and supervisor confirmation |
| Application deadline | December 15, 2012 |
| Submission location | Student Affairs Office, Room 106 |
| Phone | 86503299 |

Application materials include the Jinfeng Award application form, the candidate’s work statement explaining eligibility, and a statement from a supervisor on the candidate’s performance. Applicants must submit the materials to Room 106 of the Student Affairs Office and provide the electronic form as required before the deadline.
