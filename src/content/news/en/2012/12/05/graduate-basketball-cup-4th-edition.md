It is reported that the Graduate Student Union of Shantou University is hosting the 4th "Shining Master Cup" Basketball Tournament from December 1 to 30 at the campus basketball courts, open to all graduate students on the main campus. The tournament got underway on December 1 and will run through December 30.

The tournament aims to enrich the extracurricular life of graduate students, foster a healthy and vibrant campus atmosphere, promote physical fitness, and strengthen communication among colleges.

The detailed match schedule is available in the attachment on the OA system. All graduate students are welcome to attend and cheer for the teams.
