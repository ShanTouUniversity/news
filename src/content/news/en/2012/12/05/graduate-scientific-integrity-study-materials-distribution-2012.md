To support research-integrity and academic-conduct education, the STU Graduate School is distributing reference materials to graduate-training units on the main campus. The materials include the Reference Outline for Research Integrity and Academic Conduct Education and the Trial Study Outline for Research Integrity and Academic Conduct Development, for use in educational activities and for faculty and student reading.

The distribution covers college deans, leaders responsible for graduate work, graduate secretaries, and college resource rooms. Materials in resource rooms are available for faculty and student borrowing. Each graduate-training unit should arrange for a representative designated by its graduate secretary to collect the materials from the Graduate School on the morning of December 6, 2012.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Material 1 | Reference Outline for Research Integrity and Academic Conduct Education |
| Material 2 | Trial Study Outline for Research Integrity and Academic Conduct Development |
| Recipients | Graduate-training units on the main campus |
| Use | Educational activities and faculty and student reading |
| Collection time | Morning of December 6, 2012 |
| Collection office | STU Graduate School |

Graduate-training units may use the materials according to their circumstances to advance research-integrity and academic-conduct education.
