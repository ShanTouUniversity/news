STU’s sixth Sunshine Sports Winter Run will begin in December 2012 under the theme “Love the Motherland, Start with Physical Exercise.” Organized by the STU Sports Committee and open to all colleges and Zhicheng College, the program aims to improve students’ endurance, strengthen their will, and build a sense of community through regular running.

The opening ceremony will take place at 3:30 p.m. on Saturday, December 8, at the university motto stele. Each college or residential college must organize at least 60 students for the opening run, which will cover one lap of the campus road, approximately two kilometers. From December 2012 through April 2013, participating units will also organize regular campus-road runs according to the activity calendar, generally with at least 100 students at each session.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Love the Motherland, Start with Physical Exercise |
| Program period | December 2012 through April 30, 2013 |
| Opening ceremony | 3:30 p.m., December 8, 2012, at the university motto stele |
| Route | One campus-road lap, approximately 2 km |
| Opening-run scale | At least 60 students from each college or residential college |
| Regular-run scale | At least 100 students from each unit per session |
| Final mileage count | 5:00 p.m., April 30, 2013 |

College and residential-college Students’ Unions will also organize group runs during morning exercises and afternoon extracurricular periods. The reference distance is 2,000 meters per student per day. Units must record participation and mileage, and their sports officers should report the previous week’s total participants, mileage, and activity photographs to the Sports Office each Monday. These statistics will provide an important basis for selecting the Outstanding Unit and Outstanding Individual awards.

The university will also incorporate long-distance running into physical-education teaching, with a 1,000-meter test for men and an 800-meter test for women accounting for 20 percent of the physical-education course grade. At the end of the program, the selection panel will name four Outstanding Units and 30 Outstanding Individuals, presenting trophies to units and certificates to individuals. Unit selection will consider organization, publicity, participation coverage, completion of running distances, and safety.
