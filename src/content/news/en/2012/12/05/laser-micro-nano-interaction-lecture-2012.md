It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on December 7, 2012, featuring Prof. Lan Sheng of the School of Information and Optoelectronic Science and Engineering, South China Normal University.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Interaction of Lasers with Micro-Nano Particles/Structures and Device Applications |
| Speaker | Prof. Lan Sheng, South China Normal University |
| Time | 15:00, December 7, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, School of Science (Li-352) |
| Organizer | School of Science |

## Content

The lecture focuses on the interaction of lasers with micro-nano particles/structures, covering continuous-wave lasers, femtosecond lasers and terahertz waves with metal, semiconductor, polymer and magnetic micro-nano particles/structures, including: femtosecond-laser-induced micro-nano structures on metal/semiconductor surfaces for colored materials; continuous-laser manipulation and assembly of particles into 3D opto-matter and colloidal photonic crystals; magnetic nanoparticle manipulation for Anderson localization; terahertz wave localization and slow/fast light; and femtosecond laser interaction with gold nanorods for ultrahigh-density optical storage and encryption.

## Speaker

Lan Sheng, born in 1967, is a professor and doctoral supervisor at South China Normal University and a member of the Optical Society of America. He studied at Peking University from 1985 to 1995, earning bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral degrees in physics, followed by postdoctoral and research positions at Nanyang Technological University, University of Tsukuba and NEDO, receiving a Doctor of Engineering from the University of Tsukuba in 2002. He previously served as a professor in the Department of Physics, School of Science, Shantou University from 2003. He has published over 110 SCI papers, holds four Japanese patents, leads multiple national and provincial projects, and was selected for the Ministry of Education’s New Century Excellent Talents Program and as a Guangdong Pearl River Scholar.
