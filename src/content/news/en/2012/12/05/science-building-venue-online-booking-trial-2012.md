To improve venue management and services, STU Research Office has launched a trial of an online booking system for the Science Building with support from the Network Center. The system covers the Science Building Theater and Meeting Room 205 and entered trial operation on December 5, 2012.

At this stage, only STU employees may log in and make bookings. Users should enter the “STU Science Building Venue Booking System” from the Research Office home page, then select “Activities and Venue Booking,” “My Affairs,” and “Science Building Venue Booking.” After a booking is approved, users must print the Science Building Venue Reservation Form, have it signed by the person responsible for the organizing unit, and deliver it to the Science Building gatehouse.
