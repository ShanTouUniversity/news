The STU Student Affairs Office has requested 2012 student-affairs summaries and 2013 work plans from each college and residential college. Units should review their experience and distinctive practices from the past year in light of the university’s educational goals and plan the next year’s work based on university and unit development objectives.

The documents should cover scholarships, grants and student loans, ideological education, peer learning, residential-community advising, employment, entrepreneurship, enrichment, campus culture, and Youth League work. Major work or activities should include their start and end dates.

| Document | Main requirements |
| --- | --- |
| 2012 student-affairs summary | Review experience, distinctive practices, and results, including student and Youth League work |
| 2013 student-affairs plan | Set priorities based on university and college or residential-college objectives |
| Major work and activities | State start and end dates |

Each college and residential college must submit the paper documents to Room 106 of the Student Affairs Office by December 20, 2012. Paper copies must carry the seal of the college Party general branch. Electronic documents should be sent to the Student Affairs Office’s work mailbox as required.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Submitting units | All colleges and residential colleges |
| Deadline | December 20, 2012 |
| Paper submission | Student Affairs Office, Room 106 |
| Paper requirement | College Party general-branch seal |
| Electronic submission | Student Affairs work mailbox: o_xsc@stu.edu.cn |
| Responsible office | STU Student Affairs Office |

The Student Affairs Office will use the annual summaries and plans to review results and priorities across the university’s student-affairs work.
