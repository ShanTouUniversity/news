STU’s eighth Graduate Academic Forum, Higher-Education Forum, will be held on December 7, 2012. Organized by the Graduate School and hosted by the Institute of Higher Education, the forum aims to strengthen the graduate academic environment, encourage exchange, and improve graduate students’ research literacy.

The forum will feature six presentations on higher-education research. Topics range from the importance of death education, university institutional design, and happiness from a positive-psychology perspective to student self-aesthetic education, comparative residential-college governance, and the formation mechanisms and responses related to same-sex attraction among female university students. Together, the topics connect educational research with student development, campus governance, and social issues.

| Research direction | Main focus |
| --- | --- |
| Death education | Why death education matters |
| University constitutionalism | Problems and possible paths in university institution-building |
| Positive psychology | Happiness from a positive-psychology perspective |
| Student aesthetic education | Self-aesthetic education and the development of students’ aesthetic character |
| Residential-college governance | A comparison of Yale residential colleges and STU’s Zhicheng College |
| Gender and society | Formation mechanisms and responses related to same-sex attraction among female university students |

The forum will run from 7:00 to 9:30 p.m. on December 7 in Science Building Meeting Room 205. All graduate students, undergraduates interested in academic research, and faculty members are welcome. Ma Fengqi, Hu Wenlong, and Lin Dongyue of the Institute of Higher Education will serve as invited guests.
