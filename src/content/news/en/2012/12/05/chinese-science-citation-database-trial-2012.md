The STU Library has opened trial access to the Chinese Science Citation Database (CSCD) and invited students and faculty to use the resource and provide feedback. The trial will run through February 4, 2013.

Created in 1989 and developed and maintained by the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Documentation and Information Center, CSCD indexes Chinese and English core and high-quality journals in mathematics, physics, chemistry, astronomy, earth sciences, biology, agricultural and forestry sciences, health and medicine, engineering and technology, environmental science, management science, and other fields. The database has accumulated more than four million paper records and more than 18 million citation records dating from 1989 onward.

Students and faculty can access CSCD through the library database details page, test its scientific-literature and citation-search functions, and send evaluations to the Library Subject Services Department.
