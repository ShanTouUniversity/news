In accordance with arrangements from the National Advisory Committee on Foreign Language Teaching’s English-major oral-test office, the 2012 National TEM8 Oral Test will be held at STU on December 8. The Academic Affairs Office has reminded relevant students in the School of Liberal Arts to arrive on time and review the examination guide in advance.

The test will begin at 8:30 a.m. in Room E602. Candidates should bring the required examination and identity-verification materials and follow the test procedures. They should also read the “TEM8 Oral Test Guide” before the examination.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Test | 2012 National TEM8 Oral Test |
| Time | 8:30 a.m., December 8, 2012 |
| Venue | Room E602 |
| Intended readers | Relevant candidates in the School of Liberal Arts |
| Issuing office | STU Academic Affairs Office |
| Governing arrangement | National English-major oral-test office examination arrangements |

Candidates should confirm the venue and admission requirements in advance. The original attachment issued by the Academic Affairs Office governs the detailed guide and examination procedures.
