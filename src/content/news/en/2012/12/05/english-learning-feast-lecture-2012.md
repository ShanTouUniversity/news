STU’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host a lecture titled “How to Enjoy an English Feast” on December 5, 2012. Wang and Scott Fisher will discuss English learning and language exchange in a dialogue format.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture | How to Enjoy an English Feast |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., December 5, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture classroom, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Format | Dialogue between Wang and Scott Fisher |
| Audience | Faculty and students |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

Faculty and students interested in English learning and intercultural communication are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
