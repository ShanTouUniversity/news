To promote the volunteer spirit of participation, mutual assistance, dedication, and progress, the Shantou Federation of Trade Unions has established the Shantou Workers’ Volunteer Association as a platform for public-service volunteering. The STU Trade Union is inviting employees interested in social-service work to apply for membership.

The association will draw on resources from different industries to serve employees and other groups facing special difficulties, including people who are isolated, elderly, ill, disabled, vulnerable, or poor. Its work will focus on supporting people with disabilities and other vulnerable groups, spreading civility, and serving the community.

Employees who wish to join should complete the membership application form, attach one one-inch headshot, and submit the paper materials to the STU Trade Union Office by December 12, 2012. The electronic application form should also be submitted as required.

The Trade Union encourages employees to contribute professional and industry resources to volunteer service and help build a platform for mutual assistance. The original attachment governs the application-form format and submission requirements.
