STU Business School has opened the selection of candidates for training and participation in its 2013 international business-case competition program. Applications are open to current undergraduates in the Business School’s 2010, 2011, and 2012 cohorts. Selected students will receive training and may participate in international case competitions scheduled for the second half of 2013.

Applicants must have completed economics and business-management courses, demonstrate strong English listening, speaking, and reading skills, and have an average score of at least 80 in their completed ELC Level 1–5 courses. Registration runs from December 5 through 8, 2012, through the “International Case Competition Registration” link on the Business School website.

Shortlisted candidates will receive an English-language case on December 9. They must analyze the case and prepare an English-language PowerPoint presentation before attending interviews on the afternoon of December 12. The final team list will be posted on the Business School website.

The accompanying 2013 competition calendar lists international case events in China, Canada, the United States, Thailand, Singapore, Australia, and Hong Kong, including the CIMA Global Business Challenge, Manitoba International Marketing Competition, CaseIT, Marshall International Case Competition, mai Bangkok Business Challenge, Asia Venture Challenge, NUS.DBS International Case Competition, and Citi International Case Competition.
