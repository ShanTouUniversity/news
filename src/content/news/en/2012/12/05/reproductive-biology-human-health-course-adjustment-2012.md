The STU Academic Affairs Office has announced a course adjustment. Because the Biology Department instructor will be away attending a meeting, the two sections of Reproductive Biology and Human Reproductive Health scheduled in Week 12 will be suspended and made up on Saturday of Week 13.

| Class number | Original class | Make-up class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 56860 | Periods 6 and 7, December 6, Room G402 | AB periods, December 15, Room G402 |
| 55253 | Periods 6 and 7, December 7, Room E207 | Periods 6 and 7, December 15, Room E207 |

Students enrolled in the course should inform one another and attend the make-up classes at the revised times and locations. The make-up classrooms remain the same as those originally scheduled.
