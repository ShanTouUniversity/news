It is reported that, in a bid to further improve campus sanitation and strengthen plant pest control, the Resources Management Office of Shantou University will carry out spraying operations on greenery, flowers, trees, fruit trees and ditches across the main campus and the Continuing Education College from December 16 to 23. Teachers, students and staff are advised to take care during this period.

## Zone-by-Zone Spraying Schedule

| Date | Areas Covered |
| ---- | ------------- |
| Dec 16 | Affiliated Middle School and Primary School area up to the Mental Health Center |
| Dec 17 | Artificial Lake area, sports field |
| Dec 18 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, student dormitory areas |
| Dec 19 | Reservoir dam, Building 789, faculty housing areas A-B |
| Dec 20 | Medical Building, new teaching building, student dormitories G/H/I/J |
| Dec 21 | Around campus security office, campus gates, campus roads, perimeter of the new library |
| Dec 22 | Canteens, around the administration building |
| Dec 23 | Continuing Education College |

## Mosquito Control Comes First

Alongside the spraying program, the university also scheduled intensified mosquito control work in student dormitory areas and the new administrative center on December 8–9. According to the report, all doors and windows in the affected areas were to be closed after work hours on the 7th to support the operation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
