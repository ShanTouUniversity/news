STU Student Affairs Office is launching the Student Community X Plan’s Music Has Power series. Through relaxed musical experiences, the program aims to share ideas about beauty, stimulate students’ interest in music, introduce different musical forms, and strengthen music-appreciation skills.

The series will have three sessions. The first, “Learning to Sing,” will use video, explanations, and live demonstrations to introduce the features, shared elements, and vocal techniques of folk, bel canto, popular, and original-ecological singing. The second session will focus on classical music appreciation, while the third will focus on Romantic music appreciation. Representative composers and audiovisual materials will be used to compare the two traditions.

| Session | Content | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Learning to Sing: folk, bel canto, popular, and original-ecological singing | 3:00–5:00 p.m., December 14, 2012 |
| 2 | Classical music appreciation | To be announced through the campus information network |
| 3 | Romantic music appreciation | To be announced through the campus information network |

The sessions will be held in Science Report Hall Meeting Room 205. Interested students should register according to Student Affairs arrangements by 4:00 p.m. on December 12. If applications exceed the seating capacity, participants will be selected in registration order.
