The STU Academic Affairs Office has announced a temporary course adjustment. Because the instructor is on leave, News in-depth Reporting (class 54991), scheduled for Periods 1 and 2 in Room G306 on Tuesday of Week 12, December 4, 2012, will be suspended.

The make-up time has not yet been set and will be announced separately. Students enrolled in the course should inform one another and watch for follow-up information from the Academic Affairs Office or their college.
