The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at STU will host a media salon on December 4, 2012, under the theme “CEO and Senior Creative Director: Marketing, Entertainment and New Media Revealed.” David Ketchum, chief executive officer of Weize Public Relations, and P.T. Black, senior creative director for the Asia-Pacific region at The Media Group (TMG), will each give a one-hour presentation.

Ketchum has worked extensively in Asia-Pacific business expansion, brand building, and corporate and market communications. He has held positions at Calvin Klein, Burson-Marsteller, and Hill & Knowlton and serves as chair of the Asia Digital Marketing Association. Black is a consumer-culture specialist responsible for media licensing, brand development, and online programming in TMG’s Asia-Pacific operations, and has helped develop its creative department in Greater China.

Ketchum has published a book on new marketing strategies for Asia. Black’s views on changes in Chinese lifestyles and culture have been cited by Time, The New York Times, CNN, National Public Radio, and Fast Company. Faculty and students interested in media, branding, and the creative industries are welcome to attend.
