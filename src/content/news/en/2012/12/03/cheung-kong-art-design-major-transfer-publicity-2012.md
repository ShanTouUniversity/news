STU’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has issued a public notice on a proposed major transfer. In accordance with the university’s undergraduate credit-system student-status regulations and the school’s implementation plan for major transfers, the school reviewed the application, assessment result from the receiving major, and related materials submitted by a 2011 student in Art Design. The school proposes to approve the student’s transfer into the 2011 Industrial Design cohort.

The publicity period runs from December 3 through December 7, 2012, for five working days. Organizations or individuals with different opinions may submit comments in writing, in person, or by telephone to the school’s Academic Affairs Office during the publicity period, with the required identification.
