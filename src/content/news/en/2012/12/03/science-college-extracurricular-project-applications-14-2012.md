The Youth League Committee of STU Science College has opened applications for its 14th round of extracurricular-activity projects. Applications are invited from the college Students’ Union, Young Volunteers Association, Mingli newspaper, Radio Club, department Youth League branches, classes, and other student teams.

The program follows the college’s educational approach of “understanding principles, applying them diligently, broad knowledge, and specialized skills.” It encourages students to strengthen teamwork, communication, coordination, and leadership through self-management, self-education, and service activities. Proposals may address six themes: public service, sportsmanship, career development, humanities, leadership, and innovation and creativity. Formats are open.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Round | 14th Science College extracurricular-activity project application |
| Applicants | Student organizations, department Youth League branches, classes, and student teams |
| Themes | Public service, sportsmanship, career development, humanities, leadership, innovation and creativity |
| Format | Open |
| Application deadline | Noon, December 6, 2012 |
| Defense | Tentatively December 12, 2012 |
| Preliminary review | Science College Youth League Committee |

Applicant teams should download and complete the STU Science College Extracurricular-Activity Project Application Form and submit the electronic version as required. The Science College Youth League Committee will conduct a preliminary review under its project application and management measures, followed by defenses to determine approved projects. Selected activities will give students opportunities to broaden their horizons, develop skills, and serve the campus community.
