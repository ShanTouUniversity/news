STU General Office has launched the submission of 2012 work summaries and 2013 work plans. Colleges, functional departments, campus Party general branches, and directly affiliated Party branches are asked to review their annual work and plan the next stage of development based on their own responsibilities.

The 2012 summary should follow the annual work plan, describe basic circumstances, identify the start and end dates of major work or activities, summarize achievements and experience, and analyze existing problems and possible improvements. The 2013 plan should be based on the development goals and actual work of each unit or Party organization and should reflect the Implementation Outline for STU’s Comprehensive Reform Pilot in Independent University Administration, with practical priorities and clear focus.

| Document | Main contents | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2012 work summary | Basic information, dates of major work, achievements, experience, problems, and improvements | Prepared with reference to the annual work plan |
| 2013 work plan | Development goals, priorities, and arrangements | Prepared with reference to the comprehensive reform pilot outline |

Units must send their summaries and plans to the Secretariat Section of the General Office by the end of the workday on December 28, 2012. The submission covers teaching, research, student affairs, administration, social service, external exchange and cooperation, and Party, youth, and mass-organizational work, supporting a review of the university’s independent-administration reform pilot.
