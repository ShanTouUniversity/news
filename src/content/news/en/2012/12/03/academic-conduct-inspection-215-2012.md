It is reported that the Office for the Construction of University Culture (Sanfeng Office) of Shantou University issued Academic Conduct Inspection No. 215 on December 3, 2012, reporting checks conducted in week 11 of the fall semester 2012–2013.

## Inspection Results

| Time | Venue | Late <5 min | Late >5 min |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 08:00, November 26, 2012 | E-Building | 1 | 4 |
| 08:00, November 28, 2012 | E-Building | 2 | 11 |

The notice circulates the list of students who failed to observe university discipline (see OA attachment; names withheld for privacy) and asks colleges and residential colleges to provide guidance and continue strengthening academic conduct.

> ⚠️ The student list is available on the OA system.
