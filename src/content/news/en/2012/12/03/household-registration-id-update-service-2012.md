STU Security Office has announced an on-campus service for students, employees, and family members whose household registration has moved to the university but whose identity-card household-registration location has not yet been updated. The university estimates that about 654 people are affected. Preparations have also been completed for updating the registration information of students who entered the university in 2012.

The service will be available every Wednesday from 2:30 to 5:00 p.m. in December 2012 at the Security Office on the second floor of the campus post office. Applicants should prepare the required digital-photo quality receipt, photograph, and original identity card; the original card is for on-site verification only.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Service | Updating the household-registration location on identity cards |
| Eligible group | Students, employees, and family members whose registration moved to STU but has not been updated |
| Estimated number affected | About 654 |
| Service hours | 2:30–5:00 p.m. every Wednesday in December 2012 |
| Location | STU Security Office, second floor of the campus post office |
| Security Office phone | 86503436 |
| Household-registration office phone | 82756003 |

Applicants should bring the required materials and follow directions from on-site staff. The service arrangements also cover preparations for updating the household-registration information of new students.
