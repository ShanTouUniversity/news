The 7th issue of Zhicheng Life for 2012, the publication’s 36th issue overall, has reviewed recent activities at STU’s Zhicheng College in cultural development, public service, student growth, and residential-college exchange.

On November 24, the college held its “Mu Chunfeng” residential-college gala to celebrate its fourth anniversary. The program included songs, dances, sketches, sign-language and musical performances, as well as an interactive edition of the college’s traditional Zhicheng News Broadcast program. The final round of the “Zhicheng Style” student talent competition, held on November 4, featured 12 finalists; the top performers were to represent the college in the university’s related singing competition.

The college also organized several forms of developmental education. Chen Wong Sui, an honorary fellow of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, joined the first Zhicheng Salon on November 17 to discuss lessons from her social-service career. On November 29, Guo Shaotang, special adviser to the dean and chair professor at Hong Kong Baptist University’s Faculty of Social Sciences, visited the college and spoke with residents on education, scholarship, and life. Professor Ma Fengqi of STU’s Institute of Higher Education discussed degrees and personal development at a Mentor Face-to-Face session, while Associate Dean Chen Wenbin addressed how students can balance academic work and extracurricular activities.

Public service was another major focus of the issue. On November 10, Zhicheng students and pupils from the STU Affiliated Primary School traveled to the Niutianyang embankment for the “Beautiful China in Action” environmental activity. The program combined environmental-awareness outreach, cartoon creation, and litter collection. On November 18, the college’s public-service course and the Shantou Federation of Persons with Disabilities held an exchange event for children with cerebral palsy. About 60 children and their parents visited the campus and joined interactive activities.

On November 30, the Zhicheng College Youth League held its second Congress of Representatives. Of 113 representatives expected, 105 attended. The congress reviewed and approved the work report of the first committee and elected the second committee. The college Students’ Union also organized a sports meet on November 3 with men’s and women’s 100-meter and 800-meter races, shot put and high jump events, the men’s 1,500 meters, and other activities, followed by fun competitions.

The issue also covered residential-college exchanges. Zhicheng faculty visited Shanghai University and Fudan University on November 1 to study residential-college experience. On November 22, a delegation from Wenzhou University’s Buqing District visited Zhicheng College to learn about its educational approach and enrichment courses. The college also opened applications for its 2012–2013 Residential Student Activities Funding Plan, with submissions due December 8.
