The fifth National College Legal-English Competition was held at Beijing International Studies University from December 1 to 2. STU Law School sent 17 contestants, including five in the legal-English speech event and 12 in the legal-English paper event. The delegation earned awards in both events and received the Best Organization Award for the fifth time.

In the speech event, STU contestants won one first prize and three second prizes; one STU contestant also achieved the highest score in the competition. In the paper event, STU contestants won one first prize, six second prizes, two third prizes, and three merit awards.

| Event | STU participants | Results |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Legal-English speech | 5 | One first prize and three second prizes; one contestant achieved the highest overall score |
| Legal-English papers | 12 | One first prize, six second prizes, two third prizes, and three merit awards |
| Best Organization Award | Law School | Fifth award received by STU Law School |

The competition provides a platform for university students to demonstrate professional English communication and legal research skills. The delegation’s preparation received guidance and support from Law School and English Language Center faculty.
