It is reported that the School of Science issued a notice on December 3, 2012, announcing the results of the 2012–2013 Undergraduate Research Program (SRP).

Following review by the expert panel, 43 projects including *Methods for Generating Random Numbers Using Function Iteration* were approved; details are in the OA attachment. The school will track progress and plans to hold a mid-term defense in April 2013 to decide on additional funding.

Project leaders are asked to promptly organize members to begin research. The school will hold a project meeting in mid-December, with at least one representative per team required to attend; the exact time and venue will be announced separately.

> ⚠️ The list of approved projects is available on the OA system.
