The STU Library has announced that its trial of the HeinOnline full-text law-journal database will continue through December 31, 2012. The database provides law journals and other legal research resources, and students and faculty can access the trial platform through the library’s database page.

The access page is <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=349>. After opening the database home page, users can select “Log into HeinOnline” and then enter the Law Journal Library collection to search law journals, or choose another collection as needed. The library’s original notice recommended using Internet Explorer and opening the link in a new window.

Students and faculty are invited to test the database’s journal-search and full-text features during the trial and send feedback to the Library Subject Services Department. Login credentials are not reproduced here; users should follow the library’s official access arrangements.
