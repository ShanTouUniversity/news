STU Student Affairs Office will hold the third session of its Mental Growth Workshop series on December 8 and 15, 2012. Under the theme “I Have My Own Scenery, and I Love Myself,” the workshop will use group interaction and experiential activities to help students reflect on their experiences, understand themselves, and focus on personal growth. Sixteen participants will be accepted.

The workshop will take place in Room 726 of Building 789 across four sessions. The morning session on December 8 is titled “Scenery Over the Years,” followed by “The Road I Have Traveled” in the afternoon. On December 15, the morning session will be “When Ideals Become Reality,” and the afternoon session will be “Beautiful Wishes.”

| Date | Time | Session | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| December 8 | 9:00–11:30 a.m. | Scenery Over the Years | Building 789, Room 726 |
| December 8 | 2:00–5:30 p.m. | The Road I Have Traveled | Building 789, Room 726 |
| December 15 | 9:00–11:30 a.m. | When Ideals Become Reality | Building 789, Room 726 |
| December 15 | 2:00–5:30 p.m. | Beautiful Wishes | Building 789, Room 726 |

Applicants should be willing to engage fully in personal growth, attend all sessions on time, avoid unjustified absences, late arrivals, or early departures, and remain in the program through its conclusion. Participants must keep group information confidential and must not disclose the privacy of other members or the group. Registration closes at 7:00 p.m. on December 7; the original Student Affairs notice governs the registration form and submission method.
