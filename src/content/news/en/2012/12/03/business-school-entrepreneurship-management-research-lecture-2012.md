The 10th 2012 STU Business School academic lecture will be held on December 7, 2012. Dr. Ye Minglang, a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University’s East Asia Institute, will discuss “Frontiers in Entrepreneurship-Management Research: Opportunities and Challenges.”

The lecture will examine entrepreneurship-management research at three levels. At the individual level, it will ask how companies can retain entrepreneurial talent and sustain internal entrepreneurship. At the organizational level, it will explore entrepreneurial organizations oriented toward business models and how organizations create new business models. At the social level, it will address national entrepreneurship-environment indices, possible pitfalls in government entrepreneurship policies, and the development of high-growth companies.

Ye holds a doctorate from Tamkang University’s Graduate Institute of American Studies. He is a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University’s East Asia Institute and teaches at Chung Hua University and the School of Business at Soochow University. His teaching and research interests include entrepreneurship management, venture capital, private equity, business models, and strategic management.

The Business School welcomes students and faculty interested in entrepreneurship and business-management research.
