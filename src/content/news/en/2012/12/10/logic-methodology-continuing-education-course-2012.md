STU’s Department of Social Sciences will offer the continuing-education course *Logic Methodology* in December 2012. The course, taught by Liu Yuangen, is intended for registered participants and will meet from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on December 12, 19, and 26 in Teaching Building D Room 202.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Logic Methodology |
| Instructor | Liu Yuangen |
| Dates | December 12, 19, and 26, 2012 |
| Time | 3:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Teaching Building D Room 202 |
| Participants | Registered students |
| Offering department | Department of Social Sciences |

Registered participants can log in to the continuing-education course-selection system at <http://training.stu.edu.cn> with their campus-network accounts and passwords to review course information and attend according to the schedule.
