It is reported that Shantou University's classroom teaching quality evaluation for the fall 2012 semester was set to begin soon, with the Academic Affairs Office issuing preparatory arrangements to all teaching units on the main campus on December 10.

## Preparatory Tasks

According to the office's deployment, the preparation involved four main steps:

| No. | Task | Responsible Party |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Log into the credit-based system to verify current semester course schedule information (course names, instructors, student rosters, student numbers), and report any changes promptly to the Teaching Section of the Academic Affairs Office | Academic staff of each unit |
| 2 | Revise course-group assessment indicators based on the unit's courses | Each teaching unit |
| 3 | Submit course assessment indicators and complete course grouping in the credit-based system | Academic staff of each unit |
| 4 | Print the reviewed indicators and course groupings, obtain the signature of the unit head, and deliver them to the Evaluation Section of the Academic Affairs Office | Each teaching unit |

The deadline for submitting the printed materials is **December 18**. The office urged all teaching units to carefully check course data so that the assessment indicators matched actual course arrangements, laying a solid foundation for subsequent student evaluations of teaching and quality analysis.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
