STU Academic Affairs Office has opened course evaluations for internship courses offered in the 2012–2013 summer term. Students who participated in the relevant courses must complete the evaluation in the credit-based academic system from December 10 through 21, 2012.

After logging in, students should select “Course Evaluation” and then “View Courses Pending Evaluation,” choose the internship courses from the 2012–2013 summer term, and submit their evaluations. Students who miss the deadline must provide a written explanation to Academic Affairs; otherwise, they will not be able to view their internship-course grades.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluation period | December 10–21, 2012 |
| Courses | Internship courses offered in the 2012–2013 summer term |
| Participants | Students who took the relevant internship courses |
| System path | Credit-based system → Course Evaluation → View Courses Pending Evaluation |
| Responsible office | Academic Affairs Office |
