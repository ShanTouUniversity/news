The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education has launched a call for stories about outstanding teachers and educators. The activity seeks documentary features, academic essays, and professional reflections from teachers and educators who have received relevant national or provincial honors since 2009. The STU Publicity Department and Human Resources Office have circulated the call, asking relevant university units to submit paper and electronic materials internally by December 18, 2012.

### Eligible subjects and works

Eligible subjects include recipients since 2009 of honors such as National Model Teacher, National Advanced Worker in the Education System, National Excellent Teacher, National Excellent Educator, National Advanced Worker in Education, National Model for Teaching and Educating People, Outstanding Teacher of Southern Guangdong, Outstanding Educator of Southern Guangdong, and national or provincial teaching-master titles.

Submissions may document an outstanding teacher’s teaching and educational work or take the form of the teacher’s own academic essay, professional reflection, or discussion of teacher ethics. Works must be factual, readable or analytically substantive, no longer than 4,000 Chinese characters, unpublished in domestic publications, and free of plagiarism or fabrication.

| Item | Information |

| Host | Guangdong Provincial Department of Education |

| Formats | Biographical feature, professional reflection, academic essay, or teacher-ethics essay |
| Length | No more than 4,000 Chinese characters |
| STU internal deadline | December 18, 2012 |
| Provincial submission deadline | December 24, 2012 |

### Submission arrangements

Relevant STU units should organize the writing, collection, review, and recommendation of works in accordance with the provincial requirements and submit the materials to the Publicity Department. After internal review, universities must mail paper works to Guangdong Education Magazine by December 24 and upload the works through the “Southern Guangdong Teachers’ Spirit” section of the Nanfang Education website. Envelopes containing paper submissions should be marked “Southern Guangdong Teachers’ Spirit.”

Guangdong Education Magazine plans to select about 120 works from submissions across the province, publish them in a collection for education departments to study, and recommend selected works to *Shidao* magazine. The activity will also recognize organizations through organizational and outstanding-organizational awards.
