“Culture Dialogue 10: About Design Collecting” will be held from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on December 11, 2012, in the STU New Library Lecture Hall. Wang Shouzhi and Professor Hang Jian will discuss design collecting in a dialogue hosted by the Changjiang School of Art and Design.

Hang Jian holds a PhD in literature and is a professor and museum director at the China Academy of Art. He is also director of the China International Design Museum, a doctoral supervisor, and an adviser and professor at STU’s Changjiang School of Art and Design. He previously served as editor in chief of *Zhuangshi* magazine and as director and deputy dean of the Department of Art History and Theory at the Academy of Arts and Design, Tsinghua University. He has also curated international design and art exhibitions.

Hang Jian’s research and publications cover design history, aesthetics of crafts, folk arts and crafts, visual art, and modern design. His representative books include *The Benevolence of Design*, *The Way of Design*, *The Thought of Craft*, *A History of Chinese Craft Aesthetics*, and *Visual Revolution: A History of the Art of Looking*. The dialogue will use design collecting as a starting point to consider design history and contemporary visual culture.
