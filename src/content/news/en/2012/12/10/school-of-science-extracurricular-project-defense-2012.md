The STU School of Science will hold its 14th extracurricular-project defense from 2:00 to 5:30 p.m. on December 12, 2012, in Meeting Room 205 of the Science Lecture Hall. Thirteen extracurricular activity proposals have passed the preliminary review by the School of Science Youth League Committee and will proceed to the defense.

The defense will follow the School of Science regulations for reviewing extracurricular-project applications. Each project will have two parts: a three-minute activity-plan presentation followed by two minutes of questions from the review panel.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 14th School of Science extracurricular-project defense |
| Time | 2:00–5:30 p.m., December 12, 2012 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall Meeting Room 205 |
| Proposals admitted | 13 |
| Defense format | Activity-plan presentation and panel questions |
| Time per project | Three-minute presentation and two minutes of questions |
| Organizer | School of Science Youth League Committee |

For proposals requiring a PowerPoint presentation, project leaders must send the presentation file to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn` by 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday, December 11. Project leaders should prepare their defense materials and participate according to the review regulations.
