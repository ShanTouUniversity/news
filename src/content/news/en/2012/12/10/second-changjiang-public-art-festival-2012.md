The second Changjiang Public Art Festival will open at noon on December 12, 2012, at the STU Auditorium. The festival is organized by the Changjiang School of Art and Design, with planning and operations by its public-art team and academic support from the Houshan Forum.

The festival will feature a documentation exhibition, joint exhibition, invitation exhibition, individual exhibitions, and an invitation exhibition of public-art-festival works. Venues will include the Changjiang School of Art and Design Art Museum, Central Walkway, public areas of campus buildings, the library, and spaces around the sports facilities.

The documentation exhibition will be held at the Changjiang School of Art and Design Art Museum. The joint exhibition will be presented at the Central Walkway, the glass rooms of Building CD, and the school’s front-hall gallery. The invitation exhibition will occupy the New Administration Building staircase and the glass room of Building 8. Individual and public-art works exhibitions will extend to the New Library, campus walkways, areas around the Administration Building, the gymnasium and track, the reservoir embankment, the swimming pool, and other public campus spaces.

The Public Art Festival originated at STU in January 2008 alongside the International Public Art Forum and Education Seminar. The second festival will use public art to encourage interaction between art and shared campus spaces while presenting the teaching and practice of the public-art program. Faculty and students are invited to participate.
