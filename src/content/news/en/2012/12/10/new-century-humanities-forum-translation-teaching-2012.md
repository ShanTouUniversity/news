The 116th New Century Humanities Forum will take place from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on December 12, 2012, in the Library Lecture Hall. Dr. Yang Yaocheng, an associate professor in the Department of Applied Foreign Languages at Jinwen University in Taiwan, will speak on “Practice and Techniques in Translation Teaching.”

The lecture abstract notes that translation spans a wide range of fields. Prospective translators need not only an interest in knowledge, language, and writing, but also the ability to seek answers independently and persist when they encounter difficulties. Strong English or Chinese skills alone are insufficient; translators must be proficient in both languages and able to convert between the source language and the target language.

Yang will also discuss the knowledge needed for translation. Translators should have solid knowledge and broad general awareness, understand the disciplinary or cultural background related to a text, and develop interdisciplinary understanding. When they encounter unfamiliar material, they should be able to identify what needs to be checked and find solutions through research.
