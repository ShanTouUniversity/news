It is reported that the practice activities of "Towards the Ocean," a service course offered by the College of Science of Shantou University, have come to a successful close. The course, themed on awareness campaigns for maritime territory and marine environmental protection, attracted wide public attention, with nearly 10 newspapers and online media outlets — including New Express Daily and Shantou Metropolis Daily — covering its activities. The course team will hold a campus-wide sharing session on December 12 to help teachers and students better understand and care about the ocean.

## Three Practice Activities from Mangroves to Islands

According to reports, three practice activities were organized during the course:

| Activity | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Su'ai Bay mangrove survey | A bicycle-based service survey of the current state of the Su'ai Bay mangrove ecosystem in Shantou |
| Public awareness campaign | Outreach and research among Shantou residents on mangrove protection, marine ecology, and maritime territory awareness |
| Nan'ao Island campaign | Joint outreach on maritime territory and marine environmental protection on Nan'ao Island, together with Yun'ao Middle School and the Guangdong Nanpeng Islands National Nature Reserve |

## Sharing Session Open to All

The sharing session is scheduled as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | December 12, 14:30–17:00 |
| Venue | Classroom E-101 (E-jie lecture building) |

The session is open to all interested faculty and students. The course team welcomes more participants to hear the stories from these activities and to join the discussion on marine protection.
