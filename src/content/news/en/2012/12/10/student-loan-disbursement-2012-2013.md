It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs at Shantou University has released details on the disbursement of national student loans for the 2012–2013 academic year.

Following bank approval, a total of 631 students received loans, comprising 524 recipients of the first batch of campus-based national student loans and 107 recipients of China Development Bank home-region credit student loans. The full disbursement lists are provided in the attachments. Funds already transferred to the university's finance account will first be applied against tuition and accommodation fees, with any remaining balance credited to each student's personal Bank of China account.

Recipients are advised to log in to the Student Affairs System and check "My Information — My Personal Information — Tuition and Fee Payment Status" for their payment records, and to settle any outstanding balance at the Finance Office promptly. For further inquiries, students may contact the Student Aid Management Center at 86504749.
