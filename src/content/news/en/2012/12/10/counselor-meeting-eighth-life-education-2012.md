STU Student Affairs Office will hold the university’s eighth counselor meeting of 2012 at 3:00 p.m. on Thursday, December 13, in ACC Meeting Room 3. The meeting will include a whole-person life-education session led by a former provost of Hong Kong Baptist University.

All counselors and relevant members of the student-affairs system should make the necessary arrangements and attend on time.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | Eighth STU counselor meeting of 2012 |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., December 13, 2012 |
| Venue | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Program | Whole-person life-education session |
| Participants | All counselors and relevant student-affairs personnel |
| Organizing office | Student Affairs Office |

The meeting will combine routine student-affairs communication with life-education guidance, supporting staff members’ work with students’ development and well-being.
