STU Library is offering trial access to Oxford University Press’s Oxford Journals Current Collection. The database provides peer-reviewed journals across six subject areas: medicine, life sciences, mathematics and physics, law, social sciences, and humanities.

Oxford University Press currently publishes 238 peer-reviewed journals. About two-thirds are published in partnership with scholarly societies and international organizations, and more than 80 percent are indexed by SCI, SSCI, or AHCI. Trial access will continue through January 20, 2013.

Faculty and students can access the resource through the library’s database details page and send trial feedback to the Subject Services Department. Feedback can be emailed to `lqxie@stu.edu.cn` or `zhzeng@stu.edu.cn`.
