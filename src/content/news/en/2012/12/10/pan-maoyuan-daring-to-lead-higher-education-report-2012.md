STU’s Institute of Higher Education has invited Professor Pan Maoyuan of Xiamen University to deliver a higher-education lecture titled “Daring to Lead.” The lecture is scheduled for 3:00–5:00 p.m. on Monday, December 10, 2012, in Science and Technology Building Room 205 and is open to the university community.

Pan Maoyuan is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Xiamen University and honorary director of its Institute of Higher Education. He is recognized as one of the founders of higher-education studies as an academic discipline in China. The lecture will draw on his longstanding work in higher-education research, academic development, and talent cultivation.

Pan previously served as director of Xiamen University’s Academic Affairs Office, vice president and adviser of the university, founding director of its Overseas Education College, and convener of the Education Discipline Review Group of the State Council Academic Degrees Committee. He currently serves as vice president of the Chinese Society of Higher Education and chair of the National Society for Higher Education Studies. He has received an honorary doctorate from the University of Hull and a first-prize National Award for Teaching Achievement.
