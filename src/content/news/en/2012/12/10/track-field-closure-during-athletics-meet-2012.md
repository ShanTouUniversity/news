The STU track and football fields will be closed during selected periods for the university’s 26th Athletics Meet. Except for physical-education classes, faculty and students should not enter the facilities while they are closed.

| Date | Closure |
| --- | --- |
| Wednesday, December 12, 2012 | 3:30–6:00 p.m. |
| Friday, December 14, 2012 | All day |
| Saturday, December 15, 2012 | All day |
| Sunday, December 16, 2012 | All day |

The closure is intended to support the safe and orderly operation of the athletics meet. The university community should adjust facility-use plans in advance and follow on-site management instructions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office and Physical Education Department)
