The STU Admissions Office is recruiting 10 student publicity assistants from among current undergraduates and graduate students. The positions will offer practical experience in admissions consultation, campus reception, and publicity-content production.

### Qualifications

Applicants should have good academic and personal records, understand and identify with STU’s educational philosophy and distinctive features, and be willing to devote time to admissions publicity. Strong computer and internet skills, fast typing, written and oral communication skills, and patience and responsibility are also expected. Students who have taken part in admissions publicity during winter or summer vacations or visited their former secondary schools for outreach will receive preference under otherwise equal conditions.

### Duties

- Introduce STU and its admissions information and answer questions through platforms including Sina Weibo, Baidu Tieba, Baidu Zhidao, Tencent Gaokao, and Sina Gaokao;
- Assist with receiving primary and secondary school students and teachers visiting STU;
- Assist with secondary-school visits, admissions-promotion video production, and admissions-material design competitions.
