The final of STU’s 20th Top Ten Singers competition will take place on December 15, 2012. To give more students an opportunity to participate, the Students’ Union has launched a Most Popular Performer selection. The performer with the highest combined online and on-site vote total will receive the award, which will be presented during the final.

### Voting arrangements

Online voting runs from December 10 through 1:00 p.m. on December 14. Participants can follow the official STU Students’ Union Weibo account, `@汕头大学学生会`, and repost the union’s posts about their preferred performers. Online votes will be calculated by repost counts.

On-site voting will take place during the Top Ten Singers final on December 15. Audience members may detach the stub from the program and place it in the ballot box for their preferred performer.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Award | Most Popular Performer |
| Online voting | December 10, 2012 to 1:00 p.m., December 14 |
| Online channel | Official STU Students’ Union Weibo account, `@汕头大学学生会` |
| On-site voting | During the Top Ten Singers final on December 15 |
| Selection basis | Combined online and on-site vote totals |
| Award presentation | During the final |
| Organizer | STU Students’ Union |

Audience members who take part in online voting will have a chance to receive VIP tickets to the final: two lucky participants will be selected at random from the reposts for each performer, for a total of 20 participants. Some on-site voters will also be selected at random to receive gifts; the number will be determined by the event organizers.

Further details are available through the Students’ Union homepage and official Weibo account. Participants should follow the published voting procedures.
