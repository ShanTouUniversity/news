To advance its vision of progressive undergraduate education and promote the exchange of educational ideas, STU Academic Affairs Office and the Institute of Higher Education have invited Professor Wu Daguang, vice president of Xiamen University and a doctoral supervisor, to deliver a lecture titled “University Change and Talent Development.”

The lecture will be held from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday, December 11, 2012, in Science Building Meeting Room 205. Faculty and students across the university are welcome.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | University Change and Talent Development |
| Speaker | Professor Wu Daguang |
| Time | 3:00–5:00 p.m., December 11, 2012 |
| Venue | Science Building Meeting Room 205 |
| Speaker’s position | Vice president of Xiamen University; doctoral supervisor |
| Research areas | Higher-education theory, higher-education management, and comparative higher education |
| Organizers | Academic Affairs Office and Institute of Higher Education |
| Audience | The STU community |

Professor Wu also serves on the Ministry of Education’s Expert Committee on Discipline Development and Program Setting and as a council member of national higher-education and education research associations. He has published 11 books and more than 80 papers and has received multiple education-related awards. The lecture will address university change, talent development, and related higher-education issues.
