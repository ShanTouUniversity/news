STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has issued bulletin 216 on an academic-conduct check. During the 12th week of the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year, the conduct-check team inspected E Building and recorded class tardiness on December 3 and 4.

| Check date and time | Location | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2:00 p.m., Monday, December 3, 2012 | E Building | 7 students arrived more than five minutes late |
| 8:00 a.m., Tuesday, December 4, 2012 | E Building | 6 students arrived within five minutes; 7 arrived more than five minutes late |

The bulletin asks the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide educational guidance to the students involved and continue strengthening compliance with university rules and academic conduct. The detailed student list is available in the original OA attachment and is not reproduced here.
