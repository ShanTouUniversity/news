STU Party and Administrative Office announced the 2013 winter-break and spring-semester arrangements, covering vacation and return dates for students and faculty as well as service requirements for administrative, teaching-support, and logistics personnel.

### Student and faculty schedule

| Group | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Students | Winter break from January 20 to February 23, 2013 |
| Student registration | February 24, 2013 |
| Spring-semester classes | February 25, 2013 |
| Faculty | Break begins January 23; return February 21; classes resume February 25 |

Students staying on campus during the break must apply through the student-residential-area counselor’s office and follow arrangements by that office and the Student Community Management Service Center. Schools, residential colleges, and relevant departments should also provide safety education for faculty and students returning home.

### Administrative services and duty coverage

Administrative and service-support units must continue providing regular administrative services during the break. Units may arrange staff rotation from January 23 through February 20 based on operational needs. Staff on rotation must remain reachable and report as needed. Working hours during this period will be 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 3:00–5:00 p.m.; normal hours resume on February 21.

During the Spring Festival, February 9–15, the Party and Administrative Office, Resource Management Office, Student Affairs Office, schools, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and the D, E, and G teaching buildings must arrange duty coverage.

Units must submit their winter and summer duty schedule forms for administrative, teaching-support, and logistics staff to the Party and Administrative Office by the end of the workday on January 16, 2013.
