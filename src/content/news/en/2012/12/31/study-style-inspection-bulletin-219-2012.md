STU’s Three-Style Development Office issued Bulletin No. 219 on study-conduct inspections during Week 15 of the 2012–2013 fall semester. The inspection team conducted two checks in the E Building teaching facilities, on December 25 and December 27.

| Inspection | Lateness of five minutes or less | Lateness exceeding five minutes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| December 25, 2:00 p.m. | 7 students | 7 students |
| December 27, 8:00 a.m. | 2 students | 4 students |
| Total | 9 instances | 11 instances |

The bulletin asked schools and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to the students concerned and continue strengthening classroom discipline and study-style development. The names of students identified in the inspection are contained in the original attachment and are not reproduced here.
