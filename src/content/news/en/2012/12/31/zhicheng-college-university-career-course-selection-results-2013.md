Zhicheng College announced the selected students for *University Accumulation and Workplace Development* in spring 2013. By the application deadline, the college had received 42 application forms and, in accordance with the previously published selection requirements, selected 42 students for the course.

The course is designed to help students understand employer expectations, identify key factors in career choices, and develop professional abilities and workplace values. The selected-student list contains names, majors, and cohorts. This article reports only the number selected and does not reproduce the list.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | University Accumulation and Workplace Development |
| Semester | Spring 2013 |
| Applications received | 42 |
| Students selected | 42 |
| Offering unit | Zhicheng College |

The earlier course announcement stated a planned capacity of 50 students; this follow-up notice confirms that 42 students were ultimately selected.
