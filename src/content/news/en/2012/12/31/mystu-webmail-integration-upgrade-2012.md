STU Network and Information Center scheduled an upgrade of the MYSTU system for January 4, 2013, from 12:30 to 6:00 a.m. During the maintenance window, the MYSTU portal and Webmail system would be unavailable to all campus users.

After the upgrade, the `http://webmail.stu.edu.cn` login page would be integrated into the MYSTU portal. Users would be able to sign in to MYSTU with their campus accounts and click the “Email” icon to send and receive messages.

### Main changes after the upgrade

- Webmail would use the unified MYSTU interface;
- SSO single sign-on and HTTPS encrypted transmission would be supported;
- Mobile-device access would be improved;
- Messages sent through client SMTP would remain on the server;
- Backend mail data would use database storage for easier retention and retrieval;
- Users could query logs of deleted mail; and
- The former “Network Storage” function would be discontinued.

The center advised frequent Webmail users to back up important messages locally through Outlook or Foxmail before the upgrade. Address books and folders from the former Webmail system would be synchronized to MYSTU, although some messages from January 3 to 4 might temporarily be unavailable. The former system at `http://oldmail.stu.edu.cn` would retain messages dated before January 4 and “Network Storage” files for three months.

A subsequent notice reported that after the integration upgrade on January 4, 2013, Foxmail, Outlook, and other clients could send and receive messages normally, while Webmail remained unavailable. The center planned to take the related services offline from midnight until 6:00 a.m.; messages from January 4 could be viewed through the former mail system, which supported viewing only and could not send mail.
