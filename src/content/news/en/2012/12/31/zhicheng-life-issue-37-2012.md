Zhicheng College released the eighth 2012 issue of *Zhicheng Life* (Issue 37 overall), reviewing the college’s December activities in team development, physical activities, public service, cultural exchange, and organizational development.

### Team and physical development

The college’s *Team Theory and Leadership Practice* course took 28 faculty members and students to Guangdong Baojia Industrial Co., Ltd. and Yangzheng Shiyuan Co., Ltd. in Jieyang. The group discussed team building, leadership practice, traditional culture in enterprise management, and career planning with company leaders. The college also launched the second Advanced Training Program, or ATP. After 63 residential students applied, fitness testing and comprehensive interviews led to the selection of 20 regular participants and four alternates.

At Shantou University’s 26th Track and Field Meet, Zhicheng College scored 106 points and placed fourth overall, while again receiving the Sportsmanship Award. The issue also describes the college’s formation, cheering squad, and event-support work; individual student-athlete information is not reproduced here.

### Cultural and public-service activities

On December 1, Zhicheng College hosted the *Spring Breeze: Yao Liang Guqin Concert*. Yao Liang performed pieces including *Flowing Water*, *Three Variations on the Plum Blossom*, and *Guangling*, and later exchanged musical ideas with members of the Deyi Qin Society and local guqin practitioners. The college’s public-service course, “Home Tutoring Service for Families of Children with Cerebral Palsy,” also held a semester-end reflection and sharing session on service experience and course design.

The issue covered the 12th “Mentor Face-to-Face” session, the third Zhicheng Salon, and the 14th Zhicheng Forum. Professor Zhuang Zhemin of the School of Engineering discussed career planning; Hong Kong National People’s Congress deputy and barrister Tam Wai-chu shared reflections on legal aspirations and service to society; and Wang Xuming, president of the Language and Culture Press and former Ministry of Education spokesperson, spoke on “As a Spokesperson, I Worked for Openness,” discussing media spokespersons, ethics education, and professional learning.

### Residential community and external exchange

The 2012–2013 Residential Student Activity Funding Plan completed its project defenses. Nineteen projects participated, with 11 approved: six fully funded, three partially funded, and two self-funded. The Zhicheng Student Union’s self-management committee and the School of Science Radio Club also offered free repairs for nearly 20 small electrical appliances belonging to residential students.

The issue additionally recorded summary activities by the Zhicheng Youth League, the peer-mentor committee, the Evergreen Service-Learning Association, the Zhicheng Student Union, and grade-level student organizations. Visitors included a delegation from Beijing Institute of Petrochemical Technology, an Xiamen University doctoral study group led by Professor Pan Maoyuan, and a scholar from Hong Kong Baptist University.
