Feng Yuebing, a teacher at the STU Arts Education Center, will give a solo concert at 8:00 p.m. on January 5, 2013, in the Science Lecture Hall. The program will include songs such as *I Love You, China*, *The Sea, My Hometown*, and *The Horizon*.

Because tickets for the formal concert are limited, Feng will also hold an open rehearsal in the Science Lecture Hall on the evening of January 3. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.

### Concert and ticket information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Concert | January 5, 2013, 8:00 p.m., Science Lecture Hall |
| Ticket distribution | December 31, 2:00–4:30 p.m. |
| Distribution venue | Arts Education Center, Room 309 |
| Distribution method | Limited tickets, available on a first-come, first-served basis |
| Open rehearsal | Evening of January 3, 2013, Science Lecture Hall |

Feng graduated from the Xinghai Conservatory of Music and joined the STU Arts Education Center in 2005. He teaches *Vocal Performance Art* and *Appreciation of Chinese and Foreign Musical Masterpieces*, and has long served as a university choir instructor and piano accompanist. He has received an Outstanding Instructor award at Guangdong’s university student arts exhibitions.
