The STU Student Affairs Office announced the selection results for student volunteers for the second “Walking with Masters” event. Recruitment opened to students across the university on December 12. After interviews, the organizers assessed English application, leadership, communication and coordination, service experience, and familiarity with the campus, ultimately selecting 32 students.

The first group training session is scheduled as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | January 5, 2013, 3:00–5:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Meeting Room 726 |
| Training requirement | Volunteers should attend on time and submit a printed application form with a photograph and their signature |
| Program website | <http://www.prometheus.stu.edu.cn> |
| Inquiry email | `ppsupport@stu.edu.cn` |

Selected volunteers are expected to participate in the full event program, follow the organizers’ arrangements, and review the event information in advance. The notice states that the participation period is “February 16–21, 2012,” but this conflicts with the notice’s December 2012 date and the January 2013 training schedule. This article does not speculate about a correction; readers should confirm the event dates through official project information.

The attachment contains a student volunteer list and personal application form. Individual names, form fields, and other personal information are not reproduced here.
