STU Graduate School issued examination reminders for the 2013 master’s entrance examination. Graduate-work administrators and subject leaders at schools, departments, and institutes were asked to notify question setters to remain reachable at their designated contact numbers during the self-designed-subject examinations on January 5 and 6 so that they could answer examination-related questions.

The examination sessions are scheduled for 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 2:00–5:00 p.m. each day. Detailed arrangements for individual subjects are provided in the attachment from the Graduate School. Question setters whose contact details have changed should report the update to the Graduate School.

Graduate School examination contact: `82902424`.

The notice title identifies the 2013 master’s entrance examination, but the body displays the year before January 5–6 as “2012,” which conflicts with the examination year and the December publication date. This article therefore uses the clearly confirmed “January 5–6” dates without silently adding a year; readers should follow the Graduate School’s formal examination documents.
