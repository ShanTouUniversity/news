STU announced decisions from the 15th university leadership meeting held on December 14, 2012, adjusting policies on spouse living allowances for newly recruited faculty and research-startup funding for in-service faculty who earned doctorates.

### Broader eligibility for spouse living allowances

The university revised its interim rules for recruiting teaching and research personnel, extending spouse living-allowance eligibility to all newly recruited faculty, including international faculty. Applicants must meet all three conditions:

- hold a doctoral degree before joining the university;
- before recruitment, mainland Chinese faculty must hold a senior associate-professor-level or higher professional qualification, while international and Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan faculty must be appointed as associate or full professors; and
- the spouse must live with the faculty member at the university and have no job or a monthly salary below RMB 1,000.

The allowance begins in the month following approval. Eligible international faculty already in service may also apply; after approval, payments begin in January 2013.

### Transitional arrangements for research-startup funding

The university repealed the provision that provided research-startup funding to in-service faculty who earned doctorates. Projects that had already received university research-startup funding may continue until completion.

Full-time faculty who earned a doctorate while in service between the issuance of the 2009 policy and December 31, 2012, and who had never served as principal investigator on a funded research project, could apply for research-startup funding by January 19, 2013. Funding would be provided subject to university approval. Applications of this type would no longer be accepted from January 20, 2013.
