STU School of Science will hold a faculty meeting from 3:00 to 5:30 p.m. on January 5, 2013, in the New Library Lecture Hall. Faculty and staff from the School of Science, the Institute of Marine Biology, and the Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials will attend.

The agenda includes remarks from school leaders and a presentation by Ma Fengqi, director of the Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment, on the center and teaching methods. Ma holds a doctorate from East China Normal University.
