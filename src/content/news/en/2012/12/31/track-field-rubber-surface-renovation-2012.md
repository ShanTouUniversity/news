STU began a partial renovation project for the rubber surface of its track-and-field stadium. Shenzhen Aoshunda Industrial Co., Ltd. was commissioned to carry out the work, which had a winning contract value of RMB 477,900.

Construction was scheduled to start on December 31, 2012, and finish by February 14, 2013. Noise, vibration, and dust could occur during the work, and the university asked faculty, students, and pedestrians to plan their routes accordingly.

For safety reasons, faculty and students may not enter the construction site without authorization from the university’s relevant functional office and the contractor. People passing near the site should follow safety notices and avoid lingering in the construction area.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office)
