The STU Library published a user guide for the Web of Science database. The university had opened access to the SCI, SSCI, and A&HCI indexes, while Thomson Reuters had also provided access to Current Chemical Reactions (CCR), Index Chemicus (IC), and Medline at no charge.

### Databases and coverage periods

| Database | Coverage available in the notice |
| --- | --- |
| Science Citation Index Expanded (SCI) | 2003 to present |
| Social Sciences Citation Index (SSCI) | 2003 to present |
| Arts & Humanities Citation Index (A&HCI) | 2003 to present |
| Index Chemicus (IC) | 1993 to present |
| Current Chemical Reactions (CCR) | 1986 to present, plus INPI documents from 1840–1985 |
| Medline | 1950 to present |

SCI covers natural sciences and engineering; SSCI covers fields including politics, economics, law, education, psychology, geography, and history; and A&HCI covers philosophy, literature, linguistics, music, art, architecture, Asian studies, history, and archaeology. IC and CCR focus on organic compounds and chemical reactions, while Medline covers biomedical, clinical, pharmaceutical, and public-health topics.

### Access and training

- Web of Knowledge: <http://webofknowledge.com>
- STU Library database page: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=317>
- Product training: <http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/productraining/>
- Search tips: <http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/searchtips/WOKsearchtips/>
- Online lectures: <http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/2012wokonline/>

The library encouraged faculty and students to consult the training resources and use subject, citation, and chemical-structure search functions when locating research materials.
