It is reported that the "Science Salon" lecture series at Shantou University's College of Science will continue on the afternoon of January 3, 2013. The upcoming session features Dr. Chris Brown from Canada's Mount Allison University as the speaker, with a focus on quantitative proteomics techniques.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Using quantitative proteomics to understand algal and plant physiology |
| Time | January 3, 2013, 3:30–5:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Meeting Room 504, Science and Technology Building |
| Speaker | Dr. Chris Brown (Mount Allison University, Canada) |

According to the introduction, the talk will explore applications of quantitative proteomics in algal and plant physiology research, offering faculty members and students in related fields an opportunity to learn about cutting-edge international research.

Mei Zhiping of the Marine Biology Institute serves as the contact person for this event. Interested faculty and students may reach out in advance for further information.
