In accordance with STU’s New Year holiday schedule, the Department of Physical Education announced that the gym and indoor badminton courts would be closed from January 1 through 3.

Faculty and students should plan their exercise schedules accordingly. The Department of Physical Education will provide further information on the resumption of regular opening arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Department of Physical Education)
