Academic Affairs Office issued reminders for the second 2012 National Higher-Education Computer Proficiency Test. The test will be held in Room E101 in multiple sessions on January 5, 2013, using a fully computer-based format with papers delivered through the network.

| Session | Start time | Duration |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 001 | January 5, 8:00 a.m. | 105 minutes |
| 002 | January 5, 10:15 a.m. | 105 minutes |

### Computer-exam rules

- After the papers are opened, the examination clock is based on the time when the first student successfully logs in after the official start. Students more than 30 minutes late may not log in.
- Students should enter only their admission-card number and password. They must not click “Submit” during the examination; doing so ends the examination and requires them to leave. The system does not permit submission during the first 30 minutes.
- Students must close all running programs before submitting, or the system may not complete the submission.
- Students must bring their admission card, identity card, and student card. No other materials or items, including scratch paper, may be taken into the examination room.
