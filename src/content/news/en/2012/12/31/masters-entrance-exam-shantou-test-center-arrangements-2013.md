STU Graduate School announced the Shantou test-center arrangements for the 2013 master’s entrance examination. The examinations will take place on January 5 and 6, 2013, at the old campus of Shantou No. 1 Middle School, 64 Zhongshan Road, Shantou.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Examination dates | January 5–6, 2013 |
| Sessions | 8:30–11:30 a.m.; 2:00–5:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Old campus of Shantou No. 1 Middle School, 64 Zhongshan Road, Shantou |
| Admission card and seat information | Log in to the national graduate-admissions website to print the admission card from December 25, 2012, through January 7, 2013, and use the Shantou Education Information Network to check the venue, room, and seat |
| Venue familiarization deadline | Arrive at the test center by 5:00 p.m. on January 4, 2013 |

For registered main-campus candidates, the university will arrange a bus to the test center at 7:10 a.m. on January 5 and 6. The bus will depart from the area in front of the STU motto monument beside the national flag. Candidates who did not register for transportation must arrange their own travel. The passenger list contains personal information and is not reproduced here.

Shantou Education Information Network seat-query page: <http://www.stedu.net/smallclass.aspx?bclass=6&sclass=49>.
