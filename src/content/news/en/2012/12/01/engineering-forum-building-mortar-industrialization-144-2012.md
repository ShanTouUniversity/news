The 144th STU Engineering Research Academic Forum will be held on December 5, 2012. Professor Wang Peiming of the College of Materials Science and Engineering at Tongji University will deliver a presentation on “The Industrialization and Development of Building Mortar.”

Wang is director of the Institute of Cement-Based Materials at Tongji University and has conducted extensive research on cement-based materials, dry-mixed mortar, and building insulation materials. He has led projects under national basic-research, science-and-technology support, and National Natural Science Foundation programs, published more than 300 academic papers, and supervised nearly 100 graduate students and postdoctoral researchers.

Wang also holds leadership roles in relevant branches of the Chinese Ceramic Society, serves as president of the Shanghai Building Materials Industry Association, and is editor-in-chief of the Journal of Building Materials. Faculty members, graduate students, undergraduates, and other interested members of the university are welcome to attend.
