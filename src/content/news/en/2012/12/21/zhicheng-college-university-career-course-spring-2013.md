Zhicheng College will continue offering the general-education course *University Accumulation and Workplace Development* in spring 2013. The course is designed to help students understand employer expectations, identify key factors in career choices, and develop professional abilities and workplace values. It is included in the university’s general-education curriculum, and students who complete the coursework, practice component, and assessment can earn one credit.

The course is open on a voluntary basis to students of Zhicheng College and selected other residential areas. Fifty students will be admitted: 40 from Zhicheng College and 10 from other residential areas. If applications exceed capacity, upper-year students will receive priority under otherwise equal conditions, and remaining places will be assigned by drawing lots.

The course will be taught by Zhicheng College mentor Bei Xu. Bei is also deputy general manager of Jinfalai Women’s, Infants’ and Children’s Products Co., Ltd., a member of the China Human Resources 100 group, and a PTT professional trainer. Bei has more than 10 years of experience in corporate executive and human-resources management.
