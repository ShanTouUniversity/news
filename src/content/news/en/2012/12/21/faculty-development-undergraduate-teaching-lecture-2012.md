STU Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment and Academic Affairs Office will hold a faculty-development lecture from 4:00 to 5:30 p.m. on Tuesday, December 25, in the Science and Technology Building Room 205 Conference Room. The topic is “Teaching and Learning in University Undergraduate Education.”

The speaker will be David C. Y. Kwan (关超然), emeritus professor in the Department of Medicine, Faculty of Health Sciences, at McMaster University and a leader at the Center for Faculty Development and Education at China Medical University in Taiwan. He has worked extensively in medical education, problem-based learning (PBL), and faculty development.

The lecture is intended for deans and department chairs responsible for teaching at the university’s academic units, heads of teaching departments and centers, and faculty members across the university. It will address education reform, faculty development, and undergraduate teaching practice.
