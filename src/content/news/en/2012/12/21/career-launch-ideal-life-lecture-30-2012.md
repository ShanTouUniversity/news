The 30th STU Career Launch Lecture will be held from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on December 23, 2012, in E Staircase Lecture Room 203. The lecture, “Set Sail for an Ideal Life,” is open to faculty and students across the university.

Xiao Yingdan will deliver the lecture. Xiao is a senior interviewer and training specialist with an MBA from Hong Kong Baptist University and a nationally certified corporate-trainer qualification. Xiao has served as a guest lecturer for Fortune Global 500 companies, a guest interviewer for several state-owned enterprises, and a senior interviewer for ChinaHR.com, and has worked in consulting-based management, etiquette, and corporate professional-competence training.
