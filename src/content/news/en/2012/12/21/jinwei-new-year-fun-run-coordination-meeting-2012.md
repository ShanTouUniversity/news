To implement the Shantou Municipal Sports Bureau’s arrangements for the Jinwei New Year mass fitness run and support STU’s ongoing sixth National Sunshine Sports Winter Long-Run activity, STU plans to organize 300 students from its main-campus colleges and residential colleges to take part.

The Physical Education Department will hold a coordination meeting at 4:00 p.m. on Monday, December 24, in the multimedia classroom of the STU Gymnasium. The meeting will coordinate the event’s organization. The Student Affairs Office and each college and residential college counselor’s office should send one faculty representative; the university and each college or residential college student union should send one relevant student representative.
