It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on December 21, 2012, regarding interviews for the second cohort of student volunteers for the “Walking with Masters” program.

Since the recruitment notice on December 12, 87 students have applied. Group interviews are scheduled for the afternoon of December 26 in Room 306, Building E.

## Interview Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 14:45–17:30, December 26, 2012 |
| Venue | Room 306, Building E |
| Format | Group interviews, about 15 minutes per group, including English Q&A and leaderless group discussion |
| Criteria | English proficiency, leadership, communication and coordination, volunteer service experience, familiarity with the campus, among others |
| Requirement | Arrive 10 minutes early according to the grouping in the attachment |
| Alternative arrangements | Those unavailable at the scheduled time should email ppsupport@stu.edu.cn or call 82903299 before 17:00 on December 25 to arrange another time within the week; contact: Mr./Ms. Li |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

The grouping list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here for privacy.
