It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education issued a notice (Yue Jiao Gao Han [2012] No. 204) on December 19, 2012, announcing the results of the 2012 undergraduate-level project approvals under the province's Higher Education Teaching Quality and Reform Project. Shantou University secured 31 approved projects across seven categories, including quality open video courses, quality resource-sharing courses, experimental teaching demonstration centers, provincial teaching reform projects, comprehensive pilot programs for majors, practical teaching bases for students, and innovative talent-cultivation pilot zones.

In addition, the university received 100 recommended slots for provincial "College Students' Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program" projects. For 2012 as a whole, the university was granted a total of 5.32 million yuan in funding, including one million yuan from the National Engineering Education Center.

The approved projects and funding by category are as follows:

| Project Category | Number Approved | Funding |
| ---------------- | --------------- | ------- |
| Innovation & Entrepreneurship Training Program | 100 recommended slots | 1 million yuan |
| National Engineering Education Center | 1 project | 1 million yuan |
| Experimental Teaching Demonstration Centers | 2 projects | 1.5 million yuan |
| Practical Teaching Bases for Students | 5 projects | 750,000 yuan |
| Comprehensive Pilot Programs for Majors | 2 projects | 500,000 yuan |
| Provincial Teaching Reform Projects | 15 projects | 270,000 yuan |
| Quality Resource-Sharing Courses | 4 projects | 200,000 yuan |
| Quality Open Video Courses | 1 project | 100,000 yuan |
| Innovative Talent-Cultivation Pilot Zones | 2 projects | To be determined |

By discipline, the Medical College claimed multiple seats among the practical teaching bases and teaching reform projects, with its Clinical Skills Comprehensive Training Center selected as a student practical teaching base. The course Introduction to Civil Engineering Design was approved as a provincial quality open video course, while the Medical Imaging Teaching Demonstration Center and the Chemistry Experimental Teaching Center each received 750,000 yuan, among the largest single grants.

Under the provincial requirements, all approved projects must proceed strictly according to their declared plans to ensure that progress and outcomes meet the expected goals. The Academic Affairs Office said it will track and manage the projects and cooperate with the provincial department on inspections and acceptance reviews.
