STU Law School will hold a briefing for the 11th CIETAC Cup International Commercial Arbitration Debate from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Sunday, December 23, in Law School Room 213. The session will explain the competition’s preparation process and format and help students understand its requirements and training arrangements.

In the 10th CIETAC Cup International Commercial Arbitration Debate, the STU Law School team ranked seventh overall and reached the national top eight, marking a breakthrough for the team. The new competition may provide participants with opportunities to exchange and compete in locations including Hong Kong, Beijing, and Vienna, while developing their professional knowledge and practical skills.
