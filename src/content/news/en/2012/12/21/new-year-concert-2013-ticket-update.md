Tickets for STU New Year’s Concert 2013 have been distributed. The concert will begin at 7:30 p.m. on December 26, 2012, at the University Grand Hall. Ticket holders are asked to follow the ticket terms and take their seats 10 minutes before the performance.

Admission is limited to people aged 12 and above, and staff will enforce the age restriction. If seats remain unoccupied at 7:30 p.m. on the night of the concert, the organizer may arrange for people waiting outside without tickets to take available seats, subject to on-site conditions.
