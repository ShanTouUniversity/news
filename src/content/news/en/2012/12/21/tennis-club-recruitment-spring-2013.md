STU Physical Education Department is recruiting main-campus undergraduates for the Tennis Club in the spring 2013 semester. One of the department’s undergraduate teaching-reform pilot projects, the club provides tennis training and exchange opportunities for students and allows participants to earn physical-education course credit.

Spring-semester classes will meet during periods 8–9 on Mondays and Tuesdays. Applications are accepted from December 21 through December 27, 2012. Students may email `o_tyb@stu.edu.cn` or register in person at the tennis courts from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. on the following Monday through Wednesday.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | Main-campus undergraduates |
| Activities | Tennis training, exchange, and physical-education study |
| Class schedule | Periods 8–9 every Monday and Tuesday in spring 2013 |
| Application period | December 21–27, 2012 |
| Email registration | `o_tyb@stu.edu.cn` |
| In-person registration | Tennis courts, 4:00–6:00 p.m., the following Monday through Wednesday |
| Responsible unit | STU Physical Education Department |

Email applicants should provide their name, year, major, and student number as requested in the notice. Such personal information is used only for registration and is not reproduced here.
