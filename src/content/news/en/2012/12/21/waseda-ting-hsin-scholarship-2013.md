STU Student Affairs Office has forwarded an outline of Waseda University’s Ting Hsin Scholarship for undergraduates interested in pursuing a master’s degree in Japan. Established jointly by Waseda University and Ting Hsin International Group’s Master Kong Holding Co., Ltd., the scholarship supports outstanding Chinese students in a two-year master’s program at Waseda University.

The program is open to 2013 graduating undergraduates and previous graduates. Applicants must be Chinese nationals, including those from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan, reside in China, including Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan, when applying, be under 30 at enrollment, and may come from any major. About 80 students are planned for the September 2013 and April 2014 entry intakes in mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan.

### Funding and application

The scholarship covers admission fees, tuition, and various fees for the master’s program and provides a living allowance of JPY 1.5 million per year. Courses that a student independently takes outside the courses specified by the graduate school and that require additional tuition are excluded. The application fee is JPY 5,000, excluding overseas remittance charges. Applicants may apply in Japanese or English; specific language requirements depend on the graduate school.

Participating graduate schools and programs include Political Science, Economics, Law, Letters, Human Sciences, Social Sciences, Sport Sciences, International Culture and Communication Studies, Fundamental Science and Engineering, Creative Science and Engineering, Advanced Science and Engineering, Asia-Pacific Studies, Global Information and Telecommunication Studies, Japanese Applied Linguistics, Information, Production and Systems, Environment and Energy Engineering, the Business Administration program at Waseda Business School, and the Public Management program at the Graduate School of Political Science.
