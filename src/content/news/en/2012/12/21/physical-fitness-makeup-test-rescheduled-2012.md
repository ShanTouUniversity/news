Because it conflicts with the national College English Test Band 4 and Band 6, STU Physical Education Department has adjusted the physical-fitness makeup test for students from the main-campus colleges and residential colleges. The makeup test originally scheduled for the afternoon of Saturday, December 22, the 14th week of the semester, will move to the afternoon of Sunday, December 23. Other arrangements will follow accordingly.

| Item | Revised information |
| --- | --- |
| Test | Main-campus student physical-fitness makeup test |
| Original time | Afternoon of Saturday, December 22, 2012, Week 14 |
| New time | Afternoon of Sunday, December 23, 2012 |
| Reason | Conflict with the national College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 |
| Other arrangements | Follow the original arrangements on the revised schedule |
| Responsible unit | STU Physical Education Department |

Students involved in the makeup test should follow subsequent arrangements from the Physical Education Department and share the schedule change. Student lists and detailed arrangements in the attachment are not reproduced here.
