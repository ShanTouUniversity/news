The second Sangpu Language Forest discussion, organized by STU Graduate School and undertaken by the Graduate Student Union, was held on the evening of December 21, 2012. Its theme was “Behind My Breakup with My Ideal: Did Our Ideals Abandon Us, or Did We Abandon Them?” The event took place in the Graduate Student Union hall on the second floor of Building Y and was open to all graduate students.

The discussion examined the relationship between ideals and reality, focusing on how students understand ideals and respond to reality while making choices during personal growth. It also encouraged reflection on the relationship between personal goals and action. The activity was intended to enrich graduate student academic and cultural life and promote peer exchange.
