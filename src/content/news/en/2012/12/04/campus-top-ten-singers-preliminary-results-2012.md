The preliminary round of STU’s 20th Top Ten Singers Competition was held at the Great Hall on the evening of December 1, 2012. After the competition, 10 contestants advanced to the final.

Two special awards were also presented. Group Five received the Best Medley Collaboration Award, while one contestant received the Most Promising Newcomer Award. The organizers congratulated the finalists and award recipients and thanked faculty and students for supporting the event.

| Best Medley Collaboration Award | Group Five |

The organizers will release subsequent final-round arrangements through the STU Students’ Union social-media account and website. Individual student names are not published here.
