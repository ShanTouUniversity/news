STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will screen the classic British film noir The Third Man on December 5, 2012, as part of its Wednesday Movie program. Set in postwar Vienna, the film follows American writer Martin as he investigates the circumstances surrounding the supposed death of his friend Harry after arriving in the city.

Directed by Carol Reed, the film stars Joseph Cotten, Alida Valli, Orson Welles, and Trevor Howard. Its mystery centers on an elusive “third man,” with the investigation leading to surveillance, deaths, and questions of identity. The film won the Grand Prix at the Cannes Film Festival, the British Academy Award for Best Film, and the Academy Award for Best Black-and-White Cinematography.
