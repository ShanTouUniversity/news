The STU Sports Committee has announced that the order-of-events booklet for the university’s 26th Athletics Meet has been finalized and released electronically to colleges and residential colleges. The official booklet will govern the order of events and related organizational arrangements for the competition.

Participating units, athletes, and relevant staff should consult the booklet promptly and make preparations according to its event sequence and on-site requirements.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Event | STU 26th Athletics Meet |
| Document | Official order-of-events booklet |
| Status | Finalized and released electronically |
| Intended readers | Participating units, athletes, and relevant staff |
| Governing document | The official booklet determines the competition order |
| Issuing body | STU Sports Committee |

The detailed order of events and schedule are available in the original notice attachment.
