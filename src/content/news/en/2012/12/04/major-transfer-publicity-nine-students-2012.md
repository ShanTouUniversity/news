STU Academic Affairs Office has issued a public notice on proposed major transfers. After the relevant majors assessed applicants and submitted preliminary acceptance lists, the Academic Affairs Office reviewed the assessment results and related materials. The university is now publicizing proposed transfers for nine students.

The proposed receiving majors are Applied Chemistry, Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, Chinese Language and Literature, English, Economics, and Clinical Medicine. Two students are proposed for Applied Chemistry, two for English, and two for Economics; one student is proposed for each of the other three majors.

| Proposed receiving major | Proposed transfers |
| --- | ---: |
| Applied Chemistry | 2 |
| Mathematics and Applied Mathematics | 1 |
| Chinese Language and Literature | 1 |
| English | 2 |
| Economics | 2 |
| Clinical Medicine | 1 |
| Total | 9 |

The publicity period ends on December 12, 2012. Organizations or individuals with different opinions may submit comments in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Academic Affairs Office before the deadline and should provide the required identification.
