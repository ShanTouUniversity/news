It is reported that the Finance Office of Shantou University has begun trialing a "no-wait reimbursement" service across units on the main campus, in a bid to cut down the time staff and students spend waiting in line.

Under the new arrangement, staff and students who need to attend to other matters and do not wish to wait at the Finance Office can fill out a Reimbursement Receipt Transfer Form based on their receipts and hand both to Ms. Chen Li. Once the Finance Office has completed processing valid reimbursement requests, it will notify the individuals concerned to come back and sign off.

| Service Details | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Staff and students of units on the main campus |
| Required Materials | Reimbursement receipts + transfer form |
| Hand-in Point | Finance Office (Ms. Chen Li) |
| Follow-up | Sign-off at the Finance Office after processing |
