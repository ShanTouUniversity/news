The STU Law School team placed seventh overall and reached the national top eight at the 10th CIETAC Cup International Commercial Arbitration Moot Court Debate Competition, marking a breakthrough for the school. Organized by the China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission, this year’s competition brought together 26 teams from law schools across China, including Peking University, Tsinghua University, and Renmin University of China.

This was the second time the STU Law School team had participated in the CIETAC Cup. The team demonstrated fluent English communication and a confident competitive style. To help more students learn about international commercial arbitration moots, the team will hold a sharing session on December 15 and screen selected competition footage, introducing its preparation and competition experience.
