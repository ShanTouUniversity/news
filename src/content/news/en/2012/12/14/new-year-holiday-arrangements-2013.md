STU Party and Administration Office Document No. 7 of 2012 announces the holiday and adjusted workday schedule for New Year 2013. The holiday will run from January 1 through 3, 2013, for three days. Saturday, January 5, and Sunday, January 6, will be workdays. The Academic Affairs Office and Graduate School will arrange teaching-related work separately.

Before the holiday, each unit must inspect safety facilities in its office areas, close doors and windows, shut off water and electricity, and complete other safety preparations. The Security Office will maintain campus security and order during the holiday.

The Party and Administration Office, Resource Management Office, Student Affairs Office, all schools, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and the D, E, and G teaching buildings must arrange holiday duty coverage. By December 26, they must email the names and phone numbers of duty personnel to the Party and Administration Office.

Units should complete office safety inspections and duty preparations in advance. Faculty and students should arrange holiday matters according to the schedules issued by their units and the relevant course arrangements.
