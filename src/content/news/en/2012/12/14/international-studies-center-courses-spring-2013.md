STU’s Center for International Studies (CIS), under the College of Liberal Arts, has announced 13 general-education courses open to all students in spring 2013. Courses beginning with CIS2, including CIS2001 through CIS2052, belong to the “Philosophy and Social Sciences” category, while CIS3008 belongs to “Languages, Literature, and Arts.”

CIS noted that new courses use codes beginning with CIS: CIS2 courses and CIS3 courses correspond to the two general-education categories above. Existing courses beginning with RSP retain the category classifications in previously published notices.

The center also plans to hold a faculty-student Open House on the evening of Friday, December 21, to introduce the spring courses and teaching team. Faculty and students across STU are welcome. The specific time, venue, and program will be provided in a subsequent notice or poster.
