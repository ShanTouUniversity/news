STU’s Institute of Higher Education Studies and Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment will hold a seminar on faculty development and evaluation in higher education from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on Friday, December 14, 2012. The seminar will take place in Conference Room 330 of the New Administration Building and is open to faculty and students across the university.

The seminar will focus on faculty development and evaluation as topics in higher-education administration and research, providing an opportunity for campus discussion. The notice did not publish specific speakers or an agenda; further arrangements will be provided on site.
