The STU Youth Volunteers Association, together with the youth volunteers associations of the university’s schools and Zhicheng College’s Evergreen Service-Learning Association, will hold the “12.5 International Volunteer Day” Campus Service Street along the campus pedestrian street on December 16, 2012. The event is intended to serve faculty and students and promote the volunteer spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress.

The participating volunteer organizations will offer themed service booths:

| Organization | Services |
| --- | --- |
| College of Liberal Arts Youth Volunteers Association | Assistance for students traveling outside campus at locations including the main gate and Golden Triangle; introductory Cantonese and Chaoshan speech lessons and Mandarin correction; and practical help for students with needs |
| Medical School Youth Volunteers Association | Basic health services and first-aid instruction, including bleeding control, bandaging, and CPR demonstrations; autism-awareness videos, paper-folding activities, and knowledge quizzes |
| School of Journalism and Communication Youth Volunteers Association | A “Care Wall” for campus requests and mutual assistance, and collection of recyclable books and newspapers for public supplies, volunteer organizations, or charities |
| Business School Youth Volunteers Association | Introduction to preparing nourishing soups, sweet soups, herbal tea, and other everyday foods, with samples for faculty and students |
| School of Science Youth Volunteers Association | Free on-campus postcard delivery through the “Science Youth Volunteers Post Office” |
| Law School Youth Volunteers Association | Fan cleaning and improvement service; registration and arrangements will follow on-site requirements |
| School of Arts Youth Volunteers Association | Yarn-doll making, live portrait sketching, Chinese-knot making, calligraphy demonstrations, and a DIY hand-painted postcard sale, with proceeds donated to organizations serving people with disabilities |
| School of Engineering Youth Volunteers Association | Free repair of electric fans, rice cookers, water heaters, emergency lamps, speakers, and lights, together with a “Call of the Heart” activity encouraging volunteer service and healthy daily habits |
| Zhicheng College Evergreen Service-Learning Association | An interactive wall for mutual-assistance tasks, item exchange, and charitable collection, with a public-welfare collection project conducted jointly with the Journalism and Communication volunteers association |

The event will take place along the STU campus pedestrian street. Some services have capacity limits, eligibility requirements, or on-site registration procedures; faculty and students can consult the relevant booth. Participants in message-board and collection activities should follow on-site rules and avoid leaving unnecessary personal contact details in public areas.
