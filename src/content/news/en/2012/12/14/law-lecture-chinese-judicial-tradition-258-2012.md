The 258th lecture in the STU Law School series will be held from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Thursday, December 20, 2012, in the Science News Hall. Professor He Weifang of Peking University Law School will speak on “Reflecting on China’s Classical Judicial Tradition.” Faculty and students across the university are welcome.

He Weifang began teaching at China University of Political Science and Law in 1985, where he also led the editorial work of the quarterly *Comparative Law Review*. He moved to Peking University in 1995. He is currently a professor at Peking University Law School, editor-in-chief of *Chinese and Foreign Law Review*, and director of the Peking University Center for Judicial Studies. He also serves as vice president of the National Association for the History of Foreign Legal Systems and the Chinese Society of Comparative Law and holds honorary or adjunct professorships at several universities in China.

He has supervised doctoral students since 2000 and specializes in the history of foreign legal systems and the history of Western legal thought. The lecture will use China’s classical judicial tradition as its theme and connect it with his research in legal history.
