STU Labor Union will hold a New Year fun sports meet for faculty and staff at 2:45 p.m. on Wednesday, December 26, 2012, at the skating rink. Teams will represent departmental labor unions. A team with a large number of registrants may enter up to two teams in each event.

The participating teams will represent the College of Liberal Arts, School of Science, School of Engineering, the joint Law and Business team, the joint Arts and Physical Education team, the joint Research, Journalism and Graduate School team, the joint Social Sciences, Continuing Education, and affiliated schools team, the Library, the administrative team, and Resource Management. Teams must email their registration forms to the university Labor Union at `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn` by December 20.

### Events

| Event | Team size and outline |
| --- | --- |
| Pedal-board relay | 12 per team in four groups of three; groups complete a return relay on pedal boards, with time determining the result |
| Kangaroo melon relay | 10 per team; participants jump while holding a ball and complete the return relay in sequence |
| Figure-eight jump-rope relay | 12 per team, including 10 jumpers and two long-rope turners; participants run and jump through a figure-eight course before handing over |
| Basketball shooting relay | 10 per team; each participant completes the required rope jumps and must make a basket before returning to hand over |
| Ring-toss relay | 10 per team; each participant throws three times, with team rankings based on the combined target score |

All competitors, including team leaders, will receive a commemorative-gift coupon. Coupons will be awarded to the top six teams in each event. Participants should choose suitable events, wear flat shoes and loose clothing, follow referees’ directions, stay out of the competition area when not competing, and keep the venue clean.
