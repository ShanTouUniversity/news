STU People’s Armed Forces Department has announced that the 2012 military-training live-fire shooting assessment will take place on Wednesday afternoon, December 19. Live-fire shooting is an important part of the military-training course, and students who participated in the 2012 training are generally required to take part. The university will also select several “top shooters.”

The university will provide buses to take students to the shooting range. Participants must wear their military-training uniforms and gather by school at the university motto monument according to the schedule. They must follow instructors’ directions strictly after entering the range. Each school must arrange for counselors to lead the group and assist with organization so that the activity is conducted safely and orderly.

| School group | Gathering time | Meeting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School of Science and Zhicheng College | 12:30 p.m. | University motto monument |
| School of Journalism and Communication and School of Law | 1:00 p.m. | University motto monument |
| School of Arts and College of Liberal Arts | 2:00 p.m. | University motto monument |
| Business School and School of Engineering | 2:30 p.m. | University motto monument |

Groups will board buses in the scheduled order. Students whose group has not yet been called should wait at the meeting point. All participants must arrive at the designated point on time and follow on-site management and safety requirements throughout the activity.
