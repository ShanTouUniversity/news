The STU School of Science held the defense for its 14th extracurricular activity project round at the university’s Science News Hall on the afternoon of December 12, 2012. The judging panel reviewed 14 proposals. Following the school’s review, 10 projects were approved for support, with total funding of RMB 7,000.

The projects are intended to support the school’s project-based management of student activities and campus culture.
