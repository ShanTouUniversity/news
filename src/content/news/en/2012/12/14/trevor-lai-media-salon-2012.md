STU’s Media Salon will be held from 4:30 to 6:00 p.m. on December 18, 2012, in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center. Trevor Lai (赖嘉晟) will share his experience in marketing, advertising and media, brand-image planning, journalism, and program hosting.

Raised in Vancouver, Canada, Lai began publishing his original children’s Rhino comic-book series at the age of 17 and has since published more than 10 works. He studied at Simon Fraser University and later worked for Calling China/Identical Brand Consulting, an international brand-strategy and design firm, where he served as vice president of its China operation in Shanghai.

Lai is currently chief executive officer and chief creative director of Jiangsu Up Studios. He is also the host and chief reporter of the TMG program *Thoughtful China*. His work has received awards and been covered by media including *China Business Weekly* and *ELLE*. In July 2012, his picture book *Pig Pig Chases Love: Winter* was published in China.
