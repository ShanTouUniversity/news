Shantou Television’s News Center will hold an internship recruitment information session from 10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Saturday, December 15, 2012, in E204 of the STU Teaching Building. Organized in cooperation with the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, the session is open to students from the school and across the university who are interested in television news and program production internships.

Shantou Television serves as an educational internship base for the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication. Chen Xuanzhen, director of the station’s News Center, and deputy director Cai Linlin will introduce the internship programs and participation arrangements. The session will focus on two programs:

| Program | Internship activities |
| --- | --- |
| “Today’s View—Civil Affairs Archives” | Covering local Chaoshan culture, food, customs, tourism, and other feature news through field filming, on-site interviews, writing, and nonlinear editing at the News Center |
| “Double Bang” | Assisting with scriptwriting, props and costumes, temporary acting, and planning and organizing topics for short skits; the production team welcomes students with acting talent |

Internships are expected to take place on Saturdays, Sundays, and other times outside scheduled classes, minimizing disruption to students’ regular studies. Students can learn about the specific recruitment and internship arrangements at the session.
