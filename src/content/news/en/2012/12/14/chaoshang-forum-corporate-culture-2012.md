The 5th Chaoshan Business Forum will be held from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on Sunday, December 16, 2012, in Conference Room 3 of the STU Academic Exchange Center. Titled “Let Happiness Set Sail for Corporate Culture Development: A Corporate-Culture Sharing Session,” the event will feature Guan Suhua, general manager of China Mobile’s Shantou branch. Faculty, students, and members of the business community are welcome.

Using the management practices of China Mobile’s Shantou branch as a case, the forum will examine how the company has explored employee-value management and development through years of detailed and relationship-based management. It will discuss people-oriented corporate culture, flexible management, and new ideas, models, and methods for modern business management. The session will also connect corporate cases with corporate social responsibility, local economic transformation and upgrading, and the development of “Happy Shantou.”

The forum is also part of the continuing-education course *Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice II*, and students enrolled in the course are expected to attend.
