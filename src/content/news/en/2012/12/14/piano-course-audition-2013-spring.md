It is reported that the Art Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has announced auditions for its piano course for the spring semester of 2012–2013, taught by Dong Xuemin.

Students interested in taking the course are asked to check the OA attachment for the interview schedule, venue and specific requirements and attend the audition accordingly.

> ⚠️ The audition schedule and requirements are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center)
