The “M-Zone” STU 20th Campus Top Ten Singers final will be held at 7:30 p.m. on Saturday, December 15, 2012, in the university Auditorium. No admission ticket is required for the final. Faculty and students may attend, with general seating available on a first-come, first-served basis.

The middle seats in Rows 1 through 14 will be reserved for guests and judges, along with a limited number of ticketed student-guest seats. Other audience members may enter without tickets and must follow on-site directions for seating.

The organizers will also offer a VIP-ticket exchange through The Final Passport. Faculty and students who collect at least four different The Final Passport cards may exchange them for one VIP ticket from 10:00 to 11:00 a.m. on December 15 at Office 724 in Building 789. Thirty VIP tickets will be available, in order of arrival and while supplies last.
