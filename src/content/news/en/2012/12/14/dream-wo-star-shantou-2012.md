It is reported that the Youth League Committee, the Student Societies Federation and the Career Planning and Development Association issued a notice on December 14, 2012, to host the Shantou division of the inaugural Guangdong University Students “Dream Wo Star” Career Style Challenge.

Jointly organized by the Guangdong Youth League Committee, the Provincial Students’ Federation and Guangdong Unicom under the theme “Dream Together with Wo,” the competition offers training and inter-regional contests to enhance comprehensive abilities and employability.

## Event

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | All students (no grade or major restriction) |
| Time | 19:00–21:00, December 25, 2012 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Keynote | Be the Best Yourself, by Wang Mudi, host of Guangdong TV |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee hosting the Shantou division |

## Entry and Selection

| Stage | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Entry period | December 13–17 |
| How to enter | Create a Weibo personal show as an image long post including name, photo, contacts, university, grade and major, experiences and a dream statement, and publish with topic #Dream Wo Star Challenge - Shantou University Division# tagging @Guangdong Student Federation and two Unicom accounts; successful entry earns a lottery chance |
| Advancement | Top转发 in Shantou advances directly; another 7 are selected by a panel of Unicom staff and faculty based on content 50%, creativity 30% and popularity 20% |
| Final | 8 contestants present for 8 minutes (1-min self-introduction, 4-min themed presentation, 3-min Q&A) to select 3 finalists for the provincial final |

Awards and details are in the OA attachment.
