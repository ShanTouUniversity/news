The STU Library has announced that several LexisNexis international products will be unavailable from 5:00 a.m. to noon on Sunday, December 16, 2012, while the database provider conducts global system maintenance. Readers will be unable to log in to LexisNexis Academic during the maintenance window.

The affected products include Lexis.com, Lexis.com International, Nexis.com, LexisNexis Academic, Lexis Japan, Lexis Singapore, and Lexis HK. Service is expected to resume after noon on December 16.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Affected services | LexisNexis Academic and other LexisNexis international products |
| Downtime | 5:00 a.m.–noon, Sunday, December 16, 2012 |
| Expected restoration | After noon on December 16 |
| Reason | Global system maintenance and upgrade |
| Issuing unit | STU Library |

Readers should avoid using the affected databases during the maintenance window. The exact restoration time will depend on the database provider’s system status.
