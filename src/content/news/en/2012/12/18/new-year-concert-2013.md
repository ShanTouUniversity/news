It is reported that Shantou University's 2013 New Year's Concert will be held at 7:30 p.m. on December 26, 2012, at the University Grand Hall, with more than 100 performers welcoming the new year through music.

The concert will be hosted by Wang Shouzhi, Dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and conducted by Zhao Xintai of the Shantou Philharmonic Orchestra and Lin Weijie of the Center for Art Education, with the STU Choir also taking part. The program includes classics such as *Spring Festival Overture*, *The Moon Mirrored in Erquan*, and *Emperor Waltz*, building on the enthusiastic reception of last year's concert to further showcase the fusion of Chinese folk music and Western classical music.
