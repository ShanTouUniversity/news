The 14th Zhicheng Forum will be held at 3:00 p.m. on Saturday, December 22, 2012, in the STU Science Lecture Hall. Wang Xuming, former spokesperson for the Ministry of Education and current president of the Language and Culture Press, will speak on “For Public Disclosure, I Became a News Spokesperson: A Dialogue between STU Students and Wang Xuming.”

Wang previously worked as a reporter and head of the General Editorial Office at *China Education Daily* before joining the Ministry of Education in 1998. As a ministry spokesperson, he promoted innovations in press-conference formats and focused on communicating policy, ideas, and public information. He currently works in publishing and public communication and serves as chief planner and host of the television interview program *Questioning Education*.
