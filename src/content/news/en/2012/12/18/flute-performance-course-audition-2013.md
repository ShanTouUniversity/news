It is reported that the Art Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design announced an audition for its *Flute Performance* course for the spring 2013 semester, taught by Lin Weiliang.

The audition will be held from 16:00 to 18:00 on December 25, 2012 in Room 213, Art Education Center Building.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center)
