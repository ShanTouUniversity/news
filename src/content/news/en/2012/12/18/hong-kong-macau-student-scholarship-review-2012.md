STU International Cooperation Office and Student Affairs Office have launched the 2012 scholarship review for students from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao, and overseas Chinese communities, following the relevant notice forwarded by the Guangdong education authority from the Ministry of Education’s Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office. The university notice specifies currently enrolled Hong Kong and Macao undergraduates in Years 2, 3, and 4 as the eligible group.

The university’s quota for Hong Kong students is one first-prize, one second-prize, and three third-prize awards. The Macao quota is one first-prize, one second-prize, and two third-prize awards. The awards are worth RMB 5,000 per person for first prize, RMB 4,000 for second prize, and RMB 3,000 for third prize.

Students wishing to apply must submit the application form to the Student Aid Management Center in Administration Building Room 106 by 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 19. Student Affairs Office and International Cooperation Office will review the applications, prepare the recommended award levels, and submit the list to the Guangdong education authority.

| Student group | First prize | Second prize | Third prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hong Kong students | 1 award, RMB 5,000 per person | 1 award, RMB 4,000 per person | 3 awards, RMB 3,000 per person |
| Macao students | 1 award, RMB 5,000 per person | 1 award, RMB 4,000 per person | 2 awards, RMB 3,000 per person |

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible group | Enrolled Hong Kong and Macao undergraduates in Years 2–4 |
| Application deadline | 5:00 p.m., Wednesday, December 19, 2012 |
| Submission location | Student Aid Management Center, Administration Building Room 106 |
| Reviewing units | Student Affairs Office and International Cooperation Office |
| Non-cumulation rule | Recipients who also receive the 2011–2012 Outstanding Student Scholarship will receive the higher-value award only |

The scholarship application form contains student personal information, including names, student numbers, and dates of birth. The form and applicant details are not reproduced here.
