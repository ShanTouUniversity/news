STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has issued bulletin 217 on an academic-conduct check. During the 13th week of the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year, the conduct-check team inspected E Building and recorded class tardiness on the afternoons of December 13 and 14.

| Check date and time | Location | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2:00 p.m., Thursday, December 13, 2012 | E Building | 2 students arrived within five minutes late; 2 arrived more than five minutes late |
| 2:00 p.m., Friday, December 14, 2012 | E Building | 2 students arrived within five minutes late; 3 arrived more than five minutes late |

Across the two checks, four students were recorded as arriving within five minutes late and five as arriving more than five minutes late. The bulletin asks the relevant schools and residential colleges to provide educational guidance to the students involved and continue strengthening compliance with university rules and academic conduct.

The detailed student list was issued in the OA attachment. Student names, student numbers, and other personal information are not reproduced here.
