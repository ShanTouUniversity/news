The 13th 2012 Business School Academic Lecture at STU will be held from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. on Friday, December 21, in Academic Exchange Center Meeting Room 5. Professor Lu Jialiu of the Department of Finance and Investment at Sun Yat-sen University’s School of Business Administration, who is also a doctoral supervisor and director of the university’s Institute of Behavioral Finance and Financial Economics, will speak on “The Rise of Modern Finance and Behavioral Finance.” Professor Du Mian will chair the event.

The event also forms part of the continuing-education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice II.” Students enrolled in that course are expected to attend. The lecture is also open to STU faculty and students.

Lu’s teaching and research cover financial economics, corporate finance, monetary economics, and new Keynesian dynamic economics. He has published academic monographs including *The Structure and Change of Monetary Analysis*, *The Rise of Behavioral Finance*, and *Modern Financial Economics*, as well as more than 100 papers in relevant academic journals. He has attended international academic conferences in Sweden and Hong Kong and conducted visiting research at Iowa State University, Columbia University, and Yale School of Management.
