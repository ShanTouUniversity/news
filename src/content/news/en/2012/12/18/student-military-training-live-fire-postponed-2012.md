STU People’s Armed Forces Department has announced that the student military-training live-fire assessment originally scheduled for December 19, 2012, has been postponed. The assessment could not proceed as planned because of a temporary work adjustment by the training unit.

A new assessment date has not yet been set and will be announced by People’s Armed Forces Department. Schools and residential colleges should promptly inform participating students and follow the university’s subsequent notice.
