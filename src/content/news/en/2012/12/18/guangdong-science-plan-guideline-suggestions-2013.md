STU Research Office has forwarded a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology soliciting suggestions for the 2013 Guangdong provincial science and technology plan project guidelines. Relevant university units are invited to propose directions for future project guidelines, which will support the preparation and organization of provincial science plans.

Suggestions should align with the national and Guangdong 12th Five-Year science and technology development plans and address high-tech industries, upgrading traditional industries, strategic emerging industries such as LED, science and technology services, and social and livelihood needs. They should also emphasize key common technologies, frontier technologies, ecological development, and sustainability. Suggestions may be submitted from an industry or sector perspective, and proposals jointly submitted by multiple units will receive priority consideration.

### Submission process and deadlines

Suggestions must be submitted through the Guangdong Science and Technology Business Information Management System, followed by review by the submitting unit and the relevant competent department. Existing users may log in directly. Unregistered units must first register and then create a proposer account for the individual submitting the suggestion. Proposers should select the “Guideline Suggestions” menu and submit the completed proposal for unit review. Faculty members without a system account should contact Research Office.

| Stage | Deadline or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Internal submission of suggestions | December 23, 2012 |
| Online unit review | 5:00 p.m., December 24, 2012 |
| Online review by competent departments | 5:00 p.m., December 26, 2012 |
| Submission system | Guangdong Science and Technology Business Information Management System: `http://pro.gdstc.gov.cn` |
| Contacts | Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng |
| Telephone | 82902813 |

Units should carefully review the suggestions submitted by their members, withhold proposals that do not meet the requirements, and complete subsequent review and reporting procedures according to the schedule of the relevant authorities.
