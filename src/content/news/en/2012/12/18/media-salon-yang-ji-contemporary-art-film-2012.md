The STU Media Salon, organized by the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, will be held from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on December 20 in the Library Lecture Hall. Dr. Yang Ji, associate professor at Fudan University’s School of Journalism and research associate at the Fudan Information and Communication Research Center, will give a lecture titled “Postmodern Narrative Strategies and Contemporary Chinese Art Cinema: A Case Study of *The Piano in a Factory*.”

Yang holds a PhD in literature from Fudan University and is an associate professor at its School of Journalism. He also serves as deputy editor of *China Communication Review* and an editor at *Journalism University*. His academic interests include media and cultural studies, film and television aesthetics, media institutions, news discourse, and film and television narrative. Using *The Piano in a Factory* as a case study, the lecture will explore the relationship between postmodern narrative and contemporary Chinese art cinema.
