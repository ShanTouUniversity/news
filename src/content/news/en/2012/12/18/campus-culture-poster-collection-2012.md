To review and showcase campus culture activities held in 2012, STU Student Affairs Office plans to compile *Shantou University Campus Culture Review 2012: Poster Collection*. Schools and departments are invited to submit posters from campus culture activities they organized during the year, including sports and arts activities, lectures, and workshops.

Units should submit the materials through the channel designated by Student Affairs Office by December 31. Posters should be submitted electronically, preferably as original design files in AI, CDR, PSD, or similar formats. JPG images must have a resolution of at least 300 dpi (300 pixels per inch). Units should also complete the *2012 Activity Poster Description* form and upload it with the poster files in a folder named in the format “unit-activity name.”

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Materials requested | Posters from 2012 campus culture activities |
| Activity scope | Sports and arts activities, lectures, workshops, and similar events |
| Submission deadline | December 31 |
| Preferred formats | AI, CDR, PSD, and other original design formats |
| Image requirement | JPG resolution of at least 300 dpi |
| Additional material | *2012 Activity Poster Description* form |
| Folder naming | “Unit-activity name” |
| Responsible unit | STU Student Affairs Office |

The submission channel will be designated by Student Affairs Office. Questions may be directed to Mr. Li at 82903299 or `o_xsc@stu.edu.cn`.
