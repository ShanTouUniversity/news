STU University Union has published a notice stating that Professor Xu Zongling of the Business School meets the basic criteria for the 2012 National Women’s Workplace Pacesetter honor. Following review by the University Union Committee, STU proposes to recommend her as a candidate for the honor.

The recommendation follows the relevant documents issued by the All-China Women’s Federation General Office and the Guangdong Women’s Federation for the 2012 selection of outstanding groups and individuals in the national women’s workplace achievement program. Comments may be submitted to the University Union by December 20. Statements from units must bear an official seal, while individual statements must be signed with the submitter’s real name.
