STU Finance Office and Resource Management Office have announced the settlement arrangements for 2011 electricity allocations. Each unit’s electricity-allocation usage is listed in the notice attachment. Excess electricity charges will be deducted by Finance Office from funding items designated by the unit. Remaining allocations may be retained for 2012 electricity costs or used to supplement routine funds at half of the remaining amount.

Units that wish to designate a funding item or choose how to handle remaining allocations should notify Mr. Liang by email by December 21. If no designation is made, Finance Office will deduct the amount from the unit’s routine or public funds and, by default, retain any remaining allocation for 2012 electricity costs.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Settlement year | 2011 |
| Excess electricity charges | Deducted from a funding item designated by the unit |
| Options for remaining allocations | Retained for 2012 electricity costs, or used to supplement routine funds at half value |
| Designation deadline | December 21 |
| Contact email | `susaleung@stu.edu.cn` |
| Telephone | 86502477 |
| Issuing units | Finance Office and Resource Management Office |

Units should review the electricity-allocation usage information in the attachment, confirm their preferred treatment in time, and complete the settlement according to university financial procedures.
