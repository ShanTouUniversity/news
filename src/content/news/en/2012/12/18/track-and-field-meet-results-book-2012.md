STU’s 26th Track and Field Meet closed on December 16, 2012. The Physical Education Office has announced the release of an electronic results book to the university’s schools and residential colleges.

The results book records the competition results from this year’s meet. Relevant schools and residential colleges may consult the electronic attachment issued through the OA system. This article does not reproduce the tables containing individual participant information and event-by-event results.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Event | STU 26th Track and Field Meet |
| Closing date | December 16, 2012 |
| Released material | Electronic results book |
| Recipients | Schools and residential colleges |
| Issuing unit | STU Physical Education Office |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Office)
>
> ⚠️ The electronic results book contains individual participant information and event-by-event results; consult the OA system for the complete material.
