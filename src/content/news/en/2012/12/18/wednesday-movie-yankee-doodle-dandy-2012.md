STU’s Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication will screen the musical biographical film *Yankee Doodle Dandy* from 7:00 to 10:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 19, 2012, in the Library Lecture Hall. Peter Herford will moderate the evening.

The film follows the life of American Broadway entertainer George M. Cohan, tracing his rise from a child performer to a Broadway star and presenting the entertainment industry of the early 20th century through musical performances and stage scenes. Directed by Michael Curtiz and starring James Cagney, the 1942 film won several Academy Awards, including Best Actor.
