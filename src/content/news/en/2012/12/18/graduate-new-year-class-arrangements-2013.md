Following the New Year holiday schedule issued by STU Party and Administration Office, Graduate School has announced class exchanges for graduate courses on the main campus. During the 2013 New Year period, the following classes will be exchanged by date:

| Date | Teaching arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Wednesday, January 2, 2013 | Classes exchanged with those scheduled for Saturday, January 5 |
| Thursday, January 3, 2013 | Classes exchanged with those scheduled for Sunday, January 6 |

Graduate training units should promptly notify the relevant students and organize teaching according to the exchanged dates. Graduate students should confirm exact class times with their training unit and course schedule.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Applies to | Graduate courses on the main campus |
| Exchange dates | January 2 with January 5; January 3 with January 6 |
| Issuing unit | STU Graduate School |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
