To help candidates in the second-half 2012 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 examinations test their listening equipment in advance, STU will broadcast an examination signal from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 19, in Buildings E, G, and D.

Candidates should bring their own headphones and go to their assigned examination location during this period to confirm that they can receive the university’s listening signal. The test frequency is FM 73.2.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Test time | 3:00–5:00 p.m., Wednesday, December 19, 2012 |
| Test locations | Assigned examination locations in Buildings E, G, and D |
| Frequency | FM 73.2 |
| Participants | Candidates for the second-half 2012 CET-4 and CET-6 examinations |
| Equipment | Personal headphones |
| Issuing unit | STU Academic Affairs Office |
