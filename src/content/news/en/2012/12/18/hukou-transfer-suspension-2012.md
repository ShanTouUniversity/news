It is reported that the Security Office issued a notice on December 18, 2012, forwarding a notice from the Tuopu Police Station that intra-city household registration transfer services in Shantou will be suspended for the year-end population census.

Faculty and students who urgently need to handle such transfers should visit the Tuopu Police Station household registration office before December 26.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Suspension period | December 26–31, 2012 |
| Service suspended | Intra-city household registration transfers in Shantou |
| Office | Tuopu Police Station, household registration office |
| Phone | 82756003 |
| Issued by | Security Office |
