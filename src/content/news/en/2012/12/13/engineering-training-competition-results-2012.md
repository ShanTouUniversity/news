It is reported that the Student Science and Technology Development Center of the College of Engineering announced on December 13, 2012, the results of the campus trial for the 3rd National Comprehensive Engineering Training Ability Competition of College Students. The trial was held on the afternoon of December 12 in the Student Activity Hall of the Library, with awards decided by live demonstrations and impartial judging.
