The STU job fair for the Class of 2013 will be held at 9:00 a.m. on December 14, 2012, along the campus pedestrian street. According to the Student Affairs Office, 70 employers had confirmed participation as of December 12. Graduating students are invited to attend, learn about available positions, and take part in recruitment activities.

Confirmed participants include Tsingtao Beer South China Marketing Co., Ltd., Suning, Shantou Ultrasonic Co., Ltd., Guangdong Huaxing Bank, Shantou Frontier Defense Detachment, Shenzhen Wanxiang Jincheng Garments Co., Ltd., and Guangdong Yashili Group Co., Ltd.
