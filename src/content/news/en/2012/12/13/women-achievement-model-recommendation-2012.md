STU Labor Union has opened its campus recommendation for the 2012 National Women’s Achievement Model award. The All-China Women’s Federation plans to recognize advanced groups and individuals in urban and rural women’s professional achievement during International Women’s Day in 2013. The Shantou Women’s Federation has allocated STU one recommendation slot for the National Women’s Achievement Model award.

University units may recommend outstanding women who meet the criteria. Candidates should have outstanding performance in their positions, strong professional skills and service records, and demonstrable influence as a role model in their unit or industry. They should also demonstrate professional ethics, a practical work style, and courteous service, and should generally have received a provincial or industry-level honor.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Award | National Women’s Achievement Model |
| STU recommendation quota | One |
| Recognition period | Planned for International Women’s Day 2013 |
| Eligible nominees | Outstanding women from main-campus units |
| Main-achievement statement | No more than 1,500 Chinese characters |
| STU deadline | By the end of work on Monday, December 17, 2012 |
| Required materials | Nomination list and individual recommendation form |
| Receiving office | STU Labor Union |
| Submission email | `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn` |

The main-achievement statement should be focused and concise. Units must send their nomination list and recommendation form to the Labor Union by the deadline for the university’s campus recommendation process.

> Source: STU OA Notice and related recommendation documents from the All-China Women’s Federation and Guangdong Women’s Federation (Labor Union)
