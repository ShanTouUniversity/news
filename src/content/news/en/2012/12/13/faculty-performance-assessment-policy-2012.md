STU has issued Document No. 158 of 2012 to publish the *Trial Measures for Performance Assessment of STU Faculty and Staff*. The measures were discussed and approved at the 14th university leadership meeting on November 20, 2012. They take effect upon publication and replace the *Interim Measures for Managing STU Faculty and Staff Annual Assessment* (Document No. 114 of 2009).

The measures establish annual academic-year assessments and appointment-term assessments for full-time employees. The annual assessment forms part of the appointment-term assessment and provides an important basis for appointments and position levels in the next term. Assessment records and results are placed in personnel files. The annual assessment period runs from August 1 through July 31 of the following year and is generally conducted during the summer semester. Employees who worked at the university for less than half of the assessment period generally do not participate in that year’s assessment.

### Organization and criteria

STU will establish a university assessment leading group to approve annual assessment results from each unit, with an office attached to Human Resources. Units may establish assessment groups independently or jointly. Assessment groups in academic schools must have at least five members, while those in other units must have at least three. The groups are responsible for developing unit-level rules and reviewing results.

The assessment covers full-time employees, including full-time faculty, administrative staff, professional support staff, and skilled service personnel. Based on the duties in each employment contract, the university evaluates integrity and conduct, ability, diligence, performance, and compliance. The emphasis varies by role:

| Group | Main focus |
| --- | --- |
| Faculty | Teaching, research, and service; teaching is emphasized, with support for teaching and innovative talent development |
| Junior and mid-level managers | Work attitude, ability, conduct, collaboration, and performance |
| Senior managers | Integrity, policy knowledge, leadership, attitude, conduct, collaboration, and performance |
| Professional support staff | Service to teaching and research, technical ability, and fulfillment of duties |
| Skilled service personnel | Service attitude, operational skills, workplace safety, quantity and quality of work, and task completion |
| Academic-unit leaders | Strategic planning and implementation, teaching quality, education reform, recruitment and employment, research and disciplinary development, appointments, and leadership |

### Results and procedure

Results are divided into five levels: Very Outstanding, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, and Unqualified. The first two correspond to excellent and qualified levels, Meets Expectations corresponds to qualified, Needs Improvement to basically qualified, and Unqualified to unqualified. The number of people rated Very Outstanding may not exceed 15% of the unit’s actual participants; the combined number rated Very Outstanding or Exceeds Expectations may not exceed 30%.

Faculty submit the relevant annual assessment form and annual work statement in the first week of the summer semester. Departments or equivalent bodies conduct preliminary evaluations in teaching, research, and service and discuss them with the employees concerned. School assessment groups review the results within the first four weeks. Administrative, support, and skilled-service staff are assessed according to their duties, work performance, and service quality. Academic-unit leaders and senior managers undergo evaluations based on their management responsibilities, followed by review through the university assessment leading group.

Units must complete evaluations, discussions, reporting, and notification within the first four weeks of the summer semester. The university assessment leading-group office will publicize the names recommended for the excellent level on the internal office-information network and publish the final list after the publicity period.

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment period | August 1 through July 31 of the following year |
| Implementation | Summer semester, generally completed within the first four weeks |
| Highest-level quota | Very Outstanding: no more than 15% of actual participants |
| Combined top-level quota | Very Outstanding plus Exceeds Expectations: no more than 30% |
| Uses of results | Appointment-term review, renewal, position appointments, and performance pay |
| Appeal deadline | Within five working days after notification of the result |

Results are primarily used for appointment-term assessments, renewal and position recommendations, and performance-pay decisions. For employees rated Needs Improvement, the university may adjust their positions or compensation as appropriate. For employees rated Unqualified, it may take measures including demotion or termination. Employees who object to a result reviewed by their unit or supervising university leader may file an internal appeal under STU Document No. 27 of 2012 or submit a complaint to the university assessment leading-group office.
