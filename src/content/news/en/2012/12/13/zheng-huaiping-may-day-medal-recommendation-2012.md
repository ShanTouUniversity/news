Following the Shantou Federation of Trade Unions’ notice on recommending candidates for the Shantou May 1 Labor Medal, the STU Labor Union Committee has preliminarily determined that Professor Zheng Huaiping of the School of Science meets the basic criteria and plans to recommend him as a candidate.

Zheng Huaiping holds a doctorate and postdoctoral training in marine biology and is an associate research fellow, university-appointed professor, and doctoral supervisor in the STU School of Science. He also serves as director of the Shantou Comprehensive Experimental Station of the National Shellfish Industry Technology System and director of the Guangdong University Engineering Technology Research Center for Subtropical Marine Shellfish and Algae Aquaculture. The recommendation materials describe his work in teaching, research, and public service, including research on marine shellfish breeding and aquaculture services for local enterprises.
