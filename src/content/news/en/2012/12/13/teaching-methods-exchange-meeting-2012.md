STU Academic Affairs Office will hold a teaching-methods exchange meeting on December 21 and 22, 2012, to promote the sharing of teaching experience and innovation in classroom methods. The meeting will take place at Qianjiang Hot Spring Hotel in Fengshun.

Teachers who attended the STU–Stanford School of Medicine basic-science teaching-training program and teachers sharing experience in integrated-thinking education will discuss their training and classroom practice. Participants will also explore how to incorporate integrated-thinking development into courses and share teaching methods.

| Participant group | Number or arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Invited teachers from the training program and integrated-thinking teaching presenters | 10 |
| Teachers designated by colleges, teaching departments, or centers | 10, one from each unit |
| Teachers interested in teaching-methods exchange | 20, open registration until full |

### Schedule and registration

Participants will depart from the university motto stele at 4:00 p.m. on Friday, December 21. The full-day meeting will take place on Saturday, December 22. Designated teachers and teachers registering independently must complete the response form and email it to the Academic Affairs Office at `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn` by the morning of Tuesday, December 18.
