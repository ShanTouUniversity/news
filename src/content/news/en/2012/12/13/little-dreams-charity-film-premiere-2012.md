STU Youth Volunteers Association will host a Dream Road talk and the premiere of a locally produced Shantou film as part of the Little Dreams micro-charity event. Members of the film’s creative team will visit campus to share their experiences of pursuing their dreams. The film was made by young people from Shantou over three years and across five cities and will receive its first public screening in eastern Guangdong.

The event will begin at 7:30 p.m. on December 22, 2012, in the STU Auditorium. Entry will be open from 6:50 to 7:30 p.m. All proceeds raised will be donated to the Firefly Charity Fund.

### Tickets

A donation of at least RMB 3 entitles the donor to one charity ticket. Faculty and students may reserve campus tickets from December 15 through 21 by direct message to the Sina Weibo account “STU Youth Volunteers Association”; reservations by telephone are not accepted. On-site donations and ticket collection will be available from December 19 through 21, daily from 11:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and 5:30 to 7:00 p.m., at locations below the second dining hall and at the fourth dining hall. Donors collecting tickets or receiving delivery must sign for them and record the amount so that proceeds can be tracked.

Tickets are limited and distributed first come, first served. The premiere has no assigned seating; attendees must show a ticket and take seats in arrival order. No entry will be allowed 30 minutes after the start. If reservations exceed capacity, organizers plan an additional film-only screening on December 23, with details to be announced separately.

Recording or photographing with a phone or camera is prohibited during the screening. Because many members of the public are expected on campus, organizers ask attendees to be courteous and mindful of their conduct.
