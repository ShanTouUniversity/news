It is reported that the 15th Table Tennis Individual Open — one of the student club project activities of Shantou University in the 2012-2013 fall semester — concluded after two days of competition. The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the Student Club Federation and the university's Table Tennis Association jointly announced the results on December 13.

The tournament featured men's singles, women's singles, men's doubles and mixed doubles. Hong ZY (Electronics, Class of 2011) was the standout performer, claiming both the men's singles and men's doubles titles, while Shi ZP (Graphic Design, Class of 2011) won both the men's doubles and mixed doubles crowns.

| ----- | ---- | ----------------- |
| Champion | Hong ZY | Electronics, 2011 |
| Runner-up | Lin ZY | Communication Engineering, 2009 |
| Third | Shi ZP | Graphic Design, 2011 |
| Fourth | Zhang ZM | Electronics, 2010 |
