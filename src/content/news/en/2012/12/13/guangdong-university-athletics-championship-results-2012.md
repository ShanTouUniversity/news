The 2012 Guangdong University Student Track and Field Championships concluded at South China University of Technology on December 9. The STU track and field team won a silver medal in the men’s 1,500 meters and a bronze medal in the men’s 800 meters. It also recorded two fifth-place finishes, two seventh-place finishes, and one eighth-place finish, for 11 athlete-place finishes in total.

The championships brought together 1,167 athletes from 69 universities across Guangdong. Coached by Ding Fenghua, STU’s 12-member team competed in seven Group A events. STU placed 15th overall and 10th in the men’s team standings.
