The Ministry of Education and the Chinese Academy of Engineering have opened applications for the 2013 special-task projects on engineering and technology talent development. General projects in the program will be openly solicited and receive approximately RMB 100,000 each. The standard research period is two years, with a one-year extension possible for special projects subject to approval. Final results may take the form of a monograph, research report, or academic paper. STU may submit no more than two applications.

### Guideline areas

Applicants may choose their own titles but should follow one of the eight guideline areas:

| Area | Main focus |
| --- | --- |
| Joint university-enterprise training of engineering doctoral and master’s students | Enrollment, training models, quality assurance, cost sharing, and cooperation mechanisms |
| Joint graduate training by universities and engineering research institutes | Cases, results, challenges, and future strategies |
| Practical training in engineering and technology education | Practice models and evaluation, including safety, legal, and policy issues |
| Reform of interdisciplinary engineering graduate education | Definitions, models, barriers, and recommendations |
| Continuing engineering education through government-industry-university-research platforms | Platform models, operating mechanisms, responsibilities, and innovation |
| Training methods for engineering students at different types and levels of universities | Practical, innovation, and collaboration skills and transferable models |
| Institutional development for engineering faculty | Faculty training, selection, appointment, evaluation, mobility, and continuing education |
| Development of high-level engineering and technology personnel for the military | Talent sources, training and deployment models, incentives, and organizational support |

The call encourages cooperation across industries, departments, professions, and disciplines, and is limited to regular undergraduate universities in China.

### Eligibility and procedure

Applicants must be full-time, in-post teachers with associate senior or higher professional titles and must be able to take actual responsibility for conducting and organizing the project. Each applicant may submit only one project, and every listed team member must give prior consent. Collaboration with enterprises and research institutions is encouraged.

People currently responsible for Ministry of Education humanities and social-science projects, people whose Ministry projects have been withdrawn since 2010, people currently responsible for National Social Science Fund projects, and people also applying for another category of the Ministry’s 2013 general humanities and social-science projects may not apply. Completed projects must be supported by the required documentation.

Applicants must use the 2013 version of the *Application Review Form for Ministry of Education Humanities and Social-Science Special-Task Projects*. The *2013 Electronic Application for Special-Task Projects* is available through the Ministry platform on the China Higher Education Humanities and Social Sciences Information Network at <http://xm.sinoss.net/sinoss/business/login.jsp>. Files must be installed and kept in the same directory as instructed, and Word macros must be enabled when completing the application.

Materials must be truthful and free of intellectual-property disputes; proven fabrication will result in a three-year application ban. Late materials will not be accepted.
