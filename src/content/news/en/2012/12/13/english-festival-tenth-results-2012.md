The 10th STU English Festival, organized by the STU English Language Center, was held in the STU Auditorium from December 7 to 9, 2012. Students from STU, Seoul National University, Jinan University, South China Agricultural University, and Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University competed in speech, debate, and singing.

STU students won first and second place in the speech competition, Best Debater in the debate competition, and third place in the singing competition. Jinan University won third place in speech and first place in singing. South China Agricultural University won Best Debate Team and second place in singing. Student names are not published here.

| Competition | Results |
| --- | --- |
| Speech | First and second place: STU; third place: Jinan University |
| Debate | Best Debater: STU; Best Debate Team: South China Agricultural University |
| Singing | First place: Jinan University; second place: South China Agricultural University; third place: STU |

The festival provided a platform for English expression and exchange among students from different universities, and the competitors’ performances were recognized by the professional judges and audience.
