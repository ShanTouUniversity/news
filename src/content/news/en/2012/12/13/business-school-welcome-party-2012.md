The “Chaoyun–Youbao” 2012 STU Business School Welcome Party will be held at 7:30 p.m. on Friday, December 14, in the STU Auditorium. The party’s theme is “On the Road to Building Dreams,” and faculty and students across the university are welcome to attend.

Audience members should follow staff directions and enter and take their seats 15 minutes before the start. The middle seats in Rows 7 through 12 will be reserved for university leaders and guests; other audience members should follow the on-site seating arrangements.
