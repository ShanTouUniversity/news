STU Human Resources Office has launched performance reviews for the 2011–2012 academic year. The review is conducted under the Guangdong rules for assessing public-institution employees and STU’s provisional faculty and staff performance-management measures. The performance period runs from August 1, 2011 through July 31, 2012.

The review covers all full-time employees, including faculty members, academic-unit leaders, administrative staff, teaching-support staff, and technical and service employees. Review records and results will be placed in personnel files. Employees whose contracts are expiring will be reviewed under STU’s performance-management measures.

### Review organization and standards

The main campus is divided into 22 review units: the Colleges of Liberal Arts, Science, Engineering, Law, Business, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication; the School of Continuing Education; English Language Center; Department of Social Sciences; Physical Education Office; Library; Network and Information Center; Party and Government Office, including Foreign Affairs and Alumni Affairs; Party and mass organizations, including Organization and United Front Work, Publicity, and Labor Union offices; Student Affairs Office, including Zhicheng College, Youth League, and Admissions; Human Resources; Finance, including Supervision and Audit; Academic Affairs, including the Institute of Higher Education and university journal editorial office; Research, including the Central Laboratory; Graduate School, including the 211 Project Office; and Resources Management Office.

Employees must use the appropriate annual review form and complete self-assessment, supervisor assessment, functional-head assessment, and final university review according to the instructions. The five ratings are Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Improvement Needed, and Unsatisfactory.

| Rating limit | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Exceptional | No more than 15% of employees actually participating in the unit’s review |
| Exceptional plus Exceeds Expectations | No more than 30% of participating employees |

Faculty forms focus on teaching, research, and service achievements. Forms for academic-unit heads and other leaders address unit planning, advanced undergraduate education, research development, admissions and employment, faculty development, and internationalization. Administrative, teaching-support, subsidiary-unit, and technical employees record achievements and competencies related to their duties. The forms also record four to six major performance objectives for the next academic year; faculty members use the separate annual faculty performance form as required.

### Submission materials

Review units must submit the materials to Human Resources Office at New Administration Center Room 315 by January 10, 2013:

- One faculty and staff review summary, with full-time faculty in teaching units listed separately and the unit seal affixed. The electronic summary should be sent to `gqzhang@stu.edu.cn`.
- Annual review forms for all employees for 2011–2012, arranged in the order shown in the summary. Forms for unit-assessed employees and senior administrators should be separated and organized by rating.

The Human Resources Office asked each review unit to complete the assessments and organize the materials according to the requirements.
