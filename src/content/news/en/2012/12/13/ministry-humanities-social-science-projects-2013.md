STU Research Office has opened applications for the Ministry of Education’s 2013 general humanities and social-science research projects. The call covers 25 fields, including Marxism and ideological and political education, philosophy, linguistics, Chinese literature, foreign literature, arts, history, economics, management, law, education, psychology, physical education, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan studies, international studies, and interdisciplinary or comprehensive research. No specific project topics are prescribed; applicants may develop topics based on national and local development needs, academic frontiers, and their research foundations.

### Categories and funding

| Category | Funding or finance requirement | General duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning Fund | Ministry funding of up to RMB 100,000 | Generally three years from approval |
| Young Researchers’ Fund | Ministry funding of up to RMB 80,000 | Generally three years from approval |
| Self-funded project | At least RMB 80,000 raised from outside the university | Generally three years from approval |

Self-funded applicants must attach a finance-office receipt, bank remittance record, or other proof that the commissioning organization’s funds have arrived, and complete the “other sources of funding” section. Project funding does not include publication support for research results.

### Eligibility

Planning Fund applicants must be full-time, in-post teachers with senior professional titles, including associate senior titles. Young Researchers’ Fund applicants must be full-time, in-post teachers who hold a doctoral degree or an intermediate or higher professional title and are no older than 40 as of the stated cutoff, meaning born after January 1, 1973. Self-funded applicants must have actual financial support from an outside government department, organization, or enterprise. Foreign faculty may serve as project leaders or team members, but their units must apply to the Research Office in advance for the special application-review form.

Applicants must be able to conduct the research and take real responsibility for implementation. Each applicant may submit only one project, and every listed team member must give prior consent. People currently responsible for Ministry of Education humanities and social-science projects, people whose Ministry projects have been withdrawn since 2010, and people currently responsible for any National Social Science Fund or National Natural Science Foundation project may not apply. Completed projects must be supported by completion documentation where required. Applicants for the 2013 National Social Science Fund or National Natural Science Foundation may also apply, but if they receive a national project before the Ministry project is formally approved, the Ministry project eligibility will be canceled.

### Online application and deadlines

Applications are submitted online using the new 2013 version of the *Application Review Form for General Ministry of Education Humanities and Social-Science Research Projects*. Applicants should download the *2013 Electronic Application for Planning, Young Researchers’, and Self-Funded Projects* from the Ministry platform on the China Higher Education Humanities and Social Sciences Information Network at <http://xm.sinoss.net/sinoss/business/login.jsp>. The files in the installation directory must remain together, and Word macros must be enabled when completing the form.

| Stage | Deadline or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Online formal review | Submit the application in the system by December 25, 2012 |
| Paper materials | By January 8, 2013, submit two qualifying A4 copies of the form, bound on the left |
| Electronic materials | Confirm the upload in the system and ensure it matches the signed paper copies |
| STU contacts | Huang Yumei and Meng Fanming |
| Phone | 86502563 or 82902563 |
| Submitting office | STU Research Office |

Part B of the application is reviewed anonymously and must not contain the applicant’s name, university, or other identifying information; otherwise the application will be invalid. Applications must be truthful and complete and must contain no intellectual-property dispute. Proven fabrication will result in a three-year ban on applying. Late materials will not be accepted.
