It is reported that the Firefly Love Action Team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association will hold its "Xinhuo Rural Heritage" (Xinhuo Xiangchuan) Winter Camp at a rural primary school this winter and is now recruiting volunteers campus-wide.

The camp is scheduled for January 21–25, 2013, at Xiacuo Primary School in Leiling Town, Chaonan District, Shantou. Guided by the volunteer spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual assistance, and progress, the program aims to serve rural communities in eastern Guangdong while strengthening volunteers' capabilities and civic awareness.

Thirty volunteers will be selected through interviews and assigned to six teams:

| Team | Quota | Responsibilities |
|------|-------|------------------|
| Teaching | 11 | Lessons, recreational games, and sports competitions to enrich pupils' extracurricular learning |
| Medical Service | 3 | Basic health check-ups for pupils and villagers and health education |
| Appliance Repair | 3 | Repairing household appliances and promoting safe electricity use |
| News & Editorial | 4 | Documenting the camp through photos, field records, and live microblog updates |
| Logistics | 5 | Meals, daily living arrangements, supplies management, and cross-team support |
| Recreation & Performance | 4 | Planning and organizing the evening gala |

Applications are open from the date of the notice until 12:00 on December 16. Applicants may submit the electronic form downloaded from the campus OA system to the designated email, register at consultation points at Canteens No. 2 and No. 4 on December 13–14, or sign up on-site during interviews. Interviews will be held at Rooms D401 and D402 on December 15 and 16, from 08:30 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 17:00; any schedule changes will be announced separately. Electronic forms should be named in the format "Grade + Major + Name + Xinhuo Winter Camp Application Form."

Service hours for registered volunteers will be credited according to the number of days of the program, in line with Article 15 of the STU Youth Volunteer Registration System Management Measures (Trial). Further information is available on the Xinhuo blog at http://blog.sina.com.cn/fireflyxhxc.
