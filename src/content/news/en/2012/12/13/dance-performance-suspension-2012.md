It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on December 13, 2012, regarding suspension of the *Dance Performance Arts* course.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Dance Performance Arts (Class 54834, instructor: Dou Bo) |
| Suspended sessions | Saturday of week 13 (December 15), periods 6–7; Saturday of week 14 (December 22), periods 6–7 |
| Venue | Dance Rehearsal Hall STU102 |
| Reason | Business trip for the instructor in week 13 and the national CET-4/6 examinations in week 14 |
| Make-up | To be announced |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students enrolled are asked to inform each other.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
