The civil-engineering session of STU’s eighth Graduate Academic Forum will be held from 8:00 a.m. to noon on Saturday, December 15, 2012, in E204. Organized by the Graduate School and hosted by the civil-engineering program, the session will focus on civil-engineering research. All graduate students, undergraduates interested in research, and faculty members are welcome.

The session will feature 12 research presentations covering geotechnical and structural engineering, construction materials, engineering simulation, geographic information systems, and real-estate appraisal:

| Research area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Soft-soil dynamics | Dynamic properties of Shantou muddy soft clay |
| Steel structures | Elastoplastic analysis of top and bottom angle steel |
| Foamed concrete | Effects of expanded graphite on thermal insulation and mechanical properties |
| Pile-bearing capacity | ABAQUS-based study of an ultra-long single pile under vertical load |
| Forest-fire simulation | Three-dimensional simulation using geographic cellular automata |
| Real-estate appraisal | Application of GIS and fuzzy theory |
| Fracture evolution | Evolution of interfacial nonuniform fracture |
| Timber waterproofing | Experimental study of epoxy-adhesive-coated wood |
| Concrete interfaces | Interface performance of prefilled-aggregate concrete |
| Connection energy dissipation | Simulation of inclined-brace semi-rigid connections |
| Semi-rigid joints | ABAQUS-based analysis of double-web and top-and-bottom-angle-steel joints |
| Ultra-long piles | Research progress on bearing performance using a meshless method |

Li Gengying, Wu Ming, and Liu Jilu will attend as invited guests. Through research presentations and academic discussion, the forum aims to broaden graduate students’ scholarly perspectives, promote disciplinary exchange, and share research results.
