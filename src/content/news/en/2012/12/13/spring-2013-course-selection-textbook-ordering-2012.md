STU Academic Affairs Office has announced the course-selection and textbook-ordering schedule for the spring 2013 semester. Students must complete course selection, add/drop procedures, and textbook orders in the credit-system portal within the specified periods and are encouraged to discuss their study plans with their academic advisors.

### Course-selection schedule

| Stage | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial selection | 9:00 a.m. December 14 to 11:00 p.m. December 16, 2012 | Results will remain if the selected class is not adjusted |
| Second selection | Noon December 21 to 11:00 p.m. December 24, 2012 | First come, first served within class capacity |
| Add/drop | 9:00 a.m. December 31, 2012 to 5:00 p.m. January 5, 2013 | Add or drop in the system |
| Online application for additional selection | 9:00 a.m. January 6 to 5:00 p.m. January 9, 2013 | Mainly for general-education and some out-of-major foundation courses |
| Adjustment | 9:00 a.m. February 25 to 5:00 p.m. March 1, 2013 | Moved to the first week of the spring semester |

For an online additional-selection application, students must enter the credit-system portal and use “Course Selection—View Alternative Courses.” Courses offered by the English Language Center, Department of Social Sciences, Physical Education Office, and a student’s own major cannot use this online channel; students must submit paper applications to the relevant teaching unit. Students unable to make changes independently because of a retaken-course conflict, a double degree, excess credits, or another special reason must submit a printed course-change application during the adjustment period and obtain the required signatures, including that of the instructor.

After the adjustment period, Academic Affairs will issue the final spring course plan and will no longer accept course changes. No add, drop, or change applications will be accepted after 5:00 p.m. on March 1, 2013.

### Course-selection rules

Students should generally take no more than 25 credits per semester. Students seeking additional credits for a reasonable reason must submit an application and transcripts from the previous two semesters before the adjustment period; the department or school teaching committee will review it and report it to Academic Affairs. In principle, applicants must rank in the top 20% of their class, have passed all previous courses, and have a GPA above 3. The new-semester courses must be major requirements or English courses, and the excess may not exceed three credits. Graduating students and other special cases follow the approval procedure.

Students may generally take no more than two ideological and political theory courses offered by the Department of Social Sciences, one physical-education course, and one course from the Arts Education Center per semester. Classes are subject to resource and enrollment limits; ordinary classes generally require at least 15 students to open, with exceptions for special courses. Priority numbers apply during initial selection. During second selection, add/drop, and adjustment periods, priority numbers no longer apply; places are handled on a first-come, first-served basis according to remaining capacity, and Academic Affairs may adjust individual selections when necessary.

Courses selected in the system will have their grades recorded. Courses not selected in the system will not receive recorded grades; if a student selects a course but does not take its assessment or fails, the result will still be entered in the student’s academic record. Students must protect their own accounts and may not use or lend another person’s account. Paper course-change applications must be signed by the instructor personally.

### Textbook ordering

Textbook ordering runs from 9:00 a.m. on December 14 through 11:00 p.m. on December 24, 2012. After the deadline, Academic Affairs will generate the textbook orders and send them to the bookstore; no ordering or editing service will be available during the add/drop or adjustment periods. Students must check their current textbook list before the deadline and pay according to the order afterward.

When a course offers several alternative textbooks, selecting the order option defaults to all alternatives. Students who need only some of them can go to “Course Selection—Textbook Ordering—Current Textbook Orders” and delete the unwanted items. Prices shown in the system are estimates; “0.00” means the price is pending, and reprinting or revised editions may change the price. The system order is the basis for textbook purchasing.
