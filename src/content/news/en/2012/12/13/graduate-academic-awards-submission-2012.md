The STU Graduate School plans to recognize graduate students who actively participated in academic activities or competitions at different levels in China and abroad and achieved outstanding results during 2012. It is now soliciting award materials from graduate training units and graduate students.

Graduate students must submit their award information, an introduction to the relevant academic activity or competition, and a copy of the award certificate to the graduate secretary of their training unit by December 21, 2012. Training units must compile the information and submit the summary and supporting materials to the Graduate School Youth League by December 25.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Graduate awards from academic activities and competitions in 2012 |
| Individual deadline | December 21, 2012 |
| Individual submission | Graduate secretary of the student’s training unit |
| Required materials | Award information, activity introduction, and award-certificate copy |
| Unit deadline | December 25, 2012 |
| Submission point | Graduate School Youth League, New Administration Center Room 401 |
| Contact | Wang Fengqi |
| Electronic summary | `o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn` |

The Graduate School will use the compiled materials to prepare subsequent recognition for graduate students who performed well in academic activities and competitions. Training units must organize the paper and electronic materials as required and ensure that the summaries are complete.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
>
> ⚠️ The award summary and certificate materials contain student names and other personal information. Consult the original OA notice and the relevant training unit for the forms; individual award lists are not reproduced here.
