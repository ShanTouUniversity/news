STU Admissions Office has launched “Remembering Our Roots, Passing on Dreams,” an admissions-outreach activity for winter break 2013, and is recruiting current undergraduate and graduate students. During the break, participants will visit their former secondary schools and introduce STU’s educational characteristics, development, and their experiences of studying and living at the university.

Participants will also deliver university admissions materials and souvenirs to their former schools, ask relevant teachers to complete receipt forms, and conduct a survey of high-school students’ application intentions. After the activity, participants must complete the *Admissions Outreach Activity Feedback Form*.

Applicants should identify with the university, be willing to support admissions outreach and consultation, communicate effectively, explain the university clearly, and accept selection, training, and coordinated deployment by Admissions Office. Applications are primarily submitted in teams, generally with no more than six students assigned to each secondary school. Students unable to form a team may apply individually; Admissions Office will arrange groups based on applicants’ home regions and former secondary schools.
