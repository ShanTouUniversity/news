STU’s Center for International Studies (CIS) in the College of Liberal Arts will offer CIS2053, *Introduction to Psychology*, as a general-education course in the spring 2013 semester. The course will be taught by new CIS faculty member Wang Yuhan and will be open to students from every major and year at the university.

This is the center’s first general-education course in psychology. It will introduce students to the field of psychology and its approaches to understanding human behavior and experience. Interested students may review and select the course through the university’s course-registration system during the spring-semester selection period.
