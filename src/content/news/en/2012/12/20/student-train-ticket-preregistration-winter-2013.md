As winter break approaches, STU will provide train-ticket pre-registration services for current undergraduate and graduate students traveling during winter break 2013. The university plans to coordinate with Shantou Railway Station to open temporary ticket-sale and free ticket-collection windows at the end of December; the exact time and location will be announced later.

### Ticket options

Students who need tickets may log in to the Railway Customer Service Center at `http://www.12306.cn` and purchase tickets online. They may then bring their student cards and identity cards to the university’s temporary ticket-collection point according to the subsequent arrangements. Students unable to purchase tickets online may log in to the STU reservation system for internal pre-registration. The system will randomly assign positions to determine the ticket-purchase queue, and students will buy tickets at the temporary window in that order.

Internal pre-registration runs from 8:00 a.m. on December 21 through 4:30 p.m. on December 25, 2012. Students should use their campus-network accounts to log in to the reservation center, select “Winter Break 2013 Student Train-Ticket Pre-Registration,” and enter the outbound and return route in the notes field in the format “boarding station–(transfer station)–destination station.”

### Document requirements

Students should prepare their identity cards, student cards, and student-discount cards in advance. Student cards must bear a registration stamp, and student-discount cards must be valid. Discount cards issued after 2012 must contain real-name registration information. The home location and travel section recorded on a student card must correspond; students may not alter the travel section privately. Necessary changes should be handled through the Academic Affairs Office for undergraduates or Graduate School for graduate students.

The earliest departure and latest return dates for pre-registered tickets will by default follow the university holiday schedule. Pre-registration results will be published on the internal university information network.

**Subsequent arrangements**: On December 25, Student Affairs Office announced that 604 students had completed pre-registration and 527 registrations had been accepted. Accepted students may collect queue-number cards based on the randomized order at Building B Room 106 on December 26. Because the Spring Festival train timetable was still being compiled, ticket-sale times and specific trains remained subject to a later notice.
