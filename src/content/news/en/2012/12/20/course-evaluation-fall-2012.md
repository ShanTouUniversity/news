STU Academic Affairs Office has announced that preparations for the fall 2012 semester course-teaching evaluation are complete. All enrolled students must log in to the credit-based system and provide careful, objective, and fair evaluations of their courses and instructors.

The evaluation period runs from December 20, 2012, through January 4, 2013. Students should visit `http://credit.stu.edu.cn` and select “Course Evaluation.” Students who took public-welfare courses must also select their fall 2012 public-welfare courses under “Course Evaluation → View Courses Awaiting Evaluation” and complete the additional evaluation.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluation scope | Courses and instructors from the fall 2012 semester |
| Evaluation period | December 20, 2012–January 4, 2013 |
| Entry point | Credit-Based System: `http://credit.stu.edu.cn` → “Course Evaluation” |
| Public-welfare courses | Complete a separate evaluation under “View Courses Awaiting Evaluation” |
| Applies to | All enrolled students on the main campus |

Course-teaching evaluation results will provide reference for instructors’ teaching improvement. Students without special circumstances should complete the evaluation on time. Students who fail to do so by the deadline must submit a written explanation; otherwise, they may be unable to view their grades for courses taken during the semester.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
