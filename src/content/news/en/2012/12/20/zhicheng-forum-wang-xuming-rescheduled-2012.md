Because of a change in the speaker’s schedule, the 14th Zhicheng Forum, originally planned for the afternoon of Saturday, December 22, has been rescheduled for 3:30 p.m. on Friday, December 21. The venue remains the Science Lecture Hall.

The forum, “For Public Disclosure, I Became a News Spokesperson: A Dialogue between STU Students and Wang Xuming,” features Wang Xuming, former spokesperson for the Ministry of Education and current president of the Language and Culture Press. STU Publicity Department and Zhicheng College ask faculty and students who have seen the earlier announcement to share the change and attend at the revised time.
