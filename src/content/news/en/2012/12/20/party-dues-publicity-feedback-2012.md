STU Organization and United Front Department has forwarded Shantou Municipal Party Committee Organization Department’s notice on publicizing the income and expenditure of centrally managed Party dues for 2011. The notice asks all Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches on the main campus to brief Party members, solicit comments, and submit feedback.

Under the forwarded notice, the disclosure of centrally managed Party dues for 2011 is directed to grassroots Party organizations and Party members. The publicity period runs from December 13 through December 21, 2012, covering seven working days. Grassroots organizations should use internal networks, document circulation, Party-branch study meetings, or other appropriate channels to broaden awareness and ensure that organizations and members can review the disclosed information.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Disclosure | Income and expenditure of centrally managed Party dues for 2011 |
| Audience | Grassroots Party organizations and Party members |
| Higher-level publicity period | December 13–21, 2012 |
| Internal requirement | Party general branches and directly affiliated branches must brief members and solicit comments |
| STU feedback deadline | December 26, 2012 |
| Receiving unit | STU Organization and United Front Department |

The disclosure and comment process should follow the requirements of the responsible Party-affairs authorities. The attachment’s detailed dues figures, account information, and other internal financial materials are not reproduced; relevant Party organizations should circulate the complete document through designated internal channels.
