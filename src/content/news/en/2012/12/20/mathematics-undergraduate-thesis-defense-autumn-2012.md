STU Mathematics Department has announced the arrangements for the autumn group of undergraduate thesis and design defenses for the Class of 2013. The defenses will take place from 3:00 to 6:00 p.m. on December 25, 2012, in the Mathematics Department laboratory.

Ye Ruisong will chair the defense committee. Xie Changzhen will lead the defense group, whose other members are Wei Caimin and Li Pengtao. ### Defense procedure and assessment

The defense-group lead will announce the order of presentations and relevant arrangements. Each student will present the main arguments, purpose, structure, and features of the thesis, normally for about five minutes, and answer questions from faculty members. A recorder will document the questions and responses. After the defenses, the group will make a preliminary assessment under the grading standards. The thesis-defense committee and participating faculty will then consider the supervisor’s and reviewer’s recommended grades and determine the final grade collectively.

Thesis grades use a five-level scale with written comments. Assessment considers completion of the assigned task, the completeness and academic quality of the thesis work, independent ability to analyze and solve problems, and the student’s understanding and responses during the defense. The five levels are excellent, good, satisfactory, pass, and fail.
