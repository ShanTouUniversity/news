It is reported that the Center for International Studies (CIS) of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University will hold a CIS Open House from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on Friday, December 21, in Room E-207, open to all faculty and students.

## What to Expect

At the event, CIS faculty will introduce the center's course offerings for the spring semester of 2013 and discuss them in detail with students. The center also welcomes suggestions on what kinds of general education courses students would like, to help support their academic interests.

In addition, chocolate from different countries will be served, offering participants a global taste of not only food but also culture.
