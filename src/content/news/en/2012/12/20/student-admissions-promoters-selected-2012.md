STU Admissions Office has announced the selection of student admissions promoters. Following résumé screening and interviews, the office selected 14 students to serve as admissions promoters. The selected students come from programs including public administration, marketing, art design, journalism, law, administrative management, applied physics, accounting, journalism and communication, and Chinese language and literature.
