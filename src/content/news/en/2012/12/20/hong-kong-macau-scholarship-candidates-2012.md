STU International Cooperation Office and Student Affairs Office have announced the review results for the 2012 scholarship for students from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao, and overseas Chinese communities. Following student applications and a joint review by the two offices, the university proposes two first-prize, two second-prize, and five third-prize candidates, nine candidates in total.

The public notice identifies Xiao YY among the first-prize candidates and Zhuang SL among the second-prize candidates. The other candidate names are garbled in the archived text and are not reconstructed here. During the publicity period, units and individuals with comments may submit written statements to the Student Aid Management Center by noon on Friday, December 21. Statements from units must bear an official seal, while individual statements must be signed with the submitter’s real name.
