Under the *Interim Measures for the Management and Use of STU’s Intra-Party Care and Mutual-Aid Fund*, the STU Party Committee has arranged a survey of staff Party members who may need assistance before the 2013 Spring Festival. The university will provide assistance to staff Party members whose living conditions have become difficult for various reasons.

Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches should conduct the survey within their respective units. By December 26, 2012, they must submit to the Organization and United Front Department the names and basic information of Party members who need assistance, which will support the subsequent assistance arrangements.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Assistance recipients | Staff Party members facing living difficulties for various reasons |
| Assistance period | Before the 2013 Spring Festival |
| Surveying units | Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches |
| Submission | Names and basic information of members needing assistance |
| Deadline | December 26, 2012 |
| Receiving unit | Organization and United Front Department |
| Policy basis | *Interim Measures for the Management and Use of STU’s Intra-Party Care and Mutual-Aid Fund* |

This article reports only the public arrangements for the assistance program and does not disclose names, reasons for hardship, or personal living circumstances.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Department)
