It is reported that the 7th Costume Design Competition (Fashion Show) of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design (CKAD) at Shantou University has been launched, calling on all students passionate about fashion design to showcase their creativity.

Themed on the letter "C", the competition invites participants to draw inspiration either from the shape and pronunciation of the letter itself, or from any English word starting with C — such as colour, character, chat, or change — as the conceptual starting point for their designs.

The competition is open to all STU students regardless of major or year, with entries accepted from individuals or teams. Free team formation is encouraged, and professional mentors will be invited to provide guidance to enhance the visual impact of the works.

Entries must be original and closely tied to the "C" theme, balancing practicality with artistic expression while demonstrating a contemporary sensibility and distinctive personal style. Menswear designs will receive bonus consideration.

The competition consists of two stages. In the preliminary round, contestants will present their design sketches and concepts via a slide presentation covering personal information, color palette, design direction and draft sketches, with professional judges scoring and offering feedback. Finalists will then turn their sketches into finished garments for a runway show on the final night; models may be arranged by contestants themselves or provided through the organizing committee.

Awards include one Gold Prize of RMB 1,000, one Silver Prize of RMB 500, and one Bronze Prize of RMB 300, each with a trophy and certificate.

Applications require a completed registration form sent to the designated email address, with the deadline set for January 15, 2013. No applications will be accepted after that date. Details and the registration form are available in the attachment.
