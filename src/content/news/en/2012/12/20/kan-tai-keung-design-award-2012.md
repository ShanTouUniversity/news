It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design of Shantou University will host the Kan Tai-keung Design Award 2012 ceremony and related lectures on December 22 (Saturday) and 23 (Sunday). The touring exhibition of the 2012 award-winning works will also make a stop at the Shantou station, on display at the school.

The Kan Tai-keung Design Award is a major competition in the global Chinese-language design community. Bringing the ceremony and lectures to STU offers design students and local design enthusiasts an opportunity to view the winning works up close and hear from leading voices in design.

Details of the schedule are available on the posters released by the school.
