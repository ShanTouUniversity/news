The 130th lecture in STU’s Toward Modern Mathematics series was held at 4:30 p.m. on December 20, 2012, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei Building Room 412). Chen Zhiping, a professor and doctoral supervisor at Xi’an Jiaotong University, delivered a lecture titled “A Brief Discussion of Applications of Mathematics in Financial Information Processing and Decision-Making.” The event was open to faculty and students.

Chen is vice dean of Xi’an Jiaotong University’s School of Mathematics and Statistics, deputy director of the university’s Jinhe Center for Economic Research, and director of the editorial office of the *Journal of Engineering Mathematics*. He is also a standing council member of the Financial Engineering and Financial Risk Management Branch of the Operations Research Society of China. His research interests include operations-research methods and applications, optimization theory and algorithms, stochastic programming, integer programming, mathematical finance, mathematical models for financial management, financial optimization, financial risk measurement, and quantitative economics. He has led a key project of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and several other NSFC projects.
