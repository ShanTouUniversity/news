STU Business School will establish a division and organize undergraduates to participate in the 3rd National University Student Market Survey Analysis Competition and the mainland selection for the 2nd Cross-Strait University Student Market Survey Analysis Competition, jointly organized by the China Commercial Statistics Association and the Chinese Association of Applied Statistics in Taiwan. The competitions are intended to develop students’ professional and practical abilities in market survey analysis.

All Business School undergraduates, regardless of major, may register. The competition consists of an individual Market Survey Analysis Knowledge Competition and a team-based Market Survey Analysis Practice Competition. Students who pass the knowledge competition may form teams of three to five to enter the practice competition, which includes a selection round and a national final.

### Competition schedule

| Competition | Format | Time | Location or method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market Survey Analysis Knowledge Competition | Individual online examination | January 10–25, 2013, in two batches | Online examinations organized by participating institutions |
| Practice Competition selection round | Teams of 3–5; survey-report presentation, PPT presentation, and defense | Early April 2013 | Participating institutions |
| National final and mainland selection for the cross-strait competition | Teams of 3–5; survey-report presentation, PPT presentation, and defense | May 31–June 2, 2013 | Beijing |
| Cross-Strait competition final | Teams of 3–5 | Mid-October 2013 | Taipei |

The knowledge competition is a 120-minute online multiple-choice examination. The first batch runs from January 10 through 18, with registration closing on December 25, 2012. The second batch runs from January 18 through 25, with registration closing on January 5, 2013. The organizing committee plans to send results to participating institutions on February 1.

The detailed practice-competition plan will be published on the competition website after January 10, 2013. National-final survey reports must include at least 300 survey samples, run approximately 5,000 Chinese characters, and be accompanied by a PPT of at least 25 slides. The top six mainland finalist teams will be recommended through the relevant procedures to participate in the Taipei final.

### Registration and recognition

STU participants should email the registration form to `fengyantracy@hotmail.com` by December 28, 2012. The competition website is `http://www.chinacmrc.org`. High-performing knowledge-competition participants may receive certificates from the national organizing committee. Students who pass the knowledge competition may voluntarily apply for a relevant professional-competence certificate from the China Commercial Statistics Association. Relevant associations will issue certificates to award-winning teams and individuals in the practice and national-final competitions.

| Fee | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge and practice competitions | No entry fee charged by the organizing committee; participating institutions may charge a one-time RMB 30 registration subsidy per student at their discretion |
| National final | Finalist teams pay RMB 800 per team under the committee’s arrangements, covering venue, pre-competition guidance, materials, and judging |
| Certificates | Knowledge-competition qualifiers may voluntarily apply for a relevant professional-skills certificate; details to be announced |
