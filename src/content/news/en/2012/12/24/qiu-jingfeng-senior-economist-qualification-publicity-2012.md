Qiu Jingfeng of STU has obtained the senior economist qualification after review by Guangdong’s First Senior Economist Qualification Review Committee. The result has entered the public-notice period, which runs from 8:00 a.m. on December 25 through 5:30 p.m. on December 31, 2012.

During the publicity period, anyone with comments on the qualification result may respond by telephone or in writing to the office of Guangdong’s First Senior Economist Qualification Review Committee, the personnel office of the applicant’s institution, or the relevant municipal or provincial personnel and title-reform authorities. Telephone and written submissions must identify or be signed with the submitter’s real name; anonymous submissions will not be accepted.
