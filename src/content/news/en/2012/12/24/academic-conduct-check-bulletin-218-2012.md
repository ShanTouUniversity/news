STU’s “Three Styles” Development Office has issued academic-conduct bulletin 218. During Week 14 of the fall 2012 semester, the academic-conduct inspection team conducted two 8:00 a.m. classroom-order checks in E Building and recorded instances of student tardiness.

| Check date | Location | Findings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday, December 18, 2012 | E Building | 10 students up to five minutes late; two more than five minutes late |
| Thursday, December 20, 2012 | E Building | Five students up to five minutes late |

The bulletin asks colleges and residential colleges to provide criticism and education to the students concerned and continue strengthening conduct education and academic culture. The original attachment contains a student list; student names, student numbers, and other personal information are not reproduced.
