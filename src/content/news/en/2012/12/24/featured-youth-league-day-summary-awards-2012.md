STU Communist Youth League Committee will hold the summary and awards meeting for its 2012 “Strengthen Academic Culture, Grow and Succeed” themed Youth League Day activities at 2:30 p.m. on December 26 in the multimedia conference room, Building 7 Room 726. The meeting is open to faculty and students across the university.

The themed Youth League Day program was organized by the university Youth League Committee for young League members, with League organizations at different levels developing projects suited to their units. At the meeting, project leaders from the colleges will present project results, share experience, and answer questions from the judges. Each project presentation consists of a 10-minute statement and a three-minute question-and-answer session; the statement may not exceed 10 minutes, and the total presentation may not exceed 13 minutes.

The meeting will select two Outstanding Organization awards and first-, second-, and third-prize awards for outstanding themed Youth League Day activities: one first prize, two second prizes, and three third prizes.

| Award | Number | Prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Outstanding Organization | 2 | RMB 800 each |
| First prize for outstanding themed Youth League Day activity | 1 | RMB 600 |
| Second prize for outstanding themed Youth League Day activity | 2 | RMB 400 each |
| Third prize for outstanding themed Youth League Day activity | 3 | RMB 200 each |
