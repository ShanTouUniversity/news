STU Labor Union has announced a car-purchase service event for in-service employees. Bank of China’s STU branch and GAC Toyota Jucheng will provide car-purchase financing information and a vehicle-discount display for STU employees from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. on December 26, 2012, beside the campus skating rink.

Bank of China’s “Easy Car Purchase” installment service allows principal repayment in equal monthly installments over 12, 24, or 36 months, with an installment-service fee collected once. The notice lists preferential rates of 4 percent for 12 months, 7.5 percent for 24 months, and 11 percent for 36 months for STU employees. The financing may be used for a vehicle purchased at the event or for a vehicle purchased from another dealer, subject to the bank’s approval and processing requirements.

GAC Toyota Jucheng will display models including the Highlander, Camry, E’Z, and Yaris. Employees with a confirmed purchase interest should register by December 25, indicating the preliminary model choice and whether financing is needed, and complete the purchase arrangements at the event.
