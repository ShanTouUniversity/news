STU Business School plans to join the X-Culture international collaboration project and is recruiting on-campus MBA and undergraduate students. Through global virtual teams, the project allows students to work with peers from different countries on international-business tasks and experience cross-cultural communication and international collaboration.

### Eligibility

Applicants must be on-campus MBA or undergraduate students and must have completed one of the following courses: International Business, Management, International Marketing, Entrepreneurship Management, Business Case Analysis, or Marketing Research. Applicants must also demonstrate strong English reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills, with an average score of at least 80 percent across ELC Levels 1–5.

### Project and benefits

Project materials state that participants generally form global virtual teams with students from different countries and collaborate online for about two months, using English as the working language. Participants gain experience in cross-cultural work, develop English writing and critical-thinking skills, build an international network, and receive an achievement certificate after completing the project.

At the end of each X-Culture season, participants who complete the project may be invited to a student meeting in Florida, United States, where they can meet international teammates in person. Members of outstanding teams may receive travel stipends. Participants are expected to complete an online training module and readiness test; detailed arrangements are subject to the official website and subsequent notices.
