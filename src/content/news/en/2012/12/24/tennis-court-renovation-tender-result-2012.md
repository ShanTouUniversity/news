Following the recommendation of STU’s Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, the university has announced the result for the project to renovate the tennis courts and surrounding fencing, lighting, and seating. Guangzhou Shili Sports Equipment Co., Ltd. won the tender with a bid of RMB 490,185, excluding a 5 percent contingency allowance.

The public-notice period runs from December 24 through December 26, 2012. During the period, comments may be submitted through the contacts published for the project owner or STU Supervision and Audit Office.
