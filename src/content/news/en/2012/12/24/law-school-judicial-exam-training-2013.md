STU Law School Career Development Center will launch national judicial examination training at the beginning of the spring 2013 semester. The program will continue until the national judicial examination in September 2013. It is designed to support students preparing for the examination, with a case-and-past-paper analysis class planned for early March; the detailed schedule will be announced later.

The training is open to Law School students in the 2010 cohort and graduate students. Applicants should register through their class monitors by January 3, 2013. Students who register for the training will receive related books for the past-paper analysis component.

| --- | --- |
| Program | 2013 national judicial examination training |

| Materials | Related books for past-paper analysis |

Applicants must provide the materials required to produce training identification cards. Registration materials containing student photographs and other personal information are not reproduced.
