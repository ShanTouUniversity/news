It is reported that, following deliberation at the 15th University Leadership Meeting on December 14, 2012, the University has appointed four staff members to intermediate management positions with a six-month probation period.

The appointment was issued as document STU Personnel [2012] No. 4 to all university units, with the Human Resources Office handling related procedures.

## Appointments

| Name | Position | Probation |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Hong Mengdian | Intermediate Management | 6 months |
| Lin Yu | Intermediate Management | 6 months |
| Chen Ying | Intermediate Management | 6 months |
| Yao Shaoyang | Intermediate Management | 6 months |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
