It is reported that, to further strengthen and improve laboratory work and to promote standardized management and reform of experimental teaching, the College of Science of Shantou University has decided to establish a Laboratory Affairs Committee. The decision was issued as Document No. 05 [2012] of the College of Science.

## Committee Members

| Position | Members |
|----------|---------|
| Chair | Huang Xiaochun |
| Members (ordered by stroke count of surname) | Li Ping, Li Shaohui, Yang Shouzhi, Yang Ke'er, Tong Qingxiao, Chen Jiehui, Ouyang Yandong, Zhang Yueling, Wei Chiju |
| Secretary | Lin Yu |

The College said the committee will help coordinate and advance laboratory development and management across the college.
