The Shantou division of the first Guangdong University Students Dream Wo Star Workplace Challenge has announced its preliminary results. As of December 21, 26 students had registered. Following a preliminary review by a university-organized judging panel, eight contestants advanced to the Shantou division competition and two others were selected as alternates.

The Shantou division competition will begin at 7:00 p.m. on December 25 in the Science Lecture Hall and is open to faculty and students. The finalists come from STU and other universities in the Chaoshan region; the original attachment provides the detailed list. To protect student privacy, full names, student numbers, photographs, contact details, and personal histories are not reproduced here.
