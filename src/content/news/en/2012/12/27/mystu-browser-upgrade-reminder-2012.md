STU Academic Affairs Office and Network and Information Center advised campus-network users that the MYSTU learning platform was scheduled for an upgrade after the New Year holiday and would be integrated with the Webmail system. Because the new platform was being developed around newer Internet standards, users who continued to access it with IE6 or IE7 might be unable to log in or encounter broken page layouts.

The MYSTU development team recommended Google Chrome. Users who preferred Windows XP and its built-in Internet Explorer and did not want to use Chrome were advised to upgrade to IE8 or later. Users running the IE8 version included with Windows 7 were advised to use automatic updates to install IE9 or a later version. The MYSTU version under deployment would support IE8 and IE9, although the university continued to recommend newer browsers.

Users could visit `https://my.stu.edu.cn/browser/upgrade.html` and use the links at the bottom of the page to download or upgrade a browser. Technical support was available through:
