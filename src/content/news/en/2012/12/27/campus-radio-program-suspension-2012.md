As Shantou University enters the final-exam review period, STU campus radio will suspend its midday and afternoon programs from December 31, 2012. Programming will resume after the next semester begins.

Units with special broadcasting needs may contact the campus radio station through Teacher Liu at extension `2203` or `o_gbds@stu.edu.cn`.
