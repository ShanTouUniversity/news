Based on reports submitted by the colleges and residential colleges, STU Student Affairs Office has publicized proposed travel subsidies for 2013 undergraduate graduates who incur transportation costs while attending job fairs, seeking employment, taking civil-service examinations, and participating in similar activities. The original notice attachment lists the proposed recipients; student names, student numbers, colleges, and individual subsidy details are not reproduced here.

Organizations or individuals with comments may contact the Graduate Employment Guidance Center by noon on December 31. Organizations must affix an official seal to written feedback, while individuals must sign their real names for accountability.
