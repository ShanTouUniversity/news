The Shantou University Dance Fitness Club and dance-fitness course participants will hold their 2012 teaching performance at the university gymnasium on the evening of December 30. The course was one of the Physical Education Department’s undergraduate teaching-reform pilot projects in the fall semester of 2012–2013, and the event will showcase a semester of learning.

The performance will run from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. and will include dance fitness, modern rhythmic gymnastics, and cheerleading. The program lists Nordic-, Latin-, Indian-, and modern-style dance-fitness pieces, a fan dance, a pom-based cheerleading routine, and competitive aerobics. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

1. Opening dance, “全健大家跳”
2. “波尔卡” (Polka), Nordic style
3. “喜娃娃” group performance
4. “喜娃娃2”
5. “喜娃娃1,” Latin style
6. “印度制造” (Made in India), Indian dance
7. “回到森德,” modern style
8. Modern rhythmic gymnastics, “Fan Dance”
9. Cheerleading, “National First Prescribed Cheerleading Routine,” pom category
10. “菲斯嘟嘟” group performance, American Western style
11. “菲斯嘟嘟,” ethnic style
12. “胜利在望,” Indian dance
13. Competitive aerobics
14. “永远的朋友” (Forever Friends)

The notice body describes 13 performance programs, while the attached program lists 14 numbered items, including the opening dance. This article retains the program titles from the attachment and notes the discrepancy. Student performer names in the program are not reproduced for privacy.
