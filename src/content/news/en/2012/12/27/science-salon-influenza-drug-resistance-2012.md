The Shantou University Science Salon will hold an academic lecture on December 28. Dr. Chen Honglin of the Department of Microbiology at the University of Hong Kong Li Ka Shing Faculty of Medicine will speak on “Analysis of the Molecular Mechanisms Underlying the Spread of Drug-Resistant Mutant Strains of Influenza A.” Faculty members and students with an interest in the topic are welcome.

The presentation will address drug-resistant influenza mutations and transmission, including virus–host interactions, cross-species transmission, the molecular basis of antigenic variation, and research into new influenza vaccines.

Chen received a doctorate in microbiology from the University of Hong Kong Li Ka Shing Faculty of Medicine in 1992. Chen subsequently conducted postdoctoral research at the Royal Postgraduate Medical School in the United Kingdom and Johns Hopkins School of Medicine in the United States and has worked at the University of Hong Kong since 2003. Chen taught and conducted research in STU’s Department of Biology in 1986–1988 and 1993–1996 and has maintained research collaboration with STU’s Medical College and biology program in the College of Science since 2004.

Chen’s research focuses on molecular virology and molecular biology, including the pathogenesis of influenza and Epstein–Barr virus, virus–host interactions, the molecular basis of cross-species transmission, antigenic variation in influenza viruses, and new influenza vaccines. According to the notice, Chen has published more than 100 papers in international journals including *Nature* and *PNAS*. Representative research addresses the transmission, drug resistance, and antiviral targets of influenza viruses including H5N1 and H1N1.
