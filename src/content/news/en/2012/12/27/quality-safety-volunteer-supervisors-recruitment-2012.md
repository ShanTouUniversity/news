To promote student participation in product-quality safety volunteer work, the STU Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteers Association will recruit 30 students as volunteer product-quality safety supervisors and form the Shantou University Student Quality-Safety Volunteer Service Team. The activity supports the social participation and volunteer-service goals of the State Council’s *Outline of the Quality Development Program (2011–2020)*.

### Volunteer service

Selected students will receive on-the-job training organized by quality and technical supervision departments at different levels. They will participate in public-service activities including “We Need Quality Safety, and Quality Safety Needs Us,” assist with on-site quality consultations during Quality Month, distribute informational materials, and explain quality-safety knowledge.

The volunteer team will also participate in the “Student Quality-Safety Volunteer Service Relay” initiative. Activities include visiting enterprises and consumers, conducting quality-safety questionnaires about relevant products and enterprises, collecting public and business opinions, analyzing quality-safety issues, and submitting research reports to local quality and technical supervision departments.
