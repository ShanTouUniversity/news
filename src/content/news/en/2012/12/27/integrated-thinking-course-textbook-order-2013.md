STU Academic Affairs Office has announced that students enrolled in `[CIS2031]` STU Integrated Thinking in spring 2013 may reorder the course’s main reference book, *Principles and Methods of Critical Thinking*.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | `[CIS2031]` STU Integrated Thinking |
| Semester | Spring 2013 |
| Textbook | *Principles and Methods of Critical Thinking* |
| Author | Dong Yu |
| Publisher | Higher Education Press |
| Publication date | September 2010 |
| Price | RMB 37.50 |
| Ordering period | 8:00 a.m. January 18–9:00 p.m. January 21, 2013 |
| Method | Log in to the credit-system portal and order through the “Textbook Ordering” page |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
