STU Human Resources Office has received the 2012 academic-title application materials. The relevant colleges and departments should send representatives to New Administration Building Room 312 to collect the materials for their units by December 28.

The units named in the notice are the College of Liberal Arts, College of Science, College of Engineering, Law School, Business School, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, English Language Center, Social Sciences Department, Physical Education Department, Institute of Higher Education, and Library.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | 2012 academic-title application materials |
| Collecting units | Listed colleges and departments |
| Deadline | December 28, 2012 |
| Location | New Administration Building Room 312 |
| Method | Each unit sends a representative to collect its personnel’s materials |
| Organizer | Human Resources Office |

This article reports only the collection arrangements and does not disclose faculty members’ individual application materials.
