The Publicity Department of Shantou University asks each Party general branch and directly affiliated Party branch on the main campus to send a representative to collect study materials from the department in Room 315 of the New Administration Center on December 28.

Materials include *Study Guide to the Report of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China* and a “Sixth Five-Year” legal-education textbook, among other items. The materials distributed on site will prevail.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Collecting units | Main-campus Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches |
| Collection date | December 28, 2012 |
| Location | Publicity Department, New Administration Center Room 315 |
| Materials | Guide to the 18th Party Congress report, “Sixth Five-Year” legal-education textbook, and other materials |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department of the Party Committee)
