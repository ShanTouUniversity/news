The 132nd lecture in the College of Science’s Modern Mathematics series at Shantou University will be held on December 27. Professor Xuan Thinh Duong of Macquarie University in Australia will speak on “Weighted BMO Spaces Associated to Operators.”

The lecture will introduce basic properties including the weighted John–Nirenberg inequality, discuss the duality between weighted Hardy spaces and weighted BMO spaces, and present an interpolation theorem. Applications to the boundedness of certain singular integrals with nonsmooth kernels will also be discussed.

Xuan Thinh Duong completed doctoral studies in mathematics at Macquarie University in 1991 and worked at the University of New South Wales before returning to Macquarie University. Duong has made advances in harmonic analysis and partial differential equations, received an Australian Research Council Fellowship and three major ARC research grants, and has been invited to lecture at universities and academic conferences in Australia and internationally.
