The Shantou University Student Learning Center (SLC) will adjust its services during the New Year holiday in accordance with the university’s holiday schedule. The center will provide opening, self-study, and group-discussion services in stages, while academic inquiries will follow a revised schedule.

| Date | Service arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| January 1, 2013, Tuesday | Closed to the public |
| January 2–3, 2013 | Academic inquiry service suspended; group discussion and self-study available |
| January 4–5, 2013 | Normal opening |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
