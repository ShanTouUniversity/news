The Shantou University Youth Volunteers’ Firefly Fund is accepting applications for the “Love Accompanies the Journey” winter travel-subsidy program. The program supports undergraduate and graduate students facing financial hardship who plan to return home during the winter break. Up to 20 recipients are expected to receive a maximum of RMB 100 each.

### Eligibility and review priorities

Applicants must plan to return home during the winter break, come from a financially difficult household, live frugally, and travel by train, bus, or high-speed rail while being able to provide a ticket or other proof of travel. The detailed rules also state that the review will consider disciplinary records, the amount of travel cost, whether the applicant has received scholarships or grants, and whether the applicant has part-time employment. The fund will determine the final recipients through information review, scoring, and a board vote.

Recipients must provide proof of having returned home after coming back to campus. Applicants must ensure that all submitted materials are accurate, and recipients must sign an undertaking and receipt documentation when receiving the subsidy.

| Application deadline in notice body | Noon, December 28, 2012 |
| Materials | Application form, copies of student and identity cards, November 2012 campus-card spending record, and travel-ticket or other proof of return travel |

The application includes a form containing personal information, identification copies, and spending records. Consult the original OA attachments for the form and submission channels. The notice body gives noon on December 28 as the deadline, while a note in the attached application form gives December 27; the dates conflict, so applicants should follow the original OA notice and the organizers’ latest arrangement.
