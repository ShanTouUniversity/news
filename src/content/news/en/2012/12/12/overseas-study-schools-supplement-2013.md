STU Student Affairs Office has begun preparing the 2013 STU Overseas Study Support Plan and is soliciting additional universities for the program’s candidate list. Funded by the Li Ka-shing Foundation, the plan supports academically outstanding STU students with the potential to contribute to innovation and development, enabling them to pursue study at excellent universities outside mainland China and gain international experience and perspective.

At this stage, the plan accepts applications only from undergraduates seeking master’s study overseas. A student may apply to no more than three universities, and each university must appear on the candidate list provided by STU. The Student Affairs Office has prepared a list of universities ranked among the world’s top 200 based on a global university-ranking reference. Colleges may also recommend universities outside the top 200 that have strong quality and academic standing in particular disciplines.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | 2013 STU Overseas Study Support Plan |
| Funder | Li Ka-shing Foundation |
| Current applicants | Undergraduates seeking master’s study overseas |
| Number of university choices | No more than three |
| Candidate-list rule | Applied universities must be on the university’s candidate list |
| Additional recommendations | Universities outside the top 200 with strong discipline-specific standing |
| Submission deadline | December 24, 2012 |
| Submission email | `xyli@stu.edu.cn` |
| Responsible office | Student Affairs Office |

Colleges should email their additional university recommendations to the Student Affairs Office by the deadline. The Li Ka-shing Foundation will determine the 2013 candidate-university list after considering the colleges’ views.
