The second Walk with Masters event will be held at STU from February 18 to 20, 2013, under the theme “Information Technology and Future Medicine.” Funded and hosted by the Li Ka-shing Foundation, supported by the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Government, co-organized by the Chinese Society of Education, and undertaken by STU, the three-day event is expected to bring together nearly 100 high-school students with outstanding scientific ability from across China.

Through group discussions, lectures, and shared meals, the students will interact with leading scientists and scholars from China and abroad. STU is recruiting student volunteers across the university to support the event.

### Volunteer schedule

Volunteer service will run from February 16 through 21, 2013. Volunteers must participate throughout the service period and will mainly receive and assist the high-school participants. They may also help participants communicate with mentors and event organizers when needed. English will be the primary working language.

### Requirements

Applicants should be in good physical and mental health, have a positive attitude, and demonstrate strong practical English, communication, organizational, and coordination skills. They must be responsible and willing to follow the arrangements for the service period. Preference will be given to students who participated in the 2012 event or have related experience. A publicity coordinator will also be assigned to maintain social-media updates and write articles under the guidance of the News Center; applicants with relevant experience may indicate their interest.

Interested students must complete the application and information forms attached to the OA notice, rename them as required using their names, and email them to the designated address by the deadline. The forms request personal identity and contact details, which are not reproduced here.
