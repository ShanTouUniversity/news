STU Research Office has issued requirements for completing contracts for internal research funds. The 2012 projects for academic innovation teams, national-fund cultivation, young researchers, and humanities research have been formally announced. Funded project leaders must complete the corresponding contract forms to arrange funding disbursement and subsequent project management.

### Submission schedule

Project leaders must submit three paper copies of the contract to the Research Office by December 21, 2012, together with an electronic version. Failure to submit by the deadline will be treated as a decision to forgo the award, and the project will be withdrawn.

### Contract-management requirements

The contracts will serve as the basis for funding disbursement, annual or interim reviews, and final acceptance. Project leaders must implement the research content, schedule, and deliverables in the application and contract. Changes to the project leader, key members, research content, deliverable format, completion period, or responsible unit require a written application and approval through the prescribed process.

The four project types have different implementation and assessment requirements:

- **Academic innovation team projects** have a three-year construction period and undergo annual reviews and final acceptance. Teams must meet corresponding targets for research projects, funding, papers, awards, or patents. Categories A and B have separate assessment standards for natural sciences and humanities and social sciences. The coordinator or another core member must organize at least one university-wide academic lecture each year.
- **National-fund cultivation projects** have a two-year construction period, with a progress report for the previous year due to the Research Office by November 30 each year. Natural-science leaders must apply for a national fund each year and publish one paper indexed by SCI or EI annually. Humanities and social-science leaders must apply for a national fund each year and publish or complete two qualifying papers or monographs annually. At least one university-wide academic lecture is also required during the project.
- **Young researchers’ fund projects** require one university-wide academic lecture related to the project during the research period; projects without the lecture will not pass final acceptance. Leaders must also actively apply for national and provincial or ministerial research projects at least once a year.
- **Humanities research fund projects** likewise require one university-wide academic lecture during the research period, without which the project will not pass final acceptance. Leaders must actively apply for national and provincial or ministerial research projects at least twice a year.

Interim research outputs must include the relevant STU funding acknowledgment specified in the contract. The Research Office reminded project leaders to follow university rules on research funds and project management and complete the contracts on time.
