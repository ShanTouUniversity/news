STU will rehearse the opening ceremony for the 26th Track and Field Meet at 4:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 12, 2012, at the university track and field stadium. Colleges and residential colleges should organize their participants to assemble at the basketball courts 10 minutes before the rehearsal for coordinated arrangements.

| Assembly point | Basketball courts |
| Assembly time | 10 minutes before the rehearsal |
| Organizing office | Physical Education Office |
