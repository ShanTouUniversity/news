With support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, the STU Labor Union will host a Happy Christmas event for faculty and staff to strengthen collegial ties, build cohesion, and support a harmonious campus. The event is open to faculty and staff at the main campus, who may bring their spouses and children.

The event will take place from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. on December 23, 2012, in the second-floor banquet hall of Junhua Hotel. The program will include special performances, a buffet, and a lucky draw.

### Registration and payment

The meal fee is RMB 60 per person, with the same rate for adults and children. Faculty and staff may choose automatic payment through the campus card or cash payment in the reservation system. Those choosing cash payment must follow the notice’s bank-payment procedure and exchange the receipt for event tickets at the Labor Union; the cash-payment rate is RMB 70 per person.

Reservations may be canceled through the system without charge during the reservation period. No new reservations or cancellations will be accepted after the system closes, and tickets cannot be refunded after payment. Failure to complete cash payment on time or a failed campus-card charge due to insufficient balance will be treated as a withdrawal. If attendance exceeds the venue’s capacity, priority will be given to employees’ spouses and children.

Children must be accompanied by at least one parent. Parents are responsible for supervising children, following staff directions, and keeping personal belongings secure. The university will not arrange transportation; participants should travel independently and observe traffic safety.
