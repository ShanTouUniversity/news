STU Human Resources Office will hold a meeting on performance reviews for the 2011–2012 academic year to coordinate related work. The meeting is scheduled for 4:30 p.m. on Thursday, December 13, 2012, in the multifunction room of New Administration Center Room 330.

Heads of main-campus units and office directors or equivalent representatives are expected to attend on time.
