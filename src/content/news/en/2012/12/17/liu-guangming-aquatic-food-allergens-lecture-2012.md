STU School of Science will host a Science Salon lecture from 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. on Tuesday, December 18, 2012. Dr. Liu Guangming, professor and deputy dean of Jimei University’s School of Bioengineering, will speak on “Research on Aquatic-Food Allergens.” The lecture will take place in Science Building Room 205, and interested faculty and students are welcome.

The presentation will examine allergens in aquatic foods and related food-safety research. Liu’s long-term research focuses on applied basic research in marine-food processing and safety. He has published 62 papers in Chinese and international core journals, including 30 indexed by SCI, and holds four nationally authorized patents and five provincial- or ministerial-level science and technology awards.

Liu received his PhD from Xiamen University’s School of Life Sciences in 2003. He subsequently conducted postdoctoral research at the Basic Medical Sciences postdoctoral station of STU Medical College and later served as a visiting scholar at the U.S. Department of Agriculture’s Southern Regional Research Center and National Taiwan University.
