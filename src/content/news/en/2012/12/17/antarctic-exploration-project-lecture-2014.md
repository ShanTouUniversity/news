To help faculty and students learn about STU’s 2014 Antarctic expedition project, the Student Affairs Office will hold a project-information lecture titled “Antarctica Revealed” from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Thursday, December 20, 2012. The lecture will take place in E Staircase Lecture Room 203 and is open to the STU community.

The speakers will be Chen Jia Jun and Miriam Lee of project partner Hong Kong Discovery. Chen is the company’s founder, while Lee oversees projects and publications. They will draw on their experience with outdoor and ecological-exploration projects to introduce the Antarctic expedition.

### Lecture topics

| Topic | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Project design | How to design a meaningful Antarctic expedition project |
| Antarctic field activities | Who is in Antarctica and what they do there |
| Health guidance | Health and safety considerations for Antarctic expeditions |
| Conduct in Antarctica | Activities that are permitted or discouraged during an expedition |

Hong Kong Discovery has worked on projects related to natural and cultural heritage, ecotourism, and outdoor exploration. The lecture will help attendees understand the expedition from the perspectives of project planning, environmental awareness, and safety preparation. The notice does not provide a detailed itinerary or registration process; follow later arrangements issued by Student Affairs Office and the project partner.
