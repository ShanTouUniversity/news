As of December 17, 2012, some student residences had not paid all utility charges for November 2012 and earlier. Under university rules, residences with unpaid charges will be disconnected from December 18 until all outstanding charges are paid, after which power will be restored.

From December 18 through 20, the Network Center will process two daily deductions through the campus one-card system. The first deduction will take place at 10:30 a.m.; residences with successful payment are expected to have power restored from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. The second deduction will take place at 4:30 p.m.; residences with successful payment are expected to have power restored from 5:30 to 8:00 p.m. Residences paying after 4:30 p.m. will be scheduled for restoration from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. the following day.

After December 20, one deduction will be processed daily at 4:30 p.m. Students using the one-card system should add funds in advance. Those with insufficient bank-account balances should replenish them during banking hours and complete the required account-loading procedure. If a one-card is lost, students may use the water-and-electricity management system to set up an authorized proxy-payment method.

| Date | Deduction schedule | Restoration schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| December 18–20 | Two deductions daily, at 10:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. | Successful 10:30 a.m. payment: 2:30–4:30 p.m.; successful 4:30 p.m. payment: 5:30–8:00 p.m. |
| Payment after 4:30 p.m., December 18–20 | Included in the next day’s deduction and restoration cycle | 2:30–4:30 p.m. the following day |
| After December 20 | One deduction daily at 4:30 p.m. | According to the restoration schedule after successful payment |
