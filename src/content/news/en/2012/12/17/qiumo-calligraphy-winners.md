It is reported that the "Qiumo Cup" campus calligraphy and painting competition — one of the student club project activities for the fall semester of the 2012–2013 academic year — has concluded. The Communist Youth League Committee of STU, together with the Student Clubs Federation and the Calligraphy and Painting Association, has announced the results.

## Brush Calligraphy

| Award | Winners |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | Lin YX (2011 Landscape Architecture, graduate student); Chen YW (2012 Journalism) |
| Second prize | Cui JL (2011 Mechatronics); Wu ZL (2012 Journalism); Xie XN (2012 Chinese) |
| Third prize | Zhang YJ (2010 Landscape Architecture, graduate student); Huang SC (2012 Clinical Medicine); Yu HF (2012 Electronic Engineering); Li KG (2009 Biology) |
| Excellence | Li L (2012 Clinical Medicine); Wu YL (2012 Biology, graduate student); Kong CX (2012 Electronic Engineering); Chen LJ (2012 Clinical Medicine) |

## Pen Calligraphy

| Third prize | Zhu ZQ (2011 Civil Engineering); Wu M (2012 English); Li SZ (2012 Clinical Medicine) |
| Excellence | Zheng XY (2011 Biology); Yu HF (2012 Electronic Engineering); Liu J (2012 Chinese); Yu GF (2012 Clinical Medicine) |

## Painting

| Award | Winners |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | Xu WS (2011 Fine Arts, graduate student); Ren KN (2011 Fine Arts, graduate student) |
| Second prize | Zheng JB (2011 Fine Arts, graduate student); Qiu JS (2011 Fine Arts, graduate student); Fang CY (2009 Landscape Architecture, graduate student) |
| Third prize | Chen B (2011 Art); Cai SZ (2012 Art); He XF (2010 Art) |
| Excellence | Chen XY (2011 Landscape Architecture, graduate student); Ma XT (2011 Journalism); Lin ZF (2012 Business Administration); He S (2011 Art); Li X (2010 Art) |

The organizers congratulated the winners and thanked all faculty and students for their support and participation. The competition was among the semester's funded student club projects and drew participants from colleges and year groups across the university.
