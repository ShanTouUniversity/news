For operational needs, STU’s Student Community Management Service Center under the Resource Management Office is recruiting two administrators from the public. The positions involve rotating 24-hour shifts and responsibility for duty and patrol work in student residential areas, facility inspections and repair follow-up, greenery and cleaning checks, student consultations and visitor reception, and response to emergencies in the residences.

### Requirements and employment

Applicants should have good professional ethics, a strong sense of responsibility, teamwork skills, and the ability to work rotating 24-hour shifts. Applicants should hold a junior college diploma in property management or hotel management, or have graduated from a technical and vocational institution in electrical work with at least an intermediate technician qualification. The positions will be filled as auxiliary contract roles. The employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow relevant university standards.

Applicants must send a résumé and relevant educational, qualification, and identity-document materials to the Student Community Management Service Center by email or mail by January 10, 2013. Candidates who pass the preliminary review will be notified separately about written tests and interviews. Materials will be used only for this recruitment and will not be returned; applicants should retain their own copies.
