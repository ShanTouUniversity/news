Under the *Measures for Probation-Completion Assessment and Promotion Appointments of Management Personnel* (STU Party Committee Document No. 7 of 2011), STU will conduct probation-completion reviews for personnel appointed to senior and mid-level management posts since July 2011. Democratic assessments will be conducted during the review period.

To make the review process more transparent, the work reports of the relevant management personnel will be displayed on STU’s internal office-information network. Units and individuals with comments may submit written statements to the university Party Organization and United Front Department by December 24, 2012.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Review group | Personnel appointed to senior and mid-level management posts since July 2011 |
| Review | Probation-completion assessment, democratic assessment, and work reports |
| Report display | STU internal office-information network |
| Comment deadline | December 24, 2012 |
| Receiving office | STU Party Organization and United Front Department |
| Phone | 82902345 |
| Email | `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` |

Statements submitted by units must bear the unit’s official seal. Individual statements must be signed with the submitter’s real name for accountability. Consult the internal office-information network for the detailed work reports.

> Source: STU OA preview notice (Party Organization and United Front Department)
>
> ⚠️ The work reports contain management personnel’s work histories and internal work information. The attachments are not reproduced here; consult the internal office-information network for the displayed materials.
