STU Personnel Office has published a pre-appointment notice proposing Hong Mengdian, Lin Yu, Chen Ying, and Yao Shaoyang for mid-level management posts. The proposal followed deliberation at the 15th University Leadership Meeting on December 14, 2012.

The publicity period is conducted under the *Trial Measures for the Appointment of Management Cadres of Shantou University* (STU Party Committee Document No. 47 of 2009). Units and individuals with comments may submit written statements to the Personnel Office by December 23, 2012. Statements from units must bear an official seal, while individual statements must be signed with the submitter’s real name.
