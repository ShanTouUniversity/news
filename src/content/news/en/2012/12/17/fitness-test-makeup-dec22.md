It is reported that the unified fitness tests for first- and second-year students (Classes of 2011 and 2012) at Shantou University were completed on November 24–25, 2012. According to the Physical Education Department, notices were posted on the university's internal office network beforehand, and emails were sent to every student in those two cohorts; the vast majority of students completed all six test items on time as required, while students excused for health reasons submitted their exemption forms to the department punctually.

However, a number of students from the Classes of 2009 and 2010 have yet to be tested. These students are required to attend a makeup session on the afternoon of Saturday, December 22 (week 14), with schools arriving in batches starting from 2 p.m. The list of students required to attend and their specific testing times can be found in the makeup list published by the department.
