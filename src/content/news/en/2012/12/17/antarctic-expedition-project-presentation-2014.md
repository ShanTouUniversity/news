STU will hold a presentation on its 2014 Antarctic expedition project from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 19, 2012, in the Science Lecture Hall. The session will help faculty and students learn about the project and subsequent selection arrangements.

With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, STU has organized advanced outdoor programs since 2009, including orienteering, an outdoor survival camp in Nan’ao, an advanced Shangri-La training camp, and expeditions in high-altitude and western regions. The university describes these programs as an exploration of whole-person education and innovative talent development. They use real-world settings to develop participants’ planning, communication, collaboration, leadership, and resilience, while encouraging reflection on nature, life, and culture.

STU plans to conduct an Antarctic expedition project in 2014. The presentation will introduce the project and give faculty and students interested in the selection process an opportunity to learn more. The notice does not specify eligibility, itinerary, or costs; follow the formal arrangements subsequently issued by Student Affairs Office.
