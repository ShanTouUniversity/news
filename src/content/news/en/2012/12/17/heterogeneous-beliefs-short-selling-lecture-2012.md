STU Business School will hold its 11th academic lecture of 2012 from 10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on Friday, December 21. Dr. Xu Feng, a postdoctoral researcher at Tsinghua University’s School of Public Policy and Institute of Contemporary China Studies, will speak on “The Effects of Heterogeneous Beliefs and Short-Sale Constraints on Securities Issuance.” Professor Du Mian will chair the lecture. It will be held in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of the West Liberal Arts Building, and all faculty and students are welcome.

The presentation will examine how investor characteristics, particularly differences in investors’ views of markets and firm value, affect corporate financing. Xu will introduce a financial model that analyzes how heterogeneous investor beliefs and short-sale institutions jointly shape corporate securities-issuance decisions.

According to the lecture overview, heterogeneous beliefs may have either a positive or a negative effect on equity issuance. The positive effect of short-sale constraints on equity issuance, meanwhile, depends on specific conditions. The research combines macro-level institutional change with micro-level investor behavior to examine corporate decision-making.

Xu is a postdoctoral researcher at Tsinghua University’s School of Public Policy and an assistant researcher at the Institute of Contemporary China Studies. His research focuses on behavioral finance and corporate investment and financing. His work has appeared in *Economic Modelling*, *Science Research Management*, *Chinese Journal of Management Science*, *Economic Science*, and *Management Review*.

The lecture is also part of the continuing-education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice II”; students enrolled in that course are expected to attend.
