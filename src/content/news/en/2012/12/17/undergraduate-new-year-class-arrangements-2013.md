Following the New Year holiday schedule issued by STU Party and Administration Office, Academic Affairs Office has announced class adjustments for undergraduates. During the 2013 New Year period, selected weekday and weekend classes will be exchanged as follows:

| Date | Teaching arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Tuesday, January 1, 2013 | Classes suspended |
| Wednesday, January 2, 2013 | Classes exchanged with those scheduled for Saturday, January 5 |
| Thursday, January 3, 2013 | Classes exchanged with those scheduled for Sunday, January 6 |

Schools and departments should notify the relevant undergraduates and organize teaching according to the adjusted dates. Students should confirm the exact class time with their school and course schedule.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Applies to | Undergraduate classes |
| Suspension date | January 1, 2013 |
| Exchange dates | January 2 with January 5; January 3 with January 6 |
| Issuing unit | STU Academic Affairs Office |
