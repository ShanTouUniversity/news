STU Labor Union will hold a meeting at 4:00 p.m. on Tuesday, December 18, 2012, in the second-floor meeting room of the STU Labor Union. Participants will include heads of Faculty Congress delegations, members of the university labor-union committee, and chairs of departmental labor unions.

Those concerned should attend on time.
