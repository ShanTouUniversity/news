STU Labor Union has announced that the faculty and staff New Year fun sports meet scheduled for December 26, 2012, will include an on-site prize draw. All faculty and staff who registered for a fun-sports event may participate.

The draw will offer 11 prizes in two categories. One grand-prize winner will receive a Sakura gas water heater, model 07P15, listed in the notice at a market value of RMB 2,100. Ten lucky-prize winners will each receive a Sakura electric kitchen water heater, model 0632U, listed at RMB 710 per unit. The prizes are sponsored by the Shantou Sakura Kitchen and Bath Life Experience Center.

| Prize | Number | Award | Value listed in notice |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Grand prize | 1 | Sakura gas water heater, model 07P15 | RMB 2,100 per unit |
| Lucky prize | 10 | Sakura electric kitchen water heater, model 0632U | RMB 710 per unit |
