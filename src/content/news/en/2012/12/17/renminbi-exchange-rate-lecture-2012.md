STU Business School will hold its 12th academic lecture of 2012 from 10:00 to 11:00 a.m. on Thursday, December 20. Professor Zhang Zhibai of Zhaoqing University will speak on “Undervaluation and Appreciation of the RMB.” Professor Du Mian will chair the event. The lecture will take place in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor of the West Liberal Arts Building, and all faculty and students are welcome.

The presentation will first use econometric methods to measure the extent to which the RMB’s real exchange rate against the U.S. dollar is undervalued. Drawing on countries and regions that experienced rapid economic growth and appreciation of their real exchange rates, it will then examine an appropriate pace for RMB appreciation. Zhang will also compare possible routes for appreciation, considering the interests of China and the United States and asking whether adjustment should occur through the nominal exchange rate or through rising prices.

The lecture is also part of the continuing-education course “Modern Economic Management Theory and Practice II”; students enrolled in that course are expected to attend.
