STU Affiliated High School will host a lecture on earthquake preparedness from 4:15 to 5:15 p.m. on Wednesday, December 19, 2012. Chen Junhui, a senior engineer at the Shantou Earthquake Administration, will speak. The lecture will take place in E Staircase Lecture Room 201, and faculty, students, and parents are welcome to attend.

The session will provide public-safety education on earthquakes, disaster prevention, and emergency response, helping participants understand basic precautions and evacuation practices.
