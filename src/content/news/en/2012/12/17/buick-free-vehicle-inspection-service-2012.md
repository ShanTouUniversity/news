STU University Union has announced that Shantou Jianwei Buick will provide free vehicle inspection services on campus from 1:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, December 19, 2012. The service will be offered at the STU employee parking lot beside E Building.

The activity is open to Buick and other-brand vehicles on campus, including official and private vehicles. For Shanghai GM Buick vehicles, the service will include computer diagnostics, refilling windshield-washer and radiator water, four-door lubrication, and checks of oil, fluid, electrical, air-conditioning, braking, routine-maintenance, and wiring systems. Other-brand vehicles may also participate; the attachment notes that gifts are available in limited quantities and subject to on-site arrangements.

Vehicle owners may bring their vehicles for inspection according to their needs and should follow on-site parking and safety arrangements.
