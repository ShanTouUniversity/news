STU Academic Affairs Office has announced registration arrangements for the first-half 2013 National Computer Rank Examination. Written examinations will begin at 9:00 a.m. on March 30, 2013, while computer-based examinations will be conducted in batches starting on March 30.

Applicants may choose a level according to their computer-study and computer-use experience, but may register for only one level in each examination session. The examination has no restrictions based on age, occupation, educational background, or household registration.

### Registration

Registration will be open from December 24 through 29, 2012, at the Student Affairs Window of the Academic Affairs Office on the third floor of the New Administration Center at STU. Applicants must register in person for electronic photography, pay the examination fee, and sign the registration-information confirmation and examination-integrity pledge. Eligible candidates with a pass in only one component of the previous examination may register for the component they did not pass by presenting the previous score report as required.

### Levels and formats

| Level | Subjects or categories | Format and duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | MS Office | Computer-based examination, 90 minutes |
| Level 2 | C, Visual Basic, Visual FoxPro, Java, Access, and C++ | Paperless examination for new candidates, 120 minutes; 40 points for multiple-choice questions and 60 for practical tasks, with 60/100 as the passing score |
| Level 3 | PC Technology, Information Management Technology, Network Technology, and Database Technology | 120-minute written examination followed by a 60-minute computer-based examination |
| Level 4 | Network Engineer, Database Engineer, and Software Test Engineer | Written examination, 120 minutes |

Candidates retaking Level 2 C, Java, C++, or Delphi will continue to use the traditional format, with 90 minutes for the written examination and 90 minutes for the computer-based examination. Candidates who do not obtain a passing certificate in the paperless examination must register as new candidates at the next examination; no separate retake will be arranged.

### Fees

Under Guangdong Provincial Price Bureau Letter No. 470 of 2007, the full examination fee for Levels 1 through 4 is RMB 137. The written-only fee for Levels 2 and 3 is RMB 66 and the computer-based-only fee is RMB 71. The computer-based-only fee for Level 1 and the written-only fee for Level 4 are both RMB 137.
