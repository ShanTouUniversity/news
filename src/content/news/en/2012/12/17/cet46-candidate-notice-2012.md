The second-half 2012 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 examinations will be held on Saturday, December 22. STU Academic Affairs Office reminds candidates to study the Guangdong examination rules and candidate requirements in advance, prepare the required documents, stationery, and headphones, and observe examination rules.

The Band 4 examination will run from 9:00 to 11:20 a.m., and candidates should enter by 8:45 a.m. The Band 6 examination will run from 3:00 to 5:20 p.m., and candidates should enter by 2:45 p.m. Candidates arriving after the examination begins, at 9:00 a.m. or 3:00 p.m., will not be admitted. Candidates may not leave during the examination or submit their papers early.

### Documents and entry

Candidates must bring their admission permit, student card, and identity card, and place the documents in the upper-left corner of their desks for inspection. A campus one-card cannot replace a student card. Candidates who have lost or forgotten a required document must follow the notice’s procedure and obtain a statement signed by an academic administrator and stamped by their school or department. All candidates will undergo security checks.

Candidates should bring headphones, batteries, a 2B pencil, a black ink pen, and an eraser. Books, paper, bags, recording equipment, electronic organizers, MP3 players, mobile phones, and other items may not be brought into the examination room; items already brought in must be handed to invigilators for safekeeping. Candidates must remove their headphones when the listening section ends.

Violations will be handled under examination regulations and relevant university rules.
