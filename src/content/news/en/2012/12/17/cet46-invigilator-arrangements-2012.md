The second-half 2012 National College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 examinations will be held on Saturday, December 22. STU Academic Affairs Office has asked the schools on the main campus to ensure that invigilators study the examination rules, operating procedures, and emergency procedures in advance and attend pre-examination training.

The Band 4 examination will run from 9:00 to 11:20 a.m., and the Band 6 examination from 3:00 to 5:20 p.m. on December 22. Invigilation sessions and specific examination rooms will be announced on the day of the examination. Academic Affairs Office will issue the invigilator roster through the attachments.

### Invigilation arrangements

Invigilators must study the Guangdong rules for CET-4 and CET-6 invigilators and candidates, the Band 4 and Band 6 operating procedures and examination-room record forms, the procedures for handling examination-room incidents, and the classification of candidate violations and cheating by December 19.

Following the requirements of the Guangdong examination authority, the testing center will conduct security checks on all invigilators. Invigilators may not bring mobile phones or other communication devices into examination rooms while on duty. Band 4 invigilators must report to E Staircase Lecture Room 101 at 7:45 a.m. on the examination day for training and paper collection, and return the papers there for counting after the examination. Band 6 invigilators must complete the same procedures at 1:40 p.m.

Candidates should follow their admission permits and examination instructions. Invigilators must enforce the examination schedule and room rules and report and handle incidents involving papers, answer sheets, or examination-room order according to the prescribed procedures.
