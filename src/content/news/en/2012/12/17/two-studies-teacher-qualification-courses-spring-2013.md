STU plans to offer training courses in Education and Psychology during the spring 2013 semester for members of the 2013 and 2014 graduating classes, including undergraduates and graduate students preparing to apply for teacher qualification certification. Undergraduate course selections will be entered into the credit-based system and counted as general-education courses; graduate students and Medical College students must register in person as specified.

Each course carries two credits and 36 class hours. Selection priority this semester will be given to students graduating in 2013. Students who pass the examinations may later apply for the Certificate of Completion for Education and Psychology Training for Guangdong Teacher Qualification Certification, issued by the Guangdong Teacher Qualification Certification Office. The certificate is a required document for applying for teacher qualification and is valid throughout Guangdong Province.

### Registration

Undergraduates preparing to apply for teacher qualification certification should log in to the credit-based system during the designated spring-semester course-selection period. The exact dates will be given in the course-selection notice. Graduate students and Medical College students must bring their identity cards and register at the Academic Affairs service window on the third floor of the New Administration Center between 9:00 a.m. on December 17, 2012, and 5:00 p.m. on January 9, 2013, excluding weekends and public holidays. They must confirm the class section they intend to take.

Registration for the two courses will close after January 9, 2013. The relevant class sections will also be closed in the credit-based system, and add-or-drop requests will no longer be accepted. Students must purchase the textbooks themselves: *Fundamentals of Pedagogy* (Education Science Press, 2008) and *Contemporary Educational Psychology* (Beijing Normal University Press, January 2005).
