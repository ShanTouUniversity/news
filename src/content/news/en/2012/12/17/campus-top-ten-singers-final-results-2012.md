After preliminary and final rounds, organizers announced the results of the “M-Zone” 20th STU Campus Top Ten Singers Competition on December 17, 2012. Lai JZ of the Graduate School won the championship, Ding QH of the School of Journalism and Communication was runner-up, and Mou DM of the Medical College placed third.

Three special awards were also presented: Jiang XH of the School of Science received Best Costume, Lai JZ of the Graduate School received Best Stage Presence, and Xu L of the School of Engineering received the Most Popular Contestant award.

### Main awards

| Award | School | Recipient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Champion | Graduate School | Lai JZ |
| Runner-up | School of Journalism and Communication | Ding QH |
| Third place | Medical College | Mou DM |
| Best Costume | School of Science | Jiang XH |
| Best Stage Presence | Graduate School | Lai JZ |
| Most Popular Contestant | School of Engineering | Xu L |

The contestants receiving the “2012 Campus Top Ten Singers” title were Xu L, Li JH, Lai JZ, Mou DM, Qing SQ, Jiang XH, Ding QH, Luo RY, Chen J, and Li Y.
