STU Library is offering faculty and students trial access to the Bokan humanities and social-sciences journal database. The library says the platform contains more than 3,600 Chinese periodical and magazine titles and over 40,000 issues, with continuing updates. New issues are generally added around the time their print editions are released, while back issues remain available.

The database covers current affairs and news, digests and literature, arts and sports, management and business, culture and the arts, science and technology, and education. Examples listed by the library include *Banyuetan*, *China Newsweek*, *Beijing Literature*, *China Entrepreneur*, *World Expo*, *The Friend of Science*, and *China Teacher*. Readers can choose among original-page multimedia, electronic, audio, and downloadable versions where available.

The library welcomes faculty and students to use the database during the trial and send usage feedback to the Subject Services Department. The notice does not specify an end date for the trial; consult the library’s database details page for current access status.
