Shantou University announced the evaluation results for papers and research reports submitted to its 2012 annual meeting on Party building and ideological and political education. The expert panel reviewed 29 submissions and selected 14 award-winning papers: three first prizes, six second prizes, and five third prizes.

### First prizes (3)

- “The Leading Role of University Party Building in Tripartite Co-construction”
- “Tripartite Co-construction as Collaborative Innovation in University Management Mechanisms”
- “Reflections on Grassroots Party Organization Building in Universities under Tripartite Co-construction”

### Second prizes (6)

- “An Analysis of the Role of Patient Questionnaires Conducted by a Student Medical Ethics and Professional Conduct Publicity Team”
- “The Emotional Foundation and Practical Significance of Shantou University Campus Culture”
- “The Resource Effects of Tripartite Co-construction from Three Dimensions”
- “Path Innovation in University News Publicity in the New-Media Environment”
- “Reflections on Developing Online Party Building at Shantou University”
- “A Study of Shantou University’s Strategic Planning Based on Tripartite Co-construction”

### Third prizes (5)

- “Media Responses to University Emergencies under the New Conditions of Tripartite Co-construction: Lessons from the University of Hong Kong’s Public-Relations Concept”
- “New Reflections on Student Management under Sino-Foreign Cooperative Education: The Case of Shantou University”
- “New Reflections Based on Tripartite Co-construction: Opportunities and Challenges Facing Shantou University under New Conditions”
- “Exploring Standardized Management of University Student Party Branches: New Reflections Based on Tripartite Co-construction”
- “An Analysis of Graduate Student Party Branch Development Based on the Tripartite Co-construction Platform”

To protect personal information that may concern students, this article reports the award totals and paper titles but does not reproduce the author list from the original notice.
