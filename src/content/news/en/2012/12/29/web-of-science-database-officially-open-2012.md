The Shantou University Library announced that the Web of Science database, including the Science Citation Index (SCI), Social Sciences Citation Index (SSCI), and Arts & Humanities Citation Index (A&HCI), is now officially available on campus. Faculty and students can access the academic resources through the library’s database page.

Access point: [STU Library database details](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=317).
