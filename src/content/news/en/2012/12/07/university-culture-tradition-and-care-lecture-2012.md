STU’s Institute of Higher Education and Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment will host a lecture on December 13, 2012, titled “University Culture: Upholding Tradition and Treating the University with Care.” Professor Zhang Yingqiang, dean of the Institute of Education Sciences at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, will discuss tradition, institutional development, and educational values in university culture.

The lecture will be held from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. in Science Building Room 205 and is open to the STU community.

Zhang is dean and professor at the Institute of Education Sciences at Huazhong University of Science and Technology and a doctoral supervisor in higher education. He also directs the university’s Institute of Higher Education and serves as deputy editor-in-chief of the journal *Research in Higher Education*. He receives a special government allowance from the State Council, was selected for the Ministry of Education’s New Century Excellent Talents Support Program in 2004 and Hubei’s New Century High-Level Talent Project in 2005, and is a specially appointed professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology.
