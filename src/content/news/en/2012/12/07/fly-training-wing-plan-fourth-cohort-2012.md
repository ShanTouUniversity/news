STU Youth League Committee and Students’ Union have announced the fourth “Try Your Wings” cohort of the FLY training program, selecting 30 department heads from student organizations. FLY stands for “Form Leadership of Youth” and uses the slogan “Fly, I Can.” The program aims to develop students’ organizational, execution, decision-making, learning, creative, communication, and teamwork abilities.

FLY has three tracks for different participants: Fengyi for beginners, Shiyi (“Try Your Wings”) for intermediate participants, and Zhanyi for advanced participants. The current intermediate track is designed for department-level leaders in student organizations. It will use five modules—time management, creative thinking, team management, human-resources management, and project management—to strengthen participants’ management, planning, and team-coordination skills.

The program emphasizes four standards: character, competence, courage, and communication. Lectures, workshops, team training, group discussion, presentations, and outdoor practice will connect theory with practical tasks.
