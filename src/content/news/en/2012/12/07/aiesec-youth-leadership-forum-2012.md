The STU AIESEC Youth Leadership Forum will be held from 2:30 to 4:00 p.m. on December 9, 2012, in ACC Hall 3. Project advisers including Wang Jiangfeng, deputy secretary of the Shantou Youth League Committee, and Fang Chaowu, a senior manager at China Unicom’s Shantou branch, will exchange views with students on youth-leadership development and international volunteer practice.

Guided by the STU Business School, the STU AIESEC team was established on June 18, 2012. According to the event introduction, AIESEC operates in 107 countries and regions and provides young people with opportunities for overseas internships, leadership practice, and participation in an international learning network.

The introduction states that during the 2012 summer, the STU AIESEC team sent seven students to Tunisia, India, Kenya, Cambodia, and Ghana for international volunteer projects in education, HIV/AIDS prevention, finance, and related areas. More than 10 STU students were expected to take part in overseas volunteer projects during the winter vacation. The team was also preparing domestic projects, with plans to recruit overseas volunteers for domestic programs in summer 2013.

The forum is open to students across the university and will introduce AIESEC’s leadership development, overseas practice, and volunteer-service opportunities.
