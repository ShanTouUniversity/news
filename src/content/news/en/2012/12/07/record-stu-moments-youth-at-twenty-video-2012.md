The second video in STU’s Record STU Moments series, *About Youth, Your Twenties*, has been released. The production records STU students’ understanding of youth and pursuit of their dreams, presenting campus moments in which students express youthful energy through singing, dancing, commitment, and volunteer action.

Using the line “Life is not long, and youth is brief. Everyone should have one performance true to themselves” as a central idea, the video presents individual choices and shared memories from campus life. It also pays tribute to the 20th anniversary of STU’s Top Ten Singers competition.
