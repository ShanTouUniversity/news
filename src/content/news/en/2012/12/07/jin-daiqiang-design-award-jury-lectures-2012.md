The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Jin Daiqiang Design Award Organizing Committee will host lectures by the 2012 award’s final-round jurors at STU on December 9, 2012. Designers Bi Xuefeng and Lu Zhichang will present “Before Today” and “Encounters between Life and Cultural Spaces,” respectively.

The two lectures will run from 7:30 to 10:00 p.m. in the New Library Lecture Hall and will be open to students, faculty, and members of the public interested in graphic design, book design, and cultural spaces.

| Speaker | Lecture | Main focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bi Xuefeng | Before Today | Graphic-design practice, visual art, and design creation |
| Lu Zhichang | Encounters between Life and Cultural Spaces | Book design, publishing, and observations of cultural spaces |

Bi is a member of the Alliance Graphique Internationale (AGI), former chair of the Shenzhen Graphic Design Association, and a member of the Visual Arts Committee of the Chinese National Academy of Arts. He has served as a visiting professor at several art and design schools. After graduating from the China Academy of Art in 1989, he founded Shenzhen Yanwen Design Co., Ltd. in 1997 and established Bi Xuefeng Design Consultancy in 2007. His work has received more than 100 domestic and international design awards and has been collected by art museums in China and abroad.

Born in Hong Kong, Lu is also an AGI member. He graduated with honors from the graphic-design program at the Hong Kong Polytechnic University in 1988, worked in book design, and studied printmaking in Paris. He has lived in Beijing since 2000, working in book design and publishing strategy. Books he has designed have received more than 40 awards.
