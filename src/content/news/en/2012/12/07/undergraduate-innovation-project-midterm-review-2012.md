STU Academic Affairs Office will conduct midterm reviews of selected undergraduate innovation projects approved in 2011 with one-and-a-half- or two-year implementation periods. The review will examine research progress, interim results, expenditure, and next steps, and will provide a basis for later completion evaluation.

Project teams must submit the midterm review form, project progress log, and materials documenting interim results to the Academic Affairs service counter on the third floor of the New Administration Center by January 4, 2013. Two paper copies are required, along with an electronic version sent to `wtyang@stu.edu.cn`.

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Review subjects | 2011 projects with one-and-a-half- or two-year implementation periods |
| Materials | Midterm review form, project progress log, and interim-results evidence |
| Results evidence | Papers, research reports, prototypes, or other results, with photographs and written descriptions where appropriate |
| Deadline | January 4, 2013 |
| Paper submission | Two copies to the Academic Affairs service counter, third floor, New Administration Center |
| Electronic submission | wtyang@stu.edu.cn |
| Responsible office | STU Academic Affairs Office |

The midterm review form records research progress, interim results, existing problems and possible solutions, next-stage tasks and schedules, and expenditure. The project progress log records experiments, research data, problem discussions, reflections, and project-team meetings.

Midterm results will serve as an important basis for completion evaluation. Projects that do not pass must complete corrective work by the specified deadline. Their written plans must be submitted to the General Affairs Section of Academic Affairs, and the projects may continue and use the second-stage project funds only after passing expert review. Projects that fail to meet the requirements after correction will be terminated.

Project questions may be directed to Academic Affairs at 86502736. Teams should conduct their self-review as required and submit accurate materials to support subsequent research and completion assessment.
