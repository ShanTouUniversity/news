It is reported that the Youth League Committee, the Student Societies Federation and the Table Tennis Association issued a supplementary notice on December 7, 2012, that the 15th Table Tennis Individual Open will be held as part of the 2012–2013 fall semester student societies program.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 15th Table Tennis Individual Open |
| Time | December 8–9, 2012 |
| Venue | STU Gymnasium |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Student Societies Federation, Table Tennis Association |

Details are available in the OA attachment. Participants are asked to prepare and attend on time, and all faculty and students are welcome to watch.

> ⚠️ Details are available on the OA system.
>
