STU’s Institute of Higher Education and Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment will host a lecture on December 12, 2012, titled “New Directions in University Teaching Reform.” Professor Bie Dunrong of the Institute of Education at Xiamen University will discuss teaching reform, higher-education administration, and educational assessment.

The lecture will be held from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. in Meeting Room 330 of the New Administration Building and is open to the STU community.

| Audience | All STU students and faculty |

Bie holds a doctorate in education and is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Xiamen University’s Institute of Education. He serves as a standing council member of several specialist bodies under the Chinese Association of Higher Education, a council member of the National Association for Higher Education Teaching Research, a visiting professor at several universities, and an editorial-board member of education journals. He has led or participated in development-strategy planning and consulting for more than 30 universities. His research covers higher-education theory and administration, university strategy and planning, and university teaching and assessment.
