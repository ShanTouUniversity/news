To maintain a clean and orderly campus, the STU Publicity Department and Resources Management Office have asked units to remove outdated or damaged banners and flags displayed around campus ahead of upcoming important activities.

Units must remove the materials themselves by 5:00 p.m. on Sunday, December 9, 2012. Promotional materials left after the deadline will be cleared by the university.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | Outdated or damaged banners and flags |
| Responsible parties | Units responsible for the displayed materials |
| Self-removal deadline | 5:00 p.m., December 9, 2012 |
| After the deadline | Cleared by the university |
| Issuing offices | Publicity Department and Resources Management Office |

The university asked all units to inspect and remove their materials in order to help maintain the campus environment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department and Resources Management Office)
