The China Women’s Studies Association and the STU Women’s Studies Center will hold the 2012 Young Scholars Forum on Women’s and Gender Studies from December 15 to 16, 2012. The forum will be themed “Social Transformation and Gender Equality in the Context of Globalization” and will bring together young researchers from across China to exchange views on theory, methodology, and the social application of women’s and gender studies.

The one-and-a-half-day forum will run from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on December 15 and from 8:30 a.m. to noon on December 16. It will be held in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC) at STU, with Chinese as the working language.

Participants will include authors who received the China Women’s Studies Association’s fourth awards for outstanding doctoral and master’s theses in women’s and gender studies; young scholars funded under the 2012–2014 empirical research program on women’s and gender studies; young disciplinary leaders who submit conference papers; and young faculty members, graduate students, and undergraduates from STU.

Discussions will address women’s participation in politics, employment, education, and health, as well as gender and culture, online media, organic living, legal reform, marriage, and family. The program will present the association’s fourth doctoral and master’s thesis awards, review the 2012–2014 empirical research projects, and discuss how young scholars can build networks, support one another, and advance gender research and learning.

The forum aims to encourage new research questions and areas of focus among young scholars and strengthen the practical value and social influence of women’s and gender studies.
