The 2012–2013 Guangdong University Youth League Work Research Project call is open to Youth League officials, counselors, young faculty members, current students, and university researchers working at institutions in Guangdong. The STU Youth League Committee has circulated the call and invited interested individuals and teams to apply.

The program aims to implement the spirit of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, advance university Youth League work under new conditions, and strengthen the use of contemporary ideas to analyze and solve problems in student and Youth League work. Applicants may work on topics in student ideological guidance, student development services, and Youth League and student-organization development. Original topics outside the guide may also be considered when they offer innovative value for university work.

| Project category | Number | Funding |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Key funded projects | 15 | RMB 5,000 each |
| General funded projects | 25 | RMB 3,000 each |
| Key and general self-funded projects | Determined through review | The applicant’s university provides funding at the corresponding level |

Funding for funded projects will be released in two installments: 70 percent after approval and the remaining 30 percent after completion review. The standard research period is six months. An extension may be granted in special circumstances, but may not exceed two months.

### Application requirements

Applicants may apply individually or form a team of no more than five people. Each independent applicant or unit may submit one project, and team members may not participate in two applications. Priority areas include:

- Student ideological guidance, including student attitudes, new-media engagement, the Young Marxists training program, and civic education;
- Student development services, including academic innovation, culture and arts, social practice, employment and entrepreneurship, mental health, and volunteer service;
- Youth League and student-organization development, including Party-led Youth League development, grassroots organizations, Students’ Unions, student societies, Youth League staff development, and student-leader training.

Applicants must complete the project application form. Applications must first be reviewed by the applicant’s university Youth League Committee and submitted collectively; direct individual submissions are not accepted. The package includes the written application and the university’s consolidated project list, submitted in paper and electronic formats as required.

| Date | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| By December 24, 2012 | STU applicants send electronic materials to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn` and deliver paper materials to the STU Youth League Committee Office |
| By December 30, 2012 | University Youth League Committees submit reviewed applications collectively to the Guangdong Provincial Youth League School Department and send the electronic version |
| By the end of January 2013 | Approval and funding results announced |
| By the end of September 2013 | Universities consolidate and submit completion materials |

Approved projects must finish the research within the specified period and submit a completion application plus outputs such as papers, research reports, or monographs. The review group will allocate the remaining funding according to the completion results and may recognize, recommend, and compile outstanding research.
