Following the review and recommendation of the STU Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, Shantou Longhua Construction General Co. was named the winning bidder for the renovation project involving U-shaped Classrooms 203 and 204 in E Building. The bid amount is RMB 845,431.

The result is being publicized from December 7 through 11, 2012. Organizations or individuals with objections may contact the project owner or the designated complaint-handling office during the publicity period.
