STU Academic Affairs Office has begun completion reviews for selected undergraduate innovation projects. The review covers projects approved in 2010 with two-year implementation periods and projects approved in 2011 with one-year implementation periods. Evaluation will combine written-material review with a public defense.

Project teams must submit paper materials to the Academic Affairs service counter on the third floor of the New Administration Center by January 4, 2013. Three copies of every document are required, together with an electronic version matching the paper submission. Required materials include the STU Undergraduate Innovation Project Completion Report, the supervisor's work handbook, and evidence of results such as research reports, survey reports, patent applications, software development, physical prototypes, publications, or copies of relevant publications.

Each public defense will last 10 to 15 minutes, with approximately seven minutes for the project presentation. All project members must arrive 30 minutes early. The project leader must use a PowerPoint presentation and bring supporting materials such as prototypes, programs, photographs, or video. Teams requiring special equipment or rooms for demonstrations should notify Academic Affairs in advance.

Results will be classified as Outstanding, Good, Pass, or Fail. Projects rated Outstanding, Good, or Pass will receive two research-innovation credits, and completion results will be entered into the credit-based academic system. Projects needing an extension must submit a written extension application signed by the supervisor. Projects originally scheduled for completion in 2013 that seek early completion must also submit a written application.

For projects implemented inadequately, delayed without reasonable improvement measures, or involving improper use of funds, the university may stop allocating further research funding and recover part or all of the awarded funds depending on the circumstances. Project questions may be directed to Academic Affairs at 86502736.

## June 2013 Defense Arrangements

Academic Affairs subsequently scheduled selected 2010 and 2011 undergraduate innovation projects, together with selected 2012 undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects, for completion defenses on the afternoon of **June 9**. The attached schedule lists 62 projects: 45 innovation-experiment projects, 11 innovation-training projects, and 6 entrepreneurship-practice projects. Project leaders' and members' names are not reproduced here.

Each defense lasts 12 minutes, including a seven-minute project presentation and five minutes of questions from the panel. Each team must arrive **15 minutes early** to sign in and prepare supporting materials such as prototypes, programs, photographs, or video. Teams requiring special equipment or rooms should contact Academic Affairs in advance. Results will be classified as Outstanding, Good, Pass, or Fail. Projects rated Outstanding, Good, or Pass will receive two research-innovation credits; the completion result will be entered as a course grade in the credit-based academic system and included in GPA calculations.
