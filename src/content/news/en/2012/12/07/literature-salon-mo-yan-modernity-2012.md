The second academic salon of STU’s School of Liberal Arts will be held on December 12, 2012, under the theme “Mo Yan and Modernity in Chinese Literature.” Professor Gu Bin, an internationally recognized sinologist, writer, translator, and lecture professor at STU’s School of Liberal Arts, will deliver the keynote remarks and share his views on Mo Yan’s work and the development of Chinese literature.

Using Mo Yan’s Nobel Prize in Literature as a starting point, the salon will examine what the award means for the author, for modern and contemporary Chinese literature, and for the construction of modernity in Chinese literary culture. It will also consider how Mo Yan’s rural experience and writing respond to broader questions about the development of Chinese literature.

Discussion prompts will include what the Nobel Prize in Literature means for Mo Yan’s writing, whether the award will create new momentum for Chinese literature, and what Mo Yan’s work suggests for constructing modernity in Chinese literature. Mo Yan previously visited STU to lecture and was appointed a part-time professor and professional master’s supervisor at the School of Liberal Arts.

Professor Gu has followed and written critically about Mo Yan for many years and has maintained an academic relationship with him. The salon will combine a keynote presentation with discussion, inviting faculty and students to examine the topic through literary history, authorship, and theories of modernity.
