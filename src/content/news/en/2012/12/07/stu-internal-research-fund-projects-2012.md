STU Research Office has announced the approved projects for the university’s 2012 internal research funds. Following expert correspondence review, a vote by the Research Committee, and approval by the Academic Committee, 24 projects received support across four programs: academic innovation team development, national-fund cultivation, young-researcher research, and humanities and social-sciences research.

The program is intended to advance academic disciplines, strengthen faculty competitiveness, and help faculty members improve their research capacity and ability to compete for high-level research grants. The approved areas include comparisons of land acquisition and land-transfer policies affecting farmers in Guangdong and Taiwan, the effects of ocean acidification on phytoplankton communities, the synthesis and luminescence of photoactive coordination compounds, and Chaoshan local-culture studies.

| Fund category | Projects approved |
| --- | ---: |
| Academic Innovation Team Development | 3 |
| National-Fund Cultivation | 6 |
| Young-Researcher Research Fund | 5 |
| Humanities and Social-Sciences Research Fund | 10 |
| Total | 24 |

The Research Office asked project leaders to organize their research carefully, use research funds effectively, and ensure that projects progress as planned and meet quality requirements. Complete project titles, leaders, and host units are provided in the original OA attachment.
