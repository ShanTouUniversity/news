STU Discipline Inspection Commission Office has announced the results of the Discipline Education Month online essay activity and the second citywide Clean Reading Month book-review essay activity. Following expert review, 10 authors received excellence awards.

The winning works address integrity, ideals and convictions, and the development of an integrity culture. Several works respond to the film *Loyalty and Betrayal*, reflecting faculty and students’ understanding of discipline education and integrity culture.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Activities | Discipline Education Month online essays and Clean Reading Month book reviews |
| Award | Excellence Award |
| Number of winners | 10 authors |
| Selection | Expert review |
| Collection deadline | December 12, 2012 |
| Collection location | Supervision and Audit Office, Room 422, New Administration Center |
| Collection | Certificates and prizes |
| Organizing office | STU Discipline Inspection Commission Office |

Award recipients should collect their certificates and prizes at the designated location by December 12, following the requirements in the notice. The activities form part of the university’s effort to bring integrity culture into campus life and strengthen discipline education.
