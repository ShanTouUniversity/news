Pianist Huang Ailian will give a performance at Shantou University’s Arts Education Center on the evening of December 28. She will also share her experiences studying at the Central Conservatory of Music, performing with the Central Ballet Company, and pursuing musical activities in the United States and Italy.

### Performance details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Huang Ailian piano performance |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., December 28, 2012 |
| Venue | First-floor Music Rehearsal Room, Arts Education Center |
| Guest | Huang Ailian, pianist |

Born in Chongqing, Huang began studying piano at the age of six and entered the Central Conservatory of Music’s youth program at 11. After graduating in 1966, she performed with the Central Ballet Company. She moved to San Francisco in 1979 and earned a master’s degree in music from Mills College in 1982, receiving an award for outstanding piano performance. She settled in Positano, Italy, in 1997, founded ICMC (International Chamber Music Courses & Festival) in 1998, and held the first ICMC event in Shanghai in 2007.
