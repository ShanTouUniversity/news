It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a Wednesday Movie screening on the evening of December 26, 2012, featuring *Mister Roberts*.

## Screening Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *Mister Roberts* (1955), directed by John Ford and Mervyn LeRoy, starring Henry Fonda |
| Time | 19:00–22:00, December 26, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Host | Peter Herford |
| Seats | 50 public seats available on a first-come, first-served basis in addition to 150 class seats; students present campus card for entry |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

Adapted from the 1946 novel and 1948 Broadway play, the film depicts life aboard the U.S. Naval cargo ship USS Reluctant in the closing days of World War II, where the dutiful Lieutenant Mister Roberts longs to join front-line combat but clashes with a petty, vindictive captain and an idle crewman Pulver. The story was nominated for Best Picture and Best Sound at the 28th Academy Awards, with Jack Lemmon winning Best Supporting Actor. All faculty and students are welcome.
