The third Aizhi Workshop organized by the Social Sciences Department of Shantou University will be held on the evening of December 27. STU Vice President, Professor, and doctoral supervisor Wulanhasi will speak on “The Nobel Prize and Innovation,” introducing the history and social significance of the Nobel Prize and discussing thinking and innovation from the perspective of educational-system reform.

| --- | --- |
| Event | Third Aizhi Workshop |

| Time | 7:10 p.m., Thursday, December 27, 2012 |
| Venue | Lecture Room E201, Teaching Building E |
| Speaker | Wulanhasi, STU vice president, professor, and doctoral supervisor |
| Audience | Faculty members and students across the university |
