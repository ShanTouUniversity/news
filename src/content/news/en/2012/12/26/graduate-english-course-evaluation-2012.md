Shantou University opened the graduate English course evaluation system for the first semester of the 2012–2013 academic year on December 26. Graduate students enrolled in *Graduate English* must use their campus-network accounts to log in and evaluate the courses taught by their instructors by January 4, 2013.

The Graduate School asks students to complete the evaluation carefully and fairly. Students with questions during the process should contact the graduate academic-affairs coordinator in their college or the Graduate School.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | Graduate students enrolled in *Graduate English* |
| Evaluation period | December 26, 2012–January 4, 2013 |
| Method | Use a campus-network account to evaluate courses taught by instructors |
| Evaluation portal | `http://demo.stu.edu.cn/postsurvey/` |
| Questions | Graduate academic-affairs coordinator in the student’s college or the Graduate School |
