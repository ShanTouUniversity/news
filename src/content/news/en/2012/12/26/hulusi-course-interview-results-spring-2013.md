STU Arts Education Center and the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design have announced the interview results for *Hulusi Performance A* in the spring semester of the 2012–2013 academic year. A total of 36 students passed the interview. The course instructor is Lu Xiaoling.

Selected students must meet in Lecture Hall 3 at 12:30 p.m. on December 31 to discuss arrangements for the coming semester. The meeting is expected to last about 15 minutes. Specific arrangements for leave requests and course-registration information should follow notices from the Arts Education Center and course organizers.

Because all successful applicants are students, their names, student numbers, and personal contact information are not published here. Consult the original OA attachment for the complete list.
