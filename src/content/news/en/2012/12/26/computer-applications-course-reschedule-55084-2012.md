Because Room E101 will host the National Computer Rank Examination, STU has rescheduled section 55084 of *Computer Applications*. The classes originally scheduled for periods 1–4 on Wednesday, January 2, 2013, in Rooms E301 and E101 will instead be held during periods 1–4 on Saturday, December 29, 2012.

The make-up class will use Room E301 for periods 1–2 and Room E101 for periods 3–4. The Academic Affairs Office asks students enrolled in the course to inform one another of the change.
