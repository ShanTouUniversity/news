It is reported that the Art Education Center of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design issued a notice on December 26, 2012, regarding the final assessment for the course *Theatre Appreciation and Criticism*.

Students taking the course (class code 56562, instructor Lin Kehuan) are required to complete and submit the final paper by 12:00 on December 31, 2012.

## Assessment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Theatre Appreciation and Criticism* (Class 56562, Instructor Lin Kehuan) |
| Topic | Brief statement of your understanding of the *Theatre Appreciation and Criticism* course |
| Length | 1,000 to 1,200 words |
| Deadline | 12:00, December 31, 2012 |
| Submission | Electronic version to o_ysjyzx@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center |

Students enrolled are asked to inform each other and submit on time. Further requirements are available in the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Art Education Center)
