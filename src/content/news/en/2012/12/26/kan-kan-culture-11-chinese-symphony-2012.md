The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold the 11th Kan Kan Culture session. Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, will speak with music critic and music-history author Zhou Guangzhen about the development of Chinese symphonic music under the theme “The Spirit of Chinese Symphony: Beginning with the Rise and Fall of the China Central Philharmonic Orchestra.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Kan Kan Culture 11 |
| Topic | The Spirit of Chinese Symphony: Beginning with the Rise and Fall of the China Central Philharmonic Orchestra |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., December 27, 2012 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Speakers | Wang Shouzhi and Zhou Guangzhen |

Zhou is a senior music critic and author of *The History of the China Central Philharmonic Orchestra*. He has worked as a senior writer for the *South China Morning Post*, a music columnist for *Asiaweek*, a classical-music host on Radio Television Hong Kong’s Radio 4, and a critic for *Musical America*. In 2010, he received a commendation from the Home Affairs Bureau of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for contributions to culture and the arts.
