STU Academic Affairs Office reminds students in the 2011 and 2012 cohorts who still need to obtain a student train-discount card to complete the card-data entry and purchase procedures by December 28. Applications will not be processed after the deadline.

Under the relevant rules, students admitted from 2011 onward who need a train-discount card must have their discount station and related information registered by the university through the Ministry of Education’s CHSI system. The deadline for the university to import the information is December 31, so students who need the card should complete the university’s procedures within the stated period.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible students | 2011 and 2012 cohort students who still need a new train-discount card |
| Completion deadline | December 28, 2012 |
| Required procedures | Card-data entry and purchase |
| University information-import deadline | December 31, 2012 |
| After the deadline | This round of processing will not be available after December 28 |
