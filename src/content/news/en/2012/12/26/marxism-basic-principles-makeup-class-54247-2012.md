Because section 54247 of *Basic Principles of Marxism*, taught by Li Xi of the Social Sciences Department, was suspended during periods A–C on Thursday, November 29, 2012, the Academic Affairs Office has scheduled a make-up class.

The make-up class will be held during periods 2–4 on Sunday, December 30, in Room E204. Students enrolled in the course are asked to inform one another of the arrangement.
