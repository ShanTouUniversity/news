Because Lin Weijie of the Arts Education Center will perform in the 2013 New Year Concert, STU has suspended the class session for section 54700 of *Western Classical Music Appreciation* originally scheduled for the AB periods on Wednesday, December 26, 2012, in Room E303.

The make-up class will be held during the AB periods on Friday, January 4, 2013, in the same room, E303. Students enrolled in the course are asked to inform one another of the change.
