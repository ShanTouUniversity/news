Students from the Shantou University Affiliated Primary School participated in Guangdong’s first Poetry Festival under the guidance of their teachers and performed well through preliminary and semi-final rounds and an on-site poetry-writing event. At the awards ceremony held in the auditorium of Sun Yat-sen University on December 22, the school received the title “Model School for Poetry Education,” jointly awarded by the Publicity Department of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education.

According to the school, only two schools in Shantou received the title. The event highlighted the Affiliated Primary School’s work in poetry education and students’ literary practice.
