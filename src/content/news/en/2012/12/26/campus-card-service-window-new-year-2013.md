Because of year-end bank settlement and the New Year holiday, Shantou University will adjust the opening schedule for its campus-card service windows. The windows will close on December 31, 2012, remain closed from January 1 through 3, 2013, during the New Year holiday, and resume service on January 4.

While the windows are closed, faculty members and students may use card-loading, transfer, and inquiry kiosks for self-service top-ups. For card-loss reports or balance inquiries, users may call the voice self-service line. Other questions during the New Year holiday may be directed to the campus service hotline or the Network and Information Center’s service email.

| Date | Service arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| December 31, 2012 | Campus-card windows closed for year-end bank settlement |
| January 1–3, 2013 | Campus-card windows closed for the New Year holiday |
| January 4, 2013 | Campus-card windows reopen |

| Service | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Self-service top-up | Card-loading, transfer, and inquiry kiosks |
| Card-loss report and balance inquiry | 82903414 (campus extension 3414) |
| New Year holiday service hotline | 82903413-200 |
| Service email | `support@stu.edu.cn` |
