The Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will host a Journalism Day lecture on October 24, 2012. Li Haipeng, a senior reporter with *Southern Weekly*, will speak on “What Kind of Nonfiction Writing Do We Need?”

Li graduated from the Chinese Department of Liaoning University and has worked as a columnist for *China Business News Weekly* and a feature director for *GQ*. Since joining *Southern Weekly* as a reporter in 2002, he has developed distinctive work in feature writing, investigative reporting and explanatory journalism. His representative reports include “The Death of a Weightlifting Champion” and “The Cruel Side of Beichuan after the Disaster.” His books include the essay collection *The Buddha on Line 1* and the journalism collection *The Solitary Light of the Earth*.
