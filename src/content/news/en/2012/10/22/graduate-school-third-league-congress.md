It is reported that, with the approval of the university Youth League committee and the school's Party general branch, the third Congress of the Communist Youth League of the Graduate School of Shantou University will be held on October 23, 2012, at the Science Lecture Hall.

## Main Tasks of the Congress

The congress will review the work of the second Graduate School Youth League committee over its nearly three years in office, elect members of the third committee, and set out the tasks and direction for future work.

## Selection of Delegates and Candidates

According to the preparation arrangements, the number of delegates is set at 15% of each league branch's membership. Delegates must be Communist Youth League members or CPC members of the Graduate School, with excellent academic and moral records, close ties with fellow students, and exemplary conduct. Delegates are elected by each branch under democratic centralism, with elections and registration forms due by October 22.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

The new committee will consist of nine members. In addition to meeting delegate requirements, candidates should be innovative, dedicated to Youth League work, and possess relevant experience and strong organizational skills. Candidates are recommended by branches and vetted through consultation between the school Youth League and the Party general branch, with registration forms also due before October 22.

The Graduate School noted that the congress is a major event in the political life of the school's young league members, and branches are expected to use it as an opportunity to carry out educational activities and contribute to a positive campus culture.
