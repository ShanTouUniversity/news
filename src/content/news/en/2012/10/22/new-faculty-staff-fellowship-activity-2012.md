To encourage interaction among newly hired faculty and staff and strengthen cohesion, the Shantou University union will organize a gathering for new employees on October 27, 2012. The event is open to faculty and staff hired from October 25, 2011 through October 18, 2012, and includes an outdoor hike, an ecological-park visit and informal social activities.

Participants will gather at the university motto monument at 8:30 a.m. and travel by bus to Mengzhigu in Queshi Scenic Area for a hike at 9 a.m. Lunch will be served at Danying Ecological Restaurant at noon, followed by tea, flower viewing and informal socializing at Danying Ecological Park from 1 p.m. Buses will return to campus at 4 p.m.
