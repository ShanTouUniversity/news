The Shantou University Science School will host a Science Salon research talk on October 24, 2012. Professor Jia Tianqing of the State Key Laboratory of Precision Spectroscopy at East China Normal University will speak on “Femtosecond-Laser-Induced Nanostructures and Their Optical Properties.”

The talk will introduce the ultrafast and high-intensity characteristics of femtosecond lasers and their applications in physics, chemistry, medicine and nanostructure fabrication. Professor Jia will discuss the physical mechanisms behind laser-induced periodic nanostructures and research on micron/nanometer composite periodic structures fabricated on ZnO and ZnSe semiconductor surfaces, including their absorption, luminescence and applications.

Professor Jia received his PhD from Tongji University in 2000 and conducted postdoctoral research at the Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics and the University of Tokyo. He is a professor and doctoral supervisor at East China Normal University, has published more than 80 papers in SCI-indexed journals and has received recognition including the Ministry of Education New Century Excellent Talents program, Shanghai Pujiang Talent program and Shanghai Shuguang Scholar program.
