The Party General Branch of Shantou University Engineering School will hold a Party-affairs training meeting for all full student Party members on October 24, 2012. The session is intended to help members understand and improve their Party-affairs work.

The training will take place in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room. Anyone unable to attend for a valid reason must request leave from the student Party-branch secretary of their department and report to the school Party General Branch by 3 p.m. on October 23, explaining the reason.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Training | Engineering School student Party-affairs training |
| Time | October 24, 2012, Wednesday, 4 p.m. |
| Venue | Engineering School Academic Exchange Room |
| Participants | All full student Party members in the Engineering School |
| Leave deadline | Before 3 p.m. on October 23, 2012 |
| Procedure | Request leave from the departmental student Party-branch secretary and report to the school Party General Branch |
