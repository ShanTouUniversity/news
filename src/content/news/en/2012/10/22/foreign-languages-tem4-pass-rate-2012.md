Students in the Foreign Languages Department of Shantou University School of Liberal Arts achieved a 91.80% pass rate in the 2012 National College English Test for English Majors Band 4 (TEM4). Based on the averages reported in the notice, the result exceeded the 61.50% average for comprehensive universities and the 51.09% average for all universities nationwide.

| Comparison | Pass rate | Difference calculated from reported figures |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| STU Foreign Languages Department | 91.80% | — |
| Comprehensive universities nationwide | 61.50% | 30.30 percentage points higher |
| All universities nationwide | 51.09% | 40.71 percentage points higher |

The original notice describes the second difference as 41.71 percentage points, but subtracting 51.09% from 91.80% gives 40.71 percentage points. The School of Liberal Arts congratulated the Foreign Languages faculty and all participating students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Liberal Arts)
