The Shantou University Library will hold a lecture in its Subject Literature Services series on October 23, 2012. The session, “Quickly Mastering Ei and ScienceDirect Search Techniques,” will be led by Zeng Zhaohong and introduce faculty members and students to search methods for Engineering Index (Ei) and ScienceDirect.

The lecture will cover the new Ei interface and functions, Ei search techniques and result analysis, and the use of search language to improve ScienceDirect efficiency. A prize quiz will be held, with small gifts for participants who answer correctly.
