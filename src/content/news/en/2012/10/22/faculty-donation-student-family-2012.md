After Shantou University appealed for donations to support the family of a student who died unexpectedly, faculty members and staff responded actively. The university union reported that 107,590.1 yuan had been collected by the morning of October 19, 2012. The funds were delivered to Student Affairs that day for transfer to the bereaved student&apos;s family.

The reported donations from departmental unions, along with amounts given directly by employees to Student Affairs, were as follows:

| Department or unit | Union donations (yuan) | Direct employee donations to Student Affairs (yuan) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Law School | 8,500 | — |
| Affiliated Middle and Primary Schools | 2,456.1 | — |
| Engineering School | 11,200 | — |
| Administrative Union | 22,800 | — |
| Social Sciences and Continuing Education Union | 3,780 | — |
| Research Union | 5,847 | 1,201 |
| Science School | 6,800 | — |
| Business School | 21,151 | — |
| Physical Education Department | 1,743 | — |
| Library | 2,850 | — |
| School of Liberal Arts | 10,641 | 400 |
| Journalism School | 2,450 | 100 |
| School of Arts | 600 | — |
| Resource Management Office | 6,772 | — |

The notice states a total of 107,590.1 yuan. However, adding the listed unit and direct-donation amounts produces 109,291.1 yuan, a difference of 1,701 yuan that the original notice does not explain. This article does not publish the deceased student&apos;s name or other personal information.
