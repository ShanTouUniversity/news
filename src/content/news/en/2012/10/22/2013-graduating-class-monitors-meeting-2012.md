The Shantou University Student Affairs Office will hold a meeting for class monitors of the 2013 on-campus undergraduate graduating cohort on October 23, 2012. The meeting will explain arrangements for collecting graduate information and completing employment-related procedures.

Each school should notify its undergraduate graduating-class monitors to attend on time. If a monitor cannot attend, another class representative should attend instead.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | Meeting for 2013 on-campus undergraduate graduating-class monitors |
| Time | October 23, 2012, Tuesday, 4:10 p.m. |
| Venue | D203 |
| Topics | Graduate information collection and employment procedures |
| Participants | Undergraduate graduating-class monitors from each school; another class representative may attend if needed |
