The 134th Shantou University Engineering Research Forum will take place on October 23, 2012, on the topic of “Digital Audio Forensics.” Professor Huang Jiwu, dean and professor at the School of Information Science and Technology at Sun Yat-sen University, will give the talk. Faculty members and students from the Electronics and Computer departments are welcome to attend.
