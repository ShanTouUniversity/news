The Shantou University Three Styles Development Office has issued Bulletin No. 209 on academic-conduct checks, covering classroom attendance inspections during week 5 of the 2012–13 fall semester. The inspection team checked E Teaching Building on October 16 and 18 and found nine students late for class in total.

| Inspection date | Location | Time | Late by no more than 5 minutes | Late by more than 5 minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| October 16, 2012 | E Teaching Building | 2 p.m. | 3 | 1 |
| October 18, 2012 | E Teaching Building | 8 a.m. | 3 | 2 |
| Total | — | — | 6 | 3 |

The bulletin asks each school and residential college to provide criticism and education to the relevant students and continue strengthening academic conduct. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish the names or other personal information of the students listed in the attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Three Styles Development Office)
