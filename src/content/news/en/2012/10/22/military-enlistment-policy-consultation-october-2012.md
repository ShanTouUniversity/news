To help college graduates understand and apply military-enlistment policies, the Department of College Students of the Ministry of Education, National Student Financial Aid Administration, National Higher Education Student Information and Employment Guidance Center and Defense Ministry Conscription Office are jointly holding an online consultation from October 22 to 26, 2012. The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has forwarded the arrangements.

Graduates may visit the consultation page to submit questions, which will be answered online by relevant officials.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2012 online consultation on military-enlistment policy for college graduates |
| Dates | October 22–26 |
| Morning session | 9–11:30 a.m. |
| Afternoon session | 2–4:30 p.m. |
| Platform | [Online consultation page](http://www.ncss.org.cn/2012zbzx/) |
| Participants | College graduates |

STU encourages graduates interested in military enlistment to take part in the online consultation.
