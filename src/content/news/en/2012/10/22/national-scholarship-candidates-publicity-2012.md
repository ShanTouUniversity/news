Shantou University has published the candidate list for the 2011–12 National Scholarship. In accordance with requirements from the Guangdong Department of Education, the university organized the selection according to the number of eligible places allocated to each school and residential college. Units developed assessment rules under the *Interim Measures for National Scholarship Administration* and the *STU National Scholarship Selection Guidelines*, formed assessment committees, and selected candidates through preliminary review, interviews and rechecking.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Public notice | 2011–12 National Scholarship candidate list |
| Selection stages | Preliminary review, interviews and rechecking |
| Principles | Openness, fairness, impartiality and merit |
| Comment deadline | Before noon on October 26, 2012, Friday |
| Submission office | Student Aid Management Center, Administrative Building 106 |
| Method | Written submission |

The candidate list is provided in the original attachment. Organizations and individuals with objections may submit written materials before the deadline. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed with the submitter&apos;s real name. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish candidate names or personal information.
