It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a film screening on the evening of October 10 in the Library Lecture Hall, featuring the 1952 musical comedy *Singing in the Rain*.

## Screening Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Film | *Singing in the Rain* (1952), starring Gene Kelly, Donald O'Connor and Debbie Reynolds |
| Time | 19:00–22:00, October 10, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Host | Peter Herford |
| Seats | 50 public seats available on a first-come, first-served basis in addition to 150 class seats; students present campus card for entry |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

The film is regarded as a masterpiece of musical cinema, depicting Hollywood’s transition from silent films to talkies in the late 1920s and featuring acclaimed choreography by Gene Kelly. All faculty and students are welcome.
