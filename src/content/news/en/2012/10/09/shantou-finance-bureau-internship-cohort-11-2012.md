The Shantou University Business School has announced the selection results for the eleventh internship cohort at the Shantou Municipal Financial Work Bureau. Following discussions with the bureau, two Business School students were selected for the internship.

The Business School thanked students for their participation and asked those who were not selected to watch for future internship activity notices. The original notice governs the detailed internship information.
