Following personnel changes in the university Party leadership, the Communist Party of China Shantou University Committee has adjusted the assignments through which Standing Committee members liaise with campus democratic parties, non-Party人士 and people who have returned from study overseas. The assignments were issued in STU Party Document [2012] No. 25.

| Party Standing Committee member | Liaison assignment |
| --- | --- |
| President Xu Xiaohu | STU general branch of the China Democratic League |
| Secretary Song *zhen (one character appears garbled in the original) | STU branch of the Revolutionary Committee of the Chinese Kuomintang |
| Deputy Secretary Luo Nianchao | STU general branch of the China Association for Promoting Democracy |
| Vice President Li Dan | STU branch of the China Democratic National Construction Association |
| Vice President Lin Danming | STU branch of the Chinese Peasants and Workers Democratic Party |
| Vice President Wulanhasi | STU grassroots committee of the Jiusan Society |
| Standing Committee member Shi Ganggang | STU general branch of the China Zhi Gong Party |
| Standing Committee members Zeng Dabin and He Wenbiao | Non-Party individuals, returnees from overseas study and others |

The assignments were issued on October 9, 2012. One character in the second contact&apos;s name is garbled in the original archive; the article preserves the identifiable portion and does not infer the missing character.

> Source: STU OA document (CPC Shantou University Committee)
