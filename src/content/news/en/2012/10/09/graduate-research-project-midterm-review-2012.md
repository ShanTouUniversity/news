The Shantou University Graduate School has completed its 2012 midterm review of extracurricular technology projects. The school determined the follow-up funding after considering expert opinions, project progress, project difficulty and funding needs.

Thirty-three projects passed the midterm review and will receive additional funding. The awards are set at two levels, CNY 300 or CNY 500 per project, for a total of CNY 13,500. The Graduate School also issued reimbursement arrangements, and project leaders must complete the procedures according to the schedule in the attachments.

The school encouraged project members to continue their work during the latter stage and supervisors to strengthen their guidance, with the aim of developing candidate projects for the national “Challenge Cup” competition. The approved-project summary and reimbursement guide were issued with the notice.
