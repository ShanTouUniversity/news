The Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will hold “Study in Germany: An Introduction to the Exchange Program of the Design Department in Dessau” on the evening of October 10, 2012. Professor Uwe Gellert of the School of Design at Anhalt University of Applied Sciences in Germany will give the presentation.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Study in Germany: An Introduction to the Exchange Program of the Design Department in Dessau |
| Speaker | Professor Uwe Gellert, School of Design, Anhalt University of Applied Sciences |
| Time | October 10, 2012, Wednesday, 7:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

Faculty members and students interested in design exchange study are welcome to attend and learn about the program arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
