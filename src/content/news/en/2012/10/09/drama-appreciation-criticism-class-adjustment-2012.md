For course-scheduling reasons, the Shantou University Art Education Center has changed the schedule for Class 56562 of Drama Appreciation and Criticism. The class was originally scheduled for periods 6–8 on Thursday of Week 5, October 18, 2012. It will instead meet during periods 6–8 on Thursday of Week 4, October 11, in Lecture Hall 3.

The Art Education Center and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design ask students enrolled in the course to share the update and attend according to the revised schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Art Education Center)
