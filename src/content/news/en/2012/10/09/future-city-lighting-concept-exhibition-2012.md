The Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Art and Design is hosting the Future City Lighting Concepts exhibition from October 8 to 17, 2012, at the School Art Museum. The exhibition is open to faculty members and students on campus.

The exhibition focuses on concepts and creative design related to future urban lighting, offering the university community an opportunity to view and discuss design ideas in the field.
