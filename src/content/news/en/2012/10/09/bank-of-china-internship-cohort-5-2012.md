The Shantou University Business School has announced the selection results for the fifth internship cohort at Bank of China Shantou Branch. Following applications and discussions with the branch, 14 undergraduate and graduate students were selected for the internship.

The selected students must assemble at the STU motto monument at 8:00 a.m. on October 10, 2012, and travel together to Bank of China Shantou Branch for the internship. The Business School thanked students for their participation and asked those who were not selected to watch for future activity notices.
