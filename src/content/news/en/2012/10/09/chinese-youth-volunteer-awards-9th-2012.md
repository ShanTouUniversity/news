The Shantou University Youth League Committee is organizing eligible individuals, organizations and volunteer-service projects on campus to apply for the ninth China Youth Volunteer Individual, Organization and Project Awards. The awards are organized by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League of China and the China Youth Volunteer Association and will be selected from late September through early December 2012. The activity aims to encourage youth participation in volunteer service and promote key volunteer initiatives.

Applicants must complete their applications through the China Youth Volunteer website at <http://www.zgzyz.org.cn/> by October 15, 2012. Electronic application materials should be sent to the STU Youth League Committee at `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`, and printed copies should be delivered to the committee office, New Administration Center Room 110.

The application categories are Outstanding Individual, Outstanding Organization and Outstanding Project. The provincial Youth League Committee notice and its attachments govern the detailed eligibility and materials requirements.
