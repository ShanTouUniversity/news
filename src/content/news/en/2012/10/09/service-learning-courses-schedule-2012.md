It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on October 9, 2012, announcing the schedule for two public service-learning courses in the fall semester of 2012.

## Schedule

| Course | Code | Training | Field Practice | Sharing Session |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shuguang Tutoring Service-Learning | SER3015 | Oct 10, 17, 24, 31, 2012 | Oct 14 – Dec 2, 2012 | Dec 5, 2012 |
| Sunshine Community Service-Learning | SER3014 | Oct 10, 17, 24, 31, 2012 | Oct 14 – Dec 2, 2012 | Dec 5, 2012 |

Detailed times and venues are available in the OA attachments. Students enrolled are asked to prepare and attend on time.
