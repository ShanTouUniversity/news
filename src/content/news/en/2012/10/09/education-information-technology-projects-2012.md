Applications have opened for the 2012 special projects in education information-technology research under the 12th Five-Year Education Science Plan of Guangdong Province. The Shantou University Research Office is organizing applications from relevant university units. STU may submit a maximum of two projects this year. Projects under the provincial education science plan are generally self-funded, while the provincial education technology center may provide funding for selected projects with significant potential for wider application.

## Eligibility and requirements

A project leader may be a faculty member or university education administrator and should generally hold an associate-senior or higher professional title. Applicants without an associate-senior title must provide recommendations from two peer experts with full senior titles. Applicants' national social-science, national education-science, Guangdong philosophy and social-science, or Guangdong Department of Education research projects must have completed their final acceptance. Each applicant may submit only one project and may not join another application as a project-team member.

Applicants should design a specific topic in accordance with the special-project guide. Topics should reflect local characteristics and demonstrate originality and pioneering value. Papers and research reports should generally be completed within one or two years, while monographs should generally be completed within two or three years. A project whose final product is a research report must provide proof of adoption by the relevant organization when applying for appraisal.

| Date | Task |
| --- | --- |
| By October 17, 2012 | Log in to the Guangdong Modern Educational Technology Experimental Alliance website and submit the application online |
| By October 23, 2012 | After approval, print the application-system PDF double-sided on A4 paper in two copies and submit it to the Research Office |

Applicants must register a real-name account before completing the form and contact the Research Office for approval. Registered account names cannot be changed, and account credentials should be kept securely. Applicants who submitted projects in previous years should use their existing accounts. The online form should follow the operating instructions, with particular care taken when entering organizational and personal information in the project justification.

Application website: <http://djsylm.edugd.cn>. Enquiries may be directed to Huang Yumei or Huang Nan at `86502563` or `82902563`.
