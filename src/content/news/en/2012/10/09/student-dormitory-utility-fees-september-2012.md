The Shantou University Resource Management Office has published utility-fee information for student dormitories for September 2012. The latest meter readings were taken on September 29 and 30, following the previous readings on August 31. Students may log in to the STU utility-management system at <http://power.stu.edu.cn/> to check their charges using their individual campus-network accounts.

Students who find an error or have questions may complete and sign a Utility-Fee Review Application Form and submit it during working hours from October 10 to 15 to the Water and Electricity Center beside the Science and Technology Building at `2489`. Students whose dormitory information does not match the student list may contact the counselor's office in Building B, Room 106, at `2903355`.

| Date | Task |
| --- | --- |
| October 10–15, 2012 | Submit a utility-fee review request |
| October 16, 2012 | One-Card deductions begin for charges without objections |
| October 16–19, 2012 | Regular payment period |
| October 22, 2012 | Overdue nonpayment handled under university rules |

Students whose One-Card balances are insufficient should top up promptly. Objections raised after the review period that require correction after verification may take three to five working days to process. Questions about One-Card deductions may be directed to the Network Center at `3547-8842`.
