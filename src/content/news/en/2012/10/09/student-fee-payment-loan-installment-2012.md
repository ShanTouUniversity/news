The Shantou University Finance Office and Student Affairs Office have announced payment arrangements for students in the 2012–2013 academic year, covering tuition, accommodation fees, deferred payment for educational loans and semester-based payment for students facing financial hardship.

Students must deposit their tuition, accommodation fees and any previous outstanding fees into their personal Bank of China accounts by October 15, 2012. The university will arrange collection through the bank. Students who have changed their deduction account or have not registered their personal account for tuition and other fees with the Finance Office should complete the registration promptly.

## Loans and installment payments

Students with educational loans for the academic year may defer fees equivalent to their loan amounts but must deposit any difference into their Bank of China accounts. Students whose loan amounts have been cancelled because of academic performance or other reasons must pay their tuition and accommodation fees in full. Students applying for a credit-based loan from their home region should bring the Loan Acceptance Certificate, loan contract and other documents to the Student Affairs Office Student Aid Management Center, Administration Center Room 106.

Students who have received a university educational loan or a home-region credit-based loan must also log in to the Student Affairs System and submit installment-payment information. The payment date for the difference is October 10, 2012, and the payment date for the amount covered by the loan is May 10, 2013.

Students facing financial hardship may apply to pay by semester from October 10 to 20, 2012, through the Student Affairs System at <http://sao.stu.edu.cn/>. Counselors at the colleges and residential colleges will review the applications in the system from October 21 to 30.

Students who have not completed payment for the academic year or still have previous outstanding fees will be handled under the Interim Measures for Student Fee Payment and Registration Management of Shantou University, Document [2009] No. 54.
