It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Development and Reform Commission has issued the letter *Request for Assistance in the First Recruitment of Evaluation Experts for the Guangdong Comprehensive Evaluation Expert Pool* (Yue Fa Gai Fa Cai Han [2012] No. 2530) to recruit the first batch of experts for the provincial pool. The Human Resources Office of Shantou University forwarded the letter on October 9, asking relevant units to organize interested candidates to apply as required.

The letter specifies the recruitment for the first batch of the provincial comprehensive evaluation expert pool. Details regarding eligibility, application requirements and procedures are provided in the attachment.
