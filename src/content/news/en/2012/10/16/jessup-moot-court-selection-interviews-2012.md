To prepare for the Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition, Shantou University Law School will begin selection among students who have registered. The school plans to select five to eight team members, with five placed on the official competition roster, and four research assistants. The research-assistant places are mainly intended for students who will be in their second undergraduate year when the next semester begins.

Selection will take the form of an English interview. English expression and communication, legal knowledge, and attitude and willingness to participate will account for 40%, 40% and 20% of the score, respectively.

| --- | --- |
