To strengthen the student-affairs workforce and improve student education, administration and services, Shantou University Student Affairs Office has established the Golden Phoenix Award for the university&apos;s student-affairs system. The award honors outstanding individuals who achieve notable results in student development, service and support.

Student-affairs staff support students&apos; study, life and growth through education, awards and recognition, financial aid, work-study, campus culture, admissions promotion, career guidance and administration, mental-health education, entrepreneurship support and skills development. The award recognizes significant contributions in these areas and supports continued improvement in student development.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Award | STU Golden Phoenix Award for Student Affairs |
| Honorees | Outstanding individuals in student development, education, administration, services and support |
| Frequency | Annual |
| Annual quota | No more than one recipient |
| Application deadline | December 15 each year |
| Receiving office | Student Affairs Office |

Individuals who meet the selection requirements and wish to apply may submit materials to the Student Affairs Office. The original attachment provides the detailed selection rules and application requirements.
