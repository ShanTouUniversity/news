It is reported that the People’s Armed Forces Department of Shantou University issued a notice on October 16, 2012, to organize the 2013 selection of military pilot cadets from ordinary universities by the Air Force. The selection is open to eligible second-year undergraduates and graduating undergraduates, with requirements and procedures based on notices issued by the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Public Security and the General Political Department of the People’s Liberation Army.

## Eligible Applicants and Basic Requirements

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Second-year undergraduates | No older than 22; born after June 30, 1990 |
| Graduating undergraduates | No older than 24; born after June 30, 1988 |
| Major | No major restriction, except students with special talents in music, physical education or fine arts |
| Other natural conditions | Male and unmarried |

## Physical, Psychological and Political Requirements

Physical requirements include a height of 165–185 cm and a weight of at least 50 kg. Both unaided eyes must have visual acuity of at least 0.8 under the Air Force standard chart, or at least 4.9 on the E-chart. Resting blood pressure must not exceed 138/88 mmHg. Applicants must not have color blindness, color weakness, tattoos or scarification.

Applicants should have a strong interest in and desire to fly, quick thinking, flexible reactions, coordinated movements and strong learning ability. They should also possess leadership and decision-making ability, a spirit of teamwork, an open and positive personality, emotional stability and an enterprising spirit.

Political requirements include supporting the Party’s line, principles and policies; loyalty to the country; commitment to the military and a willingness to pursue military aviation; a clear personal history; progressive thinking; good conduct; compliance with laws and regulations; and fulfillment of the political requirements for military pilot recruitment. Applicants must volunteer to participate and have their parents’ support.

## Academic Requirements

Applicants must have entered university through the National College Entrance Examination and hold formal student status at their university. English or Russian must be the foreign language taken in the examination. Second-year undergraduates must have been admitted through the provincial admissions authority’s first or second undergraduate admission batch, completed the corresponding coursework and passed all courses. Graduating undergraduates must have been admitted through the first undergraduate admission batch and obtain a full-time undergraduate degree and bachelor’s degree.

## Selection Process

The selection will run from October 2012 through July 2013 in three stages:

| Stage | Period | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary selection | From October 2012 | Registration, recommendation and initial testing; those who pass testing at the preliminary selection station proceed to the next stage |
| Secondary selection | February–May 2013 | Cultural examination, physical examination, psychological selection and political assessment at a testing station |
| Final selection | Late May 2013 | Final selection |

The specific times and locations of the preliminary and secondary testing stations will be announced separately.
