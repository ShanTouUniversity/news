Shantou University Law School has published its 2012 National Scholarship assessment result. Following preliminary review by the school Youth League Committee, eight students entered interviews. The Law School scholarship assessment group ranked them using interview and academic performance and plans to recommend two students for the 2012 National Scholarship.

The relevant list is under public notice. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish student names; the original Law School notice governs the detailed list. Anyone with an objection may contact the Law School Youth League Committee in writing, in person or by telephone by October 21, 2012, or email wangbin@stu.edu.cn.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Interview candidates | 8 students |
| Nominees | 2 students |
| Assessment basis | Interview and academic performance |
| Public-notice deadline | October 21, 2012, Sunday |
| Receiving unit | Law School Youth League Committee |
| Feedback | Written, in-person, telephone or email |
