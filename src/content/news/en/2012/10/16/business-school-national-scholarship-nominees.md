It is reported that, in accordance with the university's notice on the 2012 evaluation of the National Scholarship, National Encouragement Scholarship and national grants, the Business School of Shantou University has recently completed its recommendation process for this year's National Scholarship. After student self-nominations and preliminary review by the college's Youth League Committee, ten students including Wu XM advanced to the interview stage.

## Evaluation Process

The school's scholarship evaluation committee conducted interviews with the shortlisted candidates on October 12. Based on interview results together with GPA and academic rankings, it proposed Wu XM and Pan FJ as recipients of the 2012 National Scholarship, and the list has now been published for public comment.

## Objection Channel

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | Before 17:30, October 21 (Sunday) |
| Method | Written submissions to the school's Youth League Committee |
| Requirements | Submissions from organizations must bear an official seal; individuals must sign their real names |
| Location | Youth League Committee office, Room 201, Wenxi Building |

Anyone with objections to the announced candidates may submit written feedback to the school's Youth League Committee within the above period.
