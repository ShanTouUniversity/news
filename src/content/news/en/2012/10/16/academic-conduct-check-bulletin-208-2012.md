Shantou University Student Affairs Office has issued Academic Conduct Check Bulletin No. 208, reporting results from week four of the 2012–13 fall semester. The conduct-check team inspected classroom attendance at the E Building on October 9 and October 11, and recorded late arrivals on both occasions.

| Check date | Location | Time | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| October 9, 2012 | E Building | 8:00 a.m. | 2 | 4 |
| October 11, 2012 | E Building | 2:00 p.m. | 2 | 8 |
| Total | — | — | 4 | 12 |

The two checks found 16 late students in total: four arrived within five minutes of class time and 12 arrived more than five minutes late. Student Affairs sent the relevant list to the schools and residential colleges and asked them to provide corrective education and continue strengthening academic conduct.

To protect student privacy, this article does not publish student names or other personal information. The detailed list is available in the original OA attachment.
