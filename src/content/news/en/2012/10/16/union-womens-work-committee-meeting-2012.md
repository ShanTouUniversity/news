| --- | --- |
| Meeting | University and departmental women&apos;s work committee meeting |
| Time | October 17, 2012, Wednesday, 4 p.m. |
| Venue | Second-floor conference room of the university union |
| Participants | University women&apos;s work committee members and departmental union women&apos;s work committee members |
