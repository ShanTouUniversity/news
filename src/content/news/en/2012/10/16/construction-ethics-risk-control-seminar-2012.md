As part of Shantou University&apos;s 2012 Discipline Education Learning Month, the university will hold a seminar on integrity-risk prevention and control in construction projects on October 19, 2012. The meeting will discuss identifying and managing integrity risks in construction work.

The seminar will take place in Conference Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center. Twenty-two relevant staff members from the Resource Management Office, Finance Office, Engineering School and Supervision and Audit Office will attend.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Integrity-risk prevention and control in construction projects |
| Time | October 19, 2012, Friday, 2:45 p.m. |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Center Conference Room 5 |
| Units | Resource Management Office, Finance Office, Engineering School, Supervision and Audit Office |
| Attendees | 22 |

The notice names Chen Shaogang, Liu Jinhua, Chen Wenzheng, Wang Yue, Chen Lihui, Lu Chunxiong, Qiu Jingfeng, Chen Changwang and Zhuang Shaoming from the Resource Management Office; Zou Zhibo, Su Shaohui and Zheng Weisheng from the Finance Office; Xiong Guangjing, Lin Peng, Chen Liqiang, Huang Diyu, Xu Pingsheng and Zeng Lijuan from the Engineering School; and Sun Fenqiang, Chen Zhongsong, Lin Ling and Huang Huan from the Supervision and Audit Office. These are employees and relevant staff members, so their names are retained from the official notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Commission Office)
