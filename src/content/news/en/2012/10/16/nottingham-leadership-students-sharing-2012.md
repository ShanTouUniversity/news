Shantou University will hold a sharing session on its leadership-student exchange program with the University of Nottingham Ningbo China on October 18, 2012. From August to September, STU sent 10 outstanding student representatives to the University of Nottingham Ningbo China for a three-week exchange. They helped prepare a series of welcome activities and experienced student study and life there.

At the session, the 10 participants will discuss what they saw, thought about and gained during the exchange. Audience interaction will also be included. This article does not publish the participating students&apos; names in order to protect their privacy.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | October 18, 2012, Thursday, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Science Report Hall |
| Speakers | 10 STU student representatives who visited Nottingham Ningbo |
| Exchange period | August–September 2012, three weeks |
| Format | Experience sharing and audience interaction |
