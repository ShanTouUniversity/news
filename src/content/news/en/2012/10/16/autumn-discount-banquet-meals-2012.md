The Shantou University Academic Exchange Service Center will introduce its fall 2012 discounted banquet meals on October 22, replacing the previous offering. Because of price fluctuations, the prices for the new season will be adjusted; the original notice attachments provide the menus and rates.

The service is available to users with a valid charge-account unit or funding project and to faculty members, staff and students who use a valid campus card with sufficient balance. Card-paying diners must be present for the entire meal, and cash is not accepted for discounted banquet meals. Outside food and drinks are not permitted.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Start date | October 22, 2012 |
| Audience | Users with valid charge-account units or projects, and faculty members, staff and students with valid campus cards |
| Payment | Charge account or campus card; cash not accepted |
| Dining rules | Card-paying diners must remain present; outside food and drinks are not permitted |
| Ordering hotline | Campus 2668 and 4138; outside 82905103 and 82905104 |

The center recommends saving the ordering attachment to a personal computer for later reference.
