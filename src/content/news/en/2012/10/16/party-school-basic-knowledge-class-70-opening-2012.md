The 70th Basic Party Knowledge Study Class of the Shantou University Party School will begin on October 21, 2012. Participants must report to their group leaders from October 18 to 19 with one one-inch front-facing, bareheaded photograph and collect study materials. Faculty and staff participants should report directly to Room 315 of the New Administration Center. The participant list is included in the original notice from the Party Organization and United Front Department and is not reproduced here.

Group leaders must attend a meeting at Wenbei 206 at 1 p.m. on October 17. Participants who fail to report without a valid reason will be treated as having given up their places. Those who need leave during the class must complete a leave-approval form, obtain review from their Party general branch and submit it to the Party School Office. Unexcused absences from class or the examination will result in cancellation of participation.
