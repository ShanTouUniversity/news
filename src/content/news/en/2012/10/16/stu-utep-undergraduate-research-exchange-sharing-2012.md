To advance international education and undergraduate education, Shantou University Science School will host a session on the STU–UTEP undergraduate research exchange program on October 19, 2012. The Science School and the University of Texas at El Paso have reached a five-year student exchange agreement. Beginning in 2012, the partnership initially selected students from applied chemistry to join a summer undergraduate research program.

From May 17 to July 28, 2012, three students from the 2009 chemistry cohort joined a roughly two-month research exchange at the University of Texas at El Paso. They will discuss their research and learning experiences and cross-cultural observations at the session. To protect student privacy, their full names are not reproduced here.
