The Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will screen the 1973 film *The Three Musketeers* during its movie night on October 17, 2012. Peter Herford will moderate the screening, which will run from 7 to 10 p.m. at the Library Theater.

Based on Alexandre Dumas&apos;s novel, the film was directed by Richard Lester and written by George MacDonald Fraser, with David Watkins as cinematographer and William Hobbs as fight choreographer. It follows the novel closely while adding humor and emphasizing period detail and sword-fight design.
