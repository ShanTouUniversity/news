It is reported that the Research Office issued the *Shantou University Research and Innovation Newsletter* Issue 3 of 2012 (No. 25 overall) on October 16, 2012, comprehensively reviewing recent progress in research.

## Research Projects

The newsletter presents the first batch of 2012 National Natural Science Foundation of China approvals for Shantou University, as well as recent applications, approvals and completions for humanities and social sciences research projects.

## Research Achievements and Awards

Recent research achievements and awards are reported, including a high-level paper by Prof. Huang Dongyang’s group from the Medical College in *PNAS*, important progress in Chemistry of the School of Science, funding for Pharmacology of the Medical College from the 2012 Central Finance Support for Local Universities, and the first Food Inspection Qualification Certificate obtained by the Analytical and Testing Center.

## Academic Exchange

Multiple exchanges are recorded: Associate Prof. Yu Chengjie of the School of Science attended the ICTP-ESF School and Conference on Geometric Analysis in Italy; Vice Dean Wang * (one character garbled in the original) of the Business School attended the 2012 Association of Asia-Pacific Business Schools conference in Singapore; faculty and students of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design undertook short-term study in Japan; Prof. Peng Xiaoyan of the College of Liberal Arts was invited as a visiting lecturer at the University of Tokyo; marine biology scholars visited Japan; the 9th congress of the Guangdong Society of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology was held in Shantou; and the Shantou Lawyers Association visited the Law School.

## Local Service and Other Activities

The newsletter also notes that the university was approved as a Guangdong intellectual property training base and signed a science and technology cooperation agreement with the Chaonan District government. Other activities include visits from the Zhejiang university newspaper research association, Hong Kong university delegations, the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, the Manitoban provincial government, as well as a visit by Vice President Wulan Hasi with Business School faculty to Shanxi Guoxin Energy Group and a visit by a consul from the U.S. Consulate General in Guangzhou.
