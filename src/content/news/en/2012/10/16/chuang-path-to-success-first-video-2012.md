The Media Center of Shantou University&apos;s Chuang Path to Success Network has released its first campus-recording video, *STU, I&apos;m Here*. The network has long provided technology training, learning resources and a platform for exchanging teaching information. In 2011, it added a Media Center that uses students&apos; professional skills to document activities and milestones in the growth of the university and its people.

Beginning in the fall 2012 semester, the Media Center plans to produce two or three short videos each semester, recording campus life through moving images and inviting the university community to follow and provide feedback. The network website, trysky.stu.edu.cn, is also being updated. A future sharing space will allow campus users to upload images and videos they have filmed.

Faculty members and students can follow the microblog accounts “@STU Chuang Path to Success Network” and “@STU Chuang Media Center” for updates.
