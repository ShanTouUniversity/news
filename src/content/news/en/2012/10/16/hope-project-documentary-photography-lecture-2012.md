Shantou University&apos;s Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication will host “The Hope of ‘Big Eyes’: A Documentary Photography Lecture by Xie Hailong” on October 17, 2012. Xie will share his experience documenting primary education in impoverished areas of China and discuss Hope Project documentary photography and news-image making.

Xie Hailong is a Chinese documentary news photographer. His honors include the Special Contribution Award for Chinese News Photography, recognition among China&apos;s top 10 young news photographers, and the “Virtue and Excellence” honor. Since 1990, he has followed primary education in impoverished regions of China. His Hope Project documentary series *Big Eyes* became an iconic image of the Hope Project in China and around the world.
