Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has released arrangements for purchasing and recharging student train-discount cards. Main-campus undergraduates who entered in 2012 and need to purchase a card must log in to the Student Affairs System and enter their boarding-station information by October 21. Beginning October 23, each major will collect the relevant student cards and purchase cash, after which two student representatives will go to the Academic Affairs service window in the New Administration Center to import the card information. Each card costs RMB 7. A card cannot be used unless its information has been imported.

Undergraduates in other main-campus cohorts may arrange recharges by class. Student cards must be collected and taken to the same service window from October 29 to November 2. The service hours are 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 2:45–5 p.m.; recharges are free.

| Group | Service | Time | Location and requirements |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main-campus 2012-entry undergraduates | Purchase and station-information import | Station information by October 21; processing from October 23 | Each major collects student cards and cash; RMB 7 per card; two student representatives visit the Academic Affairs service window in the New Administration Center |
| Main-campus undergraduates in other cohorts | Card recharge | October 29–November 2, 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 2:45–5 p.m. | Each class collects student cards and visits the Academic Affairs service window; recharge is free |

The regulations of the former Ministry of Railways and Ministry of Education on student discount tickets are provided in the original notice attachments.
