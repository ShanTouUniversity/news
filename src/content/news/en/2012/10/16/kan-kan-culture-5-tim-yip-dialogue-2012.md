“Kan Kan Culture 5” will be held at the Shantou University Science Report Hall on October 17, 2012. Under the theme “Character Design for Film and Stage through the Costume Design of *Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon*,” Wang Shouzhi, dean and professor of the Changjiang School of Art and Design, will speak with artist Tim Yip about costume, visual art, film art and stage creation.

Yip studied advanced photography at Hong Kong Polytechnic and has worked across costume, film art, stage visuals and contemporary art. In 2001, he became the first Chinese artist to receive both the Academy Award for Best Art Direction and the British Academy Award for Best Costume Design for *Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon*. He has contributed costume, art or visual design to films including *Red Cliff* and *The Banquet* and to numerous stage and dance productions while developing a contemporary interpretation of Eastern culture.

Yip worked on the art and costume design for the closing ceremony of the Athens Olympics and the handover ceremony of the Beijing Olympics. He was also visual art director for the dance production *DESH*, which won the Laurence Olivier Award for Best New Dance Production. In 2012, he worked on costume design for *Tai Chi* and *1942* and collaborated with dancer Yang Liping on the dance production *Peacock*.
