The Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host the “Let Youth Fly with Dreams” graduate career-guidance lecture on October 16, 2012. Lecturer Ding Ke will share professional experience from academic study through corporate management and marketing, offering students a perspective on employment choices and career development.

Ding studied electronic engineering at the University of Science and Technology of China and later earned an MBA from Lingnan College at Sun Yat-sen University. Ding is now deputy general manager and marketing director of Guangzhou Hancheng Electronic Products Co., Ltd.
