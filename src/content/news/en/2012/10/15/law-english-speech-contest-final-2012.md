The final of Shantou University&apos;s fifth Legal English Speech Contest will be held at the Law School on October 17, 2012. Ten students reached the final after interviews and review by the selection group. To protect student privacy, this article identifies the finalists using the surnames and initials provided in the original notice: Zhang HW, Luo HT, Wu SS, He Y, Liang AQ, Zhang WL, Zhang JM, Ye CP, Peng JY and Lu XS.

The final theme is “Express Your Legal Views in English.” Each contestant will choose a speech topic related to a current focus, hot issue or difficult question. The final has two parts: an original speech and questions from the judges. Each speech will last five minutes, after which the judges will ask questions and award scores on site.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | October 17, 2012, Wednesday, 1–2:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Law School Conference Room 213 |
| Finalists | 10 students |
| Format | Five-minute English speech and live questions |
| Theme | Express Your Legal Views in English |
| Awards | 2 first prizes, 3 second prizes and 5 third prizes |
| Audience | STU faculty members and students |

The detailed scoring standards are provided in the score sheet attached to the original Law School notice.
