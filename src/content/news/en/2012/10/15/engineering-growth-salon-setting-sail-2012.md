The 25th Shantou University Engineering School Growth Salon will be held on October 17, 2012, for the school&apos;s entire 2012 freshman cohort. Under the theme “Setting Sail,” senior students will share university experiences in study, student organizations, innovation projects and inter-university exchange.

The speakers include Zeng *Jie, a 2010 electronic information engineering student; Wang Mingjun, a 2010 computer application and technology student; Huang Jingfei, a 2010 civil engineering student; and Lin Jieqi, a 2010 mechanical design, manufacturing and automation student. Zeng *Jie serves as chair of CEC, supervisor of the Innovation Electronics Laboratory, operations executive of the Electronic Department&apos;s M@X team and leader of extracurricular volunteer tutoring for 2012 electronic information engineering freshmen. Wang is CEO of the MT organization and a software assistant for Qipu tablets and formerly headed the Engineering School Student Union&apos;s ability-development department. Huang leads extracurricular volunteer tutoring for 2012 civil engineering freshmen, formerly served as vice head of the Student Union&apos;s project-planning department and participated in the STU–MIT CDIO exchange program. Lin also participated in that exchange program and received the 2012 Maritime Academy Scholarship. The host is Chen C. One character in Zeng *Jie&apos;s name was garbled in the original notice; all student names are abbreviated here to protect privacy.
