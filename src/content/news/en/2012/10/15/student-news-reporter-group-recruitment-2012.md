With approval from the university Party Committee Publicity Department, the Shantou University News Center has established a Student Reporter Group and opened recruitment to all enrolled students. The group will edit content for the STU News website, the News Center&apos;s official microblog and *Shantou University News*. Strong work may be recommended to outside media and entered in national or provincial “Good News” competitions. Members with outstanding annual performance may be named Outstanding Student Reporters. The group will also provide media training and exchanges with student reporter teams from other universities.

The group is recruiting writers, photographers and online editors. Writers should have basic writing skills and enthusiasm for news reporting. Photographers should have a foundation in photography, with preference for strong writing ability. Online editors should have writing skills, at least one year of microblog experience and familiarity with microblog applications.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | All enrolled students |
| Application deadline | By October 18 |
| Interview | October 20, 2–6 p.m.; writers 2–4 p.m., photographers 4–6 p.m. |
| Venue | G302 |
| Writer materials | Three news or literary works |
| Photographer materials | Three to five images, each no larger than 1 MB; news or non-news images accepted |
| Online editor materials | Microblog work sent by direct message or by mentioning STU News Center by October 18 |

Applicants must submit the electronic application form and work as required. Paper work may be brought to the interview venue, and on-site registration will be accepted. Writers and photographers will be assessed on submitted work and interview performance; online editors will complete an online assessment. Results will be published on the campus office information network. The application email is o_xb@stu.edu.cn, with “Student Reporter Group Application” as the subject.
