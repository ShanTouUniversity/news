After signing a stationery procurement contract with Shantou Dasanyuan Stationery Co., Ltd., Shantou University has made it easier for units to purchase stationery. Because small orders generate a large number of settlement documents, the Finance Office and Equipment and Materials Management Office will use a centralized settlement process.

After a delivery is received and accepted, the purchasing handler and project approver must identify the funding project name and number on a purchase summary and sign it. After the supplier has accumulated purchases over a period, it will issue a consolidated invoice and submit the unit-signed purchase summaries. The Finance Office will deduct the charges from the corresponding funding projects and make one payment to the supplier.

| Stage | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery acceptance | Units confirm delivery and accept the stationery |
| Information confirmation | The purchasing handler and project approver enter and sign the funding project name and number |
| Supplier settlement | After accumulating purchases for a period, the supplier issues a consolidated invoice and submits the purchase summary |
| University payment | Finance deducts from the corresponding funding projects and pays the supplier centrally |
