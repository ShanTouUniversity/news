The Shantou University selection for the fourth AnJi Cup National Contest on Logistics Design by University Students opened on October 15, 2012. The national contest is organized by the Ministry of Education University Logistics Teaching Steering Committee and the China Federation of Logistics and Purchasing. Shanghai Maritime University will host the final, and Anji Automotive Logistics Co., Ltd. provides the cases. STU Business School&apos;s Case Competition Committee is coordinating the campus selection.

Teams must choose no more than five cases from the contest materials and develop a complete design proposal. Entries may be written materials, mathematical models, software or engineering designs. Possible directions include service-site layout and route optimization, market competition and operations, process optimization, human-resource development, emergency and risk control, logistics technology and equipment, information systems, value-added services, service marketing and quality management, performance evaluation, cost and financial management and innovation in logistics-management concepts.

Campus-selection entries are due by November 9, followed by a unified defense on November 10 at a venue to be announced. The selection cases are the same as those for the formal contest and may be downloaded from the China Federation of Logistics and Purchasing website. Teams reaching the national final will also take part in an in-person defense in Shanghai.

| Stage | Dates |
| --- | --- |
| STU selection | October 15–November 10, 2012 |
| National preliminary round | November 21, 2012–February 1, 2013 |
| National semifinal | February 2–March 24, 2013 |
| National final | March 25–April 30, 2013; defense date to be announced |

Eligible participants are officially admitted full-time undergraduate students in logistics and related programs. Teams must have five members, and a member may not join more than one team. Teams may combine students across years and majors, with no limit on the number of teams. Entries may be sent to xzpu@stu.edu.cn or qzeng@stu.edu.cn according to the original notice.
