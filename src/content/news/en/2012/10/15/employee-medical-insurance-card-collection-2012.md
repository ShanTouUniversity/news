Shantou University has asked eight employees to collect their medical-insurance manuals and IC cards. The recipients may go to the Employee Medical Insurance Office on the second floor of the campus hospital during working hours.

The notice names Zhang Xiaoling, Zhao Meini, Zhang Chunling, Li Xia, Wu Hongru, Liu Guiying, Wang Qiao and Weng Jianzhen.
