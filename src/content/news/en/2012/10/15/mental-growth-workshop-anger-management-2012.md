STU&apos;s Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will hold the second Mind Growth workshop under the theme “The Dance of Anger: How to Manage Angry Feelings.” The workshop will help students understand anger as a signal of hurt, violated rights, unmet needs or situations going off course, and reflect on the life problems and personal limits behind the emotion.

The workshop, led by Lin Rong, is limited to 12 participants and will use activities including “Meeting and Knowing One Another,” “Who Disrupted My Emotions?” and “Let Anger Dance.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | October 20, 2012, Saturday, 7–9 p.m. |
| Venue | Building 789, Room 736 |
| Theme | The Dance of Anger: How to Manage Angry Feelings |
| Capacity | 12 participants |
| Facilitator | Lin Rong |
| Registration deadline | 11 a.m. on October 19 |

Students interested in attending must complete the application form and email it to o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn before the deadline. Places will be assigned in registration order, and the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will notify selected students by email.
