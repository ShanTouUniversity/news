STU Affiliated School will host a lecture on October 17, 2012, titled “How to Read Poetry.” Zhang Weidong, an associate professor in the Shantou University Department of Chinese, will speak about approaches to reading poetry. Faculty members, students and parents are welcome.

Zhang holds a doctorate in literature from Nanjing University and is an associate professor in the STU Department of Chinese and a master&apos;s supervisor in literary theory.

| Venue | E Staircase Lecture Room 204 |
| Speaker | Zhang Weidong, associate professor in the STU Department of Chinese |
| Audience | Faculty members, students and parents |
