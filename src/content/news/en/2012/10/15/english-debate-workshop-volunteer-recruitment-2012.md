To enrich campus culture and strengthen critical-thinking and English-language skills, the Shantou University English Language Center will hold an English debate workshop at CILL, the Language Self-Study Center, from October 26 to 28, 2012. The workshop will invite Singaporean English debate educator Loke Wing Fatt and experienced student debaters from China to serve as coaches and co-trainers, giving participants opportunities to discuss current affairs and debate.

The workshop uses the British Parliamentary format, with two days of parliamentary-debate instruction and one day of mini debates. Two debaters form a team, and four teams compete in each round. The motion is announced 30 minutes before the round, teams prepare and each debater speaks for seven minutes. Judges score all eight debaters and select the best speaker and winning team.

| Participation | Places | Registration and activity arrangement |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Workshop trainees | 23 | Attend the full training and competition from October 26 to 28; selected through interviews |
| Workshop volunteers | 12 | Welcome coaches and outside debaters, manage check-in, logistics and venue setup; admitted in registration order |

Both registration tracks close at 11 p.m. on October 19. Trainees must complete the application form and attend an interview from 6:30 to 9 p.m. on October 20 at CILL. Applicants with a schedule conflict may request the additional interview session on the afternoon of October 17. Volunteers should submit registration information according to the original English Language Center notice.

The workshop will be led by Loke Wing Fatt. Co-trainers include Chanel C, Helen J and Sarah W of Beijing Foreign Studies University; Janet T of Beijing Language and Culture University; Lim WC of Fudan University; and Huang W of Sun Yat-sen University. The original notice describes their records in the FLTRP Cup English Debate Competition and other national and regional competitions, including awards, advancement, judging and coaching. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish the student debaters&apos; full names or registration contacts.
