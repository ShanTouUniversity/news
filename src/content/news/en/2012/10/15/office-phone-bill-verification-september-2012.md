China Telecom Shantou Branch has sent the electronic bills for office telephone charges incurred by Shantou University units from September 1 through September 30, 2012, to the university telephone office. The data has been imported into the telephone management system, where designated reviewers of each unit must log in at `lrmo.stu.edu.cn` to view the details.

| Time | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| From now | Units log in to the telephone management system and verify bills |
| After verification | Click “Confirm selected bills” online when there is no objection |
| By October 22 | Email discrepancies between bill details and actual use to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn |
| October 25 | Finance deducts charges using the funding account numbers provided by each unit |

At the end of the verification period, bills for which no objection has been raised and no confirmation has been submitted will be treated by the system as confirmed. Units may also email problems or suggestions about the telephone management system to the same official address.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
