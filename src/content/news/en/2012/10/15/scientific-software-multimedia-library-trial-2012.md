Shantou University Library has opened trial access to a multimedia scientific-software collection through November 30, 2012. The collection mainly contains recordings of live training courses from the Scientific Software Learning Network, edited into three-panel online video lessons that show the instructor&apos;s explanation and software operation for flexible study.

| Category | Software resources |
| --- | --- |
| Business, management and statistics | @RISK, R, SPSS Modeler, SPSS Amos, GAUSS, DecisionTools Suite, Minitab, SPSS Statistics, SAS, GAMS, Stata and EViews |
| General and mathematics | MATLAB, LabVIEW, Mathematica and Lingo |
| Engineering and simulation | ArcGIS, Adams, PSCAD and ProModel |

Faculty members and students can access the collection through the Library database page: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=355>. The original notice recommends using Internet Explorer and opening the link in a new window. Feedback on the trial can be sent to the Library Subject Services Department at extension 3247 or yxu@stu.edu.cn.
