It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on October 15, 2012, publicizing the shortlists for the 2011–2012 Academic Excellence Scholarships.

The scholarships reward outstanding academic performance: 1,500 yuan per year for first prize, 1,000 yuan for second prize and 500 yuan for third prize. Under the university’s measures, students ranking in the top 5% of their cohort and major qualify for first prize, the top 15% for second prize and the top 30% for third prize.

After student applications and university review, 182 candidates for first prize, 417 for second prize and 640 for third prize are now publicized. Candidates may check results via the Student Affairs System at http://sao.stu.edu.cn with their campus network account.
