To help students facing financial hardship with their study and living costs, alumni donors established the 2012 Alumni Financial-Aid Scholarship. The fund carries forward an alumni spirit of self-reliance, solidarity, giving back to the university and serving society. This year, it will support 35 senior students, each receiving 2,500 yuan.

Recipients were recommended after assessment by their colleges or residential colleges and confirmed through review by the Student Affairs Office and Alumni Office. The detailed recipient list is included in the original Student Affairs Office notice. This article does not reproduce student names in order to protect recipient privacy.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | 2012 Alumni Financial-Aid Scholarship |
| Beneficiaries | Senior students facing financial hardship |
| Number supported | 35 |
| Amount | 2,500 yuan per student |
| Selection | College or residential-college recommendation followed by review from the Student Affairs Office and Alumni Office |

The university encouraged recipients to continue studying diligently and respond to the care of alumni and the wider community through strong performance.
