Under the teaching schedule, Shantou University Class 55186, Career Guidance and Training, will change to a company visit on October 18, 2012. The class originally scheduled for periods 3–4 in Lecture Hall 2 that day will visit Shantou SEZ Ultrasonic Electronics Co., Ltd. for experiential learning.

The class is taught by Zhang Yan and Zhuo Yanchun. Enrolled students must gather and board at the school motto monument at 9:55 a.m. on October 18.
