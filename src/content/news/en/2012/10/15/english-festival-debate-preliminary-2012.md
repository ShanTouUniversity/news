The 2012 English Festival English Debate Contest has opened preliminary registration for STU undergraduates. The activity is intended for students interested in English debate, critical thinking and impromptu expression.

Registration closes at 11 p.m. on October 19, 2012, and on-site registration will also be accepted. Preliminary interviews will take place from 3 to 5 p.m. on October 20 at CILL, the Language Self-Study Center on the first floor of the Library. An additional interview session will be offered on the afternoon of October 17 for students with schedule conflicts; applicants should indicate their preferred session when registering.

The preliminary round consists of a critical-thinking impromptu speech and questions. Students who pass may enter the semifinal and may join pre-contest workshops organized by the English Debate Society. The contest uses the British Parliamentary format: two debaters form a team, four teams debate in each round, and the two sides each have Opening and Closing teams. The motion is announced 30 minutes before a round, after which teams prepare. Each debater speaks for seven minutes. Judges score all eight debaters and select the best speaker and winning team.
