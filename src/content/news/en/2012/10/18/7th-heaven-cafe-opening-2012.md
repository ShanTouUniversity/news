With support from the Shantou University Student Entrepreneurship Center and relevant university departments, the former Qiushi Bar in Building 789 has been renovated and renamed the Coffee Bookstore, or “7th Heaven Cafe&apos;.” The venue combines coffee, books, music and leisure space and serves coffee, tea, desserts and fashionable beverages to the STU community.

The cafe will hold its opening celebration at 10 a.m. on October 19, 2012. Customers will receive a 12% discount during the opening period from October 19 through 21.
