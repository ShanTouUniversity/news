Shantou University has publicized the estimated added costs for renovation of 36 staff homes in the west campus. The project began on August 21, 2012. Site conditions and design-review adjustments led to several items outside the original plan. The preliminary estimate is about RMB 90,000, below the 10% limit set by the university.

The added items mainly concern widening bathroom doors; reinforced-concrete caps for existing windowsills and railings; removing, clearing and rebuilding walls altered or loosened during residents&apos; secondary renovations; ordinary marble windowsills and trim on both sides of wooden doors; enclosing kitchen pipes; repairing floor openings after downpipes are removed; adding water and electrical lines for washing machines on balconies; adding bathroom taps and changing drainage routes; and adding hot-water lines for showers and washbasins following the design review.

Comments on the added-cost estimate may be submitted in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office before the deadline.
