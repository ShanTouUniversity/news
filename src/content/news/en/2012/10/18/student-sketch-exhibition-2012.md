Shantou University Changjiang School of Art and Design will host the student sketch exhibition “Searching and Drawing” from October 22 to 30, 2012. The exhibition will present sketch works by one student and is jointly organized by the Changjiang School of Art and Design and the Shantou Artists Association. Faculty members, staff and students are welcome to visit.

The original notice title included the artist&apos;s name and personal number. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish the student&apos;s full name or number and reports the exhibition as a student-art event.
