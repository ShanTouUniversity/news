It is reported that, in accordance with arrangements for the "Provincial Inspection of Personnel Selection Work at Provincial Universities," an inspection team will conduct a questionnaire survey on personnel selection work at Shantou University. The university's Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department has made corresponding arrangements for the Medical College Party Committee and all Party general branches and direct branches on the main campus.

## Survey Arrangements

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Questionnaire collection | Before 12:00 on October 19, at the Organization and United Front Work Department (Room 315, New Administrative Center) |
| Distribution scope | Principal and deputy leaders of university departments, schools/institutes (research centers), and directly affiliated enterprises and institutions; other leading personnel managed by the university Party Committee; faculty and staff representatives |
| Submission time | 8:20-9:30 a.m. on October 22, 2012 |
| Submission location | Designated ballot boxes at the south entrance on the first floor of the New Administrative Center |

According to the arrangements, after collecting the questionnaires, each Party general branch and direct branch must promptly distribute them to the relevant personnel. Respondents are required to submit their completed questionnaires in person to the designated ballot boxes, which will be opened and collected by the provincial inspection team at 9:50 a.m. for tabulation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department)
