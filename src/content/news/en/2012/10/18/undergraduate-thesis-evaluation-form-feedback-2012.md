To further standardize scoring and comments for undergraduate theses and graduation designs and guide units in evaluating outcomes and quality objectively and comprehensively, Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has drafted the *Undergraduate Thesis and Graduation Design Evaluation Form* and is seeking revision suggestions from the schools and departments.

The form has three versions: for supervisors, reviewers and defense committees. After further revision, it is planned for formal release and use from the 2009-entry cohort onward. Schools and departments must organize discussions, consolidate faculty comments and suggestions, and email them to Academic Affairs by October 30, 2012.
