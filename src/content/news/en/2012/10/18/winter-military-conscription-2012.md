Following the Guangdong Provincial Conscription Work Regulations and arrangements from the Shantou Conscription Office, the Shantou University People&apos;s Armed Forces Department has released its winter 2012 military-enlistment notice for eligible current students and graduating students.

The general age range for male recruits is 18–20; the limit is extended to 22 for university students and 24 for graduating students. The general age range for female recruits is 17–19; the limit is extended to 20 for university students and 22 for graduating students. Political review, medical examination and other requirements follow the applicable national regulations.

| Group | Age range | Registration | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current male students | Generally 18–20; university students up to 22 | Complete the registration form and email it to o_xsc@stu.edu.cn; inquiries: 86503299 | Before the end of work on November 7, 2012 |
| Male graduating students | Generally 18–20; graduating students up to 24 | Register at the military-service authority where the household registration is held | According to the local authority&apos;s schedule |
| Female students, including current and graduating students | Generally 17–19; university students up to 20; graduating students up to 22 | Register online at the national college-student pre-enlistment system: http://zbbm.chsi.com.cn | By October 31, 2012 |

Medical requirements include a minimum height of 1.62 meters for men and 1.60 meters for women. Men may weigh no more than 125% and no less than 85% of standard weight; women may weigh no more than 115% and no less than 85%. Standard weight is calculated as “height minus 110” kilograms. For personnel assigned to land forces, uncorrected vision must be at least 4.6 in the right eye and 4.5 in the left eye, with corrected vision at least 5.0. Each eye of special-condition personnel must have uncorrected vision of at least 5.1. Electrocardiography and abdominal ultrasound are routine examinations. Tattoos, scars and ear piercings are assessed under the applicable medical standards.

Political review and medical examination follow the *Regulations on Political Review of Citizens Applying for Military Service*, the *Standards for Physical Examination of Citizens Applying for Military Service* and other relevant rules. The male-student registration form and related regulations are in the original attachments.
