To understand Shantou University&apos;s personnel selection and appointment work over the past three years, summarize experience, identify and correct problems, further standardize procedures and strengthen public confidence, the Organization Department of the CPC Guangdong Provincial Committee and the CPC Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee will inspect STU&apos;s work since September 2009 from October 22 to 24, 2012.

During the inspection, cadres and members of the public may provide information or report issues to the inspection team. They may also submit information through the Organization Department&apos;s reporting telephone or website.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Inspecting bodies | Organization Department of the CPC Guangdong Provincial Committee and CPC Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee |
| Scope | STU personnel selection and appointment work since September 2009 |
| Inspection dates | October 22–24, 2012 |
| Feedback | Provide information or report issues to the inspection team |
| Reporting telephone | 12380; 020-12380-1 |
| Reporting website | www.gd12380.cn |

The inspection team consists of Zhao Songfeng, leader, and members Wang Ruibiao, Liang Qiutang, Meng Nan and Chen Jun.
