It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on October 18, 2012, announcing outstanding members in the 2011–2012 assessment of university-level student organizations.

In accordance with the *STU Student Cadre Management Regulations* and based on members’ performance over the past academic year, the list was determined through self-evaluation, internal assessment within each organization and review by the Youth League Committee. The full list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here to protect student privacy.

> ⚠️ The full list is available on the OA system.
