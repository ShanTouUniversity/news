Shantou University Admissions Office reports that collection of student records from the 2012-entry undergraduate cohort is nearly complete, but a small number of students have not yet submitted their records. Relevant schools must notify the students on the attached list to submit their records to the Admissions Office by October 29, 2012.

Records should be delivered to Room 107 on the first floor of the New Administration Center. Students unable to meet the deadline because of special circumstances must explain the situation to the Admissions Office. Submissions made late without a valid reason will not be accepted, and students will bear responsibility for consequences caused by incomplete records.
