To review volunteer-service experience, recognize exemplary work and advance youth volunteering, the Shantou University Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association have opened the 2011–12 selection for “Youth Volunteer Role Models” and “Outstanding Youth Volunteers.” Candidates should have shown active and outstanding performance in volunteer work and demonstrated the volunteer spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual aid and progress.

Candidates must show positive attitudes, observe the law and university rules, participate in volunteer work without seeking personal gain, regularly take part in on- and off-campus service and receive organizational recognition. They must also have made a meaningful contribution through volunteer activities. Youth League committees and volunteer organizations of the schools and residential colleges will make recommendations, while the university Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteers Association will review and select the honorees.

| Recommending unit | Youth Volunteer Role Models | Outstanding Youth Volunteers |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Each main-campus school | 1–2 | 2–4 |
| Medical School, including affiliated hospital | 4 | 8 |
| Directly affiliated units | 4 | 10 |

Recommendation materials must be submitted to the STU Youth Volunteers Association office by 6 p.m. on October 24, 2012. Both electronic and printed copies are required: the individual volunteer-award recommendation form, a 1,000–2,000-character description of volunteer achievements, one personal photo and a *STU Volunteer Service Hours Certificate*. Hours recorded in the STU Youth Volunteer Registration and Administration System will be used as one reference for the selection.

“Youth Volunteer Role Model” and “Outstanding Youth Volunteer” are mutually exclusive applications. The selected honorees will be recognized at the first plenary meeting of the STU Youth Volunteers Association. The selection will follow principles of fairness, impartiality and openness. Forms and submission formats are provided in the original attachments.
