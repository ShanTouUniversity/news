Shantou University Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication has published its proposed nominee for the 2012 National Scholarship. Following student applications and preliminary review by the school Youth League Committee, four students entered interviews. The school scholarship assessment committee interviewed them on October 18 and considered academic performance, professional skills, awards, and contributions to the university and school. It plans to recommend one student for the National Scholarship.

To protect student privacy, the proposed nominee is identified by family name and the initial of the given name: Lei Y.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Award | 2012 National Scholarship |
| Interview candidates | 4 |
| Proposed nominee | Lei Y |
| Assessment basis | Academic performance, professional skills, awards and contributions to STU and the school |
| Public-notice deadline | October 22, 2012, Monday, noon |
| Feedback location | Journalism School Youth League Committee office |
| Telephone | 0754-82903651 |

Anyone with an objection may submit it in writing to the Journalism School Youth League Committee before the deadline. Organizational materials must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed with the submitter&apos;s real name.
