The Shantou University Student Community X Project will host a session titled “Multiple Cultures, Multiple Ways of Thinking” on October 21, 2012. Teams that won the third STU Student Consumer Culture Research Report Award will share what they learned during their July visit to Hong Kong for the 13th Consumer Culture Research Report Award ceremony, study activities and training. They will also discuss the creative process and reflections behind their award-winning work.

Thirteen teams in the third STU award received first-, second- or third-place honors and awards including Best Topic, Outstanding Work and Excellence. Some of the leading teams attended the ceremony and related study and training activities in Hong Kong, organized by the Consumer Council of Hong Kong and the Education Bureau of the Hong Kong SAR Government.
