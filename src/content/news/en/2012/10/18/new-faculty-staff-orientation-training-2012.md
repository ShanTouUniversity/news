To help newly hired faculty and staff understand the university and the operating procedures of its administrative departments, Shantou University Human Resources Office has scheduled a training session on October 24, 2012. The training will be conducted in Mandarin.

The session is for main-campus faculty and staff hired from October 25, 2011 through October 18, 2012. Units must notify relevant employees to attend on time and email the participant list to jbfan@stu.edu.cn by the end of work on October 23. Foreign employees may decide whether to participate.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Introduction to the university and administrative-department procedures |
| Date | October 24, 2012 |
| Participants | Main-campus faculty and staff hired from October 25, 2011 through October 18, 2012 |
| Language | Mandarin |
| List deadline | Before the end of work on October 23, 2012 |
| Submission email | jbfan@stu.edu.cn |
| Foreign employees | Participation is optional |

The detailed time, venue and program schedule are provided in the original notice. The attached list of newly hired personnel is not reproduced here.
