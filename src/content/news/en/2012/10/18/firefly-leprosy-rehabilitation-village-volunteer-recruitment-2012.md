The Firefly team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association is recruiting students from across the university as long-term volunteers for 2012–13. The program will provide volunteer services at three leprosy-rehabilitation villages in eastern Guangdong and plans to recruit 50 volunteers.

The allocation is six volunteers for Linghou in Chaozhou, 12 for Ganshijing in Puning and 32 for Dongxikeng in Jieyang. Selection will use telephone interviews, and applications close at 5 p.m. on October 21, 2012.

| Service site | Places |
| --- | ---: |
| Linghou Leprosy Rehabilitation Village, Chaozhou | 6 |
| Ganshijing Leprosy Rehabilitation Village, Puning | 12 |
| Dongxikeng Leprosy Rehabilitation Village, Jieyang | 32 |
| Total | 50 |

Students may download the electronic application form from the OA notice or collect a paper form under Dining Hall 2, at the entrance to Dining Hall 4 or at the Golden Triangle consultation point. Paper forms may be submitted at the designated collection points in A210, D104, E132 and J451, or in the collection bag beside the Dining Hall 2 poster. The project will conduct telephone interviews after applications are submitted.

Consultation points will operate under Dining Halls 2 and 4 and at the Golden Triangle from October 18 to 19. Students may also consult the Firefly Project 1 Sina Weibo and NetEase blog for more information. To protect student privacy, student contact names, personal telephone numbers and personal email addresses are not published here.
