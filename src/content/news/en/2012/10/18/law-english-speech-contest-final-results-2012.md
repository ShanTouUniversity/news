Shantou University Law School has announced the results of its fifth Legal English Speech Contest. Following interviews and review by the selection group, the contest awarded two first prizes, three second prizes and four third prizes. The school will recommend the five first- and second-prize students to represent it at the fifth National University Legal English Competition in Beijing in December 2012.

To protect student privacy, names are shown using the family name and the initials of each given-name character. The award list is as follows:

| Award | Students |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | He Y; Ye CP |
| Second prize | Luo HT; Lu XS; Wu SS |
| Third prize | Liang AQ; Zhang JM; Zhang HW; Zhang WL |

The first- and second-prize winners will represent the Law School at the national university Legal English competition in Beijing in December.
