It is reported that the Trade Union of Shantou University issued a notice on October 18, 2012, that following a vote by its Women Workers’ Committee, the Physics Laboratory of the School of Science is proposed as a candidate for Shantou City’s Women’s Model Post.

## Public Notice

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate | Physics Laboratory, School of Science |
| Award | Shantou Women’s Model Post |
| Decision | Vote by the Women Workers’ Committee of the University Trade Union |
| Deadline for comments | Before October 20, 2012 |
| How to submit | In writing to the University Trade Union; organizations should affix an official seal, individuals should sign with real name |
| Phone | 82902983 |
| Email | o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Trade Union |

Those with objections may submit comments to the Trade Union within the specified period.
