The Shantou University School of Science will hold two biology talks in its Science Lecture Series on October 7 and October 10. Professor Xiong Guangming of Kiel University will discuss regulation of related gene expression by the PhaR protein and applications of short-chain dehydrogenases in medicine, pharmaceuticals and environmental biology.

| Talk | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regulation of 3,17β-SHD gene expression by PhaR protein from testosterone-degrading bacteria | October 7, Sunday, 10–11 a.m. | Room 205, Science Building |
| Applications of short-chain dehydrogenases in medicine, pharmaceuticals and environmental biology | October 10, Wednesday, 3–4 p.m. | Room 205, Science Building |

Professor Xiong has taught at Giessen University and Marburg University in Germany and is currently a professor at the Institute of Pharmacology and Virus Research at Kiel University. He also serves as an adjunct professor at Peking University, Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University and Changchun University of Science and Technology. Faculty members and students interested in the topics are welcome to attend.
