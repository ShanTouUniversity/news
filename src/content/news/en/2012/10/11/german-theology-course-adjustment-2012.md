Because Professor Gu Bin of the School of Liberal Arts will be away attending a conference, the Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has revised the schedule for Class 57347, 20th-Century German Theology. The classes originally scheduled for periods 8–9 on Tuesday, December 4, and Thursday, December 6, in D203 during Week 12 will be cancelled and made up earlier.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Make-up classes | Week 10 Saturday, November 24, periods 8–9; Week 11 Sunday, November 25, periods 8–9 |
| Make-up venue | D203 |
| Cancelled classes | Week 12 Tuesday, December 4, and Thursday, December 6, periods 8–9 |

The Academic Affairs Office asks students enrolled in the course to share the update and attend the make-up classes as scheduled.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
