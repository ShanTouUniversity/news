The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education has allocated Shantou University's quotas for the 2012 national scholarships and grants. The Student Affairs Office distributed the quotas according to student numbers, the number of students facing financial hardship and the number of applications in each college and residential college.

| Award | Quota |
| --- | ---: |
| National Scholarship | 19 students |
| National Encouragement Scholarship | 291 students |
| National Grant | 1,300 students |

Based on the applications received, the National Grant application period has been extended by two days. Students who need support must submit their applications according to their college or residential college arrangements by 5 p.m. on October 12, 2012. The Student Affairs Office attachment provides the detailed distribution by unit.
