To strengthen the Library Student Activity Hall's service to the university community and improve its content and management, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office is recruiting undergraduate management teams from across the university. The team will be responsible for the hall's daily administration and operation.

The recruitment encourages cooperation across majors and year groups. Applicant teams must complete the application form and submit an operating concept plan as an attachment to `liran@stu.edu.cn`. Applications close at 11 p.m. on October 21, 2012. Enquiries can be made at `82903299`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachments for the application form and operating concept plan template.
