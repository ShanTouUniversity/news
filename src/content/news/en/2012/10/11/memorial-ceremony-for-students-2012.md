Shantou University will hold a “May They Rest in Peace” memorial ceremony at 7 p.m. on October 11, 2012, in the open area in front of the Grand Hall. The ceremony will remember the four students who died by drowning on October 4. The arrangements were issued jointly by the Student Affairs Office, STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association.

Faculty members and students attending the ceremony are asked to:

- Wear simple, subdued clothing;
- Follow directions from on-site staff and volunteers;
- Use the flowers provided at the venue or bring flowers in plain colors if they wish.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association)
>
> ⚠️ This article does not publish the deceased students' names or other personal information.
