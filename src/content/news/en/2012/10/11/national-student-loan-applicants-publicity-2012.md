The Shantou University Student Affairs Office has publicized the review results for the first batch of national student-loan applications for the 2012–2013 academic year. Fifty students passed review by the Student Aid Management Center, and the list is now open for public comment.

Organizations or individuals with objections may submit comments in writing, in person or by telephone to the Student Affairs Office Student Aid Management Center by Wednesday, October 17, 2012. The telephone number is `82904749`. Organizations must stamp their submissions with an official seal, while individuals must provide their real names.
