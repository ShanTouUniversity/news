On October 4, four Shantou University students died by drowning while visiting Nanshan Bay in Haojiang District. The students were from the 2011 computer science cohort and the 2012 cohorts in electronics, chemistry and business administration. The University Labor Union, Student Affairs Office, STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association have jointly launched a donation appeal to provide support to the families.

Donations will be collected from October 11 through 17, 2012. Faculty members and students may contribute through their college Youth League Committee, donation points on campus or the labor union of their department.

| Donation channel | Consolidation arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Faculty and students in each college | Collected and registered by the college Youth League Committee and consolidated to the STU Youth League Committee on October 17 |
| Campus donation points | Consolidated by the STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association |
| Department labor unions | Staff donations collected by department labor unions and consolidated to the University Labor Union on October 17 |

After consolidation, the university will divide the donations equally among the four families. The organizers said the appeal is intended both to express remembrance and to provide practical support to the families during a difficult period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union, Student Affairs Office, STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteers Association)
