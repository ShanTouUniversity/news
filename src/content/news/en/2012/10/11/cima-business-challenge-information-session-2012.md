The CIMA 2013 Global Business Challenge will hold a campus information session at Shantou University on October 13, 2012. Qu Weiyi, CIMA's market development manager for Guangdong, China, will introduce the competition and its participation opportunities. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome to attend.

The CIMA Global Business Challenge launches promotion and registration simultaneously in five regions of mainland China each year. Organized according to the principles of fairness, impartiality and openness, it selects student teams with strong business potential and knowledge of finance, markets, marketing and management.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | October 13, 2012, Saturday, 3–5 p.m. |
| Venue | E Staircase Lecture Room 203 |
| Speaker | Qu Weiyi, CIMA market development manager for Guangdong, China |
| Audience | STU faculty members and students |
| Competition | A five-region mainland China competition selecting future business leaders through student teams |

More information is available through the CIMA challenge website (gbc.cncima.com/case.jsp), the CIMA global website (cimaglobal.com) and the CIMA Chinese website (cncima.com).
