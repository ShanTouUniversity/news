The Shantou University Business School has opened registration for the 2012 Global Management Challenge (GMC). Certified by the European Foundation for Management Development (EFMD), the competition is a business-management simulation contest open to undergraduates and graduate students across the university.

Applicants must have completed at least two economics or management courses and be proficient in Excel. Students may register individually, in which case the GMC organizing committee will help form teams, or register as teams of three to four. The committee is also recruiting three management staff members; applicants for those positions should write “management staff application” when submitting the form.

Registration forms must be submitted to the STU GMC organizing committee by midnight on October 13, 2012. The committee plans to notify applicants on October 14 about training and warm-up competition selection. Detailed application materials are provided in the OA attachment.
