Shanghai Yuanda Law Firm will hold an information session at Shantou University Law School on October 12, 2012. The session is intended for the 2009 and 2010 cohorts of law undergraduates and graduate students at the Law School. The firm will introduce its work, practical legal skills and the responsibilities of interns.

The session will also include time for questions and interaction, giving students an opportunity to learn about legal practice and internship opportunities.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Shanghai Yuanda Law Firm |
| Time | October 12, 2012, Friday, 7–8:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Law School 213 |
| Audience | 2009 and 2010 law students and graduate students at the Law School |
| Topics | Firm introduction, legal practice, intern work and questions |
