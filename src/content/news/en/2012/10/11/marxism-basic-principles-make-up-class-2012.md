Because Cheng Yingzi of the Department of Social Sciences will be away attending a conference, the Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has scheduled a make-up class for Class 54242, Basic Principles of Marxism. The class originally cancelled during periods A–C on Friday of Week 1, September 21, 2012, will be made up during periods A–C on Saturday of Week 4, October 13, in E208.

The Academic Affairs Office asks students enrolled in the course to share the update and attend the make-up class on time.
