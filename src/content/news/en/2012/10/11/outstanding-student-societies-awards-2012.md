It is reported that, to better guide the development of student societies and to recognize outstanding groups and individuals, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University will carry out the 2011–2012 selection of "Outstanding Student Society," "Outstanding Student Society President," and "Outstanding Student Society Worker" in October 2012.

## Selection Arrangements

The Youth League Committee has asked the Student Societies Federation and its affiliated societies to conduct self-evaluation and nomination in accordance with the respective selection plans and regulations. Societies that have already submitted materials for the awards need not resubmit.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Organizer | Communist Youth League Committee of STU |
| Awards | Outstanding Student Society; Outstanding Student Society President; Outstanding Student Society Worker |
| Academic year | 2011–2012 |
| Submission deadline | October 15, 2012 |
| Required materials | Self-evaluation form for Outstanding Student Society, application forms for Outstanding President/Worker, and supporting materials (electronic version) |
| Submission email | 11xmzheng@stu.edu.cn |
| Contact | Zheng |
| Further updates | Federation homepage http://asso.stu.edu.cn/Asso/ and the Federation section on the Tulip BBS |

Detailed regulations and forms are available in the attachments.
