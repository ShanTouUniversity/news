Shantou University's Toward the Ocean public-service course is inviting the university community to design a logo for its volunteer service team. The call supports the course action “Marine Territorial Awareness and Marine Ecosystem Protection Education,” presents the image of STU youth volunteers and strengthens the course's public-service impact.

Individuals or teams may submit designs by midnight on October 19, 2012. Designs must be submitted as electronic images in any format and may include a brief explanation of the concept. They should focus on “Caring about the ocean and protecting the ocean,” with an original concept, contemporary character and clear composition.

All submissions must be original, and contributors must ensure that their work does not infringe the rights of others. The activity offers one gift for the selected designer. By submitting a design, the contributor agrees that the organizers may propose revisions after adoption and negotiate those revisions with the author; once adopted, the public-service course team will have permanent rights to use the logo.

The original notice governs the submission email and file requirements. This article does not publish student contacts or personal contact information.
