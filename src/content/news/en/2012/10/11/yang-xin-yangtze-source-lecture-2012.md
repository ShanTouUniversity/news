To promote environmental awareness and help faculty and students understand the ecological conditions at the source of the Yangtze, Shantou University's advanced outdoor program has invited Yang Xin, president of the Green River Environmental Protection Promotion Association, to give “Green River: From Hoh Xil to the Source of the Yangtze.”

In 1996, Yang organized scientists and journalists to conduct the first comprehensive ecological survey of the Yangtze source and helped bring its environmental issues to public attention. In 1999, he proposed and planned an environmental-protection monument at the source of the Yangtze and later wrote *The Spirit of the Yangtze* based on his expedition experience. With support from the public and volunteers, the related public-service effort raised funds and built the Sonam Dargye Nature Protection Station over four years in an uninhabited area at an altitude of 4,500 meters at the Yangtze source, making it a frontline base for Tibetan antelope conservation.
