It is reported that the International Exchange and Cooperation Office of Shantou University recently forwarded a letter from the Israeli Consulate General in Guangzhou, introducing a short-term training program organized by MASHAV — Israel's Agency for International Development Cooperation under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs — for developing countries. Applications are now open, and interested faculty and students may register directly with the Israeli Consulate General in Guangzhou.

## Five Professional Fields with Flexible Durations

MASHAV is a short-term training initiative established by the Israeli government specifically for third-world countries. The training covers five major fields:

| No. | Field |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Agriculture |
| 2 | Education and Community Development |
| 3 | Economic and Social Development |
| 4 | Urban and Rural Development |
| 5 | Medicine and Public Health |

Programs run from one week to three months, offering considerable flexibility. According to the consulate's letter, an education-related course is currently being offered and has been circulated to universities for reference.

## How to Apply

Interested applicants may register directly with the Israeli Consulate General in Guangzhou. Course details and application materials are available in the attachments forwarded by the International Exchange and Cooperation Office, including course introduction documents and the 2012 application form.
