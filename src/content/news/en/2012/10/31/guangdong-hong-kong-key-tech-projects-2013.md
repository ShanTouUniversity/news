Shantou University’s Research Office has forwarded a Guangdong Department of Science and Technology call for proposals for 2013 key breakthrough projects in Guangdong–Hong Kong cooperation areas. The proposals will provide an important reference for preparing the 2013 project guide. Interested faculty members should submit two hard copies and an electronic version to the Research Office by November 7.

### Project requirements

Projects should generally run from 2013 through 2015 and demonstrate an advanced level representative of the relevant fields in Guangdong. The call encourages cooperation between Guangdong and Hong Kong and asks projects to demonstrate technological innovation and cumulative progress during implementation, providing sustained support for independent innovation in Guangdong.
