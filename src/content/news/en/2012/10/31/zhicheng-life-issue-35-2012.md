Shantou University’s Zhicheng College has published *Zhicheng Life* Issue 6 of 2012, the 35th issue overall. The issue records recent college activities in psychological resilience, career development, traditional-culture education, safety education and student organizations.

### Resilience and career development

On October 27, the college held its fifth psychological-resilience training camp. The one-day-and-one-night program used lectures, development games, self-exploration, an inspirational film and discussion. Activities such as “Blind Person and Mute Person: A Journey of Life,” “Lifeline and Breaking Through Difficulties” and “Electric Grid of Life and Death” addressed mental health, resilience, self-respect, tolerance and gratitude.

On October 19, the college held its 10th career-planning sharing session. Wang Jingjian, Chen Yanmei and Xu Siming, teachers with the Teach For China program, shared their decisions to work in public service. More than 40 residents attended and discussed communication, teamwork, university planning and career choices.

### Lectures and public-service exchange

On October 14, Professor Liu Yuli of the Department of Philosophy at the Party School of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China gave the 13th Zhicheng Forum lecture, “Traditional Culture and University Life,” in the New Library Lecture Hall. Drawing on classics including the *I Ching* and *Analects*, Liu discussed the contemporary value of traditional culture, personal development and students’ sense of mission, followed by an exchange with students.

On October 13, renowned quantum chemist Professor Pan Yugang met Zhicheng College students during a public-service visit to STU and discussed interest, practice and capability development. On October 11, Liu Lei, head of the China Roots and Shoots Rural Education and Development Promotion Association, visited the college to share public-service experience and encourage students to learn about and participate in public service.

### College administration and student activities

On October 18, Zhicheng College Dean Li Dan shared public-speaking skills with 40 residents from different majors and year groups, emphasizing confidence, preparation and connecting with an audience. On October 10, the college held a safety-education meeting for student leaders in Lecture Classroom E203. Nearly 200 student leaders attended; the meeting addressed personal and property safety and included a collective moment of silence.

On October 28, the Zhicheng Student Union held the preliminary round of the “Zhicheng Style” talent competition, providing a platform for residents and selecting representatives for the university’s Top Ten Singers competition. Twelve contestants advanced to the final, scheduled for the evening of November 4 at Yifang. The college also held its Freshman Cup basketball tournament; the Second and Fourth Squads decided the championship and runner-up with a 45–37 result on October 21.

The issue also reports themed Youth League activities on academic conduct, including discussions, outstanding-student sharing, hiking and morning reading, reading exchanges and film discussions. A delegation from Shanghai University’s Community College visited to study the residential-college model, while Zhicheng College representatives took part in a Moral Culture Forum in Pao-tai Town, Jieyang. *China Education Daily* had also published an in-depth report on the college’s residential-college reform.
