Shantou University’s Logistics Office Student Community Management and Services Center has announced seasonal schedules for hot-water supply and electricity in student dormitories. Dormitory Buildings A, B, C, D, E, F, G and J will adopt the winter hot-water and lighting schedule from November 1, 2012.

| Season | Hot-water supply | Electricity schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer (May 1–October 31, excluding summer vacation) | 5:00–6:30 p.m. and 10:00–11:00 p.m. | Available all day |
| Winter (November 1–April 30 of the following year, excluding winter vacation) | 4:30–10:30 p.m. | 6:00 a.m.–11:30 p.m. |

To switch the electricity facilities to the winter schedule, the Student Community Management and Services Center adjusted the facilities in the eight buildings from 3:00 to 6:00 p.m. on October 31. Each building was expected to experience an outage of about 15 minutes. A bell would sound 15 minutes beforehand so residents could prepare.

Residents with questions may call 82902661 or the 24-hour duty mobile at 13556304313 (short number 694313).
