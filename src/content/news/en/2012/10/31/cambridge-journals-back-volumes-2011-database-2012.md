Shantou University Library has announced that the National Library of China introduced the Cambridge University Press Journals Back Volumes 2011 database in 2012 under a national license. Nonprofit academic users in mainland China can access the archived full-text journal content free of charge through the Cambridge University Press platform. STU faculty members and students can enter through the university library’s database portal.

Cambridge University Press journals cover a broad range of humanities, social sciences and natural sciences. The collection is particularly strong in mathematics, physics, materials science, environmental science and resource conservation, agriculture and life sciences, neuroscience, history, regional studies, economics, languages and linguistics, politics and international relations, and law.

### How to access the archive

Back volumes and current issues share the same platform. After opening a journal homepage, select “Back Volumes” on the left side of the interface to browse issues by publication year.

The library’s database entry is available at [Cambridge Journals Back Volumes](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=357). The original notice recommended opening the link in a new window with Internet Explorer.
