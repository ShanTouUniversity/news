To encourage attention to and protection of the ocean, Shantou University’s public-service course *Toward the Ocean* held a call for logo designs for its practical service team. Following a review by the course instructors, a submission by Cai J, a 2011 economics student at the Business School, was selected. The organizer will present the student with a commemorative gift.

After consulting the designer, the course team refined the logo from the original concept. The central graphic represents the outlines of China’s four seas: the Yellow Sea, Bohai Sea, South China Sea and East China Sea. The two curved forms reference the “O” in “ocean” and also symbolize hands protecting China’s marine environment.

The course team thanked all participating students and encouraged the university community to continue supporting ocean conservation and related public-service activities.
