Ahead of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee Organization Department produced the eight-episode documentary series *Guangdong Party Delegates in Action*. The series presents the achievements and stories of Guangdong’s delegates to the 18th National Party Congress. In accordance with requirements from the Shantou municipal authorities, STU Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches will organize faculty and student Party members and activists seeking Party membership to watch the series and report their viewing activities to the University Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department.

| Platform | Schedule and access |
| --- | --- |
| Guangdong TV Satellite Channel | One episode at 5:30 p.m. each evening from November 1 to 8 |
| Guangdong TV News Channel | Rebroadcast at 6:00 p.m. on the same day |
| Guangdong Party Members and Officials Modern Distance Education platform | Available from 9:00 a.m. on November 2; stations across Guangdong can play the episodes on demand |
| Guangdong Rural Party Members and Officials Modern Distance Education website | Available from 9:00 a.m. on November 2 in the “Distance Education Videos” section |

Each unit should compile its viewing information and send the report to the University Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department at o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Organization and United Front Work Department)
