Shantou University Graduate School will hold a meeting on November 2, 2012, to coordinate the university’s 2012 outstanding graduate student selection. Each graduate education unit on the main campus should send its leader responsible for graduate affairs or a designated representative. Representatives who have participated in their unit’s previous selection work are preferred.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | 2012 STU Outstanding Graduate Student Selection |
| Time | 3:00 p.m., Friday, November 2, 2012 |
| Venue | Conference Room 426, New Administration Center |
| Participants | Leaders responsible for graduate affairs or designated representatives from graduate education units |
