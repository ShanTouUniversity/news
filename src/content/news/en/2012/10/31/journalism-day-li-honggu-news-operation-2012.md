The Journalism Day lecture series of Shantou University’s Yangtze River Journalism and Communication School will continue on November 2, 2012. Li Honggu, executive editor-in-chief of *Sanlian Life Week*, will present “The Practice and Communication of Event News in Three-Layer Relationships,” sharing experience in reporting and communicating event news.

Born in 1966, Li previously worked as a sports reporter and newspaper editorial board member. He was named one of China Publishing Group’s Top Ten Editors in 2012. During 12 years at *Sanlian Life Week*, he completed more than 120 cover stories and worked on major reports marking the 60th anniversary of victory in the War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression, the 60th anniversary of the founding of the People’s Republic of China, the Wenchuan earthquake, the Beijing Olympics and the centenary of the Xinhai Revolution. His published collections include *The Dispossessed* (2009), *Putin’s Politics* (2012) and *The Beginning of China as a Nation: A Revolution* (2012).
