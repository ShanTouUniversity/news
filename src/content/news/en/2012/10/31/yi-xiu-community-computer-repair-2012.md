Shantou University’s Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee has launched Yi Xiu, a Community Care Plan computer repair service for faculty and students who encounter computer problems but lack the technical skills or tools to address them. The service will run from October 2012 through June 2013, from 8:00 to 11:00 p.m. every Thursday, Friday and Saturday, excluding major national holidays.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service users | All STU faculty members and students |
| Service hours | 8:00–11:00 p.m. every Thursday, Friday and Saturday, October 2012–June 2013 |
| Location | Student Dormitory C411, Dormitory Committee Office |
| Technical support | Professional technicians from the Acer notebook general distributor at Shantou Hi-Tech Computer City |

The service covers common issues including software-related internet or startup failures, laptop dust removal and thermal-paste replacement, desktop cable management, and blue screens or startup failures caused by oxidation. Other minor problems may also be reviewed on site.

The committee will also hold occasional small lectures on common computer troubleshooting, basic computer knowledge, and routine maintenance for desktop and laptop computers. Updates will be posted on dormitory-area committee bulletin boards. Reservations and inquiries can be made through the official STU Dormitory Committee channel.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
