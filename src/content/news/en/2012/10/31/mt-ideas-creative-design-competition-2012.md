The MT Enterprise Innovation Club at Shantou University’s School of Engineering has launched the second MT-IDEAS creative design competition, inviting students across the university to submit project concepts. The competition encourages proposals that combine internet technology applications with art and design and business practice.

### Entry requirements and schedule

The competition is team-based. Each team should have two to five members, appoint a leader and choose a team name. Registration and submission close on November 11, 2012. The preliminary defense is scheduled for November 14, and the final, featuring a PowerPoint presentation and public defense, will be held on November 24.

Participants should send the submission form and project materials to moderntop@gmail.com and rename the file using the format “project name + team name.” Teams with additional multimedia materials should contact the organizers.

### Focus areas and support

Proposals should focus primarily on one of three areas:

- E-commerce applications, such as group buying, shared purchasing or online service platforms;
- Mobile game applications and mobile internet services;
- Network-system applications, such as reservation systems and online teaching systems.

Subject to the cooperation preferences of the top three teams, each winning project may apply for a technology project at the School of Engineering and receive up to RMB 2,000 in project funding. Winning teams will receive certificates and retain the commercial value generated by their IDEA projects, including prize money, copyright and operating profits, as well as ownership of the projects.
