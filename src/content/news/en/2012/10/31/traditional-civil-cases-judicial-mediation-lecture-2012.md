The 255th Law School Lecture at Shantou University will be held on November 3, 2012. Wang Yayuan and Zeng Jin of the Shenzhen Intermediate People’s Court will speak on “Judicial Mediation in Traditional Civil Cases.” Faculty members and students across the university are welcome to attend.

| --- | --- |
| Topic | Judicial Mediation in Traditional Civil Cases |
| Speakers | Wang Yayuan and Zeng Jin, Shenzhen Intermediate People’s Court |
| Time | 10:00 a.m.–noon, November 3, 2012 |
| Venue | Classroom D304 |
| Audience | All STU faculty members and students |
