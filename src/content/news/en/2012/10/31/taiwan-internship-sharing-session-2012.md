Shantou University’s Yangtze River Journalism and Communication School will hold a Taiwan internship sharing session on November 3, 2012. Eleven students who completed internships in Taiwan will discuss how Taiwanese media operate, how aspiring journalists can build their skills and how students can prepare for competition for limited internship placements.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2012 Taiwan Internship Sharing Session |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., Saturday, November 3, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Speakers | 11 students who completed internships in Taiwan |
| Media represented | Central News Agency, Want Daily, TVBS and NOWNEWS online newspaper |

The session will introduce participants to the working methods of different Taiwanese media organizations and practical experience in reporting, photography and media research. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
