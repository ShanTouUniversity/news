Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has arranged final assessments for the fall 2012 semester under the *STU Measures for the Administration of Course Assessments* (STU Document No. 78, 2007) and the 2012–2013 academic calendar. Final assessments for the semester will be scheduled during Weeks 17 and 18.

Courses whose planned teaching extends to Week 15 or later will have their final assessments scheduled in Weeks 17 and 18. All other courses require a *Shantou University Final Assessment Form*, with examination-course information completed by the teaching unit where the instructor is based.

### Scheduling and reporting requirements

The Academic Affairs Office will centrally arrange the time and venue for all courses offered by the Department of Social Sciences, Computer Teaching Center and Institute of Higher Education, as well as General Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Calculus, and Probability and Mathematical Statistics. Teaching-unit academic administrators will schedule other courses through the credit-based academic system.

Units must verify course offerings and class numbers when completing the form. The assessment method should match the course syllabus and may be an open-book or closed-book examination, oral examination, course paper or a combination. The standard examination duration is 120 minutes. For a course that cannot be completed in 120 minutes, the instructor must apply to the teaching-unit head; after approval, the academic administrator may enter the requested duration. Instructors may not change the duration without unit approval.

The Academic Affairs Office and teaching-unit academic administrators will jointly complete the examination schedule in the credit-based system. After approval and signature by the unit head, each unit must submit the forms to the Student Affairs Section of the Academic Affairs Office by Thursday, November 8, 2012, Week 8, and email the electronic version to xhx@stu.edu.cn.
