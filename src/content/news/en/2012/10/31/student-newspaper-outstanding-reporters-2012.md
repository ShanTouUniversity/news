Following a review, *Shantou University Newspaper* selected nine outstanding student reporters for the 2011–2012 academic year and announced the results on October 31, 2012. The awardees came from the 2009, 2010 and 2011 journalism cohorts: one from the 2009 cohort, six from the 2010 cohort and two from the 2011 cohort.

Student names, cohort details and other personal information are not reproduced here. One name in the preserved archive is corrupted and has not been reconstructed speculatively.
