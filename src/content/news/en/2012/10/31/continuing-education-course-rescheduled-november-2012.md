Shantou University’s Yangtze River Journalism and Communication School has announced that a continuing-education class originally scheduled for November 1, 2012, has been rescheduled for 10:00 a.m. on November 7. The class will take place in the New Library Lecture Hall. Faculty members who enrolled in the class should attend at the revised time.

The lecturer’s name is garbled in the preserved archive and has not been reconstructed speculatively.
