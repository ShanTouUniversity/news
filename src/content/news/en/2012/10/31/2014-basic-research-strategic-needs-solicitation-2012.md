In response to the Ministry of Science and Technology’s letter on soliciting strategic basic-research priorities for 2014 (Guo Ke Ji Han [2012] No. 30), Shantou University’s Research Office is collecting proposals from relevant units. Proposals should address national development needs and follow the format supplied with the notice.

Faculty members who wish to submit proposals must send the electronic form to the Research Office by noon on November 5, 2012. The form asks for the field, an analysis of national strategic needs and key scientific questions to be addressed, major research content and objectives, the proposed principal investigator and the proposing unit. The two narrative sections are each limited to 300 Chinese characters.

The available fields are agricultural science, energy science, information science, resources and environmental science, health science, materials science, manufacturing and engineering science, comprehensive interdisciplinary science, major scientific frontiers, quantum regulation research, nanoscience, protein research, development and reproduction research, stem-cell research, and global-change research.
