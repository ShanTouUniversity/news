Shantou University has issued Document No. 133 (2012). Following deliberation at the 12th University Leadership Meeting of 2012 on October 25, the university appointed Deng Jianguang executive director of the Institute for Local Government Development. Li Ping no longer serves as the institute’s director.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Appointee | Deng Jianguang |
| Appointment | Executive director, Institute for Local Government Development |
| Previous director | Li Ping no longer serves as the institute’s director |
| Deciding meeting | 12th University Leadership Meeting of 2012, October 25 |
| Document date | October 31, 2012 |
