Shantou University’s Student Entrepreneurship Center has launched a business-plan competition for campus souvenir ventures, inviting full-time undergraduate students to submit ideas for operating a campus souvenir business. The competition is intended to develop students’ entrepreneurial awareness, creativity and practical business skills. Entries must be submitted by teams with at least two core members.

### Plans and submission

Teams should submit a feasible operating plan that generally covers market analysis, market positioning, business model, capital investment, revenue forecast, and opportunities and risks. A judging panel of experts and representatives from university departments will evaluate the entries publicly, taking into account feasibility, team strength, investment and projected returns. An in-person defense may be arranged when necessary.

Materials must be submitted by November 25, 2012. Teams should email the electronic materials to zpchen@stu.edu.cn with the subject “Campus Souvenir Business-Plan Competition,” following the organizer’s requirements for identifying the authors and their year, major and school. Five sealed hard copies should be delivered to the Student Entrepreneurship Center, Room 106 of the New Administration Center.

### Selection and awards

The Student Entrepreneurship Center will organize the evaluation from November 26 to 30, with results scheduled for publication on the university’s office automation information website in early December. The competition offers one selected-project award of RMB 1,000 and five finalist awards of RMB 200 each.

Winning teams that meet the requirements of the *Interim Measures for the Administration of the Shantou University Student Entrepreneurship Fund* and the *Interim Measures for the Administration of the Shantou University Student Entrepreneurship Park* may apply for entrepreneurship funding and admission to the entrepreneurship park. For teams admitted to the park, the center will also help seek university approval to use the STU name and logo to produce and sell campus souvenirs from January 1 through December 30, 2013.
