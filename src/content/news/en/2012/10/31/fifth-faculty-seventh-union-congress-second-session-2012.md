With approval from the Shantou University Party Committee, the university plans to hold the second session of its fifth Faculty Congress and seventh Trade Union Congress in early to mid-December 2012. The session will last half a day, with the exact date and time to be announced separately.

Under the *Regulations on Faculty and Staff Congresses at Regular Institutions of Higher Education in Guangdong Province*, an original electoral unit must hold a by-election if its elected representatives have left the university or retired. The unit should submit the by-election results to the university trade union by November 15, 2012.
