Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has announced procedures for employees seeking degree education while working. Under the *STU Regulations on the Administration of Employees’ Degree Education While Working* (STU Document No. 79, 2011), employees who need to pursue further degree education, including auxiliary contract personnel, must complete an application before applying to a program. The application requires approval from the employee’s unit, review by the Human Resources Office and approval by the university leader responsible for the matter.

### Main requirements

- The regulations cover on-the-job employees, including employees within the formal staffing system and personnel agency employees. Eligible programs include doctoral, master’s and other professional degrees.
- Applicants should in principle have worked at STU for at least two years. The university will in principle approve degree education for teaching staff only when it is required by their work. Teaching support and administrative staff are generally not approved except for pursuing a master’s degree at STU while working.
- Further education should improve professional capability, remain closely related to the employee’s duties and follow the principle of field alignment. Units must ensure that leave from work for study does not affect normal operations. Employees who already hold a master’s degree will not be approved to pursue a second master’s degree.

### Tuition and approval

Teaching staff approved to pursue a doctoral degree while working and who subsequently obtain the degree may be reimbursed for 80% of tuition upon presenting tuition invoices, with reimbursement capped at RMB 25,000. For employees approved to pursue a master’s degree at STU while working, when total tuition exceeds RMB 25,000, the payable amount is calculated at 50%; if that calculation is below RMB 25,000, the payable amount is RMB 25,000. When total tuition is RMB 25,000 or less, the actual tuition is payable.

The application process consists of completing the *Application Form for Employees’ Degree Education While Working*, obtaining unit approval, receiving Human Resources Office review and submitting the application for approval by the responsible university leader. Teaching staff approved to pursue doctoral degrees while working must also sign the *STU Agreement on Employees’ Further Study While Working*. The application form and management regulations were included with the original notice.
