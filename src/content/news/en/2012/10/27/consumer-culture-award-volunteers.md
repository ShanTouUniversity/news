It is reported that the award ceremony of the 3rd STU Consumer Culture Investigative Report Award will be held on November 3, and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has recently launched a campus-wide recruitment for volunteers, with 25 places available. The application deadline is 6:00 pm on October 29.

The award originated in Hong Kong, where it has become one of the most popular project-based learning activities, having been successfully staged fourteen times. In 2009 it opened to mainland students for the first time, with Shantou University becoming the first mainland university to take part. In 2012, the program was incorporated into the university's Community X initiative and is now jointly organized by the Hong Kong Consumer Council and STU's Student Affairs Office, reaching its third edition at the university.

## Volunteer Arrangements

According to the recruitment notice, selected volunteers will receive relevant training and serve at the ceremony on November 2 and 3:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 25 |
| Eligibility | All STU students |
| Working dates | November 2–3 |
| Deadline | 6:00 pm, October 29 |

Applicants should complete the official volunteer registration form, prefix the file name with their year, major and name, and send it by email to STUCCSA@163.com.

Requirements include good communication and organizational skills, a spirit of teamwork and strong work efficiency. Candidates must also be free from classes or other commitments during two sessions on November 2 (10:00 am–1:00 pm and 2:00 pm–4:00 pm) and throughout November 3.
