Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office has issued Academic Conduct Bulletin No. 210, covering classroom checks during Week 6 of the 2012–2013 fall semester. The academic-conduct team inspected Building E on October 23 and 26 and recorded cases of lateness, wearing slippers in class and bringing breakfast into classrooms.

| Inspection date | Time | Venue | Findings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| October 23, 2012 | 2:00 p.m. | Building E | 12 students arrived within five minutes after class began; 4 arrived more than five minutes late |
| October 26, 2012 | 8:00 a.m. | Building E | 1 student wore slippers; 3 brought breakfast into the classroom; 22 arrived within five minutes after class began; 6 arrived more than five minutes late |

The bulletin asks each school and residential college to provide criticism and education to the students concerned and to continue its academic-conduct work. Student names and other personal information are not reproduced here; the detailed list is available in the OA attachment.
