Shantou University’s 20th Campus Top Ten Singers competition will run from November through December 2012. Organized by the university Youth League Committee and Student Union, the event is intended to develop student arts leaders and enrich campus cultural life. It offers two qualification tracks, through schools or residential colleges and through residential-community auditions; contestants may enter both tracks.

### Entry routes

Contestants may register through the Youth League Committee of their school or residential college. The Schools of Liberal Arts, Law, Business, Science, Engineering, Art, Medicine and Journalism, the Graduate School and Zhicheng College may each recommend two teams after holding internal selections. Teams may consist of solo, duet or group performances; standalone band entries are not accepted.

Contestants may also enter the community auditions through their residential area. The community audition and qualification round will produce five finalists eligible for the campus competition preliminary round. Contestants who have already received a school or residential-college recommendation may not enter the community auditions.

If one team qualifies through both a school or residential-college recommendation and the qualification round’s top five, it may advance only as a top-five qualifier. The relevant school or residential college must recommend another team for the preliminary round.

### Awards and scoring

Every contestant reaching the preliminary round will receive the title “Outstanding Singer of the 20th STU Campus Top Ten Singers Competition.” The preliminary round will select 10 teams for the final and present one Most Promising Newcomer Award and one Best Original Singer Award. The newcomer award is mainly intended for contestants from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts.

Finalists will receive the title “2012 STU Campus Top Ten Singer” and compete for first, second and third place, as well as awards for best costume, best stage presence, most popular contestant and best single. Scoring will comprise song content at 10%, singing ability at 70%, stage presence, including instrumental accompaniment, costume and performance, at 15%, and audience response at 5%. Detailed rules will be announced separately.

The competition will also use The Final Passport. Passes will be distributed on the evening of the community-track qualification round and at school or residential-college finals. Collecting four or more different passes will allow the holder to exchange them for one VIP ticket to the final. Redemption details will be released through the STU Student Union Weibo account and the Top Ten Singers Weibo account.
