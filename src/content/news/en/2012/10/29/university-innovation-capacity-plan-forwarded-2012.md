Shantou University’s Party and Administrative Office has forwarded a notice from the Ministry of Education and Ministry of Finance issuing the *Implementation Plan for the Higher-Education Innovation Capacity Enhancement Plan*. All university units are asked to organize study of the plan in accordance with the Guangdong education authorities’ forwarding document.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Forwarded document | Ministry of Education and Ministry of Finance notice on the implementation plan for the Higher-Education Innovation Capacity Enhancement Plan |
| Document number | Education Technology [2012] No. 7 |
| Provincial reference | Guangdong Education Science and Technology Correspondence [2012] No. 57 |
| Requirement | Each unit should organize study |
| Forwarding office | STU Party and Administrative Office |

The full implementation plan is provided in the original notice attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office)
