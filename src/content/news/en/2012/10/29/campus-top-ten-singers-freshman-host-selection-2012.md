Shantou University’s 20th Campus Top Ten Singers competition will begin in November 2012. To give freshmen an opportunity to perform and gain experience, the university Youth League Committee and Student Union are selecting hosts from the 2012 freshman class. Selected hosts will receive training from experienced hosts from previous competitions and host this year’s event alongside them.

Applicants should have strong language organization and communication skills, clear and fluent Mandarin, confidence on stage, the ability to engage an audience and the flexibility to handle unexpected situations. Careful work and attentive communication are also expected.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | 2012 freshmen |
| Registration deadline | Noon on October 30, 2012 |
| Interview | 2:00 p.m. on October 31, 2012; detailed arrangements to follow |
| Selection | 2:00 p.m. on November 3, 2012 |
| Interview and selection venue | Meeting Room 726, Building 7 |
| Application material | Form downloaded from the internal campus information network |
| Submission email | 12dscai@stu.edu.cn |

Applicants should complete the form and follow the notice’s instructions for naming the email subject and application file before sending them to the designated email address.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
