STU’s Infrastructure Office has begun renovating Buildings 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 of the Type C staff housing at Longquan Garden. The project was approved by the relevant authorities and assigned to Shantou Chaoyang No. 1 Construction and Installation Corporation. Work began on October 29, 2012, and is scheduled for completion by February 25, 2013, at a winning contract price of 3.7081 million yuan.

The work may produce noise, vibration and dust. The university asks pedestrians to take care and prohibits entry to the construction site without permission from the relevant university office and contractor. People are also asked to avoid lingering near the site.
