To assist graduating undergraduates with on-site confirmation for the 2013 national graduate entrance examination, Shantou University’s Graduate School is collecting transport requests for travel to the Shantou Admissions Office registration site. The service is for STU graduating undergraduates, including recommended students, who selected registration site code 4407.

Online registration for the 2013 national graduate entrance examination runs from October 10 through 31, 2012, from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. daily. On-site confirmation at the Shantou registration site will take place from November 10 through 14. The university will arrange a vehicle on one of those days for registered students; the specific departure time, service and boarding location will be announced later.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Examination | 2013 national graduate entrance examination |
| Registration site | Shantou Admissions Office, code 4407 |
| Online registration | October 10–31, 2012, 9:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m. daily |
| On-site confirmation | November 10–14, 2012 |
| Transport registration deadline | 5:00 p.m. on November 5, 2012 |
| Registration page | http://www.gs.stu.edu.cn/dzzw/busbook.jsp?oa_id=20 |
| Public-transport alternative | Bus routes 21 or 39; the B&Q Lotus stop is near the registration site |

Candidates should also read the *2013 Shantou Graduate Registration Instructions* issued by the Shantou Admissions Office in its announcement on the China Graduate Admissions Information Network.
