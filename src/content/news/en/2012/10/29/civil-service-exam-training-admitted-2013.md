Shantou University Law School has released arrangements for its 2013 civil-service examination training. The school received 177 applications after opening registration, and the final admitted-student list is provided in the original notice attachment.

The training will take place at the Moot Court from November 2 through 7, 2012, with sessions from 9:00 a.m. to noon and 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. each day. Wang Renfa will teach the Administrative Aptitude Test sessions on November 2, 5, 6 and 7. Guo Wulin will teach the Essay Writing sessions on November 3 and 4.

| Venue | Moot Court |
| Administrative Aptitude Test | November 2, 5, 6 and 7; taught by Wang Renfa |
| Essay Writing | November 3 and 4; taught by Guo Wulin |
| Photo and lecture-pass deadline | Noon on October 31, 2012 |
| Submission point | Career Development Center, Law School, Wenbei 309 |

Admitted students must prepare a one-inch photograph with their name written on the back and submit it to their school or department training leader for lecture-pass processing. Participants must wear the pass and attend on time. The school will provide electronic lecture notes, while purchase of reference books is voluntary.

The admitted-student list, electronic lecture notes and reference-book information are included in the original attachments. Student names and other personal information are not reproduced here.
