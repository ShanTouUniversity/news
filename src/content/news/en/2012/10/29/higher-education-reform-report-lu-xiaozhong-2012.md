Professor Lu Xiaozhong of the School of Educational Sciences at South China Normal University will give an academic lecture at Shantou University on October 30, 2012. His talk, “Reflections on Higher-Education Reform and Development: Perspectives from National Policy and International Comparison,” is organized by the STU Institute of Higher Education. Faculty members and students are welcome.
