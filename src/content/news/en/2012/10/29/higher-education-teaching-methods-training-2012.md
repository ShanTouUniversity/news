Shantou University’s Continuing Education Center and Institute of Higher Education will hold an advanced workshop on university teaching methods from November 3 to 4, 2012. The workshop is open to faculty from the main campus and Medical College and will cover teaching principles and methods, course design and educational psychology, with an emphasis on practical applications in higher education.

The four sessions will feature Professor Wang Weilian on principles and methods of university teaching; Researcher Cai Yinghui on designing individual university courses; Dr. Wang Xueju and Professor Chen Xiaohong on learning motivation, teacher psychology and professional development; and Professor Bian Junhui on effectively encouraging active learning.
