Following requirements issued by the Guangdong education authorities, Shantou University has opened its 2012–2013 tuition-assistance process for veteran students. Each school and residential college is asked to publicize the policy and organize eligible students who wish to apply.

The assistance is for veterans who entered higher education after leaving service; it does not cover veterans returning to a university after an interruption of study. Applicants must have been discharged more than one year ago and currently be enrolled. Students who enlisted while already enrolled, or who entered higher education within one year of discharge, are not eligible. Applications are made by academic year, and each application covers only that year’s tuition. The amount is based on the tuition standard and capped at 6,000 yuan per student per academic year.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Assistance year | 2012–2013 academic year |
| Eligible applicants | Veterans who entered higher education, were discharged more than one year ago and are currently enrolled |
| Excluded applicants | Veterans returning after an interruption; students who enlisted while enrolled; students entering within one year of discharge |
| Application | Complete the Veteran Student Tuition Assistance Application Form |
| Amount | Based on tuition, capped at 6,000 yuan per student per academic year |
| Printed-material deadline | November 5, 2012 |
| Submission office | Education Administration Section, Administrative Center 106 |
| Electronic materials | zpchen@stu.edu.cn |

Eligible students who entered in the 2011–2012 academic year but missed the previous application may apply retroactively this year for two academic years and explain the situation in the form’s remarks section. Students who applied last year may continue applying for the current academic year. Forms must be completed accurately, including specific start and end dates for military service, and must not be altered casually.
