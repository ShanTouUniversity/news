Shantou University has issued a notice on a new administrative arrangement establishing the Student Entrepreneurship Park Management Office. The office will operate alongside the Student Entrepreneurship Center under Student Affairs. The decision was considered and approved at the 10th university leadership meeting on September 18, 2012.

The director of the Student Entrepreneurship Center will concurrently serve as director of the new office. Yu Hong, director of Student Affairs, will concurrently serve as its director.
