It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on October 23, 2012, reporting on donations for the family of a student who passed away from drowning.

Following the university’s donation appeal, faculty and students responded actively. As of the morning of October 19, 2012, a total of 47,069.1 yuan had been collected through on-campus donation points and college Youth League Committees. The funds were handed to the Student Affairs Office on October 23 for transfer to the family. Details are available in the OA attachment.
