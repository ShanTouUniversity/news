Shantou University Business School is inviting graduate teams in business administration to enter the “mai Bangkok Business Challenge @ Sasin 2013,” a global new-venture plan competition. The competition will take place in Bangkok, Thailand, from February 28 to March 2, 2013, and invites graduate student teams worldwide to submit proactive business plans focused on sustainable development.

Business School graduate and MBA students may form teams with students from other schools or departments and may invite Business School professors to serve as faculty advisers. Each master’s- or doctoral-level business-administration program may send one representative team. An institution with multiple eligible business-administration programs may send one team for each program.

| Optional activity | Complimentary tour on March 3, 2013 |

Held annually since 2002, the competition has been co-hosted since 2007 by the Market for Alternative Investment (mai), the Stock Exchange of Thailand market for medium-sized public companies, and Sasin Graduate Institute of Business Administration of Chulalongkorn University. It became global in 2008. The 2012 competition received 55 entries from 23 countries.

Full eligibility requirements, official rules, application forms and summary-plan templates are available at the competition website, <http://www.bbc.in.th/>.
