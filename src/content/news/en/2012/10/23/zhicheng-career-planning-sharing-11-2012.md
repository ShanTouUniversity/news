Shantou University Zhicheng College will host its 11th Career Planning Sharing Session on October 29, 2012. Under the theme “Success Is a Habit: A Guide to University Students’ Workplace Development,” Wu Hao, general manager of Jucheng Enterprise Management Consulting Group Shantou Branch, will discuss career planning, social and workplace skills, and lessons from his professional experience.

The session is open to all STU students and will accept 60 participants. Applications close at noon on October 27, and admission results will be emailed by the afternoon of October 27.

Wu is also an entrepreneur speaking coach, corporate talent-systems expert, senior lecturer at Jucheng Consulting Group and public-speaking intensive-training coach. He advises multiple companies and has delivered more than 500 professional training sessions totaling over 1,000 hours.
