The Shantou University Tendering and Bidding Leading Group has completed its review of bids for the outdoor cable-channel work in the renovation of 36 west-campus staff dormitories and has published the result. Shantou Longhua Construction General Company won the contract with a bid of 48,200 yuan.

| --- | --- |
| Project | Outdoor cable-channel work for the renovation of 36 west-campus staff dormitories |
| Winning contractor | Shantou Longhua Construction General Company |
| Contract amount | 48,200 yuan |
| Public-notice period | October 23–25, 2012 |
