The Alumni Forum career-planning series at Shantou University will hold a lecture titled “Choice” on October 29, 2012. Peng Shaomin, a 1995 graduate of the STU Chinese Department, will share alumni experience on career planning and life choices. The lecture is open to all STU faculty members, staff and students.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Series | STU Alumni Forum career-planning lecture series |
| Topic | Choice |
| Speaker | Peng Shaomin, 1995 graduate of the STU Chinese Department |
| Time | October 29, 2012, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Participants | All STU faculty members, staff and students |
| Organizers | STU Alumni Association Office, School of Liberal Arts, Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication, and Shantou Association of Business Executives |
