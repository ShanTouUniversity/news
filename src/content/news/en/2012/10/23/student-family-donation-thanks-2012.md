The Shantou University Student Affairs Office, university union, STU Youth League Committee and STU Young Volunteers Association have jointly published a letter of thanks and reported the latest donations for families of students who died in a drowning incident. By October 23, 2012, faculty members, staff and students had donated a total of 156,259.20 yuan: 109,190.10 yuan from employees and 47,069.10 yuan from students.

The university plans to divide the donation equally among the four bereaved families and transfer it by bank deposit to the accounts designated by the students’ parents in the near future. The notice thanks faculty members, staff and students who contributed.

Compared with the earlier donation bulletin issued on October 22, this notice reports 1,600 yuan more in faculty and staff donations. Because this notice has the later reporting date, its figures are used here as the latest totals. To protect privacy, this article does not publish the students’ names, parents’ account information or other personal information.
