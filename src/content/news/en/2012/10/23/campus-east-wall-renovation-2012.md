To improve the campus environment, Shantou University Infrastructure Office will arrange an approved renovation of the solid wall at the junction of the east perimeter road and Nongmin Road. The project will remove the wall and treat mold on its painted surface, after which School of Design students will use it for painting.

Work is scheduled to begin on October 24, 2012, and finish in early November. The project may create noise and dust. Resource Management Office asks faculty members, staff and students passing through the area to avoid the construction zone where possible.
