It is reported that a lecture titled "Understanding Society in South China (the Chaoshan Region)" will be held on October 24 (Wednesday) from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm in Conference Room 205 of the Science Reporting Hall. All faculty members and students are welcome. The event is hosted by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and organized by the university's Xingchao Society.

The keynote speaker will be Ma Muk-chi, director of the MA Program in Comparative and Public History in the Department of History at the Chinese University of Hong Kong and a PhD graduate of Sun Yat-sen University. Chen Haizhong — dean of the Chaoshan Culture Research Institute at Hanshan Normal University, also a Sun Yat-sen University PhD holder and an STU alumnus — will attend as a guest speaker.

According to the introduction, the lecture will take a public history perspective to explain the formation of local clans in the Chaoshan region and the structure of folk deity worship, illustrating the relationship between local society and the state. The speaker will also give an overview of postgraduate programs at the Chinese University of Hong Kong.
