The China Women’s Studies Association and the Women’s Studies Center at Shantou University have agreed to cooperate on the 2012 China Women’s and Gender Studies Young Scholars Forum and the 2012–14 China Young Scholars Women’s and Gender Empirical Research Projects. The initiatives aim to bring more young scholars into women’s and gender studies and disciplinary development, encourage research on China’s political, economic, cultural and social transformation, and strengthen critical thinking, practical value and social impact.

The forum will take place at Shantou University from December 15 to 16, 2012, over one and a half days. Participants will include winners of the association’s fourth Outstanding Women’s and Gender Studies Doctoral and Master’s Thesis Awards, young scholars funded under the 2012–14 empirical research projects, young field leaders who conduct women’s studies and submit conference papers, and selected STU young faculty members, graduate students and undergraduates.

Separately, with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the association and the STU Women’s Studies Center are soliciting 2012–14 empirical research projects in women’s and gender studies from young scholars in mainland China. The project supports doctoral students and qualified young scholars conducting empirical studies based on rigorous fieldwork, with a plan to develop at least 10 high-quality research proposals.
