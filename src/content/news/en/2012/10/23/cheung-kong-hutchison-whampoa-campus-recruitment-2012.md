The first 2012 Cheung Kong Group recruitment event at Shantou University will take place from October 31 to November 2, 2012, with Hutchison Whampoa Properties Group participating. The STU Student Affairs Office asks schools and residential colleges to notify graduating students and help them prepare for the event.

Graduates interested in Hutchison Whampoa Properties Group should follow the company website for information about its 2013 STU campus recruitment, download and complete the position application form by October 31, and personally deliver it to the company exhibition area between 9 a.m. and noon on November 1. Graduates attending the company presentation, exhibition and comprehensive test at the New Library should wear formal attire.
