The Shantou University Party and Government Office has announced arrangements for staff electives. The *Official Document Writing* course will begin on October 24, 2012, and registered participants should attend on time. The *Secretary Skills* course scheduled to begin on October 26 has been canceled because enrollment did not meet the minimum required to open the class.

| Course | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Official Document Writing | October 24, Wednesday, 3:30 p.m., E204 Teaching Building |
| Secretary Skills | Scheduled for October 26; canceled because of insufficient enrollment |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office)
