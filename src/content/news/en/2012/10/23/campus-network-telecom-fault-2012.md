It is reported that the Network and Information Center issued a notice on October 23, 2012, regarding a fault on the campus network’s telecom line.

At around 15:00 on October 23, the telecom line failed, preventing off-campus internet users from accessing some services hosted on campus telecom IP addresses. After inspection and testing, the center determined the issue was on the telecom carrier’s side and is coordinating with the carrier for resolution.
