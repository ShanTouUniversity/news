The Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will screen the American Western *High Noon* on October 24, 2012, as part of its movie night. Peter Herford will moderate the event. The class has 150 seats, with another 50 seats open to the public on a first-come, first-served basis. Students must present their campus cards to enter.

Directed by Fred Zinnemann and starring Gary Cooper and Grace Kelly, the 85-minute film follows a town marshal who, unable to find support, faces four revenge-seeking outlaws alone. Its story unfolds in near real time over the period from 10:40 a.m. to noon.

*High Noon* received Golden Globe recognition for drama-category acting and score, New York Film Critics Circle awards for Best Picture and Best Director, and the Academy Award for Best Film Editing. In 1989, it was selected for preservation in the U.S. National Film Registry by the Library of Congress.
