Following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Education Examination Authority, 2012-entry undergraduates at Shantou University may not take the fall 2012 College English Test Band 4. Academic Affairs has asked Finance to return the 16-yuan registration fee to the campus-card account of each affected student who registered.

Relevant schools should notify registered students to check their accounts. Questions about the refund may be directed to the Academic Affairs Student Affairs Section. The original attachment governs the detailed refund list; student names and personal information are not reproduced here.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Examination | Fall 2012 College English Test Band 4 |
| Affected group | 2012-entry undergraduates who registered |
| Change | 2012-entry undergraduates may not take this examination |
| Refund | 16 yuan per student |
| Method | Returned to each student’s campus-card account |
| Check | Students should check their campus-card accounts |
| Questions | Academic Affairs Student Affairs Section |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
