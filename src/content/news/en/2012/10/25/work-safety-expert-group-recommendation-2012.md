The Shantou University Human Resources Office has forwarded a letter from the Shantou Work Safety Commission soliciting nominees for the Shantou workplace-safety expert group. Eligible faculty members who are interested in being recommended should prepare materials according to the letter and submit them to Human Resources by November 8, 2012.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recommendation | Nominees for the Shantou workplace-safety expert group |
| Participants | Interested faculty members who meet the letter’s requirements |
| Deadline | By November 8, 2012 |
| Submission | STU Human Resources Office |
| Contact | Fan Jibin |
| Telephone | 86502348 |
| Email | jbfan@stu.edu.cn |
| Reference | Shantou Work Safety Commission Office Letter [2012] No. 50 |

The original letter and attachments contain the detailed eligibility criteria, materials and submission method.
