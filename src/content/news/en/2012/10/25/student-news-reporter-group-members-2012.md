The Shantou University News Center has announced the first roster of its student reporter group. After volunteering to apply, the selected students completed written tests, interviews and portfolio review. The group will work on campus news writing, photography and web editing.

The original archive lists 28 members in the following roles:

| Role | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Team leader | 1 |
| Deputy team leaders | 2 |
| Secretary | 1 |
| Text reporters | 15 |
| Photographers | 6 |
| Web editors | 3 |

To protect student privacy, this article does not publish members’ names, years or majors. The original STU News Center notice governs the detailed roster.
