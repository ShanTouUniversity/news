Shantou University has published its *National Scholarship Assessment Measures*. The measures were discussed and approved at the 12th university leadership meeting on October 25, 2012, and take effect upon publication. All schools and residential colleges are required to follow them.

| --- | --- |
| Document | STU National Scholarship Assessment Measures |
| Approval | 12th university leadership meeting, October 25, 2012 |
| Effective date | Upon publication |
| Implementers | All STU schools and residential colleges |
| Document number | STU [2012] No. 129 |
| Issued | October 25, 2012 |
