Because Wu Hao, general manager of Jucheng Group’s Shantou company, has had a schedule change, the 11th Shantou University Zhicheng College Career Planning Sharing Session has been postponed. The event will no longer take place at the original time of 7:30–9:30 p.m. on October 29, 2012, in Zhicheng College J-354. A new time will be announced separately.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Zhicheng College Career Planning Sharing Session 11 |
| Original time | October 29, 2012, Monday, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Original venue | Zhicheng College Lecture Room J-354 |
| Speaker | Wu Hao, general manager of Jucheng Group’s Shantou company |
| Change | Postponed because of the speaker’s schedule |
| New time | To be announced |

Students who registered for or are following the event should watch for the follow-up notice.
