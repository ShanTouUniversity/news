To select students for the first Student Community X Plan Alumni Cup badminton friendship match, strengthen physical fitness and encourage a competitive and enterprising spirit, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office, Alumni Association Office and Physical Education Department will organize an on-campus selection tournament.

The tournament will take place in the STU Gymnasium from November 17 to 21, 2012. It is open to healthy undergraduate and graduate students from each school and residential college who are fit to compete. Each unit may enter one captain, two male athletes and two female athletes. Members of the university badminton team do not count toward a unit’s quota.
