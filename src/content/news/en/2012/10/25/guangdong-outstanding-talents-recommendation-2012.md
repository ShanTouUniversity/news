Under a Shantou Municipal Party Organization Department notice on selecting the 13th group of Shantou Outstanding Talents, Shantou University has completed expert review and received approval from the eighth Standing Committee of the University Party Committee. The university plans to recommend Professors Bao Nengsheng, Hu Zhong and Zheng Huaiping as candidates, and has opened a public-notice period.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | 13th Shantou Outstanding Talents selection |
| Nominees | Professors Bao Nengsheng, Hu Zhong and Zheng Huaiping |
| Process | Expert review and approval by the eighth Standing Committee of the STU Party Committee |
| Public-notice deadline | Before noon on October 26, 2012 |
| Feedback office | STU Party Organization and United Front Department |
| Methods | Written submission, in-person report or telephone |
| Telephone | 82902345 |
| Email | o_zzb@stu.edu.cn |
| Reference | Shantou Organization Notice [2012] No. 54 |

Comments on the nominees may be submitted to the Party Organization and United Front Department before the deadline. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed with the submitter’s real name.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Department)
