Under the Guangdong Family Planning Regulations and Shantou family-planning management requirements, the Shantou University Family Planning Office will complete its annual checks and surveys in November. The work applies to eligible employees across the university, and completion is linked to the distribution of family-planning bonuses.

Checks for married female employees of childbearing age will take place from November 12 to 14 in the afternoons. The group covered is all married female employees under 50, including established employees, personnel-agency employees and auxiliary contract workers. Checks will be conducted in the B-ultrasound room on the second floor of the University Hospital. Departmental family-planning coordinators must notify relevant employees to attend at the assigned time.

Married male employees whose spouses work for another organization or outside Shantou must cooperate with a family-planning survey. Departmental coordinators should collect survey letters from the Family Planning Office at the University Hospital and return replies within one month.
