The 53rd Guangdong Academician Forum and a Shantou University Engineering Research Forum will take place on October 26, 2012. Academician Sun Yu, director of the Guangzhou Digital Home Industry Technology Research Institute at Sun Yat-sen University, will speak on “Discussion of the Overall Concept of Telecommunication Networks.”

The talk is open to faculty members and graduate students in the Computer and Electronics departments, as well as interested undergraduates.
