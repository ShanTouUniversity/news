It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on October 29, 2012, featuring Prof. Ken Whelan.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Atlantic Salmon (Salmo salar) - The Ultimate Ocean Bio-Monitor |
| Speaker | Prof. Ken Whelan, environmental consultant, Research Director of the Atlantic Salmon Trust and Adjunct Professor at University College Dublin |
| Time | 10:00–12:00, October 29, 2012 |
| Venue | Room 205, Science and Technology Center |
| Organizer | School of Science |

The talk will cover closed containment aquaculture and alternative feed sources for farmed fish, with a focus on tracking migration and distribution of Atlantic salmon in the Northeast Atlantic using novel genetic stock identification and scale reading techniques, and advances from the SALSEA-Merge programme including genetic marker development, fine-scale growth estimates and three-dimensional Regional Ocean Modelling.
