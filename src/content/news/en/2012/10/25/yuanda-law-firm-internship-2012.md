It is reported that, following interviews conducted by Shanghai Yuanda Law Firm, the Law School of Shantou University has announced the preliminary list of students selected for its 2012 autumn internship program.

## Selection Results

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Internship host | Shanghai Yuanda Law Firm |
| Program | Autumn 2012 Internship |
| Selection method | Interview by the firm |
| Number selected | 2 |
| Selected students | Wang F and Shi F, Law, Class of 2009 |

The Law School noted that this is a preliminary selection.
