It is reported that the Student Affairs Office, the Youth League Committee, the Alumni Office and the Student Union will hold the 12th edition of the My Career Dream mock interview series — Navigating the Job Sea — on October 27, 2012.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Navigating the Job Sea — Mock Interview |
| Participants | All students |
| Content | Career interviews, English interviews and civil service interviews |
| Time | 08:45–11:30 and 14:30–17:30, October 27, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Activity Hall, Shantou University |
| Organizers | Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, Alumni Office, Student Union |

Resume templates will be available on site. Enterprise profiles, positions, templates and schedules are in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ Enterprise details and templates are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office)
