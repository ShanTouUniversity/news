The fifth Psychological Development Camp at Shantou University Zhicheng College received 22 applications, and all applicants were admitted. Participants must check in at Zhicheng College J-354 at 8 a.m. on Saturday, October 27, 2012, and attend the camp.

Participants should wear sports-casual clothing during the activity and prepare in advance. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish the admitted students’ names, years or majors; the original notice governs the detailed list.
