Shantou University reported that four students went missing while visiting Nanshan Bay in Haojiang, Shantou, on October 4. Provincial and municipal authorities and relevant departments organized search and rescue efforts, while university leaders and responsible offices went to the site to assist. A police investigation later confirmed that all four students had died in accidental drownings.

The university established an emergency response group on October 5 and coordinated follow-up matters with support from relevant departments. The related procedures were completed on October 10. On the evening of October 11, the university held a memorial ceremony in front of the Great Hall to mourn the students who had died.

The university urged faculty members and students to treat the incident as a warning, strengthen safety awareness and protect their lives and property. This article does not publish the students&apos; names or other personal information.
