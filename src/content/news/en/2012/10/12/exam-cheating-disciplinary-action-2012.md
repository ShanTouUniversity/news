Shantou University Student Affairs Office announced disciplinary decisions involving four students who violated examination rules on June 18, 2012. According to the notice, a 2010 computer science and technology student from the School of Engineering, identified only by the surname Yuan, twice turned around during the ELC4 examination and placed the test paper on a classmate&apos;s desk behind him. The student initially denied the conduct, which was later confirmed through campus surveillance. A 2011 art design student from the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, identified only by the surname Li, repeatedly looked at another student&apos;s paper during the EA2 examination despite invigilator warnings.

A 2010 public administration student from the Law School, identified only by the surname Yan, and a 2010 accounting student from the Business School, identified only by the surname Tan, had agreed before the ELC4 examination to exchange answers and copied answers onto notes during the exam. Invigilators discovered the conduct. The original notice did not provide full names, and this article preserves its abbreviated identification to protect student privacy.

Under Article 16 of the Regulations on the Administration of Students in Regular Institutions of Higher Education, Article 18 of the STU Student Disciplinary Regulations (Trial) and Article 50(2) of the Interim Regulations on Credit-Based Student Status Administration for STU Undergraduates, the university issued Yuan a serious warning and Li, Yan and Tan warnings. Each student received a zero for the course in which the cheating occurred.

The university urged students to learn from the case and help maintain normal teaching and living order and strengthen academic conduct.
