Shantou University Graduate School has opened the 2012 Outstanding Graduate Student selection. Under the university&apos;s selection rules, full-time master&apos;s and doctoral students with active enrollment may participate, but a student may enter only once while pursuing each degree. Students disciplined for violating university rules during the academic year or with a failing course grade are ineligible.

Candidates are expected to observe laws and rules, respect teachers, work cooperatively and contribute to the community. Their course average should normally be at least 80, with no individual grade below 75; the threshold may be relaxed for especially strong research achievements. Candidates must also demonstrate research ability through at least one of the following: a research paper in an important professional journal, with the student as first author or second author when the supervisor is first author; an unpublished academic conclusion or experimental result that has been evaluated or adopted and offers strong academic, social or economic value; or a provincial-level or higher award. Students who have served as a student leader in a university organization or class for at least one year or term with notable performance receive priority under otherwise equal conditions. First-prize candidates must additionally meet the requirements for the 2010 Guangdong Nan Yue Outstanding Graduate Student selection.

| Deadline or period | Work |
| --- | --- |
| By October 18 | Applicants submit the review form and research-achievement evidence to their college or graduate training unit |
| By October 23 | Colleges and training units complete reviews and recommendations; quotas are about 5% of enrolled graduate students |
| By October 25 | Units submit review forms, research evidence, transcripts and summary forms to the Graduate School |
| October 25–31 | The Graduate School reviews materials and organizes expert assessment as needed |
| November 1 | Candidate list is published and placed on public notice |

The notice for the Guangdong Nan Yue Outstanding Graduate Student selection had not yet been issued at the time. First-prize arrangements therefore depended on later university information. The Graduate School attachments provide the review forms, detailed quotas and material templates.
