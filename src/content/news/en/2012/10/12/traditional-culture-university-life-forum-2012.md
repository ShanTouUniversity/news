The 13th Zhicheng Forum, hosted by Zhicheng College, will be held at Shantou University&apos;s New Library Lecture Hall on October 14, 2012, under the theme “Traditional Culture and University Life.” Liu Yuli, a professor in the Department of Philosophy at the Party School of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, will discuss Chinese traditional culture and its contemporary value and connect traditional life wisdom with university growth.

Liu is a professor at the Party School, holds a PhD in philosophy from the University of Hull, completed postdoctoral work in philosophy at the National University of Singapore and supervises doctoral students in ethics. Her research focuses on Chinese traditional culture, pre-Qin Confucian ethics and contemporary Western virtue ethics. Her representative books include *Confucian Ethics: The Unity of Rules and Virtues* and *Mindset Is Destiny: Interpreting Traditional Wisdom on Life*. She also contributed to *Traditional Chinese Virtues: Aspiration, Diligence and Self-Cultivation* and *On Moral Development*, works that received a National Outstanding Youth Reading Award and a national “Five-One Project” award. She has delivered talks or conducted academic visits at universities in China and abroad and has taught students and professional groups for many years.
