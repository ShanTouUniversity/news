Shantou University&apos;s Student Growth Navigation Society will hold its first general meeting on October 13, 2012, and formally launch its first skills-development training activity. The general meeting will take place in Meeting Room 205 of the Science and Technology Report Hall, and all society members are expected to attend on time.

The first training session, “Integrative Thinking,” is scheduled for the afternoon of October 20 at the Integrative Thinking Office. The society asked all members to follow subsequent notices for the detailed arrangements.

| Activity | Details |
| --- | --- |
| General meeting | October 13, Saturday, 2–3 p.m., Science and Technology Report Hall Meeting Room 205 |
| First training session | Afternoon of October 20, Integrative Thinking Office |
| Training topic | Integrative Thinking |
| Audience | All Student Growth Navigation Society members |
