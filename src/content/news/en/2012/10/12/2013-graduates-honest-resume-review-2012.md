Under arrangements for the Guangdong employment and recruitment fair for the 2013 graduating class, the Guangdong online employment platform is expanding online recruitment and verified-resume services. Shantou University Student Affairs Office has asked each college and residential college to organize its 2013 graduates to complete personal resumes on the platform, while employment counselors will review the submissions.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | 2013 graduating class |
| Platform | Guangdong online employment platform (gradjob.com.cn/cms/index.html) |
| Deadline | By October 18, 2012 |
| Review standard | Resume information must be authentic and accurate |

Students should complete their resumes according to arrangements from their college or residential college so they can take part in subsequent online recruitment and employment activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
