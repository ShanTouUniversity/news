Under arrangements by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the 24th and 25th Guangdong Higher-Education Pearl River Scholar Forums will be held on October 16 and October 18, 2012, respectively. Shantou University is collecting names of faculty members who wish to attend and will submit a consolidated list to the provincial education authority's faculty management office and the personnel office of Guangdong University of Foreign Studies.

| Forum | Date |
| --- | --- |
| 24th | October 16, 2012 |
| 25th | October 18, 2012 |

Interested faculty members should register through their units. Each unit must email its list to jbfan@stu.edu.cn by 11:30 a.m. on October 15. The Guangdong education authority's notice is available on its website.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
