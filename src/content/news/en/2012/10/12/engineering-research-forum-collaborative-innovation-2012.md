The 131st Shantou University Engineering Research Forum will be held on October 12, 2012, under the theme “Reflections on Developing the 2011 Collaborative Innovation Center at the Dongguan HUST Institute.” Zhang Guojun, a professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, will lead the academic session, which is open to interested faculty members and students across the university.

Zhang holds a doctorate and is a professor and doctoral supervisor. He serves as deputy director of the National Engineering Research Center for Digitization of Manufacturing Equipment, vice dean of HUST&apos;s School of Mechanical Science and Engineering and executive vice dean of the Dongguan HUST Institute of Manufacturing Engineering.
