The 28th Career Launch Forum will be held at Shantou University&apos;s New Library Lecture Hall on the evening of October 15, 2012. Under the theme “University Planning and Career Choices,” Hou Jiechao and Li Site will share reflections drawn from their education, consulting, public-service and professional experiences. Faculty members and students across the university are welcome.

Hou Jiechao graduated from Peking University&apos;s Guanghua School of Management in 2010 with a degree in finance. He worked as a business analyst at McKinsey&apos;s Beijing office, contributing to consulting projects in banking, insurance, asset management, government, pharmaceuticals, retail and chemicals across functions including strategy, sales and marketing, innovation and corporate finance. He is now an Alumni Impact manager at Teach For China. Li Site graduated from Peking University&apos;s School of Chemistry in 2010 and served as a Teach For China program teacher, teaching ninth-grade chemistry at Qingshu Middle School in Fengqing County, Lincang, Yunnan. Li is now an Alumni Impact assistant at the organization.
