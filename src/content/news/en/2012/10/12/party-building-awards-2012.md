It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department issued a notice on October 12, 2012, announcing honors received in the campaign to create advanced grassroots Party organizations and strive to be outstanding Party members.

Since May 2010, the university has carried out the campaign, achieving notable results in Party building that have been recognized at the provincial and municipal levels.

## Honorees

| Award | Recipients |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Communist Party Members in the Provincial Education System (Provincial Education Working Committee) | Zhao Xiaohua (Professor, Department of Civil Engineering, College of Engineering), Wu Ping (Professor, Department of Physics, School of Science; Vice Director, Personnel Office) |
| Outstanding Communist Party Members (Municipal Party Committee) | Yang Qingjie (Vice Dean, College of Liberal Arts; Head, Department of Chinese; Secretary, Party Branch of Chinese) |
| Advanced Grassroots Party Organizations (Municipal Directly-Affiliated Working Committee) | General Party Branch of the Law School, Faculty Party Branch of the Business School |
| Outstanding Communist Party Members (Municipal Directly-Affiliated Working Committee) | Lin Jinxiang (Deputy Director, Office of Cheung Kong School of Art and Design) |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
