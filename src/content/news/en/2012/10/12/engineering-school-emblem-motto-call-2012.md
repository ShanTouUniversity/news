To present the Engineering School&apos;s identity and strengthen its sense of community, Shantou University Engineering School is inviting the university community to submit emblem and motto designs for prizes. The submission deadline is November 18, 2012.

Emblem designs should reflect the school&apos;s characteristics and an uplifting spirit, include the Chinese and English names of the STU Engineering School, and be clear, concise and visually engaging. Designs may use JPG, BMP, PNG or GIF files and must include an approximately 300-character design statement; hand-drawn work must be photographed or scanned into an image. Motto designs should express the school&apos;s educational philosophy and disciplinary identity, use no more than 16 Chinese characters, and be submitted as a Word document with a statement of no more than 300 characters. Individuals or teams of up to five may submit either type of work, with no limit on the number of entries. All work must be original and carry independent, lawful intellectual-property rights.

| Category | First prize | Second prize | Third prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Emblem | 1 winner, 1,000 yuan | 2 winners, 500 yuan each | 3 winners, 300 yuan each |
| Motto | 1 winner, 800 yuan | 2 winners, 400 yuan each | 3 winners, 200 yuan each |

Participants must complete the design-call registration form and submit it with their work through the campus email system according to the Engineering School&apos;s requirements. The subject line, submission address and personal contact fields are part of the registration process, so this article does not publish individual contact details. Submissions will not be returned; contributors should retain backups.

The Engineering School will appoint a review panel and publish the results on the campus office information network. Winners will receive certificates. Submission grants the organizer rights to publicize, exhibit and recommend the design for implementation. Intellectual-property rights for an adopted design will belong to STU Engineering School, and the final emblem or motto may be revised from the submitted work as needed.
