Shantou University&apos;s Engineering School has announced the midterm review results for its 2012 student extracurricular science and technology innovation projects. Forty-four projects underwent review by an expert panel organized by the school. Thirty-four passed the midterm review, while the remaining projects did not; the attachment lists the specific projects and results.

Leaders of projects that passed must prepare invoices for their initial start-up funding and complete reimbursement by December 20, 2012.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Projects reviewed | 44 |
| Projects passed | 34 |
| Reimbursement deadline | December 20, 2012 |
| Reimbursement scope | Initial start-up funding for projects that passed the midterm review |
