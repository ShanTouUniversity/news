Zhicheng College will host its 10th career-planning sharing session on October 19, 2012. Wang Jingjian and Chen Yanmei, project leaders with Meilizhongguo (Teach For China), will discuss professional development and public service under the theme “Career Development Planning and the Public-Service Journey.”

Founded in 2008, Teach For China brings young people from China and the United States to underserved areas in China for frontline teaching and works to improve local educational conditions and quality. Wang Jingjian is executive director of the organization's Shantou office, and Chen Yanmei is project manager for its Shantou area.
