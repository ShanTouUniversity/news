To help the 2012 freshman cohort learn about the library's resources and services, Shantou University Library will offer two special sessions on October 14, 2012. Combining demonstrations with hands-on online use, the sessions will introduce literature searches and reading and study methods for navigating digital and print resources.

| Session | Time | Speaker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital Reading and Digital Audio-Visual Resources | October 14, Sunday, 9:30–10:30 a.m. | Xie Luqing |
| Searching Print Books and Periodicals and Library Service Guide | October 14, Sunday, 3–4 p.m. | Xu Yong |

Both sessions will take place in the Library Subject Services Department, inside the second-floor reference desk area. A prize quiz will be included. Classes or small groups that miss the sessions may contact the library to arrange an additional session on these topics or other library-use topics. Arrangements can be made at lqxie@stu.edu.cn or extension 3247.
