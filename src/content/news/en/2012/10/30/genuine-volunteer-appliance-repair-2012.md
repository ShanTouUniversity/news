Shantou University’s Association for Science and Technology and Association for Science and Technology Creation will hold a large volunteer repair event on November 3, 2012. The “Genuine Volunteer Repair” event will provide services for faculty members and students who have faulty household appliances but lack repair skills or tools.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Genuine Volunteer Repair |
| Audience | STU faculty members and students |
| Time | 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., Saturday, November 3, 2012 |
| Locations | Golden Triangle and the entrance to Dining Hall 4 |
| Organizers | STU Association for Science and Technology and Association for Science and Technology Creation |
| Capacity | 150 appliances, accepted in arrival order until the limit is reached |

The service covers electric fans, power strips, rice cookers, electric kettles, desk lamps, telephones, emergency lights and speakers, as well as computer system reinstallation and consultation. Appliances will be accepted beginning at 9:00 a.m.

In the event of severe weather, the event will move to the area below Dining Hall 2 while keeping the same hours. The organizers ask faculty members and students to plan accordingly and follow on-site arrangements.
