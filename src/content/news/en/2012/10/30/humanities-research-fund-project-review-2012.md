Under the *STU Humanities Research Fund Management Measures*, Shantou University’s Research Office is conducting completion evaluations for its 2010 humanities research-fund projects and annual reviews of its 2011 projects. The relevant project leaders must submit materials by November 21, 2012.

The 2010 completion-evaluation leaders are Yang Mu, Zhu Wang, Su Li, Yang Weigang, Wen Bing, Li Haoyu, Zhang Ling, Lin Kailong, Huang Xuelan and Deng Xiaoqin. The 2011 annual-review leaders are Bai Youjin, Tan Kanglin, Zheng Muqiang, Liang Qiang, Tian Guang, Lin Xiaona, Zhao Jinglian, Cai Qiuyan and Wu Zhengmao.

| Year and action | Project leaders | Main materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2010 completion evaluation | Yang Mu, Zhu Wang, Su Li, Yang Weigang, Wen Bing, Li Haoyu, Zhang Ling, Lin Kailong, Huang Xuelan and Deng Xiaoqin | STU Humanities Research Fund Completion Approval Form and one copy of research outputs; one printed copy of any unpublished manuscript |
| 2011 annual review | Bai Youjin, Tan Kanglin, Zheng Muqiang, Liang Qiang, Tian Guang, Lin Xiaona, Zhao Jinglian, Cai Qiuyan and Wu Zhengmao | STU Humanities Research Fund Project Annual Review Form and one copy of supporting materials |

Materials must be reviewed and stamped by the relevant unit before submission to the Research Office. Electronic versions should be sent to ymhuang@stu.edu.cn. A project that fails to submit on time or does not pass the annual review will have its research funding delayed or suspended for the following year.
