Following requirements from the Shantou social-security authorities, Shantou University has completed enrollment procedures for the 2012 cohort in Shantou’s resident basic medical insurance. Students approved for enrollment from October 2012 are covered under Tier 1 at 30 yuan per student per year. The current deduction is 7.50 yuan for the remaining three months of the year, with coverage from October 1 through December 31, 2012. The detailed enrollment list is in the original attachment and is not reproduced here.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Enrollees | 2012 cohort |
| Insurance | Tier 1 Shantou resident basic medical insurance |
| Annual contribution | 30 yuan per student |
| Current deduction | 7.50 yuan |
| Current coverage | October 1–December 31, 2012 |
| Payment | The university finance office will authorize a bank deduction from each student’s personal account |

Before social-security cards are fully issued and in use in Shantou, enrolled students hospitalized at designated Shantou social-security hospitals may use their identity cards to verify enrollment and have expenses recorded and settled under the applicable rules. Students hospitalized outside Shantou for an acute illness during vacation, internship, teaching placement or academic leave may apply for reimbursement at the Shantou Social Security Bureau’s Jinping Branch within six months after discharge. Required materials include the original hospital receipt, itemized medical bill, disease diagnosis certificate, discharge record, identity-card copy and bank-account information; medical documents must bear the hospital seal as required.

### 2013 renewal and exemptions

Students seeking enrollment through the university from January 1, 2013, should complete an application form at the Student Medical Insurance Office on the third floor of the campus hospital between November 15 and 30, 2012. Late applications will not be processed. Students who continue to graduate study at STU and are already insured through the university may apply to renew their coverage. Shantou household-registered students already insured through their families may not enroll through the university without first suspending the family coverage.

Students eligible for an exemption from the individual contribution must also submit a copy of their minimum-living-guarantee certificate between November 15 and 30. The certificate must show a review record valid beyond December 2012. Late exemption applications will not be processed, and those who do not apply in time must pay the standard 30 yuan annual contribution.
