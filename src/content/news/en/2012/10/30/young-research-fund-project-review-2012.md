Under the *STU Youth Research Fund Management Measures*, Shantou University’s Research Office is completing the close-out review of its 2010 Youth Research Fund projects and conducting an annual review of its 2011 projects. The relevant project leaders must submit materials by November 20, 2012.

The 2010 close-out project leaders are Liu Zhu, Lu Fushen, Cai Zemin, Zhou Xiaoping, Zhang Qiong, Jiang Dazhi, Zhong Mingqi and Wu Fupei. The 2011 annual-review project leaders are Wei Chuliang, Zhou Wen, Zhang Xiaoni and Huang Bingxin.

| Year and action | Project leaders | Required materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2010 close-out | Liu Zhu, Lu Fushen, Cai Zemin, Zhou Xiaoping, Zhang Qiong, Jiang Dazhi, Zhong Mingqi and Wu Fupei | STU Youth Research Fund Project Completion Report, research outputs and completion evidence |
| 2011 annual review | Wei Chuliang, Zhou Wen, Zhang Xiaoni and Huang Bingxin | STU Youth Research Fund Project Annual Implementation Report and supporting materials |

The 2010 close-out submissions should include published papers, monographs, patent certificates and other evidence of completion. One copy of the relevant materials should be submitted to the leader’s school for consolidated delivery to the Research Office, together with an electronic version. The 2011 review materials follow the same process. A project that fails to submit on time or does not pass the annual review will have its research funding suspended for the following year.
