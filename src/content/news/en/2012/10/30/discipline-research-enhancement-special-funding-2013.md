Shantou University’s Research Office and 211 Project Office have opened applications for the 2013 Discipline Construction and Research Enhancement special fund. The program prioritizes applications and development for key disciplines, doctoral degree-authorizing programs and research platforms at or above the provincial level, supporting broader academic and research development.

Support is capped at 300,000 yuan per project, with the final amount determined by the application review and the project’s completion of the 2012 special-fund work. Eligible uses include academic exchange, applications for key disciplines, research platforms and degree-authorizing programs, operation of existing research platforms and preliminary costs for recruiting academic talent. Publication of monographs, organization of academic conferences or forums and participation in conferences may be listed as “other related expenses” for budget reference but do not count toward the special-fund application total.

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Relevant main-campus units meeting the basic conditions |
| Application structure | Apply by first-level discipline, using names from the 2011 revised Catalogue of Academic Degrees and Graduate Talent-Training Disciplines |
| Research directions | Three to five directions per project |
| Core members | One to three core members per direction, generally early- or mid-career scholars with doctoral degrees or associate-professor-level or higher titles |
| Doctoral proportion | In principle, more than 60% of core members should hold doctoral degrees |
| Participation limit | Each core member may participate in only one application |
| Development plan | Three-year goals, working concept and discipline-development plan, plus 2013 objectives, implementation plan and assessment indicators |

Disciplines that received 2012 special-fund support must also complete the 2012 performance form; new applicants do not need that form. Disciplines that established three-year goals in their 2012 application should continue using those goals, while new applicants must develop a new three-year plan.

Applicants must complete the *STU Discipline Construction and Research Enhancement Special-Fund Application Form*, print one A4 copy, obtain the school’s official stamp and submit it through the school to the Research Office. An electronic copy should be sent to o_kyc@stu.edu.cn. The deadline is November 18, 2012; late submissions will not be accepted.
