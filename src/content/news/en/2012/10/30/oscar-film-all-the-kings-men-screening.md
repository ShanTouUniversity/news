It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host a movie night on Wednesday, October 31, from 19:00 to 22:00 at the Library Theater, screening the Academy Award-winning drama *All the King's Men*, moderated by Peter Herford.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Film | *All the King's Men* (1949) |
| Time | October 31 (Wednesday), 19:00–22:00 |
| Venue | Theater in the Library |
| Moderator | Peter Herford |
| Seating | 150 seats for enrolled students; 50 seats open to the public on a first-come, first-served basis; students admitted with campus card |

## About the Film

*All the King's Men* is a 1949 drama based on the novel of the same name by Robert Penn Warren. Directed by Robert Rossen and starring Broderick Crawford as Willie Stark, the film was the 36th to receive more than six Academy Award nominations and went on to win three Oscars. In 2001, the United States Library of Congress deemed the film "culturally significant" and selected it for preservation in the National Film Registry. To date, it remains the last Best Picture winner based on a Pulitzer Prize-winning novel.

All are welcome to attend. Early arrival is recommended as seating is limited.
