To deepen teaching reform in *Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law*, Shantou University’s Social Sciences Department is organizing a Moral Civilization Practice Week for all undergraduates taking the course this semester. The activity will run during Weeks 9 to 11 on campus and is designed to connect classroom theory with practical action.

The program centers on five activities: doing one good deed, correcting one rule violation, copying one maxim, writing one reflection and taking part in one discussion.

| Week | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Week 8 | Instructors explain the practice week’s arrangements and requirements |
| Week 9 | Students conduct campus practice, complete the practical elements of the five activities and write a reflection of at least 800 Chinese characters |
| Week 10 | Students submit reflections to instructors; each class holds a discussion on moral civilization |
| Week 11 | The Social Sciences Department organizes an award-based essay competition and recognizes outstanding participants |

The essay competition will select entries from the reflections submitted by students. Each class will recommend several entries, and instructors will determine the awards. Instructors will provide detailed requirements during Week 8.
