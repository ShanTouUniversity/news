Under the *STU Measures for Academic Innovation-Team Development and National-Fund Cultivation Projects*, Shantou University’s Research Office is completing the close-out review of its 2010 National Fund cultivation projects and conducting an annual review of its 2011 projects. The relevant project leaders must submit materials by November 20, 2012.

The 2010 close-out leaders are Sun Haojun and Bao Nengsheng in the natural sciences, and Deng Jianguang, Du Mian, Shen Haijun and Song Xiaobao in the humanities and social sciences. The 2011 annual-review leaders are Jiang Yongquan, Sun Guoyong and Wang Jun in the natural sciences, and Xiong Jincai, Ji Yun and Kang Quanli in the humanities and social sciences.

| Year and action | Project leaders | Required materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2010 close-out, natural sciences | Sun Haojun and Bao Nengsheng | National Fund Cultivation Project Completion Report, research outputs and completion evidence |
| 2010 close-out, humanities and social sciences | Deng Jianguang, Du Mian, Shen Haijun and Song Xiaobao | National Fund Cultivation Project Completion Report, research outputs and completion evidence |
| 2011 annual review, natural sciences | Jiang Yongquan, Sun Guoyong and Wang Jun | National Fund Cultivation Project Annual Progress Report and supporting materials |
| 2011 annual review, humanities and social sciences | Xiong Jincai, Ji Yun and Kang Quanli | National Fund Cultivation Project Annual Progress Report and supporting materials |

The 2010 close-out submissions should include published papers, monographs, patent certificates and other evidence of completion. One copy of the relevant materials should be submitted to the leader’s school for consolidated delivery to the Research Office, together with an electronic version. The 2011 review materials follow the same submission process. A 2011 project that fails to submit on time or does not pass the review will have its research funding suspended for the following year.
