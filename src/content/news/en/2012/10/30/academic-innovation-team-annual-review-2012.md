Under the *STU Measures for Academic Innovation-Team Development and National-Fund Cultivation Projects*, Shantou University’s Research Office has begun its 2012 annual review of academic innovation-team projects from 2010 and 2011.

The projects under review from 2010 are the natural-science projects led by Li Dan, Li Yuanyou and Li * Ping, and the humanities and social-sciences project led by Bu Xiangzhi. The 2011 projects are the natural-science projects led by Zhang Xin and Yang Shouzhi and the humanities and social-sciences project led by Deng Jianguang. The middle character in the original record for “李某平” is garbled and has not been reconstructed speculatively.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Review scope | STU academic innovation-team projects from 2010 and 2011 |
| Materials | STU Academic Innovation-Team Development Project Annual Progress Report and supporting materials |
| Printed materials | One copy submitted to the relevant school for consolidated delivery to the Research Office |
| Deadline | By November 20, 2012 |
| Natural-science contacts | Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng; 86502813, 82902813; yxhu@stu.edu.cn |
| Humanities and social-sciences contact | Huang Yumei; 86502563, 82902563; ymhuang@stu.edu.cn |

Project leaders must submit the progress report and supporting materials as required and provide an electronic version. Projects that fail to submit on time or do not pass the annual review will have their research funding suspended for the following year.
