Zhang Guowei, deputy editor-in-chief of Shanghai’s *Morning News*, will give a lecture at Shantou University on November 1, 2012. In “News in History, History in News,” Zhang will share observations and research on the relationship between journalism and history.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | News in History, History in News |
| Speaker | Zhang Guowei, deputy editor-in-chief of Shanghai’s *Morning News* |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., November 1, 2012 |
| Venue | New Library Lecture Hall |

Zhang graduated from Fudan University and taught there for many years as a professor in the Department of History before entering the media industry, where Zhang has held editorial and management positions.
