Following a requirement from the Guangdong education authorities, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has set the schedule for written materials for the 2011–2012 National Encouragement Scholarship. Each school and residential college must notify proposed recipients to complete the application form from the attachment and organize review, recommendations and consolidated submission.

| Material or task | Responsible party | Deadline and requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| National Encouragement Scholarship Application and Approval Form | Proposed recipients | Submit to the school or residential-college counselor by the end of the workday on November 1; use the template, print double-sided and sign |
| Electronic recipient lists for the National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant | Schools and residential colleges | Send to xyli@stu.edu.cn by November 2 |
| Printed application forms, preliminary recipient list, selection-results report and review-committee membership list | Schools and residential colleges | Deliver to the Student Aid Management Center, Administrative Center 106, by the end of the workday on November 6 |
| Electronic versions of the printed materials | Schools and residential colleges | Send to xyli@stu.edu.cn |

Schools and residential colleges must carefully review the forms and complete the recommendation rationale and school or department comments. Proposed recipients’ names and other student personal information are not reproduced here; the relevant lists and form templates are in the original attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
