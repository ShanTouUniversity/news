It is reported that, following preliminary screening and interviews by the selection panel of the Law School of Shantou University, the final list for the 2012 internship program at the Shenzhen office of King & Wood Mallesons has been announced, with four law students from the 2009 cohort (students who enrolled in 2009) successfully selected.

According to the arrangements, the four students will intern at the firm's Shenzhen office in two batches:

| Batch | Selected Students |
| ---- | ---- |
| First batch | Zhong C, Wang YH |
| Second batch | Zhao JX, Zhu CB |

King & Wood Mallesons is one of China's leading law firms, and its Shenzhen internship program offers law students a valuable opportunity to engage with high-level legal practice. The selection outcome also reflects the Law School's continued commitment to cultivating practice-oriented legal professionals.
