The Shantou University Student Union will organize the 2012 Inter-School Cup basketball tournament from October through November. The event is intended to enrich students’ extracurricular life, strengthen teamwork and encourage interaction among schools and departments.

The tournament will include an All-Star Game on October 27, group matches from October 28 to November 14, a final-four stage from November 17 to 21, and third-place and championship matches on November 24. Most games will be played on the basketball court; the men’s and women’s finals and the three-point contest final will be held in the gymnasium.

| Stage | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All-Star Game, including three-point and red-versus-white contests | October 27, 3:00–5:30 p.m. | Basketball court |
| Group matches | October 28–November 14, Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays, 3:00–5:30 p.m. | Basketball court |
| Final-four stage | November 17–21 | Basketball court |
| Third-place match | November 24 | Basketball court |
| Championship games, including men’s and women’s finals and three-point final | November 24 | Gymnasium |

The men’s Group A comprises the Schools of Science, Medicine, Journalism, Graduate Studies and Liberal Arts. Group B comprises the Schools of Engineering, Business, Law and Arts, together with Zhicheng College. The women’s Group A comprises Engineering, Journalism, Business and Liberal Arts. Group B comprises Medicine, Law, Zhicheng College and Science.

The detailed schedule and referee assignments are provided in the original notice attachment. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend the games.
