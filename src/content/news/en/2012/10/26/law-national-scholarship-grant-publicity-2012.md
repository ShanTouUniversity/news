Shantou University Law School has published the proposed recipients of the 2011–2012 National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant. The selection involved student applications, class reviews and review by the school’s Youth League committee. The public-notice period runs from October 26 through October 30, 2012.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Academic year | 2011–2012 |
| Programs | National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant |
| Selection process | Student applications, class review and Law School Youth League committee review |
| Public-notice period | October 26–30, 2012 |
| Feedback office | Law School Youth League committee |
| Contact | Teacher Wang |
| Telephone | 0754-82903175-814 |
| Email | wangbin@stu.edu.cn |

The proposed recipient list is available in the original notice attachment. Comments may be submitted to the Law School Youth League committee by telephone or in writing during the public-notice period.
