It is reported that the Graduate School forwarded a notice on October 26, 2012, from the China Scholarship Council that the 4th International Graduate Scholarship Fair (formerly the National Government-Sponsored Postgraduate Scholarship Fair) was held in Beijing on October 20 and Chengdu on October 24 and will be held in Guangzhou on October 27 (Guangdong University of Foreign Studies Gymnasium, University Town, 09:00–17:00).

## Fair Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Name | 4th International Graduate Scholarship Fair |
| Dates and venues | October 20 in Beijing, October 24 in Chengdu, October 27 in Guangzhou (Guangdong University of Foreign Studies Gymnasium, University Town, 09:00–17:00) |
| Co-organizers | Peking University, Sichuan University, Guangdong University of Foreign Studies and Dongfang International Center |
| Overseas participants | Nearly 70 world-renowned universities from 15 countries including the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, France, Germany, the Netherlands and New Zealand, plus embassy representatives, including Oxford, Cambridge, Georgetown, McGill and Australian National University |
| Format | Open consultations, thematic lectures by the CSC, embassies and overseas universities, with some universities offering one-on-one interviews for selected registered students |
| Registration | Online registration; students can engage directly with representatives |
| Contact | 010-66411821-692/632/634 |
| Websites | www.csc.edu.cn/edufair and www.cscdf.org/igsf |
| Organizer | Secretariat, China Scholarship Council |

Since its launch in 2009, the fair has connected more than 100 overseas universities with nearly 20,000 students. Attendance exceeded 7,000 in 2011 and is expected to surpass 10,000 this year, establishing the event as an annual platform for overseas universities to promote graduate education and recruit Chinese students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School, China Scholarship Council)
