Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has begun planning for spring 2013. Each teaching unit must prepare its course offering plan, complete required course applications and arrange textbook selection and reporting. The spring semester will comprise 16 teaching weeks, and students will generally be limited to 25 credits when selecting courses.

Course plans should support students’ completion of all required courses within four years, with particular attention to required and major elective courses still needed by the graduating class of 2013. Practicum and internship courses that require concentrated periods of time should generally not be scheduled in the semester. Units must follow the university’s undergraduate course-setting and offering rules and strictly verify instructor qualifications. Part-time or externally hired instructors may teach only after approval by the Academic Affairs Office. Courses planned for bilingual or English-medium instruction and newly introduced courses require separate applications under the relevant procedures.

| Task | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Applications or filings for general-education, new, bilingual/English-medium courses and part-time instructors | Before November 16, 2012, Friday of Week 9 |
| Enter course offering plans in the credit-based system | Before November 23, 2012, Friday of Week 10 |
| Enter textbook information and link it to courses in the credit-based system | By November 30, 2012 |
| Submit printed textbook selection and instructor textbook request forms | After textbook selection is complete |
| Send electronic materials | lxl0726@stu.edu.cn |

As a general rule, each course should have a corresponding textbook, which will be ordered centrally by the Academic Affairs Office. Units selecting foreign textbooks must observe intellectual-property requirements and may not reproduce or use unauthorized foreign-language textbooks. Instructors should identify textbooks first, obtain unit approval and have the academic administrator or teaching secretary enter the information. Units should also preselect textbooks for courses whose instructors have not yet been assigned.

Lecture notes, reproduced materials and textbooks with prices to be determined must be labeled as required and given distinct names and reference numbers. Units should use the latest forms and carefully verify the links between textbooks and courses; textbook information may not be changed once the course-selection and textbook-ordering system opens to students.

The textbook catalogue, forms, request sheets and related materials are included in the original notice attachments.
