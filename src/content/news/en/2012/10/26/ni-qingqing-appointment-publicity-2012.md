Shantou University proposes appointing Ni Qingqing as deputy dean of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication. The proposal was considered at the university’s 12th leadership meeting on October 25, 2012, and is being publicized under the *Interim Measures for the Appointment of STU Management Cadres*.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed position | Deputy dean, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |
| Proposed appointee | Ni Qingqing |
| Meeting | 12th STU university leadership meeting, October 25, 2012 |
| Public-notice deadline | November 2, 2012 |
| Feedback office | STU Human Resources Office |
| Telephone | 82903390 |
| Email | rscgs@stu.edu.cn |
| Basis | *Interim Measures for the Appointment of STU Management Cadres* (STU Party [2009] No. 47) |

Organizations and individuals with comments on the proposed appointment may submit them in writing to the Human Resources Office before the deadline. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed with the submitter’s real name.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
