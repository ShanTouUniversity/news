To promote and commemorate the 13th Chinese Journalists’ Day on November 8, the Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication has previewed a lecture series titled “Conversations with Journalists We Admire.” The school will invite prominent media professionals to campus to share their experience and insights on news reporting and writing.

The series was previously held in 2010 and 2011. The school said detailed arrangements would be released through the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication website and official social-media account. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
