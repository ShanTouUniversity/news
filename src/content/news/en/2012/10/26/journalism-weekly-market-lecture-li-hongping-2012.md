The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University will hold a Journalism Day lecture on October 29, 2012. Li Hongping, executive distributor of *Vista Magazine*, will speak on “Challenges and Prospects for the Newsweekly Market.”

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Challenges and Prospects for the Newsweekly Market |
| Time | October 29, 2012, 10:00 a.m.–noon |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Speaker | Li Hongping, executive distributor of *Vista Magazine* |

Li graduated from Peking University’s Chinese Language and Literature Department in 1997. Li previously worked as an editor and reporter at *Changjiang Daily* and *Wuhan Morning Post*, and later held positions including reporter, editor, deputy news director, news director, editorial board member and Beijing bureau chief at *Southern Weekly*. Li has served as executive distributor of *Vista Magazine* since 2010.
