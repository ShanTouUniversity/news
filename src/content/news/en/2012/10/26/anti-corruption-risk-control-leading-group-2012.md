Shantou University has established a leading group for integrity-risk prevention and control, together with an office under the group. The decision follows requirements issued for Guangdong’s education system and was considered at the university’s 12th leadership meeting on October 25, 2012.

| Body or role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Leading-group chairs | Song * Zhen (the middle character is garbled in the original notice) and Xu Xiaohu |
| Deputy chairs | Luo Nianchao, Wen Xiaobo, Li Dan, Lin Danming and Wulanhasi |
| Members | Wang Kanghua, Sun Fenqiang, Liu Mingqiang, Chen Shaogang, He Wenbiao, Zou Zhibo, Hu Zhong, Bai Youjin, Yao Ming’an, Yu Hong and Zeng Dabin |
| Office director | Luo Nianchao, concurrently |
| Office deputy directors | He Wenbiao and Sun Fenqiang |

The document, STU [2012] No. 130, was issued on October 26, 2012. The group will carry out related work under the *Guangdong Education System Integrity-Risk Prevention and Control Work Plan*.
