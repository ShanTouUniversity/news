Following recent personnel changes, the Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has adjusted its Academic Committee and Degree Evaluation Subcommittee after internal deliberation. Wang Shouzhi will serve as chair and Li Lanqing as secretary. Han Ran, Chen Zhimin, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Wu Xiangyong, Li Haoyu, Yang Hua and Kuang Zhiwen will serve as committee members.

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Wang Shouzhi |
| Committee members | Han Ran, Chen Zhimin, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Wu Xiangyong, Li Haoyu, Yang Hua and Kuang Zhiwen |
| Secretary | Li Lanqing |

The committee adjustment was approved through deliberation by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design.
