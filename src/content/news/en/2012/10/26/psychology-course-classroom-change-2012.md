The Shantou University Institute of Higher Education has changed the classroom for its Psychology course. Because the original classroom was larger than needed, the course will move from Lecture Classroom 101 in Building E to Lecture Classroom 202 beginning in Week 7.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Psychology |
| Class number | 55707 |
| Instructor | Chen Xiaohong |
| Original venue | Lecture Classroom 101, Building E |
| New venue | Lecture Classroom 202, Building E |
| Effective | From Week 7 |
| Original schedule | Periods 6 and 7 on Mondays, Weeks 1–16 |

The Academic Affairs Office asked students enrolled in the course to share the classroom change with one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
