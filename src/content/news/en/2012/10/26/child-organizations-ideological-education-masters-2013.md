Shantou University has forwarded a provincial Youth League notice encouraging applications to the academic master’s program in Children’s Organizations and Ideological Education. The program is a secondary discipline under the first-level discipline of education and has been included in the 2013 full-time master’s admissions plan, supporting the professional development of work with the Young Pioneers.

Interested applicants must complete online registration by October 31, 2012, and send the application information form to the university Youth League Committee.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | Children’s Organizations and Ideological Education |
| Degree | Academic master’s degree |
| Admissions year | 2013 full-time master’s admissions plan |
| Online registration deadline | October 31, 2012 |
| Submission | Send the application information form to the University Youth League Committee |
| Email | o_tw@stu.edu.cn |

The detailed admissions requirements, application information form and original provincial notice are provided in the OA attachments.
