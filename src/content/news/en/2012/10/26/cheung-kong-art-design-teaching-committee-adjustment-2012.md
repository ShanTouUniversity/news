Following recent personnel changes, the Shantou University Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has adjusted the membership of its teaching committee after internal deliberation. Wang Shouzhi will serve as chair and Fang Ruohong as secretary. Han Ran, Chen Zhimin, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Wu Xiangyong, Li Haoyu, Yang Hua, Kuang Zhiwen, Lin Wen and Liu Guanghui will serve as committee members.

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chair | Wang Shouzhi |
| Committee members | Han Ran, Chen Zhimin, Wu Yong, Zhang Yu, Wu Xiangyong, Li Haoyu, Yang Hua, Kuang Zhiwen, Lin Wen and Liu Guanghui |
| Secretary | Fang Ruohong |

The adjustment was approved through deliberation by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design.
