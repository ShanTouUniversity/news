During Discipline Education Month, the Shantou University Commission for Discipline Inspection Office is collecting activity briefs from university units, Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches. The office plans to compile a Shantou University Discipline Education Month Bulletin based on activities carried out by the units and use it to share campus discipline-education work.

Relevant units must send their activity briefs to `o_jj@stu.edu.cn` by noon on Friday, October 12, 2012. The email subject should be “Discipline Education Month Bulletin.”

> Source: STU OA Notice (Commission for Discipline Inspection Office)
