It is reported that the Research Office issued a notice on October 10, 2012, to organize applications for the 2013 National Sport Administration Philosophy and Social Science Research Projects (Bureau-level sport social science projects).

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Categories | Key projects, general projects, youth projects |
| Eligibility | Key and general project leaders must hold associate senior title or division-level administrative post; youth project leaders must hold a master’s degree or above, have no associate senior title and be under 39 years old (as of the deadline) |
| Restrictions | One application per leader, members may join up to two projects; leaders of ongoing Bureau sport social science or National Social Science Fund projects may not apply; university quota is three projects |
| Duration | Key projects 1–2 years; general and youth projects typically 1 year |
| Submission | Send electronic *Application Form* and *Abstract* to ljwang@stu.edu.cn by October 24 for format review; submit printed *Application* (2 copies) and *Abstract* (6 copies) to the Research Office by October 31 with the final electronic version |
| Contacts | Huang Yumei, Huang Nan |
| Phones | 86502563, 82902563 |
| Issued by | Research Office |

Guidelines and forms are available in the OA attachments.
