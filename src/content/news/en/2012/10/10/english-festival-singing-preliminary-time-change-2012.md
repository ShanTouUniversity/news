Because of a venue-related change, the Shantou University English Language Center has revised the schedule for the English Festival intercollegiate English singing preliminary. The event was originally scheduled for 7:30 p.m. on October 14, 2012. It will now take place from 4 to 6 p.m. on the same day at the Student English Extracurricular Activities Center (EAC), second floor of Building 9.

The English Language Center welcomes interested students to attend or watch the event and asks participants to share the schedule change.
