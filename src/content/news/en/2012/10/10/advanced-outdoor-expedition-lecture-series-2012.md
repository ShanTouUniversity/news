Shantou University's advanced outdoor program will host three talks featuring professionals in nature conservation, outdoor medicine and environmental protection. The speakers will discuss ecosystem protection in Hoh Xil, outdoor safety and environmental issues at the source of the Yangtze.

| Talk | Speakers | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Tibetan Antelope Story | Xiao Penghu, deputy director of the Hoh Xil National Nature Reserve Administration; Lhaso Danzeng Zhaxi and Longzhou Caijia of the Wudaoliang Protection Station | October 11, 3–4 p.m. | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Mountain Traveler: Outdoor First Aid Workshop | Bi Wenzhong, standing team physician of the Chinese national mountaineering team | October 11, 4–5 p.m. | ACC Meeting Room 3 |
| Green River: From Hoh Xil to the Source of the Yangtze | Yang Xin, president of the Green River Environmental Protection Promotion Association | October 13, 7:30–9:30 p.m. | Library Lecture Hall |

The talks are open to the university community. The organizers hope the series will expand outdoor knowledge, promote environmental protection and strengthen awareness of outdoor safety.
