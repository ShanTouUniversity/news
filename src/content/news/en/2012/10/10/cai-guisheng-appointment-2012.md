Following review by the seventh 2012 meeting of the Standing Committee of the Communist Party of China Shantou University Committee, Cai Guisheng was appointed a member and secretary of the Party Committee of the STU School of Medicine and a member and secretary of the Party Committee of the STU Affiliated Cancer Hospital. The appointment carries a six-month probationary period.

The decision was issued as STU Party Document [2012] No. 26, dated October 10, 2012.
