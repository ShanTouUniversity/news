It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on October 10, 2012, regarding September utility fees for staff.

The current meter reading was taken on October 1–2, with the previous reading on September 1. Staff may log in to the STU Utility Management System (http://power.stu.edu.cn) with their campus network account to check the fees.

Those with questions or objections should visit the Hydropower Center for verification from October 11 to 15, phone 2489. If no objection is raised by the deadline, the fees will be deducted from the October 2012 salary account by the Finance Office; those without a salary account should pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
