The Shantou University Business School has opened applications for its 2013 AIA winter academic and cultural exchange, inviting students from all Business School years to apply. The program includes two courses and visits to financial and accounting institutions, universities and local attractions in Hong Kong. Detailed activities are provided in the program materials.

The program offers two sessions. Session A will take place in Guangzhou and Hong Kong from January 24 to February 1, 2013. Session B will take place in Hong Kong from January 26 to February 1.

| Item | Session A | Session B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dates | January 24–February 1, 2013 | January 26–February 1, 2013 |
| Locations | Guangzhou and Hong Kong | Hong Kong |
| Fee | HKD 9,300 per person | HKD 7,800 per person |
| Included | Accommodation, meals, classes, visits, transfers and other listed services | Accommodation, meals, classes, visits, transfers and other listed services |

Participants must pay the fee and arrange a valid permit or travel document for Hong Kong, travel insurance, round-trip transportation and free-activity expenses themselves. Students who confirm participation but are absent at the last minute on January 24 for Session A or January 26 for Session B must pay a CNY 1,000 accommodation charge.

Applications close at 5 p.m. on October 26, 2012. Interested students should send the required personal information to the Business School Office of International Affairs Programs at `10zzhou@stu.edu.cn`. Questions may be directed to Zhou Zhou at `82903382`.
