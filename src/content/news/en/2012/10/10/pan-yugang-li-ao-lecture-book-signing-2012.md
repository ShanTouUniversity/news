Renowned quantum chemist and Shantou University Honorary Professor Pan Yugang will visit STU on the evening of October 11, 2012, to give “The Best of Li Ao” and sign copies of his new book, *I Also Write About Li Ao*. Professor Pan has decided to donate all proceeds from book sales to the Pan Yugang Scholarship.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The Best of Li Ao |
| Speaker | Professor Pan Yugang |
| Time | October 11, 2012, Thursday, 7:30–9:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Science Report Hall |
| Book | *I Also Write About Li Ao* |
| Public-service use | All book-sale proceeds will support the Pan Yugang Scholarship |

Professor Pan is a tenured professor at Boston College, served as the fourth president of the Association of Chinese Americans and received an Overseas Outstanding Chinese Award. The School of Science welcomes faculty members and students to attend the lecture and signing.
