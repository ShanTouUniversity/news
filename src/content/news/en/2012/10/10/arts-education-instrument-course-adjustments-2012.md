Because the instructors are traveling, the Shantou University Art Education Center has revised the schedules for three instrumental courses: Euphonium Performance, Trumpet Performance and Horn Performance. The Academic Affairs Office asks enrolled students to note the cancelled and make-up classes.

| Class | Course | Cancelled class | Make-up class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 54697 | Euphonium Performance | Week 5 Sunday, October 14, periods A–B, STU213 | Weeks 6 and 7 Sunday, October 21 and 28, period C, STU213 |
| 57294 | Trumpet Performance | Week 4 Friday, October 12, periods 3–4, STU201 | Time and venue pending |
| 57295 | Horn Performance | Week 4 Friday, October 12, periods 6–7, STU204 | Time and venue pending |

The university will issue a further notice when the make-up arrangements for the latter two courses are confirmed. Students concerned should share the update.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
