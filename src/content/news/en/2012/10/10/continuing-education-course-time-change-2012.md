The Shantou University Engineering School has postponed two continuing-education courses for scheduling reasons. Faculty members enrolled in the courses should attend according to the revised schedule.

| Course | Instructor | Revised schedule | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Office Software Applications | Li Zi | October 20 and 27 and November 3, 9 a.m.–noon | Computer Room 102, Building E |
| Real Estate Investment Analysis | Zeng Lijuan | October 21 and 28 and November 4, 8 a.m.–noon | E208 |

Office Software Applications was originally scheduled to begin on October 13, while Real Estate Investment Analysis was scheduled to begin on October 14. Both courses have been postponed by one week. The Engineering School asks enrolled faculty members to attend on time under the revised schedule.
