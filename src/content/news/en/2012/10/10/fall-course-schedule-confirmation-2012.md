The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has announced that the fall 2012 course plan has been formally issued and will not be adjusted further. Instructors must log in to the academic system to check and print the student roster for each class they teach.

The Academic Affairs Office asks each college to notify its instructors. Faculty members should verify their teaching arrangements against the final course plan and prepare for instruction.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
