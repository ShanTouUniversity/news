With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Shantou University has organized advanced outdoor programs such as orienteering, the Nan'ao Outdoor Survival Training Camp, the Shangri-La Advanced Training Camp and “Chizha Fengyun” since 2009. In August 2012, the final teams “Walking in Hoh Xil” and “One Piece in Yarlung” traveled to Hoh Xil in Qinghai and the Yarlung Tsangpo Grand Canyon in Tibet, respectively, for three-week scientific expeditions.

To review the experience and share lessons from the expeditions, the program will hold an outdoor-skills demonstration and experience activity and the “The Heart Is High, the Road Is Underfoot” 2012 Advanced Outdoor Program Sharing Session.

| Activity | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outdoor-skills demonstration and experience | October 11 and 12, 2012, 11 a.m.–1 p.m. | Golden Triangle |
| “The Heart Is High, the Road Is Underfoot” sharing session | October 12, 2012, 7:27–9 p.m. | Science Report Hall |

The activities are open to the university community and will offer an opportunity to learn about outdoor skills, expedition experiences and practice-based learning.
