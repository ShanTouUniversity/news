It is reported that, to help the Class of 2012 freshmen better integrate into university life and strengthen their sense of belonging, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Youth association are launching the "Hello, Shantou" city exploration journey as part of the 2012 freshman League Day activities. Registration is open to all current students, including undergraduates and postgraduates.

The event adopts a team format: participating teams may freely choose topics of interest in downtown Shantou — such as architecture, customs, or local cuisine — and explore the city in a relaxed atmosphere, discovering its joys while adapting more quickly to university life and building their abilities.

**Registration requirements**: Teams must consist of 4–6 members, with no more than 2 non-2012 students per team. In addition to personal details, applicants must submit a brief plan describing the team's theme and exploration content — that is, what Shantou looks like through their eyes and how the theme will be expressed.

**Registration channels** (deadline: 12:00 noon, October 21):
- Download the registration form from the "STU Youth Online" website (shanqing.stu.edu.cn) and submit it;
- Register on-site at the promotional stands (downstairs of Canteen 2 and at Canteen 4) at noon and in the afternoon on October 18 and 19;
- Fill out a paper form (available at the poster area) and drop it off at the collection box beside the posters or at Room 732 of the STU Youth office by 12:00 on October 21.

According to the schedule, teams must complete their explorations **between October 22 and 30** and take part in a results showcase on **October 31**. The organizers will select 12 funded projects based on submitted plans, each receiving 100 yuan in startup funding. First-, second-, and third-place prizes with corresponding awards will also be presented after the event.
