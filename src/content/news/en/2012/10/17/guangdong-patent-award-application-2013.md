Shantou University Research Office has forwarded the Guangdong Provincial Intellectual Property Office notice on the 2013 Guangdong Patent Award selection and recognition activity (Yue Ren She Fa [2012] No. 210) and is coordinating applications from relevant university units and patent holders.

Because the number of application places is limited, units or individuals interested in applying must contact the Research Office by October 26, 2012, to confirm the specific application arrangements. The provincial notice governs the eligibility requirements, selection process and materials.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Award | 2013 Guangdong Patent Award |
| Forwarding agency | Guangdong Provincial Intellectual Property Office |
| STU coordinating office | STU Research Office |
| Application guidance | Organize eligible patent outcomes; places are limited |
| Contact deadline | By October 26, 2012 |
| Contacts | Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong |
| Telephone | 82903271 |
| Email | dengyong@stu.edu.cn |
