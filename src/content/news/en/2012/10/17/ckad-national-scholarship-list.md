It is reported that, in accordance with the university's directive on evaluating the 2012 National Scholarship, National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant, the scholarship selection committee of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has proposed two students as recipients of this year's National Scholarship following interview-based assessment.

## Proposed Awardees

| Name | Cohort | Major |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He JH | 2009 | Art Design |
| Xiong ZR | 2010 | Public Art |

The list is now under public notice until **12:00 noon on October 22**. Anyone with objections may submit them in writing to the school's Youth League Committee within the period, either by phone or email.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
