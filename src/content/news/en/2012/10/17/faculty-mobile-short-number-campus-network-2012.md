To make campus communications more convenient, Shantou University Network and Information Center is collecting local China Mobile numbers used by faculty and staff for unified enrollment in or registration with the campus group network. Each unit must collect numbers from faculty and staff who wish to join or already belong to the network, complete the attached form and have it stamped with the unit&apos;s official seal. Materials must be submitted to the One Card service window on the first floor of the Network and Information Center by October 19.

Campus group-network users receive free calls within the group and no monthly fee. Faculty and staff who were already enrolled will no longer be charged the group-network monthly fee from November after completing this registration. Calls between participating mobile phones and between participating mobile phones and the university&apos;s new landlines are free.
