It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs issued a second round of publicity on October 17 for the 2011–2012 Academic Excellence Scholarship selection results.

To encourage diligence and academic achievement, the University established the Academic Excellence Scholarship with three tiers: First Prize at 1,500 yuan per person per year, Second Prize at 1,000 yuan, and Third Prize at 500 yuan. Under the Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate Scholarships of Shantou University (STU Fa [2010] No. 125), students whose GPA percentile ranked in the top 5% of their cohort and major in 2011–2012 are eligible for the First Prize, top 15% for the Second Prize, and top 30% for the Third Prize.

After the initial publicity on October 15, some students reported changes in grades or majors. Upon review, the Office released an updated shortlist:

| Award | Shortlisted |
|-------|-------------|
| First Prize | 177 students |
| Second Prize | 422 students |
| Third Prize | 626 students |

Any unit or individual with objections may submit written feedback to the Educational Administration Section, Room 106, Administrative Center, before 17:00 on Monday, October 22. Submissions from units must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must include the real name of the correspondent.

Applicants can log in to the Student Affairs System (http://sao.stu.edu.cn) with their campus network account to check individual results. Full shortlists are provided in the attachments.
