Shantou University Engineering School has published its nominees for the 2012 National Scholarship. Under the university&apos;s assessment arrangements for the National Scholarship, National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant, the Engineering School National Scholarship Selection Committee conducted interview assessments and plans to recommend three students for the 2012 National Scholarship.

To protect student privacy, names are shown using the family name and the initials of each given-name character. The nominees are Hu Y from the 2009 electronics cohort, Zhou JF from the 2009 computer science cohort and Xu FJ from the 2009 mechatronics cohort.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Award | 2012 National Scholarship, Engineering School |
| Nominees | 3 students |
| Assessment | Interview assessment and review by the Engineering School National Scholarship Selection Committee |
| Named nominees | Hu Y, 2009 electronics; Zhou JF, 2009 computer science; Xu FJ, 2009 mechatronics |
| Public-notice deadline | October 23, 2012, 5 p.m. |
| Feedback location | Engineering School Youth League Committee, Gongnan 210 |
| Telephone | 0754-82902986 |
| Email | yaoyi@stu.edu.cn |

Anyone with an objection may contact the Engineering School scholarship review group during the public-notice period. Materials submitted by an organization must bear its official seal; individuals must sign their real names.
