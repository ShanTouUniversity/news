To meet undergraduate demand for multimedia software instruction, Shantou University&apos;s Student Learning Center (SLC) is offering beginner classes taught by SLC peer tutors. The classes are open to enrolled undergraduates on the main campus.

The program has two sessions. Class A meets on Sundays in weeks 6–9, on October 21 and 28 and November 4 and 11. Class B meets on Sundays in weeks 10–13, on November 18 and 25 and December 2 and 9. Both sessions run from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. in the computer lab of the School of Arts.

| Class | Dates | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | October 21 and 28; November 4 and 11 | Sundays, 7:30–9:30 p.m. | School of Arts computer lab |
| B | November 18 and 25; December 2 and 9 | Sundays, 7:30–9:30 p.m. | School of Arts computer lab |

Students must register by noon on October 19 through the Reservation Center at [booking.stu.edu.cn](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/), under “SLC Reservations – Beginner Multimedia Software Class.” Each account may reserve only once. Places are offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Registered students must attend on time each week and may not be absent without reason. The list will be published on the campus office information network on October 19.

Students seeking more advanced instruction may use the Reservation Center to book an academic consultation with an SLC peer tutor.
