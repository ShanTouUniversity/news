It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on October 17, 2012, regarding student train discount cards for the 2012 cohort on the main campus, in accordance with the Ministry of Education and Ministry of Railways notice on improving student discount ticket purchases (Jiao Xue Ting Han [2011] No. 20).

Since the fall 2011 intake, student discount cards have stored student identity information and the registered travel section between the school and the family’s location.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Online entry | By October 21, log in to the Student Affairs System (“My Affairs” → “Modify Personal and Family Information”) to enter the boarding station and submit |
| Central handling | From October 24, classes should collect student IDs, the list of students purchasing cards and cash (7 yuan per card); on October 25, handle information import at the Graduate School Student Affairs Office (Room 401, New Administration Center) |
| Note | Cards without imported information cannot be used; regulations on student discount tickets are in Attachment 1 |
| Issued by | Graduate School |

> ⚠️ Regulations and forms are available on the OA system.
