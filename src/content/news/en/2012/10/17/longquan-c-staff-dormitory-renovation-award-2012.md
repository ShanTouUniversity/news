Shantou University has published the bidding result for renovation of the Type C staff housing at Longquan Garden. Following review and recommendation by the university&apos;s bidding and tendering leadership group, Shantou Chaoyang No. 1 Construction and Installation General Company won the bid under Plan 2 for RMB 3,708,133.00. The amount includes a provisional sum of RMB 176,577.75 fully controlled by the project owner.

The result will be publicized from October 18 to 20, 2012. Organizations and individuals may contact the project owner or submit complaints to the Supervision and Audit Office during the public-notice period.
