The Shantou University Youth League Committee has published the list of student-union cadres rated excellent for the 2011–12 academic year across the university&apos;s schools and residential colleges. The assessment followed the *STU Regulations on Student Cadre Administration* and considered student cadres&apos; work during the previous academic year, individual self-assessments and evaluations by their schools or residential colleges. The university Youth League Committee reviewed the results.

The original notice attachment contains the detailed list. Because it includes student personal information, this article does not reproduce student names.
