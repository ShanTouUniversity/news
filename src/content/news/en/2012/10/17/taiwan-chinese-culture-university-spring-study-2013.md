Shantou University&apos;s Office of International Exchange and Cooperation has opened applications for a spring 2013 study program at Chinese Culture University in Taiwan. Forty-five students previously studied at the university, and their feedback indicated that the experience helped broaden academic perspectives and improve communication and practical skills. With university approval, the office will continue organizing the spring program.

The program runs from February through June 2013 and is open to enrolled second- and third-year students, excluding medical majors. Applicants must have good academic performance, no failed courses in the previous year, and no disciplinary record at or above an official university notification.

Students are responsible for tuition, accommodation, living expenses and round-trip travel. STU will recognize courses completed at Chinese Culture University and process credit transfers after students return. Courses that meet STU program requirements may be considered for course substitution.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Host institution | Chinese Culture University, Taiwan |
| Study period | February–June 2013 |
| Applicants | Enrolled second- and third-year students, excluding medical majors |
| Requirements | Good academic performance; no failed courses in one year; law-abiding conduct and no disciplinary record at or above an official university notification |
| Costs | Tuition, accommodation, living expenses and round-trip travel paid by students |
| Credits | Courses recognized and transferred after return; eligible courses may be considered for substitution |
| Student deadline | Submit an application to the academic office of the student&apos;s school by October 31, 2012 |
| School recommendation | Complete recommendations by November 5 and email the list to the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation by 5 p.m. that day |
| Recommendation limit | Generally no more than 25% of the students in the major |
| Contact | icd@stu.edu.cn |

Schools will determine recommendation numbers based on teaching arrangements, enrollment and applications so that students&apos; absence does not disrupt regular teaching. The program brochure, application form and related rules are provided in the original attachments.
