Shantou University Engineering School will hold its 132nd Engineering Research Forum on October 19, 2012. The topic, “Efficient Dimension Reduction for Large Datasets and a Biomedical Application,” will examine methods for processing large-scale data and their use in biomedicine. Dr. Qing Chen will deliver the presentation.
