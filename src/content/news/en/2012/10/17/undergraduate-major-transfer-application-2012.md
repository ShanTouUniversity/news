Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has announced major-transfer arrangements for 2011-entry undergraduates. Under the *Interim Regulations on Credit-Based Student Status Management for STU Undergraduates*, each receiving major will publish its available places, application requirements, registration time and venue, and assessment time and venue.

Students in the 2011-entry cohort who wish to transfer may complete the *STU Major-Transfer Application Form* according to the requirements of the receiving major. Applicants must first ask the academic administrator in their current department to print a transcript and obtain approval signatures from the department head and school dean. They then register with the department of the intended major and take the assessment at the published time and place.

In week 12, the departments receiving transfer students will send the assessment results, application forms and course plans to Academic Affairs. After reviewing the applications and supporting materials, Academic Affairs will publish the proposed approval list. The university plans to announce its transfer decision in week 14. Approved students will begin studying in their new majors in spring 2013.

| Stage | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | 2011-entry undergraduates |
| Materials | STU Major-Transfer Application Form, transcript and STU Major-Transfer Course Plan, among other materials |
| Prior approval | Current department academic administrator prints the transcript; department head and school dean sign approval |
| Registration | Department of the intended major |
| Assessment | At the time and venue published by the intended major |
| Material submission | Receiving departments submit materials to Academic Affairs in week 12 |
| Public notice | Academic Affairs publishes the proposed approval list after review |
| Decision | Major-transfer decision announced in week 14 |
| Transfer start | Spring semester 2013 |

Receiving quotas, application requirements and detailed registration and assessment schedules are provided in the original Academic Affairs attachments.
