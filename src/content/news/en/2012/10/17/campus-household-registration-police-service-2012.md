Shantou public-security household-registration authorities will visit Shantou University on October 20, 2012, for an outdoor consultation event titled “Extending the Household-Registration Window and Bringing Services to Campus.” The service will provide policy information, on-site processing and certificate distribution for faculty members, staff and students.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Extending the Household-Registration Window and Bringing Services to Campus |
| Time | October 20, 2012, Saturday, 10 a.m. |
| Venue | Under the banyan tree on the STU pedestrian street |
| Service provider | Shantou public-security household-registration authorities |
| Services | Policy consultation, service processing and certificate distribution |
| Audience | STU faculty members, staff and students |

The STU community may visit the site to ask questions and handle relevant household-registration matters.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office)
