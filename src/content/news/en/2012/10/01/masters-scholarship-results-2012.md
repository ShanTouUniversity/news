Shantou University Graduate School has announced the 2012–2013 scholarship results for the 2012 cohort of master's students. The graduate training units and Graduate School completed the assessment in accordance with the university's graduate scholarship evaluation measures.

A total of 105 graduate students received first-tier scholarships, 143 received second-tier scholarships and 57 received third-tier scholarships, making 305 awards in all. The detailed award list was issued in the attachment to the Graduate School notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)

> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachment for the award list. This article does not publish students' names.
