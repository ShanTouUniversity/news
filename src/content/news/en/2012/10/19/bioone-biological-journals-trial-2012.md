Shantou University Library is offering faculty members, staff and students trial access to the BioOne biological-science journal database. Founded in 1999, BioOne is a nonprofit journal consortium formed by universities, scholarly associations and publishers and focused on biological-science research.

Its journals cover agriculture, animal science, biology, biochemistry, molecular biology, biodiversity conservation, ecology, environmental science, engineering, evolutionary biology, fisheries, forestry, genetics, geography, marine and freshwater biology, microbiology, botany, public and occupational health, veterinary medicine and zoology, among other subjects.
