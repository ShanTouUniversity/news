As of October 19, 2012, some student dormitories had not fully paid their September and earlier water and electricity bills. Under university rules, Shantou University will cut power to dormitories with unpaid balances from October 22 and restore supply after all outstanding bills are paid.

From October 22 to 24, the Network and Information Center will make two daily deductions through the campus card system. After a successful 10:30 a.m. deduction, the relevant dormitory will have power restored from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. After a successful 4:30 p.m. deduction, power will be restored from 5:30 to 8 p.m. A dormitory that pays after 4:30 p.m. will have power restored from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. the following day. After October 24, one deduction will be made at 4:30 p.m. each day.

| Time | Deduction and restoration |
| --- | --- |
| October 22–24, 10:30 a.m. | First deduction; successful payment restores power from 2:30–4:30 p.m. |
| October 22–24, 4:30 p.m. | Second deduction; successful payment restores power from 5:30–8 p.m. |
| Payment after 4:30 p.m. | Power restored from 2:30–4:30 p.m. the next day |
| After October 24 | One deduction at 4:30 p.m. daily |

Students with insufficient campus-card balances should recharge at a card-loading machine. Students with insufficient bank-account balances should deposit sufficient funds during banking hours and complete the campus-card loading process. Students who have lost a campus card may use the utilities-management system to set up delegated payment or follow the original notice&apos;s bank-payment procedure and deliver the receipt to Finance for posting. Finance does not accept cash directly.

The relevant departments&apos; notice governs the detailed list of dormitories with outstanding balances, bank-payment information and payment procedures.
