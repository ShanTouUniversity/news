Shantou University Science School has published its proposed nominees for the 2012 National Scholarship. Following student applications and preliminary review by the school Youth League Committee, five students entered interviews. The Science School scholarship assessment committee interviewed them on October 19 and considered academic performance, professional skills, awards, and contributions to the university and school. It plans to recommend two students for the National Scholarship.

To protect student privacy, names are shown using the family name and the initials of each given-name character. The proposed nominees are Chen LW and Jian W.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Award | 2012 National Scholarship |
| Interview candidates | 5 |
| Proposed nominees | Chen LW and Jian W |
| Assessment basis | Academic performance, professional skills, awards and contributions to STU and the school |
| Public-notice deadline | October 22, 2012, Monday, noon |
| Feedback location | Science School Youth League Committee office |
| Telephone | 0754-82902779 |

Anyone with an objection may submit it in writing to the Science School Youth League Committee before the deadline. Organizational materials must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed with the submitter&apos;s real name.
