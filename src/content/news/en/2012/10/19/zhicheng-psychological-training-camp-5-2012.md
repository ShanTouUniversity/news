To strengthen students&apos; mental health, resilience, cognition and ability to handle setbacks, Shantou University Zhicheng College will hold its fifth Psychological Development Camp on October 27, 2012. The theme is “Strength through Mutual Support and Facing Setbacks.”

The camp is open to students from Zhicheng College and selected other residential areas who volunteer to participate. It will accept 30 participants: 24 from Zhicheng College and six from other residential areas. If applications exceed the quota, participants will be selected by lottery.

Participants should genuinely wish to learn and challenge themselves, commit fully to the activity, follow unified arrangements and treat others respectfully. They must have no other classes or activities scheduled on October 27, attend the full-day program, follow the timetable and not leave the team without permission.

| --- | --- |
| Activity | Zhicheng College Fifth Psychological Development Camp |

| Places | 30: 24 for Zhicheng College and 6 for other residential areas |
| Selection | Lottery if applications exceed the quota |
| Deadline | October 24, 2012, 5:30 p.m. |
| Application | Download and complete the form from the college website and email it to dpjiang@stu.edu.cn |

Applications must follow the original naming and submission requirements; non-compliant applications will not be accepted. The form and detailed instructions are provided in the original attachments.
