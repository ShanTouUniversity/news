Shantou University has adjusted the schedule for *Exploring Marine Biology* because instructor Sun Zewei will be away at a conference. The class originally scheduled for periods 3–4 on Tuesday of week 6, October 23, 2012, in E Staircase 101, will be canceled. A make-up class will take place during periods 1–4 on Saturday of week 7, November 3, as a field study at a mangrove reserve.

Academic Affairs asks enrolled students to inform one another about the schedule change.
