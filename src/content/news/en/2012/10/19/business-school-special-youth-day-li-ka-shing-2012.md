Shantou University Business School will hold the “Achievement through Business, Heart Reaching the World” themed youth activity from October 20 to 25, 2012. The program will combine study materials on Li Ka-shing&apos;s career and life, written reflections and a speech contest to encourage students to consider professional qualities and career direction. It is organized by the Business School Youth League Committee and undertaken by its four 2012-entry classes for all Business School students.

The program has study, preliminary and final stages. The organizing committee will send study materials to Business School students&apos; campus mailboxes by 9 p.m. on October 21. Participants will write reflections based on the materials and may also conduct their own research. Each of the four 2012-entry classes will form a judging group and select five students for the final from submitted materials. A fifth review group will handle the preliminary review for upper-year students. The final will take the form of a speech contest, with Business School faculty members invited to serve as judges.
