Shantou University Public-Service Course Working Group has announced the results of its 2012 public-service course promotion contest. Held around the arrival of the 2012-entry cohort, the activity aimed to raise awareness of public-service courses and encourage more faculty members, staff and students to join volunteer service. Experts reviewed entries in video, photography and display-board categories.

| Category | First prize | Second prize | Third prize | Excellence award |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Video | 1 entry | 3 entries | 2 entries | — |
| Photography | 1 entry | 2 entries | 3 entries | 14 entries |
| Display boards | 1 entry | 2 entries | 3 entries | 10 entries |

The winning entries cover public-service course projects including care initiatives, rural service learning, support for people with disabilities, community service and health promotion. Contributors came from the Engineering School, Journalism School, Science School, Law School, Business School, School of Arts, School of Liberal Arts and related units. To protect student privacy, this article does not publish student names, student numbers or other personal information.

University staff will contact the winning contributors and issue certificates and prize money in the near future. The original notice governs the detailed list of winning entries and contributors.
