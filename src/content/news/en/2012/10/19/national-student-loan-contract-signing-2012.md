Bank of China Shantou Branch will sign contracts with the first batch of Shantou University National Student Loan recipients for the 2012–13 academic year on October 24, 2012. Relevant schools and residential colleges must notify the students on the attached list. Witnessing faculty members from the schools and residential colleges should also attend.

Students must arrive 20 minutes before the event to collect and check their loan contracts, promissory notes and other documents. They should bring an identity card, student card, Bank of China debit card and black pen.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Matter | First-batch 2012–13 National Student Loan contract signing |
| Time | October 24, 2012, Wednesday, 3 p.m. |
| Venue | E Staircase 101 |
| Participants | Loan recipients on the attachment, Bank of China Shantou Branch staff and witnessing faculty members |
| Student materials | Identity card, student card, Bank of China debit card and black pen |
| Arrival | Arrive 20 minutes early to collect and check contracts and promissory notes |

To protect student privacy, this article does not publish the attached student list or personal information. The original OA attachment governs the detailed participant list.
