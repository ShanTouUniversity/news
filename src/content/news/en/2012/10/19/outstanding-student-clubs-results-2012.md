The Shantou University Youth League Committee has announced the 2011–12 student-club awards. Under the regulations for outstanding student clubs, outstanding student-club presidents and outstanding student-club workers, the STU Student Clubs Federation organized the review and submitted it to the university Youth League Committee. The university has awarded the title of Outstanding Student Club to 10 clubs, Outstanding Student-Club President to 10 students and Outstanding Student-Club Worker to seven students.

| Award | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Student Club | 10 clubs |
| Outstanding Student-Club President | 10 students |
| Outstanding Student-Club Worker | 7 students |

The detailed list of recognized clubs and individuals is in the original attachment. To protect student privacy, student names are not published here. The Student Clubs Federation encourages the recognized clubs and students to value the honor, continue contributing to campus club culture and serve as examples for the wider student-organization community.
