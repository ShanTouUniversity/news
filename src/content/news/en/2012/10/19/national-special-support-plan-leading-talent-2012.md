Shantou University Human Resources Office has forwarded the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security General Office notice on recommending leading talent for the 2012 National Special Support Plan Hundred-Thousand-Ten-Thousand Project and has opened the university recommendation process. Candidates will be selected from people already included in the national-level Hundred-Thousand-Ten-Thousand Talent Project and must be under 50, meaning born after January 1, 1962.

Eligible faculty members may apply through their units. Applicants must use the Hundred-Thousand-Ten-Thousand Project information-collection software, print and sign two copies of the candidate information registration form generated by the software, and prepare two copies each of their identity card, academic-degree certificates, professional-qualification certificate and evidence of achievements or honors, including the Hundred-Thousand-Ten-Thousand Talent Project certificate. Materials must be submitted to the applicant&apos;s unit by October 24, and the software-generated database file must be emailed to Human Resources.

| Stage | Timing and requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | Faculty members already selected for the national-level Hundred-Thousand-Ten-Thousand Talent Project and born after January 1, 1962 |
| Application | Complete the information-collection software; print and sign two copies of the candidate registration form |
| Materials | Two copies each of identity card, academic-degree certificates, professional-qualification certificate and evidence of achievements or honors |
| Applicant deadline | Submit to the unit by October 24, 2012, and send the software-generated database file |
| Unit recommendation | Unit drafts its recommendation and sends it with the application materials to Human Resources by October 26 |
| Submission | Human Resources Office, Room 315, New Administration Center |
| Next step | The university determines its recommendation and submits it to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security |
| Email | o_rsk@stu.edu.cn |

The information-collection software and the higher-level notice are provided in the original attachments. The attachments govern detailed application requirements.
