Health insurance for female faculty and staff at Shantou University is approaching its expiration date, and the university union will organize renewal. Women&apos;s work committees of departmental unions must carefully check the insured lists. Missing people may be added, and newly transferred female faculty and staff who are not enrolled may also apply. All additional or new enrollees must complete a health declaration.

Each unit must enter information for new enrollees in the verification form, mark “new enrollee” in the remarks field and submit the insurance list to the university union by November 10. The notice requires insured information to use valid second-generation identity-card details; no personal identity numbers are reproduced here.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Insurance | Health insurance for female faculty and staff |
| Services | Renewal, additions for omitted people, and applications from newly transferred non-enrolled employees |
| Required form | Health declaration for additional or new enrollees |
| Unit work | Check the list, enter new enrollee information and mark “new enrollee” |
| Deadline | By November 10 |
| Submission | University union |
| Responsible body | University Union Women&apos;s Work Committee |

Units should verify enrollment information and submit it promptly. The union notice governs the detailed verification form and procedures.
