Applications are open for the 2012 Guangdong Education Science 12th Five-Year Plan projects, and the Shantou University Research Office is inviting submissions from relevant university units. The projects are self-funded. Proposed topics should support a strong education province, the early realization of education modernization and the development of a leading education hub in southern China, with emphasis on major practical and theoretical issues in Guangdong&apos;s education reform and development.

Applications should be rooted in Guangdong, reflect new research in the relevant field and local characteristics, promote theoretical innovation and practical research, and demonstrate originality and initiative. Proposals with papers or research reports as final outputs are encouraged.

| Item | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Funding | Self-funded |
| STU quota | 8 submissions |
| Project leader | One application per person; leaders of unfinished National Social Science Fund, National Education Science Planning, Ministry of Education, Guangdong Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning or Guangdong Department of Education projects may not apply |
| Project members | May participate in no more than two other project applications as members |
| Research period | 1–2 years for papers or research reports; 2–3 years for monographs |
| Initial application | Email the electronic application to ljwang@stu.edu.cn by November 1 |
| Final upload and paper copies | Upload the final application through the online system and submit two A4 double-sided copies to Research Office by November 7 |

Applicants must use the online application system and select a field code from the following 14 groups according to the topic: fundamental education theory and history (jk001), educational psychology (jk002), educational information technology (jk003), comparative education (jk004), moral education (jk005), educational economics and administration (jk006), education development strategy (jk007), basic education (jk008), higher education (jk009), vocational and technical education (jk010), adult education (jk011), physical, health and aesthetic education (jk012), ethnic education (jk013), and national defense and military education (jk014). Educational information technology and national defense and military education are organized by designated bodies and are outside this call.

Contacts are Huang Yumei and Huang Nan at 86502563 or 82902563. The online application process and application form are provided in the original attachments.
