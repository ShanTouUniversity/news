Shantou University has opened project applications for student-club activities in the fall semester of the 2012–13 academic year. Student-club activities are an important channel for comprehensive practical education and help clubs present their culture, contribute to campus culture and develop activities in an appropriate direction.

Applications are open from October 19 through 28, 2012. Each club must submit the relevant materials, including an application and activity plan, by October 28. The management rules are set out in the *STU Student Clubs Federation Regulations on Student-Club Activity Applications*. Further details will be explained at the fall-semester student-club presidents&apos; meeting.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | STU student clubs |
| Term | Fall semester of the 2012–13 academic year |
| Application period | October 19–28, 2012 |
| Deadline | October 28, 2012 |
| Materials | Application, activity plan and other relevant documents |
| Follow-up | Details explained at the fall-semester student-club presidents&apos; meeting |
| Organizers | STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Clubs Federation |

Electronic submission procedures and management rules are governed by the original notice and attachments. To protect student privacy, student contact names and personal email addresses are not published here.
