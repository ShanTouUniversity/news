Shantou University Student Learning Center (SLC) has announced the admissions result for its beginner multimedia software class. Following the earlier call for applications, which received responses from students across the schools, 50 students were admitted. The detailed list is in the original attachment.

SLC peer tutors provide introductory multimedia software instruction. Students seeking more advanced software training or one-to-one support may visit the Reservation Center at [booking.stu.edu.cn](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/) to book a conversation with a peer tutor.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Class | SLC beginner multimedia software class |
| Result | 50 students admitted |
| Service | Introductory multimedia software instruction by peer tutors |
| Advanced learning | Book a conversation or one-to-one service with a peer tutor through the Reservation Center |
| Platform | [booking.stu.edu.cn](http://booking.stu.edu.cn/) |

To protect student privacy, this article does not publish the admitted-student list or other personal information.
