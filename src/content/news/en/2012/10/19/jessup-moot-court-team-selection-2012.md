Shantou University Law School has announced the selection results for its Jessup International Law Moot Court team. Following interviews and review by the Law School selection group, eight students were selected as competitors and four students joined the Jessup team as research assistants.

To protect student privacy, names are shown using the family name and the initials of each given-name character. One name contained a garbled character in the original notice; it is rendered as “Lin *D” here without attempting to reconstruct the missing character.

| Category | Selected students |
| --- | --- |
| Competitors (8) | Cong WS; Lin *D; Mo SH; Zhou YJ; Xiong ZL; Yang HY; Liao SF; Lao JJ |
| Research assistants (4) | Li J; Huang YX; Mo ZJ; Ye DW |

The eight competitors will represent the Law School in the Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition, while the four research assistants will provide team support.
