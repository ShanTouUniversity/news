To enrich faculty and staff sports and cultural life, promote fitness and encourage community exchange, the Shantou University union will hold a Cadillac–Shantou University faculty and staff hike on the Double Ninth Festival, October 23, 2012. The event is open to main-campus faculty and staff.

Participants will assemble at 2:40 p.m. in front of the E Building, follow a route through the east gate and Nongmin Road to the summit, marked by a red flag, and return to the E Building by the same route. At the summit, participants will receive lottery and gift coupons. Lottery coupons can be used at the departure station in front of the E Building, and every participant will receive a gift from the university union.

| Prize | Places | Gift |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Special prize | 10 | Cadillac model car |
| First prize | 10 | Cadillac key-shaped lighter |
| Second prize | 15 | Cadillac windproof umbrella |
| Third prize | 200 | Cadillac Centenary Classics music CD |

The prizes are sponsored by Cadillac Shantou Zhongcheng 4S Store and are available while supplies last.

Participants should decide whether to join according to their health and work schedules. The route carries some risk, and people with hypertension or cardiovascular and cerebrovascular conditions are discouraged from participating. Participants should wear loose clothing, preferably long trousers and long sleeves, and sports shoes; use sun protection, manage their pace and walk in groups. Racing is discouraged. Smoking and open flames are prohibited, and participants should dispose of waste properly. Medical service points from the university hospital will be available midway and at the summit.
