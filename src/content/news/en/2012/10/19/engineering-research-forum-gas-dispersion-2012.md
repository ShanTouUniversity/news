Shantou University Engineering School will hold its 133rd Engineering Research Forum on October 23, 2012. Dr. Jiang Guoyi will present “Wind Tunnel Experiment and Large Eddy Simulation of Gas Dispersion in Non-isothermal Boundary Layers.” The report will examine wind-tunnel experiments and large-eddy simulations of gas dispersion in non-isothermal boundary layers.

The forum is intended for Civil Engineering faculty members and other interested students in the Engineering School and will be held in the school&apos;s Academic Exchange Room.
