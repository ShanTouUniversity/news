Shantou University Science School has released its first 2012 work newsletter, issue three overall. The newsletter reviews the school&apos;s recent work in discipline development, talent development, educational reform, scientific research and campus activities.

The “School Highlights” section covers the chemistry discipline&apos;s entry into the top 1% globally in ESI, Executive President Gu Peihua&apos;s discussion with Science School faculty on advanced undergraduate education, the launch of the STU–UTEP undergraduate research exchange program, and the ninth representative congress of the Guangdong Society of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology held in Shantou.

The “Student Development” section reports on one undergraduate student&apos;s selection for a graduate mathematics foundation enhancement program at the Beijing International Mathematical Research Center and a first prize won by students from the Biology Department at the Guangdong University Biochemistry Experimental Skills Competition. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.

The newsletter also covers the following areas:

| Section | Main content |
| --- | --- |
| Undergraduate reform and discipline development | Undergraduate education exchanges; expert reviews of training standards in mathematics and applied mathematics, applied physics, applied chemistry and biotechnology; graduate-work exchange; graduate summer workshop on advanced materials |
| Administration | ISO interim audit; faculty exchange and discussion with key secondary schools in Shaoguan |
| Research and industry cooperation | 2012 National Natural Science Foundation projects; marine-biology research and industry cooperation; joint laboratory with Jinfa Labi Maternal and Infant Products Co., Ltd.; open projects at the Guangdong Key Laboratory of Marine Biotechnology |
| Faculty development | Recruitment and faculty recognition |
| External exchange and lectures | External exchanges and academic lectures |
| Student affairs | The Science School career-learning workshop, its Open Day activities and Xuanli Cup events and student achievements |
