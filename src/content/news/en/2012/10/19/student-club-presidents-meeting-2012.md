The Shantou University Youth League Committee and Student Clubs Federation will hold a fall-semester 2012–13 student-club presidents&apos; meeting and activity-project briefing on October 20, 2012. Student-club leaders will prepare for applications for the semester&apos;s club activity projects.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting | Fall 2012–13 student-club presidents&apos; meeting and activity-project briefing |
| Time | October 20, 2012, Saturday, 2:30–4 p.m. |
| Venue | Conference Room 726, Building 789 |
| Participants | Student-club leaders |
| Organizers | STU Youth League Committee and STU Student Clubs Federation |

Student-club leaders should prepare in advance and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
