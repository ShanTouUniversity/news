To implement the campaign to identify and address fire hazards and comply with the *STU Fire Safety Management System*, Shantou University Resource Management Office has asked all departments and units to conduct fire-safety self-inspections and correct problems promptly.

Units must check whether extinguishers, fire hydrants, evacuation signs and emergency lights are in good condition, confirm that fire lanes are clear and identify other hazards. They must report the inspection results and hazards to the Security Office in writing or by email by October 30, 2012. The Security Office will organize follow-up inspections.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Units | All STU departments and units |
| Inspection | Extinguishers, fire hydrants, evacuation signs, emergency lights, fire lanes and other hazards |
| Deadline | By October 30, 2012 |
| Reporting | Written report or email to the Security Office |
| Follow-up | Security Office conducts reinspection |
| Security Office telephone | 82903436 |
| Security Office email | o_bwk@stu.edu.cn |

Safety officers who have not attended training should register with the Shantou Public Security Fire Training Center. The training site is the rear building of the Fire Command Center on the middle section of Daxue Road. The fire-safety inspection record form is provided in the original attachment.
