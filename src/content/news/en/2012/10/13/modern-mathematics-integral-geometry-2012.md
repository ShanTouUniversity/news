The 125th lecture in the Toward Modern Mathematics series will be held at Shantou University on October 15, 2012. Zhou Jiazu, a professor, doctoral supervisor and director of the Institute of Mathematics at Southwest University and a standing council member of the Chinese Mathematical Society, will give an Introduction to Integral Geometry lecture for faculty members, graduate students and senior undergraduates.

Integral geometry is fundamentally part of global differential geometry. It originated in the study of geometric probability and has continued to develop in close connection with that field. Research began in the Euclidean plane and three-dimensional Euclidean space, then expanded to higher-dimensional Euclidean and non-Euclidean spaces and eventually to homogeneous spaces satisfying specified conditions.
