To meet demand from faculty members and students, Professor Pan Yugang&apos;s book *I Also Write About Li Ao* will remain available for purchase on the Shantou University campus. At Professor Pan&apos;s request, all sales revenue will be directed to the Pan Yugang Scholarship.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Sales location | Room 354, Science Building, School of Science Youth League Office |
| Sales hours | Monday–Friday, 9–11:30 a.m. and 2:30–5 p.m. |
| Use of revenue | Entirely directed to the Pan Yugang Scholarship |

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science)
