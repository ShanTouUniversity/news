The STU School of Science&apos;s Science Salon will hold an academic lecture on October 19, 2012. Yang Qingzheng, a researcher and doctoral supervisor at the Institute of Physical and Chemical Technology of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, will speak on “Building Organic Photoresponsive Systems.”

The talk will cover two lines of research: the design and synthesis of molecular mechanochemical probes based on the photoisomerization of rigid stilbenes and their application in small-molecule mechanochemistry, and the construction of light-harvesting systems through supramolecular assembly. Yang&apos;s research focuses on organic and supramolecular photochemistry. He has published 28 papers in international journals including *Nature Nanotechnology*, *Angewandte Chemie International Edition*, *Journal of the American Chemical Society* and *Chemical Communications*; 14 appeared in journals with impact factors above 5, and three were selected as cover articles. His work has been reported or reviewed positively by publications including *Nature*, *Nature Nanotechnology*, *Chemical & Engineering News*, *Chemistry World* and *JACS*.

Yang has led projects under the Chinese Academy of Sciences Hundred Talents Program, National Natural Science Foundation of China General Program and a key direction of the CAS Knowledge Innovation Program. In 2012, he received funding from the NSFC Excellent Young Scientists Fund.
