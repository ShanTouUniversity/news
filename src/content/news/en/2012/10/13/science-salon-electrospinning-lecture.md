It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University will host a new session of its "Science Salon" lecture series. Wu Dayong, a researcher at the Technical Institute of Physics and Chemistry (TIPC) of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, has been invited to give a talk titled "High-Throughput Electrospinning Technology and Functional Nanofiber Materials."

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Title | High-Throughput Electrospinning Technology and Functional Nanofiber Materials |
| Time | October 19, 2012 (Friday), 14:30–15:30 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 3#, ACC |
| Speaker | Wu Dayong, Researcher, TIPC, Chinese Academy of Sciences |

The lecture will trace the development of high-throughput electrospinning technology and present its latest advances, covering the preparation of nanofibers as well as research progress and applications of functional nanofiber membrane materials.

## About the Speaker

Wu Dayong pursued his doctoral studies at the Graduate University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences / TIPC from September 1997 to August 2002, followed by postdoctoral research at the Institute of Inorganic Chemistry, University of Tübingen, Germany. He joined TIPC in October 2005, where he served as associate researcher and later researcher, and has also served as deputy general manager of Beijing Shouke Penbo Technology Development Co., Ltd. since January 2011.

His research interests include high-throughput electrospinning, functional polymer thin-film materials, and homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis. He is currently leading the industrialization of new lithium battery separator materials as chief scientist of a major Beijing science-to-industry transformation project, "Pilot-Scale Research and Industrialization of Nanofiber Lithium-Ion Battery Separators." He has led numerous research projects in energy materials funded by the Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission, the Ministry of Science and Technology's "863 Program," the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the National Natural Science Foundation of China. He has published more than 40 papers and filed nearly 20 domestic and international patents.
