Under the power-outage plan issued by the Resource Management Office, the Shantou University Network and Information Center will shut down data-center and other equipment rooms in the outage area in advance to reduce the risk of damage to network equipment. Network services in some areas will be suspended in stages, and the center will restart suspended services as soon as power becomes stable.

| Time | Affected areas and arrangements |
| --- | --- |
| October 14, 8 a.m.–1 p.m. | Equipment rooms at the power station, Labor Union, Wen East, Wen West, Shu 202 and Xing West, as well as user areas Gong West and Gong North, may be affected |
| October 14, 1–6:30 p.m. | Network service will stop in the outage area containing 72 equipment rooms; the data-center UPS can provide more than four hours of backup power, so the campus core network, external connection, campus applications and websites should remain largely unaffected |

UPS power will prioritize the core network and critical systems. If the actual outage exceeds five hours, the center will shut down secondary services; if it exceeds six hours, all services will be stopped. Network services will restart as soon as power is restored and stable.

The center asked users to share the update. Duty officers can be reached at 82903413-100 or 86503400, campus short number 883400; the official email is stunic@stu.edu.cn. This article does not publish a personal mobile number.
