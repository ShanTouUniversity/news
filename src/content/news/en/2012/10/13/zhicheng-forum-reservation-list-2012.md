Shantou University Zhicheng College has published the reservation arrangements for the 13th Zhicheng Forum. The lecture will be held at 10 a.m. on October 14, 2012, in the New Library Lecture Hall. Faculty members and students whose reservations were confirmed must enter by 9:50 a.m.

The confirmed reservation list is included in the original Zhicheng College notice. To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce the list; relevant attendees should consult the OA system to verify their reservation status.
