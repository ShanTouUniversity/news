It is reported that the Supervision and Audit Office issued a notice on October 8, 2012, to organize a self-inspection on school affairs disclosure and democratic management, in line with documents from the Guangdong Department of Education.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Basis | Provincial documents Yue Jiao Jian Ban [2012] No. 3 and Yue Jiao Jian Han [2012] No. 12 |
| Scope | Departments are asked to summarize innovations and achievements of school affairs disclosure in promoting reform, safeguarding staff rights, building a harmonious campus and strengthening Party and leadership development; review leadership systems, operating mechanisms and regulations; and compile disclosure practices, effectiveness and problems as per the attachment |
| Deadline | October 12, 2012 |
| Submission | Send to the Supervision and Audit Office at o_jj@stu.edu.cn |
| Leading units | Office for School Affairs Disclosure and its Supervision Group |

Detailed requirements are available in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The self-inspection requirements are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Supervision and Audit Office)
