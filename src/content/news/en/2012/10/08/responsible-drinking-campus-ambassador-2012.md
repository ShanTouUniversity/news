The Shantou University SIFE team has announced recruitment for the 2012 Responsible Drinking Campus Ambassador project and invited students to form teams and design public-service actions. The project slogan is “Responsible Drinking: The Right Choice for Young People.” Actions must serve the public interest and may not be designed for profit.

## Project requirements

Each team should have four to six enrolled students who care about public service, think critically and are willing to act. Teams must submit an application and a specific action plan using the organizer's template, choosing one of two themes: “Do not drive after drinking” or “Minors should not drink.”

Teams may use an advocacy or behavior-intervention approach. Advocacy projects may use salons, lectures, publicity films, microfilms or short videos to communicate responsible-drinking ideas. Behavior-intervention projects may use training and participation in activities to encourage positive changes in the target group's drinking behavior.

## Support and awards

Teams that pass the preliminary selection will receive one free on-site training session in one of six cities: Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Wuhan, Chengdu or Xiamen. The 12 teams that pass the second selection will receive CNY 5,000 in public-service action funding. After the actions, five Responsible Drinking Ambassador teams will be selected based on their results; each will receive a certificate and CNY 8,000 for follow-up public-service work. The top team will also receive an overseas cultural-exchange opportunity provided by Pernod Ricard China.

The application and action-plan templates are provided in the attachments issued with the Business School notice.
