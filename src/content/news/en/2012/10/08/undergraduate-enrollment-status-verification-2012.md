Under arrangements from the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the China Higher-education Student Information and Career Center (CHSI) platform has opened enrollment-status verification for the 2012 freshman cohort. The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office asks colleges at the main campus to organize the verification by students admitted as undergraduates in 2012.

The verification period is October 8–23, 2012. Students may visit the Ministry of Education's CHSI platform at <http://xjxl.chsi.com.cn/xscx.do> and follow the page instructions to check their admission and registration status and student information. Admission data for students from Hong Kong and Macao had not yet been uploaded at the time of the notice, so those students could not complete this verification.

Students who need to view detailed student-status information must create a real-name CHSI archive. The Academic Affairs Office suggested that students who only need to verify admission and registration wait until their senior year to create an account, reducing the risk of forgetting account credentials. Students who need detailed information should check their registered details carefully and protect their account information.

The office reminded students that the CHSI account is important for checking student status and later verifying educational credentials. Students should review their information before registration. Problems caused by incorrect personal entries, forgotten credentials or disclosure of personal information must be handled with CHSI customer service; help is available at <https://account.chsi.com.cn/account/help/question.jsp>.

Questions during verification may be directed to Ms. Xu of the Academic Affairs Office Student Affairs Section during working hours at `86502623`.
