Shantou University Graduate School has announced the online registration and form-submission arrangements for students recommended for admission to master's programs in 2013. The students are asked to complete the sequence of contacting a receiving institution, attending its admissions defense, collecting an online-registration verification code and submitting the required documents.

Recommended students must contact their intended institution, apply for admission and attend its defense. They may register online and complete the recommended-student registration form only after receiving an acceptance letter. Students applying to STU, and students applying elsewhere who have not received an acceptance letter by October 12, should apply for STU admission under the university's rules for accepting recommended students. STU accepts recommended students in all disciplines and professional fields except the MBA and MPA; academic programs accept only academically oriented recommended students.

| Deadline | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| By October 12, 2012 | Apply to STU under the relevant rules or confirm the acceptance status from another institution |
| By October 17, 2012 | Attend the defense arranged for the applied discipline or field |
| By October 20, 2012 | Bring the acceptance letter and student card to the Graduate School to collect the verification code and form |
| By October 23, 2012 | Complete online registration with the code, fill in the form and submit it |

The verification code can be used only once, so students should confirm the receiving institution, program, research direction and supervisor before using it. Each student receives one original form; photocopies are invalid. After completion, the recommending department must stamp the form before it is submitted to the Graduate School for consolidation and subsequent stamping and mailing. The receiving institution will retain the form after it is stamped by the provincial admissions office.

The STU recommended-admission rules are available on the Graduate School website at <http://www.gs.stu.edu.cn/yjszs/showrecruit.jsp?recruit_id=6&category_id=94>.
