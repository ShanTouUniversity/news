The Shantou University Faculty Football Association is recruiting new members and players from across the university at the beginning of the new academic year. Supported by the University Labor Union, the association holds regular team training on Wednesday afternoons and occasionally represents STU in friendly matches with organizations outside the university.

Interested faculty and staff members should complete the STU Faculty Football Association membership form and email it to `ygluo@stu.edu.cn`, using “Football Association Application” as the subject. Applicants may attend a trial session after submitting the form.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Faculty and staff across STU |
| Trial sessions | Afternoon of October 10 or October 17, 2012 |
| Venue | Football field |
| Application email | `ygluo@stu.edu.cn` |
| Contact | Luo Yingguang, `82903271` |

The association said its activities help strengthen connections among faculty and staff and enrich campus cultural and sporting life.
