The Shantou University Resource Management Office Infrastructure Office has posted a proposed addition to the renovation of the Seventh Lecture Hall classroom. The renovation began on September 4, 2012. In response to the user unit's requirements and conditions encountered during construction, the project would add an aluminum window inside the south-facing steel windows to improve sound insulation.

The Infrastructure Office estimates that the addition would increase the cost by approximately CNY 4,000, representing less than the 10 percent threshold specified by the university. The notice is issued under the Shantou University Implementation Measures for the Administration of Bidding for Infrastructure Projects.

Written objections to the proposed addition may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on October 10, 2012.
