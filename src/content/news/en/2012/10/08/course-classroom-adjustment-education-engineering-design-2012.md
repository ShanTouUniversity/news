For teaching needs, Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has changed the classrooms for selected classes of Education and Introduction to Engineering Design. The changes take effect from the week of October 8, 2012, and all listed classes will meet in E Lecture Hall 201.

| Class number | Course | Original time | New classroom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 55712 | [HED1830] Education | Wednesday, periods 3–4 | E Lecture Hall 201 |
| 55830 | [ENC9105] Introduction to Engineering Design | Thursday, odd weeks, periods 1–2 | E Lecture Hall 201 |
| 55831 | [ENC9105] Introduction to Engineering Design | Thursday, even weeks, periods 1–2 | E Lecture Hall 201 |
| 55832 | [ENC9105] Introduction to Engineering Design | Monday, odd weeks, periods 8–9 | E Lecture Hall 201 |
| 55833 | [ENC9105] Introduction to Engineering Design | Monday, even weeks, periods 8–9 | E Lecture Hall 201 |

The Academic Affairs Office asks the relevant colleges and students to share the update and attend classes in the new room.
