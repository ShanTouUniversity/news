The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has arranged submission of laboratory-course schedules for the 2012–2013 fall semester. Teaching units should organize their undergraduate teaching laboratories to complete the schedule forms and submit the required materials.

The schedules must be completed by Friday, October 12, 2012. Units should submit the electronic file first, followed by printed copies. The electronic version should be sent to `yzchen@stu.edu.cn`. Two printed copies are required; the laboratory director of each department should collect them, deliver one to the Academic Affairs Office on the third floor of the New Administration Center and retain the other with the department academic administrator.
