The Shantou University School of Science will hold a Science Salon academic talk on October 12, 2012. Dr. Yang Zhaoqi of Nanyang Technological University in Singapore will present research on how human topoisomerase I (hTopo I) recognizes bent DNA and interacts with it.

hTopo I participates in DNA replication, transcription, recombination and repair and is an important subject in basic life-science and anticancer-drug research. The talk will introduce work using circular DNA molecules with different degrees of constrained bending to study interactions between the enzyme and its substrates. The research found that hTopo I tends to bind DNA with constrained bending and can distinguish the degree of bending; DNA with greater constrained bending is more suitable for binding with the enzyme. The findings were also used to explore inhibition of hTopo I activity and offer ideas for developing new inhibitors.
