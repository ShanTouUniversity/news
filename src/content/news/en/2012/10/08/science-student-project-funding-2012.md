It is reported that the Leadership Group for Extracurricular Academic Activities of the School of Science issued a notice on October 8, 2012, announcing the results of the midterm review for the 2012 extracurricular science and technology projects.

After defenses and discussions, the expert panel evaluated the 20 previously approved projects on progress, difficulty and funding needs, deciding to grant additional funding totaling 31,000 yuan to all 20 projects.

The group urged all team members to redouble efforts and supervisors to strengthen guidance to achieve better results in the later phase. Projects with strong outcomes may continue to receive support and be shortlisted for the national Challenge Cup.
