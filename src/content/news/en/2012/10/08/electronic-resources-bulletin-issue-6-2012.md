Shantou University Library has published Issue 6 of its 2012 Electronic Resources Bulletin, the sixth issue overall. The issue highlights database updates, online academic activities and research-information services.

## Highlights

Engineering Village introduced a new user interface on July 18, 2012. Features include an “Add Search Field” option, a simplified results interface, draggable function panels, easier combination of previous searches, search-history access from the homepage and options to display 25, 50 or 100 results per page.

Wiley Online Library is running an Online Research Safari from September 18 to November 18, covering life sciences, health sciences, physical and engineering sciences, and the social sciences and humanities. The online activity includes eight questions and takes no more than 20 minutes. Participants who answer every question correctly will enter a prize draw. The activity is available at <http://olabout.wiley.com/WileyCDA/Section/id-813051.html>.

The bulletin also reports Wiley's 2011 journal impact-factor data: 1,156 Wiley journals, approximately 76 percent of its journals, were included in the Thomson ISI Journal Citation Reports. Among journals that received impact factors in both 2010 and 2011, 581, or 52.4 percent, increased their impact factors; 194 recorded increases of at least 25 percent.

IEEE will provide two or three IEL database webinars each month from the new semester. Topics include database search techniques, submission procedures, member resources and librarian tools and services. Details are available at <http://cn.ieee.org/online_training.html>. The 2012 autumn Web of Knowledge online lecture series contains six themes and 24 sessions and runs through early December; the schedule is available at <http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/2012WOKonline/course.htm>.
