The Shantou University School of Science will hold a Science Salon academic talk on October 10, 2012, featuring Professor Chen Huanyang of the School of Physical Science and Technology at Soochow University.

Transformation optics has developed into a mature research field since it was proposed in 2006. The talk will introduce its basic concepts and influence and discuss optical devices designed with the technology, including cloaking devices, “through-wall” concepts, highly collimated antennas and imaging systems.

| Venue | School of Science Lecture Hall, Li-352 |

Professor Chen is a distinguished professor and doctoral supervisor at Soochow University. His research focuses on transformation optics and metamaterials. His doctoral dissertation was selected among China's 100 Outstanding Doctoral Dissertations, and he has published more than 40 papers in journals including *Nature Materials* and *Physical Review Letters*. His research has also received coverage from international science media.

Faculty members and students interested in the topic are welcome to attend.
