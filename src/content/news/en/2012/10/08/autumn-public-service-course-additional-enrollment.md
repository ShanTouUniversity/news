It is reported that after the initial enrollment and add/drop rounds, a small number of places remain in the autumn semester public service courses at Shantou University. Students who have not yet taken a public service course may register before 9:00 am on October 9.

## Available Courses

| Unit | Course | Schedule | Places |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medical College | [SER3310] Rural Health Promotion Program | Unified training on Oct 24 (10 hours); field service on Oct 27, 28 and Nov 3 (32 hours over three days); summary and evaluation on Nov 7 (6 hours) | 9 |
| College of Science | [SER1110] Toward the Ocean — Outreach on Marine Territory Awareness and Marine Ecological Protection | Tuesdays in weeks 4–10; Saturdays of weeks 6, 8 and 9, and Sunday of week 9 | 2 |

A total of 11 places are available across the two courses. Interested students (who have not yet enrolled in a public service course) must send their registration information — including grade, student ID, name, chosen course, contact details and email address — to the public service course mailbox gykc@stu.edu.cn before 9:00 am on October 9.

## Enrollment Rules

According to the Academic Affairs Office, supplementary enrollment follows a first-come, first-served principle until all places are filled, and each student may choose only one course. Questions can be directed to Ms. Liu at the office.
