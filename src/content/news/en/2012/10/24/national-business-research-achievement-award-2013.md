The Shantou University Research Office has forwarded a Ministry of Commerce notice soliciting entries for the 2013 National Business Development Research Achievement Awards. The call is open to organizations and individuals engaged in business theory, policy research or practical business work and accepts books, papers and research reports.

Established in 1999, the award aims to advance business theory and policy research, improve research quality, develop business researchers and recognize work with a significant influence on government decision-making or practical work. First, second and third prizes are offered separately for books, papers and research reports. Award-winning papers and reports will be published in collected volumes.

Eligible entries are social-science research in the business field that were publicly published in national publications from January 1, 2011, through December 31, 2012, and have substantial academic or theoretical value. Research results and reports published in internally circulated publications may also apply. Applicants and organizations must be engaged in business theory, policy research or practical business work.

### Priority Topics

The call covers China’s opening-up strategy; economic globalization and regional economic cooperation; foreign and outward investment; fair trade in imports and exports; modern circulation methods and circulation-system reform; the return of manufacturing and related theory; trade value chains; the Third Industrial Revolution; trade theory and policy; and current or difficult issues in foreign trade.

### Application Materials

| Category | Main materials |
| --- | --- |
| Books | 10 copies of the book, 10 work summaries and one application form. Summaries must not identify the author, recommender or organization. The application must carry the applicant’s organizational comments and seal. Mail or deliver materials to the secretariat and email Word versions of the summaries and application to keyanchu@caitec.org.cn. |
| Papers | One sample journal issue, 10 printed copies with the author and related information removed, 10 summaries bound to the first page of the copies, and one application form with organizational comments and seal. Mail or deliver materials and email Word versions of the work, summaries and application to keyanchu@caitec.org.cn. |
| Research reports | One sample publication, 10 printed copies with the author and related information removed, 10 summaries bound to the first page of the copies, and one application form with organizational comments and seal. Mail or deliver materials and email Word versions of the work, summaries and application to keyanchu@caitec.org.cn. |

General business inspection reports, work summaries, introductions to a single business area and purely textbook works will not be accepted, except for textbook works with breakthrough or innovative significance. Reviewers follow a recusal principle: a reviewer whose work is entered cannot serve on the panel for the same award year.

Application forms may be obtained from the award secretariat or a local commerce authority, or downloaded from the website of the Chinese Academy of International Trade and Economic Cooperation at <http://www.caitec.org.cn/>. STU contacts are Huang Yumei and Huang Nan at 82902563 or 86502563.
