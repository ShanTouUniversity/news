The Chemistry Department of the Shantou University Science School has launched its 2012 alumni scholarship selection. Proposed by Chemistry alumni and backed by a 222,800-yuan initial donation, the scholarship aims to encourage high-achieving students to apply to the Chemistry Department, support current students in their studies and assist students facing financial hardship.

The scholarship is for full-time Chemistry Department undergraduates in the Science School who demonstrate strong academic and personal records and have financial need. Twenty students will be recognized each year, including eight first-year students, and each recipient will receive 2,500 yuan.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scholarship | STU Chemistry Department Alumni Scholarship |
| Applicants | Full-time Chemistry Department undergraduates in the Science School with strong academic and personal records and financial need |
| Annual places | 20, including 8 first-year students |
| Award | 2,500 yuan per recipient |
| Initial fund | 222,800-yuan alumni donation |
| Deadline | Before 4 p.m. on October 29, 2012, Monday |
| Submission | Science School Youth League Committee, Science Building 354 |
| Materials | Completed application form, printed double-sided and signed |

Interested students must complete, print, sign and submit the application form before the deadline. The form is provided in the original attachment.
