Preparations for the Shantou University Graduate School Master’s and Doctoral Volunteer Group have been completed, and its founding meeting will take place on October 24, 2012. The group will follow the principle that volunteer service can be undertaken by everyone, at any time and everywhere. Through cooperation between the university and local communities, it will organize service activities that respond to social needs, develop graduate students’ sense of social responsibility and help participants gain knowledge and skills.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Graduate School Master’s and Doctoral Volunteer Group |
| Founding meeting | October 24, 2012, 4–5 p.m. |
| Venue | STU Science Building 205 |
| Participants | All preparatory committee members, representatives of the school’s Party branches, Youth League Committee members and a Graduate Student Union representative |
| Service focus | Organized volunteer service through university-community cooperation |
