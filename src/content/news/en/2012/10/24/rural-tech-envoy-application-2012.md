It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology has launched the application and accreditation process for the third batch of Guangdong Rural Science and Technology Envoys and their workstations, in accordance with its notice Yue Ke Han Nong Zi [2012] No. 1455. Shantou University's Research Office is now coordinating on-campus applications.

## Eligibility

### Envoy Requirements

- Expertise in agriculture-related technologies and achievements, or experience in agricultural science and technology management with knowledge of agricultural product markets;
- Rich experience working at the grassroots rural level, with relatively stable service recipients or regions;
- Good health and a strong commitment to serving rural and agricultural scientific and industrial development in Guangdong;
- Priority given to personnel with outstanding achievements in science and technology services and entrepreneurship.

Envoys are categorized into three types: individual envoys, team envoys, and legal-entity envoys.

### Workstation Requirements

Institutions affiliated with prefecture- and county-level administrative departments for science and technology, agriculture, marine fisheries, and forestry (such as productivity promotion centers), industry technology extension units, and rural economic cooperatives may apply if they meet the following criteria:

- Demonstrated achievements in advancing agricultural and rural science and technology, promoting relevant technologies and achievements, and cultivating agricultural talent;
- Willingness to assist rural envoys in their service and entrepreneurial activities within the region (prefecture-level city or county) or industry.
