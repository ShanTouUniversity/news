The Shantou University Library will hold a Subject Literature Services lecture on October 25, 2012, titled “Literature Reviews, Literature Searching and Statistical Analysis.” Led by Xie Luqing, the session is intended especially for graduate students and senior undergraduates preparing literature reviews, while all STU faculty members, staff and students are welcome.

Using China National Knowledge Infrastructure (CNKI) and Engineering Village as examples, the lecture will explain how to choose search strategies that improve recall and precision. It will also demonstrate how bibliometric functions and database statistics can help assess the current state of a research topic, leading institutions and authors, development trends and research hotspots.
