The Shantou University Science School has opened its 2012 selection for the Pan Yugang Scholarship and Sendeli Chemical Scholarship. Together, the two scholarships will recognize eight current Science School students this year, with each recipient receiving 2,500 yuan.

The Pan Yugang Scholarship was established in 2006 with a 60,000-yuan donation from Pan Yugang, an STU honorary professor and tenured professor at Boston College. It has completed six selection cycles and distributed the original donation to 24 students. Professor Pan has also donated proceeds from his book *I Also Talk about Li Ao* to continue supporting the scholarship fund.

The Sendeli Chemical Scholarship was established in 2007 with a 50,000-yuan donation from STU Science School alumni Chen Zhaohan and Qiu Liling, chair and general manager of Zhaoqing Sendeli Chemical Industrial Company. It has completed five selection cycles and distributed the original donation to 20 students. Chen and Qiu have donated an additional 100,000 yuan to continue supporting the scholarship.
