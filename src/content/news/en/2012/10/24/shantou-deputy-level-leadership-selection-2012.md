The STU Party Organization and United Front Department has forwarded a Shantou Municipal Party Organization Department notice inviting eligible interested applicants to join a public recommendation and selection process. The process covers five deputy-director-level leadership posts in municipal units: director of the Comprehensive Administrative Law Enforcement Bureau under the Transport Bureau, director of the Health Supervision Institute, director of the Urban Management Administrative Law Enforcement Bureau, minister of the People’s Armed Forces Department of a municipal institution and political commissar of a municipal institution’s People’s Armed Forces Department.

Applicants must register with the STU Party Organization and United Front Department in New Administration Center 315 before the end of the morning on October 25, 2012.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Process | Public recommendation and competitive selection |
| Posts | Five deputy-director-level leadership posts in municipal units |
| Positions | Directors of transport law enforcement, health supervision and urban-management law enforcement; minister and political commissar of a municipal institution People’s Armed Forces Department |
