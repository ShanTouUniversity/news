Employment materials printed by the Guangdong Department of Education, including the *Graduate Recommendation Form* and *College Graduate Registration Form*, have been distributed to Shantou University. Class monitors will collect the materials and organize completion by the 2013 on-campus undergraduate graduating cohort. Students should complete both forms carefully and follow the required process for comments, signatures and seals.

For the “department or school comments” section of the Graduate Recommendation Form, a graduate may choose a professional teacher in the relevant department, the professional school or a Zhicheng College Youth League counselor. The recommender must sign the form. Class monitors will collect the completed forms and take them to the professional school for its school seal or Party General Branch seal. Each school must submit the forms to the Student Employment Guidance Center in Administrative Center 106 for stamping by November 20.

The completed College Graduate Registration Form must be submitted to the school counselor by November 20 for safekeeping; it will be placed in the student’s academic record.

| Material | Completion and submission | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graduate Recommendation Form | Obtain department or school comments from a professional teacher, professional school or Zhicheng College Youth League counselor; obtain recommender’s signature, school seal and Employment Guidance Center stamp | By November 20 |
| College Graduate Registration Form | Complete the form and submit it to the school counselor for inclusion in the student record | By November 20 |

Students should treat the completion and submission of employment materials as important. The university-issued forms govern the required format.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
