It is reported that, in order to attract more outstanding students to the Physics Department of the Science School at Shantou University, encourage current physics students to study diligently and strive for excellence, and help them overcome difficulties in study and daily life, Hu Wenhao, a dedicated alumnus of the department, initiated and established the "STU Physics Department Alumni Scholarship," donating 50,000 yuan as a startup fund.

## Award Targets and Amounts

According to the announcement, the scholarship rewards full-time undergraduates of the Physics Department who excel academically and come from financially disadvantaged families. Twenty students will be selected annually, including eight places reserved for first-year students, with each recipient receiving 2,500 yuan.

## How to Apply

Applicants must complete the application form, print and sign it (double-sided printing required), and submit it to the Youth League Committee of the Science School (Room 354, Science Building) **before 4:00 p.m. on October 29**.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Awards per year | 20 (including 8 for first-year students) |
| Award amount | RMB 2,500 per person |
| Deadline | October 29, 2012, 4:00 p.m. |
| Submission | Youth League Committee of the Science School (Room 354, Science Building) |
