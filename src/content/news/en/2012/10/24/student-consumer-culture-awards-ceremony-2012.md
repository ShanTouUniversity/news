The third Student Consumer-Culture Research Report Awards ceremony and winning-work performance under the Student Community X Plan will take place on November 3, 2012. Five winning groups will perform their award-winning works, and all STU faculty members, staff and students are welcome.

Faculty members, staff and students may reserve tickets through the online reservation system at [activity.stu.edu.cn](http://activity.stu.edu.cn/index.aspx) from 3 p.m. on October 24 to 5 p.m. on October 28. Successful reservations can be collected at Reception Room 105 in the New Administration Building during office hours from October 31 to November 1. Tickets are required for entry, and seating is first come, first served.

To promote the consumer-culture research activity, previous winning works will be presented at the Golden Triangle from 11 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. on October 24. Project introductions and Q&A sessions will be held at campus locations from October 24 to 31.

The event is co-hosted by the Consumer Council of Hong Kong and STU Student Affairs. This edition attracted 102 teams; 48 groups submitted works, and 13 winning groups were selected. To protect student privacy, student names are not published here.
