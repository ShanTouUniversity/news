To improve graduates’ competitiveness in employment, Shantou University Law School plans to organize training for the 2013 national civil-service examination. The training will run from November 2 to 7, 2012, in the Moot Court. It will include four days on the Administrative Aptitude Test and two days on the Essay Test, with about 240 participants expected.

The training is primarily for Law School graduating students, with a limited number of places open to graduating students from other schools. Applications from other schools will be accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. Wang Renfa will teach the Administrative Aptitude Test, and Guo Wulin will teach the Essay Test.
