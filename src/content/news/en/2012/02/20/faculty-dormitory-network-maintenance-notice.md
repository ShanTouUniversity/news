It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University will replace network equipment at faculty dormitory buildings B2, B4, B6 and B8. During the maintenance, network service in the corresponding buildings will be temporarily interrupted.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Maintenance | Network equipment replacement at Faculty Dorms B2, B4, B6, B8 |
| B2/B4 outage | February 21, 2012, 14:30–17:30 |
| B6/B8 outage | February 22, 2012, 14:30–17:30 |
| Recovery | Service will be restored upon completion without further notice |
| Contact | 0754-82903413 ext. 100 |
| Issued by | Network and Information Center |

Residents are advised to plan their network usage accordingly ahead of the maintenance window.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
