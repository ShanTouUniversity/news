It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University has announced construction work to support the renovation of the university's new administrative building. From February 13 to 20, 2012, contractors will carry out road excavation, pipeline laying and backfilling on three road sections across campus.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Scope | Fiber pipeline installation (road excavation, pipeline laying, backfilling) |
| Road sections | Three sections (see construction drawings) |
| Duration | February 13–20, 2012 |
| Safety reminder | Pedestrians and vehicles should exercise caution near construction zones |
| Contact | 0754-82903550 ext. 8188 |
| Issued by | Network and Information Center |

The center urges the campus community to stay alert for warning signs and to proceed with caution when passing through the affected areas.
