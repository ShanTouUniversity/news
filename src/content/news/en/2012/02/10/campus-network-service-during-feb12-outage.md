It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University has issued a notice regarding campus network service adjustment on February 12, 2012, in coordination with the Resource Management Office's power outage plan.

According to the notice, campus network services will be suspended on the morning of February 12, while the center will carry out main equipment replacement in the power station area. The affected period for the power station area is from 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM on February 12, covering buildings A1-A7, A9, A10, B8-B12, Equipment Section, Auditorium, University Hospital, Youth League Committee, Arts Education Center, Project Coordination Department, and Buildings 789.

The center stated that if the actual power outage exceeds 4 hours, secondary services will be closed while prioritizing core networks and important systems; if the outage exceeds 7 hours, all services will be suspended. Services will be restored as soon as stable power supply is resumed.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|

The university urges affected users to make arrangements in advance and apologizes for any inconvenience caused.
