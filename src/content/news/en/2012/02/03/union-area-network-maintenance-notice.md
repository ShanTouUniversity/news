It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University has announced a network maintenance operation for the Union Area.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Maintenance | Backbone equipment upgrade for the Union Area equipment room |
| Downtime | February 4, 2012, 09:30–12:00 |
| Affected area | Foundation Office, Labor Union, Multidisciplinary Center, Infrastructure Office, Faculty Dormitory Building D, A8, A11–A14, B1–B7 and other faculty residences |
| Recovery | Service will be restored upon completion without further notice |
| Contact | Tel: 0754-82903413-100; Mobile: 13592895188 (campus short no. 665188); Email: stunic@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Network and Information Center |

Network service in the affected area will be interrupted during the upgrade. Users are advised to plan accordingly.
