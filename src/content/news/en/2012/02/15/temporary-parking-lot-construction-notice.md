It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University has announced that, with approval, a temporary parking lot covering approximately 2,600 square meters will be built on the vacant lot east of Teaching Building E. Shantou Longhua Construction General Company has been awarded the contract, with construction starting on February 15, 2012, and completion expected by the end of March.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Project | Temporary parking lot |
| Location | Vacant lot east of Teaching Building E |
| Area | Approximately 2,600 square meters |
| Contractor | Shantou Longhua Construction General Company |
| Start date | February 15, 2012 |
| Expected completion | End of March 2012 |
| Safety notice | Pedestrians are advised to slow down and proceed with caution; do not enter the construction site without authorization |

The office has urged pedestrians to exercise caution near the construction zone and to avoid entering the site without permission.
