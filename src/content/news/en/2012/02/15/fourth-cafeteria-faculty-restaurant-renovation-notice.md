It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University has announced plans to renovate the second floor of the Fourth Cafeteria into a faculty restaurant to improve the dining environment for staff members. Guangdong Jinhonghai Construction Engineering Co. has been awarded the contract, with construction commencing on February 15, 2012, and completion expected by the end of March.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Renovation | Second floor of Fourth Cafeteria converted to faculty restaurant |
| Contractor | Guangdong Jinhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Start date | February 15, 2012 |
| Expected completion | End of March 2012 |
| Construction impact | Noise, vibration, and dust |
| Safety notice | Visitors to the Fourth Cafeteria should exercise caution; do not enter the construction site without authorization |

The office has reminded the campus community to be aware of potential noise, vibration and dust during the construction period, and to stay clear of the work zone.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
