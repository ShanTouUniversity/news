It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University has issued a notice regarding a scheduled campus-wide power outage on Sunday, February 12, 2012, from 8:30 AM to 12:00 PM. The outage is required for preventive testing of the university's high-voltage power supply equipment.

Due to the power outage, the pressurized water pumps will be unable to operate, resulting in a temporary suspension of water supply across campus during the same period. The university urges faculty, staff and students to store sufficient water in advance.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Outage Time | Sunday, February 12, 2012, 8:30 AM – 12:00 PM |
| Affected Area | Campus-wide |
| Water Impact | Pressurized pumps inoperable; water supply suspended |
| Issuing Office | Resource Management Office |

The university apologizes for any inconvenience caused and appreciates the understanding of the university community.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
