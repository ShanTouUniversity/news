It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice regarding the 2012 acceptance review for Guangdong Province Graduate Education Reform Research Projects, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Degree Committee's working notice (Yue Xuewei Ban [2012] No. 7).

The acceptance scope covers surface projects and self-funded projects approved in 2010. Project host units are required to organize result appraisal and preliminary acceptance according to the "Interim Measures for the Implementation Management of Guangdong Province Graduate Education Reform Research Projects," and complete the project acceptance forms.

The Graduate School urged all units to carefully organize this work, strictly establish acceptance procedures, assign dedicated personnel to follow up on project implementation, and ensure both progress and quality. Written materials in duplicate should be submitted to the Graduate School by April 24, 2012, with electronic copies sent to xwb@stu.edu.cn.
