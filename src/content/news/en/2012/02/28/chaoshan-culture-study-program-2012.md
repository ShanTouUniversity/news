It is reported that Chizheng College at Shantou University has announced the launch of the 2012 Chaoshan Culture Study Program, open to all undergraduate students.

The Chaoshan culture has a long history, originating from the early Chaoshan settlers, taking shape during the Qin and Han dynasties, developing through the Tang and Song periods, flourishing in the Ming and Qing dynasties, and innovating in modern times. It is an important branch of Chinese excellent traditional culture and a significant subculture within Lingnan culture, with close ties to Min (Fujian) culture. Renowned sinologist Rao Zongyi once stated that the cultural phenomena of the Chaozhou (Chaoshan) region should be studied as an independent and in-depth research subject, comparable in importance to "Hakka Studies."

The program aims to help students appreciate the profound Chaoshan cultural heritage while developing skills in independent learning, information retrieval, comprehensive analysis, teamwork, communication, academic writing, and public speaking.

Learning activities include expert lectures and workshops, field investigations, and group discussions. Students will select their own research topics and complete projects through brainstorming, field surveys, literature review, internet research, and guidance from instructors. The program advisor is Professor Lin Kailong.
