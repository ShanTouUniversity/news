It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice regarding the 2012 acceptance review for Guangdong Province Graduate Demo Course Construction Projects, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Degree Committee's working notice (Yue Xuewei Ban [2012] No. 8).

The acceptance scope covers graduate demo course construction projects approved in 2009. Project host units are required to organize expert groups (no fewer than 5 members, with at least 3 from outside the university) to conduct preliminary acceptance according to the "Interim Measures for the Implementation Management of Guangdong Province Graduate Demo Course Construction Projects," and complete project acceptance reports.

The Graduate School urged all units to carefully organize this work, establish strict acceptance procedures, assign dedicated personnel to follow up on project implementation, and ensure both progress and quality. Written materials in duplicate should be submitted to the Graduate School by April 26, 2012, with electronic copies sent to xwb@stu.edu.cn.
