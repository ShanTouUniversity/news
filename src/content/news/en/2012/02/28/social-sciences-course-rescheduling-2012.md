It is reported that the Social Sciences Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice regarding course rescheduling due to faculty members Yang Mu and Luo Shidian's business trips.

The adjusted schedule is as follows: Undergraduate courses in "Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics" and graduate courses in "Dialectics of Nature" will be rescheduled to Saturday, March 3, with makeup classes held at the original venues.

| Course | Original Schedule | Makeup Time | Venue |
|--------|------------------|-------------|-------|
| Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought | Week 1, Wed/Thu | Week 1, Saturday (Mar 3) | Original classroom |
| Dialectics of Nature (Graduate) | Week 1 | Week 1, Saturday (Mar 3) | Original classroom |

Students are advised to note the changes and attend makeup classes on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Social Sciences Department, Academic Affairs Office, Graduate School)
