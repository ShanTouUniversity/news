It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently published the list of graduate "Three Assistants" (teaching assistant, research assistant, and administrative assistant) positions for the spring 2012 semester.

Following submissions from various position-holding units and review by the Graduate School, the employment list has been made public. The university requires all host units to properly manage the employment, supervision, and evaluation of graduate assistants in accordance with relevant regulations.

|------|---------|
| Semester | Spring 2012 |
| Position Types | Teaching Assistant, Research Assistant, Administrative Assistant |
