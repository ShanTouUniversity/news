It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice appointing the 15th Teaching Supervision Team to ensure smooth teaching operations and strengthen teaching inspection, supervision, and evaluation.

According to the "Shantou University Teaching Supervision Group Regulations," the term of office for this supervision team is two years. The appointed supervisors are:

| No. | Name |
|-----|------|
| 1 | Lan Huizhu |
| 2 | Zhu Liqun |
| 3 | Zhu Yongkai |
| 4 | Lin Xiuqing |
| 5 | Guo Xikun |
| 6 | Chu Zhiren |

The university encourages all units to actively support and cooperate with the teaching supervisors to jointly improve undergraduate teaching quality.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Term | Two years |
| Document No. | STU Fa [2012] No. 17 |
| Issuing Office | Shantou University |
