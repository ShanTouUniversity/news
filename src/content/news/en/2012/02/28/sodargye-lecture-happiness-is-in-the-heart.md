It is reported that Chizheng College at Shantou University will host the 11th session of its lecture series on Friday, March 2, 2012, featuring renowned scholar Khen Sodargye, who will deliver a talk titled "The Root of Happiness is in the Mind."

Khen Sodargye, originally from Luhuo in Garzê, Sichuan, is Honorary Chairman of the Shanghai Cihui Public Welfare Foundation and a distinguished charity theorist, activist, and translator. Known for his erudition and eloquent impromptu lectures rich in classical references, he has deep knowledge of Buddhist teachings as well as expertise spanning diverse fields from Confucianism and Daoism to Chinese emperors, from quantum mechanics to vacuum theory. He has been invited to speak at Peking University, Tsinghua University, Renmin University, Fudan University, Nanjing University and other prestigious institutions, engaging intellectuals in discussions on the true meaning of a happy life.
