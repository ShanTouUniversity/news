It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing a meeting for women committee members on February 29, 2012.

The meeting will be held at 3:30 PM in the Union Conference Room, bringing together the university labor union women's committee and department-level women's committee members. The main agenda is to discuss arrangements for International Women's Day celebrations.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Meeting Time | Wednesday, February 29, 2012, 15:30 |
| Venue | Union Conference Room |
| Attendees | University and department-level women's committee members |
| Agenda | International Women's Day event arrangements |
| Organizer | University Labor Union |
