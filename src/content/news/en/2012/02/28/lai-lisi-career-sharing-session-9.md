It is reported that Chizheng College and the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University are jointly hosting the 9th Career Planning Sharing Session, inviting Lai Lisi, a 2011 graduate and journalist at Yangcheng Evening News, to share her university life and career experiences.

Lai Lisi graduated from the Chinese Language and Literature program in 2011 and was a founding resident of Chizheng College. She received the STU Medal in 2011 and was sponsored by the Lee Shau Kee Foundation to participate in the Shangri-La Advanced Program in summer 2010. The session theme is "My University and My Journalism Journey."

| Session Theme | My University and My Journalism Journey |
| Speaker | Lai Lisi (Journalist, Yangcheng Evening News; STU Class of 2011) |
