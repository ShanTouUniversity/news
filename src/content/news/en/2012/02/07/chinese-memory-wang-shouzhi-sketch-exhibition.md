It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University is hosting the *China Memory - Drawing & Sketch of Wang Shouzhi* exhibition, open to all faculty, staff and students.

The exhibition, themed "China Memory," features a collection of sketch and drawing works by Professor Wang Shouzhi, documenting China's modern historical transformations and social changes through artistic expression.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Exhibition | China Memory - Drawing & Sketch of Wang Shouzhi |
| Dates | February 7 – March 7, 2012 |
| Venue | Art Museum, Cheung Kong School of Art & Design |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

The exhibition features multiple works. All members of the university community are welcome to visit.
