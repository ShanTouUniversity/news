It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University is hosting the *American Movie Poster Exhibition*, open to all faculty, staff and students.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Exhibition | American Movie Poster Exhibition |
| Dates | February 7 – March 7, 2012 |
| Venue | 1st Floor Exhibition Hall, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

The exhibition features a collection of American movie posters. All members of the university community are welcome to visit.
