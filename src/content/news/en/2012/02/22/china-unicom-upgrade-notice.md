It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University has announced that, per notification from China Unicom Shantou, an upgrade of the carrier's core network equipment will take place between midnight and 6:00 a.m. on February 23, 2012. During this period, campus users may experience an interruption of approximately 30 minutes when accessing China Unicom-affiliated websites and international sites.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Cause | Core network equipment upgrade by China Unicom Shantou |
| Impact time | February 23, 2012, 00:00–06:00 |
| Scope | All campus users |
| Impact | Approx. 30 minutes of disruption accessing China Unicom and international websites |
| Contact | Tel: 0754-82903413; Mobile: 13592895188; Email: stunic@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Network and Information Center |

Users are advised to plan accordingly and minimize late-night internet usage during the upgrade window.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
