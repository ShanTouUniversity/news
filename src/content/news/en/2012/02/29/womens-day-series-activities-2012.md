It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice to commemorate the 102nd International Women's Day and inspire female faculty and staff. The celebration series features the theme "Embrace Nature, Embrace Health."

**Event 1: Ecological Park and Seaside Greenway Outing**
Date: March 7, 2012 (March 8 holiday adjusted to March 7)
Participants: All female staff and faculty (relatives and children not permitted)
Organization: Optimistic Travel Service
Registration fee: 20 RMB per person (non-refundable after registration)
How to register: Sign up through your department's women's committee representative; departments must submit lists to the union by close of business, March 2.

**Event 2: "Spring Charm" Essay and Photography Competition**
Theme: The March 7 spring outing, focusing on the sea and greenway exploration.
