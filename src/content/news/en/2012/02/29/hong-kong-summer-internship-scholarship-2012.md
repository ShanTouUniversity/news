It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently announced the launch of the 2012 Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship Program, open to students from the 2009 cohort majoring in Law and Public Administration.

The application period runs from February 29 to March 7, 2012. Applicants must complete the application form and send it to cdclaw@stu.edu.cn with the subject line "Department+Name+Hong Kong Summer Application."

The Law School will form a selection committee to evaluate candidates based on the following criteria:
- Preliminary screening (50%): Academic performance 25%, English writing 15%, social service experience 5%, student activities 5%
- Interview (50%): Professional knowledge 20%, English oral skills 15%, communication skills 10%, confidence and demeanor 5%

Interviews for candidates who pass the preliminary screening will be held in mid-March, with final selections made from both departments.
