It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University recently announced a limited-time iPhone 4S discount program offered by Shantou Unicom exclusively for the university community.

Discount details: The iPhone 4S phone package, originally priced at 5,880 RMB, is now available for 5,280 RMB — a direct reduction of 600 RMB.

Promotion period: Before March 3, 2012. Shantou Unicom will be on campus from March 1 to March 3 for on-site consultations and processing.

Processing hours: 11:00 AM–12:30 PM and 4:00–5:00 PM daily.
Location: Lobby, 1st Floor, University Labor Union.
Requirements: Copy of ID card (front and back), 1,000 RMB deposit, balance paid upon device delivery the next day.
Inquiry: Mr. Zheng, 18675668688.
