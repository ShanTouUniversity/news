It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice regarding the 2011 annual undergraduate program approval results published by the Ministry of Education (Jiao Gao [2012] No. 2).

The newly approved undergraduate program is: Public Art (Program Code: 050430S).

The new program approved by the Ministry of Education will begin enrolling students in 2012. The program name, code, duration of study, and degree conferring category will be as officially announced. The university has asked the relevant college to make full use of existing resources to strengthen the new program and ensure educational quality.
