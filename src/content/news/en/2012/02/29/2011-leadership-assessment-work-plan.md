It is reported that the CPC Shantou University Committee recently issued a notice publishing the "Shantou University Leadership Team and Leading Cadres 2011 Annual Assessment Work Plan."

The plan was approved at the 12th meeting of the Party Committee Standing Committee held in 2011 and is hereby distributed to all university units for implementation.
