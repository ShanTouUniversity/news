It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently published the admission list for the 9th batch of interns recruited by the Shantou Municipal Financial Affairs Bureau.

Previously, the Business School had announced the internship recruitment notice, which received enthusiastic responses from students across the college. Following discussions with leaders of the Shantou Municipal Financial Affairs Bureau, four students have been selected for this internship program.

The Business School thanked all students for their active participation and encouraged those not selected to continue paying attention to upcoming opportunities.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Recruiting Agency | Shantou Municipal Financial Affairs Bureau |
| Positions Filled | 4 |
| Admitted Students | Liao Lei, Cao Xiaofeng, Chen Zewei, Pan Guiquan |
| Issuing Office | Business School |
