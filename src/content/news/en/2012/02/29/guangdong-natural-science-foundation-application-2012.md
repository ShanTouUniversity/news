It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently announced the opening of 2012 Guangdong Provincial Natural Science Foundation project applications.

According to the notice from the Guangdong Provincial Natural Science Foundation Management Committee, four categories of projects are available for 2012: research teams, key projects, free-applicant projects, and doctoral research startup projects. Specific application requirements and conditions can be found on the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation website (http://gdsf.gdstc.gov.cn/Index.aspx).

This year, quota-based submission is implemented. Based on the provincial foundation committee's notice and each unit's historical approval numbers, the 2012 quotas for free-applicant and key projects have been allocated to each unit. Research team projects are temporarily not accepted from mathematics, physics, and management disciplines; key projects are temporarily not accepted from mathematics and physics disciplines. Units are requested to strictly follow their allocated quotas when organizing applications.
