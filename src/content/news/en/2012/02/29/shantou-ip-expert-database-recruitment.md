It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the Shantou Intellectual Property Office is replenishing and adjusting its expert database, recruiting experts in intellectual property, technology, and economic management.

The Shantou Intellectual Property Expert Database, established in 2005, assists the city with patent award reviews, patent technology appraisals, patent strategy research, and handling cross-border intellectual property disputes.

Qualified faculty members should complete the application form in triplicate and submit it, along with the following documents, to the Research Office by March 23, 2012:
1. Copy of ID card
2. Copy of professional title and appointment certificates
3. Copies of awards, academic achievements, publications, and invention patents

Electronic copies of the application should be sent to dengyong@stu.edu.cn. Contact: Luo Yingguang or Deng Yong, Tel: 82903271.
