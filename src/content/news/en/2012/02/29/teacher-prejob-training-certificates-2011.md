It is reported that the 2011 pre-job training qualification certificates for university teachers have arrived at Shantou University. On February 29, the Human Resources Office asked relevant units across the university to complete the collection procedures promptly.

Under the arrangements, each relevant unit should send a representative to collect its staff members' certificates from the Human Resources Office (Room 406, Administration East Building) before March 2. The full list of certificate recipients is available in the attachment published by the Office.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Matter | Collection of 2011 pre-job training qualification certificates |
| Who collects | Designated representatives of relevant units |
| Deadline | Before March 2 |
| Location | Human Resources Office (Administration East, Room 406) |

In addition, training fees for this round of pre-job training will be reimbursed centrally by the Human Resources Office. The reimbursement will be deposited into the teachers' individual Bank of China accounts in March, and teachers are advised to check their accounts accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
