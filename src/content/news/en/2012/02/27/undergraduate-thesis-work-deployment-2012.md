It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice deploying the 2011-2012 academic year spring semester undergraduate thesis work.

The notice requires all colleges and departments to complete all thesis work, including proposal reports, thesis guidance, writing standards, defense and grading, before the end of the 14th week of the current semester. The university requires all units to strictly follow the "Shantou University Undergraduate Thesis Work Regulations (Revised)" and "Shantou University Undergraduate Thesis Writing Standards" to carefully organize and reasonably arrange all related work, strictly controlling thesis quality.

The Academic Affairs Office emphasized that during the thesis work period, all colleges should strictly require students to complete their assigned tasks on time and independently, prohibit plagiarism or copying others' work, and ensure that papers are written, printed and bound according to uniformly prescribed formats and requirements. Students must also attend defenses on time. Instructors are required to strengthen guidance, inspection and supervision of students.
