On February 27, 2012, the Chai Kong College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University announced that Mike Onofrio, a former CNN foreign correspondent, will visit campus on February 28 to present a documentary screening and dialogue session featuring "China Decoded."

The event will be held from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM on February 28 (Tuesday) in the Science Report Hall of the New Library, presented in original English audio with bilingual subtitles.

Onofrio served as a CNN foreign correspondent for 24 years, holding positions including CNN London correspondent, Beijing bureau chief, and Hong Kong bureau chief. From 2001 to 2006, he worked as CNN's senior Asia correspondent, covering the entire Asia-Pacific region. His journalism career began in 1970s Hong Kong, where he worked for CBS and NBC before joining CNN.

Onofrio is widely regarded as one of the most knowledgeable American journalists about North Korea, having visited the country 14 times since 1989. In 1994, he was the only American journalist to accompany former U.S. President Jimmy Carter on his historic visit to North Korea. His career has been decorated with numerous awards, including an Emmy Award, the George Foster Peabody Award, and the Alfred I. DuPont Award from Columbia University.

The Chai Kong College of Journalism and Communication stated that the event aims to broaden students' international perspectives and deepen their understanding of how China is portrayed in international media.
