It is reported that, due to venue reasons, table tennis classes at Shantou University will move from the gymnasium to the auditorium for this semester. The Physical Education Department has alerted all faculty and students through campus channels.

Those enrolled in or teaching related courses should go to the new location for class to avoid heading to the wrong venue. Other teaching arrangements remain unchanged.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
