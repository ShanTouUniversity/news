It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a personnel appointment notice.

Following discussion and approval by the Resource Management Office and consent from university leadership, Li Jinxian has been appointed as Director of the Student Community Management Service Center. The appointment was issued as STU Document No. 2 [2012].

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Position | Director, Student Community Management Service Center |
| Appointee | Li Jinxian |
| Document No. | STU Zi [2012] No. 2 |
| Issuing Office | Resource Management Office |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
