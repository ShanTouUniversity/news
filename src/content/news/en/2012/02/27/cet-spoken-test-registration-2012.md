It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently notified the College of Liberal Arts regarding the registration arrangements for the 2012 English Majors Spoken Test, as issued by the English Professional Speaking Test Office of the National Foreign Language Teaching Administration Committee.

According to the notice, the 2012 English Majors Spoken Test will be held on the morning of May 12, 2012, at 8:30 AM. Eligible candidates are limited to English majors enrolled in 2010. The registration fee is 80 RMB per person. Registration is conducted online through the credit system portal, with the registration period running from February 28 to March 7, 2012. The Finance Office will automatically deduct the fee from applicants' campus card accounts on March 8. Registration will be considered failed if the deduction is unsuccessful.
