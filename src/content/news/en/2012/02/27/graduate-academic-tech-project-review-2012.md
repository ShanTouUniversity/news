It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing final reviews for the 2011 graduate extracurricular academic technology projects.

According to the school's notice on graduate academic technology projects and the project development schedule, all project leaders who received approval must submit written completion reports (including advisor evaluations and signatures) and project outcomes to Teacher Zheng Fengchao in Room 204, Building G, Graduate School, by March 8, 2012. Electronic copies should be sent to o_yjsxsb@stu.edu.cn. Project outcomes include published research papers, business plans, patents, established models, investigation reports, and proposals.

The Graduate School reminded that projects failing to submit completion reports on time will be considered自动放弃, and additional project funding will be recovered.
