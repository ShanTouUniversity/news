On February 27, 2012, the Arts Education Center at the Chai Kong College of Art and Design announced auditions for the "Dance Arts Performance" course offered this semester.

Students interested in enrolling in the course, taught by Instructor Yu Duanrong (class code: 52458), are required to attend an audition on February 28 (Tuesday) from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM in Dance Rehearsal Room 102 of the Arts Education Center building.

Notably, the center reported a shortage of male dancers in the dance troupe and specifically encouraged male students to attend the audition and enroll in the course.

"Dance Arts Performance" is a practical art course that combines dance technique with stage expression, aiming to develop students' awareness and ability in bodily movement language. The Arts Education Center has previously offered multiple arts elective courses that have been popular among students.
