On February 27, 2012, the Graduate School of Shantou University issued a notice requiring units planning to offer graduate course programs in 2012 to complete the registration and filing process.

According to Document No. 6 (2012) issued by the Guangdong Provincial Academic Degrees Committee Office, all units must submit the following materials to the Graduate School by March 26, 2012: three copies of the "Brief Description Form for Graduate Course Programs," two copies of the proposed teaching plan, one copy of the "Summary Form for Applications," and one copy of the program data disk. All materials must be printed on A4 paper; late submissions will not be accepted.

After the Graduate School reviews and adds the university's comments, materials will be submitted collectively to the Guangdong Provincial Academic Degrees Office for approval.
