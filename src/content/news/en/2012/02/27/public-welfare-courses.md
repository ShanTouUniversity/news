On February 27, 2012, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced the launch of public welfare courses for the first time, inviting qualified instructors from across the country and graduate students within the university to serve as course instructors.

To flexibly schedule course times and expand access to quality educational resources, Shantou University is offering seven public welfare courses this spring semester. Teachers who have completed the required training and eligible graduate students may select teaching or assistant teaching positions based on course outlines and demand lists. Graduate students who have passed the training can serve as teaching assistants.

Participants are required to submit an Excel form containing their information to Instructor Liu Xiangling at the Academic Affairs Office (lxl0726@stu.edu.cn) by 5:30 PM on March 1, 2012.

Public welfare courses represent an important step toward opening shared educational resources at Shantou University, aiming to make high-quality course content accessible to a broader audience. Instructors participating in these courses will receive teaching hour recognition and compensation.
