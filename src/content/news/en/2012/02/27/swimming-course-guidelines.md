On February 27, 2012, the Physical Education Department of Shantou University released guidelines for the "Swimming and Lifeguard" and "Swimming" courses, reminding interested students of the requirements.

According to the department, the "Swimming and Lifeguard" course runs from Week 1 to Week 16. Students must be able to swim more than 200 meters continuously; those unable to meet this requirement should select alternative physical education courses. The first class will be a theory session held at the open area between the gymnasium, swimming pool, and reservoir; course materials have been uploaded to the virtual classroom platform.

The "Swimming" course is scheduled for the second half of the semester, spanning 8 weeks with two sessions per week (with additional sessions on some Sundays), totaling 16 classes. The first theory class begins on the weekend of Week 8 (specific location pending). The course selection system only shows Friday periods 34, 67, and 89; specific class schedules will be assigned by instructors after the course begins.

The PE Department stated that swimming courses aim to improve students' physical fitness and teach lifesaving skills, forming an important part of Shantou University's physical education curriculum.
