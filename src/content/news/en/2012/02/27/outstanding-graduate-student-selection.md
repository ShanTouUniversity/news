On February 27, 2012, the Graduate School of Shantou University announced that a pre-nomination meeting for the 2011 Outstanding Graduate Student selection and the preliminary nomination for the Guangdong Province Outstanding Graduate Student award will be held on February 29 at 3:00 PM in Room 205 of the Science Building.

The meeting aims to initiate the 2011 Outstanding Graduate Student evaluation process and conduct preliminary nominations for the provincial-level award. Responsible officers from each college's graduate affairs, or their designated representatives, are expected to attend on time. The Graduate School recommended that attending representatives have prior experience with the university's outstanding graduate student selection process to ensure fairness and standardization.

The STU Outstanding Graduate Student award is one of the university's highest honors for graduate students, recognizing outstanding academic achievement, research innovation, and comprehensive quality. The Guangdong Province Outstanding Graduate Student title, assessed by the Guangdong Provincial Academic Degrees Committee, represents a prestigious provincial-level honor.
