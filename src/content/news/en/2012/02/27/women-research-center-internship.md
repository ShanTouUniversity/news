On February 27, 2012, the Women's Research Center at Shantou University's College of Liberal Arts issued a recruitment notice for internship positions, seeking one research assistant and one web technology assistant.

According to the center, the research assistant position is open to undergraduate students from the classes of 2008 to 2010 as well as graduate students. Applicants should demonstrate strong responsibility and initiative, be proficient in statistical software such as Excel and SPSS, and be able to work independently. Key duties include assisting with literature searches and organization, ensuring at least 8 hours of on-site work per week at the center office, and completing other tasks assigned by the center.

The web technology assistant position requires applicants to possess basic knowledge of website management and maintenance, familiarity with web design and image editing, and the ability to work independently. Responsibilities include daily backend maintenance and updates of the center's website, assisting with photo and text processing for event coverage, and completing other assigned tasks.

Interested applicants should submit a completed electronic application form to the center's office email (cws@stu.edu.cn) by 5:00 PM on March 5, 2012.

The Women's Research Center is an academic research institution affiliated with the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University, dedicated to scholarly research and social service in the field of women's and gender studies.
