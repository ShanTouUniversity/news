The Guangdong Provincial Communist Youth League will continue its program of selecting university youth league cadres for practical training at county-level Communist Youth League committees in 2012. The initiative aims to deepen university-local cooperation, strengthen grassroots youth league organization, and enrich the work experience of youth league cadres.

According to the notice forwarded by the STU Youth League Committee, in 2011, a total of 158 university youth league cadres from Guangdong were selected for county-level postings under the unified deployment of the Central Communist Youth League. Over the past year, the posted cadres have served the people wholeheartedly and rooted themselves in grassroots communities, building university-local cooperation platforms to promote grassroots youth league organization and innovating work methods to enliven grassroots youth league activities, earning high praise from local youth league organizations and young people.

Youth league cadres and young faculty members interested in participating in this posting program should complete the "Selected Cadre Registration Form" and submit it to the University Youth League Committee Office by February 28.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee, STU)
> ⚠️ The registration form is available on the OA system.
