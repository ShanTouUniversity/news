According to the requirements outlined in the official documents for selecting and recommending candidates for the 2012 Guangdong Provincial Model Worker and Advanced Collective titles, the STU Labor Union Committee has conducted deliberations and proposed Prof. Lin Furong of the College of Sciences as a candidate for the Guangdong Provincial Model Worker title.

The public notice indicates that Prof. Lin Furong meets the basic qualifications for the provincial model worker selection. In accordance with the selection criteria, candidates must first hold the title of municipal-level model worker. The university labor union stated that any faculty, staff, or students with objections to the proposed candidate may submit feedback via email to the union office by 11:00 AM on February 25 during the public comment period.
