On February 24, 2012, the Resource Management Office of Shantou University issued a notice announcing two personnel appointments.

Following discussions within the office and approval by university leadership, Comrade Ding Rong has been appointed as the director of teaching affairs at the Shantou University Affiliated Primary School, responsible for overseeing primary school academic operations. Comrade Lu Yandan has been appointed as head nurse at the University Campus Hospital, leading the nursing team.

The appointments reflect the university's commitment to strengthening its foundational education and healthcare services. The Affiliated Primary School serves the children of faculty and staff as well as students from the surrounding community, while the Campus Hospital provides essential medical services to the university community.

The appointments take effect immediately.
