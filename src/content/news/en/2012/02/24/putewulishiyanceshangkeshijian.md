The Academic Affairs Office at STU has issued a notice arranging the schedule and venue for this semester's "General Physics Experiment" course. According to the notice, the first week of the course will be dedicated to theoretical instruction on error analysis, with laboratory sessions beginning in the second week.

The specific arrangements for the error theory lectures are as follows: students enrolled in the Monday/Tuesday lab section will attend the lecture on March 1 (Thursday) from 14:00 to 17:00 in Lecture Hall E-101; students enrolled in the Wednesday/Thursday/Friday lab section will attend on March 2 (Friday) from 14:00 to 17:00 in the same venue.

The notice reminds all relevant students to attend on time to ensure the smooth progression of the experimental course.
