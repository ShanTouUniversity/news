On February 24, 2012, the Graduate School of Shantou University announced that the results of the December 2011 College English Test (CET-4 and CET-6) have been published in the graduate student management system, and eligible students can log in to check their scores.

According to the Graduate School, students need to access the system through unified authentication. By visiting the Graduate School website homepage, clicking the "Graduate Student Management System" link, selecting "Unified Authentication," and entering their credentials, students can navigate to the "Exam Registration" section and then to "Score Inquiry" to view their results.

The College English Test is a national English proficiency examination administered by China's Ministry of Education, covering listening, reading, translation, and writing. Results are commonly used for degree conferral and employment purposes.

The December 2011 exam was held across multiple universities in Shantou. Students who have not yet accessed their results are advised to check the system regularly or contact the Graduate School academic affairs office for assistance.
