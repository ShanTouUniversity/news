To support environmental improvement around the university's main gate and to coordinate with the new gate construction project, STU is funding the construction of 15 temporary简易铺面 at the east gate to relocate existing street vendors, along with a temporary fence to close off a mountain access road. The project was tendered on August 15, 2011, and was awarded to Shantou Shenghang Construction Company with a bid price of 250,000 yuan.

However, due to the project's location at the boundary between the campus and the surrounding rural community — involving the demolition of temporary structures and closure of a path used by local farmers — the winning contractor encountered significant resistance and was unable to begin work. Following multiple rounds of coordination by district and subdistrict government leaders, the project is expected to commence shortly.

According to the notice from the Resources Management Office, the local government has requested additional works including the installation of water supply, electricity, and sewage facilities, as well as a small restroom for each stall. The additional budget is estimated at approximately 70,000 yuan. This public notice invites feedback from faculty, students, and the broader community.
