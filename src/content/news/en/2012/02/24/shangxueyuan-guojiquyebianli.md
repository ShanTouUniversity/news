STU's Business School will select students to represent the university in the 2012 International Business Case Competition, with selection activities scheduled for late February.

According to the notice, eligible applicants must be undergraduate students in their second, third, or fourth year (classes of 2008, 2009, and 2010) at the Business School, having completed coursework in economics and business administration. Applicants must demonstrate strong English listening, speaking, and reading skills, maintain a GPA of at least 3.0, and have an average score of 80 or above in all completed ELC courses (Level 1–5).

Registration is open from February 24 to 27. Students may sign up via the "International Case Competition Registration" quick link on the Business School website or through the designated portal. Shortlisted candidates will be notified for interviews, and the final team roster will be published on the Business School website. For inquiries, please email kingkng@stu.edu.cn.
