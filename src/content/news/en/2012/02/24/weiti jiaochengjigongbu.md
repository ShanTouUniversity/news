The Academic Affairs Office at STU has issued a notice publishing the list of courses whose final exam grades for the Fall 2011-2012 semester had not been submitted as of February 24, reminding the relevant instructors to complete their grade submissions as soon as possible.

A total of six courses remain outstanding, all offered by the College of Liberal Arts. The specific list is as follows:

| Course Code | Course Name |
|------------|-------------|
| 47194 [CHI2350] | Narrative Studies |
| 47195 [CHI2641] | Research on Chinese Literature Overseas |
| 48222 [CHI2381] | Death and Immortality: Explorations in Eastern and Western Philosophical and Religious Traditions |
| 48224 [CHI5061] | Psychological Counseling, Spiritual Guidance, and Personal Growth |
| 48262 [CHI2213] | Studies on Laozi |
| 47031 [AED1124] | Appreciation of Western Classical Music |

The notice requests that instructors of the above courses complete their grade entry and submission promptly to ensure the smooth progress of teaching management operations.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
