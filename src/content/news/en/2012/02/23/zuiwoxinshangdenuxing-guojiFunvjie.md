To celebrate International Women's Day 2012, the Women's Research Center and the Department of International Studies at STU jointly organized a creative exhibition titled "My Admired Woman," open to all students on campus.

According to the event notice, the exhibition welcomed submissions from STU students, with participants around 20 years of age and of any gender. The format of works was unrestricted — the organizers encouraged students to express their admiration and respect for women through diverse creative forms, including visual artworks, comedic and musical performances, poetry and painting displays, micro-films (under 5 minutes), photo collections, or personal item collections.

The organizers stated that they hoped to showcase contemporary college students' multifaceted understanding of women's roles through varied creative expressions, promoting positive values of respect and appreciation for women. The notice invited all faculty and students to actively participate, using creativity and talent to honor outstanding women around them.
