It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will host the 22nd session of the Career启航名家讲坛 on February 29, 2012, with the theme of optimal preparation strategies for the Chinese civil service examination. The lecture will be delivered by Professor Wang Renfa, one of China's Top 10 Civil Service Exam Instructors and a professor at the School of Law, Shantou University.

Professor Wang Renfa is a standing director of the Chinese Legal Logic Professional Committee, the third nationally recognized Top 10 Civil Service Exam Training Expert, and director of the Shantou Branch of the Southern Civil Service Exam Research Center. He has多年从事行政职业能力测验辅导 and is the author of the best-selling books "Administrative Professional Ability Test" and "Past Papers and Mock Tests for Administrative Professional Ability Test."

The lecture covers six key areas: establishing correct concepts, systematic preparation for aptitude tests, comprehensive strategies for essay writing, full-spectrum interview preparation, textbook selection guidance, and training program evaluation.
