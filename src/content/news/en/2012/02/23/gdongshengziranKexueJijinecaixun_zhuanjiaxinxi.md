To prepare for the 2012 review of Guangdong Provincial Natural Science Foundation projects, STU's Research Office has launched a supplementary registration of review experts, calling on eligible faculty members across campus to apply.

According to the notice, applicants must meet the following qualifications: currently working and employed full-time by a legal entity in Guangdong Province; any projects previously funded by provincial-level or above natural science foundations have been implemented according to plan or passed inspection and acceptance; hold a associate professorship or higher title; be no older than 60 years of age (academicians excepted).

Eligible faculty members are required to complete the "Guangdong Provincial Natural Science Foundation Project Review Expert Information Form" and submit it to the designated email address by March 12. After review and approval by the Guangdong Provincial Natural Science Foundation Committee, the Research Office will return the "Review Expert Registration Form" for the faculty member to print one copy (double-sided), sign and seal, and return to the Research Office by March 21.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
> ⚠️ The registration form and attachments are available on the OA system.
