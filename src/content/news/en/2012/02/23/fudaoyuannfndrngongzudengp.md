To thoroughly implement the central government's directives on strengthening and improving college students' ideological and political education, enhance the counselor workforce in higher education institutions, and recognize outstanding counselors, the "2011 National University Counselor of the Year" selection is now underway. The event is guided by the Department of Ideological and Political Work of the Ministry of Education, co-hosted by the National University Counselor Work Research Association and China Education Daily, with Xinhua Net and China College Students Online providing online support.

According to the notice from STU's Student Affairs Office, the application period has opened and will close on March 4, 2012. Colleges and schools across campus are encouraged to actively recommend qualified counselors to participate, showcasing the excellent professional quality and dedication of STU's counselor team.

The National University Counselor of the Year selection has grown into a prominent national brand event since its inception, aimed at identifying and publicizing exemplary counselors who have made outstanding contributions on the front lines of students' ideological and political education.
