The Guangdong Provincial Education Working Committee will hold the "Three Loves and One Dedication" (San Ai Yi Feng Xian) Outstanding Party-building Activities Video Conference on the afternoon of February 27, 2012, from 15:00 to 16:00. The conference aims to summarize the activities carried out across the provincial education system in 2011 and deploy the work for 2012.

STU will host a branch venue in Room 205 of the Science Building, organizing relevant personnel to attend the meeting collectively. According to the notice from the university's Organization and United Front Work Department, attendees include members of the university Party committee Standing Committee, deputy secretaries of each undergraduate campus Party general branch, secretaries of directly affiliated Party branches, as well as representatives from the University Administrative Office, the Commission for Discipline Inspection, the Organization and United Front Work Department, the Publicity Department, and the Medical College's Organization and Publicity United Front Work Department.

The "Three Loves and One Dedication" theme education and practical activities are an important component of the outstanding Party-building campaign within Guangdong's education system. Since its launch, the campaign has seen widespread mobilization and active participation across the provincial education system, achieving significant results. In 2011, the province's education system advanced the campaign under the "Three Loves and One Dedication" theme, producing a number of exemplary models and advanced individuals. In 2012, the activities will continue to deepen, further consolidating and expanding existing achievements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
