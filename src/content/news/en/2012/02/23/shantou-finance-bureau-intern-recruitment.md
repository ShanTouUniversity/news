It is reported that the Financial Affairs Bureau of Shantou Municipal People's Government is recruiting four interns from the Business School of Shantou University for a three-month position starting March 1, 2012. The target candidates are graduate students of the Business School and undergraduate students from the 2008 and 2009 cohorts, with requirements including strong writing skills and a solid understanding of current economic developments.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Recruiting agency | Financial Affairs Bureau, Shantou Municipal People's Government |
| Positions | 4 interns |
| Eligibility | Business School graduate students; Business School undergraduates (2008/2009 cohorts) |
| Requirements | Strong writing skills; basic understanding of current economic trends |
| Term | 3 months (starting March 1, 2012) |
| Application deadline | February 28, 2012, 17:00 |
| How to apply | Submit registration form and internship proposal to Mr./Ms. Chen at chenyl@stu.edu.cn, Tel: 82903496 |
| Interview time | February 29, 2012 (Wednesday), 15:00 |
| Interview venue | Business School Seminar Room (Wenxi, 2nd Floor) |

Eligible students are encouraged to apply before the deadline.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School)
