It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has announced that the results of the December 2011 National College English Test (CET-4/CET-6) have been published in the credit system. Eligible students may log in to the system to check their individual scores.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Exam | December 2011 National College English Test (CET-4/CET-6) |
| How to check | Credit system portal |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

The office has reminded affected students to log in promptly to view their results.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
