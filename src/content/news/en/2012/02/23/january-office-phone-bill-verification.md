It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University has announced that the January 2012 office telephone bills for all units have been uploaded to the Telephone Management System. Designated personnel from each unit are requested to log in to the system (http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) to review and confirm the bills. For bills with no discrepancies, users should click to confirm. Any discrepancies should be reported via email to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn by February 28, 2012. Bills that receive neither objection nor confirmation by the deadline will be deemed confirmed by default.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Billing period | January 2012 |
| Platform | Telephone Management System (http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) |
| No objection | Log in and click "Confirm" |
| Disputes | Email o_dhs@stu.edu.cn by Feb. 28 |
| Default rule | Unconfirmed and unobjected bills are deemed confirmed |
| Issued by | Resource Management Office |

The office reminds all units to complete verification promptly; overdue bills will be automatically confirmed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
