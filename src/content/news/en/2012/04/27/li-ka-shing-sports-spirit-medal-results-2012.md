It is reported that Shantou University recently issued document 汕大发〔2012〕52号 announcing the results of the 2012 Li Ka Shing Foundation STU Sports Spirit Medal selection.

To implement Mr. Li Ka Shing's educational philosophy of "having ambition, having insight, having perseverance, and having achievement," and to commend outstanding graduates who possess noble sportsmanship and moral character and have made exceptional contributions to STU's athletic programs while achieving comprehensive development, the Li Ka Shing Foundation established the STU Sports Spirit Medal.

2012 Sports Spirit Medal winner: Wu Ming Zhu (Business School, Grade 2008, International Economics and Trade), who also receives a prize of 8,000 yuan from the Li Ka Shing Foundation.

2012 Sports Spirit Medal nominees: Liu Jian Heng (Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication [Zhicheng College], Grade 2008, Broadcasting and Television Journalism) and Tan Guang Quan (Science College, Grade 2008, Applied Physics), each receiving a prize of 1,000 yuan from the Li Ka Shing Foundation.
