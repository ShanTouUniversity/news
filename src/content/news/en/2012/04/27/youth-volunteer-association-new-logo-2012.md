It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the STU Youth Volunteer Association (hereinafter referred to as "the Association"), since its founding in 1994, has consistently upheld the goal of "everyone is a volunteer" and the motto of "dedication, friendliness, mutual assistance, and progress." Using the "service learning" model, the Association actively encourages and organizes volunteer services for the community. Over 18 years, the Association has continuously grown, improving and developing its organizational structure, service system, and operational mechanisms.

The Gold Award-winning entry "Gathering" (《汇聚》) from the logo design competition has been officially adopted as the logo of the STU Youth Volunteer Association and is hereby put into use. Detailed information about the design can be found in the attachment.
