It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to a leave taken by Professor Yang Qing of the Arts Education Center, the "Pipa Performance" course (course number 52904), normally held during period 89 on Fridays in Week 9 (April 27) in room STU213, is canceled. The make-up class time will be announced separately. Students enrolled in this course are requested to inform one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
