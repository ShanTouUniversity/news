It is reported that the correspondence review fees for the 2012 general projects of humanities and social sciences research funded by the Ministry of Education have been released to Shantou University. The Research Office has begun distributing the fees to campus experts who served as reviewers.

## Review Overview

According to the list published by the Research Office, 16 faculty members served as correspondence review experts this round, completing 868 project reviews in total — an average of more than 50 per reviewer — reflecting the academic standing of STU scholars in the humanities and social sciences.
