It is reported that the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Prof. Fang Su will deliver an academic lecture at STU.

Guest speaker: Prof. Fang Su. Topic: "Time: The Journey from Reproduction to Expression." Time: May 2, 2012 (Wednesday), 10:00 AM–12:00 PM. Venue: New Library Auditorium.

Speaker profile: Native of Yiwu, Zhejiang Province, born in Wuhan in 1955. Prof. Fang Su is a professor and master's supervisor in the Visual Communication Design Department at Hubei Institute of Fine Arts. In 2006, he founded the photography program at the institute and serves as the disciplinary leader. His photographic works have won numerous domestic and international awards. He currently serves as Vice Chairman of the Photography Education Committee of the China Higher Education Society, member of the Computer Fundamentals Guidance Committee for Liberal Arts under the Ministry of Education, Vice Chairman of the National University Photography Federation, director of the Computer Fundamentals Research Association of Chinese Higher Education, chairman of the Hubei Province University Photography Society, and director of the Hubei Province Photographers Association.

The lecture has been included in the STU Continuing Education curriculum and carries continuing education credits. All are welcome to register via training.stu.edu.cn using their campus network account and password.
