It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that the Shaoguan University men's basketball team will visit STU for an invitational match on May 5 and 6, 2012, at the STU Gymnasium. The evening game on May 5 is open to all faculty and students, who are welcome to come and cheer for the home team.

Match time: May 5, 2012, 7:30 PM. Registration: via http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ActivityBooking.aspx; deadline May 2, 6:00 PM. The event also seeks 15–20 volunteers and 50–80 cheerleaders. Volunteers should email their registration in the format "Enrollment Year + Major + Name" (e.g., "07 Journalism Zhang San—Shaoguan University vs. STU万变金刚Invitational Match Volunteer Form") to 10jwang1@stu.edu.cn by 6:00 PM on May 2.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU, Physical Education Department)
