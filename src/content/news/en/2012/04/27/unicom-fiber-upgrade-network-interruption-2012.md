It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, according to the work schedule, a Unicom fiber optic line splicing and upgrade will be carried out, causing a temporary interruption.

Affected time: April 26, 2012, 5:30–8:00 PM. Affected scope: all university users accessing Unicom-affiliated websites and foreign websites. Emergency measure: during the interruption, Unicom traffic will be switched to other exit routes to alleviate access demand. Service will be restored without further notice. Users are asked to spread the word. We apologize for any inconvenience.
