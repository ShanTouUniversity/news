It is reported that Shantou University recently issued document 汕大发〔2012〕53号, providing guidance on the 2012 graduate employment work.

In 2011, the university earnestly implemented national and provincial employment policies, put students first, advanced advanced undergraduate education and teaching reforms, strengthened employment guidance and services, and successfully completed all employment tasks, achieving an initial employment rate of 96.77% and an overall employment rate of 99.58%.

This year, Guangdong Province is expected to have over 430,000 college graduates, and including graduates from other provinces seeking employment in Guangdong and those who deferred employment in previous years, over 650,000 college graduates will compete for jobs in Guangdong. The employment situation remains challenging, with prominent structural contradictions and overall pressure. The university must establish a strong sense of crisis and be mentally prepared for a tough battle.

The guiding ideology for 2012 employment work: thoroughly implement the spirit of relevant documents from the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, improve employment work quality centered on employment guidance, and carry out employment guidance work aimed at improving employment satisfaction.
