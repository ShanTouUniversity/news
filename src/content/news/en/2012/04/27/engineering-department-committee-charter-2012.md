It is reported that the Engineering College of Shantou University recently issued document 汕大工发〔2012〕2号, publishing the Charter of the Engineering College Department Committees. The charter was discussed and approved by the college administrative committee and deliberated and approved by the college academic committee. All departments are requested to carry out their work in accordance with this charter.
