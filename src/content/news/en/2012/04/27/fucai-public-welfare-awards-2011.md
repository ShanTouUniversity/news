It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on April 27, 2012, announcing the results of the 2011 Guangdong University Students Fucai Public Welfare Awards.

Organized jointly by the Guangdong Provincial Youth League Committee, the Provincial Welfare Lottery Center and the Provincial Students’ Federation to foster service awareness and social responsibility, the awards recognized STU’s Youth Volunteers Association Firefly Leprosy Rehabilitation Village Work Camp and the Dawn Study Support Chaoyang Action Team with the Outstanding Team Award. Zeng CX (Biotechnology, Class of 2010) and Huang MJ (International Economics and Trade, Class of 2009) were named Fucai Public Welfare Service Stars.

The Youth League Committee congratulated the honorees and called on all students to pursue the spirit of unity, mutual help and selfless dedication and to advance volunteer actions.
