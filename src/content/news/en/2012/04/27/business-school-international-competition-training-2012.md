It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 summer international competition training program will commence soon. The training is open to all business students across the university and covers case analysis, business plan writing, team presentations, and other essential skills for international business competitions. Students interested in participating are requested to contact the Youth League Committee of the Business School for more details.
