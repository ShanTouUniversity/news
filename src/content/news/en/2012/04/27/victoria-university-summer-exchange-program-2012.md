It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 STU Business School–University of Victoria (Canada) Business School Summer Exchange Program has officially launched. In July, the school will select several outstanding undergraduate and graduate students (including part-time MBA students) to participate in a one-month intensive business management summer program (ISIBM) at the University of Victoria.

Program details: The program runs from July 4 to July 29, 2012. Courses are divided into two modules: "Business Communication" and "International Business Practices, Entrepreneurship and Service Industry Innovation." In addition to coursework, the University of Victoria will arrange extracurricular activities and local sightseeing. Courses are taught by professors from the Gustavson School of Business at the University of Victoria; the language of instruction is English. Students who complete the program will receive 6 credits. Tuition fee: 4,500 Canadian dollars. Detailed information is available in the attached "Summer Course Introduction" and at http://www.uvic.ca/gustavson/international/international/summer/institute/index.php.

Eligibility: enrolled undergraduate or graduate students with a foundation in economics and strong academic performance.
