It is reported that the Science College of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 119th session of the "Toward Modern Mathematics" academic lecture series will be held soon.

Speaker: Prof. Zhang Chuanyi (Harbin Institute of Technology). Lecture title: Twenty Years of Pseudo Almost Periodic Functions. Time: April 28, 2012 (Saturday), 4:00 PM. Venue: Mathematics Laboratory (Room 412, Engineering North Building).

Abstract: Pseudo almost periodic functions (PAP for short) were defined in the speaker's Ph.D. thesis nearly 20 years ago (1992). Most people became aware of PAP from the speaker's two papers published in 1994. Since then, PAP has attracted great interest from many mathematicians and more and more researchers from both abroad and China, and has become an active research area. In this talk, we will introduce the background, basic concepts, and main results of PAP. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
