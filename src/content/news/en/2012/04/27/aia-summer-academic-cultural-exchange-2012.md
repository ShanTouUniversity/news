It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 summer AIA (Asia Internet Alliance) academic and cultural exchange program has been launched. The program is open to business students across the university and covers academic and cultural exchange activities. Interested students are welcome to inquire and register.
