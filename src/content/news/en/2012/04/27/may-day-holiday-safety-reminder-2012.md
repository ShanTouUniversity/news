It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that with the Labor Day holiday approaching, students are reminded to observe the following safety precautions to ensure a peaceful, harmonious, and healthy holiday:

1. When traveling, pay attention to safety advisories for your destination and choose licensed travel agencies. Before departure, properly store cash and valuables, lock windows and doors, and inform family, friends, or teachers of your itinerary and contact number. Travel in groups when possible, be mindful of personal and property safety, avoid displaying valuables in public, and guard against scams, theft, and robbery.
2. During the holiday, transportation pressure is high. Obey traffic regulations, ensure riding safety, refuse unlicensed or overloaded vehicles, do not drive after drinking, and prevent traffic accidents.
3. South China and the Jiangnan region are currently in a high-incidence period for severe convective weather. If caught outdoors during lightning, strong winds, or hail, immediately seek shelter. If no suitable shelter is available, crouch low with feet together, hands on knees, and torso bent forward—never lie flat on the ground.
4. Strengthen self-protection awareness and learn to discern truth from falsehood. Be vigilant against fake information, telephone fraud, and pyramid scheme scams disguised as online direct-purchase offers. Do not engage in "pay-first-deliver-later" online transactions.
