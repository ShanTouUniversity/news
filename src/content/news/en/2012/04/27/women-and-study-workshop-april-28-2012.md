It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to create a strong campus cultural atmosphere, foster good reading habits among STU students, and guide students to develop a habit of reading, the university will hold the "Women and the Study" workshop as part of the 9th Sangpu Mountain Reading Festival.

Event time: April 28, 7:30 PM. Venue: Science Lecture Hall 205. Event content: "Women'\''s Study" — "A free space that sees women'\''s writing, listens to women'\''s voices, and exchanges women'\''s experiences." From a historical perspective, the session will discuss reading spaces for women in China and abroad, share contemporary women'\''s reading experiences, and recommend related books.

Guest speakers: Dr. Wang Xiaoli (Lecturer, Women'\''s Research Center, College of Arts and Letters, STU); Dr. Cai Qiuyan (Lecturer, School of Journalism and Communication, STU). All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
