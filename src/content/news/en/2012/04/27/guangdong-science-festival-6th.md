It is reported that the Communist Youth League Guangdong Provincial Committee and the Guangdong Students' Federation will jointly host the sixth Guangdong College Student Science and Technology Festival. The event aims to foster a strong atmosphere of technological innovation and academic inquiry across universities in the province, encouraging young students to value science, pursue knowledge, study diligently and innovate.

## Event Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Sixth Guangdong College Student Science and Technology Festival |
| Organizers | CYL Guangdong Committee, Guangdong Students' Federation |
| Eligibility | Students at Guangdong universities |
| Campus coordinator | Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University |

The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has forwarded to all students and staff the official notice on the festival along with notices for its various sub-events. Students interested in taking part should submit their application materials within the deadlines specified in the corresponding notices.

The festival comprises multiple sub-events, each governed by its own notice and entry rules. Complete notice documents and application forms can be obtained from the university's Youth League Committee.
