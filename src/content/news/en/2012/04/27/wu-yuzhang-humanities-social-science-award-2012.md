It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding the 6th Wu Yuzhang Humanities and Social Sciences Award announcement issued by Renmin University of China. Faculty members interested in applying are requested to submit their materials to the Research Office by May 14.

The Wu Yuzhang Humanities and Social Sciences Award is awarded nationwide and primarily recognizes outstanding monographs in philosophy and social sciences with significant influence in China, aiming to promote the development and prosperity of Chinese philosophy and social sciences. On the occasion of Renmin University of China's 75th anniversary, the Wu Yuzhang Fund Committee has decided to select the 6th Wu Yuzhang Humanities and Social Sciences Award. The award covers eight disciplines: Marxist theory, philosophy, pedagogy, history, Chinese language and literature, journalism, economics, and law. Each discipline awards one special prize and one first prize, 2–3 excellence prizes, and one youth prize. Prizes are 80,000 yuan, 50,000 yuan, 20,000 yuan, and 10,000 yuan respectively. The youth prize requires the author to be 45 years old or below (age calculated at the time of the work's publication).
