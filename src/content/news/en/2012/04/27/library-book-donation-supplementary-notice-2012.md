It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a supplementary notice. Since the library published the "Notice on the China International Book Donation Network Selection" on April 17, it has received a enthusiastic response from faculty and students. However, many recommenders failed to provide their names and affiliated colleges, and some used non-campus email addresses such as 163 or QQ, making it difficult to verify the sources. To improve the effectiveness of book recommendations and the efficiency of library purchasing funds, the library issues the following supplementary requirements:

1. Please务必 use your campus email address when submitting recommendations.
2. Please务必 include your full name, college/school, and status (faculty, graduate student, or undergraduate).
