It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance document "Notice on Allocating and Issuing Temporary Meal Subsidies for Students from Economically Disadvantaged Families in Regular Higher Education Institutions and Higher Vocational Schools for the Fall 2011 Semester" (粤财教〔2011〕591号) and the university'\''s special fund work plan for the 2011 national student loan central reward and subsidy, after individual application, college (residential college) recommendation, Student Affairs Office review, and public notice, subsidies will be distributed to 1,348 economically disadvantaged students for the fall 2011 semester.

Main campus students will receive 500 yuan per person; medical college students will receive 300 yuan per person. Main campus subsidies will be issued in a lump sum to each recipient'\''s Bank of China personal account. Medical college subsidies will be transferred to the Medical College Finance Office for distribution. Recipients are advised to check their accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
