It is reported that, after registration for the first Etiquette Culture Learning Camp at Shantou University's Zhicheng College closed, 33 students had signed up by the deadline. The college confirmed all 33 as camp participants and published the list, which includes Guo DS.

All participants are required to check in at the college's J-354 lecture room at 7:45 a.m. on April 29 and attend the course.
