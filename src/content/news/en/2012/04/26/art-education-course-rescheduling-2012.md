It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a rescheduling notice on April 26, 2012, for the *Western Classical Music Appreciation* course taught by Lin Weijie of the Art Education Center, who will participate in a municipal Youth League Committee performance.

## Schedule

| Class | Cancelled | Original Venue | Make-up | Make-up Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 52452 | Week 9, Sat (April 28, 2012) periods 6–7 | Lecture Hall 2 | Week 10, Sat (May 5) periods A–B | Lecture Hall 3 |
| 52744 | Week 9, Sat (April 28, 2012) periods 3–4 | Lecture Hall 2 | Week 11, Sun (May 6) periods 8–9 | Lecture Hall 3 |

Note: The original April 30 class had already been rescheduled to April 28 for the May Day holiday; the cancelled times above are after that adjustment. Students enrolled are asked to inform each other.
