It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to enrich campus cultural life, create an atmosphere of learning about astronomy and exploring the mysteries of the starry sky, inspire students to reflect on the cosmos, spark interest in astronomy, and broaden scientific horizons, the Association for the Promotion of Science and Technology will host the "Starlight We Gazed Upon Together" astronomy activity series.

Theme: Starlight We Gazed Upon Together. Event period: April 26 to May 5. Activities:

1. Astronomy Knowledge Poster Exhibition: April 26 (Thursday). Venue: Golden Triangle.
2. Astronomy Expert Exchange Workshop: May 5 (Saturday), 3:00–5:00 PM. Venue: Room 726. Members of the Shantou Astronomy Enthusiast Association will share their experiences and exchange ideas with students. Workshop quota is capped at 30 participants; attendees will receive priority access to the stargazing session (May 5 Star Show Astronomy Observation Night). Registration is open immediately.
3. Star Show Astronomy Observation Night: May 5 (Saturday), 8:00–10:30 PM. Venue: STU Reservoir. Astronomical telescopes will be set up on-site for students to experience stargazing and explore the mysteries of the cosmos. A video titled "Exploring the Mysteries of Astronomy" will also be screened.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
