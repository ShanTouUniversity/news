It is reported that the Law School and the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that, following deliberation by the评审小组 (review panel), the results of the Law School New Emblem Design Competition are as follows:

First Prize: Huang Weiping. Second Prize: Lai Lubiao. Third Prize: Zhou Zhibin.

Congratulations to all winning students, and thanks to all faculty and students for their strong support of this event.
