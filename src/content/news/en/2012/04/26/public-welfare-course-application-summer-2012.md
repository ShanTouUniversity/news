It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to further expand course resources and provide students with richer service-learning course models, and in accordance with the "Notice on Publishing the Implementation Plan for Public-Welfare Courses at STU" (汕大发〔2010〕7号), the university will organize the 2012–2013 academic year summer semester public-welfare course application process.

Application deadline: May 11, 2012. Eligibility: applied activities must meet the basic conditions set forth in the aforementioned notice, including public-welfare course overview, talent training objectives, course implementation, course evaluation, and instructor qualifications. To provide greater flexibility in course scheduling, public-welfare course applications will now extend to every semester starting from the Spring 2011–2012 semester; courses applied for this round may be offered before or during the summer term of 2012–2013.

Application and review procedure: Each teaching unit may begin its internal selection, recommendation, and application process upon receipt of this notice. Applicants must complete the "STU New Course Application Form (Public-Welfare Course)." Each teaching unit will carefully organize the application and review process in accordance with the notice requirements.
