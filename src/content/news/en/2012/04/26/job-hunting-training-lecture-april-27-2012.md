It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the "Job Hunting Boot Camp" special lecture will be held soon.

Lecture topic: Fast & Joyful Job Hunting, Fast & Joyful Working — How to Make the Most of College? How to Find a Job Successfully? Speaker: Zhang Junyong (Chief Marketing Officer and Senior Enterprise Management Consultant, Gao Si Wei Enterprise Management Consulting Co.). Time: April 27, 2012, 7:30–9:30 PM. Venue: E-Jie 201 Classroom.

Lecture content: Fast — precise and efficient job searching; Joyful — a positive and optimistic mindset. Are you currently in college and wondering how to shape yourself into the talent that enterprises need? Are you busy job hunting and want to stay ahead of the competition? Have you thought about improving your happiness index at work and achieving a joyful work-life balance? All your job-hunting and career development questions will be answered here. Participants will have the chance to win one of 10 internship positions at Gao Si Wei Enterprise Management Consulting Co. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
