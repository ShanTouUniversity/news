It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued document 汕大团联字〔2012〕4号 that, in accordance with the "STU Student Union Charter" and with the approval of the university Party committee, the 19th STU Student Congress is scheduled to be held in mid-May.

Main tasks of the congress: review and summarize the work since the 18th Student Congress, research and deploy the student union'\''s tasks for the next phase, and elect the 19th STU Student Union Committee and Presidium.

Delegate numbers, qualifications, selection method, and submission deadline: 191 formal delegates and 5–10 non-voting delegates. Qualifications: uphold the Four Cardinal Principles, be exemplary in both character and academics, maintain close ties with the masses, play a model role in one'\''s unit, and possess a certain degree of representativeness and deliberative ability. Selection method: each unit shall democratically elect delegates in accordance with the principle of democratic centralism after thorough deliberation, with the results approved by the college Youth League committee and student union, and submitted to the Congress Secretariat for review and confirmation (specific quota allocation see attached table). Delegate list submission deadline: May 9, 10:00 PM — send to 11cqzhao@stu.edu.cn.

The 19th Student Union Committee is planned to have 21 members.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
