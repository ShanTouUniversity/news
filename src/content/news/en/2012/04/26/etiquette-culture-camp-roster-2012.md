It is reported that Zhicheng College issued a notice on April 26, 2012, announcing the participants for its first Etiquette Culture Camp.

A total of 33 students applied by the deadline and were all selected. To protect privacy, the full list is withheld here; details are available in the OA attachment.

All participants are asked to check in at 07:45 on April 29 at Lecture Room J-354 of the college and attend the camp.
