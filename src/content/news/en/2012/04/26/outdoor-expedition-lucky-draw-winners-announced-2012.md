It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that during the public voting period for the 2012 STU Advanced Outdoor Expedition Project, a total of 5,165 people participated in the voting. Fifty lucky winners were selected through a computer random lottery. The prize is a set of "Chi Zha Feng Yun" beautifully designed postcards. The full list of winners can be viewed at http://expedition.stu.edu.cn/vote/GiftResultList.aspx.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
