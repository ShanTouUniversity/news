It is reported that, as part of its work plan, the Network and Information Center of Shantou University will conduct a cutover upgrade of the campus network's China Unicom fiber line from **17:30 to 20:00 on April 26, 2012**, during which the line will be interrupted.

## Scope of Impact and Contingency Measures

The upgrade will affect all campus users' access to websites served via the Unicom line, including overseas websites. To mitigate the impact, the center will switch Unicom traffic to other egress lines during the cutover to maintain users' access needs as much as possible.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Upgrade window | April 26, 2012, 17:30–20:00 |
| Affected services | Access to Unicom and overseas websites for all campus users |
| Contingency measure | Temporary switchover of Unicom traffic to other egress lines |
| Restoration | Service resumes upon completion; no further notice will be issued |

The Network and Information Center asked users to spread the word and apologized for any inconvenience. Questions may be directed to the center at 82903413-100 or by email at stunic@stu.edu.cn.
