It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the STU Student Union Presidium will be selected through a team-based competitive process, with candidate teams recruited publicly across campus.

Selection method: Any enrolled student meeting the candidate requirements (regardless of grade or major) may form a team of four to apply. The Student Congress Secretariat will organize an assessment to determine four candidate teams and their members, submit them to the university Party committee for approval, and then present them to the Student Congress for election. The Presidium will consist of one chairman and three vice-chairmen.

Candidate requirements: In addition to meeting the general representative conditions and those specified in the "STU Student Cadre Regulations," candidates must demonstrate an innovative and pioneering spirit, enthusiasm for student union work, a serious and pragmatic work style, relevant student work experience, strong social activity capabilities, and the ability to serve a continuous term of no less than one year.

Selection process: (1) Form a candidate team. Required written materials: "Candidate Reserve Person Registration Form" (see attached table), team work proposal (or outline if lengthy), and academic transcripts for each member (certified by the college); (2) Bind all materials together and submit to the Student Congress Secretariat (Room 721, Building 789) by 5:30 PM on May 7, and send electronic copies to 11cqzhao@stu.edu.cn; (3) The Secretariat will organize an assessment panel composed of faculty and student representatives to evaluate the candidate teams.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
