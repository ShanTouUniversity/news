It is reported that, as part of its work plan, the Network and Information Center of Shantou University will conduct a cutover upgrade of the campus network's China Unicom fiber line from **17:30 to 20:00 on April 26, 2012**, during which the line will be interrupted.

## Scope of Impact and Contingency Measures

The upgrade will affect all campus users' access to websites served over the Unicom line, including overseas websites. To mitigate the impact, the center will switch Unicom traffic to other egress lines during the cutover so as to keep users online as far as possible. No separate notice will be issued when service is restored.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Upgrade window | April 26, 2012, 17:30–20:00 |
| Affected services | Access to Unicom and overseas websites for all campus users |
| Contingency measure | Temporary switchover of Unicom traffic to other egress lines |
| Restoration | No separate notice will be issued |

The Network and Information Center asked campus network users to spread the word and apologized for any inconvenience. Questions may be directed to the center at 82903413-100 or by email at stunic@stu.edu.cn.
