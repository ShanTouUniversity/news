It is reported that Shantou University recently issued document 汕大发〔2012〕51号 announcing the results of the 2011 STU University-Level Teaching Achievement Awards. In accordance with the "STU Teaching Achievement Award Interim Measures" (汕大发〔1996〕93号), after review by the STU Teaching Achievement Award Review Committee and public notice, the following main-campus awards have been determined: 1 special prize, 5 first prizes, 4 second prizes, and 5 third prizes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
