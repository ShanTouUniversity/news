It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the "3rd Macau Humanities and Social Sciences Outstanding Achievement Award," jointly organized by the Macau Foundation, Chinese Social Sciences Publishers, and the Guangdong Provincial Federation of Social Sciences, is now open for applications.

Eligible works: publications on Macau-related humanities and social sciences topics formally published or issued between January 1, 2009 and December 31, 2011, including monographs, popular science books, papers, research reports, translations, reference works, and classical text compilations (works by members of the review committee are not eligible).

Discipline categories: Economics and Management; Politics, Law, Journalism and Sociology; History, Philosophy and Literature; Language, Education and Art.

Award structure: monographs and papers, each with first, second, and third prizes plus an excellence award. Monograph prizes: 4 first prizes (30,000 MOP each), 4 second prizes (20,000 MOP), 8 third prizes (10,000 MOP), 12 excellence awards (8,000 MOP). Paper prizes: 4 first prizes (15,000 MOP), 4 second prizes (10,000 MOP), 8 third prizes, 12 excellence awards. Winners receive a certificate of merit and the corresponding cash prize.
