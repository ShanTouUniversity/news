It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to Professor Cai Ying Hui of the Higher Education Research Office needing to attend an out-of-town conference, the "Education" course (course numbers 52602 and 52603), normally held during periods 34 and 89 on Saturdays in Week 9 (April 28) in Rooms E-202 and E-204 respectively, is canceled.

Make-up sessions: Week 10, Friday (May 4), period 0A (class 52602) and periods BC (class 52603), both in Room E-202. Note: Due to the university'\''s unified holiday schedule adjustment for Labor Day, the April 30 classes have been moved to April 28; the times stated above reflect the adjusted schedule. Students enrolled in this course are requested to inform one another.
