It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "Notice on Selecting Outstanding League Branches (General Branches), Outstanding League Cadres, and Outstanding League Members of Shantou University for 2011" (汕大团字〔2012〕05号) and following recommendations from various college Youth League committees and directly affiliated units, the proposed list of collectives and individuals for recommendation is hereby published for public comment.

Any unit or individual with objections may submit written comments or visit/call the university Youth League Committee before May 2, 2012 (unit submissions must bear the official seal; individual submissions must include the real name of the submitter). Contact: Tel 82902302.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
