It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to promote the 2012 pre-enlistment program for college graduates and help graduates understand and master the relevant policies for military enlistment, the Department of Student Services of the Ministry of Education, the National Center for Student Information and Employment Guidance, and the Defense Ministry Conscription Office are jointly hosting the "2012 Online Consultation on Military Enlistment Policy for College Graduating Students."

Event period: April 23–27, 9:00–11:30 AM and 2:00–4:30 PM daily. Website: http://www.ncss.org.cn/2012zbzx/ — students may log in directly to ask questions. All students are encouraged to participate actively.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
