It is reported that the International Exchange Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to actively implement the foreign policy commitment made by State Councilor Liu Yandong at the second working meeting of the China-US High-Level Consultation on People-to-People Exchange in 2011, to encourage Chinese and American universities to establish and deepen cooperative relationships, and to attract more American young students to study in China, the Ministry of Education has established the Ten-Thousand Student China-US Humanities Exchange Special Scholarship under the framework of the China-US High-Level Consultation on People-to-People Exchange.

The university is now accepting applications for the 2012 China-US Humanities Exchange Credit Student Scholarship. Each scholarship covers a three-month partial scholarship (tuition waiver). Eligibility requirements: (1) the applying college must accept US exchange students on a credit-recognized basis (any discipline); (2) the US student must study at STU for three months or longer; (3) the agreement or memorandum with the US counterpart must have been signed after January 2011. Colleges interested in applying should contact the International Exchange Office at 82902316.
