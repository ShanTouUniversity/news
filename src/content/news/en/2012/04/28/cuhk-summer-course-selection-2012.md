It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that, with strong support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Hong Kong entrepreneur Charles Chan donated funds to the university as scholarships for overseas exchange study. Through the foundation's coordination, the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) agreed to admit STU students to its summer program this year.

According to the notice, 10 places were made available. The selection was open to full-time undergraduates of the 2009 and 2010 cohorts enrolled at the main campus, excluding the Medical College. Candidates were required to demonstrate outstanding academic performance, strong learning and analytical abilities, active participation in on- and off-campus activities, leadership potential, and an optimistic personality. Applicants also needed strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, with a TOEFL score of 71 or above, IELTS 6.0 or above, or CET-4 550 or above. The university further sought students able to promote and introduce STU and its educational features, with clear academic goals, an international outlook, and a willingness to engage in public affairs and voluntary service.

The summer program was scheduled to run from June 25 to July 31, 2012. The Student Affairs Office noted that one week of the program would overlap with STU's final examination period, and selected students were advised to plan their schedules in advance. Participants would take one course in the morning session and one in the afternoon session from the summer program offerings, each worth three credits, for a total of six credits.
