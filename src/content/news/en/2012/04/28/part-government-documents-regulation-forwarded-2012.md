It is reported that the President's Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding the "Regulation on the Handling of Party and Government Documents." The new regulation will take effect on July 1, 2012, at which point the "Regulation on the Handling of Party Documents of the Communist Party of China" issued by the General Office of the CPC Central Committee on May 3, 1996, and the "Measures for the Handling of Official Documents of State Administrative Organs" issued by the State Council on August 24, 2000, will cease to be implemented. The university has forwarded the full text of the new regulation to all units and requested that they study it conscientiously.

> Source: STU OA Notice (President's Office)
