It is reported that the 2012 Shantou University Medal selection has completed its preliminary review, with the interview stage set to begin soon.

A total of 22 students submitted application materials. After evaluation by the selection committee, 12 outstanding candidates have been shortlisted for interviews (full list available in the attachment from the Student Affairs Office).

## Interview Arrangements

The interviews are scheduled for 3:00 p.m. on Tuesday, May 8, at Meeting Room 4, ACC. Each candidate will have no more than 10 minutes, structured as follows:

| Session | Content | Duration |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Self-introduction | In Chinese | No more than 1 minute |
| Panel Q&A | Questions from judges | No more than 9 minutes |

Shortlisted candidates are required to draw lots to determine the interview order at 3:00 p.m. on Monday, May 7, in Meeting Room 108 of the Student Affairs Office.

Organized by the Student Affairs Office, the STU Medal aims to recognize students with outstanding achievements across various areas.
