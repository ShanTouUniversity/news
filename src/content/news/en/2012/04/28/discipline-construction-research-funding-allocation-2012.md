It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently released the allocation plan for the 2012 "Discipline Construction and Research Enhancement Special Fund". The plan was formed following discipline-level applications, review by the Research Committee, and final approval by the university, and has now been made public.

The special fund is divided into two categories: operating funds for the university's key research platforms, and discipline construction and research enhancement funds allocated to individual disciplines.

The first category, operating funds for the university's key research platforms, was allocated to relevant discipline platforms and is designated specifically for setting up open research funds at provincial-level research platforms. The allocation is as follows:

| Platform | Amount (RMB) |
| --- | --- |
| Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Marine Biotechnology | 100,000 |
| Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Digital Signal and Image Processing Technology | 100,000 |
| Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Structure and Wind Tunnel (in Universities) | 60,000 |
| STU Institute for Local Government Development Studies | 50,000 |
| STU Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation Research Institute | 50,000 |

The second category, the Discipline Construction and Research Enhancement Fund, was allocated to individual disciplines. Expenditures are to be jointly approved by the discipline leaders and the deans, and are primarily used for exchange visits and lectures by renowned external experts, as well as preliminary expenses for the introduction of discipline talent. The allocation is as follows:

| Discipline | Amount (RMB) |
| --- | --- |
| Marine Science | 120,000 |
| Biology | 120,000 |
| Chemistry | 120,000 |
| Mathematics | 120,000 |
| Mechanical Engineering | 120,000 |
| Civil Engineering | 90,000 |
| Business Administration | 90,000 |
| Information and Communication Engineering | 90,000 |
| Chinese Language and Literature | 90,000 |
| Public Administration | 90,000 |
| Education | 90,000 |
| Optical Engineering | 90,000 |
| Foreign Languages and Literature | 60,000 |
| Law | 60,000 |
