It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that, in accordance with relevant university regulations, student dormitories supply hot water on different schedules across the summer and winter seasons.

Starting from May 1, 2012, student dormitory Buildings A, B, C, D, E, F, G and J began operating under the summer hot water supply schedule. Students were reminded to use hot water only during the designated time periods.

The seasonal hot water supply arrangements were as follows:

| Season | Period | Hot Water Supply Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer | May 1 – October 31 (excluding summer vacation) | 17:00–18:30 and 22:00–23:00 |
| Winter | November 1 – April 30 of the following year (excluding winter vacation) | 16:30–22:30 |

For any questions or issues, students were advised to call 82902661 or the 24-hour on-duty mobile line 13556304313.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
