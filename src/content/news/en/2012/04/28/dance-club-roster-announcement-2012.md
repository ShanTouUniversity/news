It is reported that Shantou University’s dance club has completed recruitment for its first group of members. The Sports Department said that the club selected its first members based on interview results and issued the roster with its notice.

Because the roster contains students’ personal information, individual names are not reproduced here. Students concerned may consult the original OA attachment for the recruitment results.

> ⚠️ The first recruitment roster is available in the original OA attachment.
