It is reported that relevant authorities in Guangdong have decided to select the president of Zhongkai University of Agriculture and Engineering through public recommendation, competitive selection, and quantitative evaluation. Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department recently forwarded the information and began its internal recommendation process.

The selection is open to recommendations from relevant higher-education institutions across Guangdong. Candidates should have experience in higher-education teaching, research, and administration, strong coordination skills, and an academic background in agriculture, science, or engineering. They are generally expected to have held a deputy department-level leadership position for at least two years, hold at least a full-time undergraduate degree or a master’s degree, possess a senior professional title, be around 50 years old, and be in good health.

Interested members of the university community must submit the Nomination Registration Form for the President of Zhongkai University of Agriculture and Engineering to Room 405, Xingdong Building, by 12:00 on May 2, 2012. The detailed selection procedure and material requirements are provided in the forwarded notice attachments.
