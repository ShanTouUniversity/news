It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that, in accordance with relevant university regulations, student dormitories are supplied with electricity on different schedules for the summer and winter seasons. During the summer season, power is supplied around the clock, while during the winter season (November 1 to April 30 of the following year) supply is limited to 6:00 to 23:30.

Starting May 1, 2012, Student Dormitory Buildings A, B, C, D, E, F, G and J will begin operating under the summer 24-hour power supply system. The schedule is summarized below.

| Season | Period | Supply Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer | May 1 – October 31 | 24-hour supply |
| Winter | November 1 – April 30 | 6:00 – 23:30 |

To prepare for the transition, the office arranged for staff to adjust the power supply systems of the above buildings from 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. on April 30. During the adjustment, a power outage of about 15 minutes was scheduled to occur, with a bell rung five minutes in advance as a reminder. Students were asked to switch off their power supplies in time to prevent damage to electrical appliances.

For any questions, the office may be reached at 82902661 or via the 24-hour duty mobile phone at 13556304313.
