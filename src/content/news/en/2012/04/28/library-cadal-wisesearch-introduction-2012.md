It is reported that the Library of Shantou University recently issued a notice introducing two digital resource services to faculty and students.

The first is the China Academic Digital Associative Library (CADAL), also known as the China-U.S. Million Book Digital Library. Registered users can browse ancient books and English-language books free of charge and enjoy personalized services such as borrowing history, page annotations, personal bookshelves and social networking. All ancient books and English books are fully open, and registered users from participating institutions may borrow selected publications from the Republican and modern eras.

- Website: http://www.cadal.zju.edu.cn
- Registration: http://www.cadal.zju.edu.cn/account/register

The second is the WiseSearch "E-Newsstand." Backed by the world's largest Chinese media information database, WiseSearch provides access to more than 1,500 publications, covering news from across the Greater China region with daily updates. After entering the WiseSearch portal, users can click "Settings" in the upper right corner to subscribe to full-text browsing of up to four newspapers at a time according to personal preference.

- Website: http://wisesearch.wisers.net.cn/?gid=STULCN&user=ipaccess
