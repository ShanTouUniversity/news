It is reported that Shantou University’s School of Science has cancelled the 119th lecture in its “Modern Mathematics” series. The lecture had been scheduled for 16:00 on Saturday, April 28, but was cancelled because the speaker, Professor Zhang Chuanyi, experienced a travel delay.

The original venue was Mathematics Laboratory Room G-B412. The School of Science apologized for the inconvenience and said that any subsequent arrangements will be announced separately.
