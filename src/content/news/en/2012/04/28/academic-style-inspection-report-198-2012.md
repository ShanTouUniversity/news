It is reported that Shantou University’s Office for the Development of the Three Campus Cultures recently released a report on academic-style inspections during Week 9 of the 2011–2012 spring semester. Inspection team members checked class attendance at Building E at 08:00 on April 23 and April 24, focusing on late arrivals.

The two inspections recorded 25 instances of lateness in total:

| Inspection date | Location | Up to 5 minutes late | More than 5 minutes late |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| April 23 | Building E | 9 | 4 |
| April 24 | Building E | 7 | 5 |

The university said that the relevant student list was distributed with the notice. Colleges and residential colleges will provide educational guidance to the students concerned and continue their efforts to strengthen academic conduct.

> ⚠️ The student list and related attachment are available in the original OA notice.
