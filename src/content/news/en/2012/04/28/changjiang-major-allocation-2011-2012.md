It is reported that Changjiang College of Arts and Design has completed major assignments for its 2011 cohort and released the final results.

The college’s Teaching Committee previously reviewed and approved the assignment plan. After students submitted their preferences, the college carried out the assignments according to the established principles and selection criteria for each major. The final results were posted on the college notice board for public review. No objections were received during the review period, after which the results were announced to all students.

The assignment plan and detailed roster are available in the original OA attachments. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
