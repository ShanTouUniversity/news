It is reported that the President's Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice compiling the duty schedules of relevant units for the May Day holiday. To facilitate communication among departments during the holiday, the office has consolidated the rosters for reference. For inquiries, contact the President's Office at o_jdk@stu.edu.cn.
