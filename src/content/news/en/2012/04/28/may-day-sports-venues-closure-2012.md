It is reported that Shantou University’s Sports Department has announced a one-day closure of campus sports facilities on Tuesday, May 1, 2012.

The closure covers indoor sports facilities, the gym, and the tennis courts. Students and staff are advised to adjust their sports plans for the holiday. The reopening schedule will follow the university’s subsequent arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
