It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing an upcoming Media Salon session.

The lecture, titled "The Launch of the Cultural Revolution and the Red Guard Movement," will be held on May 4, 2012, from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM at the Lecture Hall of the New Library. The speaker is Prof. Yin Hongbiao of the School of International Relations at Peking University.

The notice noted that Premier Wen Jiabao, at a press conference during this year's Two Sessions in response to a major political incident in Chongqing, solemnly emphasized the need to draw lessons from history. This has triggered a renewed surge of reflection on the Cultural Revolution at home and abroad. Prof. Yin, one of the few renowned experts on Cultural Revolution history in China and internationally, has devoted more than three decades to research on the period, collecting a wealth of valuable materials, unraveling many historical mysteries, and offering profound and distinctive insights. The university welcomes all faculty and students to attend.
