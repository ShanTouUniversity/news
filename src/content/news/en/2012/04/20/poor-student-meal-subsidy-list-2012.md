It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance document \"Notice on Allocating and Issuing Temporary Meal Subsidies for Students from Economically Disadvantaged Families in Regular Higher Education Institutions and Higher Vocational Schools for the Fall 2011 Semester\" (粤财教〔2011〕591号), the university is conducting the selection work for temporary meal subsidies for students from economically disadvantaged families for the fall 2011 semester.

The subsidy standard issued in the document is 60 yuan per student per month for a period of 5 months, totaling 300 yuan per student. However, due to limited subsidy quotas and rising prices putting pressure on students\\' living costs, the university leadership meeting decided to allocate part of the 2011 national student loan central reward and subsidy special funds to increase the subsidy by 48 additional quotas and raise the standard to 100 yuan per student per month for 5 months, totaling 500 yuan per student. The medical college\\'s special fund usage plan is formulated independently by the medical college, and students from the medical college are not included in this subsidy increase.

After individual application, college (residential college) recommendation, and Student Affairs Office verification, the list of 1,348 students receiving the temporary meal subsidy for the fall 2011 semester is hereby published. Any unit or individual with objections is requested to submit written comments to the Student Affairs Office Student Financial Aid Management Center (Room 102, Student Affairs Office) by close of business on April 25 (Wednesday). Unit submissions must bear the official seal; individual submissions must include the submitter\\'s real name.
