It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to further cultivate students\\' innovative spirit and entrepreneurial ability, guide them to seek truth from facts, study diligently, courageously innovate, and actively engage in scientific practice, and to encourage a large number of talents with innovative spirit and ability to emerge, the university will hold the 8th STU \"Challenge Cup\" College Students Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Competition.

Event period: April–May 2012. Theme: \"Challenge Dreams, Create Brilliance.\" Organizer: STU Leading Group for Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities. Co-organizer: Youth League Committee of Shantou University. Supporting organizer: STU Association for the Promotion of Science and Technology. Eligibility: all students (natural science academic papers category limited to undergraduate students). Competition categories: (1) Natural science academic papers; (2) Philosophical and social science survey reports and academic papers; (3) Technological invention and production. Work requirements: competition entries are divided into the three categories above; works must demonstrate scientific rigor, advanced nature, theoretical significance, and practical relevance. Entries must be primarily designed by students, completed independently, and capable of being displayed. Graduation designs and course designs (papers), term papers and degree theses, and works selected for international competitions or awarded at various levels are not eligible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
