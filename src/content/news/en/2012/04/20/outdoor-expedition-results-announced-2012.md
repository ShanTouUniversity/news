It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the public voting for the 2012 STU Advanced Outdoor Expedition Project ended on April 18. A total of 5,165 people participated in the voting, casting 8,985 votes in total. After adding the public voting scores and judge voting scores for each team, the final selection results are as follows:

Advancing teams: 海贼王在雅鲁 (Pirate King in Yalu), 行走在可可西里 (Walking in the Hoh Xil), 狼塔C线徒步穿越 (Wolf Tower C Line Trek), 穿越罗布泊 (Crossing the Lop Nur). Detailed score statistics for each team are in the attachment.

The university thanks all faculty and students for their enthusiastic participation and welcomes continued attention to this year\\'s advanced outdoor expedition project.
