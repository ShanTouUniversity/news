It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to re-lay and fully repair multiple campus backbone fiber optic cables severed during construction on April 8, partial network outage will occur during the splicing period.

Affected time: April 22, 2012 (Sunday), 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Affected area: Western campus zone (including all faculty dormitories, Multidisciplinary Building, Resource Management Office, Foundation Office, Staff Union, Student Affairs Office, University Hospital, IT Department, Arts Education Center, Building 789, and other buildings). Mitigation measure: Users who urgently need internet access are advised to go to teaching buildings, office areas, or the library. Service will be restored without further notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
