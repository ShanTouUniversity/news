It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 STU Sports Spirit Medal interview will be held on April 25 at 10:00 AM in ACC Room 4. The interview process is as follows: self-introduction (in Chinese, no more than 1 minute), followed by questions from the panel (no more than 9 minutes). Students who have been shortlisted for the interview (see attachment for the list) are requested to attend on time.
