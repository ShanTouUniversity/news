It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the \"Capturing Mood\" photography contest, part of the 7th Mental Health Awareness Month, has officially launched.

Target participants: all faculty and students. Participation method: individual or team entries are accepted. Work requirements: participants should create a PPT or digital album using photos taken on campus, with each photo accompanied by an explanation of the emotion it portrays; total number of photos should be between 20 and 45; all works must be original and不得剽窃他人作品. Submission deadline: 11:59 PM on May 9. Submission method: download and fill out the attached form, then send it together with the work to 13592819249@126.com.

Awards: First prize — 1 recipient, 300 yuan + certificate; Second prize — 1 recipient, 200 yuan + certificate; Third prize — 2 recipients, 100 yuan + certificate each; Excellence award — 1 recipient, 100 yuan + certificate. For inquiries, contact Hou (679249, 11jhhou@stu.edu.cn) or Guan (6930, 11smwan@stu.edu.cn). The organizer reserves the final right of interpretation.
