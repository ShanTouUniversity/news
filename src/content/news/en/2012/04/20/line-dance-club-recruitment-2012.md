It is reported that, to enrich campus culture, promote the spirit of physical education and popularize "National Fitness Line Dance," and to support the development of the university's high-level basketball team, the Department of Physical Education of Shantou University plans to establish a Line Dance Club and is now recruiting its first cohort of members.

## Recruitment Quota

The first intake will recruit 25 members, including 20 female and 5 male students. The 20 female members will focus this semester on cheerleading performances at basketball games.

## Application and Interview

| Stage | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Application period | April 23–24, 2012 |
| How to apply | Complete the application form and send it to o_tyb@stu.edu.cn with subject line "Line Dance Club Application" |
| Interview date | Thursday, April 26, 2012, 12:30 |
| Venue | Gymnastics Room, Sports Hall |

The interview schedule: 12:30–13:00 instruction on interview moves; 13:00–13:15 self-practice; 13:20–13:50 formal interview.

## Interview Options (choose one)

1. Segment of the prescribed routine of the First National Cheerleading (Pom) Set (6×8 beats as illustrated in the attachment);
2. Segment of the line dance routine "Atomic Polka" (first 6×4 beats);
3. Self-prepared performance.

The Department noted that students may refer to the attachment "First Pom Routine Segment" for details or watch related videos on the department's website.
