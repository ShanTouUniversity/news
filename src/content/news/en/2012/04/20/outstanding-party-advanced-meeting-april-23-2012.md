It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that a meeting on the recommendation of outstanding party organizations and members for the 2010–2012 provincial and municipal commendation will be held on April 23 at 3:00 PM in Room 205, Science and Technology Center. Heads of the Medical College Party Committee, all general party branches of the main campus, and directly affiliated party branches are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, Party Committee)
