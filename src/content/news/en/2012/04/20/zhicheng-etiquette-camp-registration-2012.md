It is reported that Zhicheng Residential College at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to promote residential students\\' understanding of Chinese and Western etiquette, enhance college students\\' etiquette culture literacy and personal temperament, cultivate self-consciousness in observing public morality, and avoid life-stage impacts from easily overlooked basic etiquette issues, the college will hold its first \"Etiquette Culture Learning Camp\" on April 29, 2012.

Learning content: \"Basic Norms Frequently Observed\"; China — A Nation of Etiquette; The Key to Learning Etiquette; The Relationship Between Etiquette and the Individual, Family, and Society; Observing Social Interactions Through Details; Everyday Etiquette (Interpersonal Skills, Image Building). Learning format: lectures, expert videos, group discussions, reports, combining individual study with intensive集中学习. Schedule: 2 days total — April 29 is全天的 intensive集中学习; the remaining time is individual study arranged by participants. Target participants: Zhicheng College residents and students from other campus residential areas (voluntary registration). Quota: approximately 40 students — 35 from Zhicheng College residents and 5 from other residential areas. If registrations exceed the quota, a lottery system will be used.

Registration requirements: humble and eager to learn, open-minded; sincerely wish to improve oneself and are willing to experience a new way of life; able to concentrate on study and possess good teamwork spirit; follow all instructions and treat everyone with a smile; no other course or activity arrangements during the April 29 intensive session. Registration method: download and carefully fill out the attached \"Zhicheng College Etiquette Culture Learning Camp Participant Registration Form\" and submit to the designated email.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng Residential College)
