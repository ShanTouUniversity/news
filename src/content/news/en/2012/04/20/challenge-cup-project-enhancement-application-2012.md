It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice: innovation realizes dreams, and passion meets challenges. The \"Challenge Cup\" is regarded as the Olympics of Chinese college students\\' academic and technological circles. Since its inaugural competition in 1989, it has been highly favored by college students across the country. To better prepare for the 12th Guangdong Province \"Challenge Cup\" College Students Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Works Competition in 2013, the university is now accepting applications for project enhancement from outstanding students.

Eligible works must be those that participated in the 8th STU \"Challenge Cup\" Campus Selection Competition. Works must fall into one of three categories: natural science academic papers, philosophical and social science survey reports and academic papers, or technological invention and production.经过专家评审，评定认为具有提升价值的项目将获得经费支持，提升经费分为1500元、1000元、500元三个档次。Projects must complete the enhancement tasks to the expected效果 by the end of February 2013 and participate in a related defense; those that pass the review and show real progress will be concluded and represent the university at the 2013 Guangdong Province \"Challenge Cup.\"

The application form is attached. Interested participants should submit completed materials to stukexie@163.com by May 2, and also submit a paper copy to the office of the University Association for Science and Technology Promotion (Room 732, Building 789).
