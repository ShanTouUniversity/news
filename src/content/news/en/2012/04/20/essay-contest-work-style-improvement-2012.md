It is reported that the Publicity Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice forwarding the Shantou Municipal Direct Organs\"Notice on Carrying Out the Theme Essay Contest 'My Sense of Duty in My Position, Improving Work Style Starts with Me'\" (汕直发〔2012〕10号). All university units are asked to mobilize party members and cadres to participate enthusiastically.

Intended contributors should submit their manuscripts to the Publicity Department (email: o_xcb@stu.edu.cn) by May 23, along with two paper copies. The university will select 3 outstanding essays to forward to the city of Shantou and provide internal rewards for other excellent works.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department, Party Committee)
