It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the \"Voice from Letters\" dubbing competition, part of the 7th Mental Health Awareness Month, has officially launched.

Target participants: all faculty and students. Registration and submission: send works to hezijun0115@qq.com before May 3 (including grade, major, name, competition entry title, campus and mobile numbers, and a written description). Work requirements: dubbed video and dialogue content must be healthy and positive; work duration 5–10 minutes; dialogue should尽量 not duplicate the original, showcasing the participant\\'s unique interpretation of the video, free from the constraints of the original work, expressing genuine emotions; works must include a written commentary of under 100 words.

Evaluation and schedule: May 5–9,大众投票 via @汕大心协心理宣传月之配音大赛 to select the top 5 for the final; May 12 — final round. Awards: First prize — 1 recipient, 300 yuan + certificate; Second prize — 2 recipients, 150 yuan + certificate each; Third prize — 2 recipients, 100 yuan + certificate each. For inquiries, contact He (694960 or 11zjhe1@stu.edu.cn) or follow the Weibo account @汕大心协心理宣传月之配音大赛. Winning preliminary works will be published on Weibo, and participants will be notified personally. The organizer reserves the final right of interpretation.
