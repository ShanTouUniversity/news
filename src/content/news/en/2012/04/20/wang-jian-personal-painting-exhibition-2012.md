It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the \"Ink and Brush Captivate the Heart — Wang Jian Personal Painting Exhibition\" will be held from April 23 to 29, 2012, at the Gallery of the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design, Shantou University. All faculty and students are welcome to visit.
