It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the first episode of the 2012 "Mind Growth" workshop series—titled "Declaring War on Innate Behavioral Stubbornness"—will be held on April 14 from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM in Room 736, Building 789. The workshop is facilitated by Fang Ying and limited to 12 participants.

Activities include: "Guess Who I Am," "Procrastination Battle," and "Procrastination vs. Suggestions." Interested students are asked to download and complete the registration form and email it to o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn before 11:00 AM on April 12 (first come, first served). The Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will notify selected participants via email.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
