It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to enrich campus cultural life and showcase the youthful charm of STU students, the STU Student Union, together with college student unions, will host the 8th STU Girls Festival themed "Beauty and Fun" activities.

The event will be held on April 8 (Sunday) from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and from 2:30 PM to 6:00 PM at Jin San Jiao (Golden Triangle). If rain occurs, the event will be postponed to April 15. Detailed activity arrangements for each college are available in the attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
