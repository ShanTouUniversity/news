It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the spirit of the Shantou Municipal Working Committee of the CPC\'s "Opinions on Carefully Carrying Out the 2012 Democratic Evaluation of Party Members" (Shan Zhi Fa〔2012〕No. 7) and combined with the university\'s actual conditions, and with the approval of the university Party committee, the 2012 democratic evaluation of Party members will be carried out on the main campus from early April to late May.

Each general Party branch and directly affiliated Party branch is requested to carefully implement the democratic evaluation work in connection with the commemoration of the 91st anniversary of the founding of the Party and the in-depth promotion of the advanced creation activity. For Party members proposed for handling, the "2012 Democratic Evaluation Unqualified Party Member Disposal Form" (Form 2) along with relevant materials must be submitted to the university Party committee for approval before May 21. Each unit should submit one copy of the work summary and relevant forms (Forms 1, 3, 4) to the Organization and United Front Work Department (Room 405, Xingdong Building) by May 28. Email: o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.
