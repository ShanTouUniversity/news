It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to cultivate and enhance the leadership abilities of young students and promote their growth in knowledge, skills, and experience—helping them become socially responsible future leaders in various fields—the university has launched the FLY Training Program. Since its inception in 2011, the program has successfully held four training sessions.

On April 15, the university will continue with Phase 2 of the FLY Training Program—the "Wing Expansion" (advanced) plan. Training dates are April 15, 21, 22, and May 13, spanning four days. Key training content includes mock hearings, field research practice, "creativity" workshops, outdoor exchange meetings, and real-person CS field battles. The program is open to leaders of various student organizations.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
