It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, according to the university teaching schedule, the 2012 summer and fall semester teaching arrangement will proceed simultaneously. All teaching units are requested to promptly prepare the course schedules for the 2012 academic year summer and fall semesters, and to submit information on textbook adoption for each course.

The summer semester is a formal semester within the university\'s three-semester system. The total course hours for each teaching unit should be approximately 15%–20% of the spring or fall semester. Courses typically offered in the summer semester include: practical teaching segments requiring continuity; compulsory public courses such as "Ideological and Political Theory Courses" that are heavy in the spring/fall semesters; general education or major courses that cannot meet student enrollment demands in the spring/fall semesters; required courses with many students needing retakes due to failed assessments; and public welfare courses (48 hours, 1 credit each). Students\' workload in the summer semester should not exceed 6 credits.
