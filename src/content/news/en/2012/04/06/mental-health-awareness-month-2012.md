It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to enhance college students\' mental health awareness, improve their self-adjustment abilities, and promote mental health knowledge, the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center of the Student Affairs Office will hold the 7th STU Mental Health Awareness Month in April 2012.

Theme: "Heart to Heart" (心伙相传). Dates: April 17 to May 25, 2012. Main activities include: opening ceremony with large-scale on-site hypnosis (April 17, 7:30 PM, Science Lecture Hall, speaker Prof. Cai Zhonghuai); Mind Comic Workshop (April 17–30); "Photographing Mood" photography contest (theme: appearance reflects the heart, April–May); outdoor psychological counseling (April 28, 3:00 PM, Jin San Jiao pedestrian street); "Voice from Letters" dubbing competition (April–May); college student psychological drama competition; and other activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
