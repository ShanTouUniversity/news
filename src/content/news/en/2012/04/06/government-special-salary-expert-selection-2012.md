It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education\'s "Notice on Forwarding the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security\'s Notice on Carrying Out the 2012 Selection of Personnel Entitled to Government Special Allowance" (Yue Jiao Shi Han〔2012〕No. 10).各单位 are requested to carefully carry out the selection and recommendation work in accordance with the notice requirements and the conditions and procedures specified in the "Guangdong Province Provisional Measures for the Selection and Recommendation of Personnel Entitled to Government Special Allowance" (Yue Fu Ban〔2002〕No. 17).

According to the education department\'s notice, the university may recommend one person. The Personnel Office will organize an expert panel to select and ultimately determine the university\'s recommended candidate. Due to tight timing, each unit must submit the following materials to Room 406, Xingdong Building, Personnel Office, by 9:00 AM on April 12: (1) two copies of the recommended expert\'s "Expert Information Registration Form" or "Senior Professional Talent Information Registration Form"; (2) two copies of the "2012 Recommended Personnel Entitled to Government Special Allowance Information Table" (no official seal required); (3) two copies each of the recommended person\'s professional technical qualification certificate, award certificates, honor certificates, achievement appraisal and other relevant material copies.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
