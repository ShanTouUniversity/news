It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing the utility bills for student dormitories from the start of the semester through the end of March. The current meter reading was taken on March 31, with the previous reading on February 23.

Students are asked to log in to the STU Utility Bill Management System (http://power.stu.edu.cn/) to check their bills using their campus network accounts. If any errors or uncertainties are found, please complete the "Utility Bill Verification Application Form," sign it, and submit it in person at the Water and Electricity Center (located beside the Science and Technology Building, Tel: 2489) between April 6 and 12 during working hours. If the dormitory assigned does not match the student list, please contact the counselor office (Room 106, Building B, Tel: 2903355).

If no objections are raised by the deadline, deductions will begin on April 13 via the campus card system operated by the Network Center (Tel: 3547-8842). The normal payment period is April 13–17. Overdue non-payment will be handled according to university regulations as of April 18. Please top up your campus card in a timely manner if the balance is insufficient.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
