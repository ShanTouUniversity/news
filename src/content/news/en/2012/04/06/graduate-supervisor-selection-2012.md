It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "Provisions on Graduate Supervisor Work of Shantou University," the 2012 selection of doctoral and master supervisors will now be conducted.

Faculty members applying for supervisor qualifications must submit their application materials (6 copies of the doctoral supervisor application form or 4 copies for master supervisors, 1 copy of the profile form, and 1 copy of supporting materials) to their respective disciplines by April 12. Supporting materials include copies of academic papers, book cover/spine/table of contents/page information, award certificates, patent certificates, degree certificates, and professional technical qualification certificates, bound separately. Each discipline is responsible for verifying, conducting preliminary review, and publicizing applicant materials, and must submit approved materials to the Graduate School, Room G-205, by April 20. Faculty members who have already obtained supervisor qualifications at other institutions must submit one copy of their confirmation materials (confirmation form, profile form, supporting materials, and supervisor qualification proof) directly to the Graduate School, Room G-205, by April 20.
