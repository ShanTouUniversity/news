It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 3rd Academic Lecture of 2012 will be held on April 9, 2012 (Monday) from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM in Meeting Room 5, Academic Exchange Center.

The lecture topic is "The Formation, Deepening, and Prospects of Capitalist Crisis." The speaker is Prof. Zheng Zhuyuan, a tenured professor in the Department of Economics at Bowdoin College, USA. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
