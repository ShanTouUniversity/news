It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the "Non-Design — Xu Lan Design Exhibition" will be held from April 5 to 9, 2012, in the first-floor exhibition gallery of the college. The opening ceremony will take place at 4:30 PM on April 5. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
