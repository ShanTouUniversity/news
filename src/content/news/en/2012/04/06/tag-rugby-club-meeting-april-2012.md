It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the STU Touch Rugby Club has been established and will hold its first meeting on the afternoon of this weekend. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

The meeting is scheduled for April 8 (Sunday) at 4:00 PM in the Multimedia Classroom of the STU Gymnasium.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
