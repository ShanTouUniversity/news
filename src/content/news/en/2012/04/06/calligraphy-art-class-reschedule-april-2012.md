It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to a conflict involving Prof. Yang Depeng, the "Calligraphy Art" course (Class 51075, Periods 8–9; Class 51076, Periods A–B) scheduled for Thursday of Week 7 (April 12) in Room 202, Building D, is canceled. The makeup class will be held on Saturday of Week 6 (April 7) at the same time and in the same room (Room 202, Building D). Students enrolled in the course are asked to inform one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
