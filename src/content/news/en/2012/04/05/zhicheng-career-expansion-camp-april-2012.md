It is reported that Zhicheng College at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to help residential students plan their careers early, understand social needs, find their personal positioning, correctly recognize key factors in career choices, improve professional abilities, cultivate professional ethics including dedication, integrity, and contribution, and build self-confidence, independence, and social responsibility, the college will hold the 5th "Career Expansion Boot Camp" on April 14–15, 2012.

Key activities include: senior business executives sharing insights on life cultivation and decision-making; HR directors from well-known enterprises providing on-site resume guidance and interview coaching; 2012 graduates sharing their job-hunting experiences and reflections; expert lectures, leaderless group discussions, and mock interviews; and company visits with on-site exchanges with executives.

Open to Zhicheng College residents and students from other residential areas. Enrollment: 50 spots (45 for Zhicheng College residents, 5 for students from other residential areas). If applications exceed the quota, a lottery system will be used. Application requirements: a humble and eager-to-learn attitude, genuine desire to strengthen oneself, willingness to endure hardship; willingness to accept strict training, seize and cherish opportunities; obedience to all instructions, treating everyone with a smile.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
