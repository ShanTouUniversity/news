It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Career Launch Lecture Series (VOL.23) will be held on April 7, 2012, from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM in Lecture Hall E101.

The lecture topic is "Decoding 500-Firm Internship Online Applications." The speaker is Lin Jingwen (Melody Lam), a specially appointed lecturer, course consultant, and senior career counselor at J Lai Ren (Beijing) Education Technology Co., Ltd., and Director of CIC South China Region. During her junior year internship at Unilever headquarters, she stood out among over 100 interns and became one of only seven outstanding interns. Upon graduation, she received offers from over 20 well-known enterprises including HSBC Bank, Hang Seng Bank, Standard Chartered Bank, and China Merchants Bank. Guided by the motto "Help Others, Help Ourselves," she declined a 500-firm offer to pursue a career in vocational planning and counseling.

The lecture will address the following questions: Do 80% of employees recruited by top enterprises come through online applications? What are the timing, stages, and requirements of online applications at top firms? What are the secrets and solutions to online applications? How can applicants avoid having their applications go unnoticed? All faculty and students are welcome.
