It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently forwarded a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Association for International Exchange in Education. This year marks the 40th anniversary of the normalization of China-Japan diplomatic relations and the "China-Japan People-to-People Friendship Year." To further strengthen educational and cultural exchange between the two countries and promote mutual learning and understanding among youth, the association will organize a group of university students to visit Japan for cultural exchange activities in May 2012.

The activity is scheduled for May 7–12, 2012. The cost is 9,950 RMB, to be borne by each student, and includes sightseeing, accommodation, transportation, airfare, visa, entrance fees for listed attractions, and travel insurance. Eligibility criteria: love for the motherland and law-abiding; willing to communicate and participate in international cultural exchange activities, with Japanese language skills preferred; currently enrolled university students. Interested students should complete the application form and submit it to Room 104, Education Management Section, Student Affairs Office by April 10, 2012, and send an electronic copy to lslin@stu.edu.cn.
