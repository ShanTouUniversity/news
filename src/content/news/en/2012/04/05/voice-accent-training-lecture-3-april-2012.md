It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the third lecture in the 2012 Voice and Accent Training Lecture Series will be held on April 5 (Thursday) from 7:10 PM to 8:40 PM at the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL).

The topic is "Body Language," and the lecturer is Pragya Vijay. All are welcome.
