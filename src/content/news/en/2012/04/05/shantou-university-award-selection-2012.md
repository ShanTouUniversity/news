It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to honor the most outstanding graduates of STU, the Li Ka Shing Foundation established the Shantou University Award in 2002, presented annually during the graduation ceremony. The following matters regarding the 2012 selection are hereby notified.

Selection criteria: (1) excellent academic performance; (2) outstanding contributions to extracurricular activities at the university; (3) good judgment, wisdom, creativity, and broad interests; (4) dean\'s opinion from the student\'s college/residential college; (5) participation in and interest in public affairs. The award selects one winner and six finalists.

Application materials required: (1) Shantou University Award Application Form (printed double-sided on A4 paper); (2) an article written by the applicant (within 1,200 characters, printed double-sided on A4 paper), describing their academic experience at STU and how these experiences shaped their life goals and aspirations; (3) two letters of recommendation. One letter should detail the student\'s outstanding achievements and activities during their time at STU, or contributions to the off-campus community, including research, creative works, competition awards, or other non-academic activities undertaken as an STU student.
