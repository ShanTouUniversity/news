It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the staff table tennis competition will begin on April 9 (Monday) at 4:30 PM.

Group assignments: Group A — Law/Business Joint Team, Library/Science/News/Research Joint Team, Administration Team, Resource Management; Group B — College of Arts and Letters, College of Science, College of Engineering, Arts Joint Team. Group matches will be held at the Multi-purpose Hall on the sixth floor of the Attached Primary School. To avoid disrupting primary school classes, participating faculty and staff are asked to arrive at the venue by 4:15 PM on the day of the competition; staff may warm up in advance at the Staff Union first-floor table tennis room. Semifinals and finals will be held at the Staff Union first-floor table tennis room.各参赛队请根据《比赛日程表》的安排按时到场参加比赛.
