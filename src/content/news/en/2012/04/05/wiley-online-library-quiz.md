It is reported that the Shantou University Library has launched an online prize quiz for Wiley Online Library. The activity, titled "SPRING into Wiley Online Library," started on March 27 and will run until May 11, giving participants a chance to win a range of prizes.

Faculty members and students who wish to take part can simply answer the questions through the official quiz link. The library noted that users of Internet Explorer should open the link in a new window.

The quiz aims to help the campus community better understand and use the resources available on Wiley Online Library. As a leading international academic publishing platform, it offers journals, books and other scholarly resources across a wide range of disciplines.
