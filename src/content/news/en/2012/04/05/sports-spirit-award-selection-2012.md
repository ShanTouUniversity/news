It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to implement Mr. Li Ka Shing\'s educational philosophy of "having ambition, knowledge, perseverance, and achievements," and to honor outstanding graduates who possess noble sportsmanship and moral character and have made exceptional contributions to STU\'s athletic programs, the Li Ka Shing Foundation has established the STU "Sports Spirit Award."

The award selects one winner and two finalists each year. The winner receives a cash prize of 8,000 RMB and is honored during the annual graduation ceremony; each finalist receives a certificate of honor and a prize of 1,000 RMB. Eligibility criteria include: demonstrating sportsmanship and noble moral character, exhibiting fair play, friendship first, courage, and perseverance in athletic activities; possessing strong leadership and teamwork skills with significant influence in university sports; having represented STU in provincial-level or higher competitions or achieving outstanding results in major intra-university sports events, making exceptional contributions to the university\'s sports programs; and maintaining good academic standing.
