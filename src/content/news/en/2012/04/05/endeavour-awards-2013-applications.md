It is reported that applications for the 2013 Australian Government Endeavour Awards opened on April 1, with a deadline of 11:59 a.m. Australian local time on June 30, 2012.

## About the Endeavour Awards

The Endeavour Awards form part of the Australia Awards initiative and are highly competitive internationally. Administered by the Australian Department of Industry, Innovation, Science, Research and Tertiary Education (DIISRTE), the program offers people from the Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Europe and the Americas opportunities to study, conduct research and pursue professional development in Australia.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applications open | April 1, 2012 |
| Deadline | June 30, 2012, 11:59 a.m. (Australian time) |
| Administrator | DIISRTE, Australia |

Shantou University's International Exchange and Cooperation Office has forwarded the notice from the Australian Embassy in China, encouraging Chinese students and professionals to apply. Further details are available on the official Endeavour Awards website, and inquiries may be directed to Australian Education International (AEI) at the embassy.
