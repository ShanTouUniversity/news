It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the "March 8th -- Charm of Haojiang Dansheng Ecological Park and Seaside Leisure Greenway Stroll" themed activity and the accompanying "Spring Charm" female staff photography and essay competition received enthusiastic responses from female staff. A total of over 40 photos and four essays were submitted by female staff from ten department unions. These works captured wonderful moments and moving memories, vividly recreating the highlights of the outdoor spring outing and showcasing the spirit and风貌 of STU female staff, fully displaying their talents.

After expert review, the photography competition awarded 2 first prizes, 4 second prizes, and 10 third prizes. All four essays received Excellence Awards. Some winning works will be published in a special section of the university newspaper. Winning staff members are requested to collect their prizes from the Staff Union between April 6 and 12.
