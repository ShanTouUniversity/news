It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Student Union will soon undertake renovations of the bulletin boards at the Second and Third Cafeterias and in all residential buildings (Buildings A through G).

All student organizations are requested to remove posters posted by their organizations before 10:00 PM on April 8. Posters left after the deadline will be treated as discarded and removed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
