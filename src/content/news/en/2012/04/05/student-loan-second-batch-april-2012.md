It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, per a notification from the Bank of China Shantou Branch, a total of 134 students have been approved for the second batch of national student loans for the 2011-2012 academic year (the list is available in the attachment).

Students who have received national student loans are requested to log in to the Student Payment Information Query System at http://cwc.stu.edu.cn/StudentChargeQuery/frmStuentChargeQuery.aspx to check their payment status and promptly pay any outstanding balances at the Finance Office. For questions, please contact the Student Aid Work Management Center at 82904749.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
