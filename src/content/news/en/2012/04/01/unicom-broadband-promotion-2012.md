It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that Shantou China Unicom has launched a "Teacher Broadband Discount Activity" for university faculty and staff, valid throughout 2012.

Required materials for application: photocopy of ID card (front and back); work ID (campus card) or teacher certification. Pricing: 330 yuan = 4M broadband for 6 months + 3G experience for 6 months; 642 yuan = 4M broadband for 1 year + 3G experience for 1 year. China Unicom business contact: Mr. Li, 18675429062.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Staff Union)
