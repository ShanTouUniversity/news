It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to the Qingming Festival holiday schedule adjustment, the class schedule for the public welfare course "Sunshine Community Service Learning" (Class 53721) has been adjusted as follows:

Session (1), originally scheduled for Wednesday of Week 6 (April 4) from 2:00 PM to 3:50 PM in Room 204, Building D (instructors: Yao Qin, Lin Xiaoling), will be changed to group face-to-face discussions, with specific times to be coordinated between each group leader and the instructor.

Session (2), originally scheduled for Wednesday of Week 6 (April 4) from 2:00 PM to 3:50 PM in Room 207, Building D (instructors: Lin Bingfeng, Lin Feng, Zhang Zi), has been rescheduled to April 11, 2012, from 2:00 PM to 3:50 PM.

Students enrolled in the course are urged to notify each other.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
