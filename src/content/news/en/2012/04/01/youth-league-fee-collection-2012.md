It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to further improve the collection, management, and use of league fees at the university, and in accordance with the spirit of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League's "Provisions on the Collection, Management and Use of League Fees (Trial)" (团粤发〔2005〕11号), the following requirements are hereby specified.

各学（书）院团委、直属团总支（支部） are urged to fully recognize the importance of league fee collection work, and to treat the collection, management, and use of league fees as an important aspect of strengthening grassroots league organization construction and as an essential guarantee for consolidating the foundation of league work and strengthening league member education and management.各级团组织要加强团员教育管理，切实提高广大团员交纳团费的积极性、主动性和自觉性。各级团组织要把团费收缴、管理和使用摆上重要位置，严格执行相关规定，团费收缴、管理和使用工作要指定专人负责。

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
