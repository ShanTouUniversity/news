It is reported that the Shantou Education Labor Union will hold the "Strong Cup" badminton team invitational tournament for staff of the city's education, culture, and health system in late April to celebrate May Day. Shantou University will form a team for the competition, and recruitment of players from across the university is now underway.

## Event and Team Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | "Strong Cup" Badminton Team Invitational for May Day for Staff of the City's Education, Culture, and Health System |
| Time | Late April 2012 |
| Format | Mixed team (2 men's doubles pairs and 1 mixed doubles pair) |
| Squad size | 8 players per team (6 men, 2 women), plus one team leader and one coach |
| Eligibility | Registered union members currently employed by the unit |
| Sign-up deadline | Before end of work on the morning of April 6 |

Under the competition rules, the team leader should be a head of the unit or labor union; the leader or coach may also serve as a player, occupying a player slot.

Interested faculty and staff can fill out the registration form (see attachment) and send it to the designated email address before the morning deadline on April 6. The university labor union can be reached at 82902983, contact person Ms. Ye. The team formation is jointly organized by the STU Labor Union and the Faculty and Staff Badminton Association.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
