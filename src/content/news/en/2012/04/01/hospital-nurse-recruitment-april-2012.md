It is reported that the Personnel Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University jointly issued a notice that, due to staffing needs at the university hospital, two nurses are being recruited from the general public.

Requirements include: good political and ideological qualities, strong sense of responsibility and professionalism, upright conduct; under 45 years old, in good health, compliant with family planning policies; able to adapt to a 24-hour shift system; vocational school diploma or above in nursing, with practical work experience preferred.

Responsibilities include completing various treatment and nursing tasks, correctly executing medical orders and performing the "three checks and seven confirmations"; strictly abiding by all medical nursing rules and technical operating procedures to prevent medical accidents and errors; promptly replenishing medical supplies and properly保管 various items; participating in nursing duty rotations and strictly observing shift handover times; obeying position reassignment as needed and completing emergency tasks assigned by the hospital.

Employment is on a auxiliary contract basis; personnel records will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow university standards. Applicants must submit the required materials by 5:00 PM on April 8, 2012, via email or mail to the Student Community Management Service Center, Resource Management Office, Shantou University.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office, Resource Management Office)
