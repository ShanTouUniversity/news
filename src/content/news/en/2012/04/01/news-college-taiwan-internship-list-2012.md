It is reported that the College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 summer Taiwan internship and next academic year first semester exchange student admission list has been announced, with a total of 13 students selected.

Exchange students: Zhu Wenxiao, Huang Yachan. Print journalists (8): Ma Lingling, Liao Yanlin, Liu Jiangao, Zhang Xiaoyan, Fu Huiying, Yan Yuan, Li Nianzhi (graduate student), Wu Yujie (print and video). Photographer (1): Lin Wenyin. Videographers (2): Miao Mengqing, Li Yanyu.

> Source: STU OA Notice (College of Journalism and Communication)
