It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to assist in improving the administrative service level of the university, an Administrative Skills Enhancement website has been built and is now available for trial operation.

The website mainly includes sections on secretarial writing, PPT creation, conference management, and official etiquette (some materials on the site are sourced from the internet), and is expected to provide assistance to faculty and students in their work and studies. The website URL is: http://admin.stu.edu.cn (or accessible via the General Office website link). All faculty and students are welcome to provide valuable feedback and suggestions on the website content, style, and sections still under development. Contact: Secretary Section, General Office; Email: o_xzb@stu.edu.cn. The website will continue to improve.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
