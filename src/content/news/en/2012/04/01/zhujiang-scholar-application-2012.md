It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University has issued a notice on the application process for the Pearl River Scholar program.

The office noted that units and individuals interested in applying should refer to the attachments for eligibility criteria, required forms and detailed instructions, and prepare and submit materials accordingly. Specific conditions, procedures and timelines are set out in the attached documents.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
