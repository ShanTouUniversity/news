It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with Document No. 40〔2012〕of the Shantou Municipal Party Committee Office on the selection of Shantou Model Workers and Advanced Collectives, after discussion and research by the Staff Union Committee, Prof. Ma Weiping from the Physical Education Department has been found to meet the basic conditions for the Shantou Model Worker title and is proposed as a preliminary candidate.

Objections may be submitted to the Staff Union before 9:00 AM on April 5 (unit submissions must bear the official seal; individual submissions must include the real name for responsibility purposes). Tel: 82902983, Email: o_xgh@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Staff Union)
