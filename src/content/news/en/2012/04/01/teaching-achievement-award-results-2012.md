It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "Interim Measures for Teaching Achievement Awards of Shantou University," the office organized the 2011 STU Teaching Achievement Award application process in December 2011. Based on recommended projects from various units, the Academic Affairs Office established a Teaching Achievement Award Review Committee and reviewed 20 projects submitted by the main campus.

The proposed results are: one special prize, five first prizes, four second prizes, and five third prizes. The review results are hereby published for public consultation. Units or individuals with objections may submit written comments to the Comprehensive Section of the Academic Affairs Office (Room 401, Xingxi Building) by 5:30 PM on April 9. Tel: 82902406, Email: o_jwc@stu.edu.cn. Unit submissions must bear the official seal; individual submissions must include the real name and contact information.
