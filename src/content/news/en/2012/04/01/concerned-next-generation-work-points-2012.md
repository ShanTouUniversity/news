It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice distributing the "2012 Work Points of the Shantou University Working Committee on Caring for the Next Generation."

The work points have been approved by university Party leadership and are hereby distributed to all college general Party branches and college sub-branches of the Working Committee on Caring for the Next Generation, for earnest implementation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, Party Committee)
