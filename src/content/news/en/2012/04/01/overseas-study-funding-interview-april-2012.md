It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that following the release of the 2012 STU Overseas Study Funding Plan selection announcement, 44 eligible students applied. To ensure an objective and fair selection process, the university formed a review committee comprising representatives from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, university leadership, and college deans. After initial evaluation of application materials, 20 outstanding applicants were selected for interviews.

Interviews will be held on April 17 (Tuesday) at 2:30 PM in ACC Meeting Room 2, using a "leaderless group discussion" format. The main agenda includes: each group member gives a self-introduction in English (no more than 1 minute); leaderless group discussion (10 minutes, with 2 minutes preparation time); and a Q&A session by judges (10 minutes). Discussion topics will be drawn by lottery before the discussion begins. Shortlisted applicants are required to attend a meeting on April 13 at 10:00 AM in Room 726, Building 789, to learn about interview arrangements and draw lots for interview order.
