It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the lecture "The Essence of Design" will be held on April 5, 2012 (Thursday) at 7:30 PM in the New Library Auditorium. The speakers are Zhu Yingchun and Zhao Qing. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
