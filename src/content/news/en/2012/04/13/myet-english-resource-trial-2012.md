It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a notice that the "MyET English Oral Self-Study Resource Database" has been reopened for trial, with the trial period running until July 11, 2012. Access URL: http://cn.myet.com/ContestGate/stu/index.html.

Courses available include: *Peng Meng Hui English Magazine 2011*, *Sky English Classroom 2011*, *Let\'s Speak English Magazine 2010*, *MyET Business English*, *MyET Let\'s Go to America*, *International Conference Speaking English*, *Standard Oral Course*, *Campus English*, *MyET American English on the Go*, *MyET Travel English*, *CET-6 Listening Full Practice Tests*, *Fast Break English Situational Dialogue*, *BEC Vantage Cambridge Business English Intermediate*, and *BEC Higher Cambridge Business English Advanced*.

MyET is based on the "Audio-Lingual Method" and "Communicative Approach," featuring the world\'s first "Automatic Speech Analysis System" (ASASc, Chinese Patent No. 02160031.7), enabling learners to improve listening and speaking skills through the optimal language learning approach of "listening and speaking first, reading and writing later." All readers are welcome to try the database.
