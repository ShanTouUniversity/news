It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to leave taken by Prof. Dong Xuemin of the Arts Education Center, four course sections need to be canceled. The makeup class time is to be determined. Details are as follows:

"Piano Art" (Class No. 52726): Week 8, Monday (April 16), Periods 3–4, originally in STU 202. "Basic Music Theory and Sight-Singing and Ear Training" (Class No. 52728): Week 8, Monday (April 16), Periods 8–9, originally in STU 202. "Piano Art" (Class No. 52729): Week 8, Tuesday (April 17), Periods 3–4, originally in STU 202. "Basic Music Theory and Sight-Singing and Ear Training" (Class No. 52730): Week 8, Tuesday (April 17), Periods 8–9, originally in STU 202. Students enrolled in these courses are asked to inform one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
