It is reported that the Party Committee Propaganda Department and the Social Sciences Department of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that, to deepen the teaching reform of the "Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics" course, respond to the CPC Central Committee\'s "Opinions on Deepening the Learning from Lei Feng Activity," strengthen students\' attention to social moral issues, stimulate their enthusiasm for moral practice, and cultivate their rational实践能力, the university will organize the "Moral Civilization Practice Week" activity.

The target participants are all undergraduates enrolled in the "Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics" course this semester. The activity runs from Weeks 9 to 11 on campus. The activity features a "five-practices" program: do one good deed, correct one violation, copy one aphorism, write one reflection, and participate in one discussion.

Schedule: Week 8 — instructors brief students on arrangements; Week 9 — practice sessions where students go out of the classroom to "do one good deed," "correct one violation," and "copy one aphorism," then write a reflection of at least 800 characters (for a prize essay contest), to be submitted in Week 10; Week 10 — each class holds a discussion on moral civilization and the Lei Feng activity; Week 11 — the Social Sciences Department organizes a prize essay contest on moral civilization practice to recognize outstanding participants.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Propaganda Department, Social Sciences Department)
