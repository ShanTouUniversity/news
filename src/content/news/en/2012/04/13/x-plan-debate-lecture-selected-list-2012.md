It is reported that the Student Affairs Office, the Youth League Committee, and the Student Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to cultivate students\' critical thinking awareness and abilities and enrich campus cultural life in alignment with the humanistic community construction requirements of the student community X-Plan, the announcement for the lecture "The World Through a Debater\'s Eyes — Huang Zhizhong" was released on April 12, and the activity reservation system was opened for all faculty and students.

The activity received enthusiastic participation from the campus community. By 12:00 PM on April 13, a total of 376 faculty and students had registered. Due to seating limitations in the New Library Lecture Hall, the organizers adopted a first-come, first-served principle to determine the list of participants (the detailed list is available in the attachment). Students who successfully reserved a spot are requested to collect their tickets at Room 721, Building 7, between 9:00 AM and 12:30 PM on April 15, presenting their campus card or student ID. Those who were not successful in the reservation may enter the venue after 7:20 PM on the day of the event.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU, Student Affairs Office)
