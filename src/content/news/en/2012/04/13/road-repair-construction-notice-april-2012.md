It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice that due to settlement and unevenness in parts of the sloped road and sidewalk curb behind the Staff Union, repair work is planned to begin on April 14, 2012 (Saturday). The construction period is seven calendar days, with completion expected by April 20. The contracting company is Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd.

During the construction, the contractor will place warning signs in the work area. Pedestrians and vehicles are asked to exercise caution or take a detour. The university apologizes for any inconvenience and thanks everyone for their cooperation.
