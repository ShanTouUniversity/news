It is reported that since the recruitment notice was released on April 6, the FLY training program at Shantou University has drawn an enthusiastic response from students. Following voluntary applications, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Student Union have confirmed and announced the list of 25 participants for the second cohort of the "Zhanyi" (Spread Wings) program.

## About the Program

According to the announcement, the FLY training program aims to develop and enhance young students' leadership abilities, promoting their growth in knowledge, skills and experience so that, while caring for society and others, they can become future leaders with a sense of social responsibility across all walks of life. The program consists of three tiers tailored to different participants:

| Track | Level |
| --- | --- |
| Fengyi (Enrich Wings) | Elementary |
| Shiyi (Try Wings) | Intermediate |
| Zhanyi (Spread Wings) | Advanced |

## Selected Participants

All 25 members of this Zhanyi cohort were selected through voluntary application, comprising presidents of student organizations, outstanding department heads, and excellent graduates who completed the intermediate Shiyi track. The selected participants are (in announcement order):

Liu J, Zhang Y, Zhou MY, Zhao ZX, Liang JH, Huang JH, Zheng LC, Zeng CX, Fan HS, Chen WA, He WX, Ke YL, Hu YY, Zheng LM, Xie QY, Chen XC, Luo C, Wu RZ, Ding QH, Li A, Li JJ, Li XM, Wang MJ, Shi QH, Chen WC.
