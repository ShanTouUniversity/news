It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued the 2012 Issue No. 1 (Total No. 23) of the STU Research and Innovation Bulletin. This issue includes: project application status reports for 2012, humanities and social science award application reports, and project approval and completion reports; STU\'s chemistry and clinical medicine disciplines entering the ESI global top 1%, the STU Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation Research Institute approved as a provincial key research base for humanities and social sciences in higher education, the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design winning the "Contribution to Chinese Design Cause Award," and the Analytical Testing Center passing the provincial metrology certification supervision review; visits from leaders of the General Administration of Sport of China to the Physical Education Department, a visit from Mr. Jiang Minghan of Queen\'s University Development Office (Canada), an academic exchange by Prof. Michael O\'Keeffe of Arizona State University (USA), the appointment ceremony and first academic lecture by renowned German sinologist Prof. Wolfgang Kubin, a visit to Technion-Israel Institute of Technology by Zhou Kaixuan and Pei Hua, and participation of Prof. Pei Hua in an international advisory committee meeting at Cheng Kung University (Taiwan); and other research activities.
