It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that in the semifinals held the previous afternoon, the Administration Team and the Resource Management Team advanced to the championship match with scores of 3:2 and 3:1, respectively.

The finals will be held at 4:30 PM today in the Staff Union first-floor table tennis room. Matchups: Championship — Administration Team vs. Resource Management Team; Third-place match — College of Engineering vs. College of Science. All table tennis enthusiasts are welcome to come and watch.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Staff Union)
