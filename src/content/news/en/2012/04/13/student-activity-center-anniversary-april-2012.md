It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the STU Student Activity Center will hold its first anniversary celebration on the evening of April 15, 2012, from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM. The center will be open free of charge to all faculty and students throughout the day on April 15. All faculty and students are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
