It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that 24 students who were selected for the 2012 summer Hong Kong internship program will complete their internships at 14 host organizations in Hong Kong. The internship period runs from July 5 to August 1, 2012, spanning 28 days.

Thirteen students from the Law Department and eleven from the Public Administration Department will be placed at their respective assigned organizations. Detailed placement arrangements are available in the attachment.
