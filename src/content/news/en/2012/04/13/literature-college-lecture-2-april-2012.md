It is reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the second academic lecture of the 9th Student Academic Month will be held on April 18, 2012 (Wednesday) at 3:00 PM in Lecture Hall E201.

The lecture topic is "Language and Culture Issues in English Learning." The speaker is Prof. Zhu Wang. All faculty and students are cordially invited to attend.
