It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office and the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that the first swimming class this semester is a theory class, and all students enrolled in swimming are required to attend. Class times will be reassigned during this session.

Because each time slot has a limit on the number of students, participants are encouraged to choose staggered time slots to avoid everyone selecting the same period (e.g., Periods 8–9). Time: April 22 (Sunday), 7:00 PM. Venue: Lecture Hall E201. Note: The course PowerPoint has been uploaded to the virtual classroom; please read it in advance. The second swimming class on April 24 will include a physical fitness test, so students are asked to prepare accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office, Physical Education Department)
