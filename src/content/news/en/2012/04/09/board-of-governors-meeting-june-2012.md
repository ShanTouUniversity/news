It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a situation bulletin (2012 No. 1) that the university will hold the 3rd meeting of the 8th Board of Governors on June 28–29, 2012. During this period, the STU 2012 graduation ceremony and other major events will be held.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
