It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice that an all-staff meeting will be held on April 11 (Wednesday) from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM in Lecture Hall E101, Building E. All faculty and staff are requested to attend on time. For those unable to attend due to prior commitments, please send an email to the college office at o_lxyb@stu.edu.cn to request leave before 5:00 PM on April 10.
