It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to improve the software design and development abilities of STU students, the university will organize students to participate in the 1st "China Software Cup" College Student Software Design Competition.

The "China Software Cup" evolved from the "Jiangsu Software Cup" College Student Software Design Competition initiated by Jiangsu Province in 2009, a public-welfare competition for university students. Building on two editions of experience, the competition was upgraded in 2012 to a national event co-hosted by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the Ministry of Education, and the Jiangsu Provincial People's Government. The competition aims to build a free, open, and innovative software design platform, effectively connecting talent cultivation with the needs of the emerging information industry, accelerating the training of high-end software professionals, and enhancing the independent innovation capacity of the industry.

All undergraduate and graduate students at STU are eligible to participate. Registration is by project team, with one faculty advisor and up to three students per team. Team members may be freely assembled. Advisors must be full-time STU faculty. Cross-grade, cross-major, and cross-college teams are encouraged.
