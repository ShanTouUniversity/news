It is reported that the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center of Shantou University's Student Affairs Office will hold a psychodrama competition during the seventh Mental Health Awareness Month. Registration and script submissions are open to all undergraduates and postgraduates from April 7 to April 30.

## About the Event

Participants will create psychodramas based on common mental health issues among college students. The competition aims to boost student engagement in mental health education, spread related knowledge across campus and bring new forms to mental health education.

## Work Requirements

Entries must be original works by students, healthy in spirit and clear in theme, reflecting psychological knowledge and counseling skills with vivid plots and memorable characters. Acceptable formats include musicals, stage plays, sketches, pantomime and song-and-dance dramas, each running about 10 to 15 minutes. Dialogue or narration should be mainly in Mandarin, with English or dialects used where appropriate. Teams must prepare their own props; finalists will receive partial subsidies from the organizer.

## Schedule and Awards

| Stage | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Registration & script submission | April 7 – April 30 |
| Preliminary round (live performance) | May 12 – 13 |
| Final (live performance) | May 25 |

| Prize | Quota & Reward |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | 1 team: RMB 1,000 + certificate |
| Second prize | 2 teams: RMB 600 + certificate |
| Third prize | 3 teams: RMB 300 + certificate |

Paper scripts may be submitted to Room 735 at the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center, while electronic submissions and inquiries can be sent to o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn. The center can also be reached by phone at 82903380.
