It is reported that the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center of Shantou University's Student Affairs Office will hold a psychodrama competition for students during the seventh Mental Health Awareness Month. The competition aims to strengthen students' initiative and enthusiasm for mental health education, spread mental health knowledge on campus, enrich the content of that education and introduce new formats for it.

## Theme and Eligibility

Entries must be psychodramas based on mental health issues commonly encountered by college students. The competition is open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students enrolled at Shantou University.

## Entry Requirements

Entries must be original works by students, healthy in spirit and clear in theme, reflecting psychological knowledge and counseling skills, with vivid plots, memorable characters and aesthetically engaging performances. Accepted formats include musicals, stage plays, short sketches, pantomime and song-and-dance dramas, each running about 10 to 15 minutes. Dialogue and narration should be mainly in Mandarin, with English or dialects used where appropriate. Teams must prepare their own props; teams reaching the final will receive a subsidy from the organiser at an appropriate proportion of their costs.
