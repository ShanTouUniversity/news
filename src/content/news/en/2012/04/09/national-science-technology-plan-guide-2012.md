It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2013 863 Program and Supporting Program candidate project collection guidelines will be gradually released by field in the near future. The 863 Program focuses on supporting 10 high-tech fields including information technology, while the National Science and Technology Supporting Program focuses on 11 fields including energy. Currently, guidelines for 7 high-tech fields have been released. Interested parties should carefully study the guidelines and fill out project (subproject) recommendation forms accordingly.

Those intending to apply should complete the attached intent form and send it to yxhu@stu.edu.cn within one week of the guideline release. Subsequent guideline releases will be continuously updated in this notice. Contact: Hu Yunxiang, Zhu Changsheng; Tel: 82902813.
