It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology's \"Notice on Organizing the Application for the Second Batch of Guangdong Strategic Emerging Industry Core Technology Breakthrough Projects\" (Yue Ke Han Gui Hua Zi〔2012〕No. 426).

Application requirements: projects must comply with the national and provincial strategic emerging industry development \"12th Five-Year Plan\" and the project application guidelines; applicant organizations must be at the leading edge of their industry with solid research capabilities and core teams; organizations are encouraged to form industry technology alliances through provincial-ministry industry-academia-research cooperation, international science and technology cooperation, and innovation research team introduction; priority is given to organizations with good research conditions for R&D production and integration of domestic and international innovation resources, including provincial-level or above key laboratories and engineering centers supporting industrial development. The core technology of the applied project must provide relevant novelty search certificates.
