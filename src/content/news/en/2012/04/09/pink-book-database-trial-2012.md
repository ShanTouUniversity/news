It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a notice that the Pink Book Database is now available for trial. The Pink Book Database is a comprehensive academic resource hub for humanities and social sciences, built on large-scale continuous series of pink books and integrated with other domestic and international related information. It contains seven sub-databases covering over 200 themes and includes economic and social development reports from across the country spanning more than a decade, covering fields such as economics, society, politics, culture, education, and international affairs.

The seven sub-databases are: China Economics Database, China Society Database, China Competitiveness Database, World Economy and International Politics Database, China Region Database, China Industry and Enterprise Database, and China Culture and Media Database. The trial period runs until August 1, 2012. Access link: http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=347 (please use Internet Explorer and open the link in a new window). All readers are welcome to make use of the database. Trial evaluations and feedback should be sent to the Library Subject Services Department, Tel: 3247, Email: lqxie@stu.edu.cn.
