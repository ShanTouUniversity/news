It is reported that the "Three Styles" Construction Office of Shantou University recently issued Study Atmosphere Inspection Report No. 195. During the 6th week of the spring semester of the 2011–2012 academic year, members of the study atmosphere inspection group inspected teaching buildings. The inspection results are as follows:

1. Inspection time: March 31, 2012 (Saturday) at 8:00 AM; location: Building E Teaching Building; number of students arriving late (within 5 minutes): 6; number of students arriving late (over 5 minutes): 3.

2. Inspection time: April 5, 2012 (Thursday) at 2:00 PM; location: Building E Teaching Building; number of students arriving late (within 5 minutes): 6.

The list of students who failed to strictly observe school discipline and rules has been forwarded to all colleges. Each college (residential college) is requested to criticize and educate the relevant students and to persistently work on building a good study atmosphere.

> Source: STU OA Notice ("Three Styles" Construction Office, STU)
