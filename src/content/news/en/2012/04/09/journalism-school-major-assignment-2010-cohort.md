It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication on April 9 publicized the major assignment list for students of the Class of 2010.

The School developed the Assignment Plan for the 2010 Cohort and organized students to fill in the unified Major Preference Form. Following deliberation by the Teaching Committee and approval by the School's Party and administrative leadership, the assignment was completed and the final list was posted for all students.

The publicity period runs from April 9 to 12:00 noon on April 13. Feedback from students will be accepted during this period in writing, in person, or by phone, provided the correspondent signs with their real name. Contact: 82904068, ylin@stu.edu.cn. After considering feedback, the School will finalize and publish the confirmed assignment list. The detailed list is available in the attachment.
