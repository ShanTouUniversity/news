It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 3rd meeting of the 8th Board of Governors will be held on June 28–29. During this period, major events including the board meeting and the graduation ceremony will take place, and important guests will be invited for various activities.

During this period, the University Auditorium, Science Lecture Hall, Library Lecture Hall, Gymnasium, and Academic Exchange Service Center and related venues and facilities will be reserved exclusively for Board of Governors activities and will no longer be available for other events. All units are requested to take note.
