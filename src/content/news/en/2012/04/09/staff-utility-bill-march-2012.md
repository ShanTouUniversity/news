It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing the March 2012 utility bills for faculty and staff. The current meter reading was taken on April 1, with the previous reading on March 1. All faculty and staff are asked to log in to the STU Utility Bill Management System (http://power.stu.edu.cn) to check their bills using their campus network accounts.

For any questions or discrepancies, please visit the Water and Electricity Center between April 10 and 13 for verification. Tel: 2489. If no objections are raised by the deadline, the Finance Office will deduct the amount from each individual's April 2012 salary account. Those without a salary account should pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
