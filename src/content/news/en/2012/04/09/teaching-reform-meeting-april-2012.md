It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently issued a notice that a teaching reform work meeting will be held on April 11, 2012 (Wednesday) at 2:30 PM in the Science Report Hall. All faculty members are requested to attend on time. Faculty members who have classes at that time are asked to send an email to the college office to report their absence.
