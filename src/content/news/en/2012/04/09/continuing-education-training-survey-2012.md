It is reported that the Continuing Education Center issued a notice on April 9, 2012, to conduct a survey on continuing education training needs for 2012.

To make training courses more targeted and practical, the center invites faculty members to provide suggestions. Needs will be analyzed in conjunction with the university’s actual conditions to formulate the annual training plan. Faculty members are asked to complete the *2012 Training Needs Survey Form* (see attachment) and send it to stu_training@stu.edu.cn or fill it out online at http://training.stu.edu.cn by April 13, 2012.

> ⚠️ The survey form is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office, Continuing Education Center)
