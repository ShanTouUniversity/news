It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing that the Mental Health Education and Counseling Center would organize a student psychological drama competition as part of the Seventh Mental Health Awareness Month. According to the notice, the competition was designed to strengthen students' initiative and enthusiasm for mental health education, promote the spread of mental health knowledge on campus, enrich the content of such education and introduce new formats for it.

The competition invited entries drawn from mental health issues commonly encountered by college students. It was open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students enrolled at Shantou University.

Entries were required to be original works by students, sound in outlook and clear in theme, and to reflect psychological knowledge and counseling techniques through vivid plots, memorable characters and aesthetically engaging performances. Accepted formats included musicals, stage plays, short sketches, pantomime and song-and-dance dramas, with each piece running about 10 to 15 minutes. Dialogue and narration were to be delivered mainly in Mandarin, with English or dialects used where appropriate. Teams were responsible for preparing their own props, and those advancing to the final round would receive a proportional subsidy from the organizer.

The competition was arranged in three stages, as shown below.

| Stage | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Registration and script submission | April 7 – April 30 |
| Preliminary round (live performance) | May 12 – May 13 |
| Final round | Late May |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
