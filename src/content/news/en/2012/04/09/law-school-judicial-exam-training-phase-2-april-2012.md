It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the second phase of the National Unified Judicial Examination Training Program, organized by the Career Development Center of the Law School, will continue on April 14 (Saturday). The civil and criminal law preparatory class will resume. Specific course schedules are available in the timetable. Students who have registered for the training are asked to attend on time with their lecture passes.
