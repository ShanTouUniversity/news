It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the National 3D Competition (formally the "National 3D Digital Innovation Design Competition") is a newly emerging competition launched against the backdrop of the nation's vigorous push for independent innovation and the transition from a "manufacturing powerhouse" to a "creative powerhouse." The competition reflects the requirements of technological progress and industrial upgrading, marking a new development in the integration of science and technology competitions with nationwide innovation practice.

The competition is divided into "College Student Group," "Professional Group," and "Industry Group." STU students must register under the organization of a faculty advisor as an institutional team; individual student registration is not accepted. The college student team consists of student members (no more than 4) and faculty advisors (no more than 2). Each team must have a unique name. A maximum of 10 teams per college/department may advance to the regional semifinals, and the same faculty advisor may guide multiple teams to the regional semifinals and the national finals.
