It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that since 2009, the STU Youth Volunteer "Firefly" Love Foundation has established the "Xue Hai Ying Guang" (Sea of Learning, Firefly Light) scholarship program, accepting applications from financially disadvantaged students who have not received other forms of financial aid or scholarships during the academic year and who demonstrate good moral character and academic performance.

With the approval of the foundation council, the 2012 STU Youth Volunteer "Firefly" Love Foundation "Xue Hai Ying Guang" scholarship is now open to all students. The scholarship amount is 500 RMB per recipient, with approximately 20 spots available. Eligibility criteria include: no other financial aid or scholarship received during the current academic year (2011–2012); family financial difficulties and simple living; good academic performance and moral character; passion for university life and active participation in various volunteer activities organized by the university. Applications are accepted from now until 11:00 PM on April 13. Both electronic and paper copies of materials are required.
