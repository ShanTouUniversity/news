It is reported that the Student Community Management Service Center of the Resource Management Office at Shantou University recently issued a notice reminding students that summer is approaching. Based on past years' experience, fan repair requests surge in April and May. Due to the long repair time for fans, some repair requests cannot be completed on time.

Students are urged to promptly inspect the fans in their dormitories. If any malfunction is found, please report it through the online repair system (http://repair.stu.edu.cn) as soon as possible to avoid a集中 rush of repair requests that could disrupt normal study and living. If the fan exhibits abnormal rotation, unusual noises, or strange odors during use, please stop using it immediately and report via the online system to prevent fires or other accidents.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
