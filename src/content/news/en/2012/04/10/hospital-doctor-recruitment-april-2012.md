It is reported that the Personnel Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that, due to the needs of the university hospital, one medical doctor position is now open for public recruitment.

Basic eligibility requirements: good political and ideological quality, strong sense of responsibility, upright conduct, rigorous work style, teamwork spirit, and dedication; under 45 years of age, in good health, compliant with family planning policies; able to adapt to a 24-hour shift system; college diploma or above in a medical field, with practical work experience preferred. Responsibilities include: strictly observing physician regulations and medical technical operating procedures to prevent medical errors; completing all medical tasks including outpatient, emergency, duty, and home visit work; handling initial diagnoses of infectious diseases and epidemic reporting, as well as health care duties; and being available for increased night shifts during staffing shortages. Employment is on a auxiliary contract basis; personnel records will not be transferred to STU, and compensation follows university standards.

Applicants must submit their resume, photocopies of ID card, family planning certificate, academic credentials, practice certificate, and other relevant documents to the STU Hospital, Resource Management Office, by 5:00 PM on April 20, 2012, via email or mail. Qualified candidates will be notified for interview.
