It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently issued a notice that from January 7 to 29, 2012, the college collaborated with the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) to host the STU-MIT Exchange Program, sending 10 outstanding engineering students to the U.S. for a three-week exchange. The students participated in the "Gordon Engineering Leadership" program and the "Undergraduate Practical Training Program," observing and experiencing the study and life at MIT.

To help students better understand the STU-MIT Exchange Program and inspire self-improvement, the college will hold a series of events titled "思源笃行——MIT之旅" (Source and Action — MIT Journey) from April 12 to 15. Activities include: an photo exhibition (April 12–14 at Jin San Jiao/Golden Triangle) covering overseas study, daily life, and American culture; and a sharing session (April 13, 7:30–10:00 PM, New Library Lecture Hall) where the 10 students who studied at MIT will share their experiences and insights.
