It is reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Prof. Yu Xiande from Guangdong University of Foreign Studies will deliver a lecture at STU in the near future.

The lecture topic is "Hardship Tempering and Personality Sublimation — An Interpretation of Yan Geling\'s Hardship Writing." Prof. Yu Xiande is a professor at Guangdong University of Foreign Studies, specializing in literary research and criticism. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
