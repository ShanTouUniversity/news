It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that due to business trips by Prof. Liu Guanghui and Prof. Lin Weijie of the Arts Education Center, two courses need to be rescheduled. The details are as follows:

"Clarinet Performance" (Class No. 52849): originally scheduled for Wednesday of Week 7 (April 11), Periods A–B in STU 203; makeup class in Weeks 8–9, Wednesday, Period C, in STU 203. "Western Classical Music Appreciation" (Class No. 52453): originally scheduled for Wednesday of Week 7 (April 11), Periods A–B in Lecture Hall 3; makeup class in Weeks 8–9, Wednesday, Period C, in Lecture Hall 3. Students enrolled in these courses are asked to inform one another.
