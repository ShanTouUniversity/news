It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to continue providing high-quality artistic appreciation opportunities to faculty and students, the "Colorful Art Season 2012 Spring," supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, has specially invited renowned Hong Kong sand artist Mr. Hai Chao to STU to introduce sand art and deliver a live performance. Integrating years of theater experience, the short piece "Qing Sha" (Passion in Sand) will be performed in collaboration with student performers.

Performance time: April 14, 2012 (Saturday), 7:30 PM. Venue: Science Lecture Hall. Guest artist: Mr. Hai Chao.

Ticket collection: (1) Art enthusiast ticket time: April 12, 9:30–11:30 AM and 2:30–5:00 PM; (2) College ticket collection times as specified in the original notice. Tickets are distributed on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design, Arts Education Center)
