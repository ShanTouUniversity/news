It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Engineering Research Academic Forum (No. 122) will be held on April 11, 2012 (Wednesday) from 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM in the Academic Exchange Room of the College of Engineering.

The speaker is Dr. Ye Weilin from the School of Electronic Science and Engineering, Jilin University, specializing in circuits and systems. The lecture topic is "Development of Infrared Gas Detector for Mines." Faculty and students from the Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering and all interested students are welcome to attend.
