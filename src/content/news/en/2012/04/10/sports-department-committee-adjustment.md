It is reported that the Sports Department of Shantou University recently adjusted the composition of its departmental affairs committee following personnel changes within the department. The adjustment was confirmed on April 10.

## Members of the Reshuffled Committee

| Position | Name |
| -------- | ---- |
| Director | Chen Jian |
| Committee members (ordered by stroke count of surnames) | Ma Weiping, Li Yuejin, Zou Xuandong, Xu Wen |
| Secretary | Li Shengzhi |

According to the announcement, the adjustment involves six staff members in total. Chen Jian takes the post of committee director, while Ma Weiping, Li Yuejin, Zou Xuandong and Xu Wen serve as committee members, with the list ordered by the stroke count of their surnames. Li Shengzhi serves as secretary of the committee.

The affairs committee serves as an internal body for deliberation and administration within the Sports Department. Redefining its membership after the personnel changes is expected to help sustain the department's day-to-day operations.
