It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center at Shantou University recently issued a notice that renowned Hong Kong sand artist Mr. Hai Chao will visit STU to provide hands-on sand art instruction.

The workshop will be held on April 15 (Sunday), 2:00–6:00 PM, in Music Hall 101, first floor, Arts Education Center. Hai Chao (Full Theater) is a co-founder and artistic director of Full Theater, an international multimedia production company established in 2004 with Meg Blue Olena (Vancouver). The company has been active in the UK, Canada, Singapore, Thailand, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Macau, and Hong Kong. Full Theater\'s mission is to create high-quality multimedia productions that encourage creativity, aesthetics, and inspiration, blending Eastern and Western traditions to produce challenging, experimental, and innovative performances using sand art, movement, music, poetry, masks, puppets, visual art, and video.

Registration: open to all STU students regardless of major. Interested students should send their name, gender, college, year, and contact mobile number to o_ysjyzx@stu.edu.cn before 9:00 AM on April 11. Limited to 30 participants, selected on a first-come, first-served basis. Selected participants will be contacted by phone. Please bring your favorite CD for sharing and arrive 10 minutes early.
