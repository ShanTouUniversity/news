It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the National Natural Science Foundation of China has released the 2012 work progress schedule for inter-organization projects. Faculty members with cooperative relationships with foreign institutions are asked to pay attention to the scheduling and relevant notices from the foundation and the university to ensure timely progress of related work.
