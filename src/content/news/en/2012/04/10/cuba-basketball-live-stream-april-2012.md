It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the CUBA (Chinese University Basketball Association) Southeast Region games kicked off on April 7 in Nanchang, Jiangxi Province. The STU "Wan Bian Jin Gang" (Everchanging Guardians) basketball team currently has a record of 1 win and 1 loss, having defeated Hainan University 82:67 and lost to Nanjing Aerospace University 71:79.

Tonight at 8:30 PM, CNTV (China Network Television) Sports Channel will live stream the Group D group stage match: STU Wan Bian Jin Gang vs. China University of Mining and Technology. All faculty and students are welcome to watch.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
