It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that public welfare courses are an important part of the university\'s talent cultivation model reform. Teachers of public welfare courses must complete training and undertake the course before being eligible to teach. To better implement and promote public welfare courses, the university will continue to host the third phase of the Public Welfare Course Training Program.

Two types of instructors are recognized: academic-type teachers must hold a graduate degree or above (or intermediate professional title or above), be passionate about public welfare activities, and have experience leading students in planning and organizing activities; practice-type teachers must hold a bachelor\'s degree or above, have at least three years of experience in public welfare work, possess rich experience in public welfare activities, and be administrative or support staff passionate about public welfare. Since 2011, graduate students have been eligible to serve as teaching assistants for public welfare courses.
