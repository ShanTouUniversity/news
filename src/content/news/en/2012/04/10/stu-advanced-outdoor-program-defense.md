It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued an announcement to all faculty and students on April 10, 2012, stating that an open defense for the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Development Program would be held.

The Advanced Outdoor Development Program is one of STU's distinctive extracurricular development initiatives. Participating teams are required to present their project plans and preparations through an open defense stage. The holding of this defense marked a key phase in the selection process for this year's program.

Details such as the specific time and venue of the defense were subject to the detailed plan released by the Student Affairs Office. The university welcomed faculty and students across campus to follow and attend the event.
