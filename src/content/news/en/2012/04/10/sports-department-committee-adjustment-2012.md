It is reported that, following personnel changes within the department, the Physical Education Department of Shantou University issued a notice on April 10 adjusting the membership of its departmental affairs committee.

The reshuffled committee comprises six people:

| Position | Name |
| -------- | ---- |
| Director | Chen Jian |
| Members (ordered by stroke count of surnames) | Ma Weiping, Li Yuejin, Zou Xuandong, Xu Wen |
| Secretary | Li Shengzhi |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
