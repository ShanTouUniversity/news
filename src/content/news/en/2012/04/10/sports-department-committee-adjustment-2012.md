It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to personnel changes in the department, the members of the Physical Education Departmental Affairs Committee have been adjusted. The new members are as follows: Director Chen Jian; Committee members (listed alphabetically by Chinese surname): Ma Weiping, Li Yuejin, Zou Xuandong, Xu Wen; Secretary Li Shengzhi.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
