It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, due to venue constraints, the group stage venue for the staff table tennis team competition has been adjusted effective April 10. Group A remains at the Multi-purpose Hall, sixth floor, Attached Primary School; Group B has been moved to the Staff Union first-floor table tennis room.

Because playing a full five-game match takes considerably longer, the competition format has been changed from best-of-five games (11 points) to best-of-three games (11 points). All participating players are asked to inform one another.
