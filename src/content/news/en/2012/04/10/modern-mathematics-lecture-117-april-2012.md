It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the "Approaching Modern Mathematics" lecture series (No. 117) will be held on April 11, 2012 (Wednesday) at 10:00 AM in Room 208, Building E.

The speaker is Dr. Sun Shuhao, who holds a Ph.D. from the University of Hawaii, USA, is a Research Fellow at the University of Sydney, Australia, and also holds a Biomedical Ph.D. from RMIT University, Australia. The lecture topic is "Applications of Mathematics." All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
