It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that after the release of the notice on student selection for the Lady Gaga concert in Hong Kong, a total of 1,211 faculty and students registered, of whom 1,170 met the eligibility criteria.

The Student Affairs Office determined the 10 principal candidates and 8 alternates through a random lottery system. The list is hereby published.
