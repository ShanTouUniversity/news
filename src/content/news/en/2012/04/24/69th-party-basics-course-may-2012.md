It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that the 69th session of the Basic Party Knowledge Course will commence in May. All general party branches and directly affiliated party branches are requested to carry out recommendation, preliminary review, and reporting work (quota allocation and participant registration forms are in the attachment).

Candidates submitted by each unit must have submitted a party membership application for at least one year and been listed as a key development candidate. The completed registration form, signed by the general party branch or directly affiliated party branch and stamped with the official seal, must be submitted to the Party School Office (Room 401, Xingdong Building) by May 4, 2012. A summary list of participants should also be emailed to o_dxb@stu.edu.cn. Admission notices will be issued after review by the Party School Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, Party Committee)
