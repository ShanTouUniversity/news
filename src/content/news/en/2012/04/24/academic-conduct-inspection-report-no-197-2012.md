It is reported that the "Three Styles Construction Office" of Shantou University recently issued a notice that during the 8th week of the Spring 2011–2012 semester, members of the academic conduct inspection group conducted inspections of teaching buildings. The findings are reported as follows:

Inspection 1: April 17, 2012 (Tuesday), 8:00 AM, Building E. Number of students arriving late (within 5 minutes): 1. Number of students arriving late (more than 5 minutes): 1.

Inspection 2: April 19, 2012 (Thursday), 7:00 PM, Room 406, Building E. Number of students arriving late (more than 5 minutes): 14.

The list of students who failed to properly observe school discipline and regulations is circulated to all colleges. Each college (residential college) is requested to conduct批评教育 for the relevant students and persistently strengthen academic conduct construction.

> Source: STU OA Notice ("Three Styles Construction Office")
