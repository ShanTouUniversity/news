It is reported that the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that from March 29 to April 6, 2012, the school dispatched Professor Ling Xuemin and two graduate students, Wang Jinsheng (Jason) and Xu Jiaming, to Myanmar to provide full coverage of the country's parliamentary by-election. The team returned with extensive photos, texts, and video material, which were widely reposted by media outlets and generated significant response.

This Friday (April 27), the three team members and a representative from the school's support group will share their experiences and impressions from the Myanmar trip at the Science Lecture Hall. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

Time: April 27, 2012, 4:00–6:00 PM. Venue: Science Lecture Hall.
