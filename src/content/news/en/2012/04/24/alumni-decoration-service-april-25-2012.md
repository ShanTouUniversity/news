It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that Dajian Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. (an alumni company) will be on campus on April 25, 2012 (Wednesday) from 2:30 to 5:00 PM to provide decoration consultation services and special discounts for faculty members. Venue: in front of the Staff Union office. All faculty members are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Staff Union)
