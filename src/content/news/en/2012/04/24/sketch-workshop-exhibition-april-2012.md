It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Sketch Modeling Workshop Exhibition will be held from April 23 to 29, 2012, in the first-floor exhibition hall of the college.

Workshop instructors: Wang Zhenbin and Li Ning from the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts. Organizer: Cheung Kong College of Art and Design. All faculty and students are welcome to visit.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
