It is reported that the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that new media scholar and lecturer Wei Wuhui from the School of Media and Design at Shanghai Jiao Tong University will deliver two public lectures at STU on April 27 and 28.

Lecture 1: The Essential Characteristics of the Digital Economy and Media Products. Time: April 27, 7:30–9:30 PM. Venue: E-Jie 202. Lecture 2: The Future of the Digital Economy — Cloud Computing, IoT, and Mobile Internet. Time: April 28, 9:00–11:00 AM. Venue: ACC Room 3.

Wei Wuhui is a lecturer at the School of Media and Design, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and a Nan Du Visiting Scholar at Sun Yat-sen University. He is a well-known independent TMT blogger (weiwuhui.com). He entered the internet industry in late 1999 and has held positions including Operations Manager, Information Director, and COO at various internet companies. Since 2006, he has been teaching at the university level, offering courses such as "Introduction to New Media," "Internet Practice," and "Social Marketing." He was awarded "Best Web 2.0 Expert of 2007" and "Best Expert Award 2008–2009" by iResearch.

Both lectures are included in the STU Continuing Education curriculum and carry continuing education credits. Students are welcome to register via training.stu.edu.cn using their campus network account and password.
