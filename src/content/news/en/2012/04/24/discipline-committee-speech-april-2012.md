It is reported that the Disciplinary Inspection Commission of the Communist Party of China at Shantou University recently issued a notice circulates the speech by Luo Nianchao, Deputy Secretary of the University Party Committee and Secretary of the Discipline Inspection Commission, delivered at the 4th Session of the 3rd (Expanded) Meeting of the CPC Shantou University Discipline Inspection Commission (Document No. 汕大纪发〔2012〕01号). The document is addressed to the Medical College Party Committee, all general party branches of the main campus, and directly affiliated party branches, requesting each unit to organically combine the study with actual work and implement the directives.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Disciplinary Inspection Commission, CPC Shantou University)
