It is reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Dr. Amy Lee Ai, Associate Dean for Research and Professor in the College of Social Work at Florida State University, will deliver a lecture on April 27, 2012 (Saturday) from 7:00 to 9:00 PM in ACC Room 3.

Lecture topic: Research on Spirituality and Wellbeing: Advances and Pitfalls. Speaker profile: Dr. Amy Lee Ai holds a Ph.D. in Psychology and Social Work from the University of Michigan and is a Fellow of the American Psychological Association. She has received numerous research grants and awards and has published extensively in such international journals as Multiple Intelligences, The Counseling Psychologist, and Journal of Counseling Psychology.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
