It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding the call for entries for the Shantou Municipal Government Portal homepage and logo (Logo) design contest.

The Shantou Municipal Government Portal (http://www.shantou.gov.cn) is an important platform for government departments to carry out information disclosure, online services, and internet-based public engagement, and serves as a key carrier of the city government's image. The Shantou Municipal Government invites STU faculty and students to actively submit entries and participate in Shantou's informatization construction.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
