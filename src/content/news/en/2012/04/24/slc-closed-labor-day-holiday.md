It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently announced that, in accordance with the university administration office's unified arrangements for the Labor Day holiday, the Student Learning Center (SLC) will suspend its opening during the holiday.

## Adjusted Schedule

The adjusted schedule during the holiday is shown in the table below:

| Date | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| April 28 (Saturday) | Hosts the academic consultation service originally set for April 30 |
| April 29 (Sunday) – May 1 (Tuesday) | Center closed to the public |
| From May 2 | Normal operations resume |

The academic consultation service originally scheduled for Monday, April 30 has been moved forward to Saturday, April 28. Teachers and students who wish to use these services are advised to plan their visits accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
