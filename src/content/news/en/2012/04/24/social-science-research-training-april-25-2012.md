It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to improve students' abilities in social science research design and investigation, and to enhance their academic writing skills, the school has decided to include "Training on Social Science Research Design and Academic Writing Techniques" as part of the 14th Academic Month and the Research Proposal Design Competition activities.

The event has invited Professor Deng Jian Guang, who has extensive experience in the field, to share insights on research design, field investigation, and academic writing techniques, and to answer questions from students.

Time: April 25, 2012 (Wednesday), 3:00–5:30 PM. Venue: Room 213, Law School. Faculty and students of the Law School are welcome to attend.
