It is reported that the Science College of Shantou University recently issued document 汕大理发〔2012〕04号, announcing the establishment of undergraduate teaching reform working groups for each discipline to better implement teaching reform and promote the development of advanced undergraduate education at the college.

Working group members: Mathematics — Yang Shou Zhi, Yang Zhong Qiang, Lin Fu Rong, Ye Rui Song, Huang Wen Xue, Xu Fei; Physics — Li Shao Hui, Wang Jiang Yong, Su Jian Xin, Qiu Gui Ming, Huang XX, Zhu Wei An; Chemistry — Tong Qing Xiao, Fang Yi Wen, Yin Ye Gao, Zhang Xin, Gao Wen Hua, Lu Fu Shen, Chen Han Jia; Biology — Zhang Yue Ling, Chen Mei Zhen, Liu Zhu, Liu Wen Hua, Wei Chi Ju, Liu Yang, Chen Jie Hui, Xiong Ji Xian.

The college also released the April–May work schedule for teaching reform projects: April 24–28, first meeting of each department'\''s reform core team to discuss program positioning and characteristics and preliminarily establish training objectives; May 2–11, draft each major'\''s analysis report; May 14 onward, subsequent work.
