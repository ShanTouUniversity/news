It is reported that the Publicity Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that the Party Committee Center Group Study Session will be held on April 25, 2012 (Wednesday) at 3:00 PM in Room 311, Administrative Building.

Theme: Shantou University Advanced Undergraduate Education Reform and Innovation Seminar. Speaker: Professor Lu Xiaohua. Attendees: members of the university Party committee, heads of all general party branches and directly affiliated party branches, and leaders from the Discipline Inspection Commission, General Office, Organization and United Front Work Department, Publicity Department, News Center, Student Affairs Office, Social Sciences Department, University Staff Union, and Youth League Committee.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department, Party Committee)
