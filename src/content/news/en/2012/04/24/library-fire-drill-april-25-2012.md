It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a notice that, in collaboration with the campus security department, a fire evacuation drill will be held on April 25 (Wednesday) at 4:30 PM. To ensure the drill proceeds safely and orderly, the following reminders are issued:

1. When the fire alarm sounds, all personnel in the library should remain calm.
2. All participants should take the drill seriously, follow the instructions and guidance of on-site staff, and evacuate in an orderly and rapid manner along the evacuation signs (please do not take books or other items with you). The drill should be conducted with seriousness while paying attention to personal safety; running, horseplay, and pushing are strictly prohibited to prevent overcrowding, stampedes, or falls.
3. Those who evacuate the library area should assemble at the designated location outside the building as directed by staff, and return to the library after the drill concludes.
4. In case of any emergency during the drill, please contact the on-site staff immediately.

Thank you for your cooperation.
