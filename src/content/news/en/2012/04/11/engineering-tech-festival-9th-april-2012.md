It is reported that the College of Engineering at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to implement the EIP-CDIO engineering education reform objectives, further enhance students\' independent innovation abilities, and stimulate enthusiasm for scientific and technological innovation activities and competitions, the college will hold the 9th Science and Technology Festival from April to May 2012.

Theme: "Science · Practice · Innovation." Open to all enrolled students. Key activities include: (1) Opening ceremony on April 14 at 9:30 AM in the New Library Lecture Hall, featuring a ceremony, introduction to the festival\'s history and achievements, overview of this year\'s events, and interaction with senior students; (2) ACM Programming Contest on April 15 in Wenxi 302, conducted per ACM standards, accepting 18 teams of 3 members each, with winners offered a chance to join the STU ACM team. Registration deadline: April 13, 2012.
