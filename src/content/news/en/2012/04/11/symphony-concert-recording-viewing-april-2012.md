It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center at Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that the STU New Year Concert, successfully held on December 28, 2011, received high praise from faculty, students, and the public. To popularize this art form, Shantou Television Station will broadcast a live recording of the symphony concert on the evening of April 13, 2012, at 8:00 PM, with Prof. Wang Shouzhi, Dean of the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design, serving as host.

The university has secured 150 complimentary tickets for faculty and students. Dedicated shuttle buses will operate between campus and the TV station. Students needing transportation should gather at the University Motto Monument at 7:10 PM on April 13 for boarding; the return trip departs from in front of the TV station at 10:00 PM. Interested students are asked to collect their admission tickets at the Administrative Office, Third Floor, Cheung Kong College of Art and Design, on April 12 between 9:00 AM and 11:30 AM, on a first-come, first-served basis.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design, Arts Education Center)
