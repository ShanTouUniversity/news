It is reported that the Personnel Office and Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that, due to operational needs, one expert apartment attendant position is now open for public recruitment.

Basic eligibility: good political and ideological quality, strong sense of responsibility, upright conduct, rigorous work style, teamwork spirit, and dedication; middle-school diploma or above, in good health, compliant with family planning policies; female, under 40 years old, height at least 155 cm; prior hotel management experience is preferred, with age flexibility for qualified candidates. Responsibilities include: maintaining proper appearance and amiable attitude; managing expert apartment guest rooms and check-out procedures; handling daily room and public area cleaning; adapting to a 24-hour shift system; and completing other tasks assigned by supervisors.

Employment is on an auxiliary contract basis; personnel records will not be transferred to STU, and compensation follows university standards. Applicants must submit a resume, photocopies of graduation certificate, family planning certificate, ID card, and other relevant documents to the Expert Apartment, Logistics Office, Resource Management Office, by April 20, 2012, via email or mail. Qualified candidates will be notified for interview.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office, Resource Management Office)
