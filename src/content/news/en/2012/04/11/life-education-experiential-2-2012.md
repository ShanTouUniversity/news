It is reported that the Business School Youth League Committee issued a notice on April 11, 2012, to hold the second Life Education experiential program and sharing session on April 14.

The program aims to help students understand the meaning of life, cherish their own lives, respect the lives of others, reflect on their own values and positioning, and improve the quality of life through experiential activities and theoretical learning.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Undergraduates of the Business School, Classes of 2010 and 2011 |
| Places | 96 (lottery if oversubscribed) |
| Date | April 14, 2012 |
| Program | Morning 09:00–12:00 campus experiential projects (Life Combination, Know Me, Life Ladder, Life Grid, Mind Games, etc.); Afternoon 14:30–17:30 Life Challenge Camp; Evening 19:30–22:00 Life Education Learning and Sharing Session |
| Meeting point | In front of the Gymnasium, on campus and in classrooms |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Business School |

## Requirements and Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Requirements | Strictly observe discipline, no leave (special cases must be explained); good physical fitness for medium-intensity training; bring sportswear and sports shoes; submit a written reflection after the program |
| Deadline | April 11, 2012 |
| How to register | Voluntary registration with the class monitor of your zone, providing only name, gender, class and mobile number |
