It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the requirements of the "Implementation Outline for Comprehensive Reform Pilot of Autonomous Administration" to innovate talent cultivation models, and with strong support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, STU has successfully carried out a series of quality expansion projects since 2009, including "Orienteering," "Nan\'ao Wilderness Survival Training Camp," "Shangri-La Advanced Training Camp," and "Chizha Fengyun" Advanced Outdoor Expedition, benefiting a wide range of faculty and students.

To help more students gain uniquely valuable, lifelong learning experiences, the 2012 STU Advanced Outdoor Expedition Program is now underway. The program will further increase participation through student team self-planning and campus-wide public voting. The STU Advanced Outdoor Expedition Program special website, http://expedition.stu.edu.cn, is now officially online. All users are welcome to browse and check activity details.
