It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 16th "FLTRP–Amazon Cup" National College English Debate Competition will be held in Beijing in July 2012. To enliven campus culture, encourage students to follow current affairs, showcase student spirit, and improve critical thinking and English application skills, the center will host the STU campus preliminary round.

The competition will be held in three stages: campus selection (April), regional semifinals (May–June), and the national finals (July). The center has successfully hosted two campus selections, and STU contestants have achieved excellent results at the national finals. This year\'s competition will use the British Parliamentary debate format.

The selection round is scheduled for April 18 (Wednesday), 1:00–5:00 PM, at the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL). Eligible participants are all undergraduate students at STU, with each team consisting of two members. In addition, volunteers are needed for venue setup, timing, and scoring; a volunteer training session will be held on the afternoon of April 14.
