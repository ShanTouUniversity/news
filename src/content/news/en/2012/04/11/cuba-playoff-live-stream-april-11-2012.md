It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that on the evening of April 10, the STU "Wan Bian Jin Gang" basketball team narrowly lost to the China University of Mining and Technology team with a score of 51:66. In the first round of the playoff, STU will face the East China Jiaotong University team.

Tonight at 6:30 PM, CNTV (China Network Television) Sports Channel will live stream this match. All faculty and students are welcome to watch.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
