It is reported that Zhicheng College at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following the release of the registration announcement for the fifth session of the "Career Expansion Boot Camp," 50 students have been selected upon the deadline.

All selected students are asked to check in promptly at 7:45 AM on Saturday, April 14, in Lecture Room J-354, Zhicheng College, and participate in the boot camp sessions. During the camp, male participants are required to wear formal attire, and female participants are expected to dress modestly and elegantly. Students are asked to prepare accordingly. Thank you to all students for your enthusiastic participation.
