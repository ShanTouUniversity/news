It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, per the teaching schedule, the "Career Counseling and Training" course (Class No. 52548, taught by Su Wenji and Wang Bin), originally scheduled for Periods 8–9 on Monday, April 16 in Room 304, Building D, has been rescheduled as a company visit activity.

Students enrolled in this class are asked to gather at the University Motto Monument at 4:00 PM on Monday, April 16, to board the bus for a visit to Guangdong Rongcheng Food Co., Ltd. Students are asked to inform one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
