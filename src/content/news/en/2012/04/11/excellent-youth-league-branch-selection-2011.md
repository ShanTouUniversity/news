It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to commend the advanced, set typical examples, and better inspire all league cadres and members to strive forward, pioneer and innovate, remain committed to their posts, and willingly dedicate themselves—further fostering a strong atmosphere of excellence and competition across all league organizations—the committee has decided to commend a batch of excellent league branches (general branches), outstanding league cadres, and excellent league members during this year\'s May 4th celebration.

Eligibility criteria for excellent league branches (general branches): branch total assessment score of 90 or above; no disciplinary violations or sanctions among league members. For general branches, all subordinate branches must score 70 or above, with at least 70% scoring 90 or above. Excellent league member criteria include: earnest study of Deng Xiaoping Theory and the "Three Represents"重要思想, implementing the Scientific Development Concept, upholding the Four Cardinal Principles, supporting the Party\'s lines, principles, and policies, progressive thinking, active pursuit of advancement, strict observance of national laws, league discipline, and university regulations, social morality, and civilized etiquette; no disciplinary infractions within two years; resolute implementation of league resolutions, diligent fulfillment of league member obligations, active participation in organizational life and league activities, and outstanding completion of tasks assigned by the league organization.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
