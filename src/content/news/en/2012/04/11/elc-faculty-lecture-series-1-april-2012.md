It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the first episode of the 2012 ELC Faculty Lecture Series will be held on April 12 (Thursday) at 7:30 PM in the Science Lecture Hall.

The lecture topic is "American Sports: Basketball & Rugby," presented by Josh Lee, Nima Rad, and Ally Munn. All faculty and students are welcome to attend and improve their English listening and speaking skills while learning about a new topic.
