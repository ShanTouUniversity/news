It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with higher-level directives, the 2012 Labor Day (May 1) holiday schedule is as follows: Three days off from April 29 to May 1, 2012, with a makeup workday on April 28 (Saturday). Teaching arrangements will be handled by the Academic Affairs Office and the Graduate School.

All units are requested to inspect safety facilities in their offices before the holiday, close windows and doors, turn off water and electricity, and ensure security precautions are in place. The Security Department will maintain campus order and public safety during the holiday period. The General Office, Resource Management Office, Student Affairs Office, all colleges, News Center, Network and Information Center, Science and Technology Center, and Teaching Buildings D, E, and G should arrange duty personnel and submit the names and contact numbers of on-duty staff by email to the General Office (o_jdk@stu.edu.cn) before April 26. Tel: 8290-2305.
