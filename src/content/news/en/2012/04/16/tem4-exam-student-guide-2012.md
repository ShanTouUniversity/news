It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 Test for English Majors Band 4 (TEM4-2012) will be held on April 21, 2012 (Saturday) at 8:30 AM, with a total duration of approximately 135 minutes.

The exam sections and time allocation are as follows: Dictation (15 minutes, on Answer Sheet 1); Listening Comprehension (20 minutes, on Answer Sheet 2); Cloze (15 minutes, on Answer Sheet 2); Grammar & Vocabulary (15 minutes, on Answer Sheet 2); Reading Comprehension (25 minutes, on Answer Sheet 2); Writing (45 minutes, on Answer Sheet 3). The dictation will be read four times, with the final two minutes on the tape reserved for students to check their work. All multiple-choice answers must be written on Answer Sheet 2. Dictation goes on Answer Sheet 1, and the essay on Answer Sheet 3. Any answers written in the question booklet will receive zero points. Students must not write their university name or personal name in blank spaces on the answer sheets; violations will result in a score of zero. Students of the College of Arts and Letters are reminded to pay attention to these guidelines.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
