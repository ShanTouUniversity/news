It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "Interim Measures for the Integrity and Self-Discipline of Leading Cadres in Guangdong Higher Education Institutions" (粤办发〔2011〕30号), which stipulates that "the principal and secretary of a party committee generally shall not serve as the head of the university academic committee or other academic professional committees," and following the decision of the 4th university leadership meeting held on April 10, 2012:

Prof. Pei Hua is appointed as the Chair of the Shantou University Academic Committee. Prof. Xu Xiaohu will no longer serve as the Chair of the Shantou University Academic Committee.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
