It is reported that the Women\'s Research Center of the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that a public lecture titled "Feminist Media Studies: From Media Criticism to Communication Activism" will be held on April 19, 2012 (Thursday) from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM in ACC Room 3.

The speaker is Prof. Bu Wei, a researcher, graduate professor, and doctoral supervisor at the School of Journalism and Communication, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences. Prof. Bu Wei also serves on the councils of the Chinese Human Rights研究会, Chinese Women\'s研究会, and Chinese Youth研究会, and holds appointments as a特约 researcher at the Institute of Law and Gender Studies, CASS, and the Nordic Asia Institute. She is an advisor to several civil society organizations. The lecture will review the origins and development of feminist media studies and, from a critical communication studies perspective, discuss the impact of local communication activism on promoting gender equality in the context of globalization. The event is hosted by the STU Women\'s Research Center, College of Arts and Letters, and co-organized by the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication.
