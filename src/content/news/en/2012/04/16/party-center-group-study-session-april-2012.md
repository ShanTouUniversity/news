It is reported that the Party Committee Propaganda Department of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Party Committee Center Group Study Session will be held on April 18, 2012 (Wednesday) at 3:00 PM in Meeting Room 311, Administrative Building.

The theme is the 2012 "Two Sessions" (National People\'s Congress and Chinese People\'s Political Consultative Conference) study session. The lecturer is Prof. Li Ping. Attendees include members of the university Party committee, general Party branch and directly affiliated Party branch secretaries, as well as heads of the Discipline Inspection Commission, General Office, Organization and United Front Work Department, Propaganda Department, News Center, Student Affairs Office, Social Sciences Department, Staff Union, and Youth League Committee.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Committee Propaganda Department)
