It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a notice that a training lecture on ebrary and ARL databases will be held on April 18 (Wednesday) from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM at the Library Subject Services Department (inside the consultation desk, second floor, New Library). The speaker is Wang Jianfei, Training and Marketing Supervisor at ProQuest.

The lecture will cover: ebrary resource advantages, search technology, annotation methods, two browsing modes, link-out features, online account functions, case studies, saving features and offline reading implementation, integration with iPad/iPhone/iPod touch, and remote access to ebrary searches, saves, and readings. ARL resources will also be introduced, including basic and advanced search, online thesaurus, command line, related citations, common references, research report search, current awareness services, as well as saving, reading, sharing, online management, and translation features.

All are welcome to attend and win prizes for participation.
