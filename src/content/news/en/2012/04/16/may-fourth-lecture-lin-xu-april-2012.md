It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that, to enrich campus cultural life, advance the humanistic community construction under the student community X-Plan, help students better understand the importance of life aspirations for personal development, strengthen confidence in facing the hardships of entrepreneurship and life difficulties, and combine with the 93rd anniversary of the May Fourth Movement, the university will host a lecture titled "Life Aspiration — Reflections on Life for College Students."

Lecture theme: "Life Aspiration — Reflections on Life for College Students." Guest speaker: Mr. Lin Xu (Chairman of the Thai Chinese Federation, Professor at the Chinese National Academy). Time: April 20, 2012 (Friday), 2:30–4:30 PM. Venue: New Library Lecture Hall. Topics include: the significance of life aspiration and setting lofty goals; how to face the hardships of entrepreneurship and difficulties in life; the importance of gratitude for young people; the need for broad-mindedness and integrity among youth; and transcending oneself to unleash one\'s potential.

Registration: online at http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ until 5:00 PM on April 17.
