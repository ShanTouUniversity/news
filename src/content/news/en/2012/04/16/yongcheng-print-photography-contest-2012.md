It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to improve students\' photography skills, encourage them to discover and record their own Yongcheng (Shantou Special Economic Zone) stories, gain a fuller understanding of the inner charm of Shantou, and enhance students\' sense of identity and belonging to the city, the STU Youth newspaper will host the "Yongcheng · Imprints" photography competition.

Event period: April 16 to May 13. Participants: all currently enrolled students. Location: Shantou city. Theme: "Yongcheng · Imprints." Submission deadline: April 16–28. Entry methods: (1) Submit photo electronically with the entry form; (2) Each participant may choose one or more entry methods, but only one entry per method; (3) Entries may be taken with high-resolution phones, point-and-shoot cameras, mirrorless cameras, or DSLRs; (4) Submit to the designated QQ emails or via QQ directly—do not use compressed files.

Entry formats: A. Solo Shot (single photo with up to 50-character description); B. Visual Story (series of photos expressing the theme, up to 6 photos with description); C. Photo Collage.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
