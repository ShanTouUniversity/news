It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Sanlian STU Bookstore is holding a clearance sale, offering 20% off all books and stationery starting immediately. The sale is expected to run through the end of May. Non-discounted items are excluded. All faculty and students are welcome to shop.
