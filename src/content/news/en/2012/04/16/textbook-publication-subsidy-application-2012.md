It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "Shantou University Textbook Development and Publication Subsidy Management Measures" (汕大发〔2009〕148号), the office will organize the application and review process for the 2012 first-half year textbook development and publication subsidy.

Faculty members who plan to publish textbooks in the first half of 2012 and meet the subsidy eligibility requirements are asked to fill out the "Shantou University Textbook Development and Publication Subsidy Application Form" and prepare the relevant materials (in triplicate) according to the measures. Materials should be submitted to the Comprehensive Section of the Academic Affairs Office (Room 401, Xingxi Building) by May 7, 2012. Electronic copies of the application form should be sent to zhailiu@stu.edu.cn. Contact: Zhai Liu, Tel: 82902736.
