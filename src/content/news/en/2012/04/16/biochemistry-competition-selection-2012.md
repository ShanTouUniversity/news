It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 6th Guangdong College Student Science and Technology Academic Festival — Guangdong College Student Biochemistry Experiment Skills Competition — will be held on May 26–27 at the College of Life Sciences, South China Agricultural University. The college will organize students to participate.

Eligibility: currently enrolled undergraduate students in the College of Science who have completed relevant biology or chemistry lab courses. Registration: submit the attached form (Appendix 1) in person to Mr. Yang at the Biology Department Office (Room 444, Science Building) or send the electronic version to mlyang@stu.edu.cn. Deadline: April 17, 2012. All registrants must pass an interview assessment. Interview time: April 18, 2012, 3:00 PM. Venue: Science Lecture Hall, College of Science (Room 352, Science Building). Students are encouraged to sign up promptly.
