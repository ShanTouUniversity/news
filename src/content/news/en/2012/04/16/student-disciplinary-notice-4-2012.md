It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued Student Disciplinary Notice No. 4 (2012). On December 26, 2011, Li, a student in the Electronic Information Engineering major (Class of 2011) at the College of Engineering, had a verbal altercation with his roommate Lin over issues such as closing the door and smoking in the dormitory. During the dispute, when Lin struck the floor with a chair, Li punched Lin in the face, causing Lin's glasses to shatter and sustaining facial injuries. Li also injured his right hand. Both students were accompanied by roommates from a nearby dorm to the First Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University Medical College for treatment.

In accordance with Article 11 of the "Shantou University Student Disciplinary Regulations (Trial)," the university decided to impose a demerit on student Li. The university urges all students to take this as a warning, jointly maintain normal teaching and living order on campus, and promote the development of good study atmosphere and school spirit.
