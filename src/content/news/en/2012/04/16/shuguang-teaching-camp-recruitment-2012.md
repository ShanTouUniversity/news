It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to enrich the holiday life of primary and secondary school students in townships, bring science popularization knowledge, learning resources, and talent to rural areas, spread the volunteer spirit, and provide STU students with social practice opportunities under the "service learning" concept, the STU Shuguang Student Aid Brigade will hold the "Walking with Love" holiday teaching camp during the upcoming Labor Day period.

The活动拟招募15至20名志愿者，在活动开展期间为外砂镇南侨小学学生开展课堂及课外活动。课程内容以及形式由志愿者自主设计、实施。

Eligibility requirements: good physical condition with strong communication, organizational, and emergency-handling skills; serious and responsible work attitude, able to endure hardship, follow arrangements, and work well in a team; polite and文明, representing the good image of STU students; available to participate fully from April 29 to May 1; and sufficient time to complete pre-teaching course design and other preparations.

Registration: individuals or groups of 2–3 may apply; group applicants will be interviewed as a team. Send personal information (name, major, mobile numbers, email) to 11sqtian@stu.edu.cn or 11crzhou@stu.edu.cn. In-person registration and inquiries are available at Jin San Jiao (Golden Triangle) on April 13 from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
