It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that Tai\'an Real Estate is currently running a VIP pre-registration promotion for "Jinxiu Apartment." The company will hold an on-campus information and consultation session, providing real estate consultations and group-buying discount opportunities.

Event time: April 18, 2012 (Wednesday), 3:00–5:00 PM. Venue: Third Floor, Staff Union. Investment involves risks; please choose carefully and bear risks independently.
