It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art & Design at Shantou University launched a themed exhibition titled "Plastic Age" in April 2012, taking plastic — a material omnipresent in modern life — as its point of departure to prompt visitors to rethink humanity's relationship with the material world.

## About the Exhibition

"Plastic Age" centers on the pervasive presence of plastic products in contemporary society. Since the mid-twentieth century, plastic has rapidly permeated nearly every aspect of daily life thanks to its low cost, durability, and malleability. While bringing convenience, it has also given rise to a range of problems such as environmental pollution and resource depletion.

According to the organizer, the exhibition is built around this defining issue of the era. Through artistic creation, it aims to reveal the aesthetic possibilities of plastic as a material while raising public awareness of consumer culture and ecological concerns.
