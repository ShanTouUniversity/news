It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that "Kankan Culture 2: Reporting China and Reporting in China" will take place on April 18, 2012 (Wednesday) at 7:30 PM in the Science Lecture Hall.

Guest speakers include Peter Arnett (Pulitzer Prize winner, renowned American war correspondent) and Prof. Wang Shouzhi (Dean of the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design). The event is hosted by the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
