It is reported that the Discipline Inspection Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Fourth Session of the Third Plenary (Expanded) Meeting of the University Discipline Inspection Commission will be held on April 17 (Tuesday) at 3:00 PM in Meeting Room 311, Xingdong Building. Members of the university Discipline Inspection Commission, the Medical College Discipline Inspection Commission, and discipline inspection, supervision, and audit cadres from the main campus, the Medical College, and affiliated hospitals are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office)
