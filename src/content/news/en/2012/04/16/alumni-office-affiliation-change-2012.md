It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following the decision of the 4th university leadership meeting held on April 10, 2012, the Alumni Association Office will be affiliated with the General Office, and will no longer be affiliated with the Student Affairs Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
