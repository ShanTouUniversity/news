It is reported that the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Dr. Mao Si Hui, Director and Professor at the Bell English Center of Macao Polytechnic University, and Vice President of the Chinese-American Comparative Culture Research Association, will deliver a lecture at STU on April 20, 2012, from 4:10 PM to 5:30 PM in Teaching Building D504.

The lecture topic is "Critical Thinking in English Public Speaking." Dr. Mao will begin with an observation of how birds imitate each other\'s calls in nature to illustrate the diversity and individuality of sounds. Similarly, in human speech, those with rich literary foundations, thoughtful minds, innovative thinking, and logical clarity are more likely to be accepted by their audience. Dr. Mao will then use examples from U.S. President Obama\'s speeches and award-winning performances in the 21st Century National English Speaking Competition to demonstrate in an accessible manner how individuality, a spirit of innovation, and a sense of humor are essential elements of outstanding public speaking.
