It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to carry forward the volunteer spirit of "dedication, friendship, mutual aid, and progress" and to assist vulnerable groups in eastern Guangdong, the "Firefly" Love Action Brigade of the STU Youth Volunteer Association will hold the "Passing the Torch" rural primary school work camp during the upcoming Labor Day period. Volunteers are being recruited from all students.

Activity location: Longxi Primary School, Longxi Village, Xilu Town, Chaoyang District, Shantou City. Main activities: (1) Teaching: improve rural students\' interest in learning through fun English lessons, cultural games, and sports competitions (12 volunteers needed); (2) Medical aid: provide basic health check-ups for students and villagers, popularize health knowledge (3 volunteers); (3) Repair service: help villagers repair household appliances and promote electrical safety knowledge (5 volunteers); (4) News coverage: document the camp, photograph rural living conditions, and tweet全程 activities (4 volunteers); (5) Logistics: manage meals and supplies for camp participants, assist other groups (5 volunteers); (6) Entertainment: arrange film screenings (1 volunteer).

Activity dates: April 29 to May 1. Total recruits: approximately 30 volunteers. Registration period: from notice release until 12:00 PM on April 20. Registration: download the electronic form from the OA system, name it "Year+Name+Mobile Number (long and short)," and send to the designated email.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
