It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that, in accordance with Document No. 9〔2012〕of the Shantou Municipal Working Committee of State Organs, before July 1 this year, the municipal level will recommend a batch of outstanding grassroots party organizations and outstanding party members from the 2010–2012 Campaign to Create Advanced Party Organizations and Keep Members Advanced as candidates for provincial and municipal commendation. The Municipal Working Committee will also commend a batch of outstanding grassroots party organizations and outstanding party members. The municipal working committee has allocated 4 outstanding grassroots party organizations and 4 outstanding party members to STU.

Each unit is asked to make relevant recommendations according to the conditions and requirements in the document. The university Party committee will decide on and submit recommended candidates based on the recommendations received. Units recommending candidates must submit approval forms, investigation materials (approximately 2,000 characters), and事迹 materials (approximately 2,000 characters) to the Organization and United Front Work Department by April 18. Electronic copies should be sent to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. For unit recommendations, a family planning qualification certificate is required; for individual recommendations, a family planning certificate is required. Contact: Ji Rongxin, Tel: 82902345.
