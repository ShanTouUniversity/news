It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on April 16, 2012, that the third STU Public Service Course Teacher Training will open on April 18.

## Training Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Start date | April 18, 2012 |
| Scale | Six sessions, about 13 hours in total |
| Attendance | Attendance is recorded; those missing more than two sessions will be deemed to have failed; teachers with scheduling conflicts are advised to reschedule classes, and leave must be requested in advance |
| Certification | Passing the training and completing one public service project with a qualified evaluation are required to obtain the Certificate for Teaching Public Service Courses |
| Contact | Ms. Liu Xiangling, Academic Affairs Office |
| Office phone | 82902416 |
| Email | lxl0726@stu.edu.cn |
| Organizer | Academic Affairs Office |

The schedule and participant list are available in the OA attachments.
