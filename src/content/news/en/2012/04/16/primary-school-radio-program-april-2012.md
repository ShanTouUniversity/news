It is reported that the Attached Primary School of Shantou University released a brief on April 16 that on the afternoon of April 14, student representatives from the STU Attached Primary School were invited to participate in the live broadcast of the children's program "News Voice — Seven-Color Flower" hosted by Shantou Radio Station. During the Q&A with the host, the students spoke fluently and thought quickly, sharing wonderful presentations about campus life, study experiences, and extracurricular activities. They also showcased personal talents in playing musical instruments and singing, receiving high praise from the radio station staff.
