It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to better serve STU alumni, facilitate alumni access to campus and visits to the library, and enable them to enjoy discounts offered by alumni enterprises, the Alumni Office will produce free alumni cards for the class of 2012.

Required materials: (1) A fully completed application form (organized by class; file naming: "Enrollment Year + Major + Alumni Card Application"); (2) one electronic photo (minimum 100K pixels; file naming: "Enrollment Year + College + Name + Student ID + Gender"). Application method: organized by class. Class monitors should collect and consolidate the materials of graduates who wish to apply, and submit them to the Alumni Office by May 10. Materials may be delivered in person or emailed to o_xyh@stu.edu.cn; please call the Alumni Office after sending the email to confirm receipt. Collection time: mid-June. Distribution method: collected by class.

Alumni Office contact: Tel/Fax 0754-82902180; Email o_xyh@stu.edu.cn; Address: Room 801-B, Building 8, Shantou University. The Alumni Office also accepts applications from alumni of other graduating years (see attachment for details).
