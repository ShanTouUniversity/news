It is reported that Shantou University issued an official document (STU Fa [2012] No. 40) on April 19, 2012, announcing a personnel change in the Department of Electronic Engineering of the College of Engineering.

The decision was made after deliberation at the fourth university leadership meeting held on April 10, 2012. The key details are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| New appointment | Wei Chuliang appointed Deputy Head of the Department of Electronic Engineering, College of Engineering |
| Probation period | Six months |
| Stepping down | Tang Yajuan no longer serves as Deputy Head of the department |
| Decision body | Fourth university leadership meeting of 2012 |
| Document number | STU Fa [2012] No. 40 |

The notice was released to all units across the university, marking a new round of transition for the department's management team.
