It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the second regular counselor meeting of 2012 will be held on April 23 (Monday) at 3:00 PM in Room 726, Building 789. Counselors from all colleges (residential colleges), community counselors, and relevant staff from the Student Affairs Office are requested to make appropriate arrangements and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
