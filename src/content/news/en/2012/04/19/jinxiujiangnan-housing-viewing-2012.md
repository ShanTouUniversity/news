It is reported that the Trade Union issued a notice on April 19, 2012, that Tai’an Real Estate will organize a group viewing tour to its Jinxiujiangnan project on April 22 at the request of faculty and staff.

A free shuttle bus will pick up faculty and staff to visit the project site, where the Jinxiu Apartment show flat will be open with guided tours by sales consultants and refreshments. Faculty with purchase intent may pay a 5,000-yuan refundable deposit on site (UnionPay accepted) to obtain a Jinxiu Apartment VIP card, offering a 5,000-yuan deduction of 10,000 yuan, priority selection at launch, and a special group-purchase discount for STU.
