It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2012 Voice and Accent Training Lecture Series (5) will be held on April 19, 2012 (Thursday) from 7:10 PM to 8:40 PM at the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL).

Topic: Lecture on Letters and Sounds. Lecturer: Riya Dutta. All are welcome to attend.
