It is reported that the Christian Research Center of the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Prof. Zeng Huantang will deliver a lecture on April 25, 2012 (Wednesday) at 7:30 PM in the Science Lecture Hall.

Lecture topic: "Death Studies and Good Passing and Good Farewell." Speaker: Prof. Zeng Huantang (Professor, Institute of Death Education and Counseling, National Taitung University of Nursing and Health Sciences; advisory committee member for high school life education curriculum, Taiwan; guiding professor, Derong Social Welfare Foundation; committee member, Life Navigator Association, Museum of World Religions). Prof. Zeng has long been committed to research in death education, life education, funeral industry, hospice care and follow-up support, and meaning of life management. His publications include *An Introduction to Death Studies*, *Taiwan Funeral Education and Certification*, *Hospice and Follow-up Care*, *Practical Exploration of Death Studies*, *Understanding Death Studies*, *Death Issues in Society*, *Death Education*, *Christian Views on Life and Death*, and *The Role and Function of Faith in Funerals*.

Lecture abstract: Death is the most important event in life. Only by learning to live well can we learn to die well; conversely, only by learning to die well can we learn to live well. One\\'s perspective and attitude toward death will affect one\\'s perspective and attitude toward life. We should learn "good passing" — a good death. If a person reflects on their life from the perspective of a "good passing," they will gain deeper insights into the meaning of their life. We should also learn "good farewell" — saying goodbye properly, parting with family and friends without regrets, and making final plans for one\\'s life. If tomorrow we were to pass away, how would you strive for a "good passing" and "good farewell" today?
