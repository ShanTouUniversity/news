It is reported that the Library issued a notice on April 19, 2012, to open a trial of the HeinOnline Law Journal Database.

HeinOnline is a PDF-based full-text database centered on law, covering politics, international relations and diplomacy, and is regarded as one of the world’s most comprehensive law databases. It includes more than 1,600 authoritative law journals, 675 volumes of leading treatises on international law, over 100,000 cases, more than 2,000 classic legal monographs and full texts of U.S. federal government reports.
