It is reported that the English Drama Society of Shantou University is currently recruiting new members from among all students on campus. The society noted that no theatrical experience is necessary, and all interested students are welcome to join.

Students who wish to join can contact the society's Felix Giron (felix@stu.edu.cn) or Muriel Walker (muriel@stu.edu.cn) by email for registration details.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Society | English Drama Society |
| Open To | All university students |
| Experience | No theatrical experience required |
| Contact | Felix Giron (felix@stu.edu.cn), Muriel Walker (muriel@stu.edu.cn) |
