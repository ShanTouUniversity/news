It is reported that the Christian Research Center of the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Prof. Zeng Huantang will deliver a lecture on April 23, 2012 (Monday) at 7:30 PM in the New Library Auditorium.

Lecture topic: "Life Education in Taiwan: Current Situation and Future Development." Speaker: Prof. Zeng Huantang (Professor, Institute of Death Education and Counseling, National Taitung University of Nursing and Health Sciences; advisory committee member for high school life education curriculum, Taiwan; guiding professor, Derong Social Welfare Foundation; committee member, Life Navigator Association, Museum of World Religions).

Lecture abstract: Why do we need "life education"? What does "life education" teach us? How can we "love life, cherish life, and care for life"? By reviewing the past of "life education" in Taiwan and looking ahead to its future, Prof. Zeng will explore the true meaning of "life" and "life education" together with young students.
