It is reported that Shantou University recently issued document 汕大发〔2012〕41号, announcing the 2012 budget for main campus units. The 2012 main campus budget was approved at the 4th university leadership meeting held on April 10, 2012. Compared with previous years, the 2012 budget underwent thorough discussion by the Budget Committee, and teaching-related and research-related items were separately reviewed and recommended by the Teaching Committee and the Research Committee, further enhancing the科学性 of budget review.

To ensure that grassroots teaching units have adequate resources to support their operations, the following guidelines are proposed for the use of lump-sum funding allocated to teaching units: each teaching unit should allocate a portion of its lump-sum funding to cover student internship and student activity expenses (among which, student activity fees allocated by each college are to be approved by the college official in charge of student work, authorized by the college director). At least 50% of the remaining funds should be distributed to grassroots teaching units such as departments and teaching and research sections. Of the funds allocated to each grassroots teaching unit, necessary low-value consumables expenses should be covered first, and the remainder should be distributed among grassroots teaching units based on the number of faculty and the number of class hours undertaken in the previous budget year (each accounting for 50% weight). All units are reminded to observe financial discipline, arrange expenditures reasonably, practice frugality, eliminate waste, and strive to improve the efficiency of fund use.
