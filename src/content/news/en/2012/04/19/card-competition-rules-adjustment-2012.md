It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the staff card competition has been adjusted to include two events: Event 1 — "Find a Friend" (5 players per team); Event 2 — "Dou Di Zhu" (3 players per team).

Registration and team formation rules: Both events will be held simultaneously; team members cannot participate in both events; competitions are不分男女组别 (no men\\'s and women\\'s divisions); participants may form their own teams and indicate teammates in the remarks column during registration, or register individually and be randomly assigned to a team by the union; the number of advancing teams will be determined based on registration numbers; if an event has fewer than 6 teams registered, that event will be cancelled and merged into the other event.

Registered participants should fill out the registration form and send it to the Staff Union email at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn. Contact: 82902983 (Staff Union Office).
