It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to promote green and environmental concepts, improve students\\' innovative and practical abilities, enrich the extracurricular lives of faculty and students, foster communication among students, and permeate environmental awareness throughout every corner of campus, the STU Association for the Promotion of Science and Technology and the STU Youth Volunteer Association will jointly host the 6th STU \"Ingenuity DIY\" eco-friendly competition, sponsored by Herbaday (佰草集).

Theme: Caring Actions, Green Living. Target participants: all students. Event period: April 21 to May 20. Final venue: Science Lecture Hall.

Activities include: (1) DIY Master Exhibition and Environmental Protection Advocacy: April 21, 22, 28, 29; (2) Information booth: April 22, 25, 28; (3) Press conference: May 2 (Wednesday); (4) Preliminary round: May 5 (Saturday); (5) Eco-bag making: May 12 (Saturday); (6) Final: May 12 (Saturday); (7) Outstanding works exhibition: May 13 (Sunday); (8) Charity sale: May 20 (Sunday).

Competition categories: (I) Turning Waste into Treasure — Transforming the \"Rotting\" into the \"Magical,\" Fashion Show, Creative Mini Magazine, Horse-riding Lanterns; (II) I\\'m a Little Director — Eco-friendly Video Shoot, Watching Animation to Learn About Environmental Protection; (III) Charity Sale.
