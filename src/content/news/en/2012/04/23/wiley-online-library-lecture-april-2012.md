It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a notice that the seventh session of the "Subject Literature Service" series — a lecture on the Wiley Online Library platform — will be held soon.

Wiley Online Library (WOL) is Wiley-Blackwell's next-generation online platform, offering online resources across chemistry, materials science, life sciences, medicine, engineering, computer science, business management, and humanities and social sciences. With its简洁 interface, it provides users with easier viewing, searching, extended reading, and a range of personalized service features.

Lecture content: disciplines covered by WILEY Online Library; browsing, searching functions and operational methods of the WILEY Online Library platform; online submission to WILEY journals and key considerations; off-campus access to WILEY Online Library; personalized service features for WILEY Online Library users. Lecturer: Zeng Zhaohong (zhzeng@stu.edu.cn). All faculty and students (especially graduate students) are welcome to attend. Refreshments will be provided!
