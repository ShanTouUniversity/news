It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that in 2011, university-level youth league organizations and members actively advanced, broke new ground, stood firm in their posts, and were dedicated to service, effectively promoting the "争先创优" campaign. A number of collectives and individuals received municipal-level commendation. The full list of commendation results is published in the attachment.

The committee urges all recognized individuals and collectives to cherish their honors, make persistent efforts, carry forward their achievements, and strive for further success. All faculty and students are encouraged to learn from the recognized individuals and collectives, further emancipate their minds, find their positioning, innovate their work approaches, strengthen organizational foundations, and promote the development of youth league and student work under the new situation with a spirit of reform and innovation, making greater contributions to the reform and development of the university.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
