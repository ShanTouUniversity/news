It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, with the generous support of the Li Ka Shing Foundation, STU students will have the opportunity to attend a Lady Gaga concert in Hong Kong on May 5.

Quota: 10 principal candidates and 8 alternates. Participants will be divided into 3 groups for activities. Eligibility: full-time undergraduate students currently enrolled at STU. Participants must commit to the following obligations: observe laws and discipline during their stay at the university and actively fulfill student duties; love STU and actively showcase the image of STU students, promoting the university\\'s educational philosophy and characteristics; ensure completion of their Hong Kong–Macau pass by May 3; consciously abide by relevant etiquette rules during the concert; during the concert period, visit exhibitions in Hong Kong related to their major and give a themed sharing session upon return; do not absence without reason or refuse to follow the university\\'s activity arrangements.

Applicants should register via the reservation system (http://activity.stu.edu.cn/) before 5:00 PM on April 24. The university will determine selections through a random lottery.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
