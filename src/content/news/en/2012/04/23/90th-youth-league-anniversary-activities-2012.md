It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that 2012 marks the 90th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Youth League of China and the 93rd anniversary of the May Fourth Movement. To guide young people in carrying forward the May Fourth spirit centered on "patriotism, progress, democracy, and science," to fully invigorate grassroots league work, and to improve the ideological and moral quality, social responsibility, and social practice experience of all league members and youth, the committee has decided to hold a series of activities during the May Fourth period.

Theme: Hold High the League Banner and Follow the Party; Youthful Spirit Writes a New Chapter. Event period: April–May 2012.

Main activity: "Hold High the League Banner and Follow the Party; Youthful Spirit Writes a New Chapter" distinctive league day activities. Time: April–November 2012. Venue:不限. Target participants: all Communist Youth League members across the university. Each league organization is encouraged to fully leverage the innovative spirit of its members, make full use of their professional knowledge to serve society, and carry out various forms of themed league day activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
