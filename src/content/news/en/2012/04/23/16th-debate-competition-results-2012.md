It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that after intense competition through preliminary rounds, quarterfinals, semifinals, and the final, the awards for the 16th STU "Truth · Strongest Voice" Debate Competition have been determined. The results are as follows:

Final round results: Champion — School of Journalism; Runner-up — Business School; Third place — Arts College.

Best debater and Most Popular Debater winners by round:

Preliminary round: Best debaters Zeng Yun Jie (College of Arts and Letters), Zhang Hui Wen (Law School), Li Xue (Arts College); Most popular debaters Li Min (Medical College), Zhang Hui Wen (Law School), Meng Liang (Arts College).

Quarterfinals: Best debaters Zhang Jia Ming (Business School), Dong Cheng Zhi (Medical College), Zhu Dan Qing (School of Journalism), Xu Jia Mei (Law School), Chen Yi (Science College); Most popular debaters Zhang Jia Ming (Business School), Dong Cheng Zhi (Medical College), Wang Meng Qi (School of Journalism), Chen Yi (Science College).

Semifinals: Best debaters Wang Ruo Zhou (Business School), Liao Yan Lin (School of Journalism); Most popular debaters Wang Ruo Zhou (Business School), Liao Yan Lin (School of Journalism).

Third-place match: Best debater and Most popular debater — Wu Zhuo Zhi (Medical College).

Championship match: Best debater and Most popular debater — Liao Yan Lin (School of Journalism).

Congratulations to all winning teams and individuals, and thanks to all faculty and students for their strong support of this year's debate competition!
