It is reported that the Science College of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 109th session of the "Science Salon" academic lecture series will be held soon.

Speaker: Dr. Jin Zhen (Ph.D. in Analytical Chemistry, Hunan University). Lecture topic: Fluorescent Molecular Probes for the Detection of Heavy Metal Ions. Time: April 25, 2012, 2:30 PM. Venue: Science College Lecture Hall (Room Li-352).

Dr. Jin Zhen, female, holds a Ph.D. in Analytical Chemistry from Hunan University. Her research direction is "chemical sensors." Her research primarily involves connecting fluorescent probes to various新型 materials to create chemical sensors that are easy to separate or load into cells, successfully used in separation and cell imaging experiments. All interested faculty and students are welcome to attend.
