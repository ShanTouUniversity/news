It is reported that the International Research Center of the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Dr. Lars Jensen will deliver a lecture on April 26, 2012 (Thursday) at 8:00 PM in E-404.

Lecture topic: \"Golden Mountain, Australia: Globalization, Migration and Others.\" Speaker: Dr. Lars Jensen (Australia Studies, Australian National University; Cultural Studies, Roskilde University, Denmark). All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
