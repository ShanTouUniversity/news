It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 2011–2012 Spring Semester STU Student Club Activity Project Defense Meeting was successfully held on April 18, 2012. After fair and impartial review by the judges and approval by the university Youth League Committee, eight club projects were approved for support, including the Track and Field Association\\'s \"Niutianyang Trekking Activity\" outdoor expansion project. The full list of approved projects is published in the attachment.

Heads of approved projects are requested to carefully organize their activities and jointly promote campus cultural construction.
