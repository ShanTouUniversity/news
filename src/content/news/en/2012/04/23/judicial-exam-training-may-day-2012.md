It is reported that the Law School issued a notice on April 23, 2012, regarding the judicial examination training schedule during the May Day holiday.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| April 28 (daytime) | No classes |
| April 29–30 | Training as usual |
| May 1 | Holiday |

Unless otherwise notified, subsequent training will be held on weekends as usual (Friday 19:00–22:00, Saturday/Sunday 08:30–17:30). Students are asked to wear their course cards and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School)
