It is reported that the Youth League Committee of the Business School at Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to cultivate students' sense of service, enhance their sense of social responsibility and gratitude, and help socially disadvantaged groups such as the elderly and disabled in Wuqiao through practical action, the 2012 "Business for Good: Business Boats Carry Kindness" charity marketing activity will be held.

Slogan: Join hands with Business, walk together with Kindness. Date: May 5, 2012. Venue: Campus Golden Triangle and Pedestrian Street. Format: students form teams and compete as organizations, classes, or individuals; each team sets up a charity stall on the day of the event. Teams donate 60% of their proceeds as charity funds (voluntary higher donations are welcome). The organizers will award various prizes on-site and announce results university-wide. After the event, the funds will be used to support the living expenses of the elderly and disabled in Wuqiao, Shantou, including purchasing daily necessities.

Registration: fill out the paper registration form or download the electronic version from the campus intranet or Weibo cloud disk, and submit by 24:00 on April 26. Electronic copies should be sent to 11jrliang@stu.edu.cn with the email subject "Team Name + Leader Name + Contact Number."

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Business School)
