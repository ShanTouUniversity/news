It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice that a bilingual Chinese–English academic symposium titled \"Dialogues across Differences\" will be held on Saturday, May 19, 2012. The symposium aims to create an academic dialogue between STU students and faculty that highlights interdisciplinary and inter-college communication. Presentations and projects in a variety of formats are sought to explore, question, and dialogue about and across \"Differences.\" Detailed information about possible presentations and project topics is provided in the attachment.
