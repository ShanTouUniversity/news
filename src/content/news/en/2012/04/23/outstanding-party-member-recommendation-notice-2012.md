It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a public notice that, in accordance with Document No. 9〔2012〕of the Shantou Municipal Working Committee of State Organs, a batch of outstanding grassroots party organizations and outstanding party members will be commended before July 1 this year. After democratic recommendation from the bottom up and approval by the university Party committee, the following are proposed for recommendation:

Proposed Outstanding Grassroots Party Organizations (4): Party Committee of the First Affiliated Hospital of STU Medical College; General Party Branch of the STU Mental Health Center; Party General Branch of the Law School; Faculty Party Branch of the Business School.

Proposed Outstanding Party Members (4): Wu Jian Hui, Zeng XX, Yang Qing Jie, Lin Jin Xiang.

The public notice period runs until 12:00 PM on April 24. Faculty and students with objections are requested to provide feedback in writing to the Organization and United Front Work Department. Contact: Tel 82902345, E-mail o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.
