It is reported that the Science College of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 118th session of the "Toward Modern Mathematics" academic lecture series will be held soon.

Speaker: Dr. Luo Jun (Chinese University of Hong Kong). Lecture title: Topology on Fractal Sets. Time: April 25, 2012 (Wednesday), 3:00 PM. Venue: Mathematics Laboratory (Room 412, Engineering North Building). All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
