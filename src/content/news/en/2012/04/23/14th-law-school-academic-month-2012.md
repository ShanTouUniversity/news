It is reported that the Law School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to create a strong academic research atmosphere, enhance students' exploratory and learning abilities, and elevate extracurricular academic and scientific activities to a new level, the Law School will hold the 14th Academic Month series of activities from April to May 2012. The activities are open to all undergraduate students of the Law School and include a research proposal design competition and a paper writing competition.

Research Proposal Design Competition: submission deadline is May 14, 2012; format is a research proposal (a sample is attached for reference); entries may be submitted individually or by freely formed groups; topics should cover society, law, management, economics, education, etc., and应具有实际价值和现实意义; the subject matter should be a scientific, advanced, and original outline or plan for social science surveys and academic papers; each entry must have an advisor who reviews and signs it; awarded prizes include first (10%), second (20%), third (30%), and excellence awards, with certificates issued by the school. Entries may also apply for science and technology project funding, with the school providing research funding support. Projects that pass the final review will be recommended to the university for the "STU Challenge Cup."

Paper Writing Competition: submission deadline is May 14, 2012; papers must be at least 3,000 Chinese characters in length.
