It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the second session of the 2012 ELC Faculty Lecture Series will be held soon.

Lecture theme: Language Learning and Intercultural Communication. Lecturers: Yolanda, Gaelle, and Kai. Venue: Science Lecture Hall. Time: 7:30 PM, Tuesday, April 24. All are welcome to attend.
