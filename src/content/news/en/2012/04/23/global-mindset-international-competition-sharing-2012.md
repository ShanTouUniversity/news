It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to help more students learn about the international competitions participated in by Business School students and the diverse experiences of those participants, the school will host the 2nd "Global Mindset" Student International Competition Sharing Session.

Time: April 25, 2012 (Wednesday), 2:30–4:30 PM. Venue: New Library Auditorium. Organizer: Business School.

Sharing teams: SIFE Team (awarded Outstanding Team, Best Tech Innovation Project, Excellence Award); GMC Team (First Prize in China Division, Best Organization Award, etc.); MBA Case Team (Best Business Exhibition Award, Environment Focus Award, Best Teamwork Award); MHBSC Team (No Borders Consulting Award, USA); TUBC and CICC Teams (presenting case competition preparation process and methods); SICC Team (sharing interesting social activities during competitions); Mai Mai Patent Team (sharing the China College Student Entrepreneurship Challenge Competition experience).

All faculty and students are warmly welcome to attend!
