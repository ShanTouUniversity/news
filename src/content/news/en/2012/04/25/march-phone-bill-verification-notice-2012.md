It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the electronic bills for office phone charges incurred from March 1 to March 31, 2012, have been sent by China Telecom Shantou Branch to the university telephone office and imported into the "Phone Management System." Detailed itemizations are available in the bills. Each unit'\''s verifier should log in to the system (http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn) to review.

For bills with no objections, click "Confirm Selected Bills" to approve. If any discrepancies are found between the bill and actual usage, email o_dhs@stu.edu.cn by April 27 for consolidated feedback to the telecom company. To facilitate inter-campus transfer deductions and timely payment to the telecom provider, any bill not contested or confirmed after the verification period will be deemed confirmed by default.

After verification, the Finance Office will deduct charges from each unit'\''s designated fund account on April 28. Any issues or suggestions regarding the "Phone Management System" should also be emailed to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
