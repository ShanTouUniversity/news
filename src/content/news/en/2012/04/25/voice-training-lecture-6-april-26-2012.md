It is reported that the English Language Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the sixth session of the 2012 Voice and Accent Training Lecture Series will be held on April 26, 2012 (Thursday) from 7:10 PM to 8:40 PM at E-Lecture Room 101.

Topic: Cross-Cultural Communication. Lecturer: Sohani Gandhioke. All are welcome to attend.
