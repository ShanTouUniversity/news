It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 110th session of the Business School Economics Salon will be held soon.

Speaker: Xiang Bing (Professor and Founding Dean, Cheung Kong Graduate School of Business). Topic: New Commercial Civilization and New Government-Business Relations. Date: April 28, 2012 (Saturday), 3:00–5:00 PM. Venue: New Library Auditorium. Language of lecture: Chinese.

Business School undergraduate and graduate students are requested to attend; all faculty and students are welcome.
