With the approval of the Shantou Municipal People’s Government, the *Measures for Managing Special Outpatient Conditions and Home Hospital Beds under Shantou’s Basic Medical Insurance for Urban Employees* have been issued and will take effect on July 1, 2012. The measures set out the management arrangements for special outpatient care and home hospital beds for participants in the urban-employee basic medical-insurance scheme.

The document was issued by the relevant Shantou authorities on April 25, 2012. Its scope, eligibility requirements, application procedures, and benefit standards should be determined from the full text of the measures.
