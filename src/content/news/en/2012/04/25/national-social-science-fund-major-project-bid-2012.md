It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding the "2012 National Social Science Fund Major Project (Batch 2) Bid Announcement" to all units, urging active applications. Bidders may view relevant information and fill out the bid form at http://www.npopss-cn.gov.cn or http://www.gdpplgopss.gov.cn (bid forms must be completed on computer, printed double-sided on A3 paper, and bound at the fold). Submissions must be made to the Research Office by June 10, 2012; late submissions will not be accepted.

Required materials: (1) Eight paper copies of the bid form, including at least one original; (2) one electronic copy of each bid form (WORD format). Contact: Huang Yumei, Wang Liju; Tel: 82902563.
