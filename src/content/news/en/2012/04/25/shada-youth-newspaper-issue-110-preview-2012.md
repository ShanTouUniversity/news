It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to enrich campus cultural life and with the support of the Youth League Committee and the efforts of all "STU Youth" reporters, Issue 110 of the "STU Youth" newspaper has been completed.

Preview highlights: A2 Campus Dynamics — March was the "Learn from Lei Feng National Volunteer Service Action Month"; STU faculty and students demonstrated the spirit of helping others in various ways. Meanwhile, the 2012 Advanced Outdoor Expedition Project is in full swing. A3 Stories — Not everyone knows what CIS is or understands the International Research Center. This issue'\''s STU Youth reporters go behind the scenes of CIS, exploring the innovation of curriculum reform and the internationalization of teaching staff. A4 Opinions — The "STU People Ask STU People" column answers questions students are hesitant to ask face-to-face. B1 Investigation — On textbook pricing under the credit system, the Dean of Academic Affairs has something to say. B2, B3 Life & Campus — New changes around campus beyond the still-under-construction new administrative building.
