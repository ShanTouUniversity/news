It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 24th session of the "Career Navigation Master Lecture Series" — Mobile Job-Seeking Guide — will be held soon.

Topic: Mobile Job-Seeking Guide. Time: April 27, 2012, 3:00–5:00 PM. Venue: New Library Auditorium. Content: Experience the application of the "Bai Cheng Qiu Zhi Bao" (Hundred-City Job-Seeking Treasure) mobile job-seeking software. Lecturer: Zheng Yichun (Manager of Human Resources, Shantou Recruitment Network). All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
