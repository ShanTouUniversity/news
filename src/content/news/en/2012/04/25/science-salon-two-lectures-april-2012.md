It is reported that the Science College of Shantou University recently issued a notice that two academic lectures will be held as part of the "Science Salon" series.

Lecture 1: Visualizing Small Talk of Microorganisms. Speaker: Dr. Xu Yuquan (University of Arizona, USA). Content: Using novel mass spectrometry imaging technology to study the communication and dialogue of microbial secondary metabolites among microorganisms. Dr. Xu's research results over the past five years have been published in leading international journals including Nature Chemical Biology (Nature subjournal), Chemistry & Biology (Cell subjournal), PNAS, and Angewandte Chemie International Edition.

Lecture 2: Synthetic Biology: Engineering Cells to Enhance Product Yields. Speaker: Dr. Liang Quanfeng (Shandong University). Content: Synthetic biology is a rapidly developing emerging discipline with important applications across many fields. The lecture will discuss the application of synthetic biology in metabolic engineering, combining the research group's recent achievements and focusing on key factors in cell metabolic engineering, regulation of metabolic pathways, and the relationship between host cells and metabolic pathway construction.
