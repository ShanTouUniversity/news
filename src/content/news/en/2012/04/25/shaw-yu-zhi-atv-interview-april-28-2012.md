It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the popular Hong Kong cultural talk show "Gao Zhisen Wei Bo" on ATV Home Channel will broadcast an interview with Dean Shaw Yu-Zhi of the college on April 28, 2012, at 9:30 PM. Dean Shaw will enthusiastically share with host Gao Zhisen about the development and prospects of art and design education in China. All faculty and students are encouraged to watch.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design)
