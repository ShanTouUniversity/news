It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding the call for papers from the Shantou Social Science Federation for a theoretical symposium on "Promoting Shantou-Chaozhou-Jieyang Integration," to be held in the second half of 2012. Interested contributors are requested to write papers according to the notice requirements and submit the electronic final version to shekelian@21cn.com within the specified time.

The symposium aims to thoroughly implement the provincial party committee and government'\''s directives on accelerating Shantou-Chaozhou-Jieyang integration and the "Eastern Guangdong Regional Economic and Social Development Planning Outline (2011–2015)" vision for building the "Shantou-Chaozhou-Jieyang" metropolitan area, as well as the strategic部署 of the 10th Shantou Municipal Party Congress. Topics include: enhancing Shantou'\''s role as a regional center city; accelerating resource integration among the three cities; and promoting comprehensive cooperation in infrastructure, industry, culture, education, healthcare, social security, environmental protection, tourism, and public security with livelihood improvement as the entry point.
