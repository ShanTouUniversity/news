It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following review and recommendation by the STU Bidding and Tendering Leadership Group, Shantou No. 2 Architectural Design Institute has been selected to undertake the renovation design task for 36 units of faculty dormitories and Type C faculty dormitories at a total contract sum of 168,000 yuan (RMB).

Public comment period: April 25–27, 2012. Bidder: Shantou University Infrastructure Office; Contact: Mr. Liu, Mr. Lin; Tel: (0754)82903890; Fax: (0754)82902314. Complaint受理机构：汕头大学监察审计处；电话：(0754)82902346；传真：(0754)82903663。
