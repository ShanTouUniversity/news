It is reported that the Student Affairs Office, the Youth League Committee, and the Student Union of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that, to cultivate students\' critical thinking knowledge and abilities and enrich campus cultural life in alignment with the人文 community construction requirements of the student community X-Plan, a lecture activity is being organized.

Lecture topic: "The World Through a Debater\'s Eyes — Huang Zhizhong." The guest speaker is Mr. Huang Zhizhong. Lecture time: April 15 (Sunday) at 7:30 PM. Venue: New Library Lecture Hall. Registration: online at http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ until 12:00 PM on April 13. All are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee of STU)
