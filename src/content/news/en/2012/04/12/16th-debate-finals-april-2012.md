It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to carry forward the excellent tradition of debate, cultivate students\' critical thinking abilities, and enhance cultural literacy, the 16th STU College Student Debate Competition was held from March to April under the theme "Truth · The Strongest Sound." After nearly a month of fierce competition, the Journalism School and Business School debate teams have advanced to the championship match, while the Medical College and Arts College teams have advanced to the third-place match.

Third-place match: Medical College (affirmative) vs. Arts College (negative); topic: "Weibo helps the public understand突发事件 vs. Weibo hinders the public from understanding突发事件"; time: April 13 (Friday), 8:00 PM; venue: Moot Court.

Championship match: Business School (affirmative) vs. Journalism School (negative); topic: "Seeing the world online is more rewarding than seeing it in person vs. Seeing the world in person is more rewarding than seeing it online"; time: April 14 (Saturday), 7:30 PM; venue: Moot Court. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
