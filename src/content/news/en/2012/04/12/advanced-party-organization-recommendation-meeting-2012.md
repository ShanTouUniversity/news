It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that a work meeting on the recommendation of outstanding party organizations for commendation will be held on April 16 at 3:00 PM in Meeting Room 311, Administrative Building. Heads of the Medical College Party Committee, all general Party branches on the main campus, and directly affiliated Party branches are requested to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department, Party Committee)
