It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following the decision of the 4th university leadership meeting held on April 10, 2012, Wei Chuliang is proposed for appointment as Deputy Director of the Department of Electronic Engineering, College of Engineering, Shantou University. In accordance with the "Interim Measures for the Appointment of Administrative Cadres at Shantou University" (汕大党字〔2009〕第47号), a public notice is hereby issued.

The consultation period runs from April 12 to April 18, 2012. Units or individuals with objections may submit written comments to the Personnel Office before the deadline (unit submissions must bear the official seal; individual submissions must include the real name). Tel: 82903390, Email: pscu@stu.edu.cn.
