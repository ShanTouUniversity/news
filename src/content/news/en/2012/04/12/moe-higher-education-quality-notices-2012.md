It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice forwarding Document No. 44〔2012〕of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and the "Several Opinions of the Ministry of Education on Comprehensively Improving the Quality of Higher Education." All units are requested to study and implement the document.
