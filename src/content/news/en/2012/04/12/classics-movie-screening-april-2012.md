It is reported that the Christian Studies Center of the College of Arts and Letters at Shantou University recently issued a notice that a course film series will accompany Prof. Marjorie King's course "The Power of Moral Leadership: A Study of the Impact of Individual Leaders on History." From Weeks 7 to 9, a classic film related to the course will be screened every Tuesday at 7:30 PM in Room E404. The three films are: *Gandhi* (1982, dir. Richard Attenborough), *King* (1978, dir. Abby Mann), and *Erin Brockovich* (2000, dir. Steven Soderbergh). More detailed information can be found on the poster.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
