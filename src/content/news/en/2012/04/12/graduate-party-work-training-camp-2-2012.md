It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to further improve the political theory level and party work capabilities of graduate party workers, familiarize them with relevant party work procedures, and promote the standardization and institutionalization of graduate party work, the second Graduate Party Work Training Camp will be held on April 13–14, 2012. Secretaries (deputy secretaries), organization commissioners, and propaganda commissioners of all graduate party branches are requested to attend on time. Specific arrangements are available in the attachment.
