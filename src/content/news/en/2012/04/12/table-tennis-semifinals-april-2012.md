It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the group stage of the staff table tennis team competition has concluded. The group standings are as follows: Group A — 1st: College of Science, 2nd: College of Engineering, 3rd: Arts Joint Team, 4th: College of Arts and Letters; Group B — 1st: Administration Team, 2nd: Resource Management, 3rd: Library/Science/News/Research Joint Team, 4th: Law/Business Joint Team.

The semifinals were held on the afternoon of April 12 at the Staff Union first-floor table tennis room. Matchups: College of Science vs. Resource Management (Table 1), Administration Team vs. College of Engineering (Table 2).
