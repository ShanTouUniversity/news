It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold the second lecture in its advanced outdoor training series on April 14, 2012. Sun Lingye, founder of TerraTribes, also known in Chinese as Dadi Buluo Tanxian, will share his experience in outdoor exploration and related practice.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | TerraTribes outdoor exploration |
| Speaker | Sun Lingye, founder of TerraTribes |
| Time | 16:00–18:00, April 14, 2012 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |

Sun previously worked with China Central Television and Chengdu Television before settling in Yangshuo, Guangxi, where he pursued work related to outdoor sports and exploration. He trained in scuba diving and other outdoor sports in Thailand, Nepal, Vietnam and Canada, and has continued studying courses offered by the Wilderness Medical Associates North America (WMA). TerraTribes emphasizes teamwork, environmental protection, mutual respect, responsibility and an adventurous spirit.

The lecture is open to faculty and students. Further details and the speaker profile are available in the OA attachments.
