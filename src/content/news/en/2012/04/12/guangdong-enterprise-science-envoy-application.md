It is reported that Shantou University's Research Office recently circulated application arrangements for the Guangdong Enterprise Science and Technology Envoy program to relevant campus units. The program accepts applications year-round with batch-by-batch review and recognition. With the first 2012 batch of envoys already announced, the university will soon organize recommendations for the second batch.

## Basic Requirements for Envoys

Under the program, a Guangdong Enterprise Science and Technology Envoy must be a teacher or researcher holding a doctoral degree or an associate-senior professional title, with solid expertise in the relevant industry field, strong capabilities in R&D and coordination, and a strong sense of responsibility. Two groups receive priority:

| Priority Criteria | Description |
| --- | --- |
| National key talent program awardees | Supported by the Changjiang Scholars Program, the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, the Hundred Talents Program, etc. |
| Established Guangdong industry ties | Familiarity with relevant Guangdong industries and prior cooperation with Guangdong enterprises |

## Application and Renewal Procedures

The current round covers two scenarios. New applicants may register freely: they first complete the online process through the envoy management information system, then print online the watermarked "Provincial-Ministerial Enterprise Science and Technology Envoy Recommendation Form" in three copies and the "Guangdong Enterprise Science and Technology Envoy Dispatch Agreement" in five copies. After signatures and seals from both the envoy and the host enterprise, the materials go to the Research Office for centralized submission.

Envoys whose previous agreements have expired should contact their host enterprises as soon as possible to refill the agreement in the system; those switching to a new enterprise may contact it directly. Completed agreements are likewise printed in five copies online and submitted through the Research Office after signing and sealing.

## Timeline

Faculty intending to apply or renew their agreements must complete all procedures by May 21 so that the university can recommend candidates for the second 2012 batch. Applications for other batches are accepted on an ongoing basis with no deadline. On-campus contacts Luo Yingguang and Deng Yong are available for inquiries.
