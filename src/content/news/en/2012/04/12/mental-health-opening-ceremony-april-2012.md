It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the opening ceremony of the 7th Mental Health Awareness Month, along with a live hypnosis event, will be held on April 17, 2012 (Tuesday) at 7:30 PM in the Science Lecture Hall. Prof. Cai Zhonghuai will lead the hypnosis session. All faculty and students are welcome to participate.
