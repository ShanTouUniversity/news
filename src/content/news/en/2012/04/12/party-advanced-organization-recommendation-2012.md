It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Shantou University Party Committee recently issued a notice that, in accordance with Document No. 27〔2012〕of the Guangdong Provincial Working Committee of the Education Party Committee, before July 1 this year, the Organization Department of the CPC Central Committee, the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee, and the Provincial Education Working Committee will separately commend outstanding grassroots party organizations and outstanding party members from the 2010–2012 Campaign to Create Advanced Party Organizations and Keep Members Advanced.

Quotas allocated: 2 outstanding grassroots party organizations and 1 outstanding party member for the Provincial Education Working Committee from the Central Organization Department; 12 outstanding grassroots party organizations and 6 outstanding party members for the Provincial Education Working Committee from the Provincial Party Committee; 1 outstanding grassroots party organization and 2 outstanding party members for STU from the Provincial Education Working Committee. Each unit is asked to carefully review the relevant conditions and requirements and make recommendations accordingly. The university Party committee will decide and submit recommended candidates based on the recommendations received.

Units recommending candidates for national commendation must submit their recommendation reports, recommendation and approval forms, and事迹 materials (approximately 2,000 characters) to the Organization and United Front Work Department by April 15. Units recommending candidates for provincial and educational system commendation must submit their materials (approximately 1,000 characters) by April 27. Electronic copies should be sent to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn. Contact: Ji Rongxin, Tel: 82902345.
