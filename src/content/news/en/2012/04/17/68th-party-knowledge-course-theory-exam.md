It is reported that the theory examination for the 68th "Basic Party Knowledge" course of the Party School will be held from 7:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, April 22. Participants are required to attend at their designated venues.

## Venue Assignments

| Venue | Participants |
|-------|--------------|
| Lecture Hall 1 | Students from the College of Liberal Arts, Law School, Business School, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, and Veritas College |
| Lecture Hall 3 | Students from the College of Science, College of Engineering, College of Arts, Graduate School, and faculty/staff participants |

Examinees must sit at every other seat in their assigned hall.

## Eligibility and Examination Rules

The exam is open to all students of the 68th cohort and to students from the 67th cohort whose deferred or make-up exam applications have been approved. Those unable to attend due to special circumstances must obtain approval from their general Party branch (or directly-affiliated branch) and complete deferral procedures at the Party School office; unexcused absence will result in disqualification. Students who fail or whose deferral is approved may take one make-up exam in the next cohort.
