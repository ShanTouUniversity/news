It is reported that Shantou University recently issued a notice that, following the decision of the 4th university leadership meeting held on April 10, 2012, the Shantou University Laboratory Work Committee has been established, with its office located under the Academic Affairs Office.

The committee members are as follows: Director Wen Xiaobo; Deputy Directors Du Hong, Chen Yaowen, Qiu Jingfeng, and Huang Jingxia; Members (in alphabetical order): Chen Shaobo, Chen Yangzhou, Chen Jiehui, Chen Qisheng, Fan Dongsheng, Huang Xiaochun, Li Yuanyou, Li Xutao, Ouyang Yandong, Ouyang Feng, Xiong Guangjing, and Yin Yegao. Office Secretary: Chen Yangzhou. The Shantou University Laboratory Management Committee is hereby dissolved.
