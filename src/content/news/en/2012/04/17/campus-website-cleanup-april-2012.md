It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to effectively curb the spread of illegal and harmful information on the internet, the university is launching a campus website harmful information inspection and cleanup campaign in accordance with the unified deployment of higher-level authorities. All website managers are asked to conduct a thorough inspection and cleanup of websites under their management, strictly preventing the spread of illegal and harmful information through campus websites, and carrying out comprehensive sweeps of key areas to ensure no blind spots remain.

Cleanup scope includes: websites, forums, blogs, online communities, network navigation, and network resource-sharing sites (FTP, BT downloads, etc.). Key cleanup targets include: information on the sale of firearms and explosives and their manufacturing methods; personal identity information, exam candidate data, stockholder information, and medical records; online prostitution-related information and videos, animations, games, images, and links that depict obscene or pornographic content.

Website managers are expected to approach this cleanup with seriousness and responsibility. Websites with unimplemented safety management responsibilities, unverifiable operating entities or manager information, or uncleared illegal and harmful information will be taken offline. Websites with unapproved interactive sections will also be taken down and reported to the university. Unregistered websites must be registered at http://isp.stu.edu.cn. Please submit cleanup results to the Network Center (stunic@stu.edu.cn) by April 25. Contact: Mr. Li, Tel: 82903550-8811.
