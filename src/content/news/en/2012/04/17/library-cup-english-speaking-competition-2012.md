It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a notice that the 2nd "Library Cup" Guangdong College Student English Speaking Competition will be held this year. The theme is "Reading · Sharing · Competition." As part of the "World Book Day" series of activities, the competition advocates joyful reading, scientific learning, and the sharing of digital wisdom.

The competition leverages the library\'s public service characteristics, the openness of the internet, and the MyET intelligent speech analysis scoring mechanism (Chinese Patent No. 02160031.7) to host a digital, networked English speaking contest. Students can register and compete online without time or location constraints, using the scientific method of listening and speaking to improve their English proficiency. The competition also aims to boost usage of the library\'s digital resources, guide students to make better use of library materials for independent study, promote exchange and competition among students from different universities, and share the joy of reading and learning.

Eligibility: college students across Guangdong Province. Registration and competition period: April 16 to May 10, 2012, 11:59 PM. Results announced: May 15, 2012. Registration: free online. Competition format: individual ranking matches via the MyET online scoring system. Scoring: the system records all scores achieved during the competition period; the highest score counts toward final rankings. Competition content: randomly selected from classic materials in *Sky English Classroom* and *Peng Meng Hui English Magazine*.
