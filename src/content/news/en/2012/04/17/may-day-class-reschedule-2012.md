It is reported that the Graduate School and the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued notices regarding class schedule adjustments for the Labor Day holiday, in accordance with the university\'s "Notice on the 2012 Labor Day Holiday Schedule."

The adjustments are as follows: May 1 (Tuesday) — classes suspended; classes originally scheduled for April 30 (Monday) will be held on April 28 (Saturday); classes originally scheduled for April 28 (Saturday) will be held on April 30 (Monday); classes on April 29 (Sunday) will proceed as normal.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School, Academic Affairs Office)
