It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to provide more convenient and efficient services to users, the campus card voice self-service inquiry service has been launched effective April 17. Users can call 82903414 (extension 3414 from within campus) to check card balances, report lost cards, and change inquiry passwords.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
