It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice that Xiacuo Village in Leiling Town, Chaonan District, Shantou City, is a remote village. Xiacuo Primary School has 213 students, none of whom have ever had a personal photograph or a class group photo. The school has no image archives of its students whatsoever.

Photography is not merely about recording — it can also be an act of public welfare. To this end, the college is organizing the first "Childhood Impressions — Cheung Kong Journalist Rural Photography Trip," bringing faculty and students to Xiacuo Primary School to capture the smiles of children. The college hopes that through this photography activity, participating students will experience how photography can bring joy to those being photographed, and how photojournalism can convey warmth and care between people.

A campus-wide donation drive will also be held; all funds raised will be used to print photographs and produce photo albums. Your support will give the children of Leiling an unforgettable childhood memory. Donations can be made in person at the administrative office of the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication, placed in the donation box at the entrance. Any amount is welcome.
