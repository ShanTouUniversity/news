It is reported that the Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Resource Management Office of Shantou University recently issued a joint notice that, as of April 17, some dormitories still have unpaid utility bills for March 2012 and earlier periods. In accordance with university regulations, power will be disconnected for these dormitories starting April 18 and will not be restored until all outstanding fees are paid in full.

From April 18 to 20, the Network Center will perform card deduction twice daily: the first deduction at 10:30 AM, with power restored between 2:30 PM and 4:30 PM for successfully paid dormitories; the second deduction at 4:30 PM, with power restored between 5:30 PM and 8:00 PM. For payments made after 4:30 PM on any given day, power will be restored between 2:30 PM and 4:30 PM the following day. After April 20, only one daily deduction will be performed (at 4:30 PM).

Students with insufficient campus card balances should top up promptly at auto-recharge machines. Students with insufficient bank account balances should deposit sufficient funds during banking hours and complete the transfer. Students who have lost their campus cards can set up "proxy payment" through the utility management system or pay cash at the Bank of China STU Branch (account: 8030-15331308094001) and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for recording. The Finance Office does not accept cash payments directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, Resource Management Office)
