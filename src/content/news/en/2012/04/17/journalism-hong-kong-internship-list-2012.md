It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University recently issued a notice announcing the 2012 summer Hong Kong internship selection list. Thirteen students have been selected for internships at Hong Kong media organizations. The selected students are: Li Xingzhi, Xu Jiaming, Li Yuling, Li Huijuan, Lu Xuehua, Qiu Wenli, Zhao Yang, Mo Sixin, He Lanxiao, Wang Jindan, Pan Yanhong, Zhu Chaogui, and He Qiling.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Journalism and Communication)
