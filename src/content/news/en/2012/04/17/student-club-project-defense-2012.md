It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Societies Federation will hold the project defense for student club activities for the spring semester of 2011–2012 on April 18, 2012.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 14:30–17:30, April 18, 2012 |
| Venue | Room E201, Building E |
| Participants | 12 clubs including the Guitar Association |
| Requirements | Dress neatly, check in before 14:15, answer panel questions concisely without interruption, and stay until scores are announced |
