It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 109th session of the Business School Economics Salon will be held on April 20, 2012 (Friday) from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM in Meeting Room 3, Jiaoliu Building.

The speaker is Prof. Li Xinchun (Professor and Doctoral Supervisor, Sun Yat-sen Business School). The lecture topic is "Entrepreneurship and China's Economic Transformation." The lecture will be conducted in Chinese. Students from the Business School undergraduate and graduate programs are requested to attend, and all faculty and students are welcome.
