It is reported that the Cheung Kong College of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center at Shantou University recently issued a notice that the center will launch a "Cross-Strait Drama Lecture Series" this semester. The series aims to promote holistic education on campus by using drama — a comprehensive art form distinct from music — to encourage students to consciously cultivate teamwork spirit, inspire them to fully develop problem-solving abilities through artistic practice, and discover that drama art at STU possesses not only aesthetic value but also diversified capability-building potential.

Lecture topic: "Dramatic Quality and Modernity." Speaker: Lin Ke Huan. Time: April 20, 2012, 7:30 PM. Venue: Science Lecture Hall. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong College of Art and Design, Arts Education Center)
