It is reported that the STU Library recently issued a notice that the China International Book Donation Center is a non-profit public-welfare department established in 2011 by the China Education Books & Periodicals Import & Export Corporation, in accordance with regulations from the Ministry of Education and the General Administration of Press and Publication on accepting overseas publications gifted by foreign institutions or individuals. The center mainly handles the import and redistribution of books donated by the "Asia Foundation" and "Asia Bridge" programs, serving society and universities, promoting cultural exchange, and advancing educational public welfare.

The China International Book Donation Network (http://www.cidbook.org) is the center\'s online service platform. To improve the quality of gift book selection for the STU Library, all faculty and students are encouraged to visit the website to browse and recommend desired titles. When recommending books, please ensure the "donation copies remaining" column shows available copies, and promptly send the book title, donation number, and donor organization information via email to the library.
