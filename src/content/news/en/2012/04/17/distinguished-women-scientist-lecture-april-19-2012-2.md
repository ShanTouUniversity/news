It is reported that the International Exchange Office of Shantou University recently reissued a notice that the Li Ka Shing Foundation Distinguished Women Scientist Lecture Series will be held on April 19, 2012 (Thursday) from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM in the New Library Auditorium.

The guest speaker is Prof. Judith P. Klinman, Professor of Chemistry at the University of California, Berkeley. Prof. Klinman served as the first female department chair of the Chemistry Department from 2000 to 2003. The lecture title is "A Personal and Scientific Odyssey." All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
