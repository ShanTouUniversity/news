It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the National Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning Office has established the "National Philosophy and Social Sciences Outstanding Achievement Library" to集中推出反映当前我国哲学社会科学研究前沿水平的成果，充分发挥哲学社会科学优秀成果和优秀人才的示范引领作用，推进学科体系、学术观点、科研方法创新，鼓励广大专家学者以优良学风打造更多精品力作，推动我国哲学社会科学进一步繁荣发展。

The 2012 application for the "Achievement Library" has begun. Applicants should generally be full-time research personnel currently employed at the university and hold at least an associate senior professional title. Applied works must have been completed but not yet publicly published, and must not have any intellectual property disputes. Works must adhere to the correct orientation and fully reflect the positions, viewpoints, and methods of Marxism. They must possess originality, pioneering spirit, and frontier significance, making a major contribution to promoting economic and social development and discipline construction. Works must conform to academic norms, demonstrate rigorous scholarship, and adopt a plain writing style. The acceptable formats are Chinese academic monographs, thematic collections of papers, or thematic research reports (textbooks, translations, papers, reference books, data compilations, popular readings, software, and other forms will not be accepted), with a word count generally between 200,000 and 600,000 Chinese characters.
