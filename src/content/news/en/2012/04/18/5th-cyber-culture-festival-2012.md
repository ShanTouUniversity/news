It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the 5th STU Cyber Culture Festival will be held from April 18 to 27, 2012, organized by the STU "Chuàng — Path to Success" Network. The event aims to improve students\\' network culture literacy, enrich extracurricular life, and guide the reasonable and effective use of online resources.

Target participants: all faculty and students. Organizer: Youth League Committee of Shantou University. Co-organizer: STU "Chuàng — Path to Success" Network.

Activities include: (1) "Crazy Subtitle Group" — Original Subtitle Competition: submission and judging from April 18 to April 27, 8:00 PM. Participants download video materials from ftp://10.10.40.23:21/ (username and password both "ftp") and modify subtitle files to express their ideas in a humorous manner. (2) "Micro-STU" — Tell Your Story with STU: submission and judging from April 18 to April 27, 8:00 PM. Participants share stories about their experiences at STU via Weibo posts. (3) "Millionaire" — Online Knowledge Quiz: preliminary round on April 21 at 3:30 PM in Room 305, Building E; final round on April 27 at 7:30 PM.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
