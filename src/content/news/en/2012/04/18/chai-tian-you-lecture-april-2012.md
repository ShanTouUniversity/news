It is reported that the Graduate School of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to help graduate students master the norms of scientific research, establish a rigorous academic attitude, further advance the construction of scientific ethics and academic standards, and improve the quality of talent cultivation, the Graduate School has invited Academician Chai Tianyou, a renowned expert in control theory and control engineering and member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, to deliver a lecture to STU graduate students.

Lecture topic: "Innovative Talent Cultivation." Speaker: Academician Chai Tianyou. Time: April 19, 2012, 7:30–9:00 PM. Venue: Room 205, Science Building. Students from science and engineering disciplines are requested to attend on time. All faculty and students are welcome.
