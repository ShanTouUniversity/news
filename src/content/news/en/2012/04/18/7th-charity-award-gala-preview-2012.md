It is reported that the "Li Ka Shing Foundation · STU Medical College Medical Poverty Alleviation Project" recently won the "Most Influential Charity Project" award at the 7th China Charity Awards, receiving commendation from the Ministry of Civil Affairs of China.

CCTV Channel 1 will broadcast the gala live on April 20 at 8:05 PM, and CCTV Channel 12 will broadcast it on April 21 at 7:24 PM. All faculty and students are encouraged to watch.
