It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to enrich campus cultural life and advance the humanistic community建设 under the student community X-Plan, the university issued on April 16 a notice announcing the "Life Aspiration — Reflections on Life for College Students" lecture in commemoration of the 93rd anniversary of the May Fourth Movement.

As of 5:00 PM on April 17, a total of 90 faculty and students had successfully reserved spots through the activity reservation system. The list of successful applicants is published in the attachment. Those who reserved spots are requested to attend the lecture on time. The lecture will be held on April 20 from 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM in the New Library Lecture Hall.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU, Student Affairs Office)
