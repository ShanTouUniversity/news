It is reported that the final of the 3rd China Postal Savings Bank Cup Campus Entrepreneurship Challenge was held on April 13, 2012, at the China Youth University of Political Studies, where two STU student teams won awards.

Launched on December 9, 2011, in Beijing, the competition involved 10 universities nationwide, with 10 teams reaching the final after preliminary selection, project practice, business plan preparation and final defense.

## Awards

| Award | Team |
| --- | --- |
| Grand Prize (10,000 yuan) | Green Ocean (members: Luo JL and others) |
| Excellence Award | Maimai Patent (members: Yu PK and others) |

Member details are anonymized to protect student privacy. The competition provides a platform for students to develop entrepreneurship skills.
