It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, in accordance with the "Shantou University Course Assessment Management Measures" and the 2011–2012 academic calendar, final examinations for the current semester will be uniformly scheduled for Weeks 17 and 18.

All courses scheduled through Week 15 and beyond will have their final exams in Weeks 17 and 18. For other courses, departments must complete the "Shantou University Final Examination Report Form." Examination courses are to be filled by the teaching unit where the instructor is based. Courses offered by the Social Sciences Department, Computer Teaching Center, and Institute of Higher Education, as well as General Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Calculus, Probability and Statistics, will have exam times and venues arranged by the Academic Affairs Office. Other courses are to be arranged by the teaching unit\\'s academic affairs officer in the credit system. Officers are urged to carefully verify course numbers and class codes to avoid errors. Assessment formats may include open-book exams, closed-book exams, oral exams, course papers, or combinations thereof, appropriate to each course\\'s characteristics. Exam duration is generally 120 minutes.
