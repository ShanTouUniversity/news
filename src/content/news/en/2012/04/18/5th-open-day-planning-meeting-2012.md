It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to strengthen communication and coordination and better prepare for the 5th STU Open Day, a coordinating meeting will be held on April 20 (Friday) at 10:00 AM in Meeting Room 306, Gongnan Building.

Representatives from the General Office, Alumni Association Office, Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, Admissions Office, Resource Management Office, Publicity Department, News Center, Network and Information Center, Library, Physical Education Department, English Language Center, Arts Education Center, and all colleges (residential colleges) are requested to send personnel responsible for related work to attend the meeting.

Agenda: (1) Each department reports on progress; (2) Division, coordination, and implementation of cross-departmental collaborative projects; (3) Discussion and resolution of pending matters.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
