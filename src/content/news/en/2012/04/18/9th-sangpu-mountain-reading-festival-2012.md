It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University recently issued a notice that, to create a strong campus cultural atmosphere, foster good reading habits among STU students, and guide students to develop a habit of reading, the university will hold the 9th STU Sangpu Mountain Reading Festival.

Theme: Reading · Joyful Reading. Event period: April 18 to May 5, 2012. Organizers: Youth League Committee of Shantou University and STU Library. Co-organizer: STU Student Club Federation.

Main activity: "Navigating the Sea of Learning with Information" — Explore the Library. Dates: April 18 to April 30. Venue: Library. Purpose: to enhance faculty and students\\' ability to access and utilize discipline-related library information resources, and to give them a close-up view of the library\\'s subject service model. Activities include: (1) Discovering the Library You Did Not Know About — library leaders or staff will introduce management practices; participants will rotate through groups visiting the Subject Services Department, Circulation Department, Reading Room, and Acquisitions and Cataloging Department; (2) After the activity, participants may write a report to enter the library\\'s Best Report Selection Competition, with the main content being suggestions and opinions for the library; (3) Registration period: April 18 to April 24; (4) Activity date: April 25, gathering at the library service desk.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee of STU)
