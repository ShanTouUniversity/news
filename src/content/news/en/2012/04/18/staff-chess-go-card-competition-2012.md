It is reported that the Staff Union of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the union will host staff chess, Go, and card competitions.

Chess competition: April 28 to May 12, venue at the Second Floor Meeting Room of the Staff Union. Go competition: April 28 to May 12, venue at the Second Floor Karaoke Room of the Staff Union. Card competition: preliminary round on April 25 (Wednesday) at 8:00 PM; final round on April 28 (Saturday) at 8:00 PM; venue at the Third Floor of the Staff Union. The competition features men\\'s and women\\'s divisions, with the number of advancing teams determined by the number of registrations.

All interested staff members are welcome to register. Registration method: complete the registration form and send it to the Staff Union email at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn. Card competition registration deadline: April 24. Chess and Go competition registration deadline: April 26. Contact: 82902983.
