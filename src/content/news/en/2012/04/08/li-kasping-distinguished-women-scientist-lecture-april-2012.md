It is reported that the Office of International Cooperation and Exchange of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the Li Ka Shing Foundation Distinguished Women in Science Award Lecture Series will be held on April 19, 2012 (Thursday) from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM in the New Library Lecture Hall.

The speaker is Prof. Judith P. Klinman, Professor in the Department of Chemistry at the University of California, Berkeley. The lecture title is \"A Personal and Scientific Odyssey.\" All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
