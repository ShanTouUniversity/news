It is reported that the Network and Information Center of Shantou University recently issued a notice that the campus network fiber optic cable was severed due to construction at the Administrative Building. The outage began at approximately 11:40 AM on April 8, 2012. All campus network users are unable to access the internet.

The center is conducting emergency repairs and has activated partial backup lines. There will be no separate notice regarding the restoration of service. Users are asked to spread this information to one another. The center apologizes for any inconvenience caused.
