It is reported that Zhicheng College of Shantou University has published Issue 4 (No. 33 overall) of its newsletter "Zhicheng Life," reviewing recent activities at the college.

Key content of this issue includes: from July 3 to 10, the college held the fifth "Student Mentor Development Camp," in which 39 incoming (including reserve) student mentors traveled to Xilong Village, Tiepu, Chao'an to conduct education on filial piety and respect for teachers, team building and volunteer teaching, and jointly held a "Gratitude and Dedication, Filial Piety and Love for the Elderly" evening gala with the village committee that drew more than 500 villagers; on July 11, the college held a "quality development summer camp" for 53 students from Guangzhou No. 1 Middle School; on June 6, the 2011-2012 academic year commendation assembly honored outstanding student Party members, student mentors, student cadres and others; from June 30 to July 2, a 2012 integrated thinking work seminar was held; on June 10, the first Chaoshan university calligraphy and painting joint exhibition opened at the college; and on June 11, Jing Lihu, deputy secretary of the provincial education work committee, visited the college accompanied by the STU Party secretary, praising it as "a big family."
