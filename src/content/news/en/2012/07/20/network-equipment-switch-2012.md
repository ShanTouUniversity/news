It is reported that, as planned, Shantou University's Network and Information Center will switch the network access equipment in the campus network center machine room from 10:00 p.m. on July 21 to 6:00 a.m. on July 22, 2012. During the switch, each server in the machine room will experience 30 to 45 minutes of interruption, affecting internet access for all campus network users and external services of on-campus systems. The restoration time will not be separately announced.

The center asked users to inform each other and apologized for any inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
