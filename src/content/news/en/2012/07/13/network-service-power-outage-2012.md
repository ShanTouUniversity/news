It is reported that, according to the power outage plan issued by the Resource Management Office, all machine rooms — including the campus network data center — fall within the outage scope, scheduled from 8:00 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. on July 14 and July 15, 2012.

The Network and Information Center announced that the data center's UPS backup power can support operations for more than four hours, during which the outbound network, on-campus application systems and website services will remain largely unaffected; internet access for users in other areas of the campus will be affected (see the Resource Management Office's outage notice for specific areas and times), and all office-area networks will be affected during the power outage at the new library. If the actual outage exceeds four hours, the center will shut down secondary services to prioritize the core network and important systems; once stable power is restored, suspended services will be restarted as soon as possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
