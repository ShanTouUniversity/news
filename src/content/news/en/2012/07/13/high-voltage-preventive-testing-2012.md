It is reported that, in accordance with the requirements of relevant national power industry standards, high-voltage equipment must undergo regular preventive testing to assess the operating condition of high-voltage power facilities and ensure the safe and stable operation of the power supply system. To minimize the impact of outages on faculty and students, STU's utilities center has arranged preventive testing of high-voltage equipment on the high-voltage side of some campus distribution rooms during the summer vacation.

Power will be cut off zone by zone according to plan, with the first round of outages scheduled from 8:00 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. on July 14 and 15. Specific outage times for each area follow the outage plan published by the Resource Management Office.

> ⚠️ The detailed zone-by-zone outage schedule is as published on the OA system.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
