It is reported that the Resource Management Office and the Infrastructure Office issued a construction notice on July 13, 2012, announcing the installation of shading panels on Student Dormitories A and B.

The project was approved and awarded through competitive bidding to Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd., with main works including scaffolding and panel installation.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Shading panel installation and scaffolding for Dormitories A and B |
| Contractor | Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Start date | July 14, 2012 |
| Completion | Before August 20, 2012 |
| Issued by | Resource Management Office, Infrastructure Office |

During construction, noise, vibration and dust are expected. Pedestrians are advised to pay attention to safety, not to enter the construction site or closed intersections without permission, and to avoid lingering nearby.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office, Infrastructure Office)
