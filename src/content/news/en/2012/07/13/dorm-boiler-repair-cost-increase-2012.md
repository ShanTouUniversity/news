It is reported that the repair project for Shantou University's dormitory fuel hot-water boilers was approved at the university leadership meeting on June 5, 2012, with an approved budget of about 155,000 yuan, to be carried out as a major overhaul during the summer vacation.

The Equipment Office conducted an open tender for the project, with bidding closed on June 29. As this is an equipment repair project whose parts must match the original equipment to remain compatible, only the original manufacturer, Dison, submitted a bid. With the approval of the university's bidding leadership group, the evaluation panel opened the bid documents on July 3 and conducted single-source negotiations with Dison, whose quotation was 224,510 yuan. Replacing the boiler body and the water softener would cost about 155,000 yuan, but ancillary equipment such as circulating water pumps and plate heat exchangers plus installation and commissioning would add about 75,000 yuan to ensure normal boiler operation — costs not included in the original estimate, which covered only the boiler body replacement. After repeated negotiations, the company agreed to lower the total price to 210,000 yuan.

As a result, the project requires an additional 55,000 yuan for the ancillary equipment. The Resource Management Office is now publicizing the plan and welcomes comments and suggestions from faculty and students.
