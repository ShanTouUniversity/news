It is reported that, as arranged by the university, Shantou University will paint the public stairwells of dormitories H and I during the summer vacation. Construction starts on Wednesday, August 1, mainly involving removing the old paint layer, repairing hollow wall areas and repainting, and is scheduled for completion by August 14.

The construction will produce some noise, vibration and dust, causing inconvenience to students and teachers using these dormitories. The university asked faculty, staff and students for their understanding.
