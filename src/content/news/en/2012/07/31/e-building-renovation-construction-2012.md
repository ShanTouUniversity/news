It is reported that, with the approval of the relevant authorities, the university's Capital Construction Office will renovate the lecture halls and seminar rooms on the second floor of Building E, with the work contracted to Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd.

The main scope of the renovation includes replacing wooden doors, removing the concrete floor bedding and fixed seating, replacing the ceiling and curtains, laying rubber flooring, applying wallpaper and tiles to walls, and installing part of the power circuit wiring.

The contract price of the project is 943,700 yuan. The renovation is scheduled to start on August 1, 2012, and to be completed by September 20. Some noise, vibration and dust are expected during the construction period.

The Facilities Management Office reminds passers-by to stay alert for safety. Without permission from the university's functional departments and the construction unit, people should not enter the construction site, and are advised to avoid lingering nearby.
