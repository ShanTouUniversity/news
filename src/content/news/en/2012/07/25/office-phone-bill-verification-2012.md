It is reported that the electronic bills for office phone charges of all units from June 1 to 30, 2012 have been delivered by China Telecom's Shantou branch to STU's telephone office, and the data has been imported into the "phone management system." According to a notice from the Resource Management Office, verifiers of each unit should log into the system to check the bills, with all details shown in the statements.

Bills without objection should be confirmed by clicking "confirm the selected bills after verification." If any bill details do not match actual phone usage, please email the designated address before July 30 so that the issues can be reported to the telecom company collectively. After the verification period ends, bills without objection or confirmation operations will be treated as confirmed by default. After verification, the Finance Office will deduct the fees by the funding numbers provided by each unit on July 31.

Units that encounter problems or have suggestions while using the phone management system may also email the designated address so that the relevant departments can further improve the system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
