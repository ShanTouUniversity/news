It is reported that, under the campus network upgrade plan, Shantou University's Network and Information Center will readjust the servers in the university's data center from 12:00 to 6:00 a.m. on July 26, 2012. During the adjustment, each server will experience one to two hours of service interruption, affecting internet access for all campus network users and external services of on-campus systems. The restoration time will not be separately announced.

The center asked users to inform each other and apologized for any inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
