It is reported that, following review by Shantou University's bidding leadership group, the university's Resource Management Office has announced the results of two tenders: the renovation of Teaching Building E was won by Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd., while Guangzhou Guangbao Construction Supervision Co., Ltd. won the contract for construction supervision of small and medium-sized infrastructure projects.

## Bid Results at a Glance

| Project | Winner | Award Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Building E renovation | Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 943,660.31 (including a provisional sum of RMB 44,936.21 controlled by the employer) |
| Construction supervision of small/medium projects | Guangzhou Guangbao Construction Supervision Co., Ltd. | 15% discount off rates under NDRC document Fa Gai Price [2007] No. 670; delay penalty of RMB 1,530 per day |

The public notice period runs from July 27 to July 30. Anyone objecting to the results may file a complaint with STU's Discipline Inspection and Audit Office at (0754) 82902346.

The tenderer, STU's Capital Construction Office, will handle subsequent matters; contact persons Mr. Chen and Mr. Lu can be reached at (0754) 82903216, fax (0754) 82902314.
