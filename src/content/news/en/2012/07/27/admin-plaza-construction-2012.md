It is reported that the uneven concrete plaza in front of the administration building of Shantou University will undergo construction on Saturday, July 28. According to the notice from the Resource Management Office and the Capital Construction Office, the main works include roadbed treatment and concrete paving of the surface, scheduled for completion by August 20.

During the construction period, pedestrians and vehicles passing through the area should take detours and pay attention to safety. The university asked faculty, staff and students for their understanding of the inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
