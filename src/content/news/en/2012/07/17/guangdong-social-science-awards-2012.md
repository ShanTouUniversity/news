It is reported that applications for Guangdong Province's 2010-2011 philosophy and social science outstanding achievement awards have opened. The awards cover 19 disciplinary groups — including Marxism and scientific socialism, Party history and Party building, philosophy, theoretical and applied economics, political science, law, sociology, ethnology and religious studies, history and archaeology and local culture studies, literature, linguistics, education and psychology and sports science, art, journalism and communication, management and emerging interdisciplinary fields, library and information science and local chronicles, Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan and international studies, and research reports. The outstanding achievement prizes are divided into three categories — works, papers and research reports — each with first, second and third prizes, with the total number of awards determined by applications.

Research produced by individuals or collectives in Guangdong Province between January 1, 2010 and December 31, 2011 — formally published philosophy and social science works, publicly released papers and research reports, and unpublished reports adopted by Party committees or governments at or above the prefecture level or provincial department-level units and large and medium-sized enterprises — may be submitted. Works must be submitted by the chief editor (or first chief editor) or first author, with each applicant limited to one entry; a series of papers on one topic may be submitted as a single paper entry; series books may not be submitted as a whole but only as individual complete works. Results already awarded at an equal or higher level, final outputs of unfinished national and provincial social science planning projects, and essay collections compiled by groups are not eligible.

Applicants should download and complete the Guangdong 2010-2011 Philosophy and Social Science Outstanding Achievement Award Application Form, submit the draft to the Research Affairs Office for review by September 7, and submit the final materials (bound according to the requirements for works, papers and research reports) to the office for unified forwarding by September 14, along with electronic copies. The review proceeds through preliminary review, secondary review, final review and approval: preliminary review in October, disciplinary secondary review and final review by the evaluation committee in November, approval and publicity in December, with the award ceremony time to be announced separately.
