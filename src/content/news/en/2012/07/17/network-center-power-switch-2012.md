It is reported that, as planned, Shantou University's Network and Information Center will carry out a power supply renovation switch at the campus network center machine room from 12:00 to 2:00 a.m. on July 19, 2012. All network and system services will be suspended during this period, affecting internet access for all campus network users and on-campus system services. The restoration time will not be separately announced.

The center asked users to inform each other and apologized for any inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
