It is reported that the Infrastructure Office of the Resource Management Office issued a notice on July 17, 2012, regarding additional costs for the renovation of the Mechanical Engineering laboratories and offices of the College of Engineering.

The project started on May 22, 2012, in the first-floor laboratories of the Science and Technology Center. After removal of partition walls, a height difference of about 12 cm appeared on the floor, and the existing power lines were old and disordered, affecting safety and overall functionality. After on-site communication with the user unit, additional work was estimated at about 6,000 yuan for civil engineering and 16,500 yuan for electrical installation, totaling about 22,500 yuan, to be covered by the user unit’s maintenance fund.

In accordance with the *Management Measures for Bidding and Tendering of Infrastructure Projects*, the additional costs are now publicized. Those with objections may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 17:30 on July 20, 2012.
