It is reported that applications have opened for the 2012 projects of Guangdong Province's philosophy and social science "12th Five-Year" plan. This year's project categories are general projects and youth projects: about 100 general projects and 40 youth projects, with funding levels of 30,000, 40,000 and 50,000 yuan.

Eligibility: applicants should hold a senior professional title (associate professor or above) or be recommended by two peer experts with full senior professional titles; for youth projects, applicants and team members must be 39 years old or younger (as of September 30, 2012); general and youth projects are encouraged to produce series of papers or research reports as final outcomes, generally completed within 2 years, while monographs generally require completion within 3 years; a project leader may apply for only one project and may not participate as a team member in others; leaders of ongoing national social science fund projects, NSFC management science projects, national science and technology soft science projects, national education science planning projects, ministry-level humanities and social science projects, provincial social science planning projects and provincial natural science fund projects may not apply as leaders, but may join as members, with each member allowed to participate in at most two applications.

On topics: no topic guide is set for 2012; applicants may freely choose topics based on their research strengths and academic accumulation. Topics should serve the province's central work through applied and countermeasure research, value basic research and theoretical innovation, and support research on Guangdong's local characteristics and advantageous disciplines such as Cantonese, Chaoshan and Hakka culture.

Applications are submitted online (July 20 to August 24 at the designated website). After approval, applicants must print six copies of the application form and submit them to the Research Affairs Office by September 4. The system requires real-name account registration that cannot be modified afterward; the school code 10560 must be entered from the first column with no extra characters or spaces; signatures of the project leader and recommenders must be handwritten.
