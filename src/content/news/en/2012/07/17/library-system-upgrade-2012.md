It is reported that, due to a business system upgrade at the library, Shantou University's library will stop lending and return services on Thursday, July 18, and online catalog and borrowing inquiry services will be suspended from July 17 to 20. The library apologized for any inconvenience and asked patrons to plan accordingly.
