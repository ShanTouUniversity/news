It is reported that, with the 2012 Shantou senior high school entrance examination (zhongkao) results released on July 4, the middle school affiliated with Shantou University achieved excellent outcomes. Among the 35 candidates this year, 2 scored above 800 points (out of a full mark of 870), with the highest at 812; 17 candidates (48.6%) scored above 760; and 21 candidates (60%) scored above 700. The affiliated middle school announced the results on July 5.
