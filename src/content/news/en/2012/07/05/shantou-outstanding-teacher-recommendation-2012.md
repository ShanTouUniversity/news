It is reported that, in line with the Shantou Education Bureau's notice on the 2012 preliminary selection of outstanding principals, teachers and educators (Document Shan Shi Jiao [2012] No. 55), Shantou University (including its Medical College) may nominate 3 Shantou Outstanding Teachers and 1 Shantou Outstanding Educator this year.

Under the nomination procedure, each unit is asked to publicize the selection criteria and relevant rules among faculty and educators, encourage nominations and applications, and determine its own nominees after careful review. Each unit may nominate no more than 2 outstanding teachers and 1 outstanding educator. The university will form a review panel with representatives from teaching units and relevant functional departments to select the university's final nominees.

Nomination materials must be submitted to the Human Resources Office (Administration Building East 416) before July 11, including the Shantou Outstanding Teacher/Educator Report Form in 12 copies, a story of exemplary deeds of about 2,000 characters written in the third person in 3 original copies, one copy each of the nominee's diploma and professional title, teacher qualification and honor certificates, one copy of representative works on teaching quality since 2008, and one copy of the Nominee Profile Form. Electronic versions of the report form, story and profile form must also be emailed to the designated address.
