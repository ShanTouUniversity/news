It is reported that Shantou University will hold the 2012 student military training parade and summary ceremony at 4:30 p.m. on July 15 on the track and field ground. All participating trainees and college counselors are asked to assemble on the field by 4:00 p.m. Party branch secretaries (or deputy secretaries) of each college (residential college) are to take seats on the rostrum.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, People's Armed Forces Department)
