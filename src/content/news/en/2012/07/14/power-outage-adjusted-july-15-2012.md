It is reported that, to support the university's admissions work and ensure the normal operation of the power supply for the administration building and the network center, Shantou University has adjusted the high-voltage equipment testing and power outage arrangements originally scheduled for Sunday, July 15.

Under the adjusted plan: from 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., power will be cut in the gymnasium, the medical teaching building, the CDG teaching blocks and the wind tunnel structural laboratory; from 1:30 to 6:30 p.m., power will be cut in the continuing education college and Longquan Garden. Other areas — including the administration building and the network center — will not experience outages this time; the high-voltage testing and outage arrangements for those areas will be announced separately later.
