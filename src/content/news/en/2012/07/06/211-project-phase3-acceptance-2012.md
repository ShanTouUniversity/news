It is reported that the Notice on Acceptance of the Phase III Construction of Guangdong Province's "211 Project" has been formally issued by the office of the provincial 211 Project coordination group. Compared with the draft implementation plan for the acceptance of Guangdong's 211 Project Phase III, the formal notice contains changes in some content.

Shantou University's 211 Project office has forwarded the notice to all Phase III construction projects and relevant units, asking the personnel of related projects and units to carefully study the notice and carry out all work in accordance with its requirements and the university's overall arrangement for campus acceptance of key discipline construction projects under the Phase III program, so as to achieve good results in the acceptance review.

> ⚠️ The full provincial acceptance notice is available in the attachment on the OA system.
