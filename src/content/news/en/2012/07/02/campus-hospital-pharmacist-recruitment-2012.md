It is reported that, due to operational needs, Shantou University is publicly recruiting one pharmacy staff member for the campus hospital under its Resource Management Office.

Applicants should meet the following basic requirements: sound political and ideological character, strong sense of responsibility, integrity and a rigorous, dedicated work attitude; age 40 or younger and in good health; a pharmacy diploma from a secondary vocational school or above; and the ability to adapt to a 24-hour rotating shift schedule while complying with family planning policies.

Job responsibilities include: strictly following pharmacy work rules, handling drug storage and statistics, and recording daily dispensing volumes, settlement and reconciliation with the cashier; registering drug stock-in and stock-out, conducting periodic inventories, and promptly disposing of expired or deteriorated drugs; implementing all rules and technical operating standards to ensure procurement quality and accurate dispensing while preventing errors; turning over all drug revenue to the university finance office; and accepting post reassignments and completing urgent tasks assigned by the hospital.

The position is offered on an auxiliary contract basis; personnel files will not be transferred to STU, and compensation follows relevant university standards. Applicants should send their resume, and copies of their diploma, practice license, family planning certificate, ID card and other relevant certificates by email or mail to the campus hospital of the Resource Management Office before 5:00 p.m. on July 9 (address: STU Campus Hospital, Resource Management Office, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong; postal code 515063). Those passing the initial screening will be notified for an interview and must present their ID card and original certificates at that time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office, Human Resources Office)
