It is reported that, following research at the 8th university leadership meeting on June 20, 2012, Shantou University decided to appoint Huai Xiaofeng as director of the Social Science Teaching and Research Department, on a six-month probationary basis. Cheng Jiaming no longer serves as director of the department. The decision was published in STU Document [2012] No. 89 and took effect on July 1.
