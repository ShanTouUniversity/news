It is reported that the Office of the Shantou Social Insurance Fund Administration Bureau recently issued a notice to all designated hospitals and insured units, implementing pooled management of outpatient medical expenses under the urban workers' basic medical insurance, in line with the Opinions on Implementing Pooling of Outpatient Medical Expenses for Urban Workers' Basic Medical Insurance (Shan Ren She Fa [2012] No. 2) and the Notice on Including General Consultation Fees in the Scope of Basic Medical Insurance Payment (Shan Ren She [2011] No. 263), issued jointly by the municipal human resources and social security, finance, health and price authorities.

The notice covers the scope of insured persons, outpatient medical registration, outpatient pooling fund payment, and settlement of pooled outpatient medical expenses. STU's Resource Management Office has forwarded the notice to all units on campus.

> ⚠️ The full notice and attachments are available on the OA system.
