It is reported that, to support the development of public welfare in Guangdong Province, explore a platform for incubating university student public welfare groups, and broaden students' paths into public welfare entrepreneurship, the provincial Youth League Committee, the provincial welfare lottery center and the provincial student federation have launched the 2012 Guangdong University Students' "Fucai Public Welfare Award" program.

STU's Youth League Committee has forwarded the notice to the whole university. Teams and individuals that have already carried out public welfare activities, or will start public welfare activities from July 2012, may register for this year's Fucai Public Welfare Award. Eligible groups and individuals should actively apply as required by the notice, complete their online applications by July 5, and send the application materials to the university Youth League Committee's email address.

## 2012 Results

The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the 2012 results on **June 3, 2013**. The STU Youth Volunteers Association's Firefly oral-history project with people recovering from leprosy, the Dawn Study Support Project, the SIFE team, the SIFE Leiling Lychee Development and Promotion Project Group, and the "Aijia" team, making **five teams** in total, received the Guangdong University Students Fucai Public Welfare Outstanding Team Award. **Two students** also received the Guangdong University Students Fucai Public Welfare Service Star Award. The student recipients' names, cohort years and majors are not reproduced here.
