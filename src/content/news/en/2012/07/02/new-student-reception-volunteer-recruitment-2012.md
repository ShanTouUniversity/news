It is reported that, to support the 2012 freshman reception and help new students settle into STU more smoothly, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University is recruiting 180 volunteers from across the university.

Applicants should have strong communication skills and be able to handle reception work warmly, thoroughly and efficiently; a responsible, hardworking attitude with good teamwork; a commitment to follow reasonable arrangements and not leave their posts without authorization; no major timetable or activity conflicts during the reception period; and the availability to return to campus before August 26 for work arrangements and training.

The reception work starts from September 2, and volunteers will assist the university with unified freshman reception, related logistics services and registration of various procedures. To apply, students should send the application form to the designated email address and fill in their personal strengths in the "personal profile" section, attaching their class schedule if they have classes during the reception period. The deadline is 24:00 on July 6. Admission is based on applications on a merit basis, with earlier applicants given priority when qualifications are equal; the final list will be published on the OA system and the volunteer section of the BBS.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
