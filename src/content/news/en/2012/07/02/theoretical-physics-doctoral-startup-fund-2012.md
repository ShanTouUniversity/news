It is reported that the Doctoral Researcher Start-Up Project, established in 2003 under the theoretical physics special fund of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC), continues to fund researchers who obtained their PhD within the past three years and are engaged in theoretical physics research without dedicated research funding. All else being equal, priority is given to researchers working in regions with relatively difficult research conditions and limited funding but with solid research ability. The project is managed under the NSFC general program rules.

Applicants must meet all of the following conditions: hold a PhD earned within the past three years (degree certificate dated between January 1, 2009 and December 31, 2011) and be engaged in theoretical physics research; be under 35 years old (born after January 1, 1977); not currently serve as principal investigator of an active NSFC project; and not be a postdoctoral researcher on station.

Applications are prepared online. Interested faculty may obtain a username and password from the Research Affairs Office and log into the ISIS system to complete the application. The "funding category" should be "special fund project," the "subcategory" should be "department director fund," the "annotation" should be "theoretical physics special fund," and "application code 1" must be A05. Projects run for one year (January to December 2013) with funding of about 50,000 yuan each. Applicants must choose between the Doctoral Researcher Start-Up Project and the Cooperative Research Project — two applications cannot be submitted in the same year.

Applicants must complete and submit their applications online by August 27 and notify the Research Affairs Office for review; approved projects must submit two paper copies to the office by August 31. Inquiries may be directed to the Research Affairs Office.
