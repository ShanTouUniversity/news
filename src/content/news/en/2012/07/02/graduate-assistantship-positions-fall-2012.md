It is reported that Shantou University's Graduate School has published the assistantship positions (research, teaching and administrative) for the fall 2012 semester. Students interested in applying should submit their application forms to the academic staff or persons in charge of the relevant departments between September 3 and 5, and each department should compile and forward the applications to the Graduate School before 5:00 p.m. on September 7.

The Graduate School reminds applicants that they may contact the relevant departments directly, and any questions can be directed to the Graduate School (contact: Ms. Lin).
