It is reported that, since 2009, the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) has operated a "cooperative research project" under its theoretical physics special fund, supporting scholars or research groups with relatively weak theoretical physics conditions nationwide. Participants complete research tasks and improve their capacity by cooperating with domestic scholars with stronger theoretical physics research strength. The project is managed under NSFC general program rules.

Applicants must be under 45 years old (born after January 1, 1967), must not have received other foundation funding (except theoretical physics special fund projects), and may have served as principal investigator of this project type at most twice (including the former "east-west cooperation projects"). Applications should form a research group of two to three staff members from the applicant's own unit (including the principal investigator), draw up a plan for cooperative research and study, and engage a scholar with strong theoretical physics research strength as a "mentor scholar"; applicants may not choose their own graduate supervisor. The research content must fall within theoretical physics, and the mentor scholar should hold a senior professional title, lead an ongoing national-level research project, and work in a direction consistent with the application. Mentor scholars do not occupy project quotas and should not be listed in the main member table; each mentor scholar may participate in at most two cooperative research projects. During the project period, group members must spend at least one person-month per year studying at the mentor scholar's institution.

Applications are prepared online. Interested faculty may obtain a username and password from the Research Affairs Office and log into the ISIS system, selecting "special fund project" as the funding category, "department director fund" as the subcategory, "theoretical physics special fund" as the annotation, and A05 as application code 1. Projects run for three years (January 2013 to December 2015) with average funding of about 200,000 yuan each. As with the Doctoral Researcher Start-Up Project, the cooperative research project cannot be applied for in the same year as the other. Applicants must complete and submit online by August 27; approved projects must submit two paper copies to the Research Affairs Office by August 31.
