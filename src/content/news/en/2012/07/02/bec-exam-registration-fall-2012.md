It is reported that, following the arrangement of the Ministry of Education's Overseas Examination Center, registration for the second half of 2012 Business English Certificate (BEC) exams at the Shantou University overseas examination center begins on September 3 and runs through September 15.

Registration is open to everyone regardless of age, gender, occupation, region or educational background. The center is qualified to administer all BEC levels: Preliminary, Vantage and Higher. Applicants must bring their ID card, one recent one-inch photo and a bank cash deposit slip. Admission tickets are issued within two weeks before the exam, and candidates should collect them with their ID card.

Registration hours are 8:30 to 11:30 a.m. and 2:30 to 5:00 p.m. on weekdays from September 3 to 15, with a weekend session on September 15 only from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Registration takes place at the office of the STU overseas examination center (Building E, 5th floor, Room 503). Fees are 374 yuan for BEC Preliminary, 475 yuan for BEC Vantage, and 598 yuan for BEC Higher. Candidates must first pay the fee at Bank of China (account name: Shantou University; account number: 628857760147; bank: BOC Shantou University sub-branch; purpose: BEC exam fee) and then complete registration at the center with the bank-issued cash deposit slip.

Exam schedule: November 24, BEC Preliminary written test (morning) and speaking test (afternoon); December 1, BEC Vantage written (morning) and speaking (afternoon and the following day); November 17, BEC Higher written (morning) and speaking (afternoon and the following day). Exams are held on the fifth floor of Teaching Building E. To reduce the burden on candidates, the center suggests students organize themselves by school, collect each candidate's ID copy, original bank deposit slip and a photo, and send a representative to register on their behalf.
