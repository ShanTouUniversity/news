It is reported that the Student Affairs Office forwarded a notice on July 7, 2012, from the Guangdong Department of Education to hold the “National Grant Helps Me Grow” essay contest.

Organized by the provincial department, the contest aims to publicize national and provincial student aid policies and showcase achievements in supporting students from financially disadvantaged families, while strengthening inspiration and gratitude education.

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | National Grant Helps Me Grow |
| Participants | Students and recent graduates who have received national grants from regular universities, vocational schools and senior high schools |
| Length | No more than 4,000 words, any title or genre |
| Content | Reflect the spirit of overcoming difficulties, diligence, self-reliance, integrity and gratitude among aided students |
| Rules | No plagiarism, not previously published, organizers retain usage rights of winning works |
| Format | A4, 2 cm margins, 1.25 line spacing, title formatting as specified in the attachment |

## Awards and Submission

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Awards | Outstanding Essay Awards: 10 first, 20 second and 30 third prizes per category (four categories); Outstanding Organization Awards |
| Review | Tiered submission and review, final evaluation by provincial experts, with selected works recommended nationally |
| How to submit | Complete the recommendation form and send the Word manuscript to the designated email with subject “助我成才征文” + Name + College |
| Deadline | August 26, 2012 |
| Organizers | Provincial Student Aid Center and Guangdong Education Magazine |

The recommendation form and submission email are available in the OA attachment and withheld here for privacy.
