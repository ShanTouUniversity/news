It is reported that Shantou University's Network and Information Center has announced an upgrade and maintenance of the E-Learning system from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on July 6, 2012. The upgrade will move the built-in Moodle platform from version 2.1 to version 2.3.

During the upgrade, related URLs including my.stu.edu.cn, digiu.com.cn and digiu.cn will be inaccessible, and all E-Learning functions will be suspended for all users across the university. No separate notice will be issued when the service is restored. The center asked users to inform each other and apologized for any inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
