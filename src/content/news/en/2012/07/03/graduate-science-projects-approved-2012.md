It is reported that, following review and recommendation by each college, 34 proposed projects — including one on the cultural character of traditional Chinese colored glaze and its modern commercial promotion — have been approved by the Graduate School of Shantou University. The list of approved projects is provided in the attachment.

The Graduate School reminds project leaders that a midterm review will be conducted in early October this year, and whether additional funding is granted (and its amount) will depend on project progress. Project leaders are asked to organize their team members to begin the relevant research work promptly.

> ⚠️ The list of approved projects is available in the attachment on the OA system.
