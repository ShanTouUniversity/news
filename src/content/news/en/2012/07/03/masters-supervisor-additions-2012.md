It is reported that the first meeting of the 8th session of the Shantou University Degree Appraisal Committee was held on June 15, where teachers applying for master's supervisor qualifications were reviewed and put to a vote. According to the document published on July 3 (STU Degree Document [2012] No. 06), the committee agreed to add 40 teachers as master's supervisors and confirm 6 teachers as master's supervisors.

The additions span disciplines including literature, art, management, economics, law, education, science, engineering and medicine, covering majors such as English language and literature, journalism and communication, design art, business management, accounting, public administration, industrial economics, fundamental mathematics, optical engineering, marine biology, mechatronic engineering, structural engineering, computer application technology, communication and information systems, pathology and pathophysiology, forensic medicine, neurobiology, internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, ophthalmology, oncology and medical imaging and nuclear medicine (one teacher has a name partly garbled in the original notice and is not fully reproduced). The confirmed supervisors cover majors including business management, public administration, fundamental mathematics, internal medicine and anesthesiology.
