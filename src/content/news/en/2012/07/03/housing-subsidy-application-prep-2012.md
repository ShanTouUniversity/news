It is reported that applications by staff on STU's official establishment for the 2012 housing monetary subsidy are planned to be formally processed in September. To speed up the application process and pass the review of provincial and municipal authorities, the Resource Management Office (Campus Property Office) issued a notice reminding eligible staff to prepare in advance.

The subsidy applies to staff holding formal posts in units directly under Guangdong Province whose personnel records are at Shantou University and who meet the following conditions: neither the applicant nor their spouse has purchased reform housing, hardship-relief housing or joined in-unit collective housing construction; neither has received a government-recognized housing subsidy; and both have stopped enjoying current housing reform or low rents and pay rent at cost or commercial rates.

Eligible applicants should prepare the required documents, including the STU Staff Housing Monetary Subsidy Application Review Form (available for download in September), certificates of assignment or transfer, ID cards of both spouses, the divorce agreement where applicable, and the original employment contract signed with Shantou University. Originals are returned to applicants after verification and copying by the Campus Property Office.

The notice specifically reminds applicants who need the certificate that they did not purchase reform, hardship-relief or collective housing to obtain the certificate from their former unit and its local housing reform office during the summer break, or first have the Campus Property Office issue an inquiry letter; details can be arranged with the office.
