It is reported that, to cultivate and enhance students' leadership skills, Shantou University is running a leadership exchange program in cooperation with the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. Following the selection notice issued on June 19, 26 students nominated by various units were shortlisted for interviews, and 10 students were ultimately selected for the program.

The selected students come from the university student union, the university youth volunteer association, the student associations federation, Zhicheng College, and the colleges of science, business and engineering. They are asked to make relevant preparations and attend a meeting at 3:00 p.m. on August 19 in the multimedia classroom of Building 789, Room 726.

> Note: The 10 students span majors including English, Chinese language and literature, computer science, law, international economics and trade, mathematics, business administration and civil engineering. Full names are withheld in line with privacy rules; the original notice displays one garbled character in one student's name.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU, Student Affairs Office)
