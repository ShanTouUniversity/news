It is reported that Shantou University's Party and Administration Office has published the summer duty schedule for administrative, auxiliary and logistics staff of relevant units. The notice requires those units to strictly implement the relevant provisions of STU Party-Admin Office Document [2012] No. 4 to ensure timely and effective services for faculty, staff and students during the vacation.
