It is reported that Shantou University's Youth League Committee has published the specific class times and venues for the 2012 summer public-welfare courses, covering four courses: the "Three Trips to the Countryside" Service-Learning Course, Dawn Aid Service, the "Passing on the Torch" Charity Action, and the "Gathering in the Recovery Village" Charity Action. Detailed arrangements are provided in the attachments.

Students who have enrolled in the courses are reminded to make proper preparations and attend on time.

> ⚠️ Class times and venues for each course are available in the attachments on the OA system.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
