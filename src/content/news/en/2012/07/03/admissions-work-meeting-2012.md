It is reported that, to prepare for the university's 2012 admissions and enrollment work, Shantou University will hold its 2012 admissions work meeting at 10:00 a.m. on Thursday, July 5, in the meeting room at Administration Building East 311. All members of the university admissions leadership group and staff nominated by units to take part in this year's admissions work are asked to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
