It is reported that the first meeting of the 8th session of the Shantou University Degree Appraisal Committee was held on June 15, where professors applying for doctoral supervisor qualifications were reviewed and put to a vote. According to the document published on July 3 (STU Degree Document [2012] No. 05), the committee agreed to add 9 professors as doctoral supervisors and confirmed Professor Martin M. Matzuk as a doctoral supervisor.

The additions span three disciplinary fields: two professors were added in structural engineering under engineering (one of whom has a name partly garbled in the original notice and is not fully reproduced); four in biochemistry and molecular biology and one in marine biology under science; and one each in medical imaging and nuclear medicine and in surgery under medicine. The confirmed supervisor is Professor Martin M. Matzuk in pathology and pathophysiology under medicine.
