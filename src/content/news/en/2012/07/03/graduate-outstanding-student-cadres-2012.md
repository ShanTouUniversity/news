It is reported that, with the support of university leaders and relevant departments, graduate student affairs at Shantou University have progressed smoothly and achieved encouraging results over the past year. To commend exemplary students and motivate student cadres, the Graduate School decided, following applications from students, recommendations by supervising units and review by the Graduate School, to confer the title of "Outstanding Student Cadre of the Graduate School" on 23 students.

The honorees are organized into one first-prize, four second-prize and eighteen third-prize recipients. The Graduate School hopes that the honored students and all graduate students will remain proactive and diligent in contributing to the university's development. The full list of names is not reproduced here in line with privacy rules.
