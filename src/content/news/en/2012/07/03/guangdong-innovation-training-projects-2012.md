It is reported that, in line with the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education's notice on 2012 applications for quality teaching and teaching reform projects at provincial higher education institutions (Document Yue Jiao Gao Han 2012-80), STU's main campus carried out application and approval work for the 2012 Guangdong University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program in June.

Following applications from project teams, expert review and recommendation organized by each unit, and university approval, 72 projects are proposed for the program, with the project list provided in the attachment. The public comment period runs from July 3 to 8; objections to any approved project may be submitted in writing to the Academic Affairs Office during this period.

> ⚠️ The project list attachment is available on the OA system.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
