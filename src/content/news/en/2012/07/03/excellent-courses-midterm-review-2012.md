It is reported that, following the construction schedule of the 2011 university-level excellent course program, STU's Academic Affairs Office conducted a midterm review in June of the 4 university-level excellent courses approved in 2011.

After reviewing the midterm reports and supporting materials of each course, the 4 courses were found to be progressing well: each had been developed in line with its application plan across teaching content, teaching methods and tools, course features, organization of course materials and website development, meeting the expected construction goals. All 4 courses passed the midterm review.

The university will allocate the second installment of construction funding to the courses and asks each to complete the next phase of construction strictly according to plan, especially key tasks such as excellent-course websites and classroom teaching videos, so as to achieve the program's goals.
