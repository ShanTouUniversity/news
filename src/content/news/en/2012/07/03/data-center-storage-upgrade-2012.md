It is reported that Shantou University's Network and Information Center has announced that it will upgrade and maintain the data center storage system equipment from 12:30 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. on July 7 and July 8, 2012. During this period, all applications using the storage system will be interrupted.

The affected systems include the email system, the English version of the university homepage, college homepages, the office automation system, the E-Learning system, virtual classrooms, Superstar e-books, the online lecture hall and Superstar academic videos, affecting all users across the university. The center asks users to arrange their work in advance.
