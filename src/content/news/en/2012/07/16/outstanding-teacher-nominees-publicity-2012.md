It is reported that, following the Guangdong Department of Human Resources and Social Security's notice on the 2012 selection and commendation of Nanyue Outstanding Teachers and Educators (Document Yue Ren She Fa [2012] No. 135) and the Shantou Education Bureau's notice on the 2012 preliminary selection of outstanding principals, teachers and educators (Document Shan Shi Jiao [2012] No. 55), Shantou University determined its 2012 nominees for outstanding teacher awards at all levels after unit nominations, review by functional departments and evaluation by the university panel. The nominees are now publicized.

The proposed nominees are: Bao Nengsheng, Chen Jian, Han Ran and Huang Xiaochun for Nanyue Outstanding Teacher; Xiong Guangjing as the candidate for Nanyue Outstanding Educator; Du Hong and Lin Peng for Shantou Outstanding Teacher; and Li Xiangxin as the candidate for Shantou Outstanding Educator.

Units and individuals with objections may submit written feedback to the university Human Resources Office (Administration Building East 406) before July 22; materials from units must bear official seals, and personal feedback must be signed with real names.
