It is reported that, to implement the Opinions of the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Finance on Implementing the Plan for Improving Innovation Capacity in Higher Education Institutions (the "2011 Plan"), and in line with the notices of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and the Ministry of Education's Department of Science and Technology and the Ministry of Finance's Department of Science, Education and Culture on organizing the 2012 review and accreditation of "2011 Collaborative Innovation Centers," Shantou University has decided to launch applications for a Collaborative Innovation Center.

This round of applications is quota-limited, with the university allowed to apply for one center. The proposed center must meet all the basic conditions and requirements set out in the Ministry of Education and Ministry of Finance implementation plan for improving innovation capacity in higher education institutions. Colleges interested in applying should send the electronic application materials to the Research Affairs Office email by August 10; the university will organize expert review and selection before reporting the best candidate upward.
