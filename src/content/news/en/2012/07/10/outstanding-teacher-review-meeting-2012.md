It is reported that, to advance the nomination of 2012 Nanyue Outstanding Teachers (including educators) and Shantou Outstanding Teachers (including educators), Shantou University will hold a review meeting at 9:30 a.m. on Friday, July 13, in the meeting room at Administration Building East 311. According to the notice from the Human Resources Office, each college and each of the Academic Affairs Office, Research Affairs Office, Graduate School, Supervision and Audit Office, Staff Union and Human Resources Office should send one representative to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
