It is reported that recruitment of volunteers for the 2012 Western Plan and Mountainous Area Plan has been completed. In line with the notice on the 2012 recruitment of volunteers for Guangdong's Western Plan and Mountainous Area Plan programs, Shantou University proposes to send one 2008-cohort student majoring in Chinese language and literature from the College of Liberal Arts to serve in a poverty-stricken area in Tibet under the Western Plan, and one 2008-cohort student majoring in applied physics from the College of Science to serve in Haifeng County, Shanwei City under the Mountainous Area Plan.

The proposed volunteers' materials are now publicized by the university Youth League Committee; units or individuals with objections may contact the committee in writing or in person before July 12.

> ⚠️ The volunteers' application materials are available in the attachment on the OA system.
> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of STU)
