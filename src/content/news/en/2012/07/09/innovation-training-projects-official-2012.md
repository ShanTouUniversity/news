It is reported that, in line with the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education's notice on 2012 applications for quality teaching and teaching reform projects at provincial higher education institutions (Document Yue Jiao Gao Han 2012-80), STU's main campus carried out application and approval work for the 2012 Guangdong University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program in June.

Following applications from project teams, expert review and recommendation organized by each unit, and online publicity, the university has approved 72 projects — including "The Operating Status and Development Prospects of Family-Style Chaoshan Merchant Firms: A Survey Study Based on Central Guangdong" — as 2012 provincial training program projects, published in STU Academic Document [2012] No. 5.

The Academic Affairs Office requires all approved projects to carry out innovation and entrepreneurship training strictly according to their application plans, strengthen project management and ensure smooth implementation. The university will track project progress and organize midterm reviews and final acceptance.
