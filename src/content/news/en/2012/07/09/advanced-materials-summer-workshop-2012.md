It is reported that, to implement the goal of cultivating innovative talent under the key discipline construction projects of Guangdong Province's Phase III "211 Project" and to promote the training of innovative graduate students in materials-related disciplines, STU's College of Science and the Graduate School will jointly hold a graduate summer workshop on "advanced materials" from July 10 to 13 at Shantou University and Dongshan Island in Fujian Province.

The workshop features renowned experts in advanced materials from home and abroad, including academicians of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Changjiang Scholars and recipients of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, as well as professors from the university's chemistry department and the Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials. Lecture topics cover frontier research areas in materials science related to physics, chemistry and biology, including porous materials, molecular-based materials, polymer materials, biomaterials, luminescent materials and nanomaterials.

During its sessions at Shantou University, the workshop is open to all faculty and students: lectures will be held in Meeting Room 3 of the STU Academic Exchange Center from 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. and 2:30 to 6:00 p.m. on July 10, and from 8:30 to 11:30 a.m. on July 11.
