It is reported that, following recommendation by Shantou University and preliminary review and expert evaluation organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, the university plans to establish a Guangdong higher-education Pearl River Scholar position in the discipline of marine biology and appoint Ding Lanping as a Pearl River Scholar distinguished professor.

According to the implementation measures for Guangdong's higher-education Pearl River Scholar position plan, the proposal is now publicized from July 17 to 30, 2012. During the publicity period, objections may be raised by phone or in writing to the teacher management division of the provincial education department or the university's Human Resources Office. Feedback must state or sign real names and provide specific facts; anonymous feedback without specific facts will not be accepted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
