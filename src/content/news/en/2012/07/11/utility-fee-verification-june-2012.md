It is reported that Shantou University has published the June water and electricity fees for faculty and staff (meter readings taken on July 1 and 2, versus June 1 for the previous period). According to the Resource Management Office notice, staff may check the fees by logging into the STU water and electricity fee management system with their campus network accounts.

Those with questions or objections may verify the amounts at the utilities center from July 11 to 13. After that period, fees without objection will be deducted by the Finance Office from each person's July 2012 salary account; those without salary accounts should pay directly at the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
