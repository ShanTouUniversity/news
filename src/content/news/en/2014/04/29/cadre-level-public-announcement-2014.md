Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department published a pre-appointment notice on April 29. Following consideration at the fourth 2014 meeting of the university Communist Party Committee Standing Committee, the university proposed administrative rank assignments for 14 faculty and staff members.

Wen Ruifeng was proposed for a senior division-level rank; Fan Jibin and Huang Yixiong for deputy division-level ranks; and Wang Fengqi, Xu Xiaojing, Li Chang, Li Peijie, Wang Xiaojuan, Chen Li, Chen Ying, Lin Yu, Hong Mengdian, Yao Shaoyang and Huang Xianglong for deputy section-level ranks. The notice was issued under the university’s interim rules for pre-appointment announcements, with biographical information provided in the OA attachment.

Comments may be submitted in writing, in person or by telephone to the university Organization and United Front Work Department by May 6. The department can be reached at 86502345 or o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.
