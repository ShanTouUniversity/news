The Guangdong committee for student scholarships and financial aid has opened its call for papers for the 2014 annual conference. The theme is “Supporting Students and Fostering Growth,” and the call is aimed at student-aid workers, counselors and education administrators in university schools and residential colleges. Submissions are due by May 30.

Papers may address topics including university financial-aid systems, scholarship and grant management, integrity education for loan recipients, work-study models, links between aid and social practice, and performance evaluation of aid programs. Authors may also choose their own topics. Papers should be scientific, practical and innovative, approximately 5,000 Chinese characters, and include a title, author and institution information, abstract, keywords, main text, references and an author profile.

Submissions must be original and unpublished, with authors responsible for their content. Papers should be sent as Word documents to xyli@stu.edu.cn.
