The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a commendation decision on April 29 recognizing groups and individuals who made outstanding contributions to Youth League work in 2013.

The commendations cover 28 outstanding Youth League branches or general branches, 47 outstanding Youth League cadres and 261 outstanding Youth League members. The committee said the recognized groups and individuals had contributed in areas including ideological education, organization building, social practice, technological innovation, entrepreneurship and campus culture.

The committee called on Youth League organizations and members across the university to learn from the recognized examples and continue contributing to the development of campus conduct, academic atmosphere and medical ethics. The detailed list is provided in the OA attachment.
