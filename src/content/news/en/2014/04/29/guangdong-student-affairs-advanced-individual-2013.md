It is reported that the Student Affairs Office forwarded a notice on April 29, 2014, announcing the results of the 2013 Outstanding Student Affairs Workers selection among Guangdong universities.

Organized by the Guangdong University Student Affairs Professional Committee to strengthen the workforce and commend dedicated contributors, the selection recognized Su Bingdian of Shantou University as a 2013 Outstanding Individual. As a faculty member, the name is disclosed in accordance with regulations.

The university congratulated the awardee and called on student affairs staff to learn from the example and advance student work with innovation.
