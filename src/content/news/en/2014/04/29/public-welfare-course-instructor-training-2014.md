Shantou University’s fifth public service course instructor training session will begin on April 30. The program consists of four sessions across two afternoons and two evenings, totaling 14 class hours.

Attendance will be recorded. Trainees unable to attend because of unavoidable conflicts must request leave from the Academic Affairs Office in advance; missing more than one session will result in failure to complete the training. Completing the training is necessary to obtain a qualification certificate for teaching public service courses. Trainees must also undertake one public service project and pass its evaluation before receiving the qualification.

The training schedule and trainee list are provided in the OA attachments. Questions may be directed to Liu Xiangling at the Academic Affairs Office at 86502416 or lxl0726@stu.edu.cn.
