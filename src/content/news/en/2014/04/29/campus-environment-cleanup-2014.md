The Shantou University Property Management Office announced on April 29 that it was carrying out an environmental cleanup covering public areas, faculty housing and surrounding spaces.

Faculty and students were asked to remove unauthorized vegetables and plants around dormitories, items stored in stairways, rooftops and other public areas, and anything blocking fire exits by May 3. Items left after the deadline would be cleared by the university.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Property Management Office)
