It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on April 29, 2014, to hold the 2013 May Fourth Awards Ceremony and the symposium commemorating the 95th anniversary of the May Fourth Movement.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00, May 4, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 30A, Library |
| Theme | 2013 May Fourth Awards and 95th Anniversary of the May Fourth Movement |
| Requirement | College Youth League Committees (general branches) are asked to ensure attendance |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee |
