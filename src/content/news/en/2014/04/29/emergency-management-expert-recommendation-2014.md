Shantou University’s Human Resources Office forwarded on April 29 a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on recommending candidates for the second cohort of emergency management experts. The recommendation process was opened to all university units.

Units were required to complete the expert recommendation summary form and registration form and submit the paper materials and electronic files to the Human Resources Office, Room 315 of the Administrative Center, by May 14. The contact person was Yu Peisong, reachable at 86503390 or o_rsc@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
>
> ⚠️ The expert recommendation forms and other attachments are available on the OA system.
