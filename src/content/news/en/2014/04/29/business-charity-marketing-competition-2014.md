Shantou University’s Business School will hold the “Business for Good” charity marketing competition at the Golden Triangle and the pedestrian street on May 11. Under the theme “Promoting Good through Business,” students may form teams and sell products at booths during the event.

Each team will donate 50 percent of its sales proceeds as charitable funds, with the option of donating more. The funds will support students in need at Wuqiao Primary School in Shantou. A donation box will also be available. Awards will be based on total scores: the top three will receive the titles “Charity Champion,” “Charity Leader” and “Charity Master,” while places four to 10 will receive “Charity Knight” and places 11 to 25 “Charity Rising Star.”

Registration closes at midnight on May 6. Students may download and submit the form through the campus information network or their own email accounts, or register in person at the Golden Triangle on May 5. The competition is organized by the Business School Youth League Committee and co-hosted by the Business School Student Union, Business Practice Center, Youth Volunteer Association and Qipan magazine.
