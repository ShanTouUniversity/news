The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education held the Third Guangdong University Counselor Professional Skills Competition from March 19 to 23, 2014. After experts evaluated contestants in the competition events, Zhang Yan of Shantou University won third prize.

The Student Affairs Office forwarded the result on April 29 and congratulated the awardee, calling on student affairs staff to continue improving their professional skills and services.
