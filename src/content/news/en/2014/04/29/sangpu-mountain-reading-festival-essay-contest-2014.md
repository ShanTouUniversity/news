The 11th Shantou University Sangpu Mountain Reading Festival essay contest opened on April 29 for all students, including graduate students. Original works are invited in seven categories: fiction, drama, prose, modern poetry, classical Chinese poetry and prose, commentary, and English writing. Submissions close at 11 p.m. on May 3.

Works must not have been published in newspapers or periodicals, and each participant may submit no more than one work in each category. Fiction and drama should generally be no longer than 8,000 Chinese characters, while prose and commentary should not exceed 5,000. The category must be indicated next to each title. Judges will select one first prize, two second prizes and three third prizes in each category, with results expected in mid-May.

Participants should submit electronic files as required by the organizers. The event is organized by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Student Organizations Federation.
