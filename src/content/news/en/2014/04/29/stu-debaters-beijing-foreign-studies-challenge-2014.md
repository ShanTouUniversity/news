The second Beijing Foreign Studies University English Debate Challenge was held in Beijing from April 25 to 27, 2014, drawing more than 80 debaters from universities in China and abroad. The three-day competition comprised six preliminary rounds and three knockout rounds.

A public team pairing one Shantou University student with one Sun Yat-sen University student ranked fourth after the preliminary rounds and advanced to the knockout stage, ultimately reaching the semifinal top eight. The STU student also ranked third individually and received the Best Speaker award. The students are not named here to protect their privacy.
