Shantou University's Infrastructure Office planned to begin repairs and refurbishment of the swimming pool changing rooms and outdoor steel fence on April 29, 2014. Shantou Jinhai Construction Co. was contracted for the work at a cost of RMB 44,400, with completion planned by May 28.

Construction may cause noise, vibration and dust. The university advised students and staff to take care and avoid lingering near the worksite.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
