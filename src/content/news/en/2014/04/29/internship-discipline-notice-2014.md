Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication announced a disciplinary decision on April 29 concerning a graduation internship. A student in the 2010 Broadcasting and Television Journalism cohort had been recommended by the school for an internship at the Shantou Bureau of Culture, Radio, Television, Press and Publication from February 26 to April 18, 2014. After two weeks, the student left without approval from the host organization, school or supervisor and began another internship in Guangzhou.

Under the university’s undergraduate internship regulations, and taking the student’s acknowledgement of the violation into account, the school decided to issue a college-wide criticism and cancel the student’s graduation internship qualification at the original host organization. Students were reminded to value internship opportunities and follow university, school and host-organization rules.

The student’s name is withheld to protect privacy.
