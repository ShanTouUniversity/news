Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office issued arrangements on April 29 for undergraduate thesis and design defenses and assessment in the spring semester of 2013–2014. Each school or department was required to complete defenses, grading and score entry by the end of Week 14, and to publish the defense committee, panel and student grouping arrangements in advance.

Supervisors and reviewers were asked to provide written comments and recommended grades as required, while defense committees would determine final grades. Students submitting final versions also had to provide a thesis-check report and sign an academic integrity pledge. The V1.1 thesis assessment form would continue to be used, with forms for supervisors, reviewers and defense committees completed and bound according to the prescribed order.

Electronic versions of outstanding theses would be added by the library to a full-text database for access on the campus network. Their authors and supervisors had to sign authorization statements. Academic administrators were required to collect thesis appraisal books and submit them to the Student Affairs Office by Week 16. Original copies of outstanding theses would be transferred by the Academic Affairs Office to the university archives, while other materials would be retained by the schools or departments.
