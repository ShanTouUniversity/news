Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office issued Labor Day holiday safety guidance on April 29. Under the university’s holiday and class-adjustment plan, May 1–3 would be holidays, May 4 a working day, and classes scheduled for Friday, May 2 would be exchanged with those scheduled for Sunday, May 4.

The office noted that Guangdong had entered a season of frequent severe convective weather. Before traveling, students and staff were advised to monitor warnings and avoid lightning, heavy rain, strong winds, hail and related risks such as falling structures, flooding and geological hazards. Travelers should follow traffic regulations, avoid unlicensed or overloaded vehicles, and choose reputable travel services and hygienic dining venues.

Students staying on campus were reminded to follow residence and campus safety rules, avoid hosting visitors, refrain from open flames and high-power appliances, keep fire exits clear, and stay away from dangerous waters such as the campus reservoir. The university also advised against climbing walls, railings and fruit trees. Abnormal situations could be reported to the Student Community Management Service Center or campus emergency services.
