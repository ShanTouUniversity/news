Shantou University’s Student Community X initiative will hold a dormitory door DIY competition for all students, including graduate students. Entries are expected to combine visual appeal, creativity and environmental awareness.

The preliminary round will take place at the Golden Triangle and Building E classrooms from May 17 to 18, using public voting and preliminary judging. The final round will be held in residential buildings and Building E classrooms from May 24 to 25, with judges scoring entries at participants’ dormitory doors and participants presenting their designs through multimedia. Awards will include first, second and third prizes, Best Creativity, Most Economical and Eco-Friendly Design, and Most Popular Entry. All participants will receive a gift.

On-site registration will be available at the Golden Triangle on April 30, while electronic registration closes at 11 p.m. on May 11. Participants could obtain the form through the campus information network and submit it as instructed. The event was organized by the Communist Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office, with support from relevant student organizations.
