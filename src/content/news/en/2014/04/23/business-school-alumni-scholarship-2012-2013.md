Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced the results of the 2012–13 Business School Alumni Scholarship review. The notice states that 38 students were selected to receive 2,500 yuan each. Their names, year groups and majors are not reproduced to protect student privacy.

The awards cover students in business administration, accounting, international economics and trade, finance, and marketing.

| --- | --- |
| Academic year | 2012–13 |
| Recipients | 38, according to the notice |
| Award | 2,500 yuan per recipient |
| Reviewing body | Shantou University Business School review committee |
