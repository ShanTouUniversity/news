Shantou University’s Business School will hold a resume competition from April 23 to May 14 for students who entered the school in 2011. The event aims to help students understand resume design and employer recruitment needs as they prepare for employment.

Participants must submit electronic and printed resumes to their class representatives by May 4. A school review panel will provide feedback. By May 11, each class will select five strong entries; 20 resumes will advance to an in-person final review on the afternoon of May 14. The competition will award one first prize, three second prizes, six third prizes and 10 merit prizes.
