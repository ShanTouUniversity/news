It is reported that the Student Union of Shantou University will hold the 7th “Voice Day” on April 25, 2014, to strengthen communication with students and improve the rights protection platform.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Voice Day |
| Participants | All students |
| Time | 11:30–13:00, April 25, 2014 |
| Venue | Jin San Jiao |
| Content | Booth to publicize complaint channels, handling procedures and timelines, accept complaints and consultations, showcase progress, collect suggestions; launch of a lost-and-found platform |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Student Union |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Student Union)
