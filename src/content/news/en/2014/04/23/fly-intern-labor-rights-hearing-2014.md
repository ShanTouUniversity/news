The fourth “Wings” program of Shantou University’s FLY training initiative will stage a mock hearing on the protection of labor rights during student internships on the morning of April 26. The event will take place in Lecture Hall E101. Twenty-two program participants will represent government education and labor departments, legal scholars, universities, trade unions and businesses, presenting and defending proposed solutions.

The event is open to the university community. It is organized by the Shantou University Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Shantou University Students’ Union.
