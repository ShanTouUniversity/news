The Cantonese Culture Association at Shantou University will organize a short film contest on Cantonese cuisine, open to all students. Entries may show Cantonese cooking, compare the cuisine with others, or tell a related story. Films must run for three to 10 minutes and include a written description of no more than 300 Chinese characters; any language is accepted.

Registration runs from April 23 to 29, and films must be submitted from April 29 to May 6. Online voting is scheduled for May 7–14, with judging and an awards presentation on May 15. Scores will combine judges’ ratings (50%), Weibo likes (20%) and team peer assessment (30%); the top three entries by cumulative score will receive prizes.

Students may enter individually or in teams. Each team must include at least one local member whose first language is not Cantonese. An in-person registration booth will be set up at the campus Golden Triangle on April 23; applications may also be submitted using the registration form.
