Shantou University’s Snow Leopard Outdoor Association will run its second outdoor leadership instructor course from April 26 to May 25, with instruction from Li Wei of the Shenzhen Mountaineering Association. The course is open to undergraduates who entered in 2012 or 2013 and current university staff. Applicants should have substantial outdoor experience, good physical and mental fitness, a strong sense of teamwork and responsibility.

The course combines classroom instruction and practical training. An initial application review will select about 25 students and four staff members for a second-stage assessment, comprising a fitness test and a presentation about outdoor experience. Sixteen participants will be selected through this stage. Ten additional places will be filled by members of the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association, bringing the course total to 26.

Applications are due by midnight on April 25. Participants who complete the course and pass its assessment will receive certificates from the STU Student Affairs Office and are expected to assist with outdoor skills instruction and course design for at least one year. Participants will cover a total of 190 yuan for insurance and food for two outdoor activities; camping equipment will be provided.
