The Party general branch of Shantou University’s Zhicheng College will take newly admitted Party members to the Fenshui Campaign Martyrs’ Cemetery in Yuhu, Jieyang, on April 27 for an oath ceremony. Existing members will renew their oaths. The program will also include a visit to the Shenxi Patriotic Education Base and group discussions.

New members are scheduled to gather at the Zhicheng College lobby at 8:20 a.m. before departing. The event is intended to promote civic responsibility, patriotic education and communication among the college’s Party branches.
