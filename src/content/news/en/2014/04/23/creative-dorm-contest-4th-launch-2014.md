Shantou University’s fourth Creative Dormitory Competition will run from April 23 to May 18 under the theme “A Dormitory of Our Own, Youthful Creativity.” Organized by the Student Affairs Office and the Student Dormitory Self-Management Committee, the competition invites students to submit creative works made for dormitory life.

Teams must represent one dormitory or a group of dormitories and include two to six students, with members living in no more than three dormitories. Entries must be physical works, such as handicrafts, calligraphy, paintings or practical inventions. Teams may use a presentation, video or digital photo album to explain their work, with up to 10 minutes for the presentation. The creative work accounts for 75% of the score and the presentation for 25%.

Registration closes on May 3. Forms may be submitted at designated campus notice points or through the event’s official channel. Entries will be photographed and returned after the competition. Awards include first, second and third prizes, a Best Creativity Award, an Environmental Pioneer Award and a participation prize.
