Two Shantou University students received recognition in 2013 student-aid and development programs organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. Their full names are omitted to protect student privacy.

One student in business administration was named a Guangdong Outstanding Student Role Model for Inspirational Growth and Achievement. The undergraduate category recognized 25 students across the province. A second student in journalism received the Youth Inspiration Award for the essay “White Paper and Butterfly” in the “Funding, Dreams and Achievement” essay program. The program recognized 50 works: five special prizes, 15 Youth Inspiration Awards, 10 Outstanding Awards and 20 Merit Awards.
