It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will host the latest installment of its "Toward Modern Mathematics" lecture series at 3:00 p.m. on Friday, April 18, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Room 412, North Engineering Building). The speaker will be Professor Wang Xiantao of Hunan Normal University.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Prof. Wang Xiantao (Hunan Normal University) |
| Title | The quasiconformal subinvariance property of John domains in R^n and its application |
| Time | Friday, April 18, 2014, 15:00 |
| Venue | Mathematics Laboratory (Room 412, North Engineering Building) |

## Abstract Overview

According to the abstract, the talk presents a complete solution to one of the open problems posed by Heinonen in 1989 concerning the subinvariance of John domains under quasiconformal mappings in Euclidean space R^n. As an application, the quasisymmetry of quasiconformal mappings is also discussed.

The lecture is open to all faculty and students, who are welcome to attend.
