Shantou University's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold a media salon from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Friday, April 18, in the library lecture hall. The topic is how mobile internet is changing relationships between people and media, with a focus on the Guangzhou Daily's media transition.

The speaker, Liu Huida, is a data product director and front-end engineer at Guangzhou Daily's Guangbao Data Technology Co., Ltd. He graduated from Shantou University's School of Journalism in 2011. The event is open to the university community.
