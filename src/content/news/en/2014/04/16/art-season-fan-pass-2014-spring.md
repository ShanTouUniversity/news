Shantou University's Arts Education Center has introduced a fan pass for the Colorful Arts Season Spring 2014, with a limit of 200 passes. Pass holders will receive priority when collecting performance tickets and will be offered better seating. “Super fans” who attended every performance last season may renew their passes without reapplying by presenting their Fall 2013 passes. Remaining places are open to the university community through the regular application process.

Applicants should email the registration form between April 16 and 18, then verify their information and collect a pass with their campus card at Room 309 of the Arts Education Center from 2:30 to 5 p.m. on April 20. Existing pass holders may renew there from 10 a.m. to noon that day. Attendees who complete every performance this season may also enter a closing-season lucky draw, with attendance verified by ticket stubs.
