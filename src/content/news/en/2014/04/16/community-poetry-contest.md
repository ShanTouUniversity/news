Shantou University's Wanghai Chao Poetry Society has opened a poetry contest on student community life. Community students may submit modern poems or prose poems on the theme; entries should be positive and sincere, and each participant may submit up to three poems.

Entries are due by midnight on April 18. The society plans to review submissions from April 21 to 27 and announce results at the end of April. Selection includes an initial review, a judges' panel worth 70% of the final score, and a student vote worth 30%, to be held at the campus Golden Triangle. Awards include two first prizes, three second prizes, four third prizes and ten merit awards, with certificates and corresponding prizes.

Entries may be submitted as A4 printouts or electronically. See the original OA notice for the submission location and contact details.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office)
