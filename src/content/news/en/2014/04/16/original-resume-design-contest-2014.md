Shantou University's Student Affairs Office will hold an original resume competition from mid-April to mid-May 2014 for currently enrolled students. The competition has practical and creative divisions. Entries must be individually created, complete and distinctive; printed resumes are required for the preliminary round. The final, scheduled for May 16, will include presentations, speeches and judges' questions.

Registration and preliminary submissions run from April 16 to 27. A training session on resume content and design is tentatively scheduled for April 23 in the E lecture hall. The practical division emphasizes content and layout, while the creative division welcomes formats such as comic or three-dimensional resumes. Awards include one first prize, two second prizes and three third prizes, with cash awards of RMB 300, 200 and 100 respectively. Finalists will receive certificates and experience cards, and may be considered for internships offered by a co-organizer.

Applications may be submitted electronically or on paper. See the original OA attachment for the form and collection points.
