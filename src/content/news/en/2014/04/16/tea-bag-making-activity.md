Shantou University's Tea Society will hold a handmade tea bag activity on April 19, 2014, open to the university community. It will take place in two sessions: 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. in the CD Block activity hall, and 5 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. at the entrance to the EF dormitory area.

Participants can sew tea bags and sachet-style tea bags, sample gongfu tea, learn about tea, and join icebreaker games. Materials will be provided, and tea eggs will also be served. On-site registration is available. Further details are available through the society's official Weibo account.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office)
