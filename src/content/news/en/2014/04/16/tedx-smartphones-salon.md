Zhicheng College will hold a TEDx Shantou University salon on April 19 from 2 to 4 p.m. at the Coffee Book House. The discussion, titled “Smartphones Through the Years,” is organized by the college's Youth League Committee and the “Evergreen” Service Learning Association and is open to the university community.

Guests include Jerry, who works at the Convergent Media Lab of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, and Kwan Chi Man, a teacher at the Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design and founder of the Locomotive Creative Studio. The original notice renders Jerry's Chinese name illegibly, so it is not reproduced.
