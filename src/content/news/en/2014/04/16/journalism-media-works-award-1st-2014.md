It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication of Shantou University will hold the inaugural Changjiang Media Works Awards from April 15 to May 24, 2014, to honor students who uphold journalistic ideals and engage actively in media practice.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Current students of the School of Journalism |
| Period | April 15 – May 24, 2014 |
| Application deadline | April 30, 2014 |
| How to apply | Download the application form, name it as “Student Name + Category” and send it to jsprize@163.com; submit each work on a separate disc to the secretariat contact Bai Fan (short number 69112) |
| Organizer | School of Journalism |

## Categories

| Category | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| In-depth News Reporting | In-depth reporting on any topic, at least 2,500 words, individual or group |
| Photography | News and thematic photography, no more than 6 images for thematic sets with captions; cropping and contrast adjustment allowed, but no Photoshop; individual only |
| Layout and Design | Book and poster design, books may be individual or group, posters individual only |
| Interactive Visual Design | Websites, Apps, animations, individual or group |
| Video | Documentary or non-documentary video, no more than 30 minutes, individual or group |
| Advertising | Print, photographic, video, creative or planning advertising, individual or group |

## Awards

Three awards will be given in each category, with additional shortlist prizes. Winners will receive a cash prize and a certificate. Works will not be returned.

Further details are available via the official Weibo account of the School of Journalism. The application form is available in the OA attachment and contact details are withheld here for privacy.
