It is reported that, in accordance with the requirements of the Guangdong Higher Education Educational Technology Center, Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office and the Modern Educational Technology Center have recently launched the campus application process for trial courses under the "Young-teacher ICT-based New Class" program (YINC).

## Application Requirements

Trial course projects must meet the following requirements:

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project leader | Young teachers under 45 years old |
| Teaching team | Mainly composed of young teachers; senior experts may be invited as advisors |
| Online platform | Able to provide an online teaching platform with existing basic online teaching resources |

## Submission

Teachers interested in applying should complete the application form and send it as a PDF file to lxl0726@stu.edu.cn by 17:00 on April 25. The university will select and recommend candidates based on the applications received.
