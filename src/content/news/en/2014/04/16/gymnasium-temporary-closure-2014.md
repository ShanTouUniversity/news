It is reported that the Department of Physical Education at Shantou University issued a notice on April 16 that the university gymnasium will be temporarily closed.

The notice, addressed to all faculty and students, did not specify the reason, duration, or reopening date, which are expected to be announced separately. Members of the university community who plan to use the gymnasium are advised to adjust their arrangements and watch for updates on the Office Automation system.
