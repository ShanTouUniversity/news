Shantou University issued revised versions of its *Internal Audit Work Regulations* and *Measures for Auditing the Economic Responsibility of University Leaders* on April 3, 2014. The rules were discussed and approved at the fourth university leadership meeting on April 1 and took effect upon publication. They replaced the university’s 2002 internal-audit implementation measures and its 2007 interim measures on economic-responsibility audits.

The revised internal-audit rules designate the Supervision and Audit Office as the university’s audit body, operating independently under the president’s leadership. Its oversight covers university and affiliated-unit finances, budgets and final accounts, education funds, fixed assets, construction and repair projects, investments, internal controls and risk management. The process includes preparation, audit work and reporting; audited units must provide requested records and may submit written comments on draft findings.

The economic-responsibility measures cover leaders’ performance during their terms, including institutional development, compliance with policies and laws, major economic decisions, financial and asset management, internal controls and compliance with integrity requirements. Audit results are sent to relevant organizational and personnel departments to inform evaluation, appointment and discipline decisions, and the final report is placed in the leader’s personnel file.
