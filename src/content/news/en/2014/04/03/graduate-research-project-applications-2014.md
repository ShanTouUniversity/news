Shantou University Graduate School has opened applications for its 2014 graduate extracurricular academic and science projects. The program is open to all currently enrolled graduate students at the main campus. Applications will be accepted from April 10 through May 5, and approved projects are expected to be announced on the Graduate School website and the university’s OA system in mid-May.

## Eligibility and process

Projects must be completed by students in their spare time and must not form part of the teaching plan. Dissertations, graduation projects and academic-year papers are not eligible. Categories include social survey reports and academic papers, literary and artistic works, entrepreneurship plans, and inventions or creative works. Interdisciplinary and cross-major teams are encouraged.

Applicants must complete two copies of the STU Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Science Research Project Application Form and submit them to the Graduate School Youth League office. The school will organize expert reviews. Hard copies must reach Administrative Center 401 by May 5, with electronic copies sent as required.
