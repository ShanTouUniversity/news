The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office issued a reminder on April 3, 2014, for teachers who reserved textbooks but have not yet collected them to pick them up at the office service counter by the end of the workday on April 8.

Books not collected by the deadline will be returned to the supplier and will no longer be provided, according to the notice.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Action | Collect reserved teaching textbooks |
| Location | Academic Affairs Office service counter |
| Deadline | End of the workday, April 8, 2014 |
| After the deadline | Books will be returned to the supplier |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
