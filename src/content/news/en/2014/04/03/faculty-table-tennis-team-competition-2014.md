The Shantou University Labor Union will hold a faculty table tennis team competition beginning April 14, with eight teams representing schools, offices and related units.

Last year's champion, the School of Science, and runner-up, the Resource Management team, will be placed in Groups A and B respectively. The remaining teams will be assigned by draw. Each team should complete its registration form and attend the draw at the university Labor Union office at 10 a.m. on April 9.
