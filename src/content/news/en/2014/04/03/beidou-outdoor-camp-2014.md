The Beidou Outdoor Development Association of Zhicheng College will hold its second Beidou Outdoor Camp from April 12 to 13, 2014, and is recruiting about 20 college residents. The camp will include outdoor skills, camping and team-development activities. The route will begin at Fengmen Ancient Path Scenic Area in Jieyang, camp near Xin’an Reservoir in Shaxi, Chao’an, and end at Dongshan Lake Tourist Resort.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Activity period | April 12 through the afternoon of April 13 |
| Participants | Residents of Zhicheng College |
| Planned places | 20; lots will be drawn if applications exceed capacity |
| Registration deadline | 2 p.m., April 9 |
| Cost | Participants cover meals for the two-day, one-night activity |

Participants must have no conflicting classes or activities, follow team instructions, remain with the group and be prepared to cooperate. Applicants should submit personal information as required by the original OA notice; student telephone numbers and other personal contact details are not reproduced here.
