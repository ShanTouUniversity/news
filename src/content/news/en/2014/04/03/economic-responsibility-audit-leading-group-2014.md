Shantou University announced on April 3, 2014, the establishment of a leading group to promote and strengthen economic responsibility audits of officials. The decision was made at the fourth university leadership meeting of 2014 on April 1.

The group includes university leaders and relevant department representatives. One chair's name contains a garbled character in the original record and is not reconstructed here. Its office is based in the Supervision and Audit Office, which will coordinate its routine work.

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Chairs | Xu Xiaohu and another university leader (the original record has a garbled character in the name) |
| Deputy chair | Luo Nianchao |
| Members | Zeng Dabin, Wu Ping, Zou Zhibo, Chen Shaogang and Sun Fenqiang |
| Office | Supervision and Audit Office |
| Document | STU Document No. 31 (2014) |
