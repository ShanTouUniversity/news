The Shantou University Research Affairs Office relayed an initiative from the Guangdong Provincial Publicity Department on April 3, 2014, inviting ideological and political theory teachers at universities in Guangdong to propose topics for commissioned research on major issues related to fostering and practicing core socialist values.

The initiative seeks to support related theoretical research and develop high-quality results. Interested teachers may submit a proposed topic based on their teaching and research work by April 9 to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn`.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Research theme | Major issues in fostering and practicing core socialist values |
| Eligible participants | Ideological and political theory teachers at universities in Guangdong |
| Submission | Proposed research topic |
| Deadline | April 9, 2014 |
| Contacts | Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun |
