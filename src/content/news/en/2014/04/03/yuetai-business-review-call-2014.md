It is reported that the Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation Research Institute of Shantou University issued a call for papers on April 3, 2014, to launch the *Yuetai Business Review* academic series.

Published annually by Shantou University Press with an official book number, the series uses international double-blind review and publishes original, high-quality Chinese papers in economics and management, including theoretical, empirical, case, review and commentary articles. Its mission is to advance research on Taiwanese and Cantonese business and cross-strait economic cooperation.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Host | Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation Research Institute (Key Humanities and Social Sciences Research Base of Guangdong) |
| Scope | Industrial cooperation across the strait, transformation and upgrading of Taiwanese and Cantonese businesses, enterprise cooperation, and economic cooperation in the Western Taiwan Strait Economic Zone |
| Requirement | Manuscripts must not have been published |
| Submission | Word document to yt@stu.edu.cn, contact Li Yanqun, 0754-86502260 |
| Deadline | April 30, 2014 |
| Review | Results notified within one month; accepted manuscripts will be revised to meet style requirements, with two complimentary copies per author upon publication |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation Research Institute, Business School)
