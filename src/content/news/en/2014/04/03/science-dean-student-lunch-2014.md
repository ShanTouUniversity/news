It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a lunch meeting between its leaders and students on April 10, 2014, to encourage students to offer suggestions and participate in school development.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 12:15–13:45, April 10, 2014 |
| Venue | Hongtai Restaurant, Room 201 |
| Guests | Dean, vice deans, Party Branch Secretary of the School of Science and heads of departments and teacher representatives |
| Participants | All students of the School of Science |
| Places | 25 |
| Organizer | School of Science |

## Registration

Interested students are asked to log in to the reservation system at http://booking.stu.edu.cn and complete the application by 22:00 on April 8, 2014, with an opportunity to note questions or suggestions for the school in the remarks field. The system will close automatically once the quota is reached. The list of invited student representatives will be announced on the campus office information network.

The lunch will be prepared by Hongtai Restaurant as a set meal, with costs covered by the school. Participants are encouraged to serve as a bridge, collect concerns from peers in a truthful manner and engage in respectful exchange. Questions or suggestions may be sent to ltzhang@stu.edu.cn.
