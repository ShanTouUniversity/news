Shantou University Library announced on April 3, 2014, that the Shangwei science report resource service is available for trial use through December 31, 2014.

The service collects reports from organizations and institutions including the International Monetary Fund, the U.S. Department of Energy, the World Bank, NASA, IBM, Stanford University and the University of Tokyo. Users can search by title, abstract, author, report number or keywords, then request document delivery through the full-text service. Requested reports are expected to be returned by email within three to five days.

Access is available at [the library resource page](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=389). Users may send trial feedback to the Library Subject Services Department at `lqxie@stu.edu.cn` or 86503247.
