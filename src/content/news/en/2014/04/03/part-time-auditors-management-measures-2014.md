Shantou University published its management measures for part-time auditors on April 3, 2014. The measures had been discussed and approved at the university's fourth leadership meeting of 2014 on April 1, and the university's units are to follow the new arrangements.

The measures set out the framework for managing the university's part-time auditors. The detailed provisions are provided in the OA attachment.

| Approval meeting | Fourth university leadership meeting of 2014 |
| Approval date | April 1, 2014 |
| Publication date | April 3, 2014 |
| Scope | All university units |
