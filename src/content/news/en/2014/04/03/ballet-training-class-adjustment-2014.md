The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office announced a schedule adjustment on April 3, 2014, for Class 67038 of *Ballet Training*. The class scheduled for April 8 will be canceled and made up on April 23.

The make-up class will take place in Dance Rehearsal Room STU102. Relevant teaching units and enrolled students should follow the revised schedule.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Ballet Training*, Class 67038 |
| Canceled | Periods 3–4 on Tuesday, April 8, 2014 |
| Make-up | Periods 8–9 on Wednesday, April 23, 2014 |
| Location | Dance Rehearsal Room STU102 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
