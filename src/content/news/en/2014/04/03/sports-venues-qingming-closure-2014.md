The Shantou University Department of Physical Education announced on April 3, 2014, that several sports facilities will temporarily close because of filming by the China Voice production crew and the Qingming holiday.

The indoor badminton court, indoor table tennis court and gym will be closed from April 5 through 7. Faculty and students should adjust their facility-use plans accordingly.

| --- | --- |
| Facilities | Indoor badminton court, indoor table tennis court and gym |
| Closure | April 5–7, 2014 |
| Reasons | Production filming and the Qingming holiday |
| Issuing unit | Department of Physical Education |
