Zhicheng College will hold its seventh Career Development Training Camp on April 12–13, 2014, to help students understand social needs, plan their careers and improve interview and job-search skills.

The camp is primarily intended for second- and third-year students and will admit 36 participants, including six students from other residential areas. Sessions will cover mock interviews, group discussion without a designated leader, résumé guidance, advice from outstanding graduates, business exchanges and company visits.

The training will run all day on April 12 and through the morning of April 13. Applicants must have no other classes or activities during those periods. Registration closes at 5 p.m. on April 10, with applications submitted to `ATP@stu.edu.cn` in the format specified in the notice.
