Shantou University Law School has announced the final results of its 2014 Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship Program. Following preliminary screening and interviews, 12 students from the public administration program and 10 students from the law program were selected. Four additional students were designated as alternates.

The program is intended for students in relevant Law School programs and will place selected students in summer internships in Hong Kong. Because some internship places were still being confirmed, the school also established an alternate order based on the selection results. Student names are omitted from this report to protect privacy.
