Shantou University’s Communist Youth League Committee held a summary and awards event in Room E203 on April 2, 2014, and announced six winning projects from the 2013 Featured Youth League Day Project program, themed “Better Study Habits, Better Growth.” The program ran among student League members from November 2013 through March 2014.

## Winning projects

| Award | Project |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | “School of Engineering Study-Habit Improvement Series” |
| Second prize | “Learning from Outstanding Entrepreneurs”; “Building Stronger Physics Students Through Better Study Habits” |
| Third prize | “Academic Exchange Sessions for Study-Habit Development at Shantou University Medical College”; “Reflecting and Growing Together”; “Talk About Growth, Build Study Habits, Win the Future” |

Six Featured Youth League Day projects received awards. The committee asked winning units to submit the organizational and payment information required for prize distribution. Personal names, student numbers, bank accounts and contact details are omitted from this report.
