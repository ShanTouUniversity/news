It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on April 3, 2014, postponing the Heyuan Qimuzhang Yanziyan intermediate outdoor training originally scheduled for April 4–7.

According to the notice, the planned route includes valleys and slopes. Given current and upcoming weather, the mountain’s water retention is near saturation, posing a landslide risk; further rainfall could flow directly into valleys and cause flash floods; hiking on wet, slippery surfaces also increases the risk of sprains and falls. For risk management, the event has been cancelled for that period and will be rescheduled, with the new date to be announced.

For inquiries, contact details are available in the original OA notice and are withheld here for privacy.
