The selection process for the 2014 Shantou University–Technion EDC (Engineering for Developing Communities) summer course has reached the interview stage. The program received 94 applications, and the selection panel shortlisted 31 candidates for interviews.

## Interview arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Interview time | 6:30 p.m., Wednesday, April 9, 2014 |
| Interview venue | Third-floor conference room, School of Engineering (Gongnan 306) |
| Format | Questions from the panel |
| Check-in venue | Third-floor academic exchange room, School of Engineering (Gongnan 312) |

Shortlisted students must check in at Gongnan 312 20 minutes before the individual time listed in the attachment, then proceed to the interview venue. The candidate list and individual schedule contain student information and are available in the original OA notice.
