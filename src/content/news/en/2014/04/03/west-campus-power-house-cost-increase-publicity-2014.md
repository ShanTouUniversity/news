The Shantou University Infrastructure Office published a notice on April 3, 2014, about proposed additional work for the West Campus power house project. The project began on December 11, 2013; changes made during construction include adding stainless-steel rodent barriers and modifying cable trenches to accommodate equipment and improve supply-equipment safety.

The preliminary estimate for the additional work is about RMB 3,000, below the university's 10 percent limit. Written comments may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on April 8.
