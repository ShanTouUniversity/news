A workshop for Shantou University’s original resume competition will be held on April 23, 2014. The program will feature advice from guest speakers on resume writing and experience shared by previous contestants. Students who have registered for the competition are expected to attend; other students are welcome, and on-site competition registration will also be available.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 3–5 p.m., April 23, 2014 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E201 |
| Speakers | Zhu Guang and Ye Ying |
