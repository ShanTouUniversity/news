It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office announced on April 22, 2014 that it will organize final acceptance for undergraduate innovation experimental projects launched in 2011 and 2012 that have not yet been concluded.

## Scope and Format

The review covers all unfinished projects launched in 2011 and all unfinished projects launched in 2012 (see Attachment 4). Review consists of written submissions plus a public defense.

## Written Submissions

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | May 16, 2014 |
| Materials | Final Acceptance Form (Attachment 1, with supervisor comments), Supervisor Work Manual (Attachment 2), Progress Log (Attachment 3), team member summaries and supporting evidence such as reports, patents, software, prototypes or publications |
| Submission | Three printed copies to the Academic Affairs service window on the 3rd floor of the new Administration Center; electronic version to the Teaching Project Management Platform at project.stu.edu.cn (see Attachment 6 for instructions) |
| Organizer | Academic Affairs Office |

## Public Defense

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Tentatively May 28, 2014; specific time, venue and grouping to be announced |
| Format | Expert panel reviews materials and hears PPT presentations with Q&A |
| Requirements | 10–12 minutes per project (about 7 minutes presentation, 5 minutes Q&A), all members present 30 minutes early; bring prototypes, programs or photos/videos as evidence; contact the Academic Affairs Office in advance for special equipment needs |

## Other Matters

Results are graded as Excellent, Good, Pass or Fail. Projects rated Excellent, Good or Pass receive 2 credits for research and innovation, recorded in the credit system. Projects needing extension must submit a written application signed by the supervisor; projects already extended cannot be extended again. Projects with poor implementation or misuse of funds will have funding suspended or reclaimed.
