The Guangdong Department of Science and Technology plans to launch a group of major science and technology projects and is soliciting proposals for candidate projects. The projects are intended to support industrial transformation through core technology breakthroughs and integrated resources. Candidate areas include key common technologies, strategic product research and development, and integrated system construction.

STU faculty members wishing to recommend projects should complete the Recommendation Form for Guangdong Major Science and Technology Projects. Two printed copies must be submitted to the Research Office by the end of the morning on April 25, 2014, and an electronic copy sent to cszhu@stu.edu.cn. The form and instructions are provided in the OA attachments.

Contacts: Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng; 86502813.
