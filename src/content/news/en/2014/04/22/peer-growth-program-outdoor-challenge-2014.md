The Student Affairs Office and Student Learning Center will hold the fourth session of STU’s Peer Growth Program on April 26, 2014, under the theme “Set Out and Challenge Life’s Extremes.” Two student guests will discuss how outdoor experiences can support personal growth, reflection on life goals and balance between academic work and campus activities. To protect student privacy, their names are shown by family names and initials only: Luo LF and Zhang YD.

Both guests have participated in Antarctic expeditions, and one studied at Whitman College in the United States as an exchange student. Their other accomplishments include scholarships and awards in speech or student psychological theater competitions.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., April 26, 2014 |
| Venue | Student Activity Room 726, Building 789 |
| Audience | All enrolled students |
| Capacity | 25, assigned in registration order |
| Registration deadline | 5 p.m., April 25, 2014 |

Students may register at booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm and note questions of interest. After registration closes, three student representatives will be selected for further discussion with the guests. The guests will also accept one-on-one consultation appointments through the Student Learning Center for two weeks after the workshop.
