The MT Enterprise Innovation Club of Shantou University’s College of Engineering has opened recruitment for its next management team, inviting applications from students who entered the university in 2012. The club is supported by the College of Engineering and alumni-founded companies.

Five roles are available: chief executive, technology, business, finance and design directors. Their responsibilities cover the club’s overall operations, technology development, commercial projects, financial operations, and project design and event planning. Applicants must meet at least one of these criteria: prior organizational leadership experience, membership in the 2012 MT cohort, or experience leading a large project.

Applicants must submit the form by email before 11 p.m. on April 24. Interview details will be sent to applicants by email.
