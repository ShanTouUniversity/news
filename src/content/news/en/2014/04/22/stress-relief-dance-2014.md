Shantou University’s ninth Mental Health Month, themed “Breaking the Cocoon and Becoming a Butterfly,” will conclude with a stress-relief dance on April 25, 2014. The event, themed “Release Pressure, Show Your Style,” will include a stress test, circle dancing and pressure-release activities, and exercises focused on positive energy flow.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 4–6 p.m., April 25, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 726, Building 789 |
| Capacity | 15 men and 15 women, first come, first served |
| Registration | Complete the OA attachment form and email it to o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn |
| Deadline | Noon, April 24, 2014 |

> ⚠️ The registration form is available in the original attachment on the OA system.
>
