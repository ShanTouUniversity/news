Renovation of Shantou University’s Civil Engineering Materials Laboratory, formerly the Building Materials Laboratory, began on March 11, 2014. To meet user needs during construction, wall tiling, workbenches and other items were added. The Infrastructure Office estimates the additional cost at approximately RMB 3,000, within the university’s 10 percent limit.

In accordance with the university’s infrastructure project bidding and tendering rules, the university is publicizing the additional cost. Written comments should be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on April 24, 2014.
