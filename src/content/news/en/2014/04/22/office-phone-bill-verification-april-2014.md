China Telecom Shantou and China Mobile Shantou have sent the February and March 2014 electronic bills for university units to STU, and the data has been uploaded to the phone management system. Unit reviewers should log in at lrmo.stu.edu.cn and check the China Telecom landline and China Mobile landline bills separately.

Bills without discrepancies should be confirmed in the system. Errors should be reported by April 28: China Telecom bills to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn and China Mobile bills to stunic@stu.edu.cn. Bills not confirmed or disputed by the deadline will be treated as confirmed, and the Finance Office will deduct payment on April 30 using each unit’s designated funding number.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center and Resources Management Office)
