Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office will conduct final acceptance for unfinished undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training projects launched in 2012 and selected projects launched in 2013 with one-year implementation periods. The review will consist of written submissions and a public defense; the detailed project list is in the OA attachments.

## Schedule and Materials

Written materials are due by May 16, 2014. Three printed copies should be submitted to the Academic Affairs service window on the third floor of the new Administration Center, while an electronic copy should be uploaded to the Teaching Project Management Platform at project.stu.edu.cn. Required materials include the Guangdong Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Project Final Acceptance Form, a progress report, team-member summaries and supporting evidence.

The public defense is tentatively scheduled for May 28, with the exact time, venue and groups to be announced. Each project team will give a PPT presentation and answer questions for 10–12 minutes, including about seven minutes for the presentation and five minutes for questions. All members should arrive 30 minutes early and prepare prototypes, programs or photo and video evidence as appropriate.

Results will be rated Excellent, Good, Pass or Fail. Projects receiving one of the first three ratings will receive two research and innovation credits. Projects seeking an extension must submit a written application signed by the supervisor, and previously extended projects cannot receive a second extension. Contact Ms. Guo Yijia at yjguo@stu.edu.cn or 86502736 (short number 882736).
