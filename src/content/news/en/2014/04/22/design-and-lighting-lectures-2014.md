Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host two design lectures on the evening of April 24, 2014. Zheng Shaowen will present “Interpreting Design: Letting Works Speak,” while Chen Youfa will present “Variations of Light and Shadow: On Lighting Design.” The Shantou Decoration Industry Association is a co-organizer, and students and staff are welcome.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., April 24, 2014 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, first floor, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |
| Speakers | Zheng Shaowen and Chen Youfa |
