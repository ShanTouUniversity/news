Shantou University’s Mental Health Education and Counseling Center will hold an outdoor consultation event at the Golden Triangle on the pedestrian street on April 23, 2014, under the theme “Breaking the Cocoon and Becoming a Butterfly.” The program will include on-site counseling, psychological tests, games, personal messages and friendly interaction.

The event will run from 3 to 5 p.m. and is open to all STU students and staff.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
