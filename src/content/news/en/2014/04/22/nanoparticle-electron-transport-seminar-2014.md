The School of Science at Shantou University will hold a Science Salon seminar on April 24, 2014, featuring Professor Han Min of Nanjing University’s School of Modern Engineering and Applied Sciences. His presentation is titled “Electron Transport in Dense Nanoparticle Arrays and Applications in Nanosensors.”

The seminar will examine how tunneling and thermally activated transport in nanoparticle arrays relate to particle spacing, temperature and coverage, as well as applications in hydrogen and strain sensors. It will also discuss the effect of localized surface-plasmon fields under visible-light excitation on electron transport.

Professor Han’s research focuses on atomic-cluster physics, nanoparticles and assembled arrays. He has led projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the 863 and 973 programs, published more than 80 international journal papers, received more than 10 Chinese invention patents, and won national and provincial science and technology awards.
