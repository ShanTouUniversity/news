Shantou University’s ninth Mental Health Month is scheduled to host an event titled “Break Down Barriers and Make a Friend with a Stranger” on April 20. Open to the university community, the activity plans to enroll 30 participants, pair them up for a campus search and hold a social gathering later in the day.

The search is scheduled for 9:30–11:30 a.m., with participants using information provided by organizers to find their partners on campus. The gathering is set for 3–5 p.m. Participants should complete the form in the notice and email it to the official address, `o_xlzx@stu.edu.cn`, by April 17.
