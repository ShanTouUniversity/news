Shantou University’s Business School is scheduled to hold a workshop for its student leaders from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on April 16 in E102, the moot court room.

The workshop will feature sessions on developing emotional intelligence and understanding social etiquette, led by Professor Liu Dawei and manager Chen Shuping, respectively. Organized by the school’s Party General Branch and Youth League Committee, the program aims to strengthen student leaders’ communication, coordination, teamwork and organizational skills.
