Shantou University’s School of Engineering is scheduled to hold a session titled “Study Abroad: What You Need to Know” on the evening of April 18. It will introduce the school’s overseas study programs, placement outcomes, application procedures and practical considerations. The event is open to the university community and will run from 7:30 to 9 p.m. in E203.

The speaker, a School of Engineering professor who directs its joint education office and electrical engineering department, will draw on overseas academic and professional experience. Interested participants were asked to register through the booking system by 10 a.m. on April 17.
