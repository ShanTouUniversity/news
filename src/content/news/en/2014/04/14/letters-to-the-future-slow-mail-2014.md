Shantou University’s ninth Mental Health Month is scheduled to host a “Letters to the Future” slow-mail activity. Open to the university community, it will run from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on April 19 at the Golden Triangle on the campus pedestrian street.

Students who took part in the previous year’s activity may collect their stored letters. Paper, envelopes, glue and colored pens will also be available for participants to write to themselves or others one year in the future. Organizers will collect and seal the letters for planned delivery a year later.
