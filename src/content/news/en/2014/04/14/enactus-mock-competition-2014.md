Shantou University’s Enactus team, formerly known as SIFE, is scheduled to hold a mock competition at 3 p.m. on April 16 in ACC Meeting Room 3 as preparation for the South China regional round of the 2014 Enactus China National Competition. Team members will present the Lei Li Feng Xing initiative and a Fenghuang Mountain tea promotion project, with about 25 minutes allocated to each presentation.

Representatives of the Li Ka Shing Foundation, business school leaders, team advisers, and academic and business consultants are expected to attend. Founded in 2008, the team includes about 60 students from seven university schools and develops community-focused projects.
