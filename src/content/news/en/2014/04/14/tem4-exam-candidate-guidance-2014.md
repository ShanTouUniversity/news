Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office issued guidance for candidates taking the Test for English Majors Band 4 (TEM-4). The 2014 examination is scheduled to begin at 8:30 a.m. on Saturday, April 19, and last about 135 minutes. Sections include dictation, listening comprehension, cloze, grammar and vocabulary, reading comprehension and writing.

Candidates must use the designated answer sheets for each section, fill multiple-choice answers with a 2B pencil, and use black ink for dictation and writing. Late arrivals will not be admitted 15 minutes after the exam begins. Candidates must bring their admission ticket, ID card and student card; mobile phones and dictionaries are prohibited. Consult the Academic Affairs Office notice for full procedures and exam rules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
