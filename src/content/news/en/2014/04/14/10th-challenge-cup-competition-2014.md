Shantou University issued the schedule for its 10th "Challenge Cup" extracurricular academic and scientific works competition on April 14, 2014. Themed "Challenge Dreams, Create Brilliance," the competition is open to university students; natural-science academic papers are limited to undergraduates.

The competition has three categories: natural-science academic papers; social-science survey reports and academic papers; and scientific or technological inventions. Students must take the lead in designing and completing entries independently. Graduation projects, course projects, annual papers, degree theses, and works already awarded in international competitions or at the national level are ineligible.

Each school must submit its application forms and supporting materials by April 25. Five printed copies are required, with electronic files submitted on a USB drive at the temporary office in the Youth League Committee. Preliminary judging is expected in early May, followed by public defenses and an exhibition of selected works in mid-May. Entries may be further developed through February 2015 and, after review, represent the university in the provincial competition.

The competition is organized by the Shantou University Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities Committee, with the Shantou University Youth League Committee as organizer and the university's Association for Science and Technology as co-organizer.

> ⚠️ Application forms and detailed submission instructions are available on the OA system.
