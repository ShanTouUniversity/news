Shantou University announced a schedule adjustment for sections 69327 and 69328 of the Higher Education Institute course *China Higher Education Issues*. The classes originally scheduled for April 14 were rescheduled.

Both make-up classes will take place on April 21 in E202-2. Section 69327 will meet during periods 6 and 7, while section 69328 will meet during periods 8 and 9. Students should follow the revised schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
