Shantou University Library featured *Xue Kan Collection*, a volume on the Ming-era scholar Xue Kan (1486–1545), a native of Jieyang in Guangdong and an early disciple of Wang Yangming. Xue helped preserve and circulate Wang Yangming’s works and promoted Yangming learning in the Chaoshan region.

The book brings together Xue Kan’s surviving works, including *Yunmen Record*, *Researching the Subtle* and *Questions on the Book and Images*. Its appendices include oral accounts, biographies, commemorative writings and Rao Zongyi’s *Chronology of Xue Zhongli*. They also present lost writings and related biographical materials by Xue Kan’s younger brother Xue Qiao and nephew Xue Zongkai.

The library noted that the volume was edited and proofread by Chen Ye, a native of Chenghai, Guangdong, who holds a doctorate in Chinese philosophy from Sun Yat-sen University and researches Song-Ming Neo-Confucianism and Chaoshan history and culture.
