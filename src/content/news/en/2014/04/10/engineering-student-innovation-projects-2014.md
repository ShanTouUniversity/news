The School of Engineering at Shantou University has opened applications for its 2014 extracurricular student technology innovation projects. The program is open to all enrolled students in the school. It forms part of the school’s EIP-CDIO engineering education reform and is intended to build skills in project development, design and implementation, together with innovation, communication, leadership and teamwork.

## Application Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Enrolled students in the School of Engineering |
| Application period | April 10–21, 2014 |
| Typical project period | Six months to one year |
| Participation limit | Normally one project per student; students who abandoned a previous approved project without reason are ineligible |
| Team formation | Cross-disciplinary and cross-year undergraduate teams are encouraged |

The school will provide suggested topics connected with upcoming competitions and previous outstanding projects. Project leaders must complete the application form and submit three printed copies plus an electronic copy to their class representative by 10:00 a.m. on April 21. Class representatives will consolidate submissions, and each department’s Party general branch must deliver the paper and electronic versions to the School of Engineering Youth League Office by 4:00 p.m. that day. The electronic version should also be sent to `12zzli1@stu.edu.cn`.
