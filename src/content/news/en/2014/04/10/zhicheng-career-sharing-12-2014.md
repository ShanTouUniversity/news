It is reported that Zhicheng College of Shantou University will hold the 12th Career Planning Sharing Session on April 12 in Lecture Room J-354.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | My Career Path |
| Speaker | Wu Chaorong, Factory Director of Guangdong Baojia Stainless Steel Industrial Co., Ltd. (also Director of Guangdong Hongweida Stainless Steel Co., Ltd. and Secretary of the Party Branch) |
| Time | 15:00–16:30, April 12, 2014 |
| Venue | Lecture Room J-354, Zhicheng College |
| Registration | Send an email with subject “Grade + Major + Name + Mobile (short) No. – Career (12) Registration” to o_zctw@stu.edu.cn |
| Deadline | 17:00, April 11, 2014 |
| Notification | Results will be notified by email or phone before the end of work on April 11 |
| Organizer | Zhicheng College |

Mr. Wu has been honored as an Advanced Individual in Corporate Culture Construction in Guangdong and has promoted traditional culture such as *Di Zi Gui* within his enterprise since 2009, founding the corporate newsletter *Voice of Baojia*. A detailed speaker profile is available in the OA notice.

> ⚠️ The registration email is available on the OA system.
