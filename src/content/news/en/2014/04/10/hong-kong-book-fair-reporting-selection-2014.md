The Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University has opened selection for students to take part in its 2014 Hong Kong Book Fair reporting activity. The school plans to select 10 third-year journalism students and graduate students for reporting scheduled from July 16 to 22. If a selected student cannot travel, the next candidate in order will take the place.

Applicants must complete the application form and submit a printed copy by 5 p.m. on April 14. The school plans to hold interviews at 3 p.m. on Wednesday, April 23, in the school’s glass room, after which a selection committee will determine the participants.

## Selection Criteria

Applicants should have a strong GPA and skills in reporting, writing and photography. Media internship experience is an advantage. Applicants should also be in good health, diligent, resilient and able to work in a team; Cantonese ability is preferred. Students who have previously taken part in overseas internships or reporting activities arranged by the school will generally not be considered.
