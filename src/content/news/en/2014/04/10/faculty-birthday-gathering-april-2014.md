The Shantou University union will host a birthday gathering for faculty and staff whose birthdays fall between March and May at 6:30 p.m. on April 19, 2014, at a western restaurant in a downtown hotel. The event is organized by the union with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation. Employees and their families may attend with tickets.

Registration will be open through the booking system from noon on April 10 to noon on April 15. Payment is scheduled for April 15–16, and tickets can be collected from 9:30 a.m. to noon on April 18. No university shuttle will be provided, so attendees must arrange their own transportation and take responsibility for safety, including supervising children.

The union noted that tickets cannot be refunded after registration and payment. For booking-system support, contact the Network Center at 86503545.
