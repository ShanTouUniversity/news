It is reported that, as part of the 2014 Employment Service Month, the Law School of Shantou University will hold a career lecture on April 12 (Saturday) at 9:30 am in Classroom E301, under the theme "Employers' Expectations of Talent." All faculty members and students are welcome to attend.

Titled "Confessions of an HR Director — Employers' Expectations of Talent," the lecture will be delivered by Lin En. According to the Law School, the event aims to help students understand what capabilities employers seek, develop their professional competence and skills, plan their careers early, and strengthen their competitiveness in the job market.

## About the Speaker

Lin En has extensive experience in corporate management and consulting:

| Period | Experience |
| --- | --- |
| Jul 1991 – Dec 1995 | Consultant and project manager/director at SAP, involved in several short-term consulting projects |
| Dec 1995 – Jul 1997 | General Manager of a Sino-foreign joint-venture telecommunications equipment group |
| Jan 1997 – Jan 2007 | Senior manager, project director and consultant at IBM |

He is also a specially invited lecturer for Times Brightstone Management Training Network, Hong Kong Brightway Management Institute, and Qizhimingdao.
