Shantou University’s Human Resources Office opened recommendations for the 2014 domestic visiting-scholar programs for early- and mid-career university teachers. The university plans to recommend at least eight candidates. The programs include the Guangdong higher-education early- and mid-career faculty scheme and the Ministry of Education program for key young university teachers.

The Guangdong scheme primarily targets applicants with at least five years of teaching or research experience in higher education, a master’s degree or above, and an intermediate professional title or above who are no older than 40. Applicants with senior associate or higher professional titles may be up to 45. Detailed eligibility for the Ministry of Education program follows its implementation plan.

Candidates must submit the recommendation list and forms in both electronic and paper formats to Room 315 of the Administrative Center by April 16. Electronic materials should be sent to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. The Human Resources Office can be reached at 86502349. Admission and funding arrangements are scheduled to proceed between June and October.
