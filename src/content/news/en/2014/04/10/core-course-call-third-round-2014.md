Shantou University has opened its third call for core course proposals from academic units and teachers as it develops its general education curriculum. The university plans to build a more complete core-course system over two to three years. An expert group had previously approved 56 courses, which received appropriate university funding.

The system covers three fields and 13 course groups, and proposals must match the goals of a specific group. This round particularly welcomes courses in the “Citizenship and Society” field, Chinese intellectual history in “Culture and Values,” and quantitative reasoning and philosophy of science in the third field. Interdisciplinary courses on subjects such as energy and genetically modified organisms are also encouraged. Courses approved for the university’s core-course catalogue will receive a specified level of funding and must be offered every semester.

## Submission Arrangements

Applicants must submit a core-course application form and syllabus. One paper copy should go to the Teaching Division of the Academic Affairs Office, and an electronic copy should be sent to `zzhang@stu.edu.cn`. To allow courses to open in the summer and fall terms of the 2014–2015 academic year, materials are due by Monday, May 5, 2014. The core-course expert group will review proposals and provide feedback for revisions.
