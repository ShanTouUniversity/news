Shantou University launched the 2013–2014 selection for the Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Service Award. Established in 2008, the award recognizes teaching-support and administrative staff who have made notable contributions through their day-to-day service.

Relevant university units must submit candidate names and supporting materials by April 30 in accordance with the award’s guidelines and selection procedures. The contact person is Luo Feijun, who can be reached at 86502306 or `fjluo@stu.edu.cn`.
