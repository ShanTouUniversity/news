Shantou University’s School of Science will hold a mathematical modeling experience session from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on April 11 in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). Li Jian will moderate the session, with Zhang XY, Wang YJ and Bao MT sharing their experience as members of a team that won a national first prize in the 2013 National Undergraduate Mathematical Modeling Contest.

The session will cover team formation, competition participation, paper writing and preparation resources. It will also introduce the 2014 National Undergraduate Mathematical Modeling Contest, scheduled for September 12–15.
