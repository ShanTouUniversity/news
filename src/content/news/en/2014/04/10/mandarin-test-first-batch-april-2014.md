Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office relayed arrangements from the Mandarin training and testing center of Shantou Vocational and Technical College. First-batch candidates for the university’s 2014 spring Mandarin proficiency test will take the test in scheduled sessions on the afternoons of April 16 and 23. The exact session shown on each admission card must be followed.

The test will be held on the sixth floor of the Shantou Preschool Teachers College teaching building at 23 Jinyuan Road. Undergraduate candidates can collect their admission cards from their school or department teaching offices, while graduate candidates should collect them from the Graduate School. Candidates must bring both their admission card and identity card.

Because the university will not provide shuttle buses, candidates were advised to travel in groups and safeguard their personal belongings. Campus security can be reached at 82904110.
