Shantou University’s Academic Exchange Service Center will launch its 2014 spring discount group-meal service on April 16, replacing the previous discount-meal service. The service is available to units or projects with valid charge accounts and to faculty members and students who use valid campus cards.

Campus-card users must have sufficient funds on their cards. Cash payments are not accepted, and the cardholder must remain present throughout the meal. The center also reminds customers not to bring outside food or drinks.
