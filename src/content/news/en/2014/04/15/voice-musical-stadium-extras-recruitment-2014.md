It is reported that the production team of The Voice of China will conduct a two-day musical stadium shoot at Shantou University Stadium on April 19 and 20, and the Communist Youth League Committee is now recruiting 400 regular extras from across the student body to take part in the artistic production and enhance practical experience.

Participants are expected to demonstrate responsibility and punctuality, and to complete assigned tasks on time and in full as scheduled. Recruited extras will be assigned to performance slots by a student management team according to their availability.

In addition, 25 lucky participants will be drawn each day after filming to receive a group photo and autograph opportunity with cast members.

Interested students should submit their application by 24:00 on April 16 to zghsy_stu@163.com, with the email subject "Name + Extras Application" and the body including name, major, contact information, and available dates.
