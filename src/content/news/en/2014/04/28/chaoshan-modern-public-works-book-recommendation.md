Shantou University Library recommended a new title on Chaoshan literature on April 28, 2014, introducing a study of modern public works and social change in the region. The book examines the construction of the Chaoshan Railway, Han River regulation from 1921 to 1929, the development of Zhongshan Park and the Pingmin New Village in Shantou, tracing each project's origins, construction, financing and social effects.

Drawing on archives, periodicals, institutional records and local gazetteers, the authors discuss relations between state and society and between government and local communities. The book also considers public works' effects on transport, urban landscapes, recreation and housing relief. Chen Haizhong is a history PhD and associate professor; Huang Ting is a research fellow. Both study regional social history and related fields.
