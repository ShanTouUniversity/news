It is reported that the Business School issued a notice on April 28, 2014, announcing the participants for the *Campus Fruit Tree Protection in Action* public course.

A total of 74 applications were received by the deadline. Due to limited places, 40 students were selected. Questions may be directed to the contact email listed in the original OA notice. The full list is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here to protect privacy.

> ⚠️ The participant list and contact email are available on the OA system.
