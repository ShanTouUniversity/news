Shantou University Academic Affairs announced a schedule change for two sections of its advanced wind performance course on April 28, 2014. The A- and B-period classes originally scheduled for April 30 for course sections 67058 and 67033 were moved to the C period on May 7 and May 14, respectively.

Section 67058 will remain in Music Room 3 (STU203), and section 67033 in the wind ensemble rehearsal room (STU213).
