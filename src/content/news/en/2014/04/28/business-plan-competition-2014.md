Shantou University's Student Affairs entrepreneurship center and student venture park office announced the 2014 campus business plan contest on April 28. The contest will also select entries for the third “Win in Guangzhou” youth student entrepreneurship competition. Undergraduate and graduate students may enter in teams of up to 10.

Plans were due by May 8. A preliminary review would select seven entries for the final round on May 21, which would include project presentations and defenses. Awards comprised one first prize, two second prizes, two third prizes and two merit awards. The first-, second- and third-prize teams would represent the university in the Guangzhou competition and revise their plans by May 28 based on judges' feedback.
