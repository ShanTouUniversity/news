Shantou University's Party Committee Organization and United Front Department opened a campus call for papers on April 28, 2014, for the 14th Guangdong University United Front Theory Symposium. The symposium is scheduled for mid-to-late November at Shenzhen University. Topics include united front theory and practice, consultative democracy, grassroots work, ethnic and religious affairs, and the non-public sector.

Papers should generally be about 5,000 Chinese characters; research reports and substantial papers should normally not exceed 8,000. Submissions were due by August 31 by email to the university department, with authors asked to confirm receipt. The university planned to forward papers for review by September 10. The notice named Huang Shaobin as contact and listed the office number +86 754 8650 2341.
