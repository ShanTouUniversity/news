Shantou University's Student Community Management and Service Center outlined the process for obtaining dormitory air-conditioner remotes on April 28, 2014. Students can apply through the dormitory management system, print and sign the custody undertaking, and collect a remote from the service desk in their residence building.

Users are responsible for keeping the remote safe and returning it on time; loss, damage or late return may incur compensation under the applicable rules. Air-conditioner faults can be reported through the 24-hour hotline, 4008365365. Callers should provide their dormitory room number.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
> ⚠️ The application guide is available on the OA system.
