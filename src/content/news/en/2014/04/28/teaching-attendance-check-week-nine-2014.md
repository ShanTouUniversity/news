Shantou University's Office for the Development of Academic Conduct reported the ninth-week classroom checks for the 2013–14 spring term on April 28, 2014. Checks at Building E on the morning of April 23 and Building DG on the afternoon of April 25 each recorded six students arriving no more than five minutes late.

The original report said the student lists had been sent to the relevant colleges for follow-up. To protect student privacy, this article reports aggregate figures only and does not identify any students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office for the Development of Academic Conduct)
> ⚠️ The original inspection-record attachment is available on the OA system.
