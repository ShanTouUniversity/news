Shantou University's continuing education center for professional and technical personnel invited campus units to propose training courses for 2014 on April 28. Instructors could enter course information in the continuing education system themselves or ask their units to submit it on their behalf. Units were responsible for reviewing proposals and submitting them for university approval.

Course proposals were due by May 9, and each unit was also asked to email its course roster to the center. The notice named Chen Yingjie as the contact and provided the office number 86502458.
