Renovation of rooms in Shantou University's expert apartments began on April 9, 2014. During construction, the university's infrastructure office proposed additional work to meet practical needs, including plywood storage cabinets, engineered-stone washbasins, wall plastering, electrical wiring, shower mixer taps and aluminum-framed glass partitions. The preliminary estimate for the additions was about RMB 11,000, below the university's 10 percent limit.

The university published the estimate for public feedback on April 28. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision, Audit and Inspection Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on April 30.
