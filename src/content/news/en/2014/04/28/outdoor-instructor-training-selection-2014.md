Shantou University Student Affairs announced the results of the second outdoor instructor training course selection on April 28, 2014. Following assessments of physical fitness and an outdoor-knowledge presentation, 20 applicants were selected.

The original notice said details for selected participants would follow separately. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce names or personal phone numbers.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs)
> ⚠️ The full list and subsequent arrangements are available on the OA system.
