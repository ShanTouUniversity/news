Shantou University’s Student Entrepreneurship Center and Entrepreneurship Park Management Office will open the Entrepreneurship Youth Forum with a session from 3 to 5 p.m. on April 16 at the Library Lecture Hall. The session is titled “Five Practices for Sharing Undergraduate Entrepreneurship Experiences.”

Han Rongkui, founder of Shantou Sunshine Network Co., Ltd. and an STU alumnus, will share his entrepreneurial experience and answer students’ questions. Students interested in attending may register at `https://booking.stu.edu.cn/` from 6 p.m. on April 11 to 5:30 p.m. on April 14.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Entrepreneurship Center and Entrepreneurship Park Management Office)
