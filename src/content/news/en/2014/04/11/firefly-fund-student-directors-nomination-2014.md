The Shantou University Youth Volunteers Firefly Fund interviewed candidates for student representative seats on March 29. Based on candidates’ overall qualifications and interview performance, the fund proposed appointing three students to its board. Their names and class details are omitted to protect student privacy.

Comments may be submitted in writing or by phone to the University Youth League Committee by 5 p.m. on April 15. The official contact number is `86502302`, and the email address is `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
