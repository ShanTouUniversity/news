The 180th Shantou University Engineering Research Forum is scheduled for 3 p.m. on April 14. Its topic, “Why Design for Test (DFT) and Where It Goes,” will be presented by Dr. Lai Liyang, a researcher at U.S.-based Calypto Design Systems.

The lecture will take place in the Engineering Academic Exchange Room (Gongnan 312) and is open to faculty and students interested in the subject.
