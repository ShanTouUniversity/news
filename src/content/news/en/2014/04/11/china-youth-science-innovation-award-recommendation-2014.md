The Communist Youth League of Shantou University circulated arrangements for recommending candidates for the ninth China Youth Science Innovation Award. The award is organized by the Communist Youth League of China, All-China Youth Federation, All-China Students’ Federation and China Young Pioneers, as part of educational activities marking the centenary of Deng Xiaoping’s birth and encouraging innovation among young people.

Youth League committees of the university’s schools and residential colleges were asked to organize recommendations and submit two copies of the application materials to the University Youth League Committee by April 14. Electronic copies should be sent to `o_tw@stu.edu.cn`. Detailed requirements and application materials are set out in the relevant documents.
