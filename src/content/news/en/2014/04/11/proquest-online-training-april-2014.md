Shantou University Library announced two April ProQuest webinars for faculty and students interested in foreign-language electronic resources. Both sessions will be held live online, require no registration and allow participants to interact with trainers and other attendees.

| Date and time | Topic | Resource |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April 17, Thursday, 3:30–4:30 p.m. | Accessing academic literature across disciplines worldwide | ProQuest Research Library |
| April 28, Monday, 3:30–4:30 p.m. | Efficiently searching and using e-books | ebrary |

The library recommended logging in early to test equipment; first-time users may need additional time for setup. A headset or speakers are needed for audio. Join through the links for the [April 17 session](https://proquestmeetings.webex.com/proquestmeetings/j.php?MTID=mcf997b78a8eac7383dc08e5678268fc9) and [April 28 session](https://proquestmeetings.webex.com/proquestmeetings/j.php?MTID=mf4beec6263fa31c189d300ecf3b38e10).
