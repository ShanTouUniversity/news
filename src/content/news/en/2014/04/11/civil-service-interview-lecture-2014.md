Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication is scheduled to hold a lecture on civil service interview skills at Shantou University Library’s lecture hall on the morning of April 12. The speaker will be alumnus Li Yubo, who graduated in history in 1988 and was then serving as a member of the Standing Committee of the CPC Doumen District Committee and secretary of its Political and Legal Affairs Commission in Zhuhai. He had also served as an interviewer for local civil service recruitment.

The session covered preparation for provincial and municipal civil service exams, interview responses, and ways to analyze and address current affairs and national policies. It aimed to help students interested in civil service careers understand the requirements and prepare effectively.
