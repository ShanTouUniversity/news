It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on April 11, 2014, announcing the shortlist for the Student Community X-Project: Village Culture Exploration in Shantou.

By the deadline of 12:00 on April 4, 16 teams had applied. After careful evaluation of overall performance, 10 teams including “Apricot Grove Fellows” were shortlisted.

Shortlisted teams are asked to prepare for the next phase according to the activity schedule. The full list is available in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The shortlist is available on the OA system.
