Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced utility charges for faculty housing for March 2014. Meters were read on April 1 and 2, following the previous reading on March 1. Residents can log in to the university’s utility management system to review their individual charges.

Residents with questions may visit the Water and Electricity Center from April 14 to 16 for verification. Charges not disputed by the deadline will be deducted by the Finance Office from April salary accounts. The center can be reached at 2489.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
