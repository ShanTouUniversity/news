Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the call for the 18th An Zijie International Trade Research Awards. The awards are administered and reviewed by the University of International Business and Economics to promote research in international trade. Categories include outstanding book, outstanding paper and academic encouragement awards.

The book and paper categories each offer first-, second- and third-place prizes: RMB 10,000, 8,000 and 6,000 for books, and RMB 6,000, 5,000 and 4,000 for papers. The academic encouragement award carries RMB 2,000 without ranking. Eligible books and papers must have been formally published between May 2012 and April 2014. The encouragement category is for senior undergraduates and graduate students nominated by their schools and university.

Online applications close at 5 p.m. on May 20, 2014. Printed materials must be mailed by May 23, based on the local postmark. Applications are available through the [University of International Business and Economics award site](http://kyc.uibe.edu.cn/zm). Applicants should consult the organizer’s notice for full requirements.
