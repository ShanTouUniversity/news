Shantou University’s Youth League Committee launched its 2013 selection of outstanding Youth League branches, cadres and members, with awards planned for the May Fourth period. The selection assesses organizational work, member conduct and cadre performance, with criteria covering work evaluations, academic performance and disciplinary records.

Units may nominate candidates according to quotas based on the number of branches, registered members and cadres. Relevant Youth League organizations review and recommend branch, member and cadre candidates; graduate students and the affiliated hospital may organize their selections under the notice. Nominated groups and individuals must be reviewed and publicized by their units.

Application forms and supporting materials are due at the University Youth League Committee office by April 21. The committee will review nominations and announce the honorees during the May Fourth period. Student identities are not disclosed in this report.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
