Shantou University will hold a meeting on graduate education innovation from 3 to 4:30 p.m. on April 17 at the Science Building lecture hall. Participants include presidents of schools, heads of departments and institutes, leaders responsible for graduate affairs, department chairs, graduate teaching secretaries and all graduate supervisors from the university’s main campus.

The arrangement was issued jointly by the General Office and Graduate School. Relevant graduate-training units are expected to organize attendance.
