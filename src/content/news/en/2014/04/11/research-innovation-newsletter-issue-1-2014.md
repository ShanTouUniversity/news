Shantou University’s Research Office published Issue 1 of the 2014 *Research and Innovation Newsletter* (No. 31 overall), reviewing recent research and academic activities at the university.

The issue covers project applications, humanities and social sciences projects, an agricultural science and technology commercialization project that passed an on-site review, and research achievements and awards involving teams from the Medical School and School of Science. It also highlights the first higher-education research exchange among Shenzhen University, Guangzhou University and STU, the ninth international conference of the Association for Asian Studies, and academic exchanges with scholars from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Sun Yat-sen University, Soochow University, City University of Hong Kong and Peking University.

Other items include a university-industry cooperation discussion with an alumnus-founded company and the inclusion of *Chinese Literature* in the expanded CSSCI source-journal list for 2014–2015.
