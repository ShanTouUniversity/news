Results of the 2014 Mathematical Contest in Modeling were recently announced, with all three Shantou University teams receiving Honorable Mentions. Each team comprised three students and was advised by Li Jian.

This was STU’s second participation, following its debut in 2013. The competition is organized by the Consortium for Mathematics and Its Applications and the Mathematical Association of America. Teams have 96 hours to complete a paper involving mathematical modeling and computer programming. Student names and personal details are omitted to protect their privacy.
