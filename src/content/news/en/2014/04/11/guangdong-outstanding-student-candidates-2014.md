The Communist Youth League of Shantou University publicized its proposed candidates for the 2013–2014 Guangdong honors for outstanding student leaders, student unions and student organizations. The university nominated Zhao ZX of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Yang ZF of the Medical School for Outstanding Student Leader, the STU Student Union for Outstanding Student Union, and the Medical School Medical Poverty-Relief Volunteer Team and Psychology Growth Association for Outstanding Student Organization.

Comments on the proposed nominations will be accepted until 5 p.m. on April 14. Organizations and individuals may contact the University Youth League Committee by letter, in person or by telephone at 86502302. Student names are presented in abbreviated form in accordance with privacy requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
