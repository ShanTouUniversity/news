Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office opened recommendations for members of the second batch of Guangdong higher-education undergraduate teaching committees. The process seeks university, industry and business experts to support teaching reform, consultation and guidance in Guangdong higher education.

Experts in higher education should generally hold an associate-senior or higher professional title, or an equivalent administrative position. Candidates for chair or vice-chair should hold a full senior professional title. Committee candidates should generally be current university employees under 55; industry and business experts should have relevant professional knowledge, industry experience and an understanding of university teaching and talent development.

Each unit may generally recommend one university expert and one industry or business expert for the same discipline committee, with current members of Ministry of Education teaching committees receiving priority. Recommendation forms, signed by the unit’s teaching leader and bearing its official seal, are due to the Academic Affairs Office General Section by April 15. Electronic copies should be sent to `o_jwc@stu.edu.cn`; inquiries can be directed to 86502406.
