Shantou University launched its fourth student club incubation program to provide a pathway for establishing and developing new clubs. Under the theme “United in Youth, Creating Campus Clubs Together,” the program is open to all students and will take place on campus.

Applications and reviews are scheduled for April 11–16, a showcase for new clubs for April 19, and club defenses for April 23. Workshops and submission of complete documentation are both scheduled for April 30. Newly established clubs that complete the program may receive up to RMB 600 in initial activity funding. Applications should be emailed to `13hmchen@stu.edu.cn` by midnight on April 16.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
