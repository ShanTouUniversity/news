Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the call for the fifth Qianduan Sheng Law Research Awards. Initiated by China University of Political Science and Law, the national award commemorates Qianduan Sheng and aims to advance legal scholarship and rule-of-law development. Its fifth cycle is being held in 2014.

The awards include three first prizes of RMB 80,000 each, 15 second prizes of RMB 20,000 each, 30 third prizes of RMB 10,000 each, and 20 nomination awards. Eligible applicants include university teachers, researchers at legal research institutions, and legal practitioners in legislative, administrative, judicial and legal-service organizations. Eligible work includes scholarly books, academic papers and research reports adopted by provincial- or ministerial-level government bodies. Books and papers must have been published between January 1, 2002, and December 31, 2011.

Applications are accepted from April 1 through May 31. Universities are to coordinate submissions by their faculty; other eligible applicants should follow the organizer’s procedures. Consult the original call for application forms and documentation requirements.
