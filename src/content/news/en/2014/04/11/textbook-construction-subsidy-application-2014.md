Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office opened applications and review for its first-half 2014 textbook development and publishing subsidy program. The program is intended for eligible applicants planning to publish textbooks during the first half of the year.

Applicants must prepare the application form and supporting materials in accordance with the university’s textbook development and publishing subsidy regulations. Three paper copies are due to the Academic Affairs service counter on the third floor of the New Administration Building by May 13. Electronic copies should be sent to `liz@stu.edu.cn`. The office can be reached at 86502736; the contact person is Li Zhe.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
