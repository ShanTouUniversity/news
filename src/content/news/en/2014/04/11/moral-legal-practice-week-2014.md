It is reported that the Department of Social Sciences will hold a “Moral and Legal Practice Week” to deepen teaching reform for the *Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics* course and strengthen students’ ability to apply theory to practice.

## Schedule

| Section | Item | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moral Practice Week | Participants | Students taking *Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics* and *Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics (Moral)* this semester |
|  | Time/Venue | Week 9, on campus |
|  | Content | “Five Ones”: do one good deed, correct one violation, copy one maxim, write one reflection, and join one discussion |
|  | Arrangements | Briefing in week 8; practice in week 9 and a reflection of at least 800 words due in week 10; instructors recommend outstanding participants for commendation |
| Legal Practice Week | Participants | Students taking *Moral Cultivation and Legal Basics (Legal)* |
|  | Time/Venue | Week 9, on campus |
|  | Content | Each student finds a real-life case involving legal issues, compiles it in writing and submits in week 10; instructors recommend outstanding participants for commendation |
