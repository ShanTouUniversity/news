Shantou University’s Dawn Aid team will run its third May Day volunteer teaching camp from May 1 to 3 at Yingfeng Primary School in Yingfeng Village, Feng塘 Town, Chaozhou. Under the theme “Journeying with Dreams,” the camp will recruit 20 student volunteers from across the university. Planned activities include hands-on classes, everyday-life lessons, engaging subject activities and critical-thinking training.

Interviews will take place from 7:30 to 10:30 p.m. on April 14 and 15 in Room 726 of Building 789. Applications close at noon on April 15. Students may download the form from the campus office information network and send it to the designated email address, or register at the Triangle Plaza consultation desk on April 12–13.

The organizers seek volunteers who are committed to public service, responsible, patient, resilient and able to work well with others. Personal student contacts are omitted to protect privacy.
