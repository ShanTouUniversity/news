Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office reported three cases of late proctoring during final examinations in the fall 2013 semester. The exams took place on January 7, 2014, for Marxist Fundamentals, Youth Psychology and Microeconomics in rooms E207, E407 and E401, respectively.

After reviewing investigation materials submitted by the relevant schools, a panel comprising members of the university’s Teaching Quality and Evaluation Subcommittee, representatives of the units involved and Academic Affairs Office representatives met on March 12. The panel determined by discussion and vote that the conduct met the university’s definition of an ordinary teaching violation: a proctor arriving more than 10 minutes late without force majeure. The responsible parties received a university-wide criticism.
