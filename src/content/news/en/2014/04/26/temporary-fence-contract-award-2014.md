Following review by Shantou University's tendering and bidding leading group, the university announced the result of its temporary wood-plastic composite fence procurement and installation project on April 26, 2014. Shantou Longhua Construction Co. was selected, with a bid of RMB 39,820.

The result was open for public review from April 26 to 28. Objections could be submitted to the university's Supervision, Audit and Inspection Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
