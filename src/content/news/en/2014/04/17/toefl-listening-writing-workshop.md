Shantou University's English Language Center will hold a TOEFL iBT listening and writing workshop on Wednesday, April 23, from 6:30 to 9:30 p.m. at CILL. The session will be led by Gena Rhoades and Kristine Adams.

The workshop will offer strategies for preparing for the listening and writing sections of the TOEFL iBT. Seats are limited, and participants must reserve through the event booking page. Those who register should bring their admission ticket.
