It is reported that the Party Building and Ideological and Political Work Research Association of Shantou University issued a notice on April 17, 2014, to launch a call for papers for its 2014 annual meeting.

To implement the spirit of the 18th CPC National Congress, study General Secretary Xi Jinping’s discourses on carrying forward fine traditional Chinese culture, and foster core socialist values, the council decided the meeting will be themed “Carrying Forward Fine Traditional Culture and Cultivating Core Values of the Times.” Schools, departments and Party organizations are asked to mobilize faculty and staff to conduct research on the theme, and the Student Affairs Office and the Graduate School are asked to involve undergraduate, master’s and doctoral students, with papers signed by Party branches or authors.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Carrying Forward Fine Traditional Culture and Cultivating Core Values of the Times |
| Title | Self-selected |
| Format | Follow the submission guidelines of the *Journal of Shantou University (Humanities and Social Sciences Edition)* (see OA attachment) |
| Deadline | September 30, 2014 |
| Submission | WORD file to o_xcb@stu.edu.cn, with three printed copies to the Publicity Section, Publicity Department, New Administration Center |
| Review and publication | Outstanding papers will be selected by experts and eligible papers will be published as a collection |
| Issued by | Publicity Department of the Party Committee |
