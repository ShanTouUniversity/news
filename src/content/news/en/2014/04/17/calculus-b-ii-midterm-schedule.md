Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has released the midterm schedule for Calculus B-II. The exam will take place during periods 3 and 4 on Wednesday, April 23, the ninth week of the semester, for four class groups numbered 67337 through 67340.

| Class | Examination room | Students |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 67337 | Lecture Hall 5 | 122 |
| 67338 | Block D, Rooms 503 and 504 | 118 |
| 67339 | E Lecture Hall 102 | 112 |
| 67340 | Lecture Hall 1 | 118 |

Students must sit in alternate seats in their assigned rooms. Students with approved audit exemptions are also required to attend. See the original OA notice for instructors and regular class locations.
