Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has released the midterm schedule for Calculus C-II. The exam will take place during period 12 on Thursday, April 24, the ninth week of the semester, for four class groups numbered 67603 through 67606.

| Class | Examination room | Students |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 67603 | Lecture Hall 1 | 116 |
| 67604 | Lecture Hall 3 | 97 |
| 67605 | Lecture Hall 2 | 106 |
| 67606 | E Lecture Hall 101 | 105 |

Students must sit in alternate seats in their assigned rooms. Students with approved audit exemptions are also required to attend. See the original OA notice for instructors and regular class locations.
