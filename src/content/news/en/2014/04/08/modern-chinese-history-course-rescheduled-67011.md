The class schedule for Shantou University’s class 67011 in *Outline of Modern Chinese History*, offered by the Department of Social Sciences, has changed. The class scheduled for periods 3–4 on Wednesday, April 9 (Week 7) was canceled and rescheduled for periods 3–4 on Saturday, April 19 (Week 8), in Room E401.
