Shantou University has opened its 2014 Li Ka Shing Foundation Teaching Excellence Award selection. Established in 2003, the award recognizes faculty members who combine teaching and research. The main campus will select one recipient in science and engineering and one in the humanities; the Medical College conducts its own selection.

The 2014 selection committee is chaired by Lin Danming, with Deng Jianguang, Bao Nengsheng, Yin Yegao, Cheng Jiaming, Ni Qingqing and Li Yuejin as members. Relevant units must submit candidate names and supporting materials by April 28 in accordance with the award’s selection guidelines.
