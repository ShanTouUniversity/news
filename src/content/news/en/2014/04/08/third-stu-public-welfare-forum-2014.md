Shantou University will hold its third Public Welfare Forum in the Library Lecture Hall on the evening of April 12, 2014. The forum is organized by the university’s Youth League Committee and China National Radio’s Huaxia Voice, and co-organized by the STU Youth Volunteer Association and the radio program *Public Welfare China*. The event is being held in conjunction with the association’s 20th anniversary.

Speakers include Wu Yaohua (Huashan), social responsibility manager at Alibaba Group and initiator of Alipay’s public welfare platform, and Yu Zhihai (Anzhu), founder of the “Aiju” public welfare initiative and the “One Backpack” project. Talks will address public welfare innovation in internet companies, whether public welfare needs design, and how individuals can contribute. A roundtable will follow on public welfare and social innovation.
