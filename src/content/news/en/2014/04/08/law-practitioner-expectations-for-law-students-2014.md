Shantou University’s Law School will hold the 272nd lecture in its series on the evening of April 10, 2014. Chen Xikang, a partner at Guangdong Chen Liang Yongju Law Firm, will speak on “What a Legal Practitioner Expects of Law Students.”

Chen graduated from Peking University in 1992 with a bachelor’s degree in international law and qualified as a practicing lawyer in China in 1993. His practice covers corporate law and commercial arbitration. He is a council member of the All China Lawyers Association, vice president of the Guangdong Lawyers Association, and an arbitrator at several arbitration commissions. He also holds positions at university law schools.
