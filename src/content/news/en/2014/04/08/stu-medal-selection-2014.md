Shantou University has opened its 2014 University Medal selection. Established by the Li Ka Shing Foundation in 2002, the award is presented during the annual graduation ceremony to recognize outstanding graduates. One medal recipient and six finalists are planned for this year.

Evaluation will consider academic performance, contributions to extracurricular activities, judgment and creativity, and engagement with public affairs. Applicants must submit an online application by 4:00 p.m. on April 16 and provide materials including a personal statement, recommendation letters and copies of award certificates. Each school or college may nominate up to four graduating undergraduate students and must submit its candidates and supporting materials to the Student Financial Aid Center by 4:00 p.m. on April 21. Electronic materials may be sent to xyli@stu.edu.cn.

Graduating undergraduates at Zhicheng College must apply directly to their college. A university committee will review the candidates nominated by the schools and colleges.
