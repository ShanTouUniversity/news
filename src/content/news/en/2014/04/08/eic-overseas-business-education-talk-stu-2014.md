EIC Education’s campus tour at Shantou University is scheduled for the afternoon of April 9, 2014, in Lecture Hall E101. The two talks will introduce business education in the UK and student life at U.S. business schools. Speakers include Aaron Hobday, a PhD graduate of the University of Manchester, and Jie Zang, an MBA graduate of Northeastern University. The event will cover overseas business-school admissions, application planning and study experiences.

EIC also plans to hold an international university admissions fair in Shantou on April 12, from 2:00 to 6:00 p.m., at the Dijing Hall of the Dihao Hotel in Longhu District. Reservations can be made by calling 0754-88818312.
