Shantou University has opened its 2014 Sports Spirit Medal selection for graduating students recognized for sportsmanship, integrity in sport and contributions to university athletics. The award was established by the Li Ka Shing Foundation to recognize well-rounded graduates.

One medal recipient and two finalists will be selected. The recipient will receive RMB 8,000 and be honored during the graduation ceremony; each finalist will receive a certificate and RMB 1,000. Evaluation criteria include sportsmanship, leadership and teamwork, contributions to university sports, and academic performance.

Applicants must submit an online application through the scholarship website (http://sholarship.stu.edu.cn) by 4:00 p.m. on April 16, 2014, and deliver printed materials to the Student Affairs Office’s Student Financial Aid Center in Room 106 of the Administration Building. Required materials include an application form, a personal statement and two recommendation letters.
