Shantou University’s Office of International Exchange and Cooperation is continuing its Taiwan study program for the 2014–15 academic year. Eligible first- and second-year students, excluding those in medical programs, may apply to study at Chinese Culture University, I-Shou University or Chaoyang University of Technology from September 2014 to June 2015, or for one semester.

Students are responsible for tuition, accommodation, living expenses and round-trip travel. Chinese Culture University has eight places for the School of Journalism and Communication and two for each other school; I-Shou University and Chaoyang University of Technology have no enrollment quota. Credits earned abroad can be transferred after students return, and courses that meet their STU program requirements may be considered for substitution.

Students must submit applications to their school’s academic office by April 22. Schools are to complete nominations by April 25 and send the list to the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation by 5:00 p.m. that day at icd@stu.edu.cn.
