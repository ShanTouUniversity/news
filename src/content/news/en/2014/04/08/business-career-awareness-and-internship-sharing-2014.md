Shantou University’s Business School held sessions in its “Your Career, Your Choice” series on the morning of April 8, 2014, in Lecture Hall E101. The program was intended for all fourth-year students and also welcomed students from other years.

The event included a lecture on career awareness and decision-making, followed by an internship-experience session. Business School instructor Su Bingdian discussed analyzing industries, companies and roles, as well as making choices among career options. Graduates shared advice on resume writing, applying online, using professional networks to find internships, and workplace conduct during internships.
