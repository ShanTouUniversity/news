It is reported that the Student Affairs Office and the Residential Community Tutors issued a notice on April 8, 2014, to hold the X-Project Vibrant Community series.

## Schedule

| Activity | Theme | Time | Venue | Registration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AB & EF Community | Our Birthday Party — Creative Cake Making | 19:00–21:30, April 12 | Activity Room, Building A | By dorm, email to designated address before 24:00, April 10 (withheld for privacy) |
| CD & G Community | Millionaire Season 2 — Quiz Challenge | 19:30–21:30, April 13 | Activity Room, Building D | Register with community tutors or via SMS to designated number before 24:00, April 10 (withheld) |

Each dorm should have at least two participants, with random grouping. Participants are limited to the respective residential communities, with lottery if oversubscribed.

> ⚠️ Registration contacts are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
