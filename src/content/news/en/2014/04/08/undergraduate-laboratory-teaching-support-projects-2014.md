Shantou University has opened its 2014 Undergraduate Laboratory Teaching Support and Enhancement project call. Special funding will support units in improving laboratory teaching conditions, strengthening laboratory development and enhancing teaching quality.

Projects fall into three categories: support and enhancement of undergraduate laboratory teaching, including equipment purchases; laboratory environment development and renovation; and unit-proposed special projects. Units may select a category based on their laboratory development plans. Applications should generally be submitted by departments, or by schools where a departmental application is unsuitable.

Applications, including electronic files and two printed copies, must be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office by April 25, 2014. Electronic submissions may be sent to contact Chen Yangzhou at yzchen@stu.edu.cn; the office can also be reached at 86503202.
