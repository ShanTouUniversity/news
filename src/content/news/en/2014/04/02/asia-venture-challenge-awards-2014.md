Shantou University’s H-TWC team traveled to Thailand from March 11 to 13, 2014, to compete in the Asia Venture Challenge. The team won the Social Impact Award by John E. Butler and the Best Written Business Plan award.

## Project and competition

The team consisted of students from the Business School and the School of Engineering and was advised by Associate Professor Wu Jingqiang. Its project focused on a three-way catalytic converter, using an improved process to enhance performance and reduce production costs while addressing air pollution through more effective vehicle-emission purification.

Hosted by Thammasat University in Thailand, the Asia Venture Challenge brought semifinalists from China, Thailand, India, the Philippines, Singapore, Hong Kong and Taiwan. During the competition, the STU team presented its project through a booth display, a short elevator pitch and a business-plan presentation, then refined its presentation materials in response to judges’ feedback.

The competition also gave the team opportunities to exchange ideas with students from universities across Asia and broaden its international perspective. Student names are omitted from this report to protect privacy.
