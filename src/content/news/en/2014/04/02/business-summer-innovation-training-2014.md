Shantou University Business School is recruiting its students for the 2014 Summer Innovation Training Program research class. Led by faculty advisers, the program will take students to companies in Taiwan for visits and training, supporting preliminary research for innovation and entrepreneurship projects.

## Program details

Participants will choose one topic from cross-Strait retail markets, service differentiation, public-interest and commercial models, tourism development, logistics operations or traditional industry models. After completing their research and submitting a report, students in the 2012 cohort may count the project toward the Business Practice course, while students in the 2013 cohort may count it toward Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training, worth four credits. Teams may also apply for university or provincial undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training programs, with approved projects eligible for up to RMB 10,000 in funding.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Business School students |
| Planned intake | 25 students |
| Application period | Through April 18 |
| Estimated cost | About RMB 5,500 per student, paid individually; undergraduate students may receive an appropriate school subsidy |
| Information session | 2 p.m., Wednesday, April 9, 2nd-floor meeting room, Wenxi Building, Business School |
| Application location | Wenxi 211, Business School International Exchange Office |

Applicants must submit the application form and pay the entry-permit fee as required. The itinerary and application attachments should be checked in the original OA notice; inquiries may be directed to the Business School International Exchange Office.
