Following deliberation by the Standing Committee of the Party Committee of Shantou University and a university leadership meeting, Li Wanli no longer serves as deputy director of the university’s Academic Affairs Office. The decision was issued on April 2, 2014.

The decision was reviewed and approved at the third meeting of the 2014 Standing Committee of the University Party Committee and the fourth 2014 university leadership meeting. It was issued by the Communist Party Committee of Shantou University.
