Shantou University Business School has announced arrangements for students to enter the 20th Kang Teng National University Business Case Analysis Competition. Authorized by the Chinese Economists Annual Meeting, the competition is open to university students from across the mainland, Hong Kong and Macao, and promotes the application of theory through business case analysis. The national competition began on March 23, 2014.

## Schedule and registration

The competition consists of preliminary, semifinal and final stages. The organizers will select 13 teams for the semifinals and then seven teams for the national final.

| Stage | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | Late March to April 7 |
| Preliminary case submission deadline | 10 p.m., April 9 |
| Preliminary selection | April 11 |
| Semifinal case submissions | April 19 and April 21 |
| National semifinal | April 23 |
| National final | April 25 |

Each team may have up to five members, including no more than one graduate student; the other members must be undergraduates. Teams may be formed across departments and majors but not across universities, and may invite one faculty adviser. The preliminary submission must include a business case analysis report, while research videos and photographs may be submitted where required.

The Business School asked interested students to consult the competition website for detailed requirements and submit materials on time. Registration attachments contain student information and are available in the original OA notice.
