Shantou University’s Guangdong-Taiwan Business Cooperation Institute has appointed Hu Shaodong as deputy director to strengthen institute management and prepare for the evaluation of its status as a key humanities and social sciences research base among Guangdong higher-education institutions. The appointment was issued by the Business School and the institute on April 2, 2014.

In the role, Hu Shaodong will assist the institute director in implementing the base-development tasks set by the education authorities, advancing the academic development plan set by the research base’s academic committee, improving internal management rules and handling daily management, inspections and evaluations.
