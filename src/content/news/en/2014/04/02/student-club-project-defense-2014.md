Shantou University held the defense session for spring 2013–2014 student club project applications in Room D202 on the afternoon of April 2, 2014. Ten clubs, including the Roller Skating Association, took part. The session was organized by the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Student Clubs Federation.

Project representatives signed in by 2 p.m., ahead of the 2:30–5 p.m. defense session. During the session, representatives answered judges’ questions and remained until the total scores were announced. Organizers asked participants to dress neatly, maintain a positive professional demeanor and prepare carefully.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and Shantou University Student Clubs Federation)
