Shantou University Business School has established an Institute of Business Anthropology, with Professor Tian Guang serving as its director and relevant faculty members forming its staff. The decision was considered by the Business School’s administrative committee on February 26, 2014, and issued on April 2.

The institute will serve as a platform for related teaching and research at the Business School, with its staffing provided by relevant faculty members.
