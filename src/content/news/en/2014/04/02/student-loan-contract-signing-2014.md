Shantou University will hold the contract signing for the second batch of 2013–2014 national student loans at 2:30 p.m. on Wednesday, April 9, 2014, in Lecture Hall E102, the moot court. The signing will be handled jointly by Bank of China Shantou Branch and the relevant university offices.

Participating students must bring their identity cards, student cards, Bank of China debit cards and black pens. Witnessing teachers from each school or residential college should also attend on time to witness the signing for their students.

The signing list and detailed document requirements contain personal information and should be checked in the attachments to the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The signing list and related attachments are available on the OA system.
