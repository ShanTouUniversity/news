Shantou University's Youth Volunteer Firefly Love Fund opened applications for its 2014 Xuehai Yingguang grant on April 30. The program is open to students across the university, with up to 20 recipients to receive RMB 500 each.

Applicants should face financial hardship, live modestly, have strong academic and personal conduct records, and actively participate in campus volunteer service. Priority is given to students who received no other scholarships or grants during the 2013–2014 academic year. Applications require both electronic and paper materials and are due by 11 p.m. on May 5. The OA attachments contain the application forms, detailed material requirements and submission locations; student names, dormitory details and personal contact information are not reproduced here.
