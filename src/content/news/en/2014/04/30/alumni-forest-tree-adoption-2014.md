Shantou University's Alumni Affairs Office announced the launch of the Alumni Forest tree adoption program on April 30, 2014, inviting alumni to support campus greening on a voluntary basis.

The Alumni Forest is located west of the new campus gate and south of the library, inside the perimeter wall. The available trees include golden trumpet trees and red trumpet trees. Depending on size and species, tree prices range from RMB 400 to RMB 11,000 per tree, with an additional RMB 400–800 per tree for planting and maintenance. Adopters may inspect the trees, sign an adoption payment form with the Alumni Affairs Office and make payment; the university will produce and install the plaques, while its landscaping department will maintain the trees.

Adoption payments will be managed in a dedicated Alumni Forest greening account and used for planting and maintenance. The Alumni Affairs Office will issue certificates to adopters. The payment form and complete procedures are available in the original OA attachment.
