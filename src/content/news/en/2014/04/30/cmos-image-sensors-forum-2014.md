The 183rd Engineering Research Forum of Shantou University’s Engineering School will be held from 4:00 to 5:20 p.m. on May 4 in the Academic Exchange Room, Gongnan Building 312.

The forum will focus on “Developments of CMOS Image Sensors.” The speaker will be Professor Guoneng Lu of the Department of Electronic Engineering at the University of Lyon 1 in France. Faculty members, graduate students, senior undergraduates in electronic information engineering and physics, and other interested members of the university community are welcome.
