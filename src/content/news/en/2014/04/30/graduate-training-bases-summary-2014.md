Shantou University's Graduate School issued a notice on April 30, 2014, asking the university's three approved Guangdong joint graduate training demonstration bases to submit construction reports. The work follows Guangdong's provincial degree authority notice Yue Xuewei Ban [2014] No. 14.

The reports should cover the bases' overall joint training of graduate students over the past three years, achievements, operating experience, challenges and next steps. They should emphasize innovative and transferable mechanisms in the bases' construction and operation. The three bases involve mechanical and electronic engineering plus mechanical design and theory; marine biology plus environmental science; and internal medicine plus obstetrics and gynecology. Partner organizations include Shantou Light Industry Equipment Research Institute, Tuo-lian Subdistrict in Jinping District, North Guangdong People's Hospital and Shantou Central Hospital.

The bases must submit reports to the Graduate School by May 13 and send electronic copies to `xwb@stu.edu.cn`. Formatting requirements are provided in the original OA attachment.
