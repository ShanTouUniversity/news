Shantou University’s tendering and bidding leading group completed its review of the Foundation Office exterior wall repair project and announced the result on April 30. Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. won the contract for RMB 20,720.75, including RMB 986.70 in contingencies.

The result will be open for public notice from May 1 to 4. The tendering party is Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office, which can be reached at 86503890 or 86502890, with fax number 86500511. Complaints may be submitted to the university’s Supervision and Audit Office at 86502346, with fax number 86500536.
