Shantou University's Career Connections series will take place from 2:30 to 5 p.m. on May 10, 2014, on the second floor of the New Administration Building. The event is open to students across the university and is designed to connect them with business leaders and other professionals.

The organizers planned to select 48 participants. The program includes résumé reviews by guests and small-group discussions in eight groups of six, each with a business manager or STU graduate sharing workplace experience and discussing résumés. Informal networking and industry-focused questions are also planned. Applicants must submit a registration form and an electronic résumé; guests will screen applications and select participants. Guest biographies and the registration form are available in the OA attachments; student names and personal contact information are not reproduced here.
