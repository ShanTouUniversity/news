It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on April 9, 2014, that the demolition of 72 dormitory units in Zone B of the Residential College (Phase I) site will be carried out that afternoon.

## Progress

Since the demolition of old buildings on the site began on March 17, all contracted demolition tasks except for the 72 units were completed by April 5 with the support of university management departments and nearby residents. The relocation of power distribution facilities on the ground floor of the 72 units was completed on April 8 and handed over to Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd., with the final demolition scheduled for the afternoon of April 9.

## Demolition Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 14:45–16:00, April 9, 2014 |
| Direction | Buildings will topple to the north, with a momentary collapse danger |
| Traffic control | Campus roads near the 72 units in the West Zone will be closed during the demolition; vehicles are prohibited, and pedestrians should detour through the West Zone dormitories |
| Dust control | The construction team will spray water to reduce dust, but the collapse will still cause strong vibration and significant dust; residents nearby are advised to close windows and doors |
| Contractor | Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Issued by | Resource Management Office |
