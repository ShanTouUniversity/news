Shantou University’s English Language Center has introduced the “How Do I Improve My English?” consultation service, known as the HDIIME room, to help students plan their own English learning. Teacher consultants meet students one-on-one to identify specific goals, discuss suitable materials, decide how often to study and plan how to manage their time. They also provide encouragement, suggestions and ongoing support during the learning process.

## Service Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Room 6, the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL) |
| Availability | Tuesday through Friday |
| Booking | MySTU CILL Booking System: https://my.stu.edu.cn/courses/elc/mod/page/view.php?id=20537 |
| Appointment length | 30 minutes or one hour |
| Eligibility | STU students seeking to improve their English beyond class, regardless of level or enrollment in an English course |

HDIIME is intended to develop practical, real-life language skills rather than meet course requirements. Consultants will not sign extra-credit forms, and students should not request such signatures.
