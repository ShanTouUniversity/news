Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold its second IELTS preparation workshop on listening on April 16. The session will focus on developing IELTS listening skills.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 4:00–6:00 p.m., April 16, 2014 |
| Venue | CILL, the English Language Center |
| Topic | IELTS listening skills |
| Registration | Seats are limited; reserve at https://ieltslistening2.eventbrite.com |

The center advises students who are uncertain whether they can attend not to register. Those who register should attend as scheduled.
