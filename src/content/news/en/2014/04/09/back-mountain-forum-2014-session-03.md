The Changjiang School of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold the third 2014 session of the Hou Shan Forum on April 10. The session, titled “From Art and Design Academia to the Market: The Case of Jiemei Home Products,” will feature Wu Yong and Huang Yonghong.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 2:30–4:30 p.m., April 10, 2014 |
| Venue | Formative Arts Laboratory, rear building of the Changjiang School of Art and Design |
| Topic | From Art and Design Academia to the Market: The Case of Jiemei Home Products |
| Speakers | Wu Yong and Huang Yonghong |

The forum is open to interested faculty members and students.
