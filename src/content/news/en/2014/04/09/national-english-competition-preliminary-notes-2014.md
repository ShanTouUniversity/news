The preliminary round of the 2014 National College Students English Competition will take place from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. on Sunday, April 13. The Academic Affairs Office reminded contestants that the written paper is worth 150 points, including 90 points for subjective questions and 60 points for objective questions, and lasts 120 minutes.

Contestants must enter on time. Leaving is not permitted during the first 30 minutes, from 9:00 to 9:30, or during the final 10 minutes, from 10:50 to 11:00. Contestants who submit their papers between 9:30 and 10:50 must leave the answer sheet at their seats until invigilators collect it.

Contestants should bring a 2B pencil and a black pen. Books and electronic materials are prohibited, and no marks may be made on the test paper. Both the admission card and student ID are required; contestants without complete identification will not be admitted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
