Shantou University’s ninth Mental Health Awareness Month will begin on April 11 with an emotion management lecture titled “My Happiness, My Choice.” The lecture will discuss the relationship between happiness, success and well-being, happiness as a capacity for choice, and ways to cultivate positive emotions. Mental health expert Jin Ying will be the speaker.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., April 11, 2014 |
| Venue | Science and Technology Lecture Hall |
| Topic | My Happiness, My Choice |
| Speaker | Jin Ying |

The lecture is open to all STU students and faculty members.
