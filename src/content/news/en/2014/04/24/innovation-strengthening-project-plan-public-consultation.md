Shantou University has completed a draft of its 2014–2016 plan for Guangdong’s higher-education Innovation-Strengthening Project and opened it for internal public consultation on April 24.

Feedback is due by noon on April 28, 2014. Written comments and suggestions may be sent to the Development Planning Office at `o_211@stu.edu.cn` before the deadline. The full draft and consultation attachment are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Development Planning Office)
