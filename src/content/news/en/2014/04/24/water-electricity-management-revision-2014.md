It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 45 [2014] on April 24, 2014, to release the revised *Water and Electricity Management Measures of Shantou University*.

To strengthen safety and energy-saving management and to use water and electricity resources more scientifically and efficiently, the university revised the measures, which were approved at the 5th University Leadership Meeting on April 15, 2014, and are now released for implementation by all units.
