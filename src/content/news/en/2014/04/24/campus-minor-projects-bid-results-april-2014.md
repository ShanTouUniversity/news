Shantou University announced the review results for two minor construction projects. Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. was selected for both the swimming-pool repair project and the installation of stainless-steel security covers on campus.

The contract amounts are 44,354.80 yuan and 80,200 yuan, respectively. The results are open for public comment from April 24 through 28. The university’s Infrastructure Office is handling project inquiries, while complaints may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office.

| --- | ---: |
| Swimming-pool repairs | 44,354.80 yuan |
| Stainless-steel security covers | 80,200 yuan |
