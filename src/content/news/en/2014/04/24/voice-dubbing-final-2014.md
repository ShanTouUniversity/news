The final of Shantou University’s first “Voice for You” dubbing contest will take place from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Saturday, April 26, in the Science and Technology Reporting Hall. The College of Liberal Arts opened the contest to students across the university and received 24 teams for the preliminary round; 10 teams advanced to the final.

The College of Liberal Arts Youth League Committee welcomes members of the university community to attend.
