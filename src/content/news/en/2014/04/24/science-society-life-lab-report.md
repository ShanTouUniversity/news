The College of Science at Shantou University will hold a session in its “Science, Society and Life” series on April 30. Lin Bin and Yang Jianying, respectively director and deputy director of the Shantou Quality and Metrology Supervision and Testing Institute, will discuss laboratory accreditation and how testing professionals can develop their careers.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 2:30 p.m., April 30, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 305, E Building |
| Speakers | Lin Bin and Yang Jianying |
| Topics | Laboratory accreditation; professional development for laboratory testing staff |

Both speakers are senior engineers with long-term experience in product-quality testing and laboratory management. The report is open to students and faculty members.
