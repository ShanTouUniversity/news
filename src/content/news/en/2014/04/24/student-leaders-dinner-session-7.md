The seventh Shantou University dinner with university leaders will be held on April 25. The event will take place in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant and will be attended by 32 students who successfully reserved places.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 6:00 p.m., April 25, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Participants | 32 students with confirmed reservations |
| Arrival | 5:45 p.m.; participants should bring their campus cards |

The confirmed-participant list is included in the OA attachment. Student names are omitted here to protect privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
