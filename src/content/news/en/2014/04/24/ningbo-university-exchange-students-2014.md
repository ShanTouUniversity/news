Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has launched the 2014 selection of exchange students for Ningbo University. The university plans to send 20 undergraduates to Ningbo University for one semester beginning in fall 2014.

The program is open to full-time undergraduates at the main campus who have not previously participated in an exchange program. Applicants must have good conduct, no record of disciplinary action, and good academic performance without failed courses. During the exchange, students must take between 10 and 25 credits under STU’s academic regulations.

| Stage | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Student applications | April 24–May 7 |
| Schools report applicant numbers | By May 8 |
| Academic Affairs allocates quotas | May 9 |
| School-level assessment and public notice of preliminary selections | April 24–May 27 |
| Academic Affairs reviews and announces the final list | May 30 |

The application forms, selection materials and the Regulations on Undergraduate Exchange Training and Management between Shantou University and Domestic Universities are available in the original OA notice. Further information about Ningbo University can be found on its undergraduate admissions website.
