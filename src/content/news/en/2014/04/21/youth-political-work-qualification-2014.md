With the approval of the Guangdong Provincial Publicity Department, the Guangdong Youth League Committee has established a review committee for junior and intermediate professional qualifications in youth political work. The committee will handle applications and reviews for relevant youth political education and related staff.

Following the committee’s arrangement, Shantou University is surveying interest among people who wish to seek the qualifications. Applicants should complete the registration form and email it to the University Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by April 25, using “Qualification Review + Name + Registration Form” as the subject. The university will recommend participants for training based on the registrations, with details to be announced separately.

> ⚠️ The registration form and the original higher-level notice are available in the OA attachments.
