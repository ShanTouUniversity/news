Hong Kong media musician and creative director Leung Kei Cheuk will present the multimedia concert *Mars Daily* at Shantou University’s Grand Auditorium on the evening of April 25, 2014. The performance will combine sound, lighting, machinery, stage installations and video to explore ideas about the future through the relationship between technology and everyday life.

Leung previously served as music director for a concert at STU and has worked as a member of the Hong Kong collective People Mountain People Sea and as a producer for recordings and concerts. He studied composition and electronic music at the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts and has continued to develop new-media and interactive music projects.
