The opening game of the 16th CUBA China University Basketball League Southeast Division will take place at 2 p.m. on April 21, 2014, at China University of Mining and Technology in Xuzhou, Jiangsu. Shantou University’s men’s team will face the host university. The game will be broadcast live on CCTV5.
