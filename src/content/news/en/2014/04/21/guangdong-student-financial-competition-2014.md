The Guangdong University Financial Competition, part of the eighth Guangdong College Student Science and Technology Festival and sponsored by GF Securities, has opened registration. The event is jointly organized by the Guangdong Youth League Committee, Department of Education, Department of Science and Technology, Association for Science and Technology and Student Federation. It includes simulated trading and financial planning competitions.

The simulated trading competition is open to individual participants, who may register through the official competition website. The financial planning competition is for teams; interested teams should complete the registration form and email it to the University Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by April 30, 2014. The form and detailed arrangements are provided in the OA attachment.
