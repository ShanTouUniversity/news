Shantou University Library has featured *Meizhou Qiaopi*, a book by Deng Rui, vice-chairman of the Meizhou Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese. Published in September 2013, the approximately 560,000-character volume contains seven chapters on overseas Chinese communities and hometowns, remittances and finance, overseas Chinese affairs policy, itinerant couriers, and qiaopi.

The book includes archival images and traces the emergence, development, and decline of qiaopi, correspondence sent home by overseas Chinese that often served as both a family letter and a remittance record. It also examines remittances' contributions to Meizhou's social and economic development and the distinctive culture reflected in the documents. China's qiaopi archives were inscribed on the UNESCO Memory of the World Register in June 2013.
