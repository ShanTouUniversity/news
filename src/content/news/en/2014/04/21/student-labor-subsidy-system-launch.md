Shantou University's Finance Office launched its Student Compensation Management System on April 21 to centralize the processing of student wages, research assistant payments, and subsidies. Payments must be entered or imported in the system, submitted and printed, then approved before the printed documents are delivered to the Finance Office for disbursement.

The system was scheduled for a trial period through the end of April, with full implementation after the May Day holiday. Feedback during the trial can be sent to swzhang@stu.edu.cn. The system is available at https://m.stu.edu.cn/.
