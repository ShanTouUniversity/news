Shantou University's seventh University Leaders-Student Dinner is scheduled for 6 p.m. on April 25 in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. University leaders, department heads, and faculty representatives will meet with students for discussion. The event offers 32 places to full-time students.

Students wishing to attend were asked to submit their information through the university booking system by 11 p.m. on April 23 and confirm their availability. The system closes automatically when all places are filled. The university will cover the meal cost. Information from the previous dinner is also available through the student affairs notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
