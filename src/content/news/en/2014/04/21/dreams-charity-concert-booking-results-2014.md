Reservations for Shantou University’s Dreams charity interview and Bixa new-song campus concert closed at 11:55 p.m. on April 20, 2014. A total of 462 students and staff reserved 886 tickets. The successful reservation list and ticket counts were published in an OA attachment; personal details are not reproduced here.

To accommodate people who missed the reservation window, the organizers offered walk-in booking at the Golden Triangle on April 21 from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. and from 5 to 7 p.m. The event is organized by the STU Youth League Committee, STU Student Union and STU Young Volunteers Association.

> ⚠️ The successful reservation list and ticket counts are available in the original attachment on the OA system.
