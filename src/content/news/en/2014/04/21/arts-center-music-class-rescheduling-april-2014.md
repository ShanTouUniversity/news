Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office announced schedule changes for three Arts Center courses: Trumpet (class 67044), French Horn (67049), and Saxophone (67063). All make-up sessions will be held in room STU204 at the Arts Center.

| Course | Original class | Make-up class |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Trumpet (67044) | April 22, Tuesday, periods 6-7 | April 26, Saturday, periods 6-7 |
| French Horn (67049) | April 22, Tuesday, periods 8-9 | April 26, Saturday, periods 8-9 |
| Saxophone (67063) | April 24, Thursday, periods 6-7 | April 27, Sunday, periods A-B |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
