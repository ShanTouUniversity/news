Shantou University’s Engineering School will hold its 28th Growth Salon on April 25, 2014, under the theme “Fearless on the Graduate Study Journey.” Senior students Cheng Louyu, Huang Jingfei, Hu Xinhui and Zhou Feng will share advice on choosing graduate programs, preparation experiences and sources of information.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., April 25, 2014 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E201 |
| Audience | All Engineering School students |
| Registration deadline | Midnight, April 23 |

Interested students may register by emailing their name, year, program and contact details to 12wlzeng@stu.edu.cn.
