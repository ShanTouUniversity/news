Shantou University's Human Resources Office announced an internal recruitment for one non-degree training assistant in the School of Continuing Education. The position is based on campus and covers professional development programs, coordination of the partnership with The Open University of Hong Kong, training programs for social work professionals, and other non-degree initiatives.

Applicants must hold at least a full-time undergraduate degree from a regular higher-education institution. Experience managing training programs is preferred. The role also requires strong communication, organizational, document-writing, and English skills. Current university employees will retain their existing employment arrangements; compensation is negotiable.

Applications were due electronically by April 27, 2014, to recruitment@stu.edu.cn, with “STU School of Continuing Education Non-Degree Training Assistant” in the subject line. The official contact is Chang Li at 86502365. The OA notice contains the full list of required materials and examination procedures.
