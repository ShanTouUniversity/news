Shantou University will hold its fifth training program for public-service course instructors. The program is intended for faculty members and graduate students who will teach or assist with public-service courses. It counts as continuing education for faculty, and academic units are expected to arrange participation as these courses transition from electives to required courses.

Academic instructors must be full-time university employees with a postgraduate degree or an intermediate professional title, as well as experience in public-service and student activity organization. Practice-oriented instructors must hold at least a bachelor’s degree and have relevant organizational experience. Graduate assistants may choose to receive one teaching-practice credit or a subsidy of RMB 300 per course, in line with the university’s “three assistantships” standard.

## Registration

| Group | Registration method | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faculty instructors | Log in at training.stu.edu.cn and select “Professional Courses — Academic Affairs Office — Public-Service Course Instructor Training” | 5 p.m., April 28, 2014 |
| Graduate teaching assistants | Complete the registration form and email it to gykc@stu.edu.cn | 5 p.m., April 28, 2014 |

Detailed training arrangements and the graduate registration form are provided in the OA attachments.
