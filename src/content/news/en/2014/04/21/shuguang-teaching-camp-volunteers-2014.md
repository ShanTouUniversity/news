The volunteer recruitment for the third May Day teaching camp under Shantou University’s Shuguang rural school support project concluded on April 21, 2014. Following registration and interviews, 20 students were selected and three alternates were identified. Alternates will replace selected volunteers in order if needed.

## Selection Results

The selected students came from programs including international economics and trade, Chinese language and literature, communications, civil engineering, mathematics, electronics, English, journalism, finance, business administration, clinical medicine, arts, public administration and computer science, spanning the 2010 to 2013 cohorts. To protect student privacy, names are shown using family names and initials only:

- Selected volunteers: Feng Y, Hong ZG, Mo YZ, Xu PQ, Huang ZX, Zhou JJ, He GY, Lu JX, Dai LS, Zhou JY, Mo JW, Li YC, Xu QY, Zeng T, Xing MD, Liao JJ, Luo WC, Yang HZ, Luo N and Quan LQ.
- Alternates: Ling QY, Yang YY and Liang KH.

The University Youth League Committee, Young Volunteers Association and Shuguang volunteer team asked the selected students to prepare for and actively participate in the teaching camp.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)

> Student names have been anonymized in accordance with privacy rules.
