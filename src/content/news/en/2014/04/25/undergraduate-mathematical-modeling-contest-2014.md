The Youth League of Shantou University’s College of Science and the Mathematical Modeling Association will hold the 2014 undergraduate mathematical modeling contest from May 1 to 18. The contest is open to all enrolled undergraduates and is intended to develop students’ ability to use mathematics and computing to solve problems while selecting a team for the Shenzhen Cup mathematical modeling summer school paper evaluation.

Participants must register in teams of three, with members taking roles in writing, programming or modeling. Registration closes at 11:00 p.m. on April 30. Forms should be emailed to `12hxli@stu.edu.cn` or `12hfzhang@stu.edu.cn`; contest papers must be sent to `stu.mcm@qq.com` by 11:00 p.m. on May 19. The contest will be held in the Mathematics Department laboratory, and teams may choose one of the 2014 Shenzhen Cup summer-school modeling problems.

The contest will award first, second and third prizes as well as successful-participation awards, and will recommend one outstanding team for the Shenzhen Cup summer school paper evaluation. The registration form and related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
