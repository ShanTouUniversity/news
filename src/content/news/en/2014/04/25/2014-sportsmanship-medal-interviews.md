Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold interviews for the 2014 Sportsmanship Medal on May 4. Interviews are scheduled for 10:00 a.m. in Room 221 of the Administration Center. Shortlisted students must report to Office 106 at 9:00 a.m. to draw their interview order.

Each interview consists of a Chinese self-introduction of no more than one minute, followed by questions from the panel for up to nine minutes. The shortlist is included in the OA attachment; student names are omitted here to protect privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The interview shortlist is available on the OA system.
