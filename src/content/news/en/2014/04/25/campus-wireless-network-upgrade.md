Shantou University’s Network and Information Center will carry out a scheduled upgrade of the campus wireless network. The work is planned for 12:00 a.m.–6:00 a.m. on April 26, 2014, and may briefly interrupt wireless service across campus. A restoration time will not be announced separately.

For assistance, users may call 0754-86503400 (campus extension 883400) or email `stunic@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
