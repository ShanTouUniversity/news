It is reported that Prof. Dan Shechtman, a professor at the Technion — Israel Institute of Technology and winner of the 2011 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, will visit Shantou University on May 5-6, 2014, to give two lectures to faculty and students. The Student Affairs Office has opened reservations, and those interested must book through the reservation system (http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm) before 12:00 noon on April 29.

## Lecture Schedule

| Session | Topic | Time | Venue |
| ------- | ----- | ---- | ----- |
| Lecture 1 | Technological Entrepreneurship - a Key to World Peace and Prosperity | May 5, 19:30-20:45 | Auditorium |
| Lecture 2 | Challenges in Materials Science and Engineering | May 6, 19:30-20:30 | Library Lecture Hall |

The first lecture focuses on technological entrepreneurship and its connection to world peace and prosperity, while the second addresses frontier challenges in materials science and engineering. Both lectures are open to all faculty and students, with seats allocated by reservation.
