Shantou University’s 75th Party Fundamentals study class is scheduled to begin in mid-May. The United Front and Organization Department is now asking Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches to recommend and conduct preliminary reviews of participants.

Candidates must have submitted a Party membership application at least one year earlier and been designated as key development candidates. Application forms, signed and sealed by the relevant Party organization, must be submitted to the Party School Office in Room 315 of the New Administration Center by May 8, 2014. A consolidated participant list must also be emailed to `o_dxb@stu.edu.cn`; the Party School Office will determine the final list after review.

Quota allocations, application forms and the consolidated-list template are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (United Front and Organization Department)
> ⚠️ The quota allocations and application materials are available on the OA system.
