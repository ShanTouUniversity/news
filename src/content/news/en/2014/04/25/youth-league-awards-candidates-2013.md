It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a public notice on April 25, 2014, announcing the proposed awardees for the 2013 Outstanding Youth League Branches (General Branches), Outstanding Youth League Cadres and Outstanding Youth League Members.

The announcement follows the *Notice on the Selection of 2013 Outstanding Youth League Branches, Cadres and Members* (STU Youth League Document [2013] No. 06) and recommendations from college Youth League Committees and directly affiliated units.

## Public Notice

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Basis | STU Youth League Document [2013] No. 06 and unit recommendations |
| Content | Proposed list of Outstanding Branches (General Branches), Outstanding Cadres and Outstanding Members (see OA attachment; names withheld here to protect privacy) |
| Deadline for objections | 15:00, April 28, 2014 |
| How to submit | In writing or in person/by phone to the Youth League Committee; organizations should affix an official seal, individuals should sign with real name |
| Phone | 86502302 |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee of Shantou University |
