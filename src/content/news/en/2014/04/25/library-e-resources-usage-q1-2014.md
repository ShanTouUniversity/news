Shantou University Library released its first-quarter 2014 electronic-resource usage statistics on April 25. The figures cover Chinese- and foreign-language databases and include downloads, searches, reading, playback and views.

Among Chinese resources, the CNKI China Academic Journals platform recorded 117,672 downloads, Duxiu Knowledge Database recorded 72,573 page transfers, and the KUKE digital music library recorded 103,521 listens. Among foreign-language resources, Elsevier ScienceDirect recorded 35,399 downloads, Web of Science 12,380 searches, and Wiley Online Library 11,609 downloads. The databases use different measures, so their figures are not directly comparable.
