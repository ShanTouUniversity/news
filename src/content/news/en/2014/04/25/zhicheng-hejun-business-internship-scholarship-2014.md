Shantou University's Zhicheng College opened the 2014 selection for its Hejun Business Internship Scholarship on April 25. Funded by Beijing Hejun Consulting Co., the scholarship recognizes college students with strong performance in business internships.

Five students are selected each year. Each recipient receives RMB 2,000 and a certificate. Applicants were asked to submit an application to the college office by 5 p.m. on May 31 and prepare the supporting materials specified in the selection rules.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
> ⚠️ The selection rules and application form are available on the OA system.
