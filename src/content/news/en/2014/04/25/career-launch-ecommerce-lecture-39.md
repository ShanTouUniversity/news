Shantou University’s Career Launch Forum Vol. 39 will take place on April 28 under the theme “The Current State, Outlook and Strategies of China’s E-commerce Sector.” Mo Dinghong, e-commerce head at Zhejiang Tongde Import and Export Co., will discuss market trends, challenges and opportunities, as well as e-commerce planning and ways for university students to pursue opportunities in the sector.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 7:00–9:00 p.m., April 28, 2014 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E201 |
| Speaker | Mo Dinghong |
| Audience | All students and faculty members |

Mo holds an MBA in e-commerce from South China University of Technology and has 10 years of e-commerce experience. He has helped several companies develop online sales and has shared his experience at universities and training institutions.
