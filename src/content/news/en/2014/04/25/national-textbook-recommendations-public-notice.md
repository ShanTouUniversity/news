In line with national and Guangdong requirements for the second round of the 12th Five-Year national undergraduate textbook selection, Shantou University proposed two textbooks: *Digital Image Processing (MATLAB Edition)* by Yan Jingwen and *Modern British Literary Masters* by Zhu Wang.

The public-comment period ends at 5:30 p.m. on April 29, 2014. Organizations or individuals with comments may submit written feedback to the Academic Affairs Office’s teaching-reform evaluation section before the deadline. Organizational submissions should bear an official seal, while individual submissions should include the sender’s real name and contact details. The contact telephone number is 86502736 and the email address is `liz@stu.edu.cn`.
