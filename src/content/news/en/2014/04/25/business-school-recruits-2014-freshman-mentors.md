Shantou University’s Business School has begun recruiting 70 mentors for the 2014 freshman cohort. The mentors will support orientation, peer assistance and freshmen’s adjustment to campus life. Applicants should be committed to student work, responsible, able to communicate and coordinate, and have a GPA of at least 3.0 with no failed courses.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Organization recommendations due | By noon, May 10 |
| Individual applications due | By 5:00 p.m., May 10 |
| Application email | `o_stw@stu.edu.cn` |
| Interview | Tentatively 2:00–9:00 p.m. on May 16; details to follow |

The 70 places will be filled through organization recommendations and interviews. The application and recommendation forms and detailed requirements are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School)
> ⚠️ The application and recommendation forms are available on the OA system.
