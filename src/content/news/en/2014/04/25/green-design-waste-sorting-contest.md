The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the STU Youth Volunteers Association and the Green Ribbon Service-Learning Environmental Volunteer Team have launched the Green Ideas waste-sorting design contest. The contest is open to all students and seeks works centered on waste sorting and recycling.

Participants may submit a mobile or computer game application, a comic or a waste-bin design, either individually or as a team. Entries will be judged on public benefit, technical quality, innovation and visual effect. The prizes include one first prize, three second prizes, six third prizes and two popularity awards.

| Stage | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | April 25–May 5, until 11:00 p.m. |
| Submission | May 5–18, until 11:00 p.m. |
| Judging | Late May; details to follow |
| Display of outstanding works | Late May; details to follow |

Participants must email the registration form to `13zwwang@stu.edu.cn` by 11:00 p.m. on May 5. The registration form and contest attachments are available in the original OA notice.
