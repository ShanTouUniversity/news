Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office will host a lecture on “International Science and Technology Cooperation” on April 29, featuring Yin Jun, science and technology counselor at the Chinese Embassy in Italy.

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 9:00–11:00 a.m., April 29, 2014 |
| Venue | Academic Lecture Hall, third floor, Science and Technology Building, STU Medical College |
| Topic | International science and technology cooperation |
| Speaker | Yin Jun, science and technology counselor at the Chinese Embassy in Italy |
| Contact | Zhu Changsheng, 86502813 |

Yin joined China’s Ministry of Science and Technology in 1985 and has served as director of its European affairs division and China’s chief science and technology representative to the European Union. The office will arrange transport for faculty members who contact it in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Affairs Office)
