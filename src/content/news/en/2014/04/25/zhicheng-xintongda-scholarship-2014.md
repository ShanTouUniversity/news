Zhicheng College at Shantou University has opened the 2014 selection for its Xintongda Scholarship. Funded by Guangdong Xintongda Steel Pipe Co., Ltd., the award recognizes resident students who exemplify the college motto of “integrity, respect, humility and harmony.”

Up to four students are selected each year. Recipients receive a certificate and a 2,500-yuan award. Eligible students may apply individually, be nominated by a college student organization, or be jointly nominated by at least three residents. Applications and nomination forms must be submitted to the college affairs office by 5:00 p.m. on May 31.

The selection rules and application and nomination materials are available in the original OA notice.
