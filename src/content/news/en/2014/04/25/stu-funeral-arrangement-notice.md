It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University issued a death notice on April 25, 2014, detailing the funeral arrangements. Units and individuals wishing to send wreaths are asked to register with the university's Retired Staff Office (tel: 86502660) no later than 16:00 on April 25.

## Shuttle Schedule for the Send-off

According to the arrangement, faculty, staff and students attending the send-off on April 26 could board shuttle buses at the following points:

| Pick-up Point | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Reception Room entrance (main campus) | 9:10 |
| Anju entrance | 9:20 |
| Dajiao, opposite the Fourth Hospital | 9:30 |
| Baiheyuan campus bus stop | 9:40 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
