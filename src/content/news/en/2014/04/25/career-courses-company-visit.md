Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has arranged an off-campus company visit on April 30 for four career-related classes. The schedule covers class 68065, *Career Guidance*, and classes 68060, 68061 and 68063, *Career Counselling and Training*. The destination is Shantou Lekai Film Co., Ltd.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Date | April 30, 2014 |
| Assembly time | 2:15 p.m. |
| Meeting point | University motto monument |
| Return time | 5:00 p.m. |

Students in the relevant classes should assemble on time and share the schedule with classmates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
