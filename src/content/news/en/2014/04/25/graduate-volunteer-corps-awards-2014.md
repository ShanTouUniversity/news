Shantou University’s Graduate School has announced the recognition results for the second graduate volunteer corps. After a year of activities, recommendations by department heads, review by the presidium and consideration by the school, 11 members were named “outstanding cadres” and 21 members “outstanding officers.”

The recognition acknowledges the members’ work and encourages continued student participation in school development and volunteer service. Individual student names are omitted to protect privacy.
