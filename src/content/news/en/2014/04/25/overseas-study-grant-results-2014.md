Shantou University Student Affairs announced the results of the 2014 Li Ka Shing Foundation Overseas Study Grant on April 25. Following committee selection and management committee review, 20 students were selected.

Five recipients were selected for Category A grants of RMB 80,000 each, eight for Category B grants of RMB 50,000 each, and seven for Category C grants of RMB 30,000 each. Funding is conditional on recipients obtaining admission to institutions approved by the management committee. The program supports master's study at overseas universities, including the University of Chicago, the University of Edinburgh and University College London.
