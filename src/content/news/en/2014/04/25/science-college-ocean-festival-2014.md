The Shantou University Science College Youth Volunteer Association planned its second “Youth and Ocean” festival for April 27 to June 15, 2014, on campus. The program was open to students and staff and aimed to encourage greater awareness of the ocean and marine ecology.

Interactive activities at the Golden Triangle on April 27 were to include paper-boat making, ocean-knowledge quizzes, handmade animal charms and a water-based ping-pong challenge. Other events invited photography, painting, craft and game submissions on ocean themes, as well as three-line poems. The submission deadline was to be announced through later campus updates; selected works were expected to be displayed at the campus open day, with prizes and certificates planned.
