Shantou University’s infrastructure office will install steel-pipe railings along the drainage channel from Road 10 (B9) to the front of the Development Foundation building. The work has been assigned to Shantou Yueli Construction and Decoration Co., Ltd.

Construction is scheduled to begin on April 26, 2014, and finish by May 10. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during the work. Pedestrians and vehicles passing the area should take care and avoid lingering near the construction site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
