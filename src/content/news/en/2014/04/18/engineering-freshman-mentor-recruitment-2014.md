The Youth League Committee and freshman mentor team of Shantou University's School of Engineering are recruiting mentors for the 2014 freshman cohort. The opportunity is open to all School of Engineering students in the 2012 cohort. Mentors will help with materials, registration, campus and dormitory guidance during orientation and admission education, and organize activities to help new students adjust to university life.

Applicants should demonstrate responsibility, communication and coordination skills, teamwork and willingness to follow the school's arrangements. Strong academic performance, party membership or student-organization experience will be considered an advantage. Applicants must complete the form and submit it by 5 p.m. on April 25; the interview time and location will be announced later. Selected mentors must return early for orientation preparation and training.
