Shantou University's 11th Sangpushan Reading Festival will run from April 19 to May 5 under the theme “Nourished by books, enriched in spirit.” Organized by the Communist Youth League committee, the University Library, and the Student Societies Union, the festival is open to the university community and features nine reading, discussion, and creative activities.

The program includes a morning reading walk on Sangpushan, a library-themed photo exhibition, a survey comparing traditional and digital reading, hands-on experiences in library departments, a literature-and-film discussion, book recommendations and exchanges, a writing contest, and a mobile-library experience. On April 26, the reading walk will set out from the university motto monument to read classical literature on the mountain. Library visits are scheduled for April 21-27. Book recommendations and exchanges will run from April 20-30 around the library and campus dining halls; a mobile-library session is scheduled for April 22 at the Library.

The writing contest accepts fiction, drama, prose, modern poetry, classical poetry and prose, criticism, and English writing through May 5. Some activities require advance registration; detailed procedures are available in the OA notice.
