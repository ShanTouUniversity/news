Shantou University's Security Office and Party and Administrative Office have reaffirmed campus dog-ownership requirements. Departments and residents may not keep dogs on campus without approval from the public-security authorities. Existing dog owners must arrange for their dogs to leave campus within 10 days of the notice. The university will continue removing stray and ownerless dogs from campus.

Dogs that remain without authorization or a valid license may be removed by university departments or reported to the public-security authorities. Licensed dogs must wear their tags. Owners who have obtained a dog license must submit a copy to the Security Office by April 30.
