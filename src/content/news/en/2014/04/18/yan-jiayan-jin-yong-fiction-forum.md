The 134th New Century Humanities Forum at Shantou University will take place at 3 p.m. on April 23 in the library lecture hall. Yan Jiayan, professor and doctoral supervisor at Peking University's Department of Chinese Language and Literature, will speak on “My View of Jin Yong Novels.”

Yan is a literary historian and theorist who previously served as head of Peking University's Department of Chinese Language and Literature. He has also served as vice president of the Chinese Association of Fiction Studies and president of the Chinese Modern Literature Research Association. His work covers modern Chinese fiction, literary schools and Jin Yong novels, including *A History of Modern Chinese Fiction Schools* and *Studies on Jin Yong's Novels*.
