Shantou University's Infrastructure Office has announced the bid evaluation result for the renovation of its digital multifunctional studio. Guangdong Jianhua Decoration Co., Ltd. was selected as the successful bidder, with a contract value of RMB 2,079,588.12.

The result is open for public review from April 18 through 21, 2014. The university Infrastructure Office handles inquiries, while the Supervision and Audit Office receives complaints.
