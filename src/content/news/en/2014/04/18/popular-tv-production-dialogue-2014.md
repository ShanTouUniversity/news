Shantou University's Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design will hold a dialogue on “Popular TV Series Production Through *My Youth High Eight Degrees*” from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on Monday, April 21, in the university science lecture hall.

The speakers will be Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design, and Chen Yijun, veteran television producer and director of *My Youth High Eight Degrees*. The event is organized by the school and open to the university community.
