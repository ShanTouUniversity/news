Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has launched the second recommendation round for the 12th Five-Year Plan national undergraduate textbook program. Colleges and relevant units may recommend textbooks whose first authors are STU teachers, which are currently used in undergraduate teaching and have shown good results. In principle, eligible books must have been formally published between January 2010 and December 2012; multi-volume works may receive the permitted extension for some volumes.

Each college may generally recommend no more than 20% of its eligible undergraduate textbooks. A college with fewer than five eligible books may recommend one. Textbook leaders must complete the recommendation form, after which their college will review and consolidate the materials. Electronic copies of the recommendation and summary forms are due to the Academic Affairs Office by 5 p.m. on April 22. The office will arrange expert review, publicize recommended books and submit the results to the provincial education authority.

Contact: Li Zhe, Teaching Reform and Assessment Section, Academic Affairs Office; telephone 86502736; email zli@stu.edu.cn. See the original OA attachments for the forms and policy documents.
