It is reported that Shantou University will hold the second meeting of its ninth Board of Councilors on June 26 and 27. During the meeting period, the university will also host the graduation ceremony and other major events, with distinguished guests invited for various exchange activities.

To ensure the smooth running of these events, the university has made unified arrangements for its venues. According to a message released by the University Affairs Office, the following venues will be reserved exclusively for Board of Councilors activities:

| Venue | Arrangement |
| ----- | ----------- |
| Grand Auditorium | Reserved exclusively for Board events |
| Science Lecture Hall | Reserved exclusively for Board events |
| Library Lecture Hall | Reserved exclusively for Board events |
| Gymnasium | Reserved exclusively for Board events |
| Academic Exchange Service Center | Reserved exclusively for Board events |

These venues and facilities will not be available for other activities during the meeting period. The university has reminded all departments to take note of the arrangement and to select alternative venues or reschedule any activities planned for that time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Affairs Office)
