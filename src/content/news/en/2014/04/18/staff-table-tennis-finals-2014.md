The finals of Shantou University's staff table tennis team competition are scheduled for 4:30 p.m. on April 18 at two tables near the gymnastics room in the Gymnasium.

The championship match will see the School of Science face the Resource Management team, while the Administration team will play the School of Engineering for third place. The matches will take place on tables 1 and 2, respectively.
