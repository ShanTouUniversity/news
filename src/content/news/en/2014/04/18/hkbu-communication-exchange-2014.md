Shantou University's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication opened applications for its 2014-15 exchange with Hong Kong Baptist University's School of Communication. HKBU offers one place each year for a semester-long exchange with tuition waived; students cover their own living expenses, estimated at about HK$50,000 for the semester.

Applicants should preferably be in their third year, with second-year students also eligible. Requirements include a GPA of at least 3.5, IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 80, or CET-6, as well as strong participation in community service or school activities. Applications were due by April 26. The school planned to interview five candidates at 4 p.m. on April 30 in its Glass Room. Students who had previously received university or school funding for a semester-long overseas exchange were ineligible. Application forms and detailed requirements are available in the OA notice.
