Shantou University's inaugural Alumni Cup football match is scheduled for May 17, 2014. The Sports Department and Alumni Affairs Office will jointly host the event, with the STU Student Football Club serving as organizer. Alumni teams will return to campus to play alongside current students.

The Sports Department is recruiting student volunteers to assist with playing, refereeing, publicity and photography, and logistics. Places are limited and candidates will be selected. Applications are due by April 25. Applicants should provide their year, major, interests, and preferred role. The official contact is Mr. Liu of the Sports Department at xwliu@stu.edu.cn or 638587.
