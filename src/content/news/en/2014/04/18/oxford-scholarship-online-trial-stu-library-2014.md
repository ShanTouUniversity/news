Shantou University Library has opened trial access to Oxford Scholarship Online, an academic e-book platform published by Oxford University Press. The trial runs through July 15, 2014. The platform provides more than 8,000 scholarly titles in the social sciences, humanities, and natural sciences.

Students and faculty can access the resource through the library's database information page. Feedback can be sent to the Library's Subject Services Department at 86503247, lqxie@stu.edu.cn, or zhzeng@stu.edu.cn.
