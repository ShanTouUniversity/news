Shantou University and Dublin City University (DCU) will hold two information sessions on their cooperation programs on April 21 and 22. Xiaoxia Wang, chief representative of DCU's China office, will introduce the 2014-15 exchange and joint programs and answer questions.

The first session is scheduled for 7:30-9 p.m. on Monday, April 21, in E409 for students from the School of Liberal Arts and the Business School. The second will take place at the same time and venue on Tuesday, April 22, for students from the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
