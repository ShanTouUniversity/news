It is reported that Zhicheng College of Shantou University will hold a "Wilderness First Aid Workshop" from 9:00 to 11:30 a.m. on April 27, 2014, in Lecture Room J-354 of the college, open to Zhicheng College residents. The event is jointly organized by the college's Youth League Committee and its "Beidou" Outdoor Development Association.

## Echoing the "Off the Internet, Out of the Dorm, Onto the Playground" Campaign

The workshop aims to implement the spirit of the campaign launched by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League, the Ministry of Education, the General Administration of Sport, and the National Students' Federation encouraging students to spend less time online and more time exercising outdoors. It seeks to enrich residents' outdoor knowledge and strengthen their safety awareness and ability to respond to accidents.

## Professional Rescuers as Instructors

| Instructor | Background |
| --- | --- |
| Xiao Hongjie | Water rescue worker of the Guangdong Provincial Red Cross Society; captain and training group leader of the Eastern Guangdong Volunteer Rescue Team (in preparation); first aid worker of the Shantou Red Cross Society |
| Wu Yuebin | Member of the training groups of the Eastern Guangdong Volunteer Rescue Team (in preparation) and the Jieyang Cheng'anjie Volunteer Rescue Team, with two years of rescue mission experience |

The workshop covers two parts: basic first aid knowledge — including treatment of heatstroke, fainting, and poisoning, as well as bandaging and casualty transport — and outdoor rope-knot techniques.

## How to Register

Residents may sign up on the registration sheet at the notice board in the college lobby or by email in a specified format. Registration closes at 5:00 p.m. on April 24. The registration email address is available in the college's announcement.
