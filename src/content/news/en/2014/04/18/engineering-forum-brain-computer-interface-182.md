Shantou University's School of Engineering will host the 182nd Engineering Research Forum from 10 to 11 a.m. on April 22. Professor Zhou Changle of Xiamen University will speak on “Brain-Computer Interface Computing.”

The talk will take place in the School of Engineering Academic Exchange Room (Gongnan 312).
