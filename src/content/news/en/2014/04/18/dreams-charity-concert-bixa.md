Shantou University will hold the “On the Road to Dreams” interview and Bixa new-song campus concert from 8 to 9:30 p.m. on April 22 in the Grand Auditorium, with admission from 7:30 to 8 p.m. The event is organized by the STU Youth League Committee, Shantou Radio and Mengxiang Qiangyin Culture Communication (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., and co-organized by the STU Student Union and Young Volunteers Association.

The program combines music with charity. All funds raised will support the Firefly Charity Foundation's leprosy rehabilitation village project. Students and staff may register through the booking system by 11:55 p.m. on April 20 and make a donation of at least RMB 3 to receive an admission letter. Letters can be collected at the Golden Triangle booth during designated periods on April 21 and 22 or delivered by volunteers to the dormitory or office address provided during registration. Supplies are limited. Admission requires the letter; seating is first come, first served, and entry ends 30 minutes after the show starts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
