Shantou University's Human Resources Office published a proposed appointment notice on April 18. Following deliberations at the fifth university leadership meeting on April 15, Yu Dai was proposed for appointment as assistant director of the Academic Affairs Office. Wang Wei, Liu Xiangling, Xie Xuhui, Fang Ying, and Jin Jiechun were proposed for mid-level management positions.

The notice was issued under the university's provisional measures for appointing management cadres (汕大党字〔2009〕第47号). Comments were due by April 24. The Human Resources Office can be reached at 86503390 or rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
