Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has begun curriculum planning for the 2014 undergraduate cohort. Programs are to base their plans on the newly established educational objectives, standards, and knowledge frameworks, and prepare course descriptions. Every admission program must also provide plans for dual degrees and minors.

Programs were asked to submit course plans, dual-degree and minor plans, and course descriptions to the Academic Affairs Office's Teaching Section by May 30, 2014. Printed materials require the specified approvals, and matching electronic copies must also be submitted. Departments, centers, and institutes need submit course descriptions only. The office planned to publish the 2014 Academic Guide in July. Course-coding rules and detailed materials are in the OA attachments. For inquiries, contact Songqi Jiang at 86502416 or sonky@stu.edu.cn.
