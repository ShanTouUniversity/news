The Shantou University School of Science will hold a Science Salon talk on April 8, 2014. Qi Hongji, a researcher and doctoral supervisor at the Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, will discuss low-dimensional thin-film materials.

The talk will cover multilayer-film interference, optical thin-film device design, and the spectral, phase and polarization properties of films. It will also examine columnar microstructures formed by oblique deposition and their anisotropic applications, including depolarizing films, polarization-separating films and filters.

Qi received a PhD from the Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics in 2005 and has long worked on low-dimensional thin-film materials. His work has included projects funded by the National 863 Program, the National Natural Science Foundation and the Shanghai Municipal Science and Technology Commission, with more than 60 papers published or co-published.
