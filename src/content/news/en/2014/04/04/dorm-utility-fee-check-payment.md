It is reported that the Resources Management Office of Shantou University has released student dormitory utility charges covering the period from the start of the semester to the end of March. This meter reading was taken on March 31, while the previous one was on February 19.

## Verification and Payment Schedule

| Item | Time |
| ---- | ---- |
| Verification window | April 4–10 (working hours) |
| Campus card deduction begins | April 11 |
| Standard payment period | April 11–15 |

Students can check their charges on the STU utility fee management system, using their campus network accounts.

## Handling Discrepancies

If errors or questions are found, students may fill in and sign a utility fee verification application form and submit it to the utilities center during the verification window (next to the Science Building, tel: 86502489).

If the residents of a dorm room do not match the student roster, students should contact the counselor's office (Room 106, Building B, tel: 86503355).

For rooms with no objections after the verification window, deductions will be made via the campus card system by the network center (tel: 86503545) starting April 11. Those who fail to pay on time will be dealt with according to university regulations starting April 16.

It is noted that since the billing process involves multiple departments, discrepancies raised after the verification window will take 3 to 5 working days to correct even if verified. Students are also reminded to top up their campus cards in time if their balance is insufficient.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
