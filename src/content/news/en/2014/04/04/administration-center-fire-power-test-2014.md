Shantou University’s Security Office announced on April 4 that a test of the Administration Center’s fire-emergency power system was scheduled for April 7, 2014, from 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. Electricity at the center was expected to be interrupted during the test.

The test was to be conducted by security personnel together with the university’s water and electricity service center and other relevant units to check the building’s internal electrical circuits. The notice described a plan and did not report the test outcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office)
