Yangtze College of Art and Design has completed major assignments for its 2013 cohort under the college's assignment plan. The results are being posted for public notice from April 4 through 10, with the detailed list available on the college bulletin board.

Students may submit comments in writing, in person or by telephone during the notice period. Feedback must include the submitter's real name. The college will finalize the assignments after considering the feedback.
