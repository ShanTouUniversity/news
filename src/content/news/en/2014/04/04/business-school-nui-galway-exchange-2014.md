The Shantou University Business School has opened selection for a tuition-free exchange at the National University of Ireland Galway (NUI Galway). One student will be sent to the university in September 2014, with one alternate also selected.

The opportunity is open to Business School undergraduates in their second year or above, with priority for students who have not previously joined a university or school exchange. Applicants should have strong English skills, with an IELTS overall score of 6.5 and no component below 5.5, or a TOEFL iBT score of 92. They must also have good academic standing, a GPA of at least 3.0, and submit a transcript, English-test evidence and a 300–500-word English study-plan essay.

Applicants must use the STU undergraduate international exchange application system by noon on April 26, select “NUI Galway Tuition-Free Exchange Program,” and deliver printed materials to the Business School Foreign Affairs Projects Office in Wenxi 211. English-test evidence may be submitted by May 30 at the latest.

The selected student will be exempt from NUI Galway tuition but must cover application and administrative fees, travel, accommodation, meals, books and materials, insurance and medical costs during the exchange.
