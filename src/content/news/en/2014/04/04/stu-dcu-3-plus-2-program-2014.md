The Shantou University School of Liberal Arts has opened applications for a 3+2 undergraduate-to-master program with Dublin City University's SALIS. Applicants may choose Applied Language and Translation Studies, Languages for International Communication—English Studies, or Contemporary Culture and Society.

Applicants must email the application form, transcript, available English-test results and an English essay of no more than 400 words to `myzheng@stu.edu.cn` by 5:30 p.m. on April 30, 2014. Transcripts may be submitted by May 21 at the latest, and the email subject should follow the format specified in the notice.

The English requirements are a minimum TOEFL CBT score of 237, iBT score of 92 or PBT score of 580; or an IELTS overall score of 6.5 with no component below 6.0. Applicants to Applied Language and Translation Studies must have an IELTS writing score of at least 6.5.

Dublin City University offers STU School of Liberal Arts students a 15 percent reduction from its fee schedule for non-EU students. After the reduction, annual tuition is approximately EUR 11,300 or EUR 12,100 for the listed undergraduate options and EUR 13,300 for a master's year; other expenses are estimated at about RMB 100,000 per year.
