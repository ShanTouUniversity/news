Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold a resume-writing workshop on April 10, 2014. Catherine Bai and Sohani Gandhioke will lead the session, covering how to write a resume, what information to include and points to consider during the writing process.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 1:00–2:30 p.m., Thursday, April 10, 2014 |
| Location | Center for Innovative Learning and Instruction (CILL) |
| Registration | Seats are limited; reserve a place through the event page |

The organizers asked participants to register only if they could attend. Registration is available at https://resumewrtg.eventbrite.com. Those who register should bring their tickets.
