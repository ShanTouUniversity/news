The Shantou University Academic Affairs Office issued arrangements on April 4, 2014, for spring-semester final assessments. Under the 2013–14 academic calendar, final assessments for courses scheduled through Week 15 and beyond will be held in Weeks 17 and 18.

Teaching units must verify course information, class numbers and assessment methods. The Academic Affairs Office will arrange times and locations for courses offered by the Department of Social Sciences, Computer Teaching Center, Institute of Higher Education and designated foundational subjects; other courses will be scheduled in the credit-system platform by the relevant unit's academic staff. After approval by the unit head, forms must be sent to the Academic Affairs Office Student Affairs Section by April 10, with a copy emailed to `xhx@stu.edu.cn`.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment weeks | Weeks 17 and 18 |
| Scheduling | Jointly completed in the credit-system platform by Academic Affairs and teaching units |
| Submission deadline | April 10, 2014 |
| Recipient | Student Affairs Section, Academic Affairs Office |
| Email | `xhx@stu.edu.cn` |

> ⚠️ The final-assessment form and instructions are available in the original OA attachment.
