It is reported that the Student Learning Center (SLC) of Shantou University will suspend public access from Saturday, April 5 to Monday, April 7, 2014, during the Qingming holiday.

## Reason for Closure and Safety Reminder

According to the Student Affairs Office, the closure allows the SLC to replace the glass doors of its seminar rooms over the holiday. The newly installed doors need about two days to set, and, as a safety precaution, the center has asked students and staff not to walk near or touch the glass doors during this period.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Closure Period | April 5 – April 7, 2014 |
| Area Affected | Student Learning Center (SLC) |
| Work | Replacement of seminar-room glass doors |
| Issuing Office | Student Affairs Office |

Students and faculty with study plans are encouraged to adjust their schedules accordingly and visit the center after it reopens following the holiday.
