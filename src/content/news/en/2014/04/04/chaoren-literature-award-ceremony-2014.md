The first International Chaoshan Literature Awards ceremony will be held at Shantou University on April 6, 2014. It is jointly organized by Shantou University, the Chaoshan Literature Institute, the Shantou Chaoshan Historical and Cultural Research Center and the Guangdong Chaoshan Culture and Arts Exchange Association, with Anfu Culture Co., Ltd. as title sponsor.

The award recognizes writers and works that have made outstanding contributions to Chaoshan literature in China and overseas. It also aims to promote outstanding literary works and support the development of Chaoshan literary creation.
