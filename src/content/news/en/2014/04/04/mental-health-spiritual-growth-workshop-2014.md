Shantou University will hold a spiritual growth workshop as part of its ninth Mental Health Month on April 11, 2014. The workshop, themed “Encounter: The Dance of Spirituality,” will be hosted by psychologist Jin Ying, who will guide participants in exploring personal growth through spiritual dance.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Spiritual Growth Workshop |
| Theme | “Encounter: The Dance of Spirituality” |
| Time | 2:30–5 p.m., April 11, 2014 |
| Location | Room 726, Building 789 |
| Host | Jin Ying |
