Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office and Community Peer Advisors have launched “My Themed Dorm Room,” part of the Student Community X initiative. Dormitory teams are invited to decorate their rooms around a theme. Registration runs from April 1 to 7, decorating from April 10 to 15, and judging is scheduled for the afternoon of April 16.

Judging combines WeChat Moments likes (40 percent) and an in-room evaluation by judges (60 percent), assessing the theme, positive presentation and tidiness. Awards include one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes and five creative-theme prizes, with cash awards of RMB 400, 300, 200 and 100 respectively. Confirmed entrants will receive a RMB 30 decoration allowance.

Dormitories must submit an entry form by noon on April 7 and provide a photo of the finished design. Decorations must comply with dormitory rules and must not damage facilities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The entry form and full contest requirements are available on the OA system.
