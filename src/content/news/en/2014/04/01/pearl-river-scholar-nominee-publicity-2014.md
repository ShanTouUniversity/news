Shantou University plans to nominate Zhang Yueling for a Guangdong Pearl River Scholar distinguished professorship in marine biology. The proposed nomination followed an application by the relevant unit and review by the university’s academic committee, and is now being made public under the provincial program’s rules.

The public notice period runs from April 2 to 11, 2014. Objections may be submitted in writing or by email to the university’s Personnel Office by April 11. The office lists rscgs@stu.edu.cn and 86503390 as official contact details.
