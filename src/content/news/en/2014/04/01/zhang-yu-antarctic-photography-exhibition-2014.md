Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will present Zhang Yu’s photography exhibition “Elect a Representative to the South Pole” from April 2 to 9 in the lobby of the school. The school organized the exhibition with academic support from Backhill Forum.

The exhibition is curated by Lu Yuanliang. Further information about the works and exhibition is included in the OA attachment.
