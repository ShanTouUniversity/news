Shantou University’s Communist Youth League Committee will hold a review and awards event for its 2013 “Strengthening Academic Culture for Growth and Achievement” themed Youth League Day initiative from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on April 2 in room E203. The initiative ran across campus from November 2013 through March 2014, with student organizations organizing activities in varied formats.

Project leaders from participating schools will share their work. Six outstanding projects will be recognized: one first prize, two second prizes and three third prizes. Presentations may use any format; those using slides are limited to seven minutes. The event is open to the university community.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
