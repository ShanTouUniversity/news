Shantou University Business School will hold the “Career Is Yours” career development series from April to May 2014. The program is designed to help students understand the workplace and prepare for internships and employment. It is aimed at undergraduate students in the 2011 cohort. The internship and employment mobilization meeting is mandatory for that cohort, while students from other years may join the other activities voluntarily.

## Program content

The series will include a lecture on workplace awareness and career decisions, an internship and employment mobilization meeting, job-search skills workshops, a sharing session with graduates, and a Southern China market recruitment fair with interview and job-search practice. Topics will cover industry, company and position analysis, career decision-making, internship requirements and conduct, résumé writing, Chinese and English interview skills, workplace etiquette and professional emotional intelligence.

| Schedule | Specific times and venues will be announced separately |

The Business School said the series aims to strengthen students’ career-planning awareness and employment skills before their internships and job searches.
