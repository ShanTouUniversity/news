Shantou University’s Communist Youth League Committee released “Your Club, My Club,” the third video in its “Moments at STU” series. The film follows students who came together through shared interests, took part in campus clubs and continued pursuing what they love.

The video was released by the university’s Youth League Committee and the student-run “Chuan Network.” It is available on [Youku](http://v.youku.com/v_show/id_XNjkyNTEyMzQ0.html).
