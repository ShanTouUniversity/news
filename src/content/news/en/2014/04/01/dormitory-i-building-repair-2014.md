Shantou University’s infrastructure office will hire Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Co. to repair Dormitory I. The work includes painting interior walls and ceilings and replacing insect screens and curtains. The winning contract was valued at RMB 58,100.

Construction is scheduled to begin on April 3 and finish by May 2. The work may cause noise, vibration and dust. The university advised campus users to take care and avoid lingering near the work site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
