Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has adjusted the schedule for Mandarin test training in the first half of 2014. Because most students in the first group had classes at the original times, its sessions have been moved from April 3 and 10 to 2:30 p.m. on April 2 and 9. The second group’s schedule remains unchanged.

## Revised schedule

| Group | First session | Second session | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 2:30 p.m., April 2 | 2:30 p.m., April 9 | Lecture Hall 3 |
| Second | 2:30 p.m., April 23 | 2:30 p.m., April 30 | Lecture Hall 3 |

The office asked students to attend according to their assigned groups and to share the schedule change with classmates. The group lists and other attachments contain student information and are available in the original OA notice.
