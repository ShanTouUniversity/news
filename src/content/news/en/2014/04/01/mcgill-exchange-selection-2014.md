It is reported that the Law School issued a notice on April 1, 2014, launching an exchange program with McGill University in Canada, to send undergraduates for one semester in the fall of 2014.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 3 Law majors and 3 Public Administration majors, 6 in total |
| Host | Faculty of Law and Department of Political Science (Faculty of Arts), McGill University |
| Period | Fall semester 2014, one semester |
| Funding | Li Ka Shing Foundation Semester Abroad Awards (up to CAD 30,837 for Law, CAD 22,189 for PA) covering tuition, living, accommodation and flights; insurance, passport and visa at own expense |
| Organizer | Law School |

## Eligibility

1. Law undergraduates in years 2–3 (classes of 2011 and 2012) and Public Administration undergraduates in year 2 (classes of 2011 and 2012);
2. Strong English proficiency: IELTS 6.5 (no subscore below 6.0) or TOEFL 90 (no subscore below 21);
3. Solid professional knowledge with GPA in the top 30% of the major;
4. Team spirit, active in public service, strong communication skills;
5. Outgoing and adaptable to new environments;
6. Priority to those who have not previously joined overseas exchange programs under equal conditions.

## Application

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Online application | April 2–7 via STU Student Affairs System (sao.stu.edu.cn) |
| Materials | Printed application form from the system, transcript, IELTS/TOEFL certificate, and an English essay (choose one of three topics, ≤400 words) |
| Essay topics | A. Aspect of common law tradition; B. How the Chinese government could improve environmental protection; C. Meaning of Canada’s parliamentary system deriving from the Westminster tradition |
| Interview | April 10 for those passing preliminary review |
| Submission | Email electronic copies to jjyao@stu.edu.cn (subject “Name-McGill Exchange Programme”) and deliver paper copies to the Dean’s Office (Law School, Room 211, Wenbei) by April 7 during office hours 8:30–12:00 and 14:30–17:30 |
| Contact | Ms. Yao Jinjin, phone 86503906, email jjyao@stu.edu.cn |

Students who previously interviewed for HKU, Queen’s University Belfast or Yonsei may also apply but may only join one program if successful.
