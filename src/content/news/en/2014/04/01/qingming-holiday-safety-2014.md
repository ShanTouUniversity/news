Ahead of the Qingming holiday, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office issued safety reminders. The holiday falls on April 5, 2014, with Monday, April 7, designated as a compensatory day off. Students are advised to make safety preparations based on weather and travel conditions.

## Key reminders

As Guangdong enters a period of more frequent severe convective weather, students should check weather warnings before traveling for tomb-sweeping or outdoor activities and guard against lightning, hail, short-term strong winds, heavy rain, landslides, flash floods and waterlogging. During severe weather, they should move into safe buildings and stay away from trees, billboards and temporary structures. The office also encouraged civilized tomb-sweeping without carrying flammable items into the mountains.

Students were reminded to obey traffic regulations and avoid unlicensed or overloaded vehicles. They should not attempt to fight forest fires on their own in the Sangpu Mountain area, but report fires promptly. Travelers should use regulated travel services, protect their valuables and travel with companions where possible.

Students staying on campus must follow dormitory and campus safety rules. Open flames and high-power appliances are prohibited in dormitories, and fire exits must remain clear. Students should also stay away from reservoirs and other dangerous waters. Outdoor activities should be conducted safely, with no open flames or barbecues in fire-prohibited areas.
