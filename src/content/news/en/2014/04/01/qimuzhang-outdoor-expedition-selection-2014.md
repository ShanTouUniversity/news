Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced that 24 people passed selection for an intermediate outdoor expedition to Yanzi Rock at Qimuzhang in Heyuan. The group comprises 20 students and four teachers. The expedition is scheduled to run from the afternoon of April 4 through April 7.

Selected participants are required to attend a briefing from 2:30 to 4 p.m. on April 2 in room 307 of Building E. The briefing will cover the itinerary, route, preparation and insurance. The original notice attachment lists selected participants; student identities are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
