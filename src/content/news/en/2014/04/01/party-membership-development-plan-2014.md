Under a plan issued by the Shantou Municipal Working Committee, Shantou University’s target for party membership development in 2014 is 250. The university’s Party Organization and United Front Department distributed the plan among its party committees and directly affiliated branches based on recent development figures, calling on them to manage growth, prioritize quality and follow standardized procedures.
