Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the Guangdong Institute of Education Research’s 2014 project application call. Projects are divided into general and key categories, with topics spanning basic, vocational, higher and private education, educational assessment, and education communications and publishing. Applicants must fund their own projects.

Applicants must hold a mid-level or higher professional rank; key-project applicants must hold a senior rank. Any previously led project funded by the institute must have been completed, and each applicant may submit only one proposal. STU’s quota is one key project and three general projects.

Applications must be emailed to the Research Office for a formal review by April 23, with four printed copies of the final version due by April 28. The topic list and application form are in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
