Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) will offer introductory classes in Photoshop image editing, Illustrator graphic design, CAD and PowerPoint for undergraduates at the main campus. Each class has eight places and will meet in SLC 105. Participants must bring a computer with the relevant software installed.

Classes run from the seventh through the tenth week of the semester: Photoshop on Fridays from 3 to 5 p.m.; Illustrator on Tuesdays from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m.; CAD on Tuesdays from 3 to 5 p.m.; and PowerPoint on Saturdays from 3 to 5 p.m. Registration is through the online booking center by noon on April 4. Students need to book only the first session; places are allocated first come, first served. The roster was scheduled for release on the university intranet that day.
