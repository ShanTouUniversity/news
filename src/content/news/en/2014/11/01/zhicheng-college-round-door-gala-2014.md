It is reported that the 2014 "Round Door" College Evening Gala of Zhicheng College, themed "Mao Lin Xiu Zhu," will be held at the STU University Auditorium on the evening of Saturday, November 1, at 7:30 PM.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | "Mao Lin Xiu Zhu" 2014 "Round Door" College Day |
| Time | 19:30, Saturday, November 1, 2014 |
| Venue | STU University Auditorium |
| Participants | Resident students, college tutors, guests, and alumni of Zhicheng College |

It is reported that resident students will enter and take their seats 15 minutes in advance under the guidance of area tutors and league branch secretaries. Seats for college tutors, guests, and alumni are arranged in rows 6, 7, and 8.

The gala aims to strengthen the bond among college members and foster a warm and harmonious college culture.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
