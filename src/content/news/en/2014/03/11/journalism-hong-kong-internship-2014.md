STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication is accepting applications for its 2014 Hong Kong summer internship program. The school plans to send outstanding third-year students to Hong Kong for **seven weeks**, from early July to late August. Applicants must speak fluent Cantonese, have achieved the equivalent of CET-6 English proficiency and be able to cover living costs of approximately RMB 6,500. The school will arrange accommodation and workplace guidance in Hong Kong.

Applications are open from **March 11 through 5 p.m. on March 17**. Applicants must submit printed personal materials to the school assistant. A selection committee will conduct interviews, tentatively scheduled for the afternoon of March 23. Students applying for a Taiwan internship may not apply for the Hong Kong internship at the same time.
