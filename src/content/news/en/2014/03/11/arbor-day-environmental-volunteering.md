STU’s Youth League Committee and the Green Ribbon environmental volunteer team of the Youth Volunteer Association will hold an Arbor Day activity, themed **“Vigorous Growth, A New Start,” from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 12**. Volunteers will work in groups to plant trees in the open area near the East Gate substation or plant grass in the flower beds at the main gate.

The organizers planned to recruit **40 volunteers**, including 20 for tree planting and 20 for grass planting. Students interested in joining had to download the application form and send it to the designated email address by **11:00 p.m. on March 11**. Admission results would be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee, Resource Management Office and STU Youth Volunteer Association)
>
> ⚠️ The application form and activity attachments are available on the OA system.
