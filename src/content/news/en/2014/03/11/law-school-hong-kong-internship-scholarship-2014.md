STU Law School has opened applications for its 2014 Hong Kong Summer Internship Scholarship Program. Eligible applicants include Law students in the 2011 and 2012 cohorts and Public Administration or Administration students in the 2011 cohort. Applications close on **March 18**.

A selection panel of Law School teachers will assess the applications. The preliminary review accounts for 50% of the score and covers academic performance, English writing, community service and student activities. The interview accounts for the other 50% and covers professional knowledge, spoken English, communication, confidence and conduct. Interviews for students who pass the preliminary review are planned for late March, with outstanding students from both departments to be selected for the program.
