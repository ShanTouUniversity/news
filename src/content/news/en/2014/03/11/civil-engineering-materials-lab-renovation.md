STU’s Infrastructure Office began renovating the civil engineering materials laboratory in the School of Engineering on **March 11, 2014**. The project, undertaken by Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd., covers interior repairs in rooms Gongbei 201, 202 and 205 and the addition of outdoor laboratory support facilities. The contract value is **RMB 96,800**, and work is scheduled for completion by **April 14**.

The construction may cause noise, vibration and dust. The university asked faculty, students and other pedestrians to take care around the site, and advised people working nearby to avoid lingering in the construction area. Lu Chunxiong is the project lead and Chen Xiwen is the site management engineer.
