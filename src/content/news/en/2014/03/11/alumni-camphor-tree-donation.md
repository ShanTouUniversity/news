According to the Alumni Affairs Office, alumni Guo Shijie and Lin Chang will hold a camphor-tree donation ceremony at **9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, March 12, 2014**, in front of Building E. The ceremony, held around Arbor Day, is open to faculty members and students across the university.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Alumni Affairs Office)
