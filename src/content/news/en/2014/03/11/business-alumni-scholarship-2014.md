STU Business School has opened applications for its 2014 Alumni Scholarship. The program plans to support **40** full-time Business School undergraduates with **RMB 2,500 each**. The scholarship was initiated in 2012 by alumni including Guo Shijie, Lin Chang and Lin Zelian, with RMB 200,000 donated as its initial fund, and is intended for students who demonstrate academic merit and financial need.

Applicants must comply with university rules, demonstrate good character and overall qualities, have a GPA of at least 3.0 for the assessment year, and have no more than one failed course. They should also take part in public service and social practice.

Applicants must complete, print and sign the application form and submit it to the Business School Alumni Office, West Liberal Arts Building Room 202, by **5 p.m. on March 21**. The electronic version should be sent to `o_sxyh@stu.edu.cn`.
