STU has published the schedule for the **spring 2014 Shuguang Volunteer Teaching Service-Learning Course**. The course consists of four stages: a service-site visit on **March 16**; training sessions on **March 19, March 26, April 2 and April 9**; social-practice activities from **March 16 to May 25**; and a final review and sharing session on **May 28**.

Exact class times and locations are provided in the course attachment. Participants were asked to prepare accordingly and attend on time.
