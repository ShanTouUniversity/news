STU’s Youth League Committee has opened recommendations for the **2013 Shantou May Fourth awards**, inviting recommendations for individuals and organizations from school and college Youth League units. The five award categories are outstanding grassroots Youth League secretaries, outstanding Youth League cadres, outstanding Youth League members, May Fourth Red-flag Youth League committees, and May Fourth Red-flag Youth League branches or general branches.

Units were asked to submit recommended candidates and supporting materials to the university Youth League Committee office by **March 19**, with electronic copies sent to the designated university email address. Detailed criteria and document requirements are provided in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
>
> ⚠️ Recommendation forms and award-related attachments are available on the OA system.
