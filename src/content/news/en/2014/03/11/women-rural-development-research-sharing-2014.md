STU’s Women’s Studies Center will hold a sharing session on the “China Women’s Participation in Rural Development” special research program from **3 to 5 p.m. on Wednesday, March 12** in Library Seminar Room 30B. The session will feature Feng Yuan, a council member of the China Women’s Studies Association and a professor at STU’s Women’s Studies Center, and is open to the program’s 2013 project teams and interested faculty and students.

The program will include a discussion of integrating gender awareness into research, presentations and reviews of research plans, and reminders about special research procedures. The session is hosted by the STU Women’s Studies Center with support from the Office of Research. Inquiries can be sent to `cws@stu.edu.cn`.
