The School of Cheung Kong Art and Design will host a lecture titled **“Seeking Creativity, Hong Kong Creativity”** at **7:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 12** in the school’s first-floor lecture hall. Professor and PhD holder Kan Tai-keung will be the speaker. The event is open to faculty members and students across the university.
