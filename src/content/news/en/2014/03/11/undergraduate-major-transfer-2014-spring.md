STU Academic Affairs has launched the Spring 2014 undergraduate major-transfer process under the university’s revised student-status regulations. Students in the **2012 and 2013 cohorts** may apply this semester.

Applicants must obtain approval from their current department and school and submit an approved transcript to the prospective department. After the receiving department approves an application, the student may take its assessment. Academic Affairs will review the materials, publish the proposed approvals for one week and announce the final decisions by Week 12; approved students will begin studying in the new major the following semester. Restrictions in the admissions rules continue to apply to cross-category transfers involving sports, music and fine arts programs.

By **March 20**, each program must report the number of students it can accept from other majors and its requirements to the Academic Affairs Student Affairs Section, with the electronic version sent to `yd@stu.edu.cn`. The requirements will then be published for students.
