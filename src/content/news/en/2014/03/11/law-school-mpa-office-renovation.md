STU’s Infrastructure Office began renovating the Law School MPA office on **March 11, 2014**. The project, undertaken by Shantou Yueli Architectural Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd., covers the interior of Room 215 in the Audiovisual Education Building and the outdoor passage. The contract value is **RMB 29,100**, with completion scheduled by **April 9**.

The work may cause noise, vibration and dust. The university reminded pedestrians to take care around the site and advised people working nearby to avoid lingering there. Lu Chunxiong is the project lead and Chen Jinwu is the site administrator.
