Renovation of the STU central laboratory’s microbiology laboratory began on **March 11**. The project was awarded to Shantou Shenghang Construction Co. and covers the comprehensive renovation of Room 612 in the Science and Technology Building, including the installation of a ventilation system. The contract price is **RMB 46,800**, and completion is scheduled by **April 9**.

The work may cause noise, vibration and dust. The university asks pedestrians to take care near the site and especially asks staff working nearby to avoid lingering around the construction area.
