STU’s university choir has completed auditions for new members, with **12 students** selected to join. The Arts Education Center said the selection considered the choir’s format and needs as well as the applicants’ vocal qualities.

Selected students were asked to meet at **1:50 p.m. on Wednesday, March 12**, in the university choir rehearsal room on the first floor of the Arts Education Center, which was also the audition venue. The names of the selected students are included in the original OA attachment.
