It is reported that Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) has published the peer tutor interview schedule based on registrations received by March 3. Interviews will take place at SLC on Wednesday afternoon, March 5, with applicants attending the room assigned to their school and time slot.

## Interview Sessions

| Schools | Time | Room |
|---------|------|------|
| Schools of Science and Business | 2:30 p.m. | SLC202 |
| School of Engineering | 3:00 p.m. | SLC201 |
| School of Liberal Arts | 3:30 p.m. | SLC203 |
| Schools of Journalism and Arts | 4:00 p.m. | SLC204 |
| School of Law | 5:00 p.m. | SLC202 |

The original notice includes the individual interview list; names are omitted here to protect student privacy. Students who registered through the work-study system but do not appear on the list may contact the responsible liaison for verification.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The individual interview list is available on the OA system; student personal information is not reproduced here.
