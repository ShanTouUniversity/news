It is reported that the approved demolition plan for old buildings within the site of Shantou University's new residential college will enter construction. The university planned to begin demolishing 13 buildings in Plots A and B of the west campus on March 10.

Units and residents must remove equipment, furniture and other items from the demolition area by March 10. Before demolition, the relevant property and security offices will clear every room, cut water, electricity and network lines, and close stairway entrances. The contractor will install barriers and warning lines and assign guards; non-construction personnel must not enter the site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Security Office)
