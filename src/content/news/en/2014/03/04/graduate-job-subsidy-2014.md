It is reported that, in accordance with the Notice on Distributing Job-Seeking Subsidies for University Graduates (Yue Ren She Han [2013] No. 3102) issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security, the Department of Education and the Department of Finance, Shantou University has launched its 2014 graduate job-seeking subsidy program. Eligible students may voluntarily apply between **March 4 and March 31**.

## Who Qualifies and How Much

The subsidy targets university graduates who, within their graduation year (the calendar year of graduation, January 1 to December 31), have a genuine desire to work, are actively seeking employment, and come from families covered by the urban or rural minimum living allowance. The subsidy is paid as a **one-off payment of 500 yuan per person**.

## How to Apply

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Application period | March 4–31, 2014 |
| Required materials | Guangdong Application Form for Job-Seeking Subsidies for Graduates from Minimum Living Allowance Families; copies of the family's allowance certificate and the applicant's ID card; personal bank account details |
| Submission point | Graduate Employment Guidance Center, Student Affairs Office |
| Disbursement | Issued after document verification and public notice |

The Student Affairs Office reminded eligible graduates to submit all materials to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center before March 31; the subsidy will be distributed after verification and public announcement.
