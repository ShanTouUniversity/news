It is reported that the Shantou University Student Union office will resume duty on Wednesday, March 5. The office is located in Room 724 of Building 7 and will be open from 5:00 to 6:00 p.m. Monday through Friday.

The Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union have also issued guidance on office management and material requests. The guidance is intended to standardize office operations, improve service quality and help student organizations request materials.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ The Office Management and Material Request Guide is available as an attachment on the OA system.
