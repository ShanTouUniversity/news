It is reported that Shantou University's General Office has organized the filing of records from 2013 and asked all units to collect and organize materials promptly and completely. The filing scope includes research institutions and laboratories under each unit. Relevant information must be entered into the archives management system, with electronic files, paper documents and receipt forms provided as required.

## Filing Requirements

Units should ensure the complete collection and accurate description of official documents, plans and summaries, audiovisual materials, physical records and internal publications. Schools, the affiliated high school and the affiliated primary school must also file group photographs of graduating students; units without such photographs may provide individual headshots as required.

All filing work must be completed by April 30, 2014. The university planned to report completion in June. The General Archives Room can be contacted for assistance at 86502878 or 86502880. The archives system is available at http://efms.stu.edu.cn.
