It is reported that the Guangdong Student App Design and Development Competition will take place from March through May 2014. Supported by the Li Ka-shing Foundation's China Women's Project, the competition is hosted by Shantou University's Women's Studies Center and co-organized by the Gender Education Forum of Sun Yat-sen University and the Gender Studies Center of South China University of Technology. It aims to encourage students to use professional knowledge to serve women and communities.

## Participation Schedule

Undergraduates and graduate students may form cross-disciplinary, cross-university teams of three to six members. Projects may focus on legal protection, health care, learning and information services, or economic development. Apps must support Android 4.0 or later and provide usage statistics.

| Date | Activity |
|------|----------|
| March 5, 3:00–4:30 p.m. | Project design briefing at the Lecture Hall of the STU Library |
| March 7–April 2 | Team registration and proposal submission |
| April 3–May 21 | Software development and program submission |
| May 28 | Exhibition and awards ceremony at Shantou University |

Awards include Outstanding Proposal, Cross-University Collaboration, Technical Implementation, Visual Design and Overall Excellence, with prizes of RMB 500, 1,000, 2,000, 2,000 and 5,000 respectively. Registration and proposal materials should be sent to cws@stu.edu.cn. The consultation telephone number is 0754-86502695.
