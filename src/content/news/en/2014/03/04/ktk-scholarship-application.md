It is reported that the KTK Design Award Organizing Committee has established scholarships at the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. Four enrolled undergraduates will be selected, with each recipient receiving RMB 2,500.

Applicants must be first- to third-year art undergraduates who have passed the university's financial-aid assessment and come from financially disadvantaged families. One award is reserved for an outstanding financially disadvantaged member of the KTK organizing committee, while three are for students outside the committee. Applicants with awards at municipal level or above will receive priority. Family finances, conduct, academic performance and compliance with university rules will also be considered.

Applicants must submit the application form and copies of relevant award materials to the School Youth League Committee by 4:00 p.m. on March 20. Professor Cheung Tai-chung, the award-winning designer and former dean of the school, will present the scholarships to the selected recipients.
