It is reported that, in accordance with the university's notice on graduate extracurricular academic and technology projects and the project development schedule, the Graduate School of Shantou University will soon organize final reviews for all projects approved in 2013.

## Submission Requirements

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Who submits | Project leaders of all approved projects |
| Deadline | Before March 7, 2014 |
| Materials | Written closing report (including the supervisor's evaluation and signature) plus the project deliverables |
| Deliverables | Published research papers, business plans, patents, established models, survey reports, proposals, or other achieved or forthcoming project outcomes |
| Where to submit | Room 401, New Administrative Center, to Mr. Wang Fengqi of the Graduate School; an electronic copy should also be sent to fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn |

Notably, projects that fail to submit a closing report as required will be regarded as automatically abandoned, and the additional funding granted to those projects must be recovered.
