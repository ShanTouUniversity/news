Morgan, the British consul general in Guangzhou, will give a public lecture titled “The Next 20 Years of China–UK Relations” from 3 to 4 p.m. on Friday, March 28, in the Library Lecture Hall at Shantou University.

Morgan will first speak in Chinese, then use an English presentation to discuss the future of China–UK relations and answer questions from students and staff. The event is for students and staff who have successfully reserved a place; attendees should follow their original reservation arrangements.
