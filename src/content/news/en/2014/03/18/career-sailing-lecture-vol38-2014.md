The 38th session of Shantou University’s Career Launch Lecture Series will take place from **2:30 to 5 p.m. on March 25** in the New Library Lecture Hall. The session, titled “Unlocking Potential for Career Success,” is open to all STU students and staff.

The speaker will be Zhu Gelin, an international career trainer and specialist in quality education. Zhu is the author of *Unlocking Unlimited Team Potential* and a senior coach at Shenzhen Guiling Culture Communication Co., Ltd. He is also a visiting professor at the China Academy of Management Science’s Senior Economic Forum.
