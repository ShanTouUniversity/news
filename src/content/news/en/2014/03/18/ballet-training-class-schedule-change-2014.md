Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has announced a schedule change for **Ballet Training section 67038**. The class originally scheduled for **periods 3–4 on March 25** will be canceled and made up during **periods 8–9 on March 19**.

The makeup class will take place in Dance Rehearsal Hall STU102. Relevant schools and enrolled students should follow the revised schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
