Shantou University Library will hold a database training session from **7:30 to 9 p.m. on March 20** at the Subject Services Department on the second floor of the library. Titled “Combining Critical Thinking and the Lexis Database for Better Graduation Papers,” the session will be presented by Wang Juesui (Kent Wang), a LexisNexis product application consultant. Faculty and students are welcome.

The session will explain how critical thinking supports learning and academic writing, and how critical questions can be used to analyze a paper topic and organize an argument. Using practical paper topics, the presenter will demonstrate Lexis.com and LexisNexis Academic searches for journals, papers, company files, industry reports and legal materials in fields including finance, accounting, business, foreign languages, journalism, business administration, law, medicine and biology.

The training will also cover searches for international news and business information, news in less commonly searched languages, and practical ways to track developments in research fields and emerging topics.
