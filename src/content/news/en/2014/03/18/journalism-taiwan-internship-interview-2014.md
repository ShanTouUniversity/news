STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication has announced the application results for its 2014 summer Taiwan internship and related programs. A total of **46 students** applied. Ten applied for advertising placements; because places were limited, five were shortlisted for interviews based on academic performance. Applicants for writing, photography and exchange programs were all eligible to interview.

The school said that the detailed interview list and schedule were included in the notice attachments, and applicants should follow those arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
