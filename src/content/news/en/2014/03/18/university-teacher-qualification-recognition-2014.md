Following arrangements issued by the Guangdong education authorities, Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has launched the 2014 university teacher qualification certification process. Eligible applicants include full-time teachers currently teaching at universities who meet the requirements of the Teachers Law and the Regulations on Teacher Qualifications and will not have reached the statutory retirement age by **May 15**. Clinical teaching staff at directly affiliated hospitals and long-term contract teachers are also included.

Applicants must first register with their units. Units should compile the required materials and submit them to the Human Resources Office at Room 315 of the New Administration Center by **April 22**; designated electronic materials should be sent to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. The office will arrange physical examinations at hospitals designated by the provincial education authority, with details to be announced separately.

Online registration will run from **April 19 to 22** through the Guangdong Teacher Qualification website (`http://jszg.gdhed.edu.cn`). Applicants must complete and print the application form as required. Units must verify copies, compile the materials and submit them by April 22. Degree-certificate verification may be submitted by May 11 at the latest. After review by the university’s teacher qualification panel, the approved list will be publicized for seven days before being sent to the provincial office for certification and issuance.

For inquiries, call `86502340` or email `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`.
