Lu Yuanliang’s *Feidu* exhibition will be on view from **March 20 to 28** in the front hall, central courtyard and rear courtyard of Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. The opening ceremony is scheduled for **3 p.m. on March 20**.

The exhibition is organized by the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, with academic support from Houshan Forum. Faculty and students are welcome to visit.
