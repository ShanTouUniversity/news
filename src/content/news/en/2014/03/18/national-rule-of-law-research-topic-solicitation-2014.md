Shantou University’s Research Office has circulated a call from the Ministry of Justice for topic proposals for the 2014 rule-of-law and legal-theory research program. The proposals will help compile the ministry’s annual research-topic guide.

The call focuses on major theoretical and practical issues in national rule-of-law development and encourages a combination of applied policy research and basic legal theory. Proposals involving emerging, marginal or interdisciplinary fields are also welcome. There is no limit on the number of suggestions. Applicants may complete the designated proposal form or submit a topic title without a full rationale.

Proposals are due by **May 20**. They may be submitted through an institution’s research administration office or sent directly by mail, fax or email. The Ministry of Justice Institute of Judicial Research’s research administration office has provided contact details and submission information.
