Shantou University’s Engineering School will select **10 students** from across the university for the 2014 Engineering for Developing Communities (EDC) summer course jointly organized by STU and the Technion–Israel Institute of Technology. The four-week course is scheduled for **July 20 to August 15** and includes one week of fieldwork in a developing region. Classes will be taught in English.

The Technion will send 10 Israeli students and 20 other international students to STU, where the selected STU students will study alongside them. Faculty from the Technion, U.S. universities and STU will teach the course. Students who complete the assessment will receive four credits and a joint course certificate from STU and the Technion. The course focuses on engineering needs in developing regions and trains students to assess real-world problems and develop sustainable solutions.

The course is open to STU undergraduates and graduate students, with priority given to Engineering School students. Students from the Schools of Liberal Arts, Journalism and Communication, Law, and Medicine are also encouraged to apply, with at least one student to be selected from each participating school. Applicants should have strong English communication and writing skills, social-practice experience, good academic performance and teamwork skills. The fee is RMB 1,200 per student, with the remaining cost subsidized by STU.

All application materials must be prepared in English and emailed to `10yzhang3@stu.edu.cn` by **5 p.m. on March 28**. The Engineering School will review written applications and interview shortlisted candidates. Contact Teacher Ni at `86502153`.
