It is reported that Shantou University's Business School has completed the selection process for its 2014 summer course project with McGill University's Desautels Faculty of Management. A total of 25 students were selected after preliminary review, a written test, and interviews.

The project is jointly offered by the two institutions, and the selected students will take part in the 2014 summer course. The Business School has released the selection list; student names are not reproduced here.
