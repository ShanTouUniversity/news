It is reported that the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association, together with volunteer organizations from schools and colleges across the university, will hold the "Love for the Community" service event in Shipaotai Subdistrict, Jinping District, Shantou, on March 29. The event is jointly organized by the STU Communist Youth League Committee, the Youth Volunteers Association, and the Shipaotai Subdistrict Office.

## Services

Volunteers will provide medical consultations, blood-pressure and blood-glucose checks, legal advice, first-aid education, fire-safety and healthy-living information, minor repairs, eyeglass cleaning, aerobics, paper folding, and Chinese-knot making. Home visits, cleaning, and material assistance will also be offered to residents in need.

The event will run from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. on March 29. Volunteers were asked to check in at the STU Motto Monument at 7:50 a.m. before departing at 8:00. Volunteer organizations from the College of Liberal Arts, College of Engineering, Business School, Law School, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Medical College, Zhicheng College, and the Graduate School will help deliver the program.

Organizers reminded volunteers to protect their personal safety and belongings during the service.
