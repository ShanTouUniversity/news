It is reported that Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has issued a notice on a student's misconduct off campus. On January 1, 2014, a student in the 2011 Applied Physics cohort of the College of Science took clothing containing cash and other belongings at an off-campus venue. The student later acknowledged the conduct, returned the belongings, and received the owner's forgiveness.

Under the relevant provisions of STU's student disciplinary rules, the university decided to issue the student a serious warning. The Student Affairs Office urged students to learn from the case and help maintain a proper academic and residential environment.
