It is reported that the production team of The Voice of China is filming a musical at Shantou University. To support the filming, the road near the hillside graffiti wall will close temporarily during the following periods. Vehicles should use alternate routes.

| Date | Closure period |
| --- | --- |
| March 28 | 10:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. |
| March 31 | 6:30 a.m.–noon |
| April 6 | 11:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. |

The university advised students, staff, and campus vehicles to follow on-site traffic directions and use other routes during the closures.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
