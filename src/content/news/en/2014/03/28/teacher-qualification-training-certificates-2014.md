It is reported that the Guangdong Education Department Teacher Continuing Education Guidance Center has reviewed the results of Shantou University's fall 2013 pedagogy and psychology training for non-teaching students. Certificates for students who passed the training have been delivered to the university.

Eligible students may collect their certificates at the Academic Affairs Office service window on the third floor of the New Administration Center. A valid identity document is required; representatives collecting a certificate must present valid identity documents for both themselves and the certificate holder. The list of eligible students is available in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The eligible-student list and related attachment contain student information and are available on the OA system.
