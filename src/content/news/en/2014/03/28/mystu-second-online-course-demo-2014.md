It is reported that Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office and Network Center will hold the second round of demonstrations for the Mystu online-course system. The sessions will introduce the system's functions and use to teaching staff from the university's academic units and collect their feedback.

## Demonstration Schedule

| Language | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese | Wednesday, April 2, 4:00–5:30 p.m. | Room 201, Administration Center |
| English | Thursday, April 3, 4:00–5:30 p.m. | Room 222, Administration Center |

The Mystu project team will demonstrate the online-course system and has invited teaching staff from the university's academic units to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office, Network Center)
