It is reported that Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has issued a notice concerning an examination misconduct case. During the Organic Chemistry II examination on January 13, 2014, two students in the 2012 Applied Chemistry cohort of the College of Science were found copying answers.

Under the national regulations on student administration and STU's rules on student conduct and academic status, both students received warning penalties and a score of zero for the course. The notice also explained that an examination misconduct penalty may affect eligibility for a bachelor's degree. Students whose subsequent performance meets the stated requirements may apply for a review through the prescribed procedure.

The Student Affairs Office urged students to observe examination rules and help maintain a proper learning environment.
