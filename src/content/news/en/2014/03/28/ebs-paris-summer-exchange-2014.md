It is reported that the Business School, which signed a cooperation agreement with the European Business School Paris (EBS) in December 2013, has now opened applications for its 2014 summer exchange program in Paris.

Selected students will study at EBS for four weeks from July 2 to July 25, 2014, completing three undergraduate courses — European Management, International Human Resource Management, and French Cultural Studies — and participating in university-organized excursions, earning a total of eight credits.

## Eligibility

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Eligible Applicants | Business School sophomores and above, full-time postgraduates, and MBA students; priority for those who have not previously joined university or school exchange programs |
| Requirements | Strong English proficiency in listening, speaking, reading, and writing; excellent academic performance and conduct; outgoing personality with active participation in public affairs and volunteering; ability to promote Shantou University externally |

## Application Materials

- STU Business School Outbound Exchange Application Form
- Proof of English proficiency (e.g., IELTS, TOEFL, CET-4/6)
- Academic transcript in English
- English essay (300–500 words, printed on A4), on motivations for joining the program and overseas study plans
- Photocopy of valid passport information page

## Selection and Application Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Selection | Review of written materials by a school selection panel with comprehensive evaluation; no quota limit |
| Deadline | 12:00 noon, April 21, 2014 |
| Submission | Hard copy or electronic documents to the School's International Programs Office (Wenxi 211); electronic copies to outgoing@stu.edu.cn |
| Contact | Ms. Ye Dongni, 86503382 |

## Fees

EBS charges tuition of 1,850 euros, covering round-trip transport between accommodation and campus, airport transfers, and university-organized activities. Students are responsible for accommodation, round-trip travel, meals, books and materials, insurance, medical care, and administrative fees during the exchange period. Details are available in the attached program brochure.
