It is reported that interviews for Shantou University's 2014 overseas study funding scholarship will be held on April 2. The interview will consist of an English self-introduction, a leaderless group discussion, and questions from the panel, assessing the shortlisted students' overall performance.

## Interview Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Interview | Wednesday, April 2, 2014, 3:00–6:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Room 221, Administration Center |
| Stages | English self-introduction, leaderless group discussion, panel questions |
| Briefing | March 31, 10:00 a.m., Room 117, Administration Center |

Shortlisted students were asked to attend the briefing on March 31 to learn about the arrangements and draw their interview order. Each English self-introduction is limited to one minute. The group discussion will last 10 minutes, preceded by two minutes of preparation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The shortlist and interview attachments contain student information and are available on the OA system.
