It is reported that the Shantou University Bidding Leadership Group has completed its evaluation of the renovation project for Dormitory I. Shantou Yueli Construction and Decoration Co., Ltd. was selected as the winning contractor at a bid price of RMB 58,087.08.

## Public Notice

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Dormitory I renovation |
| Winning contractor | Shantou Yueli Construction and Decoration Co., Ltd. |
| Contract amount | RMB 58,087.08 |
| Notice period | March 28–31, 2014 |
| Procuring office | STU Infrastructure Office |

Objections to the evaluation result may be submitted to the STU Supervision and Audit Office. The Infrastructure Office is responsible for bidding and project matters.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office, Infrastructure Office)
