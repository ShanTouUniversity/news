It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts issued a notice on March 28, 2014, to launch the Shantou University–McGill University semester exchange, selecting two undergraduates for a one-semester study in Canada in the fall 2014 term, supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | STU–McGill Semester Exchange |
| Places | 2 |
| Term | Fall 2014, one semester |
| Scholarship | Up to 22,189 CAD (tuition 8,929, living 5,410, accommodation 4,750, airfare 3,100) |
| Other costs | Students cover overseas insurance and other expenses |
| Organizer | College of Liberal Arts |

## Eligibility

Open to full-time undergraduates of the College of Liberal Arts, Classes of 2011–2013. Applicants should have strong English proficiency with IELTS 6.5 (no band below 6.0) or TOEFL 90 (no band below 21), rank in the top 30% of the class by overall GPA, demonstrate good character and communication skills, and be willing to promote Shantou University abroad. Those who have not previously joined university or college exchange programs are preferred under equal conditions.
