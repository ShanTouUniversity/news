It is reported that the English Language Center will hold an IELTS Preparation Workshop on Listening on April 2, 2014.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | IELTS Listening — Developing Listening Skills |
| Time | 16:00–18:00, April 2, 2014 |
| Venue | CILL |
| Registration | Seats are limited; reserve at https://ieltslistening1.eventbrite.com and bring your ticket |
| Organizer | English Language Center |

The center notes that those who have registered should attend as scheduled, and those unsure about availability should not reserve.
