It is reported that Shantou University's Toward the Ocean public service course will hold an outdoor program and a field visit to the Suae Bay mangrove reserve on March 29. The event will focus on team building and a survey of the current state of the mangrove ecosystem.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Check-in | Saturday, March 29, 2014, 8:50 a.m. |
| Meeting point | STU Motto Monument |
| Morning program | Team building and outdoor training on campus, 9:00 a.m.–noon |
| Afternoon program | Visit to the Suae Bay mangrove reserve from 1:30 p.m. |
| Organizers | Toward the Ocean course instructors and Snow Leopard Outdoor Sports Association |

Students will have lunch on campus before gathering at the Motto Monument to take a bus to the reserve. Participants were asked to bring drinking water, food, and protective equipment, follow reserve rules, avoid picking mangrove plants or disturbing birds, and keep the area clean. The activity will be canceled and rescheduled if extreme weather such as rain occurs.

The Suae Bay mangrove reserve is located in the Suae Bay wetland south of Nanbin Road in Shantou. It is an important mangrove habitat and stopover for migratory birds. The field visit is intended to deepen students' understanding of the area's ecological conditions.
