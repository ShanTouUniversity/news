It is reported that 120 students and staff members took part in the first fitness test for Shantou University's Qimuzhang Yanziyan intermediate outdoor program. Thirty-six students and four staff members qualified for the second round, a wilderness-survival workshop scheduled for March 30.

## Second-Round Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date and time | Sunday, March 30, 2014, 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. |
| Check-in | 7:55 a.m. |
| Meeting point | STU Motto Monument |
| Venue | Shantou University campus |
| Format | Six teams competing in wilderness-skills challenges, scored by coaches |
| Final intake | 20 students and four staff members, 24 participants in total |

The workshop will assess wilderness skills, team building, and cooperation. All members of the top two teams, 14 people in total, will qualify; two students will be selected from each of the third and fourth teams, and one student from each of the fifth and sixth teams.

Participants were advised to wear suitable sports clothing and shoes, pay a RMB 5 food fee, bring only a reasonable amount of drinking water, and leave valuables at home.
