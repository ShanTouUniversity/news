The Business School at Shantou University has announced the spring schedule for its Tea Country public-service course, “Promoting Ecologically Sustainable Production Models.” The class taught by Zeng Fanqi will meet six times in Room E-202-1:

| Dates | Class periods |
| --- | --- |
| March 19, March 26, April 2 and April 9 | Periods 6–8 |
| April 23 and May 21 | Periods 6–7 |

The schedule covers Weeks 4 through 13 and is intended for students enrolled in the public-service course.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School Academic Affairs Office)
