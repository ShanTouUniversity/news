Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has published the bid result for the expert-apartment room renovation project. Following the review and recommendation of the university’s tendering leadership group, Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation was selected as the winning bidder at **RMB 116,493.83**.

The result is being publicized from **March 14 to March 17**. Tender-related inquiries can be directed to the Infrastructure Office at 0754-86502490. Complaints may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346.
