The College of Engineering at Shantou University will hold the campus selection for the 6th National Mechanical Innovation Design Competition from **2:00 to 6:00 p.m. on March 19** in Room E-203. Outstanding teams will represent the university in the Guangdong provincial selection.

The 2014 competition theme is “Dream Classroom,” with entries focused on designing and making classroom equipment and teaching aids. The selection emphasizes mechanical innovation, manufacturing skills, hands-on practice, engineering management and teamwork. Participants must complete a physical prototype and bring it to the campus event. Electronic registration forms are due at `11yhwei@stu.edu.cn` by **10:00 p.m. on March 18**.
