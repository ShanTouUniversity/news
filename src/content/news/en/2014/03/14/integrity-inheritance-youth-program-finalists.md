A Shantou University team formed by students Chen YP and Huang XY has reached the final of the Hong Kong Independent Commission Against Corruption’s Integrity Inheritance Youth Program 2013/14 team competition. The team was among 21 finalists selected from 269 entries worldwide; only two teams from mainland China reached the final.

The STU team will travel to Hong Kong for the final from **April 24 to 27**. The program website is [www.me.icac.hk/new/yip/index.html](http://www.me.icac.hk/new/yip/index.html).
