The opening lecture of Shantou University’s 2014 General Education Forum will be held at **7:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 19**, in the Library Lecture Hall. Professor Wu Guosheng of Peking University’s Department of Philosophy will speak on “What Is Science?” and discuss scientific thinking and the relationships between science, philosophy and the humanities.

Professor Wu is a doctoral supervisor at Peking University and serves as director of its Center for Science, Society and Development, Center for the History and Philosophy of Science, and Center for Science Communication. His research focuses on the history and philosophy of science and technology, as well as science communication and education.

The lecture is co-organized by the Institute of Higher Education and the Christian Studies Center of the College of Liberal Arts.
