Selected sessions of Shantou University Social Science course section **67019**, “Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics,” have been suspended. The classes scheduled for **March 18 and March 20** in Room E-306 will not take place; make-up sessions will be announced later.
