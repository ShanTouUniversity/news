Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold an IELTS reading workshop from **4:00 to 6:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 19**, at CILL. The session will focus on developing IELTS reading skills.

Seats are limited. Interested participants may reserve a place through the [IELTS Reading 1 event page](https://ieltsreading1.eventbrite.com). Those who register should attend unless they have a special reason; students who are unsure whether they can attend should not register.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Center)
