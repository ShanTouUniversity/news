The Guangdong Education Department will launch a logo-design campaign for its Student-Aid Policy Outreach Campaign across universities in the province. The initiative aims to strengthen public communication about student-aid policies and establish a recognizable campaign identity. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has circulated the call to interested students.

Entries must be submitted to the Student Aid Management Center in Room 106 of the Administrative Center by **April 10**. Electronic copies should be sent to `zjliu@stu.edu.cn`. Detailed design requirements and judging arrangements follow the provincial notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs)
>
> ⚠️ The design requirements, judging rules and campaign attachments are available in the original OA notice.
