It is reported that the 2nd university leadership meeting of 2014 reviewed staffing arrangements at the Academic Affairs Office on March 6. STU announced on March 14 that Mei Zhiping had been appointed deputy director of the Academic Affairs Office for a six-month probationary period.

Under the same decision, Du Hong is no longer deputy director of the Academic Affairs Office. The appointment follows the university's earlier publicity announcement.
