It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University will hold a briefing on its international cooperation programs from 3:00 pm to 4:00 pm on Sunday, March 16, in Room E-101. The session is open to all students of the college, with on-site consultations available.

The briefing will be given by Prof. Fan Zhen, director of the college's Cooperation Programs Office and head of the Department of Electronic Engineering. Four international cooperation and exchange programs will be introduced:

| Partner Institution | Program Type |
| --- | --- |
| University of Michigan-Dearborn (USA) | Joint education program |
| University West (Sweden) | Exchange program |
| University of Calgary (Canada) | Exchange program |
| University of Canterbury (New Zealand) | Exchange program |

According to the notice, Prof. Fan holds a doctorate from Michigan State University and has previously served as an assistant professor and associate professor at the Technical University of Denmark. He is a senior member of IEEE and has been selected for the "Young Thousand Talents Plan" of the Organization Department of the CPC Central Committee as well as Guangdong Province's "Yangfan Plan" for urgently needed talent.
