It is reported that the 2nd meeting of the Standing Committee of the CPC Shantou University Committee in 2014 reviewed appointments at the Medical School. In a document issued on March 14, STU appointed He Ping as director of the Medical School Academic Affairs Office for a six-month probationary period, and appointed Lin Dajing concurrently as director of the Medical School Supervision, Audit and Inspection Office.

Yang Mianhua is no longer assistant dean of the Medical School or director of its Academic Affairs Office. The decisions follow the university's earlier publicity announcements concerning the appointments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Committee)
