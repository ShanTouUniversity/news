Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has commissioned the Shantou branch of Guangdong Runmin Jian’an Engineering Co., Ltd. to upgrade power-distribution facilities in the west campus. Work began on **March 14** and is scheduled for completion by **April 17**, with a contract value of **RMB 534,200**.

The project includes electrical equipment in a new west-campus substation and outdoor power cables connecting the west pump-house substation to the west substation, 36-unit staff housing and Buildings B13–B16. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction. Pedestrians should take care and avoid lingering near the work area.

The project manager is Cai Weizhong and the site engineer is Chen Zidan. Construction inquiries can be handled through the Infrastructure Office.
