It is reported that the 2nd university leadership meeting of 2014 reviewed the appointment of an intermediate-level management position on March 6. The STU Personnel Office announced on March 14 that Wang Bin had been appointed to the position for a six-month probationary period.

The appointment follows the university's earlier publicity announcement concerning the position.
