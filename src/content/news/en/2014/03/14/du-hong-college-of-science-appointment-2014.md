It is reported that the 2nd university leadership meeting of 2014 reviewed management appointments at the College of Science on March 6. STU announced on March 14 that Du Hong had been appointed deputy dean of the College of Science for a six-month probationary period.

Under the same decision, Li Yuanyou is no longer deputy dean of the College of Science. The appointment follows the university's earlier publicity announcement.
