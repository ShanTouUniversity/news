Demolition of old buildings within Shantou University’s Phase I Accommodation College construction project is scheduled to begin on **March 15** and finish in mid-April. Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. will carry out the work across two areas, including the former Post and Telecommunications Building, Infrastructure Building, former STU store, Mutual Learning Center and several staff-residence buildings.

The site will be enclosed with temporary panels and safety nets. Drop-off arrangements for affiliated middle- and primary-school vehicles will also change: vehicles entering through the new gate should use the plaza in front of the Multi-disciplinary Center, while vehicles entering through the west gate should use the temporary parking area west of the West District pump house. Parking will be prohibited on the road between the former Post and Telecommunications Building and the West District pump house, and affected pedestrians should follow the posted detours.

Pedestrians and vehicles should slow down and stay away from the site unless authorized. The construction office can be reached at 86503890, 2332 or 2890.
