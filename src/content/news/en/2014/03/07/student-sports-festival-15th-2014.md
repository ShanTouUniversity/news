It is reported that the Sports Department issued a notice on March 7, 2014, that the 15th Student Sports Festival of Shantou University will be held from March 22 to April 27 on the university’s sports grounds.

The festival aims to enrich campus sports culture, promote mass sports and contribute to competitive sports development. It will feature 12 events: tug-of-war, 20×50m relay, 10×800m relay, roller skating, shuttlecock, basketball, volleyball, football, table tennis, badminton, tennis and martial arts.

The general regulations, individual event regulations and entry forms have been distributed with the notice (see OA attachments). Colleges and residential colleges are asked to follow the regulations and actively organize teams to participate.

> ⚠️ Regulations and entry forms are available on the OA system.
