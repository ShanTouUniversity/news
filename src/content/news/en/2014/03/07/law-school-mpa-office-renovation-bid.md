It is reported that the STU tendering and bidding leadership group completed its evaluation of the renovation project for the Law School MPA Office. Shantou Yueli Architectural Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. was recommended as the winning contractor, with a bid amount of **RMB 29,075.13**.

The result was open for public comment from **March 7 to March 10**. The project owner is the STU Infrastructure Office. Contacts are Yao Yijia and Qiu Minna at (0754) 86502490, with fax at (0754) 86502314. Complaints may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office at (0754) 86502346, with fax at (0754) 86500536.
