It is reported that STU’s Network and Information Center will remove network equipment in the west-campus demolition area and end network service there from **March 8**, following a notice from the university’s Resource Management Office. The center asked affected users to inform one another.

The center can be reached at 86503400 (campus extension 883400), 13592895188 (campus extension 665188), or stunic@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
