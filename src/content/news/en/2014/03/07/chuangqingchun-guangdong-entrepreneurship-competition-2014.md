It is reported that preparations for the 2014 Chuangqingchun Guangdong Student Entrepreneurship Competition have begun, with STU inviting students to submit entries for the university selection. The provincial competition includes the ninth Challenge Cup Guangdong Student Entrepreneurship Plan Competition, the Entrepreneurship Practice Challenge and the Public Welfare Entrepreneurship Competition. STU plans to hold its campus selection in late March.

## Competition Tracks

The entrepreneurship-plan track is open to all enrolled students and evaluates projects through business-plan review and live defense. The practice track is open to enrolled students and graduates within five years whose projects have operated for at least three months. The public-welfare track is open to all enrolled students and focuses on plans and practice for establishing nonprofit social organizations.

Interested students must submit an entry application and an electronic business plan by **March 21**. Students who previously submitted entries to the campus selection for the ninth Challenge Cup Guangdong Student Entrepreneurship Plan Competition do not need to submit again.
