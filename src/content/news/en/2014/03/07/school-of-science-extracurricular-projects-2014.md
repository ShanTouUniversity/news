STU’s School of Science held its 2014 spring extracurricular-project defense on **March 5**. The review panel assessed 11 submitted projects, and the school approved support for 10 of them, with total project funding of RMB 7,500.

The supported organizations will organize their activities under the school’s rules for extracurricular-project applications and management. The school said the project-based approach is intended to contribute to campus cultural development.
