It is reported that STU’s Personnel Office has opened a publicity period for Mei Zhiping’s proposed appointment as deputy director of the Academic Affairs Office, following the university leadership meeting held on March 6.

Comments may be submitted to the Personnel Office by **March 13, 2014**. Organization submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must include the sender’s real name. The office can be reached at 86503390 or rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
