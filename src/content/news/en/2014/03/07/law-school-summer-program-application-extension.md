It is reported that STU Law School has extended the online application period for its summer law program with McGill University because some students had not completed their information after the international-exchange platform opened for the first time. The new deadline is **5:30 p.m. on Tuesday, March 11**.

Students interested in the program should complete their applications promptly through the STU international-exchange platform at sao.stu.edu.cn.
