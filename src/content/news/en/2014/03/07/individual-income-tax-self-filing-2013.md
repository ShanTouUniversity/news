It is reported that STU’s Finance Office has reminded staff whose 2013 annual income exceeded RMB 120,000 to complete individual income tax self-filing by **March 31, 2014**.

Taxpayers may visit the Guangdong Local Taxation Bureau website at http://www.gdltax.gov.cn, enter the individual income tax section and select the self-filing service. After signing in, they should choose the annual income above RMB 120,000 self-filing option to confirm the filing online. The process requires an identification number, login password and verification code.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office)
