STU’s Resource Management Office has published information on **February 2014 utility fees for staff residences**. Meters were read on **March 1 and 2**, following the previous reading on **February 1**. Staff members concerned can log in to the university utility-management system to view their individual charges.

Questions or objections may be raised at the Water and Electricity Center from **March 12 to 14**; the center’s telephone extension is `2489`. After the inquiry period, charges without objections will be deducted by the Finance Office from March salary accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ Utility bills are personal financial information. Individual bills, account details and salary deductions are not reproduced here; staff should use the campus system to check them.
