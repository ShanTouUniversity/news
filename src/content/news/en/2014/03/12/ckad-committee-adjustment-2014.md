It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University issued a notice through the university's office system on March 12, 2014, adjusting the membership of the school's administrative committee.

The adjustment concerns the composition of the committee's members. The specific list of members and details of the adjustment can be found in the original notice published by the school.
