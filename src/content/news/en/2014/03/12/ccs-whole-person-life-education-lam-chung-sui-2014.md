The first lecture in the CCS Whole-Person Life Education series at STU’s School of Liberal Arts will take place at **7:30 p.m. on Monday, March 17** in the Library Lecture Hall. Titled **“The White Butterfly and Nursing Transformation: Dr. Lam Chung-sui’s Journey,”** the lecture will feature Dr. Lam Chung-sui.

Lam previously served as chief nursing executive at the Hong Kong Hospital Authority and has worked for many years in nursing policy, education and professional development. She is also an adjunct professor at the School of Nursing of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, among other roles. The lecture will draw on her experience to discuss the development of nursing in Hong Kong, professional education and management reform.
