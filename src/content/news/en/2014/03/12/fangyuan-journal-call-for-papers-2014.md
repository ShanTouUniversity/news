It is reported that Fangyuan, the campus journal hosted by the Graduate School of Shantou University, is now calling for submissions from all graduate students, welcoming academic papers across disciplines as well as literary works to enrich campus culture and strengthen the academic atmosphere.

Founded in 2006 and rooted in the STU campus with university culture at its core, Fangyuan aims to broaden graduate students' knowledge horizons and deepen understanding across fields. The editorial team invites contributions and suggestions to further improve the journal's quality.

The journal features two major sections. The academic paper section publishes theoretical, practical and forward-looking research from engineering, sciences, humanities and the arts. The literary works section covers a wide range of genres, including long and short stories, poetry, travel notes, campus life essays and artistic works, with columns such as Selected Short and Medium Stories, Essay Garden, Campus Notes, Selected Poetry, Exchange Travel Notes, Book Recommendations, Freshman Guide, Graffiti Wall, STU Snapshots, and Calligraphy and Painting.

For academic submissions, unpublished papers are preferred, though previously published work is also accepted. Papers should be reliable in sources, accurate in data, and demonstrate theoretical, scientific and practical value. Submissions should include title, author name, affiliation, abstract, keywords, main text, references and author biography, typically ranging from 2,000 to 7,000 words with at least six references. There is no word limit for literary works. Authors are asked to provide contact details — name, school, major, phone and email — at the end of the manuscript for editorial communication. Selected contributions will receive remuneration.
