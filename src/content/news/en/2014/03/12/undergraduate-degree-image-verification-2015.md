STU’s Academic Affairs Office has reminded the **2015 undergraduate graduating cohort** that degree-registration images are available on the China Higher-education Student Information platform (CHSI). Students should log in and use the “image verification” function to check their graduation photographs and academic-record information.

On-campus undergraduates were asked to complete verification by **March 31, 2014**. Graduating students whose photographs were taken off campus should verify them after receiving the images and submit paper photographs to the Academic Affairs Office’s Student Affairs Section by April 30. Students unable to log in because of account problems could visit the office hall on the third floor of the New Administration Building from 3:00 to 5:10 p.m. on Wednesdays and Fridays before March 31. Errors may be reported to the Student Affairs Section at `86502623`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
