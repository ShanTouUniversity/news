The **Ninth China Graduate Electronic Design Contest** has opened registration. The event is jointly organized by the Center for Degree and Graduate Education Development of the Ministry of Education, the National Steering Committee for Professional Graduate Education in Engineering and the Chinese Institute of Electronics. It is open to current graduate students and fourth-year undergraduates who have been admitted to graduate programs.

The contest uses team entries, with each team generally consisting of one faculty adviser and three students. Entries include a technical paper, demonstration video and presentation images; a business plan is optional. Awards include team, individual, outstanding adviser and outstanding organization awards. Registration and project preparation run from February through May, the registration deadline is **June 1**, and the final is scheduled for August.

STU students were asked to email their registration forms to Wang Fengqi at the Graduate School Youth League Committee by **March 23**. The contest website is <http://www.gedc.net.cn>; registration procedures and other details are provided on the website and in the OA attachment.
