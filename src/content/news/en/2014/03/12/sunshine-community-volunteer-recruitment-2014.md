STU’s Youth Volunteer Association Firefly team is recruiting volunteers for the **spring 2014 Sunshine Community project**. Established in 2007, the project has provided community services including visits, household assistance, emotional support, volunteer repairs and health consultations, academic guidance and public-information outreach.

Registration closes at **7:00 p.m. on March 13**. Students could visit the information desks below Dining Halls 2 and 4 from 11:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. or 5:00 to 7:00 p.m. on March 12 and 13, or download the application form and submit a paper or electronic application. The project encouraged electronic registration; information was available through the Firefly website at <http://firefly.stu.edu.cn>.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and STU Youth Volunteer Association Firefly team)
>
> ⚠️ The application form requests student names, contact details and personal experience. Personal information is not reproduced here; applicants should use the project’s designated channels.
