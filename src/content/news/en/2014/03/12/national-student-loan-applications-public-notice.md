STU’s Student Affairs Office has published a notice on the **second-round national student-loan applications for the 2013–2014 academic year**. Following review by the Student Aid Management Center, **62 students** were included in the public-notice process.

Objections will be accepted through **Friday, March 21, 2014**. Organizations or individuals may submit written comments or contact the Student Aid Management Center in person or by phone at `86502249`. Applicants without objections are expected to sign loan agreements in early April under arrangements by the Shantou branch of Bank of China; details will be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
