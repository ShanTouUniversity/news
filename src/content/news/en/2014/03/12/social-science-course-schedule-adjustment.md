STU’s Academic Affairs Office has published schedule changes for sections **66991, 66996 and 66997** of the Social Sciences Department course *Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Legal Foundation*. The revised make-up classes run from **March 17 through April 6**, and all make-up classes will be held in Room D504.

Section 66991 will make up classes on March 29 and April 5; section 66996 on March 29 and April 5; and section 66997 on March 30 and April 6. Students should attend the sessions corresponding to their section and class periods.
