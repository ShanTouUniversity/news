STU’s Graduate School has reminded **2014 and 2015 graduate cohorts** that their degree-registration images are available on the China Higher-education Student Information platform (CHSI). Students who took part in graduate image collection should log in and use the “degree-registration image verification” function to check their photographs and academic-record information.

The verification covers photographs, names, identity-document information, gender, ethnicity, dates of birth and majors. On-campus graduate students were asked to complete the process by **March 31, 2014**. Graduates whose photographs were taken off campus should verify them after receiving the images and send paper photographs to the Graduate School by the end of April.

The university reminded students to use their real information when registering for CHSI accounts, safeguard account details and follow CHSI procedures if errors are found.
