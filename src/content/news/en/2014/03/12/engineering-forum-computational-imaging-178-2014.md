The School of Engineering will hold its 178th Engineering Research Forum from **9:30 to 11:00 a.m. on Friday, March 14** in the Engineering Academic Exchange Room (Gongnan 312). Professor Wang Guoli, vice dean and doctoral supervisor at Sun Yat-sen University’s School of Information Science and Technology, will present **“Data-efficient computational imaging with compressed sensing and sparse Bayesian learning.”**

The forum is open to engineering faculty members, graduate students, undergraduates and other interested members of the university community. Wang has long worked in information acquisition and processing and previously served as head of the Computer Science Department at STU’s School of Engineering.
