At its second leadership meeting of 2014 on **March 6**, STU decided to adjust the affiliation of its Professional and Technical Staff Continuing Education Center. The center will be affiliated with the School of Continuing Education rather than the Human Resources Office. Liu Mingqiang will serve concurrently as the center’s director, while Wu Lanhas will no longer hold that position.

The university issued a document on **March 12** to inform its internal units of the organizational adjustment.
