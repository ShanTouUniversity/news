It is reported that Shantou University, following a study session of the second university leadership meeting on March 6, 2014, has formally established a preparation office for the Guangdong Technion Israel Institute of Technology (GTIIT). The decision was announced in an official document (STU Fa [2014] No. 16) issued on March 12 to accelerate the institute's preparation and construction work.

## Office Members

According to the document, the preparation office is jointly staffed by members from both the Chinese and Israeli sides:

| Position | Members |
| -------- | ------- |
| Directors | Gu Peihua, Paul Feigin |
| Advisor | Feng Zhaolin |
| Executive Deputy Director | Lin Danming |
| Deputy Director | Moshe Marom |
| Members | Chen Shaogang, Wu Ping, Zou Zhibo, Lu Xiaohua, Lin Feng, Liao Xuequan |
| Secretary | Liao Xuequan |

The members are listed in order of surname stroke count in the original Chinese document.

The establishment of this office marks that the GTIIT preparation work has entered a substantive phase of advancement, with both sides of the Sino-Israel cooperation set to coordinate efforts through this organizational structure.
