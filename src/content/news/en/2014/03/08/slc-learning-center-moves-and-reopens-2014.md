It is reported that STU’s Student Learning Center (SLC) has moved to the first floor of the library and will resume services on **March 10**. The center will be open daily from 3 p.m. to 10 p.m., offering academic consultations, group discussions and study classes.

The SLC is located on the first floor of the STU Library. Students and staff can visit http://slc.stu.edu.cn or contact the center at 86502250 (campus extension 882250) or slc@stu.edu.cn.
