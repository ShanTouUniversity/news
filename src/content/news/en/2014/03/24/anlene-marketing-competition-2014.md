Shantou University’s Business School Business Practice Center and Fonterra (Shanghai) Trading Co., Ltd. will hold an Anlene product marketing competition for STU students. The competition will run from March 26 to May 31, with registration closing at noon on March 25.

Students will compete in teams of three by promoting Anlene products through the campus e-commerce platform or Taobao. Results will combine sales performance and marketing creativity. Each team will have a target of 36 cans; teams reaching the target will be ranked by final sales. The awards include one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes and one best-new-customer-development team award, with cash awards of RMB 600, 300, 200 and 500 respectively. Strong performers may be recommended for internships with the company.

Fonterra will provide training and lunch for participants from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. on March 26, followed by an offline sales activity, and will provide tools including sampling tables, display stands and a bone-density testing machine. Business School students from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts may receive business-practice credits after completing the competition under the stated arrangements.

The original notice provided the registration form and registration procedure in an attachment; personal student email addresses are not reproduced here.
