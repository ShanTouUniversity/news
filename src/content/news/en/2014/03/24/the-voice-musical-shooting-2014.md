It is reported that the President’s Office issued a notice on March 24, 2014, that the musical *The Voice of China* will begin filming at Shantou University on March 24, for about two months.

Filming will use various campus locations, and the university asks for the community’s support. Questions may be directed to the contact number listed in the original notice. Faculty and students interested in participating in the artistic production, learning about professional filming and meeting stars may follow subsequent notices from the Youth League Committee on the office information network recruiting extras.

> Source: STU OA Notice (President’s Office)
