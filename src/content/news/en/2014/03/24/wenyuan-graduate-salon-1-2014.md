The first session of Shantou University’s Wenyuan Graduate Growth Salon, organized under the School of Liberal Arts, will take place from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on March 26 in ACC Conference Room 3. Its theme is “Read Ten Thousand Books and Travel Ten Thousand Miles.”

Professor Zhou Daming of Sun Yat-sen University’s School of Sociology and Anthropology will join the session as guest speaker. The discussion will examine how reading and practice can contribute to personal growth, knowledge and action. Zhou’s research covers urban migrants, rural Chinese society, ethnic groups and applied studies; he also serves in several academic organizations and journals.

The salon is managed by the School of Liberal Arts and was initiated by its graduate students as a forum for discussion of topics in the humanities. Students and staff are welcome to attend.
