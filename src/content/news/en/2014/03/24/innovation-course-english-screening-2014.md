It is reported that, because of high enrollment interest, Shantou University’s Business School will conduct a first-round English ability screening for applicants to Technion Professor Shlomo Maital’s Value-Driven Innovation course. The school will use the submissions to select candidates for a second-round interview.

Applicants may submit scores from CET-4, CET-6, IELTS, TOEFL, GRE or GMAT examinations, along with records of English study, exchange activities or training. Materials should be sent to tracyxu@stu.edu.cn by March 26; hard copies may also be delivered to the Business School Academic Affairs Office, Wenxi 204. The consultation number is 86502277.
