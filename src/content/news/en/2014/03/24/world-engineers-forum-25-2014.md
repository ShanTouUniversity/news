The 25th World Engineers Forum of Shantou University’s School of Engineering will take place from 10 a.m. to noon on Sunday, March 30, in the Library Lecture Hall. Its topic is “Source Apportionment of Fine and Coarse Atmospheric Particles.”

Professor Yaacov Mamane of Technion will deliver the presentation. The forum is open to all STU students and staff. Those interested should register through the procedure in the original notice by 11 a.m. on March 28.
