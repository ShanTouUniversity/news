It is reported that the Equipment Office of Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is seeking feedback on a draft policy for sharing large research instruments. Teaching and research units are invited to organize faculty discussions and send consolidated comments to the office by March 28.

Questions may be directed to Zheng Weisheng at wszheng@stu.edu.cn or 86502496. The draft policy is available in the original OA attachment.
