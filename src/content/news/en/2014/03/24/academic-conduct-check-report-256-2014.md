It is reported that Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office released the academic conduct results for the fourth week of the 2013–2014 spring semester. Inspection teams checked Building E at 8 a.m. on March 17 and March 19.

The March 17 inspection recorded five students arriving within five minutes after class began and four arriving more than five minutes late. The March 19 inspection recorded one student arriving more than five minutes late. The names are included in the original OA attachment, and the university asked schools and colleges to provide education and continue strengthening academic conduct.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Three-Style Development Office)
