It is reported that the third session of Shantou University’s Peer Growth Program will take place from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on March 29 in Student Activity Room 726, Building 789. Organized by the Student Affairs Office and hosted by the Student Growth Navigation Society, the session is titled “Seeking Beyond Borders.” Students Li XY and Zheng YN will share experiences in overseas exchange, campus activities and personal development.

The session is open to students interested in overseas exchange programs and has 25 places. Registration runs from 2 p.m. on March 24 to 10 p.m. on March 27 through the activity booking system. Three student representatives will be selected from successful registrants for further discussion with the speakers.
