Shantou University’s School of Engineering will hold an information session for the Engineering for Developing Communities summer course at 7 p.m. on Wednesday, March 26, in Room E203.

The course is a collaboration between Shantou University and Technion and is scheduled to run from July 20 to August 15, 2014. The session will introduce the course to interested students.
