Shantou University’s Women’s Studies Center in the School of Liberal Arts will hold a seminar titled “New Thinking on Gender Equality Advocacy Led by Young Women” from 5 to 6:30 p.m. on March 27 in Lecture Room E204.

Public-interest advocates Xiaomen and Zheng Churan will share their experience in gender-equality advocacy and related campaigns. Xiaomen has participated in efforts concerning the disclosure of information about gender discrimination in education, while Zheng has initiated campaigns including “Occupy the Men’s Toilet” and letters to major companies. The speakers will discuss young women’s participation in gender-equality advocacy.
