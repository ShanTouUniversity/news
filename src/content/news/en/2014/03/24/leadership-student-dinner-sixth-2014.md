It is reported that the sixth Shantou University leadership-student dinner will take place at 6 p.m. on March 25 in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. A total of 32 students successfully registered. Participants should bring their campus cards and arrive at 5:15 p.m., 45 minutes before the event.

The list of successful registrants is included in the original OA attachment and is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
