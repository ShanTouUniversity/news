It is reported that Shantou University’s Party Central Group will hold a thematic study session at 3 p.m. on March 26 in Room 213 of the New Administrative Center. The session will cover a series of speeches by Xi Jinping and the revised Regulation on the Selection and Appointment of Party and Government Officials.

Cheng Jiaming, a professor from the Department of Social Sciences, and Zeng Dabin, a member of the University Party Standing Committee and minister of the Organization and United Front Work Department, will deliver the briefing reports. Party committee members, branch secretaries and relevant university unit leaders will attend.
