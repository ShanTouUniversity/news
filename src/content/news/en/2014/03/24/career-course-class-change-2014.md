Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has adjusted the arrangements for two employment-related courses. During the fifth week, Career Counseling and Training (class no. 68061), originally scheduled in Room G401, will be replaced by attendance at the 38th Career Launch Lecture at 2:30 p.m. on March 25 in the New Library Lecture Hall.

Students enrolled in Employment Guidance (class no. 68065) must also attend the lecture as part of their study. The original notice identifies the instructors for both courses; other teaching requirements remain unchanged.
