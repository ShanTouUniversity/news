It is reported that the indoor badminton and table-tennis courts at Shantou University’s gymnasium will be closed to faculty and students from March 25 to 29 because of filming for *The Voice of China* musical and student organization activities. Physical education classes will continue as scheduled.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
