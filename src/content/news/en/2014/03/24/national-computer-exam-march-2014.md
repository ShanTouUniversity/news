It is reported that the first-half 2014 National Computer Rank Examination will begin at 9 a.m. on March 29 in Room E102, with candidates taking the test in groups according to the times on their admission cards. The examination covers subjects from Level 1 through Level 4 and uses computer-based testing, with individual subjects lasting 90 or 120 minutes.

Candidates must bring their identification and admission cards, and late arrivals will not be admitted after the examination begins. They should gather in Room E204 20 minutes before the computer-based test to draw their seat numbers. STU undergraduates may collect admission cards from their departmental academic affairs coordinators; other candidates may collect them from the Student Services counter of the Office of Academic Affairs with their identification. The consultation number is 86502411. Full arrangements and examination rules are in the original OA attachment.
