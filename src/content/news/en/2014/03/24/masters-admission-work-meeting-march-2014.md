It is reported that Shantou University’s Graduate School will hold a master’s admission work meeting at 4 p.m. on March 25 in Room 213 of the New Administrative Center. The meeting will convey information from the provincial education examination authority’s graduate admissions meeting, review admission progress and outstanding issues across master’s programs, and arrange interviews, medical examinations and admission recommendations.

The meeting is intended for graduate affairs coordinators at the master’s programs and faculty members responsible for graduate admissions.
