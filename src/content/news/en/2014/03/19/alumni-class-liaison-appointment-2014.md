Shantou University Alumni Affairs has begun the nomination and appointment of **class alumni liaisons for the 2014 graduating cohort**. Each college is generally expected to select one liaison for each class. Liaisons will help maintain communication among the university, the alumni association and classmates, assist with alumni activities and relay alumni information.

Candidates may volunteer or be nominated by their college. Relevant experience as a student leader or club officer, along with organizational, communication and alumni-service skills, is encouraged. Appointed liaisons will receive an official letter from the university's Alumni Affairs Office and priority access to alumni publications and activities.

Each college must complete the 2014 class alumni liaison registration form and submit it to Alumni Affairs by **March 23**. The form and submission requirements are available in the original OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
