Shantou TV's *Urban New Space* program, Longfor Properties and Shantou University Business School will hold an economic forum on **March 22** at the STU Library Lecture Hall. The theme is **Seizing Opportunities: A Smart New City and the Rise of Greater Shantou**, with discussion of western Shantou's development and future investment directions.

Shantou TV's *Urban New Space* is the organizer, Longfor Properties is the co-organizer, and STU Business School and the STU MBA Education Center will provide academic support.
