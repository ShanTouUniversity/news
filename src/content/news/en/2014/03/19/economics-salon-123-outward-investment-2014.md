The 123rd Shantou University Business School Economics Salon will be held from **7 to 9 p.m. on March 22** in the Library Lecture Hall. Professor Yeh Jih-sung, a visiting professor at Yuan Ze University’s College of Management in Taiwan, will discuss “China’s Outward Foreign Direct Investment: Multiple Perspectives.” Faculty and students are welcome.

Yeh has taught or held professorships at National Taiwan University, The Chinese University of Hong Kong and Peking University’s Guanghua School of Management, and has worked at the Taiwan Business Bank’s research institute and National Chi Nan University. His research interests include leadership, cross-cultural management, international business and strategy, and the overseas investment behavior of Taiwanese and Chinese firms.
