Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Office has circulated a Guangdong discipline-inspection report on eight typical violations of the central conduct rules. The report urges university units, officials and staff to learn from the cases and follow central and university requirements.

The cases involve misappropriating fiscal subsidies and maintaining an off-book fund, improperly distributing gifts and cash, using banquets and housewarming events to solicit money, private use of an official vehicle, lax enforcement, and dining paid with public funds. The responsible individuals received measures including referral to judicial authorities, dismissal, Party disciplinary sanctions, administrative penalties and recovery of improper expenses.

The office said discipline-inspection authorities would continue correcting misconduct through supervision, public exposure, investigation and accountability, while urging Party members and officials to observe discipline and law and sustain efforts to improve conduct.
