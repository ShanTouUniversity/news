Shantou University Engineering School will host a lecture from **3 to 4:30 p.m. on March 20** in the Engineering School Academic Exchange Room (Gongnan 312). Professor Yaacov Mamane of the **Technion–Israel Institute of Technology** will speak on **“Transport of Anthropogenic Elements on Saharan Dust Along the East Mediterranean.”** Faculty members, graduate students and undergraduates are welcome.

Professor Mamane has served as Technion deputy vice president for safety, dean of undergraduate studies and head of its Center for Environmental Engineering. His research covers climate change, environmental impact assessment, renewable energy, meteorology, atmospheric chemistry, and the transport and deposition of atmospheric pollutants.
