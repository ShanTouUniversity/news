Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will hold an awards ceremony and seminar for papers from its Integrative Thinking course from **9:30 to 11:30 a.m. on March 22** in Meeting Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center. The event will recognize students whose critical-thinking papers stood out during the fall semester of the 2013–2014 academic year and provide a forum for discussion and writing practice.

The program includes an introduction, remarks by team representatives, an awards ceremony, thematic dialogues, paper presentations with faculty comments, and a closing summary. The awardee list is included in the notice attachment.
