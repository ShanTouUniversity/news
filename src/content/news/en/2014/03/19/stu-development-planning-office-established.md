Shantou University decided at its third university leadership meeting of 2014 on **March 18** to establish a Development Planning Office. The office will coordinate work related to the university's Innovation and Strengthening Project and other development planning initiatives.

The university appointed Hu Zhong as director and Zhang Kaisheng as deputy director. The former 211 Project Office was abolished, and its functions were transferred to the new office.
