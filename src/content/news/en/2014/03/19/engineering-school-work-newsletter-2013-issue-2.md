Shantou University’s Engineering School has released Issue 2 of its 2013 Work Newsletter, the ninth issue overall. The publication reviews developments in admissions outreach, undergraduate teaching, research, academic exchange and student affairs.

It covers the school’s admissions-outreach and annual work meetings, its hosting of the 2013 CDIO Engineering Education Conference, and faculty participation in national and provincial talent programs. The school also advanced program and curriculum reform, received study visits from universities including Ningbo University, sent 10 students to the Technion for a summer program, and developed industry partnerships with Esquel, Shenzhen O-film and Liantai Group.

The research section reports on the International Workshop on Energy Conversion, the annual meeting of the Chinese Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics education committee, and faculty members’ academic exchanges in China and abroad. Student highlights include results in the National Undergraduate Electronic Design Contest and a real-estate planning competition, as well as the EIP-CDIO development camp and the “Growth Salon.”
