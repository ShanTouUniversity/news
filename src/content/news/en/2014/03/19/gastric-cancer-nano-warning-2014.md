It is reported that the School of Science will hold a Science Salon lecture on March 21, 2014, featuring Distinguished Professor Cui Daxiang of Shanghai Jiao Tong University, who will also share insights on applying for the National Natural Science Foundation.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Nano-Technology-Based Early Warning and Early Diagnosis System for Gastric Cancer (with remarks on applying for the National Natural Science Foundation) |
| Speaker | Prof. Cui Daxiang, Distinguished Professor, Shanghai Jiao Tong University |
| Time | 16:00–17:00, March 21, 2014 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center |
| Organizer | School of Science |
