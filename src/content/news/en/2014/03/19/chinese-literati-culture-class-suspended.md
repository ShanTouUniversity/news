Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has announced that section 67105 of **Chinese Culture of Reclusion**, offered by the Chinese department, will be suspended during **periods 3 and 4 on March 20**. The class was scheduled to meet in **room D406**. A makeup time will be announced later, and enrolled students should follow further updates.
