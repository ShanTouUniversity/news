The Shantou Federation of Trade Unions plans to recognize outstanding frontline workers and grassroots collectives around May 1, 2014, through the Shantou May 1 Labor Medal and Workers Pioneer titles. STU Union has opened an internal nomination process for eligible collectives at the university, its Medical College and affiliated hospital.

The Workers Pioneer title is intended for workshops, work sections, teams or offices that have performed strongly in labor competitions, major construction or research projects, public services, cultural development or environmental protection. STU, including its Medical College and affiliated hospital, has **one** collective nomination slot.

Units must submit the candidate collective's materials to STU Union by the end of the workday on **March 26** and send them to the union's official mailbox. The registration form and detailed requirements are set out in the original OA attachment.
