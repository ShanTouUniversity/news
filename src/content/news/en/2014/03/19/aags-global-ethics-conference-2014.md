It is reported that the ninth international conference of the Asia Association for Global Studies (AAGS) will be held at Shantou University on March 22–23, 2014. Organized by AAGS and hosted by the Center for Global Studies of STU's College of Liberal Arts, the conference will focus on “Ethics in a Globalizing World: Debates and Dilemmas.”

Further information is available on the [Asia Association for Global Studies website](http://asia-globalstudies.org/aags_20141).

## Ethical Questions in a Globalizing World

Global Studies examines phenomena and issues with global effects. As cross-border and cross-cultural exchanges become more common, crises and social problems arising in one place can cross national boundaries and affect other regions or the world as a whole. Against this background, the conference will examine ethical responsibilities and rights in a globalizing world, including whether a global ethic can or should emerge in a diverse world.

The discussions will address issues such as labor exploitation by multinational corporations, human trafficking, war, famine, environmental degradation and racial discrimination. Participants will also consider how ethical values should inform legal frameworks, decision-making, political and economic processes, humanitarian intervention and human interaction, as well as how local responses should relate to global action on issues that cross borders.
