Shantou University English Language Center has launched campus selection for the 18th FLTRP English debating competition. The selection is open to all STU undergraduate students. Registration runs from **March 19 to 23**, and the campus round will be held on **March 29**.

The selection uses the British Parliamentary format, with two debaters forming a team and four teams competing in each round. One team will be selected to represent STU at the national final in Beijing, which is scheduled for May. Applicants should use the registration form; the submission process and complete debate rules are available in the original OA notice and attachments.
