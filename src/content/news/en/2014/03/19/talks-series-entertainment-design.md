Shantou University's Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will hold a talk titled **Talks Series 1: Entertainment Design** at **7:30 p.m. on March 20** in the school's first-floor lecture hall. Professor Wang Shouzhi will be the speaker, and faculty and students are welcome.

The session will focus on entertainment design. Further arrangements are available from the organizer.
