Shantou University’s Zhicheng College will hold its seventh Physical-Development Training Camp from **March 29 to 30**. The camp will include an outdoor trek across Dangsangpu Mountain, camping, team development, leadership and cooperation training, and wilderness-survival activities. **Twenty** residential students of Zhicheng College will be recruited.

Applicants should be willing to undertake physical and team training, follow coaches’ instructions, observe the schedule and team rules, and have no conflicting classes or activities during the program. Participants will cover meals for the two-day, one-night activity at **RMB 40 per person**; students with financial hardship may apply for a reduction.

Applicants should complete the form and email it to `ATP@stu.edu.cn` by **5:30 p.m. on March 24**. The camp is co-organized by Zhicheng College’s Beidou Development Association.
