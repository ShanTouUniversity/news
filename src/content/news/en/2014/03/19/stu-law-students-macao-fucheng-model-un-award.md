A two-student delegation from Shantou University Law School recently attended the fourth Macao-Fucheng Model United Nations Conference. Student **Ma XY** received the conference's only **Outstanding Delegate** award. The delegation, made up of public administration students Ma XY and Wang ZY, was STU's first team to take part in an overseas Model UN conference.

The conference was jointly organized by the Model United Nations associations of the University of Macau and Taiwan's National Cheng Kung University. More than 200 students from universities in mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan attended. The STU delegation joined the Macao venue's Third Committee, discussing the promotion and protection of children's rights and the elimination of racism, racial discrimination, xenophobia and related intolerance.

During the three-day conference, the students prepared position material and analyzed the topics with guidance from Law School faculty. Ma XY was recognized by the chairing panel for English communication, subject knowledge and negotiation at the sessions.
