Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will hold its 11th Student Academic Activity Month from March 23 to April 18. The program is open to students across the university.

Activities include a writing contest, the “STU Knowledge Star” general-knowledge competition and an academic fair. The program is intended to provide opportunities for literary creation, subject learning and academic exchange. Details and participation procedures are available in the original notice attachment.
