Shantou University’s Law School will hold interviews in Room 206 of the Law School on March 24 and 25 for three overseas study programs.

The interview for the 2014–2015 overseas university exchange program will begin at 9:30 a.m. on March 24. The interview for the Prague International Commercial Transactions Summer School scholarship will begin at 11 a.m. that day. Interviews for the summer law exchange program jointly organized with McGill University’s Faculty of Law are scheduled for 3 p.m. on March 25. Each interview is expected to last about 10 minutes, and applicants should arrive in advance.

The original notice included a list of student applicants. Because it contains personal information, the list is not reproduced here; applicants should follow the Law School’s original notice for the detailed schedule.
