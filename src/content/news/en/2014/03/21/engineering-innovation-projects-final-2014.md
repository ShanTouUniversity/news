It is reported that the College of Engineering announced on March 21, 2014, the final results for the 2013 student science and technology innovation projects.

Of the 29 projects that passed the midterm review, seven applied for deferred completion and two were withdrawn. After impartial review by the expert panel, 20 projects including *Indoor Positioning System Based on Multi-Signal Methods* passed the final review, while seven projects including *Personal Volleyball Training Machine* were deferred.
