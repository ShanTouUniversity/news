Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will organize the 2014 Employment Service Month from March to May. Colleges and related units will offer career exchanges, recruitment activities, job-search skills sessions and employment-policy information for students.

The program includes the School of Science’s Career Workshop on March 23, the 38th Career Launch Lecture on March 25 and a graduate employment discussion tentatively scheduled for March 28. The School of Engineering, School of Law, Zhicheng College, School of Liberal Arts, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Business School, and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will also hold career-planning sessions, interview-skills exchanges, talks and workshops between March and May.

The Student Affairs Office and Alumni Office plan to hold a recruitment fair for alumni enterprises on March 28, followed by an online recruitment week in early May. “Employment Stars,” a display of the career paths of outstanding graduates from the previous five years, is scheduled for April to May at the pedestrian street. An electronic résumé competition is planned for early April to mid-May, while employment-procedure consultations and policy sessions will be offered from April to May.
