Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has reported that fee deductions failed for 66 students registering for the College English Tests after the initial collection arranged by the Finance Office, Campus Card Center and Bank of China.

The affected students must deposit the required fee into their campus cards by noon on March 24. The Campus Card Center will then attempt another deduction. A second unsuccessful deduction will result in cancellation of the registration. Colleges will notify students after checking the list in the attachment to the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The list of unsuccessful deductions is available in the original OA attachment.
