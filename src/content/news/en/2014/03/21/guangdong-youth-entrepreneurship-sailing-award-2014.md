Shantou University’s Youth League Committee has forwarded a plan prepared by the Guangdong Youth Entrepreneurship and Employment Federation and other organizations for the Guangdong Youth Entrepreneurship Sailing Award and television competition.

Students interested in participating must send their application materials to the committee’s official mailbox, o_tw@stu.edu.cn, by March 27, 2014. Eligibility, required materials and the judging process are set out in the forwarded plan.
