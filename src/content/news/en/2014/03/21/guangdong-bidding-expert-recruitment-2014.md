Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has forwarded a letter from the Guangdong Development and Reform Commission seeking applicants for the second-round recruitment of experts for Guangdong’s comprehensive bid-evaluation expert database.

The listed materials include a signed Expert Availability Schedule, recent one-inch color identification photographs, copies of an identity document, academic credentials, professional-title credentials, relevant professional certificates and other supporting documents. Paper materials must be submitted to the Human Resources Office in Room 315 of the Administrative Center by May 19.

Applicants should consult the original OA attachments for eligibility, document formats and detailed requirements. The office can be reached at 86503390 or o_rsc@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
>
> ⚠️ The bid-evaluation recruitment letter and related attachments are available on the OA system.
