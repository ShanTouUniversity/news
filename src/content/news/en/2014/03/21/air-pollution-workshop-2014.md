Shantou University’s School of Engineering will host the “Fundamentals of Air Pollution” workshop in Library Seminar Rooms 30A and 30B on March 25–27 and April 1–3. Professor Yaacov Mamane of Technion will lead five lectures and one field visit to a power plant, cement plant or quarry.

The workshop will cover major air pollutants, air-pollution meteorology, pollution management, particulate-matter source apportionment and pollution control. Topics include health effects, dispersion modelling, monitoring and regulations, statistical analysis tools and pollution control in motor vehicles.

Professor Mamane is a member of Technion’s Faculty of Civil and Environmental Engineering. His research interests include climate change, environmental impact assessment, renewable energy, meteorology, atmospheric chemistry, nanotechnology and the source apportionment of atmospheric particles.
