Shantou University’s Youth League Committee has begun its nominations for Guangdong honors for outstanding Youth League members, cadres and May Fourth Red-Flag organizations for 2013–2014. The provincial awards are scheduled to be presented around May Fourth in 2014.

STU has five recommendation slots: two for outstanding Youth League members, one for an outstanding Youth League cadre, one for a May Fourth Red-Flag Youth League committee and one for a May Fourth Red-Flag branch. College Youth League committees and directly affiliated branches must submit nominees and supporting materials to the university committee office by March 25, with electronic copies sent to o_tw@stu.edu.cn.

Eligibility, documentation and the review process are set out in the forwarded original notice and attachments.
