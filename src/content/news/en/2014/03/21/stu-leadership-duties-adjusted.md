It is reported that Shantou University issued a document (STU Fa [2014] No. 23) on March 21, deciding after deliberation to adjust the work divisions of some university leaders and president assistants due to work needs. The adjustment involves one vice president and one president assistant.

According to the document, the specific divisions are as follows:

| Position | Name | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vice President | Wen Xiaobo | In charge of teaching and admissions; in charge of art education and teaching and general education work related to the Social Sciences Department; in charge of university journal publications and campus-run industries; in charge of the Development Planning Office; assisting Executive President Gu Peihua with personnel work |
| President Assistant | Huang Dongyang | In charge of research and the Central Laboratory; assisting university leaders in liaising with the Medical College; responsible for coordinating the integration of the Medical College into the main campus |

Among these, the coordination of the Medical College's relocation to the main campus, assigned to President Assistant Huang Dongyang, is regarded as a key special task in this adjustment.

The document was issued to all units across the university for implementation.
