The 12th Career Workshop of Shantou University’s Science School will take place at 7:30 p.m. on March 23 in Lecture Room E201. Its theme is “Project and Platform Management Practices at International Companies.”

The speaker, Dr. Haihui Lin, graduated from the University of Toronto with a specialization in chemical engineering and polymer materials. He is now global chief technology officer at Silberline, a U.S. pigment manufacturer, and previously worked for Momentive Performance Materials and Flint Group. He will discuss portfolio management and how to present projects to a chief executive officer to gain support.

The event is open to all STU students and staff.
