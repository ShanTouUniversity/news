Shantou University’s Center for International Language Learning (CILL) will hold the second IELTS reading workshop from 4 to 6 p.m. on Wednesday, March 26, at CILL. The workshop will focus on developing IELTS reading skills.

Places are limited. Students may reserve a place through the [IELTS Reading 2 event page](https://ieltsreading2.eventbrite.com). The notice advises students who are uncertain about attending not to register and asks registrants to attend as scheduled.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Center)
