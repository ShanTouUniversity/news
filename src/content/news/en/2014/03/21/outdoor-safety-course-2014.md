Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has announced the spring schedule for the course Outdoor Safety Education for Middle School Students. Classroom sessions, field practice and a wrap-up session will take place from March 26 to May 28.

| Component | Time | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom session | March 26, April 2 and April 23, 4–6 p.m. | Room E204 |
| Classroom session | April 9 and April 16, 2–6 p.m. | Grass area beside the swimming pool |
| Field practice | April 26–May 25 | As arranged for the course |
| Wrap-up session | May 28 | As arranged for the course |

Students enrolled in the public-interest course are asked to attend each component according to the schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
