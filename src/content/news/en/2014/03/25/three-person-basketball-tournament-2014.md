It is reported that the Sports Department of Shantou University will hold the 2014 3-on-3 basketball tournament in April. The tournament is open to all with no threshold, allowing free team formation across colleges and grades. The men’s quarterfinals and women’s semifinals will be held in the air-supported basketball training hall.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Start date | From April 12, 2014 |
| Registration period | March 26 – April 8, 2014 |
| Eligibility | Open to all students, cross-college and cross-grade teams allowed |
| Awards | Champion, runner-up and third place for both men’s and women’s divisions |
| Organizer | Sports Department |

## Registration

Registration can be completed via SMS, email or Weibo private message, with team name and members’ details. Specific contacts and the application form are available in the OA attachment and are withheld here to protect privacy.

> ⚠️ Registration contacts and the form are available on the OA system.
