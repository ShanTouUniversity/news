Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office and Alumni Affairs Office will hold the 2014 Alumni Enterprise Job Fair at 9 a.m. on March 28 in the New Library Student Activity Hall. Forty-five companies are expected to recruit on campus.

Participating employers include Nanchang OFILM Technology, Sinochem Guangdong Petroleum, China Resources Yibao Beverage and Shantou Ultrasound Printed Board. Graduating students will be notified through their colleges and residential colleges. The complete list of employers and positions is provided in the OA attachment.
