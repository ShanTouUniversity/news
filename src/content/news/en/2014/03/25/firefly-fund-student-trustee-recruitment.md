Shantou University’s Youth Volunteer Firefly Charity Fund is recruiting three student representatives from across the university. Founded in 2008, the fund supports students through scholarships, direct assistance and special-purpose funds.

Candidates are expected not to be members of the Youth Volunteer Association in the next academic year, except for volunteers, and should demonstrate an interest in public service, relevant experience and a strong sense of responsibility. Representatives will participate in fund management, vote on major decisions, and oversee daily operations and the use of funds.

Applications are due by 11:30 p.m. on March 28. Students may apply in person or download the application form from the OA system and submit it as specified in the original notice. Interview details will be announced separately.

> ⚠️ The application form and event attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
