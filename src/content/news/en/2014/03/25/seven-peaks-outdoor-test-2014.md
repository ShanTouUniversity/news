It is reported that 125 faculty members and students registered for Shantou University’s intermediate Qimenzhang-Yanzi Rock outdoor program in Heyuan. To support safety screening, the first fitness test will take place from 1 to 6 p.m. on March 26 on the STU campus and at Sangpu Mountain. The test is open to registrants, including on-site registrants.

Participants will climb Sangpu Mountain carrying sandbags weighing 15 kilograms for men and 12 kilograms for women. They will start in groups of six and be ranked by individual time. The organizers will select 36 participants for the second-stage wilderness first-aid workshop at an approximate 60:40 male-to-female ratio, then select 20 participants for the outdoor program. The finish and check-in point will be in front of Zhicheng Building.

Participants should wear suitable sports clothing and shoes with good traction and prepare protective equipment according to their needs. The group list and event details are in the original OA attachment; personal contact details are not reproduced here.
