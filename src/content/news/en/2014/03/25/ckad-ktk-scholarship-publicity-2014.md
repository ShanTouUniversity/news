Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has announced the proposed recipients of the 2013 Kan Tai-keung Design Award (KTK) scholarships. Following student applications, the school assessed factors including financial circumstances, conduct and academic performance, and proposed four undergraduate recipients, each to receive RMB 2,500.

Public feedback will remain open until 10 a.m. on Monday, March 31. Concerns may be submitted in writing or in person to the school’s Youth League office; the office can be reached at 86502187 (short number 882187). The students’ names are omitted here to protect their privacy.
