Shantou University’s Zhicheng College has completed the selection of participants for its seventh physical fitness training camp. The program selected 20 students as primary participants and two as alternates. The opening session and course activities are scheduled for 7:30 p.m. on March 28 in Room J-354.

Students who were not selected may also follow the Beidou Outdoor Experience Camp later this semester. The students’ names and majors are omitted here to protect their privacy; the full roster is available in the original OA notice.
