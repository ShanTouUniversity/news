It is reported that Shantou University’s Office of Academic Affairs will hold a meeting for academic affairs coordinators at 3 p.m. on March 25 in Room 312 on the third floor of the New Administration Building. The meeting will arrange 2014 undergraduate thesis checks and introduce the main points of a draft regulation on internships.

Coordinators from the schools of liberal arts, science, engineering, law, business, arts and journalism will attend.
