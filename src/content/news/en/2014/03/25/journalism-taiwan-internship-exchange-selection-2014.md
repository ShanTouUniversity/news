It is reported that Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication announced its selections for the 2014 summer Taiwan internship and the exchange program for the first semester of the following academic year. The selection includes 10 students in writing, one in photography, one in advertising and two exchange students, for a total of 14 students.

To protect student privacy, individual names are not reproduced here; the complete list is available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
