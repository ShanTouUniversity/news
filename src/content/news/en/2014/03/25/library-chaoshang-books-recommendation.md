Shantou University Library has released the ninth installment of its featured literature series, focusing on the Chaoshan business community. The feature surveys the group’s origins, modern development and global distribution, as well as its contributions to economic and social development in the Chaoshan region.

The library describes the community as having a history of roughly 500 years. Its members are active overseas, in Chaoshan and elsewhere in China, across sectors including real estate, finance, retail, food, textiles and new energy. The library holds more than 50 books on the subject and has selected eight representative works for this installment.

Readers can visit the library website and search the catalog for more resources. The detailed reading list is provided in the original OA attachment.
