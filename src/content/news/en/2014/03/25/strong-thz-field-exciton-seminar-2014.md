It is reported that Shantou University’s School of Science will host a Science Salon seminar at 4:15 p.m. on March 27 in the School of Science Lecture Hall, Room Li-352. Xie Xiaotao, an associate professor in the Department of Physics at Northwestern University, will present “Strong-Field Physics in Semiconductor Excitons Driven by Intense THz Fields.”

The talk will discuss optical up-conversion and down-conversion and high-order terahertz sidebands under intense THz fields, as well as how exciton energy generated by near-infrared excitation affects the plateau distribution of sideband spectra.
