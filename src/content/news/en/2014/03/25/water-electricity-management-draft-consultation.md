Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has released a draft revision of the university’s water and electricity management regulations for public consultation among campus units and faculty members. The revision is intended to strengthen safety and energy-saving management and to update campus rules in line with relevant regulations and energy-reduction requirements.

Comments and suggestions should be sent to the office’s water and electricity center by March 31. The center can be reached at o_sdk@stu.edu.cn or 86502489; the listed contact is Chen Shenhao.

> ⚠️ The draft Water and Electricity Management Regulations is available in the original attachment on the OA system.
