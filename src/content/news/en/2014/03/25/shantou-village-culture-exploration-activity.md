Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office is launching a Student Community X Project on Shantou village culture. Students across the university may form teams of three to five to investigate traditional village architecture, food and customs, then prepare a research report and present their findings. Ten teams will be selected based on their proposals and teamwork.

Each team may apply for up to RMB 200 in activity funding. Its final work must include a video of at least five minutes or at least 20 photographs, together with a research report. The program will award one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes and four excellence awards; the cash prizes are RMB 400, RMB 200 and RMB 100 respectively, while excellence winners will receive certificates.

Applications are due by noon on April 4. The application form and submission process are provided in the original OA notice; personal contact details are not reproduced here.
