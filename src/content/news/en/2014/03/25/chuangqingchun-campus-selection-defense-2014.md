It is reported that the Shantou University campus selection for the 2014 Chuangqingchun Guangdong Student Entrepreneurship Competition received 25 project submissions from 25 teams after opening in December 2013. The submissions included 14 entries for the ninth Challenge Cup Student Entrepreneurship Plan Competition, five for the Entrepreneurship Practice Challenge and six for the Public Welfare Entrepreneurship Competition.

The campus selection defenses will begin at 3 p.m. on March 26. Entries for the entrepreneurship-plan competition will be defended in Room E305, while practice and public-welfare entries will be defended in Room E306. Participants should wear formal attire and check in 15 minutes early. The defenses will include project presentations, judges’ questions and comments, followed by the announcement of results.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
