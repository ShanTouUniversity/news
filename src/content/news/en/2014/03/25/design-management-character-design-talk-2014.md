It is reported that Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host a talk from 5 to 6 p.m. on March 26 in the Animation and Video Laboratory, Building 789. Mark Horneff, head of Kuato Studio, will discuss the role of design management in the design process. Kris Turvey, creative director of Kuato Studio, will discuss art direction, visual style and character design.
