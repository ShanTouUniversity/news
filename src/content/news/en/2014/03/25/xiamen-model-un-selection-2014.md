Shantou University’s Law School will select three to four students from the 2011 to 2013 cohorts in Public Administration and Administrative Management to attend the Cross-Strait Model United Nations Conference, which will be held in Xiamen from May 9 to 11, 2014.

The selection interview will take place from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 26, in Room 206 of the Law School. Candidates will give an English self-introduction and a three- to five-minute speech on “Protection of Rights of Children.” The evaluation will consider interview performance as well as relevant Model United Nations experience and knowledge.
