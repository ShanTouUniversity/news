It is reported that nine student extracurricular research projects at Shantou University’s School of Law passed the interim review for 2013 and proceeded to the final review. After assessment by the school’s student research project review committee, all nine projects passed the final review.

The approved projects included “Analysis of Campus Charitable Activity Models and Their Appropriate Institutional Design.” Project leaders who have submitted completion reports are expected to complete their project summaries. The full project list is included in the original OA attachment.
