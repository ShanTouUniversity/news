It is reported that, to support the National Natural Science Foundation of China pilot for intelligent assignment of peer reviewers and help maintain review quality, Shantou University’s Office of Research is reminding principal investigators of funded NSFC projects to log in to the ISIS system and update their research fields, profiles, research achievements and ongoing projects.

Experts are advised to list research fields in order of familiarity and enter no more than five keywords for each field. Those working in fields D01, F01 or H16 must also select the relevant research directions. Updates should be completed through each expert’s personal account.
