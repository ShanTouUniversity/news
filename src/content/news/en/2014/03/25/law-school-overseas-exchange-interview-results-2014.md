It is reported that Shantou University’s Law School announced the interview results for its 2014–2015 overseas exchange programs. Based on the interview rankings, the school plans to recommend five students for a one-semester exchange at Queen’s University Belfast, two students for study at the University of Hong Kong, and one student for a one-semester exchange at Yonsei University in South Korea. Final placements are subject to acceptance and admission by the host universities.

The school also announced the half-scholarship recipients for the Prague International Commercial Transactions Summer School: students Li LC and Zhang LJ. Student names are shown using surnames and given-name initials in line with privacy requirements.
