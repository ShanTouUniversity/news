Shantou University received Guangdong’s Outstanding Organization Award in the 2013 provincial summer cultural, science and health volunteer program known as “Three Rural Areas.” The Evergreen rural teaching service team from Zhicheng College and the “Towards the Ocean” public-service education team were named Outstanding Teams.

Nine STU teachers and students were also recognized as Outstanding Individuals. Their names are omitted here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
