It is reported that electronic bills for office telephone services in December 2013 and January 2014, supplied by China Telecom Shantou and China Mobile Shantou, have been imported into Shantou University's telephone management system. The Network and Information Center and Resource Management Office asked campus units to verify the bills.

Authorized staff can log in to the telephone management system and review the China Telecom fixed-line and China Mobile fixed-line bills. Bills without discrepancies should be confirmed online. Any mismatch with actual usage should be reported to the relevant office email addresses by March 7. Bills that are neither disputed nor confirmed by the deadline will be treated as confirmed by the system.

The Finance Office planned to deduct the charges from the funding numbers provided by each unit on March 12.
