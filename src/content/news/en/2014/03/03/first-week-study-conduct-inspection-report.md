It is reported that Shantou University's Three-Style Development Office inspected classroom attendance during the first week of the 2013–2014 spring semester and issued Report No. 253.

At 8:00 a.m. on February 26, inspectors recorded two students arriving no more than five minutes late at the E Teaching Building. At the same time and location on February 27, four students were recorded as more than five minutes late. The university asked the relevant schools and residential colleges to strengthen education and help students observe teaching-order requirements.

The student names listed in the original attachment are not reproduced here.
