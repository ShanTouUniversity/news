It is reported that the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology has opened a call for suggestions for its major basic research program guide. Shantou University's Research Office forwarded the information to relevant campus units. The program is intended to cultivate proposals that may later compete for national major basic research funding led by Guangdong.

## Scope and Submission

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Fields | Agriculture; Population and Health; New Materials and Advanced Manufacturing; Resources and Environment; Electronic Information; Comprehensive Interdisciplinary Studies |
| Directions | Suggestions should follow designated thematic directions and support the goal of subsequent national funding |
| Materials | Complete the Proposal Guide Suggestion Form |
| Deadline | March 7, 2014 |

Interested faculty members should prepare the required materials and email the electronic version to yxhu@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
> ⚠️ The Proposal Guide Suggestion Form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
