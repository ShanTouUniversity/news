It is reported that the 2014 national English major oral and interpreting test will begin at 8:30 a.m. on Saturday, May 17. According to the Academic Affairs Office, the registration is open to English undergraduates who entered the university in 2012.

The registration fee is RMB 80 per person. Online registration runs from March 4 to 11 through the credit-based education website. The Network Center will deduct the fee from registrants' campus card accounts on March 12 on behalf of the Finance Office. Students should maintain sufficient balance during the payment period; an unsuccessful deduction may result in failed registration.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
