It is reported that the Shantou University Chorus has opened recruitment for the 2013–2014 spring semester. All students may audition; no special musical training or instrument skills are required.

## Audition Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Time | Saturday, March 8, 2014, 9:00–11:30 a.m. and 2:00–4:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Music Rehearsal Room, first floor of the STU Building, Arts Education Center |
| Content | Pitch imitation and a self-selected song excerpt; students with instrumental skills may perform with an instrument |
| Registration | On-site registration on the audition day |

There are no restrictions on the type of self-selected song, and participants may bring their own accompaniment. More information is available through the chorus's official Weibo account.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Arts Education Center)
