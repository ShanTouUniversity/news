It is reported that Shantou University has confirmed the class schedule for its 2014 spring semester public welfare course "Caring for Children with Intellectual Disabilities" (Class No. 69474). The first session will be held from 14:00 to 17:00 on Wednesday, March 5, in Room E201-2.

The course is offered by the Student Affairs Office and is open to students across the university. Enrolled students have been reminded to take note of the schedule and venue, and to pass the information along to classmates so that everyone attends on time.
