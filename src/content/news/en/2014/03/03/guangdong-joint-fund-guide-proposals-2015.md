It is reported that to prepare for the 2015 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC)–Guangdong Provincial Government Joint Fund (Guangdong Joint Fund) and enhance its role in strengthening basic research in Guangdong and neighboring regions, the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology has launched a call for suggestions for the 2015 guide. The Research Office has forwarded the notice to relevant units on campus.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Fields | Agriculture; Population and Health; New Materials and Advanced Manufacturing; Resources and Environment; Electronic Information |
| Thematic directions | Proposals must follow the designated thematic directions; suggestions outside them will not be considered |
| No duplication | Content must not duplicate the Guangdong Joint Fund guides officially released by NSFC from 2006 to 2014 |
| Submission | Complete the Proposal Suggestion Form |

Faculty members who wish to participate should send the electronic form to yxhu@stu.edu.cn by April 4.
