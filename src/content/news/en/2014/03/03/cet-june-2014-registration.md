It is reported that, following arrangements by the Guangdong Provincial Examination Authority, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has arranged registration for the June 2014 national CET-4 and CET-6 exams on the main campus. The CET-4 exam is scheduled for June 14, 9:00–11:20, while CET-6 will take place the same day from 15:00 to 17:20.

## Eligibility

**CET-4**: Undergraduates placed at Level 2 or above in this semester's English placement; art majors who have completed ELC EA3 or passed the English Proficiency Test; and English majors who enrolled in 2013 are all eligible.

**CET-6**: Students who hold a CET-4 certificate or scored 425 or above on CET-4 may register. Students holding the TEM-4 certificate are also eligible. Those who have already passed CET-6 may retake it, provided undergraduates have completed ELC4 and no more than two retakes after reaching 425 points.

## Registration and Fees

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Fee | RMB 36 |
| Online registration | March 4 – March 16 |
| Channel | Credit-based system website: "Course Selection → Qualification Exam Registration" |
| Payment | Deducted from campus card accounts on March 17 via the Network Center; if unsuccessful, deducted from tuition accounts by Bank of China on the morning of March 19 |

The Academic Affairs Office reminds students to keep sufficient balance in their accounts during this period, as failed deduction means failed registration.

## Notes

Per relevant regulations, non-enrolled persons may not register for this round of exams. Candidates must bring their ID card, student ID, and admission ticket — entry will be denied without any one of them.
