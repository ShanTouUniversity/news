It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office has changed the schedule for Voice Performance Arts class 67056. The class originally scheduled for March 5, Wednesday of Week 2, periods 3 and 4, will be suspended. A make-up class will take place on March 12, Wednesday of Week 3, periods 8 and 9.

The make-up class will be held in Music Rehearsal Room STU101. Relevant schools and enrolled students should note the change.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
