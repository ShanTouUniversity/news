It is reported that the Shantou University Student Organizations Union office resumed weekday duty on March 3. The office is located in Room 731 of Building 7, and service is available from 4:15 to 5:30 p.m. Monday through Friday.

Student organizations may visit the office during these hours for relevant services.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
