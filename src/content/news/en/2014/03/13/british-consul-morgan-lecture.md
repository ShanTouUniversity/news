It is reported that Morgan, the British Consul General in Guangzhou, will visit Shantou University on **Friday, March 28**, and give a public lecture from **3:00 to 4:00 p.m.** on China-UK relations over the next 20 years.

The lecture will begin in Chinese, followed by an English presentation using slides, and will include a question-and-answer session with students and faculty. The venue will be determined according to the number of reservations. Those who wish to attend should register through the university reservation system by **noon on March 17**.
