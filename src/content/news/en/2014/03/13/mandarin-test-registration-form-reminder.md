Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has reminded **53 students** who registered and paid for the **first-half 2014 Mandarin proficiency test** but have not submitted their test registration forms to complete the process.

The students must bring their forms to the Academic Affairs Office service window for in-person confirmation by **12:00 noon on March 14**. Registrations without confirmation by the deadline will be cancelled. The detailed list is provided in the office’s attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The list of students who still need to submit forms is available in the original OA attachment.
