Following the Guangdong Education Department’s arrangements for the 2014 application for Pearl River Scholar positions at higher-education institutions, Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has asked each unit to prepare position applications and recommend candidates.

Units must submit four copies of the application form and attachments to the Human Resources Office in Room 315 of the New Administration Building by **March 28**, and send electronic copies to `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn`. The contact person is Li Zhiwei, reachable at 86502364.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
>
> ⚠️ The application forms, implementation requirements and related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
