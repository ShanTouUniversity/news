Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has announced the results of its selection for the 2014 Chinese University of Hong Kong summer study program. A total of 32 students applied, 17 of whom met the eligibility requirements. Following interviews, the university’s review committee selected **10 students** to attend the CUHK summer course.

The committee was chaired by Professor Liu Wenhua and included representatives of the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the Business School, the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, and Student Affairs. The selected students are listed in the relevant attachment.
