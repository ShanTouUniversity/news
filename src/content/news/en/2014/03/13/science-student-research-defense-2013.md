The School of Science at Shantou University will hold the final defense for its 2013 extracurricular academic and technology projects at **2:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 19**, in the school’s multimedia conference room (Room Li 352). Faculty and students are welcome to attend.

Project teams must submit their completion reports by **5:00 p.m. on March 17** and send electronic copies to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn`. The participating projects and defense order will follow the organizer’s attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Science)
>
> ⚠️ The list of participating projects, defense order and report requirements are available in the original OA attachments.
