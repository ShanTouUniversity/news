Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has launched an academic-record review for the 2015 graduating class. Undergraduate students admitted in 2011 at the main campus are asked to check key fields against their admission records, including their names, gender, dates of birth, national ID numbers and ethnicity.

College academic administrators will distribute the review lists and organize individual confirmation. Students whose information is correct should sign the form. Those who find errors should identify the incorrect fields and bring the original ID card and household register to the Academic Affairs Office’s Academic Records Section to request a correction. Students must confirm the information in person; records will not be changed without the required procedure or approval.

Colleges must collect and submit the lists to the Academic Affairs Office by **May 9, 2014**. Questions may be directed to 86502623.
