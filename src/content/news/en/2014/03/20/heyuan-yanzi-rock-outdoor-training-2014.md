STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Club will organize an intermediate outdoor training trip to Yanzi Rock in Heyuan from **the afternoon of April 4 through April 7**. The activity plans to recruit **20 participants**, including 16 students and four faculty members. The route will cover Yuantan, Yanzi Rock Valley, the Yanzi Rock summit, Tongziwo and Baixi Reservoir, with jungle hiking, camping, team training, wilderness first aid and survival exercises.

The selection process will first choose 36 applicants for a wilderness-survival workshop before confirming the final 20 participants. Applicants should be interested in outdoor activities, have suitable fitness, follow team management and be free of other commitments during the trip. Registration closes at **midnight on March 24**.

Participants must pay RMB 35 for outdoor insurance and RMB 75 for four days of food, a total of RMB 110. The organizers will provide the necessary camping equipment at no charge. The registration form, selection process and submission channel are provided in the original OA attachments.
