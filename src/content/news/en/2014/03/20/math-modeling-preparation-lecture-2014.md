The next session in Shantou University's **Into Modern Mathematics** lecture series will take place from **7:30 to 9 p.m. on March 21** in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). The lecture, titled **How to Prepare for Mathematical Modeling Competitions**, will be delivered by Dr. Li Jian and is open to interested students and faculty.

The session will introduce the background and value of mathematical modeling, use case studies to show how mathematical knowledge and skills can address real-world problems, and discuss preparation methods for the National Mathematical Modeling Competition. The 2014 competition is expected to take place in September.
