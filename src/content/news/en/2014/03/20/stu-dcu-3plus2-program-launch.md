Shantou University Liberal Arts School and the School of Applied Language and Intercultural Studies at Dublin City University have launched a **3+2 degree program**. Students complete their first three undergraduate years at STU, then study one further undergraduate year and one postgraduate year at DCU. Students who complete the requirements can receive a bachelor's degree from STU and bachelor's and master's degrees from DCU.

Students may apply for undergraduate tracks in Applied Language and Translation Studies, Languages for International Communication–English Studies, or Contemporary Culture and Society. Graduates meeting the relevant grade requirements may apply for, or receive direct admission to, postgraduate programs in Translation Studies, Translation Technology, Comparative Literature or Intercultural Studies. The program will select no more than **10 students** each year, and participants need to complete only one undergraduate thesis.

Applicants must meet the stated English-language requirements: at least 237 on TOEFL CBT, 92 on TOEFL iBT, 580 on TOEFL PBT, or 6.5 overall and 6.0 in each IELTS component. Applicants to Applied Language and Translation Studies must have an IELTS writing score of at least 6.5.

Students are responsible for tuition, accommodation, travel, insurance, medical and other expenses in Dublin. After a 15% reduction, annual undergraduate tuition is approximately RMB 83,000 to 88,000 depending on the track, while postgraduate tuition is about RMB 97,000; other expenses are estimated at RMB 100,000 per year. Visa applicants must purchase medical insurance, pay the fourth-year undergraduate tuition in full and provide proof of a deposit of at least EUR 7,000. Interested students should follow subsequent OA registration notices.
