The sixth Shantou University leadership-student dinner will take place at **6 p.m. on March 25** in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. The event will invite **32 full-time students** and provide a setting for students to exchange views with university leaders, functional-office representatives and faculty members on university development and student growth.

Interested students must log in to the reservation system at <http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm> and submit their registration by **10 p.m. on March 21**, confirming that they can attend on time. The system will close automatically when the quota is reached, and the invited-student list will be posted on the campus office-information network. The university will cover the meal cost.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
