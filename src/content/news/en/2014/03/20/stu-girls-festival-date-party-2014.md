The **“Meet You” party**, part of Shantou University's 10th Girls Festival, will take place from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on March 22**. The event will be held in front of the Auditorium, moving to Physics 201 in case of rain, and is open to all STU students and staff.

The program includes a song medley, magic, roller-skating, a lucky-girl segment and a campus waltz. Organizers ask attendees to safeguard personal belongings. Male attendees must wear long trousers and may not wear slippers. Further information is available through the official STU Student Union and Girls Festival social-media accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
