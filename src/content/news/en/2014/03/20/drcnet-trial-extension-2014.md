Shantou University Library has extended trial access to DRCnet Education Edition for faculty and student readers. The service includes full-text, statistical, research-report and topical databases. Its economics and management case database also provides business and public-administration cases, teaching cases from leading universities, practical resources, rankings and subject tags.

The trial will remain available through **September 30, 2014**. Readers can access it through the library database page at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=386>. The library welcomes feedback during the trial period.
