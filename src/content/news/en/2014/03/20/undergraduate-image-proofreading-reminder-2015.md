Shantou University Academic Affairs Office has reminded 2015 undergraduate graduates who submitted an **“incorrect”** result in the online digital-image check to complete follow-up procedures at the Student Affairs Section in the Academic Affairs Office lobby on the third floor of the New Administration Building by **March 31**.

For cases not handled by the deadline, the university will remove the original correction result, stop accepting further processing and continue using the photograph held by the China Higher-education Student Information website for degree and graduation-certificate production. Affected graduates should complete the procedure on time.
