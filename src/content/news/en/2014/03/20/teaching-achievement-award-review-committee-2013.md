Shantou University has established a committee to organize the review of its **2013 university-level teaching achievement awards**. Wen Xiaobo will chair the committee, whose members are Wang Weilian, Cai Yinghui, Du Hong, Xiong Guangjing, Yan Zhigang and Yang Qingjie.

The Academic Affairs Office issued the decision on **March 20, 2014**.
