It is reported that Shantou University and the National University of Ireland Galway have opened applications for a self-funded exchange program for the 2014–2015 academic year. The program offers 10 places to eligible second- and third-year students from across the university, except students in the Medical College. Participants may apply to NUI Galway schools other than Medicine, Nursing and Health Sciences.

## Requirements and Costs

Applicants must have a GPA of at least 3.75. English requirements are IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 80 for science and engineering, and IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 92 for arts, business, and law; no IELTS component may be below 5.5. Applicants must submit IELTS or TOEFL results to NUI Galway by May 30 at the latest.

Students are responsible for tuition, application and examination fees at NUI Galway, as well as international travel, insurance, accommodation, and living costs. As a reference, 2013–2014 tuition was EUR 6,000 per semester for arts, business, and law, and EUR 7,750 per semester for science and engineering. Estimated living costs, including accommodation, were about EUR 850 per month. Final 2014–2015 fees will be determined by NUI Galway.

## Application

Applicants must complete the form through the STU Student Affairs System, print it after obtaining their college's comments, and submit it to the International Cooperation Office with an English transcript, a copy of a birth certificate, a faculty recommendation letter, academic-contact details, a personal statement, and a passport-sized photograph. The internal deadline is April 20.

The International Cooperation Office provides project inquiries at 86503520 and wscicd@stu.edu.cn.
