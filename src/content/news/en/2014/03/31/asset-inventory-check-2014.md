Shantou University’s equipment management office plans to begin on-site asset checks on April 15, 2014, covering units that have moved to locations including the new Administration Center and the Resource Management Office building. The inventory is intended to update asset-location and usage records and prevent items from being lost during relocations.

Before the inspections, each unit should download its asset list from the university’s fixed-asset management system and check it item by item. The equipment management office will provide separate lists for equipment not yet entered into the system. Units can contact the office with questions about their lists.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
