Shantou University’s campus conduct office released findings from the fifth-week classroom checks of the spring 2013-2014 semester. Inspectors visited Building E at 8 a.m. and 2 p.m. on March 27 and recorded 12 instances of lateness in total.

The morning check found six students late by no more than five minutes and one late by more than five minutes. The afternoon check found two late by no more than five minutes and three by more than five minutes. The notice said a separate attachment contained the students’ names and had been sent to their schools; their identities are not published here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Campus Conduct Office)
> ⚠️ The student-list attachment is available on the OA system.
