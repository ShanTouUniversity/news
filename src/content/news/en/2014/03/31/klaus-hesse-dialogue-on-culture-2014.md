Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design held the 24th Dialogue on Culture at 7:30 p.m. on March 31, bringing together Klaus Hesse, dean of visual communication at Offenbach University of Art and Design in Germany, and Wang Shouzhi, dean of the Shantou University school.

The discussion took place in the school’s lecture hall and focused on design and culture in Germany. The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design organized the event with academic support from Backhill Forum.
