Shantou University Business School has launched a student exchange program with McGill University’s Desautels Faculty of Management and plans to select two students for one semester of study in Canada in fall 2014. The online application platform will be open from April 8 to 11. Applicants must submit printed materials to the Business School’s international programs office by noon on April 11 and email a digital copy to the designated school address.

The program is open to Business School undergraduates from the 2011, 2012 and 2013 cohorts. Applicants must meet the stated English requirements: IELTS 6.5 with no component below 6.0, or a computer-based TOEFL score of 90 with no component below 21. Their GPA must rank in the top 30 percent of their year. The school plans to hold interviews by April 18.

The Li Ka Shing Foundation will provide a semester exchange scholarship of up to CAD 33,236, which the notice said would cover tuition, living costs, accommodation and airfare. Students are responsible for other expenses, including overseas insurance, passports and visas. Applications require the university’s exchange form, proof of English proficiency, an English transcript and an essay of about 400 words.
