It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture on April 2. Professor Biing-Chiau Tzeng of National Chung Cheng University in Taiwan will discuss phase transitions in coordination-framework materials.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Phase Transition Materials of Dipyridylamide-Based Coordination Frameworks |
| Speaker | Professor Biing-Chiau Tzeng, National Chung Cheng University |
| Time | Wednesday, April 2, 2014, 9:30 a.m. |
| Venue | Grand Lecture Hall, Center for Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research |

The lecture will compare one-dimensional double-zigzag and two-dimensional polyrotaxane frameworks made with zinc ions and dipyridylamide ligands. It will discuss how water, anions, ligands, and metal ions affect structural conversion and luminescence, including transformations induced by heating, grinding, and moisture and the resulting mechanochromic luminescence.
