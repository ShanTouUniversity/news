It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University recently issued a notice arranging makeup sessions for missed content in the General Physics Experiment course, covering two classes numbered 68126 and 68127.

## Makeup Schedule

| Class Number | Date | Periods |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| 68126 | Wednesday of Week 7 (April 9) | Periods 5–7 |
| 68127 | Wednesday of Week 8 (April 16) | Periods 5–7 |

The Academic Affairs Office reminded students in the relevant classes to attend the makeup sessions on time as scheduled above.
