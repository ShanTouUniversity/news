Shantou University Business School will hold a briefing on its exchange program with the University of Victoria's Gustavson School of Business from 3:00 to 4:30 p.m. on Thursday, April 3. Representatives from the Canadian school will introduce the 2014-2015 exchange program and answer questions.

The briefing will take place in the seminar room on the second floor of the Business School. It is open to the school's undergraduates, graduate students, MBA students and course-program participants. The original notice said participants would cover their own tuition and living expenses; further program details will be available at the briefing.
