It is reported that Shantou University's spring 2014 Mandarin test training will be offered in two groups, with two sessions for each group. All sessions will take place in Lecture Hall 3. Students were asked to attend the sessions assigned to their group. The Mandarin test dates will be arranged separately by the Mandarin Training and Testing Center of Shantou Polytechnic.

## Training Schedule

| Group | First session | Second session | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | April 3, Thursday, 2:30 p.m. | April 10, Thursday, 2:30 p.m. | Lecture Hall 3 |
| Group 2 | April 23, Wednesday, 2:30 p.m. | April 30, Wednesday, 2:30 p.m. | Lecture Hall 3 |

The Academic Affairs Office advised students not to change their assigned group or training time except for special reasons. The test dates will be announced after the training concludes.
