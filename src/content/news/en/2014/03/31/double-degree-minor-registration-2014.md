It is reported that, in order to cultivate versatile talent and offer undergraduates opportunities for interdisciplinary and cross-major study, the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has launched registration for double degrees and minor programs for undergraduates in the 2013–2014 academic year. The registration is conducted in accordance with the Implementation Measures of Shantou University on Undergraduate Double Degrees and Minor Programs (STU Fa [2007] No. 78).

## Key Points

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Eligibility | Enrolled undergraduates on the main campus with active student status |
| Free enrollment | Double degrees and minors in English and Business Administration are free for students admitted in 2013 (Class of 2017), under the Implementation Rules for Free Double Degrees and Minor Programs (STU Jiao Zi [2013] No. 11). These two programs run as separate classes, possibly on weekends, holidays, or evenings |
| Other majors | Quotas and application requirements are listed in "Quotas and Application Requirements for Double Degree and Minor Students by Program." These programs follow the regular classes without separate sessions, so applicants should watch for timetable conflicts with their major courses |
| Course plans | Available in the Academic Guide (the Business School's plan is attached separately); questions may be directed to the academic advisors of the offering schools |

## Procedures

Students who wish to apply should complete the Registration Form for Undergraduate Double Degrees and Minor Programs, have their academic records verified by the academic advisor of their major school, and deliver the form to the academic advisor of the school offering the double degree or minor before April 11. Academic advisors of the receiving schools must compile the final list of accepted students and file it with the Academic Affairs Office by April 18.
