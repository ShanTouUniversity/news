It is reported that Shantou University's Law School will hold its 16th Academic Month from March through April. The program will feature a project-design contest and an essay competition open to Law School students.

## Two Competitions

The project-design contest accepts individual and team entries on topics related to society, law, management, economics, and education. Proposals should have practical value and demonstrate academic quality, advancement, and originality. Award-winning projects may also enter the university's undergraduate extracurricular academic and scientific project program, with projects receiving second prize or above eligible for recommendation.

The essay competition requires a topic related to the student's discipline, a main text of about 3,000 Chinese characters, an abstract of about 300 Chinese characters, and the prescribed format. Both competitions will offer first, second, and third prizes as well as merit awards, with certificates and appropriate rewards for winners.

## Submission

Electronic entries for both competitions must be submitted by April 18, together with the required supporting materials. The Law School will organize the review and recommend selected winning works for relevant university academic and research programs.
