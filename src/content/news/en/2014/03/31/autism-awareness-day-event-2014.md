The Shantou University Business School Young Volunteers Association will join the Shantou Autism Rehabilitation Center for a themed awareness event on April 2, World Autism Awareness Day. Organized by the Business School Youth League Committee and the university’s Young Volunteers Association, the event is open to the university community.

Activities at the Golden Triangle and Fourth Dining Hall will include an art display by autistic children, information materials, interactive games and a quiz. The organizers aim to increase understanding of autism.
