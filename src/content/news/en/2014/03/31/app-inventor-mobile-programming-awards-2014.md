Shantou University’s Engineering School announced the results of its fall 2013 App Inventor mobile programming contest. The competition awarded one first prize and seven second prizes. Winning projects included the campus-service app “Let the East Gate Be at Your Fingertips,” the games “Weapons” and “Tank Fighting,” as well as animation, memory-game and waste-sorting projects.

Winners were scheduled to collect prizes and certificates at 12:45 p.m. on April 4 in the academic exchange room on the third floor of the school’s Gongnan building. The students’ names are not published to protect their privacy.
