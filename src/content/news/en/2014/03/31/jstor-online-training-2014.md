It is reported that the Shantou University Library, in collaboration with East View Information Co., is holding a series of online training sessions on the JSTOR database from March 25 to May 20, 2014.

The training will be delivered live online every Tuesday and Thursday at 2:30 p.m. Each session covers the same content, allowing faculty and students to choose a time slot that best fits their schedule.

## Training Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Topic | JSTOR Database Training |
| Schedule | March 25 – May 20, 2014, every Tuesday & Thursday, 2:30 p.m. |
| Format | Live online training (WebEx Meeting) |
| Meeting ID | 194 566 072 (no password required) |
| Join Link | https://meetings.webex.com/collabs/meetings/join?uuid=M1V75JPHSW5GBEAWUFJ49WFN9I-4O2 |

The Library encourages all faculty and students to participate and enhance their academic resource retrieval skills.
