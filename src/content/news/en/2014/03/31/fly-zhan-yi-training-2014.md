Shantou University’s Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union will launch the fourth “Zhan Yi” cohort of the FLY training program in April, with 25 places planned. Training is scheduled for April 9, 13, 19, 26 and 27, and May 10.

Topics include game-theory thinking and creative leadership lectures, a professional ethics workshop, a mock hearing, a summit forum, and outdoor survival and team-building activities. The program is open to student-organization leaders and students who have completed the “Shi Yi” cohort. Applications close at midnight on April 6. If applications exceed capacity, participants will be selected at random.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
> ⚠️ The application form and training attachments are available on the OA system.
