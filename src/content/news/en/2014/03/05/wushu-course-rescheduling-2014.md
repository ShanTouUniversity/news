It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on March 5, 2014, that two sections of *Wushu (4)* (classes 67191 and 67192), taught by Ms. Zheng Liru, will be rescheduled due to the instructor’s leave.

## Rescheduling

| Class | Cancelled | Make-up | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 67191 | Week 2, Thu, periods 3–4 (March 6) | Week 2, Sat, periods 6–7 (March 8) | Pedestrian street in front of the Gymnasium |
| 67192 | Week 2, Thu, periods 8–9 (March 6) | Week 2, Sat, periods 8–9 (March 8) | Pedestrian street in front of the Gymnasium |

Students enrolled are asked to inform each other.
