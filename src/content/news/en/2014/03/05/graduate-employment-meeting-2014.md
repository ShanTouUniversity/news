According to a notice from the Student Affairs Office, Shantou University will hold a graduate employment work meeting at 10 a.m. on March 7, 2014, in Conference Room 213 of the New Administration Center. Party branch secretaries and deputy secretaries of schools and colleges, employment counselors, and staff from the Graduate Employment Guidance Center will attend.

The meeting will coordinate and arrange upcoming graduate employment work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
