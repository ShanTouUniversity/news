STU Library announced trial access to Emerald’s Emerging Markets Case Collection for university students and faculty. The online collection focuses on business decisions and management development in major emerging markets and includes nearly 300 peer-reviewed teaching cases for educators, students and business professionals.

Cases are available in PDF and HTML formats and include teaching notes, usually covering a case overview, learning objectives, suggested discussion topics and questions and answers. The collection can be accessed at http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=388. Trial access will remain available through April 30, 2014.

Feedback may be sent to the library’s subject services team at 86503247, lqxie@stu.edu.cn or zhzeng@stu.edu.cn.
