According to the Student Affairs Office, the final round of Shantou University selection for the first Guangdong student contest on industry, majors and employment talent demand analysis was held on February 26, 2014, in Lecture Hall E201. Eleven teams received first-, second- or third-place awards, or an excellence award.

| Award | Teams |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | C Future; Qiangqiang Four-Person Team |
| Second prize | NB; NINO; The Successors |
| Third prize | KY; Lao Xueshu Group; Fangzhen Team; Aiyou Not Bad; Kaosi Team |
| Excellence award | MIRACLE |

Detailed information about the winning teams is available in the attachment to the OA notice.
