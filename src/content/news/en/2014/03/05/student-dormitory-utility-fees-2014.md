The Resource Management Office announced the metering and payment schedule for student dormitory utility fees for January 2014 and the winter vacation period. January metering covered January 1 to 23. Winter-vacation charges covered January 23 and February 18, with meter reading on February 19.

Students can check their charges through the STU utility-fee management system at http://power.stu.edu.cn/. Errors or questions may be submitted between March 6 and 12 during working hours by delivering a signed review application to the Water and Electricity Center beside the Science and Technology Building. The center can be reached at 86502489. Students whose dormitory information does not match the student list should contact their counselor office at 86503355.

If no objection is raised by the end of the review period, the Network Center will deduct the fees through the campus card from March 13. The regular payment period is March 13–17; overdue accounts will be handled under university rules from March 18. Corrections requested after the review period may take three to five working days. Students were also reminded to top up campus cards with insufficient balances.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
> ⚠️ Detailed utility statements and the review application are available in the attachment on the OA system.
