The Communist Youth League of Shantou University has arranged the collection and management of 2013 membership fees. Youth League committees of schools and colleges and directly affiliated branches are required to manage collection and use according to the relevant regulations.

Each organization should designate staff to oversee the work, standardize collection procedures and submit the 2013 fees and a self-review report to the university Youth League Committee office by March 28, 2014. The work follows the Guangdong Youth League regulations on the collection, management and use of membership fees.
