It is reported that, in accordance with the notice of the Guangdong Provincial Examination Authority on the registration for the first 2014 national College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 (Yue Kao Yuan Ban [2014] No. 40), the Graduate School of Shantou University has arranged registration arrangements for graduate students on the main campus.

**Exam Schedule**

| Level | Time |
| --- | --- |
| CET-4 | June 14, 9:00–11:20 |
| CET-6 | June 14, 15:00–17:20 |

**Eligibility**

| Level | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| CET-4 | All enrolled graduate students on the main campus |
| CET-6 | Students who hold a CET-4 certificate or scored 425 or above in CET-4; students holding a Test for English Majors Band 4 certificate; those who already hold a CET-6 certificate or scored 425 or above may retake the test, but no more than twice after reaching 425 |

Candidates for CET-6 must submit the relevant certificates or score reports to the graduate secretary of their school for verification.

**Registration and Payment**

Online registration runs from March 5 to March 14. Graduate students log in to the STU graduate registration system with their campus network accounts, select Level 4 or Level 6 and submit. The registration fee is 36 yuan, which will be deducted from students' campus card accounts by the Finance Office via the Network Center on March 17; if unsuccessful, it will be deducted from tuition accounts through Bank of China on the morning of March 19. Students are reminded to keep sufficient balance during this period — a failed deduction means a failed registration.

After registering, students must also confirm at the graduate secretary's office of their school (department, institute) before the registration deadline, bearing responsibility for any overdue confirmation.

**Notes**

According to relevant regulations, non-enrolled candidates may not register for this round of the exams. Candidates must bring their ID card, student ID and admission ticket; anyone missing any of these documents will not be admitted to the exam room.
