It is reported that Shantou University and University West in Sweden have launched a graduate overseas-study program for the 2014–2015 academic year. University West will offer tuition discounts to participating STU students, and applications are now open.

## Program Information

| Program | Discounted tuition |
| --- | ---: |
| International Business, 60 credits (one year) | SEK 80,000 |
| Work Integrated Learning with a focus on Information Systems, 60 credits (one year) | SEK 80,000 |
| Work Integrated Learning with a focus on Information Systems, 120 credits (two years) | SEK 160,000 |
| Manufacturing Engineering, 60 credits (one year) | SEK 100,000 |
| Robotics, 60 credits (one year) | SEK 100,000 |

The notice used an exchange rate of approximately RMB 0.96 per SEK. Tuition does not include international travel, accommodation or living expenses.

## Eligibility and Deadline

The program is open to graduate students enrolled at STU’s main campus. Applicants must have an IELTS score of 6.5 or a CET-6 result and meet University West admission requirements. The internal application deadline is **March 26, 2014**. Applicants must submit an application form endorsed by their school, along with scans of English-language proof and an English transcript, to the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation.

The office can be reached at 86503520 or wscicd@stu.edu.cn and is located in Room 240, second floor, New Administrative Center.
