STU Engineering School has begun the final review of its 2013 student extracurricular science and technology projects that passed the interim review. Project leaders must submit printed and electronic completion reports to their department Communist Youth League offices by **March 13**. The offices must forward the collected materials to the Engineering School office by 11:30 a.m. that day.

Project leaders who need more time may submit a completion-extension report and a feasible progress plan with their supervisor's signature and comments. The final review will be held within each department rather than as a school-wide defense. Leaders must also prepare a five-minute presentation, a physical item or photographs, and other evidence of project completion.

Projects whose leaders do not attend the required final review will be considered abandoned. Related reimbursement must be completed by **March 28**.
