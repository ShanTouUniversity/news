STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication is recruiting third-year journalism students for summer 2014 internships in Taiwan and exchange study during the first semester of 2014–2015. Applications close at **5 p.m. on March 15**. Students may apply for both programs but can be admitted to only one; Taiwan and Hong Kong internships are also mutually exclusive.

The Taiwan internship program plans to select 12 students, including placements with news agencies, newspapers, online media, advertising companies and television stations. The program runs from **July 30 to September 9**. Students will intern at media organizations from August 1 to 31 and take part in group visits from September 1 to 9. The school will cover round-trip airfare, travel-document and endorsement fees, medical insurance and a housing subsidy, but not living expenses.

The exchange program plans to select two students to study at the School of Journalism and Communication at Chinese Culture University in Taiwan for up to four months during the first semester of 2014–2015. The school will cover airfare, visa and semester medical-insurance costs, but not living expenses. Interviews are tentatively scheduled for **4 p.m. on March 20** in the school’s glass room. Graduate students may also apply for self-funded study at the university’s graduate institute of journalism, with places selected separately.
