Annual 2014 health examinations for STU employees who selected the University Hospital will begin on **March 13** at the University Hospital. The standard program includes general examinations and laboratory tests. Staff meeting the age, management or professional-title criteria will also receive tests including blood glucose, blood lipids, alpha-fetoprotein, kidney function, uric acid, carcinoembryonic antigen, a frontal chest X-ray, electrocardiography and abdominal ultrasound.

Retired employees, employees aged 40 or above, mid-level and above managers, and employees with mid-level or above professional titles will receive laboratory and ultrasound examinations according to the schedule without booking. Other employees can arrange laboratory tests during their examination. Auxiliary contract workers will attend according to their units’ arrangements. The University Hospital will provide free breakfast for those receiving laboratory and ultrasound examinations.
