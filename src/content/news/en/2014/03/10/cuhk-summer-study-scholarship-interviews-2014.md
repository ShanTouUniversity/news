STU Student Affairs will hold the interview for the 2014 Chinese University of Hong Kong International Summer Study Program scholarship from **2:30 to 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 12** in Room 221 of the Administrative Center.

The interview will use a leaderless group discussion format. It will include English self-introductions, a leaderless group discussion and questions from the panel. Shortlisted students should prepare name cards and arrive at 2:20 p.m. to receive further instructions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs)
>
> ⚠️ The shortlist and interview arrangements are available in the original OA attachment.
