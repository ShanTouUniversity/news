The STU Library has featured *Selected Couplets from the Dajia Lecture Series: Professor Jao Tsung-i’s Calligraphic Couplets* in its Chaoshan literature recommendation series. The book contains **120 couplets** written by Professor Jao Tsung-i in recent years and showcases his work in seal, clerical, regular, running and cursive scripts.

It also includes notes on calligraphy from the works and a chronology of Jao Tsung-i’s artistic career, giving the collection value for calligraphy study and research into the artistic ideas of scholar-calligraphers. The book was edited by Lin Jiangshan and Xu Xiaosheng.
