STU’s School of Science will hold the next event in its Science Salon series at **4:15 p.m. on Tuesday, March 11**. Professor Chen Huanyang of the School of Physical Science and Technology at Soochow University will give a talk titled “Gradient index metamaterials and transformation optics.”

The talk will take place in the School of Science Lecture Hall (Li-352). It will discuss broadband effects including unidirectional propagation and cloaking in gradient-index metamaterials, as well as a transformation-optics framework using artificial Riemann sheets and an example of a non-Euclidean cloak.

Chen is a distinguished professor and doctoral supervisor at Soochow University whose research focuses on transformation optics and metamaterials.
