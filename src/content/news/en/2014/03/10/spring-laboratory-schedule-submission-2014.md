It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office has asked each school or department to organize the submission of laboratory schedules for the 2013–2014 spring semester undergraduate courses.

Units must complete the forms by **March 18** and submit the electronic version first, followed by two printed copies. Electronic files should be sent to yzchen@stu.edu.cn. Laboratory directors in each department will collect the printed copies and submit one to the Academic Affairs Office while the other is retained by the department’s academic administrator.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
