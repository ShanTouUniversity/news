It is reported that the Primary School Affiliated to Shantou University will hold a campus open day on March 12 and 13, featuring introductory lectures and live demonstrations on intelligent robots at the sixth-floor auditorium, open to parents.

According to the school, the event aims to advance quality education and cultivate students' innovative spirit and practical skills. During the open day, parents can also meet class teachers face to face to discuss their children's performance at school.
