It is reported that the Sasin Graduate Institute of Business Administration of Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok will hold a graduate-level business case competition, with STU Business School inviting students to participate. Each team must have four students and register online by **April 11, 2014**.

The qualifying round is scheduled for **April 28**. A judging panel will select 12 top teams for the semifinal round in Bangkok, Thailand. Teams may register at http://www.scbflc.com/apply-online/ and find competition information at http://www.scbflc.com/.

Questions may be directed to King Wu of STU Business School at kingkng@stu.edu.cn.
