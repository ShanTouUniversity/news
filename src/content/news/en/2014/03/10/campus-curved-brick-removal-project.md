STU has begun removing and recovering the curved-brick pavement between the infrastructure building and the vegetable shop. The project was commissioned to Shantou Xinhua Construction Co. and is scheduled to run from **March 10 to March 13**.

The work may cause noise, vibration and dust. The university asks pedestrians and vehicles to take care near the site and avoid lingering nearby.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
