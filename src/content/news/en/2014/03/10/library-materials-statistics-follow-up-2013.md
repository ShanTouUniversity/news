The STU Library has issued a follow-up reminder for campus resource rooms that have not yet submitted their **2013 library-material statistics**. By the time of the notice, the resource rooms of the School of Liberal Arts, Social Sciences Department, Business School and Physical Education Department had submitted their data.

The remaining resource rooms must complete the attached form and email it to `gnweng@stu.edu.cn` by **March 14**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Library)
>
> ⚠️ The library-material statistics form is available in the original OA attachment.
