STU’s Three-Style Development Office reported that its inspection team checked teaching buildings on March 6 and 7, 2014, during the second week of the Spring 2013–2014 semester.

The team inspected the DG teaching building at 8:00 a.m. on March 6 and the E teaching building at 2:00 p.m. on March 7. No violations of university rules were found in either inspection.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Three-Style Development Office)
