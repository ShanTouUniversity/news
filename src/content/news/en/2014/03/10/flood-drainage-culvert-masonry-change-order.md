It is reported that STU’s flood-drainage culvert masonry project began on **February 26**. During construction, the height difference between the reinforced-concrete culvert and the masonry retaining wall was found to require additional work, including a sloped masonry retaining wall.

The Infrastructure Office preliminarily estimates the additional work at approximately **RMB 3,800**, below the university’s 10-percent threshold for additional project costs. Under the university’s infrastructure tendering rules, the proposed increase is open for public comment. Written comments must be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by **5:30 p.m. on March 13**.
