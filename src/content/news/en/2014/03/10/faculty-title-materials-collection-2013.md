STU Human Resources has retrieved the 2013 professional-title application materials and continuing-education certificates, including annual certificates. The Schools of Liberal Arts, Science, Engineering, Law and Business, the Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design, the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, the Graduate School, the Social Sciences Department, the Physical Education Department and Zhicheng College must send representatives to Room 315 of the New Administrative Building to collect their units’ materials by **March 14**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources)
