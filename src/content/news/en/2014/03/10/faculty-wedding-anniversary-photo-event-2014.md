The STU Labor Union will organize the “Beauty of the Years” commemorative wedding-photo event from **March to April**. Employees married in 1989, marking 25 years of marriage, and those married in 1984, marking 30 years, can receive a free photo session and one free 20-inch crystal photograph.

Eligible employees who are not on the current list may also register. Registration closes on **March 20**. Applicants may email the registration form to `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn` or register by phone. Participants will use their invitations to visit Vman Vision outdoor wedding photography in Shantou for the session.
