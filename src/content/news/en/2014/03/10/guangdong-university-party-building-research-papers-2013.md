It is reported that STU’s Publicity and United Front Work Office has forwarded a call for papers for the annual conference of the undergraduate-institution branch of the Guangdong Association for University Party-Building Research. The call is open to teachers engaged in Party-building research and social-science theory research.

Unpublished papers may also be submitted, and papers should generally be no longer than 5,000 Chinese characters. STU’s internal deadline is **March 17, 2014**. Papers should be sent to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn, after which the university will recommend submissions to the conference. Topics and detailed requirements follow the original call.
