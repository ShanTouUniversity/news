It is reported that the STU Graduate School announced the list of graduate teaching and administrative assistant positions for Spring 2014 on March 10, 2014. The positions were submitted by the university units that host the assistantships and reviewed by the Graduate School.

Host units will appoint, manage and assess the graduate students in accordance with university regulations. The detailed appointment list is available in the original OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Graduate School)
>
> ⚠️ The graduate assistant appointment list and related attachments are available on the OA system.
