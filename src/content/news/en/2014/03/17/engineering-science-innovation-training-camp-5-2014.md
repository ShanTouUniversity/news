Shantou University's Engineering School will hold its fifth Student Science and Technology Innovation Camp from **March 23 to 30** under the theme “Green Technology, Harmonious Innovation.” The camp is organized by the Engineering School Youth League Committee and the school's Student Science and Technology Development Center.

The camp is intended for first- and second-year students interested in applying for the Engineering School's 2014 student science and technology innovation projects. It aims to encourage participation in innovation practice and competitions. Registration closes at **noon on March 21**. The activity plan and registration form are available in the original OA notice.
