STU's Academic Affairs Office has announced a schedule change for class 67436 of Colorful World of Biology, offered by the Institute of Marine Biology. The class scheduled for **the sixth and seventh periods on Thursday, March 20** will not be held as planned.

The make-up class will take place during the **AB periods on Saturday, March 29**, in **Room D304**. Students and relevant schools should follow the revised schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
