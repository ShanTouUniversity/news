Shantou University’s Research Office has circulated a notice opening applications for the 2014 National Philosophy and Social Sciences Achievement Library. Established by the National Office for Philosophy and Social Sciences, the library promotes outstanding research at the forefront of the field and highlights exemplary scholars and achievements.

Applicants should generally be full-time research personnel, including retired researchers, who hold an associate-senior or higher professional title and are the first author of the proposed work. A submission must be completed but not yet publicly published, have no intellectual-property dispute, and demonstrate originality, breadth and academic significance. Eligible formats include Chinese academic monographs, thematic collections of papers and thematic research reports, generally 200,000 to 600,000 Chinese characters. Textbooks, translations, individual papers, reference books, data compilations and popular works are excluded.

Applicants must email the application form to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` for preliminary review by June 2 and submit the application, printed manuscript, summary and electronic disc to the Research Office by June 9. The notice also requires application materials to avoid directly or indirectly revealing the applicant’s name, institution or other background information.
