The 74th Basic Party Knowledge Course at the Shantou University Party School will begin on **March 23**. Participants are required to register with their group leaders and collect study materials from **March 20 to 21**, bringing a one-inch, front-facing ID photograph.

Group leaders will meet at **1:00 p.m. on March 19** in Wenbei 206. Participants who need leave during the course must complete a leave-approval form, obtain review from their Party branch, and submit it to the Party School Office. Those who fail to register without a valid reason, or are absent from classes or examinations without authorization, will be handled under the relevant rules.

The participant list, group-leader contacts and course schedule are provided in the original OA attachments and are not reproduced here.
