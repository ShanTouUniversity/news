Shantou University's Study-Style Development Office issued Bulletin No. 255 on classroom inspections during the third week of the 2013-2014 spring semester. Both checks took place in **Building E**:

| Inspection time | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 2:00 p.m., March 13 | Three students were more than five minutes late |
| 10:10 a.m., March 14 | Four students were no more than five minutes late |

The bulletin asked schools and residential colleges to provide education based on the findings. It also reminded students to observe classroom rules, avoid arriving late or leaving early, and refrain from wearing slippers or bringing breakfast into classrooms. Student names in the original attachment are not reproduced.
