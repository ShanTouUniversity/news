Shantou University’s Research Office has circulated a call from the National Steering Committee for Ideological and Political Theory Teaching in Higher Education. Commissioned by the Ministry of Education’s Department of Social Sciences, the call seeks analyses of key and difficult teaching issues to strengthen research and classroom application.

Submissions should explain the different formulations and substance of an issue, provide a focused answer, identify the relevant course or chapter, and list supporting and supplementary materials. The committee asks for analyses that are thorough, targeted and accessible for classroom use. Each key issue constitutes one proposed topic; outstanding analyses reaching publication standards may receive support through a Ministry of Education special research project on ideological and political theory courses.

Applicants must be full-time or part-time teachers of ideological and political theory courses at regular Chinese universities. Each applicant may submit one topic, and a project member may participate in no more than two applications. Guangdong will impose a provincial quota of no more than 20 topics. Electronic forms are due to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` for review by April 10, while signed final forms and hard-copy research materials are due to the Research Office by April 16.
