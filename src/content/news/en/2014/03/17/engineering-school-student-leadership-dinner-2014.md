Shantou University's Engineering School will hold a dinner for students and school leaders from **6:30 to 8:00 p.m. on March 26** at Hongtai Restaurant. The dean, Party secretary, deputy dean and department heads will attend to exchange views with students and hear suggestions about the school's development.

The event is open to all Engineering School students and will invite **25 student representatives**. Interested students must submit registration materials through the university's booking system by **10:00 p.m. on March 24** and may include issues they would like to discuss. Registration will close automatically when the quota is reached, and the selected representatives will be announced on the internal office information network.

The Engineering School will cover the dinner costs. It encourages participants to collect concerns from fellow students and contribute to a constructive exchange.
