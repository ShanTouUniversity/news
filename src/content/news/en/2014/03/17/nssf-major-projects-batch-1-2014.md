The first batch of 2014 National Social Sciences Fund major projects has opened for nationwide applications. It covers **95 research directions**, with one or two winning projects generally selected for each direction. Funding is generally **RMB 800,000 per project**, and projects usually have a two-year completion period.

Applications must be submitted in the name of an institution whose research and administration conditions meet the requirements. Principal investigators must hold a senior professional title or a leadership position at bureau level or above and have relevant research and project-management experience. Principal investigators of certain ongoing national major projects may not submit another application in the same call, and limits also apply to participation by sub-project leaders and team members.

At STU, applicants must send an electronic application to the Research Office for formal review by **May 13** and submit nine signed hard copies together with the final electronic version by **May 16**. The research-direction list, application forms and detailed requirements are provided in the OA attachments.
