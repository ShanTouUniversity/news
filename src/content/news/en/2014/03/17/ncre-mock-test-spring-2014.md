The spring 2014 National Computer Rank Examination will be held in batches on **March 29**. To test the examination software and system environment, Shantou University will hold a mock test in **Room E102 on the afternoon of March 19**.

The test will operate on a first-arrival basis and have two sessions. The first, from **2:30 to 4:00 p.m.**, is for candidates registered for Levels 1 and 2. The second, from **4:10 to 5:00 p.m.**, is for candidates registered for Levels 3 and 4. All levels and language subjects will use a paperless examination format.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
