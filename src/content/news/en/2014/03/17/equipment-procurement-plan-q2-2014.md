Shantou University has opened the application process for second-quarter 2014 equipment procurement plans. Units must submit hard-copy and electronic materials to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by **April 10**. Plans submitted late will be deferred to the third quarter of 2014.

For equipment with a total budget of **RMB 20,000 or less**, departments may purchase directly through the university equipment bidding website. Projects above RMB 20,000 require a plan and proposal and must complete the relevant approval process. Equipment priced above **RMB 100,000** also requires a feasibility report. Computer and air-conditioner purchases require additional information specified in the notice.

Units should complete every field of the plan and include taxes, transportation, warranty and after-sales service in the procurement budget. The equipment catalogue, forms, proposal and report templates are provided in the original OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
>
> ⚠️ The equipment plan, proposal, feasibility report and related attachments are available on the OA system.
