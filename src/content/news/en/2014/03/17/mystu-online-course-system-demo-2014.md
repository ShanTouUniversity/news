Shantou University's Mystu online course system has completed its initial development. The Academic Affairs Office will hold demonstrations for faculty members on **March 19 and 20**, covering online course creation, student learning workflows and other functions while collecting feedback.

Each teaching unit should recommend **two to three faculty members**. The Chinese-language session is scheduled for 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. on March 19, and the English-language session for 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. on March 20. Recommended names should be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office by the end of the morning on **March 18**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
