STU's Academic Affairs Office has announced a schedule change for class 68042 of Biodiversity and Human Well-Being, offered by the Institute of Marine Biology. The class scheduled for **the 12th period on Thursday, March 20** will not be held as planned.

The make-up class will take place during the **AB periods on Sunday, March 23**, in **Room D304**. Students and relevant schools should follow the revised schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
