It is reported that, as of March 17, a number of student dormitories had still not paid their water and electricity fees for February 2014 or earlier. In accordance with university regulations, Shantou University will cut power supply to these dormitories starting March 18, and service will be restored only after all arrears are settled.

To make repayment easier, the Network Center will run campus card deductions twice a day from March 18 to 20. The first deduction takes place at 10:30 a.m., with power restored to successfully paying dorms between 2:30 p.m. and 4:30 p.m. the same day; the second takes place at 4:30 p.m., with power restored between 5:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. Dorms that pay after 4:30 p.m. on a given day will have power restored the next day between 2:30 p.m. and 4:30 p.m. From March 21 onward, deductions will be made once daily at 4:30 p.m.

## Deduction and Power Restoration Schedule

| Date | Deduction Time | Power Restoration After Successful Payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| March 18–20 | 10:30 (first round) | Same day, 2:30–4:30 p.m. |
| March 18–20 | 16:30 (second round) | Same day, 5:30–8:00 p.m. |
| From March 21 | Once daily at 16:30 | Payments after 4:30 p.m.: next day, 2:30–4:30 p.m. |

Students whose campus card balance is insufficient are advised to top up at a transfer machine promptly. Those with insufficient bank account balances should deposit enough funds during banking hours before transferring the money to their cards. Students who have lost their campus card can either set up payment via a proxy through the "Water and Electricity Management System," or deposit cash into the Shantou University account (account number: 628857760147) at the Bank of China STU branch and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for recording. The Finance Office does not accept cash payments directly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, Resources Management Office)
