The seventh National College Students Internet Business Innovation Application Contest is under way, and Shantou University’s Business School is encouraging students to take part. Guided by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the Ministry of Education, the contest aims to strengthen students’ internet application, e-commerce and innovation skills.

Students may register, form teams, choose a business problem and submit a proposal through the contest website. The six themes are internet finance and payments, internet trade, online retail, internet marketing and planning, business-model innovation, and innovative application development.

The schedule includes preliminary rounds and campus tours from March 1 to June 30, an elite training camp from May to October, the second round from July 1 to August 31, regional finals from September to mid-October, and the national final in mid-November. Each stage will include its own awards and recognition. Detailed rules are available at `www.newwinner.cn`; questions may be sent to `11llsun@stu.edu.cn`.
