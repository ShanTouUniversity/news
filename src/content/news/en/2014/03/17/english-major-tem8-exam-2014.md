The 2014 Test for English Majors-Band 8 (TEM-8) will begin at **8:30 a.m. on Saturday, March 22** and last approximately 195 minutes. The examination consists of listening comprehension, reading comprehension, general knowledge, proofreading and error correction, translation, and writing, with answers recorded on the designated answer sheets.

Candidates may not enter after 15 minutes have elapsed, leave the test site before the examination ends, or submit papers during the final 30 minutes. They may submit papers only after the first 60 minutes. Mobile phones, dictionaries and other reference books are not permitted in the examination room.

Candidates must bring their admission ticket, student ID card and national ID card. Candidates without complete identification will not be admitted. Multiple-choice questions must be answered with a 2B pencil, while the other designated sections require a black-ink pen.
