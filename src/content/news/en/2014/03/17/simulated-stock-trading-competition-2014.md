Shantou University's Business Practice Center will hold the “Stock Market Simulation” competition from **March 24 to April 18** for all students and staff. The activity is designed to help participants understand financial markets and develop financial-management and risk-awareness skills through simulated securities trading.

Registration runs from **March 17 to 23**. The opening ceremony and investment-knowledge training will take place from **2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on March 23** in Room E201. A second professional training session is scheduled for April 2, and the closing and awards ceremony for April 18; the time and venue for those sessions will be announced separately.

Participants will use simulated-trading accounts supplied by the organizers during the month-long competition. Weekly rankings will be based on investment returns, with additional awards determined after the competition. Registration details and updates will be issued through the OA system, the Business School BBS board and subsequent notices from the Business Practice Center.
