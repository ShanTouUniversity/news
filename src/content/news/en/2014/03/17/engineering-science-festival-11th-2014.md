Shantou University's Engineering School will hold its 11th Science and Technology Festival from **March to April** under the theme “Technology, Practice and Innovation.” The festival is for all enrolled Engineering School students and is organized by the school's Youth League Committee and Student Science and Technology Development Center. It combines innovation practice and competitions to develop students' project, teamwork and communication skills.

The opening ceremony is scheduled for **March 23** in Room E203. The fifth Student Science and Technology Innovation Camp will run from March 23 to 30 at the same venue. The other events are:

| Event | Date | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACM Programming Competition | April 13 | Wenxi 302 |
| Electronic Design and Production Competition | April 12-13 | Engineering School CDIO Innovation Laboratory |
| Civil Engineering Structural Design Competition | April 12 | To be confirmed |
| Closing ceremony | April | To be confirmed |

Competition rules, registration forms and detailed registration arrangements are available in the original OA attachments.
