A line fault in the Guangdong Education Network in Guangzhou disrupted Shantou University's education-network connection at 16:08 on March 15. The Network and Information Center reported that campus-network users temporarily could not access education-network websites, while off-campus education-network users could not access STU's internal network.

The notice did not give a restoration time and asked users to share the information. The Network and Information Center can be reached at 86503400 or stunic@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
