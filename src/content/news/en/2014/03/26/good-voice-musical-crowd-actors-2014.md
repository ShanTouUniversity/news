The production team of The Voice of China plans to film a musical at Shantou University’s gymnasium on April 5 and 6. To give students an opportunity to participate in artistic production and social practice, the university will recruit 400 student extras.

Applicants should be responsible and punctual and be able to follow the production team’s schedule. Applications are due by 6 p.m. on April 1. The application materials and submission process are provided in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
