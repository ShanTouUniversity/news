Shantou University’s Business School established a teaching quality assurance committee in March 2014 to advance undergraduate teaching and strengthen quality assurance. The school’s executive committee approved its membership for a three-year term.

徐二明 was named adviser and 卜祥智 director. Members were 龙月娥, 刘瑜, 池启水, 曾凡奇 and 曾宪扬, with 许筱菁 serving as secretary.
