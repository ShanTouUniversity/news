It is reported that the 18th University Student Debate Competition of Shantou University is about to begin. Organized by the Communist Youth League Committee and the Student Union, the competition is themed "Seeking Truth Through Debate, Enlightening Minds Through Discourse." It aims to carry forward the tradition of debate, cultivate critical thinking, enhance cultural literacy, and enliven campus culture.

## Schedule

| Date | Stage |
|------|-------|
| March 29 | Opening ceremony — "STU Talk" edition of the 18th Debate Competition |
| April 12–13 | First round of preliminaries (4 matches) |
| April 19–20 | Second round — repechage (2 matches) |
| April 26–27 | Quarterfinals (4 matches) |
| May 10 | Semifinals (2 matches) |
| May 17–18 | Finals — championship and third-place matches (2 matches) |

Matches will be held at the Science Lecture Hall and the Moot Court. Participating teams are the debate teams from each college.

## Format and Rules

The competition is divided into three stages and adopts a repechage elimination system, with match-ups and sides determined by drawing lots. Notably, the champion and runner-up teams from the 17th edition will enter the quarterfinals directly as seeded teams, without pre-assigned brackets, joining the general draw. The four teams eliminated in the first round of preliminaries will enter the repechage to compete for the remaining quarterfinal spots.

Each match lasts 43 minutes, with four starting debaters per team. Judges score the teams based on their performance, and the team with the higher score wins. Debate topics and the line-up for each match will be announced one week in advance.

## Awards

| Award | Selection Method | Prize |
|-------|-----------------|-------|
| Best Debater (1 per match) | Judges' evaluation | Certificate |
| Most Popular Debater (1 per match) | Audience vote | Certificate and souvenir |
| Most Popular Team (1 team) | Weibo repost count (@STU Academic Planning Department) | Certificate |
| Champion, Runner-up, Third Place | Competition results | Certificate, trophy, and souvenir |

Updates will be available via Sina Weibo @STU Academic Planning Department.
