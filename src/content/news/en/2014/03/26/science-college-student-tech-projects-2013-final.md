It is reported that the College of Science has announced the final review results for its 2013 Student Extracurricular Science and Technology Projects. A total of 16 projects were approved during the year, of which 15 were completed and participated in the final defense.

After rigorous evaluation by an expert panel, 6 projects received awards and 9 were rated as qualified. One project that failed to attend the defense was deemed to have withdrawn voluntarily, rated as unqualified, with its funding recalled. The College stated that awards and certificates will be presented to outstanding projects to encourage a spirit of exploration and innovation among students.

## Review Outcomes

| Grade | Count | Note |
|-------|-------|------|
| First Prize | 2 | Award and certificate |
| Second Prize | 4 | Award and certificate |
| Qualified | 9 | Passed final review |
| Unqualified | 1 | Did not attend defense, deemed withdrawn, funding recalled |
| Total approved | 16 | 15 completed defense |

Project leaders who have passed review and submitted closing reports are reminded to complete follow-up summaries and finalize reimbursement of project funds by April 1, 2014. The detailed list of awardees and projects is available in the attachment.
