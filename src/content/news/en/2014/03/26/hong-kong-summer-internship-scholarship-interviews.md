Shantou University’s Law School has selected 37 students for interviews for the 2014 Hong Kong summer internship scholarship program. The group includes 19 students from the Law major and 18 from Public Administration.

Interviews for the Law major will begin at 9 a.m. on Friday, March 28, while Public Administration interviews will begin at 9 a.m. on Monday, March 31. Both sessions will be held in Room 206 of the Law School’s Wenbei Building. Interviews will follow the listed order and last about 10 minutes per person; candidates are asked to dress formally. The names are omitted here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Law School)
