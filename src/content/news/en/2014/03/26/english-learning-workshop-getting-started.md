The Center for International Language Learning (CILL) at Shantou University will hold “How do I improve my English: Getting started” from 6 to 8 p.m. on Thursday, April 3, at CILL.

Zhou Qian and Nana Wei will offer practical guidance on building a language-learning plan. The workshop is intended for participants who want to improve a language skill but are unsure how to begin. Places are limited; reservations are available through Eventbrite at <https://llpws2.eventbrite.com>.
