Shantou University’s Graduate School opened nominations for the 20th Graduate Student Union executive committee and planned to hold its transition election meeting in early April 2014. The meeting would review the 19th committee’s work, elect the new presidium and set priorities for the next term.

The proposed committee would comprise the presidium and eight members. The presidium would include one chair and two vice-chairs. Presidium candidates were to apply in teams of three, while candidates for committee seats could apply individually. Applicants had to meet the university’s student-cadre requirements and demonstrate relevant experience or ability to serve. Nominations were due by 5 p.m. on April 3.
