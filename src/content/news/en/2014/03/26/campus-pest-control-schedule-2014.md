Shantou University’s Resource Management Office will conduct pest-control and sanitation work in green spaces, trees, fruit trees and drains across campus and the Continuing Education College from March 28 to April 3. Faculty, staff and students are advised to take precautions.

The schedule covers the Medical Building, New Teaching Building and G-J residence halls on March 28; the security office area, campus gates, roads and the New Library perimeter on March 29; the affiliated schools and the area toward the Mental Health Center on March 30; the dining hall, Administration Building and Administrative Center on March 31; the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, student residences, reservoir dam, Building 789 and A-B faculty housing on April 1; the artificial lake and sports field on April 2; and the Continuing Education College and Longquan Garden faculty housing area on April 3.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
