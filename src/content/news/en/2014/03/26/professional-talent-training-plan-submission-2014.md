Shantou University’s Personnel Office has forwarded a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on submitting 2014 training plans under the provincial professional talent knowledge-updating program. University units interested in organizing such training are invited to submit materials.

Units should email an electronic copy of the 2014 training-plan application form to o_rsc@stu.edu.cn by March 30. The listed contact number is 86503390; detailed requirements and the form are provided in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The forwarded notice and training-plan application form are available in the original attachments on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
