Shantou University’s trade union announced that the university trade union committee, after discussion at an expanded meeting, had recommended the intensive care unit (ICU) of the Second Affiliated Hospital of the Medical College as a candidate for the Shantou Workers’ Pioneer award. The public comment period ran from March 26 to 30, 2014. Written comments could be submitted to the university trade union during that period.

The union had previously invited units to make internal recommendations for the award. This notice identified the unit selected as the university’s candidate.
