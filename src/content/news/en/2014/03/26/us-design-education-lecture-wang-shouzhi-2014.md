Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host a lecture at 7:30 p.m. on Thursday, March 27, 2014, in the school’s tiered lecture hall. Professor Wang Shouzhi will speak on “American Design Education Through the Lens of a U.S. University.” The event is open to the university community.
