Shantou University’s Graduate School and Student Affairs Office will hold a career talk at 7:30 p.m. on March 28, 2014, in Room 207 of Building E. The session will discuss career choices for graduates and career development in foreign-invested companies, and is open to undergraduate and graduate students.

The speaker, 邝锦荣, leads national market development at Nike Sports (China). His previous roles included positions at Decathlon and adidas. The talk is part of the university’s 2014 Graduate Employment Services Month program.
