Shantou University’s Arts Education Center and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will present the Zhu Ailan and Students Singing Concert at 7:30 p.m. on March 30, 2014, in the university auditorium. Lyric soprano Zhu Ailan will perform with students from the Central Conservatory of Music. The program includes “Where Has the Time Gone?,” “Olive Tree,” “Azaleas,” “Heavenly Road” and “Looking Toward Spring.”

Tickets will be distributed at Room 309 of the Arts Education Center on March 28, from 9:30 a.m. to noon and from 2:30 to 5 p.m. Distribution is first come, first served, and will end when tickets run out. The performance is open to audiences aged 12 and older.
