Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced that all 2014 undergraduate theses must undergo initial and follow-up checks through CNKI’s university thesis system. Students were to upload their own theses, with the university allocating one check for each stage per student.

Students were asked to change their initial passwords by March 31. The initial-check allocation was expected around Monday of Week 7, with a mature draft due for checking by Friday of Week 9. A second allocation would follow completion of the initial check; the follow-up check was expected around Monday of Week 10 and had to be completed on the final thesis before the defense. Students were to revise their work based on the first result and submit the final report and thesis to their supervisors as required.

The notice described the software as an aid for measuring textual overlap, not as a tool that independently determines plagiarism. Theses with a text-similarity rate above 30% were to receive closer review by supervisors and school defense committees. Theses not checked under the required process would not be eligible for defense.
