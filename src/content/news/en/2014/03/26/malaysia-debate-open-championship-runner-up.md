The sixth Malaysia Debate Open was held from March 7 to 11 at Multimedia University in Malacca. STU Law School student He Y, competing on an open team with a debater from Sichuan University, reached the final of the English as a Foreign Language (EFL) division and finished runner-up. The University of Macau won the division.

The four-day tournament drew more than 200 debaters from China, South Korea, Japan, Malaysia, Singapore and Australia and featured eight rounds. Collin Tham of STU’s Center for International Language Learning coached He Y. The student’s name is abbreviated here to protect privacy.
