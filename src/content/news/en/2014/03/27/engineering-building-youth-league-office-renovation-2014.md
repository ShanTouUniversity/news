Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will renovate the Youth League office of the College of Engineering in Rooms 301 and 302, west wing of the Engineering Building. Shantou Shenghang Construction Co. was contracted for the project at a bid price of RMB 42,200. Work includes replacing aluminum windows and doors, building interior brick walls, and repainting walls and ceilings.

Construction was scheduled to begin on March 27, 2014, and finish by May 5. The work may cause noise, vibration and dust; nearby staff and students were advised to take care and avoid the construction area where possible.
