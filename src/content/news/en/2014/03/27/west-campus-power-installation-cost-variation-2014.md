Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office published a proposed cost addition for the West Campus power-supply installation project. Work began on March 14, 2014. To accommodate the West Campus road expansion, the cable route needed to be shifted, with 20 meters of main power cable reserved at each of two distribution rooms. The additional cost was estimated at about RMB 24,000, below the university’s 10% limit for project variations.

Written comments on the proposal could be submitted to the university’s Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on March 29.
