Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will install stainless-steel safety railings on the front and rear balconies of Type A and Type C staff housing in Longquan Garden, from the second floor up. Shantou Donghua Construction Co. was contracted for the project at a bid price of RMB 32,100.

Work was scheduled to begin on March 29, 2014, and finish by May 7. Construction may cause noise, vibration and dust. Workers will need access to residents’ homes, and residents were asked to cooperate and take care around the work area.
