It is reported that demolition work for Phase I of Shantou University's Residential College project is under way. The Resource Management Office issued safety arrangements on March 27. Construction of the project is planned to begin in May, while the current demolition work is scheduled for completion by April 20.

## Work and Safety Measures

Demolition work is scheduled in three daily periods: 7:00 a.m.–12:30 p.m., 2:00–6:00 p.m., and 7:00–9:00 p.m. The contractor planned to concentrate on demolishing Dormitory D-1 on March 29 and 30, using water spraying to reduce dust. Because falling structures may still produce dust and significant vibration, the Network Center, the affiliated schools, and nearby residents were advised to prepare equipment protection and keep windows closed.

Except for a power-distribution room that remains in service, the upper structures of the remaining dormitories in Block B were expected to be removed over the weekend, followed by the transport of construction debris. Water spraying would be used during transport, and residents in the western campus area were advised to keep windows closed during the work.

The office also arranged mosquito and rodent-control work near the construction site and reminded nearby residents to watch children closely. The affiliated schools were asked to provide safety reminders.
