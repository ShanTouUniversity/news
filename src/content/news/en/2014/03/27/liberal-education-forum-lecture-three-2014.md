Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education and the Christian Studies Center of the College of Liberal Arts will hold the third lecture in the Liberal Education Forum series at 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, April 1, 2014, in the Library Lecture Hall. The speaker will be Xu Jilin, a distinguished professor at East China Normal University and a historian.

The lecture will address the aims of university education, learning, character development, practical ability and community life. Xu’s research focuses on twentieth-century Chinese intellectual history, intellectuals and Shanghai urban culture.
