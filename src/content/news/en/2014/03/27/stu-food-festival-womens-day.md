It is reported that, to enrich campus cultural life and showcase the vitality of STU students, the Shantou University Student Union will join hands with the student unions of each college to hold a Food Festival — part of the university's 10th Girls' Festival series — on March 29 at the Golden Triangle and the pedestrian street.

## Event Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | March 29 |
| Venue | Golden Triangle and pedestrian street |
| Morning session, 10:00–13:00 | Girls' Festival event: "Colorful Youth, Vibrant STU" |
| Afternoon session, 14:30–17:30 | Food Festival: "Food, Cooked by Ourselves" |

On the day of the event, the university Student Union and the colleges will set up stalls together. The morning session will feature the Girls' Festival-themed activity "Colorful Youth, Vibrant STU," while the afternoon session turns into the Food Festival, where participants can sample food and play games at the stalls run by each college.

The event is open to all faculty and students, who are welcome to take part.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
