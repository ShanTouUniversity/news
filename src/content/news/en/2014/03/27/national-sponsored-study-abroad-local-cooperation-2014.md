The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education and the China Scholarship Council opened the 2014 local-cooperation program for government-sponsored study abroad, which supports university faculty undertaking visits to overseas universities and research institutions. Shantou University’s employed teaching and research staff could apply as senior research scholars or visiting scholars. Priority fields included marine science, electronic information, mechanical manufacturing, biomedicine and the humanities and social sciences.

Applicants had to meet the relevant professional-rank, age and service requirements, secure an invitation from an overseas adviser and host institution, and meet the national language-proficiency standard. Selection would involve individual applications, institutional recommendations and expert review. Funding would follow national government-sponsored study-abroad rules.

Online applications were scheduled for April 6–25. After applying online, candidates had to submit three printed copies of their materials to the university’s Human Resources Office by April 18. The provincial department would conduct an initial review before the China Scholarship Council’s final expert review, scheduled for June and July 2014. Formal admission notices were expected between December 2014 and early 2015. The study-abroad eligibility period would remain valid through December 31, 2016.
