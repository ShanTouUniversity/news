A student leadership camp for participants from the mainland, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan will take place at Jinan University from July 20 to 28, 2014. The event, themed “Youth, Innovation and Development: Core Values and the Mission of Youth,” is organized by the State Council Overseas Chinese Affairs Office and the China Overseas Exchange Association and hosted by Jinan University. Shantou University was invited to nominate one student.

The camp was open to undergraduates from the classes of 2011, 2012 and 2013, as well as graduate students. Its program would cover leadership development, team-building, social practice and cultural visits. The organizers would cover accommodation, meals, local transportation, study, visits and insurance; participants would pay for travel between Shantou and Jinan University.

Schools and relevant units could nominate one candidate each for review by the university Youth League Committee, which would select one nominee. Candidates were to submit an application form and copies of relevant certificates by May 7.
