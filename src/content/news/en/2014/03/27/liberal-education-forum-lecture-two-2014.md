Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education and the Christian Studies Center of the College of Liberal Arts will hold the second lecture in the Liberal Education Forum series at 7:30 p.m. on Monday, March 31, 2014, in the Library Lecture Hall. The speaker will be Xu Jilin, a distinguished professor at East China Normal University.

The lecture will consider contemporary idealism, instrumental and value rationality, and the role of public life in students’ personal development. Xu’s research focuses on twentieth-century Chinese intellectual history, intellectuals and Shanghai urban culture.
