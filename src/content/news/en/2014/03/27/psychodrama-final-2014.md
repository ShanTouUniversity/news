The final of Shantou University’s fourth student psychodrama competition will be held from 7 to 9 p.m. on March 28, 2014, in the Science and Technology Reporting Hall. Six teams advanced from the preliminary round, following the competition’s launch in November 2013. This year’s theme is “Let Dreams Take Flight, Set Sail from the Heart.”

The competition will award one first prize, two second prizes and three third prizes, with cash awards of RMB 1,000, RMB 600 and RMB 300, respectively, plus certificates. Audience members who vote will also have a chance to receive a gift.
