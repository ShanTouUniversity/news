It is reported that the university will hold the fifth meeting of the fourth Discipline Inspection Commission on April 2, 2014.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 15:00, April 2, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administration Center |
| Attendees | Members of the University Discipline Inspection Commission |
| Observers | Secretaries of Party General Branches and directly affiliated branches on the main campus, and heads of discipline inspection, supervision and audit from the main campus, the Medical College and affiliated hospitals |
| Convened by | Office of the Discipline Inspection Commission |

Relevant personnel are asked to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of the Discipline Inspection Commission)
