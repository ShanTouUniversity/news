Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office and College of Science adjusted several laboratory sessions. Classes 68126 and 68282 were canceled during periods 5 to 7 on Thursday, March 27; classes 68127 and 68285 were canceled during the same periods on Friday, March 28.

The mechanics, heat and electromagnetism sections of General Physics Lab, class 68127, would proceed as scheduled on Friday. The original notice identified the affected courses as General Physics Lab and Optics Lab.
