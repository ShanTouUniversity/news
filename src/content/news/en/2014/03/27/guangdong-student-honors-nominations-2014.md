Following a provincial student federation awards program, Shantou University’s Youth League Committee opened nominations for the 2013–14 Guangdong awards for outstanding student leaders, student unions and student associations. The university had five nomination places: two for outstanding student leaders, one for a student union and two for student associations.

Schools and student organizations were asked to nominate candidates and groups in line with the selection requirements. Paper applications were due at the university Youth League Committee office by April 8, 2014, with electronic copies also to be sent to its official mailbox.
