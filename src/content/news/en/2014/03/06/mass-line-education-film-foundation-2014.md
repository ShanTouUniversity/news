According to the university’s practice office, Shantou University is organizing party members, officials and applicants for Party membership to view and study the mass-line education film “Foundation.” Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches will organize viewing within their units.

The film can be viewed through the “Video Materials” section of the university’s special-topic site at http://qzjy.stu.edu.cn/ or directly at http://focus.stu.edu.cn/Viewer/MoveVideoInfo.aspx?SheetNo=20140305164528390.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Department of Party Organization and United Front Work)
