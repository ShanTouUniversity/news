It is reported that the 2nd meeting of the Standing Committee of the CPC Shantou University Committee in 2014 reviewed candidates for two management positions at the Medical School. The university Publicity and United Front Work Office announced on March 6 that He Ping is proposed as director of the Medical School Academic Affairs Office and Lin Dajing as director of its Supervision, Audit and Inspection Office.

Organizations or individuals with comments may submit them in writing or contact the Publicity and United Front Work Office in person or by phone by **March 12**. Organization submissions should bear an official seal, while individual submissions should include the sender’s real name. The office can be reached at 86502345 or o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.
