The English Center will hold IELTS Preparation Workshop Series 1.1, “IELTS: Getting to Know It!”, from 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 12, 2014, at CILL. The workshop will introduce the basic features of the IELTS examination.

Seats are limited. Students can reserve a place at https://gettingtoknowielts.eventbrite.com. Students who are uncertain whether they can attend are asked not to register; registrants are expected to attend.
