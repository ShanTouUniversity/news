The Communist Youth League of Shantou University will assess the university’s grassroots Youth League organizations for 2013. The assessment covers annual evaluations of Youth League branches and school or college Youth League committees. Branch evaluations include ideological education, organizational development, second-class activities and members’ exemplary roles. School and college committee evaluations cover ideological education, organizational development and standardized management, and services supporting student growth.

School and college Youth League committees must complete branch assessments by March 19 and submit stamped assessment forms and supporting materials to the university Youth League Committee. They must also submit their annual assessment materials and email an electronic copy to o_tw@stu.edu.cn. The university committee plans to assess school and college committees in April, with the date to be announced. Results will be considered in Youth League awards.

Assessment forms and related materials are available in the original OA attachment.
