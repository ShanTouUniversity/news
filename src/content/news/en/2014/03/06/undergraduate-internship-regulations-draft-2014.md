The Academic Affairs Office is seeking feedback from teaching units across Shantou University on the revised draft Regulations on Undergraduate Internships. The draft includes internship procedures and related forms, and schools and departments are asked to organize faculty discussions and consolidate comments.

Comments should be emailed to the Academic Affairs Office by Friday, March 14, 2014. The contact email is myzhou@stu.edu.cn, the telephone number is 86503202, and the contact person is Zhou Mengyi.

The draft regulations, procedures and forms are available in the original OA attachments.
