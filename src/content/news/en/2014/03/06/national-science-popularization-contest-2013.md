According to the STU Youth League Committee and Science Association, Shantou University will organize students to enter the 2013 National College Student Science Popularization Works Contest. The theme is “Protect Resources and the Environment, Build a Beautiful China.” Works will address audiences including young people, farmers, urban workers, officials and civil servants, and community residents.

Entries fall into graphic, film and game categories. Each graphic entry should contain at least eight images. Film entries may be a two- to 10-minute short film or a public-service advertisement of 60, 30 or 15 seconds. Games should contain at least three levels. Campus submissions are due March 24; preliminary review is scheduled for March 26, with final review and awards provisionally set for March 29. The national schedule covers creation from October 2013 through March 2014, review in April, a summary and awards ceremony in May, and promotion from June through September.

Students can find details through the China Digital Science and Technology Museum website at www.cdstm.cn and submit works to 12dcchen@stu.edu.cn by March 24.
