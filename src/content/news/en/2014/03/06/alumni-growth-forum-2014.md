The STU Literature School will hold an alumni growth forum from 3 to 5 p.m. on March 7, 2014, in Room W201 of the Literature School. The forum, titled “A Decade of Growth,” will feature Liang Linghui, a 2004 alumna from the school’s foreign languages program.

Liang graduated from the school’s English program in 2008 and worked in overseas sales for Midea Group’s household air-conditioning business. She was named a Midea outstanding salesperson before becoming a sales manager and later a regional supervisor. The forum is open to all students and faculty members.
