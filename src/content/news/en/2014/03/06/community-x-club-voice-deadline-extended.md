It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University, the Communist Youth League Committee and the Student Club Federation jointly issued a document on March 6, extending the project application deadline for the Community X Plan's "Voice of Clubs" activity to March 15, in order to give students more time to prepare their project proposals.

Applicants may refer to the notice on launching the Voice of Clubs activity under the Community X Plan, published by the university league committee on January 10, for details.

In addition, the university plans to hold a project approval defense session on March 22, with the specific time and venue to be announced later.

**Key Dates**

| Item | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Application deadline | March 15 |
| Project approval defense | March 22 (time and venue to be announced) |
