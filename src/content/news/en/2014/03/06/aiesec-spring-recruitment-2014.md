The Shantou University AIESEC team has opened its spring 2014 recruitment for team members and overseas volunteers. The recruitment is open to students across the university regardless of major or year. Team departments include marketing, human resources, exchange, social projects and finance. The overseas volunteer program covers more than 60 countries in Africa, India, Southeast Asia and Central and Eastern Europe, with projects involving cultural exchange, youth development, HIV/AIDS prevention, community development, environmental protection, language education and microfinance.

Team-member applications close at 11:30 p.m. on March 6. Overseas-volunteer applications are accepted from 8 a.m. on March 8 through 11:30 p.m. on March 20. Applicants can collect forms at recruitment posters near dormitories and Canteens 2 and 4, or email completed forms to stu.aiesec.app@gmail.com. The selection process includes resume screening, group interviews and individual interviews; interview details will be sent by text message.

The STU AIESEC team was established on June 18, 2012, with support from the Business School. Further information is available through the Sina Weibo account @AIESEC汕大 and the AIESEC汕大 WeChat account.
