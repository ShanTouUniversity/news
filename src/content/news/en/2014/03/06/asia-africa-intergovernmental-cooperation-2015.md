The STU Research Office has opened a call for 2015 intergovernmental cooperation projects between China and selected Asian and African countries. The call covers joint research, industrial technology research and development cooperation, and exchange projects. Joint research projects involve Israel, Japan and Singapore; industrial technology cooperation involves South Korea and Israel; exchange projects involve South Africa and Thailand.

STU faculty may apply for joint research and exchange projects. Industrial technology cooperation applications must be submitted by enterprises, while universities and research institutions are encouraged to provide technical support. For all three categories, Chinese and overseas partners must submit applications separately to their respective national authorities.

Joint research and industrial technology projects generally run for two years and no more than three years. Exchange projects run for two years, support one mutual visit each year, allow no more than 50 person-days for outgoing and visiting groups, and limit outgoing groups to five people. Four paper copies and an electronic disc must be submitted to the Research Office by April 14, 2014. Proposals, application forms and cooperation agreements should follow the requirements in the attachments.

Contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 86502813. Country details, funding levels and document formats are provided in the OA attachments.
