The Office of International Exchange and Cooperation has opened campus applications for University West’s 2014 exchange programs. The 3+1 and 3+1+1 dual-degree and graduate-degree programs are open to third-year students in business administration, computer science and mechanical engineering who have an IELTS score of 6 or an equivalent qualification. The exchange-student program is open to first-, second- and third-year students in relevant majors.

Applications are due by March 20, 2014. Applicants should complete the international exchange application at http://sao.stu.edu.cn, print it for their school or college to review, and submit it to the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation. They must also email scans of proof of English proficiency and an English transcript to wscicd@stu.edu.cn. STU can receive four tuition-waived exchange students each year for one semester; applicants will be selected competitively if demand exceeds the places available.

Further details are in the OA attachment. For inquiries, contact the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at 86503520 or wscicd@stu.edu.cn, or visit Room 240, second floor, New Administration Center. The University West inquiry QQ number is 2423599318.
