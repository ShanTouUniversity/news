The 10th Shantou University Women’s Festival will run from March 7 to 29, 2014. The Student Union will organize eight activity series focused on campus services, interaction and practical sharing.

| Activity | Time and venue | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sweet Girl | March 7–29, Canteens 2, 3 and 4 | Lucky-color gifts and Weibo interaction |
| Men’s Help | March 7–22, 8 a.m.–10 p.m. | Parcel collection, book returns and bicycle services for female students |
| Charity Breakfast Bus | March 7, 7:30–8:30 a.m., entrances to EF, GJ and LY dormitories | Breakfast service |
| Make a Wish | March 7–29, Jin San Jiao | Collecting and fulfilling wishes |
| Jin San Jiao Games | March 8, 11:30 a.m.–3 p.m., Jin San Jiao | Partner games and prizes |
| Angel in Beauty | March 12 and 19, Small Stage and Coffee Bookstore | Self-defense and beauty sessions |
| Meet You | March 22, 7:30–9:30 p.m., in front of the Grand Auditorium | Celebration party |
| Food Fair | March 29, Pedestrian Street | Food displays and judging with schools and colleges |

Further information is available through the Weibo accounts @汕头大学学生会 and @汕头大学女生节.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University and STU Student Union)
