It is reported that the 12th National Doctoral Student Academic Conference is scheduled to take place in Kunming, Yunnan, in May 2014. The event, organized by the Youth Affairs Committee of the China Association for Science and Technology under the guidance of the Academic Degrees Committee Office of the State Council, will invite Nobel laureates in fields including physics, chemistry and biophysics to meet with doctoral students.

## Conference and Call for Papers

The conference will cover four topics: physics; chemistry and chemical engineering; modern biotechnology; and computer science and technology. The Chinese Physical Society, Chinese Chemical Society, Chinese Biophysical Society and China Computer Federation will organize the respective topics. The program will include keynote reports, meetings between science masters and doctoral students, academic exchange and talent-recruitment activities.

Senior doctoral students whose research matches a topic may submit a paper as first author. Each topic is expected to select no more than 50 papers. First authors of selected papers will attend the conference, while outstanding papers may receive awards. Selected papers will be presented as posters, and some authors may give presentations during the conference.

## Registration Deadline

Interested doctoral students must register through the conference system at http://bssnh.cast.org.cn and submit the registration form and full paper by **March 28, 2014**. There is no registration fee. The organizers will cover accommodation and meals, while participants are responsible for transportation costs.

Some participants may also volunteer to assist the organizing societies and will receive volunteer certificates. Applicants are expected to have good English communication skills.
