Shantou University announced the results of its 2013 service-learning public-welfare project initiative. Launched in May 2013, the initiative received 14 project submissions from 14 volunteer organizations or teams. Following review, 10 projects received support and have since been completed.

The university held a project-sharing session on December 31, 2013, in Room 726 of Building 789, where the awards were selected:

| Award | Project | Organization or team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First prize | Angel Plan—Protecting Children | Youth Volunteer Association of the Medical School |
| Second prize | At the Critical Moment—Step Up When Needed | Student team |
| Third prize | 2013 Colorful English Festival | Youth Volunteer Association of the School of Liberal Arts |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee and Youth Volunteer Association)
