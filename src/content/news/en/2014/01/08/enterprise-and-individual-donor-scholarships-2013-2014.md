Shantou University announced the results of its 2013–2014 enterprise and individual donor scholarship selection. Following review by the selection committee and approval by the management committee, 113 students were selected for the Outstanding Senior Student Award Program and two for the Hakka Student Award Program.

The original notice attachments list the recipients. To protect student privacy, this report gives only the totals and does not reproduce individual names.

| Award program | Recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| Outstanding Senior Student Award Program | 113 students |
| Hakka Student Award Program | 2 students |
