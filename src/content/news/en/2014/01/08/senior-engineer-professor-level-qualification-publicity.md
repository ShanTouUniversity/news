The Guangdong Provincial Engineering Technology Senior Engineer (Professor Level) Qualification Review Committee approved Chen Weizhou of Shantou University’s Institute of Marine Biology for a professor-level senior engineer qualification. The result was placed under public notice from January 8 through 5:30 p.m. on January 16, 2014.

Objections may be submitted to the Inspection Office or the Professional and Technical Personnel Management Office of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security. Written submissions and telephone reports must include the reporter’s real name and specific facts.
