Shantou University’s Social Sciences Department announced a winter social survey assignment. Undergraduates from the 2010, 2011 and 2012 cohorts taking Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics (SOC6150A) in spring 2014 were required to complete a social survey. The assignment forms part of the course’s practical component, and the report would count toward the final grade.

The topic is “My Hometown, My Home.” Students were asked to write an approximately 3,000-word report connecting the survey with relevant theory and the “Chinese Dream” theme, and submit it to their instructors by the end of March 2014. The report format follows the department’s template.

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics, SOC6150A |
| Cohorts | 2010, 2011 and 2012 undergraduates |
| Topic | My Hometown, My Home |
| Length | Approximately 3,000 Chinese characters/words |
| Deadline | End of March 2014 |
