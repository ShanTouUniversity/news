It is reported that from January 9 to 10, 2014, the Institute of Higher Education Science at Shantou University will hold its first joint academic exchange with the Institute of Higher Education Research of Shenzhen University and that of Guangzhou University. The event will take place at STU's Academic Exchange Center.

Under the theme "Higher Education Research and Postgraduate Training," formal discussions ran from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on January 10 in Conference Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center. Professor Wang Jianhua of Nanjing Normal University has been invited to deliver the opening report, while scholars selected by the Shenzhen and Guangzhou institutions will present and discuss topics relating to higher education research and postgraduate training.

| Participants | Institute of Higher Education Research, Shenzhen University; Institute of Higher Education Research, Guangzhou University |
| Keynote speaker | Professor Wang Jianhua, Nanjing Normal University |

This will be the first exchange among the three higher education research institutes, building a platform for scholars in Guangdong and Hong Kong to explore discipline development and talent cultivation.
