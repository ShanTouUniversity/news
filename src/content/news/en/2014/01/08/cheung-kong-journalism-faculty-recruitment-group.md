Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication formed a faculty recruitment group to plan and implement the school’s annual talent-recruitment program and hire faculty in stages according to disciplinary needs. The group will also coordinate with the Human Resources Office and the university’s Faculty Committee to advance faculty development, with responsibilities guided by the university’s faculty recruitment regulations.

The group is headed by Fan Dongsheng. Members are Ni Qingqing, Du Shimin, Bai Jing, Wang Zongan, Cheng Jinfu, Xu Chengqun, Hu Lufeng, Fan Linjun and Xu Hui. Cai Qiuyan serves as secretary.
