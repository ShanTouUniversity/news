Registration opened for the first National Graduate Smart City Technology and Creative Design Competition. The event is hosted by the Center for Graduate Education Development of the Ministry of Education and the China Association for Science and Technology Youth Science and Technology Center, and co-hosted by two smart-city industry innovation alliances. It is open to formally enrolled master’s and doctoral students and graduating undergraduates admitted to Shantou University through recommendation.

The competition includes a video-analysis technology challenge and a smart-city creative design contest. Entries may be submitted individually or in teams of no more than four. Creative-design entries must focus on smart cities and may be creative, applied or science-popularization works. The competition will offer tiered awards and may include enterprise special awards, outstanding organization awards and outstanding instructor awards.

Interested participants were asked to send the registration form to fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn by February 25, 2014. The campus contact is Wang Fengqi at 86504000. Detailed rules, work requirements and awards are subject to the competition guide published on the official website.
