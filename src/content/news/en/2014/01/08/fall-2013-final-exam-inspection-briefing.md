Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office reported on inspections during the first two days of the fall 2013 final examinations. Vice President Wen Xiaobo, teaching supervisors, Academic Affairs Office staff and representatives from some schools took part. The office said examination rooms were generally orderly: most proctors arrived ten minutes early as required, students generally followed examination rules, and no cheating reports had been received.

Inspectors also found that some open-book examinations did not adequately remind students to place prohibited items in designated areas. Some proctors failed to arrive ten minutes early, and three examination sessions included late-arriving proctors. The office reiterated that teaching units and proctors should follow the university’s examination-proctoring rules to maintain exam order.

| Inspection findings | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Overall conditions | Generally orderly; no cheating reports received |
| Issues | Insufficient reminders about prohibited items in some open-book exams; proctors not arriving early or arriving late |
| Follow-up | Follow STU examination-proctoring rules and strengthen exam management |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
