Shantou University’s Leading Group for Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities announced an extended submission deadline for the campus selection of the Ninth Challenge Cup Guangdong Student Business Plan Competition. All enrolled students may submit entries, with the new deadline set for 6 p.m. on February 24, 2014.

The university planned written reviews and live defenses by experts in mid-March 2014, followed by one-on-one guidance for selected entries. Outstanding works would receive funding support under relevant rules, and the university planned to submit entries to the provincial competition before May 2014. Teams were required to prepare materials according to the earlier campus-selection notice.

| Stage | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Submission deadline | February 24, 2014, 6:00 p.m. |
| Campus review | Mid-March 2014; written review and live defense |
| Guidance | Mid-March 2014; one-on-one guidance |
| Provincial submission | Before May 2014 |
| Eligible participants | All enrolled students |
