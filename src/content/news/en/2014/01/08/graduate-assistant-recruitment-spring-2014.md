It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on January 8, 2014, to recruit Graduate Assistantship personnel for the spring 2014 semester for all postgraduates on the main campus.

The program aims to cultivate research, teaching and management capabilities, enhance overall competence and ease financial pressure. Teaching assistant and administrative assistant posts are available, with details in the summary tables.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | All postgraduates on the main campus |
| Application deadline | Fill out the *Graduate Assistantship Application Form* (Attachment 4) and submit to the host unit by January 17, 2014 |
| Unit review | Host units will review and confirm appointees and submit the *Appointment Summary* (Attachment 3) to the Graduate School by 11:00 on January 20, 2014, with the electronic version to fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn |
| Contact | Wang Fengqi, phone 86504000, email fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Graduate School |

The summary tables and application forms are available in the OA attachments.
