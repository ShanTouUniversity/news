The Shantou University Party Committee announced the appointment of Zhang Guojun as president of the university’s Medical College Affiliated Cancer Hospital. The appointment carries a six-month probationary period. It was approved at the 16th meeting of the university Party Committee Standing Committee in 2013 and announced on January 8, 2014.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Appointee | Zhang Guojun |
| Position | President, STU Medical College Affiliated Cancer Hospital |
| Probation | Six months |
| Deciding body | 16th meeting of the 2013 STU Party Committee Standing Committee |

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Committee)
