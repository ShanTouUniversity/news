It is reported that the Library issued a notice on January 8, 2014, that the new Web of Science (version 5.13) will be officially launched on January 12.

To help users more efficiently search and access high-quality academic literature, the upgrade introduces important changes to the user interface and database names: the current Web of Knowledge will be renamed Web of Science, and the current Web of Science will be renamed Web of Science Core Collection.

More details and the online training schedule are available at: http://app.info.science.thomsonreuters.biz/e/es.aspx?s=1556&e=793629&elq=125f25324ba842209f115a6ac636d142
