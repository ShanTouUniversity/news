It is reported that the Research Office held a mobilization and exchange meeting for the 2014 National Social Science Fund and the Ministry of Education Humanities and Social Sciences projects on the afternoon of January 7, 2014, in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center, with Prof. Chen Jinlong of South China Normal University invited to share application experience.

## Meeting Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Application for National Social Science Fund and MOE Humanities and Social Sciences Projects |
| Time | Afternoon of January 7, 2014 |
| Venue | Conference Room 3, Academic Exchange Center |
| Speaker | Prof. Chen Jinlong, South China Normal University |
| Chair | Director Hu Zhong, Research Office |
| Participants | Leaders of liberal arts units, principal investigators of ongoing projects and more than 60 humanities and social sciences faculty members planning to apply |
| Organizer | Research Office |

## Highlights

Prof. Chen gave a detailed introduction to the types, topic selection, argumentation and preparation of preliminary achievements for the National Social Science Fund projects, and vividly shared his own experience in application and academic research with examples. He then held in-depth discussions with faculty members on specific issues in project applications.

Through the meeting, faculty members gained a clearer understanding of key points and methods for topic selection and project argumentation, as well as greater confidence in preparing their applications. The venue was fully attended and the exchange was lively.
