Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced the December 2013 completion review results for undergraduate innovation experiment projects and innovation and entrepreneurship training projects. A total of 71 projects from seven schools took part; all passed, including nine rated outstanding. Another 40 projects received extensions.

The accepted projects comprised 34 undergraduate innovation experiments and 37 innovation and entrepreneurship training projects. The extensions covered 34 innovation experiments and six training projects. The office said outcomes included papers, research reports, videos, images, portfolios and entrepreneurial practice. The projects produced 25 papers, and some resulted in national patent applications or company formation.

The office said extensions were requested for reasons including job searches, internships, research obstacles or results that did not meet expectations. It also noted shortcomings in research records and review materials in some projects, and asked extended teams to improve their plans and all teams to maintain project documentation.
