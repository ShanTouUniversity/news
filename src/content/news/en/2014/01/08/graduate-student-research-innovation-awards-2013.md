Shantou University’s Graduate School announced its 2013 awards for extracurricular academic and scientific innovation. STU graduate students took part in events including the National Postgraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling and the 12th Guangdong Challenge Cup. The school selected three first-prize, three second-prize and seven third-prize research innovation projects, for 13 awards in total. Six faculty members were also named outstanding advisers.

The original notice lists the award-winning graduate students and projects in attachments. Their names and team lists are not reproduced here to avoid unnecessary disclosure of student information. The Graduate School encouraged award recipients to continue their academic work and called on other graduate students to learn from the recognized individuals and teams.
