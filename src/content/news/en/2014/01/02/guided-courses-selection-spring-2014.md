It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on January 2, 2014, that course selection for guided courses (excluding service-learning courses) for the spring 2014 semester has begun.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | January 17, 2014 |
| Applicable courses | Guided courses for the 2013–2014 spring semester (excluding service-learning courses) |
| Selection methods | Choose from provided topics; create and submit a topic independently; or have the academic officer enter the topic and student name |
| Notes | When creating a topic, accurately enter the topic title (internship unit); if not yet determined, use “to be determined” as a placeholder and update before grading |
| System closure | After January 17, the system will close guided course sections and no further selection will be allowed in principle |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Details on course offerings (titles, eligible majors and years, selection methods) are in the OA attachment.

> ⚠️ The course selection details are available on the OA system.
