The Career Development Center of Shantou University’s Law School scheduled an experience-sharing and mobilization session for its 2014 national judicial examination training program from 4 to 5:30 p.m. on January 2, 2014, in Room 502 of Building D. Students from the Class of 2010 were invited to discuss their preparation experiences.

The school reported that 68 of its 111 students who took the 2013 examination passed, a pass rate of 61.3 percent. Eighteen students scored above 400. The original notice named student speakers and listed their individual scores; those identities are not reproduced here.
