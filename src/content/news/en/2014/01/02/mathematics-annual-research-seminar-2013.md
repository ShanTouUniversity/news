Shantou University’s Department of Mathematics and Institute of Mathematics scheduled their 2013 annual research seminar for 2:30–6 p.m. on January 3, 2014, in Lecture Hall E201. The event was to review the department’s research and feature eight presentations, and was open to the university community.

Topics included soliton phenomena in nonlinear wave equations with KdV modes; spherical harmonics in binary classification; decomposition of band Toeplitz matrices; polynomial reproduction in vector subdivision schemes; topological classification in fractal geometry and function spaces; universal Teichmüller and QK spaces; and integral operators on analytic Morrey spaces.
