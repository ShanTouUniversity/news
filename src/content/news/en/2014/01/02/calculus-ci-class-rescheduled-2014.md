Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced a schedule change on January 2, 2014, for the Mathematics Department’s Calculus C-I, Section 64129. The class originally scheduled for periods 1–2 on January 2 was canceled and rescheduled for periods 7–8 on Saturday, January 4, in Lecture Hall 1.

| Original class | Make-up class |
| --- | --- |
| Periods 1–2, January 2, 2014 | Periods 7–8, January 4, 2014, Lecture Hall 1 |
