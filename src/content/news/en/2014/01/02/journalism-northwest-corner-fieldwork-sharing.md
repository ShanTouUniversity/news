The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University scheduled a presentation on its eighth “Revisit China’s Northwest” reporting expedition for 7:30–9:30 p.m. on January 3, 2014, in the university library’s lecture hall. The reporting team was led by dean Fan Dongsheng and associate professor Xie Lin. Guests included Li Jing, director of the Ejin Banner Archives, researcher Fan Xiaoke of the Academy of Agricultural Planning and Engineering, and local media representatives.
