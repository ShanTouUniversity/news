Shantou University Library announced its winter-break hours on January 2, 2014. From January 22 through February 19, the library was scheduled to open Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Service dates were January 23, 25, 28 and 30, and February 8, 11, 13, 15 and 18.

The special collections area, reading halls and seminar rooms were to remain closed during the student winter break, January 19–February 22. The library planned to resume normal operations on February 20.
