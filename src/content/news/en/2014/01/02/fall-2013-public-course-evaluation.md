Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office opened the evaluation of public-interest courses for the fall 2013–2014 semester on January 2, 2014. The evaluation period ran through January 17. Students were asked to log in to the credit-system portal, open the course-evaluation section, review their pending courses and submit feedback.

The notice said students who missed the deadline would need to submit a written explanation; otherwise, they would be unable to view their public-interest course grades.
