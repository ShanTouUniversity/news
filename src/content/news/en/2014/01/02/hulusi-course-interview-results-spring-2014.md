Shantou University’s Arts Education Center announced the results of interviews for its hulusi performance course for the spring semester of the 2013–2014 academic year. The course, taught by Lu Xiaoling, admitted 30 students across two sections: 15 for Tuesday periods 6–7 and 15 for Wednesday periods 3–4.

The original notice included students’ names, student numbers and student-representative phone numbers. To protect student privacy, this report provides only the aggregate count and class times and does not reproduce individual information.
