Shantou University’s Labor Union scheduled a birthday gathering for faculty and staff for 6:30 p.m. on January 11, 2014, at the western restaurant in Junhua Hotel. The event was for employees with birthdays from December 2013 through February 2014 and was supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation. Employees could attend with family members using admission tickets.

Reservations were scheduled to run from noon on January 2 through noon on January 7. Payments were to be collected on January 7–8, and tickets were to be distributed on the morning of January 10. The union’s OA attachment contained the reservation, payment and event details.
