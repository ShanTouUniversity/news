Shantou University’s Labor Union scheduled its staff tennis tournament for 4:20 p.m. on Monday, January 6, 2014, at the campus tennis courts. Registered participants were asked to attend a draw at the union office at 10 a.m. on January 3; players who did not attend would have a teammate draw on their behalf.

Women’s singles would be played in a round-robin format and would not require a draw. The union later published the schedule: men’s singles group matches were set for January 6–8, doubles group matches for January 9–10, and women’s singles group matches plus the second stage of men’s singles and men’s doubles for January 11. Finals were scheduled for January 12. Players were to arrange matches within the designated time windows. No dedicated referees were assigned; players were to invite another participant present to officiate and record each result on the score sheet.
