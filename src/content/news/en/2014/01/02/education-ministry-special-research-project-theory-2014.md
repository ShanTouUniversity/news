Shantou University’s Research Office announced application arrangements on January 2, 2014, for the Ministry of Education’s special humanities and social sciences research program on “Research on the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics.” The program offered two grant categories: Category I projects received RMB 50,000 for a two-year research period, while Category II projects received RMB 30,000 for one year.

Eligible applicants included full-time faculty, Party-affairs and political-work staff, and relevant administrators able to undertake substantive research. Each applicant could submit one proposal. Projects were expected to follow the program guidelines and address university practice. Final outputs had to meet publication, book-release or adoption requirements, as applicable, and acknowledge the program’s funding in the prescribed manner.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
> ⚠️ The program guidelines, eligibility conditions and application procedures are available in the OA attachments.

The Research Office also announced the Ministry of Education–Chinese Academy of Engineering special program on “Research on Engineering Science and Technology Talent Development.” General projects offered approximately RMB 100,000 each for a normally two-year period and had to include a research report as a final output. Applicants were required to be full-time faculty with associate-senior or higher professional titles; each could submit one proposal, and Shantou University could nominate up to two.

Both calls required online applications through the ministry’s system. For the theoretical-research program, electronic applications were due for preliminary review by February 21, 2014, with paper copies due by February 27. For the engineering-talent program, electronic applications were due February 21 and paper applications February 28.
