Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office outlined review and project-proposal arrangements for campus initiatives approved under Guangdong’s 2013 undergraduate teaching quality and reform program. The sessions were scheduled for early in the spring 2014 semester, with exact dates and venues to be announced separately.

Projects covering comprehensive program reform, experimental teaching centers, off-campus undergraduate practice bases and faculty development centers were to undergo expert review. Higher-education teaching reform projects were to submit proposals for written assessment. Projects subject to review were expected to submit reports and make presentations followed by expert questions.

Each project was required to submit five printed copies of the relevant materials to the Academic Affairs Office’s teaching-reform evaluation unit by March 7, 2014, and send an electronic copy to the designated email address. The notice named Li Zhe of the office as the contact, at 86502736.
