Shantou University’s Research Office released the fourth 2013 issue of its Newsletter on Research and Innovation, reviewing research projects, academic output, platform development and scholarly activities.

The newsletter reported that STU received 53 grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China in 2013, totaling RMB 28.34 million. It also received 25 Guangdong Natural Science Foundation grants worth RMB 1.73 million. In the humanities and social sciences, 42 of 189 applications for external projects were approved, with total funding of RMB 1.744 million.

Citing data from the Institute of Scientific and Technical Information of China, the newsletter said 347 STU papers were indexed by SCIE for 2012, a 48% increase over 2011. It also noted that two STU studies related to H7N9 appeared in *Science* and *Nature* in 2013. That year, the university added one Guangdong university engineering technology research center and one international, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan science and technology cooperation platform project.

The issue also reviewed academic conferences, research-platform evaluations and technology partnerships. These figures and activities are reported as presented in the newsletter.
