Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office scheduled a lecture for January 17, 2014, on university education and course development in the MOOC era.

The speakers were STU Executive President Gu Peihua and SuperStar Group deputy general managers Ye Yanming and Jiang Kai. The session was planned for the morning.
