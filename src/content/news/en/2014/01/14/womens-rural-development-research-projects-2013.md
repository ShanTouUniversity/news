Shantou University’s Research Office announced that application and review for the 2013 Women’s Participation in Rural Development program had concluded. The funded-project list was determined through correspondence review by experts from the China Women’s Research Society, China Women’s University, Shantou University and other institutions.

The office encouraged project leaders to organize their research and ensure its quality. The full list of selected projects is available in the attachment to the original notice on the university’s OA system.
