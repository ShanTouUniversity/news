Ahead of the winter break, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office issued safety guidance covering travel, health, accommodation and temporary employment. The notice referred to H7N9 avian influenza cases reported at the time and advised students to maintain good hygiene and avoid contact with poultry and dead wild birds.

Students were reminded to secure dormitory doors and windows and switch off utilities before leaving. Those staying on campus were advised to follow university rules, use electrical appliances safely and stay away from hazardous waters. Students seeking temporary work were urged to use legitimate channels and be cautious of requests for deposits or identity documents. Travelers were advised to use authorized transport services.

For unusual situations on campus, students could contact the Student Community Management and Service Center at 8650 2661 or Campus Security at 82904110.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
