Shantou University’s Network and Information Center issued a network-service advisory. According to the South China Center of the Guangdong Education and Research Network, an upgrade to backbone equipment in Guangzhou would temporarily interrupt the university’s education-network connection. The notice did not specify the maintenance time; users should follow subsequent updates from the center.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Affected service | STU education-network connection |
| Cause | Guangzhou backbone equipment upgrade |
| Expected impact | Temporary interruption |
| Source of notice | South China Center of the Guangdong Education and Research Network |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
