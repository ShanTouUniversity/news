Shantou University’s School of Engineering course team is organizing an App Inventor mobile programming contest for the fall 2013 semester. The contest is open to students on the main campus who are taking Introduction to Mobile Programming. Participation is voluntary, and instructors will select no more than five outstanding works from each class based on students’ course innovation reports.

Registration closes at 11:59 p.m. on January 12, 2014. The winners were scheduled to be announced and prizes presented in mid-March 2014. One first prize would receive a 10-inch Samsung tablet, while seven second-prize winners would receive 7-inch Samsung tablets. Winners must agree to allow their works to be used as teaching cases and, as needed, assist with the mobile-programming website, services or course support.
