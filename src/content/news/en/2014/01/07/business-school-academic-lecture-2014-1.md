Shantou University’s Business School will hold its first academic lecture of 2014 on January 9. Professor Wang Qinggang of Zhongnan University of Economics and Law will discuss “Managerial Power and Excessive Executive Compensation.” The study uses 8,245 firm-year observations from Chinese companies between 2003 and 2009 to examine the relationship between managerial power and excessive executive pay.

The lecture will be chaired by Associate Professor Song Xiaobao and is open to the university community. Wang holds a doctorate in management and accounting and previously completed postdoctoral research at Xiamen University.
