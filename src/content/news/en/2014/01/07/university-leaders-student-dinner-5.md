Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced the reservation results for the fifth university leaders–student dinner. A total of 32 students successfully reserved places. The event was scheduled for 6 p.m. on January 9, 2014, in Room 201 at Hongtai Restaurant. Participants were asked to bring their campus cards and arrive 45 minutes early.

The successful applicants’ names are not reproduced here to protect student privacy.
