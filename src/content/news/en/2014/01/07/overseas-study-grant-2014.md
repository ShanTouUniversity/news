Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office opened the 2014 selection for its overseas study grant program. Supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the program is currently aimed at graduating undergraduates seeking master’s study at outstanding institutions outside mainland China. The 2014 program planned to support 20 students, with awards in three tiers—RMB 80,000, RMB 50,000 and RMB 30,000 per student.

Applicants must be graduating undergraduates without a master’s or higher degree, demonstrate English proficiency meeting overseas-university requirements, and show strong academic performance, language ability, school and program selection, and overall qualities. Required materials included an application form, an institution-verified transcript, two recommendation letters and an English essay of no more than 500 words.

Applicants were required to complete the online application and submit written materials by 4 p.m. on March 7, 2014. Each student could apply to no more than three eligible institutions. The candidate-school list and detailed requirements were provided in the original attachments.
