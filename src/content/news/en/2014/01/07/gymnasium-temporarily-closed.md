It is reported that the Shantou University gymnasium has been temporarily closed starting today due to venue setup for university events.

The closure affects all facilities within the gymnasium, including the fitness center, indoor badminton courts, and indoor table tennis hall. According to the Physical Education Department, the gymnasium will also remain closed throughout the winter vacation.

Faculty and students who regularly use the facilities are advised to stay tuned for further notices regarding reopening and to adjust their exercise plans accordingly.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
