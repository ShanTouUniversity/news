Shantou University’s Three-Style Development Office issued Academic Conduct Inspection Report No. 252. Inspectors checked the DG and E teaching buildings at 8 a.m. on January 2 and 3, 2014, respectively. They recorded 23 late arrivals in total, including nine arrivals more than five minutes late. The student list is not reproduced here to protect privacy.

| Date | Location | Up to five minutes late | More than five minutes late |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| January 2, 2014 | DG Teaching Building | 10 | 4 |
| January 3, 2014 | E Teaching Building | 4 | 5 |
| Total | Two teaching buildings | 14 | 9 |

The university asked the relevant schools, colleges and units to provide educational guidance to the students concerned and continue efforts to strengthen academic conduct.
