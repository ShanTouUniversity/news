Shantou University’s Human Resources Office announced the results of the 2013 senior-economist qualification review on January 3, 2014. Following assessment by Guangdong’s First Senior Economist Qualification Review Committee, STU faculty member Yan Zhaoyong received the qualification. The public-notice period ran through January 10.

During the notice period, organizations or individuals could submit objections to the provincial review committee’s office or Shantou University’s Human Resources Office. The university office listed its phone number as 0754-86502340.
