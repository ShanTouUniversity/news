It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University has completed the selection of its case team members for the Fall 2014 semester. Following assessment by the case team review panel, the school has released the shortlist, with the names of admitted students published within the school.

## Requirements for Admitted Students

According to the school's arrangements, all admitted students must fulfill two requirements:

| Requirement | Details |
| ----------- | ------- |
| Course | Take BUS3186 Business Case Management and Analysis, taught by Wu Jingqiang, in the Fall 2014 semester |
| Agreement | Sign a commitment agreement with the school |

The school noted that students with questions may contact Professor Wu Jingqiang directly.

The case team serves as an important platform for the Business School to cultivate business case analysis talent. Through systematic coursework and hands-on practice, it helps students sharpen their analytical skills in preparation for business case competitions.
