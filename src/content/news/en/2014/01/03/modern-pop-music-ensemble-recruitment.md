Shantou University’s Arts Education Center plans to form a modern pop music ensemble and announced an open recruitment for students on January 3, 2014. The ensemble is seeking instrumentalists and singers for positions including drums, electric bass, electric guitar, violin, keyboards, and male and female vocals. Musician Liu Dawei will serve as its instructor.

Auditions are scheduled for the afternoons of January 13–15, 2014. Details are listed below:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Audition time | January 13–15, 2014, 2:00–4:00 p.m. |
| Venue | Music Production Room 303, third floor, Arts Education Center |
| Positions | Drums, electric bass, electric guitar, violin, keyboards, and vocals |
| Instructor | Liu Dawei |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Arts Education Center)
