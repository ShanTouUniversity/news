Shantou University’s Zhicheng Residential College announced the participants selected for its eighth Chinese Traditional Culture Study Camp. Because applications exceeded the original enrollment target, the college added places and selected 32 students. Their names and academic details are omitted to protect student privacy.

Participants are scheduled to attend the opening ceremony and begin classes at 7 p.m. on February 17, 2014, in Lecture Room J-354 at Zhicheng Residential College.
