Shantou University’s Publicity Department circulated information on January 3, 2014, about the “Our China Dream—Telling China’s Stories” arts call. The initiative was jointly launched by the Ministry of Culture’s Arts Department, People’s Daily Online, Xinhua Online and Guangming Online. It accepted written, audio and video works from December 2013 through February 28, 2014.

Organizers planned to select 60 outstanding works through public comment and expert review: 10 first-tier, 20 second-tier and 30 third-tier recommendations. Each selected work could receive up to RMB 20,000 in creative support and be promoted through discs and print publications. The Guangdong Provincial Publicity Department would provide matching funds at a 1:1 ratio.

Shantou University set an internal submission deadline of February 23, with its Publicity Department coordinating the university’s submissions. The official guidelines govern work formats and category requirements.
