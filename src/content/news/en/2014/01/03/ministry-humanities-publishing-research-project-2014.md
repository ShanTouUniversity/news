Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office announced that it is organizing applications for the 2014 Ministry of Education general humanities and social sciences research projects. The call is aimed at eligible staff members of the university press and philosophy and social sciences journals. The Ministry of Education’s project guidelines are intended to encourage research on the management, development and practice of higher-education publishing.

Applicants must be formally employed and currently working for the university press or a philosophy and social sciences journal. Planning Fund applicants must hold an editor-in-chief or associate editor-in-chief title. Youth Fund applicants must hold an editor title or above and have been born on or after January 1, 1974. Proposed titles must follow the project guidelines without alteration; application and review procedures follow those for the Ministry’s general projects.
