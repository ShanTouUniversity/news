The Ministry of Education’s Department of Ideological and Political Work launched the third National College Counselor Professional Skills Competition. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced campus registration arrangements on January 3, 2014. The competition was open to full-time counselors employed by regular higher-education institutions and comprised preliminary, regional semifinal and national final stages.

Provincial education authorities were to organize preliminary rounds, which were due to finish by March 31, 2014. Semifinals across six regions were scheduled to conclude by April 30, followed by a national final in May. Shantou University planned to select nominees from its applicants before making recommendations to the provincial education department.

Interested counselors were asked to submit electronic and printed application materials by January 10. The application form and competition plan were provided in the OA attachments.
