It is reported that Shantou University has officially published the results of its 2013 campus research fund projects, following expert correspondence review, panel review, and deliberation by the university's Research Committee and Academic Committee. The funded programs cover three categories: national fund cultivation projects, young researcher fund projects, and humanities and social science research fund projects.

## Contract Deadline Set for January 10

According to arrangements by the Research Office, leaders of funded projects are required to complete the corresponding project contract forms and deliver them in triplicate to the office by January 10, 2014, along with electronic copies, so that funding disbursement procedures can be processed. Notably, those who miss the deadline will be regarded as having voluntarily given up their project status, and the project will be cancelled.
