Following the university’s tender evaluation, Shantou Shēngháng Construction Co., Ltd. was selected to renovate the Youth League office at Shantou University’s College of Engineering. The winning bid was RMB 42,198.66. The university posted the result for public notice from January 3 through January 6, 2014.
