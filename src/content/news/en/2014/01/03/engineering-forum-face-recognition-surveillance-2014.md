The 177th session of Shantou University’s College of Engineering Research Forum was scheduled for 9–11 a.m. on January 6, 2014. Professor Lai Jianhuang of Sun Yat-sen University was to present “Face-Recognition Methods for Video Surveillance” in the college’s Academic Exchange Room (South Building 312).

The forum was open to College of Engineering faculty and students, as well as other interested members of the university community.
