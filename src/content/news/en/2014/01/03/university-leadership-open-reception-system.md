Shantou University’s General Office announced a university-leadership reception system on January 3, 2014. University leaders would take turns meeting faculty, staff and students, while the General Office coordinated appointments and follow-up.

Reception sessions were to be held in principle every other week during each semester, on Thursdays from 3 to 5 p.m. Meetings would normally take place in the host leader’s office; if a session conflicted with a holiday, it would be postponed by one week. Visitors were required to book in advance through campus notices. Staff would record concerns and arrange appointments, with each visit limited to 20 minutes in principle.

The system also established procedures for recording requests, assigning them to relevant departments and reporting back to visitors. Complex or repeated cases required further review, and staff were required to maintain confidentiality.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
> ⚠️ Specific host leaders and appointment details are provided in subsequent university notices.
