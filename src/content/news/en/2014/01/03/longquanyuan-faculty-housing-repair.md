Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has announced pre-occupancy repair arrangements for faculty housing in Longquanyuan. Because some units have remained unoccupied since the renovation project was completed and have gone through the rainy and typhoon seasons, the university will arrange for the original contractor to address issues identified during residents’ inspections.

## Reporting arrangements

The inspection covers Longquanyuan buildings C1, C2, C4, C5 and C6. Buildings C3 and C7, together with the A-type housing, were scheduled for completion on February 9, 2014. Residents who find peeling paint, wall cracks, difficult-to-operate doors or windows, or other problems should compile their reports by the end of the workday on January 10, including the building, room number and contact telephone number.

Reports may be emailed to the Infrastructure Office at `o_jjb@stu.edu.cn`. Other questions may be directed to the office at `86502490`.
