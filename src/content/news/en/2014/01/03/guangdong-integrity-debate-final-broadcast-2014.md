The final of Guangdong’s first college debate competition on “Integrity and Honesty,” organized by the provincial Party committee’s education work committee, the provincial education department and the provincial education discipline-inspection commission, was scheduled to air at 9 p.m. on January 5, 2014, on Guangdong Television’s Public Channel.

Repeat broadcasts were scheduled for 1:18 a.m. and 2:10 p.m. on January 6. Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Office notified campus units of the broadcast on January 3.
