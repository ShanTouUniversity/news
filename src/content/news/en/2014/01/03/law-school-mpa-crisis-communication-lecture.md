Shantou University’s Law School is scheduled to hold the fifth lecture in its MPA series on January 4, 2014. Mo Kailiang, director of the Shantou Municipal Government Emergency Management Office, will speak on crisis response and public opinion guidance in the multimedia era.

Mo joined the workforce in 1979 and graduated from the Central Party School’s university program. The lecture details are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | January 4, 2014, 4:00–5:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E, Room 201 |
| Speaker | Mo Kailiang, director of the Shantou Municipal Government Emergency Management Office |
| Topic | Crisis response and public opinion guidance in the multimedia era |
