Shantou University’s Network and Information Center reported a fault on the campus network’s China Mobile access link on the evening of January 3, 2014. The outage caused unstable connections and slow speeds in student residential areas. At the time of the notice, the center had contacted China Mobile and was investigating the cause; it apologized for the disruption.
