Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced the 2013 results for the Pan Yugang Scholarship and Senderly Chemical Scholarship. Following review by the college selection committees, each scholarship had four student recipients, with an award of RMB 2,500 per student. Recipients’ names and academic details are omitted to protect their privacy.

| Scholarship | Recipients | Award per student |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Pan Yugang Scholarship | 4 | RMB 2,500 |
| Senderly Chemical Scholarship | 4 | RMB 2,500 |
