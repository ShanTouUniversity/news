The China Scholarship Council approved seven Shantou University projects under its 2014 Outstanding Undergraduate International Exchange Program, with 18 planned student places for 2014–2015. The projects involve the Schools of Engineering, Business, Liberal Arts, Law, and Journalism and Communication, with partner institutions in Canada, Portugal, Ireland, New Zealand and France. Four projects involving the Business School account for 12 places.

Applicants were students in the 2011 or 2012 cohorts of the participating schools, with an average academic score of at least 85 and an invitation or admission letter from the partner institution before online registration. English qualifications included IELTS above 6.5, TOEFL above 95 or a passing WSK result. Tuition was payable for the University of Calgary, University of Canterbury and Victoria University of Wellington programs. Students approved by the China Scholarship Council could receive a round-trip international ticket and monthly living support at the applicable local rate.

Online applications were scheduled in two rounds: April 21–May 5, 2014, and September 20–30, 2014. The university and participating schools would review and recommend candidates under open, fair, voluntary and merit-based principles. Students were advised to consult their school’s academic or international office about course-credit transfer.
