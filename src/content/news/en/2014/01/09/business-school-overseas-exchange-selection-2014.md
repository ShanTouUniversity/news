Shantou University’s Business School opened selection for overseas programs for the 2014–2015 academic year. Undergraduates could apply for exchanges at ISM University of Management and Economics in Lithuania, Dublin City University in Ireland, Universidade Católica Portuguesa, the University of Manitoba in Canada, and European Business School in France. A 3+2 dual-degree and bachelor’s-to-master’s pathway at Dublin City University was also available.

Exchange programs offered two to four places per university, generally for one semester, with some allowing a full year. Applicants were generally expected to be Business School undergraduates in the 2011 or 2012 cohorts, have a GPA of at least 3.0, and submit English scores, transcripts and a 300–500-word study plan. Host universities waived tuition, but students paid travel, living, accommodation, book and insurance costs; the notice estimated monthly expenses of RMB 4,000–8,000.

The Dublin City University 3+2 pathway was limited to the 2011 cohort and offered 10 places. The University of Manitoba deadline was April 1, 2014; most other exchanges were due April 20, and the 3+2 pathway deadline was May 1.
