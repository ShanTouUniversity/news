Applications opened for the 2014 National Social Science Fund project for translating Chinese scholarship. Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office circulated the call, which supports translation of outstanding Chinese academic works for publication by authoritative overseas publishers. Supported languages include English, French, Spanish, Russian, German, Japanese, Korean and Arabic. Works must contain at least 100,000 Chinese characters and generally no more than 300,000; funding is approximately RMB 100,000 per 100,000 characters, capped at RMB 500,000 per project.

Applicants were required to have an overseas publishing contract before applying, and translation projects needed copyright authorization. Researchers, research institutions and domestic publishing organizations could apply under the eligibility rules. Preliminary-review materials were due February 24, 2014, and qualified applications were due to the STU Research Affairs Office by February 28.

| Item | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Supported languages | English, French, Spanish, Russian, German, Japanese, Korean and Arabic |
| Funding | About RMB 100,000 per 100,000 characters; maximum RMB 500,000 |
| Length | At least 100,000; generally no more than 300,000 Chinese characters |
| Preliminary review | Send to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn by February 24, 2014 |
| STU submission deadline | February 28, 2014 |
| Contacts | Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun; 86502563 |
