Shantou University’s Research Office announced the 2014 application schedule for the National Social Science Fund’s post-funding program on January 9. The program primarily supports substantially completed but unpublished basic research in the humanities and social sciences, mainly academic monographs. The general grant amount was RMB 200,000, with applicants asked to propose a figure based on research needs. Applications covered all 26 fund disciplines, including separately listed education, arts and military studies; classified research was excluded.

Applicants had to be Chinese citizens with either a mid-level or higher professional title or a doctoral degree. Works generally had to be at least 80% complete and unpublished; works submitted by retired researchers had to be at least 60% complete. A submission required recommendations from three full-professor-level peers or a designated publisher; retired applicants could be recommended by one or two experts. Each applicant could submit one work per application round.

For the 2014 rounds, electronic applications, information forms, manuscripts and project summaries were due by February 24 or August 25 for formal review, followed by eligible paper submissions to the Research Office by February 28 or August 31, respectively. The program held correspondence reviews each April and October. Inquiries could be directed to Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun at 86502563 or `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn`.
