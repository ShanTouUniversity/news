The General Education Research Branch of the China Association of Higher Education launched a selection and recognition program for excellent university general-education courses. Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office circulated the call and invited interested faculty to apply.

The program deadline was March 20, 2014. STU faculty intending to apply were asked to submit materials to the university’s contact by March 10. The original attachments contain the call, application instructions and forms.
