The 2013 Shantou Science and Technology Plan projects were approved, and Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office asked the principal investigators to sign project contracts with the Shantou Science and Technology Bureau. Research content, objectives and schedules must match the original applications; any changes require an explanation. The investigator’s school or institute should be listed as the second party and Shantou University as the third party. Participating organizations must affix their official seals where applicable.

Investigators were required to use the 2013 contract form, obtain review by the Research Affairs Office, then submit six printed copies by January 16, 2014. The project list and contract template were attached to the original notice.

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Contract content | Match research plan, objectives and schedule in the application |
| Review | Submit to the Research Affairs Office first |
| Printed copies | Six |
| Deadline | January 16, 2014 |
