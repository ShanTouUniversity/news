It is reported that renowned illustrator Jimmy Liao will deliver a lecture titled "The Creative Work I Love" at the Chinese University of Hong Kong on Friday, January 10, at 4:30 p.m. The talk is the first of CUHK's 2014 Xinxing Arts and Culture Lecture series.

## Key Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The Creative Work I Love |
| Speaker | Jimmy Liao |
| Time | Friday, January 10, 2014, 4:30 p.m. |
| Viewing | http://www.cuhk.edu.hk/cpr/lectures/jimmyliao |

According to STU's Office of International Exchange and Cooperation, CUHK invites distinguished professors and well-known public figures each year to give lectures. Under an agreement between the two universities, these talks are shared online so that STU faculty and students can access them regardless of location, strengthening academic ties between the two institutions and promoting cultural exchange between Guangdong and Hong Kong.

From 3:30 p.m. that day, viewers can visit the website above to watch the lecture live. Questions may be directed to the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation.
