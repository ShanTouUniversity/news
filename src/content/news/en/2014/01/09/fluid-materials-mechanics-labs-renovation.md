Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office planned renovations to parts of the fluid mechanics and materials mechanics laboratories in the Civil Engineering Department of the School of Engineering. Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. was selected as contractor, with a winning bid of RMB 68,700.

The work covers reconstruction of the former equipment-store room north of Lecture Hall 7 and repairs to Rooms 208 and 209 on the north side of the Engineering Building. Construction was scheduled to begin January 14, 2014, and finish by February 12. The university warned that work could cause noise, vibration and dust and advised people to keep away from the work area for safety.
