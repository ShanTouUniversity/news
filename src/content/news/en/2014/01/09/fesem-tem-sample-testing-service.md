Shantou University’s Central Laboratory arranged a limited number of field-emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM) and transmission electron microscopy (TEM) sample tests for university faculty through Tianmei Technology Co., Ltd. The service was intended to support requests for high-resolution imaging. Samples mentioned included molecular sieves, photoresists, carbon nanotubes, lithium-battery separators, nanocatalysts and polymers.

Faculty seeking the service were asked to contact a staff member surnamed Lin at the Central Laboratory by January 15, 2014, and arrange shipment by January 17. The notice did not specify the exact number of available tests.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service | FESEM and TEM sample testing |
| Eligible users | STU faculty members |
| Registration deadline | January 15, 2014 |
| Target shipment date | By January 17, 2014 |
| Contact | Lin, yjlin@stu.edu.cn, campus extension 68626 |
