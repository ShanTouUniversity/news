With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Dexiang Enterprise established a scholarship for the Chinese University of Hong Kong International Summer School program. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office opened selection for 2014, planning to choose 10 full-time undergraduates from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts at the main campus, excluding the Medical College. The summer courses were scheduled for July and August 2014.

Selected students could take one morning and one afternoon course, earning six credits in total. The overall program cost was estimated at about HK$27,000 per student, including tuition, accommodation and living costs. Each scholarship recipient would receive HK$15,000, with the remainder paid by the student. Applicants were required to submit an English application and an English essay of no more than 300 words; the university would make final selections through document review and interviews.

Applications were due by 5 p.m. on February 28, 2014, to the Student Affairs Office’s student-aid center, with electronic copies sent to xyli@stu.edu.cn.
