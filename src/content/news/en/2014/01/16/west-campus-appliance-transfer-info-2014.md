It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University is collecting information on used appliances available for transfer from households relocating from the West Campus staff quarters.

Relocating households with used air conditioners or other household appliances to sell are asked to fill out the "Staff Used Appliance Transfer Information Registration Form" and email it to the Labor Union at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn by Monday, January 20. The union will compile the submissions and publish them on the Office Automation system, where interested staff may contact sellers directly using the phone numbers listed on the forms.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Target Group | Relocating households in West Campus staff quarters |
| Items Collected | Used air conditioners and other household appliances |
| Submission Method | Complete the registration form and email to o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |
| Deadline | January 20, 2014 |
| Publication Channel | Office Automation system |
| Organizer | Labor Union |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
> ⚠️ The appliance transfer registration form is available on the OA system.
