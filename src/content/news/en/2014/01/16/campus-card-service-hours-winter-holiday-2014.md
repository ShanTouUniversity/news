The Network and Information Center announced the campus card service schedule for the winter break and Spring Festival period on January 16, 2014. Two service counters will close and reopen on different dates.

## Counter schedule

| Counter | Closed | Reopens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expert Apartments campus card counter | January 28–February 9 | February 10 |
| Campus card recharge counter beneath the Second Dining Hall | January 22–February 23 | February 24 |

During the closures, faculty and students may use the self-service deposit and transfer machines to recharge. Balance inquiries and loss reports are also available through dining-hall terminals, deposit machines, the campus card website, or the automated telephone service. Assistance was available at 86503420, 13556307566, and support@stu.edu.cn.
