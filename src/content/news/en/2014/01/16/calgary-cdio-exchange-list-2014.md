It is reported that the College of Engineering issued a notice on January 16, 2014, announcing the participants for the 2014 Shantou University–University of Calgary CDIO exchange program.

After preliminary review and interviews by the evaluation panel, 20 students were selected for this year’s program, covering majors such as mechanical and electrical engineering, communications, computer science, civil engineering and biology. Names are anonymized to protect privacy.
