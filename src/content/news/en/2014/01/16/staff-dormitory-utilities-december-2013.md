The Resource Management Office of Shantou University announced payment arrangements for December 2013 water and electricity charges in staff housing on January 16, 2014. The latest meter readings were taken on January 1–2, 2014, following the previous readings on December 1, 2013.

## Checking and review

Relevant faculty and staff may log in to the university utility management system with their campus network accounts to check the charges. Questions or objections should be brought to the Utilities Center between January 16 and 20; the contact extension is 2489.

Charges not disputed before the deadline would be deducted by the Finance Office from the individual’s January 2014 salary account.
