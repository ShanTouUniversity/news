The Resource Management Office of Shantou University issued guidance on preventing H7N9 avian influenza and other respiratory infections on January 16, 2014, reminding faculty and students to strengthen personal protection during the winter break.

## Prevention guidance

- Maintain a balanced diet, get sufficient sleep, drink enough water, and avoid excessive fatigue.
- Wash hands frequently, cover the nose and mouth when coughing or sneezing, and keep indoor air circulating.
- Seek medical attention promptly if fever, coughing, a runny nose, or other respiratory symptoms appear.
- Avoid close contact with poultry and dead wild birds, and cook chicken, eggs, and other food thoroughly.
- Minimize visits to crowded or poorly ventilated places and prepare necessary supplies before long trips.

People with underlying conditions were also advised to carry their regular medication when traveling and take precautions according to their personal health needs.
