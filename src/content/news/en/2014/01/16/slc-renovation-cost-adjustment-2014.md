It is reported that an additional cost item for the renovation of the Student Learning Center (SLC) new site has been released for public comment.

The project broke ground on December 25, 2013. During construction, at the request of the user unit, mineral wool filling was added inside lightweight steel keel double-sided calcium silicate board walls for sound insulation. Based on actual site conditions, the Infrastructure Office has made a preliminary estimate for the additional work.

The Infrastructure Office had also publicized an earlier estimated increase of approximately RMB 8,000 on January 3, 2014. After the user unit changed the room layout, existing wiring could not meet the revised needs, requiring new electrical conduits and lighting. Both items were preliminarily estimated and posted for public comment under university procedures.

## Adjustment Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Project | SLC new site renovation |
| Start date | December 25, 2013 |
| Additional work | Mineral wool filling for sound insulation in lightweight steel keel calcium silicate board walls |
| Estimated additional cost | Approx. 7,000 yuan |
| Cumulative increase | Within the university's 10% threshold |
| Previously announced item | New electrical conduits and lighting after a room-layout change, approx. RMB 8,000 |

In accordance with the *Shantou University Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering Management of Infrastructure Projects*, the estimated additional cost is now being publicized. Any objections should be submitted in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 5:30 p.m. on January 18, 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notices (Infrastructure Office and Resource Management Office, January 3 and 16, 2014)
