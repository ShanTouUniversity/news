It is reported that the results of the 2013 national human resources management qualification examination were recently released. Thirty-seven students from the 2010 public administration cohort at Shantou University’s School of Law registered for the examination. Of them, 22 passed both the theory and practical sections in one sitting, while 13 passed one section.

## Training program

The training program was jointly organized by the STU School of Law and Shantou Guohui Vocational Management Training School. The results indicated a strong performance by participating students in the professional qualification examination.
