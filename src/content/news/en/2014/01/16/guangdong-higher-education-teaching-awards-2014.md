It is reported that the Guangdong Education Department published the review results for the seventh Guangdong Higher-Education Teaching Awards. Shantou University recommended 11 projects after a competitive internal selection process, and five were included in the public list: three first-prize projects and two second-prize projects.

## Projects included

| Award | Project | Discipline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First prize | Comprehensive reform and practice for cultivating outstanding doctors from an international perspective | Medicine |
| First prize | Advancing the localization of CDIO to cultivate outstanding engineering talent | Engineering |
| First prize | Integrating public-service activities with courses: innovation and practice of STU public-service courses | Education |
| Second prize | Research and practice on reforming the training model for applied journalism professionals | Literature |
| Second prize | Developing an immersive medical professional-quality training system oriented toward service and dedication | Medicine |

The public notice period lasted 90 days. During that period, organizations or individuals with objections concerning ownership or other matters could contact the relevant provincial awards office under the published procedures.
