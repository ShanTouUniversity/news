It is reported that The Voice of China production team planned to film a music drama at Shantou University from February to April 2014. To support the production and provide students with social-practice opportunities, the STU Chai Network Media Center recruited volunteers from across the university to form a student assistance team.

## Recruitment arrangements

Volunteer roles included wardrobe, makeup, production support, external liaison, script supervision, and assistant positions in camera, lighting, and props. The production team would assign specific duties according to filming needs and provide training before work began.

The filming support activity was planned to run from February 9 to around mid-April, subject to the production schedule. Interviews were scheduled for 3–6 p.m. on January 17 at the Chai Network office, Room 734 of Building 789.

Interested students should submit materials as required in the original OA notice. The full application form and subsequent arrangements are available on the OA system.
