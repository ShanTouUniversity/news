It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts issued a notice on January 16, 2014, to launch selection for the exchange program with the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, Dublin City University (DCU), Ireland, for the fall semester of 2014–2015.

## Program

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 5 |
| Eligibility | Full-time undergraduates of the College of Liberal Arts, Classes of 2012 and 2013 (to be sophomores or juniors in fall 2014) |
| Host | Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, Dublin City University, Ireland |
| Duration | One semester or two semesters; applicants may choose “Both” to accept either, subject to adjustment |
| Fees | Pay tuition to STU as normal; DCU waives tuition for exchange students; students cover international travel, living, accommodation and other costs, about 700–900 euros per month (approx. 6,000–8,000 yuan) |

## Criteria

1. Excellent academic performance;
2. Adequate financial support;
3. Strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, with an IELTS score of 6.0 or above (for visa);
4. Maturity, independence and teamwork;
5. Willingness to promote Shantou University and its characteristics abroad.

## Application

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | Application form, GPA and ranking certificate, copies of English proficiency certificates if available |
| Deadline | Before the end of work on the morning of April 4, 2014 |
| Submission | College Youth League Committee Office (South Building 201, Liberal Arts Building), both hard copy and electronic version |
| Email | twwxy@stu.edu.cn |
| Process | Document screening → written test (reading and English writing) → interview (language ability) → 5 students recommended to DCU |

See http://www.dcu.ie/schools.shtml for reference. The application form is available in the OA attachment.
