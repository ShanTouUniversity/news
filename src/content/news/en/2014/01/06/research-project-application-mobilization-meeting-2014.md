Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office is scheduled to hold a mobilization meeting on January 7, 2014, for applications to the National Social Science Fund and the Ministry of Education humanities and social sciences projects. The meeting will explain application requirements for the 2014 cycle and feature Professor Chen Jinlong of South China Normal University sharing experience with project applications and reviews.

The meeting is intended for leaders responsible for research, department heads, research secretaries, principal investigators of provincial- or ministerial-level projects, prospective applicants and newly recruited faculty members. Graduate students are also welcome.
