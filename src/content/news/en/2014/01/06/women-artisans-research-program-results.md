It is reported that the results have been announced for the Student Research Program on "Women Artisans in Local Culture of Eastern Guangdong," organized by the Women's Studies Center of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University in collaboration with the Gender and Chaoshan Culture Research Group of Hanshan Normal University.

The program attracted 43 STU students forming 17 teams, all of whom proceeded to the interview stage. Due to the large number of outstanding proposals, the Center expanded the number of awards beyond the original three funded projects to include six additional merit awards.

| Category | Grant | Team Members | Project Title |
|----------|-------|--------------|---------------|
| Funded Project | RMB 3,000 each | Xie GH, Xiao HX, Li X, Fang JH | The Paper-Cutting Life of Chaozhou Women |
| Funded Project | RMB 3,000 each | Wang YC | Unlocking the Potential of Chaozhou Women Artisans in Local Economic and Cultural Development |
| Funded Project | RMB 3,000 each | Liu YT, Li JY | Women Woodcarvers in Local Culture of Eastern Guangdong |
| Merit Award | RMB 1,000 each | Zhang MQ, He SY, Guo ST | The Growth Story of the "Rice-Noodle Roll Master" on Tongping Road, Old Town Shantou |
| Merit Award | RMB 1,000 each | Yang ZY | Women and Joss Paper Making in the Chaoshan Region |
| Merit Award | RMB 1,000 each | Xie YX, Huang Q, Yao JY, Luo JX | Approaching Chaozhou Opera Actresses |
| Merit Award | RMB 1,000 each | Huang D | Inheritor and Innovator of Chaozhou Embroidery in the New Era — Profile of Hong Yujing of Yuhong Nail-Gold Embroidery Studio |
| Merit Award | RMB 1,000 each | Yu WY | Family and Social Status of Threading Practitioners in the Era of Cultural Industries in Chaoshan |
| Merit Award | RMB 1,000 each | Li SX, Cheng RY | Eastern Guangdong Women's Studies Program — Hakka Women Brewers |

Teams within each category are listed in no particular order. The Center noted that despite the expanded awards, many strong proposals could not be accommodated, and encouraged all applicants to pursue their creative and meaningful projects and to contribute to next year's planned publication on women artisans, exploring the role of women in local culture.
