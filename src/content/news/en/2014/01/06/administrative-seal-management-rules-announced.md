Shantou University’s Party and Government Office issued a document announcing rules for the use and management of the university’s administrative seal. The rules are intended to further standardize seal use and management, and all university units are required to follow them.

The document is numbered “STU Party and Government Office [2014] No. 1” and dated January 6, 2014. Because the full rules appear in the original attachment, this report only covers their publication and does not infer details not shown in the notice body.
