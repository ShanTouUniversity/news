Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has published the December 2013 utility charges for student dormitories. Meter readings for this billing cycle were taken on December 30 and 31, and students can check their dormitory bills through the STU utility-management system.

## Review and payment schedule

| Item | Time or method |
| --- | --- |
| Check the bill | Log in to `power.stu.edu.cn` with a campus-network account |
| Submit an inquiry | During office hours from January 6 to 10 at the Utilities Center, with a signed application form |
| Regular payment | January 13–15; deducted from the campus card by the Network Center |
| Utilities Center | Beside the Science and Technology Building; `86502489` |

Students who find errors or omissions may complete the Utilities Fee Review Application Form and submit it to the Utilities Center. Students whose dormitory assignment does not match the student list should contact their counselor’s office. Inquiries submitted after the review period that require correction may take three to five working days to process.

The university advises students to top up campus-card balances in time to ensure that the deduction is completed successfully.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
> ⚠️ Utility statements and the review application form are available on the OA system.
