Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office opened applications for the 2014 Ministry of Education special humanities and social sciences research projects on ideological and political work in higher education. The call covers Category I projects, Category II projects and a special project for core counselors, with indicative funding of RMB 50,000–60,000, RMB 20,000 and RMB 10,000 per project and research periods of two, one and one year, respectively.

The application is subject to institutional quotas, with no more than two applications from the university in each category. Each applicant may submit one project and must be able to undertake the research and implementation. The core-counselor project is open to full-time university counselors. Applicants leading ongoing Ministry of Education, National Social Science Fund of China or National Natural Science Foundation projects, among other ineligible categories, may not apply. Applications are submitted through the China Universities Humanities and Social Sciences Information Network and require electronic and printed materials.

The notice also requires anonymous review, truthful submissions and compliance with intellectual-property rules. Applicants should use the project guidelines and application templates in the original attachments.
