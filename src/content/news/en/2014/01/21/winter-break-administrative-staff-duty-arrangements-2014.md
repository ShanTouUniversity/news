The General Office of Shantou University announced the 2014 winter-break duty arrangements for administrative, teaching-support, and logistics staff in relevant units on January 21, 2014, and asked units to implement the schedule under the university’s applicable rules.

Relevant units were asked to ensure timely and effective services for faculty, students, and staff during the winter break and to follow the requirements of STU General Office document No. 6 (2013). The detailed duty roster and dates were provided in an OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office)
>
> ⚠️ The winter-break duty roster and detailed schedule are available on the OA system.
