Shantou University is recruiting two security guards for its Science Center. The positions will be hired as auxiliary contract workers, without transferring an employment relationship to the university, and compensation will follow applicable university standards.

## Requirements and duties

Applicants should be responsible, able to follow work arrangements, in good health, no older than 50, and able to work 24-hour shifts. Experience using electronic equipment would be an advantage.

Duties include managing security, fire-control, and closed-circuit monitoring systems; enforcing visitor registration; coordinating repairs to public facilities in the science building; supporting university events at the Science Lecture Hall; maintaining the venue; and completing other assigned tasks.

## Application arrangements

Applicants were asked to send a résumé and copies of their identity document, family-planning certificate, graduation certificate, and other relevant credentials to the STU Research Office by February 15. Shortlisted applicants would be invited to an interview and asked to present original documents for verification.

Applications could be sent to the Research Office at 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. The contact was Ms. Xie at 86502928, email o_kyc@stu.edu.cn.
