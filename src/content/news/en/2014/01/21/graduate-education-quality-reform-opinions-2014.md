Shantou University issued its Opinions on Improving Graduate-Education Quality on January 21, 2014. The document sets out reforms in admissions, training models, quality assurance, and international cooperation. It was developed following the 16th university leadership meeting of 2013 on December 10.

## Reform priorities

- **Admissions:** Establish a dynamic allocation mechanism for enrollment quotas. From 2014, the unified foreign-language test would be removed from the doctoral entrance examination and replaced by stronger discipline-based assessment in the retest. From 2015, doctoral selection would move to an application-review model. Doctoral students would generally complete their dissertations within three to four years, with a maximum enrollment period of six years.
- **Training models:** Revise graduate training plans, emphasizing innovation and research training for academic-degree students and professional ability for professional-degree students. New plans would apply to the 2014 cohort and include English versions. The university encouraged English or bilingual teaching and the use of original-language textbooks.
- **Supervisor responsibility:** End the qualification-review system for graduate supervisors and replace it with eligibility review for student recruitment. Supervisors would remain primarily responsible for academic guidance, research methods, and academic standards. A domestic-and-overseas co-supervisor model would be introduced for doctoral students.
- **Professional degrees:** Build practice bases and external-supervisor teams and strengthen industry-university cooperation. Professional-degree theses could take forms including research reports, designs, case analyses, news investigations, management plans, patents, and artistic works. At least one-third of thesis reviewers and defense committee members should be senior professionals from relevant industries.
- **Funding:** Add university scholarships and awards to national scholarships and grants. Teaching-assistant, research-assistant, and management-assistant positions, hardship grants, and research-assistant support would strengthen practical training and financial assistance. Beginning with the 2014 cohort, supervisor business allowances would be replaced by thesis-defense subsidies.
- **Quality oversight:** Establish a graduate-education supervision group, strengthen the role of supervisor groups, use a comprehensive graduate-education management system, and publish an annual graduate-education quality report from 2014. Anonymous thesis review would continue, and 5–15 percent of master’s theses would undergo targeted sampling reviews from 2014.
- **Internationalization:** Support graduate-training cooperation with high-level overseas universities, encourage participation in academic exchanges, and fund doctoral visits to overseas universities or research institutions.

The university aimed to establish by 2018 a well-structured graduate system covering doctoral and master’s education, academic and professional degrees, and full- and part-time study. It would also establish an implementation leadership group and use ISO standardization to support the reforms.
