The Party Committee of Shantou University announced a personnel adjustment on January 21, 2014. Following review by the first 2014 meeting of the university Party Standing Committee and the first 2014 university leadership meeting, Yao Mingan no longer serves as deputy director of the STU Supervision and Audit Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Committee of Shantou University)
