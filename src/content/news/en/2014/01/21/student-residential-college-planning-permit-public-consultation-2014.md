To improve student housing and learning conditions and promote the residential-college education model, Shantou University planned to build Phase I of a residential college in the southwest area of campus under the university’s master plan and construction procedures.

The Shantou Urban and Rural Planning Bureau posted a public consultation notice on January 20 at the new campus gate, the bulletin board west of the university motto monument, the wall east of the post office, and the wall west of the former publishing house. The consultation period ran through January 30.

Written comments could be submitted to the Resource Management Office during the notice period. Campus contacts were 0754-86502898 for the office’s general administration section and 0754-86503890 or 2890 for its key-project section. The master-plan revision drawing, public notice, and site photographs were provided in OA attachments.
