Three Shantou University submissions entered the provincial selection stage for the 2013 Guangdong university student-affairs “Quality Projects” and “Outstanding Student-Work Teams.”

## STU entries

| Selection | STU entry |
| --- | --- |
| Quality Student-Affairs Project | ATP Team Development Camp at Zhicheng College |
| Quality Student-Affairs Project | Campus Student Health Ambassadors at the Medical College |
| Outstanding Student-Work Team | Mutual Learning Center team of the Student Affairs Office |

Online voting ran from midnight on January 21 through 11:59:59 p.m. on February 20. Each Sina Weibo account had to select at least 10 options and could vote only once. Faculty and students were asked to follow the OA notice for access to the voting page.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The voting page and activity attachments are available on the OA system.
