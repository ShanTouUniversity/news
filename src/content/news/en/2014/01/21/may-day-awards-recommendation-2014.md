The Shantou University Trade Union forwarded the recommendation notice for the national and Guangdong May Day Labor Awards, Medals, and Pioneer Worker titles on January 21, 2014, opening the university’s internal recommendation process.

The All-China Federation of Trade Unions planned to recognize outstanding groups and employees before May Day 2014, while the Guangdong Federation of Trade Unions planned corresponding provincial awards. Eligible university units and individuals could participate in the recommendation process under the published criteria.

Eligible nominees were asked to complete the recommendation materials and submit them to the university Trade Union before the end of the morning on January 24, with electronic copies sent to the union’s official email. Detailed criteria, forms, and submission requirements were provided in the OA attachments.
