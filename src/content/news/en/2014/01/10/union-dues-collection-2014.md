Shantou University’s Labor Union began collecting membership dues for 2014, asking members to pay through their departmental unions before the winter break. Departmental unions were to consolidate the payments and submit them to the university union together with both printed and electronic copies of the dues-detail form.

The notice did not specify the dues amount or individual payment method; personal payment details are not disclosed here.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Members | Shantou University Labor Union members |
| Deadline | Before the 2014 winter break |
| Collection | Departmental unions |
| Required report | Dues-detail form, in print and electronic formats |
| University union email | o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
