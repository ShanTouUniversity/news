Applications opened for the Ministry of Education’s 2014 post-funded philosophy and social sciences research program. Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office circulated the requirements. Major projects offered RMB 200,000 each and general projects RMB 100,000. Eligible work had to be at least 70 percent complete and could include significant basic research, original theoretical research, documentary studies, translations, reference works and certain non-print outputs.

Applicants had to be formally employed STU faculty members capable of leading and carrying out the research. Major-project applicants needed a senior professional title. General-project applicants needed an intermediate title or above; those with an intermediate title also needed a doctorate. Works previously supported by provincial-level or higher research or publication grants, under a publishing contract, involved in intellectual-property disputes, or based on doctoral theses defended in the previous five years were ineligible.

Applicants were asked to email an electronic application for preliminary review by January 20, 2014, and submit the application, completed manuscript or other output, and supporting documents by February 18. Final works were generally to be published by Higher Education Press, and the university had a limited number of submissions.
