Shantou University’s Graduate School published the 2014 schedule for graduate thesis defenses and degree reviews. Schools and programs were asked to verify students’ progress against their training plans and complete thesis review, defense and degree-application procedures by the specified deadlines. Separate spring and fall timelines were set, with some differences between master’s and doctoral thesis review.

For the spring cycle, graduating students were expected to complete thesis writing and printing by April 10. Master’s thesis reviews were to be completed by May 20, and defenses by June 5. The corresponding fall deadlines were October 10, November 20 and December 5. Defense announcements had to be posted online at least seven days in advance; defenses without a published announcement were invalid. The University Academic Degree Committee was scheduled to review degree conferrals on June 18–23 and December 18–23.

| Stage | Spring cycle | Fall cycle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thesis preparation and training-plan review | By April 10 | By October 10 |
| Master’s thesis review completed | By May 20 | By November 20 |
| Doctoral thesis submission/review | Submit by April 14; feedback by May 20 | Submit by October 14; feedback by November 20 |
| Program-level defenses | By June 5 | By December 5 |
| School degree-subcommittee review and materials | By June 12 | By December 12 |
| University Academic Degree Committee review | June 18–23 | December 18–23 |
| Degree ceremony | June 25–27 | — |

Schools were also required to collect application and training records on time. Graduate students had to submit electronic thesis copies and degree information to the library and Graduate School. Late or non-compliant submissions could affect the school’s collection of degree certificates.
