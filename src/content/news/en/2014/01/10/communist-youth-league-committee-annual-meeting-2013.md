The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University scheduled its 10th committee’s sixth expanded meeting and 2013 annual work review for January 17, 2014. The meeting was set for 3 p.m. in the Student Activities Hall of the New Library. Members of the university Youth League Committee and representatives from school, residential-college and affiliated Youth League organizations were expected to attend.
