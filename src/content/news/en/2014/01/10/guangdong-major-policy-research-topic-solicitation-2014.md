The Guangdong Provincial Government Development Research Center opened a public solicitation for topics for its 2014 major policy consultation research projects. It invited proposals addressing strategic needs and important, emerging or difficult issues in Guangdong’s economic and social development, to inform the selection of topics for the annual competitive program.

Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office circulated the call. Interested university participants were asked to submit a printed proposal form to the office and email an electronic copy to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn by February 24, 2014. The provincial center’s overall deadline was February 28.
