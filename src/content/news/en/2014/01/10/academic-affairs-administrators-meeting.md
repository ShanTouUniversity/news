Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office scheduled a meeting for academic administrators and teaching secretaries from the university’s teaching units on January 15, 2014. The meeting was intended to strengthen communication between central and school-level administrators and discuss undergraduate education initiatives and ways to improve academic-administration services.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | January 15, 2014, 3:30–5:10 p.m. |
| Venue | Room 201, New Administration Center |
| Participants | Academic administrators and teaching secretaries from main-campus teaching units |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
