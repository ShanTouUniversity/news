Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office, Communist Youth League Committee and Student Clubs Union launched the “Voice of Clubs” project under the Student Community X Plan. Student organizations were encouraged to design activities suited to their communities around themes such as health, learning, interpersonal relationships, mental well-being and career planning. Activities were planned for the university’s student communities during the spring 2014 semester.

Project applications were scheduled for January 10–March 1, 2014, followed by approval from March 2–5 and project defenses in mid-March. A showcase was planned for May. Approved projects could apply for tiered funding of RMB 500–2,500. The top three club activities at the showcase would receive additional activity funding of RMB 1,000, RMB 800 and RMB 700, respectively.
