Shantou University’s Overseas Examinations Center has announced registration arrangements for the spring 2014 Business English Certificate (BEC) examinations. The STU test center offers Preliminary, Vantage and Higher levels, and registration is open regardless of age, gender, occupation, region or educational background.

## Registration and examination schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | March 3–21, weekdays 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 2:30–5 p.m.; March 15, 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. |
| Venue | Overseas Examinations Center, Room 504, fifth floor, Building E |
| Fees | RMB 395 for Preliminary, RMB 500 for Vantage and RMB 630 for Higher |
| Preliminary exam | May 17, written test in the morning and speaking test in the afternoon |
| Higher exam | May 24, written test in the morning and speaking test in the afternoon and following day |
| Vantage exam | May 31, written test in the morning and speaking test in the afternoon and following day |
| Test venue | Fifth floor, Building E, STU teaching complex |

Applicants must bring an identity document, a one-inch photograph and a Bank of China cash-deposit slip. Admission cards will be issued within two weeks before the examination and must be collected at the test center with an identity document. For inquiries, call `0754-86504078` or `0754-86503371`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
