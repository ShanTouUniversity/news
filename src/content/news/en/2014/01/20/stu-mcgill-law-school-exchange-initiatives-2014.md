With support from the Li Ka Shing (Canada) Foundation, Shantou University and McGill University planned university-wide faculty and student exchanges from 2014 to 2018. The collaboration includes initiatives in legal education, liberal arts exchange, and international business. Cooperation between the two law schools comprises four programs.

## Law-school programs

- **Summer law program:** A two-week program generally held in mid-to-late May. McGill Law was to send 10 students and two professors to STU in 2014, 2016, and 2018; STU Law was to select 10 students and two professors to visit McGill in 2015 and 2017. The exchange project would fund the program.
- **Exchange scholarships:** Each law school would support three students to study at the partner institution for one semester. Additional semester exchanges would connect public administration students at STU with political science students at McGill. Applications for 2014–2015 were expected to open in the following semester.
- **Doctoral fellowship in comparative legal cultures and legal education:** Each year, the fellowship would support one STU Law graduate admitted to McGill’s doctoral program. The program was expected to begin in 2014–2015; applicants would ordinarily need a master’s degree such as an LLM.
- **Junior scholar fellowship in comparative legal cultures and legal education:** Each year, one junior STU Law faculty member would be supported to undertake a one-year teaching program at McGill in collaboration with a faculty mentor.

Specific selection criteria and application arrangements were to be announced by the School of Law.
