Renovation work on the second phase of staff housing at Longquan Garden began on September 18, 2013. During construction, a large number of tree roots penetrated and blocked an outdoor sewage pipe, creating a need to relay the pipe and carry out related work.

## Proposed additional cost

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Estimated additional cost | Approximately RMB 8,000 |
| Cumulative cost share | Below the university’s 10% limit |
| Feedback deadline | 5:30 p.m. on January 22 |
| Receiving offices | Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office |

The university published the proposed cost increase under its infrastructure project tendering and bidding rules. Written objections could be submitted to the relevant offices before the deadline.
