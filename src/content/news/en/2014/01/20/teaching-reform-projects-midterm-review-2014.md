The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced preliminary arrangements on January 20, 2014, for the midterm review of the university’s 2013 teaching-reform projects. The formal review was planned for the beginning of the 2014 spring semester, with the exact date to be announced.

## Review process

| Deadline | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| By March 14 | Subprojects submit midterm forms to the overall project lead in their unit |
| By March 24 | Units complete self-reviews and submit reports and project forms to the Academic Affairs Office |
| Early April | Academic Affairs Office conducts the university-wide review and releases results and feedback |

The review would examine progress against project plans, use of the first funding allocation, interim outcomes, challenges and improvement measures, and plans for the next phase. Interim outcomes could include course notes, teaching materials, related papers, and other work. Results would be announced, and projects passing the review would receive the second funding allocation.

The project list and review forms were provided in the OA attachment.
