Shantou University published revised research-management policies on January 20, 2014. The revisions covered the university’s policies on research-fund management, the Young Research Fund, the Humanities Research Fund, academic innovation team development and national-fund cultivation, and startup research funding.

The revised drafts were reviewed and approved by the university Research Committee and Academic Committee. Their publication and implementation were decided at the first university leadership meeting of the year on January 7. University units were asked to follow the official policies.

The complete revisions and detailed provisions were provided in OA attachments and can be consulted through the OA system.
