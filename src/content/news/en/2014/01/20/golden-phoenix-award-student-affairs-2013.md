The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced the 2013 Golden Phoenix Award results on January 20, 2014. Su Bingdian was named the 2013 recipient in the university student-affairs system.

Established by the Student Affairs Office in 2012, the Golden Phoenix Award recognizes outstanding work, strengthens the student-affairs team, and supports improvements in student development. The selection followed the relevant award rules, individual applications, and review by an evaluation committee.

The office encouraged student-affairs staff to continue improving education and services and called on the wider team to maintain a student-centered approach.
