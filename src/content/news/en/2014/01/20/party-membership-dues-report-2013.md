The Organization and United Front Department of the Communist Party of China Committee of Shantou University reported the collection and use of party membership dues for 2013 on January 20, 2014. University party general branches and directly affiliated branches paid RMB 148,916.90 in dues during the year, including RMB 74,347 to the municipal authorities and RMB 74,569.90 retained by the university. With a prior-year balance of RMB 104,783.97, total spending was RMB 66,626.69 and the year-end balance was RMB 112,686.28.

## Use of dues

Spending included RMB 50,535 allocated to grassroots units from the year’s dues, RMB 14,891.69 transferred to the party care and mutual-assistance fund, and RMB 1,200 for subscriptions to *China Central Committee Bulletin*.

The care and mutual-assistance fund had an opening balance of RMB 57,272.97 and received RMB 14,891.69 in 2013. After RMB 3,600 was spent visiting veteran party members who joined before the founding of the People’s Republic of China ahead of July 1, the fund’s balance was RMB 68,564.66.

The report called on grassroots party organizations to strengthen dues collection and management and use returned funds within the prescribed scope.
