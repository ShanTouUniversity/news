It is reported that the first phase of the exchange program jointly established by the Law School of Shantou University and the Faculty of Law of McGill University in Canada will launch in May 2014. The program is funded by the STU/McGill Exchange Project.

## Phase One: McGill Delegation Comes to STU

The first phase is a two-week on-campus exchange (May 19–30) featuring a combination of separate and joint classes:

- McGill's Faculty of Law sends ten students to STU to study foundations of Chinese law, taught by STU professors;
- McGill dispatches two professors to teach Canadian law at STU, offering the course LAW-003 Contemporary Topics in Canadian Law to ten third- or fourth-year students selected from the STU Law School;
- Beyond the separate classes, joint classes and seminars will bring Chinese and Canadian students together.

Each course totals around 32 hours of lectures and discussions. The Canadian Law course is conducted entirely in English, and students who pass receive 2 credits. In addition, all participating students will visit local courts, law firms, and other legal institutions, meeting with judges, lawyers, and other legal specialists.

The second phase (2015) will send ten STU law students to McGill University for a similar two-week study program.

## Selection Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Third- and fourth-year undergraduate law students (cohorts of 2011 and 2010) |
| English Proficiency | IELTS 6.5 (no band below 6.0), TOEFL 90 (no section below 21), or CET6 score of 460 or above |
| Academic Record | Overall GPA within the top 30% of one's major |
| Other Qualities | Team spirit, active participation in public service, strong communication skills |
| Priority | Equal conditions aside, students who have not yet joined overseas exchange programs or competitions are given priority |

The Law School will organize a panel to review written application materials, followed by interviews for shortlisted candidates; interview times will be announced separately.

## How to Apply

The online application platform for STU's international exchange programs opens March 3–7, 2014. Applicants must complete the online form and submit their application materials — including proof of English proficiency, in both printed and electronic versions — to Ms Yao Jinjin at the Dean's Office of the Law School (Room 211, Wenbei Building). Emails should be headed "Apply Summer Law Programme," and all materials must be submitted before March 7, 2014.
