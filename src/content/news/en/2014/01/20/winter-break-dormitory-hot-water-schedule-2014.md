The Student Community Management and Service Center at Shantou University announced the winter-break dormitory hot-water schedule on January 20, 2014, to support students remaining on campus while conserving energy.

## Hours and locations

From January 24 to February 18, hot water would be available daily from 5 to 7 p.m. All floors of Buildings G and J would have hot water. In Buildings A through F, one designated shared washroom on each floor would remain open for hot-water use.

The Student Community Management and Service Center published the specific washroom locations for each building so students could find the nearest available facility.
