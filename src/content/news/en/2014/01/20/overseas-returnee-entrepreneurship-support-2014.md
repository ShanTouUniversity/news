Applications opened for the 2014 China Startup Support Program for Returning Overseas Scholars. On January 20, 2014, the Shantou University Research Office forwarded the relevant application notice from the Guangdong Education Department to faculty members and invited interested applicants to contact the office.

Interested faculty were asked to contact the Research Office by January 21 for application arrangements. The contacts were Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 86502813. Eligibility, required materials, and application procedures were set out in the forwarded notice and attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
