Shantou University published its Management Rules for Collaborative Research Projects on January 20, 2014. The rules were reviewed and approved by the university Research Committee and Academic Committee, and their publication and implementation were decided at the first university leadership meeting of the year on January 7.

The university said the rules aim to strengthen the administration of collaborative research projects and encourage faculty and researchers to serve local social and economic development. University units were asked to follow the official rules.

The complete rules and detailed provisions were provided in an OA attachment and can be consulted through the OA system.
