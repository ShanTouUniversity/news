The Faculty Lounge and 330 Multi-function Hall on the third floor of Shantou University’s Administration Center closed from 6 p.m. on January 17, 2014, for internal renovation.

The university planned to reopen the facilities to faculty and staff on March 1 with improved services. Questions about the renovation could be directed to the Resource Management Office at 86502443.
