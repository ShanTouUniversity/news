With the required approvals in place, the Shantou University Infrastructure Office will repair the road in front of Building 8. Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. was appointed as the contractor. The repair area extends from the east side of the fleet road to the road in front of the former mobile-campus shop.

Work was scheduled to begin on January 25 and finish by February 19. The project could generate noise, vibration, and dust. Pedestrians and vehicles were asked to exercise caution and avoid lingering near the construction site.
