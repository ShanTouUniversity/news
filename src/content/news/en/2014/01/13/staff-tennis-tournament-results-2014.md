Shantou University’s Labor Union announced the results of its 2014 staff tennis tournament. Qin Zhong won the men’s singles title, Wang Qiao won women’s singles, and Qin and Wang teamed up to take the doubles title. The tournament was held earlier in January.

| Event | First | Second | Third | Fourth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Men’s singles | Qin Zhong | Ma Weiping | Wang Yue | Wang Jiajun |
| Women’s singles | Wang Qiao | Yan Dongli | Chen Zhulin | Wu Di |
| Doubles | Qin Zhong and Wang Qiao | Wang Yue and Liao Xuequan | Zheng Muqiang and Chen Ming | Ma Weiping and Yan Dongli |
