Shantou University’s Business School scheduled its second academic lecture of 2014 for January 14, featuring Associate Professor Liu Renfan of Hangzhou Dianzi University. Liu was to discuss the motivations and effects of derivatives trading, presenting research on foreign-exchange and interest-rate derivatives, interest-rate swap choices, and pricing factors in foreign-currency loans.

Liu holds a doctorate in accounting and finance, supervises master’s students and researches derivatives accounting, financial management and corporate governance. Associate Professor Song Xiaobao of STU’s Business School was scheduled to chair the lecture, which was open to the university community.
