With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office planned a New Year’s Eve gathering for students remaining on campus during the winter break. The event was scheduled for 4:30 p.m. on January 30, 2014, on the first floor of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC).

Students could reserve a place through the university’s booking center using their campus-network accounts from 4 p.m. on January 13 through 4 p.m. on January 22. Those who reserved were expected to attend; students who were absent without a valid reason would be responsible for their individual event costs. The university planned to announce confirmed reservations and further arrangements separately.
