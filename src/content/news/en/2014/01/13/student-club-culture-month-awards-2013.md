Shantou University’s first Student Club Culture Month ran from November to December 2013 and concluded at the end of that month. Organized by the university’s Communist Youth League Committee and Student Clubs Union, the program aimed to showcase student-club culture, broaden participation and use competitions to expand students’ knowledge and overall abilities.

On January 13, 2014, the Youth League Committee announced the results of the various competitions held during the event. Detailed awards and recipient lists appear in the original attachment and are not reproduced here.
