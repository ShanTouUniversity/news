Shantou University planned its annual staff health examinations for March through May 2014, covering current and retired employees. Participants could choose the campus clinic, the university’s First Affiliated Hospital or its Second Affiliated Hospital. Auxiliary contract workers were assigned to the campus clinic.

Units were to register current employees’ choices, while the Retired Staff Affairs Office would register retired employees. Stamped registration forms were due to the campus clinic’s health-care office by January 20, 2014, with electronic copies also required. To facilitate medical-record preparation, participants were advised to choose carefully and avoid changes where possible. No individual health information is included here.
