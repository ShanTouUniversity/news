The Research Office of Shantou University launched the 2014 National Natural Science Foundation application process on January 17, 2014, and announced the internal schedule for review, revision, and submission.

## Internal timeline

| Date | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| From January 17 | Applicants begin using the 2014 application materials; General Programs use offline forms while other types use the online system |
| By February 25 | Schools complete formal review, organize expert review, and return comments |
| February 27 | Schools submit draft applications to the Research Office |
| February 27–March 8 | Research Office conducts formal review, checks application limits, and returns comments |
| March 8–12 | Applicants revise their materials |
| March 13 | Schools submit two final paper copies and required electronic materials |
| March 13–15 | Research Office conducts final review, seals documents, and uploads data |
| March 16 | The university submits applications to the National Natural Science Foundation of China |

## Application reminders

Applicants were reminded to review adjustments to grant categories, application limits, and limits on the total number of applications and active projects. Materials were to include required paper originals and electronic files, as well as supporting documents such as postdoctoral commitment letters, expert recommendation letters, supervisor approvals, and ethics review documents where applicable.

Except for General Programs, applications accepted in this round were generally to be completed online through the ISIS system. Project members were generally not to be changed after submission on February 27; any necessary change required a written report to the Research Office by March 4.

The contacts were Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 86502813. Applicants should consult the OA attachments for detailed guidelines and forms.
