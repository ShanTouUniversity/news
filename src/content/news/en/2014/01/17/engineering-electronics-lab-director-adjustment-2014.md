The director position of the Electronics Department laboratory at Shantou University’s School of Engineering has been adjusted. Following discussion and approval by the school affairs committee on January 15, the school appointed Lin Yiwen as laboratory director, and Chen Zheng no longer holds the position.

The adjustment was made in response to the school’s development needs, with the new director responsible for the transition of related work.
