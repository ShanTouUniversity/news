The Shantou University School of Law opened selection for overseas university exchange programs for the fall semester of the 2014–2015 academic year on January 17, 2014, giving interested students time to prepare their application materials during the winter break.

## Program overview

The school had signed cooperation agreements with four overseas universities, making exchange and overseas-semester programs available to eligible students. Interested applicants were encouraged to prepare in advance and choose a suitable program according to their circumstances.

The basic selection criteria, partner-university names, program arrangements, and application details were provided in the OA attachments. Applicants should follow the official materials issued by the School of Law.
