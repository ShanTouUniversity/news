The Resource Management Office of Shantou University announced adjusted canteen opening hours for the winter break on January 17, 2014. Several dining facilities would close temporarily for internal reorganization, while the Staff Dining Hall would remain open.

## Opening schedule

| Facility | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Staff Dining Hall, Second Floor of Dining Hall 4 | Open normally throughout the winter break for all faculty and students |
| Second Dining Hall | Closed January 29–February 6; reopens February 7 |
| Third Dining Hall | Closed January 19–February 7; reopens February 8 |
| First Floor of the Fourth Dining Hall | Closed January 19–February 19; reopens February 20 |
| Lanzhou Lamian counter | Closed January 22–February 18; reopens February 19 |

Faculty and students were asked to plan meals according to the reopening dates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
