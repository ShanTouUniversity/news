The Shantou University Communist Youth League Committee forwarded a call for essays on youth community-work theory from the provincial committee on January 17, 2014, inviting submissions from across the university.

## Focus of the call

The call was linked to the second round of the education campaign on the Party’s mass line. It aimed to study relevant speeches, summarize practical experience in the Communist Youth League’s youth community work, and deepen understanding of the principles governing youth community-work theory and practice.

Paper and electronic submissions were due to the university committee office by March 3. Detailed topics, formatting requirements, and submission instructions were provided in the forwarded OA document.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
