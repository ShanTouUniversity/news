The Continuing Education Center for Professional and Technical Personnel at Shantou University began issuing 2013 continuing-education course certificates on January 17, 2014. Office staff from university units were to collect the materials from Room 315 of the New Administration Center by January 20, while participating faculty members could collect their certificates through their unit offices.

## Follow-up registration

After collecting the certificate, faculty members were required to log in to the Guangdong continuing-education management system and record their hours, content, format, start and end dates, assessment results, and training provider. The center would review the information online and submit it to the human resources authorities for approval.

The registration guide, user instructions, and application reminders were provided in the OA attachments. The contact telephone number was 86502340, and the contact person was Chen Ying.
