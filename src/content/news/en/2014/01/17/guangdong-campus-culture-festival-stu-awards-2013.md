The eighth Guangdong College and Secondary School Students Campus Culture and Arts Festival, jointly organized by the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, the Guangdong Education Department, the Guangdong Culture Department, and the Guangdong Students Federation, has completed its project reviews and released the award results.

With the theme “Chinese Youth Dream, Striving Is the Most Beautiful,” this year’s festival featured 10 projects, including a new-media competition. STU students received awards in relevant projects, and the university Communist Youth League Committee announced the results.

The complete list of student recipients and projects was provided in an attachment and can be viewed through the OA system.
