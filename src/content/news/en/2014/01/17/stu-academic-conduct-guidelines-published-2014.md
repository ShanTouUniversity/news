Shantou University published its revised Academic Conduct Guidelines on January 17, 2014. The guidelines were reviewed and approved by the university Academic Standards Committee and Academic Committee, and their publication and implementation were decided at the first university leadership meeting of the year on January 7.

## Purpose

The university stated that the guidelines aim to regulate academic conduct, raise academic-ethics standards, and promote healthy, productive, and sustainable research development. University units were asked to implement the requirements of the official document.

The complete guidelines and detailed provisions were provided in an OA attachment and can be consulted through the OA system.
