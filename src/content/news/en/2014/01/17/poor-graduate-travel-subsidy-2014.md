It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on January 17, 2014, that travel subsidies for graduates from financially challenged families in the Class of 2014 have been disbursed to students’ Bank of China accounts.

Colleges (residential colleges) are asked to notify recipients to check their accounts. The list of recipients is available in the OA attachment and is withheld here to protect privacy.

> ⚠️ The recipient list is available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
