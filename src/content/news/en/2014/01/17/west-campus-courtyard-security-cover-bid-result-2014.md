The bid result for a security-cover installation project at the West Campus courtyard was published on January 17, 2014, following the recommendation of the Shantou University Tendering and Bidding Leading Group.

## Bid information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Security covers for the rooftop laundry area and exterior windows on the first and second floors of the West Campus courtyard |
| Winning bidder | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Bid amount | RMB 93,197 |
| Public notice period | January 17–20 |
| Procuring entity | STU Infrastructure Office |

Objections during the notice period could be submitted through the complaint channels published by the university Supervision and Audit Office. The procuring entity could be reached at 0754-86502490, and the complaint telephone number was 0754-86502346.
