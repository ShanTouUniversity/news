The STU Academic Exchange Center (ACC) announced its Spring Festival service schedule. The center will suspend operations from 8 p.m. on January 30, the eve of the Spring Festival, and resume normal service at 7 a.m. on February 7.

## Service schedule

| Time | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| From 8 p.m. on January 30 | Operations suspended |
| From 7 a.m. on February 7 | Normal service resumes |

Faculty, students, and university units were asked to arrange relevant business in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
