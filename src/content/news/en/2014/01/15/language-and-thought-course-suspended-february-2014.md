It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a course adjustment notice on January 15, 2014. A session of the School of Liberal Arts course *Language and Thought* will be suspended because the earlier start of the spring semester conflicts with the instructor’s teaching and research arrangements.

## Course arrangement

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Language and Thought* (Class 67444) |
| Suspended session | Week 1, Tuesday, periods 8–9 (February 25) |
| Classroom | E409 |
| Make-up session | To be announced |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students enrolled in the course should follow subsequent updates about the make-up session.
