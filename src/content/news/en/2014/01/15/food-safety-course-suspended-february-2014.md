It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a course adjustment notice on January 15, 2014. A session of the Department of Biology course *Food Safety* will be suspended because the earlier start of the spring semester conflicts with the instructor’s teaching and research arrangements.

## Course arrangement

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Food Safety* (Class 67716) |
| Suspended session | Week 1, Monday, periods AB (February 24) |
| Classroom | D306 |
| Make-up session | To be announced |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students enrolled in the course should follow subsequent updates about the make-up session.
