It is reported that the Network and Information Center reminded university units and office telephone users that the campus telephone system and its equipment are public university property. Responsible units and users should take care of the equipment and perform routine maintenance.

## Maintenance requirements

Telephones should be kept in a ventilated, dry, and clean environment away from direct sunlight, rain, liquids, and dust. A soft cloth should be used to clean the surface; corrosive cleaners and chemical solvents are not permitted. Users may not scratch, strike, dismantle, relocate, or take an installed telephone to another location without authorization.

Units and individuals should safeguard any long-distance or mobile-extension login passwords. Losses caused by loss, unauthorized dismantling, impact, liquid damage, misuse, or other improper operation will be borne by the responsible party. The center will conduct routine spot checks of telephone storage and maintenance.

For assistance, users may contact the center at stunic@stu.edu.cn or 0754-86503400-1, or visit t.stu.edu.cn.
