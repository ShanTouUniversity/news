It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a course adjustment notice on January 15, 2014. Two sessions of the School of Journalism course *News Writing* will be suspended because the earlier start of the spring semester conflicts with the instructor’s teaching and research arrangements.

## Course arrangement

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *News Writing* (Class 67601) |
| Suspended sessions | Week 1, Monday, period 12 (February 24); Week 2, Monday, period 12 (March 3) |
| Classroom | E405 |
| Make-up sessions | To be announced |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students enrolled in the course should follow subsequent updates about the make-up sessions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
