It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on January 15, 2014, that two sessions of the Department of Biology course *Food Nutrition* will be rescheduled due to a conflict between the earlier start of the spring semester and the instructor’s research schedule.

## Rescheduling

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | *Food Nutrition* (Class 67699) |
| Cancelled | Week 1, Wednesday, periods AB (February 26) and Week 1, Friday, periods AB (February 28), Room G401 |
| Make-up | Week 9, Wednesday, periods AB (April 23) and Week 9, Friday, periods AB (April 25), Room G401 |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students enrolled are asked to inform each other.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
