It is reported that the General Office of Shantou University forwarded to university units a notice from the General Office of the Communist Party of China Central Committee and the State Council on officials taking the lead in observing smoking bans in public places on January 15, 2014. Units were asked to implement the requirements of the document.

## Key focus

The forwarded document focuses on officials taking the lead in observing smoking restrictions in public places. University units were asked to organize relevant personnel to study the document and make arrangements for its implementation in accordance with their actual circumstances.
