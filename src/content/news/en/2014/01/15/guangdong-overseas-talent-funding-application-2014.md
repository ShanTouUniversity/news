It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University forwarded a notice from the Guangdong Administration of Foreign Experts on January 15, 2014, inviting eligible faculty members to apply for the 2014 funding program for high-level overseas-educated talent returning to China.

## Application arrangements

Applicants should prepare the application form for return-to-China work funding and proof of their overseas academic degrees and qualifications in accordance with the official requirements. Three paper copies of the application materials were due at Room 315 of the New Administration Center by January 20, while an electronic copy of the application form was to be sent to the designated email address.

The contact person was Chen Ying. The Personnel Office provided the telephone number 86502340 and email address wing@stu.edu.cn for inquiries. University units were asked to notify relevant faculty members and assist with the submission process.
