It is reported that Shantou University’s School of Science announced the results of its 2013–2014 undergraduate research program on January 15, 2014. Following review by the expert panel of the school’s extracurricular academic and science-technology activities working groups, 31 projects, including *Design and Difficulty Grading of Sudoku*, were approved.

## Follow-up arrangements

The school will track the progress of the approved projects and plans to conduct an interim review in May. Project progress will be considered when deciding whether additional funding should be provided and, if so, in what amount. Project leaders were asked to organize their teams and begin the research work promptly.

The complete project list and supporting materials were provided in an attachment and can be consulted through the OA system.
