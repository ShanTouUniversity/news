The STU Resource Management Office and Personnel Office are recruiting two administrators for the Student Community Management and Service Center. The positions will be hired as auxiliary contract workers, without transferring an employment relationship to the university, and compensation will follow applicable university standards.

## Requirements and duties

Applicants should have good professional ethics, teamwork skills, and physical health, be under 40, and be able to work 24-hour shifts. A junior-college qualification in property or hotel management, or an electrical qualification at intermediate technician level or above, would be preferred.

Duties include residence-area shifts and patrols, facility inspections and repair follow-up, greenery and cleaning checks, student consultation, visitor reception, and timely handling of emergencies in the residences.

## Application arrangements

Applications were open from January 25 through March 31. Applicants had to complete the recruitment form and email scans of their résumé, graduation certificate, family-planning certificate, professional-qualification certificate, and identity document to dorm@stu.edu.cn with “Student Community Center Administrator” in the subject line. Shortlisted applicants would take a written test and interview.

Applications could be sent to the Student Community Management and Service Center, Building C, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. The contact was Mr. Lin at 0754-86502661.
