Renovation work on the Fluid Mechanics Laboratory and Materials Mechanics Laboratory of the Civil Engineering Department at Shantou University’s School of Engineering began on January 14, 2014. During construction, additional fans, faucets, and related piping were proposed to meet the needs of the users.

## Proposed additional cost

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Estimated additional cost | Approximately RMB 3,000 |
| Cost share | Below the university’s 10% limit |
| Feedback deadline | 5:30 p.m. on January 24 |
| Receiving offices | Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office |

The university published the proposed cost increase under its infrastructure project tendering and bidding rules. Written objections could be submitted to the relevant offices before the deadline.
