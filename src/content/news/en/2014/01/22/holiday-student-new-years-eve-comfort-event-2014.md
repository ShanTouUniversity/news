After receiving positive responses to an earlier notice, the Shantou University Student Affairs Office announced arrangements on January 22, 2014, for a New Year’s Eve event for students remaining on campus during the winter break.

## Event information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 4:30 p.m. on January 30 |
| Venue | First floor of the STU Academic Exchange Center (ACC) |
| Entry | Arrive 10 minutes early and swipe a campus card |

Students with successful reservations were asked to sit in the assigned groups shown in the attachment and attend on time. Students unable to attend had to email the student-residence counselor office by January 26 to cancel; otherwise, the absent student would be responsible for the individual activity cost.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The successful reservation list and group seating attachment are available on the OA system.
