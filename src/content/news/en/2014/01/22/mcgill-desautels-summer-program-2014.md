It is reported that the Li Ka Shing Foundation–McGill University cooperation program will launch in 2014, with Shantou University Business School set to select 25 undergraduate students for a four-week summer program at the Desautels Faculty of Management, McGill University, in July.

The program will offer two courses — "Managing in North America" and "Global Branding" — complemented by guest lectures and cultural activities.

## Eligibility and Requirements

The program is open to Business School undergraduates from the classes of 2011, 2012, and 2013. Applicants should meet the following criteria:

| Category | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| English proficiency | IELTS 6.5 (no component below 6.0), or TOEFL 90 (no component below 21), or CET6 460+ |
| Academic performance | GPA of 3.0 or above, or ranking within top 30% of the cohort |
| Overall qualities | Outgoing personality, active in public affairs and volunteering, able to promote Shantou University |
| Priority | Under equal conditions, students with no prior outbound exchange experience are given priority |

## Fees

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Waived | Tuition, course materials, accommodation, and some meals |
| Self-funded | International airfare, personal living expenses, insurance and other costs — approximately RMB 16,000 |
| Documents | Passport and visa to be arranged by students themselves |

## Application Materials and Timeline

Applicants are required to submit: the STU outbound exchange application form, proof of English proficiency, an English transcript, and a 400-word English essay on the purpose of joining the program and overseas study plans.

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| March 3–7 | Online application platform for STU study-abroad programs opens |
| Before 12:00 noon, March 7 | Submit hard-copy and electronic materials to the International Program Office, Wenxi 211, and email electronic copies to outgoing@stu.edu.cn (subject: McGill Summer Program) |
| March 28 | Final selection results to be announced |

The Business School will form a selection panel to review written materials and conduct interviews to determine the final candidates. For inquiries, contact Ms. Ye Dongni at the Business School International Program Office, tel. 86503382, email outgoing@stu.edu.cn.
