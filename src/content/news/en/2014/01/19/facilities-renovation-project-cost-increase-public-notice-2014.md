Renovation work on the Resource Management Office’s new building and staff housing at the student dining hall began on November 20, 2013. During construction, the university proposed additional work in response to user needs and site conditions, including repairs to rooms on the ground and third floors of the office building and partitions, floor tiles, and wall tiles in the staff housing.

## Proposed additional costs

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Estimated additional cost | Approximately RMB 55,000 |
| Share of project cost | Below the university’s 10% limit |
| Feedback deadline | 5:30 p.m. on January 21 |
| Receiving offices | Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office |

The university published the proposed cost increase under its infrastructure project tendering and bidding rules. Written objections could be submitted to the relevant offices before the deadline.
