It is reported that with approval from relevant authorities, the infrastructure office of Shantou University will renovate the social science department offices on the second floor of Teaching Building G. The project has been commissioned to Shantou Longhua Construction General Company, starting on January 23 and scheduled for completion before February 27.

## Project Overview

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Scope | Social science offices, second floor of Teaching Building G |
| Main works | Full interior renovation: replacing solid wood doors, aluminum windows and stainless steel security grilles; wall painting; water and electrical wiring |
| Winning bid price | 174,000 yuan |
| Start date | January 23, 2014 |
| Completion deadline | Before February 27, 2014 |

According to the announcement, construction will generate some noise, vibration and dust, and the university asks faculty and students for their understanding of any inconvenience.

For safety reasons, passers-by are reminded to stay alert; without permission from the responsible administrative office and the contractor, teachers should not enter the construction site and should avoid lingering nearby.
