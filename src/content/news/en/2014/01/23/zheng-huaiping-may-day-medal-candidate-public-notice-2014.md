Following review by the Shantou University Trade Union Committee, Professor Zheng Huaiping of the School of Science was found to meet the basic criteria for a Guangdong May Day Labor Medal recipient. The university planned to recommend Zheng as a provincial candidate.

The university Trade Union invited feedback on the proposed recommendation. Objections were due before the end of the morning on January 27. Submissions from units required an official seal, while individual submissions had to include the writer’s real name. The contact telephone number was 696166 and the email address was o_xgh@stu.edu.cn.
