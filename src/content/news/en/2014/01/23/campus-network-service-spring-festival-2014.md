The Network and Information Center announced the campus-network service schedule for the Spring Festival period on January 23, 2014. From January 31 through February 6, duty staff would prioritize the operation of the campus backbone network and critical services.

During the holiday period, non-urgent faults and individual repairs requiring an on-site visit would be scheduled by the duty supervisor as circumstances allowed, without a guaranteed 24-hour response. Normal service procedures would resume on February 7. Users with urgent faults could call the duty number or submit a ticket through the campus-network self-service platform.

The service email was stunic@stu.edu.cn, the telephone number 0754-86503400-1, and the duty mobile number 13592895188 (campus extension 665188).
