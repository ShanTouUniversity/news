Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled a review meeting on employment work for the Class of 2013 for 3 p.m. on January 2, 2014, in Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center (ACC). Participants were to include Party secretaries or deputy secretaries and counselors from the colleges and residential colleges, as well as staff from the university’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
