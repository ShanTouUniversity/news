The School of Liberal Arts at Shantou University launched an exchange initiative with McGill University in Canada. Supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the program would select five Chinese-as-a-Second-Language undergraduates for a one-month internship in McGill’s Chinese department.

## Program schedule

Two or three students would participate from September 15 to October 10, 2014, while the remaining three or two students would participate from February 2 to February 27, 2015. The foundation scholarship would cover travel, accommodation, and meals; students would cover other expenses such as overseas insurance.

Applicants were to be 2011 or 2012 cohort undergraduates in the School of Liberal Arts Chinese-as-a-Second-Language program. They should have strong English skills, generally with an IELTS score of 6.5 or above and no component below 6.0, or a TOEFL score of 90 or above and no component below 21. Applicants should also rank in the top 30 percent of their class, demonstrate good academic performance and communication skills, and be able to promote STU internationally.

## Application and selection

The online international-exchange application platform would accept applications from March 3 to 7. Applicants had to submit proof of English proficiency and an English essay, complete the online application by noon on March 7, and submit electronic and paper copies of the application form, transcript, language score report, and essay to the School of Liberal Arts. Interviews were planned by March 14.
