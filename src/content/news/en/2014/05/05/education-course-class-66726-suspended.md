It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University has released a course adjustment announcement: for the Education course offered by the Higher Education Research Institute (class No. 66726), the session in period 12 on Wednesday of week 11 — May 7 — in classroom E208 will be suspended, and the time for the makeup class is yet to be determined.

Students enrolled in the course are advised to watch for further announcements regarding the makeup schedule.
