It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on May 5, 2014, to publicize the proposed list of students approved for major transfer.

In accordance with the *Shantou University Undergraduate Student Status Regulations* (STU Document [2013] No. 67), colleges have assessed applicants and proposed preliminary lists, which have been reviewed by the Academic Affairs Office.
