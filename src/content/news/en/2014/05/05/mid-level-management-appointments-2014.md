Shantou University’s Human Resources Office announced five appointments to mid-level management posts. Following deliberation at the **fifth university leadership meeting on April 15, 2014**, the university appointed Wang Wei, Liu Xiangling, Xie Xuhui, Fang Ying, and Jin Jiechun, each on a six-month probationary period.
