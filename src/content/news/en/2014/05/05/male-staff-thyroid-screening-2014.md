Shantou University’s campus clinic will provide free thyroid ultrasound screening to all male employees during regular working hours from **May 8 through May 16**. The daily capacity is 50 people in the morning and 30 in the afternoon; screening is offered on a first-come, first-served basis until places are filled.

For inquiries, contact the campus clinic at 693669 or 82902318. Screening for female employees had already been completed in March.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Property Management Office)
