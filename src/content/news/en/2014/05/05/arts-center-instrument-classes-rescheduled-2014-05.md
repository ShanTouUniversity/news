Shantou University’s Arts Center rescheduled its Trumpet (class 67044) and French Horn (class 67049) courses. Classes originally scheduled for **May 6** were canceled and moved to **May 10** in room STU204 at the Arts Center.

| Course | Canceled class | Make-up class |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Trumpet (67044) | May 6, periods 6–7 | May 10, periods 6–7 |
| French Horn (67049) | May 6, periods 8–9 | May 10, periods 8–9 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
