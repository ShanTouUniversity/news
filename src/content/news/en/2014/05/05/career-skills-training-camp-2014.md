Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold a career skills training camp for all enrolled students from **2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on May 10, 2014**, on the third floor of Building E.

The program will cover workplace stress management, professional certification exams, résumé writing, and interview consultations. Huatu instructors Yang Ting and Wu Jierong will join Zhu Guang, a career adviser with STU’s Student Affairs Office. The office is organizing the event with support from Huatu Hongyang’s Shantou branch and the university’s Career Planning and Development Association.

Students were asked to email a registration form to scdda2014@163.com or deliver it to collection points B304, C312, F512, G214, or J237 by **11 p.m. on May 8**. Organizers also planned an information and paper-registration booth at the Golden Triangle from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. on May 7.
