Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office opened its campus call for the **2014 Counselor Excellence Projects**. Frontline university counselors and related student-affairs staff were eligible to apply. The university would review submissions and recommend one candidate project to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education.

Projects could focus on counselor-led courses or day-to-day ideological and student education. They were expected to demonstrate distinctive, effective practice and a sustainable model with potential for wider use. Applications required a form, a project statement of no more than 3,000 Chinese characters, and optional supporting materials. Course proposals also had to include videos meeting the specified requirements.

Applicants were asked to deliver paper materials to the Student Education Management Section, room 106 of the Administration Center, and email a packaged electronic submission to xtchen@stu.edu.cn by **June 2, 2014**. The application form and video specifications were included in the original notice attachment.
