Dihai Blue Bay Marketing Center announced at **3 p.m. on May 5, 2014**, that registration had been suspended for the Shantou University employee property tour planned for May 11. All 70 places had been filled. The organizer thanked employees for their interest and said it might arrange another event at a later date.
