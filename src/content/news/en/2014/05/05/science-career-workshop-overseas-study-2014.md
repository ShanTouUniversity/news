The 13th Career Workshop hosted by Shantou University’s Science School will run from **7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on May 7, 2014**, in E Classroom 202. It will cover overseas study programs, considerations for studying abroad, and preparation for English tests such as IELTS and TOEFL.

Science School Dean Professor Liu Wenhua and Peter Xiao Liangrong, deputy director of the English Language Center, will introduce study options and answer students’ questions. Liu’s research includes aquatic toxicology, marine mammalogy, and environmental chemistry. Xiao has taught at STU since 1987 and studies pragmatics and foreign-language teaching.

Students wishing to attend were asked to register through the university booking system by 11 p.m. on May 6.
