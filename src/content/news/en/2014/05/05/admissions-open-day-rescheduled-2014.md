Shantou University’s Party and Administrative Office announced that the campus open day listed on the university calendar for **May 11** would not take place. The admissions consultation open day was rescheduled to **June 15, 2014**. The notice did not provide further details about the event schedule.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Administrative Office)
