Shantou University approved major transfers for 39 undergraduates in a decision dated May 14, 2014. The students had completed departmental assessments, and the proposed list had been publicized for a week without objection. The transfers were to take effect in the 2014–15 summer term.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
> ⚠️ The names and destination majors are in the original OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.
