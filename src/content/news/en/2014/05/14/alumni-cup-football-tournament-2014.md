Shantou University's first Alumni Cup football tournament will take place throughout **May 17** at the university football field. The event uses football to promote communication between alumni and their alma mater. Teams from Shenzhen, the Meizhou-Huizhou alumni alliance, Guangzhou and Shantou, together with the STU student football club, will participate.

The tournament is organized by Shantou University's Alumni Affairs Office and Physical Education Department and hosted by the STU Football Club. Faculty members, students and alumni are welcome to attend and take part in the alumni-community event.
