The Business School received 82 applications for its 2014 freshman mentor program. Interviews were scheduled for May 16, 19:00–22:00, in rooms G401, G402 and G403. Applicants invited to interview were asked to bring a printed application form and wait in room G404.

The original OA notice contains the individual interview schedule and applicant list.
