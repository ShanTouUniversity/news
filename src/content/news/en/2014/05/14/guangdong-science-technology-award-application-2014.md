Shantou University's Research Office has launched the internal application process for the 2014 Guangdong Science and Technology Awards in response to a recommendation notice from the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology. Projects must meet the relevant award regulations and applicable requirements for scientific-achievement registration, intellectual property, third-party evaluation or the required period of application. An individual may participate as a completing contributor on only one recommended project in the same year.

The award categories include natural sciences, technological inventions and applied technology. The first completing organization must be an independently incorporated legal entity registered in Guangdong. Requirements for representative publications, patents, application periods and sector approvals vary by category, and projects requiring approval must provide the relevant permits.

Online applications will open on **May 19 and close on June 19**. Applicants must submit materials to the university by **June 14** for a formal review, while the deadline for two sets of printed recommendation materials is **July 7**. Recommendation forms and attachments must be complete, accurate and consistent with the online version.

Faculty members interested in applying may contact Luo Yingguang at the Research Office's Science and Technology Achievement and Development Section: 86503271 or ygluo@stu.edu.cn. Detailed award requirements, forms and system instructions are provided in the original OA attachments.
