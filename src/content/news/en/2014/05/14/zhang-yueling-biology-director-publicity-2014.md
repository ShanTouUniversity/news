Following discussion at the seventh 2014 meeting of the university leadership on May 13, Shantou University has proposed appointing **Zhang Yueling** as director of the biology department in the School of Science. The Personnel Office published the pre-appointment notice under the university's appointment regulations and is inviting comments from university units and individuals.

Comments should be submitted in writing to the Personnel Office by **May 20**. Submissions from units must carry an official seal, while individual submissions must include the writer's real name. The office can be reached at 86503390 or rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
