The 19th Student Congress of Shantou University's Science College was scheduled for May 25 at 14:30 in the Science and Technology Lecture Hall. Delegates were to review the work of the 18th student union executive committee and elect the next committee, which would have nine members.

The college planned to have about 110 delegates elected by their classes. Nominations for executive candidates were due by 17:00 on May 20, and delegate lists and registration forms by 17:00 on May 22. Forms and further arrangements are in the OA attachments.
