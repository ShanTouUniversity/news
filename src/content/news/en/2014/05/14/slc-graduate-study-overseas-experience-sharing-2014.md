Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) will host an experience-sharing session at **3 p.m. on May 18** in the SLC lecture room. Prospective graduate students from the English and Chinese programs will discuss graduate entrance examinations, direct admission, overseas applications and cross-disciplinary applications. Wen Bing, a faculty member in the School of Chinese, will also share advice and experience.

The session is intended for students interested in graduate study in English or Chinese and aims to support more informed plans for further study.
