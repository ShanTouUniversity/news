The Firefly Student Aid Foundation at Shantou University has published the results of its 2014 Xuehai Yingguang student-aid review. The foundation received 19 applications, and its board proposed all 19 applicants as aid recipients. The proposed result is now open for public comment.

Comments may be submitted in writing or by telephone to the Firefly Student Aid Foundation by **5 p.m. on May 19**. The proposed recipient list and publicity materials are provided in the original OA attachment. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce applicants' names.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee, STU Youth Volunteers Association and Firefly Student Aid Foundation)
>
> ⚠️ The proposed recipient list and publicity attachments are available on the OA system.
