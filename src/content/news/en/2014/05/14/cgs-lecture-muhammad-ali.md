It is reported that the Center for Global Studies (CGS) at Shantou University's College of Liberal Arts will host a new session of its Fun Lecture Series on Friday, May 16, from 19:30 to 21:00 in classroom E409. The lecture, titled "The Amazing Cassius Clay," will be delivered by Kelly Nicholson.

The talk will focus on one of the most celebrated personalities of the twentieth century — Cassius Clay, better known by his chosen name of Muhammad Ali. An Olympic boxing gold medalist at Rome in 1960, Ali later reigned as heavyweight champion of the world. His face has been called "the most recognized on the planet," and some consider him the greatest heavyweight fighter in history.

Speaker Kelly Nicholson received his Master's degree in philosophy at the University of Washington in Seattle and completed his Ph.D. at the Claremont Graduate School in California. He has taught at the college and university level for 37 years and is the author of four books on ethics and religious philosophy. A former firefighter with the U.S. Forest Service during his undergraduate years and an amateur boxer, he has written about boxing for much of his life, including a recent two-part story on Rocky Marciano for the International Boxing Research Organization.
