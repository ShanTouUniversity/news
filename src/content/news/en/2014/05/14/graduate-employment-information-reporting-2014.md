Under arrangements from the Guangdong education authorities, members of Shantou University's 2014 graduating cohort must report their employment destinations through the Guangdong graduate employment information system (`gdc.stu.edu.cn`) by **May 28**. The information will be used to prepare the university's graduate placement plan and process employment registration certificates and archive transfers.

Graduates applying for deferred employment, further study or overseas study must follow the corresponding procedures in the relevant notices. Employment reporting and dispatch procedures affect graduate archives, household registration and related matters; operating instructions and requirements for different situations are provided in the original OA attachments.

Questions may be directed to the Graduate Employment Guidance Center at job@stu.edu.cn or 0754-86502252 and 0754-86502357.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ Employment-reporting instructions and materials for deferred employment, further study and overseas study are available on the OA system.
