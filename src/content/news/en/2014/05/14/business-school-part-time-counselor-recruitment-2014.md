The Business School opened recruitment for part-time counselors supporting its 2014 freshmen. Applicants were expected to be upper-year undergraduates with strong academic records, a sense of responsibility and relevant student leadership experience. The work includes supporting student development, academic culture, orientation and student organizations.

Applicants were asked to submit a written résumé to the Business School Youth League office by May 21. Interview details were to follow. Compensation would follow university standards.
