It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 14, 2014, on reservations for the Tech Clips Guangdong Innovation Day to be held in Guangzhou.

As of 14:30 on May 14, 535 faculty and students had registered since the call on May 13. In response to requests, the reservation system will remain open until the original deadline to give more people a chance to sign up.

After registration closes, 100 invited participants will be selected by lottery in the presence of members from the Student Union’s Rights Protection Department, with the final list to be published on the campus office information network.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
