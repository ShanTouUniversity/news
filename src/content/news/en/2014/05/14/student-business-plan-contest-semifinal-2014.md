Seven projects advanced to the semifinal of Shantou University's business plan contest, spanning areas such as smart projection, e-commerce, online sales of Chaoshan products and smart communities.

The semifinal was scheduled for May 21 at 16:00 in room E401 of teaching building E. Each team would give a presentation of up to five minutes followed by an eight-minute question-and-answer session covering topics such as market potential, promotion, operations, finance and technology.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office Student Entrepreneurship Center and Student Entrepreneurship Park Office)
