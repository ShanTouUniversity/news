Shantou University's eighth dinner session with university leaders and students was scheduled for May 20 at 18:00 in room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. Four to five university leaders, department officials and faculty representatives were expected to join the discussion. The event planned to invite 32 full-time students.

Interested students were asked to register through the university booking system by 12:00 on May 16. The invitee list was to be posted on the internal university information network, and the university would cover the meal costs.
