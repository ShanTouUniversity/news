Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) is recruiting **11 student liaisons for 2014–2015** across the School of Engineering, School of Science, School of Journalism, School of Art, School of Law, Business School and School of Chinese, generally one position for each listed department or program.

Openings are available in the engineering electronics and civil engineering programs; the science chemistry, mathematics and biology programs; the School of Journalism; the School of Art; the law and public administration programs in the School of Law; the Business School; and the School of Chinese. Applicants must be Shantou University undergraduates from the 2012 or 2013 cohort, with a strong sense of responsibility and good communication and coordination skills. Liaisons will support academic inquiries and study exchanges, help manage peer tutors and promote SLC services. The work period runs from the 2014 summer semester through the 2015 spring semester.

Applicants must submit the form through the Student Work-Study Application System (`qgjx.stu.edu.cn`) by **10 p.m. on May 22**. If more than five applications are received for a category, SLC will select candidates for interviews based on their applications; the interview shortlist is expected on May 23. SLC can be contacted at 86502250 or slc@stu.edu.cn.
