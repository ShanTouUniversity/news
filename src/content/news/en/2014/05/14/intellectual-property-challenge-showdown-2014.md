Shantou University was set to hold the “I Am the Champion” intellectual property quiz challenge for students and staff on May 25, 19:00–22:00, at the Science and Technology Lecture Hall. Contestants would compete one-on-one in a challenger-and-defender format. The final winner could enter a bonus round for a RMB 200 prize.

Registration forms were due by May 20, and registrants would receive a question bank after the deadline. The original OA notice contains the detailed rules and registration form.
