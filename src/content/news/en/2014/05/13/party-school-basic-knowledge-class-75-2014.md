The 75th Basic Party Knowledge Study Class of Shantou University’s Party School will begin on **May 18, 2014**. Participants must register with their group leaders from **May 15 to 16** and bring a one-inch, front-facing ID photo to collect study materials. Group leaders will meet at 1:00 p.m. on May 15 in Room 206 of the Wenbei Building.

Participants are expected to follow the course schedule. Those who fail to register without a valid reason will be considered to have withdrawn. Participants who need leave during the course must submit a leave form for approval through their Party general branch and the Party School office. Unexcused absences from classes or examinations will result in disqualification.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Office)

> ⚠️ The participant list, course schedule and group-leader contacts are available in the original notice on the OA system.
