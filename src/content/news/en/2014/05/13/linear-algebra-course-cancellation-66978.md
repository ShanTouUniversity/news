The Academic Affairs Office announced a schedule change for the mathematics course **Linear Algebra (Engineering)**. Section 66978 will be canceled during periods 8 and 9 in room D503 on **May 15**. A make-up class time will be announced separately.

Colleges and students enrolled in the course should watch for the follow-up make-up schedule.
