Shantou University's School of Law will begin its 2014 undergraduate thesis defenses at **8:30 a.m. on May 17**. The defenses will be divided into six panels meeting in rooms D201, D202, D203, D204, D206 and D207. A total of 125 undergraduates are scheduled to defend their theses.

The panels will conduct the defenses according to the grouping arrangements released by the School of Law. Detailed student groupings, panel members and thesis titles are provided in the original OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Law)
>
> ⚠️ Student groupings, thesis titles and the complete defense schedule are available on the OA system.
