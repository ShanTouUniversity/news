Shantou University's Three-Style Development Office issued its No. 263 study-conduct report on May 13, covering inspections conducted during week 11 of the 2013–2014 spring semester. The inspection team visited the E Teaching Building at **2 p.m. on May 8** and **8 a.m. on May 9**.

Both inspections recorded cases of students arriving late for class. On May 8, 11 students were recorded as arriving within five minutes of the start and another 11 as arriving more than five minutes late. On May 9, the corresponding figures were four and four. Relevant colleges and residential colleges were asked to use the findings in their study-conduct education work.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the student names in the original attachment. Relevant units may consult the complete materials through OA. Inquiries can be directed to the Student Education and Management Section.
