The Student Community Management Service Center arranged a temporary power outage in Residence Block A from **10 a.m. on May 13** to repair a fault in the block's electrical box. The outage was expected to last about one hour, and residents were advised to make preparations in advance.

The restoration time was subject to the progress of the repair work.
