Shantou University has announced the recipients of the 2013–2014 Li Ka Shing Foundation Outstanding Service Award. Following nominations by university units and review by the award committee, Zeng Jianping, Lin Yuejuan and Li Liping were selected for the award.

The award recognizes teaching-support and administrative staff members who have made notable contributions to the university's service and support work. The recipients were confirmed in an official university announcement.
