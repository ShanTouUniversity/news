Shantou University's Alumni Affairs Office will hold an appointment ceremony for **2014 class alumni liaisons** from **10:30 to 11:30 a.m. on May 20** at the staff recreation room on the third floor of the Administration Center. Representatives responsible for alumni affairs at the colleges and the 2014 class alumni liaisons will attend.

Participants will gather at the entrance of the Administration Center at 10:30 a.m. for a group photograph. The ceremony is part of the university's work to strengthen long-term communication among the university, its colleges and graduating cohorts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Alumni Affairs Office)
