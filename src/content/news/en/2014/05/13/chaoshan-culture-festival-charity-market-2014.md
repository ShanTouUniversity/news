Shantou University’s “Chaoshan with Affection” Culture Festival will take place at **Zhongshan Park in Shantou from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on May 31**. Organized through the university’s student-club project program by the University Youth League Committee, the Student Clubs Federation and Xingchao Club, the event aims to promote Chaoshan traditional culture and support children in need.

The program will invite outside organizations including a traditional gold-and-silk embroidery heritage center and the Chaoshan Folk Museum, together with STU’s Chaozhou Opera and Tea Ceremony clubs. A cultural corridor will introduce local history and traditions. Organizers will also recruit **10 student marketing teams** to sell Chaoshan-themed products, with part of the proceeds donated as educational assistance.

Teams could submit applications to `13crkuang2@stu.edu.cn` and `13xyyuan@stu.edu.cn` by **midnight on May 20**, or register in person beneath the No. 2 Dining Hall from noon to 1:30 p.m. on May 14.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee)
