Shantou University will host a lecture on **basic first aid at home** from **4:15 to 5:20 p.m. on Wednesday, May 21**, in Room 204 of the E Lecture Building. The Eastern Guangdong Volunteer Rescue Team will give the presentation, which is open to faculty, staff, students and parents.

Topics will include common emergencies such as fainting, shock, burns, nosebleeds and poisoning, introducing basic first-aid responses for household situations.
