Registration has opened for the Unicom Wo Pai 2014 Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao University Student Mobile Internet Innovation Design Competition, part of the eighth Guangdong College and University Students Science and Technology Academic Festival. The event is jointly organized by the Guangdong Youth League, the provincial education and science and technology authorities, the Guangdong Association for Science and Technology and the provincial students' federation. It is open to full-time graduate, undergraduate and associate-degree students with formal enrollment at universities in Guangdong, Hong Kong and Macao.

Students may form teams freely, including cross-university teams. Each team may have no more than six members and must select a team name and a captain. Teams should register through `wopai.iimedia.cn` and submit their work using the designated PowerPoint template by **5 p.m. on May 30**.

Detailed participation requirements and the submission template are subject to the original OA attachments and the competition website.
