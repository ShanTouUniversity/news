STU’s Student Affairs Office announced that **12 contestants** had reached the final of the 2014 Original Résumé Design Contest after judging. The final will take place from **7:30 to 10:00 p.m. on May 16** in the New Library Lecture Hall. Finalists must attend on time, and members of the university community are welcome to observe.

The contest aims to help students understand résumé content and design techniques while strengthening their overall skills. The finalists’ list is provided in the original attachment and is not reproduced here to protect student privacy.
