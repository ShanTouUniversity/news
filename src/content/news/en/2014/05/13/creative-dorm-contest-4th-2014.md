It is reported that the Student Affairs Office announced on May 13, 2014, the winners of the 4th Creative Dormitory Contest. Themed “Unique Dorm, Colorful Youth,” the contest aimed to enrich community life and foster creativity, hands-on skills and dormitory camaraderie.

Five teams were awarded:

| Award | Team |
| --- | --- |
| First prize | Family306 |
| Second prize | Handmade Home |
| Third prize | Dominant C105 |
| Best Creativity Award | Extra Large |
| Environmental Pioneer Award | Blue and White |
