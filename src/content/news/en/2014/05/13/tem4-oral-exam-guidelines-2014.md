## Exam Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | 2014 National TEM-4 Oral Test |
| Time | 08:30, May 17, 2014 |
| Venue | Language Lab, Building E |
| Required documents | Admission ticket, ID card and student ID card |
| Guidelines | *Guidelines for TEM-4 Oral Test Candidates* and *Exam Guide* (see OA attachments; candidates are asked to familiarize themselves in advance) |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |
