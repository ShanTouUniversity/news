The Shantou University Youth League Committee has announced a programme of activities marking the 95th anniversary of the May Fourth Movement and the 92nd anniversary of the founding of the Communist Youth League of China. Under the theme “Gather Youthful Positive Energy and Pursue New Dreams,” the programme runs from **April through June 2014** and includes themed group activities, sports, competitions, cadre training and residential-community programmes organized by the university, colleges and residential colleges.

The main arrangements are as follows:

| Activity | Schedule | Venue | Audience or organizer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Themed Youth League activities | April–June | Arranged by participating organizations | All Youth League members; University Youth League Committee |
| 2013 May Fourth commendation ceremony and commemorative symposium | May | Library Room 30A | Outstanding Youth League representatives; University Youth League Committee |
| Xuanli Cup competitions | April–May | To be confirmed | School of Science students; School of Science Youth League Committee |
| Fluorescent Run Night | May | Track and field stadium | Faculty and students; Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication Youth League Committee |
| “English, Science and the Chinese Dream” series | April | Medical Foundation Building rooms 7 and 8, Science Lecture Hall, Sangpushan and other venues | Medical School students; Medical School Youth League Committee |
| Fun sports meet | May | On campus | School of Chinese students; School of Chinese Youth League Committee |
| Electronics badminton competition | May | Gymnasium | School of Engineering electronics students; School of Engineering Youth League Committee |
| Fun carnival | May | On campus | Business School students; Business School Youth League Committee |
| “Youth in Color” show | May 17 | Auditorium | All students; Cheung Kong School of Art and Design Youth League Committee |
| “Morning Zhimei” series | May 17–18 | Track and field stadium | School of Law students; School of Law Youth League Committee |
| Fifth Youth League Cadre Training Camp | May 17–18 | Zhicheng College lecture room J-354 and other venues | Zhicheng College Youth League cadres; Zhicheng College Youth League Committee |
| Outdoor first-aid workshop | April 27 | On campus | Zhicheng College residents; Zhicheng College Youth League Committee |
| “One Team, One Dream” fifth Party and Youth League cadre camp | April 26–27 | On campus | Graduate School Party and Youth League cadres; Graduate School Youth League Committee |
| Second Graduate School Residential Culture Festival | May | Shantou University | Graduate School students; Graduate School Youth League Committee |

The activities include class documentaries, esports, graffiti, badminton, fun sports, night runs, creative clothing design, morning runs, team development, outdoor first aid and residential-community events. College Youth League organizations were asked to tailor activities to student needs and submit summaries to the University Youth League Committee by **May 25** for possible recommendation to the Guangdong Youth League Committee.
