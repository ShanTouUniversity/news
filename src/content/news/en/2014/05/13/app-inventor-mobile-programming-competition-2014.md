The Introduction to Mobile Programming course team at Shantou University’s College of Engineering will hold an App Inventor mobile-programming competition for main-campus students enrolled in the course during the spring semester of 2013–2014. The competition will draw on the course innovation reports: instructors will select no more than three outstanding works from each class.

Participation is voluntary. Students must complete the application form and submit it to the instructor’s email address listed in the virtual classroom. Registration closes at **11:55 p.m. on June 13**. The competition will award one first prize, consisting of a 10-inch Samsung tablet, and five second prizes, each consisting of a 7-inch Samsung tablet. Results and prizes will be announced and distributed by **the end of August**.

Entries must allow the course team to cite them as examples in classes and related papers. Prize winners may also be asked to help manage and support the mobile-programming website and assist with course activities.
