The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host a lecture titled **“Digital Transformation and Innovative Applications of Media”** from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, May 17**, in the University Library Lecture Hall. The speaker will be Chang Pao-fang, an associate professor at the College of Communication of National Chengchi University and a researcher at its Center for Innovation and Creativity.

Chang holds an Ed.D. from the University of Southern California and master’s degrees in industrial and management engineering and mass communication from the University of Iowa. She previously directed National Chengchi University’s master’s program in digital content and worked as a new-media consultant and technology adviser. The lecture is open to the university community.
