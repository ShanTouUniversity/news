Shantou University’s Student Community X Plan completed its “Exploring Shantou Villages” activity in April. Based on the teams’ project planning and presentations of their results, the Student Affairs Office awarded **one first prize, two second prizes, three third prizes and four excellence awards**.

The winning research topics covered Chaoshan traditional architecture, Chenghai Outing Village, embedded porcelain decoration, Chaozhou music, the Dahao Ancient Trail, traditional lantern-making, Chaoshan ancestral halls, village architecture, historic villages and dragon-boat culture. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce team leaders’ names, years, majors or residence areas.
