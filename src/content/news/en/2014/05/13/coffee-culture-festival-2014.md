Shantou University's first Coffee Culture Festival, part of the Student Community X Plan, will take place from **May 14 to 18**. The programme includes coffee-themed screenings, a lecture, a latte-art demonstration and a coffee DIY session, bringing cultural activities to the residential areas and the 789 Coffee Bookstore.

Coffee-themed videos or films will be shown from 8 to 10 p.m. each evening from May 14 to 18 in the activity rooms of residence blocks AB, CD and EF, according to the daily schedule. On the afternoon of May 18, the 789 Coffee Bookstore will host a lecture on “Coffee and Business, Coffee and Life,” followed by a latte-art demonstration. The coffee DIY session will run from 4 to 6 p.m.; the first 40 successfully registered participants will be able to make and taste freshly ground coffee.

Registration details and activity arrangements are subject to the original OA attachment and information released by the organizers. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend the public sessions.
