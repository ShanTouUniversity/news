The mathematics program in Shantou University's School of Science will hold its 2014 undergraduate thesis defenses from **2:30 to 6 p.m. on May 21**. Four panels will meet in rooms E303, E305, E306 and E307.

Ye Ruisong will chair the defense committee. Its members are Xu Fei, Yu Chengjie, Ye Ruisong, Li Pengtao, Tan Chaoqiang, Lin Xiaoping, Wei Caimin, Yang Zhongqiang, Li Junde, Lin Furong, Li Jian, Gu Minqiang and Christian Rau. Xu Fei, Ye Ruisong, Lin Xiaoping and Lin Furong will lead the four panels respectively, with the remaining members assigned according to the panel arrangements.

The thesis assessment standards and defense procedures are set out in the original OA attachments. This report does not reproduce the student groupings or thesis titles contained in those attachments.
