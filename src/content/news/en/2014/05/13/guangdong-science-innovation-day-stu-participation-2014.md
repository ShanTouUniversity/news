The opening ceremony and project showcase for **Guangdong Science Innovation Day** will be held on **May 22** at the Experimental Theater of the Guangzhou Opera House. Organized by the Li Ka Shing Foundation and the Guangdong provincial government under the guidance of the Development Research Center of the State Council, the event will present innovative projects selected worldwide and explore technology innovation and industrial applications.

The Li Ka Shing Foundation provided **100 places** for STU students and faculty. Applicants were asked to register through the university booking system at `booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm` by **noon on May 15**. The university arranged departure from campus at 1:00 p.m. on May 21 and return from Guangzhou at 2:00 p.m. on May 22; transportation, meals and accommodation were covered by the university. Participants were asked to dress formally.

Featured projects included Hampton Creek’s plant-based egg project, Medical Cancer Screening, Tipa biodegradable packaging, NanoSpun biological wastewater treatment, Nanoleaf energy-saving LED lighting and Traity’s big-data personality assessment. Inquiries: 86504749 or 884749.
