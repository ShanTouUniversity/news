The defense for the fifth service-learning project cycle at Shantou University Engineering College will take place from 3 to 5 p.m. on May 28, 2014, in Room D204. Project teams that submitted proposals will take part in the defense.

Each project must give a four-minute PowerPoint presentation followed by five minutes of questions. Participants are expected to dress appropriately. A project that fails to attend the review defense as required will be considered to have withdrawn. The detailed project schedule is provided in the Engineering College attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Engineering College)
> ⚠️ The project schedule and event attachment are available on the OA system.
