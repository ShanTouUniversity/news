The STU Affiliated Middle School scheduled registration for incoming Grade 7 students for 9:00–11:30 a.m. on June 16, 2014, at the school's first-floor Academic Affairs Office. Registration was for children of university employees. Families were asked to bring the relevant household-registration copies and the student's primary-school evaluation booklet. Children of employees at the university's main-campus medical school, excluding the affiliated hospital, also needed proof of their parents' employment issued by the school office.

Those unable to attend were asked to contact the Academic Affairs Office in advance. The school listed 86502051 as its inquiry number and said that failure to register by the deadline would be treated as forfeiture of the place.
