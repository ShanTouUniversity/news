The 185th Engineering Research Forum at Shantou University will take place from 10 a.m. to noon on May 28, 2014, in the Engineering College Academic Exchange Room (Gongnan Building 312). Zheng Ronghao, a PhD from City University of Hong Kong, will present “Ring-Coupled Vehicles: Theory and Applications.”

The forum is open to Engineering faculty members, graduate and undergraduate students, and other interested members of the university community.
