Shantou University's Physical Education Department planned to hold a swimming lifeguard training course in early June 2014 for currently enrolled students. The course had 30 places, and applicants were required to be able to swim 100 meters continuously. The application deadline was June 4.

Participants who passed the training could apply to work as lifeguards during the swimming pool's opening period. Applicants were asked to use the email address in the original notice; the required personal details and contact information should be checked in the original notice on the university's OA system.
