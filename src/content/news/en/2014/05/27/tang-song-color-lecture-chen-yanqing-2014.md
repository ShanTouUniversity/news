The Changjiang School of Art and Design scheduled a lecture for 7:30 p.m. on Wednesday, May 28, 2014. Dr. Chen Yanqing was to speak on “Concepts of Chinese Color: Prohibition, Excess, and Sentiment in Tang-Song Color,” at the school's lecture classroom. The event was open to the university community.

The lecture was associated with a Shantou University Humanities Fund project.
