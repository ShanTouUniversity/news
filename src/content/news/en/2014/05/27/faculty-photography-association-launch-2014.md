Shantou University's Faculty Photography Association scheduled its inaugural meeting and first event for 4:30 p.m. on May 30, 2014, in the lecture classroom of the Changjiang School of Art and Design. The event was for faculty who had registered with the association. It was to cover the association's formation, a tour of a professional photography studio and equipment, and an introduction to portrait photography techniques.
