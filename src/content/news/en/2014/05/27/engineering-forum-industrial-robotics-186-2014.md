The 186th Engineering Research Forum at Shantou University will take place from 10 a.m. to noon on May 29, 2014, in the Energy Research Institute Conference Room (Science and Technology Building 419). Chen Heping, director of the Robotics Laboratory at Texas State University, will present “Intelligent Industrial Robotics and Future Technology Trends.”

The forum is open to Engineering faculty members, graduate and undergraduate students, and other interested members of the university community.
