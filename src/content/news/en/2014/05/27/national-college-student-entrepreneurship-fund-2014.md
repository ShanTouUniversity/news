It is reported that the Student Entrepreneurship Center of the Student Affairs Office forwarded a notice on May 27, 2014, regarding the *Notice on Organizing Applications for the National College Student Entrepreneurship Fund* (Jiao Bi Zhi [2014] No. 29).

Interested students are asked to apply by June 30, 2014, via the National College Student Entrepreneurship Service Network at cy.ncss.org.cn and then contact the Student Entrepreneurship Center of the Student Affairs Office.
