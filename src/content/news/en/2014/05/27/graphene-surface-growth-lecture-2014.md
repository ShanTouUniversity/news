The Science College’s Science Salon series at Shantou University will hold an academic lecture at 3 p.m. on June 3, 2014, in the Science College Lecture Hall (Science Building 352). Professor Fu Lei, a “Chutian Distinguished Professor” at Wuhan University’s College of Chemistry and Molecular Sciences, will speak on “Surface-Controlled Growth of Graphene.”

The lecture will present research progress on surface-controlled graphene growth, structural regulation, and flexible electronic devices, as well as applications of two-dimensional nanomaterials in flexible electronics and energy. Fu serves on the Chinese Chemical Society’s Nanochemistry Committee and has received the Chinese Chemical Society Young Chemist Award.
