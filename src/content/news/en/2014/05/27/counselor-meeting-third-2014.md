Shantou University Student Affairs will hold its third counselor meeting of 2014 at 9 a.m. on May 29, 2014, in Library Seminar Room 30A. Professor Cheng Zhengjia of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University will provide training titled “College Student Psychological Conditions and Counseling Skills” during the meeting.

All counselors and relevant members of the student affairs system are expected to arrange their work and attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs)
