The Guangdong Federation of Trade Unions and the Guangdong Department of Education planned to hold the second Guangdong Competition for Young University Teachers from June 28 to 30, 2014. Shantou University could nominate one faculty member. Eligible participants had to be full-time, in-post university teachers born after August 31, 1974. The competition covered humanities and social sciences, basic natural sciences, and applied natural sciences.

Eligible faculty were asked to apply through their teaching units by the morning of June 6. Units were to submit their nominees and application forms to the Academic Affairs Office by the end of that day, after which the university would select its representative. Recommendations for competition judges were due by the afternoon of June 3. Competition procedures, judging details, and awards are in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union and Academic Affairs Office)
