Shantou University Business School will hold its 11th academic lecture of 2014 from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on May 29, 2014, in the Business School Academic Seminar Room on the second floor. The event is open to the university community.

The program includes three presentations: a comparison of mainland Chinese land expropriation and Taiwan’s land readjustment models; the process influencing knowledge-sharing behavior with individual learning outcomes as a mediator; and research on international trade in services models.
