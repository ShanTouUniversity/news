Following its fifth and sixth standing committee meetings of 2014, Shantou University's Party Committee decided to adjust several grassroots party organizations.

The university established a general Party branch for research affairs and dissolved the direct branch under the Research Affairs Office. Party members from the Research Affairs Office, Network and Information Center, Central Laboratory, Light Industry Equipment Research Institute, university press, journal editorial office, Institute of Higher Education, *Chinese Literature*, and Office of Development and Planning were assigned to the new branch. Gao Jian was appointed interim head to oversee its establishment and preparations for its first members' assembly. Party members at the affiliated middle and primary schools were reassigned to the general branch under the Resource Management Office; members in the student residential area office moved to the Student Affairs branch under the general branch for administrative offices; and party members with deferred employment status were placed under the Organization and United Front Work Department.
