Shantou University’s Global Studies Center of the College of Liberal Arts will host a public lecture from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on May 29, 2014, in Room E409. Dr. Sun Jinfeng, who received a PhD in philosophy from The University of Hong Kong, will speak in Chinese on “Who Owes Poor Countries What? What Is Global Justice?”

The talk will approach global justice from two perspectives. The first concerns relations among countries and the conditions of a legitimate global order. The second examines distributive justice and the fairness of the international economic system. Faculty members and students are welcome.
