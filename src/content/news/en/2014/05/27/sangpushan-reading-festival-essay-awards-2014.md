The judging for Shantou University's 11th Sangpushan Reading Festival essay contest concluded in May 2014. The contest received 41 entries: 11 fiction, two drama, nine prose, eight modern poetry, four classical Chinese writing, three criticism, and four English creative-writing entries.

Prizes were awarded across the categories. To protect student privacy, winners are identified by surname and given-name initials: first prize, Wang YF; second prize, Guo CH and Shi XL; third prize, Zhang LX, Liu M, and Fan YL. The results were announced by the Shantou University Youth League Committee, university library, and student associations federation.
