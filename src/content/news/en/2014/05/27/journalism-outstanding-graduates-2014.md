It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication issued a notice on May 27, 2014, to launch the selection for Outstanding Graduates of the Class of 2014, aimed at encouraging all-round development and fostering a positive academic climate.

The program, started in 2013, is open to all students of the Class of 2010 in the School of Journalism. Eligible students should submit the application form, named as “Name + Outstanding Graduate Application,” to the designated email address by June 5, 2014. Those who do not submit on time will be deemed to have waived the opportunity.

## Eligibility

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| General requirements | Observe laws and discipline, love the university and respect teachers, be united and cooperative; achieve all-round development with high overall quality and excellent graduation thesis; actively participate in social practice, public welfare and volunteer activities and serve the school |
| Scholarship requirements | Academic Excellence Scholarship: second prize or above for two years (including second prize); at least one university Special Scholarship |

## Selection and Awards

The School will organize a selection committee to review all applicants and announce the winners. Those selected as Outstanding Graduates will be awarded an honorary title, a certificate and a prize of 800 yuan by the School.

The application form is available in the OA attachment.
