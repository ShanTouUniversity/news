Shantou University's Infrastructure Office scheduled repairs to stairwell B12 in a staff dormitory to begin on June 3, 2014, with completion planned by July 2. Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Co., Ltd. was contracted for the work at a winning bid of RMB 13,900.

The work included painting the stairwell walls and ceiling, installing aluminum alloy windows, and replacing light fixtures. Noise, vibration, and dust were expected during construction. The university advised passersby to take care around the site and asked resident staff to avoid lingering in the work area. The original notice listed the project lead and site administrator.
