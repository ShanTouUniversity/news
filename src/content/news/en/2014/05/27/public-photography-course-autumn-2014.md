Shantou University's Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication planned a community photography course for the 2014–15 summer term. Through research, photography, writing, and project planning, participants would explore village cultural landscapes and their preservation.

The course was scheduled to run from June 28 to July 8. Theory sessions introducing service learning and community photography were set for June 28 to July 3. Fieldwork on July 4 and 5 would focus on the historic buildings and living environment of Pinghu New Village in Jingdu Town, Chaonan District. On July 6 and 7, participants would visit the ancient town of Dahao to explore its history, followed by a group presentation on July 8. Reservations were open until 7 p.m. on June 3; the application form and reservation details are available in the original OA notice.
