It is reported that the Library issued its 10th special collection recommendation for 2014 on May 27, featuring Teochew Benevolent Halls (Shantang).

Originating in the Song Dynasty under the eminent monk Master Dafeng, Teochew benevolent halls have a history of nearly 1,000 years, flourishing in the late Qing and early Republic, resuming rapid development after Reform and Opening Up, and spreading to Southeast Asia, Hong Kong and Taiwan with the Teochew diaspora. Revering Master Dafeng as their spiritual figure, these halls carry out charitable work such as poverty relief, medical aid, burial assistance and disaster relief, blending Buddhist, Taoist and Confucian culture into a distinctive folk charity institution.
