It is reported that the Business School issued a notice on May 27, 2014, that the 10th Academic Lecture of 2014 will be held on May 29.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 08:30–12:00, May 29, 2014 |
| Venue | Academic Seminar Room, 2nd Floor, Business School |
| Organizer | Business School |

## Topics and Speakers

| Speaker | Title |
| --- | --- |
| Chen Errui | Quantitative Study on Risk of ACCUMULATOR |
| Hu Shaodong | Institutions and Economic Development of Chaoshan |
| Xie Shunlong | New Development of Chaoshan Chamber of Commerce Organizations |
| Wang Ran | Rent-Seeking, Credit Constraints and Corporate R&D Intensity — Evidence from the World Bank |

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
