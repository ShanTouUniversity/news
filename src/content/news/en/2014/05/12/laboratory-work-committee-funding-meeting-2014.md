Shantou University’s Laboratory Work Committee will meet at **8:45 a.m. on Thursday, May 15, 2014**, in Room 201 of the New Administration Center. The meeting will discuss allocation of funds for the 2014 special program on supporting and improving undergraduate experimental teaching.

The meeting will be chaired by committee director Wen Xiaobo. Deputy directors Du Hong, Chen Yaowen, Qiu Jingfeng and Huang Jingxia, together with other committee members, are expected to attend. Meeting materials will be circulated electronically in advance.
