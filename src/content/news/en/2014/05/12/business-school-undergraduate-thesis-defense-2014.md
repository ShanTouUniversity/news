Shantou University’s Business School announced arrangements for its 2014 undergraduate thesis defenses. The defenses are scheduled for **May 21, 2014, from 2:00 to 6:00 p.m.** The committee will be chaired by Lü Yuan and will include Business School faculty members.

Students will present their thesis arguments, purposes and structures before answering questions from faculty panels. The panels will then use the undergraduate thesis assessment form to evaluate presentations, calculate results and complete committee comments. Outstanding theses may account for no more than 15% of the students in a panel. Cases involving an unsuccessful defense or non-compliant thesis format will be handled under university regulations.
