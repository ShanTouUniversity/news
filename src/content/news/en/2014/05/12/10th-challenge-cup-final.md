It is reported that the final of Shantou University's 10th "Challenge Cup" extracurricular academic and scientific works competition will be held at 2:30 p.m. on May 14 (Wednesday) at the library. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

## Entries and Shortlist

Since the university issued the competition notice on April 14, a total of 38 works were submitted by April 30. After preliminary review, 28 works advanced to the final defense.

| Category | Submitted | Advanced to Final |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural science academic papers | 8 | 8 |
| Philosophy and social science survey reports and papers | 15 | 10 |
| Technological inventions and productions | 15 | 10 |

## Final Arrangements

The opening ceremony will be held in the library lecture hall. Defenses for technological inventions will take place in the lecture hall, philosophy and social science works in Room 30A, and natural science papers in Room 30B.

During the defense, each contestant will present their work via slides for 6 minutes, followed by 4 minutes answering questions from judges. After all defenses, judges will give comments and the results will be announced.

Organizers require contestants to dress formally, arrive 15 minutes early for sign-in, and — for those in the invention category — bring their works to the venue.

The competition aims to cultivate students' innovative and entrepreneurial abilities and to prepare for the 13th Guangdong "Challenge Cup" competition.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
