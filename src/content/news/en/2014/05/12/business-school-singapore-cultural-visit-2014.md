Shantou University Business School opened recruitment for a 2014 Singapore cultural visit scheduled for **June 28 to July 7**. The self-funded program is intended to expose students to Singapore’s public, private and community sectors while helping them develop an international perspective.

Eligible applicants include STU full-time undergraduates, graduate students, MBA participants and course-program students, with the specific cohorts set out in the Business School notice. Applicants should have good spoken and written English skills and experience in public-service or volunteer activities. The school will review written materials first and invite shortlisted applicants to one-on-one interviews with trip leader Dennis Lee.

Applicants were asked to submit proof of English proficiency and an English résumé by **5:00 p.m. on May 14** via `outgoing@stu.edu.cn`. The detailed itinerary and budget were provided in an attachment.
