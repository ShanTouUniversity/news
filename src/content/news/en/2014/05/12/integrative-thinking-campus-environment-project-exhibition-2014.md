Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will hold the “Campus Environment Improvement: Our Ideas” exhibition from **May 12 to 25**. The exhibition will feature outstanding projects from the campus-environment improvement assignment in the creative-thinking component of the Integrative Thinking course.

The 15 projects address campus mapping and information services, multilingual exchange, broadcasting, campus beautification, tree labels, reservoir improvement, renovation of the university motto monument, bicycle assistance and dormitory lighting. Featured ideas include “STU Geographic Assistant,” “STU Map App: Click Click Click,” “Add Labels to Trees” and “Renovating the University Motto Monument.”
