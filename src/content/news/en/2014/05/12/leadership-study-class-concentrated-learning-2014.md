Shantou University’s study class for department-level leaders on studying General Secretary Xi Jinping’s important speeches will hold concentrated sessions from **May 14 to 16, 2014**. The Party Organization and United Front Office asked participants to arrange their self-study according to the program and protect the required study time.

Participants should arrive by **2:50 p.m. each day** for the concentrated sessions. The notice was circulated to the Party committee of the Medical College, Party general branches on the main campus and directly affiliated branches for distribution to enrolled participants.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Office)
