Shantou University’s International Business Case Club (IBCC) in the Business School planned a session on domestic and international business competitions for May 17, 2014, from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. in the Library Lecture Hall. Open to the university community, the event was to feature teams with competition experience sharing preparation strategies, contest experiences and award-winning approaches, with guidance from Business School professors.

The session also aimed to introduce international case competitions to students across campus and encourage Business School students to try out for the school’s case team.
