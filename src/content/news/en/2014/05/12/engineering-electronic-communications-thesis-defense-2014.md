Shantou University’s School of Engineering scheduled thesis defenses for its 2010 undergraduate cohort in Electronic Information Engineering and Communications Engineering for May 19, 2014. Four groups were assigned morning periods 3–4 and afternoon periods 6–9, in G404, E-lecture Hall 101, G303, E-lecture Hall 102, E308, D504, E402 and E405.

The defense process included a review of each project followed by a student presentation and questions from faculty. Grades were to reflect the project review, defense, supervisor recommendations and peer review. Students were asked to revise their theses after the defense and return them to the department by May 23; they were to remain until final grades were confirmed.

Group assignments and assessment criteria are in the OA attachment. Student names and individual assignments are not reproduced to protect privacy.
