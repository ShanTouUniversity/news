Shantou University’s School of Engineering scheduled thesis defenses for its 2010 cohort in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation for May 18 and 21, 2014. Groups 1–3 were scheduled for 8:30 a.m.–noon and 2:30–5:30 p.m. on May 21, in E-lecture Hall 202, the department meeting room in the Mechanical Engineering Building and E-lecture Hall 203, respectively. Group 4 was scheduled for the same time slots on May 18 in the Energy Institute meeting room.

The process included a review of each thesis, a student presentation and questions from faculty. Students were asked to bring a completed thesis defense record form and revise their work after the defense; they were to remain until their final results were confirmed. Any outstanding-defense arrangements were to be determined later.

Group assignments and assessment criteria are in the OA attachment. Student names and individual assignments are not reproduced to protect privacy.
