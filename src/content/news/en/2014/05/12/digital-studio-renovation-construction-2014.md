Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office announced a renovation project for the digital multifunction studio of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication. Guangdong Jianhua Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. was appointed as the contractor. The work will involve removing and rebuilding the existing studio on the fourth floor of the Electrical and Educational Building, including professional lighting, sound, architectural acoustics, and fire alarm and suppression systems. The renovation area is about **360 square meters**.

The project has a contracted value of **RMB 2.0796 million**. Construction is scheduled to start on **May 14, 2014**, and is targeted for completion by **July 12**. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during the work. The university advised faculty, staff and students to take care around the site and avoid lingering near the construction area.
