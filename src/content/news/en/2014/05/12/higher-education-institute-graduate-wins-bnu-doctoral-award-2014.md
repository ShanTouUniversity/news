Mao JD, a 2014 master’s graduate of Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education, was admitted to the Faculty of Education at Beijing Normal University and received its 2014 Doctoral Freshman Grand Scholarship. The award was established in 2014 to attract doctoral students with strong academic records and potential, with a maximum value of RMB 80,000 per student. Mao JD was the faculty’s sole recipient of the grand award that year.

The student’s name is abbreviated to protect privacy.
