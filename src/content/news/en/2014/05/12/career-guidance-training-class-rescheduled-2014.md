Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced a schedule change for class 68060 of Career Guidance and Training. The class originally scheduled for **May 12, 2014 (Monday of Week 12), periods 8 and 9** was cancelled and rescheduled for **May 19 (Monday of Week 13), periods 8 and 9**, in **Room G302**.
