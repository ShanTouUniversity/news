After registration, preliminary review, showcases and defense sessions, Shantou University’s fourth student-club incubation initiative proposed the establishment of six clubs: a martial-arts club, Qingling Running Club, Beatbox Club, Dreamers Graphic Design Club, Board Games Club and Creative Public Welfare Association (revived club). The proposals were reviewed and approved by the University Youth League Committee.

Organizations and individuals could submit comments by **3:00 p.m. on May 15** in writing, in person or by telephone. Organizations were asked to stamp written submissions, while individuals were asked to sign their real names. The contact number was 86502302.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee)
