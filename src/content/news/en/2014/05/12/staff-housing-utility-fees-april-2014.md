Shantou University’s Resource Management Office published utility charges for staff housing for **April 2014**. Meters were read on May 1 and 2, following the previous reading on April 1. Staff members can check their charges through the university utility-management system at `power.stu.edu.cn` using their campus-network accounts.

Questions or objections may be checked with the Water and Electricity Center from **May 12 to 14** by calling 2489. If no objection is raised by the end of the period, the Finance Office will deduct the charges from the relevant staff members’ May salary accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
