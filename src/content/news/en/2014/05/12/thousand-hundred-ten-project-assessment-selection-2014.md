Shantou University’s Personnel Office announced arrangements for assessing the sixth and seventh cohorts of the Guangdong higher-education “Thousand-Hundred-Ten” talent-development program and selecting nominees for the eighth cohort. The sixth cohort will undergo an end-of-term assessment, while the seventh cohort will undergo a mid-term assessment. The process combines individual summaries, college-level review, university review and expert assessment organized by the provincial education authority.

Based on the sixth-cohort assessment, the provincial authority plans to recognize five advanced teams and 20 advanced individuals. Units may recommend candidates, and program participants may also submit their own applications. Selection and recommendation for the eighth cohort will follow the provincial requirements.

Units were asked to consolidate and review materials and submit them to the Personnel Office at Room 315 of the New Administration Building by **May 30**. Electronic copies should also be sent to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`.
