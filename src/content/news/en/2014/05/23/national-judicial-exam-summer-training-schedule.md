Shantou University's Law School announced an adjustment to its training schedule for the National Judicial Examination. Training would be suspended on June 2, 2014, and intensive summer training would begin on June 28 at the existing venue. The adjustment was intended to accommodate students' final examinations and summer preparation plans.
