The first Business School Alumni Forum at Shantou University was scheduled for 4:00–5:30 p.m. on May 24, 2014, in Room E203. The event would focus on how university students can become professional managers more quickly.

The speakers were Hu Junbo, Li Zhongheng and Zeng Li, all alumni of the 2000 e-commerce class. Hu founded Dongfang Hexin Investment Management Co. and serves as its director. Li founded Yaonian Innovative Brand Design Consulting Co. and serves as its general manager. Zeng is a senior manager at Vipshop's Technology Center.
