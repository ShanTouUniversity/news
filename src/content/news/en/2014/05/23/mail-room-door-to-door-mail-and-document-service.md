It is reported that the Administration Office of Shantou University recently announced that the campus mail room will begin providing door-to-door collection and delivery of postal items, along with internal approval and document exchange services, for university units starting May 28, 2014 — a move intended to streamline everyday document handling across campus.

## One Daily Visit on Working Days

Under the new arrangement, the mail room will make door-to-door visits once a day from Monday to Friday. The service excludes couriers other than China Post as well as bulky or large-volume items.

During summer and winter vacations, each unit will need to provide the mail room with its collection times, locations, contacts, and phone numbers so that the service can continue without interruption.

## Clear Procedures for Handover and Payment

According to the notice, all mailed items and documents must be handed over and signed for by each unit's designated contact person. When sending items, units are required to fill in their own envelopes or postal forms and complete a mailing list.

For payment settlement, the mail room will provide each unit's contact with the postage receipts from the previous working day on the second working day following dispatch, and settle the charges accordingly.

## Units Must Submit Details Before Launch

To ensure a smooth launch, each unit must provide its delivery location, contact person, and phone number to the mail room by May 26. The mail room can be reached at 82902805.
