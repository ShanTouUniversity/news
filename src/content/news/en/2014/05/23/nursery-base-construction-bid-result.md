Shantou University announced the evaluation result for the construction contractor of its new nursery base project on May 23, 2014. Following the recommendation of the university's bidding committee, Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. was selected as the winning bidder, with a bid value of RMB 77,915.

The public notice ran from May 23 to May 26. Questions about the project could be directed to the STU Infrastructure Office at 0754-86502490. Complaints could be submitted to the STU Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346.
