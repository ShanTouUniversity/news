The School of Science at Shantou University scheduled a Science Salon seminar for 2:30 p.m. on May 24, 2014, in Conference Room 504 of the Science Academy. Shen Songdong, a professor at Soochow University, was to present research on the taxonomy of the genus Porphyra.

Shen holds a PhD and serves as an executive council member and deputy secretary-general of the Chinese Phycological Society. His research focuses on algal cell development and environmental responses. He has led four National Natural Science Foundation of China projects and more than 10 other projects, published 17 SCI papers, authored a book, received five patents and earned five awards.
