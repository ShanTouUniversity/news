Following Shantou's plan for its first Excellent Talent Awards, Shantou University's Party Organization and United Front Work Office opened internal nominations on May 23, 2014. The awards covered five categories: Outstanding Talent, Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Young Talent, Skills Excellence and Talent Service. The awards did not use ranking levels.

University units could organize eligible individuals or teams to apply. Application materials were due at Room 315 of the Administration Center by May 30, with electronic copies sent to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. Inquiries could be directed to Ji Rongxin at 86502345.

The original OA notice contains the award notice, application requirements, application form and related templates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Work Office)
