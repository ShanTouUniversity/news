Zhicheng College will run a summer rural service-learning course in mid-July 2014 in Xilong Village, Tiepu, Chaozhou, and plans to recruit 40 students from across the university. The project is part of the university’s public-service curriculum; students who complete the coursework and assessment can receive one academic credit. Nearly 10 students from Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and the Hong Kong Institute of Education are also expected to participate in the exchange.

## Service Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Seven days in mid-July, scheduled after the military training of Year 13 students |
| Location | Xilong Village, Tiepu, Chaozhou |
| Places | 40, selected through interviews |
| Activities | A summer camp for local primary and secondary students, social research and a community gala |
| Deadline | 17:00, May 30 |
| Email | ATP@stu.edu.cn |
| Interview | Planned for the afternoon of June 4 |

Applicants should follow public-service course rules and demonstrate cooperation, communication and a commitment to service. The application form is available on the OA system.
