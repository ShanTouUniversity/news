Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold *IELTS Preparation Workshop: Speaking Skills Practice* from 16:30 to 18:30 on May 28 at CILL. The workshop will be led by John Harper.

The session will provide practice tasks for the IELTS speaking exam, and places are limited. Participants must reserve a seat through Eventbrite and bring their reservation ticket when attending. Those uncertain about attendance are asked not to register, and registrants should attend as scheduled.

Reservation link: <https://www.eventbrite.com/e/ielts-prep-workshop-speaking-skills-practice-tickets-11719741061>
