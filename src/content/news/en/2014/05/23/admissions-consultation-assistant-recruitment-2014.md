Shantou University Admissions Office plans to recruit about 15 undergraduate and postgraduate students as admissions assistants for entrance-examination publicity and consultation. The work period is June 16 to July 4, with shifts from 08:30–12:00, 12:00–17:00 and 17:00–21:00. The workplace is the Admissions Office, and compensation follows the university’s hourly standard for work-study positions.

## Duties

Assistants will take turns answering admissions hotlines, respond to questions from prospective students, parents and secondary-school teachers, introduce the university through Weibo, WeChat, QQ groups and online forums, and assist with in-person, online and visitor consultations.

| Interview | 15:00–16:30, May 28, Room 306, Teaching Building E; wait in Room 307 |
