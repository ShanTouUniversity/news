Shantou University's Network and Information Center announced that April 2014 office phone bills for China Telecom fixed lines and China Mobile fixed lines had been imported into the phone management system. Designated staff were asked to log in at `http://lrmo.stu.edu.cn` and review the two types of bills.

Bills without discrepancies were to be confirmed in the system. Questions about China Telecom bills could be emailed to `o_dhs@stu.edu.cn`, while questions about China Mobile bills could be sent to `stunic@stu.edu.cn`, by May 28. Bills that were neither disputed nor confirmed by the deadline would be treated as verified. The Finance Office planned to process payment on May 30 using each unit's designated funding number.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
