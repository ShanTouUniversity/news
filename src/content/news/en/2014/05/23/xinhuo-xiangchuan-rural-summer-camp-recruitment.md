The Firefly volunteer team of the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association planned to hold the 2014 Xinhuo Xiangchuan Rural Primary School Summer Camp in July and opened 50 places to students across the university. The program counted as a university community-service course, and participants who completed the requirements could earn one service-learning credit.

The camp had two service sites. The Baiyun site at Baiyun Primary School in Dengtan Town, Chao'an District, Chaozhou, planned to recruit 20 volunteers for an initial site visit in early July, training on July 9–10, service activities on July 11–15 and a closing session on July 16. Twenty students from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University were also scheduled to join this site. The Lintian site at Lintian Primary School in Baita Town, Jieyang County, planned to recruit 30 volunteers for training on July 14–15, service activities on July 17–21 and a closing session on July 22, following an initial site visit in early July.

Teams for teaching, health services, repairs, news coverage, logistics and cultural activities were planned. Their work included lessons for rural primary pupils, basic health services and education for villagers, household-appliance repairs and community cultural events.

Applications were due by noon on May 25 through email, on-campus consultation points or the interview sessions. The application form and event attachments are available in the original OA notice.
