Ding Zejun, a professor in the Department of Physics at the University of Science and Technology of China, visited Shantou University on May 26, 2014, to give a seminar on Monte Carlo methods in surface electron spectroscopy and scanning electron microscopy. The seminar began at 9:15 a.m. in Room 352 of the School of Science.

The talk introduced Monte Carlo methods for electron-beam analysis and covered surface excitation in reflection electron energy-loss spectroscopy, deriving solid optical constants from the spectra, simulations of scanning electron images of complex surfaces, and research into electron scattering, diffraction and atomic-resolution imaging.

Ding is a professor at the University of Science and Technology of China and the National Laboratory for Physical Sciences at the Microscale. He received the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars and was selected for the Chinese Academy of Sciences' Hundred Talents Program. His long-term research focuses on computer simulations of interactions between electron beams, solid materials and surfaces. He has published more than 60 papers in the field.
