Zhicheng College has completed recruitment and interviews for its 2014–2015 student mentors and selected 14 students, including alternate candidates, as prospective mentors.

The prospective mentors will support residential-college student services during the following academic year. Individual names, years and majors are omitted to protect student privacy.
