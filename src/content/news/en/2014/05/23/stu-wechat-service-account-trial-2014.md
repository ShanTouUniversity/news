It is reported that the Network and Information Center issued a notice on May 23, 2014, that the WeChat public platform (service account “Shantou University”) has begun trial operation.

To help faculty and students stay updated and access campus information quickly, the university designates this service account along with subscription accounts “STU News Center” and “STU Alumni Association” and service account “STU Admissions Office” as officially certified WeChat accounts. Currently, it supports binding a personal WeChat account to the campus network account, campus card inquiries, library reservation and borrowing inquiries, and academic inquiries, with more features to follow.

## How to Follow

| Method | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Scan QR code | Open WeChat, go to Discover → Scan, scan the QR code and tap Follow |
| Search | Open WeChat, go to Contacts → Official Accounts → Service Accounts, tap “+”, search for “Shantou University” and select the verified account |

The QR code and detailed guide are available on the OA system. Welcome to leave comments via WeChat or email wechat@stu.edu.cn.
