The Into the Ocean community-service course team of Shantou University's School of Science was scheduled to hold the second Ocean Ecology Protection Public Day at Longhu Beach, Shantou CITIC Resort, on May 25, 2014. The event was organized with the resort under the theme “Into the Ocean, Listen to the Sea and Keep Our Promise to Protect It.”

The program included stage performances, science exhibitions, a graffiti wall, quick photography, family games, ocean-knowledge quizzes and interactive challenges. The resort planned to waive admission at the beach entrance from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on the event day, allowing visitors to learn about ocean conservation through educational and community activities.

Participants were advised to bring rain gear in case of heavy rain and to take care near the water. Minors needed parental accompaniment to swim. Teachers seeking information about the resort's special privileges could consult the attachment in the original notice.
