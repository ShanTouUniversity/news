Zhicheng College and the Law School will jointly run the “Entering University, Setting Sail for Life” high school summer camp service course for high school students in the Chaoshan region. The course is part of the university’s public-service curriculum, and students who complete the coursework and assessment can receive one academic credit.

## Course Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Places | 44 students, divided into groups of 24 and 20 |
| Training | Five evenings between June 29 and July 9 |
| Field practice | Group 1: July 13–16; Group 2: July 17–20 |
| Service recipients | Chaoshan high school students |
| Deadline | 17:00, June 1 |
| Email | o_zhicheng@stu.edu.cn |

The course covers university values, team development, research-based learning, project planning and implementation, Chinese culture and leadership practice. Applicants should download and complete the form, adding their year, major and name to the filename before submission. The application form is available on the OA system.
