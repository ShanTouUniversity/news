Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) would be closed to visitors on Monday, June 2, 2014, for the Dragon Boat Festival. It would operate as usual on Saturday, May 31, and Sunday, June 1.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
