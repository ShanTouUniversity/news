Shantou University's College of Science scheduled a Science Salon lecture for June 4, 2014, at 3:00 p.m. in the College of Science lecture hall (Science Building 352). Professor Chen Heru of Jinan University's School of Pharmacy was to speak on “Learning from Nature to Heal: Searching for Medicines in Gardenia.”

Chen is a professor of medicinal chemistry and a doctoral supervisor. He studied at Sun Yat-sen University, the Shanghai Institute of Organic Chemistry of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. He conducted research on bioactive peptides at the Montreal Clinical Research Institute in Canada and on neurological drugs at the University of Wuppertal in Germany. He previously taught in the Chemistry Department of Shantou University's College of Science.
