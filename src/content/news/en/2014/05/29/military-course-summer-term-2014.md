Shantou University's People's Armed Forces Department announced arrangements for military courses in the summer 2014 term. Military skills training and military theory were part of the credit system and were required for undergraduate and junior-college students. Students in the 2013 cohort and those with deferred training had to take the course during the term. Military training and theory were generally scheduled during the two weeks after the semester ended, while enrolling in military theory did not affect other summer-term course selections.

Students had to select the class number assigned to an instructor in their own college or residential college. Students who had changed majors had to select a class number from their new academic unit. Selecting the wrong class number or failing to register would prevent a grade from being recorded. Non-mainland students wishing to take military training, students seeking exemptions from unsuitable skills-training components for medical reasons, and 2013-cohort students seeking deferral all required approval from the relevant unit and the People's Armed Forces Department. Written applications for these special cases were due by June 5.

Instructor class numbers and detailed course arrangements are provided in the OA attachments.
