Shantou University's Legal Aid Research Center recruited new members from the 2012 and 2013 cohorts of law students. Applications were due by June 3, 2014. Members would assist with juvenile-crime background investigations commissioned by the Longhu District People's Court or Procuratorate, prepare investigation reports and appear in court to present them.

Members would also take part in the center's regular duty shifts, legal consultations and public-welfare activities, as well as legal-aid work for employees through the Shantou Federation of Trade Unions. Applicants had to download and complete the application form and submit it as specified in the original OA notice. The center's official contact email was laclaw@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Law)
