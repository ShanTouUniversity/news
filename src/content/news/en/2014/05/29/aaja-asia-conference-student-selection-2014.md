The Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication at Shantou University opened selection for graduate students to cover the Asian conference of the Asian American Journalists Association (AAJA) in 2014. About four students from the school's 2012 and 2013 cohorts would be selected. The conference was scheduled for June 7–8 at the University of Hong Kong, with all related expenses covered by the school. Applicants without previous overseas internships or exchange experience would receive priority.

Applicants had to submit an English application discussing their personal information, the conference's relevance to their careers, a reporting topic and a multimedia reporting plan. Interviews were scheduled for May 30 at 2:00 p.m. at STU's 789 Coffee Shop. Selection would consider possession of a Hong Kong and Macao travel permit, English communication skills, multimedia reporting and production ability, health, enthusiasm for learning and work, and stress tolerance.

Applications were due by 10:00 p.m. on May 29, 2014. The submission channel was provided in the original OA notice; students' personal contact details are not reproduced here.
