Shantou University's Arts Education Center will present a classroom teaching showcase on May 30, 2014, at 7:30 p.m. in the first-floor music rehearsal hall (Room 101). The performance will be open to the university community and feature hulusi, piano, wind-instrument and vocal performances, highlighting both individual skills and ensemble work.

The program will include “Where Has the Time Gone,” “Red River Valley,” “The Hawthorn Tree,” “Green Island Serenade,” “Bamboo House Love Song,” “The Fountain,” “Moonlight on the Phoenix-Tail Bamboo,” “Deep in the Bamboo Forest,” “There Is a Beautiful Place,” “My Heart Will Go On,” “Peacock Girl,” “Xiao Bu Shao,” “Amazing Grace,” “Pear Blossom Rain” and “The Same Song.” The performers will come from vocal, music fundamentals, hulusi, piano and wind-performance classes.
