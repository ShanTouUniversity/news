Shantou University's Graduate School launched an online management system for graduate assistantship positions at https://gsao.stu.edu.cn. The system covers allocation of assistantship quotas, position applications and approvals, recruitment, student applications, work assessments and allowance payments. Faculty responsible for assistantships in each college can log in and use the “Help” link in the upper-right corner for instructions.

The monthly period for graduate students, supervisors and position units to submit the previous month's assessment is before the seventh day of each month, adjusted for holidays. Late submissions will be handled one month later. Beginning in the fall semester of the 2014–2015 academic year, paper-based submission of graduate assistantship assessments would be discontinued.

System issues could be reported through the official contact channels of the Graduate School or Network and Information Center.
