The Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee has set deadlines for materials related to the departure of 2014 graduate Party members and Party statistics for the first half of 2014.

Starting May 30, the university will stop accepting probationary Party member applications for graduating students of 2014. Graduating students whose probationary period ends before July 2014 should complete confirmation procedures before leaving the university.

Materials on accepting probationary members and confirming members during the first half of the year must be submitted to Room 315 of the New Administration Center by June 10 for Party Committee review. Materials involving 2014 graduates are due by June 4 so that approval and archive transfer can be completed before departure.
