The Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee has arranged reporting of Party application personnel information for the first half of 2014. Party general branches and directly affiliated Party branches must consolidate the data and complete the relevant internal statistics.

The materials include the first-entry Party application personnel form and the list of people identified during the first half of 2014 as Party activists or development targets. Units must stamp the hard-copy forms and submit them to Room 315 of the New Administration Center by May 30, while sending electronic copies to o_zzb@stu.edu.cn.

Each unit should appoint a responsible staff member and ensure accurate, complete information. Lists and registration attachments containing personal information are available on the OA system.

> ⚠️ The Party application lists and related registration attachments are available on the OA system.
