The Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design will host the “Taikkun App Disrupt Workshop” from May 26 to 30. The program will be led by Taikkun Li, who works in technology entrepreneurship and product design in Silicon Valley.

## Schedule

| Event | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keynote talk | 19:30, May 26 | Lecture Theatre, School of Arts and Design |
| App innovation workshop | May 26–30 | Cheung Kong School of Arts and Design |
| Results exhibition | 19:30, May 30 | 789 Animation Laboratory, Space 8 |

The program will introduce first-hand experience from the Silicon Valley IT sector and let participants experience the full process of developing and producing an app. Questions may be directed to He Wang at hwang@stu.edu.cn. Faculty and students are welcome.
