The second Shantou University Business School Alumni Scholarship ceremony and donation event will be held at 15:30 on May 26 in the Business School Conference Room. The program includes remarks by university and school leaders, an introduction to the scholarship, the award presentation, alumni remarks, a donation ceremony, recipient remarks and a group photo.

## Scholarship Background

The scholarship was established in 2012 at the initiative of Business School alumni, with an initial fund of RMB 200,000. It supports full-time Business School undergraduates who perform well academically and need financial assistance. In 2013, 38 students from the school’s five programs received RMB 2,500 each; the second round in 2014 will again recognize 38 students with RMB 2,500 each.

Alumni will make an additional RMB 150,000 donation at this year’s event. Individual student names are not reproduced to protect privacy.
