Shantou University Library is offering trial access to the Dacheng Historical Documents Database, giving faculty, students and other readers access to search and download historical resources.

## Collection

The database contains about 7,000 late Qing and Republican-era periodicals, 17,000 ancient works, more than 40,000 Republican-era books, over 300 local gazetteers and more than 200 journals on the history of the Communist Party of China. The collection supports research in modern humanities and social sciences.
