The inaugural Cheung Kong Media Works Awards ceremony will be held at 19:30 on May 24 at the Lecture Hall of Shantou University. Faculty and students are welcome to attend.

The awards cover six categories: news features, photography, video, book and publication design, advertising, and interactive visual design. The event showcases teaching outcomes from journalism, broadcasting and television, editing and publishing, and advertising programs, while encouraging students to create high-quality media work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
