It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee issued a notice on May 22, 2014, on the transfer of Party membership for 2014 graduates.

## General Requirements

In line with superior documents and the principle of territorial management, all student Party members should transfer their membership to the Party organization of their employer or place of residence after graduation.

| Situation | Receiving organization |
| --- | --- |
| Employer confirmed | Party organization of the employer |
| No employer or employer has no Party organization | Talent exchange center where personnel file is kept; or subdistrict Party working committee of the employer’s location; or subdistrict/town Party committee of the student’s or parents’ household registration; or Party organization of parents’ employer |

Students should confirm acceptance with the destination organization in advance.

## Arrangements

| Step | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | Colleges compile the *Registration Form for the Transfer of Party Membership of 2014 Graduates*, verify the receiving unit and letter header with each member, and submit via o_zzb@stu.edu.cn and in hard copy by June 13 |
| Issuance of letters | General Party branches collect the *Letter of Introduction for Party Membership Transfer* from the department on June 23–24; graduates, after paying Party dues through June 2014, collect the letter in person with signature registration (no proxy collection) |
| Reporting | Members report to the destination Party committee within the required period with the letter; those transferring outside Guangdong must first transfer via the Organization Department of the Shantou Municipal Party Committee (No. 8, Haibin Road, 0754-88276114); if the receiving unit changes within 3 months, the graduate may return to the university with the original letter and ID card to reissue it |

## File Transfer

Materials for full members are filed directly into personal archives by the branch; materials for probationary members and activists are sealed and stamped by the branch and carried by the graduates to the receiving organization, with sign-off records kept for reference.

Colleges should provide training on transfer procedures and education on Party organization awareness.

> ⚠️ Registration forms and schedules are available on the OA system.
