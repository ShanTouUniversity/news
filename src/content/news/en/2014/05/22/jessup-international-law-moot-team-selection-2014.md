Shantou University Law School will select students for the Chinese round of the 13th Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition. After a strong 2013–2014 season, the school plans to form an eight-member team, including five official competitors required by the competition rules.

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Law School undergraduates and postgraduates |
| Written assessment | An approximately 500-word English essay, worth 50% |
| Interview | Oral interview in English, worth 50%, planned for June |
| Deadline | Application form and essay by June 9 |
| Email | stujessup@hotmail.com |

The selection will assess legal knowledge, English ability, legal reasoning and willingness to commit to the competition. The essay concerns whether the secession of Crimea from Ukraine and its subsequent annexation by Russia complied with international law; applicants may choose one of two supplied positions. The application form and essay attachment are available on the OA system.
