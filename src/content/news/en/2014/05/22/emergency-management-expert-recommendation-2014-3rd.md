The Shantou University Personnel Office has forwarded a notice from the Shantou Municipal People’s Government General Office on recommending members for the third Shantou emergency management expert group and is coordinating nominations across the university.

Units should organize nominations in line with the notice and submit the *Shantou Emergency Management Expert Recommendation Summary Form* and *Shantou Emergency Management Expert Registration Form* to the Personnel Office by June 19, together with electronic copies.
