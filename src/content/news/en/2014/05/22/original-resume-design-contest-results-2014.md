The final round of Shantou University 2014 Original Resume Design Contest was held at the New Library Lecture Hall on May 16. The contest recognized one first-prize winner, two second-prize winners, three third-prize winners, five excellence-award winners and one innovation-award winner. Six students also received corporate internship opportunities.

## Results

The awards were distributed as follows:

| Award | Number of recipients |
| --- | ---: |
| First prize | 1 |
| Second prize | 2 |
| Third prize | 3 |
| Excellence award | 5 |
| Innovation award | 1 |

To protect student privacy, individual names are not reproduced. China United Network Communications Group Co., Ltd. Shantou Branch provided internship opportunities for six students. The contest was organized by the Student Affairs Office, co-organized by Shantou Recruitment Network and China United Network Communications Group Co., Ltd. Shantou Branch, and undertaken by the Career Planning and Development Association.
