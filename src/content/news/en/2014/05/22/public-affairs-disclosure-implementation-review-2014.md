Shantou University’s public affairs disclosure supervision group has launched a review of disclosure implementation for the 2013–2014 academic year, covering functional departments at the main campus.

Departments must compare their implementation with previously submitted disclosure registers, complete the current-year implementation form, explain any items that should have been disclosed but were not, and identify newly added items. Hard copies are due to the Audit and Supervision Office at Room 422 of the New Administration Center by May 27, with electronic copies sent to o_jj@stu.edu.cn.

The supervision group will consolidate the results and publish them on the internal office information network for review by faculty, staff and students. The relevant register attachment is available on the OA system.

> ⚠️ The public affairs disclosure register and related attachment are available on the OA system.
