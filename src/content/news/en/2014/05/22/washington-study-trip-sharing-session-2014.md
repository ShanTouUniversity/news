The STU journalism study and reporting group will hold the “STU Meets Washington” sharing session from 19:30 to 21:30 on May 25 at the Library Lecture Hall.

During the three-week trip to the United States, the group visited media organizations, met media professionals and scholars, experienced university classes and explored local culture and daily life. The session will discuss U.S. politics and media, differences between imagined and actual university life, and what the participants learned from the trip.

All faculty and students are welcome. Interactive activities and small gifts will also be offered.
