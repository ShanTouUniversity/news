Shantou University Law School will select team members for the 2014 Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot series. The school team previously took part in events including the Beijing CIETAC Cup, the Moot Shanghai Invitational, the Hong Kong East Moot and the Vienna competition.

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Number selected | 8 to 12 |
| Eligible applicants | Law School undergraduates and postgraduates |
| Written assessment | An approximately 400-word English essay on international commercial arbitration, worth 50% |
| Interview | Oral interview in English, worth 50%; date to be announced |
| Submission deadlines | Application form by May 30; English essay by June 9 |
| Email | stujessup@hotmail.com |

Applicants should have a solid legal foundation, English communication skills and legal reasoning ability, and be willing to devote extracurricular time to preparation. The essay topic is *Party autonomy in the international commercial arbitration*. The application form and related attachment are available on the OA system.
