The 26th World Engineers Forum, hosted by Shantou University’s Engineering School, will take place from **2 to 4 p.m. on May 10, 2014**, in the library auditorium. Professor Wu Hong, general manager of GoHigh Technology (Shenzhen), will speak on the growth and development of an entrepreneurial company.

Faculty, staff, and students could register through the university booking system by noon on May 9.
