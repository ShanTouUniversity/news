It is reported that Shantou University’s Chemistry Department in the College of Science has announced the defense schedule for its 2014 undergraduate theses. The first round will be conducted in five groups on May 19 and May 21, followed by a second round on May 26, with the first-round group leaders serving as the second-round panel.

All first-round defenses are scheduled for 2:30–4:30 p.m. at G306, G303, E202-2, E207, and G304. The second round will take place from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on May 26 at E207. Student names are omitted to protect privacy.

## Defense Schedule

| Round | Time | Venues | Panel arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | May 19, 2:30–4:30 p.m. | E202-2 and E207 | Five defense groups |
| First | May 21, 2:30–4:30 p.m. | G306, G303, and G304 | Five defense groups |
| Second | May 26, 2:30–4:30 p.m. | E207 | First-round group leaders serve as panelists |
