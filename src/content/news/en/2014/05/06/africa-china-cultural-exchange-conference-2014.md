It is reported that Shantou University’s Global Studies Center will host a conference and workshop series on cultural exchange and cooperation in Africa-China relations from May 9 to 11, 2014. The program will examine culture in the era of globalization and digital technology and the development of relations between China and African countries through lectures, a computer-science demonstration, roundtables, panels, and a poetry workshop.

The event is organized by the Global Studies Center in the College of Liberal Arts together with the African Writers Trust, Shantou Writers Association, College of Engineering, Cheung Kong School of Journalism, and Center for Women’s Studies. Faculty members and students are welcome.
