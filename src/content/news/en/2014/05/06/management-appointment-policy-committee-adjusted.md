It is reported that Shantou University adjusted the membership of its Management-Position Appointment Policy Committee following a leadership meeting on April 29, 2014, as part of its administrative-management reform. Wen Xiaobo serves as chair. The listed members are Sun Fenqiang, Chen Zhulin, He Wenbiao, Li Yue, Wu Ping, Yang Qingjie, Zou Zhibo, Lin Peng, Mei Zhiping, and Zeng Dabin. The committee office is located in the Personnel Office.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Committee | STU Management-Position Appointment Policy Committee |

| Office | Personnel Office |
