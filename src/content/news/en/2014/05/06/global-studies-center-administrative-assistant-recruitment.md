It is reported that Shantou University’s Global Studies Center is recruiting one administrative assistant from the university’s in-service and personnel-agency staff. Applications close on May 12, 2014.

The position covers office administration, event and visitor coordination, Chinese-English translation, exchange projects, course administration, personnel work, asset and financial assistance, and library-room management. Applicants should have strong English skills, be proficient with office software, and demonstrate organizational, writing, and communication abilities.
