It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University forwarded a notice on May 6, 2014, regarding the 5th Guangdong University Translation Contest under the 8th Guangdong Science and Technology Festival for University Students.

Organized jointly by the Provincial Youth League Committee, Department of Education, Department of Science and Technology, Association for Science and Technology and Student Federation, the contest aims to promote translation education and cultivate young translation talent.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contest | 5th Guangdong University Translation Contest (part of the 8th Guangdong Science and Technology Festival) |
| Languages | English, Japanese (one per participant) |
| Categories | Interpreting and written translation |
| Campus deadline | May 15, 2014 |
| How to apply | Complete the application form (see OA attachment) and send to o_tw@stu.edu.cn |
| Selection | Youth League Committee will recommend candidates based on applications |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee |

Details and the form are available in the OA attachment.
