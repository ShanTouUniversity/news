It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 56 [2014] on May 6, 2014, announcing the results of the 2014 Li Ka Shing Foundation STU Sportsmanship Medal.

Established to carry forward Mr. Li Ka-shing’s educational philosophy of “Aspiration, Knowledge, Perseverance and Achievement” and to honor outstanding graduates who demonstrate noble sportsmanship and contribute to the university’s sports development, the medal was awarded after strict selection by the evaluation committee.

## Awardees

| Award | Recipient (anonymized) | College/Residential College | Program | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sportsmanship Medal | Deng PY | Business School | Accounting, Class of 2010 | 8,000 yuan |
| Nomination Award | Zhou RX | Law School (Zhicheng College) | Law, Class of 2010 | 1,000 yuan |
| Nomination Award | Ye SX | Business School | Business Administration, Class of 2010 | 1,000 yuan |
