It is reported that the International Business Case Club (IBCC) of the Business School will join the contest organizing committee in hosting the Shantou University stop of the 7th National College Online Business Innovation and Application Contest roadshow. The event will be held from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on May 7, 2014, in E207 and is open to all STU faculty members and students.

The roadshow will introduce the contest theme and participation process, promote innovation and entrepreneurship practice in online business, and encourage exchange among companies, students, and society. Representatives of the organizing committee and China Postal Savings Bank’s Shantou branch will attend.
