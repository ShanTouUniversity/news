It is reported that the Research Office forwarded a notice on May 6, 2014, from the Guangdong Intellectual Property Office on the 2014 soft science research plan projects.

Interested faculty members may select topics based on the guide and submit five printed copies of the *Project Application Form* to the Research Office by May 21, 2014, while sending the electronic version to ygluo@stu.edu.cn.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | 2014 Guangdong Intellectual Property Office Soft Science Research Plan |
| Topic selection | Self-selected based on the guide |
| Deadline | May 21, 2014 |
| Materials | 5 printed copies of the application form + electronic version |
| Email | ygluo@stu.edu.cn |
| Contact | Luo Yingguang |
| Phone | 86503271 |
| Issued by | Research Office |

The guide and template are available in the OA attachments.
