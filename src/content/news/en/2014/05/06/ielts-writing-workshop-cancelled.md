Shantou University’s English Language Center announced that the IELTS writing workshop scheduled for 4 p.m. on May 7, 2014, in E208 has been cancelled due to unforeseen circumstances. Registration information for future IELTS workshops will be announced later.

Ticket holders with questions may contact the English Language Center at hdiime@stu.edu.cn.
