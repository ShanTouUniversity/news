Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold an Antarctic culture event from **11 a.m. to 1:10 p.m. on May 7, 2014**, at the Golden Triangle on the campus pedestrian street. A sharing session, “Dreaming of Antarctica, Journeying Together,” is scheduled for **7:30 to 9 p.m. on May 10** in the Science and Technology Auditorium.

The session will recount the university’s 2014 Antarctic expedition, which ran from February 14 to March 6. A 22-member team led by Vice President Dr. Li Dan made eight landings on the Antarctic Peninsula and nearby islands and visited China’s Great Wall Station. The project also included general-education courses, lectures, and exhibitions on polar topics.

The session is open to the university community. Souvenirs will be given to the first 100 attendees.
