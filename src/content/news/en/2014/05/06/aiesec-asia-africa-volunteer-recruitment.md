It is reported that the Shantou University AIESEC team has opened spring recruitment for overseas volunteer projects in Asia and Africa. Students of all majors and year levels may apply. Projects address areas including cultural exchange, community and rural development, environmental protection, and language education. Applications close at midnight on May 9, 2014.

AIESEC will hold a “Global Village” information event at Jin San Jiao from 11:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. on May 7. Returned volunteers will share their experiences, while applicants can consult the team and register. Recruitment consists of résumé screening, group interviews, and individual interviews; the latter two stages will be announced to shortlisted applicants.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | All STU students, regardless of major or year |
| Information event | May 7, 11:30 a.m.–2:30 p.m., Jin San Jiao |
| Application deadline | Midnight, May 9 |
| Online application | Complete the form and send it to aiesec.volunteer.stu@gmail.com |
| Recruitment process | Résumé screening, group interview, and individual interview |
| Official channels | Sina Weibo @AIESEC汕大 and WeChat account AIESEC汕大 |
