It is reported that Weldon Epp, Consul General of Canada in Guangzhou, will give a public lecture at Shantou University from 7:30 to 8:30 p.m. on May 19, 2014, in the Library Lecture Theatre. The lecture is titled “Canada-China Relations,” with the subtitle “Legal and educational cooperation and beyond.”

Epp joined Canada’s Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade in 1996 and has served in Beijing, Taipei and Jakarta, as well as on short assignments in Geneva, Islamabad and Phnom Penh. Before taking up his Guangzhou post, he headed the department’s Policy Research Division.

The lecture will be delivered in English, and all STU students and faculty are welcome.
