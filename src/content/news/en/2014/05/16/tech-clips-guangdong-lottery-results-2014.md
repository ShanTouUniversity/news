It is reported that the Student Affairs Office announced the reservation results for the Tech Clips Guangdong Innovation Day. A total of 645 faculty members and students registered. The university allocated all 100 places to students, including 15 places for student volunteers recommended by schools.

On the afternoon of May 16, 2014, a computer lottery was conducted in the presence of representatives from the Student Union’s Rights Protection Department. It selected 85 primary student participants and 17 alternates. Those selected were asked to complete the follow-up confirmation by noon on May 18 so that the university could arrange the required coverage.

The event is intended to give students an opportunity to learn about technology companies and experience approaches to innovation, management and operations. The participant lists and detailed procedures are available in the OA attachments.
