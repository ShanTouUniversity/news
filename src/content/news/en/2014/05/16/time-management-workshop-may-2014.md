It is reported that the English Language Center will hold a time management workshop from 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. on May 21, 2014, at CILL. The session will be led by Yanling Cai and Jiin Yap.

The workshop will introduce strategies for managing time and completing tasks efficiently. Places are limited, and interested participants may reserve a place at https://timemanagementws.eventbrite.com. Registered participants are expected to bring their tickets and attend.
