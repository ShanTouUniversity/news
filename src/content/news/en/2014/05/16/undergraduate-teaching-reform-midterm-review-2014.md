It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office has completed the midterm review of projects launched under Shantou University’s 2013 Advanced Undergraduate Education and Teaching Reform Program. Of the 172 projects reviewed, 169 were found to be proceeding in line with their approved plans and passed the review, making them eligible for the second installment of funding.

Three projects were deferred. The university will consider whether to release their second funding installment in the following year based on their progress. Units and project teams whose work passed the review are expected to continue with the subsequent research.

Detailed project-by-project review results are provided in the OA attachment.
