It is reported that Shantou University’s Guitar Lovers Association will hold the “Zifeiyu” music festival at 7:00 p.m. on May 17, 2014, in the open area outside the Grand Auditorium. The event is open to the STU community and is planned to feature professional musicians, bands from on and off campus, campus music enthusiasts and alumni bands.

The organizers will run a social-media promotion and prize redemption activity at the venue from noon to 4:00 p.m. on the day of the event, with a ukulele donated by a local music store among the prizes. Any change caused by heavy rain will be announced through official channels. Participants are asked to follow site rules and take care of their safety.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Communist Youth League Committee)
