It is reported that the Computer Science Department of the College of Engineering has announced the defense schedule for its 2010 undergraduate thesis and design cohort. The department defense committee is chaired by Cai Hao, with committee members and group panels responsible for the defenses and assessment.

The group defenses will take place at Wenxi Building on May 23, 2014. Group 1 will meet from 13:30 to 18:00 in Wenxi 301; Group 2 from 14:30 to 18:00 in Wenxi 302; Group 3 from 8:00 to 12:30 in Wenxi 302; and Group 4 from 8:00 to 12:30 in Wenxi 301. The outstanding-thesis defense will be scheduled after the group sessions.

Students are expected to prepare a presentation and defense records. After the defenses, they must revise their theses according to the feedback, after which supervisors will submit the paper and electronic versions to the department. Detailed group lists and attachment requirements are available in the OA notice.
