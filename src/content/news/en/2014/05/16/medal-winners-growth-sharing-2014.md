It is reported that Shantou University has completed its 2014 University Medal and Sportsmanship Medal selection. To give the campus community an opportunity to learn about the awardees’ growth experiences, the university will hold a sharing session from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on May 20, 2014, in E Classroom 203.

The session is open to students and faculty and aims to encourage students to pursue personal growth in their studies and wider goals. Awardee names and other personal information are omitted here for privacy.
