It is reported that the Shantou University tendering leadership group has completed its recommendation of contractors for four construction projects: a rubble-stone wall at the affiliated primary and secondary schools, brick grease traps at the second and third cafeterias, and two campus maintenance projects.

| Project | Winning contractor | Bid amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Affiliated schools’ rubble-stone wall | Shantou Residential Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. | RMB 67,520.00 |
| Second and third cafeterias’ brick grease traps | Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 22,649.13 |
| Campus minor maintenance project 1 | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Co., Ltd. | RMB 45,000.00 |
| Campus minor maintenance project 2 | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 47,000.00 |

The public notice period ran from May 15 to May 19, 2014. Official tender and complaint contact details are provided in the announcement.
