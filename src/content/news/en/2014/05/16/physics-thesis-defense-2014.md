It is reported that the Physics Department of the College of Science has announced the 2014 undergraduate thesis defense schedule. The committee is chaired by Li Shaohui, with Chen Changjin, Wang Jiangyong, Yang Weifeng, Su Xinjian, Qiu Guiming and Zhu Weian as members, and Dai Lihua as secretary.

Group defenses are scheduled for 2:00–6:00 p.m. on May 28, 2014, followed by the outstanding-thesis defense from 8:00 a.m. to noon on May 30. The process includes presentations, questions, responses, scoring and selection of outstanding theses; students are expected to prepare presentation materials in advance.

Detailed group and venue information is provided in the attached schedule. Student names are omitted here to protect privacy; related forms and attachments are available on the OA system.
