It is reported that the Business School has opened selection for students to join its training program for an international business case competition in the first half of 2015. The opportunity is open to all STU undergraduates. Applicants should have completed relevant economics or management courses, demonstrate strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, hold an average of at least 80 in ELC Level 1–5 courses, and have a GPA of at least 3.0.

Registration is open from May 17 to May 25, 2014, through the “Case Competitions” link on the Business School website. Shortlisted candidates will receive an interview notice, prepare an English personal-introduction presentation, and attend an all-English interview on the afternoon of May 28. The final training-class list will be published online.

The registration portal and arrangements are subject to the Business School notice; students’ personal contact details are omitted here.
