It is reported that the Business School will host an academic lecture from 9:30 to 11:00 a.m. on May 20, 2014, in its seminar room. The lecture, titled “The Nonlinear Relationship Between Financial Development and Economic Growth in China: Empirical Evidence from a Dynamic Panel Threshold Model,” will be given by Huang Zhilin, a postdoctoral researcher at Peking University, and chaired by Associate Professor Ji Yun.

Using annual panel data from 31 Chinese provinces and municipalities from 1979 to 2008, the presentation will use inflation as a threshold variable and a dynamic panel threshold model to examine potential nonlinear relationships between financial development and economic growth at different inflation levels.

Huang’s research focuses on Western economics and financial econometrics, and she has published several papers in leading Chinese journals. The lecture is open to the STU community.
