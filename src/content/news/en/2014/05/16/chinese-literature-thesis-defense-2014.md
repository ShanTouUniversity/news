It is reported that the Chinese Department of the School of Liberal Arts has announced the undergraduate thesis defense schedule for its 2010 cohort. Students will be organized in five fields: modern and contemporary literature, classical literature, literary aesthetics and criticism, Chinese linguistics, and foreign literature.

All five groups are scheduled for 8:00 a.m. to noon on May 24, 2014. The venues are D-302, D-303, D-304, D-306 and D-307 respectively. The process includes student presentations, questions from faculty, responses and assessment; each group will also recommend outstanding theses for committee review.

The notice asks recorders to document the defenses accurately and faculty members to assess work based on thesis quality and defense performance. Student names are omitted here to protect privacy; detailed group information is available in the OA notice.
