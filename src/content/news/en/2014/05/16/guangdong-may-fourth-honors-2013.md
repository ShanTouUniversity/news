It is reported that two Shantou University organizations and three students received recognition in Guangdong’s 2013 selection of outstanding Communist Youth League members and cadres and May Fourth Red-Flag League organizations.

The Youth League Committee of the Second Affiliated Hospital of the College of Medicine was named a Guangdong May Fourth Red-Flag League Committee, while the General Youth League Branch of the College of Medicine’s Basic Department was named a Guangdong May Fourth Red-Flag League Branch. Individually, one student from the College of Science was recognized as an outstanding League cadre, and students from the College of Medicine and Business School were recognized as outstanding League members. Student full names are omitted here for privacy.

The university Youth League Committee encouraged League organizations and young members to learn from the honorees and continue contributing to campus reform and innovation in League work.
