It is reported that the construction tender for Shantou University’s new nursery base was declared unsuccessful after the university’s tendering leadership group found fewer than three valid bidders, which did not meet the applicable tendering requirements.

The announcement period ran from May 15 to May 19, 2014. The tendering party was the STU Infrastructure Office, whose official telephone number is 0754-86502490 and fax number is 0754-86502314. Complaints may be directed to the Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346, with fax number 0754-86500536.
