It is reported that a Shantou University Business School team won second place in the 50th International Collegiate Business Strategy Competition (ICBSC). Hosted by California State University, Long Beach, the competition brought together teams from universities in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and China.

The STU team competed as Empire Co. with four undergraduates. To protect student privacy, the members are identified by surname and initials: Sun LL as CEO, Huang JH as vice president of marketing, Ouyang YJ as COO, and Chen XJ as CFO. The team completed a 10-week remote simulation and an in-person stage in Anaheim, California, covering market analysis, a business plan, operating decisions and a board presentation. It ultimately earned second place for its operating results and presentation.

The competition included simulated operations, annual-report and strategy-plan writing, on-site stock trading and exchanges with teams from other universities. Faculty member Wu Jingqiang and other instructors supported the team. The result gave the students additional experience in international competition and teamwork.
