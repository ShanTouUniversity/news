It is reported that the Shantou University Trade Union is recruiting volunteers from the main-campus faculty for its family event. Held annually since 2009, the event involves volunteers in planning, organization and on-site order management.

Interested faculty members should complete the registration form and send it to the Trade Union’s official email, o_xgh@stu.edu.cn, by May 21, 2014. The consultation telephone number is 86502983.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union)
> ⚠️ The registration form is available on the OA system.
