It is reported that the English Language Center will hold an IELTS preparation workshop on speaking skills from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. on May 21, 2014, at CILL. The session will be led by John Harper.

The workshop will focus on points to watch for and strategies for the IELTS speaking test. Places are limited, and interested participants may reserve a place at https://ieltsspeaking1.eventbrite.com. Registered participants are expected to bring their tickets and attend.
