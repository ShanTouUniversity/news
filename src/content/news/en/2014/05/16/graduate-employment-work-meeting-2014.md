It is reported that Shantou University will hold a graduate employment work meeting at 10:00 a.m. on May 19, 2014, in Conference Room 213 of the New Administration Center. Participants include party secretaries or deputy secretaries of the colleges and residential colleges, counselors responsible for employment, and staff from the Graduate Employment Guidance Center of the Student Affairs Office.

The meeting will provide a forum for coordinating graduate employment work. Invited participants are expected to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
