The Changjiang School of Art and Design scheduled its 16th Student Representatives Congress for 2:30 p.m. on June 7, 2014, in the school's lecture classroom. Delegates were to review the work of the 15th student union and elect the executive committee for the 16th term.

The congress was expected to have about 80 delegates, representing roughly 15 percent of the student body and elected at class meetings. Delegates were to be current undergraduates able to represent their peers and participate in deliberations. The organizing committee said the meeting would discuss the student union's future work and its role in serving the school community.
