Shantou University's Social Sciences Department set out the year-specific requirements for political theory courses in fall 2014. Students in the classes of 2011 and 2012 were to take the four-credit course *Ideological and Moral Cultivation and Fundamentals of Law* under the previous curriculum. The class of 2013 was required to take separate two-credit courses in moral cultivation and law; law majors were exempt from the law course.

*Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics* was worth six credits for the classes of 2011 and 2012, and four credits for the class of 2013. *Basic Principles of Marxism* (three credits) and *Outline of Modern and Contemporary Chinese History* (two credits) were required for all students. International students would continue under the existing exemption policy unless new rules were issued.

The department urged 2015 graduates to check their records and complete any outstanding required courses promptly to avoid problems during graduation review.
