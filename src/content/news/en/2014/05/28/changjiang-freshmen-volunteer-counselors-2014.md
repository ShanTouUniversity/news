The Changjiang School of Art and Design began preparations to welcome its 2014 freshmen and recruited volunteer counselors from its current students in the classes of 2011, 2012, and 2013. Volunteers would help receive new students, distribute materials, provide directions, and answer questions during registration and orientation, then offer support with campus life and studies after students arrived.

Applications were open through June 3, with interviews scheduled for 2:30 p.m. on June 4 in F204 of the School of Art and Design. The school sought applicants who were service-minded, responsible, and able to work and communicate well in a team. The application channel is provided in the original OA notice.
