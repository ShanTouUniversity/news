It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold a lecture titled "From Beijing to Los Angeles: An Overview of Independent Animation Creativity and Design" at 7:30 p.m. on Monday, June 9, in the school's lecture hall. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

In addition to the lecture, the school will run a companion workshop from June 9 to 13, offering in-depth engagement with creativity and design in independent animation.
