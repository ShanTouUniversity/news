Following arrangements from the General Staff Department and the Shantou municipal conscription office, Shantou University opened the 2014 direct recruitment process for graduates as non-commissioned officers. Applicants had to be unmarried university graduates whose majors matched military needs. The age limit was 24 for men and 23 for women as of July 31, 2014. Political and physical requirements followed those for ordinary conscripts. Graduates in majors with national vocational skills assessments also had to hold an intermediate-level or higher vocational qualification certificate.

Major groups listed for recruitment in Shantou included mechatronics, electrical automation, computer control, computer application, networking, multimedia, computer information management, network systems management, software technology, graphic and image production, website planning and development, and embedded technology applications. Graduates in other majors could also apply if they met the provincial requirements.

The process consisted of centralized registration, medical examination and political review, approval and assignment, and reporting, scheduled respectively for May 28–31, June 1–20, June 21–August 1, and August 2–10. Interested graduates had to check the available majors, submit their information and complete online review through the national recruitment website by May 30. Follow-up contact arrangements were provided in the original OA notice.
