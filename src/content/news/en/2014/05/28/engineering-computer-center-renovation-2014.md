Shantou University's Infrastructure Office arranged renovations for the new Engineering Computer Center on the first floor of Building C. The project was contracted to Shantou Longhua Construction Corporation and covered the complete interior demolition and reconstruction of the space, including the addition of a mezzanine. The renovated area was 673.50 square meters, with a winning bid of 1.1388 million yuan.

Construction was scheduled to begin on May 29, 2014, and finish by August 16. The work could produce noise, vibration and dust. Faculty, students and passersby were advised to exercise caution and avoid remaining at the construction site, especially those working or attending classes in the building.
