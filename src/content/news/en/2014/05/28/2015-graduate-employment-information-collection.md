To prepare the employment plan and related recommendation and placement work for the 2015 graduating class, Shantou University's Student Affairs Office asked main-campus undergraduates and all postgraduate students graduating in 2015 to submit their employment information through the Guangdong graduate employment management system by June 13, 2014.

The office asked schools and colleges to share the requirement and urged graduates to check their submissions carefully. Instructions are included in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Submission instructions are available on the OA system.
