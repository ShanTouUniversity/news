Shantou University's Business School opened applications for the “Love in Action” international charity summer camp course, offering 20 places to students from across the university. The course was scheduled for July 14 through August 17, 2014. Students who completed the course and passed its assessment could receive one academic credit.

Through the AIESEC global network, the course planned to recruit six international university volunteers and work with Dongxia Middle School, the Shantou Disabled Persons' Federation and local communities. Participants would help international volunteers adapt to life in Shantou, organize charity sales and public-welfare activities for children with autism, and experience Chaoshan culture through activities involving Chaozhou ceramics, Chaoshan paper-cutting, Chinese calligraphy, traditional opera and martial arts. They would also work with high school students in Shantou to plan public-welfare activities.

The course sought applicants with a willingness to help others, basic spoken-language skills, event-organizing ability, cultural openness and familiarity with Chaoshan culture. Applications were due by 11:30 p.m. on May 29, 2014. Registration channels and course details were provided in the original OA notice.
