The College of Science at Shantou University scheduled a Science Salon lecture for June 3, 2014, at 3:00 p.m. in Room 304 of Building D. Professor Chen Honglin of the University of Hong Kong was to speak on “Regulatory Mechanisms and Clinical Applications of Epstein-Barr Viral microRNA in Latent Infection of Nasopharyngeal Cancer Cells.”

Chen is a professor and molecular virologist at the University of Hong Kong. He conducted postdoctoral research at the Royal Postgraduate Medical School in the United Kingdom and Johns Hopkins School of Medicine in the United States. He previously taught and conducted research in the Biology Department at Shantou University and has maintained research collaborations with STU's Medical School and College of Science. His work covers Epstein-Barr virus-associated tumors, interactions between influenza viruses and hosts, and next-generation influenza vaccines.
