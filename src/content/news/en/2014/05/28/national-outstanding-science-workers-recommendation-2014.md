The Guangdong Association for Science and Technology launched the sixth National Outstanding Science and Technology Workers selection, and Shantou University's Research Office began soliciting nominations from relevant campus units. Nominees were expected to work on the front line in natural sciences, technical sciences, engineering and related fields, including research and development, science outreach, talent development or work connecting science and technology with the economy.

Nominees' paper and electronic materials had to be submitted together to the Research Office's achievements section by June 9, 2014. The package included seven copies of the National Outstanding Science and Technology Worker Nomination Form, one summary information form and three copies of the nominee's basic information and brief achievements. Candidates nominated for the “Top Ten National Outstanding Science and Technology Workers” additionally had to provide two individual photographs showing their work. Materials were required to be accurate and could not contain classified information.

The forms, templates and detailed requirements are provided in the original OA attachments.
