Shantou University's College of Science scheduled a Science Salon lecture for June 6, 2014, from 8:30 to 10:00 a.m. Professor Lin Xiangmin of Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University was to speak in the College of Science lecture hall (Li-352) on “LamB-Mediated Negative Regulation of Bacterial Drug-Resistance Mechanisms.”

Lin is a Jinshan Distinguished Professor and a Minjiang Distinguished Professor at Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University, where he is also a doctoral supervisor. His research focuses on pathogen microbial membrane-protein functions and mechanisms and on proteomics. He has led projects supported by international funds, the National Natural Science Foundation of China Young Scientists Fund and university-level young scholar programs, and has published multiple SCI papers in related international journals.
