Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office announced course registration and textbook-ordering dates for the 2014 summer and fall semesters. Registration for both terms would run concurrently. The credit limits were six credits for summer and 25 for fall, excluding public-benefit courses.

| Stage | Dates |
| --- | --- |
| Initial registration | May 30, 9 a.m.–June 1, 5 p.m. |
| Second registration | June 6, 12:30 p.m.–June 8, 5 p.m. |
| Add/drop | June 18, 12:30 p.m.–June 20, 5 p.m. |
| Online requests to add courses | June 21, 9 a.m.–June 25, 5 p.m. |
| Fall adjustment period | September 22, 9 a.m.–September 26, 5 p.m. |

Textbook ordering ran alongside the initial and second registration periods and closed at 5 p.m. on June 8. Orders would then be sent to the bookstore; additions, cancellations, and changes would not be available during later add/drop or adjustment periods. Students were asked to review their orders before the deadline. Course schedules, registration rules, and ordering instructions are in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Course schedules and textbook-ordering instructions are available on the OA system.
