The 20th Shantou University Business School Alumni Lecture, titled “Career Choices and Growth for University Students,” was scheduled for 3:00–4:30 p.m. on June 2, 2014, in E207. Speakers were Zheng Zhenjie, an accounting alumnus from the class of 2007, and Liu Xiaolu, a business administration alumna from the same class.

Zheng worked in transfer-pricing tax analysis at Deloitte Hua Yong's South China office before becoming an innovative business account manager at China CITIC Bank's Shenzhen branch. Liu worked on new-media projects at *Securities Times* and was preparing to pursue an MBA.
