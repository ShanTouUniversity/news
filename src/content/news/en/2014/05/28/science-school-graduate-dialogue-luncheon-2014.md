Shantou University's School of Science scheduled a luncheon with its 2010 graduating class from 12:15 to 1:45 p.m. on June 3, 2014, in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. The school's dean, Party secretary, associate dean, department heads, and faculty representatives were expected to discuss employment feedback, undergraduate education, and student services with graduates.

The school invited 30 student representatives. Interested students were asked to register through the campus booking system by 10 p.m. on May 31. Registration would close once capacity was reached, and the invited representatives would be announced on the campus office network. The school would cover the meal costs.
