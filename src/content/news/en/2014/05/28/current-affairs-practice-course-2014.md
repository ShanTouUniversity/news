Shantou University's Social Sciences Department planned to offer SOC1162A, *Current Affairs and Policy Education (Practice)*, in fall 2014 as a required 16-hour, one-credit course for 2015 undergraduate graduates at the main campus. The course required students to write a review essay on major domestic and international current affairs rather than attend classroom sessions.

Students had to register before the 18th week of the semester and submit their papers to their assigned instructors by January 12, 2015. Credit was contingent on a passing grade. Graduates outside the class of 2011 also had to submit a paper application before week 18. The required paper length was 3,000–5,000 words; the template and teaching materials are in the OA attachments.
