Shantou University's College of Engineering and Academic Affairs Office opened campus registration for the fourth National Undergraduate Engineering Training Competition. This year's theme was an obstacle course for carbon-free vehicles. The selection was open to undergraduates in the 2011, 2012 and 2013 cohorts, and the winning participants would advance to the Guangdong provincial competition.

Applicants had to complete the registration form individually and submit the required paper and electronic materials through the channels specified in the original notice. The deadline was June 7, 2014. The CDIO Innovation Practice Center of the Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering planned to provide training, team formation, guidance and consultations, with the campus selection scheduled for completion by September 10, 2014.

The national competition consists of campus, provincial and national stages. It aims to support reforms in engineering training and develop students' engineering innovation, practical and teamwork skills. The competition brief and detailed campus arrangements are provided in the OA attachments.
