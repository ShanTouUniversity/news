The Shantou E-Government Administration Office planned to establish an expert database to provide policy consultation and professional support for e-government development. Shantou University's Research Office consequently invited qualified faculty members to seek nomination for the database.

Applicants had to submit three copies of the nomination form and a copy of their highest professional qualification or academic certificate to the Research Office's achievements section by June 16, 2014. They also had to send an electronic version as specified in the notice. Detailed eligibility requirements, the form and electronic submission instructions are provided in the original OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
> ⚠️ The detailed eligibility requirements and nomination form are available on the OA system.
