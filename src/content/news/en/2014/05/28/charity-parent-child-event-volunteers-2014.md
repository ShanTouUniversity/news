Shantou University's Business School planned to hold the “Big Hands Holding Little Hands: You and Me Together” charity parent-child event on June 7, 2014. The event was organized through the Charity Public-Welfare Marketing course and sought 40 student volunteers from across the university. It would use interactive games to encourage communication between children and their parents, with proceeds planned for donation to the Shantou Deaf-Mute School.

Volunteer duties were scheduled from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and included guiding participating families, assisting with parent-child games and maintaining order. Applications were due by midnight on June 3, or earlier if all places were filled. The organizers sought applicants who were responsible, attentive, enthusiastic about service and able to work as part of a team.

The registration channels and detailed contact information were provided in the original STU OA notice.
