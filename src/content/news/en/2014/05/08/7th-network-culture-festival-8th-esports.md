Shantou University’s seventh Network Culture Festival and eighth Esports Competition ran from April 23 to May 18, 2014. Organized by the university Youth League Committee and hosted by the “Chua—Road to Success” campus website, the program included online writing, interactive games, esports, and cybersecurity outreach.

The “Internet Stories” writing activity ran on Sina Weibo and invited posts about positive online experiences, fraud prevention, and useful technology. An interactive retro-games event took place at the Golden Triangle on April 26. The esports competition featured League of Legends and Counter-Strike: preliminary rounds were scheduled for May 10–11 in Teaching Building E, with finals on May 17–18 below the New Administration Building (with the area below Dining Hall Two as a backup venue). A cybersecurity exhibition was scheduled for May 24–25 at the Golden Triangle.

Registration forms and detailed activity rules are available in the OA attachments. Personal student email addresses are not reproduced here.
