Shantou University’s Graduate School announced arrangements on May 8, 2014, for student-ID replacement and rail-ticket discount-card magnetization. The services covered graduate students in the 2012 and 2013 cohorts; the 2011 cohort did not need card magnetization in this round.

By May 14, students were to submit their student IDs through their classes, first obtaining the required registration stamp from their academic school and then delivering the IDs to the Graduate School Youth League office in Room 401 of the Administration Center. Replacement requests were also due by that date and had to be submitted collectively by class. Inquiries could be directed to Wang Fengqi at 86504000.
