It is reported that the 2014 Shantou University 3x3 Basketball Tournament is drawing to a close, with the men's round of eight, the women's semifinals, and both the men's and women's finals scheduled for 2:00 p.m. on May 10 (Saturday) at the Air-Dome Basketball Training Hall, where the champions of each division will be decided.

Because the Air-Dome Basketball Training Hall has limited seating capacity, admission to the finals will be arranged through Weibo registration. Faculty and students who wish to watch the games on site need to repost the finals-related Weibo post published by "@汕大校会体策部" (the STU Student Union Sports Planning Department) on Sina Weibo to complete registration. On the day of the event, spectators must present both their campus card and the reposted Weibo as proof of entry. According to the organizers, seats are available on a first-come, first-served basis, and entry will close once capacity is reached.

In addition, visitors entering the air-dome hall are required to wear rubber-soled sports shoes to protect the court surface.
