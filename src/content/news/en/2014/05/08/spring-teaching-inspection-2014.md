Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office inspected classes across six time periods on May 4, 2014. A bulletin issued on May 8 said teaching was generally orderly and most instructors arrived early and prepared, while a small number of classes showed problems such as instructors arriving after the bell or attending to unrelated tasks during class.

The inspection also recorded an instructor arriving late to an Engineering Mechanics I class in the Department of Civil Engineering and an unoccupied laboratory for a chemistry experiment class. The office said it would investigate with the relevant units and handle any confirmed teaching violations under university rules. Further unannounced inspections were planned.
