Shantou University Library began collecting renewal and new-title recommendations for its 2015 foreign-language print journal subscriptions. Faculty members and graduate students were invited to submit suggestions, with schools asked to consider teaching, research, and disciplinary collection needs.

Recommendations were due to the Library’s Subject Services Department by May 16. Inquiries could be directed to Ms. Wu at xxwu@stu.edu.cn or 86503247. The recommendation form and submission instructions are available in the OA attachment.
