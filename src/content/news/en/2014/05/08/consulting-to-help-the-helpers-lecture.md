Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled its second counselors’ meeting of 2014 for 4 p.m. on May 9 in ACC Room 5. Professor Sharone L. Maital, a member of the International Advisory Board of Daimler-Chrysler Group, a distinguished researcher at BCT UnionPay, and an honorary professor at the university’s Business School, was set to speak on “Consulting to Help the Helpers.”

The event was intended for all counselors and relevant student-affairs staff. Further speaker details are available in the OA attachment.
