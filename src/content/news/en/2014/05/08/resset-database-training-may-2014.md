Shantou University Library scheduled a training session on the RESSET financial database for 2:30–3:30 p.m. on Saturday, May 10, 2014. The session was to take place at the Subject Services desk on the Library’s second floor. Gao Xiaoling, a trainer from Beijing Join-Cheer RESSET Data Technology, was set to introduce the database and its search methods.

RESSET/DB supports empirical research and model testing. Its coverage includes stocks, fixed income, funds, macroeconomics, industries, economics and law, Hong Kong stocks, foreign exchange, futures, and gold, across nearly 100 databases.
