Owing to weather conditions, the Pilates class for female staff of Shantou University originally set for Wednesday, May 7 was cancelled at short notice. The university's Pilates Association subsequently confirmed that the session will be made up this Friday, May 9.

The Pilates class is run with the support of the university labor union and is open to all female staff, aiming to help employees keep fit and relieve fatigue outside working hours. Participants are reminded to attend at the adjusted time and to spread the word among fellow classmates.
