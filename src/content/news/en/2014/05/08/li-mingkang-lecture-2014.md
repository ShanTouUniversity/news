Shantou University’s Office of International Affairs and Cooperation announced that Professor Liu Mingkang would lecture from 7:30 to 9:15 p.m. on May 12, 2014, in the University Library Lecture Hall. The talk would address challenges posed by the new technology revolution and the modernization of China’s governance system and capacity.

Those wishing to attend were asked to book through the university’s reservation system by 9 p.m. on May 9. Liu served on the International Advisory Board of Daimler-Chrysler Group and received the 2009 Global Risk Management Award from the Global Association of Risk Professionals.
