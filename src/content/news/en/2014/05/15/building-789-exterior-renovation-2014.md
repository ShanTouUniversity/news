Shantou University's Infrastructure Office awarded the exterior renovation of Building 789 to Shantou Jinhai Construction Co. for RMB 399,200. Work was scheduled to begin on May 16, 2014, and finish by June 24.

The project covers about 4,000 square meters of exterior wall columns and 2,000 square meters of corridor ceilings. Construction could cause noise, vibration and dust, and passersby were advised to take care near the work area.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
