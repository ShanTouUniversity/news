Shantou University announced the contract award for renovation of the School of Engineering's computer center. Shantou Longhua Construction General Company won the contract with a bid of RMB 1,138,759.51. The result was open for public notice from May 15 to 19, 2014.

The university's Infrastructure Office was the procuring entity. Concerns could be raised with the university's Supervision and Audit Office during the notice period.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office)
