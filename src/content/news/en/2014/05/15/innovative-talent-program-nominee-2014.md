It is reported that, in accordance with the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology's call for the 2014 Innovative Talent Promotion Program, and following recommendation by the STU Research Committee and expert review organized by the provincial department, Huang Xiaochun of Shantou University has been nominated for the Guangdong regional recommendation for the Ministry of Science and Technology's Innovative Talent Promotion Program in the category of Young and Middle-Aged Leading Scientists in Science and Technology Innovation. The university has now issued a public notice on the nominee as required.

## Notice Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Nominee | Huang Xiaochun |
| Program | MOST Innovative Talent Promotion Program — Young and Middle-Aged Leading Scientists in Sci-Tech Innovation (Guangdong) |
| Selection process | University research committee recommendation, followed by provincial expert review |
| Notice deadline | 12:00, May 19, 2014 |

Any organization or individual with differing opinions may submit written comments or contact the Research Office in person or by phone before the deadline. Submissions from organizations should bear an official seal, and individual submissions must include the person's real name. Contact: 86502813, Email: o_kyc@stu.edu.cn. Details of the nominee are available in the attachment.
