Shantou University's Youth League Committee organized themed activities across university branches from May to June 2014 under the theme “Gather Youthful Energy and Pursue New Dreams.” The program marked the 95th anniversary of the May Fourth Movement and the 92nd anniversary of the Communist Youth League. Units were encouraged to design activities around their disciplines and promote the “Three Going” initiative: leave the internet, leave dormitories and take part in sports.

College Youth League committees were asked to submit plans and summaries by May 27 and could nominate projects for university awards. The program offered two Outstanding Organization awards and six Outstanding Themed Activity awards, with a results-sharing event planned for early June.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ Nomination forms and project submission requirements are in the OA attachments.
