It is reported that the Department of Biology in the College of Science has announced the schedule for the 2014 undergraduate thesis defenses. The first round will be held in three groups on the afternoon of May 23, with a second round for outstanding theses scheduled for May 28.

## Defense Committee

The defense committee is chaired by Zhang Yueling, with members Chen Meizhen, Zheng Huaiping, Wei Chiju, and Liu Yang, and Yang Mulan serving as secretary.

## First-Round Defense Schedule

The first round is organized into three groups. Detailed grouping and thesis topics are available in the attachments.

| Group | Leader | Members | Secretary | Time | Venue |
|-------|--------|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| Group 1 | Liu Yang | Chen Meizhen, Chen Jiehui, Zhong Mingqi, Xie Liling, Yu Jie | Xiao Xiang | May 23, 14:00 (Fri) | Lecture Hall 2 |
| Group 2 | Zheng Huaiping | Mei Zhiping, Hu Zhong, Du Hong, Chen Zhikai, Chen Shanwen, Huang Tongwang | Lun Jingsheng | May 23, 15:00 (Fri) | Lecture Hall 1 |
| Group 3 | Wei Chiju | Liu Wenhua, Liu Zhu, Ding Lanping, Huang Bingxin | Li Ping | May 23, 14:30 (Fri) | Lecture Hall 3 |

## Second Round: Outstanding Thesis Defense

The second round, dedicated to outstanding theses, will be conducted by a panel comprising Zhang Yueling, Chen Meizhen, Wei Chiju, Zheng Huaiping, and Liu Yang, with Yang Mulan as secretary. It is scheduled for 14:30 on May 28 (Wednesday) in Room D-201.
