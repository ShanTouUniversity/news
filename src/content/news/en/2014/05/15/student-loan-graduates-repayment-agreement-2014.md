Shantou University's Student Affairs Office asked 2014 graduates with outstanding national student loan agreements to collect the required documents from its Student Aid Center in Administrative Center room 106 by May 30. They were then to visit the Bank of China Shantou Branch to sign a repayment agreement or arrange early repayment.

Required items included the loan contract, identification, a repayment passbook or debit card and a black pen. The OA attachments provide detailed requirements, bank hours and repayment guidance; students could also contact the Student Aid Center through its official channels.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Repayment guidance and related attachments are available on the OA system.
