Undergraduate civil engineering thesis defenses for the 2010 cohort in Shantou University's School of Engineering were scheduled in two sessions. Regular defenses were set for May 23, 14:30–18:00, in rooms G401, G404, D404 and D504. Students applying for honors were to attend a session on May 24, 08:30–12:00, in D504.

Each defense was allocated about 15 minutes for a presentation and questions. All committee members were to attend the honors session. Group assignments are listed in the OA attachment.
