The final rounds of Shantou University's 18th student debate tournament were scheduled for May 17 and 18. The Journalism and Communication and Liberal Arts teams were to contest third place at 19:30 on May 17 in the Moot Court, debating whether people are happier living for themselves or for others. The Science and Law teams were to compete for the title at 19:30 on May 18 in the Science and Technology Lecture Hall, debating whether opportunity or hard work matters more in life.

The tournament, themed “Reasoned Inquiry, Insightful Debate,” had run for about a month before the final matchups were set.
