It is reported that STU's Office of Academic Affairs has announced a make-up session for the course *Pedagogy* (class no. 66726) as part of its teaching schedule adjustment. Students enrolled in the course are reminded to note the time and venue and attend on time.

Details of the make-up session are as follows:

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Pedagogy |
| Class no. | 66726 |
| Make-up time | Friday of Week 12 (May 16), Periods A-B |
| Venue | Room E203 |

Students taking the course are advised to inform one another, plan ahead, and attend the make-up session at the scheduled time and venue to keep up with the course progress. For any questions regarding the arrangement, students may contact their college or the Office of Academic Affairs, and the official notice prevails.
