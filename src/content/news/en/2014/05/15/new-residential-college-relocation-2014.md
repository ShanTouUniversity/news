It is reported that the Resource Management Office issued a notice on May 15, 2014, announcing that the relocation of offices and shops formerly located on Block A of the Phase I New Residential College project has been completed.

## New Locations

| Former Location | New Location |
| --- | --- |
| Resource Management Office (Security Office, Key Projects Office, Infrastructure Office, Equipment Office, Logistics Office, General Office) | Siheyuan, West District |
| Student Affairs Office – Mutual Learning Center | First floor, Library |
| President’s Office Mail Room | 2nd floor, Building 8, 789 |
| Post Office | 1st floor, Building 8, 789 |
| Vegetable Market (Cailanzi) | Opposite the main entrance of the 4th Canteen |
| A-Bin Barbershop | East side of Building L, Postgraduate Dormitory |

Please be advised.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
