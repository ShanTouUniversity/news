Shantou University's Student Affairs Office and Snow Leopard Outdoor Association scheduled a briefing for the summer “Return to Shangri-La” intermediate outdoor program for May 18, 19:30–21:00, in lecture room E203. The program was planned for mid-August and emphasized public service and environmental protection, with student teams designing their own routes and itineraries.

Participants would go through team formation, fitness testing, proposal review, interviews and adaptive training. Further information was available through the association's official social media accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
