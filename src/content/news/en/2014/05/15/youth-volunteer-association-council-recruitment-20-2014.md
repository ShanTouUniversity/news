The Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association opened recruitment for its 20th council, planned to include five members: one president and four vice presidents. Candidates were expected to be committed to volunteer service, have organizational experience and be able to serve continuously for one academic year.

Applicants were asked to submit a candidate form and a work proposal of at least 1,000 Chinese characters by 22:00 on May 20. The process would include interviews followed by candidate presentations and an election at the transition meeting. Application materials are available on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ Candidate forms and related attachments are available on the OA system.
