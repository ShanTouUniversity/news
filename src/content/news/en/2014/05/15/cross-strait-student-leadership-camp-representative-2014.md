Shantou University selected an English major from its College of Liberal Arts to represent the university at a student leadership camp after reviewing eight nominees. The camp's theme was “Youth, Innovation and Development: Core Values and the Mission of Youth.” It was organized by the State Council Overseas Chinese Affairs Office and the China Overseas Exchange Association and hosted by Jinan University.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
