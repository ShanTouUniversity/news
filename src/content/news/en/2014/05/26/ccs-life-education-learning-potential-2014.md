Shantou University’s Christian Study Center of the College of Liberal Arts will host a lecture at 7:30 p.m. on May 29, 2014, in the Library Lecture Hall. Professor Cheng Zhengjia, an adjunct associate professor in the Department of Applied Social Sciences at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and director of its Manulife Centre for Children’s Learning Potential Development, will speak on “Rethinking Life Education and Children’s Learning Potential Development: Sharing 15 Years of Experience.”

Cheng has conducted research on specific learning differences, dyslexia, and the educational psychology of children and adolescents, and has participated in related teacher training. Drawing on his work with students with learning difficulties, the lecture will discuss children’s psychology, learning attitudes, active inquiry, and whole-person life education.
