It is reported that the dedicated online mock exam for the College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 (CET-4/6) at Shantou University opened on May 16 and will remain available until June 14, providing students with a pre-exam simulation and assessment service.

Students can log in to the designated website to take the mock exam. Upon submission, an intelligent computer scoring system immediately returns the total score for objective questions along with the percentile ranking among all participants nationwide, helping test-takers gauge their preparation level.

To encourage participation, the first 50 students to complete the mock exam may apply for a recorded systematic CET-4/6 tutoring course taught by instructors from a leading national English education institution. Eligible students should send an email from their registered address to the Student Learning Center at slc@stu.edu.cn before June 8.
