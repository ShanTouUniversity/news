Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) will hold an English CET-4 preparation sharing session at 3 p.m. on May 28, 2014, in the SLC lecture room. First-year students are the suggested audience.

The session will cover the CET-4 question format, last-minute preparation, test-taking techniques, examination reminders, and study resources. Student tutors with strong English examination experience will share their preparation advice; their names are rendered as Li LL and Ye SS in accordance with student privacy requirements.
