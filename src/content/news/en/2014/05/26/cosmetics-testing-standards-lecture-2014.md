Shantou University Science College will host a lecture titled “Introduction to Cosmetics Testing Standards” at 2:30 p.m. on May 28, 2014, in the Science College Lecture Hall (Science Building 352). Xu Ning, director of the Chemical Testing Laboratory at the Shantou Quality and Metrology Inspection Institute, will speak.

The lecture will introduce currently valid testing standards for cosmetics quality and explain methods by product type or testing item. Xu is a senior quality engineer who has worked in product quality testing and laboratory management for many years.
