Shantou University's School of Science opened recruitment for students at the main campus to join the Into the Ocean service course in the 2014–2015 academic year. The course was included in the university's community-service curriculum, and students who completed the course and passed its assessment could earn one credit. The summer course would also form part of an education exchange program between Hong Kong and the mainland, bringing STU students together with students from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University for ocean study and conservation activities.

The course offered a summer exchange class and a fall class. The summer class planned theoretical study for July 2–6 and field practice for July 7–11 at Dachen Bay in Raoping County, Chaozhou, a middle school in Nan'ao and the exhibition hall of the Nanpeng Islands National Nature Reserve. It offered 33 places. The fall class was scheduled for Friday afternoons during Weeks 2–7, with field activities to be completed before Week 12 at fishing villages and community sites in Dahao, Shantou, and offered 40 places.

Applications were due before midnight on May 31, with admission based on applications and interviews. The application form, course outline and related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
