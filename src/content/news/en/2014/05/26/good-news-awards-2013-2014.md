Shantou University’s Publicity Department and News Center launched the 2013-2014 Good News selection on May 26, 2014. Eligible works are text news and photographs published by university units on the campus portal or news website between June 1, 2013, and May 30, 2014. Works written or photographed by the News Center itself are excluded.

Text entries should be accurate, newsworthy, well structured, concise, and influential. Photographs should be accurate, clear, newsworthy, and captured on site; a single photograph or a set covering one news event may be submitted. Each unit may submit no more than five entries in each category. Correspondents must complete the registration form and email it to the News Center Office by May 30; late submissions will not be accepted.

| Category | First prize | Second prize | Third prize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text news | 3, CNY 300 each | 5, CNY 200 each | 10, CNY 100 each |
| Photos | 2, CNY 300 each | 3, CNY 200 each | 5, CNY 100 each |

Certificates and prize money will be awarded for all prizes. The registration form and related attachments are available on the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department and News Center)
> ⚠️ The registration form and event attachments are available on the OA system.
