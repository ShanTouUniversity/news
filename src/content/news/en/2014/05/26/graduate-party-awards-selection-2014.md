Shantou University Graduate School launched its 2013-2014 recommendation process for outstanding Communist Party members and outstanding party affairs workers on May 26, 2014, to recognize exemplary service and encourage positive models among graduate party organizations.

Eligible outstanding Communist Party members are full-time graduate student members whose party affiliation is held by the Graduate School; recommendations will be based on 6 percent of the full-time graduate student members in each branch. Outstanding party affairs workers will be selected from branch secretaries and committee members, with seven positions planned across the school. The two categories may not overlap.

Each graduate party branch will discuss recommendations through its committee and submit them to the Graduate School Party General Branch for review. After a public notice period without objection, the commendation decision will be issued. Branches must submit recommendation lists and application forms to Administrative Center 401 by June 5. The forms are available on the OA system.
