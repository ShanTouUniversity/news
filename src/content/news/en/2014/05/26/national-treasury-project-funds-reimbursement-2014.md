Shantou University’s Finance Office reported on May 26, 2014, that applications for the remaining balances of pre-2013 special projects paid through the national treasury system had arrived. Relevant staff members should visit the Finance Office and complete reimbursement procedures in accordance with university rules.

Under Guangdong Education Finance Circular [2010] No. 186, project funds must be used in the year a project is completed. Any balance remaining at the end of the year will be recovered by the provincial authority and will not be allocated again the following year.

> ⚠️ The relevant financial materials and attachment are available on the OA system.
