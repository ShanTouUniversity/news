Shantou University Student Affairs issued a Dragon Boat Festival holiday safety reminder on May 26, 2014. The university holiday falls on June 2 and connects with the weekend. In view of the flood season, students are advised to monitor weather alerts and guard against lightning, heavy rain, strong winds, flooding, landslides, and waterlogging.

## Travel and campus safety

Students should follow traffic regulations, avoid unlicensed or overloaded vehicles, and never drive after drinking. Travelers should choose regulated tour operators and restaurants with reliable food hygiene. Anyone taking part in hiking, climbing, or outdoor activities should follow local safety guidance, stay away from dangerous areas, protect valuables, and travel with companions where possible.

Students staying on campus should follow residence and safety rules, avoid accommodating visitors, and prevent strangers from entering residence halls. Open flames and high-power appliances such as immersion heaters are prohibited. Students should switch off power before leaving and keep fire exits clear. Swimming or fishing in the university reservoir or other dangerous waters is prohibited, as are fires, mountain trips with open flames, and barbecues in restricted areas.

Emergencies may be reported to the Student Community Management Service Center at 86502661 or Campus 110 at 82904110.
