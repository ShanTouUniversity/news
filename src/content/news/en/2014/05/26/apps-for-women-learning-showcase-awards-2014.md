The showcase and awards ceremony for the Apps for Women Learning Guangdong Student App Design and Development Competition was scheduled for 4:30–6:00 p.m. on May 28, 2014, in Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center at Shantou University. The event was organized by the STU Women's Studies Research Center with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation's China Women's Project. Five competition advisers and judges and approximately 45 students from 16 teams were expected to attend.

The competition focused on mobile applications designed for rural women and women in vulnerable situations, while encouraging cooperation among universities in Guangdong. The Sun Yat-sen University Gender Education Forum and the South China University of Technology Gender Studies Center joined as co-organizers. The showcase would present the entries and include the awards ceremony.
