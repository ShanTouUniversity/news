With approval from the university Party committee, Shantou University is conducting its 2014 party member review on the main campus from mid-May through late June. The review follows guidance issued by the Shantou municipal Party working committee.

Units must submit materials concerning members proposed for disciplinary action for university Party committee review by June 13. Review summaries and statistical forms are due to the Organization and United Front Work Department by June 20. Requirements and forms are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ Review documents and forms are available on the OA system.
