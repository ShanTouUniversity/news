The Shantou University union will hold chess and poker competitions for faculty and staff from May 29 to June 13. The poker preliminary round is scheduled for 8 to 10:30 p.m. on May 29, with the final at the same time on June 5, both on the union’s third floor. Chess matches will take place in the union meeting room on the second floor.

Faculty and staff wishing to participate must send the registration form to the official union email address by May 27, identifying chess or poker in the subject line. The registration form is available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Union)
> ⚠️ The competition registration form is available on the OA system.
