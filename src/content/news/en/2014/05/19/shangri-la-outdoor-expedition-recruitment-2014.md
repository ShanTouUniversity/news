Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office and Snow Leopard Outdoor Association have opened recruitment for the 2014 summer intermediate “Return to Shangri-La” outdoor program, planned for mid-August. The program will select 14 students and two faculty members and centers on public service and environmental protection, with student teams designing hiking routes and related activities.

Students apply in teams of five and go through fitness testing, proposal review, interviews and adaptive training. Recruitment primarily targets undergraduates in the 2012 and 2013 cohorts. Applications are due by midnight on May 27. Participants are expected to cover about RMB 600 each for insurance and food during implementation.

Application forms and selection details are available in the original OA notice. Personal student contacts and phone numbers in the notice are not reproduced.
