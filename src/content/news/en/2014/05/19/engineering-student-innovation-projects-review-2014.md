Shantou University’s Engineering School announced the initial review results for its 2014 student extracurricular science and technology innovation projects. The school’s expert panel reviewed 49 applications, with 45 passing the initial defense and receiving approval.

Four projects from 2013 that had requested delayed completion passed their final defenses, while three other projects requested extensions into the 2014 program’s midterm review. The approved teams will conduct research and development under faculty supervision. The full project list and review details are available in the original OA notice.
