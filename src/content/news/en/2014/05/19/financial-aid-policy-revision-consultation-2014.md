Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office is proposing revisions to the university’s provisional measures for identifying students with financial difficulties and has invited feedback from the campus community. The review reflects changes in circumstances and experience from recent years.

Comments were due by May 26 and could be delivered to the Student Aid Center in Room 106 of the New Administration Center or sent to the office’s official email address. The draft measures are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The draft measures are available on the OA system.
