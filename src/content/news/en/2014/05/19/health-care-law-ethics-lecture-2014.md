Shantou University’s Law School will host a lecture on legal and ethical issues in health care from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on May 27 in Room 213 of the Law School. The speaker, Professor Mary O’Keefe, is from the School of Nursing at the University of Texas Medical Branch. The event is open to the university community.

The lecture will address legal and ethical questions in health-care practice. Related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
