The Business School Youth League Committee at Shantou University will host a career experience-sharing session for the Class of 2014 from 3 to 5 p.m. on May 21 in E101. The program will cover job-search experiences, interview skills, experiences with different types of employers and overseas study, followed by a question-and-answer session.

Speakers come from international economics and trade, accounting and finance programs and have entered companies, banks, public institutions or overseas universities. The session is open to the university community.
