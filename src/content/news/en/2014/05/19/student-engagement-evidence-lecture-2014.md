Shantou University’s Higher Education Institute will host the second mentor lecture of 2014 from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on May 22 in Room G201. Associate Professor Wang Xueju will present empirical research on student engagement and discuss findings from STU’s participation in the 2012 NSSE-China survey.

NSSE-China was developed through a collaboration between Tsinghua University and Indiana University to assess student engagement in a way suited to Chinese higher education. The lecture is open to the university community.
