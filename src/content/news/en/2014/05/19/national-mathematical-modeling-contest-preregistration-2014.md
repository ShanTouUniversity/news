Shantou University’s mathematical modeling coaching team has opened pre-registration for the 2014 China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling. The opportunity is open to all undergraduates. Teams should have three members with complementary strengths in modeling, software programming and technical writing.

Pre-registration runs from May 20 to June 3. The coaching team plans to train applicants from August 20 to September 10 before selecting teams to represent the university. The national contest is scheduled to run from 8 a.m. on September 12 to 8 a.m. on September 15. The registration form and further details are available in the original OA notice.
