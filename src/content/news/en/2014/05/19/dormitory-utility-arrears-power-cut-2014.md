Shantou University has announced measures for student dormitories with unpaid utility bills. Starting May 20, dormitories that had not paid utility charges for April 2014 or earlier would be disconnected until their arrears were settled.

From May 20 to 22, the university would process two card-based debits daily, at 10:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m.; dormitories with successful payments would have power restored during the corresponding afternoon or evening window. From May 23 onward, one debit would be processed at 4:30 p.m. daily. Residents with insufficient balances should top up or complete bank-account transfers. Full payment instructions are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Facilities Management Office)
