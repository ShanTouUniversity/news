Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has opened a consultation on revisions to the university’s undergraduate scholarship policy. The policy had been in effect for four years since its 2010 revision, and the office is updating it in light of recent selection experience.

Comments were due by May 26 and could be delivered to the Student Aid Center in Room 106 of the New Administration Center or sent to the office’s official email address. The proposed revision is available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
