It is reported that the Integrated Thinking project of Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts is recruiting teaching assistants for small-group tutorials in the coming academic year. Current STU students and graduates with an interest in learning and teaching Integrated Thinking are invited to apply.

The “STU Integrated Thinking” course has been offered to first-year undergraduates since the fall semester of 2011. It combines large lectures with small-group tutorials, where teaching assistants help students practice thinking skills and develop productive habits of thought.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Current third-year or above undergraduates and graduates as of fall 2014 |
| Requirements | Interest in the course, strong communication and reasoning skills, and ability to work in a team |
| Preferred qualifications | Strong academic performance, successful completion of the course, or experience leading small-group seminars |
| Deadline | May 28, 2014 |
| Materials | Résumé, academic records, and other relevant materials |
| Contact | `zhenghesiwei@126.com`; use “STU Integrated Thinking Teaching Assistant” as the subject |

The project team will review applications and contact candidates selected for interviews.
