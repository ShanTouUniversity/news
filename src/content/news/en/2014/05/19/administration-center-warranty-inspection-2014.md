The renovation and refurbishment warranty for Shantou University’s New Administration Center will expire on June 18, 2014. The university’s major construction projects office and project coordination department will arrange a unified inspection and repair with the contractor, asking each user unit to check walls, floors, doors and windows, switches, furniture, air conditioners and lighting.

Units must record defects found during their inspections using the provided template and return the information to the Facilities Management Office by the end of May 23 so that repairs can be coordinated. The inspection form is available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Facilities Management Office)
> ⚠️ The defect inspection form is available on the OA system.
