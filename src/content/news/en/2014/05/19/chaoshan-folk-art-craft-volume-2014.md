It is reported that Shantou University Library has recommended *Chaoshan Folk Arts: Arts and Crafts*. The book uses a social and cultural history perspective, combining illustrated writing with a historical narrative to present the development and current condition of folk arts and crafts in the Chaoshan region.

## Key Features

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Examines Chaoshan folk crafts through religion, traditional architecture, food culture, finance and economics, and objects for appreciation and recreation |
| Perspective | Focuses on craft categories, individual works, their creators, and the communities behind them |
| Comparative scope | Discusses connections among crafts, the relationship between traditional crafts and modern industries, and the spread and localization of Chaoshan crafts |

The book also traces the historical development of different crafts and introduces researchers and scholarship related to Chaoshan culture. The library said the volume may serve as a reference for readers interested in Chaoshan folk arts and their social and cultural context.
