The 2013 China College Student Self-Improvement Star program, organized by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League of China and the All-China Students’ Federation, has announced its results. A student from the 2011 imaging class of Shantou University’s Medical School, nominated by STU, received a nomination award and RMB 2,000.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
