Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has outlined preparations for guided courses in the summer and fall 2014 semesters; public-service courses are handled separately. Academic units must submit proposed course information by May 23, create and publish guided class sections in the credit system by May 27, and create and publish department-arranged topics by June 6.

The office planned to issue course-selection instructions in week 16. Students must complete course selection by week 18, after which the system will close. The course information template is available in the original OA notice.

> ⚠️ The course information template is available on the OA system.
