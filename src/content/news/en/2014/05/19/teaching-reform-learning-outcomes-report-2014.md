It is reported that the Institute of Higher Education will hold its third 2014 mentor report from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on May 26, 2014, in Room G-201. Dr. Hu Wenlong will present on “Work-Process-Based Curriculum Development Through Learning Outcomes.”

The report is open to STU students and faculty and will discuss the relationship between learning outcomes and curriculum development.
