Shantou University’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center will hold a session on employment procedures for the Class of 2014 from 3 to 5 p.m. on May 21 in E201. The session will explain employment information reporting, personnel files, household registration, acceptance letters and deferred employment procedures.

Classes may collect questions and assign at least one class representative to attend. Other graduates with questions are also welcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
