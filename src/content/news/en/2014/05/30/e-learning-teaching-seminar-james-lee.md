Professor James Lee, dean of the School of Humanities and Social Science and Chair Professor of History and Sociology at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, will give a special seminar at Shantou University from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on June 4. The seminar will be held in Room E409 and focus on online teaching, blended learning, and flipped classrooms.

Lee will share HKUST’s experience in developing these teaching models and hold an open discussion with STU faculty, members of the E-Learning Teachers Initiative Group, and the university’s IT team. A specialist from HKUST Computer Science will also present recent work on online data-stream analytics and its integration with curricula and teaching plans. The exchange is expected to support future cooperation between the two universities in innovative teaching.
