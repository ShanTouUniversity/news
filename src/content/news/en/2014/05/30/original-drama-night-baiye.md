It is reported that the 13th Original Drama Night of the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University will be held this evening, May 30 (Friday), at 7:30 pm in the university auditorium. This year's production is the original stage play "Bai Ye" (White Night), and the event is open to all staff and students.

As one of the signature cultural events on campus, the Liberal Arts Original Drama Night has been staged for twelve consecutive editions prior to this year, with each production created and performed by students and enjoying strong popularity across the university.

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Event | 13th Original Drama Night of the College of Liberal Arts |
| Production | "Bai Ye" (White Night) |
| Time | May 30, 7:30 pm |
| Venue | University Auditorium |

The organizers noted that leaders, guests and teachers attending the performance will be seated in the middle sections of rows 6, 7 and 8.
