It is reported that Shantou University will hold its Admissions Consultation Day on June 15, 2014, to showcase the university's achievements, educational strengths, and academic programs to prospective students and the wider community. To support the event, the university is recruiting volunteers from across the student body.

## Scale and Service

A total of 172 volunteers are needed to assist departments with reception and services on the day of the event.

## Eligibility

- Strong communication skills and the ability to provide warm, attentive, and efficient reception services;
- Responsible, hardworking, and cooperative, with good teamwork and willingness to follow arrangements;
- Polite and well-mannered, able to represent STU students positively;
- Available to participate throughout the entire event day as required.

## How to Apply

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Application deadline | 12:00, June 6, 2014 |
| How to apply | Register online at http://activity.stu.edu.cn/ |
| Selection | Merit-based; where qualifications are equal, earlier applications are prioritized |
| Results | To be announced on the campus OA information website |

Applicants should indicate their preferred service unit and role in the "remarks" field; assignment details for each unit are listed in the attachment. Applicants who do not specify a preference will be allocated by the organizers. Contact information provided must be accurate.

## Other Notes

For registered volunteers, service hours will be recorded based on actual hours served on the day, in accordance with Article 15 of the STU Young Volunteers Registration Management Measures (Trial). Training time and venue will be announced separately on the campus OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
> ⚠️ The registration QR code and event attachments are available on the OA system.
