Following application screening and interviews, Shantou University’s Business School and English Language Center selected 52 students for a training class and mock competitions ahead of the Spring 2015 undergraduate international case competition. The training class will work toward forming a team to represent the Business School. Selected students must take BUS3186, Business Case Analysis, in the fall 2014 semester.

The school thanked students for their participation and encouraged those not selected to follow future case-competition opportunities organized by the Business School. The selected-student list is arranged by student number and is not reproduced here because it contains personal information.
