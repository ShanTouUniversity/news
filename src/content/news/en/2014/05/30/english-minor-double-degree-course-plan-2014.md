Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts has released the Fall 2014 course plan for the English minor and double-degree programs for students in the 2013 cohort. The plan includes the required courses [ENG1101A] Integrated English I and [ENG1201A] English Composition I, each offered in multiple sections. Credits, hours, and seat counts follow the published course plan.

The school said that students should consult the 2013 Cohort Academic Guide for the detailed plan. Full section numbers and enrollment arrangements are available in the attachment to the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Liberal Arts)
> ⚠️ The complete course plan and academic guide are available on the OA system.
