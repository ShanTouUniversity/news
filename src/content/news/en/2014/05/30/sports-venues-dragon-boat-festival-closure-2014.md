Shantou University’s Sports Department said that campus sports venues will be closed on Monday, June 2, because of the Dragon Boat Festival holiday. The closure covers the indoor badminton courts, indoor table-tennis courts, gym, gymnastics room, and tennis courts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
