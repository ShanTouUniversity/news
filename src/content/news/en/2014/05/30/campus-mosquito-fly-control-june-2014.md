Shantou University’s Resource Management Office will conduct mosquito and fly control work from June 2 to 8 in scheduled areas of the main campus, Longquan Garden, and the Continuing Education College. Students and staff are advised to take care around the relevant areas.

| Date | Work area |
| --- | --- |
| June 2 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, and student residences |
| June 3 | Medical Building, New Teaching Building, and student residences in Buildings G, H, I, and J |
| June 4 | Around the campus security office, campus gates and roads, and outside the New Library |
| June 5 | Second and Third Dining Halls and the old Administration Building |
| June 6 | Reservoir dam, A–B faculty residences, and Building 789 |
| June 7 | Longquan Garden and the Continuing Education College |
| June 8 | Affiliated Middle School, Affiliated Primary School, and the western quadrangle |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
