It is reported that the First Chaoshan Campus Lantern Riddle Contest — one of the student club project activities of Shantou University in the 2013-2014 spring semester — concluded recently. The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the university's Student Club Federation and the STU Lantern Riddle Enthusiasts Association jointly released the list of winners on May 30.

The contest featured two categories, individual written guessing and school team guessing, drawing participants from several schools across the Chaoshan region, with winners coming from four schools.

## Winners

### Individual Written Guessing

| Prize | Winner | School |
| ----- | ------ | ------ |
| First Prize | Chen DB | Shantou Private Guangsha School |
| Second Prize | Huang LQ | Hanshan Normal University |
| Third Prize | Wang WJ | Dahao Overseas Chinese Middle School |

### School Team Guessing

| Prize | Winning Team |
| ----- | ------------ |
| First Prize | "They Can Guess" — Hanshan Normal University |
| Second Prize | "Haojiang Squad" — Dahao Overseas Chinese Middle School |
| Third Prize | Shantou Private Guangsha School Team; STU "Zhaoxi Team" |

Notably, "Zhaoxi Team," one of the host university's entries, shared third prize in the team category with the Shantou Private Guangsha School team.
