It is reported that the Women’s Studies Center of Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts introduced its general education courses for the 2014–2015 summer and fall terms on May 30, 2014. Four courses were listed, covering technology and cities, social research, nonprofit management, and body and space.

The course “Social Research Methods and Women’s Studies” is designed to introduce social science research, women’s studies, and quantitative and qualitative methods, while developing students’ skills in research design, group learning, and academic presentation. “Nonprofit Management and Development” focuses on nonprofit-sector trends, management tools, regulatory mechanisms, project planning, and the relationship between nonprofit work and public policy.

“Technology and Cities” and “Body and Space” were also included in the course list. The OA notice provides course introductions, but some instructor information and course details were not fully rendered in the available text. Students should consult subsequent university course information for the complete arrangements.
