Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has announced the selection of 20 undergraduates for exchange study at Ningbo University in the fall 2014 semester. The selected students’ names are not reproduced here because the list contains student names and identification numbers.

The selected students must attend safety training, sign an undertaking, and collect departure forms at 4 p.m. on June 4 in Room D304. Except for 2013 students participating in military training, all other students should complete departure procedures after their final examinations and return the forms to the Academic Affairs Office. During the exchange period, students may not enroll in STU’s 2014–15 summer or fall courses other than the military-training course for 2013 students; any courses already selected must be dropped.

Exchange students must also complete dormitory check-out procedures. These include obtaining approval from a residential-college counselor, settling utility charges with the Water and Electricity Center, returning keys to the Student Community Property Management Center, and removing personal belongings. Counselor offices are located at B106, D103, E119, and G238. The full list and related forms are available in the original OA notice.
