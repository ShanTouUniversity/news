Shantou University’s Graduate School will hold a graduate public-welfare training camp on June 7 under the theme “Turning the Impossible into Possible: Everyone Can Become an Agent of Change.” About 30 participants are expected. The camp will introduce modern public-welfare and charity work, methods for designing community projects, and Robert’s Rules of Order for effective group discussion.

The full-day program has three sessions: an introduction to modern public welfare and charity from 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.; a discussion of modern public-welfare projects from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m.; and a session on community project design and group practice from 6:30 to 9:30 p.m. The speaker will be Hu Zhongwei.

Applicants must complete the registration form and submit it as required by 6:00 p.m. on June 4. The form format, submission instructions, and related attachments are available in the original OA notice.
