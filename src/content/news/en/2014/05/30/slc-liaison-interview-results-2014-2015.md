Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) has announced the results of its 2014–15 student liaison interviews. Eleven students were selected based on the position requirements and their interview performance. They came from the Schools of Engineering, Science, Law, Journalism, Arts, Liberal Arts, and Business; their names and individual majors are omitted here to protect student privacy.

The new liaisons will support academic services and information communication for their respective schools during the academic year.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
