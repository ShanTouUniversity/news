Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has announced the results of the 2014 Zhang Zhihuan Scholarship. Three science and engineering undergraduates were selected: one from the School of Science and two from the School of Engineering. Their names, years, and majors are omitted here to protect student privacy.

The scholarship was established by Professor Zhang Jianping of the U.S. National Science Foundation in memory of his father, Zhang Zhihuan. It aims to encourage outstanding students from Fengshun, Guangdong, to apply to STU and to support science and engineering students who demonstrate diligence and academic ambition.
