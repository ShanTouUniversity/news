The Center for Global Studies (CGS) in Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts is recruiting three student assistants. Applicants should have completed at least two CGS general-education courses, including the Integrative Thinking course and a CGS Independent Study Project, and should hold ELC Level 3 or an equivalent level of English proficiency.

The assistants will help international faculty with translation, communication, and other campus matters. They will also support CGS events, including orientation for the Integrative Thinking Program, international conferences and symposia, Open House, and lecture series, by receiving visiting scholars, arranging venues, and preparing promotional materials. The position requires two duty shifts per week, three to four hours per shift.

Applicants should complete the application form and email it to cgs@stu.edu.cn by June 7. The center will contact approved applicants to arrange interviews. Inquiries may be directed to 86504481 or 82904481.
