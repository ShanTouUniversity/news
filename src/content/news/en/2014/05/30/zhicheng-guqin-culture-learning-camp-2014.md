Zhicheng College will hold its eighth Guqin Culture Camp in Seminar Room 205A from June 30 to July 5, welcoming applications from students across Shantou University. Fifteen students will be accepted, including 10 Zhicheng College residents and five students from other residential colleges. Guqin performer Yao Liang will lead the camp, which aims to introduce students to guqin music, ritual and music culture, and related traditional culture.

The six-day camp will have two sessions each day, from 8:30 to 11:30 a.m. and from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. Topics include listening to and learning famous guqin pieces, playing techniques, guqin notation, guqin and ritual-and-music culture, and the instrument’s role in personal wellbeing. Yao Liang and the Shenzhen Chunfeng Guqin Society will cover most costs; admitted participants must pay a 100-yuan deposit and a 300-yuan program fee.

Applicants should be interested in guqin culture and available throughout the camp. Places will be assigned in order of registration, with applications closing at 5 p.m. on June 10. The registration form and submission email are provided in the original OA notice.
