The Shantou University General Office and Security Office said on May 30 that an online claim about a serious criminal incident on campus was untrue. The university said its security office had received no report of such an incident and that its investigation found no corresponding case on campus in recent days.

The university also explained that the police officers and vehicles seen on campus on May 29 were there for a safety inspection. It reminded students and staff to stay alert and contact campus security in an emergency. The campus 24-hour emergency number is 82904 110, and the Tuopu police station can be reached at 8254 0966.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office and Security Office)
