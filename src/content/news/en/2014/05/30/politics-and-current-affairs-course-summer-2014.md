Shantou University’s Department of Social Sciences will offer the one-credit theoretical course for Politics and Current Affairs Education in the 2014 summer semester. The course is intended for students in the 2013 cohort at the main campus, with 20 classes available.

Except for exchange students who cannot attend because of scheduling conflicts, students in the 2013 cohort are generally required to complete the course during the summer semester. Failure to take it may affect graduation arrangements. Class sections and enrollment information are provided in the attachments to the original OA notice.

The practical component carries a separate credit. Students are expected to study current-affairs materials during their second through fourth years and complete a current-affairs review during their fourth year under the guidance of an instructor. Enrollment and completion arrangements for the practical component will be announced in later semesters.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office and Department of Social Sciences)
