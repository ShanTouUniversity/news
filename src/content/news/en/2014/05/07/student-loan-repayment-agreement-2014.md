It is reported that Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will arrange for 2014 graduates with outstanding national student loans to sign repayment agreements with the Shantou branch of Bank of China on May 14, 2014. The session starts at 2:15 p.m. in E101. Participants should bring their loan contracts, identity documents, repayment passbooks and required copies, as well as a black pen.

Students will register and collect materials from 2:15 to 2:30 p.m.; Student Affairs staff will explain the repayment process and key points from 2:30 to 3:00 p.m. College groups will then attend in scheduled periods. Graduates who have already settled their loans do not need to sign an agreement, but must submit proof of settlement to the Student Loan Management Center for filing.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | From 2:15 p.m., May 14, 2014 |
| Venue | E101 |
| College sessions | 3:00–4:00 p.m.: College of Science, Law School, and College of Engineering; 4:00–5:00 p.m.: Business School, College of Liberal Arts, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, Cheung Kong School of Journalism, and Zhic­heng College |
| Required materials | Loan contract and specified copies, identity document and copy, repayment passbook and copy, linked-card account statement, and black pen |
| Enquiries | Student Loan Management Center, 0754-86502249, zjliu@stu.edu.cn |

Graduates unable to attend may collect the materials package from the Student Loan Management Center and complete the signing at the Personal Finance Department of Bank of China Shantou Branch. The detailed guide and participant list are available in the original OA attachments.
