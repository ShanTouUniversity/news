It is reported that Shantou University’s weekly leadership reception will take place at 3 p.m. on Thursday, May 8, 2014. Deputy Party Secretary Luo Nianchao will be on duty. Faculty members and students who wish to attend must make an appointment in advance by calling 8650 2306.
