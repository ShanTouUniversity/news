It is reported that Shantou University has announced the verification and payment schedule for April student-dormitory utility bills. The latest meter reading was taken on April 30, following the previous reading on March 31. Students may check their bills through the university utility-management system and submit correction requests to the Water and Electricity Center during working hours from May 7 to 13.

After the verification period, the Network Center will begin deducting the bills from campus cards on May 14. The regular payment period runs from May 14 to 19; overdue accounts will be handled under university rules on May 20. Students are advised to ensure that their campus cards have sufficient balance.
