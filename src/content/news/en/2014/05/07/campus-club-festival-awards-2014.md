It is reported that four STU organizations and one artistic program received honors at the sixth Shantou Campus Club Culture Festival, held under the theme “Chinese Dream, Youth Dream.”

The STU Youth League Committee received the Outstanding Organization Award. The Psychological Growth Association and Tea Ceremony Club were named Outstanding Student Clubs. The Dance Sport Association’s performance “Wu Zhi Yun” received an Outstanding Artistic Program award, while the Guitar Association was selected among the ten leading clubs in the festival’s online performance showcase.

## Awarded Entries

| Category | Recipient |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Organization Award | STU Youth League Committee |
| Outstanding Student Club | Psychological Growth Association; Tea Ceremony Club |
| Outstanding Artistic Program | “Wu Zhi Yun,” Dance Sport Association |
| Top Ten in online performance showcase | Guitar Association |
