It is reported that the “UFIDA Cup” Guangdong Collegiate Business Simulation Contest, part of the eighth Guangdong Science and Technology Festival for University Students, will be jointly organized by the Provincial Youth League Committee, education and science authorities, the Association for Science and Technology, and the provincial student federation. The STU Youth League Committee has forwarded the contest information and is collecting applications from interested teams.

Each university may register one team. Each team consists of five competitors and one or two supervising teachers. Campus applications must be sent to the STU Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by May 10. STU will recommend a team based on the applications received.

## Registration Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Contest | “UFIDA Cup” Guangdong Collegiate Business Simulation Contest |
| Festival | Eighth Guangdong Science and Technology Festival for University Students |
| Team structure | One team per university; five competitors and one or two supervising teachers |
| Campus deadline | May 10, 2014 |
| Application | Complete the form and send it to o_tw@stu.edu.cn |
| Recommending unit | STU Youth League Committee |
