It is reported that Shantou will hold an emergency-management and disaster-prevention awareness event on May 12, 2014, under the theme “Urbanization and Marine Disaster Reduction.” The STU College of Science is recruiting 25 to 30 volunteers from across the university to take part. The event will run from 9 a.m. to noon at Shantou People’s Square.

Interested students must register as instructed by emailing dolphinmbi@sina.com before noon on May 9. The activity aims to strengthen university students’ awareness of disaster prevention and reduction.
