It is reported that a lecture in the 2014 CCS "Whole Person Life Education" public lecture series will be held at 3:00 pm on Monday, May 12, at the Library Lecture Hall of Shantou University. Supported by the Hong Kong Christian Study Centre and organized by the Centre for Christian Studies of the College of Liberal Arts, the session will feature Professor Wu Fei speaking on the topic "Funeral Rites in Modern China."

As the Book of Rites states, "Rites begin with capping and marriage, and are most solemn in funerals and sacrifices." In traditional China, funeral rites and mourning attire constituted the most important ritual system. Over the past century, the reform of funeral rites has been a significant part of China's modernization, and the problems emerging from this reform reflect deeper issues in the way modern Chinese people live. The various developments surrounding funeral practices since the restoration of the Qingming Festival show that the public has begun to pay renewed attention to this issue.

Professor Wu Fei has devoted close attention in recent years to funeral rites and their place in modern China. In this lecture, he will analyze the various problems of contemporary funerals and trace their connections with traditional funeral rites.
