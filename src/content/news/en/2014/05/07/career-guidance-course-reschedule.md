It is reported that due to an instructor's business trip, one session of the Career Guidance course at Shantou University (Class No. 68065, taught by Su Junzhi and Zhu Guang), originally scheduled for Friday of Week 11 (May 9), Periods 3-4 in Room G403, will be suspended.

A makeup session has been arranged as follows:

| Item | Date | Periods | Venue |
| ---- | ---- | ------- | ----- |
| Suspension | Friday of Week 11 (May 9) | 3-4 | Room G403 |
| Makeup class | Friday of Week 13 (May 23) | 3-4 | Room G403 |

The Student Affairs Office has reminded students enrolled in the course to take note of the adjusted schedule and inform one another.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
