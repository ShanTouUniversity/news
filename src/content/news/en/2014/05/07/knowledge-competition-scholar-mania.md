It is reported that the STU Youth League Committee and the STU Youth newspaper editorial office will hold the “Top Student, Crazy Knowledge” competition for students across the university. Preliminary rounds will take place from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on May 10, 2014, in E201. The final is scheduled for 2:30–4:00 p.m. on May 18, with the venue to be announced.

Students may form teams of up to three members. The preliminary round will use required-answer and buzzer rounds to select five finalist teams. Multiple final rounds will determine one winner each for first through fifth prizes. Registration closes at 6 p.m. on May 9.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
