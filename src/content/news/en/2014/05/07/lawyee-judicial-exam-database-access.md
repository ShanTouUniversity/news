It is reported that the Shantou University Library has opened the Lawyee online judicial examination system for university users. The service is designed for judicial-examination preparation and provides mock exams, an examination syllabus, essential regulations, and legal documents, with access across the different modules.

The university has received 500 online accounts, numbered stu001 through stu500. The service is available at http://sk.lawyee.org/Default.aspx; users may follow the access instructions in the notice.
