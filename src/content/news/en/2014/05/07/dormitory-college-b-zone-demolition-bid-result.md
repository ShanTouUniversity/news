It is reported that the Shantou University bidding and procurement leading group has completed its review of the retaining-wall removal and soil-hauling project in Zone B of Dormitory College Phase I. Shantou Longhua Construction General Company was selected, with a contract value of RMB 34,950.

The bid result was posted from May 7 to May 10, 2014. Complaints are handled by the university’s Office of Supervision and Audit.
