Shantou University’s Party Standing Committee confirmed the administrative ranks of three senior management staff after their probationary appointments ended and they completed performance reviews, democratic evaluations, and organizational assessments, according to a notice issued on May 7, 2014.

Wen Ruifeng was assigned division-level rank, while Fan Jibin and Huang Yixiong were assigned deputy-division-level rank. The effective dates were April 2013 for Wen and Fan, and May 2013 for Huang. The decision was made at the committee’s fourth meeting of 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Committee)
