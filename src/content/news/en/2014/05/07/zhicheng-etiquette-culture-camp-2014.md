It is reported that Zhicheng College issued a notice on May 7, 2014, to hold the 3rd Etiquette Culture Camp on May 17.

The camp aims to deepen understanding of Chinese and Western etiquette, improve cultural literacy and personal temperament, and foster awareness of public morality.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 08:00–18:00, May 17, 2014 |
| Content | *Chang Li Ju Yao*, Chinese etiquette culture, keys to etiquette learning, etiquette in personal/family/social relations, social details and daily etiquette (communication, image building, dining etiquette) |
| Format | Lectures, expert videos and group discussions, combining self-study and collective learning |
| Eligibility | Students of Zhicheng College and other residential areas (voluntary) |
| Places | 32 (26 from Zhicheng, 6 from other areas, first-come, first-served) |
| Requirements | Modest and eager to learn, able to focus, team spirit, compliance with arrangements, no conflicting courses or activities during the camp |
| Deadline | 17:00, May 13, 2014 |
| How to apply | Log in to the booking system at http://booking.stu.edu.cn and fill in the application |
| Issued by | Zhicheng College |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Zhicheng College)
