It is reported that the College of Science’s Science Salon will host a lecture at 3 p.m. on May 9, 2014, in the Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center Lecture Hall. Chen Zhiying, a research professor and doctoral supervisor at the Chinese Academy of Sciences Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, will speak on “Minicircle DNA Vectors for Cancer Immunotherapy and Gene Therapy.”

The lecture will introduce minicircle DNA vector technology, recent developments in cancer immunotherapy and gene therapy, and plans for minicircle-DNA-based cancer treatment. Chen is director of the institute’s Gene and Cell Therapy Research Laboratory and has worked extensively on gene vectors and gene therapy.
