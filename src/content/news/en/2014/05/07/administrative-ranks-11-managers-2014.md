Shantou University’s Party Standing Committee confirmed deputy section-level administrative rank for 11 mid-level management staff after their probationary appointments ended and they completed performance reviews, democratic evaluations, and organizational assessments. The university’s Organization and United Front Work Department issued the decision on May 7, 2014.

The ranks took effect in three groups: five in November 2012, four in December 2012, and two in May 2013. The staff names are omitted from this report.

> Source: STU OA Notice (CPC Shantou University Organization and United Front Work Department)
