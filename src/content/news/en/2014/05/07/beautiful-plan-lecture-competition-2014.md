It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on May 7, 2014, to hold the “Beautiful Plan” series lecture and makeup competition under the Student Community X-Project “Voices of Associations.”

The program aims to enrich dormitory life, vitalize community atmosphere, improve aesthetic cultivation and provide a platform for sharing beauty information, fostering a harmonious and vibrant community.

## Schedule

### I. Beautiful Plan Lecture

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Sharing Beauty, Conveying Beauty |
| Time | 19:00, May 10, 2014 |
| Venue | Room E301 |
| Speaker | Chen Weisi, anchor of the current affairs program *Today’s Focus* |
| Content | Makeup (light/dramatic/cosmetics), etiquette, skincare, weight management, formal dress, hairstyles, etc. |
| Registration | Via SMS to the designated contact with dorm, name and contact details (see OA notice and attachments) |

### II. Beautiful Plan Competition

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Pure Heart Reflects Jade, Embracing Beauty Together |
| Time | May 18, 2014 |
| Venue | Jin San Jiao |
| Format | Round 1: professional makeup; Round 2: makeup theme by lottery; content may be adjusted based on the lecture |
| Scoring | 70% judges’ score + 30% audience votes; top three receive first, second and third prizes |
| Registration deadline | 24:00, May 17, 2014 |
| How to apply | Via SMS or by completing the application form and sending it to the designated email addresses (see OA attachments) |

For inquiries, contact the designated organizer. The application form and contact details are available in the OA attachments.

> ⚠️ The registration contacts and application form are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office)
