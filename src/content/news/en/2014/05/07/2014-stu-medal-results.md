Shantou University announced the 2014 Li Ka Shing Foundation Shantou University Medal results on May 7. The award recognizes outstanding graduating students. Candidates were recommended by their schools or residential colleges and reviewed by the medal selection committee.

One student received the medal and a RMB 10,000 grant from the Li Ka Shing Foundation. Six students received nomination awards, each carrying RMB 2,000. Student names, programs, and year groups are omitted to protect privacy.
