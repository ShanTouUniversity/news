It is reported that Shantou University’s Tea Society will hold its fifth Tea Culture Festival from May 21 to June 1. Using tea culture as a platform, the festival will introduce Chaoshan traditions and Chinese tea culture to members of the university and the wider tea-loving community. It is hosted by the STU College of Engineering Youth League Committee and organized by the STU Tea Society.

## Program

| Event | Time and venue | Program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Tea Brings Us Together” themed gathering | 19:30–21:30, May 21, Coffee Bookstore | Tea-culture talks, tea tasting, and an English-language exchange |
| Opening event | 11:00–13:00, May 24, Golden Triangle and Room D Activity Room | Riddles with tea prizes, porcelain painting, a milk-tea workshop, fruit-tea DIY, gongfu tea, and talks by authors |
| Tea-culture poster exhibition | One week during the festival | Posters at teaching buildings, residential areas, dining halls, and the library |
| Closing ceremony and exchange gala | 19:30–21:30, June 1, Science and Technology Report Hall | Performances and displays by STU clubs and students from the University of Calgary |

The opening event will feature talks on books including *Tea Fortune*, *Learning About Phoenix Tea*, and *Impressions of Chaoshan*. The festival is open to STU students and staff, students from the University of Calgary, and tea enthusiasts from outside the university; capacity for the themed gathering is limited.
