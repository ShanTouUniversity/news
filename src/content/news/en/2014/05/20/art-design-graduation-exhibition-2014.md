It is reported that Shantou University’s College of Art and Design will hold its 2014 undergraduate graduation exhibition from May 23 to 30 at the Hanyuan Exhibition Hall of Longhu Exhibition Hall, No. 4 Dabieshan Road, Shantou. The exhibition is organized by the College of Art and Design and is open to the public.

The exhibition will feature undergraduate graduation work in visual communication design, interior design, architecture and landscape design, interface and interaction design, video and animation design, product design, public art, and the creative industries.
