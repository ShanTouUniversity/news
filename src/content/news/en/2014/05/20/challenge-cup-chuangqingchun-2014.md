It is reported that the 2014 “Challenge Cup · Chuang Qingchun” Guangdong University Student Entrepreneurship Competition, jointly organized by the Provincial Youth League Committee, Department of Education, Department of Human Resources and Social Security, Department of Science and Technology, Association for Science and Technology and Student Federation, has concluded. Shantou University recommended 17 entries, with 10 winning awards after expert review.

## Awards

| Award | Projects |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding Organization Award | Shantou University |
| Gold (1) | Business Plan for Original Nest Honey |
| Silver (3) | Market Planning and Feasibility Study for a New Environmentally Friendly Integrated Insulation Wall; Shu Yu Xuan; Revitalization Plan for Chao Embroidery Public Welfare Organization |
| Bronze (6) | High-Efficiency Three-Way Catalytic Purifier; VHotel; Chaozhou Souvenir Development and Online Sales; PeerTrip.com; Lei Li Bee Action; Ningyang Angel Plan |

Team members and supervisors are listed in the original OA notice. Student names are withheld here for privacy; supervisors are faculty members. Some names were garbled in the original notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
