It is reported that Shantou University’s Science College will hold the 14th session of its “Career Conversations” workshop on May 23 and 24. The two sessions will focus on employment and graduate study and are intended for second- and third-year undergraduates in the college.

## Schedule

| Session | Time and venue | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Career session | 19:30–21:30, May 23, New Library Seminar Room 30A | Résumés, written tests and interviews, job-hunting experiences, and discussion |
| Graduate-study session | 09:30–11:30, May 24, New Library Seminar Room 30B | Graduate-school preparation and interviews, recommendation-based admission, overseas study, and discussion |

The career session will feature Jiang G, Gao S, Zou HB, and Chen Y, 2010-level students from mathematics, physics, and biology who will share experiences from employment and related fields. The graduate-study session will feature Zeng SJ, Li BB, Li YJ, Pi YH, and Chen SM from mathematics, physics, chemistry, and biology, discussing postgraduate preparation and study experiences.

The event is organized by the Science College Youth League Committee.
