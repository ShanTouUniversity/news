It is reported that the College of Engineering will hold the 184th Engineering Research Academic Forum on May 23, 2014, featuring Prof. Ma Hongwei.

## Forum Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Advances in Structural Health Monitoring |
| Speaker | Prof. Ma Hongwei, Changjiang Scholar Distinguished Professor, School of Science and Engineering, Jinan University, doctoral supervisor |
| Time | 10:00–12:00, May 23, 2014 |
| Venue | Academic Exchange Room, College of Engineering (Gongnan 312) |
| Organizer | College of Engineering |

Faculty, graduate and undergraduate students of the College of Engineering and other interested teachers and students are welcome. A speaker profile is in the OA attachment.
