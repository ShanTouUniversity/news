Shantou University has established a leading group for a special self-inspection of the university’s management of official hospitality and transportation funds, following requirements from the Guangdong education authorities.

Luo Nianchao serves as chair, with Sun Fenqiang, Zou Zhibo, Liao Xuequan, Chen Shaogang and Chen Zhongsong as members. The group’s office is located in the Supervision and Audit Office, which will coordinate the related work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
