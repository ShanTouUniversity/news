It is reported that Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold the fifth session of its Peer Growth Program on May 24. Titled “How Can a Dual Degree Enrich Campus Life?”, the session will invite two dual-degree students to discuss balancing academic pressure, campus activities, and overall development. All current students may attend.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, May 24 |
| Venue | Student Activity Room 726, Building 789 |
| Organizer | Student Affairs Office |
| Co-organizer | Student Growth Navigation Society |
| Capacity | 25 participants |

The speakers, Cheng RY and Chen YW, are studying law and electronic engineering respectively while pursuing a business administration dual degree. They have also received academic scholarships and taken part in campus service and case-related activities. Registration closes at 17:00 on May 23; if applications exceed capacity, places will be determined by drawing lots.
