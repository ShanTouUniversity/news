It is reported that Shantou University’s Business School will hold its ninth academic lecture of 2014 on May 22. The event will feature presentations on business operations, energy efficiency, wealth distribution, and agent-based macroeconomic modeling, and is open to the university community.

## Lecture Program

| Speaker | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| Li Jinming | Operating Models of Taiwanese Businesses in Mainland China |
| Zheng Muqiang | Energy Efficiency and Energy Strategy in the Five ASEAN Countries |
| Chen Siyan | Why the Rich Get Richer: An Explanation Based on an Individual-Based Model |
| Saul Desiderio | Monetary and Fiscal Policy in an Agent-Based Macro Model with Financial Frictions |

The lecture will take place from 14:30 to 17:40 on Thursday, May 22, in the Business School’s second-floor seminar room.
