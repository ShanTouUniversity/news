It is reported that Shantou University has opened its 2014 Zhang Zhihuan Scholarship selection for eligible undergraduates from the Science College and the College of Engineering who are from Fengshun, Guangdong. The scholarship was established by Professor Zhang Jianping in his father Zhang Zhihuan’s name to encourage strong students from Fengshun to apply to STU and to support academic effort and initiative among current students.

The selection will consider applicants’ conduct, compliance with university rules, academic performance, and demonstrated research and innovation ability. Each recipient will receive RMB 2,000.

Eligible students must submit applications to their college Youth League Committee by Tuesday, May 27. After preliminary review, the colleges will send recommended names and materials to the Student Affairs Office’s financial-aid center by May 29.

> ⚠️ The scholarship application attachments are available on the OA system.
