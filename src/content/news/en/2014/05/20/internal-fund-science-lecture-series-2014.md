It is reported that Shantou University’s Research Office will organize a science lecture series for internal fund projects from May 20 to June 10. The series is intended for project leaders, coordinators, project members, their graduate students, and faculty and students in related disciplines, with the aim of promoting research exchange and innovation.

The program is organized under the university’s management requirements for academic innovation teams, national-fund cultivation projects, youth research funds, and research start-up funds. Each session will combine a project progress report with questions and discussion and is expected to last 30 to 45 minutes.
