It is reported that the Science College at Shantou University has opened applications for its 2014 extracurricular academic and science projects. The program is open to all undergraduate students in the college. Projects must be completed by students in their spare time and must be separate from scheduled coursework; assignments, graduation theses, graduation designs, and term papers are excluded.

## Application Scope

Students may propose social science surveys or academic papers, academic papers in science and engineering-related fields, inventions, literary or artistic works, and business plans. Project members must have defined responsibilities, and each student may join only one project. Applicants may choose their own topics and seek a faculty adviser, or select from topics offered by faculty; each adviser should generally supervise no more than two projects.

Applicants must complete the university’s extracurricular academic and science research project form. Two paper copies are due at the Science College Youth League office by 17:30 on May 26, with an electronic copy sent to `o_lxytw@stu.edu.cn`. The college will arrange a faculty review of the proposals separately.
