It is reported that the School of Science Admission and Employment Committee issued a notice on May 20, 2014, to recruit volunteers for Gaokao admission counseling in Zhanjiang and Maoming.

To enhance outreach and attract outstanding applicants, the School of Science will organize counseling activities on Gaokao application during late June in the two cities.

## Recruitment

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Current undergraduates and postgraduates whose hometown is Zhanjiang or Maoming |
| Service | Return to hometown in late June to provide counseling on STU and admission information |
| Requirements | Strong identification with the university, good communication skills, willingness to undergo selection and training and follow unified arrangements |
| Support | Volunteer service counted as social practice, with transportation and meal subsidies as needed |
| Deadline | May 31, 2014 |
| How to apply | Download and complete the application form and send it to ltzhang@stu.edu.cn with subject “Admission Counseling Volunteer Application”; name the file as “College + High School + Name” |
| Contact | Youth League Committee of the School of Science, Mr. Zhang, phone 86502779 |
| Organizer | School of Science Admission and Employment Committee |

Details are available in the OA attachment.
