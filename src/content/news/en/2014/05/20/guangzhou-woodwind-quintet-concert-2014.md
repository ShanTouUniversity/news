It is reported that the Guangzhou Symphony Woodwind Quintet will perform at the STU University Auditorium at 19:30 on May 25. The ensemble consists of five full-time Guangzhou Symphony Orchestra musicians: Wang Mingzhu, Cornelius Fink, Feng Chuxing, Ge Weinuo, and Fu Lei. Each serves as a principal or associate principal in the orchestra.

Founded in 2004, the ensemble has presented chamber-music concerts and released two CDs, *Original Woodwind Quintet* and *The Great Gate of Kiev*. The program includes the overture to *The Barber of Seville* and a G-minor woodwind quintet.
