Shantou University has formed a special inspection group in response to Guangdong education authorities’ requirements for reviewing implementation of the 12th Five-Year Plan for education auditing.

Luo Nianchao serves as chair, with Sun Fenqiang, Wu Ping, Zou Zhibo and Chen Zhongsong as members. The group will conduct the university’s special inspection of implementation of the education audit plan.
