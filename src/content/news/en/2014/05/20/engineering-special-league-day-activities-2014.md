It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University’s College of Engineering will organize a series of special activities in May. The program responds to the university Youth League’s call to promote positive campus energy and encourages students to take part in extracurricular sports.

## Program

| Activity | Time and venue | Program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Back-to-Childhood Fun Sports Day | May 25, STU track and field stadium | Kites, rope skipping, beanbag games, handkerchief games, and other activities |
| “For You, Class of 2014” | May 16–30, STU | Youth League branches record videos of campus life for incoming 2014 students |
| Youth Unlimited: Activity Challenge | May, STU | Team challenges to promote cooperation among Youth League branches |
| Electronic Engineering Badminton Tournament | May, STU gymnasium | Exchange among branches and development of teamwork and sportsmanship |

The college will also organize a video-sharing and awards event. 2014 student orientation advisers and Youth League teachers will serve as judges, and outstanding works will be selected by year level. Details will be updated through the college Youth League Committee’s official social-media channels.

> ⚠️ Activity posters and other attachments are available on the OA system.
