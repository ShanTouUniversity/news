It is reported that Shantou University will hold its first Chaoshan Campus Lantern Riddle Conference from 14:00 to 17:00 on May 24 in Room E-206. The event is part of the university’s student-club project program for the spring semester of the 2013–14 academic year and aims to promote Chinese lantern-riddle culture on campus.

Riddle teams from colleges and secondary schools in the Chaoshan region will be invited to participate. Established lantern-riddle experts will serve as judges and offer commentary. The event is jointly organized by the STU Youth League Committee, the STU Student Club Federation, and the STU Lantern Riddle Society, and is open to the university community.
