It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial College Student Affairs Professional Committee has announced the results of its second selection of exemplary student-affairs programs and its 2013 selection of outstanding student-affairs teams. Three Shantou University projects or teams received recognition.

Zhicheng College’s “ATP Team Quality Development Camp” and the Medical College’s “Campus Student Health Ambassadors” were named exemplary programs in the second provincial selection. The Mutual Learning Center team was named an outstanding student-affairs team for 2013.

The selection was organized by the Guangdong Provincial College Student Affairs Professional Committee to share practical approaches to student affairs management. The recognized units are expected to continue developing student work and offer examples for other teams.
