It is reported that, as part of Shantou University's 2014 undergraduate admissions work, the College of Engineering is recruiting admission consultation volunteers from among all students whose hometowns lie in five cities — Jieyang, Huizhou, Dongguan, Shanwei and Shenzhen — to provide prospective students and parents with guidance on college application choices.

## Recruitment Overview

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Eligibility | Undergraduate and graduate students from Jieyang (including Puning City, Jiedong County, Jiexi County, Huilai County), Huizhou (including Huidong County, Huiyang District, Boluo County, Longmen County), Dongguan, Shanwei (including Haifeng County, Lufeng City, Luhe County) and Shenzhen |
| Service Period | Tentatively June 26–28, depending on when each school's graduates return to campus |
| Application Deadline | June 4, 2014 |
| Training | Group training in mid-June |

## Duties and Support

According to the report, volunteers will assist teachers dispatched by the college with consultation activities, introducing the university's admissions policies to candidates and parents and answering their questions. Applicants must be formally enrolled students who identify with STU's educational philosophy, know the university and their own college well, and demonstrate strong communication and teamwork skills.

The university will provide centralized training, cover certain transportation and meal expenses for volunteers returning to their alma maters, and honor outstanding individuals with awards.

Applications may be submitted individually or as a team (team applications must name a leader). The application form should be emailed to ydshen@stu.edu.cn by June 4, with the subject line "高考招生咨询志愿者报名" (Admission Consultation Volunteer Application) and the attachment named in the format "College + High School + Name." Contact: Teacher Shen, office phone 86502986.
