Shantou University Business School and Bank of China Shantou Branch are recruiting 10 full-time undergraduate and postgraduate students from the Business School for the eighth talent-reserve internship batch. The internship will run from July 1 to August 31, 2014, at the branch headquarters and subordinate offices.

## Internship Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Duties | Organizing, entering and maintaining non-confidential materials |
| Hours | Weekdays, 08:30–12:00 and 14:00–17:30 |
| Allowance | RMB 40 per day, with tax withheld as required |
| Other benefits | Lunch and group personal accident insurance |
| Deadline | 17:00, June 10 |
| Email | btsu@stu.edu.cn |
| Telephone | 82902725 |
| Orientation | 15:00, June 12, Business School Seminar Room |

Interns must follow the bank’s management, labor-discipline and confidentiality requirements and complete pre-service, in-service and performance assessments. Strong performers may enter Bank of China Shantou Branch’s reserve talent pool for campus recruitment. The registration form and related attachments are available on the OA system.
