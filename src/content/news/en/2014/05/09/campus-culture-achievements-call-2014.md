Shantou University’s Publicity Department opened a call on May 9, 2014, for campus culture projects in response to a Guangdong Provincial Department of Education competition for university achievements. Main-campus units were asked to submit two printed copies of project papers to Room 315 of the New Administration Center and email an electronic copy by June 13. The university would select submissions for provincial nomination.

Eligibility, formatting, and other application requirements are set out in the provincial notice attached to the OA record.
