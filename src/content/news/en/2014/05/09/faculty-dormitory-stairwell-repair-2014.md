Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office planned repairs to stairwell B11 in a faculty dormitory. Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Co., Ltd. was contracted to repaint the walls and ceiling, install aluminum windows and replace lighting fixtures. The contract value was RMB 13,900.

Work was scheduled to begin on May 9, 2014, and finish by June 7. The project could generate noise, vibration and dust. Pedestrians were advised to take care, and residents were asked to avoid lingering near the work area. Lu Chunxiong was named project lead and Chen Jinwu the site manager.
