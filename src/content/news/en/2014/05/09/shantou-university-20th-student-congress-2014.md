The Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union of Shantou University announced that the university’s 20th Student Congress was planned for early June 2014. The congress was to review work since the 19th congress, set priorities for the next stage and elect the 20th Student Union Committee and presidium.

The plan called for 189 official delegates and five to 10 non-voting attendees. Units were to elect their delegates through democratic procedures and submit the names by May 16. The 20th Student Union Committee was to have 13 members, while the presidium was to include one chair and four vice-chairs. Presidium candidates would register as teams, undergo review by the congress secretariat and stand for election at the congress; committee members would also be elected there.

Delegate allocations and detailed eligibility requirements are in the OA attachments.
