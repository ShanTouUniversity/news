Shantou University’s Business School scheduled its 125th Economics Salon for 10:00–11:30 a.m. on May 10, 2014, in the Library Lecture Hall. Economist Guan Yixin was to speak on “Building a Version 2.0 Financial Development Model: Initial Thoughts on Hot Topics in China’s Financial Reform and Innovation.”

Guan’s work has covered economics, strategic management and corporate culture. He previously served as president of the China Academy of Decision Sciences and vice president of the China Society of Development Strategy Studies, and has held adjunct professorships and doctoral-supervisor appointments at several universities.
