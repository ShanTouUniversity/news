It is reported that the preparatory committee of the 20th Student Congress of Shantou University has launched a campus-wide, team-based selection for the next Student Union presidium. The presidium consists of one president and four vice presidents, one of whom will concurrently serve as the head of the university's Student Societies Federation.

## Election Format

Under the principle of democratic centralism, all enrolled students who meet candidate requirements may form teams and apply. The secretariat of the Student Congress will organize assessments to shortlist at least three candidate teams and, with approval from the university Party committee, submit them to the congress for election. The president and three vice presidents compete as a four-member team, while the vice president overseeing student societies is nominated through the federation's own leadership election — four nominees join the candidate teams by mutual selection, forming five-member teams.

## Requirements and Application Materials

In addition to meeting delegate requirements and the criteria in the STU Student Cadre Regulations, candidates should be innovative, committed to student union work, experienced in student affairs, and capable of serving no less than one consecutive year.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Presidium structure | 1 president + 4 vice presidents (1 concurrently heads the societies federation) |
| Team size | 4 members (5 after the society vice president joins) |
| Candidate teams | At least 3 |
| Material deadline | 6:00 PM, May 16 |
| Submission venue | Student Congress Secretariat (Room 724, Building 789) |

Applicants must submit a candidate registration form, the team's work plan, and academic transcripts certified by their college. Bound hard copies should be delivered to the secretariat, with electronic versions sent to the designated email address. With Party committee approval, the final presidium will be elected by student representatives.
