The Guangdong Education Science Planning Office invited proposals for major education research topics for 2014, focusing on deeper reform in education. Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the call and coordinated campus submissions; the university could submit up to two proposals.

Proposals were expected to address issues grounded in Guangdong’s education reform, set clear research goals and priorities, and avoid duplicating already funded projects. Each submission needed an approximately 300-character summary of its objectives, expected outcomes and key research questions. Campus units were asked to email proposals and summaries to the Research Office at yczhuo@stu.edu.cn by May 15, 2014.
