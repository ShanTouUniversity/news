Shantou University’s College of Science scheduled a lecture on food safety for 2:30 p.m. on May 14, 2014, in Room E305. Yao Qingwei, chief engineer at the Shantou Institute of Quality and Metrology, was set to discuss food safety regulations and hazard analysis.

Yao has long worked in food and chemical product inspection and standardization and serves in several professional roles related to food safety and standards. The lecture was open to the university community.
