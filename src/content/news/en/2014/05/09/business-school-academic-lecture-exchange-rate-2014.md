Shantou University’s Business School scheduled its seventh academic lecture of 2014 for 9:30–11 a.m. on Wednesday, May 14, in its academic seminar room on the second floor of the Wenxi Building. Chen Ting, a doctoral student at Xiamen University’s School of Economics, was to speak on exchange-rate changes and firms’ export behavior. Associate Professor Zheng Muqiang was listed as the moderator.

The lecture was to discuss research using matched data from industrial enterprise and customs databases for 2000–2006 to examine how exchange-rate changes relate to export behavior across firms. Chen’s research interests include international finance and trade.
