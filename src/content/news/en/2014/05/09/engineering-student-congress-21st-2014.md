The 21st Student Congress of Shantou University’s School of Engineering was scheduled for 7:30 p.m. on May 11, 2014, in E-lecture Hall 101. The congress was to review the work of the 20th Student Union Executive Committee, elect the 21st committee and discuss its future priorities.

The congress was to have 104 student representatives, equivalent to about 10 percent of the school’s undergraduate enrollment. Representatives were to be elected democratically at class meetings.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Preparatory Committee for the 21st Student Congress of the School of Engineering)
