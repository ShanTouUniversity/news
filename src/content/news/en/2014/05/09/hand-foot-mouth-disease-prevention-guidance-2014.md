Shantou University’s Office of Resource Management issued guidance on hand, foot and mouth disease, a viral infection that commonly affects young children. It can spread through patients’ feces, blister fluid and respiratory secretions, as well as contaminated objects. Common symptoms include fever and rashes or blisters on the hands, feet or in the mouth; severe symptoms require prompt medical attention.

The guidance recommends that caregivers wash their hands before and after caring for children and after handling waste. Children should wash their hands after going out and before meals and after using the toilet, and avoid untreated water, raw or cold food, and contact with infected people. Toys, utensils, clothing and household surfaces should be cleaned and disinfected regularly, and indoor spaces ventilated. Chlorine-based disinfectants should be prepared according to product instructions and handled with appropriate protection.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
