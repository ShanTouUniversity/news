Shantou University’s Student Associations Union opened recruitment for candidates to its 16th Standing Committee. The union planned to hold its 16th Council Meeting on May 24, 2014, where council members would elect a nine-member committee comprising one chair and three vice-chairs.

Candidates were expected to have strong academic and personal records, understand the union’s work, support student organizations and campus culture, and be able to serve continuously for one academic year. Applications included a candidate form, a work proposal for the position sought and a presentation. Materials were due by 11 p.m. on May 14. Applicants who passed the review were scheduled to give an initial presentation at 7:30 p.m. on May 17; finalists would present at the May 24 council meeting before the vote.

Application forms and details are available in the OA system. Personal information requested on the original forms is not reproduced here.
