Shantou University’s Security Office warned that staff had recently received calls from strangers posing as university leaders and arranging meetings. Faculty, staff and students were urged to stay alert, verify unusual messages and avoid acting on suspicious instructions. Suspected fraud should be reported to relevant university offices or the police.

The campus 24-hour emergency number is 82904110; the Tuopu Police Station can be reached at 82540966.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office)
