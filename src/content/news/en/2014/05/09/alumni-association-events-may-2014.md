The Changjiang School of Art and Design scheduled several alumni association events for May 10, 2014. The inauguration of the Shantou Alumni Association and a visit by the Shenzhen Alumni Association were set for 9 a.m. to noon in the school’s first-floor lobby.

From 3 to 6 p.m., the Shenzhen alumni group planned a face-to-face career guidance session for graduates at the university’s 789 Coffee Bookstore. At the same time, the Shantou Alumni Association was to hold its first auction in the school’s first-floor lecture hall.
