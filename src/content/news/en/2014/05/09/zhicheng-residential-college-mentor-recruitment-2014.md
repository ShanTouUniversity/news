Shantou University’s Zhicheng Residential College opened recruitment for 12 prospective student mentors for the 2014–15 academic year, including four reserve positions. Mentors were expected to support residents’ development, live in the college and complete training before formal appointment.

Undergraduates who would be in their third year or above during the appointment period, as well as graduate students, were eligible. Applicants were expected to support the college’s approach, demonstrate responsibility, teamwork and communication skills, and have student-organization experience. Graduate applicants also needed a supervisor’s recommendation. Selection comprised an initial application review, interview, training and appointment.

Applicants were asked to submit the application form to 11czheng@stu.edu.cn by noon on May 16. The form and related attachments are available in the OA system.
