Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Office circulated a municipal campaign notice on May 9, 2014, concerning the use of private clubs. The requirements prohibit officials from visiting private clubs. Work-related visits to high-end restaurants, teahouses, clubs, and similar entertainment venues require approval from the head of the relevant department and filing with the university’s discipline office and Organization and United Front Work Department.

Party officials at deputy-division level and above were asked to complete a pledge and submit it to the Discipline Inspection Office in Room 422 of the New Administration Center by May 16. The full municipal plan and pledge form are available in the OA attachments. Inquiries: 86502346.
