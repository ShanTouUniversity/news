Shantou University’s Alumni Relations Office scheduled a meeting between Zhanjiang alumni and current students for 2:30–4:30 p.m. on May 10, 2014, in Room 213 of the Administration Center. Chen Chang, vice president of the Zhanjiang Alumni Association and a 1988 alumnus of the university’s arts and crafts program, was set to represent the association and discuss student life, employment, alumni contacts, and career information with students from Zhanjiang.

The meeting was open to students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Alumni Relations Office, issued through the General Administration Office)
