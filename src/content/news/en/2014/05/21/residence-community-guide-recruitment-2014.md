STU Student Affairs is recruiting students to serve as community guides in residence halls A through G. Established in 2012, the Community Guide Association supports communication among residents, collects feedback and organizes community activities to strengthen student self-management and education.

## Recruitment Details

Applicants should know the residence community and its procedures, demonstrate responsibility, teamwork and communication skills, and have no failed courses in the previous academic year. Applications close at midnight on May 25, and the service term runs from September 1, 2014, to June 30, 2015.

Applicants should download and complete the form, submit a paper copy to the current community guide for their area, and attend training. Formal appointment follows successful completion of the training. The position is part of the university’s work-study program and is paid according to work-study standards. Questions may be directed to the Student Affairs residence-counseling office at o_fdy@stu.edu.cn.
