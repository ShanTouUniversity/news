Shantou University’s College of Journalism and Communication is recruiting undergraduate and graduate students from Shantou, Zhongshan and Zhuhai to volunteer for its 2014 admission consultation service.

## Service Plan

The volunteers are expected to form publicity teams with college teachers in late June, tentatively from June 24 to 29, and visit key high schools in the three cities. They will introduce STU’s admissions information and answer questions about university application choices. Applicants should have strong communication skills and be willing to complete the college’s selection, training and scheduling arrangements.

Applications are due by May 31. Applicants should download and complete the form and submit it to the College Youth League Committee as specified in the original OA notice. Hong, a teacher with the college, can be reached at 86503651 for inquiries. The college may provide transportation subsidies based on the needs of the service.
