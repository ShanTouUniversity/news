The STU Tendering and Bidding Leading Group has completed its review of the transport-route reinforcement project for the first-phase Dormitory College. Guangdong Jinhai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. was selected as the successful bidder, with a bid amount of RMB 96,623.24.

The public notice period runs from May 21 through May 24, 2014. Contact details for the project and complaints process are listed below:
