Shantou University’s 2014 online job fair for graduates runs from May 21 to 30. Nearly 80 employers are participating, including HSBC, Shenzhen Xinhao Real Estate, Guangzhou Kunyue Sporting Goods, Shenzhen Aita Technology, China Marine Bunker (Zhanjiang) and the Guangdong Department of Health Government Services Center.

Graduates can view vacancies and participate in online recruitment through the fair website:

<http://www.stzp.cn/adv/StUniversityGetJob/index.aspx>

The fair is jointly organized by STU Student Affairs and Shantou Recruitment Network.
