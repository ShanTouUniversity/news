Shantou University’s Law School will hold its 18th Student Congress at 19:30 on Saturday, May 24, in Lecture Room E-202.

The congress will review the work of the 17th Student Union Executive Committee, elect the 18th executive committee and discuss future arrangements. Delegates will account for 10 percent of the Law School’s undergraduate population and will be nominated by each class under the principle of democratic centralism. Delegates must be enrolled Law School undergraduates with the required civic awareness, academic standing, ability to stay connected with classmates and capacity for deliberation.
