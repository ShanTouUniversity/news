STU’s Network and Information Center has asked all departments to conduct security self-checks on their websites and update registration information to strengthen campus network security.

## Requirements

The review covers websites operated by university units in campus server rooms, as well as servers and virtual hosts managed by the Data Center. Departments must check security measures, vulnerabilities, designated security responsibilities and inspection arrangements, then complete, sign and stamp the inspection form before submitting it to the center.

Website administrators must also log in to <http://netms.stu.edu.cn> to update registration information. After the center’s review, printed materials must be signed, stamped and submitted for recordkeeping. Both tasks must be completed by May 30. Sites that fail to submit inspection reports or registration information may be isolated, while unregistered or privately established sites will be handled under the applicable rules.
