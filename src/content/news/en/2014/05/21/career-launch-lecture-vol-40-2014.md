STU’s Career Launch Lecture VOL. 40 will take place from 19:30 to 21:30 on Monday, May 26, in the New Library Lecture Hall. The topic is “Do Not Wait Until After Graduation,” and all students and staff are welcome.

The guest speaker, Qiūye, whose legal name is Zhang Zhi, is an associate professor at Wuhan Institute of Technology and director of the Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center in its School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering. He also serves as a chief lecturer at Shanghai Zhuoyi Management Consulting. He has judged several PPT competitions, written for *Computer Fan* magazine and authored the *Persuasive Presentations* book series.
