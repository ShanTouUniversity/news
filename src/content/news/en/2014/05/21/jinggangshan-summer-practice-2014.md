The Central Committee of the Communist Youth League’s School Department and the National Jinggangshan Revolutionary Tradition Education Base Management Center will hold the “Jinggang Spirit, Chinese Dream” national college summer practice program from July to August 2014. STU plans to recommend two student teams to visit Jinggangshan for Party history study, revolutionary education, skills development, social practice and university exchanges.

Each team will consist of 20 students and must designate one Youth League cadre and one professional teacher as instructors. Participants should understand the history and spirit of the Jinggangshan struggle, demonstrate communication and creative skills, be physically prepared for outdoor activities, and show commitment to teamwork, discipline and campus or community service. Teams must also prepare a specific project implementation plan.

Interested teams must develop a project topic by June 2, complete the team application and member summary forms, and send the project plan and related materials to the University Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn. The committee will select teams based on applications.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee)
