STU has opened recruitment for the 2014 Western Program and Mountain Program for university student volunteers. Western Program volunteers will serve in township-level communities in western provinces and regions in areas including basic education, agricultural technology, health care, youth work, community management and service projects in Xinjiang and Tibet. Mountain Program volunteers will continue to provide community and grassroots services in less-developed townships in eastern, western and northern Guangdong.

## Application Process

1. By June 1, applicants must register through the Guangdong Volunteer website (<http://www.gdzyz.cn>) and the Western Program information system (<http://xibu.youth.cn>), as applicable.
2. Western Program applicants should download the registration form from its information system; Mountain Program applicants should download the corresponding form from the Guangdong Volunteer website, then print and complete it.
3. The form must be signed by a counselor or college Youth League official, stamped by the Party organization of the applicant’s college or residential college, and submitted to STU’s project office by June 1.
4. The university project office will review the information and enter its review opinion in the Western Program information system.

STU’s project office is located in the University Youth League Committee and handles recruitment for both programs. The consultation telephone number is 86502302. Applicants should follow the OA attachments for detailed materials and requirements.
