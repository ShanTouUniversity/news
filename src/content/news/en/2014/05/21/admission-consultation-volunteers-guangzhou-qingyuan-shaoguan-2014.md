The Youth League Committee of STU’s Literature School has announced recruitment of volunteers to support admissions consultation in Guangzhou, Qingyuan and Shaoguan.

## Schedule

| Date | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| May 20–31 | Applications |
| June 2 | Publication of selected names |
| Mid-June | Training |
| June 25–28, tentatively | Admissions consultation and outreach at schools in each area |

Application forms must be sent to the Literature School Youth League Committee by May 31 with “Admission Consultation Volunteer Application” as the email subject. The form and attachments should be obtained and submitted as specified in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Literature School)
>
> ⚠️ The registration form and event attachments are available on the OA system.
