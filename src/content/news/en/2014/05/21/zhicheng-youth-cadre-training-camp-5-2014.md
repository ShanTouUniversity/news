Shantou University’s Zhicheng College Youth League Committee will hold its fifth youth cadre training camp from May 24 to 25. The camp is designed to strengthen youth cadres’ overall abilities, service awareness and practical skills while supporting the development of college teams.

Newly elected secretaries and deputy secretaries of the seventh Youth League general branch and branch committees are required to report to Room J-354 at Zhicheng College at 14:20 on May 24 for the opening ceremony and coursework. Participants are expected to follow the camp’s dress requirements and prepare in advance.
