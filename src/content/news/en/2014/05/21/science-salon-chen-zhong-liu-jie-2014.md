It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a Science Salon lecture series on May 23, 2014, featuring Dr. Zhong Chen and Dr. Jie Liu from the National Institutes of Health (NIH), USA.

## Schedule

| Session | Title | Speaker | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | NF-kB signaling pathway and The Cancer Genome Atlas projects of lung and head neck cancers | Dr. Zhong Chen, NIH, USA | 09:30–11:30, May 23, 2014 | Lecture Hall, Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center |
| 2 | Research on new flow cytometry technology to understand the contribution of immune response to viral infection-induced immunity and immunopathology | Dr. Jie Liu, NIH, USA | 09:30–11:30, May 23, 2014 | Lecture Hall, Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center |
