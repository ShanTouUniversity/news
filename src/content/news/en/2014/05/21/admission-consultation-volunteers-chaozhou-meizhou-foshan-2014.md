Shantou University’s Law School is recruiting undergraduate and graduate students from across the university to provide admission consultation for prospective students and parents in Chaozhou, Meizhou and Foshan.

## Service and Applications

The volunteers are tentatively scheduled to return to their former high schools from June 26 to 28. They will assist teachers in introducing STU’s admissions policies and answering questions from candidates and parents. Applicants should support the university’s educational philosophy, have strong communication and teamwork skills, and complete the Law School’s selection, training and scheduling arrangements. The school may provide transportation and meal subsidies and recognize outstanding individuals or teams.

Applications may be submitted individually or as teams. The form must be submitted to the Law School as specified in the original OA notice by May 31. For inquiries, contact Teacher Wang at office extension 883908.
