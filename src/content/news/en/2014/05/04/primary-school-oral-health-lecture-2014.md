Shantou University Affiliated Primary School will hold an oral health education lecture, “A Lesson on Caring for Your Teeth,” at **2:40 p.m. on May 7, 2014**, in its auditorium. Parents of first- and second-grade pupils are invited.

The speaker, Dr. Xin Weini, is an attending dentist in the Department of Stomatology at the First Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University. Her clinical and research interests include endodontic and periodontal treatment, dental restoration, and the links between oral and overall health.
