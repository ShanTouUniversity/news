A literature and film session of the 11th Shantou University Sangpu Mountain Reading Festival will take place at 7:30 p.m. on May 5, 2014, in Room 204 of Building E. The event is open to the university community.

The program will screen *Wuthering Heights*. After the screening, Professor Cai Weiqing will discuss the original work and the connections and differences between literature and film. Participants will also discuss their viewing impressions in groups and take part in a question-and-answer activity, with small commemorative gifts for correct answers.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and Shantou University Student Associations Union)
