Guangdong’s ocean and fisheries authority opened a call for technology needs to help prepare projects and establish a research project pool. Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the call to relevant campus units, seeking proposed research priorities for **2014–2019**.

Topics include specialty aquaculture, sustainable marine aquaculture, marine bioproducts and medicine, marine and fisheries equipment, ecological conservation and restoration, aquatic species development, marine resource protection, and seafood processing and safety. Proposals addressing other key technical challenges facing the sector are also eligible.

Proposals should explain the rationale for a research direction, identify core technical challenges and priorities, and describe the submitting unit’s research foundation and capacity. Submissions were due by **May 9** and were to be emailed to yxhu@stu.edu.cn. Future special-project recommendations would be selected from the project pool.

The required proposal format was attached to the original notice.
