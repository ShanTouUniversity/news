Dihai Blue Bay Marketing Center planned a property tour for Shantou University employees on **May 11, 2014, from 7:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.** The organizer said the project would cover the event costs and provide domestic travel insurance.

Five meeting points were listed: the university motto monument, Anju STU Garden, Yidu Cinema, the main entrance of Golden Bay Hotel, and the Dihai Blue Bay marketing center at Imperial Garden, 109 Jinsha East Road. The event was limited to 70 university employees. Registration forms were to be emailed to the university union at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn by the end of the workday on May 7.

The itinerary and registration form were attached to the original notice. Participants were asked to follow the organizers’ arrangements and prepare for sun exposure.
