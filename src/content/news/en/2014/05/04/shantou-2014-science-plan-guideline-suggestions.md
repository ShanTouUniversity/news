Shantou’s Science and Technology Bureau opened a call for suggestions for the **2014 municipal science-plan application guidelines**, which Shantou University’s Research Office circulated to relevant campus units. Priority areas include high technology, industrial upgrading, emerging industries, technology services, social welfare research, and environmental protection. Suggestions on other urgent key technologies for local industries are also welcome.

Proposals should identify research directions with a sound foundation and strategic value, explain their rationale and key technical challenges, and describe the submitting unit’s relevant experience and capacity. Future project recommendations will be selected from the resulting project database. Faculty interested in submitting suggestions were asked to email the proposal form to cszhu@stu.edu.cn by **noon on May 9**.

The proposal form was attached to the original notice.
