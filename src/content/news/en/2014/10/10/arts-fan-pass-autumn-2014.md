It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center of Shantou University have opened applications for the Autumn 2014 Colorful Arts Season Arts Fan Pass. Up to 200 passes are available to faculty and students. Pass holders may collect performance tickets ahead of other audiences and receive priority access to better seats when collecting tickets.

## Application and Renewal Arrangements

Arts fans who attended every performance in the previous season may renew their passes by presenting their Spring 2014 Arts Fan Passes. The remaining passes will be issued to faculty and students on a first-come, first-served basis.

| Applicants | Time | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Previous-season arts fans | October 13, 9:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. | Present a Spring 2014 Arts Fan Pass in person to renew |
| STU faculty and students | October 13, 9:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. and 2:30–5:00 p.m. | Apply with a campus card |

Passes will be issued in Room 309 of the Arts Education Center. Interested members of the university community can follow the Weibo account “Creative Arts Ambassadors” and the “CKAD” WeChat Official Account for information about the arts season.

Students who attend every performance in the autumn season may be considered “Super Arts Fans” based on their performance ticket stubs and can enter a lucky draw at the end of the season.

> ⚠️ The Arts Fan Pass application attachment is available on the OA system.
