Shantou University published three policies for graduate students in October 2014. They covered the review of graduate National Scholarships, temporary hardship assistance, and the management of teaching, administrative and research assistant positions. The policies were discussed and approved at the university’s 13th leadership meeting on September 29, 2014.

Detailed eligibility criteria, review procedures and position requirements were included in the OA attachments.
