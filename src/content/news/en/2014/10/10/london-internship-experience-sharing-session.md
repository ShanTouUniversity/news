The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design planned a session about internships in London for 7:30 p.m. on October 13, 2014, in the school’s lecture theatre. Two undergraduates had received Horizons scholarships to intern for five weeks over the summer at London game studio Kuato Studio. They were expected to discuss industry learning, life in London and the city’s design and arts scene, as well as share advice on pursuing internships in the UK.

The session was open to the university community. The notice did not identify the students, and this report does not speculate about their identities.
