It is reported that the Law School issued a notice on October 10, 2014, to publicize the proposed recipients of the 2014 National Scholarship.

Following applications and a preliminary review by the school Youth League Committee, four students were shortlisted for interviews. After assessment by the Law School National Scholarship selection committee, two students are proposed as recipients.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

| Candidates | Wu SS and Li JH (anonymized) |
| Shortlisted for interview | 4 students including Li JH and 3 others (anonymized) |

| How to object | In writing or in person/by phone to the Law School Youth League Committee; organizations should affix a seal, individuals should sign with real name |
