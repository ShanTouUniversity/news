Shantou University opened a pre-appointment public review in October 2014 for Li Aiyi as associate dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. The proposed appointment was discussed at the university’s 13th leadership meeting on September 29 and was to proceed under the university’s appointment rules for academic-unit leaders.

Organizations and individuals could submit written comments to the university’s Human Resources Office by October 15, 2014. This report covers the proposed appointment and review only; it does not assert the final appointment outcome.
