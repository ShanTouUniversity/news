It is reported that the ninth season of Shantou University's "Colorful Art Season" is about to begin, opening with a concert by "Brilliant Metal," the brass quintet of the Guangzhou Symphony Orchestra. The concert will be held at 7:30 pm on October 15 at the STU Auditorium, featuring classic pieces in Latin, rumba and modern styles performed with distinctive character.

## About the Ensemble

Founded in 1957, the Guangzhou Symphony Orchestra has grown into one of China's largest and most artistically accomplished orchestras, and to date remains the only Chinese orchestra to have left its "musical footprint" on all five continents.

The Brilliant Metal brass quintet was formed in Guangzhou in March 2009, with all five members drawn from the Guangzhou Symphony Orchestra. In its founding year, the quintet reached the finals of the 7th Wind Chamber Music Ensemble Competition at the Central Conservatory of Music (brass quintet division) and won an excellence award. In February 2010 it was invited to perform at the Lantern Festival gala hosted by the Ministry of Culture at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing, and in September 2014 it performed as a guest artist at the National Day reception held by the Chinese Embassy in Australia.
