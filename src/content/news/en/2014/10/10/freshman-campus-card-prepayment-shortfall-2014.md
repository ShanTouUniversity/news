Shantou University’s Network and Information Centre reported in October 2014 that a small number of incoming undergraduate and graduate students had not paid the RMB 100 campus-card prepayment after a second bank debit attempt. Those affected were asked to pay in cash at the campus-card service counter by October 30.

Under the notice, cards with unpaid balances were to be frozen from October 21, preventing campus purchases and the receipt of subsidies. Accounts still unpaid after the deadline could be reported to the university for closure. The list of affected students was attached to the OA notice and is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Centre)
> ⚠️ The list of affected students is available as an OA attachment.
