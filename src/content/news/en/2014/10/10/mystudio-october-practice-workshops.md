Shantou University’s MYSTUdio announced an October 2014 workshop series on entrepreneurship, building valuable companies and brand design. The October 14 session was scheduled to cover entrepreneurship basics with Gimmy Chu, a co-founder of Nanoleaf. On October 21, the programme planned a recorded presentation by Peter Thiel on building a valuable company. The October 28 session was to feature Cheung Kong School of Art and Design lecturer Liang Rujie on building successful brands and product identities.

The notice also listed entrepreneur Li Jia Jun for a startup-experience sharing segment. Sessions were scheduled for evenings at MYSTUdio on the library’s first floor, with roughly 60 to 70 places per event and online registration. This report reflects the announced programme and does not claim that the events took place.
