Shantou University’s Resources Management Office accepted 2014 housing allowance applications from newly transferred employees who met the applicable policy criteria. Applicants were asked to submit materials to the university property office by October 24; the university planned to forward consolidated applications to relevant provincial and municipal departments in early November. Employees who had applied in previous years did not need to apply again.

The notice covered eligible public-institution employees with an employment relationship at STU who met conditions including no prior purchase of specified policy housing and no previous government housing subsidy. Existing recipients whose professional title or position had changed were also asked to submit updated information. The OA attachments contain application forms and a materials checklist; sensitive identity and family documentation details are not reproduced here.
