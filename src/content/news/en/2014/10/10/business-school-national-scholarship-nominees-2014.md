Following interviews and review, Shantou University’s Business School committee proposed two undergraduates from the 2011 cohort for the 2014 National Scholarship. They studied Marketing and International Economics and Trade, respectively.

Written feedback was accepted until 5:00 p.m. on October 15, 2014, at the Business School Youth League office on the second floor of the West Wen building. To protect student privacy, names are abbreviated as Lu JB and Li JY.
