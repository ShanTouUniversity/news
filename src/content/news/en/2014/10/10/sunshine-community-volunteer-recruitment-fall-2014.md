The Firefly volunteer team of Shantou University’s Youth Volunteers Association recruited volunteers for the fall 2014 Sunshine Community programme. Launched in 2007, the initiative served older residents living alone, single-parent households and others needing support. Activities included home visits, household assistance, emotional support, repair and health services, and academic guidance.

Applications for that semester closed at 7:00 p.m. on October 11, 2014. The original notice requested personal details, including student numbers and phone contacts, for registration; those collection channels and student contact details are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League and Youth Volunteers Association)
