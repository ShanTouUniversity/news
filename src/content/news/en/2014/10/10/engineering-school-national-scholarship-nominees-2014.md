After interviews and review, Shantou University’s Engineering School committee proposed three students from the 2011 cohort for the 2014 National Scholarship. The students were from electronics, civil engineering and mechatronics programmes.

Written feedback was accepted until noon on October 13, 2014, at the Engineering School Youth League office, Room 301 in the West Engineering building. To protect student privacy, the candidates are identified only by abbreviated names: Xie JC, Huang CR and Guo H.
