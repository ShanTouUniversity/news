Shantou University’s Graduate School opened its internal nomination process for the 2014 Guangdong Outstanding Thesis awards. The university was allocated one doctoral thesis nomination for provincial review and could select three outstanding master’s theses internally.

Eligible theses were primarily those for degrees awarded within the specified period. Some earlier theses could also qualify if they met additional conditions and received joint recommendations from three external academic supervisors. Schools were asked to submit nomination materials by October 20, 2014. Detailed eligibility rules and document requirements were included in the OA attachments.
