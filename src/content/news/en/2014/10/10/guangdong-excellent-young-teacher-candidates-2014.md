It is reported that the Personnel Office issued a notice on October 10, 2014, to announce candidates for the 2014 Guangdong Provincial Excellent Young Teacher Training Program.

In accordance with the Department of Education’s notice (Yue Jiao Shi Han [2014] No. 108), the university intends to recommend eight faculty members, now publicized for comment.

## Candidates and Public Comment Period

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed candidates | Cai Zemin, Wang Qinhua, Wei Chuliang, Wu Fupei, Zhou Xiaoping, Liang Qiang, Wang * (one character garbled in the original), Chen Fanfan |
| Public comment period | October 10–17, 2014 |
| Handling office | Personnel Office, Shantou University |
| Contact | Chen Ying |
| Phone | 0754-86502340 |
| Email | rscgs@stu.edu.cn |

Those with objections should submit written or emailed comments to the Personnel Office by October 17, providing specific facts and signing with their real name.
