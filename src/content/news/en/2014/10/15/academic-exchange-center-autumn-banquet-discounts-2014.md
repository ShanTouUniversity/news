Shantou University’s Academic Exchange Center introduced discounted autumn banquet packages from October 20, 2014, replacing its previous discounted packages. Eligible customers included units or projects with valid charge accounts and faculty, staff and students using valid campus cards with sufficient balances.

The rules at the time required cardholders to attend the entire meal; cash payment and outside food and drinks were not accepted. Menus and an order form accompanied the notice. This is historical information about a 2014 service and does not indicate current availability.
