Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office circulated arrangements for preparatory sessions for students registered for the second-half 2014 Mandarin proficiency test.

The two sessions were scheduled for October 22 and 29, 2014, at 2:30 p.m. in Lecture Room E101. The testing center was to announce the exam date separately after the training. The available notice did not report on later arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
