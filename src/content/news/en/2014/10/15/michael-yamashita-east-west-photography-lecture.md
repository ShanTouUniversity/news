Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication scheduled a lecture titled “When East Meets West” by National Geographic photographer Michael Yamashita. He was to discuss photography projects tracing the journeys of Marco Polo and Zheng He.

The lecture was scheduled for October 21, 2014, from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. in the university Library Lecture Hall. Yamashita had worked with National Geographic for 25 years, with projects including Marco Polo’s travels and Zheng He’s voyages. The available notice did not report on the lecture’s outcome.
