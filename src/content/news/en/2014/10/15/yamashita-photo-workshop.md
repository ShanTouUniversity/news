It is reported that Michael Yamashita, a legendary photographer for National Geographic, will hold a two-day photography workshop at Shantou University from October 21 to 22. The event is organized by the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication.

Selected STU students will join Yamashita on shooting trips around Shantou on the afternoon of October 21 and the morning of October 22. Two photo critique sessions will also be held during the workshop: the first is scheduled for 9:00–11:00 a.m. on Tuesday, October 21, in the university library lecture hall, open to both the public and the media; the second will take place on Wednesday afternoon, October 22, in Room 3 of the Academic Communication Center (ACC), where seats must be reserved in advance through the Mystu booking center (booking.stu.edu.cn), with telephone reservations available for media. In addition, the best photographs taken by participating students will be exhibited from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. on October 22 in the same venue.
