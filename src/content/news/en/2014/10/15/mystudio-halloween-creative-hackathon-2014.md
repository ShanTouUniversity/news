Shantou University’s MYSTUdio and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design scheduled a “Halloween Creative Hackathon” from 10 p.m. on October 24 to 10 a.m. on October 26, 2014. Teams of three students from different colleges were to create Halloween decorations based on a randomly assigned theme and take part in a campus parade on the final day.

The event was open to the first 42 registrants and provided teams with creative materials, meals and basic stationery. A campus vote was planned, with each member of the winning team to receive a 16GB Apple iPad. The available notice did not report the results.
