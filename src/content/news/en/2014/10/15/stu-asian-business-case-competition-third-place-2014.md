A Shantou University Business School team placed third in the eighth Asian Business Case Competition, hosted by Nanyang Technological University in Singapore. The competition opened on September 22, 2014, with teams from nine universities; STU was represented by a four-member undergraduate team.

The final case focused on developing Singapore’s food-waste-to-energy sector. The STU team proposed approaches to improve household waste-sorting awareness, support relevant businesses and expand industry opportunities, advancing from its group to the final round. Team members’ names, years and majors are omitted to protect student privacy.
