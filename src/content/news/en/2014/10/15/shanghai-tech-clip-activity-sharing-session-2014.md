A group of 109 Shantou University students and staff took part in the “Tech Clip” program in Shanghai from October 8 to 10, 2014, learning about the ideas, management and operations of technology companies. After the program, 10 student teams explored the city under the guidance of faculty, focusing on topics including technology, economics, architecture, history and religion.

The teams used street interviews, photography and video to document their observations and shared their work on WeChat. The university scheduled a follow-up session at MYSTUdio for the evening of October 15 to share participants’ experiences. The available notice did not report on the session’s outcome.
