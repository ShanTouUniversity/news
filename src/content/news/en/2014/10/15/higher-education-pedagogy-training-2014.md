Shantou University’s Personnel Office and Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment scheduled a higher education teaching methods program for new faculty and staff who had not attended the training before.

The program was scheduled for October 18, 22 and 25, 2014, with six sessions covering faculty development, student-centered strategies, classroom preparation and teaching, teaching principles, course design and teaching experience. The 20-hour course could count toward annual continuing education requirements, and the university was to issue certificates. Detailed times and venues were listed in the original attachment.
