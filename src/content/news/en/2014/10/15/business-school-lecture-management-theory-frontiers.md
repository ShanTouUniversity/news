Shantou University’s Business School scheduled a lecture by Heilongjiang University professor Ma Ji titled “Frontier Issues in Management Theory.” Topics included the development of Western and Chinese management theory and strategies for research into management theory with Chinese characteristics.

The lecture was scheduled for October 21, 2014, from 9 to 11 a.m. in the Business School’s Academic Seminar Room. Associate Professor Chen Siyan was to host. The available notice did not report on the event’s outcome.
