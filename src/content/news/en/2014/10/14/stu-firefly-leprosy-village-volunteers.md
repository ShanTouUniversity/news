It is reported that in October 2014, the "Firefly" Volunteer Action Brigade of the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association is recruiting across campus for long-term volunteers of its "Care for Leprosy Rehabilitation Villages" program, with a service term covering the 2014-2015 academic year. The initiative aims to carry forward the volunteer spirit of dedication, friendship, mutual aid and progress, and to support underprivileged communities in eastern Guangdong.

## Recruitment Quotas

The recruitment covered two rehabilitation villages, with the Jiedong Xikeng service offered in both one-day and two-day formats:

| Service Location | Quota |
| --- | --- |
| Linghou Leprosy Rehabilitation Village, Chaozhou | 8 volunteers |
| Xikeng Leprosy Rehabilitation Village, Jiedong (one-day) | 16 volunteers |
| Xikeng Leprosy Rehabilitation Village, Jiedong (two-day) | 12 volunteers |

## Application and Selection

According to the organizers, applicants can sign up either online by submitting a completed application form to a designated email address, or in person at promotion booths outside canteens No. 2 and No. 3 and at the entrance of Canteen No. 4 during midday and evening sessions on October 15-16.

Interviews are initially scheduled for October 18-19, with specific times and venues to be announced later. Successful candidates will be notified individually by text message after the interviews.

The Firefly brigade also shared program updates through its Weibo and WeChat accounts so that students could follow the service activities.
