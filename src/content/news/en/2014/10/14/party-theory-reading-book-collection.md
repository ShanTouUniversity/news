The Publicity Department of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China donated 1,000 copies of *A Reader on General Secretary Xi Jinping’s Series of Important Speeches* to Shantou University for theoretical study. University units based on the main campus were asked to send representatives to collect the books from Room 315, New Administration Centre, on October 14, 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department)
