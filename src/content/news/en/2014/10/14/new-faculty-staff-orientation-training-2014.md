Shantou University’s Personnel Office scheduled orientation training for newly hired employees to introduce the university and the work of its functional departments.

The session was scheduled for October 15, 2014, from 2:30 to 6:10 p.m. in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center, and was to be conducted in Mandarin. The detailed agenda was in the original attachment; the attendee list is not reproduced to protect new employees’ privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
