The Guangdong Provincial University Student Affairs Committee opened its third selection of exemplary university student affairs projects. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office invited its colleges and residential colleges to submit internal candidates.

Eligible projects were to have been implemented for at least three years by counselors or student affairs teams and to have demonstrated educational impact and wider influence. The university planned to conduct an initial review and recommend one project to the provincial committee. Materials were due to the Student Affairs Office by October 31, 2014.
