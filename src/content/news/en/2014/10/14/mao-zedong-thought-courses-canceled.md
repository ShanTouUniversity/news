Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced cancellations for two sections of Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics, classes 72323 and 72327.

| Class | Canceled sessions |
| --- | --- |
| 72323 | Periods 8–9, Thursday of week four (October 16, 2014); periods 3–4, Monday of week five (October 20) |
| 72327 | Periods 3–4, Thursday of week four (October 16, 2014); periods 8–9, Monday of week five (October 20) |

The notice did not list make-up arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
