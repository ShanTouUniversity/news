Shantou University’s Publicity Department scheduled a special report on studying the important speeches of Xi Jinping, to be delivered by Ding Jinqing, head of the theory division at the Guangdong Provincial Publicity Department.

The report was scheduled for 3 p.m. on October 17, 2014, in Room 330 of the New Administration Center. The notice asked secretaries, deputy secretaries and committee members of Party general branches and directly affiliated branches to attend. The available notice did not report on the event’s outcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department)
