The Shantou Municipal Financial Work Bureau recruited three student interns through Shantou University’s Business School. Eligible applicants included Business School graduate students and undergraduates in the classes of 2011 and 2012. Candidates were expected to have a foundation in finance, strong writing and communication skills, and availability for continuous internship work.

The placements were scheduled to begin on October 20, 2014, and last about three months, following normal office hours. Interns were to receive a stipend. Applications and interviews were scheduled for October 17; the available notice did not report the final selections.
