Shantou University’s General Education Forum scheduled a lecture titled “Reading and the World” by Fudan University professor Ge Jianxiong, exploring reading in relation to learning, research and life.

The talk was scheduled for 3 p.m. on October 18, 2014, in the Science Lecture Hall of the Science and Technology Building. Ge’s research spans historical geography, Chinese history, population history, migration, culture and environmental history. The available notice did not report on the event’s outcome.
