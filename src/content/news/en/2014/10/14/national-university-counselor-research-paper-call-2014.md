The National University Counselor Research Association opened its 2014 awards for outstanding counselor research papers, and Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office organized internal submissions. Eligible papers were unpublished and addressed ideological and political education for university students or the professional development of counselors, with academic rigor and practical relevance.

Each first author could submit one paper. Multi-author work had to be submitted under the co-authors’ names or as a research group, depending on team size. STU colleges and residential colleges were asked to submit papers and application forms by October 23, 2014; the university would then arrange expert review and select papers for recommendation.
