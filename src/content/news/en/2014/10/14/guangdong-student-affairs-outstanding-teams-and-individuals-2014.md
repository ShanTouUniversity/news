The Guangdong Provincial University Student Affairs Committee opened its 2014 awards for outstanding student affairs teams and individual staff members. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office organized the university’s internal nominations.

Eligible teams worked in student education and administration; individual candidates included counselors, deputy secretaries and relevant student affairs staff. Team candidates were expected to demonstrate effective management and strong results, while individual candidates needed at least three years of experience and notable achievements. Frontline counselors and staff with related research were given preference. Each STU college or residential college could generally recommend one team and one individual. Materials were due by October 31, 2014.
