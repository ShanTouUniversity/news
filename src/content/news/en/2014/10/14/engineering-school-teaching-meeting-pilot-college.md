Shantou University’s School of Engineering scheduled an all-school teaching meeting and a mobilization session on its pilot-college initiative, inviting all faculty members.

The meeting was scheduled for 4:10 p.m. on October 17, 2014, in Lecture Room E203. The available notice did not provide an agenda or report on what followed.
