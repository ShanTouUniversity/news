Shantou University, acting at the request of the Shantou Foreign Affairs and Overseas Chinese Affairs Office, recruited 10 students to volunteer for the “Washington State Day—Meet in Shantou” event planned for October 17–19, 2014.

Volunteer service was scheduled for October 18–19 at the Junhua Haiyi Hotel, with duties including guest reception, on-site interpretation and promotion of Chaoshan culture. Applicants needed English communication skills and availability for both days; the organizers were to provide meals, accommodation and a stipend. The available notice was a pre-event recruitment announcement and did not report on what followed. A student contact’s personal phone number is omitted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee; Office of International Cooperation and Exchange)
