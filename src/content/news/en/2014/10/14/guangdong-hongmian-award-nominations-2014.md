The Guangdong Provincial University Student Affairs Committee held its sixth Hongmian Award selection for university student affairs professionals. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office organized internal nominations.

The award was open to full-time university staff with at least 20 years of frontline student affairs experience, including department-level Party deputy secretaries, counselors and student affairs administrators. Candidates were expected to have a sustained record of service and notable achievements. Each STU college or residential college could generally recommend one person, with materials due by 5 p.m. on October 31, 2014.
