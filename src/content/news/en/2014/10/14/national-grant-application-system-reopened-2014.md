With a small number of places still available, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office reopened its national student grant application system in 2014 to broaden the program’s coverage. Applicants were asked to submit an online application by 9 a.m. on October 21 and deliver a printed form to their college or residential college counselor.

These were the application arrangements published in 2014 and do not describe current grant deadlines or procedures.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
