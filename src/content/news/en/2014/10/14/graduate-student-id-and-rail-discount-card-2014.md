In 2014, Shantou University’s Graduate School outlined procedures for student IDs and student rail discount cards, including purchases for the incoming cohort and renewals or replacements for continuing students.

New graduate students were asked to register their home-travel station in the student affairs system by October 20. New cards cost RMB 7 each, with student ID stamping and card processing scheduled to begin October 27. Renewal and replacement services for other cohorts were scheduled for October 22–24; replacement student IDs were expected to be available on October 31.

The notice said the discount then applied only to travel home during winter and summer breaks, with one annual card renewal and up to four discounted trips. These are historical 2014 policies and procedures, not current railway rules or university guidance.
