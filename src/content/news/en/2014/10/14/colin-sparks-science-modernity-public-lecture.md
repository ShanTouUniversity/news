Colin Sparks, Chair Professor at Hong Kong Baptist University, was scheduled to deliver a lecture titled “Science, modernity and the public” at Shantou University from 2:30 to 4:00 p.m. on October 17, 2014. The talk was to examine the rise of scientific research and its effects on modern society from the perspective of Western modernity. The venue was Conference Room 5 in the Academic and Cultural Centre (ACC).

Sparks has focused on globalization. He taught at the University of Westminster in the United Kingdom from 1974 to 2011, served as dean of its School of Communication Arts, and founded its China Media Centre. In 1979, he established the journal *Media, Culture and Society*.
