Shantou University’s Peer Learning Centre (SLC) began providing academic inquiry services to undergraduates across the university for the semester on October 13, 2014. The centre is open daily from 3:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.

Students can contact the centre at 86502250 or slc@stu.edu.cn. Detailed service arrangements and a user guide are included in the original campus notice; readers who need the attachments can consult the notice in the OA system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The service guide and detailed arrangements are available as attachments in the OA system.
