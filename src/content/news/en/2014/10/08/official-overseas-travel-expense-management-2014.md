The Guangdong Provincial Department of Finance and the Guangdong Provincial Foreign Affairs Office had revised rules governing expenses for official temporary overseas travel by provincial government agencies and public institutions. With approval from the university’s responsible leadership, Shantou University announced that it would apply the updated rules from October 9, 2014.

Detailed expense standards and management requirements were contained in the policy documents circulated with the notice. Campus units could consult the original OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office and Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
> ⚠️ The official temporary overseas travel expense rules are available as OA attachments.
