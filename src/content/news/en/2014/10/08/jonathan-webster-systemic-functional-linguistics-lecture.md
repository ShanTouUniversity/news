Jonathan Webster, professor in the Department of Translation and Linguistics at City University of Hong Kong, was scheduled to speak at Shantou University from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on October 9, 2014. The lecture, part of the New Century Humanities Forum, was titled “Investigating the functional and aesthetic values of a text: a Systemic Functional perspective” and was to be held in Conference Room 3 of the Academic and Cultural Centre.

Webster also directs City University of Hong Kong’s Halliday Centre for Intelligent Applications of Language Studies. His work connects systemic functional linguistics, rhetorical structure theory and multimodal discourse analysis. The talk was expected to use a range of speeches to examine how language conveys experience, attitudes and social relationships, and how texts differ in functional and aesthetic value. The notice did not report the event’s outcome.
