Chen Peixiong, a part-time researcher at Sun Yat-sen University, was scheduled to give a lecture titled “The Present and Future of China’s Capital Markets” at Shantou University from 10:00 to 11:30 a.m. on October 10, 2014. The talk was to take place in the Business School’s academic seminar room and was open to the university community. Associate Professor Chen Siyan was listed as host.

The planned discussion covered the Chinese stock market’s current state, its challenges and its future outlook. Chen’s work focuses on the management and research of futures, securities, property rights and technology markets. The notice preceded the event and did not state its outcome.
