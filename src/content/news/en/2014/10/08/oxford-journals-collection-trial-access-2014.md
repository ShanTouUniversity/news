Shantou University Library announced trial access to Oxford Journals Collection in October 2014. The resource included six collections—humanities, social sciences, law, mathematics and physics, life sciences, and medicine—with 262 journals in total. The library said at the time that about 80 percent of the titles were indexed in SCI, SSCI or A&HCI.

The trial was scheduled to end on December 31, 2014. Readers were invited to send feedback to the library’s subject services team. This article records a time-limited trial from 2014 and does not indicate the database’s current subscription status.
