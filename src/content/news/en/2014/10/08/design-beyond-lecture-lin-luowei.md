Lin Luowei, founder of Hong Kong’s Mino Design, was scheduled to deliver a lecture titled “Beyond Design” at 7:30 p.m. on October 9, 2014, in the lecture theatre of Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. The event was organized by the Cheung Kong Industrial Design Centre and the school and was open to the university community.

Lin also served as chief designer at Novah Furniture and worked with international design and Guangdong furniture-industry organizations. The notice was issued before the lecture and did not report its outcome.
