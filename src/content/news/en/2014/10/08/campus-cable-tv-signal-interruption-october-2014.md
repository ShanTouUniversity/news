To support construction of Shantou University’s new Medical School building, Shantou Cable TV planned to relocate underground television lines near the former campus gate from 9:00 a.m. to about 2:00 p.m. on October 9, 2014. Cable television service across campus was expected to be interrupted during the work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
