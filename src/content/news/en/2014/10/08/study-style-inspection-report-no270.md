It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has released its 270th study style inspection report, detailing results from checks carried out by the inspection team during week two of the fall semester of the 2014-2015 academic year.

## Inspection Results

The checks took place on two afternoons, September 29 and September 30, both at Teaching Building E. Five students were found late by less than five minutes on the first day and six on the second, for a total of eleven instances.

| Date | Time | Location | Late Arrivals (within 5 minutes) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| September 29 (Monday) | 2:00 PM | Teaching Building E | 5 |
| September 30 (Tuesday) | 2:00 PM | Teaching Building E | 6 |

## Follow-up Measures

The Student Affairs Office has forwarded the names of students who failed to observe university regulations to their respective colleges and residential colleges, asking them to provide corrective education and to continue strengthening study style development.
