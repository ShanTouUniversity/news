After interviews and a comprehensive review, Shantou University’s Science School committee proposed two students for the 2014 National Scholarship. They were enrolled in Applied Chemistry and Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, respectively. Written feedback was accepted by the school’s Youth League office until noon on October 13, 2014.

To protect student privacy, the nominees are identified only as Lu ZM and Tian Y. The original OA notice provides the school’s contact details.
