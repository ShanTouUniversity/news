Shantou University’s Zhicheng College planned a hiking program around Nan’ao Island for November 15–16, 2014. The event, the college’s eighth physical-training camp, focused on environmental awareness, fitness and teamwork. The School of Science’s “Toward the Ocean” public-service course group was a partner, and the Nan’ao Field Station of STU’s Institute of Marine Biology provided support.

The route was to start at Changshanwei Ferry Terminal and pass through Nan’ao County town, Shen’ao, Xiongzhen Pass, Qing’ao Bay and Zhuxi Bay, ending at the Nan’ao Field Station. The full route was about 40 kilometers, with a planned hiking distance of 28 to 40 kilometers. The program was open to students across the university and planned to accept 42 participants: 32 from Zhicheng College and 10 from other residential areas. Faculty and staff were also welcome. Places were to be allocated in order of registration, with applications closing at 17:30 on October 19, 2014.

Participants were responsible for their own insurance and trail food costs. The program emphasized staying with the group and protecting the environment. Application forms and detailed requirements were provided in the OA attachments.
