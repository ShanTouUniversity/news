Shantou University’s English Debate Society planned a three-day STU CUP English debate open tournament from October 17 to 19, 2014. The programme was to include one day of training and two days of preliminary rounds under the British Parliamentary format. Trainers from Hong Kong, Macao and Singapore were expected to take part, alongside teams from universities across China.

The society invited students across the university to apply as debaters or volunteers. Applicants were asked to submit their role, year and programme, and course commitments during the event by October 12. Personal student contact details requested in the original notice are not reproduced here.
