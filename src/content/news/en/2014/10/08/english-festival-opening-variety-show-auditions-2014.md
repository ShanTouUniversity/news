Shantou University’s English Language Centre opened submissions for performances at the 2014 English Festival opening-night variety show. Students and faculty could apply as solo performers or groups. Auditions were scheduled for 2:00 p.m. on November 8 at the EAC, with registration by email due November 3.

Those selected at the audition were expected to perform at the festival opening celebration in the Great Hall at 7:30 p.m. on December 5. The notice described the recruitment and audition plan but did not identify selected performers or report the show’s outcome.
