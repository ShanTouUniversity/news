Shantou University’s Resources Management Office published student dormitory utility charges for September 2014, based on meter readings from September 5 to 30. Students could check their accounts through the campus utility management system. Those disputing a charge were asked to submit a signed review request to the utility centre during business hours from October 9 to 15.

The payment period was set for October 16–20, with campus-card debits planned to begin on October 16. Students with insufficient card balances were advised to add funds. Individual bills and student lists are not reproduced here.
