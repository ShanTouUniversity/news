Shantou University’s Business School committee completed its review for the 2014 graduate National Scholarship and proposed two graduate students for the award. The results were made public on October 8, 2014.

Written comments could be submitted to the committee by October 14 at the Graduate Office, Room 210, second floor of the West Wen building. To protect student privacy, the nominees are identified only as Ling H and Wei ZC.
