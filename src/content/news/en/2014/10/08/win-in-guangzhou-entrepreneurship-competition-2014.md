It is reported that a student team from Shantou University won second prize in the 3rd “Win in Guangzhou” College Entrepreneurship Competition with its project “Smart Factory,” receiving a 30,000 yuan entrepreneurship grant under the supervision of Prof. Xu Zongling.

## Award Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | 3rd “Win in Guangzhou” College Entrepreneurship Competition |
| Project | Smart Factory |
| Award | Second prize, 30,000 yuan grant |
| Supervisor | Prof. Xu Zongling |
| Team members | Chen L (Business Administration, Class of 2013, graduate), Deng LJ (Industrial Economics, Class of 2012, graduate), Ling H (Business Administration, Class of 2012, graduate), Chen XX (Electronics, Class of 2012), among others |

## Competition Overview

Organized by the Guangzhou Municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau and co-organized by the China Southern Talent Market and related employment service agencies, the competition launched in April 2014 and attracted more than 3,000 projects from over 80 universities across Guangdong. It comprised four stages: campus preliminaries, the “40 to 15” first final, the “15 to 7” second final, and the grand final. On campus, the Student Entrepreneurship Center and the Entrepreneurship Park Management Office coordinated preliminaries, recommended outstanding entries, and organized cheer squads for the live finals in Guangzhou.
