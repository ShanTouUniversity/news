Shantou University’s Business School opened registration for the 2014 Global Management Challenge (GMC). The competition, established in 1980, uses business-management simulation as its main format. Preliminary and semi-final rounds were to take place online, while the final was planned for Beijing. STU intended to provide pre-competition training and cover the related registration fees.

Undergraduate and graduate students could apply. Applicants needed to have completed at least two economics or management courses and be proficient in Excel. They could enter individually and be matched into teams by the organizers, or form teams of three to five members. Registration closed at midnight on October 12, 2014. Student contact numbers listed in the notice are not reproduced here.
