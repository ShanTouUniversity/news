*Fangyuan*, published by Shantou University’s Graduate School, and the graduate publication *Yanhui* invited submissions from all STU graduate students for their 2015 issues. The call covered academic papers in science, humanities and arts, as well as fiction, poetry, essays, travel writing and campus-life notes.

Academic submissions were expected to use reliable sources and accurate data and to demonstrate theoretical, scientific or practical value. Non-original literary works had to identify their source. The deadline was January 20, 2015, and submissions could be sent to the publications’ designated email address. Personal contact details requested for editorial follow-up are not reproduced here.
