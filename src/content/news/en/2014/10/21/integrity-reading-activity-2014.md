It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on October 21, 2014, announcing the launch of the "Read Integrity Books, Promote Clean Culture" series of activities, running from October 21 to 29, in accordance with the university party committee's discipline education campaign.

## Activity Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | "Read Integrity Books, Promote Clean Culture" Series Activities |
| Dates | October 21–29, 2014 |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee of Shantou University |
| Purpose | To guide young students to read integrity books, learn about integrity, and practice integrity |

The series aims to help young students develop admirable moral qualities — a respect for knowledge, courtesy, honesty, and self-discipline — while fostering a campus culture of integrity.
