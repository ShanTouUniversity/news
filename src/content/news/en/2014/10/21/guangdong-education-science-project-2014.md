It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University issued a notice on October 21, 2014, announcing that the application process for the 2014 Guangdong Education Science "12th Five-Year Plan" Project in Educational Information Technology Research has officially opened.

## Application Highlights

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project Category | Guangdong Education Science "12th Five-Year Plan" (Educational Information Technology Research Special) |
| Funding | Self-funded |
| Requirements | Applicants must have completed all previously funded national social science projects |
| Limitation | Each applicant may submit only one proposal |
| Encouragement | Units are encouraged to raise funds to support faculty applications |

For detailed application requirements and schedule, please refer to the original notice on the OA system.
