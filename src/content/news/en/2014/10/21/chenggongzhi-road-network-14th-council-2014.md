It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the election results for the 14th council of Chenggongzhi Road Network (成功之路网) on October 21, 2014. Yi Sihuа and twelve other students were elected as members of the 14th council.

## New Council Leadership

| Position | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Chief Executive Officer (CEO) | Tan Yongsheng |
| Chief Operating Officer (COO) | Mo Zhehao |
| Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) | Liu Bin |
| Chief Technology Officer (CTO) | Mai Yiyun |

Chenggongzhi Road Network is a student entrepreneurship practice platform guided by the Youth League Committee, dedicated to providing entrepreneurial guidance and practical opportunities for current students. The new council will continue to advance the platform's work under the new leadership team.
