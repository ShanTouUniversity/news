It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on October 21, 2014, announcing a medium-level outdoor expansion activity at Mount Tongguzhang in Meizhou from November 7 to 9, 2014.

## Activity Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Secret Path, Quiet Energy — Traverse the Dense Trail, Discover Inner Stillness |
| Dates | November 7–9, 2014 |
| Location | Mount Tongguzhang, Shatian Town, Fengshun County, Meizhou (1,559.5m, the highest peak in Eastern Guangdong) |
| Activities | Jungle trekking, peak climbing, steep slope scrambling, wild camping experience |

The activity promotes the LNT (Leave No Trace) outdoor ethics, urging participants to enjoy nature while protecting the environment — leaving only footprints and taking only memories.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
