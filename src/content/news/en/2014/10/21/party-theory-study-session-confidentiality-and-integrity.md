Shantou University’s Publicity Department announced that a Party committee study session was scheduled for the afternoon of October 22, 2014, in Room 213 of the New Administration Center. Topics included confidentiality and key provisions of the central government’s 2013–2017 plan for preventing and punishing corruption, along with Guangdong’s implementation measures.

The notice asked Party committee members, heads of Party general branches and directly affiliated branches, and relevant department heads to attend. The available notice did not report on the meeting’s outcome.
