It is reported that MYSTUdio of the Network and Information Center will hold a Movie Night on October 23, 2014.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Popcorn Night — film screening and review |
| Film | *Wall·E* |
| Time | 19:30–21:00, October 23, 2014 |
| Venue | MYSTUdio, 1st floor, Library |
| Places | 70 participants; successful applicants will be notified by email |
| Perks | Drinks and snacks for each attendee |
| Organizer | MYSTUdio |

Set in 2700, the film follows waste-collecting robot Wall·E, the last of his kind on an Earth turned into a landfill, and his encounter with probe robot EVE, whose discovery suggests Wall·E may hold the key to Earth’s future.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration opens | October 21, 2014 |
| How to register | Log in to MYSTU, go to the event page “MYSTUdio—Movie Night 20141023” and click “I want to join” |
| Check-in | Those notified by email should report to MYSTUdio at 19:15 on October 23 |
| Inquiries | Leave questions in the MYSTU event discussion area for MYSTUdio to reply |

> ⚠️ The registration link is available on the OA system.
>
