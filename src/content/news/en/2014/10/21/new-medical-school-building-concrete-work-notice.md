In a notice issued on October 21, 2014, Shantou University’s major projects office said continuous concrete pouring was planned from 8 a.m. that day until 2 a.m. on October 23 in sections II and III of the fifth-floor beams and slab of the south tower at the New Medical School site.

The work was not suitable for interruption and was expected to cause substantial noise for nearby residents. The notice records the construction plan announced at the time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
