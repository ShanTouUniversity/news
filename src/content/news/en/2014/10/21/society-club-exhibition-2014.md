It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on October 21, 2014, announcing that the 2013–2014 Spring Semester Student Society Project Exhibition Fair will be held at Room G4024 at 2:00 p.m. on October 22, featuring eight student clubs.

## Participating Clubs

| Club | Activities |
| --- | --- |
| Lantern Riddle Enthusiasts Association | Traditional lantern riddle games and folk activities |
| Guitar Association | Guitar performance and exchange |
| Xingchao Society | Teochew culture and art showcase |
| Intellectual Property Association | IP knowledge popularization |
| Roller Skating Association | Roller skating performance and experience |
| Jianqiu Association | Featherball sports demonstration |
| Sign Language Club | Sign language exchange and performance |
| Science Fiction Association | Sci-fi works sharing and creative display |

The exhibition is open to all faculty and students. Those interested are welcome to attend and interact with the clubs.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
