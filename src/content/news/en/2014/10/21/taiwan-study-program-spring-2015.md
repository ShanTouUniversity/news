It is reported that the International Exchange and Cooperation Office of Shantou University launched the Taiwan Study Program for Spring 2015 on October 21, 2014. The program runs from February to June 2015 at three partner universities in Taiwan.

## Program Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Destination | Three universities in Taiwan |
| Duration | February–June 2015 |
| Eligibility | Second- and third-year undergraduate students (excluding medical majors) |
| Application Deadline | Before October 27, 2014 — submit application to the academic affairs office of your college |

## Eligibility Criteria

- Good academic standing with no failed courses in the past year
- Love for the motherland and law-abiding
- Tuition and living expenses are the responsibility of the student
- Courses taken at the Taiwanese institutions will be recognized by STU, with credit transfer upon return

Interested students should submit their applications to their college's academic affairs office before October 27, 2014. Detailed information on the partner institutions and program description can be found in the OA notice attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (International Exchange and Cooperation Office)
