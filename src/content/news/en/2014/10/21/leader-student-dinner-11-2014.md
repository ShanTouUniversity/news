It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on October 21, 2014, announcing the successful applicants for the 11th President–Student Dinner.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Thursday, October 23, 2014, 18:15 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Participants | 32 successfully registered students |
| Check-in | Bring your campus card and arrive 15 minutes early (by 18:00) |

The President–Student Dinner is an important channel for direct communication between university leadership and students, designed to foster teacher–student interaction and gather student feedback and suggestions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
