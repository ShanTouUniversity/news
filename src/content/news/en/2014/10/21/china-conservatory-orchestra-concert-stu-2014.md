It is reported that the Yangtze College of Art and Design and the Arts Education Center of Shantou University will co-host a concert by the China Conservatory Chamber Orchestra, featuring 14 artists performing a series of classic masterpieces for the STU community.

## Performance Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Hosts | Yangtze College of Art and Design, Arts Education Center |
| Performing Ensemble | China Conservatory Chamber Orchestra |
| Artists | Hou Junxia (double bass), Fang Yuan (violin), Matthias Buchner (German/Swiss, violin/viola), Zhao Jianan (cello), and 10 more artists |
| Repertoire | Mozart's Piano Quartet No. 1 in G minor, K478; "Love of Marimba"; Mozart's Flute Quartet in D major, K285; and other classic works |

The concert offers STU faculty and students a free opportunity to enjoy a high-level chamber orchestra performance, aiming to enrich campus cultural life and enhance artistic appreciation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Yangtze College of Art and Design, Arts Education Center)
