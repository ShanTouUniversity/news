It is reported that Prof. Zhang Xiaowei from the School of Environment at Nanjing University will deliver an academic talk at Shantou University on the morning of Monday, October 27, 2014.

## Talk Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Next-Generation Environmental Biomonitoiriting and Chemical Environmental Risk Assessment Technologies |
| Speaker | Zhang Xiaowei (Professor, School of Environment, Nanjing University; PhD Supervisor) |
| Time | Monday, October 27, 2014, 09:00–10:00 |
| Location | Room 504, 5th Floor, Science and Technology Center |

## About the Speaker

Prof. Zhang Xiaowei holds a Ph.D. in Zoology and Environmental Toxicology from Michigan State University, USA. He is currently a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Environment, Nanjing University. He has received talent program support from the Ministry of Education's "New Century Excellent Talents" program and the National Natural Science Foundation of China's "Excellent Young Scientists Fund." His main research interests include environmental biomonitoring and chemical environmental risk assessment.

Faculty and students in environmental science, toxicology, and related fields are welcome to attend.
