It is reported that, in preparation for the Guangdong Provincial Education Department's 2014 on-campus inspection of equipment construction and laboratory safety management at universities across the province in mid-November, Shantou University will conduct a campus-wide laboratory safety inspection from November 6 to 7. Organized by the Laboratory Safety Construction and Management Committee, the inspection aims to strengthen laboratory construction and safety management.

## Inspection Contents

The inspection covers three main items:

1. Verifying the completion of the "Laboratory Safety Rectification Task Follow-up Table" for each college, assigned at the laboratory safety work meeting held on October 20;
2. Hearing colleges' self-inspection reports on existing problems in laboratory safety construction and management, along with their suggestions for rectification and improvement;
3. Conducting on-site inspections of the safety environment and cleanliness of each laboratory, and assisting laboratories in formulating rectification plans.

## Schedule and Work Requirements

The specific inspection schedule is detailed in the "Laboratory Safety Work Inspection Time Table". Units wishing to request changes to the schedule should provide feedback to the Equipment and Laboratory Management Office before the end of the workday on November 3.

Each college (center) is required to designate a work contact person and submit their contact information (phone number and office address) to the Equipment and Laboratory Management Office by November 3. During the inspection, the designated contact persons must be present to coordinate and guide the inspection team, and college (center) leaders in charge are expected to accompany the inspections.

All units should carefully organize their own laboratory safety self-inspections and report and rectify any problems in a timely manner. For inquiries, contact Zhu Yueke of the Equipment and Laboratory Management Office (office phone: 86503141, email: ykzhu@stu.edu.cn).
