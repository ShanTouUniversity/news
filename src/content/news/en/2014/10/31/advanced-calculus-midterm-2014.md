It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University has announced that the midterm exam for the [MAT2801A] Advanced Calculus course will be held on Tuesday of Week 7 (November 4) during class periods 3 and 4.

## Exam Arrangements

The exam covers three class sections, with venues, instructors, and student numbers as follows:

| Class No. | Instructor | Venue | Students |
|-----------|-----------|-------|----------|
| 72616 | Lin Xiaoping | Lecture Hall 5 | 134 |
| 72617 | Tan Chaoqiang | Lecture Hall 4 | 134 |
| 72619 | Li Jian | Room 503, Building D | 115 |

Seating in Lecture Hall 5 will be arranged by the instructor on site. Students who have applied for course exemption are also required to attend the exam on time.
