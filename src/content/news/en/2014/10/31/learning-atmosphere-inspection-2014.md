It is reported that STU's study atmosphere inspection team carried out the fifth-week inspection on teaching buildings on the mornings of October 23 and 24, and the results were notified to all colleges and relevant departments by the Student Affairs Office on October 31.

The inspections focused on the first class in the morning. The details are as follows:

| Time | Location | Wearing slippers | Breakfast in classroom | Late within 5 min | Late over 5 min |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oct 23 (Thu) 08:00 | Building D, Room 504 | 1 | 4 | 9 | 15 |
| Oct 24 (Fri) 08:00 | Building E | — | — | — | 1 |

According to the data, irregularities were most concentrated in Room 504 of Building D on October 23, where 29 cases of wearing slippers, bringing breakfast or arriving late were recorded, including 15 students who were more than five minutes late. The situation at Building E on October 24 was relatively better, with only one student arriving more than five minutes late.

The Student Affairs Office stated that the list of students who failed to strictly observe school rules and regulations has been notified to all colleges, and colleges (residential colleges) are asked to conduct criticism and education for the relevant students and persist in strengthening the construction of study atmosphere. Students with questions may call the Student Education Management Section at 86504749.
