It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on October 31, 2014, announcing the shortlist for the 2013–2014 Academic Excellence Scholarships.

The scholarships were established to encourage diligent study and outstanding academic achievement. Under the *Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate Scholarships of Shantou University* (STU Document [2014] No. 83), eligibility is determined by GPA percentile within the same grade and major: top 5% for first prize (1,500 yuan), top 15% for second prize (1,000 yuan), and top 30% for third prize (500 yuan) per academic year.

## Announcement Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Shortlist | 167 first-prize, 427 second-prize and 605 third-prize recipients (see OA attachments; names withheld for privacy) |
| Basis | STU Document [2014] No. 83, GPA percentile ranking |
| Application | Submitted by students and reviewed by the university |
| Deadline for objections | 17:00, November 3, 2014 |
| Contact | Student Affairs Office, Financial Aid Management Center, Administration Center Room 106 (units should affix an official seal, individuals should provide real name) |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

> ⚠️ The full shortlist is available in the OA system.
