It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on October 31, 2014, announcing the shortlist for the 2013–2014 Academic Excellence Scholarships. A total of 1,199 students from the schools on the main campus were shortlisted: 167 for the first-prize scholarship, 427 for the second prize and 605 for the third prize.

The university established the Academic Excellence Scholarship to encourage students to study diligently, pursue in-depth research and achieve outstanding academic results. The scholarship offers 1,500 yuan per student per year for the first prize, 1,000 yuan for the second prize and 500 yuan for the third prize. Under the *Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate Scholarships of Shantou University* (STU Document [2014] No. 83), selection is based on GPA percentile within the same grade and major: the top 5% are eligible for the first prize, the top 15% for the second prize and the top 30% for the third prize.
