It is reported that Shantou University's Graduate School will hold a meeting for class monitors of the 2015 graduating class on Monday, November 3, at 3:00 pm in Conference Room 330 of the new Administration Building.

The meeting will explain employment-related procedures and distribute employment materials for graduates, including the Employment Agreement, the Graduate Registration Form and the Employment Recommendation Form. Class monitors (program coordinators) are expected to attend on time; if a monitor is unable to attend, another class cadre should attend on their behalf.
