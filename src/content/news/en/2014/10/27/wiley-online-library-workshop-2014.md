## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Wiley Online Library: Powering Your Research and Study |
| Content | Introduction to Wiley, overview of Wiley Online Library resources, platform user guide, Wiley China social media |
| Speaker | Representative, Institutional Services, Wiley China (name garbled in the original notice) |
| Time | 19:30–20:30, October 27, 2014 |
| Venue | Library Subject Services Department (inside the second-floor reference desk) |
| Organizer | Library |

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
