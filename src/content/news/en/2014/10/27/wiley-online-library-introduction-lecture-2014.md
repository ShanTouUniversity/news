It is reported that Shantou University Library will host a lecture on the Wiley Online Library on October 27, presented by Mr./Ms. Zhang from Wiley China Institutional Services, introducing this important academic resource platform to the university community.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Mr./Ms. Zhang (Wiley China Institutional Services) |
| Time | 19:30-20:30, Monday, October 27, 2014 |
| Venue | Subject Services Department, Library (2nd floor, inside consultation desk) |

The lecture will cover an introduction to the Wiley company, an overview of Wiley Online Library's online resources, a guide to platform usage, and information about Wiley China's social media channels. The session aims to help faculty and students better understand and utilize this database resource. All are welcome to attend.
