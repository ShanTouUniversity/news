It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has officially launched the 22nd "Campus Top Ten Singers" Competition series. As one of the most influential cultural events on campus, this year's competition will span over two months across multiple stages.

## Event Schedule

| Stage | Date | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freshman Host Audition | November 1 | — |
| Freshman Host Selection | November 5 | — |
| Community Preliminaries | November 15 | Various communities |
| Advancement Qualification | November 29 | — |
| College Auditions | Before November 29 | Each college |
| Semi-final | December 13 | Auditorium |
| Grand Final | December 27 | Auditorium |

According to organizers, the freshman host selection aims to discover outstanding hosting talent for the competition. College-level auditions will be held separately across campuses before November 29, with successful candidates advancing to the semi-final on December 13 and the grand final on December 27. The competition is expected to be a highlight of campus cultural life this semester.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
