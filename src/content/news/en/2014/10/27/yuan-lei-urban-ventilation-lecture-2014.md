It is reported that the College of Engineering of Shantou University will host a lecture by Dr. Yuan Lei, Associate Professor at the College of Architecture and Urban Planning, Shenzhen University, on the afternoon of October 28, 2014.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Research on Urban Natural Ventilation Assessment Methods — A Case Study of Shenzhen |
| Speaker | Yuan Lei (Associate Professor, College of Architecture and Urban Planning, Shenzhen University) |
| Time | Wednesday, October 28, 2014, 16:00–18:00 |
| Location | Room 312, Gongnan Building (Engineering Academic Exchange Room) |

The lecture is open to faculty and students of the Department of Civil Engineering at STU's College of Engineering, as well as all interested members of the university community.
