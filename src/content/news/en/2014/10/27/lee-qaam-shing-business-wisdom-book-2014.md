It is reported that the Shantou University Library recommended a new book titled *Lee Kum-Shing's Business Wisdom* by Chen Yuxin to faculty and students on October 27, 2014. The book provides a comprehensive interpretation of the business acumen and financial wisdom of Lee Kum-Shing, one of the wealthiest individuals in the Chinese-speaking world.

## About the Book

Lee Kum-Shing has been in business for several decades, navigating through two oil crises, the Asian financial storm, and the global financial crisis. His business empire spans 55 countries and regions. The book delves into his decision-making logic and business wisdom in complex economic environments, offering valuable insights into personal finance management.

The book is now available for borrowing at the library.
