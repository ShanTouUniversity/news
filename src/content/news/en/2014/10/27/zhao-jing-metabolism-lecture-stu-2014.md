It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University will host Prof. Zhao Jing from the Fourth Military Medical University for an academic lecture on October 29.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Integration and Regulation of Substance Metabolism |
| Speaker | Zhao Jing (Fourth Military Medical University) |
| Time | 8:00-10:00 AM, Wednesday, October 29, 2014 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall, Science and Technology Building |

Prof. Zhao currently serves as Deputy Director of the Teaching and Research Section of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the Department of Basic Medicine, Fourth Military Medical University, as well as a master's supervisor and a member of the PLA National Biochemistry Professional Committee. His lecture will provide an in-depth exploration of the integration and regulation mechanisms of substance metabolism. Faculty and students with interested are welcome to attend.
