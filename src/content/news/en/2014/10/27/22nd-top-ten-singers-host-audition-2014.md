It is reported that the University Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the launch of the host audition for the 22nd Campus Top Ten Singers Competition on October 27, 2014, recruiting hosts from among the 2014 freshman cohort.

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Grade requirement | 2014 incoming freshmen |
| Communication skills | Strong language organization and expression abilities |
| Mandarin | Standard and fluent pronunciation |
| Appearance | Neat and pleasant features, confident and enthusiastic hosting style |
| Quick thinking | Agile思维 and flexible problem-solving |

## How to Apply

Interested students can download the application form from the campus internal affairs network and send it to `13smzhan@stu.edu.cn`.

The application deadline is **10:00 PM on October 29, 2014**.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Youth League Committee)
