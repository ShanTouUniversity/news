It is reported that the "Toward Modern Mathematics" lecture series at Shantou University's College of Science will feature a new session on Monday, October 27, at 4:30 p.m. in the Mathematics Department Laboratory. Professor Li Zhongkai of the School of Mathematical Sciences at Capital Normal University will speak on "Inversion of the Attenuated X-ray Transform by Continuous Wavelet Transform."

Li is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Capital Normal University. He graduated from the Department of Mathematics at Shandong University in 1985, received his Ph.D. from Dalian University of Technology in 1992, and conducted postdoctoral research at Jilin University from 1992 to 1994. He was named a Beijing Youth Academic Leader in 1996 with funding from Beijing's Cross-Century Talent Program, and in 2002 received support from the Ministry of Education's Program for Outstanding Young Teachers.

**Speaker Profile**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Position | Professor and doctoral supervisor, Capital Normal University |
| Research Areas | Harmonic analysis, Dunkl theory, Radon transform, function approximation |
| Key Contributions | The "Banach operator pair" concept; advances in generalized Radon transform research |
| Research Grants | Completed 5 NSFC projects, 5 Beijing Natural Science Foundation projects, among others |

According to the college, Li's work has drawn wide attention and citations from international peers. His paper with Song Futao on the generalized Radon transform in the plane was praised as "an important advance in the field of the Radon transform," and the "Banach operator pair" concept he proposed with Chen Jianren has become a widely used notion in fixed point theory. He has also made numerous academic visits to the United States, Germany, Japan, and other countries and regions.

The lecture is open to all faculty and students.
