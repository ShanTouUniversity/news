It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University issued an explanation of the reward policy for parents of only children on October 27, 2014. In accordance with the *Guangdong Population and Family Planning Regulations*, rewards are provided to parents of only children who are urban residents within the administrative region of Guangdong Province and have practiced family planning.

## Eligibility and Benefit Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible recipients | Fathers aged 60 or above, mothers aged 55 or above |
| Residency requirement | Urban household registration in Guangdong Province |
| Benefit standard | No less than 80 RMB per person per month |

Qualified faculty and staff members may apply for the reward in accordance with relevant regulations.
