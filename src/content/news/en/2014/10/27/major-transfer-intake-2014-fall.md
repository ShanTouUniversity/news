It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on October 27, 2014, asking all programs to submit information on accepting inter-major transfers for the fall 2014 semester for students of the 2013 cohort.

According to Article 21 of the *Shantou University Undergraduate Student Status Management Regulations* (STU Document [2013] No. 67), students may apply to transfer from one major to another within the university based on expertise, interests and aspirations. Procedures are as follows: transfer applications are limited to the first and second spring semesters and the second fall semester after enrollment; each program announces intake numbers and specific requirements before week 5; students submit an application form signed by their department chair and dean before week 8 to the intended program; after review and assessment by the receiving program, materials are sent to the Academic Affairs Office before week 10 for review; the office then publicizes the proposed list for one week and issues the transfer decision before week 12, with transferred students studying in the new major from the next semester. Students admitted under sports, music and fine arts categories may not transfer across categories, and students from other categories may not transfer into these categories.

For the current semester, transfers for the 2013 cohort are open. Programs are asked to complete the *Summary Table of Information on Accepting Transfer Applications* and submit it to the Academic Affairs Section by November 25, with the electronic version sent to xhx@stu.edu.cn. Details on intake numbers and requirements for each program are in the attachments.

> ⚠️ Related forms are available on the OA system.
