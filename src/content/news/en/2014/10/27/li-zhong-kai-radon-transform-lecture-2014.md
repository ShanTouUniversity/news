It is reported that the College of Science at Shantou University will host Prof. Li Zhongkai from the School of Mathematics at Capital Normal University for an academic lecture on October 27.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Inversion of the Attenuated X-ray Transform by Continuous Wavelet Transform |
| Speaker | Li Zhongkai (Capital Normal University) |
| Time | 4:30 PM, Monday, October 27, 2014 |
| Venue | Mathematics Department Laboratory |

Prof. Li is a doctoral supervisor whose main research areas include harmonic analysis, Dunkl theory, Radon transform, and function approximation. The lecture will present recent research findings on inverting the attenuated X-ray transform using continuous wavelet transforms. Faculty and students in related fields are welcome to attend.
