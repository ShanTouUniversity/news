Shantou University’s Student Organization Federation office resumed duty hours on October 20, 2014, to serve student organizations and clubs. The office was located in Room 731 of Building 7, with the notice setting hours from 4:15 to 5:30 p.m., Monday through Friday.

These are historical office hours published in 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee)
