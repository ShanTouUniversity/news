Shantou University’s Center for Women’s Studies scheduled a public academic lecture titled “Technology Development and Women’s Empowerment” for October 22, 2014. The talk was to examine the relationship between technological change and women’s everyday practices, drawing on feminist technology studies and the history of technological development. The notice did not report on what followed.

Speaker Ren Jue holds a doctorate in gender studies from the Chinese University of Hong Kong and has worked in investigative journalism, nonprofit communications and disaster-response information services.
