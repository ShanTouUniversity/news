Shantou University opened its 2013–14 assessment for undergraduate academic excellence scholarships, special scholarships and outstanding student scholarships. Eligible main-campus undergraduates were asked to apply online and submit required materials to their college or residential college by 5 p.m. on October 27, 2014.

Applicants for academic excellence scholarships were required to rank in the top 30% of their year and major and have no failing grades. Outstanding student scholarship applicants had to rank in the top 10%, have no failing grades, and meet criteria related to campus activities, public service and recognition. Special awards covered academic and technological achievement, sports and arts, social service and leadership, among other categories.

The 2014 assessment process was scheduled to continue through late November, with recommendations and selections handled by the university and its colleges. These are historical award arrangements.
