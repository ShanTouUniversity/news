Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced on October 20, 2014, that the university would conduct family-planning checks and a related survey in November. Checks were scheduled for November 3–5, from 14:30 to 17:00 each day, in the ultrasound room on the second floor of the university clinic. They were intended for married female faculty and staff under 50, including regular employees, agency personnel and auxiliary contract staff.

The notice also called for a survey of married male employees whose spouses worked at another organization or outside the area. Unit coordinators were to collect survey letters from the clinic’s family-planning office and return them within one month. The notice said completion of the work was linked to the distribution of family-planning awards.
