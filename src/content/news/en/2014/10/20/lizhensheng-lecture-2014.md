It is reported that the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication of Shantou University issued a notice on October 20, 2014, announcing that renowned photojournalist Li Zhensheng will deliver an academic lecture at the Library Lecture Hall on October 24.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | "Let History Tell the Future" |
| Speaker | Li Zhensheng |
| Time | Friday, October 24, 2014, 19:00–22:00 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication |

## About the Speaker

Li Zhensheng graduated from the Photography Department of the Changchun Film Academy in 1963. He worked as a photojournalist at the Heilongjiang Daily for 20 years and later taught news photography at the Journalism Department of the Beijing International Politics College for 15 years. In 1996, he was invited by Harvard University to visit and lecture in the United States and currently resides in New York. In 2005, he was named one of the "54 Greatest News Photographers of the Past 150 Years Since 1855."
