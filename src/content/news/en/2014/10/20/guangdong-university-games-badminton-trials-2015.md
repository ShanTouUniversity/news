In preparation for the ninth Guangdong University Games, scheduled for 2015, Shantou University’s Physical Education Department opened badminton trials to undergraduate and graduate students on the main campus. Events included men’s and women’s singles and doubles, as well as mixed doubles.

The university planned to hold trials in its gymnasium on November 15, 16 and 19, 2014. The top eight finishers in singles and top four pairs in each doubles event were eligible for registration as STU representatives; the head coach was to select the final competition roster under the event rules. Registration closed on October 27, 2014.
