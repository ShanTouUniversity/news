Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office circulated a notice for the second online training program for university counselors organized by the National Academy of Education Administration. The program aimed to strengthen counselors’ professional competence and work skills.

Internal registration closed at 5 p.m. on October 22, 2014, and the university covered the training costs. Further program details were provided in the original attachments. Personal registration information is not reproduced here. These were the arrangements announced in 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
