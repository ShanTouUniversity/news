It is reported that the Library issued a notice on October 20, 2014, to launch a discount promotion for the China Academic Social Sciences and Humanities Library (CASHL) covering South China (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan) from October 20 to November 3.

## Promotion

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| CASHL journal articles | 100% subsidy |
| CASHL special collections | 50% subsidy |
| CASHL books (selected chapters) | 50% subsidy |
| Eligible users | Direct users of CASHL in South China |

Notes: Interlibrary loan requests and book borrowing are excluded; requests for special collections and books should be made via email.

## How to Use

**New users**

1. Register on the CASHL website (http://www.cashl.edu.cn) via “Register” at the top right;
2. Send registration details (username, name, department, year and campus card number) to yxu@stu.edu.cn for account confirmation;
3. Log in as a “CASHL Direct User” to search journals or articles and submit requests. See the library link for the detailed guide.

**Existing users** may log in with their original credentials; accounts with issues may request reactivation via email.

CASHL was launched on March 15, 2004, and holds nearly 20,000 foreign humanities and social science core journals, 1,956 e-journals, 350,000 e-books, 1.12 million foreign books and related databases, offering search, interlibrary loan and document delivery services.
