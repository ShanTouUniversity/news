It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Leading Group for Student Extracurricular Academic and Science Activities issued a notice on October 20, 2014, to carry out the midterm review for the 2014 extracurricular academic projects.

Since the launch of the program, units have advanced work in accordance with university regulations. To evaluate progress and ensure follow-up work, relevant units and individuals are asked to complete the midterm review and submit midterm summaries.

## Submission

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | Midterm summary and summary table of projects after midterm review (see attachment) |
| Electronic version | Submit to the Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by 17:00, October 31, 2014 |
| Hard copy | Stamp with the college seal and submit to the Youth League Committee for archiving |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee, Leading Group for Student Extracurricular Academic and Science Activities |

Relevant colleges and units are asked to submit on time.

> ⚠️ The summary table and attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee)
