It is reported that the University Library of Shantou University issued a notice on October 20, 2014, announcing a special exhibition of Wen Danming's literary works, showcasing his lifelong contributions to education, journalism, cultural scholarship, and local chronicles.

## Exhibition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exhibition Name | Wen Danming Literature Special Collection Exhibition |
| Venue | University Library, Shantou University |
| Organizer | University Library, Shantou University |

Wen Danming (1869–1954) was a distinguished scholar and poet of modern Eastern Guangdong. In 1902, he co-founded the Lingdong Tongwen Academy with Qiu Fengjia and others, pioneering modern education in the region. The exhibition presents his enduring academic and cultural legacy. Faculty and students are welcome to visit.
