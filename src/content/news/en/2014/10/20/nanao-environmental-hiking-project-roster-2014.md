Shantou University’s Zhicheng College announced the selection results for its “Protect Nan’ao, Head for the Sea: A Hundred-Li Hike” around-island event. The college received 399 applications and expanded the number of places to more than twice the original allocation. It selected 84 students and 10 staff members, with 20 students named as alternates.

Selected participants were asked at the time to pay a RMB 100 deposit at the Zhicheng College office, Room J-338, by the stated deadline. The deposit was non-refundable for participants who failed to take part as required. Student names are not reproduced here.
