It is reported that the Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication of Shantou University issued a notice on October 20, 2014, announcing that the second session of the Michael Yamashita Photography Workshop — a photography critique meeting — will be held on October 22.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | Michael Yamashita Photography Workshop Session 2 — Photography Critique Meeting |
| Time | Wednesday, October 22, 2014, 14:00–18:00 |
| Participants | 55 (by prior registration) |
| Organizer | Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication |

This session is the second event of the Michael Yamashita Photography Workshop, aiming to enhance the photography skills and aesthetic appreciation of participants through professional critique and instruction.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Changjiang School of Journalism and Communication)
