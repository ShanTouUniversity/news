It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism issued Document No. 01 [2014] on October 20, 2014, to establish program director posts for four majors to advance undergraduate education and program development.

Following deliberation by the school’s administrative committee, appointments are as follows:

| Program | Appointee |
| --- | --- |
| Journalism | Zhang Wei |
| Radio and Television Studies | Hu Lufeng |
| Editing and Publishing | Yun Qidong |
| Advertising | Daniel Ng |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism)
