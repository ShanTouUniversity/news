Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled a meeting for class representatives of the 2015 undergraduate graduating class for the afternoon of October 21, 2014, in Room E203. Topics included collecting graduate information, explaining employment procedures and distributing employment materials.

Colleges were asked to arrange for their graduating-class representatives to attend. Another class officer could take their place if necessary. The available OA text was a pre-meeting notice and did not report an outcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
