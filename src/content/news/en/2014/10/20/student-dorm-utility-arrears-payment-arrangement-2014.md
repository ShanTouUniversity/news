In a 2014 notice, Shantou University’s Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Resources Management Office said some dormitories had outstanding utility bills for September and earlier. The university planned temporary power cuts for those dormitories beginning October 22, with service to be restored after payment.

The notice described two campus-card payment deductions per day from October 22 to 24, with electricity restoration scheduled according to payment time; after October 24, deductions were to take place once daily. These procedures applied to the 2014 notice only and are not current guidance. The account number in the original notice is omitted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Resources Management Office)
