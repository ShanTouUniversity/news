Shantou University’s Finance Office upgraded its financial inquiry system in 2014 and reset users’ personal inquiry passwords. The university directed users to the login instructions distributed with the notice. Its online reimbursement module was still being tested and was not yet available at that time.

The notice listed 86503358 and 86502446 for assistance. This information describes the 2014 upgrade and is not current system guidance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office)
> ⚠️ The historical login-instruction attachment is available through the original OA notice and should not be treated as current guidance.
