Following interviews and review, Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design proposed two students from the 2011 cohort for the 2014 National Scholarship. They were enrolled in Graphic Design and Public Art, respectively.

Objections could be submitted to the school’s Youth League office by noon on October 13, 2014. To protect student privacy, the nominees are identified only as Zhang Y and Guo DB.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
