Shantou University’s Engineering School planned a career forum on the information technology industry from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on October 9, 2014, in Room E401. The invited speaker was Dai Yi, general manager of Shenzhen Xunfang Communications Technology.

Dai had worked at Huawei before moving into business and human-resources management at Xunfang. He also took part in university-industry partnerships and academic committees at other universities. The forum was open to the campus community and was expected to cover industry trends and career development. The notice did not report the event’s outcome.
