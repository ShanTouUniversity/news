Shantou University’s Admissions Office advertised two student-assistant positions in October 2014 to support recruitment outreach and consultation. Duties included sharing university and admissions information on online platforms, responding to inquiries, helping host visiting primary- and secondary-school groups, and assisting with outreach events and document preparation.

Applicants were expected to have strong communication, computer and online skills, as well as reliability. Previous participation in admissions consultation or related outreach was an advantage. The positions were paid under the university’s work-study rates. Applications were due by 5:00 p.m. on October 16, with shortlisted candidates to be interviewed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Admissions Office)
> ⚠️ The application form is available as an attachment in the OA system.
