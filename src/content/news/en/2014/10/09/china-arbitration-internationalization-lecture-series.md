Shantou University’s Law School planned to join the Guangdong Lawyers Association, the South China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission, and bar associations in Shantou, Jieyang and Chaozhou for a legal seminar titled “The Internationalization of Chinese Arbitration and Opportunities for Guangdong Lawyers.” The event was scheduled for 9:30 a.m. to noon on October 11, 2014, in Conference Room 3 of the Academic and Cultural Centre.

Three talks were planned on modernizing international commercial arbitration and the role of Guangdong lawyers, interpreting arbitration rules and drafting dispute-resolution clauses, and selecting and applying arbitration procedures. Speakers listed were Liu Xiaochun, secretary-general of the arbitration commission; deputy director An Xin; and arbitrator Wang Qianhua. The notice did not confirm whether the event took place.
