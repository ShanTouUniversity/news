Shantou University’s Resources Management Office planned to clear apparently abandoned vehicles from campus between October 25 and 28, 2014. The notice covered vehicles left for long periods that occupied limited parking space and showed clear signs of disuse. Owners were asked to remove them by October 25; remaining vehicles were to be moved to an area in front of the east-gate transformer station.

Owners whose vehicles were mistakenly removed could contact campus security and claim them by December 1. Unclaimed vehicles after that date were to be handled under national and university rules.
