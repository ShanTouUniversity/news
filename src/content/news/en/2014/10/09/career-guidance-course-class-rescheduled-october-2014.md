Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced on October 9, 2014, that a session of Career Guidance and Training, class 72431, would be cancelled because of a scheduling change. The class had been scheduled for periods 8–9 on Thursday of Week 3 in Room G404.

The make-up session was scheduled for periods 8–9 on Thursday of Week 13, also in Room G404. The original notice asked enrolled students to share the update with classmates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
