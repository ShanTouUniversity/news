Shantou University’s Engineering School opened campus selection for a 2014 C programming skills contest. The competition had separate divisions for computer-science and other majors. Online preliminary rounds were scheduled for October 25, with an on-site final for qualifying contestants on November 8. Participants needed a foundation in C programming and were expected to attend pre-contest training.

Students were asked to register by October 12, 2014. Computer-science undergraduates who had already registered did not need to submit a second application. The school planned to organize instructor-led selection and training; personal student identifiers and contact details required for registration are not reproduced.
