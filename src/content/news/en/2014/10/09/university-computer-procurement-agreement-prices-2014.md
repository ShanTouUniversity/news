To streamline computer purchases, Shantou University established long-term supply arrangements in 2014 with vendors for Lenovo, HP, Dell and Apple products. Participating suppliers offered computers with services including delivery, installation of licensed operating systems and configuration. The university published prices and specifications for faculty and staff to compare.

Employees could select a suitable model and submit a purchase plan to the Equipment Management Office for consolidation. The notice said prices and models would be updated monthly and invited staff to monitor the offers. Detailed configurations and price lists were attached to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
> ⚠️ Model specifications and price lists are available as OA attachments.
