Shantou University’s Party Affairs Office asked campus units in October 2014 to compile information on off-campus appointments and part-time roles held by serving and retired leaders at or above deputy-division level. Units were asked to submit the completed form in print and electronically by noon on October 11.

Current staff appointments were to be reported by their respective units, while the retired-faculty Party branch was responsible for information on retired personnel. The attached spreadsheet contained internal personal appointment information and is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The internal reporting form is available in the OA system.
