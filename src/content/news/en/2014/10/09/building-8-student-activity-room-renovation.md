Shantou University planned to convert a former retail unit on the east side of the third floor of Building 8 into a Youth League student activity room. The project was intended to support facilities for an international kindergarten class and improve infrastructure for student clubs. The notice said construction by Shantou Jinhai Construction Co. had started on October 4, 2014, and was expected to finish by November 17.

The work could produce noise, vibration and dust. The university advised pedestrians to take care and avoid lingering near the site. The notice did not confirm the project’s eventual completion.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office, circulated by Resources Management Office)
