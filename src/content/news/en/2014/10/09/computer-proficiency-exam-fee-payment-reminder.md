Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office said on October 9, 2014, that examination fees for some applicants to the year’s second computer proficiency test had not been collected because their campus-card balances were too low. Affected students were asked to add funds by noon the following day so the Finance Office could make another debit that afternoon. A second failed debit would result in an unsuccessful registration.

The list of students with failed payments accompanied the OA notice and is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The list of failed payments is available as an OA attachment.
