The National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) and the Israel Science Foundation announced a 2015 call for joint research projects to support collaboration between scientists in the two countries. Eligible areas were mathematics, earth sciences, nanotechnology and computer science. Projects were to run from October 2015 through September 2018, with up to 25 awards planned. The NSFC offered up to RMB 2 million per Chinese project, matched by support from the Israel Science Foundation for Israeli researchers.

Chinese applicants needed a senior professional title and had to be leading or have led an NSFC project lasting at least three years. Applications were to be jointly prepared by Chinese and Israeli researchers in both Chinese and English. The deadline was 4:00 p.m. on January 7, 2015. The call document provided full eligibility and submission instructions.
