Hutchison Whampoa Property Group planned a dedicated recruitment event at Shantou University from October 14 to 16, 2014. The university’s Student Affairs Office circulated the recruitment schedule and job openings to graduating students.

Applicants were asked to download and complete a position application form by October 14, then deliver it to the company’s recruitment staff at the campus exhibition from 9:00 a.m. to noon on October 15. Graduates attending the company presentation, exhibition or second-round interview were advised to wear formal attire. The full schedule and list of vacancies were attached to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The recruitment schedule and vacancy list are available as OA attachments.
