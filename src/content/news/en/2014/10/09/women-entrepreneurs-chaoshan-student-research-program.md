Shantou University’s Women’s Studies Centre opened a student research programme on “Women Entrepreneurs in the Local Economy and Culture of Eastern Guangdong.” Undergraduate and graduate students could apply individually or in teams of up to four; cross-disciplinary teams were encouraged. Proposals were expected to use empirical research to examine local women entrepreneurs and their contributions to economic, social and cultural development.

The centre planned to fund eight to ten selected projects at RMB 2,000–3,000 each. The research period was November 2014 to March 2015, with faculty mentoring and progress reviews. Applications were due October 29, 2014, and selections were expected in early November. A presentation and exchange of research findings was scheduled for April 2015.
