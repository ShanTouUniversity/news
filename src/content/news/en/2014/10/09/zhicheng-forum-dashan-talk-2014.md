Canadian host and performer Da Shan was invited to the 16th Zhicheng Forum at Shantou University for a talk titled “Chatting About the Mountains with Da Shan.” The lecture was scheduled for 7:30 p.m. on October 15, 2014, in the university’s Science and Technology Lecture Hall. Faculty and students could reserve a place through the campus booking system by 1:00 p.m. on October 13.

Da Shan studied Chinese studies at the University of Toronto before studying at Peking University and became known to Chinese audiences through his comedy performances and cultural-exchange work. The notice preceded the event and did not report its outcome; this article records only the plan announced at the time.
