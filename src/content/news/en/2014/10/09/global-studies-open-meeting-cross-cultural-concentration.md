Shantou University’s Centre for Global Studies planned a student-faculty meeting from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. on October 10, 2014, in Room E409. The session was to introduce the Cross-Cultural concentration and the Global Studies and Overseas Practice honors programme.

The invitation was addressed to 2012 students already in the concentration, 2013 students interested in joining it, and 2014 freshmen considering the honors programme. The agenda included a programme introduction, open discussion and interactive games. The notice did not report the event’s outcome.
