Shantou University’s Network and Information Centre and Resources Management Office asked campus units in October 2014 to review electronic bills for office landlines used in September. Bills from China Telecom Shantou and China Mobile Shantou had been uploaded to the university’s telephone management system, where unit reviewers could check and confirm the two sets of accounts.

Discrepancies were to be reported to the designated university email addresses by October 15, 2014. The Finance Office planned to debit the relevant unit accounts on October 20. Bills neither disputed nor explicitly confirmed during the review period would be treated as confirmed by the system.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Centre; Resources Management Office)
