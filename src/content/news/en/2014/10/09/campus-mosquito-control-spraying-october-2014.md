As part of dengue prevention, Shantou University’s Resources Management Office planned mosquito-control spraying in phases from October 10 to 14, 2014, across Longquanyuan and the Continuing Education College areas. The schedule covered the kindergarten, staff housing and drainage channel; the Administration Centre and nearby dining facilities; the affiliated middle and primary schools through the West Campus courtyard; student residences; and, on the final day, Longquanyuan and the Continuing Education College.

The university asked faculty, staff and students to cooperate and urged residents to clear stagnant water and other mosquito-breeding sites. This report describes the schedule announced at the time and does not infer whether the work was completed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
