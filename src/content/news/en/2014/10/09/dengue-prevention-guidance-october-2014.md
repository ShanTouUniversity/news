Guangdong was experiencing a high incidence of dengue in October 2014. In response to provincial education guidance, Shantou University’s Resources Management Office asked its schools and colleges to record and follow up on student absences due to illness. The notice advised students with symptoms including fever and rash to seek medical care and take personal measures to avoid mosquito bites.

The original notice cited provincial case figures from that period and listed the university clinic’s contact details. This is historical information from 2014, not current outbreak data or present-day medical advice.
