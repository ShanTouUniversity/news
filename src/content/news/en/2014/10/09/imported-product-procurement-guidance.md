Shantou University’s Resources Management Office advised campus units in October 2014 that applications to import products for undergraduate teaching using public funds were generally not expected to be approved when comparable domestic products were available. The notice said import-procurement reviews required additional supporting documents and typically took about three months.

To shorten procurement timelines and reduce the risk of allocated public funds being reclaimed, the university recommended considering domestic products when they could meet requirements. The relevant procurement rules and provincial review comments on the university’s equipment plan were attached to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
> ⚠️ Procurement rules and review comments are available as OA attachments.
