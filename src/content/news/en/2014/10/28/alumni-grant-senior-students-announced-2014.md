It is reported that to carry forward the alumni spirit advocated by Mr. Lee Kum-seung — "striving for self-improvement, working together, giving back to the alma mater, and contributing to society" — a caring alumnus established the 2014 Alumni Grant to assist students from low-income families at Shantou University. Following recommendations and assessments由各学院（书院）and review by the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Office, the university has decided to award the 2014 Alumni Grant to 35 senior students including Xie XY, at a rate of 2,500 yuan per person.

## Grant Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | 35 senior students |
| Amount | 2,500 yuan per person |
| Funding source | Alumnus donation |
| Reviewing units | Student Affairs Office and Alumni Office |
| Recommendation method | Assessed and recommended by各学院（书院） |

The university encourages recipient students to continue working hard and striving to become individuals of ambition, insight, perseverance, and capability, and to repay the care and support of alumni and all sectors of society with outstanding academic achievements.
