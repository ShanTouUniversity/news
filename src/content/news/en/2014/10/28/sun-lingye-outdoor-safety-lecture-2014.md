It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University issued a notice on October 28, inviting Sun Lingye — General Manager of Wilderness Medical Associates International (WMAI) China and the organization's first Chinese-language lead instructor — to deliver an outdoor safety lecture on the evening of October 29 at 19:30 in Lecture Hall E-203.

## About the Speaker

Sun Lingye is a pioneer in outdoor education in China and one of the founders of Yangshuo Dabudou Tribe Co., Ltd. He previously served as the wilderness medical consultant for the Shantou University Antarctic Expedition Team, accompanying the team on their Antarctic research mission and accumulating extensive experience in wilderness medicine and outdoor safety. For years, Sun has been dedicated to popularizing wilderness first-aid knowledge and promoting outdoor safety education.
