It is reported that renowned folk photographer Mr. Cai Haisong will deliver an academic lecture titled "Traces of Time: Interpretation of Modern Architecture in Old Shantou" at the STU Library Lecture Hall on the evening of Thursday, October 30, 2014, at 7:45 PM.

## Lecture Details

| Speaker | Cai Haisong |
| Time | 19:45, Thursday, October 30, 2014 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | STU Library |

## About the Speaker

Cai Haisong (born 1955) is a native of Shantou, Guangdong Province. He is a Master Fellow of the China Folk Photography Association, a council member of the Shantou Folk Photography Association, and a member of the Guangdong Photographers Association. He has published several books including *Chaoshan Vernacular Architecture* and *Chaoshan Residential Architecture*, and has long been dedicated to the research and photographic documentation of Chaoshan folk culture and traditional architecture.

The lecture will combine rich photographic works to provide an in-depth interpretation of the historical origins, architectural styles, and cultural value of modern buildings in old Shantou.
