It is reported that the first "Super Orator" speech competition, co-hosted by the Business School Youth League Committee and the Business Practice Center at Shantou University, has officially launched.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizers | Business School Youth League Committee, Business Practice Center |
| Format | Preliminary round + Final |
| Preliminary Dates | Evening of October 31 / November 1, 18:30–21:30 |
| Venue | Building E |
| Training | Top nine finalists will receive targeted speech training |

The competition aims to enrich campus cultural life, foster a campus-wide atmosphere of public speaking and critical thinking, and further improve students' speaking and expression skills. The top nine finalists will also receive targeted speech training to help them perform at their best in the final round.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School, Youth League Committee)
