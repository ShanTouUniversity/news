It is reported that the Personnel Office of Shantou University issued a notice on October 28, publicizing the proposed appointments for two leadership positions at the School of Engineering. Following the deliberations of the 14th university leadership meeting held on October 24, 2014, Lin Peng is proposed as Vice Dean of the School of Engineering, and Zhang Jie is proposed as Head of the Department of Civil Engineering.

## Proposed Appointments

| Position | Proposed appointee |
| --- | --- |
| Vice Dean, School of Engineering | Lin Peng |
| Head, Department of Civil Engineering | Zhang Jie |

## Public Comment Period

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Publicity period | Until November 3, 2014 |
| Receiving unit | Personnel Office |
| Feedback method | Written submission or telephone inquiry |

During the publicity period, any individual or unit with differing opinions regarding the proposed appointments may submit feedback to the Personnel Office in writing or by phone, in accordance with relevant regulations.
