It is reported that the University Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the list of outstanding members from university-wide student organizations on October 28, 2014. In accordance with the *STU Student Cadre Management Regulations*, the evaluation was based on the work performance of members across all university-level student organizations over the past academic year. Following internal evaluations by each organization and review and public notice by the University Youth League Committee, the final list of outstanding members was determined.

The evaluation covers all student organizations at the university level, including the Student Union and the Federation of Student Societies, aiming to recognize members who have demonstrated exceptional contributions and to promote continued development of campus student affairs.
