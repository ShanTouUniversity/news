It is reported that the "Green Ribbon" Environmental Volunteer Team of the STU Youth Volunteer Association has launched volunteer recruitment for Green Sprout Action Phase 5, themed "Walking with Primary Schools on Environmental Protection, Rebuilding Harmony with Green."

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Green Sprout Action Phase 5 Environmental Class |
| Organizer | "Green Ribbon" Environmental Volunteer Team, Youth Volunteer Association |
| Theme | Walking with Primary Schools on Environmental Protection, Rebuilding Harmony with Green |
| Volunteer Training | November 5 and 12 |
| Primary School Service | Afternoons of November 19 and 26 |
| Service Location | Peimiao Primary School, Shantou |
| Recruitment | 20 volunteers |

After training, volunteers will visit Peimiao Primary School in Shantou to conduct environmental education activities, spreading eco-friendly concepts to primary school students through engaging and interactive methods.
