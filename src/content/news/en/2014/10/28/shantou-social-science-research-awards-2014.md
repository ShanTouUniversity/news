It is reported that the results of the 2014 Shantou Philosophy and Social Science Planning Projects have been announced, with 7 projects from Shantou University approved for funding. The projects cover a range of important research areas including the Maritime Silk Road, overseas Chinese investment, ecological civilization, and new-type urbanization.

## Approved Projects

| No. | Project Title | Research Area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Research on the 21st-Century Maritime Silk Road and Shantou's Development | Regional Development |
| 2 | Research on Overseas Chinese Investment and Industrial Structure Upgrading in Shantou | Economic Research |
| 3 | Research on Ecological Civilization Construction in Nan'ao County | Environmental Ecology |
| 4 | Research on Overseas Chinese and Qiaoxiang Cultural and Creative Industries | Cultural Studies |
| 5 | Research on Housing Security for Migrant Workers in Shantou's New-Type Urbanization | Urbanization Studies |
| 6 | Research on Rural Grassroots Governance Models and Stability Mechanisms | Social Governance |
| 7 | (One additional project) | — |

The approved projects address key issues in Shantou's economic and social development, reflecting STU's academic strength in serving local development and conducting applied research.
