It is reported that twelve journalism students from Shantou University's School of Journalism and Communication completed a one-month internship and exchange program in Taiwan during the summer of 2014. During their stay, the students interned at media organizations including Central News Agency, Want Daily, NOWNEWS, and Zhides Communication, and also undertook a circumnavigation tour of Taiwan to gain a deeper understanding of the island's culture and media landscape.

## Sharing Session Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Taiwan: Reflections |
| Speakers | Twelve summer 2014 Taiwan internship students |
| Organizer | School of Journalism and Communication |
| Host organizations | Central News Agency, Want Daily, NOWNEWS, Zhides Communication, and others |
| Duration | One month, Summer 2014 |

The sharing session will cover the students' internship experiences, media observations, and cultural encounters, offering insights into the similarities and differences in cross-strait journalism and opportunities for future development.
