It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced the schedule for 2016 graduates' diploma photo collection on October 28, 2014. The collection targets 2012-grade undergraduates at the main campus, as well as students from the 2009–2011 grades who have not yet participated in photo collection.

## Collection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | November 7, 2014 |
| Location | Moot Court, Teaching Building E |
| Target students | 2012-grade undergraduates (main campus) |
| Makeup collection | 2009–2011-grade students who previously missed collection |

Diploma photo collection is a required step for producing graduation diplomas. Students concerned are reminded to attend on time and not to miss this important arrangement.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
