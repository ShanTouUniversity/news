It is reported that the Sports Department at Shantou University issued a notice on October 28, formally launching the selection process for the 2015 National Universiade table tennis registration athletes. The selection is open to graduate and undergraduate students on the main campus.

## Selection Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Sport | Table tennis |
| Event | 2015 National Universiade |
| Eligibility | Graduate and undergraduate students on the main campus |
| Tryout date | November 9, 2014 |
| Tryout venue | Gymnasium |
| Registration deadline | Before November 5, 2014 |
| How to register | Send an email to Sports Department at o_tyb@stu.edu.cn |
| Email subject | Please use "大运会乒乓球注册运动员报名" |

Students interested in participating in the selection should register via email before the deadline, with the email subject line reading "大运会乒乓球注册运动员报名".

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
