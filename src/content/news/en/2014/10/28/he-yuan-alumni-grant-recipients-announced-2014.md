It is reported that to carry forward the alumni spirit advocated by Mr. Lee Kum-seung — "striving for self-improvement, working together, giving back to the alma mater, and contributing to society" — the Heyuan Alumni Association of Shantou University donated 85,000 yuan to establish the "Heyuan Alumni Grant" to assist undergraduate students from low-income families in the Heyuan region (including Yuan Cheng District, Dongyuan County, Longchuan County, Zijin County, Heping County, and Lianping County). The grant supports 10 students per year at 2,000 yuan each.

Following student applications and review by the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Office, the university has decided to award the 2014 Heyuan Alumni Grant to 10 students including Liu DX.

## Grant Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | 10 undergraduates from low-income families in Heyuan |
| Amount | 2,000 yuan per person |
| Funding source | Heyuan Alumni Association of STU donation (total 85,000 yuan) |
| Reviewing units | Student Affairs Office and Alumni Office |
| Application method | Self-application by students |

The university encourages recipient students to continue working hard and striving to become individuals of ambition, insight, perseverance, and capability, and to repay the care and support of alumni and all sectors of society with outstanding academic achievements.
