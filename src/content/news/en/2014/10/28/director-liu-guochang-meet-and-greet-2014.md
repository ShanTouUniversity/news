It is reported that renowned Hong Kong director Lau Kwok-chung will hold a meet-and-greet session at Shantou University on the afternoon of October 29, 2014, from 3:00 to 5:00 PM at Conference Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center, sharing his film creative experience and artistic philosophy with faculty and students.

## About the Guest

Lau Kwok-chung was born in 1949 in South Africa and hails from Shunde, Guangdong. He graduated from the Film Department of the University of Southern California. Lau is a celebrated Hong Kong director and is widely regarded as the founder of Hong Kong cinematic realism, having directed numerous classic Hong Kong films that have profoundly influenced the development of Hong Kong cinema.
