It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University issued a notice on October 28 to adjust the laboratory director positions in the Department of Physics and the Department of Chemistry. Following deliberations by the School Administrative Committee, the following personnel appointments were made:

## Appointments

| Department | New Laboratory Director | Outgoing Director |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physics | Lv Xiupin | Ouyang Yandong |
| Chemistry | Yang Yingyi | Yang Keer |

The adjustments aim to better advance the development work of the School.
