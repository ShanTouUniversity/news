It is reported that the Research Office of Shantou University announced on October 28, 2014 that the application process for the 2014 Defense Education Research Grants, organized by the Guangdong Provincial Social Science Federation, has been opened. STU is allocated a quota of 2 applications for this round.

## Application Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Organizing body | Guangdong Provincial Social Science Federation |
| Grant title | 2014 Annual Defense Education Research Grants |
| STU quota | 2 applications |
| Preliminary review deadline | Before November 14, 2014 (electronic copies to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn) |
| Final submission deadline | Before November 17, 2014 (4 copies each of application and论证活页 to Research Office) |

Faculty members interested in applying are requested to send electronic versions of their application and argumentation materials to the designated email address before the preliminary review deadline, and to submit four hard copies of each to the Research Office by November 17, 2014.
