It is reported that the Subject Services Department of Shantou University Library will hold a workshop on October 28 to help graduate students and senior undergraduates master literature review writing, database retrieval, and data analysis skills essential for thesis work.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | Xie Luqing |
| Time | 19:00-20:00, Tuesday, October 28, 2014 |
| Venue | Subject Services Department, Library (2nd floor, inside consultation desk) |
| Target Audience | Graduate students, senior undergraduates |

The workshop will combine practical examples from CNKI, Engineering Village, and Web of Science to demonstrate literature review writing methods. It will also introduce the bibliometric functions and statistical analysis methods available through library databases. A prize quiz segment will be included. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
