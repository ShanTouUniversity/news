It is reported that the Party and Government Office and the Network and Information Center of Shantou University issued a joint notice on October 28, announcing that the university's new Collaborative Office System (OA system) will officially launch on November 1, 2014.

The existing office information system, in operation since 2001, has run for 13 years. With the steady advancement of the university's informatization initiatives, the aging system has increasingly failed to meet the demands of modern digital office work. To further improve administrative efficiency and information transparency, the university has decided to deploy the new collaborative office system.

## New OA System Features

| Feature Module | Description |
| --- | --- |
| University Document Publishing | Online publishing and management of official university documents |
| Work Notices | Online publishing and querying of notices from various departments |
| Contact Directory | Online querying and maintenance of the faculty and student directory |
| Document Management | Online archiving and retrieval of university documents |
| Announcement Browsing | All faculty and students can browse and query various announcements and notices |

## Access Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |

The launch of the new OA system is expected to significantly advance the standardization of automated office operations and information disclosure at STU, providing more convenient and efficient information services for faculty and students.
