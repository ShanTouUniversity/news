It is reported that the University Youth League Committee of Shantou University announced the list of outstanding student cadres from various schools and colleges on October 28, 2014. In accordance with the *STU Student Cadre Management Regulations*, the evaluation was conducted based on the work performance of student council cadres over the past academic year, involving self-assessment, evaluation by each school/college, and review by the University Youth League Committee.

The selection aims to encourage student leaders to actively serve their peers and to strengthen the role of student cadres as a bridge between the university administration and the student body.
