It is reported that Shantou University's School of Arts and Design hosted a professor dialogue themed "A Beautiful Life" on the evening of October 28, 2014 (Tuesday) at 7:30 PM in the First-Floor Lecture Hall of the school. Professor Wang Shouzhi, Dean of the School of Arts and Design, and Professor Celia Caturelli from the Kunstakademie Düsseldorf, Germany, took the stage to engage in an in-depth conversation on design education and life experiences.

The dialogue explored the academic pursuits and personal artistic journeys of both professors in the field of design, discussing the similarities and differences between Chinese and German design education and cutting-edge topics in contemporary design development.
