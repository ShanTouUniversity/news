The fourth issue of Shantou University’s *Teaching Communication* (No. 21 overall), published by the Academic Affairs Office on October 28, 2014, reviewed results announced that September for the Seventh National Higher Education Teaching Achievement Awards. Two university projects received first and second prizes, respectively.

| Award | Project | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First prize | “Comprehensive Reform and Practice in Training Outstanding Doctors with an International Perspective” | Medical College, led by Professor Yang Mianhua and colleagues |
| Second prize | “Construction and Promotion of a New CDIO Engineering Education Model in China” | School of Engineering, led by Professor Bao Nengsheng and colleagues |

The medical project was one of two first-prize projects from undergraduate institutions in Guangdong, while the engineering project was one of 16 second-prize projects from the province. The newsletter described the result as a breakthrough for STU’s national-level first-prize record.

The issue also covered the university’s annual Teaching Development Month, launched in the 2014 summer term. Its activities included an annual teaching conference, awards, lectures, exchanges and training. The 2014 program focused on outcome-based education, teaching practice and assessment, and evaluations of teaching units.
