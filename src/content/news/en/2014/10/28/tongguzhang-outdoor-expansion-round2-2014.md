It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced the first-round physical fitness test results for the Mount Tongguzhang medium-level outdoor expansion activity on October 28. A total of 99 faculty and students registered for the initial selection, among whom 36 students including Deng ZK and 4 teachers including Xiao YJ passed the assessment.

## Second-Round Selection Details

Participants who passed the first round will enter the second round — a wilderness survival workshop. The arrangements are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Activity Date | Saturday, November 1, 2014, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM |
| Assembly Time | 7:50 AM |
| Assembly Point | STU Motto Stone (校训碑) |
| Activity Content | Wilderness survival skills challenge, team building and cooperative能力提升 |

## Selection Process

The second-round selection uses a scoring system across eight activities for student candidates divided into groups, including tent setup, sky shelter practice, compass map navigation, compass campus navigation, invincible caterpillar wheel, outdoor knowledge quiz, sandbag relay, and overall team performance. The top two teams earn direct selection spots for 14 students; the third, fourth, and fifth teams each select 3, 3, and 2 members through anonymous voting, totaling 22 students. The four teachers participate throughout the selection process, with two chosen by anonymous student vote.

The activity promotes LNT (Leave No Trace) outdoor ethics. Participants are advised to wear suitable sportswear and shoes according to the weather and bring their own drinking water. Each student is required to pay 8 yuan for food, with lunch provided uniformly by the organizer.
