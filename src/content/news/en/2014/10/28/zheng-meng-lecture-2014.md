It is reported that Zheng Meng, Editor-in-Chief of Changjiang Net, will deliver an academic lecture at Shantou University on the morning of October 29, 2014, from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM in the Library Auditorium. The lecture, titled "What Kind of Media Professional Does the Age of Convergence Need?", is hosted by the School of Journalism and Communication.

## About the Speaker

Zheng Meng graduated from the Journalism Department of Wuhan University in 1992 and is a senior journalist. He currently serves as Editor-in-Chief of Changjiang Net. A recipient of Wuhan City's government special allowance, Zheng has been awarded the Wuhan City "May 1st Labor Medal." Over his 22-year journalism career, he has repeatedly received national and provincial honors including the "China News Award," "Hubei News Award," and "Wuhan News Award," accumulating extensive practical experience in news reporting and media convergence.
