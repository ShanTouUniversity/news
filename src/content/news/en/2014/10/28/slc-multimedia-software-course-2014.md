It is reported that the Study Learning Center (SLC) of Shantou University launched a multimedia software introductory course on October 28, 2014, offering free skills training open to all faculty and students.

## Course Schedule

| Course | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Photoshop Image Processing | Basic operations including editing, color correction, and compositing |
| Illustrator Graphic Design | Vector drawing, LOGO design, and foundational skills |
| PS Poster Design Intro | Poster creation workflow and practical techniques |
| Integrated Software Learning | Multi-software collaborative workflows |

Classes will be held at the SLC Computer Lab. All interested faculty and students are welcome to sign up.
