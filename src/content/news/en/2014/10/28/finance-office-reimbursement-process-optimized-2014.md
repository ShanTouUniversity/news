It is reported that the Finance Office of Shantou University announced an optimization of the reimbursement process on October 28, 2014. Starting October 29, reimbursement staff can choose to drop off their sorted receipts at the Finance Office without queuing on-site.

## Process Optimization Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Effective date | October 29, 2014 |
| New process | Submitters drop off completed receipts at the Finance Office without on-site queuing |
| Recommendation | Self-check receipts against the "Reimbursement Checklist" before dropping off |

The Finance Office advises submitters to carefully verify their receipts before submission, checking each item against the "Reimbursement Checklist" to ensure all procedures are in order and to minimize returns due to incomplete documentation.
