It is reported that to share experiences and deepen mutual understanding between universities, the STU Youth League Committee will host a sharing session on the Ningbo Notthingham University exchange program on the evening of October 31.

## Background

From August to September this year, STU and Ningbo Notthingham University co-hosted a Leader Exchange Program, sending 10 outstanding student representatives for a three-week exchange. The students actively participated in the preparation of orientation activities at Ningbo Notthingham University and assisted student organizations with recruitment.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, Friday, October 31, 2014 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Organizer | Youth League Committee, Student Affairs Office |
| Audience | All STU students |

The sharing session will feature personal accounts from participating students about campus life, academic exchanges, and club activities at Ningbo Notthingham University, providing valuable reference information for other students.
