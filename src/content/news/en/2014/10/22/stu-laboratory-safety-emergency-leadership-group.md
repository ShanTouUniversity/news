Shantou University established a laboratory safety emergency leadership group to strengthen safety management and support the normal operation of laboratory teaching, research and technical services. The decision was approved at the 13th university leadership meeting on September 29, 2014.

Wen Xiaobo was appointed group leader, with Chen Shaogang, Chen Yaowen and Wen Ruifeng as deputy leaders. Members were Mei Zhiping, Huang Jingxia, Qiu Jingfeng, Chen Yangzhou, Guan Rongsheng, Li Yuanyou, Chen Jiehui, Chen Shaobo, Gao Wenhua, Yang Xiaodi and Chen Liqiang.
