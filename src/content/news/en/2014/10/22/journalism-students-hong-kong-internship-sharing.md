Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication scheduled a session for 14 students who had spent two months interning in Hong Kong during summer 2014 to share their experiences. Topics were to include Hong Kong’s media environment, differences in news coverage across the two regions, local living costs and commuting.
