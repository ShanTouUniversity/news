It is reported that the Resource Management Office of Shantou University will organize a fire evacuation drill and live extinguisher exercise at the Science and Technology Building on the afternoon of Thursday, October 23, 2014. The event aims to popularize fire safety knowledge, eliminate fire hazards, and improve emergency escape and firefighting capabilities.

## Drill Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Thursday, October 23, 2014, 16:30–17:10 |
| Location | Science and Technology Building |
| Content | Fire evacuation drill + live extinguisher experience |

## Participation

The drill is open to all faculty, staff, and students of the Science and Technology Building, and members of the broader campus community are also welcome to participate or observe. Through practical evacuation and fire extinguisher operation, participants will gain essential skills in fire emergency response.
