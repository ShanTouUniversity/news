It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 125 [2014] on October 22, 2014, to establish the Laboratory Safety Construction and Management Committee.

Decided at the 13th university leadership meeting on September 29, the move aims to strengthen laboratory safety management, improve managerial standards, and ensure the orderly conduct of experimental teaching, scientific research and technical services. Members are appointed by the university for a three-year term, with adjustments as needed, and the committee has a full-time secretary.
