It is reported that Professor Celia Caturelli of the Düsseldorf Institute of Art and artist Celia Caturelli will deliver a lecture titled "Between the lines" at the Gallery of the Yangtze College of Art and Design, Shantou University, on the evening of Thursday, October 23, 2014.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | "Between the lines" |
| Speaker | Celia Caturelli (Professor, Düsseldorf Institute of Art; Artist) |
| Time | Thursday, October 23, 2014, 19:30–22:00 |
| Location | Gallery, Yangtze College of Art and Design, STU |

## About the Speaker

Celia Caturelli graduated from the University of Arts Berlin and has long been engaged in research in visual arts, oil painting, and modern literature. Her artistic career has received multiple international recognitions: the Albrecht Dürer Prize in 1984, the title of Budapest Artist in Hungary in 1987, and a grant from the Pollock-Krasner Foundation in 1994–1995. Her work spans visual arts and literature, reflecting a unique interdisciplinary creative perspective.

Faculty and students interested in contemporary visual arts and cross-cultural creation are welcome to attend.
