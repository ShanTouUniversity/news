Shantou University’s Graduate School scheduled a career development seminar titled “One Choice Can Shape a Lifetime” for October 29, 2014. The discussion was to cover career choices, job seeking and workplace development.

The speaker, Lin Yueli, was training director at Baicheng Business School. She had worked in training, human resources and operations at companies including Kodak, Merck, Allianz, Wyeth and Gymboree.
