It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University will host a lecture by Assoc. Prof. Yang Hui from Beijing National Accounting Institute on the afternoon of Wednesday, October 22, 2014, interpreting the latest changes in enterprise accounting standards issued by China's Ministry of Finance in 2014.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Interpretation of the Latest Changes in Enterprise Accounting Standards |
| Speaker | Yang Hui (Assoc. Professor, Beijing National Accounting Institute) |
| Time | Wednesday, October 22, 2014, 14:00–16:00 |
| Location | Room E303, Business School |
| Moderator | Long Yue'e (Assoc. Professor) |

## Key Topics

The lecture will cover changes in the following seven enterprise accounting standards issued by the Ministry of Finance in 2014:

1. Fair Value Measurement
2. Presentation of Financial Statements
3. Employee Benefits
4. Consolidated Financial Statements
5. Joint Arrangements
6. Long-term Equity Investments
7. Disclosure of Interests in Other Entities

Faculty and students in related disciplines are welcome to attend.
