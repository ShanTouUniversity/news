Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced a schedule change for class 73766, Nutrition, Environment and Health, offered by the Institute of Marine Biology. The session originally set for periods 1–2 on Tuesday of week six, October 28, was canceled and rescheduled for periods 8–9 on Saturday, November 1, in Room G304.
