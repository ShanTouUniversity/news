Shantou University’s School of Science opened applications for its 17th extracurricular project round. Eligible applicants included the school’s student union, youth volunteer association, student publication, radio club, departmental Youth League branches, classes and student teams.

Proposals could focus on public service, sports, career development, humanities, leadership or innovation. The notice set October 25, 2014, at 9 p.m. as the deadline for electronic submissions and tentatively scheduled project presentations for October 29 at 2:30 p.m. The school’s Youth League committee was to conduct an initial review under its project management rules.
