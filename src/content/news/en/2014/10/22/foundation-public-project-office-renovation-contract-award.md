Following a recommendation by Shantou University’s Tendering and Bidding Leadership Group, Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. was selected for the renovation of the university’s Foundation Public Project Office. The winning bid was RMB 146,816.

The award was open for public review from October 22 to 24, 2014. Contact details for inquiries and complaints were provided in the university’s original notice.
