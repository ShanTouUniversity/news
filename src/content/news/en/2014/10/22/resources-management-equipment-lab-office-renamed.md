Shantou University decided to rename the Equipment and Materials Management Office under its Resources Management Department as the Equipment and Laboratory Management Office, adding responsibilities for laboratory development and safety management.

The university said the change aimed to strengthen laboratory safety and improve management while supporting teaching, research and technical services. The decision was approved at the 13th university leadership meeting on September 29, 2014.
