Artist Celia Caturelli was scheduled to present her solo exhibition “101 Teppiche” at the Art Museum of Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. The opening ceremony was set for 11 a.m. on October 24, 2014.

Caturelli is a professor at Düsseldorf University of Applied Sciences and a graduate of the Berlin University of the Arts. Her work and research span visual art, oil painting and modern literature. She has received support from the Pollock-Krasner Foundation and earned the title of Budapest Artist and the Albrecht Dürer Award.
