Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office scheduled a demonstration of the MYSTU Instant Response System (IRS) for October 22, 2014. The system supported real-time classroom questions and displayed response statistics, and the demonstration was to cover classroom use and gather feedback from teachers.

The session was scheduled for Room 201 of the New Administration Building. Teaching units were asked to recommend teachers; personal contact information requested for registration is not reproduced here. The available notice did not report on the event’s outcome.
