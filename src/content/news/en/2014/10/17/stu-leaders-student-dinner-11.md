Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled the 11th dinner dialogue between university leaders and students for October 23, 2014. The event aimed to provide a channel for students to discuss university development with university leaders and representatives of relevant departments.

The plan called for 32 full-time students to attend at Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. The available notice was a registration announcement and did not provide attendance details or discussion outcomes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
