Shantou University’s School of Science scheduled two Science Salon talks on selective breeding and industry trends for marine pearl oysters, and genetic improvement and sex-control technologies for large yellow croaker.

The speakers were Professor Wang Aimin of Hainan University and Professor Wang Zhiyong of Jimei University. The talks were scheduled for the morning of October 21, 2014, in Room 504 of the Science and Technology Center. The available notice did not report on the sessions’ outcomes.

| Topic | Speaker | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selective breeding and industry trends for marine pearl oysters | Wang Aimin, Hainan University | October 21, 2014, 9 a.m. |
| Genetic improvement and sex-control technologies for large yellow croaker | Wang Zhiyong, Jimei University | October 21, 2014, 10:30 a.m. |
