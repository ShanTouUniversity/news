It is reported that the recruitment results for volunteers of the "Washington State Day — Meet in Shantou" event were announced on October 17.

Following preliminary screening and interviews, ten students have been selected as volunteers for the event:

| No. | Volunteer | No. | Volunteer |
|-----|-----------|-----|-----------|
| 1 | He WY | 6 | Wang YN |
| 2 | Lin ZB | 7 | Liang JR |
| 3 | Ao J | 8 | Zhu KX |
| 4 | Li ZW | 9 | Cao Y |
| 5 | Zhang JH | 10 | Dai LS |

The selected volunteers checked into a downtown hotel on the evening of October 17 for pre-service training.

The recruitment was jointly organized by the STU Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation. The organizers expressed their gratitude to all students who applied.
