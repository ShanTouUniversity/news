In 2014, Shantou University’s Graduate School opened a public review of proposed recipients of the 2013–14 national graduate scholarships. Following student applications, preliminary reviews by academic units and university-level assessment, 52 master’s students and four doctoral students were proposed for awards.

Written objections were due to the Graduate School by 5 p.m. on October 23, 2014. The proposed recipients’ names appear in the original OA attachment and are omitted here to protect student privacy.
