In 2014, Shantou University’s English Language Center introduced The Writing Studio, a writing support service based in the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL). Students could book one-to-one sessions for help organizing papers, finding writing resources, brainstorming ideas and receiving general writing advice.

The notice encouraged students to seek support before assignment deadlines. Its service description and booking arrangements reflect information published in 2014 and should not be taken as current availability.
