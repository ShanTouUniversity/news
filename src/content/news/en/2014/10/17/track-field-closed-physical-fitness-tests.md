It is reported that, in line with arrangements by the Ministry of Education, Shantou University will hold physical fitness tests for all undergraduates over the coming weekends. To ensure order and safety, the Security Office and the Physical Education Department jointly announced temporary closures of the track and field ground.

| Date | Closure hours |
| ---- | ---- |
| October 18 (Saturday) | 8:00–18:00 |
| October 19 (Sunday) | 8:00–18:00 |
| October 26 (Sunday) | 8:00–12:00 |

During these periods, the track and field ground will be closed to regular users and used exclusively for student fitness testing. Staff and students who wish to exercise are advised to plan around these times or use alternative facilities.

The university asked for understanding and cooperation regarding any inconvenience caused.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office and Physical Education Department)
