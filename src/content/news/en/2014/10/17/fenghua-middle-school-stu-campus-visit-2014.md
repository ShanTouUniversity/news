Shantou University’s Graduate School scheduled a campus development event under the theme “Discover STU, Inspire Dreams” for Grade 8 and 9 students and teachers from Shantou Fenghua School on October 18, 2014.

The planned activities included team games and group development exercises, aiming to encourage interaction between university and middle school students and introduce younger students to university life. The Graduate School’s graduate-student volunteer group organized the event. The available notice did not report on what followed.
