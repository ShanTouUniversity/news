Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced that the October 20, 2014 session of class 72992, Introduction to Mobile Programming, would not take place. The class was scheduled for periods 8–9 in the E101 computer lab. A make-up date had not been set in the notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
