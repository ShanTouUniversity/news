Shantou University’s Laboratory Safety Development and Management Committee scheduled a meeting for October 20, 2014, to address provincial requirements for laboratory safety at universities.

According to the advance notice, the agenda included briefings on provincial documents, the university’s preparations and next steps, discussion of laboratory safety and protective measures, and the planned signing of safety responsibility agreements by university and school leaders. The meeting was scheduled for Lecture Room E203. The available notice did not report on what followed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Laboratory Safety Development and Management Committee)
