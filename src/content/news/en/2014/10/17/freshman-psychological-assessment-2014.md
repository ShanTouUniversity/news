Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office scheduled psychological assessments for all 2014 freshmen on October 18, 2014, as part of the establishment of student psychological records. The assessments comprised the University Personality Inventory (UPI), the 16 Personality Factors Questionnaire (16PF) and the Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90). They were to be completed in computer rooms 101 or 102 in Building E, with about 60 minutes allowed.

The system was to open after 18:00 on October 17. Students were asked to complete all three questionnaires and could view their reports and submit counseling requests through the system. The original notice included account-specific login instructions, which are not reproduced here; students should consult the university’s official counseling channels for operational guidance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Assessment instructions and related attachments are available on the OA system.
