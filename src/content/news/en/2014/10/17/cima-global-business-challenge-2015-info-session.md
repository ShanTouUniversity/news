Shantou University Business School’s International Business Case Center (IBCC) scheduled a briefing on the 2015 CIMA Global Business Challenge, an international competition for undergraduates spanning nearly 30 countries. STU Business School and IBCC were to host the South China regional final.

The briefing was scheduled for October 22, 2014, in E-Classroom 202. The speaker was Liang Yan (Frank), business development manager for CIMA Greater China. The available notice did not report on what followed.
