To mark the 15th Chinese Journalists’ Day, Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication organized a lecture series under the theme “Ideals and Love: Never Give Up.” The first speaker was Xie Deju, an alumnus of the school’s inaugural journalism class and a producer at Guangzhou Television.

His talk, “Maintaining Ideals and Passion in the Routine of Hard and Solitary Work,” was scheduled for the morning of October 20, 2014, in Lecture Room E204. Xie had worked as an on-camera reporter for Guangzhou Television’s *News Today* and, from 2014, as producer and director of *Lingnan Heroes*. The available notice previewed the event but did not report on its outcome.
