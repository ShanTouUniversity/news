Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office reported two examination-misconduct cases on October 24, 2014. According to the notice, two students from the School of Engineering brought and used exam-related materials during course examinations on June 16 and June 20, respectively. The university issued each student a warning under its student-management rules and recorded a zero for the affected course.

The notice also said that, under the university’s degree-award rules, disciplinary action for cheating may affect eligibility for a bachelor’s degree. Students who meet the stated conditions and demonstrate improvement after disciplinary action may apply for review through the prescribed process. To protect student privacy, no names or other identifying details are included here.
