It is reported that a delegation from Dublin City University (DCU), led by Vice President Professor James Mary Francis Dowling, will visit Shantou University on Monday, October 27, 2014.

## Visit and Information Session Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Visit Date | October 27, 2014 |
| Information Session Time | Monday, Oct 27, 2014, 19:30–21:00 |
| Venue | Lecture Hall E203 |
| Content | Introduction to the 2015 exchange student program and joint training programs between the two universities |

Dublin City University is a well-known higher education institution in Ireland with a solid cooperation foundation in academic exchange and student mobility with STU. This visit and information session will provide interested students with an opportunity to learn more about collaboration opportunities between the two universities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (International Exchange and Cooperation Office)
