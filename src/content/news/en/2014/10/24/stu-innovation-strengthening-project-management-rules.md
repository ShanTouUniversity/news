Shantou University issued management measures for projects under its Innovation Strengthening Initiative and for the initiative’s dedicated funds. A university document said both measures had been approved and took effect upon publication, directing all university units to comply.

The full rules were included as attachments to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Document No. 128 [2014])
> ⚠️ The full management measures are available in the original OA attachments.
