It is reported that the Subject Services Department of Shantou University Library will host a workshop on interlibrary loan and document delivery services on the evening of Friday, October 24, 2014, presented by Xu Yong.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Interlibrary Loan and Document Delivery Services |
| Speaker | Xu Yong (Subject Services Department, University Library) |
| Time | Friday, October 24, 2014, 19:30–20:30 |
| Location | Subject Services Department, 2nd Floor, University Library (inside the reference desk) |

## Content

The workshop will provide a comprehensive overview of the library's interlibrary loan and document delivery services, covering how to request materials not held by STU's collections from the following external platforms:

- CALIS (China Academic Library & Information System)
- CASHL (China Academic Social Sciences and Humanities Literature Database)
- National Library of China
- Xiamen University Library

Faculty and students interested in expanding their research resources through inter-institutional services are welcome to attend.
