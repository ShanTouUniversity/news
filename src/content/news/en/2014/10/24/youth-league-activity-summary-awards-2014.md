It is reported that the STU Youth League Committee recently held the summary and awards ceremony for the distinctive league day activity campaign themed "Gathering Youth Positive Energy, Chasing New Dreams in Life."

## Awards

| Award | Quantity |
|-------|----------|
| Outstanding Organization Award | 2 |
| Outstanding Distinctive League Day Activities | Several |

The distinctive league day activities, themed around "Gathering Youth Positive Energy, Chasing New Dreams in Life," were carried out across all grassroots league branches university-wide. The activities featured diverse content and broad participation, fully showcasing the youthful spirit and social responsibility of contemporary STU students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Committee of the Communist Youth League)
