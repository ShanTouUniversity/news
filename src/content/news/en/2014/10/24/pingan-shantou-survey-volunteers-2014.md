It is reported that Shantou University will support the Office of the Leading Group for Building a Safe Shantou in conducting a citywide survey, and is recruiting about 30 student volunteers from across the university to assist.

Scheduled for November to December 2014, the survey will target residents in all districts and counties of Shantou, aiming to collect around 3,000 samples. Conducted primarily through phone interviews supplemented by questionnaire distribution, it will cover awareness of the Safe Shantou campaign, citizens' sense of security, and satisfaction with political and legal work. The municipal office will provide pre-service training, and volunteers will, under instructors' guidance, contact residents by phone or distribute questionnaires in person, then record, compile, and analyze the responses.

## Recruitment Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Volunteers needed | About 30 |
| Application period | Until 24:00, Tuesday, October 28, 2014 |
| How to apply | Fill in the application form and send it to the designated email address, with the subject line: Safe Shantou Survey Volunteer - Name - Grade - Major |
| Survey period | November–December 2014, mainly evenings and weekends with flexible scheduling |
| Subsidy | 5 yuan per valid sample completed |
| Volunteer hours | Registered hours determined by valid samples and actual fieldwork time, per the STU Volunteer Registration Management Measures (Trial) |

## Eligibility

- Enthusiasm for public service with good moral character and dedication;
- Responsible and able to follow arrangements on working hours and assignments;
- Careful, patient, and hardworking;
- Prior social survey experience and familiarity with Wenjuanxing and data-analysis software preferred.

Interested students may submit the application form to the designated email address. For inquiries, contact Student Li.
