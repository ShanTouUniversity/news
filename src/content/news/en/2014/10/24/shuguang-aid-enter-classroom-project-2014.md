It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University has officially launched the Shaguang Aid "Enter the Classroom" project at STU. After consultation with the Jinping District Education Bureau, the program will recruit campus volunteers as intern teachers to provide after-school tutoring at selected schools in the Tuopu area starting in the new academic term.

## Project Background

The Shaguang Aid program has been operating at Shantou University since 2002. Over 12 years, it has organized nearly 4,000 volunteers who have collectively tutored over 30,000 student sessions. In December 2013, a pilot "Enter the Classroom" sub-program was launched at Yuting Primary School in Shantou City, achieving notable success.

## Project Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Program | Shaguang Aid "Enter the Classroom" |
| Target Students | Students at selected schools in the Tuopu area |
| Volunteer Source | STU undergraduate and graduate students |
| Pilot Site | Yuting Primary School, Shantou City |

According to organizers, volunteers will serve as intern teachers in primary and secondary school classrooms, providing academic tutoring and extracurricular interest development for local students. The project has received strong support from the Jinping District Education Bureau, creating broader practical platforms for STU volunteers while benefiting students in underserved areas.
