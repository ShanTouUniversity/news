Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced that the Fall 2014 course plan had been finalized and would not be adjusted further. Instructors were asked to log in to the university’s teaching system to check their schedules and view and print class lists.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
