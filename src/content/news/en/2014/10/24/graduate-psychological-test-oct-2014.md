It is reported that the Student Affairs Office at Shantou University issued a notice on October 24, 2014, announcing psychological testing for all 2014 graduate students to be held on October 25, aiming to assess the mental health status of incoming postgraduates and provide appropriate counseling services.

## Test Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date & Time | October 25, 2014, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM |
| Participants | 2014-grade graduate students |
| Format | Online test |
| Test URL | http://xjbx.stu.edu.cn/psytest6 |

The assessment uses three scales: the University Personality Inventory (UPI), the Cattell 16 Personality Factors Questionnaire (16PF), and the Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90), providing a comprehensive evaluation of the psychological well-being of incoming graduate students.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
