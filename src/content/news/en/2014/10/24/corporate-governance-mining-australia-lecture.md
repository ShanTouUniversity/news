It is reported that Assistant Professor Lin Teng from Xiamen University will deliver an academic report at Shantou University's Business School on Wednesday, October 29, 2014.

## Report Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Title | Do Corporate Governance Mechanisms Matter to Community Engagement in Australian Mining Industry |
| Speaker | Lin Teng, Assistant Professor, Xiamen University |
| Time | Wednesday, Oct 29, 2014, 9:00–11:00 |
| Venue | Business School Academic Seminar Room (2nd Floor, Wenxi Building) |
| Moderator | Associate Professor Long Yue'e |
| Language | English |

The report examines the relationship between corporate governance mechanisms and community engagement in the Australian mining industry. Faculty and students from relevant disciplines are welcome to attend.
