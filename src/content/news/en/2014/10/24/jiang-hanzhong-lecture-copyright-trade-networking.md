It is reported that Jiang Hanzhong, director of the Copyright Department at New World Publishing, will deliver two lectures at Shantou University's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication on October 27, 2014.

## Lecture Schedule

| Lecture | Title | Time | Venue |
|---------|-------|------|-------|
| First | How to Become an Expert in Copyright Trade | Monday, Oct 27, 2014, 8:00–10:00 | Room E308 |
| Second | How to Build Professional Networks in the Cultural Creativity Industry | Monday, Oct 27, 2014, 19:00–21:00 | Lecture Hall E201 |

## About the Speaker

Jiang Hanzhong previously served as deputy director of the English Editorial Department at China Sports Magazine, published by the former State Physical Culture and Sports Commission, and as deputy director of the English Editorial Department at New World Publishing. He currently holds the position of Director of the Copyright Department at New World Publishing.
