It is reported that the campus qualifier for the 4th National College Student Engineering Training Comprehensive Competition will take place on Sunday, October 26, 2014, at Shantou University. Winning teams will represent STU in the provincial competition.

## Competition Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Event | 4th National College Student Engineering Training Comprehensive Competition – STU Campus Qualifier |
| Date & Time | Sunday, Oct 26, 2014, 13:00–18:00 |
| Venue | CDIO Innovation Practice Center, Dept. of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering (West side, 1st Floor, Science and Technology Center) |
| Advancement | Outstanding teams from the campus qualifier will represent Shantou University in the provincial competition |

## Background

The National College Student Engineering Training Comprehensive Competition is a national academic contest sponsored by the Department of Higher Education of the Ministry of Education, designed to assess and promote the cultivation of college students' engineering practice abilities and innovative spirit. The campus qualifier is organized by STU's School of Engineering.
