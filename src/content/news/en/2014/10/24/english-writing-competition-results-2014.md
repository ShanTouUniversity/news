It is reported that the STU qualifier for the Guangdong Provincial University English Writing Competition and the FLTRP Cup National English Writing Competition has been successfully completed, and the results have been announced by the English Language Center.

## Competition Overview

A total of 58 students participated in this year's qualifier. Each contestant was required to write two compositions of distinctly different genres, assessing their overall English writing proficiency.

The competition used the JuKu (句酷) online grading system for scoring, ensuring objective and standardized evaluation.

## Results

The English Language Center has compiled the competition results. Participating students may inquire about their scores through their respective schools (and colleges) to prepare for the upcoming provincial round.
