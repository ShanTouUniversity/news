It is reported that the Chong Kon Edmond Academy of Design (CKAD) at Shantou University will host an internship sharing session on October 29 at MYSTUdio, featuring two undergraduates who undertook a five-week internship at Kuato Studio, a game development company in London, UK.

## Session Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | London Game Studio Internship Sharing |
| Speakers | Two CKAD undergraduates (Horizons Scholarship recipients) |
| Time | 3:00 PM, Wednesday, October 29, 2014 |
| Venue | MYSTUdio |

The two students completed their internship at the renowned London game company Kuato Studio during the summer vacation of 2014, gaining valuable hands-on experience in game development projects. According to the organizers, the session aims to provide faculty and students interested in internship opportunities in the UK with firsthand insights. All are welcome to attend.
