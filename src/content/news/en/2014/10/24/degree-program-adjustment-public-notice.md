It is reported that Shantou University has released the results of its dynamic adjustment of degree authorization points for public comment: the university plans to revoke the second-level discipline master's authorization point in Engineering Mechanics while adding a professional degree authorization point for the Juris Master program. The public comment period runs from October 24 to November 2, 2014.

## Adjustment Based on National and Provincial Pilot Schemes

The adjustment was carried out in accordance with the notice of the Academic Degrees Committee of the State Council on piloting the dynamic adjustment of doctoral and master's degree authorization disciplines and professional degree categories (Xuewei [2014] No. 2), as well as Guangdong Province's implementation plan for the dynamic adjustment of degree authorization points (Yue Xuewei [2014] No. 14).

On the afternoon of October 23, 2014, Shantou University held the fourth session of its eighth Academic Degree Evaluation Committee in Room 201 of the New Administrative Center, where the adjustment plan was reviewed and approved.

## Written Comments Accepted During Publicity Period

According to the arrangement, any organization or individual may submit written comments to the Graduate School during the publicity period. Notably, commenters must provide their real name, work unit, mailing address, and contact telephone number; otherwise, the comments will not be accepted.
