It is reported that following recommendations and assessments by schools (and colleges) and review by the Student Affairs Office and the Alumni Office, 35 senior students are proposed to receive the 2014 Alumni Grant, with the funding standard of 2,500 yuan per person. The list is now publicized for comments.

## Publicity Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | 35 senior students (e.g., Xie XY and others) |
| Funding standard | 2,500 yuan per person |
| Publicity period | From October 24, 2014 until 12:00 on October 27 |
| Reviewing units | Student Affairs Office and Alumni Office |
| Recommendation method | Nominated and assessed by schools/colleges |

## Feedback

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | Before 12:00 on Monday, October 27 |
| Form | Written submission |
| Location | Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administrative Center) |
| From institutions | Official seal required |
| From individuals | Real name signature required |

During the publicity period, any unit or individual with differing opinions may submit feedback as required above.

> ⚠️ The full list of recipients is available in the attachment on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
