After reviewing student applications, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office and Alumni Relations Office proposed awarding 2014 Heyuan Alumni scholarships to 10 students, with each grant set at RMB 2,000. The university opened a public review of the proposed recipients.

Objections could be submitted in writing to the Student Financial Aid Management Center, Room 106 of the Administration Center, by noon on October 27, 2014. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The proposed recipients’ list is available in the original OA attachment.
