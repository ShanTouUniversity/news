It is reported that the Australian Research Center at Shantou University's Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication was officially established in October 2014, with Professor Zhang Wei appointed as its director.

## Center Mission and Objectives

The Australian Research Center aims to advance academic exchange and cooperation between the school and Australian universities and research institutions. Through bilateral research projects, joint programs, and scholar exchanges, the center will expand the school's international academic network and further enhance its global influence in journalism and communication scholarship.

## Leadership

Professor Zhang Wei serves as the director of the center, responsible for academic planning and daily operations. The center will build upon the school's existing teaching and research resources to gradually establish a normalized mechanism for Sino-Australian journalism and communication academic cooperation.
