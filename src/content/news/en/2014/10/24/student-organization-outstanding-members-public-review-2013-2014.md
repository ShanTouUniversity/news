Shantou University’s Youth League Committee opened a public review of members proposed for outstanding performance in university-level student organizations during the 2013–14 academic year. The proposed list was based on the student cadre management rules, members’ work during the previous academic year, self-assessments and internal organization reviews, and was reviewed by the committee.

Feedback could be submitted in writing or by phone to the committee by 5 p.m. on October 28, 2014. The names are provided in the attachment to the original OA notice and are not reproduced here to protect student privacy.
