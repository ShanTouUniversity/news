It is reported that the Chong Kon Edmond Academy of Design (CKAD) at Shantou University will host a "Parallax" themed workshop from October 28 to 30, open to all currently enrolled STU students.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Parallax |
| Time | October 28-30, 2014, 14:00-17:00 daily |
| Venue | Room 402 Multimedia Classroom, CKAD Front Building |
| Participants | All current STU students |
| Quota | 25 spots, first come first served |

According to the organizers, registration is available starting today at the academic affairs office outside the 3rd floor of the CKAD Front Building. The three-day intensive workshop will cover the principles and practical applications of parallax effects, helping participants develop relevant creative skills.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Chong Kon Edmond Academy of Design)
