It is reported that Henry Zheng, an alumnus of Shantou University's English Major (Class of 1994), will return to campus on October 25, 2014, to deliver a sharing session titled "Think new! Life new!" for faculty, staff, and students.

## About the Speaker

Henry Zheng entered Shantou University in 1990 and graduated from the English Major of the College of Foreign Languages in 1994. After graduation, he pursued a career in international coaching and is now a certified coach registered with the MCF International Coaching Association and an MCF ACMC Professional Coach, dedicated to helping individuals and organizations unlock their potential.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | "Think new! Life new!" |
| Time | Saturday, October 25, 2014, 9:30 AM |
| Location | Conference Room 3, Academic Exchange Center |

## Significance

It is reported that the event provides a valuable platform for students to engage directly with an accomplished alumnus. Henry Zheng will draw on his personal journey and professional experience to discuss how to maintain innovative thinking and chart a meaningful career path in a rapidly changing world, offering students practical insights into navigating their own futures.
