It is reported that the Labor Union of Shantou University has announced the group allocations for the 2014 staff basketball tournament. Three teams have been placed in each of Group A and Group B, with matches kicking off on the afternoon of October 27.

## Group Allocation

| Group | Teams |
|-------|-------|
| Group A | Resource Management, College of Engineering, Administrative Staff |
| Group B | Arts/Business Combined, Law/Research/News/Library Combined, Arts/Social Sciences/Continuing Ed/Affiliated School Combined, College of Science |

## Match Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Kickoff | Monday, October 27, 2014, 4:30 p.m. |
| Refreshments | Provided by the university Labor Union |

The union encourages faculty, staff, and students to come and watch the games to enjoy the spirited competition and team spirit on display.
