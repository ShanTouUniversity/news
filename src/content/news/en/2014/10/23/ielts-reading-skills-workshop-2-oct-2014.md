It is reported that the Center for Integrated Language Learning (CILL) at Shantou University will host the second session of its IELTS Reading Skills Workshop on the afternoon of October 29, helping students build on previously learned strategies to further improve their reading test performance.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | IELTS Reading Skills (Session 2) |
| Date & Time | Wednesday, October 29, 2014, 4:15–6:00 PM |
| Venue | Center for Integrated Language Learning (CILL) |

This session focuses on the reading section of the IELTS exam, guiding students to apply strategies learned in prior workshops and systematically train reading techniques to boost both speed and accuracy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Center for Integrated Language Learning)
