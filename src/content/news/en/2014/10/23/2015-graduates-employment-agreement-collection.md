Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced that employment agreements for the undergraduate Class of 2015 had been distributed to class monitors. Graduating students could collect their copies from their class monitors.

The office noted that each agreement was linked to the student’s ID number by a barcode and advised students to keep the document secure.
