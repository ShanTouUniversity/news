It is reported that the 10th Graduate Academic Forum at Shantou University has been officially launched, with the theme "Innovation · Development."

## Forum Content

The forum will feature the following components:

- **Invited Keynote Reports**: Renowned scholars from within and outside the university will deliver academic presentations
- **Independent Academic Exchange**: Each training unit will organize graduate students to conduct autonomous academic exchanges and research activities according to their assigned sub-forum themes

Each training unit will establish sub-forums based on its research directions, guiding graduate students to explore cutting-edge academic issues in depth around the forum theme. Outstanding papers will be centrally showcased at the conclusion of the forum to promote academic interaction and result sharing among graduate students.
