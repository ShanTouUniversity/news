It is reported that the 2014 Sendeli Chemical Scholarship at the College of Science, Shantou University, is now accepting applications. Four undergraduate students from the College of Science will be awarded this year.

## Scholarship Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Name | STU Sendeli Chemical Scholarship |
| Eligibility | Full-time undergraduate students of the College of Science |
| Annual quota | 4 students |
| Award amount | 2,500 yuan per recipient |
| Established | 2007 |
| Donors | Mr. Chen Zhaohan and Ms. Qiu Liling (distinguished alumni of the College of Science) |

## Background

It is reported that the Sendeli Chemical Scholarship was established in 2007 with a donation of 50,000 yuan from Mr. Chen Zhaohan and Mrs. Qiu Liling, distinguished alumni of the College of Science, to reward outstanding undergraduate students. In 2012, the couple made an additional donation of 100,000 yuan to further support the scholarship.

Students wishing to apply are required to submit their application materials according to the prescribed procedures.
