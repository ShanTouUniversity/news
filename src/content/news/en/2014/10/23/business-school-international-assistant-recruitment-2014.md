It is reported that the Business School at Shantou University issued a recruitment notice on October 23, 2014, seeking one full-time International Exchange and Cooperation Project Assistant to support the school's international engagement activities.

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Education | Master's degree or above |
| Language | Strong Chinese and English writing skills and proficiency in English |
| Skills | Familiarity with Microsoft Office and other office software |
| Preferred | Prior overseas study or work experience is an advantage |

Interested candidates should submit their application materials in accordance with the announcement requirements.
