It is reported that the first phase of the new residential college construction project at Shantou University officially broke ground in October 2014, with three new residential colleges expected to be completed and ready for occupancy by early September 2015.

## Background of the College System

Zhicheng College was established in July 2008 as the first undergraduate four-year residential college in mainland China. After six years of exploration and practice, it has developed a relatively mature residential college model dedicated to creating a warm and harmonious living and learning environment.

## Core Philosophy of the New Colleges

The new residential colleges will continue to build on the experience of Zhicheng College, fostering students’ confidence, independence, and sense of social responsibility through cross-disciplinary residential living and the collision of diverse modes of thinking. This cross-disciplinary residential learning model is regarded as an important component of STU’s undergraduate education reform.

## Construction Progress

According to the project schedule, construction of the three new colleges will proceed steadily on the existing campus, with completion and acceptance targeted for September 2015. The new facilities will accommodate more undergraduate students in the residential college system, further expanding its coverage.
