Shantou University’s School of Science established an International Cooperation Office to advance the school’s international exchange and cooperative education work. The decision was approved at a school affairs meeting on October 14, 2014.

The office is headed by Xin Weixian, with Xu Fei, Chen Changjin, Lu Fushen and Mei Zhiping as members and Xin Qiting as secretary.
