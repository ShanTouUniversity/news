Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Commission announced the results of its 2014 integrity-themed reading response contest. An expert panel selected 44 winning entries: four first prizes, six second prizes, 12 third prizes and 22 merit awards.

The contest was held during the university’s annual discipline education month to promote integrity culture on campus. Student names and identifying year and program details are omitted to protect privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Discipline Inspection Commission)
