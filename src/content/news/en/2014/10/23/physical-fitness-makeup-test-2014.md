It is reported that the Physical Education Department of Shantou University has announced the schedule for the 2014 undergraduate physical fitness test makeup session, which will be held on the morning of October 26.

## Test Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Test type | Makeup physical fitness test for undergraduates |
| Makeup test date | Sunday, October 26, 2014, morning |
| Regular test date | October 19, 2014 |
| Organizing department | Physical Education Department |

It is reported that the regular physical fitness testing for all undergraduates was successfully completed on October 19. Students required to attend the makeup session are expected to arrive on time in accordance with the department's regulations. Further details will be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
