It is reported that the 2014 Chemistry Alumni Scholarship at the College of Science, Shantou University, has officially opened for applications. The scholarship is awarded to full-time undergraduate students in the Chemistry Department who demonstrate both academic excellence and financial need.

## Scholarship Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Name | STU Chemistry Alumni Scholarship |
| Eligibility | Full-time undergraduates in the Chemistry Department with academic merit and financial need |
| Annual quota | 20 students (including 8 first-year students) |
| Award amount | 2,500 yuan per recipient |
| Application deadline | Before 12:00 noon, Monday, October 27, 2014 |
| Submission location | Youth League Committee, College of Science |

## Purpose

It is reported that the scholarship was established through the initiative of dedicated alumni of the Chemistry Department. Its purpose is to encourage outstanding students to enroll in the Chemistry program at STU, motivate current students to study hard and pursue excellence, and assist students facing financial difficulties in completing their education.

Students wishing to apply are required to submit their application materials to the Youth League Committee of the College of Science before the deadline.
