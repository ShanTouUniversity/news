It is reported that the STU Youth League has completed the volunteer selection process for its Leprosy Rehabilitation Village project, with 36 students chosen to participate.

## Project Overview

The Leprosy Rehabilitation Village project is a volunteer service initiative organized by the STU Youth League, aimed at encouraging students to care for vulnerable groups and develop a sense of social responsibility. The project attracted 75 applicants from across campus.

## Selected Volunteers

After a comprehensive review and selection process, 36 students, including Peng Xiqi, were selected as volunteers for this year's Leprosy Rehabilitation Village project. The full list of volunteers has been published on the Youth League's official website.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Applicants | 75 |
| Selected | 36 |
| Organizer | STU Committee of the Communist Youth League |

The Youth League stated that the project will further promote volunteer service culture on campus and encourage more students to engage in social practice and public welfare activities.
