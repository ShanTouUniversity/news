The Firefly volunteer team of Shantou University’s Youth Volunteer Association announced that it had completed selection for the Fall 2014 Sunshine Community service project. Recruitment opened university-wide on October 10; 106 students applied and 41 were selected.

The project aimed to support disadvantaged groups in eastern Guangdong and strengthen young volunteers’ sense of service and social responsibility. The selected volunteers’ names appear in the original OA attachment and are not reproduced here.
