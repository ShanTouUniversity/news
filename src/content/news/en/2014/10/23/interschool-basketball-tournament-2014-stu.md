It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University officially launched the 2014 "Inter-School Cup" basketball tournament on October 23, 2014. The competition begins with an All-Star exhibition game on October 25 and concludes with the championship match on November 22.

## Schedule

| Stage | Date | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All-Star Game | Saturday, October 25 | Opening exhibition |
| Group Stage | October 26 – November 12 (Wed, Sat, Sun) | Round-robin by school |
| Semifinals | November 15–16 | Top four compete |
| Third-Place Match | November 19 | Bronze medal game |
| Championship | November 22 | Finals |

## Significance

The "Inter-School Cup" is an annual tradition at Shantou University, designed to cultivate students' teamwork abilities, strengthen friendships across schools, and enliven campus cultural life. The tournament progresses from group matches to the finals, serving as a key platform for each school to showcase team cohesion and sportsmanship.
