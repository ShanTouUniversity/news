Shantou University’s School of Journalism and Communication completed selection for “Public Photography: A Hundred Villages of Native Chaoshan,” a public course for the fall 2014–15 semester, and announced that participants had been chosen from among applicants across the university. The course team said teaching would follow its planned schedule.

The participant list and course schedule were attached to the original OA notice. Student names are not reproduced here to protect privacy.
