Ahead of the 2015 National Natural Science Fund application cycle, Shantou University’s Research Office recommended resources to help faculty prepare proposals and understand funding trends and review criteria.

Suggested materials included the Fund section of ScienceNet, the National Natural Science Foundation of China’s journal *Fund Review*, and other literature. Topics included framing research questions, selecting discipline codes, citing literature properly and addressing common issues raised in peer review. The office also invited faculty to share useful materials.
