The Shantou University Youth League announced the fall 2014 schedule for the “Dawn Volunteer Service-Learning Course.” A service-site visit was planned for October 12, followed by training sessions on October 15 and 22, October 29, and November 5. The community practice period was scheduled to run from October 19 to December 7, with a wrap-up and sharing session on December 10.

Detailed class times and venues were included in the OA attachment. This report reflects the schedule announced at the time and does not infer whether the activities took place as planned.
