It is reported that the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL) under the English Language Center (ELC) has put its four core services into full operation this week, with appointments available through the ELC/CILL Booking System.

Each service addresses a different aspect of language learning for the STU community:

| Service | Format | Focus |
|---------|--------|-------|
| How Do I Improve My English? Independent Language Learning Consultation | One-on-one with teacher-consultants | Helps students identify personal language goals, build a personalized learning plan and receive ongoing support through follow-up meetings |
| Voice & Accent Consultation | One-on-one or small group with trainers | Covers pronunciation, intonation, accent and all aspects of speaking style |
| The Writing Studio (TWS) | One-on-one with peer tutors | Assists with ELC writing assignments or other writing, including brainstorming, using reference materials and organizing papers; proofreading is not provided |
| Speech & Presentation Studio (S&P Studio) | Small group with peer guides | Students rehearse English presentations and discuss content, delivery, body language and audience impact |

Booking links for each service:

- HDIIME Consultation: https://my.stu.edu.cn/courses/elc/mod/page/view.php?id=20537
- Voice & Accent Consultation: https://my.stu.edu.cn/courses/elc/mod/page/view.php?id=20536
- The Writing Studio: https://my.stu.edu.cn/courses/elc/mod/page/view.php?id=20538
- Speech & Presentation Studio: https://my.stu.edu.cn/courses/elc/mod/page/view.php?id=20539

Students and staff in need of language support may choose the relevant service and book online.
