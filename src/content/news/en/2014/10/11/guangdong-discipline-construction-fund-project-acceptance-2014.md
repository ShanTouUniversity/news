Shantou University’s Research Office set out the 2014 closeout review process for projects funded through Guangdong’s university discipline-development programme. General projects classified as seed projects were to undergo document review, while science and technology innovation projects were to receive expert correspondence review; both were assessed against their project contracts. The reviews were due to be completed by December 15, 2014.

The Guangdong Department of Education was responsible for reviewing key science and technology innovation projects, with details to be coordinated directly with project leaders. Project lists, review guidance and report templates accompanied the OA notice; individual project information is not reproduced here.
