The 2014 National Scholarship review committee at Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts proposed a student from the 2011-entry English programme for the award after its assessment. Written feedback was accepted until noon on October 13, 2014, at the school’s Youth League office, Room 201 in the Wen building.

The student’s name is withheld to protect privacy. The original notice provided the school’s telephone number and email address for inquiries.
