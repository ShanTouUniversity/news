Applications opened for the General Administration of Sport of China’s 2015 sports philosophy and social science research projects. The programme included key, general and youth projects. Key projects usually ran for one to two years, while general and youth projects typically ran for one year. Shantou University could submit no more than three applications.

Applicants for key and general projects needed a senior professional title or an administrative rank of deputy division level or above. Youth applicants needed a master’s degree or higher, no senior professional title, and had to be under 40 as of October 25, 2014. Applicants leading certain active national or Ministry of Education projects were ineligible. Campus review materials were due by October 21, with printed applications and project summaries due to the Research Office by October 23. Forms and the project guide accompanied the OA notice.
