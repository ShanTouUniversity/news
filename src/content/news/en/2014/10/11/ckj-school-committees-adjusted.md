It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication decided on October 9, 2014, after deliberation by its executive committee, to adjust the membership of four bodies: the school's Executive Committee, Teaching Committee, Academic Committee, and Laboratory Work Committee. The reshuffle stems from internal personnel changes and the needs of the school's development.

## Composition After the Adjustment

| Committee | Chair | Key Members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive Committee | Fan Dongsheng | Ni Qingqing, Du Shimin, Bai Jing, Wang Zongan, Mao Liangbin (secretary: Li Jing) |
| Teaching Committee | Fan Dongsheng | Bai Jing (convener), Wang Zongan, Yun Qidong, Xie Lin, Hu Lufeng, Fan Linjun, Daniel Wu, Zhang Wei, Lai Mingming, Cai Qiuyan (secretary: Lin Yan) |
| Academic Committee | Fan Dongsheng | — |
| Laboratory Work Committee | Hu Lufeng | — |

According to the roster, Dean Fan Dongsheng simultaneously chairs the Executive, Teaching, and Academic Committees, while the Laboratory Work Committee is led by Hu Lufeng. The teaching committee's lineup includes senior faculty members as well as international teaching staff, reflecting the school's overall governance structure across teaching, research, and laboratory operations.
