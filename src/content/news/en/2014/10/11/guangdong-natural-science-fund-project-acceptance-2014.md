Shantou University’s Research Office opened the 2014 closeout process for projects funded by the Guangdong Natural Science Fund. Under the notice, key projects were to undergo meeting-based review and a special financial audit; their leaders were asked to submit proposed review dates and preliminary expert lists by October 24. General projects were to be assessed by external review. The project list was attached to the OA notice and is not reproduced here.

Project reports and expert reviews were scheduled for completion by November 2. Electronic materials were due to the Research Office from November 3 to 5, followed by two printed copies of approved materials between November 6 and 10. The notice said extensions would no longer be accepted that year. Projects failing review would need to submit corrective measures and undergo re-evaluation within the prescribed periods.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
