Shantou University’s MYSTUdio and the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design planned a Hakitzu robot game event from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on October 17, 2014, at MYSTUdio on the library’s first floor. Participants would use code to control robots in the game; some of its settings were created by STU art students.

The event was intended for students familiar with Hakitzu and was limited to 16 participants, with successful registrants to be notified by email. Organizers planned a brief training session on October 15 and intended to announce winning teams and award prizes at the event. As the notice did not include an outcome, this report describes the announced plan only.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Centre; Cheung Kong School of Art and Design)
