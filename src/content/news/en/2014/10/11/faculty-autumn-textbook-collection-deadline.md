Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office asked faculty members who had not yet collected their fall 2014 teaching books to bring a textbook requisition form to the service counter in the New Administration Centre by October 15. Books still unclaimed after that date were to be returned to the supplier.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
