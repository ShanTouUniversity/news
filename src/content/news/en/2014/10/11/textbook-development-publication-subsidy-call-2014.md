Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office opened its fall 2014 application round for textbook development and publication subsidies. The call was for applicants planning to publish textbooks in the second half of the year who met the university’s subsidy criteria.

Applicants were asked to complete the application form and prepare three copies of the supporting materials for delivery to the Academic Affairs service counter on the third floor of the New Administration Centre by November 7, 2014. An electronic copy of the form was also required. The subsidy policy and form were attached to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The subsidy policy and application form are available as OA attachments.
