Applications opened for Guangdong’s 2014 innovation plan for degree and graduate education. Categories included graduate academic forums, summer schools, research on graduate education reform, model graduate courses, joint graduate training bases and other graduate education initiatives.

Proposals had to be included in each university’s 2014–2016 “Innovation and Strengthening Universities” plan, with funding arranged by the institution. Shantou University set category-specific submission limits. After internal selection, recommended proposals were to be publicly posted for at least seven days before provincial review. Printed applications and funding commitment documents were due to STU’s Graduate School by November 3, 2014, along with an electronic summary. The OA attachments contain category limits, application forms and project guides.
