Shantou University’s Research Office began its 2014 implementation review for projects funded by the Guangdong Natural Science Fund. The review covered active projects, projects concluding that year and follow-up projects from earlier cycles. Project leaders were asked to prepare annual implementation or follow-up reports, as applicable.

Electronic reports were due to the Research Office for review by November 6, 2014. Signed paper copies of reports that passed review were due by November 10. Templates and project lists accompanied the OA notice. Individual project personnel and identification numbers are not reproduced here because they are not needed to explain the process.
