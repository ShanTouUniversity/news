Shantou University’s MYSTUdio planned a TED talk screening and discussion from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on October 16, 2014, in the university library. The session was to feature selected talks and invite participants to exchange views on society, technology and personal development.

Places were limited to the first 50 confirmed registrants, who were to be notified by email. Registration opened on October 11, and attendees were asked to check in by 7:15 p.m. The notice did not report the event’s outcome; this article records only the arrangements announced at the time.
