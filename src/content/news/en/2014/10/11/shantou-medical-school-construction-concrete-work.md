Contractors for Shantou University’s new Medical School project planned to pour concrete for a beam-and-slab section of the south tower’s fourth floor from 2:00 p.m. on October 11 until 3:00 a.m. on October 12, 2014. The work was expected to continue without interruption and could create considerable noise. The university’s key construction projects office alerted nearby residents and apologized for the disturbance.
