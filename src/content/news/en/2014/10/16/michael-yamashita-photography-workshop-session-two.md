Due to a change in the speaker’s schedule, Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication moved the start of National Geographic photographer Michael Yamashita’s second photography critique session from 1 p.m. to 2 p.m. on October 22, 2014. The session was still scheduled to end at 6 p.m.

The event was to take place in Meeting Room 3 at the Academic Exchange Center (ACC). The available notice recorded the schedule change but did not report on the session’s outcome.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication)
