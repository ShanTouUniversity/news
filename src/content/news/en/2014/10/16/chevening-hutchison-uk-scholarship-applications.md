It is reported that the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University recently forwarded a notice to all schools (residential colleges) announcing that applications for the 2015/16 Hutchison Whampoa Chevening scholarships for study in the United Kingdom are now open. Students aspiring to pursue postgraduate studies in the UK may visit the official website for details and submit their applications.

## Over 700 Master's Students Supported in Twelve Years

The Hutchison Whampoa Chevening scholarship program was established in 2002. Over the past twelve years, Hutchison Whampoa has donated more than £6.4 million in total, providing scholarships for over 710 master's students from Hong Kong and mainland China.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Established | 2002 |
| Cumulative donations | Over £6.4 million |
| Recipients | More than 710 master's students from Hong Kong and mainland China |
| Funding covers | Full-time master's degree programs at UK universities, generally lasting one year |

## How to Apply

The scholarship program provides funding for students pursuing full-time master's degree courses at UK universities, typically for one year. Interested students may visit http://www.chevening.org/hongkong for specific application requirements and procedures.
