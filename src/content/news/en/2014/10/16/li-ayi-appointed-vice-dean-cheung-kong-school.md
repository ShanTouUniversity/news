In October 2014, Shantou University appointed Li Aiyi vice dean of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. Under STU Document No. 122 [2014], Li no longer served as the school’s administrative director or office director.

The appointment was considered at the 13th university leadership meeting on September 29, 2014.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Document No. 122 [2014])
