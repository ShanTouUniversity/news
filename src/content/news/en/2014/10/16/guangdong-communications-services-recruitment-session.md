Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced that Guangdong Communications Services Co., Ltd. was scheduled to hold a special recruitment session on October 18, 2014, in the open-floor area of the Administration Building. Follow-up interviews were scheduled for the following morning at the Student Learning Support Center.

The recruitment brochure was included with the original notice. The available OA text gave the session and interview times and venues but did not list positions or report hiring outcomes.
