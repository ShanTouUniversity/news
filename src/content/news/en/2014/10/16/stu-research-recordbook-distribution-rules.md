Shantou University’s Research Office outlined distribution, inspection and archiving procedures for research recordbooks under the university’s rules on research activity records, approved and issued in July 2014. Eligible projects included active provincial- or ministerial-level projects (except those concluding by the end of 2014) and all newly approved projects.

The allocation was one to three books per national-level project, one or two for provincial- or ministerial-level projects, and one for other projects, according to need. Leaders of newly approved projects could collect the recordbook with the project funding book; secondary units were to collect books for active projects and distribute them to project leaders.

The Research Office planned spot checks during annual, midterm and completion reviews. Records were to be retained carefully; those requiring formal archiving had to be submitted with completion materials for university safekeeping, while other books were to remain with the project team for reference, generally for at least five years.
