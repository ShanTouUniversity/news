Shantou University Library scheduled a training session on the Web of Science platform under the theme “See the Present, Discover the Future.” Ma Yapeng, a senior trainer from Thomson Reuters, was to cover literature searches, finding new research directions, tracking developments, managing references and improving writing and submission workflows.

The session was scheduled for October 17, 2014, from 4:10 to 5:30 p.m. in Lecture Room E101. The available notice did not report on what followed.
