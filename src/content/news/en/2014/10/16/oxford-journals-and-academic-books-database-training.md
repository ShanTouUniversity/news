Shantou University Library scheduled online training on the Oxford Journals and Oxford Academic Books databases, covering platform features, how to use the two resources together, and recommended journals and titles.

The speaker was Zhou Chen, a trainer from Oxford University Press. The session was scheduled for October 17, 2014, from 7:30 to 8:30 p.m. in the Subject Services Department inside the Library’s second-floor service desk area. The available notice did not report on the session’s outcome.
