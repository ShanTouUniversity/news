Shantou University’s Office of International Cooperation and Exchange updated arrangements for a lecture by Zhuang Peiyuan scheduled for October 17, 2014, from 7:30 to 9 p.m. More than 180 reservations had been received, exceeding the original venue’s 60-seat capacity. The event was moved to Meeting Room 3 at the Academic Exchange Center, which could accommodate 100 people.

The notice said successful reservations were allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. The lecture was to be recorded for later release by the university news center. The attendee list is omitted to protect privacy.
