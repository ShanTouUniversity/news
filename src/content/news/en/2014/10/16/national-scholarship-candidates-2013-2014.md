It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on October 16, 2014, to publicize the candidates for the 2013–2014 National Scholarship.

Following the provincial Department of Education’s notice, the university organized the selection. Colleges (residential colleges) formulated detailed rules under the *Shantou University National Scholarship Evaluation Measures*, set up committees and determined candidates through preliminary selection and interviews in line with openness, fairness and merit.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Publicized | 2013–2014 National Scholarship candidates (list in OA attachment, withheld here for privacy) |
| Deadline for objections | 12:00, October 20, 2014 |
| How to object | Submit in writing to the Student Aid Management Center (Room 106, Administration Center); organizations should affix a seal, individuals should sign with real name |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |
