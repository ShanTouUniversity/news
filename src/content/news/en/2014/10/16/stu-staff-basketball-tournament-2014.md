Shantou University’s labor union scheduled its 2014 staff basketball tournament for October 27 through November 5. Games were planned for Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays at 4:30 p.m. on campus courts 5–8.

The notice listed seven teams, including joint teams from the Colleges of Liberal Arts and Business, the School of Science, the School of Engineering, and several other university units, along with the administration and Resources Management teams. Teams were asked to register by October 21 and attend a draw on October 22. The available notice did not report on tournament results.
