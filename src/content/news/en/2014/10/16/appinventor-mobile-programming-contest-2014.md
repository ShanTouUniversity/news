To promote the application of App Inventor technology and use it to showcase traditional and modern Chinese culture, the Introduction to Mobile Programming course team of the Computer Science Department at Shantou University's College of Engineering will join hands with the Global Research Center of the College of Liberal Arts to organize an App Inventor mobile programming contest.

According to the organizers, two groups may enter: teaching assistants of this semester's Introduction to Mobile Programming course, and STU undergraduates in their second year or above — the latter must have their work recommended by a teacher from the Computer Science Department or the Global Research Center, with each teacher allowed to recommend at most two students. All text in the entries must be in English (Chinese is permitted when introducing Chinese characters), and the apps may present Chinese arts and culture such as traditional crafts, cuisine, and customs, as well as cultural themes from anywhere in China, not limited to Guangdong.

Prizes include one first prize — an Honor 6 mobile 4G smartphone — and six second prizes, each a Redmi Note mobile 4G smartphone. The registration form, detailed design report, source code, and an APK file no larger than 5 MB must be submitted as one packaged attachment by 23:59 on Friday, November 28, 2014, with winners announced and prizes awarded before the end of December.

Notably, all entries — including images, text, audio, and video within the apps — must be original works. The installation packages will be hosted on the digital humanities international research platform website of the Global Research Center (http://gdh.stu.edu.cn/) for download worldwide. Entrants must also allow the teaching team to cite their apps as cases in classes and related papers, and winning works must accept help from international teachers at the Global Research Center with text revisions.
