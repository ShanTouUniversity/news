In 2014, Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office outlined preliminary work for undergraduate theses and projects in the 2014–15 spring semester. Colleges and departments were asked to follow university procedures for eligibility checks, topic approval, student topic selection and assignment issuance.

As a general eligibility rule, students needed to have earned credits plus credits in current courses totaling at least 85% of their program’s graduation requirement, and to have completed all foundational and core courses in their major. The schedule used semester-week milestones—weeks 6, 7, 9, 13, 14 and 16—for launching thesis work, checking eligibility, reporting student counts, approving topics, completing selections and issuing assignments.

Topics were to align with program objectives and generally be assigned one per student. Supervisors were required to hold at least an intermediate professional title and have research experience. These were historical academic arrangements published in 2014.
