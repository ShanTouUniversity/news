In 2014, Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design invited faculty and students to submit proposals for the third Public Art Festival, under the theme “20 Years Later (Red Year).” Proposals could be sent to the school’s official email address or delivered to its modeling laboratory.

The notice set October 28, 2014, as the submission deadline and scheduled judging for December 29. It said the selected artists’ list and a meeting date were to be announced on October 30. These were the arrangements published at the time.
