Shantou University’s School of Science scheduled a talk in its “Toward Modern Mathematics” series by Yuan Wenjun, professor and doctoral supervisor at Guangzhou University’s School of Mathematics and Information Science. His topic was “Hayman’s Conjecture on Normal Families and Research Progress.”

The talk was scheduled for 9 a.m. on October 17, 2014, in the Mathematics Department laboratory, and was to cover normality criteria for families of meromorphic functions and related research. Yuan’s work focuses on complex analysis and its applications. The available notice did not report on the lecture’s outcome.
