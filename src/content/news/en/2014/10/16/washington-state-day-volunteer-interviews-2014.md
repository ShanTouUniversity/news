More than 50 students applied to volunteer for the “Washington State Day—Meet in Shantou” event. A review panel shortlisted 20 students for interviews, which were scheduled for the afternoon of October 16, 2014, in Room 222 of the Administration Center.

Candidates were asked to introduce themselves, Shantou University and Shantou in English. The interview schedule and names are in the original OA attachment and are omitted here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Cooperation and Exchange; Shantou University Youth League Committee)
> ⚠️ The interview list and schedule are available in the original OA attachment.
