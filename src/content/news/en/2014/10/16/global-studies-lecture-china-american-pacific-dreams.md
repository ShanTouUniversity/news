Shantou University’s Center for Global Studies in the College of Liberal Arts scheduled the second open lecture of the Third STU Global Studies Symposium under the title “China Dream, American Dream, or Pacific Dream?” The speaker, Patrick Mendis, was then a distinguished senior fellow and affiliate professor in public and international affairs at George Mason University and a commissioner of the U.S. National Commission for UNESCO.

The lecture was scheduled for October 19, 2014, from 7:30 to 9 p.m. in Room E409. Mendis was to discuss the history of China–U.S. relations and the concept of a “Pacific Dream.” The available notice did not report on the event’s outcome.
