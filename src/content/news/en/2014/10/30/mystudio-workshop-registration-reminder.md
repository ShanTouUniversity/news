Shantou University’s Network and Information Center issued a registration reminder for the MYSTUdio Workshop. The notice said that clicking “I want to attend” indicated a commitment to participate and advised people not to register unless they could confirm their availability.

The original notice provided an on-campus event registration page, where further workshop arrangements could be checked.
