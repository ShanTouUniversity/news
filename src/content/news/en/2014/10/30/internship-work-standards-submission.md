It is reported that, to implement the requirements of the "STU Undergraduate Student Internship Management Regulations" (STU Fa [2014] No. 80), Shantou University is advancing the formulation and review of internship work standards across its teaching units. These standards serve as an important basis and guide for organizing internship work.

The Academic Affairs Office had previously sent the new internship regulations and related templates to the heads of each teaching unit by email on June 18, 2014, asking them to prepare and submit their standards. To speed up the review process and facilitate the implementation and supervision of internship work, the Office now requires all teaching units to submit their internship work standards in paper form — signed by the department head — **by November 7, 2014**. The Office will then organize experts to review the submitted standards and publish them once approved.
