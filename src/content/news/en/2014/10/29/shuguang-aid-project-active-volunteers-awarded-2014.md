It is reported that over the past year, all volunteers of the Shuguang Aid Project at Shantou University have worked actively and selflessly, carrying out long-term and effective volunteer tutoring programs for children from low-income families, migrant workers' children, and visually impaired children under the care of the Shantou Disabled Persons' Federation.

To honor outstanding contributors, the STU Youth League Committee and the University Youth Volunteer Association have decided to award the title of "Active Volunteer" to 65 students, including Chen Jiahong.

## Award Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Shuguang Aid Project, STU |
| Honorary Title | "Active Volunteer" |
| Recipients | 65 students |
| Awarding Bodies | STU Youth League Committee, University Youth Volunteer Association |
| Beneficiaries | Children from low-income families, migrant workers' children, visually impaired children |

The Shuguang Aid Project has been committed to educational support for disadvantaged groups in Shantou, with volunteers providing academic tutoring and companionship to children in need during their spare time.
