Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office published a proposed cost addition for a laboratory renovation on October 29, 2014. The project began on July 1. Based on site conditions, exterior window-sill glass mosaic work and other items were added, with the preliminary estimate putting the increase at about RMB 9,000. Cumulative additions remained below the university’s 10% limit for the project.

Under the university’s construction tendering rules, written objections could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on October 31.
