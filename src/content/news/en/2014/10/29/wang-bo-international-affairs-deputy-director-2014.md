It is reported that after review at the 14th STU leadership meeting on October 24, 2014, Wang Bo, currently serving at the Li Ka Shing Shantou University Development Foundation Office, is proposed as senior management personnel for the International Exchange and Cooperation Office, to serve as Deputy Director. The appointment does not occupy university cadre staffing quota and carries no administrative rank.

## Appointment Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed Position | Deputy Director, International Exchange and Cooperation Office |
| Current Position | Li Ka Shing Shantou University Development Foundation Office |
| Employment Type | Secondment; does not occupy cadre quota, no administrative rank |
| Public Notice Period | Until November 3, 2014 |

Any objections regarding the above appointment during the public notice period may be reported to the Human Resources Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
