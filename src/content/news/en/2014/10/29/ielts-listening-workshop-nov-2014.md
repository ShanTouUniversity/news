It is reported that the Center for Integrated Language Learning (CILL) at Shantou University will host an IELTS Listening Skills Workshop on the afternoon of November 5, aiming to help students master the techniques and strategies required for the IELTS listening section.

## Workshop Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | IELTS Listening Skills |
| Speaker | Ms. Deepali Anand |
| Date & Time | Wednesday, November 5, 2014, 4:15–6:00 PM |
| Venue | Center for Integrated Language Learning (CILL) |
| Admission | By ticket only |

The workshop will focus on the listening portion of the IELTS exam, systematically covering problem-solving techniques and answer strategies to help candidates improve their listening scores. Students who wish to participate are required to bring their tickets for admission.
