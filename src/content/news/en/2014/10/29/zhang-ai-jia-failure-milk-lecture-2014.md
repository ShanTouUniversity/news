It is reported that the Shing Hing Arts Lecture Series of the Chinese University of Hong Kong will hold a lecture at Shantou University on October 31 (Friday) at 4:30 PM. The keynote speaker is the acclaimed actress, screenwriter and director Ms. Zhang Aijia. The lecture topic is "Failure Is the Milk of Success."

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Series | Shing Hing Arts Lecture Series, Chinese University of Hong Kong |
| Speaker | Zhang Aijia (actress, screenwriter, director) |
| Topic | Failure Is the Milk of Success |
| Date | Friday, October 31, 2014 |
| Time | 16:30 |
| Language | Mandarin |
| Organizer | International Exchange and Cooperation Office, STU |

It is reported that the lecture series aims to break down geographical barriers through online sharing, foster closer academic ties between CUHK and STU, and promote cultural exchanges between Guangdong and Hong Kong. A live streaming button will be displayed on the webpage for viewers to watch the lecture online.

> Source: STU OA Notice (International Exchange and Cooperation Office)
