It is reported that the Infrastructure Office issued a notice on October 29, 2014, regarding additional costs for the Building 8 student activity room renovation project.

The project commenced on October 4, 2014. During construction, additional power sockets, power pipelines and plywood ceilings were required due to functional partitioning of indoor rooms. A preliminary estimate puts the additional cost at about 5,000 yuan, with the cumulative increase remaining within the university’s 10% threshold.

In accordance with the *Shantou University Infrastructure Project Bidding and Tendering Management Measures*, the additional cost is now publicized. Those with objections may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 17:30 on October 31, 2014.
