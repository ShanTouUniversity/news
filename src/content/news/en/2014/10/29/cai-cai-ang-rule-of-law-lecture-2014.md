It is reported that to implement the spirit of the Ministry of Education and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education regarding integrating theory with practice in ideological and political theory courses, and to help college students understand national conditions, social realities, and social work methods, the Department of Social Sciences at Shantou University has invited Dr. Cai Cai'ang, Executive Deputy Director of the Shantou Municipal Social Work Committee, to deliver an academic lecture.

## Lecture Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture Topic | Building Reform Consensus through the Rule of Law |
| Speaker | Dr. Cai Cai'ang |
| Date & Time | Saturday, November 1, 2014, 9:00–11:00 AM |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall, Science Building |
| Audience | All students enrolled in the 6-credit course "Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics" (Classes: 72323, 72324, 72325, 72326) |
| Organizer | Department of Social Sciences |

## About the Speaker

Dr. Cai Cai'ang currently serves as Executive Deputy Director of the Shantou Municipal Social Work Committee and as a member of the Legal Advisory Committee of the Shantou Municipal People's Government. He holds visiting professorships at several universities across China. In 2005, he went to Columbia Law School as a senior visiting scholar representing Chinese judges. In 2012, he joined the Post-Doctoral Research Station in International Economic Law at East China University of Political Science and Law. He has published numerous papers in core domestic journals and authored one book.

## Attendance Notes

Students are requested to enter the venue between 8:45 and 8:55. Course instructors are asked to organize attendance.
