Historian Huw Lewis-Jones was scheduled to give a public lecture titled “From Everest to Antarctica” at Shantou University’s Science and Technology Lecture Hall at 7:30 p.m. on October 17, 2014. The event was jointly organized by Student Affairs, the Centre for Global Studies and Zhicheng College. The lecture was to be delivered in English with bilingual slides.

Lewis-Jones previously served as curator of the humanities and history collections at the Scott Polar Research Institute, University of Cambridge, as a visiting scholar at Harvard University, and as curator of maritime history at the National Maritime Museum in the UK. His research includes polar exploration and maritime history. The notice did not confirm whether the lecture took place, so this report reflects the plan announced at the time.
