Shantou University’s Engineering School planned to hold an outdoor development programme for its 2014 freshmen on October 19, 2014, at the Jieyang Quality Education Training Centre. Freshmen, academic mentors, department heads and student leaders were included, with the stated aims of strengthening student–mentor interaction and developing teamwork and organizational skills.

Participants were scheduled to meet at the university motto monument at 7:15 a.m. before travelling to the centre; training was set to run until 5:00 p.m. The school advised participants to wear school shirts, loose sports trousers and athletic shoes and to follow safety instructions. Students unable to attend for health or other special reasons were asked to submit a written leave request to the school’s Youth League office by 5:30 p.m. on October 17. The notice did not report the event’s outcome, so this article describes only the plan announced at the time.
