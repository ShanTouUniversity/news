Applications opened for the 2014 moral-education research projects under Guangdong’s 12th Five-Year Education Science Plan. Projects were to run for two years. Each key commissioned project carried RMB 30,000 in funding, while general projects were unfunded. Shantou University had a campus quota of four applications.

Eligible applicants included university staff working in moral education, administration and related research. Key commissioned proposals had to use a topic from the published guide, while other applicants could develop their own titles. Applicants were asked to email materials for an initial review by October 17, 2014, submit the final version online by October 22 after passing the review, and deliver six printed copies to the Research Office by October 23. The topic guide and application forms were attached to the OA notice.
