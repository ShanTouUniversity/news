Zhuang Peiyuan, chairman of the Chinese Chamber of Commerce of Los Angeles, was scheduled to give a talk titled “Cross-Cultural Communication: Experience and Reflections” at Shantou University from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. on October 17, 2014. The venue was Lecture Hall 5 at the Academic Exchange Centre.

Faculty and students could reserve a place through STU’s mobile website by 5:00 p.m. on October 15. Attendance was limited to 60 people on a first-come, first-served basis, and an admission notice was required. The speaker’s biography is included with the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
> ⚠️ The speaker’s biography and admission notice are available as OA attachments.
