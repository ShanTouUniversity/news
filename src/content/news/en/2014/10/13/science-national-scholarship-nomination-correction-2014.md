Shantou University’s Science School issued a correction on October 13, 2014, to its proposed nominees for the 2014 National Scholarship. The originally nominated student, a member of the 2011 cohort in Applied Chemistry, had taken make-up examinations after participating in an overseas exchange. Once the results were available, the student’s GPA ranking for the 2013–14 academic year was found not to meet the university’s top-10-percent requirement, and the nomination was withdrawn.

After further review, the school’s evaluation committee proposed another student from the 2011 cohort, in Applied Physics. Feedback on the proposed selection was accepted in writing until noon on October 18, 2014, at the Science School Youth League office. The listed contact number was 0754-86502779. Student names are omitted to protect privacy.
