Shantou University’s staff choir club divided its activities into two groups in October 2014 in response to high registration. The first group was scheduled to meet on Wednesdays from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m., and the second on Thursdays at the same time. Both groups were to meet in the multipurpose hall on the third floor of the university union building.

The first group’s opening-week session was moved to Tuesday evening, October 14. Group assignments and instructor information were included in the original OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Trade Union)
> ⚠️ Group assignments and instructor information are available as an attachment in the OA system.
