Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office scheduled a meeting on outstanding Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program projects and guidance for new applications from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. on October 22, 2014, in Room 203 of the E teaching building. Project leaders and finance officers for projects approved in 2014 were expected to attend, with at least one member from each project team present. Other interested students were welcome.

The agenda included a review of earlier undergraduate innovation projects, student presentations on entrepreneurship, patents and academic papers, and guidance on project procedures, interim reviews, final acceptance, reimbursements and the project system. The notice listed Ding Qianhang of the Academic Affairs Office as the contact, at 86502736 or qhding@stu.edu.cn.
