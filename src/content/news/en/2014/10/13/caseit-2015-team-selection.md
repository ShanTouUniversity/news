Shantou University’s Business School opened an internal selection for the CaseIT 2015 international business case competition. Focused on management information systems (MIS), the undergraduate event was scheduled for February 11–14, 2015, at the Beedie School of Business at Simon Fraser University in Canada. Teams would analyze business cases and demonstrate collaboration, problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.

Eligible applicants were full-time undergraduates in the Classes of 2011 or 2012, including Business School students and students from other schools minoring in business. Applicants had to have completed or be taking BUS3186, Business Case Analysis, or a management information systems course, demonstrate strong English listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, and agree to attend pre-competition training. International competition experience was preferred.

Applicants were asked to email a résumé to kingkng@stu.edu.cn by October 22, 2014, with “Participating in the CaseIT Business Competition” in the subject line. The school planned to hold interviews and publish the results on the campus information network and Business School website. The competition package is available with the original OA notice.
