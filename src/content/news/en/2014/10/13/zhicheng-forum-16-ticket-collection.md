Reservations for “Chatting About the Mountains,” the 16th event in Shantou University’s Zhicheng Forum series, exceeded 400. Organizers closed registration because of limited capacity and confirmed the first 400 requests in order of submission.

Confirmed guests were scheduled to collect tickets at Zhicheng College’s lobby with their campus cards on October 13–14, 2014, during either 11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. or 5:00–6:30 p.m. Unclaimed tickets would be forfeited. The lecture was scheduled for October 15, with ticket holders asked to arrive at the Science and Technology Lecture Hall by 7:20 p.m. The reservation list was attached to the OA notice and is not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department and Zhicheng College)
> ⚠️ The reservation list is available as an attachment in the OA system.
