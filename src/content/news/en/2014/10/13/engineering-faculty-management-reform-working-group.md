Shantou University’s Engineering School established a working group on faculty management reform in October 2014 as part of its pilot-school initiative and efforts to address issues in undergraduate teaching reform. The dean was named group leader, with school leaders and programme heads as members; Hu Wenlong was appointed secretary.

The group was tasked with applying the concept of “scholarship of teaching” alongside the school’s CDIO-OBE engineering education reforms. Its work would examine faculty evaluation, appointments, support and role-transition policies, aiming to develop an integrated approach to improving teaching capability and engagement.
