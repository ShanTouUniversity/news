Shantou University’s Business School opened recruitment for the CIMA Global Business Challenge (GBC). The South China regional final was scheduled to be held at STU in mid-to-late December 2014. Preliminary reports were due between October 27 and November 18, and the six regional finalists were expected to be announced on November 26.

The competition was open to full-time undergraduates, including Business School students and students in other schools minoring in business. Teams could enter with four members; individual applicants could also apply and be matched into teams by the competition organizers. Applicants were expected to have strong English skills. The notice recommended prior or current enrollment in BUS3186, Business Case Analysis, and said teams should ideally include students from different disciplines and independently find an adviser.

Teams were asked to register on the CIMA-GBC website by November 14, 2014, and email the entry form to 13jhguo@stu.edu.cn. Individual applicants had to submit their forms to the same address by midnight on October 20. The original OA attachments contain the form, preliminary case and competition guidance. A student contact number from the notice is not reproduced.
