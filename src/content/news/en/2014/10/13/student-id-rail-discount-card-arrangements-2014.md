Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced arrangements in October 2014 for undergraduate student IDs and railway student-discount cards. Students seeking a new discount card were asked to enter their home-travel section in the student services system by October 20 and write the same information in ink on their student IDs. The notice said late entries would not be processed that year.

For 2014-entry students, card payment and information entry were scheduled by school from October 27 to 30; each card cost RMB 7. Students in other cohorts could recharge cards at no charge at the Academic Affairs counter on the third floor of the Administration Centre from October 22 to 24. Replacement fees during that period were RMB 7 for a discount card and RMB 3 for a student ID. The original notice attachment gives school-specific time slots and details for freshman ID processing.
