It is reported that the Resources Management Office of Shantou University has released the September 2014 water and electricity fees for faculty housing. The latest meter readings were taken on October 1 and 2, compared with the previous reading on September 1.

## How to Check and Verify

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Query System | STU Utility Fee Management System |
| Login Account | Personal campus network account |
| Verification Period | October 13–15 |
| Verification Venue | Utilities Center (Tel: 2489) |

Faculty members can log in to the university's utility fee management system to review their personal usage and charges. Anyone with questions or objections should visit the Utilities Center for verification between October 13 and 15.

According to the report, if no objections are raised by the deadline, the Finance Office will deduct the corresponding fees directly from each individual's October 2014 salary account.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
