Shantou University Library has opened trial access to the Peking University legal database. Developed by the Peking University Legal Information Center, the database includes laws and regulations, judicial cases, legal journals, English translations, reference materials, law-firm practice resources, legal video courses, and a judicial examination platform.

## Trial Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Access | [Library database page](http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=397) |
| Trial period | Through January 31, 2015 |
| Coverage | Laws and regulations, judicial cases, legal journals, and related legal information |

The Library welcomed faculty, staff, and students to try the database and provide feedback.
