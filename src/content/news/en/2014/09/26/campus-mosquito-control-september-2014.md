Shantou University’s Resources Management Office will conduct mosquito-control spraying from September 26 to 30, 2014, across the campus, Longquan Garden, and the Continuing Education College as part of local dengue-fever prevention efforts. Faculty, staff, and students were asked to cooperate with the work and take safety precautions.

## Spraying Schedule

| Date | Areas |
| --- | --- |
| September 26 | Kindergarten, A–B faculty residences, and flood-drain channels |
| September 27 | New Administration Center and surrounding areas, dining halls, and Academic Building |
| September 28 | Student residence areas |
| September 29 | Affiliated middle and primary schools through the western residential quadrangle |
| September 30 | Longquan Garden and the Continuing Education College |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
