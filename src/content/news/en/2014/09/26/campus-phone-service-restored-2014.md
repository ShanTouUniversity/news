Shantou University reported that construction to splice and relocate the underground telecommunications lines near the former campus gate had been completed and that telephone lines beginning with 8290 returned to normal on September 26, 2014. The Resources Management Office advised university units to resume normal communication arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
