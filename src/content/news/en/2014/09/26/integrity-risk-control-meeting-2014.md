Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Office will hold a meeting on integrity risk prevention and control at 10:00 on September 30, 2014, in Room 330 of the New Administration Center. The meeting is part of the university’s 2014 Discipline Education Month program and will bring together the principal officials of university units and staff responsible for risk prevention and control.

Participating units must submit the names of their attendees to the Discipline Inspection Office by September 28. Materials may be sent to o_jj@stu.edu.cn.
