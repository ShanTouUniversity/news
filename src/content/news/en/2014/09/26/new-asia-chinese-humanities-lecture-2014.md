The Chinese University of Hong Kong will hold an academic lecture marking the 65th anniversary of New Asia College on September 27, 2014. Sinologist Professor Yu Ying-shih will speak on “New Asia College and Chinese Humanities.” The lecture will be transmitted by video and livestreamed online for viewers at Shantou University and elsewhere.

| Language | Putonghua |
