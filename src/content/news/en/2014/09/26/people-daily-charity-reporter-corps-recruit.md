It is reported that the People's Daily Online Charity News Student Reporter Corps is recruiting new members at Shantou University. Founded jointly by the Charity Promotion Committee of China Charity Alliance's press volunteers and the website, together with universities across the country, the corps consists of college students who are passionate about charitable causes and strong in news writing and overall ability.

The recruitment at STU carries the theme "Gathering the Power of Youth," aiming to report on the university's charity activities and local civic developments in Shantou while offering students practical opportunities and a platform to showcase their talents.

**Member Benefits**

- An official press credential and formal registration as a volunteer on the website
- Training opportunities from journalists and editors at central-level media
- Bylined publication of news works on the website
- Winter and summer internship opportunities and job referrals, with top performers invited to intern at headquarters
- Access to major website events and an annual outstanding student journalist award, including a scholarship for winners

**Application Requirements**

| Requirement | Details |
| ----------- | ------- |
| Year | Sophomores or above, including postgraduates; all majors welcome |
| Qualities | Passion for charity and journalism, responsibility, communication skills |
| Skills | Basic writing skills or other special talents |
| Balance | Ability to manage academic studies alongside social practice |
| Preferred | Editing or reporting experience, or prior publications |

Applicants must submit an electronic application form and samples to dxsjzt2014@yeah.net by October 10, marking the subject line as "Student Reporter Corps Application." Text reporters may submit up to three news or literary works, while photojournalists may submit three to five images (each under 1 MB). Candidates who pass the initial screening will receive a written test notice by SMS, followed by unified written tests and interviews.
