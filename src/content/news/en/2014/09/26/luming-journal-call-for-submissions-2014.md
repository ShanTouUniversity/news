Luming, the journal of Shantou University’s College of Liberal Arts, has opened its fall call for submissions to all current undergraduates. The college said the initiative aims to encourage student writing and support the development of academic and professional skills.

Submissions are due by 11 p.m. on November 1, 2014, and review is scheduled for November 2–10. The notice also listed an information booth near campus dining halls on October 13. It did not provide complete submission specifications; personal student contact details in the notice are not reproduced here.
