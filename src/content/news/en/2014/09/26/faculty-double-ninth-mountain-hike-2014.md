Shantou University’s Trade Union will organize a Double Ninth Festival mountain hike for faculty and staff on September 30, 2014. Participants will gather outside the graduate residence at 3:45 p.m. and depart at 4:00 p.m. The route will pass through Gate 5 of the campus wall and Nongmin Road before reaching Cixin Pavilion, after which participants will return to the starting point to collect gifts and join a lucky draw.

## Activity Arrangements

The activity will offer three prize tiers: three first prizes of limited-edition basketballs, five second prizes of Zhang Jike table-tennis racket sets, and 10 third prizes of fitness sets. Dongfeng Yueda Kia provided the prizes, which will be distributed while supplies last. Gift coupons, drinking water, and medical services from the University Hospital will also be available.

The Trade Union advised participants to consider their health before joining, wear suitable hiking clothes and athletic shoes, and take precautions against sun, mosquitoes, and other safety risks. Smoking and open flames are prohibited, and participants should protect the environment.
