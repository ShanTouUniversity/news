The Russian National Ballet will perform The Sleeping Beauty at 8:00 p.m. on October 12, 2014, at the auditorium of the Lin Bai Xin International Convention and Exhibition Center in Shantou.

## Performance and Ticket Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Performance time | 8:00 p.m., October 12, 2014 |
| Venue | Auditorium, Lin Bai Xin International Convention and Exhibition Center |
| Ticket prices | RMB 100, 180, 280, 380, 480, 680, and VIP 880 |
| Ticket sales | 9:00–18:00 on October 9 |
| Sales location | In front of the Trade Union office on the first floor |
| Campus discount | STU faculty, staff, and students receive 15% off with a campus card |

Purchases of RMB 280 or more include a Tchaikovsky music CD while supplies last. The first 10 buyers will also receive a RMB 30 voucher.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union)
