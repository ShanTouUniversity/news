China Telecom Shantou will implement its 2014 “FlyYoung Youth, Wings for Dreams” university student aid program at Shantou University. The program supports students from financially disadvantaged backgrounds and participants in the FlyYoung work-study program.

The program has places for 110 financially disadvantaged freshmen entering in 2014 and 20 students participating in the FlyYoung work-study program. Each aid package includes a phone valued at 599 yuan and a mobile SIM card with phone credit. Activating the card requires a 100-yuan advance payment, with the included credit released according to the program terms.

Financially disadvantaged freshmen must apply through their school or college youth league by 5 p.m. on September 11. After review and public notice, schools and colleges must submit the materials to the Student Financial Aid Management Center by 5 p.m. on September 15. FlyYoung participants should submit the required application form; full procedures and consultation arrangements are in the original STU OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
