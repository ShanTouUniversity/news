Shantou University's Network Center has begun the annual renewal process for staff and graduate student internet accounts. The renewal period covers internet plans from October 2014 through September 2015.

University units and schools must submit stamped renewal lists to the All-in-One Card Service Window on the first floor of the Network and Information Center by September 23. One original and one copy are required. Plan types and procedures are provided in the attachments to the original STU OA notice.

For inquiries, contact 86503400-2 or email cxfang@stu.edu.cn.
