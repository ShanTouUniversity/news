Shantou University has published a notice on additional items for the renovation of Levels 3 and 4 of Building G. The project began on July 1, 2014. Adjustments to door-frame thickness caused by uneven original walls, together with requests for anti-static dustproof coatings and epoxy self-leveling flooring, have led to an initial estimated increase of about 22,000 yuan. The cumulative additional cost remains below the 10 percent limit set by the university.

Comments may be submitted in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 6.
