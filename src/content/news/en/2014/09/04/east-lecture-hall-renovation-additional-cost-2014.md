Shantou University has published its third notice on additional items for the renovation of the East Lecture Hall. The project began on July 1, 2014. Additional drainage pipes were needed to accommodate air-conditioner installation, while design revisions added concealed light tubes and wiring. The initial estimate for the additional work is about 39,000 yuan, with the cumulative additional cost below the university's 10 percent limit.

Comments may be submitted in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 6.
