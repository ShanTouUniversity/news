Shantou University's Office of Resource Management is collecting information on the secondary-school entrance examinations of employees' children for the 2014 education advancement award and aid program. In-service employees whose children study and took the examination in urban Shantou may submit the original and a copy of the child's admission ticket, admission notice and score report.

The materials should include the employee's name, unit and contact details and must be delivered to the General Office of the Office of Resource Management by September 25. For inquiries, call 86502827.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management)
