Shantou University's 189th Engineering Research Forum will take place at 3 p.m. on Friday, September 5, 2014, in the Electronic Conference Room of the School of Engineering. He Lanlan, a PhD researcher from the University of Hong Kong, will present “Data detection for cooperative vehicular communication systems with unknown channels.”

The forum is intended for graduate students, doctoral students and relevant faculty in the School of Engineering's Department of Electronics. Other interested members of the university community are also welcome.
