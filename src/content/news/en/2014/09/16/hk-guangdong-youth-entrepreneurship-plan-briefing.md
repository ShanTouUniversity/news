The third Hong Kong-Guangdong Youth Entrepreneurship Plan briefing will be held at Shantou University on September 18, 2014. The plan is jointly organized by the Hong Kong Cyberport Enterprise Development Centre and the Guangdong Software Industry Association to train young people in business skills, promote cooperation between Hong Kong and Guangdong youth, and assist entrepreneurship.

## Event Details

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 2:30–4:30 p.m., September 18 |
| Venue | E204 |
| Speakers | Experts from Hong Kong Cyberport Management Company and the Guangdong Software Industry Association |

Supported by the Cyberport Creative Micro Fund (CCMF), the plan runs for six months, offers up to HKD 100,000 in cash funding and supports prototype development. The School of Engineering welcomed students from all schools to attend.
