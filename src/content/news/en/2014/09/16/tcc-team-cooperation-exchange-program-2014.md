It is reported that Shantou University’s Graduate School launched the second TCC Team Cooperation Exchange Program on the afternoon of September 16, 2014. The program will run through the afternoon of September 19 and is intended for graduate freshmen on the main campus, excluding graduate students from the Medical College.

Participants should wear sportswear and sports shoes and bring their own water bottles. They will meet at the library entrance, with group assignments announced on site. Participants were also advised to monitor the weather and prepare accordingly.
