It is reported that the computer rooms in Shantou University’s E Building will host sessions of the National Computer Rank Examination from 9:00 a.m. to 1:20 p.m. on September 20, 2014. To ensure orderly examinations, the rooms will be closed during the examination period and will reopen by 2:30 p.m.

Faculty and students are advised to adjust their plans for using the computer rooms and share the schedule with others.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
