It is reported that Shantou University will hold its 2014 opening ceremony at 9:00 a.m. on Friday, September 19, in the Auditorium. Colleges will organize the 2014 freshmen to enter in advance, from 8:25 to 8:50 a.m.

## Ceremony Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 9:00 a.m., September 19, 2014 |
| Venue | Auditorium |
| Entry | 2014 freshmen enter from 8:25 a.m. and finish by 8:50 a.m. |

College and administrative leaders seated on the platform should gather in the south VIP lounge of the Auditorium by 8:50 a.m. Attendees should dress appropriately and follow venue procedures. Phones should be set to silent or vibrate, and anyone taking photographs should remain in place.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office and Student Affairs Office)
