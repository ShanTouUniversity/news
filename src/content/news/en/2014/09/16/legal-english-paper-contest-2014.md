It is reported that the Career Development Center of Shantou University’s Law School will hold a legal English paper competition for all Law School undergraduates. The competition aims to foster an academic atmosphere and improve students’ legal English writing skills.

Submissions will be accepted from September 15 through October 30, 2014. Details on submission, paper requirements and awards are available in the original OA notice.
