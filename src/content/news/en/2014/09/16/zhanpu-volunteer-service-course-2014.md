It is reported that Zhicheng College at Shantou University opened registration on September 16, 2014, for the second Zhanpu volunteer service course. The course supports a training program jointly implemented by the Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Li Ka Shing Foundation. Volunteers will assist with organization, reception, conference services, interviews, photography and project summaries, and may receive one academic credit after completing the assessment.

## Course Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | Students from across the university |
| Places | 20, selected through interviews |
| Course period | October 12–December 3, 2014 |
| Intensive training | October 12–13 |
| Registration deadline | 5:00 p.m., September 25 |

The volunteer work will support female village officials and grassroots civil affairs officials participating in the Zhanpu training program. Applicants should follow the public service course requirements, work well with others and be willing to participate in volunteer work.
