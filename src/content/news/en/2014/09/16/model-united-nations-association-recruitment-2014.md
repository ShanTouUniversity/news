It is reported that Shantou University’s Model United Nations Association will recruit new members from across the university on September 17 and 18, 2014, regardless of year or major. The association uses simulated United Nations conferences to engage students in debate and negotiation on international issues.

## Recruitment Schedule

| Date | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 17 | 9:00 a.m.–noon; 2:30–5:30 p.m. | Room 213, Law School |
| September 18 | 2:30–5:30 p.m. | Room 213, Law School |

Founded in 2013, the association has participated in the China Model United Nations Conference, the Macao–Fucheng Model United Nations Conference and the Cross-Strait Model United Nations Conference. On-site registration is available; personal contact details from the original notice are not reproduced here.
