It is reported that Shantou University Library has issued its 12th Chaoshan Studies new-book recommendation for *External Networks and Innovation of Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises*. The book examines businesses in cluster networks in the Chaoshan region and systematically analyzes how external networks affect enterprise performance and innovation.

## Overview

The nine-chapter book uses hierarchical regression and path analysis to study how external networks influence innovation performance through absorptive capacity, and tests the findings through case studies. It also draws on a survey of 224 small and medium-sized enterprises in networked clusters in eastern Guangdong to examine how cluster relationships can strengthen technological innovation.

The authors are Zheng Muqiang, associate professor and master’s supervisor at Shantou University Business School, and Xu Zongling, assistant to the president, director of the Shantou University Guangdong-Taiwan Enterprise Cooperation Research Institute, and professor at the Business School. Their teaching and research covers enterprise clusters, business networks and applied economics.
