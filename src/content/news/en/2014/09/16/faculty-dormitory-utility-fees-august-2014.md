It is reported that Shantou University’s Property Management Office announced the August 2014 utility fees for faculty dormitories. The latest meter readings were taken on September 1 and 2, following the previous reading on August 1. Faculty members may log in to the university’s utility management system to check their fees.

Questions or objections may be raised at the Water and Electricity Center from September 16 to 18. The inquiry telephone number is 2489. Fees without objections by the deadline will be deducted by the Finance Office from September salary accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Property Management Office)
