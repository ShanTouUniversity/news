Shantou University’s Office of Resource Management will collect and process hazardous waste from university units. The collection covers toner, cartridges, ink cartridges, used batteries, correction fluid, electronic components, empty chemical containers, and expired, leftover or waste laboratory chemicals.

Units must complete the Shantou University Hazardous Waste Collection and Transfer Form, print two copies and arrange the handover. Each type of waste liquid must be placed in a separate sealed bottle with a label identifying the submitting unit, chemical name or composition, weight and collection date, then packed securely in a carton. Laboratory waste must not contain highly toxic or explosive materials, radioactive substances, polychlorinated biphenyls or cyanides.

## Collection Arrangements

Collection will take place on September 23, 2014, from 8:30 to 11:00 a.m. and 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. at Huanrong Company’s waste collection room, located at the gatehouse of the former Mental Health Center used by the Office of Resource Management.

For later office-waste collection, contact Wu Peiliang of Huanrong Company at 13929682308 or 622308. The Logistics Office of Resource Management can be reached at 8650 3385.
