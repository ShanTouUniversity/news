Shantou University forwarded a notice from the Education Discipline Inspection Commission of Guangdong and is organizing an integrity reading essay contest for faculty, staff and students as part of the fourth Provincial Integrity Reading Month.

Entries may address integrity reading, anti-corruption efforts, correcting the “four undesirable work styles,” and fostering and practicing core socialist values. Topics and formats are open, with a recommended length of about 3,000 Chinese characters. Entries must be original, unpublished in print or online, submitted only once and free of plagiarism.

The deadline is October 14, 2014. Participants should email electronic entries to o_jj@stu.edu.cn and submit a printed copy to the Discipline Inspection Office, Room 422, New Administration Center. The email subject should read “Integrity Book Review Essay.” Inquiries: 86502346.
