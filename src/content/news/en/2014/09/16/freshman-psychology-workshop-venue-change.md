It is reported that Shantou University Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 16, 2014, regarding a venue change for the psychology adjustment workshop for the 2014 freshmen. The workshop, scheduled for 8:30 a.m. on September 17, will move from the library lecture hall to Room 101 of the E Building stepped lecture hall (E 101) because of venue arrangements.

The university asked the 2014 freshmen to note the new venue and attend on time.
