It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design at Shantou University will host a lecture at 7:30 p.m. on Thursday, September 18, 2014. Professor Lü Jingren will speak on “Book Play: Reading and Being Read.”

The lecture will take place in the first-floor stepped lecture hall of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. Faculty and students from across the university are welcome.
