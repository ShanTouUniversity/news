The Business School of Shantou University is selecting students for CICC 2014, the Citi International Case Competition. The competition will be held at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology from October 26 to 31, 2014, and focuses on strategic crises and management challenges facing international business leaders while offering participants opportunities for cross-cultural exchange and case practice.

## Eligibility

Business School undergraduates from the 2011 and 2012 cohorts may apply. Students who have completed BUS3186, Business Operations Case Analysis, and those with international case competition experience are preferred.

Applicants must email a résumé to King Sir at kingkng@stu.edu.cn by September 20. Competition details are available at cicc.ust.hk.
