It is reported that the Law School issued a notice on September 16, 2014, to hold the 7th Legal English Speech Contest to improve oral English and foster an English learning atmosphere. All undergraduates and postgraduates of the Law School are welcome to participate.

## Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Express Your Legal Views in English |
| Format | Interview-based selection; top 10 scorers in the preliminary advance to the final |
| Preliminary | 15:00–17:30, October 22, 2014 |
| Final | 15:00–17:00, October 29, 2014 |
| Venue (preliminary) | Room 206, Law School (Wenbei) |
| Organizer | Career Development Center, Law School |

## Requirements

Contestants should choose a topic related to current focal issues and deliver a speech by reading or recitation. Judging will be based on pronunciation, expression and language organization, with works required to be 200–500 words. Personal information fields have been withheld for privacy; the application form and contacts are available in the OA attachment. Final requirements will be announced separately.
