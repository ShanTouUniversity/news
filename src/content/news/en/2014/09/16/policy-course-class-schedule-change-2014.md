It is reported that Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office adjusted the schedules for Classes 71926 and 71927 of the Social Sciences Department’s Policy Education course during Week 4.

## Revised Schedule

| Class | Original time | Make-up time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 71926 | Periods 1–3, Friday, September 19 | Periods 1–3, Thursday, September 18 | E Building stepped lecture hall 203 |
| 71927 | Periods 6–8, Friday, September 19 | Periods A–C, Thursday, September 18 | E Building stepped lecture hall 203 |

The relevant colleges and students should attend according to the revised schedule.
