It is reported that in appreciation of teachers, the Shantou Pangda FAW-Volkswagen dealership, in partnership with the Shantou University Labor Union, will launch a Teachers Day promotion themed "Autumn Gifts: Gratitude to Teachers" for STU faculty from September 6 to 10, 2014. Teachers holding a valid STU staff ID card can enjoy exclusive benefits when purchasing a vehicle at the showroom opposite Longteng Xiyuan on Taishan Middle Road, Longhu District.

## Five Layers of Benefits

The highlight of the campaign was "a one-person group deal" — any teacher who successfully purchased a car with their staff ID could secure group-pricing terms, with an additional RMB 1,000 discount off the showroom's lowest transaction price across all models. Five accompanying gifts are also on offer:

| Benefit | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Test-drive gift | A complimentary gift for visiting and taking a test drive |
| Purchase gift | A package worth RMB 8,800 (original sun film, chassis paint, original floor mats and dash cam), plus an RMB 880 home vehicle diagnostic device and an RMB 680 annual membership card with year-round maintenance discounts |
| Tribute gift | 10 limited-edition models with up to RMB 20,000 off; buyers will also receive a RMB 3,800 extended warranty signed by the general manager and 10 years of free engine oil |
| Trade-in gift | Up to RMB 3,000 exclusive trade-in cash toward a new car purchase |
| Care service | Door-to-door pickup upon phone call, with purchase procedures handled at home |

## Eligibility

According to the promotion plan, the offers apply not only to STU faculty themselves but also to customers referred by them: teachers need to present their own staff ID cards, while referred customers must provide a copy of the referring teacher's staff ID card.

For inquiries during the event period, a hotline is available at 0754-86712888.
