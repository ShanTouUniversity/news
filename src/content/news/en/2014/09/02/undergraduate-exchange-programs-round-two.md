Shantou University will accept applications from September 20 to 30, 2014, for the second round of its national undergraduate exchange programs. Eight funded places remain across partner programs in Portugal, France, New Zealand and Ireland.

The five programs are with the Catholic University of Portugal, the European School of Management in Paris, Victoria University of Wellington, the University of Canterbury and Dublin City University. They offer one, two, one, two and two places, respectively. Eligible fields include finance, accounting, marketing, business administration, international economics and trade, English, law, journalism and communication, and civil engineering.

Applicants must have strong academic records, good health and an average score of at least 85 out of 100. They must also meet the stated IELTS 6.5 requirement and obtain an invitation or admission notice from an overseas institution. Applicants should apply through their schools first; nominees then submit materials through the China Scholarship Council's online system. School international-program coordinators and Mr. Huang at the Office of International Affairs can provide guidance at 86502131.

Application documents and program details are available in the original STU OA notice and its attachments.
