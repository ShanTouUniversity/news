Zhicheng College at Shantou University will hold a second pre-service training session for its seventh cohort of student mentors on September 5, 2014. The session begins at 9 a.m. in Lecture Room J-354 and is intended for all mentors.

The college said the training is intended to strengthen mentors' capabilities and prepare them to support students. The course schedule is available in the attachment to the original STU OA notice.
