Shantou University's Office of Development Planning made the university's 2014 self-assessment materials for the Innovation and Strengthening Project available for review from September 2 to 3, 2014. Staff may view the materials in Room 305 of the New Administration Center and submit comments to the office.

The office can be reached at 0754-86503298 or o_211@stu.edu.cn.
