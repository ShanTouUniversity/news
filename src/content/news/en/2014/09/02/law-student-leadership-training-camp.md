Shantou University's Law School will hold the first phase of its student leadership training camp on September 3–4, 2014, in Room 213 of the Law School building. A date for the second phase has not been set.

The program is intended for members of the Law School Student Union, Law Society, Youth Volunteers Association and debate team, as well as volunteer mentors for the 2014 freshmen. The school said the camp aims to develop student leaders' capabilities, teamwork and commitment to service. The detailed course schedule is available in the attachment to the original STU OA notice.
