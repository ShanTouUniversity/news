Shantou University Library has featured *Remembering Lin Ruo* in its series on new works related to Chaoshan. The collection, about 300,000 Chinese characters long, brings together 42 commemorative essays in four sections: a preface, personal recollections, tributes and family history. The essays look at Lin Ruo's life and character from different perspectives.

Born in Chao'an, Guangdong, in 1924, Lin held positions including secretary of the Dongguan County Committee of the Communist Party of China, secretary of the Zhanjiang Prefectural Committee, Guangdong provincial party secretary and chair of the Guangdong Provincial People's Congress Standing Committee. The library's introduction names Zhang Yangding and Cai Long as editors and outlines Zhang's research interests and selected publications.
