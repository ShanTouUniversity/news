The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University will hold a presentation of university-level student organizations for all freshmen from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. on September 9 at the Science Lecture Hall.

The event is intended to introduce freshmen to student organizations and campus culture, and freshmen were invited to attend and learn more about participation opportunities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
