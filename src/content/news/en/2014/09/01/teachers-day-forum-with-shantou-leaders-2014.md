Shantou municipal leaders are scheduled to hold a Teachers’ Day forum at 9 a.m. on September 3 in Meeting Room 3 of Shantou University's Academic Conference Center. Each relevant university unit is to send one representative, who should arrive by 8:50 a.m.

The university will provide transportation, departing at 8:45 a.m. from two pickup points: the small bamboo grove at the Administrative Center and the intersection behind the College of Science. Attendees were asked not to drive themselves.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and University Administration Office)
