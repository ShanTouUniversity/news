It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University held a leadership meeting on August 27 and decided to adjust the division of work among members of the school's leadership team. The changes were announced to all faculty and students in an official document (Shanda Shangfa [2014] No. 08) dated September 1.

## Adjusted Division of Responsibilities

| Name | Position | Scope of responsibilities |
| ---- | -------- | ------------------------- |
| Lyu Yuan | Dean | Overall responsibility for all work of the school; direct oversight of discipline development, the MBA Center, administration, personnel, research and finance |
| Chen Yinglin | Party Committee Secretary (Chief Supervisor of Student Affairs) | Student ideological and political education, admissions and employment, alumni management and Party building, in accordance with university regulations |
| Song Xiaobao | Vice Dean | Teaching implementation and management for undergraduates, postgraduates, MBA and postgraduate course programs; student internships; laboratory and training center; website development |
| Bu Xiangzhi | Vice Dean | Assisting the dean with international accreditation, recruitment of international faculty and student participation in international competitions; external exchange and cooperation, academic lectures, and English website content management |

Under the new arrangement, each leader's portfolio is clearly delineated: Dean Lyu Yuan coordinates the overall operation of the school while directly heading core areas such as discipline development and the MBA Center; Secretary Chen Yinglin oversees student affairs including ideological education and employment; Vice Dean Song Xiaobao focuses on teaching operations and practice platforms; and Vice Dean Bu Xiangzhi leads the school's internationalization efforts.

The document was addressed to the University Party and Administration Office, with copies sent to the Personnel Office, Academic Affairs Office, Research Office, Finance Office and Graduate School.
