Shantou University's Infrastructure Office announced on September 1 that it proposed handling the College of Art and Design classroom renovation as a special project through a price-comparison tender. The work must be completed by September 22 for the fall semester, and the university said the standard open-tender process could not meet the teaching schedule.

The proposed approach was approved by email on August 25 by the three deputy heads of the university's tendering leadership group. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 3.
