Shantou University's Business School announced the establishment of a Professional Development Committee in a document issued on September 1. The decision was made by the school's affairs committee on August 27. The new committee will advise on academic program development and planning and comprises industry experts, corporate executives and alumni with frontline management experience.

Members include Chen Lewu, chairman and general manager of Guangdong Mangshi Power Technology; Chen Enmin, board secretary and deputy general manager of Guangdong Orient Zirconic; alumnus Ye Qichang, vice president of Morgan Stanley Capital International (Hong Kong); Zhang Nan, deputy director of the Shantou Municipal Financial Affairs Bureau; alumnus Wu Weizhong, regional manager at Guangzhou Watsons; Paul Theil, chairman of Shenzhen Zhong'an Credit; and Moshe Maron, a professor at the Technion and its resident representative at STU.
