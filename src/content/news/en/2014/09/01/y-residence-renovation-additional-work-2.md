Shantou University's Infrastructure Office posted a second notice on September 1 about proposed additions to the first-phase renovation of Y Residence. The work is estimated to add about RMB 18,000 and includes installing a drainpipe and floor drain in a ground-floor pipe shaft, as well as bathroom mirror lights and concealed switches suggested by students.

The renovation began on July 1, 2014. The university said the cumulative added cost remains within 10 percent of the project cost. Written objections could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 3.
