Shantou University will hold its Tianyi FeiYoung campus singing contest from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on September 12 on the third floor of the university labor union building. The selection is open to all students, including graduate students, with registration running from September 1 to 11.

Contestants may choose a song and are encouraged to sing without accompaniment. Solo, duet and group performances are allowed, and there is no entry fee. Those who advance will represent STU in the Guangdong regional selection; outstanding performers may be recommended for the national finals. Further information is available through the STU Student Associations' WeChat account. The OA notice has the registration method and full requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ Registration instructions and contest attachments are available on the OA system.
