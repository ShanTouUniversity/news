Shantou University's Party Organization and United Front Work Department issued instructions in September 2014 for faculty and student Party members to subscribe to the Communist Party member WeChat and Yixin platforms. The initiative followed requirements from central, provincial and municipal Party organization departments. University units were asked to introduce the platforms and explain how to use them to local Party organizations and members.

The platforms feature sections on Party affairs, new perspectives, exemplary figures and other topics, with new content published each working day. Units must compile subscription totals by the 15th of each month and submit them to the university department, which will report them to the municipal-level work committee's organization department.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ Subscription instructions and the reporting form are available in the OA attachments.
