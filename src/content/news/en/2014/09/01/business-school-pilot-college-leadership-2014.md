It is reported that the Business School issued Document No. 09 [2014] on September 1, 2014, to establish a leadership group for the autonomous Pilot College initiative.

Following deliberation by the Business School Administrative Committee on August 27, 2014, the group was formed to advance the Guangdong Pilot College program. Members are as follows:

| Role | Members |
| --- | --- |
| Head | Lyu Yuan |
| Members (ordered by stroke count) | Bu Xiangzhi, Wang * (one character garbled in the original), Song Xiaobao, Chen Yinglin, Ouyang Feng |
| Secretary | Huang Xianglong |
