It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University issued Document No. Shan Da Shang Fa [2014] 11, announcing an adjustment to the composition of its Teaching Quality Assurance Committee following organizational and personnel changes. The adjustment was considered at the Business School Affairs Committee meeting on August 27, 2014.

## New Committee Composition

| Position | Name(s) |
| --- | --- |
| Adviser | Xu Erming |
| Chair | Yao Mingan |
| Members (ordered by stroke count of surname) | Liu Yu, Chi Qishui, Zhang Ling, Zeng Fanqi, Zeng Xianyang |
| Secretary | Xu Xiaojing |

The current committee was established in March 2014 and will serve a three-year term. The document was copied to the Academic Affairs Office.
