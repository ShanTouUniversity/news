Shantou University's labor union will hold a Teachers' Day forum for full-time main-campus employees with at least 30 years of service at 10 a.m. on September 10 in Room 330 of the Administrative Building. The program also includes a group photo in front of the Old Administrative Building and lunch at the Hongtai staff dining hall.

Alongside the forum, the union is inviting employees to submit old and recent photographs related to STU that show changes over time, accompanied by captions or stories. At least two photos are required, with a resolution of at least 2 MB. Submissions are due by September 4 at the union's official email address, o_xgh@stu.edu.cn. Photos may be used in the forum presentation and university publications.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
