It is reported that the Business School issued Document No. 06 [2014] on September 1, 2014, to adjust the heads of its Accounting and Finance programs following deliberation by the college’s administrative committee on August 27 due to personnel changes.

| Position | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Head of Accounting | Long Yue’e appointed; Song Xiaobao steps down |
| Acting Head of Finance | Chen Siyan appointed |
