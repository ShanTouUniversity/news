It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University forwarded a notice on September 1, 2014, from the Communist Youth League of Shantou regarding the city-wide “Shantou Top Ten Touching Stories” campaign.

In line with directives from central and provincial civilization offices and the city’s 2014 spiritual civilization plan, the Municipal Spiritual Civilization Commission and the Publicity Department will jointly organize the selection starting in August. Faculty and students wishing to participate should submit the recommendation form electronically to the Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by October 15, 2014.

The form and attachments are available in the OA system.
