Shantou University published its Management Measures for Recommending Undergraduates for Master’s Degree Study Without the Entrance Examination. The measures were reviewed by the University Teaching Committee on August 27 and discussed and approved at the 11th university leadership meeting on September 2 before being issued for implementation.

The measures set out the organization and implementation requirements for recommending undergraduates to pursue master’s degrees without the national entrance examination. The complete text was provided in the university’s attachment.
