Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office published a notice on projected additional costs for the renovation of the university’s digital multifunctional studio. The project began on May 14, 2014. Design changes required a silent ceiling-mounted air conditioner, while a revised outdoor power-cable route added cable length, steel cable trays and conduit. The estimated additional cost was approximately RMB 70,000.

The office said the cumulative additional cost remained below the 10 percent ceiling set by the university. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 8.
