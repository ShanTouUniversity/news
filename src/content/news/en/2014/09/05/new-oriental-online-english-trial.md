Shantou University Library opened trial access to New Oriental Online’s College English Test platform and micro-courses. The trial was scheduled to run through December 4, 2014.

Users could access the test platform and micro-courses through the corresponding database pages on the Library website. First-time individual users of the micro-course service had to register, while users with an existing New Oriental multimedia learning account could log in directly without an IP restriction.

The Library’s subject services team invited feedback on the trial at +86 754 8650 3247 or lqxie@stu.edu.cn.
