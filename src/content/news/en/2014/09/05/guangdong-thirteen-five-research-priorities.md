Shantou University’s Research Office circulated a call for proposals on major development directions in Guangdong’s priority areas for basic research during the 13th Five-Year Plan. The provincial science and technology authorities asked contributors to formulate directions around the designated priority topics and complete the required proposal form.

The areas included biodiversity conservation, stem cells and regenerative medicine, innovative medicines, diseases characteristic of Guangdong, ecological protection, South China Sea resources, intelligent and low-carbon engineering, energy, energy conservation and emissions reduction, cloud computing and big data, green materials, major scientific facilities, physics, and resource allocation and macro-management innovation during Guangdong’s reform process.

Interested faculty members were asked to email their proposal forms to yxhu@stu.edu.cn by September 14. The attachment contained the required format and submission details.
