Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Office organized the collection of materials for 2014 freshman Party members to support the transfer of organizational relations and verification of Party-member status.

Party general branches of the schools were asked to collect valid letters of introduction for Party-member organizational relations, together with the applicants’ application forms for Party membership and other archival materials. The materials were to be checked and submitted with the freshman Party-member statistical form to the office in Room 315 of the New Administration Center by September 19.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office)
