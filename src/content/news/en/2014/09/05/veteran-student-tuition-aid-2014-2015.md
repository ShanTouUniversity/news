Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has arranged tuition aid applications for the 2014–15 academic year. The aid is for veterans entering higher education in that academic year; it does not cover current students who return to study after completing compulsory military service. Eligible students who enrolled in 2012–13 or 2013–14 but missed the earlier reporting process may also apply retroactively under the relevant rules.

Beginning this year, eligible full-time undergraduates may apply for up to 8,000 yuan per year, or the actual tuition amount when it is lower. Students who have received tuition compensation or loan repayment assistance under the policy for students serving compulsory military service cannot receive this aid again. The aid is provided by waiving tuition first and reimbursing the university after approval.

Schools and colleges must organize applications and submit preliminary lists, application forms and supporting materials to the Student Financial Aid Management Center by October 13. Paper materials should be delivered to Room 106 of the Administration Center. For inquiries, call 86502249; electronic materials may be sent to zjliu@stu.edu.cn.
