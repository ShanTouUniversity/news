Shantou University’s General Office published procedures for using the university seal on certificates for non-degree education, including short-term training, and for archiving the related records.

Before requesting a seal, the host unit must email a summary of completing participants together with the host unit and training dates, then take an approved Seal Use Form and the certificates to Room 237 of the Administration Center for verification and stamping. Within one month after the training ends, the relevant records must be transferred to the unit’s part-time archivist.

Archive materials include participant information, completion lists, training content and other materials with preservation value. Both electronic and paper versions are required, and paper materials must bear the unit’s official seal. The listed contacts included the Secretarial Section at jyk@stu.edu.cn, the Comprehensive Archives at o_das@stu.edu.cn and the Alumni Office at o_xyh@stu.edu.cn.
