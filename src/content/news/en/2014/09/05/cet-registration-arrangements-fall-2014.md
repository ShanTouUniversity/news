Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced registration arrangements for the fall 2014 College English Test (CET). The examinations were scheduled for December 20, with CET-4 from 9:00 to 11:25 and CET-6 from 15:00 to 17:25.

Undergraduate CET-4 applicants were required to meet the applicable English placement requirements; newly enrolled 2014 undergraduates were excluded from this round. Arts undergraduates needed to have completed ELC EA3 or passed the English application ability examination. CET-6 applicants, including eligible undergraduates and graduate students, had to meet requirements based on prior CET or English major test results and observe the limits on repeat registration.

Undergraduates were to register online through the credit-based education website from September 5 to 21. The registration fee was RMB 36. The Finance Office, through the Network Center, would deduct the fee from campus card accounts on September 22; an unsuccessful deduction would result in failed registration. Candidates were required to bring their identity card, student card and admission card to the examination.
