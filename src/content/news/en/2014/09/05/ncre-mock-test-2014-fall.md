It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on September 5, 2014, regarding a mock test for the fall 2014 National Computer Rank Examination (NCRE).

The NCRE will be held in batches on September 20, 2014. To test the exam software and system environment and ensure smooth operation, the university will hold a mock test from 16:00 to 17:00 on September 10 in Room E102. The test will be arranged on a first-come, first-served basis, and registered candidates are asked to attend as scheduled.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | Fall 2014 NCRE, held in batches on September 20 |
| Mock test time | 16:00–17:00, September 10, 2014 |
| Mock test venue | Room E102 |
| Arrangement | First-come, first-served |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Students are asked to inform each other.
