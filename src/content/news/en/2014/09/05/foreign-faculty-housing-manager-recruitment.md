Shantou University’s Human Resources Office announced an open recruitment for one manager of foreign faculty housing in the Office of Resource Management. Applicants were expected to hold at least a college diploma, communicate effectively in spoken English and use computers proficiently. Customer-service experience was preferred, and applicants had to comply with China’s family-planning policy.

The position covered check-in and check-out procedures, furniture and appliance inventories, repair services, coordination of holiday maintenance, public-facility repairs, emergency assistance and mediation of resident disputes. It was an auxiliary contract position; the employment relationship would not be transferred to Shantou University, and compensation would follow university standards.

Applicants had to submit a résumé and copies of their identity card, family-planning certificate, academic credentials and relevant certificates to the Office of Resource Management by September 20. Materials could be sent by email or sealed letter. Shortlisted applicants would be contacted for an interview and asked to present originals for verification. Inquiries could be sent to o_sdk@stu.edu.cn or +86 754 8290 2491. The mailing address was the Office of Resource Management, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063.
