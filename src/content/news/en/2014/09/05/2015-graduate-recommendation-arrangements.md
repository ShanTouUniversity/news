Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced arrangements for recommending outstanding graduating undergraduates for postgraduate study in 2015 without the national entrance examination. Each school was asked to publish detailed rules based on the university’s management measures and establish a recommendation group led by its vice dean in charge of teaching.

The process was to follow principles of openness, fairness and impartiality, with attention to students’ abilities, consistent performance, research potential and professional aptitude. Students were required to submit paper and electronic applications to their schools by September 15. Schools had to complete recommendations by September 19 and deliver applications, comprehensive scoring forms and implementation rules to the Academic Affairs Office by 5 p.m. that day, while sending electronic files to xhx@stu.edu.cn.

The university noted that, starting in 2014, the Ministry of Education would no longer separate academic and professional degree quotas when allocating recommendation places, and that the recommendation process had to finish by September 25 each year.
