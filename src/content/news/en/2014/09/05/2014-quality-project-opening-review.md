Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office launched opening reviews and expert evaluations for the university’s 2014 provincial undergraduate education quality and teaching reform projects. The work was scheduled for the beginning of the fall 2014 semester, with specific times and venues to be announced. The office would invite internal and external experts to evaluate demonstration projects and review opening reports in writing for teaching-reform projects.

The process covered 19 quality-engineering projects for evaluation and 14 higher-education teaching-reform projects for opening review. Evaluation projects were required to submit an evaluation report and give a 10-minute presentation before answering questions and receiving expert feedback. Opening-review projects were required to submit an opening report for written review.

Each project had to submit five paper copies to the Teaching Reform and Assessment Section of the Academic Affairs Office by September 19 and email an electronic copy to qhding@stu.edu.cn. The working contact was Ding Qianhang at 8650 2736.
