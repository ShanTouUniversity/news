It is reported that the 2014 Shantou Youth Swimming Championships were held at the Shantou Diving Hall from September 20 to 21. Two sixth-grade students from the Shantou University Affiliated Primary School earned multiple places in the boys' and girls' Grade B events.

Li JM placed first in the boys' Grade B 100-meter backstroke, second in the 100-meter freestyle and third in the 50-meter freestyle. Qiu H placed first in the girls' Grade B 50-meter breaststroke, second in the 100-meter breaststroke and fourth in the 50-meter butterfly.
