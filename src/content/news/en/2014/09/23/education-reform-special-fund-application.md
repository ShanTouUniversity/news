Applications have opened for Guangdong special-fund projects on comprehensive education system reform. Shantou University's Office of Development Planning and Office of Research have published the application arrangements for relevant university units and eligible teaching and research staff.

The call covers three categories: provincial comprehensive education reform pilot projects for relevant university units; provincial education reform pilot projects limited to the Medical College; and key provincial education reform research projects for in-service teaching and research staff who meet the provincial requirements and hold senior professional titles.

Applicants must prepare materials according to the relevant guidelines and send electronic copies to the designated contacts for a formal review by October 10. Applicants passing the review must then submit 14 paper copies as required. Contact details and application guidelines are provided in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Development Planning and Office of Research)
> ⚠️ Application guidelines and attachments are available on the OA system.
