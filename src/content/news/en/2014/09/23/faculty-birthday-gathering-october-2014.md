It is reported that, with support from the Li Ka-shing Foundation, the Shantou University Union will hold a birthday gathering on October 12 for faculty and staff whose birthdays fall from August through October, along with their families.

The gathering will take place from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Zhuangshi Xiangji's Guorui Branch. Registration is available through the union's booking system from 8 a.m. on September 24 to 3 p.m. on September 30. Payment will be processed from October 8 to 9, and tickets may be collected from 9:30 a.m. to noon on October 11. No university transportation will be provided.

The union notes that tickets cannot be refunded after registration and payment. Family members must present tickets, and children must be accompanied by at least one parent. The activity plan and booking instructions are available in the OA attachment.
