It is reported that the English Language Center will hold its first IELTS preparation workshop, “Getting to Know IELTS,” on October 8. The session will introduce the basics of the IELTS examination.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 4:15–5:15 p.m., October 8, 2014 |
| Venue | CILL |
| Content | Introduction to IELTS |
| Registration | Reserve a place through Eventbrite at https://fa2014ieltsintro.eventbrite.com |

Places are limited. The English Language Center advises students who cannot confirm their attendance not to register; those who register are expected to attend.
