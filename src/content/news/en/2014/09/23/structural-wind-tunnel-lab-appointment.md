It is reported that a September 16 meeting of the Shantou University leadership decided to appoint Zhao Xiaohua director of the Guangdong University Key Laboratory of Structures and Wind Tunnel. Xiong Guangjing is no longer the director of the laboratory.

The university announced the appointment on September 23, 2014.
