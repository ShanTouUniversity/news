The Shantou University Television Station is recruiting students from all years and majors for roles including copywriting and planning, on-camera hosting and reporting, and camera reporting. The station has produced a range of programs and provides students with opportunities for media practice and internships.

Copywriting applicants should be able to identify story topics and plan and promote content. On-camera applicants should have standard Mandarin, communication skills, and a news perspective. Camera reporters should be interested in photography and videography and be ready to participate in shoots and team projects.

Applications are open from September 22 to 26. Students may complete the application form and email it to the station's official mailbox or apply in person. Interview times and locations will be announced through the station's official Weibo account and WeChat subscription account after applications are reviewed. The application form is available in the original OA notice.
