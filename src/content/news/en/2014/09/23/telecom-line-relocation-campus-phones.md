China Telecom will cut over and relocate underground telecommunications lines near the former campus gate for the new Medical College project. Work is scheduled from 7 p.m. on September 24 to 6 a.m. on September 25, and again from 7 p.m. on September 25 to 6 a.m. on September 26.

During the work, campus telephone numbers beginning with 8290 may experience signal interruptions. Internal network phone numbers beginning with 8650 will not be affected. The Office of Property and Resources Management has advised relevant units to make communication arrangements in advance and apologized for the inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Property and Resources Management)
