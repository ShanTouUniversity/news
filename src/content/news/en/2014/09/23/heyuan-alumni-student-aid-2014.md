It is reported that the STU Heyuan Alumni Association has donated RMB 85,000 to establish the Heyuan Alumni Student Grant, which supports financially disadvantaged undergraduates from Heyuan. The 2014 assessment is now underway.

The grant supports 10 students each year, with RMB 2,000 awarded to each recipient. Eligible applicants must be financially disadvantaged undergraduates from Yuancheng, Dongyuan, Longchuan, Zijin, Heping or Lianping County in Heyuan, and must pledge to give back to the university through donations when their financial circumstances permit.

Students who have violated national laws, regulations or university rules, engaged in excessive spending, or caused adverse effects by violating public ethics are not eligible. Applicants must submit the application form to the Student Aid Management Center of the Student Affairs Office, Administrative Center Room 106, by 5 p.m. on September 30.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office, Alumni Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The application form and related attachments are available on the OA system.
