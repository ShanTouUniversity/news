It is reported that the Youth League Committee and the Business School will hold a cooperation signing ceremony between the Shantou Branch of the People’s Bank of China (PBC) and Shantou University, along with financial knowledge lectures, on September 24.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | PBC Shantou Branch and STU Cooperation Signing Ceremony and Financial Knowledge Lectures |
| Time | 14:00, September 24, 2014 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall |
| Topics | Knowledge of Authenticating RMB, Bank Card Transactions and Risk Prevention, Credit for Growth and Youth Life |
| Format | On-site consultations by banking professionals and thematic lectures |
| Organizers | Youth League Committee, Business School |

The lectures cover practical topics such as credit card use, loans and online banking, helping faculty and students better manage banking transaction risks. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee, Business School)
