It is reported that the additional enrollment results for the "Our Common Future" Environmental Service-Learning Course were announced on September 23.

Based on the course design needs, priority was given to students who speak the Chaoshan dialect or possess skills in painting, photography, news writing, and software such as Photoshop, Painter, and video production. After review by the course team, 13 students were selected (full list available in the attachment from the Law School).

All students enrolled in the course are required to attend the first class session at 2:30 p.m. on September 24 in Room E409.

The organizers expressed gratitude to all students who have shown interest in the course and look forward to continued support.
