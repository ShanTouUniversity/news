It is reported that the 2014 Female Student Employment and Entrepreneurship Initiative, jointly organized by the All-China Women's Federation, the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, has opened. Shantou University will organize recommendations of outstanding female students for its role-model program.

The initiative is undertaken by the National University Campus Website Alliance to provide career development and employment guidance for female students. Its central “Search for Female Student Employment and Entrepreneurship Role Models” program will publicize participating institutions' progress and role-model stories through a dedicated website and the China University Student Online platform.

Recommending units must submit a role-model information form, an identification photograph and one to five profile photographs by October 9. Materials should be emailed to job@stu.edu.cn with “Shantou University Female Student Employment and Entrepreneurship Role Model” in the subject line. The identification photograph must measure 135 by 185 pixels, while profile photographs must be at least 500 by 400 pixels. The contact telephone number is 86502357.
