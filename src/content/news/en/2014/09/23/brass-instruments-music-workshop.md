It is reported that the School of Cheung Kong Art and Design and the Arts Education Center will host a music workshop titled “The Charm of Brass Instruments” on September 25. Associate Professor Guo Zheng will be the guest speaker.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The Charm of Brass Instruments |
| Guest speaker | Associate Professor Guo Zheng |
| Time | 7:30–9 p.m., September 25, 2014 |
| Venue | Science Lecture Hall, Science and Technology Building |
| Organizers | School of Cheung Kong Art and Design and Arts Education Center |

Faculty and students are welcome to attend.
