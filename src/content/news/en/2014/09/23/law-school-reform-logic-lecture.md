It is reported that the Law School will host a lecture titled “The Logic of Reform: A Review and Outlook of Institutional Reform” on September 25. Zhu Qianwei, a professor at Fudan University's School of International Relations and Public Affairs and director of its Department of Public Administration, will deliver the lecture.

Zhu is a member of the National Steering Committee for MPA Education and a doctoral supervisor. His teaching and research interests include public administration theory and comparative administration. He is also a visiting professor in the Department of Government and Public Administration at the University of Macau.
