It is reported that the Shantou University “Return to Shangri-La” intermediate outdoor development team completed its expedition from August 10 to 21. After departing from the university and resting in Lijiang, the team traveled to Shangri-La, hiked through Potatso National Park to Niru Village, completed a round trip to the Seven-Colored Waterfall and a three-day, two-night crossing of Nanbao Grassland, and then visited Luoji Township Primary School for public-service activities including the Hot Spring Project and a book donation.

To share what the team learned and promote outdoor environmental protection and public service, the Student Affairs Office will organize a cultural exhibition and project presentation on September 25 and 26.

## Event Schedule

| Event | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shangri-La Cultural Exhibition and Experience | 11 a.m.–1:10 p.m., September 25 | Golden Triangle area, Campus Pedestrian Street |
| “Walking on the Vanishing Horizon” project presentation | 7:30–9 p.m., September 26 | Science Lecture Hall |

The first 50 people arriving for the presentation will receive souvenirs such as Shangri-La postcards. The events are open to the university community. Further information is available from the STU Snow Leopard Outdoor Association's Sina Weibo account.
