It is reported that the Engineering School is recruiting 18 undergraduates from across Shantou University for the “Caring Tutoring and Volunteer Teaching” service-learning course for the fall semester of the 2014–2015 academic year.

The course consists of three stages: theory study and lesson-plan discussion, field service and special-topic research, and a final review. Theory study and planning will take place on Wednesday afternoons in weeks 3 to 5; field service and research will take place on Saturday afternoons in weeks 5 to 12 at Shantou Ai Xin School; and the final review will take place on Wednesday afternoon in week 13. The service component will use team teaching or one-to-one tutoring, together with activities in music, dance, English, handicrafts and science.

Undergraduate applicants must submit the required application information by 5 p.m. on September 26. The course team plans to announce the results by 5 p.m. on September 28. The course consultation telephone number is 86502986. The syllabus and application form are available in the OA attachments.
