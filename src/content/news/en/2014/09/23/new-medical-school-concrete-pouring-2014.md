It is reported that the Office for Key Construction Projects issued a construction notice on September 23, 2014, regarding concrete pouring for the New Medical School project.

According to the contractor, the project plans to pour concrete for the beams and slabs of Zone II on the third floor from 09:00 on September 24 to 07:00 on September 25. The operation cannot be interrupted and requires overnight continuous work. Relatively loud noise will be generated during the process, which may affect residents near the construction site.

Nearby faculty, students and residents are thanked for their understanding and cooperation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office, Office for Key Construction Projects)
