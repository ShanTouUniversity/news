It is reported that 1,529 students registered for the “Technology Clips Shanghai” activity before registration closed. On the morning of September 23, student representatives from the Student Union's Rights and Interests Department witnessed a computer-drawn lottery that selected 50 primary participants and 10 alternates.

Group assignments for participants will be announced through the university's internal office information network or email. Primary participants who cannot attend for personal reasons must email the activity contact by noon on September 24 so that alternates can be admitted in order. The Student Affairs Office reminded participants to arrange their class schedules appropriately.

The list of primary and alternate participants is available in the Student Affairs Office's OA attachment.
