It is reported that Shantou University has opened the gates of Riyuekeng Reservoir and drained it to support dam reinforcement work and preliminary surveys for nearby construction projects, including the new air-supported sports facility.

The Resource Management Office warns that the reservoir area has complex geological and water conditions. Faculty and students should not enter the drained reservoir to walk or wade because of potential safety risks. The university asks the campus community for understanding and cooperation during the related work.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
