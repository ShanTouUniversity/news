Zhicheng College will hold its 13th ATP (Action-Teamwork-Passion) team development camp from September 20 to 21, 2014, for members of the 2014 freshman cohort and related volunteers.

The camp is designed to help freshmen adapt to university life, plan their studies and strengthen communication, collaboration and leadership skills. Team development is included in the university’s general education curriculum. Students who complete the camp’s required sessions and assessment may earn one credit. The camp is also a required course for Zhicheng College residents.

The college asked freshmen to attend the opening ceremony and related sessions according to the arrangements, while volunteers prepare for service duties.
