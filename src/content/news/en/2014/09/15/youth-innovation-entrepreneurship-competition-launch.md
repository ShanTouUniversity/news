Shantou University has joined the Communist Youth League Shantou Committee, the city’s human resources and social security, industry and information technology, science and technology and agricultural authorities, the Shantou branches of the People’s Bank of China and Postal Savings Bank, and the Shantou Youth Federation to organize the “Set Sail for Dreams, Create the Future” Shantou Youth Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition.

The competition aims to support guidance on youth employment and entrepreneurship while providing young people and university students with a platform for innovation, entrepreneurship and exchange.

## Launch Ceremony

The launch ceremony will take place at the STU Science Lecture Hall from 9:00 to 11:30 a.m. on September 16, 2014, and is open to the university community.
