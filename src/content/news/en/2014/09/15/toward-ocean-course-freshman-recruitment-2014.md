Shantou University’s College of Science is recruiting 20 additional students from the 2014 freshman cohort for its “Toward the Ocean” public-welfare course. Students will take part in social research in Dahao fishing villages, marine education at Dahao Overseas Chinese School, and marine ecology research and outreach on Nan’ao. Students who complete the course and pass its assessment can earn one public-welfare course credit.

Fall-term theory sessions are scheduled for Friday afternoons in weeks 2–7, with some Saturday classes. Weekend fieldwork is to be completed before week 12 at sites including Dahao fishing villages, Dahao Overseas Chinese School, and the Nan’ao Island Ring Road. Applicants will be selected through interviews. Registration closes at **midnight on September 17**, and interviews are scheduled for **7–10 p.m. on September 18**. Student identity and contact details requested by the notice are not reproduced.
