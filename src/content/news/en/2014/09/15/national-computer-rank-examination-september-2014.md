The second-half 2014 National Computer Rank Examination will take place on September 20. At Shantou University, sessions will begin at 9:00 a.m. in computer room E102, with each candidate’s specific time listed on the admission ticket. Candidates must gather in classroom E204 20 minutes before the computer-based examination to draw their seat numbers.

The examination covers subjects from Levels 1 through 4 and uses a paperless format. Depending on the subject, the test lasts 90 or 120 minutes. Candidates must bring both their identity card and admission ticket and place them at the upper-left corner of their desks for inspection. Candidates without complete documents or those arriving after the examination begins will not be admitted.

STU undergraduates may collect admission tickets from their school or department academic offices. Other candidates may collect them at the Academic Affairs Office student services window with an identity card. Exam inquiries: 86502411. Candidates were also asked to read the examination rules in advance.
