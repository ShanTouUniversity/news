Shantou University’s Student Community Management Service Center, under the Office of Resource Management, opened recruitment for one administrator. The position will be hired as an auxiliary contract worker, with employment relations remaining outside the university and compensation following university standards.

## Position

Applicants should have sound professional ethics, a responsible attitude and the ability to work rotating 24-hour shifts. Applicants are generally expected to be under 40 and hold at least a high school diploma. Candidates with an associate degree in property or hotel management, or a technical-school electrical qualification at intermediate level or above, will receive preference.

The administrator will oversee dormitory patrols, facility inspections and repair follow-up, greenery and sanitation checks, student inquiries, visitor reception and urgent matters in the residential area.

## Application

Applicants must submit an application form, résumé and copies of relevant certificates to the Student Community Management Service Center by October 13, 2014, by email or mail. Candidates who pass the initial screening will be contacted for written tests and interviews.

Applications may be mailed to the Student Community Management Service Center, Office of Resource Management, Shantou University, University Road 243, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Contact: Ms. Lin; telephone: 0754-86502661; email: dorm@stu.edu.cn.
