The 18th Shantou University Student Club Night, scheduled for the evening of September 16, 2014, was postponed because of typhoon conditions. The university said a new date would be announced later and asked students and staff to follow subsequent updates.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
