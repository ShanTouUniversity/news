The Network Center and Office of Resource Management began verification of university units’ August 2014 office telephone bills. China Telecom Shantou and China Mobile Shantou had sent electronic bills to the university, and the data had been imported into the telephone management system for unit reviewers to check.

Units with no objections were asked to confirm the bills in the system. Discrepancies had to be reported by September 19 to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn for China Telecom landlines or stunic@stu.edu.cn for China Mobile landlines. Bills would be deemed confirmed if no objection or confirmation was submitted by the deadline. After verification, the Finance Office would deduct the charges from each unit’s designated funding account on September 22.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network Center and Office of Resource Management)
