Shantou University opened submissions for fourth-quarter 2014 equipment procurement plans. Units must submit paper and electronic materials to the Equipment and Materials Management Office by October 10, 2014. Late submissions will be deferred to the first quarter of 2015.

Under the university’s government procurement rules, plans with a total budget of no more than RMB 20,000 may be purchased through the university’s equipment bidding platform. Plans above RMB 20,000 require a plan form and proposal and must follow the applicable approval process. Equipment costing more than RMB 100,000 per item also requires a feasibility report.

Plans involving computers or air conditioners require the relevant additional forms or notes on installation locations and existing equipment. All fields must be completed, with item information filled in according to the government centralized procurement catalog and thresholds.
