The Guangdong Yueke Student Entrepreneurship Fund has been established and begun accepting financing applications from university student entrepreneurs in Guangdong and graduates within five years of graduation.

The fund was jointly launched by the Communist Youth League of Guangdong Province and Guangdong Yueke Financial Group to support policies promoting connections between technology and finance and to encourage student entrepreneurship. Eligible applicants may submit materials under the fund’s application requirements to seek venture funding.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ The fund’s application requirements and materials are available in the original notice on the OA system.
