The 7th National University Top Websites selection will soon open registration as part of a national university online culture series guided by the Ministry of Education’s Department of Ideological and Political Work and organized by China University Student Online, Yiban and other organizations.

STU units interested in participating were asked to accelerate website development and improvements and complete the work by mid-October in line with the selection requirements. The detailed registration schedule and arrangements will be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
> ⚠️ Detailed selection requirements and attachments are available in the original notice on the OA system.
