The freshman session of Shantou University’s 10th president-student dinner has completed its reservation draw. A total of 136 students in the 2014 cohort registered, and 32 participants were selected through a computer-generated random draw.

## Event details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 6 p.m., September 23, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Participants | The 32 freshmen selected through the reservation draw |
| Arrival requirement | Bring a campus card and arrive by 5:45 p.m. |

The Student Affairs Office has published the draw results in an attachment. Selected students should arrive on time and bring their own campus cards.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The participant list is available in the original OA attachment.
