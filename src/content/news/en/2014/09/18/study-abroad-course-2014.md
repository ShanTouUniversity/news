Shantou University’s English Language Center is offering the Study Abroad elective in the fall 2014 semester. Eligible students are invited to register through the credit-based academic system.

## Course information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Study Abroad |
| Instructor | Myra Anne Ingmanson |
| Duration | Weeks 1–16, once a week |
| Class periods | Tuesday periods 8–9 or Thursday periods 6–7 |
| Venue | CILL |
| Requirement | Completion of ELC4 |
| Registration | Register through the credit-based academic system |

The course description and detailed teaching content are provided in the course information circulated by the English Language Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Center)
> ⚠️ The course description attachment is available on the OA system.
