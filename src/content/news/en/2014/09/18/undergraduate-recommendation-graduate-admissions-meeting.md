The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University will hold a meeting of the undergraduate recommended graduate admissions leading group at 09:00 on Tuesday, September 23, in Conference Room 312 of the Administration Center.

The meeting will discuss the university's proposed 2015 recommendation list based on nominations from the schools and the quota allocated by the Ministry of Education's Department of Higher Education. Each of the Business, Liberal Arts, Law, Science, Engineering, Journalism and Communication, Art and Design, Medical, and Graduate schools, together with the Academic Affairs Office, is expected to send one representative.
