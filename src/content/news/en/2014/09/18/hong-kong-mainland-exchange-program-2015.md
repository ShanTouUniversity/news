Shantou University’s Office of International Exchange and Cooperation is inviting campus units to submit projects for the 2015 Hong Kong–Mainland University Faculty and Student Exchange Program. The program supports Hong Kong students and accompanying faculty in exchange courses, research, internships, lectures, academic competitions and social-service activities at STU.

## Application highlights

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Implementation period | January 1–December 31, 2015 |
| Funding categories | Short-term projects (3 days to 3 months) and long-term projects (3 months to one academic year) |
| Participants | Students and accompanying faculty from Hong Kong universities |
| Deadline | October 10, 2014 |
| Submission location | Office of Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan Affairs, Room 240, New Administrative Building |

Short-term projects may seek support for accommodation, meals, local transportation and visits during the exchange. Long-term projects may seek support for teaching, accommodation, living expenses and transportation as specified by the program. Applying units should develop project plans with partner universities and submit the application, confirmation and project-information forms in paper and electronic formats.

The notice lists the University of Hong Kong, the Chinese University of Hong Kong, the Open University of Hong Kong, City University of Hong Kong, the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, Hong Kong Baptist University, the Hong Kong Institute of Education, Hong Kong Polytechnic University, the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts, Hong Kong Shue Yan University, Chu Hai College and Lingnan University as participating institutions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
> ⚠️ The application forms, confirmation form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
