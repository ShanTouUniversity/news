The Shantou University Youth League Committee and Student Clubs Union will hold the 18th Student Club Night on September 20. The event will showcase student clubs and enrich campus cultural life.

## Event details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 18th Shantou University Student Club Night |
| Theme | “Flowers of STU, Colors of Clubs” |
| Time | 7:30 p.m., September 20, 2014 (Saturday) |
| Venue | Outside the university auditorium |
| Audience | The entire university community |

The organizers welcome faculty members and students to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee and Student Clubs Union)
