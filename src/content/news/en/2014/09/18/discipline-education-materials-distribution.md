It is reported that the Disciplinary Committee Office of Shantou University has begun distributing the Anti-Corruption Education Reader for Guangdong's Education System (2014 Edition), jointly compiled by the Provincial Education Disciplinary Work Committee and the Propaganda and Education Office of the Provincial Disciplinary Committee, in support of this year's discipline education learning month.

## Distribution and Collection Details

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Study material | Anti-Corruption Education Reader for Guangdong's Education System (2014 Edition) |
| Compiled by | Guangdong Provincial Education Disciplinary Work Committee; Propaganda and Education Office of the Provincial Disciplinary Committee |
| Recipients | All general Party branches and directly affiliated Party branches; officials at division level and above; section-level officials working in personnel, finance or asset-related roles |
| Collection deadline | Before 5:00 p.m., September 22 (Monday) |
| Collection location | Disciplinary Committee Office, Room 422, New Administrative Center |

According to the arrangement, each unit should send a representative to collect the readers from the Disciplinary Committee Office before the deadline. In addition to printed materials, the office also holds warning-education documentary films produced by the provincial disciplinary committee, which units may borrow to organize group viewing sessions.

The main study materials for this year's discipline education month have also been circulated alongside the announcement for use in organized study activities.
