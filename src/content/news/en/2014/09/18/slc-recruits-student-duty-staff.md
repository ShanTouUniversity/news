Shantou University's Student Learning Center (SLC) is recruiting six to seven duty staff members from the 2014 undergraduate cohort at the main campus. Medical College students are excluded from this recruitment. Duties include front-desk service, reception, center administration, service promotion and other temporary assignments. Working hours will be arranged during the 2014–2015 academic year.

## Requirements and Application

Applicants should demonstrate a service-oriented attitude, responsibility, a serious and friendly manner, and some planning and organizational ability. Applications are due at 22:00 on Monday, September 22, through the university's student work-study application system. If applications exceed capacity, SLC will screen applicants and arrange interviews, with the interview list expected on September 24.

Compensation follows the university's work-study pay standard.
