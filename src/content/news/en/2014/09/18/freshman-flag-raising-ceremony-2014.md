The Shantou University Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office will hold a freshman flag-raising ceremony on September 22 for all undergraduate and graduate students in the 2014 cohort.

## Ceremony details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Assembly time | 6:10 a.m., September 22, 2014 |
| Ceremony time | 6:30 a.m. |
| Venue | Flag platform by the university motto monument |
| Participants | All freshmen in the 2014 cohort, including graduate students |
| Main program | Flag raising, national anthem, freshman oath and singing of “The University Motto” |

Schools and residential colleges will organize students to arrive early, form ranks and record attendance. Participants should dress neatly, maintain order and bring their signed oaths. Each unit should also bring its school flag and become familiar with the group song in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The ceremony attachment is available on the OA system.
