The Shantou University Chorus is recruiting new members from across the university and will hold auditions on September 20 and 24. No special musical training or instrumental skills are required; students interested in singing are welcome to apply.

## Audition details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Applicants | All students |
| Audition times | 1:30–5:30 p.m., September 20 (Saturday), and September 24 (Wednesday) |
| Venue | Music Rehearsal Hall, first floor, STU Building, Arts Education Center |
| Registration | Register on site for an audition |
| Content | Self-introduction, pitch imitation and a self-selected song excerpt; accompaniment or instruments may be used |

Applicants may choose any type of song and perform a cappella or with accompaniment. Students with instrumental skills may also bring an instrument for an additional presentation. Further information will be posted on the chorus’s official Weibo account.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Arts Education Center)
