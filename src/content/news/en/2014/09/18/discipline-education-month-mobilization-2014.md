Shantou University’s Office of the Disciplinary Committee will hold a mobilization meeting on September 23 for the 2014 discipline education month and integrity-risk prevention work. The meeting will communicate the university’s activity arrangements and deploy related education and risk-prevention tasks.

## Meeting details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 3:40 p.m., September 23, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 213, New Administrative Center |
| Participants | Heads of university offices, Party general-branch secretaries and secretaries of directly affiliated Party branches |
| Agenda | Deploying discipline education month and integrity-risk prevention work |

The Office of the Disciplinary Committee asks the relevant officials to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of the Disciplinary Committee)
