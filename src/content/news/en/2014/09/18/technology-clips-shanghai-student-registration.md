The Li Ka-shing Foundation and the Shanghai municipal government will hold Technology Clips Shanghai at Shanghai Grand Theatre on October 9, 2014. The event will present selected innovation projects and technology companies. The foundation has reserved 100 places for full-time Shantou University students, with travel scheduled from October 8 to 10.

## Registration

Half of the places will be allocated through an online lottery and the other half through recommendations by relevant schools. Eligible students could register through the booking system from 09:00 on September 18 to 17:00 on September 22. Students who attended the May 2014 Technology Clips Guangdong event in Guangzhou are not eligible. If applications exceed the quota, the online lottery will be held at 09:00 on September 23 in the presence of members of the university student union rights department.

The university will arrange transportation, accommodation and meals, sponsored by the Li Ka-shing Foundation. Participants will travel in 10 groups of 10 and will prepare WeChat content after the event for a related selection activity.
