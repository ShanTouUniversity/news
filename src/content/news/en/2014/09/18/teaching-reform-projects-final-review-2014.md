Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office will conduct final reviews of university-level teaching reform research projects launched in 2011 that received extensions, in accordance with the university’s management measures for undergraduate teaching reform research projects.

## Review arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | University-level teaching reform research projects launched in 2011 with approved extensions |
| Paper materials | Three copies each of the completion review form and research-results materials |
| Electronic materials | Electronic versions matching the paper submissions |
| Deadline | October 17, 2014 (Friday) |
| Submission location | Teaching Reform Evaluation Section, third floor, New Administrative Center |

Project leaders must complete the completion review form and submit originals or copies of research results together with corresponding electronic files. For book-length results, a scan of the cover and copyright page is sufficient; published papers may be downloaded from journal websites.

Project leaders should complete their submissions by the deadline. The specific forms and submission instructions are provided in the attachments issued by the Academic Affairs Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The completion form, project list and other attachments are available on the OA system.
