The Physics Department of the Science College issued a notice on September 18 announcing the schedule for the error-theory sessions of the fall 2014 General Physics Laboratory course. The first week is devoted to theory, with laboratory work beginning in the second week.

## Class Schedule

| Laboratory section | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday section | September 22, Monday, periods 6–8 (14:00–17:00) | E408 |
| Tuesday section | September 27, Saturday, periods 2–4 (09:00–12:00) | E306 |
| Thursday section | September 27, Saturday, periods 6–8 (14:00–17:00) | E306 |

Students enrolled in the relevant sections were asked to share the schedule with their classmates.
