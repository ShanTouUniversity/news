It is reported that Shantou University has announced the recipients of its 2014 Outstanding New Postgraduate Scholarships. Following individual applications, recommendations from graduate training units, and review by the Graduate School in accordance with the university's evaluation measures, a total of 47 new master's students were selected.

The Outstanding New Student Scholarship was established to improve the quality and structure of the postgraduate intake and to encourage outstanding undergraduates to apply to STU. The results are as follows:

| Award | Number of Recipients |
|-------|----------------------|
| First Prize | 5 |
| Second Prize | 42 |

The evaluation was organized by the Graduate School, and the results were issued to all graduate training units under document No. 101 [2014] of STU. The full list of recipients is available in the attachment.
