Shantou University’s School of Law is recruiting 13 additional undergraduates from across the university for its “Our Common Future” environmental research and service course. The course combines academic learning with community environmental practice, including research, project design and service activities focused on environmental issues in villages near the university.

## Course details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Openings | 13 undergraduate places |
| Course period | Weeks 1–9 of the fall 2014 semester |
| Activity time | Wednesday afternoons |
| Practice site | Villages in the Wupu subdistrict of Shantou |
| Credit | One public-interest course credit upon passing the assessment |
| Application deadline | 5 p.m., September 22, 2014 |

The first four weeks will focus on theory, field research and project discussions. Weeks five through eight will involve field service and topic-based research, followed by a final summary in week nine. All undergraduates may apply; the instructor will select students according to the course’s needs.

Applicants should submit the personal and academic information requested in the notice. Admission results were expected by 5 p.m. on September 23. Students may contact the relevant School of Law faculty member for course inquiries.
