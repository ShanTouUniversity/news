Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has launched the internal selection and recommendation process for 2014 national experimental teaching demonstration centers in accordance with a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. The university will select existing provincial-level demonstration centers with distinctive features and strong development results.

## Application arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible units | Relevant units with established provincial-level experimental teaching demonstration centers |
| Process | Online application and submission of supporting materials |
| Online platform | The relevant Guangdong Provincial Department of Education application system |
| Materials deadline | September 27, 2014 |
| Submission location | Teaching Reform Evaluation Section, third floor, New Administrative Center |

The recommended materials include an application form, a video introducing the experimental teaching center, university policies and support measures, and a recommendation summary. Two printed copies must be prepared and bound as required. Each applying center should also build an independent website presenting its application, operations, teaching resources and development results.

After the internal selection, the university will publish the recommendations for at least seven days. Relevant units may contact the Teaching Reform Evaluation Section of the Academic Affairs Office for inquiries.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The application-system instructions, application form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
