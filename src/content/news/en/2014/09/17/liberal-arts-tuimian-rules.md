It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts at Shantou University issued on September 17, 2014 the "Implementation Rules for the Management of Recommending Undergraduates for Exemption-Exam Admission to Master's Degree Programs" (STU Wen Fa [2014] No. 1), setting out specific provisions for the selection and administration of exempted postgraduate recommendations within the college.

## Toward Institutionalized Postgraduate Recommendation

Recommendation of outstanding graduating undergraduates for master's programs without examination — commonly known as "tuimian" — is a key pathway for undergraduates to pursue graduate studies. The new rules refine the university-level management framework at the college level, establishing concrete operating procedures for the College of Liberal Arts.

The document was addressed to all departments and centers within the college, with a copy to Shantou University's Office of Academic Affairs. The full text of the rules was distributed as an attachment; specific eligibility criteria, scoring methods, and recommendation procedures can be found in the original attachment.
