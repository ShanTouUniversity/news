Shantou University’s School of Science will host a science salon on September 19, featuring Yu Kunqian, a researcher and doctoral supervisor at the Shanghai Institute of Materia Medica, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

## Lecture details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | Protein dynamics related to major infectious diseases and virtual drug screening |
| Speaker | Yu Kunqian, researcher and doctoral supervisor at the Shanghai Institute of Materia Medica, Chinese Academy of Sciences |
| Time | 10 a.m., September 19, 2014 (Friday) |
| Venue | School of Science Lecture Hall (Science 352) |

Yu’s research focuses on biomolecular computer simulation, computer-aided drug design and the use of grid computing in drug-design applications. He has also studied small-molecule screening against important targets related to avian influenza, *Staphylococcus epidermidis* and HIV. He has led projects funded by Shanghai, the Chinese Academy of Sciences and national science and technology programs, and has published research in international journals.

The lecture is open to interested faculty members and students.
