It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host a lecture titled "Arthouse Film Promotion Strategies Through The Golden Era" on the evening of Friday, September 19, 2014, at the first-floor lecture hall of the School, followed by a documentary screening.

## Event Schedule

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Topic | Arthouse Film Promotion Strategies Through The Golden Era |
| Speaker | Cheng Yuhai (Executive Producer of The Golden Era, renowned film critic) |
| Date & Time | September 19, 2014, 7:00 p.m. – 10:45 p.m. |
| Venue | Lecture Hall, 1F, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design |

The evening is divided into two parts: a keynote lecture from 7:00 to 8:15 p.m., and a screening of the documentary *She Recognized the Storm* — the companion piece to *The Golden Era* — from 8:15 to 10:45 p.m.

## About the Speaker

Cheng Yuhai is the executive producer of the film *The Golden Era* and a well-known film critic. He previously served as deputy editor-in-chief of *Movie View* magazine and editor-in-chief of *Hong Kong Film* magazine, with extensive expertise in arthouse film production and marketing.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
