It is reported that the group stage of the Shantou Workers’ Games football competition will take place on the afternoon of September 19, 2014. The Shantou University team will play Bank of Communications at the football field of the Shantou Table Tennis School.

The match is for staff members included in the participating roster. The roster was supplied as an attachment to the original notice and is not reproduced here; participating players should prepare in advance and arrive on time.

> ⚠️ The participating roster is available in the original OA attachment.
