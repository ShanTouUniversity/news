At the request of faculty and staff, the Shantou University Union is continuing Pilates classes for female faculty and staff in the fall 2014 semester. Participants may choose between Monday and Wednesday sessions.

## Class schedule

| Session | Regular time | First class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 5:40–6:40 p.m. every Monday | September 29, 2014 |
| Wednesday | 5:40–6:40 p.m. every Wednesday | September 24, 2014 |

Existing participants should confirm their preferred session by September 19. Those who do not confirm by the deadline will be treated as withdrawing from the semester, and the places may be offered to new participants. Registration and confirmation can be made through the campus telephone number published by the Union.

Participants should bring their own yoga mats and wear loose-fitting clothing. Classes will be suspended during public holidays.
