It is reported that Shantou University has launched the 2014 selection and recommendation process for a national virtual simulation experiment teaching center in accordance with requirements from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. The proposed development would combine virtual reality, multimedia, human–computer interaction, databases and network communications to provide safe and economical alternatives for experiments that are hazardous, extreme, costly or otherwise difficult to conduct.

## Application Schedule

| Item | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Electronic project summary | 5:00 p.m., September 22, 2014 |
| Complete application materials | September 30, 2014 |

Applicants should already have a national or provincial experimental teaching demonstration center and have established information platforms, mature virtual simulation technology and sound laboratory teams. Materials include an application, project summary, supporting policies and safeguards, and a video of the center. Detailed formats and file-size requirements are specified in the OA attachments.

Recommended projects must also undergo an internal public notice period of at least seven days. The Academic Affairs Office contact is Liu Xiangling, telephone 86502736, email lxl0726@stu.edu.cn.
