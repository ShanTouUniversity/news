The Guangdong Provincial Education Department Teacher Continuing Education Guidance Center has confirmed the certificates earned by eligible non-teacher-training students who completed Shantou University’s spring 2014 education and psychology training courses. The Academic Affairs Office asks students who passed the training to collect their certificates as arranged.

## Collection details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Collection location | Academic Affairs service window, third floor, New Administrative Center |
| Eligible students | 2014 graduating undergraduate and graduate students who attended the training and passed |
| Identification | Students collecting their own certificates must show valid identification; authorized representatives must show valid identification for both parties |

The notice explains that non-graduating undergraduates who take education and psychology through the credit-based system will receive course credit records only, not teacher-qualification training certificates. The Academic Affairs Office asks relevant students to share the information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The list of students who passed is available in the original OA attachment.
