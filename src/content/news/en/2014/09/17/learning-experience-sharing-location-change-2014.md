Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has announced a venue change for the “Different Learning Experiences” session scheduled for the evening of September 18. The event time remains unchanged.

## Updated details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | “Different Learning Experiences” session |
| Time | 7:30–9:30 p.m., September 18, 2014 (Thursday) |
| Venue | Lecture Room 101, Building E (E101) |
| Audience | Students in the 2014 freshman cohort |

The session was originally scheduled to take place in the library lecture hall. The Student Affairs Office asks the relevant freshmen to note the new venue.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
