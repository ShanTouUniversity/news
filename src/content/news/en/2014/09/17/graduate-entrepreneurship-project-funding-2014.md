Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has circulated a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security on applications and reviews for 2014 funding for outstanding entrepreneurship projects by university graduates.

## Application details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible applicants | Graduates interested in applying for funding for outstanding entrepreneurship projects |
| Deadline | Before the end of the workday on September 23, 2014 (Tuesday) |
| Paper submission | Student Entrepreneurship Center, Room 106, Administrative Center |
| Electronic submission | Send materials to the designated Student Affairs Office email address as well |

The Student Affairs Office asks interested applicants to prepare their materials according to the provincial notice and submit both paper and electronic versions before the deadline. The original circulated notice provides the detailed eligibility, documentation and review requirements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The application notice and related attachments are available on the OA system.
