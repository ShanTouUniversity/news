It is reported that Shantou University will hold its 2014–2015 teaching work meeting from 3:00 to 4:30 p.m. on Friday, September 19, 2014, in the Science and Technology Lecture Hall. The meeting is intended for all faculty on the main campus.

Relevant faculty members are asked to attend on time.
