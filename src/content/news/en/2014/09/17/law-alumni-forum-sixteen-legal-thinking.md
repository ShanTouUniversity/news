It is reported that the 16th alumni forum of Shantou University’s Law School will take place at 3:30 p.m. on September 19, 2014. The forum, titled “Thinking Like a Legal Professional,” will be held in E Building stepped lecture hall 204 and is open to faculty and students across the university.

The speaker, Yang Zejun, is an alumnus of the Law School’s Class of 1989. He holds a doctorate in management and serves as director of Guangdong Zhuofan Law Firm, vice president of the Huizhou Lawyers Association and legal adviser to the Huizhou Municipal People’s Government. His work includes legal consulting for government bodies and large enterprises, as well as non-litigation legal services.
