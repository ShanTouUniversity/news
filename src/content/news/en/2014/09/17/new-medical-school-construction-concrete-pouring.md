It is reported that the New Medical School construction project at Shantou University will pour concrete for the beams and slabs in Zone I of the third floor from 10:00 a.m. on Thursday, September 18, 2014, to 8:00 a.m. on Friday, September 19. The work will continue overnight without interruption and may generate considerable noise.

The project office asked nearby residents to make arrangements in advance and apologized for the potential disturbance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Key Construction Projects Office and Property Management Office)
