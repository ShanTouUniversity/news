Shantou University’s Party Committee has deployed its 2014 discipline education month in line with requirements for Guangdong’s education system. The activities will run from September 23 to October 23 and cover faculty, staff and students, with particular attention to Party members, officials at all levels and employees in key personnel, finance and asset-related positions.

## Focus areas

The program will be organized around the theme “Upholding organizational discipline and building sound conduct.” It will cover education on ideals and the core socialist values, political and organizational discipline, the Party’s traditions and conduct, and warning education through case studies. Participants will also study regulations related to integrity and clean governance, the development of an anti-corruption system and confidentiality, strengthening awareness of discipline, integrity and responsibility.

Party general branches, directly affiliated Party branches and university units will develop activities suited to their circumstances and provide targeted education for different groups. The Party Committee has asked the relevant units to implement the study program, improve work conduct and strengthen organization and oversight.
