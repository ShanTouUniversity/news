It is reported that the ninth Traditional Chinese Culture Study Camp at Shantou University’s Zhicheng College will take place from October 2 to 6, 2014. The camp is open to Zhicheng College residents and students living in other university residences on a voluntary basis. It plans to admit 24 students, including 18 from Zhicheng College and six from other residences.

## Program

The camp will include readings of Confucian classics such as *The Classic of Filial Piety* and *The Analects*, group study, expert video lectures, experiential exchange and off-campus visits. Applicants should have a foundation in traditional Chinese culture, work well in teams and follow the program arrangements. Participants will cover part of the food and other expenses during the four-day outing, at 60 yuan per person; students with financial need may apply for a reduction.

Registration closes at 5:00 p.m. on September 24. Applicants should complete the form and submit it to ATP@stu.edu.cn according to the original OA attachment. Places will be allocated in order of registration until full.

> ⚠️ The application form and submission requirements are available in the original OA attachment.
