Shantou University’s Women’s Studies Center in the School of Liberal Arts is recruiting undergraduate and graduate interns for project assistant, research assistant and design assistant positions. The interns will support public-interest activities, research-related work and communications design.

## Positions

| Position | Openings | Main responsibilities |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Project assistant | 2 | Collecting and organizing project materials, supporting data analysis and activities; one position focuses on student activities and news writing |
| Research assistant | 1 | Supporting information searches, organization and research tasks |
| Design assistant | 1 | Designing posters, electronic newsletters, videos and presentations |

The project assistant and research assistant positions are open to undergraduate and graduate students in the 2012–2014 cohorts. Applicants should be responsible, proactive and able to complete assigned tasks. Research assistants should be able to use statistical software such as Excel and SPSS, while design assistants should have relevant design or video-production skills.

Applicants should submit the completed application form to the Women’s Studies Center office by September 26. Candidates who pass the initial screening will be contacted for an interview and should bring original or electronic samples of relevant work.
