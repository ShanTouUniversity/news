Shantou University Law School held the 280th lecture in its series at Lecture Hall 6 on September 27. Chen Xikang, vice president of the Guangdong Lawyers Association and director of Guangdong Chen Liang Yongju Law Firm, spoke on “Designing a Competency Model for Training New Lawyers.”

Chen is a practicing lawyer and partner at the firm and has held professional roles with lawyers’ associations, arbitration institutions and universities. The lecture was open to the university community.
