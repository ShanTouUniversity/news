Shantou University reported that government use of the university gymnasium would temporarily require related classes, training sessions, and activities to move to other venues. The Academic Affairs Office and Physical Education Department will notify the affected teachers and students of the specific arrangements.

Relevant units and members of the university community were advised to watch for follow-up information and follow the revised venue arrangements for classes, training, and activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office of the Party and Administration)
