It is reported that the Business School Case Competition Committee issued a notice on September 30, 2014, to select students for the 15th ICBC (The Inter-collegiate Business Competition) International Business Case Competition.

Organized by McGill University, Canada, ICBC is Canada’s longest-running undergraduate case competition since 1978, inviting teams from top business schools worldwide to compete across eight areas including accounting, business strategy, ethics, debate, finance, human resources, marketing and MIS. The 15th edition will be held March 16–21, 2015.

## Selection

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | 15th ICBC, March 16–21, 2015 |
| Eligibility | Full-time sophomores, juniors and seniors of the Business School and students minoring in Business; must have taken or be taking King Sir’s BUS3186 Business Case Analysis; international competition experience preferred; must agree to pre-competition training |
| Deadline | October 15, 2014 |
| How to apply | Send resume to King Sir at kingkng@stu.edu.cn with subject “Participate in ICBC Business Case Competition” |
| Process | Interview selection; results to be announced on the campus intranet and Business School website |
| Contact | Student Guo |
| Organizer | Business School Case Competition Committee |
