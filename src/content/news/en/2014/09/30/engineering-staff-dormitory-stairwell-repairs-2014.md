Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office plans to begin repairs to stairwells in staff dormitory buildings B1 and B10 on October 6, 2014, with work expected to finish by November 4.

The project includes repainting stairwell walls and ceilings, installing aluminum windows and replacing lighting fixtures. Construction may cause noise, vibration and dust. The university advised passersby to take care near the work areas and asked residents to avoid remaining at active construction sites.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
