Shantou University Library has opened trial access to the VLex legal information database. The database brings together Chinese and English legal content from VLex Global and China, including laws and regulations, cases, practice guides, news and international treaties, with search and update services.

The trial will run through November 28, 2014. Students and staff can access it through the library database page and send feedback to the Library Subject Services Department.
