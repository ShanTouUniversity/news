Shantou University’s MYSTUdio plans to hold a movie night on October 9, 2014, screening the technology-themed film *Robot & Frank* and inviting attendees to discuss it afterward.

The event is scheduled for 7:30–9 p.m. at MYSTUdio on the first floor of the library. Places are limited to the first 50 successful registrants. The film follows an older man with dementia whose son provides a robot to help care for him, leading to a developing relationship between the two.

Registration runs from September 30 to October 8 through the “Movie Night” event page on MYSTU. Those who receive a confirmation email should check in at 7:15 p.m. on October 9. Drinks and popcorn will be provided.
