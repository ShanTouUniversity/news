Shantou University is recruiting one laboratory safety supervisor for the Equipment Office of the Resource Management Office through an internal search. Applicants should hold at least a bachelor’s degree and have five or more years of experience in laboratory management or laboratory safety management. A background or related experience in chemistry, biology or medicine is preferred. Applicants should also demonstrate professional conduct, communication skills and analytical ability.

## Responsibilities and Application

The position will coordinate university laboratory safety construction and management, inspect safety facilities and policies, organize safety training and drills, and support the daily work of the Laboratory Safety Construction and Management Committee.

Applicants must send the Shantou University Staff Recruitment Form to the Human Resources Office recruitment email by October 13, 2014. Candidates will be selected for interviews after qualification review; interview and written-test arrangements will be announced separately. The position has a one-year probation period, followed by grading under university rules after a satisfactory assessment.
