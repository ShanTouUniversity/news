Shantou University Business School has opened selection for participants in the 51st ICBSC (International Collegiate Business Strategy Competition). Hosted by California State University, the competition begins with about ten weeks of online business simulation, followed by business-plan and annual-report work, on-site decisions and presentations.

## Application Details

Full-time undergraduates in the Business School, as well as students from other schools minoring in business, may apply, with preference given to second-, third- and fourth-year students. Applicants should have taken or be taking BUS3186 Business Case Analysis and agree to pre-competition training. Experience in online international business simulations or case competitions is preferred.

Applicants must submit a personal resume by October 15, 2014. The Business School will conduct interviews, with the results posted on the campus information network and the Business School website.
