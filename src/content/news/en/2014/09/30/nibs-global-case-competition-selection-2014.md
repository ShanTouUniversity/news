Shantou University’s Business School Case Competition Committee has opened campus selection for the 20th Network of International Business Schools (NIBS) International Business Case Competition. Member schools of the international business-school alliance take turns hosting the event; this edition will be hosted by Carleton University in Canada.

Online preliminary rounds are scheduled for November 2014, with teams submitting their reports online. The top 16 teams will be invited to the advancement and final rounds at Carleton University’s Sprott School of Business from February 15 to 21, 2015.

The selection is open to full-time sophomores, juniors and seniors in the Business School or students minoring in business. Applicants must have completed or be taking BUS3186, Business Case Analysis, and agree to pre-competition training; prior international case-competition experience is preferred. The school will select participants through interviews and publish the results on its internal office network and website.

Applicants must submit a résumé by October 15, 2014. The official rules were included as an attachment to the notice.
