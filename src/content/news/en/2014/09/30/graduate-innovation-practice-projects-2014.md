Shantou University’s Graduate School has opened applications for graduate innovation and creative-practice projects in two tracks: research innovation, supporting extracurricular research and commercialization, and creative entrepreneurship, supporting cultural and business initiatives.

Full-time master’s and doctoral students may apply individually or in teams. Each student may join only one project and may not apply for another before it concludes. Every project must have at least one faculty member or administrator with a senior professional title or doctoral degree as an adviser. Projects generally run for one year and proceed through application, approval, implementation, midterm review, completion review and potential commercialization.

Applicants may propose topics related to research, their areas of expertise or service to society. Research proposals should explain the state of the field, originality, research foundation, expected results and budget. Creative-enterprise proposals should align with relevant industry policies and demonstrate cultural value and application prospects.

Applications must be submitted to the Graduate School Youth League Committee by October 24, 2014, in two copies printed double-sided. The university plans to organize an expert preliminary review and announce selected projects on its internal office network in November.
