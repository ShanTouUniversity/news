Shantou University’s Business School Case Competition Committee has opened campus selection for the 15th McGill Management International Case Competition (MMICC). Hosted by McGill University in Canada, the competition is scheduled for March 16–21, 2015. The selection is open to full-time undergraduates in the Business School and students minoring in business.

Applicants must be sophomores, juniors or seniors and must have completed or be taking BUS3186, Business Case Analysis. Previous international competition experience is preferred, and selected students must agree to pre-competition training. The school will select team members through interviews and publish the results on its internal office network and Business School website.

Applicants must submit a résumé by October 15, 2014. Competition information is available on the MMICC website.
