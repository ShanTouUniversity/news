MYSTUdio, a student studio under Shantou University’s Network and Information Center, is scheduled to hold an open day on October 10. Students and faculty are invited to visit the studio on the first floor of the library. It provides a collaborative space for students from different disciplines to develop technology-based ideas.

The open day is scheduled from 9 a.m. to noon and from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m. Projects in development include character and environment design for a UK game developer’s mobile game, an app to support communication among residents of Hong Kong’s outlying islands, interactive multimedia connected with traditional instruments, and sound-insulating, fire-resistant materials designed to biodegrade.

MYSTUdio previously held its “Creative Hackathon: Scene Models” in May and mid-September. The Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has also held activities at the studio, including film nights, game events, TED talks and practical workshops.
