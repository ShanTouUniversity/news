The School of Science will host a seminar in its “Toward Modern Mathematics” series. Chen Xiaopeng, PhD, of Peking University, will present “Center manifolds for infinite dimensional random dynamical systems.”

The seminar will begin at 2:30 p.m. on October 8, 2014, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). All students and staff are welcome.
