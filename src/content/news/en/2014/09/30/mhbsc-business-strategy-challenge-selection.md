Shantou University Business School has opened selection for participants in the 12th MHBSC (McDonough-Hilltop Business Strategy Challenge). Hosted by McDonough School of Business, the competition asks teams to develop sustainable solutions to business challenges over a period of about one week.

## Application Details

The competition is scheduled for February 2015. Full-time undergraduates in the Business School, as well as students from other schools minoring in business, may apply, with preference given to second-, third- and fourth-year students. Applicants should have taken or be taking BUS3186 Business Case Analysis and agree to pre-competition training. Experience in international business case competitions is preferred.

Applicants must submit a personal resume by October 15, 2014. The Business School will conduct interviews, with the results posted on the campus information network and the Business School website. Further details are available through the MHBSC website.
