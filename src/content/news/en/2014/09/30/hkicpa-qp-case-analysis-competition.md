The HKICPA 2014 Qualification Programme (QP) Case Analysis Competition, organized by the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants, is open to Shantou University undergraduates. The competition focuses on company business cases and asks accounting and business students to apply knowledge of finance, marketing, management and related fields.

## Competition Information

Participants must form teams of four. All team members must come from the same university, and each participant may join only one team. Registration is free. Strong teams may receive travel and accommodation support, while the competition also offers cash prizes, QP fee waivers and internship opportunities.

Interested students must register through the competition website and submit team and participant information by October 15, 2014. Further details are available from the HKICPA website.
