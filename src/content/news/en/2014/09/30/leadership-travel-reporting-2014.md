It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 115 [2014] on September 30, 2014, to standardize reporting for leaders traveling outside Shantou.

Except for picking up or seeing off guests at Chaoshan Airport or Chaoshan High-Speed Railway Station and non-official travel during holidays, reporting is required as follows:

| Personnel | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Deputy heads of units | Report to the head of the unit and inform the unit office; for trips of 7 days or more, also report to the supervising university leader |
| Heads of units and assistants to the president | Report to the supervising or liaison university leader and inform the unit office, while emailing the President’s Office (jyk@stu.edu.cn) for the record |
| Vice presidents | Report to the Secretary or Executive President and inform the President’s Office |
| Secretary and Executive President | Inform each other and inform the President’s Office |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
