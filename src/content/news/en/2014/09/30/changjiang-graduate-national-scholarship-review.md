The Graduate National Scholarship Review Committee of Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design has completed its 2014 review and recommended three graduate students for the award. The proposed recipients are 2011 cohort students Bai BC, He RL and Chen JN.

Organizations or individuals with objections may submit written materials to the school’s Academic Affairs Office by October 10, 2014. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed.
