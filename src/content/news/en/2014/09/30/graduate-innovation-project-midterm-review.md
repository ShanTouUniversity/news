Shantou University Graduate School will conduct midterm reviews in late October 2014 for the graduate extracurricular academic and technology projects approved in 2014. The reviews will be held through the Business School, School of Engineering, Institute of Higher Education, Department of Social Sciences, and Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, with venues to be announced.

## Review Requirements

Project teams must complete the 2014 Graduate Extracurricular Academic and Technology Project Midterm Review Form, submit two paper copies by October 14, and send an electronic copy. The review will take the form of a defense. Each team must prepare a five-minute presentation covering progress to date and its plan for the remaining work.

Projects that fail to submit a midterm report or miss the defense without a valid reason will be treated as withdrawn, with a written explanation and return of the initial project funds required.
