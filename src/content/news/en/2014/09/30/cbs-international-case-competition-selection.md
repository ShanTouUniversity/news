Shantou University Business School has opened selection for participants in the 13th CBS International Case Competition. Hosted by Copenhagen Business School, the competition will be held from February 21 to 28, 2015, in Copenhagen, Denmark. Participants will develop strategies for a real company case and complete an intensive case preparation followed by a presentation and question-and-answer session.

Full-time 2011 and 2012 cohort undergraduates in the Business School, as well as students from other schools minoring in business, may apply. Applicants should have taken or be taking BUS3186 Business Case Analysis and agree to pre-competition training. Experience in international competitions is preferred.

Applicants must submit a personal resume by October 13, 2014. The Business School will conduct interviews, with the results posted on the campus information network and the Business School website.
