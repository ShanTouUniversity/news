The 2014 Graduate National Scholarship Review Subcommittee of Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts has completed its review and recommended two graduate students for the award. The proposed recipients are Li YJ and Huang J.

Organizations or individuals with objections may submit written materials to the review subcommittee at the School of Liberal Arts Graduate Administration Office, Room Wennan 203, by October 10, 2014. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed.
