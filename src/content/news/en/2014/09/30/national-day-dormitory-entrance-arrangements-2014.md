Shantou University will temporarily close two passages in its student-dormitory area from October 1 to 7, 2014, as part of holiday security arrangements. The affected routes are the passage from Building A to the Third Dining Hall and the passage between Building D and the dining hall.

During the closure, people using the Building A route should enter through the main entrance of Building B; those using the Building D route should use the main entrance of Building C. Both passages are scheduled to resume their regular opening hours on October 8. The arrangements were issued by the university’s Logistics Office, Student Community Management and Service Center, and dormitory adviser office.
