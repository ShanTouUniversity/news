Shantou University’s Office of Academic Affairs has completed its selection process for the 2014 national experimental teaching demonstration center application in response to a Guangdong provincial education notice. After reviewing application materials, the Teaching Conditions Subcommittee of the University Teaching Committee voted to propose the Medical Imaging Teaching Center of the Medical College.

During the public-comment period, organizations or individuals with objections may submit written materials to the Office of Academic Affairs by 5 p.m. on October 8, 2014. Materials should be submitted at the third floor of the New Administration Center. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, and individual submissions must be signed.
