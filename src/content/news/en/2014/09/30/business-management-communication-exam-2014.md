It is reported that the Business School has announced the exam schedule for its 2014-2015 summer term course *Business and Management Communication*.

The exam is scheduled for Wednesday, October 8 (Week 3), from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. (Periods 6-7). Students enrolled in the course are reminded to bring their student ID cards and arrive on time.

## Exam Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Course | Business and Management Communication |
| Term | 2014-2015 Summer Term |
| Date & Time | October 8 (Wednesday), 14:00-16:00 (Periods 6-7) |
| Requirement | Bring student ID card and arrive punctually |
