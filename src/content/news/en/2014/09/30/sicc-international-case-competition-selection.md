Shantou University Business School has opened selection for participants in the 26th SICC (Scotiabank International Case Competition). Hosted by Ivey Business School and Scotiabank, the competition will be held from March 25 to 28, 2015, at Ivey Business School at Western University in Canada.

Full-time 2011 and 2012 cohort undergraduates in the Business School, as well as students from other schools minoring in business, may apply. Applicants should have taken or be taking BUS3186 Business Case Analysis and agree to pre-competition training. Experience in international case competitions is preferred.

Applicants must submit a personal resume by October 15, 2014. The Business School will conduct interviews, with the results posted on the campus information network and the Business School website.
