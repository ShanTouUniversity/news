Shantou University sports facilities will be closed to students and staff during the National Day holiday. The closure covers the indoor badminton courts, indoor table-tennis facilities, gym and gymnastics room. The reopening date will follow a subsequent university announcement.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
