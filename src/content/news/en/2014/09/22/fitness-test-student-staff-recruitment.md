The Shantou University Physical Education Department is recruiting 27 on-campus undergraduates, including 12 positions for women, to assist with this semester's National Student Fitness Standard tests. The tests will take place on the mornings of September 27, October 12, October 18, October 19, and October 26.

Student staff will help teachers move equipment, give starts, keep time and count results, maintain testing equipment, supervise order, and organize and enter data. Applicants should be in good health, able to work outdoors, available on all listed dates, and capable of organizing and managing tasks.

Applicants must email their student ID number, name, year and major, and mobile number to the department's official mailbox by the end of the workday on September 23, using “Physical Fitness Testing Staff” as the subject. The department will separately announce interview and training arrangements. Student application details are not reproduced here.
