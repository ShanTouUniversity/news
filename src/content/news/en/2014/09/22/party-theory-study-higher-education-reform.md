Shantou University will hold an expanded Party Central Group study session at 3 p.m. on Wednesday, September 24, in Conference Room 5 of the Academic Exchange Center.

Professor Gu Peihua has been invited to deliver a report titled “Deepening Comprehensive Higher Education Reform Through Institutional Innovation and Building a High-Level University.” Members of the Party Central Group and administrative leaders of all secondary units will attend the session.
