It is reported that Shantou University will conduct physical fitness tests for all on-campus undergraduates over four weekends — September 27, October 12, October 18 and October 19 — in order to complete this semester's national student fitness assessment within October.

## Test Schedule

| Date | Colleges |
| --- | --- |
| September 27 (Saturday) | Journalism; Science |
| October 12 (Sunday) | Law; Art |
| October 18 (Saturday) | Liberal Arts; Business |
| October 19 (Sunday) | Engineering |

The test comprises eight items, including height and weight, lung capacity, eyesight, a 50-meter sprint, standing long jump, pull-ups (men) or one-minute sit-ups (women), and a 1000-meter (men) or 800-meter (women) run. All eight items must be completed.

## Key Reminders

The Physical Education Department has reminded students of the following:

- All eight scores are required; missing any one item invalidates all other results, which will be treated as an incomplete test. Students should confirm their scores are recorded in the teacher's register;
- Students must attend strictly at their assigned time; those listed but absent without reason will face further action by the university;
- Class monitors should organize their classes, ideally in student-ID order, for greater efficiency;
- Sportswear and athletic shoes are required, and students should warm up beforehand to prevent injury.

Students with physical disabilities, chronic illnesses, heart conditions, congenital developmental disorders or other conditions making them unfit for testing may apply for exemption by submitting the exemption application form with supporting medical documentation into the drop box at the gymnasium office before October 12.

According to the department, test data must be reported to the Ministry of Education in late October. As the ministry is stepping up verification of nationwide submissions, all undergraduates are encouraged to cooperate to ensure the smooth completion of this year's fitness testing.
