It is reported that Guangzhou and other areas have entered a period of heightened dengue fever activity. Shantou University Hospital has reminded students and staff to understand how the disease spreads, remove standing water, and take personal precautions.

Dengue fever is transmitted by the bite of an infected female mosquito and is not spread directly through contact between people. Common symptoms include severe headache, pain behind the eyes, bone, joint or muscle pain, a rash after several days of fever, and generalized itching. Some patients may also experience nausea, vomiting, or bleeding.

The hospital recommends removing standing water indoors and outdoors and cleaning, changing, or covering water containers. People should use mosquito repellent on exposed skin, wear long sleeves and trousers where possible, and use mosquito nets or other protection while sleeping. Anyone with suspected symptoms should visit a hospital within 24 hours.
