Shantou University's Graduate School has announced the 2014 National Day class schedule adjustments for graduate students on the main campus. Under the relevant university Party and government office arrangement, classes on Monday, October 6, will be exchanged with those on Sunday, September 28. Classes on Tuesday, October 7, will be exchanged with those on Saturday, October 11.

The arrangement applies to graduate students on the main campus. Faculty and students should adjust their teaching and study plans accordingly.
