It is reported that the Mutual Learning Center (SLC) of the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on September 22, 2014, to recruit tutors for the fall 2014 semester.

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Undergraduate students at the main campus (second year and above) |
| Criteria | Strong sense of responsibility, friendly attitude and good communication; excellent academic performance, with grades in the course to be tutored ranking within the top 20% (exceptions require proof from the instructor) |

## Responsibilities

Tutors are expected to foster a culture of self-directed learning, share study methods and experiences, answer questions patiently, organize academic discussions in the discipline and provide tailored support based on students’ progress and needs. The work period is generally weeks 3 to 16 of the semester.

## Benefits and Application

Tutors are paid according to the university’s work-study standards.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | September 28, 2014 |
| How to apply | Log in to the Student Work-Study Application System (http://qgjx.stu.edu.cn) and submit the application with detailed contact information |
| Inquiries | Contact the SLC student liaison for your school; the list of courses and liaisons is in the OA attachment |
| SLC contacts | Phone 86502250, email slc@stu.edu.cn, website http://slc.stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office, Mutual Learning Center |

> ⚠️ The list of courses and liaison contacts is available on the OA system.
