Shantou University Library has uploaded orientation materials to help 2014 freshmen learn about the library's resources and services.

The sessions were held for undergraduates in the auditorium on September 11 and for graduate students in the Science and Technology Lecture Hall on September 15. Faculty and students can download the materials through the library website's “Services and Guides — Library Resource Use Guide” section or follow the links in the original OA notice.
