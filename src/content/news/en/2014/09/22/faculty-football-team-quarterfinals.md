It is reported that the Shantou University faculty football team won every group-stage match at the Shantou Workers' Games and advanced to the quarterfinals as group winners.

The knockout schedule is as follows: Shantou University will play Shantou Customs at 3:30 p.m. on September 22, followed by a semifinal or fifth-to-eighth-place playoff at 5 p.m. Fifth-to-sixth- and seventh-to-eighth-place playoffs will be held at 3:30 p.m. on September 23, with the final and third-place match scheduled for 4:30 p.m. Final pairings remain subject to the competition's on-site arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
