Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has announced the National Day holiday and class adjustments for undergraduates on the main campus. The holiday will run from October 1 to 7, with workdays on Sunday, September 28, and Saturday, October 11.

Classes on Monday, October 6, will be exchanged with those on Sunday, September 28. Classes on Tuesday, October 7, will be exchanged with those on Saturday, October 11. Main-campus undergraduates should attend classes according to this arrangement.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
