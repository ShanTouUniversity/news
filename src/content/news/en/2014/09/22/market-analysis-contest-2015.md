It is reported that the Business School of Shantou University is selecting participants for the 2015 Cross-Strait College Student Market Analysis Competition. Applications are open to all undergraduates — especially sophomores — to gain competition experience and aim for strong results next year.

## Competition Schedule

| Stage | Time | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic preliminary round | May 2015 | Mainland China |
| Cross-Strait competition | August 2015 | Taiwan |

## Selection Details

As the competition centers on market research, the Business School encourages interested students to take or audit the course *Market Research* and participate in its class projects. Five to six spots will be reserved for students who are not marketing majors.

A detailed briefing will also be given during the *Market Research* class on Thursday (periods 6–7) in Classroom G304, and interested students are welcome to attend.

## Contact Information

Mr. Huang Wangming, the faculty member in charge of the selection, noted that students may reach out with any questions (Email: wmwong@stu.edu.cn, office phone: 8650-2786).
