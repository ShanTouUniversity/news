The Shantou University Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Office of Property and Resources Management reported that some student dormitories had not paid utility fees through August 2014 as of September 22. The university will cut power to dormitories with outstanding balances from September 23 and restore service after payment is completed.

From September 23 to 25, the Network Center will process payments through campus cards twice daily. Dormitories paid at 10:30 a.m. are expected to have power restored between 2:30 and 4:30 p.m.; those paid at 4:30 p.m. are expected to have service restored between 5:30 and 8 p.m. Payments made after 4:30 p.m. will be followed by restoration between 2:30 and 4:30 p.m. the next day. From September 25 onward, one payment run will take place daily at 4:30 p.m.

Students should top up campus cards when balances are insufficient. Students who have lost a campus card may use the utility management system's delegated-payment function. The university does not accept cash at the Finance Office; the campus system and OA notice provide the applicable payment instructions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office, Student Affairs Office, and Office of Property and Resources Management)
