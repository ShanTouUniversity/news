The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education has released the review results for provincial experimental teaching demonstration centers and construction projects launched between 2004 and 2010. Six Shantou University projects, including the Basic Medical Experimental Teaching Demonstration Center, passed the review.

The approved projects have been recognized as provincial experimental teaching demonstration centers for five years and will be reassessed at the end of that period. The university will continue supporting their development, improve operating systems, coordinate experimental teaching funds, and ensure operating support. The centers will also continue improving teaching conditions and reforms and prepare subsequent construction plans.
