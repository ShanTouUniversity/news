MYSTUdio and the Yangtze College of Art and Design at Shantou University will hold a Hakitzu Robot Battle Game Night from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on Friday, September 26, at MYSTUdio on the first floor of the library.

Hakitzu is a game in which players use programming to control robots in battle. Participants must have downloaded the game and be able to play it. The event is limited to 16 participants. The organizers will announce the winning team at the end of the event and present prizes to two outstanding participants. A short training session is expected on September 24.

Registration is open from September 22 to 23 through the MYSTU event page. Participants who receive a confirmation email should check in at MYSTUdio at 7:15 p.m. on the event day. The MYSTU event page remains the reference for registration details.
