It is reported that Shantou University Library has extended the trial period for the Euromonitor Passport economic and business database through December 31, 2014. Faculty and students may access the database through the library's database services page.

Passport covers more than 200 countries and provides business information at the national and regional, industry, and company levels. It can be used to study business environments, industry trends, and competitive landscapes.

The library's subject services team welcomes feedback on the trial. Login credentials are not reproduced in this article; readers should consult the original OA notice or the library for access information.
