Shantou University has opened applications for national student loans for undergraduates in 2014–2015. An information session for new students will be held at 2:30 p.m. on September 24 in Lecture Room E101.

Students planning to apply must log in to the student affairs system by September 26 to submit both a financial-need certification application and a loan application. Supporting materials include the loan application form, account inquiry documents, a household financial circumstances form, identification copies for the student and parents, a student-card copy, and a debit-card copy. Materials should be prepared as required and submitted to the counselor of the relevant college or residential college. Students who have not completed all documents may submit the system application first and provide the remaining materials by the deadlines in the notice.

Colleges and residential colleges will conduct an initial review from September 28 to 30, and counselors must complete system reviews by October 24. Reviewed materials will be submitted to the Student Aid Management Center from October 27 to 30 according to the published schedule. Students with a home-region loan may not apply for another national student loan, while students who submitted materials in the previous academic year do not need to submit them again.

Applicants must complete both applications in the system and ensure that all materials are accurate, complete, and free of unauthorized changes. Detailed requirements and attachments are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ Loan application templates and attachments are available on the OA system.
