The Shantou University labor union plans to establish a faculty and staff vocal society to promote exchange among vocal music enthusiasts and improve singing skills. The society is open to in-service employees who have an interest in vocal music, a basic foundation, and a willingness to follow its rules and participate in activities.

The society plans to accept 20 members. If applications exceed the limit, an instructor will select members through interviews. Eligible employees should complete the application form and send it to the university labor union's designated mailbox by September 27, using “Vocal Society Application” as the subject. The application form and related materials are available in the original OA notice.
