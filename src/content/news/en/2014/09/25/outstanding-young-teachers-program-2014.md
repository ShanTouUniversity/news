Shantou University has opened its 2014 recommendations for the Guangdong program for outstanding young university teachers and may recommend eight candidates. Applicants are generally required to hold a higher-education teaching qualification, a doctoral degree and a senior professional title at associate level or above, be no older than 35, and demonstrate strong potential in teaching, research or social service.

Eligible young faculty members must prepare an application and supporting documents, including evidence of identity, education, professional title, teaching qualification, publications, projects and awards. Each unit may recommend no more than two candidates. Materials are due at the Personnel Office, Administrative Center Room 315, by September 29, 2014.

Detailed requirements and forms are provided in the OA attachments.
