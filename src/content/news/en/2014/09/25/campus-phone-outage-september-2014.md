Shantou University reported that telephone lines beginning with 8290 remained unavailable as of September 25, 2014, following construction to splice and relocate underground telecommunications lines near the former campus gate. A recovery time had not yet been announced.

For business inquiries with the Academic Exchange Service Center, callers may use 8650 2666 (front desk), 8650 2668, or 8650 4138 (office). The Resources Management Office advised relevant units to make alternative communication arrangements and apologized for the inconvenience.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
