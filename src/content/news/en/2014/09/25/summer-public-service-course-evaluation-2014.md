Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has launched an evaluation of public-service courses offered in the 2014–2015 summer term to review teaching and support quality improvement.

## Evaluation Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluation period | September 25 to October 17, 2014 |
| Participants | Students enrolled in the relevant summer-term public-service courses |
| Method | Log in to the credit system, open “Course Evaluation,” and complete the listed courses |

Students must complete the evaluation within the stated period. Those who miss the deadline must submit a written explanation and otherwise will be unable to view their public-service course grades.
