Shantou University’s College of Science has adjusted the schedule for the Toward the Ocean public-service course in the 2014–2015 autumn term. Because the Friday of Week 2 falls during the National Day holiday, the course will be postponed by one week and run from Week 3 through Week 10. Most classroom sessions will take place in Room E404.

## Course Schedule

| Date | Time and venue | Main content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October 10 | 14:00–15:50, E404 | Chinese people’s awareness of maritime territory |
| October 17 | 14:00–15:50, E404 | Public-service learning, project design, and social-science research methods |
| October 24 | 14:00–15:50, E404 | Overview of marine life |
| October 31 | 14:00–15:50, E404 | News photography, reporting, and short-film production |
| November 7 | 14:00–15:50, E404 | Project presentations and review |
| November 14 and 21 | 14:00–15:50, E404 | Polar biodiversity and protection; coastal ecological protection |
| November 15–16 | 08:00–18:00, Nan’ao Island | Team building, coastal fieldwork, environmental action, and social surveys |
| November 22–23 | 08:00–18:00, Haojiang District | School outreach, fishing-village visits, and fieldwork |
| November 28 | 14:00–15:50, E404 | Summary, evaluation, and experience sharing |

The course team noted that any changes to the time or venue should be reported to the Academic Affairs Office in advance.
