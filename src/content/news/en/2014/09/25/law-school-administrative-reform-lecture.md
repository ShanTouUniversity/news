Shantou University's Law School will host a lecture titled “Legal Reflections on Administrative System Reform” on September 26, 2014. Shi Youqi, vice president and law professor at Guangdong University of Foreign Studies, will give the lecture. Shi's research focuses on administrative law and administrative litigation; he has been selected for the National New Century Talent Program and nominated for the seventh National Outstanding Young Jurist award.
