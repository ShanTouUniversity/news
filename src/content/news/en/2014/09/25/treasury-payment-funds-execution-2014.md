Shantou University's Finance Office has asked units on the main campus to accelerate execution of centrally managed treasury payments in order to improve the use of public funds and balance budget execution.

Project leaders are required to speed up spending from the relevant funds. Special treasury funds remaining from 2013 and earlier years will be returned by the finance authorities if payment is not processed by mid-December.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office)
