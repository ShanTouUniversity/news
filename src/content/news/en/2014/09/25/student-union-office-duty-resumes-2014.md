Shantou University’s Student Union Office resumed weekday duty service on September 25, 2014. Staff are available from 17:00 to 18:00, Monday through Friday, in Room 838 of Building 8. The office also released procedures for student organizations requesting posters and materials.

Posters for the G residence buildings may not exceed 40 × 60 centimeters, while posters for the Second Dining Hall and the ABCDEF residence buildings may not exceed 70 × 100 centimeters. Material request forms must be prepared in duplicate, with one copy kept by the Student Union Office and one by the requesting organization. Organizations borrowing or returning materials outside duty hours must call the staff one hour in advance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and Shantou University Student Union)
