Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has relayed a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education that a new application approval form will be used for the 2014 National Encouragement Scholarship. Applicants must download and print the new form and submit it to their school counselor by 12:00 on October 8, 2014.

The office has also provided a sample personal statement to help National Scholarship applicants prepare their materials. The new form and sample are available in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The new application form and personal-statement sample are available on the OA system.
