Shantou University’s Business School will host its fifth MBA lecture on September 27, 2014. Professor Ye Risong, a distinguished professor at the Business School, will speak on “China’s Outward Investment.” The lecture will examine the rapid growth of Chinese outward investment from the perspectives of firms and individuals, industries and countries, and the global system, and will conclude with implications for MBA students’ career planning.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | China’s Outward Investment |
| Speaker | Professor Ye Risong |
| Time | 19:30–21:30, September 27, 2014 |
| Organizer | Shantou University Business School |

Ye holds a PhD in management from Temple University and has taught at universities in China and abroad. His teaching and research interests include international business, strategic management, organizational behavior, and cross-cultural management.
