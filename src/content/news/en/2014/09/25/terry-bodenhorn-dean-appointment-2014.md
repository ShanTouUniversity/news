It is reported that Shantou University issued Document Shan Da Fa [2014] No. 111 on September 25, announcing a personnel appointment.

## Appointment Decision

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Document No. | Shan Da Fa [2014] No. 111 |
| Deliberation | 10th University Leadership Meeting on June 24, 2014 |
| Position | Dean, College of Liberal Arts |
| Appointee | Terry Bodenhorn |
| Issuing unit | Shantou University |
| Issue date | September 25, 2014 |

The university has decided to appoint Terry Bodenhorn as Dean of the College of Liberal Arts.
