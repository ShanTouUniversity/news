Shantou University's Infrastructure Office has announced the results of bidding for two renovation projects following a review by the university's bidding and tendering leadership group. Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. won the renovation of the Student Activities Room in Youth League Building 8 for 86,098.20 yuan. Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. won the laboratory floor renovation project for the School of Engineering's Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering for 87,117.44 yuan.

The results are open for public comment from September 25 through September 28, 2014. Contact details for inquiries and complaints are provided in the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Resource Management and Infrastructure Office)
