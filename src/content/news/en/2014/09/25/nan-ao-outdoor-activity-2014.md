Shantou University's Student Affairs Office and Snow Leopard Outdoor Association will hold an introductory outdoor program in Nan’ao from October 5 to 6, 2014. The program will include outdoor camping and team-building activities and will accept up to 32 participants from across the university, including 30 students and two faculty members. A draw will be used if applications exceed capacity.

Applications close at 12:00 on September 28, with results announced that day. Participants must attend a briefing on October 4 and follow the coaches' instructions throughout the program. The organizers will cover round-trip transportation and camping equipment; participants will pay a combined 50 yuan for insurance and meals during the two-day, one-night program.

Applicants must complete the form and email it to the Snow Leopard Outdoor Association's designated address. The form and detailed requirements are provided in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The application form and event attachment are available on the OA system.
