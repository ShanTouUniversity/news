It is reported that the Graduate School issued a notice on September 9, 2014, to launch the review for the 2014 National Scholarship for postgraduate students.

## Review Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Full-time postgraduate students with Chinese nationality and STU status; graduating students in the current year are not eligible |
| Award | 30,000 yuan per doctoral student per year; 20,000 yuan per master’s student per year, funded by the central government |
| Application period | September 6–17:30, September 18, 2014 |
| How to apply | Apply via the Student Affairs System, print the *Application Form for National Scholarship for Postgraduates* double-sided, sign with a black pen, and submit with supporting materials to the college review committee by September 18 |
| Procedure | Individual application → college committee preliminary review and public notice (no less than 5 working days) → university leading group review and public notice (no less than 5 working days) → submission to the provincial department |
| Materials to submit | Summary tables of doctoral/master’s awardees, research & awards, application forms, review rules and report, etc. |
| Contacts | Wang Fengqi, Huang Meiping, phone 86504000 (short: 884000), email mphuang@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Graduate School |

Eligibility also requires support for the Party’s leadership, observance of laws and university regulations, good moral character, excellent academic performance and strong research ability.

> ⚠️ Application forms and related tables are available on the OA system.
