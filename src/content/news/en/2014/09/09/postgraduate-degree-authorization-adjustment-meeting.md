Shantou University’s Graduate School announced that the university will hold a meeting on the dynamic adjustment of doctoral, master’s and professional degree-authorization categories at 15:00 on September 10, 2014, in Room 213 of the Administration Center.

The meeting will convey relevant documents from the Academic Degrees Committee of the State Council and the Guangdong Academic Degrees Committee, and arrange the university’s follow-up work. Heads responsible for postgraduate affairs and discipline leaders from training units are expected to attend; the Medical College will send a representative.
