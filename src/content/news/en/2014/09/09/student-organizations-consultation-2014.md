The Communist Youth League Committee and Student Union of Shantou University will hold a consultation fair for university-level student organizations from 10:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. on September 10 at the Golden Triangle.

The Student Union, Student Associations Federation, Youth Volunteers Association, Student Youth newspaper editorial office, Student Association for Science and Technology, and “Chua—Road to Success” network would introduce their organizations and recruit members on site. Freshmen were invited to attend and participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
