It is reported that Shantou University issued Document No. 96 [2014] on September 9, 2014, to release three regulatory documents.

Following deliberation at the 7th university leadership meeting on May 13, 2014, the *Measures for the Administration of Undergraduate International Exchange Students*, the *Interim Measures for Scholarships for Students Studying Abroad* and the *Measures for the Selection of Undergraduate Exchange Students* are now promulgated for implementation.

| --- | --- |
| Document | STU Document [2014] No. 96 |
| Approved at | 7th leadership meeting, May 13, 2014 |
| Documents released | Administration of undergraduate international exchange students; scholarships for overseas study; selection of undergraduate exchange students |
