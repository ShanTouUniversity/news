Shantou University published the bid result for the classroom renovation project at the Changjiang School of Art and Design. Following the recommendation of the university’s tendering leadership group, Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. was selected as the contractor, with a winning bid of RMB 123,469.17.

The public-notice period ran from September 9 through 11. The tendering party was the university’s Infrastructure Office, reachable at +86 754 8650 2490 by phone or +86 754 8650 2314 by fax. Complaints were handled by the university’s Supervision and Audit Office at +86 754 8650 2346 by phone or +86 754 8650 0536 by fax.
