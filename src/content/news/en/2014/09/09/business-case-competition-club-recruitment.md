The International Business Case Competition Club (IBCC) of the Business School announced recruitment for Shantou University’s 2014 freshmen on September 9, 2014.

## Interview Schedule

| Date | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 10 | 14:00–17:00 and 18:00–21:30 | Seminar Room, second floor of the Business School |
| September 12 | 8:00–12:00 and 14:00–17:00 | E207 |
| September 13 | 8:00–12:00 and 14:00–17:00 | E407 |

The club said it provides opportunities for members to discuss business topics, take part in competitions and develop practical skills. Detailed registration materials and arrangements are available in the original notice attachments.

> ⚠️ The registration form and event attachments are available on the OA system.
