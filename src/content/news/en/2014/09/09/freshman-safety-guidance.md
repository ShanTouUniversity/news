The Security Office of Shantou University issued safety guidance for incoming freshmen on September 9, 2014, urging them to protect personal property and learn how to seek emergency assistance.

## Key Reminders

The guidance covers protection against online and telephone fraud, theft in dormitories and public areas, and fire prevention related to electricity, open flames and laboratory work. Students are advised not to disclose personal information or bank details to strangers, transfer money to unknown accounts, use high-power or substandard appliances, or leave dormitory doors and windows unsecured.

The university also warned students not to swim, fish or play in the campus reservoir or other unfamiliar waters. During holidays, students should use legitimate transportation, travel with companions where possible and stay in contact with family members.

The campus 24-hour emergency numbers are 82904110 and 86504110. The local police station can be reached at 82540966.
