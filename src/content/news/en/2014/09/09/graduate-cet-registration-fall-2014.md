Shantou University’s Graduate School announced registration arrangements for the fall 2014 College English Test. The examinations were scheduled for December 20, with CET-4 from 9:00 to 11:25 and CET-6 from 15:00 to 17:25.

All current graduate students at the main campus could register for CET-4. CET-6 applicants needed a qualifying CET-4 result or an English major test certificate, among other conditions; repeat applicants could not register more than twice after reaching a score of 425. The fee was RMB 36. Graduate students had to use their campus-network accounts to access the graduate management platform, complete registration and pay online from September 9 to 21. Insufficient account funds would cause registration to fail.

Candidates were required to bring an identity card, graduate student card and admission card to the examination. Non-enrolled graduate students were not eligible for this registration round. Technical questions could be directed through the Graduate School’s listed office channels.
