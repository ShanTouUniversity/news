It is reported that the Student Affairs Office forwarded a notice on September 9, 2014, from the Provincial Department of Education to collect Teachers’ Day greetings under the theme “I Will Always Be Your Student — The Warmest Greetings for Teachers’ Day 2014.”

September 10 marks the 30th Teachers’ Day in China. To promote the tradition of respecting teachers, the “Guangdong Education” WeChat account invites submissions of heartfelt greetings to beloved teachers.

## Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | I Will Always Be Your Student — The Warmest Greetings for Teachers’ Day 2014 |
| Deadline | September 10, 2014 |
| How to submit | Leave a message on the “Guangdong Education” WeChat backend, or email gdjytwx@163.com |
| Eligible contributors | Anyone who is or has been a student |
| Content | Greetings should be creative, vibrant and heartfelt, around 100 words |
| Bonus | Including photos — such as old class photos with teachers or recent photos — is encouraged |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office (forwarded from the Department of Education) |

See the OA notice for format examples and submission notes. All faculty and students are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
