The Network Center and Office of Resource Management began verification of university units’ July 2014 office telephone bills. China Telecom Shantou and China Mobile Shantou had sent electronic bills to the university, and the data had been imported into the telephone management system. Unit reviewers could log in and check the landline bills from each provider.

Units with no objections were asked to confirm the bills in the system. Discrepancies had to be reported by September 12 to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn for China Telecom landlines or stunic@stu.edu.cn for China Mobile landlines. Bills would be deemed confirmed if no objection or confirmation was submitted by the deadline. After verification, the Finance Office would deduct the charges from each unit’s designated funding account on September 15.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network Center and Office of Resource Management)
