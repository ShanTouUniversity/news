The Academic Affairs Office published practical teaching workload records for the summer, autumn and spring terms of the 2013–2014 academic year on September 9, 2014. Teachers can log in to the credit system to review their records.

Questions or comments should be sent to the Academic Affairs Office by September 16. The contact number is 86503202.
