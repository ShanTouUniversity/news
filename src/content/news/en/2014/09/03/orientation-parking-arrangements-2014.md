Several open ground-floor areas at Shantou University will serve as reception points during the university's 2014 orientation, including the south, north and east sides of the Administration Center and areas below the Schools of Business and Liberal Arts.

Motorcycles, bicycles and electric bicycles may not be parked in these areas on September 6–7. Vehicle owners should move them to other parking areas near the Administration Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office, Student Affairs Office and Office of Resource Management)
