The first edition of Shantou University's exhibition of modern Chinese painting and calligraphy from its collection will open on September 4, 2014, in the Comprehensive Archives Room on the fourth floor of the Administration Center. It will be closed on public holidays.

The exhibition includes works by Rao Zongyi, Wu Nansheng, Liu Changchao, Guan Shanyue and Wang Lanruo, and is open to all faculty and students.
