It is reported that the Finance Office of Shantou University has published the university's education fee items and charging standards for 2014, in accordance with the Education Fee Disclosure System issued by the former State Development Planning Commission, Ministry of Finance and Ministry of Education (Ji Jiage [2002] No. 792), as well as the Implementation Rules for University Affairs Disclosure at Shantou University (Shanda Dangzi [2009] No. 27).

## Inquiry and Complaint Channels

| Channel | Contact |
| ------- | ------- |
| Municipal public service hotline | 12345 |
| Guangdong provincial price service hotline | 020-12358 |
| STU Finance Office telephone | 86502101 |

According to the announcement, the detailed fee schedule has been released as a spreadsheet attachment covering all education-related charges of the university. Students or members of the public who have objections to any fee item or standard may file complaints or make inquiries through the municipal service hotline, the provincial price service hotline, or the university's Finance Office.

The education fee disclosure system is designed to improve the transparency of university charges and safeguard the right of students and parents to be informed and to supervise.
