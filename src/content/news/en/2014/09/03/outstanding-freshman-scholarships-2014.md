Shantou University has opened its 2014 selection for outstanding freshman scholarships. The selection is for full-time undergraduate freshmen who listed STU as their first-choice institution and enrolled on schedule. Applicants must meet at least one criterion, such as scoring at least 30 points above the first-tier admission threshold in their province, earning a third prize or higher in a national or international competition during high school, or holding a national second-level athlete title or higher and meeting the required test standard.

The scholarships have two tiers: five first-prize awards of 20,000 yuan each and 25 second-prize awards of 5,000 yuan each. The university will also select 25 outstanding female students from outside Guangdong based on enrollment conditions and donation plans, with each receiving 5,000 yuan. These awards cannot be combined.

Applicants must submit an online application through the Student Affairs System by noon on September 10 and deliver the signed form and supporting materials, including award certificates and portfolios, to the Student Financial Aid Management Center. The university will assess academic performance and overall qualities through preliminary screening and interviews.
