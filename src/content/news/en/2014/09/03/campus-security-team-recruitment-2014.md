Shantou University's campus security team under the Office of Resource Management is recruiting several members from the public. The positions are auxiliary contract roles; the employment relationship will not be transferred to STU, and compensation will follow university standards.

Applicants should have sound professional conduct and good health, at least a junior-high-school education, be men aged 50 or younger, and be able to work 24-hour shifts. Experience in security or fire safety, or military service, is preferred. Duties include gate security, campus patrols, order maintenance and operation of fire-control and monitoring equipment.

Applicants must send a résumé and copies of relevant certificates and documents by email or mail to the campus security team by September 20. Candidates who pass the initial screening will be invited to an interview and must bring the originals for review. For inquiries, call 0754-86503439 or email o_xwd@stu.edu.cn. The office is at Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong.
