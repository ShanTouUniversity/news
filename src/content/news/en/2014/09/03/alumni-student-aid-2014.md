Shantou University has established its 2014 Alumni Student Aid Fund for 50 financially disadvantaged students. The fund will support 15 freshmen and 35 continuing students, with each recipient receiving 2,500 yuan. Alumni donated 125,000 yuan to establish the fund.

Freshmen must apply through their school or college youth league by 5 p.m. on September 11. After review and public notice, schools and colleges must submit recommended lists and application forms to the Student Financial Aid Management Center by 5 p.m. on September 15. The deadline for continuing students to apply is 5 p.m. on September 26, and their schools or colleges must submit materials by 5 p.m. on September 30. Reviews will consider financial circumstances, conduct, academic performance and compliance with university rules.

Recipients of the 2013 Alumni Student Aid Fund must also submit a summary of their study and daily life during the previous academic year by 5 p.m. on September 30. Full application materials and allocation quotas are available in the attachments to the original STU OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office and Alumni Affairs Office)
