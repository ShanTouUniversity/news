Shantou University has announced registration arrangements for its fall 2014 Mandarin proficiency test. From September 3 to 14, candidates must use the Guangdong Mandarin Proficiency Test administration system, select the Shantou Vocational and Technical College testing station and the session listed as the 2014 STU fall test, then complete the online form and print the registration form.

Candidates must bring the form to the Academic Affairs Office service window on the third floor of the Administration Center between 2:30 and 5:30 p.m. on September 17 and pay the 85-yuan training and testing fee. The training and test dates and location will be announced after the registration data are reviewed.

Because the online form contains personal information, candidates should check it carefully before submission. The form cannot be printed after the system closes, and submitted information cannot be edited online. Full instructions and the registration link are available in the original STU OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
