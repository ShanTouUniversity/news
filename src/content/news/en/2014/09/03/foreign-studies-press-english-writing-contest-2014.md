Shantou University has opened its campus selection for the FLTRP English Writing Contest to currently enrolled undergraduates with Chinese nationality. Participants must register on both Pigai and the FLTRP writing contest website. Registration closes on September 25, and the selection will be held on October 12; the exact time and location will be announced separately.

The contest uses timed online writing. Participants submit two works within the allotted period, choosing two of three formats: narrative, argumentative or expository writing. Automated scoring and judge scoring each account for 50 percent of the result. Participants may not use online resources or electronic devices other than the contest platform, and all work must be original.

Two special prizes will be awarded, while first-, second- and third-prize winners will account for 1, 3 and 6 percent of the total participants, respectively. Special-prize winners will represent STU at the Guangdong university English writing final and the Guangdong round of the national contest. All winners will receive certificates and prizes.

The registration links and full rules are available in the attachments to the original STU OA notice.
