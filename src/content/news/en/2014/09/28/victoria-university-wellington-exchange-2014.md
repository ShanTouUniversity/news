It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts of Shantou University issued a notice on September 28, 2014, announcing the candidate for the Shantou University–Victoria University of Wellington (New Zealand) exchange program. Supported by the China Scholarship Council under its 2014 Excellent Undergraduate International Exchange Program, the program will send outstanding undergraduates to Victoria University of Wellington for the spring semester of the 2014–2015 academic year.

## Selection and Announcement

Following voluntary application, selection, college review and reconsideration, and university-level review under the principles of fairness, openness, impartiality and merit-based selection, one candidate met the recommendation criteria and is now announced for public comment. The name is anonymized to protect student privacy.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Host university | Victoria University of Wellington, New Zealand |
| Exchange period | Spring semester, 2014–2015 academic year |
| Funding program | CSC Excellent Undergraduate International Exchange Program 2014 |
| Number of candidates | 1 |
| Candidate | Lu F, female, Class of 2012, English major |
| Public comment period | September 28–30, 2014 |
