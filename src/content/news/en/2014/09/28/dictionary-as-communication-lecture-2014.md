The 137th New Century Humanities Forum at Shantou University will take place from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. on September 30 in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center. Yong Heming, vice president and second-level professor at Guangdong University of Finance, will speak on “The Dictionary as Communication — Exploring the nature of the dictionary and its making.”

Yong is an academic leader in English language and literature and a doctoral supervisor at Soochow University. His research has focused on lexicography, and he has studied or conducted visiting research at universities overseas and published multiple academic works.
