Shantou University is recruiting two residential-area counselors for the Student Affairs Office and one cash cashier for the Finance Office from its existing administrative staff. Eligible applicants include in-system and agency administrative staff in the Publicity Department, Student Affairs Office and Youth League Committee, Law School, Business School, College of Arts and Design, School of Journalism and Communication, Institute of Higher Education, Sports Department, Library, and Network and Information Center.

## Application arrangements

Applicants must email the Shantou University Public Recruitment Application Form to recruitment@stu.edu.cn by October 13 and identify the position in the subject line. Candidates will be selected for interviews after a qualification review, with interview arrangements to be announced separately. Employment arrangements and compensation will follow university policies.
