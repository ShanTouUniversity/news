Shantou University Library has featured two new books edited by Li Dan: *The Unrestricted Mind: A Collection of Shantou University Advanced Outdoor Programs* and *Polar Academy: Shantou University Students in Antarctica*.

*The Unrestricted Mind* documents the university’s advanced outdoor programs from 2009 to 2012, covering challenges, nature, humanities and personal growth. It was published by Shantou University Press in June 2013.

*Polar Academy* introduces STU’s Antarctic expedition project. The university’s Antarctic expedition team conducted a 21-day expedition from February 14 to March 6, 2014. The book was published by Shantou University Press in July 2014.
