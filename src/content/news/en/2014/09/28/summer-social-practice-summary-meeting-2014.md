Shantou University will hold a review and recommendation meeting for its 2014 student volunteer summer “Three Rural Areas” social practice projects at 3 p.m. on September 29 in Meeting Room 118 of the Administrative Center. The meeting is for teachers who applied to serve as instructors for 2014 Guangdong student summer social practice teams and will include project summaries and sharing.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
