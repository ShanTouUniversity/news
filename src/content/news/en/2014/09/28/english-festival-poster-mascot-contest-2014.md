Shantou University’s English Language Center will hold the 2014 English Festival from December 5 to 7 and is inviting student teams to design its promotional poster and mascot.

Teams must register by emailing englishfestival@stu.edu.cn by October 3. Designs are due on October 17. The winning team’s work will be used to promote the festival, and the team will receive a certificate and a cash prize.

> Source: STU OA Notice (English Language Center)
