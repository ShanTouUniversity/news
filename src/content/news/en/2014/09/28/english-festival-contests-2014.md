Shantou University’s 2014 English Festival will run from December 5 to 7 and feature speech, singing and debate contests. The English Language Center is recruiting STU students for the speech and singing contests.

## Contest information

- The Intercollegiate English Speech Contest uses “Our Hidden Heroes” as its theme. Entrants must submit a speech of no more than three minutes by 6 p.m. on October 24.
- The Intercollegiate English Singing Contest preliminary round will begin at 3 p.m. on November 1 at the English Activities Center. Entrants may compete alone or in pairs and will sing for one minute; finalists will compete in the semifinal round on November 22.
- Questions may be directed to dianat@stu.edu.cn for the speech contest or elizabethl@stu.edu.cn for the singing contest, or raised at the English Activities Center.
