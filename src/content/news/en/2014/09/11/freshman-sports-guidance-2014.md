To support the arrival of the 2014 freshmen, Shantou University’s Sports Department arranged exercise guidance from 17:00 to 18:00 each day from September 11 to 14, 2014. Activities include table tennis, badminton, swimming, football and basketball.

The activities will take place at the gym, swimming pool, football field and outdoor basketball court. Consultation points staffed by sports teachers and student organization members will provide guidance, and freshmen may participate with the experience voucher in *New Mileage*.

Participants should wear sports clothing and shoes and follow venue rules. Swimmers must take appropriate safety precautions, and non-swimmers may not enter the deep-water area.
