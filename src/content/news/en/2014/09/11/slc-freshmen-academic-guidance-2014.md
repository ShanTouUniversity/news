It is reported that Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) will begin providing academic adaptation guidance for freshmen on September 14, 2014. The fixed session schedule for each school is as follows:

## Session Schedule

| School | Date | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School of Engineering | September 14 | 8:30–12:00 |
| School of Law | September 14 | 14:30–17:00 |
| Business School | September 15 | 14:30–18:00 |
| College of Liberal Arts | September 16 | 14:30–16:00 |
| School of Science | September 16 | 16:00–18:00 |
| Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication | September 17 | 14:30–16:00 |
| Cheung Kong School of Art and Design | September 17 | 16:00–18:00 |

During the university’s course registration and add-drop periods, peer tutors from the guidance program will provide freshmen with course-selection guidance at the SLC. Freshmen may register in advance at the SLC front desk to book one-to-one tutoring with a peer tutor or use an SLC computer. The SLC also welcomes freshmen to consult about academic matters, and all services are free of charge.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
