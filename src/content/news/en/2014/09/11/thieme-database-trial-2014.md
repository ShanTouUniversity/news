Shantou University Library announced trial access to the Thieme chemistry and pharmacy e-journal database for faculty and students through November 9, 2014.

Founded in 1886, Thieme publishes materials focused on medicine and organic synthetic chemistry. Users can access the trial through the library’s database information page and send feedback to the library’s subject services team.

Access page: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=394>
