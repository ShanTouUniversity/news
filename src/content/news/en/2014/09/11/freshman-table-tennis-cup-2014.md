Shantou University’s Athletics Department will hold the 2014 Freshman Table Tennis Cup at the campus gym from **2 p.m. to 6 p.m. on September 20–21**. The Student Table Tennis Association will organize the tournament, which is open to new undergraduate and graduate students.

The competition will feature men’s and women’s singles, with group round-robin play followed by knockout rounds. The top eight finishers in each event will receive awards. Registration closes at **11 p.m. on September 17**. Participants should bring their own paddles and drinking water and wear suitable sports clothing and shoes.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Athletics Department)
