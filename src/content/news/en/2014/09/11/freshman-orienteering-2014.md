Shantou University’s Youth League Committee and Student Associations Union will hold the 2014 freshman orienteering event from **1:30 p.m. to 5 p.m. daily, September 14–17**. It is open to undergraduate newcomers except those at the Medical College; participants will gather at the gym before taking part.

Participants should wear sports clothing and shoes and bring water. Organizers advised students to monitor the weather and prepare accordingly. Colleges were scheduled to collect event armbands from the Youth League Committee office on September 12. Students at Zhicheng College were to collect theirs from their academic schools.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee)
