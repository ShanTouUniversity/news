Shantou University’s Property Management Office plans to spray green areas, plants, fruit trees and drains across campus and at the Continuing Education College from September 13 to 19, 2014, to control plant pests and improve the campus environment. Faculty, staff and students are advised to take precautions during the work.

The work will be carried out by area on different days, covering locations including the Academic Exchange Center, student residential areas, the affiliated schools, the Medical Building, the New Teaching Building, the canteen, the Administration Center, the artificial lake, sports grounds, the Continuing Education College and Longquanyuan residential area.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Property Management Office)
