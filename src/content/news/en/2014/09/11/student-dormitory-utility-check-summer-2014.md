Shantou University’s Property Management Office published the summer student-dormitory utility review and payment schedule on September 11, 2014. Charges were calculated from meter readings taken on July 1, July 14, August 20 and September 5, and students can check their records through the university utility management system.

Students who identify an error may complete and sign a review application and submit it during working hours from September 12 to 17. Regular payment will be collected through campus cards from September 18 to 22; unpaid charges will be handled under university rules from September 23. Corrections requested after the review period may take three to five working days.

> ⚠️ Utility details and review forms contain personal accommodation and payment information and are available in the original OA notice or utility management system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Property Management Office)
