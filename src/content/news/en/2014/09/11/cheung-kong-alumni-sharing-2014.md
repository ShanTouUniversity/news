Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will host an alumni sharing session at **2 p.m. on September 12** in the school’s tiered lecture hall on the first floor. Zhong Xinqiu, a 1999 graduate, will speak on “Changing Value.” The event is open to the university community.
