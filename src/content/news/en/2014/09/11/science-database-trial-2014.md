Shantou University Library announced trial access to the Science database for faculty and students through November 11, 2014.

Science is published by the American Association for the Advancement of Science. Related publications include *Science*, *Science Translational Medicine* and *Science Signaling*. Users can access the trial through the library’s database information page and send feedback to the library’s subject services team.

Access page: <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=395>
