MYSTUdio and Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design will hold the “Creative Hackathon: Interactive Instruments” from **10 p.m. on September 19 to 10 a.m. on September 21** at the former Student Activities Hall on the first floor of the library. Teams will have 36 hours to design and build a conceptual interactive instrument.

Teams of three or four students from different schools will work from randomly assigned themes and receive starter kits. The event will accept the first 40 successful registrations. Registration closes on **September 16**, and participants should check in at **6:30 p.m. on September 19**. Some prize details in the source text are corrupted and are not reproduced.
