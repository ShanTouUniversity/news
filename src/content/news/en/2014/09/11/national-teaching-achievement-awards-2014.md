Two Shantou University projects received awards in the Ministry of Education’s 2014 National Teaching Achievement Awards. The project “Comprehensive Reform and Practice in the Education of Outstanding Doctors from an International Perspective,” led by Professor Yang Mianhua and colleagues at the Medical College, won first prize. The Engineering College project “Building and Promoting China’s New CDIO Engineering Education Model,” led by Professor Bao Nengsheng and colleagues, won second prize.

Guangdong universities received 18 awards that year: two first prizes and 16 second prizes. The National Teaching Achievement Awards are held every four years to recognize work in higher-education reform and talent development.
