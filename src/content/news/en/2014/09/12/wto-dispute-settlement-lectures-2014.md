Shantou University’s School of Law will host two lectures by Professor Zhang Yuejiao, a member of the World Trade Organization’s Appellate Body. The first, on international investment and trade dispute resolution, is scheduled for **7:30–8:30 p.m. on September 13** in Lecture Hall 3. The second, on current WTO dispute settlement and legal issues, will take place **11 a.m.–noon on September 14** in ACC Room 3.

Zhang formerly directed the Treaty and Law Department of China’s Ministry of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation and held legal posts at the Asian Development Bank and World Bank. Both lectures are open to the university community.
