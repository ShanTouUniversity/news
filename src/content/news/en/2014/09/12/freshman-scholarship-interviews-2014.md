Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold interviews for the 2014 outstanding freshman scholarships at **2:30 p.m. on September 15** in Room 118 of the Administration Center. The interview format includes brief self-introductions, a randomly assigned discussion topic, a 10-minute group discussion, and questions from the panel.

The detailed schedule is in an attachment. To protect student privacy, candidate names and individual time slots are not reproduced.
