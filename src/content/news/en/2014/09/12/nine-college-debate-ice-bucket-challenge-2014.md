Shantou University’s 2014 Nine-College Star Debate will be held from **7:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. on September 14** in the Science Lecture Hall. Representatives of debate teams from the university’s nine colleges will discuss whether the Ice Bucket Challenge should be encouraged.

Each side will have four debaters. The format includes opening arguments, questioning, open debate, closing statements, and a final head-to-head round. Judges will select the winning team based on its performance. The event is organized by the university’s Youth League Committee and Student Union.
