Shantou University’s Business School opened campus recruitment for the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants’ 2014 Qualification Programme (QP) Case Analysis Competition. The English-language competition is open to full-time undergraduates. Each team must comprise four students from the same university, and each student may join only one team.

Registration is free. The champion, runner-up, and third-place teams are eligible for cash awards of HK$10,000, HK$6,000, and HK$4,000, respectively, along with QP fee waivers, trophies, and certificates. Additional awards recognize written reports, speakers, and outstanding teams. Online registration closes on **October 15**. Student personal contact details in the notice are not reproduced.
