Shantou University’s News Center student reporter group has opened recruitment to students across the university. It is seeking writers, photographers, text editors, art editors, and new-media reporters and editors. The group contributes campus coverage to the university news website, newspaper, and social-media platforms; members can also take part in professional training and exchanges with other universities.

Applicants for the reporter and editing departments were asked to submit an electronic application by **September 22**. Work samples were optional and could include writing, photographs, feature proposals, or design work, depending on the role. New-media applicants could submit social-media posts. Interview times and locations were to be announced through the group’s official account. Student personal contact details in the notice are not reproduced.
