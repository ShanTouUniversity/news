Shantou University’s tenth leadership–student dinner, a session for the 2014 freshman cohort, is scheduled for **6 p.m. on September 23** in Room 201 at Hongtai Restaurant. University leaders, department representatives, and faculty members will meet with students. The event has 32 student places.

Full-time students entering in 2014 could apply voluntarily by **5 p.m. on September 16**. If applications exceeded capacity, student representatives would witness a lottery to select attendees. Applicant identities are not included.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
