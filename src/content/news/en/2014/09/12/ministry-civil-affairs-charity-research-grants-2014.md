Applications opened for the Ministry of Civil Affairs’ 2014 research projects on innovation and development in the charity sector. The program offers key projects with RMB 10,000 in funding and self-selected projects without funding. Topics include charity-sector oversight, links between charity and social security, asset management, information disclosure, and workforce development.

Applicants were to submit materials for an initial review by **September 22** and deliver printed applications to Shantou University’s Research Office by **September 26**. Successful projects were required to submit reports by December 31, 2014. Reports were expected to be at least 20,000 words, including at least 3,000 words of policy recommendations.
