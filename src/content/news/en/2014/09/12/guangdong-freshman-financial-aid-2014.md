Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office opened applications for provincial financial aid for first-year undergraduates entering in 2014. The program supports students from Guangdong facing financial hardship. Award amounts will be set according to assessed need and provincial review, up to **RMB 6,000 per student**. The freshman grant is a one-time award.

Applicants were asked to submit an application, a student-and-family circumstances form, and supporting materials to their college by **5 p.m. on September 18**. Colleges were to complete selection and public notice before sending materials to the Student Affairs Office by **5 p.m. on September 25**. The final aid list would be posted on campus for five working days. Personal application details are not reproduced.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The application and family circumstances forms are available as OA attachments.
