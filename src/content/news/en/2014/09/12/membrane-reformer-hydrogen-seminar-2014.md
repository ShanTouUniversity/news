Shantou University’s College of Science will host a “Science Salon” seminar from **3 p.m. to 5 p.m. on September 15** in ACC Room 3. Professor Moshe Sheintuch of the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology will speak on pure hydrogen production in membrane reformers, including demonstrations and macro- and atomic-scale modeling.
