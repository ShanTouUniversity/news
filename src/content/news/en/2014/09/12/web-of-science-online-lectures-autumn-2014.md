Shantou University Library highlighted the Web of Science Autumn 2014 online lecture series. The semester program includes 20 free online courses in which university experts will share research practices and workflows, offering researchers guidance on finding emerging topics and strengthening their research capabilities.

Course information and access are available on the [online lecture series page](http://ip-science.thomsonreuters.com.cn/WOSOnline/Autumn2014/). The original notice did not provide specific session dates, so none are added here.
