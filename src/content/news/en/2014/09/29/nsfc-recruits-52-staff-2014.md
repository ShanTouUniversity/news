It is reported that the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) is recruiting 52 mobile-position staff members nationwide due to work needs. Shantou University's Research Office has relayed the announcement, reminding interested faculty to contact the office before October 17.

## Positions

The recruitment covers two categories:

| Position Category | Openings | Involved Departments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business Supervisors (mobile position) | 10 | Information Center, Projects Division of the Planning Bureau, Policy Bureau, Bureau of International Cooperation, Personnel Bureau, Discipline Inspection and Audit Bureau, etc. |
| Project Directors (mobile position) | 42 | Relevant divisions under the eight departments: Mathematical and Physical Sciences, Chemistry, Life Sciences, Earth Sciences, Engineering and Materials, Information Sciences, Management Sciences, and Health Sciences |

Notably, the business supervisor post at the Personnel Bureau requires CPC membership, while the project director post at the Comprehensive Division of the Department of Information Sciences requires an automation background, with familiarity in instruments and equipment preferred.

## Eligibility

According to the announcement, applicants should be upright, impartial, and dedicated to serving scientists; hold a senior professional title in a relevant field (associate senior titles generally requiring at least three years in the post); possess solid theoretical foundations and working backgrounds with outstanding achievements; project director posts in the science departments additionally require familiarity with international frontiers and trends in the relevant field. Applicants must also be able to work full-time at NSFC during employment, be in good health, and be no older than 55 years of age.

Two groups are barred from applying: those who are spouses, lineal relatives, relatives within three generations, or close in-laws of NSFC staff or staff of its affiliated units; and those who previously served as mobile-position staff but left less than two years ago.

## Term and Compensation

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Term | Generally two years (including a two-month probation), with a tripartite employment agreement |
| Salary and Benefits | Paid by the original employer |
| Post Allowance | 2,000 yuan per month (tax inclusive) from NSFC |
| Other Benefits | Equal access to official business trips (including overseas), academic exchanges, training, annual leave, and other benefits as regular NSFC staff |

It is worth noting that mobile-position staff may not apply for or participate in any NSFC-funded projects during their term; the positions are not open to fresh graduates (including master's and doctoral students) or postdoctoral researchers.

## How to Apply

Applicants must register through NSFC's online recruitment system and complete the application form online. The printed form should be reviewed and stamped by the applicant's personnel or organization department and mailed to the Personnel Bureau of NSFC at 83 Shuangqing Road, Haidian District, Beijing. Applications are valid only upon receipt of the paper materials, with a deadline of October 31, 2014 (based on the postmark). The inquiry phone number is 010-62326459/6928.

Interested STU faculty should contact Hu Yunxiang at the Research Office before October 17, by phone at 86502813.
