Shantou University Library is offering trial access to the Kluwer Arbitration database. The resource is jointly provided by the International Council for Commercial Arbitration, the Permanent Court of Arbitration, the International Association of Arbitrators and Kluwer Law International, and contains materials related to arbitration.

Faculty and students can access it through the library’s database page. The trial will remain available through November 28, and feedback may be sent to the library’s subject services department.
