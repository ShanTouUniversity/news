Shantou University’s Publicity Department and Discipline Inspection Office will jointly hold an exhibition on clean-governance rules introduced since the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, as part of the university’s monthly discipline-education programme.

The exhibition is scheduled to run from September 29 through October 31, 2014, on the staff union notice board beside the post office. An electronic edition will also be posted on the university homepage and the “Qingfeng Songyuan” website of the Supervision and Audit Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Publicity Department and Discipline Inspection Office)
