The Book Design 2 course presentation at Shantou University’s College of Arts and Design will take place from 8:30 a.m. to noon on September 30 at the college gallery. The presentation will feature students from the 2011 graphic design class and is open to the university community. The instructors are Lü Jingren and Wu Yong.
