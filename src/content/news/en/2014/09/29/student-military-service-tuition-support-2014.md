Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has published application arrangements for 2014 national aid for students performing compulsory military service. The support covers tuition compensation, repayment of national student loans and tuition waivers for students returning to study after service.

Eligible applicants include 2014 graduates and current students who entered military service, students who have returned after service, previously eligible applicants who missed earlier applications, and returning students whose remaining years qualify for a top-up under the revised standards.

Beginning in the fall 2014 semester, annual support is capped at RMB 8,000 per undergraduate and RMB 12,000 per graduate student. The revised standards apply to students who entered service after July 1, 2014. Returning students receive waivers under the new standard starting this semester; earlier semesters remain subject to the previous rules.

## Application Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Application method | Complete and print the form online through the national conscription website; handwritten forms are not accepted |
| Required materials | Copy of the enlistment notice and two original copies of the tuition-compensation/student-loan-repayment application form after review and endorsement |
| Additional documents for returning students | Copies of the discharge certificate and proof of reenrollment; former non-commissioned officers must also provide an enlistment-notice copy |
| Submission location | Student Aid Management Center, Student Affairs Office, Administration Center Room 106 |
| Deadline | October 21, 2014 |

Applications submitted after the deadline will be deferred to the following year.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
