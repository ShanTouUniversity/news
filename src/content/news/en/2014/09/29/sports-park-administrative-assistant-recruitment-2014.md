It is reported that the Personnel Office issued a recruitment notice on September 29, 2014, for one Administrative Assistant for the Sports Park Office.

Designed by David Manica of the US firm Manica Architecture, the Sports Park covers 60,000 sqm with a total floor area of 52,000 sqm, comprising a 6,278-seat basketball arena, an Olympic-standard swimming pool, a conference center and a 170-room hotel, with a vegetated roof and walkways. Funded entirely by the Li Ka Shing Foundation with an investment of about 560 million yuan, construction began in 2014 and is expected to complete in 2015.

## Qualifications

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Education | Bachelor’s degree or above, preferably in public or business administration |
| Skills | Strong interpersonal and organizational coordination, fluent written and spoken Chinese and English, Cantonese preferred; ability to analyze and translate complex information |
| Other | Good health and compliance with family planning policy |

## Responsibilities

Assist the management team in building and optimizing administrative systems and regulations, perform administrative duties, coordinate with university departments, assist the general manager with research, reports and minutes, and handle translation and document management.
