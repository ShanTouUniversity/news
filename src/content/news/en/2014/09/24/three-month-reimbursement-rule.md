It is reported that Shantou University will introduce a rule requiring expense claims to be submitted within three months, in a move designed to improve the timeliness of financial information and better reflect the university's financial position. The measure was recommended by PricewaterhouseCoopers and approved by the university leader in charge, and takes formal effect on **December 1, 2014**.

## Key Points

Under the new rule, all units on the main campus must complete reimbursement procedures at the Finance Office **within three months** of completing a transaction and obtaining the relevant receipts. Once the measure takes effect, receipts older than three months will not be reimbursed without exceptional reasons.

## Transition Arrangements

Given that some units may still hold unreimbursed receipts older than three months, a transition period has been set: units should clear their backlog of old receipts **before December 1**, after which such claims can no longer be processed.
