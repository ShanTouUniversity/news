It is reported that the Library has opened trial access to the Huayi Taiwan Academic Literature Database for faculty, students and other readers through December 31, 2014.

The database includes the Taiwan Science and Technology Journal Database and the Taiwan Science and Technology Dissertation Database. It covers natural sciences, applied sciences, medicine and life sciences, and contains 441 Taiwan academic journals and approximately 49,000 dissertations from leading Taiwanese universities.

The database is available through the Library's database page at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=396>. Feedback can be sent to the Library's subject services team at 86503247 or lqxie@stu.edu.cn.
