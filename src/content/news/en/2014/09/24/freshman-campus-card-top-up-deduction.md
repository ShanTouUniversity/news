It is reported that Shantou University preloaded RMB 100 onto the campus cards of its 2014 undergraduate and graduate freshmen so that the cards could be used upon arrival. The Finance Office commissioned the Bank of China to process the first batch of deductions on September 19, but deductions for 90 freshmen were unsuccessful.

The Network and Information Center advises the affected freshmen to deposit RMB 100 into their corresponding Bank of China accounts as soon as possible. The Finance Office plans to commission the bank to make a second deduction on September 29. The list of unsuccessful deductions is available in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
> ⚠️ The list of unsuccessful deductions and attachment are available on the OA system.
