It is reported that the "Love in Ya'an" volunteer team — supported by the Li Ka Shing Foundation and STU's Academic Affairs Office — will hold a sharing session on Friday, September 26, from 7:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. in Room G306, recounting their service-learning trip to Shangli Ancient Town in Ya'an, Sichuan.

## Background: Reconstruction Continues

The magnitude-7.0 Ya'an earthquake of April 20, 2013 caused severe damage to Shangli Town in the north of Yucheng District, which is still rebuilding housing and other infrastructure. The one-to-three-year period after the quake is a high-frequency phase for secondary disasters such as floods and landslides, and an earlier episode of torrential rain and flooding had already inflicted a second wave of damage on the town.

## A 14-Day Service Journey

In August 2014, fifteen students and two STU faculty members formed the volunteer team and traveled to Shangli Ancient Town — itself affected by the Wenchuan earthquake — to carry out disaster-prevention education and life-education service-learning activities. The program aimed to help residents strengthen their disaster awareness and coping methods, value life and live positively, thereby mitigating the impact of secondary disasters and psychosocial problems.

Jiang WY, a team member from Mianyang who lived through the Wenchuan earthquake himself, reflected: "Looking again at my hometown, those children are like I once was — still fearless after the quake. I think that's a good thing. The shadow of disaster must not overshadow an entire life, otherwise saplings without sunshine will eventually wither."

Team members noted that while many see volunteering as a serious undertaking, they believe it can also be fun — done "playfully but earnestly." Of their 14-day program, six days were devoted to service learning in Shangli, and the sharing session will tell how they played seriously with local children while doing good.

The session is open to all faculty and students. The *Love in Ya'an* course group welcomes more people to learn about and join the journey of public service.
