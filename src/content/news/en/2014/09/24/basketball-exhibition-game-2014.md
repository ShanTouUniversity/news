With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the Shantou University Warriors will host the men's team from Dongguan Basketball School for an exhibition game. The game will tip off at 19:30 on Saturday, September 27, 2014, at the Shantou University Gymnasium. Admission is free for students, faculty and other university viewers.

Viewers should enter in an orderly manner before 19:15. A cheering section for up to 40 people will be arranged courtside; interested viewers may arrive at 19:00 for a brief orientation by staff. The organizers encourage attendees to wear orange to create a home-court atmosphere.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
