Shantou University has adjusted the opening hours for its badminton activity courts during the autumn semester. Starting Wednesday, September 24, 2014, the courts will be open from 18:00 to 20:00 every Wednesday.

## Opening Schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Day | Every Wednesday |
| Hours | 18:00–20:00 |
| Effective date | September 24, 2014 |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Trade Union)
