It is reported that the Research Office issued a notice on September 24, 2014, to launch the application for the 2014 Guangdong General University Education Science 12th Five-Year Plan project recognition.

## Category and Requirements

The projects are provincial major and key projects in the characteristic innovation category (education science) and must meet the following:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Planning affiliation | Must be within the university’s 2014–2016 Higher Education “Innovation and Strong University” overall plan |
| Principal investigator | May undertake no more than three provincial financially supported projects; must have good political quality and independent research capability |
| Eligibility | All on-duty teachers |
| Research focus | Major practical and theoretical issues in education reform and development, aiming to build a strong education province and achieve modernization |
| Disciplinary fields | Choose from 11 groups (jk001, jk002, jk004, jk006–jk013), excluding educational information technology and moral education |
| Expected output | Papers and research reports encouraged (1–2 years), monographs 2–3 years |
| Quota | Five projects for the university |

## Procedures

Applicants should complete the *Application Form* (Attachment 1) and send a draft to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn by October 13 for formal review. The university will select the best candidates for recommendation, with follow-up procedures notified by email. Late submissions will not be accepted. Applications outside the specified fields will be deemed ineligible.
