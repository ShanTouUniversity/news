Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office has begun collecting experimental course schedules for the 2014–2015 autumn semester. Schools and departments are asked to organize undergraduate teaching laboratories to complete the schedules. The request mainly covers experiments attached to theory courses; experiments offered as independent courses in the credit-based system generally do not need to be submitted again.

Electronic files and printed copies must be submitted by Wednesday, October 8, 2014. Laboratory directors at each department will collect the materials, send one printed copy to the Academic Affairs Office and retain the other with the department's academic administrator.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The schedule template is available on the OA system.
