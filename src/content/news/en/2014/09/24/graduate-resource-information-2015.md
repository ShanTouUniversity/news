The Student Affairs Office of Shantou University has launched the collection of employment information for the 2015 graduating class. The process covers undergraduates at the main campus and all graduate students, whose information will support employment-plan preparation and subsequent recommendation and placement work.

Schools have been asked to remind graduates who have not completed their basic information to log in to the Guangdong graduate employment information system and submit it by October 10, 2014. Detailed operating instructions are available in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The detailed operating instructions are available on the OA system.
