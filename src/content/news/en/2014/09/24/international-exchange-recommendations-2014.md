Shantou University's Office of International Affairs has published the proposed second-batch recommendations for its outstanding undergraduate international exchange programs. Six programs in Canada, Portugal, France, New Zealand and Ireland received support from the China Scholarship Council, and the university plans to send undergraduates to partner institutions during the 2014–2015 academic year.

Eight undergraduates were recommended after applications, school-level selection and university review. To protect student privacy, the names below are shown as surnames followed by initials.

## Proposed Recommendations

| Exchange program | Proposed candidates |
| --- | --- |
| Universidade Católica Portuguesa | Mao SY |
| European School of Management | Chen X; Zeng ZZ |
| Dublin City University | Lin YS; Wu ZH |
| University of Canterbury | Sun J; Chen YS |
| Victoria University of Wellington | Lu F |

The public-comment period ends on September 28, 2014. Comments may be submitted in writing to the Office of International Affairs in accordance with the OA notice.
