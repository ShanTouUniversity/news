It is reported that the Business School will hold an information session on September 26 for CommTECH Camp 2015, an international cross-cultural experience program for students. The 2015 program is scheduled to take place in Surabaya, Indonesia, from February 2 to 10, and will include activities such as experiencing Indonesian culture, learning the local language, tasting local food, city tours and rafting.

The program fee is US$1,600, covering accommodation, meals, local transportation and airport pickup in Indonesia. The information session will begin at 4 p.m. on September 26 in the fourth-floor Wenxi meeting room of the Business School. Registration closes at 2 p.m. on September 30.
