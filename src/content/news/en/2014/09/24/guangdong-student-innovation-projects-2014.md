A total of 136 projects were selected for the 2014 Guangdong provincial program supporting key student science and technology projects after expert review. All three projects recommended by Shantou University were approved.

## Selected Projects

| Project | Student applicant | Faculty adviser |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research on a direct methanol fuel-cell power generation system | Zhang GY | Zhang Xin |
| Research on existing family issues and social welfare needs of children with cerebral palsy in Shantou | Li JY | Cai Zhengzhong |
| Survey and intervention experiment on empathy among medical students | Chen ZM | Zhang Yanzhen; Chen Maohuai |

The university's Youth League Committee said the project teams would begin their research. Funding and related management arrangements will be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
