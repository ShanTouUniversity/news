It is reported that, ahead of the National Day holiday running from October 1 to 7, 2014, the Student Affairs Office of Shantou University issued holiday safety tips for all students, highlighting prevention of the then-raging dengue fever outbreak.

## Dengue Fever Alert

According to the notice, Guangdong Province was then in a high-incidence season for dengue fever, with Guangzhou, Foshan, Zhongshan and Jiangmen among the high-risk cities. By midnight on September 22, the province had reported 6,089 dengue cases. Students were advised to monitor outbreaks at their destinations and take personal anti-mosquito measures, including applying repellent to exposed skin, wearing long sleeves and trousers, and using mosquito nets when sleeping.

## Travel Safety Tips

With heavy holiday traffic expected, students are urged to obey traffic laws and refuse rides in unlicensed or overloaded vehicles. Those traveling should heed safety advisories from destination cities, choose licensed tour operators and hygienic restaurants, follow local guidance during hiking or outdoor adventures, avoid restricted areas, keep valuables secure, and inform family, friends or teachers of their whereabouts.

## Rules for Students Staying On Campus

Students remaining on campus must observe university schedules and safety regulations: no hosting outside visitors or members of the opposite sex overnight; no open flames or high-power appliances such as immersion heaters in dormitories; no obstructions in fire evacuation routes; no fishing or swimming at the university reservoir or other dangerous waters; and no candles or fireworks during campus activities. Climbing walls, railings or fruit trees is also discouraged.
