The Guangdong University Track and Field Championships and University Soccer League will be held in Guangzhou from November to December 2014. The Competitive Training Center of Shantou University's Physical Education Department is selecting undergraduate athletes from the main campus to represent the university. Selected athletes may also have the opportunity to participate in the ninth Guangdong University Games in 2015.

Applicants must be full-time undergraduates who meet the applicable registration and competition requirements. The track-and-field selection is open to students of all genders, while the soccer selection is for male students. Track-and-field events include sprints, middle- and long-distance races, hurdles, relays, high jump, long jump, triple jump, shot put and javelin; the soccer selection format will be announced separately.

Applicants must complete the registration form and email it to the Physical Education Department by October 12, 2014. The selection format, time and venue will be announced by the Competitive Training Center.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
