Shantou University's Student Affairs Office has opened the 2014 assessment process for the National Scholarship, National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant. The National Scholarship is for outstanding full-time undergraduates in their second year or above and provides 8,000 yuan per recipient. The National Encouragement Scholarship is for high-achieving undergraduates with financial need and provides 5,000 yuan, while the National Grant provides 3,000 yuan to undergraduates with financial need.

Applicants must complete the online application by 08:00 on October 8 and submit the required materials to their school counselor by 12:00 the same day. National Scholarship applicants are generally expected to rank in the top 10 percent academically and have no failing grades. Schools will organize reviews, public notices and submissions: National Scholarship materials are due by 17:00 on October 13, and National Encouragement Scholarship and National Grant materials are due by October 31.

Application forms, templates and detailed assessment requirements are provided in the OA attachments.
