STU Campus Radio announced recruitment on September 10, 2014, seeking students for its broadcasting, reporting, publicity and secretarial departments. Students from all years and majors may apply.

Registration closes on September 14. Interviews will be held that day from 9:00 to 12:00 and from 13:00 to 18:00; the venue will be announced through the radio station’s official Weibo account. The registration form and detailed requirements are available in the original notice attachments.

> ⚠️ The registration form and event attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (News Center)
