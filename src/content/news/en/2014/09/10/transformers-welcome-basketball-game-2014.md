It is reported that, to welcome new students and introduce the Shantou University men's basketball team, the STU Transformers have invited the Hanshan Normal University men's team for a welcome invitational game at 4:30 p.m. on Saturday, September 13, at the STU Gymnasium, with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation. All faculty and students are welcome to cheer on the home team.

## Game Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Matchup | STU Transformers vs. Hanshan Normal University |
| Time | 4:30 p.m., Saturday, September 13, 2014 |
| Venue | STU Gymnasium |
| Admission | Free of charge |

Exciting performances and games will be presented during breaks, with gifts for the audience.

## Spectator Tips

The team reminded attendees of the following:

1. Admission is free; spectators should enter in an orderly manner and be seated by 4:15 p.m.;
2. A cheerleader section seating 40 people will be set up beside the court. Interested fans may arrive by 3:45 p.m. to take their seats, where staff will provide brief training;
3. Spectators are encouraged to wear orange-themed outfits to create a home-court atmosphere.

In addition, spectators can follow the STU Transformers on Weibo (http://weibo.com/STUTransformers) to join a lucky draw.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
