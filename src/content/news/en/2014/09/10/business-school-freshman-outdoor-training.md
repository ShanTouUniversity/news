The Business School will hold an outdoor training camp for its 2014 freshmen at Lianhuashan in Chenghai from September 14 to 15, 2014. The programme includes outdoor survival, team development, themed discussions and social activities, with the aim of supporting student adjustment and teamwork.

A briefing for all 2014 freshmen and 2013 student leaders is scheduled for 16:30 on September 12 in Room 101 of the E Lecture Hall. Students unable to attend for health or other special reasons must submit a leave request to the Business School Youth League office by 17:00 that day.
