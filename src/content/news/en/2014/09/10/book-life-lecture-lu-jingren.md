Graphic designer Lü Jingren gave a talk titled “The Life of Books” at 19:30 on September 10, 2014, in the lecture hall on the first floor of the College of Cheung Kong Art and Design. The event was the 28th session of the “Culture Talks” series and was open to the university community.
