Renovation of Shantou University’s East Lecture Hall began on July 1, 2014. Design changes during construction added entrance steps and stainless-steel protective railings for Lecture Hall 5, among other items. The Infrastructure Office estimated the additional cost at about RMB 5,000, keeping cumulative additions below the university’s 10% limit.

Organizations or individuals with comments may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 17:30 on September 12.
