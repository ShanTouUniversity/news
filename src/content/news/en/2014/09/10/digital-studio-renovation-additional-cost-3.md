Renovation of Shantou University’s digital multifunctional studio began on May 14, 2014. Construction changes added fire alarms, fire equipment and piping above the ceiling, revised the ventilation system, modified the keying backdrop and added green epoxy self-leveling flooring.

The Infrastructure Office estimated the additional cost at about RMB 45,000, keeping cumulative additions below the university’s 10% limit. Organizations or individuals with comments may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 17:30 on September 12.
