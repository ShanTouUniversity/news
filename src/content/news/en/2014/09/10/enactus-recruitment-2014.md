The Shantou University Enactus team announced recruitment on September 10, 2014, welcoming students from all majors and years. The team uses business and economic ideas in social projects intended to create positive change in local communities.

Registration closes at midnight on September 15. Students may apply through the team’s registration form or attend its information session at the Science and Technology Lecture Hall on the evening of September 13. The team also provides consultation through its QQ group and WeChat account. Details of paper registration points and the form are available in the original notice attachments.

> ⚠️ The registration form and event attachments are available on the OA system.
