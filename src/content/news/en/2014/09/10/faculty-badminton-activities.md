The Shantou University Union opened the gym badminton court for faculty and staff from September 10, 2014. Activities are scheduled every Wednesday from 19:00 to 21:00 and will continue through Week 18 of the semester.

The Union said the arrangement is intended to enrich the cultural and recreational life of faculty and staff, who are welcome to participate.
