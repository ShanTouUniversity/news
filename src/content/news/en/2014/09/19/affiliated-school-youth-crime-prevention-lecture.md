The STU Affiliated School will hold a lecture titled Building a Protective Network Against Juvenile Crime at 16:15 on September 24 in E203 of the E Lecture Hall. Shen Yiyong, an associate professor and master's supervisor in Shantou University's Social Sciences Department, will speak. The lecture is open to faculty members, students and parents.
