It is reported that Shantou Municipality will hold an Advanced Training for Intellectual Property Experts on the afternoon of September 25, 2014, to support the national “Escort” campaign for intellectual property enforcement and to improve the professional capacity of experts in the municipal expert database and partner organizations.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Afternoon of September 25, 2014 |
| Organizer | Shantou Intellectual Property Office |
| Host | Guangdong Intellectual Property Training (Shantou University) Base |
| Co-organizers | China (Shantou) Intellectual Property Rights Aid Center and Shantou Patent Protection Association |
| Speakers | Experts including Fan Xiaodong, Deputy Director of the Design Litigation Division, Patent Reexamination Board, State Intellectual Property Office |
| Topics | Determination of patent infringement, patent award evaluation, etc. |
| Registration | Send the registration form to chenjs@stu.edu.cn before the end of work on September 22, 2014 for shuttle arrangements |
| Issued by | Research Office |

The training is open to STU members of the Shantou intellectual property expert database and other interested faculty.

> ⚠️ Registration forms and attachments are available on the OA system.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
