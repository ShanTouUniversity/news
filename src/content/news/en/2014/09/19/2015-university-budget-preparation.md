Shantou University began preparing its 2015 budget. Units are to prepare their budgets from September 19 to November 2 and send proposals reviewed by their supervising university leader to the Finance Office by the end of November 3.

## Budget Priorities

The budget includes special projects for teaching reform, employment and unit-proposed initiatives. The university will also provide separate funding for discipline development and research enhancement, which will be proposed by the relevant disciplines. The Admissions Office will submit the admissions budget, while the Finance Office and Human Resources Office will centrally calculate routine operating costs and salary and benefits for university-employed personnel.

Units must provide basic data, annual target results, development plans, budget expenditure analyses and a summary of proposed projects using the forms and instructions in the attachments. The Medical College may use its previous method or follow the main-campus model.

Budget forms, expenditure details and review guidance are available in the original OA attachments.
