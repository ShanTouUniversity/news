It is reported that, to implement guidance from central, provincial, and municipal authorities on promoting youth employment and entrepreneurship, encourage young people with entrepreneurial aspirations and capabilities to start businesses scientifically, and stimulate entrepreneurial enthusiasm among youth and university students, the "Dream Sets Sail, Creating the Future" Shantou Youth Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition has officially opened registration to young people and university students across the city.

The competition is jointly organized by the Youth League Committee of Shantou University, the Communist Youth League Shantou Municipal Committee, the Municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau, the Municipal Economic and Information Technology Bureau, the Municipal Science and Technology Bureau, the Municipal Agriculture Bureau, the People's Bank of China Shantou Central Sub-branch, the Postal Savings Bank of China Shantou Branch, and the Shantou Youth Federation. It aims to create favorable conditions and a broad stage for youth innovation and entrepreneurship while identifying and cultivating young entrepreneurial talent.

Teams intending to start a business or already operating one are all encouraged to apply. According to the arrangements, participating teams must send their registration materials electronically **to the university Youth League Committee's email at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by October 10**; detailed requirements are available in the competition notice attachments.
