It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the Party Committee issued a notice on September 19, 2014, that the 76th Introductory Party Knowledge Class is scheduled to open in mid-October.

The class is for applicants who have submitted Party membership applications for more than one year and been designated as key training targets, as well as Party members among the 2014 undergraduate freshmen.

## Registration

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled opening | Mid-October 2014 |
| Eligible applicants | Key training targets with applications over one year old; Party members among 2014 freshmen |
| Submission requirements | The *Application Form*, signed by the general Party branch or directly affiliated branch and stamped, must be submitted to the Party School Office (Room 315, New Administration Center) by September 29, 2014; the *Participant List Summary* (including approved deferred students from Class 75, not counting toward this quota) should be sent to o_dxb@stu.edu.cn |
| Quotas | See OA attachments |
| Admission | Participants will be confirmed after review by the Party School Office |
| Issued by | Organization and United Front Work Department |

> ⚠️ Quota and forms are available on the OA system.
