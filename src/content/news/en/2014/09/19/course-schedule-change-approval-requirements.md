The Academic Affairs Office reiterated the procedures for changing undergraduate class schedules. Instructors at the main campus must complete a leave or business-trip form and obtain approval before changing a class.

When an unexpected event makes advance processing impossible, the instructor must complete the relevant procedures after returning to campus in accordance with the university's staff attendance regulations. The approved form is required for a class change, and requests without the required approval may not be approved.
