The Science College of Shantou University completed interviews for the fall 2014–2015 Toward the Ocean public-interest course on the evening of September 18. Held in E201, the interviews included self-introductions, a leaderless group discussion, and questions and answers. Two course instructors and one course assistant scored the participants.

Twenty-two students were selected for the fall course and four others were placed on the waiting list. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the names, years or majors in the original notice.
