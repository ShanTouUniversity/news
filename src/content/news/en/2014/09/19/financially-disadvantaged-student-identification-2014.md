The Student Affairs Office began the 2014–2015 identification process for undergraduate students with financial need. Each school will establish a review group and class-level evaluation groups to handle applications, evaluations, public notices and final review in accordance with the university's policies.

## Timeline

Students seeking identification must submit an application through the student affairs system by September 26 and provide the required household financial survey and supporting materials to their class evaluation group. Class and youth league evaluation groups will review applications from September 28 to October 11. School-level groups will review the results from October 13 to October 20 and publish the names and categories within classes for five working days.

Previously identified students whose financial circumstances have not changed substantially may submit the application through the system only, while completing the required information on volunteer service participation. Applicants with questions should contact the Student Aid Management Center through the channels listed in the student affairs system.

The original OA attachments contain the operating instructions, forms and policy documents.
