The 41st STU Career Launch Forum will be held from 19:00 to 21:00 on September 23 in E102 (Moot Court) at the E Lecture Hall. Its topic is student career planning and preparation for the 2015 national civil service examination.

Li Binghui, a senior researcher at the Huatu Civil Service Examination Research Center, will discuss career planning, the importance of a first job, examination trends, the latest developments in the national examination, and high-score preparation strategies. The forum is open to all faculty members and students.
