The Physical Education Department of Shantou University announced the top three finishers in the 2014 Freshman Football Festival. Fu QS of the School of Engineering ranked first with 380 points, Li YM of the School of Law ranked second with 375 points, and Shin W of the Business School ranked third with 365 points.

The winners were asked to contact the relevant teacher through the Physical Education Department to collect their prizes. Student names are presented in abbreviated form for privacy, and the personal telephone number in the original notice is not reproduced.
