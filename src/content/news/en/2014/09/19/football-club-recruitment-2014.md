The Physical Education Department of Shantou University is recruiting student members for its football club. As a pilot undergraduate teaching reform project, the club promotes football through training, matches, refereeing and event organization; participation can count toward physical education credits.

The club plans to admit 10 students. If applications exceed the limit, applicants will be selected through a football skills assessment. Applications are due by 17:00 on September 25. Students may apply through the online course-selection system or submit the registration form as instructed. Sessions will take place during periods 8–9 on Tuesdays, and applicants must ensure that the time does not conflict with other courses.

The registration form and submission details are available in the original OA attachment.
