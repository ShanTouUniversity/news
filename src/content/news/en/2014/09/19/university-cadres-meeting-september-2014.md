Shantou University will hold a university cadres meeting at 15:00 on Tuesday, September 23, in the Science and Technology Center auditorium. Participants include university leaders; heads and deputy heads of administrative offices; cadres at deputy-division level and above; leaders of teaching and support units; and secretaries and deputy secretaries of party general branches and directly affiliated branches. Medical College leaders at deputy-division level and above are also expected to attend.

Each unit must submit its attendee list to the Organization and United Front Work Department by noon on Monday, September 22. Participants should arrive 15 minutes early.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
