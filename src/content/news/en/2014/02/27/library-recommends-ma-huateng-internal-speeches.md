Shantou University Library has featured *Ma Huateng’s Internal Speeches* in its Chaoshan literature book recommendation series. Divided into five sections, the book traces Tencent’s growth from an instant-messaging tool into a comprehensive internet company and compiles speeches by Ma Huateng from the company’s entrepreneurial and management experience.

The book discusses product development, user service, decision-making, organizational change, the QQ brand, team culture and technology trends. It also examines management practices associated with Tencent’s expansion and organizational growth, offering reference material for entrepreneurs and readers interested in business management.

The author information identifies Xing Qunlin, who writes under the name “我邢我宿,” as a senior manager at a Tencent subsidiary, a business writer and a publisher who has long researched corporate management and Tencent’s corporate culture. His published works include *Methods Are Always More Than Difficulties*. The other author, Ou Jun, is a book planner and business writer whose works include *Fifty CEOs on Business Management*, *Economics Can Lie* and *Ren Zhengfei’s Internal Speeches*.
