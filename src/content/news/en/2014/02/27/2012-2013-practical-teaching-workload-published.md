Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has activated the workload publication function for practical teaching in the credit-based education system and published the practical teaching workload for the 2012-2013 academic year.

Teachers concerned may log in to the credit-based education system to review their workload. Questions or comments may be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office by March 7. The notice lists 86503202 as the contact telephone number and Zhou Mengyi as the contact person.
