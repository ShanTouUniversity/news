The first phase of Shantou University Law School’s 2014 National Judicial Examination training will begin on March 1. The phase, a case-analysis course, will cover Criminal Law, Criminal Procedure Law, Civil Law, Civil Procedure Law, Administrative Law, Jurisprudence, Commercial and Economic Law, and the Three Countries Law module.

The training will take place at the Moot Court on March 1, 2, 8 and 9. Sessions will run from 8:30 a.m. to noon and from 2:30 p.m. to 6 p.m. on each date. Students who registered for the training should bring their attendance cards and study handouts. Those with classes at the same time should attend their scheduled classes first and join the training afterward.
