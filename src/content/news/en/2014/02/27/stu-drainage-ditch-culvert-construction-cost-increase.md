Shantou University’s Infrastructure Construction Office published a notice on February 27 concerning additional items for the university’s drainage ditch and culvert construction project.

According to the notice, construction began on December 11, 2013. Because the north and south sides of the site differ in elevation by approximately 3.60 meters, concrete steps and other items had to be added during construction. Based on the actual construction conditions, the office initially estimated that the additions would increase the project cost by approximately RMB 20,000. The cumulative value of the additional items remains within the 10 percent limit set by the university.

The notice stated that the additional project costs were being published in accordance with the Implementation Measures for Bidding and Tendering Management of Shantou University Infrastructure Projects. Written comments may be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Construction Office by 5:30 p.m. on March 1.
