It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office will convene a working meeting for laboratory directors on the afternoon of February 28 to discuss the implementation of the teaching laboratory information platform portal.

## Meeting Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Topic | Implementation of the teaching laboratory information platform portal |
| Time | 2:40 PM, Feb. 28 |
| Venue | Meeting Room 312, New Administration Building |
| Attendees | Laboratory directors from all colleges and departments |

All laboratory directors are requested to attend on time to jointly advance the digitalization of teaching laboratories.
