It is reported that the Business School will offer the *Value Based Innovation* course from May 5 to 10, 2014, taught by Prof. Shlomo Maital of the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, with Dr. Liang Qiang as teaching assistant.

Prof. Maital is a world-renowned scholar in entrepreneurship and innovation, Senior Research Fellow at the Neaman Institute, former Academic Director at the Technion and long-time Visiting Professor at MIT. He has authored books including *The Technion Nation* (2012) and *Concepts & Tools for Growth & Profit*. The course, taught at MIT for 20 years, uses lectures, interaction, project internships and hands-on practice to develop students’ innovation and business skills.
