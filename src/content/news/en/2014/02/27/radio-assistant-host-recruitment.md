Shantou Radio and Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication are recruiting assistant hosts from the school’s students. The partnership offers practical experience in radio production and hosting through a campus selection followed by an interview with radio presenters.

## Recruitment schedule

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| First-round selection | 2:30–5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, March 5 |
| Venue | Small Glass Room, School of Journalism |
| Second round | Expected in late March; shortlisted applicants will be notified separately |
| Training | Expected to begin in April |

Applicants should prepare a 60-second program script for an audio-and-video review in standard Mandarin. Students who speak Teochew may also prepare a 60-second Teochew script. A résumé with a photograph is required at the first round. Selected students will receive guidance from radio presenters, take part in studio production and program planning, and gain practical experience.

Applicants may contact a teacher in the School of Journalism at `xuchengqun@stu.edu.cn` or assistant Zhou Yanqiong at short number `662176`.
