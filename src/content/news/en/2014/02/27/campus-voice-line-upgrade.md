It is reported that China Mobile's Shantou branch will upgrade Shantou University's voice line and short-number cluster network to improve service quality. The upgrade is scheduled from 22:30 on February 27 to 5:00 a.m. on February 28, 2014.

According to the notice forwarded by the university's Network and Information Center, the upgrade will have the following effects:

- Telephones at the new campus will be unable to make or receive calls;
- Mobile numbers in the university's short-number cluster network will not be able to call other short numbers or fixed-line extensions within the network;
- Dialing between full mobile numbers will not be affected.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Upgrade Time | 22:30, Feb 27 – 5:00 a.m., Feb 28 |
| Scope | Campus voice line and short-number cluster network |
| Contractor | China Mobile (Shantou Branch) |
| Inquiry Line | 86503400-1 |

For questions, faculty and students may contact the Network and Information Center. The university has asked campus users to plan their communications accordingly and apologized for any inconvenience caused during the upgrade.
