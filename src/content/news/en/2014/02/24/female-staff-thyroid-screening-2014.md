As part of its International Women’s Day activities, Shantou University will provide free thyroid screening for female staff. The screening will be available at the university hospital during regular working hours from February 27 to March 7.

The daily capacity is 50 people in the morning and 30 in the afternoon. Screening will be provided in order of arrival until the daily quota is filled. Thyroid screening for male staff is scheduled for May.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
