It is reported that, in order to implement the spirit of the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China and the 11th National Women's Congress, and to promote the volunteer spirit of "dedication, friendship, mutual assistance and progress," the Shantou Women's Federation has decided to form the city's first batch of 20 women's volunteer teams (Shan Fu Tong [2014] No. 4). The university's labor union has called on female faculty and staff at Shantou University to take part.

## Registration Details

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Who May Apply | Female faculty and staff with an interest in volunteering |
| Application Form | Shantou Women's Volunteer Registration Form |
| Deadline | February 28 |
| Submission Email | o_xgh@stu.edu.cn |
| Contact Phone | 86502983 |

According to the report, interested staff members should complete the registration form and submit it before February 28. The form and other materials are available in the attachments.
