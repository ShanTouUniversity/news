The Chinese Social Sciences Research Evaluation Center at Nanjing University released the 2014–2015 CSSCI source-journal directory on February 8. *Chinese Literature*, a bimonthly journal hosted by Shantou University, was selected for the expanded edition. Of the 16 journals listed in the Chinese-literature field, five were included in the expanded edition, with *Chinese Literature* ranked first among them.

Founded in 1985, *Chinese Literature* was among the earliest mainland Chinese journals dedicated to literature and criticism from Taiwan, Hong Kong and overseas Chinese communities. It became a purely academic journal in 2000. In recent years, it has supported STU’s internationalization efforts by inviting scholars from China and abroad to lecture, collaborating with academic and literary institutions, and publishing work by overseas scholars and research issues on leading writers and scholars in the Chinese-speaking world.

Its selection for the CSSCI expanded edition reflects the journal’s influence in Chinese-literature creation and research and is expected to support further improvements in editorial quality and academic impact.
