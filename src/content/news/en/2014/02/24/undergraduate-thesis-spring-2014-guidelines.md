Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has outlined arrangements for undergraduate theses and design projects in the spring semester of the 2013–2014 academic year. Schools and departments are required to complete all stages, including thesis defenses and grading, by the end of Week 14.

## Key milestones

| Stage | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Students complete thesis proposals | By the end of Week 2 |
| Students write and submit theses or design projects | By the end of Week 10 |
| Preliminary plagiarism screening | Around Week 10 |
| Establish defense committees and assign groups | Weeks 11–12 |
| Supervisors and reviewers assess submissions | Weeks 12–13 |
| Hold defenses, determine grades and enter results | Weeks 13–14 |

Schools are expected to follow the university’s undergraduate thesis regulations and writing standards, covering proposals, supervision, writing, defenses and grading. Students must complete their work independently, follow the required format and attend their defenses on schedule. Each student must also sign an undergraduate thesis academic-integrity pledge before submitting the final version.

Beginning this year, the university plans to use CNKI’s undergraduate thesis plagiarism-detection system. The specific schedule and procedures will be announced separately. Academic administrators at each teaching unit should collect thesis covers from the Academic Affairs Office before Week 9 and distribute them to students.
