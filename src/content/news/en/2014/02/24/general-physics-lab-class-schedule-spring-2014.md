Shantou University’s Department of Physics has announced the schedule for the spring General Physics Laboratory course. Students will study error theory during the first week and begin laboratory work in the second week.

## Error-theory classes

| Laboratory section | Class time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday section | 2nd–4th periods, 9 a.m.–noon, Saturday, March 1 | E203 |
| Tuesday section | 6th–8th periods, 2–5 p.m., Saturday, March 1 | E203 |
| Thursday section | 2nd–4th periods, 9 a.m.–noon, Sunday, March 2 | E203 |
| Friday section | 6th–8th periods, 2–5 p.m., Sunday, March 2 | E203 |

Students enrolled in the course should share the information with classmates and attend the error-theory class corresponding to their laboratory section.
