Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has forwarded a notice from the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation on applications to establish new postdoctoral research stations. Disciplines that meet the criteria are invited to apply.

## Application arrangements

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | March 17 |
| Printed materials | Four copies of the application form, submitted to the Human Resources Office, Room 315, Administrative Center |
| Electronic materials | Email to `o_rsk@stu.edu.cn` |
| Contact | Li Zhiwei, `86502364` |

Requirements for online applications will be announced separately. Applying disciplines should prepare their materials in accordance with the relevant higher-level notice and application form.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office, forwarding a notice from the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation)
> ⚠️ Eligibility requirements and application forms are available in the attachments on the OA system.
