Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has announced the cancellation of course 68309, *Fan Changjiang and Northwest China*. Students interested in the course may select it when it is offered again in the following semester.

Students affected by the change should follow subsequent course-selection announcements and refer to the course information published for the new semester.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
