Shantou University’s Business School has announced the spring 2014 schedules for three public-interest courses focusing on charitable marketing, Mandarin promotion and support for children with autism.

## Course schedule

| Course | Class number | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charitable Marketing | 69713 | 3–5 p.m., Wednesday, February 26 | E203 |
| One Chinese Family, One Voice — Promoting Mandarin | 69709 | 3–5 p.m., Wednesday, February 26 | D201 |
| Caring for Children with Autism | 69708 | 2–6 p.m., Wednesday, March 5 | E204 |

The Business School asks students enrolled in the courses to share the information and attend according to the listed arrangements.
