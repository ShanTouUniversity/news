Zhicheng College will hold the 15th Zhicheng Forum on March 2, featuring Wu Hongqing, a professor at the School of Liberal Arts of the Open University of China. The talk will focus on the Chinese Dream and people-centered quality education.

## Event information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 9 a.m. on Sunday, March 2 |
| Venue | Library Lecture Hall |
| Speaker | Wu Hongqing, professor at the School of Liberal Arts, Open University of China |
| Reservation deadline | 1 p.m. on Friday, February 28 |
| Registration | `booking.stu.edu.cn` |

Wu has served on the Editorial and Publishing Committee of the Chinese Calligraphers Association and on the Ministry of Education’s teaching-guidance committee for higher vocational cultural programs. He has also hosted the Open University of China course *The Art of Calligraphy* and calligraphy lectures for China Education Television. He designed and patented the Qiankun Grid Writing Paper and a pen-holding correction device.

In 2006, Wu founded the Fuxi experimental class in Gangu County, Tianshui, Gansu, to explore people-centered quality education. After years of practice, the class developed distinctive approaches to teaching with the standard curriculum, calligraphy and martial arts, and its work attracted attention from media and the wider community.
