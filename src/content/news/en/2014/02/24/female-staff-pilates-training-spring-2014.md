In response to interest from Pilates participants, the Shantou University union will continue its training classes for female staff this semester. Classes will meet from 5:40 to 6:40 p.m. every Monday and Wednesday. The Monday class begins on March 3, and the Wednesday class on March 5.

Returning participants should confirm their preferred class time by February 26. Those who need to change their time or cannot attend this semester should notify the association in advance. Returning participants who have not confirmed by 5:30 p.m. on February 26 will be considered not to be continuing; their places will be offered to new participants, with limited places allocated in order of registration.

Participants should bring a yoga mat and wear loose-fitting clothing. Classes will not meet on public holidays. For registration or confirmation, call `2654` (short number `678222`).

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Union)
