Shantou University has opened registration for the spring 2014 Mandarin proficiency test. Applicants must complete pre-registration through both the STU credit-system portal and the Guangdong Mandarin Proficiency Test administration system. The examination date will be announced after the registration data are reviewed by the provincial testing office.

## Registration schedule

| Stage | Time or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Online pre-registration | Register through “Course Selection → Qualification Examination Registration” in the STU credit-system portal and through the Guangdong online registration system |
| Print registration form | March 1–7; print the Guangdong Mandarin Proficiency Test Registration Form |
| Payment | RMB 85 per student for training and testing; deducted from the campus card account on March 10 |
| In-person confirmation | 2:30–5:30 p.m. on March 12 at the Academic Affairs service window, third floor, Administrative Center |
| Test venue | Shantou Vocational and Technical College Mandarin Training and Testing Center |

Applicants should select Shantou as the examination area, the Shantou Vocational and Technical College Mandarin Training and Testing Center as the testing station, and “Spring 2014 Shantou University Test” as the session. Submitted information cannot be edited online, and duplicate registration is not permitted. Errors should be corrected on the printed form and submitted to the testing-station administrator.

No electronic photograph is required during online registration; the testing station will take photographs 30 minutes before the examination. Registration will be unsuccessful if payment cannot be collected or the application is not confirmed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
