The Shantou University Football Club is recruiting undergraduate students on the main campus. The club is one of the Physical Education Department’s pilot projects for undergraduate teaching reform and provides football enthusiasts with opportunities for training, competition and exchange.

## Club activities

Participants can meet relevant physical-education course requirements while taking part in football training, matches, refereeing and event organization. The program also aims to promote students’ physical and psychological well-being and encourage teamwork, determination and a competitive spirit.

Applications are due by February 28, 2014. Interested students may email their registration information to `xwliu@stu.edu.cn` with “Football Club Registration” in the subject line. The club’s activities are scheduled for periods 8 and 9, so they must not conflict with other courses or the online course-selection system. Students who have already selected a physical-education course this semester are not eligible to join.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
> ⚠️ Registration attachments and detailed arrangements are available on the OA system.
