The Shantou University Library has released Issue 1 of its 2014 Electronic Resources Bulletin, presenting usage statistics for electronic resources in the fourth quarter of 2013. The report covers Chinese- and foreign-language databases and measures including downloads, searches, views, plays and clicks.

| CNKI China Master’s Theses Full-text Database | 40,270 | downloads |

| VIP Chinese Science and Technology Journals | 103,239 | downloads |
| RESSET Financial Database | 774 | searches |

| China InfoBank | 18,199 | downloads |
| Duxiu Knowledge Base | 85,363 | pages delivered |
