In accordance with arrangements by the Guangdong provincial authorities, the Shantou University Party Committee will hold a summary meeting on the university’s mass-line education campaign on February 26. The meeting will review the implementation of the campaign at STU.

## Meeting information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 10 a.m. on Wednesday, February 26 |
| Venue | Science and Technology Center Lecture Hall |
| Arrival | Attendees should arrive 20 minutes early |
| Roster deadline | Before the end of the workday on February 25, submitted to the campaign office secretariat |

Attendees include members of the university Party leadership, current officials at deputy-director-general level or above, secretaries of general and directly affiliated Party branches, administrative officials at deputy-division level or above, all Party members and officials in university offices, heads and deputy heads of schools, colleges, institutes and centers, faculty representatives from each school, leaders of major democratic parties, representatives of retired staff, and representatives to the 11th Guangdong Party Congress, the Guangdong Provincial People’s Congress and the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference.

Units should inform relevant attendees and submit the roster to the campaign office secretariat at `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn` or `86502345`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Work Department)
