With approval in place, the Shantou University Infrastructure Office will repair roof insulation tiles above the U-shaped classrooms in the north wing of E Building. Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. was appointed to carry out the work, with a winning contract value of RMB 69,200.

Work began on February 10 and was scheduled for completion by March 11. Noise, vibration, and dust could occur during construction. Pedestrians, especially faculty living in the nearby residence building, were asked to exercise caution and avoid lingering near the site.

The project manager was Lu Chunxiong and the site construction manager was Chen Jinwu; contact details were provided in the construction notice.
