It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University forwarded a notice on February 13, 2014, from the Provincial Youth League Committee on nominations for the 2013 National Outstanding Youth League Members and related honors.

## Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Honors | National Outstanding Youth League Members, National Outstanding Youth League Cadres, National May Fourth Red Flag Youth League Committees (Branches) |
| Basis | Central Youth League Document Zhong Qing Ban Fa [2010] No. 19, Trial Measures for Evaluation |
| Method | College Youth League Committees and directly affiliated branches select and recommend outstanding candidates |
| Deadline | Submit electronic application materials to the Youth League Committee at o_tw@stu.edu.cn by February 18, 2014 |
| Issued by | Youth League Committee of Shantou University |

The honors, the highest conferred by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League, will be commended around May Fourth 2014. The full notice is available in the OA attachment.
