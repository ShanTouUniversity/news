The Central Committee of the Communist Youth League of China and the All-China Youth Federation planned to hold the 18th China Youth May Fourth Medal awards during the 2014 May Fourth period. The STU Communist Youth League Committee forwarded the nomination notice on February 13, 2014, inviting schools and directly affiliated branches to recommend eligible candidates.

The China Youth May Fourth Medal is an honor presented to outstanding young people by the two national organizations, highlighting the spirit and values of contemporary youth and encouraging contributions to social development.

Units were asked to recommend candidates under the published criteria and send electronic nomination materials to o_tw@stu.edu.cn by February 16. Detailed eligibility and submission requirements were provided in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
>
> ⚠️ The China Youth May Fourth Medal criteria and nomination materials are available on the OA system.
