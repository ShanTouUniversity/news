The STU Research Office forwarded the Ministry of Science and Technology guidelines for 2015 applications to the National Basic Research Program, National High-Tech Research and Development Program, and National Science and Technology Support Program on February 21, 2014.

Interested faculty were asked to contact the Research Office promptly and complete online applications through the project application center from March 15 to April 13. The contacts were Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 86502813.

Detailed guidelines, eligibility criteria, and required materials were provided in the forwarded document and OA attachments.
