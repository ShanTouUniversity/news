The Academic Affairs Office announced a course adjustment on February 21, 2014. Because the earlier start of the spring semester conflicted with instructor Chen Hongxi’s teaching and research schedule, selected sessions of three Higher Education Institute sections of *Science and Technology History* would be suspended.

## Suspended sessions

| Class | Session |
| --- | --- |
| 67113 | Week 1, Monday, period 12 (February 24) |
| 67114 | Week 1, Wednesday, periods AB (February 26) |
| 67115 | Week 1, Thursday, periods AB (February 27) |

Students should follow subsequent Academic Affairs updates for any make-up arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
