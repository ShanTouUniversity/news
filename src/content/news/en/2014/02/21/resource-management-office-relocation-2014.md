The STU Resource Management Office announced on February 21, 2014, that its affiliated units had moved to the former Jingwei Center building in the West Campus.

The relocation affected the Security Office, Key Projects Office, Infrastructure Office, Equipment Office, Logistics Office, and General Affairs Office. The units’ office telephone numbers remained unchanged, so university units and community members could continue using their existing contact details.
