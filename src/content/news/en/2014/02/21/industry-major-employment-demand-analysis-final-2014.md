The STU Student Affairs Office announced arrangements for the university final of Guangdong’s first Industry-Major-Employment Demand Analysis Competition on February 21, 2014. Following strong student participation and a judges’ review, 12 teams reached the final.

The final was scheduled for 2:30–5 p.m. on February 26 in E Lecture Hall 201. The competition aims to strengthen career-development awareness and social research skills, improve students’ innovation, entrepreneurship, and employability, and develop feedback from employment services to talent training.

Faculty and students were welcome to attend, and finalist teams were asked to participate on time.
