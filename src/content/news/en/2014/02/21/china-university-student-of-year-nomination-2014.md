The Ministry of Education’s Department of Ideological and Political Work decided to hold the ninth China University Student of the Year selection. The STU Student Affairs Office forwarded the notice and opened the university’s internal nomination process on February 21, 2014.

## Eligibility and nomination

Eligible candidates were registered Chinese students at full-time ordinary higher-education institutions, including graduate, undergraduate, and junior-college students. Candidates should have demonstrated outstanding performance in 2013 in areas such as public service, academic work, innovation, volunteer service, helping others, integrity, family respect, or perseverance. Previously recognized students could also participate.

Each school or college could nominate one student and submit the recommendation form to the Student Affairs Office Education Management Section by the end of February 28. The university would organize an internal selection and recommend no more than two students to the provincial preliminary review.
