During discussions on project cooperation between Shantou University and the National University of Ireland Galway, Brian Hughes, the university’s director of international affairs and a senior lecturer in psychology, will give an English lecture for the STU community on the relationship between psychological stress and physical well-being.

## Lecture information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Title | *Psychological Stress and Physical Well-being: Research on Cultural, Social, and Personal Aspects* |
| Time | 4–5 p.m. on Wednesday, February 26 |
| Venue | E409 |
| Language | English |
| Speaker | Dr. Brian Hughes |

Hughes received a PhD in psychology from NUI Galway in 1998, later established a psychology department at Dublin Business School, and served as president of the Psychological Society of Ireland. His research focuses on psychological stress, particularly its effects on cardiovascular health. The lecture was open to the entire STU community.
