Following notices from the Ministry of Education and Guangdong Education Department, Shantou University opened its 2014 selection and recommendation process for a national quality video open course on February 20, 2014. The university planned to recommend one course in the science and cultural literacy category.

## Selection requirements

Courses had to be completed video open courses meeting the 2013 technical production standards. Except for distinctive, high-level courses, applicants were asked to avoid duplicating similar courses published on the iCourse website or submitted to the Ministry of Education in the previous two years. Priority would be given to widely relevant cultural-literacy courses and academic lectures, with encouragement for bilingual courses on traditional Chinese culture.

Interested applicants were to contact Academic Affairs by March 10 to confirm eligibility and submit materials by March 24. The university would organize expert review, select one recommendation, publish it internally, and submit it to the Education Department.

Materials included the course application, complete course videos, paper metadata, and a course disc. Electronic applications were to be sent to liz@stu.edu.cn. The contact was Li Zhe at 86502736.
