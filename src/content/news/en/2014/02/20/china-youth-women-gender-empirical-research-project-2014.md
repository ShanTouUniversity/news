With support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the China Women’s Studies Association and the Shantou University Women’s Studies Center launched the 2014–2016 China Young Scholars Women and Gender Empirical Research Program. The program aims to encourage empirical research from a gender perspective and develop new researchers in the field.

## Fields and eligibility

The program supports empirical research on women and gender issues arising during comprehensive reform, including economic development, democratic politics, social development, cultural development, and ecological civilization. Applicants should be doctoral students or young scholars with a master’s degree or above and research experience, generally no older than 40.

Applicants had to complete five copies of the Chinese application form and mail them to the China Women’s Studies Association office by March 15, while submitting an electronic copy at the same time. Research reports were due by August 31, 2016. Applications had to explain the background, aims, methods, academic and policy value, and provide a detailed budget.

## Funding

The program planned to fund 10–15 projects with a total budget of RMB 150,000. Each project would receive RMB 5,000–10,000. The organizers planned to announce selected projects by the end of April. Funding would be distributed in two installments: 60 percent at project launch and the remaining 40 percent after the final report passed review.
