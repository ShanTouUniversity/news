Three Shantou University Arts Education Center classes will have schedule changes: Wind Instrument Performance Advanced Class (course no. 67052), Clarinet Performance (course no. 67034) and Bassoon Performance (course no. 67037). Their classes scheduled for March 3 will be canceled and made up on March 8 in Room 203 of the Arts Education Center.

The make-up session for course 67052 will be held during periods 6–7, course 67034 during periods 8–9, and course 67037 during periods A–B.
