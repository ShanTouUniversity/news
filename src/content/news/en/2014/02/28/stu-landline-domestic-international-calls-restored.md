Shantou University’s Network and Information Center reported on February 28 that domestic and international calling from campus landlines had returned to normal.

According to the center, Guangdong Mobile’s Shantou branch upgraded the university’s voice lines and short-number network early that morning. A subsequent backend data adjustment by the carrier temporarily prevented campus landlines from making domestic and international calls after 10:30 a.m. The carrier responded urgently, and the service was restored.

Users who continue to experience problems may contact the Network and Information Center at 86503400-1.
