It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on February 28, 2014, to carry out the mid-term review for the Teaching Reform Special Projects approved in 2013.

## Review Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Projects for review | Teaching Reform Special Projects approved in 2013 (see attachment) |
| Review method | 1. Sub-projects submit mid-term review forms to the unit leader and the university’s Quality Project Management website (by March 14); 2. Units conduct self-inspections and submit a summary table to the Academic Affairs Office (by March 24); 3. The Academic Affairs Office organizes the review and announces results in early April |
| Review content | Progress, use of the first batch of funding, interim achievements, main problems and improvement measures, and plans for the next stage; interim achievements include teaching materials, courseware, papers and other outputs |
| Use of results | Announcement of the list of projects that passed and feedback, with the second batch of research funding to be allocated |

## Timeline and Submission

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project submission | By March 14, electronic version to the unit leader and the Quality Project Management website at http://project.stu.edu.cn (log in with the project leader’s campus network account) |
| Unit submission | By March 24, complete the self-inspection and submit the *Shantou University Teaching Reform Special Project Self-inspection Summary Table* to the Academic Affairs Office, contact email liz@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Academic Affairs Office |

Details and attachments are available on the OA system.
