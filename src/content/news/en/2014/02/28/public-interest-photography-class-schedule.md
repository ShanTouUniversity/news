Shantou University’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication announced the spring 2014 schedule for the Public-Interest Photography course. Course no. 69741 will meet from 2 to 4 p.m. on Wednesday, March 5, in Room 207 of Building E.

The school asked students enrolled in the course to share the information with one another.
