It is reported that the Organization and United Front Department of the Party Committee of Shantou University announced on February 28 that the 74th Party Basic Knowledge Training Class is scheduled to open in March this year. Party general branches and directly affiliated party branches have begun recommending and reviewing candidates.

## Eligibility and Nomination Requirements

Under the arrangement, nominees must meet two conditions: they must have submitted their application for party membership more than one year ago and been designated as key development targets, and they must be able to attend all sessions normally.

Each branch needs to sign off on the registration forms, affix its official seal, and submit them to the Party School Office (Room 315, Administrative Center) by March 13, 2014, along with sending the summary list of trainees by email to o_dxb@stu.edu.cn. The final list will be confirmed after review by the Party School Office.
