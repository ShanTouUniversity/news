Shantou University’s Infrastructure Construction Office published the bidding result for the stainless-steel balcony railing construction and installation project at the Longquan Garden staff dormitories on February 28. Following the recommendation of the university’s Tendering and Bidding Leading Group, Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd. was selected as the winning bidder, with a bid amount of RMB 32,069.90.

## Bid Information

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Project | Longquan Garden staff dormitory stainless-steel balcony railing construction and installation |
| Winning bidder | Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Bid amount | RMB 32,069.90 |
| Publicity period | February 28 to March 3, 2014 |
