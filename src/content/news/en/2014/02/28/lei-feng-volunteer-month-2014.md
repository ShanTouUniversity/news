It is reported that Shantou University will hold a "Learn from Lei Feng Volunteer Service Month" campaign throughout March 2014 to promote the Lei Feng spirit and volunteerism. Coordinated by the Communist Youth League Committee and the Youth Volunteers Association, the campaign involves volunteer organizations from all colleges and comprises 15 service projects.

## Program Overview

| No. | Project | Time & Venue | Highlights | Organizer |
|-----|---------|-------------|------------|-----------|
| 1 | "Love Fills the Community" | March, downtown streets and neighborhoods | Street service fair; "Sunshine Community" home visits, housekeeping, tutoring, free repairs, legal outreach | University Youth League Committee / Youth Volunteers Association |
| 2 | "Close Family" | March, TBD | Parent-child games, massage exercises, calligraphy gifts | Liberal Arts Youth League Committee |
| 3 | Sunshine School Tutoring | Saturdays in March, Sunshine School | Interest classes: painting, brush calligraphy, origami, music | Science Youth League Committee |
| 4 | Caring for Elderly | March 22, Cunxin Nursing Home | Cleaning, companionship, and interactive activities | Engineering Youth League Committee |
| 5 | Sanpu Mountain Cleanup | March, Mt. Sanpu | Litter collection and environmental awareness signage | Engineering Youth League Committee |
| 6 | Welfare Home Visits | Saturdays in March, Jinping Welfare Home | Cleaning, decorating, chatting, dumpling-making | Law School Youth League Committee |
| 7 | Special Children Care | March, Cunxin Special Education School | Volunteer care for children with special needs | Business School Youth League Committee |
| 8 | Wuqiao Tutoring | March, Wuqiao Community | Academic tutoring and mentoring for assisted students | Business School Youth League Committee |
| 9 | Dahua No. 2 Primary School Tutoring | Sundays in March, Dahua No. 2 Primary School | One-on-one tutoring and outdoor games | Science & Journalism Youth League Committees |
| 10 | Disabled Children Tutoring | March, Disabled Persons Activity Center | Painting and collage to develop creativity | CK Art and Design Youth League Committee |
| 11 | Home Rehabilitation for Children with Cerebral Palsy | March, Shantou urban area | Long-term home visits and social integration support | Zhicheng College / Ivy Service-Learning Association |
| 12 | Used Textbook Sale & Clothing Drive | March, STU campus | Collecting and selling used books; donating clothes to underprivileged schools in Anhui | Zhicheng College / Ivy Association |
| 13 | "Youth, Volunteerism, Chinese Dream" Free Clinic | March, communities and parks | Blood pressure/glucose checks, doctor consultations, health education | Medical College Youth League Committee & Hospice |
| 14 | Rural Medical Poverty Alleviation | March, TBD rural area | Free consultations, medicine, checkups, and health outreach | Medical College Youth League Committee |
| 15 | "Be a Little Lei Feng" | Late March, Zoutang Primary School | Role-model sharing, family-themed activities, team-building games | Graduate School Youth League Committee |

The campaign spans community service, tutoring, elderly and disability care, environmental protection, and medical outreach, aiming to spread positive energy and inspire more young people to engage in volunteer service.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee)
