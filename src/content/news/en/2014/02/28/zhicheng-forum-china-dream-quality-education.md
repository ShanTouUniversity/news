The 15th Zhicheng Forum, organized by Zhicheng College, will be held at 9 a.m. on March 2 in the Library Lecture Hall. The forum will focus on the Chinese Dream and people-centered quality education.

Attendees who successfully registered are asked to arrive 15 minutes before the event.
