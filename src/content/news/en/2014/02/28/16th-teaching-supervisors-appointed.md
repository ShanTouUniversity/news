It is reported that Shantou University has appointed its 16th teaching supervision team under the university's regulations for teaching supervision. The team will serve a two-year term and take part in undergraduate teaching inspection, supervision and evaluation to help improve teaching quality.

## Team Members

The team consists of Lan Huizhu, Zhu Liqun, Lin Xiuqing, Zheng Xiaoxin and Guo Xikun, listed in the order of Chinese surname strokes.

The university asked relevant units to support and cooperate with the teaching supervisors.
