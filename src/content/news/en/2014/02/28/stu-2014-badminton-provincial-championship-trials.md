The Guangdong Universities Badminton Championship will be held in May. To select student badminton athletes to represent the university, Shantou University’s Sports Department will hold the 2014 “Road to the Provincial Championship” badminton trials at the gymnasium from March 19 to 30.

The trials will take place on Wednesday afternoons and throughout Saturdays and Sundays. The Sports Department invited schools and residential colleges to organize teams and register for the trials. Detailed competition arrangements and registration requirements are provided in the Regulations for the 2014 STU “Road to the Provincial Championship” Badminton Trials.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
> ⚠️ The competition regulations and related attachments are available on the OA system.
