The China Life Insurance Cup New Year Long-Distance Run will take place in downtown Shantou on Saturday, March 15, with the race venue at Haibin Road. Following previous arrangements, Shantou University plans to form a team for the event, which includes the men’s and women’s adult 10-kilometer races among other events.

Prospective participants should send their registration information to the Sports Department at o_tyb@stu.edu.cn by 5 p.m. on March 3. Participants should be in good health, exercise regularly by running and have no condition that makes physical activity unsuitable. They must provide a resident identity card as proof of age and sign a Voluntary Participation Responsibility Form. Participants are responsible for the relevant insurance and accident insurance arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department)
> ⚠️ The race regulations and related attachments are available on the OA system.
