It is reported that, in line with documents from the State Council Academic Degrees Committee and Ministry of Education requirements, the Graduate School of Shantou University has arranged the 2014 application process for master's degrees by applicants with equivalent education qualifications. Applicants must complete online registration and information submission within the prescribed time, upload electronic photos, and visit the campus for on-site confirmation, image and fingerprint collection, and signing of an integrity commitment letter.

## Qualification Review Materials

Applicants must complete registration at the Graduate School, submitting originals and copies of their bachelor's degree certificate, final diploma and ID card (originals returned after verification), a copy of undergraduate transcripts stamped by the personnel department of the graduating institution or employer, and sealed duplicate sets of materials from their employer covering their résumé, political conduct, work performance, research achievements, professional competence and foreign language proficiency. Applicants also need to fill in the required forms and pay a qualification review fee of RMB 150.

## Confirmation Process

According to the arrangement, applicants who completed on-site confirmation last year do not need to repeat it this year. On the confirmation day, applicants must present valid identification and submit originals and copies of their bachelor's diploma, degree certificate (or master's certificate) and second-generation ID card, two copies of the completed qualification review application form, and their platform registration ID; all photocopies must be personally signed to guarantee authenticity. The "personal commitment" section must be signed by the applicant and validated with seals from the school or institute.

The university will then collect the applicant's photograph and fingerprints — thumbs, index and middle fingers of both hands, at least six fingers with four captures each — before generating a qualification review form for the applicant to confirm and sign.

The university reminded applicants to complete on-site confirmation on schedule; otherwise, they will bear the consequences of being unable to sit the national equivalent-education master's degree examination on time.
