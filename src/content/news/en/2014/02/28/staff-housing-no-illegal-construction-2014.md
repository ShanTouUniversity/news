It is reported that the Resource Management Office and the Security Office jointly issued a notice on February 28, 2014, reiterating the ban on unauthorized construction in staff housing areas.

Staff housing is provided only to eligible faculty members for transitional use, with ownership remaining with the university. Modifications or structures in public areas such as corridors, stairwells and rooftop terraces are strictly prohibited to avoid damaging structures, blocking fire exits and affecting cleanliness.

Following complaints and inspections, unauthorized structures were found in the Siheyuan area of the West Zone. Residents concerned are required to remove them by March 10, otherwise relevant departments will arrange demolition at the residents’ expense.
