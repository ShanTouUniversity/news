The China Society for Human Rights Studies has opened nationwide applications for its 2014 research projects. The national academic organization in the field of human rights holds special consultative status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council. Shantou University’s Research Affairs Office circulated the application information to relevant university units.

This year’s projects are divided into key projects, policy-oriented projects and general projects. Key projects address issues of major theoretical and practical significance in human rights research; policy-oriented projects respond to human rights research needs proposed by relevant government departments; and general projects focus on forward-looking topics with academic value.

| Category | Funding per project | Required research output |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Key project | RMB 80,000 | Approximately 200,000 Chinese characters in papers, research reports and other outputs |
| Policy-oriented project | RMB 20,000 | 30,000 to 50,000 Chinese characters in papers, research reports and other outputs |
| General project | RMB 20,000 | 20,000 to 30,000 Chinese characters in papers, research reports and other outputs |

For key and policy-oriented projects, half of the funding is paid as start-up funding after approval and the remainder after a successful completion review. General projects also receive funding in approval and completion stages. Applicants should have strong research capabilities and a relevant research foundation, hold an intermediate or higher professional title, or have obtained a doctoral degree. They must submit related papers, monographs or research reports. Each applicant may apply for only one project, and researchers with unfinished projects from the society may not apply this year.

Projects have a one-year research period. Applicants should email an electronic copy of the Project Application Form to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn for a formal review by March 11. By March 13, they should submit 10 printed copies of the qualified application to the Research Affairs Office and send the final electronic version to the same address. The contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun, and the telephone number is 86502563.
