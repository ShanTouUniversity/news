Shantou University’s International Business Case Competition Club (IBCC) will hold its spring recruitment interviews on March 8. Students from all schools and programs may apply, regardless of major or year of study.

IBCC works with companies, organizes business competitions on and off campus, and provides a channel for university-business cooperation. The club aims to give students opportunities to practice and improve problem-solving skills while helping companies connect with potential talent.

Interviews will begin at 9 a.m. on March 8 in Room D201. The process will include a one-minute self-introduction, a 10-minute leaderless group case discussion and a 15-minute question-and-answer session. Applications close at 10 p.m. on March 6. Applicants may submit a paper form at the collection folder near the poster board area of the Second Dining Hall, use the electronic application form or register at the on-site booth at the Golden Triangle on the morning of March 7. Submission details for the electronic form are provided in the OA attachment.
