Shantou University’s Research Office has forwarded a call for papers from the Guangdong Federation of Social Sciences for the 3rd Southern China Think-Tank Forum. The forum is planned for May and will focus on creating new advantages for Guangdong’s reform and opening up.

Submissions should address the forum theme, follow academic standards, be no longer than 3,000 Chinese characters and not have been published previously. Authors should include the topic area, name, affiliation and other required information, and email papers to `tqizhong@126.com` or `13922281819@139.com` by April 20. Selected authors will be invited to attend the forum.

Suggested areas include reform and opening up, government functions, social governance, ecological development, cultural development, rule-of-law construction, Party-building innovation and the Maritime Silk Road.
