Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has disclosed a proposed cost increase for the water-pipeline renovation project in the faculty housing area. The project began on December 11, 2013. During construction, additional meter and valve wells were deemed necessary to connect the new and existing water pipelines. The preliminary estimate for the added work is about RMB 8,000, below the university’s 10% limit.

The disclosure is made under Shantou University’s regulations on bidding for infrastructure projects. Anyone with comments on the proposed cost increase may submit them in writing to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on February 27, 2014.
