Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will build a campus produce store on the vacant land opposite the Fourth Dining Hall and has appointed Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. as the contractor. The project involves a single-story steel-structure building with sandwich-panel walls and roof, covering about 50 square meters. The awarded contract value is RMB 59,200.

Construction is scheduled to begin on February 26 and finish by March 27. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during the work. The university asks students and staff to take care near the site and avoid remaining in the construction area.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Infrastructure Office)
