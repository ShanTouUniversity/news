The Christian Studies Center at Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts is recruiting an academic and publishing assistant to work on campus. The position supports publishing projects, publicity for conferences, courses and academic exchanges, website maintenance, archive management and routine administrative work.

## Application information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Requirements | Bachelor’s degree or above; organizational, office-software and communication skills; publishing, website or academic-administration experience preferred |
| Deadline | March 20, 2014 |
| Submission | Send a résumé and copies of relevant certificates by email or post |
| Address | Christian Studies Center, School of Liberal Arts, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063 |
| Contact | Ms. Chen |
| Telephone | 0754-86503655 |
| Email | `chenchen@stu.edu.cn` |

Shortlisted applicants will be contacted for interviews. The center said application materials will be used only for this recruitment; original documents will be returned after interview review, while other materials will not be returned.
