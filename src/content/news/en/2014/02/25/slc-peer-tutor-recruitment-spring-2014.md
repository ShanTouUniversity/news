Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) is recruiting peer tutors for the spring 2014 semester from undergraduates on the main campus. Tutors will help classmates understand courses, share study strategies and organize course-related discussions. Recruitment is coordinated by SLC student liaisons in the relevant schools and departments.

## Eligibility and responsibilities

Applicants must be second-year or above undergraduates on the main campus, demonstrate responsibility and good communication skills, and have strong academic performance. Their grade in a course they intend to support must rank in the top 20% of that course; in special cases, a supporting statement from the instructor or another knowledgeable faculty member may be provided.

Peer tutors share learning strategies and experience, answer classmates’ questions, organize subject discussions and provide support based on learners’ progress and needs. The usual service period is Weeks 3–16 of the semester. Compensation follows the university’s work-study peer-tutor standard.

Applicants should first contact the SLC student liaison in the relevant school or department to learn about specific course needs and criteria. Applications must be submitted through the student work-study application system by midnight on Monday, March 3. SLC can be reached at `86502250` (short number `882250`) or `slc@stu.edu.cn`.
