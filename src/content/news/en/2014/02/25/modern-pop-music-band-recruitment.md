Shantou University’s Arts Education Center is forming a modern pop music band and recruiting students from across the university. The group will include jazz drums, electric bass, electric guitar, violin, keyboard and male and female vocalists.

The band will be directed by Liu Dawei, who previously worked with American director Ryan on the musical *PIPPIN* as its music director.

## Audition information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 2–4 p.m. on March 5, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 303, third floor, Arts Education Center (Music Production Room) |
| Applicants | Students interested in modern pop performance or singing |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Arts Education Center)
