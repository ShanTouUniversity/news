Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will build stone retaining walls on both sides of the flood-drainage culvert project and has appointed Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. as the contractor. The work includes stone foundations and retaining walls, totaling about 150 cubic meters, with an awarded contract value of RMB 75,000.

Construction is scheduled to begin on February 26 and finish by March 27. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during the work. The university asks pedestrians to take care and avoid remaining at the construction site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office and Infrastructure Office)
