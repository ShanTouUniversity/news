To support physical activity among students and staff, Shantou University sports venues will reopen for campus use and booking from February 26. Physical-education classes and athletic-team training will continue to receive priority.

Detailed opening hours, booking procedures and precautions will be published on the Physical Education Department’s website. Students and staff should follow the department’s updates for the latest arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
