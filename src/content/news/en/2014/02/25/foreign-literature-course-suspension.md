Shantou University’s Chinese Department has announced a temporary adjustment to Class 67102, *Introduction to Foreign Literary Classics*. The class scheduled for periods 6 and 7 in E409 on February 27 will be suspended, and a make-up session will be arranged later.

Students enrolled in the course should follow subsequent announcements about the make-up class.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
