With approval in place, the Shantou University Infrastructure Office will renovate the north-side office on the second floor of the Network and Information Center. The work involves removing the interior and rebuilding it. Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. was appointed as contractor, with a contract value of RMB 30,600.

Work was scheduled to begin on February 19 and finish by March 20. Noise, vibration, and dust could occur during construction. Staff working in the building and passing pedestrians were asked to exercise caution and avoid lingering near the site.

The project manager was Lu Chunxiong and the site management engineer was Chen Xiwen; contact details were provided in the construction notice.
