Shantou University and the National University of Ireland, Galway, will hold an information session on their exchange program on February 26. Representatives from NUI Galway will introduce the 2014–2015 exchange program and answer questions from students.

## Session information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 2:30–3:30 p.m. on Wednesday, February 26 |
| Venue | Room E409 |
| Exchange places | Five students each year |
| Eligible students | Second- and third-year students outside the School of Medicine; applicants are first- and second-year students at the time of application |

Exchange students continue to pay tuition at STU while studying in Ireland, but do not pay tuition, application fees or examination fees to NUI Galway. Students may apply to four NUI Galway colleges other than the colleges of medicine, nursing and health sciences. STU medical students are not included in the receiving scope.

## Indicative costs

For reference, 2013–2014 tuition for non-EU students was €6,000 per semester or €12,000 per year in the humanities, business and law, and €7,750 per semester in science and engineering. If six or more STU students were successfully admitted in a semester or academic year, the tuition could be reduced to €5,000 per semester or €10,000 per year. Average living expenses, including accommodation, were estimated at about €1,000 per month.

Course schedules and application details will be subject to the information published on NUI Galway’s international-student website.
