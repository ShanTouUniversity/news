Shantou University’s Research Office has forwarded a call from the Guangdong Provincial Publicity Department for research topics under the 2014 Theory Guangdong priority-project program. The university is seeking forward-looking and strategic topics from relevant units.

Proposals should reflect STU’s strengths and Guangdong’s current and future development needs, address major economic and social issues, and provide theoretical support and policy reference for deepening reform and opening up. Interested researchers should email their topic titles to `ymhuang@stu.edu.cn` by February 28. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun at `86502563`.

The notice also includes the 2013 list of funded major practical and basic-theory projects for reference. Detailed application requirements are provided in the Research Office’s OA attachments.
