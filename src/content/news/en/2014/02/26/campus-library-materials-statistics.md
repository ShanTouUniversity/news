The Shantou University Library has begun collecting 2013 book and materials statistics. Resource rooms on the main campus should complete the required data form and email it to `gnweng@stu.edu.cn` by March 7.

Questions may be directed to the Library at `86502021`. The specific fields and reporting format are provided in the table attached to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Library)
> ⚠️ The book and materials statistics form is available in the OA attachments.
