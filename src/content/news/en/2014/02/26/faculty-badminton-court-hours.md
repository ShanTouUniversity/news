To enrich staff cultural and sports activities and promote fitness, the Shantou University union will continue providing a badminton court in the gymnasium this semester. Sessions run from 6 to 8 p.m. every Wednesday, beginning this week and continuing through the end of the semester.

Staff members are welcome to participate.

> Source: STU OA Notice (University Union)
