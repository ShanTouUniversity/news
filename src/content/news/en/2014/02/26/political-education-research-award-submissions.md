Shantou University’s Publicity Department has forwarded a call from the National Association for Research on Political Education in Higher Education for outstanding works marking the 30th anniversary of the establishment of political education as an academic discipline.

## Scope and deadline

The call covers academic monographs, papers and research reports publicly published from March 1984 through December 2013. Units on the main campus should compile their materials and submit them to the Publicity Department by March 5 for centralized submission to the Guangdong association. Electronic materials should be sent as required to `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn`.

Detailed requirements and submission formats are provided in the OA attachments.
