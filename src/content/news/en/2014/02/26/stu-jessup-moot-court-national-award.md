The 12th Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition was held at East China University of Political Science and Law from February 19 to 21. The undergraduate team from Shantou University’s School of Law ranked sixth nationally, reached the quarterfinals, won a national first prize and received the Best Respondent Memorial award.

## Competition performance

The event drew 40 university teams, including those from Peking University, Renmin University of China, China University of Political Science and Law, Xiamen University and Wuhan University. Rounds were conducted in English and judged by scholars, judges, prosecutors and lawyers from around the world.

During the preliminary rounds, STU faced teams from Fudan University, China Foreign Affairs University, Shenzhen University and Peking University Law School. The team advanced with strong English advocacy, international-law knowledge and courtroom adaptability. It later lost 2–3 to the University of International Business and Economics in the quarterfinals and did not advance to the global finals in Washington.

This was the second consecutive year that STU Law reached the national top eight and won a first prize, and the university’s third appearance in the competition. The undergraduate team began selection and preparation in June 2013, with support from the School of Law’s faculty and coach Gu Junfeng.

Organized by the International Law Students Association, the Jessup competition is widely regarded as the “Olympics of the legal world.” China has participated since 2002.
