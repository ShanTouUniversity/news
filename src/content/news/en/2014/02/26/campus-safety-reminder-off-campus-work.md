Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has issued a safety reminder urging students to stay alert during off-campus internships, job searches and part-time work. Students should improve their ability to identify false information and disreputable intermediaries, tell family members, friends or teachers where interviews or work will take place, and keep their phones available.

Students are also advised to check dormitory doors and windows when leaving or sleeping at night, travel with companions where possible and stay in touch with family and friends to reduce the risk of fraud, theft and robbery. Suspicious situations should be reported to university departments or the police.

The campus 24-hour emergency number is `82904110`; the local police-station number is `82540966`.
