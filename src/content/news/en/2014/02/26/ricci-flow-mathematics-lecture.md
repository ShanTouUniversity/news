Shantou University’s School of Science will host a “Toward Modern Mathematics” lecture by Zhu Xiping, professor, doctoral supervisor and vice president of Sun Yat-sen University. His talk will focus on “The Ricci Flow and Its Applications.”

## Lecture information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 10:30 a.m. on Monday, March 3 |
| Venue | Mathematics Laboratory |
| Topic | *The Ricci Flow and Its Applications* |
| Focus | An overview of Ricci-flow theory and recent applications to four-dimensional manifolds with positive curvature |

Zhu’s research focuses on the geometry and topology of manifolds. He has received the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, a second prize from the Chinese Academy of Sciences for natural science and the ICCM Morningstar Award in Mathematics.
