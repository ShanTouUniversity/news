Shantou University’s Human Resources Office has forwarded a notice from the Shantou E-Government Administration Office on establishing an e-government expert database and is inviting recommendations from university units.

Applicants should complete two copies of the Expert Recommendation Form for the Shantou E-Government Expert Database and provide a copy of their highest professional qualification or highest academic degree. Materials must be submitted to the Human Resources Office, Room 315, Administrative Center, by March 30. Eligibility and form requirements are provided in the OA attachments.

For inquiries, call `86503390` or email `o_rsc@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
> ⚠️ The expert-database notice and recommendation form are available in the OA attachments.
