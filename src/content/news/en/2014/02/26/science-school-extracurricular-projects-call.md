Shantou University’s School of Science has opened applications for extracurricular projects in the spring semester of the 2013–2014 academic year. Student organizations, classes and independent teams are invited to design activities that support personal development and social adaptability.

The six themes are public service, sportsmanship, career development, humanities, leadership and innovation. Eligible applicants include the School of Science Student Union, the Youth Volunteer Association, Mingli Newspaper, the Radio Club, departmental Youth League organizations, classes and student-formed teams.

Applicants should complete the School of Science Extracurricular Project Application Form and email the electronic version to `13sjjin@stu.edu.cn` by 5 p.m. on February 28. A preliminary presentation is tentatively scheduled for 2:30 p.m. on March 5, after which the school Youth League Committee will conduct an initial review.
