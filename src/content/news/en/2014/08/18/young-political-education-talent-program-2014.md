Shantou University opened its internal recommendation process for the Young and Middle-aged Outstanding Talent Support Program in Political Education and could recommend one candidate. Applicants should have achievements and development potential in political education research, teaching, talent development or related practice and should demonstrate research, innovation and public-education abilities, as well as the capacity to connect theory with practice and apply results.

Applicants should generally hold an associate senior or higher professional title and be no older than 45. The program was open to relevant staff engaged in political education research and practice at universities nationwide, as well as outstanding applicants from other disciplines interested in the field. Each university unit could recommend no more than one person, after which a university panel would select the final candidate.

Units were required to submit the application form, work plan and supporting materials to the Human Resources Office in Room 315 of the Administration Center by September 5, with electronic copies sent to `wing@stu.edu.cn`. Detailed requirements for the form, recommendation and supporting materials were provided in the OA attachments. The contact was Chen Ying at 86502340.
