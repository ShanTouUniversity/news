Shantou University's Student Affairs Office scheduled a training session on the Mental Health Law for September 2, from 3 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. Zhao Jingbo, a professor at Southern Medical University, will lead the session in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Conference Center (ACC).

The session is intended for faculty at the university's counseling center and student-affairs staff, including Party secretaries and counselors from schools and colleges. It will address the law and responses to mental health issues, with the stated aim of improving staff members' ability to recognize mental illness among students and intervene early in psychological crises.
