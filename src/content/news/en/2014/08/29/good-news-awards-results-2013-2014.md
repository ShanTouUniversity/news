Shantou University's News Center announced the results of its 2013–14 Good News awards. The competition received 59 text entries and 27 photo submissions (including photo series). An anonymous review by external experts selected 18 text entries and 10 photo submissions for first-, second- and third-place awards.

The text category had three first prizes, five second prizes and 10 third prizes; the photo category had two, three and five, respectively. The full list of awardees and placements is in the OA attachments. Unit correspondents were asked to collect certificates and sign for prize money at Room 315 of the New Administrative Center by September 3.
