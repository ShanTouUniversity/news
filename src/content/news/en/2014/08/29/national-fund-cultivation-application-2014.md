Shantou University's Research Office has opened applications for its 2014 National Fund Cultivation Program. Eligible faculty working at the main campus may apply through their units under the university's management measures and must complete the program application form.

The program continues to prioritize three humanities and social sciences fields: local government development, Guangdong–Taiwan business cooperation, and Chaoshan history and culture. Applicants may also propose their own topics, which must identify a clear research subject or question. Applications in a priority field must specify that field on the form.

Units must review applications and submit printed materials and electronic files by September 30. Natural sciences applications should be emailed to cszhu@stu.edu.cn; humanities and social sciences applications should be sent to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn. Full eligibility and application details are in the OA attachments.
