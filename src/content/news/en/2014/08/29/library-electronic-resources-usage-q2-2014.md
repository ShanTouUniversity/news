Shantou University's Library released its electronic-resource usage statistics for the second quarter of 2014. Among Chinese resources, CNKI's China Academic Journals database recorded 161,768 downloads, the Bokkan periodicals database logged 612,669 pages read, Duxiu recorded 115,548 pages delivered, and the Kuke digital music library logged 207,532 tracks played. The report also covered Chinese journals, theses and dissertations, e-books, academic videos and financial databases.

For international resources, EI Village recorded 21,037 searches, Elsevier ScienceDirect had 38,734 downloads, and Web of Science recorded 14,833 searches. Other journals, databases and e-books were also included. The report used different measures across platforms, including downloads, searches, pages read, plays and clicks.
