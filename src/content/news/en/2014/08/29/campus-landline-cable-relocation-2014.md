Telecom cables serving campus landlines will be relocated on August 31 and September 1 to accommodate construction on site A of the first-phase Student Residence College project. Landline service in affected areas will be interrupted during the work; IP phones used by university units will remain available.

From 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. on August 31, the interruption will affect staff residences A8 and A11 through A14, the affiliated middle and primary schools, the experts' apartments, the Network Center, the Multidisciplinary Center and the labor union. During the same hours on September 1, affected locations will include residences B1 through B12, A1 through A7, A9 and A10, as well as the university hospital, Building 789 and the seismic station.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Campus Resources Office)
