Shantou University's Infrastructure Office posted a public notice on August 29 concerning proposed additional work for the first-phase renovation of Y Residence. The work, which began on July 1, is estimated to add about RMB 38,000. The additions cover water-pipe work intended to maintain supply to student residences on the east side that were not included in the renovation, as well as repairs to flaking and hollow plaster in corridors and rooms.

The university said the proposed amount is below 10 percent of the project cost, the limit set by its rules. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 1.
