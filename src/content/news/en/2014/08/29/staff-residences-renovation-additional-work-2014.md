Shantou University's Infrastructure Office posted a notice on August 29 proposing additional work for the 2014 repairs to staff residences A and B. The added cost is initially estimated at about RMB 3,500, or approximately RMB 700 per unit.

The project began on August 11. Severely aged and corroded cast-iron waste and kitchen drain pipes were found during construction. Because replacement was not feasible, the proposed work uses specialized measures to prevent seepage and leaks. The university said the addition is within 10 percent of the project cost. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on September 1.
