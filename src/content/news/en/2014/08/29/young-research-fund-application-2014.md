Shantou University's Research Office has opened applications for its 2014 Young Researchers Fund. The program is for full-time natural sciences faculty employed at the main campus. Applicants must hold an intermediate-level or higher professional title or a master's degree or higher, be no older than 40, and have not led a provincial- or ministerial-level research project. The fund provides guidance-oriented support, generally does not fund the same applicant more than once, and is limited to one award per person.

Applications must be submitted through the applicant's supporting unit; direct applications are not accepted. Applicants should provide one printed application and an electronic copy. Units must review the materials and send them to the Research Office by September 30. Electronic files should be sent to cszhu@stu.edu.cn. For inquiries, contact Hu Yunxiang or Zhu Changsheng at 8650 2813.
