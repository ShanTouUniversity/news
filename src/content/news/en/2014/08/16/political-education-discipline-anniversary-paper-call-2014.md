The National Association for Political Education Research in Higher Education planned a conference to mark the 30th anniversary of political education as an academic discipline and invited papers in related fields. Shantou University\'s Publicity Department forwarded the call and invited interested faculty members to prepare submissions according to the conference requirements.

Papers were due by August 30 and should be emailed to the Publicity Department at `o_xcb@stu.edu.cn`. The contact telephone number was 2343.

> ⚠️ Detailed submission requirements are available in the OA attachment.
>
