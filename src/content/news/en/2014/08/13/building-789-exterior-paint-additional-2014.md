Shantou University began renovating the exterior paint of Building 789 on May 16. During construction, the actual exterior paint area increased by 314 square meters from the provisional tender quantity, while interior wall and ceiling latex-paint areas decreased by 96 square meters, resulting in additional work.

The Infrastructure Office preliminarily estimated the additional cost at about 26,000 yuan, below the 10 percent limit set by the university. Units or individuals with concerns could submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 15.
