Shantou University began repairing the East Lecture Hall on July 1, 2014. During construction, the project added repairs to a ceiling ventilation opening after removal of a central air-conditioning unit, removal of a reinforced-concrete equipment-room slab and a new ventilation opening, as well as blackout curtains for lecture halls 4 through 6.

The Infrastructure Office preliminarily estimated the proposed additions at about RMB 26,000, keeping cumulative additions below the university limit of 10 percent. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 25.
