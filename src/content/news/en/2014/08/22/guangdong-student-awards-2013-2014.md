Five individuals or organizations from Shantou University received honors in Guangdong’s 2013–2014 selections for outstanding student cadres, student unions and student societies. The selection was organized by the Communist Youth League Guangdong Provincial Committee and the Guangdong Students’ Federation in March 2014.

The outstanding student-cadre honors went to Zhao ZX of the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and Yang ZF of the Medical College. The Shantou University Students’ Union was named an outstanding student union, while the Medical College Medical Poverty-Alleviation Volunteer Team and the Psychological Growth Association were named outstanding student societies.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
