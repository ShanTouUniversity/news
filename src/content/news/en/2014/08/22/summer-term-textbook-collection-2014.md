Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office reminded faculty members on August 22, 2014, to complete collection of summer-term teaching books by August 29. They were asked to bring the textbook issue form to the Academic Affairs service window in the New Administration Center.

The office said timely collection would help ensure that teaching arrangements proceeded with the required materials.
