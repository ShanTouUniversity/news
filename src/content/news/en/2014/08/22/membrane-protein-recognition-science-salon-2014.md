Shantou University’s College of Science will hold a science salon at 10 a.m. on August 25, 2014, in Lecture Hall 352 of the Science Building. Zhang Yubo, a postdoctoral researcher at the Max F. Perutz Laboratories of the Medical University of Vienna, will present “Understanding the recognition mechanism of membrane proteins by crystallography and MD simulation.”

Zhang earned his bachelor’s degree from Xiangfan University and his master’s and doctoral degrees from Wuhan University. He has conducted postdoctoral research at the Medical University of Vienna since 2012. His interests include biochemistry and biophysics, membrane proteomics, protein crystallography and computer-aided protein-design simulation.
