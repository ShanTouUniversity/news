Shantou University’s Business School announced the establishment of an MBA Enterprise Advisory Committee on August 22, 2014. The committee was created to support the development of the MBA program and its industry-oriented work.

Lin Danming serves as chair. Cao Anding, Chen Enmin, Chen Lewu, Chen Wenbiao, Deng Wenhao, Lin Jiali, Lü Yuan, She Yuanqing and Zhang Nan serve as members, with Yin Xiaoqing as secretary. The committee was formed in July 2014 and will serve a three-year term.
