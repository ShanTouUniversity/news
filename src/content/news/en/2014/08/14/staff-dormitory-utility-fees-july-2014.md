Shantou University announced the July 2014 utility fees for staff dormitories. Meters were read on August 1 and 2, with the previous reading taken on July 1. Staff could log in to the university utility-fee management system at `http://power.stu.edu.cn` using their campus network accounts.

Staff with questions or objections could visit the Water and Electricity Center for verification from August 13 to 15 or call 2489. After the verification period, the Finance Office would deduct the fees from the relevant August 2014 salary accounts if no objections had been raised.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
