Shantou University scheduled a review meeting on preparations for the 2014 freshman orientation for 3 p.m. on September 1 in Room 201 of the Administrative Center. The meeting will review arrangements for the new academic year, with one representative from each relevant school, college and administrative unit asked to attend.

Participating units include the Party general branches of the schools, Zhicheng College, and offices responsible for admissions, student affairs, academic affairs, finance, campus resources, security, the library and network and information services, among other teaching and support units.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and University Administration Office and Student Affairs Office)
