Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department began a review and clean-up of cadres’ participation in commercial training under requirements from higher authorities. Units were asked to investigate fee-based training attended by in-service cadres at deputy-section level or above and complete the relevant forms.

Stamped forms were due to the Organization and United Front Work Department by noon on August 29 for consolidation and submission to the provincial education authorities. Training organized or jointly organized by personnel and organizational departments, as well as training for internal university participants, was outside the scope of this review.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ The higher-level notice and statistical forms are available on the OA system.
