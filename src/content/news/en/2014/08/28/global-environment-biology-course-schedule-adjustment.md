Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced schedule changes for Institute of Marine Biology courses in Global Environment and Biology, covering classes 71732 and 71742. The changes included cancellations on September 1 and 3 and make-up sessions on September 9, 13 and 17 in rooms E201-2 or E203.

For class 71732, the September 1 third-and-fourth-period class would be made up during periods A and B on September 9 in E201-2, while the September 3 third-and-fourth-period class would be made up during periods six and seven on September 13 in E201-2. For class 71742, sessions cancelled on September 1 and 3 would be made up during period C on September 9, period C on September 17, and periods A and B on September 13, in E201-2 or E203 as listed.
