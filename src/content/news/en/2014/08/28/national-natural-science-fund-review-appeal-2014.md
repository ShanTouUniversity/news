After the National Natural Science Foundation of China released its 2014 project review results, Shantou University’s Research Office announced arrangements for appeals against unfunded decisions. Applicants with objections could obtain the required login information from the Research Office and complete, confirm and submit an appeal through the foundation’s online information system.

Online appeals were due by September 6. After submitting online, applicants also had to print two identical paper copies, sign them and deliver them to the Research Office by September 4. Appeals based only on disagreement with expert academic judgments, appeals filed by someone other than the applicant, late submissions or incomplete procedures would not be accepted.

The contacts were Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 86502813.
