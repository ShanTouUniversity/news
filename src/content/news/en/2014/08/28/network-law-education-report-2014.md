Shantou University’s Social Sciences Office was scheduled to hold a report titled “Problems and Countermeasures in Internet Legal Development” from 9 to 11 a.m. on August 30, 2014, in the university auditorium. Shen Yiyong was listed as the speaker, and all students taking the summer 2014 Ideological and Political Education course were expected to attend.

The report formed part of the course’s unified teaching content. Students were required to write a paper related to the report or course material and submit it to their instructor. Students were asked to enter the auditorium between 8:45 and 8:55 a.m.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Social Sciences Office)
> ⚠️ The paper template and supplementary study materials are available on the OA system.
