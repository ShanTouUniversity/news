Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Department launched a follow-up review of cadres’ outside company appointments and positions. Units were asked to recheck whether there were unauthorized appointments, improper compensation, or unreported or underreported appointments, and to complete the relevant statistical form.

The review covered current administrative cadres at deputy-division level or above, comparable cadres who had left their posts but had not retired, and cadres at that level who had resigned or retired. Forms signed by the unit’s principal and bearing its official seal were due to the Organization and United Front Work Department by noon on August 29. Units with no such cases were also required to submit a form marked “none.”

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Department)
> ⚠️ The statistical form is available on the OA system.
