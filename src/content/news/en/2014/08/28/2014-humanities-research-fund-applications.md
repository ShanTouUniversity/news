Shantou University opened applications for its 2014 humanities research fund. Eligible applicants were full-time humanities and social sciences faculty appointed at the main campus. Applicants were generally required not to have led a provincial or ministerial-level research project and to have relevant preliminary research experience.

The fund offered priority-area projects and self-selected projects. Priority areas included local government development, Guangdong-Taiwan enterprise cooperation, and Chaoshan history and culture. Applications were generally submitted through the applicant’s unit rather than individually, with the unit reviewing the application before submission.

Units were required to submit paper applications and a consolidated list to the Research Office by September 30 and send electronic materials to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn. The contacts were Huang Yumei and Zhu Yanchun at 86502563.
