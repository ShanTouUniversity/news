After more than 50 millimeters of rain fell in Shantou over three hours, the Shantou Meteorological Observatory issued an orange rainstorm alert at 5:06 p.m. on August 12. Shantou University then asked its units and community members to prepare for the storm.

Units were asked to close windows and doors and switch off power in offices, laboratories, classrooms, dormitories, passageways and nearby public areas. Staff of the university's flood-control command, reservoir and power-management teams, security team and hospital were to strengthen duty coverage. The Infrastructure Office was to notify construction contractors to protect underground works and improve drainage.

Emergency teams and unit duty staff were required to remain at their posts, maintain readiness through available communications equipment, and inspect reservoirs, dams and drainage channels. The university would activate its flood-control emergency plan if necessary.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Flood-Control Command)
