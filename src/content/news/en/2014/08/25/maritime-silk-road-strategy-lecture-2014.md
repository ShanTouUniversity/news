Shantou University will host a lecture titled “The 21st-Century Maritime Silk Road Strategic Concept” at 3 p.m. on August 27, 2014, in Meeting Room 3 of the Academic Exchange Center. Wen Xianyuan, vice president and research fellow of the Guangdong Academy of Social Sciences, will be the speaker.

Wen is also vice president of the Guangdong Maritime Silk Road Research Institute and deputy director of the Guangdong Deng Xiaoping Theory Research Center. He is a principal expert on major National Social Science Fund projects, has led research on major topics and published more than 100 articles and over 10 books.
