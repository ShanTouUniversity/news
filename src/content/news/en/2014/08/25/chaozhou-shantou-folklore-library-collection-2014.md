Shantou University Library’s 11th special-literature feature focused on Chaoshan folk customs. The feature introduced local culture through traditional architecture, household items, food customs, wedding and celebration practices, festival activities, religious culture, Chaoshan-language songs, Chao opera and gongfu tea.

The library noted that these customs reflect local ways of life as well as the values, thinking patterns and aesthetic interests of the Chaoshan community. Its collection contains more than 40 books on the subject, and the feature selected 10 representative titles.

Readers can search the library catalog through the library homepage for further information and a fuller list of the recommended works.
