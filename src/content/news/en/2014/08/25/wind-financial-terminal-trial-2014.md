Shantou University Library will offer trial access to the Wind financial information terminal from September 1 to November 30, 2014. The platform serves financial institutions and academic researchers with domestic and international market news, quotations, data and quantitative tools covering stocks, bonds, commodities, foreign exchange, funds and macroeconomic information.

The trial included a standalone PC version without campus-IP restrictions and a mobile version. Users could download the PC software from the Wind website or search for the relevant app in a mobile app store. After registering on the PC version, users could access the mobile service with an iwind account. Account-application and operating details were provided in the original OA notice.
