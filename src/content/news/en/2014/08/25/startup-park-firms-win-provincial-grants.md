It is reported that, under a notice issued by the Guangdong Department of Human Resources and Social Security on allocating funds for outstanding graduate entrepreneurship projects of 2013, two firms based in the Student Startup Park of Shantou University have received funding: Shantou Chengu Network Technology Co., Ltd. with 100,000 yuan and Shantou Zhichi Interactive Network Technology Co., Ltd. with 50,000 yuan. The grants will be used for production and business activities related to their projects.

## About the Program

Under Guangdong's evaluation measures, the province selects a batch of outstanding graduate entrepreneurship projects each year and provides one-time grants. Applicants must be full-time university students in their graduating year or graduates within three years after graduation within Guangdong. Grants come in three tiers: 50,000, 100,000 and 200,000 yuan.

## The Funded Companies

| Company | Grant | Main Business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shantou Chengu Network Technology | 100,000 yuan | Android/iOS/HTML5 app development, UI/UE design, software-hardware integration |
| Shantou Zhichi Interactive Network Technology | 50,000 yuan | Mobile internet products (mobile apps) and IoT technology R&D |

According to reports, Chengu was founded by a group of like-minded partners at the early stage of mobile internet development. Its core products include "E Zhang Tong," "E Xun Tong," a digital media publishing solution platform and "Zhi Ju Tong," and it provides one-stop technical services for well-known brand enterprises.

Zhichi is also an investee of Hong Kong Cyberport's CCMF fund. Its representative products include WeChat account service tool "WeChat Tong," conference check-in service "Huiwu Bao" and interactive venue marketing system "WeChat Big Screen." The company has provided smart event solutions for China's IXDC Interaction Design Week, Chow Tai Fook Jewellery in Shenzhen, the World Health Organization and the Shanghai Blood Center.
