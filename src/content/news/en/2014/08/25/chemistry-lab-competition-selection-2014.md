Shantou University’s College of Science opened an internal selection for the Second Guangdong Undergraduate Chemistry Laboratory Competition. The selection was open to third-year applied chemistry students, with the provincial competition scheduled to take place at Guangzhou University in November 2014.

The first round will be a 90-minute written test from 3 to 4:30 p.m. on September 24 in Room E406. The top 10 students will advance to the practical-operation final, whose detailed arrangements will be announced later.

Applicants had to complete the registration form and email it to the College of Science by September 22. The form was included in the OA attachment.
