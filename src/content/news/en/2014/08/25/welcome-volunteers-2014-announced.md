It is reported that the Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Youth Volunteers Association have jointly announced the volunteer roster for the 2014 new student orientation.

Following an open recruitment call to all students, the initiative received an enthusiastic response. After selection, a total of 180 students have been appointed as volunteers for this year's orientation program to help the Class of 2014 freshmen settle into campus life and begin their studies with a positive outlook.

## At a Glance

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Volunteers Selected | 180 students |
| Announced By | Youth League Committee & Youth Volunteers Association |
| Date | August 25, 2014 |
| Next Steps | Training and work assignments to be notified separately |

The Youth League Committee asks all selected volunteers to prepare for the upcoming training and assignments and to welcome the new cohort with dedication and enthusiasm. The full list is available in the OA system attachment.
