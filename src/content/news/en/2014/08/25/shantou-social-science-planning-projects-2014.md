Shantou University’s Research Office forwarded information on August 25, 2014, about the Shantou Social Science Planning Leading Group Office’s call for 2014 projects. Research areas included the 21st-century Maritime Silk Road and Shantou’s development, overseas Chinese communities, special economic zone reform, urban and rural development, industrial upgrading, ecological development, and social governance and public services.

Project leaders generally needed a senior professional title; those without one required written recommendations from two peers holding senior titles. Each leader could submit only one project, and each team had to include at least three people. After approval, teams were expected to conduct research and submit a final paper or research report of at least 10,000 Chinese characters by March 27, 2015.

Applicants had to submit applications to the Research Office for formal review by September 22 and provide six paper copies of the approved application plus an electronic copy by September 26. The application form was included in the OA attachment.
