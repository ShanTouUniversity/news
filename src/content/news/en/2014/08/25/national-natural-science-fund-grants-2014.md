As of August 25, 2014, Shantou University had received 28 National Natural Science Foundation of China grants for the year, with total funding of RMB 16.69 million. The awards included 21 general projects and seven Young Scientists Fund projects.

The detailed list of funded projects was included in the related attachment and provided a fuller view of the university’s natural-science research awards.
