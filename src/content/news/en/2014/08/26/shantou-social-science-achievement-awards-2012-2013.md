Shantou University’s Research Office released arrangements for the 2012-2013 Shantou Philosophy and Social Sciences Achievement Awards. Eligible submissions included books and papers formally published during the period, as well as unpublished theoretical or research reports adopted by county-level or higher government bodies and relevant municipal departments. Works already honored at provincial or higher-level awards, unfinished project results, literary works, translations and works with disputed copyright were excluded.

Applicants were generally limited to one submission, with additional restrictions for collaborative works. Applicants were asked to complete the application form and send scans of publications, papers or adoption certificates to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn for formal review by September 28. Six paper sets and the required electronic materials were due to the Research Office by October 8; submissions without originals would not be accepted.
