Shantou University’s Personnel Office and Audit and Supervision Office displayed application materials for 2014 professional qualifications in library resources, publishing and performance. The materials were available in Room 315 of the Administrative Center from August 26 to September 1, during weekday hours of 8:30 a.m. to noon and 3 p.m. to 5:30 p.m.

Faculty and staff with concerns about an application or its supporting work were asked to provide specific evidence to the Audit and Supervision Office or the Personnel Office by September 1. The published contacts were 0754-86502346 and o_jj@stu.edu.cn for the former, and 0754-86502340 and rscgs@stu.edu.cn for the latter.
