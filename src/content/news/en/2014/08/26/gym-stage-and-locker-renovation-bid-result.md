Shantou University announced the evaluated result for construction of a stage in the gymnasium exercise room and double-sided lockers in the fitness room. Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd. was selected as the winning contractor, with a bid amount of 16,578.40 yuan.

The public notice ran from August 26 to 28, 2014. The Infrastructure Office was the project procuring office. Complaints could be submitted to the Audit and Supervision Office; the published contact numbers were 0754-86502490 for the Infrastructure Office and 0754-86502346 for complaints.
