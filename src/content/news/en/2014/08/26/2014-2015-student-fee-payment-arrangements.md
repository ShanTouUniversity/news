Shantou University’s Finance Office and Student Affairs Office released payment arrangements for enrolled undergraduates and graduate students for the 2014-2015 academic year. Students were asked to deposit tuition, accommodation fees and any previous outstanding charges into their registered Bank of China accounts by September 9 for collection through the university’s payment process. Students with changed or unregistered deduction accounts were asked to update their information with the Finance Office.

Students with approved loans could defer the portion covered by the loan but were required to pay any difference. Students applying for a home-region credit loan were asked to take the loan acceptance documents to the Student Financial Aid Center and complete the installment process in the student affairs system. The repayment date was September 9 for the difference and May 10, 2015, for the loan-covered amount. Undergraduates with financial difficulties could apply online for semester-based payment from September 9 to 25; college counselors were scheduled to review applications by September 30.

Detailed payment, registration and loan requirements were provided in the university attachments and student affairs system.
