Shantou University began replacing the gymnasium’s main lighting on July 20, 2014. During construction, the project required 26 additional 400-watt metal halide light sources to match the lighting system.

The Infrastructure Office estimated the additional cost at about 7,500 yuan, below the university’s 10 percent limit for project additions. The university invited written objections to be submitted to the Audit and Supervision Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 28.
