Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office and Research Office arranged the collection of laboratory information statistics for the 2013–2014 academic year on August 21, 2014. The survey covered teaching, research and other laboratories across the university and included five forms covering laboratory listings, experiment records, personnel, basic conditions and funding.

The reporting period covered the summer, fall and spring semesters of the 2013–2014 academic year. Units had to complete the forms by September 11, first submitting electronic files and then paper copies collected by laboratory directors. Teaching laboratories were to report to Academic Affairs, while research and other laboratories were to report to the Research Office.

The OA attachments contained the templates and instructions, which units were asked to review before checking and submitting their data.
