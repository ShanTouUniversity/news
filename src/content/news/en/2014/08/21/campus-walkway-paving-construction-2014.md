To advance the separation of pedestrian and vehicle routes, Shantou University planned to install paving bricks for a pedestrian walkway north of faculty housing blocks B1 to B11. The Infrastructure Office appointed Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. to carry out the work, which was scheduled to begin on August 21, 2014 and finish by September 19.

Construction could cause noise, vibration and dust. Pedestrians and vehicles passing the site were advised to take care and avoid lingering nearby. Official contact details for the project personnel are available in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office)
