Shantou University opened applications for the 2014 National Education Science Planning projects on August 21, 2014. Categories included key, general and youth projects under the National Social Science Fund education program, as well as key and youth special projects under the Ministry of Education. Approved projects were generally expected to be completed within one to three years and no later than five years.

Applicants generally needed an associate-senior or higher professional title; those without one could apply with recommendations from two peers holding senior professional titles. Youth-project applicants and team members had to be no older than 35. A project leader could submit only one application in the same year, and STU was limited to three submissions.

Applicants for key projects had to send electronic materials for formal review by August 28 and submit final paper materials by September 2. Other categories also required paper and electronic materials by September 2, while online submission was due by September 12. The application guides, forms and management rules were included in the OA attachments.
