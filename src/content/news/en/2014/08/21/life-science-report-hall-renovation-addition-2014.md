Shantou University began repairing the lecture hall at the College of Science Life Science and Advanced Materials Research Center on July 29, 2014. During the work, a damaged ceiling air outlet connected to a multi-split air-conditioning system required reconstruction and installation.

The Infrastructure Office preliminarily estimated the addition at about RMB 7,500, below the university limit of 10 percent. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 25.
