Shantou University began repairing the roof of the West Campus courtyard on July 1. During construction, the existing lightning protection strip was found to be corroded and in need of replacement; trees and a wasp nest also interfered with the installation of exterior scaffolding, resulting in additional work.

The Infrastructure Office preliminarily estimated the additional cost at about 12,500 yuan, below the 10 percent limit set by the university. The university published the proposed addition for public comment. Units or individuals with concerns could submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 11.
