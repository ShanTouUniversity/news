To help researchers understand the 2014 Guangdong Natural Science Foundation application policies, the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation Commission scheduled a Shantou session of the provincial Science Sunshine Renewal Initiative and 2014 Natural Science Foundation application policy tour at Shantou University on August 12.

The lecture was scheduled for 9 to 11 a.m. in STU's Science Lecture Hall and was intended for college leaders responsible for research, research secretaries and prospective applicants for provincial foundation projects. It would cover new application and management policies and provide training on the Guangdong Science and Technology Business Management Sunshine Government platform.

The program included policy and platform-operation lectures, question-and-answer sessions and a closing summary. Wang Yulong, deputy director of the Department of Basic Research and Research Conditions of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology, was scheduled to lead the policy lecture.
