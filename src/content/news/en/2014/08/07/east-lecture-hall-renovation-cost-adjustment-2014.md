Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office published a proposed cost addition for repairs to the East Auditorium on August 7, 2014. The project began on July 1. To meet fire-safety needs, the work added an outdoor fire-water pipe and replaced leaking indoor cast-iron pipes. The preliminary estimate was about RMB 32,000, keeping cumulative additions below the university’s 10% limit.

Written objections could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 11.
