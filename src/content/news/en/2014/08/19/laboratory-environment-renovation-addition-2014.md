Shantou University began renovating its laboratory environment on July 1, 2014. During construction, the Department of Electromechanical Engineering requested terrazzo floors for Laboratories 102 and 103, while the Department of Biology requested latex-paint work for laboratory and corridor walls.

The Infrastructure Office preliminarily estimated the proposed additions at about RMB 30,000, below the university limit of 10 percent for cumulative additions. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 21.
