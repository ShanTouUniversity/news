Shantou University began renovating the Student Affairs Office counseling room on July 30, 2014. During construction, the project added yellow and blue latex paint and rubber flooring in response to the users’ needs.

The Infrastructure Office preliminarily estimated the additions at about RMB 4,500, below the university limit of 10 percent. Written comments could be submitted to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by 5:30 p.m. on August 21.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office)
