To improve staff housing, Shantou University planned to renovate several aging Type A and Type B staff dormitories. The listed locations included A9-101, B5-302, B9-402, B9-501, B12-202 and B16-501. The project was assigned to Shantou Residential Construction Engineering Co., Ltd.

Construction was scheduled to start on August 11 and finish by September 29. Noise, vibration and dust could occur during the work. Pedestrians and vehicles should take care near the construction area and avoid lingering nearby.

Lu Chunxiong was the project manager and Chen Jinwu the site construction supervisor. Project-related matters could be handled through the university's relevant office.
