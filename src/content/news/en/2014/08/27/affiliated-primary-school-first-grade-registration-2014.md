Shantou University Affiliated Primary School announced that orientation and registration for 2014 autumn first-grade pupils would take place at 2:40 p.m. on August 29. Parents could collect admission notices from the principal’s office between 3 p.m. and 5 p.m. on August 28.

Families with special circumstances could contact the school at 86502262.
