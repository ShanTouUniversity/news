For the 2014 freshman education program, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office published auditorium seating arrangements for activities including university and English-enhancement introductions, credit-system briefings, library orientation, safety and confidentiality education, student conduct education, Party-knowledge lectures, guest lectures, career planning, arts-course introductions and integrative-thinking training.

The office asked colleges to organize freshmen to attend according to the published arrangements and noted that seating for the opening ceremony would be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
