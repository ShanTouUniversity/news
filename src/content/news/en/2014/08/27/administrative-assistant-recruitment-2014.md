Shantou University opened two administrative assistant positions for internal units: one supporting non-degree training at the School of Continuing Education and one supporting degree and graduate training administration at the Graduate School. Duties included training-project coordination and management for the first post, and degree-program administration, graduate records, supervisor management and graduation registration for the second.

Eligible applicants were administrative staff, including establishment-based and agency staff, from units including the Publicity Department, Student Affairs Office, School of Law, Business School, School of Arts and Design, School of Journalism and Communication, Institute of Higher Education, Department of Physical Education, Library and Network and Information Center. Applications were due September 2 at recruitment@stu.edu.cn; interview details were to follow.

The contact was Li Chang at 86502365. The office address was Personnel Office, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong.
