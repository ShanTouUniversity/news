Shantou University’s Finance Office reminded faculty and staff that the new finance query system had reset personal query passwords on August 25. Users were asked to sign in with the system-provided credential and change the password after their first login.

Questions about the upgraded system could be directed to the Finance Office at 86503358 or 86502446. To protect personal information, the initial credential and identity-number details are not reproduced here.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office)
> ⚠️ Detailed login and password-reset instructions are available on the OA system.
