Shantou University’s Network and Information Center and Resource Management Office asked university units to verify electronic bills for office telephones in June 2014. China Telecom and China Mobile landline bills had been imported into the telephone management system, where designated staff could review the two types of accounts.

Bills without discrepancies were to be confirmed in the system. Discrepancies could be reported by September 2 to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn for China Telecom lines or stunic@stu.edu.cn for China Mobile lines. Bills without an objection or confirmation by the deadline would be treated as confirmed, and the Finance Office planned to charge the relevant unit accounts on September 4.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center and Resource Management Office)
