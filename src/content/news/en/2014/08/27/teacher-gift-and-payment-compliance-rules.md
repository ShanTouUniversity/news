Shantou University’s Discipline Inspection Office forwarded Ministry of Education rules and related notices prohibiting teachers from improperly accepting gifts or payments from students and parents. University units were asked to publicize the rules and communicate them to faculty and staff, students and parents.

The notice called on all faculty members to follow professional ethics requirements, comply with the prohibitions and accept supervision to prevent violations involving gifts or payments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Discipline Inspection Office)
> ⚠️ The Ministry of Education rules and provincial forwarding documents are available on the OA system.
