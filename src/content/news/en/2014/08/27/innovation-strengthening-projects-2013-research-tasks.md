Shantou University’s Research Office launched research-platform and project tasks funded by the 2013 provincial Innovation Strengthening Project. The work covered the key-platform advancement plan, major research-project cultivation plan, international and Hong Kong-Macau-Taiwan cooperation platforms, and collaborative innovation-center cultivation.

Relevant units were asked to select the appropriate task document according to each project’s category and funding, submit an electronic version for formal review by September 1, and deliver two stamped paper copies to the Research Office by September 3. Huang Yumei and Zhu Yanchun were listed for humanities and social sciences projects, Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng for natural science projects, and Xie Yuanfang for collaborative innovation centers.
