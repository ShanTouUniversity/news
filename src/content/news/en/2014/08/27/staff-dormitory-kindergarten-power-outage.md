Cable-connection work in a staff-housing power distribution room was scheduled to cause a temporary outage in parts of the staff housing area and the kindergarten from 8:30 to 10:30 a.m. on August 29, 2014. The university asked users in the affected areas to plan ahead and apologized for the inconvenience.

To protect residents’ privacy, this report does not reproduce the individual building list; the OA notice remains the reference for the affected locations.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
> ⚠️ The detailed list of affected buildings is available on the OA system.
