The plan presentation for the third-round selection of Shantou University’s 2014 summer “Return to Shangri-La” intermediate outdoor program will take place from 2 to 4 p.m. on June 11 in Room 201 of the E Lecture Hall. The organizers plan to recruit 25 STU undergraduates to form a public review panel. The panel will assess the plans together with the review committee and account for 50 percent of the review score. Team members whose plans are being reviewed are not eligible; if applications exceed the quota, participants will be selected at random.

Registration closes at 10 p.m. on June 9. Applicants should complete the form in the OA attachment and email it to `snowleopardstu@163.com`. The form contains personal information and is not reproduced here. Further updates were available through the Shantou University Snow Leopard Outdoor Association’s social-media accounts.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
