As the end of the semester approached and student-club activity declined, the Shantou University Student Club Union office in Room 731 suspended duty service from June 9, 2014. Approvals for office use and materials loans were suspended at the same time.

Organizations with urgent requests during the suspension should contact Student Club Union staff through the channels listed in the original campus notice. This article does not reproduce students’ private telephone numbers.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University, Shantou University Student Club Union)
