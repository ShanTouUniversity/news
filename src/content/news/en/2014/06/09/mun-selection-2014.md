Shantou University Law School has opened a selection for students to attend the 11th China Model United Nations Conference, scheduled to take place in Nanjing in November 2014. Three students will be selected from the 2012 and 2013 cohorts of the Public Administration and Administration majors.

The selection interview will be held from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on June 11 in Room 213 of the Law School. Applicants will give a self-introduction in English and speak in English on the assigned topic “The United Nations and Human Rights of the Elderly”. English expression and communication account for 60 percent of the score, while attitude and willingness account for 40 percent. Relevant Model United Nations experience or knowledge is an advantage.

Applicants should email their basic information to `12mlshan@stu.edu.cn` by 11 p.m. on June 10. The requested information includes name, student number, cohort, major, phone numbers, email address and Model United Nations experience; this article does not reproduce students’ personal information.
