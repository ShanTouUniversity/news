Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has issued Academic Conduct Inspection Report No. 267. During the 15th week of the 2013–2014 spring semester, inspectors conducted two checks in the E Building teaching area. The 8:00 a.m. check on June 4 found two students more than five minutes late; the 8:00 a.m. check on June 6 found eight students late by up to five minutes.

The Student Affairs Office reported the cases to the relevant schools and colleges and asked them to strengthen academic conduct education. The student list is in the OA attachment and personal information is not reproduced here. Inquiries may be directed to the Student Education and Administration Section at 86504749 (short number 884749).
