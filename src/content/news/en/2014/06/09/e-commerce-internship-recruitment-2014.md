An e-commerce platform has released an internship recruitment notice through Shantou University Business School. Students from the 2011-2013 cohorts can apply for positions in product research and development, marketing, administration and human resources, and operations and finance. Openings include planning, development, marketing planning, business development, promotion, customer service, administration and human resources, and finance. Marketing or accounting majors are preferred for some positions; the business-development team plans to recruit more than six interns, while other roles offer one to four positions.

The internships will begin in July 2014 and last two months. Outstanding interns may later be considered for full-time employment. Interns will receive a monthly subsidy of RMB 500, shuttle transportation between STU and the company, and work meals. The workplace is in Waisha, Longhu District, Shantou.

Applicants should email a résumé identifying the position applied for to `wwcui@stu.edu.cn` by noon on June 12. Interviews are scheduled for 3 p.m. on June 13 in the Business School seminar room. Recruitment inquiries can be made at 86502726.
