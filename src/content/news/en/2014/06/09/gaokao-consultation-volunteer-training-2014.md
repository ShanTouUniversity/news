Shantou University’s Business School will hold training for volunteers registered to provide Gaokao admissions consultations in Jiangmen, Zhaoqing, Yunfu, Yangjiang and Heyuan. The session will begin at 2:30 p.m. on June 11 in the E Staircase Building’s Moot Court, Room 102.

The training will cover arrangements for admissions publicity and questions related to advising prospective students on university choices. Students registered to visit secondary schools in late June should attend. Those unable to attend for special reasons may contact the Business School Youth League Committee’s designated teacher.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School Youth League Committee)
