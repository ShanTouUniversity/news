The review committee for Shantou University Zhicheng College’s Hejun Commercial Internship Scholarship completed its assessment on June 5 and selected 10 candidates for the 2014 scholarship. The candidates come from communications, English, Chinese, computer science, journalism, chemistry, marketing and accounting, and include students from the 2010-2012 cohorts and graduate students.

Candidates are required to complete the internship and submit an internship report in mid-September. The committee will determine the final scholarship recipients based on the reports and a public presentation. To protect student privacy, this article does not reproduce candidates’ names; the detailed list can be consulted in the original OA notice.
