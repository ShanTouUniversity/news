Shantou University’s School of Law has completed interviews for the 2014 summer and autumn terms of its Blue Sky Creative Workshop service course. The interviews lasted nearly five hours, after which the course management team selected 75 students based on interview results and score statistics.

The course management team will enter the selected students into the credit system, so they do not need to register for the course themselves. Questions about the result should be sent to the course team by June 16. The selected-student list is in the OA attachment and is not reproduced here.
