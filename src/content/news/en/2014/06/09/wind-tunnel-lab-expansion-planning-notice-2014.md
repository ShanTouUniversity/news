It is reported that Shantou University has applied to expand a management building south of its wind tunnel laboratory on campus, and the review of the construction design plan has entered the pre-approval public notice period. The Resource Management Office issued a notice on June 9, 2014, forwarding the information published by the Direct Branch of the Shantou Municipal Bureau of Urban and Rural Planning.

## Project Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Notice number | 汕规直公示〔2014〕009号 |
| Applicant | Shantou University |
| Application | Review of the construction design plan |
| Project | Expansion of the management building south of the wind tunnel laboratory |
| Scale | One two-story management building, with a floor area of 404 square meters |
| Location | On the Shantou University campus |
| Public notice date | June 6, 2014 |

## Information and Statements

Detailed information about the project was available from the Direct Branch of the Shantou Municipal Bureau of Urban and Rural Planning at Xiehua Building, 11th Floor, No. 130 Zhongshan Road, Shantou.

Organizations or individuals with a significant interest in the application may submit a written statement to the relevant authority within ten days from the date of the public notice, in accordance with the *Guangdong Regulations on the Management of Detailed Control Plans for Urban and Rural Areas*. Individual statements must be personally signed and accompanied by a copy of the identity document. Statements from organizations must bear the signature or seal of the legal representative and the organization seal. Statements submitted after the deadline will be regarded as a waiver of the relevant rights.
