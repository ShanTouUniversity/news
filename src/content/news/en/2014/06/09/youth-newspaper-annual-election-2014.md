The 21st annual meeting of STU Youth Newspaper and election of its 22nd editorial board will take place at 2:30 p.m. on June 11 in Room 204 of Building E. The meeting will review the 21st editorial board’s annual report, recognize outstanding workers and departments from 2013, and elect the 22nd editorial board.

The 22nd editorial board is planned to have 10 members. The Youth League committee previously identified 14 candidates through college recommendations and student applications. Candidates will present their plans at the annual meeting, and all members of the 21st editorial board will vote. Ten members will be selected based on the vote count and interview results. After the meeting, the new editorial board will hold its first meeting to elect an editor-in-chief and a deputy editor-in-chief.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
