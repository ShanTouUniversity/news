It is reported that final exam arrangements for the 2014 spring semester courses at Shantou University have been finalized, with all exam information published on the credit system website. The Academic Affairs Office reminds teachers and students to log into the system themselves to check their invigilation and examination schedules.

## Examination Room Arrangements

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival time | Both invigilators and students must arrive 10 minutes early |
| Seating | Invigilators seat students according to seat numbers printed from the credit system's candidate list |
| Seat inquiry | Students can look up their own exam seat numbers via the credit system |

According to the office, in order not to disturb the order of the examination rooms, both invigilators and students should follow the university's invigilation rules and examination room discipline.

## Exam Paper Management

For question setting, each course assessment must have two parallel papers (A and B) of roughly equal length and difficulty. Course instructors must complete grading and record grades within three days after the assessment (or within one week for courses with large enrollments).

For printing, instructors submit a paper-printing application form along with the test questions and candidate list to the office's printing center (Wendong 404) one week before the exam. On confidentiality, under the university's rules on test paper security, those who set the questions must deliver them to the printing center in person; chief invigilators collect papers on the day of the exam — or the afternoon before for exams starting at 8:00 am — and anyone collecting papers on an instructor's behalf must carry a written authorization or proof from the instructor's unit.

After the exams, instructors self-check materials against the exam paper inspection form and hand the exam materials together with the completed form to the academic secretary of the offering unit for archiving.
