Shantou University Business School will hold an internship and employment briefing for all undergraduate students in its 2011 cohort at 9 a.m. on June 10 in Room 101 of the E Lecture Hall.

The meeting is intended to help students understand internship and employment arrangements, strengthen career-planning awareness and prepare for upcoming internships and job searches. The original notice did not include a detailed agenda; further arrangements were to be announced at the meeting.
