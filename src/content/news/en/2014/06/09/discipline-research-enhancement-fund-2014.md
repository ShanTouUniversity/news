Shantou University’s Office of Scientific Research has opened applications for the 2014 Discipline Construction and Research Enhancement Special Fund. The fund supports academic exchanges, applications for key disciplines, research platforms and degree programs at or above the provincial level, operation of existing research platforms, and preliminary work for talent recruitment. Proposed support is capped at RMB 300,000 per project.

Eligible units on the main campus should apply by first-level discipline. Each project should include three to five research directions, one to three core members for each direction, a three-year development objective and a 2014 work plan. Applicants must complete the special-fund application form, submit one stamped paper copy through their school to the Office of Scientific Research, and email an electronic copy to `o_kyc@stu.edu.cn`.

Applications are due June 20; late submissions will not be accepted. The OA attachment contains the detailed format and requirements.
