Shantou University Student Affairs Office has announced the reservation results for the ninth leaders-student dinner. Thirty-two students were confirmed to attend. The dinner will take place at 6 p.m. on June 11, 2014, in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. Confirmed students should bring their campus cards and arrive at 5:45 p.m.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 6 p.m., June 11, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Confirmed students | 32 |
| Arrival time | 5:45 p.m. |

The confirmed-student list is in the OA attachment and student names are not reproduced here.
