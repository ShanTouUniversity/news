Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will hold its 22nd Student Congress at 2:30 p.m. on June 11, 2014, in Lecture Room 204 of the E Building. Delegates should arrive and check in at 2:15 p.m.

The congress will review the work of the school’s 21st student union and elect the executive committee of the 22nd student union. About 80 delegates will be selected, representing roughly 15 percent of the school’s undergraduate student population. Classes will elect delegates through class student meetings.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organizing Committee of the 22nd Student Congress, School of Liberal Arts)
