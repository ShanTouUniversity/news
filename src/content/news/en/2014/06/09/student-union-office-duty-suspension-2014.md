As the end of the semester approaches and student-organization activity declines, the Shantou University Student Union office will suspend office duty service from June 11, 2014. Approval of applications for audio equipment, other materials and poster boards will also be suspended.

Student organizations with urgent poster-board or materials requests after the suspension should contact the relevant student staff through the channels listed in the original campus notice. This article does not reproduce students’ personal contact information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University, Shantou University Student Union)
