The School of Science at Shantou University has completed interviews for the 2014-2015 summer and fall “Toward the Ocean” public courses. The interviews were held from 1 to 4 p.m. on June 8 in Room D301 and included self-introductions, a group discussion without a designated leader, and questions and answers. Three course instructors and two course assistants jointly scored the applicants.

The results selected 33 students for the summer course and 20 for the fall course. Six volunteers were also recruited for the two courses. The detailed lists are in the OA attachment and student names are not reproduced here.
