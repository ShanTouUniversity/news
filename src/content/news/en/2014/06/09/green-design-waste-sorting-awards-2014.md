Shantou University’s Green Design waste-sorting competition has completed its judging, with 19 teams participating. The organizers announced one first prize, three second prizes, six third prizes and two popularity awards. The winning entries included computer and mobile games, environmentally friendly waste-bin designs and an environmental comic.

The first-prize entry was the computer game “Earth Defense”. Second-prize entries included the mobile games “Recycle” and “Super Garbage”, as well as an environmentally friendly waste-bin design. Third-prize entries included an environmental comic, the mobile game “Defend the Earth”, waste-bin designs and a comic. The popularity awards went to “Super Garbage” and an environmentally friendly waste-bin design. To protect student privacy, student names are not reproduced; the detailed results are available in the OA attachment.
