The “Shantou Old Images: Wartime Photographs” exhibition opened at the Shantou University Library on June 10, 2014. It presents images of Shantou’s port, streets and landmarks during the War of Resistance against Japanese Aggression, along with documentary photographs of the Japanese invasion of Shantou.

The photographs record changes in the city’s wartime landscape and have historical documentary value. The exhibition is open to the STU community.
