Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office circulated registration information for the third China Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition. Students may enter either the Guangdong division or the Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan division, which also serves as the first cross-strait, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan university student innovation and entrepreneurship competition. The events are jointly organized by the Torch High Technology Industry Development Center of the Ministry of Science and Technology and the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology.

Interested students should email their name, contact details and chosen competition division to `luolie@stu.edu.cn` by June 17. For inquiries, contact Luo Lie at the Student Entrepreneurship Center, Student Affairs Office, on 86502255. Competition rules and details are in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The competition notice and registration attachments are available on the OA system.
