Shantou University Graduate School opened its 2014 graduate extracurricular research project call on April 3 and received 45 applications. Following expert review, 38 projects, including “Innovative Recycling of Discarded Bicycles for Public Benefit”, were approved for funding. The detailed project list is in the OA attachment.

Start-up funding is RMB 400 per project for humanities and social-science papers and RMB 500 per project for invention and production projects. The university plans to organize a midterm review in mid-to-late September and select some projects for additional Graduate Innovation and Creative Practice Fund support. After completion, project leaders may seek reimbursement under the applicable rules using invoices for experimental materials or publication fees.
