Shantou University’s Student Extracurricular Academic and Science-Technology Activities Leading Group has opened applications to enhance outstanding student academic and science-technology works. The works will support preparations for the 14th National Challenge Cup Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic and Science-Technology Works Competition in 2015, and selected works will be recommended for Guangdong’s key student innovation-project evaluation.

Eligible works must have entered STU’s 10th Challenge Cup campus selection. Categories include natural-science academic papers, humanities and social-science survey reports and academic papers, and inventions and productions. Works judged by experts to have enhancement potential may receive RMB 500, 1,000 or 1,500 in enhancement funding. Projects must be completed by the end of February 2015 and take part in a defense; works that pass review will represent STU in the 13th Guangdong Challenge Cup competition in 2015.

Applicants should complete the application form in the OA attachment and email it to `12kzhang1@stu.edu.cn` by noon on June 12. A paper copy should also be submitted to the University Science and Technology Association office, Room 732, Building 789, from 5 to 6 p.m. The university will recommend three works for the Guangdong evaluation; the Medical School may recommend no more than three works for campus review.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University, Student Extracurricular Academic and Science-Technology Activities Leading Group)
> ⚠️ The application form and related activity attachments are available on the OA system.
