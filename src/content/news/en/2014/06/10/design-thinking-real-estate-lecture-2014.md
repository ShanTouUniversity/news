It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, the Cheung Kong Industrial Design Center, the Business School and the MBA Center of Shantou University will jointly hold a lecture on *Design Thinking and Real Estate Planning and Design* on June 13, 2014, to celebrate the 2014 Commencement.

In the era of rapid scientific and technological development and widespread information networks, traditional thinking is insufficient to address complex social issues. Design thinking offers a new perspective and methodology, moving beyond the studio into broad social fields and serving as an engine for organizational innovation, and has become a tool for entrepreneurs to develop new products and reshape business models. Architecture, often called frozen art, reflects designers’ aesthetics and cultural connotations and embodies cultural thinking of a historical period; applying design thinking to real estate planning and design can enhance aesthetic and commercial value.
