It is reported that the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will hold its 2014 Student Works Exhibition on Sunday, June 15, from 10:00 to 16:00 in Room E-207, Building E.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Date | June 15, 2014 (Sunday), 10:00–16:00 |
| Venue | Room E-207, Building E |
| Organizer | Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication |

## Program

The exhibition will feature:

- Opening remarks by Vice Dean Ni Qingqing of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication;
- Showcase of winning works from the inaugural Cheung Kong Media Awards with commentary by awardees;
- Outstanding works by the Class of 2010 graduates and sharing by their creators;
- Sharing session on the school's distinctive overseas internship program;
- Student talent performances and interactive prize activities;
- Outdoor displays and video screenings open for free viewing.

A detailed program is available in the attachment. All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
