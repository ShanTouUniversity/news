The eighth Guqin Culture Camp of Shantou University Zhicheng College received 38 applications. The college selected 15 regular participants and five alternates.

Regular and alternate participants should collect camp materials and pay a RMB 100 deposit at the Zhicheng College administration office, Room J-338, during working hours from June 11 to 13. Those who fail to complete the process by the deadline will be considered to have withdrawn. The camp will run from June 30 to July 5, beginning with an opening ceremony and classes at 2 p.m. on June 30 in Room J-205A. To protect student privacy, participant names are not reproduced; the detailed list can be consulted in the original OA notice.
