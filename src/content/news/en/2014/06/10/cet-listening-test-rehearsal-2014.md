To help candidates check their listening equipment in advance, Shantou University will run a signal test for the 2014 first-half national College English Test from 3 to 5 p.m. on Wednesday, June 11, in Buildings E, G and D.

Candidates should bring their own headphones and test them at their assigned examination location. The listening signal will use FM 73.2.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
