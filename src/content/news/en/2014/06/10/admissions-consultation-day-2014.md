Shantou University will hold its 2014 Admissions Consultation Day on campus from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on June 15. Campus working hours for the event will be 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The event will present the university’s development, educational features, faculty and student life, and campus environment to visitors.

The program will include lectures by Gaokao application consultant Qiu Jianshi and CCDM career planner Wang Tao for high school students. On-site consultations will cover admissions policies, application choices, major changes and dual-degree study, overseas exchange, scholarships and financial aid, employment and entrepreneurship, career planning, and mental-health education. Visitors can also tour the library, auditorium, language self-study center, Arts Education Center, Student Learning Center, Zhicheng College and college laboratories, and join workshops, technology displays and interactive games.

The detailed schedule is in the OA attachment. The university also called on students on campus to welcome visitors and present student life at STU.
