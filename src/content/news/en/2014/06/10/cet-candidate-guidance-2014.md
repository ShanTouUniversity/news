Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has issued guidance for candidates taking the 2014 first-half national College English Test (CET-4 and CET-6) on June 14. CET-4 will run from 9:00 to 11:25 a.m. and CET-6 from 3:00 to 5:25 p.m. Candidates should enter their rooms by 8:45 a.m. and 2:45 p.m., respectively, and check their headphones in advance; the listening frequency is FM 73.2.

Candidates must bring their national ID card, student ID and admission ticket; a campus card is not an acceptable substitute for the student ID. Anyone who has lost an ID must present a statement signed by the department academic administrator and stamped by the school. Permitted items include headphones, batteries, a 2B pencil, a black pen and an eraser. Books, paper, bags, recording devices and communications equipment such as mobile phones are prohibited. Late candidates may not enter after the test starts, and candidates may not leave early or exit during the examination. The examination office is in Room 101 of the E Lecture Hall in Building E and can be reached at 2402 or 2623.
