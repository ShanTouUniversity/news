Shantou University published the implementation status of public-affairs disclosures by its functional departments for the 2013-2014 academic year. The disclosure is intended to protect faculty, staff and students’ rights to know, participate and supervise, and to advance democratic administration and oversight at the university.

Members of the university community with questions may contact the Public-Affairs Disclosure Oversight Group. The supervision telephone number is 86502346 and the email address is `o_jj@stu.edu.cn`. Detailed implementation items are provided in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Supervision and Audit Office)
> ⚠️ The detailed public-affairs disclosure items and attachment are available on the OA system.
