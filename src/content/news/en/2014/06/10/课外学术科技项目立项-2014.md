Shantou University has launched its 2014 program for student extracurricular academic and science projects. The program follows the university’s project application and management rules, with the project cycle running from launch through March 1, 2015.

Schools and colleges will organize applications and expert reviews. Within one week after completing a review, each unit must submit a proposed-project list, applications, an analysis of expected outcomes and benefits, and a work schedule to the University Youth League Committee office. The university’s student extracurricular academic and science activities leading group will review the proposals and allocate corresponding project funds. After projects conclude, units must arrange final reviews and submit completion reports, funding summaries and performance evaluations.

Detailed application materials and management requirements are in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee)
