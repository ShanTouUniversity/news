The Center for Global Studies in Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts will offer CIS6900A Independent Study Projects to all undergraduates in the fall semester of 2014-2015. Students should contact a supervisor in their field and develop a project proposal by June 20, the Friday of Week 17. Supervisors will confirm students through an interview or meeting and complete the registration confirmation through the credit system.

The course covers history, philosophy, political science, anthropology, film studies, American studies, cultural studies and psychology. Projects generally last 10-12 weeks, or 40-50 hours. Students work under supervision and submit a report or paper for comprehensive assessment; successful completion earns one credit. The working language may be Chinese or English depending on the supervisor and project.
