The 2014 first-half national College English Test (CET-4 and CET-6) will be held on June 14. Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office asked schools on the main campus to ensure invigilators review examination rules, operating procedures, room records and incident-response requirements in advance.

CET-4 invigilators should report to Room 101 of the E Lecture Hall at 7:45 a.m. for training and paper collection. CET-6 invigilators should report to the same room at 1:45 p.m. After each exam, papers must be returned there for counting. Invigilator assignments and room locations will be announced on exam day. Invigilators will undergo security screening and may not improperly bring or use communications or recording devices. The examination office is in Room 101 of the E Lecture Hall; its telephone numbers are 2402 and 2623.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
> ⚠️ The invigilator roster and examination attachments are available on the OA system.
