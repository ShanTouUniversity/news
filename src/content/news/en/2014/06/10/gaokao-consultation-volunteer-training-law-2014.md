Shantou University Law School will train volunteers who registered to support Gaokao admissions consultations in Chaozhou, Meizhou and Foshan. The sessions will introduce STU, explain parallel-choice admissions and answer questions about applications, while also outlining arrangements for the consultation events.

| Session | Time | Venue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2:30-4:30 p.m., June 11 | E306 |
| 2 | 7-8:30 p.m., June 13 | Law School Room 213 |
| 3 | 10:30 a.m.-noon, June 14 | Law School Room 213 |

Registered volunteers should attend according to the schedule. Those unable to attend for special reasons could contact the responsible teacher through the Law School Youth League office. The original notice included campus office contacts; personal contact details are not reproduced here.
