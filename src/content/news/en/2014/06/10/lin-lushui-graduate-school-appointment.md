It is reported that, following deliberation at the 7th meeting of the Standing Committee of the CPC Shantou University Committee in 2014 and the 9th university leadership meeting of 2014, the university proposes to appoint Lin Lushui as Deputy Secretary of the Party General Branch of the Graduate School of Shantou University (presiding over the branch's work) and Director of Student Affairs. The Organization and United Front Work Department has issued a pre-appointment public notice in accordance with the STU Trial Measures for Pre-Appointment Public Notice of Cadres.

## Channels for Feedback

Under the public notice system, any unit or individual with differing opinions on the proposed appointee may report in writing, in person or by phone to the Organization and United Front Work Department **before June 16, 2014**. Reports from units must bear the official seal, while individuals must sign their real names to take responsibility for their submissions.

## Background of the Proposed Appointee

Public records show that Lin Lushui was born in April 1975 in Chaoyang, Guangdong, is of Han ethnicity, holds a self-taught bachelor's degree, and is an economist. He began working in July 1997 and joined the Communist Party of China in March 1997. His career history includes:

| Period | Position |
| --- | --- |
| 1994.09–1997.06 | Studied mathematics at the Science School of STU |
| 1997.07–2001.12 | Staff member, Dormitory Management Section, Student Affairs Office, STU |
| 2001.12–2007.07 | Political counselor, Science School, STU (deputy section level from March 2003; section level from October 2006) |
| 2007.07–2013.10 | Head of Student Education and Management Section, Student Affairs Office (appointed to intermediate management post in July 2010) |
| 2013.10–present | Interim head of the Party General Branch of the Graduate School (presiding over its work) |

He also studied business secretarial studies at Sun Yat-sen University from September 1998 to February 2002.
