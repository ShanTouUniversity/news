The Shantou University Graduate Volunteer Team will host a campus exchange for middle school students from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. on June 15. Participants will include about 30 students from Fenghua School and about 20 students from another school in the Beautiful China program.

The program will include a campus tour, interactive games and conversations with volunteers about university study and life. It is intended to help the visiting students learn more about university life and encourage their interest in learning.

STU students who wish to volunteer should email their name, school, major and contact information to `13lxzhang@stu.edu.cn` by June 12. Student registration details are not reproduced here.
