Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced the renewal of accident insurance for full-time students at the main campus, including undergraduates and graduate students. The policy runs from May 1, 2014, to April 30, 2015. Its maximum payout is RMB 50,000; together with RMB 10,000 under the existing policy, the total maximum coverage is RMB 60,000.

Coverage applies within mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. Students traveling to other countries for study must notify the insurer in advance and provide itinerary and study details; the Resource Management Office’s Logistics Office can assist with the filing. Any accident must be reported to the insurer within 24 hours. Official contact channels are listed in the original notice.
