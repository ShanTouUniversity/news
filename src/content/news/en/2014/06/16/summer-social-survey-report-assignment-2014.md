Shantou University’s Social Sciences Department required undergraduates in the 2011 and 2012 cohorts enrolled in its fall 2014 course *Introduction to Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics* (SOC6150A) to conduct a summer social survey and submit a report to their instructor by the end of October.

The theme is “My Hometown, My Home.” The report should be about 3,000 words and relate to the decisions of the Third Plenum of the 18th CPC Central Committee and the “Chinese Dream.” The survey is a practical component of the course, and the report will count toward the overall course grade. Students should follow the department’s template in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Social Sciences Department)
> ⚠️ The social survey report template is available on the OA system.
