Shantou Qiyu Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. and Shantou University’s School of Engineering are recruiting summer interns from across the university’s 2011 and 2012 cohorts. The internship will be based at STU’s 789 Student Entrepreneurship Park, and applications are due by June 29, 2014.

Openings include microcontroller development, hardware design, embedded development, mobile application development, desktop software development, and marketing and sales. Applicants with relevant project experience or entrepreneurial interests may receive priority. Applicants must complete a form and email it to the company’s recruitment address; see the OA attachments for role requirements and application materials.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Engineering)
> ⚠️ The internship application form and attachments are available on the OA system.
