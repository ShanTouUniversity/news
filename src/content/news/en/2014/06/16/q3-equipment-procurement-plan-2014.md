Shantou University’s Equipment and Materials Management Office opened applications for third-quarter 2014 equipment procurement plans. University units must submit both written and electronic materials by June 30; late submissions will be deferred to the fourth quarter.

Purchases budgeted at RMB 20,000 or less may be submitted through the university’s equipment bidding website. Lenovo, HP, Dell and Apple computers remain subject to the university’s agreement-supply arrangements. Purchases above RMB 20,000 require a plan form and proposal, followed by approval under the university’s spending authority rules. Equipment priced above RMB 100,000 also requires a feasibility report. Computer purchases require a registration form, while air-conditioner requests must specify the installation location and the condition of existing units.

Forms and the government procurement catalog are included in the OA attachments. All plans must be completed and submitted to the Equipment and Materials Management Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
> ⚠️ Procurement forms, feasibility report and related attachments are available on the OA system.
