It is reported that the sixth lecture of Shantou University’s Aizhi Workshop will be held at 3 p.m. on Wednesday, June 4, 2014, in the Social Sciences Department meeting room, Room G209 of the teaching building. The lecture will address how academic journal editors select and publish manuscripts.

The speaker will be Chen Jinqing, a research fellow, editor-in-chief of Jianghan Tribune, and council member of the Hubei Philosophical Society. Faculty members and students are welcome to attend.
