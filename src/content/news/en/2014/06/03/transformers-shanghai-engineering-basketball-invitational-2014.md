It is reported that the Shantou University Transformers will face the men’s basketball team of Shanghai University of Engineering Science at 7:30 p.m. on Wednesday, June 4, 2014, in the university gymnasium. Performances and interactive games will be held during breaks, with Now TV sports host Emil Cheung serving as the event DJ.

Faculty members and students can obtain tickets through prize-based activities at the Golden Triangle from the afternoon of June 3. Spectators should enter in an orderly manner before 7 p.m. A temporary cheer section for up to 40 people will be set up beside the court; interested spectators may arrive at 6:45 p.m. for a brief orientation. Organizers encouraged spectators to wear orange clothing and follow the STU Transformers’ Weibo account for a prize draw.
