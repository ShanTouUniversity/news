It is reported that the theory examination for Shantou University Party School’s 75th Basic Party Knowledge Class will be held from 7:30 to 9 p.m. on Sunday, June 8, 2014. Class 75 participants and Class 74 participants approved for deferred or make-up examinations must attend the designated hall assigned to their school or college.

Participants from the Schools of Liberal Arts, Science, Engineering, and Law will take the examination in Lecture Hall 1. Participants from the Business School, School of Journalism and Communication, School of Fine Arts, Zhicheng College, and Graduate School will take it in Lecture Hall 3. Those unable to attend for special reasons must obtain approval from their Party general branch or directly affiliated Party branch and complete the deferral procedure with the Party School office by June 6. Unexcused absence will result in cancellation of class membership.

| Participant unit | Examination hall |
| --- | --- |
| Schools of Liberal Arts, Science, Engineering, and Law | Lecture Hall 1 |
| Business School, School of Journalism and Communication, School of Fine Arts, Zhicheng College, and Graduate School | Lecture Hall 3 |
