It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Publicity Department and the Guangdong Federation of Social Sciences continued their 2014 funding program for the publication of outstanding social science books by retired experts. The program planned to support the publication of 10 books. STU’s Research Affairs Office forwarded the call on June 3, inviting eligible retired social science researchers at the university to apply.

Applicants must be retired experts in social science research in Guangdong who hold a senior professional title and have completed but not yet published a scholarly manuscript. Experts who received funding in 2013 are not eligible for another award. Submissions should demonstrate academic value and originality, follow academic standards, and have no copyright disputes.

Applicants must email the application form to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` for a formal review by June 20. By June 23, they must submit printed copies of the application form, book contents, a Chinese abstract of no more than 1,000 Chinese characters, and book samples to the Research Affairs Office, while also sending electronic copies. The office lists Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun as contacts at 86502563.
