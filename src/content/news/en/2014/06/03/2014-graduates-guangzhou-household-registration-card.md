It is reported that STU’s Student Affairs Office forwarded a Guangdong Education Department notice on June 3, 2014, concerning Guangzhou urban household registration cards for graduates of the 2014 cohort. Relevant graduates were reminded to follow the notice and complete the application procedures with the designated authorities.

The notice applies to 2014 graduates of regular higher-education institutions. Eligibility, required materials, and detailed procedures are set out in the original Guangdong Education Department notice, which the Student Affairs Office provided as an OA attachment.

> ⚠️ Please consult the original notice and attachment on the OA system for eligibility, required materials, and procedures.
>
> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
