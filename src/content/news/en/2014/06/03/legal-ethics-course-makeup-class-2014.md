It is reported that STU’s Academic Affairs Office announced makeup arrangements on June 3, 2014, for sections 67001 and 67002 of the Social Sciences Department course Ideological, Moral and Legal Education (Law). Both classes will meet in Room E308 on Sunday, June 8, during the 16th teaching week.

Section 67001 will meet during periods 1–2, while section 67002 will meet during periods 3–4. Students should attend according to their section and scheduled period.

## Makeup Class Details

| Section | Time | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 67001 | Periods 1–2, June 8 | E308 |
| 67002 | Periods 3–4, June 8 | E308 |
