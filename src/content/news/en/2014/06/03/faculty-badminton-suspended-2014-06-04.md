It is reported that Shantou University’s faculty badminton session scheduled for June 4, 2014, will be suspended because the university gymnasium is hosting a high-level men’s basketball game and related preparations that day.

The Labor Union asked participating faculty members to pass the information along. Future session arrangements will follow subsequent union notices.
