It is reported that Shantou University alumna Chen Yanhong will hold a sharing session titled “An Alumna’s Dreams and Practice in Tibet” from 4 to 5:30 p.m. on June 4, 2014, in Room 201 of the Administrative Center. The session, jointly announced by the Alumni Affairs Office and the School of Liberal Arts, is open to the university community.

Chen graduated from STU’s Business English program in the School of Liberal Arts in 1997. She went to Tibet to work in 2001 and currently teaches in the Foreign Languages Department of Tibet University. She has participated in the “Morning Star” one-to-one education support initiative for children around Lhasa, assisted local welfare institutions, and helped organize winter-clothing donations for remote areas of Tibet.
