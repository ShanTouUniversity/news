It is reported that the Shantou University Engineering School “To the Class of 2014” video showcase was held on May 30, 2014. The event received videos from 14 classes and formed part of the school’s themed Youth League Day activities, using student-made videos to present campus life.

After an open judging process, the 13 Electronics, 12 Civil Engineering, and 11 Computer Science classes received the grade-level excellence award. The 13 Communications, 12 Communications, and 11 Mechanical and Electrical Engineering classes received the grade-level merit award. The Engineering School Youth League Committee announced the results on June 3.
