Shantou University's School of Science is scheduled to host a talk at 2:30 p.m. on June 5, 2014. Associate Professor Xu Liang of the School of Pharmacy at Tianjin Medical University will speak on “Interface Reactions and Analytical Chemistry.” The talk will take place in ACC Meeting Room 5 and is open to the university community.

The presentation will cover surface modification of silica and its applications in protein capillary electrophoresis and electrochromatography, as well as nanoparticle modification, antibody immobilization and related immunoassay methods.
