Shantou University's School of Science is scheduled to hold a science salon at 10:15 a.m. on June 4, 2014. Professor Cui Zhifeng of Anhui Normal University will discuss laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy of multiple metal elements in mixed solutions. The talk will be held in the School of Science lecture hall, Room 352 of the Science Building, and is open to the university community.

Cui's research focuses on atomic and molecular spectroscopy dynamics and laser-induced plasma dynamics.
