The “Blossom” exhibition of paintings by Li Kexian is scheduled to run from June 6 to 12, 2014, at the gallery of Shantou University's School of Cheung Kong Arts and Design. The exhibition is co-hosted by the school and the Shantou Artists Association. Its opening ceremony is scheduled for 10 a.m. on June 6.

The exhibition is open to the university community.
