It is reported that the STU School of Science’s Modern Mathematics lecture series will host a lecture at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, June 4, 2014, in the Mathematics Laboratory, Room G412. Feng Guocan, a professor and doctoral supervisor at Sun Yat-sen University, will present “Applications of Sparse Coding and Dictionary Learning in Face Recognition.”

The lecture will address sparse regularization and dictionary selection in face recognition, introducing grouped SRC algorithms, low-rank supervised dictionaries, sparse within-class variation dictionaries, and supervised structured dictionary-learning algorithms incorporating classifier design. The lecture is open to the university community.
