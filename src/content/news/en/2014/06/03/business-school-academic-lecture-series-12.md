It is reported that the STU Business School’s 12th academic lecture of 2014 will be held from 8:30 a.m. to noon on Wednesday, June 4, 2014, in the Business School’s second-floor Academic Seminar Room. The program includes four presentations on venture-capital value-added services, services outsourcing, IPO underpricing, and the development of Taiwan-based HTC.

Liang Qiang will discuss value-added services in venture capital and the growth of new ventures. Zeng Qiang will compare Chinese and Western models of services outsourcing. Li Bo will analyze IPO underpricing from the perspective of pricing deviations. Chen Ge will examine the development of Taiwan-based HTC through the dynamic-capabilities framework. The event is open to the university community.

## Lecture Program

| Speaker | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| Liang Qiang | Value-added services in venture capital and new-venture growth |
| Zeng Qiang | Services outsourcing: a comparison of Chinese and Western models |
| Li Bo | IPO underpricing from the perspective of pricing deviations |
| Chen Ge | The development of Taiwan-based HTC through the dynamic-capabilities framework |

Time: 8:30 a.m.–noon, Wednesday, June 4, 2014. Location: Business School Academic Seminar Room, second floor.
