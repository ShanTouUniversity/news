It is reported that the School of Science of Shantou University will hold a lecture in its “Toward Modern Mathematics” series at 10:00 a.m. on June 4, 2014. Shi Yongjie of the Department of Mathematics will present a lecture titled “Fast Solutions for a Class of Convolution-Type Integral Equations.”
