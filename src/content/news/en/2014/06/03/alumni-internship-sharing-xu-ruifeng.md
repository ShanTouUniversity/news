It is reported that STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication held the “Learning Through Internships” alumni session from 4:10 to 5:30 p.m. on June 3, 2014, in Room E208 of the teaching building. Xu Ruifeng, a 2007 alumnus of the university’s Radio and Television Journalism program, served as the speaker and discussed media internships, job applications, and résumé preparation.

Xu previously worked as an editor at 21CN and a reporter at Southern Metropolis Daily, and is now a public relations manager at Qihoo 360. Drawing on his experience recommending more than 100 students for media internships, he shared advice on choosing internships and preparing résumés for graduation and employment.
