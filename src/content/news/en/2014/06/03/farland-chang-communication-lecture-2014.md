It is reported that STU’s Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication will host a public lecture from 7 to 9 p.m. on Wednesday, June 4, 2014, in the Library Lecture Hall. The lecture is titled “Great Communicators: The Art of What to Say and How to Say It,” with the subtitle “Tips on Being the Best Ambassador for Your Cause.”

The speaker, Farland Chang, previously served as a national correspondent for NBC Network News and an economic news anchor for CNN International. He is an Asian American broadcaster who has worked in U.S. television news and has won an Emmy Award. The event is open to the university community.
