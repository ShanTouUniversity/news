Shantou University's Academic Affairs Office scheduled classroom teaching evaluations for the spring semester from June 3 to 17, 2014. Students enrolled in courses could submit evaluations through the University's credit system. Evaluations for public-service courses were to be announced separately.

The University said the evaluations support teaching improvement. Students who did not complete the evaluation by the deadline were required to submit a written explanation; otherwise, access to their semester grades would be restricted.

## Evaluation Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Period | June 3–17, 2014 |
| How to participate | Sign in to the credit system at http://credit.stu.edu.cn and select “Course Evaluation” |
| Scope | Courses taken that semester; public-service courses to be announced separately |
