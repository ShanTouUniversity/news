It is reported that the Shantou University Student Community X-Project Voices of Associations showcase was held in classroom E303 on May 28, 2014. Nine student groups participated in the defense, including the Handicraft Club, Cantonese Culture Association, Community Mentors Association, Food and Beverage Entrepreneurship Practice Association, Tea Ceremony Association, Wanghaichao Poetry Society, Beauty Association, Chess Association, and Xingchao Association.

Following judging and review by the STU Youth League Committee, the Handicraft Club, Cantonese Culture Association, and Community Mentors Association placed first, second, and third, receiving awards of RMB 1,000, RMB 800, and RMB 700, respectively. The Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office announced the results on June 3.
