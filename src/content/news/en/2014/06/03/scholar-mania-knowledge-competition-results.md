It is reported that Shantou University’s “Top Student, Crazy Knowledge” competition concluded in E203 on May 25, 2014. A total of 41 teams took part in the preliminary and final rounds. The STU Youth League Committee and the STU Youth newspaper editorial office announced the results on June 3.

The Flander Team won first place, the Radish Team took second, and the “Students Crushing Top Students” Team placed third. The participating students were Wu JP, Wang Y, and Lin RJ; Li Y, Hong Y, and Li XM; and Zheng Y, Xiao JJ, and Wu WT, respectively. Student names are abbreviated here to protect privacy.
