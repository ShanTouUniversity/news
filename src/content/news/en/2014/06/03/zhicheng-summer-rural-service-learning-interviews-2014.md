It is reported that Zhicheng College at Shantou University received 78 applications for its summer rural service-learning public-welfare course. After an initial review of application materials, the project team selected 65 students for interviews, which will take place on the afternoon of Wednesday, June 4, 2014, in Room J-305A at Zhicheng College.

Selected applicants must attend according to the schedule in the OA attachment. Student names are omitted here for privacy; the attachment contains the detailed interview times.

> ⚠️ Please consult the original OA attachment on the OA system for the interview list and detailed schedule.
