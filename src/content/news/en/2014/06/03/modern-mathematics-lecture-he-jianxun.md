It is reported that the STU School of Science’s Modern Mathematics lecture series will host a lecture at 11 a.m. on Wednesday, June 4, 2014, in the Mathematics Laboratory, Room G412. He Jianxun, a professor and doctoral supervisor at Guangzhou University, will present “Wavelet Transform and Radon Transform on the Quaternion Heisenberg Group.”

The lecture will introduce continuous wavelet transform theory on the quaternion Heisenberg group, discuss the construction of radial wavelets and their use in inverse wavelet transforms, and address an inversion formula for the Radon transform associated with the sub-Laplacian. The lecture is open to the university community.
