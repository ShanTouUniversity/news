Shantou University's School of Science is scheduled to hold a science salon from 8 to 9:30 p.m. on June 3, 2014. Professor Zhou Qicun of Ningbo University will present on advances in cobia nutrition research. The talk will take place in Room 504 of the Academy Building and is open to the university community.

Zhou's research background includes aquatic animal nutrition and feed science, as well as marine biology.
