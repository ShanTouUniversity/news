Shantou University's People's Armed Forces Department announced its 2014 summer-autumn enlistment schedule. Online registration for men runs from April 10 to August 5, while registration for women runs from June 20 to August 5. Applicants register through the national conscription website. University students who complete online registration are required to download the relevant forms and submit them to the University's People's Armed Forces Department by June 20.

Students may choose the university's location, their pre-enrollment household-registration location or their usual place of residence as their enlistment location. After enlistment is approved, the University will process academic-status retention, awards and tuition compensation according to the required documents. Academic status is retained during service, and former service members may return to study within two years after discharge under applicable rules, with relevant financial support.
