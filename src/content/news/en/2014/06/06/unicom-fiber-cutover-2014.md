It is reported that the Network and Information Center issued a notice on June 6, 2014, that the Unicom fiber line will be cut over for optimization as planned by the Shantou branch of China Unicom, causing an interruption.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 00:30–06:00, June 7, 2014 |
| Scope | All campus users accessing Unicom websites |
| Recovery | No further notice will be issued |
| Contacts | Phone 86503400-1, mobile 13592895188 (short 665188), email stunic@stu.edu.cn |
| Issued by | Network and Information Center |

Users are asked to inform each other.
