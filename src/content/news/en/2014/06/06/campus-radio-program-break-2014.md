Shantou University Campus Radio will suspend its midday and afternoon programs from June 9, 2014, because the university has entered the final examination preparation period. Broadcasting is expected to resume when the next semester begins. Units with special needs may contact the station at 2203 or o_gbds@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (News Center)
