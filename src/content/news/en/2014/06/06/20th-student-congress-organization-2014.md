Shantou University’s 20th Student Congress will be held in the Science Lecture Hall on June 8, 2014. Delegates should check in at the entrance from 8:10 to 8:30 a.m., and the meeting will begin at 8:40 a.m.

The organizing committee asked delegations and relevant units to coordinate attendance and help ensure the meeting runs smoothly. Detailed meeting materials are in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organizing Committee of the 20th Student Congress)
> ⚠️ The meeting attachment is available on the OA system.
