Shantou University’s fourth summer teaching camp under the Shuguang rural school support project completed its volunteer recruitment on June 6, 2014. Fifty-six people registered, and 30 students were selected after registration review and interviews.

## Volunteer Teams

The selected volunteers will work in five teams: academic affairs, planning, logistics, research and publicity. The teams have seven, six, five, six and six members respectively. The University Youth League Committee, Young Volunteers Association and Shuguang volunteer team asked the selected students to prepare for the summer teaching service.

Student names are not reproduced to protect personal privacy.
