Shantou University’s School of Law has announced nominees for its outstanding party affairs worker and outstanding Communist Party member awards. The nominees were identified through democratic reviews conducted by the school’s party branches. The school plans to recognize seven outstanding party affairs workers and 19 outstanding Communist Party members for their work in party organization and service.

Written comments may be submitted to the School of Law’s party branch by June 12. Submissions must include the sender’s real name. The published contact telephone number is 86502215.

The nominee list is not reproduced here.
