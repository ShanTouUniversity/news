It is reported that the new medical school construction project at Shantou University will begin pouring foundation concrete for the basement cap base at 10:00 a.m. on June 6. Undertaken by Guangzhou Third Construction, the continuous pour totals approximately 380 cubic meters.

## Construction Schedule

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Work | Basement cap base foundation concrete pouring |
| Contractor | Guangzhou Third Construction |
| Start time | 10:00, June 6 |
| Expected completion | 03:00, June 7 |
| Volume | Approx. 380 m³ |

As the base slab pour requires uninterrupted continuous operation, the process will generate considerable noise and may affect residents near the construction site. The Project Coordination Office and the Key Construction Projects Office have expressed appreciation for the community's understanding and cooperation.
