The final round of the 2014 FLTRP CUP National English Debating Invitational Challenge was held at Beijing Foreign Studies University from May 18 to 23. About 105 teams participated, with judges from the Philippines, Malaysia and Singapore also joining the event.

Shantou University’s English Debate Society, a student organization of the English Language Center, sent teams STU1 and STU2. Both teams performed well, and STU1, competing as a reserve team, reached the top 40 in the elimination rounds. The teams were supported by the English Language Center and guided by the event’s coaches and experts.

Student debaters’ names are not reproduced to protect personal privacy.
