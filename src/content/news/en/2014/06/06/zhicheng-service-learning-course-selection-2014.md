Shantou University’s Zhicheng College has completed registration and interviews for its summer rural service-learning public-interest course. The project received 80 registrations, held interviews at Zhicheng College on June 4, and selected 40 students and two alternates.

Selected students and alternates should report to Lecture Room J-354 at Zhicheng College at 8:00 a.m. on July 14 for the opening session and course activities. Student names and majors are not reproduced to protect personal privacy.
