Shantou University will clear abandoned bicycles and unregistered motorcycles from student residential areas, staff residential areas and public areas in stages from June 9 through July 25, 2014. Designated periods and locations will be provided for owners whose vehicles may have been removed by mistake.

| Period | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| June 9–13 | Cleanup in student and staff residential areas and public areas |
| June 14–24 | First claim period; the student residential area will use its abandoned-vehicle parking point, while other areas will use nearby designated points |
| June 25–July 25 | Second claim period at the East Gate bridge |
| After July 25 | Unclaimed vehicles will be treated as ownerless, with bicycles entering a recycling program |

The university asks faculty and students to pay attention to vehicle arrangements during the cleanup and claim periods.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office, Student Affairs Office, Zhicheng College, Resource Management Office and Graduate School)
