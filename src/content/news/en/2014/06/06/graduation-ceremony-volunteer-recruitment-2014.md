Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office is recruiting 65 volunteers for the 2014 graduation ceremony. Volunteers will support reception and related services. Applicants should have communication, organizational and teamwork skills and be available on the afternoon and evening of June 26 and the morning of June 27.

Applicants must complete the registration form through the reservation system by noon on June 11 and describe relevant practice or volunteer experience in the remarks field. The university will select volunteers according to registration numbers and event needs. Training details will be announced separately.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
