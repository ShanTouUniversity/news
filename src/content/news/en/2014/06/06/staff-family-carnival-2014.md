The Shantou University Labor Union will hold the “Summer Carnival Family Garden Party” from 9 to 11:30 a.m. on Saturday, June 14, 2014, at the University Gymnasium. The event is open to faculty and staff at the main campus and their families and will include family sports and special performances.

Faculty and staff may register through the online reservation system from June 6 to 11. The fee is CNY 35 per family when paid through the campus card system or CNY 45 per family when paid in cash; each employee may register up to four places. Payment and ticket collection will take place from June 11 to 13. Each child must be accompanied by at least one parent, and participants should wear suitable sports clothing and shoes and follow on-site instructions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
