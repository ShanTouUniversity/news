It is reported that the Affiliated Primary School of Shantou University issued a notice on June 6, 2014, to handle registration for the fall 2014 first-grade intake.

According to the Shantou Municipal Education Bureau, children of main-campus staff who have reached 6 years old (born on or before August 31, 2008) are eligible. Parents are asked to complete registration at the primary school principal’s office from 15:00 to 17:00 on June 11, 2014. Those unable to register on time due to special circumstances should contact the school in advance to avoid delays in textbook ordering.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration time | 15:00–17:00, June 11, 2014 |
| Venue | Principal’s office, Affiliated Primary School |
| Eligibility | Children of main-campus staff born on or before August 31, 2008 |
| Required documents | Original and copy of household register and parent’s work permit |
| Phone | 86502262 |
| Contact | Ms./Mr. Weng |

Per Document Shan Shi Jiao [2014] No. 29, no school may enroll children under 6. Children who need to defer enrollment due to health or special circumstances must have parents or legal guardians submit a written application with supporting proof.
