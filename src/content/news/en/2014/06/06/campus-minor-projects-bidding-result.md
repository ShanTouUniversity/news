It is reported that the Shantou University Bidding Leadership Group has completed the evaluation of two minor campus projects procured through competitive price comparison, with the results announced on June 6.

## Winning Bids

| Project | Winning Bidder | Amount |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Centralized transfer station for branches and leaves | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 67,205.80 |
| Campus building expansion joint renovation | Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. | RMB 29,600.00 |

Both projects were awarded to Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd.

The public notice period ran from May 6 to May 9, 2014. Complaints may be directed to the STU Supervision and Audit Office at tel. 86502346 (fax: 86500536). For bidding inquiries, contact Qiu Minna or Yao Yijia at the Infrastructure Office, tel. 86502490.
