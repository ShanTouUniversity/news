The School of Science and Technology will host a Modern Mathematics lecture at 4:30 p.m. on June 9, 2014, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). Zhu Kehe, a professor and doctoral supervisor at the State University of New York, will present “Commutativity of Operator Tuples.”

The lecture is open to faculty and students. The presentation abstract is provided in the event attachment.
