Shantou University will organize a donation drive for the 2014 Guangdong Poverty-Relief Day among faculty and staff. The campaign theme is “Helping the Poor and Relieving Their Hardship.” Unions in each unit will collect and register donations and submit consolidated contributions to the university labor union by June 20.

The university labor union will process the donations in accordance with relevant provincial and municipal requirements. A list of donations will later be published on the university internal information system. The registration form and submission instructions are provided in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office and Labor Union)
>
> ⚠️ The donation registration form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
