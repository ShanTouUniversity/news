Shantou University has announced recognition results for its 2013 student extracurricular academic and technology activities. Under the university’s student technology competition evaluation rules, six teachers received the title of Outstanding Instructor, researchers from 13 student research projects received Student Research Innovation Awards, and three units including the School of Science received Outstanding Organization Awards.

The university encouraged the recognized individuals and units to serve as examples and continue advancing student academic and technology activities. The full list of recipients and project materials are in the OA attachments; student names are not reproduced here.
