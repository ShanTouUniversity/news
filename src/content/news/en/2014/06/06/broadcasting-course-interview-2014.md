Class 71464 of the Broadcasting and Program Hosting course at Shantou University’s School of Journalism and Communication will hold interviews from 1:00 to 4:00 p.m. on June 7, 2014, in the relevant teacher’s office at the school. Students who had already selected the course must still pass the interview.

Students unable to attend for special reasons may submit a personal video as directed by the course team. The video may include recitation, news broadcasting or a prepared hosting script and should be one to two minutes long. Submission details and course arrangements are provided in the OA attachment and by the course team.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Journalism and Communication)
> ⚠️ Video submission details and the activity attachment are available on the OA system.
