Shantou University’s School of Law and Zhicheng College have announced admission results for the “Enter University, Set Sail for Life” public-interest summer camp. The program received 54 registrations. After review, 28 students were admitted to Class A and 17 to Class B, for a total of 45 admitted students. Further training arrangements will be sent by email.

The admitted-student list is not reproduced to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (School of Law and Zhicheng College)
