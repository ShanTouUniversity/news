It is reported that the "Toward the Ocean" public service course group at Shantou University has announced that its spring 2014 sharing session will be held on Sunday, June 8, from 9 to 11 a.m. in lecture room E203. The event is open to all students and staff.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Spring 2014 "Toward the Ocean" Course Sharing Session |
| Time | Sunday, June 8, 2014, 9:00–11:00 a.m. |
| Venue | Lecture Room E203 |
| Organizer | "Toward the Ocean" course group |

"Toward the Ocean" is one of Shantou University's public service courses. At the session, the course group will present and discuss the outcomes and learning experiences of this semester's program, welcoming the campus community to attend.
