Shantou University’s Continuing Education Center for Technical Professionals has published its 2014 continuing education arrangements for university faculty and staff of affiliated units. Under the relevant requirements, annual continuing education generally must total at least 12 days or 72 hours and includes public, professional and elective courses.

The public-course requirement is 20 hours, the professional-course requirement is at least 42 hours, and the elective-course requirement is at least 12 hours. Public courses mainly use in-person instruction and include an open-book assessment. Professional hours may be earned through intensive training, lectures, study programs and eligible research achievements. Participants can select courses through the university training website and receive certificates after completing study requirements.

Public courses cost RMB 60 under the applicable standard, while professional courses offered by the university are free. Registration, hour reporting and certificate verification follow the OA attachments and training-system instructions.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Continuing Education Center for Technical Professionals)
> ⚠️ Course schedules, user guides and related attachments are available on the OA system.
