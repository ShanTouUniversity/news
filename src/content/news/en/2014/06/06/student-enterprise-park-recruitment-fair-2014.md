A recruitment fair for companies in the Shantou University Student Enterprise Park will take place from 3 to 5:30 p.m. on June 11, 2014, in Room E301. Applicants should submit a résumé when applying for a position, dress neatly, behave appropriately and follow the instructions of on-site staff.
