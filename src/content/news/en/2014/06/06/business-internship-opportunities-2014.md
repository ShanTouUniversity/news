Shantou University’s Business School has published internship opportunities at Shante Certified Public Accountants and Shantou Lecai. The internships are provisionally scheduled from early July to early August, with specific positions and requirements set out in the job information.

Interested students should send their resumes to `o_stw@stu.edu.cn` by June 12. Position details are available in the original OA notice.
