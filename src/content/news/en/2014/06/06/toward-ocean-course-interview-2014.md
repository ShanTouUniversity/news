Shantou University’s School of Science announced interview arrangements for its Toward the Ocean public-interest course after receiving nearly 100 registrations and completing an initial application review. Interviews will take place in Room D301 from 1:00 to 3:20 p.m. on June 8. Applicants will introduce themselves, draw topics, join a leaderless group discussion, answer questions and receive feedback.

The interview will begin at 1:00 p.m., with each group taking about 10 minutes. Applicants should wait in Room D302. On-site registration will be accepted from 3:40 to 4:00 p.m. Because places in the summer term are limited, applicants may also learn about the autumn-term course during the interview. Applicants needing a schedule adjustment should contact the course team by 5:00 p.m. on June 7.

The interview list and detailed course information are in the OA attachment; student names are not reproduced here.
