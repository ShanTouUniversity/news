STU Marine Ecology and Environmental Research Group, together with the Pearl River Estuary Chinese White Dolphin National Nature Reserve Administration, has launched a prize writing contest for STU students and faculty. The contest will collect original content for a marine-conservation science book aimed at primary-school readers. Submissions may include comics, picture books, fiction and popular-science writing.

Participants must work in teams of no more than 10 people. Entries should focus on Chinese white dolphins or other rare marine species and be suitable for readers in Grade 4 or below. Teams may register from June 6 to 27 by sending the application form to dolphinmbi@sina.com. The organizers plan to select shortlisted entries on August 20 and a final winning entry on October 1; the final winning work will be considered for publication.

One final winning team will receive CNY 18,000, while shortlisted teams representing 30 percent of entries will receive CNY 1,000 each.
