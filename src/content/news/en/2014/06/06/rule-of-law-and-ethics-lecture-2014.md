The Social Sciences Department will host a social-practice lecture from 9 to 10:30 a.m. on June 7, 2014, in the University Science Lecture Hall. Cai Caiang, deputy director of the Shantou Municipal Social Work Committee, will speak on “The Rule of Law and the Rule of Ethics: Humanistic Care in Law.”

The lecture is intended for students taking Mao Zedong Thought and the Theoretical System of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics this semester, specifically classes 67018, 67019, 67020 and 67021. Students should arrive between 8:45 and 8:55 a.m.

Cai is also a member of the Shantou Municipal Government Legal Advisory Committee and a visiting professor at several universities.
