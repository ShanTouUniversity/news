Shantou University’s hospital will provide follow-up chest X-ray examinations on June 9 during normal working hours for 2012 cohort undergraduates on the main campus who did not complete the examination during the May health check.

Eligible students should attend according to arrangements communicated by their school youth league organizations and Zhicheng College. The specific time slots follow the internal notice.
