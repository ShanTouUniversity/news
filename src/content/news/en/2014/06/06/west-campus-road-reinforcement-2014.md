To support construction of the Residential College Phase I project, Shantou University will repair and reinforce damaged sections of a western-campus road. Work is scheduled to begin on June 7, 2014, and finish in mid-July. Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. will carry out the project.

The work will include removing and repaving damaged concrete, replacing load-bearing storm-drain covers and repairing curbs. Pedestrians and vehicles are asked to slow down and take care during construction.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Key Projects Office, Resource Management Office)
