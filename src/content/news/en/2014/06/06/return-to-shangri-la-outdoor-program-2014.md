It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs announced on June 6 arrangements for the 2014 summer intermediate outdoor program "Return to Shangri-La."

According to the notice, shortlisted teams are required to continue refining their proposals and submit follow-up materials on schedule. The deadline for revised proposals is 12:00 midnight on June 9, and the deadline for PPT and other defense materials is 12:00 midnight on June 10. All materials should be sent to snowleopardstu@163.com.

Further details are available via Sina Weibo (http://weibo.com/u/3021556445) and the WeChat official account "Snow Leopard Outdoors" (雪豹户外). Attachments were released together with the notice.
