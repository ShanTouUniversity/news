The Shantou University Finance Office and Resource Management Office announced arrangements for settling each unit’s 2013 electricity-use index. Excess electricity charges will be deducted from the designated fund projects of the relevant units. Surplus indices may be retained to pay 2014 electricity charges or converted into supplementary daily operating funds at half their value.

Units that wish to designate how surplus indices are used should contact Liang by email at susaleung@stu.edu.cn or by phone at 86502477 by June 25. If no instructions are provided, the Finance Office will deduct excess charges from the unit’s daily or public funds and retain surplus indices by default for 2014 electricity charges.
