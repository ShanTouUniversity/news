Shantou University’s 2014 graduation ceremony will be held at the university Gymnasium on June 27. The General Administration Office invited faculty and staff representatives to attend and allocated 100 tickets, distributed among units according to staff numbers.

Faculty members could collect tickets from their school offices on the morning of June 25. Staff in other teaching and academic-support units could apply through the Academic Affairs Office, while administrative and service staff could apply through the labor union. Units were asked to send a representative to collect tickets from the confidential office, Room 237 of the Administration Center, before noon on June 25. The unit-level allocation is listed in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Administration Office)
