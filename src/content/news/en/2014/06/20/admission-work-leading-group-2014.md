Shantou University has established a leading group to coordinate its 2014 national college-entrance-examination admissions work in accordance with guidance from the relevant higher authorities.

Wen Xiaobo serves as chair, with Sun Fenqiang, Lu Xiaohua, Yu Hong, and Zeng Rui as members. The group will coordinate the university’s admissions work for the 2014 examination cycle.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University)
