Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office, Graduate School, Zhicheng College and Resource Management Office announced arrangements for students staying on campus during summer 2014. The stay period is June 29 to August 23. Resident undergraduates in the 2011–13 cohorts and graduate students may apply through the dormitory management system by 10 a.m. on June 30, selecting their stay period and providing a reason.

Applications will be reviewed by the dormitory counselor offices, and students should check the system for results. Students who need to change rooms because of maintenance must complete the room-transfer process before updating their summer-stay application. Those who miss the main application deadline may submit a late application through the system by 4 p.m. on a working day during the vacation.

Some medical-school cohorts are restricted to the first stay period. Undergraduates and graduate students with extended study periods must first obtain approval from the dormitory counselor office. Students staying on campus are subject to the detailed requirements in the original OA attachment.

> ⚠️ Detailed stay periods and accommodation requirements are available in the original OA attachment.
