Shantou University’s Network and Information Center and Resource Management Office asked units to review their May 2014 office phone bills. Telecom landline and mobile landline bill data had been entered into the university’s phone-management system, where designated staff could check and confirm each type of account.

Discrepancies should be reported by June 25: telecom landline issues to o_dhs@stu.edu.cn and mobile landline issues to stunic@stu.edu.cn. Bills not confirmed or disputed during the review period would be treated as confirmed. After verification, the Finance Office planned to deduct charges from the funding accounts designated by each unit on June 27.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center and Resource Management Office)
