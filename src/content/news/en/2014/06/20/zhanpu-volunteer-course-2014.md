Zhicheng College is recruiting students from across Shantou University for a volunteer-service course supporting the Guangdong project of the Zhanpu Program. The program, a collaboration between the Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Li Ka Shing Foundation with support from the Communist Party of China Organization Department and the All-China Women’s Federation, provides training for women serving on village committees and local civil-affairs officials.

The course plans to admit 30 students through interviews. It will run from August 22 to October 19, 2014, including intensive training on August 22–23 and community-service practice during the remaining period. Volunteers will assist with event organization, reception, conference services, interviews, photography and project summaries. Students who complete the course and pass its assessment will receive one credit.

Applications are due by 5 p.m. on June 25. The application form is available in the original OA attachment and should be emailed to o_zhicheng@stu.edu.cn.
