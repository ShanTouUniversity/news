Shantou University will organize its 2014 higher-education teacher induction training during the summer. The program is intended for newly recruited teachers, education staff, and other relevant personnel, as well as those who have not completed induction training or need to retake examinations or courses. Eligible graduates in relevant fields may apply to waive certain courses in accordance with applicable requirements.

The residential program will run from July 14 to 27 for a total of 100 class hours. Detailed course arrangements and venues are provided in the course schedule. Each university unit must compile participants’ materials and submit them to the Human Resources Office, Administrative Center Room 315, by June 23, while electronic materials should be sent to the designated email address. Participants must register through the STU Training Website. For participants based at the main campus, the 300-yuan training fee will be deducted from their salaries through the university’s Finance Office; after completion and receipt of a certificate, the university will reimburse the fee under applicable rules. Other expenses during the training are borne by participants.

Chen Ying is the contact person for registration and can be reached at 86502340. Textbooks, registration forms, and material requirements are specified in the original notice attachments.
