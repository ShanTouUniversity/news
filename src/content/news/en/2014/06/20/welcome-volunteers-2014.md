It is reported that, in support of Shantou University's 2014 freshman welcome efforts and to help new students settle into campus life more quickly, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University is recruiting 180 welcome volunteers from across the university. Applications are due by midnight on June 29.

## Recruitment Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Openings | 180 |
| Application deadline | Midnight, June 29 |
| Work period | Welcome period (starting September 6) |
| Return requirement | Report back to campus before August 25, 2014 for training |

## Requirements

According to the recruitment requirements, volunteers should have strong communication skills and be able to provide warm and attentive reception services that reflect the good image of STU students. They should also demonstrate a responsible attitude and team spirit, commit to staying at their posts, and follow reasonable arrangements. In addition, volunteers should not have courses or other activities during the welcome period that would create major conflicts.

## Application and Selection

Applicants should send the application form to the Youth League Committee's email at o_tw@stu.edu.cn, listing their personal strengths in the "personal profile" section. Those with course arrangements during the welcome period should fill in their class schedule in the designated field.

Selection will be based on merit; where candidates are equal, earlier applications will be given priority. The final list will be published on the office automation system and the Volunteer board of the campus BBS. During the welcome period, volunteers will assist with freshman reception, logistics support, and registration procedures.
