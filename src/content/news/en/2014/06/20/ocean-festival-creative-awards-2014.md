Shantou University’s School of Science announced the results of the second “Youth and the Ocean” festival for the 2013–14 academic year. The photography contest awarded one first-place, two second-place and three third-place entries. The judges also selected nine outstanding three-line poems by five students. Student names are omitted to protect their privacy.

The award-winning works and prize list are included in the original OA attachments. Recipients may collect their prizes from the School of Science Youth League Committee by June 25. The event was organized by the committee, the school’s “Into the Ocean” service-learning course team and the School of Science Youth Volunteers Association’s ocean-protection team.
