Zhicheng College will hold its seventh student-mentor development camp from June 30 to July 3, 2014. The program is for incoming mentors and alternates for the 2014–15 academic year, with training focused on communication, teamwork and leadership.

The opening and first-day sessions are scheduled to begin at 7:45 a.m. on June 30 in Lecture Room J-354 at Zhicheng College. The first day’s on-campus training calls for neat, appropriate dress. On the second day, participants will travel to a training base in Meizhou and should wear sports clothing.
