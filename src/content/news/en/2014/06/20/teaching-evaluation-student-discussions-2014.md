Shantou University’s Institute of Higher Education invited undergraduates to discuss improvements to the teaching-evaluation indicators and procedures in the university’s credit-based system.

The institute planned two sessions, from 8 a.m. to noon on June 26 and June 28, at Room G201 in the Social Sciences Department. Each session was to include 10 students. Applicants were expected to be interested in improving teaching quality and able to express evidence-based views; strong English skills were preferred. Students were asked to contact the institute at liyl@stu.edu.cn by midnight on June 23 for registration details.
