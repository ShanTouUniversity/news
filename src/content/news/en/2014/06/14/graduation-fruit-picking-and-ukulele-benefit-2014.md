Shantou University's Business School and STU Dazhan Tuan will hold an additional fruit-picking session for graduates on the afternoon of June 25, 2014. Participants will work in groups of five to seven and use picking tools under staff guidance; climbing trees is prohibited. The time and location will be confirmed according to fruit ripeness and weather.

A SEE Ukulele graduation benefit party will also take place. A donation of at least RMB 3 will secure admission. After necessary expenses, proceeds will go to Leiling Jimei Shuguang Primary School in Chaonan to help improve its campus environment. Registration will select 300 participants, with larger donations increasing the chance of selection. Registration for both the June 25 graduates' session and the June 28 themed event closes on June 22.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Business School and Office of Resource Management)
