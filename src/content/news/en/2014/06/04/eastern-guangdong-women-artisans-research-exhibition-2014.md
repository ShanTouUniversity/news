The “Women Artisans of Eastern Guangdong” student research project, organized by the STU Women’s Studies Center and a Hanshan Normal University research group on gender and Chaoshan culture, will hold an exhibition from 2:30 to 6 p.m. on June 8, 2014, in the STU Science Lecture Hall.

The project involved five months of field research and interviews. The exhibition will present techniques and objects associated with women artisans of eastern Guangdong, covering topics such as Chaoshan needlework and crochet, Chaoyang paper cutting, wood carving, Chaozhou opera and Hakka wine culture, along with stories about local women’s craft practices.
