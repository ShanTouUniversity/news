Shantou University’s Youth Volunteers Association will hold a blood drive on June 7 and 8, 2014, from 9:00 to 18:00 each day. The drive will take place outside the Second Canteen, opposite the supermarket, under the theme “Blood Connects Us.”

The activity is open to eligible students and staff. The notice states that donors should be between 18 and 55 years old, with a minimum weight of 50 kilograms for men and 45 kilograms for women, and should be in good health. Participants should rest adequately the day before, eat normally on the day of donation and avoid donating again within six months. First-time donors should bring an identity card; other participants may bring an identity card or donor card.

The organizers will also collect feedback and questions from students and staff during the drive.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth Volunteers Association, Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
