Shantou University's Business School is scheduled to hold an academic workshop titled “How to Write a Good Journal Article” from June 7 to 10, 2014. Professor Hui Tak-Kee of the National University of Singapore will lead the workshop, which will take place in the Business School's second-floor seminar room and is open to the university community.

The workshop aims to help faculty members improve their academic writing and publication and support the Business School's research development.
