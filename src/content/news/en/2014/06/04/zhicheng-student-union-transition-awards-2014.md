Zhicheng College held its student union transition and outstanding student leader awards ceremony at 3:30 p.m. on June 4, 2014, in Lecture Room 201 in Building E.

The ceremony reviewed the work of the sixth student union, featured the incoming seventh student union president’s address and introduced the new student leadership team. It also recognized outstanding student leaders for the 2013–14 academic year. Award recipients and student union officers attended, and members of the college student union were welcome.
