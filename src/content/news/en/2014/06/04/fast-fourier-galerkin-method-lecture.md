As part of its “Towards Modern Mathematics” lecture series, the STU School of Science will host a lecture by Professor Xu Yuesheng of Sun Yat-sen University at 2:30 p.m. on June 5, 2014. The lecture will be held in the Mathematics Laboratory, Room G-B412.

Titled “A Fast Fourier-Galerkin method for solving a boundary integral equation for the biharmonic equation,” the presentation will introduce a fast method for solving a boundary integral equation associated with the biharmonic equation. It will discuss operator splitting, Fourier basis functions, sparse matrix compression and numerical validation of approximation accuracy and computational complexity.
