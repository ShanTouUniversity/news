The 10th Shantou University Challenge Cup Competition for Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works concluded after more than a month of judging. Students from across the university submitted 38 entries.

The results included one first prize, three second prizes and four third prizes in inventions and innovations; one first prize, two second prizes and five third prizes in natural-science academic papers; and one first prize, three second prizes and six third prizes in social-science research reports and academic papers. A total of 26 works received awards.

The complete list of winning works is provided in the OA attachment. The organizers encouraged the winners to continue developing the university’s extracurricular academic and scientific activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities Leading Group and Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
