Shantou University's Youth League Committee announced on June 4, 2014, that college-level youth league committees and student clubs should update their administrative records by June 12. The requested materials cover club adviser information, club publications, images of existing club seals, and registration documents for approved college-level clubs.

Digital materials were to be sent to the designated University office, while printed documents were to be delivered to the Student Clubs Federation. Personal student contact details and a dormitory address in the original notice are omitted to protect privacy.

## Requirements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | June 12, 2014 |
| Records | Adviser information, club publications, seal images and approved college-level club registration |
| Submission | Digital copies to the address designated in the notice; printed copies to the Student Clubs Federation |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
