The 14th council election meeting of STU Cui Network is scheduled for June 11, 2014, from 3:00 to 5:30 p.m. The venue had not been finalized when the notice was issued. The meeting will review the 13th council’s annual work and elect members of the new council.

The new council is planned to include one chief executive officer, chief technology officer, chief operating officer and chief administrative officer. Students from across the university may apply. Candidates will be selected through applications, assessment interviews and review by the Communist Youth League committee. They will present their plans and answer questions at the election meeting, with each position decided by a vote of eligible members.

Applicants must submit an application form and a personal work plan to `11lfluo1@stu.edu.cn` by 11 p.m. on June 7. The venue and application attachments should be confirmed in the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University)
