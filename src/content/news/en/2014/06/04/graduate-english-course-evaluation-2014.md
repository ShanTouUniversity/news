Shantou University's Graduate School opened course evaluations for the current Graduate English course from June 4 to 11, 2014. Master's students enrolled in the course could sign in to the Graduate Affairs platform with their campus accounts and complete the evaluation under “Teaching Evaluation.”

Students were asked to rate each item carefully and objectively. Questions could be directed to the graduate academic affairs contact in their school or to the Graduate School.

## Evaluation Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Period | June 4–11, 2014 |
| Participants | Master's students taking Graduate English |
| Platform | STU Graduate Affairs System (https://gsao.stu.edu.cn) |
| Menu | “Teaching Evaluation” |
