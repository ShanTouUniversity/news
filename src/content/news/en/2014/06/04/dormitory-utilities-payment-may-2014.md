Shantou University's Resource Management Office published May 2014 dormitory utility bills on June 4. The meter-reading date was May 31. Students could check bills through the University's utility management system and submit a signed review request from June 4 to 10 during office hours.

Bills without disputes were scheduled for deduction from campus cards starting June 11. The regular payment period was June 11–13; overdue bills could be handled under University rules from June 16. Corrections requested after the review period were expected to take three to five working days to process.

## Payment Schedule

| Item | Schedule and method |
| --- | --- |
| Bill review | Submit requests to the Water and Electricity Center during office hours, June 4–10 |
| Regular payment | June 11–13 by campus-card deduction |
| Overdue handling | From June 16 under University rules |
| Bill lookup | STU Utility Management System (http://power.stu.edu.cn/) |
| Water and Electricity Center | 86502489 |
