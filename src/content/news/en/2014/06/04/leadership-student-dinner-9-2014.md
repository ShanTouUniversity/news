Shantou University's ninth student dinner with university leaders is scheduled for 6 p.m. on June 11, 2014, in Room 201 of Hongtai Restaurant. The event plans to invite 32 full-time students to exchange views with university leaders, department heads and faculty representatives. A university leader will chair the discussion.

Students were asked to register through the campus booking system by 11 p.m. on June 5. The invited student representatives were to be announced on the University's internal information network. The University will cover the meal costs.

## Event Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 6 p.m., June 11, 2014 |
| Venue | Room 201, Hongtai Restaurant |
| Participants | 32 students, university leaders and four to five department and faculty representatives |
| Registration deadline | 11 p.m., June 5, 2014 |
| Registration | Campus booking system (http://booking.stu.edu.cn/index.htm) |
