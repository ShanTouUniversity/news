The Shantou Federation of Trade Unions and Shantou Sports Bureau will hold the 2014 Shantou Workers’ Sports Meet from August to November. The STU Trade Union will form teams from staff based at the main campus.

Events include badminton, Chinese chess, snooker, table tennis, tai chi, basketball, tug-of-war, swimming, football, broadcast gymnastics, tennis, track and field, rope skipping, go and several recreational sports events.

Staff members who wish to participate must complete the registration form and email it to the university trade union at `o_xgh@stu.edu.cn` by June 12, using “Workers’ Sports Meet Registration” as the email subject. Each person may register for no more than two events. Events without enough participants to form a team may be cancelled, while oversubscribed events may require selection.

Competition rules, schedules and registration forms are provided in the attachments and should be confirmed in the original OA notice.
