Shantou University’s Engineering School held the defense for its fifth service-learning project cycle on May 28. After reviewing the projects by type and practical impact, the school approved nine projects for funding.

The approved projects are Borrowing Books: Passing on Culture, DIY Postcard Charity Sale, Books Send Love, Beautifying Dormitories, Renewing Vehicles, Clear Mirrors and Bright Eyes, Sign Language for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing People, Clothes Pass on Love, and Warmth and Sincere Haircuts. Project teams will organize their activities according to plan and apply service-learning principles in the follow-up work.
