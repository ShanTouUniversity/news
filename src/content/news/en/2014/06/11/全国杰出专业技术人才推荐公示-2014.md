Shantou University has issued a public notice on its nominee for National Outstanding Professional and Technical Talent. Following nominations by university units and review by the relevant offices, the university proposes Li Yuanyou as a candidate for the national award.

The notice is open for public comment from June 11 to 13. Units or individuals with objections should send written comments or an email to the Personnel Office by June 13, stating specific facts and using their real name. The office is in Room 315 of the New Administration Center and can be reached at 86502349 or `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
