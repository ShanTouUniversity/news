Shantou University has announced course selection arrangements for public-interest courses in the 2014-2015 summer and autumn terms. The university will offer 24 courses with 1,091 places. The courses are part of the general education common core, and students who complete the course and pass its assessment receive one credit.

Each course includes 48 class hours covering service learning, training, public-interest practice, reflection and assessment. Most sessions will take place on Wednesday afternoons or weekends in July or the autumn term. The courses are open to all undergraduate students, with one course allowed per student and places allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

Students who have not taken a public-interest course may make an initial selection from 9 a.m. on June 12 to 5 p.m. on June 13. All undergraduates may add or drop courses from 9 a.m. on June 16 to 5 p.m. on June 17; no changes will be accepted afterward. Students should use the credit system’s course guidance function to select a course beginning with code SER. Detailed course information is in the OA attachments. Academic Affairs Office inquiries may be directed to Liu at 86502736 or short number 882736.
