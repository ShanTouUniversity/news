Shantou University’s Law School will hold an internship and employment briefing together with workplace etiquette training on June 11 and 12. The activity is intended to help students understand workplace etiquette, strengthen professional skills and improve their competitiveness in internships and employment.

The original OA notice did not provide a specific time or venue; students should follow arrangements announced by the school.
