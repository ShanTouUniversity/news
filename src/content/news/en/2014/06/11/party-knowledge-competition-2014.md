Shantou University’s Organization and United Front Work Office circulated a Guangdong initiative for a knowledge competition on the Third Plenum of the 18th Communist Party of China Central Committee and a series of speeches by Xi Jinping. University units were asked to organize faculty, staff, students and community members to participate. The competition runs from June 1 to July 31.

Participants can access the Guangdong Party-member distance education website or use the mobile platform at `http://js.gdycjy.gov.cn`. Further details are provided in the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office)
> ⚠️ The competition attachment and mobile QR code are available on the OA system.
