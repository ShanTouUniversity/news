Shantou University will hold a coordination meeting on the departure of the 2014 graduating class and 2014 new student orientation at 9 a.m. on Monday, June 16, in Room 201 of the Administration Center.

The meeting is intended for heads of party branches, residential colleges, and relevant university offices. Its agenda covers graduate departure, admissions, and new student registration. Representatives from the Party and Government Office, Admissions Office, Student Affairs Office, Academic Affairs Office, Finance Office, Security Office, Library, Network and Information Center, Youth League Committee, and Resource Management Office are among those expected to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party and Government Office and Student Affairs Office)
