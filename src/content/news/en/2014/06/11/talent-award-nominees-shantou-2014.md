Shantou University has released its nomination information for the first Shantou Outstanding Talent Awards and proposed eight faculty and staff members for consideration. The nominees are divided among one Outstanding Talent Award, three Innovation and Entrepreneurship Awards in the innovation category, and four Young Talent Awards.

The names legible in the OA text are Li Dan, Li Yuanyou, Chen Yan, Zheng Huaiping, Zhang Xingwei, Fan Lisheng, and Zhou Xiaoping. One additional name contains an encoding error in the original text and cannot be identified accurately. The public notice runs from June 11 to 13. Comments should be submitted to the university’s Personnel Office by June 13 with specific facts and the sender’s real name. The office is in Room 315 of the New Administration Center and can be reached at 86502349 or `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Organization and United Front Work Office)
