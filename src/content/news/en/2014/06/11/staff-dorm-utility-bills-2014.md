Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has announced arrangements for checking May 2014 utility bills for staff dormitories. Meter readings were taken on June 1 and 2. Staff can sign in with their campus network accounts at `http://power.stu.edu.cn` to review their bills.

Anyone with a question may request a check at the Water and Electricity Center from June 11 to 13 by calling 2489. After the review period, undisputed charges will be deducted from staff members’ June 2014 salary accounts by the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
