Shantou University’s School of Arts and Design and the Watercolor Art Committee of the Guangdong Artists Association will hold the “2014 Chaoshan Watercolor Nomination Exhibition” and a symposium titled *Inheritance and Development* from June 23 to 30. The exhibition will be held at the school’s art museum.

The opening ceremony is scheduled for 9:30 a.m. on June 23, followed by the symposium from 10 a.m. to noon. Professor Wang Shouzhi, dean of the School of Arts and Design, will chair the discussion. The program aims to present Chaoshan watercolor works and examine the region’s watercolor tradition.

The organizers noted that watercolor painting has developed in Chaoshan for nearly a century, with regional works performing strongly at recent provincial exhibitions. Hanshan Normal University, Shantou Polytechnic, Jieyang Polytechnic and the Chaoshan Watercolor Institute are supporting organizations.
