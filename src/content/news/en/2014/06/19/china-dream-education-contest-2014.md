The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education launched a “My Chinese Dream” education program for students at universities across the province, inviting them to take part in creative projects and community activities. Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office circulated the announcement and encouraged schools and residential colleges to organize participation.

The program includes five competitions: an essay contest on cultural roots and the country, a blog contest on community care, a themed video contest, a campus micro-film contest, and a dormitory-culture presentation contest. Eligibility, entry requirements and forms are available in the original OA attachments.

Students must submit both printed and electronic materials by September 23, 2014. Printed entries should be delivered to the Student Affairs Office Education Management Section in Room 106 of the Administration Center. Electronic entries should be emailed to xtchen@stu.edu.cn, with the student’s name and competition category in the subject line. Large files may be submitted on a USB drive. Each school or residential college may also recommend one student community-education project aligned with the annual theme and guided by a counselor.
