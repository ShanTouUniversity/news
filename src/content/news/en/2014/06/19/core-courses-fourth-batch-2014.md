Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced 15 courses in its fourth core-course cohort. The university received 21 submissions in the third round and selected the courses after two expert reviews and discussions with instructors. Funding will be provided to support the selected courses.

The 15 courses span three fields. Citizenship and society includes *Global Justice: Poverty, Morality and World Order*, *World Leadership*, *The Concept of Freedom*, and *History of Chinese Intellectual Debates: From the Hundred Schools of Thought to the New Culture Movement*. Culture and values includes *Introduction to Western Culture*, *Topics in the Humanities*, *Philosophical Foundations of Scientific Methods*, *Introduction to Digital Humanities*, *Anthropology of Globalization*, *The American Dream and the Chinese Dream*, *The Evolution of Western Culture*, and *Cultural Interpretations of Shakespeare’s Plays*. Science and scientific methods includes *Mind, Thought and Behavior*, *Sixteen Lectures on Psychological Questions*, and *Nutrition, Environment and Health*.

The Academic Affairs Office and the Core Course Expert Group said they would continue soliciting core-course proposals each semester.
