Shantou University’s School of Journalism and Communication will present a showcase of films by its 2014 graduates at the Library Lecture Hall on June 21–22, 2014.

Screenings are scheduled for 10 a.m., 4 p.m. on June 21, 3 p.m. on June 22, and 7 p.m. The program includes works such as *Goodbye Daydream*, *The Old Man’s Blessing*, *Young People in a Small Town*, *Chaoshan Sugar* and *A Man Lives by His Breath*.
