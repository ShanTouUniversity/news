Shantou University’s 2014 regular Gaokao admissions period was scheduled to begin in early July and conclude later that month. The Admissions Office asked designated university units to recommend staff, with names due by June 25.

The Inspection and Audit Office, Student Affairs Office and several schools were each asked to nominate one person; the Academic Affairs Office and Network and Information Center were each asked to nominate two. The Resource Management Office (Security Office) was also to assign a leader to oversee security and utilities during admissions.

Nominees were expected to be impartial, disciplined, comfortable with computer operations and available throughout the admissions period. Anyone with an immediate family member taking the Gaokao that year was ineligible. The recommendation form is available in the original OA attachment.
