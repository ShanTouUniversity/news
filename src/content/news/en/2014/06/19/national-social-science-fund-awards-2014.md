It is reported that the 2014 results for annual and youth projects under the National Social Sciences Fund were recently released. Shantou University received funding for eight projects: one key project, five general projects and two youth projects.

The university had submitted 27 applications, including one key, 21 general and five youth projects. The formal-review pass rate was 100% at both the provincial and national social-sciences planning offices. STU’s funding rate was 29.63%, above the national average of 13.6%.

## Project Distribution

| Project type | Number | Main participating units |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Key projects | 1 | Social Sciences Department |
| General projects | 5 | Social Sciences Department, School of Arts, School of Law, Business School |
| Youth projects | 2 | School of Arts, Business School |

The funded research covers modern Chinese legal culture, normative and virtue ethics, reading cognition, academic publishing by foreign-language teachers, legal safeguards for elderly-care services, informal governance in online-shop startup teams, Chaoshan business culture and family-business succession. The Research Office asked project leaders to organize their studies carefully and ensure quality completion.
