It is reported that the Shantou Municipal Government Working Committee for Offices, Shantou Sports Bureau and Shantou Go Association will jointly hold the sixth Taienkang Cup Shantou Go Masters Tournament on July 5–6, 2014. The venue will be in urban Shantou and was yet to be confirmed.

The tournament is open to Go enthusiasts among deputy-division-level-or-higher officials, senior professionals and business managers working in Shantou. Participants will compete in five rounds under a Swiss-system format across three groups: officials, senior professionals and business managers. The top eight in each group will receive medals, and selected finishers may receive amateur-rank certificates from the Chinese Weiqi Association.

STU faculty and staff may register from June 20 to 26 by completing the registration form and emailing it to the Labor Union at o_xgh@stu.edu.cn with “Taienkang Cup Go Masters Tournament Registration” as the subject. Inquiries: 86503197, Ms. Ye.

> ⚠️ The registration form and competition attachments are available on the OA system.
