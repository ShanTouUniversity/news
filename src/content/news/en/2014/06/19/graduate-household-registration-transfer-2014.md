Shantou University’s Resource Management Office announced household-registration transfer arrangements for the Class of 2014. Graduates who moved their household registration to the university when they enrolled may complete the process from June 20 to July 3 using their diploma number. The list for checking diploma numbers is attached to the original OA notice and is not reproduced here.

Graduates should collect their household-registration card from the university’s Security Office and follow the procedure for their destination. Those moving to another area of Shantou, including Chenghai, Chaoyang, Chaonan and Nan’ao, may go directly to the police station serving their destination. Those returning to their place of origin must write the destination address on the back of the registration card. Those moving to an employer must provide a registration certificate or an acceptance letter from the employer’s personnel department.

Applicants should bring their ID card and household-registration card. A proxy must also provide an authorization letter and copies of the graduate’s and proxy’s ID cards, as well as a copy of the diploma. After the transfer, graduates should register at the destination police station within the one-month validity period. Contact details are listed in the original OA notice.

> ⚠️ The diploma-number lookup list and full procedures are available in the original OA attachments; personal identification numbers are not reproduced here.
>
