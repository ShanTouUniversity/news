Lai Lisi, a 2011 Shantou University alumna and in-depth reporter for *Shenzhen Evening News*, will return to campus on June 26 to discuss university life, career planning and employment. The talk is titled “My Journey Across Guangzhou, Foshan and Shenzhen: Self-Development, Career Planning and Time Management.”

The session will run from 10 a.m. to noon in Lecture Room J-354 at Zhicheng College. Interested attendees may register by emailing o_zctw@stu.edu.cn by noon on June 25. The college planned to notify selected participants by email or phone before the end of that day.

Lai previously worked for *Yangcheng Evening News* and led its Shunde bureau. She now leads the “Shenzhen Oral History” project and contributes to the alumni exchange platform ohmystu.
