The third meeting of Shantou University’s Eighth Degree Evaluation Committee was held on June 18, 2014, to review degree awards for that year’s undergraduate graduates. The committee approved bachelor’s degrees for 1,607 graduates across regular and adult higher-education programs.

Regular-program graduates received degrees in literature, law, management, science, engineering, economics, arts and medicine. Adult-program graduates received degrees in literature, law, management, engineering, medicine and science. The committee also approved 10 second bachelor’s degrees and an additional bachelor’s degree in economics for a 2013 finance graduate.
