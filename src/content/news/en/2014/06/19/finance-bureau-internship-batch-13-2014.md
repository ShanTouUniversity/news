It is reported that the Shantou Municipal Government Finance Bureau is recruiting three interns from STU’s Business School. Eligible applicants are graduate students and undergraduate students in the 2010 or 2011 cohorts who can complete a continuous internship. Applicants should have strong writing skills and an understanding of current economic development and reform.

The internship begins on July 1, 2014, and lasts three and a half months during normal working hours. Applicants must email the *Shantou Municipal Government Finance Bureau Internship Registration Form* and an internship proposal to o_stw@stu.edu.cn by 11:00 a.m. on June 23. Interviews are scheduled for 3:00 p.m. on June 23 in the Business School seminar room on the second floor of the Wenxi Building.

Selected interns will attend pre-internship training, and the Finance Bureau will provide an appropriate work allowance. Inquiries: 86502725, Ms. Su.

> ⚠️ The internship registration form and other attachments are available on the OA system.
