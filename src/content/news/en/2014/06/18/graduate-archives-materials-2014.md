It is reported that the archives of Shantou University’s graduating class of 2014 were being prepared for dispatch. The Student Affairs Office’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center asked relevant units to collect and transfer materials that must be placed in the archives.

All relevant units must send the materials to Room 106 of the Graduate Employment Guidance Center in the New Administration Center by June 26, 2014, so that filing can be completed.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
