It is reported that, following deliberation at the 7th meeting of the Standing Committee of the CPC Shantou University Committee in 2014 and the 9th university leadership meeting of 2014, the university appointed Lin Lushui Deputy Secretary of the Graduate School Party General Branch, presiding over its work, and Director of Student Affairs. The appointment carries a six-month probation period.

The CPC Shantou University Committee issued the appointment document on June 18, 2014.

The university had previously published a pre-appointment notice for the position; the new notice confirms the formal appointment.
