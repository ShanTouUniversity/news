It is reported that the third meeting of the eighth Shantou University Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee was held on June 18, 2014, to review the degree applications of 25 doctoral students. The committee decided to award them doctoral degrees.

The awards comprise 13 doctorates in science, one in engineering and 11 in medicine.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the recipients’ names. The complete list is provided in the original Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee document.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee)
