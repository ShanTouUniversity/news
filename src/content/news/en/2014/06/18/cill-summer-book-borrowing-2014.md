It is reported that the Center for Independent Language Learning (CILL), a unit of Shantou University’s English Language Center, is located on the left side of the first floor of the New Library and holds about 2,000 foreign-language books. Faculty and students with a valid campus card may borrow books.

To support summer reading, books borrowed from the date of the notice may be returned by the beginning of the fall semester rather than within the usual two-week lending period. CILL encouraged faculty and students to borrow foreign-language reading of interest during the break.
