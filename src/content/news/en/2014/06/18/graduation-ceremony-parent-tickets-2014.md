It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on June 18, 2014, to open reservations for parent tickets to the 2014 Commencement.

The university will hold the 2014 Commencement on June 27 in the Gymnasium, reserving seats to invite parents of graduates.

## Reservations

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 2014 Commencement, June 27, Gymnasium |
| Reservation period | 09:00, June 19 – 16:00, June 20, 2014 |
| System | STU Reservation Center http://activity.stu.edu.cn (log in with personal campus network account) |
| Rules | Each graduate may invite up to two parents in principle; actual tickets allocated depend on overall demand; non-graduate accounts may not apply |
| Tickets available | About 450 |
| Selection | After the deadline, the university will count applications and draw lots randomly to determine the recipients and number of tickets; results will be announced via OA |
| Issued by | Student Affairs Office |

Graduates wishing to invite parents are asked to log in and complete the information within the reservation period; late applications will not be accepted.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
