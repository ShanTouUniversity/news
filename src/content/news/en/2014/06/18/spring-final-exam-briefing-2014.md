It is reported that, two days into Shantou University’s Spring 2014 final examinations, university supervisors, Academic Affairs Office staff and staff from some schools had inspected examination rooms. The first briefing said invigilators generally arrived on time and organized examinations according to the rules, while overall order was maintained.

The inspections also found that some students did not bring their student cards. During the fourth examination session on June 16, a student from the Department of Electronic Engineering was found carrying examination-related materials in Room E405 during the “ELC4” examination and was deemed to have cheated.

The Academic Affairs Office reminded students and invigilators to comply with the university’s invigilation rules and examination discipline.
