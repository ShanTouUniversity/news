It is reported that, with support from the Li Ka Shing Foundation, the Shantou University Labor Union will hold a birthday gathering for faculty and staff celebrating birthdays in June and July from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on June 29, 2014, at the Guorui branch of Zhuangshi Xiangji.

Online reservations will run from 8:00 a.m. on June 19 to 3:00 p.m. on June 25. Payment deductions will take place on June 25–26, and tickets will be distributed from 9:30 a.m. to noon on June 27. Faculty and staff may register through the reservation system; those paying in cash should follow the union’s payment and ticket-exchange procedure.

The event is open to faculty and staff and their families. The university will not provide transportation. The activity plan and detailed registration process are available in the OA attachments.
