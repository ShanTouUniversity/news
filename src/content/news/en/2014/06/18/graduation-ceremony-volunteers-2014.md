It is reported that the Shantou University Student Affairs Office received 363 applications after recruiting volunteers across the university on June 6 for the 2014 graduation ceremony. The office selected 65 volunteers and five alternates.

Selected volunteers must attend training at 7:00 p.m. on June 23 in Room 204 of Building D, take part in the graduation rehearsal beginning on the afternoon of June 26, and attend the graduation ceremony on the morning of June 27. Those unable to attend training because of examinations or other reasons were asked to email an explanation by noon on June 20.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the names of volunteers or alternates. The complete list is available in the OA attachment.
