It is reported that, during Week 16 of the 2013–2014 spring semester, Shantou University’s study-style inspection team inspected Building E and issued Report No. 268.

At 8:00 a.m. on Wednesday, June 11, 19 students were recorded as arriving within five minutes after the start of class. At the same time on Thursday, June 12, 13 students were recorded as late at the same location. The university notified the relevant schools and residential colleges and asked them to strengthen education on university rules and study-style development.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the names of the students concerned. The list and original inspection material are available on the OA system.
