It is reported that the third meeting of the eighth Shantou University Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee was held on June 18, 2014, to review the master’s degree applications of 706 graduate students. The committee decided to award the corresponding master’s degrees.

The recipients included 422 graduating graduate students, 270 professional-degree students, 11 candidates receiving degrees through equivalent education, and three in-service master’s candidates who were university teachers. The degrees covered the humanities, education, law, management, economics, arts, science, engineering and medicine.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the recipients’ names. The complete list is provided in the original Academic Degrees Evaluation Committee document.
