It is reported that the Li Ka-shing Foundation is giving each member of Shantou University’s graduating class of 2014 a copy of *The Ladder of Innovation*. The Student Affairs Office asked each school and residential college to send a representative to collect the books.

Collection must be completed by 5:00 p.m. on June 23, 2014, at the Graduate Employment Guidance Center in Room 106 of the New Administration Center. The book is by Ari Ladenberg and Shalom Meitner.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
