It is reported that Shantou University Library has officially opened the HeinOnline full-text law database. The database contains more than 2,200 law journals, 3,800 volumes of authoritative international-law works, 100,000 cases and 4,500 scholarly law monographs.

Faculty and students can access HeinOnline through the library’s database page at <http://www.lib.stu.edu.cn/dbdetail.aspx?id=349>.
