It is reported that the Organization and United Front Work Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee forwarded the *Rules for the Development of Party Members of the Communist Party of China* (Zhong Ban Fa [2014] No. 33) on June 18, 2014.

The notice requires the Party Committee of the Medical College and all general Party branches and directly affiliated Party branches on the main campus to organize Party members to conscientiously study and implement the rules. The full text is available in the OA attachment.
