It is reported that the Party General Branch and Youth League Committee of Shantou University’s School of Law recognized 30 student cadres as “Outstanding Student Cadres” for the 2013–2014 academic year after a democratic selection.

The recognition acknowledged students’ work in student organizations, self-management, extracurricular academic and science activities, cultural and sports programs, and social practice. The recipients were encouraged to continue serving the school and their fellow students.

To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the recipients’ names or class information.
