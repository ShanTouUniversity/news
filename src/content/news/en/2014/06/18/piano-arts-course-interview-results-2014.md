It is reported that Shantou University’s Arts Education Center completed interviews for the fall 2014–2015 Piano Arts and Advanced Piano Arts courses and announced the successful interview arrangements. Both courses are taught by Dong Xuemin.

The notice provided the results to the relevant students. To protect student privacy, this report does not reproduce the successful applicants’ names or student numbers; the complete list is available in the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Arts Education Center)
