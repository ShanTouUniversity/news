It is reported that Shantou University announced the winning contractor for the West Campus Courtyard roof-repair project on June 18, 2014. Guangdong Yusheng Construction Co., Ltd. won the bid at RMB 225,494.85.

The public-notice period runs from June 18 through June 20. The university’s Infrastructure Office was the procuring entity, and objections may be submitted to the university’s Supervision and Audit Office.
