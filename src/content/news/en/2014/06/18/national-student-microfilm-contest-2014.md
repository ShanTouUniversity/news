It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University forwarded the School Department of the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League’s notice on the 2014 National Microfilm Competition for University and Secondary Students. With “Core Socialist Values: Campus Action” as its theme, the competition is open to university and secondary students nationwide.

The competition consists of a university-student microfilm creation category, a secondary-student microfilm category, and the G-Ke G-Pai Youth Image microfilm category. STU’s Youth League Committee planned to collect works on campus in late August and recommend selected teams or individuals.

Detailed entry requirements and registration procedures are available in the OA attachment.
