It is reported that registration has opened for the second-half 2014 National Computer Rank Examination. The examination will be held in sessions beginning at 9:00 a.m. on September 20, 2014. Levels 1 through 4 cover computer fundamentals, programming, network technology, databases, software testing, information security and embedded-system development, among other subjects.

## Registration Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Registration period | June 23–27, 2014; no weekend registration |
| Registration venue | Student Affairs service window, third floor, New Administration Center |
| Required procedure | Bring the original ID card, be photographed electronically and sign the printed registration form |
| Examination date | September 20, 2014, in sessions from 9:00 a.m. |
| Examination fee | RMB 137 for Levels 1–4 |
| Inquiries | 86502402, 86502411 |

Candidates must personally confirm and sign their registration information. The full subject list and related instructions are available in the OA attachment.
