Shantou University’s Party and Administrative Office reported on the university’s 2013 records filing on June 24. A total of 44 units transferred 11,886 archival items. The office said document quality and data-entry accuracy had improved over the previous year, while duplicate filings had declined.

The report identified incomplete collections, missing seals, and errors or omissions in electronic files among the remaining issues. Part-time records officers were asked to collect materials systematically and file official party and administrative documents as they are processed. Both electronic and paper copies should be filed, except that paper copies are not required for short-term, non-official electronic records. Photographs should include the event, date, photographer and location, with representative images selected. Units with outstanding materials were asked to complete their submissions by August 31.
