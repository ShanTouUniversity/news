Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office completed the 2014–2015 academic calendar. University units at the main campus could collect copies from the office service counter on June 24. Units were asked to count their current faculty and students, excluding that year’s graduates and the incoming 2014 class. A digital version was available on the office website.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
