Shantou University’s Network and Information Center will adjust campus network accounts and university email services for 2014 undergraduate and graduate students. Internet access through campus accounts will stop on July 1, while application authentication permissions for systems such as the credit-based education, utilities and MYSTU systems will be adjusted by the relevant units.

University email accounts will be converted to alumni email accounts. After August 20, the server will no longer retain graduates’ personal email data, so graduates should transfer their messages in advance. Graduates who remain at the university for work may reapply for the services through the Network Center after completing personnel-registration procedures.

Questions may be directed to 86503400-2 or support@stu.edu.cn.
