Shantou University’s Business School has announced the selection results for the 13th internship cohort with the Shantou Financial Work Bureau. After consultation with the bureau, three Business School students were selected. Other applicants were advised to follow subsequent activity notices from the school.

To protect student privacy, this report does not publish the selected students’ full names. The complete list is available in the original OA attachment.
