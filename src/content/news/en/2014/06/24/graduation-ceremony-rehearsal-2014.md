Shantou University will hold a rehearsal for its 2014 graduation and awards ceremony at the gymnasium on June 26. The wind band, choir and instructors, and members of the Antarctic expedition project are to assemble at 15:00. Faculty and students receiving the awards for teaching excellence, outstanding service, the STU Medal and sportsmanship, as well as doctoral-degree candidates and school deans, are to assemble at 19:00.

Relevant staff from the General Office, Student Affairs Office, Youth League Committee, News Center, Network and Information Center, Sports Department and Resource Management Office, together with counselors responsible for graduation-ceremony matters, are also required to attend on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (General Office and Student Affairs Office)
