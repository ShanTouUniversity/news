The 188th Shantou University Engineering Research Forum will take place at 10:00 on June 26 in the College of Engineering Academic Exchange Room (Gannan 312). Professor Tianjian He of the University of Wollongong will give a talk titled “The 3-legged Stool for Railway Development: Industry, Education and Research.”

The forum is open to graduate students, doctoral students and relevant faculty members in the College of Engineering, as well as other interested members of the university community.
