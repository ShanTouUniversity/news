Shantou University’s Graduate School and the Foshan Shunde Graduate Education Development Center plan to establish a graduate innovation-practice base and recruit current graduate students for placements at enterprises in Shunde through the Guangdong (Shunde) Industrial Design Research Institute. Participants will receive accommodation, reimbursement for hard-seat train travel and living subsidies, including RMB 500 per month from the Shunde Graduate Education Development Center; enterprise subsidies will depend on the project.

Interested graduate students must obtain their supervisor’s consent and email the application form to fengqiwang@stu.edu.cn by June 30. The subject line should include the applicant’s name, institution, major and contact details as required by the notice. Placements will generally last at least three months, with the exact period determined by each project.

The contact is Wang Fengqi, reachable at 86504000. The Graduate School will compile applications and submit them to the partner organizations, which will recommend applicants according to project needs and preferences.

> ⚠️ The application form and detailed project information are available in the original OA attachments.
