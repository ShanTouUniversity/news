Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has published the evaluation result for the contractor selection for renovation of the third and fourth floors of G Building. Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Co., Ltd. won the bid with an amount of RMB 410,888.80.

The public-notice period runs from June 24 to June 26. Tender-related questions may be directed to the university Infrastructure Office at 0754-86502490, and complaints to the Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346.
