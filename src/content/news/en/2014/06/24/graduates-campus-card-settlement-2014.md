Shantou University’s Finance Office and Network and Information Center will settle and close the campus cards of 2014 undergraduate and graduate students on July 7. Remaining balances will be returned to graduates’ accounts at the Shantou University branch of Bank of China, with refunds planned by August 27.

Graduates whose registered account has been closed may visit the [campus-card closure refund website](http://smartcardcancel.stu.edu.cn) from June 26 to confirm and enter a valid personal bank account. The university noted that student accounts do not need to be closed before graduation and will close automatically two years after their balance reaches zero.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Finance Office and Network and Information Center)
