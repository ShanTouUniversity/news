Shantou University’s Student Community Management and Service Center has announced the 2014 graduate dormitory-checkout guide. Undergraduate checkout will take place from June 27 to June 30, while graduate-student checkout will take place from June 27 to June 28; both periods run from 8:30 to 17:00 daily. Undergraduates must finish checkout and leave by 17:00 on June 30, and graduate students by 17:00 on June 28.

Graduates should pack their belongings and clear their beds, wardrobes and drawers before checking out. At the assigned building service point, they must present the departure form, return keys and air-conditioner remotes, receive an inspection form, and complete the final stamp at the Student Community Management and Service Center on the first floor of Building C.

After checkout, graduates may not continue staying in the dormitory, borrow keys or store belongings there. The university recommends avoiding the 15:00–17:00 peak period. Graduates should also lock doors and windows and turn off water and electricity if a non-graduating roommate is away.
