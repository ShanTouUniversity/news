Shantou University has published its revised charter for public comment after a drafting process that began in September 2011. The draft was discussed and reviewed by the university’s faculty congress, leadership meetings and the Standing Committee of the Party Committee before being released in accordance with guidance from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education.

University units may submit comments or suggestions to the General Office by 17:00 on June 27. The contact is Qiu Saiqing, reachable at 86502317 or sqqiu@stu.edu.cn.
