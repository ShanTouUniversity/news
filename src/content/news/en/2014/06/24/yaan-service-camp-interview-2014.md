The “Love for Ya’an” service camp for Sichuan disaster areas received more than 70 applications and will arrange group interviews according to applicants’ preferred times.

The interviews will take place on June 25 at Wenbei 403, in sessions from 16:00 to 17:40 and from 19:00 to 21:00. The format includes self-introductions, a randomly assigned topic, an unmoderated group discussion, comments and questions, and participant questions. Applicants are asked to arrive 10 minutes early; failure to attend will be treated as withdrawal.

The course team is also seeking two volunteers to assist with registration and contacting absent applicants. Questions may be directed to Teacher Wang at 665506.

> ⚠️ Group assignments and individual interview times are available in the original OA attachment.
