It is reported that the STU Party Committee has approved the election results of the first general meeting of all Party members of the Research General Branch, following review at the 8th meeting of the Standing Committee of the University Party Committee in 2014.

According to the official document (Shan Da Dang Zi [2014] No. 13), the composition of the Research General Branch Committee is as follows:

| Position | Name | Note |
|----------|------|------|
| Secretary | Gao Jian | Associate division level |
| Member | Lin Yuejuan | — |
| Member | Hu Yunxiang | — |
| Member | Lu Jiandong | — |
| Member | Liu Xingming | — |

The election outcome from the first general meeting of the Research General Branch has been formally endorsed by the University Party Committee.
