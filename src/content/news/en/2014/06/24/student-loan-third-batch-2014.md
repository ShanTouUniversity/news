Following bank approval, 61 Shantou University students were approved for the third batch of national student loans for the 2013–2014 academic year. The loans will be disbursed soon to offset tuition and accommodation fees; any remaining balance will be transferred to the student’s personal Bank of China account.

Approved students may check their fee status through the Student Affairs System and must collect their loan contracts from the Student Aid Management Center in Room 106 of the Administration Center by July 1 using their campus cards. The center can be reached at 86502249.
