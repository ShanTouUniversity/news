Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has published the evaluation result for the contractor selection for its laboratory environment renovation project. Shantou Dahao Construction General Co., Ltd. won the bid with a reduction rate of 28.80%.

The public-notice period runs from June 24 to June 26. Tender-related questions may be directed to the university Infrastructure Office at 0754-86502490, and complaints to the Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346.
