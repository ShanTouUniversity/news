Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office has published the evaluation result for the contractor selection for renovation of the East Lecture Hall. Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. won the bid with an amount of RMB 868,646.02 under Option 1.

The public-notice period runs from June 24 to June 26. Tender-related questions may be directed to the university Infrastructure Office at 0754-86502490, and complaints to the Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346.
