Shantou University’s security department will implement temporary traffic controls to keep campus roads clear during the Ninth Board of Trustees meeting.

The controls will run from 00:00 on June 26 to 18:00 on June 27 around the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and the Academic Exchange Center. Except for authorized vehicles, access to the affected area will be restricted; vehicles may park in front of P Building. Motor vehicles may not park on the pedestrian street or at either entrance.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
