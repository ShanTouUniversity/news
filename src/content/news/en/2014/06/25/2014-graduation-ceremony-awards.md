Shantou University’s 2014 graduation ceremony and awards presentation was scheduled for 7:50 a.m. on Friday, June 27, at the university gymnasium. Graduates were asked to assemble by 6:30 a.m. and begin entering in groups with their counselors at 6:35. College leaders due to sit on the platform were asked to gather in the gym’s fitness room by 7:00.

Ticketed family members could enter from 6:40 and take their assigned seats. Holders of east-section tickets were to use the main entrance, while west-section ticket holders were to use the west entrance. Family members without reserved seats could watch a live broadcast in the Science and Technology Reporting Hall from 7:30 to 9:30 a.m. Children under 12 were not admitted. Attendees were asked to follow the dress code and staff instructions.
