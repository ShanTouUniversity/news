Shantou University’s Human Resources Office published a public notice on June 25 naming its proposed candidates for the Guangdong higher education Qianbai Shi talent program. Following review by university departments and an expert panel, the university proposed Zhang Yueling and Zheng Huaiping as advanced individual candidates.

The notice period ran through July 1. Units and individuals wishing to raise an objection were asked to submit written comments or email the Human Resources Office by that date, with specific supporting facts. The office is in Room 315 of the Administration Center. Phone: 86502349; email: rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
