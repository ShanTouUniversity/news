Shantou University’s Law School was scheduled to host a lecture on June 27 titled “Law Reporting as Social History: ‘The Artificial Reason and Judgment of Law’,” also presented in Chinese as “From Case Reports to Social History: Human Factors and Legal Judgment.” The talk was set for 10:00–11:30 a.m. in Room 213 of the Law School and would be delivered in Chinese and English. It was open to the university community.

The speaker, Peter Lo, is a senior partner at Hong Kong law firm Woo Kwan Lee & Lo. He formerly served as president of the Law Society of Hong Kong and has held posts with several Hong Kong public and cultural organizations. He is qualified to practise in Hong Kong, the United Kingdom and Australia.
