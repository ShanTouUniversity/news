Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced arrangements for 2014 graduates with outstanding national student loans or unpaid fees to collect degree documents and related certificates. Processing was scheduled for June 27 through July 1 at the Student Financial Aid Center, Room 106, Administration Center. Required documents and collection procedures varied according to each graduate’s loan repayment and fee status.

Graduates who had signed repayment agreements were asked to complete departure procedures and bring their original identity documents. Those without an agreement needed to contact their bank to confirm repayment. Students with unpaid fees needed university approval for deferred payment and a repayment agreement. Graduates who had settled their loans and submitted clearance notices could collect original degree certificates or copies as specified in the notice. Representatives collecting documents were required to provide authorization and identification for both parties.

Graduates continuing to domestic postgraduate study or a second degree who sought an extension of their loan term were asked to bring their admission notice and loan documents to Bank of China’s Shantou branch by July 1 to sign a supplementary agreement.
