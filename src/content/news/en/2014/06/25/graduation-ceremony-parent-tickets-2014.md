Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced that family tickets for the 2014 graduation ceremony had been distributed to the colleges. Graduates who received tickets could collect them from their college Youth League committee. Postgraduate and doctoral students could collect tickets from the Graduate School Youth League committee; medical students could collect them from the Medical School’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center.

The names of ticket recipients and ticket quantities are listed in the OA attachment and are not reproduced here to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
