Shantou University Affiliated Primary School was scheduled to hold an orientation for incoming first-grade pupils at 2:40 p.m. on August 29, 2014, in the first-grade classroom on the school’s second floor. Parents were asked to accompany their children and bring vaccination records and three one-inch color photographs.

The school also encouraged families to support children’s health and study habits as they adjust to primary school, and to maintain communication with teachers.
