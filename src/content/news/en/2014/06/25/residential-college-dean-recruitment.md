Shantou University’s Human Resources Office announced on June 25 that it was recruiting several deans for new residential colleges. The university planned to complete three colleges in August 2015.

Applicants were expected to hold a doctoral degree and professorial rank, or an associate professorship or higher at a recognized overseas university. The university also sought candidates with higher-education management experience, strong academic credentials, an international outlook and a willingness to live in a residential college. Responsibilities would include college planning, student development, staffing and daily operations, alongside teaching and research in an academic school.

The posts were full-time, five-year appointments. The university offered housing of about 140 square meters within a college and competitive compensation based on experience and qualifications. Applicants were asked to email a letter, detailed CV and supporting documents to recruitment@stu.edu.cn with “Residential College Dean Application” in the subject line. Selection was scheduled to begin on August 16, 2014; applications would be accepted until the university announced its appointments. Contact: Li Chang, 86502365.
