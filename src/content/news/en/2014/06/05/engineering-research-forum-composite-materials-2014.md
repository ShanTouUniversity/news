The 187th STU Engineering Research Forum will take place from 4 to 6 p.m. on June 6, 2014, in Room G302. Professor Yuan Hong of the College of Science and Engineering at Jinan University will give a presentation titled “Composite Laminate Theory and Its Application in Structural Strengthening.”

Faculty, graduate students and undergraduates in the Civil Engineering Department, as well as other interested members of the university community, are welcome.
