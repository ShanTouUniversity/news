The third meeting of Shantou University eighth Degree Evaluation Committee will be held at 2:30 p.m. on June 18, 2014, in Room 201 of the New Administration Center. Relevant offices are asked to notify their committee members. Because a statutory quorum is required, members should attend as scheduled; anyone unable to attend for special reasons should contact the Degree Office before the end of the morning workday on June 16.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Degree Office and Graduate School)
