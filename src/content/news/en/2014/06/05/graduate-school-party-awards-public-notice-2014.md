The Party General Branch of STU’s Graduate School publicized proposed award recipients for the 2013–14 academic year. Following reviews by student Party branches and the Graduate School Party General Branch, eight people were proposed for outstanding Party affairs worker awards and 23 for outstanding Party member awards.

Comments may be submitted in writing, in person or by telephone to the Graduate School Party General Branch by 5 p.m. on June 10, 2014. Respondents must provide their real names. The contact telephone number is 86504000. This report does not reproduce students’ names or other personal information.
