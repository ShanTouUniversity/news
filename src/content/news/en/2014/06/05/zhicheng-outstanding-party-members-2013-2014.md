It is reported that the General Party Branch Committee of Zhicheng College of Shantou University issued Document No. 2 [2014] on June 5, 2014, to honor outstanding student Party members and Party affairs workers for 2013–2014.

Over the past year, Party members studied Deng Xiaoping Theory and the important thought of Three Represents, practiced the Scientific Outlook on Development, fulfilled their duties and contributed actively to the college’s development. Upon recommendations by Party branches and deliberation by the General Party Branch Committee, honors were conferred.

## Awardees (anonymized)

| Award | Number | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outstanding Communist Party Members | 14 | Student Party members with outstanding performance |
| Outstanding Party Affairs Workers | 2 | Members with outstanding contributions to Party affairs |

The full lists are available in the original OA notice and are withheld here for privacy. The committee called on all student Party members to learn from the honorees and play an exemplary role.
