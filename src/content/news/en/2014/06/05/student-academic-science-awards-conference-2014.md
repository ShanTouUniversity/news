STU will hold its 2013 Student Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities Review and Awards Conference at 7:30 p.m. on June 6, 2014, in Seminar Room 30A of the Library.

The conference will review the previous year’s student academic and scientific activities and recognize individuals and groups with outstanding results. It will also honor winners from the 10th Challenge Cup Competition for Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works and the STU selection round of the 2014 Create Youth Guangdong College Student Entrepreneurship Competition.

Members of the student extracurricular academic and scientific activities leading group, relevant school and college representatives, award representatives and student representatives were invited to attend.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University and STU Student Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Activities Leading Group)
