As part of the STU School of Science’s Science Salon series, Professor Liu Jianzhong of Sun Yat-sen University will give a lecture from 10 a.m. to noon on June 9, 2014. The lecture will take place in the Marine Biology Institute Lecture Hall, Room 504 of the Science and Technology Building.

Titled “Producing High-Value Bioactive Substances with Engineered Escherichia coli,” the lecture will cover research in microbial metabolic engineering and synthetic biology, including the production of gluconate, glucose oxidase, vitamin B12, lycopene, coenzyme Q10 and ornithine. Professor Liu has published extensively in the field and received national and provincial or ministerial science and technology awards.
