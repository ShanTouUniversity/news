STU’s Student Affairs Office opened registration for supplies for the 2014 student military training. The training is scheduled for the two weeks after the spring semester ends; specific dates and plans will be announced separately.

Students taking part must use the uniform and shoe size chart to register their sizes at `http://gdc1.stu.edu.cn/junxun` by 6 p.m. on June 12, and accurately enter their school or college affiliation. Each supply set includes a cap, camouflage uniform, webbing belt and military shoes. The set costs approximately CNY 50–60, half of which will be subsidized by the university. Students who register must preload enough funds in their campus card account by June 15 to cover their share.

The size chart and further training arrangements should be confirmed in the OA attachment and subsequent notices.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
>
> ⚠️ The military training size chart and related attachments are available on the OA system.
