Following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security, STU may recommend one candidate for the national outstanding technical talent program or one unit for the national advanced technical talent collective program. The Human Resources Office is inviting eligible individuals or collectives from university units.

Units must submit recommendation materials to the Human Resources Office in Room 315 of the Administration Building by June 9. Written materials include a recommendation report, the relevant forms and an achievements statement. Electronic materials must be generated with the designated information-management software and include one headshot and two work photographs; they should be sent to `jbfan@stu.edu.cn`. Eligibility, forms and software instructions are provided in the OA attachments. The contact telephone number is 86502349.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
>
> ⚠️ Recommendation criteria, forms and information-collection software are available on the OA system.
