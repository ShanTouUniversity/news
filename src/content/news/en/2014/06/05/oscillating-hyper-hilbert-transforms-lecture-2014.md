The School of Science and Technology will host a lecture in its Modern Mathematics series at 4:40 p.m. on June 6, 2014, in the Mathematics Laboratory (Gongbei 412). Chen Jiecheng, a professor and doctoral supervisor at Zhejiang Normal University, will present “On Oscillating Hyper Hilbert Transforms along Curves.”

The lecture will introduce the research background of oscillating hyper Hilbert transforms along curves, related boundedness conditions, and the main ideas behind the proofs. Chen’s research focuses on harmonic analysis and its applications.
