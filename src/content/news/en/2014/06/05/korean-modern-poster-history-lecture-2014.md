Professor Baek Geumnam will give a lecture titled “70 Years of Change in Modern Korean Posters” at 7:30 p.m. on June 6, 2014. The event will take place in the first-floor lecture theater of STU’s Cheung Kong School of Art and Design and is open to students and staff across the university.
