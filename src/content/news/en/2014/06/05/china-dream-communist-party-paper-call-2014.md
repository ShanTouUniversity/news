The National Association of Party Building Research planned to hold a symposium in Qinghai in August 2014 on “The Chinese Dream and the Communist Party of China: Studying General Secretary Xi Jinping’s Series of Important Speeches.” STU’s Party Organization and United Front Office is inviting submissions from party-member faculty and students across the university.

Units should organize eligible authors to prepare papers in line with the call requirements and submit them to the STU Party Organization and United Front Office by June 18. Electronic materials may be sent to `o_zzb@stu.edu.cn`. The topic, format and other submission requirements are set out in the OA attachment.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Party Organization and United Front Office)
>
> ⚠️ The detailed call-for-papers requirements and attachment are available on the OA system.
