STU announced the evaluated results for contractors for three renovation projects. The public notice period runs from June 5 through June 9, 2014.

| Project | Winning contractor | Winning bid |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 2014 teaching-area public restroom renovation | Shantou Dahao Construction General Company | CNY 321,733.91 |
| Kindergarten restroom renovation | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. | CNY 168,000 |
| Campus drainage-system renovation | Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. | CNY 238,581.53 |

The tendering entity is the STU Infrastructure Office, reachable at 0754-86502490. Complaints may be directed to the STU Supervision and Audit Office at 0754-86502346.
