The “Meeting Blue Skies” creative study service course at STU’s School of Law received more registrations than available places. The course management team will use group interviews to select participants. Interviews are scheduled for June 7, 2014, from 1:30 to 5:30 p.m. Candidates should wait in E-406; group discussions will take place in E-402, E-409 and E-405.

Each group will include six students, with approximately 15 minutes for each interview. The process will include a group discussion and focus on communication, teamwork, responsibility and relevant experience or skills. Participants may bring proof of skills or work samples. Exact interview time slots are provided in the OA attachment, and candidates should arrive early to prepare.
