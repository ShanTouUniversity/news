The Christian Studies Center at STU’s School of Liberal Arts is recruiting four student assistants for administrative, design and publicity, and equipment-support roles. Duties include supporting student activities, producing posters and presentations, photography, maintaining office equipment and assisting with equipment for academic events. Each role requires at least three fixed hours of weekly duty.

Applicants should be responsible and proactive, with skills in Chinese writing, design and production, photography or office-equipment support as appropriate to the role. Applicants must email an electronic application form and class schedule to `ytao@stu.edu.cn` by June 20, 2014. Shortlisted applicants will be contacted for interviews; design applicants may bring relevant work samples.

The contact person is Ms. Tao, at 0754-86503655. The application form and detailed requirements should be confirmed in the OA attachment.
