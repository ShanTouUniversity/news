The STU Physical Education Department will arrange the next stage of training for students who registered for the swimming lifeguard training course. Registered participants must meet at 9 a.m. on Saturday, June 7, 2014, in the open area behind the Gymnasium, at the junction between the Gymnasium and the swimming pool.

Training arrangements and the participant list should be confirmed in the OA attachment and on-site instructions. Participants should arrive on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Physical Education Department)
>
> ⚠️ The training arrangements and related attachment are available on the OA system.
