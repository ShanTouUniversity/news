STU’s Student Affairs Office publicized the award list for 2014 main-campus undergraduate graduates recognized for proactive employment. The complete list is provided in the OA attachment; this report does not reproduce students’ personal information.

Organizations or individuals with comments may submit written feedback to the Student Affairs Office’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center by 5 p.m. on June 11, 2014, or respond in person or by telephone. Organizations must provide an official seal, while individuals must sign their real names. The contact telephone number is 0754-86502357.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
