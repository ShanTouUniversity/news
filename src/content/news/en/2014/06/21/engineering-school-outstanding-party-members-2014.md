The Party committee of Shantou University’s Engineering School has announced its candidates for the 2013–2014 Outstanding Party Member honor. Following reviews by student Party branches and the school’s Party committee, 24 individuals are proposed for the recognition.

The public notice period ends at 5 p.m. on June 26. Anyone with an objection may submit a written statement, visit the office, or call the Engineering School Party committee. Reports must include the reporter’s real name. Shen Yude is the contact person and can be reached at 86502986.
