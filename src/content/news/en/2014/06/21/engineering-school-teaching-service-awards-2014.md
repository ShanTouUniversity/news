Shantou University’s Engineering School has announced its candidates for the 2013–2014 teaching and administrative service awards. Following nominations by departments and centers, recommendations by the school’s executive committee, and review by its academic committee, Wang Yingzi of the Civil Engineering Department is proposed for the Outstanding Teaching Award, Lin Yiwen of the Electronic Engineering Department for the Best Teaching Service Award, and Ni Xiaodan of the school administrative office for the Best Administrative Service Award.

Objections may be submitted in writing, in person, or by telephone to the Engineering School Office before the end of working hours on June 25. Deng Yong is the contact person and can be reached at 86502153.
