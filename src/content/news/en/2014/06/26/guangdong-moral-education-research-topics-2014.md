Shantou University’s Research Office circulated a call on June 26 for proposed topics for Guangdong’s 2014 school moral education research guide. The higher-education section covered nine areas: collaborative innovation in ideological and political theory courses; practice-based education; students’ everyday ideological and political education; mental health; professional development for counselors; ideology and online public opinion; employment and entrepreneurship guidance; student financial aid; and campus safety and stability.

Proposals were expected to reflect the goal of fostering virtue, address clearly defined issues and demonstrate academic rigor, foresight and practical value. Each submission needed an approximately 300-word statement explaining its value, research focus and challenges, and expected outcomes. Units were asked to organize expert reviews and email topic titles and statements to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn by July 28.
