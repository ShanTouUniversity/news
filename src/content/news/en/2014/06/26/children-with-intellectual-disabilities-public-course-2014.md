Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office announced the schedule for its summer 2014 public-interest course, “Supporting Children with Intellectual Disabilities.” The course was set for Monday, July 14, in Room E201-1, with sessions from 9 a.m. to noon and 2 p.m. to 6 p.m. Enrolled students could attend according to the schedule.
