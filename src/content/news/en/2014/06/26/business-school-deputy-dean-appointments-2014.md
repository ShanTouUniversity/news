Shantou University’s Human Resources Office issued a pre-appointment notice on June 26. Following discussion at the university leadership’s 10th meeting on June 24, the university proposed appointing Bu Xiangzhi and Song Xiaobao as associate deans of the Business School.

The notice was issued under the university’s Measures for Appointing Heads of Academic Units (汕大党字〔2014〕7号). Comments were due by July 2. Units and individuals could submit written comments to Human Resources; submissions from units were required to bear an official seal, while individuals were asked to sign their real names.
