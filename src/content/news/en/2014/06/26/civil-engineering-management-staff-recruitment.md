Shantou University’s Construction Office, part of the Resource Management Office, opened recruitment for one civil engineering management engineer and one construction supervisor. Both roles required a relevant civil engineering background, some project or site experience, familiarity with CAD and standard office software, and strong coordination and teamwork skills. The engineer position generally required a college diploma or higher and an intermediate-level construction engineering title; the supervisor position required a college diploma or higher and a relevant professional title, work certificate or practice qualification.

The engineer would support project preparation, tendering, construction quality and schedule oversight, quantity review and acceptance. The supervisor would assist with renovation projects and technical management at construction sites. The positions were auxiliary contract roles; employment relationships would not transfer to the university, and compensation would follow university standards.

Applicants were asked to submit a CV and copies of identification, education, training and professional qualification documents by July 10, 2014, by post to the Construction Office or by email to o_jjb@stu.edu.cn. Shortlisted applicants would be contacted for interviews. Phone: +86 754 8650 3216 or +86 754 8650 2490.
