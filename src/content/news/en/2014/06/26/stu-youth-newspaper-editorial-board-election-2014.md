The election meeting for the 22nd editorial board of the STU Youth newspaper was held on the afternoon of June 11, 2014. Participants heard and approved the 21st board’s work report and elected the new editorial board by ballot.

The new board has 10 student members. Yang ZY was named editor-in-chief, with Luo MT and Qiu M as deputy editors-in-chief. The other student members are not named here to protect their privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League of Shantou University and STU Youth newspaper editorial office)
