Shantou University began its internal recommendation process in June 2014 for the national honors of Outstanding Teacher and Outstanding Education Worker. Under the relevant higher-level requirements, the university may recommend one candidate for each honor, and each unit is expected to publicize the criteria, conduct an internal review and submit its nominees.

A review panel made up of representatives from teaching units and relevant administrative offices will select the university’s nominees from the recommendations. Units must submit the approval form, candidate list and a statement of achievements of no more than 2,000 Chinese characters to the Personnel Office at Administrative Center Room 315 by July 2. Electronic copies must also be sent to the designated email address.

The detailed eligibility requirements and document formats are set out in the related attachments and higher-level documents. This report does not reproduce individual application materials.
