It is reported that the Infrastructure Office issued a construction notice on June 27, 2014, regarding renovation of aluminum alloy windows in the stairwells of Buildings 4, 8 and 10 in the teaching area.

## Project Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Remove existing exterior steel windows and replace with aluminum alloy windows |
| Contractor | Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Winning bid | 163,600 yuan |
| Schedule | July 1 – August 29, 2014 |
| Impact | Noise, vibration and dust are expected during construction |

For safety, pedestrians are advised to take care and faculty working nearby should avoid lingering at the site.
