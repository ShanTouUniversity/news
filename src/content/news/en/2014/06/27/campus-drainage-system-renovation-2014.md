Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will renovate the drainage system along the south side of the main campus road, between the gymnasium and the artificial-hill area by the lake. Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. is the contractor. Work is scheduled for July 1 to August 29, 2014, with a winning bid of RMB 238,600.

The project mainly involves relaying DN400 high-density polyethylene double-wall corrugated pipes and building sewage inspection wells. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction, and people passing the site are asked to take care and keep away from the work area where possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
