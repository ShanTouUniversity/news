The People’s Armed Forces Department and Student Affairs Office of Shantou University announced the arrangements for the university’s 2014 student military training. The training will take place on campus from June 30 to July 13. Military skills training is scheduled daily from 8:00 to 10:30 a.m. and from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m.; military theory classes will be held in classrooms according to the course schedule.

The opening assembly is scheduled for 8:30 a.m. on June 30 in the auditorium, while the closing assembly is scheduled for 4:00 p.m. on July 13 at the athletics field. Military lectures will also be arranged during the training period. Uniforms and related supplies will be collected by college on June 29 from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. at Administrative Center Room 105. Each set costs RMB 54, half of which is subsidized by the university, leaving students to pay RMB 27.

Detailed schedules, training locations, seating arrangements and special-event plans are provided in the OA attachments. This report does not reproduce personal information or lists from the attachments.
