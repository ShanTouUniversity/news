It is reported that, with approval from the relevant authorities, the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University will renovate and upgrade the west side of the Block Y graduate dormitory, contracting Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. for the construction work.

The project covers the complete demolition and reconstruction of bathrooms, repainting of walls and ceilings, and replacement of all water supply and drainage pipes, covering a floor area of 3,023 square meters. The winning bid price is 1.2957 million yuan.

**Project Overview**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Project Name | Block Y Graduate Dormitory Renovation Project (Phase I) |
| Scope | West side of Block Y graduate dormitory |
| Contractor | Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. |
| Floor Area | 3,023 square meters |
| Main Works | Full reconstruction of bathrooms; wall and ceiling repainting; replacement of all plumbing |
| Contract Price | 1.2957 million yuan |
| Schedule | Construction from July 1, 2014; completion before September 13 |

The university noted that noise, vibration and dust are expected during construction and asked for understanding. For safety reasons, passers-by should stay alert, and teachers and students living in the building are advised to avoid lingering near the site.

On-site managers are Lu Chunxiong and Cai Weizhong (tel: 13902723275, 13802717206), with site engineers Lin Longsheng and Chen Zidan (tel: 13058295997, 13502776861).
