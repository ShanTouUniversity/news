The “Love in Ya’an” public-service course at Shantou University held interviews on June 24, 25 and 27, 2014. Following self-introductions, a leaderless group discussion and a question-and-answer session, the course team selected 15 formal members and four alternates.

The selection considered applicants’ performance during the interview, registration information, academic year and major, as well as the gender balance of the group. Alternates would replace formal members who withdrew. Because the original notice’s attachment contains the student list, this report does not reproduce personal names or student identification numbers.
