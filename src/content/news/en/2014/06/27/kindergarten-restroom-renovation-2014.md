Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will renovate the restroom at the university kindergarten. Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. is the contractor. Work is scheduled to start on July 1, 2014, and finish by August 29, with a winning bid of RMB 168,000.

The renovation includes a complete interior rebuild, ceiling and wall-and-floor work, partitions, washbasins, a solid-wood door and electrical and plumbing work. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction. People in the area are asked to take care and avoid staying near the work site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
