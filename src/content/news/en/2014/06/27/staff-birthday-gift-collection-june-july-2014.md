The Shantou University labor union announced that birthday benefits for June and July are ready for collection. Each departmental union should send a representative to the university labor union to collect them.

The notice does not publish specific collection hours or other personal information; departments should follow the labor union’s arrangements.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Labor Union)
