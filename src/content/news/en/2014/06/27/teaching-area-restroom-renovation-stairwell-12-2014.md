Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will renovate the public restroom at Stairwell 12 in the teaching area. Shantou Dahao Construction General Company is the contractor. Work is scheduled to start on July 1, 2014, and finish by August 29, with a winning bid of RMB 321,700.

The project includes a complete interior rebuild, ceiling and wall-and-floor work, partitions, washbasins, doors and electrical and plumbing work. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction. People passing the site are asked to take care and avoid staying near the work area.
