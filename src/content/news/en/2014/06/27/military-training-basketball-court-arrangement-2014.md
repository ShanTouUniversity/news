The People’s Armed Forces Department of Shantou University announced that the 2014 student military training period will run from June 30 to July 13. Military skills training will take place daily from 8:00 to 10:30 a.m. and from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m.

Some basketball courts will be used as military-training venues during that period. Students and staff are asked to arrange other forms of exercise during the training hours and to avoid courts where training is in progress.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
