Shantou University’s 2014 Xinhuo Xiangchuan rural summer service camp selected 58 student volunteers. Of these, 26 were assigned to the Baiyun Primary School service site in Baiyun Village, Chao’an County, Chaozhou, and 32 to the Lintian Primary School site in Baita Town, Jieyang.

The selection was organized by the university’s Youth League Committee, the Shantou University Youth Volunteer Association, and the “Fireflies” charity action team. The individual names in the original attachment were not available in the source material reviewed for this report and are therefore not reproduced.
