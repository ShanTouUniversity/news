In June 2014, Shantou University’s Finance Office announced a system upgrade to support the new accounting rules for higher education. The accounting section planned to suspend reimbursement services from June 18 through June 25, with the upgrade scheduled to begin on June 18.

During the suspension, university units with urgent funding needs could apply for advances under the arrangements then in place. Graduating students were advised to submit reimbursement claims before June 18. The office said the upgrade involved adjustments to first-half accounting vouchers and initialization of the financial records.
