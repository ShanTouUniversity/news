Shantou University’s Science Association scheduled its 14th executive committee election for 7:30 p.m. on June 13, 2014, in Seminar Room 30A of the Library. The association is guided by the university’s Youth League committee and organizes student participation in extracurricular science and technology activities, including the “Challenge Cup.”

The meeting was to review the 13th committee’s work and elect a new committee of nine members, including one chair and two vice chairs. Candidates were to be recruited publicly, assessed in interviews, and reviewed by the Youth League committee before giving speeches at the election. Members of the 13th committee were to vote.
