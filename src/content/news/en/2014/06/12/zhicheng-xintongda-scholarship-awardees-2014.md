Shantou University’s Zhicheng College announced the recipients of its 2014 Xintongda Scholarship. The college’s review committee met on June 5 and selected four students.

The recipients are Li JX, Liu GZ, Zhang JF, and Zhou RX. Under the previously published award criteria, the scholarship recognizes residents who exemplify the college’s guiding principles.
