The Shantou University Overseas Examinations Center will accept registration for the second half of the 2014 BEC Business English examinations from September 1 to 17, with an additional weekend registration session on September 13. Registration will take place at the examination office in Room 504 on the fifth floor of Building E. Applicants may choose BEC Preliminary, Vantage or Higher, with no restrictions based on age, occupation or educational background.

The registration fees were RMB 395 for Preliminary, RMB 500 for Vantage and RMB 630 for Higher. The written tests were scheduled for November 22 for Preliminary, November 29 for Vantage and November 15 for Higher. Speaking tests would be arranged on the written-test day or the following day, and all exams would be held on the fifth floor of Building E at STU.

Applicants had to bring an identity card, a one-inch photograph and a bank cash-deposit slip. Detailed registration procedures, payment information and test-center contacts were provided in the original OA notice.
