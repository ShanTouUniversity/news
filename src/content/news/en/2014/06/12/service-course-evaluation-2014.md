It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University announced on June 12 that it will organize students to evaluate the teaching quality of service courses offered in the 2013-2014 spring semester through the university's credit system platform. The move aims to keep track of how these courses are taught and to drive continuous improvement in course quality.

## Evaluation Schedule

| Item | Details |
| ---- | ------- |
| Courses Covered | Service courses of the 2013-2014 spring semester offered by teaching units on the main campus |
| Evaluation Period | June 12 to June 27, 2014 |
| How to Evaluate | Log in to the credit system → Course Evaluation → view pending service courses |

According to the arrangement, students should log in to the credit system during the period, enter the "Course Evaluation" section to find the service courses pending review, and complete evaluations for each course they took this semester.

## Deadline Matters

The office reminded students to finish all evaluations within the specified time frame. Those who fail to do so must submit a written explanation; otherwise, they will not be able to view their service course grades.
