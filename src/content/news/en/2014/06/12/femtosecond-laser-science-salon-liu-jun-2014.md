Shantou University’s College of Science scheduled a science salon for 10:15 a.m. on June 16, 2014. Liu Jun, a researcher at the Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, was set to speak in the college’s lecture hall, Room 352.

The talk was to cover femtosecond laser technology and its applications, including multicolor femtosecond lasers generated through four-wave mixing, pulse-measurement methods, and deep-ultraviolet femtosecond pulses used in ultrafast dynamics research. Liu’s work focuses on ultrashort-pulse laser technology and applications.
