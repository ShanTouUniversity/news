Shantou University’s Zhicheng College named seven residents outstanding peer mentors for the 2013–14 academic year. The review considered work reports, presentations, self-assessments, and feedback from fellow residents.

The recipients had contributed to community life in the college over the past year. Their names are not reproduced to protect student privacy.
