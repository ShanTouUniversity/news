Ahead of the June 27 commencement, Shantou University scheduled academic gown collection by college from 2:30 to 5:30 p.m. on June 19 at the auditorium foyer. The collection slots were 2:30–3:30 p.m. for the School of Journalism, School of Arts, and Graduate School; 3:30–4:30 p.m. for the School of Liberal Arts, Business School, and School of Law; and 4:30–5:30 p.m. for the School of Engineering, School of Science, and Continuing Education College.

Colleges were to collect the gowns together and distribute them to their departments. Gowns were due back at the same foyer from 2 to 5 p.m. on June 27. The notice stated that damaged or overdue items would be charged at their original price. College-specific quantities and collection lists were provided in the original attachments.
