Shantou University’s Continuing Education College launched its redesigned website on June 12, 2014, at sce.stu.edu.cn. The college said the new site had been prepared and tested with technical support from the university’s Network Center.

The former site, cjxy.stu.edu.cn, was to remain available temporarily but would no longer be updated. Students and staff could consult the new site for college information.
