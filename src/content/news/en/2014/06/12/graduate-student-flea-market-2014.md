Shantou University’s second graduate student flea market was scheduled to run each afternoon from June 18 to 23, 2014, on the campus pedestrian street. The market was open to current graduate students and organized by the Graduate School Youth League, with the Graduate Student Volunteer Group as the organizer and class Youth League branches as supporting partners. Its theme was “Mutual Benefit and a Civilized Departure.”

Organizers required stalls to remain within the curb and not obstruct the road. Further details were available in the original OA notice and its attachments.
