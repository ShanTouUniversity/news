Guangdong’s Zhan Chi Plan offered university students summer internships lasting from two weeks to two months. The initiative was launched by the provincial Communist Youth League and student federation together with the provincial human resources and social security and education authorities, among other agencies. Shantou University’s Youth League committee issued guidance on participation in June 2014.

Applicants had to complete their profiles on the program website and obtain university verification before applying for positions. Placement took place through employer selection or public-interest assignment. Students, employers, and the relevant reviewing bodies were required to complete the agreement process online after a match. The university also advised applicants to verify employers and partner relationships, follow internship agreements, and prioritize personal safety.

Position listings and application requirements were available through the program platform and the original OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Shantou University Youth League Committee)
