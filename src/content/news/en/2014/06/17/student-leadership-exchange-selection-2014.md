Shantou University and the University of Nottingham Ningbo China will hold a new student leadership exchange from late August to mid-September 2014. Each university will send 10 representatives for a three-week exchange, including work on new-student welcome activities and opportunities to experience student life at the host institution.

Applicants must have at least one year of experience in a relevant student organization, good academic standing, and strong English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. Candidates will be recommended by their schools or student organizations, reviewed, and interviewed. Applications are due to the applicant's unit by 10 a.m. on June 25, with unit recommendations due to the university Youth League Committee by 4 p.m. that day. Two interview rounds were tentatively scheduled for the mornings of June 27 and the afternoon of June 30; 10 representatives will be selected.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee and Student Affairs Office)
