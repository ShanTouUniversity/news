Shantou University's Youth League Committee announced on June 17, 2014, that the sharing session for the themed activity “Gathering the Positive Energy of Youth, Pursuing New Dreams in Life” had been moved from early June to early September. The change was made because students were busy with end-of-term studies. A new date and venue will be announced later.

> Source: STU OA Notice (STU Youth League Committee)
