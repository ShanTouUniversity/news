Shantou University's Office of Resource Management and Student Affairs Office issued a campus snake safety advisory. Snakes are more likely to be active in dense vegetation during hot, humid weather and around thunderstorms or after prolonged rain. The offices advised students and staff to avoid isolated, overgrown areas, especially at night.

Anyone who encounters a snake on campus should not disturb it or shake the surrounding ground or plants. If bitten, remain calm, rest, call for help, and note the snake's features for medical staff. Campus Security is available around the clock at 82904110 and 86504110. The university clinic's emergency numbers are 82902651 and 86502651. The Chaozhou Medical Research Institute clinic also provides antivenom treatment at 0768-2297007.
