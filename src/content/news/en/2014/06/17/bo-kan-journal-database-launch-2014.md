Shantou University Library opened access to Bokannet's humanities and social sciences journal database in June 2014. The platform hosts Chinese periodicals in page-image, digital, audio, and downloadable formats, and also provides access to back issues.

Users can find access information through the database page on the library website. The mobile app trial runs from June 17 through December 31, 2014. Shared account credentials included in the original notice are not reproduced here.
