It is reported that the Sports Department and the Resource Management Office jointly issued a notice on June 17, 2014, that the university swimming pool will open to all faculty and students starting June 18.

## Opening Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Daily hours | 16:30–18:30 |
| Start date | June 18, 2014 |
| Management | Campus card swipe, 5 yuan per visit |
| Daily limits | Staff card: up to 3 visits per day; student card: up to 2 visits per day; temporary card: up to 1 visit per day |
| Issued by | Sports Department, Resource Management Office |

Safety reminder: Swimmers should observe pool rules and not enter outside opening hours. Vehicles should be parked as directed by security personnel and not obstruct campus traffic.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Sports Department, Resource Management Office)
