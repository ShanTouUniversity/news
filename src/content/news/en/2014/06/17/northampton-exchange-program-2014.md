Shantou University and the University of Northampton will continue undergraduate exchange and 3+1+1 dual-degree and undergraduate-to-master's pathways in 2014. Based on credit recognition, the programs plan to admit 20 degree students and 30 exchange students, with study beginning in September.

The 3+1+1 pathway is for undergraduates who have completed three years at STU and meet the relevant language requirements. Students may complete the remaining undergraduate year at Northampton and continue to a master's program if eligible. The exchange program is open to STU sophomores, who may apply for courses across several Northampton faculties. Applicants must email an application form, transcripts, proof of enrollment, a list of outstanding courses, and proof of English proficiency to STU's Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at `wscicd@stu.edu.cn` by June 28. Further application and fee details are available in the university notice.
