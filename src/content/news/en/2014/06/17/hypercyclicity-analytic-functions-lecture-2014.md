Shantou University's School of Science will host a lecture in its “Toward Modern Mathematics” series at 4:30 p.m. on June 17, 2014. The speaker is Kit C. Chan, a professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Bowling Green State University. The talk, “Hypercyclicity of Operators on Spaces of Analytic Functions,” will take place in the Mathematics Laboratory, Room 412 of the Engineering North Building.

The lecture will introduce hypercyclicity for bounded linear operators on Banach spaces and discuss examples involving spaces of analytic functions, including operators that provide counterexamples in the theory. Chan earned his PhD in mathematics from the University of Michigan in 1988 and has been a professor at Bowling Green State University since 2002.
