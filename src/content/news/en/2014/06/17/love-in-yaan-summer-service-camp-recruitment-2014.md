Shantou University's Love in Ya'an service camp in Sichuan is recruiting 15 undergraduates for a program in Shangli, Yucheng District, Ya'an, from August 7 to 20, 2014. Activities are planned in disaster-risk reduction education, life education, and psychosocial support to help residents strengthen disaster preparedness and address post-earthquake risks and psychological needs.

Applicants must not already be enrolled in a 2014-15 summer public-service course and should be willing to contribute to community service, work with others, and adapt to challenging conditions. Applications are due by 4 p.m. on June 24, with interviews expected on June 25. The application form and full course schedule are available in the OA attachments.
