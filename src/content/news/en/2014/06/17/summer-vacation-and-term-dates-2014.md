Shantou University's Party and Administrative Office announced the 2014 summer break and academic schedule on June 17. Current students begin summer break on June 29, register on August 24, and start the summer term on August 25. The Student Affairs Office will arrange military training for the 2013 undergraduate cohort separately. New students report on September 7; students register for the fall term on September 21, with classes beginning September 22.

Faculty begin their break on July 2 and return on August 22 to prepare for the new term. Students wishing to stay on campus during the break must apply through their residential college's student adviser office, with arrangements coordinated by the relevant offices. Administrative, teaching support, and logistics staff will continue providing services. Units may arrange staff rotation from July 2 through August 20; summer office hours are 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 3–5 p.m. Normal hours resume on August 21. Units were asked to submit summer staffing schedules to the Party and Administrative Office by the end of the day on June 27.
