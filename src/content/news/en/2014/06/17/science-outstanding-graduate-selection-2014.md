It is reported that the College of Science has launched its selection for outstanding graduates among the Class of 2014, aiming to recognize well-rounded students and foster a positive academic culture.

## Eligibility

Applicants must meet all of the following criteria:

| Area | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Conduct | Love for country and university, respect for teachers, unity with peers, no legal or disciplinary violations |
| Academic standing | Well-rounded development, strong grades, no retaken courses |
| Engagement | Active participation in collective activities, including volunteer or public-welfare service |
| Scholarships | Academic Excellence Scholarship at third prize or above for two years, plus at least one university special scholarship |
| Thesis | Excellent graduation thesis grade |
| Graduation conduct | Active cooperation with university employment and graduation procedures, orderly departure |

## Selection Process

1. **Application**: Candidates complete the *STU College of Science Outstanding Graduate Application Form* and submit it to the College Youth League Committee by 12:00 on Friday, June 20, 2014;
2. **Review**: A college selection committee evaluates all applicants and determines the awardees;
3. **Recognition**: The college confers the title of "Outstanding Graduate" and presents certificates and monetary awards.

Interested graduates should submit materials before the deadline; late submissions will not be accepted.
