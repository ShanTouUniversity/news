Shantou University’s Personnel Office circulated a Guangdong Provincial Department of Education notice on recommending candidates for the 2013 and 2014 Yangtze River Scholars Program and opened the university’s internal recommendation process.

Relevant university units were asked to organize applications from qualified high-level talents and review the application materials. Candidate recommendation forms must be completed, edited and checked using the designated application software. Detailed software, discipline-grouping and materials requirements are provided in the attachments.

Candidates must email recommendation forms and supporting materials to o_rsk@stu.edu.cn by July 2. By July 7, they must submit three printed copies of the recommendation form and two bound sets of supporting materials to the Personnel Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
> ⚠️ The application software, discipline groupings and recommendation-material requirements are available in the attachments on the OA system.
