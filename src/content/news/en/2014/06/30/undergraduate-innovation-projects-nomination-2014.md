Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office announced the results of its selection for the 2014 Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program. Following project review, the university proposed 79 projects for university-level designation, while recommending 75 projects for provincial-level designation and 24 for national-level designation.

The full list of proposed projects and project titles appears in the attachments. The public-comment period runs from June 30 to July 3, 2014. The notice lists 86502736 as the contact telephone number for feedback.
