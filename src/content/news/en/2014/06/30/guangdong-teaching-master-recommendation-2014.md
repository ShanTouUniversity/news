Following a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, Shantou University began its internal recommendation process for the seventh Guangdong Higher Education Teaching Master Award. The university may recommend one candidate.

Each teaching unit is to publicize the selection requirements, organize recommendations and applications, and assess candidates with reference to the award evaluation criteria. Each unit may generally recommend no more than one candidate. A university review group will select the final university candidate from the unit recommendations.

Units must submit the candidate summary form, recommendation form and supporting evidence of teaching ability and achievements to Room 315 of the Administrative Center by July 3. Electronic copies must also be sent to wing@stu.edu.cn. Printed materials must be printed double-sided and bound as required.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
> ⚠️ The selection scope, criteria, evaluation framework and recommendation forms are available in the attachments on the OA system.
