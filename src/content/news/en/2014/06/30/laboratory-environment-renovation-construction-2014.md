Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will carry out environmental renovations in several teaching-area laboratories. Shantou Dahao Construction General Company is the contractor. Work is scheduled to start on July 1, 2014, and finish by November 1, with a winning bid of RMB 1.6 million.

The work covers the physics, biology and chemistry departments in the Science Building; the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Department in the Technology Building; the Electronics Department and the structural laboratory in the Engineering Building; and the Cheung Kong School of Art and Design. The project includes ceiling and door replacement, wall and floor work, painting, laboratory-bench installation and partial electrical work.

Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction. People working near the sites are asked to take care and avoid staying near the work areas.
