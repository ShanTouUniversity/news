The China Law Society opened applications for its 2014 ministerial-level legal research projects, and Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the application arrangements to relevant units. Project categories include major, key, general, youth and self-selected projects, with funding determined by category. Applicants must meet the corresponding research and eligibility requirements.

Major and key projects are generally to be completed within one year of approval; general, youth and self-selected projects are also generally expected to be completed within one year. Each project leader may submit only one application, and applications must meet requirements for qualifications, research foundations and deliverables.

Applicants must email an electronic application to yczhuo@stu.edu.cn for formal review by July 28 and submit nine printed copies of the final application to the Research Office by July 30. The project guide, management measures and application templates are provided in the attachments.
