It is reported that the Office of International Affairs of Shantou University has issued a disciplinary decision imposing a university-wide reprimand on the Business School Enactus team.

## Background

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Unit | Business School Enactus Team |
| Travel dates | April 21–23, 2014 |
| Number of students | 28 |
| Purpose | To attend the South China Regional of the 2014 Enactus World Cup China Station in Hong Kong |
| Violation | Traveling to Hong Kong without completing the university's foreign-affairs approval procedures |

The university noted that all official overseas travel, including to Hong Kong, must be applied for and approved in advance, and the team's self-arranged trip violated the relevant regulations.

## Decision and Reminder

After deliberation, the university decided to circulate a university-wide reprimand against the team. The Office of International Affairs stressed that official overseas travel is highly political, policy-oriented and principled, and urged all faculty and students to take this as a warning and strictly comply with the relevant rules.
