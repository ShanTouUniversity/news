It is reported that the Regulation of Shantou University on the Management of Undergraduate Student Internships was officially released for implementation on June 30 (STU Fa [2014] No. 80), providing a dedicated institutional framework for the internship work of undergraduate students at Shantou University.

The regulation went through a complete review process: it was first examined by the university Teaching Committee and then approved at the 10th meeting of university leadership held on June 24, 2014. As a university-level regulatory document issued to all units on campus, it will serve as the unified basis for organizing, managing and evaluating undergraduate internship programs.

The full text of the regulation was published as an attachment to the document, and faculty and students may consult its specific provisions through the OA system.
