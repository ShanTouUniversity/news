Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will repair the public classrooms on Floors 3 and 4 of G Building. Shantou Yueli Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. is the contractor. Work is scheduled to start on July 1, 2014, and finish by September 10, with a winning bid of RMB 410,900.

The project includes replacing wooden doors and roof insulation tiles, painting walls and ceilings, installing rubber-sheet flooring in some areas, replacing curtain fabric and carrying out partial electrical work. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction. People passing the site are asked to take care and keep away from the work area where possible.
