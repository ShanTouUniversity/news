Shantou University designated each summer semester as “Teaching Development Month” to strengthen the central role of teaching, improve educational target management, promote excellence in education and build a strong teaching culture. The decision was discussed and approved at the 10th university leadership meeting on June 24, 2014, and issued on June 30.

Activities will include the annual teaching work conference, teaching awards, lectures on educational concepts and emerging developments, teaching exchanges and training. Formats will include university-wide meetings, training organized by the Faculty Development and Educational Assessment Center, exchange workshops organized by the university, and workshops and seminars held by individual schools and departments.

The Academic Affairs Office and the Faculty Development and Educational Assessment Center will jointly coordinate the program.
