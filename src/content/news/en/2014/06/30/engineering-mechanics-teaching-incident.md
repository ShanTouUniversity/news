A routine teaching inspection on May 4 found that the instructor of Engineering Mechanics I, offered by the Civil Engineering Division of the School of Engineering in Room E408, was absent during class. The course had class number 68648, and the instructor had not completed the required class-adjustment procedure.

Under the relevant provisions of Shantou University’s provisional measures for identifying undergraduate teaching incidents, the Academic Affairs Office classified the matter as a general teaching incident and identified the instructor as responsible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
