New graduate students entering Shantou University in 2014 will register on September 14, and orientation work will continue through September 21. The Graduate School will coordinate reception and orientation activities with the university’s arrangements.

On September 14, reception stations will operate at the exits of Shantou Railway Station, including the coach terminal, Chaoshan High-Speed Railway Station and Shantou Coach Station. Information and guidance points will also be set up at the university motto monument and graduate residences.

The Graduate School will operate a registration point in the open ground-floor area of the Administrative Center from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. on September 14. New students should bring their admission letters, identity documents, files and household registration transfer certificates, then collect a housing form and complete check-in at the graduate residence duty office. Each training unit will also arrange meetings with supervisors, program introductions and laboratory visits.
