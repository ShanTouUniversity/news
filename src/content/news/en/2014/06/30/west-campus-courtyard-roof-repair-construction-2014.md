Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will repair the roofs of Fuxiangyuan and Huichunyuan in the West Campus Courtyard. Guangdong Yusheng Construction Co., Ltd. is the contractor. Work is scheduled for July 1 to August 14, 2014, with a winning bid of approximately RMB 225,500.

The work includes removing glazed roof tiles, repairing leaks and cracks in reinforced-concrete sloped slabs, applying waterproof mortar and painting the exterior surface. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction, and people passing the site are asked to take care and keep away from the work area where possible.
