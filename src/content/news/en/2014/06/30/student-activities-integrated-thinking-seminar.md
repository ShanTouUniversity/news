Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold the fifth seminar on integrated thinking in student activities from 10 a.m. to noon on July 1 in Room 330 of the Administrative Center. Relevant project leaders will attend, and the office asked participants to make work arrangements and arrive on time.
