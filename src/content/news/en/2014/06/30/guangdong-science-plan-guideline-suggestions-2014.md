The Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology opened a call for suggestions for the 2014 provincial science and technology plan application guidelines. Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the arrangements to relevant university units.

Suggestions should support national and provincial science and technology plans, emphasize basic, strategic, frontier and public-interest research as well as common technology development, and balance advancement, practical value and ecological sustainability. Units may submit suggestions related to industrial development, public services and social livelihood technologies.

Suggestions must be submitted through the Guangdong Science and Technology Business Integrated Management System by selecting “Application Management,” “Guideline Suggestion Management” and “Submit Guideline Suggestion.” Online submissions close at 4 p.m. on July 3. Faculty members without system accounts may contact the Research Office. Contacts are Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng at 82902813.
