The second round of applications for Shantou University’s Outstanding Undergraduate International Exchange Program will open on September 20. The 10 students who received funding from the China Scholarship Council in the first round have entered the overseas visa process.

Applicants in the second round are generally expected to have an average course score of at least 85 and an IELTS score of at least 6.5, as well as an invitation or admission letter from an overseas partner institution before applying. Participating institutions include the Catholic University of Portugal, EBS University of Business and Law, Victoria University of Wellington, the University of Canterbury and Dublin City University. Specific funding quotas and application arrangements are subject to the program notice.

Applicants may contact Ms. Huang at the Office of International Exchange and Cooperation at rghuang@stu.edu.cn or 86502131, or consult the international exchange coordinator at their college. Relevant colleges will help students in the 2012 and 2013 cohorts prepare for language tests and admission materials.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of International Exchange and Cooperation)
