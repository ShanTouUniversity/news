Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office will hold a graduate employment work meeting at 3 p.m. on July 1 in Room 213 of the New Administrative Center. Party secretaries or deputy secretaries of the colleges and residential colleges, employment counselors, and staff from the Student Affairs Office’s Graduate Employment Guidance Center will attend.

The meeting will coordinate work related to graduate employment, and attendees are expected to arrive on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
