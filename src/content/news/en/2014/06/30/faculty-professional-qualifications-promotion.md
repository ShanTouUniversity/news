Following review by the relevant Guangdong professional qualification committees and approval by the Guangdong Department of Human Resources and Social Security, 32 Shantou University faculty and staff members obtained professional qualifications. Twenty-six obtained senior qualifications and six obtained intermediate qualifications.

The senior qualifications cover positions including professor, associate professor, research fellow, professor-level senior engineer, first-grade performer, senior laboratory technician and senior economist. Intermediate qualifications cover lecturer, laboratory technician and third-grade performer. The qualifications were obtained in November or December 2013, depending on the individual.

The university also noted that, under the relevant rules, professional qualifications and professional appointments in Guangdong are handled separately. Holding a professional qualification does not directly determine salary or other benefits.
