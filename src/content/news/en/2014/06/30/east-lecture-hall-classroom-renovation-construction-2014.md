Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will renovate the public classrooms in the East Lecture Hall. Shantou Nanhua Construction Co., Ltd. is the contractor. Work is scheduled to start on July 1, 2014, and finish by September 20, with a winning bid of RMB 868,700.

The project includes a complete interior rebuild, removal of wooden doors and fixed seating, ceiling replacement, rubber-sheet flooring, wall painting and partial electrical work. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during construction. People passing the site are asked to take care and keep away from the work area where possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
