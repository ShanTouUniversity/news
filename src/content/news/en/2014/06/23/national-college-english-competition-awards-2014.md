The results of the 2014 National College English Competition preliminary round have been announced. A total of 164 Shantou University students entered the preliminary round, and 10 received awards: two first prizes, three second prizes, and five third prizes.

The two first-prize winners represented STU in the Guangdong final hosted by Guangdong University of Commerce. One STU graduate student went on to receive the national special prize in the 2014 competition. The Academic Affairs Office organized registration and examination arrangements, while the English Language Center collectively marked the examination papers.
