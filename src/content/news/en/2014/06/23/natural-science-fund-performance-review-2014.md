In accordance with a Guangdong provincial finance notice, Shantou University has begun performance reviews for projects supported by the Guangdong Provincial Natural Science Fund from 2011 through 2013. Projects that received funding during this period, including rolling grants, are within the review scope.

Project leaders must truthfully complete a basic information form, evaluation form, questionnaire, and self-assessment report, and provide supporting materials. A separate set of materials is required for each project. Paper and electronic submissions should follow the requirements in the notice, while supporting materials need only be submitted electronically. Please consult the original attachments for the project list, project numbers, and forms.

Electronic materials must be sent to cszhu@stu.edu.cn by June 29. Following review, the Research Office will submit three paper copies by June 30. Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng are the contacts and can be reached at 86502813.
