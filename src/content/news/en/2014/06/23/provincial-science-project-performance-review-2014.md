In accordance with a Guangdong provincial finance notice, Shantou University has begun performance reviews for provincial science-plan projects approved between 2011 and 2013. The review applies to the leaders of the relevant projects.

Each project leader must prepare a basic information form, evaluation form, questionnaire, self-assessment report, and supporting materials. The basic information form, evaluation form, self-assessment report, and questionnaire must be submitted in both paper and electronic form, while supporting materials need only be submitted electronically. Materials for each project must be bound and include a self-assessment cover.

Electronic materials must be sent to cszhu@stu.edu.cn by June 29. Following review, the Research Office will submit three paper copies by June 30. Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng are the contacts and can be reached at 86502813. Please consult the original notice attachments for the project list, forms, and detailed requirements.
