It is reported that the Office of Student Affairs issued a notice on June 23 detailing arrangements for collecting dispatch certificates (报到证) and signing deferred employment agreements for the Class of 2014 on the main campus.

Graduates who have applied for deferred employment should sign the agreement on June 24–25 at the Career Guidance Center (Reception Room 104, New Administrative Center); the list of applicants is provided in the attachment. Graduates who have not applied for deferred employment and are not pursuing further studies should collect their dispatch certificates at the same location during the same period. Those continuing to further studies (including overseas) should submit their file transfer letter or admission notice to the Center as soon as possible and sign the Application Form for Graduates of Guangdong Higher Education Institutions Not Participating in Employment Due to Further Studies at Room 106, New Administrative Center.

The notice also highlighted points to note after receiving the dispatch certificate: verify the certificate's details and contact the Career Guidance Center (Tel: 0754-86502357) if any issues arise; certificates may be collected on behalf of another person but must be forwarded promptly; report to the designated address within the specified period, with overdue consequences borne by the individual; and students who have not yet met graduation requirements (including those still taking courses this semester) should not collect the certificate, and those who have done so must return it, otherwise a replacement cannot be issued upon normal graduation. Dispatch procedures concern the destination of graduates' files and should be taken seriously.
