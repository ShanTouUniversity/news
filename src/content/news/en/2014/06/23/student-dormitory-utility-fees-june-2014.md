Shantou University’s Resource Management Office has announced the June billing schedule for student dormitory utilities. The billing period covers usage from June 1 through 22; charges incurred from June 23 onward will be collected separately.

The Water and Electricity Center will read meters on June 22, verify the data on June 24, and have the Network Center deduct the charges through the campus card system on June 25. Students can check their charges through the STU utility management system. Those who find an error or have questions may complete and sign a verification application and visit the Water and Electricity Center beside the Science and Technology Building during working hours on June 24. Students should add funds to their campus cards in time to avoid problems with graduation departure procedures.

The Water and Electricity Center can be reached at 86502489. Questions about student residence lists can be directed to the counselor’s office at 86503355. Please consult the original notice attachments and system records for the verification form and detailed charges.
