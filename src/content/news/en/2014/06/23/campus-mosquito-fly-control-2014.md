It is reported that to further improve campus sanitation, the university will carry out a mosquito and fly control operation from June 24 to 30 across the campus, including Longquanyuan and the Continuing Education College. The Assets Management Office has released a day-by-day schedule and advised all faculty, students and staff to take safety precautions.

## Schedule by Zone

| Date | Areas |
|------|-------|
| June 24 | Around the Academic Exchange Center, Building P, student dormitory area |
| June 25 | Around the campus security office, campus entrances, campus roads, surroundings of the new library |
| June 26 | Around the 2nd and 3rd canteens and the old administration building |
| June 27 | Reservoir dam, A–B faculty dormitory area, Buildings 789 |
| June 28 | Affiliated middle and primary schools to the West District quadrangle |
| June 29 | Medical building, new teaching building, student dormitories G, H, I, J |
| June 30 | Longquanyuan, Continuing Education College |

Members of the campus community are advised to heed on-site notices and take personal precautions during the operation.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Assets Management Office)
