Shantou University Library has announced its opening arrangements for the 2014 summer vacation, providing service information for students and staff.

## Schedule

During the administrative and support-unit rotation period from July 2 to August 20, the library will open from 9:00 to 17:00 on July 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 17, 19, 22, 24, 26, 29 and 31, and August 2, 5, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16 and 19. Regular opening will resume on August 21.

The Special Collections Area, Reading Hall and Seminar Rooms will be temporarily closed to students on campus during the student summer period from June 29 to August 23, 2014.
