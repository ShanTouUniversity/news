To prepare housing for the 2014 incoming class, Shantou University’s Student Community Management Service Center will clear and inspect empty beds throughout the student dormitories from July 1 through September 5, including beds vacated by graduating students.

Staff will remove non-university property placed on or within the projected area of empty beds. Students should keep personal belongings, chairs, and storage cabinets beneath their assigned beds to prevent accidental removal. After the clearing work, the university will clean dormitory bathrooms, balconies, doors and windows, furniture, and floors. Long-standing items left around other beds may also be handed to cleaning staff or taken to waste collection points.

The relevant empty beds have been marked and registered. Beds may not be changed without approval. Errors in the markings can be reported to management at 86502661.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Community Management Service Center)
