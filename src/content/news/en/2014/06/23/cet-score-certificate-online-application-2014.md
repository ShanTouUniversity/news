Following a notice from the provincial examination authority, the office of the National College English Test Committee no longer accepts applications to reissue CET score certificates starting in 2014. Applicants must now complete the process online.

Candidates can apply through the National Education Examinations Authority’s comprehensive query website at http://chaxun.neea.edu.cn. The service is available only for examinations taken in 2005 or later.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
