With the summer break approaching, Shantou University’s Student Affairs Office has issued safety guidance reminding students to protect their property, plan travel carefully, and remain alert to potential risks.

Students should lock dormitory doors and windows and turn off utilities before leaving, and should not keep cash or valuables in their rooms. When traveling home or elsewhere, students should use properly licensed transportation, travel with companions where possible, and monitor weather alerts. During the flood season, students should stay away from reservoirs, lakes, beaches, and other dangerous waters, and seek shelter in safe buildings during lightning, heavy rain, or strong winds. Students seeking part-time work should use reputable channels and watch for recruitment scams involving deposits or confiscated identity documents.

Students remaining on campus must follow university schedules and safety rules, may not accommodate visitors, and may not use open flames or high-power appliances. They should avoid dangerous areas and unprotected activities. Campus emergencies can be reported to the Student Community Property Management Service Center at 86502661 or 13556304313, or to Campus 110 at 82904110.
