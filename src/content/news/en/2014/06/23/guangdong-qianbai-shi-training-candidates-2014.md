Shantou University has announced its candidates for the eighth batch of the Guangdong Higher-Education Qianbai-Shi Talent Development Program. Following review by relevant university offices and an expert assessment, the university proposes one national-level candidate, five provincial-level candidates, and 10 university-level candidates.

The public notice period runs from June 23 through June 29. Organizations and individuals with objections should submit a written statement or email to the university Human Resources Office by June 29, providing specific facts and the reporter’s real name. The office is located in Administrative Center Room 315 and can be reached at 86502349 or rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
