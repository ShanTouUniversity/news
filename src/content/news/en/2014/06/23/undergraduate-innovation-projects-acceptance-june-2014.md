Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office has published the June 2014 completion-review results for undergraduate innovation experiment projects and innovation-and-entrepreneurship training projects. Of the 78 projects from seven schools that took part, 75 passed the review, including 11 rated outstanding; 14 projects received extensions and two were terminated.

## Review summary

| Project type | Passed | Other outcomes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Undergraduate innovation experiments | 41 | 3 extensions, 2 terminated |
| Innovation and entrepreneurship training | 34 | 11 extensions, 3 failed |
| Total | 75 | 14 extensions, 2 terminated |

The expert panel reviewed completion materials, heard project defenses and assessed objectives, innovation, outputs and use of funds. The projects produced research reports, software, physical designs, websites, visual works, papers and entrepreneurial-practice results. A total of 29 papers were produced, with some published in Chinese or international journals or submitted to competitions.

The office said some teams requested extensions because of job searches, internships, difficulties with fieldwork or results that did not meet expectations. Some projects also need to improve their documentation and process records.
