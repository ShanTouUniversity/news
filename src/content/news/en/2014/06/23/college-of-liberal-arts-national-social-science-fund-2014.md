The results of the 2014 National Social Science Fund annual and youth project review have been announced. Shantou University received funding for eight projects, including two projects from its Liberal Arts Faculty.

Peng Jian’e of the Department of Foreign Languages received funding under the general project category for “The Status and Influencing Factors of International Academic Paper Publication by Foreign-Language Teachers in Higher Education,” while Wang Yuhan of the Global Studies Center received youth project funding for “The Effects of Reading Order on Readers’ Cognitive Processing: An Eye-Tracking Study.” Their approval numbers are 14BYY067 and 14CYY016, respectively.
