In June 2014, Shantou University’s Student Learning Center (SLC) announced plans to recruit 20 student IT tutors for the 2014–15 Zhanpu program. Tutors were to help women officials in rural communities and grassroots civil-affairs staff learn computer fundamentals, office software, online search, and commonly used mobile devices.

The positions were open to on-campus undergraduates in the classes of 2011, 2012, and 2013. Applicants were expected to have basic computer skills, communication ability, and patience. Training sessions were scheduled to begin on August 19, followed by courses typically held twice a month on Wednesdays in a campus computer lab. Applications were due through the student work-study system by June 23.
