Shantou University published the results of two minor construction tenders on June 13, 2014. Following the university tender panel’s review, Shantou Jinhai Construction Co. won the auditorium fire-control room repair project for RMB 16,554.40. Shantou Yueli Construction and Decoration Co. won the project to install air-conditioning power lines on the fifth and sixth floors of Building E for RMB 36,825.

The notice period ran from June 13 to 16. Objections could be submitted to the university’s Supervision and Audit Office as specified in the original notice.
