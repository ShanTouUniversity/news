Jieyang Chaoshan Airport will hold an “Aviation Services on Campus” event at the entrance to Shantou University’s gymnasium from 8:30 a.m. to noon on June 14. Open to STU students, faculty and their families, the event will provide information on the Chaoshan aviation network and air travel rules, as well as consultations on routes, fares and domestic and international travel products.

China Southern Airlines’ Shantou operation, Air China’s Shantou office, Spring Airlines’ Shantou office and other airlines, travel agents and agencies will take part. Visitors who consult at the event may receive gifts from exhibitors while supplies last.
