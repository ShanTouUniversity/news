On June 13, 2014, Shantou University published its proposed nominees for the 2014 Young Academic Leaders Support Program. Following recommendations from university units and review by functional offices, the university proposed Yang Weifeng of the College of Science and Zhao Yongjie of the College of Engineering. The notice period ended on June 18.

Organizations and individuals could submit comments to the university’s Human Resources Office during the notice period. Submissions were required to include specific factual grounds and the sender’s name.
