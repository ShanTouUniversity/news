Shantou University selected two of the six teams that applied for the 2014 “Jinggangshan Spirit, China Dream” National College Student Summer Practice Program to advance to the national selection. The applicant teams included a total of 132 students and faculty members.

The selected teams are “Da Qingnian” and “Jinggangshan China Dream Team.” The notice named the student team leaders; their names are omitted here to protect their personal information.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
