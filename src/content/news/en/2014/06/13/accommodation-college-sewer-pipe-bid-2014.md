Shantou University published the evaluation result for a sewer-pipe upgrade at Site A of its Phase I Accommodation College project on June 13, 2014. Following review by the university’s tender panel, Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Co. won the contract for RMB 56,481, including RMB 2,689.59 in contingency costs.

The result was open for comment from June 13 through 16. Comments could be submitted to the university’s Supervision and Audit Office as specified in the notice.
