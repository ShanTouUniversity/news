Shantou University’s Academic Affairs Office reported a serious teaching incident after a News Writing class in the School of Journalism did not begin as scheduled. According to the notice, on September 26, 2013, students waited about 40 minutes in Room E208 before reporting that the instructor had not arrived.

An investigation by the School of Journalism and the Academic Affairs Office found that the instructor had gone out after misremembering the class time and could not return in time. Under the relevant provisions of the university’s undergraduate teaching-incident rules, the case was classified as a serious teaching incident and the responsible instructor was publicly censured. The notice did not identify the instructor by name.
