Shantou University announced the reviewed results for two teaching-area projects on June 13, 2014. Shantou Yueli Construction and Decoration Co. won the public-restroom renovation at Stairwell 13 for RMB 310,800. Shantou Nanhua Construction Co. won the teaching-area stairwell aluminum-window renovation for RMB 163,552.33.

Both results were open for comment from June 13 through 16. The university’s Supervision and Audit Office was designated to receive objections.
