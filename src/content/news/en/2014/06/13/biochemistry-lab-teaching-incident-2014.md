It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on June 13, 2014, regarding teaching incidents found during an inspection of the “Biochemistry Lab” course on August 27, 2013.

At 14:00 on August 27, an inspection of the Science Building found that for Class 62876 no instructor or students were present, and for Class 62877 the instructor was absent.

Following a joint investigation by the School of Science and the Academic Affairs Office, the causes were identified:

| Class | Situation | Determination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 62876 | Instructor rescheduled the class without formal procedures | Constitutes an ordinary teaching incident |
| 62877 | Instructor was unwell but did not complete leave or substitute procedures, only asking another teacher of the same course to look after students verbally | Constitutes an ordinary teaching incident |

In accordance with Article 1(3)(7) of Part I and Article 3 of Part II of the *Shantou University Measures for Determination of Undergraduate Teaching Incidents (Trial)*, both cases were determined as ordinary teaching incidents, with the respective instructors held responsible and given a circulated criticism.

The office reminded teaching units and faculty to strictly observe teaching management regulations and to complete required procedures in advance when special circumstances arise.
