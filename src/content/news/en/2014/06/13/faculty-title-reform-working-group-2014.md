Shantou University established a working group to advance reform of the university’s faculty title system. Following national guidance on reforming titles for university teachers, the university was approved by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security as a pilot institution in the province.

A university leadership meeting decided to form the group on June 10. Wen Xiaobo was named its head, and its office will be based in the university’s day-to-day office for the senior academic title review committee. STU issued the notice on June 13.
