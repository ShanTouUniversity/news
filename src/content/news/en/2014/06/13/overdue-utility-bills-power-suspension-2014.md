Shantou University’s Finance Office, Student Affairs Office and Resource Management Office said on June 13 that power to dorms with unpaid utility bills for May 2014 or earlier would be suspended from June 16 and restored after payment.

From June 16 to 18, the university planned two stored-value card deductions each day. Dorms whose payments succeeded in the 10:30 a.m. deduction were expected to have power restored between 2:30 and 4:30 p.m. Those paid in the 4:30 p.m. deduction were expected to have power restored between 5:30 and 8 p.m. Payments made after 4:30 p.m. would be followed by restoration between 2:30 and 4:30 p.m. the next day. From June 19, deductions would take place once daily at 4:30 p.m.

The offices advised users with insufficient card balances to top up, and those with insufficient bank balances to deposit funds and complete account loading during banking hours. Users who had lost their cards could set up proxy payment in the utility management system or pay in cash at the Bank of China’s STU branch and submit the receipt to the Finance Office for posting. The Finance Office would not accept cash. The notice did not disclose specific dorm identities.
