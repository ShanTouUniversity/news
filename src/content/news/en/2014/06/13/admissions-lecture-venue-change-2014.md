Shantou University’s Admissions Office changed the arrangements for two lectures during the June 15 Admissions Consultation Day. Registrations for the Gaokao application guidance lecture exceeded the Science Lecture Hall’s capacity, so the lecture will instead take place in the auditorium at 9 a.m.

The time and venue for the high school career-planning lecture that afternoon remain unchanged. The notice did not announce changes to other consultation-day activities.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Admissions Office)
