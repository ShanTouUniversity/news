Shantou University's Admissions Office scheduled its 2014 admissions and enrollment work meeting for 3 p.m. on Wednesday, July 9, 2014, in Room 213 of the Administration Center. Members of the university admissions and enrollment leadership group and representatives recommended by university units were expected to attend.
