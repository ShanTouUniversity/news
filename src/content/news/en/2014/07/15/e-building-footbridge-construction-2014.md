To improve pedestrian access among Buildings D, G and E, Shantou University plans to build a pedestrian bridge over the drainage channel north of Building E. The bridge will connect the walkways between the three buildings. Following a quotation review, Guangdong Jinzhonghai Construction Co., Ltd. was selected as the contractor.

Construction is scheduled to begin on **July 16, 2014**, and finish in mid-September. Work will include a cofferdam, foundation treatment for the channel bed and retaining wall, stone masonry, and a reinforced-concrete bridge and railings. Pedestrians and vehicles should slow down and take care near the work area.
