The Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association will hold a departure ceremony for the university’s summer cultural, science and health social-practice program at **8:00 a.m. on July 17, 2014**, at Truth Clock Square.

Teachers and students participating in the summer public-welfare courses “Three Rural Areas” Service Learning, “Passing on the Torch” Community Care Action and Dawn Student Aid will attend the ceremony. The organizers asked relevant participants to arrive on time.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and Shantou University Youth Volunteers Association)
