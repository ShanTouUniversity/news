Renovation of the School of Engineering computer center at Shantou University began on May 29, 2014. During construction, changes were required after fire-safety review, floor treatment and adjustments to the amount of outdoor power cable. The additional work includes fire doors, renewed flooring and power cables.

The Infrastructure Office initially estimated the additional cost at **approximately 55,000 yuan**. The cumulative increase remains below the university’s prescribed 10 percent limit for the project. The university opened the estimate for public comment, with written comments accepted by the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office until **17:30 on July 17, 2014**.
