Shantou University issued the 2014 budget for its main campus on July 4, 2014. The budget had been reviewed and approved by the university leadership, and units were expected to plan expenditures accordingly while observing national financial rules.

The university advised teaching units to reserve part of their allocated operating funds for student internships and activities. It recommended that at least half of the remaining funds be allocated to departments, teaching and research sections, and other basic teaching units. Those units were to prioritize necessary low-value consumables, then allocate the balance with teacher numbers and teaching hours from the previous budget year each carrying a 50 percent weight.

Detailed unit allocations are provided in the OA attachment.
