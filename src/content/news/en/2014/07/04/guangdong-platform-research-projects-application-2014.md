Shantou University's Research Office released application arrangements on July 4, 2014, for the recognition and application of Guangdong provincial key platforms and major research projects. The arrangements covered key platform enhancement and development projects as well as major research project applications.

Categories included international and Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan cooperation platforms, engineering technology research centers, and major basic, applied, international cooperation, innovation-team, young-innovator, and special innovation projects. Proposed projects had to be included in the university's 2014–2016 Innovation-Driven Development Initiative plan. The notice also stated that a project leader could apply for or undertake no more than three provincially funded projects in principle.

Interested applicants were required to send the project leader, application category, and project title to the Research Office by noon on July 8. The university would organize a selection process, with follow-up procedures to be announced separately.

> ⚠️ Complete category-specific requirements, forms, and contact assignments are available on the OA system.
