To improve the gymnasium’s infrastructure, Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will replace its main lighting. Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. will carry out the work.

Construction is scheduled to begin on **July 20, 2014**, and finish by August 8. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during the work. Pedestrians should take care and avoid stopping in the construction area where possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
