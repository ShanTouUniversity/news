Shantou University’s Business School announced selection arrangements for spring 2014–2015 overseas exchange programs and Dublin City University’s “3+2” dual-degree and bachelor’s-to-master’s programs. Exchange destinations include ISM University of Management and Economics in Lithuania, Dublin City University in Ireland, University Catolica in Portugal, the University of Manitoba in Canada, European Business School in France and Ecole de Management de Normandie. Exchanges generally last one semester, with some options lasting one year.

Exchange programs offer two to four places each. Applicants are generally expected to be Business School undergraduates with strong academic performance and English skills, and must submit an application form, proof of English proficiency, a transcript and a 300–500-word study plan in English. The Dublin City University “3+2” program offers 10 places. Its language requirement is IELTS 6.5, with at least 6.5 in writing and speaking and 6 in listening and reading, or TOEFL 92 with at least 21 in each section.

Deadlines for the different programs fall between **September 1 and 30**. Applicants must submit materials to the Business School’s international programs office as required; national scholarship applicants must also follow China Scholarship Council procedures. Host-university tuition is waived for exchange students, while travel, living, accommodation, books and insurance costs are borne by students.
