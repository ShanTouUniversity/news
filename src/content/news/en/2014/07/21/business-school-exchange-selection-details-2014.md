Shantou University’s Business School announced selection arrangements for spring 2014–2015 overseas exchanges, dual degrees and bachelor’s-to-master’s programs. Undergraduates from the 2011–2013 cohorts may apply for six exchange programs and one Dublin City University “3+2” program. Applicants should have strong English and academic performance; students who have not previously joined a university or school exchange are given priority, and a GPA of at least 3.0 is generally required.

Exchange destinations are ISM University of Management and Economics in Lithuania, Dublin City University in Ireland, University Catolica in Portugal, the University of Manitoba in Canada, European Business School in France and Ecole de Management de Normandie. Each offers two to four places, generally for one semester. IELTS requirements range from 6.0 to 6.5, with section requirements varying by program. Applications include a form, English-score proof, an English transcript and a 300–500-word study plan.

The Dublin City University “3+2” dual-degree and bachelor’s-to-master’s program offers 10 places. Students who complete three years at STU and meet the language and partner-university requirements may complete undergraduate study in Dublin and earn two bachelor’s degrees. Eligible students may continue to a one-year master’s course. The requirement is IELTS 6.5, with at least 6.5 in writing and speaking and 6 in listening and reading, or TOEFL 92 with at least 21 in each section.

Deadlines fall on **September 1, 20 or 30** depending on the program. Exchange students pay no tuition to the host university, while the “3+2” program offers a 15 percent tuition reduction; travel, living, accommodation, books and insurance costs are borne by students, estimated at 4,000–8,000 yuan per month. Applicants should contact the Business School’s international programs office for forms and details.
