Shantou University published utility charges for staff dormitories for June 2014, based on meter readings taken on July 1 and 2. Staff could sign in with their campus network accounts to check the charges through the [utility management system](http://power.stu.edu.cn/).

Staff with questions could visit the Water and Electricity Center from July 14 to 16. The campus extension was 2489. Fees without objections by the deadline would be deducted from staff members’ July 2014 salary accounts by the Finance Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resources Management Office)
