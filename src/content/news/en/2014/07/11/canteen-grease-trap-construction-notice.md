It is reported that, with approval from relevant authorities, the STU Infrastructure Office will build a brick grease trap on the open ground south of Canteens 2 and 3, with construction entrusted to Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Project | Brick Grease Trap for Canteens 2 and 3 |
| Location | Open ground south of Canteens 2 and 3 |
| Contractor | Shantou Donghua Construction Co., Ltd. |
| Schedule | July 12 to August 10, 2014 |
| Project Leader | Lu Chunxiong |
| Site Manager | Chen Jinwu |

The Infrastructure Office noted that some noise, vibration, and dust are expected during construction and advised pedestrians and vehicles to take care and avoid lingering near the work site.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
