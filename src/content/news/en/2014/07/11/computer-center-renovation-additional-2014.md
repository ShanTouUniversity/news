It is reported that the Infrastructure Office issued a notice on July 11, 2014, regarding additional costs for the Computer Center renovation project of the College of Engineering.

The project commenced on May 29, 2014. Additional work, including fire water supply piping and the use of PVC power and network distribution columns to match furniture selected by the Department of Computer Science, has added about 56,000 yuan (including 19,000 yuan for the Network and Information Center’s network works), keeping the cumulative increase within the university’s 10% threshold.

Per the Infrastructure Project Bidding Management Measures, the additional cost is now publicized. Those with objections may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office before 17:30 on July 15, 2014.
