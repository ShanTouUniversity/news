Shantou University announced the tender result for its 2014 minor repairs project for staff housing blocks A and B. Following review and recommendation by the university’s tendering and bidding leading group, Shantou Residential Construction Engineering Co. was selected, with a bid of RMB 271,806.14.

The result was open for public notice from July 11 to 14, 2014. Objections could be submitted to the university’s Supervision and Audit Office.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office)
