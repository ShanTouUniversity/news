Results for the Ministry of Education’s 2014 general humanities and social sciences research program showed that three Shantou University projects received grants: one planning fund project, one youth fund project, and one project under the engineering and technology talent-development research program.

The university submitted 30 applications for planning and youth funds and one application to the engineering and technology talent-development program, for 31 applications in total. Details of the funded projects and application statistics by university unit were provided in the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Research Office)
