Shantou University was scheduled to hold its 2014 student military training parade and recognition ceremony at 4:30 p.m. on July 13 at the university athletics field. All participating students and college counselors were to assemble at 4 p.m. Party secretaries of each college or residential college were to take seats at the presidium.

> Source: STU OA Notice (People’s Armed Forces Department)
