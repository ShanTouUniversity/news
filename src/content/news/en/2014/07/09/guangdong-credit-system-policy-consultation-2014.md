Guangdong’s Department of Education sought feedback on draft guidance for credit-based management at regular universities and a provisional measure on credit-based tuition fees. Shantou University’s Office of Academic Affairs forwarded the drafts and invited university units to submit comments.

Feedback was due to the Office of Academic Affairs’ general section before the end of the working morning on July 15, 2014. The full provincial draft documents were attached to the OA notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Office of Academic Affairs)
>
> ⚠️ The two draft documents are available on the OA system.
