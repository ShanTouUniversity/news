According to the public announcement of the 2014 National Teaching Achievement Awards for undergraduate education, two Shantou University achievements were included in the proposed award list. The project led by Professor Yang Mianhua and colleagues at the Medical College, “Comprehensive Reform and Practice in Educating Outstanding Doctors with an International Perspective,” was proposed for first prize. The project led by Professor Bao Nengsheng and colleagues at the School of Engineering, “Building and Promoting a New CDIO Engineering Education Model in China,” was proposed for second prize.

The Academic Affairs Office is publicizing the project applications from **July 14 to 23, 2014**. Comments may be submitted in writing to the office during this period. Contact: 86502736 or o_jwc@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Academic Affairs Office)
