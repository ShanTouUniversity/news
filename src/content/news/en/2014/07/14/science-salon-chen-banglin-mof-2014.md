## Speaker Profile

Prof. Chen received his B.S. from Zhejiang University in 1985, M.S. in inorganic chemistry in 1988, and Ph.D. from the National University of Singapore in 2000. He is currently Professor in the Department of Chemistry at UT San Antonio. His research focuses on the synthesis and properties of porous metal-organic framework materials and their applications in the field of photoelectric sensing including hydrogen and natural gas storage, gas recognition and separation, environmental sensing and detection of bioactive molecules.

He has published 140 papers in top journals including Science, Nature Communications and Chemical Reviews, holds 10 patents (2 transferred), with over 14,000 SCI citations. He was ranked 15th in the Thomson Reuters Top 100 Chemists 2000–2010 and listed as a Most Highly Cited Researcher in Chemistry in 2014, and was selected for the national Recruitment Program of Global Experts (short-term) in 2012, with an appointment at Zhejiang University.
