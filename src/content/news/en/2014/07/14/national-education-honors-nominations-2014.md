Shantou University’s Personnel Office announced that, following recommendations from university units, departmental review and evaluation by the Teaching Committee, the university plans to recommend the School of Science for the **2014 National Advanced Collective in the Education System** and Lin Peng of the School of Engineering for **National Model Teacher**.

The announcement is open for public comment from July 14 to 18, 2014. Organizations and individuals with comments may submit written or email statements to the Personnel Office before the deadline, including specific facts and their real names. The office is located in Room 315 of the Administration Center and can be reached at 86502349 or rscgs@stu.edu.cn.
