To support its infrastructure work, the Infrastructure Office of Shantou University’s Resource Management Office is recruiting one civil engineering manager. The position will be hired as an auxiliary contract worker; the employment relationship will not be transferred to the university, and compensation will follow the university’s relevant standards.

Applicants are generally expected to hold at least a junior college qualification in architectural engineering, a mid-level or higher technical title in architectural engineering, and experience in engineering management and on-site construction. The role includes technical management, inspection of site materials and quality, review of quantities and site records, and participation in concealed-work and completion acceptance.

Applicants must submit a résumé and copies of identification, academic, professional and technical certificates by **July 27, 2014**. Materials may be mailed or emailed to the Infrastructure Office, Room 223, Resource Management Office Building, Shantou University, 243 Daxue Road, Shantou, Guangdong 515063. Contact: o_jjb@stu.edu.cn; 0754-86503216 or 86502490.
