The 20th council transition meeting of Shantou University’s Youth Volunteers Association was held in the university’s Science Lecture Hall on May 24, 2014. Of 124 delegates, 99 attended, and the meeting approved the work report of the 19th Council.

A competitive election selected five members for the 20th Council, along with the association president and four vice presidents. Student names are omitted to protect privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and STU Youth Volunteers Association)
