Shantou University Law School announced the results of its 16th Academic Month review. The event received 40 paper entries and 26 project-design entries. Paper awards included three first prizes, eight second prizes, 16 third prizes, and 13 merit awards. Project design awards went to two A-level, nine B-level, and 15 C-level groups.

The names of winning works and project teams were listed in OA attachments. Student names are omitted here to protect privacy.
