Guangdong’s Department of Education announced the 2014 provincial undergraduate teaching quality and reform projects. Shantou University had 25 projects selected across nine categories: off-campus practice bases, talent-development innovation pilot zones, teaching teams, experimental teaching demonstration centers, discipline reform pilots, outstanding talent programs, pilot colleges, applied-program demonstration majors, and high-quality shared courses.

The Office of Academic Affairs will monitor project implementation and assist with pre-implementation reviews, midterm checks, completion reviews, and dissemination of results. The full list of selected projects was included in the OA attachments.
