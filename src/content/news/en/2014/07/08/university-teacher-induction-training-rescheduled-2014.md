A change in the responsible department’s training schedule prompted a tentative rescheduling of university teacher induction training originally planned for July 14–27, 2014, at Shantou University. The training was tentatively moved to the period from October 1 to December 1.

The preliminary plan announced by the Human Resources Office was to hold classes during the National Day holiday and an examination on December 1. Further arrangements were to be announced later.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Human Resources Office)
