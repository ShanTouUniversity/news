It is reported that Shantou University, with approval from the relevant authorities, will build a centralized transfer station for dry branches and fallen leaves west of the pump house on the West Campus. The project is being implemented by the university's Capital Construction Office, with Shantou Jinhai Construction Co., Ltd. commissioned as the contractor.

According to the construction schedule, work is set to begin on July 10, 2014 and is expected to be completed before August 23, 2014. The university noted that noise, vibration and dust are inevitable during construction, and asked for understanding from students and staff affected by the works.

For safety reasons, pedestrians passing through the area have been advised to stay alert and avoid lingering near the construction site.
