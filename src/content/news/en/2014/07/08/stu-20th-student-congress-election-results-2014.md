The 20th Shantou University Student Congress was held at the university’s Science and Technology Building on June 8, 2014. Of 189 delegates, 126 attended. The congress heard and approved the work report of the 19th Student Union Committee.

A competitive election selected 13 members for the 20th Student Union Committee, as well as the student union president and four vice presidents. Names are omitted to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University)
