The 16th congress of Shantou University’s Student Associations Union was held in Room 201 of the E teaching block on May 24, 2014. Of 61 delegates, 42 attended, and the congress approved the work report of the 15th Standing Committee.

A competitive election selected nine members for the 16th Standing Committee and four members for its presidium. At the university’s 20th Student Congress on June 8, the union president was also elected a vice president of the university Student Union and continued as union president; three other students were elected union vice presidents. Names are omitted to protect student privacy.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and Shantou University Student Associations Union)
