Shantou University's Personnel Office announced on July 2, 2014, that the venue for the university teacher orientation training and examinations had changed because Room 503 in Building D was being renovated. The sessions would be held in Staircase Rooms 203 and 204 in Building E, while the schedule remained unchanged.
