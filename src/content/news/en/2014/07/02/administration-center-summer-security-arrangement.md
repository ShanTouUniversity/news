Shantou University's Security Office issued a summer security notice for the Administration Center on July 2, 2014. From July 3 through August 20, staff on duty and visitors conducting business were required to use the center's main entrance. Other entrances would be closed, and the elevators would be out of service.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Security Office)
