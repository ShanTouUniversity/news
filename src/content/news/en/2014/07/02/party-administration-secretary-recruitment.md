Shantou University announced an internal recruitment on July 2, 2014, for one bilingual secretary in the Party and Government Office. The position required at least a master's degree, strong Chinese writing and English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, proficiency with office software, and well-developed analytical, communication, coordination, and teamwork abilities.

Applicants were required to email the STU application form and an English résumé to recruitment@stu.edu.cn by July 31, with “Application for Bilingual Secretary” in the subject line. The Personnel Office would conduct a qualification review and arrange interviews separately. The listed contact was Li Chang, at 86-754-86502365.
