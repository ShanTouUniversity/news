It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on July 2, 2014, that five STU projects won the 7th Guangdong Teaching Achievement Awards (Higher Education).

According to the provincial notice (Yue Jiao Gao Han [2014] No. 93), 300 projects were awarded across the province, including 128 first prizes and 172 second prizes. STU received three first prizes and two second prizes.

Awarded units and individuals are expected to build on their experience, further refine and promote the achievements, and fully leverage their exemplary role. The detailed list is available in the OA attachment.
