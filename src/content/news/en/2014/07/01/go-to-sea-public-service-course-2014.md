The School of Science announced the schedule for “Go to the Sea,” a public-service course in the 2014–2015 summer semester. The course runs from July 1 to 11 and combines lectures, discussions and field activities.

Classroom topics include Chinese maritime territorial awareness, service-learning models, marine ecological conservation, shark awareness and project reflection, with most sessions held in Room E405. Field activities include team development at Zhongxin Longhu Beach, an ecological survey of Dacheng Bay in Raoping County, a round-island ecological study in Nan’ao, and marine knowledge training and a competition for middle school students in Nan’ao County.

The course is led by Sun Zewei, Wang Junying and other instructors. Exact times, locations and responsibilities follow the published course schedule.
