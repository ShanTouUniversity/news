Shantou University's Network and Information Center announced the summer campus-card service schedule on July 1, 2014. The top-up counter below the Second Dining Hall would be closed from July 2 through August 20 and was scheduled to reopen on August 21. The service counter at the Expert Apartments would continue operating on its regular schedule.

During the closure, students and staff could add funds through the self-service transfer and inquiry machines. The automated service line for reporting lost cards and checking balances was 82903414. Summer service inquiries could be directed to 86503400-2 or support@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
