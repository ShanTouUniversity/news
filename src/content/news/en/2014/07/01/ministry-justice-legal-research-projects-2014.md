Applications opened for the 2014 Ministry of Justice National Legal Governance and Legal Theory Research Projects. The program includes key, general, young scholar and special-task projects, with funding limits and eligibility requirements varying by category. Funding for key projects with formally published deliverables is capped at 100,000 yuan, while the corresponding cap for general and young scholar projects is 50,000 yuan; special-task applicants must provide their own funding.

Applicants should generally hold at least a bachelor’s degree. Project leaders must also meet category-specific requirements involving academic rank, administrative position or a doctoral degree. Leaders and members of young scholar projects must be no older than 39. Applicants may not apply if they have an unfinished related project, are submitting substantially similar work under another central-government program, or were found to have committed fraud in an application during the previous two years.

Applicants must email the Application and Review Form to yczhuo@stu.eud.cn for formal review by September 10. After approval, they must submit 11 printed copies to the Research Office and send an electronic copy by September 17. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhu Yanchun at 86502563.
