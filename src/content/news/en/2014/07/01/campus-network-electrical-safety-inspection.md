The Network and Information Center will conduct annual electrical safety inspections in secondary campus network server rooms on July 2, 3 and 5. Network service in the affected areas will be interrupted for about 30 minutes during the inspections, and the restoration time will not be announced separately.

The specific affected areas are listed in the attachment. For inquiries, users may contact the Network and Information Center at 0754-86503400 (campus extension 883400) or stunic@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
> ⚠️ The specific affected areas for each date are listed in the attachment on the OA system.
