Shantou University's Research Office circulated on July 1, 2014, a call from the Shantou science authority for experts to join its expert roster, together with a review of existing qualifications. The call covered prospective experts in science and technology, management, and finance. Those who had received roster qualifications by December 31, 2013, were required to take part in the review.

The notice set out general expectations for professional ethics and relevant expertise, with additional degree, rank, experience, and age criteria for different expert categories. Both new applicants and current experts undergoing review were required to submit the relevant form and supporting documents. Paper materials were due at the Research Office by July 18, and electronic forms were to be sent to cszhu@stu.edu.cn. The OA attachments contain the full category-specific criteria and forms.

> ⚠️ Application forms and complete eligibility criteria are available on the OA system.
