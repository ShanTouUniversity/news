Issue 2 of the 2014 Shantou University Research and Innovation Briefing, the 32nd issue overall, has been published. It covers research projects, research achievements and awards, research platform development, development planning, academic conferences and academic exchanges.

The briefing reports on newly approved humanities and social sciences projects, a Guangdong Science and Technology Award, inspections and evaluations of Guangdong university research platforms, and work connected with the university’s Innovation and Strengthening Project. It also summarizes academic events including the International Chaoshan Literature Award ceremony, a legal seminar, a workshop on cultural exchange and cooperation, and visits and exchanges involving scholars and experts.
