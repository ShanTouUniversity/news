Shantou University's School of Law issued a notice on July 1, 2014, reminding undergraduates interested in the selection for a program at Canada's McGill University to submit their application materials on time.

Students who had applied and attended the selection interview were required to submit IELTS or TOEFL score reports by August 1. Scanned copies could be emailed to jjyao@stu.edu.cn, while paper copies could be delivered to Room 211 of the School of Law after the next semester began. Other interested students were also required to submit all application materials, including any available English test scores, by August 1.
