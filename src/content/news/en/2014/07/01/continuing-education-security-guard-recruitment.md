Shantou University's Continuing Education College announced on July 1, 2014, that it was recruiting one security guard as an auxiliary contract employee. Duties included overnight security for campus and other areas, visitor and vehicle registration, management of the student activity room, and typhoon-prevention work. Applicants were expected to be healthy, responsible, and able to work 24-hour shifts; security experience was preferred.

Applicants were required to submit a résumé and copies of identification and relevant certificates by July 10. Those who passed the initial screening would be invited to an interview. The notice listed Ms. Chen as the contact, at 86502459 or o_cjxy@stu.edu.cn. Part of the mailing address in the notice is corrupted and is not reproduced here.
