It is reported that to cultivate and enhance students' leadership skills, Shantou University will carry out the Leaders Exchange Program (Phase 4) in partnership with the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. Since the selection was launched on June 17, recommendations from various units produced 25 shortlisted candidates, and after two rounds of interviews, 10 students including Ren XX were finally selected for the program.

## Selected Delegates

| Recommending Unit | Selected Students |
| --- | --- |
| Student Union | Ren XX (Law, enrolled in 2012), Cai RH (Chinese Language and Literature, enrolled in 2012) |
| Youth Volunteers Association | Li YQ (English, enrolled in 2012) |
| Chengzhi College | Zheng S (Mathematics, enrolled in 2011) |
| College of Engineering | Chen YS (Electronic Engineering, enrolled in 2012), Tang YL (Communications Engineering, enrolled in 2012) |
| Law School | Lin W (Law, enrolled in 2012), Kuang HJ (Law, enrolled in 2012) |
| College of Liberal Arts | Mao YY (English, enrolled in 2011) |
| FLY Training Program | Zhang JH (Journalism, enrolled in 2012) |

The organizers stated that if any of the selected students are unable to participate, substitutes will be drawn in order from the ranking of the second interview round, and any changes will be communicated promptly to the students concerned.

The selected students are expected to make preparations and return to campus for relevant training before the summer term begins on August 25.
