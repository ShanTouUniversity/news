To improve teaching facilities and support academic exchanges, Shantou University\'s School of Science planned to renovate the academic auditorium on the first floor of the Biomedical and Advanced Materials Research Center. The project was assigned to Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd.

Construction was scheduled to start on July 29 and finish by September 6. Noise, vibration and dust could occur during the work. Members of the university community should take care near the construction area, and staff working in the building were asked to avoid lingering at the site.

Lu Chunxiong was the project manager and Lin Longsheng the site management engineer. Project-related matters could be handled through the university\'s relevant office.
