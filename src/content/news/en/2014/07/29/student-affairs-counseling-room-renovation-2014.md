To improve the counseling environment and support student counseling services, Shantou University\'s Student Affairs Office planned to renovate Rooms 735 and 736 in Building 789. The project was assigned to Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd.

Construction was scheduled to start on July 30 and finish by August 28. Noise, vibration and dust could occur during the work. Members of the university community should take care near the construction area and avoid lingering at the site.

Lu Chunxiong was the project manager and Lin Longsheng the site management engineer. Project-related matters could be handled through the university\'s relevant office.
