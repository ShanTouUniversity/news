The Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology opened its 2014 recognition process for engineering technology research centers. Shantou University’s Research Office invited eligible research platforms to apply.

Centers will be recognized against standards through a competitive funding process. Applicants meeting the expert-review requirements may receive recognition and a plaque. Centers that meet the annual guidelines may also apply for construction funding to purchase advanced instruments, equipment and technical software needed for research and testing.

Leaders of interested platforms should complete an online application through the Guangdong Science and Technology Business Management Sunshine Government Affairs Platform and submit it to the Research Office for review by **August 4, 2014**. Contact: ygluo@stu.edu.cn or 86503271.
