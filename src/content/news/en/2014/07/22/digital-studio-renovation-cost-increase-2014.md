Renovation of Shantou University’s digital multifunction studio began on May 14, 2014. During construction, additional work was required for sound insulation, room layout and fire safety, including lightweight brick walls, window replacement, floor and wall alterations, fire doors and fire-resistant plywood for the stepped ceiling.

The Infrastructure Office initially estimated the additional cost at **approximately 34,000 yuan**. The cumulative increase remains below the university’s prescribed 10 percent limit for the project. Organizations and individuals may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by **17:30 on July 25, 2014**.
