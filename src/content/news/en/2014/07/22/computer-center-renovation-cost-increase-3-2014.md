Renovation of the School of Engineering computer center at Shantou University began on May 29, 2014. Site conditions led to additional work, including reinforced-concrete window-sill caps, glass mosaic on walls, raised ceilings and related finishes, expanded aluminum partitions, a glass partition facing the software laboratory and granite finishes on an indoor staircase.

The Infrastructure Office initially estimated the additional cost at **approximately 16,000 yuan**. The cumulative increase remains below the university’s prescribed 10 percent limit for the project. Organizations and individuals may submit written comments to the Supervision and Audit Office or the Infrastructure Office by **17:30 on July 25, 2014**.
