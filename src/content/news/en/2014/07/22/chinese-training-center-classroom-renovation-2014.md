Shantou University’s School of Liberal Arts plans to convert Room 308 on the east side of the Liberal Arts Building into a classroom for its Chinese Language Training Center. Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. will carry out the renovation, which includes replacing wooden doors and aluminum windows, installing curtains, painting walls and laying strong- and weak-current wiring.

Construction is scheduled to begin on **July 23, 2014**, and finish by August 21. Noise, vibration and dust may occur. Staff and students working in the building and passing pedestrians should take care and avoid stopping near the work area where possible.
