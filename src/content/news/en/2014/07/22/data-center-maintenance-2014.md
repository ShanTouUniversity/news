As part of its annual work plan, Shantou University’s Network and Information Center will conduct an electrical-safety inspection and maintenance of the university data center. Core campus network equipment and related services will be interrupted during the work, affecting users across the university.

The maintenance is scheduled for **00:00–06:00 on July 23, 2014**. The restoration time will not be announced separately. Users may contact the Network and Information Center at 86503400-1 or stunic@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
