It is reported that the Research Office issued a notice on July 22, 2014, to organize the recommendation for the 2014 Outstanding Scientific Research Achievement Award (Science and Technology) for institutions of higher education and the selection for the 2015 National Science and Technology Awards.

According to the Ministry of Education, the recommendation and review will begin in late August. The university awards include four categories: Natural Science Award, Technological Invention Award, Scientific and Technological Progress Award (including the promotion category) and Patent Award, with no limit on the number of recommended projects. Achievements that have already received a provincial or ministerial second prize or above may be directly recommended for the national awards.
