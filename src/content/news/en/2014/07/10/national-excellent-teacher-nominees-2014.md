Shantou University announced its proposed nominees for the 2014 National Outstanding Teacher and National Outstanding Educator awards. Following recommendations from university units, administrative review, and review by the university’s Teaching Committee, the university proposed Ma Fengqi of the Institute of Higher Education Research for Outstanding Teacher and Du Shimin of the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication for Outstanding Educator.

The public notice period ran from July 10 to 16, 2014. Comments could be submitted to the Human Resources Office by email or in writing by July 16, with specific supporting facts and the submitter’s real name. Supporting nomination materials were attached to the OA notice.
