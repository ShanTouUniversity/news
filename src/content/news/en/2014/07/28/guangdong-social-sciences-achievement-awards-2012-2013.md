Guangdong opened applications for the 2012–2013 Philosophy and Social Sciences Achievement Awards. The awards cover 20 subject groups, including Marxism and scientific socialism, philosophy, economics, law, literature, education, journalism and communication, and management. They are divided into book, paper and research-report categories, each with first, second and third prizes; the total number of prizes would be determined by the applications received.

Eligible submissions include relevant books formally published in Guangdong, publicly published papers and research reports dated from January 1, 2012, through December 31, 2013, as well as qualifying unpublished research reports. In principle, a chief editor, first chief editor or first author should submit each result, with one submission per person. Results that had already won an award at the same or a higher level, unfinished project results and other excluded categories were not eligible.

Applicants should send a draft application form to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` for formal review by September 12 and submit paper materials and an electronic copy to the Research Office by September 23. The schedule called for preliminary review in October, further and final review in November, and approval and public notice in December; the award ceremony date would be announced separately. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun at 86502563.
