STU Affiliated Middle School has announced the enrollment arrangements for new first-year students entering in autumn 2014. Students should report to the first-year classroom on the second floor at 8:30 a.m. on Friday, August 29, to complete registration. A new-student orientation session will be held from 3 to 5 p.m. that day.

Students should prepare four one-inch color photographs and submit them to their homeroom teacher after the semester begins.
