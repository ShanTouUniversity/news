Guangdong has opened its second selection of Outstanding Social Scientists, and Shantou University is inviting eligible social science researchers to apply. About 15 people will be selected across the province, each receiving a 60,000-yuan award; Shantou University may recommend up to five candidates.

Applicants should have worked in social science research in Guangdong for at least 10 years and held a senior professional title for at least 10 years. They must also have made significant achievements in academic research or applied policy research and meet the other criteria in the selection notice.

Applicants should send a draft application to `yczhuo@stu.edu.cn` for formal review by September 12 and submit paper materials to the Research Office, along with an electronic copy, by September 23. The review was scheduled for October and November, with approval and public notice in December; the award ceremony date would be announced separately. Contacts are Huang Yumei and Zhuo Yanchun at 86502563.

> ⚠️ The application form and event attachments are available on the OA system.
