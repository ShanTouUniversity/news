Shantou University’s Personnel Office announced that, following unit recommendations, departmental review and evaluation by the Teaching Committee, the university plans to nominate Wu Renhua of the Medical College for the Seventh Guangdong University Teaching Master Award.

The public-comment period runs from **July 23 to 29, 2014**. Organizations and individuals with comments may contact the Personnel Office in writing or by email before the deadline and should provide specific facts. Organizational submissions must bear an official seal, while individual submissions must be signed. Contact: Room 315, Administration Center; 86502340 or 66748; rscgs@stu.edu.cn.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
