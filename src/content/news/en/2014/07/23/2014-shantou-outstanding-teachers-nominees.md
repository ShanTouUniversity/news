Shantou University has announced its nominees for the 2014 Shantou Outstanding Teacher and Outstanding Educator selections. Following recommendations from university units, departmental review and evaluation by the university panel, Long Yue'e, Chen Shaoke and Li Gengying were nominated for Outstanding Teacher, while Zheng Shunqin was nominated for Outstanding Educator.

The public notice ran from July 23 to July 27. Units or individuals with concerns could submit written comments or an email to the university's Human Resources Office by July 27, together with specific factual evidence. The office is located in Room 315 of the Administration Center and can be reached at 86502340 or 66748, or at `rscgs@stu.edu.cn`.
