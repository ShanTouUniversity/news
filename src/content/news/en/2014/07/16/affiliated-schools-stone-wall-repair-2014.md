To protect teachers and students at its affiliated middle and primary schools, Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will repair the existing loose stone wall on their west side. Shantou Residential Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. will carry out the work.

Construction is scheduled to begin on **July 17, 2014**, and finish by August 25. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during the work. Pedestrians and vehicles should take care and avoid stopping near the construction area where possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
