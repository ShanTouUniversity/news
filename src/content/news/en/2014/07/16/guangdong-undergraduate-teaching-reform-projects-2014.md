The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education announced its 2014 list of approved undergraduate higher-education teaching reform projects. **Nineteen projects** from Shantou University were approved, covering undergraduate mentoring, medical-student assessment, residential-college education, course evaluation, integrated-thinking instruction and discipline-based curriculum reform.

The university asked project teams to prepare their opening work during the summer and plans to organize expert reviews at the beginning of the fall semester. The project list, including project leaders, codes, categories and proposed completion dates, is provided in the OA attachment.
