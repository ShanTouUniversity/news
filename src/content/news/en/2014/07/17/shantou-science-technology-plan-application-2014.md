Applications for the 2014 Shantou Science and Technology Plan have opened, and Shantou University’s Research Office circulated the application arrangements to relevant units. The plan covers industrial, agricultural and social-development research, innovation platforms and carriers, science and technology exchange and cooperation, as well as special programs for small and medium-sized enterprise innovation funds, science finance, soft science and science popularization.

Projects should comply with national, provincial and Shantou development plans and the relevant application guidelines. Projects may generally be ineligible when a project leader has two or more ongoing municipal projects, the project has already received related fiscal support, or required materials are incomplete.

Applicants must complete the application and email it, together with any cooperation agreement, to cszhu@stu.edu.cn by **August 11**. After preliminary review, online submission is due by August 15, and printed materials must reach the Research Office by August 21. Contacts: Hu Yunxiang and Zhu Changsheng, 86502813.
