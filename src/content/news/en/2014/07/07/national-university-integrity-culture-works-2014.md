Shantou University began organizing campus participation in the 3rd National University Integrity Culture Works Collection and Integrity Education program. Staff and students were invited to submit performing arts, painting, calligraphy, photography, design, and online new-media works. Students were also encouraged to take part in a national integrity knowledge quiz.

Entries and registration materials were due to the Student Affairs Office’s education administration section by August 11, 2014. Electronic materials were to be sent to `xtchen@stu.edu.cn` with “3rd National University Integrity Culture Works” in the subject line. The university would review submissions and recommend selected works to the provincial education department. Participants were asked to contact the office before submitting during the summer break.
