Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office began exterior repairs to staff housing block B16 and the Resource Management Office on July 7, 2014. Shantou Shenghang Construction Co., Ltd. was contracted for the work, which was scheduled for completion by August 5.

Construction could cause noise, vibration, and dust. The university advised pedestrians to take care and avoid lingering near the construction site. Lu Chunxiong was the project lead, and Chen Jinwu was the on-site construction supervisor.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Infrastructure Office)
