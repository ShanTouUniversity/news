STU Affiliated Middle School reported strong results in the 2014 Shantou high school entrance examination. Of the class’s 45 candidates, 15 scored above 700, accounting for 33.3 percent. The school reported that 7.8 percent of candidates across Shantou scored above 700.

Two students tied for the highest score at 749, and one of them earned a perfect score in physics. Their names are omitted to protect student privacy.
