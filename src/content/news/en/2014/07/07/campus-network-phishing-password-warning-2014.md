Shantou University’s Network and Information Center recently warned campus network users about spam emails seeking to obtain passwords. Campus accounts are used for email, network authentication, and access to university information platforms. The center said it would never ask users for passwords by email or phone.

The center advised users to safeguard their passwords and change them regularly. Password changes can be made through the [campus account management platform](https://netms.stu.edu.cn/). Weak passwords may lead to account theft, information disclosure, or attacks on applications.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Network and Information Center)
>
> ⚠️ An example phishing email is available on the OA system.
