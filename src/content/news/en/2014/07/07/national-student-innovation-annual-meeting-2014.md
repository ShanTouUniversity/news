Shantou University opened its internal recommendation process for the 7th National Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Annual Meeting. The university planned to recommend two papers and two project exhibits from its 2012 and 2013 national student innovation and entrepreneurship training projects. The meeting was scheduled for mid-to-late October at Xi’an Jiaotong University.

The meeting was to include an academic session, project exhibits, and an entrepreneurship pitching session. The academic session would feature papers and student presentations from innovation-training projects. Innovation-training, entrepreneurship-training, and entrepreneurship-practice projects could be considered for exhibits, while the pitching session was for entrepreneurship-training and entrepreneurship-practice projects.

Eligible applicants were projects established at STU in 2012 or 2013 under the national training program. Submissions, including recommendation forms and relevant papers or exhibit information, were due by 5 p.m. on July 11, 2014. Detailed submission requirements were provided in OA attachments.
