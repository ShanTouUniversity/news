To improve safety for teachers, students and passing vehicles, Shantou University’s Infrastructure Office will repair the sunken concrete surface east of the small square. Shantou Xinhua Construction Co., Ltd. will carry out the work.

Construction is scheduled to begin on **July 18, 2014**, and finish by August 6. Noise, vibration and dust may occur during the work. Pedestrians and vehicles should take care and avoid stopping near the construction area where possible.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Resource Management Office)
