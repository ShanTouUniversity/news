Based on the relevant Shantou education authorities’ documents, Shantou University, including its Medical College, began its internal recommendations for the 2014 Shantou Outstanding Principal, Outstanding Teacher and Outstanding Education Worker selections. The university may recommend **three outstanding teachers and one outstanding education worker**.

Each unit is to publicize the criteria and organize recommendations and reviews. A unit may recommend no more than two outstanding teachers and one outstanding education worker. A review group made up of representatives of teaching units and administrative departments will select the university’s nominees.

Units must submit recommendation forms, achievement statements, certificates and supporting materials to Room 315 of the Administration Center by **July 21** and email designated materials as required. The selection criteria and forms are set out in the OA attachments.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Personnel Office)
> ⚠️ The selection notice, recommendation forms and material requirements are available in the OA attachments.
