It is reported that, according to the meteorological department, a tropical depression formed on the ocean east of the Philippines at 05:00 on July 23. At 08:00, its center was about 1,560 km southeast of Shantou, with maximum winds of force 7 (15 m/s) near the center. It is expected to move west-northwest at about 30 km/h, develop into a numbered typhoon within the next 12-24 hours, enter the northeastern South China Sea around the early morning of July 25, and make landfall on the central-eastern coast of Guangdong Province from the night of July 25 to the early morning of July 26 as a typhoon or strong typhoon (force 12-14), possibly striking Shantou head-on.

Based on the Shantou Flood Control, Drought Relief, Typhoon Prevention and Freeze Prevention Emergency Plan and consultations, the Municipal Flood and Typhoon Command decided at 13:00 on July 23 to activate the Level IV typhoon response. The STU Flood and Typhoon Command has simultaneously issued a notice deploying various defense tasks.

## Defense Measures

- **All units**: Before leaving offices at the end of the day, fully close and reinforce the doors and windows of offices, laboratories, classrooms, corridors and adjacent public areas, and turn off power and water supplies. Windows and doors that are damaged and cannot be reinforced should be reported immediately to the Maintenance Management Office of the Infrastructure Department for repair. Check and ensure the drainage pipes on the balconies or rooftops of the unit are clear. Valuable instruments and important materials in laboratories and archives should be moved to safe corners and raised to prevent damage from soaking; gaps around the doors and windows of important departments should be stuffed with soft materials such as old newspapers to prevent rainwater from entering.
- **Resources Management Office**: Conduct comprehensive checks on vacant faculty housing, student dormitories and public rooms, closing and reinforcing doors and windows and ensuring drainage is clear; take protective measures for public outdoor equipment vulnerable to typhoons and notify user units to inspect and patrol; clean up and remove banners, decorations (such as colored flags) hanging on campus before the typhoon; strengthen patrol and repair of weak points in water, power and communication facilities and lines; lower the reservoir water level to below 16.2 meters in advance per the flood control plan to free up flood storage capacity, check flood gate facilities, prepare flood control materials and clear obstacles in drainage channels; and organize personnel to check, close and reinforce the doors and windows of public areas.
- **Infrastructure Department**: Arrange the construction units of ongoing projects to take typhoon prevention measures and verify them one by one, and organize repair personnel to stand by for dispatch to repair sites at any time.
- **Landscape Management Center**: Check and appropriately protect campus trees and flowers in advance to reduce losses caused by the typhoon.
- **Property Management Department**: Inspect low-lying areas, building rooftops, rain drains and other public facilities in advance to ensure they are clear and promptly eliminate relevant safety hazards.

All departments, faculty and students are required to implement the defense measures of the Level IV response in strict accordance with the STU Flood Control and Typhoon Prevention Emergency Plan, minimize losses caused by the typhoon, and make every effort to safeguard life and property.
