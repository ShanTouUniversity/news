It is reported that, to serve the country's opening-up, the Belt and Road Initiative and the building of a community with a shared future for mankind, and to accelerate the training of country and region studies researchers with an international perspective and knowledge of international rules, the China Scholarship Council (CSC) will continue to implement the Country and Region Studies Talent Support Program in 2027. The Office of International Cooperation and Exchange has issued a notice launching the on-campus application and selection work.

## How to Apply

The program continues to operate on a project-based model, i.e., "approve the project first, then select candidates." The applicants are teaching units. Units apply for projects first; after the CSC organizes review and approves projects, project units select and recommend candidates according to the approved projects and project information, and candidates are dispatched after review and admission by the CSC. Approved projects can be supported for three consecutive years, with a cumulative maximum of 20 dispatched persons over the three years.

The categories include senior research scholars, visiting scholars, postdoctoral fellows, doctoral degree candidates studying abroad, jointly trained doctoral candidates, and jointly trained master's candidates. Units may apply to the CSC for projects based on their needs for training country and region studies talent and through talent training cooperation channels with overseas institutions. A single project may cover multiple study statuses, and selected researchers may be dispatched to different countries or institutions.

For details, see the official website: https://www.csc.edu.cn/article/4236.

## Target Candidates

The program mainly supports specialized talents researching the politics, economy, culture and education of countries and regions along the Belt and Road, with a focus on the development of the "country and region studies" discipline in universities. It selects fresh undergraduate and master's graduates to pursue doctoral degrees abroad (including combined master's-doctoral programs) as reserve talent for country and region studies, as well as current master's and doctoral students in related disciplines for joint training abroad.

All related disciplines of country and region studies are eligible for support, covering both research coordinating cooperation across different disciplinary fields targeting the same country or region, and research coordinating cooperation across different target countries or regions within the same disciplinary background.

## Application Requirements

The two sides of a project application must have signed a cooperation agreement that is still in effect. The agreement must be directly related to the applied project and not a framework agreement; it should specify the cooperation fields or majors, the study status of selected personnel, training plans or duration of study, rights and responsibilities of both sides, and the degree awarding method. Each approved project may have no more than five foreign partner institutions.

Units applying for 2027 projects are requested to design their projects carefully based on discipline development, faculty building and talent training needs, and submit the relevant materials in time. The university will review and forward them to the CSC. The program is currently at the project application stage and does not involve individual applications.

## Submission Deadline

Units should submit electronic materials, including the project application form and the Chinese/foreign versions of substantive cooperation agreements signed with overseas institutions, to the Office of International Cooperation and Exchange at icd@stu.edu.cn before September 10, 2026.
