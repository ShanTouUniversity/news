It is reported that, to innovate online ideological and political education and showcase outstanding online cultural achievements of faculty and students across the province, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education has decided to hold the 11th Guangdong University Online Media Showcase. Under the theme "Youth Take Up the Mission, Strive Together for the 15th Five-Year Plan," the showcase features five categories.

## Categories

| Category | Type |
|---|---|
| Yueyi Guangying | Visual works |
| Yueyi Secai | Animation works |
| Yueyi Wenhua | Online article works |
| Yueyi Chuangxin | Creative works |
| Yueyi Lexue | Educational works |

All works must have been published online between January 15, 2025 and September 20, 2026, and must be original and independently completed by faculty or students.

## Awards

First, second and third prizes will be awarded in each of the five categories, along with several Outstanding Organization Awards based on the quantity and quality of works submitted by each university. The provincial department will select outstanding works to be recommended for the National College Students Online Culture Festival and the national selection and showcase of outstanding online education works.

## Work Requirements

Works should be politically correct and conform to the requirements of the core socialist values. Creation is encouraged around hot topics such as the opening of the 15th Five-Year Plan, new quality productive forces, scientific and technological self-reliance, the 105th anniversary of the CPC, the 90th anniversary of the Long March, the construction of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, Lingnan intangible cultural heritage, AI-enabled digital ideological and political education, a clean online environment, and rural revitalization. Works must be free of intellectual property disputes; plagiarism is strictly prohibited. Works from previous editions or those similar in concept to previous works will not be accepted, and no submitted works will be returned.

The organizer holds the right to use the submitted works for non-commercial online dissemination, adaptation, compilation, online and offline exhibitions, publicity and publishing without additional remuneration. The author bears all legal liability arising from ownership disputes over portraits, reputation, privacy, copyrights or trademarks.

## Submission

Colleges are requested to organize the event carefully, encourage student participation, and designate staff to handle registration, recommendation, selection and review. Recommendation forms, summary forms and works must be sent to jlyou@stu.edu.cn before 23:00 on September 13, 2026, and will be submitted by the university on behalf of the participants. Files should be named in the format "Name + Category + Work Title"; late submissions will not be accepted.

Contact: You Jialong, tel: 86502203.
