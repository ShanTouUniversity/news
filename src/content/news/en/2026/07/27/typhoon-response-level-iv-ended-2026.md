It is reported that, according to the Municipal Meteorological Bureau, Typhoon No. 12 "Hongxia" made landfall on the coast at Pinghai Town, Huidong County, Huizhou City at around 03:50 on July 26, and has since continued to move north-northwest, gradually weakening and moving away from Shantou.

Based on the Shantou Flood Control, Drought Relief, Typhoon Prevention and Freeze Prevention Emergency Plan and consultations, the Municipal Flood and Typhoon Command decided at 09:00 on July 27 to end the Level IV typhoon response, while continuing to maintain the Level IV flood response.

All departments, faculty and students are required to act in accordance with the STU Flood Control and Typhoon Prevention Emergency Plan and continue to implement the defense measures of the Level IV flood response to ensure the safety of life and property.

24-hour campus emergency alarm phones: Sangpu Shan Campus 0754-86504110, Donghai'an Campus 0754-86500110.
