It is reported that, to assist with the 2026 undergraduate freshman orientation and help the incoming 2026 cohort adapt to STU more quickly and smoothly, the STU Youth League Committee and the STU Young Volunteers Association are recruiting volunteers from all undergraduate students. A total of 412 volunteers will be recruited and assigned to 16 working groups to assist the university with freshmen reception, orientation planning and organization, and related logistics services.

## Requirements

Volunteers must be current undergraduate students of STU and registered on the Guangdong Volunteer platform (www.gdzyz.cn). They should have strong communication skills and be able to handle reception work with enthusiasm, courtesy, care and efficiency; demonstrate a serious, responsible and hardworking attitude; have good teamwork spirit; obey reasonable arrangements; stay at their posts; and arrive at work on time. Volunteers responsible for reception work on September 11, 12 and 14 must arrange to return to campus in advance and attend the relevant training, with the specific time and place to be notified separately.

This is a university-level volunteer recruitment; applicants should not register for both university-level and college-level volunteer positions simultaneously to avoid schedule conflicts.

## How to Apply

Please fill in the application form via the link: https://v.wjx.cn/vm/Q7Sa0Bw.aspx#, or scan the QR code in the notice. The deadline is 21:00 on July 30, 2026 (Thursday).

After applying, volunteers should scan the QR code to join the consultation group with their real names. Volunteers not admitted in the first round should stay in the group, as replacement volunteers will be admitted in order if any volunteers withdraw, and the results will be announced in the consultation group. If the consultation group QR code expires, send "迎新志愿者" to the "STU汕大青协" WeChat official account to obtain the latest consultation group QR code.

## Service Guarantees and Selection

Volunteers will receive unified skills training and safety protection instructions before going on duty, and will be issued volunteer supplies. In accordance with the relevant provisions of the STU Youth Volunteer Registration and Management Measures (Trial), total service hours for this event will be recorded based on actual service time, and will also be recorded on the Guangdong Volunteer platform.

Volunteers will be selected on merit based on the registration situation and the specific needs of the event. The admission list will be announced in the consultation group, on the campus office information network, and via the "STU汕大青协" WeChat official account.
