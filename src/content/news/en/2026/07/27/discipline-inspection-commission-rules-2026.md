On July 27, the CPC Shantou University Committee issued the "Rules of Procedure for Plenary Sessions of the STU Discipline Inspection Commission" (STU Party Document [2026] No. 41), requiring all units of the university to comply with them.

It is reported that the rules were deliberated and approved at the 17th meeting of the Party Committee Standing Committee held on July 2, 2026. Details of the rules are available in the attached document.
