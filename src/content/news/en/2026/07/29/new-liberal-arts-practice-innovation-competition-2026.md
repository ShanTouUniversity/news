It is reported that, to deepen the development of new liberal arts and accelerate the reform and innovation of liberal arts practical teaching, the 2026 National College Students New Liberal Arts Practice Innovation Competition will be held. The STU Academic Affairs Office has forwarded the competition notice and launched the on-campus organization of the event.

## Categories

The competition is divided into four categories based on disciplinary direction. Participating teams are expected to focus on real social issues and carry out practical and innovative activities such as research, social practice, internships, volunteer service and international exchange combined with their academic studies:

| Category | Focus Areas |
|---|---|
| Literature, History & Philosophy | Systematic theoretical research and interpretation of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, theoretical interpretation of the Chinese path, Marxist theory and Chinese practice, inheritance and innovation of Chinese culture, digital humanities, language intelligence, international communication, etc. |
| Economics, Management & Law | Party building, economic development, national governance, corporate governance, rural revitalization, opening up, global governance, scientific and technological innovation, rule of law, social security, green development, national security, etc. |
| Education | Innovation in ideological and political education, comprehensive education reform, expansion and improvement of educational resources, teacher workforce development, education digitalization and AI empowerment, internationalization of education, etc. |
| Arts | Art research, art creation, art management, production and marketing of art products, art performance, art therapy, integration of technology and art, aesthetic education, etc. |

## Schedule

- Registration: closes September 11. Teams register and submit materials through the competition system (https://xwkds.sdu.edu.cn/); the system will be closed after the deadline.
- On-campus preliminary: held in September-October, consisting of an online review and an on-site competition. The university will organize expert review, with the best entries advancing to the on-site round.
- National final: based on the on-campus preliminary results, the university will recommend outstanding projects in mid-October and organize teams to compete in the national final at Chongqing University.

## Eligibility and Requirements

The competition is open to full-time undergraduate and graduate students (excluding in-service education) of various higher education institutions, and teams compete as a unit. Each team consists of 3-15 students and 1-3 advisors, with one team leader who must be an undergraduate majoring in a liberal arts discipline included in the Catalogue of Undergraduate Majors of Regular Higher Education Institutions; cross-disciplinary, cross-major and cross-level team formation is encouraged. Graduate students may account for no more than 30% of team members. Each person may lead one team but may also join other teams as a member; each advisor may advise no more than two teams.

Each team may register for only one category that matches its background. Cross-university teams are allowed, and the team leader's university is taken as the participating institution. Works that won gold or silver awards in previous editions of the competition final, or works that have already won bronze or above in national competitions such as the China International College Students Innovation Competition, the "Challenge Cup" China College Students Entrepreneurship Competition and the "Challenge Cup" National Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Works Competition as of the notice date, are not eligible.

Participating teams must submit a Practice Innovation Report, presentation materials and relevant supporting materials. The Practice Innovation Report will serve as the main basis for evaluation; presentation materials must fully present the whole process of the practice and innovation activities, covering key elements such as practice duration, form, scale and frequency of visits, and may be delivered in PPT or PDF format; supporting materials include footage of the practice, field visit records, research reports, papers and news reports reflecting the practice process and outcomes.

Colleges are urged to attach great importance to the event, organize it well, and actively encourage faculty and students to participate, with teachers encouraged to guide student projects. Contact: Ms. Weng, Academic Affairs Office, tel: 86502416, email: zjweng@stu.edu.cn.
