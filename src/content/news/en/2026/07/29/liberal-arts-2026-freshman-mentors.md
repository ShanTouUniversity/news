It is reported that the College of Liberal Arts has published the results of its recruitment of mentors for 2026 undergraduate freshmen. Following student applications, college interviews and a review of each applicant's daily performance, the college has appointed 45 students as mentors for the incoming freshman cohort. All appointed mentors are expected to take an active part in the college's training sessions, serve a peer-support role, and help freshmen develop good study and life habits so that they can adapt to campus life as quickly as possible. The college also expressed its gratitude to all students who applied for the 2026 freshman mentor program.
