It is reported that the STU Library has released its electronic resource usage statistics for the second quarter of 2026. Bookan Periodical Database led all Chinese databases with 367,137 views, while Web of Science topped the foreign databases with 254,525 searches.

## Chinese Databases

Among Chinese databases, CNKI China Academic Journals (Online Edition) recorded 290,415 downloads, Superstar Discovery logged 287,136 visits, Duxiu Knowledge Base delivered 166,694 pages, and Zhangyue Selected Digital Reading & Audiobook Library saw 116,432 books read or listened to.
