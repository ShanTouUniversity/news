It is reported that the selection for the 12th Guangdong Patent Award has been launched. According to the notice issued by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security and the Guangdong Provincial Administration for Market Regulation on selecting the 12th Guangdong Patent Award (https://amr.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/tzgg/content/post_4934515.html), the university is now organizing applications, and relevant faculty members are encouraged to take part.

The award is applied for through a recommendation scheme. STU patent projects (or patent inventors) can be recommended through the Shantou Municipal Administration for Market Regulation. Faculty interested in applying should contact the Research Office before August 10, 2026 so that the application can be prepared in advance.

Contact: Zheng Xiangli, tel: 86502583, email: o_kyc@stu.edu.cn.
