It is reported that, to ensure the normal and stable operation of fire protection facilities, the Donghai'an Campus Security Office will conduct linkage tests on the fire systems of buildings in the teaching, experimental and administrative zones from July 30 to July 31.

## Test Schedule and Scope

| Date | Time | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| July 30 (Thursday) | 9:00-17:00 | 2nd floor of Building A (Library), 2nd floor of Building B (Comprehensive Building), 2nd floor of Building E (Teaching Building) |
| July 31 (Friday) | 9:00-17:00 | 2nd floor of Building C (Administration Building), 2nd floor of Building D (Laboratory Building) |

## Test Content

The tests cover smoke and heat detectors, audible and visual alarm devices, manual alarm call points, fire broadcast systems, gas fire suppression systems, fire shutters, indoor and outdoor hydrants, fire pumps and other fire water facilities, smoke exhaust fans, emergency lighting and evacuation signs, and fire control room equipment.

## Reminders

During the linkage tests, fire alarms, activation of smoke exhaust fans, elevator forced-down and brief power outages may occur. Faculty, staff and students in the affected areas, as well as people entering or leaving the vicinity, are advised to stay calm and pay attention to safety. When a building is under testing, people on site should follow the guidance of the staff and avoid taking elevators while the fire warning tone is being broadcast on the floor.

If any abnormality occurs, call the campus alarm number 86500110 or the fire alarm number 86500119.
