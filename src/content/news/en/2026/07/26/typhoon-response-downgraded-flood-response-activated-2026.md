It is reported that Typhoon No. 12 "Hongxia" made landfall on the coast at Pinghai Town, Huidong County, Huizhou City in the early hours of July 26, and will continue moving north-northwest, gradually moving away from Shantou. Affected by Hongxia's circulation and the monsoon trough, Shantou is still expected to see heavy to torrential rain from July 26 to 29, with local short-duration heavy rain and severe convective weather such as thunderstorm gusts of about force 8. The cumulative rainfall of the process is 100-150 mm, mainly concentrated during the daytime of July 26 and July 28, with maximum hourly rainfall reaching 40-70 mm; rainfall is expected to weaken significantly from July 30.

Based on the Shantou Flood Control, Drought Relief, Typhoon Prevention and Freeze Prevention Emergency Plan and consultations, the Municipal Flood and Typhoon Command decided at 16:00 on July 26 to downgrade the typhoon response from Level III to Level IV, and simultaneously activate the Level IV flood response.

All departments, faculty and students are required to act in accordance with the STU Flood Control and Typhoon Prevention Emergency Plan and continue to implement the defense measures of the Level IV response to ensure the safety of life and property.
