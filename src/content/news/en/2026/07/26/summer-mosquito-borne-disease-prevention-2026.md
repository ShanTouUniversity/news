It is reported that, amid the high temperatures and humidity of summer, the breeding of Aedes mosquitoes has accelerated, and dengue fever and chikungunya fever (the "two fevers") have entered their peak season. With frequent travel and more outdoor activities during the summer vacation, the risk of imported cases and local transmission has increased significantly. To safeguard the health of faculty and students during the summer, the university hospital has compiled key prevention measures and practical knowledge on the two diseases and shared them with all faculty and students.

Dengue fever and chikungunya fever are mosquito-borne diseases transmitted through the bite of Aedes mosquitoes. The university hospital reminds everyone to take the matter seriously, learn the prevention knowledge proactively, and incorporate mosquito prevention and control measures into daily holiday life so as to jointly prevent the spread of mosquito-borne infectious diseases.
