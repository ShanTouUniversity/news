It is reported that Typhoon No. 12 "Hongxia" made landfall in Huidong County, Huizhou City, Guangdong Province in the early hours of July 26. The Central Meteorological Observatory issued an orange typhoon warning, a red rainstorm warning and a yellow severe convective weather warning, with strong winds, heavy rainstorms and locally extreme rainstorms in many areas. To help affected students, STU has launched temporary hardship assistance for students affected by the typhoon and rainstorm disasters, providing aid to those who have suffered major economic losses, injury to immediate family members or other significant impact from the disasters.

## Survey Target and Content

The survey targets students who have suffered major economic losses, injury to immediate family members or other significant impact from the typhoon and rainstorm disasters. The survey covers the affected students' family addresses, disaster situations and family economic conditions.

## Reporting

Colleges are requested to reach out to affected students by phone, online or other means to express care and gather information about their situations, and to report each case as soon as it is identified.

- Undergraduate students: contact Ms. Xu, email scholarship@stu.edu.cn, tel 0754-86502249.
- Graduate students: contact Mr. Wang, email o_yjsgzb@stu.edu.cn, tel 0754-86504000.

The university runs temporary hardship assistance year-round. Students who encounter temporary financial difficulties due to unexpected situations may submit a temporary hardship assistance application at any time.

> ⚠️ Temporary hardship assistance application forms are available on the OA system.
