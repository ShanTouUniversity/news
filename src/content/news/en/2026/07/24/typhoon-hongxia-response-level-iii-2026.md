It is reported that, as Typhoon No. 12 "Hongxia" approaches, the city has raised its typhoon response to Level III. The STU Flood and Typhoon Command has issued a notice requiring all departments, faculty and students to implement defense measures in accordance with the STU Flood Control and Typhoon Prevention Emergency Plan.

According to the Municipal Meteorological Bureau, the tropical depression east of the Philippines strengthened into Typhoon Hongxia in the early hours of July 24. At 08:00, its center was about 950 km southeast of Shantou, with maximum winds of force 8 (20 m/s) near the center. Hongxia is expected to move west-northwest at 25-30 km/h while strengthening gradually, enter the northeastern South China Sea from the night of July 24 to the early morning of July 25, and make landfall on the coast of central-eastern Guangdong from the night of July 25 to the morning of July 26 (as a typhoon of wind force 12-13, possibly a strong typhoon of force 14, most likely in the Huiyang-Huilai area). Serious wind and rain impacts are expected in Shantou.

Based on the Shantou Flood Control, Drought Relief, Typhoon Prevention and Freeze Prevention Emergency Plan and consultations, the Municipal Flood and Typhoon Command decided at 11:00 on July 24 to raise the typhoon response from Level IV to Level III.

The university requires all departments, faculty and students to act in strict accordance with the STU Flood Control and Typhoon Prevention Emergency Plan, implement all defense measures, minimize losses caused by the typhoon, and make every effort to safeguard the safety of life and property.

24-hour campus emergency alarm phones: Sangpu Shan Campus 86504110, Donghai'an Campus 86500110.
