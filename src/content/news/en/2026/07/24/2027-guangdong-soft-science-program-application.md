It is reported that, to enhance the province's science and technology consultation service capacity and the modernization of innovation governance, and to advance the construction of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area international science and technology innovation center and a stronger province in scientific and technological innovation, the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology has launched the application for the 2027 Guangdong Provincial Soft Science Research Program projects. The Research Office has forwarded the application notice.

## Application Requirements

Applicant units must be independent legal entities in Guangdong with strong research strength and the necessary conditions for carrying out research work (with some key projects excepted). Individual applications are not accepted, and each project leader may apply for only one project. Applicant units should have long-term engagement in decision consultation research and should provide materials such as representative achievements and application certificates related to the research field when applying.

Project research should be application-oriented and mainly serve scientific and technological decision-making. Applications that are purely theoretical in technology, natural science or humanities, or other non-applied research, are not supported. Applicant units and project leaders should have no adverse credit records, and the application content must be truthful and credible. Research content involving scientific and technological security, sensitive scientific data, novel technologies or major innovations should generally not be published externally.

Project leaders or applicant units under any of the following circumstances are not eligible to apply: having more than three (including three) ongoing Guangdong provincial science and technology projects, having a project overdue for acceptance by more than one year, or having already applied for two projects in the current year; major violations found in audits or inspections of provincial special financial funds; applying for the same project under different titles; project content that has already been applied for and approved by the unit alone or jointly with others; projects not recommended by the competent science and technology department; records of serious scientific research misconduct under disciplinary enforcement or relevant social credit "blacklist" records; or violation of research ethics.

## Collaboration Requirements

If project participants include persons outside the applicant unit (including graduate students), the unit of those domestic participants is deemed a participating unit, and its information must be filled in the application with a collaboration agreement uploaded. Overseas personnel (other than those from Hong Kong and Macao) generally participate individually and must upload an electronic scanned copy of the "Informed Consent Letter for Overseas Personnel" in the online application system; personnel from Hong Kong and Macao may participate individually or as a collaborative research unit. If a participating unit is outside the province, out-of-province enterprises cannot be allocated funds, and the applicant unit should in principle receive the largest share of provincial financial funds.

## Organization and Deliverables

General projects are selected through competitive evaluation, while key projects are assigned through designated commissioning, with projects selected through application, recommendation, review and evaluation procedures. Each topic supports one general project; if a topic receives fewer than three valid applicant units, it will not be reviewed for approval. Approved projects are included in the project library after review and approval, and will be funded in 2027.

In principle, the research period for general projects runs from April 1 to October 31, 2027, with acceptance completed by December 31, 2027; the research period for key projects is determined separately based on research needs. Project teams must rely on facts, figures and cases, and focus on producing substantive research findings and recommendations; the adoption of findings serves as an important basis for acceptance and for priority support in future applications. Research reports must be published for the first time and submitted to the designated email (skjt_qbszhyjzx@gd.gov.cn) within the research period; decision consultation reports of key projects should be formally submitted to the provincial department in the name of the unit.

## How to Apply

Applications are submitted online. Eligible applicants log in to the "Guangdong Government Service Network" or the "Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Business Management Sunshine Platform (http://pro.gdstc.gd.gov.cn)" to submit materials. Applicants without a system account should send their name, department, email and mobile phone number to o_kjxmptk@stu.edu.cn to have an account opened. No paper application is required during the application stage; after a project is approved, the task book (six copies) and application (one copy) should be submitted to the Comprehensive Business Hall of the provincial department.

The application deadline is August 5, 2026.
