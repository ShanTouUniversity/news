It is reported that the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology has recently released a batch of application notices for the provincial Key Area R&D Program, covering five special topics: "biological breeding," "modern agriculture," "key new materials," "new-generation communications and networks," and "new energy." The Research Office has issued a notice urging relevant units to organize project applications actively.

## Application Guides and Deadlines

| No. | Special Project and Application Guide Link | Application Window |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Biological breeding (https://gdstc.gd.gov.cn/zwgk_n/tzgg/content/post_4928012.html) | July 22 - August 21, 2026, 17:00 |
| 2 | Modern agriculture (https://gdstc.gd.gov.cn/zwgk_n/tzgg/content/post_4928008.html) | July 22 - August 21, 2026, 17:00 |
| 3 | Key new materials (https://gdstc.gd.gov.cn/zwgk_n/tzgg/content/post_4927741.html) | July 22 - August 21, 2026, 17:00 |
| 4 | New-generation communications and networks (https://gdstc.gd.gov.cn/zwgk_n/tzgg/content/post_4927740.html) | July 22 - August 21, 2026, 17:00 |
| 5 | New energy (https://gdstc.gd.gov.cn/zwgk_n/tzgg/content/post_4927738.html) | May 20, 8:00 - June 20, 16:00, 2026 |

## How to Apply

Applications are submitted online in a paperless manner. Applicants should log in to the Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Business Management Sunshine Platform (http://pro.gdstc.gd.gov.cn) to view the specific guides, fill in the forms online as required, and finalize the submission five days before the deadline. Applicants without a system account should send their name, department, email and mobile phone number to o_kjxmptk@stu.edu.cn to have an account opened.

Contacts: Zhu Changsheng and Chen Jinshan, tel: 86502813, email (Science and Technology Projects and Platforms Section, Research Office): o_kjxmptk@stu.edu.cn.
