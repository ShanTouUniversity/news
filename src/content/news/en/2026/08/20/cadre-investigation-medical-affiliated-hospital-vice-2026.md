It is reported that, in accordance with the spirit of the "Regulations on the Selection and Appointment of Party and Government Leading Cadres", the Organization Department of the CPC Shantou University Committee issued a cadre investigation notice on August 20. A cadre investigation team will conduct an organizational investigation on August 21 for candidates for vice positions at the Affiliated Hospital of the Medical College.

## Investigation Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Investigation date | August 21, 2026 |
| Candidates | Li Zhi, Liu Muyuan, Peng Hanwei (3 persons) |
| Proposed positions | Vice positions, Affiliated Hospital of the Medical College |
| Organizing body | Cadre investigation team |

## How to Provide Feedback

According to the notice, individuals and organizations may provide information on the candidates to the investigation team via letter, telephone call, or visit. Individuals are encouraged to sign or state their real names; organizations should affix their official seal. All information should be provided in a truth-seeking manner, without resorting to slander or false accusations. Supervision of the work of the investigation team is also welcomed.
