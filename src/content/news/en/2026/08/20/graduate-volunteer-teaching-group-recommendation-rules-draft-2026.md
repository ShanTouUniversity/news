It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on August 20 to solicit comments from all faculty and students on the "Implementation Rules for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Recommendation of Shantou University (Draft for Comments)".

## Background

According to the notice, the draft was prepared to further regulate the recommendation of the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group for postgraduate admission without examination. It was formulated in accordance with relevant requirements of the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Ministry of Education, as well as the "Administrative Measures of Shantou University for Recommending Undergraduate Students for Postgraduate Admission without Examination" (Shan Da Fa [2025] No. 176) and other documents, in light of the university's actual work.

## How to Submit Comments

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Draft document | Implementation Rules for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Recommendation of Shantou University (Draft for Comments) |
| Consultation scope | All faculty and students |
| Deadline for comments | Before August 22, 2026 |
| Email for feedback | o_tw@stu.edu.cn |
| Issuing body | Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University |
| Issue date | August 20, 2026 |

Faculty and students with comments or suggestions may send their feedback to the above email address before the deadline.

Details of the draft are provided in the attachment "Implementation Rules for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Recommendation of Shantou University (Draft for Comments).docx".
