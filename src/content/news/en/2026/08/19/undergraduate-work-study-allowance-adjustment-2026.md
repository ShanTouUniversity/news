It is reported that the Student Affairs Office issued a notice on August 19 on adjusting the work-study allowance for undergraduates, raising the hourly rate to RMB 20.2 per hour effective September 1, 2026.

## Basis for Adjustment

According to the notice, the adjustment follows the "Notice of the People's Government of Guangdong Province on Adjusting the Minimum Wage Standards in Guangdong" (Yue Fu Han [2026] No. 188), under which the hourly minimum wage for part-time work in Shantou will be adjusted to RMB 20.2 per hour from September 1, 2026.

It is also in accordance with Article 28, Chapter 7 of the "Implementation Measures of Shantou University for Student Work-Study Management", which stipulates that on-campus work-study allowances are calculated by the hour: the hourly rate for fixed positions is reasonably determined with reference to the minimum wage standard set by the Shantou municipal government or relevant departments for the same period, and the hourly rate for temporary positions is reasonably determined with reference to the hourly minimum wage standard set for the same period.

## New Standard

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Effective date | September 1, 2026 |
| Applicable to | Undergraduate work-study positions |
| New hourly rate | RMB 20.2 per hour |
| Fixed positions | Determined with reference to the Shantou minimum wage standard |
| Temporary positions | Determined with reference to the Shantou hourly minimum wage standard |
