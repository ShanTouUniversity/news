It is reported that, in accordance with the "Notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security and the Guangdong Administration for Market Regulation on Selecting the 12th Guangdong Patent Award", the Research Office on August 19 publicized the project for which the university, as the patentee, has applied.

## Project for Publicity

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Patent title | A Multifunctional Waterborne Coating and Its Preparation Method and Application |
| Patent number | ZL202410635306.8 |
| Patent type | Invention patent |
| Patentee | Shantou University |
| Inventors | Wang Shuangxi, Lyu Yang, Zhai Chenyang, Chu Yuanzhen, Deng Chuheng |

## Publicity Period and Feedback

The publicity period runs from **August 19 to August 25, 2026**. During this period, any organization or individual with objections or other concerns about the project may submit written, real-name comments to the Research Office.
