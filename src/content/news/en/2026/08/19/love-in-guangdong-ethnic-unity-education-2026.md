It is reported that, to thoroughly implement the spirit of General Secretary Xi Jinping's important speech at the National Commendation Conference for Ethnic Unity and Progress and to strengthen education on fostering a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation, the Guangdong Department of Education will hold the 2026 "Love in Guangdong" ethnic unity and progress education campaign in schools. The Student Affairs Office forwarded the notice on August 19 to organize colleges and residential colleges to carry out registration, recommendation and review.

## Significance

According to the notice, the campaign takes fostering a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation as its main line, closely centering on the major anniversaries of the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China and the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March. It aims to deepen ethnic unity and progress education among students, guide students of all ethnic groups to firmly identify with the great motherland, the Chinese nation, Chinese culture, the Communist Party of China and socialism with Chinese characteristics, promote extensive interaction, comprehensive exchange and deep integration among students of all ethnic groups, unite together like pomegranate seeds, build a solid ideological Great Wall for safeguarding national unity and ethnic solidarity, and strengthen their sense of mission to strive together for building a modern socialist country and advancing the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

## Program Categories

The campaign includes five categories, with detailed plans in Attachments 1–5:

| Category | Theme |
| --- | --- |
| Encounter Guangdong | Themed speech collection |
| Discover Guangdong | Themed photography collection |
| Understand Guangdong | Campus story collection |
| Integrate in Guangdong | Micro-video collection |
| Harmony in Guangdong | Comprehensive design collection |

## Awards

Each category will have a number of First, Second and Third Prizes. The organizers will select outstanding works for exhibition and promotion.

## Requirements

1. Works should have a correct political orientation and conform to the requirements of core socialist values.
2. Works must be original, independently completed by students, with no intellectual property disputes; plagiarism is strictly prohibited, as defined by the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China. Works from previous years or with ideas similar to previous works will not be evaluated; no submitted works will be returned.
3. The organizers hold the rights of online dissemination, adaptation, compilation, exhibition and publication of the works, but assume no legal liability for disputes involving portrait rights, reputation rights, privacy rights, copyright or trademark rights.

## Work Requirements

Colleges should strengthen overall coordination, organize recommendation and review, take the "Love in Guangdong" campaign as an important means to deepen education on fostering a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation, implement it carefully, mobilize students of all ethnic groups to participate widely, ensure effectiveness, and rigorously review works to ensure correct political direction and value orientation.

## Submission Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quota | Each college may submit 1–3 entries per category; limits on number of authors and advisors are detailed in each plan |
| Materials | Recommendation form, summary sheet and electronic version of the work |
| Deadline | Before September 30, 2026; late submissions will not be accepted |
| Email | Student Affairs Office, Ideological Education Section: o_xssw@stu.edu.cn |
