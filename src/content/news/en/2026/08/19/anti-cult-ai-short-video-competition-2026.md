It is reported that, in accordance with higher-level requirements, the Department of Party Committee Publicity and United Front Work issued a notice on August 19 to organize faculty and students to participate in the 2026 Anti-Cult AI Short Video Competition, guiding teachers and students to enhance their ability to identify, prevent and oppose cults through creative practice.

## Overview

The competition is co-hosted by the Guangdong Anti-Cult Association and Southcn.com under the theme "Cults Must Not Be Believed; Participation Is a Crime". As a key campus anti-cult publicity project in Guangdong this year, it aims to further popularize anti-cult knowledge, expand its reach and awareness, and foster a strong anti-cult atmosphere on campus.

## Timeline

The official deadline for submissions is **24:00 on August 31, 2026**. Participants should complete creation and submission in time to avoid missing the deadline.

## Entry Requirements

### Content Directions

Entries should focus on the core anti-cult theme and may address:

- Exposing the illegal nature of cults based on national laws and regulations
- Interpreting anti-cult policies and regulations to improve public identification and prevention
- Explaining the criteria for distinguishing lawful religion, unlawful religion and cults
- Deconstructing harmful training schemes involving spiritual control under the guise of spiritual cultivation, pseudo-traditional culture or pseudo-science
- Sharing frontline anti-cult work experience and positive stories

Content should be substantive, avoiding empty sloganeering.

### Technical and Format Specifications

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Core production method | AI-based creation; AI painting, AI animation and other intelligent tools may be used alone or in combination, with conventional editing software allowed for transitions, music, subtitles and color grading |
| Genre and duration | No genre restriction; vertical short videos under 1 minute optimized for new media are preferred, with a maximum duration of 3 minutes |
| Resolution and frame rate | No less than 1920×1080, frame rate no less than 25fps |
| Format and subtitles | MP4 format with clear, synchronized Chinese subtitles and smooth playback |
| Labeling | The video must prominently display "This work is generated by AI" |
| Title and synopsis | A precise title within 25 Chinese characters and a synopsis within 140 Chinese characters must be submitted together |
| Other | No third-party advertising icons, watermarks or logos; use of the organizer's official "Yue Xiaozheng" IP assets is encouraged |

Official "Yue Xiaozheng" asset download: https://pan.baidu.com/s/1pzaMrno659OgFx_ulu1LLg (access code: 66ht)

### Copyright and Compliance

- All entries must be original and unpublished on public platforms; contributors must hold full copyright and plagiarism is prohibited
- Participants must verify intellectual property ownership of AI tools and generated materials, must not use unauthorized portraits of real persons for AI generation or deepfakes, and must not infringe copyright, portrait rights or reputation rights of third parties; legal liability for copyright disputes rests with the participant
- Content must comply with national laws, regulations and public morals, and must not contain illegal, violent, pornographic or discriminatory content
- Authorship remains with the contributor or contributing organization; exhibition, reproduction, translation and broadcasting rights are jointly held by the organizers and the creators; all entries are deemed to consent to editing and derivative creation by the organizers for non-commercial public-interest anti-cult promotion

## Judging and Awards

The organizers will form a professional judging panel and, in combination with open online voting, evaluate entries on thematic relevance, AI technology application and creative expression to select tiered awards and excellence awards. Popularity awards will be determined solely by the number of public votes. The process will be fully open and transparent. All selected outstanding works will be officially recommended to the 2026 national short video collection and exhibition campaign "Recognize and Reject Cults, Safeguard Happiness" for broader national exposure.
