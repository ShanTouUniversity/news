The red-themed play “Pioneer Youth” was created under the leadership of the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League and premiered at the Guangdong Art Theater in October 2024. It has since toured Zhuhai, Heyuan, Meizhou, and other cities in Guangdong, receiving broad public acclaim. The production is recognized for its substantial ideological content and high artistic quality. As the production prepares for a Shantou tour, the Communist Youth League Shantou University Committee and the College of Liberal Arts are organizing a campus production scheduled for October 2026 and are recruiting student actors from across the university.

## Eligibility

- Currently enrolled Shantou University students with a strong interest in theater performance;
- Applicants with acting ability or stage experience are preferred;
- A strong sense of responsibility and teamwork.

## Registration

Interested students may complete the [actor recruitment questionnaire](https://v.wjx.cn/vm/wZ9vhCL.aspx) by **August 14, 2026**. The consultation-group QR code is available in the original OA notice.

## Plot

Set against the 1925 Canton–Hong Kong General Strike, “Pioneer Youth” follows impoverished children including Deng Jindi (邓金娣) and Luo Zhiqun (罗志群). Guided by Communist Party member Chen Yannian (陈延年) and Communist Youth League member Long Yenai (龙业鼐), they join the Labor Children's Corps, develop revolutionary ideals, and take part in the children's movement before growing into revolutionary fighters under the Party's care and guidance. The play is a story of youth, growth, and faith, presenting a spiritual legacy passed across a century.
