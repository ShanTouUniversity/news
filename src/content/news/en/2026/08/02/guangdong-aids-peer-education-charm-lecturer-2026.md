On August 2, the Communist Youth League Shantou University Committee forwarded a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education announcing the 12th Guangdong University Student HIV/AIDS Prevention Peer Education “Charm Lecturer” Competition, which will be held from September to November 2026. The competition seeks to implement the Healthy China 2030 Outline and the Guangdong Action Plan for HIV/AIDS Containment and Prevention (2025–2030), support the Healthy Guangdong initiative, develop young university HIV/AIDS prevention leaders, improve students' sexual-health and HIV/AIDS prevention literacy and peer-education skills, and promote regular and diverse campus HIV/AIDS education.

## Competition Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Competition | 12th Guangdong University Student HIV/AIDS Prevention Peer Education “Charm Lecturer” Competition |
| Organizing body | Guangdong Provincial Department of Education |
| Period | September–November 2026 |
| Focus | University student sexual-health and HIV/AIDS prevention peer education |

## Registration

STU students who wish to participate must prepare electronic application materials in accordance with the provincial competition notice and email them to the STU Youth League Committee at **o_tw@stu.edu.cn** by **September 11, 2026**. Materials should be named in the format “College + Name + Topic.”

Detailed eligibility requirements, application materials, and competition arrangements are subject to the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education notice.

> ⚠️ Please refer to the attachments in the original OA notice for the full competition notice and detailed application requirements.
