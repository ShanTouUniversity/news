Registration is open for the Northeast Revitalization Industry Upgrade Special Competition under the 15th “Challenge Cup” China College Student Entrepreneurship Competition. Organized in accordance with a notice from the Guangdong Provincial Committee of the Communist Youth League, the competition will channel student innovation toward the development needs of Heilongjiang, Jilin, Liaoning and Inner Mongolia and support the revitalization of Northeast China.

## Five Competition Directions

Projects should address concrete issues in Northeast China through practical research. The special competition has five directions:

| Direction | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Agricultural Green Development | Modern large-scale agriculture, food security, new productive forces in agriculture and animal husbandry, livable rural development, urban-rural integration and rural revitalization. |
| Digital and Intelligent Industry Empowerment | Intelligent, green and integrated upgrading of traditional industries, including equipment manufacturing, petrochemicals, metallurgy, materials and consumer goods. |
| Clean Energy Transition | Wind, solar and nuclear power; integrated wind-solar-thermal-nuclear-storage energy bases; new power systems, energy equipment and energy-use upgrades. |
| Ecological Resource Conservation | Modern biotechnology, the ice and snow economy, forest-understory industries and the marine economy, with a focus on coordinating ecological protection and green, low-carbon growth. |
| International Exchange and Cooperation | Northeast Asian cooperation, the Belt and Road Initiative, the China-Mongolia-Russia economic corridor, border-area development, and international cooperation in industry, science and technology, and culture. |

The International Exchange and Cooperation direction requires at least 30% of team members to be currently enrolled at overseas regular higher-education institutions. Students with dual enrollment under a Chinese-foreign joint program are excluded from this calculation.

## Eligibility and Teams

Applicants must be students officially registered by June 1, 2026, at domestic full-time, non-adult-education higher-education institutions. Eligible participants include associate-degree, undergraduate, master's and doctoral students; in-service graduate students are excluded. Participants must be under 40 years old under the competition rules, meaning they were born on or after June 1, 1986. Overseas students are encouraged to compete independently or form joint teams with Chinese students.

Entries may be submitted individually or by teams. Each team may have no more than 15 members, including the applicant, and each project may have no more than five faculty advisers. Teams may cross academic disciplines, institutions and regions. When a project has multiple collaborators, its category will be determined by the highest degree held among the authors: associate-degree, undergraduate or graduate level.

## Project and Submission Requirements

Projects should respond to specific economic and social challenges in Heilongjiang, Jilin, Liaoning or Inner Mongolia. Teams are encouraged to connect “Three to the Countryside” and “Return to Hometown” social practice with academic research and innovation, producing solutions with practical relevance. Projects must comply with the charter of the “Challenge Cup” China College Student Entrepreneurship Competition and should go beyond the concept or basic-research stage by offering an application-oriented solution or proof-of-principle prototype, together with an intention to pursue proof of concept and develop a business plan.

Required materials include:

- A project summary of no more than 500 Chinese characters. It will be published in the “Challenge Cup” project database and must be de-identified in advance;
- A business plan;
- A project presentation of no more than 20 PPT pages;
- Supporting materials and a project introduction video.

All project materials must omit the submitting institution's name, logo, department name or logo, and the names of all advisers, including off-campus advisers. Participating students' names must remain. Projects entered in this special competition may not also enter another track of the 15th “Challenge Cup” China College Student Entrepreneurship Competition.

## Online Submission

Students should log in to the [national competition submission website](https://2026.tiaozhanbei.net/) between **9:00 a.m. on August 6 and midnight on August 20**, complete the category, team, adviser and project information, and submit the entry online.

The business plan and project presentation must be uploaded as PDF and PPT files respectively, using the templates issued by the national organizing committee. Teams may also provide videos, images, patents, papers, media reports and award certificates as supporting materials. Where business registration, papers or patents are cited, complete details must be entered individually on the project details page. The competition website's submission instructions will govern the specific process.

## Competition Schedule

| Stage | Time | Arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| University recommendation | 9:00 a.m. August 6–midnight August 20 | Universities will mobilize participants, review entries and select projects for recommendation. Teams are encouraged to submit by August 15 to allow time for review and revisions. |
| Provincial recommendation | 9:00 a.m. August 21–midnight August 31 | Provincial Youth League committees will conduct eligibility checks, evaluate and rank projects, and submit recommendations. Project titles, authors and adviser information may not be changed after provincial submission. |
| National preliminary review | To be announced | Project materials will be reviewed in a process modeled on the main competition, producing the final-review list and several third-prize winners. |
| National final review | Held alongside the main competition | Each project may send up to three members for its presentation and defense. Experts will assess development prospects, market value, practical work, innovation and teamwork before selecting several grand-prize, first-prize and second-prize winners. |

Winning projects with strong growth potential and an intention to establish operations in Northeast China will be eligible for targeted services and policy support.

## Key Submission Rules

- The business plan and project presentation must use the national organizing committee's templates, and all supporting materials must follow the same anonymity requirements.
- Business registration, paper and patent information cited in the materials must be entered completely and accurately on the project details page. Counts and descriptions must match across materials, mutually support one another and be truthful.
- Entries must be student-generated outcomes completed within the two years before June 1 of the year of the national final review. For this submission, the applicable period is June 1, 2024 through June 1, 2026. Patents and papers outside that period may not appear in the online system or PPT, but may be included in supporting materials.
- If an entrepreneurial project has established a company, its legal representative must be the project applicant.
- Teams are encouraged to turn outcomes from the “Challenge Cup” National College Student Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Works Competition into entrepreneurial projects. Projects that have won a national award since January 1, 2021 in a previous “Challenge Cup” China College Student Entrepreneurship Competition, the China Youth Chuang Qingchun Competition, the student innovation and innovation track of the “Revitalization Cup” National Youth Vocational Skills Competition, or another national competition co-organized by the host of this competition may not register again.
- Interested teams may scan the QR code in Attachment 3 to join the consultation group. Further competition notices will be posted in the WeChat group.
