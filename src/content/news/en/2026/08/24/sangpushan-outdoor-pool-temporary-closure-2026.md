It is reported that due to equipment inspection and maintenance, the outdoor swimming pool at Sangpushan Campus of Shantou University will be temporarily closed for one week.

## Closure Details

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Facility | Outdoor swimming pool, Sangpushan Campus |
| Reason | Equipment inspection and maintenance |
| Closure period | From August 24 until August 30 |
| Announcement date | August 24, 2026 |
| Issuing unit | Department of Physical Education |

The Department of Physical Education has issued a notice advising all faculty and students to take note of the arrangement and to inform each other. Visitors are advised not to visit during the closure, and the reopening time will be subject to further official notice.

> Source: STU OA Notice (Department of Physical Education)
