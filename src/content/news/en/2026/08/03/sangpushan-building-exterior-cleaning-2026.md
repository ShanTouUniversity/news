To keep campus buildings clean and improve the overall campus appearance, the Resources Management Office will carry out exterior wall cleaning at selected buildings on Sangpushan Campus from August 3 to 30, 2026. The work will be conducted in phases according to the following schedule.

## Work Schedule

| Building | Scheduled work |
| --- | --- |
| Liberal Arts Building | August 5–7 |
| Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Design | August 8–10 |
| Lecture Hall Building | August 11 |
| Science Building | August 12–14 |
| Engineering Building | August 15–17 |
| Administration Building | August 18–20 |
| College Laboratory Building | August 21–24 |
| Air-supported Basketball Hall | August 25 |
| Teaching Building E | August 26–27 |
| Library | August 28–29 |

## Safety During Construction

Pedestrians and vehicles should watch for construction warning signs, follow the posted detours, and avoid entering or lingering around work areas. In an emergency, contact on-site staff promptly.
