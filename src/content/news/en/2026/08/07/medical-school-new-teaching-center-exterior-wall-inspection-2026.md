The General Affairs Office of Shantou University Medical School issued a construction notice on August 7. To eliminate safety hazards caused by hollow and potentially falling exterior wall tiles on the west side of the New Teaching Center, Guangdong Tianhua Electric Power Construction Co., Ltd., the building maintenance and repair contractor, plans to begin inspection work on August 9. The work is expected to last four days.

## Construction Schedule

The inspection will cover the west-side exterior wall of the New Teaching Center. Work is scheduled to begin on August 9, 2026, and is expected to continue for four days. Safety warning signs will be installed at the site. Pedestrians and vehicles should follow the signs, detour around the construction area, and avoid lingering near the work zone.
