It is reported that, in accordance with the "Notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education Forwarding the Notice of the Center for Educational Technology and Resources Development, Ministry of Education (National Center for Educational Technology) on Carrying Out the 2026 Collection of Digital Aesthetic Education Resources" (hereinafter referred to as the Notice), the Academic Affairs Office issued a notice on August 15 to organize the 2026 collection at STU.

## Content and Requirements

The collection covers three categories, all of which must be achievements from the past two years:

| Category | Platform Column and Format | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artistic Skills Resources | Included in the "Artistic Skills" section of the Aesthetic Education Channel on the National Smart Education Platform for Primary and Secondary Schools; video format, no more than 8 authors | Each course consists of 2–8 lecture videos, each 10–20 minutes; format of "teacher explanation + multimedia large screen" with appropriate teacher appearance, quiet environment, sufficient and even lighting, standardized language and clear voice; courseware and embedded media must be clear, concise, well-laid out and stylistically consistent; template available in the "Operation Guide" on the collection system homepage; must include opening and closing credits, with a 5-second opening containing art category, course title and instructor information |
| Artistic Activity Resources | Included in the "Artistic Activities" section; video format, including "Elegant Arts on Campus" and "Special Exhibitions" | Each activity includes 1–8 videos, each 20–50 minutes; multi-camera shooting to ensure visual quality; must include 5-second opening and closing credits, with opening containing activity name, repertoire or exhibition title and performer information |
| Intelligent Application Cases | Focusing on application of digital technologies such as AI and big data in aesthetic education, summarizing replicable typical cases | Includes one written summary (no more than 5,000 words, authentic, innovative, well-structured and properly formatted) and one supporting introductory video (narrative, within 10 minutes, with 5-second opening/closing credits containing case theme and unit information) |

## Video Technical Specifications

Videos must have smooth playback, clear images, synchronized voice, subtitles and lip movements, and clear subtitles, avoiding blurry quality or unauthorized footage. Each video must not exceed 3GB, with aspect ratio 16:9, H.264 encoding at 25fps, resolution 1920×1080P and bitrate 8Mbps; audio AAC encoding at 128Kbps.

## AI Application Requirements

If resources contain AI-generated images, audio or video (AIGC), they must strictly comply with the "Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services" jointly issued by the Cyberspace Administration of China and six other departments, and must be clearly labeled and explained in the resources.

## General Requirements

- Units should strictly ensure correct political orientation and quality, with scientifically accurate content, no infringement of intellectual property rights, no illegal collection or use of personal sensitive information, no commercial advertising, and voluntary authorization for free use on the National Smart Education Platform for Primary and Secondary Schools, with timely submission
- Resources already included in the National Smart Education Platform or selected in the 2025 collection will not be resubmitted

## Quotas and Submission

According to the Notice, STU may recommend no more than 3 artistic skills resources, no more than 3 artistic activity resources and no more than 3 intelligent application cases.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | Before September 1, 2026 |
| Materials | 1. Editable electronic version of the application form and summary sheet (Attachment 1); 2. Resource materials (videos for artistic skills/activities, written material plus video for intelligent cases), submitted via university shared drive link due to large file size |

| Selection | If applications exceed the quota, the university will organize a selection process and recommend the best entries |
