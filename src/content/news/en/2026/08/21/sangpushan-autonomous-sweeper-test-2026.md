It is reported that to upgrade campus cleaning services and evaluate the feasibility of unmanned intelligent operating equipment, the Resource Management Office of Shantou University issued a notice on August 21 announcing on-site application tests of autonomous sweeping vehicles at Sangpushan Campus in the coming days.

## Test Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Content | On-site application test of autonomous sweeping vehicles |
| Test area | Main roads and sidewalks at Sangpushan Campus |
| Test period | In the coming days (subject to on-site operation schedule) |
| Issuing unit | Resource Management Office |
| Issue date | August 21, 2026 |

## Safety Notes

To ensure the tests proceed safely and orderly, the office has issued the following guidance for faculty and students:

| No. | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | During the test period, the autonomous sweepers will operate automatically. Pedestrians are advised to give way and keep a safe distance of 1–2 meters or more from the equipment |
| 2 | Do not touch or knock the vehicle's radar, cameras, buttons or other components; do not operate or adjust the equipment without authorization |
| 3 | Do not gather around or chase the vehicles while they are operating; do not throw objects at or block the vehicles; please help maintain the campus environment and cooperate with the test |
| 4 | If equipment malfunction or abnormal operation is observed, do not handle it on your own; please report it promptly to the property management department |
