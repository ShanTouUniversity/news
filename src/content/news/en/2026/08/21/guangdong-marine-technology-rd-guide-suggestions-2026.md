It is reported that to thoroughly implement the important discourse on building a strong maritime nation, deliver the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee's "1310" deployment and promote high-quality development of the province's marine economy, the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology plans to compile the 2026–2027 guide for the "Marine Technology" special project under the Provincial Key R&D Program for Key Fields. The department is now soliciting proposals for key core technology projects in the marine field. The STU Research Office forwarded the notice on August 21 and is organizing the campus collection.

## Background

The solicitation focuses on bottlenecks and challenges facing high-quality development of Guangdong's marine economy, targeting frontier disruptive technologies, key core technologies, and R&D and application of key components and equipment.

## Requirements

Proposals should be closely integrated with industrial reality and focus on bottlenecks and difficult issues in the field. They must meet the following four criteria:

| No. | Criterion | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Industrialization value | Solve key core technology problems of the industry and ensure tangible new materials, products, processes, technologies or equipment applicable within 3 years |
| 2 | Research base in Guangdong | At least 3 research institutions or technology enterprises in the province should possess high technical level, innovation capability and industrialization conditions in the proposed direction |
| 3 | Technical advancement | Benchmarked against domestic and international progress, the technology readiness level should improve by at least 3 levels, reaching industry-leading standards |
| 4 | Distinct innovation | Topics already supported by the Provincial Key R&D Program in the past 3 years will not be repeatedly funded |

## Scope

Focusing on bottlenecks in high-quality marine economy development, the call covers the following areas:

| Field |
| --- |
| Marine equipment manufacturing |
| Deep-sea exploration |
| Marine electronic information |
| Marine biotechnology |
| Marine resource development and utilization |
| Marine disaster prevention and mitigation |
| Marine ecological and environmental protection |
| AI + marine science |

The above includes frontier disruptive technologies, key core technologies, and R&D and application of key components and equipment.

## Content to Be Submitted

Proposals should be concise and include:

- Proposed guide title, background and necessity, research content, and assessment indicators;
- Total planned investment and proposed provincial fiscal funding;
- Feasibility analysis, expected outcomes and benchmarking against domestic and international progress;
- Information on other competitive teams capable of participating, proposer (team) profile, prior research foundation, supporting institution;
- Liaison of the science and technology business office of the department/unit.

The background should answer "why"; the research content should clarify what will be done and what problems will be solved; assessment indicators should be scientific and quantifiable, avoiding generic or macro-level descriptions.
