It is reported that, in accordance with the *Notice of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education on Conducting the "My Story with the Rule of Law" Collection Campaign*, the Department will carry out the campaign across the province from August to September 2026. The STU Student Affairs Office (Office of Student Work, Party Committee) and the Law School are jointly organizing the campus collection and issued a notice on August 21 setting out submission arrangements. The campus deadline is September 1, 2026.

## Theme and Participants

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | "My Story with the Rule of Law" — works must comply with national laws, regulations and policies, and be truthful and positive |
| Provincial campaign period | August to September 2026 |
| Campus organizers | Student Affairs Office jointly with the Law School |
| Participants | All faculty and students |

## Content Requirements

Submissions should focus on vivid stories of learning legal knowledge, promoting rule-of-law culture and practicing the spirit of the rule of law in the course of study and growth, education and teaching, law-based school governance and work practice. Topics include but are not limited to:

### Student Category (500–2,000 words)

- Reflections and growth insights from studying the Constitution and legal knowledge;
- Experiences and gains from participating in "Study the Constitution, Talk about the Constitution" activities, rule-of-law lectures, moot courts and rule-of-law social practice;
- Real cases of using rule-of-law thinking and legal knowledge to safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of oneself or others and to solve practical problems in study and life;
- Vivid stories of consciously abiding by laws, regulations and school rules and practicing core socialist values in daily life.

### Teacher Category (including full-time teachers and general staff, 1,000–3,000 words)

- Innovative methods, typical cases and reflections on rule-of-law education and teaching;
- Innovative practices and outcomes in integrating socialist rule-of-law concepts into education and teaching in line with educational principles and students' age characteristics;
- Work cases and experience in guiding students to participate in "Study the Constitution, Talk about the Constitution" or other rule-of-law practice activities;
- Explorations and achievements in integrating rule-of-law education into subject teaching, class management and campus culture development;
- Stories of consciously respecting, learning, abiding by and applying the law as cultivators of students' rule-of-law literacy and promoters of campus rule-of-law development.

## Format Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Genre | No restriction |
| Images | 2–5 clear images related to the story, each under 500KB; submission must be illustrated with text and images |
| Synopsis and cover | A synopsis within 150 words and one horizontal cover image (under 500KB) |
| Author information | Name, affiliation and other basic information required at submission |

## Submission Method

All units are asked to widely mobilize participation, encourage creation, and conduct recommendation, selection and review to ensure correct political orientation and value guidance.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Campus deadline | Before September 1, 2026; late submissions will not be accepted |
| Recipient | Youth League Committee of the Law School |
| Email | o_fxytw@stu.edu.cn |
| Materials to submit | Electronic version of the work, synopsis, cover image, etc. |
| File naming | "X School + 'My Story with the Rule of Law' Materials" |

## Other Notes

- Works must be original completed in 2026. Authors must hold full intellectual property rights. Plagiarism, imitation and AI-generated submissions are prohibited. Once verified, eligibility will be cancelled and the recommending unit or school will be notified.
- The Guangdong Provincial Department of Education reserves the rights to publish, edit, publicize and otherwise dispose of all works, including online publishing rights; authors retain the right of attribution.
