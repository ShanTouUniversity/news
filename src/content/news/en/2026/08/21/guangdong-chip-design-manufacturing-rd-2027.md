It is reported that to implement the spirit of the 20th National Congress of the Communist Party of China and the series of important speeches on strengthening key core technology breakthroughs, and in accordance with the deployments of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee and Government on sci-tech innovation and the *Action Plan for the 15th Five-Year Period of the Guangdong Key R&D Program for Key Fields*, the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology has launched the 2027 application for the "Chip Design and Manufacturing" special project under the Provincial Key R&D Program for Key Fields. The STU Research Office forwarded the notice on August 21. Online centralized submission is open until September 16, 2026.

## Guide Access

Applicants should log in to the Guangdong Science and Technology Business Management Sunshine Government Affairs Platform (http://pro.gdstc.gd.gov.cn) to view the guide. Details are also available from local science and technology authorities or the provincial department.

## Application Requirements

### (1) Applicant Institution

The lead applicant must be an enterprise, research institute, university, other public institution, industry organization, or higher education/research institution in Hong Kong or Macao, registered in Guangdong with independent legal personality. The lead should have notable advantages in the field, strong R&D strength or resource integration capability, and undertake core research organization. Emphasis should be placed on industry-university-research cooperation and integration of advantageous resources inside and outside the province, with carefully selected partners. In principle, the total number of lead plus participating institutions per project shall not exceed 5, with clear division of tasks, and each participant must undertake substantive work.

### (2) Budget

Budgets must be truthful and comply with the guide. All projects must include self-raised funds:

| Scenario | Self-raised funds requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Guide requires enterprise as lead | Generally no less than 70% of total investment |
| No requirement for enterprise lead | Generally no less than 50% of total investment |

Joint funding from the city where the project is located is encouraged. Fiscal funds allocated to each institution should correspond to workload, with the lead institution in principle receiving the largest share. Enterprises outside Guangdong shall not receive fiscal funds.

### (3) Application Limits

There is generally no cap based on the number of ongoing projects, but dispersing efforts of the same team or institution is discouraged. For the same special program, the same legal entity (except universities) may in principle lead and participate in no more than 3 projects; high-tech enterprises with average annual R&D expenses of at least RMB 50 million in the past three years may be allowed up to 6 projects (lead no more than 2).

### (4) Project Leader

The leader should play a coordinating role, in principle from the lead institution and substantively involved in implementation, to avoid nominal participation of renowned experts. If a non-lead institution member must serve as leader, supporting documents such as an employment agreement, consent letter from the original institution, and a commitment letter on time investment must be provided.

### (5) Authenticity

Content must be truthful and not exaggerated. Applicants are responsible for authenticity and must comply with the *Provisions on Investigation and Handling of Violations in Scientific and Technological Activities* (Ministry of Science and Technology Order No. 24) and the *Measures for Integrity Management of Provincial Science and Technology Plan Projects* (Yue Ke Gui Fan Zi [2024] No. 2). Applicants and leaders must sign the *Commitment Letter on Authenticity of Application Materials* (template downloadable from the platform, with official seal). Once approved, technical, product and economic assessment indicators shall not be adjusted without proper justification.

### (6) Ineligibility

A project leader or applicant with any of the following shall not apply or shall fail qualification review:

1. The leader has 3 or more Guangdong provincial sci-tech plan projects pending acceptance (excluding platform, inclusive policy, and post-subsidy types), has a project overdue by one year without acceptance, or has already applied for 2 projects in the current year;
2. The leader has an ongoing Guangdong Major Sci-Tech Special Project or Key R&D Program project pending acceptance (the leader may still participate as a team member);
3. The same project is submitted multiple times under different titles;
4. Main content has already been funded under the provincial sci-tech plan, alone or jointly;
5. Projects of Guangdong institutions not recommended by the competent science and technology authority;
6. Records of serious research misconduct under punishment, or court dishonest person / "blacklist" records;
7. Other relevant circumstances.

### (7) Other Requirements

Projects must meet the specific conditions of each topic in the guide, and research must strictly follow technical standards and ethical norms and comply with laws and regulations.

## Application Method

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Method | Online, paperless |
| Platform | Guangdong Science and Technology Business Management Sunshine Government Affairs Platform http://pro.gdstc.gd.gov.cn |
| Account creation | Applicants without an account should send name, department, email and mobile number to o_kjxmptk@stu.edu.cn |
| Supporting materials | Upload as attachments: technical, financial, IP, cooperation agreements, commitment letters, recommendation letters, etc.; enterprises must also upload prior year's corporate income tax return; if lead and participants are related parties, provide explanation |
| Offline application | If online submission is not feasible, submit a written application for review by the provincial department |
| Guide use | Guide documents are for reference only and shall not be reproduced or distributed |
| Post-approval materials | After approval, hard copies of the application and task agreement (signed and sealed) will be submitted to the Comprehensive Service Hall of the Provincial Department (time to be notified) |

Written materials required during review will be notified separately by the professional agency.

## Review and Approval

Third-party professional agencies will organize reviews of background, rationale, technical route, research capability, schedule, budget and performance goals, plus specialized evaluations on technology readiness and intellectual property:

| Evaluation | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Technology readiness | Mainly supports projects at TRL 3–6, which should generally reach TRL 7–9 upon completion, with an improvement of at least 3 levels. Elaborate in the feasibility report with supporting evidence |
| Duplicate check and advancement analysis | Big data analysis will be used for duplication check and advancement analysis of proposed projects |
| IP analysis | Projects should generally yield high-quality IP. Propose high-quality IP goals per the guidance and elaborate in the feasibility report; do not simply use patent or paper counts as goals |
| Project pool management | Approved projects will be managed in a project pool and funded in batches according to annual budget and landing conditions, with staged disbursement |
| Competitive selection | For the same direction (or project) under competitive selection, if fewer than 3 valid applications are received, it will be deemed insufficiently competitive and will not enter review or be funded (unless otherwise specified); with 3 or more, generally only 1 will be funded after review |

Feasibility report outlines, IP guidance and TRL details are downloadable from the platform.
