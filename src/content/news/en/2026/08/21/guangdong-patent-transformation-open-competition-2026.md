It is reported that the Guangdong Administration for Market Regulation has issued the "Guidelines for the 2026 Open Competition Projects for Patent Commercialization in Universities and Research Institutes of Guangdong Province" (https://amr.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/tzgg/content/post_4940046.html). The Research Office of Shantou University has forwarded the notice and is organizing applications on campus.

## Application Method and Timeline

According to the guidelines, the key arrangements are as follows:

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Application limit | Each applicant may apply for a maximum of one project per year; if applications exceed the limit, a selection process will be conducted |
| Campus pre-selection submission | Interested faculty should send electronic application materials to the contact person of the Research Office by 17:00 on September 1, 2026 |
| Submission after selection | Projects recommended after selection should submit electronic materials via the Guangdong Administration for Market Regulation Fiscal Fund Management Platform (https://amr.gd.gov.cn/zscq/) by 17:30 on September 5, 2026, and submit paper copies to the contact person for unified submission |
