The School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering (College of Carbon Neutrality Future Technology) will host an online academic lecture series from 09:00 to 12:00 on Thursday, August 6, 2026. The event is also part of the lecture series of the Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory for the Preparation and Application of Ordered Structural Materials and the Hong Kong-Guangdong Joint Laboratory for the Preparation and Application of Ordered Structural Materials. Four professors from Shaanxi Normal University, Tongji University, Sichuan University, and Hunan University will speak on energy conversion, hydrogen electrocatalysis, water electrolysis for hydrogen production, and single-particle collision electroanalytical chemistry.

## Speaker Profiles

### Chen Yu: Optimizing Electrode Reactions for High-Efficiency Energy Conversion

Chen Yu is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shaanxi Normal University. Chen Yu is a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher, a Shaanxi Provincial Leading Scientific and Technological Innovation Talent, and the leader of a Shaanxi Provincial Scientific and Technological Innovation Team. Chen Yu's research focuses on low-temperature fuel cells and hydrogen production through water electrolysis. Chen Yu has led one National Key Research and Development Program project and four National Natural Science Foundation of China projects. As the first principal recipient, Chen Yu has received one Second Prize of the Shaanxi Provincial Natural Science Award and four First or Second Prizes of the Shaanxi Provincial Higher Education Institutions Scientific and Technological Research Outstanding Achievement Award. Chen Yu has also supervised undergraduates who won two First Prizes in the Shaanxi provincial Challenge Cup competition and graduate students who received six First or Second Prizes at the Shaanxi Graduate Innovation Achievement Exhibition.

As a corresponding author, Chen Yu has published multiple papers in journals including Chemical Society Reviews, Angewandte Chemie International Edition, Journal of the American Chemical Society, Advanced Materials, Energy & Environmental Science, and Science Bulletin. Chen Yu's work has received more than 23,300 citations and Chen Yu's H-index is 88. Two of Chen Yu's papers have received the Shaanxi Provincial Outstanding Academic Paper Award in Natural Sciences.

### Sheng Wenchao: Hydrogen Electrocatalysis

Sheng Wenchao is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Tongji University. Sheng Wenchao earned bachelor's and master's degrees from the Department of Chemistry at Tongji University and received a PhD from the Department of Chemistry at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2010. Sheng Wenchao subsequently conducted postdoctoral research in the Department of Chemical Engineering at the University of Delaware and the Department of Chemical Engineering at Columbia University. From 2015 to 2017, Sheng Wenchao served as an associate research scientist at Columbia University. In 2017, Sheng Wenchao was selected for a high-level young-talent program.

Sheng Wenchao's research focuses on fundamental and applied aspects of new-energy technologies, including fundamental interfacial electrochemical processes, the kinetics of hydrogen electrocatalytic reactions, hydrogen production and fuel cells, and the value-added conversion of chemicals.

### Kang Yijin: Water Electrolysis for Hydrogen Production and Related Chemical Processes

Kang Yijin is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Sichuan University. Kang Yijin received a bachelor's degree from Fudan University in 2005 and a PhD from the University of Pennsylvania in 2012, followed by postdoctoral research at Argonne National Laboratory. In 2015, Kang Yijin was selected for a high-level young-talent program and joined the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China. Kang Yijin joined Sichuan University after 2023 and has also served as a visiting professor at the University of Illinois Chicago and Northwestern University.

Kang Yijin's research focuses on water electrolysis for hydrogen production, fuel cells, and carbon dioxide conversion. Kang Yijin has published more than 70 papers in leading journals including Science, Nature Catalysis, and JACS, holds multiple Chinese and US patents, and founded Tianrui Technology Co., Ltd. to pursue the commercialization of hydrogen energy.

### Zhou Yige: Single-Particle Collision Electroanalytical Chemistry

Zhou Yige earned bachelor's and master's degrees from Nanjing University and a PhD from the University of Oxford, followed by postdoctoral research at the University of Toronto and Northwestern University. Zhou Yige is currently a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Hunan University, and a recipient of the National Science Fund for Excellent Young Scholars. Zhou Yige's research focuses on the fundamentals and applications of single-entity collision electroanalytical chemistry.

Zhou Yige's research has received the First Prize of the China Association for Analysis and Testing Technology Award, the First Prize of the Hunan Provincial Natural Science Award, and the Hunan Youth Science and Technology Award, among other honors. Zhou Yige serves as an advisory editorial board member of ACS Sensors, an editorial board member of Chinese Chemical Letters, a young editorial board member of Electrochemistry and Fundamental Research, and a youth council member of the Chinese Society of Particuology. Zhou Yige previously served as deputy director of the State Key Laboratory of Chemical Biosensing and Metrology.

All faculty and students are welcome to attend.
