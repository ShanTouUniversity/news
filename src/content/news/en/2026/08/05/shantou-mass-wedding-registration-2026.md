The Shantou Municipal Civil Affairs Bureau and other organizations will jointly organize the 2026 Shantou mass wedding under the Chinese theme “家国许幸福·一诺越山海”. The event is a public-welfare activity and is entirely free of charge. STU's Labor Union is inviting eligible faculty and staff who wish to participate to register.

## Eligibility and Quotas

The mass wedding will invite 60 couples in total:

| Category | Quota | Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Newlywed couples | 50 couples | Couples who registered their marriage between July 1, 2025 and September 10, 2026; places are available until the quota is filled |
| Anniversary couples and representatives | 10 couples | Couples celebrating their 10th, 20th, 30th, 40th, or 50th wedding anniversary, along with couple representatives from model-worker, overseas-Chinese-family, and “Most Beautiful Family” groups |

## Event Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Morning of September 27, 2026 |
| Venue | Xiaogongyuan (Little Park), Jinping District, Shantou |
| Cost | Free of charge |

## Registration and Notes

Eligible faculty and staff who wish to participate may complete the [online registration form](https://v.wjx.cn/vm/Ottbh7u.aspx). The notice does not specify a single overall deadline; the newlywed quota will remain open until all 50 places are filled.

For safety reasons, the notice recommends that a couple not participate if either partner is pregnant. The organizers will protect the security of registration information and use it only for this mass wedding.
