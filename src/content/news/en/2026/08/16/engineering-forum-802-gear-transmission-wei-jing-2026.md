## About the Speaker

Prof. Wei Jing, born October 1978, Ph.D., is a Second-Class Professor and doctoral supervisor at Chongqing University, Changjiang Scholar Distinguished Professor of the Ministry of Education, and Deputy Director of the State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmission for Advanced Equipment at Chongqing University. He also serves as Vice Director of the National Standardization Technical Committee for Reducers (SAC/TC357), Vice Director of the Cycloid and Planetary Subcommittee of the National Technical Committee for Gears (SAC/TC52/SC1), Director of the Expert Committee of the Reducer Branch of China General Machinery Industry Association, Vice Director of the Rotor Dynamics Committee of the Chinese Society for Vibration Engineering, member of the Teaching Guidance Subcommittee for Mechanical Fundamentals of the Ministry of Education, member of the ZSJ Platform Technology Expert Group of the Army Equipment Department, and member of the Chinese Committee of IFToMM.

Prof. Wei has long focused on high-speed heavy-duty transmission and systems for aviation and new energy equipment, conducting research on dynamics and vibration-noise control, transmission mechanics, and digital twin and intelligent technologies. He has led more than 40 projects including a JKW 16X thematic key project, National Key R&D Program projects (sub-topics), National Natural Science Foundation projects and commissioned development projects from leading enterprises. As first or corresponding author, he has published more than 140 papers in internationally renowned journals, authored 3 monographs, and participated in formulating/revising 9 national/industry standards. As first inventor, he holds 39 invention patents granted in Europe, the United States and China. As first awardee, he has won three first prizes of provincial/ministerial Science and Technology Progress Awards, one second prize from the Ministry of Education, and one National Patent Excellence Award. His research achievements have been applied to aviation transmissions for strategic aircraft model XX18C and main model XX10, with patented technologies industrialized and equipped on XX20, and related technologies extended to the development of 1.5 MW and above series large-megawatt wind power transmission systems and high-speed EMU traction transmission systems.

All interested faculty and students are welcome to attend!
