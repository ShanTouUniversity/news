On August 13, the Resources Management Office announced that the fault in the off-campus section of the 10KV Yuda Line serving Sangpushan Campus has been repaired and normal power supply restored. To ensure the reliable operation of the university's power grid, part of the campus power load will be switched from the 10KV Diexue Line to the 10KV Yuda Line at 13:00 on August 15 (Saturday). During the switching, users in the affected areas may experience brief power outages of 5 to 15 minutes.
