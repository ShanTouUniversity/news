On August 13, the university issued the Notice on the 2026 Orientation Work (STU Document [2026] No. 226). According to the notice, the incoming class of 2026 will register on September 11, with orientation arrangements running from September 11 to 19. Freshmen are required to arrive on September 11 and should not arrive earlier.

## Pickup Arrangements

From 9:00 to 17:00 on September 11, reception points will be set up at Shantou Railway Station, Chaoshan High-Speed Rail Station, Shantou South High-Speed Rail Station, and Chaoshan Airport. Pickup services are arranged by the Resources Management Office, with assistance from the Medical College.

## Registration Locations

Among undergraduate freshmen, those of the College of Science, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, College of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Medical College, and Department of Physical Education will register at the Sangpushan Campus; those of the College of Liberal Arts, College of Engineering, Law School, Business School, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, and School of Mathematics and Computer Science will register at the East Coast Campus. Each college will set up a registration point downstairs in the dormitory area, where freshmen, accompanied by their families and presenting their admission notices, may walk into the campus and complete registration and check-in.

Among graduate freshmen, those of the College of Science, College of Engineering, Cheung Kong School of Art and Design, and College of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering will register at the Sports Park of the Sangpushan Campus; those of the College of Liberal Arts, School of Mathematics and Computer Science, Law School, Business School, Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, and School of Marxism will register on the first floor of Graduate Dormitory J at the East Coast Campus.

## Registration Method

Freshmen will complete a one-click registration: using their personal QR code in the freshman registration system, they scan the code at their college's registration point and receive their dormitory form to check in directly. The collection and verification of admission notices, ID documents, personal archives, household registration transfer certificates, and Party/League membership transfer materials, as well as facial recognition data collection, will be arranged by each college after freshmen have moved in.

The Admissions and Employment Office and the Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan Affairs Office will set up admission consultation points and consultation points for freshmen from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan at both campuses. Youth League committee volunteers from each college will return to campus early to assist with orientation work.

The university emphasized that all service points and registration points are uniformly planned by the university, and no individual or organization may set up stalls or hang purely commercial banners or posters on campus without permission.

## Requirements

Units are required to practice frugality, keep orientation venues simple but distinctive, and avoid duplicating welcome banners. All orientation staff must wear work badges. Each college should carefully verify freshmen's admission notices, ID documents, and archive photos, and promptly contact the Admissions and Employment Office to check any incomplete materials or questionable information. Freshmen found to have committed fraud or entered under false names will have their admission revoked in accordance with regulations.

The detailed schedules of freshman orientation education, military training, and the opening ceremony are available in the Attachment 2026 Undergraduate Freshman Orientation Activity Schedule and the 2026 Graduate Freshman Orientation Activity Schedule.
