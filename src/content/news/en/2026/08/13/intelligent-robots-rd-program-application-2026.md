On August 13, the Research Administration Office issued a notice calling on relevant units of the university to apply for the 2026–2027 Guangdong Province Key-Area Research and Development (R&D) Plan "Intelligent Robots" special project. The special project was launched by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology in accordance with the "15th Five-Year" Action Plan for the Guangdong Key-Area R&D Plan, guided by the principles of the 20th National Congress of the CPC and President Xi Jinping's important remarks on scientific and technological innovation and the development of the robotics industry, and in implementation of the province's "1310" specific deployment. The application guide is available on the Guangdong Science and Technology Business Service Sunshine Government Platform (http://pro.gdstc.gd.gov.cn).

## Applicant Eligibility and Funding Requirements

- The lead applicant must be an enterprise, research institute, university, other public institution, or industry organization registered in Guangdong Province with independent legal personality, or a higher-education institution or research institution in the Hong Kong or Macao special administrative regions. In principle, the combined number of lead and participating units for one project must not exceed five, and every participating unit must undertake substantive research tasks
- Projects must include self-funded investment, with self-raised funds accounting for no less than 70 percent of total project investment in principle, and joint funding by the project's host city is encouraged. The share of fiscal funds allocated to each unit should match the tasks it undertakes: the lead unit should in principle receive the largest share, and enterprises outside the province do not share in fiscal funds
- For the same special project, the same legal entity (excluding universities) may in principle lead or participate in no more than three applications; high-tech enterprises with average annual R&D expenditure of no less than RMB 50 million over the past three years may lead or participate in up to six (leading no more than two)
- The project leader should in principle come from the lead application unit and substantively participate in project organization and implementation. If the leader must be appointed from a participating unit, supporting materials such as an employment agreement, a consent letter from the original employer, and a commitment letter on the time input of the leader (and team) must be provided

## Integrity Requirements and Disqualifications

Applicants and project leaders must sign a "Commitment Letter on the Authenticity of Application Materials" (template available for download from the Sunshine Government Platform, stamped with the official seal) and are responsible for the authenticity of the application materials. Once a project is approved, its technology, product, and economic performance targets may not be adjusted without justification.

Applications are not accepted where: the project leader has three or more pending provincial science and technology projects awaiting acceptance review (excluding platform-based, inclusive policy, and post-subsidy projects), has a project overdue for more than one year without acceptance, or has already filed two applications in the current year; the project leader has an ongoing major provincial science and technology special program or key-area R&D project that has not passed acceptance (in which case they may still participate as a team member); the same project is submitted repeatedly under renamed titles; the project's main content has already been funded under a provincial science and technology plan; an application from a unit within the province was not recommended by the science and technology authorities; the applicant has an active record of serious scientific research misconduct or is a court judgment debtor on a social credit blacklist; or other relevant circumstances exist.

## How to Apply and Deadline

Applications are submitted online in a paperless manner through the Sunshine Government Platform, where applicants can view the guide and submit the required materials. Applicants without a system account should send their name, department, email address, and mobile number to o_kjxmptk@stu.edu.cn to have an account activated. Enterprises must also upload their corporate income tax return for the previous year. The guide is for applicants' reference only and may not be republished.

Online applications close on September 2, 2026.

## Review and Approval

Projects are reviewed by third-party professional organizations, which evaluate the background, basis, technical route, research capability, schedule, budget, and performance targets of each application, and conduct duplication checks, technological-advancement analysis, and intellectual property assessment. For a given application direction, fewer than three valid applications will be deemed insufficiently competitive and will not proceed to review; with three or more valid applications, in principle only one project will be approved for funding after review. Approved projects are entered into a project pool and released in batches depending on the annual fiscal budget and project progress, with fiscal funds disbursed in stages.

For Direction 2 of Topic 3, a province-city joint project organized by Dongguan City, applications must be formally recommended by letter from the Dongguan Municipal People's Government.
