On August 13, the Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication issued a notice soliciting comments from all faculty members and students on the revised "Rules for Recommending Outstanding Undergraduate Graduates for Postgraduate Admission without Examination (Draft for Comments)".

According to the notice, the revision aims to standardize the School's work of recommending outstanding graduating undergraduates for postgraduate admission without examination, implement the fundamental task of fostering virtue through education, and intensify the selection of top-notch innovative talents. The draft was developed in accordance with relevant documents of the Ministry of Education and the university, and in light of the School's actual circumstances, as a revision of the existing rules.

## How to Submit Comments

Comments and suggestions must be sent via campus email to zhangjiayu@stu.edu.cn before August 17, 2026.

The specific revisions are detailed in the attachment, "Rules for Recommending Outstanding Undergraduate Graduates for Postgraduate Admission without Examination (Draft for Comments)".
