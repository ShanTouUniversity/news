On August 7, the Guangdong Provincial Philosophy and Social Science Planning Special Group issued the notice on applications for the 2027 annual provincial philosophy and social science planning projects. On August 13, the Research Administration Office forwarded the notice and called on relevant units and faculty members of the university to apply. Applications are subject to quotas and organized by each college through review and ranking.

The 2027 projects do not set a topic guide; applicants choose their own topics. Applications are submitted through the Guangdong Provincial Philosophy and Social Science Planning Project Management Platform (https://www.gdppssp.com.cn).

## Project Categories and Funding

| Category | Funding | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Project | RMB 30,000 | Basic research advancing theoretical and academic innovation, and applied research advancing Guangdong's economic and social development |
| Youth Project | RMB 20,000 | Cultivating young researchers and advancing knowledge, theoretical, methodological, and applied innovation |
| Lingnan Culture Project | RMB 30,000 | Excavation, research, preservation, and innovative use of Guangdong's historical and cultural resources |
| Post-subsidy Project | RMB 30,000 | Basically completed, unpublished research results of high academic value that received no prior funding |
| Greater Bay Area Research Special Project | RMB 30,000 | Major theoretical and practical issues in the construction of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area |
| Eastern/Western/Northern Guangdong Research Special Project | RMB 30,000 | Research on economic and social development of the eastern, western, and northern parts of the province and designated cities; open to institutions in 15 cities including Shantou, Shaoguan, Heyuan, Meizhou, Shanwei, Yangjiang, Zhanjiang, Maoming, Qingyuan, Chaozhou, Jieyang, Yunfu, Zhaoqing, Jiangmen, and Huizhou |
| Unpopular Academic Fields Special Project | RMB 50,000 | Research in traditional humanities fields with low academic attention and scarce research communities, including endangered disciplines |

## Application Requirements

- Applicants must be active staff members of universities, party schools, research institutes, or similar institutions within the province, holding an intermediate (or higher) professional title or a doctorate, or serving in a leadership position at the deputy division level or above
- Each applicant may apply for only one project and may not participate in other applications as a team member; team members may participate in up to two project applications in the year
- Project leaders of ongoing National Social Science Fund projects or provincial planning projects (including sub-project leaders) may not apply as leaders; leaders whose projects were terminated within the past three years or rescinded within the past five years may not apply (both calculated as of August 7, 2026)
- For the Youth Project, male applicants must have been born on or after August 7, 1991 (age 35 or under), and female applicants on or after August 7, 1986 (age 40 or under)
- Post-subsidy applicants must provide a plagiarism-check report and, within the past five years, have published related papers as first author in Peking University-core or Nanjing University-core journals or in journals indexed by SSCI, SCI, EI, etc.; where based on a doctoral dissertation or postdoctoral research report, the defense date must be before August 7, 2023, and additions, deletions, and revisions must amount to at least 30 percent of the original text

## Topic Directions

This round sets no topic guide. Research is encouraged in the following key directions: systematic and theoretical research and interpretation of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era; General Secretary Xi Jinping's important instructions given during his inspections of Guangdong; major theoretical and practical issues in implementing the province's "1310" specific deployment; development of Guangdong's county economies; development of Guangdong's marine economy; research on and theoretical summary of Guangdong's reform and opening-up practice; new quality productive forces and new industry tracks in Guangdong, including artificial intelligence and robotics, marine ranching, the low-altitude economy, and commercial spaceflight; Guangdong's local revolutionary history and the legacy of Southern Guangdong pioneers; development of Guangdong's lifelong learning system; Southeast Asia studies and research on Guangdong's historical emigration to Nanyang; and research on the Global South and South-South cooperation.

## Output Requirements

For the General, Youth, Lingnan Culture, Eastern/Western/Northern Guangdong and Unpopular Academic Fields projects, expected outputs are research reports (no less than 30,000 characters), papers (three or more published in academic journals), and monographs (manuscript of no less than 100,000 characters), with applicants choosing one, two, or three forms. The research period is three years, with early completion allowed. The Greater Bay Area Special Project requires one high-quality research report of no less than 30,000 characters within a two-year research period.

## Timetable at Shantou University

According to the Research Administration Office notice, each college should organize review and ranking of applications from its units and submit the ranked list to o_wkb@stu.edu.cn before September 3. Applicants selected through the screening should upload their Application Form and Argumentation Leaflet to the application system before September 4, submit the final version of the application through the system before September 15, and deliver the university-approved paper materials (A3 double-sided printing, bound at the center spine, with the version number consistent with the system) to the Research Administration Office before September 18.

Paper copies required: for the General, Youth, Lingnan Culture, Greater Bay Area, Eastern/Western/Northern Guangdong and Unpopular Academic Fields projects, two copies of the Application Form and six copies of the Argumentation Leaflet; for the Post-subsidy Project, two copies of the Application Form, eight copies of the Results Summary (Leaflet), one bound printed copy of the results (A4 double-sided printing, bound on the left), and one plagiarism-check report, plus paper copies of papers published as first author in core journals within the past five years (journal front page and table of contents for Chinese papers, or a library-stamped indexing certificate for English papers). Where the application is based on a doctoral dissertation or postdoctoral research report, the original text and a statement of revisions must also be submitted.

## System Application Period

The application system opens from 10:00 on September 1 to 12:00 on September 17. The university's review deadline is 17:00 on September 18, and paper materials must be mailed by September 21 (via China Post EMS or SF Express, based on the dispatch date).
