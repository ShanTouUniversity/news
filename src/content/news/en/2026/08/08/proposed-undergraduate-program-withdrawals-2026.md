The Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University published a public notice on August 8 regarding five undergraduate programs proposed for discontinuation in 2026. According to the notice, the proposal was made under the Ministry of Education Higher Education Department's Letter on Carrying Out the 2026 Undergraduate Program Setting Process for Regular Higher Education Institutions (Jiao Gao Si Han [2026] No. 7). It followed consideration of the university's discipline and program development plan, talent training quality, and employment market demand, as well as submissions by the relevant schools, deliberation by the University Teaching Committee and Academic Committee, and final review by the President's Executive Meeting.

## Programs Proposed for Discontinuation

According to the notice, all five programs have not admitted students for more than five consecutive years and have no currently enrolled students.

| Program Code | Program | Disciplinary Category | Reason for Proposed Discontinuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 070202 | Applied Physics | Science | No admissions for more than five consecutive years and no currently enrolled students |
| 050302 | Broadcasting and Television Studies | Literature | No admissions for more than five consecutive years and no currently enrolled students |
| 050305 | Editing and Publishing | Literature | No admissions for more than five consecutive years and no currently enrolled students |
| 120401 | Public Administration | Management | No admissions for more than five consecutive years and no currently enrolled students |
| 120405 | Management | Management | No admissions for more than five consecutive years and no currently enrolled students |

## Public Notice and Objections

The public notice period runs from August 8 through August 18, 2026, for a total of seven working days. During this period, organizations and individuals may submit written objections. Submissions must be factual and include necessary supporting materials. Submissions made on behalf of an organization must bear the organization's official seal; individual submissions must include the submitter's real name, national identification number, and telephone number.

Objections may be submitted to the Academic Affairs Office at o_jwc@stu.edu.cn or by telephone at 0754-86503202.

The university stated that the final outcome for the proposed discontinuations will be subject to Ministry of Education approval in 2027. It will complete the related program closure work in accordance with the Ministry's requirements.
