On August 8, the Residential College Headquarters issued a notice asking each residential college to draft and submit an Overall Development Implementation Plan (2026-2029), systematically planning key work over the next three years in education system building, space upgrades, distinctive development, and brand cultivation, to advance the high-quality construction and development of residential colleges in a more standardized, systematic, and distinctive manner.

## Core Content of the Plans

Each plan must cover the overall development approach, development goals, key construction content, phased implementation plans, safeguard mechanisms, and expected outcomes, including:

- Overall profile and development positioning: clarifying the college's educational philosophy, positioning, target students, partner discipline features, three-year overall goals and staged tasks, and reviewing existing foundations, strengths, and shortcomings
- Education system planning: systematically planning courses, academic atmosphere building, ideological and political education, cultural development, peer support, psychological education, innovation and entrepreneurship, and comprehensive quality cultivation, and improving the dual-college collaborative education mechanism
- Key work and distinctive brand building: mapping out priority education projects, signature activities, brand competitions, innovation incubation, interdisciplinary exchange, and college cultural development over the next three years
- Phased implementation plan: dividing the work into three phases - pilot consolidation in 2026-2027, comprehensive quality improvement in 2027-2028, and consolidation and excellence in 2028-2029 - with tasks, measures, timelines, and goals for each phase
- Safeguard mechanisms and assessment system: defining organizational, staffing, funding, venue, and operation guarantees, incentive mechanisms, and performance assessment, and establishing a routine iteration and optimization mechanism
- Expected outcomes: quantified, achievable results in space-based education, digital education levels, student comprehensive quality indicators, academic atmosphere, dual-college collaboration, and brand influence

## Drafting Requirements

The Headquarters requires each college dean, as the primary responsible person, to lead and coordinate the drafting, organizing college mentors, counselors, and student leaders to discuss with faculty of partner colleges; to differentiate planning based on each college's discipline advantages and educational features, avoiding template copying and uniform plans; and to keep content practical and results-oriented, with emphasis on the actual application of future learning centers in after-class course extension, AI-enabled learning, peer tutoring, and innovation practice.

## Submission Arrangements

Each college must complete the drafting, review, and sealing of its plan before September 20, 2026. Paper copies (stamped with the college seal, two copies) should be submitted to Room 223, Administration Center, Sangpushan Campus (Headquarters General Office), and the electronic Word version to the Residential College Management Office at o_fdy@stu.edu.cn.

The Headquarters will review and file all plans, select outstanding ones as demonstration benchmarks, and coordinate the integrated development of the university-wide residential college education system and distributed future learning centers.
