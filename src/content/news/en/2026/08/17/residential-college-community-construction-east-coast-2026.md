It is reported that the Student Affairs Office launched the application for the 2026 Shantou University Residential College (Community) Construction Projects on July 14, with active submissions from residential colleges (communities) on the East Coast Campus. Following review, the Student Affairs Office announced the approved list on August 17.

## Approved Projects

| No. | Project | Construction Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Public Education Space Upgrade Project, Building A, Shanhai Residential College | Shanhai Residential College |
| 2 | Public Education Space Upgrade Project, Building D, Mingde Residential College | Mingde Residential College |
| 3 | Public Education Space Upgrade Project, Building G, Gezhi Residential College | Gezhi Residential College |
| 4 | Public Education Space Upgrade Project, Building F, Xingchuan Residential College | Xingchuan Residential College |

> Source: STU OA Notice (Student Affairs Office)
