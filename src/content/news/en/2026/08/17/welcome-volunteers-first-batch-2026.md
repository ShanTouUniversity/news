It is reported that to support the 2026 welcome activities and help freshmen of the Class of 2026 integrate into campus life, the university launched a campus-wide volunteer recruitment drive. Following compilation and review, the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University and the STU Young Volunteers Association announced on August 17 the first batch of welcome volunteers for 2026, with 199 students including Wu SJ selected. The full list is available in Attachment 1.

Selected volunteers should check their assigned positions on the admission list and scan the corresponding QR codes to join the respective group chats (see Attachment 2). Relevant training and work arrangements will be notified separately.

Recruitment for welcome volunteers remains open, and all students are welcome to apply. For questions about the list, volunteers may contact staff in the group chats. Requesting units may contact Fang for coordination, Tel: 15016864487, Email: 25xwfang@stu.edu.cn.
