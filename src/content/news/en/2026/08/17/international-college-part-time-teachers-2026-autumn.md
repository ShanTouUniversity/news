It is reported that, due to teaching needs, the International College is recruiting part-time teachers from inside and outside the university to undertake teaching tasks for the Chinese Language Training Center (Chinese Training Program) in the Fall Semester of 2026–2027.

## General Requirements

- Master's degree or above in Teaching Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages or a related discipline of the course to be taught
- Good Mandarin proficiency, with Level 2 Grade A or above preferred
- Strong foreign language ability and cross-cultural communication skills
- Under equal conditions, preference will be given to those with experience in teaching Chinese as a foreign language, holders of the Certificate for Teachers of Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages issued by Hanban or the International Chinese Language Teachers Certificate issued by the Center for Language Education and Cooperation, those with experience in similar courses, and those with experience teaching international students
- Able to complete the entire semester's teaching and undertake at least 2 teaching hours per week (Monday to Friday); those able to undertake at least 4 hours per week will be given priority
- According to the college's overall arrangements, part-time teachers may be required to serve as class advisors and academic mentors
- In-service administrative staff on campus must obtain approval from their home unit in advance

## Course List for Chinese Training Program

| Course | Hours per Week | Campus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comprehensive Chinese | 4 | East Coast Campus |
| HSK Intensive Training | 4 | East Coast Campus |
| Chinese Audio-Visual | 4 | East Coast Campus |
| Spoken Chinese | 4 | East Coast Campus |
| Chinese Characters Reading and Writing | 2 | East Coast Campus |
| Daily Chinese | 2 | East Coast Campus |

Note: Actual course offerings (campus, whether a class is opened, number of classes, etc.) are subject to the final arrangement of the International College.

## Application Procedure

Interested applicants should send application materials (CV and supporting documents) to o_sie@stu.edu.cn, indicating the available workload and intended courses in the email. Application deadline: August 20, 2026. Shortlisted candidates will be notified for an interview; those not shortlisted will not be notified separately. The interview time will be announced separately.
