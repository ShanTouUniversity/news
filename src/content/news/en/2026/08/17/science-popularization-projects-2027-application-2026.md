It is reported that the Guangdong Department of Science and Technology has recently launched the application for 2027 Science Popularization Special Projects, and the Research Office forwarded the notice on August 17 to organize applications from relevant units.

## Organization Method

Projects are selected through competitive merit-based selection, targeted entrustment and other methods according to procedures. For competitive categories, if fewer than three eligible applicants apply for a topic, it will not be reviewed. Proposed projects will be included in the project pool and funded subject to the 2027 fiscal budget.

## Application Requirements

### Applicant and Materials

- Applicants must be enterprises, public institutions or social organizations operating in Guangdong with independent legal personality
- Applicants should provide required materials, with cooperation agreements, commitment letters and other supporting documents uploaded as attachments, and are responsible for the authenticity, legality and validity of the materials; the project leader should play a coordinating role and substantially participate in implementation, avoiding nominal participation by unit leaders or renowned experts; once approved, the system will automatically generate the contract from the application, which will not be modified without proper reason

### Ineligibility

In principle, the following project leaders or applicants may not apply or pass qualification review:

1. Project leaders with three or more outstanding Guangdong provincial science and technology plan projects, with a project overdue for more than one year, or who have already applied for two projects in the current year
2. Those found to have committed major violations in audits or inspections of provincial fiscal funds
3. Multiple applications for the same project under different topic names
4. Projects whose main content has already been applied for and granted under the provincial science and technology plan by the same or joint units
5. Projects not recommended by the competent authority
6. Project leaders or applicants with an ongoing record of serious research misconduct or on a social credit blacklist
7. Those violating research ethics

### Joint Application Requirements

- No more than two participating units besides the lead unit
- If major participants include personnel from other units (including students), their affiliated units are regarded as participating units, must be listed in the application and sign a cooperation agreement; participation in a personal capacity is not allowed
- The lead and participating units should sign a cooperation agreement before submission, specifying cooperation mode, task division, fund allocation and ownership of achievements; the lead unit should have strong research capability or resource integration ability and in principle receive the largest share of provincial funding

## Method and Procedure

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Online deadline | September 7; Topic 1 is quota-limited and the university will select the best entries if the limit is exceeded |
| Platform | Guangdong Science and Technology Business Management Sun Government Affairs Platform (http://pro.gdstc.gd.gov.cn); applicants without an account should send name, department, email and phone number to the Research Office to create one |
| Paper materials | No paper application is required at the application stage; after approval, submit six copies of the task statement and one copy of the application together to the Comprehensive Service Hall of the Provincial Department of Science and Technology |
