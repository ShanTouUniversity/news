On August 10, the Research Administration Office forwarded the notice on applications for the 2027 consultation research projects issued by the Guangdong Research Institute for Engineering Science and Technology Development Strategy. The consultation research projects fall into three categories - major projects, key projects, and special projects - with a research period of no more than one year for all projects (extensions are not allowed in principle). The funding amounts are determined by the Institute based on actual circumstances.

## Project Categories and Requirements

| Category | Funding | Main Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Major Project | RMB 1-2 million | Focused on overarching major engineering science and technology issues in Guangdong's high-quality economic and social development, with no fewer than five academicians participating in the main research; at least one research report and at least one policy recommendation submitted to the CPC Central Committee, the State Council, relevant national ministries, or provincial bodies; at least one "Academician Forum" event; industry-oriented science and technology innovation activities reaching at least two prefecture-level cities in Guangdong |
| Key Project | RMB 500,000-1 million | Focused on key issues in major fields and industries of Guangdong, with no fewer than three academicians participating in the main research; at least one research report and at least one policy recommendation submitted to the central or provincial level; at least one "Academician Forum" event; reaching at least one prefecture-level city in Guangdong |
| Special Project | RMB 300,000-500,000 | Focused on important engineering science and technology issues in Guangdong; at least one research report and at least one policy recommendation submitted to relevant provincial departments or prefecture-level city governments |

## Application Conditions

- The supporting institution must be a higher-education institution, research institution, or relevant enterprise or public institution within the country with independent legal personality, and must have the professional personnel and facilities for consultation research, as well as dedicated project management, financial management, and internal control (internal audit) structures and systems
- The project leader of a major or key project must be an academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences or the Chinese Academy of Engineering (applicants for major projects must in principle be academicians of the Chinese Academy of Engineering); the leader of a special project must in principle be a well-known expert with a senior professional title at the level of full professor or above
- The supporting institution of a major project must be the institution of the academician's personnel relationship or the part-time institution approved by the Chinese Academy of Engineering
- Sub-topics may be set up: in principle no more than five for major and key projects and no more than two for special projects. Non-academician leaders of sub-topics or special projects must submit a written application with justification or provide certificates of senior professional titles
- Project teams must include experts who have drafted policy recommendations that received approving instructions from provincial Party committees, provincial governments, or relevant national ministries, and should in principle include representatives of universities and research institutes, government departments, mass organizations, and key technology enterprises

## Application Requirements

Applications must strictly comply with the Administrative Measures for Strategic Research and Consultation of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, the Administrative Measures for Academician Science and Technology Consultation Special Funds, and the Institute's relevant management measures. The application form should highlight the basis for the project, research content, main innovation points, expected outcomes and performance indicators, and include a scientific and reasonable budget. The Institute does not accept applications for classified projects.

Applicants with ongoing or overdue-unsettled Institute projects may not apply for new projects in principle; the same applicant may in principle apply for only one 2027 consultation research project; units placed on the Institute's negative list are barred from applying. All applications must include a Guangdong research team as one of the supporting units.

At project completion, major and key projects require at least one responsible academician to attend the acceptance review meeting organized by the Institute, and special projects require the project leader to attend.

## Campus Application Arrangements

The Research Administration Office reminds interested faculty to fill in the application form in accordance with the guide and submit the electronic version together with three bound paper copies (A4 double-sided, adhesive binding, cover page with seal on art paper, signed and stamped) to the Research Projects and Platforms Section of the Research Administration Office before September 8.
