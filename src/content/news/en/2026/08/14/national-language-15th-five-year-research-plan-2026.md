It is reported that the Research Office forwarded on August 14 the "Notice of the Office of the Leading Group for Scientific Research Planning of the State Language Commission on the Application for 2026 Projects under the 15th Five-Year Research Plan for the National Language" and is organizing applications from relevant units and faculty.

## Application Arrangements

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Application system | Research Service Platform Project Application Management System of the State Language Commission (http://www.ywky.edu.cn/) |
| Online submission deadline | Before September 4, complete online filling, submission and export of the Project Application Form and send it to o_wkb@stu.edu.cn for formal review |
| Paper submission | Applicants who pass the review should submit two paper copies of the application form (A4 double-sided printing) to the Research Office before September 9 |
| Collection | Collect one copy of the application form before September 10 |

Detailed requirements are available in the attachment.
