The 2026 Guangdong University Presidents' Cup Tennis Competition and the 2026 China University Presidents' Cup Tennis Competition were held in Guangzhou and Huzhou, Zhejiang, respectively, from July 4 to August 1. Shantou University's delegation was led by Wang Yingwei, director of the Physical Education Teaching and Research Department, with Geng Ruiyuan and Ou Yuanping serving as coaches and Discipline Inspection Commission Secretary Feng Xinglei and Vice President Yu Hong competing as athletes. The delegation won the Guangdong event's Group A doubles title and fifth place in Group B doubles at the national competition.

## Guangdong University Presidents' Cup

The 2026 Guangdong University Presidents' Cup Tennis Competition was held at Jinan University from July 4 to 5 and organized by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education. A total of 249 university and education-department leaders from 126 universities across Guangdong, Hong Kong, and Macao, as well as the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education, took part. Feng Xinglei and Yu Hong won the Group A doubles title.

## China University Presidents' Cup

On July 28, the STU delegation moved to the International Red Clay Tennis Center in Huzhou, Zhejiang, for the 2026 China University Presidents' Cup Tennis Competition, organized by the China Student Sports Federation. According to the notice, the event is among the highest-level competitions for university leaders in China. It attracted 453 athletes, coaches, and team leaders from 115 universities across 28 provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities, and special administrative regions.

Feng Xinglei and Yu Hong placed fifth in Group B doubles after managing the physical demands of competing in both events in succession.

## Sports and Education

The Physical Education Department stated that the results reflect the spirit of the Outline of the Healthy China 2030 Plan and the National Fitness Plan (2026–2030), while also demonstrating the university leaders' role in developing campus sports culture. STU will continue to use sports to support character development, encouraging faculty and students to build physical strength through exercise, develop perseverance through competition, and promote the balanced development of moral, intellectual, physical, aesthetic, and labor education.
