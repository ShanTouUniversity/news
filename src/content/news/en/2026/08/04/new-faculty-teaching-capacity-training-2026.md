To strengthen the teaching capabilities of newly appointed faculty, the STU Center for Faculty Development and Educational Assessment will hold a teaching capacity development program from September 15 to 18, 2026. The program will focus on hands-on AI-empowered teaching, applied effective instructional design, and classroom teaching skills, and is open to eligible faculty members.

## Eligible Participants

The primary participants are faculty members who joined STU within the past year and have not previously attended the teaching capacity development program for new faculty. Faculty members who joined within the past three years and are interested may also apply voluntarily.

## Schedule and Locations

| Session | Time | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening ceremony | Morning of September 15, 2026 | Library Lecture Hall, Sangpushan Campus, Shantou University |
| Training sessions | September 15–18, 2026; check-in on the afternoon of September 15 and return on September 18 | Beijing Normal University Zhuhai Campus |

## Program Focus

The program will use lectures, hands-on workshops, model-class observations, and microteaching demonstrations to develop teaching skills. The schedule may be adjusted according to operational needs, with changes announced separately. Expert profiles are provided in OA Attachment 1.

## Registration and Submission

- Units must confirm their participants by **August 12, 2026**, send the participant list in Attachment 2 to the Center at **o_fdea@stu.edu.cn**, and ask participants to submit their information through the [registration information form](https://v.wjx.cn/vm/wFYdxCt.aspx).
- Attachment 3 is a reference list, current as of July 22, 2026, of faculty members who joined within the past year and have not attended the program. It covers faculty members who joined between November 24, 2025 and July 22, 2026. Eligible faculty members outside the list may also be included in Attachment 2. Faculty members who joined within the past year are generally expected not to take leave; those with necessary reasons must state them when the unit submits the list.
- Faculty members who joined within the past three years may apply voluntarily through their units, which will submit applications collectively. As places are limited, the Center will make an overall selection based on applications and provide results by email by **August 15**.

## Travel Arrangements

Participants are generally expected to travel by high-speed rail. The Center will purchase tickets for the following trains:

| Date | Route | Scheduled train |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 15 | Chaoshan–Zhuhai | 16:44–20:14 |
| September 18 | Zhuhai–Chaoshan | 18:08–21:50 |

Participants with other special circumstances should note them in the registration form and purchase their own high-speed rail tickets.
