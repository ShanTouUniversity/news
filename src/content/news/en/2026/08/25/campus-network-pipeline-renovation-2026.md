It is reported that to eliminate campus network safety hazards and ensure stable network services for teaching, research and daily use, the Network and Information Center of Shantou University has organized a campus network pipeline renovation and comprehensive overhaul starting from the summer vacation, scheduled from August 25 to October 25, 2026, with daily working hours from 07:30 to 18:00.

## Background

According to the notice, the university's existing campus network pipelines, building cable trays and related facilities have been in use for many years. Common problems include aging and tangled pipelines, congested pipe wells, irregular occupation by carrier lines, damaged or missing protective facilities, incomplete pipeline ledgers and labeling, and rodent damage to cables. These issues pose considerable safety hazards and have seriously affected the secure and stable operation of the campus network. Through outdoor pipeline repair and renewal and optimization of building fiber-optic cabling, the project aims to achieve standardized management of pipeline facilities and strengthen the foundation for network transmission.

## Main Renovation Tasks

| No. | Task | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Outdoor communication pipeline inspection and repair | Comprehensive inspection of communication pipelines and wells along main campus roads and around buildings; dredging, repair and capacity expansion; replacement of aging or damaged components and repair of faulty sections |
| 2 | Localized pipeline renewal and excavation | For outdoor pipeline sections that are aging, ineffective or irreparable, excavation of road surfaces and green belts in accordance with regulations, re-laying of standardized communication pipelines and optimization of overall network layout |
| 3 | Indoor fiber-optic cabling optimization in buildings | Removal of outdated network equipment in various campus buildings to prepare for standardized fiber-to-the-room upgrades, achieving high-bandwidth, low-latency coverage throughout buildings |

Localized excavation will cover parts of main campus roads and areas around academic buildings and dormitories.

## Expected Impacts

**1. Temporary traffic restrictions**

Excavation areas will be enclosed with construction barriers and safety warning signs. Some pedestrian walkways and non-motor vehicle lanes will be temporarily closed. Parking and stacking of materials near construction areas will be prohibited, which may cause brief inconvenience to daily commuting.

**2. Minor construction noise**

Outdoor excavation and equipment installation will generate a small amount of construction noise. The university will strictly require the construction contractor to regulate working hours and control noise levels to minimize impacts on teaching, office work and rest.

**3. Short-term indoor impacts**

The fiber-to-the-room upgrade will proceed building by building, floor by floor and room by room. Work will involve line removal and installation, drilling at network points, fiber splicing and equipment commissioning, which may produce brief, slight noise and a small amount of dust. The duration for a single room will be short, and the site will be cleaned and restored immediately after completion, without long-term impact on building facilities or office and accommodation functions.

## Notes and Precautions

- **Strengthen safety awareness**: All faculty and students are advised to comply with construction site management rules, stay away from barriers, foundation pits and pipeline work areas, and not to climb over barriers, trample on construction green belts or enter construction zones without authorization;
- **Ensure civilized construction**: The university will supervise the contractor throughout to ensure civilized and safe construction, prompt removal of construction waste, and timely restoration of road surfaces and green belts to restore normal passage and network services as soon as possible;
- **Feedback channels remain open**: During construction, any network anomalies or disturbances caused by construction can be reported to the Network Center's duty office at any time.
