It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University issued a notice on August 25 to launch the formation of the 29th Chinese Young Volunteers Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group (1st STU cohort), recruiting volunteers across the university for one year's basic education service in Xinjiang.

## Background

The recruitment is organized in accordance with relevant superior documents, following the principles of open recruitment, voluntary application, and merit-based selection. As the first Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group organized by Shantou University, this cohort is part of the 29th national Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group program.

## Service Content and Location

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Service location | Southern Xinjiang, Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region (specific site to be determined) |
| Service duration | 1 year |
| Primary duties | Basic education work |
| Additional duties | Rural revitalization, volunteer service and related work |
| Concurrent post | May concurrently serve as deputy secretary of the local township or school Youth League Committee |

## Recruitment Quota

| Category | Quota | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outstanding graduating seniors | 3 | Will return to STU for postgraduate studies after completing service |
| Current graduate students | No quota limit | Subject to supervisor approval |

At the university-level selection stage, 3 candidates are to be selected with 3 alternates.

## Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must meet all of the following basic requirements:

- Outstanding graduating seniors must meet the relevant provisions of Article 7 of the "Administrative Measures of Shantou University for Recommending Undergraduate Students for Postgraduate Admission without Examination"; current graduate students must obtain approval from their supervisors;
- Be a member of the Communist Youth League, support the leadership of the Communist Party of China, and meet relevant political requirements;
- Be honest and trustworthy, with no record of examination cheating, plagiarism of academic achievements, or disciplinary or legal violations;
- Be in good physical and mental health and capable of undertaking basic education volunteer service in western China.

Under equal conditions, priority will be given to applicants who meet one or more of the following:

| Priority Criteria |
| --- |
| Member of the Communist Party of China (including probationary members) |
| Has served as a key student leader at the university, college, or major student organization level |
| Has received "May Fourth" commendation at the provincial or municipal level or above |
| Has obtained a teaching qualification certificate or has registered for the teaching qualification examination |
| Has extensive volunteer service experience |

## Selection Procedure

The selection consists of individual application, college recommendation, university-level selection, and psychological assessment and medical examination.

### 1. Individual Application

Applicants should prepare materials as required in Attachment 7 and submit them according to the specific notice of their college. Note that applications for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group special recommendation and the regular college recommendation for postgraduate admission without examination are mutually exclusive and may not be submitted concurrently.

### 2. College Recommendation

The Youth League organization of each college will review applicants and submit results to the college's Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Recruitment Working Group for deliberation, followed by approval from the college Party organization and a public notice period of no fewer than 2 days. Upon no objection after the notice period, the college will submit materials on behalf of applicants.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Public notice period | No fewer than 2 days |
| Deadline for college submission | Before September 7 |
| Email for electronic materials | o_tw@stu.edu.cn |
| Address for hard copies | Room 223, Administration Center, Sangpu Mountain Campus (University Youth League Committee) |
| Submitting body | Colleges (individual submissions not accepted) |

### 3. University-level Selection

The university-level selection includes qualification review, comprehensive assessment, and public notice.

**Scoring formula:**

| Component | Weight | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comprehensive assessment score | 85% | Including interviews and professional competency evaluation |
| Bonus points | 15% | Recognized according to relevant bonus-point rules |

Composite score = Comprehensive assessment score x 85% + Bonus points x 15%

Candidates will be ranked by composite score and subject to a public notice period of no fewer than 3 days, followed by psychological assessment and medical examination. Those who fail the psychological assessment or medical examination will be replaced by alternates in order. The final list will be submitted to the University Leading Group for Postgraduate Recommendation without Examination for approval. Once the list has been submitted, withdrawal without justified reason will not be permitted.

### 4. Psychological Assessment and Medical Examination

Selected candidates and alternates must participate in the psychological assessment and medical examination as uniformly arranged by the university.
