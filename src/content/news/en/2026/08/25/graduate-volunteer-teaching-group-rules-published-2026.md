It is reported that the Communist Youth League Committee of Shantou University officially released the "Implementation Rules for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Exemption Recommendation of Shantou University" on August 25, providing a formal institutional basis for the university's Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group recommendation work.

## Basis for Formulation

According to the notice, the rules were formulated to further regulate the recommendation of the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group for postgraduate admission without examination. They were drawn up in accordance with relevant requirements of the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League and the Ministry of Education, as well as the "Administrative Measures of Shantou University for Recommending Undergraduate Students for Postgraduate Admission without Examination" (Shan Da Fa [2025] No. 176) and other documents, in light of the university's actual work.

## From Draft to Official Release

The promulgation of the rules followed a process of public consultation, clearance, and deliberation:

- **Public consultation**: On August 20, the Communist Youth League Committee released the "Implementation Rules for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Recommendation of Shantou University (Draft for Comments)" for campus-wide consultation, with feedback accepted until August 22 via o_tw@stu.edu.cn;
- **Clearance without objection**: The draft was subsequently put on public display and received no objections;
- **Deliberation and approval**: The rules were deliberated and approved by the Leading Group for the Recruitment of the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group of Shantou University;
- **Official release**: The final version was officially released to all faculty and students on August 25.

## Content of the Rules

The officially released "Implementation Rules for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Exemption Recommendation of Shantou University" sets out relevant requirements and procedures for the recommendation work. Details are provided in the attachment "Implementation Rules for the Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group Exemption Recommendation of Shantou University.docx".
