It is reported that the Academic Affairs Office of Shantou University issued a notice on August 25 outlining arrangements for recommending outstanding current-year undergraduate graduates for postgraduate admission without examination in 2027. The work will be carried out in accordance with the "Administrative Measures of Shantou University for Recommending Undergraduate Students for Postgraduate Admission without Examination" (Shan Da Fa [2025] No. 176).

## Basis and Principles

According to the notice, the recommendation work adheres to the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality, with comprehensive evaluation and merit-based recommendation. Each school (department) is required to formulate detailed implementation rules for the recommendation work — the "Implementation Rules for Recommending Outstanding Current-Year Undergraduate Graduates for Postgraduate Admission without Examination" — tailored to its own circumstances, in strict accordance with the university's administrative measures and other regulatory documents. The rules must be reviewed and approved by the school's joint Party-administration meeting before being made public to students.

## Organizational Structure

Each school shall establish a working group for recommending students for postgraduate admission without examination. The group shall be headed by the vice dean in charge of teaching, and its members shall include the Party secretary (or general Party branch secretary) of the school, the dean, heads of departments (programs), and faculty representatives.

## National Recommendation Service System

The Ministry of Education's postgraduate exemption recommendation service system is available at http://yz.chsi.com.cn/tm . Verification of eligibility, applications, and admission procedures for exempted students are all conducted through this platform.

## Schedule

| Deadline | Task | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| By September 4 | Schools publish rules; students submit applications | Schools release their implementation rules; students intending to apply fill out the "Application Form for Recommending Students for Postgraduate Admission without Examination of Shantou University" and submit both hard and electronic copies to their respective schools |
| By September 10 | Schools complete recommendations and publicize results | Each school completes its recommendation process and publicizes the results within the school |
| By 12:00 on September 11 | Schools submit materials to the Academic Affairs Office | Each school submits the application forms, comprehensive score sheets, and its implementation rules to the Student Status and Examination Section of the Academic Affairs Office; electronic copies should be sent to xhx@stu.edu.cn |
| By September 15 | University finalizes and publicizes the proposed list | The university's leading group for postgraduate exemption recommendation will determine and publicize the proposed formal recommendation list and the waiting list |

## Special Programs and Notes

Students applying for special postgraduate exemption programs (including the University of Macau program) must participate in this registration and recommendation process and will be included in the school-level recommendation procedures.

In addition, students who intend to apply for the STU Graduate Volunteer Teaching Group program may not submit a duplicate application through this channel; the two recommendation tracks are mutually exclusive.

Attachments including the Administrative Measures, the application form, and the comprehensive score sheet are detailed in the original notice.
