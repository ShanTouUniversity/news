It is reported that to standardize the work of recommending outstanding graduating undergraduates for postgraduate study without examination and to strengthen the selection of top innovative talents with both virtue and ability, the Business School of Shantou University has revised its *Implementation Rules for Recommending Outstanding Graduating Undergraduates for Postgraduate Study Without Examination* in accordance with relevant university documents and in light of the school's actual circumstances. The revised rules were released on August 25.

## Revision and Approval

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Basis for revision | Relevant university documents on postgraduate recommendation, combined with the school's actual circumstances |
| Purpose | Standardize the school's recommendation work and enhance selection of top innovative talents |
| Review meeting | 19th Joint Party-Government Meeting of the Business School on August 2, 2026 |
| Outcome | Approved |
| Release date | August 25, 2026 |
| Issuing unit | Business School |
| Target group | Outstanding graduating undergraduates of the Business School who meet the eligibility requirements |

## Main Content

The revised implementation rules provide for the organization, eligibility criteria, comprehensive evaluation methods, recommendation procedures, and publicity and supervision of the school's postgraduate recommendation work. The full text shall prevail as provided in the attachment.

The Business School reminds faculty and students that the full text of the rules has been published as an attachment to the notice. Subsequent applications, recommendations and related arrangements will be carried out in accordance with the university's unified schedule for the 2027 postgraduate recommendation cycle and the school's implementation rules.
