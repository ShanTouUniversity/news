It is reported that to further promote employment for the Class of 2026, the Admissions and Employment Office issued a notice on August 18 to hold a recruitment event for dispatch personnel of SDIC Human Resources Service Co., Ltd. (SDIC HR), helping graduates learn about dispatch employment to Menzies CNAC at Hong Kong Airport and other employers.

## Recruitment Schedule

| Stage | Time and Method |
| --- | --- |
| Resume submission | Before 24:00 on August 20, send resume to job@stu.edu.cn with subject line "Name + SDIC HR Application + English Proficiency"; indicate foreign language level and Cantonese fluency; applicants with CET-4 but fluent spoken English may attach a self-introduction video in English; CET-6 or IELTS scores may be noted on the resume |
| Recommendation and assessment | The university will compile and recommend resumes to the employer for assessment on August 21 |
| On-site interview | Candidates who pass the assessment will attend an on-site interview in Zhuhai on September 3 |

## Company Profile

Founded in 1995, SDIC Human Resources Service Co., Ltd. (SDIC HR) is a centrally administered state-owned enterprise and a wholly owned subsidiary of State Development & Investment Corp., Ltd. It is a talent intermediary service institution affiliated with State Council departments, a talent development and exchange platform for central enterprises under the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, and one of five central enterprises designated by the Ministry of Commerce for overseas labor poverty alleviation. It provides cross-border human resources services for all countries and regions except Taiwan, and is one of 14 enterprises designated by the Ministry of Commerce with qualification for labor export to Hong Kong.

Through campus recruitment, the company cultivates professional talent for airports, high-speed rail and tourism nursing, building talent reserves while addressing graduate employment.

Dispatch services include:

- **Before entry to Hong Kong**: quota application, recruitment, interview venue arrangement, pre-entry physical examination, signing of tripartite contract, entry procedures and pre-departure training
- **After entry to Hong Kong**: ID card and bank card processing, accommodation, commuter bus, work meals and accident insurance
- **Upon expiry**: renewal or departure formalities
