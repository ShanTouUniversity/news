据悉，汕头大学文学院基督教研究中心近日公布2013年度"基督宗教、生命教育与宗教文化"专项研究资助计划立项结果，共14个课题项目获得立项资助。

该项目自发布以来得到校内教职工的积极响应。据统计，中心回收到的课题报告数量超过预期三倍之多，反映出校内学者对相关研究领域的浓厚兴趣和积极参与。

鉴于申报热情高涨，中心决定加大项目经费投入，增加了入围课题数量，让更多学者有机会开展相关研究。本次资助计划分三个等级：全额资助、70%资助及半额资助。

Christianity Studies Center of the College of Humanities at Shantou University recently announced the 2013 research grant awards for its special project on "Christianity, Life Education and Religious Culture," with 14 projects approved for funding.

The call for proposals received an enthusiastic response from university faculty and staff. The number of applications received more than tripled the initial projection, reflecting strong scholarly interest in the research area.

Due to the high level of participation, the Center decided to increase the project budget and expand the number of awarded topics, enabling more scholars to pursue related research. The funding is divided into three tiers: full funding, 70% funding, and half funding.
