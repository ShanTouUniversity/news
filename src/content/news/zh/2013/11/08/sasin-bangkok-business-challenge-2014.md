It is reported that the Sasin School of Business at Chulalongkorn University (Thailand) has issued an invitation to graduate student teams at the School of Business, Shantou University, to submit business plans oriented toward sustainable development for the "mai Bangkok Business Challenge @ Sasin 2014."

The competition aims to cultivate business leaders with awareness of sustainable development, encouraging student teams from diverse backgrounds to propose innovative business solutions to sustainability challenges. STU Business School graduate teams can represent the university and engage in academic exchange and business competition with institutions across Southeast Asia.

Interested teams should contact the School of Business or relevant faculty advisors for details and registration.
