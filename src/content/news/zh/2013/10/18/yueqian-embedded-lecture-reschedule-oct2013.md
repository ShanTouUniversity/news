The Yueqian embedded system technology experience lecture, originally scheduled for October 19 from 14:30 to 17:30, has been rescheduled to 19:30–21:00 on the same day, according to a notice from the College of Science.

The venue remains unchanged — Building E, Room 303. The college invites interested students from all departments to attend.
